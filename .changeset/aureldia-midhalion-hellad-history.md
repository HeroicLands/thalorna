**History**

- Aelwyth gains a history: the joint Sinalë–Khazári crown, the Great War's ending, how Aldorath, Ondareth and Dúnavarre began, and the Saelvri crusade.
- Vylaria's history runs from the Eagle City's founding through the Western Wars, the Eastern Line, the Archive Fire, the three claimants and the Restoration.
- Heliónis has a history from the Landings to the fall of Thyrenae, with the Sour Plain blood-field, the Sanctuary of the Games and the mage-warlords' ruined seats.

**Corrections**

- Provènzia won its independence in the War of the Charter, no emperor has ruled all of Aurèldía, and Aurèliane began as a Republican colony.
- Nartûm is an independent kingdom, the Cervaron a claim on paper since the early Republic, and Tarûzel joined the Collegium's charter in the generation after its founding.
- Byzaría dates by the Common Calendar, Heliónis's cities kept their own councils after the conquest, and the Iron Wolves were founded in the Amradi border country.
