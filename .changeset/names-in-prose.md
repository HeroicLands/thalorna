**Names**

- The first time a page mentions a named person, place, body or tradition, it links to that entry, or highlights the name where the setting has no page for it yet.
- Names read the way the text uses them, such as Vedyara, the Arcane Order and the Khazari, rather than as full catalogue titles.
- Baron Selvare Zuravel and other Provenzian nobles go by given and house name together.

**Faiths**

- Pages on the faiths tell what believers hold to be true without the narration confirming or dismissing divine action, and they keep each mortal faith distinct from the god it honors.
