- Tarveni words are shorter and blunter, mostly one or two syllables, like the everyday speech they evoke.
- Tarveni verbs end in _-ad_, _-ed_ or _-id_.
- The old mountain houses of Tarvénia take Basque-sounding names such as _Ugarreta_.
- A few Tarvenan characters' names change, among them Nizel Ganotis, Tholmar Gútharen and Gaimel Korvathandis.
