**Language dictionaries**

- A noun in the Vylari, Áelendi, Provenzal and Tarveni dictionaries shows its gender again.
- A dictionary shows a noun's plural where its language has a plural rule, as in Vylari _marendos_, _pl. marendoi_.
- A dictionary says so where its language's verbs do not change.
