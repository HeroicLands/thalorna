The five nations north of the Ki'ich'chik desert are now peoples of their own: the Woodland League, the River Kingdom, the Grass People, the Mesa Towns and the Cedar Kingdom, each with its own way of governing, bound by the Red Stone Compact to stand together against outsiders. The desert lets only a trickle of traders and pilgrims through, the north works copper against the south's steel, and Nordmen raiders, arctic newcomers and southern expeditions all press on the compact.
