**Sinalë literature**

- _Ellevirsa_ (Guest-Right): the origin song cut on a stone at Sirmennë, telling of the valley council that left the valley open to the young human bands.
- _Ansorunnoren saunari_ (The Long-Watcher's Sayings): a Long-Watcher's sayings to the Seer he taught, gathered at Aelirossë by a foster-child.
- _Haulonnaren nelthava_ (The Lament of Haulonna): the Hallowwood's lament for five of Haulonna's dead, whose closing line stays unsung until the valley is freed.
- _Olvandiren aulirarno_ (The Gathering on the End): a convocation on how the world ends that has never agreed, as carried down from Ethalossë.
- _Tirvali_ (The Long Road): a comic tale of a Sinalë scholar in Tānvür whom the imperial roll registers as a road for forty years.
- _Sendevinnoren thellame_ (The Praise of the One Who Stays): the Vardain's praise-song for the unnamed king of Aelwyth's joint crown.
