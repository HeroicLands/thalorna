**Sinalë literature**

- Six Sinalë works are added: Guest-Right, The Long-Watcher's Sayings, The Lament of Haulonna, The Gathering on the End, The Long Road and The Praise of the One Who Stays.
- The Asking, a Sinalëan lay in nine cantos, tells of the Pelwar band that crossed the eastern pass and the herdsman who sat four days at the wood's edge to ask; it is held in Élavendre.
- The Sinalë tongue gains words for the unfallen leaf, a vigil kept outside and a clearing round one tree, and Mallonna and Lauva stand beside Haulonna among its oldest names.
- The Áelendi lexicon lists the names of the lay's people and places.
