**Sinalë literature**

- Seven Sinalë works: The Asking, with Guest-Right, The Long-Watcher's Sayings, The Lament of Haulonna, The Gathering on the End, The Long Road and The Praise of the One Who Stays.
- They tell of the Sinalë's coming, a Seer's schooling, the unreleased dead of Haulonna, an unclosed council on the world's end, and a king who stayed.
- The Sinalë and Áelendi lexicons carry the names and words these works use.
