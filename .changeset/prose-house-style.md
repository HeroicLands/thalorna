**Prose style**

- Every page reads in American spelling: color, honor, labor, defense, license, center, traveler, gray, judgment, plow, draft.
- Dashes in the prose are spaced en-dashes, the punctuation the setting's own voice uses.
- Place names, titles and offices keep their own spelling, so Greyfjord, the Theatre District and the Harbourmaster are unchanged.
