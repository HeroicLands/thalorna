**Kalihara**

- Kalihara's ranks and offices are filled out: who sits on the Council, where the Keeper of the Failing Works and the Master of the Terraces keep house, and how the custodians' four ranks sit in the island's order.
- The works answer people unevenly and not by rank, and Thári'vaan describes who is answered, what the failing does to them, the Sílhari circle and the island's reckoning of years in Walks.
- A new mystical ability, Hearing the Stone, lets a character a work answers know that work's state, and nothing more.
- When a living held stone's work dies, its lineage gives the stone up to a sanctuary.
