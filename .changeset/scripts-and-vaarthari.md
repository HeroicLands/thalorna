- The Dunhara and the desert tribes keep no writing; the settled desert courts write in Qalezu.
- Several scripts are now named varieties of the setting's main scripts: Tharavarkon and Thurarth of the Thuravark runes, Okhatha-zethu and Merazan of Qalezu, and Veldikko of the Semaron.
- The K'otsel cord records are described knot by knot.
- Kaliharans keep their knowledge by memory through the new Vaarthari skill, whose masters, the Vaarnei, hold agreements, laws and histories on record.
- Only Kalihara's keepers know the meaning of the Ithari's few remaining stone marks, which are no script.
- The Gar-Au's regalia are described: the gold serpent crown, the banded thresher, the amber orb, and the formal cape and gold disk.
