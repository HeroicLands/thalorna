**Northern names and words**

- Nordmal and Varokhi names and words are respelled so they read and say more easily.
- Clan names in the Nordlands lose their final _h_, so Snarvarukh is Snarvaruk.
- The midwinter feast is Jel, and the other turns of the year are Somarmál, Midsomar and Vetrnetr.
- Notable renamed characters include Dagbán yra Vethar, Wurlár and Dorghár in the Vristwald, and Skraldthira, the ancestor of the Spear.
