**New skills**

- The Charissa, the Bethuan unarmed art, is a combat skill, with the Charissa Chop, Jab and Kick as its techniques.
- The Charissa's page describes its maneuvers: a throw, a parry, a lock, a choke and a strike at vital points.
- The Path of Breath, Koremdanis Barkratis's sword discipline, is a combat skill. Its page describes its five maneuvers, and the order of its masters has its own page.
