**Languages**

- Élavendri words and names that carry the vowel of _day_ are spelled with _ei_, so it reads as it sounds: _Taeren_ is now _Teiren_, _Raenor_ is _Reinor_, _gaele_ is _geile_, _gaeron_ is _geiron_ and _gaer_ is _geir_. _Araelwen_ keeps its spelling.
- The Tantsou word for _this_ is spelled _wye_, and the phrases built on it follow.
- The Jenchai festival and rite names use the current Tantsou spelling: _Chai Jwi_, _Leng Rwi_, _You Jwi_ and _Shwi Nye Wo_.
