**Languages**

- Most Élavendri words and names that were spelled with _ae_ are now spelled with _ei_, so the vowel of _day_ reads as it sounds: _Taeren_ is now _Teiren_, _Raenor_ is _Reinor_ and _gaele_ is _geile_. A few words keep _ae_ because the _ei_ spelling is already another word, and _Araelwen_ keeps its spelling.
- The Tantsou word for _this_ is spelled _wye_, and the phrases built on it follow.
- The Jenchai festival and rite names use the current Tantsou spelling: _Chai Jwi_, _Leng Rwi_, _You Jwi_ and _Shwi Nye Wo_.
