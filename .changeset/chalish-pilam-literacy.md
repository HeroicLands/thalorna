Ch'alish P'ilam is described as a script of concepts, not spellings, with 24 core glyphs almost everyone knows, a common hand set in columns of two on chalk, slate, bark paper and scribes' vellum, and literacy that grows from workers to priests and scribes. The five northern nations do not use it. Ki'i'chek's list of word-closing consonants matches its words.
