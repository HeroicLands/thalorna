**Site structure**

- The site is its homepage and its pages. The package root at `/thalorna/`
  publishes the homepage; every other note publishes at
  `/<type>-<shortcode>/`. No section directory, tag page, or section landing
  answers any longer. A per-type index is authored as a `doc` note and linked
  from the homepage body.
