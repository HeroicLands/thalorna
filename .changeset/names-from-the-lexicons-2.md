**Real-world names replaced**

- A new Grukar Lexicon gives the calls the Grukar's neighbors write down, the castes' names (the warrior caste is Grukar-Hushk) and how a Grukar name is made, and drops the real-world Grukar names.
- Supporting characters in the Tarûzelian, Byzarian, Khazryn and Vedyaran notes, the factor on the Bethûan quay, and the captains of the Iron Wolves Company now carry names from their own tongues.
- The Ātárzád tribes, their leaders and the Āsháian priesthood carry Ātárzādi names (the war-tribe Zhōqarōd, the crisis judge Zhūdek, the fire-priest Shēkhulsīghot); a Tellumi prince is a relvan, and the Grazian labyrinth is Vurdish.
- Amradad is the Kingdom of Amradad, ruled by the Shêbarôr through a Grand Vashkôr and his vashkôrs; the Mezhvôr is the Shêbarôr's principal wife, and his great officers are ghedvôr.
- The Helonic, Bethuan, Okháric, Provenzal and Vylari name lists offer coined names in place of real-world ones, and the Helionite and Vylarian characters who bore those names are renamed.
- The Élavendri village in the Silver Forests is Sóurimael.
