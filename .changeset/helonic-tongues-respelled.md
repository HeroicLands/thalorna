**Helonic, Bizarian and Bethuan names and words**

- Names and words in the Helonic, Bizarian and Bethuan tongues are respelled so they read more easily at the table.
- Helionite ranks and offices use the setting's own words, such as arsir for the year-naming magistrate and strátimor for the elected commander.
- The Order's currents are written Rhezin Pirethos, Rhezin Zefaris and so on, and the current of metal is Rhezin Sideris.
- Characters such as Júbenos Safithes, Kofios Bolalidek and Kelilazul Frizipáhr have new spellings, and the Bizarian city is now Krusteikhos.
