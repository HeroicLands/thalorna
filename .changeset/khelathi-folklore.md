**Khelâthi literature**

- Five Khelâthi works are added: The Hymn of the Sun-Lord, The Instruction of Khelayu, The Tale of Gezerker, The Suit of the Scribe and the Builder, and The Stela of Quz'Uqa at Zel-Halzi.
- They tell of the sun's walk down the night road, a Keeper's advice to his son, a famine scribe who came home to be asked, two gods suing over the first house, and the stone on the Unmeasured Field.
- The Khelâthi language page gives the words for a hymn, a spell, an instruction, a tale, a lament, a suit at law and an inscription.
