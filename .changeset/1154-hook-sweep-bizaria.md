**Characters**

- Ten people of Krisamar, Thálassos, Bizaris and Órashion, among them the priest Bazia Lokios, the healer Fodios Rores, the innkeeper Agélara Stetes and the shipwright Trufios Veghekos, carry plot hooks that name their own situations in place of stock adventure titles, and each has a short description.
- Each person's private matters sit in referee-only sections, and the priests, officers and guilds around them match the faiths and offices of the Bizarian League.
