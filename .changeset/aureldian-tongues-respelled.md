**Aureldian names and words**

- Names and words in the Vylari, Áelendi, Élavendri, Provenzal, Tarveni, Valtari and Aelwythi tongues are respelled so they read more easily at the table.
- Characters such as Fetar Darel have new spellings, and the Aelwythan characters have new names.
- The goddesses are written Morvana and Lusinia everywhere.
- The free port of Calypsa is now Kalipsa, and its people the Kalipsi.
- The Provenzal words for magic and for a tongue each stand as one entry that carries both senses.
