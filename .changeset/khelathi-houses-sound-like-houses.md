**A Khelâthi house sounds like a house.** Every house name in Aû'Khelâthu now closes on the collective
that says "the people of" and marks the seam between its elements, so Izaret of the Gul'Zekeru and
Thalura of the Iqe'Lêru are heard as members of a line rather than as two people with long names.

**A Khelâthi person sounds like a person.** The seam has come out of the personal names that were
wearing one — Lersaîs, Mertelu, Parri, Gatnefur, Ziprahu, Thamiyra, Thotkar — so a name spoken at a
door says at once whether it is the caller or the caller's house.
