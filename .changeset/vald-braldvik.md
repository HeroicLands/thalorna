**Vald**

- The fishing town Brennvik is now Braldvík, the one place on the Vylarian coast with a northern name, left by a raiding crew stranded there in 523 AF.
- Its note tells how the bay burned, how the town and the raiders got through the winter together, and why its smoke-fish barrels carry runes nobody there can read.
- Coal Night, the Old Coal and the town's yearly bill to the Castamar admiralty give a GM four ready hooks.
- A new history note, The Winter of the Banked Fire, dates the landing in both the Common Calendar and the Dómsey count.
