- The rainforests of central Xerathia have their peoples, the Biko: many small hunting and foraging tribes, each with its own leader and nothing above it.
- They follow four loose traditions, the river-keepers, the canopy foragers, the court-keepers and the edge-people, each with its own page on how it lives, makes its leaders and buries its dead.
- All of them speak one tongue, Ekpelu, a tonal language, with a word list of their names, the tones of every word and the sacred words each tradition keeps.
- The Leopard King is now the feared sorcerer-leader of a single forest tribe, whose neighbours bring him gifts out of fear, not a king taking tribute from several peoples.
