**Languages and scripts**

- Each language's page says how its words are made: which sounds it favours, where it puts the stress and how it builds new names.
- Every tongue's stress follows the one rule in Pronouncing Thalorna, so a long word in Áelendi, Nordmal, Sinale, Sovidur or Tellumi is stressed on its second-to-last syllable.
