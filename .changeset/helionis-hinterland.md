**Places**

- Helionis has a hinterland: Khaddaria, the mountain mining country behind the coast, with twelve fortified towns, the villages around them, and the marauders and the Black Flame that threaten them.
- Each of the twelve towns has its own page, stating the city-state that holds it, what feeds it and what it sends out. Eight are mining towns, among them Khasidon (iron), Nafrithos (lead and silver) and Sufanos (alum and sulfur). Four are caravan towns at the foot of the last ridge, on the roads to the inland seas, among them Mubaria and Zarakia.
- Thirenae, Theradon, Athenikos, Kalidria and Korinthea link the hinterland towns they hold, and the Helionis page links Khaddaria.
