- Sinale words and names are respelled to sound closer to the old northern speech they evoke: harder _t_, _k_ and _p_, held consonants and no _th_.
- Sinale given names run two to four syllables, and a name keeps its spelling when it is called.
- Several Sinale characters' and lineages' names change, and the Sinale script takes the name _Veldikko_.
