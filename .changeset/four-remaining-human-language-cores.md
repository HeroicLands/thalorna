**Core vocabulary and everyday speech**

- Tellumi, Sovidur, Ekpelu and Taruzeli gain ordinary expressions for the full 921-concept core, with worked examples for reference, possession, questions, negation, time, comparisons, conditions, ability, permission and obligation. Existing words, names and pronunciation are retained.
