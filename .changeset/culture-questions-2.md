**Peoples at home** — The culture accounts of three more peoples now describe ordinary life from childhood to the deathbed, who each admires, and what each gets wrong.

- **Byzarians** treat the kept bargain as a moral center, and the account follows it into their guilds, their tombs and the village the League will not name.
- **Provenzians** live by patronage and taste, and the account follows it into their festivals, their dead and the field they farm in silence.
- **Tarvénans** are judged by their word, and the account follows it into oaths on a stone, lanterns for the dead and what the hill-baronies shelter.
