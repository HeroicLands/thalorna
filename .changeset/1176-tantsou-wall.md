**Places**

- Tantsou's great wall, the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires, has its own page: its carved and gilded stone near the capital, its brick and rammed earth farther out, its ancient sigil-carved north against the undead, and the papers its gates demand. The Tantsou, Khazrin and Elder Races pages now mention it.
