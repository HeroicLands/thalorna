**Kalihara**

- The island is a ring of tall, cloud-wrapped mountains around a great central bowl, with a lake in the bowl and a massif standing in it; the Ring, the Great Bowl, the Bowl Lake and the Massif each have a place note.
- Six forest and shore animals have entries with Kalihári names and stats: the Canopy Glider, Ember-eyed Cat, Dusk Swift, Terrace Mole, Island Turtle and Reef Lantern.
- The Bowl Lake has the Reed Heron and the Deepwater Fish, and the Ring's cliffs the Cloud Goat.
- The Mangrove Behemoth has a Kalihári name, Vaarvanu, and a Flora of Kalihara page describes the Stilt Tree and the Ember Bloom.
- The Kalihara pages link to the creatures found nowhere else.
