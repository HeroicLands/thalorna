**Places**

- The northern peoples, the uninhabited desert barrier, the city-state heartland and the southern wastelands of Ki'ich'chik are set out clearly.

**Languages and scripts**

- Ki'i'chek has usable expressions for all 921 core concepts and worked everyday grammar, keeping its established names and vocabulary and telling glottal catches from ritual pitch.
- Ch'alish P'ilam has compact working signs and elaborate pictorial ceremonial forms, with shared readings, limited occupational literacy and wild fig inner-bark paper.
- Ki'i'chek and Ki'ich'chik names and pronunciations are standardized.
