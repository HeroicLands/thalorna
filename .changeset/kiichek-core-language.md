**Language and writing**

- Develop Ki'i'chek with usable expressions for all 921 core concepts and worked everyday grammar, preserving established names and vocabulary and distinguishing glottal catches from ritual pitch.
- Describe Ch'alish P'ilam's compact working signs and elaborate pictorial ceremonial forms, their shared readings, limited occupational literacy, and wild fig inner-bark paper.
- Standardize Ki'i'chek and Ki'ich'chik names, pronunciations, filenames, shortcodes and references, and clarify the northern peoples, uninhabited desert barrier, city-state heartland and southern wastelands.
