**History**

- Provènzia gains a history from Aurèliane's founding through Tiravlen, the war for the Charter and the War of the Princes; Tarvénia from the lowlands taken to the first High King and the Order of the Sealed Word.
- Byzaría gains the Battle of the Wells, the Compact of the Five that founded the League, the Empire's annexation and the siege of the Silver Citadel; Heliónis gains the Academy of the Silver Veil.
- Vylaria's history runs on through the Black Marsh, the Settlement of the Crowns, the Twelfth's loss in the Cervaron and the reform of the commissions; from the Settlement, Heliónis is a province on the imperial roll only.
- The Cervaron passage-gift, Moktur's march, the Empire's military and the unpaid fort in the Hellád now name the events behind them.
- New blood-fields: the Blood Hills, the Horn Gorge, the Thirsting Ground and the Black Marsh, with the Twelfth's Last Camp as a ruined frontier fort.

**Corrections**

- The adventure's Provènzia summary agrees with the setting: the Thânevar are the country's old people, and its kings carry their setting names.
