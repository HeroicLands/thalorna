**Aû'Khelâthu**

- The temple count of years is divided into eight ages, from the First Tablets to the House of Meqes, each with the rulers the king-list remembers.
- Twenty events explain the present: the crossing to Galezkara, the War of the Two Thrones, the hill-nomads' and sea-raiders' thrones, the False Uqa'â's famine and judgment, the Vylarian conquest and the Great Removal.
- Eleven sites carry that history on the ground, among them three cursed battlefields the Khelâthi call _zelqezat_, each with adventure hooks.
- Galezkara, the Guild notes, Okháris and Bethûa now agree with the king-list about dates, conquests and directions.

**The Vylarian Calendar**

- The shared year-count is called the Vylarian Calendar everywhere, since it belongs to the Vylarian Empire.
