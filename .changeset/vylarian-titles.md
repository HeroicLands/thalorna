**Ôrzâra.** The inner city, the great palace, the imperial guard and the six hills now carry Vylari names, from the Arvon and the Palvar to the Sorvan and the hills Arvel, Selvin and Antel.

**The empire's bodies.** The treasury, the tax office, the senate house, the city council, the moneylenders' guild and the great college of guilds are the Tarmex Orzan, Tolvan, Seldan, Verdan Ôrzâra, Danvar and Tevran Maldor.

**Priesthoods.** The Aurèldían faiths' ranks take Vylari titles: the Mentara of Mârendâs, the Lizena, the Volsarn of Árdavon, the Bravsarn and the Nirvena.

**Ranks, coin, legions and law.** The sovereign is the Ôrzâs, and the legions, their ranks, the empire's coins and its taxes carry Vylari names, from the Ardin and Solden to the Pelsan Hilvan, while the law of magic is the Kandrol.

**Provenzia and Tarvénia.** Towns, hills, clans and characters whose names were borrowed words take short names from their own tongue's roots, such as Kalvèza, Jalnazdàl, Volsathèr, Herthéma, Thórgila and Dálven.

**Valtári.** The harbor pidgin is named for the port of Valthári, is called a pidgin rather than a creole, and draws its words from Tarûzeli, Helonic, the Vylari family, Nordmal and other tongues of the setting.
