**Places**

- Bizaria has nine more towns: Zalvarion, Siderash and Bruzash in the northern mining mountains; Tolzur, Parzhenar, Vezhrelion and Krenel along the march roads to Amradad and Dunhara; Oinelena in the lowland wine and oil country; and Halzikos at an inland sea. Each page says what feeds the town, what it sends out and which city-state holds it.
- The Black Flame has a strong presence in the country around Tolzur, Parzhenar and Vezhrelion.
