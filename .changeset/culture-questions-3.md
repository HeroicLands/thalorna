**Peoples at home** — The culture accounts of three more peoples now describe ordinary life from childhood to the deathbed, who each admires, and what each gets wrong.

- **Vedyari** read a person's station from the wrist, and the account follows that into their temples and sabhās, their dead and the fields left unburned.
- **Tellumi** keep their record on clay, and the account follows it into their water, their exile, and the names that must be read before the dead count as received.
- **Tarûzelians** live by the kept bargain, and the account follows it into their houses, their credit and the promise the revolution broke.
