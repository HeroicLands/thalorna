**Places**

- Grukarholm has places to go: the Drowned Country marsh, a cause-walk of sunken logs and a raid landing, each with its present and hooks, and Grimak linked as its blood-field.
- Velanthia's paid wardens have two stone forts, Torvogor Fort and Sedrovod Fort, with late pay, sold passage and the bell and signal fire of the village beneath.
