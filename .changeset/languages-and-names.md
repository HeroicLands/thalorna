**Languages and names**

- Khazari words and names are respelled to sound like the stone-tongue they evoke, house and hold names are written in two dashed parts (_Dalk-thumár_), and several characters, places and titles are renamed, among them _Hulabat_ and _Hudakal_.
- The Khazari pages carry a phrasebook, a gate-cry, a forge oath, a maker's mark, a toast and the words over a tomb, each with its pronunciation and meaning.
- Sinale, Tarveni and Vedyari words and names are respelled closer to the speech they evoke, and many characters, lineages, places and Janpodas are renamed in each.
- Khelathi names write _ch_ for _tj_ (_Chaq'ur_, _Chelsur_), the longest names are shorter, and Khelathi gains numerals, ten everyday verbs and the words of the sacred river hunt.
- Vylari asks, commands and exclaims with words of its own (_eza_, _areza_, _vez_, _voza_, _aia_), and reports speech with _vethor_, "says".
- The pronunciation guide covers vowel _y_, long vowels, doubled consonants, _ph_ and the vowel pairs _ie_, _uo_ and _ae_.
- Vylari gains some ninety words, among them the senses, the passions and four kinds of love, and its grammar page sets out its plurals, its six present forms, "of", time words and "let, must, may and can".
- Vylari is stressed by the pronunciation guide's rule, and its third-person verbs end in _-zor_: _lokazor_, _falvazor_, _yarezor_.
