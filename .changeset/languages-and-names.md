**Languages and names**

- Khazari words and names are respelled to sound like the stone-tongue they evoke, house and hold names are written in two dashed parts (_Dalk-thumár_), and several characters, places and titles are renamed, among them _Hulabat_ and _Hudakal_.
- The Khazari pages carry a phrasebook, a gate-cry, a forge oath, a maker's mark, a toast and the words over a tomb, each with its pronunciation and meaning.
- Sinale, Tarveni and Vedyari words and names are respelled closer to the speech they evoke, and many characters, lineages, places and Janpodas are renamed in each.
- Khelathi names write _ch_ for _tj_ (_Chaq'ur_, _Chelsur_), the longest names are shorter, and Khelathi gains numerals, ten everyday verbs and the words of the sacred river hunt.
- Vylari asks, commands and exclaims with words of its own (_eza_, _areza_, _vez_, _voza_, _aia_), and reports speech with _vethor_, "says".
- The pronunciation guide covers vowel _y_, long vowels, doubled consonants, _ph_ and the vowel pairs _ie_, _uo_, _ae_, _ao_ and _ui_.
- Vylari gains some ninety words, among them the senses, the passions and four kinds of love, and its grammar page sets out its plurals, its six present forms, "of", time words and "let, must, may and can".
- Vylari is stressed by the pronunciation guide's rule, and its third-person verbs end in _-zor_: _lokazor_, _falvazor_, _yarezor_.
- Vylari has words for "perhaps", "truth" and "truly" (_zikai_, _varelza_, _varlai_), and the Morning Call and the Second Reading turn on _zikai_, the word that marks a thing as only alleged.
- The setting lists the core words every human language covers, and Vylari, Tantsou, Áelendi and Élavendri have a word for each; Provenzal and Tarveni share Vylari's.
- Áelendi borrows from Sinale alone, and its everyday words are its own; Élavendri takes some Vylari words for the city's furniture, tableware and dress, among them _ómbela_ (a chair) and _vémrai_ (clothes).
- The Grukar converse by body language and scent; their grunts and bellows only lend emphasis, they keep no names or script, and only the Grukar-Sha learn to speak human tongues.
- Vedyari's word endings are shown as they are written: _-oka_, _-ona_, _-uta_, _-ru_ and _-ija_, and the station names are spelled _Karmija_, _Danhija_, _Sentija_ and _Ritrija_.
- The language pages no longer carry sound-change step tables or the sections that listed them: Provenzal, Tarveni, Áelendi, Élavendri, Vylari, Helonic and Jurthati keep their words, sources and sound rules.
