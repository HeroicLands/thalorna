**History**

- Heliónis gains the War of the Harbors, the Truce of the Games that followed it, and the Concordat of Theory between its cities and the Ordo Arcanis.
- Byzaría gains the Re-swearing of the Compact, which made the League and its three marches, and the Ordinance of Arbiters, the origin of professional arbitration in place of feud.
- The Unpaid Fort joins the gazetteer, a Vylarian-age fortress whose commander files a yearly report with an Empire that does not answer.
- The Separation of Bethûa, the Decline Begins and the Settlement of the Crowns record how Pelagora and the Byzarian League remember them.
- Places across Heliónis, Byzaría and the Hellád interior now name the events behind them, from the Silver Veil's founding to the Warden of the Weights.
