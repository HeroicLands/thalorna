**Peoples at home** — The culture accounts of three more peoples now describe ordinary life from childhood to the deathbed, who each admires, and what each gets wrong.

- **Tānvüri** live by classification, and the account follows it into the household ledger, the working dead, the festivals that double as filing days and the deed the empire cannot admit.
- **Nyáluba** live by the Pact and the spoken record, and the account follows it into the griots, the guides, the drums and the edge of the five.
- **Okhárins** live between the herd and the flame, and the account follows it into the cairn and the pyramid, the two accounts of the dead and the refusal the cities punish.
