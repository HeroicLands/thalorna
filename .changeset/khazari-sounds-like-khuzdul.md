- Khazari words and names are respelled to sound more like the stone-tongue they evoke: harder stops, and every name ends on a consonant.
- House and hold names are written as two dashed parts, for example _Dalk-thumár_, with the stress on the second.
- Several Khazari characters, places and titles have new names, among them the lost city _Hulabat_ and the Vardain village _Hudakal_.
- The Khazari language page describes the new sounds, name shapes and stress.
