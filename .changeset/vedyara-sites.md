**The places the notes name**

- The ground where the janapadas hold their twelve-yearly assembly has a page of its own: the plain at the Mahānadi's great confluence, which nobody lives on in the eleven years between.
- Sangama, the temple on that ground, whose senior priest convenes the assembly, keeps the roll of the janapadas and holds one of the three seats on the Council of Three.
- Bharanya, the pilgrim town of the lower Mahānadi, built on mounds around one of the three great Mahájaya temples of inland Vedyara.
- The Bhārava-Devasthāna, the twenty-three free hostels that keep traveling priests and ascetics on the pilgrimage roads at Suvarnagiri's charge.
