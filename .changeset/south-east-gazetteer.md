**Tāntsou, Kalihara, Dunhara and Nyáluba**

- The Tāntsou castes, laws, faith, calendar and threats now read as a guide teaching a newcomer, and the empire's currency has a full account of its own.
- Kalihara is one island, about four hundred miles across, a week's sail west of Okháris and Provènzia, and its four port characters are recast to live on it.
- The Dunhara's tribes, its oases and the Sultanate of Amradad are written up in full, and the Warrior's Circle and the storm cults meet together at the season-turns.
- The Nyáluba confederation numbers some two million people across its five clans.
