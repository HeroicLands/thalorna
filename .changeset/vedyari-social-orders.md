**Vedyara's four social orders speak Vedyari.** The orders a Vedyaran is born
into now carry names in their own tongue, and each name says what the order
does: the work, the wealth, the host, and the rite.

**The ladders say what a standing is.** Every rank that names one of the four
tells a referee plainly what the person may do and owes, so a ladder reads
without the name having to teach it.
