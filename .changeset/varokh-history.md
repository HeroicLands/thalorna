**Vrystwald**

- Vrystwald has a written history in eight notes, counted in Varokh winters, from the Taking of the Wood to the Sons of Eldheim.
- Every Varokh is named in full, with the clan after _yra_ and the village after _wur_, and two blood-fields, Skathhreth and Grimak, join the register.
- The Varokh tell their side of the Hrindstead settlement and the Black Marsh, in everyday words coined to match no real language.

**Nordlands and Provènzia**

- The Nordland characters carry Nordmal names in place of Old Norse and English ones, each keeping one name wherever they appear.
- The Provenzians are heirs of the old Vylarian provinces, and Yhârvalen Henge stands on the Sathvòthe coast where the Nordheim raiders came ashore.
