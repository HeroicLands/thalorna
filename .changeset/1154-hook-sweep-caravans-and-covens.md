**Characters**

- Ten characters of Bizaria, Helionis and Nordheim, among them the caravan guard Ághrazur Pirgikot, the marine Habidon Ghanides, the harbor watchman Gnirthann Veturdomur, the judge-priest Dumares Rafiros, the alchemist Talira Basiron and the potter Kalida Kourkásios, now each have a short description and plot hooks that name their own situations in place of stock adventure titles, with what only the game master should know kept in a titled secret block.
