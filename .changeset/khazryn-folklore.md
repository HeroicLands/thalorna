**Folklore of the Khazryn**

- The Tellumi tell their own stories: the epic of the lost town of Veshkun, the roll of the seven peaks, and the recitation of the Breaking.
- The steppe speaks for its dead in the calling of the names of the tribe killed at Vömek, and the Sahri sing of the lake under the sand.
- A caravan-master of Kethramír tells a young factor the tales of the road, one at each halt, from the first toll-camp to Sovid.
- The Khazryn lexicon carries the words and names the tellings use.
