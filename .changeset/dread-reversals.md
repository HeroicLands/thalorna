**Dread adventures**

- Each of the ten dread adventures now turns on a reversal partway through: the frightened girl, the patron, the witness or the monster is not what the party believed.
- Every adventure plants fair clues on every route, lands its turn in the third or fourth scene, and gives the referee read-aloud text and running advice.
- The places they are set in describe what a visitor sees and what locals say, and keep the truth in sections for the referee alone.

**Undead**

- A tereb rises again wherever it is destroyed, its Hallowed Ground included; desecrating that ground is what ends one before its thirteenth life.

**Language pages**

- Each language page lists the names its speakers actually carry, the same names characters are given.
