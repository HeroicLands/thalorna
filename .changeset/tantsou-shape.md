**Languages and scripts**

- Tantsou words and names drop their accent marks, and the Tantsou page sets out the tongue's twenty opening consonants and eighteen finals.
- A few Tantsou names are respelled to match their roots: chaos is _huen_ (_Huenlei_, _Huenzhuo_, _Genhuen_), the boar is _zhin_ (_Zhinho_), and the slave caste is the _Nuitsou_.
- Tantsou gains words for lightning, a hand and an account, so _Lanei_, _Tsaihuo_, _Wanduo_, _Wansou Zhuo_ and _Wantou_ read as the words they are built from.
- _Dou Chou_ is glossed as the Record of the Road.
- Some Tantsou given names change, and the moon goddess is _Youbui_.
