**Tarvénan literature**

- Six Tarvénan works are added, from the long poem of the Horn Gorge's lower end to a hill valley's tale of the adept who sold it a contract against the hail.
- A fair's verse-duel, the Mother's two words, the dead who walk home behind a crossbowman, and a company's roll at the Thresholds complete the set.
