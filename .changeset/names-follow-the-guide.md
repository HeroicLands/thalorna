- Names and words in every tongue now follow the pronunciation guide: Lîzêlâs is written Lizelas, and a mark appears only for unexpected stress or a vowel sounded apart.
- Long titles and names are shorter: marendandos is marendos, Pyrethos is Pirethos and Qudumel is Kudumel.
- Words that used x, tz, a hard c, ph or bh take the guide's letters, as in Ts'en, Hildrik and Fisera, and the continent Xerathia is now Zerathia.
- Vedyari words and names keep their sound and take forms of their own: Maharaja is Maharava, Chandrapur is Chendrapur and Senapati is Senapavi.
