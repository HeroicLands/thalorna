**Nyáluban original readings**

- Nyáluban pronunciation follows first-syllable stress, with an acute for a different stressed syllable and no required tone marks. The language note and Pronouncing Thalorna distinguish readable spelling from optional performance directions.
- Nyáluban has no writing tradition of its own. The language note removes the default script and identifies written versions as foreign transcriptions of oral works.
- The Swearing Under the Baobab has a Nyáluban original and fresh English, with matching extracts in the culture, Lion Totem and Tribal Nation notes and the short griot recital in parallel.
