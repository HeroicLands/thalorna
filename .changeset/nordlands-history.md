- The Nordlands have a written history: the north dates its years by the sittings on Dómsey, and ten history notes run from the first sitting's slaughter to the land-taking at Hrindstead.
- The five kingdoms name their royal lines and present kings, and Targud's two most recent kings carry northern names.
- Three new places of the dead in the north, Dómsey, the lake Skalfvatn and the ford Dvulgvangr, join the blood-fields, with the burned royal hall of Nulthgard and the tide-islet hof of Glurvholm.
- The heroes of the old Nordlands songs belong to the age before the kings, and the places that remember them keep their customs rather than their living company.
- Several contradictions in the northern notes are settled.
