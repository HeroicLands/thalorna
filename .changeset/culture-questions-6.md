**Peoples at home** — The culture accounts of three more peoples now describe ordinary life from childhood to the deathbed, who each admires, and what each gets wrong.

- **Hvarn** live by the rope and the roll, and the account follows it into the winter long house, the rotation of the crossings, the recovery of the dead and the sentence nobody passes.
- **Ösket** live by the road and the door, and the account follows it into the guide-mother's word, the cord, the opening on the col and the road once sold.
- **Élavendri** live by the house, the song and the tree line, and the account follows it into the feast that sings a descent, the two ways of keeping the dead and the welcome the ancestors never forgave.
