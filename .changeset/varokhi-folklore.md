**Varokh literature**

- _Dorgbán drúnga_ (The Lay of the Giant Slayers): how the people came to the wood and raised a mound over the last giant, with the Deepwood's objection.
- _Dunkelwald vreld_ (The Dunkelwald Charms): nine household charms and a run of small ones, as a Dunkelwald mistress teaches them in deep winter.
- _Mahnrýth meldra_ (The Mahnrýth Lament): the women's lament over the empty mound for the six the Grukar carried off.
- _Óthlin vorth_ (The Youngest Son's Telling): a feud-payment carried over the ice by a youngest son and his household's southern woman, with two endings.
- _Skathdreth drúnga_ (The Lay of the Feud-Cord): a vow set against a full blood-price, and the three winters that emptied both halls.
- _Eichthúl eldskorn_ (The Tally of the Oak Wardens): a kindred's dead said at Weskskald, reaching the ancestors who lie under the settlers' posts.
