The bison hunters and earth-lodge farmers of the northern plains are now the Grass People, the Tenlaya in their own tongue: a culture with its drives over the cliffs, its giveaways and year-robes, the burned village it still answers for, the river village of Tanshe Yawa where the Corn Pipe is kept, and the Tenlaya Language with its own words and names.
