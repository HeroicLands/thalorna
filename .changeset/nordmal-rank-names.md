**Northern standings** — Every rank of the Asguardian orders, the faiths of the
Ten, the Compact and the Skalds' Circle carries a Nordmal name, from Sárborinn
and Lögvördr to Blótlok and Skaldefnir, with the reader's-tongue form among each
page's alternative names.

**Two orders** — The Giant's Children and the Green Wardens are titled Thursbörn
and Grönverdir.

**Shared standings** — The apprentice, bondservant, elder and kinsman pages carry
a northern word beside the common one, so northern prose names the standing in
Nordmal and still points at the page every culture shares.
