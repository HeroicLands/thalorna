**Kalihara**

- Kalihara's pages agree that it is a tropical island in the western ocean, with no gods and no nobility.
- The island lies 450 to 700 miles west of Okháris and Provènzia and is some 400 miles across: a ring of volcanic mountains around a forested bowl with a lake and a mount at its heart.
- The crossing is about a week out of sight of land in fair weather, often longer, and the way home swings north across the Doldrum Sea.
- Kalihara is one island polity under a Council at Ilovanu, not a confederation of ports and clans.
- The script and language notes agree on who writes, and the custodians' ranks and the island's year-count are told the same way everywhere.
