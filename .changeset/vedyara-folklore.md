**Vedyara literature**

- _The Measuring Cord_, the temple school's recitation of how the world was measured, kept, broken and measured again, and why death has a gap in it.
- _The Ferryman at the Ford_, the upper-river villages' legend of the ancestor of the Mahānadī kings, the oath they swore to feed him, and the stone they turned face-down.
- _The Borrowed Lamp_, the Agnī-panthā's song of the season its founder spent on the unburned ford with a widow's threshold lamp, sung over the common fire at the Night of the Kindled Road.
- _The Five Grounds_, the march companies' lament for the kingdom that died at Lanthusthalī, five poems sung in winter in the empty temple of Lūdrapur.
- _The Recall_, the dialogue read at Dhanurkota's Festival of the Returning: two brothers of one bow argue through the night the masters' recall reaches the camp, and one stands on each side of the gorge.
