**Places**

- In the Old Ranges of the One Fire, the Stone Door is a cave over a hill town's salt lick, shut by a slab of stone that still swings, which the town's Mound Chief wants for a refuge before a war party returns.
- Above the Grease Trail out of Wolf Beach stand the High Floors, three cedars grown into floors high over the forest, where two runaway slaves are hiding.
- The foothills at the western end of the Grazian wall have two places to visit: the Shuttered Cliff above the March Road, held by a band of Kinless, and the Root Stair, a spring under a ladder of fig roots where two tribes dispute the order of watering.
- Deep in Hilen's imperial game-forest lies the Linden Walk, a roofed walk of living lindens that a scholar of the Arcane Order wants to enter and the wardens will not let him.
