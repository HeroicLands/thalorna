**People**

- Velanthia's three river princes, Zhitovod Dobrovodich, Milogor Yarogorich and Krodogor Brevogorich, are written as people with wants, gear and hooks, and hold their princedoms as office-holders.
- The atamans of the Tarvál, Sevret and Khorad Hosts, Volkar Kodogorich, Sovidan Volketich and Belukan Kelodov, are people who command their Hosts and speak for them to the prince they serve.
- The starbrat of the great granary at Zhitavesh and the Master of the Houses of the Rivers are named, each with what they want of the princes and what they answer for.
- The captain of the Brevavesh forts, the port-warden of Brelavesh, the keeper of the princely granary and the reeve of the Tveskavesh pass market are people, linked from the towns they serve.
