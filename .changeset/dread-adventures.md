**Adventures of mounting dread**

- Ten new adventures: each begins with the party caught somewhere remote and wrong, and tightens scene by scene to a final horror and a treasure.
- They range from a funerary house of sewn-mouthed dead in Aû'Khelâthu to a jarl's furnace in Norgaad, a fae hunter in the Silver Forests and a cursed battlefield in Vedyara.
- Every scene offers more than one way through, with clues and ways back for players who miss or bypass a step.
