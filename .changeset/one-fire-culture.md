The River Kingdom of the northern continent is now the One Fire, Tokchi Aska in its own tongue: mound towns of the great river and shell towns of the gulf that relight every hearth each midsummer from a Great Sun's fire, gathering into a kingdom and scattering again. Its people, the Tokchili, have a culture note with their heroes, the funeral they still argue over and the strain on the present Gathering, and their unwritten tongue has a language note with a full everyday vocabulary. The chief town is Hayo Pokta, the Sun Mound.
