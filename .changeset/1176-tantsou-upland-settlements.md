**Places**

- The Mwonou Uplands of Tantsou have their towns and villages: Jenshou on its nine channels, the terrace villages of Rinnou and Chwonou, the market towns of Weichai, Tengmou and Jaunou, the last at the heart of the southern famine, and Kwisen, the frontier way-station below the Scholars' Door.
- The District Hall of Jenshou, where the district's register is kept and its orphaned dead are fed, has its own entry.
- The places named in the Tantsou annals, tales and accounts link to their entries.
