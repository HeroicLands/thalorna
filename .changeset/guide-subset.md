**Languages and scripts**

- Most language pages state no stress, vowel-length, tone or pitch rule of their own, so their words are said as Pronouncing Thalorna reads them.
- The Alessa street article and the old Vylari catch are written with the guide's apostrophe, as in the Morning Call's refrain _A' thelmos lokazor_.
