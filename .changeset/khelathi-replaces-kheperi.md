**The empire has its own tongue.** The realm on the Zumélesh is Aû'Khelâthu and its people the Khelâthi, and every god, selat, settlement, guild, house and person in it carries a Khelâthi name.

**An institution's name says what it is.** A guild opens on _Lin'_, a temple or estate on _Lut-_, a house on _Gar-_, an order on _Lem'_ and a council on _Genzet'_.

**The language note carries the dictionary.** Its head-words, its genitive and its worked examples are written down, so a new name can be coined that fits the rest.

**The English by-names survive as aliases.** The Court of the Nine Moons, the Merchants' Circle and their like still find their pages.

**Links into Khelâthi material need revisiting.** Every address here changed with the names, so anything pointing at one must be pointed at its replacement.
