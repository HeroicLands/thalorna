**Tāntsou**

- The empire once called Tānvür is Tāntsou, and its people and tongue share the name.
- Every Tāntsou name, place, office, god and caste is respelled in a four-tone sound system, with tone marks on each syllable: Tengwou Rong Lei, Raogui, Youwéng.
- The island empire of Jürthāt is Jénchài.
- The language page describes the new sound system and the Tāntsou lexicon lists every older spelling beside the one that replaced it.
