**Literature and verse**

- _The Keeping of the Silk House_, _The Sayings of the First Warden_ and _The Song of the Two-Sealed_ pair their original Bizarian readings with fresh English and Roman readings.
- The Five Defects includes the full apprentice’s telling and Declaration in native, fresh English and Roman readings.
- The Hymn of the First Terms retains the prelude, nineteen stanzas, refrains and native/English signature acrostics.
