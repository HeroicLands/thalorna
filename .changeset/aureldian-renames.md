**Gods.** The goddess of knowledge, the god of storms and the goddess of flowers and healing now carry Vylari names, Mârendâs, Brâzêmâs and Lîzêlâs, in their lore notes, faiths, rituals and every place they are mentioned.

**Months.** The Vylarian calendar's first, fourth and sixth months take the same gods' names: Lîzêlândi, Brâzêmândi and Mârendândi.

**Places.** Four places of Vylaria now have Vylari names: the capital's great racecourse, the imperial guard's fortress, the Alyssa market town on the capital road and the Hylen market town.
