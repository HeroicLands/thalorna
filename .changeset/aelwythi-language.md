**Aelwyth's language**

- Aelwythi, the speech of the island, has a language page and a word list of the elements Aelwythan places, people and families are named from.
- Aelwyth's three realms list both Aelwythi and Élavendri as common tongues, and the language pages describe how Aelwythi, Élavendri and Vylari stand to one another.
