**Aureldian culture**

- A new Aureldian culture note describes what the Vylarian, Élavendri, Calypsan, Aelwythan, Tarvénan and Provenzian peoples share: the twelve gods, their faiths and the sworn orders.
- The Aureldian faiths, orders, gods and the Aurèldía region belong to that culture.
