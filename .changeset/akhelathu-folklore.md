**Aû'Khelâthu literature**

- Six Khelâthi works to read and quote at the table, among them a dawn hymn to the sun-lord and a burial club's road-spells.
- The others are a Keeper's instruction to his son, the tale of a scribe who came home to close, a comedy of two gods at law, and the victory stela at Zel-Halzi.
- The Khelâthi language page gives the words for hymn, spell, instruction, tale, lament, suit at law and inscription, and how a work's title is built.
