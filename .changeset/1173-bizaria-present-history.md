**Places**

- Bizaria, the Bizarian League, its Northern and Southern Marches, its five seats and several towns, Nikhtion and the Thirsting Ground say what is happening there now: the League governed by its own councils under imperial officers who hold the passes, the tariffs and the muster, the tolls on the roads, and the marauders of the eastern hinterland. The League, Krusteikhos, Nesthiros, Argirteikhos and the Thirsting Ground also tell their past.
