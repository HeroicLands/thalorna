**Lore — The Ithári.** The people who came before Kalihara now have their own
account: what they left behind, the single substance it is all made of, the
works that run with nothing inside them to explain how and then one day stop,
the sites scattered far beyond Kalihara, and the one afternoon an Elder Race
met them and was politely declined.

**The Sinalë met the Ithári.** The Kalihara pages had the Elder Races arriving
long after the Ithári were gone; they arrived in time to be found quaint. The
island's custodians have been keeping the works since the departure in 5,100 BF
rather than for the whole life of their civilization, and the pages now say so.
