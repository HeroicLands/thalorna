**Vylarian literature**

- Seven Vylarian works are added: The Walking of the Line, The Song of the Gold Band, The Praise of the Masks, The Morning Call, The Words Kept from the Fire, The Second Reading and The Evening Songs.
- They tell of the Founder's nameless stone, the eagle taken from the reeds, a Hylen house's masks and a client's morning call.
- They also keep a lost jurist's rules, an Omelar's second reading and a watch officer's elegies.
