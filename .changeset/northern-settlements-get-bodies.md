**Twenty-four northern settlements carry full accounts** — Every market town,
fishing village, mining camp, frontier fort and Vrystwald clearing now reads as
a place: who holds it, what its people live on, and what they argue about.

**Authority reads by title** — A settlement's rulers appear as a named office
holding a named district or province, or a Vrystwald chieftain's own hall,
rather than an unnamed lord.
