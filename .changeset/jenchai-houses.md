**Jénchài**

- The island empire the islanders call Tēyan Jenjai, the Nine Mountains Above the Clouds of Heaven, has a culture page on how its people serve, keep faith and die.
- Eight great houses rule eight mountains of the great island and the lesser one, each with its castle town, its villages and the retainers who hold them.
- The poor and powerless Emperor keeps his court at Léngwou, on the lowest slope of Zojai, and the office of Heaven's Blade stands empty while the houses contend for it.
- Jénchài has its own volume of the gazetteer.
