**Canon agreement**

- Thálassos keeps its sea-shrine to Pôzêrâs, god of voyages, and the temples at Nýkhtion honor Sélmoros, so both name gods of the Aurèldían pantheon.
- Therádon stands inland, and the Aelwyth notes agree that the island lies some two hundred and thirty miles off the Élavendren coast across the open sea.
- The Kingdom of Provènzia lies on the southern coast of Aurèldía.
- The Vylarian military motto is now in Vylari, _Zêl ôrkhaima thâlentôr ûthrimê_, "beneath the eagle stand the borders."
- The legion's eagle is the _ôrkhaimâ_ and its bearer the _ôrkhaimândôs_ in the Vylarian notes, and the coin inscriptions carry the Vylari motto.
