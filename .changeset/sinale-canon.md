**Canon agreement**

- The Sinalë build rarely, and rarely in stone, preferring to craft or grow their dwellings; Ethalossë's stone court is one of the few stone works they raised.
- The Sinalë and the Khazári parted in 2427 BF, then joined in one kingdom on Aelwyth until the Sinalë king fell at the Three Sisters; Aelwyth's Sinalë live in the Hallowwood, the Khazári in the mountains.
- The Sinalë have kings, queens and lords of their own, though they do not govern as human ones do; the Sinalë of Tāntsou are a case of their own.
- Élavendre's royal family and aristocracy hold the formal relation with the Sinalë, while everyday contact runs through Áelendan intermediaries.
- Dunhara's Warrior's Circle meets at the four season-turns, the Khazryn exile houses match their cities, and Shankhadvāra's crossing is held by the Hvarn hearth assigned it for the season.
