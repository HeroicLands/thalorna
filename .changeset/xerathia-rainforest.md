**Peoples and places**

- The Central Rainforests have two named rivers, the Luwa Bolu and the Luwa Gbasa, and ten tribes with a camp, a leader and an Adventurer's Guide to start from.
- The Leopard King and his Court are a tribe with a Speaker and a war-leader, and the Gorge-folk portage tribe has a leader.
