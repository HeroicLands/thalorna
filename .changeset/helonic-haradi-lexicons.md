**Lexicons**

- The Helonic family (Helonic, Byzarian and Bethuan) and Haradi each have a word-hoard of shared roots, listing every name built from them.
- The Helonic, Byzarian and Bethuan language pages use the same articles and particles as the word-hoard.

**Vylaria**

- The imperial capital is Ôrzâra, the sovereign's city, and its temple of the Twelve is Sârkhenâ Dôrêkhis.
- The gods of dreams and voyages are Zûnêlâs and Pôzêrâs, and six months of the Vylarian Calendar take Vylari names.
- The coastal province is Sâlkhâlon, with Vylari town and village names; its largest fishing town, Braldvík, keeps the northern name raiders left there in 523 AF.
- Braldvík's note and a new history, The Winter of the Banked Fire, tell how the bay burned and give a GM four ready hooks.
