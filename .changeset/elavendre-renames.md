**Regions.** Élavendre's five regions (the heartland basin, the western shore, the eastern hills, the northern coast with its bay, and the high mountains) now carry Élavendri names, wherever they are mentioned.

**Spirits.** The eight spirits of the Áelendan Kindred and the ancient tree of the Silver Forests now carry Áelendi names, in their lore notes, spirit powers and every place they appear. The hearth-kin of Ésalosa is renamed in _The Asking_ too, to a name that scans in the same place in the line.

**Hill village.** The hill village of the eastern interior takes its Áelendi name from the cold spring at its head.

**Fate.** The rune-priests of the Nordlands speak of fate in a Nordmal word, in the Asguardian pages, the god Ódvar's note and the verses of the Nordland gods.
