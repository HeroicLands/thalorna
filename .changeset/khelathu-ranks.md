**Khelathu ranks**

- Every Khelathu body now ranks its people as Outcast, Bondsman, Zelar (a commoner) and Zabu (a noble), with the empire's Iru'palu and Gar-Au above them.
- Halzi'a, Legha'lutu, Zabet and the other old posts are offices held by those ranks, and Khelathi characters sit on the new ladder.
