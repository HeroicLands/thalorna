The Ki'ich'chik calendar is set out in one note: the solar year of eighteen named months and five liminal days, the 260-day count of twenty day-signs, the moon and the stars, and the fifty-two-year round the Living Flame closes, with the next Living Flame at the winter solstice of 722. The Ki'i'chek lexicon names every month, day-sign and liminal day.
