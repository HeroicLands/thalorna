**Languages**

- Nine Élavendri words changed form so that each keeps its own meaning next to the word that now takes the _ei_ spelling: _intended_ is _dernar_, _accidental_ is _andernar_, _guilt_ is _darnis_, _embarrassment_ is _geldis_, _across_ is _gande_, _even_ is _geri_, _a cheek_ is _gernis_, _the penis_ is _garnon_ and _metal_ is _gerone_.
- Five Tantsou words have new forms that the spelling guide reads as written: _five_ is _chi_, _belly_ is _twi_, _permission_ is _zhyau_, _to lie down_ is _tye_ and _soft_ is _gwen_. _To hold_ moves to _gwin_ and _bed_ to _tyemou_. The festival of five elements is _Chi Tsai Jwi_.
- The Tantsou word for _warrior_ is _wo_, as in the caste names _Wohou_ and _Wozhwo_, and _wo_ also means _divine_. _Wuodao_ is now _Wodao_, _Wuoja_ is _Woja_, _Houwuo_ is _Houwo_ and the personal name _Bwi Ja Wuo Rau_ is _Bwi Ja Wo Rau_.
