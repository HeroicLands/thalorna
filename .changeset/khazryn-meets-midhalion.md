**Places**

- The ferries from the Amradi coast to Kudumel and Pashinar are a day's sail, and Kudumel's market says where its desert goods come from and who brings them over the strait.
- The Khazryn's own pages tell of the last Tellumi rising at Shirvan and of Taruzel's war across the strait, and name the old Vylarian fort of Oroqid on the western margin.
