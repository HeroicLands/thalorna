**Names that fit their tongues**

- Hundreds of Áelendi, Élavendri, Provenzal, Tarvéni and Vylari names are respelled to fit their tongues, among them Péravel (Béravel before), Alêssâ (Alyssa before) and Ólvestra.

**Vylarian offices and institutions**

- The Vylarian Empire's offices and noble estates go by Vylari names, such as the _tâvândôs_ (consul), the _kurthândôs_ (senator) and the _elthêzênoi_ (the gold estate).
- The Arcane Order is the _Thôrdimâlon_, the Academy of Knowledge the _Plûmepistémion_, and the Héx Hodäi the _Ksêmor Hodhîs_, whose six schools are the _rhéshâ_.
- Tarvénia's orders, Ondareth's and Calypsa's magistrates and the twelve Ilthêrâlon birthsigns carry Vylari names, borrowed from the Empire.

**Languages**

- Vylari is the language of learning across the Empire and the lands that look to it, and Helonic is the language of culture and the arts.
