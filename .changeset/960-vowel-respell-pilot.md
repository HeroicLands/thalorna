**Languages and scripts**

- Taruzeli, Khelathi and Provenzal words whose two vowels are said apart now carry a diaeresis, so _shiä_, _zethuär_ and _orfeäte_ read as they are spoken.
- The Provenzal formal greeting is written _quë la preja soie talv vei_.

**Literature and verse**

- Khelathi and Provenzal songs, hymns and tales spell these words the same way; no line or wording changes.

**Characters**

- Three Khelathi houses are written Gez'Aqeü, Iüthi'Gehtiü and Melu'Khelariü, in the names of Thirye, Retheku and Qelatha and wherever they appear.
