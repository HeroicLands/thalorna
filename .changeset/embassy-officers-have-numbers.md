**Adventures** — Five members of the Dúnavarren embassy to Tānvür can be put on the table: Sir Arren Thábavel and Dame Toradh Taravren, Marric Háravrel, and the mission's two planted enemies, Loris Taravel and Geldar Panivel. Each arrives with attributes, skills, a body and a move, so a referee can roll for them instead of narrating around them.
