**Kheperi names sound like one tongue.** The given names and the house names are written in
the sounds the language itself keeps, so a Kheperian roster no longer reads as two peoples
sharing an empire.

**A Kheperi house is named as a people.** A house name stands apart from a personal one at a
glance: it is built as a compound and it names the folk of its founder, its shrine or its
land rather than one of them.
