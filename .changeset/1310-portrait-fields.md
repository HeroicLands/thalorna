**Characters**

- Generated people now draw their hair texture, hairstyle, face shape, nose, brows and mouth from the look of their culture, so people of different peoples no longer share one face.
- Khazari are generated with beards, and Senali are beardless.
- Named people state the hair, face and beard their descriptions already give, so a generated portrait matches the text.
