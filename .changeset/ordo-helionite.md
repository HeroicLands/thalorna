**The Arcane Order and Heliónis**

- The dragon lease now runs nearly three centuries across the history notes, and the Arcane Order's records and the time since the lease was refused are counted correctly.
- The Arcane Order counselled the Senate when the dragons' lease was not renewed; the Senate decided.
- A few Helionite masters were never lent out by the Kandral, and nobody says which of them drafted the Arcane Order's charter clauses on property.
- Therádon fell first, in 330 BF, and its chapterhouse does not teach that year; the Heliónis chapter's oldest teaching line runs there.
- Dragons are mortal in the Divine Action chapter, as in Souls and Spirits.
