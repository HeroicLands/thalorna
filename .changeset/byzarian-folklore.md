**Byzarian literature**

- Six Byzarian works are added: The Hymn of the First Terms, The Life of Kîtvârion of the Gate, The Song of the Two-Sealed, The Five Defects, The Keeping of the Silk House and The Sayings of the First Warden.
- They tell of the world sealed by the gods, a monk through the Two Winters, a borderer who wrestled the Collector, and the League's founding.
