**Nordmal**

- The language note gains a vocabulary of the north's own words: the kinds of verse a skald makes, the hearth and the high seat, whale and seal, the body and the grave, and the ages of the world.
- It explains how a seeress comes by her name, and that the published name lists are samples a hall may go beyond.
- Northern names that broke the tongue's rules are renamed: the Flarnfjall mountains, Hrindstead, Skulfheim, Kruldheim, Hörgsvík, the sword Vrellbrandr and the hrúmverdir, the howe-wardens.
- The grove the Nalthmark settlers took belongs to the Eichthúl, a Varokhi clan.
- An apprentice skald's first poem is a gyldra, and the Skalds' Circle writes its rank Skáld.
