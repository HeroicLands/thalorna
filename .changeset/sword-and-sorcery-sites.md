**Adventure sites**

- Twenty-five wild places each have a site page and a five-scene sword-and-sorcery adventure, marked as drafts.
- The Khazryn Desert: the Lost Town, the Empty Tower, Zargandûr's Trench, the Glass Princes' Palace and the Dry Well of Shirvan.
- Dunhara: the Tower of the Lightning-Marked, Mezhvekh and Vazhmekh; Okháris: the Ash-Fold; Aû'Khelâthu: the Burial Club's Last Vault.
- Central Xerathia: the Drowned Ziggurat, the Leopard King's Court and the Silent Clearing; Vedyara: Sorhupura, Madhukūpa and Dahanaghatta.
- Élavendre: the Ring of Turned Paths, the Kept Fire and the Dusk Ford; Aelwyth: Wenlorne Stage, Nethermoot Hollow and Blackkiln Priory.
- The Nordlands: Unthmýlsfell, Thraldhöll and Dvilgvatn.
