**Names rebuilt from their own tongues**

- The Byzarian League cities are Khrýshteíkhos, Argýrteíkhos, Órashion, Karvashion and Nêsthiros, and Byzarian, Helonic and Bethûan characters with real-world names or English aliases take Helonic ones, the Mêtríssa's house among them.
- In K'ich'chik, Tz'ikin is Xelem and Ha' Xaman is P'enqal Ch'akal; the glyph script is Ch'alix P'ilam, the knotted cord-records are the K'otzel, and the count to nineteen has new number words.
- The Okháric Spirit Path is the Yénor Zohiring, the Nyáluba Great Brightness is Shorínya Ndáfo, and Kalihara's ports are Ilovanu and Nalurithan.
- Amradad's officers, the storm cults' ranks and towns such as Qarvelakhend carry Dunhari names, and Tānvür's supreme god is Tānzhös and its spirit animals include the Star Bird.
- Harad is now Tarûzel, with the Tarûzeli tongue and Merâzan script; its guild council is the Vezâkûnî, and its places and people take Tarûzeli names.
- New Lexicon pages for Ki'ichek, Okháric, Nyáluban, Dunhari, Tānvüri, Kalihári, Hvarn, Ösket and Valtári give each tongue's words and every name it gives, and the Valtári trade pidgin has new words.
