**The Bestiary**

- About sixty creatures read as field-guide entries, each saying where it lives, what it does, what makes it dangerous or valuable, and how the people around it deal with it.
- Each of these entries gains a list of the signs that give the creature away, and an account of what the local people do about it.
- The creatures of the Khazryn, the Grazian north face, Vedyara, Tānvür, Xerathia and Vrystwald are tied to the wells, ridges, ruins, blood-fields, villages and peoples they share their country with.
- Attack names now match the attacks on each creature's sheet.
