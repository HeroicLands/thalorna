**Characters**

- People of Malagna, Nordheim, Norgaad, Targud and Vithgard, among them the tentmaker Glirvrinna Snarvumak, the singer Ralthira Drekanótt, the bodyguard Thraldorv Vornheim, the innkeepers Hvalgvir Veturdomur and Thilmselda Drekanótt, the woodcutter Kralmil Veturdomur and the skald Knilthann Skraldumak, carry plot hooks that name their own situations in place of stock adventure titles, and each has a short description.
- Each person's private matters sit in referee-only sections, and the guilds, councils and offices that the Nordlands do not have are replaced by the hersvaldar, landvordar, hovs and circles they do.
- Several people and houses named in these notes take Nordmal names, among them the seid of Norgaad, now Tvarninda, and the wandering skald who travels with her, now Ralthvir.
