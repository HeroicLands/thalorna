**Rules and items**

- Vylaria's everyday gear is written: Vylarian Steel, the veteran's pension token and citizenship diptych, senatorial rings, the diadem and racing-faction rings.
- Vylaria's guarded things join them: the treasury's strongroom keys, the Kervaron passage-gift, Nartun's anointing oil, the heralds' cipher-tablets and the Sekran's Standing Writ.
- Taruzel's traders and captains carry factors' seals, striking-writs, League warrants, Kudumel credit-tallies, Free Captains' charts, ash-amulets of the Two Fires and Khelathi bonds.
- Three one-of-a-kind items are written for play: the Old Coal of Bralvik, the lost eagle of the Twelfth and the rostral beaks of Tamzir Shoals.
