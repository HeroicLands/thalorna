**Languages and scripts**

- Sinale words for "in" and for qualities follow its sound rules, as _roselo_ "in the morning" and _pattenersa_ "a coming back", in the dictionary and the verse.
- Sinale has one word each for "knee" and "a lie"; _kippel_ means a trick, a deception done rather than said.
