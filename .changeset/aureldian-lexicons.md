**Vylari**

- A new Vylari Lexicon gives over four hundred words of the empire's tongue, ancient and imperial, grouped by what they say about the Vylarians.
- Ancient Vylari has a fourth, hallowed gender for gods, the sovereign and the founders of houses, and the lexicon explains what its endings on a noble name tell a listener.
- The Vylari language page now sets out the grammar in full, with a declined model noun for every class, the verb, and new sample phrases.
- The lexicon shows the rules for Vylari given names, house names and calling names, and sets roots side by side with their forms in Áelendi, Élavendri, Provenzal and Tarveni.
