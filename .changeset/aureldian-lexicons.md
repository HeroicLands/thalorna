**Vylari**

- A new Vylari Lexicon gives over four hundred words of the empire's tongue, ancient and imperial, including a fourth, hallowed gender whose endings on a noble name tell a listener what the name claims.
- The Vylari language page sets out the grammar in full, with a declined model noun for every class and new sample phrases, and the lexicon gives the rules for given, house and calling names.

**The daughter tongues**

- A new Áelendi Lexicon gives the highland tongue's words, inherited from ancient Vylari or borrowed from the Sinalë of the deep wood, with the elements its names are built from.
- A new Provenzal Lexicon gives the coastal tongue's words, inherited, taken back from the empire's law books or borrowed from the north, arranged around the city, patronage, love and song.
- A new Tarvéni Lexicon gives the mercenary tongue's words, with the old mountain speech beneath it: the earth mother, the caves, the flocks and the dead who walk the roads.
- A new Élavendri Lexicon gives the lowland kingdom's words, descended from Áelendi with the Sinalë layer carried down, and the learned words the empire's clerks brought north.
