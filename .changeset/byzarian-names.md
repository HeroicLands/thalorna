**Byzarian names** — The League's scribe-cities are now Soridion and Khalumion, the Lord Commander of the Eastern March is Mazitos, and the Byzarian name lists offer given and house names built in Byzarian's own sounds. Many Byzarian, Helionite and Bethûan figures, among them Fótianos Hodíkot and Grégoras Efthymiópoulos, carry new names in place of ones that read as real-world Greek.
