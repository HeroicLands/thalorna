**Dunhara**

- Dunharan towns, oases, camps and forts carry place endings of their own: Qarvelakhend, Ghavûlâvad, Kherzûlakhêm, Mezhvadêz and their neighbours.
- Dunharan offices and ranks take new endings, clear of the forms the Khazryn tongues use.
- Several Dunharan people are respelled, among them Farazûn Afrun, Darzash Shavan and Shâdezan Kamruz.
