**Provenzian literature**

- Seven Provenzian works are added, from The Song of the Last Shrine to How the South Bought Longing.
- A navigator turns back short of the far shore and goes out again alone, while the women wait on the salt terraces.
- A lord's clients fetch him off a loud field, two singers argue love's place in duty, and a song mocks a nameless jar.
