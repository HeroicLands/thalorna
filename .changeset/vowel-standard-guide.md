**Languages and scripts**

- Pronouncing Thalorna gives one vowel standard for every tongue: a grave marks the short English vowels, a diaeresis a vowel sounded on its own, and two other vowels together sound only the first.
- The common vowel groups are _ai_, _au_, _ei_, _oi_ and _ou_, with the held _aa_, _ii_ and _uu_; a tongue lists a further group only for a sound these cannot write.
- Aelwythi follows the same standard as every other tongue, its English-derived words included.
- Sinale's pitch and emphasis marks appear only in verse and performance.
