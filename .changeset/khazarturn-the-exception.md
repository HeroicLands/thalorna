**Khazártúrn**

- The city is the one work where the Khazári built what they wanted rather than what the stone offered, and its sealing gives the mountain back to itself.
- Its seven towers are spurs the cliff already held, finished until they could not be told from the rock.
- It had a refuge, built for an enemy that tires and breaks; the horde that took it did neither.
