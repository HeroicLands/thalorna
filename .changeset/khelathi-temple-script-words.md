- The Khelathi temple script has about four hundred and seventy picture-signs, written in right-to-left lines or top-down columns, with royal names in a corded oval.
- Qalezu, the everyday Khelathi alphabet, is written right to left.
- Khelathi gains numerals, ten everyday verbs, and the words of the sacred river hunt, from the harpoon to the harpooner; its verbs do not change.
