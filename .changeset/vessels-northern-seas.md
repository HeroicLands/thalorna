**Vessels**

- The Nordmen's four ships of the northern seas have their own pages, one for each size: the Hafthresk open-sea trader, the Strandarv coastal and fjord trader, the Vredgnum bulk carrier and the Ormthresk longship, each with its dimensions, cargo, crew, cost, build time and the odds of each day's distance under sail and oar.
- Nordmal lists the four ship names among its words.
