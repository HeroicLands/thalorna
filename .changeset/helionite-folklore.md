**Helionite literature**

- Six Helionite works are added: The Burning of the Keel, The Measures of the Terraces, The Dinner of the Silent Passage, The Unreceived, The Lessons of the Silver Veil and The Herald Who Asked for the Dead.
- They tell of the founder who burned his ship to light the others in, the gods voting the world into measure, a dead levyman's return, the Sour Plain's waiting dead, and the first herald to ask for the dead.
