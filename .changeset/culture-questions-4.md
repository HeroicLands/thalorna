**Peoples at home** — The culture accounts of three more peoples now describe ordinary life from childhood to the deathbed, who each admires, and what each gets wrong.

- **Dunhari** live by salt and the word, and the account follows it into their feuds, their storm-readers and the promise the warriors' Circle keeps at a price.
- **Bethûans** live by the House, and the account follows it into the place of men, the Spádai and the women cast out of their Houses.
- **Kaliharans** live by looking and tending, and the account follows it into their works, the way those works answer some households and not others, and the failing they do not explain.
