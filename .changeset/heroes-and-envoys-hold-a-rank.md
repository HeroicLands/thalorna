**Standings and calendars** — A character's sheet now shows standing within
the realm, tribe, cult or city they belong to, rather than a blank where that
should be. Gods, Nightwights and outlaws among the Heroes of Asguard carry the
standing their own story gives them, from outcast to ruler. A handful of
calendar epochs and birth dates that ended in a zero now keep that digit
instead of losing it.
