**Characters**

- The twelve towns of the Helionite hinterland, eight Bizarian towns and the cities of Kostaros and Krusteikhos each list someone a party can meet there: a wall-warden for the twelve hinterland towns, a caravan-master for the steppe-road towns, a grain patron of Kostaros, a water-warden for the Karvashion caravan towns, the toll warden of Tolzur, a bell-founder of Bruzash, the bailiff of Siderash and a cellar master of Oinelena, each ready to drop into play.
- Selvazhikon Zigakit lives in Krusteikhos, Karvashena Halikot oversees an adit at Zalvarion, and the March garrison soldier draws his posting at Parzhenar.
