- Khelathi-zethu, the temple script of Au'Khelathu, is hieroglyphic: about a hundred and fifty picture-signs in sixteen groups, read by context.
- Qalezu, the everyday hand, is an alphabet of twenty-four signs that writes only consonants, quick to learn and written left to right.
- Khelathi names written with _tj_ now use _ch_, as in _Chaq'ur_ and _Chelsur_, and the longest single names are shorter, as in _Amqelina_.
