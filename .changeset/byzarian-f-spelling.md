**Byzarian spelling**

- Byzarian names, house names and sample phrases now spell the sound _f_ with `f`, as the language does, where they had carried the Helonic `ph`.
- Eight Byzarian characters change name accordingly, among them Fótianos Hodíkot, Fýléze Steníkot and Lôfáhr Filomédis.
