**Places**

- Aelirosse's own office is the Oak-Warden, taught to the Oak-Pupil; Long-Watcher and Seer belong to the Faith of Bjart alone, and the Sayings, Aelirosse and the Sinale pages use the Aelirosse names.
- Serenthale is the oldest Sinale enclave, and the Sinale keep the stone court at Ethalosse without being named as its builders.
