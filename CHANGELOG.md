# thalorna

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

This package is the setting of Thalorna for the Song of Heroic Lands system, shipped as Foundry compendiums, as a website at heroiclands.org/thalorna and as a PDF sourcebook, _The World of Thalorna_. It needs Foundry 14.359 or later (verified on 14.364) and Song of Heroic Lands 0.8.8 or later; the journals, scenes and macros read under any other system as well.

**The setting**

- Three continents (Ankaris, Xerathia and K'ich'chik) are mapped region by region, from great realms down to provinces, cities, towns, villages and landmarks, each place named under the region that holds it.
- More than two dozen cultures are described by their values, customs, naming and speech, from the Nordmen and the Vylarians to the Khelâthi, Khazári, Varokhi and the peoples of K'ich'chik.
- Dozens of deities and their faiths come with clergy, rites, holy days and the orders devoted to them, including the ten gods of Asguard and the faiths of Aû'Khelâthu.
- Guilds, temples, noble houses, crime syndicates, arcane colleges and other organizations each have their own page, with the standings a member can hold and the rank ladders of their peoples.
- Histories and chronologies for the kingdoms and empires, myths and sagas, and a lexicon of titles for rank and office around the setting.
- Languages and scripts have their own pages with grammar, sample phrases and word lists for naming villages, priests and clans at the table.
- Coinage for each realm is described with its denominations, weights and values.
- Five calendars (the Common, Khelâthi, Khazryn, Mādhavendra and Tānvüri reckonings) each name their era and their years before it.
- Twenty-four Adventurer's Guides open a culture or region to play, covering who holds power, daily life, faith, places, people and ways into an adventure.

**Compendiums**

- The module carries Item, Journal and Actor compendiums, with folders that follow the setting's own order.
- Each journal opens with the entry itself and closes with sections listing what touches it: places within, governing bodies, chronology, events and related accounts.
- Links between entries resolve inside Foundry, so a faith, a city or a person is one click from any page that names it.
- The Common Calendar becomes the world calendar for a world that has none of its own; the five calendars also install as Foundry calendars and Calendaria import files.

**Characters and creatures**

- Hundreds of named characters, from heroes and knaves to occupational types and common folk, come as actors with an appearance, a dossier and the standing they hold in their faiths and organizations.
- Heroes of Asguard and the Varokhi heroes bring campfire tales and playable character sheets.
- The bestiary holds animals, dreadspawn, spirits, elementals, mythic beasts, constructs and undead, with accounts of how each culture meets them.
- Totems and spirits have their own entries, with the animals a village may take as its totem.

**Rules content**

- Skills cover mystical skills, the setting's languages and its scripts.
- Around two hundred spells, the Lekhau incantations and nineteen arcane talents each say what they do.
- Twelve Astrokýklos birthsigns, with an overview that each links to.
- Potions and elixirs, weapons with their strike modes, and equipment such as jewelry, religious items, scribe's tools, instruments and expedition gear.

**Adventures**

- Two scenarios ship with the setting: the embassy from Dúnavarre to Tānvür, and Blood and Ashes in Ceravel Vale, each with its cast.

**The website**

- Every note is published as a page, with a banner for its kind and its own hero image where the note has one.
- Portraits and icons appear in the page and in the compendium, and each carries its artist, source and licence.
- The site's description and the Foundry package listing each read text written for them.

**The book**

- _The World of Thalorna_ is set as a PDF sourcebook: lore, places, affiliations, beings, skills, mystical abilities, mysteries, concoctions, weapons, scenarios and reference.
- Each part opens with a table of contents and the book closes with an index of every entry and the pages that mention it.
