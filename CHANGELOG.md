## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

Thalorna is the World of Thalorna, a fantasy setting for Song of Heroic Lands, delivered as Foundry VTT compendium packs, PDF books and a website built from the same notes. This first release contains:

- **Foundry:** the module installs in any Foundry VTT 14 world; its journals read under every system, and its creature, character and item packs appear under Song of Heroic Lands 0.8.8 or later.
- **Books:** a PDF gazetteer for each of twenty-four regions and peoples, and _The World of Thalorna_, _The Rules of Thalorna_ and _The Bestiary of Thalorna_ sourcebooks.
- **Calendars:** the setting's calendars ship as Foundry calendars and Calendaria imports; Calendaria is optional.
- **Peoples:** every culture has an account of ordinary life from childhood to the deathbed, and an Adventurer's Guide that opens on an arrival, covers power, faith and daily life, and ends with a glossary.
- **Places:** nearly nine hundred places, from cities to wild sites, each opening on what it is known for and naming the body that governs it.
- **History:** every region has a written history in ages or eras, with king-lists, dated events, blood-fields and sites that carry adventure hooks.
- **Faiths:** the Ten and their twenty-nine devotional orders, the Aurèldían, Khelâthi, Vedyaran, Itzáni and Nyáluba gods, the north's cosmology, and the Shadow behind every undead.
- **Ranks and bodies:** every character shows the standing held in each faith, house, guild or order, read from that body's own rank ladder, with offices held apart from rank.
- **Characters:** the people the setting names have pages with a description, an appearance, a home and their patrons, rivals and affiliations, and three Varokhi heroes come with playable sheets.
- **Bestiary:** creatures read as field-guide entries with signs and local customs, ninety-one ordinary animals live in Thalorna's own lands, and every creature has a sheet with its attacks and icon.
- **Adventures:** ten dread adventures that turn on a reversal, and twenty-five wild places, each with a site page and a five-scene sword-and-sorcery adventure.
- **Literature:** every culture keeps its own songs, sagas, laments and recitations, quoted in the entries they concern.
- **Languages and names:** names read easily at the table, every tongue has its own sound, and lexicons give the words, name elements and naming customs of each people.
- **For the referee:** passages meant only for the referee are secret sections in Foundry and hidden spoilers on the website, and unfinished draft pages say so above their text.
- **Reading:** the first mention of a named person, place or body links to its entry or is highlighted, and every page ends with what touches it: places held, chronology, events and appearances in song.
