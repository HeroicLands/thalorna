Hvarn is the tongue of the [[affiliation-hvarnguides|Hvarn]] of [[place-estrnreach|the Eastern Reach]]. Fluency measures how much of it a speaker commands, from the fort-contract vocabulary a lowland factor picks up to the full speech of the winter house. As with all specific languages, this skill inherits its mechanics from the general [[sohl-none-docskill-lang|Language]] skill.

## Overview

Hvarn is not related to [[skill-osketlng|Ösket]], which surprises everyone who has assumed it must be. The two peoples live three hundred miles apart along the same wall, do much the same work, marry across the gap occasionally, and speak languages with no demonstrable common stock – a fact both peoples state without interest and lowland scholars find difficult to accept.

It is the trans-Grazian language outsiders most often learn, which is to say that perhaps forty lowlanders speak it. They are the factors who renegotiate the garrison contract, a handful of eastern janapada clerks, and the occasional soldier of [[place-sanghafort|the Saṅgha-fort]] who has wintered at [[place-nurvhrn|Nürvhrn]] and come back fluent and strange.

## Phonology

### Consonant System

**Unvoiced:** p, t, k

**Voiced:** b, d, g

**Affricates:** ts, dz

**Fricatives:** f, v, s, z, sh, zh, h

**Nasals:** m, n

**Liquids & Approximants:** l, r, rh (voiceless), w

### Vowel System

**Vowels:** a, e, i, o, u, ü (front rounded)

**Diphthongs:** ei, ou

Hvarn admits syllabic liquids and nasals, so that a word may be spoken with no vowel in it at all: _Nürvhrn_ carries its second syllable on the r, and _hvrn_, the people's own word for themselves, has none. Lowlanders supply a vowel where there is none, which is why the name is written with one.

### Phonotactic Patterns

- **Consonant Clusters:** Heavy in every position, and the initial cluster _hv-_ is the people's own name
- **Syllable Structure:** Closed, and frequently vowelless; stress falls on the first syllable without exception
- **Final Consonants:** Unrestricted, and a final cluster of three is ordinary
- **Word Length:** Short and dense; a two-syllable Hvarn word routinely carries what Vedyari needs five for

## Grammar Notes

Word order is verb-final. The verbal system is built on aspect rather than tense: what matters is whether an action is finished, continuing, repeated or abandoned, and when it happened is left to the adverbs. Nouns are not inflected for number. The most elaborated part of the grammar is a system of paired forms distinguishing what a speaker has done himself from what he has done as one of a party, which is exactly the distinction a people who work in roped groups would elaborate.

## Script & Literacy

There is no script and there is no cord. What the Hvarn keep, they keep by recitation: the roll of the dead and where each was found, the rotation of the crossings, the debts between hearths. The Bone-bringer recites the roll at the winter council each year, and a fault in the recitation is corrected from the floor by anyone who catches it.

## Naming Traditions

A person carries a given name and a hearth name, and the hearth name changes if he is taken into another hearth. Given names are drawn from the roll: a child is named for a person in it, and the naming carries the obligation to know where that person was found.
