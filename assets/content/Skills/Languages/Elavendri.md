Élavendri is the lyrical tongue of the realm of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], a language steeped in magic, nature, and the influence of the fey-touched peoples who dwell in the borderlands between human and faerie realms. Where [[skill-vylarilng|Vylari]] is precise and administrative, Élavendri is fluid and suggestive—a language that reaches toward meanings beyond the grasp of ordinary words. It is the language of courts and verse: in western [[place-aureldirgn|Aureldia Region]] Élavendri is held to be the modern language of love, the courts and art, with [[skill-helonclng|Helonic]] its classical counterpart. The [[lore-vylarianclt|Vylarian]] people do not hold it so. Its grammar draws fine distinctions of rank, courtesy and feeling that the other western tongues leave unsaid, and nobles beyond Élavendre learn it as a mark of breeding. On [[place-aelwyth|Aelwyth]] the travelled and educated commonly speak it beside [[skill-aelwthlng|Aelwythi]], and the learned of Élavendre commonly speak Aelwythi in return; the two tongues have long borrowed words from each other.

## Overview

Élavendri speakers understand their language as more than communication; it is an art form and a path to understanding the deeper patterns of magic and nature. The language employs numerous words for subtle states of being, gradations of light, types of growth, and magical phenomena that have no equivalents in neighboring tongues.

The language is marked by a preference for alliteration, assonance, and poetic phrasing even in everyday speech. A simple greeting in Élavendri carries overtones of blessing and ritual. The language's connection to music is profound—many Élavendri linguistic features are better understood through melody than grammar.

The [[skill-elvndrlng#lexicon|Élavendri Lexicon]] gives the words with their sources, each traced to its [[skill-aelendlng|Áelendi]] or imperial original, and the changes that made them.

## How Élavendri sounds

Élavendri is the soft lowland daughter of Áelendi. Its vowels break into pairs, its consonants loosen between vowels, and its words end on a murmured _-e_.

**The vowels.** Vowel pairs carry the language: _ie_, _ou_, _ae_ and _ei_, as in _ríetheson_ and _sóurimathen_. A final _-e_ closes many words.

**The consonants.** _l_, _v_, _r_, _n_, _s_, _m_ and _th_ do most of the work. _ny_ and _sh_ appear before a vowel.

**Clusters.** _lv_, _rd_, _nd_, _nth_ and _thr_ meet inside a word.

**Endings.** Élavendri words end in _-e_, _-el_, _-en_, _-and_, _-is_ or _-on_.

**What Élavendri leaves out.** It has no _kh_, _gh_ or _dh_, which are Áelendi's, and no apostrophe. It avoids words of more than four syllables.

## Phonology

Élavendri employs one of the richest vowel systems of any Pelwar language, with extensive use of diphthongs and subtle vocalic gradations. Consonants are relatively soft, with the language favoring liquids and sonorants over hard stops:

**Consonants:** The language reduces the number of stops (only t, d, k, g are common) and favors fricatives (th, s, sh, ch) and liquids (l, r). The combination of sounds creates natural palatalization—many consonants are pronounced with a slight y-glide in certain contexts. Aspiration (marking with h after a consonant) is phonemic and creates meaningful distinctions.

**Vowels:** Élavendri distinguishes at least seven vowel positions, many with both oral and nasal variants. The system employs systematic length distinctions and allows complex diphthongs that change meaning based on speed of articulation. Vowel harmony principles influence word formation—words tend to maintain vowel colors across morphemes.

**Prosody:** Élavendri employs melodic stress, with pitch patterns carrying as much information as stress accent. The language naturally divides into musical phrases, each with characteristic pitch contours. This has made Élavendri ideal for poetry and music, and difficult for non-native speakers to acquire.

**Distinctive Features:** The language makes heavy use of the "fey fricative" /th/ (voiced dental fricative /d/), which features prominently in words related to magic, fey beings, and abstraction. The combination th- at the beginning of words often indicates magical or abstract concepts. Nasal vowels occur in both oral and nasal variants, creating subtle distinctions.

## Grammar Notes

**Word Order:** Élavendri employs a relatively free word order. The basic order is VSO (Verb-Subject-Object) in formal contexts, but poetic reordering is common and expected.

**Gender:** Gender is somewhat less important than in Vylari or [[skill-tarvenlng|Tarveni]], with only two primary genders (animate/inanimate) in standard speech, though archaic texts preserve three-gender systems.

**Adjectives and Adverbs:** The language employs a complex system of derivational morphology to create adjectival and adverbial forms. Diminutive, augmentative, and affective suffixes are productive and frequently used. The language readily creates new descriptive forms on the fly.

**Article System:** Élavendri employs a well-developed article system distinguishing definite, indefinite, partitive, and demonstrative forms. The choice of article can carry significant meaning in poetic contexts, indicating levels of reality or abstraction.

## Script & Literacy

Élavendri traditionally employs [[skill-thrvrdhscrpt|Thurarth]], the old [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan rune-row]], but this script has been largely supplemented by the [[skill-semrnscrpt|Semaron]]—the same alphabet used in Vylari and neighboring regions. Thurarth persists in magical texts, poetry, and items of great antiquity. The [[skill-clthndscrpt|Veldikko]] of the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale]] never came down the passes: the highlanders write Áelendi in it and the lowlands took imperial letters instead, so two peoples who can hold a conversation cannot read a word of each other.

A distinctive scribal tradition employs flowing, connected letterforms that reflect the language's musical nature. Decorative elements (flourishes, miniatures) are common in high-quality texts. True literacy is confined to the priesthood, professional scribes, those training for magical arts (for whom reading is an absolute necessity), and perhaps one noble in four. The common folk of Élavendre, like those elsewhere on [[place-worldthlrn|Thalorna]], are almost entirely non-literate and rely on scribes for any written communication.

## Historical Development

Élavendri developed from Proto-Pelwar stock but in isolation from the other branches, in the northern forests and mountains of Élavendre. The language absorbed deep influence from the non-Pelwar populations of the region—people of fey heritage, indigenous forest dwellers, and magical practitioners—resulting in a tongue with features quite foreign to other Pelwar languages.

The most conservative member of the Pelwar family is [[skill-aelendlng|Áelendi]], spoken by the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] people in the mountainous interior of Élavendre. Élavendri proper represents a "modernized" variant that has absorbed more external influences and grammatical simplification, particularly the loss of one gender and some synthetic verb forms.

## Regional Dialects

**Coastal Élavendri:** Spoken in the port cities and plains, showing some influence from trading contacts with speakers of [[skill-provnzlng|Provenzal Language]] and Vylari. More straightforward phonetically and grammatically than inland variants.

**Sylvan Élavendri:** The dialect of the deep forests and rural areas, preserving more archaic features and fey vocabulary. More complex phonetics and more archaic grammar.

**Magical Register:** A specialized, highly formal variant used in magical rituals and high ceremony, employing archaic vocabulary and conservative phonetics. Knowledge of this register marks one as educated in magical arts.

For the ancestral Pelwar tongue of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]]—the most conservative member of the family, spoken in the mountainous interior—see [[skill-aelendlng|Áelendi Language]].

## Related Languages

Élavendri stands somewhat apart from its Pelwar siblings, having developed in relative isolation and under heavy influence from fey and pre-human populations. Its deep connection to Áelendi marks both as descendents of a northern branch of Proto-Pelwar, distinct from the southern branch that gave rise to Vylari, Tarveni, and the others.

Élavendri speakers find Provenzal somewhat intelligible, particularly in poetic contexts—Provenzal has absorbed substantial Élavendri vocabulary in the domains of art, emotion, and magic (words like _thel_, _sil_, _daren_, _thal-_, _ael-_), and Provenzal sílador poetry frequently employs Élavendri word order and the copula _en_. An Élavendri speaker hearing a Provenzal love song will catch familiar words amid the foreign grammar. Vylari and Tarveni speakers find Élavendri quite difficult. The northern languages [[skill-nordmalng|Nordmal Language]] and [[skill-varokhlng|Varokhi Language]] share some phonetic features with Élavendri but remain largely mutually unintelligible.

## Naming Traditions

Élavendri names are often compound forms, combining roots related to nature, magic, and abstract qualities. A name might literally translate as "Bright-Stream" or "Shadow-Watcher" or "Keeper-of-Deep-Words." The meanings are taken seriously—a name is considered to shape character and destiny.

Many Élavendri individuals have both a "sun name" (used in formal and public contexts) and a "shadow name" (used only among intimates and at night). The shadow name is considered the true name, encoding the person's essential nature.

The [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] of the interior name from a stock of their own, built on roots the lowlands no longer say and closed with endings the lowlands have lost; see [[skill-aelendlng|Áelendi Language]] for it. The two sets are not interchangeable, and an Élavendri hearing an Áelendi clan name knows the mountains are being spoken of before the speaker says so.

Élavendri names are typically melodic and employ frequent use of the fey fricative (th) and liquid consonants (l, r). Many names incorporate the morpheme _-ael_ (light), _-thal_ (shadow), or _-síl_ (song).

## Élavendri Lexicon {#lexicon}

A bard of a great house in [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] is reciting the house's line at a feast, and he is doing it in two tongues at once without anyone at the table remarking on it. The names of the founders are highland names, said the way the highlands say them, with the clicks in; the words that join them are Élavendri, the same words with the clicks gone, the long vowels opened into diphthongs and the last vowel of each one let fall to a murmur. The house's claim to the old blood rests on the guests hearing the difference and believing the line runs across it.

This is the word-hoard of Élavendri, the tongue of the lowland kingdom. It is the daughter of Áelendi, the highland tongue that came over the eastern pass, made over by the lowlands in the centuries since: its clicked stops merged with the plain ones, its back breathed stops weakened or fell, its long vowels broke into the diphthongs it is admired for, and its final vowels wore down to a reduced _ë_ that is written so that it is said. Because it is Áelendi's daughter it carries Áelendi's great layer of [[skill-sinalelng|Sinale]] words, the trees, the light, the veil and the art, and several of them came back nearly to their Sinale shape on the way down. Because the kingdom owed the empire service for centuries, it carries a smaller layer of imperial words as well: the law, the coin, the letters and the University.

### Classes

## From Áelendi {#from-aelendi}

Thirteen changes, in this order, make an Élavendri word of an Áelendi one. The clicked stops merged with the plain ones. The breathed `kh` weakened to `h`; `gh` lost its breath at the start of a word and fell everywhere else, and the like vowels it had stood between ran together into one. A long vowel in the stressed open syllable broke into a diphthong, long `a` to `ae`, long `e` to `ei`, long `i` to `ie`, long `o` to `ou` and long `u` to `ye`, and every other long vowel shortened. Between vowels `p` softened to `v`, as it did after a nasal or a liquid, and `t`, `d` and `k` softened to `d`, `dh` and `g`. Before the new front diphthongs `k`, `g` and `s` became `ch`, `j` and `sh`. A final vowel other than _-a_ after a consonant was reduced to a weak vowel, and the stress stayed on the first syllable of the root.

So the highland name _Khalden_ is _Halden_, _kuthresa_ (a forfeit) is _chyethresa_, _sirothathen_ (to seat a guest) is _shírothathen_, and the Sinale loan _kálpirno_, a stand of birch, comes back to _kálvirne_, close to the Sinale _kalpikko_ it started from.

The Sinale words ride the same table, and many of them come back toward their Sinale shape on the way down, because the lowland softening undoes what the highland mouth did to them: the Sinale `v` the Áelendan said as `p` is `v` again between vowels. This is where the "fey" sound of Élavendri comes from. The words that open on _th-_ are disproportionately the Sinale words, and the tongue's whole Brittonic colour, the colour of the lays, is the Sinale layer heard through two mouths.

## The learned words {#the-learned-words}

The imperial words came with the clerks in the centuries of vassalage and with the [[skill-semrnscrpt|Semaron]] and the University after. A learned word keeps its imperial shape more than an inherited one does: it loses its masculine ending and its long vowels, reduces its last vowel to _ë_, and takes the stress on its first syllable, but it keeps `b`, `f` and `z`, which no inherited word has. Several stand beside the inherited form of the same root: the inherited _lóuresand_ is a reciter who keeps a house's records in his head, and the learned _lórezand_ is a lecturer at the University.

## Words {#words}

### The house and the old blood

An Élavendri house is the whole descending line, the dead included, and its standing rests on one claim: that the line runs back to the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]]. The words the houses use to make it are the highland words, worn down. A clan name in the highlands is the founder's name with _-ndi_; in the lowlands the old houses carry the same ending as _-nde_, and a house that can show _sóurethende_, kin of one blood, on a genealogy that goes back to the crossing outranks one that cannot, whatever its acreage.

An old line is _théilaralon_, the word the empire uses for a noble house with a seat in its [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] (Senate), and an earned name, _kíramarlen_, is the one a bard gives. Whether all the claims are genuine is another matter, and a claim kept up gracefully for four hundred years has earned a standing of its own.

### The land in keeping

Land is a duty and not a possession. A lord who lets his people go hungry has failed the one thing his rank is for, and the land words are the herders' words of the highlands turned to the lowland estate. _Órmalis_ is the land a house keeps, _tárhelon_ the common store held against a bad winter, and _háresand_ the steward who answers for it. A house is judged by the state of the land in its keeping as much as by its bards.

### The bard and the word

Poetry is how a house's history is held, how a prophecy is passed on and how a judgment is made memorable enough to bind, and the bard's words are the oldest in the tongue's care. _Sóurimand_, a singer, is the highland singer at the gathering grown into a court officer; _lóuresand_, a reciter, is the highland Tradition Keeper, and in the lowlands he keeps a house's records in the head. A satire composed by a bard of standing is a weapon the law recognizes, and it is made of these words.

The letters came twice. The rune-row is still cut for magic and old poetry, and its staves are _shéimarson_. Everything else is written in the empire's [[skill-semrnscrpt|Semaron]], whose letter is _sémarzon_, the same word borrowed back from the coast.

### The art and the thin places

Magic is ordinary here, studied and discussed at table and expected of the well-bred, and its words came down through the highlands from the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale]]. That is where the tongue's _th-_ comes from: so many of the Sinale words open on it that _th-_ is the sound of the uncanny in the tongue. The art is _simme_, a thin place _sírnaste_, a spirit _áitheve_, and the fae, one at a time, _pénallane_. The lowland mage, _thórdimand_, is the word the highlands use for him, and the Élavendri do not mind it.

### The Pact, the oath and the judgment

The [Kindred Pact]{.name data-type="lore/law"} is the last thing an Élavendri owes, and he does not explain it to a foreigner, partly from courtesy and partly because he is not sure of the terms. Its words are the highland words: _tháurathen_, to keep, _thaurand_, a keeper, _thólmesa_, a vow. In the lowlands they serve the courts as well as the groves. A judgment is _nasdis_ and the council that gives it _kúrthema_, the word the empire keeps for its Senate.

### The hall and the guest

The lodge of the highlands is the lowland hall, and the courtesy of the hall is the highlands' own, refined. The kept fire is _káralson_; the order of the seats, _shírothalon_, is worked out for every feast; and the welcome, _éllevirsa_, is the Sinale word the highlands took and the lowlands kept.

### Light, sky and the turning year

The light words are the most Sinale of all, and the bards' favorites: _áulira_, light, _olthe_, a cast shadow, _írvale_, dawn. The years are counted from the crossing, _sérthasis_, like the highlands' and unlike the empire's.

### The wood and its weather

The kingdom is a woodland kingdom, and every tree in it has a Sinale name brought down through the highlands: _hersel_, a pine, _sálvurne_, a grove. Several came back to their Sinale shape on the way, the lowland changes undoing what the highland mouth had done to them.

### Mountain, water and stone

The kingdom's interior is the Áelendan's mountains, and the lowland words for it are the highland ones softened: _nélamis_, snow, _néiralis_, a valley, _úlmera_, water.

### The body and the dead

The dead are part of the house, and the words for them are many. The highland cairn, _démrolon_, is a lowland tomb, and the Sinale words for death and grief, _sélvore_ and _nélthava_, are the bards' words for them.

### Arms and the border

The kingdom fights seldom, and its war words are the highland [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]]' words with little added.

### Craft, coin and the market

The craft words are inherited, and the coin words came from the empire with its coins.

### The crown, the law and the University

For centuries the kingdom owed the empire service, and the words of that service came north with the imperial clerks and stayed: the law, _vornon_, the charter, _záktela_, the tax farmer, _sóhatand_. They are the only words in the tongue with `b`, `f` or `z`, and the three letters mark a clerk's word. The University and the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] brought a second band, and the kingdom treats the Order as a rival to be balanced, licensed and argued with, in the Order's own words: a lecturer is _lórezand_, the learned twin of the highland _lóuresand_, a reciter.

### Doing, going and being

The verbs are cited by the infinitive, as the highlands cite them.

### Numbers and grammar words

The article _al_ is the highland _al_ with the lowland stress it takes like every word.
