Khazri is the tongue of the [[affiliation-khzrncnfdrtn|Khazryn Confederation]], the indigenous oasis-civilization of the Khazryn Desert. The Khazryn name the Catastrophe in it as Vēštákán, "the Breaking." Fluency measures the sophistication of expression in the language, from the halting phrases of a traveler to the nuanced discourse of a native speaker. As with all specific languages, this skill inherits its mechanics from the general [[sohl-none-docskill-lang|Language]] skill.
