Ātárzādi is the tongue of the [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Tribes of Ātárzád]], the twelve tribes who hold the choicest oases of the Khazryn Desert. Fluency measures the sophistication of expression in the language, from the halting phrases of a traveler to the nuanced discourse of a native speaker. As with all specific languages, this skill inherits its mechanics from the general [[sohl-none-docskill-lang|Language]] skill.
