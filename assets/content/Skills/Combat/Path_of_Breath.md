The **Path of Breath** is a meditative martial discipline of the sword, practised in the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Empire of Vylaria]], that unites physical technique with spiritual discipline. It is a specialization of Melee. Its maneuvers do not give the sword a new way to strike; they change how the swordsman uses the strikes the sword already has.

## The discipline

The Path is learned after the fundamental forms of swordsmanship, not instead of them. A student comes to it already a swordsman, through years of study of the forms and of the ancient texts on the blade, and its manuals are studied alongside practice. What it adds is the spiritual half: an understanding of conflict, of honor, and of the responsibility that comes with the ability to kill. Those who master it treat combat as a form of communication, and a perfect duel as a conversation between two skilled practitioners.

Mastery is rare. Those who have reached it are the masters of the order that bears the same name, the [[affiliation-pthbrthordr|Path of Breath]], and each wears a tattoo of a dragonfly on the neck. [[being-krmdnsbrkrtys|Koremdanis Barkratis]] of [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]] is one of the three now living.

## Maneuvers

Each maneuver is used with a sword, and the swordsman chooses one for each exchange or fights plainly.

**Heron Counterstrike**
: Used with a thrust when counterstriking. The counterstrike is harder to make, but one that wins earns an extra Impact advantage.

**Flowing Guard**
: Used with any of the sword's strikes, to block. It takes a better block modifier, and it cannot be used to attack.

**Precision Strike**
: Used with a cut or a thrust, to attack. It takes a bonus to the attack and places the blow more tightly.

**Bind and Disarm**
: Used with a cut, to attack. A winning exchange may be turned into a Take, prising the opponent's weapon from his hand, with a bonus to the Strength Trial.

**Tempo Form**
: Used with any of the sword's strikes, to attack. A winning exchange earns an extra Action advantage.
