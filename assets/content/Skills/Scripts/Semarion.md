The Sêmarion is the working alphabet of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]—twenty-four letters written left to right, each with a majuscule and a minuscule form, one sign to one sound. Its name is simply the Vylari word for _the letters_, from _sêma_, "mark"; a single character is a _sêma_, and to a Vylarian clerk the word carries no more romance than "the alphabet" does. That flatness is a large part of why it traveled.

As with any script, it is a writing system and not a tongue. A character needs **both** the Sêmarion and the relevant [[sohl-none-docskill-lang|Language]] to read anything set down in it, and the Sêmarion carries a good many languages that have nothing to do with each other—see [[sohl-none-docskill-script|Script]] for how the two skills combine.

## The letters

The letterforms descend from the older carved runes of [[skill-thrvrknscrpt|Thâravárkon]], but centuries of reed and quill have rounded every angle out of them. Strokes are continuous and slightly forward-leaning; the pen is meant to leave the page as seldom as possible. Vowel length—the long _â ê î ô û_ that Vylari depends on—is carried by a bar above the letter, and the aspirates (_ph_, _kh_, _th_) by a small hook, which is why a hurried provincial hand is so often ambiguous about precisely which lord is meant.

## Hands

The Guild recognizes three, and a reader's fluency is not the same in all of them:

- **_Sêmarion Kûriale_**, the chancery hand—upright, wide-spaced, unligatured, and slow. Edicts, land registers, treaty texts, anything that must still be legible in two hundred years.
- **_Sêmarion Kursîva_**, the running hand—the daily hand of clerks, factors and correspondents, heavily ligatured and abbreviated, three times faster to write and markedly harder to read. Most surviving Vylarian documents are in it.
- **Provincial hands**—the local drifts of the cursive, in Hylen and Alyssa and the frontier garrisons. A scribe raised on one reads another slowly.

## Who has it

Literacy in the Sêmarion is what the [[affiliation-imprlscrblgld|Imperial Scribal Guild]] sells, examines and defends. Its seven-year course teaches the formal hand, the cursives, the certification formulas and the detection of forgery, and a Guild seal at the foot of a document is what makes the document enforceable. Outside the Guild, the script is held by the clergy, by perhaps a fifth to a third of the nobility, and by the senior clerks of the great trading houses. Guildsmen and prosperous merchants commonly know their numerals, their trade marks and the shape of their own names and no more, and hire a scribe for anything longer.

## Beyond the Empire

The Sêmarion is the nearest thing Mídhalión has to a common alphabet. [[skill-elvndrlng|Élavendri]] largely abandoned its own runes for it; [[skill-provnzlng|Provenzal]] is written in nothing else; the Byzarian League keeps its ledgers in it whatever tongue the entries are in; and Varokhi speakers who want anything written down borrow it, while insisting it is a foreign tool and not Varokhi writing. Foreigners generally call it "the Vylari letters," a name no Vylarian uses.

## In play

A character with the Sêmarion and a language written in it can read a clean text at ordinary speed. Cursive and provincial hands bite: they are exactly the sort of poor hand the Script rules penalize, and a document that has been copied twice by clerks who did not understand it will have collected errors that no amount of Mastery recovers. Forgery is the standing plot: Guild authentication is a Sêmarion test in its own right, and the empire's whole administrative fabric rests on the assumption that it works.
