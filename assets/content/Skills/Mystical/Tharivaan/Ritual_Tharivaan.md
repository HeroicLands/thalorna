Ritual: [[affiliation-tharivaan|Thári'vaan]] is the custodians' craft of the tending-acts: the [Dawn Tending]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, the [Seasonal Recitations]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, the extended tendings of the [Tenth-Year Walk]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, the liturgies that open a work's oval and the [Rite of Last Tending]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}. Each is a fixed sequence in the archaic tongue, with hands laid on named places in a named order. Any trained custodian performs them, and a work accepts them correctly performed from anyone. The skill has nothing to do with the answering, which no training brings and which is described under [[mysticalability-hearingstone|Hearing the Stone]].
