Ritual: Thári'vaan is the custodians' craft of the tending-acts: the Dawn Tending, the Seasonal Recitations, the extended tendings of the Tenth-Year Walk, the liturgies that open a work's oval and the Rite of Last Tending. Each is a fixed sequence in the archaic tongue, with hands laid on named places in a named order. Any trained custodian performs them, and a work accepts them correctly performed from anyone. The skill has nothing to do with the answering, which no training brings and which is described under [[mysticalability-hearingstone|Hearing the Stone]].
