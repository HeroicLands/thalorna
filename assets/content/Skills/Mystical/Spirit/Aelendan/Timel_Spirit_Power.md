Standing with Timel measures how well a shaman keeps the terms of the hawk of the Spine and reads what he shows. Timel holds the high air over the Drenavar Spine and is met by going up, not at the lodge or the hearth. The appeasement is territorial: the nesting crags are not climbed between the thaw and midsummer, without exception, and what is given otherwise is small and given high. He is consulted for sight, meaning weather coming over the Spine, movement on the far slopes and the state of a pass. A shaman with good standing is worth a great deal to a tribe, and for that reason reluctant to travel far from the Spine.
