Standing with Tena measures how well a person keeps and invokes the peace of the hall at Ésalosa. Tena is the oldest tended fire in Áelendan country, and everything under her roof is under her notice: no weapon carried past her fire, no oath sworn at it and afterward broken, no guest received and then harmed. The skill is used to swear an oath at her fire, to claim guest-right under her roof, and to carry an ember from her hearth so that a distant camp burns under her notice. A violation is not forgiven by the Áelendan, who have no standing to forgive for her, and what follows is between Tena and the offender.
