Standing with Óssenda measures how well a shaman or Death-Speaker approaches the white owl of the Owl Hollow. Óssenda keeps what is said aloud at night in her wood, and the skill covers both the small standing offering at the wood's edge and the careful speech that goes with it. Its chief use is to ask her about the recently dead, not to commend them but to learn whether they have gone. The nearest tribe lets Death-Speakers of other tribes come to the Hollow for this and charges nothing, because the answer is hers to give and not theirs to sell. She remembers everything spoken within her territory after dark, so a speaker who uses the skill badly is remembered too.
