Standing with Sálenda measures how exactly a shaman meets the river-mother of the Long Stair. Sálenda is neither malicious nor kind. She has specific requirements and accepts no approximation of them, and the skill is the exactness. The offering is made at the lowest pool at first light, in silence: something the giver made with their own hands and would rather have kept, never retrieved. She permits fishing in the lower water and forbids it above the third fall, permits crossing and forbids swimming, and nobody climbs the Stair by daylight. The skill gives no way past those bounds. It keeps the shaman on the right side of them.
