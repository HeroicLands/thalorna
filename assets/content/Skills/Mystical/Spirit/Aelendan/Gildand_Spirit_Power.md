Standing with Gildand measures how well a shaman or traveler speaks to the west wind in the moment. Gildand holds no territory and keeps no pact, so there is nothing to bargain over and no offering to owe. The skill is the address itself: short, spoken aloud into the wind at the turn of a season, before a crossing or when a storm builds off the water. A good result means the address was made cleanly and at the right time. It does not tell the speaker whether Gildand heard, because nobody can tell. He deceives no one, bargains with no one and stays nowhere, so the skill buys no favor and gives no lasting claim. It is used once, in the moment, and begun again each time the wind rises.
