Standing with the Uron Tekhi measures how well a shaman approaches the old tree in its clearing in the Silver Forests, and how much the shaman can bear of what it says. The Tekhi is place-kin that holds its clearing as far as its shadow reaches, and it is counted fae-adjacent, so its protocols borrow from the Kindred Pact. It is approached rarely, formally and for a reason, since nobody is confident what would be accepted. It answers questions. The answers are accurate, are not explanations and tend to be about something other than what was asked. The skill covers the asking and the reading of the answer, not any claim upon the tree.
