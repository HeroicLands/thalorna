Standing with Urnel measures how well a shaman keeps the right posture for the season with the bear of the high corries. From the first hard frost to the thaw Urnel is warded against: the corries are not entered, the protective songs are carried by anyone who must pass below, and nobody goes to look for the lost. From the thaw to the frost he is venerated, credited with the health of the high pasture, the summer honey and warning of avalanche, and the summer rite in the corries is among the larger occasions of the Áelendan year. The skill is knowing which Urnel is present. He is one spirit, wanting different things at different times of the year.
