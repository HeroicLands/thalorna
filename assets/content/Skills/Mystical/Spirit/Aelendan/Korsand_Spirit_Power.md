Standing with Korsand measures how well a hunter or shaman keeps the terms of the gray wolf of the ridge. Korsand is the ancestral presence of the wolves of one long ridge in the Drenavar Spine, and most hunt-rites of the Áelendan in that country are addressed to him. The terms are fixed: a share left where the kill was made, not carried home first, and an honest count of what was taken. Using the skill means making that rite correctly and reporting truly. He cannot be deceived about the count, and a false report is not punished by him: the ridge gives the tribe's next hunt nothing, and the tribe deals with the hunter.
