## Ritual: Zhausou

Rite
: The [[affiliation-nuotsourui|Nwotsou Rwi]] invokes Zhausou in its recruitment rituals and in the ceremonies by which new members mark their passage beyond the reach of imperial classification. The cult recruits the classification-condemned, whose moral ledgers carry debits so severe that no ritual redemption is possible, and offers them a reframe: if a being like Zhausou exists among the celestials, their own condition is a cosmically grounded state

Cost
: Standing within the imperial order, which the recruit gives up in passing beyond its classification

Performed by
: The Nwotsou Rwi, for its recruits and new members

Licensed by
: No one. Every bureau that has claimed jurisdiction over Zhausou has found its classification failing, and the cult's doctrine holds that its endurance proves the [Great Sorting]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"} cannot be completed

See [[affiliation-zhaosou|Zhausou]].
