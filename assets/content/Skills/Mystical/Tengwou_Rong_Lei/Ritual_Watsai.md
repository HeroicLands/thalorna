## Ritual: Watsai

Rite
: The deliberate corruption of the imperial classification apparatus from within. The agents of the [[affiliation-nuotsourui|Nwotsou Rwi]] in the lower bureaucracy, among them clerks, copyists and registration functionaries, understand their mis-filings, duplications and planted errors as service to the Forger. Small Watsai-sigils are cut into the undersides of desks and hidden in the bindings of defaced registers, and a successful archive corruption is said to be signed by the Counterfeit Scribe

Cost
: The patience a long corruption takes, and the risk of discovery by the archive's inspectors and the Celestial Marshals

Performed by
: Cult agents placed in the lower bureaucracy

Licensed by
: No bureau. Watsai fled the [[affiliation-wantou|Archive of All Records]] into the [[lore-infrnlrlms|Infernal Realms]] with records no other being holds, and the cult regards each corruption as proof that the empire's records are partly false
