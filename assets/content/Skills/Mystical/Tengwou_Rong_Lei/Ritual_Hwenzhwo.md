## Ritual: Hwenzhwo

Rite
: No petition is filed and no prayer is said, because invocation requires categorizing and Hwenzhwo is the refusal of category. The [[affiliation-nuotsourui|Nwotsou Rwi]] takes Hwenzhwo as its foundational figure and imitates it. A member who accepts the classification-condemnation rather than seeking redemption, who refuses to petition the bureaus for reclassification and chooses to stand outside the categorical order, is said to walk in the First Refusal

Cost
: The member's place in the order. Those who walk too far in the First Refusal begin to lose the distinctions that make thought possible: self and other, past and future, waking and sleep

Performed by
: Members of the Nwotsou Rwi, each for themselves

Licensed by
: No one. Hwenzhwo is not a bureau and issues no license
