## Ritual: Wansou Zhwo

Rite
: A petition to Singwou, the Ministry of Final Account, for the safe passage of a relative who has died. At death the Chief Auditor reviews the moral ledger line by line, the running account of credits and debits that the household's Tsaiken has filed all along, and the audit determines the quality of the afterlife and the power the spirit will have to advocate for its descendants

Cost
: A fee among the largest a household will pay, in addition to the tribute that carries the petition

Performed by
: A Clerk, the temple's ordinary officer, who drafts the petition in the correct forms. The Seal-Holder stamps it, and the Clerk of Transmission burns it at the bureau's own censer; a petition burned at the wrong censer reaches the wrong bureau and is lost. The Halei escort souls from death to processing, and the Ten Magistrates of the Dead each preside over a category of moral failing

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-wansouzhuo|Wansou Zhwo]], the Teng Gwi of the ministry, which cooperates with the other bureaus when it chooses and cannot be compelled, since every file ends on its desk
