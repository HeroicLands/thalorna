## Ritual: Wodou

Rite
: A petition to Dou Sei, the Bureau of Passage, which administers movement between classified regions and so the boundaries themselves. Travelers and merchants file for safe passage, and its filings are the cheapest routine business in the empire because so many are made. The bureau's Douzhi keep the waymarks that say where one classification ends and the next begins, and its Zhowo govern safe transit on waterways

Cost
: The temple's fee, since preparing a petition correctly is expertise, and the tribute that pays the petition's carriage (coin, grain, cloth or livestock), which the Receiver of Tribute enters to the petitioner's credit

Performed by
: A Clerk, the temple's ordinary officer, who drafts the petition in the correct forms. The Seal-Holder stamps it, and the Clerk of Transmission burns it at the bureau's own censer; a petition burned at the wrong censer reaches the wrong bureau and is lost

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-wodou|Wodou]], the Teng Gwi of Dou Sei, the bureau most damaged by the Youweng's Gap-Makers, whose forged boundaries leave waymarks that mark nothing
