## Ritual: Raugwi

Rite
: A formal petition to the Bureau of Thunder and Rain for an emergency water allocation in drought or for storm suppression in a critical harvest, citing the precedent of earlier droughts, the moral standing of the village's ancestors and the cost to the agricultural schedule. A village does not pray for rain. After the petition is filed it is burned, which transmits it to the celestial filing system, and the rain is held to come according to the restored schedule. A family struck by lightning petitions for an investigation into whether the strike followed proper procedure; most such petitions fail, but the appeal is a recognized social practice

Cost
: Substantial fees, since the petition needs scholars to research precedent, and the standing of the village's ancestor spirits, whose advocacy speeds a petition and whose low standing slows it

Performed by
: The priest-clerks of the bureau's temples, found in the significant farming regions and every major port city, who know the correct forms, seals and calendrical schedules

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-raogui|Raugwi]], the Minister of Storms. A petition that conflicts with another approved allocation is not honored, and an appeal against a punitive strike goes to [Tengwou Dau]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, the Supreme Administrative Court
