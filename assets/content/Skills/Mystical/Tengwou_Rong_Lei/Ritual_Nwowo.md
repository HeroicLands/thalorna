## Ritual: Nwowo

Rite
: A petition to Nwo Sei, the Bureau of Returns, the reclamation office where what has finished being one thing is broken down and returned as material for something else. Mortals rarely petition it directly and are wary when they must: a request to Nwo Sei is a request that something be ended, and the bureau is scrupulous about establishing that the petitioner understands what has been asked

Cost
: The temple's fee, since preparing a petition correctly is expertise, and the tribute that pays the petition's carriage (coin, grain, cloth or livestock), which the Receiver of Tribute enters to the petitioner's credit

Performed by
: A Clerk, the temple's ordinary officer, who drafts the petition in the correct forms. The Seal-Holder stamps it, and the Clerk of Transmission burns it at the bureau's own censer; a petition burned at the wrong censer reaches the wrong bureau and is lost. Its Nwolei break down expired classifications, and its Zhawo feed the recycled material back to the Bureau of Creation

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-nuowo|Nwowo]], the Teng Gwi of Nwo Sei, which quarrels persistently with the Bureau of Allocation over what should be distributed and what reclaimed
