## Ritual: Yozhwo

Rite
: A petition made directly to the Custodian of Lost Seals, who deals with mortals only rarely and only with those who understand what a stolen or forged celestial seal can accomplish: a noble who wants a reclassification order made, a scholar who wants a destroyed record recovered, a cult operative who needs documents that survive celestial audit. In return the petitioner receives access to the [Infernal Archive]{.name data-type="place/site"} or a single seal, fixed to a mortal-made document, that grants it temporary authority within the classified cosmos

Cost
: A document from the celestial bureaucracy, preferably one bearing an actual seal, which Yozhwo studies and copies

Performed by
: A petitioner of sufficient understanding or ambition. The [[affiliation-nuotsourui|Nwotsou Rwi]] reaches Yozhwo through a single trusted intermediary, a former records clerk who knows the protocols of celestial authentication

Licensed by
: No one. Yozhwo keeps the Infernal Archive by tacit understanding among the lesser demons, and no Demon Lord appointed it. A counterfeit seal is fragile, because celestial inspectors have ways to detect it
