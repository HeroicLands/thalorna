## Ritual: Tanzho

Rite
: There is no rite of invocation. Tanzho is the principle of classification and is not prayed to, because a prayer assumes a being able to respond. What mortals do is study: scholars, [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] and petitioners who want to understand the justice beneath a ruling petition Chou Sei, the [[affiliation-wantou|Archive of All Records]], for access to the original classifications of the [Great Sorting]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"}, and trace a single classification back through time to infer its principle. To contemplate the Great Sorting is to glimpse Tanzho's work, and not Tanzho

Cost
: For some of the most devoted scholars, a lifetime of study. The aim is to petition the bureaus correctly and to avoid being reclassified, the gravest terror in Tantsou theology, which strips ancestors of standing and descendants of privilege

Performed by
: Scholars of the celestial order, members of the Tanchei caste and petitioners seeking the grounds of a ruling

Licensed by
: No bureau of its own. The Archive of All Records grants access to the written record of the original act, and no temple is dedicated primarily to the First Classifier

See [[affiliation-tanzho|Tanzho]].
