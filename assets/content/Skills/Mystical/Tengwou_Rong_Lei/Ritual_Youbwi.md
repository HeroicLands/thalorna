## Ritual: Youbwi

Rite
: Mortals do not petition Youbwi. A clerk-priest at a temple of the Tengwou Rong Lei checks that every field is filled, that the seals of the lower bureaus are affixed and that the precedents are correctly cited, then burns the petition. At that moment, if the petition is destined for transmission between bureaus, Youbwi is held to appear as a function and not in person, and the Jade Seal appears on the document's spiritual form. A document rejected as improperly sealed may be appealed to her for re-examination, but only with an appeal that is perfectly formed

Cost
: Offerings of paper, ink and wax at the small altar kept in most temples of the Jade Sovereign, which acknowledge her role and are not supplication. An appeal requires hiring a scholar of celestial law

Performed by
: A clerk-priest at a temple, who performs the burning

Licensed by
: Youbwi herself, the Keeper of the Jade Seal, who holds no bureau and may refuse to seal a document only for violating procedure
