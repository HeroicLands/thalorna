## Ritual: Genhwen

Rite
: A petition to Hwen Sei, the Bureau of Unraveling, which conducts authorized unraveling: the collapse of a structure that has outlived its classification, the failure of a lineage that should not continue, the flood that clears ground. Someone must be responsible for things ending, and the [Great Sorting]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"} appointed Genhwen. Mortals who take the bureau for malevolent misread it, since an unauthorized dissolution is as much its problem as anyone's

Cost
: The temple's fee, since preparing a petition correctly is expertise, and the tribute that pays the petition's carriage (coin, grain, cloth or livestock), which the Receiver of Tribute enters to the petitioner's credit

Performed by
: A Clerk, the temple's ordinary officer, who drafts the petition in the correct forms. The Seal-Holder stamps it, and the Clerk of Transmission burns it at the bureau's own censer; a petition burned at the wrong censer reaches the wrong bureau and is lost. Its Hwenwo execute authorized unraveling

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-genhen|Genhwen]], the Teng Gwi of Hwen Sei. The bureau's Yolei, trickster spirits at the edge of the [Hwenlei]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"}, are technically under his authority and hard to administer
