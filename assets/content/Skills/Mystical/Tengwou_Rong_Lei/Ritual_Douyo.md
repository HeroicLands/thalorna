## Ritual: Douyo

Rite
: A small rite the handlers of the [[affiliation-nuotsourui|Nwotsou Rwi]] perform just before going to work, invoking the Guide Who Leads Astray to confuse the paths of those who follow. It serves the cult's operational discipline: leading imperial investigators down false trails, losing pursuers in bureaucratic labyrinths, and steering an interrogation into unresolvable disputes over category

Cost
: The risk of the Celestial Marshals, whom the cultus's Watcher keeps it ahead of, and the Watcher's sentence when a handler's loyalty fails

Performed by
: Cult handlers, couriers and operatives of the Nwotsou Rwi, who call what they do Douyo's work

Licensed by
: No bureau. Douyo is a corrupted waymark spirit of the Bureau of Passage, and the rite stands outside the celestial administration
