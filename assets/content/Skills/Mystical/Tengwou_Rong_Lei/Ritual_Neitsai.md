## Ritual: Neitsai

Rite
: A petition to Tsai Sei, the Bureau of Creation, which keeps things what they were classified as: a lineage that breeds true, a craft transmitted intact, a boundary of the [Great Sorting]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"} still where it was drawn. Petitions for a difficult birth, an unbroken inheritance or the survival of a craft go here. The bureau resists change by design and is the natural rival of Zhinho's Bureau of Transformation

Cost
: The temple's fee, since preparing a petition correctly is expertise, and the tribute that pays the petition's carriage (coin, grain, cloth or livestock), which the Receiver of Tribute enters to the petitioner's credit

Performed by
: A Clerk, the temple's ordinary officer, who drafts the petition in the correct forms. The Seal-Holder stamps it, and the Clerk of Transmission burns it at the bureau's own censer; a petition burned at the wrong censer reaches the wrong bureau and is lost. Its [Wouchei]{.name data-type="lore/spirit"} preserves the transmission of knowledge and technique across generations, and its Zhawo see that living things reproduce according to their classification

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-neitsai|Neitsai]], the Teng Gwi of Tsai Sei. The Chief Intendant of the bureau's temples signs what is transmitted upward
