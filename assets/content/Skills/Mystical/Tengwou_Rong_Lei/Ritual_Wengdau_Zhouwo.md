## Ritual: Wengdau Zhouwo

Rite
: A petition to the Lord of Earth, made by offerings at earth shrines, by pouring blood into the soil, by burying a spirit-document inscribed with the petition, or through the [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] priesthood and the territorial Touwo. Communities ask for fertile soil and protection against erosion, or for leave to clear new farmland. Quarry operators ask for a stone license, builders for leave to level ground or break bedrock, and explorers for authority to enter uncharted earth. Petitions that lack documentation or spiritual merit are rejected, and a petition about sinking foundations or crop failure is read as a confession of the petitioner's moral deficit

Cost
: Documentation, spiritual merit, and, for a quarry license, years of waiting while the petitioner proves administrative compliance

Performed by
: Farming communities through their Touwo, quarry operators and stone-workers, builders, miners and explorers, with the Tanchei priesthood mediating

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-wengdaozhouwo|Wengdau Zhouwo]], the Lord of Earth, who answers directly to [[affiliation-weizhuo|Weizhwo]]. A quarry license is a significant celestial authorization, and a mining petition must also satisfy [[affiliation-daotouwochai|Dautou Wochai]]
