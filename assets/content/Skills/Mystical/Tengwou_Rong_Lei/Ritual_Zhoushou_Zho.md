## Ritual: Zhoushou Zho

Rite
: A petition to the Lord of Water, made by offerings at water shrines, by pouring water-offerings and spirit-documents into rivers or wells, or through the [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] priesthood. It is often less rigid in form than a petition to the Lords of Metal or Fire, though protocol still holds, and some communities hold its outcome less certain than that of the other Elemental Lords, reflecting the Lord's fluid boundaries. Fishing communities ask for steady fish and flow and honor him at the start of spawning season. Farmers ask for water in the growing season, often alongside a petition to [[affiliation-raogui|Raugwi]]. Ship-captains pair their offerings with the Minister of Roads, and millers ask for steady flow. A community in drought or flood files an emergency petition that is often denied

Cost
: The offering at the water shrine, and the community's own moral ledger, since a drought or flood read as an appointed condition or a sign of imbalance is not relieved by petition

Performed by
: Fishermen, river-workers, farmers, sailors, merchants and millers, with the Tanchei priesthood mediating

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-zhoushouzho|Zhoushou Zho]], the Lord of Water, who answers directly to [[affiliation-weizhuo|Weizhwo]]
