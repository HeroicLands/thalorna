## Ritual: Nwoho

Rite
: Nwoho is invoked and never worshipped by those who wish to destroy something so completely that the celestial administration loses track of it: fire that annihilates instead of purifying, dissolution that erases instead of recycling. What it consumes does not return to its elemental categories but falls back into the [Hwenlei]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"}. The [[affiliation-nuotsourui|Nwotsou Rwi]] performs small rituals invoking the Unmaker before a strike, and dedicates its suicide incendiaries, archive burnings and festival atrocities to it

Cost
: Whatever the invoker is willing to see destroyed. Celestial law forbids the invocation, and the Celestial Marshals treat any evidence of it as a matter of the highest urgency

Performed by
: Operatives of the Nwotsou Rwi, the Cult of the Forsaken, before a strike

Licensed by
: No one. Nwoho arose from a jurisdictional failure between the Bureau of Returns and the Bureau of Transformation, and no bureau licenses it
