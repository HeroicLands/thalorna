## Ritual: Meiwo

Rite
: A dream-petition filed with Mei Sei, the Bureau of Night Counsel, by a Tantsou who wishes to be answered in sleep. The answer is delivered as an omen or a night-counsel report. A dream that arrives unbidden is understood as a memo from a clerk and not a message from a god

Cost
: The temple's fee, since preparing a petition correctly is expertise, and the tribute that pays the petition's carriage (coin, grain, cloth or livestock), which the Receiver of Tribute enters to the petitioner's credit

Performed by
: A Clerk, the temple's ordinary officer, who drafts the petition in the correct forms. The Seal-Holder stamps it, and the Clerk of Transmission burns it at the bureau's own censer; a petition burned at the wrong censer reaches the wrong bureau and is lost. The bureau's Lengwo deliver auspicious dreams and its Hayo deliver warnings; a nightmare is simply an urgent memo

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-meiwo|Meiwo]], the Teng Gwi of Mei Sei. The Chief Intendant of the bureau's temples signs what is transmitted upward
