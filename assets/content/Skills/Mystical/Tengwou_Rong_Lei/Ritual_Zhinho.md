## Ritual: Zhinho

Rite
: The burning that files every petition. Ho Sei, the Bureau of Transformation, administers the postal system of the cosmos, because fire is the medium by which a document passes from the mortal plane to the celestial one. Burning a correctly addressed petition files it, burning spirit money credits an account, and burning offerings for the dead provisions them. The bureau also seats the Tsaiken in every household, and families make offerings to the Hearth Monitor to ensure favorable annual reports

Cost
: The temple's fee, since preparing a petition correctly is expertise, and the tribute that pays the petition's carriage (coin, grain, cloth or livestock), which the Receiver of Tribute enters to the petitioner's credit

Performed by
: A Clerk, the temple's ordinary officer, who drafts the petition in the correct forms. The Seal-Holder stamps it, and the Clerk of Transmission burns it at the bureau's own censer; a petition burned at the wrong censer reaches the wrong bureau and is lost. The Dauho Wo, wildfire spirits, are the bureau's enforcement arm for punitive fire

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-zhenho|Zhinho]], the Teng Gwi of Ho Sei. The other overseers resent depending on him to deliver their mail, and no bureau has successfully petitioned to change the arrangement
