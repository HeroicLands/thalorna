## Ritual: Wojing Ja

Rite
: A petition to the Lord of Fire, made by formal offerings at a temple furnace, by burning a spirit-document stamped with his seal, or through the [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] priesthood. The petitioner acknowledges that the Lord is a fundamental force and not a bureau. A master smith asks that the fire stay stable, hot enough and responsive to technique, and in most craft guilds the first coal in a new forge is lit with offerings and petitions. An alchemist asks him to certify a transformation as orderly reclassification. A household whose hearth will not kindle makes a special offering and may file for reclassification of its spiritual status. A mortuary worker asks that the fire burn with dignity at a cremation

Cost
: The offerings at the furnace, and for a household in spiritual extremity the cleansing and ritual maintenance of the hearth

Performed by
: Smiths and metalworkers, alchemists, households and mortuary workers, with the Tanchei priesthood mediating. The local Tsaiken is consulted when a hearth refuses to kindle

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-wojingja|Wojing Ja]], the Lord of Fire, who answers directly to [[affiliation-weizhuo|Weizhwo]] and not to a bureau
