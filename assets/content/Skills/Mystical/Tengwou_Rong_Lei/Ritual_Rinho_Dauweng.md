## Ritual: Rinho Dauweng

Rite
: A petition to the Lord of Wood, made by offerings at a temple shrine, by burning a spirit-document stamped with his seal, or through the [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] priesthood. It allows more variation in form and language than a petition to the Lord of Metal, though the essential protocol holds. A master logger petitions before working a new forest region, a carpenter for permission to work wood from a new source or in an unprecedented form, a shipbuilder for the wood's integrity through the building, and a farmer for the wooden tools, gates and stores of the farm. Temples file an annual petition to keep their wooden furnishings approved

Cost
: The offering at the shrine and the fees of the temple that prepares the petition

Performed by
: Foresters, loggers, timber merchants, carpenters, shipbuilders and farmers, with the Tanchei priesthood mediating. Logging communities keep shrines to the Lord of Wood and mark the turn from growing season to harvest

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-rinhodaoweng|Rinho Dauweng]], the Lord of Wood, who answers directly to [[affiliation-weizhuo|Weizhwo]]. A shipbuilder also petitions [[affiliation-wodou|Wodou]], because a ship is both wood and vehicle and the two offices must agree on its classification
