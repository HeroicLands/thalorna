## Ritual: Dautou Wochai

Rite
: A formal petition to the Lord of Metal, made by offerings at a temple shrine, by burning a spirit-document, or through the [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] priesthood. The Lord of Metal accepts no improvised or emotional prayer: the spacing, the seal placement and the wording must follow protocol. A smith asks that the iron, once registered as Wochai-grade base metal, keep its purity in the forging and answer to temperature and technique; a prospector asks leave to classify ore as extracted metal; a trader asks that precious metal stay unmolested and unadulterated on the road; a temple official registers a new seal or retires an old one

Cost
: The offering made at the shrine, and the care of a petition drawn to the letter

Performed by
: Blacksmiths and metalworkers above all, then miners, traders in precious metals and the temple officials who keep seals. The Tanchei priesthood mediates

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-daotouwochai|Dautou Wochai]], the Lord of Metal, who answers directly to [[affiliation-weizhuo|Weizhwo]] and not to a bureau. A mining petition is first filed with the territorial Touwo and is in practice forwarded to his office for final approval
