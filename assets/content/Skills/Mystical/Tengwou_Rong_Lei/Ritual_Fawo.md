## Ritual: Fawo

Rite
: A petition to Falou Sei, the Bureau of Allocation, which holds the schedules by which harvest quality and commercial fortune are distributed. A merchant house's good years are its allocation rather than its luck. Farming communities petition for the quality of the harvest and traders for the flow of commerce. The bureau is in standing conflict with Nwowo's Bureau of Returns over whether a resource should be distributed or reclaimed, and the two file counter-petitions against each other as routine

Cost
: The temple's fee, since preparing a petition correctly is expertise, and the tribute that pays the petition's carriage (coin, grain, cloth or livestock), which the Receiver of Tribute enters to the petitioner's credit. Merchants who understand the conflict pay to have a filing timed for when Falou Sei is winning

Performed by
: A Clerk, the temple's ordinary officer, who drafts the petition in the correct forms. The Seal-Holder stamps it, and the Clerk of Transmission burns it at the bureau's own censer; a petition burned at the wrong censer reaches the wrong bureau and is lost. Its Nouwo distribute harvest quality to farming communities, and its Falei influence the flow of trade

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-fawo|Fawo]], the Teng Gwi of Falou Sei. The Chief Intendant of the bureau's temples signs what is transmitted upward
