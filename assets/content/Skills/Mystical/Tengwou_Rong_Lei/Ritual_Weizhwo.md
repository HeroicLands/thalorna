## Ritual: Weizhwo

Rite
: The celestial notarization of a record. A petition that fits no substantive bureau, a request to reclassify a boundary, or an appeal alleging a procedural error goes to the Supreme Administrative Court; so does a contract or oath that must outlast its makers. A merchant who wants a contract honored after her death takes it to a temple of the Jade Sovereign, has it witnessed and sealed, and has it burned in the ritual offering, after which the record stands in Weizhwo's registry for every spirit to consult

Cost
: The appropriate fees, and patience, because the office prioritizes consistency and not urgency

Performed by
: An official witness at a temple of the Jade Sovereign. The faithful who come study precedent and have documents sealed; they do not pray for blessings

Licensed by
: [[affiliation-weizhuo|Weizhwo Daunei]], the Jade Sovereign, through [Tengwou Dau]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, which settles jurisdiction between the bureaus by precedent
