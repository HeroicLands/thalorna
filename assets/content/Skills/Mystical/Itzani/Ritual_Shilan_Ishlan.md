## Ritual: Shilan Ish'lan

Working
: The [Ish'lan Tsoqol]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, the traveler's blessing, spoken over a caravan or a runner at the outset. A party anointed with it keeps the road: it finds the water it was told of, makes its stages on time and does not lose the way. Every road shrine keeper also speaks the day at dawn, and on that speaking rests the Reckoners' claim that the white roads carry the count

Position
: The day of departure. A family planning a journey pays to find it a day opened to the road, and the blessing is given on it. The blessing reaches as far as the shrines that keep the count, and no further

Cost
: Light and often: a thumb's blood at the shrine stone on leaving, a few drops at a crossroads, something of the journey left on returning. The branch holds that a traveler gives his god the miles

Performed by
: The Master of the Blessing for a caravan; any Road Shrine Keeper for a single traveler and for the dawn speaking

The blessing always includes the state of the road, and a blessing that leaves out the bandits and the washed-out stage is held to be incomplete. It is one of the ten workings of [[lore-chumtsena|Ch'um Ts'ena]].
