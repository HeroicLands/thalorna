## Ritual: Ts'uqil Ish'bal

Working
: [The Recitation of the Trials]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, spoken over the newly dead through the night after death, naming each chamber of [[place-chaltzumaq|Ch'al Ts'umaq]] in the order the soul will meet it and each deception in the order it will be offered. A soul recited for correctly passes through. A misspoken line, or a recitation in the wrong form for the night, delays it or strands it among the ts'uqilob. At each turning of the moon the [Taq'tsuqalil]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, the Calling of the Dead, speaks the names of everyone lost since the last turning at the sacred pools

Position
: Death keeps no calendar, so the funerary codices hold a form of the recitation for each night of the Ts'uqal count, and the death-walkers learn all thirty. The night of the moon on which a person dies decides which is said. The Calling falls at the dark of the moon, when the count turns

Cost
: Little blood. The Ish'bal'ob give their own at the third landing of the descent. The family pays a fee, and the fee buys the length and accuracy of the recitation, not the god's favor: the priests sell memory, not mercy

Performed by
: The Speaker of the Trials and the Ish'bal'ob; a road shrine keeper says the short form over a traveler who dies on the road

This is the working the whole city relies on and the one it most often pays for short. A shortened recitation is a real working, correctly spoken, that names fewer of the chambers, and the family that buys it knows what it has bought. It is one of the ten workings of [[lore-chumtsena|Ch'um Ts'ena]].
