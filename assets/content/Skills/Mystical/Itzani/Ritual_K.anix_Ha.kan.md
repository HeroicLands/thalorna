## Ritual: K'anish Ha'kan

Working
: The blessing of the seed at the [Chuqtik]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}. Seed inspected by the Ul'omal, blessed, and sown on a day opened to the Giver comes up true to its line and suited to its soil. The working has a second form, the speaking over a failed field, by which a crop that did not ripen is cut, burned and given back as an offering, so that the field's debt is paid

Position
: The seed month of the Ts'en count, Shikal, on the days the temple has opened for sowing; the harvest rites fall in K'anishal, the golden flower

Cost
: Light: a few drops on the seed at planting and on the first ear at harvest. The god prefers the labor to the blood, and the farmer's work in the field is counted as part of the price

Performed by
: The Ul'omal for maize and the Ts'ultsik for cacao, under the Master of the Chuqtik

A farmer who comes to the Chuqtik with poor seed leaves with good seed from the temple's stores and a debt, so the working that blesses his field also binds him to the branch for a season. It is one of the ten workings of [[lore-chumtsena|Ch'um Ts'ena]].
