## Ritual: Ish'ilan Ch'alish

Working
: [The Reckoning]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, the computation of where the planets of the Tq'an count meet and part, from which the Reckoner of Conjunctions issues the opened and closed days to every other branch. At a smaller scale it is the prediction an astronomer derives from the record and stakes his name on: an eclipse, a conjunction, the hour of a solstice, worked so that it holds

Position
: The instant of a conjunction, read against the Ish'ilan count of the fixed stars and the Tq'an count of the planets. The great festivals that mark the ages fall in the stellar count, and the observatory's night watches are kept whether or not anything is expected

Cost
: The smallest and most exact in the tradition: a few drops of the astronomer's own blood, let at the instant of the conjunction and offered on the page rather than at an altar

Performed by
: The Reckoner of Conjunctions for the city's days; any Ch'alishbal of the observatory for a prediction, examined in the open before the assembled priests

Every other working depends on this one, and it is the only one whose arithmetic is never shown. The Reckoners publish conclusions and never their workings, and they hold that they read the days the goddess opened at the creation rather than open them themselves. It is one of the ten workings of [[lore-chumtsena|Ch'um Ts'ena]].
