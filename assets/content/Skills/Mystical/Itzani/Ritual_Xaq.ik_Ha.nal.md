## Ritual: Shaq'ik Ha'nal

Working
: The [Ts'uqal Ne']{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, the Moon Water, in which every well, spring, reservoir and sacred pool of a city is visited and blessed in a single night, so that the water stays clean through the year. Its lesser form is healing: blessed water poured before treatment, with the remedies of the physic gardens, takes a sickness the remedies alone would not

Position
: The Moon Water falls on the first full moon of the rains, in the Ts'uqal count. Healing is worked on days the Reckoner opens to the water. The water-marking of a newborn keeps no day at all and is given the hour the child is born

Cost
: Little blood and a great deal of water: clean water carried some distance and poured, with flowers, incense and jade

Performed by
: The Master of the Moon Water and her procession; the Ch'alishbal Pul'ik for healing; the Speaker of the First Water at a birth

The branch holds that a birth opens its own day. The Reckoners hold that the water-marking is a rite that works nothing and so needs no day. The water is given either way. It is one of the ten workings of [[lore-chumtsena|Ch'um Ts'ena]].
