## Ritual: Trimūrti-sampradāya

- **Rite:** The Triple Blessing – the formulas of shaping, preserving and transforming spoken in sequence by the three Ácāryas or their deputies. It is the sect's one civic instrument, performed at every groundbreaking and seasonal transition, at a coronation, at the sighting of the year at the Sūrya temple, and over the mountain's gold at a Weighing when a court asks the orthodox hierarchy rather than the Gaṇaka-śālā to set the date
- **Cost:** Nothing from the petitioner beyond standing to witness; from the Ácāryas, the coordination of three separate offices – [[affiliation-vyalendra|Vyālendra]]'s, [[affiliation-mahajaya|Mahájaya]]'s, [[affiliation-rasikara|Rásikara]]'s – none of whom the sect will let a civic occasion honor alone
- **Performed by:** The three Ácāryas of a temple, one per form, or a single Triyācārya ("Master of the Three") ordained to serve all three at once – a rank few priests attain, most specializing in one form and serving under the Triyācārya's direction
- **Licensed by:** The [[affiliation-trimurtisampradaya|Trimūrti-sampradāya]] alone, the largest and most orthodox of the Varṇakan sects, whose calendar sets the public year and whose Council of the Triyācāryas – the senior Triyācārya of every great Vedyari city – settles any dispute the rite raises

The path to the office runs through **the Triple Year**, three successive years of service kept in each form's chamber to the full liturgical calendar, or **the Architect's Ordeal**, in which the aspirant designs and helps build a small three-chambered shrine, unassisted and unpaid, where none stood before.
