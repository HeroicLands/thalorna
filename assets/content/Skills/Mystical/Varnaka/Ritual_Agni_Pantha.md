## Ritual: Agnī-panthā

- **Rite:** The Purgation – a fast, a formal confession before the priest's own carried ember-vessel, and the burning of an object representing what is to be released. Performed for an individual, a household, or a whole community, and lasting hours or days depending on the subject
- **Cost:** To the petitioner, the fast and the confession; to the priest, the readiness to be the uncomfortable guest a comfortable city would rather not have called
- **Performed by:** A Jvālita ("Kindled One"), the sect's ordinary itinerant priesthood, each carrying an ember-vessel of their own; the rare extraordinary form, the Scouring of the Temple, is undertaken by a company of Jvālita under a Dahana-Mūla's direction against a community judged corrupt
- **Licensed by:** The [[affiliation-agnipantha|Agnī-panthā]] alone. Ordination to Jvālita requires the Firebrand's Year already completed, and the Walk of Coals is performed in this sect's form with coals drawn from the aspirant's own vessel rather than a temple's consecrated fire

**The Walk of Coals**, **the Firebrand's Year**, and **the Vigil at the Threshold** – a three-day fast held at the door of a community that has refused the priest, seated in silent witness until the refusal is reconsidered or the vigil's period elapses – are this sect's own path to ordination, shared in substance with the wider Rásikaran devotion but sat for as a condition of the priesthood rather than as a private undertaking.
