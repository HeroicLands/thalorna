## Ritual: Agmi-pantra

Rite
: [The Purgation]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}. A fast, a formal confession before the priest's own carried ember-vessel, and the burning of an object that stands for what is to be released. It is performed for an individual, a household or a whole community, and runs from hours to days with the subject

Cost
: To the petitioner, the fast and the confession. To the priest, the readiness to be the uncomfortable guest a comfortable city would rather not have called

Performed by
: A Jvelita ("Kindled One"), one of the sect's ordinary itinerant priests, each carrying an ember-vessel of their own. The rare extraordinary form, the [Scouring of the Temple]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, is undertaken by a company of Jvelita under a Dahona-Mulra's direction against a community judged corrupt

Licensed by
: The [[affiliation-agnipantha|Agmi-pantra]] alone. Ordination to Jvelita requires the [Firebrand's Year]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} already completed. The [Walk of Coals]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} is performed in this sect's form, with coals drawn from the aspirant's own vessel and not from a temple's consecrated fire

**The Walk of Coals**, **the Firebrand's Year** and **the [Vigil at the Threshold]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** are this sect's path to ordination. The Vigil is a three-day fast held at the door of a community that has refused the priest, seated in silent witness until the refusal is reconsidered or the period elapses. The three are in substance part of the wider devotion to [[affiliation-rasikara|Rasikara]], serving here as a condition of the priesthood.

See [[affiliation-agnipantha|Agmi-pantra]].
