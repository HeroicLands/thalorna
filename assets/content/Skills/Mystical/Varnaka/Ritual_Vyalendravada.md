## Ritual: Vyalendravada

Rite
: [The Tracing of the Pattern]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}. The temple's central diagram is refreshed in chalk on the sanctum floor each day and the priest walks its lines, naming each aspect of [[lore-vyalendradty|Vyalendra]] in turn as he crosses it. When a temple or a major civic work is finished, the same reading is made at the larger scale as [the Consecration of the Pattern]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, setting the finished proportions against the canonical diagram

Cost
: A morning's chalk and an unhurried walk for the daily rite. At a Consecration the finished work stands or falls by the reading, and a building whose proportions do not hold the pattern is not consecrated

Performed by
: An Achorya, a scholar-priest trained in architecture, mathematics and theological argument as well as liturgy; the Parema-Achorya ("Supreme Master") presides over the [Great Dialectic]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} and any reading of consequence

Licensed by
: The [[affiliation-vyalendravada|Vyalendravada]] alone, the monistic sect whose doctrine holds that [[lore-vyalendradty|Vyalendra]] is the single deity from whom all others emanate. In that doctrine, to read his pattern correctly is to read the whole of the divine order at once

Ordination to Parema-Achorya follows one of two ordeals. In **[the Geometer's Ordeal]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** the aspirant designs a small temple from first principles, holding every proportion to the canonical diagram, and defends the design in public before a council of Acharyas. **[The Silent Reading]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** is a year of philosophical study in the temple library, without speech and without the company of any living soul but the librarian.
