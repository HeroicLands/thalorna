## Ritual: Jñānasūra

- **Rite:** One of Jñānasūra's Ordeals for Favour — a trial of the mind rather than the body, undertaken alone and without preparation from any school
- **Cost:** Time and isolation rather than coin: seven days under open examination, a year of silent reading, or a full day answering whatever the community asks
- **Performed by:** Any Upāsaka keeping the household observance; the standing of Vratin, once earned, is held for life
- **Licensed by:** No one. Jñānasūra keeps no priesthood of his own — Varṇaka ordains to a school, not to a god — and the Ordeal answers to no sampradāya

His three Ordeals test, at a harder pitch, the same clarity the household observance asks for daily. **The Examination Beyond the Veil** puts the aspirant before a council drawn from every scholarly specialisation, over seven days, on subjects never assigned in advance. **The Silent Year of Reading** is spent in a temple library, speaking to no one and living on what the library provides. **The Contest of Open Questions** is public: the aspirant answers whatever any member of the community asks, from dawn to sunset, refusing none of it.
