## Ritual: Mahájaya

- **Rite:** One of Mahájaya's Ordeals for Favor, an extended public trust rather than a single act – kept where the community can see it fail or hold
- **Cost:** A sleepless watch over the communal granary through a single night, or a full year of public measurement – grain, cloth, wine, oil – with no complaint of short weight upheld against the devotee
- **Performed by:** Any Upāsaka keeping the household's scale and sheaf; the standing of Vratin, once earned, is held for life
- **Licensed by:** No one, formally – but the rite's honesty is answerable to whoever is watching, which at Suvarṇagiri is the sabhā and any Suvarṇagiri who cares to attend the monthly Weighing of the mountain's gold, the same public-accountability principle carried to its civic extreme

**The Granary Vigil** is a sleepless watch kept over the community granary through a night when the spirits of the fields are said to walk. **The Fair Measure** asks a full year of public measurement – grain, cloth, wine, oil – completed without one sustained complaint of short weight from any party. Both ask the same thing the household scale asks daily, held to a harder and longer standard: that nothing is gained by weighing wrong.
