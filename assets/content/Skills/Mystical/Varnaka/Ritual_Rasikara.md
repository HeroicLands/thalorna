## Ritual: Rasikara

Rite
: One of [[lore-rasikaradty|Rasikara]]'s Ordeals for Favor, each a controlled encounter with the fire the household hearth-flame is kept to avoid

Cost
: A barefoot walk across coals drawn from a consecrated fire; a three-day fast beside a cremation's cooling ashes; or a year of itinerant service carrying a lit torch from settlement to settlement, kindling purgation-fires wherever asked

Performed by
: Any Upasoka; the standing of Vretin, once earned, is held for life. Within the [[affiliation-agnipantha|Agmi-pantra]], the [Firebrand's Year]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} is required before ordination to Jvelita, and the [Walk of Coals]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} is performed with coals drawn from the aspirant's own carried ember-vessel

Licensed by
: No priesthood of Rasikara's own. The [[affiliation-agnipantha|Agmi-pantra]], the ascetic reformist sect built around his purging fire, licenses its own working priesthood to perform these same Ordeals, and requires them for ordination

**The Walk of Coals** goes barefoot across coals drawn from a consecrated fire. An inch of uninjured skin is a sign of favor; a burn is a sign of work yet to be done. **[The Vigil in Ashes]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** is a three-day fast beside a cremation's cooling ashes, meditating on the change the fire performed. **The Firebrand's Year** is a year of itinerant service, carrying a lit torch from settlement to settlement and kindling purgation-fires wherever asked.

See [[affiliation-rasikara|Rasikara]].
