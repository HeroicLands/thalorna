## Ritual: Rásikara

- **Rite:** One of Rásikara's Ordeals for Favour, each a controlled encounter with the fire the household hearth-flame is kept to avoid
- **Cost:** A barefoot walk across coals drawn from a consecrated fire; a three-day fast beside a cremation's cooling ashes; or a year of itinerant service carrying a lit torch from settlement to settlement, kindling purgation-fires wherever asked
- **Performed by:** Any Upāsaka; the standing of Vratin, once earned, is held for life. Within the [[affiliation-agnipantha|Agnī-panthā]], the Firebrand's Year is required before ordination to Jvālita, and the Walk of Coals is performed with coals drawn from the aspirant's own carried ember-vessel
- **Licensed by:** No priesthood of Rásikara's own, though the [[affiliation-agnipantha|Agnī-panthā]] — the ascetic, reformist sect built entirely around his purging fire — licenses its own working priesthood to perform these same Ordeals as a condition of ordination rather than a devotion undertaken once

**The Walk of Coals** — an inch of uninjured skin is a sign of favour; a burn is a sign of work yet to be done. **The Vigil in Ashes** is a three-day fast beside a cremation's cooling ashes, meditating on the transformation the fire performed. **The Firebrand's Year** is a year of itinerant service, carrying a lit torch from settlement to settlement and kindling purgation-fires wherever asked.
