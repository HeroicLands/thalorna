## Ritual: Meghanātha

- **Rite:** One of Meghanātha's Ordeals for Favor, met at the mercy of actual weather rather than performed on a schedule of the devotee's choosing
- **Cost:** Standing atop a high place through the passage of a named storm, sounding a hand-drum through the worst of it; a voyage in the monsoon's first days aboard a boat the devotee helped build; or a year speaking only while the temple drum sounds
- **Performed by:** Any Upāsaka; the standing of Vratin, once earned, is held for life – the god's gifts arrive with violence and so, by design, does the trial that earns his favor
- **Licensed by:** No one. Meghanātha keeps no priesthood of his own, and his clergy's forthrightness is famous for not softening that fact

**The Storm Watch** is kept atop a high place during the passage of a named storm, a hand-drum sounded through the worst of the rain and wind. **The Fisher's Voyage** is undertaken in the monsoon's first days, on a fishing boat the devotee has helped to build. **The Silent Year of Thunder** permits speech only when the temple drum is sounding – silence bought back, one storm at a time, across a full year.
