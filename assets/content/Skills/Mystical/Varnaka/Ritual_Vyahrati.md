## Ritual: Vyāhrati

- **Rite:** One of Vyāhrati's Ordeals for Favor—an extended act of receiving an ending rather than causing one
- **Cost:** A year at a temple garden, receiving whatever is brought for composting and returning it to the ground with the proper rite; or three days of silent witness inside a house emptied by death or dissolution before it passes to new keeping
- **Performed by:** Any Upāsaka who keeps the petal bowl; the standing of Vratin, once earned, is held for life
- **Licensed by:** No one. Vyāhrati keeps a thin formal priesthood and her shrines are modest alcoves beside louder gods' temples—the smallness of her institution is itself part of what her devotees hold sacred

**The Year of Gentle Release** takes in, at a temple garden, whatever is brought for composting—kitchen waste, household refuse, the petals of weddings and funerals alike—and returns each to the ground with the proper rite. **The Walk of the Empty House** is three days of silent witness spent inside a house emptied by death or dissolution, before the property passes to its new keeping.
