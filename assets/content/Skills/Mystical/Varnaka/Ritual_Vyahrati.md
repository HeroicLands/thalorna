## Ritual: Vyāhrati

- **Rite:** One of Vyāhrati's Ordeals for Favour. It is a long act of receiving an ending, and not of causing one
- **Cost:** A year at a temple garden, receiving whatever is brought for composting and returning it to the ground with the proper rite; or three days of silent witness inside a house emptied by death or dissolution before it passes to new keeping
- **Performed by:** Any Upāsaka who keeps the petal bowl; the standing of Vratin, once earned, is held for life
- **Licensed by:** No one. Vyāhrati keeps a thin formal priesthood, and her shrines are modest alcoves beside the temples of louder gods. Her devotees hold that smallness sacred

**The Year of Gentle Release** takes in at a temple garden whatever is brought for composting, kitchen waste, household refuse, and the petals of weddings and funerals alike, and returns each to the ground with the proper rite. **The Walk of the Empty House** is three days of silent witness inside a house emptied by death or dissolution, before the property passes to its new keeping.
