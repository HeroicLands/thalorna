## Ritual: Pavanajitras

- **Rite:** One of the Pavanajitras' Ordeals for Favor, kept on the road itself and at no shrine
- **Cost:** A year of continuous travel visiting every shrine of the Wandering Wind within a named region, keeping a record of what was left at each; or a full season accompanying a merchant caravan, speaking only the ceremonial words and serving as directed
- **Performed by:** Any Upāsaka who has tied the traveler's cord; the standing of Vratin, once earned, is held for life
- **Licensed by:** No one. The Pavanajitras keep no priesthood. They are honored by a gesture at every crossroads and every waterway

**The Pilgrim's Year** sends the devotee traveling continuously for a year, visiting every shrine of the Wandering Wind within a named region and keeping a record of the offerings left at each. **The Silent Caravan** has the devotee accompany a merchant caravan through its full season, speaking only the ceremonial words of the rites and serving the company as directed. The knotted pledges of the traveler's cord are lived out one shrine at a time.
