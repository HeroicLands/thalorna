## Ritual: P'iqal Kul'qat

Working
: Two, at a work's beginning and its end. The [Kul'qat Tik'il]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, the foundation-laying, opens the ground, sets the lines and judges whether the ground will carry what is proposed. A work founded by it stands under loads it should not carry. The [Kul'qat Ts'apel]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, the dedication, declares a work finished, and no work is finished until it is spoken

Position
: A day the Reckoner opens to the Builder, with the lines set from the astronomers' alignments, so that the stellar count and the Tq'an count both stand in the first course

Cost
: Jade and blood go into the first course, and blood again at the dedication; between the two the goddess takes work rather than blood, and the branch's festivals are working days on which the city's craftspeople repair the temples for nothing

Performed by
: The Master of the Foundation and the Master of the Dedication; the Master of the Cord sets the lines and answers for a structure out of true

The thick-walled binding temples of P'uqil Ch'akun are founded by this working to specifications the serpent-keepers supply, and the builder-priests who speak it do not ask what the walls are meant to survive. It is one of the ten workings of [[lore-chumtsena|Ch'um Ts'ena]].
