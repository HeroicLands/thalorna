## Ritual: Svapnasari-sameja

Rite
: The dream-reading. A petitioner sleeps at the monastery and a Dorshaka records what is dreamed. For a great question the Svopna-Achorya convenes a [Great Vigil]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, three nights in which several sleepers' dreams are pooled and read as one body before a formal statement is issued

Cost
: To the petitioner, three nights' sleep under a Nidrapala's watch and the fee the monastery names, or none at all from a Dorshaka undertaking the [Mirror Year]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}; to the reader, the years of training a true oneiromancer's reputation is built on

Performed by
: A Dorshaka ("Seer"), the sect's working priesthood, trained in dream-reading, meditation and the careful recording of what petitioners bring; a Svopna-Achorya presides over the Great Vigil in person

Licensed by
: The [[affiliation-svapnasarisameja|Svapnasari-sameja]] alone. The orthodox hierarchy does not contest the sect's competence here. The [[lore-svapnadevasdty|Svapnadevas]], the Dreaming Host this rite serves, keep no priesthood of their own, and this is the whole of their formal devotion made a vocation

[The Seven-Night Vigil]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} and **the Mirror Year** are the path into the priesthood proper. The Vigil is seven consecutive nights of light sleep in a monastery chamber, reported each morning, and is rarely completed without a Dorshaka's intervention. The Mirror Year is a year spent reading the silver mirror for every petitioner who comes, refusing no reading and accepting no fee.
