## Ritual: Meghonata

Rite
: One of [[lore-meghonatadty|Meghonata]]'s Ordeals for Favor. The weather sets the hour, and the devotee does not

Cost
: Standing atop a high place through the passage of a named storm, sounding a hand-drum through the worst of it; a voyage in the monsoon's first days aboard a boat the devotee helped build; or a year speaking only while the temple drum sounds

Performed by
: Any Upasoka. The standing of Vretin, once earned, is held for life. His gifts arrive with violence, and so does the trial that earns his favor

Licensed by
: No one. [[lore-meghonatadty|Meghonata]] keeps no priesthood of his own, and his clergy's forthrightness is famous for not softening that fact

[The Storm Watch]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} is kept atop a high place during the passage of a named storm, a hand-drum sounded through the worst of the rain and wind. [The Fisher's Voyage]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} is undertaken in the monsoon's first days, on a fishing boat the devotee has helped to build. [The Silent Year of Thunder]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} permits speech only while the temple drum is sounding. Silence is bought back one storm at a time, across a full year.

See [[affiliation-meghonata|Meghonata]].
