### Summary of Provenzia

#### Geography

In Summary of Provenzia, the [[place-provenzrgn|Provenzia Region]] is a region of diverse landscapes and climates, ranging from temperate plains to rugged mountains rich in minerals. Key geographical features include the Áelendan ranges, known for their deposits of precious metals and gemstones, and the fertile river valleys that are essential for agriculture and trade. The region also contains coastal settlements and riverine trade hubs that connect it to neighboring realms.

The kingdom’s strategic position as a crossroads of trade and its mineral wealth make it both an economic hub and a target for external aggression. The Áelendan ranges host hidden mining communities, while urban centers like [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] serve as the royal and commercial capital and [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]] as the seat of law and learning.

#### Cultural and Political Dynamics

Provenzia's cultural landscape is marked by tension between the **Tanevar**, the old people of the Provenzal country, who emphasize traditional local governance and remember their petty kings, and the **Provenzians**, the city-dwelling heirs of the old Vylarian province. These groups clash over land, governance, and cultural identity. A centralized monarchy governs from the cities, but local traditions persist in rural areas, fueling resentment and periodic rebellions.

The kingdom's religious tolerance is another area of cultural complexity. While the Church of [[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]] is officially favored and politically powerful, other faiths, including the traditions of the [[affiliation-brazemas|Faith of Brazemas]] and [[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]], coexist uneasily. This pluralism stems from policies established by the first king, [Ylarven Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, to unify the estates that chose him. However, the growing dominance of the Forge faithful has caused friction, particularly in regions where traditional or minority religions hold sway.

#### Historical Primer

Provenzia’s history is marked by cycles of conquest, rebellion, and consolidation. A Vylarian colony was planted at Aurelane under the [Vylarian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} ([[lore-aurelianefound|The Founding of Aurelane]]), and the legions made the country a province by breaking the Tanevar kings at [[lore-tiravlenbattle|Tiravlen]] in about 152 AF. The kingdom’s foundation lies in the province’s refusal of its _therkos_ (governor), a little over a century before the present year of 720 AF, after a decade of Nordland raids (519–530 AF) had shown how little the empire’s _therkoi_ could defend. The war was fought for a [Charter of Rights]{.name data-type="lore/law"} and not for a crown, and the estates chose a king to hold the charter ([[lore-charterwar|The War of the Charter]]). The first kings pursued pragmatic policies of religious tolerance and power-sharing between the great houses and local leaders, which created a fragile unity, repeatedly tested over the centuries.

One of the most significant events in Provenzia’s history since then was **[[lore-warprinces|The War of the Princes]] (653–655 AF)**, also known as the **Kéravian Rebellion**. This civil war erupted over succession disputes following the death of King [Padran Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who left no clear heir. Competing claims from rival factions of the royal family and their noble allies plunged the kingdom into chaos. The Provenzian factions, which emphasized centralized rule and military strength, clashed with Tanevar factions advocating for a return to regional autonomy.

The war devastated large swathes of the kingdom. Regional economies collapsed, and [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] became the battleground for opposing forces. The conflict ended with the accession of King [Dalenar Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a compromise candidate supported by both moderate Provenzian and Tanevar factions. However, the scars of the war linger, with noble families still nursing grudges and local governance weakened in many regions.

The _War of the Princes_ also highlighted the deep cultural divide between the Tanevar and Provenzian populations. The Tanevar resistance to Provenzian dominance crystallized during the Kéravian Rebellion, as traditional leaders leveraged popular discontent to rally their followers. This divide remains a source of tension in modern Provenzia, particularly in rural regions where memories of the war are still vivid.

Provenzia’s subsequent history has been shaped by efforts to recover from the war’s devastation. While successive monarchs have worked to strengthen royal authority, the balance of power between the crown, nobles, and local leaders remains precarious. This legacy of division continues to influence both internal politics and the kingdom’s capacity to respond to external threats.

#### Major Current Conflicts

Provenzia faces a multi-layered crisis of internal and external conflicts, each feeding into the other and threatening the kingdom’s fragile stability. Internally, the noble families vie for influence, with long-standing rivalries between magnates like the Earl of Degavel and the Count of Atavel creating political gridlock. Local unrest in the towns, fueled by factional disputes and economic instability, has further eroded the monarchy's control. The cultural tension between the Tanevar and Provenzian populations continues to simmer, with rural Thanevari resisting central authority and perceived Provenzian dominance, occasionally erupting into open rebellion. Religious divides compound these issues, as the Church of the Sacred Forge’s growing power alienates other faiths, particularly in regions with strong Black Flame or Táranite traditions.

Externally, Provenzia remains under constant threat from Tarvénia, whose rulers exploit Provenzia’s internal weaknesses to press territorial claims. Border skirmishes are frequent, particularly in contested regions like the southeast, where Tarvénia forces test Provenzia’s defenses. The kingdom also faces the resurgence of banditry and piracy, disrupting trade and straining regional lords’ ability to maintain order. These external pressures exacerbate internal divisions, as magnates and military leaders, distracted by personal rivalries, struggle to present a united front against external aggression. The interplay of these conflicts creates a precarious balance that threatens to tip the kingdom into chaos.

#### Keravel, Tarvénia, and Leravren

Keravel serves as a critical frontier region for Provenzia, encompassing several manorial estates and villages anchored by its castle town. Keravel is commanded by Jarvel al Pélanvri, who manages defenses against Tarvénia. The area is also troubled by banditry, which local lords, such as **[Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}**, work to suppress. Its position near the southeastern border makes it a frequent flashpoint for conflict, with local lords balancing the demands of defense, governance, and trade.

[[place-leravren|Leravren]] lies to the northeast of Keravel and serves as a gateway to the mountains and forests vital for resources and trade. The region is also closely associated with the Black Flame Order, particularly through the [Zuravel]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} clan. **[Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}**, lord of Leravren, is a key player in the unfolding political and religious conflicts. His connections to the Zuravel clan and the Black Flame faith deepen the complexity of both local governance and regional intrigue.

**Tarvénia** remains the greatest external threat to Provenzia, persistently challenging its southeastern borders. A larger and more unified neighbor, Tarvénia exploits Provenzia’s internal divisions and vulnerabilities. Its skirmishes along the contested territories exacerbate tensions, draining resources and testing the kingdom’s fragile unity.
