[[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] is a frontier vale of the [[place-talothe|Talote]], [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Provénzia]]'s mountain march against [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]], and this season its own roads are more dangerous than the border. The outlaws who have harried the vale for years now strike as one band, under a leader who seems to know every escort's orders. This frame runs through one raiding season, from the first caravan of the spring to the battle-night vigil in the Blood Hills, and it begins as a bandit hunt for a lord nobody in his village loves.

## The Situation and the Stakes {#the-situation}

**The Marshal.** [[being-jarvelplnvr|Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]], Marshal of the Western Army, commands the frontier from [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]] and is liege of the vale's lesser lords. He deposed the Tanevar house that held [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] on a charge of treason and gave the lordship to his kinsman. His brother the Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]] wants the vale quiet before the Council of Peers sits, and the Count of [[place-athavel|Atavel]] asks in Council why the Pélanvri cannot keep their own roads.

**Belsaren.** [[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren]] holds the lordship and has until the autumn to end the raids. He answers each one with a hanging, and each hanging sends another Tanevar family's sons to the woods. His village is divided: a Tanevar majority who remember the old lords and feed the outlaws, and a Provénzian minority who have done well under the new one.

**The outlaw.** [[being-diravekldrm|Dirave al Kaldremis]], the deposed heir, leads the outlaws from a cave below the ridge south of the village. He promises the Tanevar their old lord's justice, and he is growing desperate enough to spend them to get it.

**Ilovren.** [[being-kilanilvrl|Kilan Ilovrel na Ilovren]], lord of the neighboring lordship, offers the Marshal his men and his advice, and his advice is that Belsaren needs a new lord.

**The hills.** East of Belsaren rise [[place-bloodhills|the Blood Hills]], the blood-field of [[lore-bloodhillsbtl|the battle]] in which the Cohorts of Gashang and the Knights of the Sacred Forge destroyed each other about 248 AF. The legend puts the Black Flame's maul in a trapped cave shrine there, and the Flame's keepers at the shrine are more numerous this year than the Forge's knights can remember. Beyond the vale to the northeast, [[being-kaldinlnvl|Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren]], a lord of the Zuravel clan and an open adherent of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]], holds [[place-leravren|Leravren]] and the road to the mountains.

The stakes are who holds Belsaren when the season ends, whether its Tanevar village survives the holding, and whose hands the [[scenario-maulgashng|Maul of Gashang]] comes out of the hills in, if it comes out at all.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The frame begins as a bandit hunt and does not have to end as anything else. A party that hangs Dirave and takes Tarlas's silver has finished a story. Each later adventure opens only if the party follows what it found in the one before.

## Who Moves It {#who-moves-it}

[[being-jarvelplnvr|Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]], the Marshal of the Western Army
: Hard, practical and quick to decide. He judges a man by results and wants the vale quiet before anyone at court asks how it got that way.

[[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren]], lord of Belsaren
: Cordial in a room and cold behind its door. He hires strangers because strangers owe his village nothing.

[[being-diravekldrm|Dirave al Kaldremis]], the outlaw
: Proud, eloquent and wronged, and becoming what he hates. He does not ask where his help comes from.

[[being-kilanilvrl|Kilan Ilovrel na Ilovren]], lord of Ilovren
: Patient, soft-spoken and exact. He is the vale's most helpful lord.

[[being-darisenlrn|Darisen al Olrun]], scholar of Torvila Keravel
: Gentle, learned and generous with what he knows of the Black Flame. He asks nothing for his help.

[[being-sateretrvl|Satere Torvilis]] and [[being-revotakrms|Revota Kiramis]]
: The Tanevar innkeeper and the Provénzian reeve of Belsaren, each the voice of half the village.

[[being-nilaratlgn|Nilara Tolgen]], archer of Ilovren
: A yeoman of Kilan's household who has begun to wonder why its patrols are always late.

[[being-branwldrgr|Branval Dorgal]] and [[affiliation-slntlncmpny|the Silent Talon]]
: The mercenaries Tarlas hired before the party, quartered at Belsaren and failing. Branval's own house lost its lands in the vale, and he means to have them back.

## The Arcs

The five adventures run in order, and each opens from what the one before it uncovers.

1. **The village.** [[scenario-bndtscrvlvl|The Bandits of Keravel Vale]]: Tarlas hires the party, the village will not talk, the outlaws are baited on the Old South Road and strike back in the village square, and the village gives up the cave.
2. **The cave.** [[scenario-caveoutlin|Cave Outline]]: the assault on Dirave's cave, his last stand, and the flame emblems and the letter the party finds there.
3. **The lord of Ilovren.** [[scenario-webdeceit|The Web of Deceit]]: the letter leads to a scholar and a grove, the grove to Kilan's dealings with Leravren, and Kilan turns the Marshal against the party before it can prove anything.
4. **The hills.** [[scenario-firsbldhls|The Fires of the Blood Hills]]: the party goes into the blood-field with Kilan's map, with Kaldin's soldiers behind it and the Forge's knights at the vigil before it.
5. **The shrine.** [[scenario-maulgashng|Maul of Gashang]]: the trapped cave, its guardian, the maul, and the choice of what to do with it.

The first two can be played as one evening's adventure and its sequel, and they finish on their own. The last three need the first two behind them. A party that skips the village and goes straight for the relic meets the same people in a worse order.

## Ways In {#ways-in}

1. **For the lord.** Tarlas writes to the party promising coin and the gratitude of his house for help with a pressing matter at Belsaren.
2. **For the Marshal.** The Marshal wants the outlaws broken before the Council sits, and pays well and asks few questions about how it was done.
3. **For the company.** Branval Dorgal hires strangers to ride with a timber convoy, because someone in Belsaren tells the outlaws the escorts' orders and he does not know who.
4. **For the village.** A Tanevar elder asks the party to carry a message to the lord in the woods.
5. **For the relic.** A party drawn by the legend of the maul comes to the vale for the Blood Hills and finds it cannot reach them without crossing Belsaren.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

- The raids go on through the summer. Tarlas hangs the innkeeper in the autumn, and the Tanevar of Belsaren go over to the outlaws to a family.
- The Marshal loses patience and gives Belsaren to Kilan, who restores order in a month, because he is the one who has been disordering it.
- Kilan's household, filled with Black Flame soldiers, holds Belsaren and its road into the Blood Hills. At the battle-night vigil the Forge's knights find the shrine's traps passed and the maul gone to Leravren.
- Dirave, no longer useful, is betrayed to the Marshal by the man who fed him and hanged at Torvila Keravel. His last words name Kilan, and nobody writes them down.

## The Secret {#the-secret}

**The charge.** The treason charge against Dirave's house was fabricated. The Marshal brought it to put Belsaren's timber and ore in Pélanvri hands. A clerk who drew the accusation is alive in Belsaren and remembers what he was told to write. Tarlas does not know it.

**The hand behind the outlaws.** Kilan feeds Dirave supplies and word of the escorts through go-betweens, enough to keep the raids going and never enough to let them grow, so that the Marshal removes Tarlas and gives Belsaren to Kilan. Kilan is a secret devotee of the Black Flame. Kaldin of Leravren has lent him Black Flame soldiers who serve in his household, and three of them serve in Dirave's band as lieutenants. Kaldin has promised Kilan a high place in the faith if he brings the maul out of the hills.

**The friend.** Darisen al Olrun is a Limor of [[affiliation-selmoros|the Faith of Sélmoros]]. He has watched Kilan's go-betweens for a year, wrote the letter that offers Dirave proof of Kilan's lies, and wants the maul carried out of the shrine and laid in a Sélmoran crypt so that the dead of the Blood Hills can be given their rites. His faith will not go where the Black Flame keeps a shrine, so he needs someone else to fetch it.

**The deeds.** The burned manor of [House Dorgal]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} stands a morning's ride from Belsaren, and the family's deeds lie walled up in its cellar, where the vale says they are. Branval Dorgal wants them found.
