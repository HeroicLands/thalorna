## Overview {#overview}

The outlaws of [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] are broken, and [[place-ceravelvale|the vale]] should be quiet. The party holds a letter that says the outlaws were bait, a cipher that names [[being-kilanilvrl|Kilan Ilovrel na Ilovren]], and flame emblems taken from the bodies of Dirave's lieutenants. Kilan is the most helpful lord in the vale, an old name with the Marshal's ear, and the party has nothing a court would hear.

The party follows the letter to its writer, the scholar [[being-darisenlrn|Darisen al Olrun]] at [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]], and from him to a meeting in the grove near [[place-ilovren|Ilovren]], where Kilan confers with envoys of the Black Flame lord of Leravren. Kilan learns he is being watched and moves first, at the Marshal's audience. The adventure ends in Kilan's hall, where the party finds his plans for the shrine in the Blood Hills.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**Kilan.** Kilan is a secret devotee of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]]. He fed the outlaws to make Tarlas fail, so that the Marshal would give him Belsaren and its road into [[place-bloodhills|the Blood Hills]]. His ally [[being-kaldinlnvl|Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren]], of the branch of the Zuravel clan that keeps the Flame, has lent him Black Flame soldiers who serve in his household as his own men, and has promised him a high place in the faith if he brings the [[scenario-maulgashng|Maul of Gashang]] out of its shrine. Kilan has spent a year gathering what the old accounts say of the shrine: where it lies, what guards it, and the prayers that pass its traps.

**The envoys.** Kaldin's envoys meet Kilan at the grove near Ilovren at each new moon, away from his hall and his household's archers. At this month's meeting they bring what Kaldin's priests know of the shrine's defenses and take back what Kilan knows of its place.

**The scholar.** Darisen is a Limor of [[affiliation-selmoros|the Faith of Sélmoros]], living quietly as a copyist. He has watched Kilan's go-betweens for a year, and wrote the letter that tried to turn Dirave against him. He wants the maul out of the shrine and laid in a Sélmoran crypt, so that the dead of the Blood Hills can be given their rites. His faith will not go where the Black Flame keeps a shrine, so he needs the party to fetch it. He helps the party honestly with everything but that.

**The archer.** [[being-nilaratlgn|Nilara Tolgen]], a yeoman archer of Kilan's household, has noticed that its patrols reach every raid too late and that the new men keep to themselves. She suspects her lord and has no one inside the household to tell.

**Kilan's answer.** When Kilan learns the party has the letter, from a lieutenant who escaped the cave or from the grove, he goes to the Marshal first. He tells [[being-jarvelplnvr|Jarvel]] that the party are hirelings who have let Dirave's band escape, extorted the Tanevar and forged letters to set the vale's lords against each other. The Marshal trusts results, and Kilan has always given him results.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Nothing here requires a fight in Kilan's hall. A party that brings the Marshal proof he cannot ignore can have Kilan arrested. One that cannot, and still wants him, must take him in his own hall, against his household and the Flame's soldiers in it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The letter.** The letter from the cave names a grove, a new moon and a friend. The party has only to keep the appointment Dirave did not.
- **The informant.** A Dirave who bought his freedom has named Darisen, the copyist over the chandler's shop at Torvila Keravel.
- **The late patrol.** Nilara Tolgen asks the party to ride the Belsaren road on a night she knows the patrol will be sent late, and to see what happens.
- **The emblem.** Tarlas, uneasy, wants to know what the flame emblems mean, and sends the party to the scholar the Marshal's clerks use.

## The Opening {#opening}

Torvila Keravel on a market morning, the castle above the town and its hall full of petitioners: merchants who lost caravans, Tanevar villagers come to beg for their sons, and the lord of Ilovren's steward. Over a chandler's shop in a lane below the castle, an old man in a scholar's gown opens his door before the party knocks.

> "You've brought the letter," he says. "Come in. Mind the books."

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Scholar

**Purpose.** Give the party the Black Flame, the shrine and its first ally.

**Situation.** Darisen's two rooms, stacked with books and copying. He reads the emblem, the cipher and the journal, and he is eager to help and unwilling to take payment.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-darisenlrn|Darisen al Olrun]], gentle and learned. He tells the party the story of [[lore-bloodhillsbtl|the Battle of the Blood Hills]], the Ashlord Hazarak and his maul, and the legend of the shrine and its traps. He names the emblem as that of a Black Flame cell in Leravren.

**Player choices.** Ask who wrote the letter, ask what he wants, take his help on trust, or have him followed.

**Clues and rewards.** Darisen admits he wrote the letter, because he has watched Kilan's go-betweens and feared what Kilan wanted from Belsaren. He says the meeting in the grove happens every new moon, and the next is in four nights. He does not say what he is. A party that searches his rooms finds a Sélmoran prayer book among the copying.

**Consequences.** The party has an ally who knows more than he says, and a night to be at the grove.

**If bypassed.** Darisen comes to the party in Belsaren, with the same story.

### Scene Two: The Grove at the New Moon

**Purpose.** Catch Kilan with the Black Flame, and take the evidence.

**Situation.** A grove of old oaks off the road below Ilovren, on a moonless night. Kilan comes with his household captain and four guards, and stations them round the grove. Two envoys of Kaldin come in from the northeast road with a Black Flame priest.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-kilanilvrl|Kilan]], who speaks little and listens to everything. The envoys, who carry a sealed packet. The guards, and a hidden signal, a cord strung between trees that rings a bell at Kilan's horse, which the party may trip. [[being-nilaratlgn|Nilara Tolgen]], who may be in the trees with her bow, because she found the letter's copy first or followed her lord.

**Player choices.** Watch and listen, take an envoy, seize the packet, or let the meeting finish and follow the envoys home.

**Clues and rewards.** The talk in the grove is of a shrine in the Blood Hills, a guardian, and a gate that wants blood. The packet holds a coded letter from Kaldin to Kilan describing the shrine's defenses as Kaldin's priests know them, and what Kaldin will give Kilan when the maul is his. A captured envoy is a Black Flame soldier of Leravren who knows the men in Kilan's household as his brothers.

**Consequences.** If the party is found, Kilan's guards and the envoys turn on it, outnumbering it, and try to take a prisoner to learn how much it knows. If the party escapes with the packet, Kilan knows it has it.

**If bypassed.** Kilan learns from an escaped lieutenant that the cave fell and that the party found a letter, and moves anyway.

### Scene Three: The Marshal's Audience

**Purpose.** Kilan strikes first, and the party must prove its case.

**Situation.** The Marshal's hall at Torvila Keravel. Kilan has reached it first and stands at the Marshal's side. Tarlas is present, nervous for his lordship.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-jarvelplnvr|Jarvel]], hard and impatient, who has heard that the party let Dirave's band escape, frightened the village and forged letters to set his lords against each other. Kilan, courteous and regretful. [[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas]], who will back whoever looks likely to keep him his lordship.

**Player choices.** Lay the packet and the letter before the Marshal, call Nilara to say what she has seen, call Darisen, appeal to Tarlas's interest, or leave and take Kilan another way.

**Clues and rewards.** Kaldin's packet, read aloud in the hall, is proof the Marshal cannot dismiss. A captured envoy or Nilara's word gives it weight. Darisen will speak to the history and nothing else. A party that has found the clerk in Belsaren who drew the treason charge against Dirave's house holds something more dangerous: proof that the Marshal's own charge was false. Jarvel will deal with a party that holds it, quietly, and never forgive it.

**Consequences.** If the party convinces the Marshal, he sends riders to arrest Kilan, and Kilan does not wait for them. If it fails, the Marshal orders the party out of the vale, and Kilan has bought the time he needs.

**If bypassed.** The Marshal sends men to arrest the party at Belsaren, and Tarlas warns it, because he has begun to fear Kilan more.

### Scene Four: The Hall at Ilovren

**Purpose.** Confront Kilan.

**Situation.** Kilan's fortified hall above the village of Ilovren. He has barred the gate and gathered his household, archers of the vale and the Black Flame soldiers among them, and he is packing the year's gathered papers to ride for the hills.

**NPCs and threats.** Kilan, his household captain and the Flame's soldiers, who see him as the faith's coming man in the vale. The yeoman archers of the household, who are not sure which side they are on. Nilara, who can open a postern if the party has won her.

**Player choices.** Storm the gate, come in by Nilara's postern, persuade the yeomen to lay down their bows, or wait for the Marshal's riders.

**Clues and rewards.** Cornered, Kilan says what he wanted: the maul would let him take Belsaren, rally the vale and hold it for the Flame, and Kaldin has promised him a high place in the faith for it. He tries to buy the party with a share. His private study holds the year's work: a map of the Blood Hills marked with the shrine, the old prayers of the Flame copied from Darisen's own sources, and Kaldin's priests' notes on the shrine's guardian.

**Consequences.** A Kilan taken alive is the Marshal's to try. A Kilan killed takes his account of Kaldin with him. A Kilan who escapes rides for the hills with the map's copy and his soldiers.

**If bypassed.** The Marshal's riders storm the hall, Kilan escapes with his soldiers, and the study burns with half the papers.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

Kilan tells the Marshal that Tarlas let the outlaws escape, and the Marshal gives him Belsaren. His household, filled with the Flame's soldiers, holds the road into the hills. At the next new moon Kaldin's priests ride through Belsaren for the shrine.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

Kilan is taken, dead or fled, and the party holds the map, the prayers and the notes on the guardian. It must decide what to do with Kilan's loyalists and any envoys it captured, and whether to tell the Marshal about Kaldin, knowing that Kaldin's kinsman [[being-selvarezrvl|Selvare Zuravel]], Baron of Zuravlen, holds his barony of the Earl of Hárivren, the kingdom's senior Black Flame noble and a Peer. Darisen asks, gently, to see the map, and offers to tell the party everything his books say about the shrine. The road leads to [[place-bloodhills|the Blood Hills]] ([[scenario-firsbldhls|The Fires of the Blood Hills]]), and Kaldin's men know it too.
