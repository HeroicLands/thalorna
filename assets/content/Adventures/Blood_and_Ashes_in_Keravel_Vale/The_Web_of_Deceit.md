## Teaser

In The Web of Deceit, with the brigands scattered and their leader defeated, [[place-ceravelvale|the Vale]] should be at peace—but the shadows of Keravel conceal deeper threats. Evidence points to a cunning noble manipulating the chaos for his own gain, stoking unrest to weaken his rivals and further his ambitions. As the adventurers unravel the threads of deception, they must navigate political intrigue, confront dangerous allies of their new enemy, and uncover a secret alliance that threatens to plunge the region into greater peril. Can they expose the puppet master pulling the Vale's strings, or will they fall victim to his carefully laid plans? The stakes rise, and the lines between friend and foe blur in this tale of betrayal and ambition.

## Background

In this part, the adventurers track down [Kilan Ilovrel na Ilovren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}'s true motives and confront him. They realize that Kilan's actions are not just a power grab but also involve his devotion to [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] and secret alliance with [Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the [Zuravel]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} clan (a fierce Black Flame), with Kilan intending to use the power of an ancient Black Flame artifact hidden in [[place-bloodhills|the Blood Hills]] east of [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]].

## Adventure Structure

Goal
: Expose Kilan's manipulation and defeat him.

Kilan Ilovrel na Ilovren
: Lord of [[place-ilovren|Ilovren]]

Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren
: Lord of [[place-leravren|Leravren]], and member of the Zuravel clan of the Black Flame.

## Adventure Outline

### Uncovering Kilan's Allies

Description
: The adventurers start by investigating Kilan's secretive dealings, likely by following a suspicious emissary or intercepted message. They eventually uncover Kilan's connections with Kaldin al Lénavel, a Black Flame nalor from the Zur clan, who is working with Kilan to destabilize the region. Through conversations or clues from locals, the adventurers learn of a mysterious Black Flame envoy in the area.

Challenge
: This part requires social skills and deduction, as the adventurers must carefully extract information from unwilling locals and avoid suspicion. They learn that the Zur clan sees the Keravel region as an opportunity to spread Black Flame influence and is particularly interested in an ancient Black Flame artifact rumored to be hidden in the Blood Hills.

Clue
: The adventurers gain intelligence on a planned meeting between Kilan and Kaldin's emissaries in a nearby village, where they hope to gather more information on the artifact and Kilan's plans.

### Ambush at the Meeting

Description
: The adventurers track Kilan to a secluded meeting point, where he plans to confer with Kaldin's emissaries. Kilan has stationed guards around the area to prevent interruptions, but the adventurers manage to infiltrate the meeting site.

Challenge
: Just as the adventurers prepare to observe or intervene, they trigger a hidden signal, alerting Kilan's men, who spring an ambush. The adventurers are outnumbered and must fight while protecting themselves from both Kilan's guards and the Black Flame emissaries. If captured, Kilan's followers will attempt to interrogate them to determine how much they know.

Reward
: The adventurers may capture a Black Flame emissary or intercept a coded document that reveals Kilan's recent communications with Kaldin, detailing their search for a secret Black Flame shrine in the Blood Hills and describing the artifact's rumored power.

### Investigating the Shrine's Location

Description
: Armed with evidence, the adventurers pursue leads to the location of the hidden Black Flame shrine, piecing together fragments of lore from texts, symbols, or clues they've uncovered. This part emphasizes exploration and research.

Challenge
: They may need to decipher ancient symbols, investigate cursed relics, or navigate dangerous terrain in search of the Blood Hills. Additionally, Kilan's followers attempt to slow their progress, setting traps or ambushes to hinder their approach to the shrine.

Role-Playing Opportunity
: The adventurers encounter a reluctant informant—a scholar or hermit familiar with the shrine's history. This person may know the general location or key features of the shrine but warns of its deadly defenses and curses on intruders. They may offer further assistance for a price or moral persuasion.

Clue
: The adventurers receive a map fragment or cryptic description pointing toward the Blood Hills, where the shrine and its artifact lie hidden.

### Confronting Kilan in His Stronghold

Description
: The adventurers track Kilan to his stronghold, where he is preparing for his next step—securing his position as a future lord by destabilizing Belsaren and obtaining the powerful Black Flame artifact. Kilan, however, is not ready to abandon his plans and has fortified his stronghold, preparing to confront the adventurers directly.

Challenge
: The adventurers must find a way to breach Kilan's defenses, which include both conventional guards and Black Flame zealots who view Kilan as a future ally of their faith. Combat is intense, with Kilan using his lieutenants to hold the adventurers back while attempting to finish a dark ritual or complete preparations to march on Belsaren.

Twist
: Kilan hints at the power the artifact could give him, claiming he could use it to rally support across the region, seize Belsaren, and even unite the area under Black Flame rule. He may reveal that Kaldin and the Zur clan have offered him a high place in their ranks if he succeeds in retrieving it.

### Aftermath and Revelation of the Shrine's Location

Description
: With Kilan defeated or driven off, the adventurers gain access to his private study or war room, where they discover detailed plans about the shrine and clues to the artifact's final location.

Reward
: Among Kilan's documents, they find a detailed account of his efforts to locate the Black Flame shrine, possibly a complete map of the Blood Hills and notes from Kaldin's advisors on the shrine's defenses. This may include details about the powerful protections, curses, or a guardian that the Zur clan believes protects the artifact.

Resolution
: The adventurers decide how to handle Kilan's remaining loyalists and any Black Flame emissaries they've captured. They may also choose to report their findings to local authorities in Belsaren, foreseeing the political consequences of exposing Kilan's alliance with the Zuravel clan of the Black Flame. Most importantly, they now hold the knowledge needed to locate and face the shrine, setting up Part 3 as they prepare to journey into the Blood Hills in pursuit of the artifact.
