## Overview {#overview}

The raiders of [[affiliation-rinjaidmn|Rinjai Domain]] took a [[being-tantsouprfct|Tantsou Prefect]] off the mainland coast and brought him home to [[place-rinwu|Rinwu]], where he is a guest in the red hall of [[being-hojarinjai|Rinjai Hoja]]. Three demands fall on that hall. The court wants the man returned as a gesture of heaven's magnanimity, and a [[being-tantsouenvoy|Tantsou Envoy]] has asked at [[place-lengwou|Lengwou]] for the same. [[being-henzoshuiwo|Shuiwojai Henzo]], whose liege-oath Hoja swore, wants the ransom. Hoja holds the prefect and owes Henzo his oath, and whichever demand he satisfies, he disappoints the others.

The Jenchai find the mainland decadent and scheming, and the mainland finds the Jenchai crude and violent. Each side needs the other's goods, and [[place-easternstrt|The Eastern Strait]] between them is busy and watched. A prefect is not worth a war to either side, and neither side can be seen to let him go for nothing.

The party enters as the envoy's hired hands, as Henzo's, as hands the court sends to learn what the mainland will pay, or as a crew whose hull happens to be in the strait when a war junk shows off the coves.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The prefect.** A mainland official of some rank, taken by Rinjai's raiders in a coastal raid and kept in the red hall as a guest and a pledge. He is not mistreated. His family's silver and the empire's pride are both worth something, and neither has been offered.

**Hoja.** Hoja is twenty-nine, quick and bold and loyal, and he decides by the nearest obligation and answers for it afterward. He wants to keep the oath and the prefect both, and he wants the one deed that leaves him standing before the court and before Henzo. He has not found it, and until something makes one obligation nearer than the others, he keeps the prefect and chooses nothing.

**The court.** [[being-weilengjnch|Weileng]] grants the mainland envoy an audience, and he wants to learn what the envoy will offer. The audience costs the court nothing, and a gesture of heaven's magnanimity is one of the few things the throne can give that nobody else can take.

**The Strait.** Henzo's silver and fleet keep the water in front of [[place-rojai|Rojai]], and the ransom he wants adds to the silver he spends on the court's behalf. The mainland's coastal magistrates want the raids stopped and their captured officers returned. The empire has no fleet that can clear the strait and wants the islands' ships off it.

**The war junk.** A mainland war junk has been sighted off the coves. It may have come to fetch the prefect, to carry a message for Hoja or to burn the harbor at [Jawo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, and the referee decides which. Whatever its errand, its captain, a [[being-tantsoujunkcpt|Tantsou War-Junk Captain]], is ordered to hold at anchor until word comes to him from the envoy or from the shore, or until he is attacked. He does not know what the envoy has asked at Lengwou, and Hoja does not know what he has been told. The [[being-rinjairaider|Rinjai Raider]] crews on the rocks watch the junk and are held from the first arrow by Hoja's word.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Nobody here is a villain and nobody wants a war. The scenario ends when Hoja has handed the prefect to someone, or has chosen to hand him to no one.

## What the Game Master Decides {#what-the-game-master-decides}

- The junk's errand: the prefect, the harbor at Jawo, or a message for Hoja.
- Whose silver bought the reading that favors Henzo.

## The Clock {#clock}

- **Day One.** The audience at Lengwou.
- **Days Two and Three.** The crossing. The party reaches Rinwu at dusk on Day Three, as the junk is sighted.
- **Night of Day Three.** The red hall.
- **Day Four.** The coves and the junk.
- **Day Five, first light.** Hoja chooses.
- **Without word from the shore,** the junk holds until the weather turns, and then sails.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The courtier: the price of a guest.** Hoja keeps the prefect as a guest in the red hall. A party hired by a mainland envoy offers to buy the man out, and a party hired by Henzo offers to take him to Hawu.
- **The mariner: the junk off the coves.** A war junk has been sighted off the coves, and Hoja wants a party to learn whether it comes to fetch the prefect or to burn the harbor at Jawo.
- **The infiltrator: what the envoy would give.** A Tantsou envoy asks in Lengwou for the return of the prefect held at Rinwu. Weileng grants the audience and wants a party to learn what the envoy will offer.
- **The cleric: the bought reading.** A shrine [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] of Rinjai brings a reading of the mountain that favors Henzo's claim, and Hoja wants a party to learn who paid for it.

## The Opening {#opening}

Rinwu's main harbor at dusk, with twenty-five hulls on the water and one that is not Jenchai in the offing. A watchman on the red hall's spur strikes a bell once and then again, and the raiders on the quay stop what they are doing and look out at the strait.

> "That is not a Rinjai sail," says a captain at the rail, and nobody answers him.

A crew already in the strait begins here, on the evening it reaches Rinwu, and plays Scene Three next. A party hired at court plays Scene One and Scene Two first and arrives at this dusk.

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Audience at Lengwou

**Purpose.** Hear the mainland's request, and learn what the court wants of the party.

**Situation.** Lengwou, the Dawn Court, a town of 9,000 on the lowest slope of the Emperor's mountain. Foreign envoys are received here and go no farther. Weileng receives the mainland envoy in a hall of cedar and lacquer, sitting very still.

**NPCs and threats.** Weileng, patient, formal and watchful, who lets a silence stand until the other speaker fills it. The mainland envoy, who asks for the prefect and promises nothing, and who treats the court's ceremony as a performance he has agreed to endure. The [[being-jnchhsguard|Imperial Household Guard]], who stand where they are put.

**Player choices.** Carry the court's request to Rinwu, learn what the envoy will offer, offer the envoy a passage, or ask Weileng what the throne wants in return.

**Clues and rewards.** The envoy offers no coin and a good deal of language about the empire's goodwill. Weileng asks the party to learn what the envoy would give if goodwill were not enough.

**Consequences.** The party leaves with a request from the throne to Hoja, and the envoy's attendants know who they are.

**If bypassed.** The court sends a household guard with the request, and the party meets Hoja already told what the throne wants.

### Scene Two: The Watched Water

**Purpose.** The crossing to the lesser island, and the strait's tolls.

**Situation.** [[place-rinjai|Rinjai]], the red mountain of the lesser island, stands at the mouth of the strait, and every hull that passes between the mainland and the Nine Mountains passes within sight of its coast. A hull bound for Rinwu from the great island crosses water that the fleet of Shuiwojai and the raiders of Rinjai both watch.

**NPCs and threats.** The pilot, a [[being-straitpilot|Strait Pilot]], who takes the party's coin for the set of the water. A Shuiwojai toll-boat, its crew each a [[being-tsaihwosldr|Tsaihwo Soldier]], who ask the hull's errand. The pilot says the mainland merchants he carries pay tolls to the lords and to the empire's bureaus for the same passage and hate both.

**Player choices.** Pay the toll and give the errand, hide the errand, ask the pilot about the junk, or leave an offering at the first headland shrine.

**Clues and rewards.** The pilot says that the junk lies at the western cove and that it has not lowered a boat. A crew that leaves an offering at the first shrine is treated as a crew that knows the water.

**Consequences.** The party arrives at Rinwu with its errand known to the fleet, or hidden from it.

**If bypassed.** A Rinjai ship picks the party up in the strait and brings it to the harbor.

### Scene Three: The Red Hall

**Purpose.** Meet Hoja and the prefect, and hear each demand.

**Situation.** The hall at Rinwu, lacquered red, on a spur above the main harbor, with the strait in front of it. A captain's seat goes by the hulls his household commands. The prefect sits in the place of a guest, and he is not shackled.

**NPCs and threats.** Hoja, who is quick and bold and who answers a hard question by looking at the nearest obligation. The prefect, who is composed and who treats Hoja as an equal he has not yet decided to forgive. Henzo's liaison, a [[being-jnchagent|Jenchai House Agent]], who has come to remind Hoja whose oath he swore, and the envoy's agent, one of his attendants, played as a [[being-tantsouenvoy|Tantsou Envoy]], if the party brought one.

**Player choices.** Offer to buy the prefect out for the envoy, offer to take him to Hawu for Henzo, offer to carry the court's request, or ask the prefect what he would give for his freedom.

**Clues and rewards.** Hoja listens to every offer and answers none. The prefect says that his own family can be told he is alive, and that his family will pay only for a man known to be alive.

**Consequences.** Every party that makes an offer has made an enemy of the others. Hoja has heard all three and has said none of them is the one he wants.

**If bypassed.** Hoja hears no offer from the party. The court's request reaches him by the household guard, Henzo's liaison repeats his liege's demand, and Hoja keeps the prefect and chooses nothing.

### Scene Four: The War Junk

**Purpose.** Read what the mainland ship has come for.

**Situation.** The coves at the strait's mouth, where the junk lies at anchor and the raiders watch it from the rocks. It carries the empire's flag and has not lowered a boat. A shrine Tanchei of Rinjai has brought Hoja a reading of the mountain that favors the Strait.

**NPCs and threats.** The junk's captain, who is stiff and cautious, and who is under orders the party can learn only by going aboard. The raiders on the rocks, who want to fire the first arrow and are held by Hoja's word. The shrine's Tanchei, a [[being-smtshrntnch|Summit Shrine Tanchei]], who brought the reading that favors Henzo.

**Player choices.** Go aboard under a flag of parley, carry a letter from Hoja, send word to Hawu, or ask the Tanchei who paid for the reading.

**Clues and rewards.** The referee decides what the junk wants, from the three options of the prefect, the harbor or a message for Hoja. A party that goes aboard learns what the captain knows of the envoy's request. A party that asks the Tanchei learns whose silver bought the reading.

**Consequences.** A parley is the word from the shore the captain waits for: he answers it according to his errand, and the way to an exchange opens. A party that fires on the junk, or lets the raiders fire, releases him from his orders to hold. A refusal to go aboard leaves the junk at anchor and the raiders restless.

**If bypassed.** No word goes to the junk. It stays at anchor with its boat unlowered, and the raiders keep watch on it from the rocks.

### Scene Five: The Prefect's Road

**Purpose.** Hoja chooses, and the party sees what it costs.

**Situation.** The red hall at first light, after the party has made an offer in the red hall, brought word from the junk or put a third course to Hoja. The prefect, Henzo's liaison, the party and the envoy's agent, if the party brought one, are all in the same room. The junk lies in the offing, unless a parley or an attack has sent it away.

**NPCs and threats.** Hoja, who chooses now. Henzo's liaison, who stands by the door. The prefect, who says nothing until he is asked.

**Player choices.** Support the return, support the ransom, propose a third course that gives both something, or leave him to choose.

**Clues and rewards.** Hoja chooses now because the party has given him a nearer obligation than the others. A third course exists for a party that finds it: the prefect goes home through the court as the throne's gift. The throne gives Hoja a dignity that costs it nothing, and the prefect's family on the mainland pays Henzo's ransom in goods. Hoja takes it if it leaves him standing before the court and before Henzo.

**Consequences.** Whichever demand Hoja satisfies, he disappoints the others. The prefect leaves Rinwu, or stays.

**If bypassed.** What happens depends on what the party has already done.

- **No earlier intervention.** If the party made no offer in the red hall, sent no word to the junk and put no third course to Hoja, nothing makes one obligation nearer than the others. He keeps the prefect, and the season runs as described under If the Party Does Nothing.
- **An offer already made.** If the party made an offer in Scene Three and then stays away from the dawn meeting, the offer stands without it. Hoja listened to it and answered none, as he does with every offer, and at first light he answers the one that is nearest: he hands the prefect to the side the party spoke for, through Henzo's liaison, the envoy's agent or the court's household guard, and the side he disappoints learns that the party was not in the room. If the party spoke for more than one side, the oath to Henzo is the nearer obligation and Hoja keeps it.
- **Word from the junk.** A parley in Scene Four has already been answered, and the junk acts on that word according to its errand whether or not the party comes back. If Hoja hands the prefect to the mainland and the junk's errand is to fetch him, he leaves aboard it. A junk that the party or the raiders fired on is released from its orders to hold and does not wait for the meeting.
- **A third course already proposed.** If the party put the third course to Hoja and leaves, Hoja takes it only if the court's dignity and the family's goods are both on offer when he chooses. Otherwise it is one more demand with no one to press it, and he answers the nearest obligation as above.

The party hears Hoja's choice from the quay.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

Hoja keeps the prefect in the red hall through the season and chooses nothing. No word goes to the junk, and nobody fires on it, so it lies at anchor off the coves with its boat unlowered until the weather turns, and then it leaves without a message. The court's request and the envoy's go unanswered, and Henzo's liaison reminds Hoja whose oath he swore in a voice that carries through the hall. The prefect writes a letter home. Sooner or later a raid on the mainland coast goes wrong, and Hoja loses hulls he cannot replace.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

- **The prefect returned.** The court has its gesture of magnanimity and Henzo has no ransom. Hoja has satisfied the throne and offended his liege.
- **The prefect ransomed.** Henzo has his silver and the court has an empty gesture. A mainland envoy leaves Lengwou with nothing.
- **The third course.** The prefect goes home as the throne's gift, and his family on the mainland pays the ransom in goods. Everyone is slightly dissatisfied and nobody is shamed.
- **The junk answered.** If the party parleyed, the mainland knows what Rinjai will accept. If it did not, the next ship comes with more men.
