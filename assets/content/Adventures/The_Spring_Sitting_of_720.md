[[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]] was elected Harbormaster of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]] at the spring sitting of 717 for a single three-year term, and at the spring sitting of 720 it ends. She may not stand again. The [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]] elects her successor from among its own members, by voices weighted by the tonnage each house brought to the spring census, and the two houses that want the office are counting the same hulls. This frame runs through the year to the sitting. The party leaders hire outsiders to find leverage, votes and tonnage, and the campaign is one of intrigue that ends when the Council puts the question.

## The Situation and the Stakes {#the-situation}

**The contest.** [[being-knilthtvrn|Knilthorv Tvarnidrok]], head of [[affiliation-hsetvarnidrok|Tvarnidrok]], the oldest house on the Council, leads the old houses' party and has the voices to win on last year's census. [[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]], head of [[affiliation-hsenorodari|the Norodari]], has bought hulls all year to change the count, and the newer houses follow him because a Tvarnidrok Harbormaster would hold the levy on the old houses' terms. Neither party is the Harbormaster's. Hrundselda belongs to neither, and her house, [[affiliation-hsethraskumak|Thraskumak]], builds as many hulls as it sails.

**The lost hulls.** Three Norodari hulls went down in the autumn fog on [[place-kalipsarun|the Kalipsa Run]]. If [[affiliation-bondsyndcts|the Bond Syndicates]] pay their bonds, the Norodari can replace the hulls before the census. If the arbitrators of [[place-sealawhall|the Sea-Law Hall]] find that the masters lost them by refusing a pilot, the syndicates pay nothing and the Norodari come to the census three hulls short. The Syndicate of the Gull has refused the bonds before the hearing, its syndic [[being-vraldkrldd|Vraldselda Kruldidrok]] says that paying all the autumn's losses would wind the syndicate up, and the pilots' guild under [[being-knurvhnrvn|Knurvorv Hnirvendik]] holds that none of the three masters took a pilot. Both parties have taken counsel among the arbitrators, and each says the other has tried to buy one.

**The census rule.** The old houses hold that a hull bought in the winter should not count at the spring census until it has made a voyage for its house. The rule is unwritten, and the Clerk's roll has never applied it. Kubasor calls it a rule invented for the occasion. The Harbormaster certifies the roll, and she has not said how she will count. The yards of Thraskumak are building two of the Norodari hulls for delivery before the census, so the woman who certifies the count is also the builder of some of what is counted.

**The levy.** The tonnage levy that pays for the squadrons has risen three years running. Both parties promise to lower it, and the houses that pay most of it want it lowest. Hrundselda has told the Council that it cannot be lowered while the squadrons sail.

The stakes are who holds the harbor, the forts, the customs and the navy for the next three years, and whether the count that decides it is one the city accepts.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Nobody in this frame decides who is elected. The notes give the parties, the levers and the date, not the outcome. Play every Councilor as a head of house counting voices, and let the table's findings move the count.

## Who Moves It {#who-moves-it}

[[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]], Harbormaster
: In the last year of her term, belonging to neither party, and certifying the census that decides who follows her. She wants a count taken by someone in neither party's pay.

[[being-knilthtvrn|Knilthorv Tvarnidrok]], head of Tvarnidrok
: Head of the old houses' party, who wants the office next and has asked the Harbormaster to apply the census rule.

[[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]], head of the Norodari
: Leader of the newer houses, who wants proof that the pilots' boat was off its station and proof that the old houses have bought an arbitrator.

[[being-skruldhvrn|Skruldthann Hvarnumak]], eldest living Past Harbormaster
: Harbormaster from 714 to 717, who speaks before the others at every sitting.

[[being-mirvirnlth|Mirvir Nulthumak]], Clerk of the Council
: A salaried officer, not a Councilor, who keeps the census roll and the record of every sitting, and counts the voices when the Harbormaster puts the question.

[[being-tvilgrlthr|Tvilgorv Rultharuk]], Customs Officer
: Takes the tonnage census with tally-men whom both parties have bought, and checks the totals with the Clerk each spring.

[[being-vraldkrldd|Vraldselda Kruldidrok]], syndic of the Syndicate of the Gull
: Writes the bonds the Norodari claim, and has asked the Two Ships to share the loss.

[[being-thilmrlthm|Thilminda Ralthumak]], master arbitrator of the Sea-Law Hall
: Can sit in the third chair if both parties accept her, and neither trusts her.

[[being-knurvhnrvn|Knurvorv Hnirvendik]], Guild Master of the pilots
: Keeps the pilots' roster and holds that none of the three Norodari masters took a pilot.

[[being-telusorbzn|Telusor Bazunari]], Kalipsa's Consul at Sarmelis
: A Valtari broker of hull shares who brokered the Norodari's winter hulls and holds the city's seal for its traders' disputes on the outer quay.

The bodies
: The Council and its houses; [[affiliation-bondsyndcts|the Bond Syndicates]], among them the Gull and the Two Ships; the arbitrators of [[lore-sealaw|the Sea-Law]]; the pilots' guild; and the Customs Officer's tally-men.

## The Arcs {#arcs}

1. **The count.** [[scenario-kalipsahulls|The Third Night and the Spring Count]] joins the hearing on the lost hulls to the census, and is the frame's main arc.
2. **The shore's water.** [[scenario-vrellnesspring|The Key Above the Dye Vats]] runs beside the election: the South Shore villages have no voice on the Council, the city and the villages dispute the springs after a dry summer, and the Harbormaster has not told the watch to force the spring-house door.
3. **The Mole.** [[scenario-oldmoleface|Before the Stone Is Covered]] asks the Harbormaster for leave that she can grant only in part, and she can call a sitting rather than claim a power she does not have.

## Ways In {#ways-in}

1. **For the Harbormaster.** She wants strangers to walk the quays beside the tally-men and record each hull once, because both parties' counters are in the other's pay.
2. **For the old houses.** Knilthorv wants a master arbitrator to recite a verdict that supports the census rule, and someone to find one who remembers such a verdict.
3. **For the newer houses.** Kubasor pays for proof that the pilots' boat was off its station the nights his hulls were lost, and for a witness from Sarmelis who saw the masters leave.
4. **For the syndicates.** The Gull needs to know how much of its book has been sold abroad before word of the losses reaches the buyers, and how many of them will sell.
5. **For the yards.** Thraskumak needs seasoned oak for the Norodari hulls, and the timber ship from the Nordlands is late.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

- The hearing at the Sea-Law Hall goes on with the third chair unfilled until the two chosen arbitrators agree. If the syndicates pay, the Norodari buy hulls before the census. If the arbitrators find that the masters refused a pilot, the syndicates pay nothing and the Norodari come to the census three hulls short.
- The tally-men count for both parties, and the Clerk enters the totals on the census roll the Harbormaster certifies at the spring sitting.
- The Harbormaster has not said how she will count the winter hulls, and the census rule stays unwritten until the roll she certifies shows it.
- At the spring sitting the Council elects a Harbormaster, and Hrundselda, who may not stand, leaves the office. The tonnage levy stays where the Council puts it, and she holds that it cannot be lowered while the squadrons sail.
