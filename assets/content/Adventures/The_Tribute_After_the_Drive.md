## Overview {#overview}

The clans of the [[place-sthmrchsrgn|South Marches]] pay the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethua]] tribute in stock for the right to bring their herds to the edge of its irrigated country in the dry season. This year the tribute is late. The winter rains came short, the herds went into the dry months thin, and [[being-mbareloddrmb|Mbarelo of the Dorumbe]], headman of the [[affiliation-dorumbetribe|Dorumbe Tribe]], has sent word to the Hégissa of the southern cantons that the stock comes after the autumn drive and not before. Bethua wants it sooner. The [[affiliation-spearsisterhood|Spear-Sisterhood]]'s patrols are stretched between the eastern frontier and the southern posts, the levies of the southern villages man the posts, and [[affiliation-jcklsthmrchs|the Jackals of the South Marches]] hold the western stages of [[place-marchesroad|the Marches Road]] with fewer eyes on them than ever.

Then a Dorumbe clan reports that riders of Bethua have come south and taken the stock it had set aside for the tribute. The frontier posts say no patrol of theirs crossed the line. In the elders' circle at [[place-dorumbecamp|Dorumbe Camp]], the voices that wanted to pay nothing until the grass returns now say the tribute has been paid already, by force.

The party enters between the posts and the clans: sent south to learn why the tribute is late, hired to carry Mbarelo's word to the posts, or asked to find out who took the herd.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The late tribute.** The delay is honest. The herds are thin, the Dorumbe riders are home for the autumn drive, and Mbarelo means to pay in full and in stock, because a late tribute must not be called a refusal. His place rests on descent and on the consent of the elders, and the thin herds are costing him the consent.

**The taken herd.** Before the drive, riders came by night to [[place-thumbelcamp|Thumbel Camp]], a clan's tents around a rock-cistern in the western belt, and drove off the stock the clan had set aside as its share of the tribute. The herders saw women with spears and a few men who fought on foot in a close line with steel, as only the [[affiliation-spadai|Spadai]] of Bethua fight. They say a company of the Lorkhai, the Spear-Sisterhood, took the tribute by force, and in their telling Bethua has paid itself.

**Who took it.** The riders were the Houseless core of the Jackals: exiled Bethuan women and cast-out Spadai, riding without the hired marcher men, who would have been known. [[being-argyrirjckl|Argirira the Jackal]] sent them. A tribute the Dorumbe believe Bethua has seized becomes a refusal, a refusal brings raids across the frontier in both directions, raids pull the Spear-Sisterhood back to the line, and a quarrel between Bethua and the clans makes the clans need her more. The road is hers while it lasts. The clans tolerate her because she is useful against the north, and raiding a Dorumbe well in her own name would cost her that, so she raided it in Bethua's.

**Where the stock is.** The herd stands in the pens of a western stage of [[place-khuqetzalu|Khuqet-Zalu]] that the Jackals hold, its ear-marks freshly recut. Argirira means to sell it east once the elders' circle has spoken, and she has hired two young riders of the [[affiliation-hasembetribe|Hasembe Tribe]] to drive it. One has been paid ahead and has gone home to [[place-hasembecamp|Hasembe Camp]] with more coin than a season's riding earns, which is the question [[being-khandoruhsmb|Khandoru of the Hasembe]] is asking.

**The posts.** No patrol crossed. The posts are held by Lorkhai officers and by levy women drawn from the villages of the southern cantons, among them [[place-sehela|Sehela]], who are short-handed, short-tempered and a long way from home before harvest. A Dorumbe rider who comes to a post angry in the dusk may be taken for a raider.

**The elders.** The [[being-wellselder|Elder of the Wells]] is not sure of the herders' tale. In the Elder's reckoning no Bethuan company has ridden as far as Thumbel's cistern in living memory without being seen on the road, and the tracks of a company bound home go north. The Elder has said so in the circle and has been answered with the herders' own eyes.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The scene the party must reach is the elders' circle with something it can show. Until Thumbel's herd is found, the circle decides between Mbarelo's word and the herders' eyes, and the herders' eyes are winning.

> [!WARNING]
> Argirira's people look like a Lorkhai patrol because they were trained as one. A party that meets the Jackals before it has seen Thumbel's tracks may carry the herders' story to the posts and set the frontier alight itself. Let the tracks come first if the party gives you the chance.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The envoy or courtier.** The Court of the Veil wants someone to ride to the Hégissa of the southern cantons, learn why the tribute is late, and come back with the truth rather than the posts' report of it.
- **The mercenary or soldier.** Mbarelo needs an escort for the tribute stock when it moves, and someone who can carry his explanation to the captain of a frontier post and be believed. He pays in water-rights at [[place-longwells|the Long Wells]], since he has little coin.
- **The leader.** [[being-tazithakhlthn|Tázitha of House Khalithon]] has had her village levy drawn south to the posts in the weeks before harvest, and she wants someone to count the women who have gone and tell her how many come home in time.
- **The thief-taker or tracker.** Khandoru wants to know who is paying his riders to leave, and a party that follows the money finds the herd.

## The Opening {#opening}

A frontier post south of Sehela, past the last cistern, at dusk. A dozen levy women, each a [[being-levywoman|Levy Woman of the Southern Cantons]], and two Lorkhai officers stand behind a mud-brick wall built for twice their number. A rider of the Dorumbe sits his horse just out of spear-throw, alone, and calls across the ground in a Marcher Okharik the officers half understand.

> "You have taken Thumbel's stock," he calls. "Count it as paid. We count it so."

The officer beside the party says, under her breath, that no company of hers has been south of this wall since spring.

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Post and the Village

**Purpose.** Establish Bethua's side, and the posts' honesty.

**Situation.** The frontier post and Sehela behind it. The levy drills under veterans of Tázitha's household guard, and the [[affiliation-spearsisterhood|Spear-Sisterhood]] counts it among the hands it can call. The [[being-postofficer|Post Officer]] keeps the list of patrols and the list of stages the companies hold.

**NPCs and threats.** The Post Officer, curt and certain; the levy women, who want to go home for the harvest; Tázitha, who has ridden down to see what the posts have done with her tenants.

**Player choices.** Read the patrol list, speak to the Dorumbe rider before he rides off, or ride south at once.

**Clues and rewards.** The patrol list shows no company south of the post in a season. Tázitha offers a letter that gives the party water and lodging in her House's villages on its way back north.

**Consequences.** If the party lets the rider go unanswered, he tells the circle that Bethua denies the theft, which the circle takes as the insult it sounds like.

**If bypassed.** The party learns the posts' account from Tázitha's levy women at Sehela.

### Scene Two: The Elders' Circle

**Purpose.** Meet the Dorumbe and the danger to Mbarelo's place.

**Situation.** Dorumbe Camp, a ring of black goat-hair tents and stock pens around a deep well at the foot of the highland spurs, a long ride south of the last post. The elders sit at the well. A stranger asks the Elder of the Wells for leave to water before letting a horse drink.

**NPCs and threats.** Mbarelo, slow to speak and quick to count; the Elder of the Wells, who doubts the herders' tale and says so; the [[being-tributebrer|Tribute-Bearer]], who must drive whatever the circle decides to the posts; elders of the western clans who want to pay nothing; Thumbel's herders, who saw what they saw.

**Player choices.** Ask leave and sit with the circle, carry the posts' word, or offer to find the herd.

**Clues and rewards.** The herders' account: spears and close-order steel, women leading, riders who did not speak the Marcher tongue among themselves. The Elder of the Wells points out that Bethua's companies ride home north.

**Consequences.** The circle gives the party until the drive to bring something it can show. If the party argues Bethua's case without proof, the elders hear a foreigner speaking for the north.

**If bypassed.** Mbarelo meets the party on the road and gives them the same deadline.

> [!TIP]
> Play Mbarelo as a man doing arithmetic in front of you. He does not ask the party to believe him; he shows them the thin herds and the count of what each clan owes. His fear is not Bethua but his own circle.

### Scene Three: Thumbel's Cistern

**Purpose.** The tracks.

**Situation.** Thumbel Camp's tents around a rock-cistern in the western belt, and the empty pen where the clan's tribute share stood.

**NPCs and threats.** The [[being-thumbelhead|Clan Head of Thumbel]] and his riders, each a [[being-dorumbrider|Dorumbe Rider]], who are angry and armed and want someone to blame; a [[being-thumbelboy|Herder Boy of Thumbel]] who saw the raid from the thorn.

**Player choices.** Read the ground, question the boy, or ride out along the trail.

**Clues and rewards.** The tracks of the driven herd and its riders go east toward the road and the western stages, not north toward the posts. The boy remembers that one rider called another "sister" in Bethuan and that none wore a House's ornament.

**Consequences.** A party that follows the trail reaches the western stage within a day.

**If bypassed.** Khandoru's rider, at Hasembe Camp, tells whoever pays him that he was hired at a western stage to drive stock east.

### Scene Four: The Western Stage

**Purpose.** Find the herd, and meet the Jackals.

**Situation.** A walled Khelathi stage of Khuqet-Zalu on the road, with a standing cistern, a broken tower and a vaulted store fitted with a new door. The Jackals hold it. Thumbel's stock stands in its pens with fresh cuts over the old ear-marks.

**NPCs and threats.** A [[being-jcklieutent|Lieutenant of the Jackals]], a cast-out Spada or an exiled woman, who holds the stage and its well for Argirira; the Houseless core, each a [[being-houslessftr|Houseless Fighter]], and a few hired marcher men, each a [[being-hiredmarchr|Hired Marcher Rider]]; Argirira herself if the party is worth her time. A fight against the Houseless core inside its own walls is one a party loses. On the open road, against a riding-party, it is a fight a seasoned party wins at a cost.

**Player choices.** Pay the toll for water and look, parley for the herd, bring away a hired man as a witness or a beast with Thumbel's mark, or go back for the clan's riders.

**Clues and rewards.** Thumbel's marks under the new cuts, and the hired marcher men, who have no love for the Houseless and say who rode out that night and who stayed home.

**Consequences.** Argirira learns that someone has seen the herd. She is willing to hear an offer, and she has a price: a party that can give her something she wants more than this quarrel, in coin or in news of the Hound of the Veil, may buy the herd back. A party that crosses her makes an enemy who remembers.

**If bypassed.** The herd is driven east within days, and only the Hasembe rider's coin is left to show for it.

> [!NOTE]
> Argirira's attention is divided this season. A fugitive Grammatissa, [[being-rshrhssfrn|Rashira of House Safiron]], is crossing the Marches on the same road with the thief-taker [[being-lmbhrpdhnn|Lámbira Pédhinion]] behind her, and Argirira wants Lámbira dead by her own hand. A party that knows this has something to sell.

### Scene Five: The Drive North

**Purpose.** The climax: the tribute moves, or it does not.

**Situation.** The elders' circle hears what the party found. If the circle holds with Mbarelo, the Tribute-Bearer gathers the stock in the pens beside the cistern after the drive and starts north along the road past the western stages to the posts.

**NPCs and threats.** Mbarelo and the Tribute-Bearer; the Dorumbe riders, back from the drive; a Jackal riding-party that watches the stock go by the stage; the posts, which have orders to receive the tribute and too few hands to drive it further north.

**Player choices.** Escort the stock, broker the handover at the post, ask the Spear-Sisterhood for riders, or bargain with the Jackals for safe passage.

**Clues and rewards.** Mbarelo's water-rights at the Long Wells, which the Dorumbe honor for as long as he holds his place; the posts' thanks and the Court's.

**Consequences.** Stock handed over at a post is Bethua's, and a Bethuan target. If the party does not see it north from the post, the Jackals take it the next night, and the Dorumbe have paid a tribute Bethua never received.

**If bypassed.** The stock reaches the post, and what follows depends on whether the party has warned the officer.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

The elders' circle declares the tribute paid by Bethua's own hand. Mbarelo cannot carry the circle against Thumbel's herders, and his consent fails. Young riders of the western clans raid the edge of the irrigated strips in the dry months, the levy women at the posts answer, and the raids cross the frontier both ways. The Hégissa of the southern cantons reports a refusal, the Court of the Veil cannot press the clans while the grain question is open, and the Spear-Sisterhood draws more patrols back to the line. Argirira sells Thumbel's herd east and holds more of the road than before.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

If the herd is found and the tribute delivered, Mbarelo keeps his place, the circle owes the party water at its wells, and the posts know who carried the truth across the line. Argirira knows it too. The Jackals lose a season's sale and gain a grudge, and the clans learn, quietly, that the Houseless raid a marcher well in Bethua's name. That knowledge sits in the circle for a long time and changes what the Dorumbe hire the Jackals to do. If the party bought the herd from Argirira, the frontier holds, and the Court of the Veil's frontier is bought back from the band it exiled.
