## Groa, the Seid of Malagna

### Campaign Overview:

[[being-grosdrnrgd|Groa the Seid of Norgaad]]’s vision of the future is one of three interconnected threats: **internal strife** within the [[affiliation-kingdomlgn|Kingdom of Malagna]], **foreign invasions**, and, most urgently, the rise of [[being-njorven|Njorven]], the Sea Wraith. As the kingdom spirals into chaos, with rival factions vying for power and external enemies closing in, Njorven’s influence seeps back into the land, stirring violent storms and awakening ancient curses. Groa and her companions—though initially minor figures—will rise as protectors of the common folk, navigating a path through these tensions and facing the Sea Wraith’s growing power.

This is a campaign where wisdom, diplomacy, and strength of character are valued over brute magical force. While Groa’s Seid powers and her companions' skills will play important roles, the solutions to problems will often involve non-magical strategies. The internal political instability, foreign threats, and the plight of the people will shape much of the campaign.

### The Three Great Challenges

Internal Strife
: Malagna is teetering on the edge of civil war. [[being-knghlrthni|King Hlurthann III]] is aging and ineffective, and various factions within the kingdom, including powerful clans like the [[affiliation-hrafnvar|Hrafnvar]] and [[affiliation-grimvar|Grimvar]], vie for dominance. Groa and her companions find themselves in a kingdom where the real threat is not only the ancient sea spirit, but also the unraveling of their homeland’s social fabric.

Foreign Invasions
: In addition to internal turmoil, foreign threats loom on Malagna’s borders. [[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Kingdom of Nordheim]] and the [[affiliation-kingdmtrgd|Kingdom of Targud]], emboldened by Malagna’s political weakness, launch raids along the coast. Villages are left defenseless, and the kingdom’s military is too fractured to protect the people. Groa and her companions will need to confront these invaders, rally the common folk, and defend their homeland against these external dangers.

Njorven’s Rise
: The ancient Sea Wraith, Njorven, is beginning to stir from his watery prison. His wrath is manifesting in destructive storms, and his influence grows as the seas become more perilous. However, Njorven’s awakening is not only a spiritual threat—it is also a political one, as some coastal clans begin questioning their allegiance to [[lore-thrunvalddty|Thrunvald]], the god whom those clans hold to have once vanquished Njorven. Groa’s mission to retrieve the three relics and stop Njorven’s rise is the overarching quest, but much of the campaign will be about dealing with the tangible effects of his influence.

## Campaign Structure:

### Act I: The Gathering Storm

This section focuses on establishing the political and social instability within Malagna and setting up Groa and her companions as protectors of the people.

Internal Tensions
: Early on, Groa and her companions will find themselves caught in the crossfire of Malagna’s political factions. The royal clan, once dominant, is fractured by infighting, with several powerful heirs vying for the throne. Groa must navigate these tensions carefully, as different factions try to pull her into their schemes. She and her companions are primarily concerned with the well-being of the common folk, often finding themselves opposing local lords who are too focused on power struggles to defend their people.

Foreign Raids
: Villages along the coast are regularly attacked by invaders from Nordheim and Targud. In one pivotal moment, [[being-hlrthsldhrngsdtr|Hlirthselda Hringsdottir]] will lead the defense of a small village, rallying the villagers to fight back against overwhelming odds. Her successful defense will not only earn her and Groa valuable allies but also show the common people that they are champions of the people. This victory will lead to unforeseen assistance from one of the coastal clans later in the campaign.

### Act II: The Quest for the Relics

Groa’s visions tell her of the three ancient relics—[[miscgear-sprsigrid|The Spear of Skraldthira]], [[miscgear-crwnwyrm|The Crown of the Wyrm]], and [[miscgear-hornnjordur|The Horn of Hafvald]]—needed to seal away Njorven. Each relic presents unique challenges, and retrieving them will require more than magical power; it will demand diplomacy, strategy, and careful decision-making.

The Spear of Skraldthira
: This relic is hidden within an ancient shrine, guarded by a powerful **spirit guardian**. To gain the Spear, Groa must confront this guardian not through battle but by journeying into the spirit world. There, she will face trials that test her wisdom, wit, and resolve. The spirit world will reflect not only the physical realm but also the deep fears and desires of Groa and her companions. Solving these trials will unlock the Spear, but brute strength will lead to failure.

Hlirthselda’s Role in the Defense of the Realm
: After defending the village, Hlirthselda’s influence among the coastal clans grows. When Groa and her companions need support on their quest, Hlirthselda’s new allies from the village defense will come to their aid, offering ships, supplies, or warriors to help the group continue their journey. This assistance will be crucial in navigating the dangers posed by foreign raiders and the storm-wracked seas as they pursue the other relics.

### Act III: The Rising Threat of Njorven

As Groa and her companions acquire more relics, Njorven’s influence grows stronger, and Malagna’s internal divisions deepen. The kingdom’s fractured clans are slow to react, more concerned with their political rivalries than with the looming supernatural threat.

Negotiating with Factions
: Groa will have to interact with the major factions in Malagna—perhaps brokering a fragile alliance between rival clans in order to muster a defense against Njorven’s influence. Some factions will oppose her outright, while others may try to manipulate the situation to their own advantage. Groa and her companions must tread carefully, knowing that any misstep could result in disaster.

The Growing Influence of Njorven’s Cult
: As Njorven’s power grows, some coastal clans begin to abandon their faith in Thrunvald, turning instead to the ancient sea spirit. Members of [[affiliation-njorvencult|Njorven's Cult]] actively work to undermine Groa’s efforts, spreading chaos along the coast. Groa’s group will need to expose and dismantle this cult while convincing the people to resist Njorven’s allure.

### Act IV: The Final Showdown

The final act sees the kingdom on the brink of collapse. Civil war looms, foreign invaders press in from the borders, and Njorven’s full power is about to be unleashed.

The Ritual of Binding
: With the three relics in hand, Groa must perform [[lore-njordurritlbinding|The Ritual of Binding]], an ancient rite, to seal Njorven away. The ritual must be performed at a specific location—perhaps a hidden coastal temple—but it will require both spiritual and material preparation. Groa will need to ensure that her companions can protect her during the ritual while also dealing with the external threats of rival factions, invaders, and Njorven’s minions.

A Non-Magical Solution
: Though magic will play a role in the final battle, the ultimate solution to sealing Njorven away will rely on Groa’s knowledge, strategy, and diplomacy. Perhaps convincing a key faction to support her, or finding an ancient piece of lore hidden in the archives of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]], will turn the tide of the battle.

## Key Themes and Non-Magical Elements

The People Over Politics
: Groa and her companions care more about the people than about which noble ultimately sits on the throne. This detachment from the political scheming will give them moral authority and allow them to rally the common folk, positioning them as protectors of the realm’s future rather than the aristocracy.

Diplomacy and Strategy Over Brute Force
: While the relics are important, the campaign will emphasize that brute force or excessive reliance on magic is often the wrong approach. Negotiating alliances, outsmarting political rivals, and using wits to overcome supernatural and human threats will be central to success.

Hlirthselda’s Rising Leadership
: Hlirthselda’s early defense of a village will be pivotal, as it cements her as a leader among the coastal people. This new leadership role allows her to gain important allies, which will help the group in their later quest, showing how individual acts of bravery and defense can ripple through the campaign's story.

Consequences of Internal Strife
: The chaos within Malagna is a crucial backdrop to the campaign. Groa’s quest for the relics must proceed in a kingdom at war with itself, and her success will depend on how well she and her companions manage the various tensions that arise from this political instability.
