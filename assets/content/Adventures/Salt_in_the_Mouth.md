At dawn a rider of the [[affiliation-mrjlrtrb|Mirjolur]]'s younger horde is found dead at a salt cutting on the western rim of [[place-ewod|Evod]], his mouth and throat packed with salt. The younger horde says only the [[affiliation-khldrtrb|Khilodur]] kill that way, and its head wants blood before the riders of a dovaq gathering the steppe under his banner reach the rim. The Khilodur say nobody kills that way, and they expect to be blamed whatever the truth is. Nobody killed him. He went to the stair his horde found under the crust, against his elders' word, and the crust gave way under him. His companion carried him to the cutting and left him there, and that companion is the son of the man calling for blood.

## Overview {#overview}

The adventure runs over three days on the rim of Evod, in the [[place-hskrrgn|Ghoshiqor]], from the morning the body is found to the moot at which the Mirjolur speaker hears the case. Its clock is the banner: the gathering man's riders are expected at the younger horde's camp on the third evening, and [Orqazjilaq Mirjolur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, head of the younger horde, means to meet them with the Khilodur driven off the western rim and the stair his to give.

This is a quarrel between two tribes and three hordes, not a murder with a culprit to unmask. The party can find out how the rider died, and finding out does not end the quarrel. Every faction has something it wants that the truth helps or hurts, and the moot on the third day decides what is done with it.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

Ten days ago a salt-cutter's son of the younger horde put his pick through the crust on a canal bank and broke into a dry stair going down. The horde's elders told no one outside and forbade anyone to go near it until Orqazjilaq decided what to do with it. Orqazjilaq has decided: the stair is a gift for the gathering man, worth the whole western rim to the horde that gives it.

Two young riders could not wait. Two nights ago [Yovaqtoliz Mirjolur]{.name data-type="being/npc"} and Orqazdovaq, Orqazjilaq's son, made the salt-cutter's son take them to the foot of the canal bank by dark. The boy would go no farther, and the two followed the bank out onto the flat with a lamp to see the stair and bring something up from it. Short of the stair mouth Yovaqtoliz stepped off the canal bank onto crust laid over brine and mud, broke through and went under. Orqazdovaq dragged him out dead, his mouth and throat full of brine that dried to salt by morning, and hauled him back along the bank in the dark. By daylight he could not have found the place again, and the boy is still the only one who can. Afraid to say where they had been, he roped the body to his horse, dragged it off the flat to the nearest cutting, which the Khilodur salt-lot gave to a Khilodur household three springs ago, and left it there.

Orqazjilaq does not know his son was on the flat. He knows that a dead rider at a Khilodur cutting gives him a feud, and that a feud gives him the western rim. Mirjoltoliz, the dovaq and head of the elder horde, wants no feud and no banner, and suspects the younger horde of wanting both. [Iqodlun Khilodur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Khilodur dovaq, wants her tribe out of the quarrel and into the union before it is decided without them.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The death is an accident and a lie, and the lie is a frightened boy's, not a plot. Play Orqazdovaq as a young man who could not bring himself to say where he had been, and whose silence has grown into a feud he cannot stop. His father's case is honest, and wrong.

### How the reveal lands

The truth is complete when the party knows that Yovaqtoliz drowned at the stair and that Orqazdovaq brought him to the cutting. It lands in the open only if someone says it at the moot in Scene Five, and that is a choice:

- **Said at the moot.** The speaker hears it, and the younger horde's case falls. Orqazjilaq is shamed before the three hordes, the stair is known to every tribe on the rim by nightfall, and the Khilodur owe the party.
- **Said to Orqazjilaq alone.** He pays for the party's silence and withdraws his case before the speaker. The stair stays the younger horde's gift, the feud quiets, and the Khilodur never learn why.
- **Said to Mirjoltoliz alone.** The old dovaq uses it to break Orqazjilaq's hold on the younger horde, and asks the party to say it at the moot.
- **Never said.** The speaker decides on what the moot hears, and the moot hears a dead rider at a Khilodur cutting.

> [!TIP]
> Do not let the reveal end the adventure. The party's real decision is who hears the truth first, and the scenes give every faction a reason to want it first or want it buried.

### What the party wants afterward

Once the party knows, the stair is the prize and the truth is the price of it. Orqazjilaq pays for silence; Mirjoltoliz pays for the moot; Iqodlun has nothing to pay with and the most to lose. Orqazdovaq, if the party speaks with him alone, wants to be the one who says it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The thief.** A buyer at [[place-qisomrktcmp|Qiiso]] has heard that a salt-cutter's boy on the rim broke into something under the crust, and pays the party to reach it before the tribe opens it to anyone else.
- **The mercenary or common soldier.** The younger horde hires the party to carry its demand for blood to the Khilodur well at Iqod, because no Mirjolur can ride there and come back.
- **The leader.** Mirjoltoliz wants to know what the younger horde found before the gathering man does, and hires a party whose faces his own riders do not know.
- **The lone wanderer.** The wanderer is a guest at Iqod, salt eaten and owed protection to the edge of the Khilodur country, when the younger horde's demand arrives.

## The Opening {#opening}

Read or paraphrase:

> You reach the cutting at first light, with the Mirjolur riders. The rim falls away below you to the salt, white to the edge of the world, and the cutting is a long bite out of the shore with slabs stacked beside it ready for camels that did not come. The dead man lies on his back on the slabs, his arms at his sides. His mouth is open, and it is full of salt.
>
> Across the cutting, on the dune, six Khilodur riders sit their horses, pale-haired, saying nothing. Nobody on either side has drawn. Everybody is holding a hilt.

> [!TIP]
> Start with the stand-off, not the investigation. The first question the table must answer is how not to start a fight over the body.

## Scene One: The Cutting

**Purpose.** Stand-off and first clues. Put the party between two tribes over a body, and let it look at the body before anyone takes it away.

**Situation.** Orqazjilaq's riders found the body at dawn and sent for him. Khilodur riders saw them from the dune and came down to see what the Mirjolur were doing at a Khilodur cutting. Orqazjilaq arrives within the hour and calls it murder in front of both.

**NPCs and threats.**

- **Orqazjilaq Mirjolur**, head of the younger horde, forty-five, hard and plain-spoken. He says the salt in the mouth is a Khilodur killing, says it to the Khilodur riders' faces, and calls them the builders' kin.
- **Six Khilodur riders**, young, under an elder who tells them to hold. They say nobody kills that way and that this cutting is not theirs this spring.
- **Mirjolur riders** of the younger horde, angry and ready.

**Player choices.**

- _Force:_ back one side down. Either side fights if the other draws first, and a death here makes the feud real.
- _Parley:_ get both sides to agree that the body is looked at before it is moved. The Khilodur elder agrees at once; Orqazjilaq agrees if the party is not Khilodur kin.
- _Cleverness:_ examine the body while the two sides argue.
- _Giving something up:_ offer to carry the body to the younger horde's camp, so that the Mirjolur leave the Khilodur cutting with it.

**Clues and rewards.** The salt in the mouth and throat is packed, not placed. Black mud under the nails and in the hair, where the rim has none. Boots and trousers crusted white to the waist. No wound, his knife sheathed. A furrow down the dune from the direction of the flat, with hoof-marks beside it. The cutting's slabs are old, stacked last year, and nobody has cut there this spring.

**Consequences.** The body goes to the younger horde's camp with the party or without it. Orqazjilaq sends a demand for blood to Iqod and names the third day for the speaker's moot. The gathering man's riders are expected the same evening.

**If bypassed, failed or cut short.** A party that comes late finds the body already at the younger horde's camp, where the women are washing it and the salt is gone from the mouth; the mud and the white boots are still there. A fight at the cutting leaves a Khilodur dead, and Iqodlun's moot in Scene Two is about vengeance as well as the demand.

## Scene Two: Iqod

**Purpose.** The other side. Show the Khilodur's case and their fear, and give the party the salt-lot.

**Situation.** The Khilodur moot sits at [Iqod]{.name data-type="place/site"}, its chief well, in a hollow of the southern dunes. The younger horde's demand has arrived: the killer's life or the western cuttings. Iqodlun wants neither, and she wants to send riders to the gathering man before the quarrel is decided for her.

**NPCs and threats.**

- **Iqodlun Khilodur**, the dovaq, a woman in her thirties, the youngest dovaq on the central sands. She says that her tribe killed no one, that her tribe is blamed for everything on the rim, and that a quarrel with the Mirjolur is the quarrel the Khilodur cannot afford.
- **The lot-keeper**, an old woman who draws the salt-lot each spring. She has the year's lot by heart.
- **The elders**, who want to stay silent and give the Mirjolur nothing.

**Player choices.**

- _Parley:_ ask for the salt-lot; ask why the Khilodur are said to kill that way.
- _Cleverness:_ ask the lot-keeper how a drowned man looks. She has pulled cutters out of the brine pans and knows.
- _Force or threat:_ carry the younger horde's demand as given. The moot hears it in silence and gives the party a guest's meal before an answer.

**Clues and rewards.** The lot-keeper says the cutting where the body lay fell to no Khilodur household this spring, and nobody of hers has been near it. Asked about a man with brine in him, she says the brine dries white on the skin and fills the mouth like salt, and that the mud of the flat is black. Iqodlun's offer: if the party can show the moot what happened, the Khilodur speak for the party at Qiiso for as long as she is dovaq.

**Consequences.** The Khilodur refuse the demand. Iqodlun sends two riders north to look for the gathering man's camp, and the elders do not stop her.

**If bypassed, failed or cut short.** A party that never comes to Iqod meets the lot-keeper at the moot in Scene Five, where she says what she knows in front of everyone.

## Scene Three: The Younger Horde's Camp

**Purpose.** The other half of the truth. Bring the party into the camp that is mourning the dead man, among the people who know more than they say.

**Situation.** The younger horde's camp lies at its wells a day back from the salt. Yovaqtoliz is laid out for the laying-out ground, and his kin are keening. Orqazjilaq is making ready for the gathering man's riders. The salt-cutter's son and Orqazdovaq are both in the camp.

**NPCs and threats.**

- **The salt-cutter's son**, eleven, frightened of everyone. He broke into the stair and has been told not to speak of it.
- **Orqazdovaq Mirjolur**, Orqazjilaq's son, nineteen. He has not slept, his hands are cracked raw, and he will not go near the body.
- **Orqazdovaq's mother**, who knows her son came home at dawn two days ago, soaked white to the waist, and has said nothing.

**Player choices.**

- _Parley:_ the salt-cutter's son talks to anyone kind to him, out of sight of the men. He says he took two riders to the foot of the canal bank by night and came home alone, and names them.
- _Cleverness:_ watch Orqazdovaq at the keening; ask about his hands.
- _Pressure:_ confront Orqazdovaq. In front of others he denies everything and leaves the tent. Alone, he breaks.
- _Giving something up:_ offer the salt-cutter's son something he wants (a knife, a promise his father will not beat him) for the way to the stair.

**Clues and rewards.** The two names. Orqazdovaq's hands. His mother's silence, which she keeps unless the party has already found the second track at the stair. The way to the stair, from the boy.

**Consequences.** If Orqazdovaq breaks, he begs the party to let him tell it himself at the moot, and does not know how. If he does not, he rides out at night to find the stair again, to bring something up from it and make his silence worth something, and he cannot find it.

**If bypassed, failed or cut short.** A party that never comes to the camp meets the salt-cutter's son on the rim in Scene Four, sent by his father to show strangers the place, because his father wants to know whether the boy was lying.

## Scene Four: The Stair Mouth

**Purpose.** The place. Take the party out onto the crust, show it the stair and the drowning place, and give it the tracks.

**Situation.** The stair lies half a day out along a canal bank from the nearest cutting, where the bank runs straight out of the dunes and under the salt. The bank holds; the crust beside it does not. The salt-cutter's shirt still covers the hole he made. A few paces short of it, the crust is broken through in a ragged pit of black mud, and a lamp lies half sunk at its edge.

**NPCs and threats.**

- **The crust.** Off the bank it is a skin an inch thick over brine and mud. A character who steps off goes through to the waist at once and deeper if he struggles; a character on the bank can haul him out with a rope or a pole.
- **Orqazdovaq**, if he rode out the night before, on the flat in the glare, off the bank and lost, a step from the crust that took his friend.
- **The glare.** From mid-morning the mirage takes the far bank and the dunes, and a party that leaves the bank to cut across loses its way back.

**Player choices.**

- _Cleverness:_ read the tracks along the bank before the wind takes them.
- _Caution:_ rope together and keep to the bank.
- _Daring:_ lift the shirt and look into the stair.
- _Rescue:_ bring Orqazdovaq back to the bank, and hear it from him.

**Clues and rewards.** Two sets of footprints out along the bank and one coming back beside a furrow, and hoof-marks where a horse waited at the bank's foot. The broken pit, black mud thrown out on the crust, and the lamp. Under the shirt, a hole into a stair of baked brick going down into the dark, dry, its walls faced near the top with glazed tiles in blue and green, its lower steps beyond the reach of a lamp.

> [!NOTE]
> The stair is a work of [[lore-basinbldrs|the Basin Builders]], and it may be described: dry brick, glazed tile, steps going down. This adventure does not go below the first landing. What lies at the foot of the stair is left for the referee, and the quarrel above it does not depend on it.

**Consequences.** A party that brings something up from the stair has a gift worth the western rim in its hands, and every faction learns of it by the moot.

**If bypassed, failed or cut short.** A party that never reaches the stair can still put the death together from the body, the lot-keeper and Orqazdovaq; it reaches the moot without the tracks, and Orqazjilaq says the party is guessing.

## Scene Five: The Speaker's Moot

**Purpose.** The decision. Bring every faction to one place and make the party choose what is said there.

**Situation.** On the third day the speaker of the Mirjolur, [Kodubeyuk Mirjolur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, hears the younger horde's case on the rim above the cutting, with Mirjoltoliz and the heads of the three hordes seated and the Khilodur under Iqodlun across the cutting. The gathering man's riders are a dust cloud to the north by noon.

**NPCs and threats.**

- **Kodubeyuk Mirjolur**, the speaker, an old man chosen for the force and justice of his words. He hears anyone the moot lets speak, outsiders included, and his judgment binds the three hordes.
- **Mirjoltoliz Mirjolur**, the dovaq, past seventy, who wants no feud and no banner.
- **Orqazjilaq**, who asks for blood or the western cuttings.
- **Iqodlun** and the Khilodur elders.
- **The gathering man's riders**, who arrive before the judgment and sit their horses on the dune to watch.

**Player choices.**

- _Speak:_ put the truth before the speaker, in front of everyone.
- _Bargain:_ sell the truth to one side before the moot sits, and keep silent or speak as paid.
- _Protect:_ let Orqazdovaq say it himself, if he can.
- _Force:_ nothing at a moot is settled by force without the speaker's word, and a party that draws has made itself a feud with three hordes.

**Clues and rewards.** None left to find. The reward is the judgment, and whose debt it leaves the party in.

**Consequences.** The speaker's judgment, by what he has heard:

- **The truth told.** No blood is owed. The younger horde owes the Khilodur a payment for the name it called them, which Orqazjilaq pays or refuses. The stair is known to all.
- **The truth bought silent.** Orqazjilaq withdraws his case. The speaker finds no killer and sets no payment, and the feud over the dead man quiets without the Khilodur learning why.
- **Nothing found.** The speaker gives the younger horde the western cuttings and a feud.

The gathering man's riders leave with whatever the moot gave them: a stair, a feud they can settle in their man's name, or nothing.

**If bypassed, failed or cut short.** If the party is not at the moot, the speaker hears what the moot brings him, and the feud follows.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

- The body is laid out, and the salt in its mouth is the only evidence anyone repeats.
- On the third day the speaker gives the younger horde the western cuttings, and the Khilodur refuse to leave them. The first killing of the feud follows within the season.
- Orqazjilaq meets the gathering man's riders with the stair and the western rim, and the younger horde swears under the banner. The elder horde does not, and the three hordes are three answers in the open.
- Orqazdovaq goes out onto the flat alone to find the stair again and does not come back.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

- **If the truth was told at the moot.** Orqazjilaq loses the younger horde's trust and keeps its headship for now. Iqodlun has a place at Qiiso she did not have. The Mirjolur and the Khilodur are still at feud over the western cuttings, but nobody owes blood for Yovaqtoliz.
- **If the party sold its silence.** It has Orqazjilaq's silver, and it owes the younger horde the silence the horde bought. The Khilodur never learn why the case was dropped, and would pay to hear it.
- **If the stair is known.** Every tribe on the rim, the buyers at Qiiso and the gathering man's riders want it opened, and the Mirjolur and the Khilodur both claim the bank it lies under.
- **If Orqazdovaq lives.** He owes the party his life or his name, and he has stood at the stair mouth.
