Ancient ruins in the borderlands hold secrets from before human memory, guarded by the reclusive [[skill-aelendlng|Áelendi]] tribes and their [[lore-flksinale|Sinalë]] allies. The artifacts within could reshape Provenzian power—if the party can uncover them without awakening what guards them.

## Overview

Deep in the borderlands between [[place-provenzrgn|Provènzia Region]] and [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], the crumbling ruins of Sirmennë stand—a sacred site shared between the [[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]] (the reclusive human tribes who are the original people of Élavendre) and the [[lore-flksinale|Sinalë]] who dwell in the hidden forests nearby. Sirmennë predates any Provenzian or even Élavendren settlement by centuries, and its purpose remains a mystery to outsiders. Local legends speak of artifacts of incredible power left behind when the Sinalë withdrew deeper into their enclaves. The party is contracted to explore the ruins, document findings, and retrieve anything of value. But the Áelendan have guarded this place for generations, and the Sinalë did not leave their creations unprotected.

## Stage One: The Commission & Preparation

The party is hired by:

- A scholar or antiquarian seeking knowledge of the ancients
- A noble seeking to expand Provenzian prestige and power
- The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]], claiming authority to study arcane artifacts
- A mysterious patron with their own hidden agenda

**Initial Information:**

- Sirmennë lies in remote, difficult terrain (mountains, forests, cursed lands)
- Previous expeditions have vanished or returned traumatized and empty-handed
- The Áelendan tribes fiercely protect the site and refuse all outsiders
- The ruins are said to be warded by ancient magic far older than the Ordo's traditions
- An artifact recovered centuries ago caused unforeseen consequences for whoever possessed it

**NPCs:**

- The hiring authority (with unstated expectations)
- A scholar with partial translations of ancient inscriptions
- An Áelendan elder who is deeply opposed to the expedition
- A survivor of a previous expedition (if any exist)

## Stage Two: The Journey

The party travels to Sirmennë, facing challenges both natural and political:

- Difficult terrain: mountain passes, dense forest, cursed valleys
- Encounters with Áelendan scouts who challenge, warn, or outright attack trespassers
- Environmental hazards: cave-ins, unstable cliffs, magical phenomena
- Warnings from the land itself—visions, whispers, time distortions
- The Áelendan may attempt to misdirect, delay, or frighten the party into turning back
- If the party earns Áelendan trust (a difficult feat), they may receive cryptic guidance instead

## Stage Three: Approaching the Ruins

Upon arrival at Sirmennë, the party discovers:

- Buildings of elegant, impossible architecture partially reclaimed by nature—clearly not built by human hands
- Inscriptions in an ancient script (decipherable with effort or magical aid, possibly requiring Áelendi or Sinalë knowledge)
- Evidence of deliberate sealing—doorways blocked, entrances barred
- Lingering magical wards that react to intrusion
- Signs that the Áelendan maintain the outer grounds as a sacred precinct

**Initial Encounters:**

- Activation of dormant magical defenses (illusions, barriers, minor constructs)
- Sinalë-created guardians (golems, animated sentinels) responding to trespass
- An ancient spirit bound to the site that manifests to challenge or question the intruders
- Environmental hazards (crumbling structures, magical energy flux)

## Stage Four: Exploration & Discovery

The party must navigate the ruins, overcome obstacles, and locate the artifact(s). Key elements include:

- **Puzzle Chambers:** Tests of wit, morality, or worthiness designed by the builders
- **Guardian Constructs:** Magical beings created to preserve and protect the ruins
- **Layered Mysteries:** Understanding the shared Áelendi-Sinalë history and the purpose of this place
- **The Artifact:** What the party seeks may be dangerous, cursed, or far more significant than expected
- **A Final Guardian:** Perhaps an ancient Sinalë spirit, or a powerful Áelendi shaman bound to the site by oath

**Significant Discoveries:**

- The Sinalë built Sirmennë in partnership with the Áelendan, not as conquerors
- They withdrew deliberately, entrusting the site's protection to the Áelendan tribes
- An ancient threat prompted the withdrawal—a threat that may still be relevant
- The artifact sought carries consequences: power and danger in equal measure

## Stage Five: Aftermath & Complications

**If the party succeeds:**

- The artifact is secured (but what to do with it remains the question)
- Knowledge of the ancient partnership between Áelendi and Sinalë is recovered
- The party may have awakened attention from the Sinalë themselves (who may not appreciate disturbance)
- Possession of the artifact brings interest from the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]], foreign powers, and hidden factions
- The Áelendan tribes' reaction depends on how the party treated them and what was disturbed

**Consequences of the Artifact:**

- It may enhance or expand Provenzian power significantly
- It could cause unforeseen harm if used or studied carelessly
- It might grant the party incredible advantages or dangerous curses
- Other powers may pursue the party to claim, destroy, or study it
- The awakening of ancient magic may trigger larger consequences in the region

**If the party fails:**

- The ruins seal themselves again, or become more dangerous
- The artifact remains hidden, potentially to be claimed by others
- The Áelendi and their Sinalë allies become hostile to all future expeditions
- The party may be bound by oath or curse to never return
