## Overview {#overview}

[[place-kyvarel|Kivarel]] is a barony in the western valleys of the Zuravlen seaboard, held with the [[affiliation-brnyzrvln|Barony of Zuravlen]] by [[being-selvarezrvl|Selvare Zuravel]] from his hall above [[place-elthrembis|Eltrembis]]. Its best land is the [[place-bldfldkyvarel|Blood-field of Kivarel]], a quickening where the ground grows too well: grass comes weeks early, grain comes in heavy and first, and fruit sets in years when the rest of the district loses its blossom to frost. The tenants who inherited the holdings farm it thoroughly and do not talk about it. A stranger who raises it at table is answered politely once and not answered again.

The field's silence is under strain. A factor from the southern ports has traced the barony's earliest and largest fruit to the field and means to sell it under a name. The priests of [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]] have asked a third time to perform the rite of settlement. The baron's steward has told both that the field is the tenants' business. The tenants want the field farmed through this harvest and the next without anyone asking why the fruit is so large, and they will pay a party a season's wages to see that nobody does.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The debt.** The field's tenants have no drying yards of their own. They dry their crop in the baron's yards at Eltrembis and pay the share, and three frosts in five years on their other holdings have left them owing the yards more than one harvest can pay, and at the share they owe it will take this year's harvest and next year's to clear it. [[being-trlmzdms|Tarlema Zedimis]], who speaks for the tenant families, has agreed with them that when the debt is clear they will give the priests of Sélmoros their answer, and that the answer will be yes. Nobody knows whether settling a quickened field ends the quickening, and the tenants cannot afford to find out until they owe nothing.

**The factor.** [[being-lvnslkms|Lauvina Solkimis]], factor of a Válaren house, has seen what the field's fruit fetches in the south and wants it sold under its own mark, at a price that would pay the tenants' debt in one year. She does not care that the field is a battlefield. Her mark would make the field's name known on every table in Válaren, and the field a byword with it.

**The baron.** Selvare wants the question kept quiet, because a baron whose best land is a battlefield is a gift to his rivals at Hárivren. He is a patron of the [[affiliation-brnngbrnd|Order of the Burning Brand]], which stands with the [[affiliation-sacredforge|Sacred Forge]], and the Forge's petition to take the western march from the Black Flame earl lies before the Council of Peers. His steward, [[being-plvzornts|Palveze Ornetis]], is keeping the factor and the priests at arm's length and would quietly forgive part of the tenants' debt to keep the field out of anyone's mouth, if the baron let him.

**Hárivren.** [[being-avaarlzlvrn|Avaarl Zilavren]], Earl of Hárivren and the most senior Flame lord in the kingdom, has a factor in Eltrembis for the tolls. The earl's people have heard of the field. A Burning Brand baron whose best fruit grows on a blood-field, sold under a famous name, would answer the Forge's charges against the earl at the Council with laughter. The earl's factor means to see that the field's name reaches Jalnasdal, and he is offering Lauvina a better road south than the baron's if she uses it.

**The priests.** The priests of Sélmoros want the field settled because the dead in it were not judged in order. They are candid that the rite works perhaps half the time. They do not know about the tenants' agreement.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The field harms nobody, and nobody in the scenario is a villain. It ends at the fair, when the harvest is dried and sold, with the field's name kept or spread and the tenants' debt paid or not.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The wanderer.** Tarlema Zedimis hires a party to see the field through this harvest without a factor, a priest or a Peer asking why the fruit is so large.
- **The leader.** Selvare Zuravel asks a party that can turn away questions politely to keep the field out of anyone's mouth until after the fair.
- **The merchant.** Lauvina Solkimis hires a party to escort her through the western valleys and to witness the field's yield, so that her mark cannot be called a lie.
- **The faith.** A priest of Sélmoros asks a party to carry the temple's third request to the tenants and stand by while they answer.

## The Opening {#opening}

The edge of the field at Kivarel in the first week of harvest. The fruit on its trees is the size of a child's head, the grass under them is green while every other field in the valley is brown, and the tenants work in silence. A woman in a factor's good linen stands at the hedge with a clerk, counting baskets.

> "Every grower in Ankaris would sell his mother for this ground," she says. "Why won't anyone tell me what it is?"

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Field

**Purpose.** Show the field and the people who farm it.

**Situation.** The tenant holdings on the [[place-bldfldkyvarel|Blood-field of Kivarel]] at harvest, the fruit coming in, the carts going to the yards at Eltrembis. Nobody works the field after dark.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-trlmzdms|Tarlema Zedimis]], weathered and blunt with everyone but a stranger. The tenant families, who answer a question about the field by talking about the yield. Lauvina's clerk, counting.

**Player choices.** Help with the harvest, ask about the field, watch the factor's clerk, or ask why nobody stays after dark.

**Clues and rewards.** A tenant who trusts the party says the debt to the yards is the whole of the matter. The clerk's tally is honest and will be in Válaren within the month.

**Consequences.** The tenants decide whether the party can be trusted with the field's silence.

**If bypassed.** Lauvina's tally goes south, and the field's yield is known in Válaren before the fair.

### Scene Two: Eltrembis

**Purpose.** The town, the steward, and the offers.

**Situation.** [[place-elthrembis|Eltrembis]] at harvest: the grading table, the drying yards working by night, the market hall with the water-rolls above it, the room where the priests of Sélmoros lodge when they come down the valley, and the earl's toll office.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-plvzornts|Palveze Ornetis]], who wants the field kept out of every mouth and asks what the party is being paid. A priest of Sélmoros, patient, who wants an answer. [[being-lvnslkms|Lauvina Solkimis]], who offers the party a share of her mark. The earl's factor, who offers her a road south that avoids the baron's yards.

**Player choices.** Bargain with the steward over the tenants' debt, persuade the priests to wait, warn Lauvina off, or learn what the earl's factor wants.

**Clues and rewards.** The yards' rolls show the tenants' debt and how much of it two harvests clear. The earl's factor, drunk, says the field's name would be worth more at Jalnasdal than any toll.

**Consequences.** If the steward forgives part of the debt, the tenants can answer the priests this year. If the earl's factor and the factor make their bargain, the field's name goes south by the earl's road.

**If bypassed.** The steward hears of the earl's factor too late.

### Scene Three: The Road to Hárivren

**Purpose.** The earl's interest, stopped or not.

**Situation.** The road out of the western valleys toward [[place-harivrencity|Hárivren City]], where Lauvina's first carts of the field's fruit go under the earl's seal with a letter that names the field.

**NPCs and threats.** The carters, who know nothing. The earl's riders, who escort the carts. Lauvina, if she has gone with them.

**Player choices.** Stop the letter, let it go, persuade Lauvina to sell without the field's name, or carry a letter of the baron's own to Hárivren.

**Clues and rewards.** The letter, addressed to a secretary of the earl at Jalnasdal, describes the field and the baron's patronage of the Burning Brand side by side.

**Consequences.** A letter stopped keeps the field out of the Council for a year. A letter delivered puts it there.

**If bypassed.** The letter reaches Jalnasdal.

### Scene Four: The Fair

**Purpose.** The resolution.

**Situation.** The eight days of the autumn fair at Eltrembis. The year's rents, debts and disputes are settled at the manor courts, and the factors buy the dried fruit.

**NPCs and threats.** The baron, the steward, the tenants, the factor and the priests, all in one town for eight days.

**Player choices.** Settle the tenants' debt, let the factor sell under her mark, bring the priests to the field, or leave it all as it was.

**Clues and rewards.** If the debt is cleared, Tarlema gives the priests the tenants' answer at the fair, and it is yes.

**Consequences.** The rite, performed after the fair, ends the quickening or does not. Either way the tenants owe nothing.

**If bypassed.** The tenants pay what they can, the debt rolls over and the priests wait another year.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

Lauvina's mark goes south by the earl's road, and the field's fruit is famous in Válaren by midwinter, with the field's story attached. At the Council of Peers the earl's friends ask whether the Forge's champions dry their fruit on battlefields, and the Forge's petition against the earl is laughed out of the year's sittings. Selvare blames his steward, the tenants' debt rolls over, and the priests are refused a fourth time.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

A party that saw the field through the harvest has the tenants' trust, which is the field's silence shared. The baron owes it, and the steward knows exactly how much. Whatever the priests' rite does to the field, the barony has to decide what its best land is worth without it, and a party that kept the field's name out of Jalnasdal has done the Forge's side of the Council a service it will not hear about.
