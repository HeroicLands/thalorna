## Overview {#overview}

[[affiliation-provincvld|Salkalon]]'s three powers want different things from Taruzel, and none can afford its wish. Its merchants want a trade treaty with the Confederation. Its admiralty at [[place-castamar|Kastamar]], under the _vormeks_ [[being-tharkasgurim|Tharkas Gurimandis]], wants the war resumed and cannot pay for the fleet it has. Its customs are farmed to a man in debt to the islands. Across the strait the Taruzelian admiral [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]] presses for a League toll on every hull that passes under [[place-straitfortress|the Strait Fortress]]. The party is drawn in on the Vilérian coast by a widow's question, a merchant's petition or an admiral's suspicion, and the trail crosses the strait to the roads under the fortress, where both navies' secrets lie at anchor.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The two hulls.** Three years ago the Senate voted the money for four new galleys at Kastamar and the treasury paid for two. The master shipwright, [Kaldremos Holimandis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, had laid down all four, and the admiral would not see the yard idle. Tharkas Gurimandis let him finish the two unpaid hulls and sell them without fittings to a Helionite captain who said he was hiring out to the hunt for privateers. The Helionite was buying for the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]]. Both hulls were fitted out at the Naval Yards of [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]] and now row in the League's convoys under the fortress. The Roll of the Fleet still carries them in Kastamar's sheds, under names they never bore. The admiral does not know where they went. Kaldremos suspects, because the Helionite paid in Taruzelian Solden and asked for oars of a League pattern, and has said nothing, because the sale was his idea.

**Who wants it known.** If the Senate learns of the sale, the admiral who wants war with Taruzel stands as the admiral who armed it, and the war party in Orzara loses its best voice. The shipping houses of [[place-mercavia|Delalara]], whose first voice is [Ornetha Venthelis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, would pay a great deal to be the ones who tell it. A Taruzelian merchant captain lodging at the Slipway inn has recognized one hull's lines and said so to a sailor.

**The strait.** On the islands' side, Delzuru Tanaduni wants the toll. The foreign masters waiting in the roads for a convoy mean to fight it, and the fortress's day-book is the only honest count of what the toll would raise. A Vilérian agent lies in the roads in a merchant hull, holding the lines of the League's newest galley, bought from a Yamuzel shipwright with a stolen keel as proof. The lines are [[being-brshrskn|Abarash Rusakuni]]'s, and the League's Master of the Naval Yards has told Abarash first and wants them back before more copies leave the agent's hands.

**The oarsmen.** Kastamar's oarsmen are paid in notes a waterfront moneylender buys at half face from men who believe the next quarter is not coming. Some walk up the coast to [[place-kurzalora|Kurzalora]], vow themselves to the god and go down the landward side by night to berths on merchant ships under other names.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The two hulls are proof of one embarrassment, not a cause for war. Whoever holds the proof chooses whom it embarrasses: the admiral, the war party, the League's captains or the Delalara houses. The scenario offers the choice and never makes it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The thief.** The League's Master of the Naval Yards brings the news to Abarash, then hires a party to recover the drawings of the newest galley from the Vilérian agent's hold in the roads and learn who else has a copy.
- **The mercenary or soldier.** The admiral at Kastamar wants to know who has been telling his oarsmen that the next quarter is not coming, and hires a party to sit in the waterfront taverns and find out.
- **The leader.** Ornetha Venthelis hires a party to carry the shipping houses' treaty petition to Orzara rather than trust the post, and asks them first to look at the League galleys in the roads of Yamuzel.
- **The wanderer.** A widow has found her husband's name cut on the mole among the dead of the Tamzir Shoals, and a sailor home from Taruzel says he is alive and rowing in the islands. She asks a party to find out which is true before she remarries.

## The Opening {#opening}

The mole at Kastamar at evening, beside the names of the Shoals dead. Half the ship-sheds behind it stand shut. Oarsmen queue at a moneylender's table, selling the admiralty's notes.

> The moneylender pays in Solden, and nobody in the queue remarks on it.

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Slipway

**Purpose.** Introduce the war-port and the rumor.

**Situation.** The Slipway inn, full of shipwrights, marines and laid-up crews. A Taruzelian merchant captain drinks in the corner.

**NPCs and threats.** The merchant captain, who recognized a hull's lines and regrets saying so; the sailor who repeated it; marines of the admiralty, who listen for talk of desertion.

**Player choices.** Draw the captain out, follow the sailor, or ask the yard.

**Clues and rewards.** The captain names the hull by its League name and the convoy it rows in, and describes a stern carving only Kastamar cuts.

**Consequences.** The rumor reaches the admiral's officers within a day of the party asking.

**If bypassed.** The widow's sailor tells the same story, less exactly.

### Scene Two: The Yard

**Purpose.** A roleplay with a frightened man.

**Situation.** The ship-sheds, where the Roll of the Fleet places two galleys that are not there.

**NPCs and threats.** [Kaldremos Holimandis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, master shipwright, proud of his yard and frightened of the sale; the shed-keepers, who know the sheds are empty.

**Player choices.** Count the sheds, press Kaldremos, or bargain with him.

**Clues and rewards.** Two empty sheds under named galleys on the Roll. Kaldremos's own memorandum of the sale to the Helionite, paid in Solden.

**Consequences.** Kaldremos begs the party to say nothing until the next vote of ship-money, and offers his own savings.

**If bypassed.** The party crosses with only the merchant captain's word.

### Scene Three: The Height at Kurzalora

**Purpose.** An optional side road that shows where the fleet's men go.

**Situation.** The shrine of Brazemas above the sea. A party of marines and a surveyor has come to measure the Vigil Tower with a letter of leave from the _therkos_, and the Priest of Storms, [[being-yalzemakaldr|Yalzema Kaldremis]], doubts that he signed it.

**NPCs and threats.** The marines, who have orders and no wish to use them; the priest; and a vowed oarsman who means to go down the landward side tonight.

**Player choices.** Help the oarsman down, read the letter, or leave the height alone.

**Clues and rewards.** The oarsman sailed in the galleys before they were sold and can name the two sheds.

**Consequences.** A party that helps the oarsman has a witness the admiralty would hang.

**If bypassed.** The marines measure the tower, and the oarsman goes down the hill without help.

### Scene Four: The Roads Under the Fortress

**Purpose.** The crossing and the evidence.

**Situation.** The roads below the Strait Fortress, crowded with foreign hulls waiting for a convoy. The League's galleys come and go under the walls, and the fortress logs every hull.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]]'s League captains; the foreign masters, who would pay for the day-book; the Vilérian agent's merchant hull with its guarded hold. Boarding the agent's hull is a fight against a small crew that wants to slip anchor, and a party that does it brings the League's attention.

**Player choices.** Row past the convoy to see the hulls, board the agent's ship, buy the day-book, or present themselves to the Tanaduni.

**Clues and rewards.** One League galley's stern carving is Kastamar's. The widow's husband rows in her, alive and under another name. The agent's hold has the drawings and the keel.

**Consequences.** A party with the drawings has something both navies want. A party seen at the agent's hull is taken for Vilérian agents by the League, which is close enough to the truth to be dangerous.

**If bypassed.** The party sees the hulls only from a distance and carries word without proof.

### Scene Five: Whom to Tell

**Purpose.** The choice, on whichever coast the party ends.

**Situation.** The party holds some of: the merchant captain's word, Kaldremos's memorandum, the oarsman, the widow's husband, the drawings and the day-book.

**NPCs and threats.** Each buyer is a power: Ornetha Venthelis and the Delalara houses; Tharkas Gurimandis, who would want the shame buried; Delzuru Tanaduni, who would like to know that Kastamar builds better than his own yards; [[being-brshrskn|Abarash Rusakuni]], who wants his lines back; and the war party's enemies in Orzara.

**Player choices.** Sell, bury, trade or publish.

**Consequences.** If the Senate hears it, the war party is shamed and the treaty petition gains a season. If the admiral hears it first, he buries the Roll and the yard's men pay. If the League hears it, it knows Kastamar's lines are better than its own, and wants more.

**If bypassed.** The rumor stays a rumor in the Slipway.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

The rumor spreads along the waterfront of Kastamar until the admiral's officers hear it. Kaldremos is questioned and says nothing. The Delalara houses send their petition by post, and it reaches the Senate a month late and opened, as last year's did. In the islands Delzuru presses his toll, and the foreign masters in the roads hire advocates. The widow remarries.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

Whatever the party proves, the admiral who wants war with Taruzel and the League that guards the strait each owe them a debt or a grudge. The drawings of the newest galley, if the party holds them, are worth a fortune in a Bizarian yard and a hanging in Yamuzel.
