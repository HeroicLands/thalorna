Twelve years ago [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel]] won its independence from the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], and its merchant guilds wrote the Confederation's charter for themselves and discharged the veterans who won it with one purse of silver each. On the fourteenth of Brazemandi, the day of the victory at [[place-tamzirshoals|the Tamzir Shoals]], Yamuzel holds the [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]], and the councilors of the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]] stand on the wall of [[place-straitfortress|the Strait Fortress]] to take the salute, within a bowshot of the veterans' boats. This frame runs through the month before it. The halls mean to rise, the outlaw captains mean to be on the water, and the rising has no head.

## The Situation and the Stakes {#the-situation}

**The rising.** The [[affiliation-unchained|Unchained]] of the veterans' halls, the [[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captains]] and the harbour veterans of Yamuzel mean to rise at the Review. Their boats close the line through the narrows, and what happens after that is not settled. [[being-rzznpddn|Razzina Padduni]]'s hall in the Veterans' Quarter is where the plan is talked over. She argues every night that it needs a captain the whole coast would follow, and the man every side courts is [[being-zbzmjkdn|Zabuzum Jakuduni]], captain of [[affiliation-thetamzir|the Tamzir]] and a hero of the Shoals, who refused the guilds. The halls have asked him, a guild has offered him work and passage, and a Vylarian agent's servant has been seen on his quay. He has answered none of them.

**The squeeze.** [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Au'Khelathu]] financed the war, spent more on it than it meant to, and now presses the houses quietly for a war debt the Confederation denies in public. The houses pay a little each quarter, and they find the coin by calling in what the cities owe the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]] sooner and dearer. The calls fall on shopkeepers, fishermen and the veterans' halls. Every man bound for a called debt is another strongroom for the halls' Breakers to empty, and another voice for the rising.

**The indemnity.** Vylaria refuses the war indemnity the Confederation claims, publicly and every year, and the claim stands in the Council's petitions again. [[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]] presses for an audit of the [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]]'s strongroom, and has a candidate for [[being-yshdyjkdn|Yushuduya Jakuduni]]'s chair.

**The guilds.** The guilds divide over the rising as over everything. The Compact would crush the outlaws and enforce the debts. The [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]]'s captains warn the outlaws before a sweep, and some of its admirals would hire them; [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]] presses the Council for a League toll on every hull through the narrows. The [[affiliation-mrchntryvl|Merchantry of the Veil]] sells to both sides. The guilds divide over [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Thordimalon]] too: the Compact would expel it as Vylaria's instrument, the Merchantry hires its mages, and the League wants its battle-magic aboard its galleys.

**The empire's hand.** Vylaria meddles deniably. Crossbows, boarding pikes and Vylarian silver come into a [[affiliation-hitusuni|Hitusuni]] warehouse in [[place-sazumun2|Sazumun]] under a Merchantry seal, and go out by night to the outlaws' coves and the halls of Yamuzel, much as Khelathi gold once fed Taruzel's rebels. Discovery would be a cause for war, and a war is the one thing the empire's treasury cannot pay for.

**The seats.** Tirmana Qizikuni has sounded the heads of the other four houses on council seats that pass from a head to an heir. The other houses are tempted and wary, nobody has put it on the order, and the Arch-Consul, [[being-dnshkmn|Danashu Kumuni]], will not while he holds the seal.

The stakes are whether the Confederation pays what it owes the people who won it, and whether it survives the attempt to make it.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Nobody in this frame decides what Zabuzum Jakuduni does. He is courted by every side, and whether he leads the rising, refuses it or turns it to something else is never settled by a scenario. Play him as a man who listens to every offer and answers none, and let the table, if anyone, move him.

## Who Moves It {#who-moves-it}

[[being-rzznpddn|Razzina Padduni]], keeper of a veterans' hall in Yamuzel
: An archer of the harbour boats at the Shoals, who has stopped believing in petitions and has not stopped loving the Confederation.

[[being-zlmrzzn|Zilmar Ezzinu]], elder of a waterfront hall in Besikar
: He carries the veterans' petition to the Arch-Consul before every session and preaches patience, and he is trusted by both cities' halls.

[[being-nzzntrshn|Nazzuna Tarshuni]], Free Captain
: Keeper of the warehouse receipts the outlaw captains hold for their prize cargoes. She wants the Confederation to pay what it owes, in public.

[[being-zbzmjkdn|Zabuzum Jakuduni]], captain of the Tamzir
: Too famous to ignore, and loyal to his crew before any cause.

[[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]], admiral of the Tanaduni
: He would rather hire the outlaws than hunt them, and he thinks a rising that frightens the Compact would serve the League.

[[being-ashurazhitus|Ashuraz Hitusuni]], merchant prince of Sazumun
: His warehouses hold half the city's goods under seal, the empire's crates among them.

[[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]], head of the Qizikuni
: The war's first lender, who wants the house to outlast the Confederation as it outlasted the empire.

[[being-yshdyjkdn|Yushuduya Jakuduni]], the Kubasum
: The banker who built the treasury out of nothing, and who signs nothing she has not read twice.

[[being-dnshkmn|Danashu Kumuni]], Arch-Consul
: A coffee merchant who wants a quiet year and has promised the veterans' petition the first reading of the summer session.

[[being-llmnblnn|Ellamuna Balinuni]], head of the Balinuni
: Her votes named the Arch-Consul, and her steward buys the order of business before dawn.

[[being-brshrskn|Abarash Rusakuni]], master designer
: His lines row in the League's galleys, and copies of them are leaving the islands.

The bodies
: The Grand Council and its weighted votes; the three guilds; the Merazuni; the Commander of the Marines at the Strait Fortress, whose sergeants grew up in the Veterans' Quarter; the [[affiliation-shoalsbrthd|Shoals Brotherhood]]; and the Khelathi factorage on the Guild Quarter of Besikar, which holds the houses' notes.

## The Arcs

1. **The Review itself.** [[scenario-lstbtnrrws|The Last Boat Through the Narrows]] runs the month's last days in Yamuzel, the plots on Khamisar and the morning of the Review.
2. **The receipts.** [[scenario-frmnspttn|The Freeman's Petition]] asks the party to carry the outlaws' claim into the Grand Council Hall.
3. **The strait.** [[scenario-hllsndrfrtrs|The Hulls Under the Fortress]] crosses to the narrows from the Vylarian side, where the empire's hand and the League's toll meet.
4. **The seats.** [[scenario-clsdroll|The Closed Roll]] follows the Qizikuni's letters, the audit and the treasury's missing clerk through Besikar.

## Ways In {#ways-in}

1. **For the halls.** A veteran bound for a debt he says he paid needs his bond found, or a hall needs a message carried to another city by strangers.
2. **For a house.** A house wants the halls watched, a cargo moved, a letter delivered to another house's head or an answer brought back from the Council.
3. **For the outlaws.** A Free Captain takes a merchantman with the party aboard and offers passage to any cove in exchange for a letter carried ashore.
4. **For the empire.** A Vylarian factor on the quay of Sazumun asks for nothing but a crate's safe passage, and pays three times what a crate is worth.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

- The Compact's calls come sooner, the halls fill, and the crates go on reaching the coves. The Compact learns three delivery dates and makes three arrests.
- The admirals' family clears the plots on Khamisar with marines who are veterans' sons, and the Veterans' Quarter watches it done.
- On the morning of the Review the veterans' boats come down the narrows at the tail of the line. Whether they close it, and what follows if they do, depends on who is on the water and who has been warned. A League galley waits off the cape for the last boat.
- The summer session opens after the Review with the debt's interest read first. The petition, the toll, the audit and the indemnity follow in the order whoever bought the list has paid for.

## The Secret {#the-secret}

**The treasury is short.** Among the reserves the Merazuni enters as held is the war indemnity Vylaria refuses to pay, written in the ledger as though it were gold in the vault. It made the first script good when there was almost nothing behind it. The coast's paper is sound so long as nobody counts the strongroom against the book, and the Qizikuni's audit would count it. The Khelathi factorage has twice offered Yushuduya bullion to close the gap, in return for the Confederation's acknowledgment of the war debt, and she has twice refused.

**One hall is sold.** Zilmar Ezzinu has informed for the Auric Compact since the first year of the outlawing, when its enforcers took his son for a debt and gave him back for a name. Razzina has sent him the day of the rising. He has not passed it on, and his next meeting with the Compact's enforcer falls before the Review.

**The crates are sold twice.** The Vylarian agent who pays for the crates deals through a broker and has never shown his face. Ashuraz Hitusuni has sold the dates and coves of three deliveries to a Compact enforcer, choosing which three. He would use the rising if it comes, and he has offered Zabuzum more coin than any cargo is worth.

**The last boat.** The last boat of the Review belongs to the Free Captains, brought through each year by a captain chosen by lot. This year the lot fell to Nazzuna Tarshuni, who carries the rebels' original written promise of prize money, and the Commander of the Marines has put a League galley off the cape to wait for her.
