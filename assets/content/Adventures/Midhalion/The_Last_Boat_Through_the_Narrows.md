## Overview {#overview}

On the fourteenth of Brazemandi [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]] holds the [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]] for the victory at the Tamzir Shoals. The Corsair League's galleys call their oars under the Grand Council's stand on the wall of [[place-straitfortress|the Strait Fortress]], and the veterans' small boats come down the narrows at the tail of the line. This year the halls of the Veterans' Quarter, the outlaw captains and the harbour veterans mean to rise on that morning. The party arrives in the last days before the Review, on a hall's errand, a guild's job or a captain's passage, and the plan reaches it from every side. Nobody has agreed to lead the rising, and the man the halls want has not answered them.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The plan.** The Old Hands of the Yamuzel halls of the [[affiliation-unchained|Unchained]], the [[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captains]] and the harbour veterans mean the veterans' boats to close the line through the narrows on the morning of the Review, while the councilors stand on the fortress wall within a bowshot of them. What happens then is not settled, because the rising has no head. [[being-rzznpddn|Razzina Padduni]]'s hall is where it is talked over.

**The captain.** Every side courts [[being-zbzmjkdn|Zabuzum Jakuduni]], captain of [[affiliation-thetamzir|the Tamzir]]: the halls, [[being-ashurazhitus|Ashuraz Hitusuni]] of the Merchantry, and a Vylarian agent whose servant has been seen on the Tamzir's quay. He has answered none of them.

**The crates.** Crossbows, boarding pikes and a chest of Vylarian silver lie under the floor of a cooper's shop below Razzina's hall. They came by night from a Hitusuni warehouse in Sazumun under a Merchantry seal, paid for by an agent of the empire. Ashuraz has sold the dates and coves of three deliveries to an enforcer of the Auric Compact.

**The informer.** Razzina has sent the day of the rising to the Besikar halls by [[being-zlmrzzn|Zilmar Ezzinu]], whose hall informs for the Compact. He held it back at his meeting with the enforcer on the first night of the month. The enforcer heard something held back and has called him to a second meeting, on the eve of the Review.

**The plots.** In the war's second year Yamuzel's admirals had the south slope of [[place-khamisar|Khamisar]] surveyed into plots and promised one to every man who served through the war. The survey book lies in the Strait Fortress archive among the muster rolls, and the admirals' family that leases the slope has offered the old navy clerk who keeps it a pension to burn it before the Review.

**The last boat.** The Review's last boat belongs to the Free Captains, and this year the lot fell to [[being-nzzntrshn|Nazzuna Tarshuni]], who carries the rebels' original written promise of prize money and means to read it to the coast from the narrows. The Commander of the Marines has put a League galley of his own choosing in the roads, out of the line, with orders to wait off the cape for her. She does not know.

**The admiral.** [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]] knows the halls talk of more than a strike, and has told nobody. He thinks a rising that frightens the Compact without burning the yards would serve the League. He has offered the Free Captains pardons in Tanaduni service if the Council grants his toll.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Zabuzum Jakuduni is never resolved. He hears every offer the party carries, and he may help the party, refuse it or go his own way, but no scene decides whether he leads the rising. If the table asks, he has not decided, and nothing in this scenario decides for him.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The thief.** The stewards of the admirals' family pay a party to see that the Khamisar survey book in the fortress archive is burned before the Review.
- **The mercenary or soldier.** A party is hired to guard a Merchantry delivery to a cove on the outer islands.
- **The leader.** [[being-rzznpddn|Razzina Padduni]] wants a party with no stake in the plots on the Khamisar terraces the morning the marines come, so that the Council hears an account it will believe.
- **The wanderer.** A sergeant of marines who grew up in the Veterans' Quarter asks a party to carry word to Razzina's hall that the Commander means to catch the last boat this year.

## The Opening {#opening}

The Veterans' Quarter of Yamuzel at dusk, a week before the Review. Every hall is lit. In one of them the old men sing [[lore-paidattheshoals|What Was Paid at the Shoals]], and the young ones listen from the door.

> The song stops when a stranger comes in, and starts again when Razzina nods.

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Terraces

**Purpose.** Show the promise that was broken, on the ground.

**Situation.** Dawn on the south slope of Khamisar. Veterans plough marked plots with painted stones. Marines come up the path to clear them, and the marines are veterans' sons.

**NPCs and threats.** The ploughmen; their wives with bread; the marines' sergeant, who does not want this; the admirals' stewards, who watch from the road. A fight here is a brawl the marines do not want to win.

**Player choices.** Bear witness, help the marines, help the ploughmen, or find the survey book first.

**Clues and rewards.** One ploughman's widow keeps her husband's discharge papers, with a copy of the list of names folded inside the cover. She does not know what it is.

**Consequences.** A cleared terrace sends more veterans to Razzina's hall. A party that saves the list holds the veterans' proof.

**If bypassed.** The marines clear the plots slowly, and the ploughmen come back the next night.

### Scene Two: The Archive

**Purpose.** A puzzle and a moral choice.

**Situation.** The Strait Fortress archive, where the towers' monthly logs are kept. The old navy clerk has not yet decided whether to take the stewards' pension.

**NPCs and threats.** The clerk; the marines who guard the fortress; and the stewards' man, who comes to collect the ashes.

**Player choices.** Burn the book for the stewards, steal it for the veterans, or persuade the clerk to keep it.

**Clues and rewards.** The survey book, plot by plot, with the signatures. Filed beside it, a day-book of hulls under the fortress that both sides of the toll quarrel would buy.

**Consequences.** The book in the veterans' hands starts a fight in the Admiral's Council on the eve of the festival of the war.

**If bypassed.** The clerk takes the pension.

### Scene Three: The Wrong Cove

**Purpose.** An action scene that shows the empire's hand and the Merchantry's double dealing.

**Situation.** A Merchantry delivery of crates to a cove on the outer islands. A League galley waits there.

**NPCs and threats.** The outlaw crew that receives the crates; the League galley's marines. The galley is a fight no party wins at sea, and a party that runs must run fast. The outlaws believe the party sold them.

**Player choices.** Fight, flee, surrender the crates, or bargain with the galley's captain.

**Clues and rewards.** The crates bear a Merchantry seal over a Vylarian armourer's mark, filed half away. The galley's captain had the date and the cove from a Compact enforcer.

**Consequences.** The outlaws spread word that the party betrayed them, and only Ashuraz Hitusuni, who sold the date, knows otherwise.

**If bypassed.** The delivery is taken without the party, and the halls hear of it.

### Scene Four: The Tamzir's Quay

**Purpose.** A roleplay with the man nobody can command.

**Situation.** The quay where the Tamzir lies. Every side has sent someone, and the party carries an offer from one of them, or comes on its own business.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-zbzmjkdn|Zabuzum Jakuduni]] and his crew; a Merchantry factor with a purse; a servant who is not what he seems.

**Player choices.** Carry an offer, warn him of another, ask his help with something smaller, or leave him alone.

**Clues and rewards.** The crew talk of the Shoals, of a betrayal from within the rebel fleet, and of a captain's window in Yamuzel that lost a ship model.

**Consequences.** Zabuzum remembers who treated him as a man and who as a weapon. That is all this scene settles.

**If bypassed.** The offers reach him by other hands.

### Scene Five: The Evening Before

**Purpose.** The climax: who knows what in the morning.

**Situation.** The eve of the Review. The League galley waits off the cape for the last boat. Zilmar's second meeting of the month with the enforcer is tonight, in the chandler's back room on the quay at Besikar where they met on the first.

**NPCs and threats.** Razzina, who decides the morning; Nazzuna, who must bring the last boat; Zilmar, if the party is in Besikar; the Commander's marines.

**Player choices.** Carry word of the galley to Razzina's hall, warn Nazzuna, watch the chandler's back room, tell the Commander what the halls plan, or do nothing.

**Clues and rewards.** Whatever the party has carried this week decides what the halls know.

**Consequences.** If Razzina learns of the galley, the last boat sails differently, or does not sail. If the Compact learns the day, its enforcers are on the quays at dawn. If Zilmar is unmasked, the Besikar halls turn on him before the Review.

**If bypassed.** The morning comes with each side knowing what it knew.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

The marines clear the plots and the clerk burns the book. Zilmar passes the day to the enforcer, or holds it one more month. On the morning of the Review the League's galleys call their oars, the veterans' boats come down at the tail of the line, and Nazzuna brings the last boat through the narrows into the galley's path. What the boats do when the councilors stand on the wall depends on who has a captain that morning, and the halls do not.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

The Review ends with the rising begun, broken, put off or never attempted, as the party's choices and the referee decide. Each of those leaves the summer session of the Grand Council with a different petition at the head of its order. The halls remember who stood on the terraces, and the Compact remembers who was on the water.
