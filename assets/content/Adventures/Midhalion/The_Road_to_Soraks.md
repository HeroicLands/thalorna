## Overview {#overview}

The _therkos_ of [[affiliation-provinclys|the Province of Alessa]], [[being-kldrmslthzs|Kaldremas Elthezis]], has not been seen in [[place-alyssar|Alessar]] since the spring, and his acts come down from his villa at [[place-vennara|Vennara]] sealed. His freedman secretary, [[being-nethoren|Nethoren]], reads them from the steps of the Governor's House. The party is hired to carry a sealed packet of the province's business up the north road to [[place-skoraz|Soraks]], a city built for thirty thousand where four thousand now live, among colonnades the council sells a column at a time to meet a large city's assessment. The road leads the party into the province's two quiet scandals: the north road's bridge levy, spent elsewhere, and the seal that has sealed every act since spring without its holder.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The ring.** Kaldremas Elthezis is dying, lost in his mind on most days, and his seal-ring has been in Alessar since spring in Nethoren's keeping. Nethoren seals what the _therkos_ would have sealed, refuses every bribe for anything else, and keeps a ledger of every act in his own hand. In law that changes nothing: an act sealed without its holder is a forgery, and every judgment, lease and assessment since spring can be challenged by anyone who proves the _therkos_ never saw it. The _karezos_ knows, because his own assessments went out under the same seal, and he has paid the _therkos_'s steward to send him word the day his master dies, a day before anyone else.

**The act.** The packet the party carries holds an act moving the Northern Bench and its registry from Soraks south to [[place-lentora|Lentora]], where the roads are good. Without the Bench the litigants stop coming, and without them Soraks is a quarry with a council. Nobody in Soraks has seen it.

**The bridge.** The north road's levy is assessed every year for the Broken Bridge beyond Soraks, and is spent on the roads nearer the capital, where the senators drive. The senior _tavemos_ of Soraks, [Iltheros Gurimandis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, has asked to see the accounts four times and been refused by letter.

**The page.** The registry's page naming the buyer of the [[affiliation-zarkedis|Zarkedis]] house at the creditors' sale has been cut from the book. The buyer was a factor of [[affiliation-thalurates|House Thalurates]], and the Zarkedis ancestor masks are still in the house's cupboard. [[being-iltherethalu|Ilthere Thalurates]] means them to be the price she names to [[being-klstrtsxrchds|Kalestratos Zarkedis]], the fallen house's heir. The clerk who cut the page has heard of the act and hopes to go south with the registry.

The reveal lands when the party learns that the packet they carry would take the town's court away, and that its seal is a forgery a clever advocate could prove.

> [!TIP]
> Nethoren is honest and frightened. Let the party like him. His ledger is a year of the province's business in one book, and the first person to read it holds every family that lost a case this year.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The thief.** A cousin of the fallen House Zarkedis hires a party to take the house's ancestor masks from the cupboard of the house on the Bench Square in Soraks.
- **The mercenary or soldier.** Nethoren hires guards to carry a sealed packet of the province's acts up the north road to the Northern Bench, and pays them from his own purse.
- **The leader.** A party's patron has a lease refused in an act read from the steps of the Governor's House, and wants to know who in Vennara wrote it.
- **The wanderer.** [[being-phltsklfts|Filezos Klethes]] buys strangers a supper at the Two Seals in Alessar and asks what they would charge to carry a sealed letter north without opening it.

## The Opening {#opening}

Morning on the steps of the Governor's House in Alessar. Nethoren reads three acts aloud, and the crowd of petitioners listens for their own names. Afterward he comes down the steps with a packet under his arm and looks for someone who is not from the province.

> He holds the packet with both hands. The seal is the _therkos_'s, a ram above a wheatsheaf, and the wax is fresh.

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Two Seals

**Purpose.** Introduce the province's business and the people who trade in it.

**Situation.** The inn of the Two Seals, full of petitioners, advocates and copyists. The board of the court's list hangs by the door, and the innkeeper sells places on it.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-phltsklfts|Filezos Klethes]], once a strategist of the legions, buys suppers and asks questions. An advocate for the _karezos_ drinks at the next table and watches who Nethoren hired. Nobody here is violent; everybody is for sale.

**Player choices.** Take Filezos's letter as well, learn the packet's contents from the advocate, or keep their counsel.

**Clues and rewards.** The advocate lets slip that the _karezos_'s own assessments went out under the _therkos_'s seal this year, and that the _karezos_ has sent nothing about it to the capital.

**Consequences.** The _karezos_ knows the party carries the packet.

**If bypassed.** The party leaves at dawn, and the _karezos_ learns of them from the gate.

### Scene Two: The North Road

**Purpose.** A journey through a province spending itself, with one danger on the way.

**Situation.** The road runs straight across farmland with grass in the paving. The post-house at the last mile is shut. A day out, the road passes Lentora's land, where the baths smoke and the council's seats fall empty.

**NPCs and threats.** Two men from the capital ride the same road and keep a mile behind; their errand in Soraks is their own (see the town's hook _The monograph_), and they take no interest in the party. A Lentora steward overtakes the party in a hurry: he means to reach his master before the _tavemoi_ name him to an empty council seat in his absence. On the second night, hired hands try to take the packet. The _karezos_ of Alessar sent them, because he wants to know which acts under the seal leave the city. A watchful party beats them off.

**Player choices.** Help the steward, wait for the men behind, or press on.

**Clues and rewards.** One of the hired hands carries a note in a clerk's hand describing the packet's seal and asking for the act about the Bench. The hand is that of the _karezos_'s advocate from the Two Seals.

**Consequences.** Someone besides the party knows what the packet holds.

**If bypassed.** The party reaches Soraks with the packet unopened and no notion of its contents.

### Scene Three: The Column Sold Twice

**Purpose.** Show Soraks living on its stone.

**Situation.** The Long Colonnade, ropes round the columns sold and red marks on the council's. Two villa-builders' agents hold bills for the same twelve columns of the western portico, sealed a month apart, and Iltheros Gurimandis's ledger shows one sale.

**NPCs and threats.** Iltheros; the two agents; their carters, who would rather fight each other than the party.

**Player choices.** Settle the quarrel, take a side, or learn who sealed the second bill.

**Clues and rewards.** Both bills bear the council's seal, and only one bears Iltheros's signature: someone with the use of the council's seal is selling the town's stone twice. Iltheros, grateful, shows the party his four letters asking for the bridge accounts, and the four refusals.

**Consequences.** Iltheros trusts the party, which matters when the packet is opened.

**If bypassed.** The carters fight in the colonnade and a column falls on one of them.

### Scene Four: The Cut Page

**Purpose.** A puzzle in the registry, and a temptation.

**Situation.** The registry behind the court-house of the Northern Bench, where the packet is to be delivered. Its clerks are provincial men, and a clerk outranks a _tavemos_ in the town's own street.

**NPCs and threats.** The clerk who cut the Zarkedis page, who knows of the act and wants to go south with it; the Bench's judge, appointed from Alessar.

**Player choices.** Deliver the packet as hired, open it, or show it to Iltheros first.

**Clues and rewards.** The cut page is in the clerk's room. It names a factor of House Thalurates as the buyer of the Zarkedis house. The packet holds the act moving the Bench to Lentora, sealed with the _therkos_'s ring.

**Consequences.** A party that shows the act to Iltheros gives Soraks its one weapon: if the council can prove the _therkos_ never saw the act, the act is void. Proving it means proving every act since spring void too, and pulling down a year of the province's business with Soraks's court.

> [!WARNING]
> Opening a sealed provincial act is a crime the party's employer will not forgive. Let the table weigh it.

**If bypassed.** The packet is delivered unopened, and the act is read from the court-house steps the following week.

### Scene Five: The Broken Bridge

**Purpose.** An open ending at the end of the road.

**Situation.** The Broken Bridge north of town: three arches standing, the middle one in the river. A Lentora merchant says the bridge could be mended in a summer if someone found the stone, and Soraks has more stone than anything else. Across the river the road runs on, paved, into empty country.

**NPCs and threats.** Iltheros, if he trusts the party, comes to the bridge to ask what they mean to do. The two men from the capital are lodged at the Last Mile.

**Player choices.** Carry the cut page to Kalestratos Zarkedis, carry the act's news back to Nethoren, sell the ledger's existence to the _karezos_, or put the town's case to the province.

**Consequences.** The party's choice decides whether Soraks fights, bargains or empties.

**If bypassed.** Soraks learns of the act when it is read.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

The packet reaches the Bench by another hand, and the act is read. The registry goes south to Lentora within the year, and its clerk goes with it. Soraks sells its colonnades faster. The _therkos_ dies at Vennara, the _karezos_ hears a day early and clears the Governor's House, and Nethoren's ledger goes into the fire or into a drawer. The new _therkos_'s staff reads whatever is left, and the province's lawsuits multiply.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

If Soraks challenges the act, the question of the ring reaches the capital, where every act a province issues under a dying man's seal becomes a court case. A party that holds Nethoren's ledger holds the power to reopen a year of judgments. Kalestratos Zarkedis, if he learns who bought his house, has a quarrel with House Thalurates and a reason to want the masks back before Ilthere names her price.
