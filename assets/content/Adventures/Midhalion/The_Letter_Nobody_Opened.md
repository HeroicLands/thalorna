## Overview {#overview}

The capital can neither lose [[affiliation-provncmktr|Moktur]] nor pay to hold it, and the province governs itself in the gap. Its _therkos_, [Tholmaras Nelamen]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, has held it from [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]] for eleven years. He pays some twelve hundred Varokh-born auxiliaries on the first of every month from the tolls of [[place-korrast|Korras]] and the province's share of the metal, and they are sworn to him by name. The legion at [[place-dolvar|Dolvar]] is paid when the treasury's coin arrives. The party comes north on a road errand and is drawn into the month's three needs: the pay chest must come down from Korras past a war-band, a surveyor from the capital must measure a road that does not want measuring, and the Nordman company on the towers has a letter from the _therkos_ to answer.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The recall.** Last autumn the chancery sent Tholmaras a letter of recall under the old Orzas's seal, naming a _kurthos_ of the capital as the next _therkos_. The post-master at the provincial boundary signed for the bag, and the receipt went south. The letter lies in the _therkos_'s strongroom with its seal unbroken. In law he has not received it, so he has not answered it. The capital holds the receipt, believes the letter delivered and waits to see whether he comes. With [[being-zrmlshlrkls|the Orzas]] failing and the succession in doubt, nobody in Orzara will ride north to ask why.

**The vandraks.** The legion's commander at Dolvar, [Satheras Kiramaris]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, received his copy of the recall with an order to see it carried out, and has not acted. Half his legion could reach Tirellan in a week and would meet twelve hundred auxiliaries paid on the day. Through the last two winters, when the treasury's coin did not come, his legion was paid from the _therkos_'s strongrooms against his receipt.

**The road.** Every dispatch from Dolvar to the capital is opened at the posting-station below Tirellan's North Gate, read by the _pozerus_'s clerk, resealed with a copy of the _vandraks_'s seal and sent on a day late. Satheras has known for years and writes accordingly.

**The gate.** A herders' path runs along a ledge under the cliff above Korras and down into the gorge below the wall, out of sight of every tower. Every summer the Varokh war-bands take it by night, guided by the half-Varokh herding kindreds of the upper pasture, who keep their own steadings safe by it. The herder found dead below the cliff was the one man among them who wanted to tell the gate.

**The towers.** [[affiliation-hrendskari|Hrendskari]], an unsigned Nordman company under [[being-knrvsldvrnrk|Knarvselda Dvurnaruk]], holds seven watchtowers above Dolvar, paid by the month. The _therkos_'s letter to her offers the company pay on the first of every month from the province's tolls, as his auxiliaries are paid, in return for its word given to him by name. Taken, it puts the towers under the man the capital has recalled.

> [!TIP]
> The recall is the scenario's hinge and its one way to force the matter. Whoever brings the letter into Tirellan's forum and reads it aloud makes the _therkos_ obey or refuse in public. Nobody in the province wants that done, including the capital's own officers.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The thief.** An agent of the _kurthos_ named as the next _therkos_, waiting in Orzara for word that never comes, hires a party to learn whether the recall reached the Keep, and to bring back proof either way.
- **The mercenary or soldier.** The _tarkelos_ at Tirellan hires a party to bring the month's pay chest down from Korras past a war-band on the march, by the first of the month.
- **The leader.** [[being-knrvsldvrnrk|Knarvselda Dvurnaruk]] asks a party she trusts to carry her reply to the _therkos_'s sealed offer, and to watch who opens it on the road.
- **The wanderer.** A clerk of the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]] has come north to measure the province's road and write down where its milestones stand. The last man sent on the errand came back with a broken leg and no notes, and the clerk wants a guard.

## The Opening {#opening}

Tirellan's forum at the state's offerings, on the last days of the month. The border lords stand apart and watch one another. In the Eagle Shrine someone has left a cup of wine and a strip of red cloth, the old colour of the cashiered Fourth. Riders of the auxiliaries come down from the Keep with their pay-tallies, waiting for the first.

> The grey walls hold the morning cold. A rider at the Keep gate says to another, in the Varokh tongue, that the chest is two days late.

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Pay Chest

**Purpose.** An action scene that shows why the province is loyal to the man who pays it.

**Situation.** The chest waits at Korras under the gate commander, a younger son of a border house with sixty men of his own. A Varokh war-band is on the march below the pass.

**NPCs and threats.** The gate commander; his toll-keepers; and the war-band, which a seasoned party can outfight on open ground and cannot outfight in the gorge at night. A border lord refused the command this spring means to come for the gate at midsummer.

**Player choices.** Bring the chest by the road, by night, or by the herders' path, if they have found it.

**Clues and rewards.** The widow of the dead herder will speak only to a party with no lord behind it. She tells them of the path and of why her husband died.

**Consequences.** A chest delivered on the first earns the _therkos_'s thanks. A party that knows the path holds the one fact that would make Korras what it seems, and the herding kindreds know who told.

**If bypassed.** The chest arrives a day late under a strong guard, and the riders are paid late for the first time.

### Scene Two: The Surveyor

**Purpose.** A journey with a courteous obstacle.

**Situation.** The clerk measures milestones down the posting-road, and the _pozerus_'s officers are courteous to him at every station.

**NPCs and threats.** The clerk, precise and nervous; the station-keepers; and the _pozerus_'s clerk at the station below the North Gate, who opens the legion's dispatches. Nobody in Tirellan knows who broke the last surveyor's leg; the clerks at the North Gate station know why.

**Player choices.** Guard the clerk as hired, look in the station's back room, or let the clerk measure and move on.

**Clues and rewards.** The back room holds a copy of the _vandraks_'s seal and a bag of dispatches waiting a day. The cloth merchant's factor's letters wait there too.

**Consequences.** A party that finds the back room knows how the province reads the capital's mail.

**If bypassed.** The clerk measures his milestones and writes that the road is long.

### Scene Three: The Offer to the Towers

**Purpose.** A roleplay: the Nordman company's choice.

**Situation.** Dolvar, below the towers, before the truce market. The _vandraks_ has asked Knarvselda for thirty more men above the pass, and she does not have them. The _therkos_'s rider waits for her answer.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-knrvsldvrnrk|Knarvselda Dvurnaruk]], who wants her company paid, her towers held and her dead sent home; Satheras Kiramaris, who asks the party what Tirellan is saying.

**Player choices.** Advise her, carry her answer, or carry a copy of it to the _vandraks_.

**Clues and rewards.** The _therkos_'s offer in writing. Knarvselda's reply is hers to write; the party may read it if she lets them.

**Consequences.** The company leans to the man who pays on the day, or to the legion that pays when it can.

**If bypassed.** The rider waits a week and takes her answer back himself.

### Scene Four: The Strongroom

**Purpose.** The discovery and the choice.

**Situation.** The Keep at Tirellan. The recall lies in the strongroom with its seal unbroken.

**NPCs and threats.** Tholmaras Nelamen, courteous and very well informed; his _tarkelos_; [[being-tharozatalot|Tharoza Talothates]], the province's commander of order, who keeps the Keep's peace; and the auxiliaries, who would fight for the _therkos_ against anyone.

**Player choices.** Learn of the letter and keep silent, carry the news south, take the letter, or read it in the forum.

**Clues and rewards.** The letter, the receipt's date and the _kurthos_'s name.

**Consequences.** If the recall is read in the forum, Tholmaras must obey or refuse in public, and the capital can do nothing about either. Satheras must then march, or say plainly that he will not.

> [!WARNING]
> Reading the recall in public is the choice that turns the province. Let the party know the auxiliaries are the _therkos_'s and the towers may be soon.

**If bypassed.** The letter stays in the strongroom.

### Scene Five: The Border Lord's Banner

**Purpose.** An optional ending: the frontier's own politics.

**Situation.** Midsummer at Korras. The refused border lord comes for the gate with his household. [[being-korvathosvol|Korvathos Volkemates]], a lord of the march, has dealt with a Varokh chief across the line in ways the capital would call treason, and his huscarl [[being-arznlsbrds|Arzanalas Baredas]] swore to the house, not to Orzara.

**NPCs and threats.** The border lord's men; the gate commander's; Arzanalas, old and stiff and still the best captain in Tirellan; and a war-band over the march.

**Player choices.** Stand on the wall for the commander, broker a truce between the lords, or tell the gate about the herders' path.

**Consequences.** Whoever holds the gate holds the _therkos_'s pay, and the _therkos_ cares more that it is held than who holds it.

**If bypassed.** The lords fight over the gate, and the war-band takes the path while they do.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

The chest comes down late under a strong guard. The surveyor measures his milestones and writes that the road is long. Knarvselda takes the _therkos_'s pay, or keeps the legion's, and the towers above the pass go into the truce market thirty men short. The recall stays in the strongroom. The capital holds its receipt, and the old Orzas's chancery sends nobody north.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

A recall read aloud in Tirellan reaches Orzara as a governor's obedience or a governor's refusal. Obedience leaves a province whose auxiliaries were sworn to the man who has gone. Refusal comes in a season when the throne cannot afford to answer one. A party that knows of the herders' path, the station's back room or the letter in the strongroom knows how Moktur holds itself together, and each of those secrets has a man who would kill to keep it.
