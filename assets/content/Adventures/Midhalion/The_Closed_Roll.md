## Overview {#overview}

[[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]], head of the oldest banking house on the quay at [[place-besikar2|Besikar]] and the Confederation's first lender in the war, has sounded the heads of the other four great houses on council seats that pass from a head to an heir. Nobody has put it on the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]]'s order. In the same season she presses the Council to audit the [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]]'s strongroom, and she has a candidate for the treasurer's chair. The party is hired to carry her sealed letters to two other houses, and each head they reach asks what the others answered. The letters lead into the treasury, a missing clerk and a marriage contract, and to a debt the Confederation denies in public and pays in private.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The closed roll.** Tirmana wants the house to outlast the Confederation, as it outlasted the empire. A treasury in the Compact's hands, a council roll that can change and a Thordimalon outside the walls are, to her, three dangers of one kind. The other houses are tempted and wary. The Arch-Consul, [[being-dnshkmn|Danashu Kumuni]], will not put the closed roll on the order while he holds the seal: a closed roll would have kept him off Kudumel's council, and would keep his sons off it for good.

**The audit.** Tirmana believes the treasury's strongroom is short, though she does not know by how much or why. She is right. [[being-yshdyjkdn|Yushuduya Jakuduni]], the Kubasum, entered Viléria's unpaid war indemnity in the ledger as gold held in the vault. It was the only way to make the first script good, and it has stayed on the books since. An audit would find the gap in a day. Tirmana has not considered what the coast's paper is worth on the morning after.

**The house's own debt.** When the Qizikuni strongroom ran low early in the war, Tirmana borrowed from Khelathi lenders through a broker of the [[affiliation-mrchntryvl|Merchantry of the Veil]], on notes in the house's name entered in none of its books. The notes lie in the head factor's strongbox at the Khelathi factorage on the Guild Quarter. [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Au'Khelathu]] spent more on Taruzel's war than it meant to, and the head factor now asks, gently, when the houses mean to pay something down. Tirmana pays a little each quarter and finds the coin by calling in what the cities owe the Compact sooner and dearer. The calls fall on the harbor streets and the veterans' halls.

**The factor's offer.** The same head factor has twice offered Yushuduya bullion to make the book and the vault agree, against the Confederation's acknowledgment of the war debt. She has refused twice.

**The clerk.** A clerk of the treasury's ledger kept the strongroom's tally one night when its keeper was ill, saw both the vault and the book, and has not come back from a holiday on [[place-khamisar|Khamisar]]. His desk has been searched by someone who knew what to look for. Yushuduya wants him home quietly, and the Qizikuni want him first.

**The marriage.** [Katira Tanaduni]{.name data-type="being/npc"} has asked to read the Qizikuni books before she signs a marriage contract with the house. Tirmana wants to know what Katira suspects and who told her, and wants the factor's strongbox to hold nothing in the house's name before the wedding.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The closed roll is proposed and never moved in this scenario. The audit is pressed and never held. The party's choices shift who supports each, and what each house knows about the others.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The thief.** Tirmana hires a party to see that the factor's strongbox at the Khelathi factorage holds nothing with the house's name on it before Katira Tanaduni signs.
- **The mercenary or soldier.** [[being-yshdyjkdn|Yushuduya Jakuduni]] wants the clerk who has not come back from Khamisar found and brought home quietly.
- **The leader.** Tirmana sends a party with sealed letters to the heads of two other houses, and each head asks the party what the others answered.
- **The wanderer.** [[being-ashurazhitus|Ashuraz Hitusuni]] wants guests at his dinner for the treasury's clerks who can tell him afterward what the clerks said when he left the room.

## The Opening {#opening}

The Qizikuni counting-house on the quay, at the hour the evening clearing closes. Tirmana gives the party two letters sealed with the house's mark and a list of whom to see.

> "Bring me what they say, not what they write," she says. "What they write I can read myself."

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Letters

**Purpose.** The houses, and what each wants from the closed roll.

**Situation.** The party carries one letter to [[being-llmnblnn|Ellamuna Balinuni]] at [[place-qudumel2|Kudumel]] and one to [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]], admiral of the Tanaduni of Yamuzel, or to [[being-brshrskn|Abarash Rusakuni]] of Bazunel if the referee prefers.

**NPCs and threats.** Ellamuna, whose votes named this year's Arch-Consul and who wants the whole caravan trade of the strait landing at Kudumel; Delzuru, who wants the League above the Compact and his house out of debt; Abarash, who wants his lines on every sea. Each asks what the others answered.

**Player choices.** Report truly, shade the answers, sell them to the third house, or open the letters.

**Clues and rewards.** Ellamuna wants a hereditary seat and fears the Qizikuni holding it beside her. Delzuru's house is mortgaged to the Qizikuni, and he answers carefully. Abarash does not care who sits on the Council.

**Consequences.** Whatever the party reports shapes whether Tirmana presses the closed roll this season.

**If bypassed.** Tirmana sends her steward, and the houses answer in writing.

### Scene Two: The Clerks' Dinner

**Purpose.** The treasury from outside.

**Situation.** [[being-ashurazhitus|Ashuraz Hitusuni]]'s dinner for the clerks of the treasury's script, where he courts them for a moneylender's charter.

**NPCs and threats.** The clerks, flattered and cautious; Ashuraz, who leaves the room to let them talk; a Qizikuni clerk of the ledger, the house's candidate for the treasurer's chair.

**Player choices.** Listen, draw the clerks out, or warn them.

**Clues and rewards.** The clerks speak of the missing colleague and of notes with a false star passing on the quays of Sazumun. One says the strongroom's second key is to pass to a marine captain the Admiral's Council has put forward.

**Consequences.** Ashuraz learns what the party heard, and pays for it.

**If bypassed.** The party hears of the missing clerk from Yushuduya directly.

### Scene Three: The Clerk on Khamisar

**Purpose.** A search, a witness, and a choice of master.

**Situation.** Khamisar, the fortress island, where the clerk has gone to ground in a lodging below the signal towers. Every traveler on the ridge is logged.

**NPCs and threats.** The clerk, terrified; a Qizikuni agent who is also looking; and the tower-wardens, whose logs say who went where. The agent prefers a bribe to a fight and is good with a knife if it comes to one.

**Player choices.** Bring the clerk to Yushuduya, to Tirmana, or let him run.

**Clues and rewards.** The clerk tells what he saw: a vault lighter than the book by a sum close to the indemnity Viléria refuses to pay.

**Consequences.** Whoever holds the clerk holds the audit's answer before it is asked.

> [!WARNING]
> The clerk's testimony could ruin the treasurer and the coast's paper together. A party that carries it to the Qizikuni has given them the chair and the morning after.

**If bypassed.** The Qizikuni agent finds him first.

### Scene Four: The Factor's Strongbox

**Purpose.** Optional: a heist, and the house's own secret.

**Situation.** The Khelathi factorage on the Guild Quarter of Besikar. The head factor's strongbox holds the Qizikuni notes.

**NPCs and threats.** The head factor, a patient host; his clerks; the factorage's guards. A party caught inside has offended a foreign power with a treaty port, and the house that hired it denies them.

**Player choices.** Steal the notes, copy them, leave them, or bargain with the factor.

**Clues and rewards.** Notes in the Qizikuni name, signed by Tirmana in the first months of the war.

**Consequences.** With the notes, the party holds the house that presses for the audit by the throat. Katira Tanaduni would pay for them, and so would the Compact.

**If bypassed.** The notes stay in the strongbox, and Katira reads only the books.

### Scene Five: The Arch-Consul's Table

**Purpose.** The climax: what goes on the order.

**Situation.** Danashu Kumuni's residence before the summer session, where every house sends someone to his table.

**NPCs and threats.** Danashu; Tirmana's clerk with the audit; Delzuru in person for the toll; and the Balinuni, who read the order before he does.

**Player choices.** Persuade Danashu to put the audit on the order, keep it off, raise the closed roll, or tell him the order is sold.

**Consequences.** The order of business decides what the session hears first. The party may have decided it.

**If bypassed.** The order goes to the Clerk as Danashu planned, and is sold before dawn.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

Tirmana's steward carries the letters, and the houses answer in writing what they would never say aloud. The Qizikuni agent finds the clerk on Khamisar and brings him to Besikar. Tirmana presses the audit at the summer session, and Danashu reads it after the veterans' petition. The head factor asks again, at dinner, when the houses mean to pay something down.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

The closed roll outlasts the session as talk at the houses' tables. If the audit is ordered, the treasury's gap is found, and the coast's paper is tested on the quays the next morning. Whoever holds the clerk's testimony, the Qizikuni notes or the houses' answers holds leverage over the next Grand Council and the houses that sit in it.
