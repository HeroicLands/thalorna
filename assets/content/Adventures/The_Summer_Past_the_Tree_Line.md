## The Situation and the Stakes {#the-situation}

The kingdom of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] keeps a balance that no reign has kept without incident. The crown is warm to the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] inside the walls and guides it beyond them, and the guides lead nowhere that matters. The [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] tolerate the arrangement because the alternative is worse, and the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] keep the [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]], the thin places of the mountains and deep forests, against every outsider.

This season the balance is under more weight than it was built for. The Order has stopped relying on the crown's guides. It hires mercenaries and guards in the inns of [[place-beravel|Péravel]] and [[place-liranel|Liranel]] to keep its scholars alive past the margins, and it sends them in numbers. By the thaw an Order party is camped below [[place-hldhr|the Held Hour]], another waits above [[place-sngngtrn|the Singing Tarn]], a survey on [[place-wndrngrdg|the Wandering Ridge]] has lost a guard, and the watch at [[place-fndr|the Fen Door]] on the Lísere coast is down to a single Warden until the passes open.

The frame runs one season, from the thaw to the autumn fair at [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]]. Three dates drive it:

- **The thaw**, when the passes open, the Wardens' relief can come down to the coast, and the Order's parties go up.
- **The prince's summer**, when [[being-ielaraskir|Prince Yélaras]] means to ride to [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]] with his University friends and ask the Wardens for leave to walk the margin of [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]].
- **The autumn fair at Elanmere**, where the heir's line is to be sung before the people who remember the genealogy differently, the House of Hálvireth's claim to the old blood must stand again before the Dhárgandi, and the great council of the tribes meets at the turn of the season.

The stake is the crown's ceiling. Élavendren legitimacy runs through the old blood, and the crown governs the interior only through Wardens who answer to the tribes. A crown believed to have taken the Order's side would face no rebellion. It would face the quiet verdict that it is no longer quite of the old blood.

## Who Moves It {#who-moves-it}

**The crown.** [[being-aurenthekir|Queen Áurenthe Kírethel]] keeps the balance as her house has always kept it, and cannot be seen to send anyone after her son. Her Liaison to the Tribes, [[being-chenirahares|Chénira Háresende]], carries the crown's word up to the tribes from [[place-gandelis|Gandelis]] and hires the hands the crown cannot be seen to hire. The crown guide [[being-lenirasrdln|Lénira Sérdalnde]] has brought every party she led home alive, and will not go past the line.

**The heir.** Yélaras holds that the old blood and the Order's books are not enemies, and means his request to the Wardens sincerely. His friends at the University mean to use his company as cover. His tutor, [[being-hlmsrthlmrnd|Hálmesar Thólmarnde]], has already gone inward from Liranel.

**The University.** The Rector, [[being-thorenveldar|Thoren Véldarin]], keeps the Order warm and the queen informed, and does not discuss what the fellows do beyond the tree line. The Fitter of Expeditions, [[being-pelraorsel|Pelra Orsel]], buys the stores, wagons and guards for every licensed survey and settles their accounts in the river-quarter inns. The Keeper of the Collections, [[being-netharahalre|Néthara Halren]], catalogs what comes back and records no provenance.

**The Wardens and the tribes.** The Warden-Captain [[being-golmarenkara|Gólmaren]] counts the places the Wardens cannot cover. At the Fen Door [[being-keldiradhar|Kéldira]] keeps the watch alone. At the Lodge, the common council of the tribes under its Speaker, [[being-khoradeldhrg|Khoradel]], is divided over whether to tell the crown plainly that a hired guard taken on an Araelwen is dealt with as a desecrator, and the [[affiliation-mtslvrfrsts|Moot of the Silver Forests]] under [[being-talmenatirin|Tálmena]] is divided over what to carry to the Sinale about a strip of bark cut from [[place-wkngstnd|the Waking Stand]].

**The Colleges and the groves.** [[being-kurthandlauv|Kurthand Láuvinnde]], Master of [[affiliation-clgline|the College of the Line]], has not said whether the College will sing the heir's line at the fair. The [[affiliation-groveconcl|Grove Council]] petitions the court against the Order's licenses every season and has begun to say that the crown that permits the hiring shares the breach. At Elanmere the grovekeeper [[being-lanthevileha|Lánthevile Halren]] is mapping burned shrines that the crown's garrison cannot explain.

## The Arcs

1. **The thaw: the coast and the Spine.** The Fen Door needs hands before the relief comes down ([[scenario-brdfrthdr|Bread for the Door]]), and the Order's camps below the Held Hour and above the Singing Tarn go to work ([[scenario-rpctfrmblw|The Rope Cut from Below]], [[scenario-sngwrttnnwx|A Song Written in Wax]]).
2. **The spring: Lirve's forest.** An Order fellow takes a cart and nets to [[place-wrghthllw|the Wrought Hollow]] ([[scenario-crpntrsnght|The Carpenter's Night]]), and a licensed block behind Liranel is found to have been felled on Áelendan ground ([[scenario-grvthtsntrs|The Grove That Is Not Ours]]).
3. **The summer: the prince.** Yélaras rides for Ésalosa with the Order's friends behind him ([[scenario-thprncssmmr|The Prince's Summer]]).
4. **The autumn: the capital and the fair.** A copied page of the Keeper's cipher surfaces in the book quarter ([[scenario-kprscphr|The Keeper's Cipher]]), and everyone who has a claim to make goes up to Elanmere to make it.

## Ways In {#ways-in}

1. **For the Order.** A fellow of the University, or the Fitter of Expeditions, hires a party in a Péravel or Liranel inn to keep a scholar alive for a season in the interior, half in advance, the ground unnamed until the party is past the tree line.
2. **For the crown.** The Liaison to the Tribes hires a party to ride with the prince's company, or to find out where an Order party means to go before it goes.
3. **For the Wardens.** A Warden, or a beacon family that sells the watch its bread, asks a party that is not the Order's to carry a warning, sit a watch or find a man the Wardens cannot go down to Péravel to ask.
4. **For the groves and the Colleges.** A grove-elder wants an object in a chapterhouse collection proved; a College master wants a pupil found, a report traced or a line sung.
5. **For a house.** A noble house sends a party up to the Elanmere fair with its bard, or wants a son brought home from the Order's pay before the Wardens find him.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

The thaw comes and the Wardens' relief reaches the Fen Door late; whatever came through at midwinter has had a season on the coast. The Order's camps on the Spine lose men, and one of them brings something down that the Wardens come to Péravel to ask about, which they have never done. The prince rides to Ésalosa with his friends, and one of them is caught cutting at the Waking Stand in the prince's company. The tribes' council speaks to the crown at the autumn turn, plainly and in public, and the heir's line is sung at Elanmere before an audience that has already judged him. The queen keeps her throne and loses the thing her house has always kept: the tribes' belief that the crown is of the old blood.

## The Secret {#the-secret}

**The ledger.** Néthara Halren's catalog records no provenance because she keeps it elsewhere: a private ledger in a cipher of her own, entering each object with the ground it came from, the day it was cut or lifted and the name of the guard who did it. She keeps it for the Order, so that an Araelwen can be found again, and for herself, because she believes the day will come when what has been taken must be put back. The Fitter of Expeditions does not know it exists. [[affiliation-sekran|The Sekran]] does. The frame does not say who reads it first.

**The survey of the Cut.** The royal archive keeps the survey that Hóuradel's engineers made of [[place-kngscut|the King's Cut]], adit by adit. Yélaras had it copied for his tutor at the University as a matter of history, and the tutor who has gone inward from Liranel is carrying the copy. The queen does not know. If the Wardens take it and read it, they know at once where it came from, and the Áelendan judge the prince by it. The deepest adit of the Cut is a grave.
