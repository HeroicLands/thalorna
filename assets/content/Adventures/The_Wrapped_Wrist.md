A Vedyari carries station inside the left wrist: two signs in the old abjad, one for the tarona and one for the kulpa, with an **Outcaste**'s struck through. A temple gate reads it, a sabhra reads it before it seats a speaker, and a court reads it before it hears a party. Wrapping the wrist is common and no offense in itself. The places where the wrap is never asked about are few: a ship's deck, the road under a traveler's cord, the high country above the last [[place-pssshrines|the Pass-Shrines]], and the pass-kingdoms. This frame follows people making for those places and the people sent after them, and it is built to carry a player character who has something on the wrist to hide.

## The Situation and the Stakes

**The courier.** [[being-pydvrnt|Piyadi Viranat]], an Outcaste born under the smithing clans' toll-gate in [[place-slvrgorges|the Silver Gorges]], carried the clans' sealed tally down to the [[affiliation-mrchntclctvvdyr|Collective]]'s agent at [[place-suryagarha|Suryagarha]] for eight years. She cannot read it. She was in the room every time the weighing was called aloud, and she remembers every figure. What the Collective returns to the customs-house is not what the gate weighed, and she knows the difference to the basket. At the end of the last open season she walked south instead of coming back up, with her wrist wrapped, making for [[place-chandrmukha|the Moon-Mouth]], where a crew signs on at the quay and nobody asks to see a wrist.

**The mines.** Most of the men below the Gorges' water-line are bonded. The Collective's senior foreman has warned its overseer four times in writing that the new faces are unsafe. The clans leased the rock and not the danger, and the bonded men have no seat in either quarrel.

**The river road.** A bonded weaver has run from a loom village of [[affiliation-vyalendra2|Vyalendra City-State]] and is hiding two days above [[place-nilatir|Nilatir]], where the households' agents watch the road. A runaway who reaches the yards is not sent back. One taken on the road above the town is.

**The deck.** On a ship nobody asks to see a wrist, and a coastal city that lives by its ships lives by men its temples will not admit. [[being-vjrtnsrntk|Vajratan Sirnataka]] was born an Outcaste on the beach at Chendramukhva. He commands [[affiliation-chendrapur|Chendrapur]]'s fleet as **Nau-senapavi** and goes up the river stair to the Moon House every season with his wrist bare, and for three years the temples have neither confirmed his appointment nor refused it.

**The other doors.** An [[affiliation-agmipantra|Agmi-pantra]] [Purgation]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} readmits an Outcaste to the order, and the orthodox hierarchy does not recognize the readmission. The academies of [[place-danurkot|Danurkot]] give every graduate the right to bear arms and hold land, whatever the wrist says, and the classical orthodox find it scandalous. A [[affiliation-thresholdkeepers|Threshold-keepers]] may put one question to the newly dead for an inheritance or a murder, and for nothing else.

The stakes are a woman's life and the figures she carries, whether the sea is still the door out of the order, and, for a player who hides a station, whether the party's own deeds hold when the wrap comes off.

## Playing a Station Hidden

Every station can be played, and each has a place where it adventures without asking leave:

- An **Outcaste** on a deck; on [[place-gudesroad|the Guides' Road]], hooded behind an [[affiliation-osketguides|Osket]] guide; in the pass-kingdoms, where an Outcaste bears arms freely; or above the last Pass-Shrine, where no rule of station reaches.
- A **Bonded Servant** as a household's agent carrying its token, or as a runaway making for the sea or the pass-kingdoms, where a runaway is not returned.
- A **Karmija** with an academy's grant of arms, as a levy, or on the road under the traveler's cord.
- A **Ritrija** on a sanctioned ordeal year, which is a reason to be anywhere; as a Pass-Shrine priest, a dream-reader at a foreign court or a Jvelita of the Agmi-pantra, who travels continually. A Ritrija's ordination is known at every temple gate, so a Ritrija leaves an office by walking out as an ascetic and never by hiding it.
- A **Sentija** in a host, a hired company of the western march, or as an academy graduate commanding a watch.

A character who wraps the wrist should know where the wrap stops working: at every temple gate, every sabhra and every court, and in a village, where a wrapped wrist means more than it does in a city that wears the wrap as a fashion.

## Who Moves It

[[being-pydvrnt|Piyadi Viranat]], the courier who walked south
: Watchful and quick, with a bleak humor that comes out by a fire at night. She wants a berth on a hull that clears the bar before the rains, and has never seen the sea.

The Collective's agent at Suryagarha
: He received the sealed tally for eight years and made the returns to the customs-house. He wants Piyadi found before she reaches the coast, and he wants it quiet.

[[being-tarvikatamrak|Tarvika Tamraksha]] and [[being-shlbrvdsmndl|Shilabir Vidsamondal]], of the Collective in the Gorges
: The overseer who holds the lease and the senior foreman who wrote the four letters.

[[being-jslvrdb|Jasili Varidaba]], keeper of the largest lodging house at Nilatir
: She lodges Outcastes and runaways on account and finds them berths. She does not ask to see a wrist and does not count the men who came down the river without a token.

[[being-garunyataray|Garunya Tarayara]], master of a coaster out of Nilatir
: A Karmija who went to sea at twelve because a deck was the one place in the town where nobody asked to see his wrist. He keeps it wrapped ashore out of habit.

[[being-vjrtnsrntk|Vajratan Sirnataka]], Nau-senapavi of Chendrapur
: Blunt, patient and very hard to embarrass. His confirmation is the question every runaway on the coast is waiting on, whether or not they know it.

[[being-blknsrtn|Balakin Saratin]], a bonded drover of the plateau
: Pledged against his father's debt at nine. He carries his household's token, notched with what he still owes, and has watched a dead droveway for twenty nights.

[[being-umrtdrtn|Umarti Daritan]] and [[being-nagaritgaj|Nagarit Gajabir]], academy graduates
: A Karmija who commands a march company, and the Commander of the Watch at [[place-vyalendra3|the City of Ten Thousand Looms]]. Both hold what an academy's grant gives, and both hold it among men who were born to it.

The bodies
: The smithing clans; the Collective; the households' agents on the river road; the **Koshadyoksha**'s shed at the head of Nilatir's street, where every bale is opened; the Agmi-pantra and its Jvelita; the Threshold-keepers; and [[affiliation-twilighths|the Twilight House]], which teaches the wrap as a fashion.

## Ways In

1. **Find the courier.** The Collective's agent hires the party to find Piyadi before she reaches the coast. He gives them her face, her cough and the road she must have taken, and does not say what she carries.
2. **Out of the bale.** Jasili asks the party to bring the runaway weaver down the river to the yards inside a bale, past the households' agents and out of it before the Koshadyoksha's shed opens it.
3. **A wrist of their own.** A player character's wrapped wrist is read at a temple gate, a sabhra or a bride's household, and the party has to decide how far its friends will stand by what is under the wrap.
4. **A field across the border.** A Karmija graduate of [[place-highdraw|the Academy of the High Draw]] has bought a field in a patron kingdom whose court will not register it. He asks the party to put his case before that kingdom's Dandanayeka, where the academy's grant has to be argued against the kingdom's own reading of his wrist.

## If the Party Does Nothing

- The agent's men reach Chendramukhva a week behind Piyadi. She is aboard a coaster outside the bar three days before the rains, and the figures go wherever the hull goes. The Collective's returns at Suryagarha stay short, and nobody at the customs-house asks why.
- The weaver is taken on the road above Nilatir and sent back to his loom village. His household adds the cost of the agents to his bond.
- A new face in the Gorges falls in during the open weeks, and the bonded men below it are entered in nobody's register.
- The temples go another season without confirming Vajratan, and the Nine Houses press their own candidates for the fleet.

## The Secret

One of the [Nine Houses]{.name data-type="affiliation"} of Chendrapur has a candidate of its own for the fleet. It has asked the **Purohata** to rule that a crew's wrists be read at the quay of Chendramukhva before a hull is let out over the bar. The temples of the port stopped saying anything about the deck long ago. A ruling would make them say it, and it would put Vajratan's bare wrist in front of every temple of the coast at once. The ruling is meant for him. It would also end the coast's one settled exception in the season Piyadi arrives, and every runaway on the waterfront would make for [[place-nilatir|Nilatir]], which answers to Vyalendra and not to the Moon House.

The Purohata has not decided. He is waiting to see whether Vyalendra's offer to speak for Vajratan's confirmation is made in earnest, and a party that learns of the ruling before he gives it can warn Vajratan, Jasili or Vyalendra, and each warning starts a different quarrel.
