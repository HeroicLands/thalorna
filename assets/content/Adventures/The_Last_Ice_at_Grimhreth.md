## Overview {#overview}

At the end of winter the ice on [[place-skathengrund|Skathengrund]], the Drowned Country, goes gray, and within days the thaw closes the cause-walk at [[place-grimhreth|Grimhreth]] for weeks. [[being-skthrkrvndvth|Skathrik ira Vandvith wur Mahnreth]], a [[lore-varokhiclt|Varokhi]] fur hunter of [[place-mahnryth|Mahnreth]], has a season's furs on a trapline of forty snares along the margin beyond the walk, more than one man can carry out in a day. He has hunted alone since a hunting-band he joined was lost to a nest. This year he needs carriers, and he needs them before the ice goes.

The walk is one nest's own path into the marsh, laid by the [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar Folk]] who keep it, and the Varokh villages of [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]] watch the paths to it. At [[place-hildholt|Hildholt]], the refuge village of the battle copse, [[being-wlfrnrfrdrnd|Wulfrún ira Frodrúnd wur Hildholt]] heard a step on the logs three nights ago that no other watcher heard, and has stood every watch since without blowing the horn. One horn from Hildholt brings the neighboring villages to its gate with their families, and at the end of winter the refuge has stores for its own people and no more.

The work is recovery from very dangerous ground: a season's furs, and what is left of a lost man, brought back out of the margin of the nests before the walk closes. The party enters as carriers Skathrik hires for a share of the bales, as strangers Wulfrún asks to look at the edge of the copse before the horn is blown, or as hunters who want a season's furs of their own and have heard that Skathrik knows the walk.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The dry logs.** Skathrik has watched the nest's logs at Grimhreth for three seasons and knows which are rotten and which are sound. He sells the crossing times to hunters of the Velanthian side for a pelt. Until this winter the nest that keeps the walk has never once failed to lay the logs again as they sink.

**The step on the logs.** A hunter of the Velanthian side bought a crossing time from Skathrik in the autumn, and three nights ago he went out on the walk at that hour to reach the margin before the thaw. That is the step Wulfrún heard: one man, going in. He has not come back.

**The Mire's crossing.** Before the freeze the [[being-ncrtcmr|Necrotic Mire]] crossed the walk far out, where it meets the deep marsh, and left a stretch of its logs black, soft and rotted through. The nests do not use a cause-walk the Mire has crossed until a season has passed, so the nest that keeps Grimhreth has neither walked it nor laid a log on it since. Skathrik went in on the winter ice, which bore him over the rotten stretch under the snow, and he has not seen it. His crossing times, and the one he sold, are good for a walk the nest keeps, and this winter the nest is not keeping it. The Mire lies up where creatures must cross and learns where they cross, and it waits in the deep water beside the rotten stretch.

**The nest's hunters.** With its own walk closed, the nest's hunters go out over the ice for food, and they have found Skathrik's line, which is what he wants least in any season. Some of his snares are empty and some are cut. A party on the line is food to them, as any person is.

**The horn.** Wulfrún is never wrong about a sound, and she knows that she heard one walker and not a raiding party. The elders take her warnings without a vote, so the horn waits on her word. If she gives it, the neighbors come in with their families, the Other Chief keeps the promise of room, and the stores of the last winter weeks are shared out among far more people than they were laid in for. She wants to know what she heard before she says anything.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> No raiding party comes out along Grimhreth this season: the nest that keeps the walk will not use it until a season has passed. The danger is on the walk and beyond it, to the people who go in: the rotted logs, the Necrotic Mire and the nest's hunters on the ice. The Grukar are never bargained with and never help anyone; they hunt, and a person they take is eaten. Nobody at Hildholt knows this. The party learns it by seeing the rotted logs, and Skathrik, who knows what a Mire's crossing means to a nest, can tell them.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The mercenary or soldier: carriers for the bales.** Skathrik wants a party that can carry his bales out across the walk in a day, and he pays in pelts.
- **The wanderer: the step on the logs.** Wulfrún wants a party to look at the edge of the copse before the horn is blown.
- **The thief: a season's furs.** A party that wants furs of its own learns at Hildholt that Skathrik knows the walk and is looking for hands, and that he alone knows where his snares are set.

## The Opening {#opening}

The palisade of Hildholt in the last hour of the night, three nights after the step, in a ring of short trees above a narrow stream. The snow in the hollows has gone gray and the air smells of thaw. A young woman with a burned hand stands at the gate with the horn on its peg beside her, and she has not touched it since.

> "One," she says, without turning from the dark. "Three nights ago, on the logs, out past the copse. Not a party. One. Nobody has come back." She keeps her hand away from the horn. "If I blow for nothing, every family on the neighbor tracks eats from our stores until spring."

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Edge of the Copse

**Purpose.** Learn what Wulfrún heard, and keep the horn on its peg or blow it.

**Situation.** First light at the edge of the battle copse, where the trees give way to the alder and reed of the marsh edge and the paths toward Grimhreth begin. The families who sheltered at Hildholt last winter have already begun bringing food to the gate against the promise of room, and a few of them are there this morning, asking whether the horn will blow.

**NPCs and threats.** Wulfrún, quiet and blunt, who answers what she is asked and no more. The families at the gate, who remember the promise.

**Player choices.** Walk the edge of the copse with Wulfrún, follow whatever track the party finds toward the walk, report to the elders, or wait at the gate for the hunter everybody says is coming.

**Clues and rewards.** In the crusted snow at the edge of the alder runs a single line of boot prints, one man under a heavy pack, three nights old, going toward the walk. No line comes back. Wulfrún reads it in a moment and does not blow the horn. A party that asks her why she does not simply call the neighbors in learns the arithmetic of the stores: the refuge feeds its own people to the spring and no more.

**Consequences.** The horn stays on its peg, and Wulfrún wants to know who the walker was. The party knows that someone has gone out onto the walk and not come back.

**If bypassed.** Wulfrún keeps the horn silent and stands the next night's watch as well, and she does not sleep.

### Scene Two: The Hunter at the Gate

**Purpose.** Hire on with Skathrik, and learn that he knows more about the walker than he says.

**Situation.** Mid-morning. Skathrik comes down the forest track to Hildholt's gate with a hand-sledge, snowshoes and coils of cord, looking for hands. Hildholt gives him water at the gate and no place inside, as no village on the margin gives a hunter a place to stop.

**NPCs and threats.** Skathrik, silent, watchful and dry, who says nothing about the marsh to a person who has not seen it. He offers a share of the bales, to be paid in pelts at the end, and wants to be on the walk by noon while the morning cold still holds the ice.

**Player choices.** Take his terms, bargain for a bigger share, tell him about the line of boots, or ask why a man who hunts alone wants carriers.

**Clues and rewards.** Told about the boots, he goes still and asks what hour Wulfrún heard the step. Given the hour, he says only that the walk is sound and that he knows it log by log. A party that watches him sees that he knew the walker would cross and when. He does not say that he sold the time, and he walks faster after that.

**Consequences.** The party goes out with Skathrik, or follows him at a distance, or stays behind with what it has learned.

**If bypassed.** Skathrik goes out alone at noon, and the party has only the boot prints to follow.

### Scene Three: Grimhreth on the Gray Ice

**Purpose.** The walk, and what has happened to it.

**Situation.** The walk runs from the forest edge out across the marsh, a line of sunken logs under the black water and a crust of gray ice above it, without rail or marker. Off the walk the ice still bears in the cold of the morning and gives under a foot by afternoon. Skathrik walks it by the logs he knows and sets every step where the last one was. The boot prints of the walker go ahead of the party all the way out.

**NPCs and threats.** The ice. The silence that comes on far out, where no bird calls for half a mile. Beyond it a stretch of the walk where the ice is stained black from beneath, fish lie frozen belly up in it, and the logs under it are soft. When the sun warms the crust a smell of carrion, sulfur and grave-earth comes off it.

**Player choices.** Cross the stained stretch on the ice while it bears, go around it on the open marsh ice, test the logs, or turn back.

**Clues and rewards.** Skathrik stops at the edge of the stained stretch and says nothing for a long time. Then he says that the Mire has crossed here, and that the nest will neither walk this path nor lay a log on it for a season. A party that understands this knows that no raiding party comes out of Grimhreth this season, which is news Hildholt's elders need, and that the hour Skathrik sold was good for a walk the nest keeps. The walker's prints cross the stretch on the ice and go on.

**Consequences.** The party is across the stained stretch with daylight left, or back at the forest edge, and Skathrik knows that his three seasons of knowledge are wrong this year.

**If bypassed.** If the party turns back here, Skathrik goes on alone, and the scenario closes as if the party did nothing beyond this point.

### Scene Four: The Line

**Purpose.** The furs, the nest's hunters and the walker.

**Situation.** Skathrik's forty snares run along the margin of the marsh, in reed and alder at the edge of the forest, where beaver and marten come to the slow water under the ice. His bales hang in a tree above the snow at the near end. Along the line the snow is trampled.

**NPCs and threats.** A handful of [[being-grukaruk|Grukar-Uk]], the nest's hunters, out over the ice for food: broad, splay-toed prints in an orderly file, a rank musky smell at the edge of the alder, snares emptied and some cut. A handful of Uk is a fight a seasoned party wins, but the noise carries across the ice and brings more of them from the nest. Somewhere beyond the line is what is left of the walker.

**Player choices.** Clear the line, fight the Uk or slip past them, look for the walker, or start back at once with what is in the tree.

**Clues and rewards.** The bales in the tree are the season's work and worth a season's pay at the forts' yards at [[place-brvvsh|Brevavesh]]. Beyond the end of the line, on an island of alder, lie the walker's pack, his bale of furs and his spear in snow trampled round with broad, splay-toed prints: the nest's hunters found him before anyone else did. The bale is a season's work for a family on the Velanthian side, and the pack holds a tally-cord knotted at the hour he crossed.

**Consequences.** The party leaves the line with full bales, half the line or none, and with the walker's pack or without it. The nest knows a party is on the margin.

**If bypassed.** Skathrik clears what he can alone in a day, and the Uk take the rest.

> [!WARNING]
> The Uk on the line do not take prisoners and do not trade. A party that stays on the margin after dark has the nest's hunters coming to its fire; the safest night on the line is the one spent walking back.

### Scene Five: The Thaw

**Purpose.** Getting out across the walk with the furs and the people.

**Situation.** The morning the thaw comes, the ice beside the walk turns to slush and open water, and the stained stretch can be crossed neither on the ice nor on its logs. The Mire has come up out of the deep water to the stained stretch, where everything that leaves the margin must cross: a heaving mound of black sludge, and a stench that turns the stomach at fifty paces.

**NPCs and threats.** The Mire, which surges over one victim at a time and rots whoever stays near it; a long fight with it is a losing one. The cold water. Skathrik, who wants his bales across before anything else.

**Player choices.** Burn the reed-beds in front of the Mire to turn it, lay cut alder across the rotten logs, leave the bales to lighten the crossing, or wait on the margin for a hard frost with the nest's hunters about.

**Clues and rewards.** Fire turns the Mire aside: the dry reed of the margin burns even at the end of winter, and a burned strip of bog sends it back into the deep water. Cut alder laid across the rotten logs bears a person at a time, and a laden carrier only if someone has thought to test it.

**Consequences.** The party comes out with the bales, without them, or not at all, and the thaw closes the walk behind it.

**If bypassed.** Whoever is still on the margin when the thaw comes waits for a frost that may not come before spring.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

Wulfrún hears no second step and keeps the horn silent. Skathrik goes in alone, finds the stained stretch, crosses it on the last of the ice, and comes out with what one man can carry; the rest of his line rots under the thaw. The Velanthian hunter's pack and bale stay on the bog. When the walk opens again, the hunters of the Velanthian side ask who sold him his hour, and Skathrik stops going to the forts' yards. Hildholt does not learn that Grimhreth was closed to the nest all season, and it stands every watch of the spring against a raid that never comes.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

- **Out with the bales.** Skathrik pays the party in pelts and sells the rest at the forts' yards. He hunted alone for ten years, and next autumn he asks for the same hands.
- **The walker's pack brought out.** His bale and pack go back to his people on the Velanthian side, and the knotted cord tells them the hour he crossed. If the party says who sold him that hour, Skathrik's trade in crossing times ends, or the party is asked to keep quiet about it.
- **The closed walk told.** If the party tells Hildholt's elders that the nest will not use Grimhreth this season, the horn hangs quiet until summer and the families stop bringing food to the gate. The nest lays the walk again when its season has passed, and the elders want to know when that is.
- **The nest at the end of the walk.** The party has seen the walk's far end and the nest's hunters on the ice. When the walk opens, the Skathwald villages want the nest found and burned, as they burned the nest at [[place-grimak|Grimak]], and the party knows the way in.
