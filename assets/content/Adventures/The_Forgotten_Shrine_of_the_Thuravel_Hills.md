## The Forgotten Shrine of the Thúravel Hills

A shrine hidden in the Thúravel Hills holds secrets of pre-Aurèldían traditions. The party must locate and explore it, uncovering what knowledge or power the ancients left behind – and what prices came with it.

### Overview

Deep in the Thúravel Hills of southern [[place-provenzrgn|Provènzia Region]] stands a shrine forgotten by official history but remembered in old songs and fragmentary records. Built long before the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían]] faith took root in the region, the shrine was dedicated to deities and traditions that predate human civilization or were supplanted centuries ago. Local legends speak of the shrine as a place of great power and terrible danger, where those who worship there gain favor or incur wrath from forces beyond human comprehension. The party is hired to locate the shrine, document its contents, and recover any artifacts or knowledge it contains.

### Stage One: The Commission & Research

The party is approached by:

- A scholar or antiquarian seeking pre-Aurèldían knowledge
- An agent of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] claiming authority over arcane sites
- A noble seeking to expand personal power through ancient practices
- An Áelendi guide or fae-touched individual with family memory of the shrine
- A priestess or priest of the Aurèldían faith concerned about the shrine's influence

**Initial Information:**

- The shrine's exact location is unknown; only fragmentary directions remain
- Historical records and old songs provide cryptic clues
- Local villagers speak of the shrine with mixture of reverence and fear
- Some believe the shrine holds a key to pre-Aurèldían magic or power
- Others believe approaching the shrine invites curse or divine wrath
- The shrine has not been disturbed in centuries, if not millennia

**NPCs:**

- A scholar with partial translations of old texts
- An elder villager who recalls old stories
- A suspicious Aurèldían priestess concerned about heresy
- A mysterious figure with knowledge of or connection to the old traditions

### Stage Two: The Search

The party must locate the shrine, a journey that may prove challenging:

- Navigate the Thúravel Hills using fragmentary maps and riddles
- Decipher old Provenzian or pre-Aurèldían language clues
- Encounter natural hazards: difficult terrain, weather, wild creatures
- Question locals who may know oral traditions or old paths
- Uncover markers or signs left by ancients
- Possibly contact fae-touched Áelendi who have cultural memory of the site

**Complications:**

- The shrine is protected by natural or magical wards that hide it
- Someone actively works to prevent the shrine's discovery
- The party is followed or harassed by those who consider the shrine profane
- Environmental hazards grow more severe as the party approaches
- A party member is drawn by dreams or visions toward the shrine (welcome or unwelcome)

### Stage Three: Approaching the Shrine

As the party draws near, they discover:

- A clearing or hidden valley containing the shrine
- Architecture that predates Provenzian settlement (possibly connected to the Sinalë or other ancients)
- Inscriptions in archaic languages and pre-Aurèldían symbols
- Weathered statues or idols of forgotten deities
- Signs of both religious veneration and deliberate damage (the shrine was likely desecrated or sealed)
- The air feels thick with age and power; reality seems thin

**Encounters:**

- Manifestations of the shrine's spiritual power: visions, whispers, presences
- Guardians or servants of the old traditions (spectral, fae, or otherwise)
- A rival expedition or faction seeking the shrine's knowledge
- Environmental hazards woven into the sacred space

### Stage Four: Exploration & Discovery

Within the shrine, the party discovers:

**Physical Elements:**

- An inner sanctum or altar chamber
- Artifact(s) of significance: relics of the old worship, tools of power, or records
- Murals, carvings, or texts describing pre-Aurèldían beliefs, practices, or history
- Multiple chambers dedicated to different deities or purposes
- Hidden chambers containing dangerous secrets or powerful items

**Spiritual Elements:**

- Contact with the spiritual presences or entities bound to the shrine
- Tests or challenges of worthiness or understanding
- Visions revealing pre-Aurèldían history or cosmic truth
- A direct manifestation of a forgotten deity or powerful spirit
- The consequences of disturbing the shrine: what obligations or curses attach to those who enter

**Key Discoveries:**

- The shrine was sealed or abandoned intentionally (why?)
- The old traditions are not entirely dead (worshippers still exist)
- The shrine holds power that could influence [[place-provenzrgn|Provènzia Region]] (political, magical, or spiritual)
- A warning or prophecy inscribed in the shrine
- The shrine's true purpose is different from what history or legend claims

### Stage Five: Resolution & Consequences

The party must decide what to do with the shrine and its secrets:

**Options:**

- **Preserve & Protect:** Seal the shrine again, keeping it hidden from those who would exploit it
- **Document & Report:** Share findings with scholars, the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían]] Church, or the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]]
- **Claim & Exploit:** Attempt to use the shrine's power for personal benefit or to advance the party's agenda
- **Cleanse & Consecrate:** Perform rituals to bring the shrine under Aurèldían authority or neutralize its power
- **Leave & Warn:** Depart without disturbing further and warn others away

**Complications & Consequences:**

- The shrine is sacred to hidden worshippers, who oppose its disturbance
- The [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían]] Church demands the shrine be desecrated or brought under their control
- The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] insists on studying any artifacts or magical sites discovered
- Possession of items from the shrine draws unwanted attention (fae entities, ancient spirits, cults)
- The shrine's power cannot be fully contained; knowledge of its existence spreads
- The party is bound by oath or curse to protect or further the shrine's original purposes
- A powerful entity awakens or manifests as a result of the shrine's disturbance

**Lasting Impact:**

- The party's knowledge of pre-Aurèldían traditions makes them targets or assets for various factions
- Artifacts recovered may prove invaluable or dangerously cursed
- The shrine becomes a focus point for larger conflicts (factions vying for its control or power)
- The party may be drawn into worship of the old traditions or become their defenders
