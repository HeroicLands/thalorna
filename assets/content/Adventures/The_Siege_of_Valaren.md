The coastal City of Válaren lies under blockade by forces unknown. Adventurers must break the siege and uncover who benefits from the city's starvation.

## Overview

Válaren, a crucial trading port on the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Provenzian]] coast, has been cut off from supply routes by a mysterious naval blockade. The city's defenders are few, supplies dwindling, and morale cracking. Local nobles blame foreign powers – some whisper of [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian]] imperial intrigue, others suspect [[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Kingdom of Nordheim]] raiders seeking plunder. The truth may be far more complex.

## Stage One: Initial Investigation

The party arrives in or near Válaren as the blockade enters its third month. They encounter desperate refugees, inflated prices, and conflicting rumors. Key information includes:

- Three ships maintain the blockade, rotating positions to avoid being spotted together
- A local merchant family has mysteriously gone silent
- The city's grain reserves will last another six weeks at best
- A prominent Provenzian noble recently visited Válaren before the blockade began

**NPCs:**

- The city's military commander (desperate but honorable)
- A merchant captain trying to break through the blockade
- A mysterious messenger with cryptic warnings

## Stage Two: Reconnaissance

The party must investigate the blockading ships and gather intelligence. They might:

- Pose as merchants attempting to trade with the blockaders
- Sneak out to reconnoiter the naval positions
- Interview prisoners or defectors from the blockade
- Investigate the silent merchant family's connections

**Complications:**

- Patrols increase as the party shows interest
- A spy in the city reports their activities to the blockaders
- Weather changes make naval operations dangerous

## Stage Three: Uncovering the Conspiracy

Investigation reveals that a powerful Provenzian noble – seeking to drive down grain prices or consolidate power – hired mercenaries to blockade the city. Alternatively, foreign agents have orchestrated the blockade to weaken [[place-provenzrgn|Provènzia Region]]'s economy.

**Key Revelation:**

- The blockading ships are crewed by mercenaries, not military forces
- A corrupt local official provided intelligence on supply routes
- Letters or documents implicate the true conspirators

## Stage Four: Breaking the Blockade

The party must devise and execute a plan to break the blockade. Options include:

- Gather defenders and launch a coordinated attack from the city and sea
- Use espionage to turn the blockaders against one another
- Arrange a supply convoy while the blockade is distracted
- Discover and exploit an unblockaded supply route inland
- Arrange a parley and negotiate or bribe the mercenary captain

**Combat/Challenge Encounters:**

- Naval combat (if the party can acquire ships)
- Skirmish with blockade patrols
- Infiltration of the blockade commander's ship

## Stage Five: Aftermath & Consequences

- If the party succeeds, Válaren is relieved and supplies begin flowing again
- The conspiracy is exposed, bringing political consequences for the conspirators
- The party may face retaliation from powerful interests
- Evidence of the conspiracy becomes leverage in larger political games
- The city's gratitude opens doors – or closes them, depending on who was behind the blockade
