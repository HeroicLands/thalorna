### Background and Creation

The **Maul of Gashang** is a brutal spiked mace crafted by the priest-artificers of the Black Flame, designed to embody the god’s cruel and destructive nature. Forged from **blackened steel**, the head of the mace features eight blades, with jagged, cruel spikes meant to tear flesh and break bones. The weapon's sinister design reflects its purpose: to vanquish foes in the most gruesome and painful manner possible.

The blades seem to glow with a faint ember-like intensity when soaked in blood. The handle is wrapped in red leather taken from flayed Sacred Forge priests, inscribed with prayers to the Black Flame, calling for violence, destruction, and torment.

Legends suggest that the maul was forged in the heart of a volcano by Black Flame priest-artificers, who channeled the pure essence of fire and pain from the demons, the Black Flame’s demonic legions of flame. Each of the eight blades of the maul are said to represent a key battle or sacrifice offered to the Black Flame. Legend holds that the spikes were cooled in the blood of enemies and then blessed in the flames of the Black Flame's altar.

### The Lost History of the Maul of Gashang

### **The Last Battle in the Blood Lands**

The **Maul of Gashang** was last wielded in a brutal conflict known as the **Battle of Céravel’s Blood Hills**, fought in the highlands near Céravel. This battle was a climactic confrontation between the forces of the Black Flame, led by the Cohorts of Gashang, and the Knights of the Sacred Forge. The site became infamous for its sheer brutality, as the followers of the Black Flame reveled in slaughter and pain, while the Forge faithful sought to stem the tide of chaos.

The **Maul of Gashang** was wielded by a Black Flame warlord named **Hazarak Bloodhand**, known for his cruelty and mastery of war. Hazarak led the charge against the Sacred Forge forces, and it is said that he wielded the maul with such ferocity that even the most devout Sacred Forge knights faltered in his wake. The weapon’s unholy aura terrified even the most battle-hardened soldiers, while its flames set the battlefield alight, turning the hillside into a scene of unrelenting carnage.

As the battle raged on, Hazarak’s forces began to gain the upper hand, pushing back the Sacred Forge lines. However, in the chaos of battle, Hazarak was struck down by a surprise flank led by **Sir Gavain**, a renowned Sacred Forge commander. With Hazarak’s death, the Black Flame forces began to falter, and the maul, still clutched in the hands of its master, fell to the blood-soaked ground.

### **The Maul’s Disappearance**

After Hazarak’s fall, the battle did not end cleanly. Both sides were devastated, and the bloodied fields became the final resting place of countless warriors. The maul, now abandoned, was sought after by both Black Flame cultists and the Forge faithful, but a sudden violent storm – a phenomenon some claimed was caused by the Black Flame itself – forced both sides to retreat, leaving the battlefield strewn with bodies and the sacred relic lost.

For years after the battle, the area became known as the **Blood Hills**, and few dared to approach the cursed land. Legends grew about spirits wandering the battlefield, and those brave enough to venture into the hills reported seeing flickers of light – like embers in the dark – where the maul was thought to have been lost.

### **The Cave and the Traps**

Over time, the maul was moved from the battlefield to a nearby cave, a secret Black Flame shrine. Black Flame zealots who remained loyal to Hazarak’s memory found the weapon and sought to keep it hidden until a worthy successor could reclaim it. They created a series of traps and obstacles within the cave to ensure that only those truly devoted to the Black Flame, and worthy of wielding such a powerful weapon, could reach the **Maul of Gashang**.

1. **Blood Gate**: The first obstacle is a stone door etched with symbols of the Black Flame. It is locked by a **blood sacrifice** mechanism, requiring the blood of a willing devotee of the Black Flame to open (hint: the blood doesn't have to be fresh). Without the proper ritual or sacrifice, the door remains sealed.

2. **Fire Pits**: After entering the cave, the adventurers must navigate a series of narrow passages over natural pits filled with smoldering coals. Though not as deadly as lava, the heat is suffocating, and any misstep could send someone falling into the embers, causing grievous burns. A devotee of the Black Flame can find the path in Black Flame prayers (known by Black Flame devotees).

3. **Trapped Altar**: Deeper within the cave is an altar that appears to be a place of worship, but in truth, it is a trap designed to test the faith of those who approach. If someone without the proper devotion or understanding of Black Flame rites approaches, the altar releases a burst of **flaming gas**, causing the cave to momentarily fill with scorching fire. Knowledge of Black Flame prayers can disarm this trap.

4. **Guardian of Gashang**: The final obstacle is a guardian left behind by the zealots. This **Vayêra**, a demonic being, has been bound to protect the relic. It will attack any intruders it deems unworthy, but those who pass the Black Flame’s tests or show proper devotion may avoid conflict with the guardian.

5. **The Maul's Resting Place**: At the end of the cave, in a chamber wreathed in shadow and flickering with ember-like light, lies the **Maul of Gashang**. The relic rests upon a stone pedestal surrounded by inscriptions detailing its power and history. However, touching the maul without the proper rites invokes the Black Flame’s curse, causing the weapon to burn the unworthy with unholy fire.

### Properties and Abilities

**Flaming Wrath**: The handle of the **Maul of Gashang** is hollow, and is filled with a concoction called "Fire of Gashang." When swung, small holes in the handle at the base of the blades leak this fluid onto the blades, which can then be set on fire. When a foe is hit, the concoction sprays on the opponent, dealing additional fire damage to enemies with each strike and leave the victim’s wounds burning with an "unholy fire."

- **Mechanics**: The fiery concoction, containing naptha and quicklime, is reactive to both heat and water, and is viscous. When hit, the victim suffers an additional d4+0f damage each round for 1-3 rounds, possibly igniting fabric or leather armor or clothing.

2. **Bloodletting Spikes**: The spikes on the **Maul of Gashang** are designed to maximize pain and suffering. Strikes with the weapon greatly increase the chance of causing grievous, bleeding wounds, making it difficult for enemies to stop the bleeding.

- **Mechanics**: Increases the chance of causing **bleeding wounds**. All S3 and higher injuries are treated as bleeders.

3. **the Black Flame’s Favor**: When wielded by a true servant of the Black Flame, the maul may grant the wielder **the Black Flame’s Favor**, enhancing their strength and resilience in battle. The wielder gains temporary resistance to pain and increased strength after killing an enemy.

- **Mechanics**: After slaying an opponent, for one minute (10 rounds) the wielder is immune to all shock except KIA, although their injuries continue to compound normally.

4. **Unholy Aura**: The **Maul of Gashang** exudes a palpable aura of dread and terror.

- **Mechanics**: Enemies, in particular the Forge faithful, within 30 feet of the wielder suffer ABE 1.

5. **Holy Relic of the Black Flame**: The weapon is obviously a weapon of the Black Flame. the Forge faithful will correctly fear using it.

- **Mechanics**: Any follower of the Sacred Forge who uses the weapon and injures an opponent must make a test against Will. MF will result in loss of action in the next turn, while CF will result in the attacker suffering shock where Shock Index is 6 + Shock Roll.
