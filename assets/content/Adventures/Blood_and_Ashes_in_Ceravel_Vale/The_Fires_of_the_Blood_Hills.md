## Teaser

The secrets of Céravel Vale lead to its most perilous chapter yet. Deep within the ominous Blood Hills lies a place shrouded in legend, rumored to conceal a powerful artifact capable of altering the balance of power in the region. The adventurers must race against time to uncover its secrets before it falls into the wrong hands. Along the way, they will face treacherous terrain, shadowed ruins, and unknown challenges that will test their mettle at every turn. Harrowing choices await—choices that will not only define their legacy but determine the fate of the Vale itself.

## Overview

In the final part, the adventurers venture into the Blood Hills to find the shrine where the Black Flame artifact is hidden. The adventurers must decide whether to claim the artifact for themselves, destroy it, or return it to a Sacred Forge authority.

## Adventure Outline

### The Perilous Path through the Blood Hills

- **Description:** The adventurers follow Kílan's notes and the map they obtained, venturing into the desolate and cursed Blood Hills. The land itself seems hostile, with twisted trees, sudden rock slides, and an unnatural silence. The area is dotted with the remnants of old Black Flame battles, like abandoned shrines, skeletons, and burned soil.
- **Challenge:** The Blood Hills are treacherous, with natural hazards like unstable terrain, sinkholes, and sudden steep inclines. To complicate matters, the adventurers encounter lingering traces of Black Flame magic—areas tainted with curses that sap their strength or fill them with unease.
- **Optional Encounter:** They may encounter the restless spirits of warriors who died in ancient battles, haunting the area as shades that test the adventurers' resolve and try to scare them away.
- **Clue:** The adventurers come across faded carvings or symbols that reinforce they're on the right path. These carvings mention both the shrine and the V'hir, a powerful demon-like guardian bound to protect the artifact, hinting at the final challenge ahead.

### Black Flame Ambush in the Hills

- **Description:** As the adventurers approach the shrine's location, they are ambushed by a group of Black Flame soldiers, led by an emissary of Kâldin al Lénavel. These soldiers have been following the adventurers, hoping to seize the artifact themselves or prevent the adventurers from interfering.
- **Challenge:** The ambush is well-planned, with soldiers taking advantage of the rocky terrain for cover. The adventurers must contend with ranged attackers on high ground, skilled Black Flame zealots in melee, and perhaps a Black Flame priest using dark magic to weaken them.
- **Reward:** If the adventurers defeat the ambush, they capture the emissary or find notes on the soldiers, revealing that Kâldin has been tracking their movements. These notes reinforce the artifact's importance to the Zûr clan, and hint that the shrine is extremely close.

### Entering the Shrine and Overcoming Traps

- **Description:** The adventurers reach the hidden shrine, a foreboding structure built into the mountainside and decorated with Black Flame symbols. The air is thick with the smell of burned incense and ash. They notice ancient runes on the stonework and faint lights flickering within the darkened halls.
- **Challenge:** The shrine's entrance is guarded by deadly traps, remnants of Black Flame ingenuity designed to deter intruders. These traps include a **Blood Gate** (requiring a blood sacrifice to unlock), smoldering fire pits with narrow, crumbling pathways, and pressure-plate triggered gas traps that fill the room with choking smoke or flame.
- **Role-Playing Opportunity:** The adventurers might encounter a surviving Black Flame devotee or a Zûr clan loyalist who believes the adventurers are unworthy. This individual might offer guidance in exchange for a promise to leave the artifact untouched or as a bargaining chip for their life.
- **Clue:** Carvings near the inner chamber describe the V'hir, the demon guardian bound to protect the artifact. They warn that only a true servant of the Black Flame can survive its wrath, raising the stakes for the adventurers as they move forward.

### Confrontation with the V'hir, Guardian of the Shrine

- **Description:** Deeper in the shrine, the adventurers reach a large chamber wreathed in shadow and flickering with eerie light. In the center stands the V'hir—a fearsome demon bound by Black Flame priests to guard the artifact. It's a towering, flame-wreathed creature exuding a palpable aura of dread.
- **Challenge:** The V'hir attacks intruders with devastating strength and dark fire-based magic, and it's impervious to standard weapons. The adventurers must find creative ways to weaken or evade it, potentially using Black Flame prayers they've discovered or exploiting weaknesses in its binding ritual.
- **Twist:** During the battle, the adventurers may notice that the V'hir hesitates slightly when they invoke certain Black Flame phrases or symbols, suggesting that it's bound by magical commands. If they use these phrases, they may force it into submission temporarily, though it will regain control and strike again if the adventurers don't act quickly.
- **Reward:** Once they've subdued or banished the V'hir, they gain access to the final chamber, where the artifact rests on an altar surrounded by engraved Black Flame prayers and spells.

### The Black Flame Artifact and Final Decision

- **Description:** In the shrine's inner sanctum, the adventurers find the artifact resting on an ornate, blood-stained altar. The room is filled with a sinister energy, and the artifact—the [[scenario-maulgashng|Maul of Gashang]]—radiates a dark, intoxicating power that calls to anyone who enters.
- **Challenge:** The adventurers face an intense moral and practical decision. They can take the artifact for themselves, destroy it, or choose to bring it to a Sacred Forge authority. Each choice carries risks: possessing it might corrupt or curse the wielder, destroying it could unleash its power unpredictably, and turning it over to authorities could earn them enemies among Black Flame cultists or the Zûr clan.
- **Resolution:** As they make their choice, they may experience visions or whispers from the Black Flame, promising them power if they embrace the artifact's purpose. This moment is tense, filled with the potential for betrayal, temptation, or introspection. The decision they make will determine the campaign's legacy and the adventurers' role in the fate of the Céravel Vale.

### Possible Ending Paths

- **Taking the Artifact:** If the adventurers claim the artifact, they might gain dark powers but incur the wrath of both the Church of the Sacred Forge and Black Flame cults, who will seek it out. This choice opens the door for future conflict with these factions.
- **Destroying the Artifact:** Destroying the artifact prevents its misuse, but it could also have unforeseen consequences, such as unleashing a curse tied to the relic. This act may enrage the Zûr clan and Black Flame followers, making the adventurers lifelong enemies.
- **Handing Over the Artifact:** Giving the artifact to a Sacred Forge authority may prevent immediate dangers, but it could draw the Zûr clan's ire and incite a conflict between the Forge faithful and Black Flames in the region.
