### Summary of Provènzia

#### Geography

Provènzia is a region of diverse landscapes and climates, ranging from temperate plains to rugged mountains rich in minerals. Key geographical features include the Áelendan ranges and Lúnavren mountains, known for their deposits of precious metals and gemstones, and the fertile Ypena and Gaira river valleys, essential for agriculture and trade. The region also contains coastal settlements and riverine trade hubs that connect it to neighboring realms.

The kingdom’s strategic position as a crossroads of trade and its mineral wealth make it both an economic hub and a target for external aggression. The Áelendan ranges host hidden mining communities, while urban centers like Páravel serve as royal and commercial capitals. Towns such as Ménavrel and Galíshavel are vital trade and production centers, though Ménavrel suffers from political unrest.

#### Cultural and Political Dynamics

Provènzia's cultural landscape is marked by tension between the indigenous **Thânevari**, who emphasize traditional local governance, and the **Provenzians**, descendants of Nordheimer settlers. These groups clash over land, governance, and cultural identity. The Nordheimer conquest led to the establishment of a centralized monarchy, but local traditions persist in rural areas, fueling resentment and periodic rebellions.

The kingdom's religious tolerance is another area of cultural complexity. While the Church of the Sacred Forge is officially favored and politically powerful, other faiths, including Táranon and Black Flame traditions, coexist uneasily. This pluralism stems from policies established by the first king, Bjan Lédravren, to unify his supporters. However, the growing dominance of the Forge faithful has caused friction, particularly in regions where traditional or minority religions hold sway.

#### Major Personalities

- **King Aldrévan alrí Lédravren**: The reigning monarch of Provènzia, King Aldrévan strives to maintain unity in a kingdom marked by cultural and political divisions. Known for his pragmatic leadership, he faces challenges from both ambitious nobles and external threats from Tarvénia.

- **Princess of Gwéfavel**: A powerful magnate whose clan's influence was diminished under the previous king. Resentful of past failures, she focuses on restoring her family’s prestige, leveraging her stronghold in Gwéfavel to exert significant regional influence.

- **Earl of Dégavel (Rútal alrí Pélanvri)**: A prominent and influential noble, the Earl of Dégavel is embroiled in political rivalries, particularly with the Count of Áthavel. His leadership in the southern regions of Provènzia gives him significant sway in both court politics and military strategy.

- **Jârvel al Pélanvri**: Brother to the Earl of Dégavel and a key military leader, Jârvel serves as the Élgâr of Céravel Castle and **Marshal of the Western Army** of Provènzia. Tasked with defending the kingdom’s easternmost frontier, his command is critical in managing both banditry and incursions from Tarvénia.

- **Count of Áthavel**: A politically embattled noble facing internal family disputes. His clashes with the Earl of Dégavel reflect broader struggles among the magnates, which weaken the kingdom’s cohesion during times of external threat.

- **Álegar alrí Zûravel**: Baron of Zûravlen and Kývarel, Álegar is a prominent supporter of the Order of the Burning Brand. His clan’s alignment with this religious faction places him at the center of significant political and spiritual influence.

- **Avaârl alrí Zylávren**: Earl of Hárivren and the most senior Black Flame noble in Provènzia, Avaârl commands great respect and power. As Álegar’s overlord, he exemplifies the integration of nobility with the religious hierarchy, further complicating Provènzia’s cultural and political landscape.

#### Historical Primer

Provènzia’s history is marked by cycles of conquest, rebellion, and consolidation. The kingdom’s foundation lies in the **Nordheimer Invasion of TR513**, led by Bjan Lédravren. His coalition of Nordheimer warlords overthrew the petty kingdoms of Thânevar, establishing Provènzia as a unified realm. Bjan’s pragmatic policies of religious tolerance and power-sharing among the Nordheimer elite and local leaders created a fragile unity, which has been repeatedly tested over the centuries.

One of the most significant events in Provènzia’s history was the **War of the Princes (TR653-655)**, also known as the **Céravian Rebellion**. This civil war erupted over succession disputes following the death of King Almed Lédravren, who left no clear heir. Competing claims from rival factions of the royal family and their noble allies plunged the kingdom into chaos. The Nordheimer-descended Provenzian factions, which emphasized centralized rule and military strength, clashed with Thânevar factions advocating for a return to regional autonomy.

The war devastated large swathes of the kingdom. Regional economies collapsed, and towns such as Ménavrel became battlegrounds for opposing forces. The conflict ended with the accession of King Ervas Lédravren, a compromise candidate supported by both moderate Nordheimer and Thânevar factions. However, the scars of the war linger, with noble families still nursing grudges and local governance weakened in many regions.

The _War of the Princes_ also highlighted the deep cultural divide between the Thânevar and Provenzian populations. The Thânevar resistance to Nordheimer dominance crystallized during the Céravian Rebellion, as traditional leaders leveraged popular discontent to rally their followers. This divide remains a source of tension in modern Provènzia, particularly in rural regions where memories of Nordheimer conquest are still vivid.

Provènzia’s subsequent history has been shaped by efforts to recover from the war’s devastation. While successive monarchs have worked to strengthen royal authority, the balance of power between the crown, nobles, and local leaders remains precarious. This legacy of division continues to influence both internal politics and the kingdom’s capacity to respond to external threats.

#### Major Current Conflicts

Provènzia faces a multi-layered crisis of internal and external conflicts, each feeding into the other and threatening the kingdom’s fragile stability. Internally, the noble families vie for influence, with long-standing rivalries between magnates like the Earl of Dégavel and the Count of Áthavel creating political gridlock. Local unrest in towns like Ménavrel, fueled by factional disputes and economic instability, has further eroded the monarchy's control. The cultural tension between the Thânevar and Provenzian populations continues to simmer, with rural Thânevari resisting central authority and perceived Nordheimer dominance, occasionally erupting into open rebellion. Religious divides compound these issues, as the Church of the Sacred Forge’s growing power alienates other faiths, particularly in regions with strong Black Flame or Táranite traditions.

Externally, Provènzia remains under constant threat from Tarvénia, whose rulers exploit Provènzia’s internal weaknesses to press territorial claims. Border skirmishes are frequent, particularly in contested regions like the southeast, where Tarvénia forces test Provènzia’s defenses. The kingdom also faces the resurgence of banditry and piracy, disrupting trade and straining regional lords’ ability to maintain order. These external pressures exacerbate internal divisions, as magnates and military leaders, distracted by personal rivalries, struggle to present a united front against external aggression. The interplay of these conflicts creates a precarious balance that threatens to tip the kingdom into chaos.

#### Céravel, Tarvénia, and Léravren

**Céravel** serves as a critical frontier region for Provènzia, encompassing several manorial estates and villages anchored by its castle town. Céravel is commanded by Jârvel al Pélanvri, who manages defenses against Tarvénia. The area is also troubled by banditry, which local lords, such as **Târlas al Pélanvri**, work to suppress. Its position near the southeastern border makes it a frequent flashpoint for conflict, with local lords balancing the demands of defense, governance, and trade.

**Léravren** lies to the northeast of Céravel and serves as a gateway to the mountains and forests vital for resources and trade. The region is also closely associated with the Black Flame Order, particularly through the Zur clan. **Kâldin al Lénavel**, lord of Léravren, is a key player in the unfolding political and religious conflicts. His connections to the Zur clan and the Black Flame faith deepen the complexity of both local governance and regional intrigue.

**Tarvénia** remains the greatest external threat to Provènzia, persistently challenging its southeastern borders. A larger and more unified neighbor, Tarvénia exploits Provènzia’s internal divisions and vulnerabilities. Its skirmishes along the contested territories exacerbate tensions, draining resources and testing the kingdom’s fragile unity.
