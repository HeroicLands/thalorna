## The Hush at the Heart of the Wood

In the deep forest there is a circle where nothing grows and no bird calls, and the forest's dead come out of it at dusk. The party must cross the [[place-slntclrng|Silent Clearing]], reach its heart, and face what sits there. The wound cannot be closed. It can only be held, and the cost of holding it is paid by someone.

### Hook

Offer the entry that suits the hero.

- **The thief.** A dead hunter's tally-cord, sold in a river-port, lists a cache of ivory and trade-iron left at the rim of a place where the birds stopped. The hunter did not come back for it.
- **The mercenary or soldier.** A river-tribe's war-leader has lost three villages to the Quiet Dead and hires spears to go to the source. The pay is in dyes, hardwood and a promise of tribute.
- **The leader.** The chieftain's own scouts came back silent: they could speak but not hear, and they cannot say what they saw. The chieftain must decide whether to take the party's counsel or the shamans'.
- **The lone wanderer.** The wanderer has walked a ten-day trail toward a silence no map shows. Behind them a forest tribe has been moved on by something at night.

The place note is [[place-slntclrng|The Silent Clearing]].

## Scene One: The Line of Bells

**Purpose.** Introduce the Hush, the rim's watchers and the first guardian.

**Situation.** At the forest's edge the birds fall silent, then the insects. A circle of dark-wood posts hung with clapperless bronze bells stands in the trees. Beyond them is a ring of scorched earth, and beyond that the grey bare soil of the Clearing.

**NPCs and threats.**

- **The Ash-Warden.** A gaunt, soot-streaked forest shaman who keeps the rim. He burns the Quiet Dead when they rise and renews the ash-paths each season. He is half mad from long silence, fiercely protective, and suspicious of anyone who wishes to go in. He speaks little and hits first.
- **Bone-grey carrion-lizards,** which feed on the leavings of the dead at the rim.

**Player choices.**

- _Force:_ fight the Ash-Warden. He is tough, knows the ground and burns what he fights.
- _Stealth:_ circle the rim and cross at a gap in the bells.
- _Guile:_ claim to be the Leopard King's men. The Warden knows the Court's hunters and may know the lie.
- _Parley:_ the Warden answers gesture. He has lived for years among those who do not speak, and he shows the safe paths to a party that signs honestly.

**Clues and rewards.** The ash-paths run in spiral lines, each one marked at its end with a skull. The Warden's tally-cord records the dead he has burned: more than a person can count.

**Consequences.** Killing the Warden leaves the rim unwatched. Befriending him earns torches, oil and a map.

**If bypassed.** A party that crosses without meeting the Warden meets him in Scene Three, at dusk, among the dead.

## Scene Two: The Hush

**Purpose.** A puzzle of silence: crossing without sound, and with it.

**Situation.** Beyond the bells, sound dies. A shout is lost within a few paces, and the air feels thick. The ground slopes toward the centre. Flat stones set in the grey soil mark safe paths.

**NPCs and threats.**

- **The Hush itself.** It swallows speech, bells and the ring of metal. It does not swallow sight. The party must communicate by signs. A lantern's light carries.
- **Dead ground.** Some of the stones are false. They are cold and flat and mark graves.

**Player choices.**

- _Force:_ cross in a rush, ignoring the stones. The soil beneath gives way in patches.
- _Stealth:_ follow the ash-lines, step by step.
- _Guile:_ rig a rope-line from the bell-posts to the centre.
- _Parley:_ the Warden, if he lives and was befriended, walks the party across.

**Clues and rewards.** Hunters' remains lie at the false stones, with their gear: copper arrowheads, a clay whistle, a bone flute, a few trade-beads. The paths bend toward the centre in a spiral.

**Consequences.** A false step wakes a grave, and one of the Quiet Dead rises early.

**If bypassed.** A party that skips the Hush has no map and reaches the heart blind, arriving at dusk.

## Scene Three: The Rising

**Purpose.** A setback: dusk comes, and the dead rise.

**Situation.** As the light fails, the grey soil heaves. Hands push up through the ground. Figures climb out, silent and wet with no water, and turn toward the party. They make no sound, because in the Hush there is none.

**NPCs and threats.**

- **The Quiet Dead.** [[being-damut|Damutu]] of every kind of person the forest has lost: hunters, children, a Khelâthi factor in a rotted coat. They are slow in the open and fast in the dark. They fight in silence, grasping and dragging, and their bites and scratches leave a cold in the wound. They hunt by sight and are drawn to light, though they cannot be turned by it.
- **Any party member who died** in earlier scenes. They rise among the others.

**Player choices.**

- _Force:_ fight a rear-guard action along the ash-path. Fire holds them back for a time.
- _Stealth:_ put out the lights and lie still in the dead ground. The dead pass over those who do not move.
- _Guile:_ lead the dead into the false graves, or onto the thin soil.
- _Parley:_ the dead cannot be bargained with. The Warden, if present, burns a path.

**Clues and rewards.** The dead turn inward, toward the centre, as the night deepens. Whatever is at the heart is calling them back.

**Consequences.** Each wound taken in the Rising is cold and slow to heal. A wounded character who survives to dawn is marked.

**If bypassed.** A party that stays out of the Clearing until dawn arrives at the heart at first light, with the dead still.

## Scene Four: The Listener

**Purpose.** The climax: the heart of the wound.

**Situation.** At the centre the ground is black, and the light dims in a ring around it. A figure sits cross-legged at its edge, facing outward, wrapped in a grey cloak that has rotted into the soil. It does not move.

**NPCs and threats.**

- **The Listener.** A [[being-tereb|tereb]] who sat down at the wound long ago and listened to the void until he became more of it than of himself. He was a priest or a sorcerer; no living person knows. He speaks without sound, in the party's own voices. He offers bargains: a lost child returned, a dead partner's name, knowledge of the Shadow. His tactics are temptation first and a surge of the dead second. He cannot leave the wound's edge. He is not the wound, only its keeper, and killing him does not close it.

**Player choices.**

- _Force:_ attack the Listener. Blows cause him to flicker, and the dead rally.
- _Stealth:_ slip around the edge and reach the black ground, to seal or measure it.
- _Guile:_ feed him a bargain with a hidden clause, or promise a thing the party does not have.
- _Parley:_ listen. His account is true, and it is not what the party hoped.

**Clues and rewards.** The wound is growing by a hand's breadth a year. The ring of bells would not hold it for another generation.

**Consequences.** Killing the Listener ends the Rising for a season. A bargain with him ties the party to the Shadow.

**If bypassed.** A party that retreats from the heart meets the Listener's voice at the rim for the rest of their lives.

## Scene Five: What the Wound Holds

**Purpose.** Reward, revelation and twist.

**Situation.** At the Listener's feet is a small cairn of grave-goods, the offerings of the dead he drew in: ivory beads, hide pouches, bronze arm-rings. At the cairn's centre lies a flat black stone that is cold in the hand.

**NPCs and threats.** The surviving dead, the Ash-Warden if he lives, and the dark.

**Player choices.**

- _Force:_ take the grave-goods.
- _Stealth:_ take the black stone and leave.
- _Guile:_ plant a false marker for later seekers.
- _Parley:_ carry the Warden's tally-cord to the Leopard King's Court.

**Clues and rewards.** The treasure is modest: beads, arm-rings and hide-work, and it is stained with the wound. The black stone is a shard of the wound itself, a bit of the Shadow's touch. It confers no power, but a sorcerer would give a great deal for it. The revelation: the Listener says there are other wounds. This is the one the forest tribes know. It is not the first, and it is not the largest.

**Consequences.** The black stone draws the dead. Whoever carries it is followed.

**If bypassed.** The party can learn the same from the Warden's tally-cord, if they brought it out.

## Aftermath

- **If the Listener is killed,** the Rising stops for a season and the tribes return to their villages. The wound keeps growing.
- **If the Ash-Warden lives,** he keeps the rim, and the rite of the bells passes to a successor.
- **If the black stone is carried out,** a sorcerer in the north learns where the wound is.
- **If the party strikes a bargain with the Listener,** the Shadow follows them to the river-ports.
- **If the Clearing is left alone,** the Court's hunters patrol the rim through another season, and the circle grows.
