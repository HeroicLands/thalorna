## Overview {#overview}

[[place-talothe|The Talote]] is the mountain march of Provénzia against Tarvénia, and its war has a calendar. Raiding begins when the snow clears from the passes and ends when it returns. The levy musters at [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] every spring and disperses every autumn, and the [[affiliation-wntrassize|Winter Assize]] sits in winter because nothing else can be done then. The border families on both sides of the passes have fought the same quarrel for so long that it is less a conflict than a way of life.

The Talotis hold high pastures above [[place-pirnebris|Pirnebris]], under the passes, and they have fought the family across the pass for longer than anyone remembers. This summer their sons are out with the levy along the road below the passes, the pastures are thinly held, and the family across the pass is coming down. [[being-vlrtlts|Velire Talotis]], head of the family, has come down to Pirnebris to hire hands for the season.

The party enters as hired hands, as the Warden's eyes, or as the herald's escort over the pass.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The prisoner.** Last autumn, in the last raid before the snow, the Talotis took a son of the family across the pass and have held him all winter in their tower for ransom. His family's price came before the Winter Assize, and the justice, [[being-dilmaravlch|Dilmara Volchemis]], could bind the Talotis and only send word to the far side. Velire refused every price. He does not want silver. He wants his daughter.

**The daughter.** Two summers ago Velire's daughter went over the pass with the far family's eldest son, and married him in his valley's chapel under the March's law. Velire calls it a taking and has never spoken her name since. The prisoner in his tower is her husband's younger brother.

**The raid.** The far family is coming down this summer with more riders than any year in memory, to bring their son home. Velire's daughter rides with them. She means to stand in the pasture between her father's men and her husband's and make them talk, and she has told her husband that if blood is spilled she will not go home with either.

**The march.** The Warden of the Talote, [[being-talvenekmrl|Talvene Komrelis]], wants a quiet season above Pirnebris, because his levy is strung out along the road and a feud that draws in the March's barons draws in the whole frontier. His herald, [[being-arislkrsvr|Arisla Kórisvar]], carries the march's word over the passes under a white staff, and she has carried this ransom across twice. She knows about the daughter. She has not told the Warden, because it is a family's business.

**The pasture.** The far family will come down to the saddle pasture below the pass, as they always do. Whoever holds it holds the Talotis tower's water.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Both families are in the right by their own lights, and neither is cruel. The scenario ends when the riders go home, with their son or without him, and when the families' quarrel reaches Durvalis as a plea or as a killing.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The mercenary.** Velire Talotis hires a party at the Pirnebris market to hold his pastures through the raiding season, at a fair wage and a share of any stock recovered.
- **The leader.** Talvene Komrelis asks a party to keep the Talotis feud from spreading while his levy holds the road.
- **The herald.** Arisla Kórisvar asks a party to ride with her over the pass under the white staff, to carry a ransom answer that Velire has not given.
- **The wanderer.** A party crossing the pass meets a young woman riding south with Tarvénian riders, who asks in Provenzal whether her father is well.

## The Opening {#opening}

The last market of Pirnebris before the passes, inside the walled yards, the orchards in flower outside. A big gray man with a shepherd's crook and a sword stands by the well and looks over the hands for hire.

> "Six weeks on the high pasture," he says. "Bring your own boots. The Tarvénians come down when the snow goes off the saddle, and the snow is going."

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Last Market

**Purpose.** Show the march's season and hire on.

**Situation.** [[place-pirnebris|Pirnebris]], the cherry town built so that it can be shut, its market inside the walls, its merchants doing very well out of the frontier. The road climbs from here toward the passes.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-vlrtlts|Velire Talotis]], gruff and honest. His youngest son, too young for the levy, who is to show the party the pastures. Merchants selling boots, rope and salt at frontier prices.

**Player choices.** Hire on with Velire, ask in the market about the Talotis, or go on to Durvalis first.

**Clues and rewards.** The market knows the Talotis hold a Tarvénian prisoner, and that the ransom went to the Assize and stuck. The youngest son, asked about his sister, goes red and says he has none.

**Consequences.** The party is Velire's for the season, or it is not.

**If bypassed.** Velire hires the market's worst, and the pastures are held by men who will run.

### Scene Two: The High Pastures

**Purpose.** The pastures, the tower and the prisoner.

**Situation.** The Talotis tower above the pastures, the flocks on the slopes, the saddle pasture below the pass with the spring the tower drinks from. The snow going off the pass a little more every day.

**NPCs and threats.** The prisoner, a Tarvénian of about twenty, courteous, held in the tower's upper room. The Talotis women, who keep him fed and do not speak of the daughter in Velire's hearing. Scouts from the far side, seen on the snow.

**Player choices.** Fortify the saddle, talk to the prisoner, scout the pass, or ask the women.

**Clues and rewards.** The prisoner says his brother's wife is a Talotis, and that she is coming. The women confirm it, and beg the party not to let the men kill each other in front of her.

**Consequences.** The party knows what Velire will not say.

**If bypassed.** The party learns of the daughter in the saddle pasture, when she rides into it.

### Scene Three: Durvalis

**Purpose.** The march's authority, and the law.

**Situation.** [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]], the fortress town below the passes, full in the campaign season: soldiers, carters, remount lines. The Warden's hall and the herald's house.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-talvenekmrl|Talvene Komrelis]], busy and short of men. [[being-arislkrsvr|Arisla Kórisvar]], who knows the whole story. [[being-dilmaravlch|Dilmara Volchemis]], the justice of the Assize, who lives in Durvalis the year round and remembers the stuck ransom.

**Player choices.** Ask the Warden for men, ask the herald to carry word over, or ask the justice what the Assize could do with a marriage it cannot bind.

**Clues and rewards.** Dilmara says that a marriage made under the March's law is still a marriage, and that a plea brought by both families together could be heard at the Assize as a settlement rather than a ransom. Arisla will cross the pass under the white staff if the party will escort her.

**Consequences.** A party that brings a settlement to the families has the march's law behind it.

**If bypassed.** The Warden sends ten men from the levy, and the saddle becomes a skirmish.

### Scene Four: The Saddle Pasture

**Purpose.** The climax: the riders come down.

**Situation.** The saddle pasture below the pass, in the first clear week. Riders on the snow, a score of them, and at their head a young woman in a Tarvénian cloak. Velire's men and the party at the spring.

**NPCs and threats.** Velire, who sees his daughter and does not move. Her husband, at her side. The riders, who have come for their man and will fight for him. The Talotis, who will fight for the pasture.

**Player choices.** Hold the spring, let the daughter speak, bring the prisoner down, or put the herald's white staff between the lines.

**Clues and rewards.** If the daughter is let speak and the prisoner is brought down, Velire says her name for the first time in two years. The two families agree to carry their plea to Durvalis together before the passes close.

**Consequences.** A settlement ends the feud for a generation, which in the Talote is a long time. A fight in front of the daughter ends it another way: she goes home with neither family, and both blame the other forever.

**If bypassed.** The riders take the saddle, and the Talotis tower goes without water until the prisoner is given up.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

The riders come down to the saddle pasture and Velire's hired hands run. The far family take the spring and besiege the tower for its water. Velire gives up the prisoner rather than lose his flocks, and his daughter, who rode down to make her father talk, watches him refuse to look at her. The March's barons hear that the Talotis gave way, and two more families come down next summer.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

A party that brought the families to talk has carried a plea to the Winter Assize that the Assize can actually settle, and earned the Warden's thanks for a quiet season above Pirnebris. A party that only held the pasture has kept the Talotis tower and kept the feud. Either way the Talotis daughter knows who stood in the saddle, and so does the family across the pass.
