The hearth in [[place-lezkorahall|the High Hall]] burns while there is a High King and goes out when there is none. The present High King, [[being-ularrekgarr|Ularrek Garrundeta]], is old and has not come down to the hall for a month. He holds his court as the guest of the king of Asturath in the king's city, behind the king's gate, and every power in Tarvénia has begun to decide what it will pay for the crown when he dies. This frame runs through one such season, from the thaw that opens the plateau road to the first snow, with the hearth still lit and nobody willing to say what happens when it goes out.

## The Situation and the Stakes {#the-situation}

**The crown.** The High King is elected at [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] by every lord of the three kingdoms who brings his banner to the ban, about a hundred in all, and a candidate needs two voices in every three. He calls the ban only against an enemy from outside Tarvénia, judges quarrels between lords, keeps the charter of Valthari and the old arrangement with Eskarath, and takes nothing from any lord's land that the terms do not allow. He has no army and no bureaucracy, and every king who has tried to have either has ended deposed, exiled or dead ([[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]).

**The mountain.** [[being-alzemozold|Alzemo Zolduregi]], king of Asturath, holds the plateau gate, the plateau road and the bread that climbs it, and the old king sits in his city. He wants the crown back among Asturath's own and cannot be seen to hasten its coming. His mountain lords count lowland men on the road and talk about Valthari, which they think soft and foreign.

**The lowlands.** [[being-zenotholtr|Zenoth Oltris]], king of Lyenrik, means to stand. His host is the largest and his clerks the best. He prefers a record to an oath, and he wants a free hand on Valthari's charter and the toll at [[place-bruthena|Bruthena]]. His lowland lords are sending men up the road one and two at a time.

**The coast.** [[being-ganothervo|Ganoth Ervothandis]], king of Galvaren, owes the High King his presence and his levy and nothing else. His crown lives on the salt it buys and sells, and a harbor side at the Long Table is learning to resent it. His voice at the election goes to whoever can keep the crown solvent.

**The free city.** The [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]] of Valthari has no voice in the hall and a charter that is one of the terms. It cannot agree whether to pay the year's tribute and no more or to back the likeliest lord. Its seamen are on strike, its idle houses hold paper on half of Tarvénia, and its debts reach farther up the plateau road than its councilors know.

**The highlands.** The Eskárathi send their warriors when the High King calls the ban against a foreign enemy, and the rest of Tarvénia leaves them alone in return. The Speaker of the Biltzarrath, [[being-gaztorogerd|Gaztoro Gerdoiena]], goes to Lezkora to hear the terms sworn, because the arrangement is one of them. A candidate who offers the highlands a road, a gift or a muster to be promised in advance is a candidate who has not read the arrangement.

**The Order and the roll.** The brothers of the [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|Order of the Sealed Word]] keep the hall's roll between elections and write down each voice. The keeper of the roll writes the terms. Whoever keeps the terms keeps the crown.

The stakes are who swears the terms at the stone, whether they are kept in the hall's roll or the Court of Pleas', and whether the arrangements that hold Tarvénia together hold through a contested vote.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Nobody in this frame decides when Ularrek dies or who is elected. The old king is alive when the frame begins. The party meets the powers deciding what to pay, each keeping the price to itself. Play every price as a secret held against the others, and let the table, if anyone, bring them into the open.

## Who Moves It {#who-moves-it}

[[being-ularrekgarr|Ularrek Garrundeta]], the High King
: Slow, dry and exact, and elected because the greater candidates could block one another. He wants the terms kept and the hearth lit when he dies, and he has not left his rooms in a month.

[[being-alzemozold|Alzemo Zolduregi]], king of Asturath
: Large, unhurried and watchful. He controls the electors' climb and counts the lowland lords' men at the gate.

[[being-zenotholtr|Zenoth Oltris]], king of Lyenrik
: Smooth, patient and exact about precedence. His clerks have written the terms of the crown fair.

[[being-ganothervo|Ganoth Ervothandis]], king of Galvaren
: Quiet and careful with the crown's money. The salt price and a harbor side are his two troubles.

[[being-orthemaiken|Orthema Ikentsis]], First of the Council of the Konsezarath
: Quick and impossible to hurry. Her house's trade and her office point the same way, and the houses that disagree use it against her.

[[being-elutsaribot|Elutsar Ibotsena]], eldest elder of Ibotsain
: Mild, slow and thorough. He remembers every ruling of every Water-Warden, and he distrusts anyone who writes things down.

[[being-ervothotholm|Ervotho Tholmaris]], the Herald Emeritus
: Retired chief herald of the crown. He sits on a bench in the yard below the hall, holds the old court in his memory and has no use for writing.

[[being-carengrvth|Kárenna Graveth]], Herald of the Crown
: Warm, calculating and pragmatic. She carries the High King's word and the lowland court's patronage, and she is not sure which is heavier.

[[being-hiltheratsol|Hilthera Tsoluregi]], Master of Commerce of Lyenrik
: Old mountain blood and a lowland council seat. She designs Lyenrik's answer to the free city.

[[being-dalenavlrn|Dálena Valaron]], pilot of Valthari
: Her house's note and her crew's refusal pull her two ways.

The bodies
: The council of mountain lords in the castle at Lezkora; the Court of Pleas and the Roll of Houses at Torvaleth; the Long Table at Durvolka; the owners' council and the Arbitrators at the Charter Hall; the Biltzarrath at Gaztorai; the brothers of the Sealed Word.

## The Arcs

The frame follows the season and points beyond its end, each arc turning one strand of the choosing.

1. **The free city.** [[scenario-crssbwsndlqy|Crossbows on an Idle Quay]] runs the strike in Valthari, where a company from the old king's own valley waits for a ship and a Valthari house holds the note on its pay.
2. **The coast.** [[scenario-nghtshp|The Night Ship]] runs the salt, the herring crews and the Masters' Hall, where the silver of a Valthari house is buying a seat that would cut the crown of Galvaren's purse.
3. **The plateau.** [[scenario-trmsnwrtng|The Terms in Writing]] runs Lezkora, where a clerk carries the terms of the crown, written fair by Lyenrik's clerks, to a king who will not leave his rooms.
4. **The highlands.** [[scenario-gftfrldrs|A Gift for the Elders]] runs the solstice meadow, where Lyenrik asks the Biltzarrath for a road and the old arrangement is tested over a spring.
5. **The hearth.** The arcs end at the stone. The party stands in the hall when the roll is written, in the city while the lords bargain between the days of voting, or at the foot of the climb when the heralds ride out.

The arcs run in the order of the season or out of it, and each moves the choosing whether or not the party finishes it. A party that carries one fact up the road carries it past everyone else who has been asked.

## Ways In {#ways-in}

1. **For a king.** Zenoth Oltris, Alzemo Zolduregi or Ganoth Ervothandis hires a party to count another king's men, carry a letter, or learn what a rival pays.
2. **For a house.** A Valthari house, a councilor of the Konsezarath or a master of the hiring yards pays a party bound upcountry to carry a sum, a debt or a count of the lords' men.
3. **For the crown.** A herald of the crown, or the Herald Emeritus, asks a party to learn who has gone through the old king's door.
4. **For the companies.** A captain of a high valley, such as [[being-kanarrektsuk|Kanarrek Tsukurreta]], hires a party to see his company aboard in time, or to carry its pay home.
5. **For the highlands.** An elder of Eskarath, or a Warden of the Toravar such as [[being-tergadanmiri|Térgadan Mirindi]], asks a party to learn whom a gift is meant to serve.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

- The strike ends when the council moves the share, and a Valthari house holds a debt on the valley of the High King of Tarvénia.
- The Masters' Hall elects its three, the harbor side hardens, and the crown of Galvaren loses a seat's worth of salt.
- The terms in writing are read in the inns to lowland lords, and the mountain lords hear of them at the stone for the first time.
- The Biltzarrath hears a herald and a factor in the same hour, and the survey stakes stay in the ground.
- The old king stays in his rooms, and the lords go on climbing. When the hearth goes out, the heralds ride to every lord who has a seat in the hall, and each finds the others already in the city.

## The Secret {#the-secret}

**The debt on the High King's own valley.** A Valthari house holds the paper on the valley of Ularrek Garrundeta, bought through a factor in the Taruzeli Quarter from a steward who never told his lord. The Konsezarath does not know the house holds it, and the house means to spend it on the charter. The frame does not say who learns it first, or what Alzemo does when he does.

> [!NOTE]
> The steward and the factor are run as [[being-valarnlvnr|Valaren Álvanar]], a stock clerk.

**The terms in writing.** Zenoth's clerks have written the terms so that every word is the old word and the keeping of them is Lyenrik's. Two people outside Lyenrik have read the draft: the Herald Emeritus, who has told the old king, and a clerk of the Court of Pleas, [[being-kolsailthe|Kolsa Iltheris]], who will swear to it. The frame does not say whether the hall hears it read before the voting.

**The silver on the coast.** The same house that holds the debt sells salt to Durvolka's curing houses past the crown, and its silver is buying the herring crews' votes. Ganoth Ervothandis does not know the salt is the target, and the Konsezarath does not know it is the house's doing.

**The road.** Lyenrik's Master of Commerce has surveyed a road over Leparrun's pass to the Élavendre border. It would give Lyenrik a northern market and an open door to the highlands. The frame does not say whether any clan carries it home.

**The ledge.** The outer stone of the ledge beside the High King's chair has been worked with a chisel this spring, and the Keeper of the Hall, [[being-hurdathol|Hurda Tholmaris]], knows that one of the two High Kings who died there did not fall. The frame does not say who worked the stone, or whether the old king knows.
