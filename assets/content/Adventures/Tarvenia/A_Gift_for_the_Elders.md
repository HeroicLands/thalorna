## Overview {#overview}

A week before the summer solstice a Tarvenan factor, [[being-galtsathol|Galtsa Tholmaris]], comes up to [[place-ibotsain|Ibotsain]] with a gift for the elders and a request. He asks that the [[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]] let a toll-road cross the pass above [[place-leparrun|Leparrun]], and he asks it with the courtesy of a man who has been told to be courteous. [[being-elutsaribot|Elutsar Ibotsena]], the eldest elder of the village, will say nothing of it to the clans until a party has learned whom the factor serves. The spring's water order is already in quarrel, an offering at its shrine has been taken before dawn, and survey stakes have been driven near a grove across the border that the Wardens hold sacred.

The party arrives for any of those: a gift to carry, a messenger to follow, a guide's hire over the pass, or the Warden's errand from the other side of the range.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The factor.** Galtsa Tholmaris is a factor of Lyenrik's Master of Commerce, [[being-hiltheratsol|Hilthera Tsoluregi]]. The road is Lyenrik's. Valthari buys most of what Lyenrik sells, at prices Valthari sets, and a toll-road over Leparrun's pass to the Élavendre border would give the lowland kingdom a northern market and a second buyer. Zenoth Oltris wants it laid, or promised, before the next election, as proof that Lyenrik does not need the free city's charter to be rich.

**The road is more than a road.** The terms a High King swears allow the ban only against an enemy from outside Tarvénia, and the Biltzarrath weighs every call against them before the Host-Caller, [[being-arrunomendur|Arruno Mendurena]], summons a warrior. A road a host can use lets a king's men enter the highlands without asking. The factor does not say so, and he does not know it. His mistress does.

**The quarrel.** A neighboring clan, the Otsanena, has drawn from Ibotsain's spring out of its turn three times. Their elder, [[being-zorganotsan|Zorgan Otsanena]], claims an older order and has not carried the claim to the Water-Warden, [[being-soraineleper|Soraine Leparena]]. The factor has sat at his hearth twice. He has offered to pay the blood owed in a feud the Otsanena cannot end, a debt that the Keeper of the Feud, [[being-aidarnearrun|Aidarne Arrunena]], counts in the meadow, and he has not yet said what he wants in return.

**The offering.** The offering left at the spring's shrine has been taken before dawn. The clan that took it is the same. Zorgan took it as a sign that his claim is heard by the spirit of the place, and no one else knows.

**The herald.** [[being-carengrvth|Kárenna Graveth]], Herald of the Crown, is on the road to Gaztorai with the High King's greeting for the solstice, which heralds have carried to the meadow for as long as there has been a Biltzarrath. Hilthera Tsoluregi is her patron. The greeting is true. Kárenna does not mean to lie, and she will say nothing of the road until the council has taken the greeting. She is a mediator who argues for peace and who can argue for what she does not believe. A herald from the crown standing in the meadow beside a factor from Lyenrik looks like the crown's request, and Kárenna knows how it looks.

**The Warden.** [[being-tergadanmiri|Térgadan Mirindi]], who settles quarrels with the barons below the range, has found survey stakes near a sacred grove on the northern arm. He does not know who drove them, and the clans of the Toravar will not wait for the Biltzarrath to speak.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The Biltzarrath decides by agreement and no one delegate can bind a clan. The scenario does not decide whether the road is agreed. A party's work is to see that each clan carries the true account home, and that no clan is bought in the dark.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The wanderer.** Elutsar asks a party to learn whom the factor serves, and to carry the gift's price back to the elders without being seen to ask.
- **The thief.** Someone offers silver to learn who took the offering at the shrine, and someone else offers more not to learn.
- **The leader.** Térgadan Mirindi asks a party to find who is paying for a road through the high reaches.
- **The mercenary.** The factor hires guides over Leparrun's pass for a survey he will not explain.

## The Opening {#opening}

Ibotsain at dusk. The village stands round its spring, and the factor's gift lies on a cloth at the water's edge: salt, iron needles and a bolt of dyed wool, set out in a line with a polite distance between each.

> Elutsar leans on his staff and speaks to nobody in particular. "A gift that asks nothing is a gift that asks later," he says. "Tell me what it asks, and I will tell you what I owe."

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Spring

**Purpose.** The village, the water order and the shrine.

**Situation.** Ibotsain, the spring and the shrine to its spirit. Households come down in turn to draw, and the Water-Warden's order is kept by memory.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-elutsaribot|Elutsar Ibotsena]], mild and thorough. The household heads, who speak for their flocks. The factor, polite and watchful, with a guide hired in Leparrun.

> [!NOTE]
> The household heads are run as [[being-alvrkrnthl|Alvarik Árenthar]], a stock farmer, and the factor's guide as [[being-zoriladmvr|Zórila Dómivar]], a stock hunter.

**Player choices.** Take Elutsar's errand, ask the factor what he serves, watch the spring at dawn, or look for who took the offering.

**Clues and rewards.** The offering was taken by someone who knew the shrine's path in the dark. The gift's salt carries the mark of a Lyenrik royal store.

**Consequences.** Elutsar trusts a party that respects the order of the water. He distrusts one that writes it down.

**If bypassed.** The offering stays unfound, and the Otsanena draw again out of turn.

### Scene Two: The Neighbor's Valley

**Purpose.** The clan that draws out of turn and what has been offered it.

**Situation.** The Otsanena's pastures in the next valley, where an old feud lies unended.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-zorganotsan|Zorgan Otsanena]], proud and wronged by an old order he cannot prove. The factor's offer, carried by a clerk of his who sits at the hearth. The kindred the Otsanena owe blood to.

> [!NOTE]
> The factor's clerk is run as [[being-valarnlvnr|Valaren Álvanar]], a stock clerk, and the men of the kindred as [[being-alvrkrnthl|Alvarik Árenthar]].

**Player choices.** Carry Elutsar's account to Zorgan, carry Zorgan's to the Water-Warden, expose the offer, or let the factor go on.

**Clues and rewards.** Zorgan took the offering and says so, once, and will not say it again. The factor's offer is silver for the blood owed, and nothing written.

**Consequences.** A party that carries the account to the Water-Warden before the meadow sits may end the quarrel on the water, and the factor's lever with it.

**If bypassed.** The quarrel arrives at the meadow unended.

### Scene Three: The Pass

**Purpose.** The road and what lies on it.

**Situation.** Leparrun, the stone of the pass, and the shrine to the spirit of the pass. Survey stakes in the snow-patches, and a Lyenrik surveyor with two guides.

**NPCs and threats.** The surveyor, who works for the factor and answers only to him. [[being-tergadanmiri|Térgadan Mirindi]], who has come down from the high reaches and warns once. A guide of Leparrun who has taken the factor's silver.

> [!NOTE]
> The surveyor is run as [[being-valarnlvnr|Valaren Álvanar]], and the guides as [[being-zoriladmvr|Zórila Dómivar]].

**Player choices.** Pull the stakes, warn the surveyor off, follow the line, or carry the stakes' placing to Elutsar.

**Clues and rewards.** The line runs beyond the pass into ground the clans hold sacred. The surveyor's map shows the road on the Élavendre side to a market town.

**Consequences.** A party that pulls the stakes has told the factor it knows. One that follows the line learns where the road ends.

**If bypassed.** The stakes stand, and Térgadan's clan sends its young men.

### Scene Four: The Meadow

**Purpose.** The Biltzarrath, the greeting and the gift.

**Situation.** [[place-gaztorai|Gaztorai]], the high meadow, on the day of the solstice. The council sits in the open under the Truce-Warden's peace. There is no hall, no clerk and no record.

**NPCs and threats.** The Speaker of the Council, [[being-gaztorogerd|Gaztoro Gerdoiena]]; the Lawkeeper, [[being-ibunaregoz|Ibunar Egozena]]; the Truce-Warden, [[being-uzarneolden|Uzarne Oldena]]; the Keeper of the Feud, Aidarne Arrunena; and the Host-Caller, Arruno Mendurena. [[being-carengrvth|Kárenna Graveth]], who gives the High King's greeting and waits to be asked about the road. The factor, who waits to be asked.

**Player choices.** Speak through a delegate, bring the Water-Warden's account, name the road's purpose, or say nothing.

**Clues and rewards.** A delegate can argue for his clan and cannot bind it. The Keeper of the Feud will not close the sitting until he has given his count, and the factor's silver can be named at the count.

**Consequences.** If the factor's offer is named, the Biltzarrath refuses the road and the offer together. If it is not named, the clans carry home a question none of them can answer alone, and the road goes on as a promise.

**If bypassed.** The council hears the herald and the factor, and the Otsanena take the offer on their way down.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

The factor pays the blood the Otsanena owe. The Otsanena carry the road to the meadow as their own proposal, and the Biltzarrath, which cannot bind a clan, sends the question home to the valleys. The surveyor completes his line. In a year there is a road the Eskárathi did not agree to, and the arrangement has been quietly pressed.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

The old arrangement holds or it does not by the next ban. A party that named the factor's offer at the count has kept the highlands' answer in the meadow and made an enemy of Lyenrik's Master of Commerce. One that carried the account to the Water-Warden has ended a quarrel and left a clan with no use for the factor. One that pulled the stakes has told the Warden's clans a road was meant.
