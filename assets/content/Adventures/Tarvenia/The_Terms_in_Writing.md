## Overview {#overview}

The plateau road is open, and the lords of the lowlands have begun sending their men up to [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] one and two at a time. The Warden of the Plateau Gate, [[being-thelvarougar|Thelvaro Ugarreta]], counts them through, and the High King, [[being-ularrekgarr|Ularrek Garrundeta]], has not come down to [[place-lezkorahall|the High Hall]] for a month. At the foot of the climb, in [[place-ardalis|Ardalis]], a woman in a lowland clerk's gray waits for a party bound upward. She says she has a quarrel with a lord of her own kingdom that only the High King can hear, and the lord is already in the city. She needs someone to walk her through the gate before the lord's men do.

The party enters on any road up the plateau: a guard hire, a pilgrim's path to the Sacred Forge's hearth, a letter for a lord. Every person in Lezkora serves one of two courts, and the party is the only company in the city that serves neither.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The draft.** Clerks of the Court of Pleas at [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]], on the word of [[being-zenotholtr|Zenoth Oltris]], have written the terms of the crown fair. Each term is the one a High King swears at the stone: the ban only against an enemy from outside Tarvénia, the judging of quarrels, the charter of Valthari, the old arrangement with Eskarath, and nothing taken from any lord's land that the terms do not allow. The draft changes only who keeps them. The terms are to be entered in the roll of the Court of Pleas as well as in the hall's roll, and the Court's copy decides a doubt. The ban is to be called from a muster roll of every lord's banner and men, kept by the Marshal of Lyenrik, and a lord who does not come answers for it at the Court. The draft breaks no term a mountain lord would hear read aloud, and that is the danger. A king who keeps the roll and the muster needs no army, and every lord who has gripped his forearm has agreed to words that mean what the Court says they mean.

**The clerk.** [[being-kolsailthe|Kolsa Iltheris]], a clerk of the Court of Pleas, copied the draft twice and refused a third copy. The Keeper of the Roll of Houses, [[being-tholmarvolk|Tholmar Volkemis]], has moved her house down a place for it. She knows the draft is meant to be sworn at the next election, and that Zenoth's men in the city are carrying fair copies to read to lords in the bargaining after the voting. She has a copy of her own, written in her own hand, and she will swear at the stone to what she copied.

**The old king.** Ularrek has read the draft. [[being-ervothotholm|Ervotho Tholmaris]], the retired chief herald, sits on a bench in the yard below the hall most days and talks to the clerks of both courts. He brought the old king a copy a month ago. Ularrek has not come down because every step into the hall is a step the lowland lords' men would see, and a High King who denounces the draft before the lords gather gives Zenoth the month to rewrite it. He wants the mountain lords to hear it for the first time in the hall from a voice they trust, and he wants a witness who will swear to it. The clerk is the witness. Ervotho, who has no office, is the voice.

**The king of Asturath.** [[being-alzemozold|Alzemo Zolduregi]] has asked the Warden of the Plateau Gate for the names of the lowland lords' men and for nothing else. He does not know the draft exists. If he learns it from the party, Asturath unites behind him within the week, and he will be tempted to claim the crown before the old man dies. He cannot be seen to hasten the old man's end, and his council of mountain lords is not so careful.

**The hall.** [[being-hurdathol|Hurda Tholmaris]], the Keeper of the Hall, has kept the benches and the roll's chest for three kings. She knows that one of the two High Kings who died on the ledge beside the chair did not fall. The outer stone of the ledge has been scraped again this spring, and she does not know by whom. She has chained the ledge door and the old king has had the chain taken off.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The draft is not a plot to murder anyone. Zenoth means to be elected and to be a High King who needs no stone to be obeyed. The scenario ends with the draft read aloud, burned, sold or buried, and the party holding a fact the mountain lords will act on.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The leader.** A herald of the crown, [[being-carengrvth|Kárenna Graveth]], asks the party to learn who has gone through the old king's door in a month, because the Warden counts the lords' men and nobody counts the king's visitors.
- **The thief.** A lowland steward pays a party to take a clerk's copy of the draft from her lodging before she carries it upstairs.
- **The wanderer.** Kolsa Iltheris asks a party to get her through the gate and into the hall before the Keeper of the Roll of Houses finds her.
- **The mercenary.** The Warden of the Plateau Gate hires guards to stand watch at the cisterns, because one of the western cisterns has turned in a dry spring.

## The Opening {#opening}

The yard between the gates at midday. The clerk stands among carters and mule-boys with a gray satchel held in both hands, and the Warden's men are counting a lord's twelve through the inner gate.

> "My quarrel is with a Liénriki lord," she says. "He is in the city. Only the High King may judge a lord in this hall, and I shall not live to see the judging if I wait my turn."

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Gate Yard

**Purpose.** Show how the plateau is held, and bring the clerk in.

**Situation.** The two-gated tower of gray stone. The Warden's men hold the party between the gates while they look it over.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-thelvarougar|Thelvaro Ugarreta]], the Warden of the Plateau Gate, a lord for life who knows every face in the climb. A lowland steward whose lord's twelve have just gone in. The clerk, afraid.

> [!NOTE]
> The Warden's men are run as [[being-tarsilathelv|Társila Thelvaris]], a stock man-at-arms, and the lowland stewards as [[being-valarnlvnr|Valaren Álvanar]], a stock clerk.

**Player choices.** Vouch for the clerk, hide her among the carters, send her up alone, or leave her at the gate.

**Clues and rewards.** The steward carries the same gray satchel the clerk does. The Warden says plainly that he counts every man who climbs and has counted more clerks this month than in ten years.

**Consequences.** The party is known at the gate, which means it is known to the king of Asturath.

**If bypassed.** The clerk goes up the next morning with a lord's twelve.

### Scene Two: Two Courts

**Purpose.** The city that serves two kings.

**Situation.** Lezkora: the crowded lanes, the price of bread, the castle at the north end, the High Hall on the eastern lip, and the temple of the Sacred Forge on the square between them.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-alzemozold|Alzemo Zolduregi]], who receives the party in the castle and asks for the names of the lowland men; the lowland lords' stewards in the inns; [[being-ervothotholm|Ervotho Tholmaris]] on his bench, who knows a party has a clerk and does not ask her name.

**Player choices.** Give Alzemo the names, tell him of the draft, trade with the stewards, or sit down with Ervotho.

**Clues and rewards.** Ervotho says the king receives nobody by day. The cistern that turned is a small matter and a useful excuse for the Warden to put guards where he likes.

**Consequences.** A party that tells Alzemo of the draft sets Asturath in motion and has made the old king's quiet month unsafe.

**If bypassed.** The stewards' copies are read to three lowland lords in the inns that night.

### Scene Three: The High Hall

**Purpose.** The oath stone, the hearth and the ledge door.

**Situation.** The long gray hall at midday, empty. The hearth burns. The roll's chest stands under the brother of the Order of the Sealed Word who keeps it between elections, and the door beside the High King's chair stands unchained.

**NPCs and threats.** [[being-hurdathol|Hurda Tholmaris]], who lives in two rooms behind the west door and opens the hall only to lords. A brother of the [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|Order of the Sealed Word]] who sits by the chest. A lowland steward looking for the way up.

> [!NOTE]
> The brother of the Sealed Word is run as [[being-tarsilathelv|Társila Thelvaris]], a stock man-at-arms.

**Player choices.** Ask the Keeper for the old king's hour, test the ledge, copy the roll's order of voting, or carry the clerk's satchel to the stone.

**Clues and rewards.** The outer edge of the ledge has been worked at with a chisel, recently. The Keeper says the old king takes the air there before dawn.

> [!WARNING]
> The ledge is the one place in the hall where the High King stands alone. A party that tells the wrong person about the clerk's draft has given a lowland steward the reason to look at the stone.

**Consequences.** The Keeper will get the party onto the ledge before dawn.

**If bypassed.** The old king keeps his hour and nobody watches the stone.

### Scene Four: The Ledge at Dawn

**Purpose.** The old king hears the quarrel and decides how it is heard.

**Situation.** The ledge over the Horn Gorge at first light, in cloud. [[being-ularrekgarr|Ularrek Garrundeta]] stands at the rail in plain wool with no weapon and asks the party to speak low.

**NPCs and threats.** The High King, dry and exact. The Keeper of the Hall, who watches the west door. Whoever scraped the outer stone, if the party has told the wrong person.

**Player choices.** Put the clerk before him, give him the draft, keep the draft, or ask what he will do.

**Clues and rewards.** Ularrek asks for the clerk's oath and for no more. He wants Ervotho Tholmaris to read the draft aloud at the stone on the first morning, before the lords vote, and he asks whether the party will stand in the hall with the clerk when the herald reads.

**Consequences.** A party that agrees has put itself between two courts for a season. One that refuses leaves the clerk to the Keeper of the Roll of Houses.

**If bypassed.** The old king sends for the clerk himself by the Keeper's hand, and the party does not learn what he said.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

The stewards read their fair copies to the lowland lords in the inns, one lord at a time. The clerk is found in her lodging and sent back down the road. Alzemo learns of the draft from a mountain lord who was offered a place in it, and the old king stays in his rooms. A mason of the city, run as [[being-jolrenlvnr|Holren Álvanar]], is paid to work the outer stone of the ledge, and the Keeper finds the scrape before the king does.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

The draft is known or it is not by the time the hearth goes out. A party that carried the clerk to the stone has given the mountain lords a thing to hear in the hall, and Zenoth Oltris a reason to change the draft. One that told the king of Asturath has put his valley lords in motion. One that sold the copy has put Zenoth's terms in the hands of the lords he meant to read them to.
