[[place-marchroad|The March Road]] is the one way into [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]] that crosses no pass. It goes round the western end of the Grazian wall through [[place-wstrndscnt|the Western Descent]], and four centuries ago [[lore-hndrdbnnrs|the Storm of the Hundred Banners]] came down it. Every fort on the road was built after that season, and with it in mind. This frame is a lean season on that road. The grazing beyond the last wells has failed, the sheikhs of the sand are meeting, the companies that keep the road are two seasons in arrears, and the king at [[place-sandyapur|Sandyapur]] is a young man who inherited his father's debts with his father's crown.

## The Situation and the Stakes

**The road and its price.** [[affiliation-bhumipal|Bhumipal]] keeps no standing host. Five hired companies keep the road, paid out of the escort money, which is sold by the stage between water: at Sandyapur for the first stage and at [[place-ashvapada|Ashvapada]] for the second. A company that is paid keeps the road. A company that is not paid keeps it anyway and collects what it is owed from the traffic, and the difference between that and banditry is a season's arrears. A Maharava of the march who cannot pay the companies is not deposed. He is simply not obeyed at the wells.

**The lean season.** The [[affiliation-dunhartrbs|Dunhara Tribes]] signed nothing and hold that the road crosses their grazing by their forbearance. They raid in lean seasons and trade in fat ones. This spring the grazing beyond [[place-marukupa|Marukupa]] failed, the camels are thin, and the sheikhs of the road tribes are meeting.

**The late king's debts.** [[being-devanurvasa|Devanur Vasavesha]] ruled for twenty-eight years by promising, and died four years ago owing the captains. His last order, a collar for his heir, was never finished. The gold, the lapis and the half-fee sit with [[being-prtmjkydr|Pritimaja Kayadara]] in [[place-chendrapur2|the Moon City]], and the **Koshadyoksha** calls their keeping theft.

**The tower's record.** [[place-svapnastambha|Svapnastambha]], the dream-tower above the wells at Marukupa, is the last roof a traveler sleeps under before the sand. A master going west sleeps there and is read before he loads, and the house has kept the record of those readings for three hundred years. The court's **Montrin** wants to know whether the record shows a raid coming. The post's commander wants to sit in on the readings. The tower has refused both in writing.

**The title.** Bhumipal's title to the march rests on the march kingdom of [Marupal]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} having died with its last king on [[place-lantustal|the Banner Ground]] and left no heir. A claimant with a seal and a genealogy through an unrecorded daughter has reached Sandyapur in the worst year to be asked the question.

The stakes are whether the road stays open through the lean season, whether the crown is obeyed at the wells at the end of it, and what the sheikhs decide about a frontier that once watched a host come down out of the north.

## Who Moves It

The Maharava of Bhumipal
: Devanur's son, confirmed in the old collar and still paying his father's arrears. His **Montrin** wants the tower's record read, and his **Koshadyoksha** wants the collar's gold.

[[being-umrtdrtn|Umarti Daritan]], captain of a march company
: A Karmija graduate of the Swift Hand who rides the second stage past Lantustal. Her company has not been paid in full for two seasons, and she has begun looking for the money elsewhere.

[[being-svnshvrdzn|Sivanash Vardozan]], the Dunhari factor at Sandyapur
: The only man of the sand with a house in the town and the only one with a [[affiliation-mrchntclctvvdyr|Collective]] letter of credit. He has bought every bale he can carry on that letter, and if the road closes the letter is called.

[[being-drtpjdvtn|Dritapaja Devatuni]], Keeper of the Dream-Record
: A Dorshaka of the [[affiliation-svapnasarisameja|Svapnasari-sameja]] who has kept the tower's record for twenty years. He has refused the court and the post in writing and has not said why.

[[being-prtmjkydr|Pritimaja Kayadara]], goldsmith of Chendrapur
: She holds the unfinished collar's materials and has abandoned the piece. Everyone on the march has a claim to the gold, and none of them is in Chendrapur.

The bodies
: The Martial Council, which is the Samontas of the forts and the captains of the companies; the five companies; the sheikhs of the road tribes; the Collective's house at Sandyapur, the westernmost it keeps; and [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|the Kingdom of Amradad]], bound to Bhumipal by a treaty that does not bind the tribes.

## Ways In

1. **The second stage.** A caravan needs escort from Ashvapada to the wells at Marukupa, and Umarti's company takes the money. The stage passes Lantustal, which the road bends round and the companies chain off at dusk. A caravan sold to that field has its own adventure: [[scenario-lastwagonadv|The Last Wagon on the Banner Ground]].
2. **The sheikhs' meeting.** Sivanash asks the party to ride to the sheikhs with his offer for the tribes' surplus and to keep the road open on whatever terms they take. He has no standing to bind a sheikh and lies fluently in both languages.
3. **The refusal.** Dritapaja asks the party to carry his refusal to Sandyapur and stand by it before the court. The Montrin receives them with courtesy and asks them what they think the record says.
4. **The collar.** Umarti hires the party to recover the late king's gold from Chendrapur and bring it to [[place-ludrapur|Ludrapur]], where her company winters, before the Koshadyoksha's men bring it to Sandyapur.

## If the Party Does Nothing

- The escort money comes in short at the season's end, the Martial Council is told the treasury will pay next season, and Umarti's company collects its arrears from the traffic on the second stage. Two other captains do the same within the month.
- Sivanash's bales sit unsold at Sandyapur, the Collective calls his letter, and the crown loses the one man on the sand who could buy a raid before it rode. The sheikhs who would have sold to him raid the wells instead.
- The road closes for the rest of the season. Grain and cotton stay in the river country, salt and horses stay on the sand, and the crown is obeyed at the wells by nobody.
- The claimant with the seal finds a Samonta willing to hear him at the next council.

## The Secret

The sheikhs are not meeting to plan a raid. Word has come down the oasis-belt that a **dovaq** of the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn]] steppe, north of the Descent, is drawing other tribes under his own banner by force. Whether he becomes an orghun is for the GM to decide. The sheikhs know what the last orghunoq did on this road. They are deciding whether to stand with the march, to move their herds west toward the Kingdom's walls, or to bargain with the north before it arrives. A road kept open through the lean season, by Sivanash's offer or the party's, is the evidence that decides most of them for the march.

Dritapaja has read the same thing in the record. Over the last three seasons the masters going west have brought back one dream far more often than in any run of seasons the record holds: horsemen on the Descent, coming down out of the north, and none from the west. He refused the court because a crown that reads a raid in the record closes the road, and a road closed in a lean season makes the raid it fears.
