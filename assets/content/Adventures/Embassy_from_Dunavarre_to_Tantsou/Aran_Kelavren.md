# Dossier {#dossier}

Aran Kélavren leads the embassy from [[place-dunavarre|Dúnavarre]] to the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], holding its commission and making its decisions. The mission seeks direct relations with the eastern court, independent of Vilérian mediation.

**Background.** A _marendos_ (master) of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Order]] and a noble of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]], an experienced diplomat and a trusted adviser of the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Dúnavarre Synod]]. As the kingdom's custom requires, the Ambassador is a mage.

At [[place-beravel|Péravel]], he offers the embassy’s assistance when [[being-aelithraenewyn|Áelithra Énevin]] asks for an expedition to retrieve the [[miscgear-heartofdhirikri|Heart of Dirikri]]. He needs his agents alive and the mission intact; a precise report can give him grounds to reconsider an imprudent promise.

## Plot Hooks

The ambassador’s commission
: Aran asks trusted staff to attend the Loremaster and report what retrieving the Heart requires. He promises to recognize their service before the retinue.

The return
: An expedition comes back with a report instead of the promised artifact. Aran must account for his offer at court and asks the travelers to set out exactly what they agreed to do and what they learned.

:::secret {title="Favor and mistrust"}
[[being-marricharavrel|Marrik Háravrel]] deliberately confides his office as Spymaster only to Aran. [[being-loristaravel|Loris Taravel]] has discovered it independently; neither Aran nor Marrik knows of that discovery.

Promoted to the post recently, and part of the Synod's service holds that his rise owes more to private dealing and favor than to merit. He needs to prove his worth in everything he does, which strains his relations with his own party—and [[being-baridanalendavel|Baridan Aléndavel]] resents his authority outright, believing the position should have been his.
:::
