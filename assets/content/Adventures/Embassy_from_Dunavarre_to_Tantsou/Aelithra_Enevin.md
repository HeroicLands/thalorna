# Appearance {#appearance}

Áelithra Énevin is a 46-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She is neat and quick, with dark brown hair pinned close and green eyes that read a room as they read a page. She dresses as one who is wealthy without being noble: good cloth in the Order's manner rather than a house's colors, and a Mistress's gray sash. At court she carries a writing-case, and at the party given in [[being-arancelavren|Aran Kélavren]]'s honor she carries the [[miscgear-temprlcmpss|Temporal Compass]] in it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Áelithra Énevin is the Royal Loremaster of the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]], who keeps the scrolls the court holds, speaks for the crown's learning and sends the crown's parties after what the scrolls describe. She is a Mistress of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] and a wealthy commoner of [[place-beravel|Péravel]], the daughter of a paper-merchant of the book quarter, and the first of her family at court.

She holds a recently found ancient scroll that places the [[miscgear-heartofdhirikri|Heart of Dirikri]] in [[place-ukuaraelwen|Úkua-Araelwen]]. The scroll is in poor condition and much of it cannot be read, but it implies that music or sound has something to do with the Heart itself. At the party given in honor of the ambassador of Dúnavarre she proposes sending a party into the Araelwen to bring the Heart out, and the ambassador offers members of his mission ([[scenario-embsydnvrtantsou|Embassy from Dunavarre to Tantsou]]).

She serves the crown and the Order at once, and at court she has never had to choose between them. An Araelwen is forbidden to every outsider, the crown's oath stops at the tree line, and the Heart lies past it. A party of foreigners is not the crown's to answer for.

## Psyche

### Personality

Áelithra is brisk, courteous and certain. She explains a thing once, clearly, and is impatient with anyone who needs it twice.

### Motivation

She wants the Heart of Dirikri read, measured and understood before anyone else reads it. She wants the scroll's broken lines made whole, and she wants her name on the reading.

### Strengths

She reads the old hands better than anyone at court, she has the Queen's ear on matters of learning, and she owns the one instrument that can take a living party through Úkua-Araelwen in something like the time it expects.

### Weaknesses

She believes a thing understood cannot be desecrated. The Wardens believe the opposite, and she has never sat with one long enough to hear why.

## Social

### Companions

A copyist of the book quarter who works on the scroll at night, and [[being-netharahalre|Néthara Halren]], Keeper of the Collections at the University, with whom she argues about provenance over supper.

### Patrons

[[being-aurenthekir|Queen Áurenthe]], who appointed her, and the Fisera house of the Order, which paid for her schooling.

### Enemies

The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]], who keep Úkua-Araelwen and count a device of the art carried inside one as a working, and a working there as desecration. The Grove Council's petitioners at court, who know what she wants.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Áelithra holds the office of Royal Loremaster, as a Freeman.
- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|The Thordimalon]]**: She holds the rung of Marendoi.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Heart.** At the party for the ambassador of Dúnavarre, the Royal Loremaster asks for a party to go into Úkua-Araelwen and bring out the Heart of Dirikri, and lends it the Temporal Compass to do it.
2. **The scroll's missing lines.** A copyist of the book quarter has found a second fragment in the same hand as Áelithra's scroll, and sells it to whoever pays first. The Loremaster wants it, and so does the Order's Sekran.
3. **The compass comes back alone.** A party that took the Temporal Compass into the Araelwen has not come out, and the Compass has turned up on a stall in Péravel's book quarter. Áelithra wants to know who brought it out, and how long they were inside.

:::secret {title="The Order's copy"}
Áelithra made a fair copy of the scroll before she showed it to the Queen, and sent it to her house's chapter at Péravel. What she reads in the scroll and what she tells the court are the same. What the Order reads in it she does not know, because the chapter has not answered.

**Hook: the second party.** An Order party with hired guards has already left Péravel for the Provénzian frontier, with no royal guide and no word to the Loremaster.
:::
