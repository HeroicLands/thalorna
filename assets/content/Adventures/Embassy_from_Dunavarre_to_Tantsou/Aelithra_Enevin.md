# Appearance {#appearance}

A woman of [[place-beravel|Péravel]] and a Mistress of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]], she dresses as one who is wealthy without being noble: good cloth in the Order's manner rather than a house's colors. At the party given in [[being-arancelavren|Aran Kélavren]]'s honor she is the Royal Loremaster at once, and the one thing she lends a party is the [[miscgear-temprlcmpss|Temporal Compass]], an ornate enchanted device that glows in her hand when pointed in the safest direction.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Role.** Royal Loremaster of [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Élavendre]] and leader of the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]] (the Academy of Knowledge). At the party given in [[being-arancelavren|Aran Kélavren]]'s honor at [[place-beravel|Péravel]] she proposes sending a party into the [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] to retrieve the [[miscgear-heartofdhirikri|Heart of Dirikri]], and the ambassador offers members of his mission.

**Background.** A Mistress of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] and a wealthy commoner of Péravel. She holds a recently discovered ancient scroll that places the Heart in [[place-ukuaraelwen|Úkua-Araelwen]]; the scroll is in poor condition and much of it cannot be read, but it implies that music or sound has something to do with the artifact.

**Possessions.** She gives the party the [[miscgear-temprlcmpss|Temporal Compass]], an ornate enchanted device that glows when pointed in the safest direction, vibrates as it nears the Heart, and steadies time around its bearer so the party does not lose weeks or months in the zone.
