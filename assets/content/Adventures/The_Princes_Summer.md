## Overview {#overview}

[[being-ielaraskir|Prince Yélaras]], heir to the crown of Élavendre, was schooled at the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]], and his friends there are fellows of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]]. This summer they plan a survey of the thin places. Yélaras means to ride to [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa]] and ask the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] for leave to walk the margin of [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]], as a prince of the old blood who has read the Order's books. His friends mean to use the prince's company as cover to bring back a living cutting from [[place-wkngstnd|the Waking Stand]].

[[being-aurenthekir|Queen Áurenthe]] keeps the balance as her house has kept it, warm to the Order inside the walls and guided beyond them. She cannot forbid her heir, and she cannot be seen to send anyone after him. An officer of the crown, the Liaison to the Tribes, hires the party to ride with the prince's company in the hope that the party will be a hand on the rein.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The ceiling.** The crown's cordiality toward the Order has a hard ceiling. Élavendren legitimacy runs through the old blood, and the crown cannot govern the interior at all except through Wardens who answer to the tribes. A crown believed to have taken the Order's side faces no rebellion. It faces the quiet verdict that it is no longer quite of the old blood. The heir riding up to Ésalosa at the head of the Order's friends is the thing the ceiling is built against.

**The prince.** Yélaras holds that the old blood and the Order's books are not enemies, and wants to say so to the people it matters to. He means his request to the Wardens sincerely. He does not know what his friends have planned.

**The friends.** [[being-ilvanethors|Ilvane Thorsel]] and [[being-pelradareth|Pelra Dareth]] are fellows of the University who believe the prince's presence will carry them past the Wardens. A third, [[being-kelvanorsel|Kelvan Orsel]], holds a crown license to bring a living cutting out of the Silver Forests, and has hired guards. The license is real and gives him no leave from anyone who keeps the stand. The Royal Loremaster, [[being-aelithraenewyn|Áelithra Énevin]], a Mistress of the Order, knows of the license and has said nothing.

**The stand.** The trees of the Waking Stand move when nobody is watching them. They remember every cut. Whoever carries wood, bark or leaf from the stand is followed, and the trees close round him wherever he sleeps within the Silver Forests, first in a ring and on the second night tighter. A cutting carried out of the forests lives as long as its bearer keeps moving and is never left in the dark within them.

**The Wardens.** Warden leave never takes a stranger into an Araelwen. A prince is a stranger. The Wardens hear his request at Ésalosa, and the Lodge's council answers it in its own time. The Wardens of the Silver Forests are looking for the person who cut a strip of bark from the stand and sold it in Lísere. They do not know the Order has licensed another cutting.

**The liaison.** The crown's Liaison to the Tribes, [[being-chenirahares|Captain Chénira Háresende]], carries the crown's word to the tribes and theirs back. She is paid to avoid exactly this and has no authority over the heir. She hires the party in her own name, and the queen has asked nothing of her.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The liaison.** Captain Chénira hires the party to ride with the prince's company and to see that it stops at the margin.
- **The prince's friends.** Ilvane and Pelra hire the party as guards for a summer's ride, and the contract names the prince, a season and no ground.
- **The Loremaster.** Áelithra Énevin asks the party what the Order's friends have in their saddlebags, and says she is only curious.
- **The Warden.** A Warden of the Silver Forests asks the party to find who cut the bark, and mentions that the Order has a licensed fellow in the prince's company.

## The Opening {#opening}

A courtyard of the royal court at Péravel, an hour before the prince's company rides out. The prince stands among his friends in plain green with ink on his second finger. The liaison, in the shadow of the gate, puts a purse into the party's hands.

> "The queen has asked me nothing," Captain Chénira says. "I am asking you to ride with the prince. If he reaches Ésalosa and asks the question he means to ask, nobody need do anything. Ride beside him all the same."

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Courtyard

**Purpose.** The prince, his friends and the crown's quiet request.

**Situation.** The royal court at Péravel, in the courtyard, in the light of a summer morning.

**NPCs and threats.** Yélaras, easy and argumentative. Ilvane and Pelra, who laugh too much. Kelvan, who rides at the back and watches the baggage. Captain Chénira, who is not there.

**Player choices.** Take the liaison's purse, take the friends' contract, ask the prince where he means to go, or ask the prince's friends what they carry.

**Clues and rewards.** The prince says plainly that he means to ask the Wardens for leave. His friends do not say plainly that they mean to take a cutting. A saddlebag at the back of the company holds a case of moss, sacking and a trowel.

**Consequences.** A party that rides with the company is part of the prince's household for the summer.

**If bypassed.** The company rides without a party that knows what the cutting is for.

### Scene Two: The Road

**Purpose.** The prince's mind and the friends' plan.

**Situation.** The road up the Berav and the lake road to Elanmere, three days of hospitality at the inns and the houses of the nobility. The prince is asked about the Order, and answers well.

**NPCs and threats.** Yélaras. Ilvane and Pelra. Kelvan, who is sometimes alone with his guards. A noble host who asks which side the prince is on.

**Player choices.** Befriend the prince, watch Kelvan, warn Yélaras, or say nothing.

**Clues and rewards.** Yélaras is moved when the party speaks of the Wardens, and says he has read the Asking in the Colleges' copy. Kelvan tells the party the license is the crown's. It is.

**Consequences.** A prince who has been told the plan is a prince who has to choose. A prince who has not been told will find out at the stand.

**If bypassed.** Yélaras discovers the cutting at the margin, from a Warden.

### Scene Three: Ésalosa

**Purpose.** The ceiling made visible.

**Situation.** The Lodge: long timber halls, stone-walled councils and a great ceremonial hall. The senior Wardens have come in early for the great council and are counting the places they cannot cover.

**NPCs and threats.** The Wardens' captain, who receives the prince as a stranger and a courtesy. A Tradition Keeper, who asks the prince why he has come. The Lodge's council, which answers in its own time. The Wardens, who count the company's baggage.

**Player choices.** Stand beside the prince, speak for him, say nothing, or tell the Wardens about the cutting.

**Clues and rewards.** The Wardens say that Ésalosa has never been entered by a monarch without invitation. The prince asks for leave to walk the margin. They say the margin and the road are the Wardens' to give, and that a Warden will walk beside him. They do not say what the Wardens know about the baggage.

**Consequences.** The prince gets leave for the margin, with a Warden at his side. Kelvan does not. The Warden's presence ends the cutting, or makes it a desecration done in front of the heir.

**If bypassed.** The prince is received and asked to wait, and Kelvan's party takes the waiting as an opening.

### Scene Four: The Stand

**Purpose.** The cutting, the heir and the Wardens' judgment.

**Situation.** The Waking Stand, in the deep of the Silver Forests, beyond Sóurimel. Trees of a kind no woodsman has named, with a sheen of metal in moonlight, in a shape that was different last season.

**NPCs and threats.** Kelvan and his guards, with a case of moss and a trowel. The trees, which move behind a sleeping camp. The Warden, who walks beside the prince and has not seen the case. The Wardens of the Silver Forests, who patrol the stand's edge by day.

**Player choices.** Stop Kelvan, let him cut, tell the Warden, tell the prince, or take the cutting for the Wardens.

**Clues and rewards.** The trees close round a camp that has cut. If the party moves, the trees do not follow. If it sleeps, they do.

**Consequences.** A cut in sight of the prince is a desecration done before the heir, and the Wardens' judgment on it is final. A cut made behind his back is one he learns of afterward.

> [!WARNING]
> The Wardens answer to the tribes and not to the crown. If the heir is present at a desecration, the Wardens' judgment is not softened by his name. The verdict that matters is the one the Áelendan pass on him afterward, quietly.

**If bypassed.** Kelvan cuts at night, the trees close round the camp by the second night, and the company runs for the margin with a living cutting in a case.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

Yélaras asks his question at Ésalosa and is told to wait. Kelvan cuts a living cutting from the Waking Stand on the second night, and the trees close round the company on the third. The company reaches the margin with Kelvan carrying the case. The Wardens of the Silver Forests take the cutting and the case, and put the company out of the forest. Yélaras returns to Péravel in silence. The queen asks him nothing.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

The prince is known in Ésalosa as the heir who came to ask, or as the heir who rode with the Order's friends. The Áelendan count the one or the other. The crown receives a petition from the Grove Council and the Wardens' account, and the queen takes the second more seriously than the first. Kelvan's license is withdrawn or it is not. The prince keeps his friends at the University, or loses them.
