## Overview {#overview}

At the margin of [Siralonke]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, where the licensed coppice behind [[place-liranel|Liranel]] gives way to old oak and silver birch, cutters under a crown license have felled a ring of great oaks. A grove-elder of the [[affiliation-groveconcl|Grove Council]], [[being-evranetolsel|Evrane Tolsel]], holds that it was a Council grove, and asks the party to learn whose it is before the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] act. The Wardens say it is Áelendan ground, and the nearest Warden has given the cutters the one warning the law gives.

The quarrel is the clearing. A charter to fell is the crown's to grant, and the license to cut is issued at Liranel. Neither reaches an Áelendan grove. Every new clearing is a negotiation with the nearest tribe, and the license-holder here has not negotiated.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The grove.** The oak ring is Áelendan ground, kept by a Tradition Keeper, [[being-lumredamoghor|Lúmreda]], and guarded by the Wardens of Siralonke. A shaman's cairn lay under the largest oak, and the cutters' sledges buried it. The Council's grove, with its own elder's seat, lies two miles to the north. The elder's belief rests on a boundary she learned as a girl, from a grove-keeper who is dead.

**The license.** [[being-garrelpelth|Garrel Pelthal]], a timber factor of Port Aelion, holds a crown license for a block of the licensed belt. The block's bounds are written in the tally-house roll at Liranel, and the roll puts the oak ring a hundred paces inside them. It did not always. The tally-clerk, [[being-orlenveldar|Orlen Veldarin]], was paid to move the bounds on the roll, and the boundary stone on the ground was set by a surveyor who took the clerk's figures.

**The stacks.** Stems found felled and stacked overnight in the deep wood are not counted against a license. The woodcutters of Liranel say the forest's carpenters do it, the made things of [[place-wrghthllw|the Wrought Hollow]], and a woodcutter who finds such a stack leaves it. Garrel's foreman has been writing his own overcut up as stacks found. Some of the stacks are the carpenters' work. Most are the foreman's.

**The village.** Kardeth's levy takes the cut stems at a discount, and [[being-auliranekard|Lady Áulirane Kardeth]] has a roof to mend before winter. She is pragmatic about the gap between public virtue and private necessity. She does not know the oak ring was Áelendan, and learns it when the Wardens act.

**The Wardens.** The graduated response has begun. [[being-thandreltirin|Thandrel]] of the [Tírindi]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} turned the cutters' carts back once and left the stems where they lay. The next step is not a warning. The cutters are woodcutters of Kardeth and Liranel, poor men who were told the license covered the ring.

**The law.** A license from Liranel does not reach Áelendan ground, and the royal magistrates hear no complaint from anyone hurt on it. Their first question is whether the complainant was warned.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The grove that is not ours.** Evrane asks the party to learn whose the oak ring is before the Wardens act.
- **The cutters.** A foreman's wife in Liranel asks the party to see that her husband's crew are out of the margin before the dark of the moon.
- **The factor.** Garrel hires the party to guard his cutters against the Wardens.
- **The stack.** A woodcutter finds a stack of felled oak in the deep wood, leaves it, and wonders why the tally-clerk wants the count.

## The Opening {#opening}

The Liranel tally-house at the hour the clerks close their rolls. Evrane stands at the long table with a circlet of woven living oak on her brow and a map in her hand that she has not shown to anyone.

> "This is my grove," she says. "The roll says it is Garrel's and the Warden says it is the Keeper's. I would like to know which of us is mistaken before someone dies of it."

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: The Tally-House

**Purpose.** The roll, the court and the license.

**Situation.** The crown's forest court at Liranel, and the tally-house where every licensed stem is measured. A table of rolls, a rack of cords and a clerk who closes his book when the party comes in.

**NPCs and threats.** Orlen, polite and frightened. The Forester's deputy, who keeps the court and does not want a scandal. Evrane, who will not stay at the table.

**Player choices.** Ask for the roll, ask for older rolls, ask the clerk about the bounds, or search the tally-house at night.

**Clues and rewards.** The roll in use puts the oak ring inside Garrel's block. An older roll in the court's keeping puts it outside. The clerk cannot say which is right.

**Consequences.** A party that finds the second roll holds proof that the bounds were moved. A party that asks too loudly alerts the clerk.

**If bypassed.** The old roll is burned by the clerk that night.

### Scene Two: The Margin

**Purpose.** The felled ring and the Wardens' first answer.

**Situation.** The oak ring at the edge of Siralonke. Great stumps, sledge-ruts, a boundary stone set in the wrong place, and a cairn under a stem that has been dragged half off it.

**NPCs and threats.** Thandrel, who stands at the edge of the felled ground and says nothing. The cutters' foreman, who is armed. The cutters, who are afraid.

**Player choices.** Ask the foreman about the license, ask Thandrel about the cairn, look at the stacks, or pull the stem off the cairn.

**Clues and rewards.** The cairn is a shaman's. The boundary stone's tool-marks are fresh. A stack of felled oak nearby has the foreman's saw-marks on every cut, and no made thing leaves a saw-mark.

**Consequences.** Thandrel tells the party that the Wardens act at the dark of the moon, and gives the party the night between to learn what they can.

**If bypassed.** The foreman and the cutters are found in the ring at dawn, alive and unhurt, with their hands bound.

### Scene Three: Kardeth and Port Aelion

**Purpose.** The village that profits and the factor who paid.

**Situation.** Kardeth, a village of about two hundred and fifty, where the cut stems are piled in the lady's yard. Port Aelion, where Garrel keeps a counting-house on the Keeper's wharves.

**NPCs and threats.** Lady Áulirane, who is intelligent, ambitious and not happy to be asked. Garrel, courteous and certain the license covers him. The Port Aelion town council, which does not want its timber trade questioned.

**Player choices.** Tell the lady, tell the factor, tell neither, or ask the foreman's crew to walk off.

**Clues and rewards.** The lady is willing to pay to have the stems returned to the ring. The factor is willing to pay to have the clerk's change forgotten. Orlen's bribe is in the factor's ledger.

**Consequences.** The lady's roof is the price of an honest answer. The factor's ruin is the price of an honest ledger.

**If bypassed.** The cutters go back to the ring on the dark night to finish.

### Scene Four: The Dark of the Moon

**Purpose.** The terms at the margin.

**Situation.** The felled ring at the moon's dark. Thandrel and three Wardens at the edge. Lúmreda, the Tradition Keeper, on the stump of the great oak. Evrane at the ring with her circlet.

**NPCs and threats.** Lúmreda, who names the terms. Thandrel, who carries them out. Garrel's foreman, who has not left. Evrane, who has to face the fact that the grove was never hers.

**Player choices.** Put the roll before Lúmreda, plead for the cutters, bring Lady Áulirane, or step back and let the Wardens act.

**Clues and rewards.** Lúmreda asks that the stems lie where they fell, that the cairn be uncovered, and that no license cross the ring again. She asks nothing of the party for the cutters.

**Consequences.** A party that stands with the cutters and the roll turns the Wardens' second step into terms. A party that stands aside sees the second step done.

> [!WARNING]
> The Wardens' response is graduated, and the next step is not a warning. A license from Liranel does not reach this ground, and no court in Élavendre hears the cutters' side.

**If bypassed.** The cutters leave the ring in the dark and are found in the morning on the coppice road with their tools broken.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

The Wardens act at the dark of the moon. The cutters are bound in the ring and escorted out of Siralonke with their tools broken, and the foreman is put out of Lirve's woods for good. Evrane learns whose the grove was from Lúmreda, and says nothing at the Council. Garrel's license is quietly renewed. Orlen's roll stands.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

The oak ring lies as it fell, with a cairn at its center. The Council petitions the crown against the license, and the crown receives the petition with deference. The old roll is found or it is not. The tally-house changes its measures, or it keeps them. Whatever the Wardens exact from the cutters, Kardeth's roof is paid for by someone.
