Tensions boil beneath the surface in the coastal City of Batáren, where Provenzian rulers and the indigenous [[lore-elavendrnppl|Élavendren]] people – descendants of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] who settled in these borderlands centuries ago – clash over land, rights, and tradition. A hidden hand stokes the conflict to destabilize the region.

## Overview

Batáren, a prosperous coastal city in southern [[place-provenzrgn|Provènzia Region]], sits at the intersection of two cultures. The city's Provenzian ruling class and merchant establishment maintain order through law and tradition, while the Áelendan people – descendants of those who dwelt in the borderlands before Provenzian expansion – chafe under restrictions and marginalization. For decades, an uneasy peace has held. Now, carefully orchestrated incidents are pushing both communities toward open conflict, and the party must uncover who benefits from this war.

## Stage One: Arrival & Observation

The party arrives in Batáren (or is already present) and begins to notice:

- Rising tension between Provenzian citizens and Áelendan residents
- Isolated violence: attacks on Áelendan merchants, vandalism, murder
- The Provenzian authorities seem more interested in suppressing news than investigating causes
- Áelendi leaders are meeting secretly, preparing for organized response
- Rumors of foreign influence: whispers of [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian]] agents, [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] manipulation, or northern interests

**NPCs:**

- An Áelendan community leader concerned about escalation
- A Provenzian city official dismissive of the conflict's seriousness
- A sympathetic merchant or priest attempting to mediate
- An agitator on either side of the conflict (possibly working for hidden interests)

## Stage Two: Investigation

The party must uncover the root causes and hidden agents behind the escalating tensions:

- Track the origins of violent incidents (who committed them, why, on whose orders)
- Discover a pattern suggesting coordinated provocation
- Identify individuals meeting with both communities, stirring unrest
- Find evidence of payment or coercion linking to foreign powers
- Uncover the destabilizer's goals: chaos, conquest, economic advantage, or political leverage

**Complications:**

- Both communities become suspicious of outsiders investigating
- The party may be mistaken for agents of one side or the other
- Witnesses are intimidated or paid off
- A party member is personally drawn into the conflict (past connection, forced allegiance)
- The Provenzian authorities openly obstruct investigation (if they're complicit)

## Stage Three: The Conspiracy Revealed

Investigation reveals that a foreign power is deliberately stoking conflict to destabilize Batáren:

**Possible Architects:**

- **Vylarian Agents:** The [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] seeks to undermine Provenzian stability and justify imperial intervention
- **Ordo Arcanis Manipulation:** The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] manipulates both sides to weaken Provenzian independence or create a pretext for magical research
- **Nordheim Interests:** [[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Nordheim raiders]] or their agents seek to damage the city's naval power and trade influence
- **Internal Conspiracy:** A Provenzian noble faction uses foreign agents to advance their own position

**Key Evidence:**

- Correspondence between agitators and foreign handlers
- Payment records tracing to distant power centers
- Intelligence reports about shipments of arms or incitement materials
- A captured agent with incriminating documents

## Stage Four: Preventing Catastrophe

As the conspiracy is exposed, the community stands on the brink of open warfare. The party must:

- Prevent or disrupt planned violent acts (an assassination, a massacre, arson)
- Publicly expose the conspiracy to both communities
- Mediate between leaders and restore dialogue
- Secure evidence and present it to legitimate authority
- Ensure that the foreign conspirators are identified and neutralized

**Possible Conflicts:**

- A violent incident occurs during the party's investigation (they must choose sides or prevent escalation)
- The foreign agents attempt to complete their sabotage before exposure
- Both communities' hotheads move toward violence regardless of the truth
- The Provenzian authorities attempt to suppress the truth or blame the party
- A direct confrontation with the foreign power's representatives (diplomacy or combat)

## Stage Five: Aftermath & Lasting Impact

**If the party succeeds:**

- The conspiracy is exposed and foreign agents are removed or neutralized
- Batáren's communities begin healing, though deep wounds remain
- Relations between Áelendan and Provenzian leadership improve significantly
- The party gains allies in both communities
- They may be commissioned to prevent similar infiltration in other cities
- Foreign powers may seek subtle revenge or renewed manipulation later

**Ongoing Complications:**

- Some hotheads refuse to believe the conspiracy and blame the party
- Economic or cultural tensions remain even if violence is prevented
- The foreign power establishes new agents or strategies to achieve their goals
- The Provenzian authorities may take credit for resolving the crisis or blame the party for disruption

**If the party fails:**

- Open warfare erupts between communities
- Batáren is crippled economically and socially
- The foreign conspiracy succeeds in weakening [[place-provenzrgn|Provènzia Region]]
- The party may be blamed for the violence or forced into one side of the conflict
