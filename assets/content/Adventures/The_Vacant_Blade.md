Every year the New Year begins at dawn on the summit of [[place-zojai|Zojai]], when the Emperor performs the [Dawn Rite]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} and the envoy of every lord stands in the order the court has set. This year the places are not yet set, because the office of Teng Ja, Heaven's Blade, has been vacant since the death of [[being-henwohojujai|Hojujai Henwo]], and every house has an opinion on who should hold it next. This frame runs from the first frost to the morning of the rite. Through that winter every lord who wants a place near the front, every envoy who wants an answer and every merchant who holds a debt of the court must choose a bloc, a price or a silence, and the choosing has begun.

## The Situation and the Stakes {#the-situation}

**The vacancy.** Teng Ja is the Emperor's sword-arm. The Emperor confers it on a Dauzhwo who has brought enough of the others to heel, and only he can confer it. [[being-weilengjnch|Weileng]] is nineteen and of age, and the eight Dauzhwo are testing whether he rules or is ruled. The office has not been conferred, and every house is waiting on one act that it cannot make for him.

**The favorite.** [[being-henzoshuiwo|Shuiwojai Henzo]] has covered the court's shortfall with his silver for three years, so the court is in his debt, and he has petitioned for the office. The Emperor has answered with a sealed letter that only he and the hands that sealed it have read. The houses that fear an office bought with money are the houses Henzo needs.

**The three blocs.** [The Strait]{.name data-type="affiliation"} is the [[affiliation-shuiwojaidmn|Shuiwojai Domain]] with [[being-hojarinjai|Rinjai Hoja]] of the [[affiliation-rinjaidmn|Rinjai Domain]] sworn to Henzo: 2,600 men and 85 ships, and they want Henzo named Teng Ja. The imperial loyalists are the [[affiliation-hojujaidmn|Hojujai Domain]], held for a boy of fourteen by his mother, and the [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|Muwojai Domain]]: 2,500 men and 20 ships, and they want the office for the Hojujai heir when he comes of age, or for nobody. [The Independents]{.name data-type="affiliation"} are the [[affiliation-wojaidmn|Wojai Domain]], the [[affiliation-zenjaidmn|Zenjai Domain]], the [[affiliation-wejaidmn|Wejai Domain]] and the [[affiliation-tsuijaidmn|Tsuijai Domain]], with 3,970 men and 55 ships among them, and each has its own price.

**The bidding.** If Zenjai or Wojai joins either bloc, the balance of the Nine ends, so both blocs are bidding for them now, and both have named a price. Zenjai wants grain. Wojai has named his price to nobody. Wejai and Tsuijai have named theirs to no one and want to be asked next.

**The poor court.** The Emperor does not leave Zojai, so the realm comes to him. The court received 4,100 bales against the 9,000 it spends, and the household of the Retired Emperor in [[place-lengwou|Lengwou]] is part of that cost. What the Emperor can give costs him nothing: a patent, a dignity, a place at the front of the Dawn Rite. The lords prize them above silver.

**The rite.** At first light on the first day of the year the bronze bell of the Dawn Rite sounds. This year it lies in pawn in a merchant house of [[place-yuwu|Yuwu]]. Whoever redeems it buys the Emperor's gratitude.

The stakes are the balance of the Nine, the Emperor's standing among his lords, and whether the next year is one of open war or of a new balance.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The frame prescribes no recipient for the office and no outcome for the contest. The Emperor is met at his own pace, and what the party meets are the houses that want it: each with a price, a route and a reason to read someone else's letter. Play every bid as a quiet matter, and let the table, if anyone, bring them into the open.

## Who Moves It {#who-moves-it}

[[being-weilengjnch|Weileng]], Emperor of Jenchai
: Nineteen, patient, formal and watchful, enthroned at eleven. He issues the patent of every mountain and has a household guard and no other force. He has not conferred the office, and he lets a silence stand until the other speaker fills it.

[[being-fuzoretired|Fuzo]], the Retired Emperor
: His father, who abdicated as the price of the peace and took vows. His household in Lengwou costs more than the court can pay, and the lords resent it. He advises and holds no authority, and a house that wins his ear has a voice the throne hears.

[[being-henzoshuiwo|Shuiwojai Henzo]], Dauzhwo of Shuiwojai
: Forty-seven, hearty and calculating, who speaks to envoys as a creditor. He leads the Strait bloc, holds the court's debt and wants the office.

[[being-hojarinjai|Rinjai Hoja]], Dauzhwo of Rinjai
: Twenty-nine, a raider lord sworn to Henzo, who holds a Tantsou prefect that the court wants returned and Henzo wants ransomed.

[[being-reweihojujai|Hojujai Rewei]], regent of Hojujai
: The widow of the last Teng Ja, who holds the house for her son on a reading of the mountain that the next tremor can reverse. She has named a price to Zenjai and Wojai.

[[being-zijahojujai|Hojujai Zija]], Dauzhwo of Hojujai
: Fourteen, earnest and proud, the heir for whom the loyalists would hold his father's office.

[[being-chaiwomuwoj|Muwojai Chaiwo]], Dauzhwo of Muwojai
: Sixty-one, slow and dutiful, with no grown heir. He keeps the ferry, the passes and the roads to the Emperor, and every bloc is courting his possible successors.

[[being-wojaidoja|Wojai Doja]], Dauzhwo of Wojai
: The lord of the best harbor in the realm, who stands with neither bloc and has named his price to neither. He can redeem the bell and is choosing whom to let do it.

[[being-zenjaitsuiwo|Zenjai Tsuiwo]], Dauzhwo of Zenjai
: Blunt and hungry, who will put his spears behind whichever bloc feeds his province before the first snow.

[[being-wejairowo|Wejai Rowo]], Dauzhwo of Wejai
: Shrewd and restless, who holds a foreign hull's cargo and a survivor who claims to be an envoy to the court, and who wants to be asked next.

[[being-tsuijairezo|Tsuijai Rezo]], Dauzhwo of Tsuijai
: Precise and dry, whose forge arms every house and whose price is the forging of the blade, whoever carries it.

The bodies
: The Strait bloc, the loyalists and the Independents; the toll-keepers of Muwojai at the ferry; the [Weiju]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} who keep the court's roll at [Tsaimu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}; the [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] of the court's Omen-Reader, whose instruments fix the day of every imperial act; the merchant house of Yuwu that holds the bell; the [Jaju]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} smiths of Tsaigen; and the envoy from the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] who has asked at the Dawn Court for a prefect.

## The Arcs

The frame follows the year's end from the first frost to the rite, and each arc turns one strand of the contest.

1. **The court.** Lengwou is where the envoys wait for their places and where the Retired Emperor keeps his household. The court's roll is kept at [[place-tsaimustrng|The Court Strongroom at Tsaimu]] by the Weiju, in the ground of Muwojai, and it holds the court's debt to Henzo. The day any imperial act can be performed is fixed from the readings kept at [[place-zijaiobsrvt|The Observatory of Zijai]].
2. **The road.** Every petition and every answer crosses [[place-zujufrry|The Zuju Ferry]], where the toll-keepers enter the name of every party that goes over. [[scenario-ltrforhawu|A Letter for Hawu]] runs the one letter that every bloc wants to read.
3. **The bidding.** Zenjai and Wojai are courted in rice and in silver. At [[place-buiwu|Buiwu]] the north counts its granaries, and at Yuwu a merchant house's strong-room holds the bell. [[scenario-rcbfrsnw|Rice Before the Snow]] runs the race to fill the north before the first snow.
4. **The strait.** Shuiwojai, whose castle town is [[place-hawu|Hawu]], and Rinjai, whose raiders hold the red hall at [[place-rinwu|Rinwu]], make [[place-easternstrt|The Eastern Strait]] pay, and the empire across it has asked for a prefect. [[scenario-prfctrdhll|The Prefect in the Red Hall]] runs the one prisoner every side wants.
5. **The independents and the rite.** At [[place-tsaigenfrg|The Forge of Tsaigen]] the smiths are being asked to begin a blade early, and at [[place-weworeefs|The Wewo Reefs]] a foreign survivor's letters wait on a lord's choice. At [[place-zojaishrn|The Summit Shrine of Zojai]] the envoys take their places at dawn and the bell sounds, or does not.

The arcs run in any order, and each moves the contest whether or not the party finishes it. A party that carries one answer between two houses carries it past everyone else who has asked.

## Ways In {#ways-in}

1. **For a bloc.** The Strait's steward, the Hojujai regent's steward or Muwojai's household hires a party to carry a price, a count or a letter, and on the road someone asks it to carry something else.
2. **For the independents.** Zenjai wants grain counted, Wojai wants a carrier for a question and not an answer, Wejai wants a letter read without learning more than he intends, and Tsuijai wants to know who asked his smiths to start the blade early.
3. **For the court.** Weileng's household sends a party to carry a request for rice to a lord behind on his court estate, to learn what a foreign envoy will offer, or to escort a lord's retinue up Zojai on the morning of the rite.
4. **For the strait.** A Tantsou envoy, a captain without a berth or a Rinjai raider hires a party to cross it, and it learns what the strait's watchers want.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

- The envoys wait through the cold in Lengwou's inns, and the innkeepers count out the rice by the handful.
- The bidders reach Zenjai and Wojai with their prices, and the first snow closes the shoulder roads to the north with the granaries half empty.
- The bell stays in its merchant house's strong-room, or goes up Zojai with whichever house Doja lets pay, and its frame is empty or full at first light.
- The letters on the typhoon coast are read by the first house that gets to them.
- The Dawn Rite comes with the office vacant. The seating is the year's verdict on every house, and every house reads its place.

## The Secret {#the-secret}

**Why the office stays vacant.** The vacancy is Weileng's policy. The Sealed Letter withholds the office, asks Henzo for more silver toward the court's budget and names no price at which the office would follow. A lord needs the Emperor only until he has conferred the office, so while it is unconferred the Strait bloc and the loyalists both need him.

**The household's unpaid bills.** The household owes the rice factor in Lengwou for months of deliveries, and it hides the bills from the Dauzhwo. If the factor stops delivering or talks, every lord learns that the court's poverty has reached the Retired Emperor's table.

This frame does not say whom the office goes to, or whether it is conferred at all. The Game Master decides, and may let the party's actions decide.
