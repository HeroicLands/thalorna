## Overview {#overview}

In the inns of [[place-liranel|Liranel]], between the crown's forest court and the guides' stand, an [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] fellow is hiring guards for one night in [[place-siralonke|Siralonke]]. [[being-ivelinnareth|Ivelin Nareth]], a fellow of [[affiliation-neumenos|Neumenos]], wants a made thing that still walks. The one the Order keeps in a cellar at Péravel stands folded and silent, and he means to bring out a second with nets and a cart on a moonlit night, and be out of the forest before dawn. The crown's guides are not going with him, and the sellswords who are going are paid for the cart and for keeping the Wardens off.

Two other people in Liranel want something of the party at the same time. A woodcutter wants his grandfather's axe, which he lost in the deep wood. A guide's wife wants her husband, who took the fellow's scouts to the lip and has not come home.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

**The hollow.** [[place-wrghthllw|The Wrought Hollow]] is an [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] whose door lets in the fae as made things of root, stone and antler. They carry what they find down into the hollow, and nothing carried in is ever carried out. A thing taken from the floor is followed: the made things come for it the next night and every night after, and they take it back from whoever holds it, with whatever stands in the way. They do not pursue past the edge of Siralonke.

**The cellar at Péravel.** The construct in the Order's cellar stopped when it was carried out of the forest. Last winter a chapterhouse servant saw its hand close in its case, and nobody has been told. It is not dead. Brought back within Siralonke, it walks to the hollow. Ivelin does not know this.

**The plan.** A made thing taken out in a cart stops once the cart is out of Siralonke, as the cellar construct did, and Ivelin counts on it. The danger lies in the road to the forest's edge: the others pursue what leaves the hollow until it is out of the trees, and the edge is a night's walk from the lip.

**The axe.** The woodcutter, [[being-ormenhalren|Ormen Halren]], lost his grandfather's axe at the edge of the coppice, and a neighbor has told him where lost things in that wood are found. The axe lies in the hollow on the floor beside a stacked log and a dead deer laid out straight. Whoever lifts it from the floor is followed. The axe is the smallest test of the Wardens' law, and the party can choose to run it.

**The guide.** [[being-berinsalth|Berin Salthar]] took the fellow's scouts to the lip. A Warden of Siralonke, [[being-kelpanedhar|Kélpane]] of the [Dhárgandi]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}, warned them once and turned them out. When Berin led a second party in, the Wardens held him at a turf watch-hut at the forest edge, and they are sending him out of Lirve's woods for good. His wife, [[being-sedrasalth|Sedra Salthar]], does not know. The Wardens would let her see him.

**The law.** Warden leave never takes a stranger into an Araelwen. A trespasser is warned once. One who returns, or who takes anything from the floor, is dealt with finally, and no court in Élavendre hears his side.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The mercenary.** Ivelin hires a company to guard his night in Siralonke and a fast drive out of it, half in advance.
- **The thief.** Ormen offers silver to anyone who will bring him his grandfather's axe out of the wood.
- **The wanderer.** Sedra asks the party to find her husband before the Wardens do.
- **The leader.** A Warden of Siralonke has heard that someone is asking the way to the hollow, and asks a party in Liranel whether it is theirs.

## The Opening {#opening}

The Hart and Tally, an inn behind the forest court, on the evening the Order fellow's cart is loaded. Ivelin stands among his nets with a lantern and a map he does not unfold.

> "One night," he says. "Moon at the full, a cart, eight nets, and a road. If a thing comes after us, we run for the coppice, and it stops."

## Scenes {#scenes}

### Scene One: Liranel

**Purpose.** The hirers, the guides and the town's two sorts of guide.

**Situation.** Liranel at dusk. The forest court's tally-house, the squared timber in the yards, the inns, and the guides' stand where the crown's guides wear their badge and the others do not.

**NPCs and threats.** Ivelin, courteous and intent. Ormen, who has a story about his grandfather and has told it to everyone. Sedra, who has not slept. The crown's guides, who tell a party the road is closed. The town's magistrates, who take a dim public view and a pragmatic private one.

**Player choices.** Take Ivelin's contract, take Ormen's job, ask after Berin, or ask the crown's guides what they know.

**Clues and rewards.** A guide who will not give his name says that the woodcutters find tracks and leave stacked logs alone. A tally-clerk says no license runs within a long way of the hollow.

**Consequences.** The party leaves Liranel with at least one of the three errands, and Ivelin's cart moves at dusk whether or not the party is with it.

**If bypassed.** Ivelin hires the sellswords at the next table, and the cart goes without a party that knows the way.

### Scene Two: The Rides

**Purpose.** The road in and the Wardens' first warning.

**Situation.** The licensed coppice thins to old oak and silver birch. Rides the woodcutters use run out. A track of round prints, deep and evenly spaced, ends at a stream.

**NPCs and threats.** Kélpane and a second Warden, who stand in the ride and warn once. Blinkfoxes, the small dreadspawn of the deep forest, which hunt round the thin places and step out of the world to bite.

**Player choices.** Turn back, argue, go around, or pretend not to have heard.

**Clues and rewards.** Kélpane names the law and describes the hollow in a sentence. She says what the made things do to what is taken from the floor. She will not say how she knows that.

**Consequences.** A party that turns back is not troubled again. A party that goes on past a Warden's word is a party the Wardens know.

**If bypassed.** The cart goes round by a longer ride, and the Wardens find it at dawn.

### Scene Three: The Lip

**Purpose.** The hollow at night and the made things.

**Situation.** A ring of old oak round a hollow a long stone's throw across. At the bottom a heap of stones without mortar and, around it, what the made things have carried in: a stacked log, a dead deer laid out straight, a woodcutter's lost axe, a lantern.

**NPCs and threats.** The made things, which come up between midnight and the gray one or two at a time and pay no heed to watchers who keep still. Ivelin's men, who do not keep still. Blinkfoxes, which take whoever steps away from the group.

**Player choices.** Net one, stop the nets, take the axe, take nothing, or leave the lip.

**Clues and rewards.** The made things do not tolerate being held. They defend what they carry. A party that stays still sees them walk off into Siralonke on errands of their own.

**Consequences.** Whatever the party lifts from the floor, a thing or a tool, is followed from that hour.

> [!WARNING]
> The made things carry what they find into the hollow and nothing back out. A party that carries an axe, a lantern or a net-bound figure to the road finds them on its heels by midnight, and every night after that, until the thing is returned.

**If bypassed.** Ivelin's nets fall on a made thing, and the thing takes four men with it into the dark.

### Scene Four: The Road Out

**Purpose.** The pursuit, and what the party owes the Wardens.

**Situation.** The night's walk to Liranel with a cart or without one, a made thing or an axe or nothing, and the Wardens at the forest edge.

**NPCs and threats.** Made things in the trees on either side of the ride. Kélpane, who walks beside the cart and does not touch the party's weapons. Ivelin, who counts every step toward the coppice.

> [!NOTE]
> Ivelin's hired men are run as [[being-belrktrvnl|Melrik Tárvenel]], a stock man-at-arms.
>
> A made thing is run as a [[being-rtwrth|Rootwraith]]: patient, hard to hold and fixed on what it came to take back.

**Player choices.** Run for the edge, put the thing down where it was found, bargain with the Wardens, or leave Ivelin's cart to its fate.

**Clues and rewards.** A figure that stops at the coppice's edge is still, and walks again if it is carried back among the trees. A figure put back on the floor goes down into the hollow of its own accord.

**Consequences.** A thing put back is a thing the made things do not take back. A thing carried out is a thing the Order keeps in a cellar in Péravel.

**If bypassed.** The cart reaches the edge with a stilled figure in it. Four men do not come out of the wood.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

Ivelin takes his cart and eight sellswords into Siralonke on the full moon. A made thing is netted, and the others take four men and the cart's lantern into the hollow. The cart reaches the coppice at dawn with a stilled figure under a net. Ormen's axe stays where it lies, Berin is sent out of the woods, and Sedra waits a season for news.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

The Order holds two made things in cellars at Péravel. The Wardens of Siralonke close the rides around the hollow to every guide. The crown receives a petition from the Grove Council and lets it lie. Berin's family leaves Liranel for the coast, and the guides' stand gives the next scholar a longer price.
