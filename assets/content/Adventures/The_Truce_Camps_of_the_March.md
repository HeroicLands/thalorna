Every autumn the camps of the [[affiliation-bjlrtrb|Bujalur]] come in under the truce of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]] to winter in the foothills of the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]], and every spring they go back out onto the grass. This year word has come down the road that a dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes under his banner, and the March is where the League hears of a union first. [[being-mazitosrekha|Mazitos Rekhazur]], master of the March, has ordered the Bujalur camps counted. Before the count is done, the [[affiliation-grnrtrb|Gorenur]] ride on the March's foothill herd in the first storm of spring and drive it east through the Bujalur winter ground. The League must decide whether its guests are its enemies, and it must decide before the thaw lets them go.

## Overview {#overview}

The adventure runs over the last weeks of winter in the March's foothills, from the day the count begins to the day the Lord Commander gives his order. Its clock is the thaw. When the passes open, the Bujalur camps go out onto the grass whether the League has decided or not, and a camp that leaves under suspicion leaves as an enemy.

This is a quarrel among three bodies: a tribe that needs the truce, a tribe that wants it broken, and a frontier power that has to guess which steppe rider is which. The Gorenur's raid is real, and the suspicion of the Bujalur is half true, because one Bujalur camp head has hosted the gathering man's riders and told no one. The party can find out who did what. What the League does with it is Mazitos's choice, and what the Bujalur do with their own camp head is theirs.

## What Is Really Happening {#what-is-really-happening}

The League's officers began counting the Bujalur camps after a caravan master told the March that a Bujalur camp head had fed the gathering man's riders at the end of summer. The tale is true. [Bujalyitboq Bujalur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, head of the easternmost camp, gave the riders salt and heard their offer of herds, and said nothing of it to the dovaq, who told the riders the tribe swears to no one. Bujalyitboq has not sworn. He has not refused either.

The Gorenur know the March is counting. Gorenyemok means the spring's first raid as a gift to the gathering man and a ruin for the Bujalur: in the first storm his riders take the March's foothill herd from the pastures below the third spring-fort, drive it east straight through the Bujalur winter ground, and cut a dozen branded horses out into the Bujalur herd as they pass. For two months before, Gorenur riders sold March horses cheap to Bujalur boys at the springs, so that branded beasts are already in the camps when the patrols come looking.

Mazitos does not know what to believe and knows he has to act. He has offered Lofáhr Vuzaghios a contract to escort the Bujalur camps out of the March before the thaw, which means in the snow.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The Bujalur are not innocent and not guilty. One camp head has listened to the union, and the rest of the tribe did not know. Play Bujalqiimod as a man who learns it during the adventure and must decide what his tribe's custom asks of him: a camp that breaks the truce is put out of the winter ground by its own tribe, before the League can do it.

### How the reveal lands

The reveal has two halves, and the party may find them in either order:

- **The raid was Gorenur, and the frame was deliberate.** The track, the felt and the fifth line's white-beards prove it. This clears the Bujalur of the raid.
- **Bujalyitboq fed the union's riders.** His own camp knows it, and a guest who has eaten his salt hears it at his fire. This does not clear the Bujalur of the suspicion; it narrows it to one camp.

The party decides who hears each half first. Bujalqiimod, told of Bujalyitboq, puts the camp out of the winter ground himself. Mazitos, told of Bujalyitboq before Bujalqiimod is, has the name he needs to turn out every camp.

> [!TIP]
> Let the party hold both halves at once before Scene Five if it can. A party that brings the Gorenur track to Mazitos without the Bujalyitboq half is believed, and then finds out it was half right in front of him.

### What the party wants afterward

Once it knows, the party is the only body that holds the truth about both tribes. The Bujalur want the first half told and the second kept; the March wants both; the Gorenur want neither. Lofáhr wants a reason to refuse his contract, and the truth gives him one.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The thief.** A horse-dealer of the outer caravanserais pays the party to find the March's raided herd before the League does, because a herd recovered by a dealer is sold back to the League at his price.
- **The mercenary or common soldier.** Mazitos hires the party, through a captain of the outer forts, to ride with the count and tell him which camps lie.
- **The leader.** Bujalqiimod wants someone the March trusts to stand beside his truce-rider at the count, because the count is the first step of something.
- **The lone wanderer.** The wanderer is a guest in a Bujalur camp for the winter, salt eaten and owed its protection, when the patrols come.

## The Opening {#opening}

Read or paraphrase:

> The third spring-fort squats over its spring in a fold of the foothills, and the trough below its wall is cut long enough for a garrison and a camp to fill at once. Today the soldiers fill their jars at one end and the Bujalur women their skins at the other, and between them an officer with a wax tablet is writing down every skin.
>
> Below the fort, in the brown winter grass, the Bujalur tents stand in a long loose line under the snow-streaked hills, and the smoke goes straight up. An old man in a felt coat stands by the officer and answers his questions in the League's tongue, slowly, one at a time.

> [!TIP]
> Open on the shared trough. [[lore-twosealed|The Song of the Two-Sealed]], which the way-forts sing, ends at a grave beside this spring, and a garrison soldier who hums it under his breath while the officer writes is all the scene needs.

## Scene One: The Count

**Purpose.** The suspicion. Put the party at the count, and let it meet both sides of the truce.

**Situation.** The March's officers count the Bujalur camps along the foothills: tents, riders, horses. Bujalqiimod rides from camp to camp ahead of them so that each camp head hears it from him first. The count reaches the easternmost camp last, and Bujalyitboq gives his numbers short.

**NPCs and threats.**

- **Bujalqiimod Bujalur**, the dovaq, about fifty-five, patient and tired, who has sold horses to the March for thirty winters.
- **A captain of the outer forts**, carrying the Lord Commander's order and a caravan master's tale.
- **Bujalyitboq Bujalur**, head of the easternmost camp, about forty, polite and closed.
- **Bujalur boys** with good horses that their fathers did not breed.

**Player choices.**

- _Ride with the count:_ watch every camp.
- _Parley:_ eat at a Bujalur fire, and hear what the camp says of the count and of Bujalyitboq.
- _Cleverness:_ look at the horses. A few in three camps carry the March's brand.

**Clues and rewards.** The caravan master's tale, from the captain. The branded horses, and the boys' account of buying them cheap at the springs from riders of no camp they knew. Bujalyitboq's short count.

**Consequences.** The captain reports the branded horses to the Lord Commander. Bujalqiimod asks the party, quietly, what the League means to do.

**If bypassed, failed or cut short.** A party that misses the count meets the captain at the third spring-fort with his tablet and his tale, and Bujalqiimod at the trough.

## Scene Two: The First Storm

**Purpose.** The raid. Let the storm do the Gorenur's work in front of the party.

**Situation.** Ten days into the count the first storm of spring comes down off the passes, wind and sleet, and closes the foothills for a night and a day. The March's herd is in the pastures below the third spring-fort; the patrols are in the forts. In the morning the herd is gone.

**NPCs and threats.**

- **Gorenur raiders**, in the storm. A party out in it may meet them at the ford below the pastures, driving the herd east at a run. They fight to keep the herd and ride off without it if they must.
- **The garrison**, which turns out at dawn and follows the track.
- **The storm** itself: sleet, darkness and a frozen ford.

**Player choices.**

- _Fight:_ meet the raiders at the ford. A party that does turns part of the herd back and is certain who took the rest.
- _Track:_ follow the track at dawn with the garrison, or ahead of it.
- _Shelter:_ stay in camp or fort, and hear the storm.

**Clues and rewards.** The track runs from the pastures straight through the middle of the Bujalur camps, and out again on the far side wider than it went in, turning east toward [[place-hekardesert|Hek'ar]]. At the ford a door-felt cut from a tent lies frozen to the bank, woven with a Gorenur line's mark. A dozen March horses are found among the Bujalur herd by noon.

**Consequences.** The garrison finds the horses in the Bujalur herd and the track through the camps. The captain sends to Mazitos that the Bujalur rode with the raid.

**If bypassed, failed or cut short.** A party that did not see the raid hears of it at dawn and can still walk the track before the snow fills it; it has until midday.

## Scene Three: The Horses in the Camps

**Purpose.** The stand-off. Bring the League into the Bujalur camps and put the party between them.

**Situation.** A patrol comes into the camps to take back the March's horses and the boys who hold them. Lofáhr Vuzaghios arrives at the third spring-fort the same day with his company, under contract. Bujalyitboq's camp has struck its tents and is ready to ride east.

**NPCs and threats.**

- **The patrol**, angry and outnumbered.
- **Bujalur riders**, who give up the horses and not the boys.
- **[[being-lofrflmds|Lofáhr Vuzaghios]]**, tall, heavy and cold-voiced, who has agreed to escort the camps out of the March without seeing what that means.
- **Bujalyitboq**, who wants to be gone before anyone asks him anything.

**Player choices.**

- _Parley:_ get the horses back to the patrol without the boys.
- _Show:_ take Lofáhr along the track and show him where it came out.
- _Stop Bujalyitboq:_ hold his camp until the dovaq has spoken to him. His riders resist strangers and give way to Bujalqiimod.
- _Ask:_ at Bujalyitboq's fire, as a guest, ask about the riders who came at the end of summer. His wife answers before he can.

**Clues and rewards.** Bujalyitboq's secret, from his own camp. Lofáhr's terms: he escorts fighting men out of the March for the League's silver, and he does not drive women and children into snow for anyone's.

**Consequences.** If Bujalqiimod learns of Bujalyitboq here, he calls the camp heads together that night.

**If bypassed, failed or cut short.** If the party is not in the camps, the patrol takes three boys to the fort, and the Bujalur riders ride after them to the gate and sit their horses there all night.

## Scene Four: Khelid

**Purpose.** The other tribe. Take the party east into the grass after the herd, to the Gorenur well, where the raid's truth and the Gorenur's own quarrel are.

**Situation.** The track runs east for five days, into the Gorenur range at the edge of Hek'ar. The herd is at [Khelid]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the Gorenur's chief well, under a split ridge. Four of the five Gorenur lines are camped round it; the fifth is a day off, apart.

**NPCs and threats.**

- **Gorenyemok Gorenur**, the dovaq, about thirty-five, who leads the raids and has never come home without horses. He offers the party a share of the herd to go home and say the Bujalur took it.
- **The white-beards of the fifth line**, old men who remember the winter after the last union broke, and who want no war with the March and no banner.
- **Gorenur riders**, many, and on their own ground.

**Player choices.**

- _Bargain:_ take Gorenyemok's share, or buy the herd back.
- _Parley:_ go to the fifth line. Its white-beards will say before the League's captain that the four lines rode and the Bujalur did not, if the League promises the fifth line its spring grass.
- _Steal:_ take the herd back at night.
- _Fight:_ the Gorenur are on their own ground and in numbers, and a party that fights at Khelid has made a feud with five lines.

**Clues and rewards.** The herd, with the March's brand on every shoulder. The fifth line's word. A weaver who will sell a felt of any line's mark to anyone.

**Consequences.** Gorenyemok sends riders north to the gathering man with the best of the herd, whatever the party does.

**If bypassed, failed or cut short.** A party that does not ride east can still bring the fifth line's word in Scene Five, if a white-beard of the fifth line comes to the third spring-fort on his own, to ask the League for his line's spring grass.

## Scene Five: The Lord Commander's Order

**Purpose.** The decision. Bring the bodies to the Lord Commander, and let the custom of the Bujalur and the order of the League meet.

**Situation.** Mazitos comes down from Argirteikhos to the third spring-fort to give his order before the thaw. The Bujalur camp heads come to the trough. Lofáhr waits for his order too.

**NPCs and threats.**

- **[[being-mazitosrekha|Mazitos Rekhazur]]**, master of the Eastern March, fifty, the League's senior soldier, who has held the March for two decades and has seen camps turned out before.
- **Bujalqiimod** and the Bujalur camp heads, with Bujalyitboq among them.
- **Lofáhr Vuzaghios**.
- **The captain** with his tablet.

**Player choices.**

- _Tell it all:_ the Gorenur raid and Bujalyitboq both, before Mazitos and the camp heads together.
- _Tell half:_ clear the Bujalur of the raid and keep Bujalyitboq for the Bujalur to deal with.
- _Let the Bujalur act first:_ if Bujalqiimod already knows, he tells Mazitos himself that the tribe has put Bujalyitboq's camp out of the winter ground, and asks the truce for the rest.
- _Stay silent:_ let Mazitos decide on the captain's report.

**Clues and rewards.** None left. The reward is the order.

**Consequences.** Mazitos's order, by what he has heard:

- **Both halves told, the Bujalur having acted.** The truce holds for every camp but Bujalyitboq's, which Lofáhr escorts out with its fighting men, its women and children riding behind on their own horses. The League sends a patrol east to Khelid.
- **The raid cleared, Bujalyitboq kept.** The truce holds, and Bujalyitboq's camp winters in the March next year with the gathering man's offer still in its head.
- **Nothing cleared.** Every camp is turned out before the thaw. Lofáhr refuses the contract, and the League sends conscripts.

**If bypassed, failed or cut short.** Mazitos gives his order on the captain's report; see If the Party Does Nothing.

## If the Party Does Nothing {#if-the-party-does-nothing}

- The captain reports the branded horses and the track through the camps.
- Mazitos orders every Bujalur camp out of the March before the thaw. Lofáhr refuses the contract, and conscripts from the forts drive the camps out into the sleet.
- The Bujalur lose beasts and children on the road east, and their young riders ride north to the gathering man in spring. Bujalyitboq's camp goes first.
- The Gorenur's herd reaches the gathering man, with word that the March has turned its own guests out.
- The next autumn no camp comes in under the truce, and the March's patrols meet Bujalur riders on the grass instead.

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

- **If the truce held.** The Bujalur winter in the March again, and Bujalqiimod owes the party a horse of its choosing every autumn he is dovaq. The March and the Gorenur are at war over a herd.
- **If Bujalyitboq's camp was put out.** It rides north to the gathering man, and the first Bujalur under his banner are the ones the League let go.
- **If the fifth line spoke.** The Gorenur are split, four lines against one, and the fifth line asks the League for its spring grass; the white-beards of the four lines call it the Bujalur's way.
- **If Lofáhr refused his contract.** He has done something he can defend, and he tells the party so over a drink, once.
