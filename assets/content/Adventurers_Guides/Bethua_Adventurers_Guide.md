> The ship comes into the bay of Bethûra in the cool of the morning, oars dipping slow, and the water inside the headlands lies flat and green as glass. The whole city faces you as it rises: the quays and the long stone warehouses along the harbor-front, then roofs of red tile climbing the slope among orchards, then the temple roofs at the top, white columns catching the early sun. A breeze off the land brings orange blossom, wet stone and the brine smell of the murex vats, where the dyers' cloth hangs drying in sheets of deep purple. The quay is busy and orderly. Bare-chested men in linen kilts roll oil jars down the gangplanks and stack them without a word wasted, and a woman in a gray mantle walks the line of them with a wax tablet, marking each jar as it passes. Two women of the Spear-Sisterhood stand at the head of the quay in bronze and white linen, spears grounded, watching the porters, not the ships.
>
> A woman in a mantle of saffron edged with purple comes down to the foot of your gangplank with a clerk at either shoulder, both of them women, each carrying a sealed case. Her eyes go to the sword at your hip, rest there a moment, then move to the Spear-Sisters and back. "House Othris-Khanu," she says, before anything else. "I am Dámìra, its factor on this quay." Then, in the brisk speech of the docks: "_En-telekâs'sh khabetûn pleôs et-degân_." Her clerk gives it in plain words, without being asked: the quay's warehouse is full; pay the customs-due. Dámìra holds out her hand, palm up, for your manifest.

[[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Bethûa]] is a matriarchy on the northern shore of [[place-xerathia|Xerathia]], between [[affiliation-okharis|Okháris]] and [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Aû'Khelâthu]], facing [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris]] across the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]]. Its coast is orchard country of citrus, olive, vine, fig and pomegranate, and its old ports are among the most cosmopolitan in the south. Inland the rain fails, and the interior lives on water that a sworn order of engineer-priestesses brings to it by aqueduct, tunnel and canal. Bethûa won its freedom from a Helonic mother-country with Khelâthi help, and its treasury, its water and its money still carry Khelâthi names.

**The one thing to understand first: in Bethûa a man is in someone's charge.** Property, title and authority pass from mother to daughter. A Bethûan man cannot own property, inherit, make a contract in his own name or stand in court except through the woman responsible for him, and no man may carry a weapon or armor on pain of death unless he is authorized. The law is written for Bethûan men, and a foreign man with a sword at his hip is something the Spear-Sisters must decide about.

## What You Meet First

The House is the unit of everything. A Dómissa leads it, it owns the land, the workshops, the boats and the cargoes, and a Bethûan woman introduces herself by her House before her own name. The realm stands on two pillars: the **Sacred College** of high priestesses of [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Lúsinía]], [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aethería]] and [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Ólvenía]], and the **Council of Houses** of the matriarchs. The Mêtríssa, queen and high priestess at once, sits where they meet and wears the **Purple Veil**.

A foreign man who trades here is served fairly, his coin taken and his contracts honored, and he is met throughout with a cool condescension. A foreign woman trading on her own account is dealt with woman to woman and may find Bethûa the easiest market on the coast. An official of Aû'Khelâthu, man or woman, is received with marked deference, and everyone watching is meant to notice. Bethûa strikes no coin of its own; purses hold [[lore-aukhlthcrncy|Khelâthi weight-pieces]], and large sums move as letters of credit sealed by the [[affiliation-bthntrdhss|Trade-Houses]].

## Where to Go

[[place-bethura|Bethûra]], the capital, seats both councils and the federation of the Trade-Houses; a Great House's letter is good at any other Great House, and a Junior House's paper is rarely taken outside the city that issued it. [[place-ashira|Ashíra]] on the coast is the seat of **House Khalath-Mara**, the oldest and proudest of the Great Houses. Inland, the line between green wheat and straw-colored grass is wherever the water stops, and the southern borderlands are shared with nomads who pay tribute rather than obey. The [[affiliation-bethuanflt|Bethûan Fleet]], the best navy in the region, escorts the convoys and wages a deniable privateer war on Vylarian shipping.

Do not mistake an unarmed Bethûan woman for a defenseless one: the Charíssa, an open-hand and kicking art built on speed, is taught to every woman of the **Spear-Sisterhood** and to many free women besides.

## Ways In

A Trade-House pays you in paper and a rival House refuses to honor it three ports along. A keeper of the sealed rolls of the **Purple Warrant**, the crown's commission for privateers, has fled south with one of them, and a party that rides with the thief-taker [[being-zahirmlkht|Zahira Malkhet]] may be sent after her. A qanat in the interior has failed, the engineer-priestesses want to know whether the collapse was an accident, and the village beside it is watching its fields brown. A Vylarian captain wants a crew to find out which Bethûan galley took his cargo.
