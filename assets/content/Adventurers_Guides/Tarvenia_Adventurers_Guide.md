> It is barely past dawn in [[place-valthari|Valthari]] and already hot. The sun comes off the sea in a hard gold bar and lies along the harbor wall, and the stones of the quay are warm through the soles of your boots. Wine casks stand in ranks waiting for the lighters, oil jars sweat in the shade of a warehouse built of gray stone bound with black iron, and the whole waterfront smells of tar, olive pressings and fish frying in a cookshop doorway. Somewhere behind the customs house a crossbow string slaps, then another, and a man counts the hits aloud. Valthari talks loudly and all at once: a Taruzeli factor bargains in two tongues, a woman in a black headscarf calls numbers to a clerk from a ledger she will not let him hold, and three young men in patched brigandines sit on an upturned boat oiling their swords and arguing about a contract they have not yet signed.
>
> The man they are waiting for comes down the steps from the hiring yard, gray at the temples, one ear notched, a buff coat open over a shirt the color of old wine. He stops in front of you, takes your right hand, turns it over and runs his thumb across the calluses before he lets it go. Then he grips your forearm, hard. "_Kothren meru, ath predhen est enthráth nos_," he says, loud enough for the young men to hear: my brother, peace is between us. "[Taviro]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, son of [Gerdan]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, of the [Gray Highlands]{.name data-type="place/region"}. I captain the company you came to join." He nods at the upturned boat. "Show me what you can do with a crossbow before the sun gets up."

[[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]] is a standing argument that nobody intends to settle: three kingdoms, a free city, a free territory and a patchwork of fiefdoms in the southern mainland of [[place-aureldirgn|Aureldia]], bound by the [[skill-tarvenlng|Tarveni tongue]], the Aureldian faiths and a shared conviction that nobody tells a Tarvenan what to do. A High King reigns at [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] in the mountains of [[place-asturath|Asturath]], elected by the great lords and kept weak enough to argue with. [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylaria]] never held the mountains, and the people remember it.

**The one thing to understand first: a Tarvenan is his word.** The mercenary trade that sends Tarvenan swordsmen and crossbowmen from Vylaria to [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Taruzel]] runs on the contract, and a company whose captain broke one does not get another. Contract-breaking is the worst thing a person here can be known for, worse than cowardice, because it shames everyone who marched under that captain. A lord is owed what was agreed and nothing more.

## What You Meet First

Selling a sword is an honorable career, the respectable road for a second son, and a village of no importance may be surprisingly well armed. Men go abroad under contract for years, and women run the holdings, the accounts and the contracts while they are away; in the free city and the free territory women hold office outright. A household takes in cousins, fosterlings and veterans of a dead brother's company, and a man who has fought four seasons beside another is kin.

Honor is transacted in public. An insult given before witnesses is answered before the same witnesses, in proportion and not to the death, and a stranger who escalates too fast has misread the grammar. Magic is distrusted. A village wise-woman is a neighbor and answerable, and she is respected; an [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Arcane Order]] mage who throws his weight about tends to end up with a knife between his ribs, and the investigation is slow. The faith keeps its village shrines and its own priests, and favors [[lore-ardavondty|Árdavon]] of gates and oaths. The coin is [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vylarian]]: the Solden, the Ardin and the Bit.

## Where to Go

Valthari, the richest port, governs itself through the [Consejarath]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, a council of merchant houses under a charter bought from the High King, and the mountain lords call it soft. [[place-olvestria|Ólvestra]] is a moneylenders' city behind the [Twin Gate]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, where the [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|Order of the Sealed Word]] keeps the register of every contested oath and the [[affiliation-twinbldrdr|Twinblade Order]] keeps the gate; it is also where the [[affiliation-ordershnvw|Order of the Ashen Vow]] keeps its largest cell. At [[place-polthamis|Polthamis]] the [Great Temple of Árdavon]{.name data-type="place/structure"} trains priests who are taught law and the sword together. [[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Galvaren]] on the foggy west coast sends out the largest fishing fleets in the west, [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Lienrik]] holds the richest farmland and the most ambitious lords, and the [[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]] keeps a people older than the Tarveni and their own law.

The western border with [[place-provenzrgn|Provenzia]] is fought over every summer in raid and counter-raid that neither crown calls a war, and the passes close in winter. In the north, the [[place-toravarrange|Toravar Range]] is [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan high country]], and a baron who pushes his herds or his loggers up into it can start a small mountain war.

## Ways In

A company signs you on for a season abroad, and the employer begins to bend the terms. A widow who has run her husband's holding for six years learns that his captain sold his contract, and she wants the captain found before witnesses. A brother of the Sealed Word needs riders to bring an oath-breaker to a hearing, and the oath-breaker is a baron with friends in the hills around Ólvestra. An Arcane Order factor in Valthari hires guards who will not ask why the town has gone quiet around him.
