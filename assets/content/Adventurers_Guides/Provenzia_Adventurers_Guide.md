> The ship comes into Válaren in the full heat of the afternoon, and the light off the harbor is so white that you squint at the city through your fingers. White stone rises from the water in tiers, every gallery shuttered in green or blue against the sun, and the breeze off the Vylarian Sea carries tar, hot dust and the sweetness of fruit going soft in the sun. The quay is loud. Porters argue the weight of a crate in quick, rising voices that sound like a quarrel and end in laughter; a boy runs past with a basket of peaches packed in straw, each one wrapped in its own leaf; a glassblower's runner carries a rack of green goblets on his head and calls a running string of compliments to anyone who steps out of his way. A merchant in a doublet slashed with saffron silk reads a letter under an awning while a clerk at his elbow fans him with a ledger.

She looks you over from boots to hat, takes in the salt stains, and smiles as if you had arrived in velvet. [[place-provenzrgn|Provènzia]] is the rich, clever southwest of [[place-aureldirgn|Aurèldía]], a country of marble cities, vineyards and academies between the warm [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]] and the cold ocean. Half of its stone was inherited from the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] and half was built since out of trade money. The [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provènzia]] has a king, a **Council of Peers** and chartered cities, and the cities matter most: a Provenzian names his city before his family and treats the man forty miles up the coast as a foreigner with a funny accent.

**The one thing to understand first: in Provènzia, nobody stands alone.** A patron gives money, protection, introductions and the use of his name; a client gives attendance, loyalty, votes and the visible fact of one more person in the patron's train. A person with no patron is unattached, and that word describes a danger. Arrive with a patron's letter and doors open. Arrive without one and you are charming for an evening.

## What You Meet First

Manners here are ornate, indirect and fast. A compliment is a coin of exchange, a direct refusal is a discourtesy, and an exact and cutting thing can be said inside the ornament where another Provenzian hears it perfectly. The code of courtly love governs far more than romance: it is how patronage is negotiated without either side saying the ugly part aloud. Dress, table and house are the public record of a family's standing, and a family that lets them slip is known to be in trouble before anyone has seen its books.

Learning is prized, and rhetoric above all. The academies of the great cities teach natural philosophy, law and the magical arts, and the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] works openly here under royal oversight. Temples to [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Ménérva]] and [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Ólvenía]] double as galleries and concert halls. The coin is [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vylarian]]: the Aurion, the Argo and the Bit.

## Where to Go

[[place-lunacorte|Lunacòrte]], the capital, is a port as well as a court, and the crown and the Council of Peers conduct their long contest in its anterooms. [[place-valarencity|Válaren]] lives wholly on its harbor; nobody has ever taken it by assault, and a blockade is the one thing it fears. On the cold western coast of [[place-zuravlenrgn|Zûravlen]], peach orchards and fast boats race fruit to the southern tables. In the north, [[place-tramontana|Tramontàna]] holds the Élavendren borderland, the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] mountains no crown truly holds, and the port of [[place-batarencity|Batáren]], where an old quarrel over land and rights is still unsettled.

The east is the [[place-serramarca|Sèrramarca]], the mountain march against [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]]. Raiding begins when the snow clears from the passes and ends when it returns, the levy musters at [[place-chastelclr|Chastèlclair]] every spring, and the courts sit in winter because nothing else can be done then.

## The Blood-fields

Provènzia names the ground rather than the battle. Scattered across the kingdom are the [[lore-bloodfields|blood-fields]], where so many died at once that the boundary with the spirit world tore and never closed. The commonest kind, a recurrence, only repeats the battle on its night. A quickening makes the land grow too well, and the farmers there do not talk about it. A silence keeps every bird away, and a dread returns the panic or rage once felt on it. The worst is a withering, and the one at [[place-bldfldyharvalen|Yhârvalen]], is killing the country around it, and the [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] is rousing it on purpose.

## Ways In

A patron sends you north with a letter for an Élavendren house in Batáren, and two other patrons pay to know what it says. A merchant house in Válaren hires you to break a blockade's grip on its credit before its partners desert it. A baron in Zûravlen wants a quickened field farmed through one more harvest without anyone asking why the fruit is so large. A frontier family in the Sèrramarca needs hands for the raiding season, and the far side of the pass belongs to another family that has fought this one for longer than anyone remembers.
