> You come into Kaljékor in the last heat of the afternoon, walking behind a herd. The dust the cattle raise hangs red-gold in the low sun, it is in your teeth and the creases of your hands, and the lowing of a thousand animals rolls ahead of you into the town. Then the pyramid stands up out of the haze, terrace on terrace of red sandstone, and every terrace is a market: awnings of striped cloth, copper pans flashing, bolts of linen and stacked hides, and at the top a thin column of smoke going straight up into the still air. The festival has filled the place. Herdsmen with spears and wool cloaks crowd the lower steps, foreheads smeared gray with ashmark; a priest-noble in a collar of beaten gold is carried through them in a chair, and nobody makes room until he is nearly on top of them. Merchants up from Aû'Khelâthu weigh silver on hand scales. Children run between the legs of the cattle, and somewhere a drum is keeping time for dancers you cannot see.
>
> A tall woman with close-cropped hair and a cloak of brown and white calfskin pushes out of the crowd, an ox-goad over one shoulder and a gourd in her hand. She counts the herd as it passes her, lips moving, before she looks at you. "_Okháré, né thalé_," she says, loud and cheerful, a greeting that hails you as one of the people: Okháric one, greetings. "Ádira, of the line of Kíma, of the Táluméng, of Kaljékor." She pulls the stopper from the gourd with her teeth and hands it to you, and it is soured milk, cold from the shade, while her herders stand by watching to see you drink.

[[affiliation-okharis|Okháris]] is a hot kingdom on the southern margin of [[place-xerathia|Xerathia]]'s fertile belt, between [[place-bethuargn|Bethûa]] to the east and the impassable [[place-cntrlrnfrsts|Central Rainforests]] to the south. In the west the rain does the work that canals do elsewhere: barley, sorghum, flax, dates and figs grow on it, and the village tribes farm, fish and fell timber from fixed seats. Eastward the land dries to savanna, and the people follow cattle, sheep, goats and small sturdy horses between seasonal grazings on cycles so old they are a religious duty. Three temple-cities stand above it all, and the herds and the cities need each other.

**The one thing to understand first: wealth walks.** A rich Okhárin's wealth is livestock that can be counted at a glance, raided, and never hidden, so a display of hospitality is also a display of accounts. Land is not owned; grazing is held by customary right, this clan in that valley in that season, and a clan that yields a right once has yielded it for good. Grazing rights are the commonest cause of bloodshed in the realm, and feuds are long and settled in cattle.

## What You Meet First

An Okhárin names his lineage, then his clan, then his city, and places you by the same three. He belongs to a clan, which moves, and to a city, which does not and which keeps his festivals, his ironwork and his god. The compact between the two halves is the **Three-Flame Settlement**: the cities give religious legitimacy, metal and trade, and the clans give the herds, the wool and leather, and the warriors. The rural clans often feel neglected, and the cities rarely notice.

The faith is one god, [[affiliation-nkaruthar|Nkaru'thar]], the eternal flame of creation, destruction and renewal, read through three cities and never as three gods. On an ordinary day a herdsman with a sick calf prays to the [[lore-zohira|zohira]], the spirits of fertility, storm, river and death whose shrines dot the grazing country. The festivals hold the realm together: clan leaders gather, disputes are heard, marriages are made, and herds change hands. Okháris strikes no coin; the [[lore-aukhlthcrncy|Khelâthi weight-pieces]] of gezan and qelu pass everywhere.

## Where to Go

[[place-zarhanis|Zarhánis]], the First Flame, keeps the largest temple and the **First Ember** that has never gone out, and the [[being-takorozanethar|Overlord Tákoro Zanethar]] lives there beside the High Pyremant. [[place-kaljekor|Kaljékor]], the Golden Flame, sits on the trade roads and is ruled by a council of priest-nobles; merchants swear oaths on its gilded **Scales of Kaljékor**. [[place-vuthraka|Vuthráka]], the Wild Flame, guards the southern frontier under the Warden of the Flame, who brands proven warriors in the **Trial of Flames**. Beyond the settled country, the mountains of [[place-yandamalaika|Yanda Malaika]] are held to be the dwelling of the gods and the seat of the [[lore-orclbonesspr|Oracle of Bones]], and the shamans who claim to have reached it do not agree on where it is.

The dangers are the herds' dangers. Raiders take cattle, a clan pushes into a valley that is not its season, and the eastern clans pay tribute to Bethûa for grazing that their grandfathers held free. Trade wealth feeds Kaljékor's ambitions while Vuthráka demands more for defense, and the Overlord holds the balance by ceremony. South lies the green wall of the rainforest, which nobody has charted.

## Ways In

A clan hires you to drive its herd to the festival through country where a rival clan claims the grazing. A merchant swore falsely on the Scales of Kaljékor, the year and a day is nearly out, and his family wants someone to find out what is coming for him. The [[affiliation-magaratrb|Magara]] have lost a Spirit Blade to raiders. A shaman of the [[affiliation-shokumatrb|Shókuma]] wants an escort into Yanda Malaika and will not say what she means to ask.

## Glossary

| Word                  | Meaning                                                                                                       |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ashmark               | Temple hearth-ash mixed with sacred oils, applied to forehead and hands at passage, mourning and consecration |
| Crown Moneylender     | One of the crown-chartered moneylenders who keep accounts with the Gár-Hálzi and issue letters of credit      |
| grazing right         | A clan's customary claim to a valley in a season, the commonest cause of bloodshed in the realm               |
| High Pyremant         | The supreme spiritual leader of Okháris, who rules Zarhánis and advises the Overlord                          |
| priest-noble          | A member of the priestly aristocracy whose council rules Kaljékor                                             |
| pyrestone             | A fragment of volcanic glass carried by the faithful as a link to the Eternal Flame                           |
| Spirit Blade          | A weapon blessed by the zohira, kept by the Magara                                                            |
| Steward of the Trades | The officer who presides over the crown-chartered trades                                                      |
| temple-city           | One of the three pyramid cities that tend an aspect of the Eternal Flame                                      |
| three-wick lamp       | The prayer lamp of the faith, one wick for each aspect; a wick put out before the others is ill-omened        |
| Warden of the Flame   | The warrior-priest who rules Vuthráka and commands the armies in war                                          |
| zohira                | The intermediary spirits of fertility, storm, river, death and the hunt, servants of the Eternal Flame        |
