> The last hour of the climb is open ground, a long grass slope between birches with nowhere to hide on it, and you understand halfway up that you have been watched since you left the trees. The sun is down behind the passes. The sky over the high wood is the deep clear blue of a lake in shadow, and the first stars are out, though the light on the slope does not fade the way evening light should; the birch trunks hold a pale shine, as if the moon had already risen behind you. The air smells of crushed fern and cold water. Your boots are soaked with dew, your breath smokes, and the only sound is a stream you cannot see and the creak of the Áelendan escort's bow case as she walks ahead of you.
>
> At the top stands a court of fitted gray stone, its joints so close that moss has not found them, and it is older than the trees that have grown up around it. Three figures wait there without moving. They are taller than any of your party, dressed in cloth the color of dusk that does not stir in the wind, and one of them speaks to your escort in a voice so slow and level it sounds like a line of song. Your escort listens with her head bowed. Then she turns, a broad woman in a cloak of undyed wool with a bone-handled knife at her belt and gray braids bound in leather, and gives you the words exactly as she received them, pitch and pause and all. "_Luuro nuuva liseriho_." Then, in plain words: starlight sings on still water. "I am Kírnala, of the Dhárgandi—those of Dhárgan. I carry." She steps aside so that you stand facing them, and waits for you to answer.

The [[lore-flksinale|Sinalë]] are the eldest of the speaking peoples, the Firstborn, who came to Thalorna from some prior realm roughly eight thousand years ago, when the humans they found here lived in bands without cities or letters. They are tall, still and very long-lived, they build nothing, and what they make they sing. Fewer than one in ten thousand of the world's people is a Sinalo, and most humans never see one. They live in hidden enclaves in deep forests, mist-filled valleys and island sanctuaries, and a path that leads to an enclave uninvited has a way of leading somewhere else.

**The one thing to understand first: you do not deal with the Sinalë; you deal with the people they trust to carry.** The Sinalë do not treat with human powers. In [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] they will deal with the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]], and the Áelendan carry their words exactly, adding nothing, and never claim to speak for them. A warning, a refusal, the return of something taken: all of it passes through an Áelendan who was asked to carry it.

## What You Meet First

Élavendre holds three enclaves. [[place-ethalosse|Ethalossë]], at the eastern edge of the [[place-alndntrblnds|Áelendan Tribal Lands]], is the smallest, about seventy Sinalë, and the only one placed for access: it is where the Sinalë do business with the world, and the one enclave a human has a realistic hope of being invited to. [[place-aelirosse|Aelirossë]], deep in the [[place-silvrfrsts|Silver Forests]], is home to about a hundred and twenty, with dwellings worked into living silver birch, and its Áelendan neighbors come and go without ceremony. [[place-serenthale|Serenthalë]] faces the western sea in [[place-glanmor|Glanmor]], keeps to itself even from the Áelendan, and is guided to by nobody.

Elsewhere the welcome is thinner. On [[place-aelwyth|Aelwyth]] the Sinalë of the [[place-hallowwood|Hallowwood]] tolerate embassies from [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Dúnavarre]], welcome Élavendre and receive the Áelendan above all; they once held the island under a Sinalë king, together with the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazári]], until he was killed at the [[place-threesisters|Three Sisters]]. Enclaves are known in [[place-vrystwald|Vrystwald]] too. The court of [[affiliation-tanvurempr|Tānvür]] keeps a formal contact with its enclaves that exists nowhere else.

A Sinalo carries a given name and a lineage name, the name of the hearth that keeps it, and is called by the whole given name always; an epithet is added after it and never replaces it.

## What Gets a Stranger into Trouble

Do not ask about the Khazári. The two elder peoples parted after a Sinalë outlaw destroyed the Khazári city of [[lore-khazarturn|Khazártúrn]] in 2427 BF, and they have not spoken since; a scholar who presses the question meets a silence cold enough to end the conversation for good. Do not bring the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]]: it has tried to study Sinalëan arts and failed, and the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] who patrol the approaches turn its people back at the tree line. And do not mistake the Sinalë for the [[lore-faecourts|Fae Courts]]. The Sinalë keep ordinary ground in this world and speak of the deeper places beyond them as something they do not rule.

## Ways In

An Ordo chapterhouse in [[place-beravel|Béravel]] holds an object taken from a grove, and an Áelendan elder has been asked to carry its return. A warning comes down from Ethalossë about a disturbed site, and the party is the nearest help the elder can find. A bard of the [[affiliation-bardicolgs|Bardic Colleges]] wants to know why [[lore-seventowers|The Seven Towers]] breaks off where it does. In the deep interior of [[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Nordheim]], the destroyed enclave of [[place-haulonna|Haulonna]] still holds its people's spirits captive, and the Sinalë have not forgotten it.
