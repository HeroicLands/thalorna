> Dawn on the crossing is gray, and it stays gray. Fog lies on the water so thick that the ship's lantern makes a yellow room around the bow and nothing more; the deck is slick, the rigging drips, and the cold works through your cloak to the skin. Then the fog thins, and a brown moor rises out of it, heather and black peat cut in steps, and below it the sheltered anchorage of [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]]. Stone houses roofed in gray slate crowd the slope, peat smoke lies flat over the chimneys, and gulls scream over a fishing boat unloading herring in silver baskets. Nobody on the quay is in a hurry. A cooper hoops a barrel with slow taps of his mallet, two women in dun shawls mend a net between them, and a line of ships rides at anchor waiting its turn: Aldorathi cogs riding low under bales of fleece, and an Ondarethan trader with a red mantle painted on her stern.
>
> A boat brings the harbor officers out to you. The first aboard is a woman in a long coat of charcoal wool with a brass chain of office at her throat, followed by a clerk with a writing board and a young man carrying a tray lined with gray felt. She looks at the ship before she looks at anyone on it, then at you. "Welcome to [[place-aelwyth|Aelwyth]], friend, and well met," she says, quietly, in the formal way, and again in the court register of the [[skill-elvndrlng|Élavendri Language]] a ship from the mainland is owed: "_Shalor en kir a thel_," welcome, friend of the light. "I am [Ívelin of Ravenmoor]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, and I serve the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]]." Her clerk dips his pen. She nods at the felt tray. "Your lading may bide. Hath your ship aught aboard that ye cannot explain?"

Aelwyth is a large island of about seven hundred thousand people off the western coast of [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris]], a long and dangerous sail from the mainland. Four realms share its coasts, a third of it is old forest that no plow has touched, and fog is the ordinary weather of the west. The island runs its affairs with the entire seriousness of a continent and almost no news of one: its lords hold court as though their precedence mattered beyond the next valley, and most Aelwythans have no idea how small the island is.

**The one thing to understand first: every ship that crosses calls at Ravenmoor.** The Synod of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] mages that governs [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Dúnavarre]] requires every vessel between Aelwyth and the mainland to put in at Ravenmoor first, inbound and outbound. Its officers look for prohibited goods, and they record and often detain anything magical. The Synod meets every visitor before anyone else on the island does, and it is the only body on Aelwyth that knows how the island compares with the world.

## What You Meet First

The realms are [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Aldorath]], the feudal breadbasket of the interior, where four in five people are serfs on manorial estates; Dúnavarre, the smallest and the most powerful, where the Synod rules through stewards and the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]]; the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]] on the west coast, a senate of mantled senators built in imitation of a Vylarian republic few there have seen, whose offices are for sale; and [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Stormveld]] in the north-east, where Nordmen took the plain sixty years ago and work it with thralls. Every court keeps Arcane Order advisors, and every ruler knows the Synod can withdraw them.

An Aelwythan attends the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian]] temple and also leaves something at the stone, the crossroads and the lake, and of all his duties those observances are the last he neglects. A farmer may say something naive about the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Empire]] and then name exactly which crossroads not to stand at after dark. The coin is [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vylarian]]: the Solden, the Ardin and the Bit.

## Where to Go, and What to Fear

[[place-wraymouth|Wraymouth]] is Dúnavarre's working harbor and [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]] Aldorath's walled seat. [[place-sunnerslade|Sunnerslade]] ripens a wine in the lee of [[place-ironfells|the Ironfells]] that has no business existing. In the high valleys of the Ironfells the [[lore-vardain|Vardain]], free remnant of the conquered north-east, live under the protection of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] of [[place-vorgald|Vorgald]].

The island's reputation abroad is wrong about its people and right about its land. There are places where the fae are strong, deep in the forest and in the wild north, and the borders of the realms bend around them. [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] have lived in the empty third of the island for seven hundred years. The tribes of the [[place-pshtrwldrns|Peshtar Wilderness]] live outside every realm, and the forest peoples are the most warlike of them. [[place-hallowwood|The Hallowwood]] in the south is the forest of Aelwyth's [[lore-flksinale|Sinale]], and it does not receive visitors.

## Ways In

An Ondarethan merchant hires you to get a relic past the officers at Ravenmoor, and the Synod already knows it is aboard. A lord in Aldorath has lost his Arcane Order advisor, his harvest has failed, and he wants to know what he did. A Vardain family asks strangers to carry word to kin who are thralls on [[place-stormplain|the Stormplain]]. An Ondarethan fisherman who could never afford to buy a court turns to the local temple of the [[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]], whose priests serve the god who cannot be bought, and needs hands for what follows.
