This folder gathers the notes of the Shuqash, the Shell-Bead People, a people of [[place-northkiichchik|northern Ki'ich'chik]] outside the Red Stone Compact. Its members are the [[affiliation-shuqash|Shuqash]] affiliation, the [[place-shuqhalaqrgn|Bead Coast]] and the [[place-tulishlumuqrgn|Oak Valleys]], and the towns of [[place-kulaywil|Kulaywil]], [[place-shuqwil|Shuqwil]], [[place-hipalhalaq|Hipal Halaq]], [[place-tulishwil|Tulishwil]] and [[place-tikoqhipay|Tikoq Hipay]]. It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Ki'ich'chik.
