This folder gathers the notes of the Osetakona, the Maple People, a polity of [[place-northkiichchik|northern Ki'ich'chik]]. Its members are the [[affiliation-osetakona|Osetakona]] affiliation, the [[place-osetakonargn|Osetakona Region]] and the council village of [[place-ostakaten|Ostakaten]]. It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Ki'ich'chik.
