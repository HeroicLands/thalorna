This folder gathers the coins that circulate as cash: the gold, silver and lesser issues struck by the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]], the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] and the [[affiliation-janpdsvdyr|Janpodas of Vedyara]], and the counted money of Kì'ìch'chik, which strikes no coin: the cacao bean, the jade bead and the shell strand. It sits within the jewelry and cash shelf of the miscellaneous gear folders, so currency is kept apart from the general run of trade goods and equipment.
