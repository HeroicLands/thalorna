This folder gathers the Heroes and Knaves characters whose home is Harad, the Midhalion region of Ankaris. Its members are Haradi people such as Eshârum Tarûzî and Kasûra Damzarû, together with the occupational figures who belong to the same country. It sits within the Midhalion folder of the Ankaris characters.
