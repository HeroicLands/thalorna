This folder gathers the notes of the Kelwanuk, the Cedar People, a polity of [[place-northkiichchik|northern Ki'ich'chik]]. Its members are the [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Kelwanuk]] affiliation, the [[place-kelwanukrgn|Kelwanuk Region]] and the village of [[place-tsilkasis|Tsilkasis]]. It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Ki'ich'chik.
