This folder gathers the Nyáluba polities, a subfolder of the polities folder. Its entries are the [[affiliation-nylbtrblntn|Nyáluba Tribal Nation]], also called the Long Pact, the [[affiliation-ekunda|Ékunda]] and [[affiliation-fenjara|Fénjara]] tribes, and two works the Nyáluba keep: [[lore-swearbaobab|The Swearing Under the Baobab]] and [[lore-stonetongue|The Stone Tongue]].
