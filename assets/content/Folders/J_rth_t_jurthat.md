This folder gathers the notes of the Jürthāt polity in Ankaris. Its members are the Jürthāt Empire, also called the Nine Mountains and the Kingdom of Mountains and Seas, and the Jürthāt Region, also called the Jürthāti Archipelago. It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Ankaris.
