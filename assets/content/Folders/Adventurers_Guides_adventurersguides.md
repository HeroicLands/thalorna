This folder gathers the Adventurer's Guides, the introductory notes that open a region to travelers. Each guide offers one entrance to a land, such as the [[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu Region]] or the [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara Region]], and routes the reader onward to the places, peoples, affiliations and characters that belong to it.
