This folder gathers the deities of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani pantheon]]. Its members are the lore notes of the gods, among them [[lore-kanixhakandty|K'anish Ha'kan]], [[lore-kayikchalixdty|K'ayik Ch'alish]], [[lore-pikultzumaqdty|Pik'ul Ts'umaq]], [[lore-ixilanchalixdty|Ish'ilan Ch'alish]], [[lore-tzuqilixbaldty|Ts'uqil Ish'bal]] and [[lore-xilanixlandty|Shilan Ish'lan]]. It sits within the Deities folder of the setting lore.
