This folder gathers the deities of the Itzani pantheon. Its members are the lore notes of the gods, among them K'anix Ha'kan, K'ayik Ch'ul, Pik'ul Tz'umaq, Ix'ilan Ch'alix, Tz'uqil Ix'bal and Xilan Ix'lan. It sits within the Deities folder of the setting lore.
