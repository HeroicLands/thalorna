This folder gathers the deities of the Itsani pantheon. Its members are the lore notes of the gods, among them K'anish Ha'kan, K'ayik Ch'alish, Pik'ul Ts'umaq, Ish'ilan Ch'alish, Ts'uqil Ish'bal and Shilan Ish'lan. It sits within the Deities folder of the setting lore.
