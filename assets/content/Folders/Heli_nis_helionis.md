This folder gathers the notes of Heliónis, the polity of Hellád in Ankaris. It holds the city-states and their affiliations, such as Korinthea, Thyrenae, Pelagora, Kalydria and the City-State of Kostaros, along with the places that belong to them, among them the Kostaros District. It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Hellád.
