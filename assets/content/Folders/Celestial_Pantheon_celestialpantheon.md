This folder gathers the Celestial Pantheon of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Téngwou Rong Lei]] tradition. It divides into two sub-folders, one for the Elemental Lords and one for Youwéng, the infernal realm and its Demon Lords, and holds the affiliation notes belonging to those two groups.
