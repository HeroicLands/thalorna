This folder gathers the Celestial Pantheon of the Tëngvōk Vān Lëi tradition. It divides into two sub-folders, one for the Elemental Lords and one for Yokveng, the infernal realm and its Demon Lords, and holds the affiliation notes belonging to those two groups.
