This folder gathers the affiliations of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani pantheon]]. Its members are the pantheon itself, also called The Ten of the Celestial Round, and the faiths and cults of its gods, among them the [[affiliation-tzenultqan|Faith of Ts'en'ul Tq'an]], the [[affiliation-naliktzuqal|Faith of Nal'ik Ts'uqal]], the [[affiliation-xilanixlan|Faith of Shilan Ish'lan]], [[affiliation-pikob|Pik'ob]] and [[affiliation-pikultzumaq|Pik'ul Ts'umaq]]. It sits within the Pantheons folder of the affiliations.
