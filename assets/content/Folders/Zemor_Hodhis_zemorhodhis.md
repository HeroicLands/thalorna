This folder gathers the mystical skills of the [[affiliation-zemorhodhis|Zemor Hodhis]] tradition. Its members are the Incantation skills, among them [[skill-fisera|Incantation Fisera]], [[skill-hidalis|Incantation Hidalis]], [[skill-kentra|Incantation Kentra]], [[skill-pirethos|Incantation Pirethos]], [[skill-zefaris|Incantation Zefaris]] and [[skill-neumenos|Incantation Neumenos]]. It sits within the Mystical Skills folder of the Esoteric tradition.
