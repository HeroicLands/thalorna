This folder gathers the places of [[place-southkiichchik|Southern Kì'ìch'chik]] in the [[place-kiichchik|Kì'ìch'chik Continent]]. Its subfolders hold the journals of the three realms, [[affiliation-ixbaltzaku|Ish'bal Tsa'ku]], [[affiliation-kuxiixbal|K'ushi Ish'bal]] and [[affiliation-qapteltzakl|Q'aptel Tsa'kul]], with their towns, and the material of the [[place-xelemixbalrgn|Shelem Ish'bal Region]] wastes.
