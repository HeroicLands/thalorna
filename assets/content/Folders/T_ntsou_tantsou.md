This folder gathers the material of Tantsou, the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] in the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]]. It holds its places, its polity, its calendar and astrology, its caste laws and its histories, and contains the folders for the Castes, the Celestial Order and the Threats to the realm.
