This folder gathers the notes of the Khazryn Desert in Ankaris. Its members are:

- the Khazryn Desert Region, the Tellumi Confederation, the Tribes of Ātárzád and The Tellumi Calendar;
- the sub-regions: Tellumel, Dikraqor, Gwatekor, Hosikor, Idwakor and Welqator;
- the places of the oasis country: Ashkarad, Caldar, Kethramír, Mehrnāgord, the Oasis of Shirvan, Ushtra-bēr, Vahúrdash and Zargandûr, with Beit Shofar and Mount Shofar;
- the waypoints of the Celestial Road: Qìso, Roqid, Sowid Well and Yemok Well;
- the steppe tribes: the Azardan, Durmand, Ruweles, Sahri, Shirzari and Zarnush;
- the ruins: Wilud, Ewod, Qelod, Osikor, Ruweq, Velkun, the Northern Holds, the Ruins of Arkor, the Desert of Hek'ar, Gurdi and the Grazian Ruins;
- the blood-fields: Jilaq, Wemaq, Qìmod and Henhulpedin.

The creatures of the region are filed with the animals and spirits of the bestiary and are listed in the Khazryn Desert Region. The folder is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Ankaris.
