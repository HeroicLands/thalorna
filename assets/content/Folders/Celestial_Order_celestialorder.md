This folder gathers the lore of the Celestial Order of Tāntsou: its theology and cosmology, the Mandate of Heaven, ancestor worship, the Celestial Pantheon, the Five Elemental Lords and the Infernal Realms. It sits beneath the Tāntsou polity folder and holds the notes that describe how the Tāntsou understand the heavens and what lies beneath them.
