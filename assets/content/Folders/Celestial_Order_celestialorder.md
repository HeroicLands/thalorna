This folder gathers the lore of the [[lore-celestlrdr|Celestial Order]] of [[lore-tantsouclt|Tantsou]]: its theology and cosmology, the [[lore-mandatehvn|Mandate of Heaven]], [[lore-ancstrwrshp|Ancestor Worship]], the [[lore-clstlpnthn|Celestial Pantheon]], the [[lore-fvlmntlrds|Five Elemental Lords]] and the [[lore-infrnlrlms|Infernal Realms]]. It sits beneath the Tantsou polity folder and holds the notes that describe how the Tantsou understand the heavens and what lies beneath them.
