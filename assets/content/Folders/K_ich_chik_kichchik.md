This folder holds the notes of K'ich'chik, the continent that bears the name. It contains the [[place-kchchkcntnnt|K'ich'chik Continent]] note and the notes for the [[place-nrthrnkchchk|Northern K'ich'chik]], the [[place-cntrlkchchk|Central K'ich'chik]] and the [[place-sthrnkchchk|Southern K'ich'chik]]. It is divided into folders for its regions and polities, among them South, [[affiliation-neokbaate|Ne'ok Ba'ate]], [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]] and [[affiliation-ixtemtzul|Ishtem Tsul]], and it sits within the folder of the regions.
