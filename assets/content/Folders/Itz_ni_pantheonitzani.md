This folder gathers the affiliations of the Itzáni pantheon. Its members are the Itzáni Pantheon itself, also called The Ten of the Celestial Round, and the faiths and cults of its gods, among them the Faith of K'in'ul Tq'an, the Faith of Nal'ik Tz'uqal, the Faith of Xilan Ix'lan, Pik'ob and Pik'ul Tz'umaq. It sits within the Pantheons folder of the affiliations.
