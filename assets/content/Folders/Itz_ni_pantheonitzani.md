This folder gathers the affiliations of the Itsani pantheon. Its members are the Itsani Pantheon itself, also called The Ten of the Celestial Round, and the faiths and cults of its gods, among them the Faith of Ts'en'ul Tq'an, the Faith of Nal'ik Ts'uqal, the Faith of Shilan Ish'lan, Pik'ob and Pik'ul Ts'umaq. It sits within the Pantheons folder of the affiliations.
