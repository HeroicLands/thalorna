This folder gathers the material of [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm Region]], a frontier country of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]] where Grukar nests are thickest. It is a region and not a state: it has no ruler, no capital and no drawn border, the Grukar have no structure and do not cooperate outside their own nests, and the humans named the area because it is infested by them. It lies south of Norgaad, southeast of Nordheim and southwest of Malagna, as the easternmost part of Vristwald and the westernmost part of Velanthia. It sits within the Ankaris folder of the region tree and holds the notes that describe Grukarholm.
