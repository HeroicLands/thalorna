This folder gathers the Aelendan spirits among the Spirits of the lore. Its notes describe each spirit in turn, among them Aranwy of the Long Stair, Arthgen, Awelon, Cadwyr, Gwalchen, Nyfain, Rhysgen and Tanwen.
