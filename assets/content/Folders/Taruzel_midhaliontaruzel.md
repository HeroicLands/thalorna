This folder gathers the Heroes and Knaves characters whose home is Tarûzel, the Midhalion region of Ankaris. Its members are Tarûzeli people such as Zabûzum Jakûdûnî and Belgina Rusâkûnî, together with the occupational figures who belong to the same country. It sits within the Midhalion folder of the Ankaris characters.
