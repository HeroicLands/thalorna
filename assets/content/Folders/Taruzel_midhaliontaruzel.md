This folder gathers the Heroes and Knaves characters whose home is the [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]], within the [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión Region]] of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]]. Its members are Taruzeli people such as [[being-zbzmjkdn|Zabuzum Jakuduni]] and [[being-blgnrskn|Belgina Rusakuni]], together with the occupational figures who belong to the same country. It sits within the Midhalion folder of the Ankaris characters.
