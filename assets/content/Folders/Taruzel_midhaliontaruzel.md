This folder gathers the Heroes and Knaves characters whose home is Taruzel, the Midhalion region of Ankaris. Its members are Taruzeli people such as Zabuzum Jakuduni and Belgina Rusakuni, together with the occupational figures who belong to the same country. It sits within the Midhalion folder of the Ankaris characters.
