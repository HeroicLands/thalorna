This folder gathers the notes of the Tikupilarit, the Ice-Edge People, a people of [[place-northkiichchik|northern Ki'ich'chik]] outside the Red Stone Compact. Its members are the [[affiliation-tikupilarit|Tikupilarit]] affiliation, the [[place-tikumanikrgn|Ice Coast]] and the [[place-taluqnapiqrgn|Lake Tundra]], and the villages of [[place-kavrumiq|Kavrumiq]], [[place-napamiq|Napamiq]], [[place-imlakkagaq|Imlak Kagaq]], [[place-taqvamiq|Taqvamiq]] and [[place-simimanik|Simi Manik]]. It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Ki'ich'chik.
