This folder gathers the Arcana within the Khelathi lore folder. It holds the lore notes of the [[lore-khelunulekha|Khelunu Lekhau]] and of each of its arcane groups, among them [[lore-anlaghkhlth|Anlagh'Khelathu]], [[lore-athenuqa|Thelqen'Uqa]], [[lore-gezrulutu|Gezru'lutu]], [[lore-ithnetau|Ithnetáu]], [[lore-qeltnu|Qelt'nu]] and [[lore-rethzethu|Reth'zethu]].
