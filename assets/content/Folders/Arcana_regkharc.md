This folder gathers the Arcana within the Khelâthi lore folder. It holds the lore notes of the Khelunu Lekhau and of each of its arcane groups, among them Anlagh Khelathu, Athen Uqa, Gezrulutu, Ithnetau, Qeltnu and Rethzethu.
