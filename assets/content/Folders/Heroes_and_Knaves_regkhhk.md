This folder gathers the Heroes and Knaves characters of the Khelathi region, the ready-made people of [[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu]] and its neighbours. Its members include [[being-imhoqar2|Imhoqar]], [[being-ziprahuigmth|Ziprahu Igu'Maathu]], [[being-rethutrthllr|Rethute Reth'el'Leru]] and [[being-legiretilmyz|Legiret Ilmy-Za]]. It sits within the Characters folder of the Khelathi regions.
