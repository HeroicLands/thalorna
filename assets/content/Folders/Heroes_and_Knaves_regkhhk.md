This folder gathers the Heroes and Knaves characters of the Khelathi region, the ready-made people of Au'Khelathu and its neighbours. Its members include Imhoqar, Ziprahu Igu'Maathu, Rethute Reth'el'Leru and Legiret Ilmy-Za. It sits within the Characters folder of the Khelathi regions.
