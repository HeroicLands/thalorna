This folder gathers the Heroes and Knaves characters of the Khelâthi region, the ready-made people of Aû'Khelâthu and its neighbours. Its members include Imhoqar, Ziprahu Igu'Maâthu, Rethutê Reth'el'Lêru and Legiret Ilmy-Zâ. It sits within the Characters folder of the Khelâthi regions.
