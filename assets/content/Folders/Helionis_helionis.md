This folder gathers the notes of Helionis, the [[affiliation-ctysttshlns|City-States of Helionis]], in the [[place-heladrgn|Hellád Region]] of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]]. It holds the city-states and their affiliations, such as [[affiliation-korinthea|Korinthea]], [[affiliation-thyrenae|Thirenae]], [[affiliation-pelagora|Pelagora]], [[affiliation-kalydria|Kalidria]] and the City-State of [[affiliation-kostaros2|Kostaros]], along with the places that belong to them, among them the [[place-kostarosdst|Kostaros District]]. It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Hellád.
