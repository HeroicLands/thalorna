This folder gathers the Ashalan mystical skills within the Mystical Skills folder. Each note is the ritual of one Ashalan deity, among them [[lore-ahurdaendty|Ahurdaen]], [[lore-angarosdty|Angaros]], [[lore-aravestadty|Araveshta]], [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]], [[lore-azravandty|Azravan]], [[lore-bahramisdty|Báhramis]] and [[lore-khorsadradty|Khórsadra]].
