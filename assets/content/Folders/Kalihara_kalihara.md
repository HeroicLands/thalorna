This folder gathers the notes of Kalihara. Its members are the [[affiliation-kalihara|Kalihara]] affiliation, the [[place-kaliharargn|Kalihara Region]] and the places of the country, among them [[place-vashuran|Vashurán]], [[place-ilovanu|Ilovanu]], [[place-telvari|Telvari]] and [[place-nalurithan|Nalurithan]]. It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under the regions.
