This folder gathers the folk of the bestiary, the peoples that appear as creatures, filed beneath the Creatures folder. It holds the [[being-cvgbln|Cave Goblin]] and the [[being-frstgbln|Forest Goblin]], two goblin kinds, and the four castes of the Grukar, a separate species: [[being-grkrhshk|Grukar-Hushk]], [[being-grukaruk|Grukar-Uk]], [[being-grkrahk|Grukar-ahk]] and [[being-grkrsh|Grukar-Sha]].
