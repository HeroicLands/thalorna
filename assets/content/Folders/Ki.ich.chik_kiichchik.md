This folder holds the notes of Ki'ich'chik, the continent that bears the name. It contains the [[place-kiichchik|Ki'ich'chik Continent]] note and the notes for the [[place-northkiichchik|Northern Ki'ich'chik]], the [[place-centralkiichchik|Central Ki'ich'chik]] and the [[place-southkiichchik|Southern Ki'ich'chik]]. It is divided into folders for its regions and polities, among them South, [[affiliation-neokbaate|Ne'ok Ba'ate]], [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]] and [[affiliation-teyumi|Teyumi]], and it sits within the folder of the regions.
