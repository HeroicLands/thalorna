This folder gathers the lore of the setting. Its members include The Kindred, The Long Inheritance, Aralwen, The Truth of Divine Action and The Truth of Magic, along with the places and gear that the lore describes. It is divided into folders for Iltheron (the Star Circle), Totems, Spirits, the Khelunu Lekhau, Deities and the Zemor Hodhis, and sits within the Setting folder.
