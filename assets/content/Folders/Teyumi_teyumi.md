This folder gathers the notes of the Teyumi, the Turquoise People, a polity of [[place-northkiichchik|northern Ki'ich'chik]]. Its members are the [[affiliation-teyumi|Teyumi]] affiliation; the [[place-teyumirgn|Teyumi Region]] and its three countries, the [[place-hosaoyalargn|Canyon of Houses]], the [[place-wahaulahuvorgn|Cave Mesas]] and the [[place-kenuvasokorgn|Canal Plain]]; the towns of [[place-yaahosa|Yaahosa]], [[place-hepuhosa|Hepuhosa]], [[place-kenuvahosa|Kenuvahosa]], [[place-kwatuhosa|Kwatuhosa]], [[place-yaluhepu|Yaluhepu]] and [[place-teyupotama|Teyu Potama]]; and their faith, the [[lore-lumaponu|Rain Road]]. It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Ki'ich'chik.
