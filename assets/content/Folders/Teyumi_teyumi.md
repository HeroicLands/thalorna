This folder gathers the notes of the Teyumi, the Turquoise People, a polity of [[place-northkiichchik|northern Ki'ich'chik]]. Its members are the [[affiliation-teyumi|Teyumi]] affiliation, the [[place-teyumirgn|Teyumi Region]] and the great house of [[place-kwatuhosa|Kwatuhosa]]. It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Ki'ich'chik.
