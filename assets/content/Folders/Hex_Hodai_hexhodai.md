This folder gathers the mystical skills of the Héx Hodäi tradition. Its members are the Incantus skills, among them Incantus Physéra, Incantus Hydälis, Incantus Kentra, Incantus Pyréthos, Incantus Zephäris and Incantus Pneuménos. It sits within the Mystical Skills folder of the Esoteric tradition.
