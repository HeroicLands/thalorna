This folder gathers the settlements of Khelathi. Its entries are place notes for towns and cities of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]], among them [[place-garshebazet|Gar-Shebazet]], [[place-izet|Izet]], [[place-khelaga|Khelaga]] and [[place-garanlaghet|Gar-Anlaghet]].
