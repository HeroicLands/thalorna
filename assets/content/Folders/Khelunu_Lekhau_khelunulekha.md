This folder gathers the notes of the Khelunu Lekhau, the Eight Powers, which is the arcane tradition of the Khelâthi. Its members are the arcane affiliations of the tradition, such as the Gar Anlagh Khelâthu, Gar Athen Uqa and Gar Gezrulutu. It is filed with the affiliations, and its journals appear under the setting lore.
