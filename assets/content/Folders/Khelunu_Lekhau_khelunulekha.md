This folder gathers the notes of the [[lore-khelunulekha|Khelunu Lekhau]], the Eight Powers, which is the arcane tradition of the Khelathi. Its members are the arcane affiliations of the tradition, such as [[affiliation-garnlghkhlth|Gar-Anlagh'Khelathu]], [[affiliation-garathenuqa|Gar-Thelqen'Uqa]] and [[affiliation-gargezrulutu|Gar-Gezru'lutu]]. It is filed with the affiliations, and its journals appear under the setting lore.
