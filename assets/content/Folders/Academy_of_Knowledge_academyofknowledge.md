This folder gathers the notes for the Academy of Knowledge, the Vylarian institution of learning, within the Organizations folder. It holds the affiliation notes for the Academy's orders and bodies, among them the Ordo Arcanis and the Panepistemium, so that the whole institution is found in one place.
