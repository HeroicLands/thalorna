This Academy of Knowledge folder gathers the notes for [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]], the Vylarian institution of learning, within the Organizations folder. It holds the affiliation notes for the Academy's orders and bodies, among them the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] (the Arcane Order) and the Epistémion, so that the whole institution is found in one place.
