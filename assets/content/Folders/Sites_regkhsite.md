This folder gathers the sites of Khelathi. Its entries are place notes for landmarks and sacred places of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]], among them [[place-zuletqar|Zulet'Qar]], [[place-anlaghura|Anlaghura]] and [[place-lutzelutha|Lut-Zelutha]].
