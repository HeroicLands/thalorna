This folder gathers the mystical skills of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani]] tradition. Its members are the ritual skills, each named for a deity of the pantheon, such as [[skill-naliktzuqal|Ritual: Nal'ik Ts'uqal]], [[skill-xaqikhanal|Ritual: Shaq'ik Ha'nal]], [[skill-kanixhakan|Ritual: K'anish Ha'kan]] and [[skill-kayikchalix|Ritual: K'ayik Ch'alish]]. It sits within the Mystical Skills folder of the Esoteric tradition.
