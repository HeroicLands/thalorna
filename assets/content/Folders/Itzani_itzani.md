This folder gathers the mystical skills of the Itsani tradition. Its members are the ritual skills, each named for a deity of the pantheon, such as Ritual: Nal'ik Ts'uqal, Ritual: Shaq'ik Ha'nal, Ritual: K'anish Ha'kan and Ritual: K'ayik Ch'alish. It sits within the Mystical Skills folder of the Esoteric tradition.
