This folder gathers the mystical skills of the Itzani tradition. Its members are the ritual skills, each named for a deity of the pantheon, such as Ritual: Nal'ik Tz'uqal, Ritual: Xaq'ik Ha'nal, Ritual: K'anix Ha'kan and Ritual: K'ayik Ch'ul. It sits within the Mystical Skills folder of the Esoteric tradition.
