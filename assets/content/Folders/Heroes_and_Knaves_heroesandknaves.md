This is the top folder of the Heroes and Knaves characters, the ready-made people of the setting. It is divided by region into the [[place-klhrcntnnt|Kalihara Continent]], the [[place-isrncntnnt|Iseron Continent]], the [[place-arnscntnnt|Aurionis Continent]], the [[place-kchchkcntnnt|K'ich'chik Continent]], the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]] and [[place-xerathia|Zerathia]], and each of those holds the characters who live there.
