This folder gathers the deities of the setting, filed beneath the setting lore folder. It divides into one sub-folder for each pantheon or tradition, among them Asguardian, Tengvok Van Lei, Ashalan, Aurèldían, Nyaluba, Nkaruthar, Khelâthi, Itzani, Varnaka and the First Gods.
