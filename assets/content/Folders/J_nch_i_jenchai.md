This folder gathers the notes of the Jenchai polity in [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris]]. Its members are:

- the [[affiliation-jenchaiempr|Jenchai Empire]], also called the Nine Mountains and the Kingdom of Mountains and Seas, and the [[place-jenchairgn|Jenchai Region]], also called the Jenchai Archipelago;
- the eight domains: [[affiliation-shuiwojaidmn|Shuiwojai Domain]], [[affiliation-hojujaidmn|Hojujai Domain]], [[affiliation-wojaidmn|Wojai Domain]], [[affiliation-zenjaidmn|Zenjai Domain]], [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|Muwojai Domain]], [[affiliation-wejaidmn|Wejai Domain]], [[affiliation-rinjaidmn|Rinjai Domain]] and [[affiliation-tsuijaidmn|Tsuijai Domain]];
- the nine mountains: [[place-rojai|Rojai]], [[place-hojai|Hojai]], [[place-yujai|Yujai]], [[place-zenjai|Zenjai]], [[place-zijai|Zijai]], [[place-wejai|Wejai]], [[place-rinjai|Rinjai]], [[place-tsuijai|Tsuijai]] and [[place-zojai|Zojai]];
- the nine settlements: [[place-hawu|Hawu]], [[place-yanwu|Yanwu]], [[place-yuwu|Yuwu]], [[place-buiwu|Buiwu]], [[place-kowu|Kowu]], [[place-zuwu|Zuwu]], [[place-rinwu|Rinwu]], [[place-tsuiwu|Tsuiwu]] and [[place-lengwou|Lengwou]].

It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Ankaris.
