This folder gathers the notes of the Jénchài polity in Ankaris. Its members are the Jénchài Empire, also called the Nine Mountains and the Kingdom of Mountains and Seas, and the Jénchài Region, also called the Jénchài Archipelago. It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Ankaris.
