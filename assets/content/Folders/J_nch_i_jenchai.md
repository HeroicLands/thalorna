This folder gathers the notes of the Jénchài polity in Ankaris. Its members are:

- the [[affiliation-jenchaiempr|Jénchài Empire]], also called the Nine Mountains and the Kingdom of Mountains and Seas, and the [[place-jenchairgn|Jénchài Region]], also called the Jénchài Archipelago;
- the eight domains: [[affiliation-shuiwojaidmn|Shuiwōjai Domain]], [[affiliation-hojujaidmn|Hojūjai Domain]], [[affiliation-wojaidmn|Wōjai Domain]], [[affiliation-zenjaidmn|Zenjai Domain]], [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|Mūwōjai Domain]], [[affiliation-wejaidmn|Wējai Domain]], [[affiliation-rinjaidmn|Rīnjai Domain]] and [[affiliation-tsuijaidmn|Tsuijai Domain]];
- the nine mountains: [[place-rojai|Rōjai]], [[place-hojai|Hojai]], [[place-yujai|Yūjai]], [[place-zenjai|Zenjai]], [[place-zijai|Zījai]], [[place-wejai|Wējai]], [[place-rinjai|Rīnjai]], [[place-tsuijai|Tsuijai]] and [[place-zojai|Zojai]];
- the nine settlements: [[place-hawu|Hawū]], [[place-yanwu|Yanwū]], [[place-yuwu|Yūwū]], [[place-buiwu|Buiwū]], [[place-kowu|Kowū]], [[place-zuwu|Zūwū]], [[place-rinwu|Rīnwū]], [[place-tsuiwu|Tsuiwū]] and [[place-lengwou|Léngwou]].

It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Ankaris.
