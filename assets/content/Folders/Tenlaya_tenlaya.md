This folder gathers the notes of the Tenlaya, the Grass People, a polity of [[place-northkiichchik|northern Ki'ich'chik]]. Its members are the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Tenlaya]] affiliation; the [[place-tenlayargn|Tenlaya Region]] and its four countries, the [[place-hesunshewanrgn|Village River]], the [[place-wehioshtergn|Cairn Plain]], the [[place-watotenlargn|Long Grass]] and the [[place-potontayuntorgn|Root Fields]]; the villages and camps of [[place-tansheyawa|Tanshe Yawa]], [[place-kopenmokin|Kopen Mokin]], [[place-henushewan|Henu Shewan]], [[place-kuntontoshe|Kunton Toshe]] and [[place-hiyespito|Hiye Spito]]; and their faith, [[lore-nipanheshehin|Earth's Breath]]. It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Ki'ich'chik.
