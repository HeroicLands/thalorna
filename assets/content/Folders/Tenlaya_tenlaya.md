This folder gathers the notes of the Tenlaya, the Grass People, a polity of [[place-northkiichchik|northern Ki'ich'chik]]. Its members are the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Tenlaya]] affiliation, the [[place-tenlayargn|Tenlaya Region]] and the river village of [[place-tansheyawa|Tanshe Yawa]]. It is filed with the polities among the affiliations, and its journals appear under Ki'ich'chik.
