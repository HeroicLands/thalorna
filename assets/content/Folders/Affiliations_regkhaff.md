This folder gathers the affiliations of the Khelâthi within the Khelâthi region folders. It holds the notes for the Gar, the groups of the Khelunu Lekhau, among them Gar Anlagh Khelathu, Gar Athen Uqa, Gar Gezrulutu and Gar Ithnetau.
