This folder gathers the affiliations of the Khelathi within the Khelathi region folders. It holds the notes for the Gar, the groups of the Khelunu Lekhau, among them Gar Anlagh Khelathu, Gar Thelqen Uqa, Gar Gezrulutu and Gar Ithnetau.
