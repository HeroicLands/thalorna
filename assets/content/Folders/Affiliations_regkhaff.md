This folder gathers the affiliations of the Khelathi within the Khelathi region folders. It holds the notes for the Gar, the groups of the [[lore-khelunulekha|Khelunu Lekhau]], among them [[affiliation-garnlghkhlth|Gar-Anlagh'Khelathu]], [[affiliation-garathenuqa|Gar-Thelqen'Uqa]], [[affiliation-gargezrulutu|Gar-Gezru'lutu]] and [[affiliation-garithnetau|Gar-Ithnetáu]].
