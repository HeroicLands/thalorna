## Overview

**Vandgrund** (370 people) occupies the exacting valley floor in [[place-vandstein|Vandstein]]. A steep valley below the Vylarian passes gives just enough arable ground to feed its households. The Other Chief counts every sack that travels uphill and sets aside grain for the road’s winter closure. A family trading with pass guides has hidden part of its harvest rather than surrender it to the common store. The Shaman supports a public reckoning; the War Chief fears a harsh judgement will drive the guides away.

The Other Chief holds the strongest of the village's three seats, but the other two elders retain their own authority. The Shaman keeps the [[lore-goatttm|goat]] as the village’s single totem. The neighboring villages are reached by narrow forest tracks, used by messengers and small parties; no village commands the next one along the path.
