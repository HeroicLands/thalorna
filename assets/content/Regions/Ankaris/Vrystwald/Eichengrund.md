## Overview

Eichengrund is a woodland village within the [[place-vethwald|Deepwood]], home to two hundred and fifty people and the seat of [[being-athlwvthrnd|Athalwa Vithrúnd]], War Chief of the **Vithrúnd** clan and one of the few women in living **Vrystwald** memory to hold the seat by the warband's own acclamation rather than by a council's preference. Her hall is the largest building the village has, and a visitor who comes expecting the quiet woodland clearing the description promises finds instead a War Chief's seat with the traffic of envoys, petitioners and her own sworn warriors that a leader of her reach draws to it.

The forest around Eichengrund is worked rather than merely lived in: **Óthwin Dómhár** traps its game, **Thornak Blodtusc Bar** leads a hunting band with a deep bond to the boar, Eichengrund’s shared _wesk_, whose rites are kept by the village’s Weskár, and **Sundwíra Hildvith** keeps the stable that a War Chief's hall needs for her own mounts and her visitors' both. **Wulfrád Hildvith** stands among the sworn warriors who answer Athalwa's call, trained as much for siegecraft as for the open-field fighting most Varokh warbands expect to do.

Eichengrund's tension is the one that follows its War Chief rather than the village itself: Athalwa's seat is contested by kin who would rather see a man hold it, and a visitor who stays long enough hears both sides argued with equal conviction—by her sworn warband, who credit her visions and her victories, and by elders who have never fully forgiven the council that let her claim the hall at all.
