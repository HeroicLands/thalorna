## Overview

**Eichholt** (390 people) occupies the oak copse in [[place-edriwald|Edriwald]]. Oak trees crowd a bayward ridge above the landing, and the Shaman has marked the oldest as the deer’s shelter. The village cuts only fallen timber there. A young warband has begun taking live oak for boats after a raid burned its own hulls; the War Chief will not disarm men whose boats keep rival raiders away. Their quarrel is now over which trunks can be called fallen after an axe has touched them.

The Shaman holds the strongest of the village's three seats, but the other two elders retain their own authority. The Shaman keeps the [[lore-deerttm|deer]] as the village’s single totem. The neighboring villages are reached by narrow forest tracks, used by messengers and small parties; no village commands the next one along the path.
