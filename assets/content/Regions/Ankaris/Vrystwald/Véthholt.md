## Overview

**Véthholt** (270 people) occupies the hallowed copse in [[place-thornwald|Thornwald]]. An owl grove stands above a ford on the eastern frontier. The Shaman reads its night calls before anyone crosses, and her warnings have saved one warband from a Velanthian ambush. She now forbids the ford on nights when the owls are silent. Boat traders say silence is ordinary in winter; the Other Chief must choose between missed markets and a rite the village credits with keeping its people alive.

The Shaman holds the strongest of the village's three seats, but the other two elders retain their own authority. The Shaman keeps the [[lore-owlttm|owl]] as the village’s single totem. The neighboring villages are reached by narrow forest tracks, used by messengers and small parties; no village commands the next one along the path.
