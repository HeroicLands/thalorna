## Overview

**Grimak**, the fierce thicket, is a ring of burned stumps around a black hollow in a marsh of [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]], a day's walk east of the village of [[place-mahnryth|Mahnreth]]. Thorn has grown back over the stumps and the hollow stands full of black water, and no bird nests in any of it. A Grukar tribe woke a nest here about 532 AF and took three Varokh villages in a winter; in 536 the Skathwald villages came in over the ice and burned the nest with its spawner in it ([[lore-grimakburning|The Burning at Grimak]]). The muster's own dead were carried out and named. The people the Grukar had taken in the four winters before were never buried, because nothing of them was left, and nobody knows what received them.

## What Crosses

Grimak is a [[lore-bloodfield|blood-field]] of the silence. Nothing sings in the thicket: no birds, no frogs in the marsh in summer, no insects, and no sound but the weather and the water. It runs moderate across most of the ring and thin at the burned spawn-chamber in the hollow, where the predators gather.

What crosses there calls. A voice comes out of the thorn by name, a Varokh name, in a grandmother's tone or a child's, asking the listener to come and help. The Varokh read it as the forbidden question offered by something hungry: the dead do not ask the living for anything, and a voice that asks is not the dead. Every Shaman in Skathwald forbids answering it.

## How the Varokh Keep Their Distance

Mahnreth raises its lament at the empty mound, [[place-mahnhrom|Mahnhrum]], and never at the thicket. The grove council at [[place-radholt|Radholt]] sounds its alarm the moment anyone is missing near Grimak, and the whole village goes out with torches before dark, because a person who has answered the voice walks toward the hollow and does not stop. Nobody has ever tried to call the taken home. A Shaman can only call home the dead whose names are known and whose bodies were given a rite, and the taken had neither.

## Who Goes There

- **Young men proving themselves**, who walk the ring by night to say they did and do not always walk out.
- **A new Grukar tribe.** One is budding at the thicket's edge, and nobody in Skathwald can say whether the place draws it or whether it simply found unclaimed ground, as the first one did.

## Hooks

- A Mahnreth girl answers the voice and walks into the thicket. Her mother wants her brought back before dark, and the Shaman wants nobody else going in after her.
- A new nest is found inside the old ring of burned stumps. Skathwald must decide whether to muster again, and the elders who must agree on it are the grandchildren of the villages that argued last time.
