## Overview

**Wulfstein** (320 people) occupies the wolf crag in [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]]. This rocky lookout is named for wolves seen on its ledges, though its village keeps the lynx as its totem. The Shaman maintains small warning fires on the high stones. The War Chief has ordered them dark during Grukar incursions so the sentries can see movement below. A missing hunting party has made both arguments urgent: the fires might guide survivors home, or guide pursuers after them.

The Shaman holds the strongest of the village's three seats, but the other two elders retain their own authority. The Shaman keeps the [[lore-lynxttm|lynx]] as the village’s single totem. The neighboring villages are reached by narrow forest tracks, used by messengers and small parties; no village commands the next one along the path.
