The **Swift-Elk Camp** is one of the three seasonal camps of the [[affiliation-wldjgrrndgrskld|Wuljagar erund Garskald]] hunting company, and it stands along the [[place-moldunriver|Moldún River]]. The river crossings there make good ambush points for the migrating herds.

The other two camps are the [Summer Lodge]{.name data-type="place/site"} in the eastern meadows and the [Winter Hold]{.name data-type="place/site"} in the protected valleys of [[place-vethwald|Vethwald]] (the Deepwood). The Moldún is the southern bound of the [Garskald]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}'s hunting grounds, and a Vylarian logging operation has set itself up along it within sight of the clan's southern boundary markers.
