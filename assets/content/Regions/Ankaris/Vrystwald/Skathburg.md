## Overview

**Skathburg** (480 people) occupies the stronghold against harm in [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]]. A double timber ring encloses the last reliable spring before the Grukar nests. The War Chief drills a large defensive muster but wants every family to keep a watchman on the outer wall. The Other Chief says the demand leaves too few hands to repair houses and bring in stores. At four hundred and eighty residents, the village is nearing the point at which a daughter clearing would be customary; nobody agrees who could safely leave.

The War Chief holds the strongest of the village's three seats, but the other two elders retain their own authority. The Shaman keeps the [[lore-wolfttm|wolf]] as the village’s single totem. The neighboring villages are reached by narrow forest tracks, used by messengers and small parties; no village commands the next one along the path.
