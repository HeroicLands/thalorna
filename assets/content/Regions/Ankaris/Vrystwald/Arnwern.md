## Overview

**Arnwern**, the eagle lake, was a village of the [[place-falkhaven|Falkhaven]] shore behind a lake where the eagles fish. The [[affiliation-sonsmuspell|Sons of Eldheim]] came up from the shore at night, killed or took its people and burned its Shaman before the eagle stone at the lake's edge, and it was the first village they burned and the one they burned most thoroughly.

What remains is a burned clearing on the lake, which the Sons have begun to fortify with the village's own timber. Varokh scavengers go in by night for what the fire left, and the Sons' patrols wait for them. The eagle stone still stands, blackened. No Shaman has called Arnwern's dead home, because Arnwern's Shaman was the one who would have done it. [[lore-westernshore|The Sons on the Western Shore]] tells the campaign.
