**Theódhaven**, the folk's haven, is a stone landing with rock-cut shrines on the bay of [[place-edriwald|Edriwald]]. The [[lore-pelwarpepl|Pelwar]] who would neither fight nor marry the Varokh went west along the bay shore and gathered here, and crossed in whatever would float; [[lore-takingwood|the Taking of the Wood]] tells their leaving.

No Varokh village uses the landing. Varokh boat crews cut across it, and the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] turn them back.
