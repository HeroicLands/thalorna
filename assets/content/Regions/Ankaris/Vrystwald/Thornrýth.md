## Overview

**Thornrýth** (360 people) occupies the thorn clearing in [[place-thornwald|Thornwald]]. A cut through thorn scrub opens toward the Velanthian grain fields. The War Chief has made the gate broad enough for the neighboring prince’s wagons and keeps armed escorts ready for them. The women who bargain at the gate say his escorts have begun deciding prices as well as safety. The Other Chief holds the customary right to speak for the village in trade and now refuses to witness bargains struck under arms.

The War Chief holds the strongest of the village's three seats, but the other two elders retain their own authority. The Shaman keeps the [[lore-foxttm|fox]] as the village’s single totem. The neighboring villages are reached by narrow forest tracks, used by messengers and small parties; no village commands the next one along the path.
