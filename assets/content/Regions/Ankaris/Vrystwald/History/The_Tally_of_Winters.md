Ask a [[lore-varokhiclt|Varokh]] how old a feud is and the answer is a count of winters from something everyone remembers: "the fortieth winter since the posts burned", "the ninth winter since the rivers opened". The **Tally of Winters** is that count, and the reciters keep it. Nothing in [[place-vrystwald|Vrystwald]] is written down, so a year is a winter that somebody can name, and an age is the stretch of winters counted from one remembered thing before the next one starts the count again. The present winter is the thirteenth since the settlers planted their posts at [[place-hrindstead|Hrindstead]], written 13 PH.

## How the Forest Counts

A Varokh dates an event three ways, and a reciter at Weskskald uses all three.

- **By the winter's name.** Every village names a winter for what happened in it: the winter the whale came ashore at [[place-sundhaven|Sundhaven]], the winter the ice took the ford, the winter of the Waldburg killing. A village's own winters are what its old people argue about.
- **By the count since.** When something happens that every hearth in the forest remembers, the reciters begin the count again from it, and a winter is "the twentieth since". That count is the Tally, and the seven stretches it divides into are the ages below.
- **By the _eldskorn_ (ancestor tally).** At Weskskald a kindred recites its dead name by name, and an event is placed by how far back in the tally it falls: "it was in Hródmund's time, eleven names back." At about thirty winters to a name, the deeds a tally remembers reach some three centuries; beyond that the names run on bare, the ones who are the forest now.

The three do not always agree, and the reciters do not pretend they do. A named winter belongs to one village, the count since belongs to the forest, and the _eldskorn_ belongs to a kindred, and a dispute over ground is usually a dispute over which of the three to believe.

## The Year

The year turns four times, and each turn is also the season that follows it. The Varokh reckon no months finer than that, because the forest's work does not divide finer: the rivers decide where people can go, and the rivers change four times a year. The year opens at the thaw, on the morning the [[lore-commoncal|Vylarian Calendar]] calls 1 Floralis, and runs the world's three hundred and sixty-five days.

| Season | Days | What it holds                                                                                                           |
| ------ | ---: | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lúdra  |   91 | the thaw: the rivers break and run high, the war-bands go out, and the first boats go down to the landings              |
| Lóvra  |   91 | the leaf: the raiding and trading season, Weskmund at its end, and the markets at Waldburg                              |
| Dróva  |   91 | the turning: the harvest, the slaughter, the last boats home and the dead carried in before the ground hardens          |
| Grema  |   92 | deep winter: the frozen rivers become roads, the trap-lines are walked, and the kindreds recite their dead at Weskskald |

A date is given as the day of the season and the winter of the age: the elders broke the talisman at Waldburg in the first days of Lúdra, 173 LR.

### Before the Kindreds (BK)

The forest of the tellings: the _dorg_ (giants) who walked it before the clans, the stone circles beyond [[place-blackthornrdg|Blackthorn Ridge]] carved with marks that are neither Varokh nor Vylarian, and the paths in the deep wood that have led somewhere else since the Sinalë came. The [[lore-pelwarpepl|Pelwar]] lived in the clearings then, without walls. Nothing here is counted, and the reciters disagree about whether it happened to the Varokh at all or to somebody they replaced.

### The Taking of the Wood (TW), from about 1250 BF

The mixed host the [[lore-varkhadcnq|Varkhad conquest]] made out of the eastern grasslands came into the forest from the east, broke the Pelwar who stood and married the ones who stayed. The rest went west along the bay toward the sea and [[place-aelwyth|Aelwyth]], and the forest's oldest mounds hold both stocks side by side. The tellings keep two accounts of this age at once, one in which the people came out of the east and one in which they came out of the oaks. [[lore-takingwood|The Taking of the Wood]] tells it.

### The Mound Centuries (MC), from about 1100 BF

Twelve centuries of villages, palisades, totems and mounds, and the longest age by far. The three seats took their shape in it, and so did the custom of splitting a village at five hundred, which the tellings give to [[place-vrystengrund|Vrystengrund]], a Deepwood village whose elders were offered a chief over them and walked out instead. Villages pushing into the eastern forest found the [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] already there, sent parties to treat with them that never came back, and named that country [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]], the harm wood. The Nordmal kingdoms rose to the north, and Vylaria was a rumor of the south.

### The Legions' Road (LR), from about 120 AF

The [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Empire]] at its height cut a road north out of [[place-moktur|Moktur]] through [[place-vandstein|Vandstein]], raised forward posts along it and bought the War Chiefs nearest the road. In 291 a legion marched up it to burn [[place-waldburg|Waldburg]], and the first common muster anyone can name took it at the Moldún ford; the place is [[place-skathhreth|Skathhreth]] now, and nobody crosses it in the thaw. The War Chief who won would not give the command back, and the elders broke the talisman so that nobody ever could. [[lore-legionsroad|The Legions' Road]] and [[lore-skathhrethlss|The Loss of the Legion at Skathhreth]] tell this age.

### The Southern Raiding (SR), from about 340 AF

The Empire gave up its posts, and the Varokh burned what would burn. With nothing between the forest and Moktur's valleys but the mountains, raiding south became a generation's habit and then a way of life. The famine of the Three Winters sent the war-bands over the passes in numbers Moktur had never met, and a host of them died in the Black Marsh with a legion's eagle among them ([[lore-hungerraids|The Hunger Raids]]). In the emptied east a Grukar tribe woke inside Varokh country and took three villages in a winter, and Skathwald burned the nest four winters later at [[place-grimak|Grimak]] ([[lore-grimakburning|The Burning at Grimak]]). On the Velanthian frontier a prince's shared fort failed when he asked for tribute ([[lore-princesfort|The Prince's Fort]]).

### The Thrall Rivers (TR), from 660 AF

The Nordmen took Stormveld and needed thralls by the tens of thousands, and the reciters began the count again from the summer the first thrall-boats went down the rivers full. Waldburg grew past every limit, the women who run the landings grew rich, and a killing at the crowded market gave the market peace its present form. A Nordmal company of six hundred came over the Falkenstein frontier in 692 and did not go home ([[lore-thrallrivers|The Thrall Rivers]], [[lore-stormhandfld|The Stormhand's Field]]).

### The Posts at Hrindstead (PH), from 708 AF

The present age. Nordheim settlers planted their hall-posts in the Eichthúl grove at Hrindstead and the Eichthúl burned the first stockade ([[lore-nalthmrktkng|The Nalthmark Land-Taking]]). The Empire has drawn back to its core and Moktur's governors act on their own; Vylarian loggers fell sacred trees on the Moldún upstream of Skathhreth; and the Sons of Eldheim have come ashore on the western bay and burned a village ([[lore-westernshore|The Sons on the Western Shore]]). The reciters expect the next thing every hearth remembers to start a new count, and they cannot say whether it will be a muster.

## Carrying a Winter to the Vylarian Calendar

Each age's count is a fixed offset from a Vylarian year, and a day of the season is the same day of the Vylarian year, so only the year changes.

- **The Posts at Hrindstead:** add 707 for the year AF. 13 PH is 720 AF.
- **The Thrall Rivers:** add 659. 1 TR is 660 AF.
- **The Southern Raiding:** add 339. 176 SR is 515 AF.
- **The Legions' Road:** add 119. 172 LR is 291 AF.
- **The Mound Centuries:** the first 1,100 winters fall before the Founding, so subtract the winter from 1,101 for the year BF; from 1,101 MC, subtract 1,100 for the year AF.
- **The Taking of the Wood:** subtract the winter from 1,251 for the year BF. 1 TW is 1250 BF.

No reciter counts a Mound Century winter, and nobody in the forest has ever needed to. The early ages are counted here so that a Varokh account and a Vylarian record can be set side by side.
