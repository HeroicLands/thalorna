## Overview

**Hildholt** (250 people) occupies the battle copse in [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]]. A ring of short trees hides a refuge beside a narrow stream. The Other Chief has promised neighboring villages room inside its palisade during a Grukar alarm, though there are stores for only two hundred and fifty residents. The War Chief wants to close the gate after the first warning horn. Families who sheltered here last winter remember the promise and have begun bringing food in advance, making the refuge a test of whose word binds the village.

The Other Chief holds the strongest of the village's three seats, but the other two elders retain their own authority. The Shaman keeps the [[lore-crowttm|crow]] as the village’s single totem. The neighboring villages are reached by narrow forest tracks, used by messengers and small parties; no village commands the next one along the path.
