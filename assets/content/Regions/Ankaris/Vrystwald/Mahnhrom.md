**Mahnhrum**, the kinsmen's mound, stands beside [[place-mahnryth|Mahnreth]] in [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]]. It was raised for the people the [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar Folk]] took in the winters of the Waking, of whom nothing was left to bury, so it holds grave goods and no dead. Mahnreth raises its lament here at the seasonal turning, and every Skathwald child is taken to it once and told why the village sings at the mound and never at the thicket a day's walk east.

A Shaman can call home only the dead whose names are known and whose bodies were given a rite, and the taken had neither, which is why the mound stays empty. A Grukar raid that takes another of the village's people opens the old argument at once: whether to open the mound for the new name, or raise another.
