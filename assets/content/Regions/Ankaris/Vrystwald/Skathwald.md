## Where the Grukar Thicken

Skathwald lies along the part of Vrystwald's arc where [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] nests become more common toward [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm]]. No line separates the two: Grukarholm names a concentration, and a tribe can bud into forest far from it. A village here watches its paths even when it has seen no raiders for a season.

Grukar parties come in small numbers and repeatedly. No ahk can command all the others into one campaign, and no Varokh victory can end the incursions. There is no trade, ransom or envoy to make the danger predictable. Villages hold fewer goods and spend more labor on watches and defenses; a traveler who follows a familiar river toward Grukar country cannot assume its next landing is still occupied.

The concentration gives this reach its character, but it grants no common command to the Varokh villages. Their elders may join only for a danger they cannot meet alone, and that command ends with the response.
