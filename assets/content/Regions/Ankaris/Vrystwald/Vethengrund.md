## Overview

Véthengrund is a hidden valley floor in the forests of [[place-vrystwald|Vrystwald]], and its name sets it apart: _véth-_, hallowed, joined by _-en-_ to _-grund_, bottom land. It holds **the Sacred Pools of Véthengrund**, a series of ancient springs the Varokh believe are the dwelling places of water spirits, and those spirits are said to hold knowledge of the natural world and the secrets of immortality.

## The Pools and Their Guardians

The legends promise much: bathing in the waters of Véthengrund is said to cleanse the soul, grant visions of the future, and give a warrior deep wisdom. Nature spirits guard the pools, and nobody reaches the water by walking up to it. A pilgrim must first perform a long series of rituals, offerings and trials—purification rites, the riddles the water spirits pose, and the ground itself around the pools—and may find rivals there who want the same blessing. Those who prove themselves are said to come away with foresight or with waters that heal.

A Varokh hunter close to his clan's spirits is the kind of pilgrim the pools draw. [[being-thrnkbldtscbr|Thornak Blodtusc Bar]], whose bond is with the boar, could undertake the pilgrimage to seek a deeper understanding of the spirits.
