## Overview

Thornhaven sits where the forest thins toward the [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthian]] grain-belt, on the eastern stretch of [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]] that meets the [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia Region]], within reach of the [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm Region]] enclave, close enough to that frontier that the three hundred people who live here look as much like Velanthian river-folk as they do like their kin deeper in the forest, a mixing every village along this stretch of the frontier shares after generations of intermarriage and shared landings. Thornhaven's Shaman keeps the fox as the village's totem, an animal that survives contested ground by reading both sides of it rather than committing fully to either, which is as near as the village comes to naming what frontier life has made of it. [[being-erminfrdbn|Ermina yra Frodbán wur Thornhaven]] fishes the lake the village sits on, and [[being-thldrdrthgr|Thaldrá yra Druthgar wur Thornhaven]] crews the river-boats that carry Thornhaven's goods toward the same markets its neighbors across the frontier use.

A War Chief's standing at Thornhaven rests on managing that frontier rather than on war alone—a departure from every other village in Vristwald, where it is the Other Chief's seat that treats with outsiders. Here the two have folded into one man's hands, because a Velanthian river-prince's agent does not wait for a council to convene before he wants an answer, and the War Chief is the seat that is already armed and awake. The habit began when a Thornhaven Other Chief refused to witness a prince's bargain at the shared fort about a century ago, and the princes' agents stopped asking her seat for anything ([[lore-princesfort|The Prince's Fort]]). The cost falls on the Other Chief: the seat that elsewhere speaks for the village to the world has, at Thornhaven, almost nothing left to say that the War Chief has not already said first, and the elders who hold it are left arguing custom and kinship debts while the frontier's real business is settled around them. [[being-wlfhrthdwld|Dorghár yra Theodwald wur Thornhaven]] serves the War Chief as his huscarl, a sworn man kept close in a village where a Velanthian river-prince's agents and a [[lore-varokhiclt|Varokh]] war-band's scouts are equally likely to be the next visitor through the gate. [[being-vndrcgrskld|Vandarik yra Garskald wur Thornhaven]] keeps the stable that both kinds of visitor need serviced before they move on.

Thornhaven's tension runs along the same frontier that built it: a village this mixed is useful to the Velanthian princes as an auxiliary and trusted by them as nothing more, and the village's own elders know it, which is why Thornhaven's loyalty to the wider [[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vristwald Tribes]] is asserted more often and more loudly here than in a village that has never had reason to doubt it—and it is the Other Chief who does the asserting loudest, since a seat with less left to decide has more left to prove.
