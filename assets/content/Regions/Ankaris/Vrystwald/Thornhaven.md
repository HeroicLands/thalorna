## Overview

Thornhaven sits where the southern forest thins toward the [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthian]] grain-belt, close enough to that frontier that the three hundred people who live here look as much like Velanthian river-folk as they do like their northern kin, a mixing the whole southern **Vrystwald** frontier shares after generations of intermarriage and shared landings. **Ermína Fródbán** fishes the lake the village sits on, and **Thaldrá Druthgar** crews the river-boats that carry Thornhaven's goods toward the same southern markets its neighbors across the frontier use.

A War Chief's standing at Thornhaven rests on managing that frontier rather than on war alone, and **Wulfhár Theódwald** serves as his huscarl, a sworn man kept close in a village where a Velanthian river-prince's agents and a Varokh war-band's scouts are equally likely to be the next visitor through the gate. **Vandaric Garskald** keeps the stable that both kinds of visitor need serviced before they move on.

Thornhaven's tension runs along the same frontier that built it: a village this mixed is useful to the Velanthian princes as an auxiliary and trusted by them as nothing more, and the village's own elders know it, which is why Thornhaven's loyalty to the wider Vrystwald tribes is asserted more often and more loudly here than in a village that has never had reason to doubt it.
