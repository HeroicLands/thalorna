## The Western Shore

Falkhaven is the western end of Vrystwald's arc, south of Nordheim and against the ocean. The Nordmal kingdoms are close enough for trade to matter more here than anywhere else in the Varokh forest. Furs, amber and captives reach buyers by water; goods and travelers from the western ports enter by the same landings. A boat's arrival is a village matter, settled by the women who keep its stores and accounts, rather than by an authority over the coast.

[[place-falkensten|Falkenstein]] stands at the western forest's edge and guards an easier crossing into the Nordlands. Its contested hill explains the other face of this contact: a market season can follow a raid, and neither erases the memory of the other. Inland, the taiga grows thinly inhabited and difficult to cross. Along the shore and the rivers, people and news move more readily than they do through the trees.
