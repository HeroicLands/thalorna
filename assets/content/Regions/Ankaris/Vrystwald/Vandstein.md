## The Eastern Mountains

Vandstein is the far eastern end of Vrystwald's arc, against the southwestern side of the mountains facing [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylaria]]. These heights belong to the same diagonal belt that begins by Elavendre, not to a second range. The few crossings make travel and fur trade possible, but neither a pass nor an old road gives an outside ruler authority over the forest villages below it.

Overgrown Vylarian posts survive in the eastern woods, traces of an earlier imperial reach. A ruin can offer shelter or buried goods to a party that finds it, and can also draw an imperial claimant into ground a nearby village considers its own. Conflict here is intermittent, carried by travelers and claims through difficult passes rather than by a continuous line of forts.
