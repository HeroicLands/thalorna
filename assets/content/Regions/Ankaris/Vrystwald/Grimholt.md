## Overview

Grimholt sits at a river landing good enough to have grown past what a **Vrystwald** clearing usually holds, and four hundred and fifty people live by exactly that: furs coming down from the northern taiga and grain-belt goods coming up from the [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthian]] frontier both change hands at Grimholt's wharves, and the village has fortified itself the way any landing worth raiding eventually has to. It remains, by the only measure that matters here, a village rather than a town—nothing about how it is governed changes with its size, and the same council of elders that would run a clearing of fifty runs this one of four hundred and fifty.

The women who work the landing set the prices and keep the reckonings, as they do at every Varokh trade site, and a foreign merchant who arrives expecting to bargain with a chieftain learns otherwise before his first trade closes. **Waldaric Ríkhild** sews the tent-cloth the river-traders carry, and the village's taverns and lodging-houses do the business a landing this size generates: **Theódris Sundrýth** begs at the wharves where a successful season leaves traders generous, and **Rádrún Vithrúnd** works a trade of her own among the crews who winter over waiting for the thaw.

Grimholt's one standing argument is with the tribes further up the same river, who hold that a landing fortified this well has started acting like something more than a village even if its council insists otherwise—a complaint the moot has heard and never once resolved, because resolving it would mean admitting a Varokh settlement can become a town.
