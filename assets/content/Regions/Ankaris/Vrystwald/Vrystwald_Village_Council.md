The **Vrystwald Village Council** is the form of government every Varokh village of the [[place-vrystwald|Vrystwald]] shares and none shares with another. Three seats govern a village: the Shaman, the War Chief and the Other Chief. The Shaman holds the strongest, and the other two elders keep their own authority. Each village has its own council, and no village commands the next one along the forest tracks. The wider web of independent villages is [[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vrystwald Tribes]].
