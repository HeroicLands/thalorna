## Overview

**Mahnrýth** (240 people) is a Varokh village of [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]], and its name means the clearing of the kinsmen. The survivors of three villages that a Grukar tribe took in one winter walked west under Mahnilda yra Erthrúnd wur Mahnrýth, a household mistress of one of them, and cut the clearing a day's walk from the nearest village; [[lore-grimakburning|The Burning at Grimak]] tells the whole of it. The Other Chief holds its leading seat, and the village keeps the [[lore-rabbitttm|rabbit]] as its totem.

The village sings its lament at the turning of the seasons beside [[place-mahnhrom|Mahnhróm]], the empty mound it raised for the people the [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] took, and it does not sing at [[place-grimak|Grimak]], the thicket a day's walk east. Its old people can still say which of the three lost villages their kindred came from.
