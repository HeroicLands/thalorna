## Overview

**Arnstein** (340 people) occupies the eagle crag in [[place-falkhaven|Falkhaven]]. A bare stone above the western forest holds an eagle nest and a sightline across the Nordmal frontier. The Shaman forbids quarrying the crag and has stopped a family from cutting a winter shelter into it. When the last storm took their roof, the Other Chief offered timber from his own stores; accepting it would concede that a household debt can answer a sacred prohibition.

The Shaman holds the strongest of the village's three seats, but the other two elders retain their own authority. The Shaman keeps the [[lore-eaglettm|eagle]] as the village’s single totem. The neighboring villages are reached by narrow forest tracks, used by messengers and small parties; no village commands the next one along the path.
