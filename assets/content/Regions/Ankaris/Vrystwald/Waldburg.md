## Overview

Waldburg carries the [[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vrystwald Tribes]]' seat on its own name, and that is nearly the whole of what the word means: it is the village where the common council meets, where a [[lore-chieftainrnk|Chieftain]] of enough standing calls the seasonal moot, and where the **Sundered Talisman**'s fragments are laid together on the rare occasion every tribe agrees one crisis is worth a single War-chief—and nothing more than that, because a village is still what it is once the council disperses. Eight hundred people live behind its timber palisade, the largest number gathered in one place anywhere in **Vrystwald**, drawn here the way every larger Varokh settlement is drawn: a river crossing good enough that furs, amber and captives taken on three frontiers all pass through it on the way to somewhere else.

The Truce-Warden who holds the peace at Waldburg's seasonal market answers to no crown and commands no army, only the custom that a killing at the market is answered by every tribe present rather than by the dead man's kin alone, which is the one law strong enough to let rival war-bands trade face to face. **Vithar Hildvith** forges the weapons a Varokh warrior trains his whole working life to use, **Witharic Druthgar** builds the river-boats that carry furs and captives both down to the Nordmen buyers, and **Vethar Balthhelm**'s hall houses whatever chieftain or envoy the season's business has brought to the village.

Waldburg has no treasury, no standing garrison and no wall around anything but the village itself, and that absence is not a weakness anyone here has had reason to correct: there is nothing at Waldburg worth an army's season to take, which is exactly what has kept it standing while armies that tried to take something else never left the forest again.
