## Overview {#overview}

**Tanv-durákh** (_tahnv-doo-RAHKH_), "the holding of the gate", is the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazárian]] stronghold at the western end of the north-western mountain line of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], where the line turns to face the passes toward the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]]. It stands inside [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]], toward the end of the line nearest the gate on the western pass, where [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] comes through the wall. The imperial registers enter it as the Western Stronghold. About nine hundred Khazari belong to it, counted with those of its people who work in the imperial cities and those away on the wall, and about three thousand [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] farm the terraces of its valleys.

From the valleys the stronghold is a long gray face above the terraces, ledged and stained, with dark hollows that are windows and a haze along the face on still mornings where its flues let out the smoke.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The stronghold belongs to the Khazari of [[affiliation-tanvdurakhhld|Tanv-durákh Hold]], who govern themselves there under the empire's recognition of the Elder lands; [[lore-elderraces|Elder Races]] gives the arrangement. The empire leaves the stronghold alone on condition that its people help defend the empire in war.

The terrace villages are entered on the empire's registers as Nouwen and live under the stronghold's protection. They send grain, meat, milk, cheese, hides and wool up to its gate, and the stronghold sends down tools and iron, keeps their water-works and terraces, and keeps the approaches. In a hungry year it opens its caches to them, and in a war its refuge. The villagers know the gate closely and have never been inside.

## Layers {#history}

The cliff held its caves before the Khazari found them, and the hold counts its years from that finding. No imperial record says when it was. The Senali and the Khazari were among the first builders of the wall, before the empire's dated record opens, and the stronghold stands inside it. The walls of the gate on the western pass are Khazari stone.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The stronghold's people keep the wall's gates by turns, with the people of [[place-garnguraz|Garn-guráz]], and the gate on the western pass is the one nearest them. That gate is under strain. The tribes of [[place-idwkrrgn|Idvaqor]] sell the Celestial Road's last stage under the eye of Tantsou, and the oasis-holders raise their tolls when the frontier garrisons are thin and shelter smugglers running silk out without the bureaus' seal and sorcerers fleeing the [[lore-wanduo|Wandwo]] compounds. A sorcerer who gets past the wall is one the empire cannot recover.

At home, the stronghold sends its turns north as well as to the gates, and every turn away from the gates is one the oasis-holders count.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

- **The passes.** Beyond the wall the high deserts of the Khazrin begin, and smugglers and fugitives use the passes the caravans do not.
- **The Chaizho** are confined to the broken country at the other end of the line.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

Seen from the valley track, the stronghold is cliff, and a party does not know it for a city unless someone tells it. With papers a party can climb to the terrace villages, which are well kept and well supplied with iron, and to the foot of the cliff, where a stranger is turned away or ignored. A party on imperial business is heard at the gate in the rock and answered there.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The escort.** Tantsou sends riders a week out from the pass to escort caravans over the last stretch, and when the garrison is thin the escort is short. A caravan master waiting at the gate wants hands to ride out with it.
- **The silk runners.** Smugglers running silk out without the bureaus' seal shelter with the oasis-holders beyond the pass, and the gate garrison's Wohou commander wants a party that can pass for caravan hands to find out which oasis.

# The Truth of Tanv-durákh {#truth .secret}

**The hold.** Behind the face, passages run back into the mountain along its joints and faults to the council-hall, the forges, the archive, the tombs and the refuge, sealed and provisioned for the whole hold and its villages.

**The sigils.** The Khazari of Tanv-durákh who serve their turn in the north keep the sigils carved in the wall's northern stretch with the Khazari of Garn-guráz, and the sigils hold.
