## Overview {#overview}

**Eikkasto**, "the place of the yew" in the [[skill-senalilng|Senali language]], is a [[lore-flksenali|Senalian]] enclave of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], an old forest of yew and other dark trees toward the western end of the north-western mountain line, below the stronghold of [[place-tanvdurakh|Tanv-durákh]] and inside [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]]. The imperial registers enter it as the Yew Wood. About a hundred and fifty Senali live there.

The court knows the wood for its sages. Senalian scholars of ancient lore, the stars and the natural world come out of it to advise the human elite, classed [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]], and their counsel is valued and mistrusted in equal measure.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The forest belongs to the Senali of the [[affiliation-nclvkkst|Enclave of Eikkasto]], who govern themselves there, magic included, under the empire's recognition of the Elder lands; [[lore-elderraces|Elder Races]] gives the arrangement. The empire leaves the wood to them on the understanding that they stay loyal.

## Layers {#history}

The Senali raised their enclaves as the Elder peoples raised the wall, and no imperial record says when Eikkasto was raised. The Senali and the Khazari were among the first builders of the wall, before the empire's dated record opens, and the forest lies inside it.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The empire's chroniclers hold that every great work of the Elder peoples was made before the Mandate of Heaven and none after it, and read the fact as a transfer of office from the Elder age to the throne. The Tanchei who share a caste with Senalian scholars take care not to raise the claim in their hearing. The wall's first raising predates the Mandate, so it does not answer the claim, and the sages of Eikkasto refuse to argue it.

The [[lore-wanduo|Wandwo]] watch the wood's edge for Senalian magic leaving it, as they watch every enclave.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The forest harms nobody who keeps to its edge. The passes toward the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]] lie west of it, beyond the wall, where smugglers and fugitives go.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A party with papers reaches the villages at the forest's edge, whose people treat the wood as strange country and do not go in. A path into the yews without the Senali's welcome leads somewhere else, and a traveler comes out where he went in.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The question.** A young Tanchei at court has raised the chroniclers' claim in front of a Senali sage of Eikkasto, and the sage has gone home to the wood. The hall the sage advised wants the counsel back and an apology carried to the wood's edge by someone who is not a chronicler.

# The Truth of Eikkasto {#truth .secret}

**The enclave.** The hearths of Eikkasto are grown among the oldest yews. The paths that turn a traveler back are the forest's keeping, as in every enclave, and they harm nobody.
