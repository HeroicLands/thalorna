## Overview {#overview}

**Kalpisto**, "the place of pale wood" in the [[skill-senalilng|Senali language]], was a [[lore-flksenali|Senalian]] wood of pale-barked trees in the country below the northern end of the north-western mountain line of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], inside [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]]. The Earthshatter tore it up by the roots, the whole of it, and the imperial registers enter it as the Torn Wood. It lies in the country the earthquake broke, and the [[lore-chaizho|Chaizho]] hunt through it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody holds it. It lies in the Elder lands the empire recognizes, and no Senalo lives there.

## Layers {#history}

The Senali raised their enclaves as the Elder peoples raised the wall, and no imperial record says when Kalpisto was raised. The Senali and the Khazari were among the first builders of the wall, before the empire's dated record opens, and the wood lay inside it.

The Earthshatter, the great earthquake of the northwest, tore up wide stretches of Senalian forest by the roots, and Kalpisto went down entire. The Chaizho came up out of the fissures that opened through it.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The Torn Wood is Chaizho country, the deepest of it, and the packs hunt there in numbers that grow every season. The Senali of [[place-orvakkasto|Orvakkasto]], whose own forest lost half its trees to the same earthquake, hold the packs at the edge of their standing wood, and the Khazari of [[place-garnguraz|Garn-guráz]] hold the mountain line above. The court is divided over whether the northwest is the Elders' own affair, and the shortest road for the Emperor's small companies marching north to the fallen holds crosses the Torn Wood.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

- **The Chaizho.** Packs of four to eight, taller than a man and faster than a horse over broken ground, which close on their prey from several sides at once.
- **The ground.** The fissures the earthquake opened run under the fallen trunks, and a trunk that looks like a bridge can roll.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A waste of fallen trees, pale trunks lying every way across one another, their root-plates standing up taller than a rider and the earth torn open beneath them. Here and there a trunk is shaped along its length into a curve no wind or fall made, as if it had grown that way on purpose, and cords grown from bark still bind one fallen trunk to another. New growth is coming up between the trunks. The fissures run through the whole of it, and the ground around them is scored with claw-marks.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The crossing.** An imperial company has orders to reach the fallen Khazari holds by the shortest road, and the shortest road is across the Torn Wood. Its Wohou officer wants guides who have been in Chaizho country and come out.

# The Truth of Kalpisto {#truth .secret}

**The hearths.** The shaped trunks are the hearths of Kalpisto, grown from the living trees and tied with cord, and they went down with the trees.
