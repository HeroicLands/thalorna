## Overview {#overview}

The **Enclave of Nelpesto** is the community of about two hundred and fifty of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]] in the old forest of [[place-nelpesto|Nelpesto]], below the middle of the north-western mountain line of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]. _Nelpesto_ is "the place of herbs", from _nelpe_, "herbs", and _-sto_, "the place of it". It is the largest of the three Senalian enclaves the empire recognizes, and its keeper is the Senalo the court deals with first.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

A Senalo of Nelpesto belongs to a hearth the enclave keeps. The hearths raise a lord for things done, by every voice, and a lord's standing is a rank and not a holding. The enclave's head office is the **Nelpenno**, the Keeper of the Herbs, raised by every voice of the hearths and held for as long as they do not raise another. The Nelpenno keeps the forest, says aloud what the hearths agree and nothing they have not, and drives out an outlaw, a _vyerka_, when the hearths ask it.

The empire files the enclave's Senali in its caste order by the work they do for it. Most are [[lore-tsaihuo|Tsaihwo]], for their woodwork, weaving and jewelry; sages of ancient lore, the stars and the natural world are [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]]; archers and scouts who serve as officers hold [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] rank; and the Nelpenno is [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]], one of the very few Elder lords the empire ranks so, who manages the enclave's dealings with the throne.

## Current Situation {#present}

- **The call north.** The court has sent imperial companies north against the [[lore-chaizho|Chaizho]] and wants Senalian scouts and archers to go with them. The Nelpenno has to answer for the enclave, and human commanders already count Senalian archers unreliable for holding back.
- **The watch on the edge.** The [[lore-wanduo|Wandwo]] watch the forest, as they watch every enclave, for Senalian magic leaving it, and the laws that limit the Elder peoples' influence bear on the Nelpenno more than on any other Senalo of Tantsou.
- **The herbs.** The empire counts on the herbs and medicinal plants the forest sends. They are the enclave's side of the understanding that leaves the forest to it.

## Defense {#arms}

Walls and armies do not protect the enclave. The land does. A path into the forest without the Senali's welcome leads out again by the way it went in, and a traveler who will not be turned walks in circles until he gives up. The enclave fights for the empire on its own ground, and in a great war the throne may call on it.

## History {#history}

The Senali raised their enclaves as the Elder peoples raised the wall, and no imperial record says when Nelpesto was raised. The Senali and the Khazari were among the first builders of [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]], before the empire's dated record opens. The arrangement under which the Senali deal with the court is ancient, and the Senali do not say how it began.

## Relations {#relations}

- **[[affiliation-tantsouempr|The Empire of Tantsou]]** leaves the forest to the enclave on the understanding that it stays loyal, and receives its herbs, medicinal plants and, now and then, an enchanted thing.
- **[[affiliation-nclvkkst|Enclave of Eikkasto]]** and **[[affiliation-nclvrvkkst|Enclave of Orvakkasto]]**, the other enclaves of the line, keep their own counsel.
- **The Khazari** of the line's strongholds are neighbors on the same mountains, and the Senali do not speak to them.
