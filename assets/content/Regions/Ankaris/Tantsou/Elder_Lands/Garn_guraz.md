## Overview {#overview}

**Garn-guráz** (_gahrn-goo-RAHZ_), "the guarding of the ridge", is the largest of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazárian]] strongholds of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]. It stands in the cliffs toward the northern end of the north-western mountain line, toward [[place-blackwilds|the Black Wilds]], inside [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]] and a little south of the ancient northern stretch of the wall that is carved with sigils. The imperial registers enter it as the Ridge Stronghold. About fifteen hundred Khazari belong to it, counted with those of its people who work in the imperial cities and those away on the wall. Below the cliffs, about five thousand [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] farm the terraces of the stronghold's valleys.

Seen from the valleys, the stronghold is a cliff: weathered rock, ledges, dark hollows and the long stains where water runs down the face. The hollows are windows and the ledges are sills.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The stronghold belongs to the Khazari of [[affiliation-garngurazhld|Garn-guráz Hold]], who govern themselves there, as the empire allows the Elder peoples in their own lands; [[lore-elderraces|Elder Races]] gives the arrangement. The empire leaves the stronghold alone on condition that its people help defend the empire in war, and it depends on the weapons, armor and fortifications they make.

The terrace villages are entered on the empire's registers as Nouwen, and they live under the stronghold's protection. They send grain, meat, milk, cheese, hides and wool up to its gate, and the stronghold sends down tools and iron, lays out and mends their water-works and terraces, and keeps the approaches. In a hungry year it opens its caches to the villages, and in a war its refuge. The villagers know the gate and the Khazari who keep it by name, and none of them has been inside.

## Layers {#history}

The cliff already held its caves when the Khazari found them, and the hold counts its years from that finding. No imperial record says when it was. The Senali and the Khazari were among the first builders of the wall, before the empire's dated record opens, and the stronghold stands inside it.

When the Earthshatter broke the country at the stronghold's edge, it brought down Khazari holds in the cliff faces and tore up stretches of Senalian forest by the roots. Garn-guráz stood. The [[lore-chaizho|Chaizho]] came up out of the new fissures after it.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The stronghold stands toward the northern end of the line that holds the Chaizho in the broken country. Its sentries see packs on the ridges every season, and every season the count is higher. The Khazari and the Senali are holding the packs there at heavy cost, and the holds that fell in the Earthshatter cannot be rebuilt while the hunters range through their ruins.

The court is divided over whose fight it is. The Emperor has taken the view that the Elder holds are all that stand between the Chaizho and the rice basins, without announcing it, and small companies of imperial soldiers armed with Khazárian-forged weapons are marching north to fight beside the Elders. They come to the stronghold's valleys under their own Wohou officers.

Its people serve their turns on the wall as well. Those in the north face the riders of [[being-wogenhenzhuo|Wogen Henzhwo]] and the undead hordes beyond the sigil-carved stretch, at the same time as the stronghold faces the Chaizho at home.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

- **The Chaizho.** Packs of four to eight, taller than a man and nearly proof against ordinary steel, range the broken country at the stronghold's edge and test its walls at night.
- **The north.** Beyond the wall lie the Black Wilds and the undead hordes the sigils keep out, and the [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] raid the northern frontier. The Khazari kill Grukar on sight.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A party needs papers to come this far north, and the Nouwen of the terrace villages ask to see them. The villages are well kept and better supplied with iron than any village of the basins. The track ends at the foot of the cliff, at a gate in the rock that does not look like a gate until someone opens it. A stranger is turned away there, or ignored; a party on imperial business is heard at the gate and answered there, and goes no farther.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The soldiers below the gate.** An imperial company sent north against the Chaizho needs guides through the stronghold's valleys and someone to speak for it at the gate, and its Wohou officer has no one who knows how to ask a Khazár for anything.
- **The count.** A Khazár sentry's count of the packs on the ridges is higher than the court's, and the officer who must carry it south wants witnesses who have seen the packs themselves.

# The Truth of Garn-guráz {#truth .secret}

**The hold.** Behind the face, passages run back into the mountain along the rock's own joints to the council-hall, the forges, the archive, the tombs and, deepest, the refuge, sealed and provisioned for the whole hold and its villages to hold out a long time.

**The sigils.** The Khazari of Garn-guráz who serve their turn in the north keep the sigils carved in the wall's northern stretch, which the Elder peoples carved when they first raised it. The sigils hold.
