## Overview {#overview}

**Sarv-rumák** (_sahrv-roo-MAHK_), "the laying of the slate", was a [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazárian]] hold in a cliff of layered gray stone near the northern end of the north-western mountain line of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], inside [[place-tantsouwall|the Palewall]]. The Earthshatter brought its face down, and the imperial registers enter it as the Fallen Stronghold. It lies in the heart of the country the earthquake broke, and the [[lore-chaizho|Chaizho]] range through it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody holds it. It lies in the Elder lands the empire recognizes, and no Khazár lives there while the Chaizho range through the ruin.

## Layers {#history}

The cliff held its caves before the Khazari found them, and the hold kept its years from that finding, as every hold does. No imperial record says when it was. The Senali and the Khazari were among the first builders of the wall, before the empire's dated record opens, and the hold stood inside it.

The Earthshatter, the great earthquake of the northwest, brought down Khazari holds in the cliff faces, and Sarv-rumák was one of them. Its face came down in slabs along the layers of the stone, and the Chaizho came up out of the new fissures below it.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The fallen holds cannot be rebuilt while the hunters range through their ruins, and the Chaizho den in Sarv-rumák's open halls. The Khazari of [[place-garnguraz|Garn-guráz]] and the Senali of [[place-orvakkasto|Orvakkasto]] hold the packs in the broken country around it, and every season more are counted. The court argues over whether the northwest is the Elders' own affair, and the Emperor's small companies are marching north to fight beside the Elders.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

- **The Chaizho**, in packs of four to eight, nearly proof against ordinary steel, who use the fallen halls as dens.
- **The rock.** The face is down in slabs, the fissures below it are open, and what still stands has lost the cut that held it.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A slope of gray slabs, each split clean along the stone's layers, piled against the foot of a cliff whose upper face is still standing. In the standing face are windows with no rooms behind them and sills that open on air. Behind the slabs the first halls of the hold lie open to the sky, their pillars the rock left standing, and the floors are scored with claw-marks and littered with bones. A passage curves back into the cliff along a joint of the rock and ends in a fall.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The dens.** An imperial company sent into the broken country wants the packs that den in the ruin driven out, and needs someone who can read a Khazari passage to find where they go.
- **The archive.** Every hold keeps its record in its archive, and the Khazari of Garn-guráz want to know whether Sarv-rumák's archive came through the fall. Nobody can look while the packs den in the halls.

# The Truth of Sarv-rumák {#truth .secret}

**The deep rooms.** The fall took the face and the halls behind it. The council-hall, the archive, the tombs and the refuge lie deeper in the rock, past the fall that closes the passage, and no one has reached them since the Earthshatter.
