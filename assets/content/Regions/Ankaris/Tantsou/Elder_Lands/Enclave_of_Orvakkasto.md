## Overview {#overview}

The **Enclave of Orvakkasto** is the community of about a hundred of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]] in [[place-orvakkasto|Orvakkasto]], below the northern end of the north-western mountain line of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], on the edge of the country the Earthshatter broke. _Orvakkasto_ is "the place of the living wood", from _orvakka_, "the living wood", and _-sto_, "the place of it". The Earthshatter tore up half of its forest by the roots, and the enclave holds the standing half against the [[lore-chaizho|Chaizho]] that hunt in the other.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

A Senalo of Orvakkasto belongs to a hearth the enclave keeps. The hearths raise a lord for things done, by every voice, and a lord's standing is a rank and not a holding. The enclave's head office is the **Orvakkanno**, the Keeper of the Living Wood, raised by every voice of the hearths and held for as long as they raise no other. The Orvakkanno keeps the standing forest, says aloud what the hearths agree, and drives out an outlaw, a _vyerka_, when the hearths ask it.

The empire files the enclave's Senali by the work they do for it. Most are [[lore-tsaihuo|Tsaihwo]], and its archers and scouts who serve as officers hold [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] rank, prized for skirmishing and scouting.

## Current Situation {#present}

- **The torn half.** The Chaizho hunt through the fallen half of the forest in packs of four to eight, and more are counted every season. The enclave holds them at the edge of the standing half at heavy cost.
- **The companies.** The Emperor's small companies of imperial soldiers are coming north to fight beside the Elders, and their commanders want the enclave's scouts to lead them into the broken country. The commanders count Senalian archers unreliable for holding back; the archers of Orvakkasto have been holding the line since the Earthshatter.
- **The court.** One party at court holds that the northwest is the Elder peoples' land and its troubles theirs to settle. The Half Wood is the enclave that stands to lose most if that party wins.

## Defense {#arms}

Walls and armies do not protect a Senali enclave; the land does, and a path into the standing forest without the Senali's welcome leads out again where it went in. Against the Chaizho the enclave's defense is its archers and its blades, the few that pierce a Chaizho's hide reliably.

## History {#history}

The Senali raised their enclaves as the Elder peoples raised the wall, and no imperial record says when Orvakkasto was raised. The Senali and the Khazari were among the first builders of [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]], before the empire's dated record opens. The Earthshatter tore up the northern half of the forest, and the Chaizho came up out of its fissures after it.

## Relations {#relations}

- **[[affiliation-tantsouempr|The Empire of Tantsou]]** leaves the forest to the enclave on the understanding that it stays loyal, and sends its soldiers north to fight beside the Elders without announcing why.
- **[[affiliation-nclvnlpst|Enclave of Nelpesto]]** and **[[affiliation-nclvkkst|Enclave of Eikkasto]]**, the other enclaves of the line, keep their own counsel.
- **[[affiliation-garngurazhld|Garn-guráz Hold]]** holds the north of the line against the same packs. The Senali do not speak to the Khazari.
