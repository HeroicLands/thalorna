## Overview {#overview}

The **Enclave of Eikkasto** is the community of about a hundred and fifty of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]] in the old yew forest of [[place-eikkasto|Eikkasto]], toward the western end of the north-western mountain line of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]. _Eikkasto_ is "the place of the yew", from _eikka_, "a yew", and _-sto_, "the place of it". The court knows it for its sages.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

A Senalo of Eikkasto belongs to a hearth the enclave keeps. The hearths raise a lord for things done, by every voice, and a lord's standing is a rank and not a holding. The enclave's head office is the **Eikkanno**, the Keeper of the Yew, raised by every voice of the hearths and held while they raise no other. The Eikkanno keeps the forest, says aloud what the hearths agree, and drives out an outlaw, a _vyerka_, when the hearths ask it.

The empire files the enclave's Senali by the work they do for it: most are [[lore-tsaihuo|Tsaihwo]], and the sages of ancient lore, the stars and the natural world who advise the human elite are [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]]. A sage of Eikkasto who advises a hall at court answers to the hearths of the wood for it.

## Current Situation {#present}

- **The sages at court.** Every scholar at court knows that a Senalo's opinion rests on more centuries of watching than any human library holds, so the sages' counsel is valued and mistrusted in equal measure, and the halls they advise compete for it.
- **The chroniclers' claim.** The empire's chroniclers hold that the Elder age closed with the Mandate of Heaven. The sages of Eikkasto refuse to argue it, and the Tanchei who share their caste take care not to raise it in their hearing.
- **The watch on the edge.** The [[lore-wanduo|Wandwo]] watch the forest, as they watch every enclave, for Senalian magic leaving it.

## Defense {#arms}

Walls and armies do not protect the enclave. The land does: a path into the yews without the Senali's welcome leads out again where it went in. The enclave fights for the empire on its own ground, and in a great war the throne may call on it.

## History {#history}

The Senali raised their enclaves as the Elder peoples raised the wall, and no imperial record says when Eikkasto was raised. The Senali and the Khazari were among the first builders of [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]], before the empire's dated record opens.

## Relations {#relations}

- **[[affiliation-tantsouempr|The Empire of Tantsou]]** leaves the forest to the enclave on the understanding that it stays loyal, and takes its sages' counsel.
- **[[affiliation-nclvnlpst|Enclave of Nelpesto]]** and **[[affiliation-nclvrvkkst|Enclave of Orvakkasto]]**, the other enclaves of the line, keep their own counsel.
- **[[affiliation-tanvdurakhhld|Tanv-durákh Hold]]** keeps the stronghold above the wood, and the Senali do not speak to the Khazari.
