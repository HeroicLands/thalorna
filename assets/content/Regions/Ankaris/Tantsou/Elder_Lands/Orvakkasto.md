## Overview {#overview}

**Orvakkasto**, "the place of the living wood" in the [[skill-senalilng|Senali language]], is the smallest of the three [[lore-flksenali|Senalian]] enclaves of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], and the one on the front against the [[lore-chaizho|Chaizho]]. Its old forest lies below the northern end of the north-western mountain line, south of the stronghold of [[place-garnguraz|Garn-guráz]] and inside [[place-tantsouwall|the Palewall]], on the edge of the country the Earthshatter broke. The Earthshatter tore up half of the forest by the roots, and the imperial registers have entered it as the Half Wood since. About a hundred Senali live in the half that stands.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The forest belongs to the Senali of the [[affiliation-nclvrvkkst|Enclave of Orvakkasto]], who govern themselves there, magic included, under the empire's recognition of the Elder lands; [[lore-elderraces|Elder Races]] gives the arrangement. The empire leaves the wood to them on the understanding that they stay loyal.

## Layers {#history}

The Senali raised their enclaves as the Elder peoples raised the wall, and no imperial record says when Orvakkasto was raised. The Senali and the Khazari were among the first builders of the wall, before the empire's dated record opens, and the forest lies inside it.

The Earthshatter, the great earthquake of the northwest, tore up the northern half of the forest and opened fissures through it. The Chaizho came up out of the fissures after it.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The torn half is Chaizho country. Packs of four to eight hunt through the fallen trees, and every season more are counted. The Senali of Orvakkasto hold them at the edge of the standing half at heavy cost, with arrows and blades of their own make, the few that pierce a Chaizho's hide reliably.

The small companies of imperial soldiers the Emperor has sent north are coming into the broken country. Their commanders count Senalian archers unreliable for holding back, and the archers of Orvakkasto have been fighting the packs since before the first company marched.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

- **The Chaizho.** Taller than a man, faster than a horse over broken ground, and nearly proof against ordinary steel; a pack closes on its prey from several sides at once.
- **The ground.** The fissures in the torn half are still open, and the fallen trunks lie across them.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A party with papers and a reason to be in the broken country reaches the edge of the standing forest, where the old trees end in a line of torn roots and the fallen half lies beyond. A path into the standing half without the Senali's welcome leads out again where it went in.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The scout.** A Senalian scout of Orvakkasto has been asked to lead an imperial company through the torn half to the ruins of the fallen holds, and the company's Wohou officer wants a second opinion of the scout's route from someone who is not Senali.
- **The count.** The court's count of the packs is lower than the forest's, and the enclave wants it carried to the capital by someone the court cannot call an Elder.

# The Truth of Orvakkasto {#truth .secret}

**The enclave.** The hearths of Orvakkasto stand in the old trees of the standing half, grown from the living wood. The paths that turn a traveler back are the forest's keeping, as in every enclave, and they harm nobody.
