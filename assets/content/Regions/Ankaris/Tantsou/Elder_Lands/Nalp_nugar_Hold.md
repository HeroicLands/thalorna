## Overview {#overview}

**Nalp-nugár Hold** is the community of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]] at [[place-nalpnugar|Nalp-nugár]], the northernmost Khazárian hold of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], in the cliffs behind the sigil-carved northern stretch of [[place-tantsouwall|the Palewall]]. Its name, _nalp-nugár_, is "the watching of the cold". About twelve hundred Khazari live in the rock, and about four thousand [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] of the valleys below the hold live under its protection. The throne recognizes the hold as Elder land and leaves its government to the Khazari.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

**Inside the rock** the council of the elders of the hold's houses governs, with no one first among them, as in every Khazari hold. It judges from the archive, apportions the stores against the long winters, and keeps the watch. It sends one elder to the gate as **Speaker**, to hear the valleys and the officers of the wall's garrisons. A second elder is **Master of Works**, over the valleys' water-works and terraces and the hold's own works, and a third is **Warden of the Watch**, over the approaches.

**In the empire** the hold's Khazari carry classification tablets like every Elder in the empire, most of them classed [[lore-tsaihuo|Tsaihwo]] for their craft.

**In the valleys** the Nouwen villages keep their own heads, who bring the villages' word up to the gate.

## Current Situation {#present}

- **The north.** The riders of [[being-wogenhenzhuo|Wogen Henzhwo]], the raids of the [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] and the undead hordes press the stretch of the wall below the hold, and the garrisons along it are thin.
- **The Wandwo.** The [[lore-wanduo|Wandwo]] posted at every garrison on the stretch are on guard for Elder magic, and the hold is in sight of them all year.

## Arms {#arms}

The hold's defense is its cliff, its watch and its refuge, and its people kill Grukar on sight as every hold does. The empire leaves it alone on condition that its people help defend the empire in war.

## History {#history}

The houses of the hold found its caves and count the hold's years from that finding. The Senali and the Khazari were among the first builders of the wall, before the empire's dated record opens, and the hold stands behind its oldest stretch. The account of [[lore-khazarturn|the Fall of Khazartúrn]] is kept in the hold's archive and read at intervals.

## Relations {#relations}

- **[[affiliation-tantsouempr|The Empire of Tantsou]]** recognizes the hold as Elder land and leaves it alone on condition that its people help defend the empire in war.
- **[[affiliation-kwozhengprv|Kwozheng Province]]** holds the northern marches and their garrison colonies.
- **[[affiliation-garngurazhld|Garn-guráz Hold]]** and **[[affiliation-tanvdurakhhld|Tanv-durákh Hold]]** send the Khazari who keep the wall's north by turns.
