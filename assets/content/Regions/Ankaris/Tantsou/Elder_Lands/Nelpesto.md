## Overview {#overview}

**Nelpesto**, "the place of herbs" in the [[skill-senalilng|Senali language]], is the largest of the three [[lore-flksenali|Senalian]] enclaves of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]. It is an old forest below the middle of the north-western mountain line, inside [[place-tantsouwall|the Palewall]], between the strongholds of [[place-garnguraz|Garn-guráz]] to the north and [[place-tanvdurakh|Tanv-durákh]] to the west. The imperial registers enter it as the Herb Wood, for what comes out of it: rare herbs, medicinal plants and, now and then, an enchanted thing. About two hundred and fifty Senali live there.

The trees are older and taller than any in the basins, and few humans go in among them. To the Nouwen villages at the forest's edge the wood is strange country, and they leave it alone.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The forest belongs to the Senali of the [[affiliation-nclvnlpst|Enclave of Nelpesto]], who govern themselves there, magic included, under the empire's recognition of the Elder lands; [[lore-elderraces|Elder Races]] gives the arrangement. The empire leaves the forest to them on the understanding that they stay loyal, and the herbs and simples that come out of it are their side of the understanding. The enclave's keeper is classed [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]] and manages the enclave's dealings with the throne.

## Layers {#history}

The Senali raised their enclaves as the Elder peoples raised the wall, and no imperial record says when Nelpesto was raised. The Senali and the Khazari were among the first builders of the wall, before the empire's dated record opens, and the forest lies inside it. The arrangement under which the Senali of Tantsou deal with the court is ancient, and whether it was negotiated or compelled is a question the Senali do not answer.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The Emperor has sent small companies of imperial soldiers north to fight beside the Elders against the [[lore-chaizho|Chaizho]], and the court has turned to the Senali for their archers and scouts. Human commanders count Senalian archers unreliable, because they will not commit to a human war before they must, and the Senali find the commanders short-sighted. The keeper of Nelpesto is the Senalo the court asks first, and every answer the keeper gives is weighed against how little a Zhazho rank weighs if the court turns.

The [[lore-wanduo|Wandwo]] watch the forest's edge, as they watch every enclave, for any sign of Senalian magic leaving it.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The forest harms nobody who keeps to its edge. The Chaizho are confined to the broken country north of it, and the packs grow in number every season.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A party with papers can walk the Nouwen villages along the forest's edge, where the herbs come out of the forest and the empire's factors take them. A path that leads into the wood without the Senali's welcome leads somewhere else: a traveler walks in circles, loses the hour, or finds himself convinced that turning back is right, and comes out where he went in.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The physician's order.** A court physician in the capital wants a medicinal plant that only the Herb Wood sends, more of it than the forest has sent before, and the factor at the forest's edge needs someone to carry the request to the keeper.
- **The archers.** A Wohou officer marching north with an imperial company wants Senalian scouts for the broken country, and has been told to ask at the Herb Wood.

# The Truth of Nelpesto {#truth .secret}

**The enclave.** The hearths of Nelpesto stand among the oldest trees of the forest, grown from the living wood and tied with cord, and no stone is laid among them. The herbs the empire receives are grown and gathered in the forest's clearings.

**The paths.** The paths that turn a traveler back are the forest's keeping, the same that every enclave has, and they harm nobody.
