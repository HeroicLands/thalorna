## Overview {#overview}

**Tanv-durákh Hold** is the community of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]] at [[place-tanvdurakh|Tanv-durákh]], the Khazárian stronghold at the western end of the north-western mountain line of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], toward the gate on the western pass. Its name, _tanv-durákh_, is "the holding of the gate". About nine hundred Khazari belong to it, and about three thousand [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] of the terrace villages below the stronghold live under its protection. The throne recognizes the stronghold as Elder land and leaves its government to the Khazari.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

**Inside the rock** the council of the elders of the stronghold's houses governs, with no one first among them, as in every Khazari hold. It judges from the archive, apportions the stores, and sets each house's turns at the wall's gates and in the north. It sends one elder to the gate as **Speaker**, to hear the villages and the officers of the empire. A second elder is **Master of Works**, who keeps the terraces' water-works, the stronghold's own works and the Khazari stone of the gate on the western pass, and a third is **Warden of the Watch**, over the approaches and the passes.

**In the empire** the stronghold's Khazari carry classification tablets like everyone else. Most are classed [[lore-tsaihuo|Tsaihwo]] for their craft, masters of engineering are [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]], and those of proven courage or generalship hold [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] rank and command at the wall's gates.

**In the valleys** the Nouwen villages keep their own heads, who bring the villages' word up to the gate.

## Current Situation {#present}

- **The gate on the western pass.** The oasis-holders of [[place-idwkrrgn|Idvaqor]] raise their tolls when the frontier garrisons are thin, and shelter silk smugglers and sorcerers fleeing the [[lore-wanduo|Wandwo]] compounds. The stronghold's Khazari command at the gate by turns, and a sorcerer who gets past the wall is one the empire cannot recover.
- **The north.** The stronghold sends turns north as well, to the stretch of the wall that faces [[place-blackwilds|the Black Wilds]], and every turn sent north leaves the gates thinner.
- **The Chaizho.** The Chaizho are confined to the broken country at the far end of the line, and the court's party that holds the northwest to be the Elders' own affair counts Tanv-durákh as one of the Elders who ought to be fighting there.

:::secret {title="What the turns keep"}
The Khazari of Tanv-durákh who serve their turn in the north keep the sigils carved in the wall's northern stretch with those of Garn-guráz. The sigils hold.
:::

## Arms {#arms}

The stronghold's defense is its cliff, its watch on the passes and its refuge, and its people kill Grukar on sight as every hold does. Its smiths make weapons and armor for the empire. On the wall its Khazari serve by turns, with those of [[affiliation-garngurazhld|Garn-guráz Hold]], at the gates on the major transit roads and in the north, and those of Wohou rank command there with human soldiers under them.

## History {#history}

The houses of the stronghold found its caves and count the hold's years from that finding. The Senali and the Khazari were among the first builders of [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]], before the empire's dated record opens. The account of [[lore-khazarturn|the Fall of Khazartúrn]] is kept in the stronghold's archive and read at intervals.

## Relations {#relations}

- **[[affiliation-tantsouempr|The Empire of Tantsou]]** recognizes the stronghold as Elder land and leaves it alone on condition that its people help defend the empire in war.
- **[[affiliation-doulwenprv|Doulwen Province]]** holds the basins below the pass, where the caravans the gate admits are documented and taxed.
- **[[affiliation-garngurazhld|Garn-guráz Hold]]** shares the wall's turns with the stronghold.
- **The Senali** of the line's enclaves are neighbors on the same mountain line, and the Khazari do not speak to them.
