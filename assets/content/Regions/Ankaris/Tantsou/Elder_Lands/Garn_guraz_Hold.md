## Overview {#overview}

**Garn-guráz Hold** is the community of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]] at [[place-garnguraz|Garn-guráz]], the largest of the Khazárian strongholds of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], toward the northern end of the north-western mountain line. Its name, _garn-guráz_, is "the guarding of the ridge". About fifteen hundred Khazari belong to it, and about five thousand [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] of the terrace villages below the stronghold live under its protection. The throne recognizes the stronghold as Elder land and leaves its government to the Khazari.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

**Inside the rock** the stronghold is governed as every Khazari hold is, by the council of the elders of its houses, sitting together with no one first among them. The council judges the stronghold's quarrels from its archive, apportions its stores and sends its people to their work, the wall included. It sends one elder to the gate as **Speaker**, to hear the villages and the empire's officers and to give the stronghold's answer. A second elder is **Master of Works**, over the water-works of the terraces and the stronghold's own works, and a third is **Warden of the Watch**, over the approaches and the broken country.

**In the empire** the Khazari of the stronghold carry classification tablets and treat them as paperwork. Most are classed [[lore-tsaihuo|Tsaihwo]], the artisans, for their smithing and stonework; masters of engineering who design the empire's fortresses and mines are [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]]; and those of proven courage or generalship hold [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] rank on the wall.

**In the valleys** the Nouwen villages keep their own heads, who bring the villages' word up to the gate.

## Current Situation {#present}

- **The Chaizho.** The stronghold holds the north of the line against the [[lore-chaizho|Chaizho]] in the country the Earthshatter broke. Every season more packs are counted, and the Warden's count runs ahead of the court's.
- **The companies from the south.** The Emperor's small companies of imperial soldiers, armed with Khazárian-forged weapons, are marching north to fight beside the Elders. The council has to decide where they camp, who feeds them and whose orders they take in the broken country, and the terrace villages are the only larder within reach.
- **The turns on the wall.** Every turn the council sends to the wall is a turn not at home against the Chaizho, and the north the stronghold's people keep faces the riders of [[being-wogenhenzhuo|Wogen Henzhwo]] and the undead hordes at once.

:::secret {title="What the turns keep"}
The Khazari of Garn-guráz who serve their turn in the north keep the sigils carved in the wall's northern stretch, which the Elder peoples carved when they first raised the wall. The council cannot send fewer to the north without the sigils going unkept, whatever the Chaizho cost it at home.
:::

## Arms {#arms}

The stronghold's own defense is the cliff, its watch and its refuge, and its war against the Grukar is the one every hold wages: a Grukar is killed on sight. Its smiths make the weapons the empire depends on, and the heaviest of them are among the few that pierce a Chaizho's hide reliably. On the wall, the stronghold's Khazari serve by turns, with the Khazari of [[affiliation-tanvdurakhhld|Tanv-durákh Hold]], at the gates on the major transit roads and in the north, and those of Wohou rank command the garrisons there with human soldiers under them.

## History {#history}

The houses of the stronghold found its caves and count the hold's years from that finding. The Senali and the Khazari were among the first builders of [[place-tantsouwall|the Palewall]], before the empire's dated record opens. The account of [[lore-khazarturn|the Fall of Khazartúrn]] is kept in the stronghold's archive and read at intervals, as it is in every hold. The Earthshatter brought down holds near Garn-guráz and spared it.

## Relations {#relations}

- **[[affiliation-tantsouempr|The Empire of Tantsou]]** recognizes the stronghold as Elder land and leaves it to govern itself, on condition that its people help defend the empire in war. The stronghold keeps the compact and shares a fraction of what it knows.
- **[[affiliation-tanvdurakhhld|Tanv-durákh Hold]]** sends its people to the wall by turns, as Garn-guráz does, and the two councils share the turns between them.
- **[[affiliation-nalpnugarhld|Nalp-nugár Hold]]** keeps the hold at the sigil-carved stretch to the north.
- **The Senali** of the three enclaves of the line hold the Chaizho beside the Khazari, in their own forests. The Khazari do not speak to them.
