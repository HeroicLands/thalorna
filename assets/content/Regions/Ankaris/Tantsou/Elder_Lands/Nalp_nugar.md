## Overview {#overview}

**Nalp-nugár** (_nahlp-noo-GAHR_), "the watching of the cold", is the northernmost of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazárian]] holds of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]. It stands in the cliffs where the north-western mountain line comes up against [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]], behind the wall's ancient northern stretch, the stretch that is intricately carved with sigils. [[place-blackwilds|The Black Wilds]] lie beyond the wall. The imperial registers enter it as the Stronghold at the Wall. About twelve hundred Khazari live there, and about four thousand [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] farm the terraces of the valleys below it.

From the valleys the hold is a cliff above the wall, ledged and stained, with hollows that are windows. The wall's garrisons stand every five miles along the stretch below it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The hold belongs to the Khazari of [[affiliation-nalpnugarhld|Nalp-nugár Hold]], who govern themselves there under the empire's recognition of the Elder lands; [[lore-elderraces|Elder Races]] gives the arrangement. The empire leaves the hold alone on condition that its people help defend the empire in war.

The terrace villages are entered on the empire's registers as Nouwen and live under the hold's protection. They send barley, meat, milk, cheese, hides and wool up to its gate, and the hold sends down tools and iron, keeps their water-works and terraces, and keeps the approaches. In a hungry year it opens its caches to them, and in a war its refuge. The villagers know the gate and have never been inside.

## Layers {#history}

The cliff held its caves before the Khazari found them, and the hold counts its years from that finding. No imperial record says when it was. The Senali and the Khazari were among the first builders of the wall, before the empire's dated record opens, and the hold stands inside the wall's oldest stretch.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The north is pressed from three sides. The riders of [[being-wogenhenzhuo|Wogen Henzhwo]] raid the northern districts from beyond the marches, the [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] raid the frontier, and beyond the sigil-carved stretch the undead hordes gather. Infighting among the Wohou lords of the north has thinned the garrisons below the hold, and the garrisons that remain face all three.

Every garrison on the stretch has its [[lore-wanduo|Wandwo]], posted there from the Zhazho houses, and the Wandwo are always on guard for Khazárian influence, the one power in the empire their monopoly cannot reach. Here they are posted within sight of an Elder hold year round, and they watch it as closely as they watch the north.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

- **The undead hordes** beyond the wall, which the northern stretch keeps out with the sigils' help.
- **The Grukar**, whom the Khazari kill on sight, and who raid the frontier.
- **Wogen's riders**, who come over the marches from the Black Wilds.
- **The cold.** The north is cold-temperate, with hard winters and short summers.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A party needs papers, and the garrisons below the hold ask for them at every post. The valley track climbs from the wall road through the terrace villages, whose people are wary of strangers and well supplied with iron, to the foot of the cliff. A stranger is turned away at the gate in the rock or ignored there. A party on imperial business is heard at the gate and answered there.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The short garrison.** A garrison below the hold is short of men against Wogen's riders and the undead at once, and its Wohou commander asks a party to carry his request for hands to the hold's gate.
- **The posting.** A Wandwo serving his year at a garrison below the hold has written down every light he has seen in the cliff, and wants a party to carry his notes south to his house without the garrison's commander reading them.

# The Truth of Nalp-nugár {#truth .secret}

**The hold.** Behind the face, passages run back into the mountain along its joints to the council-hall, the forges, the archive, the tombs and the refuge, sealed and provisioned for the whole hold and its villages.

**The sigils.** The sigils on the stretch below the hold were carved by the Elder peoples when they first raised the wall, and the Khazari who serve their turns in the north, from Garn-guráz and Tanv-durákh, keep them. They hold.
