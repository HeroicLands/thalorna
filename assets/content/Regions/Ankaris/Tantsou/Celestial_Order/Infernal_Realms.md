A Tanchei lecturer warns her first-year students off the word "hell" before she teaches them anything else about the dark. The Infernal Realms of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]'s faith are no place of punishment, no kingdom of evil and no domain of a fallen god. They are the places where the [Great Sorting]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"} was left unfinished: where the [Huenlei]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"}, the Unclassified, was never fully processed, or where the classification has begun to come apart. The celestial cosmology calls the realm the [[affiliation-youweng|Youweng]], and its inhabitants the Youweng, the demons.

## What the Infernal Is

In the [[lore-phlsphyleiwo|Leiwo philosophy]] the Great Sorting pushed the Huenlei to the margins and never destroyed it. The Infernal Realms are those margins: the gaps between categories, the cracks in the classification, the places where sorted things blur back into chaos.

They have no fixed place on any map. They lie wherever classification fails: in deep wilderness no surveyor has ever entered on a register, in the gaps between elemental domains where no bureau's jurisdiction is clear, in the dreams of the deranged where waking and sleeping run together. They do not oppose heaven the way the demons of a western sermon do. They are where heaven's administration does not reach.

The orthodox teaching holds that the Youweng is a containment zone, the shadow the Sorting cast. The celestial administration moves what it cannot cleanly sort into it, so that its influence on the ordered world is kept small: refusers of the Sorting, corrupted functionaries, things that fit no category. The Tanchei admit privately that the account is incomplete, because the realm is also where the Demon Lords draw their power, and their power comes from the system's failure to contain them.

## The Demons

The Tantsou call the Youweng demons for want of a better word. They are beings the classification cannot hold: some never sorted at all, some sorted and then lost, a few officials of heaven who fell. They have no bureau, no portfolio and no assigned work.

That is what makes them dangerous. A bureau's power reaches only as far as its jurisdiction, and a being outside every jurisdiction answers to none of them. No bureau has authority over it, no seal binds it and no petition compels it. A demon is not strong because it is wicked. It is strong because the administration has no procedure for it.

At the top of the realm stand the five **Demon Lords**, each the embodiment of one way the order fails: [[lore-henzhuodty|Huenzhuo]], the First Refusal, lord of the Unclassified; [[lore-nuohodty|Nuoho]] the Unmaker; [[lore-watsaidty|Watsai]], the Forger of False Records; [[lore-zhaosoudty|Zhaosou]], the Walking Contradiction; and [[lore-douyodty|Douyo]], the Guide Who Leads Astray. Beneath them are the [[affiliation-youwenglsrdmns|lesser demons]], and among the fallen functionaries the strangest is [[lore-yozhuodty|Yozhuo]], the Custodian of Lost Seals, a scribe of heaven who fled with a hoard of counterfeit seals and now keeps the [Infernal Archive]{.name data-type="place/site"}.

The naming of these powers troubles the [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]]. To give an unclassified being a name and a title is to classify it a little. Some scholars hold that naming weakens it, others that a name is a label on something that refuses labels, and so a false comfort, and the argument has run for centuries without a ruling.

## The Empire's Defense

The Infernal Realms are one of the strongest arguments for the empire's rigid order. Only a perfectly kept classification can hold off the Unclassified, the argument runs; every breach of caste, every rite done badly, every failure of administration opens a small gap in the sorting, and the Huenlei seeps through. Seen this way, the empire's harshness is a quarantine on the cosmos.

The Warlords of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]] are the empire's first defense against an incursion, with their armies and the [[lore-wanduo|Wanduo]] sorcerers behind them. The Tanchei watch the records for signs that the classification is failing. The Wanduo turn their magic directly on the unclassified, trying to force-classify them: to bind them with seals, file them and so impose an order on beings that have none.

Whether the threat is as near as the ruling castes say, or swollen to justify their power, is a question a few people ask, and only in private. The empire's growing troubles—plague, war on its borders, whispers that the [[lore-mandatehvn|Mandate of Heaven]] is failing—may mean the classification is truly fraying, or may be the ordinary friction of a vast administration straining under its own weight. A great deal turns on which, and the Tanchei are divided.
