In the faith of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], the gods keep office hours. The great powers of the Celestial Pantheon are the senior staff of the celestial bureaucracy: beings of immense power who hold posts in the cosmic classification and whose authority comes from the office, not from any divinity of their own. The Tantsou approach them by petition, by transaction and by following the correct procedure, and an Aureldian priest would hardly recognize what they do as worship.

At the head of the pantheon stands [[lore-tanzhodty|Tanzho]], the principle of classification. Beneath him [[lore-weizhuodty|Weizhuo]], known as Weizhuo Daonei, the Jade Sovereign, runs the administration, the Minister of Storms and the Minister of the Moon hold their portfolios, and below them the [[lore-fvlmntlrds|Five Elemental Lords]] each govern one of the material domains.

## Tanzho Zhuowo, the First Classifier

**Domain:** classification, the cosmic order and the [Neutral Lei]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"}.

Tanzho is no administrator. He is the principle of classification itself, the first act of telling one thing from another. The Tantsou do not petition him, make him offerings or expect him to intervene, and the Tanchei teach that he is not a person, not an official and not a being in any ordinary sense. He is why categories exist.

The imperial line claims descent from Tanzho, and in Tantsou theology that is a descent of office, not of blood: the classification needs a mortal administrator, and the imperial line is the lineage named to the post.

Art shows Tanzho as a single vertical line, the First Distinction, the stroke that divided above from below and cut the [Huenlei]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"}. His temples hold no statues, no offerings and no petition forms: each is an empty room with one vertical mark on the far wall. Only the Emperor performs rites in them, and what those rites are is told to no other caste.

## Weizhuo Daonei, the Jade Sovereign

**Domain:** administration, the coordination of the bureaus and the [Dao Choulei]{.name data-type="lore/theology"}, the Master Registry.

Weizhuo Daonei is the chief administrator of heaven, the official who makes the system work. Tanzho set the classifications; Weizhuo keeps them. She settles disputes between bureaus, hears appeals between departments and keeps the Dao Choulei, the Master Registry, the index of every classified thing in existence.

She is the most petitioned power in Tantsou, because every matter that does not fall clearly to one bureau goes up to her office. Artists show her seated behind a vast desk walled in by stacks of documents, a jade seal in one hand and a ledger in the other. Her face is always neutral, neither kind nor stern, because she is working.

The [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] count themselves servants of her office and model their scholarship on its procedures.

## Raogui Wodao, the Minister of Storms

**Domain:** thunder, storms, justice and punishment.

[[lore-raoguidty|Raogui]] keeps the weather, and with it the enforcement of heaven's displeasure. In the Tantsou cosmos a storm is an administrative act. Rain is allotted to a district by his office, lightning is an order to strike, and thunder is the sound of heaven enforcing its rulings. A farmer petitioning for rain writes to him.

He is the patron of the [[lore-wohou|Wohou]], because the warrior nobility are the mortal arm of enforcement as he is the celestial one. His temples are military houses, where officers file requests for authorization before a campaign so that the violence they are about to commit carries heaven's sanction.

Art shows him armored, with lightning in one hand and in the other the seal of enforcement that authorizes a punishment.

## Youbui Tengyou, the Minister of the Moon

**Domain:** the moon, night, dreams, cycles and prophecy.

[[lore-youweidty|Youbui]] governs the night, the hours when [Ha]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"}, the dark flow, is strongest, classifications loosen and the boundaries between sorted things grow thin. That makes her office both necessary and dangerous. At night dreams carry messages from heaven through the [Bureau of Night Counsel]{.name data-type="affiliation"}, and at night the Huenlei presses closest to the sorted world.

Her work is to keep the classification whole through its weakest hours, so that the night's loosening never becomes a dissolution. The Tanchei consult her office for prophecy, which they understand as advance notice: word of decisions already made in heaven and not yet carried out on earth.

Art shows her pale, a lantern in one hand for the light of classification lasting through the dark, and in the other a sealed scroll of the night's dispatches waiting for morning.
