# Appearance {#appearance}

A dead woman in the war-gear of a Tantsou champion, upright and unhurried, wearing a hairpin set with a splinter of black stone. Armed dead gather around her and move when she moves.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Shou Zhiwengmei, "the Star-Wind Dream", is one of the four human champions of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] who became Dreadlords of [[place-nuuvakoldi|Nuuvakoldi]]. The garrisons of the Wall call such a lord a Chin Zhwo. The word is the living's, made from what soldiers on [[place-tantsouwall|the Palewall]] see of the dead, and nothing shows that the dead keep ranks of their own.

## Background

Shou Zhiwengmei was the greatest seer-champion of her generation. Her power as a seer was unmatched, and she was almost always right. She read the north in dreams and went to meet it. Dreams are the province of [[lore-youweidty|Youbwi]], the Keeper of the Jade Seal, whose [Bureau of Night Counsel]{.name data-type="affiliation"} carries heaven's messages by night, and the court's Tanchei consult her office for prophecy as advance notice: word of decisions already made in heaven and not yet carried out on earth. Shou was a seer of that tradition. Her given name is a granted name, the honor the empire confers for outstanding service, set after her family name Shou.

**The foretelling that sent her north.** She foretold that a great seer would rise among the Dreadlords and let the dead see every existing prophecy and new ones still unknown. She was certain of her gift, and she read the foretelling as her tradition reads every prophecy, as a verdict already given in heaven. With so much at stake she felt she must act at once and be the one to confront that seer. She believed she had prepared protection against the undead and knew the key to reaching the black artifact in the north and destroying it. She was terribly wrong. She was turned, and the seer she went north to confront was herself.

**Her blind spot.** The foretelling spoke of every existing prophecy, and nobody short of omniscient sees them all. She did not know the dead had prophecies of their own until she was undead herself. Until then the dead's prophecy was a scattered series of fragments from nameless lesser seers among them. Since she became a Dreadlord it has taken form around her: a possible future in which the white artifact is destroyed and the Sovereign rules a world of the dead. [[being-fivid|Fivid]] keeps it in written form.

**Her sight now.** She is still a seer, and her gift is undiminished. Every seer sees through a lens, and hers turned with her death. Every prophecy she receives is oriented around the undead, and her thoughts, which once turned on how to enable life, turn on how to destroy it and sow chaos.

**What passed to the Sovereign.** Everything she knew of prophecy passed to [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]]: the White Prophecy, a possible future in which the artifacts are sealed back together and the Sovereign's sway ends; most of the court of Tantsou's own prophecies; everything then known of the steps toward the White Prophecy; and how to prevent it.

She is among the champions who replaced the fallen Elder lords of the council, and commands a Host, which the garrisons name by its commander: the host of Shou Zhiwengmei.

The fragment of Hagau, the court's term for the black artifact, is set in a hairpin. She serves [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]], whom the court of Tantsou calls Ha Chen, because there is no choice. Removing the fragment ends her command, and the Host answers to her no more.

**What the fall cost.** The loss of the greatest seer-champion of her generation was a catastrophe of morale: despair and anguish across the realm, as if the marshal who held the armies of the empire had joined the enemy before the war was done. It was also a catastrophe of knowledge. Everything she knew passed to the Sovereign: the dispositions of the Wall, its wards, the two stones, the court's politics, the passwords and ciphers in use. She uses what she knows to gain status under the Sovereign, and in doing so betrays the people she protected.

## Psyche

### Personality

Sentient, and bound to the Sovereign's will. She is as stubborn as she was in life, more megalomaniacal, and certain of her own sight.

### Motivation

To destroy life and sow chaos, and to win status under the Sovereign with what she knows of the realm and what she sees.

### Strengths

Still the greatest seer of her age, and a champion in her own right. She senses the future and which prophecies connect, and the dead's prophecy takes form around her. What she knows of the realm reached the Sovereign and, through him, the undead. The enemy's knowledge of any matter is as old as the last death that carried it.

### Weaknesses

Her certainty. In life she received a prophecy with high stakes, trusted her own reading and acted at once, and it made her what she is. She acts the same way now, and her actions show what she believes is coming.

The fragment. A hand that removes it from her hairpin ends her command over the Host.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

She serves the Sovereign, [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]]. The Dreadlords are ancient, sentient and rivals. The three other human champions share the fall: [[being-doutuijingwo|Dou Tuijingwo]], [[being-kwodaurauzho|Kwo Daurauzho]] and [[being-leiweiyanleng|Lei Weiyanleng]].

## Plot Hooks

1. **The fragment.** A party that fights through to the lord of a Host can end the command by removing the fragment, if it can reach her hairpin.
2. **What she knew.** A sortie fails because the enemy was waiting where only her old knowledge could send it. The party learns that every disposition, password and cipher she knew has to be changed wherever it stands.
3. **The steps are known.** Every step that was known toward the White Prophecy reached the Sovereign through her, so a party that works toward it may be expected, and the dead know how to prevent it. The party has to learn which steps the dead already know.
4. **Acting on the knowledge.** A living agent of the Sovereign works through the court to use something she carried north, and the party has to learn what it was before it is used.
5. **The bait.** A verse shaped to make her act at once reaches the dead. Her certainty commits her Host before anyone weighs it, and the party has to be ready where the verse sends her, or far from it.
