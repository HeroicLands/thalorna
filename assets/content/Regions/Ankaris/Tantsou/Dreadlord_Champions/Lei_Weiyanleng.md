# Appearance {#appearance}

A dead woman in the war-gear of a Tantsou champion, upright and unhurried, wearing a bracelet set with a splinter of black stone. Armed dead gather around her and move when she moves.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lei Weiyanleng, "the Bright Jade Cloud", is one of the four human champions of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] who became Dreadlords of [[place-nuuvakoldi|Nuuvakoldi]]. The garrisons of the Wall call such a lord a Chin Zhwo. The word is the living's, made from what soldiers on [[place-tantsouwall|the Palewall]] see of the dead, and nothing shows that the dead keep ranks of their own.

## Background

Lei Weiyanleng was a warrior-sage without equal in healing or in the sword. Her given name is a granted name, the honor the empire confers for outstanding service, set after her family name Lei.

She is among the champions who replaced the fallen Elder lords of the council, and commands a Host, which the garrisons name by its commander: the host of Lei Weiyanleng.

The fragment of Hagau, the court's term for the black artifact, is set in a bracelet. She serves [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]], whom the court of Tantsou calls Ha Chen, because there is no choice. Removing the fragment ends her command, and the Host answers to her no more.

**What the fall cost.** The loss of a warrior-sage without equal was a catastrophe of morale: despair and anguish across the realm, as if the realm's marshal had passed into the enemy's camp with the war still being fought. It was also a catastrophe of knowledge. Everything she knew passed to the Sovereign: the dispositions of the Wall, its wards, the two stones, the court's politics, the passwords and ciphers in use. She uses what she knows to gain status under the Sovereign, and in doing so betrays the people she protected.

## Psyche

### Personality

Sentient, and bound to the Sovereign's will.

### Motivation

Status under the Sovereign, won with what she knows of the realm.

### Strengths

A healer and a swordswoman with no equal in either, and a sage's knowledge of the realm. What she knows of the realm reached the Sovereign and, through him, the undead. The enemy's knowledge of any matter is as old as the last death that carried it.

### Weaknesses

The fragment. A hand that removes it from her bracelet ends her command over the Host.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

She serves the Sovereign, [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]]. The Dreadlords are ancient, sentient and rivals. The three other human champions share the fall: [[being-doutuijingwo|Dou Tuijingwo]], [[being-kwodaurauzho|Kwo Daurauzho]] and [[being-shouzhiwengmei|Shou Zhiwengmei]].

## Plot Hooks

1. **The fragment.** A party that fights through to the lord of a Host can end the command by removing the fragment, if it can reach her bracelet.
2. **What she knew.** A sortie fails because the enemy was waiting where only her old knowledge could send it. The party learns that every disposition, password and cipher she knew has to be changed wherever it stands.
3. **Acting on the knowledge.** A living agent of the Sovereign works through the court to use something she carried north, and the party has to learn what it was before it is used.
