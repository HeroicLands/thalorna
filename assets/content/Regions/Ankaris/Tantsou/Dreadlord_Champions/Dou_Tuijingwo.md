# Appearance {#appearance}

A dead man in the war-gear of a Tantsou champion, upright and unhurried, wearing a ring set with a splinter of black stone. Armed dead gather around him and move when he moves.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Dou Tuijingwo, "the Iron-Gold Warrior", is one of the four human champions of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] who became Dreadlords of [[place-nuuvakoldi|Nuuvakoldi]]. The garrisons of the Wall call such a lord a Chin Zhwo. The word is the living's, made from what soldiers on [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]] see of the dead, and nothing shows that the dead keep ranks of their own.

## Background

Dou Tuijingwo was the Emperor's own champion and was never beaten. He led the longest campaign beyond the Wall. His given name is a granted name, the honor the empire confers for outstanding service, set after his family name Dou.

He is among the champions who replaced the fallen Elder lords of the council, and commands a Host, which the garrisons name by its commander: the host of Dou Tuijingwo.

The fragment of Hagau, the court's term for the black artifact, is set in a ring. He serves [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]], whom the court of Tantsou calls Ha Chen, because there is no choice. Removing the fragment ends his command, and the Host answers to him no more.

**What the fall cost.** The loss of the realm's undefeated champion was a catastrophe of morale: despair and anguish across the realm, as if the marshal who had led the realm's armies had gone over to the enemy in the middle of the war. It was also a catastrophe of knowledge. Everything he knew passed to the Sovereign: the dispositions of the Wall, its wards, the two stones, the court's politics, the passwords and ciphers in use. He uses what he knows to gain status under the Sovereign, and in doing so betrays the people he protected.

## Psyche

### Personality

Sentient, and bound to the Sovereign's will.

### Motivation

Status under the Sovereign, won with what he knows of the realm.

### Strengths

A commander of the first rank who knows how the empire fights beyond the Wall, because he led its longest campaign there. What he knows of the realm reached the Sovereign and, through him, the undead. The enemy's knowledge of any matter is as old as the last death that carried it.

### Weaknesses

The fragment. A hand that removes it from his ring ends his command over the Host.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

He serves the Sovereign, [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]]. The Dreadlords are ancient, sentient and rivals. The three other human champions share the fall: [[being-kwodaurauzho|Kwo Daurauzho]], [[being-leiweiyanleng|Lei Weiyanleng]] and [[being-shouzhiwengmei|Shou Zhiwengmei]].

## Plot Hooks

1. **The fragment.** A party that fights through to the lord of a Host can end the command by removing the fragment, if it can reach his ring.
2. **What he knew.** A sortie fails because the enemy was waiting where only his old knowledge could send it. The party learns that every disposition, password and cipher he knew has to be changed wherever it stands.
3. **Acting on the knowledge.** A living agent of the Sovereign works through the court to use something he carried north, and the party has to learn what it was before it is used.
