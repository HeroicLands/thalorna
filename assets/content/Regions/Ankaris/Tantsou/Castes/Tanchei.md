A Warlord with his army mustered and the passes open waits for a woman in a blue robe to finish reading the sky, and if she says the omens are against him, he waits a month. The Tanchei are the scholarly and spiritual elite of [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Tantsou]], the fourth caste, trained in the reading of omens, the mathematics of astrology and the keeping of the sacred texts, and they run the empire's administration from the ministries down to the frontier registers. Their counsel is what the empire's highest authorities act on when the question is whether heaven agrees.

## What the Tanchei Do

**They read the sky.** A Tanchei astrologer casts the charts that set the date of a coronation, a campaign, a wedding or the laying of a foundation, and reads the unlooked-for sign—a comet, a two-headed calf, a well that runs red—as a message from the bureaus of heaven. Her report says whether the empire still stands in harmony with the celestial order, and a Warlord who acts against it does so knowing every scholar in his province has the document. [[lore-clndrstrlgy|Calendar and Astrology]] sets out the reckoning they use.

**They keep the texts.** Certain sacred books and relics may be held, read and interpreted by the Tanchei alone: the cosmological treatises, the formulae of the great rites and the oldest records of the empire's place in the sorted order. Possessing one of them is a crime for anyone of another caste.

**They counsel and they draft.** The Tanchei advise the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]] and the [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] on the [Teng Lou]{.name data-type="lore/law"} (Heavenly Law) and on what the classification requires. They draft new law in consultation with the Warlords, and the clerks, registrars and census officials who keep the [Dao Nong Chou]{.name data-type="lore/law"} are theirs.

## Rights and Restrictions

The [[lore-castelaws|Caste Laws]] give the scholars three rights:

Counsel
: the exclusive right to advise the Zhazho and Wohou on celestial matters and the reading of heaven's will.

The texts
: the sole right to possess, study and interpret the forbidden books and relics.

No arms
: release from the obligation to bear arms or march with an army, though many advise commanders in the field.

They hold the scholars to two restrictions:

Fidelity to the Teng Lou
: every counsel and every interpretation upholds the Heavenly Law, and a scholar found shading a reading to please a patron is stripped of rank.

No magic
: astrology and foretelling are the only arts the caste may practice. They are reckoning, not sorcery, and a Tanchei who works anything more is executed like anyone else who poaches on the [[lore-wanduo|Wanduo]] monopoly.

## Naming and Honors

A scholar whose reading of the heavens changes the empire's course may be granted a personal name, and scholars' names are drawn from the stars and the auspicious signs: Zhi Zhai Leng Cheng (Rising Bright Star Bird) and Teng Zho Leng Wuen (Fifth Auspicious Star of the Celestial Dragon). [[lore-personlnms|Personal Names]] describes the grant.

## Elder Scholars

[[lore-flksinale|Sinale]] sages, who know more of the stars and of the natural world than any human school, are often classified Tanchei. Their counsel is valued at court and also mistrusted, because they will not say where their knowledge comes from and seem to care little what humans do with it.

[[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] masters of engineering, mining and metallurgy are classified Tanchei when an imperial work—a dam, a mountain road, a fortress cut from the rock—cannot be built without them. Their knowledge of the deep places is held in awe and some fear, and court scholars are uneasy sharing a title with people who will not explain how they find water in stone.

## Philosophy of Lei

In the [[lore-phlsphyleiwo|Leiwo]] framework the Tanchei are the keepers of intellectual and spiritual Lei: the records, readings and rites that keep the sorted order sorted.
