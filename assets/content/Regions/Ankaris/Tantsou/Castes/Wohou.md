The Wòhou are the warrior nobility of [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Tāntsou]], the third of the eight castes, and the hands through which the provinces are held. A Warlord (_Wòzhuo_) is [[lore-zhazho|Zhazhò]], of the Dragon's blood; the commandant who leads his field army, the officer who holds a district's revenue, courts and corvée, and the swordmaster who drills his household are Wòhou, serving under him. The caste alone may raise and command armed force, and its members are bound by a code of honor whose breach is answered with exile or a state-ordered death.

## Authority and Responsibilities

**Military command.** The Wòhou alone hold the right to lead soldiers. Every garrison, field army and campaign is commanded by an officer of this caste, and the [[lore-tsaihuo|Wònong]], the common soldiers of the Tsàihuo, carry their spears on Wòhou authority and under Wòhou orders. A Warlord's strategy is worked out with his Wòhou commanders and his [[lore-wanduo|Wánduo]] counselors together, and executed by the former.

**Judicial authority.** The Warlords hold the law in their provinces, and the magistrates and administrators who hear cases, investigate crimes, settle disputes and enforce the [[lore-castelaws|Caste Laws]] in a Warlord's name are drawn from this caste. A Wòhou provincial administrator holds a district's revenue, its courts and its corvée under the Warlord's appointment, and answers to him for all three.

**Territorial governance.** Wòhou receive lands and titles in exchange for military service. The holding grants authority over the people living on it, and the [[lore-nouwen|Nouwén]] who work it render their tribute of produce and labor to the Wòhou lord as they do to the Zhazhò above him.

## The Code of Honor

The caste lives under the **Wò Hou Lòu** (Warrior Balance Law), a strict code governing conduct in war, in office and at home. A breach can bring severe punishment, up to exile or _Ruìsing_, the state-ordered ritual suicide described under [[lore-tseising|Law and Honor]]. Personal honor is everything to a Wòhou: an accusation of dishonor opens a provisional debit on his ledger that stands until it is resolved, and the consequences of a proven one run past the legal penalty into ostracism and the disgrace of his household.

## Rights and Duties

**Rights**

- **Command of arms**: the sole right to raise, lead and direct armed force
- **Authority in justice**: the power to judge, punish and enforce the law within their domains, under the Warlord
- **Honor and status**: recognition of service through lands, titles and standing

**Duties**

- **The code**: unwavering adherence to the Wò Hou Lòu, whose violation falls on the man and his house
- **Defense of the realm**: the obligation to protect the empire and its people from every threat
- **Service to the Zhazhò**: loyalty to the Emperor and to the Warlord each serves

## Naming and Honors

A personal name is granted only to a Wòhou of exceptional service, usually for a deed of great prowess or a stroke of military genius, and the name is a phrase that evokes power, speed and the fight. Three such names: Zhò Shòu Téng Ja (Dragon's Rain of Heavenly Arrows), Buì Ja Wò Rao (Ice Blade of Thunderous Battle) and Wéng Hèn Rao Chài (Wind Tiger Roars Across Thunder Mountain). See [[lore-personlnms|Personal Names]] for how the grant is made.

## Elder Races Among the Wòhou

[[lore-flkkhazar|Khazári]] warriors of exceptional bravery and strategic gift are honored with the Wòhou title. They serve the empire as defenders of its mountain borders, and the fortifications they lay out are the reason those borders hold. [[lore-flksinale|Sinalë]] are granted the standing now and then for military service, and they are valued as scouts and skirmishers for their speed and their silence in woodland; their reluctance to commit to a human war until they must is a standing source of friction between Sinalëan officers and the commanders they serve under.

## Philosophy of Léi

In the [[lore-phlsphyleiwo|Léiwō]] framework the Wòhou are the enforcing Léi of the empire: the power that keeps classifications in place by force, exercised through disciplined martial practice and service to the realm.
