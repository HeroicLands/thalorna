## Overview {#overview}

Weichai, the Jade Peak, is a market town of [[affiliation-mwonouprv|Mwonou Province]] in [[place-mwonouplnd|the Mwonou Uplands]], with about 4,800 people. It stands at the foot of a hill terraced from top to bottom, between the terrace villages above it and the river village below, and its households describe themselves, in the register and to strangers, as being "near Weichai".

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The terraces feed the town. The [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] households of the hill, each holding a terrace its lineage cut and keeps, bring their rice and millet down to the Weichai market, where the [[lore-shuonong|Shwonong]] sell them salt, cloth and iron, and a merchant who sands his salt answers for it in the magistrate's court. The town's men go to the corvée on the river dike for thirty days in every Month of Earth.

The town's clans reach far beyond it. The scholar [Zhi Zhai Leng Cheng]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, whose new star stands under her name in the court registers, was a daughter of the [Zhi Zhai Clan]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} near Weichai, and the [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] of the [Clan of Moon Rises Over Still Water]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} keep registers as far off as the frontier way-station at [[place-kwisen|Kwisen]].

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The Prince of Mwonou holds the district through his [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] officers, and a magistrate reads the [Teng Lou]{.name data-type="lore/law"} in his court at Weichai, hears the disputes the clan elders cannot settle and fines both parties where both are at fault.

The Leigen of Weichai, the spirit masters of [[lore-ancstrwrshp|Ancestor Worship]], keep the households' dead. They stand beside each heir at the [Festival of the Dead]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} and give the answers of the ancestors, and they teach the form of the reckoning from [[lore-hearthaccount|The Hearth Account of the Third Terrace]], the account of a household of [[place-zhengnou|Zhengnou]], the terrace village on the hill.

## The Present {#present}

The rain has failed over the uplands. The terraces at the top of the hill are the first to go dry, and households that planted rice there plant millet. The district granaries are open, and the famine petitions of the hill come down to the magistrate's court.
