Kwisen, the Gray Gate, is the frontier way-station beyond [[place-jnanadvar|the Scholars' Door]], on the Tantsou side of the pass, where the road from the [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara Region]] comes down into [[place-mwonouplnd|the Mwonou Uplands]]. It is a walled court of gray brick among the pines, with green-glazed tiles on its roofs, red-lacquered gates and a bronze bell under the eaves. About 70 soldiers, porters and clerks keep it.

The station feeds every traveler who comes down the pass and houses him for as long as the weather requires. Its [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] classify each one and enter him in a register that has been kept without a gap for four hundred years, and no Vedyari visitor is ever shown it. The frontier officers send an escort to meet a party at the col and not a step sooner, and [[affiliation-hvarnguides|the Hvarn]] who guide travelers over the pass go no farther than the station, where they are fed at a table of their own. Below its eastern gate the road runs down the valley into the empire.
