## Overview {#overview}

Kwisen, the Gray Gate, is the frontier way-station beyond [[place-jnanadvar|the Scholars' Door]], on the Tantsou side of the pass, where the road from the [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara Region]] comes down into [[place-mwonouplnd|the Mwonou Uplands]]. It is a walled court of gray brick among the pines, with green-glazed tiles on its roofs, red-lacquered gates and a bronze bell under the eaves. About 70 soldiers, porters and clerks keep it.

Inside the gate everything happens in order. Porters in undyed hemp set a traveler's bales down in a chalked square and step back from them. Soldiers in lacquered scale check the wooden tablet on a cord at every belt. In a long room of low desks, clerks in dark blue robes write in columns with fine brushes. Below the eastern gate the road runs down the valley into the empire.

## The Present {#present}

The station feeds every traveler who comes down the pass and houses him for as long as the weather requires. Its [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] classify each one and enter him in a register that has been kept without a gap for four hundred years, and no Vedyari visitor is ever shown it. A merchant carrying foreign coin is told here what it is worth in the empire, and the figure is not negotiable.

The [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] holds that the Vedyari shrenis are a tributary trade of no administrative consequence, and its frontier officers keep an exact register of every Vedyari caravan all the same. They send an escort to meet a party at the col and not a step sooner, and [[affiliation-hvarnguides|the Hvarn]] who guide travelers over the pass go no farther than the station, where they are fed at a table of their own. The registrar who receives foreigners is a Tanchei of the [Clan of Moon Rises Over Still Water]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, near [[place-weichai|Weichai]], who names herself by her description and opens a fresh page for each newcomer.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **Instruments for the far side.** A reckoner of [[affiliation-ganokashalda|The Reckoners' College]] sends a case of instruments over the pass to a colleague in the empire, and it must reach the station whole.
- **How the register is kept.** A Vedyari scholar wants to know why a frontier that calls the shrenis of no consequence keeps a register of them, and asks a party to winter at Kwisen and learn how it is kept and who reads it.
- **The fresh page.** The registrar finds that a party has no caste to answer with, and wants an account of it that her register can hold before she lets it go down the valley.
