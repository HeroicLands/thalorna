## Overview {#overview}

Kwisen, the Gray Gate, is the frontier way-station beyond [[place-jnanadvar|the Scholars' Door]], on the Tantsou side of the pass, where the road from the [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara Region]] comes down into [[place-mwonouplnd|the Mwonou Uplands]]. It is a walled court of gray brick among the pines, with green-glazed tiles on its roofs, red-lacquered gates and a bronze bell under the eaves. About 70 soldiers, porters and clerks keep it.

Inside the gate everything happens in order. Porters in undyed hemp set a traveler's bales down in a chalked square and step back from them. Soldiers in lacquered scale check the wooden tablet on a cord at every belt. In a long room of low desks, clerks in dark blue robes write in columns with fine brushes. Below the eastern gate the road runs down the valley into the empire.

## The Present {#present}

The station feeds every traveler who comes down the pass and houses him for as long as the weather requires. Its [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] classify each one and enter him in a register that has been kept without a gap for four hundred years, and no Vedyari visitor is ever shown it. A merchant carrying foreign coin is told here what it is worth in the empire, and the figure is not negotiable.

The [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] holds that the Vedyari shrenis are a tributary trade of no administrative consequence, and its frontier officers keep an exact register of every Vedyari caravan all the same. They send an escort to meet a party at the col and not a step sooner, and [[affiliation-hvarnguides|the Hvarn]] who guide travelers over the pass go no farther than the station, where they are fed at a table of their own. The Tanchei who receives foreigners is the registrar's second daughter, of the [Clan of Moon Rises Over Still Water]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} near [[place-weichai|Weichai]]. She names herself by her description and opens a fresh page for each newcomer.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **Before the escort comes.** Weather shuts the col with a party on it, and the escort meets a party at the col and not a step sooner. The party must get itself down to the place where the escort starts, or wait for a thaw that the station will not hurry.
- **The figure is not negotiable.** A merchant's venture depends on his foreign coin being worth more in the empire than the clerks will say, and the station names the figure without argument. He wants the party to learn whether the figure can be argued at all, and what the arguing costs him.
- **The fresh page.** She finds that a party has no caste to answer with, and wants an account of it that her register can hold before she lets it go down the valley.
