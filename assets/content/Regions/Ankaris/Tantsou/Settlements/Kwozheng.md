Kwozheng, the Old Wall, is the seat of [[affiliation-kwozhengprv|Kwozheng Province]], a city of about 110,000 people in the north of the [[place-tantsouregin|Tantsou Region]], behind the fortified marches that face the far-northern [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] nests and the steppe beyond them.

The garrisons of the marches take their pay, their orders and their grain through Kwozheng, and the Prince's commanders sit here. The north's own fields are thin and its winters long, so the barges that carry the tribute grain north to the garrisons feed the city as well.
