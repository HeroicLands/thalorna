Mwonou, the Green Field, is the seat of [[affiliation-mwonouprv|Mwonou Province]], a city of about 160,000 people in the southwest of the [[place-tantsouregin|Tantsou Region]]. It stands in an upland basin below [[place-graznmntns|the Grazian Mountains]], where the long passes toward [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]] come down.

In an ordinary year the terraces and paddies of the basin feed the city. In a bad year the district granaries are opened, and when they are empty the city eats rice bought from the granaries of [[affiliation-wongsenprv|Wongsen Province]].
