Zhowen, the Dragon's Center, is the capital of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] and the seat of the Emperor, a city of about two million people on one of the great rivers of the [[place-tantsouregin|Tantsou Region]]. It is two cities, one on each bank. Between them, on an island in the middle of the river, stands the walled enclave of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]], which nobody but the Zhazho and their personal attendants may enter. A long bridge crosses to the island from each shore, and anyone who crosses without permission is shot from the enclave walls by its archers.

The rest of the city has no wall. The administrative centers stand on the two shores facing the island, and the quarters of the castes spread outward from them in strict order, each bank the mirror of the other. Zhowen does not follow the land: where the ground does not fit its plan, the ground is changed.

The river and the canals carry the tribute grain of the provinces to the city's granaries, and that grain feeds it.
