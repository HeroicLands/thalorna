Rinnou, the Red Field, is a terrace village of the district of [[place-jenshou|Jenshou]] in [[place-mwonouplnd|the Mwonou Uplands]], with forty-one households and about 290 people. Its oldest household cut the first terrace above the Red Field thirty-four generations ago, and the village has farmed the terraces ever since.

Every person of Rinnou is entered as [[lore-noutsou|Nwitsou]], the property of the house of the Prince of [[affiliation-mwonouprv|Mwonou Province]], with every child born to them. A Prince's court ordered it when the village headman was convicted of harboring through one winter a person of the gift who was not entered as [[lore-wanduo|Wandwo]], and the village had kept his secret. The shrines of Rinnou stand empty. Its tablets were lifted from them unbroken, by the proper rite, and carried to [[place-jenshouhall|the District Hall of Jenshou]], where the district feeds them. The order and the protest against it are the matter of [[lore-lastrecordiron|The Last Record of Iron Gate of the Winter Mountain]].
