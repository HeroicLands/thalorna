## Overview {#overview}

Tengmou, Heaven's Wood, is a town of [[affiliation-mwonouprv|Mwonou Province]] in [[place-mwonouplnd|the Mwonou Uplands]], with about 3,500 people. It stands above a valley of terraces watered by ditches, and the [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] households of the valley keep the ditches by lineage. The valley's rice and millet come up to the town's market.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The valley feeds the town. Each lineage keeps its stretch of ditch, and a ditch left unkept dries the terraces below it, so the households settle between themselves who clears which stretch. The town's market takes the valley's rice and millet and sends the cloth and tools of the town back down.

On the night of the [Moon Festival]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} the valley lights its lanterns along the terraces, and the old women tell [[lore-petitionseals|The Petition with Every Seal]] with one lantern on the floor that nobody may let go out. Nobody in the valley writes _not this office_ on anything.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The Prince of Mwonou holds the district through his [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] officers, and a magistrate reads the [Teng Lou]{.name data-type="lore/law"} in his court at Tengmou. The [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] of the [Zhowou Clan]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} near Tengmou send their sons to arms, and the most famous of them is the admired soldier [[being-wenghenrauchai|Weng Hen Rau Chai]]. The town's [[lore-tsaihuo|Tsaihwo]] send theirs to the garrisons of the northern marches; [[lore-unnumberedtwr|The Song of the Unnumbered Tower]] names a potter's son near Tengmou among the nine who hold the tower.

## The Present {#present}

The rain has failed over the uplands, and the ditches of the valley run low. The lineages at the head of the valley take the water first, and the terraces at its foot go dry. The households whose sons are with the garrisons of the north are short of hands to clear the ditches, and the district's corvée falls on the households that remain.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The dry foot of the valley.** The terraces at the foot of the valley go dry while the head takes the water first, and a household there asks a party to learn whether the ditches are being kept as the lineages settled.
- **Hands for the ditches.** The sons are with the garrisons and the corvée falls on those who remain, and a party that passes through can be hired to clear the ditches that nobody has hands for.
