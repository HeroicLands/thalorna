Doulwen, the Road's End, is the seat of [[affiliation-doulwenprv|Doulwen Province]], a city of about 150,000 people in the west of the [[place-tantsouregin|Tantsou Region]]. It stands below the passes toward the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]], where [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] comes down from its customs-house on the western pass.

The caravans unload here. Their silver, amber and horses are documented, classified and taxed before they go on to the river cities, and the silk, porcelain and tea bound west are weighed and sealed for the road. The irrigated basin around the city grows most of its grain, and the silver of the road trade buys the rest.
