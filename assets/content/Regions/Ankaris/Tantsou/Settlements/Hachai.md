Hachai, the Hidden Peak, is a village of about 180 people in the high country of [[place-mwonouplnd|the Mwonou Uplands]], a long day's climb above the uppermost terraces. Its stone houses stand in a fold of the ridge where no road reaches, and the people live on millet, goats and the trade of the herders who cross the pass.

The village has no shrine in the lowland style, no registrar and no magistrate. Strangers are looked over before they are fed.

:::secret {title="Who lives in Hachai"}
Every household of Hachai is struck from the register: outlaws who fled a sentence, and [[lore-noutsou|Nwitsou]] who escaped their owners. [[place-jenshouhall|The District Hall of Jenshou]] has no page for the village, and so no tribute, levy or tablet is owed for it. The [[being-hachaihdmn|Headman of Hachai]] sets the village's rule himself, and the first rule is that nobody leaves to be counted.
:::

## The Failing or the Quarrel {#present}

Hachai trades little with the lowlands. What it sells goes down to the Weichai market with one household's boy.

:::secret {title="How Hachai could be found"}
A year in which the register is checked against the land is the year Hachai is found. The household that sends a boy down to trade at the Weichai market takes the risk for the village, and a magistrate who learns of the place can sell his silence or make his name.
:::
