## Overview {#overview}

Jaunou, the Yellow Field, is a market town in the south of [[place-mwonouplnd|the Mwonou Uplands]], in [[affiliation-mwonouprv|Mwonou Province]], with about 4,200 people. It is named for the ripe millet of the fields around it in an ordinary year. In this year the fields are the yellow of dust: the southern harvests have failed several years running, and Jaunou stands at the heart of the famine.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

In an ordinary year the town lives by the rice and millet of its paddies and terraces, watered by a small river that comes down from the hills, and by the market where the villages of the district sell their harvest. The river is dry in its bed. The district granaries are open and empty, and the rice in the market is rice the province buys from the granaries of [[affiliation-wongsenprv|Wongsen Province]], at the price the Prince of Wongsen names.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The Prince of Mwonou holds the district through his [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] officers. A magistrate, [[being-jaunoumgstrt|Eldest Son of the Commandant of the Clan of Red Gate in Autumn]], reads the [Teng Lou]{.name data-type="lore/law"} in his court at Jaunou, hears the famine petitions of the district and collects the tribute, which the district owes in a bad year as in a good one.

## The Present {#present}

Jaunou is hungry. The households feed their dead before their children, as the ledger of [[lore-ancstrwrshp|Ancestor Worship]] requires, and a household that cannot keep the [Festival of the Dead]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} properly faces a year with weakened advocates in heaven. The famine petitions come to the magistrate faster than he can answer them.

The [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] of the district are divided over the cause. One school reads a breakdown of classification in the domains of Earth and Water; the other reads a sign that the [[lore-mandatehvn|Mandate of Heaven]] is weakening, and puts nothing in writing. At court, some say the drought is the work of the water-gifted [[lore-wanduo|Wandwo]] of the Prince of Wongsen, who sells the rice that feeds the town; nobody has proven it. In other districts of the uplands the [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] have risen against their lords and against the magistrates who collect the tribute.
