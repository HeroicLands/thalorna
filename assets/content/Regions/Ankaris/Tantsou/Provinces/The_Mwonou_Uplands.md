## Overview {#overview}

The Mwonou Uplands are the land of [[affiliation-mwonouprv|Mwonou Province]], in the southwest of the [[place-tantsouregin|Tantsou Region]]: terraced uplands below [[place-graznmntns|the Grazian Mountains]], crossed by the long passes that come down from [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]]. About 26 million people live here. The city of [[place-mwonou|Mwonou]], in an upland basin, is the province's seat.

## The Land {#the-land}

Terraced hillsides climb the foothills in staircases, kept by villages whose terraces have passed down through named lineages for generations, and the basins below them are paddy. Above them the Grazian Mountains stand between the empire and Vedyara, crossed by the passes of [[place-suryadvar|the Sun-Door]], [[place-jnanadvar|the Scholars' Door]] and [[place-shankhadvr|Shankhadvar]].

The rain has failed here. The southern harvests have withered several years running, and the smaller rivers have dried in their beds.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The Prince of Mwonou, of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]], holds the uplands for the Emperor from the city of Mwonou, through his [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] officers and the magistrates who hear the famine petitions of each district. He and the Prince of [[affiliation-kwozhengprv|Kwozheng Province]] are bitter rivals, over an old marriage between their houses and an heir who died.

## Places {#places}

[[place-mwonou|Mwonou]], the Green Field, is the seat: a city of about 160,000 people in an upland basin where the passes toward Vedyara come down. The terraces and paddies of the basin feed it in an ordinary year.

## Current Situation {#present}

The famine of the southern uplands is this province's famine. The district granaries have been opened, and when they are empty the province eats rice bought from the granaries of [[affiliation-wongsenprv|Wongsen Province]], at the price the Prince of Wongsen names. Whether the drought is heaven's or the work of that Prince's water-gifted [[lore-wanduo|Wandwo]] is asked at court and proven by nobody.

The [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] are divided over the cause. One school reads a breakdown of classification in the domains of Earth and Water, and the other a sign that the [[lore-mandatehvn|Mandate of Heaven]] is weakening, a reading no scholar puts in writing. In some districts the [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] have risen against their lords and against the magistrates who collect the tribute.

The Prince of Kwozheng takes every loss of the Prince of Mwonou as his own gain, and the famine is his rival's loss.

## Roads and Trade {#roads-and-trade}

The roads from Vedyara come down through the passes into the uplands. Contact with Vedyara is old and runs on mutual respect: Vedyaran scholars travel to the Tantsou courts and Tantsou monks study Vedyaran texts. Every traveler who comes down a pass is documented and classified before going on.

## Dangers {#dangers}

Hunger, and the risings of the Nouwen in the districts where the tribute is collected.
