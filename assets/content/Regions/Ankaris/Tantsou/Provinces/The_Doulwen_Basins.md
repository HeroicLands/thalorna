## Overview {#overview}

The Doulwen Basins are the land of [[affiliation-doulwenprv|Doulwen Province]], in the west of the [[place-tantsouregin|Tantsou Region]]: the basins below the western mountains and the passes toward the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin Desert Region]], where [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] comes down into the empire. About 22 million people live here. The city of [[place-doulwen|Doulwen]], where the caravans unload, is the province's seat.

## The Land {#the-land}

The basins are irrigated and grow most of the grain their city eats. Above them the mountains wall in the west of the empire, and beyond the passes lie the high deserts of the Khazrin. The [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]] keep halls in the western mountains and seldom come out of them.

## Peoples {#peoples}

The mountain villages above the passes and the soldier families of their garrisons are of the [[lore-tantsoumrchclt|Tantsou Frontier Peoples]]. They have married into the soldier-settlers of the northern marches for generations, and the register records them as Tantsou like everyone else.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The Prince of Doulwen, of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]], holds the basins and the passes for the Emperor from the city of Doulwen. He is a younger Prince, rising fast, and he stands for reform and has the young Emperor's ear.

## Places {#places}

[[place-doulwen|Doulwen]], the Road's End, is the seat: a city of about 150,000 people below the passes, where the Celestial Road comes down from its customs-house on the western pass.

## Current Situation {#present}

The other three Princes watch the Prince of Doulwen in [[lore-weizhairui|the Jade Fan Dance]], since the court's favor shifts with every reading of the stars, and a Prince who rises quickly is the one they work hardest to bring down.

The Prince of Doulwen seeks foreign weight to add to his hold on the Emperor's ear, and he is the Prince who backs the request of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]] for direct relations with the court at [[place-zhowen|Zhowen]] ([[scenario-embsydnvrtantsou|Embassy from Dunavarre to Tantsou]]).

## Roads and Trade {#roads-and-trade}

The Celestial Road runs west from Doulwen through the Khazrin toward the [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión Region]]. The caravans' silver, amber and horses are documented, classified and taxed at Doulwen before they go on to the river cities, and the silk, porcelain and tea bound west are weighed and sealed there for the road. The bureaucracy classifies every significant movement of goods, and smuggling on the road is a highly developed profession.
