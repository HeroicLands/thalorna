## Overview {#overview}

The Kwozheng Marches are the land of [[affiliation-kwozhengprv|Kwozheng Province]], in the north of the [[place-tantsouregin|Tantsou Region]]: the cold coast, the hard-wintered basins of the north and the fortified marchland of watchtowers and garrison colonies toward the nests of the far-northern [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar Folk]]. About 20 million people live here. The city of [[place-kwozheng|Kwozheng]], behind the marches, is the province's seat.

## The Land {#the-land}

The north is cold-temperate, with hard winters and short summers, and its fields are thin. Beyond the marches lies the steppe of the [Black Wilds]{.name data-type="place/region"}.

## Peoples {#peoples}

The soldier-settlers of the marches are of the [[lore-tantsoumrchclt|Tantsou Frontier Peoples]]. A march village keeps its signal fires and its muster rolls as carefully as the core keeps its registers, and the soldiers' song of the northern marches is [[lore-unnumberedtwr|The Song of the Unnumbered Tower]].

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The Prince of Kwozheng, of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]], holds the north for the Emperor from the city of Kwozheng, where his commanders sit. He and the Prince of [[affiliation-mwonouprv|Mwonou Province]] are bitter rivals, over an old marriage between their houses and an heir who died.

## Places {#places}

[[place-kwozheng|Kwozheng]], the Old Wall, is the seat: a city of about 110,000 people behind the marches, through which the garrisons take their pay, their orders and their grain.

## Current Situation {#present}

The steppe beyond the marches is united under the warlord [[being-wogenhenzhuo|Wogen Henzhwo]], and his riders are in the northern districts. The empire treats Grukar raids as a nuisance; the Black Wilds under Wogen are another matter.

In [[lore-weizhairui|the Jade Fan Dance]] the Prince of Kwozheng takes each loss of the Prince of Mwonou as his own gain, and the Prince of Mwonou does the same by him.

## Roads and Trade {#roads-and-trade}

The barges that carry the tribute grain north to the garrisons feed the city of Kwozheng as well.

## Dangers {#dangers}

The riders of the Black Wilds in the northern districts, and the Grukar raids from the far-northern nests.
