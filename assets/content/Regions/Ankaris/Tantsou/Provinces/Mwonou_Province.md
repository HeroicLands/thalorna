Mwonou Province is the southwestern province of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], with about 26 million people. It holds the terraced uplands below [[place-graznmntns|the Grazian Mountains]] and the long passes toward [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]], and its Prince, [[being-hozhomwnou|Hozho]], of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]], rules from the city of [[place-mwonou|Mwonou]] in the upland basin.

## Current Situation {#present}

The rain has failed in its country, and the famine of the southern uplands is its famine. Its Prince buys rice from the granaries of [[affiliation-wongsenprv|Wongsen Province]], whose Prince's sorcerers bring rain to the delta, and pays the price that Prince names.

The Prince of Mwonou and the Prince of [[affiliation-kwozhengprv|Kwozheng Province]] are bitter rivals, over an old marriage between their houses and an heir who died.

## History {#history}

The province's past is known through the records of its districts, which date by reigns and give no year. The frontier register at [[place-kwisen|Kwisen]] has been kept without a gap for four hundred years.

[[lore-annaljenshou|The Annal of Jenshou]] records the reign of [the Houweng Emperor]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, of a dynasty long since passed to another house. In the third year of that reign a Prince of the province began an ancestral hall at Mwonou, nine terraces high, and for the next three years he called the corvée of [[place-jenshou|Jenshou]] upriver to build it; in two of those years the dikes of Jenshou were not mended. The hall was dedicated in the sixth year with nine bells hung in it. In the seventh year it rained for nineteen days, [[place-tabletbank|the Tablet Bank]] above Jenshou opened, and two thousand six hundred and ten people of the district were not found at the count. The Prince's court judged the loss and gave the magistrate of Jenshou Rwising, and the next year the Prince sent nine thousand measures of grain from the provincial granaries to the district. The annal enters the hall to nobody's credit.

In the third year of a reign the record does not name, a Prince of the province ordered the village of [[place-rinnou|Rinnou]] entered whole as [[lore-noutsou|Nwitsou]], because its headman had been convicted of harboring an unbound sorcerer. The order also required the tablets on the village's shrines to be broken. The administrator of the district of Jenshou took his own life in the protest form, and the one thing his last record asked was that the tablets be carried unbroken to [[place-jenshouhall|the District Hall of Jenshou]] ([[lore-lastrecordiron|The Last Record of Iron Gate of the Winter Mountain]]). The Prince granted it, and Rinnou was entered as Nwitsou in the spring all the same, by the officer sent to take the administrator's place.
