A Sinalean scout who saw the first pack says the ground had not stopped moving when they came up out of it: tall as a man at the shoulder, gray-green and fast, five of them running in a line along a fresh fault as if they had been born knowing the ground. The Chaizho ("mountain dragon") are the predators the great earthquake loosed on the remote northwest of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], the country the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale]] hold in their old forests and the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] in their mountain strongholds, and they are the one threat in the empire's reckoning that nobody filed a petition against, because nobody knew they existed.

## The Hunters

A Chaizho stands taller than a man, runs faster than a horse over broken ground, and hunts in packs of four to eight that close on their prey from several sides at once. The packs plan. Survivors describe one animal showing itself on a ridge while the rest came in through the trees, and Khazari sentries report packs testing a wall at night, one point at a time, as if looking for the weak course.

Their hide is the problem. It is thick, scaled and layered over plates of bone, and an ordinary spear or arrow skids off it. Only the best Sinalean blades and arrowheads and the heaviest Khazárian weapons pierce it reliably, and even the Elder peoples, who have both, are losing ground. Claws and teeth do the rest: a Chaizho opens a man in armor with one rake.

## The Earthshatter

The earthquake the northwest calls the [Kuojen Touhuen]{.name data-type="lore/history"} ("Millennial Earthshatter") brought down cliffs that held Khazari cities and tore up wide stretches of Sinalean forest by the roots. The Chaizho came up out of the new fissures in its wake, as if the tremors had woken them from somewhere deep in the earth. Where they lived before, and whether more remain below, nobody knows.

## The Line in the Northwest

The packs are confined, for now, to the country the earthquake broke. The Sinale and Khazari are holding them there at heavy cost, and the numbers are against them: every season more packs are counted, and the Elder holds that fell in the earthquake cannot be rebuilt while the hunters range through their ruins.

The court is divided. One party holds that the northwest is the Elder peoples' land and its troubles theirs to settle. The other has looked at a map and seen that the only things between the Chaizho and the rice basins are the Elder holds and a stretch of mountain no pack has yet tried to cross. The Emperor has taken the second view without announcing it, and small companies of imperial soldiers armed with Khazárian-forged weapons are already marching north to fight beside the Elders.
