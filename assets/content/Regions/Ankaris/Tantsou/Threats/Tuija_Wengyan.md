# Appearance {#appearance}

A lean man in his middle years, in a Wòhou officer's lacquered harness stripped of every badge of rank, stands with his weight square over his feet as if the ground might be taken from him. His face is hard and closed; a pale line where a rank-cord once crossed his throat has never tanned. He speaks with the precise courtesy of the imperial staff and gives orders without raising his voice.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Tuìja Wéngyàn**, granted the name Iron Spear Piercing the Clouds for his service, was one of the most respected generals of the [[lore-wohou|Wòhou]] caste. He is now the empire's most dangerous rebel, and the court made him one.

## Background

**The disobedience.** On a critical campaign he received a direct imperial order to hold his position. He judged it a mistake, attacked, and won. The victory was the problem: a battle won by defying an order is proof the order was wrong, and an imperial order cannot be wrong without the Mandate behind it being questioned. The court stripped him of his rank and exiled him, and the registry recorded the victory under another commander's name.

**The exile.** He did not accept it. In exile he gathered soldiers disaffected by the purge that followed him and minor [[lore-zhazho|Zhazhò]] nobles with grievances of their own against the court. The nobles brought their [[lore-wanduo|Wánduo]] with them, because a Zhazhò's sorcerers go where their owner goes.

**The rebellion.** He means to return, not as the empire's servant but as its conqueror. His forces have made real gains in the border provinces, and the alliance that carried them there is beginning to crack.

## Psyche

### Personality

Proud, controlled and exact. He was a great general because he was right more often than his superiors, and he has never learned to hide that he knows it.

### Motivation

To be proved right. He wants the empire to admit that the order was wrong, and he has come to believe that only a new throne will ever admit it.

### Strengths

A first-rate commander who knows the imperial army's doctrine from the inside, its officers by name and its supply routes by heart. He understands the [[lore-weizhairui|Wéi Zhài Ruì]] well enough to see his allies playing it against him.

### Weaknesses

He needs nobles he does not trust and sorcerers he does not own. His rebellion has no claim to the Mandate except his own grievance, and every province he takes he must hold with someone else's troops.

## Social

### Patrons

- **Zhaowéi Léngzhài**: called the Radiant Phoenix, the shrewd matriarch of the Zhaowéi clan, who wants her family's influence back after years of decline. Her Wánduo work fire and earth. She favors open battle and thinks Tuìja a necessary tool to be put down once the work is done.
- **Wènzhao Wéngdao**: called Wind's Roar, the young head of the Wènzhao clan, too impatient to wait out his family's slow rehabilitation at court. His Wánduo work air and spirit. He prefers subterfuge, and he also thinks Tuìja expendable.

### Enemies

- **The imperial court**: the officials who condemned him, and the commander whose name the registry gave his victory.

### Affiliations

- **The rebellion**: his own command, held together by his generalship and the two clans' sorcerers. Tuìja plays the two nobles against each other to keep either from taking it from him, and both have begun quietly to look for escape routes and friends beyond the borders, because a failed rebellion ends for each of them in torture and execution.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The True Record**: A clerk in a provincial archive has found the original battle report, with Tuìja's name on the victory. Both the rebels and the court want it, for opposite reasons.
2. **The Matriarch's Exit**: Zhaowéi Léngzhài wants safe passage out of the empire for her heirs before the rebellion's luck turns, and she will pay with Tuìja's plans.
3. **Sorcerers Without Owners**: Two of Wènzhao Wéngdao's Wánduo have slipped away from his camp and are trying to reach the Shadow Sect. Their owner, the rebels and the throne all want them back.
4. **The Northern Envoy**: An envoy from [[being-wogenhenzhuo|Wògèn Hènzhuo]] reaches the rebel camp. Tuìja must decide whether a steppe army in the north is an ally or the end of the empire he means to rule.
