## Overview {#overview}

Tantsou is one continuous civilization, whose eight castes have outlasted every dynasty and whose celestial bureaucracy understands this land as the one place where the cosmos has been properly classified. The Tantsou Region is the far east of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]]: a vast expanse of river basins, temperate plains and coastal lowlands walled in by mountains, which has been the cradle of [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Tantsou civilization]] for more than three millennia. An outsider remembers it for its porcelain and its silk, and for the registers. Where the [[place-aureldirgn|Aureldian west]] is a mosaic of sovereign kingdoms bound by related faiths, the political form of Tantsou is nearly always imperial. The west of Ankaris has no emperor and does not want one. The east has had little else.

## Geography

Mountain ranges enclose the region and divide it into a series of great river basins. The northern lands reach a cold coast. To the west lie the passes and high deserts of the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]] frontier, where [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] ends. To the southwest the unaligned range of [[place-graznmntns|the Grazian Mountains]] stands between Tantsou and [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]], crossed by the long passes of [[place-suryadvar|Suryadvar]], Jnanadvar and [[place-shankhadvr|Shankhadvar]]. To the east lies the open ocean and [[place-easternstrt|The Eastern Strait]] that separates the mainland from the [[place-jenchairgn|Jenchai]] archipelago.

On land the empire's boundary is [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]], which runs thousands of miles from the far northern frontiers to the Grazian Mountains in the far south. The laws and rules of Tantsou end at the wall, and nobody passes its gates, in or out, without papers and approvals.

Three great rivers make the basins. Fed by mountain glaciers, they run through the heartland to the eastern ocean and carry commerce, tribute and imperial edicts on the same water. The valleys between them are the most intensively cultivated land in the world: rice, millet, mulberry for the silkworm, tea and orchard fruit feed the crowded river cities. Above the valleys, terraced hillsides climb the foothills in staircases, kept by villages whose terraces have passed down through named lineages for thirty generations or more. A terrace is the first thing a Tantsou points to when asked what the registers are for.

The climate runs from one extreme of the region to the other. The north is cold-temperate, with hard winters and short summers; the central basins are continental, with sharp seasons and monsoon rains; the southern coast is subtropical, warm and wet, and gives way to mangrove shoreline at its margins. Earthquakes are common everywhere, and the imperial bureaucracy keeps ministries for predicting, tracking and answering them, an administrative tradition older than any other on the continent.

## Peoples and Culture

The Tantsou are overwhelmingly human. The rare [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] who walk among them do so under a formal arrangement, possibly a coerced one, that ties certain mountain enclaves to the imperial court; the rarer [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] command [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]] at its gates, along the stretches near the capital and in the north, where the Elder enclaves of the north-western mountain line stand inside it. Everything else is shaped by the eight castes, from the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]], the Sons of the Dragon at the top, through the [[lore-wanduo|Wandwo]] sorcerers bound in service beneath them, the warrior nobles, the celestial scholars, the artisans, the peasant farmers and the merchants, to the outcast [[lore-noutsou|Nwitsou]] at the bottom. A person's caste fixes their trade, their dress, their name, whom they may marry and which protections the law extends to them.

Cultural life is refined and deeply formal. The imperial tradition prizes calligraphy, poetry, painting, tea-craft, music and a complex cuisine whose techniques pass down through lineages. The civilization that makes some of [[place-worldthlrn|Thalorna]]'s finest porcelain and its most subtle lyric poetry is the one whose caste laws sanction collective punishment, and whose prisons hold the grandchildren of disfavored families as a matter of routine. Tantsou intellectuals treat the two as a single thing: classification is refinement, and refinement is classification.

Family honor is kept on the ledger of [[lore-ancstrwrshp|ancestor worship]]. Every household keeps an altar, every generation's conduct is weighed, and when the alternative is dishonor, [[lore-tseising|Tseising]], ritual suicide, is the established means of restoring a lineage's standing. The poorest [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] villages keep these rites as carefully as the court does.

## Religion

The religion of Tantsou is the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]], the Celestial Order: a pantheon conceived as a vast spiritual bureaucracy, of which the earthly government is a working mirror. At its apex is [[lore-tanzhodty|Tanzho]], the Celestial Dragon, who is the principle of classification itself, a sorter and not a creator. Beneath the Dragon, [[lore-weizhuodty|Weizhwo]], the Jade Sovereign, the Minister of Storms ([[lore-raoguidty|Raugwi]]), the Keeper of the Jade Seal ([[lore-youweidty|Youbwi]]) and the [[lore-fvlmntlrds|Five Elemental Lords]] each administer a domain. Common piety runs through ancestor worship, which treats virtue as spiritual capital and vice as debt.

Against this ordered cosmos stand the [[lore-infrnlrlms|Infernal Realms]], the zones where the [Great Sorting]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"} was left unfinished. Demons, foreign gods and the Unclassified at large are understood as pressure on the borders of the classified world, and imperial ritual and the work of the Wandwo sorcerers are, among other things, the continuous keeping of that border. Other faiths are neither tolerated nor persecuted; they are ignored, since a foreign pantheon is by definition an attempt to classify the same things less well.

## Politics and Power

The political order is the Empire itself. A hereditary Son of Heaven rules from the capital of [[place-zhowen|Zhowen]], on one of the great rivers, through the [[lore-mandatehvn|Mandate of Heaven]], a cosmic appointment, which is a different thing from divine blessing. Beneath him, the Princes (_Zho Zhei_) of the Zhazho hold the four provinces and the armies and balance the throne; the Wandwo sorcerers are the imperial family's owned property and indispensable to its governance; and the Tanchei scholars run the administration. The [[lore-weizhairui|Wei Zhai Rwi]], the court's game of political manipulation, is understood as part of imperial statecraft and not as a corruption of it.

No serious threat has ever reached the Empire from the west. Its worst enemies have come from within, in succession crises, caste revolts and Wandwo conspiracies, or from the east, from Jenchai, the breakaway archipelago whose "rebellious" status has been a thousand-year stalemate of raid and counter-raid.

## Current Situation {#present}

The Emperor on the throne at [[place-zhowen|Zhowen]], [[being-tengrauemp|Tengrau]], is young and newly crowned, and the Retired Emperor, [[being-yanzhortrd|Yanzho]], lives in the background of the court. The young Emperor's chief counselor is the Caste-Registrar, [[being-cstrgstrtnts|Third Son of the Archivist of the Clan of Lanterns Along the Bank]], and the four Princes each mean to be the one who shapes him: [[being-rinzhowngsn|Rinzho]] of Wongsen, the strongest and the most feared; [[being-chengzhodlwn|Chengzho]] of Doulwen, younger, rising fast, a reformer with the young Emperor's ear; and [[being-hozhomwnou|Hozho]] of Mwonou and [[being-tuizhokwzng|Tuizho]] of Kwozheng, bitter rivals of each other. The embassy of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]] asks for direct relations with the Empire, which the court reads as a breach of the Speaker's protocol; Chengzho takes it up, and the court's game decides it ([[scenario-embsydnvrtantsou|Embassy from Dunavarre to Tantsou]]).

The rain has failed in the south. The southern rice harvests have failed several years running, smaller rivers are dry in their beds, and in some districts the hungry [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] have risen against their lords and the magistrates who collect the tribute. The delta is the exception: the water-gifted Wandwo of the Prince of [[affiliation-wongsenprv|Wongsen Province]] bring rain to it, and the dry provinces buy his rice at the price he names. Some at court say his sorcerers are behind the drought, and nobody has proven it.

Every frontier is pressed at once. The warlord [[being-wogenhenzhuo|Wogen Henzhwo]] has united the tribes of [[place-blackwilds|the Black Wilds]], and his riders are in the northern districts; the rebel general [[being-tuijawengyan|Tuija Wengyan]] holds gains in the border provinces; the [[lore-chaizho|Chaizho]] range through the remote northwest, where the Senali and Khazari hold them at heavy cost; and Jenchai's raiders work the eastern and southern coasts. The [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] note gives the court's side of each.

## Trade

The region stands at the eastern end of the Celestial Roads, the overland routes that run west through the Khazrin and reach [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión]] at last. Silk, porcelain, tea, lacquer, jade, spices and finished metalwork go west; silver, gold, amber, coral, glass and horses come east. The imperial bureaucracy taxes, documents and classifies every significant movement of goods, so smuggling is a highly developed profession.

Coastal trade carries less prestige and far more volume. Tantsou trading ships work the southern ports and reach Vedyara's outport at [[place-nilatir|Nilatir]] and the northern coasts of [[place-xerathia|Zerathia]]. The port cities hold large foreign merchant quarters, carefully walled, carefully watched and carefully taxed.

## Neighbors

To the east, across the narrow [[place-easternstrt|Eastern Strait]], is Jenchai. The Empire calls the archipelago a rebellious province; the archipelago calls itself the true inheritor of the Celestial Mandate and the Tantsou ruling house usurpers. The result is a perpetual low war at sea, punctuated by the occasional disaster in either direction.

To the southwest, beyond the eastern arc of the Grazian Mountains, contact with Vedyara is old and substantial and runs on mutual respect with almost no political integration. Vedyaran scholars travel the Tantsou courts and Tantsou monks study Vedyaran philosophical texts, and neither side has ever seriously tried to rule the other.

To the west, beyond the mountain passes and the gates of the wall, the Khazrin Desert is a buffer crossed by caravans and, now and then, by an imperial expedition. The Aureldian kingdoms beyond it are real and remote, a faint rumor of cathedrals and princes.

To the north, the frontier toward the far-northern [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] nests is a fortified marchland of watchtowers and garrison colonies, and there the wall also keeps out the undead hordes. The Empire treats Grukar raids as a nuisance. The steppe beyond, [[place-blackwilds|the Black Wilds]], is another matter this generation.
