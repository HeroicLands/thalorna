The Empire of Tantsou is the oldest continuously governed state in the world. It has held the eastern mainland of [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]] for more than three millennia, and it has watched every other great power on the continent rise, flourish and decline from the cold vantage of an institution that counts its own history in dynasties. The [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] was a republic, became an empire and began its long decline inside fourteen centuries; Tantsou's archive is older than the Vylarian republic by more than eighteen hundred years, and Tantsou takes seven hundred years over one serious succession crisis. It governs the [[place-tantsouregin|Tantsou Region]], some 120 million people, and it holds that it is not a state at all but the mortal face of a cosmic order.

That claim is the key to everything else. The celestial bureaucracy of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Celestial Order]] administers the heavens by the same principles, and through the same offices, that the imperial bureaucracy uses to administer the earth, and the Tantsou hold that these are two faces of one reality and not two systems that happen to resemble each other. Outsiders, missionaries and foreign theologians have argued with the claim for centuries. The Tantsou consider the argument beneath attention.

## At a Glance

The throne
: the Emperor, the **Son of Heaven** (_Tengzhei_), who holds the [[lore-mandatehvn|Mandate of Heaven]] by cosmic appointment and rules by being correctly classified.

The Warlords
: the _Wozhuo_ of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]], each holding a province with its armies and answering to the Emperor alone; the kingmakers of every succession dispute.

The sorcerers
: the [[lore-wanduo|Wanduo]], property of the imperial family, ranked second of the eight castes and owning nothing.

The administration
: the [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] scholars, who read the omens, keep the sacred texts and staff the ministries.

The law
: the [[lore-castelaws|Caste Laws]] and the [[lore-tseising|Teng Lou]], enforced by the Warlords' magistrates; every subject is recorded in the census and every traveler carries a classification tablet.

The frontiers
: [[affiliation-jenchaiempr|Jenchai]] across the eastern strait, the fortified [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukari]] marches to the north, the quiet, expensive passes into the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn]] to the west.

The game
: the [[lore-weizhairui|Wei Zhai Rui]], the court's contest of manipulation, in which every house of consequence is a player.

## The Celestial Administration and the Emperor

Religion, politics, magic and social rank are one system in Tantsou, viewed from different angles. The Emperor is the celestial bureaucracy's appointed administrator of the mortal plane. His line holds the [[lore-mandatehvn|Mandate of Heaven]] (_Tengwo_), which is a cosmic appointment and not a divine blessing: it grants administrative authority over the empire's classification system, and nothing else. The Son of Heaven does not rule by divine right in the Aureldian sense. He rules by being correctly classified, and a line that loses that correct classification, through dynastic decay, ritual failure or the corruption of its household, loses the Mandate to a line more correctly classified.

Power sits in three places. The Emperor and his household hold the ritual center and the final authority. The Warlords, who are [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]] like the Emperor, hold the provinces and the armies, and they have repeatedly made and unmade emperors in succession disputes. The [[lore-wanduo|Wanduo]], the chattel sorcerers who are the legally owned magical slaves of the imperial family, channel the cosmic energy through bureaucratic forms and are indispensable to every function of government that mortal administration alone cannot perform.

## The Hierarchy of Castes

Tantsou society runs in eight castes, which fix a person's profession, legal standing, dress, marriage and the name, if any, by which they may be known. From the highest:

[[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]]
: Sons of the Dragon: the Emperor, his family and the Warlords

[[lore-wanduo|Wanduo]]
: chattel sorcerers, bound to serve the Zhazho

[[lore-wohou|Wohou]]
: warrior nobility: commanders and administrators

[[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]]
: celestial scholars: omen-readers and keepers of the sacred texts

[[lore-tsaihuo|Tsaihuo]]
: artisans, craftsmen and common warriors

[[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]]
: peasant farmers and laborers

[[lore-shuonong|Shuonong]]
: merchants and usurers

[[lore-noutsou|Nuitsou]]
: slaves and outcasts

The [[lore-castelaws|Caste Laws]] set out the rights, duties and restrictions of each. The classification of persons is the subject of the legal code and the foundation of the administration, to a degree no Aureldian kingdom's class system approaches: Tantsou civilization treats the sorting of people as one part of the sorting of reality, and the ministries that track the movements of comets also track the ritual purity of provincial officials.

## The Celestial Order

The religion of Tantsou is a celestial bureaucracy, a vast spiritual administration on which the earthly government is modeled. The Celestial Dragon ([[lore-tanzhodty|Tanzho]]) is the First Classifier, the principle of order itself; the Jade Sovereign ([[lore-weizhuodty|Weizhuo]]), the Minister of Storms ([[lore-raoguidty|Raogui]]), the Minister of the Moon ([[lore-youweidty|Youbui]]) and the [[lore-fvlmntlrds|Five Elemental Lords]] administer their own domains of classified existence. The Emperor's authority flows down from the Mandate of Heaven, and the common people sustain their spiritual lives through [[lore-ancstrwrshp|Ancestor Worship]], a system of moral ledgers in which merit and default are recorded and inherited.

Against the order stand the [[lore-infrnlrlms|Infernal Realms]], the zones where the [Great Sorting]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"} was never finished and the Unclassified presses on the borders of the sorted world. Imperial ritual, the work of the Wanduo and the moral discipline of the whole population are understood as the keeping of that boundary, without which, in the Tantsou view, the cosmos would in time dissolve. The [[lore-celestlrdr|Celestial Order]] note sets out the whole framework.

## History and Current State

The dynastic record the imperial archive can document begins around 2500 BF and runs through more than a dozen dynasties, each a reassignment of the Mandate from a decaying house to a more correctly classified successor. The Celestial Order traces the same line very much further, to [[lore-greatcnvrg|the Great Convergence]] at the zero of the [Celestial Calendar]{.name data-type="lore/calendar"}; the empire holds both accounts at once and has a doctrine for doing so, set out in [[lore-clndrstrlgy|Calendar and Astrology]]. The most recent reassignment is several centuries old. The present dynasty is in the period its own official historians call mature: past the vigor of its founding centuries, not yet in the terminal corruption that would warrant a transfer of the Mandate, and showing the strains of a late dynasty on every frontier.

Those strains are easy to see. [[affiliation-jenchaiempr|Jenchai]] has been a rebellious province for a full millennium, and no imperial expedition has ever reasserted sovereignty over the archipelago; the imperial position is that it remains a province, and the practical reality is that it is a sovereign kingdom that raids the coast. The frontier with the [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm Region]] is a marchland of watchtowers and garrison colonies that needs constant attention, and beyond it the steppe warlord [[being-wogenhenzhuo|Wogen Henzhuo]] has united the tribes of the [Black Wilds]{.name data-type="place/region"} and sent his riders into the northern provinces. The western passes into the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn]] are quiet and expensively garrisoned. Within the empire, caste revolts, Wanduo conspiracies and the manipulations of the [[lore-weizhairui|Wei Zhai Rui]] occupy the court without pause; the general [[being-tuijawengyan|Tuija Wengyan]], condemned for a disobedience that won the battle it defied, is in open revolt, and the house of [[affiliation-yanchaizhao|Yanchai Zhao]] has staked what is left of its standing on a secret alliance with the [[affiliation-haluozhao|Ha Luo Zhao]], the Black Lotus Society.

The Empire's external trade is enormous. It flows west along [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Roads]] through the Khazryn toward [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]], and by sea south toward [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]]. The bureaucracy taxes, documents and classifies every significant movement of goods; smuggling is a highly developed profession; the port cities keep large foreign merchant quarters, carefully walled and watched. No serious threat has ever come from the west. The real ones have come from within, or from Jenchai, whose "rebellious" status is a thousand-year stalemate of raid and counter-raid.
