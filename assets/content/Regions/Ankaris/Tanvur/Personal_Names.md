Most people in the [[affiliation-tanvurempr|Empire of Tānvür]] never have a name of their own. A personal name is an honor the empire grants for outstanding service, and until it is granted, a person is identified by clan, by position within the clan, and by place. The [[lore-nokvur|Nōkvür]], [[lore-zathuk|Zāthük]] and [[lore-shuknurk|Shüknürk]] live and die that way, and so do most of the castes above them.

## Identification Without a Name

An unnamed person is known by a description that a registry clerk could file: "Fourth Son of the Battle Warden of the Clan of Moon Rises Over Still Water, near Vëithāt." It says who his father is and what his father does, which clan holds them, and where they live, and it is exactly what the classification tablet he carries records. In daily life his neighbors shorten it to "Fourth Son" or "the Warden's boy," and a magistrate reads it out in full.

## The Grant of a Name

Only the [[lore-zhekzhos|Zhëkzhös]], the [[lore-vushok|Vüshōk]] and the [[lore-tanthei|Tānthëi]] may be granted personal names, and only for outstanding service or achievement. The grant is a ceremony. A person's liege lord or a senior member of the clan confers it before witnesses, and the registry enters it beside the old description, which is never struck out. The name itself is a phrase describing what the person did, what they are, or what heaven seems to intend for them.

## Warriors' Names

A warrior's name speaks of power, speed and battle, and it is usually drawn from weather, beasts and weapons:

- Zhös Shüt Tëng Jëk—"Dragon's Rain of Heavenly Arrows"
- Bëit Jëk Vüs Rëk—"Ice Blade of Thunderous Battle"
- Vëng Hürt Rëk Thāt—"Wind Tiger Roars Across Thunder Mountain"

## Scholars' Names

A scholar's name speaks of the stars and of insight, and it is usually drawn from the heavens and the auspicious signs:

- Zhīr Zhāt Lëng Thëng—"Rising Bright Star Bird"
- Tëng Zhös Lëng Vürt—"Fifth Auspicious Star of the Celestial Dragon"
- Kōt Vüt Vōk Tëng—"Ancient Tortoise with the Celestial Platform"

## Two Names Earned

A Vüshōk officer known for years as "Second Son of the Shield Bearer of the Zhösvōk Clan, near Tëngmüt" led his troops to a victory that saved a province. His liege lord granted him the name Vëng Hürt Rëk Thāt, "Wind Tiger Roars Across Thunder Mountain," for the fury of his charge, and he has signed nothing else since.

A Tānthëi scholar known as "Third Daughter of the Sage of the Zhīr Zhāt Clan, near Vëithāt" observed and recorded a new star that her seniors had missed. She was granted the name Zhīr Zhāt Lëng Thëng, "Rising Bright Star Bird," for the discovery, and the star carries her name in the court registers.
