The [[affiliation-tanvurempr|Empire of Tānvür]] has always feared its own provinces more than any foreign power, and for most of its history it has been right to. Three frontiers trouble it now. The steppe warlord in the north is the gravest, and [[being-vusgurthurtzhuk|Vüsgürt Hürtzhük]] has his own note. The other two are the old enemy across the eastern strait and the long, thin western passes.

## Thātvōng

**Thātvōng** ("Kingdom of Mountains and Seas") is the empire's name for [[affiliation-jurthatempr|Jürthāt]], the ring of nine volcanic islands across the strait to the east. The archipelago broke from the empire more than a thousand years ago, and the court has never recognized the break: in every register it is still a province in rebellion. The islanders keep the empire's script, calendar and castes, bent around a warrior aristocracy, and they call themselves the true holders of the Mandate and the mainland's ruling house usurpers.

The war between them is a thousand-year stalemate of raid and counter-raid. The strait has wrecked every invasion fleet the empire has sent, and Jürthāt has never had the numbers to hold ground on the mainland. What it can do is take the sea. Its navy is better than the empire's, and it hires mercenary captains and pirates to raid the eastern and southern coasts, seize the empire's offshore islands and fishing grounds, and choke the coastal trade on which the southern ports live. A Jürthāti flotilla off a harbor mouth in the rice-shipping season costs the empire more than a lost battle.

## The Western Passes

West of the mountains lies the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn]], a waste of ruins and owned oases that the [[lore-celestialroad|Celestial Roads]] cross on their way to [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]]. No army from the west has ever reached the empire across it, and the Aurèldían kingdoms beyond are a rumor at court. The danger from the west is smaller and closer. The oasis-holders who sell water and safe-conduct to the caravans play the empire against the western traders, raise their tolls when the frontier garrisons are thin, and shelter whoever pays them: smugglers running silk out without the bureaus' seal, Shadow Sect sorcerers fleeing the [[lore-venduk|Vëndük]] compounds, and Ordo agents bringing gold in. Every rogue sorcerer who crosses the passes is one the empire cannot recover, and every one the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] receives tells it something about the Vëndük it wants to know.
