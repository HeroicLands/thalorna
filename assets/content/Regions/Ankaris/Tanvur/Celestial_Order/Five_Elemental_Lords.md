A ship's captain in a Tānvüri port burns a petition to the Bureau of Air before he sails, a smith burns one to the Bureau of Transformation before he lights a new forge, and neither of them would call it prayer. The **Five Elemental Lords** are the bureau chiefs of the [[lore-clstlpnthn|Celestial Pantheon]], each holding the portfolio of one of the five material domains that make up the physical world in the faith of the [[affiliation-tanvurempr|Empire of Tānvür]]. They were appointed at the Great Sorting to keep the elements in their categories and the physical world from dissolving back into the Hürnlëi, and the Tānvüri petition them as they would petition a magistrate.

The [[lore-phlsphylvr|Lëivōr philosophy]] recognizes seven domains of classification in total, but only five are material—Air, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water—and each of these is administered by an Elemental Lord. The remaining two domains are too fundamental to the classification system itself for any single bureau chief to hold: **Spirit (Vōrlëi)** is governed collectively by the celestial bureau system (particularly the Ministry of Final Account and the Bureau of Night Counsel), and **Neutral (Hōk)** is the domain of Tānzhös himself—the unsorted energy that sustains all sorting.

Each Elemental Lord governs a domain established by Tānzhös during the [[affiliation-tngvkrrklei|Dāk Zhāklëi]] and serves beneath the senior officials of the pantheon. Their authority belongs to their posts: they administer because the classification assigned them to.

## Vëngdāk Zhütvōr—Azure Wind of Sacred Breath

- **Element:** Air
- **Domain:** Breath, Communication, and the Medium of Classification
- **Bureau:** Administers the channels through which all classifications flow—the carrier medium that connects sorted things to each other and prevents the classified world from stagnating.

Vëngdāk Zhütvōr is the administrator of the Air domain, responsible for ensuring that Lëi flows between classifications rather than pooling or stagnating. Wind is the circulation of Lëi through the classified world, keeping sorted things connected. A windless day is the circulation slowing, and a gale is the circulation running fast; a storm's thunder and its rain belong to the Minister of Storms, and the wind that carries them belongs to this bureau.

He is depicted as a towering figure in billowing robes, his form half-dissolved into wind, with a great war banner that snaps in a gale that affects nothing else around him. His weapon is a spear that trails a perpetual streamer of cloud. Petitions filed with his bureau request favorable winds for travel and sailing, the dispersal of miasma and stagnant air, and, above all, the unimpeded flow of communication across distances. Messages, omens, and bureaucratic dispatches all travel through his domain.

Sailors, messengers, and those who depend on the movement of goods and information have the most frequent administrative contact with this bureau. The [[lore-nokvur|Nōkvür]] caste petitions for favorable conditions at planting and harvest, when the proper circulation of air is critical to crop health.

## Rīrnhöt Dākvëng—Blazing Flame of Glorious Fire

- **Element:** Fire
- **Domain:** Fire, Transformation, and Transmission
- **Bureau:** The Bureau of Transformation ([[affiliation-tngvkrrklei|Höt Sëi]])—administers all processes that convert things from one classification to another, including the critical function of spirit-burning that transmits documents to the celestial filing system.

Rīrnhöt Dākvëng governs the element that matters most in daily practice, because fire is the channel between the mortal and celestial planes. Every spirit document burned, every offering transmitted, every petition filed through flame passes through his bureau's processing channels. Without the Bureau of Transformation functioning properly, the mortal plane would lose its administrative link to the celestial bureaucracy entirely.

He is depicted as an armored figure wreathed in flames, with a burning sword that represents the transformative power of reclassification—the capacity to change one sorted thing into another. His bureau also handles the more destructive aspects of fire, treating conflagrations and volcanic activity as transformation events that must be processed and contained.

Smiths, who transform raw ore into metal through fire, are understood to be performing a physical act of reclassification and therefore maintain close administrative ties to this bureau. The [[lore-venduk|Vëndük]] who channel cosmic energy through bureaucratic forms frequently address their most complex workings to the Bureau of Transformation, since transformation is the most common magical effect.

## Dāktūr Vōrthāt—Warm Stone of Deep Earth

- **Element:** Earth
- **Domain:** Stability, Foundation, and Persistence
- **Bureau:** Administers the classification of all stable, enduring material—stone, soil, minerals, and the structural foundations that keep sorted things in place.

Dāktūr Vōrthāt is the administrator of the Earth domain, responsible for the _persistence_ of classification. Where other elements govern change, flow, or transformation, Earth governs the resistance to change—the structural inertia that prevents sorted things from drifting out of their categories. The Tānvüri say a mountain stands because Dāktūr Vōrthāt's bureau keeps it classified as solid, enduring and here.

He is depicted as a massive, solid figure, with skin like stone and eyes like deep ancient caves. His weapon is a hammer made from the heart of the earth, representing the bureau's capacity to enforce stability. The Tānthëi read an earthquake as a disruption in the Earth domain that needs correcting, and never as a punishment, and the empire's ministries of earthquakes work from that reading.

Builders, miners, and engineers file petitions with this bureau for structural integrity, safe excavation, and the stability of foundations. The [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazári]], whose legendary mastery of stone and engineering falls within this domain, have a complex relationship with the bureau—they work within its jurisdiction when operating in the empire, while understanding their craft through an entirely different framework within their own enclaves.

## Vōrjīk Jëk—Sword of Pure Metal

- **Element:** Metal
- **Domain:** Distinction, Boundary, and Enforcement
- **Bureau:** Administers the classification of boundaries and separations—the sharp lines between categories, the enforcement of distinctions, and the domain of law and judgment.

Vōrjīk Jëk governs the element that _draws the lines_. Metal is the domain of classification at its most precise: the blade that separates one category from another, the boundary that prevents sorted things from blurring together. This makes his bureau the elemental counterpart to the legal system—where the Tëng Lüt defines caste boundaries in law, Vōrjīk Jëk's bureau maintains those boundaries in the cosmic classification system.

He is depicted as a warrior clad in gleaming armor, holding a sword that shines with the light of a thousand stars—the blade of distinction that separates what is classified from what is not. His bureau processes petitions related to judgment, precision, the forging of tools and weapons, and the maintenance of clean boundaries between categories.

Weaponsmiths and judges alike petition this bureau. Warriors of the [[lore-vushok|Vüshōk]] caste, who enforce the empire's classification boundaries through force, have a natural administrative relationship with Vōrjīk Jëk's domain—they are the mortal enforcement arm, as his bureau is the elemental one.

## Zhütshüt Zhös—Blue Water Dragon

- **Element:** Water
- **Domain:** Flow, Transition, and Adaptability
- **Bureau:** Administers the classification of all processes involving flow, transition, and the movement of Lëi between categories—rivers, tides, the circulatory systems of living things, and the transitions between sorted states.

Zhütshüt Zhös governs the element that carries Lëi between classifications, enabling things to change from one sorted state to another without passing through the Unsorted. Water is the medium of change, the domain that lets the classification move. Without Water's bureau functioning, sorted things would remain frozen in their categories, unable to grow, change, or adapt.

He is depicted as a majestic dragon, his scales shimmering in shades of blue and turquoise, with a sinuous body that flows like a river that moves without ever leaving its course, as Water's changes stay within the sorted world and never fall into the Hürnlëi. His bureau processes petitions for safe passage by water, healing (which is a reclassification of the body from disordered to ordered), purification, and the regulation of tides and rivers.

Sailors, healers, and the Yātvōr physicians of the [[lore-zathuk|Zāthük]] caste maintain close administrative ties to this bureau. The healing arts in particular draw heavily on the Water domain, since restoring a body's health is understood as realigning its internal classification channels—a process that Zhütshüt Zhös's bureau administers.
