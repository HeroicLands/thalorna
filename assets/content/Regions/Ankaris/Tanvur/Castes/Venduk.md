**Chattel Sorcerers**

Though technically enslaved, the Vëndük occupy a unique and highly respected position within [[affiliation-tanvurempr|Tānvür]] society. Born with powerful innate magical abilities, they are bound to the service of [[lore-zheklung|Zhëklüng]] from birth. Their magical mastery makes them invaluable counselors, artificers, and ritualists, elevating them far above the status of common slaves despite their legal bondage.

## Roles and Responsibilities

### Advisors and Protectors

Vëndük counsel the highest ranks of Tānvür leadership on matters of magic, military strategy, and spiritual balance. Their arcane knowledge directly shapes imperial decision-making and the defense of the realm.

### Arcane Artificers

Vëndük craft magical artifacts and weapons imbued with elemental energies, creating tools of war and governance that lesser craftsmen cannot replicate. These creations are treasured throughout the empire.

### Ritual Magicians

Vëndük perform powerful rituals capable of influencing weather patterns, strengthening or disrupting the classification of lands and territories, and even manipulating time flow itself. Such rituals require both precision and immense magical reserves, making Vëndük irreplaceable in matters requiring arcane intervention.

## Magical Monopoly

The Vëndük possess an absolute and legally enforced monopoly on the practice of magic within the empire. Non-Vëndük discovered practicing magical arts face immediate execution. This restriction ensures that arcane power remains consolidated under Zhëklüng control and that no rival magical practitioners can emerge to threaten imperial stability.

## Rights and Restrictions

### Rights

- **Protected Status**: Valued and protected by Zhëklüng; their lives are more precious than those of lower castes
- **Magical Authority**: Exclusive right to perform rituals, craft artifacts, and offer arcane counsel to the empire
- **Autonomy in Service**: Within their magical purview, Vëndük exercise considerable independence in how they accomplish their assigned tasks
- **Practice of Magic**: The only caste legally permitted to engage in magical practice

### Restrictions

- **Bound Status**: Legally enslaved to their Zhëklüng masters; cannot leave their estates without permission
- **Absolute Loyalty**: Disobedience is met with execution or permanent severing of magical abilities
- **Prohibition of Marriage**: Vëndük cannot enter legal marriages; they may engage in sexual relations with other Vëndük only with their masters' consent
- **Cannot Hold Property**: All possessions and earnings belong to their masters

## Identification and Selection

All children throughout [[affiliation-tanvurempr|Tānvür]], regardless of caste, are tested at age ten for magical inclinations and potential. Those showing signs of magical affinity are identified, claimed, and converted to the Vëndük caste, separating them from their families of origin.

Vëndük children who fail to demonstrate magical ability during this period are not returned to their birth families; instead, they are adopted into the [[lore-nokvur|Nōkvür]] caste with love and respect, providing them with a path to a productive life despite their unique origins.

## Threats to the Order

The empire remains vigilant against rebellious nobles who have attempted to leverage Vëndük power for their own ends. Historical records show that insurgent Warlords have brought loyal Vëndük under their banners, including practitioners with formidable command over elemental forces—fire and earth mages, wielders of air magic and spirit manipulation—posing significant threats to imperial stability when factional conflicts arise.

## Elder Races as Vëndük

The Vëndük caste maintains constant vigilance against potential influence from [[lore-flksinale|Sinalë]] and [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazári]] magic. The possibility that elder race sorcerers might exert unchecked power within the empire creates persistent tension between the magical castes.
