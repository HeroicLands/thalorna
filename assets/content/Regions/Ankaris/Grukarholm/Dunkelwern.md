> **GM-only note.** The fur hunters of the margin know that a nest stands on a dark lake in the marsh east of Grimhreth, and that smoke rises over it in some seasons. What it hunts, what presses on it and what lies on its shore are for the GM.

## Overview {#overview}

**Dunkelwern**, the dark lake, is the name the Varokh fur hunters of [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]] give a lake of black peat water deep in the western marsh of [[place-skathengrund|Skathengrund]], the Drowned Country, east of [[place-skathak|Skathak]], and the Grukar nest on its shore. It lies farther into the marsh than the end of [[place-grimhreth|Grimhreth]] or any canoe-way a fur hunter uses, within [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm Region]]. Open water, reed-beds and low islands of alder and birch surround it, and no cause-walk reaches it. A party comes to it by canoe through the reed or on foot over the winter ice.

:::secret {title="The rise"}
The nest stands on a low rise of sand and firm peat on the lake's north shore, the one dry ground for a long way in any direction. Its longhouses are long and well kept, roofed with reed, and its spawn-chamber is dug into the crown of the rise. Below it the tribe's canoes lie drawn up on a landing of trodden peat. The tribe numbers several hundred, more than most nests of the western marsh, and the lake feeds it better than the margin feeds its neighbors.
:::

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

One tribe of the [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] holds the lake, centered on its spawner. Its canoes are the only boats on the open water, and its hunters work the reed-beds round the shore. No other nest is seen on the lake.

## Layers {#history}

No fur hunter of the margin remembers a time before the nest on the lake. The hunters know it by the smoke that rises over the marsh east of Grimhreth in some seasons and not in others, and by what the few who have gone in over the ice have seen from the edge of the open water.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The nest holds the lake, and nobody from the forest edge or the forts goes near it. The fur hunters who work the margin watch for the smoke. [[being-brlndvshv|Brelana Duvashov]] asks at every landing where the [[being-ncrtcmr|Necrotic Mire]] was last seen, and a column of smoke over the marsh east of Grimhreth is one of her answers. [[being-skgrdbrshv|Sokogor Dobrashov]] reads the paths from the marsh for the warden of [[place-sdrvdfrt|Sedrovod Fort]], and the gray reeds of the Mire's track are among them.

:::secret {title="The hunting range"}
The nest hunts about a league out from its rise: the open water, the reed-beds round the shore and the islands of alder and birch, by canoe in summer and over the ice in winter. It takes fish, waterfowl and their eggs, and the small game of the islands. The Ahk paces the hunt, and in a lean season the hunters leave the nesting reed alone. To the west its league meets the league of [[place-skathak|Skathak]], and the hunters of the two nests fight on the open water where they meet. How long a league takes by canoe through the reed is for the GM to set.

Raiding is another matter. The nest's raiding parties go out by the canoe-ways to the fur hunters' camps on the margin, well beyond the league it hunts, and how far that is from the lake is for the GM to set.
:::

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The [[being-ncrtcmr|Necrotic Mire]] moves through the marsh, and the nests keep off its track, the [Black Road]{.name data-type="place/feature"}: a road of gray, rotted reed and dead water across the bog. The marsh tribes burn reed against it.

A [[being-gntbvr|Giant Beaver]] family holds the lake's outflow with a dam of stripped trunks higher than a man, and attacks anything that comes onto the dam or into the water beside it.

Other nests lie in the marsh round the lake.

:::secret {title="What hems the nest in"}
The Black Road runs to the lake from the south, and the Mire has learned the lake. Its landing is where the tribe's canoes put in and out every day, the kind of crossing where prey comes and the Mire waits, and the Mire comes back along its road whenever it has healed. The tribe does what the marsh tribes do: it burns the reed-beds in front of the Mire, and the burned strip turns it aside. Each burning costs the tribe the reed where its waterfowl nest and its fish spawn, the reed grows back slowly, and the dead water the Mire leaves behind kills the fish in the lake's southern bay for a season. The tribe cannot fight the Mire hand to hand, and it does not touch the beavers, whose dam holds the lake at the level its canoes need. To the west lies Skathak's league, where any hunting the tribe takes is taken by fighting.
:::

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A party that reaches the lake finds a ring of burned reed round the shore, black stubble standing in the shallows, and the smell of old smoke over the water. On the far shore the reeds stand gray and rotted, and in the water beside them nothing lives. The nest's canoes cross the open water at dawn and dusk, and the longhouses stand on the rise above them. The smell of the nest reaches a party before the longhouses do. The tribe fights hardest at the spawn-chamber, and the Grukar on the lake do not take prisoners or trade. A person taken is eaten.

:::secret {title="What lies on the shore"}
The burned shore is the treasure. The burning has stripped the reed and the turf from the peat of the lake's eastern shore, the spring flood washes the bared peat out, and [[miscgear-amber|amber]] lies in the shallows and in the cut face of the peat in lumps from a thumbnail to a fist. The Grukar have no use for it, and it lies where the water leaves it. Amber goes down the rivers from Vristwald beside the furs, and a bed like this one is worth more than a fur party's season, but it lies inside the nest's league with the Mire's road on the far shore.

The Black Road itself carries what the Mire could not eat. Spearheads, knife blades and axe-heads, pitted and eaten, lie on the surface of the bog along its track in a line, where they dropped out of the Mire as it went, the iron of the hunters and the Grukar it has taken in the marsh east of Grimhreth.
:::

:::secret {title="Why the nest does not last"}
The nest's water is failing. The lake feeds the tribe well in every season the Mire stays away, and the Mire comes back more often than the reed grows. The Ahk answers as an Ahk does: it rations, lays Uk instead of Hushk, lays fewer, and sends its hunters farther out, west into Skathak's league, where they meet Skathak's hunters.

Two endings stand open, and the GM chooses. In a dry summer the tribe burns the last standing reed and the Mire comes up the landing onto the rise anyway; the tribe fights it with fire, and what the Mire leaves dies with the fish in the fouled water. Or a Sha sees the end coming, gathers a second Sha and a Hushk with a grievance, and carries a proto-Ahk out into unclaimed land, while the nest left behind declines in good order and dies on its rise all the same. Either way the dead nest leaves the amber of its shore to whoever reaches it first, with the Mire still on its road.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **Smoke over the open water.** [[being-brlndvshv|Brelana Duvashov]] has seen smoke rising over the marsh east of Grimhreth three days running, and she wants a party to go in far enough to see which way the Black Road runs before she takes her boat along the stretch of margin nearest the smoke.
- **The road nobody walks.** [[being-skgrdbrshv|Sokogor Dobrashov]] has followed the gray reeds of the Black Road in from the marsh edge and found no tracks on it, Grukar or any other: a way toward the lake that no nest watches, with the Mire somewhere along it.
