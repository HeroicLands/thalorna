## Overview {#overview}

**Volkobor** is a low rise of dry ground in the deep forest belt of [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm Region]], on its Velanthian side, between the eastern edge of [[place-skathengrund|Skathengrund]] and the pine ridges above the rivers, several days' walk beyond the last of the forts. Its name is [[skill-velanthlng|Velanthian]], "the wolf pine wood", from _volk_, a wolf, and _-obor_, a pine wood, and the fur hunters who go out past the forts gave it. On the rise a dozen oaks of a girth no other oak in Velanthia reaches stand in a loose ring, every one of them hollow, among the gray trunks of pines that have been dead for many years. Between the oaks lie the fallen frames of [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] longhouses, and no smoke has risen from the rise in the fur hunters' memory.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody holds it. The wolves that give it its name den in the hollow oaks, and hunting parties from the nests round about cross the rise without stopping.

## Layers {#history}

The oldest fur hunters say their fathers saw smoke over the rise and kept away from it, and that the smoke stopped one winter and did not come back. They say the Grukar did not hollow the oaks, and that whoever did it worked without an axe, because the doorways in the trunks are rimmed with bark grown smooth over the edges. A hunter who sheltered in one of the oaks from a storm came out with the fever and said the tree had spoken to him; the others say he had the fever before he went in.

## What Is There Now {#present}

Last summer a fur hunter came out past [[place-trvgrfrt|Torvogor Fort]] with an iron helmet he had dug out of the fallen longhouses, and the helmet bore the mark of the [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]]. The warden wants to know whether the gear of other men the forts have lost lies there too, and he has not the men to send. This spring the fur hunters have seen a new column of smoke a few miles north of the rise, where none stood before.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The wolves of the rise are many and bold, and they come out of the oaks at dusk. Hunting parties of the nests cross the rise, and the new smoke to the north is near. The ground round the greatest of the oaks is sunken and cracked, and it gives underfoot.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A rise of sandy ground among the pines, where the pines are dead: gray trunks with the bark stripped off in a ring at chest height, standing or fallen across one another. Inside them, the oaks. Each is broad enough for a family to sleep inside, and each has a doorway on its southern side, round-headed and rimmed with smooth bark. Inside, the hollow is dry and swept bare by the wolves, and its walls are smooth wood with no mark of a blade. The greatest oak stands in the middle of the ring, with a doorway wide enough for two to walk through abreast and a space inside like a small hall.

Between the oaks lie the collapsed frames of long, low houses of pine poles, their roofs fallen in, with bones, antler and broken pots on their floors. A pit opens among the roots of the greatest oak, its edges fallen in, and a smell of old earth comes out of it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The warden's helmet.** The warden of Torvogor Fort pays for any gear that bears the princedom's mark and for word of whose it was, and pays more to know how it came to the rise.
- **The new smoke.** The fur hunters want to know what the smoke to the north is before they work that stretch of the margin again.
- **The fevered hunter.** The hunter who heard the tree speak wants someone to go back with him and prove to the others that he did.

# The Truth of Volkobor {#truth .secret}

**The enclave.** The rise is **Koldisto**, "the place of the hall", an enclave of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]], overrun. Its people worked their dwellings into the living oaks of the rise, one hearth to a tree, and the greatest oak was the enclave's hall. They led the trees for centuries before the first Grukar came into the forest, and the oaks grew to their girth in the enclave's keeping.

**Its overrunning.** As the nests filled the forest, a tribe budding out of the marsh came up onto the rise. The enclave held against it for a season. Then its people went west through the forest toward the deep woods of [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]], and the tribe took the oaks.

**The nest.** The tribe ringed the pines to kill them for longhouse poles, walled the oaks' doorways with mud and used them as store-rooms, and dug its spawn-chamber down among the roots of the hall-oak. The digging broke into the course of the spring that fed the rise from under that oak. The spring sank and failed, the tribe carried its water from farther and farther off, and its Ahk drew in the hunting and laid fewer clutches until two Sha and a Hushk cut a proto-Ahk out of the chamber and took it away with a band of Uk. The old tribe died on the rise. Whether the new smoke to the north is the nest that band founded, or another tribe's, is the game master's to set.

**What is left.** The tribe kept what its raids brought home. Under the fallen longhouses lie spearheads, axes, a [[miscgear-ploughshare|ploughshare]] or two, iron pots, and the war-gear of men taken on the margin and eaten, the Brevavesh helmet among it. The pit under the hall-oak is the spawn-chamber, half fallen in, and the roots that hold the hall-oak up run through it.

**The oak.** The hollow oak the fevered hunter slept in is a dwelling like the others, and nothing in it speaks; the wind across its doorway sounds through the hollow like a voice. The hall-oak is undermined by the spawn-chamber and leans a little more each year. A party that digs in the pit for the nest's leavings brings down the roof of the chamber, and perhaps the oak.

> [!TIP]
> The rise's iron is the reward and the wolves are the guard. The spring under the hall-oak, the season the enclave held, its people's road west and the band that carried the proto-Ahk away are this note's own account; a game master may change them.
