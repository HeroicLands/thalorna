Grukarholm names a landlocked concentration of [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] nests amid dense mixed and coniferous forest, roadless marshland, slow brown rivers, and shallow lakes. It overlies the northwestern part of [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia]] and the eastern edge of [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]], and it lies south of [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]], southeast of Nordheim and southwest of [[place-malagna|Malagna]]. It is the easternmost part of Vristwald and the westernmost part of Velanthia, and no coast or drawn border fixes its extent. Its forests and swamps impede organized armies for most of the year. It is a region and not a state: the Grukar have no structure or cooperation outside their own nests, and the area is simply infested by them, so the humans gave it a name. The place is a shifting mosaic of Grukar tribal ground, not a confederation or a kingdom. It is laid over parts of two lands and has no polity of its own: every fort, village and landing within it belongs to Velanthia, under its river-princes, or to Vristwald, among the villages of the [[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vristwald Tribes]].

## Geography

Grukarholm is an area of prevalence rather than a bounded territory. Its western part lies within the eastern edge of Vristwald's forest, around [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]]; its eastern part lies within the northwestern reaches of Velanthia. No line separates it from either. The terrain where Grukar are thickest has three interlocking landscapes:

The **deep forest belt** covers most of the region—dense mixed and coniferous woodland (spruce, pine, oak, birch) interspersed with clearings and old fields long since reclaimed by trees. The forest is the principal Grukar habitat: the longhouses, the war-camps, the spawn-chambers of the [[being-grkrahk|Grukar-ahk]], and the hunting-grounds all sit within forest cover. Roads through the forest belt are few, narrow, and easily blocked.

The **marshlands** of the central and southern lowlands are the region's most distinctive geographic feature—a vast complex of swamp, peat-bog, slow water, and seasonal flood that covers perhaps a quarter of the total territory. The marshes are passable on foot only in the depths of winter when they freeze, or in a few weeks of dry summer; the rest of the year they are functionally roadless. The Varokh call them [[place-skathengrund|Skathengrund]], and the Nordmal, who have learned not to send armies into them, call them the Drowned Country. Grukar settlements within the marshlands are reached by canoe, by cause-walks of sunken logs, or by knowledge of paths invisible to outsiders.

The **river systems**—the slow brown rivers that wind across the country toward the open Velanthian grain country—provide what long-distance transport the region has. Grukar war-canoes move warriors and goods along these rivers in summer; in winter the frozen rivers become the highways of overland movement. The rivers also carry raiding parties toward the Velanthian reaches. A landing can be watched or attacked, but it is no meeting place for an agreed exchange.

The climate is continental: cold snowy winters, warm humid summers, distinct spring thaw and autumn freeze. It is not the killing extreme of the far-northern taiga—Grukarholm's winters are survivable, by Grukar standards even comfortable—but the marshlands' summer mosquitoes are legendary, and the spring thaw turns the country into an impassable mire for weeks every year.

## There Is No Kingdom

**Grukarholm is not a polity, has no ruler, no capital and no borders anyone drew.** It is a name given
to a country from outside, by people describing where the Grukar are.

The land came by the name honestly enough. Nobody else wanted it—cold, wet, forested, marshy,
agriculturally worthless—so nobody else took it, and over a long time it filled up with Grukar. Naming
it after them is simple accuracy on a chart. It is not a statement about how they are organized, and
Nordmen and Vristwaldik usage that speaks of "the Grukar king" is a convenience of grammar with nothing
behind it.

**Ahks do not cooperate.** Every tribe is its own thing, centered on its own spawner, and there is no
mechanism by which two of them combine, ally, treat, or acknowledge a superior. There are scores of
tribes in this country. There is no confederation of them, there has never been one, and one would be the worst calamity the north could suffer.

## Fifty Thousand

The whole region holds perhaps **fifty thousand Grukar**, which surprises everyone who has met one.

They are foragers—hunters, trappers, fishers, gatherers of what the marshes give—and forager
densities are low everywhere. Grukar densities are lower than human ones, because a Grukar tribe converts
food into warriors at a rate no human band attempts: a [[being-grkrhshk|Hushk]] clutch is an enormous quantity of meat, and
the dosing cannot outrun the larder. Fifty thousand spread through the forests and marshes along the eastern edge of Vristwald and the northwestern reaches of Velanthia means
tribes of a few hundred to a couple of thousand, and a raiding party of dozens.

**They hold themselves there.** The endless Grukar-on-Grukar fighting that outsiders take for mindless
savagery is, functionally, the thing that keeps this country habitable: tribes that outgrow their range
fission, tribes that crowd each other fight, and the population is pinned below what the land will bear
by its own violence. Left alone, the Grukar do not eat their country bare. They never get the chance.

Which is also why they have never been more than a frontier problem to anybody. There is no shortage of
ferocity in Grukarholm. There is a permanent, structural shortage of **concentration**.

## Peoples and Culture

The region holds about fifty thousand Grukar in many separate tribes, each centered on one fertile Ahk. The Ahk chooses which caste each clutch becomes. [[being-grukaruk|Uk]] work and fight with what they know at hatching; Hushk learn from battle; [[being-grkrsh|Sha]] can plan beyond the next fight and carry an idea back to the nest. None of those differences creates a ruler over another tribe. A nest's stores, watch paths and spawn-chamber belong to that tribe alone.

Humans appear at the margins as hunters for furs, and as soldiers or fugitives passing through dangerous ground, not as a settled client population of the nests. The Grukar take people in raids and eat them. Human flesh is a delicacy to them; it is distinct from the rite in which an Ahk consumes a Grukar's nervous tissue to pass acquired knowledge into a later clutch. A captured human is not kept as a servant or held for ransom.

## Each Tribe Alone

No Ahk acknowledges another as a superior, and no tribe can make terms for its neighbors. Tribes grow and split when a faction carries an infertile Ahk beyond its parent's suppressing influence; the new spawner establishes a new nest rather than a province of the old one. Their collisions are local and frequent. The name Grukarholm describes where nests are thickest, not a kingdom, a league or a single front an army can defeat.

## The Frontier Gives Nothing Back

There is no trade, envoy or ransom with a Grukar nest. An Uk cannot learn a bargain; a Hushk understands the immediate fight but not the obligation beyond it. Only a Sha can take in an outsider's words, and it uses that knowledge for its own spawner. Hearing a Sha speak is not a parley or a promise of safe passage. No neighbor takes Grukar as slaves either: the Uk cannot be taught new work, the Hushk learns only tactics, and a captive Sha would work toward escape.

The result is a costly frontier without the returns other frontiers offer. No captive can be sold, no captured neighbor bought home, and no market repays the labor of keeping watch. Along [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]], villages store less and spend more on their walls; a path through the nests has no protected stopping place. The account of caste learning, appetite and the Ahk's inheritance rite belongs to the [[lore-grukarfolk|folk note]].

## Sites: the Nests {#places}

The region's sites are the Grukar nests, and nothing else in it is a site of the Grukar country's own. A nest is a tribe's ground: its longhouses, its war-camps and the spawn-chamber of its [[being-grkrahk|Grukar-ahk]]. Where the nests lie is the knowledge that matters most to anyone going in. The hunters of the margin, the Skathwald villages and the wardens of the forts can each name the nests they know, and many more are known to nobody.

That knowledge does not keep. Nests appear and die: a tribe that outgrows its ground buds a new nest at the edge of unclaimed land, and a tribe whose place fails dies in it. A list of nests from a local is never complete and never current; a nest a party is told of may be gone, and a new one may stand where none was. Finding a nest nobody knows, clearing a known one, and recovering what a dead one left behind are each an adventure.

The nests known as the setting stands:

- The nests of [[place-skathengrund|Skathengrund]], the Drowned Country, the marsh at the center of the region. They are scattered through it and reached by canoe, by cause-walks of sunken logs, or by paths no outsider can see.
- [[place-skathak|Skathak]], the nest at the far end of [[place-grimhreth|Grimhreth]], a cause-walk of sunken logs that this nest lays from the forest of [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]] into the marsh.
- [[place-lervobor|Lervobor]], the nests upstream of [[place-zvekbregad|Zvek Bregad]], whose raiding canoes come down the rivers to that landing in summer. The landing itself is a Velanthian bank.
- [[place-grimak|Grimak]], the blood-field in Skathwald's marsh, where the Skathwald villages burned a nest with its spawner in it, and [[place-thraholt|Thraholt]], where a new tribe is budding at the thicket's edge. Grimak is what a dead nest leaves, and the budding nest is where a new one stands.

The forts within the region are Velanthian: [[place-trvgrfrt|Torvogor Fort]] and [[place-sdrvdfrt|Sedrovod Fort]] are the paid forts of the [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]]. They stand inside the region, on the Velanthian edge of the thickest Grukar country, and they watch the rivers and the raiding paths. The Skathwald villages that face the nests on the west are Varokh villages of Vristwald.

## Current Situation {#present}

The nests hold the country and no neighbor contests it with an army. The Skathwald villages spend on their walls. On the Velanthian side the stone forts of the [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]] watch the raiding paths from the southeast. Their prince, [[being-krdgrbrvgrch|Krodogor Brevogorich]], appoints every warden and cannot pay them on time, and the wardens, paid, tired and owed months, sell a night's passage to the fur hunters who work the margins. The hunters come out with what they can carry, and a hunter who goes in past the last fort is on his own: no warden follows him and no nest bargains with him. The warden of [[place-trvgrfrt|Torvogor Fort]], [[being-krvgrlrvkv|Karvogor Lervikov]], keeps the bell and the roll for the landing at [[place-zvekbregad|Zvek Bregad]], and [[being-skthrkrvndvth|Skathrik ira Vandvith wur Mahnreth]], a Varokh hunter, crosses the cause-walk at [[place-grimhreth|Grimhreth]] to reach the margin.

Raids come as small parties along the rivers and the cause-walks, and the villages beneath the forts ring their bells and burn signal fires as each party passes. The tribes' own fighting keeps their numbers below what the land could feed, and none of them has combined with another.

## History {#history}

The country's history is told through two of its worst disasters. About 532 AF a nest woke in a marsh thicket in Skathwald and took three villages in a winter ([[lore-grimakburning|The Burning at Grimak]]). From 604 to 614 a tribe from the marsh country burned the east of Targud ([[lore-burningeast|The Burning of the East]]).

## Relations with Neighboring Regions

Grukarholm lies south of [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]] and southwest of [[place-malagna|Malagna]] in the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]]; [[place-targud|Targud]] lies farther northeast and has no border with it, and the rest of the Nordlands lies beyond them and beyond the western Vristwald reaches. Nordmal accounts remember Grukar raids and punitive expeditions, but no Nordmal kingdom draws a line around the Grukar nests. The worst raid in their memory was one tribe and not a league: from 604 to 614 a tribe whose Sha had watched [[affiliation-kingdmtrgd|Targud]]'s frontier for seasons spawned for war, burned the kingdom's east and then spent itself in its own quarrels ([[lore-burningeast|The Burning of the East]]).

The eastern edge of Vristwald lies within this area. The stretch called Skathwald is where Grukar nests become more prevalent within Varokh forest, with no border to cross. Grukar-Varokh relations are uniformly hostile. Villages there endure repeated small incursions rather than a war with a single front, and a person taken cannot be recovered through ransom.

Velanthia lies on the southeastern side of the thickest Grukar country, which overlies its northwestern reaches. Velanthia is raided from the nests and avoids them. Its river-princes, chiefly the prince of [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Brevavesh]], pay for stone forts against the Grukar, the wardens are paid, tired and corruptible, and the villages beneath the forts ring bells and burn signal fires along the same raiding paths the Varokh watch. A Sha may understand a warning, but no Velanthian prince can make an agreement that binds a nest or the other tribes behind it.
