## Overview {#overview}

**Dorgenstein** is a tor of gray granite that stands out of the spruce in the forest belt of northern [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm Region]], in the eastern forest of [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]], several days into the trees from the clearings of [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]]. Its name is [[skill-varokhlng|Varokhi]], "the giants' crag", from _dorg-_, one of the great ones of the old wood, the joining _-en-_, and _-stein_, a crag. The tor is a heap of rounded blocks the height of a long hall, split from crown to foot by a single cleft, and steps run up the cleft. The fur hunters of [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]] and the trappers of Norgaad both know it, and both know that a [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] nest holds it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

A Grukar nest holds the tor and the forest round it. Its fires burn in the cleft, its watchers sit on the crown, and its hunting parties come and go by the steps. No other people keeps a camp within a long day of it.

## Layers {#history}

The Varokh tell that the giants of the old wood cut the steps for their own feet, and that the Grukar came on the crag standing empty and took it. The trappers of Norgaad say only that the crag has had a nest in it for as long as anyone has trapped that far south. When the nests of the south pressed on the kingdom, the [[affiliation-kingdmnrgd|Kingdom of Norgaad]] called the moot, and a company the moot sent into the forest went up against the cleft and came home without taking it.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The nest has raided north this autumn. Its parties have taken stock from the most southerly clearings of Norgaad and a trapper from his lines, and the Skathwald hunters who work the forest's northern edge have found its tracks across their own paths twice since the first snow. The trapper's kin want his sword back, which the raiders carried off with him. In the clearings men are talking of asking the moot for another company.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The nest is the danger. Its watchers on the crown see anything that crosses the open ground at the tor's foot, and its parties hunt the forest a long way round. The spruce round the tor stands on wet ground that swallows a man to the knee from the thaw until midsummer, and bears den among the blocks at the tor's southern foot through the winter.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A gray tor above the spruce, with a smudge of smoke over the cleft on a still day. The ground round its foot is cleared of trees for a long bowshot and trodden bare. The cleft opens on the north side, the width of two men, and steps cut square in the granite climb it, worn hollow in the middle. Near the top a wall of fitted blocks, laid without mortar, closes the cleft, with a gap no wider than a man's shoulders and a square socket cut in the rock on either side of the gap at the height of a man's waist. The gap is choked with thorn and brush. Below the steps the cleft floor is a litter of bones, antler and broken spear shafts, and the smell of the nest fills it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The trapper's sword.** The trapper's kin pay a good price for his sword, which the raiders took with him, and the nest keeps what it takes.
- **The raiding path.** The southern clearings of Norgaad want the path the nest's parties use found and watched before the thaw, and the Skathwald hunters know where its tracks crossed theirs.
- **The moot's company.** If the moot sends a company against the crag, it hires guides who know the forest and men willing to go up the cleft first.

# The Truth of Dorgenstein {#truth .secret}

**The cavern.** The tor is **Madak-durákh**, "the holding fast of the living rock", a fortified cavern of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]], abandoned. Its keepers found the cleft and the fissures that run back from it into the blocks, and they made a hunting cavern and a watch over the forest of them with the least cut that served: the steps up the cleft, the wall and gate at its head, and short passages joining the fissures behind the wall into dry rooms. The gate's bar sat in the two sockets. The hold whose houses kept it, and how far it lay from the forest, are the game master's to set; every hold's archive keeps where the cavern stands.

**Its leaving.** The keepers held the tor against the nests as the nests filled the forest, killing every Grukar that came within reach of the crown. The forest filled faster than the hold could clear it. The keepers gave up the tor and took the gate bar with them, and at the foot of the cleft they sealed their store behind a slab fitted into the rock, meaning to come back for it. They did not come back.

**The nest.** A tribe took the empty tor, and the nest in it now is a few hundred around its spawner, which lies in the deepest fissure, where the rock holds the warmth of the tribe. It hunts about a league round the tor: elk and deer in the spruce, boar on the drier ground to the east, and the beaver ponds of the streams that run north. Its watchers on the crown see the forest to the edge of its league in every direction, and north toward the clearings of Norgaad.

**What hems it in.** To the south its league meets the league of another nest, and the hunters of the two fight where they meet. From the north the moot's companies come against it, seldom, and each one costs the tribe its best Hushk at the cleft. The tor holds water only in a cistern the keepers cut at the back of the rooms, and the cistern runs low in a dry summer. Then the tribe carries water up from the streams in the open, and its parties are smaller.

**What is left.** The store at the cleft foot is untouched. The nest has no use for a fitted slab and cannot open one. Behind it lie the keepers' spare hunting iron, spear heads and arrowheads in bundles, a small anvil and the tools of a smith, all dry. Khazari iron of this kind is worth a great deal in Norgaad. The slab is the plain gray of the granite round it, and the litter of the cleft floor has buried its lower edge.

**Why the nest does not last.** The crown is the one height in this part of the forest from which a watch sees to the clearings of Norgaad, and the moot wants it as badly as the tribe holds it. A company that clears the cleft takes the tor; a dry summer that drains the cistern while the southern nest presses on the league may do it first.

> [!TIP]
> The trapper's sword lies in the litter below the steps, with the other iron the parties have brought home. The keepers' long hold against the nests, the sealed store and its iron, the cistern and the moot's earlier company are this note's own account; a game master may change them.
