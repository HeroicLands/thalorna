## Overview {#overview}

The Varokh of [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]] call the marshlands of the Grukar country **Skathengrund**, the harm's bottom land, and the Nordmal call them the Drowned Country. They cover perhaps a quarter of [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm]], across its central and southern lowlands: swamp, peat-bog, slow water and seasonal flood with the nests scattered through it.

## The Land

The marsh is passable on foot only in the depths of winter, when it freezes, or in a few weeks of dry summer. The rest of the year it is roadless. The spring thaw turns it into an impassable mire for weeks, and the summer mosquitoes are legendary. The slow brown rivers of the Grukar country rise in it and wind out toward the grain country of [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia]].

The nests inside are reached by canoe, by cause-walks of sunken logs such as [[place-grimhreth|Grimhreth]], or by paths no outsider can see.

## Who Uses It

The Grukar are foragers here: hunters, trappers, fishers and gatherers of what the marsh gives. Fur hunters work its margins and come out with what they can carry. Soldiers and fugitives cross it only when they have to.

## Dangers

The Nordmal have learned not to send armies in, and no neighbor contests the ground. A party that enters meets Grukar who do not trade, treat or ransom: a person taken is eaten. The thaw and the mosquitoes are dangers of their own, and a party caught on a cause-walk by a thaw has nowhere to go. In Skathwald's marsh the thicket at [[place-grimak|Grimak]] is a [[lore-bloodfield|blood-field]] of the silence.

## Converging Interests {#present}

Nobody holds the marsh, and everyone who borders it pays for that. The Varokh villages of Skathwald spend on their walls and stand watches whether or not raiders have come. Velanthia's paid forts watch the rivers and raiding paths that leave it, from [[place-trvgrfrt|Torvogor Fort]] and [[place-sdrvdfrt|Sedrovod Fort]]. Fur hunters want what its margins hold and have no protected place to stop. The tribes inside fight one another, and that fighting keeps their numbers below what the land could feed. Small raiding parties come out along the rivers and the cause-walks.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- A fur hunter wants to be out of the margin before the thaw closes it, and every way in or out is a river or a cause-walk that a raiding party can use as well.
- A raiding party comes down a river out of the marsh while a fort's warden is owed months of pay, and the village below has only its bell and its signal fire.
