## Overview {#overview}

The Varokh of [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]] call the marshlands of the Grukar country **Skathengrund**, the harm's bottom land, and the Nordmal call them the Drowned Country. The Velanthians name the rivers that leave it, not the marsh. It covers perhaps a quarter of [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm Region]], across its central and southern lowlands: swamp, peat-bog, slow water and flood, with the nests scattered through it.

## The Land

The marsh is passable on foot only when it freezes in deep winter or in a few weeks of dry summer; otherwise it is roadless. The slow brown rivers of the Grukar country rise in it and wind out toward the grain country of [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia Region]].

The nests inside are reached by canoe, by cause-walks of sunken logs such as [[place-grimhreth|Grimhreth]], or by paths no outsider can see.

## Who Uses It

The Grukar forage here as hunters, trappers and fishers. Fur hunters work its margins and come out with what they can carry.

## Dangers

No neighbor contests the ground. A party that enters meets Grukar who do not trade, treat or ransom: a person taken is eaten. A party caught on a cause-walk by the thaw has nowhere to go. A [[being-ncrtcmr|Necrotic Mire]] moves through the marsh, and the nests move away from its track. In Skathwald's marsh the thicket at [[place-grimak|Grimak]] is a blood-field of the silence, and [[lore-bloodfield|Blood-fields]] says what that is.

## Converging Interests {#present}

Nobody holds the marsh, and everyone who borders it pays for that. The Varokh villages of Skathwald spend on their walls and stand watches whether or not raiders have come, and Velanthia's paid forts watch the rivers from [[place-trvgrfrt|Torvogor Fort]] and [[place-sdrvdfrt|Sedrovod Fort]]. Fur hunters have no protected place to stop. The tribes inside fight one another, which keeps their numbers low. Small raiding parties come out along the rivers and the cause-walks. A Necrotic Mire crosses cause-walks as it goes, and a nest does not use a walk it has crossed until a season has passed.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- The Necrotic Mire has crossed the cause-walk that a fur hunter meant to use, and the walk stays closed to the nests for a season.
- A river-trader asks at a landing where the Necrotic Mire was last seen, and the answer decides whether he goes in.
