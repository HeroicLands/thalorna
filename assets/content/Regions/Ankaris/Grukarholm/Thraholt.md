> **GM-only note.** Skathwald knows that a new tribe is budding at the edge of the Grimak thicket. How small it is, what it hunts and what its ground holds are for the GM.

## Overview {#overview}

**Thráholt**, the unyielding copse, is the name the Varokh of [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]] give the new nest at the edge of [[place-grimak|Grimak]], the burned thicket in Skathwald's marsh a day's walk east of [[place-mahnryth|Mahnreth]]. It lies at the western edge of [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm Region]], within the eastern reach of [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]]. The nest stands in a copse of birch and alder on firm ground against the outer stumps of the old ring, with the silent thicket at its back and the black hollow of the burned spawn-chamber a short walk inside. Its longhouses are few and newly roofed, and its spawn-chamber is a pit dug into the copse's highest ground.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

A young tribe of the [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] holds the copse: a few dozen gathered round a spawner that woke here when a faction carried it out of another tribe. Most of them are Uk. A few Hushk lead its hunters, and a Sha or two keep the watch on the villages. The tribe grows by its spawner's clutches, and the nest is still small enough that the villages of Skathwald could burn it in a day.

## Layers {#history}

A Grukar tribe woke a nest in this thicket about 532 AF and took three Varokh villages in a winter. In 536 the Skathwald villages came in over the ice and burned the nest with its spawner in it ([[lore-grimakburning|The Burning at Grimak]]). The thicket grew back as thorn over the stumps, and nothing sings in it. The tribe at its edge is new, and nobody in Skathwald can say whether the place drew it or whether it found unclaimed ground there, as the first one did.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The tribe is budding, and Skathwald has not decided what to do about it. A muster needs the elders of the villages to agree that the danger is common to all of them, and the elders who must agree are the grandchildren of the villages that argued over the first muster. The grove council at [[place-radholt|Radholt]] sounds its alarm whenever anyone is missing near the thicket, and young men walk the ring at night to say they did. [[place-frithholt|Frithholt]] is the nearest village to the old lost ground, and Mahnreth's old people can say which of the lost villages their kindred came from.

:::secret {title="The hunting range"}
The nest hunts about a league out from its copse. That league covers the ground of the three villages the first nest took: their old clearings and fields, long since grown back to birch and pine, and the marsh between them. It does not reach Mahnreth, a day's walk west. How near it comes to Frithholt and Radholt is for the GM to set.
:::

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The thicket feeds nothing. Grimak is a [[lore-bloodfield|blood-field]] of the silence: no birds, no frogs and no insects live in it, and the game that comes near it does not stay. The predators of the marsh gather at the burned hollow, where the field runs thin, and they hunt the same ground as the nest.

The voice in the thorn calls Varokh names and draws people toward the hollow, and the villages come after them with torches.

The villages of Skathwald stand round the nest on every side but the marsh, and they burned the last nest on this spot.

:::secret {title="What hems the nest in"}
The young tribe is weak in the way every new tribe is. Its spawner lays Uk because Uk are cheap and the larder is thin, and a hungry tribe cannot spawn Hushk. The thicket at its back gives it no game, the predators at the hollow take what comes in, and the villages hold the forest it would hunt outward into. The first nest here grew in a forest the famine had emptied. This one stands among the villages that burned it.
:::

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A party that comes in from the west crosses old clearings grown back to young wood, with the line of a field wall under the moss here and there. The copse at the thicket's edge smells of the nest before it is seen, the ground round it is trampled, and the Uk at work there are few enough to count. Behind the copse the thicket is silent, and the voice in the thorn calls by name. A Grukar party does not take prisoners or trade, and a person taken is eaten.

:::secret {title="What the ground holds"}
The lost villages' ground is the treasure. Their clearings and fields are the best-drained ground in Skathwald's east, and they wait only for the trees to be cut back; the families of Mahnreth and Frithholt know whose they were. While a nest hunts over them, nobody clears them.

The iron of the lost villages lies in the black hollow. What the first nest took in its four winters went into its stores, and when the muster burned the spawn-chamber the stores burned with it and sank into the water that stands in the hollow now: [[miscgear-ploughshare|ploughshares]], [[miscgear-scythe|scythes]], [[containergear-cauldronir|iron cauldrons]], knives and spearheads, the iron of three villages. Recovering it means wading into the thinnest part of the blood-field, where the predators gather and the voice is loudest.
:::

:::secret {title="Why the nest does not last"}
A tribe that buds at the far edge of viable land starts small, and this one has budded among villages that have done this before. If Skathwald musters while the nest is a few dozen, the copse burns as the thicket burned. If the elders argue through a winter, the tribe grows, its hunters go out from the poor ground of the thicket to the villages' pens and paths, and its raids fall on Skathwald as the first nest's did. Then the muster comes later, against a larger nest. Which tribe this one budded from is for the GM to decide; [[place-skathak|Skathak]], at the end of the Grimhreth walk, is one nest on Skathwald's side of the marsh whose ground is failing.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The muster.** A new nest stands against the old ring at Grimak, and Skathwald must decide whether to muster again before it grows.
- **The voice.** A Mahnreth girl answers the voice in the thorn and walks toward the hollow, with the new nest's hunters in the copse beside it.
- **The lost ground.** The families of [[place-mahnryth|Mahnreth]] name the villages their kindred came from, and their old fields lie inside the nest's hunting.
