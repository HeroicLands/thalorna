## Overview {#overview}

**Grimhreth**, the fierce ford, is the Varokh name for a cause-walk that crosses the edge of the [[place-skathengrund|Drowned Country]] from the forest of [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]]. The Nordmal would call it a sunken walk. Logs are laid in the mud and sink until they bear a footing under the black water, and a walker who keeps to them crosses ground that cannot be crossed at all off them. The Varokh call it a ford because it is the one crossing into the marsh that they can name.

## The Land

The walk runs through peat-bog and standing water, a narrow line without rail or marker, and a step off it is a step into the marsh. It can be walked in the dry weeks of summer and on the winter ice, and the spring thaw closes it for weeks. The nests beyond it are reached by canoe, by other cause-walks, or by paths an outsider cannot find.

## Who Uses It

Raiding parties come out along the cause-walks of the Grukar country, and this is one of them. Fur hunters use it to reach the margin and to leave it. The Varokh villages of Skathwald watch the paths that lead to it.

## Dangers

A person on the walk is on a line the Grukar know and an outsider does not. Nothing on it offers a stopping place, no Grukar met on it treats or takes a message, and a thaw takes the footing from under the walker and leaves the water.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The Skathwald villages spend on their walls and stand their watches whether or not raiders have come, and they cannot block the walk for long: logs are easy to lay again. Fur hunters go in at their own risk, and a raiding party that leaves the walk comes out into village country with only the villages' watch between it and the nearest door.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- A fur hunter must be across the walk before the thaw closes it, and the villages on the margin have a watch to keep and nothing spare to give him.
- A Skathwald village sees a raiding party leave the walk at dusk and has only its watch to answer with.
