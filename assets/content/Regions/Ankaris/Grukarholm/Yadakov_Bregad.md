## Overview {#overview}

**Yadakov Bregad**, the raid's landing, is what the river-folk of [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia]] call a bank where the slow brown rivers of [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm]] open toward their grain country. A Velanthian landing is a place where boats take on cargo. This one is a place where raiding parties come ashore. The Grukar use it in summer, when their war-canoes move warriors along the rivers; in winter the frozen river is a road across the country.

## The Land

The landing is a low bank on a slow river with the forest behind it and the marsh upstream. The water brings canoes down from the nests and gives them no cover from the bank.

## Who Uses It

The Grukar come to it as a raiding party and for nothing else. The wardens of [[place-trvgrfrt|Torvogor Fort]] watch it, and the village beneath the fort rings its bell and lights its signal fire when canoes are seen.

## Dangers

A landing can be watched or attacked, and it is no meeting place for an agreed exchange. A Grukar party that lands does not parley, does not ransom what it takes and does not leave a message. The Uk and the Hushk cannot bargain, and a Sha that hears a word of the river-folk uses it for its own nest.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The wardens watch the landing for the princes who pay them, and the pay is late. The village beneath the fort gives a man to the watch in turn and burns a fire when canoes come down. Raiding parties are small and come out along the rivers and cause-walks, and what a warden can do against one depends on the hours between the fire and the fort. Fur hunters pass the landing on their way to and from the margin, and a warden may sell one a night's passage.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- A warden of [[place-trvgrfrt|Torvogor Fort]] is owed several months of pay, and a fur hunter offers silver for a night's passage past the fort and the landing.
- Canoes are seen on the river at dusk and the signal fire is lit, but the men of the village are at their own work and the watch is one man.
