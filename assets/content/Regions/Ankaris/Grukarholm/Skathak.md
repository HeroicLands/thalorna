> **GM-only note.** The Skathwald villages know that a nest stands at the end of the walk at [[place-grimhreth|Grimhreth]]. What it hunts, what holds it in and what lies on its island are for the GM.

## Overview {#overview}

**Skathak**, the harm thicket, is the Varokh name for the nest at the far end of [[place-grimhreth|Grimhreth]], the cause-walk of sunken logs that runs from the forest of [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]] into [[place-skathengrund|Skathengrund]], the Drowned Country. It lies in the western edge of the marsh, where [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm Region]] overlies the eastern reach of [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]], out of sight of the forest edge.

:::secret {title="The island"}
The nest stands on an island of alder and firm peat that rises a little above the bog. Its longhouses are low and roofed with reed and turf, and its spawn-chamber is cut into the island's highest ground. Black water surrounds the island, and the walk is the only way onto it that does not need a canoe or the winter ice. The tribe on it numbers a few hundred.
:::

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

One tribe of the [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] holds the nest, centered on its spawner. The tribe lays the logs of Grimhreth and lays them again as they sink, and no other nest uses the walk. Its raiding parties come out along it into village country, and its hunters go out over the ice when the walk is closed.

## Layers {#history}

How long a nest has stood at the end of the walk is not known in Skathwald. The walk is older than the memory of any hunter who uses it, and the villages have never seen the nest itself. They know the nest by its logs, its raiding parties and the people it has taken.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The nest holds its walk and the marsh beyond it, and the villages of Skathwald hold the forest edge. At [[place-hildholt|Hildholt]] [[being-wlfrnrfrdrnd|Wulfrún ira Frodrúnd wur Hildholt]] watches the walk for parties coming out. [[being-skthrkrvndvth|Skathrik ira Vandvith wur Mahnreth]] runs his trapline along the margin and knows which of the nest's logs are sound. The villages want the nest burned, as they burned the nest at [[place-grimak|Grimak]], and burning it needs a muster willing to go out along the walk itself.

:::secret {title="The hunting range"}
The nest hunts about a league out from its island, wherever the marsh lets it go: along its own walk toward the forest edge, along the canoe-ways of the open water to the east, and over the whole of it on the winter ice. The Ahk paces the hunt. In a lean season the hunters spare the beaver lodges and the marten runs, and so the fur-bearers inside the nest's league are thicker than anywhere on the margin. How long a league takes on the logs and the canoe-ways is for the GM to set.

Raiding is another matter. A raiding party goes as far as the walk and the forest paths carry it, and the villages beneath the walk lie well beyond the league the nest hunts.
:::

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The [[being-ncrtcmr|Necrotic Mire]] moves through Skathengrund, and the nests keep off its track. A walk it crosses stays closed to them for a season, and a crossing on Grimhreth cuts the nest off from the forest edge until that season has passed.

The fur hunters of the margin take beaver and marten from the slow water under the forest edge, and in some seasons they find their snares emptied and cut.

Deeper in the marsh to the east lie other nests.

The villages of Skathwald pull the logs out of the walk when they can and stand their watches when they cannot.

:::secret {title="What hems the nest in"}
The nest's league is shrinking. The Mire's track closes the walk for a season at a time, the hunters on the margin strip the near water, and the nests to the east hold the open marsh beyond the island; their hunting ground meets this one's, and their hunters fight wherever the two ranges cross. What is left is the island, the far end of the walk and the ground the nest's hunters can reach over the ice. When the walk closes, the hunters go out onto the fur hunters' traplines, where a person is food like any other.
:::

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A party that goes out along the walk finds a line of logs under black water, sound in some stretches and rotten in others, and the nest's hunters on the ice in winter. At the walk's end the alder closes in, the ground firms under the reed, and the smell of the nest reaches a party before the longhouses come into view. The tribe fights hardest at the spawn-chamber, and the Grukar on the island do not take prisoners or trade. A person taken is eaten.

:::secret {title="What lies on the island"}
The fur ground is the treasure. The beaver and marten that the nest spares in lean seasons make the water inside its league the best trapping in the western marsh, where a month's work fills a season's bales. The hunters who tried lie in it. Their packs, spears and [[miscgear-traphuntin2|hunting traps]] lie on the island and along the walk's far end with whatever bales of pelts they carried, where the nest's hunters dropped them after the kill.
:::

:::secret {title="Why the nest does not last"}
The nest's ground is failing. The Ahk answers as an Ahk does: it rations, lays Uk instead of Hushk, lays fewer, and sends its hunters farther out. Farther out is the fur hunters' margin and the villages beneath the walk, and each raid that comes out of Grimhreth hardens Skathwald toward a muster.

Two endings stand open, and the GM chooses. The Skathwald villages raise a muster and go in along the walk or over the ice, and the island burns as Grimak burned. Or a Sha sees the end coming, gathers a second Sha and a Hushk with a grievance, and carries a proto-Ahk out into unclaimed land, while the nest left behind declines in good order and dies on its island. A tribe budded that way can stand anywhere in Skathwald's marsh, and nobody in Skathwald can say where.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **Before the thaw.** [[being-skthrkrvndvth|Skathrik ira Vandvith wur Mahnreth]] has a season's furs on the margin, and the walk closes within days of the ice going gray ([[scenario-lstcgrmhrth|The Last Ice at Grimhreth]]).
- **The step on the logs.** [[being-wlfrnrfrdrnd|Wulfrún ira Frodrúnd wur Hildholt]] hears a raiding party on the walk, and one horn from Hildholt brings the neighboring villages to its gate.
- **The sound logs.** The Skathwald villages want the nest burned, and [[being-skthrkrvndvth|Skathrik ira Vandvith wur Mahnreth]] knows which logs of the walk bear a footing, the one way in that does not wait for the ice.
