> **GM-only note.** The wardens of the forts know only that the canoes come down from nests upstream. Where those nests stand, what they hunt and what their ground is worth are for the GM.

## Overview {#overview}

**Lervobor**, the meadow pines, is the name the river-folk of [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia Region]] give the stretch of the slow brown rivers above [[place-zvekbregad|Zvek Bregad]], where the rivers leave [[place-skathengrund|Skathengrund]] and wind out toward the grain country. The wardens of the forts call its nests the canoe nests. It lies in the northwestern reaches of Velanthia that [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm Region]] overlies, in the [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]]'s country. The rivers run between broad meadows that flood in spring, and dry ridges of pine stand above the meadows. The nests stand on the ridges, above the flood. There is more than one nest on these rivers, and the GM sets how many.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Each nest is one tribe of the [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]], a few hundred around its own spawner, and no nest holds the reach for the others. Each keeps its own canoes, its own landing places on the bank and its own hidden walks along the water. Which nest sends the canoes of a given summer is not known at the forts, and the canoes of one summer need not come from the nest of the last.

## Layers {#history}

The forts' rolls record the canoes at Zvek Bregad every summer, and no roll records where they came from. No warden has gone upstream to the nests and come back to say what is there.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The nests hold the ridges and the river meadows below them, and the forts of Brevavesh watch the landing. [[being-krvgrlrvkv|Karvogor Lervikov]] keeps the bell and the watch on Zvek Bregad from [[place-trvgrfrt|Torvogor Fort]]. [[being-skgrdbrshv|Sokogor Dobrashov]] tracks the marsh edge for [[place-sdrvdfrt|Sedrovod Fort]]. [[being-brlndvshv|Brelana Duvashov]] poles her flat boat along the river edge and hears at the bank villages which pens have lost their stock. Finding the nest that raids the landing, and stamping it out, is work the prince's silver does not pay for.

:::secret {title="The hunting range"}
Each nest hunts about a league out from its ridge, along both banks, through the backwaters and into the pine. The leagues of neighboring nests meet and overlap on the rivers, and the hunters of two nests fight where they meet. How long a league takes by canoe against the slow current is for the GM to set.

The canoes carry a raid much farther than a nest hunts. A raiding party goes down the river to Zvek Bregad and beyond it to the fields of the bank villages; how far upstream the nests lie from the landing is for the GM to set.
:::

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The bank villages lose penned pigs and sheep to the nests' parties, and the fur hunters who pass the landing take what fur comes down the water.

The forts of Brevavesh stand below the nests, with their wardens and their village watches, and the villages ring their bells and burn signal fires when canoes come down.

The [[being-ncrtcmr|Necrotic Mire]] moves through the marsh the rivers rise in, and a nest keeps off any walk it crosses for a season.

:::secret {title="What hems the nests in"}
The game is thin. The nests crowd the same rivers, each hunts the meadows to its league's edge, and the fur hunters take what fur the nests would eat. The nests fight one another for the reaches: a nest that loses its stretch of river loses its hunting, and a nest that gains one grows.

The raids on the pens are a sign of hunger. The Ahk leaves alone a neighbor whose answer could break the tribe, and a settlement under a paid fort is such a neighbor. A nest that keeps taking stock from beneath Torvogor's hill has either a lean range or a Hushk pushing for the raid, and its Ahk tolerates the risk only while the meadows fail to feed the tribe. The hidden walk that runs along the bank from above the landing to the fields below the fort belongs to one such nest ([[scenario-rlftrvgr|The Relief of Torvogor]]).
:::

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A party going upstream finds the meadows empty of people, the reeds full of canoe channels and the banks marked by landing places too small to be a Velanthian landing. On the ridges the pine is cut back around the nests, and the longhouses stand above the flood line. Logs laid under the water along the banks carry the nests' parties where no canoe mark shows. The Grukar here do not parley, ransom or trade, and the Uk and Hushk among them cannot bargain at all.

:::secret {title="What the meadows are worth"}
The river meadows are the treasure. Flat, flood-fed and deep in silt, they are the kind of ground the rest of Velanthia grows its grain on, and they are the one good ground in a country otherwise worthless to a plough. They give no return while a nest hunts over them, because nobody ploughs them. A nest stamped out leaves a stretch of meadow the Princedom of Brevavesh could settle, and holding it gives the princedom ploughland on a frontier where its forts cost only silver.

The nests also hold what the canoes carry home: penned stock, and the iron of the bank villages' fields, a [[miscgear-ploughshare|ploughshare]] or a [[miscgear-scythe|scythe]] taken from a field left at the bell. Iron lies in the longhouses as the tribe left it, and the bones of the stock lie with it.
:::

:::secret {title="Why the nests do not last"}
These nests crowd one another on rivers that do not feed them all. A nest that loses its reach to a neighbor declines in place, its Ahk rationing to the end. At most a Sha carries a proto-Ahk out to found a tribe elsewhere, and the old nest dies all the same. A nest that keeps raiding beneath the forts draws the wardens upstream at last, once someone finds its walk and its ridge. The nests on these rivers change from summer to summer, and the canoes at the landing are a different nest's as often as the same one's.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The canoes at dusk.** The canoes come down to [[place-zvekbregad|Zvek Bregad]], the one man on the watch must reach the hill, and [[being-krvgrlrvkv|Karvogor Lervikov]] marches the garrison to the landing when the fire burns ([[scenario-rlftrvgr|The Relief of Torvogor]]).
- **Logs under the water.** [[being-skgrdbrshv|Sokogor Dobrashov]] has a track at the marsh edge that stops at a bank with no crossing, and following it upstream leads to a nest.
- **Lost stock.** [[being-brlndvshv|Brelana Duvashov]] hears at the bank villages that pens lose their stock with no canoe mark on the mud, and the nest that takes them is somewhere upstream.
