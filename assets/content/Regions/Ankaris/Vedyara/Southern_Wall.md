The **Southern Wall** is the long face of the [[place-graznmntns|Grazian Mountains]] that stands over [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]]. It is why the one road into the subcontinent that crosses no pass runs round the western end of the range, through [[place-wstrndscnt|the Western Descent]], and why every other way in is a climb.

For some fifteen hundred miles the escarpment runs without a gap a traveler could use. The peaks along it are the highest on [[place-worldthlrn|Thalorna]]—[[place-suryashkhr|Sūryashikhara]] first among them—and behind the summit line the ice lies in fields that have never gone off in any season anyone has recorded. Four crossings pierce it: [[place-meghadvara|Meghadvāra]] in the west, [[place-gudesroad|the Guides' Road]] between the two western doors, [[place-suryadvara|Sūryadvāra]] under the fortress, and [[place-tamradvara|Tāmradvāra]] up the [[place-slvrgorges|Silver Gorges]] in the east. Everything else is wall.

## What Comes Off It

The four great rivers of Vedyara begin here, each at a glacier-spring the [[affiliation-varakpnthn|Varnaka]] tradition holds to be the literal source from which that river flowed at the beginning of time: [[place-chandrprbh|Chandraprabhava]] for the [[place-chandrmahi|Chandramahī]], [[place-sarvaprbhv|Sarvaprabhava]] for the [[place-sarvadarivr|Sarvada]], [[place-mahaprbhva|Mahāprabhava]] for the [[place-mahanadi|Mahānadi]], and [[place-bhrvprbhav|Bhāravaprabhava]] for the [[place-bharavarivr|Bhārava]]. Half the population of the subcontinent drinks off this face of the range.

The melt is not steady. A warm spring on the Wall puts the rivers up three weeks early and drowns the seed of whoever planted by the old reckoning. The rivalry between the temple astronomers who set the year and the reckoners who compute it is, at bottom, an argument about this snow.

## Sacred Ground

The Wall's peaks are the dwelling-places of the [[affiliation-varakpnthn|Varnaka]] gods, and the pilgrimage to the lower slopes is an ordinary part of a devout life. Hundreds of thousands walk to the shrine-terraces in the favorable months, and a very small number go higher, to the springs themselves, where the temples stand.

Above the last of the [[place-pssshrines|Pass-Shrines]] no rule of station reaches. The [[affiliation-osketguides|Ösket]] who live up there are not in the tharana, and they are not outside it the way an **Outcaste** is outside it. The order does not address them at all, and orthodox Vedyari jurists have left the matter undisturbed for as long as anyone has been writing the question down.
