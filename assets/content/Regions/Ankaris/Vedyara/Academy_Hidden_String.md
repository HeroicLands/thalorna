A recruiting officer for one of the patron kingdoms, briefing the junior who will carry his offer to [[affiliation-dhnrktjnpd|Dhanurkota]], puts it this way: "Write the contract as though the master were reading over your shoulder, because he will. He alone decides what is dishonorable, and nobody can overrule him, not the sabhā and not the other three masters."

The **Academy of the Hidden String** teaches ambush and concealment archery. Its graduates serve as scouts and on the operations a kingdom does not name in its dispatches, and it is the hardest of the four halls to get into and the hardest to recruit out of.

Its hall is against the west wall of [[place-bowfort|the Bow-Fort]], it has no shooting ground anyone can point to, and its students train in the broken country upstream for weeks at a time.

## The Vow

The academy's discipline carries a vow of selectivity. It refuses any contract it considers dishonorable, and its master is the judge of what that is, without appeal to the sabhā or to the other three masters.

The refusals are not published and are not explained. The Hidden String has turned down work from two of the janapada's own patron kingdoms within living memory, and neither patronage was withdrawn.

## What It Is Suspected Of

A hall that teaches concealment and declines to say whom it works for draws the obvious suspicion, and the classical-orthodox raise it whenever Dhanurkota's customs of station are under attack for other reasons. The academy answers that an archer who kills from cover in a war is a soldier and that it trains nothing else.

The sabhā has never inquired. The four reserved seats include the Hidden String's, and the master has voted on every military question of the last thirty years.
