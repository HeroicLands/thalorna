**The temple of Lower Suvargeri** stands at the foot of [[place-goldmountain|the Gold Mountain]], where [[place-bharavarivr|the Bharava]] proper begins. It is the temple outsiders meet, because the road up the valley reaches it first and because the jewelers' quarter is at its gate.

Its altar, its fabric and its endowment are the equals of the other two. What it has that they do not is traffic.

## Its Priest

[Vajranata Tomra-Mukhva]{.name data-type="being/npc"} holds it. He is in his early forties, the youngest of the three by a good margin, recently elevated, and still establishing a voice of his own against two priests who were seniors when he was a student.

## The Gate and the Quarter

Four hundred households of goldsmiths, gem-setters and enamelers work below the temple, buying stones by the year out of the coastal trade and selling finished work through factors who come no further up the valley than the bridge. The temple takes its share of that trade as offerings and takes no part in the trade itself, which is a distinction the [Gold Constitution]{.name data-type="lore/law"} cares about and the jewelers observe.

Pilgrims and buyers arrive at the same gate, and the temple keeps two courts so that they do not have to share one.
