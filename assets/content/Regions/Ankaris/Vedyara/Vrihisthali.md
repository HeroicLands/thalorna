**Vrīhisthalī** (830) is the largest of the rice villages of the [[place-rajapurjnpd|Rājapur Janapada]]. It stands a mile back from the water, as the farming villages of the [[place-mahanadi|Mahānadi]] do, and the river reaches its fields by channel and not by flood.

Like every village below the head-gate at [[place-khandapura|Khandāpura]], it takes its water from the share divided there at planting. It is the largest grower of rice, so the tenth of the year's water that is argued over matters more to Vrīhisthalī than to any village in the janapada, and its headman sits through all three days of the argument.
