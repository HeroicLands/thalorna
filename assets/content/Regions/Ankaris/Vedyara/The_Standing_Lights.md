## Overview {#overview}

**The Standing Lights** is what the gatherers and the hostel-keepers of the upper [[place-bharavarivr|Bharava]] call a long wooded ridge in [[place-forestnoroad|the Forest of No Road]], three or four days' walk above the last estate on the river. Nobody has stood on it. The name is for what is seen from the margin on a clear night: a scatter of lights high along the crest, pale and steady, that stand where they are for hours and do not flicker, drift or die down as a camp's fire does.

Lower down, among the trees below the crest, other lights show: soft green, blue and violet beads that pulse slowly and smell, when the wind comes down off the ridge, of overripe fruit. The gatherers know those for [[being-glowvine|glowvines]], burn the ones they find near their paths in daylight, and never walk toward a light in the forest after dark. The pale lights on the crest are not glowvines, the gatherers say, and they say nothing more about them.

## What the Margin Sees

The gathering peoples who work the forest for resin and lac turn their season in at the margin, and they go no deeper than the ground below the ridge. Asked about the ridge, they say three things. A path that runs straight toward the lights on one evening is not there in the morning. A party that walks toward them walks all day and comes down at dusk on the bank it left. And on still nights, from the right place below the ridge, there is a sound like many voices a long way off, which stops when anyone speaks.

Nobody has brought anything back from the ridge, and nobody claims to have seen whoever keeps the lights. The ascetics who pass through the forest say less about the ridge than about any other part of it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody holds the ridge. The ground above the last estate on the upper river is endowed to no temple and claimed by no janpoda, and the gatherers who work below it take their licenses from the estate they turn their season in at.

## Layers {#history}

The tellings of the pale lights are as old as anything the hostel-keepers repeat about the forest, and they have not changed: the lights stand on the crest, the paths toward them do not stay, and nobody who has gone up has come back with more. The line between the last estate and the unendowed forest is the line the gatherers have always turned back at.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The ordained keeper of the last estate on the upper river holds that his endowment runs to the foot of the ridge, past the line the hostel-keepers and the gatherers have always called the boundary, and this season he has licensed the gathering peoples to take lac and hardwood beyond it. No other keeper disputes it, since the ground beyond belongs to no estate, and the [[affiliation-bhrvdvbhon|Bharava-Devabhona]] has no body above the keepers to hear a claim on unendowed ground.

Two gathering bands took the license. One came back to the margin after nine days with a full season of lac and could not say which way it had gone. The other, five gatherers, went up the stream below the ridge a month ago and has not come back. Their kin are camped at the hostel nearest the margin, the hostel-keepers are feeding them out of stores meant for the pilgrim road, and every other band refuses to go past the old line for the keeper's license or any other price.

# The Truth of the Standing Lights {#truth .secret}

**The enclave.** The ridge is **Tilvaisto**, "the place of the seam of light", an enclave of about a hundred and fifty [[lore-flksenali|Senali]], the [[affiliation-nclvtlvst|Enclave of Tilvaisto]]. Its hearths are grown into the old trees along the crest, and the pale lights are their lamps, seen where the forest lets them be seen. The voices are the hearths singing. The paths that vanish and the walk that ends on the bank it began from are the ridge's keeping, the same that every enclave has, and they harm nobody.

**The glowvines.** The low lights are glowvines, as the gatherers say. Many of them hang in the deepest hollow below the ridge, under the stream the second band followed, and over many seasons one and another creeps out of it along the roots into the smaller hollows toward the crest. The enclave's wardens burn each one that reaches them. The deep hollow itself the wardens have never cleared.

**The lost band.** The five gatherers walked into that hollow. The warden [[being-hirrettinlmnt|Hìrretti Nelminto]] found them there: three were dead in the vines, and two were alive. He led the two out by a path the ridge keeps, and they came out of the forest far to the east, among the Hvarn herders under [[place-estrnreach|the Eastern Reach]], where they are now, sick and a long way from anyone who knows them. Nobody at the hostel knows that two of the five are living.

**The council.** The enclave has not agreed on what to do about the license. [[being-ossendattahndn|Ossendatta Haundento]], the Tilvainno who keeps the enclave, holds that the ridge's keeping turns the gatherers back as it always has. [[being-seltavommttnt|Seltavo Ommottinto]], a lord of the enclave, wants the deep hollow burned out entire before more gatherers come, which means a fire on the ridge that the whole margin can see. Hìrretti wants the gatherers warned away from the hollow by a sign they cannot mistake, which no hearth of the enclave has ever given a human.

> [!TIP]
> Let a party reach the ridge only as far as the enclave allows. The hollow, the glowvines and the two survivors among the Hvarn are all within reach without anyone on the ridge being seen.
