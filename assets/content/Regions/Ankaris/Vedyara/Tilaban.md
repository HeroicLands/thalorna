**Tilaban** (540) is a sesame village on the dry terrace above the flood-line, in the [[place-danurkotjnpd|Danurkot Janpoda]], and the smell of the oil-presses reaches the road before the village does. It presses the janpoda's lamp-oil, the oil that fills every lamp in the valley.
