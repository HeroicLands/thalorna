The **Forest of No Road** is the country below [[place-estrnreach|the Eastern Reach]], at the headwaters of [[place-bharavarivr|the Bhārava]] above the gold highlands. No janapada has ever sent an assembly into it. No sabhā meets in it, no temple-domain claims it, and the name is what the pilgrim road's hostel-keepers call the ground the road does not enter.

There are ways through it. There is no road.

## The Rumor

Enclaves of [[lore-flksinale|Sinalë]] are rumored there, and [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazári]] halls above the tree line on the far side of the same range. The rumors are old, consistent and entirely unconfirmed, which is the condition in which such rumors are most useful to the people who repeat them.

What is repeated is always the same: lights at a distance that do not move as a camp's fire moves, a path that is clear for a day and gone the next, and a traveler who came out at a different place from the one he expected. Nobody produces a Sinalë. Nobody produces a body, an artifact or a name.

## Who Goes In

Hunters from the upper villages work the margin and do not go deep. The forest peoples the lowland records describe only in the aggregate gather what they gather and trade it at the edge, and what they say about the interior is not written down by anyone who asks them.

Ascetics go in. It is one of the places a renunciant disappears into for years at a time, and the ones who come back are received with reverence and are not cross-examined. The accounts that reach the hostels come from them, at third hand, and are the least reliable of all.
