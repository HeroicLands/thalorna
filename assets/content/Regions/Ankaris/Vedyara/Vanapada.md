**Vanapada** is a charcoal village at the forest edge, in the [[place-suvarnagirijnpd|Suvarnageri Janapoda]]. Its burners cut the slope wood for the jewelers of **Lower Suvarnageri**, which is the wood the smelters of [[place-tamravana|Tamravana]] also want. That makes Vanapada the jewelers' supplier in the long quarrel over the cutting-rotation, a quarrel the sabhra takes up about once in five years.
