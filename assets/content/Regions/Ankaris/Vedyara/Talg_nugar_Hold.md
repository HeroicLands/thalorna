## Overview {#overview}

**Talg-nugár Hold** is the hold of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]] in the cliff at the head of a high valley, the cliff its farmers call [[place-hvolvern|Hvolvern]] in the tongue of the [[affiliation-hvarnguides|Hvarn]], on the side of [[place-estrnreach|the Eastern Reach]] that faces [[place-tantsouregin|Tantsou]], and the valley beneath it. Its name, _talg-nugár_ (_tahlg-noo-GAHR_), is "the watching of the snow", the work it was cut for: the hold looks out over the cols of the Reach and the snowfields above them. About six hundred Khazari of six houses live in the rock, and about eighteen hundred of the [Lunvharn]{.name data-type="affiliation"}, a Hvarn-speaking people, farm the valley below.

Outside the valley the hold is a rumor of Khazari halls above the tree line. The Lunvharn tell no outsider anything more than that the cave houses are full.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

**Inside the rock** the hold is governed as every Khazari hold is, by the council of the elders of its houses, sitting together with no one first among them. The council judges the hold's quarrels from its archive, apportions its stores and sends its people to their work. It sends one of its elders to the gate as **Speaker**, to hear what the valley brings up and to give the hold's answer. A second elder is **Master of Works**, who keeps the channels, terraces and granaries of the valley and the hold's own works, and a third is **Warden of the Watch**, who keeps the watch on the cols.

**In the valley** the Lunvharn keep their own hearths and choose their own hearth-heads, and the eldest of them comes up to the gate for the valley. The hold does not choose him and does not question the choice.

## Current Situation {#present}

- **The prospector.** A silver prospector of [[place-slvrgorges|the Silver Gorges]] came up the valley last summer and was walked back down from the upper fields. He is hiring porters to climb the slopes above the terraces at the thaw. Ourn has asked the Hvarn of [[place-nurvhrn|Nurvharn]] to sell him no rope, and the council of the hold is divided on what it does if he climbs. The Speaker wants him turned back by the valley as every stranger is. The Warden wants the slope brought down before the thaw. The Master of Works holds that a fall brought down on a sound slope is a forced cut, entered against the hold for as long as the slope stands.
- **The winter.** The thaw came late last year, the barley on the highest terraces did not ripen, and the council has set the hold's draw on its stores below the count so that the valley's share comes up whole.

:::secret {title="The seam under the slope"}
The silver the prospector is following runs north under the Reach, and the hold's lowest working follows the same seam. The slopes above the terraces where he means to dig lie over that working, and two seasons of digging there break into it. Hamak Talg-hunáth knows it, the council does not, and the prospector's piece of dressed silver fell from a load the hold sent down to the valley.
:::

## History {#history}

The houses that found the cave behind the cliff came east along the range from the country of [[lore-khazarturn|Khazartúrn]] in the centuries the city stood, and the hold's archive counts its years from that finding. They taught the people of the valley to lay terraces, lead the water and keep a granary, and the valley has fed the hold since. The Lunvharn took the Hvarn tongue from the long house of their neighbors long after, and they have never wintered under its roof.

Khazartúrn fell in 2427 BF to [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] driven by a Senali outlaw, and since then the hold has read the account of the fall in the deep at intervals, as every hold does, and kept a watch on the cols against Grukar as well as men.

## Relations {#relations}

The hold has no treaty, embassy or correspondence with any realm, and no realm knows it is there. The Hvarn of Nurvharn know the Lunvharn as kin who keep another roof, and do not ask what the yak-trains carry up the valley head. [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Tantsou]]'s frontier officers register the caravans on the Jnanadvar road and have never registered the valley.
