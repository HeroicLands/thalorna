**Girimārga** is a village astride the mountain road in the [[place-suvarnagirijnpd|Suvarnagiri Janapada]], and anyone going up or down the slope walks through it. The road is the village's work. Its people keep the surface mended and the verges clear, and the sabhā pays them for it out of the common share, the quarter of the mountain's gold that is held back for famine, irrigation and whatever else the sabhā authorizes.
