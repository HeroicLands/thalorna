**Virastali** (680) is the lineage village of **Senaja** households on the hill above the ford, in the [[place-dhanurkotajnpd|Danurkota Janapoda]]. Born to arms, its men owe service in the host, and when the sabhra calls the muster they come down off the hill in greater numbers than any other village sends.
