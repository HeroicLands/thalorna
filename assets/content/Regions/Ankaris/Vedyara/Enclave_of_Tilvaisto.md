> **GM-only note.** The gatherers and hostel-keepers of the upper Bharava know the ridge as [[place-standlights|the Standing Lights]] and know nothing of who lives there.

## Overview {#overview}

The **Enclave of Tilvaisto** is the community of about a hundred and fifty of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]] on the ridge in [[place-forestnoroad|the Forest of No Road]] that the gatherers of the upper [[place-bharavarivr|Bharava]] call [[place-standlights|the Standing Lights]]. _Tilvaisto_ is "the place of the seam of light", from _tilvai_, "the seam of light", and _-sto_, "the place of it". No Vedyari record names it, and no human has been on the ridge.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

A Senalo of Tilvaisto belongs to a hearth the enclave keeps. The hearths raise a lord for things done, by every voice, and a lord's standing is a rank and not a holding. The enclave's head office is the **Tilvainno**, the Keeper of the Seam of Light, raised by every voice of the hearths and held for as long as they do not raise another. The Tilvainno is [[being-ossendattahndn|Ossendatta Haundento]]. She keeps the ridge, says aloud what the hearths agree and nothing they have not, and drives out an outlaw, a _vyerka_, when the hearths ask it.

The lords of the enclave include [[being-seltavommttnt|Seltavo Ommottinto]]. Among its members are the warden [[being-hirrettinlmnt|Hìrretti Nelminto]], [[being-seippelalntpp|Seippela Lanteppinto]], who keeps the paths at the foot of the ridge, and [[being-eikkalavttsnt|Eikkala Vettisento]], who sings the trees of the crest into hearths.

## Current Situation {#present}

The ordained keeper of the last estate on the upper river has licensed the gathering peoples to work past the old line toward the ridge. One band came back after nine days, turned by the ridge's keeping without knowing it. The other walked into the glowvine hollow below the stream; three died there, and Hìrretti led the two living out to the Hvarn country under [[place-estrnreach|the Eastern Reach]].

The hearths have not agreed on an answer. Ossendatta holds that the ridge's keeping turns the gatherers as it has always turned the people of the margin, and that the enclave owes the license nothing. Seltavo wants the deep hollow burned out entire before more gatherers come, though a fire of that size on the ridge is seen from every hostel on the upper river. Hìrretti wants the gatherers warned away by a sign they cannot mistake, which no hearth of Tilvaisto has ever given a human. Until the hearths agree, the wardens keep burning the small hollows and leave the deep one standing.

## Defense {#arms}

Walls and armies do not protect the enclave. The ridge does. A path toward the crest is clear one evening and gone the next morning, a party that walks toward the lights comes down at dusk on the bank it left, and a traveler who will not be turned comes out of the forest somewhere other than where he went in. Many glowvines hang in the deep hollow below the stream, and over many seasons one and another creeps out of it along the roots into the smaller hollows toward the crest. The wardens walk those hollows and burn each vine that reaches them, because a glowvine kills whatever the ridge sends wandering.

## History {#history}

No Vedyari record says when the Senali came to the forest below the Eastern Reach, and the tellings of the lights at the margin are old. Nothing of Tilvaisto's own past has come out of the enclave.

## Relations {#relations}

- **The gathering peoples** work the margin below the ridge and turn back when the ridge turns them. The enclave has never spoken with them.
- **The keeper of the last estate** claims the ground to the ridge's foot under his endowment, and the enclave does not recognize the claim or answer it.
- **The [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]]** keep [[affiliation-talgnugarhld|Talg-nugár Hold]] above the tree line on the far side of the Reach. The enclave has no dealings with it, and the Senali do not speak to the Khazari.
- **[[affiliation-nclvhlsst|Helsaisto]]**, the other enclave of the forest, keeps its own counsel, and the two take no part in each other's affairs.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The two who came out.** Two gatherers of the lost band are alive among the Hvarn under the Eastern Reach. [[being-hirrettinlmnt|Hìrretti Nelminto]] led them there and wants them home before their kin at the hostel decide they are dead and go up the stream after them.
- **The fire on the ridge.** [[being-seltavommttnt|Seltavo Ommottinto]] wants the deep hollow burned out before the next band comes. A party at the margin on the night the hearths agree to it sees the ridge burn.
- **The keeper's line.** The keeper of the last estate wants proof for the mother-temples that his endowment runs to the ridge, and pays a party to walk the ground and mark it.
