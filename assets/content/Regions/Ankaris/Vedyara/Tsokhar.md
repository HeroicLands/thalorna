**Tsökhar** sits in a high valley between [[place-meghadvara|Meghadvāra]] and [[place-gudesroad|the Guides' Road]], on the only ground in the western wall that will carry a herd through a winter. Three hundred and eighty people live there. It has a weekly market in the open season, a smith, and nothing else a lowlander would call a trade.

By the standards of [[place-oskhelt|Öskhelt]] it is poor. Two caravans a year come over the storm-door in a good season, the [[affiliation-osketguides|Ösket]] share of two caravans does not keep a village, and Tsökhar accordingly lives off its yaks and its horses the way it did before there was any trade at all.

## What It Is Actually Worth

The lineages of Tsökhar hold the sixth road. That is the village's real position, it is not spoken of in the village any more than anywhere else, and it means that a settlement of three hundred and eighty herders is visited, in most years, by at least one person who has come a very long way and will not say from where.

The money that arrives by this route is not spent here. What it buys is winter grain, salt and iron carried up out of the marches by drovers who are paid in coin and asked nothing, and a stranger who noticed how much iron a poor village was buying would be the first in four hundred years to make anything of it.

## The Watch

Tsökhar keeps a watch on its approaches through the open months, which no other village on the wall does. The Ösket describe this as a precaution against weather.
