**The Mahajaya Temple at Danurkot** keeps a vigil for men who are not there. On the night before any major engagement involving Danurkot graduates, anywhere in [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]], its bowmen-priests stay awake for them.

The Mahajaya temple inside [[place-bowfort|the Bow-Fort]] is the religious and political heart of the [[affiliation-dnrktjnpd|Danurkot Janpoda]], and one of the major establishments of the goddess in inland Vedyara. Its great hall is where the sabhra meets, and its senior priest convenes it.

[[lore-mahajayadty|Mahajaya]] is the preserver-goddess of the [[affiliation-vrnkpnthn|Varnuka pantheon]], mistress of order, of prosperity and of the keeping of dorma. She suits a janpoda whose whole identity is disciplined martial training in the service of legitimate authority, and every Danurkot sermon on the subject says so.

## Three Observances of Its Own

The temple keeps the standard [[affiliation-mahajaya|Mahajaya]] devotional cycle and adds three observances:

- **The [Festival of the Drawn Bow]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}** falls at the spring equinox, when each year's incoming academy students present themselves for blessing.
- **The [Festival of the Returning]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}** falls in autumn, when graduates back from service abroad ritually re-enter the janpoda and are welcomed. The festival was first kept for the graduates who came home at the recall of [[lore-hghlndwar|the Highland War]].
- **The [Watch of the Bow-String]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** is kept the night before any major engagement involving Danurkot graduates anywhere in Vedyara, when the temple's bowmen-priests hold vigil for the safety of their own.

The Watch is the one that costs. A janpoda of thirty thousand people has graduates in the field of half a dozen kingdoms at once, and the temple keeps the vigil for every engagement it hears of in time.

## The Priesthood

The **Ritrija** households of the janpoda sit in and around the temple, and the donations of academy graduates have made them wealthy by janpoda standards. The senior priest is one of the senior voices at every [Mahva-Melka]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}, and the four academy masters sit with him at every formal session of the sabhra.

The temple deals with the cremation-ground priests outside the walls at arm's length, and has done so for a very long time.
