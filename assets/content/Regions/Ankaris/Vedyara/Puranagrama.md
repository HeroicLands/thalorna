**Puranagrama** (410) is a village a mile below [[place-rajapur|Rajepur]], and it is built out of the old kingdom. Its houses are brick robbed from the ruins of the old kingdom, so that the walls of this [[place-rajapurjnpd|Rajepur Janapoda]] village carry the kingdom's masonry.

The sabhra put the palace's stones into the temple by decree. The brick in Puranagrama went into its houses without one.
