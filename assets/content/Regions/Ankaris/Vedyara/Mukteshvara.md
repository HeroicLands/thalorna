The dead of every village in the janapoda come to **Muktoshvara** (780), the downstream village, and the [[affiliation-rasikara|Rasikara]] cremation-ground that receives them runs along a half-mile of bank below the houses. Its ghats are stone and are kept by the village at the janapoda's charge.

Forty families live by the burning: the wood-sellers, the ghat-keepers, the barbers who shave the mourners, and the men who tend the fires. They stand lowest in the janapoda, and they are the only people in it who are never short of work.

The village farms as well, and farms badly, because the best of its ground is what the burning-ground stands on.
