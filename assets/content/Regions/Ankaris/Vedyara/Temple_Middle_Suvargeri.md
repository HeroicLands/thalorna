**The temple of Middle Suvargeri** stands in the central valley below the principal panning grounds, and [[place-weighingstn|the weighing-station]] and the common treasury stand beside it. The sabhra meets here one year in three like anywhere else, but the gold comes here every month of every year.

That gives the middle temple a weight the constitution never granted it. The priests of the other two say so at intervals, and the answer is that the treasury has to be somewhere.

## Its Priest

[Podmashri Kaneka-Hasta]{.name data-type="being/npc"} holds it. She is a woman in her fifties, reckoned the most politically able of the three senior priests, and she is the convening priest by the rotation as it stands. She is expected to be a major voice at the next [Mahva-Melka]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}.

## The Treasury

One-quarter of everything the mountain yields is deposited here as the common share, against famine, irrigation works and whatever else the sabhra authorizes. The cap on a household's private holdings is enforced here too: gold over the weight goes into the treasury, earns a small return and comes back on request.

Six centuries ago a wealthy family tried to buy an amendment to the panning rolls in its own favor. It was discovered, stripped of all its gold and exiled, and the cap was codified in the same session. The treasury has held the difference ever since.
