## Overview

The **Megha-samudra** is the ocean south of Vedyara, and the name means the sea the cloud comes from. The rain that makes the subcontinent crosses this water, and the coast that takes it first takes the heaviest fall in Thalorna. The [[place-chandrmahi|Chandramahī]] comes down to a delta on this sea, and the deep-water trade of [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]] is worked from the harbors at its mouth.

The shore is tropical and lush the whole of its length. Behind it the ground rises into the southern interior, where the forest products are gathered and the temple-domains hold what no janapada holds.

## The Monsoon

The rains build over this sea and come ashore in a few days. They do not creep up the coast. A port has its last clear week and then has four months of it, and every master on the coast knows the date the way a farmer knows the sowing.

The wet season is a working season ashore and a dead one afloat. Hulls come up the rivers or lie behind the bars, crews are paid off, and the yards do their building. When the rain stops the season opens on both coasts at once, and the [[place-suryasamdra|eastern sea]] and this one are sailed on the same calendar.

## The Kheperi Passage

The long voyage from this coast runs west and then north-west to the delta cities of [[affiliation-empirtkhpr|Ta'Kheperu]], and it is the most valuable passage any Vedyari ship makes. It is a month's sailing in a fair season. Vedyari spice, gems, worked ivory and fine cotton go out on it; Kheperi grain, linen, papyrus and gold come back.

What reaches the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]] from Vedyara goes through Kheperi hands and at Kheperi rates. The Vedyari houses know this and have never found a way around it. Ta'Kheperu keeps its own year, its own weights and its own account of what a cargo is worth at the quay, and a Vedyari factor at the delta learns all three or is beaten on every contract.
