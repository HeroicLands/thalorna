Kanakasetu (720) stands at the bridge where the pilgrim road crosses [[place-bharavarivr|the Bharava]] below the mountain. Everything that goes up to [[place-suvargeri|Suvargeri]] comes through it. The bridge is stone, of four spans, and is kept by the janpoda out of the common share.

The village holds one of the twenty-three houses of [[place-bhrvdvstna|the Bharava-Devastena Hostels]], which lodge traveling priests and ascetics free and turn nobody away. It sleeps sixty and is full through the pilgrim season. The warden keeps a register of who has slept there, and that register is the best record anyone has of what goes up the Bharava in a year.

Two panning families work the bar below the bridge. It is thin ground and yields about what two families eat.
