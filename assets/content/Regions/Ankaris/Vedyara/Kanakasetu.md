Kanakasetu (720) stands at the bridge where the pilgrim road crosses the Bhārava below the mountain. Everything that goes up to Suvarnagiri comes through it. The bridge is stone, of four spans, and is kept by the janapada out of the common share.

The village holds one of the twenty-three houses of the Bhārava-Devasthāna, which lodges traveling priests and ascetics free and turns nobody away. It sleeps sixty and is full through the pilgrim season. The warden keeps a register of who has slept there, and that register is the best record anyone has of what goes up the Bhārava in a year.

Two panning families work the bar below the bridge. It is thin ground and yields about what two families eat.
