Lipigrāma (720) copies. Four scriptoria work here besides the temple's own at Rājapur, and about a hundred and thirty men and women of the village earn their bread with a stylus.

The work came with an endowment six hundred years old. It pays a copyist by the page and asks only that the temple's own texts be kept current. Everything beyond that the scriptoria take on commission, and the commissions are what the village lives on.

Its hands are reckoned plain and accurate. A court wanting a fair copy sends here. A court wanting a beautiful one sends up the Sarvada to [[place-dharmapur|Dharmapur]] and pays four times as much for it.
