**Setugram** (610) stands where the road to [[place-rajepur|Rajepur]] crosses the flood channel, on a causeway, and the village is simply what has grown up at its foot. A causeway is built to stand above the water it crosses, so the height of this one is a fair measure of what the channel carries in the wet season.

A carter on the road describes the crossing before he mentions the village.
