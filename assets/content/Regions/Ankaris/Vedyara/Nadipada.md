**Nadīpāda** (350) is the one village of the [[place-rajapurjnpd|Rājapur Janapada]] that does not keep its distance from the [[place-mahanadi|Mahānadi]]. The farming villages sit back from the water; Nadīpāda's fishermen live on the bank itself, with the boats drawn up at the door and the nets spread over the house-walls to dry, and the river has moved the village twice.

A fisherman here will tell you the bargain is a fair one: a fishing village a mile from the water would be a farming village. When the channel shifts, the houses follow it. If you are marking Nadīpāda on a map, mark it lightly.
