## Overview {#overview}

**Vrundelv** is a bridge in the high northern country where [[place-graznmntns|the Grazian Mountains]] begin their arc, far above the last trees. It is one span of stone, no wider than two yaks abreast, thrown across a gorge whose river runs out of sight below. On one side it touches a ledge at the foot of a cliff, and on the other it lands on a hanging meadow that nothing else reaches. The [[affiliation-hvarnguides|Hvarn]] who drive their yaks up to that meadow in summer call the place Vrundelv, "the old door", from [[skill-hvarnlng|Hvarn]] _vrun_, "old", and _delv_, "a door", because they say there is a door in the cliff and nobody can find it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody holds it. The herders of the Klemverd hearth drive their beasts over the bridge to the meadow in the open months and bring them back before the snow, and no other hearth uses the crossing. No guide takes a caravan this way, and no state of the lowlands has ever claimed the ground.

## Layers {#history}

The Hvarn say the bridge is older than their tongue. The Klemverd hearth's tellers count the hearths that have grazed the meadow back as far as their roll goes, and the bridge stands at the start of the count, already old. Some herders say the Deep Folk cut it, as lowland rumor puts Khazari halls above the tree line somewhere in the range. Others say it was there before any people came. Nobody has found a mark on it.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The bridge stands sound, and the hearth crosses it every summer, but it has lost a stone. Last spring a block of the parapet on the downstream side fell into the gorge, and the gap is wide enough for a yak to go through. No Hvarn mason can lift a stone that size to the height of the span, and the Klemverd hearth now drives its beasts over one at a time on a short rope.

The other trouble is a sound. [Termen]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the Klemverd hearth brought the last of the herd down late in the autumn, in the first hard frost, and he swears he heard knocking from inside the cliff at the bridge's foot: slow, three or four blows at a time, and then nothing. He will not go back up. [Melerhu]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the hearth-head, needs the meadow's grass after a thin year in the lower pastures, and she means to send the herd over at the thaw with or without him.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The weather comes up the gorge with little warning, and a storm on the span is worse than a storm anywhere else on the range. The gorge is deep enough that nobody has ever recovered anything that fell into it. Wolves follow the herds up to the high pastures and wait below the ledge.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A single arch of dressed stone across the gorge, its joints so fine that a knife blade does not go into them, with a low parapet on each side and the gap where the block fell. At the cliff end the bridge meets a ledge cut level along the foot of the face. The ledge runs for some sixty paces each way and stops against rock. The face above is gray, weathered and unbroken. At the meadow end the bridge lands on turf, and the meadow climbs to a snowfield.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The thaw herd.** Melerhu wants a few steady hands to go up with the herd at the thaw, stand on the ledge in the first frost nights and tell her what Termen heard.
- **Termen's knocking.** Termen wants someone who is not of his hearth to hear the cliff and say he is not a liar, and he guides anyone who goes as far as the bridge.
- **The fallen stone.** The Klemverd hearth pays in butter and wool for anyone who can set the parapet block back, or cut and lift one to match it.

# The Truth of Vrundelv {#truth .secret}

**The hold.** The cliff at the bridge's foot is the face of **Ladhav-hunáth**, "the spanning of the river", a sealed [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] hold in good order. Its gate opens on the ledge behind stone dressed flush with the face, at the ledge's western end, and its homes, halls, forges and deep lie in the cliff behind it. The bridge is the hold's work: it carried the hold's road from the gate-ledge across the gorge to the meadow, which was the hold's pasture. The hold stands in the record of the holds, and [[affiliation-talgnugarhld|Talg-nugár Hold]], the nearest lived-in hold, knows where it lies.

**The sealing.** The holds' record gives the year the hold was sealed and the reason, and no decided account gives either. A GM who needs one takes it from what the accounts allow: the hold sealed with its dead in it, as [[place-khazarturn|Khazartúrn]] was, or sealed when its count fell too low to keep it and its houses went to other holds, as holds were being closed across the world while the Khazari dwindled. The hold is intact either way. The skylights and openings are closed with blocks of granite set flush, the water is shut off at its source, and the halls stand clean and dark.

**The knocking.** In the first hard frosts the granite blocks set flush in the face and the stone around them move against each other, and the passages behind the face carry the sound. It is the cold and nothing else.

> [!TIP]
> Let the ledge do the work. Its sixty level paces stop against rock at both ends, and nobody levels a ledge to nowhere. A party that reads that much has found the hold without opening anything.

**Opening it.** A sealed hold's door stays shut, and every Khazari hold keeps that rule. A party that tries the gate is trying a door the holds mean to keep shut, and the GM decides when a hold learns of it.
