> **GM-only note.** Zaghar-gunáv lies two days from [[place-khazarturn|Khazartúrn]], and where Khazartúrn stands is the one thing every Khazari hold keeps from outsiders. No public note places the ridge, and nothing in this note belongs in one.

## Overview {#overview}

**Zaghar-gunáv**, "the delving of ore", is the first hold the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]] cut anywhere but in Khazartúrn, and the first place their record calls a hold, _khardev_. It lies in a cave on the ridge north of the city's valley in [[place-sthrnwall|the Southern Wall]] of [[place-graznmntns|the Grazian Mountains]], two days' walk from the city. Its sister hold, [[place-farzsumad|Farz-sumád]], lies a day farther along the same ridge. Both are sealed. The holds count them among their closed places, and every hold's copy of [[lore-clansquarrel|The Quarrel of the Clans]] descends from the one the city sent up to Zaghar-gunáv.

## What the Ridge Shows

The cave mouth opens on a south face above a stream, and the hold's homes stood in the face around it. From the stream bed the face is rock like the rest of the ridge: the openings are walled and dressed to the stone, and the gate is shut and finished flush. The stream still comes out of the hill below the mouth, and in hard frost a thread of mist stands over the crack it breathes through. The vein the finder followed runs along the ridge in its outcrop, and the outcrop shows the old workings only to a reader who knows the cut of a Khazari chisel.

## Who Knows

Every Khazari hold keeps the founding of Zaghar-gunáv in its archive, because the account of the quarrel that sent the first holds out is read to every elder before she takes a council seat. The holds know where the ridge lies because they know where the city lies. No human record names the hold, and no road, shrine or herders' path comes near the ridge.

## The Present {#present}

Nothing at Zaghar-gunáv has moved since the sealing. The face is whole, the gate is shut, and the stream runs below it. The nearest human account of the country is the pilgrim's entry in the register of a glacier-spring temple of the Wall, which the temple's ushtaka has copied for the register of [[place-pssshrines|the Pass-Shrines]]. The entry tells of a still lake under a cliff with seven ribs of rock and no track to it. It says nothing of the ridge, which lies two days north of the lake and is reached the same way, over the ice in the few open weeks of the year.

:::secret {title="Why the ridge is shut"}
The holds' records give the year each ridge hold was sealed and the reason, and no account a GM can read in these notes gives either. Two readings fit what the accounts say. In the first, the remnant sealed the ridge holds with the city after [[lore-khazarturn|the Fall of Khazartúrn]], as part of its closing, so that nothing within two days of the valley marks it. In the second, the ridge holds were sealed earlier, when their counts fell too low to keep them, as holds were being closed across the world while the Khazari dwindled, and their houses went back to the city or on to other holds. Choose one and keep [[place-farzsumad|Farz-sumád]] to the same reading.
:::

> [!TIP]
> A party that finds the ridge is two days from the city. Treat anyone who reaches Zaghar-gunáv, or gathers the means to open it, as the holds treat anyone who finds Khazartúrn.

## History {#history}

In the year of the city 435 [Rikid of Khard-vurán]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a finder of the first tower, went along the ridge north of the valley with two of her house, on her own asking, and entered a cave at two days' walk: its mouth on a south face above a stream, dry, with air moving in it, and the vein in its floor. In 438, during the quarrel over the city's farmland, mines and the share of its gate, the council sent the first sending out by the gate: three lines of [Rabad-runát]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, the houses of [Parn-sumád]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} and [Ganv-humák]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, and two lines each of [Kaval-zunáth]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} and [Dalk-thumár]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, 796 in all, with a season's grain by head and sixty-four bars of iron. Rikid read the cave on the fourth day and marked the refuge on the fifth, in a chamber above the stream that breathes through its own crack, before any home was cut. The sending named the hold Zaghar-gunáv, and the city entered it as a hold.

In the year after, the first iron came down from the ridge: 128 bars, smelted at a hearth of stones in the open by a house with no forge, carried four days to the city for sixty-four measures of grain. It is the first exchange in the record of the city between the city and a hold. In 443 the second sending passed through Zaghar-gunáv on its way to cut Farz-sumád, and in 447 the elders of the two holds sat together at Zaghar-gunáv, the first sitting in any record of the elders of more than one hold. The copies of the account of the quarrel that every hold keeps descend from the one the city sent up to Zaghar-gunáv when the account closed in 458.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The pilgrim's entry.** A party that sets out to find the still lake from the Pass-Shrines' register comes within two days of the ridge, and a finder's eye sees the old workings in the outcrop.
- **The breathing crack.** The mist over the stream in hard frost is the refuge's air coming out of the hill, and it is the one sign on the ridge that anything is cut behind the face.
- **The holds' answer.** A party that tries the sealed face draws on itself the doctrine every hold keeps for the city, and the GM decides how soon a hold learns of it.
