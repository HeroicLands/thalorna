**Nilakshetra** is a village of rice terraces on the eastern shoulder of the gold mountain, in the [[place-suvarnagirijnpd|Suvarnageri Janapoda]]. Rice wants standing water, and on a mountain ground that holds water is made rather than found. These terraces are the largest block of watered ground in the janapoda, and from the road below they show as steps of green against the gray rock above.
