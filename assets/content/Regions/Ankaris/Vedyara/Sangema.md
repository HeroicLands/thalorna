**Sangema** (confluence) is the temple on [[place-melkaground|the Melka Ground]], and the only building of any consequence on it. It stands above the highest water [[place-mahanodi|the Mahanodi]] has reached in written record, on a mound raised higher still, and it is the one thing on the plain that does not have to be laid out again after a flood.

## The Sabhapavi

The senior priest of Sangema is **Sabhapavi of the Melka**. He convenes the twelve-yearly assembly of the [[affiliation-janpdsvdyr|confederation]], puts its questions and declares what it has agreed, and he holds no authority at all between assemblies. The office is the nearest thing the confederation has to a head, which is to say that it is not one.

[Mahaprasoda of the Sarveda]{.name data-type="being/npc"} holds it. He is about seventy, presided over the most recent [Mahva-Melka]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} four years ago, and is reckoned the most respected priestly authority in the inland part of the [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara Region]]. The next Mahva-Melka is his last as convening priest, and the succession is under negotiation now.

## The Roll

The temple keeps the roll of the janpodas: which of them sent a delegation to each Melka, which circuit each belongs to, which have been recognized as newly formed, and which have collapsed since the previous assembly and been formally mourned. The reckoning of about six thousand temple-republics in the confederation is the roll's, and everyone who quotes it quotes Sangema. The most remembered entry is [Tirnugram]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}, mourned at the Melka of 320 in the same ten days [[lore-thirdcmpct|the Third Compact]] was sworn ([[lore-silenthall|the Silenced Hall]]); its descendants have never accepted the division of its land.

The roll is also the standing argument. Which circuit a border janpoda belongs to has gone to litigation, in one case for two hundred and ten years, and the temple is where the litigation is heard.

## The Council of Three

Sangema's senior priest is one of the three whose informal consultations are called [The Council of Three]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, with the senior priests of [[place-surtatempl|the Surta temple]] at the source of [[place-chandrmahi|the Chandramahi]] and of [[place-bhrnytemple|the Mahajaya Temple at Bharanya]], the great temple at [[place-bharanya|Bharanya]]. A joint pronouncement of the three on a matter before the confederation carries near-universal weight. It works as an executive committee, and the priests deny that it is one.
