**Shankhadvar**, the conch-door, is the last crossing of the wall and the worst. It runs beneath [[place-kalashkhr|Kalashikhar]] at the eastern end of [[place-estrnreach|the Eastern Reach]] and takes two months to reach anywhere in [[place-tantsouregin|Tantsou]] worth reaching. The name is for the sound the wind makes in the upper gorge, which is audible from the col on a still night and which nobody who has heard it describes twice.

Commerce does not use it. Two or three parties a year go over, and in a bad year none.

## Why It Is Garrisoned

Four centuries ago a Tantsou-backed adventurer came through this door with a few hundred men behind him, in [[lore-conchdoor|the Conch-Door Incursion]], and the [[affiliation-janpdsvdyr|Mahva-Sangra]] raised the last army the temple-republics have ever raised to put him back out of it. What the janpodas took from the affair was not a victory but a precedent, and they have been jealous of it ever since: [[place-sangrafort|the Sangra-fort]] on the col is the only standing garrison the confederation maintains, it has never been withdrawn, and the question of who may raise armed men in the name of the whole is settled by the fact that this one exists.

[[affiliation-shikhalaya|Shikhalaya]] reads the fort as a rival's outpost inside a frontier the kingdom considers its own to watch, and says so at every [Mahva-Melka]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}.

## The Crossing Itself

The upper road is a shelf cut in rock above a gorge that no one has ever measured, in a place where the wind changes without warning. The [[affiliation-hvarnguides|Hvarn]] hearth that is assigned it for the season is small, is related by marriage to half the garrison, and has never lost a party—a record it declines to explain and which the fort's commanders have long since stopped asking about.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The record nobody explains.** The [[affiliation-hvarnguides|Hvarn]] hearth that draws the conch-door has never lost a party. The [[place-sangrafort|Sangra-fort]]'s commander, in the first of his three years, asks the party to cross with it and learn how, which his predecessors stopped asking long ago.
- **Half a garrison.** The janpoda whose turn it is to man the fort has sent half its men. The commander asks the party to go down into the Bharava circuit and bring up the rest before the window closes, because a fort of thirty is a gift to [[affiliation-shikhalaya|Shikhalaya]]'s complaint at the next [Mahva-Melka]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}.
- **Late in the season.** A small party has asked the fort's leave to go over the conch-door into [[place-tantsouregin|Tantsou]] late in the season, carrying nothing a caravan carries. The commander asks the party to go with it as far as the col and learn its business. The last armed men to use this door came the other way, with Tantsou backing.
