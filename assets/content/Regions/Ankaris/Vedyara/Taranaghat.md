A loaded cart crosses [[place-sarvedarivr|the Sarveda]] above [[place-bowfort|the bow-fort]] at one place only, and the village that holds it is **Taranaghat** (820). It stands where the river leaves the hills. The ford works from the end of the rains to the start of them, so plan a heavy cart for the dry months: through the wet season the village runs two flat ferries and a rope.

The janpoda takes a toll here on everything but pilgrims and academy aspirants. The toll is a fifth of what the sabhra spends in a year, and the sabhra's reckoner comes up twice a season to see it counted.

The village is walled, which no other village of the janpoda is. The wall is low and would not stop a determined force. It stops the kind of trouble a crossing attracts, which is what it was built for.
