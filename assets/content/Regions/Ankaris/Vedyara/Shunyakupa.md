**Shūnyakūpa**, the empty well, is a great lineage's well on the eastern edge of [[place-vandhyabhumi|Vandhyabhūmi]], and it has had no water in it for nearly three centuries. About 440 AF a raid out of the river country took the lineage's wells and the king of [[affiliation-gomarga|Gomārga]] did not ride to defend them. The lineage filled this well with stones so the raiders could not water at it, and walked its herds off the plateau ([[lore-emptycrct|the Second Breaking and the Empty Circuit]]).

## What Is There

The well-head is a ring of cut stone wide enough for four troughs, with the troughs still standing round it and the stone channels that fed them dry and cracked. The shaft is stopped to within a man's height of the lip with fieldstone, carried there by the lineage's own households in the days before they left. A droveway runs past it, east and west, that nobody drives any more, and along it stand the well-villages the Empty Circuit emptied: mud walls, the roofs fallen in, threshing floors grown over, and at each one a smaller well that dried when nobody was left to clear it.

## What It Means

The plateau says "he filled the well" of a man who broke his word, and this is the well. A lineage that fills its own well is saying the bargain is over, and Shūnyakūpa is the one great well on the plateau anyone has ever filled.

Drovers will not water near it, and the droveway that passed it has a new course two days to the south. The dead well-villages are not a _shorukshetra_ (blood-field); nobody died here in any number. The drovers keep away from them at night all the same, because empty villages on the plateau are where anything that hunts the herds goes to ground.

## Hooks

- **Reopening the well.** The lineage that filled it proposes to clear it and water at it again. The crown must decide whether a reopened Shūnyakūpa is a new well, the lineage's to keep on its own terms, or the old one, still owed the old guarantee the crown failed to honor. Either answer sets a precedent for every well on the plateau.
- **Something in the villages.** Herds driven near the dead droveway have been losing stock at night, and what took them left no tracks a drover recognizes. The lineage that wants the well reopened needs the villages cleared first, and is offering water-rights for the work.
- **The pledge.** The lineage's head carried the well's pledge on his person when the lineage walked off, as the great lineages still do. It was lost on the road a century later, and whoever holds it now can argue the well is theirs.
