**Dahanaghat**, the burning landing, is a broad stone stair on the north bank of [[place-sarvedarivr|the Sarveda]], a mile above [[place-naughat|Naughat]] where the river bends, and it is where the dead of the [[affiliation-srvdmthjnpd|Sarveda Mouth Janpoda]] are carried to be burned. A household that lives within a day's cart of it brings its dead here. Beside the stair stands the janpoda's temple of the passage, and the river carries away what the pyres leave.

## What Is There

Twelve stone slabs, each a _dahanashila_ for a single pyre, stand in a row along the foot of the stair, with a drain behind each for the river to take the ash. Above them a roofed gallery shelters the mourners, and at its head stands the shrine of [[affiliation-kalavrata|Kalavrata]], a plain stone room with a black conch on a pedestal and a bowl of blank-filed copper coins beside it. The gallery has a bench for the pyre-keeper's family and a long table for the weighing of urns.

The hereditary keeper of the ground is [Kholu]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a heavy, soft-spoken man of the third generation of his house, who tends the pyres, sounds the conch, hands the coin to the household and gives the ash back in an urn. His family's work is old and respected. The household of the dead sits in the gallery until it is done, then goes home with the urn.

## What Is Said of It

The river villages say that something is wrong at the Landing. They say it quietly, at funerals.

- The pyres at the Landing burn all night, and the families gho home with urns that are too light.
- A body is laid on the slab and in the morning the slab is clean, and the family is told the fire took it quickly.
- The dead stand in the river below the stair at night, up to their knees, looking at the steps.
- The conch at the Landing is sounded later than it should be, and sometimes not at all.
- A widow in white has been seen at every large funeral for two years, and nobody knows her family.

## What It Is

Between the sounding of the conch and the lighting of the pyre there is a gap, and the Vedyari know it. It is the space in which the way a soul has just gone is still open behind it. [[affiliation-thresholdkeepers|Threshold-keepers]] work in that gap by warrant. At Dahanaghat, someone else does.

A fallen Ritrija, [Dakunata]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, uses the gap. He has learned that a body not burned within the day, with its coin withheld and the conch held back, stays in the gap, and that what stays can be asked a good deal more than one question. He pays Kholu to set certain bodies aside, and Kholu, who is in debt to a lender with no name, does. The bodies gho down a stair under the cenotaph beyond the gallery, where a cell of the [Chinnasutra]{.name data-type="affiliation/criminal"}, the craft-lines [[affiliation-adanshreni|the Adanshreni]] cut away, keeps its takings. Its sentries are the unburned dead.

The gap, left open too often in one place, stops closing. The ground at the Landing is becoming a [[lore-bloodfield|_shorukshetra_]]. [[lore-theshadow|The Shadow]] has found a place where the way behind the souls is wide.

## Who Comes

- **Households** of the janpoda, with their dead and their small coin.
- **A Threshold-keeper**, now and then, under warrant from a court, to put the one question.
- **The [[affiliation-agmipantra|Agmi-pantra]]**, who have been asked to purge the ground and have not been told why.
- **Factors of [[affiliation-twilighths|the Twilight House]]**, who attend the funerals of the rich as mourners and buy the news of the gallery.

## Reaching It

A road runs upriver from Naughat along the north bank, a mile of packed earth between rice-ground and reeds. Carts of the dead come down it at dawn. The Landing can also be reached by river, and the keeper keeps a landing-boat. At night the road is empty.

## Hooks

- **The light urn.** A Danhija household buried an urn that rattled. The widow has been told to say nothing, and wants someone to ask the ash.
- **The cell.** The Sabhra of the shrena wants a Chinnasutra cell removed and cannot be seen to, and the cell has chosen the one place nobody searches.
- **The ember.** A Jvelita has been asked to purge the Landing and arrives to find the pyre-keeper's family watching him from the gallery.
