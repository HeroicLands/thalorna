**Haritagrāma** is a small terrace village on the northern slope of the [[place-suvarnagirijnpd|Suvarnagiri Janapada]], and it is two panning families and the households that live beside them. The two are among the twenty-eight hereditary families, take the sabhā's fixed wage like the rest, and carry what their stream gives down to the weighing-station. The village has no other trade.
