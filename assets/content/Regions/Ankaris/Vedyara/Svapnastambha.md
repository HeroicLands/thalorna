**Svapnastambha** (the dream-tower) stands on the rock shelf above the wells of [[place-marukupa|Marukūpa]], and it is the westernmost house of the [[affiliation-svapnasarisamaja|Svapnasāri-samāja]] anywhere. It is a square tower of four stories with a walled court beside it, a cistern under the court, and room for nine of the sect and about thirty guests.

The upper two stories are dream-chambers. The sect's business here is what it is everywhere: a petitioner sleeps, a keeper watches, and what he brings back is read in the morning and written into the house's record. The house has kept that record for three hundred years, and it is consulted more often by caravan masters than by anybody in a capital.

## Why It Is Here

The tower is the last roof in Vedyara a traveler sleeps under before the crossing. The sect built here for that. A master going west sleeps here and is read before he loads. The reading costs nothing and the grain left afterward is what keeps the house, and no master has been known to leave less than the man ahead of him.

The crown's post below has no authority over the tower and the tower asks nothing of the post. The two institutions share a cistern and, in a bad season, a wall.
