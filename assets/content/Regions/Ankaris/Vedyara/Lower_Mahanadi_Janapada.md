The **Lower Mahānadī Janapada** is the janapada of the Mahānadī circuit to which [[place-bharanya|Bharanya]] belongs. The town holds no seat of its own at the Mela; what it has instead is the standing of its [[affiliation-mahajaya|Mahājaya]] temple, which is greater than the janapada's and older.
