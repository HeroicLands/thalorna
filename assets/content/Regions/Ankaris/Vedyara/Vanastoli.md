**Vanastoli** (330) is a small woodland village at the head of the valley, in the [[place-danurkotjnpd|Danurkot Janpoda]], where the sound of axes carries down the slope all day. Its sawyers cut the timber of the academy halls and the fort, and the beams of [[place-danurkot|Danurkot]]'s halls and walls started on its hillsides.
