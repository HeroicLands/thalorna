**Pushpavana** (270) grows flowers for the temple, and once a year the town of [[place-rajapur|Rajepur]] depends on it. The village is a ring of gardens in the [[place-rajapurjnpd|Rajepur Janapoda]], and its garland-makers supply the [Spring Festival of Vyalendra]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} (see [[affiliation-vyalendra|Vyalendra]]), when the town takes perhaps fifteen thousand visitors, better than fifty times Pushpavana's own number.

Come for the festival and every garland you see in the temple precinct was made from a flower grown here.
