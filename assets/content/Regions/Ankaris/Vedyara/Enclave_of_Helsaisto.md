> **GM-only note.** The hostel-keepers and renunciants of the upper Bharava know the valley as [[place-hermitshollw|the Hermits' Hollow]] and know nothing of who lives there.

## Overview {#overview}

The **Enclave of Helsaisto** is the community of about ninety of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]] at the head of the valley in [[place-forestnoroad|the Forest of No Road]] that the hostel-keepers of the upper [[place-bharavarivr|Bharava]] call [[place-hermitshollw|the Hermits' Hollow]]. _Helsaisto_ is "the place of shade", from _helsai_, "shade, shelter from the light", and _-sto_, "the place of it". Renunciants sit at its edge, and none of them has seen it.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

A Senalo of Helsaisto belongs to a hearth the enclave keeps. The hearths raise a lord for things done, by every voice, and a lord's standing is a rank and not a holding. The enclave's head office is the **Helsainno**, the Keeper of the Shade, raised by every voice of the hearths and held for as long as they do not raise another. The Helsainno is [[being-rissettilssnt|Rìssetti Loussinto]]. He keeps the hollow and the [[lore-ellevirsa|guest-right]] of its edge, says aloud what the hearths agree, and drives out an outlaw, a _vyerka_, when the hearths ask it.

The lords of the enclave include [[being-ellinelatnnvn|Ellinela Tunnevinto]]. Among its members are the hunters [[being-huvettavotrknt|Huvettavo Tìrkente]] and [[being-lernuvovltnt|Lernuvo Vaultinto]], and the healer [[being-nelpelaaunkkn|Nelpela Aunekkento]].

## Current Situation {#present}

A renunciant came down from the stone this spring after twelve years, told to go home by Lernuvo Vaultinto, who found him in fever with the rains coming and spoke to him. No hearth of Helsaisto has spoken to a renunciant before. Forty pilgrims now wait at the hostel at the foot of the stream for the renunciant to lead them up the stream, and he refuses.

The hearths are divided twice over. On the crowd, Rìssetti holds that guest-right covers one who sits and asks nothing, and that forty who come asking to be led are not guests and are turned like any traveler; Ellinela wants the hollow closed to everyone, the solitary renunciants at the stone included, until the crowd has gone home. On Lernuvo, some hearths hold that he broke the enclave's silence and must answer for it, and Nelpela Aunekkento, who left leaves for the renunciant's fever on the stone for three nights before Lernuvo spoke, holds that he kept guest-right better than the silence did.

## Defense {#arms}

Walls and armies do not protect the enclave. The hollow does. A renunciant who walks on past the stone is led round in the green shade and comes out at the hostel, and a traveler who comes up the stream without sitting finds the stream divides where it does not divide and brings him down again. Huvettavo Tìrkente keeps the paths above the stone and sees every traveler before the traveler sees the stream.

## The Hollow {#holdings}

[[place-hermitshollw|The Hermits' Hollow]]
: the valley of great trees where the hearths stand, with the flat stone at the head of the stream where the renunciants sit.

## History {#history}

No Vedyari record says when the Senali came to the forest below the Grazian foothills, or when renunciants first went up the stream. Nothing of Helsaisto's own past has come out of the enclave.

## Relations {#relations}

- **The renunciants** sit at the stone by the enclave's leave and never know it.
- **The hostel-keepers** see renunciants off up the stream and ask nothing, and the enclave has never spoken with them.
- **[[affiliation-nclvtlvst|Tilvaisto]]**, the other enclave of the forest, keeps its own counsel, and the two take no part in each other's affairs.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The renunciant's refusal.** The crowd at the hostel at the foot of the stream wants the renunciant who came down this spring to lead it into the hollow. [[being-lernuvovltnt|Lernuvo Vaultinto]] wants him kept from it, and watches the hostel yard from the trees.
- **The closed hollow.** If the hearths agree with [[being-ellinelatnnvn|Ellinela Tunnevinto]], the next renunciant to go up the stream finds no stone, no shade and no way in, and the hostel-keepers ask a party to learn why.
- **Fever at the stone.** [[being-nelpelaaunkkn|Nelpela Aunekkento]] has seen two more sitters at the stone fall sick this season, and the leaves she sets out for them are not enough.
