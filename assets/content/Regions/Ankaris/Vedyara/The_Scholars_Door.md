**Jnanadvar**, the scholars' door, crosses [[place-estrnreach|the Eastern Reach]] north-east to the western frontier of [[place-tantsouregin|Tantsou]]. It is a month and a half of travel and the least dangerous of the eastern crossings, which is a comparison and no recommendation.

It carries the goods a caravan can afford to move slowly and the people nobody else will carry: manuscripts, medicines, instruments, the occasional astronomer, and the envoys of both sides. The name is not ironic. More scholarship has crossed the wall by this road than by all the others together. The [[place-pssshrines|Pass-Shrine]] at its summit keeps the fullest crossing-register on the range, because the people who use it are the people who write things down.

## The Way-Station

Beyond the throat, on the Tantsou side, stands a frontier way-station of the celestial administration. It feeds travelers, houses them for as long as the weather requires, and records each of them in a register that has been kept without a gap for four hundred years. Vedyari visitors are treated with courtesy and are never permitted to see the register.

The [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]'s official position is that the Vedyari shrenis are a tributary trade of no administrative consequence. Its frontier officers keep a register, send escorts, and stand under orders to meet a Vedyari party at the col and not before. That is the practice of a frontier that is watched.

## The Guides

This is [[affiliation-hvarnguides|Hvarn ground]]. The Hvarn work the eastern crossings as [[affiliation-osketguides|the Osket]] work the western, are a smaller people, and are on noticeably better terms with the temple-republics than with the kingdom—[[place-sangrafort|the Sangra-fort]] two passes east is supplied through Hvarn hands, and everyone on both sides finds it convenient not to discuss what that costs.

## Hooks

- **Instruments for the far side.** A reckoner of the [[affiliation-ganokashalda|Reckoners' College]] must take a case of instruments over to a colleague beyond the wall, and the case will not survive a fall. He asks the party to carry it with him and bring it to the way-station whole.
- **How the register is kept.** Vedyari visitors to the Tantsou way-station are fed, housed and never shown its register. A Shikhalayan Dutsa, who wants to know why a frontier that calls the shrenis of no consequence keeps one, asks the party to winter at the station and learn how it is kept and who reads it.
- **What the fort costs.** The [[place-sangrafort|Sangra-fort]] two passes east is supplied through Hvarn hands, and nobody on either side discusses what that costs. A treasurer of the Bharava circuit, whose janpodas pay for the fort, asks the party to cross by this road with the Hvarn and learn what the contract buys besides grain.
