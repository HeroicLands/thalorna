"He filled the well," the plateau says of a man who broke his word, and the saying comes from this century. The **Empty Circuit** is the hundred years in which [[affiliation-gomarga|Gomārga]] was a kingdom of wells with no herds at them.

## The Second Half

The plateau's bargain has two halves: the crown takes a share of what a lineage's well earns, and the crown guarantees the lineage against anyone who tries to take the well. The first half was broken by a king's greed about 265 ([[lore-wellbrk1|the First Breaking]]). The second was broken by a king's absence.

About 440 AF a raid came up out of the river country and took the wells of a great lineage on the eastern edge of [[place-vandhyabhumi|Vandhyabhūmi]]. The lineage sent to the crown. The king, **Vishānapāla**, was on his circuit three wells away, and he did not ride. The standing camp's record enters that he did not, and does not say why.

## The Walk-Off

The lineage did not wait for him. It filled its own great well with stones so that the raiders could not water at it ([[place-shunyakupa|Shūnyakūpa]]), and walked its herds off the plateau. The other great lineages, seeing what the guarantee was worth, walked theirs off after it. A herd-owning lineage can move its whole wealth out of a bad district in a fortnight, and that season the great lineages moved theirs out of the kingdom.

They kept them out for a century. The moving court went on traveling the circuit, arriving at each well as the herds had always arrived, and found the troughs dry and the keepers gone. The well-villages along the droveways emptied. The crown's revenue, counted in watering dues, fell to almost nothing, and the kingdom's worst century followed.

## The Return

About 540 the great lineages walked back onto the circuit, on terms the crown has kept since: the _rājñī_ keeps a standing camp at the winter wells with the treasury, the written record and the hostages, so that the crown's word is held somewhere the crown cannot walk away from.

## What It Left

- **The empty well-villages** along a dead droveway on the eastern plateau, and Shūnyakūpa among them.
- **The standing camp**, and its hostages.
- **The saying.** A filled well is the plateau's sign for a broken word.
