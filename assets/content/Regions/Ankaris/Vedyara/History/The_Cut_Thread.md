The [[affiliation-adanasreni|Ādānashrenī]] works under taboos, and the first of them is that nobody is killed during a taking. Two generations ago some of its craft-lines decided that taboo was a luxury. The shrenī cut them off, and the **Cut Thread** is what the shrenī calls that night.

## The Schism

About 660 AF the craft-lines that rejected the taboos, chiefly the prohibition on killing, were cut off from the shrenī by its own rite. The lines cut off kept their skills, their contacts and their knowledge of how the shrenī works, and they took a name from what had been done to them: the **Chinnasūtra**, the cut thread.

## Why It Matters

The shrenī treats every other rival as competition. It treats the Chinnasūtra as a matter of its survival, for a plain reason: a killing during a taking is read as a shrenī killing, whoever did it, and charters have been suspended for Chinnasūtra work the shrenī had nothing to do with. Every Chinnasūtra killing costs the Ādānashrenī its standing with the courts that tolerate it.
