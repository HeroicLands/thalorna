The roll at [[place-sangama|Sangama]] carries every janapada that has collapsed since the confederation began counting, and most of them went quietly: a drought, a river that moved, villages that drifted into a neighbor's sabhā. **Thirnugrāma** did not go quietly. Its entry is the reason the priests of [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]] will not say the conch-door adventurer's name, and the **Silenced Hall** is what they call the night it ended.

## The Example

About 320 AF an adventurer with Tānvüri backing brought a few hundred picked men over the conch-door ([[lore-conchdoor|the Conch-Door Incursion]]) and went through the eastern janapadas one at a time. Most of them submitted. A janapada fields a few hundred militia at short notice, and none of them could field enough alone.

Thirnugrāma, the village of the bell, in the hill country below the conch-door road, would not. Its sabhā met in its temple hall to refuse his terms. He shut the doors on it while it sat and fired the roof.

## Why It Is Not a Blood-field

The hall burned with the sabhā inside it, and the hall was its own pyre. That is the difference between this ground and a _shorukshetra_ (blood-field): the dead of Thirnugrāma had fire, and they passed. What did not survive was the janapada. Its record, its survey, its boundary stones' register and its genealogies were kept in the temple, as every janapada's are, and they burned with the men who kept them. A janapada with no record and no sabhā has nothing left to govern with.

## The Mela of 320

The janapadas' third confederation army put the adventurer out through the door he had come in by. That year the Mela met, and in the same ten days it swore [[lore-thirdcmpct|the Third Compact]], it mourned Thirnugrāma on the roll. It divided the janapada's land among its neighbors, ordered the hall's doors bricked up with the bell still hanging in the gate, and the priests agreed among themselves that the adventurer's name would not be spoken. [[lore-conchdoor|The Conch-Door Incursion]] still withholds it.

## What It Left

- **The hall.** [[place-thirnuhall|The Silenced Hall of Thirnugrāma]] stands roofless with its doors bricked and its bell unrung.
- **The division.** The neighboring janapadas hold Thirnugrāma's land by the Mela's decision. The janapada's scattered lineages have never accepted it.
- **The silence.** The adventurer is unnamed in every Vedyari account.
