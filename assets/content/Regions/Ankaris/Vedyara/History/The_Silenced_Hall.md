The roll at [[place-sangema|Sangema]] carries every janpoda that has collapsed since the confederation began counting, and most of them went quietly: a drought, a river that moved, villages that drifted into a neighbor's sabhra. [Tirnugram]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} did not go quietly. Its entry is the reason the priests of [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]] will not say the conch-door adventurer's name, and **the Silenced Hall** is what they call the night it ended.

## The Example

About 320 AF an adventurer with Tantsou backing brought a few hundred picked men over the conch-door ([[lore-conchdoor|the Conch-Door Incursion]]) and went through the eastern janpodas one at a time. Most of them submitted. A janpoda fields a few hundred militia at short notice, and none of them could field enough alone.

[Tirnugram]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, the village of the bell, in the hill country below the conch-door road, would not. Its sabhra met in its temple hall to refuse his terms. He shut the doors on it while it sat and fired the roof.

## Why It Is Not a Blood-field

The hall burned with the sabhra inside it, and the hall was its own pyre. That is the difference between this ground and a _shorukshetra_ (blood-field): the dead of Tirnugram had fire, and they passed. What did not survive was the janpoda. Its record, its survey, its boundary stones' register and its genealogies were kept in the temple, as every janpoda's are, and they burned with the men who kept them. A janpoda with no record and no sabhra has nothing left to govern with.

## The Melka of 320

The janpodas' third confederation army put the adventurer out through the door he had come in by. That year the [Mahva-Melka]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} met, and in the same ten days it swore [[lore-thirdcmpct|the Third Compact]], it mourned Tirnugram on the roll. It divided the janpoda's land among its neighbors, ordered the hall's doors bricked up with the bell still hanging in the gate, and the priests agreed among themselves that the adventurer's name would not be spoken. [[lore-conchdoor|The Conch-Door Incursion]] still withholds it.

## What It Left

- **The hall.** [[place-tirnuhall|The Silenced Hall of Tirnugram]] stands roofless with its doors bricked and its bell unrung.
- **The division.** The neighboring janpodas hold Tirnugram's land by the Melka's decision. The janpoda's scattered lineages have never accepted it.
- **The silence.** The adventurer is unnamed in every Vedyari account.
