If the **Age of Copyists** has a sound, it is a stylus on a palm leaf. For four centuries after [[lore-fortydays|the Forty Days]], [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]] wrote things down: [[place-rajavalilib|the Rājavalī Library]] kept the dissolved kingdom's record whole, the copying country of the Sarvada began, endowments were written and land-tenure was recorded, and the jurists began to write their commentaries. [[place-madhavendra|Madhusthāna]] emptied in the same centuries without any event to explain it, and the scholars who name the age do not try.

## What Happened

The age was quiet and was not peaceful. About 125 BF a lord of the indigo valleys took the cloth city and was crowned in it ([[lore-vylndrcrwn|the Raising of the Vyālendri Crown]]). About 80 BF raiders from a neighboring hill kingdom took a month's gold off Suvarnagiri's weighing-station, and the king of Bharyastān brought it back by weight ([[lore-lastgldrd|the Last Gold Raid]]), which is the last time the gold mountain has lost gold to anyone. About 200 BF the [[affiliation-agnipantha|Agnī-panthā]] was put out of the city temples for the first time ([[lore-agnipartng|the Three Partings]]).

About 60 AF the clans of the northern highlands chartered a king to hold the road through Sūryadvāra ([[lore-passcharter|the Charter of the Pass-Road]]). About 120 AF the Mahānadī moved and [[lore-drwnngseat|drowned a royal seat]].

## Rulers

- **Vyālendra:** **Nīlapati**, crowned about 125 BF, then nine kings nobody outside the cloth-court remembers, then the eleventh and last, unnamed in every account.
- **Bharyastān:** **Dhūrsavīra**, who swore the gold treaty about 80 BF; his line has held the kingdom by inheritance since, broken twice by acclamation.
- **Shikharālaya:** **Himavīra**, chartered about 60 AF.

## Its End

About 150 AF the eleventh Vyālendri king's line ended, and the Loom-Council sat down in his audience hall ([[lore-vylndrkngs|the End of the Vyālendri Kingship]]). The [[lore-hundredcrwn|Hundred Crowns]] follow.
