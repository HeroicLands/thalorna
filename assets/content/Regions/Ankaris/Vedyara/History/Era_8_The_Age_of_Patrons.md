A Vedyari polity of the present rarely fears a neighbor's army. It fears its patron's displeasure, its own succession and the factor of [[affiliation-mrchntclctvvdyr|the Merchant Collective of Vedyara]] at the gate, and **the Age of Patrons** is the two centuries in which that became true. It is the age a traveler walks into.

## What Happened

About 525 AF a kingdom up the river took [[affiliation-rajeprjnpd|Rajepur Janpoda]]'s head-gate village for a season and was put out by [[affiliation-chendrapur|Chendrapur]]'s money and a hired company, and the janpoda, which had kept no soldiers since the dissolution, signed a formal protection with the city ([[lore-rjprprotct|Rajepur Under Protection]]). Small mountain kingdoms became patrons of [[affiliation-dnrktjnpd|Danurkot Janpoda]] and [[affiliation-suvrgrjnpd|Suvargeri Janpoda]] on the same terms, and protection replaced conquest as the way a strong polity held a weak one.

About 560 the [[affiliation-agmipantra|Agmi-pantra]] scoured a temple in [[place-vyalendra3|Vyalendra]] and was banned from the city for it, and a generation later the council that banned it put up a statue of the priest ([[lore-agmipartng|the Three Partings]]). About 590 the [Martial Council]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} at [[place-suryagarha|Suryagarha]] acclaimed a dead king's widow ([[lore-widowaccl|the Widow's Acclamation]]). About 660 [[affiliation-adanshreni|the Adanshreni]] cut off the craft-lines that would kill during a taking ([[lore-cutthread|the Cut Thread]]). Two winters ago the spring at [[place-chandrprbh|Chandraprabhava]] froze for nine days, and the temple has not written it down ([[lore-nineice|the Nine Days of Ice]]).

## Rulers

[[affiliation-shikhalaya|Shikhalaya]]
: the widow [Jvalasundi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, acclaimed about 590; three reigns since have kept the present policy on [[place-gudesroad|the Guides' Road]]; the present Maharava's successor is disputed between the two councils.

Chendrapur
: about 600 the temples refused a claimant for the second time, and about 640 the crown refused a House's succession for the second time. Neither refusal has been explained.

[[affiliation-bhumipal|Bhumipal]]
: a line confirmed by the Martial Council of the forts since [Kavrabira]{.name data-type="being/npc"}.

## The Present

The age's quarrels are a traveler's quarrels: the Shikhalayan councils at odds over the next acclamation, [[being-anrjhrshkr|Anuraja Harshakar]]'s letters urging further dissolutions, the succession to [[place-sangema|Sangema]]'s convening priest before the next [Mahva-Melka]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}, and a spring that froze and was not recorded.
