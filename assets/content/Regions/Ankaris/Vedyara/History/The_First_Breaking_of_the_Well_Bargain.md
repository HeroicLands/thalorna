A [[affiliation-gomarga|Gomārgi]] well-keeper tells a lowland drover that the wells are not the crown's, and that the crown knows it better than anyone because it once forgot. The **First Breaking of the Well Bargain** is the forgetting.

## The Bargain

The plateau's wells were cut by named lineages, and each is heritable within the lineage that cut it. The crown takes a share of what a well earns from passing herds and guarantees the lineage against anyone who tries to take the well. That is the whole of the bargain, and both halves of it have been broken, once each.

## The First Half

About 265 AF the king, **Vrishasūra**, wanted a war on the river. The plateau's wealth is stock and watering dues, and a host is paid in coin and grain bought from the valleys. He took the wells of three great lineages into the crown's own hand, named his own keepers to them, and spent what they earned on the war.

The lineages did what the plateau's herd-owners can do and nobody else in [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]] can. They walked their herds off the circuit, out of the kingdom's reach, and left the crown holding three wells with nobody coming to drink at them. The war was lost about 270 at the plateau's foot ([[lore-fieldelephnt|the Field of Elephants]]), and the crown, with no host and no revenue, gave the wells back.

## The Clause

At the next accession the oath every Mahārāja swears gained a clause. The oath had always said the crown holds the wells and the droveways and does not adjudicate grass. It now says, after that, that the crown holds no lineage's well in its own hand. Every Mahārāja has sworn it since.

## What It Left

- **The clause**, recited at every accession on the circuit.
- **The pledge on the person.** The lineages took to keeping a well's pledge, the token of the lineage's title, on the body of the lineage's head and not in the standing camp's record. The great lineages still do.
- **The second half.** The guarantee half of the bargain held for another century and a half, and then broke too ([[lore-emptycrct|the Second Breaking and the Empty Circuit]]).
