The **Compact Peace** is two centuries in which no Vedyari polity destroyed another, and they were not quiet centuries. The [[lore-thirdcmpct|Third Compact]] made unification unnecessary: the seats built roads, caravanserai and watch posts in common, and the [[affiliation-mrchntclctvvdyr|Merchant Collective]] learned to be the thing that crossed every border. Wars went on. They stopped ending kingdoms.

## What Happened

About 415 AF the mountain kingdom of **Dhaunapur** marched on Suvarnagiri with Dhanurkoti graduates in its companies. The four academy masters recalled every graduate in its service, and those who came home rode with Bharyastān's horse to the gorge-mouth below the gold road and stopped the host there ([[lore-hghlndwar|the Highland War]]). It is the last time either janapada was seriously threatened.

About 440 a raid out of the river country took a great lineage's wells on the plateau and the king of [[affiliation-gomarga|Gomārga]] did not ride to them. The lineages walked their herds off the circuit, and for a century the moving court arrived at empty wells ([[lore-emptycrct|the Second Breaking and the Empty Circuit]]).

About 470 the Council of the Dream condemned the dream-line that works on the waking ([[lore-chayacondm|the Condemnation of the Chaya-vrata]]), and the vow went on outside the monasteries, one student at a time.

## Rulers

- **Gomārga:** **Vishānapāla**, who did not ride.
- **Bharyastān:** the council passed over an heir who had not ridden to Sharamukha, one of the kingdom's two acclamations in four centuries.
- **Shikharālaya:** the Martial Council acclaimed a cousin over the heir about 450.
- **Chandrapur:** about 450 the crown refused a House's succession for the first time, and that House lost its seat within a generation.

## Its End

About 540 the plateau's great lineages walked back onto the circuit, and the empty century ended. By then Rājapur had already gone under Chandrapur's protection, and the [[lore-agepatrons|Age of Patrons]] had begun.
