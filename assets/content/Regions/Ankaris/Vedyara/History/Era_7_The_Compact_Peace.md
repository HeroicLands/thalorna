**The Compact Peace** is two centuries in which no Vedyari polity destroyed another, and they were not quiet centuries. [[lore-thirdcmpct|The Third Compact]] made unification unnecessary: the seats built roads, caravanserai and watch posts in common, and [[affiliation-mrchntclctvvdyr|the Merchant Collective]] learned to be the thing that crossed every border. Wars went on. They stopped ending kingdoms.

## What Happened

About 415 AF the mountain kingdom of [Daunapur]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} marched on [[affiliation-suvrgrjnpd|Suvargeri]] with Danurkot graduates in its companies. The four academy masters recalled every graduate in its service, and those who came home rode with the horse of [[affiliation-bharyastan|the Kingdom of Bharyastan]] to the gorge-mouth below the gold road and stopped the host there ([[lore-hghlndwar|the Highland War]]). It is the last time either janpoda was seriously threatened.

About 440 a raid out of the river country took a great lineage's wells on the plateau and the king of [[affiliation-gomarga|Gomarga]] did not ride to them. The lineages walked their herds off the circuit, and for a century the moving court arrived at empty wells ([[lore-emptycrct|the Second Breaking and the Empty Circuit]]).

About 470 the [Council of the Dream]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} condemned the dream-line that works on the waking ([[lore-chayacondm|the Condemnation of the Chaya-vreta]]), and the vow went on outside the monasteries, one student at a time.

: the [Martial Council]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} acclaimed a cousin over the heir about 450.

[[affiliation-chendrapur|Chendrapur]]
: about 450 the crown refused a House's succession for the first time, and that House lost its seat within a generation.

## Its End

The age ends in 524 AF, 190 CP, the year before a kingdom up the river took [[affiliation-rajeprjnpd|Rajepur Janpoda]]'s head-gate and the janpoda went under Chendrapur's protection. [[lore-agepatrons|The Age of Patrons]] opens with that protection. The plateau's empty century outlasted the peace by fifteen years: the great lineages walked back onto Gomarga's circuit about 540, in the new age.
