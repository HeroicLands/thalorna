The **Hard Generation** is the crisis of Vedyari history, and the shape of the present was set in it. In about twenty years [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]] was invaded from both ends at once, lost a kingdom and a janapoda, raised an army it had no mechanism for raising, and signed the law it has lived under since.

## What Happened

In the spring of 315 a steppe orkunoq came down the Western Descent ([[lore-hndrdbnnrs|the Storm of the Hundred Banners]]). The march kingdom of **Marupala** met it where the foothills open onto the road and died there with its last king ([[lore-marupalafl|the Fall of Marupala]]); the field is [[place-lanthusthli|Lantustali]], the thinnest _shorukshetra_ in Vedyara. The host burned the capital, [[place-ludrapur|Ludrapur]], swept the march, and turned west across Dunhara.

While the kingdoms were looking west, an adventurer brought a few hundred picked men over the conch-door in the east ([[lore-conchdoor|the Conch-Door Incursion]]) and took the eastern janapadas one at a time. The one that would not submit he shut in its temple hall and burned ([[lore-silenthall|the Silenced Hall]]). The janapadas' third confederation army put him out, and at the Melra of 320 the assembly mourned the dead janapoda on the roll in the same ten days it swore [[lore-thirdcmpct|the Third Compact]].

On the empty march a clan of the salt coast, the Bhumipala, took the land, seated its court at the far end of the road and began the forts ([[lore-bhmplraise|the Raising of Bhumipala]]).

## Rulers

- **Marupala:** **Ashvakirti**, the last king, dead at Lantustali.
- **Bhumipala:** **Kavravira**, the first Maharava, confirmed by a council of the men who built the forts.
- The storm-orqun and the conch-door adventurer are unnamed in every Vedyari account, the first because nobody recorded his name and the second because the priests will not speak it.

## Its End

The age ends in 334 AF, 22 HG, when the Compact's roads and the march forts stand. The [[lore-compactpeace|Compact Peace]] follows.
