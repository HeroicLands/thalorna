**Aruti's hermitage** is a cell and a walled yard high on [[place-goldmountain|the Gold Mountain]], above the panning grounds and off the path to the observation posts. [Aruti the Wise]{.name data-type="being/npc"} lives in it. She is a holy woman of indeterminate age and a considerable reputation, and the reputation travels further than her address does.

Ascetic traditions are respected across [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]] and the devout keep those who follow them. Aruti is kept by the villages below, who leave what they bring at the gate of the yard and do not come in unless they are asked.

## Who Comes

Few know where she is, and those who do are not in the habit of saying. Some of them have come a long way, and at least one of them is a woman holding a house against an orthodoxy that has barred her supporters from the temple's well and court.

What a visitor gets is counsel on a matter of conscience, given in terms that are cryptic at the time and are generally found to have been right afterward. She does not adjudicate, she does not intercede, and she has never been heard to comment on the [Gold Constitution]{.name data-type="lore/law"}.
