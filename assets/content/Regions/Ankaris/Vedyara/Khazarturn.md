> **GM-only note.** Where Khazartúrn stands is the one thing every Khazari hold keeps from outsiders. No public note places it, and nothing in this note belongs in one.

## Overview {#overview}

**Khazartúrn**, the city of the seven towers, is the greatest work of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]], the Khazari, and the greatest grave in the world. The seven clans raised it in 5123 BF around seven spurs of rock that stood out from the cliff of a mountain valley, finished each spur as a tower, one to each clan, and held the city beneath them together ([[lore-khazturnrsd|The Raising of Khazartúrn]]). It stood close to twenty-seven centuries as the seat of the whole people's craft-records. In 2427 BF a [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]] outlaw wielding [[miscgear-secondvoice|the Second Voice]] drove tens of thousands of [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar Folk]] into it and killed all thirty thousand who lived there ([[lore-khazarturn|The Fall of Khazartúrn]]).

The Khazari laid their dead in their own chambers, cleaned the city to the condition they had kept it in, unmade the skylights and set granite flush in their place, shut the fountains at their source, and sealed the gate for all generations ([[lore-sealingcity|The Sealing of the City]]). The city is not a ruin. It is intact, in good order, dark, silent and full of its people.

:::secret {title="Where it stands"}
Khazartúrn stands in a high valley of [[place-sthrnwall|the Southern Wall]] of [[place-graznmntns|the Grazian Mountains]], behind the summit line, where the ice fields feed the valley from three sides and no road comes near. The valley lies well away from the Wall's four crossings, the Guides' Road among them, and no village of [[affiliation-osketguides|the Osket]] stands within its watershed. Its floor holds a still lake, and the cliff above the lake carries seven ribs of rock standing out from the face. The Wall is the highest ground in the world, and the glacier valleys behind its summit line are shut by ice and weather for all but a few weeks of the year, which close the city off from every road more completely than any wall the Khazari could have cut.

The two first holds outside the city, [Zaghar-gunáv]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} and [Farz-sumád]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, stand on the ridge north of the valley, two days' walk from the city and a day from each other. Both are sealed.
:::

## What the Valley Shows

From the valley floor the city is a cliff. The seven towers read as seven ribs of the rock, weathered as the rest of the face is weathered, and the skylights above the halls are granite set flush with the mountain, so that nothing marks a seam. The gate is walled and dressed to the face around it. The lake below lies still enough to give the ribs back twice, as the Senali songs say, and nothing in the valley tells a traveler that anyone ever lived there.

## Who Knows

Every Khazari hold knows where the city stands and keeps it from everyone outside the rock. Each keeps the account of the fall in its working archive and reads it in the deep at intervals, and none speaks of the city to an outsider: pressed, a Khazár declines to answer, and none has ever said it did not happen. The holds' doctrine is that the city is not to be disturbed, ever, and that anyone who finds it, speaks credibly of having found it, or gathers the means to open it is a problem the Deep Folk solve quietly and completely.

The Senali saw the city while it lived, and their songs carry pieces of it at three and four removes into human hands. The Osket of the western and central crossings know nothing of it. No register of [[place-pssshrines|the Pass-Shrines]] and no Vedyari temple record names it.

## The Present {#present}

A pilgrim who went higher than the glacier-springs of the Wall in the open weeks of the last season came down nine days late and half frozen, and the ushtaka of the spring-temple who took him in wrote his account into the temple's register: a still lake under a cliff with seven ribs of rock standing out of it, and no track to it or from it. The ushtaka has copied the entry for the register of the Pass-Shrines, as an unusual ascent is copied. No Khazari hold has heard of it.

## History {#history}

The seven clans reached Thalorna about 5300 BF ([[lore-khazararrv|The Coming of the Khazari]]) and raised Khazartúrn in 5123 BF. In its early centuries one clan held the leadership of the city, and the others contested farmland, the mines and the share of the gate. The council sent houses of two or three clans out to cut the first holds on the ridge north of the valley, and every hold since has been governed by its council so that no single house can lead it ([[lore-clansquarrel|The Quarrel of the Clans]]). Holds spread out from the city across the high places of the world through the centuries it stood.

The Fall in 2427 BF killed everyone in the city. The relic was never recovered, and the Khazari have killed Grukar on sight and have not spoken to the Senali since ([[lore-fallkhazturn|The Fall of Khazartúrn]]). They sealed the city, and nobody has opened it.
