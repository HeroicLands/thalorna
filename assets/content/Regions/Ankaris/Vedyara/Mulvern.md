## Overview {#overview}

**Mulvern** is a cliff at the head of a high valley in the northern country where [[place-graznmntns|the Grazian Mountains]] begin their arc. Below it the valley floor is stepped into terraces, their walls standing and their beds grown over with turf, and below the terraces lies a field of low cairns. A spring comes out of the foot of the cliff into a channel cut in the rock and led along the slope. High in the face a narrow cleft runs up from a ledge, and from the valley floor it looks like any crack in the rock. The [[affiliation-hvarnguides|Hvarn]] who summer their yaks in the valley call the place Mulvern, "the empty house", from [[skill-hvarnlng|Hvarn]] _mulv_, "empty", and _vern_, "a house", and they call the cleft its gate.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody holds it. The Hvarn hearths that drive their herds to the northern high country in summer graze the old terraces, and the Dzelbern hearth and the Vrestun hearth have both done it for as long as their tellers count. No state has claimed the valley, and no caravan road comes within days of it.

## Layers {#history}

The herders say a people farmed the terraces before the Hvarn came up the range, and that they starved in a winter that never ended, which is why the cairns are there. Nobody has counted the cairns. Some say the people of the terraces kept the gate and were kept by whoever lived behind it; some say the Deep Folk were behind it, as lowland rumor puts Khazari halls above the tree line in the range; and some say the house was always empty. The channel is the oldest work in the valley, and the herders call it the house's water.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The channel is the quarrel. Last summer was dry, and [Rhanvi]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the Dzelbern hearth cleared the fallen stones out of the old channel where it leaves the terraces and cut a ditch to carry the spring across the slope onto her hearth's grazing. The water that ran down through the terraces to the lower meadow now runs east. [Zhavel]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the Vrestun hearth, whose herds water at the lower meadow, says the spring is the house's and not Rhanvi's to lead anywhere. Rhanvi says the house is empty, and she wants it proved: she means to pay whoever goes in at the gate and comes out able to say whose house it is, so that nobody can claim its water. The two hearths have put the matter to the winter council at [[place-nurvhrn|Nurvharn]], and the council has not answered.

The other talk is of a cave. Rhanvi's son, looking for a strayed yak on the east face of the valley above the terraces, found a dry cave facing east with marks cut in a row across a seam of its wall. He made a rubbing of them in soot on a strip of hide, and the rubbing has been passed from fire to fire all winter without anyone reading it.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The northern high country closes early. Snow comes onto the passes out of the valley weeks before it comes to the Reach, and a party caught above the terraces waits out the winter or dies there. The cleft in the face opens on a ledge that only a climber reaches, and the rock below it is rotten with frost. The Vrestun herders do not take kindly to strangers who come up the valley in a summer when the water is disputed.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

Terrace walls of fitted stone climbing both sides of the valley head, their beds under turf; a stone channel, cut rather than built, leading the spring along the slope and down through the terraces in short falls; Rhanvi's new ditch beside it. Below the terraces, the cairns, low and grassed, in rows. In the cliff, the cleft: a climb of some forty feet to a ledge, and at the ledge a passage the width of a door going straight into the rock, with four square sockets cut in each side of it, empty. On the east face above the terraces, the cave of the marks.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The water.** Zhavel wants someone with no hearth to walk the channel and say where the spring ran before Rhanvi cut her ditch, before the winter council sits on it.
- **The rubbing.** Rhanvi's son wants the marks in the cave read, and he takes anyone who can read old letters up the east face to see them.
- **The gate.** No herder goes past the empty sockets at the top of the climb, and Rhanvi pays well for anyone who goes in and comes out to tell her whose house it is.

# The Truth of Mulvern {#truth .secret}

**The hold.** The cliff is the face of **Basur-khunák**, "the leading of the spring", an abandoned [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] hold of the early centuries. The cleft is its gate, the four sockets on each side held the bars that went across it at evening, and the channel is the hold's work, leading the spring out of the rock and down through the fields of the tribe that farmed, herded and mined for the hold as its subjects. The hold taught that tribe the gate-row of [[skill-drthrkscrpt|Pirzath]] for tally, mark, measure and oath, and the cairns are the tribe's dead. The hold stands in the record of the holds, and [[affiliation-talgnugarhld|Talg-nugár Hold]] knows where it lies.

**The record.** The holds copy one entry from the record of Basur-khunák, the sentence of [Gisvin of Tanv-guráz]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, keeper of its gate, who in a hard winter carried the hold's stores out through the gate to the starving tribe and was put out of the rock ([[lore-balvfunad|The Going of the Exile]]). The tie the hold kept with its tribe failed. The tribe starved at the hold's gate, and a hold with no tribe at its gate cannot eat, so its houses went out of it to other holds and carried its record with them, which is why the holds can copy it.

**The cave of the marks.** The dry cave on the east face is the one the record names, where the bone of Gisvin's name, _g-s-v_, is cut in the gate-row in a straight line across a seam, split at the third letter where the line crosses it. The holds keep two endings to the song and have never agreed between them. In one, [Varan of Talg-hunáth]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, apprenticed to the finders, cut the third letter again on the seam's side and scored the binding _i_ beside it, so that the name is mended. In the other, she left it as she found it. The GM chooses which of the two the party finds on the wall, and Rhanvi's son's rubbing shows the same.

**Inside.** Behind the gate the hold is empty and in order: the halls swept, the hearths cold and laid, the caches open and bare. Its dead lie in the deep with their names cut over them. The spring rises inside the rock and comes out to the channel by a cut the hold made for it, and nothing Rhanvi has done reaches that cut.

> [!TIP]
> The rubbing is the way in. A party that brings it to anyone who reads Pirzath learns that the marks are a name, and a name cut by a hold, and the valley stops being a quarrel about water.
