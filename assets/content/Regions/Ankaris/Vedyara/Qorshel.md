## Overview {#overview}

**Qorshel** is a hanging valley high on the Vedyari face of [[place-graznmntns|the Grazian Mountains]], west of [[place-forestnoroad|the Forest of No Road]] and well above the last trees, where the eastern end of [[place-sthrnwall|the Southern Wall]] meets [[place-estrnreach|the Eastern Reach]]. A herders' path climbs to it in two days from the lower road of [[place-tamradvar|Tamradvar]]. Its name is [[skill-osketlng|Osket]], "the green", from _qorsh_, "green", and it is the right name: the valley's turf is thick and bright when every other valley at that height is gravel and thin grass, and its flowers open at the thaw and are still open at the first snow.

In the middle of the valley floor stands a ring of junipers, each far taller than a juniper grows at that height, their trunks twisted together so that the ring is one wall of living wood with gaps in it like doorways. A spring rises inside the ring and runs out under it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The valley is the high pasture of an Osket lineage, the hearth of Tsolang, which holds it with its herds and its road as every [[affiliation-osketguides|Osket]] lineage holds its own. No lowland power claims it. The smithing clans of [[place-slvrgorges|the Silver Gorges]] hold that a pasture is not a road, and that what grows above the tree line belongs to whoever cuts it.

## Layers {#history}

The Tsolang say the ring stood in the valley before their lineage first drove yaks up to it, and that their lineage has never cut it or let a yak inside it. Their guide-mothers tell the young that the ring belongs to whoever made the valley green and is to be left as it was found, and give no other reason. No Osket telling says who made it.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The charcoal-burners are the trouble. The smithing clans of the Gorges burn charcoal for their forges, and the wood within reach of the workings is long gone. Last autumn a party of their burners came up the herders' path after the Tsolang had driven the yaks down, found the junipers, and cut two from the ring's outer side for a trial burn. The charcoal was the best the clans' smiths have seen, and the clans mean to send the burners back as soon as the snow lets them.

[Ghulmash]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Tsolang guide-mother, found the stumps at the spring drive. She has sent word down to the clans' gate that the valley is Tsolang pasture and that a burner found in it is turned back off the mountain with his axe left behind. The clans' gate has answered that the Tsolang hold a road, not a forest. Both sides mean to be in the valley at the thaw.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The cold kills first: the valley lies above the snow line for most of the year, and the path up to it crosses two snowfields that slide in a warm spell. [[being-clffstlk|Cliff Stalkers]] work the ledges of the valley walls, [[being-crglrkr|Crag Lurkers]] hide in the cracks of the scree below the ring, and the [[being-stoneram|Stone Rams]] that graze the rim defend it against anything that climbs with a weapon. In the coming season the Tsolang herders and the clans' burners are the likeliest danger to each other.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A level valley floor of bright, close turf, starred with small blue and white flowers, under bare gray walls of rock and ice. The ring of junipers stands in the middle of it, about forty paces across, its trunks grown into one another to the height of two men and its gaps arched over at the top. Two trunks on the outer side are cut through near the ground, and green shoots stand up from both stumps. Inside the ring the floor is laid with flat stones, mossed over, and the junipers on the inner side are bent into a circle of low alcoves, each roofed with living branches and floored with stone. The spring rises in a basin of worked stone at the center, and the rim of the basin is carved with a row of marks that no Osket reads.

# The Truth of Qorshel {#truth .secret}

**The site.** The valley is **Saikisto**, "the place of flowers", a summer site of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]], abandoned. Senali came up to it in the warm months and went down before the snow. The junipers are their work: each was led and bent as it grew until the ring made a wall against the wind and the alcoves made rooms, and the flowers are a mountain flower the Senali sowed for its long blooming. The marks around the basin are [[skill-clthndscrpt|Veldikko]], naming the place. Nobody on either face of the range knows when the Senali last came up, and the Senali do not say.

**The junipers.** The ring is alive and nothing in it harms anyone. The cut trunks grow back from the stump within a few summers, faster than any juniper grows. The charcoal is good because the wood is old and dense, and nothing else about it is strange.

**The quarrel.** Ghulmash knows what the Tsolang guide-mothers have always told the young, and no more: the ring is to be left as it was found. The clans need the charcoal and have no other wood above the tree line within a day of the workings. Neither side is wrong about what it needs, and the valley cannot serve both.

> [!TIP]
> A party that reads the marks or tells the Tsolang what the alcoves are gives Ghulmash an argument the clans' gate cannot answer with a weight or a toll. A party that helps the burners finds the charcoal sells, and that the Tsolang road is closed to it afterward.
