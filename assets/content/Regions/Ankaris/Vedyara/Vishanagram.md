**Vishanagram** (720) is the horn and sinew village below the fort, in the [[place-danurkotjnpd|Danurkot Janpoda]], and the soaking-pits make it a village to approach with the wind in your favor. It supplies the bowyers their facings and backings, the horn and sinew that give a Danurkot bow its strength.
