**Nilavana** (510) is the indigo village of the lower valley, in the [[place-dhanurkotajnpd|Danurkota Janapoda]], and its dyers can be told by their hands, which are blue to the wrist. It dyes for the weavers of [[place-karpasagrama|Karpasagrama]] and sends them the color the valley wears.
