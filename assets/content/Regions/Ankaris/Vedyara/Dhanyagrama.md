**Danyagrama** is a millet village on the dry western terraces of the [[place-suvarnagirijnpd|Suvarnageri Janapoda]], and no village puts more into the janapoda's famine store. Millet asks little of a terrace, and these terraces have little to give, so the village is dry ground put to the one use it suits. A lean year elsewhere in the janapoda is eaten out of what these slopes grew.
