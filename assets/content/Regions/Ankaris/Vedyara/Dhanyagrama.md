**Dhānyagrāma** is a millet village on the dry western terraces of the [[place-suvarnagirijnpd|Suvarnagiri Janapada]], and no village puts more into the janapada's famine store. Millet asks little of a terrace, and these terraces have little to give, so the village is dry ground put to the one use it suits. A lean year elsewhere in the janapada is eaten out of what these slopes grew.
