**Madupada** (380) is an orchard and bee-keeping village of the northern hills, in the [[place-dhanurkotajnpd|Danurkota Janapoda]], and it is the smallest of the villages that feed the bow trade. Its wax goes to the fletchers of the janapoda for their bindings, so every arrow shot by an academy graduate carries a little of Madupada's hives.
