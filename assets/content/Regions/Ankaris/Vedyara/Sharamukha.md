**Sharamukha**, the mouth of arrows, is where the gold road comes down out of the hills below [[place-suvargerijnpd|Suvargeri]] through a gorge barely wide enough for two carts, and opens onto the valley floor. About 415 AF the mountain kingdom of [Daunapur]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} came up this road toward the gold, and [[affiliation-bharyastan|Bharyastan]]'s horse and the [[affiliation-dnrktjnpd|Danurkot Janpoda]]'s recalled graduates stopped it at the mouth ([[lore-hghlndwar|the Highland War]]).

## What Is There

A cairn of broken arrows stands at the side of the road where the gorge opens, waist-high and grown over with lichen at the bottom. Beside it is a small shrine of [[affiliation-mahajaya|Mahajaya]], a roofed stone niche with a lamp-ledge, kept by the nearest village. Every autumn the Danurkot graduates coming home for the [Festival of the Returning]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} who pass this way break one arrow over their knee and leave it on the cairn. Whether the gorge lies on their road home depends on where they served, and those who served in the river country come round by it on purpose.

## Not a Blood-field

The dead were few by the standard of the river wars, and both sides carried theirs home and burned them. Sharamukha is a battlefield and not a _shorukshetra_ (blood-field). The village keeps the lamp lit, and the tribute escort and Bharyastani horse on exercise water at the stream below the shrine by day or night without a second thought.

## Who Comes

- **The tribute escort** from Bharyastan, each spring on its way to the gold.
- **Returning graduates**, each autumn.
- **Bharyastani horse** on exercise, who ride the gorge to keep the road known.

## Hooks

- **The struck roll.** The academies struck from their rolls the names of the graduates who stayed in Daunapur's service. One of those names belongs to a family now prominent in a patron kingdom, and the family wants it restored before a marriage. The academy masters want evidence of what that graduate did at Sharamukha, and the only witness left is the cairn.
- **The recall.** Daunapur's present dynasty has quietly hired graduates of [[place-hiddenstring|the Hidden String]], and the [[affiliation-suvrgrjnpd|Suvargeri Janpoda]]'s sabhra has noticed. The masters must decide whether the Highland War's recall is a precedent or an exception before anyone asks them in public.
