**Shaligroma** (910) is the largest of the rice villages of the [[place-dhanurkotajnpd|Danurkota Janapoda]]. It lies on the flood-plain that the [[place-sarvadarivr|Sarveda]] silts and does not cut, so each flood leaves the fields fresh soil and takes none away. The village lives by the flood, and its fields are the best-watered in the janapoda.
