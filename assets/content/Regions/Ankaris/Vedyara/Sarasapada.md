**Sarasapāda** is a village at the foot of the great tank, in the [[place-suvarnagirijnpd|Suvarnagiri Janapada]]. The tank's sluices water six villages below it, and Sarasapāda sits at the head of that water. The share each village draws is for the sabhā's reckoner to compute, and the level of the tank is the first thing anyone in Sarasapāda reads in a dry year.
