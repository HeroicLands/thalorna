## Overview {#overview}

**The Hermits' Hollow** is the hostel-keepers' name for a deep, shaded valley on the western side of [[place-forestnoroad|the Forest of No Road]], where a stream comes down out of the trees below the Grazian foothills. It is the place in the forest that renunciants go into. A renunciant who means to disappear for years takes the pilgrim road up [[place-bharavarivr|the Bharava]] to [[place-bhrvdvstna|the free hostel]] at the foot of the stream, turns off the road, and walks up into the shade, and the hostel-keepers do not ask him where he is bound.

Most of them do not come back. The ones who do come back after five years, or ten, or twenty, thin and quiet, and they are received at the hostels with reverence and not questioned, as the observance requires.

## What the Returned Say

What reaches the hostels comes from the returned, at second and third hand, and they say little. They speak of a hollow under great trees where the light is green at noon, of a flat stone at the head of the stream where a renunciant sits, and of fruit found on the stone in the morning that nobody saw set there. Several say that they set out one morning to walk deeper into the trees and were back at the hostel by evening, with no memory of the way down.

The hostel-keepers hold that a renunciant fed in the forest is fed by the devout, as he is fed on the road, and they do not inquire further. The gatherers who work the margin keep away from the stream and say the hollow belongs to the people who sit in it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody holds the hollow. The ground up the stream is endowed to no temple and claimed by no janpoda, and the hostel at the stream's foot holds only its yard, its well and its endowment.

## Layers {#history}

Renunciants have gone up the stream and come down it again for as long as the hostels have fed them, and the tellings of the returned have not changed: the green shade, the flat stone, the fruit, and the walk down that nobody remembers. No renunciant has said who feeds the sitters at the stone, and the hostel-keepers have never asked.

## What Is There Now {#present}

A renunciant who went up the stream twelve years ago came down this spring and has sat in the yard of the hostel at the stream's foot ever since. He says only that he was fed, that he was told to go home before the rains, and that he went. Word of him has gone down the road, and forty pilgrims and would-be renunciants have come up to the hostel to ask him to lead them into the hollow. He refuses. They stay, and wait for him to change his mind.

The hostel feeds them, because the endowment of [[affiliation-suvrgrjnpd|Suvargeri]] feeds whoever presents himself as an ascetic, and its doorkeeper has turned away only three of them. The hostel's stores are meant for a few travelers at a time, and they are half gone before the pilgrim season is properly open. The doorkeeper has written down the river to the temple that keeps the hostel, asking whether he may send the crowd on, and has had no answer.

# The Truth of the Hermits' Hollow {#truth .secret}

**The enclave.** The head of the hollow is **Helsaisto**, "the place of shade", an enclave of about ninety [[lore-flksenali|Senali]], the [[affiliation-nclvhlsst|Enclave of Helsaisto]]. Its hearths stand under the great trees above the flat stone, a short walk from where a renunciant sits. No renunciant has seen them, and until this spring no hearth had spoken to one.

**The renunciants.** The enclave lets a renunciant who comes up the stream sit at the stone for as long as he sits there and asks for nothing. The fruit on the stone is left by the hearths, and the paths turn him back if he walks on toward them. A renunciant who has sat long enough to be no trouble to anyone is left alone; one who goes looking is led round in the shade until he finds himself back at the hostel. The hearths count this a keeping of guest-right, and they keep it without ever speaking to the man.

**The one who spoke.** The renunciant now at the hostel was told to go home by [[being-lernuvovltnt|Lernuvo Vaultinto]], the youngest of the enclave's hunters, who found him at the stone with fever coming on and the rains a month away, and spoke to him rather than watch him die there. No hearth of Helsaisto has spoken to a renunciant before, and the hearths have not agreed on what to say to Lernuvo.

**The crowd.** [[being-rissettilssnt|Rìssetti Loussinto]], the Helsainno who keeps the enclave, holds that guest-right covers one who sits and asks nothing, and that forty who come up the stream asking to be led are not guests. [[being-ellinelatnnvn|Ellinela Tunnevinto]], a lord of the enclave, wants the hollow closed to everyone, the solitary renunciants included, until the crowd at the hostel has gone home.

> [!TIP]
> The renunciant at the hostel is the key to the hollow, and he knows it. What he refuses to do is the clearest sign of what he found there.
