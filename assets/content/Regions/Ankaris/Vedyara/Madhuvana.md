**Madhuvana** is an orchard village on the sheltered southern slope of the [[place-suvarnagirijnpd|Suvarnagiri Janapada]]. It grows the temperate fruit that the lowland plain will not take, and the fruit goes down the valley as part of the mountain produce the janapada sells. In the fruiting season the whole slope is orchard, and a traveler coming up from the plain is eating fruit he could not have bought at home.
