The **Sarveda Mouth Janapoda** is the janapoda in which [[place-naughatta|Naughatta]] sits. The landing is held by the temple of [[affiliation-mahajaya|Mahajaya]] at the town and by the sabhra of the janapoda, and the anchorage dues are the sabhra's largest single revenue.

The janapoda burns its dead at [[place-dahanaghatta|Dahanaghatta]], the stair on the north bank a mile above the town.
