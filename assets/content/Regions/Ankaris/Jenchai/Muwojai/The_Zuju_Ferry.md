## Overview {#overview}

The ferry at [Zuju]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} (Blue River) crosses the river that drains the valley of [[place-zijai|Zijai]], on the road to [[place-lengwou|Lengwou]]. The Emperor's district opens onto no other ground than that of [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|Muwojai Domain]], and no road to the capital avoids the river, so every envoy, petitioner and bale of the court's rice crosses here. The village of Zuju, 300 people on the bank, is the demesne of the lord of Muwojai, and the ferry and its toll are its livelihood.

A party comes to cross: an envoy bound for the capital, a carter with rice for the court, a petitioner with a letter for the throne, or a rider whose house would rather his name stayed out of the roll.

## Inside {#inside}

There is a landing on each bank and a toll house at Zuju. The ferrymen work the crossing, and the clerks of the toll-keepers sit at the landing and enter each party in the roll as it embarks. The toll goes to the lord's granary at [[place-kowu|Kowu]]. The garrisons that hold the passes of the district are posted on the road on either side.

## Who Is There {#who-is-there}

The toll-keepers are [Muwojai Chaiwo]{.name data-type="being/npc"}'s servants, and they enter the name of every party that goes over, with its size and its errand when it states one. They record who crosses and what it carries, and they do not break seals.

## The Present {#present}

Chaiwo has no grown heir, and each bloc is courting the candidates to succeed him. The Strait bloc wants the toll roll read, the loyalists want the gate kept shut, and the independent houses want to cross unwatched.

A sealed letter from the throne bound for [[place-hawu|Hawu]] must cross here too, and [Shuiwojai Henzo]{.name data-type="being/npc"} does not trust the hands that carry it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The crossing.** A party bound for Lengwou before the New Year has to give its name at the landing, and one of its number is a man whom Muwojai has reason to remember.
- **The roll.** A house's agent wants to know who crossed in the last month, and a clerk's price is a letter delivered to Kowu.
- **The carrier.** A messenger with a sealed letter has to cross under his own name, and a party is hired to ride with him past the garrisons on the far side.
