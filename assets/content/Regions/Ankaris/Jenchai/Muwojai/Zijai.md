Zijai (Star Peak) stands in the interior of the great island, beside [[place-zojai|Zojai]], the Emperor's mountain, and is held by [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|House Muwojai]]. The two peaks rise from the same cedar valleys, and the road to the Emperor passes along the foot of Zijai.

The slopes are cedar forest below and bare rock above. The terraces at the mountain's foot bear rice at [Zainu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, and the timber that is felled above them supplies the joinery of [Tsaimu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} and the halls of the court. The river that drains the valley is crossed at the ferry of [Zuju]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, on the road to [[place-lengwou|Lengwou]]. The castle town of [[place-kowu|Kowu]] stands on the lower slope.

The summit holds a shrine and the **observatory** of the omen-readers. The Tanchei who serve as Omen-Reader to the court keep the heavens from it, and the timing of imperial acts is fixed from what they record there. The air above the interior is clearer than the coast's, and the instruments stand on the open rock. At the [Festival of the Mountain]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} (_Chai Jwi_) the lord's roll is closed on the same summit, beside the instruments of the court.

42,000 people live on Zijai: the whole of the [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|Muwojai Domain]], which holds the castle town of [[place-kowu|Kowu]] (3,200 people) and the villages of its roll.

## Current Situation {#present}

In 720 AF the road along the foot of Zijai is the one every house watches. [[being-chaiwomuwoj|Muwojai Chaiwo]], who holds the mountain, is sixty-one and has no grown heir, and each bloc is courting the men who would succeed him, since whoever does will hold the gate to the Emperor on [[place-zojai|Zojai]].

The Emperor's sealed letter to Shuiwojai crosses the ferry at [Zuju]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} on its way to the Strait. The omen-readers' observatory on the summit fixes the timing of imperial acts from what it records, so the household of the Omen-Reader is watched while [[being-weilengjnch|Weileng]] leaves the office of Teng Ja unconferred.
