**Zījai** (Star Peak) stands in the interior of the great island, beside [[place-zojai|Zojai]], the Emperor's mountain, and is held by House Mūwōjai. The two peaks rise from the same cedar valleys, and the road to the Emperor passes along the foot of Zījai.

The slopes are cedar forest below and bare rock above. The terraces at the mountain's foot bear rice at Zainū, and the timber that is felled above them supplies the joinery of Tsaimū and the halls of the court. The river that drains the valley is crossed at the ferry of Zūjū, on the road to Léngwou. The castle town of [[place-kowu|Kowū]] stands on the lower slope.

The summit holds a shrine and the **observatory** of the omen-readers. The Tānchéi who serve as Omen-Reader to the court keep the heavens from it, and the timing of imperial acts is fixed from what they record there. The air above the interior is clearer than the coast's, and the instruments stand on the open rock. At the Festival of the Mountain (_Chài Juì_) the lord's roll is closed on the same summit, beside the instruments of the court.
