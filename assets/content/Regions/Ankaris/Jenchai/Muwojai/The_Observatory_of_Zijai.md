## Overview {#overview}

The observatory stands on the summit of [[place-zijai|Zijai]], beside the shrine of the mountain, on bare rock above the cedar forest. The air over the interior is clearer than the coast's, and the instruments stand in the open. It is the working post of the court's Omen-Reader, the [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] who reckons the heavens for the Emperor, and the timing of imperial acts is fixed from what is recorded here.

A party comes for a reading: to learn which days the heavens leave open for an act of the court, to carry a lord's request for a day to be fixed, or to look at the record. The mountain belongs to the [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|Muwojai Domain]], and the way up begins at [[place-kowu|Kowu]] on the lower slope.

## Inside {#inside}

The instruments are set on the open rock of the summit, and the record of the readings is kept beside them with the mountain record that the domain's Tanchei hold in the shrine. At the [Festival of the Mountain]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} (_Chai Jwi_), when the harvest is in and the assessment paid, the lord's roll is closed on the same summit, beside the instruments of the court.

## Who Is There {#who-is-there}

The Omen-Reader's assistants and the Tanchei who tend the instruments are on the summit day to day. The domain's Tanchei hold the mountain record in the shrine beside it, so the instruments of the Emperor's court stand on a mountain that belongs to one house.

## The Present {#present}

An imperial act waits on a reading. The Emperor confers the office of Teng Ja, which stands vacant, and the day on which he acts is fixed from the heavens as the Omen-Reader records them. A house that wants the office conferred has to learn which days the readings leave open. Every road to Zijai runs over ground that [Muwojai Chaiwo]{.name data-type="being/npc"} keeps, so each retainer who goes up with such a question is known at Kowu before he reaches the instruments.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The reading.** A house sends a retainer up Zijai to ask which days the heavens leave open, and learns that the record has been consulted by another house first.
- **The closing of the roll.** At the Festival of the Mountain the lord's roll and the court's readings are on the same summit, and a retainer who is not of Muwojai wants a copy of one or the other.
