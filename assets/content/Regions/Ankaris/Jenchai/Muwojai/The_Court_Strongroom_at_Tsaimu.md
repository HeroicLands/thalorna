## Overview {#overview}

The strongroom of the Dawn Court stands at [Tsaimu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} (Craft Wood), a village of 220 people on [[place-zijai|Zijai]] whose lacquerers and joiners work the timber felled above it. The village lies on the ground of [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|Muwojai Domain]], and the [Weiju]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} hold it as their fief and keep the strongroom in their charge. The Emperor's treasury is therefore in the hands of the house that keeps every road to him.

A party comes here to pay: a Dauzhwo's steward forwarding the court's share of a court estate, or a merchant carrying a loan for the court's budget. Others come to read the roll, or to take something out of the room that was not paid into it.

## Inside {#inside}

The strongroom is a store of cedar and stone among the joiners' workshops, small beside the court it serves. It holds the silver that the houses forward to the Emperor, and beside it the Weiju keep their roll, in which every bale and every measure of silver paid to the court by the other houses is entered under the house that paid it.

## Who Is There {#who-is-there}

The Weiju hold the office of Keeper of the Court Treasury and answer for what the roll says is in the room. The lord of Muwojai, [Muwojai Chaiwo]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, holds the road to the village as he holds every road to the Emperor.

## The Present {#present}

The court spends more than it receives. Each Dauzhwo collects the court's share of his court estate through his own granary and forwards what he chooses, so what reaches Tsaimu is what the houses choose to send. The silver of [[affiliation-shuiwojaidmn|Shuiwojai]] has covered the shortfall for three years, and the roll in which every house's payment is entered is the court's own record of its debt to [Shuiwojai Henzo]{.name data-type="being/npc"}. The roll is kept by the retainers of a loyalist house, and the Strait bloc, which wants Henzo named Teng Ja, has no hand in it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The steward.** A house's steward arrives with the court's share and asks that the roll record the payment in the form his lord prefers, since the roll is the record of what the court owes to Shuiwojai Henzo.
- **The reading of the roll.** A lord who is courting the Emperor's favor wants to know what another house has paid in, and a clerk of the Weiju is offered more than his stipend to let him read it.
- **The shortfall.** The court spends more than it receives, and a party with a claim on the court's silver wants to learn from the roll whether the room can pay it.
