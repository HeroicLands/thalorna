## Overview {#overview}

The bell of the Dawn Rite is a bronze bell that belongs to the court. It sounds at first light on the first day of the year on the summit of [[place-zojai|Zojai]], when the Emperor performs the [Dawn Rite]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} (_Leng Rwi_) at [[place-zojaishrn|The Summit Shrine of Zojai]], and its frame beside the shrine stands empty while the bell is away. It is the court's, and the court has pawned it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

A merchant house of [[place-yuwu|Yuwu]] holds the bell against the court's debt, in the strong-room that the house keeps in the town. Yuwu is the castle town of the [[affiliation-wojaidmn|Wojai Domain]], whose lord, [[being-wojaidoja|Wojai Doja]], is independent of both blocs. The house trades under his judges, and the bell does not leave the town unless he allows it.

## Its Story {#its-story}

The court pawned the bell to the merchant house for a sum it has not repaid. Its receipts fall short of its spending, and the silver of Shuiwojai has covered the gap for three years. Whoever redeems the bell before the New Year pays the sum and carries the bell home to Zojai.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

The bell opens the Dawn Rite with its sound, and it has no other use. Its value to the person who redeems it is the Emperor's gratitude, which costs the Emperor nothing: a court dignity, or a place nearer the front among the envoys on the summit at the next rite. The redeemer buys favor, and the Emperor gives it without spending a bale.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **Wojai Doja** has the means to redeem the bell himself, and he is deciding which of the houses to let do it. A house that is let pays for the bell, and the house whose silver redeems it owes its place to him.
- **The Strait bloc**, led by [[being-henzoshuiwo|Shuiwojai Henzo]], who has covered the court's budget for three years, has the silver to buy the Emperor's gratitude and wants Henzo named Teng Ja.
- **The loyalists**, [[affiliation-hojujaidmn|Hojujai Domain]] and [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|Muwojai Domain]], want the office for the Hojujai heir or for nobody, and would rather the Strait bloc did not earn the Emperor's gratitude.
- **The Emperor**, [[being-weilengjnch|Weileng]], is the one the bell belongs to, and he cannot redeem it himself.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- A house sends a party to Yuwu with silver to redeem the bell, and the house that holds it takes no payment from a house that Doja has not named.
- A party is hired to carry the redeemed bell from Yuwu to Zojai before the New Year, by the roads of Muwojai and over the ferry at Zuju.
