**Yuwu** (Moon Hall) is the castle town and seat of Wojai Doja, a town of 9,000 people on the northern shore of the bay of Yujai. It is the largest of the eight houses' seats. The lord's hall stands above the quays on a spur of the mountain, with the whole anchorage below it.

The quays run for the length of the shore. They hold the domain's forty hulls and the foreign ships that come to trade, and behind them stand the **customs hall**, where the Woju enter every berth and toll, and the **granary**, where the lord's four parts in ten are delivered. The retainers' houses cluster near the hall, and the Tsaihuo soldiers are raised from the dock streets.

The town lives on trade. Warehouses hold lacquer, silk, salt, blades and ceramics bound for the Vedyaran coast, and the counting-houses of the Shuonong lineages lend against cargoes and finance the voyages. Wealth sits with the merchants of Yuwu and rank with the retainers, and the two meet only in the lord's accounts. The merchant house that holds the pawned bronze bell of the Dawn Rite keeps its strong-room in the town.

Most of the cases Doja's judges hear in the town are harbor disputes.
