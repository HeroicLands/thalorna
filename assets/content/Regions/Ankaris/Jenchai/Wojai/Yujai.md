**Yūjai** (Moon Peak) stands on the south coast of the great island, where the climate is mild, and it is held by House Wōjai. The mountain curves round a deep bay that is the best harbor in the realm.

The south face is the domain's market. The anchorage at Showō fills the bay with the hulls of the trade, and the pack road from Shodū climbs inland to the rest of the island. Rice, mulberry and silk grow on the terraced shoulders at Ronū, and the castle town of [[place-yuwu|Yūwū]] stands on the bay's northern shore.

The summit is kept as a shrine of the Minister of the Moon, and the domain's Tānchéi hold the mountain record there. Yūjai's Festival of the Mountain (_Chài Juì_) waits until the tolls of the year are counted as well as the harvest, and only then is the lord's roll closed.
