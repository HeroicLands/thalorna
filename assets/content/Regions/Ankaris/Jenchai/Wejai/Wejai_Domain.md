**House Wējai** (Wind Peak) holds **Wējai**, the mountain of the open-ocean coast on the east of the great island, by patent of the Emperor. Its lord is the Daozhuo **Wējai Rōwo** (Thunder Warrior), forty years old, who sits in the castle town of **Zūwū** (Blue Hall), population 2,200. The domain holds 38,000 people, is assessed at 24,000 bales a year, and musters 600 men and 15 ships. The house is independent of both blocs.

## The Land

The mountain faces the open ocean, and the typhoons of the summer break on it first. Dunes and reef line the shore, and little ground is fit for terraces; the people live by the sea. Whalers and line-fishers work the coast, the reefs yield shellfish and kelp, and the pilgrim road to the cape shrine brings custom to the villages along it. The roll is paid in fish and whale-oil more than in rice; of the named villages, only Hanū pays in rice.

The three villages below are named on the roll; smaller landings and hamlets under their headmen make up the rest of the 24,000 bales.

| Village | Meaning       | Kind         | Holder                                    | Livelihood                | Assessed |
| ------- | ------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | -------: |
| Rēwō    | Dawn Sea      | Demesne      | The lord's granary at Zūwū                | Fishing and whaling       |      800 |
| Wēwō    | Wind Sea      | Shrine land  | The cape shrine of the Minister of Storms | Reef fishing and pilgrims |      500 |
| Hanū    | Dark Terraces | Court estate | The Emperor, through the lord's granary   | Rice behind the dunes     |    1,000 |

The summit shrine of Wējai keeps the storm aspect of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Celestial Order]], and its Tānchéi are provisioned from the lord's villages. The cape shrine of the Minister of Storms at Wēwō holds its village as shrine land: the four parts of Wēwō's assessment go to the shrine, not to the lord.

## Retainers

The Wòhou of Wējai serve the house for stipends in bales, since no retainer family of the domain holds a fief. The Shuìzhuo of the domain hold one to three villages each and owe Rōwo men in war. The coast is law to itself in one matter: whatever the sea throws on the reefs belongs to the lord by wreck-right, and his officers walk the strand after every storm.

## Fleet and Muster

The muster is 600 men and 15 ships. The ships are whalers and coastal guard boats, built to keep the reefs and to stand off the typhoon coast.

## Relations

Wējai belongs to neither bloc. Its lord is one of the four independent houses, who together muster 3,970 men and 55 ships and who each have their own price.

## The Wreck on the Reefs

A typhoon drove a foreign hull onto the reefs of Wēwō. By wreck-right the cargo is the lord's. A survivor claims to be an envoy to the court, and his letters are in the cargo. If Rōwo keeps the cargo, he keeps the letters, and with them knowledge that no other house has; if he sends the man to Zojai, the letters cross Mūwōjai's roads.
