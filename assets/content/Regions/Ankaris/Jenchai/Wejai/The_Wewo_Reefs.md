## Overview {#overview}

The reefs begin at the point where the cape shrine of the Minister of Storms stands above [Wewo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} (Wind Sea), on the open-ocean coast of [[place-wejai|Wejai]]. Beyond the dunes of the shore they run out into water that the summer typhoons strike before any other coast of the Nine. The cape shrine is kept in the name of [[affiliation-raogui|Raugwi]], the Minister of Storms, and it holds Wewo as shrine land: the four parts of the village's assessment go to the shrine, not to the lord.

## The Land {#the-land}

The shore below the mountain is dune and reef, and the reef is the livelihood of the village. It yields shellfish and kelp, and the fishermen of Wewo work its channels from small boats. The coast road runs along the dunes to the point, and it carries pilgrims to the shrine and the custom of the villages along it.

## Who Uses It {#who-uses-it}

The fishermen of Wewo make their offerings at the cape shrine before a storm season opens. Pilgrims come to it by the coast road. After every storm the wreck-officers of [Wejai Rowo]{.name data-type="being/npc"} walk the strand and the reef, since by wreck-right whatever the sea throws on the reefs belongs to the lord, and they report to the hall at [[place-zuwu|Zuwu]], where what they bring in is entered in the granary roll. The shrine's keepers enter every typhoon, with its year and its wrecks, in the mountain record that the domain's Tanchei keep.

## Dangers {#dangers}

The reefs sink ships. The typhoon coast takes hulls that stand in too close, and a boat that works the channels in a rising wind does not come back. The wreck-right makes the strand dangerous in another way: a party that salvages from the shore without the lord's leave is taking what is his.

## What Is Told {#what-is-told}

The shrine's keepers say that the storm is the Minister's attention and that a season opened without an offering is paid for in boats. The fishermen make the offering and say nothing more of it.

## The Wreck {#present}

A typhoon has driven a foreign hull onto the reefs of Wewo. By wreck-right the cargo is the lord's, and a survivor claims to be an envoy to the court, his letters among the cargo. If Rowo keeps the cargo he keeps the letters, and with them knowledge that no other house has. If he sends the man to [[place-zojai|Zojai]], the letters travel over the roads of the [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|Muwojai Domain]], a loyalist house that keeps every road to the Emperor.

The hull lies on the reef below the cape shrine, in sight of the shore.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The cargo.** The cargo is in Rowo's keeping, and a house with a stake in what the letters say wants a party to read them before the lord decides.
- **The envoy.** The survivor wants to reach Zojai, and the lord's price for letting him go is a favor that a party can fetch.
- **The offering.** A party hired to guard a boat on the reefs is asked by the fishermen to make an offering at the cape shrine before it sails, and learns why from the keepers.
