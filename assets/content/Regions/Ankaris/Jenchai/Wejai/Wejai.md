Wejai (Wind Peak) stands on the east of the great island, where the coast faces the open ocean and the braided outer chains, and it is held by [[affiliation-wejaidmn|House Wejai]]. The typhoons of the summer reach it before any other mountain of the Nine.

The seaward slope is bare and wind-cut, and the shore below it is dune and reef. The landing at [Rewo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} fishes and hunts whales along the coast, and the terraces at [Hanu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} bear rice in the lee of the dunes. The cape shrine of the Minister of Storms at [Wewo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} stands on the point where the reefs begin; pilgrims come to it by the coast road, and the fishermen of the reef make their offerings before a storm season opens. The castle town of [[place-zuwu|Zuwu]] stands in the lee of the mountain, in the one sheltered bay.

The summit shrine keeps the storm aspect of [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|the Celestial Order]], and the domain's Tanchei hold the mountain record there. Wejai's record enters every typhoon with its year and its wrecks beside the eruptions and earthquakes, and the Tanchei read the year from it at the [Festival of the Mountain]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} (_Chai Jwi_).

38,000 people live on Wejai: the whole of the [[affiliation-wejaidmn|Wejai Domain]], which holds the castle town of [[place-zuwu|Zuwu]] (2,200 people) and the villages of its roll.

## Current Situation {#present}

In 720 AF a typhoon has driven a foreign hull onto the reefs below the cape of [Wewo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, and its cargo and its one survivor are the mountain's present business. The survivor claims to be an envoy to the court, and his letters are in the cargo that wreck-right gives to [[being-wejairowo|Wejai Rowo]].

If Rowo keeps the cargo he keeps the letters, and knowledge that no other house has; if he sends the man to [[place-zojai|Zojai]], the letters cross [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|Muwojai]]'s roads. The Tanchei of the summit enter the wreck in the mountain record beside the year's typhoons. Rowo has named his price to no bloc and wants to be asked after Zenjai and Wojai.
