Wejai (Wind Peak) stands on the east of the great island, where the coast faces the open ocean and the braided outer chains, and it is held by the [[affiliation-wejaidmn|Wejai Domain]]. The typhoons of the summer reach it before any other mountain of the Nine.

The seaward slope is bare and wind-cut, and the shore below it is dune and reef. The landing at [Rewo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} fishes and hunts whales along the coast, and the terraces at [Hanu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} bear rice in the lee of the dunes. The cape shrine of the Minister of Storms at [Wewo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} stands on the point where the reefs begin; pilgrims come to it by the coast road, and the fishermen of the reef make their offerings before a storm season opens. The castle town of [[place-zuwu|Zuwu]] stands in the lee of the mountain, in the one sheltered bay.

The summit shrine keeps the storm aspect of [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Téngwou Rong Lei]], and the domain's Tanchéi hold the mountain record there. Wejai's record enters every typhoon with its year and its wrecks beside the eruptions and earthquakes, and the Tanchéi read the year from it at the [Festival of the Mountain]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} (_Chai Jui_).
