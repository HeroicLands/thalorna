Zuwu (Blue Hall) is the castle town and seat of [[being-wejairowo|Wejai Rowo]], a town of 2,200 people in the sheltered bay under [[place-wejai|Wejai]]. The hall is roofed in blue-glazed tile that shows from far out to sea, and the whalers steer by it. It stands on a shoulder of the mountain above the quay.

The castle holds the **granary**, where the lord's four parts in ten are delivered from the demesne at [Rewo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} and the rice of [Hanu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, and the hall. The fifteen ships of the domain lie in the bay behind a breakwater that the town rebuilds after every typhoon, and the Tsaihwo soldiers are raised from the quay streets.

The town lives on whale and fish. Flensers, coopers, net-makers and oil-boilers work along the quay, and Shwonong merchants ship the oil and dried fish by coaster to the other mountains.

The wreck-officers report to the hall after every storm, and what they bring in from the reefs is entered in the granary roll.

## History {#history}

Zuwu is the seat of a house that has belonged to neither bloc since the [[lore-empseatwar|War of the Empty Seat]] ([[lore-hstryjenchai|The History of Jenchai]]).
