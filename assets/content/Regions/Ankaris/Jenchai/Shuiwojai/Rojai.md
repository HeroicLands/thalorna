**Rojai** (Thunder Peak) is the westernmost of the Nine Mountains on the great island. It stands on the west coast, facing the strait, and the storms that cross the water break on it first. It is held by House Shuiwojai, and the domain takes its name from the house.

The mountain rises straight from the sea. Rice grows on its terraced shoulders at Rounu, the court estate, and salt is boiled on the shore at Beshenu. The sheltered inlets at Kowo hold the shipyards, and the fleet anchors in the lee of the mountain. The summit is kept as a shrine of the storm aspect of the Celestial Order, and the Tanchéi who keep it hold the mountain record, in which every eruption, earthquake and spring is entered under the year and the lord in whose time it fell.

At the Festival of the Mountain (_Chai Jui_) the Tanchéi read Rojai's year as a year of storms, and Shuiwojai Henzo's claim to heaven's favor rests on that reading.

The lord's castle town, [[place-hawu|Hawu]], stands on the mountain's shore.
