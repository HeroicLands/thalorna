Hawu (Dark Hall) is the castle town and seat of [[being-henzoshuiwo|Shuiwojai Henzo]], a town of 7,500 people. The lord's hall stands above the harbor on a shoulder of [[place-rojai|Rojai]]; the town fills the ground between the hall and the water.

A ship coming up the strait raises the harbor first: a long mole, the naval yards and a double row of fleet berths for the sixty ships of the domain. Behind the quays stand the **granary**, where the lord's four parts in ten are delivered from the demesne at [Kowo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, and the **hall**, where each retainer's seat shows his standing. The retainers keep their houses in the streets below the hall, and the Tsaihwo soldiers are raised from the town.

The town lives on the fleet and the salt. Shipwrights, sailmakers, rope-walkers and smiths work for the yards; the salt that comes up from [Beshenu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} is weighed, entered in the roll of the [Besheju]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} and shipped to the markets of the great island. Shwonong merchants handle the trade, and the law ranks them beneath the peasants whose rice they carry.

Henzo's judges, chosen from his retainers, sit in the hall and hear the cases of the town and the harbor. When the strait fleet sails, it sails from Hawu's mole, and the lord's envoy to the [Dawn Rite]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} on [[place-zojai|Zojai]] leaves from the same quay.

## History {#history}

Hawu is the seat of the lord whose house pressed the court against the regency, and whose silver and ships carried the Strait's side of the [[lore-empseatwar|War of the Empty Seat]] ([[lore-hstryjenchai|The History of Jenchai]]).

## Current Situation {#present}

In 720 AF Hawu waits on a letter. [[being-henzoshuiwo|Shuiwojai Henzo]] has petitioned the Emperor for the office of Teng Ja, and the answer is sealed and on its way by roads that Muwojai keeps. Until it arrives he does not know what is asked of him. His envoys leave from the quay to carry his price to Zenjai and Wojai, and the Shuiwojai envoy at [[place-yanwu|Yanwu]] sends word back after every tremor of Hojai.

Merchants and captains from the mainland land here, since the harbor is the strait's busiest, and they pay Henzo's tolls. The town holds the mainland in contempt and trades with it all the same.
