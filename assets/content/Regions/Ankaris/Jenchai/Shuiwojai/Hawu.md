**Hawū** (Dark Hall) is the castle town and seat of Shuiwōjai Henzo, a town of 7,500 people. The lord's hall stands above the harbor on a shoulder of Rōjai; the town fills the ground between the hall and the water.

A ship coming up the strait raises the harbor first: a long mole, the naval yards and a double row of fleet berths for the sixty ships of the domain. Behind the quays stand the **granary**, where the lord's four parts in ten are delivered from the demesne at Kowō, and the **hall**, where each retainer's seat shows his standing. The retainers keep their houses in the streets below the hall, and the Tsàihuo soldiers are raised from the town.

The town lives on the fleet and the salt. Shipwrights, sailmakers, rope-walkers and smiths work for the yards; the salt that comes up from Beshenū is weighed, entered in the roll of the Beshejū and shipped to the markets of the great island. Shuonong merchants handle the trade, and the law ranks them beneath the peasants whose rice they carry.

Henzo's judges, chosen from his retainers, sit in the hall and hear the cases of the town and the harbor. When the strait fleet sails, it sails from Hawū's mole, and the lord's envoy to the Dawn Rite on Zojai leaves from the same quay.
