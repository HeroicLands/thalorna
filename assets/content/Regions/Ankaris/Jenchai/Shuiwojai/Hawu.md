**Hawu** (Dark Hall) is the castle town and seat of Shuiwojai Henzo, a town of 7,500 people. The lord's hall stands above the harbor on a shoulder of Rojai; the town fills the ground between the hall and the water.

A ship coming up the strait raises the harbor first: a long mole, the naval yards and a double row of fleet berths for the sixty ships of the domain. Behind the quays stand the **granary**, where the lord's four parts in ten are delivered from the demesne at Kowo, and the **hall**, where each retainer's seat shows his standing. The retainers keep their houses in the streets below the hall, and the Tsaihuo soldiers are raised from the town.

The town lives on the fleet and the salt. Shipwrights, sailmakers, rope-walkers and smiths work for the yards; the salt that comes up from Beshenu is weighed, entered in the roll of the Besheju and shipped to the markets of the great island. Shuonong merchants handle the trade, and the law ranks them beneath the peasants whose rice they carry.

Henzo's judges, chosen from his retainers, sit in the hall and hear the cases of the town and the harbor. When the strait fleet sails, it sails from Hawu's mole, and the lord's envoy to the Dawn Rite on Zojai leaves from the same quay.
