**House Shuiwōjai** (Strait Place) holds **Rōjai** (Thunder Peak), the mountain on the west coast of the great island where it faces the strait, by patent of the Emperor. Its lord is the Daozhuo **Shuiwōjai Henzo** (Tiger Lord), forty-seven years old, who sits in the castle town of **Hawū** (Dark Hall), population 7,500. The domain holds 105,000 people, is assessed at 78,000 bales a year, and musters 1,950 men and 60 ships—the largest army and the largest fleet of the eight houses. Henzo leads the Strait bloc, and the office he wants is that of Téng Ja.

## The Land

Rōjai's flank falls to the sea in steps. Rice grows on the terraces under the mountain, salt is boiled on the shore below them, and the sheltered inlets along the coast hold the domain's shipyards. The villages pay the roll in what the shore gives them: rice from the terraces, salt from the pans and dried fish from the boats.

Three of Rōjai's villages are named here. The rest of the 78,000 bales comes from smaller terraces and coastal holdings, each entered under its headman.

The summit shrine of Rōjai keeps the storm aspect of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Celestial Order]], and its Tānchéi are provisioned from the lord's villages.

## Retainers

The **Beshejū** keep the salt records and hold Beshenū as their fief. No salt leaves the pans without an entry in their roll, and the granary at Hawū is paid from it. Beneath Henzo the domain's Shuìzhuo hold one to three villages apiece for the men they owe him in war.

## Fleet and Muster

The muster is 1,950 men and 60 ships. The yards at Kowō build and refit the fleet, and the ships keep the water between Rōjai and the mainland coast; the raids on Tāntsou's shore that the lords of the Nine count as their right sail from Rōjai among other harbors. The [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tāntsou]] counts House Shuiwōjai its nemesis. Rīnjai Hoja, the lord of the lesser island, is sworn to Henzo and adds 650 men and 25 ships, so the Strait bloc stands at 2,600 men and 85 ships.

## Relations

For three years Henzo's silver has covered the shortfall in the court's budget, and the court is in his debt. The lord of Rōjai is the rival of Hojūjai, the house of the last Téng Ja: in 709 AF Shuiwōjai and Wōjai pressed the court not to confer the office on a regency, and the War of the Empty Seat followed. The Strait bloc wants Henzo named Téng Ja. The loyalist bloc of Hojūjai and Mūwōjai wants the office for the Hojūjai heir when he comes of age, or for nobody.

## The Sealed Letter

Henzo has petitioned for the office, and the Emperor has answered with a sealed letter. The letter must leave Zojai by the roads that Mūwōjai keeps and cross Mūwōjai's ferry at Zūjū, and Mūwōjai is a loyalist house. Until the letter reaches Hawū, Henzo does not know whether he holds the office, and he does not trust the hands that carry it.
