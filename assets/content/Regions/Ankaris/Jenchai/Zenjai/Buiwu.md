Buiwu (White Hall) is the castle town and seat of [[being-zenjaitsuiwo|Zenjai Tsuiwo]], a town of 2,800 people on the lower slope of [[place-zenjai|Zenjai]]. Snow lies on its roofs from the first frost until the thaw, which gives the hall its name.

The lord's hall stands on a terrace above the streets. Below it are the **granary**, where the lord's four parts in ten are delivered from the barley and late rice of the terraces and the timber and charcoal of [Buimu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, and the **stable-yard**, where the [Maiju]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} enter the horses that come down from [Maidu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}. The northern levy is raised from the town's streets and the villages.

The town lives on timber, charcoal and horses. Carters take the charcoal south by the shoulder roads while they are open, and the horses go with them. In winter the town is shut in, and the granary feeds it.

Tsuiwo's judges, chosen from his retainers, hear the town's cases in the hall.

## History {#history}

Buiwu is the seat of a house that has belonged to neither bloc since the [[lore-empseatwar|War of the Empty Seat]], and both blocs bid for it ([[lore-hstryjenchai|The History of Jenchai]]).

## Current Situation {#present}

In 720 AF Buiwu is the seat of Zenjai Tsuiwo, thirty-three years old, whose house belongs to neither bloc. The office of Teng Ja stands vacant. Tsuiwo's 1,520 men tip the balance of the Nine, and both the Strait bloc and the loyalists are bidding for them. Both bids are in rice, and neither bloc has the rice to deliver.

The north came through last winter on half rations. Tsuiwo knows the cost village by village, and his price is grain: he will put 1,500 spears behind whichever bloc feeds his province. He wants the granaries full before the first snow, and the question of who holds the office matters less to him.

The stable-yard still enters the horses from Maidu, and the levy rolls are kept ready. Until grain arrives, neither bloc has Zenjai.
