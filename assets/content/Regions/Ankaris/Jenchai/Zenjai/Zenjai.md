Zenjai (Snow Peak) stands in the north of the great island, where the cold is hardest, and it is held by [[affiliation-zenjaidmn|House Zenjai]].

The mountain is under snow for much of the year. The terraces at its foot take one harvest a year, barley and a late rice that ripens only in a warm summer, at [Chonu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}. Above them the slopes are forest, cut for timber and burned for charcoal at [Buimu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, and the high pastures of [Maidu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} raise horses. The castle town of [[place-buiwu|Buiwu]] stands on the lower slope. The roads of the north run south over the shoulders of the mountain, and the snow closes them in winter.

The summit shrine is kept by the domain's Tanchei, who hold the mountain record of eruptions, earthquakes and springs. Zenjai's [Festival of the Mountain]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} (_Chai Jwi_) falls late, when the harvest is in and the assessment paid, and the lord climbs to the shrine through the first snow.

52,000 people live on Zenjai: the whole of the [[affiliation-zenjaidmn|Zenjai Domain]], which holds the castle town of [[place-buiwu|Buiwu]] (2,800 people) and the villages of its roll.

## Current Situation {#present}

In 720 AF the north has come through a winter on half rations, and both blocs are bidding for the 1,500 spears of House Zenjai in rice that they do not have. [[being-zenjaitsuiwo|Zenjai Tsuiwo]] means to sell his choice for grain and not for titles, and he wants the granaries of the mountain full before the first snow.

The snow closes the roads of the north over the shoulders of the mountain in winter, so a bloc that cannot deliver its rice in time loses the bid to the other. The vacant office of Teng Ja leaves the north's hunger as the realm's hinge, and the lord who climbs to the shrine through the first snow comes with that choice still open.
