**House Zenjai** (Snow Peak) holds **Zenjai**, the mountain of the cold north of the great island, by patent of the Emperor. Its lord is the Daozhuo **Zenjai Tsuiwo** (Iron Warrior), thirty-three years old, who sits in the castle town of **Buiwū** (White Hall), population 2,800. The domain holds 52,000 people, is assessed at 38,000 bales a year, and musters 1,520 men, a northern levy of four men for every hundred bales. Zenjai keeps no fleet. The house is independent of both blocs, and Tsuiwo's choice between them ends the balance.

## The Land

The north is harsh and cold, and the mountain carries snow for most of the year. The terraces bear one crop, barley and a late rice that ripens only in a warm summer; the slopes above them are timber and charcoal, and the high pastures raise the horses of the Nine. Its villages pay the roll in barley and late rice, timber, charcoal and horses.

The roll's 38,000 bales come from the three villages named here and from smaller terraces and hamlets, each answering through its headman.

The summit shrine of Zenjai is provisioned from the lord's villages, and its Tānchéi read the mountain for a lord whose year is decided by the snow.

## Retainers

The **Maijū** keep the stables and hold Maidū as their fief; every horse of the domain's levy is entered in their roll. The Shuìzhuo of the north hold one to three villages each and answer for their share of the levy.

## Muster

The muster is 1,520 men, and none of them sails: the domain has no fleet. The levy is raised in the northern manner, four men to the hundred bales against two and a half in the other houses, and the stables of the Maijū mount its officers.

## Relations

Zenjai belongs to neither bloc, and its joining either the Strait bloc or the loyalists ends the balance of the Nine. Both are bidding for it.

## The Half-Ration Winter

The north came through last winter on half rations. Tsuiwo will put 1,500 spears behind whichever bloc feeds his province, and both blocs are bidding in rice that they do not have.
