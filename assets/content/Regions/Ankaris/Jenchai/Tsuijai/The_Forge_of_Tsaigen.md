## Overview {#overview}

The forge stands in [Tsaigen]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} (Craft Master), a village of 160 people on a shelf of [[place-tsuijai|Tsuijai]] above the castle town of [[place-tsuiwu|Tsuiwu]]. The [Jaju]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} hold the village as their fief, the smiths are their dependents, and the blades they make are the blades of the Nine. Tsuijai alone has the iron and the swordsmiths, and the other lords come here to buy.

A party comes to buy or commission a blade, to carry one down to a lord who has paid for it, or to find out who has asked the smiths for something they have not yet begun.

## Inside {#inside}

The yard lies behind a gate, with the forge, and the charcoal-stores. The iron sand of the streams and the ore that comes down the road from the mines of [Jidu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} are smelted below at [[place-tsuiwu|Tsuiwu]] with charcoal cut from the upper slopes, and the smiths work the iron into blades that go down to the armory there. The Jaju keep a roll in which every blade that leaves the forge is entered. An [[being-embrhnd|Emberhound]] lies across the threshold of the gate and guards the yard and the charcoal-stores.

## Who Is There {#who-is-there}

The Jaju keep the armory and the forge, and the Keeper of the Armory is held by the lineage. The smiths bind the fire elemental to the gate, and the lineage renews the binding with each master of the forge. [Tsuijai Rezo]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Dauzhwo, sits in Tsuiwu below and arms his 550 men from the same forge.

## The Present {#present}

The office of Teng Ja stands vacant, and a new holder receives a blade forged at Tsaigen. The smiths will not begin the blade until the Emperor names a holder. Someone has asked them to begin early, and the Jaju have not said who asked.

Meanwhile the lords of the other houses send buyers, and Tsuijai, which is independent of both blocs and has its own price, sells to all of them.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The early order.** A house's agent is sent to Tsaigen to learn who asked the smiths to begin the blade, and finds that the Jaju answer a question about the Emperor with a question about the buyer.
- **The roll of blades.** A lord wants to know which houses have bought from the forge in the last year, and the Jaju's roll is the only record.
- **The gate.** The Emberhound's binding is renewed with each master of the forge, and a party is hired to keep the yard while the lineage renews it for a new master.
