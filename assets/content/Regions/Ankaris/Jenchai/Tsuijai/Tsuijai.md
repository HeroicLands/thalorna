Tsuijai (Iron Peak) stands in the north-west uplands of the great island, in the harsh cold of the north, and it is held by [[affiliation-tsuijaidmn|House Tsuijai]]. The streams that run off it carry iron sand, and the rock is iron-bearing to the summit.

The mines open on the middle slopes, and the ore road of [Jidu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} carries the ore down to the smelters. The upper slopes are cut for charcoal, and the terraces at [Kodu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} bear rice and fuel. The forge-village of [Tsaigen]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} lies on a shelf above the castle town of [[place-tsuiwu|Tsuiwu]], where the smiths of the [Jaju]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} make the blades of the Nine. An [[being-embrhnd|Emberhound]] lies across the gate of the forge-yard.

The summit shrine keeps the earth and metal aspects of [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|the Celestial Order]], and the domain's Tanchei hold the mountain record there. Tsuijai's roll is closed beside it at the [Festival of the Mountain]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} (_Chai Jwi_), when the harvest is in.

40,000 people live on Tsuijai: the whole of the [[affiliation-tsuijaidmn|Tsuijai Domain]], which holds the castle town of [[place-tsuiwu|Tsuiwu]] (2,600 people) and the villages of its roll.

## Current Situation {#present}

In 720 AF the smiths of the [Jaju]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} at Tsaigen have been asked to begin the blade of the next Teng Ja, though [[being-weilengjnch|Weileng]] has not conferred the office. They will not begin until the Emperor names a holder, and the Jaju have not said who asked. An early order suggests that someone is preparing for the office, though a display of the blade order can also be a bluff.

[Tsuijai Rezo]{.name data-type="being/npc"} wants to know who asked. He has named his price to no bloc: it is the forging of the blade, whoever carries it. Every lord buys the mountain's iron, so he wants the balance of the Nine to stand and the Nine to keep buying.
