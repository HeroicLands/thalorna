**Tsuijai** (Iron Peak) stands in the north-west uplands of the great island, in the harsh cold of the north, and it is held by House Tsuijai. The streams that run off it carry iron sand, and the rock is iron-bearing to the summit.

The mines open on the middle slopes, and the ore road of Jidu carries the ore down to the smelters. The upper slopes are cut for charcoal, and the terraces at Kodu bear rice and fuel. The forge-village of Tsaigen lies on a shelf above the castle town of [[place-tsuiwu|Tsuiwu]], where the smiths of the Jaju make the blades of the Nine. An [[being-embrhnd|Emberhound]] lies across the gate of the forge-yard.

The summit shrine keeps the earth and metal aspects of the Celestial Order, and the domain's Tanchéi hold the mountain record there. Tsuijai's roll is closed beside it at the Festival of the Mountain (_Chai Jui_), when the harvest is in.
