**House Tsuijai** (Iron Peak) holds **Tsuijai**, the iron mountain of the north-west uplands of the great island, by patent of the Emperor. Its lord is the Daozhuo **Tsuijai Rēzo** (Bright Dragon), fifty-two years old, who sits in the castle town of **Tsuiwū** (Iron Hall), population 2,600. The domain holds 40,000 people, is assessed at 22,000 bales a year, and musters 550 men; it keeps no fleet. The house is independent of both blocs, and it has the iron and the swordsmiths of the Nine.

## The Land

The uplands are cold and rocky, and the mountain is iron-bearing from its foot to its shoulders. The iron sand of the streams and the ore of the mines are smelted with charcoal from the slopes, and the terraces below the mines bear rice where the ground allows. The villages pay in ore, charcoal and rice, and Tsaigen pays its holders in blades.

Tsuijai's roll names the three villages below; smaller terraces and mining hamlets under their headmen make up the rest of the 22,000 bales.

The summit shrine of Tsuijai keeps the earth and metal aspects of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Celestial Order]], and its Tānchéi are provisioned from the lord's villages.

## Retainers

The **Jajū** keep the armory and hold Tsaigen as their fief; the smiths of the forge-village are their dependents, and every blade that leaves the forge is entered in their roll. The smiths' lineage binds a fire elemental to the gate of its forge-yard: an [[being-embrhnd|Emberhound]] lies across the threshold and guards the yard and the charcoal-stores, and the lineage renews the binding with each master of the forge.

## Muster

The muster is 550 men, and none of them sails. The domain's own forge arms them, and the blades of the Jajū are what the other lords come to buy.

## Relations

Tsuijai belongs to neither bloc. Its lord is one of the four independent houses, who together muster 3,970 men and 55 ships and who each have their own price. Tsuijai alone has the iron and the swordsmiths.

## The Blade for the Téng Ja

The office of Téng Ja stands vacant, and a new holder receives a blade forged at Tsaigen. The smiths will not begin it until the Emperor names a holder, and someone has asked them to begin early. The Jajū have not said who asked.
