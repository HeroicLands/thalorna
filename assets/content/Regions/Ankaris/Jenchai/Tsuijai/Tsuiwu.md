Tsuiwu (Iron Hall) is the castle town and seat of [[being-tsuijairezo|Tsuijai Rezo]], a town of 2,600 people on the slope of [[place-tsuijai|Tsuijai]]. The hall is framed in iron and stone, and it stands above the smelters' smoke on a terrace cut from the rock.

The castle holds the **granary**, where the lord's four parts in ten are delivered from the demesne at [Jidu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} and the rice of the domain, and the hall. The armory of the [Jaju]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} stands beside it, and the Tsaihwo soldiers it arms are raised from the town's streets.

The town lives on iron. Smelters, charcoal-burners, carters and ore-weighers work along the road from the mines, and the iron is sold at the town's market to the buyers of the other houses; the blades come down from [Tsaigen]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} with an entry in the Jaju roll. Shwonong merchants carry the iron away, and the buyers' envoys lodge in the town through the year, each waiting on the Jaju.

## History {#history}

Tsuiwu is the seat of a house that has belonged to neither bloc since the [[lore-empseatwar|War of the Empty Seat]] ([[lore-hstryjenchai|The History of Jenchai]]).

## Current Situation {#present}

In 720 AF Tsuiwu is the seat of Tsuijai Rezo, fifty-two years old, who still works the forge at [Tsaigen]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} himself. His house belongs to neither bloc. The office of Teng Ja stands vacant, and the Strait bloc and the loyalists each send envoys to the town.

Rezo has named his price to neither bloc: it is the forging of the blade a new Teng Ja receives, whoever carries it. The smiths will not begin the blade until the Emperor names a holder. Someone has asked them to begin early, and the Jaju have not said who. Rezo wants to learn who it was.

The market sells to every buyer, and Rezo lets none of them own the house. Until the Emperor names a holder the blade stays unforged, and Rezo gives neither bloc a claim on it.
