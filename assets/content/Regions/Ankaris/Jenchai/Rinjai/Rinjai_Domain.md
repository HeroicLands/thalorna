**House Rīnjai** (Red Peak) holds **Rīnjai**, the mountain of the lesser island at the strait's mouth, by patent of the Emperor. Its lord is the Daozhuo **Rīnjai Hoja** (Fire Blade), twenty-nine years old, who sits in the castle town of **Rīnwū** (Red Hall), population 3,000. The domain holds 41,000 people, is assessed at 26,000 bales a year, and musters 650 men and 25 ships. Hoja is sworn to Shuiwōjai Henzo, and Rīnjai stands with the Strait bloc.

## The Land

The lesser island lies at the mouth of the strait, and every ship that crosses between the Nine Mountains and the mainland passes within sight of Rīnjai's coast. The mountain is steep and the shore is cut with coves, so there is little level ground; the terraces are small and the harbors are many. The domain's rice is thin, and its wealth comes in by sea.

Rīnjai's roll names the three villages below; smaller terraces and landings under their headmen supply the rest of the 26,000 bales.

| Sūdū    | Serpent Road   | Fief         | A Shuìzhuo of the domain                | The switchback road over Rīnjai |      900 |

The summit shrine of Rīnjai is provisioned from the lord's villages, and its Tānchéi keep the mountain record for a house that holds the sea gate of the realm.

## Retainers

The Wòhou of Rīnjai serve the house for stipends in bales, and the retainers are first of all captains: a household's standing is measured in hulls. The Shuìzhuo who holds Sūdū keeps the switchback road over the mountain and owes the lord men in war.

## Fleet and Muster

The muster is 650 men and 25 ships. The ships lie at Jawō, and they are the raiders of the Nine: they cross the strait to the southern coast of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tāntsou]], which the lords of the Nine count their right, and the empire counts House Rīnjai its nemesis. Hoja adds his 650 men and 25 ships to Shuiwōjai's 1,950 and 60, so the Strait bloc stands at 2,600 men and 85 ships.

## Relations

Rīnjai is sworn to Shuiwōjai and allied with it, and the two houses together form the Strait bloc, which wants Shuiwōjai Henzo named Téng Ja. Hojūjai is the rival of Shuiwōjai.

## The Prefect

Hoja's raiders took a Tāntsou prefect off the mainland coast. The court wants the man returned, as a gesture of heaven's magnanimity; Shuiwōjai wants the ransom. Hoja holds the prefect and owes Henzo his oath, and whichever of the two he satisfies, he disappoints the other.
