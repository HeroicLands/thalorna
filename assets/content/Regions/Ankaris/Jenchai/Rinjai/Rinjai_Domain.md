The Rinjai Domain, held by House Rinjai (Red Peak), holds [[place-rinjai|Rinjai]], the mountain of the lesser island at the strait's mouth, by patent of the Emperor. Its lord is the Daozhuo [Rinjai Hoja]{.name data-type="being/npc"} (Fire Blade), twenty-nine years old, who sits in the castle town of [[place-rinwu|Rinwu]] (Red Hall), population 3,000. The domain holds 41,000 people, is assessed at 26,000 bales a year, and musters 650 men and 25 ships. Hoja is sworn to [Shuiwojai Henzo]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, and Rinjai stands with [The Strait]{.name data-type="affiliation"} bloc.

## The Land

The lesser island lies at the mouth of the strait, and every ship that crosses between the Nine Mountains and the mainland passes within sight of Rinjai's coast. The mountain is steep and the shore is cut with coves, so there is little level ground; the terraces are small and the harbors are many. The domain's rice is thin, and its wealth comes in by sea.

Rinjai's roll names the three villages below; smaller terraces and landings under their headmen supply the rest of the 26,000 bales.

| Village | Meaning        | Kind         | Holder                                  | Livelihood                      | Assessed |
| ------- | -------------- | ------------ | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | -------: |
| Jawo    | Blade Sea      | Demesne      | The lord's granary at Rinwu             | The raiders' harbor             |      700 |
| Hennu   | Tiger Terraces | Court estate | The Emperor, through the lord's granary | Rice                            |    1,100 |
| Sudu    | Serpent Road   | Fief         | A Shuizhuo of the domain                | The switchback road over Rinjai |      900 |

The summit shrine of Rinjai is provisioned from the lord's villages, and its Tanchei keep the mountain record for a house that holds the sea gate of the realm.

## Retainers

The Wohou of Rinjai serve the house for stipends in bales, and the retainers are first of all captains: a household's standing is measured in hulls. The Shuizhuo who holds [Sudu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} keeps the switchback road over the mountain and owes the lord men in war.

## Fleet and Muster

The muster is 650 men and 25 ships. The ships lie at [Jawo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, and they are the raiders of the Nine: they cross the strait to the southern coast of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], which the lords of the Nine count their right, and the empire counts House Rinjai its nemesis. Hoja adds his 650 men and 25 ships to Shuiwojai's 1,950 and 60, so the Strait bloc stands at 2,600 men and 85 ships.

## Relations

Rinjai is sworn to [[affiliation-shuiwojaidmn|Shuiwojai]] and allied with it, and the two houses together form the Strait bloc, which wants Shuiwojai Henzo named Teng Ja. [[affiliation-hojujaidmn|Hojujai]] is the rival of Shuiwojai.

## The Prefect

Hoja's raiders took a Tantsou prefect off the mainland coast. The court wants the man returned, as a gesture of heaven's magnanimity; Shuiwojai wants the ransom. Hoja holds the prefect and owes Henzo his oath, and whichever of the two he satisfies, he disappoints the other.
