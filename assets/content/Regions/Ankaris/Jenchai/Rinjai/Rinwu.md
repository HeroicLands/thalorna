Rinwu (Red Hall) is the castle town and seat of [Rinjai Hoja]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a town of 3,000 people on the lesser island. The hall is lacquered red, and it stands on a spur above the main harbor with the strait in front of it.

The castle holds the **granary**, where the lord's four parts in ten are delivered from the demesne at [Jawo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} and the rice of the island, and the hall, where a captain's seat goes by the hulls his household commands. The twenty-five ships of the domain lie in the harbor below, and the Tsaihuo soldiers are raised from the town.

The town lives on the fleet and on what the fleet brings home. Shipwrights, rope-walkers and smiths work for the yards, the prize-keepers weigh and enter the cargoes landed from the mainland coast, and Shuonong merchants buy what the raiders sell.

Hoja's judges, chosen from his retainers, hear the town's cases.
