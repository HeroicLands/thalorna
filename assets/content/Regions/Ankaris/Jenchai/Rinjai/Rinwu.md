Rinwu (Red Hall) is the castle town and seat of [[being-hojarinjai|Rinjai Hoja]], a town of 3,000 people on the lesser island. The hall is lacquered red, and it stands on a spur above the main harbor with the strait in front of it.

The castle holds the **granary**, where the lord's four parts in ten are delivered from the demesne at [Jawo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} and the rice of the island, and the hall, where a captain's seat goes by the hulls his household commands. The twenty-five ships of the domain lie in the harbor below, and the Tsaihwo soldiers are raised from the town.

The town lives on the fleet and on what the fleet brings home. Shipwrights, rope-walkers and smiths work for the yards, the prize-keepers weigh and enter the cargoes landed from the mainland coast, and Shwonong merchants buy what the raiders sell.

Hoja's judges, chosen from his retainers, hear the town's cases.

## History {#history}

Rinwu is the seat of the house sworn to Shuiwojai, which stands in the Strait bloc that the [[lore-empseatwar|War of the Empty Seat]] left behind ([[lore-hstryjenchai|The History of Jenchai]]).

## Current Situation {#present}

In 720 AF Rinwu is the seat of Rinjai Hoja, twenty-nine years old, who holds the red mountain and the oath to [[being-henzoshuiwo|Shuiwojai Henzo]]. The office of Teng Ja stands vacant. The Strait bloc, Rinjai and the [[affiliation-shuiwojaidmn|Shuiwojai Domain]] together, wants Henzo named to it, and the loyalists around the regent [[being-reweihojujai|Hojujai Rewei]] contest it. Hoja adds his 650 men and 25 ships to the Strait bloc.

The Tantsou prefect his raiders took off the mainland coast is held in the town. The court wants him returned as a gesture of heaven's magnanimity, and Henzo wants the ransom. Hoja answers to Henzo, so whatever he does with the prefect disappoints one of them: returning the man displeases the lord he is sworn to, and ransoming him displeases the court.

The prefect stays in Rinwu until Hoja chooses between the ransom and the return.
