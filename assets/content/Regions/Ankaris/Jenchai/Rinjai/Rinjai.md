**Rīnjai** (Red Peak) is the ninth of the Nine Mountains and the only one off the great island. It stands on the lesser island at the strait's mouth, and it is held by House Rīnjai. Its rock is red where the slopes are bare.

The mountain rises steeply from the sea, and its coast is cut with coves that shelter a ship from the strait's storms. The raiders' harbor of Jawō lies in the deepest of them. Small terraces of rice at Hennū climb the sheltered slope, and the road over the mountain, which the Shuìzhuo of Sūdū keeps, runs in switchbacks from the harbors on one side of the island to those on the other. The castle town of [[place-rinwu|Rīnwū]] stands above the main harbor.

The summit shrine is kept by the domain's Tānchéi, who hold the mountain record. The Festival of the Mountain (_Chài Juì_) is held there when the harvest is in.
