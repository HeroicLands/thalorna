## Overview {#overview}

The summit shrine of [[place-zojai|Zojai]] stands on bare rock above the cedar, at the top of the one mountain of the Nine that no Dauzhwo holds. It is the Emperor's own ground: the imperial person may not set foot beyond Zojai, and the shrine is the place on it where he acts for the whole realm. On the first day of the year he climbs to it from the palace above [[place-lengwou|Lengwou]] and performs the [Dawn Rite]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} (_Leng Rwi_) at first light.

A party comes here for that morning: in the retinue of a lord's envoy, as one of the household guard that escorts the Emperor up the mountain, or as a petitioner who has spent the months before it in Lengwou's inns. Foreign ambassadors are received in Lengwou and go no farther, since the upper slope is closed to them.

## Inside {#inside}

The shrine is a small building on the summit rock, with an open court of bare stone before it where the envoys stand. Beside it a frame holds the bronze bell, [[miscgear-dwnrtbll|The Bell of the Dawn Rite]], which sounds at dawn on the first day of the year. The mountain record is kept in the shrine with the other records of the imperial house, and the Emperor makes the claim to the whole [[lore-mandatehvn|Mandate of Heaven]] here.

## Who Is There {#who-is-there}

The [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] of the court keep the shrine all year. On the first morning of the year the Emperor, [Weileng]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, comes up with his household guard, and the envoy of every lord stands in the court in the order of precedence the court has assigned. Every household of the Nine lights a lamp at the same dawn, so the rite is the one act the whole realm performs together, and the order of the envoys on the summit is the year's political map.

## The Present {#present}

The bell is not in its frame. The court pawned it to a merchant house of [[place-yuwu|Yuwu]], and whoever redeems it before the New Year earns the Emperor's gratitude, which costs the Emperor nothing and is prized above silver. If the frame is still empty at first light, the Emperor performs the rite without the bell, in front of every envoy of the Nine.

The envoys have been in Lengwou for months, bargaining for their places. With the office of Teng Ja vacant, the places of the Strait bloc's envoy and the loyalists' envoy are watched by every other house, and so are those of Wojai and Zenjai, whom both blocs are courting. A place nearer the front costs the court nothing to give, so the houses that cannot buy the office bid for the seat.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The escort.** A lord sends his envoy up the mountain with a retinue, and the retinue has to be chosen from men whom the court's guard will let through the upper gate.
- **The place in line.** An envoy has been told that his lord stands one place further back this year, and he wants to know who asked for it.
- **The empty frame.** A house wants the bell redeemed and on the summit before dawn, and will not say why it prefers that the redeemer's name go unspoken.
