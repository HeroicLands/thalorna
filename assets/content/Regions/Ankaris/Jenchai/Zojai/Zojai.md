**Zojai** (Lord's Peak), the Dragon's Mountain, stands in the interior of the great island beside [[place-zijai|Zijai]], among the cedar valleys. It is the one mountain of the Nine that no Daozhuo holds. The Emperor holds it, and with the capital [[place-lengwou|Léngwou]] on its lowest slope it makes up the imperial district, 20,000 people assessed at 6,000 bales a year.

The syllable _zo_ serves for lord and for dragon, which fall together at a single pitch; no other mountain is named for both. Its lower slopes are terraced to the limit of the district, its middle slopes are cedar, and its summit is bare rock with the shrine on it.

The Emperor does not leave the mountain: the imperial person may not set foot beyond Zojai. His palace stands above Léngwou, and every envoy, petition and bale of rice comes to him over the ground of House Muwojai. Foreign ambassadors come as far as Léngwou, and the upper slope is closed to them. On the first day of the year the Emperor climbs to the summit shrine and performs the **Dawn Rite** (_Leng Rui_) at first light. The envoy of every lord stands on the mountain for it, in the order of precedence that the court has assigned them, and every household of the Nine lights a lamp at the same dawn. The order on the summit is the year's political map.

The summit shrine is kept by the Tanchéi of the court, and the mountain record is kept there with the other records of the imperial house. The Emperor holds the one claim to the whole Mandate of Heaven, and Zojai is where he makes it.
