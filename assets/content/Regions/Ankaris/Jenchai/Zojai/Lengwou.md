**Léngwou** (the Dawn Court) is the capital of Jénchài, a town of 12,000 people on the lowest slope of [[place-zojai|Zojai]], below the Emperor's palace; the islanders say _Rēwū_. With Zojai it forms the imperial district of 20,000 people, assessed at 6,000 bales a year. It is the only town of the Nine that no Daozhuo holds.

## The Court

The Emperor is **Wéiléng** (Jade Dawn), nineteen years old, enthroned in 712 AF at eleven, when his father abdicated as the price of the peace that ended the War of the Empty Seat. He is styled _Rongléng Téngzhéi_, Son of Heaven of Ten Thousand Dawns. His father, the **Retired Emperor**, lives in Léngwou and keeps a household that the court cannot afford. The Emperor has a household guard and no other force.

The court is a pageant of ancient protocol, and its business is honor. The Emperor issues each Daozhuo's patent of the mountain on every succession, and he grants the court dignities: **Celestial Minister**, **Caste-Registrar** and **Censor of Purity** confer precedence and no function, while **Omen-Reader** and **Keeper of the Sacred Texts** remain working posts of the Tānchéi, the first with its observatory on Zījai. He holds the Dawn Rite on Zojai each New Year, where the order in which the lords' envoys stand is the year's political map. He also confers the office of **Téng Ja**, which stands vacant.

## The Treasury

The court's nominal income is 6,880 bales a year: four parts in ten of the district's assessment, which is 2,400 bales, and four parts in ten of eight **court estates**, which is 4,480 bales of the 11,200 assessed. One village in each domain is a court estate, and each Daozhuo collects its share through his own granary and forwards what he chooses. In 719 AF the court received 4,100 bales and spent 9,000. The Retired Emperor's household is part of that cost.

Silver from Shuiwōjai has covered the gap for three years, and the court is in Henzo's debt. The strongroom of the court stands at Tsaimū in Mūwōjai ground, in the keeping of that house's Weijū.

## The Town

Léngwou lives on the court. Its 12,000 people are the Emperor's household and guard, the Tānchéi of the shrine and the offices, the craftsmen who serve the palace, and the inns and lodgings of the envoys, who come from every house for the Dawn Rite and stay for months to bargain over their places. The town is poor beside the castle towns of the lords, since it has no fleet, mine or harbor of its own, and the rice that feeds it arrives through the granaries of the houses.

The Emperor's judges hear the town's cases, and a village of the district settles its own quarrels through its headman. Foreign ambassadors are received at Léngwou and go no farther, since the upper slope of Zojai is closed to them.
