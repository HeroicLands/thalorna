Yanwu (Cloud Hall) is the castle town and seat of [[affiliation-hojujaidmn|House Hojujai]], a town of 6,000 people on the lower slope of [[place-hojai|Hojai]]. The steam of the springs lies over it in the cold months, which gives the town its name. The hall of the lord stands on a terrace above the streets, with the mountain's smoke behind it.

The castle holds the **granary**, where the lord's four parts in ten are delivered from the demesne at [Yanju]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, and the hall. [[being-reweihojujai|Hojujai Rewei]] holds the keys of both while her son is a minor. The retainers of the house, who served the last Teng Ja, are quartered in the town, and the Tsaihwo soldiers of the domain are raised from its streets.

The town lives on its water. Bathhouses fed from the springs take pilgrims on their way to the summit shrine and retainers on leave from the other mountains, and the sulfur dug at the vents is weighed and sold at the town's market. Rice from the ash-soil terraces of [Fanu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} and [Weinu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} comes in by the mountain road.

Rewei governs through judges the household appoints from among the retainers. The envoys of the other houses watch the town; the [[affiliation-shuiwojaidmn|Shuiwojai]] envoy there sends word to [[place-hawu|Hawu]] after every tremor.

## History {#history}

Yanwu is the household seat of the last Teng Ja, Hojujai Henwo, who died in 709 AF; his widow has held it for their son since, through the [[lore-empseatwar|War of the Empty Seat]] ([[lore-hstryjenchai|The History of Jenchai]]).

## Current Situation {#present}

In 720 AF Yanwu waits on the mountain. [[being-zijahojujai|Hojujai Zija]] is fourteen, and every tremor of [[place-hojai|Hojai]] sends the town's Tanchei and sorcerers to argue over what it says of a widow's regency.

[[being-reweihojujai|Hojujai Rewei]] has named a price to Zenjai and Wojai, and the granary above the town must meet it. Her steward has not been shown the figure.
