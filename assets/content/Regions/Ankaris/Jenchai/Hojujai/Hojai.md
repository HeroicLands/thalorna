**Hojai** (Fire Peak) is the most active mountain of the Nine. It stands in the south of the great island, where the climate is mild, and it is held by House Hojūjai.

The summit smokes, and the mountain trembles often enough that the households of the slopes sweep their shrines twice over and pay their arrears first. Its ash has made the terraces at its foot the best rice ground in the realm, at Fanū and Weinū. Hot springs rise on the southern slope, where the bathhouses of Yanjū stand, and sulphur is dug near the vents and carried down to the coast. The castle town of [[place-yanwu|Yanwū]] stands on the lower slope.

The summit shrine is kept by the domain's Tānchéi, who hold the mountain record: every eruption, earthquake and spring of Hojai is entered there under its year and the lord in whose time it fell. While the lord is a boy, the record is read at the Festival of the Mountain (_Chài Juì_) to a house governed by his mother, and every tremor entered in it is weighed against her regency. An eruption is heaven's attention, and a [[being-pyrclsm|Pyroclasm]] that walks out of the crater is heaven's instrument.

When the mountain erupts, the retainers of the house clear the villages in its path and hold the roads while the people leave.
