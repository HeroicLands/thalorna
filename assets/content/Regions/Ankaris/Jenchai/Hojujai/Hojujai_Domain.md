**House Hojūjai** (Hot Spring Place) holds **Hojai** (Fire Peak), the most active mountain of the Nine, in the south of the great island, by patent of the Emperor. The Daozhuo is **Hojūjai Zīja** (Star Blade), fourteen years old, and the domain is governed in his name by his mother **Hojūjai Rēwei** (Dawn Jade), thirty-eight, widow of the last Téng Ja, who has held it for him since 709 AF. She sits in the castle town of **Yanwū** (Cloud Hall), population 6,000. The domain holds 92,000 people, is assessed at 70,000 bales a year, and musters 1,750 men and 20 ships. Hojūjai leads the loyalist bloc.

## The Land

Hojai smokes. Its ash has made the best rice soil in the realm on the terraces at its foot, its springs feed the bathhouses on its southern slope, and its sulphur is dug and carried down to the coast. The villages pay in ash-soil rice, and Yanjū pays in sulphur and in the takings of its bathhouses.

The roll names three villages here; smaller terraces and hamlets, each under its headman, make up the rest of the 70,000 bales.

| Fanū    | Fortune Terraces | Court estate | The Emperor, through the lord's granary | Rice on ash soil, the best in the realm |    2,200 |

The summit shrine of Hojai is provisioned from the lord's villages, and its Tānchéi keep the mountain record on which the house's claim to heaven's favor rests.

## Retainers

Rēwei holds the keys of the castle and its granary and decides in her son's name, and the retainers who serve her are the household of the last Téng Ja. They serve the house for stipends in bales or for villages held as fiefs. The Shuìzhuo who holds Weinū owes the house men in war.

## Muster

The muster is 1,750 men and 20 ships. When the mountain erupts and a [[being-pyrclsm|Pyroclasm]] walks out of it, the retainers clear the villages in its path, and the lord's sorcerers and the shrine's priests try to turn it toward the sea; the muster is called out for it as it is for a war.

## Relations

Hojūjai and Mūwōjai together form the loyalist bloc, with 2,500 men and 20 ships. They want the office of Téng Ja for the Hojūjai heir when he comes of age, or for nobody. Shuiwōjai is the rival: in 709 AF Shuiwōjai and Wōjai pressed the court not to confer the office on a regency, which was the quarrel that began the War of the Empty Seat.

## The Mountain's Verdict

Hojai is rumbling. The omen-readers are divided on whether the mountain condemns a widow's regency. An eruption before Zīja comes of age settles the argument against his mother, and until he does, Rēwei holds the house on a reading that the next tremor can reverse.
