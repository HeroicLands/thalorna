A graduate of the [Academy of the Silver Veil]{.name data-type="affiliation"} asked why [[affiliation-kalydria|Kalidria]] teaches its daughters to listen answers by quoting the first rule of her school, set out at The Founding of the Silver Veil, and she quotes it in the founder's words: the city could not keep its freedom by arms, and would keep it by conversation.

## The Founding

By about 260 BF the war was fifty years over. Kalidria had come under the [Vylarian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} with the rest of [[place-helionis|Helionis]], and it kept its council, its theaters and its patron families. What it did not keep was any influence over the men who now governed Helionis from the Republic's seat. The _therkoi_ (governors) and the _kurthoi_ (senators) who came to the province were not educated men, and their households were not educated households. The patron families saw that whoever taught those households would be heard in [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]], the Senate.

[Lékhisira Kálides]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, of a patron family, founded the Academy to do the teaching. She took girls from the city's best houses and trained them in music, philosophy, rhetoric and the social arts, and she placed them as companions in the households of _therkoi_ and _kurthoi_. Each was educated to be better company than anyone else in the house.

## The First Rule

The Academy's first rule is the sentence its graduates carry. The city could not keep its freedom by arms and would keep it by conversation. The rule says nothing of the Republic's soldiers or of the Senate, and the omission is the point: the Academy never names an opponent, and a graduate never admits to being an instrument.

## What the Academy Left

- **The Academy of the Silver Veil**, the most distinctive institution of Kalidria, whose graduates are hetairin and are never called courtesans in the city.
- **The graduates' web** across [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]], an unofficial intelligence service with no office and no master.
- **The root of [[affiliation-velvethand|the Velvet Hand]].** The syndicate recruits from the Academy's graduates and dropouts alike, and its trade is the Academy's skill turned to another purpose.
- **A reputation.** Kalidrian taste, in manners and entertainment, travels farther than Kalidria's arms ever could.
