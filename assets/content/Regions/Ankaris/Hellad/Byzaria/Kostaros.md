## Overview

Six thousand people live in Kostaros, one of the lesser harbors that string out along the coast of [[place-byzariargn|Byzaría]] beside the great port of [[place-denizara2|Denizara]]. To the counting houses of [[affiliation-altinkale|Altinkale]] a harbor this size is a line of dues. To the people who come out of it, it is home, and the character notes name three of them.

## Who Comes from Kostaros

- [[being-theklastnkt|Thêkla Steníkot]] is a mercenary whom the Village Council of [[place-marshfeld|Marshfeld]] hires every year against bandits and raiders.
- [[being-klxnsprklvs|Kallíxenos Paraklávos]] is a litigant in the League's commercial courts.
- [[being-agapevrzns|Agápeia Varzánês]] is a miner.
