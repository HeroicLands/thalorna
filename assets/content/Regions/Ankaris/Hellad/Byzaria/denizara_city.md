## Overview

Altinkale has the gold, a Denizaran says, but Denizara has the ships. The city sits on a fortress isle in the straits that carry its name, and it is the principal seaport of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Byzarian League]], the gateway where the overland caravan routes of the east meet the sea-trading powers of the west. Its harbor is one of the busiest in the eastern [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]], and about 70,000 people live and work around it.

## The Waterfront

The waterfront is a maze of warehouses, taverns and chandleries where a dozen languages can be heard on any given day. The docks are crowded with [[affiliation-cnfdrtnhrdnstts|Haradian]] merchantmen, Vylarian galleys and the occasional vessel from as far away as [[affiliation-kalihara|Kalihara]]. Inland, the city climbs a series of terraced hills, and the wealthier districts rise above the harbor's noise and smell. A Dunhari and a Khazryni merchant quarter keep their own tongues, faiths and law within the city.

## Who Governs

A council of merchant-princes and the admiralty govern Denizara. The admiralty maintains a fleet of fast war-galleys to protect League shipping from pirates and from Haradian competition, and Denizara's shipwrights are among the finest in the eastern sea. That naval strength gives the city considerable influence in the League council.

## Making a Living

Denizara lives on transfer. Eastern goods arrive from [[place-altinkale2|Altinkale]] by the lowland road and leave in League and foreign hulls; western goods make the journey in reverse. Its captains have traded with the coasts of [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]] and [[place-xerathia|Xerathia]] for a thousand years.
