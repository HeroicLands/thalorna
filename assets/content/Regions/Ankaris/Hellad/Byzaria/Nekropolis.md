Nékropolis is no ordinary city of the living—it is a sprawling necropolis complex and city of the dead, one of the most extensive burial grounds in all of western Ankaris. Located in the arid highlands inland from [[affiliation-denizara|Denizara]], the site has been continuously used as a sacred burial ground for over two thousand years, accumulating wealth, monuments, and spiritual significance that draws pilgrims and mourning families from across the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Byzarian League]] and beyond.

The complex is organized into distinct districts corresponding to different eras, classes, and religious traditions. The oldest quarter contains monumental tombs built in styles now lost to living memory—stepped pyramids and underground chambers carved from limestone bedrock. Later sections feature the columned mausolea typical of Byzarian merchants, while the newest sections sprawl across the plateau with the simpler stone markers favored by common families. Interspersed throughout are temples dedicated to [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèl]] and the ancestral spirits, maintained by the priesthood and funded by perpetual endowments established by the dead's heirs.

The city functions with its own governance, distinct from any of the League's city-states. A council of senior priests and hereditary keepers of the tombs administers the necropolis, collecting fees from bereaved families, maintaining the burial chambers, and overseeing the elaborate festivals that honor the dead. The population is relatively small—priests, custodians, caretakers, and merchants who service the continuous stream of mourners—but Nékropolis wields considerable soft power in League politics, as every major merchant family maintains ancestral tombs there and no noble wishes to offend those who guard their ancestors' rest.
