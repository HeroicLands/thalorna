"We do not sell graves," a hereditary tomb-keeper of Nékropolis tells a merchant's widow who has come to arrange a tomb for her husband. "We keep them, and the keeping is what you pay for."

Nékropolis is a city of the dead, a sprawling necropolis complex in the arid highlands inland from [[affiliation-denizara|Denizara]] and one of the most extensive burial grounds in all of western Ankaris. It has been in continuous use as a sacred burial ground for over two thousand years, and in that time it has gathered wealth, monuments and spiritual weight that draw pilgrims and mourning families from across the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Byzarian League]] and beyond.

## The Districts

The complex is organized into districts that correspond to different eras, classes and religious traditions. The oldest quarter holds monumental tombs built in styles now lost to living memory: stepped pyramids and underground chambers carved from limestone bedrock. Later sections hold the columned mausolea typical of Byzarian merchants, and the newest sprawl across the plateau with the simpler stone markers favored by common families. Temples dedicated to Aurèl and the ancestral spirits stand throughout, maintained by the priesthood and funded by perpetual endowments that the dead's heirs establish.

## Who Keeps It

Nékropolis governs itself, apart from any of the League's city-states. A council of senior priests and hereditary keepers of the tombs administers it: they collect fees from bereaved families, maintain the burial chambers and oversee the elaborate festivals that honor the dead. The population is small, made up of priests, custodians, caretakers and the merchants who serve the steady stream of mourners.

Its weight in the League is out of proportion to that number. Every major merchant family keeps ancestral tombs here, and no noble wishes to offend the people who guard his ancestors' rest, so the council holds a soft power in League politics that no census of its residents would suggest.
