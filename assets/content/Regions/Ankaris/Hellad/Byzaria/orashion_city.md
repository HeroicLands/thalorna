## Overview

In **Órashion** the forges burn day and night, and the dark city seems to burn with them. The League calls it Blackstone, after the black basalt of its local quarries, which gives its walls their dark, sturdy look. Órashion is a mining and manufacturing city of about 45,000, built on deposits of iron, copper and semi-precious stones in the mountainous interior of [[place-byzariargn|Byzaría]], and it produces the arms, armor, metalwork and finished goods that the other [[affiliation-byzarianlg|League]] cities trade across the continent. Its output runs from fine jewelry to military-grade weapons.

## The Roads and the Mines

The mountains around the city are riddled with mines, and the roads leading out of it are clogged with ore wagons heading in and finished goods heading out. Where [[place-khryshteikhos2|Khrýshteíkhos]] glitters and [[place-nesthiros2|Nêsthiros]] bustles, Órashion works, and the League's commerce ultimately depends on its having goods to sell.

## Who Governs

A guild council and merchant-princes govern Órashion, and the guild masters sit on the city council with as much authority as the merchant-princes. The craft guilds control the quality and quantity of production, so a visitor who wants a blade, a cast or a commission negotiates with a guild. The guild system gives ordinary craftsmen a political voice that workers in the other League cities lack, which makes Órashion a working-class city whose politics run more egalitarian, or at least more contentious, than the merchant oligarchies of Khrýshteíkhos and Nêsthiros. A master's seal from Órashion or [[place-byzaris|Byzaris]] opens doors from [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Tarûzel]] to [[affiliation-jurthatempr|Jürthāt]].
