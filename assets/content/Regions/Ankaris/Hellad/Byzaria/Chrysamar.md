"Ask the shipper what is in the crate," a Chrisamar captain tells a new mate. "Ask me where it is going and when." The harborfront behind him is a wall of warehouses, and most of them belong to merchant consortiums that operate independently of the strict governance of the larger cities of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]].

Chrisamar is a secondary port city of the League, about 25,000 people strong, placed on the coast to rival the supremacy of [[affiliation-nesthiros|Nesthiros]]. Its name carries poetic weight in the [[skill-byzarnlng|Bizarian Language]]—_chrysos_ (gold) and _mar_ (sea)—and its merchants have done much to live up to it, amassing considerable wealth through maritime trade that competitors claim rivals that of the League's premier harbor.

## What Passes Through

The city's strength lies in swift, high-value trade rather than bulk cargo. Chrisamar is the primary entrepôt for spice, fine wines and luxury goods moving between the merchant fleets of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]] and the inland markets of the [[place-provenzrgn|Provenzia Region]] and the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]. It lacks Nesthiros's deep-water harbors and larger carrying capacity, and it makes up the difference with agility, quick turnarounds and a reputation for favorable terms with foreign trading partners.

## A Looser Town

The consortium warehouses give Chrisamar a more informal, entrepreneurial character than its larger sister cities. Smugglers, privateers and independent traders find it more welcoming than the bureaucratic apparatus of [[affiliation-khryshteikhos|Krusteikhos]], and the taverns are thick with tales of daring voyages, merchant ventures and fortunes won and lost.

## The Rivalry with Nesthiros

Relations with Nesthiros stay perpetually tense, with commercial rivalry, occasional naval skirmishes between merchant fleets and careful diplomatic posturing at League councils.
