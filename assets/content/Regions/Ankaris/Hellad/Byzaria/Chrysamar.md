"Ask the shipper what is in the crate," a Chrysamar captain tells a new mate. "Ask me where it is going and when." The harborfront behind him is a wall of warehouses, and most of them belong to merchant consortiums that operate independently of the strict governance of the League's larger cities.

Chrysamar is a secondary port city of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Byzarian League]], about 25,000 people strong, placed on the coast to rival the supremacy of [[affiliation-denizara|Denizara]]. Its name carries poetic weight in Byzarian—_chrysos_ (gold) and _mar_ (sea)—and its merchants have done much to live up to it, amassing considerable wealth through maritime trade that competitors claim rivals that of the League's premier harbor.

## What Passes Through

The city's strength lies in swift, high-value trade rather than bulk cargo. Chrysamar is the primary entrepôt for spice, fine wines and luxury goods moving between the merchant fleets of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtnhrdnstts|Confederation of Haradian States]] and the inland markets of [[place-provenzrgn|Provènzia Region]] and the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]. It lacks Denizara's deep-water harbors and larger carrying capacity, and it makes up the difference with agility, quick turnarounds and a reputation for favorable terms with foreign trading partners.

## A Looser Town

The consortium warehouses give Chrysamar a more informal, entrepreneurial character than its larger sister cities. Smugglers, privateers and independent traders find it more welcoming than the bureaucratic apparatus of [[affiliation-altinkale|Altinkale]], and the taverns are thick with tales of daring voyages, merchant ventures and fortunes won and lost.

## The Rivalry with Denizara

Relations with Denizara stay perpetually tense, with commercial rivalry, occasional naval skirmishes between merchant fleets and careful diplomatic posturing at League councils.
