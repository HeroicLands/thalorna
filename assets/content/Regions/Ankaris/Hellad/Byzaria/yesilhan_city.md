## Overview

After the dry eastern hills, **Yeşilhan** is green. The city lies in a lush river valley, an oasis amid the drier country around it, and it takes its name from the valley and from its caravanserais: fortified inn-complexes that house hundreds of merchants, their servants and their pack animals, the largest of them small cities in their own right. This is the Green Caravanserai of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Byzarian League]], a city of about 40,000, and it sits where the caravan roads divide, one road east toward the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn Desert]] and one south toward [[place-dunharargn|Dunhara]].

## The Bazaars

Eastern goods first pass into League hands here: spices, silk, incense and gemstones. They are bought in bazaars famous across the continent for the variety and quality of what they sell, then distributed to the other League cities and onward to [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]]. A buyer who wants something from beyond the passes comes to Yeşilhan, and a seller from beyond the passes comes here to meet him.

## Who Governs

A council of caravansary masters and merchant-princes governs Yeşilhan. The caravansary masters are shrewd negotiators, and the political influence of the men who run the great caravanserais rivals that of the merchant-princes, so a traveler who needs lodging, a guide, or an introduction deals with them. Beyond the city the [[place-southrnmrch|Southern March]] carries the road toward Dunhara.

## Character

Yeşilhan has a stronger eastern character than the other League cities. [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Āsháian]] worship has a significant following, particularly among the Dunhari and Khazryni merchants who keep permanent trading quarters here with their own tongues, faiths and law. The city has a reputation for gardeners, merchants and peaceful culture.
