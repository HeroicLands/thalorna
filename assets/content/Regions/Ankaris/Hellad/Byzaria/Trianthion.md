## Overview

Trianthion was a small village in the hinterland of [[place-byzariargn|Byzaría]], and its name is now the name of a massacre. Twenty-six years ago the village refused to submit to a local lord: it turned away his tax collectors, refused his garrison, and sheltered a fugitive bandit chief who had robbed the lord's caravan. The lord hired [[affiliation-irnwlvscmpny|the Iron Wolves Company]] to take it.

## The Massacre at Trianthion

The company's instructions were the standard ones for such work: enter the village, seize the bandit chief, and show the cost of resistance, which usually meant burning a few houses and hanging the chief in public. What happened was far more. By the end of the day every adult in Trianthion had been killed, and the bandit chief could not be told from any other body when they were counted. The company collected its standard rate and left. The lord paid without comment.

## What It Left

Trianthion is the wound the Iron Wolves have never closed. It produced the company's most famous deserter, [[being-kyrksptrks|Kyriákos Patrikîos]], whom the company still looks for; it produced the company's veterans, such as **First-Sergeant Karûsh**, who was a lieutenant there and has never forgiven the desertion; and it produced the reputation the company trades on, which makes the Iron Wolves synonymous, in the politics of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Byzarian League]], with the work the League prefers not to admit buying. If a survivor ever came forward to testify in public, the League would have to act on what happened there.
