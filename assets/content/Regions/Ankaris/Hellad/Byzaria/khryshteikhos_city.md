## Overview

Nothing from the east reaches the western sea without passing through **Khrýshteíkhos**. The lowland road from [[place-nesthiros2|Nêsthiros]] climbs the last escarpment to the edge of the plateau, and the first thing in sight is the gilded domes of the counting houses. This is the Golden Fortress, the largest city of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Byzarian League]] at 80,000 people and its wealthiest and most influential, and its banking houses finance trade across half of the continent. Eastern goods cross the plateau to Khrýshteíkhos, are banked here, and then descend to the coast to be shipped west.

## Who Governs

A council of merchant-princes governs Khrýshteíkhos, and the ruling families have held their seats for generations. Politics here is a long exchange of alliances, marriages and financial maneuvers. The League council meets in the **Merchant Hall**, a vast, ornate building that is parliament and exchange at once, and the city's representatives have dominated League policy through the plain weight of their money.

## Character

The city is renowned for its goldsmiths, its bankers and its silk markets. Centuries of accumulated wealth show in its architecture: gilded domes, marble colonnades and ornamental gardens that blend Helonic elegance with eastern opulence. The banks hold their wealth in the Vylarian currency and the paper-script of the [[affiliation-danvar|Danvar]], and Khrýshteíkhos is a member of the post-imperial commonwealth, taking part in the whole banking system without answering to imperial governance or paying imperial tax.
