The **Merchant Consortia of Chrisamar** hold the warehouses of the harborfront at [[place-chrysamar|Chrisamar]], a secondary port city of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]]. They operate independently of the strict governance of the League's larger cities, which gives the port a looser, more entrepreneurial character.
