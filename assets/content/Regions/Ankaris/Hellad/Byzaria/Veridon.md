## Overview

Veridon is a small town in the hinterland of [[place-byzariargn|Byzaría]], and the thing a traveler notices before the town is the compound outside it: the fortified permanent base of [[affiliation-irnwlvscmpny|the Iron Wolves Company]], with barracks, training fields, an extensive armory, stables for the cavalry detachment and the company's archives. Veridon depends on the company for its living.

## A Company Town

The arrangement has given Veridon stability, and it has given its people a manner a stranger cannot read: their attitudes toward the company are uniformly opaque to outsiders. The company is the town's employer, its customer and its protection, and it is also the company that did what it did at [[place-trianthion|Trianthion]].
