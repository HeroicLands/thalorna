## Overview

The caravans that cross the eastern mountains reach **Gümüşhisar** before they reach anything else in the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Byzarian League]], and the first thing they meet is a wall. The Silver Citadel sits astride the principal pass connecting Byzaría to the eastern lands, and its fortress walls are among the most formidable in the region. About 35,000 people live in it, the smallest of the five League capitals, and it carries the League's weight in the east.

## Who Governs

A military governor and a council of merchant-princes govern Gümüşhisar. The governor also sits on the League council beside the merchant-princes, a standing reminder that the League's prosperity rests on the security of its trade routes. The garrison keeps the mountain routes open and discourages the ambitions of eastern warlords, desert raiders and bandits, and the city's visible readiness to fight is meant to make fighting unnecessary. Beyond the pass lies the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]], whose Lord Commander is the senior soldier of the League.

## Character

The city is more austere than the other League cities: less ornate, more functional, with the disciplined air of a garrison. The silver mines in the surrounding peaks fund the League's collective defense and give the city a political weight beyond its size, and the mines support a community of skilled engineers and metalworkers. The monasteries of the eastern rite are especially influential here. Their monks serve as chroniclers and healers, and as occasional diplomatic intermediaries with the peoples beyond the passes.
