## Overview {#overview}

"Twenty years I have paid the tax and stood the levy," says a metic who has kept a shop on the Kostaros coast for that long, "and the assembly has never once asked what I think." A resident foreigner's lot is the same in every Helionite city-state, and what marks Kostaros is its land. It is a coastal city-state of about 70,000 people that governs the district around it, the [[place-kostarosdst|Kostaros District]], which takes in the city of [[place-myrtillos|Mirtelos]]. Kostaros and Mirtelos hold about 50,000 of those people, and the land cannot feed them: the city-state buys its grain by sea. An oligarchy of its wealthy patron families holds the government.

> Not to be confused with the Bizarian port town of the same name in the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]], which is the city-state's daughter-port from the colonizing centuries ([[lore-daughtercts|the Daughter-Cities]]).

## Who Decides

Kostaros works inside the forms the seven city-states share. The assembly of citizens is sovereign, a council chosen by lot prepares its business for a year, magistrates answer to an auditor, the citizens elect a strátimor and the arsir names the year. The patron families hold the city's government within those forms.

Standing is the common Helionite ladder, and the metic's complaint belongs to its middle rung: a resident foreigner is taxed and liable to the levy, with no vote and no right to own land. A citizen by descent votes in the assembly and serves in the militia.

## Relations {#relations}

Kostaros is aligned with the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian pantheon]] and shares the [[skill-helonclng|Helonik tongue]] and the festivals of the other six city-states. Within its land lies Mirtelos, a city of 20,000.
