## Overview {#overview}

**Zarakia** is the smallest of the caravan towns, about 2,000 people on the western shore of an inland sea. There is nothing much at the water: a mud beach, a belt of reeds, a bitter well that the town has dug deeper every generation. The town is a wall, a caravanserai and a market yard, and a stranger who arrives at dusk finds the gate already shut and the watch looking him over from above.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Zarakia keeps the last stop on the road before the mountains, and its trade is the trade of a place travelers pass: lodging, water, fodder, farriers and guards. The market yard is small and busy, and most of what changes hands has come a long way and will go farther. The town's own people are innkeepers, stablers, carters, guides and the hired swords who wait in its taverns for a train that needs an escort, and the garrison watches the last group closely.

The land lends little. A scatter of villages in a dry valley a half-day behind the town grows barley and a little wheat under irrigation, and the people of those villages sleep inside the town walls during the raiding months and walk out to their fields by day. They cannot grow enough, and the town buys the rest from the caravans, at a price set by whichever train comes in hungry.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

[[affiliation-kalydria|Kalidria]] holds Zarakia through a wall-warden who commands a small garrison of levy spearmen. The caravan-masters' council runs the market yard and the wells, and the warden keeps the gate. Neither trusts the other's accounts.

## The Present {#present}

The hired swords who wait in Zarakia work for whoever pays, and some of them follow [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]]. The Flame's presence is quiet, a few men who keep to themselves and a shrine in a back room of one inn, and the garrison has not decided whether it is a danger or a convenience. Steppe bands test the road in the spring, and the garrison keeps the beacon on the north tower fed.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The escort for hire.** A caravan-master needs guards for a journey and has been turned down by every free sword in the town, and he will not say why.
- **The back-room shrine.** The innkeeper whose back room holds the shrine asks the party to carry a sealed package to Kalidria and not to ask what is in it.
