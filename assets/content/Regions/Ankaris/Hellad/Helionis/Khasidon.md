## Overview {#overview}

**Khasidon** is the largest of the mountain towns of [[place-khaddaria|Khaddaria]], about 3,500 people inside a wall of dark stone laid across a spur above a ridge of red iron. The smelters stand inside the wall, because ore carts left outside it do not survive a bad season, and the smoke of their furnaces is the first sign of the town from the last pass below. The people are miners, furnace crews, charcoal-burners and the carters who move what they make.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Shafts follow the red seams into the ridge, and the ore comes down on sledges to furnaces fed with charcoal. The charcoal comes from the beech and oak slopes on the far side of the ridge, where dozens of villages lie within a morning's walk of the gate. Their people grow barley and pulses on narrow terraces, keep goats, and burn charcoal through the months when the terraces rest. Each village is ringed by a palisade, and the nearest ones keep a watch-fire that the town's wall answers.

The terraces cannot feed 3,500 smelters and miners, so Khasidon buys its grain. Wheat comes up the lower road from the foothill lowlands, and more arrives over the northern pass from [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia]]. Bar iron and blooms go out by mule train to the smiths of [[place-thyrenae2|Thirenae]] and on to the coast. The town sends iron and receives bread, and a closed road stops both.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

Khasidon answers to [[affiliation-thyrenae|Thirenae]], whose council appoints the wall-warden. He commands the garrison, a citizen levy backed by hired spearmen, keeps the gate shut at night and the road to the first pass patrolled. A guild of mine-masters sets the ore tallies and the shares of the diggers.

## The Present {#present}

[[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]] has a presence among the smelters: some of the furnace crews gather at a hearth behind the slag yard after the night shift, and the town is wary of it and does not speak of it. The wall-warden's men watch who goes there and write the names down. Hill bands raid the pass road every autumn, when the carts carry the year's heaviest loads.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The cold furnace.** The great furnace goes out on the third night of a long run, and the smelter-masters say the charcoal that came down from the slopes was green wood sold as seasoned. The carter who sold it cannot be found.
- **The late wheat.** The grain from the lowlands is three weeks overdue, the price of bread has doubled, and the furnace crews are asking who profits.
