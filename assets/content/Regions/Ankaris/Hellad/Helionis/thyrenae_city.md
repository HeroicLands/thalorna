## Overview

A scholar's first lesson in **Thyrenae** is where the credential comes from: a Thyrenean education is the most prestigious a scholar can claim. The city is the oldest of the three that lead the seven, home to 60,000 people and the birthplace of the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Plûmepistémion]], the scholarly federation that gave rise to the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thôrdimâlon]]. Its academies remain the most respected centers of learning in western Ankaris, and students travel from across [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]] to attend them. Its patron is [[lore-marendasdty|Mârendâs]], goddess of knowledge and wisdom.

## Character

The city is built to suit its students. Its architecture is graceful and restrained—white marble colonnades, shaded courtyards, and terraced gardens designed for contemplation and debate. The great **Library of Thyrenae** is said to hold more manuscripts than any collection outside [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Aû'Khelâthu]], and scholars travel across continents for the privilege of consulting its archives.

## Who Governs

A philosophical council governs Thyrenae, drawn from the senior scholars of the great academies and the heads of the city's oldest patrician families, who have endowed chairs of philosophy, funded libraries and patronized research for generations. Power here follows intellectual prestige, so the most influential citizens are the most respected thinkers, and not necessarily the wealthiest.

## The Commissioners' Gate

A gate in the western wall is walled up and has never been reopened. It is the breach through which the Republic's commissioners entered in 312 BF ([[lore-heladthyrsiege|the Siege of Thyrenae]]), and the colleges keep the day as a day of silence. Antiquarians visit it, and nobody else uses the street. A proposal to reopen the gate for traffic has been met with the colleges' refusal.

## The Arcane Order

Thyrenae's relationship with the Arcane Order is the most complicated in Heliónis. The Arcane Order was born here, and the city's academies still preserve the non-arcane colleges of the original federation with real authority. Thyrenean philosophers may study arcane theory without joining the Arcane Order so long as they do not practice, and the boundary between theory and practice is a standing source of tension.
