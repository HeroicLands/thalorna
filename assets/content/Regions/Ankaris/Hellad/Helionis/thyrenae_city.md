## Overview

A scholar's first lesson in **Thyrenae** is where the credential comes from: a Thyrenean education is the most prestigious a scholar can claim. The city is the oldest of the three that lead the seven, home to 60,000 people and the birthplace of the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Panepistemium]], the scholarly federation that gave rise to the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]]. Its academies remain the most respected centers of learning in western Ankaris, and students travel from across [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]] to attend them. Its patron is [[lore-menervadty|Ménérva]], goddess of knowledge and wisdom.

## Character

The city is built to suit its students. Its architecture is graceful and restrained—white marble colonnades, shaded courtyards, and terraced gardens designed for contemplation and debate. The great **Library of Thyrenae** is said to hold more manuscripts than any collection outside [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Aû'Khelâthu]], and scholars travel across continents for the privilege of consulting its archives.

## Who Governs

A philosophical council governs Thyrenae, drawn from the senior scholars of the great academies and the heads of the city's oldest patrician families, who have endowed chairs of philosophy, funded libraries and patronized research for generations. Power here follows intellectual prestige, so the most influential citizens are the most respected thinkers, and not necessarily the wealthiest.

## The Ordo

Thyrenae's relationship with the Ordo is the most complicated in Heliónis. The Ordo was born here, and the city's academies still preserve the non-arcane colleges of the original federation with real authority. Thyrenean philosophers may study arcane theory without joining the Ordo so long as they do not practice, and the boundary between theory and practice is a standing source of tension.
