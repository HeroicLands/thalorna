Mirtelos has 20,000 people. It is a coastal city of the [[place-kostarosdst|Kostaros District]], held by the Helionite city-state of [[affiliation-kostaros2|Kostaros]], and its people are of the [[lore-humanflk|Human Folk]]. The land behind the quays is too thin to feed 20,000 people, so the grain ships that call here carry the city's bread, and the fishing fleet and the sea trade pay for it.

Two of its people are worth a visitor's time. The first is [[being-sthrnkhlthn|Sotharion Khalithon]], a lord of Helionite family who is known for his skill in war and for the loyalty of the vassals who follow him. The second is [[being-jdhrsbshrn|Jadharos Basiron]], a common seaman of thirty, reliable and well liked by his crewmates, who means to captain a ship of his own.
