## Overview

The students of **Theradon** argue in marble colonnades that climb terraced hillsides, and they argue with their teachers. This is a Helionite city of 50,000, known for philosophical inquiry and for the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]] chapterhouse it holds. Scholars from across the realms come to study with its philosophers, physicians and natural historians, and its libraries contain some of the oldest written texts in the western part of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]]. The [Vylarian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} took it in 330 BF, the first city of the war to fall, and the chapterhouse does not teach that year.

## Who Governs

The constitution is oligarchic. An assembly of the wealthiest and most educated citizens governs [[affiliation-theradon|]], and they believe themselves uniquely qualified to guide the city toward justice and virtue. The arrangement produces real intellectual achievement and real arrogance. The city's disdain for the "barbarous" lands beyond [[place-helionis|Helionis]] is barely concealed, and it is delivered politely, by people who have read more than their listener. The markets are orderly and regulated, and the temples of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian pantheon]] keep high standards of ritual propriety, but the center of the city's spiritual life is its academy, where the pursuit of wisdom is treated as a sacred duty.

## Character

Theradon is sublime and stifling in the same afternoon: art and architecture built to elevate the soul and sharpen the intellect, and a narrow certainty about what counts as truth and a proper life. Its philosophers have nevertheless taken the hard questions of justice, knowledge and the good life seriously, and their work is argued over throughout the civilized world.
