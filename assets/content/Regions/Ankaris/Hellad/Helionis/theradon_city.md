## Overview

The students of **Therádon** argue in marble colonnades that climb terraced hillsides above the Theradic Gulf, and they argue with their teachers. This is a Helionite city of 50,000, and it is small beside its sisters in everything but learning. Its significance comes from its role as a center of philosophical inquiry and from the Panepistemium chapterhouse it holds. Scholars from across the realms come to study with its philosophers, physicians and natural historians, and its libraries contain some of the oldest written texts in western Ankaris.

## Who Governs

The constitution is oligarchic. An assembly of the wealthiest and most educated citizens governs, and they believe themselves uniquely qualified to guide the city toward justice and virtue. The arrangement produces real intellectual achievement and real arrogance, and a visitor from beyond Heliónis should expect to be called a barbarian, politely, by someone who has read more than he has. The markets are orderly and regulated, and the temples of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían]] pantheon keep high standards of ritual propriety, but the center of the city's spiritual life is its academy, where the pursuit of wisdom is treated as a sacred duty.

## Character

Visitors remark that Therádon is sublime and stifling in the same afternoon: art and architecture built to elevate the soul and sharpen the intellect, and a narrow certainty about what counts as truth and a proper life. Its philosophers have nevertheless taken the hard questions of justice, knowledge and the good life seriously, and their work is argued over throughout the civilized world.
