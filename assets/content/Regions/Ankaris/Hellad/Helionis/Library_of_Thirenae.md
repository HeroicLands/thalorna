## Overview

The **Library of Thirenae** is the great library of [[place-thyrenae2|Thirenae]]. It is said to hold more manuscripts than any collection outside the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]], and scholars travel across continents for the privilege of consulting its archives. It stands among the colleges of a town built to suit its students, in white marble colonnades, shaded courtyards and terraced gardens meant for contemplation and debate.

A Thirenean education is the most prestigious a scholar can claim, and the Library is the collection behind it. Thirenae's academies draw students from across [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión]], most of them arriving by sea at [[place-pelagora2|Pelagora]].

## Inside

The collection is manuscripts, and what is said of it is its size: more of them than any collection outside the southern empire.

In Thirenae's agorin, before the library, stands Thirenae's keel-stone, the stone every Helionite city-state shows as proof of its founding ship. A stranger is taken to it and told what [[lore-keelburning|the Burning of the Keel]] tells: this is our first citizen, he burned his ship, and he brought you here.

## Who Is There

The library belongs to the common assembly of the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]] at Thirenae, and a head librarian whom the assembly chooses keeps it. It is open to the scholars of the colleges, and to others by letter. Scholars and students come to read. A philosophical council of senior scholars of the great academies and the heads of Thirenae's oldest families governs [[affiliation-thyrenae|Thirenae]] around the library, and power in Thirenae follows intellectual prestige, so the most influential citizens are the most respected thinkers.

A student from abroad who stays to study is a metic, taxed and liable to the levy, with no vote and no right to own land. A person can earn the most prestigious education in the west and never vote in the city-state that gave it.

The colleges of the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]] stand around the library, among them the [[affiliation-cllgofethcs|College of Ethics]], which gives foreign students its guide to Helionis in their first week.

## The Present {#present}

Thirenae's academies keep the non-arcane colleges of the original federation with real authority, and the library stands among them. A Thirenean philosopher may study arcane theory without joining the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]], as long as he does not practice. The line between theory and practice is where the tension lives, and a clerk of an Arcane Order chapter in [[place-helionis|Helionis]] puts the Order's side of it in two sentences: read what you like and argue what you like, and our interest begins the day you do it.

A scholar who comes to consult the archives comes into a town where that line is a standing source of tension.

## History {#history}

The library stood through the siege of Thirenae. In 312 BF the city fell on terms its arsir negotiated: the walls were breached at one gate only, the libraries and the colleges were left untouched, and the Republic's commissioners entered through the breach the same month ([[lore-heladthyrsiege|the Siege of Thirenae]]). The library stands whole beside the walled-up [[place-cmmssnrsgate|Commissioners' Gate]], and the library and the colleges around it are the reason Thirenae still teaches.

What the commissioners carried out of the college's rooms that month is unresolved: no inventory the city kept survives to name it.
