## Overview

The **Academy of the Silver Veil** is the most distinctive institution of [[place-kalydria2|Kalidria]]. It trains the hetairin: educated companions schooled in music, philosophy, rhetoric and the social arts, who leave to serve as companions and advisors in courts and salons across [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión]]. The Academy stands for the Helionite belief that education, social grace and intellectual accomplishment cannot be separated.

The patron families of Kalidria founded it about 260 BF, half a century after the conquest, to teach the households of the Republic's governors and senators. Its first rule is the sentence every graduate carries: the city could not keep its freedom by arms, and would keep it by conversation ([[lore-heladsilverveil|The Founding of the Silver Veil]]). The rule names no opponent, and a graduate never calls herself an instrument.

Helionites are impatient with one confusion above all. A graduate is a hetairin, and a visitor who calls her a courtesan has ended the conversation.

## What It Controls

The Academy controls the license. A girl enters at twelve, spends eight years on music, dance, poetry, rhetoric, etiquette, herblore, law and the art of reading people, with politics and philosophy beside them, and comes out at twenty a licensed companion. A junior, asked what the first year teaches, answers: "To remember what was said at dinner, and by whom."

It controls the web. Its graduates keep in touch, and the network they form has no office and no master. It is an unofficial intelligence service, and it is the reason [[affiliation-kalydria|Kalidria]], small in military strength, carries weight in rooms where armies do not reach.

It controls a body of sayings, [[lore-veillessons|the Lessons of the Silver Veil]], one year's lessons for each of the eight years. The Academy holds that its founder said them, and the schools of [[affiliation-thyrenae|Thirenae]] hold that she said the first rule and nothing else. Copies of the first seven years circulate in every city of Midhalión where a graduate has served. The Academy neither owns nor disowns them, and it has never said whether a copy that claims the eighth year's lessons is a forgery or a joke.

## Ranks

A **Student** is a girl in her eight years. At the end of each year she says all the Lessons she has learned, from the first year's to the year she is finishing, and she goes on to the next year only when she has said them without a fault. A **Hetairin** holds the license. A **Dropout** is a girl who left before it, and the Velvet Hand recruits from her as readily as from a graduate.

The first graduates were placed as companions in the households of the Republic's governors and senators, and the Academy's own account of its founding leaves that out.

## Masters

The Academy's founder is [Lékhisira Kálides]{.name data-type="being/npc"}. A living headmistress leads it, and its teachers hear each year's Lessons. Whether the founder wrote the first rule herself or only signed it is not settled.

A graduate, [Sothamira Thelkhides]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, came to the Games as the companion of a Kalidrian herald, spoke at the heralds' altar after the War of the Harbors, and proposed that the cities write down what they had done in the war and keep to it ([[lore-heladtruce|the Truce of the Games]]). The Academy has kept the reputation of a broker among the cities since.

## The Present Quarrel {#present}

The Academy's web belongs to no office, and that is both its strength and its trouble. A hetairin carries Kalidrian opinion to a governor's table, and nobody answers for what she carries. [[affiliation-velvethand|The Velvet Hand]] recruits from the Academy's graduates and its dropouts alike, on the ground that the skills that make an excellent hetairin make an excellent spy. Whether the Hand began with the Academy's graduates is disputed: the households that employ them say it did, and the Hand says it was born from the Helionite mystery cults and the Vilérian court.

The Academy protects its graduates, and the two accounts of what it is for do not agree. Kalidria says that the city did not lose the war, that it declined to fight a second one and sent its daughters to win it at dinner. The syndicate says the Academy taught the art of being listened to, and that what its graduates heard they kept.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The eighth year.** A seller in a Midhalión market offers a copy of the Lessons that claims the eighth year, which no copy has ever honestly held. The Academy has never said whether such a copy is a forgery or a joke, and a graduate who sees the copy wants to know who is selling it.
- **The recruiter.** A girl who left the Academy before her license is courted by the Velvet Hand, and a party is asked to find out what she has already said and to whom.
