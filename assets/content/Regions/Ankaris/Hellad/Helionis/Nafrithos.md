## Overview {#overview}

**Nafrithos** is built over its own mine. About 3,200 people live inside a wall that closes a narrow upland valley, and the galleries run beneath the houses, so that the town's cellars open into old workings and the oldest streets are propped on timber. The ore is lead, rich enough in silver to be worth the labor, and everything the town does serves it: the stamping mills, the roasting hearths, the cupellation sheds where the lead is burned off the silver, and the long sheds where the lead is cast into pigs.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Lead is the bulk and silver is the prize. The pigs of lead go down to [[place-theradon2|Theradon]] and the ports for roofing, pipes and shot, and the silver goes in small, heavily guarded loads to the city-state's treasury and its moneychangers. Nafrithos is the richest of the hinterland towns per head, and its people eat better than the miners of any other, because the town can pay for the grain it cannot grow.

The land does not help. The valley floor is a few narrow fields, and the villages in the side valleys within half a day of the town raise barley, goats and charcoal, burning wood for the hearths under palisades of split logs. The grain that matters comes by caravan from the steppe margin and up the foothill roads from the lowlands, bought with silver coin that circles back to the mint with each season's trade.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The assembly of [[affiliation-theradon|Theradon]] holds Nafrithos and keeps the silver closely. A wall-warden commands the garrison and answers for every weight of metal that leaves the gate, and a treasurer sent from Theradon counts each load before it goes. The mine-masters own the galleries by shares and manage the diggers.

## The Present {#present}

The silver loads are the mark every marauder in the mountains wants, and the escorts are strong and slow. Lead poisoning is a fact of the town's life, and the people who have worked the hearths longest are the least steady, a thing the mine-masters do not discuss.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The old working.** A cellar collapses into a gallery nobody has mapped, and what is found there is far older than the town.
- **The treasurer's count.** The load that reaches Theradon is lighter than the load the treasurer counted at the gate, and the treasurer is the one man who says the road is clean.
