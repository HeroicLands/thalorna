A shepherd above any of the older Helionite cities can point to a tower on the next ridge that no one repairs and no one demolishes. The **Seats of the Mage-Warlords** are those towers and the workshops walled into the hillsides beneath them, the places where the mage-warlords of the war with the Republic did the work their cities could not control. They stand in the hills of several cities, and each is a ruin that the city that owns it has agreed to leave alone.

## What Stands

Each seat is a tower with a walled workshop behind it, cut into rock or built against it, with a cistern and a yard. Roofs are gone, floors are fallen in, and the glass in the upper rooms went generations ago. The walls hold, and the cellars are mostly intact. The seats are the only part of the warlords' work that a person can still touch.

**Therádon's seat** is a walled workshop on the terraces above the gulf. The Republic stripped it in 330 BF, and its master died when the city fell. The racks along the north wall are empty, and the numbers painted above them are still legible.

## Who Goes

- **Scholars**, [[being-blnhdrks|Bûlêlon Hâdrîkês]] among them, who come for the walls, the marks and the shape of the rooms.
- **Ordo surveyors**, who inspect a seat and write it into a register.
- **Treasure-seekers**, whom the Ordo arrests when it finds them, and whom the cities' own magistrates mostly ignore.

## Hooks

- **The bricked cellar.** At one seat the cellar was bricked up from the inside, and the mortar is the same age as the walls.
- **The numbers.** A Thyrenaean factor has begun paying for any rubbing or copy of the numbers painted above the empty racks of Therádon's seat. He will say only that his principal collects.
