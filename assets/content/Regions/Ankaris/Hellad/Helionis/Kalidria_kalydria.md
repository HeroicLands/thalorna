## Overview {#overview}

Never call a graduate of the [[affiliation-slvrvlacdmy|Academy of the Silver Veil]] a courtesan in Kalidria. She is a hetairin, an educated companion trained in music, philosophy, rhetoric and the social arts, and Helionites are impatient with the confusion. The Academy is the most distinctive institution of a city-state that is renowned across [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión]] for its artists, playwrights and musicians. Where [[affiliation-thyrenae|Thirenae]] pursues truth and [[affiliation-pelagora|Pelagora]] pursues power, Kalidria pursues beauty. About 180,000 people live under its patron families, and 5,000 of them in the town of [[place-kalydria2|Kalidria]].

## Who Decides

Kalidria's government is its patron families, and they compete by spending: each wants to fund the most lavish production, the most magnificent temple and the most celebrated festival. A Kalidrian chorus-master, drilling a chorus in the weeks before a festival, says it as a working rule: "The patron pays for the masks and the dinner afterward, and then he sits in the front row to find out what his money bought." The people who hold power in the city are the people who pay for what the city comes to see.

The families rule inside the forms every Helionite city keeps: a sovereign assembly, a council chosen by lot, audited magistrates, an elected strátimor and a year-naming arsir.

## The Academy of the Silver Veil

The Academy represents the Helionite belief that education, social grace and intellectual accomplishment cannot be separated. Its graduates occupy a distinct position in courts and salons across Midhalión: socially elevated, intellectually formidable and well informed. A junior of the Academy, asked what the first year teaches, says: "To remember what was said at dinner, and by whom." The graduates keep in touch, and the network they form is an unofficial web of intelligence. It makes Kalidria far more influential than its military strength would suggest.

The patron families of Kalidria founded the Academy about 260 BF, half a century after the conquest ([[lore-heladsilverveil|The Founding of the Silver Veil]]).

## Art and Influence

Kalidria's theater festivals draw audiences from across the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]]. Playwrights premiere their tragedies and comedies here, and a Kalidrian prize is the highest honor a dramatist can receive. Kalidrian sculptors and painters are sought by wealthy patrons across Midhalión, and Kalidrian training is considered the finest available.

Kalidria's strength lies in influence and not in arms. The patron families use art and social connection as instruments of soft power, and Kalidrian taste shapes fashion, entertainment and courtly manners across the western part of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]].

## Commerce and Currency {#commerce}

Kalidria uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian currency]] (Solden / Ardin / Ogdel) as its standard currency, with full access to the paper-script system through [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]] moneylenders chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]]. [[affiliation-tolvan|The Tolvan]] still claims the cities' taxes on paper, and the cities do not pay them. See [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian Currency]] for the full system.
