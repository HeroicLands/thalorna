## Overview {#overview}

**Sufanos** stands on a mountainside bleached white and yellow, where the rock steams after rain and the air smells of burnt stone. About 3,400 people live behind its wall, in a town whose roofs are crusted pale and whose wells run warm. Its two products have nothing in common but the hillside: alum, the white salt dyers cannot work without, and sulfur, the yellow stone that burns with a blue flame and a choking smoke.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The alum is leached from the pale rock in great stone vats and boiled down in shallow pans, and the white crystals are packed in barrels for the dyers of the city-states and the cloth-towns of the coast, who use it to fix color in wool. The sulfur is dug from the vents, melted off the earth in clay pots and cast into cakes. Healers, bleachers and fire-makers buy it, and so does anyone who wants a thing to burn that water will not put out.

The mountainside is poisoned ground, and no crop will take in it. Villages of terrace-farmers live in the cleaner side valleys a few hours from the gate, raising barley and pulses, and they sell their harvest to the town in the evening before they shut their palisades. The town's grain mostly comes from the foothill lowlands over the lower road, and a share of it arrives from the steppe margin by caravan. A cake of sulfur and a barrel of alum go down the road and a sack of grain comes up, and the town watches the rate of exchange.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

Sufanos is held by [[affiliation-korinthea|Korinthea]], whose assembly sends a wall-warden, and a judge of the [[place-tmplrdvn|Temple of Árdavon]] rides out each year to hear the town's disputes, since the alum guild and the sulfur-cutters do not agree about much. The garrison is a levy supplemented with hired guards.

## The Present {#present}

[[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]] has a following in Sufanos, drawn to a town whose ground burns and whose smoke is yellow. Its followers keep a shrine near the vents, and the sulfur-cutters who work the hottest ones tend to attend it. The garrison treats the shrine as a nuisance and no more. The vents have also been spreading, and some of the old workings are too hot to enter.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The new vent.** A vent opens beneath a street in the lower town, and the people who live above it are told to move and have nowhere to go.
- **The steppe buyer.** A caravan from the steppe buys most of a season's sulfur at twice the price and will not say what it is for.
