## Overview

Say you reach **Kalidria** in the weeks of a theater festival. The inns are full, a crowd is outside every playhouse an hour before the performance, and the talk in the street is about which family paid for which play. Where [[place-thyrenae2|Thirenae]] pursues truth and [[place-pelagora2|Pelagora]] pursues power, this city of 45,000 pursues beauty, and it is the cultural jewel of [[place-helionis|Helionis]]. Its patron is [[lore-zunelasdty|Zunelas]], goddess of dreams and visions.

## Who Governs

An oligarchy of wealthy patron families governs [[affiliation-kalydria|Kalidria]], and the families compete by spending. Each one wants to fund the most lavish production, the most magnificent temple and the most celebrated festival, so the people who hold power in the city are the same people who pay for what the city comes to see. Kalidrian theater, sculpture, poetry and music set the standard by which most of the western part of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]] measures its own. Playwrights premiere their tragedies and comedies here, and a Kalidrian prize is the highest honor a dramatist can receive.

## The Academy of the Silver Veil

The city's most distinctive institution is the **[Academy of the Silver Veil]{.name data-type="affiliation"}**, which trains the hetairin: educated companions schooled in music, philosophy, rhetoric and the social arts. Its graduates leave to serve as companions and advisors in courts and salons across [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]], socially elevated and intellectually formidable, and they stay in touch. That unofficial web of well-informed women is why Kalidria, small in military strength, matters in rooms where armies do not.

## Making a Living

Kalidria sells what it makes. Sculptors and painters trained here are sought by wealthy patrons across Mídhalión, and artists from elsewhere come to be trained. The city uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vylarian currency]], with full access to the banking houses of [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]].
