## Overview {#overview}

**Ussarea** stands on the shore of one of the inland seas, where there is nothing much to see but gray water, reed beds and flats white with salt. About 2,300 people live inside a square wall of mud brick and stone with a gate at each side, and most of the ground within it is caravanserai: courtyards for beasts, long rooms for sleepers and warehouses that open onto both. The town exists because the road from the mountains to the steppe passes along this shore and there is no other place on it to stop.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The sea gives almost nothing. The water is bitter, the fish are few and small, and the shore yields reeds, salt crust and little else. Ussarea sells what the caravans need: fresh water from deep wells, fodder, stabling, lodging, repairs and a guarded storehouse. It takes a toll on every loaded animal, and salt from the flats goes out with the trains as a trade good.

A stream that feeds the sea at its western end waters the one stretch of green in the country. Villages crowd along it within an hour's walk of the town, growing barley, onions and fodder in irrigated plots behind palisades and ditches, and the town buys their harvest before it buys any other. Most of its bread, though, arrives on the caravans themselves: wheat from [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia]] and the foothill lowlands, hauled in to be sold on at a profit to the mining towns in the mountains. Cloth, oil, wine and finished metal come in from the west, and wool, hides and horses from the steppe.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

[[affiliation-thyrenae|Thirenae]] holds Ussarea, and its wall-warden commands a small garrison of levy spearmen and hired riders. A council of caravan-masters settles disputes between travelers, sets the lodging prices and keeps the wells. The warden has the gates and the council has the town.

## The Present {#present}

Mounted bands from the steppe test the shore road each spring, and the garrison's riders are fewer than the town would like. A caravan that arrives late is counted as lost on the third day, and the council has begun to hold the gates against any train that cannot show where it has been.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The unscheduled train.** A caravan arrives from the north-east with no trade goods the warden can name, a column of armed guards and a request for the whole of a caravanserai.
- **The salt claim.** Two houses of caravan-masters both claim the same stretch of flats, and the claim has begun to be argued with spears.
