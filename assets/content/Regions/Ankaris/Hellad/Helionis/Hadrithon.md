## Overview {#overview}

**Hadrithon** is cut into a cliff of white marble, about 2,900 people in a town whose lower streets are old quarry floors and whose upper ones are the ledges the cutters have not yet reached. The wall closes the one approach, a shelf above the quarry road, and the quarries themselves lie outside it, guarded by their own towers. The marble is the finest in Helionis, and the temples and courts of the city-states are built of it.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The cutters split blocks from the cliff with wedges and saws, dress them on the floor and lower them on timber slips to the sledges. Everything the town earns leaves on the one paved road, which runs down from the quarry gate to the lowlands: the blocks, the columns, the carved lintels and the slabs for floors. Stonemasons' shops make up much of the lower town, and some of its carvers are hired by the temples of [[place-korinthea2|Korinthea]] and sent for years at a time.

The cliff grows nothing. A few villages stand on a high meadow behind the town, within an hour of the upper gate, where the people graze oxen for the sledges and raise barley behind a stone wall, and the town depends on them for the draft beasts it cannot keep alive anywhere else. The bread comes from the lowlands by the same paved road that carries the stone away. Every ox-train that goes down with marble comes back up with sacks, and the road is the town's only supply line.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

Hadrithon belongs to [[affiliation-korinthea|Korinthea]]. A wall-warden commands the garrison, and the temple of [[affiliation-ardavon|Árdavon]] sends a steward who keeps the quarry's records and judges the claims of the cutters' guild, since the stone is used for the temples. The quarry-masters hold the cliff in shares and hire the crews.

## The Present {#present}

Marauders cannot carry marble, and they leave the blocks alone. They do take the oxen, the grain going up and the cutters' wages, and the paved road is a long, exposed ribbon that the garrison cannot hold end to end. The town's walls are strong, and its road is the weak point.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The flawed block.** A column ordered for a temple at Korinthea cracks on the sledge, and the cutter who dressed it swears the stone was sound.
- **The cut road.** A section of the paved road is torn up in the night, and the ox-trains stand idle above it while the town's grain runs low.
