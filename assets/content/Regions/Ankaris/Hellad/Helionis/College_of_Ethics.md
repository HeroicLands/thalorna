## Overview

The **College of Ethics**, also called moral philosophy, is the Epistémion's college for moral values, principles and the question of how one ought to act. It stands at [[place-thyrenae2|Thirenae]], the Epistémion's birthplace, among the colleges that the terms of 312 BF left whole. The ethical colleges of [[place-helionis|Helionis]] produced some of the most influential moral frameworks in western Ankaris, several of which still underpin Vilérian law.

At the founding of the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]], around 400 BF, ethics stood level with every other college, the arcane among them. It is the college that supplies the moral vocabulary the Vilérian Empire uses to justify its policies.

## What It Controls

The college controls a first week and a first course. It gives foreign students, in the week they arrive, the guide to Helionis that begins with the college's admission that it would rather they made their first mistakes on paper. It opens its first course by reading the assembly of the gods from [[lore-terracemsrs|the Measures of the Terraces]] aloud and asking the students what is wrong with it, since the schools have argued with that half of the poem for as long as there have been schools.

On the first night of the Silent Passage, when the temples of [[affiliation-selmoros|the Faith of Sélmoros]] are shut to the living, the college reads [[lore-silentdinner|the Dinner of the Silent Passage]] with its students. The dialogue sets the poets' claim that glory is the second life against a story of the dead speaking their lives before the Silent Judge ([[lore-silentpassage|the Festival of the Silent Passage]]).

## Ranks

One the college has dismissed or refused is **Sent Down** and has no seat in its courses. A **Student** reads and argues. A **Lecturer** teaches the courses. The head of the college is a **Senior Scholar**, and sits with the heads of the other colleges in the Epistémion's common assembly.

## Masters

A lecturer of the Epistémion at Thirenae puts the matter to new students on their first morning: everything the Order teaches about the six schools was argued out in rooms like this one, by people who also argued about justice. The Order kept the first argument and dropped the second, and the students are there for both.

A lecturer of the college, asked what the Epistémion is, takes the questioner to the gate of a chapterhouse and points at the sign: the seal of the oldest scholarly body in the west. Now ask who pays for the roof, the lecturer says, who appoints the head of the house, and whose students are quietly counted for a talent the Order wants. The college refused its seal to the charter that made the Order, and the seal is on the door anyway.

## The Present Quarrel {#present}

The colleges of Ethics, Political Philosophy and Law keep more independence than the Epistémion's other colleges, largely because their work is useful to power. The Vilérian legal system draws on the [College of Law]{.name data-type="affiliation"}'s scholarship and this college supplies the empire's moral vocabulary, and both work in the shadow of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]].

The Epistémion began as a federation of all inquiry in which a scholar of ethics stands equal to a scholar of magic, and the Helionite academies are the last place in western Ankaris where that first vision still has force. The Order controls the whole institution, and the college works inside it. The colleges of Thirenae keep the day of the extraction of the College of Arcane Philosophy as a day of silence, and they have refused to see the [[place-cmmssnrsgate|Commissioners' Gate]] reopened.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The manuscript.** A student of the college has found a manuscript that proves an Order theorem was argued first in a different college, by someone who also argued about justice, and she needs witnesses before it is learned in an Order chapterhouse.
- **The count.** A lecturer says students are quietly counted for a talent the Order wants. A party is asked to find who keeps the count.
