## Overview {#overview}

The [Council of Korinthea]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} consults the [[place-tmplrdvn|Temple of Árdavon]] on matters of law and civic honor before it acts. The council is the visible government of a city-state of about 160,000 people, held by an oligarchy of its wealthy patron families, and the temple's judges sit beside the families who rule it, in the service of a god of order and justice. [[place-korinthea2|Korinthea]], with 5,000 of the people, is the seat.

## Who Decides

Two bodies share the work. The council governs inside the forms every Helionite city-state keeps: a sovereign assembly, a council chosen by lot, audited magistrates, an elected strátimor and an arsir who names the year. The temple judges. A priest-judge of [[affiliation-ardavon|Árdavon]] explains the division in a sentence: "The council writes the rules, and the temple says what they mean when two men disagree about them."

The High Judge of the Temple, [[being-dumarsrfrs|Dumares Rafiros]], holds a regional rank beneath the Volsarnor, the one High Judge of the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]], who sits in the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]]. He is consulted by the council on law and civic honor, and his judgment on a dispute is final in most cases. The Patriarchate of the temple is an ancient honorary title that no one now holds, and he answers to it nominally, as to a tradition. Being a judge of Árdavon is a civic office in Korinthea, in line with the Helionite habit of holding priesthood and magistracy together. The temple has kept the city-state's law since 327 BF, when its assembly lost its standing after [[lore-heladturnedwrk|the Turned Working]] (the battle on [[place-sourplain|the Sour Plain]]) and the first High Judge of the temple at Korinthea, [Nomason]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, took the law into the temple's keeping; the present High Judge sits in an unbroken line from him.

## Standing and Relations

Standing in Korinthea is the common Helionite ladder, from the citizen by descent down to the metic who pays tax without a vote. Korinthea is aligned with the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian pantheon]], and it shares the [[skill-helonclng|Helonik tongue]], its gods and its festivals with the other six city-states.

## Current Situation {#present}

Korinthea holds two of the twelve hinterland towns, [[place-hadrithon|Hadrithon]], whose marble builds the temple, and [[place-sufanos|Sufanos]], whose alum and sulfur the dyers and fire-makers buy. Marauders take the oxen and grain on Hadrithon's road, and a following of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] keeps a shrine at Sufanos. The council and the temple's High Judge divide the work of rule between them.

## History {#history}

In 327 BF, in the eighth year of the [Vilérian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}'s war, a Korinthean mage-warlord broke a legion on the western road with the war's greatest working, and when the [Dragon Riders]{.name data-type="affiliation"} came over the field the working turned back through the Korinthean citizen levy drawn up behind him and killed it in its ranks ([[lore-heladturnedwrk|the Turned Working]]). The plain is [[place-sourplain|the Sour Plain]]. Korinthea opened its gates to the legion without a siege, its assembly lost its standing, and [Nomason]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the first High Judge of the temple at Korinthea, took the city-state's law into the temple's keeping, where it stays.
