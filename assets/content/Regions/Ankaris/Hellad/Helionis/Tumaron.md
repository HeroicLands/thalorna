## Overview {#overview}

**Tumaron** stands where a gorge opens onto a stony fan, and its wall is the only thing between the gorge and the plain. About 2,700 people live there. The tin is not in the rock but in the gravels of the stream that comes down the gorge, a dark, heavy sand that the washers shovel into sluices and pan out grain by grain. It is among the rarest metals in the west, and the town knows what that makes it.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The washing works from the thaw to the first frost. Crews work the stream bed under guard, the concentrate is smelted at furnaces behind the wall, and the tin is cast into small ingots that a single mule can carry a long way. Most go down to the bronzefounders of the coast, some go over the border passes to the smelters of Bizaria, and the buyers pay in silver, in cloth or in grain by the sack.

The gorge floor grows nothing, and the fan below the town carries only a few fields. Villages along the fan's lower edge, close under the wall, farm what they can and keep dogs and watch-towers against raiders, and the town's bread comes up from the foothill lowlands on carts hired for the season. Sacks of Tumaron's tin go out and sacks of barley come in, one for one, and the washers are paid in grain as often as in coin.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

Tumaron belongs to [[affiliation-theradon|Theradon]], whose assembly sends a wall-warden who commands a small garrison and holds the keys of the tin-house, where each ingot is weighed, stamped and recorded. A council of the washing crews' foremen speaks for the workers and settles the claims on the stream.

## The Present {#present}

Every season the hill bands try the gorge road, because tin travels light and sells everywhere. The garrison keeps the stream guarded from the first washing to the last, and the washers go to the stream in groups with spears.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The upstream claim.** A crew has staked a stretch of the stream above the town's claims, and the foreman who staked it cannot say where he found the money to hire so many.
- **The stamped ingot.** A tin ingot with Tumaron's stamp turns up in a market where none should have gone, carrying a mark the tin-house does not cut.
