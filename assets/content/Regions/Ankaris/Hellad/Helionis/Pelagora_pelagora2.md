## Overview {#overview}

**Pelagora** settles things by shouting, and the shouting decides a fleet. The town of 6,500 is the naval power of [[place-helionis|Helionis]], a democracy in which an assembly of free citizens votes on war, trade and law, and every citizen is expected to serve in the fleet or the militia. Its patron is [[lore-ardavondty|Árdavon]], god of order and justice, and the assembly is the loudest place in the town: debates turn into shouting matches, demagogues rise and fall with the public mood, and the town lives with it because a man who owns a share of the town fights for it.

## The Harbor

The town is built around its great harbor, a natural bay sheltered by rocky headlands, one of the finest anchorages in the central sea. The waterfront is a forest of masts—warships, merchant vessels, and fishing boats packed together in organized chaos. [[affiliation-pelagora|Pelagora]] commands the most important harbors in Helionis and controls the sea lanes of the eastern [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]]. Its shipwrights build the fastest galleys on that sea, and its fleet is the largest in the region.

## Making a Living

Pelagora is more commercial than scholarly [[place-thyrenae2|Thirenae]]. Its merchants are wealthy and influential, and its markets are stocked with goods from across the sea. The fleet earns the town its standing as well as its living: its admirals have defended Helionite waters against [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Taruzelian]] incursions, Vilérian ambitions and the occasional pirate fleet.

The hills behind the harbor cannot feed the town, so its bread is bought overseas and from the coastal villages, and the fleet carries it in. Pelagoran ships also take grain up the coast toward the passes that feed the mountain towns, and carry ore from the mountains across the sea.

## The Treaty

The bronze copy of [[lore-heladpelagtrty|the Pelagoran Treaty]] stands in the agorin. Thirenaean visitors spit beside it, and a Thirenaean student has chiseled one line off it.

## Neighbors

Thirenae needs Pelagora's fleet, and Pelagora needs Thirenae's diplomatic connections, so the two are rivals and partners at once.

## The Present {#present}

Pelagora holds none of the twelve hinterland towns, which five of the seven city-states hold between them, and its stake in the mountains is the carrying trade. The fleet brings the grain that the lowland roads take up the passes to the mining towns, and it carries their ore across the sea. The assembly votes each season's voyages. The treaty's bronze copy in the agorin still has a line chiseled off it, and Thirenaean visitors still spit beside it, yet Thirenae needs Pelagora's fleet and Pelagora needs Thirenae's diplomatic connections, so the two stay rivals and partners at once.
