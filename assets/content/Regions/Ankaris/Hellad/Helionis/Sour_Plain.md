The road carters take into Heliónis from the west bends a day's walk out of its way for a plain it passes at a distance, and the carters will tell a stranger why before he asks. The **Sour Plain** is a blood-field of the withering kind, the rarest of the five, and the worst in the region. A legion and the citizen levy of [[affiliation-korinthea|Korinthea]] died on it in one afternoon in 327 BF ([[lore-heladturnedwrk|the Turned Working]]), and no herald could cross the ground to take them home.

## The Ground

The working that killed the dead spent its own poison within a generation. The withering it left did not. The soil is sour, wells bitter, grass grey, and beasts that graze the edge sicken. The dead ground spreads, a few yards in a bad decade, and this is the plainest evidence that the field is alive. A ring of boundary stones set by Korinthea after the war marks its edge, and the ring has been reset outward three times.

## What Crosses

The dead of both armies stand on the plain on the anniversary of the battle, worn down to the posture of men waiting for an order that never came, ranks of them in the grey grass. Between anniversaries the field is thin: predators gather where unreceived dead are, and the carters who go round by daylight do not go near it at night. Nothing living crosses into the spirit realm there, and a traveler who walks in comes back out, or does not come back.

## The Stones and the Judge

Korinthea's [[place-korinthea2|Temple of Árdavon]] sends a judge each year to walk the ring and record where the stones stand. It is a custom and not an office. The settlement rite of [[lore-selmorosdty|Sélmoros]] has never been tried on the plain, because in Heliónis a temple is civic property and the rite needs an assembly's vote, and no Korinthean assembly has voted to say aloud what its city did here.

## Who Goes

- **Carters**, who go round it by daylight and give the plain a wide berth.
- **Scholars**, [[being-blnhdrks|Bûlêlon Hâdrîkês]] of Therádon among them, who come for the ground.
- **Ordo surveyors**, whom Korinthea refuses at the ring.
- **The Black Flame**, which works fields anywhere, and for which a withering that spreads is the work already half done.

## Hooks

- **The moved stone.** The boundary stone nearest the road has moved overnight, by hand, and a farm on the wrong side of it is for sale cheap.
- **The motion.** A Korinthean assembly has, for the first time, a motion before it to pay for the settlement rite, and the families who oppose it have a reason they will not give.
