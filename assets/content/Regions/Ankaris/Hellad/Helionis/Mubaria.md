## Overview {#overview}

**Mubaria** is the largest caravan town of the Helionite hinterland, about 3,000 people on the shore of an inland sea, and it has no reason to exist except the roads. The sea beside it is salt and shallow, the shore is bare, and the nearest hills are a day away. Two roads meet here: the one that comes down from [[place-byzariargn|Bizaria]] through the passes, and the one that comes in from the steppe. The town is a gate, a market and an inn laid across their meeting.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Mubaria makes almost nothing. It sells passage: wells, fodder, stabling, lodging, guards for hire, brokers who speak the steppe tongues and the Bizarian bazaar speech, and a market square where wool, horses, hides, salt and Bizarian cloth change hands for oil, wine, finished metal and coin. A toll on every beast that enters pays the wall.

Its bread is bought. A belt of villages lies along the two streams that feed the sea, within a few hours of the gate, growing barley, onions and fodder behind ditches and palisades, and they supply the town's daily needs. The grain for the mining towns passes through here as a trade of its own, bought from the steppe margin and from the north and sold on by the mule-load to the towns in the mountains.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

Mubaria is held by [[affiliation-theradon|Theradon]]. The wall-warden commands the garrison, a company of levy spearmen with hired riders, and a council of the caravan-masters keeps the market, the wells and the quarrels. Disputes between a Helionite merchant and a steppe trader are heard before the council, and both sides have learned to bring witnesses.

## The Present {#present}

The steppe bands that raid the shore road also trade at the market, sometimes the same men in the same season, and the garrison cannot always say which they are. Mubaria is the first stop on the road for anyone leaving Bizaria for Helionis, and the town watches who comes through.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The sealed wagons.** A train from Bizaria arrives with a dozen sealed wagons and a master who will say only that they are bound for Theradon.
- **The market truce.** A steppe trader is killed in the square, and the clan he belonged to demands the council's answer before the next market day.
