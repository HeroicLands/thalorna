## Overview

A long scar runs down the western side of [[place-thyrenae2|Thirenae]]'s walls, where a gate was walled up and never reopened. It is the **Commissioners' Gate**, and it marks where the last free city of [[place-helionis|Helionis]] let the [Vilérian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} in.

Antiquarians visit it, and nobody else uses the street.

## Inside

The gate is a breach in the western wall that masonry fills, and the street that leads to it carries no traffic. The wall shows the long scar of the closing.

## Who Is There

Antiquarians go to see it. The colleges of Thirenae keep the day the gate came down as a day of silence.

## The Present {#present}

The gate is shut because the colleges hold it shut. The day it came down is kept as a day of silence, and a proposal to reopen it for traffic has been met with the colleges' refusal.

The commissioners entered through the gate to take the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|College of Arcane Philosophy]] out of the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]], and what they carried out of the college's rooms is unresolved. The city's own account is that it opened one gate so that the rest of the city, and every book in it, could stand.

## History {#history}

By 313 BF Thirenae was the last city of Helionis that had not yielded. The legions drew their lines on the landward side, Pelagoran ships held the coast, and the city held out through the winter.

The arsir of the year, [Sothanos Kosamides]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, sent to the legate and came back with terms: the walls would be breached at one gate and no other, the libraries and the colleges would stand untouched, and the Republic's commissioners would enter through the breach the same month. The council voted for them, and in 312 BF the gate came down and the commissioners went in. They took the College of Arcane Philosophy out of the Epistémion that month, and [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]], the Senate, placed it under [[affiliation-kandral|the Arcane Magistracy]] ([[lore-heladthyrsiege|the Siege of Thirenae]]). The gate was walled up in the days after and never reopened.

Thirenaeans say the arsir did not surrender the city: he bought it. The Republic says the last city fell on terms and that the terms were honored to the letter, and it does not say what the commissioners took.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The reopening.** A proposal to open the gate for traffic has met the colleges' refusal, and a party that wants the street opened has to argue the colleges out of it.
- **What went through.** No inventory the city kept survives to name what the commissioners carried out of the college's rooms. An antiquarian who visits the gate asks a party to look for a record in the colleges' keeping.
