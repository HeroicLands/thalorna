## Overview {#overview}

**Jalimea** stands among hills banded red, yellow, green and blue, where the weather has laid every mineral of the mountain in stripes along the slopes. About 2,200 people live inside its wall, and the stonework of the town carries the colors of its trade: the miners' houses are rust-red with ochre dust, and the grinding-sheds near the gate are stained green and blue to the roof. It is the only town in the hinterland that mines for beauty.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The galleries follow veins of red and yellow ochre, green malachite, deep-blue azurite and the pale sulfides that make the yellows and oranges. The miners cut the ore, and a large part of the town's people grind and wash it in stone basins until it sorts into pigment, which is packed in clay jars sealed with wax. [[place-kalydria2|Kalidria]]'s painters, the scene-painters of its theaters and its dyers buy nearly all of it, and the [[affiliation-slvrvlacdmy|Academy of the Silver Veil]] has its own supply contract. Pigment is light and valuable, and a single mule carries a season's worth.

The hills grow little but thyme and thorn. Goat-herding villages within an hour of the gate keep the town in cheese and meat, and their huts are sprayed with pigment dust until their roofs are the color of the nearest vein. The grain comes up from the foothill lowlands on the lower road, bought with the paint, and the town counts the number of jars it needs to send to buy a winter's bread.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

Jalimea answers to [[affiliation-kalydria|Kalidria]], whose patron families send a wall-warden, usually a younger son with a taste for paint, and the garrison is a levy of hill-born spearmen. The grinders' guild controls the washing basins and the grades, and a pigment sold under Jalimea's seal is worth more for the guarantee.

## The Present {#present}

The town is a quiet one, and its walls are old and not high. Marauders prefer the richer towns, and Jalimea's greatest danger is the road, where a train of jars is worth a raid even if the jars hold only colored dust.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The unknown blue.** A grinder washes a blue unlike any the Academy's painters have seen, and she will sell it only to the buyer who can name what it is made from.
- **The tainted green.** A batch of malachite green sold to a theater in Kalidria has sickened the painters who used it.
