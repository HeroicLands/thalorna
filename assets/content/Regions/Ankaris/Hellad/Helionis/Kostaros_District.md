## Overview {#overview}

The Kostaros District is the stretch of Helionite coast that the city-state of [[affiliation-kostaros2|Kostaros]] governs, and its one named city besides Kostaros itself is [[place-myrtillos|Mirtelos]], where about 20,000 people live. Like the rest of the Helionite coast it is warm, dry and bright, its hills cut into terraces and its water open to the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]]. The district is the city-state's whole territory, so a person who asks where Kostaros ends is asking about the shoreline and the hills behind it.
