## Overview {#overview}

**Krenel** is a walled caravan town of about 3,300 people on the road that runs from [[place-karvashion2|Karvashion]] to [[place-dunharargn|Dunhara]]. It stands on the one strong spring in a day's ride of dry upland, and its name comes from _krenir_, the Bizarian word for a spring of water, closed with _-el_. The spring rises inside the wall, so the town can be besieged but not thirsted out.

## Arriving {#arriving}

Caravans reach Krenel after two dry days from the south or one from Karvashion, and the sight of its poplars and garden walls is the reason the animals are driven faster at the end. The caravanserai stands against the spring, with troughs in its courtyard, and the town spreads along the road behind it.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Water is the product. The spring waters the caravans for a fee, and its overflow irrigates garden plots and a few orchards of fig and pomegranate outside the wall. The herding villages of the [[place-southrnmrch|Southern March]], with their sheep, goats and camels, bring wool, hides and cheese to the town's weekly market and take away salt, cloth and iron. Grain does not grow here in quantity, and the town buys it from the river valley of Karvashion, a day away by road, in wagons that return carrying wool. Krenel sends out wool, hides, camel strings for hire, guides who know the road to Dunhara, and the caravan stores that outfit a party for the dry stretch beyond.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

[[affiliation-karvashion|Karvashion]] holds the town, and its caravansary masters appoint the spring-warden, who sets the water fee and settles disputes over the troughs. The Lord of the Southern March rides in for the market, and a League detachment stationed at the town's caravanserai-fort guards the road. The warden and the lord keep a courteous distance.

## The Present {#present}

Dry-country bands that raid the Dunhara road drive off herds from the outlying villages, and the villagers blame the town for not riding out. Dunhari caravan masters complain that the water fee has risen, and the warden answers that the spring is the town's only income. A season of poor winter rain would test both claims.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The caravanserai has bed space and stabling. Rooms above the water-sellers' stalls are cheaper and quieter at night.
