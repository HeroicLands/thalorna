The **Village Council of Marshfeld** governs the farming village of [[place-marshfeld|Marshfeld]] in [[place-byzariargn|Bizaria]]. Each year it contracts [[being-fylzstnkt|Fileze Stenikot]], a mercenary of [[place-kostaros|Kostaros]], to protect the village against bandits and raiders. Its senior figure is Elder Magistrate [[being-razaagorikot|Raza]].

## History {#history}

Fileze Stenikot has returned to the village every year of her contract, and Elder Magistrate Raza has become her mentor over those years.
