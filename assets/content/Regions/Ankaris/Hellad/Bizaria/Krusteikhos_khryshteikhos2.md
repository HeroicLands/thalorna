## Overview {#overview}

Nothing from the east reaches the western sea without passing through **Krusteikhos**. The lowland road from [[place-nesthiros2|Nesthiros]] climbs the last escarpment to the edge of the plateau, and the first thing in sight is the gilded domes of the counting houses. This is the Golden Fortress, the League's capital, a city of 15,000 people and its wealthiest and most influential seat, and its banking houses finance trade across half of the continent. Eastern goods cross the plateau to Krusteikhos, are banked here, and then descend to the coast to be shipped west.

## Who Governs

A council of merchant-princes governs [[affiliation-khryshteikhos|Krusteikhos]], and the ruling families have held their seats for generations. Politics here is a long exchange of alliances, marriages and financial maneuvers. The League council meets in the [Merchant Hall]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, a vast, ornate building that is parliament and exchange at once, and the city's representatives have dominated League policy through the plain weight of their money.

## Character

The city is renowned for its goldsmiths, its bankers and its silk markets. Centuries of accumulated wealth show in its architecture: gilded domes, marble colonnades and ornamental gardens that blend Helonik elegance with eastern opulence. The banks hold their wealth in the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian currency]] and the paper-script of [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]], and Krusteikhos, a member of a province of the empire in law, takes full part in the whole banking system.

Krusteikhos grows little on the edge of the plateau and eats what the villages of the valley bottoms send in and what the grain road brings down from Velanthia, whose contracts its bankers hold and whose wagons its merchants own. What it sends out is credit: the goods and tolls of the hinterland towns are counted, banked and lent against here, and the plateau's wool, hides and horses are bought for the silk markets.

## History {#history}

The councils of the five cities met at the plateau's edge in 157 BF to swear [[lore-heladcompact|the Compact of the Five]], and the Merchant Hall stands where they met. The League council ceased to meet in the Hall when [[lore-heladeastwar|the Eastern War]] annexed the League about 209 AF. The city weigher proved the tax-farmers' brass standard heavy in the Hall about 455 AF ([[lore-heladweighing|The Weighing at Krusteikhos]]), and the councils swore the Compact again in the Hall about 571 AF ([[lore-heladreswear|The Re-swearing of the Compact]]).

## Current Situation {#present}

The League council meets in the Merchant Hall on the forts' pay, the common tolls, the joint fleet and army, the three marches and quarrels between city-states.
