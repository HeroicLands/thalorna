## Overview {#overview}

**Bruzash** is the smallest of the mining towns of the northern range, about 2,700 people in a hill valley west of the main ore roads. Its name is the Bizarian _bruzin_, bronze, which is what the town makes. The hills hold copper, and tin comes up from the south in small sealed bags, so Bruzash smelts the one, alloys it with the other and casts the result in the foundries that fill the lower quarter of the town.

## Arriving {#arriving}

The valley road is narrow and worn, and a visitor hears the foundries before seeing them: the ring of chisels on fresh castings and the crack of molds being broken open. The streets of the lower quarter are black with casting sand.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The town's own fields are small. The plateau villages below it, mostly barley and herding settlements, sell grain, hides and charcoal wood, and the town pays them in cooking pots, plow fittings and tools. The Velanthian grain road a half day to the north supplies a share of the bread when the plateau harvest is poor. Bruzash sends out cast bronze: the bells that caravans hang on their lead animals, the bells of temples, harness rings, buckles and lamp stands. It sells them to the caravan outfitters of [[place-khryshteikhos2|Krusteikhos]] and to the workshops of [[place-byzaris|Bizaris]], and the caravan trade carries a few of them far beyond the League. The trade works by contract: a founder who loses a contract with an outfitter loses a season.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

[[affiliation-khryshteikhos|Krusteikhos]] holds the town. The founders' guild sets the alloy, and a casting that fails the guild's test is broken up in public. A reeve from the capital collects the town's tax in castings and in coin, and a small guard post keeps the gate.

## The Present {#present}

The founders disagree about tin. The southern merchants who bring it raise the price each year, and one faction of the guild wants to buy from a different supplier, which the reeve will not allow without the capital's word. A seized shipment of smuggled tin sits in the gatehouse, and the guild has not said whose it is.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The founders' guild keeps a guest hall for buyers, and a bell-maker's widow lets rooms above her shop.
