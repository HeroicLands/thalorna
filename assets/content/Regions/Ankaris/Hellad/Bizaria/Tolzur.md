## Overview {#overview}

**Tolzur** is a walled toll town of about 3,500 people at the foot of the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]]'s dry foothills, where the south-eastern fork of the pass road turns toward the oasis belt and the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]. Its name is built on the Bizarian _tolzát_, a toll, and ends in _-ur_, one of the endings of Bizarian words. Every caravan bound for Amradad passes through its gate, and every caravan coming north from Amradad is counted at it.

## Arriving {#arriving}

A traveler reaches the town after a day of dry hills with no water but the springs the way-forts guard. The road enters a single gate where a toll-keeper in the town's color weighs, counts and marks each load, and a queue of mules usually stands outside it. Beyond the gate the main street is a caravanserai row, and the houses behind it are low and flat-roofed against the wind.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The town's own water is a line of springs along a gravel stream, and the villages that use them are small: barley and onion plots, goat herds, and a few orchards, all within half a day's walk. They cannot feed the town, and the rest comes up from the plateau on the pass road in the supply wagons that serve the March's garrisons. Tolzur makes its living from the road: gate tolls, storage, fodder and water for the animals, and the fees of the guides and caravan clerks. It sends out the horses it buys from the steppe clans who winter under the League's truce, hides, wool and the grain-fed remounts that the garrisons order. Most of what the town sells passes through rather than comes from it.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

[[affiliation-argyrteikhos|Argirteikhos]] holds the town. A toll warden sits in the gatehouse and answers to the military governor, and the League's own tariff post stands outside the wall under an officer of the March, so a caravan pays twice and argues about both. A company drawn from the March's joint army patrols the road, and the town's gate watch takes its orders from the warden.

## The Present {#present}

The warden and the March officer quarrel over which of them may seize a load that has dodged a toll. Merchants use the quarrel, and the smugglers of the mule-tracks have grown bolder. The Black Flame has followers in the town, a few families among the muleteers and clerks who gather in a back room after the gates close. The governor has been told, and he treats it as a matter for the Lord Commander, who has not acted. Steppe raiders test the foothills each spring, and a caravan that leaves after sunset is a caravan that has chosen its risk.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The caravanserai row has beds in a courtyard and in rooms above it, and the better inns take payment in goods. The warden keeps a guest room for officials.
