## Overview {#overview}

**Zalvarion** is a mining town of about 3,600 people in the range that walls [[place-byzariargn|Bizaria]] off from [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia]]. Its name takes the Bizarian _zalzhel_, a mine, for its root and closes on _-ion_, the ending the language gives to names. The seams of copper and lead run under the slopes above the town, and the adits, the stamping floors and the washing sheds climb the valley in steps behind its single wall.

## Arriving {#arriving}

The road from the plateau climbs a gorge for most of a day, and the town appears as a line of slag heaps before it appears as roofs. Ore wagons descend on the road and grain wagons climb it, and the drivers pass each other at the one place where the gorge is wide enough. Snow closes the upper road for much of the winter. In those months the town lives on what its granaries hold.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The mines feed nothing, and the town depends on the farms below it. The barley villages of the high valleys, with their cattle and their small terraced fields, supply most of its bread and meat, and the grain road from Velanthia, which runs through the [[place-northrnmrch|Northern March]], tops up the granaries each autumn. Charcoal burners from the pine slopes supply the smelting fires. Zalvarion sends out copper and lead as ore and as rough ingots, carried down to [[place-orashion2|Órashion]], whose forges turn them into the metalwork the League trades. Without the villages the mines would starve their own miners, and without Órashion the ore would have no buyer. The town sells to Órashion and buys from the valleys.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

[[affiliation-orashion|Órashion]] holds the town, and its guild masters set the weights of the ore and the price of a day underground. A mine overseer for each of the great adits answers to the guild council in Órashion, and a small company of the council's guards keeps the gate and the ore road. The League's tariff posts on the frontier are the Northern March's business, and the town's own toll on ore leaving the valley is the guild's.

## The Present {#present}

Bands from the high country come down to the lower valleys when the snow lies deep, and the ore trains are the first thing they look for. The guards escort every train in winter, and the mine owners resent paying for it. A miners' fraternity that hires out its own armed men has grown strong in the lower adits, and the overseers say it keeps the peace.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The wagoners' inn at the foot of the main adit takes anyone who pays in advance. Better rooms are the mine overseers' guest house, which hosts buyers and surveyors, and a monastery cell in the upper valley.
