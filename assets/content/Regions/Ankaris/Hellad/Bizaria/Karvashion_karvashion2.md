## Overview {#overview}

After the dry eastern hills, **Karvashion** is green. The town lies in a lush river valley, an oasis amid the drier country around it, and it takes its name, the caravan-place, from its caravanserais: fortified inn-complexes that house hundreds of merchants, their servants and their pack animals, the largest of them small towns in their own right. This is the Green Caravanserai of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]], a town of about 6,000, and it sits where the caravan roads divide, one road east toward the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin Desert]] and one south toward [[place-dunharargn|Dunhara]].

## The Bazaars

Eastern goods first pass into League hands here: spices, silk, incense and gemstones. They are bought in bazaars famous across the continent for the variety and quality of what they sell, then distributed to the other League seats and onward to [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión]]. A buyer who wants something from beyond the passes comes to Karvashion, and a seller from beyond the passes comes here to meet him.

The river valley's gardens and orchards feed the town, and the herders of the [[place-southrnmrch|Southern March]] bring wool and meat to its bazaars. The caravanserais sell lodging, fodder and guides to every train and buy the spices, silk, incense and gemstones the trains carry.

## Who Governs

A council of caravansary masters and merchant-princes governs [[affiliation-karvashion|Karvashion]]. The caravansary masters are shrewd negotiators, and the political influence of the men who run the great caravanserais rivals that of the merchant-princes, so a traveler who needs lodging, a guide, or an introduction deals with them. Beyond the town the [[place-southrnmrch|Southern March]] carries the road toward Dunhara.

## Character

Karvashion has a stronger eastern character than the other League seats. [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian worship]] has a significant following, particularly among the Dunhari and Khazrini merchants who keep permanent trading quarters here with their own tongues, faiths and law. The town has a reputation for gardeners, merchants and peaceful culture.
