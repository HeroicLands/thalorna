## Overview {#overview}

Selímara is a market town of about 5,000 on the inland roads of [[place-byzariargn|Bizaria]]. The maps in the counting houses of [[place-khryshteikhos2|Krusteikhos]] record a place like it as tolls collected, and that is all the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|League's ledgers]] say of it.

The villages of the plateau valleys around it feed the town: grain, wool and hides come in on market days, and Selímara sells them on up the inland roads and keeps the tolls. What it buys back with the proceeds is salt, cloth and finished metal.

## Who Lives Here

Two of the people in the character notes call Selímara home: [[being-morzhrxylkt|Morzáhr Zilakit]], a timberwright, and [[being-alykosmpsn|Alikos Bridutis]], a physician.
