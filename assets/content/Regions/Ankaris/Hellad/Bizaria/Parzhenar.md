## Overview {#overview}

**Parzhenar** is a garrison town of about 4,200 people where the pass road leaves the mountains and the foothills of the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]] begin. Its name joins _parzhen_, the Bizarian word for a garrison, to _-ar_, a Bizarian word ending. A day's march from the town the last of the way-forts guards a spring, and Parzhenar is the place the way-forts draw their soldiers, fodder and replacements from.

## Arriving {#arriving}

The town fills a shelf above a stream with long barracks, stables and storehouses, and the habit of the place is the march: drums at dawn, patrols leaving at the bell, and wagons lined up at the commissary yard. Strangers are asked their business at the gate and again at the inn, and the answers are written down.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The garrison is the town's economy. Families of the soldiers who have settled around the forts live in its lower streets, and they supply the bakers, farriers, saddlers and laundries. The farms along the stream grow barley and hay, and herders on the foothills supply meat and wool, but the town buys most of its grain from the plateau and the merchant houses of [[place-argyrteikhos2|Argirteikhos]] that hold the garrison contracts. Parzhenar sends out soldiers, remounts bred on the foothill pastures, leather goods, forged arrowheads and the mule trains that supply the way-forts. Its market serves the troops.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

[[affiliation-argyrteikhos|Argirteikhos]] holds the town, and a commandant who answers to the military governor of Argirteikhos runs it. The Lord Commander of the March draws on its garrison, and the two commands overlap in the town, so orders reach the barracks from two directions. The town's own watch is drawn from veterans, and its justice is a soldier's: quick, written and rarely appealed.

## The Present {#present}

The dry ground where the [[place-thirstground|Thirsting Ground]] lies is a morning's ride from the barracks, and the patrols that keep its jar come from here. The [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] has a strong presence in this country, and in Parzhenar it shows in a handful of garrison families and a stable hand or two who keep its rites. The commandant knows, and he watches them because the clans of the high passes that share the order's faith also fight the raiders the garrison cannot reach. Mountain bands and steppe raiders both test the supply wagons on the road below, and the commissary guards its stores closely.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The officers' mess takes travelers with a commission or a letter. The inn at the commissary gate takes the rest, at a price that rises when the garrison's pay is late.
