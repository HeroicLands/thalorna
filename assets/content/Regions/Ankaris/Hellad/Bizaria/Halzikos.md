## Overview {#overview}

**Halzikos** is a caravan stop of about 3,000 people on the shore of one of the three inland seas that lie in the dry country east of the plateau. Its name sets _halzin_, the Bizarian word for salt, under _-ikos_, the Helonik ending for names. The sea is salt and shallow, its shore is white with crust, and nothing else for a long ride in any direction offers a caravan a town, a well and a market.

## Arriving {#arriving}

The road to Halzikos crosses a plain of grass and thorn that dries out as it nears the shore. The town appears as a low wall, a cluster of reed roofs and the pale rectangles of the salt pans. The wind off the water is hot in summer and bitter in winter, and the water in the shore wells is brackish. Caravans refill their skins at the sweet-water cistern in the middle of the town, which is the town's most valued building.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Halzikos has little to feed on. The sea gives salt and a small catch of fish that are dried on racks along the shore, and the reed beds give mats and roofing. The herders' camps on the plain bring sheep, goats and felt in exchange for salt. Grain comes by caravan from the valley of [[place-karvashion2|Karvashion]], and a bad season on that road is a hungry season at the sea. The town sends out salt in blocks and sacks, dried fish, reed mats and felt, and it earns the rest from caravans that stop for water, fodder, repairs and fresh guides on the way to [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Amradad]]. The caravans are its custom.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

[[affiliation-karvashion|Karvashion]] holds the town, and its caravansary masters send a cistern-warden who keeps the sweet water, sets the price of a skin and decides who camps where. A small garrison from the joint army holds the wall, and the town's salt-masters have a guild hall by the pans.

## The Present {#present}

Steppe and desert bands raid the herders' camps on the plain and the caravans that leave late, and the garrison is too small to patrol more than the road. The salt-masters want a wall around the pans, and the warden says that the cistern is the one thing he must guard first. Amradi customs officers have taken to crossing the line to inspect loads headed for their country, and the warden has complained of it to Karvashion without result.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The caravanserai by the cistern is the only large lodging. The salt-masters rent rooms above the pans, where the smell of brine is strong and the price is low.
