## Overview {#overview}

**Vezhrelion** is a walled pass town of about 3,000 people where the road from [[place-orashion2|Órashion]] meets the high country that leads up to the eastern passes. Its name is _vezhrel_, the Bizarian word for a pass, with _-ion_, the ending Bizarian takes from Helonik for names. The town sits across the narrowest part of the valley, and its wall closes the valley from slope to slope with a single gate.

## Arriving {#arriving}

The road climbs steadily for two days after Órashion, and the last mile is a stair of paving stones. The gate is shut at dusk and opened at dawn by the gatekeepers, and a stranger who arrives after the bell sleeps outside the wall with the muleteers. Above the town the slopes belong to the [Vezhnur holds]{.name data-type="place/region"}.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The town stands where goods change hands between the League and the clans of the high holds. The holds are a clan-country of warrior families who herd hardy cattle and horses on the upper slopes and grow little grain, and they come down to Vezhrelion to buy bread, salt, cloth and iron. The barley villages of the valley below supply the town's own grain, and Órashion's wagons bring the rest. Vezhrelion sends out horses and cattle bought from the clans, the pelts of the high country and the tolls of the pass road, and it sells the holds the metalwork of Órashion through a handful of merchant houses that have dealt with the clans for generations. It is the only market many of the clans use.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

[[affiliation-orashion|Órashion]] holds the town, and the merchant-diplomat house that brokers with the holds keeps a factor in it. A guild bailiff runs the gate and the market, a company of Órashion's guards holds the wall, and the League's pass-fort stands above the town under an officer of the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]]. Clan envoys are given a guest hall, and no clansman goes armed through the market street.

## The Present {#present}

The cohort whose sons became the clans of the holds took the Black Flame with it, and the order's presence in this country is strong ([[lore-heladvezhnur|The Vezhnur Settlement]]). In Vezhrelion it is a quiet one: a few merchant families and a chapter of men who gather at the clan guest hall, and the town's other residents know who they are and ask no questions. Stories of the order's rival factions reach the town with every clan caravan, and the name heard most often is [[being-zekaztrosusk|Sir Zekáz Trosuskar]], a Bizarian knight who leads a faction within a rival Black Flame. The League keeps the clans as an eastern shield and does not move against the order. Mountain bands that owe nothing to any hold raid the lower road, and the clans and the town each say the other should deal with them.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The pass inn takes caravan guards and muleteers in a long common room, and rooms above are rented by the night. The factor's house hosts visiting merchants of Órashion.
