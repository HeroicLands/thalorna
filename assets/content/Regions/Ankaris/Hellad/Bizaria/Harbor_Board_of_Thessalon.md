The **Harbor Board of Thessalon** governs [[place-thessalon|Thessalon]], a harbor of [[place-byzariargn|Bizaria]] that its harbormaster runs day to day.

## History {#history}

The board answers to the Bizarian League, which the Eastern War annexed to the empire about 209 AF, and Thessalon's harbormaster has run the harbor day to day under it.
