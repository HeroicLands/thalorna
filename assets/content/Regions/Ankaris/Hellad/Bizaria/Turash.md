## Overview {#overview}

**Turash** is the miners' name for a passage deep in one of the great adits of [[place-zalvarion|Zalvarion]], the copper and lead town in the range between [[place-byzariargn|Bizaria]] and [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia]]. The name is [[skill-byzarnlng|Bizarian]], "the door", from _turin_, a door, closed with the word ending _-ash_. A new drive following the lead broke through its wall this autumn into a passage nobody in the town had cut. The passage winds upward through the rock for a few hundred paces and ends at a slab of gray stone fitted across it from wall to wall, the shape and height of a door, with no handle and no hinge.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The adit belongs to the guild masters of [[affiliation-orashion|Órashion]], and so does anything the drive breaks into. The adit's overseer has charge of the drive and the passage, and the miners' fraternity of the lower adits has the men who refuse to work there.

## Layers {#history}

Nobody in Zalvarion knew of the passage before the drive broke into it. The older miners say it is one of the tunnels of the stories every mining town tells, too smooth to be a miner's work and winding the way water would, and that the hammering men hear in such places is the work of whoever cut them. The overseer's surveyors say it is an old working of the first miners in the range, before the town, and that its makers sealed it because it ran out of ore.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The drive is stopped. The guild masters of Órashion have sent word that the slab is to be broken and the passage behind it worked for ore. The miners of the drive refuse to go up to it, because they have heard a knocking from behind the slab on quiet shifts, slow and uneven, and the fraternity backs them. The overseer has offered double pay to anyone who will take a hammer to the slab, and nobody in the town has taken it. Buyers staying at the overseers' guest house have asked to see the passage, and the overseer has kept them out of it.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The fraternity's armed men stand between the overseer and the miners, and either side may hire strangers. Bands from the high country come down to the lower valleys in deep snow and watch the ore road. Inside, the drive's broken wall is loose, and stones still fall from the edge of the breach.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A breach in the side of a working drive, waist-high and ragged, and beyond it a passage of a different kind: round in section, the height of a short man, its walls and floor worn smooth and dark as if by water, climbing and turning as it goes. The air in it is cold and damp and moves a little toward the breach. At its end the slab fills the passage, its edges fitted to the rock so closely that a knife blade does not go in. Its face is wet and colder than the rock round it, and a line of small, square marks is cut across it at chest height. On a quiet shift the knocking comes through the slab itself, as a tremor in the stone.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The guild's hammer.** The overseer pays double wages and a bonus to a party that breaks the slab and goes through.
- **The fraternity's proof.** The fraternity pays a party to find out what knocks behind the slab before anyone breaks it, and to bring proof the guild masters will accept.
- **The marks.** A buyer at the guest house pays well for a copy of the line of marks on the slab, and the overseer does not want one made.

# The Truth of Turash {#truth .secret}

**The working.** The passage is the lowest reach of **Pabak-rubád**, "the following of the lead", a working of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]], abandoned and sealed. Its makers came to the range for the lead and for the silver the lead carries, and they followed a vein down through the mountain to the lode where several veins meet. They cut as little as they had to: the passage is a water-worn channel in the rock, widened and smoothed, and the deeper galleries follow the vein. Which hold sent its miners is the game master's to set.

**The sealing.** When the working was given up, its makers sealed it for all generations, as the Khazari seal what they close. The slab is one of two seals. The line of marks across it is [[skill-drthrkscrpt|Pirzath]] and says that: the working's name and the word _buggán_, "sealed for all generations". The other seal is the working's own door, higher on the mountain, in a cliff face above the town's highest adit, fitted into the rock so that it reads as rock.

**The water.** The galleries behind the slab have filled with water since the sealing, and the slab holds it back. The knocking is the water and the air trapped above it, shifting in the drowned galleries as the rains soak down through the mountain. A party that breaks the slab lets out a flood that fills the passage in moments and pours through the breach into the drive and down the adit, drowning the drive's face and whoever is in it, and flooding the adit's lower levels for a season.

**What is left.** The lode lies above the water and is reached dry from the upper door. The Khazari took out what silver they needed and left the rest; the lode is richer in silver than anything the town works. Opening that door is the act of breaking a Khazari seal, and every hold's archive records where its sealed workings lie.

> [!TIP]
> The fraternity is right and the guild masters are wrong, and a party that proves it saves the adit. The flood behind the slab, the knocking, the upper door and the silver of the lode are this note's own account; a game master may change them.
