## Overview {#overview}

**Oinelena** is a market town of about 4,500 people on the terraced hills where the lowland belt of [[place-byzariargn|Bizaria]] begins to rise toward the plateau. Its name grows out of _oinel_, the Bizarian word for wine, and ends in _-ena_. It is the largest market of the lowland belt outside the seaport, and the lowland road from [[place-nesthiros2|Nesthiros]] to [[place-khryshteikhos2|Krusteikhos]] runs through its square, a day from the capital and two from the seaport.

## Arriving {#arriving}

The approach is a climb through terraces of vines and olive groves, with a village in every fold of the hills. The town sits at the head of a shallow valley, its white walls low and its press-houses larger than its temple. Grape and olive harvests fill the square with carts for weeks.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The coastal belt is the most densely settled part of the League, and Oinelena stands at its inland edge. Dozens of farming villages ring the town, each holding a few terraces of vines and olives, and they bring the crop to its presses and pay a share of the oil and wine for the pressing. The belt's grain does not suffice, so the town buys barley from the plateau and Velanthian wheat that comes down the grain road. Oinelena sends out wine, olive oil, raisins and dried figs, carried by mule and cart to the quays of Nesthiros and sold on to the ports beyond the sea. Its cellar masters and oil factors sell by the cask and the jar at the market.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

[[affiliation-khryshteikhos|Krusteikhos]] holds the town, which lies nearer the capital than the seaport, through a prefect appointed by the Merchant Hall. The council of the oil and wine guilds sets the price of the pressing and the seal on each cask. The capital's guards patrol the road to the seaport, and the town's watch keeps the market. This is the most peaceable of the League's towns, and its disputes are over cask marks and terrace boundaries.

## The Present {#present}

The oil guild and the wine guild share the town's presses and disagree about their share of the water that turns the mills. A dry winter has left both short, and each blames the other. Smugglers working the road from the coast sell uninspected wine without the guild's seal, and the Warden of the Weights has promised to deal with them before the autumn fair.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The inns of the square are clean and close, and the best of them keeps its own cellar. The prefect's house takes officials.
