## Overview {#overview}

The caravans that cross the eastern mountains reach **Argirteikhos** before they reach anything else in the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]], and the first thing they meet is a wall. The Silver Citadel sits astride the principal pass connecting [[place-byzariargn|Bizaria]] to the eastern lands, and its fortress walls are among the most formidable in the region. About 5,500 people live in it, the smallest of the five League seats, and it carries the League's weight in the east.

## Who Governs

A military governor and a council of merchant-princes govern [[affiliation-argyrteikhos|Argirteikhos]]. The town held out for two years of siege about 212 AF, and the imperial _usos_'s office it accepted in the settlement survives as the military governor's seat ([[lore-heladcitadel|The Siege of the Silver Citadel]]). The governor also sits on the League council beside the merchant-princes, a standing reminder that the League's prosperity rests on the security of its trade routes. The garrison keeps the mountain routes open and discourages the ambitions of eastern warlords, desert raiders and bandits, and the town's visible readiness to fight is meant to make fighting unnecessary. Beyond the pass lies the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]], whose Lord Commander is the senior soldier of the League.

## Character

The town is more austere than the other League seats: less ornate, more functional, with the disciplined air of a garrison. The silver mines in the surrounding peaks fund the League's collective defense and give the town a political weight beyond its size, and the mines support a community of skilled engineers and metalworkers. The monasteries of the eastern rite are especially influential here. Their monks are the town's chroniclers and healers and its occasional diplomatic intermediaries with the peoples beyond the passes.

The pass has no farmland, so the garrison and the miners eat bread carried up from the plateau villages, and the town pays for it with silver from the peaks. Its granaries are kept full against a siege like the one of 212 AF.

## History {#history}

The five cities walled the foothill springs against a steppe host in 158 BF ([[lore-heladwells|The Battle of the Wells]]). The town held out for two years of siege from about 212 AF, and it surrendered on terms that left it its walls, its own garrison and a seat beside an imperial _usos_ ([[lore-heladcitadel|The Siege of the Silver Citadel]]).

## Current Situation {#present}

The citadel's silver pays the League's way-forts through the common treasury, and the governor sits on the council that spends it.
