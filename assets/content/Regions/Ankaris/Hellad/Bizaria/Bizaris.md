"Your master's name opens the door," a guild examiner of Bizaris tells a journeyman who has arrived with a letter of introduction, "and what you made opens the workshop."

Bizaris is the foremost artisan quarter and craft hub of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]], though its exact location has shifted historically as it has expanded from a small workshop district into a town of its own. The name comes from the ancient Bizarian word _bizur_, meaning "the place of making" or "the workshop." About 5,000 people live in it, and it is known across the eastern part of the [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión Region]] for the quality and innovation of its craftspeople.

## Workshops and Guilds

The town is organized around guild halls and workshop complexes. The [[affiliation-hideworkerscltv|Hideworkers' Collective]] keeps one of its most prestigious chapters here, and the herds of the [[place-southrnmrch|Southern March]] send Bizaris the greater part of their leather. Ceramicists, metalworkers, jewelers and textile artisans crowd the narrow streets and compete for supremacy in their crafts, holding all the while to the fierce loyalty that marks Bizarian professional societies.

The town lacks the political grandeur of [[affiliation-khryshteikhos|Krusteikhos]] and the military reputation of [[affiliation-orashion|Órashion]], but its merchants command respect and considerable wealth. A master ceramicist or weaponsmith from Bizaris carries credentials that open doors from the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]] to [[affiliation-jenchaiempr|Jenchai]].

The guilds' workshops eat from the farming villages of the plateau valleys around the town, and the herds of the Southern March also send it hides. Bizaris sends out ceramics, leather goods, metalwork and finished textiles, and buys its iron and copper from the mines of Órashion and its silk thread from the eastern caravans.

## The Council of Twelve

The heads of the twelve greatest guilds make up the [[affiliation-cncltwlvbzrs|town's council]], which makes Bizaris the only League city-state with formal guild governance. The arrangement gives the town stability and endless subtle negotiation, as artisan interests jockey for precedence and resources. In League politics Bizaris keeps a careful neutrality, because its influence depends on staying indispensable to all of its rivals.
