## Overview {#overview}

Brevavesh is the seat of the [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]], a fortress town of about three thousand people on the forest edge. A stone fort of the paid wardens stands over the river crossing, the prince's captain of the forts keeps his rolls there, and the town below it lives on the garrison's wages, the fur hunters who outfit in its yards and the grain that comes up from the lowlands. It eats what the lowlands send and grows almost nothing.
