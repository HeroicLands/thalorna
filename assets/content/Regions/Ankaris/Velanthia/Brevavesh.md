## Overview {#overview}

Brevavesh is the seat of the [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]], a fortress town of about three thousand people on the forest edge. A stone fort of the paid wardens stands over the river crossing, the prince's captain of the forts keeps his rolls there, and the town below it lives on the garrison's wages, the fur hunters who outfit in its yards and the grain that comes up from the lowlands. It eats what the lowlands send and grows almost nothing, because the ground beside the Grukar country gives no returns to the plow. From its walls the forest begins at a bowshot, and the bell on the fort's tower can be heard in the villages on both banks.

## History {#history}

The town is the oldest of the frontier forts: a crossing guarded first by a timber stockade and then by the stone fort that the princes of the house raised and have held since. The frontier princes learned the cost of a fort they could not keep at Theodburg, which a prince of this frontier raised with the war-bands of [[place-thornwald|Thornwald]] about 590 AF and which burned as it was abandoned ([[lore-princesfort|The Prince's Fort]]). The stone forts of the princedom, this one first among them, were built to be held by Velanthian wardens on the prince's pay and not by borrowed war-bands. From 660 AF the frontier's unransomed captives went down the rivers of [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]] to Nordmen buyers ([[lore-thrallrivers|The Thrall Rivers]]), and the town's ransom brokers have been busy since.

## Current Situation {#present}

The silver of the port and the grain of the lowlands both arrive late, and the garrison is owed several months. The wardens answer by selling a night's passage to a fur hunter and a day's leave to a villager, and the margin is watched by tired men who are not paid to be careful. Varokh war-bands raid across the forest edge in autumn, and the prince has no bargain with the Grukar and wants none. The prince knows which of his wardens sell passage and weighs the cost of hanging one.

:::secret {title="The Captain's Rolls"}
The captain of the forts keeps two sets of rolls: the one he shows the prince each quarter and the one that says who is actually in the barracks.
:::

## The Holder {#holder}

The prince, [Krodogor Brevogorich]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, holds the town and the fort as the heart of his princedom and lives in the fort's upper hall, not in a house of his own. He appoints every warden of the princedom and sees the rolls each quarter, when the silver arrives from the port. His judge rides circuit between the forts and sits in the town for the cases the wardens and the villages cannot settle.

## Garrison {#garrison}

The fort holds the largest body of paid wardens in the princedom, with the captain of the forts over them. The wardens are tired, paid late and corruptible, and the garrison is thin on the days after a pay-day passes without silver. Each warden holds one post: the gate, the river tower, the forest watch or the bell. The bell and the signal fire on the hill above the town are the princedom's alarm; the villages below each fort ring and burn in turn and send a man to the watch. When the bell rings twice, the town closes the river gate and the fur hunters in its yards are held until the wardens know what crossed the margin.

## Government, Faith and Trade {#government-faith-trade}

The prince governs by the forts, and the town has no council: the captain keeps its order and the judge its law. Its trade is the garrison's wages spent, the grain barges that come up from the lowlands under escort, and the fur hunters who outfit in the town before the cold months and sell what they bring out of the forest to the buyers who wait at the river gate. A small house of the [[affiliation-lizelas|Faith of Lizelas]] at the foot of the fort keeps the hours for the garrison and buries its dead. The ford town of Yaravesh, where ransoms are counted out and captives handed back, lies down the river, and the ransom brokers of the town ride between the two.

## Within the Walls {#within-the-walls}

The fort is a square of gray stone with a tower at the river corner and a barracks yard behind the gate. Below it the town is a close huddle of timber houses, smokehouses and horse-yards, with narrow lanes that smell of tar and wet fur. The yards of the fur hunters line the road to the river gate, each with a drying rack, and the armorer's forge and the farrier's shop stand under the fort's wall where the wardens can reach them. The granary is the one stone building in the town besides the fort, and it is kept locked by the captain's own key.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- A fur hunter needs a night's passage across the forest edge and has the coin for a warden who will sell it.
- A family has the price of a captive's ransom and needs someone to carry it to the ford at Yaravesh.
- The judge on circuit wants someone outside the prince's pay to count the wardens at their posts, since the garrison is owed months and passage is being sold.
