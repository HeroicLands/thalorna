## Overview {#overview}

**Zvek Bregad**, the bell landing, is the name the river-folk of [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia Region]] give a bank where the slow brown rivers of [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm Region]] open toward their grain country; the bell is the one that hangs over it. A Velanthian landing is where boats take on cargo; this one is where raiding parties come ashore, in summer, in war-canoes. The bank is Velanthian ground in the [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]], in the northwestern reaches that the Grukar country overlies.

## The Land

The landing is a low bank on a slow river with the forest behind it and the marsh upstream. Canoes come down from the nests upstream and have no cover from the bank. Which nests send them is not known at the fort; nests appear and die, and the canoes of one summer need not come from the nest of the last.

## Who Uses It

The Grukar come to it as a raiding party and for nothing else. The wardens of [[place-trvgrfrt|Torvogor Fort]], under [[being-krvgrlrvkv|Karvogor Lervikov]], watch it, and the village beneath the fort rings its bell when canoes are seen. [[being-brvnbrvkv|Brevana Brevekov]] goes down to it with a cart after a raid.

## Dangers

A Grukar party that lands does not parley, does not ransom what it takes and does not leave a message. The Uk and the Hushk cannot bargain.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The landing lies some hours' travel from the fort that watches it, and the bell and signal fire stand on the hill beside the fort, not at the bank. The fire warns the garrison and the village the moment it is lit, so the delay lies between the landing and the fort: the village beneath the fort gives a man to the watch in turn, and at dusk, when the canoes come down, that one man must see them and carry the word, or reach the hill, before anything is lit. What can be done against a party that comes ashore depends on how long that takes. Fur hunters pass the landing on their way to and from the margin.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- Canoes come down the river at dusk and the one man on the watch at the landing sees them but is hours from the hill, so the bell and the fire are not yet lit, and the village is at its own work.
- A fur hunter coming out of the margin by the river must pass the landing, and the canoes of a raiding party lie on the bank.
