## Overview {#overview}

The Princedom of Brevavesh holds the frontier of forts on the edge of the Grukar country, in the forest-steppe of north-western Velanthia. It is the smallest of the three and the poorest in grain, and it spends more on soldiers than the other two together. Its forts are stone, raised by the prince and held by paid wardens, and its villages lie under them. The lowland prince's grain feeds the forts and the port prince's silver pays the wardens. About 300,000 people live on its land, the fewest of the three, and no Host serves it. The seat is [[place-brvvsh|Brevavesh]].

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The prince is [Krodogor Brevogorich]{.name data-type="being/npc"}. He rules by the forts: every fort has a warden he appoints and the port's silver pays, the wardens answer to his captain of the forts, and a judge on circuit hears the cases that wardens and villages cannot settle. The wardens are paid, tired and corruptible, and the prince knows which of them sell passage past the forts to the fur hunters. Each village below a fort rings its bell and burns a signal fire when a raiding party crosses the margin, and sends a man to the watch in turn.

## Current Situation {#present}

The prince cannot feed his garrisons from his own fields, since the ground beside the Grukar country gives no returns to the plow, so by agreement the lowland prince sends grain to his forts and the port prince sends the silver that pays his wardens. Both deliveries are late. The wardens answer by selling a night's passage to a fur hunter and a day's leave to a villager, and the margin is watched by tired men who are owed several months. Varokh war-bands raid across the forest edge in autumn, captives taken in them are ransomed when a family or a prince will pay, and those nobody ransoms go down Vristwald's rivers. The Grukar, whom no one bargains with, press the margin from the north-west. The prince has no bargain with a nest and wants none.

## History {#history}

About 590 AF a prince of this frontier raised the fort at Theodburg with the war-bands of Thornwald, and his son asked tribute for it; the war-bands walked out, the prince could not hold it alone, and it burned as it was abandoned ([[lore-princesfort|The Prince's Fort]]). From 660 AF the frontier's captives went down Vristwald's rivers to Nordmen buyers ([[lore-thrallrivers|The Thrall Rivers]]). The frontier princes have hired Varokh war-bands since only by the season and for pay, an heir of the house wants Theodburg back, and the stone forts have held.
