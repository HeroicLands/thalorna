## Overview {#overview}

Zhitavesh is the seat of the [[affiliation-prncdmzhtvsh|Princedom of Zhitavesh]], a walled town of about eight thousand people on the river meadows of the lowland grain belt. Its prince's hall stands beside the princely granary, and the great granary monastery of the lowlands stands on the other side of the market square. The town buys nothing it can grow: the barns, mills and ferries of its own villages feed it, and the barges that load at its landing carry the surplus to the port.
