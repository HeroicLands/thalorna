## Overview {#overview}

Sedrovod Fort, the _sedrovod tverad_, is a stone fort of the prince of the [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]] on the last road before the forest, facing the Grukar country from the southeast. Its wardens watch the raiding paths that leave the marsh, and the forest edge where Varokh war-bands cross in autumn.

## Inside

The fort has a stone wall and a gate on the forest road, with a hall for the garrison and stores of the lowland prince's grain. A village lies beneath it, with its own bell and signal fire on the hill.

## Who Is There

The warden is the prince's man, appointed by him and answering to his captain of the forts. He and his garrison are paid in the port prince's silver and fed on the lowland prince's grain. The village sends a man to the watch in turn. A hunter outfitting for the margin comes to the fort for passage, and a warden who is owed enough sells it.

## The Present {#present}

The garrison watches the road, the forest edge and the paths from the marsh, and it is paid late. Varokh war-bands raid across the edge in autumn, and the captives they take are ransomed when a family or a prince will pay; those nobody ransoms go down Vristwald's rivers. The princes hire Varokh war-bands only by the season and for pay, never by oath ([[lore-princesfort|The Prince's Fort]]). Between the raids the fort sells passage to the hunters and watches for the small parties that come out along the cause-walks.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- A war-band crosses the forest edge in autumn and takes villagers, and the captives are ransomed only if a family or a prince will pay before they go down the river.
- A hunter sold a night's passage by the warden is not back by the thaw, and the fort is owed several months of pay.
