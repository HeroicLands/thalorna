## Overview {#overview}

Karvogora is the mountain line along the whole southern rim of the [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia Region]]. The Velanthians name it for what it is made of, _karv_, stone, and _gor_, a height: the Stone Height. In [[place-helionis|Helionis]] it is Borethin Órasin, the north mountain, and in the [[place-northrnmrch|Northern March]] it is simply the watershed that the frontier follows. It is one range with three names, and three countries argue over who keeps its passes.

North of it lies the black-earth grain belt, and south of it lie the mining towns of [[place-khaddaria|Khaddaria]] and the upland pastures of the March. Grain goes over the passes in one direction, and ore and iron come back in the other.

## The Land {#the-land}

At its western end the range stands above the thinning forest of the [[place-nartumkngdm|Nartun Kingdom]], where the foothills begin many days' travel from the Velanthian fields. Eastward it grows higher and drier, with stony ridges, pine in the sheltered valleys and snow on the passes for a third of the year. On the Velanthian side its foothills are farmed to the last terrace. On the far side the ground is mining country: red and green rock, spoil heaps and palisaded towns.

## Who Uses It {#who-uses-it}

The range has several passes. The grain road crosses at the lowest, which descends into the March toward Krusteikhos, and a lesser drove road farther east carries horses south. The northern passes lead into the mining towns of Khaddaria. From late summer until the snow closes them, wagon trains climb out of the Velanthian grain belt, and the same wagons carry ore and bar iron north. Velanthian drovers winter their herds on the March side by long custom, and the [[affiliation-hstsvrt|Sevret Host]] patrols the Velanthian approaches and rides escort for the drovers. The March keeps customs posts and way-forts on its side, and the Khaddarian towns buy the grain without which their mining towns cannot eat.

## Dangers {#dangers}

Marauders come down from the mining country on the far side, and the [[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]] is strong there. It holds nothing inside Velanthia: the danger belongs to the passes, and the patrols and way-forts meet it there. A rockfall can close a pass in the week the grain is due, and the three countries do not clear it with the same urgency, because each loses something different when the wagons stop.

## Converging Interests {#present}

Every party that uses the passes waits for the others to move first. The rain came late to the eastern fields, and a short harvest would leave the grain that crosses the passes thin and the Host's pay late. [Sovidan Volketich]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the Sevret Host patrols the approaches for pay that arrives at harvest, and if it is late his riders collect their own tolls. The March's customs officers tax every wagon that crosses, and the Khaddarian towns pay for grain in ore that their own smelters would sooner keep. Each of them watches the weather over the passes.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **Escort before the snow.** [Sovidan Volketich]{.name data-type="being/npc"} needs riders for the last wagon trains, and the pay that would fill his roster comes only at harvest.
- **The two tolls.** A lowland wagon master needs a go-between to settle with the March's customs officers and the Sevret riders what each will take from his wagons, since each waits for the other to name a figure and the last trains of the season are loading.
