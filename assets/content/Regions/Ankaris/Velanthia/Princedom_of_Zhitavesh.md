## Overview {#overview}

The Princedom of Zhitavesh holds the grain lowlands of the black-earth belt, from the river meadows at its seat to the foot of the southern range. It has more people than either of the other two river-princedoms and the fullest barns in Velanthia: its villages sow wheat, rye and flax, its mills grind for the barges, and the monasteries of its towns keep the reserve that carries the land through a bad year. About 650,000 people live on its land, among them the 180,000 of the [[affiliation-hstsvrt|Sevret Host]] and the [[affiliation-hstkhrd|Khorad Host]], which hold its south-eastern margin under the prince. The seat is [[place-zhtvsh|Zhitavesh]].

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The princedom passes in the prince's house, and the prince is [Zhitovod Dobrovodich]{.name data-type="being/npc"}. He holds the landings, the mills, the ferries and the tithe of grain, which he takes in kind, so that his wealth is the sacks in his granary and not the silver in his mint. His hall keeps a retinue of sworn riders under a captain, a judge who rules by the customary law the princely houses share, and a keeper of the princely granary who answers for the stores and counts them against what the monasteries hold. The prince pays each Host in grain and silver at harvest, and the pay is the whole of his hold on them.

## Current Situation {#present}

The prince's difficulty is that his grain is worth what other people will pay for it. The barges that carry it go down to the port of [[place-brlvsh|Brelavesh]], where the merchant houses set the price, and every year he asks the same question: how much of the harvest can he sell upriver, at the passes, to the mining towns of Helionis and Bizaria, without the port noticing? The monasteries hold a reserve he cannot audit, and the keeper of his granary has twice been refused entry to a barn. This year's rain came late to the eastern fields, the Hosts' pay falls due at harvest, and a short harvest would leave him unable to pay both. The prince knows that an ataman who goes unpaid listens to the steppe tribes. His grain also goes up to the forts of the [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]], whose garrisons cannot feed themselves, and a short harvest would leave them hungry too.

## History {#history}

The lowland houses count the oldest princely line on the central river. When the third union of the steppe overran the eastern fringe about 548 AF ([[lore-thrdorqwnq|The Third Orghunoq]]), the lowlands held and the fringe was lost, and the grain villages of the heartland resettled the ground over the next two generations. The Hosts of the south-eastern margin have held it for the princedom since.
