## Overview {#overview}

Dobravesh is a walled town of about five thousand people in the lowland grain belt, held by the [[affiliation-prncdmzhtvsh|Princedom of Zhitavesh]] and the second grain market of the lowlands, after the seat. A house of the [[affiliation-grnrymnstrs|Granary Monasteries of Lizelas]] stands beside the prince's granary on its market square, and the two barns face each other across the cobbles, each with its own keeper and its own ledger. The monks keep the town's hours, teach the sons of the neighboring halls to read, and take in the hungry in a bad year. [[being-lrvshvldgrch|Lervash Vlidogorich]] keeps the barn's keys. The prince's keeper counts the sacks he can see, and the monastery's keeper declines to say how many he cannot. Growers from a day's ride around bring their harvest here to be weighed, sold, stored or ground by [[being-dbrndvkv|Dobrena Duvekov]], and the barges carry the surplus down the river to [[place-zhtvsh|Zhitavesh]] and the port.
