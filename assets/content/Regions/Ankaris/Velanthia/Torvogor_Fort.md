## Overview {#overview}

Torvogor Fort, the _torvogor tverad_, is one of the stone forts that the prince of the [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]] has raised on the edge of the [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm]] country. It stands on a rise over a river that comes out of the marsh and it watches the stretch of bank where raiding canoes come ashore at [[place-yadakovbregad|Yadakov Bregad]].

## Inside

The fort is stone, with a gate toward the river and a hall for the garrison. A bell hangs on the hill beside it and a signal fire is laid beneath the bell. A village of the princedom lies under the walls, and the fort eats the lowland prince's grain.

## Who Is There

The warden is appointed by the prince and answers to his captain of the forts, who keeps the rolls at [[place-brvvsh|Brevavesh]]. The wardens are paid, tired and corruptible. The silver that pays them comes from the port prince and the grain that feeds them from the lowland prince. A circuit judge hears the cases that the warden and the village cannot settle. The village gives a man to the watch in turn.

## The Present {#present}

Both the grain and the silver come late, and the garrison is owed several months. The warden answers by selling a fur hunter a night's passage past the fort and a villager a day's leave. The watch is tired men owed money, and the bell and the fire on the hill are what the village has between itself and a raiding party. Hunters outfit in the villages and the fort towns of the princedom and go in by the river or the cause-walks.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- A fur hunter wants a night's passage past the fort, and the warden is owed months of pay.
- Canoes are on the river at dusk and the fort has a short watch, because the warden has given a day's leave to half the men.
