## Overview {#overview}

The Princedom of Brelavesh holds the river-port where Velanthia meets everyone else. Grain barges from the lowlands, fur boats from the northern forest and the horse-traders of the Khazrin all come to its quays, and the merchant houses that handle them are richer than most of the princes' vassals. The port is the one city in the land, and the prince is the only one of the three whose wealth is silver. The princedom lies on the central river below the grain lowlands, and its land runs east to the caravan roads of the Hosts' country. About 550,000 people live on it, 120,000 of them in the country of the [[affiliation-hsttrvl|Tarvál Host]], which holds its eastern roads under the prince. The seat is [[place-brlvsh|Brelavesh]], and the fur boats of the northern forest unload at the landing town of [[place-rdvsh|Radavesh]].

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The prince is [[being-mlgryrgrch|Milogor Yarogorich]], and he rules the port by tolls and by his mint. His port-warden, [[being-trvkmvrnch|Torvik Mevranich]], keeps the landings and the order of the quays, his judge of the quays hears the disputes of boatmen and merchants by the shared customary law, and his keeper of the mint strikes the silver that the river trade runs on. A retinue of riders holds the walls. The prince takes a toll on every boat and every wagon, and the [[affiliation-mrchnthsrvr|Merchant Houses of the Rivers]] pay it as a sum and not by the load, which the port-warden audits and the houses resent.

## Current Situation {#present}

The houses lend against the next harvest, and the prince lives on what they pay in tolls, so each depends on the other and neither trusts it. The prince wants a larger share of the grain trade for his own barges and has begun to buy mills upriver, which the lowland prince reads as a threat. The houses want the port's tolls fixed by contract and resist the prince's habit of raising them in a thin year. The Tarvál Host holds the eastern caravan roads for him and takes its own toll, and the prince pays its ataman more each year to prevent his taking the caravans' side of it. The silver that pays the wardens of the forts of the [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]] leaves his mint each quarter and reaches the forts late by the river road; it is the one payment he makes without bargaining. A port that is the richest place in the land and has the fewest soldiers is a port that the other princes watch.

## History {#history}

The port grew where the three trades met, and the house that holds it has held it since the quays were timber. It has never needed a larger army than its neighbors lent it: the forts of Brevavesh guard its northern river, and the lowland barns feed its quays. The country of the Tarvál Host passed to it from the [[affiliation-prncdmzhtvsh|Princedom of Zhitavesh]] in 697 AF, and it has paid the Host since ([[lore-tarvalturn|The Tarvál Host Changes Sides]]).
