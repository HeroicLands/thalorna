## Overview {#overview}

The merchant houses of the rivers are the firms that move Velanthia's grain, furs and horses: each a family and its ledgers, its boats and its warehouses, and a household that eats at one table. The great houses stand at the port of [[place-brlvsh|Brelavesh]] and the market towns of the grain belt, and they hold more credit than any river-prince holds coin. The Velanthians call a merchant house by one word, and a house's name is its credit.

## What It Controls {#what-it-controls}

The houses own the credit that carries a harvest from the field to the buyer. They lend against the next crop, finance the barges and the fur boats, set the standard weights used at the landings and settle debts between members by a body of their own. They do not own land or garrisons, and they lend to every prince.

## Masters {#masters}

The heads of the great houses sit together as the body's assembly, and the master of the houses, [[being-tshdrslvnch|Teshodar Sulvanich]], presides over it. An arbiter of debts hears disputes between houses, and a keeper of the weights holds the standard against which a landing's scales are tested.

## The Present Quarrel {#present}

The houses of the port and the houses of the grain towns disagree over the price of the coming harvest. The port houses want to cut the credit they extend to the grain towns, since the harvest is uncertain; the grain-town houses say the cut is a way to buy their crop cheap. The princes, who each depend on one side, are drawn into the quarrel and cannot end it, and each hopes the assembly will decide before the rains.
