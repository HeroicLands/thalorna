## Overview {#overview}

**Teshobor** is a pine wood on a slow river in the northern forest belt of [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia]], a day's walk above the last village on the water. Its name is [[skill-velanthlng|Velanthian]], "the quiet pine wood", from _tesh_, "quiet", and _-obor_, "a pine wood". In the middle of the wood the river makes a wide bend around a meadow, and along the bank of the bend stand old willows, far older than willows grow, each bent over toward the water and grown into the next, so that the bank is a row of green arches with the river running past their open sides. The village below mows the meadow at midsummer and keeps away from the willows.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The meadow is the village's hay, by a custom older than any prince's grant of the village's fields. The wood and the willows belong to nobody the village knows of, and no prince has claimed them.

## Layers {#history}

The village says the willows were grown together before its first houses were built, and that whoever bent them did not cut them, because no blade-mark shows on any trunk. Its people mow the meadow, take the hay down the river on rafts, and leave the arches alone, as their grandparents did. The village's priest of the [[affiliation-lizelas|Faith of Lizelas]] says the arches are older than the Aureldian faith in the country, and blesses the hay and not the trees.

## What Is There Now {#present}

A family of newcomers is the trouble. They came up the river from the grain lowlands after a bad harvest and took up a clearing at the edge of the pine wood, and last winter they cut two of the willows for withy and firewood, because they were nearer than any other wood that would burn green. Since then two of the village's hayricks have rotted through, and the headman says the newcomers are the cause and wants them gone before the mowing. The newcomers say the trees are only trees and the ricks rotted because the hay was stacked wet. Both sides have put the matter to the priest, and the priest wants it settled before the mowing by anyone who can say what the willows are.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The northern forest belt is raided from the west, and a raiding party coming down the river in summer looks for meadows and unguarded villages. The river in the bend runs slow and deep, with soft mud under the willow roots that holds a man's legs. The village and the newcomers are close to blows, and the village's young men have been seen at the newcomers' clearing after dark.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A meadow of tall grass in a river bend, closed in by pines on the landward side. Along the bank stand a dozen willows, each trunk as thick as a cart, bent toward the river and joined overhead to the next, so that the row of them makes a long gallery of arches with its floor above the water. The floors of the arches are flat stones laid level and grown over with moss. Two arches at the downstream end stand open to the sky, their trunks cut through at the height of a man's knee, the stumps already putting up green shoots. In the shallows below the middle arch, a rounded stone the size of a sheep stands out of the water with a line of marks cut around it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The priest's question.** The village priest pays whoever can say what the willows are, and whether cutting them spoils the hay, before the mowing.
- **The newcomers' clearing.** The newcomers want someone to stand by them against the village's young men until the priest decides.
- **The stone in the river.** The priest also wants the marks on the stone in the shallows copied and carried to someone who can read them, and pays for a copy that is exact.

# The Truth of Teshobor {#truth .secret}

**The camp.** The bend is **Lantavosto**, "the place of the slow river", a summer camp of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]], abandoned. Senali traveling the river by boat put in here in the warm months, when the water ran low and slow, and slept in the willow arches over the water with their boats drawn up below; willows were their choice because a willow takes root from a cut bough and bends where it is led. Before the autumn rains they went on downstream or home.

**Its leaving.** The river traffic of the Senali ended when they left the open country of the river valleys for the remote forests, long before the village was built. Nothing in the arches harms the village or the newcomers.

**The hay.** The two ricks rotted because the hay in them was stacked wet, as the newcomers say. Cutting the willows does nothing to the meadow. The two cut trees are living still and grow back from the stump within a few summers, faster than any willow grows.

**The stone.** The stone in the shallows is the camp's mooring post, and the line around it is [[skill-clthndscrpt|Veldikko]], naming the place for boats coming up the river. No one in Velanthia reads it.

> [!TIP]
> The priest wants an answer, not the truth. A party that proves the hay was stacked wet settles the quarrel, and one that shows the priest how fast the stumps grow makes the willows a matter for the faith and the prince, not the village.
