## Overview {#overview}

Semukarov is a village of about four hundred fishers and barley farmers at a bend of the river on the Khazrin frontier, one of the oasis-and-river communities that the Velanthian coronation rolls list as Velanthian and the nearest tribe claims as its own. The [[affiliation-sqrtrtrb|Soqritur]] hold it. Its households net the river and fish through the ice in winter, sow barley on the river flats, and keep the river law as they kept it before the tribe came; the tribe's river-warden sits beside the village headman, [[being-byknsmkrv|Beyukan Semukarov]], and takes a share of the fish and grain. The village speaks Velanthian with Sovidur words in it. The tribe trades horses for Velanthian grain and iron here, and the [[affiliation-hstkhrd|Khorad Host]] has told the village to stop paying the tribe. The village answers that it pays for protection.
