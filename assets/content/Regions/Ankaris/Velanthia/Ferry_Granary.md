## Overview {#overview}

The Ferry Granary is a house of the [[affiliation-grnrymnstrs|Granary Monasteries of Lizelas]] on the river that the lowland grain road crosses a day's ride below the foot of the southern range. The Velanthians call a granary and the monastery that keeps it by one word, _zhitnad_, and this is the one that every wagon passes. It has a barn of dressed stone with a roof of oak shingles, a wharf for the ferry, a chapel of the goddess [[lore-lizelasdty|Lizelas]], and the cells and fields of its monks, laborers and ferrymen.

People come for the crossing. A wagon train that reaches the river at dusk can unload under the barn's roof and cross at first light, and a drover can ask the granary-keeper for fodder against the price of the herd. The monks also copy the customary law and teach reading to the sons of the neighboring halls.

## History {#history}

The house began as a ferryman's shed and a shrine of Lizelas on the bank where the first drovers' escorts crossed with grain. Monks of the lowland houses came to keep the shrine's offerings, and the offerings became a barn. The barn grew with the traffic on the road until the house ranked beside the great granary at [[place-zhtvsh|Zhitavesh]].

## Current Situation {#present}

Two men want something from the house that it will not give on its own word. The prince's keeper at Zhitavesh, [Duvek Radovodich]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, wants a count of the barn, because the late rain makes a short harvest likely and the prince cannot pay both his forts and his Hosts from what he can see. [Sovidan Volketich]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the [[affiliation-hstsvrt|Sevret Host]] wants grain advanced against the pay due at harvest, so that his riders can eat while they escort the last wagon trains. The starbrat will give the keeper no count and the ataman no loan on his word alone, and has sent to the abbess of the great granary at Zhitavesh, [Radora Boskvanich]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who mediates between the princes and the Hosts and will hear both men.

## Who Rules It {#who-rules-it}

The house keeps the order of the network: a starbrat chosen by the monks, a granary-keeper who answers for every sack, and a scribe of the house who writes its ledgers. The starbrat answers to no abbot of another house and attends the heads' meeting when the abbess of the great granary calls it.

## Lands and Income {#lands-and-income}

The fields on the river meadows feed the house, and the ferry toll and the stabling of the wagon trains pay for its repairs. Drovers and wagoners leave grain as a gift to the barn in a bad year, and it keeps a ledger of what it lends.

## Visiting the House {#visiting}

The ferry runs from first light until the bell at dusk, and the guest-barn takes any traveler for a night against a bowl's worth of labor or its price in grain. A guest eats at the monks' long table and keeps the hours. Visitors do not enter the barn, and the granary-keeper does not discuss what it holds.

:::secret {title="What the barn holds"}
The barn is nearly empty. The house lent most of its reserve to the merchant houses of the rivers against the next harvest, and it has advanced grain to the Sevret Host on the same terms. The starbrat will not give the keeper a count because the count would show how little a bad year could draw on, and the network's name for holding the land's reserve depends on nobody checking.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The count.** [Duvek Radovodich]{.name data-type="being/npc"} needs a witness he can trust to watch the barn opened, and the starbrat has refused him.
- **The advance.** [Sovidan Volketich]{.name data-type="being/npc"} wants a go-between who can carry his pledge to the abbess at Zhitavesh before the snow closes the pass.
