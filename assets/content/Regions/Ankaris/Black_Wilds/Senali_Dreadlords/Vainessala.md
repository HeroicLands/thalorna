# Appearance {#appearance}

Vainessala stands as tall as any Senalo and as still as stone. No breath moves her, and the cold of her presence reaches you before her eyes do. On her person is a bracelet set with dark ore, and the cold is strongest at it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Vainessala is a Senalo lord of the far north, beyond [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]]. Everything else about her is Game Master material.

## Background

:::secret {title="What the survivors of the north remember"}
Vainessala was a healer of the council of [[place-nuuvakoldi|Nuuvakoldi]], in the compact of the Senali and the Khazari under the council of elders. The black artifact worked slowest on her, and she remembers most of what she was. The black artifact, Naldumervel, warped the councillors' minds, and she is now one of the Dreadlords, the rank by which the living Senali, Khazari and Tantsou understand the lords of the undead. The rank is the living's word: nobody knows what the undead call themselves, or whether they hold ranks at all.

She remembers having been a Senalo. It may mean nothing to her now.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

:::secret {title="The Dreadlord"}
Vainessala is sentient, ancient and a rival of the other Dreadlords. Her portrayal beyond that is the Game Master's.
:::

### Motivation

:::secret {title="The Dreadlord"}
Vainessala serves [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]] because she has no choice. Her hunger, like that of the risen Elder dead, is to destroy all life.
:::

### Strengths

:::secret {title="The Dreadlord"}
Vainessala commands a Host, with the Harrowers and Wights beneath it. A fragment of Naldumervel is set in a bracelet, and only a sentient undead can use a fragment. She can enter the Spirit World at will, as every Senalo can.
:::

### Weaknesses

:::secret {title="The Dreadlord"}
Taking the fragment from a bracelet ends her command. The white artifact nullifies the black on contact, which destroys the undead.
:::

## Social

### Patrons

:::secret {title="The Sovereign and the Host"}

- [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]]: the Sovereign, whose will binds every undead of the city. Everything Vainessala knew passed up to the Sovereign when she became undead, and what she knows of [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]] and of the compact since reached her from others. What she learns now passes up to the Sovereign in turn.
  :::

### Enemies

:::secret {title="The Sovereign and the Host"}

- The other Dreadlords: ancient, sentient rivals, each commanding a Host of their own.
  :::

## Plot Hooks

:::secret {title="Hooks of the north"}

1. **A meeting in the spirit world.** A living Senalo who transitions into the Spirit World within two or three leagues of [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]] may meet Vainessala there. The echo of the Wall keeps the undead Senali local to it.
2. **What a lost party knew.** A party that fails in the north feeds the Host, and everything its members knew passes up to the Sovereign and from the Sovereign to every undead, Vainessala among them. Any password, cipher or plan the party carried is compromised.
3. **The fragment.** The fragment set in a bracelet can be taken, and taking it ends Vainessala's command. A living person who keeps a fragment slowly dies in mind and body, and wakes as a sentient undead.
   :::
