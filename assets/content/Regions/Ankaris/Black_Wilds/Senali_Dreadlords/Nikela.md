# Appearance {#appearance}

Nìkela stands as tall as any Senalo and as still as stone. No breath moves her, and the cold of her presence reaches you before her eyes do. On her person is a collar set with dark ore, and the cold is strongest at it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nìkela is a Senalo lord of the far north, beyond [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]]. Everything else about her is Game Master material.

## Background

:::secret {title="What the survivors of the north remember"}
Nìkela was a councillor of [[place-nuuvakoldi|Nuuvakoldi]] who held the long view, in the compact of the Senali and the Khazari under the council of elders. The black artifact, Naldumervel, warped the councillors' minds, and she is now one of the Dreadlords, the rank by which the living Senali, Khazari and Tantsou understand the lords of the undead. The rank is the living's word: nobody knows what the undead call themselves, or whether they hold ranks at all.

She remembers having been a Senalo. It may mean nothing to her now.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

:::secret {title="The Dreadlord"}
Nìkela is sentient, ancient and a rival of the other Dreadlords. She was a councillor of the long view, and she is patient still. Her portrayal beyond that is the Game Master's.
:::

### Motivation

:::secret {title="The Dreadlord"}
Nìkela serves [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]] because she has no choice. Her hunger, like that of the risen Elder dead, is to destroy all life.
:::

### Strengths

:::secret {title="The Dreadlord"}
Nìkela commands a Host, with the captains and the lesser undead beneath it. A fragment of Naldumervel is set in a collar, and only a sentient undead can use a fragment. She can enter the Spirit World at will, as every Senalo can.
:::

### Weaknesses

:::secret {title="The Dreadlord"}
Taking the fragment from a collar ends her command. The white artifact nullifies the black on contact, which destroys the undead.
:::

## Social

### Patrons

:::secret {title="The Sovereign and the Host"}

- [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]]: the Sovereign, whose will binds every undead of the city. Everything Nìkela knew passed up to the Sovereign when she became undead, and what she knows of [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]] and of the compact since reached her from others. What she learns now passes up to the Sovereign in turn.
  :::

### Enemies

:::secret {title="The Sovereign and the Host"}

- The other Dreadlords: ancient, sentient rivals, each commanding a Host of their own.
  :::

## Plot Hooks

:::secret {title="Hooks of the north"}

1. **A meeting in the spirit world.** A living Senalo who transitions into the Spirit World within two or three leagues of [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]] may meet Nìkela there. The echo of the Wall keeps the undead Senali local to it.
2. **What a lost party knew.** A party that fails in the north feeds the Host, and everything its members knew passes up to the Sovereign and from the Sovereign to every undead, Nìkela among them. Any password, cipher or plan the party carried is compromised.
3. **The fragment.** The fragment set in a collar can be taken, and taking it ends Nìkela's command. A living person who keeps a fragment slowly dies in mind and body, and wakes as a sentient undead.
   :::
