# Appearance {#appearance}

Ellantetti stands as tall as any Senalo and as still as stone. No breath moves him, and the cold of his presence reaches you before his eyes do. On his person is a ring set with dark ore, and the cold is strongest at it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ellantetti is a Senalo lord of the far north, beyond [[place-tantsouwall|the Palewall]]. Everything else about him is Game Master material.

## Background

:::secret {title="What the survivors of the north remember"}
Ellantetti was the loremaster and singer of the council of [[place-nuuvakoldi|Nuuvakoldi]], in the compact of the Senali and the Khazari under the council of elders. The black artifact, Naldumervel, warped the councillors' minds, and he is now one of the Dreadlords, the rank by which the living Senali, Khazari and Tantsou understand the lords of the undead. The rank is the living's word: nobody knows what the undead call themselves, or whether they hold ranks at all.

He remembers having been a Senalo. It may mean nothing to him now.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

:::secret {title="The Dreadlord"}
Ellantetti is sentient, ancient and a rival of the other Dreadlords. His portrayal beyond that is the Game Master's.
:::

### Motivation

:::secret {title="The Dreadlord"}
Ellantetti serves [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]] because he has no choice. His hunger, like that of the risen Elder dead, is to destroy all life.
:::

### Strengths

:::secret {title="The Dreadlord"}
Ellantetti commands a Host, with the captains and the lesser undead beneath it. A fragment of Naldumervel is set in a ring, and only a sentient undead can use a fragment. He can enter the Spirit World at will, as every Senalo can.
:::

### Weaknesses

:::secret {title="The Dreadlord"}
Taking the fragment from a ring ends his command. The white artifact nullifies the black on contact, which destroys the undead.
:::

## Social

### Patrons

:::secret {title="The Sovereign and the Host"}

- [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]]: the Sovereign, whose will binds every undead of the city. Everything Ellantetti knew passed up to the Sovereign when he became undead, and what he knows of [[place-tantsouwall|the Palewall]] and of the compact since reached him from others. What he learns now passes up to the Sovereign in turn.
  :::

### Enemies

:::secret {title="The Sovereign and the Host"}

- The other Dreadlords: ancient, sentient rivals, each commanding a Host of their own.
  :::

## Plot Hooks

:::secret {title="Hooks of the north"}

1. **A meeting in the spirit world.** A living Senalo who transitions into the Spirit World within two or three leagues of [[place-tantsouwall|the Palewall]] may meet Ellantetti there. The echo of the Wall keeps the undead Senali local to it.
2. **What a lost party knew.** A party that fails in the north feeds the Host, and everything its members knew passes up to the Sovereign and from the Sovereign to every undead, Ellantetti among them. Any password, cipher or plan the party carried is compromised.
3. **The fragment.** The fragment set in a ring can be taken, and taking it ends Ellantetti's command. A living person who keeps a fragment slowly dies in mind and body, and wakes as a sentient undead.
   :::
