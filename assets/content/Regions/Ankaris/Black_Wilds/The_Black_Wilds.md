## Overview {#overview}

The Black Wilds, _Hwenhatou_ in Tantsou, are the steppe and broken hill-country north of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], beyond the fortified marches of [[place-kwozhngmrch|the Kwozheng Marches]]. The Tantsou name joins _hwen_, disorder, _ha_, dark, and _tou_, earth: the dark and disordered land. This generation one man commands their tribes, and his riders are in the empire's northern districts.

## The Land {#the-land}

North of the last watchtowers the hard-wintered basins of the [[place-tantsouregin|Tantsou Region]] give way to grass and broken hills. The winters are hard. Nests of the far-northern [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] lie in the wilds as well.

## Peoples {#peoples}

The people of the wilds are the [[lore-henhatoutsouclt|Hwenhatoutsou]], the Tribes of the Black Wilds: horse-riders who live from grazing. Their quarrels are over grazing grounds, wells and blood debts.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

No register of the empire covers the wilds. The tribes swear to [[being-wogenhenzhuo|Wogen Henzhwo]], the Master Tiger of the Steppes, as their single war-leader, in the [Hwenhatou confederation]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}. He began as the war-leader of a minor tribe and bound the others to him by marriage, by treaty and by slaughter. The confederation is held together by Wogen himself and by plunder.

## Current Situation {#present}

Hard winters and failing grazing have driven the tribes south, and Wogen's riders raid the empire's northern districts. They ride in disciplined companies, strike supply lines and outposts rather than towns, and withdraw before an imperial army can force a battle. The empire treats Grukar raids as a nuisance; the Black Wilds under Wogen are another matter.

:::secret {title="What the Master Tiger wants"}
Wogen wants land and grain for the tribes first, and after that a throne: he means to rule a piece of the empire, and he studies its registers and castes as carefully as its forts. The campaigns he loses are the ones he meant to lose. His envoys are seeking out exiled [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] and rebel factions with offers of places in the realm he means to carve from the north, and one of them has approached the rebel general [[being-tuijawengyan|Tuija Wengyan]]. A season without victories, or a blow to Wogen himself, would set the tribes at each other's throats again, and every chief he subdued remembers how.
:::

## Roads and Trade {#roads-and-trade}

Wogen talks in the clipped trade-speech of the frontier markets; the notes name no road or market inside the wilds.

## Dangers {#dangers}

Wogen's riders in the northern districts, and the raids of the Grukar nests.

:::secret {title="What the notes leave open"}
How far the wilds run to the north and west, how many people they hold, the names of the tribes, their chiefs, wells and camps, and where Wogen keeps his own camp are the game master's choice. So is whether the wilds reach the Grukar country of [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm]]; the notes state only the border with Tantsou.
:::

## History {#history}

For generations the tribes of the wilds fought one another over grazing, wells and blood debts, and the empire's border garrisons kept them divided with gifts to one chief and raids against another. Wogen Henzhwo bound them together while infighting among the Wohou lords of the north thinned the frontier garrisons and the court looked to its own intrigues. A run of hard winters and failing grazing then drove the united tribes south against the marches.
