# Appearance {#appearance}

A tall Senali stands before you, and a dark fragment of ore is set into his brow. He speaks, and the sounds come in pieces that do not join into sense.

# Dossier {#dossier}

> **GM-only note.** The living know a title and a rumor. Who he is and what rules him are for the GM.

Nalduvorkas is the ruler of the dead of the far north and is called by his title alone. The Senali call him Nalduvorkas, the dark master. The Khazari call him Hadar dhamív, which means the same. The court of Tantsou calls him Ha Chen, the dark king, a court term the people do not use. Each is the living peoples' word for what they understand of him. Nobody knows what the dead call him.

He is one individual. His title is not an office and not a rank, and it stands in no list beside the Dreadlords, who are the commanders under him.

## Background

:::secret {title="What the Senali and Khazari know"}
Nalduvorkas is the old head of the council of [[place-nuuvakoldi|Nuuvakoldi]]: a Senali, aloof and powerful in magic, who led the compact that joined the Senali and the Khazari. He is barely comprehensible now and is not the person he was. All the undead of the city are bound to his will.
:::

:::secret {title="The Prime fragment"}
The Prime fragment of the black artifact, which the Senali call Naldumervel, the Khazari Dhamv-vulád and the court of Tantsou Hagau, is embedded in his brow. He is the only one with a fragment set into him; every other fragment sits in an item. The black artifact controls him, and nobody realizes it.

A fragment in a living person's keeping slowly kills the person in mind and body until the person wakes as sentient undead, and only a sentient undead can use a fragment.
:::

:::secret {title="Everything the dead knew"}
Whenever anyone becomes undead, all of their knowledge goes to their master, and what matters travels up the chain to him. Every death near the horde carries something to him, and each of the four human champions who became Dreadlords carried the secrets of the realm. He knows where the white artifact lies, how it is protected, and the passwords and ciphers in use when the last person who knew them died. What he knows is as old as that last death, and information that has changed since is information he lacks.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

He has no personality the living can reach. His speech does not hold together, and nothing in his manner answers to what is said to him.

### Motivation

The living can say nothing of what he wants.

:::secret {title="What he wants"}
The hunger of the dead is to destroy all life. He is constantly seeking a way to take the white artifact, Hwomamervel to the Senali, Malz-bugán to the Khazari and Bwigau at the court of Tantsou, because its essence in the Wall keeps the horde at bay. If the lords ever held it and returned it to the north, the Wall could be neutralized.
:::

### Strengths

Every undead of the city is bound to his will, and the Dreadlords each command a Host.

:::secret {title="What he commands"}
The Dreadlords serve him because they have no choice. Removing a Dreadlord's fragment ends that lord's command, and the fragment in his own brow is the Prime fragment. Because the undead cannot go where the white artifact lies, the living agents of the Sovereign, the [[affiliation-hazhao|Shadow Sect]] among them, are the means by which he acts on what the dead know.
:::

### Weaknesses

He cannot pass the Wall, and he cannot take the white artifact: it nullifies the black on contact, which destroys the undead.

## Plot Hooks

The Title in the Sleeper's Mouth
: A soldier back from beyond the Wall is battle-worn and sleeps badly, and the title he cries out in his sleep is a court term. Nobody at home believes his account of the walking dead, and the party must find where he heard it.

:::secret {title="The Sovereign's Agents"}
The Hand That Acts
: The dead cannot go where the white artifact lies, so the Sovereign's living agents act for him. A Shadow Sect cell that moves on what only the dead could know has used a secret that was lost with someone who died near the horde.
:::

:::secret {title="The Lost Expedition"}
What the Party Knew
: A party sent north fails, and its failure adds to the army of the damned. What each member knew has reached the Sovereign, and what he learned from them bears on the next party that sets out.
:::
