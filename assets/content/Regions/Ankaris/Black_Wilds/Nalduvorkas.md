# Appearance {#appearance}

A tall Senalo stands before you, and a dark fragment of ore is set into his brow. He speaks, and the sounds come in pieces that do not join into sense.

# Dossier {#dossier}

> **GM-only note.** The living know a title and a rumor. Who he is and what rules him are for the GM.

Nalduvorkas is the ruler of the dead of the far north and is called by his title alone. The Senali call him Nalduvorkas, the dark master. The Khazari call him Hadar dhamív, which means the same. The court of Tantsou calls him Ha Chen, the dark king, a court term the people do not use. Each is the living peoples' word for what they understand of him. Nobody knows what the dead call him.

He is one individual. His title is not an office and not a rank, and it stands in no list beside the Dreadlords, who are the commanders under him.

## Background

:::secret {title="What the Senali and Khazari know"}
Nalduvorkas is the old head of the council of [[place-nuuvakoldi|Nuuvakoldi]]: a Senali, aloof and powerful in magic, who led the compact that joined the Senali and the Khazari. He came to the head of the council by competence and, not least, by politics; nothing in his birth set him there. His highly attuned connection to magic left him particularly vulnerable to the black artifact, and the council's decision to keep it in its own chambers was the artifact's work. He is barely comprehensible now and is not the person he was. All the undead of the city are bound to his will.
:::

:::secret {title="His old name"}
Before he became the Sovereign he was Sìlketti, the name he was born with. The survivors of Nuuvakoldi know it and do not use it for what he has become, and the Khazari keep it from the humans. A party that learns it learns that the ruler of the north is no stranger but the former head of the council.
:::

:::secret {title="The Prime fragment"}
The Prime fragment of the black artifact, which the Senali call Naldumervel, the Khazari Dhamv-vulád and the court of Tantsou Hagau, is embedded in his brow. He is the only one with a fragment set into him; every other fragment sits in an item. The black artifact controls him, and nobody realizes it.

A fragment in a living person's keeping slowly kills the person in mind and body until the person wakes as sentient undead, and only a sentient undead can use a fragment.
:::

:::secret {title="Everything the dead knew"}
Whenever anyone becomes undead, all of their knowledge goes to their master, and what matters travels up the chain to him. Every death near the horde carries something to him, and each of the four human champions who became Dreadlords carried the secrets of the realm. He knows where the white artifact lies, how it is protected, and the passwords and ciphers in use when the last person who knew them died. He knows both prophecies through [[being-shouzhiwengmei|Shou Zhiwengmei]], and every step that was then known toward the White Prophecy. What he knows is as old as that last death, and information that has changed since is information he lacks.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

He has no personality the living can reach. His speech does not hold together, and nothing in his manner answers to what is said to him.

### Motivation

The living can say nothing of what he wants.

:::secret {title="What he wants"}
The hunger of the dead is to destroy all life. He is constantly seeking a way to take the white artifact, Hwomamervel to the Senali, Malz-bugán to the Khazari and Bwigau at the court of Tantsou, because its essence in the Wall keeps the horde at bay. If the lords ever held it and returned it to the north, the Wall could be neutralized.
:::

### Strengths

The Dreadlords each command a Host.

:::secret {title="What he commands"}
Every undead of the city is bound to his will. The Dreadlords serve him because they have no choice. Removing a Dreadlord's fragment ends that lord's command, and the fragment in his own brow is the Prime fragment. Because the undead cannot go where the white artifact lies, the living agents of the Sovereign, the [[affiliation-hazhao|The Shadow Sect]] among them, are the means by which he acts on what the dead know.
:::

### Weaknesses

He cannot pass the Wall.

:::secret {title="Why he cannot take it"}
He cannot take the white artifact: it nullifies the black on contact, which destroys the undead.
:::

:::secret {title="Running water"}
He is one of the black artifact's dead, made by it directly or indirectly, as are the Dreadlords and every undead under them, and like them he cannot cross a river deeper than his midsection without a ship, a bridge or the like, and the water slows him and makes him clumsier even then. A creek, or water no higher than his shins, stops him not at all. He cannot swim: he sinks to the bottom and the current carries him away. A lake or a sea leaves him slumped as if dead until he is moved onto land, where he soon recovers and rises. This is the tactic most used against the dead. The rule covers only the black artifact's dead and says nothing of other undead in the setting, which may or may not be sensitive to water.
:::
