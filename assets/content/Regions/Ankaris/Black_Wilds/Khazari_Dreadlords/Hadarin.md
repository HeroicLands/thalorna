# Appearance {#appearance}

Hadarin was a Khazár man, short and broad in the build of his people and bearded as every adult Khazár is.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**A councillor of [[place-nuuvakoldi|Nuuvakoldi]], a Khagalir, a Dreadlord, destroyed in the battles.**

Hadarin was of Clan [Gullád]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}. His given name is built on a master. The Khazari word for what the living understood him to be is _Khagalir_, "one of dread"; the garrisons of [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]] say _Chin Zhwo_. Both are the living peoples' terms. Nobody knows what the undead call themselves, or whether they have ranks at all.

## Background

The compact that joined the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] and the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] was governed by a council of elders: an aloof Senali head, now the Sovereign, and twelve members split between the two peoples, six Senali and six Khazari. Hadarin sat among the Khazari of the council.

A traveler from the north gave the council a large artifact of unknown ore, which the Khazari now call _Dhamv-vulád_ and the Senali _Naldumervel_. The council kept it in its own custody. Its members spent more and more time alone with it, and their minds were warped. Then the council became undead and killed hundreds in the city, and the dead rose within moments to kill others.

Hadarin was one of the twelve original elders who became Dreadlords, and one of the four who were destroyed over the millennia in untold battles. Human champions of Tantsou replaced the four. The Dreadlords were sentient, ancient and rivals of one another.

Everything Hadarin knew as a councillor passed to the Sovereign when he rose, and nothing that came after his destruction takes it back. His knowledge dated from before the Wall.

## Psyche

### Personality

Hadarin was sentient and ancient. A risen Elder remembers having been Senali or Khazár, and whether it meant anything to him is not known.

### Motivation

The risen Elder dead hunger to destroy all life. Hadarin could not refuse the Sovereign.
