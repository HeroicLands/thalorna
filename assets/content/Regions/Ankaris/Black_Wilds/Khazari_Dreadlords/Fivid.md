# Appearance {#appearance}

Fivid is a Khazár woman, short and broad in the build of her people and bearded as every adult Khazár is. She wears a bracelet on one wrist.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Archivist of the council of [[place-nuuvakoldi|Nuuvakoldi]], now a Khagalir, a Dreadlord. Commands a Host and serves the [[being-nalduvorkas|Sovereign]] because she has no choice.**

Fivid is of Clan [Lummáv]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}. Her given name is built on warranting a claim. The Khazari word for what the living now understand her to be is _Khagalir_, "one of dread"; the garrisons of [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]] say _Chin Zhwo_. Both are the living peoples' terms. Nobody knows what the undead call themselves, or whether they have ranks at all.

## Background

The compact that joined the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] and the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] was governed by a council of elders: an aloof Senali head, now the Sovereign, and twelve members split between the two peoples, six Senali and six Khazari. Fivid was the council's archivist.

A traveler from the north gave the council a large artifact of unknown ore, which the Khazari now call _Dhamv-vulád_ and the Senali _Naldumervel_. The council kept it in its own custody. Its members spent more and more time alone with it, and their minds were warped. Then the council became undead and killed hundreds in the city, and the dead rose within moments to kill others.

Fivid is one of the eight original elders who survive as Dreadlords: five Senali and three Khazari. Four of the twelve lords have been destroyed and replaced by human champions of Tantsou. The Dreadlords are sentient, ancient and rivals of one another.

A fragment of the black artifact is set in her bracelet. A fragment kept by a living person slowly kills the keeper in mind and body until the keeper wakes as a sentient undead, and only a sentient undead can use one. Fivid commands a Host through it. The Khazari have no word for a Host and name a body by its commander. **Removing the fragment from the bracelet ends her command.**

Everything Fivid knew as the council's archivist passed to the Sovereign when she rose, and what any lost person has known since passes up from the dead to her in turn. What she holds of her own is the council's knowledge from before the Wall; what she knows of the Wall reached her from others who died after.

## Psyche

### Personality

Fivid is sentient and ancient. She remembers having been a Khazár and the council's archivist, and whether it means anything to her now is not known.

### Motivation

Her hunger is to destroy all life. She cannot refuse the Sovereign.

### Strengths

She commands a Host and, through the Harrowers (_Gazadir_) and Wights (_Mankir_) beneath her, its Legions and Cohorts. She holds the knowledge of the council's records from before the Wall.

### Weaknesses

Her command rests on the fragment in her bracelet and ends when it is removed. The white artifact in the Wall, _Malz-bugán_ to the Khazari, nullifies the black on contact, which destroys the undead.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **The Sovereign**: [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]], her master. All the undead of the city are bound to his will.
- **The Dreadlords**: Her fellow Khazari lords are [[being-gatharan|Gatharan]] and [[being-nagaran|Nagaran]]. The fallen Khazari lords are [[being-zuthuv|Zuthuv]], [[being-hadarin|Hadarin]] and [[being-dhalaz|Dhalaz]].

## Plot Hooks

1. **The bracelet.** A party that learns which wrist wears the bracelet holds the one way to end her command, and has to reach her through her Host to use it.
2. **What the archive knew.** The council's records passed to the Sovereign with her, so any secret the council kept before the Wall is no secret to the north.
3. **The expedition that failed.** A party lost in the north tells her Host what it knew, and any plan, password or cipher it carried is hers to act on.
