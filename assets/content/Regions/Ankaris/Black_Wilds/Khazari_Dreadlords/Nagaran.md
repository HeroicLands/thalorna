# Appearance {#appearance}

Nagaran is a dead Khazár, short and broad in the build of his people and bearded as every adult Khazár was, and as still as stone. No breath moves him, and the cold of his presence reaches you before his eyes do. On his person is a headband set with dark ore, and the cold is strongest at it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Guardian of the gates of [[place-nuuvakoldi|Nuuvakoldi]], now a Khagalir, a Dreadlord. Commands a Host and serves the Sovereign, [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]], because he has no choice.**

Nagaran is of Clan [Gurráz]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}. His given name is built on watching the approaches. The Khazari word for what the living now understand him to be is _Khagalir_, "one of dread"; the garrisons of [[place-tantsouwall|the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]] say _Chin Zhwo_. Both are the living peoples' terms. Nobody knows what the undead call themselves, or whether they have ranks at all.

## Background

The compact that joined the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] and the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] was governed by a council of elders: an aloof Senali head, now the Sovereign, and twelve members split between the two peoples, six Senali and six Khazari. Nagaran was the guardian of the gates.

A traveler from the north gave the council a large artifact of unknown ore, which the Khazari now call _Dhamv-vulád_ and the Senali _Naldumervel_. The council kept it in its own custody. Its members spent more and more time alone with it, and their minds were warped. Then the council became undead and killed hundreds in the city, and the dead rose within moments to kill others.

Nagaran is one of the eight original elders who survive as Dreadlords: five Senali and three Khazari. Four of the twelve lords have been destroyed and replaced by human champions of Tantsou. The Dreadlords are sentient, ancient and rivals of one another.

A fragment of the black artifact is set in his headband. A fragment kept by a living person slowly kills the keeper in mind and body until the keeper wakes as a sentient undead, and only a sentient undead can use one. Nagaran commands a Host through it. The Khazari have no word for a Host and name a body by its commander. **Removing the fragment from the headband ends his command.**

Everything Nagaran knew as the guardian of the gates passed to the Sovereign when he rose, and what any lost person has known since passes up from the dead to him in turn. What he holds of his own is the council's knowledge from before the Wall; what he knows of the Wall reached him from others who died after.

## Psyche

### Personality

Nagaran is sentient and ancient. He remembers having been a Khazár and the guardian of the gates, and whether it means anything to him now is not known.

### Motivation

His hunger is to destroy all life. He cannot refuse the Sovereign.

### Strengths

He commands his host and, through the Harrowers (_Gazadir_) and the empty ones (_Mankir_) beneath him, directs it. He holds the knowledge of the approaches and gates of the city from before the Wall.

### Weaknesses

His command rests on the fragment in his headband and ends when it is removed. The white artifact in the Wall, _Malz-bugán_ to the Khazari, nullifies the black on contact, which destroys the undead.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **The Sovereign**: [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]], his master. All the undead of the city are bound to his will.
- **The Dreadlords**: His fellow Khazari lords are [[being-gatharan|Gatharan]] and [[being-fivid|Fivid]]. The fallen Khazari lords are [[being-zuthuv|Zuthuv]], [[being-hadarin|Hadarin]] and [[being-dhalaz|Dhalaz]].

## Plot Hooks

1. **The headband.** A party that learns what the headband holds has the one way to end his command, and has to reach him through his Host to use it.
2. **The way in.** What Nagaran knew of the gates and approaches of Nuuvakoldi passed to the Sovereign, so a party that plans to enter the city by them plans against a lord who watched them.
3. **The expedition that failed.** A party lost in the north tells his Host what it knew, and any plan, password or cipher it carried is his to act on.
