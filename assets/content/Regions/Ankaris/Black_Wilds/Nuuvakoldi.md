> **GM-only note.** The Wall's soldiers know that the dead are restless in the north. What holds the city, and how it came to hold it, is for the GM.

## Overview {#overview}

**Nuuvakoldi** is the name of a city in the far north. The name is Senali and means the Starhall; every other tongue uses it as a loanword and has no word of its own for the city. Beyond the [[place-tantsouwall|Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires]] the land works against travelers: trees that seem hostile, twisted beasts, and creatures that are plainly dead. Soldiers who have served on the Wall for a year or more know that the dead are restless there. Ordinary people hear the same account as a tall tale told by men who came home from the north not quite right.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

:::secret {title="The seat of the Sovereign"}
The dead hold Nuuvakoldi, and every one of them is bound to the will of [[being-nalduvorkas|Nalduvorkas]], the Sovereign, whose seat the city is. Each of the twelve living Dreadlords commands a Host: [[being-ellantetti|Ellantetti]], [[being-vainessala|Vainessala]], [[being-rummatti|Rummatti]], [[being-nikela|Nìkela]], [[being-terkatti|Terkatti]], [[being-gatharan|Gatharan]], [[being-fivid|Fivid]], [[being-nagaran|Nagaran]], [[being-doutuijingwo|Dou Tuijingwo]], [[being-kwodaurauzho|Kwo Daurauzho]], [[being-leiweiyanleng|Lei Weiyanleng]] and [[being-shouzhiwengmei|Shou Zhiwengmei]]. Dreadlord, Harrower, Wight and the units of a Host are the living peoples' terms for what they have seen and understood of the dead. Nobody knows what the dead call themselves, or whether they hold ranks at all.
:::

## Layers {#history}

:::secret {title="What the Senali and Khazari remember"}
Nuuvakoldi was the city of the council. A compact joined the Senali and the Khazari under a council of elders: a Senali head, aloof and powerful in magic, and twelve members split between the two peoples. A well-spoken traveler from the north gave the council a large artifact of unknown ore, the black artifact, which the Senali call Naldumervel, the Khazari call Dhamv-vulád and the court of Tantsou calls Hagau. The council kept it in its own custody, and the councillors spent more and more time alone with it, until their minds were warped.

The council became undead and killed hundreds. The dead rose within moments to kill others, and a fifth of the people survived. The city became the city of the Damned. The survivors fled south, and the Senali and Khazari among them raised the Wall of the Ten Thousand Watchfires against the horde that followed.
:::

## What Is There Now {#present}

:::secret {title="The city today"}
The city is the seat of the Sovereign and the dead of the city answer to him. The land around it feeds the horde: failed expeditions add to the army of the damned, and what each lost person knew passes up through the dead to the Sovereign. The layout of the city, its districts and its buildings are not set down, and the GM decides them.
:::
