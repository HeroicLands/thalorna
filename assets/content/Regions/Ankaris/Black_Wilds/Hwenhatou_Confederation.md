## Overview {#overview}

The Hwenhatou Confederation is the union of the steppe tribes of [[place-blackwilds|the Black Wilds]], the [[lore-henhatoutsouclt|Hwenhatoutsou]], under one man. The tribes swear to [[being-wogenhenzhuo|Wogen Henzhwo]], the Master Tiger of the Steppes, as their single war-leader, and his riders are in the northern districts of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]. In Tantsou the wilds are called _Hwenhatou_, the dark and disordered land.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The confederation is Wogen. He began as the war-leader of a minor tribe and bound the other tribes to him one at a time: by marriage where a chief had a daughter, by treaty where a chief had sense, and by slaughter where he had neither. The chiefs he bound keep their tribes and answer to him. He listens far more than he speaks, sits his horse a little apart from his captains and lets them do the talking, and keeps his promises to allies and enemies alike. What holds the tribes together is Wogen himself and the plunder he brings them.

## Current Situation {#present}

Hard winters and failing grazing have driven the tribes south, and Wogen's riders raid the northern districts of [[affiliation-kwozhengprv|Kwozheng Province]] from beyond the fortified marches of [[place-kwozhngmrch|the Kwozheng Marches]]. The empire treats Grukar raids as a nuisance; the Black Wilds under Wogen are another matter.

:::secret {title="What the Master Tiger wants"}
Wogen wants land and grain for the tribes first, and after that a throne. The campaigns he loses are the ones he meant to lose.

A season without victories, or a blow to Wogen himself, would set the tribes at each other's throats again, and every chief he subdued remembers how. His envoys are seeking out exiled [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] and rebel factions with offers of places in the realm he means to carve from the north, and one of them has approached the rebel general [[being-tuijawengyan|Tuija Wengyan]].
:::

## Arms {#arms}

The confederation's strength is its horsemen. Wogen has turned raiders into soldiers: they ride in disciplined companies, strike supply lines and outposts rather than towns, and withdraw before an imperial army can force a battle.

## Holdings {#holdings}

The confederation keeps no seat. Its ground is the grazing and wells of the Black Wilds, and its riders range into the northern districts of the empire. No register of the empire covers the wilds, so how many people the confederation holds is unknown.

## History {#history}

For generations the tribes of the wilds fought one another over grazing, wells and blood debts, and the empire's border garrisons kept them divided with gifts to one chief and raids against another. Wogen Henzhwo bound them to him while infighting among the Wohou lords of the north thinned the frontier garrisons and the court looked to its own intrigues, and by the time a Prince's report reached the capital, the tribes answered to one man. A run of hard winters and failing grazing then drove the united tribes south against the marches ([[lore-wogenunion|Wogen's Union of the Black Wilds]]).

## Relations {#relations}

The confederation is at war with the Empire of Tantsou along its northern frontier. [[being-tuizhokwzng|Tuizho]], the Prince of Kwozheng, whose province the riders raid, would win the court's favor by bringing back Wogen's head.
