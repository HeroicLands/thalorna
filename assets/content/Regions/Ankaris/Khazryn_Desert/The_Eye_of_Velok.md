## Overview {#overview}

The Eye of Velok is the relic of [[lore-ustarondty|Ústaron]] that [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] has sent its knights into the [[place-hekardesert|Desert of Hek'ar]] to find. The faith describes it as a gem holding the essence of a fire demon bound to its god, and teaches that it lies in the [[place-ruinsarkor|Ruins of Arkor]], the buried tower city the tribes call Bolid. Nobody outside the faith claims to have seen it, and the tribes of the [[place-wlqtrrgn|Volketor]] call it a rumor.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody is known to. The [Blazing Oath]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"}, a faction within the Black Flame's [Servants of Vezhnurikos]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"}, digs for it from a camp beside Arkor's tower under [[being-drzhvzhnr|Drazha Vezhnur]], and kills anything that approaches the camp without the order's badge. Rival factions of the faith, among them the order called [Nakarys the Scourge]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"}, dig in the same sand. The knights have gone down into the buried streets more than once and lost men in the dark each time, and none of them has brought the Eye out.

## Its Story {#its-story}

The Black Flame teaches that Arkor was a seat of the fire demon whose essence the Eye holds, and that the desert buried the city to hide that fact. The tribes hold that the sand is a patient animal that took Arkor in its sleep. Neither reading is confirmed. What is known of the city settles neither: it was the westernmost of [[lore-towercities|the Tower Cities]], the league that lit a tower in each city of the first road across the waste from about 1000 BF until about 190 BF; its tower burned every night for eight centuries; and the sand buried it quietly rather than an army sacking it. The league left no tablet in Arkor and no name for anything in it.

The Servants of Vezhnurikos crossed into the Hek'ar for years on punitive raids before they had this reason. The Blazing Oath was raised among them to recover the relics attributed to Ústaron, and Drazha Vezhnur, who made his name leading the raids, was given the Eye to find.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

The Black Flame holds that the bearer of the Eye can command fire on a great scale, and Drazha Vezhnur believes it could bring about the world the faith foresees, engulfed in flame. Nothing done with it is on record. The tribes say only that they keep clear of the tower and of the shallow bowl at its foot where the sand is oddly cold, and give no reason.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **Drazha Vezhnur** wants it out of the buried city before a rival faction reaches it, as the proof of his faith and of his command.
- **High Blazewarden [Hadrik]{.name data-type="being/npc"}**, who leads the Servants of Vezhnurikos, hopes the search either raises Drazha or ends him, and is content with either.
- **[[being-zekaztrosusk|Sir Zekáz Trosuskar]]**, once of the Blazing Oath and now of Nakarys the Scourge, wants the Eye kept from his old order at least as much as he wants it for his new one.
- **The tribes of the Volketor** want nothing from it. They sell passage, water and riders to every faction at once.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- Drazha Vezhnur hires foreign swords for a descent into the buried streets under the tower, and the knights who went down last time speak of ancient traps, some of them webs ([[place-ruinsarkor|Ruins of Arkor]]).
- A knight of Nakarys the Scourge pays a party to reach the Eye first, or failing that to see that the Blazing Oath never carries it out of the Hek'ar.
- A factor at [[place-qisomrktcmp|Qiiso]] buys a lens of tower-glass from Arkor's lamp-chamber, and a knight of the Oath arrives the same week to ask where it came from.

:::secret {title="What the Eye is"}
The Eye is real, and it is no gem. It is the great lens of Arkor's lamp-chamber, a disc of the league's clear tower-glass as broad as a shield, ground to throw the city's light two nights' march across the waste. When the sand began to take the city, the league's last keepers lifted it down and sealed it in the strongroom under the tower's foot, under the league's seal and behind a guardian of the kind that still walks the counting-houses of [[place-wilud|Vulud]]. A whole league seal pressed on the strongroom door lets a bearer past the guardian; the Black Flame has none and does not know one is needed.

Nothing is bound in it. Set in full sun, it gathers the light to a point that kindles wood and cloth at a bowshot and blisters skin farther off. Set at night with a lamp behind it, it throws a beam a caravan can steer by from the next well. The Black Flame's demon is the story of that light, told for centuries by people who never saw the tower lit.
:::
