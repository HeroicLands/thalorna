The steppe tribes count years from the last great union. A rider asked how old a well-bond is answers with the banner it was sworn under: "the ninth year of the Silent Market." The present year is the hundred and seventy-third of the **Silent Market**, written 173 SM, and the next union that rises will end the count and begin another from its first day.

No tribe keeps a count of its own. Each [[skill-sowideslng|Sowides]] tribe is independent, with its own dowek, its own wells and its own grazing, and the only thing the tribes of the whole steppe share is the memory of the three times a single will forced them together. An orqwen is a rare dictator who does that, and the union he or she makes, the orqwenoq, falls apart on the day the orqwen dies. The three great ones are the reckoning's eras. The count therefore resets at each union: a year is numbered inside the banner it was lived under, and nobody adds the banners together.

## The Year

The year is four seasons, and each season is also a month. The tribes divide the year no finer, because the work of the steppe does not: grass, heat, wind and cold decide where the herds are and which wells are open, and a day is named by how far into the season it falls. "The forty-fifth of Orkes" is the forty-fifth day of the heat. The year opens with the spring grass, on the same morning the [[lore-commoncal|Vylarian Calendar]] calls 1 Floralis, and its length is the world's three hundred and sixty-five days.

- **Welqat**, the grass, ninety-one days. The spring rain falls on the north and the western margin, the herds fatten and the tribes move onto the high grazing. The late storms that bury a camp in an hour come before it ends.
- **Orkes**, the heat, ninety-one days. The wells drop, the tribes draw in to the water they own, and the caravans that dare the crossing march by night.
- **Ruweq**, the wind, ninety-one days. The heat breaks, the autumn markets are held, and the cold winds that close the northern and eastern stretches begin before the season ends.
- **Wàtri**, the cold, ninety-two days. The tribes winter at the stone wells. The year closes on the day before the grass returns.

## The Eras

Each era opens with a union and ends when the next one opens. Between a union's rise and its fall the dates run on in the era's own count, and the era's name is what the tribes remember the orqwen for.

### Before the Lantern Union (BLU)

The years before the first union are counted backward from it, and the tribes tell almost nothing of them. The tribes were the same independent herders then, and the [[lore-towercities|Tower Cities]] strung their league along the first road across the waste. No tribe's tale reaches back past the first banner, so a date in this count is an outsider's reckoning.

### The Lantern Union (LU), from about 190 BF

The first orqwen forced the tribes together and put the same demand to every city of the Tower League: pay tribute for the road. The cities refused, as their charter required, and the host took them one after another. The chief city of the league, [[place-jilaq|Jilaq]], held a season and fell in a day and a night. The union broke up when its orqwen died, and the tribes went back to their wells.

What the era left is a road with no cities on it. The light-towers still stand along it, the league's wells passed to the tribes who now sell water from them, and the dead of Jilaq are a thin blood-field that the road bends three days to avoid. The era runs five hundred and one years before the next banner rises. Its history is told in [[lore-frstorqwnq|The First Orqwenoq]].

### The Hundred Banners (HB), from about 312 AF

The second orqwen came to [[place-wemaq|Wemaq]] at the head of the tribes already sworn to him and killed the last tribe that refused his banner. Three years later the union went round the western end of the Grazian wall and became [[lore-hndrdbnnrs|the Storm of the Hundred Banners]]. It broke up over the winter its orqwen died, after it had reached the walls of Amradad.

The era left the green oasis nobody camps at, the banner ground at the foot of the Western Descent and a march road lined with forts. It left the tribes with a story about themselves too: that the steppe, united, can take what it likes, and that it never keeps it. The era runs two hundred and thirty-six years. The battle that opened it is told in [[lore-wemaqbattle|The Battle of Wemaq]].

### The Silent Market (SM), from about 548 AF

The third orqwen rode down the spur in the week of the autumn market. The caravan town of [[place-qimod|Qìmod]] was full, its gate open for the fair, and by nightfall everyone in its walls was dead. The union overran the eastern fringe of Velanthia and never threatened Tānvür seriously. It broke up on its orqwen's death like the two before it.

The era left a town with its walls whole and nobody in it, a silence that kills anyone who sleeps there and a silence that the Shirzâri sell water beside. It is the current era, and the tribes expect a fourth union and cannot say when it will come. Its history is told in [[lore-thrdorqwnq|The Third Orqwenoq]].

## Carrying a Date to the Vylarian Calendar

Each era's count is a fixed offset from a Vylarian year, and the day of the season is the same day of the Vylarian year, so only the year changes.

- **The Silent Market:** add 547 to the year for the year AF. 173 SM is 720 AF.
- **The Hundred Banners:** add 311 for the year AF. 1 HB is 312 AF.
- **The Lantern Union:** the first 190 years fall before the Founding, so subtract the year from 191 for the year BF. 1 LU is 190 BF and 190 LU is 1 BF. From 191 LU, subtract 190 for the year AF.
- **Before the Lantern Union:** add 190 for the year BF. 1 BLU is 191 BF.
