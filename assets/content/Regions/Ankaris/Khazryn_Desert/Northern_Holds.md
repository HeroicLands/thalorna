## Overview

North of the dry grass the Khazryn ends in forest. The Gwatekor, the forest range, runs unbroken to the icy coast: dark spruce, bogs that never fully thaw, and long lakes locked under ice for eight months of the year. Small unorganized bands live here, sealing and fishing on the coast and hunting deer in the interior, and not one of them has a chief or a town. It is the same belt of taiga that runs through upper Velanthia, all of Targud and much of Malagna, and at its southern edge it is the least known part of the region.

The holds are reports, not sites. A sealer, working a sea-cliff in a bad summer, describes a doorway at the height of two men with its sill below the tide line. A deer-herder tells of a hillside where moss lies in sheets over squared stone, and where his animals will not graze. A trader from Targud says he heard from a man who saw a dry hall with cut seats along its wall. Nobody has been twice to the same place, and none of these accounts agree on where.

## What Is Said

Some of the bands say the holds are the work of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár]], the Deep Folk, cut into the northern cliffs in the days when the Khazári raised cities in every high place. Others say they are older than any people, the work of the folk the old songs call [[lore-theithari|Those Who Came Before]]. The coast-hunters say simply that the holds were made by someone else and that the sea keeps what it is given. Nobody living can settle it: the Khazári say nothing of their own halls, and no one who could tell has seen a site.

## Danger

The north is dangerous on its own account. Winter lasts most of the year, the bogs take a rider with his horse, and the bands have no reason to be kind to outsiders, who are all, to them, thieves of the seal-ground or the deer-run. Whatever a hold contains has not been tested; the stories give only the signs. A hold with a dry hall is also the best shelter for forty miles, and whatever lives in the forest knows it.

## Treasure

If the holds are what the stories say, they are a great discovery, and a scholar who proved one would have a name in the east for the rest of his life. A party that reaches one first may find nothing at all.

## A Hook

A Targud fur-trader pays well for any word of the doorway under the tide line, and he is not the only one asking. A man who knew the coast went north with a hired boat and a lamp, and the sealers say he has not come back.
