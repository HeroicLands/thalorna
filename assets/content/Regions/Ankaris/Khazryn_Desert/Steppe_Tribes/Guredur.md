The **Guredur**, "the camel people", are a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe of the eastern sand seas of the [[place-hskrrgn|Ghoshiqor]], in the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]], between the end of the [[affiliation-qbdrtrb|Qibudur]] grass and the well at [[place-sowidwell|Sovid]]. They keep camels and almost nothing else, and they go where a horse dies. The tribes of the grass hand a caravan to them at the edge of the sand, and the Guredur carry it across the dunes.

## The Range

The Guredur range is dune and gravel, with the occasional black mesa standing out of it. Their chief water is [Uruchaq]{.name data-type="place/site"}, "the black", a well at the foot of a black mesa where the dovaq's guest-tent stands. Their other waters are deep wells of the road, three to five days apart, and seeps in the dune hollows that only a camel can reach.

A caravan coming east takes the Guredur token at the edge of the Qibudur grass, where the Qibudur rider hands it over, and a Guredur guide takes it across the sands to Sovid. The Guredur also sell camels, and a caravan that lost beasts on the grass buys its remounts at Uruchaq at their price.

## How the Tribe Is Led

The Guredur are a tribe of camel-camps, each under its own head, and the dovaq is the first of the heads, not their master. He holds the office by keeping the largest guest-tent on the sand: any traveler, rider or stranger who reaches it is fed, and a dovaq whose tent turns a guest away or runs short of meat is replaced by a head whose tent does not. He judges quarrels inside the tribe, and his judgment is taken because refusing it would shame the camp that refused.

The dovaq is [Guredkenod Guredur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "camel-sword", a lean man past fifty who owns fewer camels than three of his camp heads and feeds more guests than all of them.

## The Brother-Share

The weaker camps on the Guredur sand, families of no great tribe who water at seeps the Guredur could take, pay a share of their herds each year. The Guredur call it the brother-share and the payers brothers. It buys protection: a raid on a brother's camels is answered by the Guredur as a raid on their own. It does not buy a place in the tribe's feuds, and a brother's blood-debts are his own.

The Guredur raid camels in their turn, from the tribes at the edges of their sand, and they raid by a strict rule: herds are driven off, nobody is killed who does not fight, and a camel taken from a brother is returned.

## Feuds

On the western edge of the sand the grass gives out in a strip of seeps that the Qibudur brand and the Guredur claim. Blood has been shed over them both ways since the drought year, when the Qibudur came onto the sand's edge with their horse herds. The Guredur are also at feud with the [[affiliation-bodekrtrb|Bodekur]] over a well in the deep sands whose mark has been cut over twice. The Guredur marked it while the Bodekur were away from the sands, and they hold that a well left is a well given up.

## The Price of Camels {#present}

A dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes by victory and by marriage, and a host needs camels to cross the sands. The gathering man's riders have come to Uruchaq to buy them, and Guredkenod has sold them a string at his price and sworn nothing. The camp heads are divided between those who want to keep selling to every side and those who say that a tribe selling camels to a union has chosen already, whatever it swears.

The seeps make the choice harder. The Guredur believe the Qibudur are bargaining with the same man, and they expect that whichever of the two tribes swears first has his help with the seeps. Two brother-camps on the western edge have stopped paying the brother-share this year and are watering at the seeps under Qibudur protection.

## Where They Come From {#history}

The Guredur count their camps back to Uruchaq and to no other water, and they hold that the well was theirs before the first banner. The deep wells of the road that they draw from were cut by the builders of [[lore-towercities|the Tower Cities]], and passed to the tribes when the league fell.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- Guredkenod wants the two brother-camps that stopped paying brought back to the share without a raid, and he wants the go-between to be nobody's kin.
- A caravan that bought Guredur passage was robbed in the dunes by riders with no token, and the Guredur owe it restitution under their word. They say the riders were Qibudur.
