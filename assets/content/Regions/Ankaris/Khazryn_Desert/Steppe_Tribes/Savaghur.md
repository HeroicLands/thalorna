The **Savaghur**, "the moon people", are a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe of the cold northern grass of the [[place-hskrrgn|Ghoshiqor]], in the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]], east of the [[affiliation-jbzrtrb|Jobizur]], where the steppe gives out into the spruce and bog of [[place-gwtkrrgn|Ghotaqor]]. Their grass is the best in the summer and the cruelest in the winter, and last winter it killed most of their herds. The eldest shaman of the Jobizur, [[being-tijodorquns|Tijodorghun Savaghur]], was born to the tribe and keeps its name.

## The Range

The Savaghur summer on the high grass at the forest's edge, where creeks run until midsummer and the herds fatten fast. Their gathering place is [Bovi]{.name data-type="place/site"}, "the snow", a deep spring in a fold of the grass that does not freeze, where the summer moot sits. In winter the tribe scatters into the sheltered valleys along the forest edge, a camp to a valley, each drawing from its own creek or spring.

No branch of [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] crosses the Savaghur grass, and the tribe sells no passage. Its riders bring horses and felt to the autumn market at [[place-qisomrktcmp|Qiiso]] and carry home grain for the winter.

## How the Tribe Is Led

The Savaghur are a summer tribe and a winter scatter. From the first frost to the thaw each camp lives alone in its valley under its own head, who rules it without answer to anyone. At the thaw the camps come together at Bovi, the moot chooses or keeps the dovaq, and for the summer the dovaq speaks for the whole tribe. A camp head who has lost his herds in the winter has lost his seat by the summer.

The dovaq is [Savaghbiiloq Savaghur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "moon-banner", a man of about forty-five who was chosen at Bovi this spring, after the winter killed the old dovaq's herds and the moot put him out.

## The Ice Winter

Last winter a thaw came early and froze again, and the grass in the valleys lay under a skin of ice that no hoof could break. The horses and sheep starved standing. Some camps lost half their herds, and some lost all of them. Four families went in to the Jobizur in the spring, who took them in as they take in every family that comes in a bad year, and let them keep the Savaghur name for as long as they choose.

The rest of the tribe stayed, with too few herds for the grass it holds. The young riders raid south for horses wherever a herd is thinly watched, and the elders cannot stop them, because a raid is how a poor tribe lives through a bad year.

## Feuds

The Savaghur winter valleys run along the forest edge, and the [[affiliation-ghtkrtrb|Ghotakur]] claim the same valleys. The two tribes have been at feud over them for three generations, and in the ice winter a Savaghur camp that moved into a Ghotakur valley for its grass was driven out of it in the snow.

## Two Offers {#present}

The Savaghur have two offers and one summer to choose. The Jobizur dovaq, [[being-khilodlunbii|Khilodlun Biilokur]], takes in families one at a time, and every family that goes in leaves fewer to hold the grass. A dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes by victory and by marriage, and his riders have offered the Savaghur herds to replace the dead ones if the tribe swears under his banner.

Savaghbiiloq knows what each offer costs. To go in to the Jobizur family by family is to become a name, as the [Biilokur]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} became one. To swear to the gathering man is to give up the right to refuse his muster, and to take herds from a man who has them to give because he has taken them from somebody. The camp heads who lost most want the herds. The Jobizur families with Savaghur names want their kin to come in.

## Where They Come From {#history}

The Savaghur have held the northern grass at the forest's edge as long as their tellings run, and their tellings are of winters: which ones closed the grass, which camps went in to the Jobizur, and which came back.

- A Savaghur raiding party has taken a Jobizur horse herd by mistake in the dark, and the Savaghur families living among the Jobizur want it returned before anyone counts it.
