The **Kesukur**, "the eagle people", are a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe of the [[place-hskrrgn|Ghoshiqor]], in the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]], whose range lies a day's ride from the oasis of [[place-wemaq|Vomek]]. They took in the few who escaped when the second orghun killed the [Khuvanur]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} at the oasis, and their elders hold the names of the killed by heart. They speak those names in their own camp and never at the water, and [[lore-wemaqcalling|the Calling at Vomek]] is their calling.

## The Range

The Kesukur graze the gravel reaches and the edges of the sand seas on both sides of the stretch of [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] that passes Vomek. Their central well is [Todva]{.name data-type="place/site"}, "the elder", where the moot sits and where the elders spoke the witnessed word that the calling repeats. The tribe's other waters are small wells and seeps along its circuit, each with the eagle cut above it.

The Kesukur sell passage to every tribe on the road, which is the reason the calling gives for not saying the names at the water. A caravan that has bought their stretch gets a rider who takes it past Vomek by day, lets the camels drink, and has the string moving again before the light turns yellow. The tribe claims nothing at Vomek, and its riders eat none of the dates.

## How the Tribe Is Led

A Kesukur belongs to the line of his mother. The tents and the herds are the women's and pass from mother to daughter, and a man rides out with his wife's herds and comes home to his mother's line. The office of dovaq goes the same way: when a dovaq dies, the moot chooses his successor from among the sons of his sisters, never from his own sons.

The dovaq is [Khuvankharu Kesukur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "deer-patience", a man in his fifties chosen from among the sons of the last dovaq's sisters. His name carries the deer of the Khuvanur. The eldest, who speaks the calling, sits at the moot beside him and speaks before him on anything touching the dead.

## Feuds

The Kesukur are at blood-feud with the [[affiliation-vghlrtrb|Voghalur]] of the eastern stone desert, who keep the words of the three great unions at their standing stones, the words of the banner that killed the Khuvanur among them. The two tribes' riders keep apart under the truce at the hand-off wells and meet nowhere else by choice.

## Before the Banner {#present}

A dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes by victory and by marriage, and his riders have come to Todva. Khuvankharu has not answered them. The calling makes the Kesukur the one tribe on the road that cannot hear the word "orghun" without hearing the names, and every child of the tribe has said the Khuvanur dovaq's refusal after the eldest since it could talk.

The elders' witnessed word says that the Kesukur say the names at the water when another tribe stands with them at the pool. Some of the young riders now argue that the gathering man is that tribe: that a uniter who stood at Vomek and heard the names said would be no orghun the calling warns against, and that nobody has asked him. The eldest answers that asking him hands him the steppe. The moot has not ruled, and the man's riders are coming back for an answer before the autumn market.

The Voghalur feud sharpens it. The Voghalur want the union more than any tribe on the road, and the Kesukur expect that a union with the Voghalur in it has a use for the calling's silence.

## Where They Come From {#history}

About 312 AF the survivors of Vomek came over the dunes to the Kesukur, and the Khuvanur dovaq walked in after them on foot, said the names from dawn to dawn, and died on the Kesukur laying-out ground. The elders spoke their witnessed word at Todva in the first year of [[lore-hndrdbnnrs|the Hundred Banners]], and every eldest since has repeated it.

## Hooks

- The eldest wants a witness who belongs to no tribe at Vomek on the day the Kesukur say the names there, if that day comes, and wants to know first how the gathering man's riders would take it.
- A Voghalur rider has been found dead on Kesukur grass, outside every truce, and Khuvankharu must decide whether to answer for him before the Voghalur decide it for him.
