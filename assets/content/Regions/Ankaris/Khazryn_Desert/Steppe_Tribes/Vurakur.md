The **Vurakur**, "the silver people", are a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe of the northern [[place-wlqtrrgn|Volketor]], in the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]], whose grass faces the eastern reach of [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia]]. Across the frontier lie the Velanthian oasis-and-river communities, half steppe in tongue and blood, which answer to whichever side last had the better year. For some years now the better years have been the Vurakur's. The communities pay the tribe a yearly silver so that its riders stay on their own grass, and their headmen's houses take Vurakur sons and daughters in marriage.

## The Range

The Vurakur graze the northern margin, grass and scrub where the spring rain falls heaviest, with springs a day apart and the clay pans full for a week after the rain. Their chief well is [Jonat]{.name data-type="place/site"}, "the wealth", a spring in a broad hollow where the moot sits and the silver is shared out each autumn.

The northern branch of [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] comes down from Velanthia's eastern reach toward the central grass, and the Vurakur sell its first stretch on the Khazrin side: water, a rider and the tribe's token. They carry Velanthian grain east and take horses and furs west.

## The Silver

Each autumn the silver-rider rides to the frontier communities with a column of riders behind him and asks for the year's silver, and the communities pay it. Nobody calls it tribute on either side of the frontier. A community that pays is a community the Vurakur do not raid, and it is also a community the other tribes of the margin leave alone, because the Vurakur answer for it.

The bride-speaker makes the marriages. The dovaq's sons and daughters and the clan heads' children marry into the headmen's houses of the paying communities, and a headman with a Vurakur son-in-law pays without being asked twice. A community that stops paying finds its daughters-in-law riding home.

## How the Tribe Is Led

The silver goes to the dovaq, and the dovaq gives it out to the clan heads by her own hand, a share to each. A clan head's share is the dovaq's gift, and a clan head who crosses her finds it smaller. The moot is heard, and the dovaq decides.

The dovaq is [Vuraqlun Vurakur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "silver", a woman of about fifty with four children married across the frontier, one into each of the four largest paying communities.

## Feuds

The Vurakur are rivals of the [[affiliation-sqrtrtrb|Soqritur]], their neighbors on the margin, who hold a river community on the frontier under a law of their own and take its fish and grain. The Vurakur hold that every community on that stretch of the frontier is theirs to ask, and the Soqritur hold that the one they keep is not. No blood has been shed over it, and both tribes count each other's riders.

## The Frontier's Better Year {#present}

The Velanthian Hosts of the frontier have told the communities to stop paying, and two have stopped. Vuraqlun has sent the silver-rider back to both with more riders than before, and one of the two headmen is her son's father-in-law.

A dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes by victory and by marriage, and the Vurakur would swear to him to keep the frontier paying. The clan heads fear the price: a union takes what the tribes take, and the silver of the frontier is the first thing the gathering man would ask for. Vuraqlun has answered his riders that the Vurakur ride with whoever leaves them their silver.

## Where They Come From {#history}

The Vurakur hold that they have grazed the northern margin since before the first banner. When the last union overran the eastern fringe of Velanthia, the frontier communities answered to it; when it broke, they answered to the tribes again, and the Vurakur were the strongest of the tribes on that stretch. Their tellings are of marriages, which houses took Vurakur brides, and which sent them home.

## Hooks

- Vuraqlun wants a party to ride with the silver-rider to a community that has stopped paying, because a stranger at his side looks like a sign that the asking has friends.
- A Vurakur bride has come home from a headman's house with her children and says the house has given its silver to a Velanthian Host, and her husband has followed her to the Vurakur well to get the children back.
