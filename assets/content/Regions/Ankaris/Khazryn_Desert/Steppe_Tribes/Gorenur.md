The **Gorenur**, "the storm people", are a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe of the southern [[place-wlqtrrgn|Volketor]], in the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]], where the grass gives out into the [[place-hekardesert|Desert of Hek'ar]] below Bizaria's [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]]. They are five lines descended from one man, and every Gorenur can say his fathers back to him. They raid in storms, when no patrol can see a column, and they have raided the March's herds for as long as the March has kept herds.

## The Range

The Gorenur range is the dry southern edge of the margin, grass for six weeks in spring and dust after, with springs and small wells a day or two apart along the edge of Hek'ar. Their chief well is [Khelid]{.name data-type="place/site"}, "the lightning", a spring under a split ridge where the white-beards of the five lines sit and where treaties are sworn before lightning when the weather allows.

They herd horses and sheep and breed a lean, long-legged horse that can go three days between waters, which they sell only to each other. They do not sell passage on the road, which runs north of their range, and they take from it only what an unescorted string leaves them.

## How the Tribe Is Led

Each of the five lines is ruled by its white-beards, its eldest men, and a line-teller among them holds its descent and can say whether a man belongs to it. The white-beards of the five lines sit together at Khelid as the tribe's moot, and they choose the dovaq to lead the raids and speak for the tribe to other tribes. Inside a line he has no word at all. The weavers keep each line's mark and weave it into every felt and carpet the line makes, so that a tent is known by its door-felt and a Gorenur saddle-rug by its border.

The dovaq is [Gorenyemok Gorenur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "storm-oath", a man of about thirty-five who has led the spring raids for six years and has never come home without horses.

## Raiding

A Gorenur raid rides out in the first storm of spring, when wind and sleet come down off the passes, crosses the open grass inside it and comes on the March's foothill herds while the patrols are in the forts. The herd is driven east into the grass and the storm closes behind it. Nobody is killed who does not fight, and a herd taken from a tribe that has paid the Gorenur is returned.

## Feuds

The Gorenur are at feud with the [[affiliation-bjlrtrb|Bujalur]], who winter inside the March under the League's truce and sell it horses. The Gorenur hold them soft, kept by the League like its dogs. When a Gorenur raid drives a March herd east, its track crosses the Bujalur winter ground, and blood has been owed between the tribes since the March answered one such raid by burning a Bujalur camp.

## The Spring Storms {#present}

A dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes by victory and by marriage, and the Gorenur want to ride with him. A union means the March's whole herd, the outer caravanserais and the silver of the road, and the white-beards of four lines have said so at Khelid. The fifth line has said nothing, because its white-beards remember the winter after the last union broke.

Gorenyemok means the first raid of this spring to be a gift to the gathering man: the March's horses, driven east through the Bujalur winter ground, with the League hunting the Bujalur for them while the Gorenur ride north.

## Where They Come From {#history}

The Gorenur count themselves from one father, whose five sons founded the five lines, and their line-tellers say the lines back to him by name. They hold that they have watered at Khelid since before the first banner. Their raids on the March are as old as its herds, and the feud with the Bujalur dates from the camp the March burned for a Gorenur raid.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- A Gorenur weaver will sell a door-felt with another line's mark on it to whoever pays enough, and the felt makes a tent look Gorenur to anyone who reads marks: [[scenario-trcecmpsmrch|The Truce Camps of the March]].
- Gorenyemok offers a party a share of the spring raid's horses if it keeps a March patrol in its fort on the night of the first storm.
