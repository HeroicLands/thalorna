The **Naqkhakur**, "the sheep people", are a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe of [[place-wlqtrrgn|Volketor]], the western margin of the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]], and the nearest thing the steppe has to a settled people. They winter in houses, sow barley in the clay pans, and carry the grain of [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia]] east along the road. The hard tribes call them soft, and on the rest of the steppe "sheep people" is an insult.

## The Range

The Naqkhakur hold a stretch of the margin a few days' march east of the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]], where the spring rain fills the clay pans and the springs lie a day apart. Their winter ground is [Jonkhiq]{.name data-type="place/site"}, "the ground", a cluster of mud-brick houses round a deep spring, with the barley fields in the pans below it. The houses stand empty from the grass to the cold, when the flocks are out on the margin and the families with them, and the sown ground is left to a few old men and the dogs.

The tribe sells its stretch of [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] like any tribe of the margin, and it sells more. A caravan halting at Jonkhiq can buy barley, bread and fodder, and the Naqkhakur grain-factors carry Velanthian grain east by the camel-load as far as the springs before [[place-yemokwell|Yemok]], where the tribes of the sands buy it.

## How the Tribe Is Led

The moot chooses the dovaq as every Sovidur moot does, and the moot is where the Naqkhakur differ. The heads of the winter houses sit in it beside the heads of the herding tents, and the housemasters outnumber them. Grain, sown ground and the bargains of the March decide more at a Naqkhakur moot than grazing does, and a herder who wants a raid answered finds the moot counting what a raid costs the grain trade first.

The dovaq is [Vilaqlun Naqkhakur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "cloth", a woman in her forties who has been to Velanthia twice and sits as easily in a Velanthian factor's hall as at her own fire.

## Feuds

The [[affiliation-chylrtrb|Chuyelur]] to the south raid the Naqkhakur flocks every spring and carry off girls as brides, and send the bride-price after them in horses. The Naqkhakur pay ransom for those they can and take the horses for the rest, and their herders count every bride the Chuyelur have taken in the last ten springs. The two tribes have been at blood-feud since a Naqkhakur herder killed a Chuyelur raider at a pan two springs ago.

## Soft Water {#present}

The tribes of the steppe say that a union always comes to the soft first, and the Naqkhakur believe them. Word of a dovaq of the northern steppe gathering tribes by victory and by marriage reached Jonkhiq with the grain-factors, and Vilaqlun has made up her mind to pay rather than fight: a tithe of barley and a road open to his riders, the bargain the Tellumi oases weigh. The herders in the moot say that a tribe that pays first is the tribe the union eats first, and that the grain buys the Naqkhakur a place at the back of the line and nothing more.

The Chuyelur raids make it worse. A Chuyelur warband that rides under the gathering man's banner could take Jonkhiq's grain and call it a tithe, and the housemasters want a guarantee against it that the man has not been asked for.

## Where They Come From {#history}

The Naqkhakur tell no tale of the year they first sowed. The hard tribes say the Vilérian forts on the margin taught them, when the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] held a handful of outposts on the grass, and the Naqkhakur say the clay pans did.

## Hooks

- Vilaqlun wants an escort for the barley she means to offer the gathering man, and wants it to reach his riders before a Chuyelur warband reaches the barley.
- A girl the Chuyelur took last spring has sent word by a caravan rider that she wants to come home, and her new husband's kin have sent the bride-price twice.
