The **Khilodur**, "the sun people", are a small [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe of the [[place-hskrrgn|Ghoshiqor]], in the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn]], who live on the southern rim of the dead lake [[place-ewod|Evod]] and cut its salt. They look like nobody else on the steppe. Their hair is fair, often nearly white, and their eyes are gray or pale blue, and the other tribes say they are kin of [[lore-basinbldrs|the Basin Builders]], whose lake it was. The Khilodur deny it.

## The Range

The Khilodur graze goats and camels on the dunes of the old southern shore, where the stepped mounds stand, and draw from wells a day or two back from the salt. Their chief well is [Iqod]{.name data-type="place/site"}, "the white", a deep well in a hollow of the dunes within sight of the flat, where the moot sits and the salt strings are loaded.

They cut salt along the rim in slabs a yard long, as the tribes of the nearest wells do, and carry it to [[place-qisomrktcmp|Qiiso]] for grain, felt and iron. They work no cutting in the middle of the flat and sleep out on the salt no more than any other tribe. The road does not cross their range, and the Khilodur sell it nothing but salt.

## How the Tribe Is Led

Every householder sits at the Khilodur moot, which chooses the dovaq as every Sovidur moot does. Each spring the lot-keeper draws the salt-lot, and each household cuts where the lot sends it for the year, so that no family holds the best of the rim for long.

No other tribe gives the Khilodur a daughter, so they marry within themselves, and the elders keep the count of every line so that no marriage joins close kin. A Khilodur who marries out leaves the tribe, and none has come back.

The dovaq is [Iqodlun Khilodur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "white", a woman in her thirties, the youngest dovaq on the central sands.

## The Builders' Kin

The grass tribes hold that the lake went down into the earth and will come up again where the last of its people is buried. They say the Khilodur are those people's last kin, that they bury their dead in the dry as the builders did, and that the tribe keeps the mounds. The Khilodur lay out their dead under open sky on the dunes, faces to the sky, as every rider does, and they say so to anyone who asks. They take nothing from the mounds, and they say that too. Nobody on the steppe can say where the Khilodur look came from, and the Khilodur do not try.

## Feuds

The Khilodur are at feud with the [[affiliation-mrjlrtrb|Mirjolur]], whose younger horde waters its herds at wells on the western rim and who cut salt there. The quarrel is over the western cuttings, and the Mirjolur riders call the Khilodur the builders' kin to their faces, which the Khilodur answer with blood.

## Nobody Has Asked {#present}

A dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes by victory and by marriage, and no rider of his has come to Iqod. Iqodlun wants to send riders to him first, so that the tribe has a place in the union before it is decided without them. The elders want to stay silent: a tribe nobody counts is a tribe nobody comes for, and they doubt a union of riders counts the Khilodur as riders at all.

The rim makes it urgent. Word has come round the salt that a stair has been found going down under the crust of a canal bank, and that the tribe whose boy found it has told no one outside. The Khilodur did not find it, and they expect to be named for it whoever did.

## Where They Come From {#history}

The Khilodur hold that they have cut salt on the southern rim since before the first banner. Their tellings say nothing of the lake when it held water, and their elders refuse to speak of the builders at all.

## Hooks

- Iqodlun wants an outsider to carry the Khilodur's offer to the gathering man, because she doubts the man's camp lets a Khilodur rider speak.
- A Mirjolur rider has been found dead on the western rim with salt in his mouth, and the Mirjolur say only the Khilodur kill that way. The Khilodur say nobody does: [[scenario-sltnthmth|Salt in the Mouth]].
