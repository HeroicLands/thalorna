The **Qibudur** hold the eastern grass beyond the [[affiliation-ksrrtrb|Koserur]], and their wells held in the drought that broke the Koserur. They breed the horses the central steppe rides: long-legged, hard in the hoof and trained to run from a standing start. A [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] rider who owns a Qibudur horse knows it by the brand on the shoulder and the price he paid.

## The Range

The Qibudur graze the grass of the eastern [[place-hskrrgn|Ghoshiqor]], in the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn]], east of [[place-qisomrktcmp|Qiiso]] along the first stretch of [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] toward [[place-sowidwell|Sovid]]. The grass runs out a week's march east of the market-camp, where the sand seas close in, and the Qibudur range ends with it.

Their great well is [Bulach]{.name data-type="place/site"}, "the dust", a stepped shaft of the road that has never gone dry in any year a Qibudur elder can name. It is where the herds winter and where the moot sits. Along the grass they own a string of small springs and seeps, a dozen or more, each marked with the Qibudur brand cut in the stone, and the horse herds move between them by a circuit the foals learn before they are weaned.

## The Round of the Year

The herds winter at Bulach, where the shaft is deep enough to water every horse the tribe owns. The mares foal on the spring grass. For the ten days of each market the dovaq's people sit at the road wells east of Qiiso, selling passage and horses to the caravans going on. In summer the traders ride out to every camp that owes them, leading strings for sale and carrying the accounts of what is owed.

## Horses and Credit

The Qibudur trade as much as they raid. A Qibudur horse-trader carries the tribe's credit in his head and sells on loan to riders he trusts, and debt is how the Qibudur bind other tribes to them. A rider in debt to a Qibudur trader fights beside the Qibudur and sells them news, and the trader keeps the account.

The dovaq is [Kodutoliz Qibudur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "horse-spear", a breeder who sold his first string at twelve and keeps the lines of the tribe's stallions by heart, back seven generations. He owes his office to the traders, whose accounts bind half the central steppe to the tribe, and he consults them before the elders.

## The Raid

In the year the Koserur wells failed, a Qibudur riding party drove off nearly a third of the Koserur horses and killed two warriors. The Qibudur call it a dry-year bargain and say they have no quarrel with the Koserur. The Koserur call it an insult they cannot yet answer. The Qibudur horse-trader [Kodujibun Qibudur]{.name data-type="being/npc"} now holds a Koserur archer's debt and wants it repaid in information about the Koserur wells.

## Caravans

A caravan that crosses Qibudur grass pays in horses or in goods the Qibudur can trade onward, and it buys a guide from the tribe. The guide takes it to the edge of the sand seas and hands it, with the Qibudur token on its string, to the tribes of the eastern sands. The Qibudur prefer paid passage to plunder, because a caravan that returns next year is worth more than one stripped once.

They also sell horses to the road. A caravan going east takes remounts at Bulach, and a herd driven east as goods is often Qibudur-bred, walking itself to the Tantsou market with a Qibudur brand on every shoulder.

## Raids and Fears {#present}

The Qibudur raid in a dry year and trade in a wet one. In a dry year their traders come to a camp with no strings to sell. They raided the Koserur and they want the [[affiliation-kndrtrb|Kenodur]] well at [[place-qimod|Qiimod]]. On the eastern edge of their grass they are at feud with the [[affiliation-grdrtrb|Guredur]] of the sands over a strip of seeps that both tribes mark. They fear one thing above the rest: a drought that reaches Bulach. They know what they did when the Koserur wells failed, and they expect the same from every tribe that holds a debt of theirs.

## The Dry Year {#history}

Bulach held through the drought that dried the [[affiliation-ksrrtrb|Koserur]] seasonal water, and the Qibudur came out of that year with their herds whole and nearly a third of the Koserur horses added to them.

## Hooks

- Kodujibun Qibudur offers a party Qibudur horses on credit, a string of the best on the grass. The debt is a service he will name later, at a time he will choose, and no silver settles it.
- The Qibudur want the Kenodur's tolls at Qiimod. They will pay a share of them to a party that draws the town's water from inside the walls, by day, and carries the proof out, and they have not said what they mean to do with the proof.
