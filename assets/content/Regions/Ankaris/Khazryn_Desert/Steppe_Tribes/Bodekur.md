The **Bodekur**, "the owned-water people", are a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe of the deep eastern sands of the [[place-hskrrgn|Ghoshiqor]], in the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]], north of the [[affiliation-grdrtrb|Guredur]] and well away from the road. When the last union of the steppe rose, the Bodekur rode away from their wells rather than swear to it, and they came back to the sands when it broke. They found other tribes' marks over their water, and they have been cutting their own back over them since.

## The Range

The Bodekur range is dune and gravel with black mesas standing out of it, and their water is a handful of deep wells and the seeps in the dune hollows between them. Their chief well is [Akhaq]{.name data-type="place/site"}, "the father", a stepped shaft at the foot of a mesa, where the tribe's mark is cut over the oldest marks on the stone and the moot sits.

The Bodekur keep camels and goats and a few horses, and they sell the road nothing: its deep wells lie south of their sand, in Guredur hands. Their riders take hides and camels to the autumn market at [[place-qisomrktcmp|Qiiso]] and come home with grain.

## The Word

The clans that came back to the sands swore a code among themselves at Akhaq before they cut a single mark, and they call it the Word. It sets a price in beasts for every wrong one Bodekur does another, from a fouled skin to a killing, so that a quarrel inside the tribe ends in a payment and never in a feud. It sets the dovaq under it, and it says that no Bodekur swears to an orghun.

The Word-Keeper holds it by heart and says it at every moot, and when a Bodekur wrongs another, he says what the Word prices the wrong at. Neither the dovaq nor the moot can change a word of it, and the tribe says it as their grandfathers swore it.

## How the Tribe Is Led

The moot chooses the dovaq from any line, and the dovaq leads the tribe in everything the Word does not settle: the wells, the raids, the price of a camel at Qiiso. The clan heads hold the wells, and each clan has a well-cutter who rides its water once a year and recuts its mark wherever another tribe has cut over it.

The dovaq is [Bodeklun Bodekur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "owned water", a woman of fifty-five who can say the Word as well as its keeper.

## Feuds

The Bodekur and the Guredur both mark [Jibun]{.name data-type="place/site"}, "the debt", a deep well in the sands between their ranges. The Guredur cut their mark on it while the Bodekur were away. The Bodekur cut theirs over the Guredur's when they came home, the Guredur cut theirs back over the Bodekur's, and blood has been owed both ways since. The Word prices a killing between Bodekur; it says nothing about a killing by a Guredur, and the tribe answers those with blood.

## The Word Against the Banner {#present}

A dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes by victory and by marriage, and his riders have come to Akhaq. The Word gives the Bodekur's answer, and Bodeklun gave it to them. The young riders are less sure. Their grandfathers rode away from the last union, and they say there is no empty sand left to ride to and no tribe that would give them water on the way.

The Guredur are selling the same man camels at their well, and the Bodekur believe the Guredur have bought his help with Jibun at the price. The Guredur well-cutter has been seen at Jibun with riders who are not Guredur.

## Where They Come From {#history}

The Bodekur hold that Akhaq was theirs before the first banner. When the last union rose, the tribe left its wells and rode away rather than swear, and it came back when the union broke. The clans swore the Word at Akhaq on their return, and the feud over Jibun began the same year.

## Hooks

- Bodeklun wants a witness from outside the steppe at Jibun when the Bodekur well-cutter recuts the mark this autumn, because a witness the Guredur cannot call kin is the only one both tribes might hear.
- A Bodekur rider has killed another Bodekur, and the Word prices the death at more camels than his line owns. His line wants to know whether a stranger's camels pay a Bodekur debt.
