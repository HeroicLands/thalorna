The **Mirjolur**, "the herd people", are a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe of the [[place-hskrrgn|Ghoshiqor]], in the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]], whose grass runs west and north of the dead lake [[place-ewod|Evod]]. They are one of the largest tribes of the sands and the least of one mind, because they are three hordes, each from one of three brothers, and each horde keeps its own wells and its own herds and answers to its own head.

## The Range

The elder horde holds the best of the grass, north of Evod, and the middle horde the gravel reaches west of it. The younger horde holds the dunes of the lake's old western shore and waters at wells a day back from the salt. The three hordes meet once a year at [Voyer]{.name data-type="place/site"}, "the milk", a deep well on the grass where the brothers are said to have divided the herds, and where the three moots sit together.

The younger horde cuts salt on the western rim of Evod in slabs a yard long and carries it to [[place-qisomrktcmp|Qiiso]] for grain, as the tribes of the nearest wells do. The road's wells lie south of the Mirjolur grass, and the tribe sells it horses and salt and no passage.

## How the Tribe Is Led

Each horde has its own head and its own moot. The head of the elder horde is the dovaq, and he speaks for all three at Voyer and at the moot of Qiiso where they agree. Where they do not, he speaks for his own horde, and the other two go their own way.

A quarrel between the hordes goes to a speaker, a man chosen for the force and justice of his words, who hears both sides at Voyer and gives a judgment the three moots take. The Mirjolur hold that a horde which refuses a speaker's judgment has left the tribe, and none has.

The dovaq is [Mirjoltoliz Mirjolur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "herd-spear", a man past seventy who has held the elder horde for thirty years. The head of the younger horde is [Orqazjilaq Mirjolur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "heat-lantern", a man of forty-five who holds the western cuttings and wants the rest of the rim.

## Feuds

On the western rim the younger horde cuts salt beside the [[affiliation-khldrtrb|Khilodur]], who cut the southern rim, and the two quarrel over the western cuttings. The Mirjolur call the Khilodur the builders' kin to their faces, and the Khilodur answer with blood. The elder horde holds no feud with the Khilodur and does not answer for the younger's.

## The Stair and the Banner {#present}

A dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes by victory and by marriage, and the three hordes are three answers. Mirjoltoliz will not swear, and has said so at Voyer. Orqazjilaq wants the younger horde under the man's banner before the Khilodur think of it, with the western rim promised to it. The middle horde waits to see which of the two the speaker favors.

The rim is the younger horde's secret. This season a salt-cutter's son of the younger horde put his pick through the crust on a canal bank and broke into a dry stair going down. The horde's elders have told no one outside, and Orqazjilaq means the stair to be a gift worth the western rim when the gathering man's riders come. Now a younger-horde rider has been found dead on the western rim with salt in his mouth, and the younger horde says only the Khilodur kill that way.

## Where They Come From {#history}

The Mirjolur count themselves from three brothers who divided their father's herds at Voyer, the eldest taking the grass, the middle the gravel and the youngest the shore of the lake. The younger horde has cut salt on the western rim since before the first banner, and its quarrel with the Khilodur is as old as its cuttings.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- The younger horde is sure the Khilodur killed its rider and wants outsiders to carry its demand for blood to the Khilodur well, since none of its own can go there and come back: [[scenario-sltnthmth|Salt in the Mouth]].
- Mirjoltoliz wants to know what the younger horde found on the rim before the gathering man does, and he does not want his own riders seen asking.
