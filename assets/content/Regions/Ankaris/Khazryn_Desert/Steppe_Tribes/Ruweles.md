The **Ruweles**, "the water people," are not a tribe but a name for every band of the deep [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn]] sands that keeps a water nobody else knows. A band is a few families, a dowek and one or two wells or seeps that the band alone can find. The [[lore-sowidesclt|Sowides]] of the grass call them Ruweles, and the bands call themselves by their wells.

## The Secret

A band's water is its whole wealth, and its secret is kept with a discipline no other tribe matches. The water is marked on no stone, and a child learns the way to it on foot with an elder, in stages, over years. A caravan master who wants to cross the sands in the dry season hires a Ruweles guide, who takes the caravan by a route that touches the band's water once and leaves the caravan unable to find it again.

## Guides for Hire

Guiding is the Ruweles' trade. A band sells its knowledge for horses, silver or a standing arrangement with a caravan house, and a guide who has lost a caravan to thirst loses his name among the bands. A Ruweles guide keeps the whole bargain: the caravan arrives, or he stays with it until it cannot. They are the most trusted people on the road, and they charge more than anyone else for it.

## Neighbors

The Ruweles stay out of the quarrels of the grass tribes. A band that refuses to guide a raiding party is left alone, because the grass tribes need the guides more than they need the grievance. The bands bargain for their water with whoever controls the nearest well on the rim of the sands, and a rim tribe that harms a band loses its guides for a generation.
