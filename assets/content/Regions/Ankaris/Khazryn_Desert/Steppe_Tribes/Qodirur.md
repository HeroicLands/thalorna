The **Qodirur**, "the mountain people", are a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe of the black mesas of [[place-dkrqrrgn|Diqraqor]], in the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn]]. Where the other tribes of the plateau wall their wells with stacked stone, the Qodirur have walled their whole country's water into one ring and keep everything they own inside it. The poorer upland families round them herd for the right to drink there.

## The Ring

The Qodirur hold a cluster of mesas on the northern half of the plateau, west of the southern spur of [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]]. In the ravine between the three largest lies [Beyuk]{.name data-type="place/site"}, "the gift", where two deep seeps and a rock cistern cut in the ravine wall are closed inside a ring of stacked stone higher than a rider and half a mile round. One gate opens in it. Inside are the wells, the dovaq's tents, the winter pens and the tribe's store: the silver and goods from escort pay and raids, the horses taken in feud, and the gifts every client family brings at the turn of the year.

The ring-warden keeps the gate. A Qodirur enters freely; a client family enters to water its flocks and leaves when they have drunk; a stranger enters as the dovaq's guest or not at all.

## The Clients

The families who herd for the Qodirur are upland kin of no great tribe, too poor to hold water of their own against the Qodirur riders. Each pays a share of its flocks, herds the Qodirur's own flocks in season and waters at the ring, and answers to the dovaq through its own head. Each owes a rider to the warband in a raiding year. The dovaq protects them as her own, and the tribe's feuds are theirs.

## How the Tribe Is Led

The dovaq is [Diqkhunlun Qodirur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "red", a woman of about fifty who holds the ring, the store and the clients, and who rules all three. The moot sits inside the ring and gives its counsel, and she takes it when she chooses. It is more than any other Sovidur moot accepts, and the Qodirur accept it because the ring has never fallen.

The Qodirur hire out as escorts on the southern spur, as the plateau tribes do, and an escorted string waters at the ring once on its way down to the [[place-swoasisbelt|Tellumel]].

## Feuds

The Qodirur quarrel with the [[affiliation-kndrtrb|Kenodur]] over the ravines at the northern edge of the plateau, where the Kenodur winter. Each autumn the Qodirur set stones across the mouths of the ravines they claim, and each autumn the Kenodur throw them down. Their bitterer feud is with the [[affiliation-dqrkrtrb|Diqrakur]] of the southern plateau, who raid the Qodirur clients and raise a stone for every Qodirur they kill.

## Clients Asking {#present}

A dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes by victory and by marriage, and his riders have offered the Qodirur clients water and a place under his banner in their own name. Two client heads have not come to the ring with their gifts this year. Diqkhunlun has sent no answer to the gathering man and has closed the gate to his riders, and she holds that a tribe whose wells are walled has no need to swear to anyone.

The Diqrakur are using the moment. They raided a client camp at the turn of the year, and the Qodirur warband has not answered, because Diqkhunlun refuses to send it out of the ring while the clients are deciding whom they belong to.

## Where They Come From {#history}

The Qodirur say the ring was raised when the tribe came onto the plateau, and they give no year for it. The client families have herded for the ring for as long as their own heads remember.

## Hooks

- Diqkhunlun wants the two client heads who kept back their gifts brought to the ring to explain themselves, and the clients want a stranger present who carries no tribe's word.
- The Kenodur have sent word that their winter ravines are open to the Qodirur clients if the clients water at Kenodur wells instead. The ring-warden wants to know which client head carried the message.
