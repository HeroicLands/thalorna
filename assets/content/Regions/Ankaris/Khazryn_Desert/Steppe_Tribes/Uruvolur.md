The **Uruvolur**, "the water people," are no single tribe: the name covers every band of the deep sands of the [[place-hskrrgn|Ghoshiqor]], in the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn]], that keeps a water nobody else knows. A band is a few families, a dovaq and one or two wells or seeps that the band alone can find. The [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] of the grass call them Uruvolur, and the bands call themselves by their wells.

## Where the Bands Are

The bands live in the sand seas on both sides of [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]], from the edge of the [Long Dry]{.name data-type="place/feature"} east of [[place-yemokwell|Yemok]] to the last dunes before [[place-sowidwell|Sovid]], and in the empty ground round the dead cities. No band holds grass. Their waters lie in the dune hollows and under the black mesas, far from the road's named wells, and the nearest grass tribes on the rim of the sands know roughly how many bands live beyond them and nothing of where.

A band names itself by its water and gives that name to no stranger, so a caravan master knows a band by its guide. The best known is the [[affiliation-rqdrtrb|Oroqidur]], the largest of them, who keep a hidden oasis and count themselves a tribe.

## The Secret

A band's water is its whole wealth, and its secret is kept with a discipline no other tribe matches. The water is marked on no stone, and a child learns the way to it on foot with an elder, in stages, over years. A caravan master who wants to cross the sands in the dry season hires an Uruvolur guide, who takes the caravan by a route that touches the band's water once and leaves the caravan unable to find it again.

## The Round of the Year

In the summer heat each band sits at its own water and nobody sees it. In spring and autumn, when the fleets cross, the guides come out to the rim: to Yemok, where the strings face the Long Dry, to the halts along the sands, and to the fire at [[place-qisomrktcmp|Qiiso]], where a guide sits down uninvited and is poured tea. In winter the bands move to the seeps that run only in the cold months, and some of them trade salt and guiding for grain with the rim tribes.

## Guides for Hire

Guiding is the Uruvolur's trade. A band sells its knowledge for horses, silver or a standing arrangement with a caravan house, and a guide who has lost a caravan to thirst loses his name among the bands. An Uruvolur guide keeps the whole bargain: the caravan arrives, or he stays with it until it cannot. They are the most trusted people on the road, and they charge more than anyone else for it, often a weight of silver a camel-load for the Long Dry alone.

A guide sells more than the way. The bands are the first to know when the dunes open a street of [[place-wilud|Vulud]], and [Uruvolun]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a guide who has sold such news before, is known at Qiiso for selling it twice. One road they never sell: a band guides across the empty ground south of [[place-jilaq|Jilaq]] and refuses the ground north of its wall at any price.

## Current Situation {#present}

The bands sell guiding and news and stay out of the grass tribes' quarrels. This season a street of Vulud stands open, and Uruvolun has sold the news of it to a Bizarian factor and a Tantsou collector at Qiiso and guides whichever arrives first. A band's standing rests on its guides, and a band that loses a caravan, or the secret of its water, loses that standing.

## The Dovaq of the Long Dry

Each band has its own dovaq, and no dovaq speaks for another band. The one a master is likeliest to meet is [Numaqtoliz]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "night-spear", dovaq of the band whose guides work the Long Dry. His band has kept the five waterless days for longer than the road's oldest masters remember, and its guides wait at the trough at Yemok each season with their price already named. Numaqtoliz sends a guide with a string and never goes himself, and few masters have seen him at all.

## History {#history}

Numaqtoliz's band has guided strings across the Long Dry for longer than the road's oldest masters remember.

## Neighbors

The Uruvolur stay out of the quarrels of the grass tribes. A band that refuses to guide a raiding party is left alone, because the grass tribes need the guides more than they need the grievance. The bands bargain for their water with whoever controls the nearest well on the rim of the sands, and a rim tribe that harms a band loses its guides for a generation.

## Raids and Fears

The bands raid nobody, and a band is too small to fight a grass tribe. What they fear is a stranger who can find the water again, a rim tribe that has decided it needs a band's water more than its guides, and a caravan lost on a guide's stretch, which shames the whole band.

## Hooks

- A guide of Numaqtoliz's band is selling a Bizarian master the way to the band's own water, and the master does not know what he is buying. The band knows a sale is being made and does not know which guide is making it.
- A rim tribe has killed an Uruvol guide in a quarrel over a seep, and every band on the road has stopped work until it pays. A caravan already at Yemok has no guide for the Long Dry and a master who will not wait.
