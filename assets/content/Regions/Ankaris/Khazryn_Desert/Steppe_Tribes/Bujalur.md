The **Bujalur**, "the seal people", are a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe of the [[place-wlqtrrgn|Volketor]], the western margin of the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]], who winter inside the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]] of [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizaria]]. Each autumn they come in under the League's truce to graze the March's foothills, and they sell its garrison horses at the outer caravanserais. In spring they go back out onto the grass, and whenever the steppe stirs, the March asks what its winter guests have seen.

## The Range

The Bujalur summer on the grass of the southern Volketor, two weeks' ride east of the March, and draw from springs a day apart that they own as every tribe of the margin owns its water. Their summer gathering place is [Tuvaq]{.name data-type="place/site"}, "the fur", a spring under a low ridge where the camp heads choose the dovaq.

At the end of summer the camps ride west to the March's foothills and come in under the truce. They graze the foothills through the winter beside the upland herders of Bizarian stock, sell horses at the outer caravanserai-forts, and fill their skins at the spring-forts at the same troughs as the garrison. They go out again at the thaw, before the March's herds go up to the spring grass.

The road crosses the southern Volketor east of the March, and the Bujalur sell a stretch of it as every tribe of the margin does: water, a rider and the tribe's token.

## How the Tribe Is Led

Each winter camp has its own head, who answers for every rider of it to the dovaq and to the League. The camp heads choose the dovaq at Tuvaq, and the dovaq holds the truce in the tribe's name: each autumn a truce-rider goes to the outer caravanserais to swear the camps in, and he answers there for any rider who breaks it. The Bujalur put a camp that breaks the truce out of the winter ground themselves, before the League can, because a truce broken by one camp is a truce lost for all.

The dovaq is [Bujalqiimod Bujalur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "seal-silent", a man of about fifty-five who speaks the League's tongue and has sold horses to the March for thirty winters.

## Feuds

The Bujalur are at feud with the [[affiliation-grnrtrb|Gorenur]] of the Hek'ar edge, who raid the March's herds every spring and drive them east across the grass. The League follows the tracks, and the tracks cross the Bujalur winter ground. Blood has been owed between the two tribes since a Gorenur raid that the March answered by burning a Bujalur camp, and the Bujalur hold that the Gorenur drove the herd through that camp on purpose.

## The Guests Under Suspicion {#present}

A dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes by victory and by marriage, and the March is where the League hears of a union first. The Lord Commander's officers have begun counting the Bujalur camps, the riders in each and the horses they hold. Bujalqiimod knows what the count is for: if a union rises, the tribe wintering inside the March is the first the League suspects of riding with it.

The tribe has answered the gathering man's riders that the Bujalur winter in the March and swear to no one else. Some of the young riders want the camps out of the March before the League turns them out in the snow. The camp heads want the truce kept, because the foothills are the only winter grass the tribe has.

## Where They Come From {#history}

The Bujalur hold that they have wintered in the foothills under one truce or another since before the March had walls. Their tellings of the League are of horse fairs, of truces sworn at the spring-forts, and of the winters when a union rose and the March turned the camps out.
