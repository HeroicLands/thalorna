The **Diqrakur**, "the stone people", are a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe of the southern [[place-dkrqrrgn|Diqraqor]], in the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]], on the plateau's southern slopes where the [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Atarzád]] herds come up from round [[place-mountshofar|Mount Shofar]]. For every enemy a Diqrakur man kills, the tribe raises a standing stone, and a man is read by his row. Their feud with the Atarzád is the most active on the plateau, and it lengthens the rows every year.

## The Range

The Diqrakur graze goats, sheep and the plateau's small hardy horses across the ravines and mesas of the southern plateau, down to where the streams off [[place-graznmntns|the Grazian Mountains]] sink into the stone. Their wells are seeps in the ravine floors and cisterns cut in the walls, each behind a wall of stacked stone. The tribe's gathering place is [Khamaq]{.name data-type="place/site"}, "the skull", a cistern-ravine where the rows of standing stones climb both slopes above the water, one row to a man, some of them three generations old.

## The Rows

A stone is raised for a kill the moot has heard witnessed: a man slain in a feud, or in answer to a raid. The stone-setter raises it at the end of the killer's row, uncut and unmarked, and knows whose death each stone answers for. A man's seat at the moot is set by the length of his row, a man with no stones has no seat, and the dovaq is chosen from the longest rows. When a man dies, his row stays, and his sons stand at the end of it when they are named in the moot.

The rows are the tribe's account of its feuds, and the stone-setter can say, row by row, how much blood each enemy tribe owes and is owed.

## How the Tribe Is Led

The dovaq is [Atazyaqnar Diqrakur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, "great dusk", a man in his late forties whose row runs twenty-two stones, eleven of them for Atarzád herdsmen of the [Burqalod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}. The Diqrakur hire out as escorts to caravans bound for the [[place-swoasisbelt|Tellumel]] and [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Amradad]], because a tribe that guards a string does not raid it, and they raid the outer gardens of the Tellumel when they dare.

## Feuds

Since the Atarzád took four of the Tellumi cities and their flocks came up onto the plateau's southern slopes, the Diqrakur have fought them over the grazing, and the line between the two runs along wells. The Burqalod herds on the highland round Mount Shofar are the nearest, and most of the stones raised since then answer for Burqalod dead.

To the north the Diqrakur raid the client families of the [[affiliation-qdrrtrb|Qodirur]], whose ring the Diqrakur call a pen, and a Qodirur killed in that feud is a stone like any other.

## A Host from the North {#present}

A dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes by victory and by marriage, and the Diqrakur think of one thing: a host strong enough to clear the Atarzád off the plateau's slopes. Atazyaqnar has sent riders north to say so. The elders are less sure. A union has no law but the orghun's word, and a man who can forbid a feud can forbid the rows.

The Atarzád have heard of the gathering too, and [[affiliation-cncltwelve|the Council of the Twelve]] at [[place-beitshofar|Beit-Shofár]] is weighing whether to acclaim a war-leader against the steppe. The Diqrakur expect every raid of theirs on the slopes to be counted there as a raid by the union, and they do not mind it.

## Where They Come From {#history}

The oldest rows at Khamaq are worn round and lean, and the stone-setter names the dead each one answers for back to his grandfather's grandfather. The Atarzád stones are the newest rows on both slopes.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- Atazyaqnar wants an outsider to carry his offer north to the gathering man, because a Diqrakur rider who goes himself can be followed, and the Burqalod are watching the plateau's northern trails.
- A Burqalod herd-chief offers a price for the return of his son's body from below the rows, and the stone-setter has raised no stone for him.
