## Overview

Ushtra-bēr lies in the deep north of the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn Desert]], the last-founded of the seven Khazryn cities and one of the three still in Khazryn hands. Some twenty thousand people live there under the **House of Ushtra-bēr**. It is remote, austere and deeply traditional, and a visitor who reaches it after the long northern crossing finds a court that does things the way the Khazryn did them before anyone now living was born.

## Its Ruler and Court

The ruler, **Vāhrām of Ushtra-bēr**, is a hard man of fifty, and his court ceremonial is said to preserve forms forgotten everywhere else. Learn the forms before you go: a stranger who presents himself at Ushtra-bēr as he would at an exile court in Amradad will find that the court notices. The house keeps a household guard of perhaps fifty men, enough for ordinary security and for ceremony and no more; like every Khazryn house, it maintains no army.

The oasis lives by dates, palm-sugar, garden produce and the caravans that pass, and on the [[affiliation-khzrncnfdrtn|Khazryn Confederation]]'s Council it is one of the three houses that still govern their own ground.
