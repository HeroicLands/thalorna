## Overview

The **House of Shirvan** is the senior of the seven princely houses of the [[affiliation-khzrncnfdrtn|Tellumi Confederation]], the house of the first city and of the snow peak, [Gilsumtenhur]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the highest of the seven peaks the Tellumi root their houses in. It ruled the [[place-oasishirvn|Oasis of Shirvan]] until the last year of [The Breaking]{.name data-type="lore/history"}, and it has kept its court in exile in the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]] since. By custom so old nobody can date it, the prince of Shirvan speaks first at every session of the [[affiliation-cnclprinces|Council of the Princes]].

## The Heir and the Case

The heir-in-exile is [Muresha of Shirvan]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a scholar and theologian of about forty, unmarried, who has spent her life building one argument: that Shirvan belongs to her house by right, and that the Atarzád have nothing to set against the right but a promise. The case fills a small library at the court, and it is never finished. Every tablet that surfaces when a channel is dug out under the lower basin of Shirvan, and that the Tellumi water-masters carry away before the overseers see it, is a tablet the house wants to read, and the treaties between the seven cities are the ones it wants most. Some exile factions see Muresha as the focus of a more active phase of the claim. She has not said whether she wants to be one.

The court keeps [[miscgear-shirvantab|the first tablet]]. The roll of the peaks says that the first lineage came down from the snow peak carrying a tablet of wet clay with nothing pressed on it, and pressed it at the spring with one word, _here_. The house shows it to nobody outside the house, and every Shirvan funeral answers the snow peak with the city's name.

## What It Wants, and Who Stands in the Way

The house wants Shirvan, and until it has the city it wants proof. Beside the tablets of the archive, it wants its house-sign: the old houses hold that the first lineage cut the sign of the snow peak on the lintels of [[place-vurdil|Vurdish]] as it came down, and the House of Shirvan has hired climbers into the passage for ninety years to find it.

The [Ozhmarod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}, the Atarzád tribe that holds Shirvan and the strictest of the twelve, stand in its way in the city itself. Their fire-wardens hunt the [Hidden Delkoshekh of Shirvan]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, whom the quarter believes still keeps the old observances near the spring, and their informers know every Tellumi household in the lower town. At the Council the house's rival is the [[affiliation-hsemernagord|House of Mernagord]], which leads the houses of the west in denying that any lineage came down through Vurdish, and whose prince listens to Atarzád merchants about a compact the House of Shirvan calls treason.

## Hooks

- **A tablet out of the basin.** A water-master of the Shirvan quarter has a tablet from the channel diggings and no safe way to send it. The court pays well for anyone who carries it to Amradad past the water-tax assessor at the basin gate, and pays more to learn who else was offered it.
- **The climbers.** The house is hiring again for Vurdish. It wants the snow peak's sign found on a lintel and copied exactly, and a rival house pays the same climbers to come back with nothing.
