## Overview

Nobody has counted the peaks of the Grazian Mountains, and the glaciers that lie between them are older than any name for them. The range is a wall of ice and rock two thousand miles long, and its north face above the Khazryn is the least traveled part of it: a few known passes, each open for a handful of weeks in late summer, and between them ridge after ridge of blue ice that no caravan has crossed and few climbers have named.

Under that ice are the places this note collects. A shepherd's boy chasing a goat finds a doorway in a rock face. A glacier draws back from a valley it has held for ten centuries and lays bare a stair. A guide leads a cold, sick party into a cleft to shelter from a storm and finds the cleft is a passage, with a floor, and the floor is worn. These are the labyrinths of the Grazian: cut passages that climb and branch and join, caverns whose floors have been leveled, halls whose roofs are lost in the dark, and sealed doors. [[place-gurdi|Gurdi]], above the Tellumi belt, is the one the lowlands know.

Who made them is not known, and no note records a claim. The Tellumi trace their descent through one of them. The Ösket and the Hvarn, the guides of the passes, know more than that and give it only to those who travel with them.

## What the People Believe

The Tellumi hold the doorways as the road of their ancestors, and the old houses make pilgrimage to the one they claim. The Vedyari on the south face call the high valleys the dwellings of their gods and want no one in them. The guides say what they say to every lowlander who asks, which is that the mountains keep their own counsel and that a party in them is a party on sufferance. A Tānvüri military map of the Eastern Reach marks three places with a single character, which the garrison's own scholars translate as "do not enter."

## Danger

The cold is the commonest killer. A party above the snow line without guides, furs and a way down by dark does not return. Beyond the cold are the creatures of the high places: Cliff Stalkers on the ledges, Crag Lurkers in the cracks, Stone Rams that defend their ridges against anything with a weapon, and the Roc, which takes pack animals from a ridge in front of the herdsman. In the labyrinths themselves the danger is the dark and the cracked floors, and something that follows at the edge of the light.

## Treasure

The rewards are as uncertain as the places. Carved lintels and floor-signs that prove a house's claim; worked metal that the lowlands do not make; ice-preserved wood, cloth and dyes, undamaged by time in the cold dark; and the chance of what no lowlander has seen. A merchant house in Chandrapur pays well for anything from the high places, and asks no question about where.

## A Hook

A guide of the Ösket, old and dying, wants one last crossing of the Eastern Reach and has a place he will take a party to on the way, if they carry his body down. He will not say what is there. His own people have told him he may not go, and have told the party's patron the same.
