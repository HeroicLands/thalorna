## Overview

Mehrnāgord is an oasis city on the western fringe of the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn Desert]], the sixth of the seven Khazryn cities in the order of their founding, and the largest of the three that remain in Khazryn hands. Some thirty thousand people live there under the **House of Mehrnāgord**, which still rules.

## Its Living

Mehrnāgord lives the traditional oasis life: dates, palm-sugar and garden produce from the irrigated ground, trade with the caravans that pass, and, unusually among the oases, salt from the pans of the western fringe. It is a modest economy, and like every still-Khazryn oasis its wealth is reckoned in continuity rather than volume: its walls stand on the foundations of the original city, rebuilt by every generation since.

## Its Prince

The ruling prince, **Pārván of Mehrnāgord**, is in his sixties, cautious, and inclined to accommodation—with the Sultanate of Amradad and, warily, with the Ātárzád who hold the four lost cities. That makes him the voice of accommodation on the [[affiliation-khzrncnfdrtn|Khazryn Confederation]]'s Council, and the exile houses consider him insufficiently militant. Ātárzád merchant interests have quietly sounded him out as a possible partner for limited normalization; he has not answered either way. His household guard of perhaps a hundred men is the largest of the three ruling houses', which says how little armed force any Khazryn prince commands.
