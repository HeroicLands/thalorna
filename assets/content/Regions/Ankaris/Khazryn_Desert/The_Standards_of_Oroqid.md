## Overview {#overview}

The standards of [[place-roqid|Oroqid]] lie in the strongroom under the headquarters of the fort, wrapped in oilcloth, beside the garrison's pay-chests, sealed and never opened. Oroqid is a fort of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] on the [[place-wlqtrrgn|Volketor]], the grass range, a hundred miles beyond the last caravanserai of the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]]. Over its gate a square of plaster still carries the painted number of a legion. Whatever the chests hold, the standards are the thing a Vilérian wants.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody has taken them. The strongroom is shut by an iron door at the foot of a stair, and a deadfall of dressed stone waits on the stair for the first person to step wrongly. The tribe that waters at the fort's cistern keeps the gate clear and the courtyard swept for the Vilérians' return, and does not go into the headquarters, because it is theirs. It charges for every skin of water, more to go into the headquarters, and does not sell the knowledge of which stone on the stair is loose.

## Its Story {#its-story}

The empire drove a handful of forts into the western margin of the Khazrin in its height, to watch the grass road east, and Oroqid is one of them. The tribes say the garrison was not driven out but called away, and that a force which leaves a fort with its roofs on, its pay in the strongroom and its standards wrapped in oilcloth means to come back for them. The tribes have swept the fort for longer than anyone counts.

[[lore-roadnights|The Nights of the Road]] keeps the story the tribe's riders tell of the one man who went down for the standards. A Bizarian came three years before the telling with a paper that said what was in the strongroom, paid for the water, the yard and the house, and went down the stair with a lamp and two men without asking the tribe about the stair. The loose stone went, a slab came down from the wall above it, and his two men came out without him. He is still on the stair, under the slab, and the standards are still wrapped.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

A legion of the [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|Vilérian Military]] marches beneath its eagle, and each cohort beneath a banner of its own. A standard left behind in a fort is a thing the army has not given up, and the tribes' reading of the fort, that its owners mean to return, rests on it. Nothing else is claimed for them.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **A Vilérian official** who arrived in a Bizarian port with money and no explanation wants one standard out of the strongroom, a particular one, and will not say why. He means to see that nobody else brings it out.
- **The tribe that waters at the fort** must decide whether a man who comes to take the legion's goods away is the return it has swept for.
- **A dovaq gathering the northern tribes** has a reason to want the fort that has nothing to do with the standards: the best cistern on the Volketor's southern road.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- The official pays a party to bring the one standard out of the strongroom, and the tribe's elders pay another to learn what he wants before he arrives ([[place-roqid|Oroqid]]).
- A party that pays to go into the headquarters finds the Bizarian's paper in the dust of the stair, with the strongroom's contents listed in a clerk's hand.

:::secret {title="What the oilcloth holds"}
The bundles hold the banners of the garrison's cohorts, each on its staff with its cohort's number and the legion's number worked under the crossbar. The legion's eagle is not among them. A legion marches with its eagle, and the garrison that was called away took it. The banners were left because the garrison expected to come back to the fort it had built, exactly as the tribes say.
:::
