## Overview

Vahúrdash is the fifth of the seven Khazryn cities, and for a long time it was only ruins. A drought long ago gave it to the desert, and its broken walls stood visible in the deep interior of the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn Desert]] for generations with nobody living in them. In the last generation determined Khazryn reclamation brought people back, and a small settlement of perhaps four thousand now lives among the ruins, kept barely alive by the people who chose to return.

## A Revived House

The **House of Vahúrdash** was revived with the settlement. Its head, **Khoršád-Vahūr**, holds the title by recent acclamation rather than ancient right, and on the [[affiliation-khzrncnfdrtn|Khazryn Confederation]]'s Council he is treated as a kind of seventh among equals. The house keeps only a token guard. It is the one Khazryn city ever taken back from the desert; the four greatest are held by the Ātárzád.
