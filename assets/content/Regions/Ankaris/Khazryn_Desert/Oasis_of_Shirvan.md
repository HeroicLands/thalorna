The Oasis of Shirvan is an oasis settlement in the [[place-swoasisbelt|Southwestern Oasis-Belt]] of the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn Desert]], the irrigated corner of the desert where well-watered oasis cities and agricultural districts line its southern margin, held by the Ātárzád and the Khazryn and claimed by both.
