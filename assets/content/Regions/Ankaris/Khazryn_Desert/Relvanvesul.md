## Overview

Two miles east of [[place-zargandur|Zargandur]], where the snowmelt river's old bed runs out into the gravel and the weirs have left it dry, a dry wash cuts a long shallow scar across the plain. The Tellumi of the quarter call it **Relvanvesut**, "the princes' bones". The [Zhoqarod]{.name data-type="affiliation"} garrison does not call it anything. They mark it on no map and keep no picket there.

It is a second grave of the fall, distinct from the field of [[place-henhulpedin|Henhutpedin]] north of the walls, where the war-house's riders died on the pasture. The riders were the army. The bones in the wash are the household: the princes' women and children, the old officers who stayed to keep the palace, the palace guard, the clerks and the priests of the shrines, taken from the palace court on the third day after the fall and driven into a trench cut across the wash. They were not read and no lamps were lit. The grave was never consecrated.

## What Lies in It

The treasure of the palace lies with the bodies. The fire-priests of the [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Tribes of Atarzád]], of the Zhoqarod, judged the gold vessels of the Tellumi shrines, with their images of the twelve gods, to be unclean to the Flame and unfit to melt for any use of the Flame-born, and they ordered it buried with those who had kept it. Cups, lamp-stands, a prince's chain of office, seals and signets, and the thin plates of beaten gold that the Tellumi lay on the eyes of their great dead went into the trench with the household. The Zhoqarod who filled it took none. The soldiers who stood at the lip said afterwards that the dead looked at them.

The lowest layer holds sixty Zhoqarod soldiers in leather, laid with their hands folded and tied: the garrison men killed in the palace court when the household rose, whom the priests would not burn because the court was defiled. The household above them lies layer on layer, black and matted and packed with sand, with a plate of beaten gold on every closed eye.

Ninety years of wind have uncovered part of the wash and wind-blown gravel has covered the rest. It is not hard to find.

## The Raiders

The grave-gold has reached the tribes. A band of fifteen or twenty, Sovidur riders of three small tribes and a few hired diggers, has made a camp at the lower end of the wash and has been cutting into the trench for a season. They have taken out some gold. They have lost a third of their number, two to the dead and the rest to what the dead do to the mind of a man who sleeps near them. The survivors keep a fire going at all hours and sleep in the middle of the camp.

## What Answers

The dead of the trench are unreceived, some six hundred of them, which is enough to make a field. Left alone it would have been a quiet, thin one. It has been worked on. A field that has been dug into is thin whatever it was, and the dead come out of it.

What comes out is not the household. [[lore-theshadow|The Shadow]] comes in by the opening the raiders have cut and puts on the dead as a man puts on a coat. The thing that stands up in the wash on a dark night wears a prince's chain and a cracked gold mask and moves with the stiff patience of a corpse that has been told what to do. It does not speak.

The Atarzád fire-priests of the garrison say that only fire puts these dead down. They say it flatly, and they do not say how they know. A Zhoqarod Delkoshekh will give a party a fire-pot and a lamp kindled at the temple altar, on terms, and will not say what is owed.

## Who Knows

- **The Tellumi quarter.** Some say the lamps lit at every door until dawn are for the household in the wash, though no one in the quarter will say so to an outsider.
- **The Zhoqarod.** The garrison has been ordered to keep away from the wash. The order came from the prefect in the old palace and carries no reason.
- The [House of Zargandur]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}. [Khavar of Zargandur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, in exile in the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]], wants a signet from the trench to prove who died in the palace and where. He would pay for a seal and for a count of the dead.
- **The Zhoqarod fire-priests.** A Delkoshekh on picket duty above the wash knows what lies in its lowest layer: the garrison dead of the third day, whom the priests would not burn. The knowledge is why the priests know that fire works, and it is a secret the garrison's priests keep from the prefect.
- **The raiders.** The band's chief believes the Zhoqarod poisoned the grave. He is wrong, and he does not say so aloud because it keeps the others digging.

## Reaching It

The wash is a two-hour walk from the east gate over open gravel, no cover and no water. The Zhoqarod do not patrol it. The raiders' camp is at the lower end, in the lee of a bank, and its fire can be seen from the walls on a clear night.
