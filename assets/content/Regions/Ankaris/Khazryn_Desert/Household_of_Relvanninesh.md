The **Household of Relvanninesh** governs [[place-relvanninel|Relvanninesh]], the palace of [Nimeshun]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, three days north-west of [[place-ashkarad|Ashkarad]]. Nimeshun is a cadet line of the craft house of Ashkarad, one of the princely houses of the [[affiliation-khzrncnfdrtn|Tellumi Confederation]], and its household still counts itself the senior house's dependent: each midwinter it sends silver to the [House of Ashkarad]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} in exile at [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Amradad]].

## Who Sits at the Table

Twelve people make up the household. The prince, [Garmelul]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, sits at the head of the long table in the great hall, with his wife, his daughter [Yelhule]{.name data-type="being/npc"} and his brother beside him. Below them sit the steward, the physician, the lamp-maker and five servants. The prince decides; his kin give their counsel at the table, and a Tellumi prince who disregards his brother's counsel does so at his cost. The steward carries out what is decided.

## The Law of the Guest

The household is governed above all by the Tellumi law of the guest, and keeps it with an exactness no other house manages. Every traveler who comes to the gate is a guest. The steward welcomes him, gives him water to wash and a seat at the table, and asks him nothing. The household refuses no guest.

## Meeting It

A party that comes up the line of glass rods from Ashkarad meets the steward at the gate, and within the hour is seated at the long table with the prince's family. The household is polite, curious and very hungry for news, and the prince asks for every scrap of it from the belt and from Amradad.

## Hook

- **The midwinter silver.** For the first time in living memory the household's silver has not reached Amradad. Nimeshay of Ashkarad sends the party to the palace with a letter for his kinsman, and wants to know what answer the prince gives, and whether he gives one at all.
