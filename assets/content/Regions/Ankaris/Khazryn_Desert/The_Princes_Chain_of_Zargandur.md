## Overview {#overview}

The chain of office of the princes of [[affiliation-hsezargandur|the House of Zargandur]] is a heavy chain of gold made to lie across a prince's chest from shoulder to shoulder. A tag of beaten metal hangs from it, cut with a name in the old hand of the [[lore-khazrynclt|Tellumi]]. It lies in [[place-relvanvesul|Relvanvesut]], the trench in a dry wash east of [[place-zargandur|Zargandur]] where the war-house's palace household was buried after the city fell.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The dead do. The chain went into the trench with the household and the gold of the palace shrines, and the [Zhoqarod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} who filled the trench took none of it. A band of grave-robbers under the Sovidur rider [Hosikqenod]{.name data-type="being/npc"} has cut into the trench for a season and has not found it. Since they cut in, the thing that stands up in the wash on a dark night wears a prince's chain across its chest and a cracked gold mask where its face should be.

## Its Story {#its-story}

Zargandur fell in the second year of the Breaking, about 620 AF ([[lore-zargandfall|The Fall of Zargandur]]). The war-house's cavalry rode out of the north gate and died on the pasture, the house's princes among them, and the city fell by noon. On the third day the Zhoqarod drove the palace household out of the palace court and into a trench cut across the wash: the princes' women and children, the old officers, the guard, the clerks and the priests of the shrines.

The fire-priests of the [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Tribes of Atarzád]] judged the gold of the Tellumi shrines unclean to the Flame and unfit to melt for any use of the Flame-born, and ordered it buried with those who had kept it. Cups, lamp-stands, seals and signets, the thin plates of gold the Tellumi lay on the eyes of their great dead, and the prince's chain of office went into the trench with the household. No names were read over it and no lamps were lit.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

The chain does nothing a goldsmith could not make. Its worth is the name on its tag. The Tellumi receive their dead by reading their names, and nobody has read the names of the trench; the chain carries the one name that heads them. The quarter of Zargandur counts its return as a debt to the household, and prices it accordingly.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **A broker in Zargandur's Tellumi quarter**, a soft-spoken man who weighs silver with his eyes shut, wants it more than any seal out of the wash and will not name it. The last two men he sent for it did not come back to be paid.
- **The Tellumi of the quarter** pay a party that returns it a good deal more than a goldsmith would.
- **[[being-gureshayzarg|Gureshay of Zargandur]]**, heir of the house in exile in [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|the Kingdom of Amradad]], wants proof of who died in the palace and where. A signet buys a place at his table; the chain is more than a signet.
- **The prefect's riders** ask for the chain from any party that comes up out of the wash with it, and the Zhoqarod captain who leads them has long wanted an excuse to hang a foreigner.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- Hosikqenod hires guards and diggers at a share of the gold and tells each new hand that the Zhoqarod poisoned the grave ([[scenario-trenchprinces|Masks in the Dry Wash]]).
- The broker in the quarter pays for any seal out of the wash, no questions, and lets a party understand that the chain is the piece he truly wants.
- A Zhoqarod Delkoshekh on picket above the wash lends a party a fire-pot and a lamp kindled at the temple altar, on terms, and asks for nothing gold in return.

:::secret {title="The name on the tag"}
The name cut on the face of the tag is the prince's who rode out of the north gate and died on [[place-henhulpedin|Henhutpedin]]. When word of the riders reached the palace, the household took the chain from its chest and put it on the youngest boy of the line, seven or eight years old, as the last prince in the palace, and scratched his name on the underside of the tag. He went into the trench wearing it, in the third layer, under more gold than anyone near him.

[[lore-theshadow|The Shadow]] wears his body as the Gold Mask. Read over it, the boy's name from the underside of the tag stops it, and the mask falls and shows the child's face. Read over the trench with the rest of the household's names, it quiets the dead rank by rank.
:::
