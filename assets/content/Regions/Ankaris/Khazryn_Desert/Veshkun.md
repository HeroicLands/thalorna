## Overview {#overview}

The people who live in [[place-vahurdash|Vahurdash]] walk on Veshkun every day. The settlement of four thousand is a small thing set down in the northern corner of a much larger plan, and what the sand has not taken of the old city surrounds it on three sides: a grid of streets forty feet wide, houses without roofs, courtyards with the stumps of palms in them, and at the dry rim, where the belt's last streams fail, a long wall of fitted stone with the sand banked to its parapet. This is the fifth city of the Tellumi, in the dry northern rim of the belt, the place where the streams ran out.

The Tellumi call the ruin Veshkun, the lost town, to keep it apart from the living settlement that bears the old name. The clay archive keeps its wells, its debts and its dead in the records of the houses that left it.

## Layers {#history}

Veshkun was the fifth of the seven Tellumi cities, founded after [[place-oasishirvn|Shirvan]], [[place-ashkarad|Ashkarad]], [[place-zargandur|Zargandur]] and [[place-kethramir|Kethramír]] at the rim where the belt's last streams reach. A drought emptied it, and it stood empty for generations; [[lore-velkunsong|The Song of the Lost Town]] is the cloth-tellers' account of how it was lost. Within the last generation Tellumi settlers came back, traced the buried channels from copies of the old plans kept in the other cities' archives, and built the new [[place-vahurdash|Vahurdash]] in its northern corner.

## What the Tellumi Believe

A drought took it, and the Tellumi who tell the story do not say a drought only. They say the springs failed because the city was judged. The princes of the house that ruled it had broken their guest-right and the water left; the people who stayed learned the rest by the empty wells. The house that has reclaimed the ruins hears the story as an insult and counters it with another, that the drought was a trial and that returning is the answer.

## Danger

The danger is the ground. The old cellars run under the new settlement and under the open streets alike, and a floor that has held since the deep past gives way beneath a man's weight and drops him into a vault with a roof of cracked stone over him. Giant Camel Spiders nest in the vaults and come up at dusk to hunt the settlement's goats and sometimes its children; the people of Vahurdash keep their cellar doors barred. Beyond the settlement, at the foot of the great wall, the sand moves, and a street open at noon may be closed by dusk.

## Treasure

The temple quarter is the prize: courses of carved stone, columns with their lettering still sharp, and the foundation of a great hall under the sand. The stones are the only pre-drought work in the belt that has not been quarried by a later house. Below the quarter, in the cellars of the record-keepers, is the thing the [House of Vahurdash]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} wants most, the clay tablets of the old archive.

## The Settlement among the Streets {#present}

[[place-vahurdash|Vahurdash]] lives on the ruin and against it. Its four thousand settlers bar their cellar doors at dusk against the spiders, bar the [[place-velkunvartun|Barred Gate]] at nightfall against the knocking, and lose every caravan that reaches the rim after dark. The revived house holds its title by acclamation, and the exiled houses in the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]], who sit beside it on the [Council of the Princes]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, say that is not a title. The genealogies that would settle the quarrel lie in the clay tablets under the record-keepers' quarter, and nobody has yet brought them out. The house wants them out unbroken. A rival house in exile wants them too, for the opposite purpose.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The tablets unbroken.** [Khorsád-Vahur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, head of the House of Vahurdash, will pay a party to enter the cellars of the record-keepers' quarter and bring the old archive out whole, so that his house can answer the exiles with its own genealogies.
- **The opposite purpose.** A rival house in exile has made the same offer, and wants the tablets in its own hands before the Council of the Princes sits again.
- **The vaults under the lanes.** The camel spiders that come up at dusk take the settlement's goats and sometimes its children, and the settlers want the vaults under their own lanes cleared.
