## Overview {#overview}

The banner-staff of the war-house of [[place-zargandur|Zargandur]] stands upright in the filled trench of [[place-henhulpedin|Henhutpedin]], the blood-field beyond the city's northern wall. The cloth has rotted off it, and what is left is a tall shaft with the house's fittings at its head, leaning a little toward the north gate. It can be seen from the wall by day. Nobody goes to it by night.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody has touched it. The [Zhoqarod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} who filled the trench would not touch the war-house's weapons or its horse-furniture, and they left the staff standing where it was found. A Zhoqarod picket sits at the whitewashed stakes on the field's edge by day and is relieved before dark. The [[affiliation-hsezargandur|House of Zargandur]], in exile in [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|the Kingdom of Amradad]], keeps the names of the riders who lie under it.

## Its Story {#its-story}

On the last morning of Zargandur, in the second year of the Breaking, about 620 AF, the war-house's cavalry rode out of the north gate to meet the Zhoqarod host on the pasture and died there to the last rider ([[lore-zargandfall|The Fall of Zargandur]]). The [[lore-turemrecital|Recitation of the Breaking]] names the riders one by one, and among them is Budishai, who carried the house's banner and did not drop it. The Recitation says the staff was found standing in the trench with the cloth rotted off it, and he beneath. No names were read over the field, and three days after the battle the Zhoqarod drove the bodies into the trench and filled it.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

It marks where the war-house's banner stood when its last rider fell, and where Budishai lies. The field around it is a recurrence: by night the sound of the sortie goes by in the dark, and anyone who sleeps within sight of the stakes dreams of riding out and being cut down. Nothing of that is said of the staff in particular. The Tellumi of the quarter speak of it only as the place where the reading of the riders' names would begin.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **[[being-gureshayzarg|Gureshai of Zargandur]]**, the house's heir, trains in the Kingdom's forces for a reclamation, and the first thing of the war-house's ride to come home to Amradad would be a standard to raise.
- **The Tellumi of the quarter** want the field read before anything is taken from it, and count lifting the staff from unread dead as robbing them.
- **The Zhoqarod** keep the field for their own use and want nothing taken off it, by anyone, for any reason.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- The Tellumi of the quarter want a Delkoshekh got into the city and out again to read the riders' names over the field, beginning at the staff, though the reading is forbidden in Zargandur on pain of hanging.
- A clerk of the House of Zargandur offers a party the price of a good horse to bring the staff's fittings to Amradad, and does not say whether the heir knows of the offer.

:::secret {title="What the field answers to"}
The field does not answer to the staff. A party that lifts it out and carries it off changes nothing on Henhutpedin: the sound comes the next night as before, and the dreams with it. What quiets the field is the reading of the names on the house's tablet, and Budishai's is among them. Lifting the staff before the reading only tells the Zhoqarod picket, at first light, that someone crossed the stakes in the dark.
:::
