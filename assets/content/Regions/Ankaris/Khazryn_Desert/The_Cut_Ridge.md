## Overview {#overview}

**The Cut Ridge** is a long hump of gray rock standing out of the spruce and bog of [[place-gwtkrrgn|Ghotaqor]], many days north of the steppe, where the forest is thickest and no band keeps a summer camp. From the treetops it looks like any ridge of the forest. Close under it, the rock of its southern face is cut square: walls of fitted stone close the mouths of caves, doorways stand in the walls, and passages run back from the doorways into the dark. The deer hunters of the bands know of it and do not go near it, and they call it nothing but the cut ridge.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody holds it. No band claims the ground around the ridge, and the deer herds cross it without a hunter behind them. The wolves of the forest den in the outer caves in some winters.

## Layers {#history}

The bands say the walls were standing before their own people came into the forest, and that nobody they know of cut them. They say that the men who go into the passages come out sick in the head, or do not come out, and that the ridge is no place to shelter in a storm, though it is the best shelter for many days in any direction. Some travelers from the south call the walls the halls of the [[lore-khazariclt|Khazari]], as they call the cut stone of the [[place-nrthnhlds|Old Holds of the Northern Taiga]] on the coast; the bands say only that the cut stone was there before them.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The scholars of [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia]] want the ridge mapped, and the only person who has seen it closely is a deer hunter's widow of an inland band. Her husband followed a wounded deer into the passages two winters ago, came out on his hands and knees, and died in the spring without ever knowing his own children again. She keeps the way to the ridge to herself and guides no one. For the second summer running a scholar has sent silver north for whoever brings back a map of the walls, and word of it has reached the bands by way of the Sovidur traders bound for [[place-qisomrktcmp|Qiiso]]. Two young hunters of the widow's band went looking for the ridge at the first snow to earn it, and they have not come back.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The forest around the ridge is bog to the hock until the new year, and a loaded sled that leaves the frozen ground sinks with its dogs. Wolves den in the outer caves, and a great forest cat hunts the deer that cross the ridge. Inside, the passages themselves are the danger the bands fear: the men who have gone down them came back sick in the head, or not at all.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A ridge of gray rock a quarter of a mile long, its southern face broken by the mouths of caves. Each mouth is closed by a wall of dressed stone, fitted without mortar and standing straight, with a doorway in it the width of one man and the height of a tall one. Behind the walls the caves have been squared and leveled: dry rooms with smoke-blackened ceilings, benches cut into the rock, and passages leading back and down. Square shafts open in the ceilings of the inner rooms, choked with roots and fallen stone. Below the first stair the air is still and cold, and a lamp burns low in it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The widow's way.** The widow will not guide anyone, but she may be persuaded to tell a party where the ridge is if they bring the two young hunters of her band home alive.
- **The scholar's silver.** A scholar of Velanthia pays well for a map of the walls and the doorways, and more for one of the passages behind them.
- **The best shelter.** A party caught by an early storm in the deep forest has the ridge or nothing, and the wolves in the outer caves have the same idea.

# The Truth of the Cut Ridge {#truth .secret}

**The cavern.** The ridge is **Garn-khurád**, "the holding of the ridge", a fortified cavern of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]], abandoned. It was never a hold with its own houses and its own refuge. The houses of the coast holds, the [[place-nrthnhlds|Old Holds of the Northern Taiga]], kept it as their hunting cavern and their watch over the deer country inland, and they found it as they found their holds: a ridge with caves already in it, which they closed with walls, leveled and joined with the least cut that served. It stands in the record of the holds, and every hold's archive keeps where it lies.

**Its leaving.** The cavern was left with the coast holds, when their counts fell as the counts of holds were falling across the world while the Khazari dwindled. Its keepers swept the rooms, laid the hearths cold, took down the racks and carried everything of use back to the coast before the coast holds themselves were left.

**The air.** The lower passages fill with a bad air that has no smell, which makes a man dizzy, then foolish, then still. They run down to a deep chamber where the cavern's keepers stored meat and hides in the cold, and the bad air rises out of a fault in its floor. The keepers drew it up and out of the ridge through the square shafts. Roots and fallen stone have closed the shafts, and the bad air stands in the lower passages up to the first stair. A party that clears one shaft from above makes the passages safe to the deep chamber within a day.

**What is left.** The rooms are empty and in order. Over the door of the deep chamber the keepers cut the cavern's name in [[skill-drthrkscrpt|Pirzath]], and in the floor of the outermost room are the sockets of a gate-bar like a hold's. Nothing in the cavern is valuable to the holds, but a Khazari lintel cut with a name is worth more to a scholar of Velanthia than every fur in the forest.

> [!TIP]
> The widow's husband went down past the first stair after the deer. The bad air explains him, and a party that watches its lamp knows it before it reaches the stair.
