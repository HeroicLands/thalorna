**The Long Dry** is the five-day waterless reach of [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] east of [[place-yemokwell|Yemok]], in [[place-hskrrgn|Ghoshiqor]]. The grass and the close-set springs of [[place-wlqtrrgn|Volketor]] are behind the caravan; sand seas and gravel lie ahead, with [[place-qisomrktcmp|Qiiso]] still far to the east. Nothing here replaces the water loaded at the last well.

The camels carry skins for the horses as well as the light, expensive goods that make the crossing worth its cost. A two-humped camel can go five to seven days between wells with a full load; the horses drink from what the camels carry. This is the first long gap after the easy waters of the western margin, before the road reaches the deep wells of the central sands, three to five days apart.
