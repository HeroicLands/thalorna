**The Long Dry** is the five-day waterless reach of [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] east of [[place-yemokwell|Yemok]], in [[place-hskrrgn|Ghoshiqor]]. The grass and the close-set springs of [[place-wlqtrrgn|Volketor]] are behind the caravan; sand seas and gravel lie ahead, with [[place-qisomrktcmp|Qiiso]] still far to the east. Nothing here replaces the water loaded at the last well.

The camels carry skins for the horses as well as the light, expensive goods that make the crossing worth its cost. A two-humped camel can go five to seven days between wells with a full load; the horses drink from what the camels carry. This is the first long gap after the easy waters of the western margin, before the road reaches the deep wells of the central sands, three to five days apart.

## The Crossing This Season {#present}

Every caravan that enters the Long Dry settles its account at Yemok first, and this season settling is slow. The riders of the sands are demanding a larger share of the toll and are holding a string at the well until they are paid, and a master who waits there buys water at the second-highest price on the road for every day he waits. Out on the crossing, caravans tie a bell to every animal so that nobody drifts out of hearing, and travelers who fall behind say they hear their names called from the dunes. The dust storms of midsummer blow strings off the track, and a guide who does not know the mesas leaves his string three days from any water.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The held string.** At Yemok the riders of the sands hold a string until their share is raised, and its master needs it across the Long Dry before his water money runs out.
- **The bells.** A caravan master who lost a man on his last crossing wants an escort who will ride at the tail of the string and keep every bell in hearing.
- **Off the track.** A string blown off the road by a dust storm reaches an owned spring under twelve palms whose well is chained shut: [[scenario-weightwell|The Weight upon the Well]].
