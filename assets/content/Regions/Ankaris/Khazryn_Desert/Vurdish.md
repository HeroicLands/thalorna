## Overview {#overview}

Above the Tellumi belt the north face of [[place-graznmntns|the Grazian Mountains]] rises in a single gray wall, and at one place in it, a morning's walk above the last terrace, there is a doorway. It is forty feet high and plain, with no carving on it, and the passage beyond slopes upward into the mountain at the pitch of a long staircase. The floor is worn. The walls are dressed smooth, and where the passage branches, which it does within a hundred paces, every junction is cut square with a lintel overhead.

Vurdish, which the Tellumi call by the word for a pass, is a labyrinth. No one has found where it ends. The passages climb, branch and climb again; some open on ledges of the face high above the belt and are sealed with ice, and some run to chambers whose roofs are lost in the dark. The cold deepens as you go up, and the air carries a sound, faint and regular, like water falling on a flat stone a long way off. Who cut it is not known. It was there before the Tellumi had a record.

## Layers {#history}

The passage was cut before the Tellumi kept any record, and nobody names who cut it. The old houses hold that their first lineages came down through it to the springs of the belt in the first age, about 2300 BF, and the houses of [[place-wstrndscnt|the Western Descent]] deny it. Since [The Breaking]{.name data-type="lore/history"} the exiled houses have sent climbers in after the signs on its lintels, and the [[affiliation-hseshirvan|House of Shirvan]] has hired them for ninety years.

## What the Tellumi Believe

The old houses hold that their ancestors came down through Vurdish from the peaks above, in the first age, to the springs of the belt, and that the houses' genealogies root in particular summits that the passage reaches. The houses that trace their descent to [[place-wstrndscnt|the Western Descent]] instead call this a lie told to flatter, and the quarrel is old and bitter. Both agree that a house whose peak is lost has lost its claim to it.

## Danger

The cold is the first danger: a party that goes in at noon in light clothing is in trouble by the second junction. The passages are ancient and cracked, and ice has opened new fissures in some, so a floor that was sound last spring can fall away. Crag Lurkers hunt the ledges and the first mile of the passage from the ceiling, dropping on whoever carries the lamp. Deeper in, a Gloomhound, the pale hound of the lightless places, follows a party at the limit of its light and does not attack until the light fails.

## Treasure

What a party can carry out is not silver. In the upper passages are signs cut into the lintels, and a house that can show a sign from the passage that leads to its ancestral peak can prove a claim the exiled houses in the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]] have been unable to prove since [The Breaking]{.name data-type="lore/history"}. Beyond that, nothing is certain. Cold air preserves things, and no one has gone far enough to see what.

## Two Houses at the Doorway {#present}

An exile house at Amradad has hired an expedition to find the passage to its ancestral peak, and a rival house on the [Council of the Princes]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} of the Tellumi has hired another to stop it. Neither has been told what the other was told. A sign cut on a lintel of the right passage would prove a claim the exiles have been unable to prove since the Breaking, and the Tellumi hold that the owner of a peak owns the water that comes down from it. The houses of the Western Descent watch both, because a sign from Vurdish would weigh in the oldest quarrel the Council keeps unruled.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The passage to the peak.** The exile house wants a sign from the passage that leads to its peak, carried down and shown at Amradad.
- **Stop them.** The rival house wants the first expedition turned back, by persuasion or by the mountain.
- **The western houses.** The [[affiliation-hsemernagord|House of Mernagord]], which holds that the first lineages walked down the Western Descent, wants to know what comes out of Vurdish before the Council does.
