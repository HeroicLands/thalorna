Coming along the belt road from the west, you see the cliff a day before you see the city: a wall of pale rock across the southern sky where the Grazian foothills close in on the belt, white in the morning and the color of honey by evening. Closer, the wall turns out to be full of holes. Rows of doorways and windows are cut into its face, stairs zigzag between them, and at night a few lamps burn in the high chambers, so that the cliff looks like a city standing on end above the real one.

## Overview {#overview}

**Vurinkarvem**, "the holy stone" in the [[skill-tellumilng|Tellumi Language]], is the cliff over the [[place-oasishirvn|Oasis of Shirvan]], the first and holiest of the seven Tellumi cities. It is a face of pale sandstone some four hundred feet high and more than a mile long, and the great spring of Shirvan breaks from its foot into a basin the size of a market square. Everything in the city is arranged by its distance from that water, and the cliff stands over all of it.

The face is cut with chambers and pilgrim stairs, and some of the chambers are older than the city. Since [The Breaking]{.name data-type="lore/history"} the cliff has belonged to the [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Tribes of Atarzád]] with the rest of Shirvan, and the great fire-temple of the [[affiliation-ataros|Faith of Ataros]] on the basin's edge keeps its offering flame burning at the cliff's foot.

## The Face

The cliff reads from the bottom up.

- **The foot.** The spring comes out of the rock at the base of the cliff in several mouths, the largest under an overhang as high as a house, and fills the basin. The Tellumi draw on the lower side of the basin, on the days their quarter is given; they do not drink at the fire-temple's side, and they have not told any Atarzád official why.
- **The quarry ledges.** At the western end the face is stepped where the stone has been cut away for building, and the quarrymen still dress the white sandstone there and lower it on ropes. The caravans from [[place-kethramir|Kethramír]] carry it east.
- **The stairs and chambers.** Above the basin the face is honeycombed. Stairs cut into the rock climb from ledge to ledge, some with a rail of rope and most with none, and chambers open off them, from niches a man can kneel in to halls with pillars left standing in the stone. The chambers are empty of images. The guides of the lower town take groups of pilgrims of both peoples up the stairs for a fee, and they know which stairs are sound this year.
- **The lower galleries.** Below the level of the basin, other galleries run into the cliff. After rain they flood, and a flood can fill a chamber in the time it takes to climb out of it. Pilgrims are told the lower galleries are flooded and closed.

## Pilgrims and Guides

The cliff is the reason many pilgrims come to Shirvan at all. Tellumi from the free oases and from the other held cities climb to the high chambers on the old feast days, leave a lamp, and come down without saying a prayer aloud, because no worship of any god but Ataros may be heard on Atarzád ground. Atarzád pilgrims climb too, for the view of the basin and the fire-temple, and the guides are careful to keep the two sets of pilgrims on different stairs.

The guides are Tellumi of the lower town, licensed by the tribe that holds the city, and they are the people to ask about anything on the face: which chamber is dry, which stair lost a step in the winter, where the lamps are lit and by whom. Their fee goes up after rain.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The cliff is Atarzád ground with the rest of Shirvan, and no worship but that of Ataros may be heard on it. The guides keep Tellumi and Atarzád pilgrims on different stairs, and the [Ozhmarod]{.name data-type="affiliation"} fire-wardens want to know who lights the lamp in a chamber no stair reaches. At the cliff's foot the great spring stands lower than the old men remember, and the water-masters of the city have reset the channel-hours twice without finding the cause.

## Hooks and Dangers

- **The fall.** A stair on the upper face gave way under a party of pilgrims last spring, and the family of a man who died wants to know whether the step was cut. The guide who led the party has left the city.
- **The lamp in the high chamber.** For a month a lamp has burned every night in a chamber nobody can reach by any stair the guides know. The fire-wardens want to know who lights it, and so does the quarter.
- **The quarry.** The quarrymen have cut back into the stone at the western end and opened a chamber that was sealed. They have bricked it up again and are asking a price to unbrick it for anyone who wants a look.
- **The flood.** Rain on the foothills fills the lower galleries in minutes. A party below the basin level when the water comes has to find a stair up, and not every stair goes up.

:::secret {title="The lower galleries"}
Not every lower gallery floods, and not every one is closed. An old pilgrim stair that begins in a courtyard of the Tellumi quarter goes down below the basin to a gallery of seven bays, and at the bottom of it is [[place-peldenderal|Peshdenderat]]. The guides who tell pilgrims the lower galleries are flooded keep that stair, and the quarter's water-masters watch the courtyard.
:::
