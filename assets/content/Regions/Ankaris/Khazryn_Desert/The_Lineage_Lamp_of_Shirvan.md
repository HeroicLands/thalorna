## Overview {#overview}

The lineage lamp is the lamp of the great fire-temple of [[affiliation-ataros|Ataros]] at the [[place-oasishirvn|Oasis of Shirvan]], whose offering flame burns at the foot of the [[place-vurinkarvem|Vurinkarvem]] cliff. It is a lamp of dark bronze with a closed cover, made to carry a flame down a mountain stair in wind. Every festival the temple's Shekhul carries it up [[place-mountshofar|Mount Shofar]] and brings it down relit at the [[place-unconsflame|Unconsuming Flame]].

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The temple holds it, and its Shekhul carries it. The Shekhul is [[being-valsuma|Valsuma]], the first woman to hold the flame of Shirvan, who has carried the lamp up the mountain at every festival for twenty-six years. She is too frail to carry it at the next, and the senior priests of the temple disagree about who carries it in her place.

## Its Story {#its-story}

The [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Atarzád]] raised the great fire-temple of Shirvan from the stones of the Tellumi shrines after they took the city. When a new fire-temple is consecrated, its first flame is carried down from the Unconsuming Flame in a sealed lamp, and the fire-temples of the four held cities each record such a lamp as the first entry in their fire-lineage. Shirvan's record begins with this one. The temple has never let its fire fail, and the lamp goes up the stair each festival with the pilgrims so that the flame of Shirvan is seen to come again from the mountain.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

It carries the temple's flame to the mountain and back, and with it the temple's standing as the most important fire-temple of the held cities. The Atarzád say that a pilgrim who has seen the Flame carries it home in the lamp. Nothing else is claimed for it. Who carries it at the festival is a matter of rank, and the priest who carries it in Valsuma's place is widely taken for her successor.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **The senior priests of the temple** each want the carrying for a candidate of their own, and [[being-shrmnhthvn|Sharminah Tahvan]], the temple's warrior-priestess, is their favorite to succeed Valsuma.
- **Valsuma** wants a priest to carry it who would keep the flame and not the sword.
- **The [Ozhmarod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}**, who hold Shirvan and revere the temple, want the lamp to go up and come down as it always has, by whatever hand.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- Valsuma asks a party of pilgrims to escort the priest who carries the lamp up Mount Shofar in her place, because the senior priests disagree about who that should be ([[being-valsuma|Valsuma]]).
- A priest who lost the carrying pays a party to see that the lamp comes down the stair unlit, so that the festival shames the one who carried it.

:::secret {title="What is under the foot"}
The lamp is older than the temple. It is Tellumi work, a bronze lamp from the shrine of the Mother of the Oasis at the spring, taken in the sack of Shirvan. The shrine's sign is hammered flat under its foot and blacked over with pitch, and the priests who chose it for the first flame either did not see the sign or did not say so. Valsuma has seen it. She recognized the work for what it is the first year she carried the lamp, and has kept it with the other things she has not told the temple.
:::
