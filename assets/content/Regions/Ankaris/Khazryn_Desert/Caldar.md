## Overview

Caldar is known across the scholarly world for one institution: **the University of Caldar**, whose research faculty in alchemy has made the study of mineral transmutation—the stable conversion of base metals and common stones into substances of greater rarity and value—its specialty.

## The University

The renowned **Master Aldrin Kovoss** trained [[being-kayvondzrd|Kayvonad Zarid]] there from the age of fifteen, and Kayvonad's thesis on the heat underlying copper-to-silver transmutation was hailed as groundbreaking. The faculty is also a place of long quarrels. When **Dârvazad Kesvarûn** published a paper showing mathematical errors in Kayvonad's foundational work and Kayvonad answered with accusations of plagiarism, the scandal divided the department. Kesvarûn is now the University's favored elder statesman of alchemical research; Kayvonad keeps the title of Senior Researcher and little else, and a faction of younger scholars, **the Cabal of Progressive Theorists**, openly calls his methods outdated. Wealthy mineral merchants of the Khazryn fund research there in exchange for first option on any transmutation with a commercial use.
