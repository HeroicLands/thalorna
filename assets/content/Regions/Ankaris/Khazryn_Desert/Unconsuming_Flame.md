The stair from Beit-Shofár ends on a shelf of black stone high on the southern face of [[place-mountshofar|Mount Shofar]], and the Flame stands in the middle of it. It is about the height of a kneeling man, pale yellow at the root and clear at the tip, and it comes straight out of the bare rock: no hearth, no wood, no oil, and no crack in the stone beneath it. It makes no sound. The wind bends it and lets it go. A pilgrim who reaches the top of the stair at dawn sees it before seeing anything else on the shelf, and most kneel there, at the last step, before going any nearer.

## Overview {#overview}

The **Unconsuming Flame** is the first holy site of the [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Atarzád]]. In their teaching it is the flame in which [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]] revealed himself to the prophet [Yotekhor]{.name data-type="being/npc"} and gave him the [Promise of the Tellumel]{.name data-type="lore"}, burning in the open air on bare stone for three days and nights without fuel and without diminishing, and it still burns. Below it on the slope stands the tomb of Yotekhor, the second holy site, and at the mountain's foot lies the priestly seat of [[place-beitshofar|Beit-Shofár]].

The Flame is kept by the Toqel, the Keeper of the Unconsuming Flame and the senior religious authority of the tribes, who is always a [Burqalod]{.name data-type="affiliation"}, always elderly, and raised only when the previous Toqel dies. The present Toqel is [Sighotor son of Nozhikor]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, about eighty winters old.

## Layers {#history}

The Atarzád date the Flame from the Promise. At the end of their Exodus their forebears came down off the Diqraqor past Mount Shofar, and here, in their account, Ataros revealed himself to Yotekhor in a flame that burned three days and nights on bare stone. Yotekhor died on the slope below without entering the belt, and his tomb stands there. Under his successor, the Zhudek [Nozhikor]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the tribes took four Tellumi cities in the War of the Twelve Years, about 619 to 630 AF, and the fire-temple of each city records as its first flame a lamp carried down from the shelf.

## The Shelf

The shelf is a flat step of the mountain's black rock perhaps sixty paces across, open to the sky and to the drop on its southern edge. A ring of black stones set at twenty paces from the Flame marks where pilgrims stop, and only the Toqel and the keepers who serve him go inside it. A low stone house at the back of the shelf, against the rock face, is where they sleep and eat. There is no roof over the Flame, no altar, no image and no wall, because the [[affiliation-ataros|Faith of Ataros]] makes no image of its god and the Flame alone stands for him.

There is no ash on the shelf and no soot on the rock round the Flame. Rain falls on it in spring and snow in winter, and the keepers record nothing different in either.

## The Keepers

In an ordinary fire-temple the Fire-Keepers feed the flame, shelter it from wind and rain and collect its ashes, and a candidate is ordained by keeping a portion of a temple fire for a year without its failing. On the shelf there is nothing to feed and nothing to collect. The keepers keep watch instead, day and night, in turns of four, and they keep the ring, the stair and the words. A Keeper's year is counted in nights sat beside the Flame.

The keepers light lamps from the Flame for the tribes. At each festival every pilgrim family carries a lamp up the stair and brings it down lit, and when a new fire-temple is consecrated its first flame is carried down from the mountain in a sealed lamp. The fire-temples of the four held cities each record such a lamp as the first entry in their fire-lineage.

## The Festivals and the Council

Three times a year, at the spring equinox, at midsummer and at the autumn equinox, the tribes gather at the Flame for [The Festival of the Promise]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} and hear the [Covenant]{.name data-type="lore/law"} recited in full before it. At midsummer upward of fifty thousand pilgrims come, and the keepers bring them up the stair tribe by tribe, in an order they set, so that the shelf is never more than full.

The Flame also has a part in government. When the [Council of the Twelve]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} considers acclaiming a Zhudek, a judge with broad authority for the length of a crisis, it consults the Toqel first, and the Toqel reads the will of Ataros in the Flame. A Toqel who comes down from the shelf and says nothing has given an answer too.

## What People Say

- **The Atarzád** say the Flame has burned since the Promise and will burn as long as a flame burns on a Flame-born hearth, and that a pilgrim who has seen it carries it home in the lamp.
- **The keepers** say that the Toqel sees in the Flame what the rest of them do not, and they do not ask him what it is.
- **The Tellumi** do not climb the stair. Few strangers do, though the keepers do not turn away one who comes up in peace outside a festival and keeps behind the ring.
- **Scholars of [[place-caldar|Kaldar]]** have asked more than once to examine the shelf and the stone under the Flame. The Toqel has refused every one of them.

:::secret {title="What the keepers know"}
Nothing feeds the Flame that anyone has found. The keepers have sat with it in every season, and in their keeping it has not changed: not its height, not its color, not in wind, rain or snow. A lamp lit from it is an ordinary flame, which burns its own oil and goes out when the oil is gone, and every keeper has seen one go out. They do not tell the pilgrims, who believe the lamp they carry home holds the Flame itself.
:::

## Two Voices for Ataros {#present}

The keepers sit their turns of four as they always have, and the Toqel who reads the Flame is about eighty. Another voice now claims to know the will of [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]]: [Aqeshat]{.name data-type="being/npc"} the Prophet, who says he has shown her the return of Yotekhor in the flesh. Sighotor has not spoken of her from the shelf, and the tribes read his silence each in their own way. The Council of the Twelve may soon need the Flame's answer on a second matter, because word has come from the steppe of a union rising in the north, and a Zhudek is never acclaimed without the Toqel. Every pilgrim family that climbs the stair this year asks the keepers what the Flame has shown, and the keepers answer that they keep the ring, the stair and the words.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **Behind the ring.** A scholar of [[place-caldar|Kaldar]], refused by the Toqel, wants a close description of the stone under the Flame and pays a party to climb outside a festival, when the keepers let a stranger who comes in peace stand at the ring.
- **What the Flame has shown.** The followers of Aqeshat want the Toqel to speak for her or against her before the midsummer recitation, and they press any pilgrim who is admitted to the shelf to bring down what the keepers say of her.

:::secret {title="What the keepers know"}

- **The lamp that goes out.** Every keeper has watched a lamp lit from the Flame burn its oil and go out. A Kaldar scholar who learned it would hold something the keepers have never told the pilgrims, and the keepers do not want it spoken in a year when a prophet's following grows each season.

:::
