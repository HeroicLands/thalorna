## Overview {#overview}

The First Tablet is the oldest thing the [[affiliation-hseshirvan|House of Shirvan]] owns and the one it shows to nobody. It is a small tablet of clay pressed with one word, _here_. The house holds that the first lineage of the [[lore-khazrynclt|Tellumi]] pressed it at the great spring of [[place-oasishirvn|Shirvan]] when it came down from the snow peak, and every Shirvan funeral answers the snow peak with the city's name.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The house keeps it at its court in exile in [[place-shebarakhend|Shebarakhend]], the court city of [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|the Kingdom of Amradad]]. Its keeping is an office, the Keeper of the First Tablet, held by an Elder of the House. [[being-mureshashirv|Muresha of Shirvan]], the heir-in-exile, had it from her father with the house, the seat at the Council and a debt she paid off in nine years.

## Its Story {#its-story}

[[lore-comingdown|The Coming Down]] gives the story in its roll of the seven peaks. From Gilsumtenhur, the snow peak, the first and highest, the first lineage came down carrying a tablet of wet clay with nothing pressed on it, and pressed it at the water with a reed: _here_. That is the first tablet, the roll says, and the house keeps it and does not need to say more. The lineage settled the great spring under the pale cliff, and that settlement is Shirvan.

The house ruled Shirvan until the eleventh year of the Breaking, when the city fell and its archive burned. What the fleeing households carried out of the city went with them into exile, and the First Tablet went with the house. How it was carried out the house does not tell.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

It is the first word of the house's right to Shirvan. Muresha's case, a library of treaties, deeds and water-rolls built to prove that the city belongs to her house by right, begins where the tablet does. The house says nothing more of it than the roll says, and claims for it no power but its age.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **The House of Shirvan** wants it kept exactly as it is, unseen, by an Elder of the House.
- **The [[affiliation-hsemernagord|House of Mernagord]]** leads the houses of the west in denying that any lineage came down through [[place-vurdil|Vurdish]], and a tablet nobody outside the house has seen is a claim it has every reason to want examined.
- **Collectors in Amradad** who buy Tellumi clay know the tablet exists, and name a price for it now and then to see who answers.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- A collector in Shebarakhend lets it be known that he has been offered the First Tablet, and the house needs to learn who made the offer and what they are selling.
- An elder of the House of Mernagord proposes at the Council that the princes' oldest tablets be laid on the table together, and the House of Shirvan wants the motion dead before the session.

:::secret {title="What the clay will bear"}
The tablet was dried in the sun at the spring and never fired. It has been crumbling at its edges for longer than anyone in the house has kept it, and the word on its face is whole only because nobody handles it. The Keeper carries it in a cedar box packed with wool and opens the box only for the house, and never in a draught. A hard knock would break it. Muresha knows that a house which showed its First Tablet at the Council might bring home a handful of dust, and that is the whole reason it is shown to nobody.
:::
