## Overview

**The Tellumel**, "the land of the Tellumi", is the irrigated strip along the north foot of [[place-graznmntns|the Grazian Mountains]], on the southern edge of the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn Desert]]. It is the country of the [[affiliation-khzrncnfdrtn|Tellumi Confederation]] and the [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Tribes of Atarzád]]: the Tribes hold its four great cities, the Confederation the peripheral oases, and each claims the whole.

The strip is forty to eighty miles deep where the snowmelt streams of the Grazian Mountains reach it and broken where they do not, and it runs perhaps seven hundred miles from [[place-wstrndscnt|the Western Descent]] eastward. Every city of the Tellumi stands in it. North of it the black rock of [[place-dkrqrrgn|Diqraqor]] begins within a day's ride, and beyond that the sand. About 1,800,000 people live in the strip, which is most of the region's population on a small fraction of its ground; the tribes of the waste regard it as a foreign country, and raid its outer gardens when they dare.

## Economy

The principal exports of the Atarzád lands are dates, palm-sugar, leather, salt (from pans on the western fringe), and the great **Khazryn horses**—the long-legged desert breed, prized across [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris]] for endurance, that the Atarzád raise on the open pasturelands between the oases. Imports are metals, fine cloth, glass, books, and the exotica of the wider world; the [Teghumod]{.name data-type="affiliation"} merchant-houses run caravans to [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Amradad]], to the Taruzelian ports, and (during the better-relations decades) into the Kingdom proper.

In the still-Tellumi oases—[[place-mehrnagord|Mernagord]], [[place-ushtraber|Ushtra-ber]], the small [[place-vahurdash|Vahurdash]] settlement—the economy is the traditional oasis economy: dates, palm-sugar, garden produce, salt (in Mernagord's case), and trade with passing caravans. These are small economies; the populations are not large, the surpluses are modest, and the wealth of the still-Tellumi lands is reckoned in continuity rather than in volume.
