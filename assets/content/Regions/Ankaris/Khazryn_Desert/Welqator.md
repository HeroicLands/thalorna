The last caravanserai of the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]] stands on a bare rise, and from its roof you can see everything the Byzarian League claims: a paved road, a water tank, a stable yard, and eastward a band of grass the color of straw. Past the end of the paving no one patrols. By the second evening a rider has appeared on a ridge, paced the caravan for an hour and gone, and the master has begun counting silver into small bags.

**Welqator**, "the grass range" in [[skill-sowideslng|Sowides]], is the western margin of the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn]], where the dry foothills below the March fall away into steppe. It faces [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia]] to the northwest, Byzaría to the west and the [[place-dunharargn|Dunhara]] to the southwest. The first five hundred miles of [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] cross it.

## Land and Water

The margin is grass and scrub in spring, green for six weeks and then gold, and dust by midsummer. Rain falls here as it falls nowhere else in the region, a thin spring shower that fills the clay pans and the dry creek beds for a week. Springs and small wells lie a day or two apart, close enough that a caravan reaches water every evening, and each is owned by a tribe. The easy water is the reason the margin is the most crowded part of the waste outside the south, with about 280,000 people.

In the southwest the grass gives out into the [[place-hekardesert|Desert of Hek'ar]], a barren tract below the March where nothing grows and nothing invites a traveler. Between the springs the ground is flat enough that a rider sees dust from ten miles off, and nothing hides a column.

## The People

The tribes of Welqator are the best practiced with outsiders on the whole road, because they have had the longest dealings with the League and with Velanthia. They sell passage at the edge of the March and expect it bought. A caravan that arrives with silver and the right courtesies finds a sowid, a rider, waiting to join it and a price ready; a stranger who arrives with neither is stripped of everything but his clothes and sent back west, and killed only if he fights.

Their herds are horses and sheep, and the tribes test the March's herds and unescorted strings in spring. They also carry the Velanthian frontier trade, grain east and horses and furs west, and some of their chiefs are as comfortable in a Velanthian host's hall as in their own tents. No tribe here holds more than a stretch of road a few days long. A caravan crosses perhaps eight ranges in the first five hundred miles, and the master pays each separately.

## Dangers

Raiders test unescorted strings; a string with a rider of the right tribe is rarely touched, and a string with none is lawful prey. Dust storms blow up in midsummer and last three days. In Hek'ar, knights of the [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] hunt the Eye of Velok among the [[place-ruinsarkor|Ruins of Arkor]] and fight one another over how. A fort from the days of the Vylarian Empire, [[place-roqid|Roqid]] ("the camp"), stands on a low ridge near the road with its cistern still filling, and the tribe that waters there charges for the water and for its silence.

## Hooks

- The Black Flame faction in Hek'ar has run out of coin and offers a caravan master double rates to carry a sealed chest east, and will not say what is in it.
- A Welqator tribe holds a Vylarian legion standard from the fort at Roqid, and an envoy of the Empire has come asking to buy it back without saying why it matters.
- A rider who joined a caravan at the March's edge has been stealing its tokens one at a time, and the master must decide whether to say so at the first hand-off well, [[place-yemokwell|Yemok]], where the rider's own tribe will hear.
