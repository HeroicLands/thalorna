## Overview

A square of dressed stone stands on the grass-and-dust country of the [[place-wlqtrrgn|Volketor]], the grass range, a hundred miles beyond the last caravanserai of Bizaria's [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]]: four walls twenty feet high, a tower at each corner, a single gate facing west. It is the work of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], which in its height drove a handful of such forts into the western margin to watch the grass road east. The tribes call it Oroqid, the camp, as they would any place a force of strangers halted, and they never found a reason to rename it.

Inside, the plan is the one every legion builds: a parade ground, a headquarters at its center with a strongroom below, barrack blocks along the walls, and a cistern under the courtyard that is fed by a spring the engineers found and the garrison never lost. The roofs are gone and the timbers with them. The walls are whole, and over the gate a square of plaster still carries the painted number of a legion.

## Layers {#history}

The [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] drove the fort into the western margin at the height of its reach, one of a handful built to watch the grass road east, and its engineers found the spring that feeds the cistern. The garrison left with its pay-chests and standards still sealed in the strongroom, and the tribes hold that it was called away rather than driven out. The tribe that waters at the cistern has kept the gate clear and charged for the water ever since.

## What the Tribes Believe

The tribes hold that the Vylarians will come back for it. They were not driven out, the tribes say; they were called away, and a force that leaves a fort fully furnished means to return to it. So the tribes that water at the cistern keep the gate clear and the courtyard swept, and will not set foot in the headquarters. Generations of herders have swept it.

## Danger

The danger is the tribe that holds the water. The cistern is the best on the Volketor's southern road, and the tribe that holds it charges a price for every skin, charges a larger one to anyone who is not a friend of the tribe, and charges a larger one still to anyone who goes into the headquarters. A party that refuses to pay may camp outside the walls and take its chances with the riders who sit on the ridge. The strongroom below the headquarters is shut by an iron door, and a deadfall of dressed stone waits on its stair for the first person to step wrongly.

## Treasure

The strongroom holds the fort's pay-chests, sealed and never opened, and [[miscgear-roqidstds|the legion's standards]], wrapped in oilcloth. Whatever the chests hold, the standards are the thing a Vylarian wants.

## The Swept Fort {#present}

The tribe that waters at the cistern keeps the gate clear and the courtyard swept for the Vylarians' return, charges for every skin, and keeps riders on the ridge. It has had word that a Vylarian is coming at last: an official who arrived in a Bizarian port with money and no explanation, and who wants one of the standards from the strongroom, a particular one. The tribe must decide whether a man who comes to take the legion's goods away is the return it has swept for. Word has reached the [[place-wlqtrrgn|Volketor]] too of a dovaq gathering the northern tribes, and the best cistern on the southern road is a thing any union would want.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The standard.** The Vylarian official wants one standard out of the strongroom and will not say why. He pays a party to bring it out, and he means to see that nobody else does.
- **The tribe's price.** The tribe that holds the cistern has been told the official is coming. Its elders want to know what he wants before he arrives, and they charge a larger price still to anyone who goes into the headquarters.
