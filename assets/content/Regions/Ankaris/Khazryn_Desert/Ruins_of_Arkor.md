## Overview

Bolid, the tower, is what the tribes of the Welqator call it, and it is the one word of theirs for a city they never saw standing. The [[place-hekardesert|Desert of Hek'ar]] has buried it to the second story: a mile-wide rectangle of low dunes with an even, rectilinear shape, and at its center a single tower top, thirty feet of cracked brick, standing out of the sand with a lamp-chamber's black ring around its crown. The Black Flame calls the city Arkor, and that name is the one on every map in the Eastern March.

Arkor is the westernmost of the [[lore-towercities|Tower Cities]], the lamp-lit league that carried the first road across the waste from about 1000 BF until the steppe broke it about 190 BF. Its builders left no name for it and no tablet. What is known of it is that the road reached it first from the Eastern March side, that its tower was lit every night for eight centuries, and that it was buried when the desert moved over it, quietly, rather than being sacked.

## What Is Said

The tribes hold that the sand is a patient animal and that it took Arkor in its sleep. They keep clear of the tower and of the shallow bowl around its foot where the sand is oddly cold, and a herder will not drive stock within a mile of it at night. The Black Flame says something else. In its teaching the city was a seat of the fire demon whose essence the **Eye of Velok** holds, a relic of Ústaron, and the desert buried it to hide that fact. Neither reading is confirmed.

## The Search for the Eye

The **Blazing Oath**, a secretive faction within the Black Flame's **Servants of Sycânus** devoted to recovering the ancient relics of Ústaron, has been sent to retrieve the Eye, and [[being-srdrzhsycnknghtblckflm|Sir Drazha Sycâni]] leads it. An expedition into Arkor faces ancient traps, the harsh conditions of the desert, foreign mercenaries and rival factions of the Black Flame itself. Anyone who goes in after the Eye, or after the knights who went in first, goes in with all of them.

## Danger

The danger is the knights, who have a camp near the tower and will kill anything that approaches it who does not carry their badge. Beyond them, the city under the sand is full of chambers whose roofs hold a ton of dune each and whose floors are dry as chalk. Wells, stair-shafts and air-holes are open traps that the league built to carry light and air down to its cellars, and the sand has hidden them. Giant Camel Spiders and Bone Scorpions nest in the buried streets and strike from the dark.

## Treasure

If the Eye exists, it is the treasure the Black Flame wants, and the tribes say it is a rumor. What the buried league certainly left is tower-glass, the clearest ground lenses anyone has seen, in the lamp-chamber of the tower and in the cellars of a dozen counting-houses.

## A Hook

The canon hook stands as it is: the Black Flame's factions are fighting each other for the Eye, and a party that goes into Arkor goes in among them. The tribes of the Welqator have noticed, and they sell passage to every faction at once, with riders for each; a party that buys it learns who else has.
