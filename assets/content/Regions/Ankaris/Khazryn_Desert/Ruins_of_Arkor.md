## Overview

The Ruins of Arkor are an ancient city buried beneath the sands of the [[place-hekardesert|Desert of Hek'ar]]. Nobody living remembers who built it, and what draws people to it now is what it is believed to hold: **the Eye of Velok**, a legendary gem said to carry the essence of a powerful fire demon bound to Ústaron, and to give its bearer command of fire on a massive scale.

## The Search for the Eye

The **Blazing Oath**, a secretive faction within the Black Flame's **Servants of Sycânus** devoted to recovering the ancient relics of Ústaron, has been sent to retrieve it, and [[being-srdrzhsycnknghtblckflm|Sir Drazha Sycâni]] leads it. An expedition into Arkor faces ancient traps, the harsh conditions of the desert, foreign mercenaries and rival factions of the Black Flame itself. Anyone who goes in after the Eye—or after the knights who went in first—goes in with all of them.
