East of the second hand-off well the sand stops as if cut. The ground is black rock and pale gravel, the wind has a bite in it even at noon, and the nights freeze the water in the skins. A caravan that crossed the [[place-longdry|Long Dry]] in a sweat finds itself wrapped in felt and burning dung, and the camels, which can stand heat for a week, begin to lose condition in the cold.

**Idvaqor**, "the east range" in [[skill-sowideslng|Sovidur]], is the last stage of the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]]: stone desert that climbs by long steps to a cold high steppe, and then to the western passes of [[place-tantsouregin|Tantsou]]. [[lore-celestialroad|The Celestial Road]] crosses it in about eight hundred miles and thirty-five days, from the well at [[place-sowidwell|Sovid]] to the Tantsou customs-house on the pass. The southern edge touches [[place-estrnreach|the Eastern Reach]] of [[place-graznmntns|the Grazian Mountains]], where [[place-tamradvar|Tamradvar]] climbs south toward [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]].

## Land and Water

Idvaqor is flat for a hundred miles and ridged for the next, with dry watercourses cut deep into the stone and wells that lie in the beds of them. Water is rare, and it lies far apart: stages of four and five days between wells are the norm, and the long ones run to seven. A few rock cisterns catch the winter snow. In the hills, caverns open in the ridges, and some of them hold cut cisterns that fill by seepage.

Snow falls in winter, and the road closes from first frost to the thaw. Even in spring and autumn the nights are bitter and the days long, and the wind comes down off the Eastern Reach with a taste of ice.

## The People

The tribes of Idvaqor sell the hardest bargain on the road. They are few, perhaps 330,000 across the whole range, and each tribe's wells are life itself to it. A stranger with no passage and no Tantsou pass-token is killed on sight; one with both is a customer, to be escorted, charged and watched. They herd horses, camels and yaks in the high steppe in summer and drive them down to the stone desert's wells in winter.

They live in the shadow of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire]]. Tantsou has treated its western frontier with these tribes as a standing problem for centuries, giving gifts and titles to some chiefs, paying others to keep the pass clear, and sending patrols out from the garrison. Tantsou riders are on the road from about a week out of the pass, and a caravan that is not under a tribal rider's escort by then meets the Empire's wrath as well as the tribes'.

## Current Situation {#present}

The tribes sell the last and hardest passage on the road under the Empire's eye: Tantsou gives gifts and titles to some chiefs, pays others to keep the pass clear, and rides the road a week out from the pass. At [[place-ruweq|Uruvaq]] the tribes have named their price for the painted script, and it is not silver but a thing brought out of Tantsou, which a Tantsou scholar at the western passes wants badly enough to weigh.

## Dangers {#dangers}

The cold takes animals first and men second. [[being-sandwurm|Sand wurms]] live in the deeper desert, and the caravans reroute for a season when one is reported. [[being-erthrvr|Earthreavers]] tunnel under the cavern floors in the ridges.

The tribes hold the caverns as the country's own lungs and bar them to strangers. In one of them, [[place-ruweq|Uruvaq]], "the wind", cisterns cut in the rock hold sweet water in a waterless land, and the walls beside them are painted with a script no one reads. Whoever cut the cisterns left no name behind.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- A Tantsou scholar wants a copy of the painted script from the caverns, and the tribe that holds them wants a price no scholar can pay.
- A Tantsou patrol officer is selling pass-tokens to caravans that have not paid a single tribe, and a tribe has noticed.
- A caravan reached the customs-house with a dead rider's token in the master's pack, and the Tantsou clerk will release the cargo only to the rider's kin.
