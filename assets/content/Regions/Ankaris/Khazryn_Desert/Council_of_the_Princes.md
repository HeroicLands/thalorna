## Overview

The **Council of the Princes**, the _tselvun_ in [[skill-tellumilng|Tellumi]], is the one body that speaks for the whole [[affiliation-khzrncnfdrtn|Tellumi Confederation]]. It meets once a year in the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]], on ground the Kingdom lends it, and it seats the seven princely houses of the Tellumi: the three that still rule their oases and the four whose cities the [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Tribes of Atarzád]] hold. It coordinates, and it does not govern. No oasis takes a law from it, no prince takes an order from it, and the one thing it has never done in two thousand years of sessions is decide a question that would unseat a house.

## The Session

Each house comes in a party. The prince sits for it, or, for the four houses in exile, the man or woman who would rule if the house were restored. The senior elders of the house come behind the prince, and the senior Delkoshekh of its court comes with them. The High Shekhul of [[affiliation-ahurdaen|Ahurdaen]] at the [Great Fire-Temple of Amradad]{.name data-type="place/structure"} convenes the session as a neutral party, a role the Kingdom's Shekhul took up about sixty years ago, when he agreed to underwrite the Council in perpetuity. The present convenor is [Aravdán the Stargazer]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, an astronomer of about sixty who has held the exile community together through three decades of small crises.

The prince of the [[affiliation-hseshirvan|House of Shirvan]], the senior house, speaks first on every question by custom so old that nobody can name its beginning, and the present First of the Seven is the heir-in-exile, [Muresha of Shirvan]{.name data-type="being/npc"}. The [[affiliation-hsevahurdash|House of Vahurdash]] sits as a kind of seventh among equals, because its head holds his title by the acclamation of settlers and not by ancient right.

The Council acts where the seven agree, and it acts on three things:

- **One position toward the Atarzád.** No recognition, no treaty, and a perpetual claim of sovereignty over [[place-oasishirvn|Shirvan]], [[place-ashkarad|Ashkarad]], [[place-zargandur|Zargandur]] and [[place-kethramir|Kethramír]]. An envoy who carries anything softer to a foreign court carries it for his own house.
- **The codes of the quarters.** The Tellumi who live inside the four held cities govern their own affairs by codes the Council keeps, and a Delkoshekh hearing a case in a back room of the Shirvan quarter judges by them.
- **The money.** The Council funds Tellumi shrines and schools in Amradad and the Taruzelian ports, and it pays a quiet network of agents who tell the exile houses how matters stand in the lost cities. The money comes as donations, much of it from the wages of Tellumi water-masters, bankers and physicians who work for Atarzád employers.

## What It Leaves Alone {#present}

Two questions come to every session and leave it unanswered. The first is the coming down. The old houses hold that the first lineages came down through the labyrinth of [[place-vurdil|Vurdish]], and the houses of the west, the [[affiliation-hsemernagord|House of Mernagord]] first among them, hold that they walked down [[place-wstrndscnt|the Western Descent]]. The Council keeps both accounts in the same hall and has never ruled between them, because a ruling for either would cost a house its standing.

The second is the sword. [Gureshai of Zargandur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the heir of the [[affiliation-hsezargandur|House of Zargandur]], trains in the Kingdom's forces for a reclamation, and the Council neither endorses his preparations nor forbids them. It does not debate them openly. Privately its consensus is that no reclamation will come in this generation.

[Hemvanai of Mernagord]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the ruling prince of the largest free oasis, is the voice of accommodation in the room, and the Atarzád merchants who have sounded him out about a trade compact have made every session since a quarrel about him. The houses in exile call the idea treason. He has answered neither them nor the merchants.

This year a third question has reached the session from outside it. Word comes down the caravan road that a dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes under his banner by victory and by marriage. Every oasis on the steppe's road must choose whether to pay him, wall itself or bargain, and the free oases must choose with the rest. Some in the exile houses say that a union pressing the Atarzád from the north is the moment a reclamation waits for. Others answer that the steppe does not distinguish a Tellumi garden from an Atarzád one. The single position says nothing about the steppe, and the Council has not changed it.

## Dealing with the Council

The Council receives no petitions. A Tellumi with a grievance takes it to the Delkoshekh of his quarter or the court of the house his household stood to, and the house brings it to the session if the house chooses. What the Council has for an outsider is work: carrying a report out of a held city to the exile courts, carrying a sum of money to a school in a Taruzelian port, or carrying the single position to a court that would prefer to hear something else. It pays in silver, and a messenger who alters what he carries is never hired by any of the seven houses again.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **A report out of Shirvan.** Muresha of Shirvan pays a courier for the news of the [[place-oasishirvn|Shirvan]] quarter, and has begun to ask how the quarter knows so much. She wants someone who is not her courier to find out.
- **The merchants' answer.** The Atarzád merchants who sounded out Hemvanai of Mernagord want their terms carried to him where the exile houses will not see them, and the exile houses pay well to see whatever is carried to him.
- **The union and the sword.** Gureshai of Zargandur wants to know whether the northern union will press the held cities, and asks a messenger with no seat to lose to go north and see it for him.
