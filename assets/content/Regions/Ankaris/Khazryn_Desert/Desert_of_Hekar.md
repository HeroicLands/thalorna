## Overview

The Desert of Hek'ar is a barren tract at the edge of the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn Desert]], the margin that lies below the passes beyond Byzaría's [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]]. Nothing in it invites a traveler. What comes down the passes into it, more often than travelers, is raiders.

## Raids and Relics

The **Servants of Sycânus**, a feared military order of [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] knights, have led punitive raids down out of the passes into the Hek'ar to spread terror and assert the Black Flame's dominance, and [[being-srdrzhsycnknghtblckflm|Sir Drazha Sycâni]] made his name as a Blazewarden leading them. The order now has another reason to cross: the [[place-ruinsarkor|Ruins of Arkor]], an ancient city buried beneath the Hek'ar's sands, where the **Eye of Velok** is believed to lie hidden. Rival Black Flame factions, among them the order called **Nakarys the Scourge**, are in the desert too, and they and the Servants fight each other there as readily as anyone else. An expedition into the Hek'ar meets the desert, the knights, and their quarrels together.
