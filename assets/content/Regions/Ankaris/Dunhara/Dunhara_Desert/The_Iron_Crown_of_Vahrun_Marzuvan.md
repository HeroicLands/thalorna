## Overview {#overview}

The iron crown is the crown that [[being-vhrnmrzvn|Vahrun Marzuvan]] forged for himself, of iron. The iron torc of a vessel was never given to him, and he wore the crown in its place. Its home is the [[place-twrlghtnmrkd|Tower of the Lightning-Marked]] on the Pale Floor, where the lightning finds the spike above the gallery every night the sky offers weather.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody does. The elders of the Lightning-Marked lineage say Vahrun climbed to the gallery alone in a great storm and was gone by morning, with no body, no ash and no crown. The tribes treat the crown as lost in the sky above the Floor, with him.

## Its Story {#its-story}

Vahrun was a storm-walker of the _ghuzmogar_ and a _ruzhogar_ in his own lineage. He gathered the cults' youngest and boldest on the Pale Floor, taught them to call the lightning down to a spike and a place, and took the title Master of the Tower, which the Council of Elders never conferred. When he claimed to be a vessel of [[lore-bazhramisdty|Bazhrámish]] without the gathered elders' assent, the Council at the [[place-stoneofranaz|Stone of Ranaz]] denounced him. The crown was his own work, and the one mark of rank he gave himself.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

The crown is said to be iron that lightning cannot melt. That is a rumor.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **A fence in [[place-qarvelakhend|Qarvelakhend]]** pays well for the crown and asks no questions. A rival thief went south first and did not come back.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- A thief hears that the crown went into the storm with Vahrun and sets out for the tower ([[scenario-irncrwnstrm|What Walks Above the Pale Floor]]).

:::secret {title="The crown on the brow"}
The crown that the elders call lost is the true crown, a ring of dull black metal burned into Vahrun's brow. He has never taken it off. He did not go into the storm to escape death: he went up to hold shut a crack that his own working made, and [[lore-theshadow|the Shadow]] answers him through it. He is not dead, and cannot let go.

A thin copy, iron with fused glass for gems, sits on a stool in the sealed room below the gallery. It is a lure wired to the iron of the stair: lifting it or moving the stool sends a bolt down every rung and marks the bearer to the storm.

The lineage holds the iron to be tempered by the god's own fire, and in the right hands it steadies a storm-walker against any storm. When the storm ebbs Vahrun offers the true crown to a storm-walker, or to anyone the storm accepts, who is willing to stand in his place. The cost falls on the wearer, and Mehrava, the lineage's senior elder, says so plainly. A crown brought to the Stone sets the Council arguing for a season, and the Warrior's Circle is asked whether the Old Promise binds against a Dunhari who has become a door.
:::
