## Overview {#overview}

The Pale Floor is the bed of a dead lake in the far south of the [[place-dunhardsrtrgn|Dunhara Desert]], beyond the last chain of wells: a plain of white salt wider than a day's ride, flat to the horizon, with one dark thing standing out of the glare at its middle, the [[place-twrlghtnmrkd|Tower of the Lightning-Marked]]. Caravans cross it because it saves ten days on the road between the southern wells and the markets of [[place-amradadrgn|Amradad]], and they cross on the day the storm-readers name or not at all.

A party comes to the Floor to cross it, to reach the tower, to take service at the [[place-kinlesswatch|Kinless Watch]] on its western edge, or to learn what is drawing the herders' people out onto the salt.

## The Land {#the-land}

The salt lies in a crust a hand thick over gray mud, cracked into plates that ring underfoot. There is no water on the Floor, no shade and no grazing, and the glare by day blinds a traveler who does not veil his eyes. Storms gather over it in the season, and lightning falls on it more often than anywhere else in the desert. Around the low mound at the middle the flat is pocked with fulgurites, rooted tubes of glass the lightning makes of the sand beneath the salt, some as long as a spear. On the western side a rock stands clear of the salt, and the Kinless Watch stands on it, a day's ride from the mound. The Floor's edges are poor grazing where a few herding camps keep their goats.

## Who Uses It {#who-uses-it}

The Floor belongs to no tribe. [The Lightning-Marked]{.name data-type="affiliation"}, the lineage of the [[affiliation-strmcltsbzhrms|Storm Cults of Bazhrámish]] held to have been touched by the god, keep a toll tent at its northern edge. Their apprentices, the _hamelogar_, read the sky for each caravan and name the day it is safe to go, and take one load in twenty of the salt the caravans cut from the flat as they cross. The tribes count the toll a hospitality gift in the old sense, the same as a sheikh's; the merchants of the walled cities count it extortion and pay it all the same. The salt goes north with the caravans to the oasis markets and comes back as dates, grain, cloth and the water the apprentices drink.

The Kingdom of Amradad keeps the Kinless Watch on the western edge, and the herders at the edges graze what the salt allows and drive their goats a long way north to the last wells to water them.

## Dangers {#dangers}

Thirst kills on the Floor as it kills everywhere in the desert, faster here, because the salt gives nothing back. The storm is the danger the Floor is known for. A caravan that pays and crosses on the named day is rarely troubled. A caravan that refuses, or crosses on the wrong day, is said to be taken by the storm, and its dead are found on the flat with glass rooted in their wounds. Travelers who go near the mound at night speak of a sky that clears after a bolt, and of stars that stand wrong.

## What Is Told {#what-is-told}

The poets call the Floor the floor of a sea that is dead. [[lore-blackstonesode|The Ode of the Black Stones]] crosses it at night on a gray she-camel, on the day the apprentices at the toll tent name as safe, and its rider crosses without looking at the tower. In [[lore-lastguestreading|The Last Guest]], the reading the voice-bearers recite on the last night of the Summer Vigil, the walking sand buries the Floor and the tower, and the lightning comes and finds nothing. The herders say a voice in the storm draws their own people out, and that those who answer walk out across the salt and are not found.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The Floor is busier this season than its toll tent likes. Herders from its edges have carried their missing to the [[place-stoneofranaz|Stone of Ranaz]] and asked [[affiliation-wrrscrcldnhrtrbs|the Warrior's Circle]] to rule on what draws them out, while the Council of Elders there is deadlocked: [the Wind-Readers]{.name data-type="affiliation"} hold that a lineage which keeps its readings of the Floor from the Council has no business taking a toll in the god's name, and the Lightning-Marked answer that the Floor is theirs to read. [[being-mhrv|Mehrava]], the senior elder of the Lightning-Marked, comes out to the tower on the nights of the great storms and tells outsiders nothing of what she reads.

Thieves are crossing as well. A fence in [[place-qarvelakhend|Qarvelakhend]] pays well for [[miscgear-crwnvahrun|The Iron Crown of Vahrun Marzuvan]], said to have gone into the storm with the self-styled Master of the Tower, and one thief who went out before the spring has not come back. The Kinless Watch is short of men, and its _ghedvader_ in Qarvelakhend has asked the Kingdom for more.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The named day.** A caravan-master in a hurry wants to cross two days before the day the apprentices have named, and offers the party a share of his salt to guide him.
- **The missing.** A herding camp at the Floor's edge has lost a daughter to the voice in the storm, and her father wants her brought back, or her body, before the season-turn.
- **The toll's ledger.** The Wind-Readers want proof of how much salt the Lightning-Marked take and where it goes, and a party that sits a night at the toll tent can count it.
