The Oasis of Tevrazekh is a small, permanent oasis in the [[place-dunhardsrtrgn|Dunhara Desert]], a pool under date palms fed by a spring that has never been known to fail. A tribe that holds a spring that never fails holds a fortune, and the tribe that holds Tevrazekh by right of usage camps there through the hottest months, when every other water on its circuit has shrunk to mud.

The oasis is the summer seat of [[being-trvshshrdr|Torvâsh Shiradar]], the chieftain who ended his brothers' war over their father's place by persuading the tribe to govern itself through a council, and whose camp is now a place other sheikhs send their quarrels to be heard.
