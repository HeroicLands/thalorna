## Overview {#overview}

Kherzulakhem is a hunters' camp at a small oasis in the north of the [[place-dunhardsrtrgn|Dunhara Desert]], where the sand seas run toward the long, vague frontier with the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]]. A ring of stunted palms, a seep that fills a stone basin overnight and a scatter of tents make up the whole of it, and nobody lives there the year round: the four hundred counted there are the camp at its peak, when hunters and trackers from both deserts are in together. They come to water, trade hides and sleep in the shade, and the [Laws of the Well]{.name data-type="lore/law"} keep the peace between them however their tribes stand.

The camp lives on the hunt. Gazelle, oryx and wild ass range the gravel between the dunes, and the hunters bring in hides, horn and dried meat. They trade these at the basin for dates, for grain off the caravans and for the felt and horses of the steppe, and the trade is the reason both deserts tolerate each other here. The seep gives enough for people and riding animals and none for a herd, so no tribe has ever tried to hold the place.

It is the camp [[being-atenhershr|Atazkharu]], the Silent Jackal, calls home between his journeys, a Khazrin wanderer who found the Dunhari camp more to his liking than any of his own.

## The Present {#present}

A [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] storm-shaman who preaches the steppe's storm-worship in Dunhari camps watered at Kherzulakhem in the spring and left a following among the young trackers. The northern chieftains want him handed over to answer before the [[affiliation-strmcltsbzhrms|Storm Cults]] at the Stone of Ranaz the next time he comes in. The Laws of the Well forbid any hand raised against a man at the water, and the hunters of both deserts, who need the basin more than they need either faith's quarrel, mean to keep them.
