## Overview {#overview}

**The Shuttered Cliff** is a face of brown rock below the western end of [[place-graznmntns|The Grazian Mountains]], at the edge of the [[place-dunhardsrtrgn|Dunhara Desert]], where the last of the foothills run out above [[place-marchroad|The March Road]], west of the wells of [[place-marukupa|Marukupa]], on the desert margin. High on the face a seam runs level for a hundred paces, and along it are openings shaped to the seam, each closed by a slab of the same brown rock. In some years one slab stands a hand's width open and in others a different one does. The guides who bring caravans along the road point it out from below as one of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] halls that the fireside stories put in the mountain margins, and say that nobody they know has been inside.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The stretch of the road below the cliff lies on the circuit of a tribe of the [[affiliation-dunhartrbs|Dunhara Tribes]] that takes its hospitality gift from the caravans there and owes them protection in return. The cliff itself is no tribe's: there is no water at its foot and no grazing above it, and no tribe has ever claimed a rock it cannot use.

## Layers {#history}

The tribe's poets have a few verses on the cliff and no history of it. In the verses the slabs are doors that the people inside open to look out and shut when they are seen, and the people inside are never described. The guides say the slabs have moved within their own memory and that the openings have never all stood open at once.

## What Is There Now {#present}

A band of Kinless is in the cliff. Men cast out of their tribes and owed water by no one, they found a way up to the seam last winter and found that the slabs slide, and they have held the cliff since, coming down at night to strike the caravans that come round the end of the wall without a guide. When the tribe of the circuit rode against them in the spring, the Kinless slid every slab shut, and the tribe's warriors stood under a blank face and shot at rock.

The tribe has lost the gift of two caravans this year, which went by another road rather than pay for protection the tribe could not give. Its sheikh needs the Kinless out before the autumn caravans, and his warriors will not climb a face where they cannot see who is waiting at the top.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The Kinless are a dozen or more, hungry and with nothing to lose, and they know the face now. The climb to the seam is a narrow stair along the bedding, open to anyone above it. Summer heat on the brown rock is enough to kill a man who climbs at midday, and the foothills have no water closer than half a day.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A face of bedded brown rock, its layers lying almost level, with a shelf of broken stone at its foot. A stair of steps runs up the face where one bed steps back from the next, each step the rock's own, joined here and there by a short cut. At the top the stair meets the seam, and the seam is a ledge the width of a man, with the openings along its back wall. Each opening follows the line where two beds part, wider in the middle than at the ends, and each slab sits in a groove cut along the bed above and below it, so that it slides along the seam and does not swing. Behind the open slab is a long room with a floor laid level on the lower bed, and doors of open rock lead from it to the rooms behind the other openings. At the back of the long room an inner door of the brown stone stands shut, flush with the wall, with no handle, and the Kinless have not moved it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The sheikh's gift.** The sheikh of the circuit gives a share of the next caravan's gift to a party that clears the Kinless from the cliff before the autumn caravans come round the wall.
- **The way up.** A caravan guide says there is a second way onto the seam, from above, that the Kinless have not found, and will show it for a price.
- **The inner door.** A Kinless who wants to go back to his tribe offers to open a slab at night for a party that will speak for him, and says the band is afraid of the inner door and will not sleep near it.

# The Truth of the Shuttered Cliff {#truth .secret}

**The hall.** The cliff is **Zalv-nugár**, "the watching of the margin", a watch-hall of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]], abandoned. A hold of the Grazian high country kept it to watch the low country where the desert meets the end of the wall, which is the one way round the mountains that needs no pass, and to turn back anyone who came up from that low country toward the hold's ground. The openings on the seam are its windows, and the slabs are its shutters, which the watch slid shut against the sun, the sand and the eyes of the road. A small company lived in it by turns and was supplied from the hold.

**Its leaving.** The hold that kept it closed as the Khazari dwindled, and its houses went to other holds. The watch was called in with them. They left the hall swept and its shutters closed, and the wind and the heat of the rock have worked a slab or two open since, a little at a time.

**The inner door.** The inner door leads to the hall's water: a cistern in the rock, fed by a seep along the bedding and led to the cistern by a channel along the seep's own course, with a store-room beside it, empty. The door lifts and slides back into the wall when it is pressed at the right place, low on its left side. The cistern holds water still, clean and very cold, and it is the reason the Kinless could hold the cliff through a summer if they found it.

> [!TIP]
> The Kinless are afraid of a door, and the water behind it is what they lack. A party that finds the cistern can wait the band out, or offer it water for its surrender. The watch, the hold that kept it, the shutters' purpose, the inner door's catch and the cistern are this note's own account of the hall; a game master may change them.
