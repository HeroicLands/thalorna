## Overview {#overview}

The Oasis of Tevrazavad is a small, permanent oasis in the [[place-dunhardsrtrgn|Dunhara Desert]], a pool under date palms fed by a spring that has never been known to fail. A tribe that holds a spring that never fails holds a fortune, and the [[affiliation-trbtvrzvd|Tribe of Tevrazavad]], which holds this one by right of usage, camps there through the hottest months, when every other water on its circuit has shrunk to mud.

A few households stay at the spring the year round to keep the palms and the garden plots under them, and the rest of the tribe comes in at midsummer with the herds; the six hundred counted there are the tribe gathered at the spring in summer. The oasis feeds itself on dates, milk and the barley of its own gardens in a good year, and buys the rest from the caravans with camels. Other tribes pay to water at the spring in a drought, in stock or in promises, and the tribe's Keeper of the Feud remembers every promise.

The oasis is the summer seat of [[being-trvshshrdr|Torvash Shiradar]], the chieftain who ended his brothers' war over their father's place by persuading the tribe to govern itself through a council, and whose camp is a place other sheikhs send their quarrels to be heard.

## The Present {#present}

More quarrels come to the spring this season than the tribe's council can hear. Torvash's own poets went to [[place-vzhmvd|Vazhmavad]] with a dispute and have not returned, and he sends no others after them. Sheikhs bring the cases that belong at the weighing-place to Tevrazavad instead, and the tribe's council hears them under the palms, which the common council at [[place-ghavulavad|Ghavulavad]] takes as a slight to its Lawkeeper. Torvash keeps a trusted man ready to ride and says nothing of where.
