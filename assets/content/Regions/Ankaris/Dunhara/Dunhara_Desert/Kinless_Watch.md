## Overview {#overview}

The Kinless Watch is a fort on a rock that stands clear of the salt at the western edge of the [[place-palefloor|Pale Floor]], a day's ride west of the [[place-twrlghtnmrkd|Tower of the Lightning-Marked]]. The [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]] pays and supplies it through the southern host at [[place-qarvelakhend|Qarvelakhend]], and it watches the road by which the southern caravans come north across the Floor toward the Amradi markets. No tribe can command its garrison, and that is why the Kingdom keeps it.

A party comes here posted by the Kingdom, hired to carry its pay and stores south, sheltering on the way to the tower, or looking for a man who went to the Kinless because no one else would have him.

## Inside {#inside}

A wall of dry stone follows the edge of the rock, with a single gate on the north side where the track climbs from the sand. Inside are a barrack, a storehouse, a stable for the camels, a lookout tower that commands the Floor to the mound, and the cistern, cut into the rock under a locked cover and guarded as closely as the gate. Nothing on the Floor gives water, so every drop in the cistern came from Qarvelakhend on a camel's back, and the garrison's first duty is to keep it.

## Who Is There {#who-is-there}

The garrison are **Kinless**: men cast out by their own kin and claimed by none, the one rank of the [[affiliation-dunhartrbs|Dunhara Tribes]] owed no hospitality. The Kingdom hires them with coin because no tribe's feud can reach them and no sheikh's word can order them. Some forty serve at a time, under a captain who is Kinless himself and answers to the _ghedvader_ of the southern host in Qarvelakhend.

The fort keeps the desert's law strictly and in its own way. A Dunhari who comes to the Kinless Watch owes the garrison nothing and is owed nothing. A stranger who eats its salt is a guest for the stated days and no more, since the salt is the host's, and its water is sold, never given.

## The Present {#present}

The watch is short of men. Some have deserted, and some have walked out onto the Floor at night and have not come back; the captain's reports to Qarvelakhend say only that the garrison is short, and the _ghedvader_ there has asked the Kingdom for more men. The garrison does not speak of what it sees on the flat at night, to its paymasters or to anyone else.

Meanwhile the Floor is crowded. Thieves bound for the tower after [[miscgear-crwnvahrun|The Iron Crown of Vahrun Marzuvan]] water at the fort's cistern if they can pay, herders from the Floor's edges come asking after their missing, and [The Lightning-Marked]{.name data-type="affiliation"} at the toll tent on the northern edge regard a fort of outcasts in the Kingdom's pay with no favor at all.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The posting.** The _ghedvader_ at Qarvelakhend posts the party to the Watch to stiffen the garrison, and to report what the captain's letters leave out.
- **The pay convoy.** The Watch's pay, its grain and its water go south from Qarvelakhend on one camel train, through country where the Kinless who are not in the Kingdom's pay know exactly when it travels.
- **The deserter.** A Kinless of the garrison walked out of the gate a month ago, and his brother in a tribe that cast him out wants to know whether he is alive, though the tribe will not say so aloud.
