## Overview

The Stone of Ranâz is an ancient hill-shrine where three tribal territories of the [[place-dunhardsrtrgn|Dunhara]] meet, and because it belongs to all three it belongs to none. It is where the Dunhari gather when something concerns more than one tribe: the warriors at the quarter-days, the shamans at the season-turns, and petitioners whenever a wrong is too large for a single chieftain to answer.

## Two Gatherings

The [[affiliation-wrrscrcldnhrtrbs|Warrior's Circle]] of the Dunhara tribes meets at the Stone every quarter, with no captain over it, its rulings made by whoever comes and its order kept by a Voice drawn by lot at each meeting. The vat of indigo dye for the _vâst-bahâr_, the strip of dyed leather an acclaimed warrior wears on the upper arm, is kept here, and a strip is renewed only by dipping it in that vat at a quarterly meeting; a strip that has gone three quarters unrenewed fades to a paler blue and marks its wearer as inactive. Foreign emissaries who arrive uninvited are received with formal courtesy and dismissed without commitment.

The [[affiliation-strmcltsbhrms|Storm Cults of Bahramish]] share the shrine. At the season-turns the **Council of Elders**—the senior elders of all five lineages—sits at the Stone to deliberate on what concerns the cults as a whole, and its weightiest act is the recognition of a vessel, conferred by the assent of the gathered senior shamans and binding on every lineage. If a Khazryn storm-shaman ever crossed into Dunhari country to preach his own tradition, the formal religious confrontation would be held here.
