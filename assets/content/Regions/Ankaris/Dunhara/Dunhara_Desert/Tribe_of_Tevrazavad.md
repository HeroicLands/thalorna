The Tribe of Tevrazavad holds the [[place-oasistvrzvd|Oasis of Tevrazavad]] by right of usage and camps there through the hottest months. Its chieftain [[being-trvshshrdr|Torvash Shiradar]] persuaded it to govern itself through a council, and its camp is a place other sheikhs send their quarrels to be heard.

## History {#history}

The tribe has held the spring by right of usage for as long as its poets recite: its herds have watered there every summer of its genealogy, and other tribes have paid it to water there in every drought. It was led by sheikhs in the ordinary way until the death of Torvash's father, when his two elder brothers went to war over their father's place and the tribe began to split between them. Torvash ended the war by raising neither brother. He persuaded the tribe to govern itself through a council that shares the leadership with him, and the council's fairness brought other sheikhs' quarrels to the spring to be heard.
