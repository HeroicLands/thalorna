## Overview {#overview}

The king's signet is the seal of the king whose household was buried in the vault the tribes call [[place-mezhvadez|Mezhvadez]], the Dry Stair. His name is struck from every wall of the place. The signet lies in the vault, in the deep sand south of the long wells, under a dune that opens only when the wind and the season allow.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The vault does. A guide who has been down it cannot be hired for any price.

## Its Story {#its-story}

The vault was cut by the household of the king whose name was struck. The tribes say the keeper of that household was the one who could not be struck, because she held the keys.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

The signet is the king's seal. In the hands of a claimant it would prove a line older than the Shebarader's.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **A tagemader of [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|the Kingdom of Amradad]]** hungers for the king's seal and pays a thief to bring it out of the vault, however it is taken.
- **Merchants of [[place-qarvelakhend|Qarvelakhend]]** buy grave-goods from any guide willing to bring them out.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- The tagemader, a polished man named [Ghezrun]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, hires a party to bring out the signet ([[scenario-kprdrystr|A Stair of Veiled Women]]).

:::secret {title="The lock of the door"}
The signet lies in the keeper's hand, on the dais at the middle of the vault, and taking it wakes the cold. The seal is the lock of the door of black stone behind her. The king bargained with a thing outside creation for time, and the thing asked for his household one by one; the keeper agreed to be the last, and holds the door shut by her will.

A party that carries the signet back to the door and speaks the keeper's name closes the door, at the price of her last rest. Whoever carries the signet out of the vault is followed. A tagemader who gets it has the proof he wanted and a debt he does not know about.
:::
