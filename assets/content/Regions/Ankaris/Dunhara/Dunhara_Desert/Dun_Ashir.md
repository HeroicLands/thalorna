Dûn Ashir is a settlement built around an oasis in the [[place-dunhardsrtrgn|Dunhara Desert]], the open desert of the Dunhara beyond the settled cities, where sand seas and mesas are held by no fixed border and the tribal confederations of the Dunhari range freely.
