## Overview {#overview}

**Golmakhend**, the planting town, is a walled market town of about 5,000 in the wadi country of the [[place-amradadrgn|Amradad Region]], between [[place-tahrodan|Tahrodan]] and the coast, where a long wadi spreads into groves of date palms. The groves are its wealth.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The villages of the wadi grow dates, figs and barley under the palms, and the town dries, packs and sells the dates to the cities, to the caravans and across the strait from [[place-valmakhend|Valmakhend]]. Its market serves the wadi villages for a day's ride around.

## The Present {#present}

Golmakhend lies far from the desert edge and from the border, and raiders do not reach it. Its quarrel is over water. The groves need more of the wadi's flow than the villages below them can spare, and the clerk of the Keeper of the Wells at [[place-tazhinakhend|Tazhinakhend]] rules against the town as often as for it.
