## Overview {#overview}

**Tazhinakhend**, the threshing town, is a walled market town of about 6,000 on a caravan road of [[place-amradadrgn|Amradad]], where the longest wadi of the region spreads into a plain of barley and wheat. The fields green for one season after the winter rains. At harvest the threshing floors that ring the town outside its wall run from dawn to dark, and the chaff hangs over the road for a month.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The villages up and down the wadi grow the grain, and the town threshes, weighs, stores and sells it. Its granaries feed [[place-shebarakhend|Shebarakhend]] first, and its surplus goes south to [[place-qarvelakhend|Qarvelakhend]] to be traded to the tribes for herds. The town's _tagemader_ answers to the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]] for the count in its granaries, and a clerk of the Keeper of the Wells sits beside him and decides when each village may open its channels.

## The Present {#present}

The last winter's rain was thin. The upper villages accuse the lower ones of taking water out of turn, the clerk's rulings satisfy nobody, and the court's buyers want their share all the same.
