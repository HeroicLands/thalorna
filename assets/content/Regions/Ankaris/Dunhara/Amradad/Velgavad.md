## Overview {#overview}

**Velgavad**, the lined well, is a walled town of about 3,500 on the Khazrin road east of [[place-tahrodan|Tahrodan]], in the dry middle country of the [[place-amradadrgn|Amradad Region]], and the last town before [[place-threeseas|the Three Seas]]. Its first well is lined with stone, and eight more stand beside it.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Its wells water the caravans of the Khazrin road and of the road from Bizaria that joins it at [[place-dorvakhend|Dorvakhend]], and the town sells water by the skin and fodder by the load. Villages along a short wadi to the south grow barley and keep goats, and a caravan carrying grain east leaves some of it here in exchange for water.

## The Present {#present}

In a dry year Khazrin steppe bands ride this far west, and the garrison patrols the road a day's ride either side of the town. The wells are the Kingdom's, and the _tagemader_ raises the price of water in a dry season, which the caravan-masters protest to Tahrodan.
