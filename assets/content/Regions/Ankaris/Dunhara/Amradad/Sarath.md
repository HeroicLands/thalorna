## Overview

Sarath is a significant rural settlement of [[place-amradadrgn|Amradad]], governed by **the Village Elders of Sarath**, who decide its affairs collectively. A farming settlement lives by the seasons, and the elders do not decide when to plant, when to move water or when to store against a bad year without first hearing what the stars say.

## The Astrologer on Retainer

For that the elders keep [[being-fakhrdtmrn|Fakhrad Tamârân]], an astrologer of [[place-shamsun|Shamsun]], on retainer as their adviser on seasonal matters and on the settlement's longer-term planning. A newcomer who wants the elders' agreement to anything that touches the land learns quickly that the question will also be put to Fakhrad.
