## Overview {#overview}

**Venzelakhend**, the oil-pressing town, is a walled market town of about 5,000 on a caravan road of [[place-amradadrgn|Amradad]], where the sides of a wadi are terraced into olive groves. The presses stand inside the wall, and the whole town smells of crushed fruit from autumn into winter.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The grove villages on the terraces grow barley between the trees and send their olives to the town's presses; the town buys its bread from [[place-tazhinakhend|Tazhinakhend]]. Its oil goes to the kitchens of the cities, to the [[affiliation-balinuni|Balinuni]] buyers at [[place-valmakhend|Valmakhend]], and above all to the fire-temples, whose lamps and eternal flames burn Venzelakhend oil. The town's woodworkers turn the prunings into bowls and boxes, and the woodcrafter [[being-darzshshvn|Darzash Shavan]] keeps her workshop by the wadi. Its _tagemader_ takes the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]'s share of every pressing in jars sealed at the press.

## The Present {#present}

The temples buy their oil at a price their endowments fix, below the market's. The press-owners hold back the best pressing for buyers from [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel]], and the priests have asked the vazhmader to rule that the endowments are owed it.
