## Overview {#overview}

**Nozhavad**, the night-halt, is a walled oasis town of about 3,000 on [[place-marchroad|the March Road]] between [[place-gharzadez|Gharzadez]] and [[place-tahrodan|Tahrodan]], near the southern edge of the [[place-amradadrgn|Amradad Region]] where it gives onto the [[place-dunhardsrtrgn|Dunhara Desert]]. Caravans on the road west from the Mirror Sea make their longest halt here.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

A spring waters palms, barley and fodder, enough for the town and the beasts of the trains. Caravanserais fill half the town, and it lives on lodging, fodder and repairs, and on the dates and goats it sells to the trains and to the tribes of the desert edge.

## The Present {#present}

The kinless of the desert raid the road on both sides of the town, and its garrison is small. Half the caravans wait inside the walls for a larger train to form before they go on.
