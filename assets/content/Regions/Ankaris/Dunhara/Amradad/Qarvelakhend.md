## Overview {#overview}

**Qarvelakhend**, the walled town, is the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]'s gate on the open desert: about 8,000 people on an oasis where the last wadi villages give out and the [[place-dunhardsrtrgn|Dunhara Desert]] begins. The Kingdom buys the tribes' herds here and sells them grain and iron, its southern host watches the desert from here, and what comes out of the desert's old places, honestly or otherwise, is sold here first.

## Arriving {#arriving}

From the desert side the town shows first as its herd-pens, a ring of thorn and mud walls outside the gate where camels, sheep and goats wait for buyers. Tribesmen leave their spears with the gate-ward and take them back on the way out, a rule both sides keep. From the north the road comes down a wadi lined with barley plots and palms, and the town wall, higher than any other in the south, stands across the end of it.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The oasis and the villages strung up the wadi grow barley, dates and vegetables, enough for the town in a good year and never enough for the host as well. The rest is grain sent down from the wadi towns, and much of that grain goes straight back out of the gate to the tribes, in exchange for the herds that Qarvelakhend drives north to [[place-shebarakhend|Shebarakhend]] and [[place-tahrodan|Tahrodan]]. Hides, wool and camel-hair cloth are the town's own trades. Its goldsmiths and jewelers, the jeweler [[being-prynbrmndn|Paryan Barmandan]] among them, work the old gold that guides bring out of the desert, and the town's merchants buy grave-goods from [[place-mezhvadez|the Dry Stair]] from any guide who will go.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

A _tagemader_ holds the town, its walls and its revenue. A _ghedvader_ commands the southern host, which keeps its barracks inside the wall and pays and supplies the [Kinless Watch]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, the fort on the road to the [[place-twrlghtnmrkd|Tower of the Lightning-Marked]] whose garrison are men cast out by their tribes. The two men answer to different vashkaders and keep separate accounts of the tribes. Sheikhs bring their grievances to whichever of the two will hear them.

## The Present {#present}

The herd treaties hold at the pens and fray at the wells. Some tribes owe the town blood for past raids and are owed it in turn, and a tribe with an open blood-debt sells its herds through another tribe's hands or not at all. The tagemader pays guides and thieves to dig at the Dry Stair for reasons he keeps to himself, the priests of the town's fire-temple preach against the grave-goods trade, and the merchants buy anyway.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The fence's buyer.** A fence of the town pays well for anything from the desert's old places and asks no questions; what he will not say is whom he sells to. The southern host's ghedvader wants the name, because one of the pieces came from a guide his own patrol was sent to find.
- **Two herds, one brand.** A tribe that owes the town blood sells camels at the pens under another tribe's brand. Closing the pens to both breaks a treaty the Shebarader signed, so the tagemader needs proof he can lay before the Shebarader's envoy.
