## Overview {#overview}

**Shorvakhend**, the drovers' town, is a walled market town of about 4,000 on the northern border of the [[place-amradadrgn|Amradad Region]], where the drove and caravan road from the [[place-southrnmrch|Southern March]] of Bizaria comes down into the Kingdom. Sheep are its business.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Amradi drovers buy sheep in the Southern March, rest them on the town's pastures and drive them south to [[place-tahrodan|Tahrodan]] and the cities. The town lives on that trade, on the wool it sells, and on the barley that villages grow on the dry fields round it after the winter rain. Bizarian customs posts stand an hour's ride to the north, and the town's own officers take the Kingdom's dues on every flock.

## The Present {#present}

[[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] warbands crossing from the Bizarian hinterland ride west along the border in summer, and in a bad year they reach the flocks on the town's northern pastures. The garrison and the Bizarian posts watch the same border and do not share what they see.
