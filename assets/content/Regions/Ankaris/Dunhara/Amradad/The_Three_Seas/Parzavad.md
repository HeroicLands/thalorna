## Overview {#overview}

**Parzavad**, the penning oasis, is a walled town of about 3,500 on the open plain of [[place-threeseas|the Three Seas]], about midway between [[place-bittersea|the Bitter Sea]] and [[place-mirrorsea|the Mirror Sea]]. The stone pens round its wall hold more beasts at the autumn fair than the town holds people.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The herders feed it. They sell sheep, goats, horses and camels at the fair and pay the Shebarader's herd tax in animals, and the town sells them grain, iron, cloth and salt. A spring and a ring of gardens give fodder and vegetables, and the bread comes from the caravans that buy the fair's beasts. A clerk of the Keeper of the Wells sits here and rules on the herders' wells.

## The Present {#present}

Two kinds of raider want the fair's herds: Khazrin steppe bands from the north-east and the kinless of the desert from the south. The clans bring their own riders to the fair, and in a bad year the town holds more armed herders than garrison.
