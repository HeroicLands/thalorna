## Overview {#overview}

**Rezhakhend**, the raking town, is a walled salt town of about 2,500 on the flat western shore of [[place-farsea|the Far Sea]], in [[place-threeseas|the Three Seas]], two days' ride north-east of [[place-dorvakhend|Dorvakhend]]. Its people rake the salt from the pans the summer leaves on the shore.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Salt is all it makes. It goes west in blocks on the Khazrin road and north into Bizaria, where it undersells the salt of [[place-halzikos|Halzikos]]. The town buys its grain from Dorvakhend's villages and its meat from the herders who come to the pans for salt for their flocks. A deep well inside the wall gives the only sweet water on the western shore.

## The Present {#present}

The town lies on the northern border with no stream and no fields, and it is the first place a [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] warband crossing at the sea reaches. Its wall is low and its garrison small. Its salt-masters pay a Khazrin steppe band to leave their trains alone, and the _tagemader_ does not ask where the money goes.
