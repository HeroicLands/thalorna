## Overview {#overview}

**Delmavad**, the dug spring, is a walled oasis town of about 3,000 on the western shore of [[place-mirrorsea|the Mirror Sea]], in [[place-threeseas|the Three Seas]], where fresh springs rise in a line of palms a mile from the salt. Its channels run in the cuts its first settlers dug down to the water.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The springs water date palms, barley, melons and fodder. The town feeds itself and sends dates and fodder round the shore to [[place-gharzadez|Gharzadez]]. Tribes of the [[affiliation-dunhartrbs|Dunhara Tribes]] water their herds at the outer springs under a compact with the Kingdom, and sell the town camels, goats and wool.

## The Present {#present}

The kinless of the desert raid the outer gardens at night and take what the herd-tribes do not guard. The tribes' compact holds while the springs run, and the garrison at Gharzadez wants to draw more of Delmavad's water, which the tribes say breaks it.
