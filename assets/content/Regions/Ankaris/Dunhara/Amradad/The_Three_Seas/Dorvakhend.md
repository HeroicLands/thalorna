## Overview {#overview}

**Dorvakhend**, the loading town, is a walled caravan town of about 4,000 on the southern shore of [[place-bittersea|the Bitter Sea]], in [[place-threeseas|the Three Seas]]. The road from Bizaria comes round the sea's western shore to meet the Khazrin road here, and every train bound west from the seas makes up its loads at Dorvakhend and pays the Kingdom's dues.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Two fresh streams come down to the shore beside the town, and the villages along them grow barley, melons and fodder enough to feed it in a fair year. The rest comes from the caravans: the town sells water, fodder, beasts and guides, and its _tagemader_ takes the dues on every load from [[place-halzikos|Halzikos]] and the north. His customs officers cross the border to inspect loads at Halzikos before they set out.

## The Present {#present}

[[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] warbands cross from the Bizarian hinterland each summer and burn the herders' camps west of the town, and Khazrin steppe bands raid the winter camps in the reeds. The garrison holds the walls and the road and rides after neither. The herders want it to, and they pay their herd tax late until it does.
