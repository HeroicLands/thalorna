## Overview {#overview}

The Far Sea is the eastern of the two inland seas on the northern border of [[place-amradadrgn|Amradad]], at the corner where Amradad, [[place-byzariargn|Bizaria]] and the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]] meet. It lies farther from the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] than any other water of the Kingdom, almost 600 miles, and the Amradi name it for that. It is salt and shallow, about eighty miles across, and beyond its eastern shore the steppe runs on to the edge of the world as a caravan reckons it.

## The Land {#the-land}

A river comes down from the steppe into the north-eastern end of the sea in spring and dries to a chain of pools by midsummer. The southern shore is a long spit of gravel with a sheltered bay behind it, the western shore is flat salt pan, and the reed beds of the southern shore and the bay are the widest of the three seas. North of the sea the grass is taller than anywhere in Amradad; east of it the ground turns to stony steppe.

## Who Uses It {#who-uses-it}

[[place-morvadez|Morvadez]] holds the spit and the Khazrin road, which leaves the Kingdom there, and [[place-lemazakhend|Lemazakhend]] cuts reeds and dries fish across the bay. [[place-rezhakhend|Rezhakhend]] rakes salt from the pans of the western shore. Khazrin camel trains water at the river mouth before the last stage to Morvadez. Amradi herders winter on the southern grass, and Khazrin herders summer on the northern.

## Dangers {#dangers}

Khazrin steppe bands raid the trains on the last days before Morvadez and the flocks wintering south of the sea. [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] warbands cross the border at the sea in summer, coming down out of the Bizarian hinterland along the river. The wind off the dry flats raises salt dust that blinds a rider and sickens a beast.

## What Is Told {#what-is-told}

The Khazrin camel-men hold that the steppe's law ends at the sea's shore: a feud carried past it is set down, and a man who kills across that line owes blood twice. The Amradi of Morvadez do not keep the custom, and the camel-men count that against them.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The tagemader of Morvadez wants riders enough to hold the road beyond his day's patrol, and the Host has none to send. The Khazrin tribes of the road beyond sell passage on it a stretch at a time and want no Amradi riders on their ground. Black Flame warbands come and go across the border, and Lemazakhend, open across the reeds, wants the tagemader to guard it before the road.
