## Overview {#overview}

The Mirror Sea is the southern of the three inland seas of [[place-amradadrgn|Amradad]], on the border with the [[place-dunhardsrtrgn|Dunhara Desert]], about 300 miles south of the two northern seas. It is salt and shallow and about eighty miles across. Its name comes from the flats round it, which throw back the sky so that the sea looks twice its size, and caravans coming out of the desert see water days before they reach it.

## The Land {#the-land}

Wide salt flats ring the sea, wider every summer as the water draws back. On the western shore a line of fresh springs rises a mile from the salt, and the palms and gardens of Delmavad stand on them. The eastern shore faces the desert, and the dunes come down almost to the water.

## Who Uses It {#who-uses-it}

[[place-gharzadez|Gharzadez]], on the eastern shore, holds the end of [[place-marchroad|the March Road]], where caravans from [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]] enter the Kingdom. [[place-delmavad|Delmavad]] on the western shore waters them and feeds the fort. Tribes of the [[affiliation-dunhartrbs|Dunhara Tribes]] water their herds at the outer springs under a compact with the Kingdom.

## Dangers {#dangers}

The kinless of the desert raid the last stage of the March Road and the outer gardens of Delmavad. The flats are the sea's own danger: by day the mirage shows water where there is none, and a traveler who walks out toward it finds salt crust over mud.

## What Is Told {#what-is-told}

The desert guides tell that the sea shows a traveler the water he dies beside, and a guide who sees two seas where there is one turns his train back to the last well.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The garrison of Gharzadez wants more of Delmavad's water for the fort, and the tribes at the outer springs hold that any more breaks their compact. The caravan-masters of the March Road pay either a sheikh's escort or the Kingdom's for the last stage, at about the same price, and the tagemader of Gharzadez wants every one of them to pay the Kingdom's. The kinless bands live on the gap between the two escorts.
