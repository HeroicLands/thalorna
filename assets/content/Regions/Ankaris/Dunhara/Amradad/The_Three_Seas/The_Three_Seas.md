## Overview {#overview}

The Three Seas is the far east of the [[place-amradadrgn|Amradad Region]]: the country more than about 400 miles from the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]], where the wadi country gives out into dry steppe and three salt inland seas lie on the open plain. It covers about 75,000 square miles, more than a third of Amradad, and about 250,000 people live in it. The [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]] holds it through six walled towns at the shores of the seas, a seventh on the plain between them, and the caravan roads that join them. Herders graze the country between the towns, and marauders ride its edges.

## The Land {#the-land}

The ground rises gently eastward from the wadi country into a plain of grass, thorn and stony rises that greens for a few weeks after the winter rain and lies brown the rest of the year. Acacia stands thicken on the plain between the two northern seas, where the rain is least thin. South of them the steppe dries toward the [[place-dunhardsrtrgn|Dunhara Desert]], and along the southern edge the first dunes come up out of the scrub.

The three seas are each about eighty miles across, salt, shallow and rimmed with reeds, mud and white crust, and none of them gives drinking water. The fresh water of the country is in the streams that run down into the seas after the rains, in springs along the shores and in the deep wells the towns guard. [[place-bittersea|The Bitter Sea]] and [[place-farsea|the Far Sea]] lie on the northern border with the [[place-byzariargn|Bizaria Region]], the Far Sea at the corner where Bizaria, Amradad and the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]] meet. [[place-mirrorsea|The Mirror Sea]] lies about 300 miles to the south, on the edge of the open desert.

## Peoples {#peoples}

Most people of the Three Seas are Amradi herders. Their clans move sheep, goats, horses and camels between winter grazing near the seas and summer wells on the plain, sell wool, hides and animals at the town fairs, and pay the Shebarader's herd tax there. They live in tents and in reed-and-mud villages along the fresh streams; only the towns are walled. Tribes of the [[affiliation-dunhartrbs|Dunhara Tribes]] graze the desert edge round the Mirror Sea under compacts with the Kingdom. Caravan folk of every country pass through the towns: Bizarian traders from the north, Khazrin camel-men from the east and Vedyaran caravan-masters on [[place-marchroad|the March Road]].

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The Kingdom holds the Three Seas as it holds the rest of Amradad, through a _tagemader_ in every town. Here each tagemader also commands a garrison of the Host, and most are larger than any town guard in the west. The towns are the Kingdom's hold on the country: its writ runs on the roads between them while a patrol is in sight, and on the open plain the herding clans settle their own affairs and come to a town for its judge and its fair.

## Places {#places}

At the Bitter Sea:

- [[place-dorvakhend|Dorvakhend]]: the caravan town where the road from Bizaria meets the Khazrin road, about 4,000 people.
- [[place-rezhakhend|Rezhakhend]]: the salt town of the eastern shore, about 2,500.

At the Far Sea:

- [[place-morvadez|Morvadez]]: the fort town where the Khazrin road leaves the Kingdom, about 4,500.
- [[place-lemazakhend|Lemazakhend]]: the reed and fishing town across the bay from it, about 2,000.

At the Mirror Sea:

- [[place-gharzadez|Gharzadez]]: the fort town where the March Road comes out of the desert, about 4,000.
- [[place-delmavad|Delmavad]]: the spring oasis of the western shore, about 3,000.

On the plain between the seas:

- [[place-parzavad|Parzavad]]: the oasis of the herd fair, about 3,500.

## Current Situation {#present}

The Kingdom pays more to hold the Three Seas than the country returns, and it pays for the roads. Every tagemader wants a larger garrison than the Host can spare, and those of the northern seas want it most, because [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] warbands cross the border there each summer. On the plain the herding clans and the towns quarrel over water: a town that deepens its wells lowers the springs the flocks drink from, and the clans bring their complaints to the clerk of the Keeper of the Wells at Parzavad, who rules slowly and seldom in their favor.

## Roads and Trade {#roads-and-trade}

Three roads cross the Three Seas and give the towns their living. The Khazrin road runs east from [[place-tahrodan|Tahrodan]] by way of [[place-velgavad|Velgavad]] to Dorvakhend, along the southern shore of the Bitter Sea, and over the plain to Morvadez at the Far Sea, where it leaves the Kingdom for the steppe. At Dorvakhend it meets the road from Bizaria, which comes down from the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]] past [[place-halzikos|Halzikos]] and round the western shore of the Bitter Sea. The March Road comes out of the desert from [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]] to Gharzadez at the Mirror Sea, and runs west by way of [[place-nozhavad|Nozhavad]] to Tahrodan.

Wool, horses, hides, salt and dried fish go west on all three roads. Grain comes east, carried by the caravans and bought with the towns' salt and the dues they take on every load.

## Dangers {#dangers}

**Marauders.** Two kinds of band raid the Three Seas. Khazrin steppe bands ride in from the north-east for herds, caravans and whatever a thin patrol leaves unguarded, most often in winter when the flocks are down by the seas. The kinless of the desert, men cast out by their tribes and claimed by none, raid out of the south, against the March Road and the gardens of the Mirror Sea. The town walls protect the streets and the wells. The herders' camps protect themselves, with riders, dogs and a rule that no camp pitches out of sight of the next.

**The Black Flame.** [[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]] comes into the Three Seas only as warbands. They cross the northern border from the hinterlands of [[place-byzariargn|Bizaria]] and [[place-helionis|Helionis]], at the two northern seas and along the plain between them, burn herders' camps and caravans, and go back across the border with what they take. The faith has no temple and no convert among the Amradi. The fire-temples condemn it, and the Kingdom's garrisons fight its warbands wherever they meet them.

**The ground.** Salt crust over mud at every shore holds a loaded camel fast. Summer dust storms off the dry flats close the roads for days at a time, and a train caught between wells in one runs short of water.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The burned camp.** A herding clan's camp north of Parzavad is burned and its flocks driven off. The clan blames a Black Flame warband, the tagemader of Morvadez blames a Khazrin band, and the tracks lead west toward the Bitter Sea and the border.
- **The low spring.** The springs of Delmavad drop a hand's depth in the season the garrison of Gharzadez sinks a new well. The desert tribes say the compact is broken, and the clerk at Parzavad wants someone who belongs to neither side to measure the water before the tribes take their herds into the gardens.
