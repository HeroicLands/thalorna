## Overview {#overview}

**Lemazakhend**, the reed-cutters' town, is a walled town of about 2,000 on the western shore of [[place-farsea|the Far Sea]], in [[place-threeseas|the Three Seas]], across the bay from [[place-morvadez|Morvadez]]. Its roofs, mats, baskets and boats are all of reed.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Small fish that run up the stream mouths in spring are taken in reed traps, dried on racks and sold to Morvadez, to the caravans and to the herders. The reed beds give mats and roofing for every town of the seas. The town buys its grain from the trains bound for Morvadez and keeps its sweet water in two cisterns that fill in the rains.

## The Present {#present}

The town has a mud wall and a small garrison, and it lies open across the reeds on the border side. [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] warbands cross the border at the sea in summer, and Khazrin steppe bands come in winter, when the reeds are dry enough to burn. The watchmen keep a boat ready to carry warning across the bay to Morvadez.
