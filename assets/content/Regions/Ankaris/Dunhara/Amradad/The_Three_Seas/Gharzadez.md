## Overview {#overview}

**Gharzadez**, the barred fort, is a walled town of about 4,000 on the eastern shore of [[place-mirrorsea|the Mirror Sea]], in [[place-threeseas|the Three Seas]], where [[place-marchroad|the March Road]] comes out of the [[place-dunhardsrtrgn|Dunhara Desert]] into the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]. Its gate faces the desert road.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The town lives on the March Road and the Kingdom's pay. Grain and salt from [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]] come in with the caravans, and the town takes its toll, sells water and fodder, and buys enough of the grain to feed itself. [[place-delmavad|Delmavad]], on the western shore, sends dates and fodder round the sea. Here the trains change their desert escorts for the Kingdom's riders on the road west to [[place-nozhavad|Nozhavad]].

## The Present {#present}

The kinless of the desert raid the last stage of the March Road, where the trains are slowest and their escorts' pay runs out, and the garrison rides out to meet the trains it has word of. A caravan-master pays either a sheikh's escort or the Kingdom's for that stage, and the _tagemader_ wants every one of them to pay the Kingdom's. Two tribes of the edge each claim the right to escort trains across the desert stage beyond the town, and while they quarrel over it, caravans wait inside the walls.
