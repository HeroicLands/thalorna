## Overview {#overview}

**Morvadez**, the watch fort, is a walled town of about 4,500 on a gravel spit of the southern shore of [[place-farsea|the Far Sea]], in [[place-threeseas|the Three Seas]], where the Khazrin road leaves the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]] for the steppe. The citadel at the end of the spit is the strongest wall the Kingdom keeps in the east.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Camel trains coming in from the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin]] pay their toll here and buy water, fodder and fresh beasts, and Khazrin horses, felt and hides are sold on the shore market. The town's grain comes east by caravan from [[place-dorvakhend|Dorvakhend]], its meat from the herders, and its fish from [[place-lemazakhend|Lemazakhend]] across the bay. The Shebarader pays its garrison, the largest in the Three Seas.

## The Present {#present}

Khazrin steppe bands raid the trains on the last days before the town and the flocks beyond its walls, and [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] warbands cross the border at the sea in summer. The _tagemader_ holds the road for a day's ride out and no farther, and a caravan-master who hires a Khazrin escort beyond that pays twice.
