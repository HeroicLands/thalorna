Shamsûn is the oasis city of the Sultan's court and the seat of the [[affiliation-sultntmrdd|Sultanate of Amradad]]. It is not the largest city of [[place-amradadrgn|Amradad]], with fifteen thousand people to [[place-tahrodan|Tahrodan]]'s thirty, and it has no need to be: the Sultan, his Grand Vizier, the divans and the households of the great families live here, and the business of the whole realm comes through its gates.

The city is a place of gardens behind walls, fed by the oasis that first drew the founders of the Sultanate to settle, and of libraries, academies and fire-temples whose scholars set the standard of [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Āsháian]] learning for the desert. It is a city of astrologers too, since no great house of the court marries, travels or quarrels without consulting one, and the astrologers of Shamsûn take retainers far beyond the walls: the elders of [[place-sarath|Sarath]] keep [[being-fakhrdtmrn|Fakhrad Tamârân]] on retainer to read the seasons for their fields.
