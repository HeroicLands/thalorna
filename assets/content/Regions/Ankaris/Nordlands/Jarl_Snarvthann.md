# Dossier {#dossier}

Jarl Snarvthann is a wealthy and corrupt jarl of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]], arrogant and greedy. [[being-dvrnmyldcvr|Dvirnmil the Deceiver]] posed before him as a disgraced nobleman fallen on hard times, with forged documents and a crafted history, and took a place in his court. He convinced the jarl that he knew where treasure lay hidden, and led him to a remote mountain place where bandits he had hired were waiting. With the jarl stranded there, Dvirnmil raided the estate and turned its assets into a private fortune.

The jarl's heirs, humiliated, have hired a relentless bounty hunter to track Dvirnmil down. They want their wealth returned and Dvirnmil's head.
