A bard who walks through the gates of Tvurnholm is looked at twice. The city of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands Region]] is known for its strict laws and its rigid social order. Everyone in Tvurnholm knows their place, and the city's rulers have long held that order to be its greatest strength.

## The Riots

The saga of [[being-sklfmylfrtng|Skulfmil Fire-Tongue]] tells how he brought Tvurnholm to its knees with nothing but his voice. He came in disguised as a traveling bard and told stories that questioned the city's laws, mocked its rulers and urged its people to think for themselves. As his influence grew the people rebelled, and the city was engulfed in riots. By the time the rulers understood what had happened, Skulfmil had moved on and left the city in ruins.

The city's rulers outlasted the riots and have not forgiven them. In the song, **[The Rulers of Tvurnholm]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}**, the city's governing council, place the largest bounty in northern history on Skulfmil's head, dead or alive. The council of today still reads that bounty into its rolls at every new year, though the song sets the riots in [The Age of Halls]{.name data-type="lore/calendar"}, and any bard who comes through the gates is a reminder of the man it names.

## The Name

The name is a reckoner's word: _tvurn-_ is two things that must be counted as one, and the generic that follows is the one for an islet.
