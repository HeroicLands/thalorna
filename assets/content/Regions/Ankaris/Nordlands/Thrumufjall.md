Thrumufjall is a sheer seamount on the [[place-nordheim|Nordheim]] coast, topped by an ancient temple of [[lore-thrunvalddty|Thrúnvald]] and the mother-hall of the [[affiliation-ordrstrmspkrs|Storm-Speakers]]. The exposed summit serves their weather-craft training and the receptive examination held during thunderstorms. The god’s Hofgodi keeps the hof’s rites and sea-blessings beside the order, whose craft answers to different vows. In [[being-skrldmylstrmbrn|Skrildmýl Stormborn]]’s saga, the mountain is where Thrúnvald is said to have struck the earth with [[lore-thrunhamarr|Thrúnhamarr]], and the hero leads his people there through winter storms that cease after his appeal at the summit.

## The Climb Through White Weather

A coastal telling follows Skrildmýl's winter ascent and the clearing of the storm, then returns to the mountain's hof and mother-hall. The saga's answer to his appeal is remembered under the dangerous skies that still visit the summit.

```poetry {form=epic lang=en}
Name the mountain under snow;
name it above the sea.
Thrumufjall thrusts through white weather;
thunder finds its naked summit.
Stone shoulders the storm's weight;
salt water pounds its footing.
High holds the ancient hof;
hard wind visits the mother-hall.
Climb while the coast vanishes;
keep your companions beside you.

Down lies the dark village;
doors bow against winter's breathing.
Smoke scatters over frozen thatch;
snow fills the familiar lanes.
Ropes wear their rigid ice;
roofs groan beneath white burdens.
Hands hover over failing embers;
hunger waits beyond the hearth.
Has the Thunderer turned away?
Has he forgotten our faces?

Skrildmýl stands among his people;
sorrow weighs their searching eyes.
He hears the hearthside asking;
he hears it without words.
The storm shall test faith;
still he holds that belief.
Yet lives lie in his keeping;
yet he must lead them.
Mountainward he makes their going;
men gather their worn cloaks.

There the Thunderer struck earth;
there Thrúnhamarr met the mountain.
That name needs no sunshine;
night cannot hide its meaning.
They carry the coast within;
cold goes ahead of them.
Their doors dwindle behind snow;
darkness takes the last roofing.
Climb while the village vanishes;
keep your companions beside you.

Wind sets its shoulder forward;
weary shoulders answer the pushing.
Cold creeps beneath every fastening;
cuffs gather the cutting snow.
Feet feel the hidden footing;
fresh tracks vanish behind them.
The leader lets his pace
lie within his people's strength.
No boast bears anyone upward;
breath buys the next step.

Bent goes the burdened line;
backs bow before the gale.
A hand reaches backward blindly;
here another hand takes hold.
No shield shines in formation;
no shout orders the weather.
Cloaks cling to soaked bodies;
courage smells of damp wool.
They wanted their doors whole;
they walk for their hearths.

Nearer looms the nameless whiteness;
now the mountain hides itself.
Stone shows through the drifting;
sharp are its dark edges.
Below beats the breaking sea;
bone feels its heavy rhythm.
Words whip from windward mouths;
wind takes them before hearing.
Climb while the sky vanishes;
keep your companions beside you.

Skrildmýl's name steadies the line;
someone sends it back whispering.
Hard is the hero's going;
he has no sheltered breathing.
His cloak cracks against stone;
cold threads through his mail.
One word wins another footing;
one hand finds another sleeve.
Come. Keep close through whiteness;
climb where my foot stands.

Hammer-marked lies the mountain beneath;
hard stone receives their soles.
The summit shoulders the sky;
storm presses its bare head.
White water swallows the coastline;
wind blots out the distance.
Between them the bodies labor;
between them their village climbs.
Hearth-smoke lives in their longing;
home waits within their names.

Fire shall warm the listeners;
frost still holds the climbers.
We know their welcome ending;
wind gives them no knowledge.
Their weariness wins the summit;
words cannot carry them there.
The last steps scrape stone;
legs tremble beneath the standing.
Skrildmýl turns toward the thunder;
silent people gather behind him.

Raw is the throat calling;
rain runs over his lips.
Thrúnvald, hear your people here;
Thrúnhamarr, remember the earth's striking.
His breath breaks upon cold;
behind him their breath gathers.
Faces lift into the weather;
faith stands with frightened hearts.
Let the hall hold still;
let the last cry travel.

Storm ceases upon the summit;
sudden silence fills their hearing.
Blue breaks through broken clouds;
bare heaven spreads above them.
Sea finds its far horizon;
stone finds its sheer distance.
White masks fall from faces;
weary eyes meet other eyes.
The people stand beneath clearing;
the prayer has found answer.

Hammers need no further striking;
here the great name suffices.
Light lies upon the mountain;
laughter wakes among its climbers.
Skrildmýl stands with his people;
strong was their joined walking.
They brought the village upward;
bright weather meets its longing.
Climb, and carry your neighbors;
come home with faces remembered.

Years yield to other winters;
yet the stone stands seaward.
The ancient hof holds rites;
harbor ships receive its blessing.
The Hofgodi keeps the offering;
his words rise toward Thrúnvald.
Near stands the Speakers' mother-hall;
new hands learn its weather-craft.
Storm visits the shared summit;
separate vows receive its coming.

One height, the winds' highway;
one horizon above the water.
Two callings keep their promises;
two paths meet upon stone.
The priest presents the offering;
praise answers the approaching storm.
The Speaker studies its shape;
speech waits upon the reading.
Shared clouds crown their labor;
separate vows receive their burden.

An aspirant stands under thunder;
air sharpens upon his tongue.
Rain runs across his eyelids;
rough sleeves darken upon him.
His hands hang open waiting;
he hears the sky's crashing.
Deep must the weather stir;
deep waits his answering sense.
The teacher turns toward him;
the thunder takes their breathing.

Clouds cross the teacher's seeing;
careful eyes follow their gathering.
The priest lifts his offering;
pale rain threads his beard.
Below waits the harbor's mouth;
boats pull against their ropes.
A sailor studies the spray;
sea throws salt against him.
Skrildmýl's sky shines in remembrance;
still the dark weather threatens.

Thrumufjall takes the returning rain;
thunder shakes its high silence.
Salt whitens the sea-washed stone;
squalls shorten the coast's horizon.
The hall holds to craft;
the hof holds to rites.
Below them the roofs need mending;
below them the ropes need testing.
Sacred stands the storm's mountain;
small hands keep their labor.

Sing the steps through snow;
sing the hands reaching backward.
Give the leader his courage;
give his people their weariness.
Faith followed the frozen way;
feet bore the seeking upward.
A name keeps a direction;
night cannot scour it away.
Climb while the coast vanishes;
keep your companions beside you.

Low sings the sea;
listen beneath the summit's stone.
High holds the clearing;
here burns our hearth.
Wind walks outside the doorway;
we draw the benches closer.
Thrumufjall, thrust through white weather;
the threatened coast remembers you.
Come. Keep close through whiteness;
carry the hands reaching backward.
```
