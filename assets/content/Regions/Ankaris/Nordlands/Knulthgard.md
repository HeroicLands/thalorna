## Overview

Knulthgard is a city of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] known for its strict laws and its rigid social order, and the name was given in the same spirit: _knulth-_, an oath that binds without being written, before the generic for an enclosed yard and its buildings. Everyone in Knulthgard knows their place, and the city's rulers have long held that order to be its greatest strength.

## The Riots

The saga of [[being-sklfmylfrtng|Skulfmýl Fire-Tongue]] tells how he brought Knulthgard to its knees with nothing but his voice. He came in disguised as a traveling bard and told stories that questioned the city's laws, mocked its rulers and urged its people to think for themselves; as his influence grew the people rebelled, and the city was engulfed in riots. By the time the rulers understood what had happened, Skulfmýl had moved on and left the city in ruins.

The city's rulers outlasted the riots and have not forgiven them. **The Rulers of Knulthgard**, the city's governing council, have placed the largest bounty in northern history on Skulfmýl's head, dead or alive, and any bard who comes through the gates is a reminder of the man they want.
