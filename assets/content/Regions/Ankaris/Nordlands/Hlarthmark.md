## Overview

Hlarthmark is march country of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]], held at an edge as its name says: _hlarth-_, the slope a hall is set on, before the generic for a march, ground held at an edge. It is farming country, and its people remember the year their crops failed better than any year they succeeded.

## The Blight and the Rune

A terrible blight once beset Hlarthmark, one no healer could cure, and the people turned to [[being-rlthrvrnskld|Rilthorv Runaskald]]. The saga tells that, guided by [[lore-odvardty|Ódvar]], he spent three days and nights in a trance seeking the root of the curse and came out of it on the third night with a single rune cut into a piece of bark. He went to the heart of Hlarthmark, sang the old verses of Ódvar and pressed the rune into the earth, and the blight lifted.

The people of Hlarthmark hailed Rilthorv as their savior, and his song entered their harvest rituals. It is still sung at the realm's harvest feasts, generations later, by farmers who have never seen him.
