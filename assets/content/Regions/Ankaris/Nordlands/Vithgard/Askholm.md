## Overview

Askholm is a bare skerry in the waters off [[place-dvarnvik|Dvarnvík]] in [[place-vithgard|Vithgard]], and nobody lives on it. Forty-one years ago, after a winter of failed whaling, a ting at Dvarnvík accused eleven people of working the winds, and all eleven were burned here: seven women, two plateau men who had sold wind-knots, and two Dvarnvík men accused of _ergi_. The boatmen who row past it still name the rock and say nothing more.

## The Judgment That Survived

**Völva Aldrhildr** was accused with the eleven but never tried. A Sworn Hand of the [[affiliation-eidhond|Order of the Sworn Hand]] ruled that foretelling alone is no working against a named person, and that ruling is the reason a seer's prophecy in Vithgard is not treated as proof of sorcery. The precedent survives in every ting of the kingdom; it does not vindicate the burning of those who were tried, and the deaths remain a grievance. Some plateau clans still avoid Dvarnvík, and the descendants of the accused keep their own accounts of who spoke against whom.
