## Overview

Families with more sons than holdings send the extra ones to Snilthborg. The fortress-barracks of [[place-vithgard|Vithgard]] sits on a height and takes boys, and it returns warriors-for-hire. The name is the mark winter leaves on its walls: _snilth-_, the line frost leaves along a stone, before the generic for a stronghold on a height.

## The Training

The training at Snilthborg is brutal by design, made to strip away softness and put reflexive violence in its place. The famous sword-master **Glirvir Marvarukh** trains there. [[being-thrldrvvrnhm|Thraldorv Vörnheim]] came to him at eleven, a jarl's third son with no claim to his father's holding, and had drawn blood in earnest by sixteen. Not every boy survives the regime: Thraldorv watched his own brother die in training under Glirvir, nearly twenty years ago.

Ask Thraldorv, who now guards merchants at [[place-dvarnvik|Dvarnvík]], what Snilthborg did to him, and he gives a short answer: it took what was soft.

## The Brother

That death has outlived the boy. If a young warrior ever turned up in Vithgard's settlements claiming to be that lost brother, bearing his mark and with no history before three years past, the trail would lead back to Snilthborg and whatever happened there.
