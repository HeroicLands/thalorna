## Overview

Thrúnborg is the seat of the [[affiliation-kngdmvthgrd|Kingdom of Vithgard]], a fortified town of roughly 700 souls built around the royal hall where the king holds court and the kingdom's jarls gather to settle disputes and plan its defense. Its name is drawn from [[affiliation-thrunvald|Thrúnvald]], the Thunderer of the [[affiliation-asguardian|Asguardian Pantheon]]: a `-borg`, a stronghold on a height, held from a god rather than from any mortal founder.

The town's garrison guards the approach from the ice-choked coast, and its markets draw traders from across [[place-vithgard|Vithgard]]'s scattered settlements. Winters bring the kingdom's Hersvalds within its walls for the long dark, when the ting's business is conducted by firelight and the völvur read the season's portents for the year ahead.
