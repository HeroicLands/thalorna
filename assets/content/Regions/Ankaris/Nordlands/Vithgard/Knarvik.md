## Overview

Knarvík takes its name from a joint built to take strain, and the four hundred people here have made that the town's whole trade: this is where a **Vithgard** ship comes to have its ribs and strakes reinforced before a whaling run or a winter crossing, and a joint cut at Knarvík has a reputation for holding when a cheaper repair elsewhere would open under ice-pressure. The harbor itself is built for the work rather than for shelter, with slipways and timber-yards crowding what would otherwise be fishing frontage.

A [[lore-hersvaldrnk|Hersvald]] holds the district and a Skipstjóri answers to him for the town's own vessels in the kingdom's levy, a double authority that suits a settlement whose livelihood is ships rather than fish. **Dvarnvir Hrafnsvald** works the yards as both tentmaker and a trained hand should the levy call him, which is the ordinary arrangement for a craftsman in a town this close to the ice edge—there is no trade here that war cannot interrupt, so most of the four hundred keep some skill at arms besides their craft.

Knarvík's standing tension is with the ships' owners rather than the crown: a repair that takes longer than a captain wants to wait costs the yard its reputation for one job and its custom for the next ten, and the yardmasters argue constantly with a Hersvald who would rather see every hull patched fast than patched well.
