## Overview

A whaling skipper with a cracked rib in his hull puts into Knarvík and does not ask when he will sail. He asks what it will cost to do the job properly. This is the harbor where a [[place-vithgard|Vithgard]] ship comes to have its ribs and strakes reinforced before a whaling run or a winter crossing, and a joint cut at Knarvík has a reputation for holding when a cheaper repair elsewhere would open under ice-pressure. The harbor is built for the work and not for shelter, with slipways and timber-yards crowding what would otherwise be fishing frontage. Four hundred people live here, and the town's name is a joint built to take strain.

A hersvald holds the district, and a skipstjóri answers to him for the town's own vessels in the kingdom's levy, a double authority that suits a settlement whose livelihood is ships and not fish. [[being-dvrnvrhrfnsvld|Dvarnvir Hrafnsvald]] works the yards as both tentmaker and a trained hand should the levy call him, the ordinary arrangement for a craftsman in a town this close to the ice edge. There is no trade here that war cannot interrupt, so most of the four hundred keep some skill at arms besides their craft.

Knarvík's standing tension is with the ships' owners and not with the crown. A repair that takes longer than a captain wants to wait costs the yard its reputation for one job and its custom for the next ten, and the yardmasters argue constantly with a hersvald who would rather see every hull patched fast than patched well.
