## Overview

Hluskdal is a dale of [[place-vithgard|Vithgard]] whose farms stand empty. Ninety years ago sickness went up the dale in the polar dark, and when the light came back one child was alive: the girl remembered as the **Loft-Girl**. Nobody has farmed Hluskdal since, and a traveler who takes the dale road passes roofless steadings with their doors still standing.

## Held for the Dead

The emptiness is a ruling as much as a memory. The [[place-hrandstead|Hrandstead]] ting held the empty farms for the dead, on a Sworn Hand's word, until their last owner is laid—and the dead of Hluskdal have not all been laid. The ruling stands as a claim against any descendant who wants to reclaim the dale, so the farms stay empty however good the grazing looks from the road.

A second telling gives the sickness a body: a woman called the **Grey Walker**, carrying a sieve and a sack. Some say she served [[affiliation-nahild|Náhild]]; others say she is only a face given to disease and winter. The emptying and the ting's judgment are remembered history. Who the Walker is, nobody knows.
