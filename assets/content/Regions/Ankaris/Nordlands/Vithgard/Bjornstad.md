## Overview

Hrandstead sprawls across a plateau in the heart of Vithgard, a fortified settlement of roughly 1,000 souls that serves as the seat of its own province and one of the kingdom's principal strongholds. Wild bears roam the forests around it, and the noble line that has held power here for three generations, the Bjorn-Königers, take their own name from them, their banner showing a rampant bear worked in silver thread. The settlement is dominated by a massive timber fortress with stone foundations, its walls twice as tall as a man and its watchtowers visible for leagues across the plateau.

Hrandstead is first and foremost a military strongpoint, though time and prosperity have allowed it to develop the amenities of a true town. The harbor—accessed by the deep rivers that cut through Vithgard's interior—links the settlement to maritime trade, and merchant vessels bring goods from across the northern kingdoms. The settlement is ringed by workshops, tanneries, and smithies where the tools of war are forged and maintained. Regular markets draw traders from as far as Norgaad and the frozen wastes to the north, and the town has developed a reputation for fair dealing and strong enforcement of contract.

The jarl's hall is the administrative and ceremonial heart of Hrandstead, where the jarl of the Bjorn-Königers holds court and settles the province's disputes. The settlement serves as a refuge during harsh winters and a mustering point for the province's defense, and its garrison of professional soldiers is regarded as one of the finest in the north. The town's reputation is one of martial strength tempered by pragmatic rule—a place where law is enforced, but not with the capricious cruelty of some northern strongholds.
