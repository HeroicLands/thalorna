## Overview

"Hold it to the light," an antler-carver at Marvstead says, turning a length of walrus tusk in the lamp's glare for the southern buyer who has come to bargain. "Here the grain runs for a comb. Here it wants a hilt. This end is only good for trade-weight beads." Five hundred people here turn walrus tusk, narwhal horn and reindeer antler into the carved goods that carry [[place-vithgard|Vithgard]]'s name as far south as [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]]. A carver reads the grain the way a shipwright reads a plank, and the settlement's reputation rests on knowing which piece of ivory wants which use.

The name is the grain inside worked antler. The town is one of the larger Vithgard settlements, and a hersvald holds the district from a hall at its center, collecting the dues that ivory and whale-oil both owe the crown before either leaves for a southern market. [[being-tvlgthnblthl|Tvalgthann Blóthöll]] earns his bread as a jester in a town whose winters run as dark and long as any in the kingdom, work that counts for more here than it would in a sunnier country.

Marvstead's quarrel with its own hersvald runs on supply and not on law. Ivory is only as good as what the hunters bring in, and a thin season at the ice edge means carvers with empty workbenches and a hersvald still expecting the same dues he collected the year before.
