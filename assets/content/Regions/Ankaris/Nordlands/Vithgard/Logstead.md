## Overview

Lögstead is held from the law, and its name says so: _lög-_, the law as it is recited, set before the generic for a farmstead. It is where the [[affiliation-kngdmvthgrd|Kingdom of Vithgard]] holds its own ting, and where the hof of [[lore-eidgardty|Eidgar]] swears its blót into the same oath the assembly swears over it. Ask anyone on the road in why the place matters and you get the same answer in fewer words: the offering and the law are bound together at one hearth here, and nowhere else in the north binds them so tightly.

## The Hof and the Hall

Two bodies keep one roof at Lögstead and serve one god, and a newcomer does well to learn the difference before speaking to either. The **Hofgodi of Lögstead** keeps the hof of the [[affiliation-eidgar|Faith of Eidgar]]: he consecrates it, ordains the godar who serve under him, and keeps the ting that can be kept. The [[affiliation-eidhond|Order of the Sworn Hand]] keeps its mother-hall beside him, and its knights ride out to where the ting cannot reach. "A knight enforces a ruling with his own sword," the Hofgodi tells visitors. "I bind the oath the ruling rests on."

The order's whole governance sits here. The **Council of Hands**—twelve Elder Hands drawn from the five kingdoms—meets at Lögstead every quarter, examines candidates in a final personal interview, tries a Hand accused of a broken ruling, and elects the order's chair for life. That chair is the **Voice of Lögstead**, _Lögstadar Mál_ in the north's own speech, and the present Voice is **Knilthvir Lögstadar**, a man in his late sixties whose judgments are quoted in halls that have never seen him. His wand of office is wrapped in black leather, and a traveler who sees a black-wrapped wand at a ting has seen the order's highest authority in person.

## What a Visitor Meets

Lögstead lives at the pace of its courts. When the ting sits, the stead fills with claimants, witnesses and their kin, and every bench in every hall is spoken for; when the Council of Hands meets, the white surcoats of Sworn Hands from all five kingdoms crowd the yard of the mother-hall. Between sittings it is a working stead like any other, where the talk at the hearth still turns on precedent. Swear nothing here lightly: an oath made at Lögstead is made before Eidgar's own hof, and the north holds the breaking of such an oath the gravest offense its faith knows.
