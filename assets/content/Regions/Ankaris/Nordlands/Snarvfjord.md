## Overview

Snarvfjord is the seat of **Jarl Hvurnmýl of Snarvfjord**, and its name warns of the water: _snarv-_, the hour before weather arrives, before the generic for a fjord. Squalls come down the fjord fast, and the boatmen read the sky before they read anything else. The jarl's court sits at the head of the fjord, where a hall can see what is coming up the water a long way off.

## A Cautious Court

Jarl Hvurnmýl is a cautious ruler, and his court runs at the pace of his caution. He makes no major decision without first hearing [[being-hnirvmylwise|Hnirvmýl the Wise]], to whom he gives shelter, resources and access to his court in exchange for guidance on war and diplomacy. A petitioner who wants an answer from Snarvfjord learns to put the case to Hnirvmýl first, because the jarl will ask him anyway; an envoy who tries to hurry the jarl past his counselor finds the hall suddenly in no hurry at all.
