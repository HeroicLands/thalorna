An envoy who has ridden three days to see the jarl at Snarvfjord is sent to put his case to the jarl's counselor first, because the jarl will ask him anyway. An envoy who tries to hurry the jarl past his counselor finds the hall suddenly in no hurry at all.

Snarvfjord is a fjord seat of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]], and the songs of the [[doc-heroessgrd|Heroes of Asguard]] make it the court of **Jarl [[being-hvurnvir|Hvurnvir]] of Snarvfjord** in [The Age of Halls]{.name data-type="lore/calendar"}. The court sits at the head of the fjord, where a hall can see what is coming up the water a long way off, and squalls come down the fjord fast. The boatmen read the sky before they read anything else.

## A Cautious Court

In the song, Jarl Hvurnvir is a cautious ruler who makes no major decision without first hearing [[being-hnirvmylwise|Hnirmil the Wise]], to whom he gives shelter, resources and access to his court in exchange for guidance on war and diplomacy. The jarls of Snarvfjord have kept a counselor's seat beside their own since, and the court still runs at the pace of that caution. A petitioner who wants an answer from Snarvfjord learns to put the case to whoever sits in Hnirmil's seat first.

## The Name

The name warns of the water: _snarv-_ is the hour before weather arrives, and the generic that follows is the one for a fjord.
