## Overview

Hrundborg is a fortress of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] built on a height, and its name is a promise its builders meant: _hrund-_, a thing set down that will not be moved, before the generic for a stronghold on a height. Its walls shelter refugees—people driven off their land who have nowhere else to go—and the fortress is worth exactly as much as its walls are worth on the day an enemy comes up the slope.

## The Siege

A fortress full of refugees is a fortress full of mouths, and Hrundborg's danger is a siege by a host far larger than anything its garrison can meet in the field. Holding it means organizing a defense out of whatever the refugees can carry and keeping the walls manned long enough for reinforcements to arrive, if they come at all. [[being-krldthnshldbr|Krildthann Shieldbearer]] is the kind of defender the fortress needs on such a day, and the GM who brings the siege of Hrundborg into play has a ready reason to put him on its walls.
