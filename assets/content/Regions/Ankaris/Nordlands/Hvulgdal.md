## Overview

Hvulgdal is a farming dale of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]], held by **Jarl Hlaskynda of Hvulgdal**, and its name says what its first settlers valued: _hvulg-_, a bay that shelters in any wind, before the generic for a dale. The halls stand on the slopes and the fields lie below them, and in a good year the dale feeds more than its own people. In a north where most land is rock, fell or fjord, a dale that grows grain reliably is wealth of the kind a jarl guards closely.

## The Jarl and Her Steward of Harvests

Jarl Hlaskynda is known as a wise ruler, and the clearest proof of it is whom she listens to. She values the counsel of [[being-brldrvgrnshld|Bruldorv Greenshield]] on everything that grows, and she has given him authority over the management of her lands' harvests: when the fields are sown, what is stored against the winter, and what goes to market. A farmer of Hvulgdal with a quarrel over seed-corn or a share of the threshing takes it to Bruldorv before the jarl, and the dale accepts his word on it.
