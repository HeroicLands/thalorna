A suitor at the Court of Justice brings a witness and brings patience, because Lögthul takes the cases the district tings cannot. The town is built around its court. The **Court of Justice at Lögthul** is the oldest seat of law in the northern territories, and it is the court the north reaches for when a case is too great for a district ting.

## The Great Trial

The town's most famous day is the trial in the saga of [[being-knlthnlwgvr|Knalthann Lawgiver]]. A powerful hersvald stood accused of treason against the king, the evidence was scarce and the witnesses divided, and Knalthann spent days hearing testimony and weighing the principles of [[lore-eidgardty|Eidgar]] before he gave judgment. On the day of the verdict he produced what everyone else had missed, an incriminating rune hidden among the accused's own belongings, and the hersvald was convicted.

## The Court Today

The Court grants standing to judges who ride far beyond the town. [[being-vrldrvonhnd|Vraldorv One-Hand]] holds permanent standing there as an itinerant judge, with authority across clan boundaries, and a ruling he gives in a hall many days away carries the court's weight with it.

## The Name

The name is a plain statement of what the town is for: _lög-_ is the law as it is recited, and _-thul_ is a seat where the law is recited.
