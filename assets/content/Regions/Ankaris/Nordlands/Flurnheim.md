At Flurnheim's harvest feasts the farmers sing the verses attributed to [[lore-odvardty|Odvar]] that [[being-rlthrvrnskld|Rilthorv Runaskald]] sang to lift the blight, and not one of the singers has ever seen him. Flurnheim is farming country in the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]], and its people remember the year their crops failed better than any year they succeeded.

## The Blight and the Rune

In [The Age of Halls]{.name data-type="lore/calendar"}, the songs say, a terrible blight beset Flurnheim, one no healer could cure, and the people turned to Rilthorv. The saga tells that, guided by Odvar, he spent three days and nights in a trance seeking the root of the curse. He came out of it on the third night with a single rune cut into a piece of bark. He went to the heart of Flurnheim, sang the old verses of Odvar and pressed the rune into the earth, and the blight lifted.

The people hailed Rilthorv as their savior, and his song entered their harvest rituals. It is still sung at the harvest feasts, centuries later, by farmers who have never seen him.

## The Name

The name is a hard country's: _flurn-_ is a flake of stone split off by frost, and the generic that follows is the one for a settled place.
