## Overview

Vrathavn—the Temper-Haven—is [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]]'s principal port on the southern coast, a fortified settlement of roughly 800 souls built around a natural harbor protected by towering headlands. The town bristles with the apparatus of war: a timber palisade with stone-faced watchtowers overlooks the harbor, a longship berth can shelter a dozen raiders at a time, and the streets are laid out with military precision. Iron bells hang from the watchtowers; their chiming at dawn and dusk is as reliable as the tide. The settlement's heart is the Shield-Hall, where the town's most distinguished shield-maiden holds court and where the honor-bonds between crews are sealed.

Vrathavn is foremost a military settlement, though commerce inevitably follows strength. The harbor teems with activity: longships being repaired and provisioned, crews recruiting for summer raids, and a constant flow of tribute from outlying settlements. The town maintains a garrison of professional warriors—the closest thing Norgaad has to a standing army—and young fighters from across the kingdom come to Vrathavn seeking fame and fortune, hoping to prove themselves worthy of a seat in a raider's crew.

The shield-maiden who commands Vrathavn's respect is legendary for her victories in the shield-wall; she has trained generations of warriors and her word is law in the settlement. The taverns here are rowdy, the smithies work day and night, and the atmosphere crackles with the kinetic energy of a place perpetually preparing for war—or for the wealth that war brings.
