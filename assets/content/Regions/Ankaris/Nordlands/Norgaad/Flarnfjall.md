The **Flarnfjall** are the mountains of central [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]]. [[place-tvarnmark|Tvarnmark]] stands on their southern edge, at the confluence of two swift rivers, where merchants from the coast meet traders from the high passes and the interior valleys. A pass road climbs from the town through the high Flarnfjall country toward the mining valleys beyond, past the [[place-shtrdpks|Shattered Peaks]], the massif that frost has split into leaning spires.

The high shoulders above Tvarnmark are summer grazing for caribou, and the scree, cirques and boulder-fields of the range are the ground of the [[being-rckbckbr|Rockback Bear]].
