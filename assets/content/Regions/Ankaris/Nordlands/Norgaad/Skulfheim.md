## Overview

Before you go into a gallery at Skulfheim, you knock on the timber, and then you listen. The miners of this [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]] mining place say that the _thursar_ (giant-kin) knock back when a mountain is unsafe, and none of them goes in without waiting for the answer. The place is named for the same caution: _skulf-_ is the shudder in ice before it gives, set before _-heim_, a settled place.

## The Knock

The knock is the first thing a new hand learns at Skulfheim and the last thing an old one forgets. You rap the timber that holds up the gallery roof and you wait; a knock in reply means the mountain is not safe that day, and you do not go in. Whether anything in the rock knocks belongs to mountain lore, and the miners do not argue it. The habit is not in doubt: nobody at Skulfheim enters a gallery without knocking first.

## The Cloaked Stone

The same lore reaches the saddle on the upper road from [[place-tvarnmark|Tvarnmark]] toward the [[place-shtrdpks|Shattered Peaks]]. A great stone there has gathered cloaks, left for travelers the mountains took. Some are for men of the king's brother's expedition, which went up that road thirty-two years ago seeking mithral. The king's brother and most of his forty companions never came back, and memorial stones on the upper road name some of the lost. Mountain lore holds that the great stones are the walking remnants of [[lore-hrimthurspr|Hrímthur]], the Rime-Giant. The memorials and the miners' caution stand without proving it.
