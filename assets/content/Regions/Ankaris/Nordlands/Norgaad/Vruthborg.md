## Overview

High in the [[place-flarnfjall|Flarnfjall]], above a pass that snow closes for half the year, a fortress of dry-laid stone stands on a spur over the mouths of its own mines. It is **Vruthborg**: _vruth-_, the temper iron takes from the fire, in its deep grade, with _-borg_, a stronghold on a height. The iron and silver that come out of the rock under it are some of the best in [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]], and the fortress was built to hold them: a curtain wall with a gatehouse, a hall, a barracks range for the jarl's drengir and miners, a treasury cut into the rock, and a foundry whose furnace was the pride of the district.

Nobody has come down from Vruthborg in a long time. The chimney still smokes.

## The Jarl

Vruthborg was the odal of **Brildorv Braldendikh**, a jarl of the interior who held the workings and the pass from the king and paid his tally of iron and silver to the king's smiths at [[place-asgarthul|Asgarthul]]. He was a smith before he was a jarl, and he never stopped going down to the foundry. In his last winters the forge-cough took his breath, and he had no heir of his body. His odal would pass by the ting to a kinsman he despised, and his forge to whoever the king sent.

A guest came up the pass that winter and stayed. The smith's rune-staves call him only the guest, and they do not describe him. After he came the jarl ordered a sealed chamber built under the furnace, lined with iron, with iron doors cut with runes the smith did not know. The north credits that kind of carving to the [[affiliation-nghtwghtcncl|Nightwight Council]], and nobody at Vruthborg lived to say whether the north is right.

## The Night the Household Died

On one night of deep winter the furnace was fired harder than it had ever been fired. Flues from the foundry ran under the hall and the barracks to warm them, as the jarl had laid them, and before morning the floors were hot enough to char the sleepers in their beds. Nobody carried the dead out through a gap cut in the wall, as the [[lore-nrdlndsfnrl|rite]] requires. Nobody was left to.

The dead of Vruthborg did not stay down. They are _hélthrall_ now, [[lore-undead|Helspawn]] of the driven kind, bound by [[lore-theshadow|the Shadow]] and moved by the will in the furnace: they stoke it, they keep the walls, and when the heat runs through the fortress they move, and when it ebbs they stand where they are and the frost takes them. The jarl's body was never brought out of the foundry. The hammering in it has not stopped.

## Who Knows of It

- **The district's officer** comes up from the valley to weigh the ore of the mining camps against the king's tally, as he does at [[place-flarnbrekka|Flarnbrekka]], and Vruthborg's tally has not come down since that winter. He has not gone up to ask why.
- **The miners of [[place-skulfheim|Skulfheim]]** knock on their timbers and listen before they enter a gallery. They say the Vruthborg workings knock back every time.
- **Travelers on the pass road** see the smoke from below and take it for a fortress in good order, until they come close enough to feel the gate.

## Reaching It

By the pass road through the Flarnfjall, a day's climb above the last summer pasture, and then by a switchback track up the spur to the gatehouse. The workings under the fortress run through the mountain, and an old haulage tunnel carries ore down from them to a sledge-road on the far side of the pass, below the snow line. Late in the year, snow closes the pass road behind a party that goes up.
