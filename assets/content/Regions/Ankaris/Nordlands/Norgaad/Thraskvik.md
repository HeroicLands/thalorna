## Overview

Thraskvík is a port town on the coast of [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]], counted among the most vital coastal towns of the [[affiliation-kingdmnrgd|Kingdom of Norgaad]], and its name carries the sound its harbor is known for: _thrask-_, a keel taking shingle, set before the generic for an inlet. Stand on the strand at any tide and you hear it—hulls drawn up the beach, hulls shoved off it, and in the yards above the waterline the adzes of the shipwrights who build the sleek longships Norgaad is famous for.

## The Yards

The town's standing rests on its shipwright families, and a renowned yard passes its craft down the line from parent to child. Children of those families learn to shape a strake before they learn their letters, and a yard's pride is measured in the hulls still sailing that it built. Not every child stays: [[being-vrthmylfrhr|Vrathmýl Firehair]] was the son of one of Thraskvík's renowned shipwright families and trained in the craft from a young age before the arcane drew him away from the yard, for all his family's pride in their trade.
