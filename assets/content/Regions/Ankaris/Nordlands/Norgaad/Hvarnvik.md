## Overview

Every other harbor in the kingdom is shut by the hard months of winter, and the harbor-reeve posted to Hvarnvík for the cold season calls his own the door. A current running close against this stretch of coast keeps the inlet open when ice locks every bay for leagues in either direction, so a ship that times its northern run to reach Hvarnvík before the freeze can still move goods when no other port in [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]] can. The name says it plainly: _hvarn-_ is water that will not freeze.

That single advantage is the whole of the village's standing, and it makes its two hundred people far more important to the kingdom than their number would suggest. The [[lore-hersvaldrnk|hersvald]] of the coastal district treats Hvarnvík as a winter lifeline and not an ordinary fishing village, and a harbor-reeve is posted here through the cold months specifically to keep the lane open to whatever the inland mines need to move before spring. [[being-knlthrnhfrngr|Knalthrinna Hafringr]] works as a teamster between the harbor and the inland roads, hauling in winter what the frozen rivers elsewhere in the kingdom cannot carry.

## The Season and the Sea

Hvarnvík's tension is the ordinary one of a place whose value is seasonal and whose population is not. The village earns the kingdom's attention for four months and is left to its own fishing for the other eight, and the hersvald's visits thin out exactly when the winter work is hardest.

Fishers here tell two tales of the sea: an island that rises without birds and takes a boat down with it when it dives, and a thumb tucked under when the [[affiliation-asguardian|Ten]] are counted at [[lore-sumarmal|Sumarmál]]. [[lore-nrgadhstry|Histories and Legends of Norgaad]] records both, and neither adds an eleventh god to the Ten.
