## Overview

Hvarnvík's water does not freeze even in the deep of a [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]] winter, which the stem names plainly and which makes the village's two hundred people disproportionately important to a kingdom whose other harbors shut for months at a time. A current running close against this stretch of coast keeps the inlet open when ice locks every bay for leagues in either direction, and a ship that times its northern run to reach Hvarnvík before the freeze can still move goods when every other port in the kingdom cannot.

That single advantage is the whole of Hvarnvík's standing. The [[lore-hersvaldrnk|Hersvald]] of the coastal district treats it as a winter lifeline rather than an ordinary fishing village, and a Harbour-reeve is posted here through the cold months specifically to keep the lane open to whatever the inland mines need to move before spring. [[being-knlthrnhfrngr|Knalthrinna Hafringr]] works as a teamster between the harbor and the inland roads, hauling in winter what the frozen rivers elsewhere in the kingdom cannot carry.

Hvarnvík's tension is the ordinary one of a place whose value is seasonal and whose population is not: the village earns the kingdom's attention for four months and is left to its own fishing for the other eight, and the Hersvald's visits thin out exactly when the winter work is hardest.
