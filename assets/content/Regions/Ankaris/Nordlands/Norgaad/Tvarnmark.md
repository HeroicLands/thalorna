## Overview

Tvarnmark rises on the southern edge of the Kjaldar mountains, at the confluence of two swift rivers, where merchants from the coast meet traders from the high passes and the interior valleys. The town of perhaps 600 souls is built of stone quarried from the surrounding hills—unusual for a [[lore-nordheimnclt|Nordmen]] settlement—and its sturdy buildings seem to grow directly from the rock. The marketplace is the settlement's beating heart, a broad square surrounded by warehouses where furs, amber, and whale bone from the coastal settlements meet timber, iron, and salt brought south from the mining regions beyond the mountains.

Tvarnmark's reputation rests on the skill of its craftspeople, particularly its weavers and textile workers. The looms of the town produce cloth of exceptional quality—linen so fine it's almost gossamer, and wool dyed with plant-dyes from the southern forests that holds color like no other. A master textile craftsperson of extraordinary renown maintains a workshop here, and her commissions—garments for jarls, trading cloths for merchants—draw customers from as far as Vylaria and [[place-provenzrgn|Provènzia]]. Young craftspeople apprentice to her workshop to learn secrets jealously guarded across generations.

The settlement sits at the intersection of power and commerce, close enough to the coast to feel the maritime culture of [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]], but rooted firmly in trade and craft. The town council is dominated by the great merchant families and the guild masters, and decisions are made over ale rather than in the shield-hall. Despite its commercial focus, Tvarnmark maintains a strong militia; the road from the mountains is not always peaceful, and caravans require protection.
