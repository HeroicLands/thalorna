## Overview

Once a year, at the turn of winter, someone cuts into the rock at Holafell, and nobody mends the cut before the next one. Holafell is a bare hill in [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]] with a sanctuary cut into its stone, and it is the oldest hof of [[lore-hrimthurspr|Hrímthur]], the Rime-Giant whose faithful teach that the world was cut from a living body by a violence nobody has stopped committing. The [[affiliation-hrimthur|Faith of Hrímthur]] has few hofs and all of them are hard to reach. Holafell is the eldest, and the **Northern Hall in Targud** is the largest. The name is the description: _hola-_ is a sanctuary cut into the rock, set before the generic for a bare hill.

## The Renewal of the Wound

What matters at Holafell happens at the turn of winter. Whichever adherent the Holafell faction has put forward that year cuts into the rock, and as many of the [[affiliation-thursborn|Giant's Children]] as have made the journey stand by to see it done. The faith calls the rite **the Renewal of the Wound**, and the Giant's Children hold their senior initiations here in the same season.

The pilgrimage that leads to it is **the Walk to Holafell**. It is made on foot in winter, by the high ground and not the easier valley road, and it counts only if the pilgrim arrives in time for the cutting. An adherent who has kept Holafell for most of a long life explains the faith to newcomers in four flat sentences: "The Ten have gods who listen. I have a giant who is listened to. He does not answer. He was never built to."

## Holafell's Wound

The Giant's Children at Holafell tell the famine of the **Three Winters** differently from the halls below. In their account a predecessor failed to keep the wound open, and winter did not end until the next elder cut it again. That is their explanation of the famine and not an attested cause of it; the valley halls remember failed harvests, deaths and disputed acts without accepting the Children's claim. A visitor who repeats the Holafell telling at a valley hearth starts an argument.
