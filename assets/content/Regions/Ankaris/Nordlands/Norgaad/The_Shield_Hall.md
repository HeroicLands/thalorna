The **Shield-Hall** stands at the heart of [[place-vrathavan|Vrathavan]]. The honor-bonds between crews are sealed there, and the town's most distinguished shield-maiden holds court in it.

She is renowned for her victories in the shield-wall and has trained generations of warriors. Her word is law in the settlement.
