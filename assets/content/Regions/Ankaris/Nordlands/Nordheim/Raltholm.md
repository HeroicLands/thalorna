## Overview

Raltholm takes its name from how far a shout carries over flat water on a still morning, and the Hersvald who holds the district has made the fact into an institution: the horn that calls the levy is blown from the holm itself, and a farmstead three fjords distant that cannot see a beacon can still hear the call carried across the water on a clear day. Seven hundred people answer to that horn, worked across scattered farmsteads and the holm's own waterfront rather than packed behind any wall, because Raltholm has never needed **Knalthstead**'s ramparts to hold what it has.

The [[lore-hersvaldrnk|Hersvald]] who leads those farmsteads to the muster and speaks for them at the ting keeps his hall on the holm, and a Lawspeaker riding circuit out of Knalthstead stops here often enough that a dispute can wait for him rather than traveling to the capital. The district feeds itself and the fleet both: **Nulthynda Eldskari** works the inland fields each summer, **Kraldmýl Vetrdómr** cuts the timber a longship needs, and a standing complement of [[lore-bondirnk|bóndi]] owe the fort-watch and the archery a frontier district expects of its free men—**Ilthvir Blóthöll** among them, a yeoman archer by the duty his land carries rather than by trade. **Nilthselda Dróttgar** keeps the apothecary that a muster-point this size cannot do without, treating the Hersvald's men as readily as she treats a farmer's burned hand.

Raltholm's grievance is the ordinary one of a district asked to send men and grain both: a hard winter that costs the horn's answer costs the Hersvald his standing as surely as a lost battle would, since the men who answer the horn are the same men whose consent keeps him in his hall.
