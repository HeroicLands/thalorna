## Overview

Blód—the Place of Blood-Sacrifice—is the oldest inhabited settlement in Nordheim, a site of profound religious significance that predates the current kingdoms by centuries, perhaps millennia. The settlement is small and austere, no more than 300 souls, built around a sacred grove and standing stones of unknown origin. The very landscape seems ancient and worn, as if the gods themselves had walked the earth here and left their footprints impressed forever into the soil. The settlement's remoteness is deliberate; it lies in the interior of Nordheim, far from the coast and merchant routes, accessible only through difficult terrain that few outsiders willingly traverse.

The primary function of Blód is sacred ritual. The settlement is home to an order of priestesses—völvas and seidrs—who have maintained unbroken tradition and knowledge stretching back into the mists of time. They perform the great seasonal sacrifices, interpret omens from the flight of birds and the patterns of runes, and conduct the deepest and most powerful rituals of the Asguardian faith. The nature of their sacrifices is not spoken of outside the settlement; outsiders whisper of blood offerings, ritual combat, and ceremonies conducted under winter stars that seem to shift and move in ways natural stars should not. Whether these accounts are fact or fearful fantasy, none who visit Blód are willing to discuss what they have witnessed.

The settlement attracts pilgrims of a particular kind—those seeking communion with the divine at the deepest levels, those willing to endure hardship and reveal their innermost selves, and those whose questions demand answers that only the priestesses of Blód can provide. The settlement's influence extends far beyond its modest size; the word of Blód's priestesses carries weight throughout Nordheim and beyond, and regional chieftains will journey great distances to seek their counsel on matters of grave importance.
