## Overview

"Before anything else, learn this: you do not throw a stone at a raven in Ódholm, not even to drive it off your bread," a rune-priest of the temple tells a pilgrim who has just come up from the quay. "They are his eyes. Harm one and you answer for it with your home or your life." Ravens wheel over Ódholm in far greater numbers than the settlement's size would explain, and the people feed them with reverence and read their flight and calls as the will of [[lore-odvardty|Ódvar]], the All-Father. Harming a raven is the gravest blasphemy in the settlement, punished by exile or death.

Ódholm sits on the northern coast of [[place-nordheim|Nordheim]]. About 500 people live there, around a temple of Ódvar that local tradition says was founded in the age of heroes, when the god himself walked the earth and blessed the place. The temple is built of dark stone brought across the winter ice from quarries in the far north, and it dominates the settlement.

## The Temple

A hierarchy of priests and priestesses keeps the rites, interprets the ravens and advises the hersvaldar of the surrounding districts. The settlement is revered across the frozen north, and pilgrims come from all over Nordheim and beyond. Many seek Ódvar's favor for something dangerous: warriors before a great battle, explorers before an expedition, and people carrying a hard decision who want divine counsel before they make it.

## The Raven Guard

The temple keeps a garrison of elite warriors, the **Raven Guard**, who are sworn to the temple and to the defense of the north's holy places. That combination of mystical authority and martial strength gives Ódholm real influence for its size, and it is part of why Nordheim counts the settlement as strategic as well as holy. Trade flows through it, and merchants come as pilgrims to offer tribute and ask a blessing on their ventures.
