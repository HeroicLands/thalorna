## Overview

Ódholm sits on the northern coast of [[place-nordheim|Nordheim]], a settlement of perhaps 500 souls that has grown around an ancient temple dedicated to [[lore-odvardty|Ódvar]], the All-Father. The site is revered throughout the frozen north; local tradition holds that it was founded in the age of heroes, when Ódvar himself walked the earth and blessed the place with his presence. Ravens wheel constantly above the settlement—far more than natural population would suggest—and the locals regard them as direct extensions of Ódvar's will, his eyes watching all that transpires below. The birds are fed with reverence and never hunted; to harm a raven in Ódholm is considered the gravest of blasphemies, punishable by exile or death.

The settlement's primary function is religious. The temple, built of dark stone brought across the winter ice from quarries in the far north, dominates the settlement. A hierarchy of priests and priestesses maintain the sacred rites, interpret the flight and calls of the ravens, and serve as advisors to the regional Hersvalds. The settlement draws pilgrims from across Nordheim and beyond, particularly those seeking Ódvar's favor for dangerous ventures—warriors before great battles, explorers before expeditions, and those burdened by difficult decisions seeking divine counsel.

Beyond its religious significance, Ódholm serves a strategic role in Nordheim's defense. The settlement maintains a garrison of elite warriors—the Raven Guard—sworn to the temple and to the defense of the north's holy places. The combination of mystical authority and martial strength makes Ódholm a place of considerable influence despite its modest size. Trade flows through the settlement, and merchants make pilgrimages to offer tribute and seek blessings for their ventures.
