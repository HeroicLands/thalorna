## Overview

Vrithholm is an islet trading post of [[place-nordheim|Nordheim]], named for the iron that passes through it: _vrith-_, the temper iron takes from the fire, before the generic for an islet. Its wharves serve a foreign buyer as much as any Nordman: the agents of [[affiliation-linzwrtkhlth|Lin'Zuwaret elu Aû'Khelâthu]], the merchant consortium of [[place-aukhelathrgq|Aû'Khelâthu]], keep a house here and buy the timber of the forests behind the coast.

## The Consortium's Ambition

The consortium's agents at Vrithholm want more than a share of the trade: they want timber harvested at industrial scale, and the independent woodcutters of the hinterland stand in their way. They have tried repeatedly to buy out or absorb [[being-krldmylvtrdmr|Kraldmýl Vetrdómr]] of [[place-raltholm|Raltholm]], whose independent operation they see as an obstacle, and he has refused them every time. A woodcutter who sells to Vrithholm sells on the consortium's terms.
