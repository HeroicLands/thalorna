## Overview

Vyldvangr is an open field of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] set with ancient standing stones, and the ground is named for what it bears: _vyld-_, grain, before the generic for an open field. The faithful of the [[affiliation-frodvin|Faith of Fródvin]] hold that [[lore-frodvindty|Fródvin]] himself blessed the earth here, and the **Standing Stones of Vyldvangr** are kept by keepers who live by that belief.

## What the Stones Are For

A farmer comes to Vyldvangr when the fields have already failed. The saga of [[being-mrvrnhrvstblsd|Mirvrinna Harvest-Blessed]] tells how the rains failed after years of plenty, and how she walked to the stones, offered the last of her village's grain to the earth and prayed for Fródvin's mercy; a wind rose, the sky darkened, rain fell, and the harvest that followed was the largest anyone remembered. Her standing at the stones gave her the power to call rain once a season, and the keepers count her among their own and give her the place for her most important rites.
