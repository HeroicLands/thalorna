The Spear of Skraldthira is one of the **three regalia** required to seal away [[being-njorven|Njorven]], with
[[miscgear-crwnwyrm|The Crown of the Wyrm]] and [[miscgear-hornnjordur|The Horn of Hafvald]].

The Spear is hidden within an **ancient shrine**, guarded by a powerful **spirit guardian**, and it is
the regalia whose recovery is most explicitly _not_ a fight.

## The Trial

To gain the Spear the seeker must confront the guardian not through battle but by **journeying into
the spirit world**. There they face trials that test wisdom, wit and resolve. The spirit world
reflects the physical realm, but it also reflects the seeker's own deep fears and desires, and the
trials are drawn from those as much as from anything external.

Solving the trials unlocks the Spear. **Brute strength leads to failure**—not to a harder fight, but
to failure, and a seeker who tries force does not get a second attempt at the same trial.

This makes the Spear the natural first regalia in play, because it establishes the campaign's thesis
before the party has invested in the wrong approach: Njorven is not a problem that can be hit.

## Skraldthira

An ancestral figure of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands Region]] whose name survives on the spear and very little else. The
sagas that mention her do not agree on when she lived or what she did with it, and the shrine is not
hers—it merely holds the weapon.
