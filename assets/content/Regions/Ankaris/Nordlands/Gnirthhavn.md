## Overview

Gnirthhavn is a city on a haven of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]], and its name fits the way its people do business: _gnirth-_, the set of a jaw that will not open, before the generic for a haven. It is rich enough to support a class of influential figures who trade in secrets as readily as in goods, and organized enough to support a criminal network that runs heists and cons the way other cities run guilds.

## Who Holds Gnirthhavn

Three powers pull at the city, and a newcomer should know all three before choosing a tavern.

- **The Gilded Cage** is the most exclusive gathering place in the city, a luxurious salon where Gnirthhavn's most influential figures come for both pleasure and guidance, and an invitation to it is worth more than gold. It began as a brothel; [[being-knrvthyrgldnhr|Knirvthýra Goldenhair]] was sold into it, rose within it, took control of it, and made it what it is. Her network of informants and clients reaches the highest levels of Gnirthhavn society.
- **The Underworld Guild of Gnirthhavn** is the city's organized crime network. It hires [[being-dvrnmyldcvr|Dvirnmýl the Deceiver]] for its high-profile heists and cons, paying him a share of the profits and protection from the law.
- **The Morality Council of Gnirthhavn** is a group of religious conservatives who regard the Gilded Cage as a den of sin and Knirvthýra's influence as a corruption of the city's values. They want the salon shut and its mistress exiled.
