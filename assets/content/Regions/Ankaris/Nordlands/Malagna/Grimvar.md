Ask a Grímvar who owes whom in [[place-malagna|Malagna]] and the answer starts three generations back, in a shipyard at [[place-gnarthborg|Gnarthborg]], with a boy killed in a quarrel. The Grímvar are one of the two great clans of the kingdom, under the crown at Gnarthborg like every other, and they remember that killing as a debt that wergild could not close.

## What the Grímvar Say

A [[affiliation-hrafnvar|Hrafnvar]] shipwright killed the boy. The Hrafnvar offered wergild, and the Grímvar refused it and took a life; the Hrafnvar answered with two. A priest of [[affiliation-frodvin|Fródvin]] walked between [[place-braldheim|Braldheim]] and [[place-malstead|Málstead]] to seek peace, and both jarls marked a split oath-stave. Then the Grímvar jarl's brother burned his clan's half before the priest. The Grímvar say that a burned half leaves no binding bargain, and that the dead are counted differently in their halls than in the Hrafnvar's.

## The Crown in the Howe

The Grímvar keep [[miscgear-crwnwyrm|the Crown of the Wyrm]] in a howe at Braldheim. Their tellers say a _landormr_ lies beneath it and warms the chamber, and that one man who went down thought the Crown looked back at him. They read the guardian as a sign that the Crown is more than a king's ornament. The Hrafnvar read the same story the other way and say fear, not piety, keeps the Grímvar from wearing it.
