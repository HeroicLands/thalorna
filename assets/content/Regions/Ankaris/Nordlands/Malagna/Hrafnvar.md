The Hrafnvar keep their half of a split oath-stave, and they say the stave still binds. They are one of the two great clans of [[place-malagna|Malagna]], under the crown at [[place-gnarthborg|Gnarthborg]], and the half they keep is the reason they say the quarrel with the [[affiliation-grimvar|Grímvar]] is settled.

## What the Hrafnvar Say

Three generations ago a Hrafnvar shipwright killed a Grímvar boy in a shipyard quarrel at Gnarthborg. The Hrafnvar offered wergild. The Grímvar refused it and took a life, and the Hrafnvar answered with two. A priest of [[affiliation-frodvin|Fródvin]] walked between [[place-braldheim|Braldheim]] and [[place-malstead|Málstead]] to seek peace, and both jarls marked the stave, one half for each clan. The Grímvar jarl's brother then burned his clan's half before the priest. The Hrafnvar answer that a half survives, and that a settlement a priest witnessed does not fail because one party burned its token.

## The Crown They Do Not Fear

The Grímvar keep [[miscgear-crwnwyrm|the Crown of the Wyrm]] in a howe at Braldheim, and Grímvar tellers say a _landormr_ lies beneath it. Hrafnvar tellers say it is fear, not piety, that keeps their rivals from wearing the Crown.
