## Overview

Braldheim's name is the fire a household banks at night rather than lets die, and the three hundred and fifty people who live scattered through its homesteads are known even among Malagnans for keeping that custom harder than most: a cold hearth here is read as a sign the house inside it has failed, and a neighbor who finds one is obliged to ask why before asking anything else.

No Hersvald or Landvördr keeps a permanent seat at Braldheim; the district's officers ride a circuit out of **Gnarthborg** and stop twice a season, which suits a settlement built on homesteads rather than a market square. **Glirvrinna Stormrót** sews the tents and hide-coverings that keep a charcoal-burner's camp dry through the autumn cutting, and most of Braldheim's working year runs on the same charcoal that feeds Gnarthborg's forges—wood cut, banked and burned down slow through the settlement's own long nights before the carts take it to the shipyards.

The one quarrel Braldheim takes to the ting more than any other settlement its size is a boundary dispute over cutting rights in the forest behind it, since a charcoal camp that burns another household's stand of trees has taken something that does not grow back before the next winter needs it.
