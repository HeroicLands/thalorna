## Overview

Ask the boatman who rows you across to Glarvey what the island is known for, and he points at the roofs. Glarvey is an island holding in [[place-malagna|Malagna]], the seat of a thegn, the regional lord who holds it and the steadings around it. The present **Thegn of Glarvey**, **Lord Erikson**, is a careful steward of what he can afford, and his roofs are his pride.

## The Thegn's Holdings

For the past decade every roof on Lord Erikson's holdings has been thatched by one hand: [[being-hrndrndrknt|Hrandrinna Drekanótt]], a thatcher of [[place-hvalgvik|Hvalgvík]], whom he values for the quality of her work and for her willingness to work within what he can pay. She crosses to the island each season, and the thegn counts her among his most reliable people. A roof that holds through a Malagna winter is worth more on an island than anywhere ashore, because nothing on Glarvey comes from the mainland quickly.

The island's name describes the water around it on a still day: _glarv-_, light lying flat on water, set before the generic for an island.
