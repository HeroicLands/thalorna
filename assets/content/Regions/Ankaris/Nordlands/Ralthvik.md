"You sail against raiders, you sail on the widow's keel," says a hand on one of the inlet's boats, shouldering a coil of line along the strand at Ralthvík. "Ask anyone here whose keel that was." The fishing village sits on an inlet of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] coast, and its boats go out in every season the sea allows. Its people live by what they bring back.

## The Raid That Failed

The village remembers, in the songs of the [[doc-heroessgrd|Heroes of Asguard]], the day in [The Age of Halls]{.name data-type="lore/calendar"} when sea raiders came for it and did not take it, because [[being-sklfrvthndrstrk|Skalforv Thunderstrike]] stood between them and the strand. In the song the village paid that debt through **Widow [Hvarnselda]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Ralthvík**, its wealthiest fisherwoman, who supplied Skalforv with provisions, a ship and a crew whenever he asked. Ralthvík pays it still: the best-kept boat on the inlet is called the widow's keel, and the village fits it out with a crew and gear for anyone who comes to the strand with a just quarrel against sea raiders. The name is a fisherman's measure: _ralth-_ is how far a voice carries over water, and the generic that follows is the one for an inlet.
