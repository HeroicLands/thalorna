## Overview

Ralthvík is a fishing village on an inlet of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] coast, and its name is a fisherman's measure: _ralth-_, how far a voice carries over water, before the generic for an inlet. Its boats go out in every season the sea allows, and its people live by what they bring back.

## The Raid That Failed

The village remembers the day sea raiders came for it and did not take it, because [[being-sklfrvthndrstrk|Skalforv Thunderstrike]] stood between them and the strand. Ralthvík has paid that debt ever since through **Widow Hvarnselda of Ralthvík**, its wealthiest fisherwoman, who supplies Skalforv with provisions, a ship and a crew whenever he asks. Her boats are the best-kept on the inlet, and a crewman who sails with Skalforv goes with the village's blessing and its gear.
