"You sail for Skalforv, you sail on Widow Hvarnselda's keel," says a hand on one of the inlet's boats, shouldering a coil of line along the strand at Ralthvík. "And she will tell you so herself." The fishing village sits on an inlet of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] coast, and its boats go out in every season the sea allows. Its people live by what they bring back.

## The Raid That Failed

The village remembers the day sea raiders came for it and did not take it, because [[being-sklfrvthndrstrk|Skalforv Thunderstrike]] stood between them and the strand. Ralthvík has paid that debt ever since through **Widow Hvarnselda of Ralthvík**, its wealthiest fisherwoman, who supplies Skalforv with provisions, a ship and a crew whenever he asks. Her boats are the best-kept on the inlet, and a crewman who sails with Skalforv goes with the village's blessing and its gear. The name is a fisherman's measure: _ralth-_ is how far a voice carries over water, and the generic that follows is the one for an inlet.
