# Dossier {#dossier}

Jarl Braldorv Embersteel is a warlike jarl of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands Region]] who gathers fighters and craftsmen round his household. He keeps [[being-vrthrvflmhrt|Vrithorv Flameheart]] close as both champion and bodyguard, knowing that the warrior's reputation alone deters many would-be challengers. He values [[being-vrthvrirnfst|Vruthvir Ironfist]] for his smithing, and the jarl's household guard bears Vruthvir's arms and armor.

He distrusts [[being-mrvsldenchntrs|Murvselda the Enchantress]] and her influence over the king, and sees her as a manipulative sorceress rather than a wise counselor.
