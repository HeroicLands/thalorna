A farmer comes to Vuldvangur when the fields have already failed. The ground is an open field of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] set with ancient standing stones, and the faithful of the [[affiliation-frodvin|Faith of Frodvin]] hold that [[lore-frodvindty|Frodvin]] himself blessed the earth here. The **Standing Stones of Vuldvangur** are kept by keepers who live by that belief.

## What the Stones Are For

The saga of [[being-mrvrnhrvstblsd|Mirvrinna Harvest-Blessed]] tells why people come. The rains failed after years of plenty, and she walked to the stones, offered the last of her village's grain to the earth and prayed for Frodvin's mercy. A wind rose, the sky darkened, rain fell, and the harvest that followed was the largest anyone remembered. In the song her standing at the stones gave her the power to call rain once a season. The keepers count her among their own, though the saga sets her in [The Age of Halls]{.name data-type="lore/calendar"}, and they keep the place where she prayed for the rites of a dry year. The ground is named for what it bears: _vuld-_ is grain, and the generic that follows is the one for an open field.
