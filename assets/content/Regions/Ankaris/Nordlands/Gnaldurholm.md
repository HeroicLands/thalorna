The ferry to Gnaldurholm is paid before the crossing, and a purse stays out of sight until the quay: the islet is rich, and it knows how to keep what it earns. The port city stands on an islet of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] coast, rich enough to support a class of influential figures who trade in secrets as readily as in goods, and organized enough to support a criminal network that runs heists and cons the way other cities run guilds.

## Who Holds Gnaldurholm

Three powers pull at the city, and a newcomer should know all three before choosing a tavern.

- [The Gilded Cage]{.name data-type="place/structure"} is the most exclusive gathering place in the city, a luxurious salon where Gnaldurholm's most influential figures come for both pleasure and guidance, and an invitation to it is worth more than gold. It began as a brothel. [[being-knrvthyrgldnhr|Knirvthira Goldenhair]] was sold into it, rose within it, took control of it, and made it what it is, and her network of informants and clients reaches the highest levels of Gnaldurholm society.
- [The Underworld Guild of Gnaldurholm]{.name data-type="affiliation"} is the city's organized crime network. It hires [[being-dvrnmyldcvr|Dvirnmil the Deceiver]] for its high-profile heists and cons, paying him a share of the profits and protection from the law.
- [The Morality Council of Gnaldurholm]{.name data-type="affiliation"} is a group of religious conservatives who regard the Gilded Cage as a den of sin and Knirvthira's influence as a corruption of the city's values. They want the salon shut and its mistress exiled.

## The Name

The name fits the way the city does business: _gnaldur-_ is floes grinding on one another, and the generic that follows is the one for an islet.
