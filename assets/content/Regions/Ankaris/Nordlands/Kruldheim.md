## Overview

A household of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] that wants a thrall buys one at Kruldheim or along the [[place-nalthmark|Nalthmark]] road, and the settlement's name is blunt about the trade: _kruld-_, a load a beast carries without complaint, before _-heim_, a settled place. A buyer comes away from Kruldheim with a thrall, and a person sold there leaves it as one.

## What Changes Hands

A [[lore-thrallrnk|thrall]] is a man owned outright, bought or taken rather than born free. From the day of the sale he owes his owner labor itself, not a share of what his labor produces. He bears no weapon by right and speaks at no ting, and no kin stand bound to answer for harm done to him the way a free man's kin answer for harm done to a free man. Whatever shelter, food or protection he has afterward comes from the standing of the household that bought him.

The sale holds wherever the buyer takes him. The five kingdoms hold the standing under one Nordmal law, and the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] holds it under the same name, so a thrall bought at Kruldheim is a thrall in every hall from the Nalthmark to the northern coast.
