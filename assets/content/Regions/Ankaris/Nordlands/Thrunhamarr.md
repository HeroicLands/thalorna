"When the thunder comes up the seamount," a Storm-Speaker on [[place-thrumufjall|Thrumufjall]] says, "the hof says you are hearing the hammer. I read the storm; I do not argue with the hof." **Thrúnhamarr** is the hammer of [[lore-thrunvalddty|Thrúnvald]], depicted as powerful enough to crush mountains and summon thunder and lightning. The [[affiliation-thrunvald|faith]] treats it as the god's weapon against the forces pressing on the ordered world and as a tool of blessing and protection, so that battle and the sanctifying of ships meet in one symbol. The hammer stone stands for that strength and that protection.

In the saga of [[being-skrldmylstrmbrn|Skrildmýl Stormborn]], Thrumufjall is the mountain where the god is said to have struck the earth with the hammer. In the standard telling of [[lore-aldarlok|Aldarlok]] it lies where Thrúnvald drops it, and **Hamarberi**, his daughter and one of the [[lore-tvelgaett|Tvelgaett]], lifts it out of the fire.

## The Hammer Held

A coastal telling joins the hammer's battle and blessing in one refrain, carrying its praise from the harbor to the god's final encounter.

: The Hammer Held

```poetry {form=epic lang=en}
Hark to the hammer's head;
hear how the heavens answer.
Clouds close over the coast;
cold lies the waiting water.
White hangs the wheeling gull;
waves hold their breath below.
Stone stirs beneath the storm;
strength wakes in the sky.
Thrúnhamarr, thunder above us;
the threatened shore awaits you.

Broad stands the bearded god;
behind him the hearths burn.
Dark presses against the door;
deep gathers the hunger beyond.
Walls hold the wind outside;
within them the children sleep.
Smoke climbs from small suppers;
shelter smells of bread baking.
High is the heavy hammer lifted;
hard is the hand defending.

Mountains meet that mighty stroke;
marrow of stone is scattered.
Lightning leaps from lowering clouds;
long roads of fire open.
Crags crack beneath the blow;
cliffs cast their crowns downward.
Giants gathered before the banishment;
great strength stood against them.
Thrúnhamarr, thunder before us;
the threatened world awaits you.

Roofs ring beneath the rain;
rafters remember the loud heavens.
Shields shine along the wall;
shadows shake upon their faces.
Young ears yearn for thunder;
young mouths learn the god's battle.
Old mouths offer the song;
oak beams bear its burden.
Hear the hammer held high;
hear whom its shadow shelters.

Lower the loud hall singing;
let the shore wind enter.
Timber tastes of new shaping;
tar smells along the seams.
A ship shoulders the shallows;
sea waits beyond the harbor.
Ropes rasp beneath rough palms;
rowers ready their hard hands.
Thrúnhamarr, thunder behind us;
the traveling ship awaits you.

Mountain-breaker, mighty in battle,
meet now the prow's blessing.
The godi gives his words;
godward rises the crew's asking.
Bent backs bear the leaving;
below them the tide gathers.
Bright is the boundary crossed;
black is the water beyond.
Hold the hammer over them;
hold all their names together.

The shell shapes an ear;
sea speech sleeps within it.
The stone stands for strength;
still rests the hammer's likeness.
Salt takes the sailor's tongue;
storm takes the lifted voices.
Small stand the faithful here;
sparks beside the god's iron.
Shore and ship share asking;
strength be given their keeping.

Out moves the oaken boat;
oars bite the widening water.
Wood wears the working palms;
wake closes behind the vessel.
Dark grows the dwindling shore;
distance draws out the farewell.
Mother lifts her hand once more;
mist takes the moving fingers.
Home hangs in every name;
hearts carry their hidden freight.

Warriors wait on the whale-road;
weapons sleep beneath their shields.
Reavers raise the god's name;
rough seas swallow their boasting.
Bright iron bears salt stains;
battle pride tastes the brine.
Strong arms serve at rowing;
strength spends itself before fighting.
Hold the hammer over them;
hold the hands that tire.

Heavy lies the trial's iron hammer;
hard is the road beneath.
Shoulders shake beneath its burden;
steps shorten against the hill.
Rain runs through the watcher;
roofless stands the night's guardian.
Cold counts the breathing hours;
courage keeps the feet planted.
Hold the hammer held high;
hold what the darkness threatens.

Thrumufjall thrusts above the tide;
thunder strikes its stone crown.
There the Thunderer struck earth;
there the saga sets him.
Winter whitened Skrildmýl's weary people;
winds drove them toward climbing.
Mountainward moved the faithful feet;
men steadied their stumbling neighbors.
Thrúnhamarr, thunder above them;
the threatened village awaits you.

Feet find the frozen stone;
fingers fasten upon cold sleeves.
White weather worries their breathing;
wind wrestles with every mouth.
Words want the warmth below;
weary throats labor toward prayer.
High hangs the hammer's shadow;
hard lies the climb beneath.
Step shall follow sore step;
strength shall reach hands backward.

The summit stands under heaven;
storm shoulders its bare height.
A god's great grip shatters mountains;
gathered hands can steady people.
Strong is the stone-breaker's striking;
small is the breath of asking.
Breath bears the village upward;
bodies bear each other onward.
Hold the hammer over them;
hold the hands that tremble.

Far burns the final fire;
fate fills the dark horizon.
Aldarlok enters the hall singing;
all cups halt in passing.
The wyrm waits on Vígvöll;
wide lies its dreadful shadow.
Heimsormr heaves before the god;
high lifts the hammer again.
Knowingly the knuckles close;
knowledge bears the weight within.

Down falls the deadly stroke;
down falls the World-Wyrm's body.
Venom vies with victorious strength;
victory carries its bitter cost.
Nine steps the god walks;
night gathers upon his breathing.
The tenth lies beyond telling;
the Thunderer's feet go still.
Hold the hammer held high;
hold the price within praise.

Low lies the living fire;
logs loosen into red embers.
The cup crosses warm hands;
careful fingers pass it onward.
Outside the ocean strikes stone;
our old coast keeps listening.
Inside the iron grows dim;
images fade upon the shields.
Death draws close in singing;
dear grow the gathered faces.

Dawn shall demand another blessing;
deep water waits another ship.
Rain shall reach broken roofing;
ready hands must mend it.
Guards shall grow weary standing;
greater darkness lengthens their vigil.
Bread shall break among neighbors;
burdens pass between their shoulders.
Hold the hammer over them;
hold all their names together.

Look long upon the water;
let the lookout keep watching.
Rope shall answer ready hands;
rowers shall husband their strength.
The blessing bears their asking;
breath must bear the labor.
Strong shoulders share the weight;
small hands find other hands.
Thrúnhamarr, thunder behind us;
the traveling ship awaits you.

Sea-shell, sing the shore's need;
stone, stand for the shelter.
Mountain and mast remember strength;
men remember the last steps.
The living world waits between blows;
within it the small hearths burn.
Hands hold a mortal shelter;
heaven holds the hammer's thunder.
Fragile is the freight defended;
faithful be those who carry.

Lift for the lives threatened;
let the great arm answer.
Strike where the dark presses;
strength stand beside the weary.
Bless the boat going outward;
bear the words past hearing.
Thrúnhamarr, thunder before us;
the threatened world awaits you.
Hold the hammer held high;
hold all our names together.
```
