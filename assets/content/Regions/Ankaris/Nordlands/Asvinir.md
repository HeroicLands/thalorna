Ásvinir is the archaic liturgical name for the defending kin of the gods. Their kinship rests on common cause against [[lore-nahilddty|Náhild]], [[lore-svartbrandrdty|Svartbrandr]] and the corruption they represent, despite the quarrels and failings of the gods themselves. It names the defending kin within the [[affiliation-asguardian|Asguardian tradition]] rather than every member of the Ten: Náhild and Svartbrandr belong to the broader pantheon.

In the coastal tellings, the Ásvinir largely displaced the older tradition of Hafvald, whose name survives in place-names, coastal observances and the [[miscgear-hornnjordur|Horn of Hafvald]]; the sagas leave whether that elder sea-power was a god or a spirit unresolved.
