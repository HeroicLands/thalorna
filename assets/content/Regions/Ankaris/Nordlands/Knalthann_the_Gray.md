# Dossier {#dossier}

A mighty warrior of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]], now retired from active combat but still influential among the northern clans. He is the father of [[being-hvlgthyrknlthnsdtr|Hvilgthýra Knalthannsdóttir]], who strives to surpass his legacy through the runes, an understanding he never achieved.

He gives her counsel and connections. Their relationship is complicated by his disappointment that she chose the path of the runes over pure warriorship.

He is not [[being-knlthnlwgvr|Knalthann Lawgiver]], the judge of the trial at [[place-logthul|Lögthul]]; the byname tells them apart.
