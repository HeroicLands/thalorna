Hugvin is the raven of thought in the [[affiliation-odvar|Faith of Ódvar]], paired with [[lore-munvin|Munvin]], the raven of memory. The faith depicts the two flying across the world each day to gather knowledge and news for [[lore-odvardty|Ódvar]], and reads the raven feather as their shared sign. Inside Ódvar’s rune-hall, some hold them literal, thought and memory made bird-shaped, departing at dawn and returning in the evening; ordained acolytes are taught instead that the birds are a teaching figure for the unordained, and that waiting on their reports means neglecting the work of the runes. [[being-hnirvmylwise|Hnirvmýl the Wise]] claims to hear their whispers in the wind guiding him toward hidden knowledge and lost lore.
