Heimsask is the world-ash of Asguardian cosmology, rooted in [[place-asguard|Asguard]] and crowned over [[place-worldthlrn|Mannguard]], holding the gods’ world and the living world in one growth. The tradition ties their fate to the tree: while it stands, both worlds stand, and when it burns, both burn with it. The skalds name [[lore-aldarlok|Aldarlok]] the Fall of the Ash because its falling closes the age. Details beyond that shared account, including the number of its roots, remain matters on which rune-priests and skalds disagree.
