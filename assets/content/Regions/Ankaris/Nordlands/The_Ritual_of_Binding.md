The **Ritual of Binding** is the rite by which the Sea Wraith [[being-njorven|Njörven]] is sealed
away. It is the object of [[scenario-groascmpgn|Gróa's Campaign]], and the campaign's final act
consists of getting it performed.

## Requirements

- **All three regalia.** The [[miscgear-sprsigrid|Spear of Sigrid]], the
  [[miscgear-crwnwyrm|Crown of the Wyrm]] and the [[miscgear-hornnjordur|Horn of Njörvar]]. Two will
  not do.
- **A specific place.** The rite must be worked at a **hidden coastal temple**, and nowhere else. Its
  location is part of the quest.
- **Preparation, both spiritual and material.** Neither half can be skipped, and the spiritual
  preparation falls on the invoker personally.
- **Protection.** The invoker cannot defend themselves while working it. Their companions must hold
  the ground for the duration—against Njörven's minions, and equally against rival factions and
  foreign invaders who have their own reasons to want the rite to fail.

## The Shape of It

The Ritual is long, and that length is the point of the encounter it produces: the climax of the
campaign is not a duel but a defense, in which the party protects someone who is deliberately
helpless while everything that has been building arrives at once.

It is also not a purely magical solution. Sealing Njörven ultimately turns on knowledge, strategy and
diplomacy as much as on the rite—on having brought [[place-malagna|Malagna]]'s fractured clans to the point where the
temple can be held at all. A party that has the regalia and no allies will not finish it.

## Binding, Not Destroying

The Ritual seals. It does not kill, and it has evidently been worked before, since Njörven was in a
watery prison to begin with and is stirring out of it now. Whatever is achieved at the coastal temple
is a reprieve of the same kind, and everyone involved knows it.
