The rite that binds the Sea Wraith is not for the strong; it is for the patient, and for the ones who stand around the patient. The **Ritual of Binding** is the rite by which [[being-njorven|Njörven]] is sealed away. It is the object of [[scenario-groascmpgn|Gróa's Campaign]], and the campaign's final act consists of getting it performed.

## What the Rite Needs

The völva counts four things on her fingers, and the sagas agree with her.

- _All three regalia._ The [[miscgear-sprsigrid|Spear of Sigrid]], the [[miscgear-crwnwyrm|Crown of the Wyrm]] and the [[miscgear-hornnjordur|Horn of Hafvald]]. Two will not do.
- _A specific place._ The rite must be worked at a hidden coastal temple, and nowhere else. Its location is part of the quest.
- _Preparation, both spiritual and material._ Neither half can be skipped, and the spiritual preparation falls on the invoker personally.
- _Protection._ The invoker cannot defend themselves while working it. Their companions must hold the ground for the duration, against Njörven's minions and equally against rival factions and foreign invaders who have their own reasons to want the rite to fail.

## The Shape of It

The Ritual is long, and that length is the point of the encounter it produces: the climax of the campaign is a defense in which the party protects someone who is deliberately helpless while everything that has been building arrives at once.

Sealing Njörven turns on knowledge, strategy and diplomacy as much as on the rite, and on having brought [[place-malagna|Malagna]]'s fractured clans to the point where the temple can be held at all. A party that has the regalia and no allies will not finish it.

## Binding, Not Destroying

The Ritual seals; it does not kill. It has evidently been worked before, since Njörven was in a watery prison to begin with and is stirring out of it now, and whatever is achieved at the coastal temple is a reprieve of the same kind. The völva says it last, and plainly: everyone involved knows it.
