Once a year, at the turn of winter, an adherent of the [[affiliation-hrimthur|Faith of Hrimthur]] cuts into the rock of [[place-holafell|Holafell]], and nobody mends the cut before the next one. The **Renewal of the Wound** is the faith's chief rite, and it is performed upon the god rather than offered to him: [[lore-hrimthurspr|Hrimthur]] grants nothing and answers nothing, and the cut is the memory of what was done to him.

## The Cutting

Whichever adherent the Holafell faction has put forward that year makes the cut. As many of the [[affiliation-thursborn|Giant's Children]] as have made the journey stand by to see it done, and the order holds its senior initiations at Holafell in the same season. What is cut stays cut through the year; the wound is kept open on purpose, because the faith's whole teaching is that the world was made by a violence done to a living giant and held together by that violence being renewed.

## The Walk

The pilgrimage to the cutting is the Walk to Holafell. It is made on foot in winter, by the high ground and not the easier valley road, and it counts only if the pilgrim arrives in time for the cutting. A pilgrim who reaches Holafell after the rock has been cut has walked, and has not made the pilgrimage.

## How the Halls Read It

The hovs of the [[affiliation-asguardian|Ten]] treat the Renewal as an accurate rite and refuse Holafell's rejection of the word _blet_; [[lore-thursblot|The Thursblet]] sets out the dispute. The Giant's Children stand closest to Holafell's reading. Their doctrine holds that the world's continued existence depends on the founding violence being renewed, and they fight in that conviction.

Holafell also ties the rite to the famine of the [[lore-threewinters|Three Winters]]. In the account kept there, a predecessor failed to keep the wound open, and winter did not end until the next elder cut it again. That is Holafell's explanation of the famine and not an attested cause of it; the valley halls remember failed harvests, deaths and disputed acts without accepting the claim.
