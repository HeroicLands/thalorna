At Rilthheim a saga is heard before it is read, and it is read only by those the Hall has taken in; a traveler who asks for one is seated on the long bench to listen. The settlement is known across the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] for one building and the people who gather in it: **the Skalds' Hall of Rilthheim**, a prestigious gathering of northern skalds and loremasters.

## The Hall and Its Archives

The Hall keeps archives of ancient sagas, and its members guard both the archives and the right to speak in the Hall's name. A skald the Hall counts among its finest may speak with its authority and read in its archives. [[being-rlthrvrnskld|Rilthorv Runaskald]] holds both rights, and the Hall considers him one of the best it has.

A traveler who comes asking for a saga is welcome to hear it recited in the Hall. Reading it from the archive is a privilege the Hall grants to its own. _Rilth-_ is how far a voice carries over water, and the settlement that grew up around the Hall is a place where voices are the trade.
