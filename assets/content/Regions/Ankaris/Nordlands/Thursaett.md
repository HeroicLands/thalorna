"They lost," a rune-priest at [[place-odholm|Ódholm]] tells a newcomer who asks about the giant-kin, "and that is the one fact about them every hall agrees on." The **Thursaett** are the giant-kin the [[affiliation-asguardian|Ten]] fought before the world stood. In the Asguardian account the gods broke them and drove the survivors out past the edge of anything made, into the void, and into [[place-thursguard|Thursguard]], a plane of its own walled in that void. The tradition says nothing of their life inside it.

One of the kin was killed instead of banished. [[lore-hrimthurspr|Hrímthur]] died on the field, and the Ten cut [[place-worldthlrn|Mannguard]] out of his body. Every hall that teaches the war teaches that much.

Where the kin came from is another matter, and [[lore-aldarhringr|Aldarhringr]] gives both accounts. Ódholm's rune-hall holds that they are the part of the old age's remnant that would not take a new shape. The adherents of the [[affiliation-hrimthur|Faith of Hrímthur]] hold that they are the old age's own folk, older than the Ten, and that the gods are the newcomers. Both are taught openly.
