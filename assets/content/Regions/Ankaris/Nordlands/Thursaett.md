Thursaett names the giant-kin the [[affiliation-asguardian|Ten]] fought before the world stood. In the Asguardian account, the gods defeated them and drove the surviving kin into [[place-thursguard|Thursguard]], a plane of their own. [[lore-hrimthurspr|Hrímthur]] alone was killed rather than banished, and the Ten cut [[place-worldthlrn|Mannguard]] from his body. The tradition gives the giant-kin their place on the defeated side of that founding war without describing their life inside the plane that confines them.
