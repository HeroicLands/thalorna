## Overview

Svalthvík is a fishing village on an inlet of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] coast, and its name is what the wind brings in: _svalth-_, the cold that comes off open water, before the generic for an inlet. Its boats go out in every season the sea allows, and its people live by what they bring back.

## The Raid That Failed

The village remembers the day sea raiders came for it and did not take it, because [[being-sklfrvthndrstrk|Skalforv Thunderstrike]] stood between them and the strand. Svalthvík has paid that debt ever since through **Widow Astridr of Svalthvík**, its wealthiest fisherwoman, who supplies Skalforv with provisions, a ship and a crew whenever he asks. Her boats are the best-kept on the inlet, and a crewman who sails with Skalforv goes with the village's blessing and its gear.
