Among the [[lore-nordheimnclt|Nordmen]] of [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]], the **Vuldgeldra** is the praise-poem the Hirvskáld [Hliskvir]{.name data-type="being/npc"} made for **Jarl [Krildorv Kraldaruk]{.name data-type="being/npc"}**, who holds one of the five [[affiliation-kingdmnrgd|Norgaad provinces]] whose jarls sit at [[place-domsey|Domsey]], for a winter in which the jarl's fjords went hungry and nothing happened that a _geldra_ ordinarily sings. It was first given at the close of [[lore-sumarmal|Somarmál]], after the kings came home from Domsey and its skalds had begun reciting, and [[affiliation-skaldscrcl|the Skalds' Circle]] has carried it as standing repertoire since the [[lore-skaldating|Skaldating]] after: it is the piece an apprentice's own _geldra_ is measured against, and the Circle teaches it as the model of the truth of the recital. The text below is the repertoire text as one Stóraldstjori teaches it to apprentices at the Skaldating, with his gloss of the kennings between the parts; the gloss is his, and other masters read one or two of the _hulmur_ otherwise.

```verse {form=prose lang=en numstep=5 cols=2}
1 [setva. vri metra geldra na thuv miv hern. darni thuv hlun it staf eiv nel lest. hveri lanma hav seks atkvad. tvei lanma hav eiv kindhlun. hluna samna inn lanma nel at lorka. gandma gang tvar andra gandma. hollva tharv ji firn hugskel. geldra hav skuldnod na hringva. hringva skurvi kopra dingi. hrug nel kan felga it.]{lang=nord enc=nordthuravarkenc}
2 [Sit. We measure your praise-poem against this, so you hear it first, not last. Six syllables in every line. Two lines share their opening sounds. A chime within the line, none at its end. Clauses cross other clauses. The hall must work for understanding. A praise-poem is paid with a ring. A ring ought to buy something a thrall cannot follow.]{lang=en}
2 [setva. vri metra geldra na thuv miv hern. darni thuv hlun it staf eiv nel lest. hveri lanma hav seks atkvad. tvei lanma hav eiv kindhlun. hluna samna inn lanma nel at lorka. gandma gang tvar andra gandma. hollva tharv ji firn hugskel. geldra hav skuldnod na hringva. hringva skurvi kopra dingi. hrug nel kan felga it.]{lang=nord}

1 [ir nel skelma nafma firn thuv. krildorv es nafma. kraldaruk es aettma. vri skelma hvilma tiv skunlos na spurnma. vri nel vendri it tiv andra gandma. ir skelma ul andra dingi inn it. utva eiv dirmlan.]{lang=nord enc=nordthuravarkenc}
2 [I will not explain names for you. Krildorv is a name. [Kraldaruk]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} is a clan. We explain a stem to a stranger who asks. We do not turn it into another word. I will explain everything else within it, except one stanza.]{lang=en}
2 [ir nel skelma nafma firn thuv. krildorv es nafma. kraldaruk es aettma. vri skelma hvilma tiv skunlos na spurnma. vri nel vendri it tiv andra gandma. ir skelma ul andra dingi inn it. utva eiv dirmlan.]{lang=nord}
```
