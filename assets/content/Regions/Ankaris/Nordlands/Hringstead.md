## Overview

Hringstead sits on the border between [[place-nordheim|Nordheim]] and [[place-vithgard|Vithgard]] and belongs to neither. All five kingdoms of the north guarantee it as neutral ground, and the first thing anyone tells a stranger at the gate is the rule that makes the guarantee real: on Compact-Hall ground every signed warrior is under truce, and to kill a man at Hringstead, even your worst enemy, is an act for which there is no expiation. Men who have hunted each other across three kingdoms drink at the same board here and keep their hands on the table.

## The Compact-Hall

The [[affiliation-malldbndlg|Málalidabandalag]], the compact of the north's free companies, keeps its seat in the Compact-Hall. A captain swears his company into the Compact before the **Council of the Hall**, nine seats that meet four times a year at the Compact's gathering-feasts; contracts are annulled here by both parties, and a company that means to take a contract against another signed company must first declare it at the Hall. The Hall's own garrison is the **Hringstead Long-Ship Fellowship**, the largest signed company, nearly four hundred warriors and twelve ships under **Captain Brynhildr Eldskari**. The [[affiliation-skaldscrcl|Skalds' Circle]] counts Hringstead as its birthplace too: tradition holds that the Circle was sworn at a great gathering here five generations before any present Nordland kingdom was founded, and skalds still carry word between the Hall and the scattered companies.

## The Ride over Hringstead

People at Hringstead tell of a high ride on the ninth night of [[lore-jol|Jól]] that once came down after a killing under the Truce and carried the killer off. The Truce's living keepers need no supernatural verdict to forbid a murder, and whether the ride passed overhead, or answered that particular breach, is a matter of belief. The tale is told at every Jól all the same, and nobody at the Hall sleeps easily on the ninth night.
