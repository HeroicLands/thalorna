## Overview

The Alz River reaches the sea at [[place-azhun2|Azhûn]], and the natural harbor at its mouth is the reason the largest of the Haradian city-states stands where it does. The river is the conduit between the [[place-dunharargn|Dunhari]] desert and the western sea routes: goods come down the Alz to the white-walled warehouses on the harbor, and go out from there on every tide.

## The Alz Valley

Upriver the valley climbs into hill woods, and the trees of the Alz woods are what Azhûn's shipwrights build with. Timberwright families have cut there for generations; [[being-nulazizrsh|Nûlazî Zarrâshî]], born to such a family up the Alz valley, now chooses the standing trees that become Azhûn's ships and furniture, reading the lean of a trunk and the run of its grain before anyone lifts an axe. A keel that sails out of Azhûn usually grew on the Alz.
