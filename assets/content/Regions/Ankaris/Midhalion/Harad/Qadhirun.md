Above the port of Qadhirun the hillside is cut back in quarries, and below them is an arena whose fighters belong to a gladiators' guild. Qadhirun is a port town of about 30,000 in the [[place-haradregin|Harad Region]], the coastal and island country of city-states within [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]], and it is among the most populous towns of the region.

The town keeps guilds of its own, and its guild families buy the work of its furnace-houses: [[being-tirushnmrz|Tirrûsh Namrûzî]] makes the glass birds, ships and figured lamps that stand in their halls. The arena has its own history with the guild government. In the first year of the Confederation a magistrate of the new guild government sentenced the quarry laborer [[being-girdanrshm|Girdanû Rashîmû]] to the arena for a theft he did not commit, and five years of fights there earned him his freedom.
