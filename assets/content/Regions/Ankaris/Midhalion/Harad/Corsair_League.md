"Under the League's flag you can carry a sword on any quay in Harad and watch the customs house wave your cargo through," a Corsair League captain tells a newly made master. "Out from under it, every harbor is slow to find you a berth, and some night a ship you have never seen will mistake you for a pirate." The Corsair League is Harad's maritime guild, and it blurs the line between legitimate shipping, naval power and outright piracy according to the political weather. The captains and admirals who won the Battle of [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzîr Shoals]] founded it as a fraternity of war veterans; it is now a powerful shipping cartel that controls much of Harad's merchant marine and serves the [[affiliation-cnfdrtnhrdnstts|Confederation]] as its de facto navy.

## Members and Privileges

The League's members are ship captains, fleet owners and naval officers who count themselves the heirs of the independence fleet, and its identity is half military and half mercantile. It controls the convoy routes, negotiates with pirates or hunts them, and hires out its warships to the Confederation when naval force is needed. In exchange its members enjoy preferential access to harbor facilities, customs exemptions and the right to carry weapons in Haradian ports. Its roots are deepest in [[affiliation-kethara|Kethara]].

## The League and the Compact

The League and the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]] need each other and distrust each other. The Compact finances voyages and the League protects them. The League resents the Compact's financial dominance, and the Compact distrusts the League's military power and its romantic attachment to the war's revolutionary ideals.

## The Privateer Problem

The League's charter gives its captains broad authority to "protect Haradian commerce," and they read that language widely: it has covered attacking competitors' ships in disputed waters, seizing cargoes from vessels trading outside guild agreements, and occasional outright piracy against targets of opportunity. The Grand Council periodically threatens to revoke these privileges, and the League reminds the Council each time that it is the only naval force standing between Harad and a [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian]] reconquest.

Independent operators such as the [[affiliation-thetamzir|Tamzîr]] live in a gray zone. The League wants every Haradian-flagged vessel under its umbrella, and captains who refuse meet harassment, port restrictions and the occasional "mistaken" confrontation at sea.
