## Overview

_To be written._ Azhûn is the capital and principal settlement of [[affiliation-azhun|Azhûn]].

Azhûn sprawls along a natural harbor at the mouth of the Alz River, its white-walled warehouses and bustling docks defining one of the Vylarian Sea's most vital trading posts. The harbor never sleeps—merchant vessels arrive and depart with the tides, their holds brimming with spices, silks, and rare woods.

Street markets overflow with languages, accents, and goods so exotic that locals joke the sea itself disgorges treasures at Azhûn's feet.
