## Overview

The Tamzîr Shoals lie in the channels of the **Haradian archipelago**, the chain of islands divided from the mainland of [[place-haradregin|Harad]] by a strait, and every pilot on the coast gives a newcomer the same first warning about them: do not take a deep-keeled ship into those waters without someone aboard who knows them. The Shoals are a maze of sandbars, submerged rocks and hard currents, and a ship that does not know the channels finds the sand.

## The Battle of Tamzîr Shoals

That warning won Harad its independence. During the war against the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], a Vylarian squadron—the largest the empire had ever gathered in the eastern sea—came to break the Haradian blockade of the strait. The Haradian captains met it in the Shoals with a fleet of warships, armed merchantmen, fishing boats and harbor craft under captains who had never fought together, and they had chosen the ground. The deep-drafted imperial warships could neither maneuver nor retreat nor bring their weight to bear, and the lighter Haradian vessels worked the channels around them like wolves around elk. In two days of fighting the squadron was burned, captured or driven onto the sand, and its admiral was killed.

**The Battle of Tamzîr Shoals** broke the empire's power in Haradian waters. Its land garrisons, cut off from supply, fell one by one, and Vylaria recognized Haradian sovereignty within the year.

## What the Shoals Mean Now

The battle is the founding myth of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtnhrdnstts|Confederation of Haradian States]]. [[place-kethara2|Kethara]], whose yards built the fleet, holds an annual Fleet Review in its memory, and [[place-tamavar2|Tamavar]] keeps a captured Vylarian warship in dry-dock as **the Tamzîr Monument**. The [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]] grew out of the captains who fought there. The veterans remember the Shoals differently: the dock workers, fishermen and tribal warriors who sailed into the imperial fleet were promised land, guild membership and a voice in the new government, and received a discharge and a small purse of silver instead. Their children have inherited the grievance along with the story. One trading vessel on the Vylarian Sea, [[affiliation-thetamzir|the Tamzîr]], carries the battle's name.
