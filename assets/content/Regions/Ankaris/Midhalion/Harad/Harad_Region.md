## Overview

Harad Region is the eastern shore of the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]] and the archipelago that lies off it, within [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión Region]]. It is the land of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtnhrdnstts|Confederation of Haradian States]]—five merchant city-states that won their independence from the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] at sea and are governed, in everything but name, by the guilds that paid for the war. Some twelve million people live here, in great port cities, inland caravan towns, fishing villages, and the tribal country of the hills.

Harad is the premier maritime region of western Ankaris. Its harbors carry the trade between the Vylarian Sea, the caravan roads of the east and the southern shore of [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Aû'Khelâthu]], and its bazaars, its cuisine and its fleets are known in every port of Mídhalión.

## Geography

The Haradian coast is deeply indented, and its bays make the finest natural harbors on the Vylarian Sea. Behind them the land climbs from fertile coastal plains through rolling hills to arid scrub, and the scrub gives way in the east to the wastes of [[place-dunharargn|Dunhara]] and the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn Desert]]. The climate is warm and dry, with hot summers tempered by the sea breeze.

Off the coast lies the **archipelago**, a chain of islands divided from the mainland by a strait. Its channels, shoals and sheltered coves serve as naval bases, trading posts and—for those who know the passages—smuggling havens. The **Tamzîr Shoals**, a maze of sandbars, submerged rocks and hard currents in those waters, are where the Haradian fleet destroyed the Vylarian squadron that came to break its blockade, the battle that won the war of independence.

## Cities and Towns

The five city-states of the Confederation each hold a city and the country around it:

- [[place-tamavar2|Tamavar]], the seat of the city-state of [[affiliation-tamavar|Tamavar]], is the largest and wealthiest city of Harad and its de facto capital. The Grand Council meets in its Grand Council Hall, the great guilds keep their headquarters along its Grand Harbor, and a captured Vylarian warship stands in dry-dock there as the Tamzîr Monument.
- [[place-kethara2|Kethara]], the seat of [[affiliation-kethara|Kethara]], is the naval stronghold on the strait to the archipelago. Its Strait Fortress commands the passage, its yards build the Confederation's warships, and its Veterans' Quarter houses the sailors and marines of the war.
- [[place-varoshan2|Varoshan]], the seat of [[affiliation-varoshan|Varoshan]], is the easternmost city, a polyglot crossroads of mudbrick and tile where the coastal trade meets the caravans from Dunhara and the Khazryn at the Caravan Gate.
- [[place-ashkabel2|Ashkabel]], the seat of [[affiliation-ashkabel|Ashkabel]] on the southern coast, is the cultural heart of the Confederation—its Design Yards conceive the finest Haradian ships, and its Academy of the Tides teaches navigation, astronomy and natural philosophy.
- [[place-azhun2|Azhûn]], the seat of [[affiliation-azhun|Azhûn]], spreads along a natural harbor at the mouth of the Alz River, the conduit between the Dunhari desert, the inland city-states and the western sea routes.

Smaller places fill the country between them. [[place-qadhirun|Qadhirun]] is a port town and one of the most populous in the region, with its own guilds and arena. [[place-kashmuret|Kashmuret]] is an inland trading town where the caravan routes from the Dunhari desert and southern [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]] converge, restock and settle their credit. [[place-miravel|Míravel]] is a merchant town, and [[place-sulun|Sulûn]] a coastal village.

## Peoples and Culture

Harad is a human country, sharply divided. The guild families live in palatial urban estates and educate their children in rhetoric, mathematics and mercantile law. Below them stands a large middle class of independent merchants, skilled craftspeople and ship captains, and below that the dock workers, sailors and urban poor of the crowded harbor districts. Wealth, reputation and the ability to strike a profitable deal are the virtues the coast honors most. Spiced meats, flatbreads, honeyed pastries and strong coffee are its staples, and the bazaars of its port cities offer goods from across the known world.

The **inland tribes** are culturally distinct from the coast. They keep older traditions, speak archaic dialects, and resent the cities' dominance. They sent many of the foot soldiers of the war of independence and received nothing from the peace.

The language of the region is [[skill-haradilng|Haradi]]. Each city-state keeps its own laws, militia and council, and the patchwork of regulations between them makes smuggling and legal arbitrage profitable trades.

## Faith and Magic

Religion in Harad is syncretic. The [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]], brought by the Vylarian conquest, dominates the coastal cities, where the merchant class favors Vénusia and Ménérva and sailors honor Táranon. The eastern states and the inland tribes keep to the older [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Āsháian Pantheon]], and in Varoshan it is the dominant faith. In many cities the temples of the two stand side by side, and a single family often patronizes both.

The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] keeps chapters in the major cities, but the guilds regard it as a possible instrument of Vylarian influence and restrict its work. Hedge mages, alchemists and fortune-tellers are more common here than in Vylaria, and in Varoshan the eastern traditions that come in with the caravans are practiced openly.

## Power and Trade

The Confederation's Grand Council gives each city-state an equal voice in theory; in practice the wealthiest cities and their guilds decide its business, and the rotating Arch-Consulship is more ceremonial than powerful. Three great guilds rule Harad in fact:

- The [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]], the banking cartel headquartered in Tamavar, which financed the war and holds the Confederation's debt.
- The [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]], the shipping cartel rooted in Kethara, which grew out of the veterans of Tamzîr Shoals and serves as the Confederation's de facto navy.
- The [[affiliation-mrchntryvl|Merchantry of the Veil]], the secretive dealers in luxury goods and information, who move what the other guilds will not touch.

The [[affiliation-sodnaqirin|Sôd-Naqîrîn]], the Council of the Guild-Wardens seated at Tamavar, is the Confederation's guild umbrella, and its treasurer runs the Bayt al-Khazînah, the house behind the [[lore-hardncrncy|Haradian currency]]: Vylarian denominations struck in Harad, with an Aurion slightly underweight by the Vylarian standard and script that Vylarian houses do not honor.

Beneath the prosperity the region is restless. The veterans who won the war and their children nurse the betrayal of its promises; outlawed captains raid guild shipping from the island channels; and harbor strikes, tax riots and tribal raids break out and are put down by guild enforcers, revoked licenses and called-in debts. The [[affiliation-thetamzir|Tamzîr]] and her captain, a veteran of the Shoals, run cargo on the margins of that order.

## Neighbors

Across the sea to the west lies [[place-helionis|Heliónis]], Harad's natural trading partner and its cultural counterpart. Along the northern shore lies [[place-vylariargn|Vylaría Region]], whose empire has not forgiven the loss of its eastern province and keeps agents in the Haradian cities. To the north, the Eastern March of the [[place-byzariargn|Byzaría Region]] faces Harad's hinterland, and the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Byzarian League]] is Harad's commercial rival. To the east the hills fall to the deserts of [[place-dunharargn|Dunhara]] and the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn]], whose peoples hold the overland routes the coastal cities depend on. Across the sea to the south, the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Aû'Khelâthu]] is Harad's most important long-distance partner, and the trade between their ports runs directly, without Vylarian intermediaries.
