The smell of Míravel's market square comes from a perfume shop: attar of rose from the island valleys, resins off the Khelâthi ships, ambergris when the corsairs have some. Míravel is a merchant town of about 12,000 in the [[place-haradregin|Harad Region]], the island country of city-states within [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]], and it is among the more populous towns of the region. Its guild families commission scents made for a single wearer from the perfumer [[being-rimarushur|Rimmârû Ushûrî]], and the locksmith [[being-belsharglm|Belshar Gûlamû]] has spent twenty years here making high-security locks for nobles and wealthy merchants.
