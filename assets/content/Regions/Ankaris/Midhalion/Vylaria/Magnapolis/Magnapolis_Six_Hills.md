The Six Hills are the cluster of hills on the eastern bank of the River Vylaris on which [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]] rises. Highest is [[place-monsaquila|Mons Aquila]], crowned by the walled [[place-urbsaquiln|Inner City]] and the [[place-palatimgnm|Grand Palace]]; around it stand Mons Corónus and [[place-monslaurine|Mons Lauríne]], which together make the Heights, Mons Sápiens, Mons Vétus and Mons Rípae. Their contours, rather than any drawn circle, shape the city's bounds.
