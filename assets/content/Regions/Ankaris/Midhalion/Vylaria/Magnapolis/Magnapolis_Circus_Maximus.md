## Overview

On a race day a veteran of the City Watch can tell where the Circus is without looking, because the roar carries across half of [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]]. The Circus Maximus is a colossal oblong arena, the largest in the world, with tiers of seating rising in a vast U around a central spine. It lies along the valley between Mons Vétus and Mons Rípae, and its crowd can reach a quarter-million.

A Watch veteran who has worked the Circus Circle on race days since his discharge teaches the new men what the day asks of them: "Know the colors. The racing factions wear them in the stands and in the streets, and the rivalry that begins on the track ends in a brawl by the third race. We do not take sides. We stand between the colors until the last chariot is home and the crowd turns toward the gates, and then we stand between them again."

## What the Circus Stages

The Circus holds the great events of the people:

- **Chariot races**, the obsession of the city, run in the colors of the racing factions whose rivalries spill into bloody street brawls.
- **Gladiatorial combats**, beast-hunts and public executions.
- **Mock sea-battles**, staged on the grandest occasions by flooding the arena floor from the aqueducts, with real ships and real, dying men.

The Circus is one half of "bread and games": the capital's dole includes the price of the games. An augustar ignores the mood of those crowds at his peril. The [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|City Watch]] keeps a watch-house at the Circus Circle, where the race crowds and the factions gather and, often enough, brawl.
