The Circus Maximus is a colossal oblong arena in [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]], the largest in the world, with tiers of seating rising in a vast U around a central spine. It stages chariot races run in the colors of the racing factions, gladiatorial combats, beast-hunts and public executions, and on the grandest occasions its floor is flooded for mock sea-battles before a crowd of a quarter-million.
