## Urbs Aquilion — the Inner City of Magnápolis

At the summit of **Mons Aquila**, the highest of the six hills of [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]], behind its own ancient ring of walls, stands **Urbs Aquilion** — the Inner City, the sacred and administrative core of the Empire. The name is old and freighted with glory: _Urbs Aquilion_, the **Eagle City**, is the first city — the original Magnápolis, raised on the Eagle Hill in the Empire's founding age, around which the whole metropolis afterward grew — and it takes its name from Mons Aquila beneath it and from the imperial Aquila whose wings shadow the throne. To a Vylarian it means simply _the heart of the world_; in daily speech it is clipped to **the Aquilion**, or merely **the Urbs**. Entry is controlled; common citizens may pass through certain precincts on feast days or to petition the bureaucracy, but much of it is the preserve of priests, senators, soldiers, magistrates, and the great families. Within it lie the temples of the state, the seats of its government, the imperial guard, the engine of its bureaucracy, and the Grand Palace itself.

### Regio Templum — The Temple District {#regio-templum-the-temple-district}

The [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían]] pantheon are the state religions of the Empire, and the Regio Templum contains their grandest expression anywhere in the world. But the City rules a continent, and into it flow the gods of every people Vylaria has conquered, courted, or traded with. The result is the most crowded sacred precinct on Thalorna: a forest of domes, pylons, spires, and timber halls where, by ancient toleration-edict, nearly every faith of the known world keeps a house. Vylarian law outlaws no religion whatsoever — but it expects every faith to behave itself within the City. Open conflict between religious groups — riot, arson, the public spilling of a rival's blood — is forbidden absolutely and punished without mercy. Covert conflict, of course, is abundant: the Regio Templum seethes with quiet feuds, poisonings dressed as fevers, bribed magistrates, and slanders whispered into senatorial ears.

The rule of the district mirrors the rule of the Regio Palatium beside it. A major or wealthy religion raises its own temple; a minor or poor one is granted space in a shared compound alongside others of compatible character, sometimes dividing a single sanctuary across a roster of holy days.

**The Aurèldían establishment.** The heart of the district belongs to _the Twelve_. The greatest of all is the **[[place-pnthnrldnm|Pantheon Aurèldiānum]]**, a single colossal sanctuary honoring the whole [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]] at once beneath a dome so wide that visiting architects refuse to believe it stands without [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|arcane]] assistance — and they are not entirely wrong. The main temple is attended by the priests of Janus. Around it cluster the individual great temples: [[affiliation-janus|Jánus]] of the Gates, fittingly enormous in the capital of order; wise [[affiliation-menerva|Ménérva]]; bountiful [[affiliation-venusia|Vénusia]]; [[affiliation-taranon|Táranon]] the Thunderer; [[affiliation-thanatos|Thánatos]] the Silent Judge; and the rest, each with its own priesthood, endowments, and feast-day processions. The forge-fires of [[affiliation-sacredforge|Vúlcan's Sacred Forge]] burn here too. But not so the **[[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]]**: eschewed by the other faiths of the pantheon, the Black Flame have their own magnificent temple within the district, and are amply attended, for its creed — purification through fire, the rightful dominance of the strong over the weak — sits closer to the Vylarian imperial temper than the Aurèldían's gentler priests care to admit. Sacred Forge and Black Flame loathe one another across the district, but their war is waged in the senate and the shadows, never in the streets.

**The foreign great temples.** Two foreign faiths are wealthy and ancient enough to rival the Aurèldían houses on their own ground. The **[[affiliation-kemtnpnthn|Kemetían]]** temple-compound — serving the gods of [[affiliation-empirtkhpr|Ta'Kheperu]], whose civilization was old before the Aurèldían names were spoken — is a walled precinct unto itself, its painted pylons and the smoke of its mortuary rites a dignified, deliberate reproach to Vylarian pretension. The **[[affiliation-asguardian|Asguardian]]** faithful of the north keep a great timber **hof**, smoke-darkened and carved with runes, where the merchants, mercenaries, and envoys of [[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Nordheim]] swear their binding oaths and pour mead to the defending gods. And even **Hél** — pariah to her own kin across the Asguardian north — is no outcast in Magnápolis. Her clergy are quietly powerful here, for they share with the Aurèldían priests of [[affiliation-thanatos|Thánatos]] the keeping of the dead, and the dead of a city this vast are a charge of enormous weight, wealth, and influence.

**The lesser and shared houses.** Beyond the great temples spreads a denser warren of smaller shrines, where the minor and provincial faiths keep what they can afford. The fire-and-light worship of the eastern **[[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Āsháians]]**, the cyclic devotions of **[[affiliation-varakpnthn|Varṇaka]]** pilgrims from Vedyára, the bureaucratic celestial rites of **[[affiliation-tngvkvnlei|Tëngvōk Vān Lëi]]** carried in by Tānvuri traders, and a hundred smaller cults from the provinces and beyond all maintain modest sanctuaries — many co-located in shared compounds, dividing the calendar and the altar-space between faiths judged compatible enough to share a roof. No faith here is barred from worship; the City taxes them all, tolerates them all, and watches them all — stepping in only when a quarrel between them threatens to break into the open.

### Regio Palatium — The Palace District

The Palace District is the Empire's diplomatic heart: its principal purpose is to house the **embassies** of the powers that must treat with Vylaria. Foreign **polities** keep their legations here — the great realms of [[affiliation-empirtkhpr|Ta'Kheperu]], [[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Nordheim]], the eastern empires, the [[affiliation-cnfdrtnhrdnstts|Haradian states]], and dozens of lesser kingdoms, princedoms, and city-states — but so do foreign **organizations** with standing business before the throne: merchant leagues and trading houses, craft and banking guilds, mercenary companies, temple-states, and chartered orders, all of which need a permanent voice in the City.

As in the Temple District, the rule is wealth and standing. A great power or a rich league raises its **own embassy** — a palatial compound behind its own walls, with gardens, guard, and private quarters, flying its own colors like a small sovereign island within the City. Smaller and poorer delegations are **co-located in shared compounds**, where a clutch of minor realms or modest guilds keep neighboring suites of offices and apartments under one roof, sharing a courtyard, a gate, and the watchful attention of the imperial protocol-officers. Interspersed among the embassies stand the city palaces of the greatest senatorial families and priestly houses, for whom an address in the diplomatic quarter is itself a statement of reach.

In addition to the embassies, this district also houses the **ceremonial palace of the [[affiliation-magnumclgm|Magnum Collegium]]** — the seat of the Curia Collegiorum and the face the guild-of-guilds turns toward the throne. This is ceremonial quarters only: the working guild-halls of its constituent trades keep no place inside the Inner City. Those stand out in the city proper, each lodged within or beside the ward where its trade is practised, under the same district law that governs every other craft.

### Curia Vylaria — The Palace of the Senate

The **[[affiliation-curiavylar|Curia Vylaria]]**, seat of the Vylarian Senate. Beneath its great bronze doors and within its tiered semicircular chamber, the patrician class debates, postures, conspires, and — in theory — advises the Augustar. In practice its real power waxes and wanes with the strength of whoever wears the imperial diadem.

### Castra Praetoria — The Imperial Barracks

The **[[place-castraprtr|Castra Praetoria]]** — fortified barracks of the **[[affiliation-pratrgstrm|Praetar Augustarum]]**, the imperial guard: the Augustar's personal bodyguard and the keepers of order and justice within the Inner City itself. From here the throne's grip on its own heart is enforced. The tramp of drilling soldiers is a constant background rhythm of the precinct, and the sight of the guard's polished armor is meant to reassure the loyal and unsettle the ambitious. The policing of the city _beyond_ the Inner Wall is no part of their charge; that falls to the [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|City Watch]].

### The Officia Imperii — The Imperial Bureaucracy

The whole administrative apparatus of the Empire is gathered into a single walled compound of the Inner City: the **Officia Imperii** — _the Imperial Offices_, known to everyone simply as the **Officia**. It is a sprawling complex of **administrative halls, archives, and counting-houses** that sets the great organs of imperial governance side by side: the [[affiliation-aerarimmpr|Aerarium Imperii]] (the imperial treasury and central bank), the [[affiliation-curiafscls|Curia Fiscalis]] (tax collection and administration), the **imperial tribunals** (the throne's own courts — see [[affiliation-curiaurbis|Curia Urbis]]), and the central imperial archives. It is the true engine of empire. Here thousands of clerks, scribes, tax-assessors, censors, and magistrates process the endless paper of governing a continent: census rolls, tax ledgers, military levies, grain dole records, court judgments, and the correspondence of a hundred provinces. The archives are said to be so vast that documents are lost in them for generations, and that a man with the right friends can make an inconvenient law, debt, or birth-record simply _disappear_.

### Aula Panepistemium — The Court of the Academy of Knowledge

Tha [[place-aulpnpstmm|Aula Panepistemium]] is the ceremonial headquarters of the sprawling Panepistemium, the Academy of Knowledge; the small, rarefied counterpart to the great campus that lies beyond the Inner Wall (the Scholars' District; see [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]]). This is no place of teaching. It is a palace of counsel and of keeping: a standing body of the institution's most senior scholars who answer, at the throne's summons, the questions of the Augustar, the [[affiliation-curiavylar|Senate]], and the great houses — a point of law construed, a history settled, a treaty's precedent traced, a portent weighed. Its treasure is the **Bibliotheca Reservata**, a deep and warded library of the rarest knowledge the Panepistemium holds: singular manuscripts, the archives of conquered peoples, and arcane artifacts too valuable — or too dangerous — to keep in open hands. Entry is by the throne's leave, behind the same Ordo wards that guard the imperial precincts. That the Order already works openly in the Grand Palace makes the arrangement natural: the Panepistemium's quiet inner seat and the Ordo's brilliant displays share one hill, while the bustle of the university proper is kept, by design, beyond the walls of the Urbs Aquilion.

### Palatium Magnum — The Grand Palace

Rising above all of it is the **[[place-palatimgnm|Palatium Magnum]]** — the largest and grandest single structure in Magnápolis, and therefore in the known western world. It is less a building than a small city of its own: tier upon tier of marble halls, colonnaded courtyards, hanging gardens, private baths, throne rooms, and audience chambers, all rising in terraces up and over the crown of the hill. Gold leaf, porphyry, and rare colored marbles from every conquered land sheathe its surfaces. Its private gardens are watered by dedicated aqueducts; its great bronze gates are taller than three men.

It is here, within the Palatium Magnum, that the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] is permitted — _encouraged_, even — to show off (see the Hand of the Ordo Arcanis, and the Mechanical Court, in the [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]] note).
