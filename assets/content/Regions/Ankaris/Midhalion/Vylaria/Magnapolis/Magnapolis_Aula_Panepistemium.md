## Aula Panepistemium – The Court of the Academy of Knowledge

Within the Inner City of [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]], sharing the palace hill with the throne itself, stands the **Aula Panepistemium** – the ceremonial headquarters of the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Panepistemium]], the Academy of Knowledge. It is the small, rarefied counterpart to the great campus that lies beyond the Inner Wall in the Scholars' District (see [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]]). The bustle of the university proper – the lecture halls, the wrangling students, the chapterhouse traffic – is kept, by deliberate design, outside the walls of [[place-urbsaquiln|Urbs Aquilion]]. What remains within is something quieter and far more select.

### A Palace of Counsel

The Aula is no place of teaching. It is a palace of counsel and of keeping: the seat of a standing body of the Panepistemium's most senior scholars, who answer, at the throne's summons, the questions of the Augustar, the [[affiliation-curiavylar|Senate]], and the great houses. A point of law to be construed, a contested history to be settled, the precedent of a treaty to be traced, a portent to be weighed – these are the matters carried up to the Aula. Its scholars do not lecture; they advise, and their advice can decide an inheritance, justify a war, or quietly bury a claim. To be called to the Aula is, for a scholar of the Panepistemium, the summit of the institution's honors; to be consulted by it is, for the great, a mark of standing in itself.

### The Bibliotheca Reservata

The Aula's true treasure is the **Bibliotheca Reservata** – a deep and warded library holding the rarest knowledge the Panepistemium possesses: singular manuscripts that exist nowhere else, the captured archives of conquered peoples, and arcane artifacts too valuable, or too dangerous, to leave in open hands. It is not a library one visits. Entry is by the throne's leave alone, behind the same [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo]] wards that guard the imperial precincts, and what is kept within is known in full to very few. Rumor in the City holds that the Reservata contains books the Empire has officially burned, the only surviving records of nations Vylaria erased, and relics the Ordo Arcanis would rather no rival ever study – and rumor, in this case, is not far wrong.

### Two Faces on One Hill

That the Aula and the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] share a single hill is no accident but a designed arrangement. The Ordo already works openly in the [[place-palatimgnm|Palatium Magnum]], turning out the brilliant displays the throne wants seen; the Panepistemium keeps its quiet inner seat in the Aula, turning the same proximity to the opposite use. The Order's marvels and the Academy's secrets are two faces of one strategy – knowledge as the ornament of power, and knowledge as the hoarded reserve of it. The warded calm of the Reservata and the gleaming spectacle of the Mechanical Court are, in the end, the same instrument held by the same hand.
