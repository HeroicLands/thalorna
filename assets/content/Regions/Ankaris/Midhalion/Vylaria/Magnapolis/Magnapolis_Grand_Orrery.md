## Overview

The doors of the Grand Orrery open at the great hours with a sound of deep bells, and a junior of the [[affiliation-pratrgstrm|Praetar Augustarum]] who has stood the hall's door at the changes of the watch can describe the next three minutes with his eyes shut. The Orrery, also called the Mechanical Court, is an enormous arcane-mechanical automaton housed in a hall of the [[place-palatimgnm|Grand Palace]] in [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]]. It is part clock, part theater and part diplomatic weapon, built of brass, crystal, enchanted gears and animate puppets the size of living men.

The guard shows a new recruit what to expect: "Stand to the side and keep your face still. The bells come first. Then armored legionaries roll out on hidden tracks and salute together, and gilded provinces come after them as crowned women who bow toward the throne. A clockwork eagle spreads its wings. Miniature augustars of the past pace a circuit and return. The joints whisper and the eyes glow with a soft light, and the first time you see it you will want to look at the visitors instead. Do that. Watching a stranger's face while the eagle opens its wings is the best part of the post."

A celestial dial above the pageant tracks the wandering of sun, moon and stars in real time. The Orrery tells the hour and it tells a story as well: the inevitability of Vylarian order, the obedience of the world and the eternity of the throne. Travelers cross a continent to see it, and ambassadors are meant to carry the story home.

## Keeping the Machine

The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] maintains the Orrery jealously. Only a handful of its Magi-Artificers know the full secret of its workings, and the cost of keeping it running is a closely guarded line in the imperial accounts. The guard on the door knows only what the pageant does, and he knows it better than anyone who has ever watched it from the floor.
