The Grand Orrery, also called the Mechanical Court, is an enormous arcane-mechanical automaton housed in a hall of the Grand Palace in [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]]. Part clock, part theater, it brings brass, crystal and enchanted gears to life at the great hours, when mechanical figures roll forth and a celestial dial above tracks the sun, moon and stars. A handful of Magi-Artificers of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] know the full secret of its workings.
