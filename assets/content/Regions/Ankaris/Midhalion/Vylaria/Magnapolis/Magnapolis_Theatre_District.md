The **Theatre District** is the quieter of [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]]'s two quarters of pleasure, the Circus quarter about the **Circus Circle** being the loud one, and by some distance the more celebrated abroad. A pedicab runner who works the avenues from the **Grand Market** to the Circles knows its habits better than the ticket-sellers do: "Nobody hires me to the Theatre District before noon, and I do not take anyone out of it before midnight. The place keeps its own clock, and you will keep it too."

Its great **semicircular theaters and odeons** stage tragedies, comedies, recitations and music throughout the season. The Vylarian stage is famous across the world for its masked players and for stage-machinery elaborate enough that visitors regularly assume magic and are regularly wrong.

## Character

The district works to a different clock from the rest of the city. Mornings are for rehearsal and for the trades that live off the stage—mask-makers, costumers, machinists, copyists, instrument-makers—and the quarter is at its busiest and least respectable well after dark.

Its population is correspondingly mixed: players, who occupy an ambiguous social position that the Vylarian elite enjoys far too much; the wealthy patrons who fund companies and quarrel about them; and the enormous supporting trade that a permanent theater season requires.

## Notable Features

- **The theaters and odeons**, the largest seating a substantial fraction of the city.
- **The machinists' yards**, where the stage engines are built and jealously guarded.
- **The mask-makers' street**, a hereditary trade and the district's most recognizable one.
