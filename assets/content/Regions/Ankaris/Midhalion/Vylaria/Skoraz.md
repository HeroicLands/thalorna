## Overview

In Skoraz a family's name counts for more in the courts than in any ledger at [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]], because the city lies far enough from the capital. It is one of the lesser cities of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Empire of Vylaria]], large enough to carry a provincial administration, local courts and a set of old houses who measure themselves against one another. Its life is the life of the empire's provinces: magistrates, bureaucrats, land, and the slow rise and fall of houses.

## Houses Rising and Falling

The story the city tells about itself is the story of its houses, and the clearest example is **House Exarchides**. Three generations ago an Exarchides served as a provincial magistrate, and the family name still carried weight in the local courts long after the wealth behind it had gone: land sold, alliances fraying, the family's seat in Skoraz growing shabbier while newer houses climbed past them.

The end came through a commercial venture on borrowed money, faithless partners, and creditors placed to profit from the fall. The father, a trusted bureaucrat in Skoraz's provincial administration, was disgraced; the land was seized and the house sold to cover the debts. The son, [[being-klstrtsxrchds|Kallistratos Exarchides]], was by then a [[affiliation-pyrethos|Pyréthos]] mage of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] at [[place-velysara|Velysâra]], and the fall of his house made him what he is.
