## Overview

Skoraz is one of the lesser cities of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Empire of Vylaria]]: large enough to carry a provincial administration, local courts and a set of old houses who measure themselves against one another, and far enough from the capital that a family's name counts for more in its courts than in any ledger at [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]]. Its life is the life of the empire's provinces—magistrates, bureaucrats, land and the slow rise and fall of houses.

## Houses Rising and Falling

The story the city tells about itself is the story of its houses, and the clearest example is House Exarchides. Three generations ago an Exarchides served as a provincial magistrate, and the family name still carried weight in the local courts long after the wealth behind it had gone: land sold, alliances fraying, the family's seat in Skoraz growing shabbier while newer houses climbed past them. The end came through a commercial venture on borrowed money, faithless partners and creditors placed to profit from the fall. The father, a trusted bureaucrat in Skoraz's provincial administration, was disgraced; the land was seized and the house sold to cover the debts. The son, [[being-klstrtsxrchds|Kallistratos Exarchides]], was by then a Pyréthos mage of the Ordo Arcanis at [[place-velysara|Velysâra]], and the fall of his house made him what he is.
