## Overview

The Province of Hilen holds the land of [[place-hylen|Hilen]], and its landed aristocracy is the most conservative faction in Vylarian politics: traditionalist, suspicious of change, and deeply invested in the old social order. The _thêrkhôs_ (governor) sits at [[place-aravantia|Aravantâra]].

A steward of one of the great estates, asked by a visiting _gûlmâlênôs_ (equestrian) what Hilen makes of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thôrdimâlon]] and its growing weight at court, does not trouble to be polite: "The empire was built on soldiers and farmers. The mages and the money-changers came afterward, and they will be gone before the vines are."

## Character

The estate lords live well, though not with the ostentatious wealth of the capital. Theirs is the quiet confidence of people who know that everyone else depends on what they grow.

They resist the growing influence of the merchant class and of the Arcane Order alike, and they see themselves as the true guardians of Vylarian tradition. That makes Hilen a bastion of the **Imperial Cult** and of traditional [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían]] practice. The estates have a horror of civil war that the Empire's other provinces do not share, because the one battle the Empire fought between its own legions was fought on an estate of this province ([[lore-olcassabtl|The Battle of Olkassâ]]). The field itself is the [[place-olcassafld|Blood-field of Olkassâ]].

## Notable Features

- The conservative stronghold of traditional Vylarian values
- A minimal Thôrdimâlon (the Arcane Order) presence, because the aristocracy keeps the Arcane Order at arm's length

## Life in the Province

Hilen is settled but not urban. It is a managed countryside of great estates, vineyards, olive groves and the imperial game-forests that the crown reserves from the plow. Power belongs to the old landed families, not to merchants or the Arcane Order, and the prevailing temper is conservative, pious and slow to change. A settlement here sits on or beside a great estate, and its real lord is whichever family owns the land.

Trouble in Hilen is trouble over land: estate and inheritance disputes, tenant grievances and the occasional rising, banditry in the wooded margins, and the quiet feuds of neighboring great houses. Poaching is the sharpest of them. A game-warden at [[place-cervalia|Kervalâra]] tells each new forester the rule before he is handed a bow: "A hungry man who takes the emperor's deer is a hanged man, and it does not matter whose tenant he was. Do not argue the case with him in the forest. Argue it with the magistrate, after."

What is abundant is food, wine and labor. What is scarce is hard coin, news, and anyone who welcomes change.

## Commerce and Currency

The Province of Hilen uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vylarian currency]] (Solden / Ardin / Ogdel) as its standard currency, with full access to the paper-script system through the [[affiliation-danvar|Danvar]] moneylenders chartered under the [[affiliation-tevran|Tevran Maldor]]. Imperial taxation is administered by the [[affiliation-tolvan|Tolvan]] and revenues flow to the [[affiliation-tarmex|Tarmex Orzan]]. See [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vylarian Currency]] for the full system.
