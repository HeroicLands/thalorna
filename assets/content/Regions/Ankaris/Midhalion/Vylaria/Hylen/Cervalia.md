## Overview

Cervalia is the gateway to the great imperial game-forest that the crown reserves from [[affiliation-provnchyln|Hylen]]'s farmland — a town of wardens, houndsmen, and lodge-keepers that swells when the court comes to hunt and drowses the rest of the year. Poaching the imperial deer is a serious crime, and the foresters of Cervalia are as much game-wardens and law-keepers as woodsmen; the tension between the reserved forest and the hungry tenantry around it is a standing feature of local life.
