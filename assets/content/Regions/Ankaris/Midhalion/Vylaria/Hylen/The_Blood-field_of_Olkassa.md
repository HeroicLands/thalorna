## Overview

A grain factor of one of the great houses, riding the Hilen estates in the week before harvest, makes a point of reaching Olkassâ last. "It is cut by the time I get there," he says, "and the sheaves are heavier than anyone else's, and I have never asked the steward why. The wheat comes off the field of both eagles a fortnight before the neighbours' and sells for the dole at the top of the price. Do not ask the tenants. They know, and they will not tell you."

The **Blood-field of Olkassâ** is a [[lore-bloodfield|blood-field]] on the Olkassâ estate, one of the great holdings of [[place-hylen|Hilen]]. It is where the Senate's army and the Moktur legions killed one another about 401 AF ([[lore-olcassabtl|The Battle of Olkassâ]]), under the same eagle and with no quarter given. Its kind is a **quickening**, and it is among the best farmland in the province.

## The Quickening

The ground is moderately thin. Wheat on the field comes in a fortnight early and half again as heavy as the estate's other fields. The vines on its edge set fruit twice in a year. The Olkassâ estate's grain is the best in Hilen, and a good deal of it is milled into the dole.

Nothing crosses that a reaper sees by day. On the anniversary of the battle the estate's dogs will not go into the standing grain, and men who sleep in the field-huts that week dream of being buried by their own side.

## How It Is Treated

The estate farms the field, sells its grain into the state granaries, and does not discuss it. The tenants leave the last sheaf standing for the dead. The Imperial Cult keeps a small shrine to both armies' Ôrzândoi at the field's edge, the only shrine in the Empire to a defeated claimant.

The clergy of [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]] and [[affiliation-nahild|Náhild]] hold the Empire's dead between them, and it is Náhild's priests who go onto a field.

## Who Goes

- **Grain factors**, who buy the heaviest wheat in Hilen and ask nothing about it.
- **Priests of Náhild**, on the anniversary.
- **Arcane Order surveyors**, whom the estate turns away at the gate.

## Hooks

- **The dole's flour.** A grain factor finds that the capital's dole is milled from Olkassâ wheat, and a preacher of the [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] in the Warrens tells the poor why the city has been angry for a year.
- **The heir's wedding.** The estate's heir wants the field settled by Sélmoros's rite before his wedding. The rite works perhaps half the time, and nobody knows whether settling it would end the harvest.
