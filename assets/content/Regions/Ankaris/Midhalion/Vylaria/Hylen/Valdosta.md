## Overview

Valdosta is a market town of about five thousand in the estate country of [[place-hylen|Hylen]], the breadbasket of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]. The fields and vineyards around it belong to great houses and are worked by tenants, and on a market day the wagons that crowd its streets belong to their stewards.

That is the whole of Valdosta's work: it is where the estate country's produce is weighed and sold on. A steward who has sold in its market for a few seasons says the town has no character of its own, which is how its buyers like it.
