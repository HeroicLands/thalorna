## Overview

Messora is quiet for most of the year and loud at harvest. It is a harvest-market town of about four thousand serving the estate country of southern [[affiliation-provnchyln|Hylen]], and when the tenant villages bring in their grain, the estate-factors settle the season's accounts in its market square.

The great houses of the district keep storehouses and counting-rooms here, which is why the square matters. A factor who has spent twenty seasons at Messora tells a young colleague to arrive early and stay late: the grain is only half of what changes hands in those weeks, and the other half is paper.
