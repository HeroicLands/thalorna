## Overview

Ashenmere is where Hylen's estates run out. A town of about four thousand on the march of [[place-hylen|Hylen]], the breadbasket of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], it is the province's border town, and a carter coming in from the edge of the province passes the last estate's boundary stones a few miles before its gate.

An estate steward from the interior, sent to Ashenmere with a wagon of tenants' goods, would call it a foreign posting. Inland the lord is whoever owns the land, and every road leads to a manor. On the march the roads lead out of the province as well as into it, and the town is where the two kinds of traffic meet.
