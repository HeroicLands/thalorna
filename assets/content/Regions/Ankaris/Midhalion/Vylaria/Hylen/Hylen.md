## Overview

Hylen is the land that feeds the empire. It is the territory of the [[affiliation-provnchyln|Province of Hylen]] in the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], seven million people on the fertile lowlands and terraced hillsides of [[place-vylariargn|Vylaría Region]], where a few aristocratic families hold estates as large as valleys and grow the grain, wine and olive oil that the empire lives on. Its seat is [[place-aravantia|Aravantia]].

An estate steward, riding the boundary with a newly hired bailiff on his first morning, gives the whole lesson in a few sentences: "Everything between this road and that ridge belongs to the house. Everything past the ridge belongs to the neighbors, and they sit in the Senate too. Between us we send the capital its bread, and in all my years nobody in Magnápolis has asked how it gets there."

## Character

Where [[affiliation-provinclys|Alyssa]] runs on politics and intrigue, Hylen runs on land and harvests. Each great estate is a self-contained world: a manor house at its center, surrounded by fields, vineyards and orchards, and by the villages of the tenant farmers and laborers who work them. A traveler here passes from one world to the next by crossing a boundary stone, not by passing a town gate.

## Economy

Agriculture dominates. Hylen's grain feeds the capital and the legions, its wine and olive oil are exported across the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]], and its estates add wool, linen and leather. Almost nothing is manufactured here. The lords prefer to sell raw goods and buy finished ones from [[affiliation-provinclys|Alyssa]] and [[affiliation-provincvld|Vald]], so the province sends out what it grows and brings in what it wears and uses.

## Notable Features

- The great aristocratic estates, some encompassing entire valleys
- The breadbasket of the empire, which controls the food supply
- The imperial game-forests, woodland reserved by the crown for the hunt

## Settlements

Hylen is a country of estates and market-towns: great aristocratic domains, each a small world of manor, fields, vineyards and tenant villages, linked by the towns where their produce is weighed and sold on.

**Towns.**

- [[place-aravantia|Aravantia]] (~28,000)—the provincial capital and the great grain-and-wine market of the breadbasket.
- [[place-granaria|Granaria]] (~7,000)—the chief grain-market of the central province, all granaries and weighing-yards.
- [[place-vindara|Vindara]] (~6,000)—a prosperous town of the terraced wine country.
- [[place-olivenna|Olivenna]] (~5,000)—a town of presses and oil-merchants in the olive country.
- [[place-valdosta|Valdosta]] (~5,000)—a market town of the estate country.
- [[place-messora|Messora]] (~4,000)—a harvest-market town of the southern estates.
- [[place-ashenmere|Ashenmere]] (~4,000)—a border town on the province's march.
- [[place-cervalia|Cervalia]] (~2,500)—the forest town at the edge of the imperial game-reserve.

**The great estates and manorial holdings.** The countryside belongs to vast aristocratic domains, not to free villages. Each is a manor with its tenant village, chapel and labor force of 200–1,000 souls. The named holdings include **Caldovia**, **Sennfeld**, **Aurvale**, **Olcassa**, **Brennfeld**, **Granholt**, **Vellora**, **Calvenna**, **Marenna**, **Tallenza**, **Oxenna**, **Harvenna**, **Wynnara** and **Stonemere**. Several are the seats of senatorial families whose word is effectively law within their own bounds. These are only the great houses; the province holds hundreds of lesser estates and tenant villages besides.
