## Overview

Hylen is the land of the [[affiliation-provnchyln|Province of Hylen]] of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], and lies within [[place-vylariargn|Vylaría Region]].

Hylen is the breadbasket of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] – a province of fertile lowlands, terraced hillsides, and vast aristocratic estates that produce the grain, wine, and olive oil that feed the empire.

## Character

Where [[affiliation-provinclys|Alyssa]] runs on politics and intrigue, Hylen runs on land and harvests. The great estates are self-contained worlds, each centered on a manor house surrounded by fields, vineyards, orchards, and the villages of tenant farmers and laborers who work them.

## Economy

Agriculture dominates. Hylen's grain feeds the capital and the legions; its wine and olive oil are exported across the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]]. The province also produces wool, linen, and leather. There is little manufacturing – Hylen's lords prefer to sell raw goods and buy finished products from [[affiliation-provinclys|Alyssa]] and [[affiliation-provincvld|Vald]].

## Notable Features

- The great aristocratic estates – some encompassing entire valleys
- Breadbasket of the empire; controls the food supply
- The imperial game-forests – woodland reserved by the crown for the hunt

## Settlements

Hylen's settlement pattern is the estate and the market-town: great self-contained aristocratic domains – each a small world of manor, fields, vineyards, and tenant villages – linked by the towns where their produce is weighed and sold on.

**Towns.**

- **[[place-aravantia|Aravantia]]** (~28,000) – the provincial capital and the great grain-and-wine market of the breadbasket.
- **[[place-granaria|Granaria]]** (~7,000) – the chief grain-market of the central province, all granaries and weighing-yards.
- **[[place-vindara|Vindara]]** (~6,000) – a prosperous town of the terraced wine country.
- **[[place-olivenna|Olivenna]]** (~5,000) – a town of presses and oil-merchants in the olive country.
- **Valdosta** (~5,000) – a market town of the estate country.
- **[[place-messora|Messora]]** (~4,000) – a harvest-market town of the southern estates.
- **Ashenmere** (~4,000) – a border town on the province's march.
- **[[place-cervalia|Cervalia]]** (~2,500) – the forest town at the edge of the imperial game-reserve.

**The great estates and manorial holdings.** Hylen's countryside is held in vast aristocratic domains rather than free villages – each a manor with its tenant village, chapel, and labor force (200–1,000 souls). Among the named holdings are Caldovia, Sennfeld, Aurvale, Olcassa, Brennfeld, Granholt, Vellora, Calvenna, Marenna, Tallenza, Oxenna, Harvenna, Wynnara, and Stonemere – several of them seats of senatorial families whose word is effectively law within their own bounds. These named domains are only the great houses; the province holds hundreds of lesser estates and tenant villages besides.
