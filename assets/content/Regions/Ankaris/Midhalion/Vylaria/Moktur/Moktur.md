## Overview

Two million people live in the mountains along the northern edge of [[place-vylariargn|Vylaría Region]], against the [[place-vrystwald|Vrystwald]], among mines, timber and fortified valleys. This is Moktur, the land of the [[affiliation-provncmktr|Province of Moktur]] of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]. Its seat is [[place-tyrellan|Tyrellan]].

A watchtower sergeant, handing a new recruit his first night on the wall, teaches the one skill the province asks of everyone: "Down there is the Vrystwald and up here is the empire, and the line between them is whatever you can see from this wall. Count the smoke. One column is a hearth. Three is a camp. Wake me for three."

## Character

Life here is harder, colder and more dangerous than in the southern provinces. The settlements are fortified: even market towns have walls and watchtowers. The population is smaller and more spread out than in the south, clustered in mountain valleys and along the fortified roads that connect the frontier garrisons.

## Economy

Mining and timber are the primary industries, supplemented by herding and limited highland agriculture. Moktur's mountains hold iron, copper and silver deposits that feed the empire's metalworking industry.

## Notable Features

- Fortified frontier garrisons along the Vrystwald border
- Rich mineral deposits fueling the empire's metalworking

## Settlements

Moktur's settlements are few, fortified, and strung along the mountain valleys and the garrison roads.

**Towns.**

- [[place-tyrellan|Tyrellan]] (~15,000)—the provincial capital, seat of the military governor.
- [[place-ferrovan|Ferrovan]] (~7,000)—the chief mining town, above the iron, copper and silver lodes.
- [[place-castrovar|Castrovar]] (~6,000)—a legion fortress-town on the Vrystwald frontier.
- [[place-korrast|Korrast]] (~5,000)—a fortified toll-town commanding a principal mountain pass.
- [[place-stennvold|Stennvold]] (~4,000)—a high mining-and-smelting town.
- [[place-pinnhald|Pinnhald]] (~3,000)—a timber town of the forested slopes, half-Varokh in custom.
- [[place-sanctara|Sanctara]] (~2,000)—a temple town among the heights.

**Hamlets, camps and border communities.** Between the towns lie scattered settlements of 100–800 people: fortified herding steads, mining and cutting camps, watchtower-villages, and the [[lore-varokhiclt|Varokh]] communities that sit within Moktur's nominal borders but answer to no Vylarian magistrate. Among them are **Greythorn**, **Ironcamp**, **Skarrholt**, **Vargmoot**, **Highfell**, **Drekkar's Steading**, **Wolfstead**, **Coldreach** and **Stennvik**, several of them more Varokh than Vylarian in tongue and custom. By the empire's standards Moktur is thinly peopled, and its mountains hold far more sheep, ore and timber than men.
