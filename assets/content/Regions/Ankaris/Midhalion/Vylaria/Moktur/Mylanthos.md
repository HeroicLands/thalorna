## Overview

Mylânthos is a farming village on the rural circuit of [[place-tyrellan|Tyrellan]] in [[place-moktur|Moktur]], one of the places [[being-azendsdmtr|Azéndas Dâemâtôr]], Senior Priest of [[affiliation-florania|Flórania]] in the Tyrellan region, visits on his rounds. Its farmers leave part of their harvest at the temple, and they do it because they have seen the priest's blessing turn into a harvest that keeps them alive.

## The Elder

The village's voice is **Elder Vasken of Mylânthos**, a former militia commander now in his seventies. It was Vasken who first encouraged Azéndas to take up healing, and when the Temple hierarchy in the capital questions the priest's methods, Mylânthos is where Azéndas finds shelter and Vasken is the one who provides the local political cover.
