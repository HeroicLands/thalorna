## Overview

A frontier garrison drills against Varokh raids behind the walls of Dolvar, a legion fortress-town of about six thousand on the [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]] march, the hard edge of the northern frontier. Before them lies a debatable land of watchtowers, truces and border-clans whose loyalties shift with the season.

The town lives by the legion: its taverns, smiths and traders all serve the soldiers. A smith who has shod the garrison's horses for twenty years can tell a new arrival how things stand: "The commander answers to the capital, and the capital is a long way south. Up here he is a small king, and his word carries further than any letter from [[place-orzara|Orzara]] does." That is the practical meaning of the military _therkoi_' autonomy in [[affiliation-provncmktr|Moktur]].

## Arriving

The military road north from [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]] climbs for three days through the narrowing valleys, past towers that stand a smoke's sight apart, and comes out on a shoulder of bare hillside with the valley falling away below it. Dolvar is two walls on that shoulder. The upper one is the fortress, a square of gray stone raised in the hunger raids to hold a legion, with a gate-tower in each side and the legion's eagle in a shrine over the north gate. The lower one is the town that grew against it, a ring of rough stone round taverns, smithies, stables and the houses of soldiers' families, and its gates are shut at dusk with the fortress's.

From the north gate the valley runs down to the marsh at its foot, gray-brown and flat, with the passes of [[place-vandstein|Vandstein]] above it and the forest beyond. The town's people do not look at the marsh. A newcomer learns that on his first evening, standing on the wall to count the smoke with a sergeant who keeps his eyes on the forest ([[place-blackmarshfld|The Blood-field of the Black Marsh]]).

Inside the fortress, against its east wall, stands the [House of Guests]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, a long building with a garden and its own guard. The boys who live in it are the sons of Varokh War Chiefs from the Vandstein villages nearest the passes, sent down under the truce and kept as its surety, a boy from each village. They learn to read Vilari, ride with the cavalry and eat at the officers' table, and in the summer they are shown to their villages' envoys at the truce market.

## What Dolvar Lives By

Dolvar lives by the legion. Its cohorts and their animals eat grain brought up the road from Tirellan, wear out boots, harness and spearshafts, and spend their pay in the lower town when it comes. The town's smiths, saddlers and tavern-keepers work for the camp, and its women are soldiers' wives who cannot marry them in law until their twenty years are served.

The watchtowers report to Dolvar. Every tower on the march keeps a _karvumrozima_ (smoke tally), a wax board for each evening on which the watch officer marks the smoke counted from the wall at dusk, and when a board is full it comes down to the fortress with the dispatch. The clerks at Dolvar read them, copy the figures into the march's book and send the boards back to be smoothed. The book is how the _vandraks_ knows where the forest is gathering.

The truce market is the town's one other trade. Four times each summer, on days fixed by the truce, Varokh traders from the Vandstein villages come down to a marked field below the town with furs, honey, wax and hides, and Vilérian traders meet them with iron, salt and cloth. The legion's horse stands at both ends of the field. Nobody crosses the stones that mark it without leave, and the market has run every summer since the truce was sworn.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order

Dolvar is not a chartered town. It is the legion's ground, and the _vandraks_ (legion commander), [[being-sthrskrmrs|Satheras Kiramaris]], rules it by camp law: the fortress's officers judge the lower town's quarrels, the camp prefect sets its market prices, and a thief caught in the lower town is flogged at the fortress gate like a soldier. Satheras is a _kurthos_ (senator) of an old house of the capital, a careful, cold officer in his eighth year on the march, and he is the _therkos_'s deputy in the field. He keeps the truce, the towers and the House of Guests, and he answers to the capital by the road that runs through Tirellan.

The legion's centurions keep order in the town as they keep it in the camp. A tavern-keeper with a brawl on his hands sends for the century whose men are in it, and that century's _Hundar_ settles it before the watch is needed.

## History {#history}

The fortress was raised in the hunger raids to hold a legion on the march, and the lower town grew against its wall. In 520 a Varokh host was driven into the marsh at the valley's foot and died there to the last man ([[lore-blackmarshbtl|The Battle of the Black Marsh]]). Since the truce with the nearest Vandstein villages was sworn, their War Chiefs' sons have lived in the House of Guests and the market below the town has run every summer. Satheras Kiramaris took command eight years ago.

## Current Situation {#present}

The truce holds, and every part of it is under strain. The boys in the House of Guests are shown to their villages' envoys this summer as every summer. A tower's smoke tally is three days late, and the _vandraks_, Satheras Kiramaris, will not send a century into the hills on a guess. The legion is paid when the treasury's coin comes up the road through [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]], the road whose stations the _therkos_ keeps. At the truce market a Vilérian trader has crossed the marked stones in the night and is missing, and his partner fears the Varokh envoys will call it a breach.

## Where a Party Lodges

The lower town has three taverns with beds, and the best of them is the [Gold Band]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, named for the honor on the legion's name and kept by a retired centurion who will not let the road verses of [[lore-goldbandsng|the Song of the Gold Band]] be sung under his roof before the officers have left. A party with business at the fortress is lodged in the visitors' rooms over the south gate, which are cold, clean and watched.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The tally that did not come.** A tower's smoke tally is three days late and its dispatch rider has not come down. The _vandraks_ will not send a century into the hills on a guess, and he hires a party to ride up and see why the tower is silent.
- **The boy who wants to go home.** One of the boys in the House of Guests asks a party, through the cook, to carry a token across the passes to his mother. The token is harmless. Carrying anything across the line from the House of Guests is forbidden by the truce.
- **The trader at the stones.** At the truce market a Vilérian trader has crossed the marked stones in the night and is missing, with his mule and his goods. His partner needs him found before the Varokh envoys decide the truce has been broken by the other side.

# The Truth of Dolvar {#truth .secret}

One of the boys in the House of Guests is not who he is said to be. [Edrarith]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the son of the War Chief of [[place-vandgrund|Vandgrund]], died of a winter fever in the House of Guests in the second month of last winter. Vandgrund's men are the ones who come over the nearest pass, and the truce that keeps them on their side of it is held by that boy. Satheras Kiramaris did not send word north. He had the body buried at night in the garden of the House of Guests and put in Edrarith's place a stable-boy of the same age from [[place-pinnhald|Pinnhald]], half-Varokh, who speaks the forest tongue and has been taught since the thaw to answer to the dead boy's name.

At the truce market the envoys see the boys across the marked stones, at the length of a spear-throw, with the cavalry between, and the stable-boy has passed once already. This summer Vandgrund's envoy is to be the dead boy's uncle, who knew him from the cradle and has asked to speak with him. The boys who share the House of Guests know, and two of them are sons of other War Chiefs. Satheras has bought their silence with the promise of their own early return, which he has no authority to give.

The _vandraks_ believes that the truth would break the truce in the first week of summer and bring Vandgrund over the pass in the second, with the towers short of men and the legion short of pay. He may be right. He has not told the _therkos_, because a man who opens every dispatch from Dolvar is a man Satheras will not hand a secret to.
