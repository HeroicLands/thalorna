## Overview

Tyrellan is the capital of Moktur and the seat of its military governor, and with about fifteen thousand people it is the largest town in a province where towns are small. It belongs to the [[affiliation-provncmktr|Province of Moktur]], the empire's northern shield of mines, timber and fortified garrisons on the [[place-vrystwald|Vrystwald]] march, and it is walled like everything else in [[place-moktur|Moktur]].

A farmer from one of the villages on the Tyrellan circuit, in town to sell a season's wool, describes the city to a neighbor as the place where the governor's word is law. For the villages themselves the person who matters is the healer-priest [[being-azendsdmtr|Azéndas Dâemâtôr]], Senior Priest of [[affiliation-florania|Flórania]] in the Tyrellan region, who takes the blessing of the fields and the harvest thanksgiving out to the farming communities around the city, among them [[place-mylanthos|Mylânthos]].
