Tyrellan is a provincial city in [[place-moktur|Moktur]], the mountainous northern shield of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]. It belongs to the [[affiliation-provncmktr|Province of Moktur]], a frontier province of mines, timber and fortified garrisons guarding the Vrystwald march and governed by semi-autonomous military lords.
