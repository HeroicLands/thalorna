## Overview

A deserter from a Mokturan garrison, caught by a patrol that would not follow him the last mile, explained where he had been sleeping. "The marsh," he said. "Nobody comes for you in the marsh. You lie in the reeds and you want to run, and you cannot say why, and in the morning you are glad of a wall."

The **Blood-field of the Black Marsh** is a [[lore-bloodfield|blood-field]] in the valleys of northern [[place-moktur|Moktur]], below the passes of [[place-vandstein|Vandstein]]. It is where a Varokh host of the hunger raids was driven into the water and died to the last man in 520 AF ([[lore-blackmarshbtl|The Battle of the Black Marsh]]). Its kind is a **dread**, and it runs thin.

## The Dread

The dread returns what was felt on the ground: the terror of men in mail sinking to the waist with the ridges closed behind them. It comes over anyone who lingers past dusk, and it does not announce itself as anyone's but their own. A watchman who sleeps within sight of the reeds wakes wanting to run from something he cannot name.

Lights move in the reeds on windless nights, and there is a sound of many men breathing hard. These are the host's dead. The Varokh hold that a soul no Shaman calls home wanders outside the totem's land, where the hungry spirits hunt, and nobody called these. The hungry spirits hunt at its edge, which is why the field is thin and not worse: a body found there in the thaw is not a drowning.

## How It Is Treated

The garrison of the watchtower on the causeway counts the smoke from the forest and does not look at the marsh. Officers who know their trade avoid fighting near it. The practice of driving an enemy onto a field like it is known in Moktur and not discussed.

The half-Varokh villages of the border leave food at the marsh's edge in the winter months. The garrison does not see them do it.

The Varokh villages of Vandstein that sent the host keep an empty place for each of the dead and recite the names with nothing after them. A Shaman cannot cross the passes to call them without being taken for a raider.

## Who Goes

- **Varokh Shamans**, who would call their dead home and need a safe-conduct to do it.
- **Deserters**, who hide in the reeds because the patrols will not follow.
- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] surveyors**, who want to measure it.

## Hooks

- A Varokh Shaman of [Gargrund]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} asks a party bound north to carry her to the marsh, and a garrison officer would rather the dead stay where they are.
- A deserter's account of the reeds names a place where the ground is firm, and the firm ground is where the host stood last.
