## Overview

In the Province of Moktur the _therkos_ (governor) is a soldier, and some _therkoi_ behave as semi-independent warlords whose loyalty to the capital is a matter of convenience as much as conviction. The province is mountainous, lies on the [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]] border and answers for the defense of the frontier against Varokh raids and incursions, and its military _therkoi_ command wide autonomy. The Province of Moktur holds the land of [[place-moktur|Moktur]], and its _therkos_ sits at [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]]. The [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Empire]] organized the province as a march when it cut [[lore-legionsroad|the Legions' Road]] north into the Vristwald, and the marshes below its passes saw [[lore-blackmarshbtl|the Battle of the Black Marsh]].

## Character

Moktur is the most martial province of the empire. Its people are proud, self-reliant and faintly contemptuous of the soft southerners of [[place-alyssa|Alessa]] and [[place-hylen|Hilen]]. Military service here is expected as well as honored, and the province produces the empire's best soldiers: its legions are the most battle-tested in [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|the Vilérian military]].

The frontier is not a clean line. Varokh communities sit within Moktur's nominal borders, and some Mokturan border lords have more in common with the chiefs across the line than with the _kurthoi_ (senators) in [[place-orzara|Orzara]]. Intermarriage, trade and occasional truces make a border culture that neither side fully controls. A militia elder explains it to a new magistrate: "You will be told the Varokh are on the other side. Some of them are, and some are your neighbors, and the ones across the line have cousins on this side. Learn who is whose before you pass a judgment."

## Economy

Beyond the mines and timber described under [[place-moktur|Moktur]], the province profits from the overland trade routes that pass through its mountain passes. Tolls and customs duties are a major source of revenue for the military _therkoi_.

## Notable Features

- Semi-autonomous military _therkoi_ with their own armies
- A hybrid border culture blending Vilérian and Varokh traditions

## Life in the Province

Moktur is the wild end of the empire: mountains, forested hills, fast cold rivers and a short, hard growing season. It is the province for mines, logging, hunting and frontier soldiering, and the one place inside the empire where the wilderness, and the people who live by it, lie beyond the capital's reach.

Power belongs to the military _therkoi_ and the border lords, some little better than warlords, and their loyalty to Orzara is a matter of convenience. A settlement here is walled, watchful, and loyal first to whoever can defend it.

Trouble in Moktur is frontier trouble: raids and reprisals across the Vristwald march, feuds between border lords, contested mining claims, smuggling over the passes, monsters and Varokh war-bands in the high country, and the murky politics of a half-Vilérian, half-Varokh borderland. Where the Varokh tribes themselves are meant, rather than the communities inside the border, see [[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|the tribes of Vristwald]].

What is abundant is metal, timber, game and danger. What is scarce is imperial authority, soft living and trust.

## Current Situation {#present}

The capital can neither lose Moktur nor pay to hold it, and the province governs itself in the gap. The _therkos_, [[being-thlmrsnlmn|Tholmaras Nelamen]], has held it from [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]] for eleven years. He pays some twelve hundred Varokh-born auxiliaries from the tolls of [[place-korrast|Korras]] and the province's share of the metal, on the first of every month, and they are sworn to him by name. The legion at [[place-dolvar|Dolvar]] under the _vandraks_ [[being-sthrskrmrs|Satheras Kiramaris]] is paid when the treasury's coin arrives. The capital's letters take longer to come back than to go, and the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]] have sent a clerk north to measure the province's road.

The province's other quarrels are the frontier's.

- **The truce.** Dolvar keeps the sons of the Vandstein War Chiefs as surety, and the envoys come down to look at them at the truce market each summer.
- **The gate.** The border lords bid for the command of Korras each spring, and a lord refused this year means to take it.
- **The forest.** [[place-pinnhald|Pinnhald]]'s headmen keep the town's customs hard, and a raft of mast timber and its four raftsmen have vanished on the spring run.
- **The metal.** Every power in the province wants a share of [[place-ferrovan|Zargonara]]'s silver, a clerk of the revenue finds its books too tidy, and the children of [[place-stennvold|Stennvold]]'s furnace crews are dying of a winter cough.

## Commerce and Currency

The Province of Moktur uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian currency]] (Solden / Ardin / Ogdel) as its standard currency, with full access to the paper-script system through [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]] moneylenders chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]]. Imperial taxation is administered by [[affiliation-tolvan|the Tolvan]] and revenues flow to [[affiliation-tarmex|the Tarmeks Orzan]]. See [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian Currency]] for the full system.

## History {#history}

Moktur became a march when the Empire went north. From about 120 to 180 the legions cut a road out of the province over the Vandstein highlands into the Vristwald and raised a chain of forward posts along it, and the province's valleys filled with the road's garrisons ([[lore-legionsroad|The Legions' Road]]). From about 200 to 260 its _therkoi_ bought the War Chiefs nearest the road with iron and silver, and the kindreds who followed those chiefs south settled on land the Empire granted them. They are the Varokh of Moktur, and the half-Varokh towns of [[place-pinnhald|Pinnhald]] and [[place-stennvold|Stennvold]] began with them. About 291 a legion of about five thousand marched up the road to burn Waldburg, and the first common muster of the forest took it at the ford of the Moldún; the province lost its field army in an afternoon ([[lore-skathhrethlss|The Loss of the Legion at Skathhreth]]). About 340 the Empire gave the forward posts up one by one, and the frontier fell back to Moktur's mountains.

Moktur's legions made the reigning house. When the Orzas Barnakis died in 389 they acclaimed their _felzos_, Makandas, and marched south ([[lore-brkensuccsn|The Broken Succession]]). About 401 they beat the Senate's army on the Olkassa estate in Hilen and lost half their number doing it ([[lore-olcassabtl|The Battle of Olkassa]]), and about 406 Makandas took Orzara and founded House Helarkales ([[lore-restorehouse|The Restoration]]). He rewarded the northern legions, and the province keeps the memory that its _felzos_ made the house.

The Drift began here. After [[lore-threewinters|the Three Winters]] of 512 to 514, Varokh war-bands came over the Vandstein passes from 515 to 528 for cattle and grain, the valleys of the north emptied, and [[place-dolvar|Dolvar]] was raised to a legion fortress ([[lore-hungerraids|The Hunger Raids]]). In 518 a war-band burned the camp of the [Pelsan Hilvan]{.name data-type="affiliation"} and carried off its eagle. In the spring of 520 the Karsan Soldan Korvan closed the valley above the host that carried it and drove the host into the marsh below, where it died to the last man ([[lore-blackmarshbtl|The Battle of the Black Marsh]]). The northern revenue failed in those years, and the Empire counts the raids among the causes of its decline ([[lore-empirdclne|The Decline of the Vilérian Empire]]). From 660, when the Nordmen took Stormveld and needed thralls, captives taken in the Moktur passes went down the Vristwald's rivers to Nordmen buyers by the boatload ([[lore-thrallrivers|The Thrall Rivers]]).

The watchtowers of the northern march count the smoke at dusk, and their sergeants teach recruits that the count was right at the Black Marsh. The field itself is [[place-blackmarshfld|the Blood-field of the Black Marsh]].

- **The pay chest.** The _tarkelos_ at [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]] needs the month's coin brought down from [[place-korrast|Korras]] past a war-band, because the auxiliaries have never been paid late.
- **The surveyor.** The clerk of the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]] measuring the province's road wants a guard, because the last man sent on the errand came back with a broken leg.
