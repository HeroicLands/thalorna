## Overview {#overview}

In a province where towns are small, Tirellan is the largest: about fifteen thousand people, the capital of [[affiliation-provncmktr|Moktur]] and the seat of its military _therkos_ (governor). It belongs to the Province of Moktur, a land of mines, timber and fortified garrisons on the [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]] march, and it is walled like everything else in [[place-moktur|Moktur]].

A farmer from one of the villages on the Tirellan circuit, in town to sell a season's wool, describes the city to a neighbor as the place where the _therkos_'s word is law. For the villages themselves the person who matters is the healer-priest [[being-azendsdmtr|Azendas Damator]], Senior Priest of [[affiliation-lizelas|Lizelas]] in the Tirellan region, who takes the blessing of the fields and the harvest thanksgiving out to the farming communities around the city, among them [[place-mylanthos|Milanthos]].

## Arriving {#arriving}

[[place-orzara|Orzara]] lies twenty days south by the posting-road.

The road from the south climbs out of the lowlands into a long valley of stone-walled fields, sheepfolds and barley, and for its last two days it runs between the farms of the Tirellan circuit. Every half-day there is a posting-station, a walled yard with a tower, a stable and a bench for the post-master, and every mile there is a milestone. A traveler from the capital notices the milestones first. In the rest of the empire they give the distance to Orzara. In Moktur the old figures have been cut away with a chisel, and new ones count the miles to Tirellan. The post-masters say the road was measured again after the hunger raids, when half its stones lay in the ditches, and that a carter in Moktur needs to know how far he is from Tirellan, not from a city he has never seen.

The city stands on a spur of gray rock where two cold rivers meet, and the valley road climbs the spur in three turns under the wall. The wall is gray stone, twice the height of the one at [[place-aravantia|Aravantara]], with a square tower every fifty paces, and every tower carries an iron beacon-basket on a pole. On the ridges above the city a second line of towers runs north toward the frontier, each in sight of the next, and on a clear evening the smoke of five of them shows from the South Gate. The towers are how word of a war-band reaches Tirellan a day before the riders who bring it.

At the top of the spur stands the [Keep]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, the fort the city grew around. It was a legion's camp when the empire first came north, and its walls are still the camp's: a square of stone with rounded corners, a gate in each side, and a ditch outside kept clean and deep. The _therkos_ holds the province from it. Below the Keep the [Horse Yards]{.name data-type="place/site"} run down the north slope in terraces, long stone stables with a training ground at the foot, and they belong to the _therkos_'s auxiliaries. The riders are [[lore-varokhiclt|Varokh]]-born, from the half-Varokh towns and hamlets of the province, and they wear the _therkos_'s colors, gray and black, and not the empire's red.

The rest of Tirellan is packed tight inside the wall. Its streets are steep, narrow and paved with slabs, its houses are stone below and timber above, with deep eaves against the snow, and every house of standing has a square tower of its own at the street corner. The towers are the townhouses of the border lords, and a lord's men sleep in their lower floors. [[being-korvathosvol|Korvathos Volkemates]] keeps one of them, and his household's yard is where the young men of the house learn the spear. The small forum lies on the one level ground below the Keep, with the provincial temple of the [[affiliation-imperialcult|Imperial Cult]] on its south side, the [Temple of Wisdom]{.name data-type="place/structure"} of [[affiliation-marendas|Marendas]] on its east, and the hall of the town council on its west.

Off the forum, in a court of its own, stands the [Eagle Shrine]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, a _nekaimon_ (shrine) of dark stone no bigger than a farmhouse. It holds the eagle of the Fourth, the legion whose camp was burned in the hunger raids and whose eagle was carried north into the forest and brought back out of the reeds at [[lore-blackmarshbtl|the Battle of the Black Marsh]]. The Fourth's name was struck from the roll in the same year, as the army's manual requires, and its eagle has stood in the shrine ever since, a brass-and-gilt bird on a pole in a niche lit by one lamp. Soldiers of the north touch the doorpost on their way past, and recruits are brought to see it before they take the oath.

## What Tirellan Lives By

Tirellan lives by the valley, the garrison and the road. The valley gives wool first. The farms of the circuit send their fleeces in at shearing, and the [Wool Hall]{.name data-type="place/structure"} outside the South Gate, a long barn on stone piers, holds the fair where the buyers for the weavers of the south take them. The valley also gives barley, hard wheat in the good years, cheese, hides and mutton, and the city eats from it. The growing season is short. A wet summer in the valley is a hungry winter in the city, and the granaries under the Keep are kept full against that before anything else is paid for.

The garrison gives the city its trades. Armorers, saddlers, farriers, bowyers, tanners and the makers of felt for saddle-cloths and winter boots work in the lanes below the Horse Yards, and the inns of the North Gate live on soldiers going up to the frontier and coming down from it. The legion at [[place-dolvar|Dolvar]] draws its grain, its leather and its remounts through Tirellan, and its quartermasters keep a house by the North Gate.

The road brings the metal. Twice a month a convoy of ingots comes down from the furnaces of [[place-stennvold|Stennvold]] under guard, iron and copper in open wagons and silver in locked chests, and stops at the [Weighing House]{.name data-type="place/structure"} below the Keep. There the imperial revenue takes the emperor's share and the _therkos_'s officers take the province's, each on its own scale, and the convoy goes on south with fewer chests than it came in with. The tolls of [[place-korrast|Korras]] come down the other road, in coin, to the _therkos_'s strongrooms.

The road south is the city's complaint. The province keeps its own stretch well, its bridges sound and its stations manned. Beyond the provincial boundary the imperial road is patched, its fallen bridges are timbered over, and fewer merchant caravans come up it each year. The weavers' buyers still come for the wool. The cloth merchants, spice-sellers and wine-shippers who once filled the inns of the South Gate every summer come rarely now, and the city drinks its own barley beer.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order

The _therkos_ holds the province in the emperor's name, commanding its garrison, its revenue and its courts and answering to him alone. The present _therkos_ is [[being-thlmrsnlmn|Tholmaras Nelamen]], a soldier of a Mokturan provincial family named for its estate of [Nelamenna]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} under the snows, who commanded a cohort on the frontier before he was given the province eleven years ago. He holds his audience in the hall of the Keep at first light, standing, in a soldier's cloak, and hears petitions, appeals from the town benches and disputes between border lords in the same hour. Some of Moktur's _therkoi_ behave as semi-independent lords, and in Tirellan the phrase has two plain meanings.

The first is the auxiliaries. The legions are paid by the treasury in the capital. The _therkos_'s auxiliaries, some twelve hundred Varokh-born horse, slingers and light foot, are paid by the _therkos_ himself, in coin, on the first day of every month, in the yard of the Keep, from the tolls of Korras and the province's share of the metal, and they are sworn to him by name. The riders are paid on the day. The legion at Dolvar is paid when the treasury's coin arrives.

The second is the road. The _vandraks_ (legion commander) at Dolvar commands the legion stationed in the province and is the _therkos_'s deputy in the field, and he reports to the capital by the provincial road, which runs through Tirellan. Its posting-stations are kept by the _pozerus_ (warden of the roads), who is the _therkos_'s officer, and its post-masters wear the _therkos_'s gray. Every letter from the north to the capital, the _vandraks_'s and the revenue officer's included, passes through a station the _therkos_ keeps.

The province's other officers have their rooms in the Keep and round the forum:

- **The _karezos_** (revenue officer) takes the emperor's share at the Weighing House and reports past the _therkos_ to the emperor, by the same road.
- **The _tarkelos_** (treasurer) keeps the provincial treasury in the strongrooms under the Keep. The auxiliaries' pay comes out of them.
- **The _zoralus_** (keeper of the peace) commands the province's urban cohorts. The city watch of Tirellan is one of them, and it is where [[being-xrphnklmkns|Zerofon Kalmakanes]] served before he went to the province's great prison above [[place-sanctara|Ulthora]].
- **The _pozerus_** keeps the roads, the posting-stations and the bridges, and levies the work to keep them from the valley's farms.
- **The _zalvos_ of the province** presides in the provincial temple over the rites that bind Moktur to the capital. On the state's days the _therkos_ makes the offering before the Orzas's image with the border lords behind him, and the city watches to see which lords have come.

Tirellan is also a chartered town, with two _tavemoi_ (magistrates) elected each year and a council of _kurthaloi_ (councilors) who answer in their own persons for the town's taxes. Three _kurthoi_ (senators) of the province's senatorial families keep their houses in the city and hold the first seats of that council, and between them they govern the town. The merchant houses fill most of the other seats, the [Damzanis]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} among them, whose son [[being-azatodmzns|Azato Damzanis]] is the city's best-known scholar.

Order in the streets rests on the towers. A shopkeeper robbed in the night goes in the morning to the tower at the head of his street, and the border lord whose men sleep in it sends them to find the thief, because a lord whose street is not safe loses its rents and its custom. A quarrel between two towers goes to the _tavemoi_, and one between two lords goes to the _therkos_'s audience. The watch keeps the gates, the forum and the market. An auxiliary in trouble belongs to the _therkos_ alone: the town's benches do not try his riders, and nobody in Tirellan has asked them to.

## Politics {#present}

Tirellan is the province in small, governed by a man the capital neither commands nor replaces. Tholmaras Nelamen pays his riders on the day, keeps the posting-road with his own officers, and holds the frontier quiet, and the city counts that as loyalty. The legion at [[place-dolvar|Dolvar]] is paid when the treasury's coin arrives. Letters from the south reach the city in eleven days and answers take thirty to come back, and a cloth merchant of the capital wants to know where his wait. A clerk of the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]] has come up to measure the road and write down where its milestones stand, and the _pozerus_ is courteous to him. In the forum the border lords watch one another at the state's offerings, and someone leaves wine and red cloth in the Eagle Shrine by night for the cashiered Fourth.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The inns of the North Gate take soldiers, couriers and anyone going up to the frontier. The best of them is the [Counted Smoke]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, whose host is a veteran of the towers and keeps a slate by the fire on which the evening's smoke from the ridge is chalked, so that a guest knows before supper whether the road north is open. Its rooms are clean and cold, and its stable is good.

The carters' inns outside the South Gate are cheaper, emptier and closer to the Wool Hall, and in the weeks of the wool fair every bed in them is taken. Inside the wall, a party carrying a letter to a border lord lodges in his tower as a guest, eats at his table with his men, and goes nowhere in the city without a man of his household beside it. The lord means it as courtesy, and it is also how he learns what his guests do.

## History {#history}

The Keep was a legion's camp when the empire first came north, and the city grew around it on the spur where the two rivers meet. The hunger raids stripped the valleys, threw the road's milestones into the ditches and burned the Fourth's camp. A legion won the Fourth's eagle back out of the reeds at [[lore-blackmarshbtl|the Battle of the Black Marsh]] in 520, the Fourth's name was struck from the roll that year, and its eagle went into the shrine off the forum. When the road was measured again the milestones were recut to count the miles to Tirellan. Through the long decline the imperial road south of the province has fallen into patches and timber, and the southern merchants who once filled the inns of the South Gate come rarely. Tholmaras Nelamen was given the province eleven years ago from command of a frontier cohort.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The pay chest.** The month's coin for the auxiliaries comes down from Korras under the _therkos_'s guard, and this month the guard is needed against a war-band on the march. The _tarkelos_ hires a party to bring the chest to the Keep by the first of the month, because the riders are paid on that day and have never once been paid late.
- **The offering at the shrine.** Someone has been leaving offerings in the Eagle Shrine by night: a cup of wine and a strip of red cloth, the old color of the Fourth. The keeper of the shrine wants to know who. The families of the Fourth's cashiered officers, whose names went off the roll with the legion's, want the offerings left alone.
- **The surveyor.** A clerk of the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]] has come up from the capital to measure the province's road and write down where its milestones stand. The _pozerus_ is courteous to him. The clerk asks a party to guard him on the road, because the last man sent on the same errand came back with a broken leg and no notes.
- **The slow reply.** A cloth merchant of the capital who has kept a factor in Tirellan for twenty years finds that his letters reach Orzara in eleven days and his factor's answers come back in thirty. He wants a party to ride the road with his next letter and see where it waits.

# The Truth of Tirellan {#truth .secret}

Tholmaras Nelamen has been recalled. Last autumn the chancery in Orzara sent a letter of recall under the old Orzas's seal, naming a _kurthos_ of the capital as the next _therkos_ and ordering Tholmaras south to give account of the province's revenue. The letter came up the road in the post-bag. At the posting-station on the provincial boundary the post-master signed for the bag, as post-masters do, and the receipt went back south. The letter went on to the Keep and into the _therkos_'s strongroom, and its seal has not been broken. Tholmaras has not answered it, because in law he has not received it. His routine reports go south every month as before, and they say nothing of it.

The capital holds the receipt and believes the letter delivered. It is waiting to see whether he comes. The _kurthos_ named to succeed him is waiting in Orzara for word that the province is ready to receive him, and no word comes. The chancery, with the Orzas failing and the succession in doubt, has found nobody willing to ride north and ask why.

The _vandraks_ at Dolvar, [[being-sthrskrmrs|Satheras Kiramaris]], knows. By custom a recall is copied to the commander of the legion with an order to see it carried out, and his copy came by the legion's own courier. He has not acted on it. Half his legion could reach Tirellan in a week, and it would meet twelve hundred auxiliaries who are paid on the day and sworn to the man they would be asked to give up. If the legion marched south, the frontier towers would stand empty in the raiding season. And through the last two winters, when the treasury's coin did not come, the legion was paid from the _therkos_'s strongrooms against the _vandraks_'s receipt. So the two men have said nothing to each other, the capital has said nothing to either, and the province goes on.

The road does the rest. Every dispatch from Dolvar to the capital is opened at the station below the North Gate, read by the _pozerus_'s clerk, resealed with a copy of the _vandraks_'s seal cut by a Tirellan engraver, and sent on a day late. Satheras has known this for years and writes accordingly. The cloth merchant's letters wait in the same room. The recall in the strongroom is the one thing in Tirellan that could force the matter: whoever brings it into the forum and reads it aloud makes the _therkos_ obey or refuse in public, and the capital can do nothing about either.
