## Overview

In a province where towns are small, Tirellan is the largest: about fifteen thousand people, the capital of the [[affiliation-provncmktr|Province of Moktur]] and the seat of its military _therkos_ (governor). It belongs to the Province of Moktur, a land of mines, timber and fortified garrisons on the [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]] march, and it is walled like everything else in [[place-moktur|Moktur]].

A farmer from one of the villages on the Tirellan circuit, in town to sell a season's wool, describes the city to a neighbor as the place where the _therkos_'s word is law. For the villages themselves the person who matters is the healer-priest [[being-azendsdmtr|Azendas Damator]], Senior Priest of the [[affiliation-lizelas|Faith of Lizelas]] in the Tirellan region, who takes the blessing of the fields and the harvest thanksgiving out to the farming communities around the city, among them [[place-mylanthos|Milanthos]].
