## Overview

Pinnhald is a logging town of about three thousand on the forested slopes of Moktur, where timber is felled and floated down the mountain rivers toward the lowland yards and the shipwrights of [[affiliation-provincvld|Vald]]. A raftsman tying off at the town landing tells you who to ask for when you arrive: the headmen, and after them the timber-lords who hold the cutting-rights.

The people are half-[[lore-varokhiclt|Varokh]]. They speak the border patois and keep customs the southern priests frown on. The imperial chancery counts Pinnhald Vylarian, but the town answers first to its own headmen and to the timber-lords.
