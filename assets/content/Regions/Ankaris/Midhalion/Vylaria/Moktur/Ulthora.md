## Overview

Most of [[place-moktur|Moktur]]'s towns are garrisons, mines or timber camps. Ulthora is a temple town, about two thousand people among the heights of Moktur, the mountainous northern shield of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]], a land of mines, timber and fortified valleys on the [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]] march.

A watchtower sergeant on the high road, asked which of the province's towns he would choose to be posted to, names Ulthora without hesitating. Everything else on his list is a place to hold or a place to dig, and Ulthora is the one where a soldier's work is not the first thing anyone thinks of.

## Arriving

The high road reaches Ulthora along the side of a mountain, and the town appears all at once round a shoulder of rock: a single street climbing a steep spur in steps and ramps, with houses of pale stone on either side and a temple at the top. The street is the [Shrine Stair]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, and it is most of the town. It begins at a gate in the town wall by the road and climbs for a quarter of a mile, and along it, between the inns, the candle-sellers and the carvers of votive figures, stand the shrines of more faiths than any other street in the province holds.

At the foot of the Stair, by the gate, is the shrine of [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]], where the miners and furnace-men of [[place-ferrovan|Zargonara]] and [[place-stennvold|Stennvold]] hang small iron anvils from the rafters in thanks for a season without a fall. Above it is a shrine of [[affiliation-lizelas|Lizelas]], and above that a shrine of [[affiliation-karnavos|Karnavos]], the Wild Beast, a cave in the rock with a pair of great antlers over its mouth. The Karnavos shrine is the one the [[lore-varokhiclt|Varokh]] come to. The people of the half-Varokh towns and hamlets of the province walk to Ulthora once in their lives, in summer, and hang a token of their kindred's totem in the cave, a bear's claw or a heron's feather or a strip of lynx fur, and the priests of Karnavos let them. The antlers are hung so thick with tokens that the bone does not show.

Halfway up the Stair the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian]] faith has its own fire-shrine, kept by a priest from the south for the merchants of that faith who come through the town, and next to it stands the house of the Teghumod merchants of the [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Atarzád]], a tall stone house with a plain door and a fire kept burning in its court, with no image of any kind. The Teghumod keep a factor there to buy the province's copper and silver. Above them are shrines of [[affiliation-pozeras|Pozeras]] for travelers and of [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]] for the dead, and at the top of the Stair, on a terrace cut out of the mountain, stands the temple of [[affiliation-brazemas|Brazemas]] the Thunderer, a long hall of dark stone with a bronze roof that the lightning strikes most summers. The town says the god takes his offering that way.

Above the temple, on the crag that overhangs the town, is [Kezrora]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, the province's great prison: a square stone keep with no windows on the side that faces the town, a single gate reached by a road cut in the rock, and a wall round it that a goat could not climb. The town looks up at it every day and does not talk about it.

## What Ulthora Lives By

Ulthora lives by the faithful. The pilgrims who climb the Stair buy candles, votive figures, offerings and lodging, and the priests of the shrines buy bread, oil, wax and wine. The miners of the province come up after a bad season to make offerings at the Forge shrine, the Varokh come in summer, and in the weeks round the storm-god's festival at midsummer the Stair is full from gate to temple and the inns sleep three to a bed. The carvers of Ulthora make the votive figures, small men and beasts of pine and box, and they are the best in the province.

The town's second trade is the prison. Kezrora's guards, cooks and carters live in the town and buy in its shops, and its supplies go up the rock road every morning on mules. The Teghumod factor is the third: twice a year he buys bar copper and silver from the lessees' agents in the house on the Stair and sends it south under his own guard.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order

Ulthora is a chartered town with two _tavemoi_ (magistrates) and a council, and the priests of the shrines hold most of its seats. The high priest of Brazemas presides over the council in practice, and the town's peace is the temples' business: a pilgrim robbed on the Stair goes to the shrine he came to visit, and the priests between them find the thief, since a town that robs its pilgrims loses them.

Kezrora answers to the province, not to the town. Its warden, [Nethoren Melvarandis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, is a _kurthos_'s cousin appointed by the court at Tirellan, who visits the prison once a month and lives in the town in the best house below the temple. Its jailer, who runs it day to day and has for eighteen years, is [[being-xrphnklmkns|Zerofon Kalmakanes]], who came to it from the city watch of [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]]. The prison holds the province's long-term prisoners, debtors to the revenue and men condemned to the mines who are too dangerous to send there. Its inner keep is held by [[affiliation-sekran|the Sekran]], the Arcane Inquisition of the Thordimalon, which keeps its prisoners for this part of the north there under its own seal. Zerofon keeps the keys to every door in Kezrora but the inner one.

## Current Situation {#present}

The shrines live on the pilgrims, and the prison on the crag lives on silence. The council of priests under the high priest of Brazemas must find the Varokh pilgrim's token taken from the Karnavos shrine before his kindred come to look for it. In Kezrora a guard is selling a letter smuggled out by a prisoner who claims to be held without trial, and the jailer, [[being-xrphnklmkns|Zerofon Kalmakanes]], wants the guard named. The warden, Nethoren Melvarandis, visits once a month. The inner keep is the [[affiliation-sekran|Sekran]]'s, and Zerofon keeps the key to every door but that one.

## Where a Party Lodges

The inns are on the Stair, one above the next, and the higher an inn stands the more it charges and the less it smells of the road. The [Lightning Bell]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, just below the temple terrace, is the best, with a balcony over the town. The [Antler]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, below the Karnavos shrine, takes the Varokh pilgrims, and its keeper speaks the forest tongue.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The token.** A Varokh pilgrim's token has been taken from the antlers at the Karnavos shrine, and his kindred hold that a stolen totem-token is a stolen bond. The priests want it found before the pilgrim's kinsmen come to look for it.
- **The prisoner's letter.** A guard of Kezrora offers to sell a party a letter smuggled out of the prison, written by a prisoner who claims to be held without trial. Zerofon, who does not allow letters out, wants the guard named.
- **The factor's guard.** The Teghumod factor needs men to escort his copper and silver south, because his own guard has fallen ill. He pays well and asks no questions about who his escort are, and he expects the same courtesy in return.

# The Truth of Ulthora {#truth .secret}

The inner keep of Kezrora holds a Varokh Shaman. Twelve years ago [Vithwin]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Shaman of [Gargrund]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, one of the [[place-vandstein|Vandstein]] villages that lost their sons in [[place-blackmarshfld|the Black Marsh]], came over the passes alone in winter to call those dead home. A patrol from [[place-dolvar|Dolvar]] took her on the march as a raider. What the soldiers saw her do when they took her, they reported to the _vandraks_, and the _vandraks_ of the day reported it to the Sekran, and the Sekran's men came and took her to Ulthora as an unregistered practitioner of arcane arts. She has been in the inner keep since. Gargrund believes she died in the snow, and it has another Shaman now.

The Sekran's register for Kezrora does not carry her name. It carries a number, because her name in her own tongue was never written down by anyone who heard it. Zerofon's own logbook has the number too, and beside it, in his hand, the name Vithwin, which he learned from her in the second year by asking every day. He does not have the key to the inner keep, but he feeds its prisoners, and he has spoken with her through the door every week for ten years. He believes she is held against the law, since the Kandrol gives the Sekran its powers over the empire's subjects and she is not one, and he has written to the warden saying so three times. Nethoren Melvarandis has sent the letters on to nobody.

The prisoner who claims to be held without trial is not Vithwin, who cannot write Vilari. The letter is from a man in the outer prison who heard the guards talk of her and thought her story would sell. Zerofon wants the guard named because a guard who sells one letter sells the next, and the next may be the one that tells Gargrund where its Shaman is.
