## Overview

Edrenburg is a town of [[place-moktur|Moktur]] on the edge of [[place-vrystwald|Vrystwald]], where the forest meets the roads to [[place-vylariargn|Vylaria]], and its name states what it is: _edr-_, a hedge, the bound set about a holding, joined by _-en-_ to _-burg_, a stronghold. Vrystwald has no towns, and Edrenburg is not one of its villages: it is one of the half-Varokh border towns inside the province, like [[place-pinnhald|Pinnhald]], where Varokh families kept the name their ground already had and the empire built a garrison beside it. Varokh and Vylarian business meet there, and a Vylarian merchant can count on friends in the garrison.

## The Town's Trade

Edrenburg's taverns are where a con man finds marks and a merchant finds dice, and its garrison is what turns a quarrel at the dice into a pursuit. [[being-vndrcgrwld|Vandaric yra Garwald wur Fródhaven]] learned that at Edrenburg: he cheated the wrong man at dice there, a Vylarian merchant with connections to the garrison, and fled into the pines with soldiers on his heels and a price on his head. The merchant has not forgotten him, and there may be warrants under several names waiting for him in more than one border town.
