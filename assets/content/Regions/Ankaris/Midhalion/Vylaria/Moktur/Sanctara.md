## Overview

Most of Moktur's towns are garrisons, mines or timber camps. Sanctara is a temple town, about two thousand people among the heights of [[place-moktur|Moktur]], the mountainous northern shield of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], a land of mines, timber and fortified valleys on the Vrystwald march.

A watchtower sergeant on the high road, asked which of the province's towns he would choose to be posted to, names Sanctara without hesitating. Everything else on his list is a place to hold or a place to dig, and Sanctara is the one where a soldier's work is not the first thing anyone thinks of.
