## Overview

Korrast commands one of the principal passes through [[affiliation-provncmktr|Moktur]]'s mountains – a fortified toll-town grown fat on the customs levied on the overland trade that crosses between the empire and the lands beyond. Its garrison and its toll-keepers answer to the military governor, and control of Korrast's tolls is a prize worth killing for in Moktur's rough politics; more than one border-lord has risen or fallen on who holds the gate.
