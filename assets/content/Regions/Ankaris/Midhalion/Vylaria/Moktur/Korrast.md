## Overview

Korrast commands one of the principal passes through [[affiliation-provncmktr|Moktur]]'s mountains, and the first thing a carter learns about it is the price of getting through. The town of about five thousand is a fortified toll-town grown fat on the customs levied on the overland trade between the empire and the lands beyond.

Its garrison and its toll-keepers answer to the military governor. Control of Korrast's tolls is a prize worth killing for in Moktur's rough politics, and more than one border lord has risen or fallen on who holds the gate. A carter who has paid at Korrast for years advises a younger one to be civil to the toll-keepers and to remember the name of whoever is in command that season, because it changes.
