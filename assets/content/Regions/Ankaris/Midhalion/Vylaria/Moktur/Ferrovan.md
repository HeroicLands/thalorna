## Overview

Iron, copper and silver come down to the imperial forges from the lodes above Ferrovan, the chief of [[affiliation-provncmktr|Moktur]]'s mining settlements, a smoke-stained town of about seven thousand clinging to the slopes beneath them. Its people are miners, smelters, and the hard overseers who drive them. Its wealth is real but grim.

A muleteer who hauls ore down from the lodes to the furnaces of [[place-stennvold|Stennvold]] tells a new driver to keep his mouth shut about the silver: "Everyone from the tax-farmers to the governors wants a share of it, and a carter who knows too much becomes a witness." The silver draws the close attention of imperial tax-farmers and the military governors alike, and control of Ferrovan's output is a perennial prize in Moktur's rough politics.
