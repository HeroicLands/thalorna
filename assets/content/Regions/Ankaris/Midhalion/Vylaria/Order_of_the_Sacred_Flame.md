## Overview

The Order of the Sacred Flame is a body of knights who keep a lamp. They are the knights of the [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]], about four hundred sworn knights with their novices, sergeants and grooms, some sixteen hundred men in all, and among the finest heavy cavalry in the western part of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]]. They hold the walls and gates of the holy city of [[place-nartum|Nartun]], keep chapels at the kingdom's borders, and ride as the heavy horse of the king's host. Their faith is the faith of [[lore-ustarondty|Ústaron]], the Forge-Lord, which they keep under its old name for the god's fire, the Flame.

An elder knight, receiving a new novice at the gate of the chapter house, gives him the order's whole rule before he has unpacked: "The Flame in the chapel never goes out. The gate of the city never opens to an enemy. Every other thing you will learn here serves those two."

## The Lamp

Every house of the order keeps a lamp that is never let go out, and the lamp is the order's sign and its rite. A chapel is a plain hall of whitewashed stone with one window toward the road and the lamp in it, so that the order's houses are known at night by a single steady light. The knights keep the lamp in turns, one at a time, through every night of the year, and a knight's oath is sworn to it.

The rites are said in Nartun's ritual dialect, the oldest form of [[skill-vylarilng|Vylari]] still spoken, and in that dialect Ústaron's fire is _karvila_, the Flame. In the empire the god's orthodox faith calls itself [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]], and its own hearths and anvils are its signs. The order shares that faith in everything but the word, and its knights pray in the Forge-priests' temples when they travel. The word puts it at odds with [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]], whose priests claim the Flame as their faith's own name and call the knights the other Flame. The Black Flame is the enemy the order was raised against, and its knights hold that a faith which burns for the joy of burning has stolen the god's word.

## Joining the Order

The order takes boys of fourteen or fifteen, most of them younger sons of the priest-noble families and of the lay barons who hold temple forest, and a few freemen's sons whom an elder knight has sponsored for a deed. A novice serves three years in the chapter house over the main gate of the holy city: he grooms, carries, learns horse and lance, and learns the rites by heart in the ritual dialect.

At the end of the third year comes the vigil. The novice keeps the chapter house lamp alone from sunset to sunrise, and if it is still burning at dawn, he swears his oath to it before the Master and the assembled knights: to keep the Flame, to hold the gate, and to obey the Master and the Holy Mother. He receives a white surcoat with the lamp on its breast and is a Knight of the Flame for life.

A knight who breaks his oath is Forsworn. His own small lamp, lit for him on the night of his vigil and kept in the chapter house with every other knight's, is put out before the assembled knights, his name is read aloud once, and the order never says it again. A Forsworn knight is put out of the order's houses, and in the kingdom no priest-noble family will receive him.

## The Order's Houses

The chapter house stands over the main gate of the holy city, a tall block of gray stone with stables in the gate-passage below and the order's lamp in a window over the arch, where it is the first light a traveler on the road sees of Nartun. The Master lives there, with the council of elder knights and about a hundred knights and their novices.

The chapels stand at the kingdom's borders and at the crossings of its forest roads, each kept by an elder knight called the Keeper of the Lamp with a dozen knights or fewer. The best known stands on the kingdom's bank of the border stream below the Hilen march-town of [[place-ashenmere|Ashenmere]], directly opposite a forge-shrine of the Sacred Forge, and the stretch of water between the chapel lamp and the forge-fire is called the Two Lights.

## Who Commands

The Master of the Order is an elder knight chosen for life by the council of elder knights and confirmed by the Holy Mother, who may refuse him. He answers to the Holy Mother and the high priesthood, and he serves the king in the field. In war the order rides with the king's host in companies under captains. In peace it answers to the priesthood, and its knights escort the temples' coin between treasuries, guard pilgrims on the holy road, and arrest at a priest's word those whom an examiner has summoned.

## What It Wants Now {#present}

The Order holds the holy city's walls for a crown and a priesthood that are moving apart. Its Master, [[being-sldrsplvrn|Saldoros Pelvaren]], answers to the Holy Mother, [[being-ilvnssrkls|Ilvenas Sarkelis]], and serves the king, [[being-klvrsdrns|Kelvaras Daronis]], in the field, and the high priests are gathering votes to judge the king. In the empire the Order's enemy is rising. [[being-trzmsbrkrts|Torzemos Brakarates]], High Flame-Warden of the [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] in Orzara, has drafted a petition for the Forge-Lord's niche in the Temple of the Twelve, and the Sacred Forge's keepers of the niche are the Order's allies however much they wince at its name. The Black Flame calls the Withering a sign against the Order.

# The Truth of the Order {#truth .secret}

The order takes people out of the empire. Knights of the Ashenmere chapel ford the border stream by night and carry worshippers of the Black Flame out of the villages of the Hilen march to be judged in the holy city, and the chapels on the kingdom's other borders do the same where they can. The Master acts on the Holy Mother's warrant. The king has never been shown it, and the order's captains in the king's host are under orders not to speak of the crossings in his hearing.
