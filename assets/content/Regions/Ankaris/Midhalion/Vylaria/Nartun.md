## Overview

The **Holy Mother** has her seat in Nartun, and so does the [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]]: the holy city of the [[place-nartumkngdm|Nartun Kingdom]], the independent Vylari-speaking realm ruled under its priestly aristocracy. With about sixty thousand people it is among the largest cities of the region.

It is a sanctuary city where the sacred and the secular intertwine under priestly rule, and the city whose high priesthood withdrew from imperial obedience about 224 AF ([[lore-nartumrefuse|Nartun Refuses the Empire]]). A priest-noble's secretary, meeting a newly arrived scholar at the gate, tells him not to ask which clerk handles the temple's business and which the crown's: in Nartun the same offices answer for both, and the question only marks the asker as a foreigner.
