## Overview

The Province of Salkalon holds the land of [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]], and its merchant class has the most dealings with the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]], the [[affiliation-ctysttshlns|City-States of Helionis]] and the other maritime powers, which makes Salkalon the most cosmopolitan and commercially minded province of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|empire]]. The _therkos_ (governor) sits at [[place-mercavia|Delalara]].

A customs factor at the Delalara quay, checking a new clerk's manifests, makes the political point in a sentence: "Every cask that crosses this wharf has been taxed by [[place-orzara|Orzara]] and argued over by Delalara. Learn to keep both sides of the account, and learn not to say which side you are on."

## Character

Salkalon is where the old empire meets the new world. The province's port cities bustle with foreign merchants, sailors and travelers, and the province's aristocracy has adapted to this reality more readily than its counterparts elsewhere. The great families made their fortunes in shipping and trade, and they think in terms of markets and margins rather than ancestral land and senatorial tradition.

That commercial outlook sets Salkalon at odds with the conservative faction in [[affiliation-provnchyln|Hilen]] and sometimes with the political establishment in [[affiliation-provinclys|Alessa]]. The province's merchants argue for trade liberalization and engagement with Taruzel, and the capital's politicians see this as dangerously close to collaboration with the enemy.

The loss of Taruzel hit Salkalon hardest. Before the war of independence, its ports were the gateway to eastern trade. Now Taruzelian ships compete with them directly, and its merchants find themselves squeezed between resentment of Taruzelian commerce and dependence on it.

## Notable Features

- The most commercially engaged province, with strong Taruzelian trade connections

## Life in the Province

Salkalon is the empire's seafront: a long worked coast of harbors, shipyards, curing-sheds and fishing fleets, backed by an inland belt of farm estates that feed the ports. The coast lives by the sea, with the navy at [[place-castamar|Kastamar]], trade at Delalara and fish everywhere between. The interior around [[place-terravia|Therkara]] is quiet estate-country, closer in temper to [[place-hylen|Hilen]].

Power is divided three ways among the merchant houses of Delalara, the naval command at Kastamar, and the imperial customs that tax it all. Since Taruzel's secession broke Vilérian sea-supremacy, all three are anxious and at odds, and the constant friction is between merchants who want Taruzelian trade and admirals who want Taruzelian blood.

The province has been raided from the north once. In 523 a crew out of [[lore-southrnfleets|the Southern Fleets]] brought a captured galley through the western strait to the bay of [[place-braldvik|Bralvik]], lost it to a Kastamar squadron whose fire burned the village's boats with it, and went to the imperial oars the next spring; the town still carries their name for it and still bills the admiralty for the boats.

Trouble in Salkalon is maritime: smuggling and customs-running (a [[place-tholkezora|Tholkezora]] specialty), shipping intrigue and insurance fraud, naval politics and ship-money, piracy and wrecking along the lonelier coast, and foreign agents in the cosmopolitan ports.

What is abundant is fish, ships, salt and foreign news. What is scarce is grain, which comes from the interior and from Hilen, and any love for the imperial tax-farmer.

## Current Situation {#present}

The province's three powers want different things from Taruzel, and none of them can afford its wish. The _therkos_ at [[place-mercavia|Delalara]], [[being-krvthsrvthnds|Korvathas Revothandis]], was sent by a Senate that wanted Salkalon governed by a man with no ships of his own, and he receives the shipping houses and the admirals' officers on alternate days.

- **The merchants** want a trade treaty with the Confederation. Their first voice, Ornetha Venthelis of the Venthelis shipping house, sends the houses' petition to the Senate each year, and last year's arrived a month late and opened. Taruzelian and Helionite factors live in Delalara by a yearly waiver that the _therkos_ renews, a fortnight late every time.
- **The admiralty** at [[place-castamar|Kastamar]], under the _vormeks_ Tharkas Gurimandis, wants the war resumed and cannot pay for the fleet it has. Its oarsmen sell their notes at half face and drift away, half its warships lie laid up, and Bralvik's Boat-Bill is still unpaid after two centuries.
- **The customs** are farmed. Solteras Tharozates bid more for the Delalara customs than any _tolar_ ever had, and a cargo can be chalked twice on the same quay.

Behind them all the province's trade passes a strait where the Taruzelian admiral [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]] presses for a toll on every hull. The capital calls the merchants collaborators and the treasury calls the admirals a cost, and the inland estates of [[place-terravia|Therkara]] take the admiralty's notes for their grain.

## Commerce and Currency

The Province of Salkalon uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian currency]] (Solden / Ardin / Ogdel) as its standard currency, with full access to the paper-script system through [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]] moneylenders chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]]. Imperial taxation is administered by [[affiliation-tolvan|the Tolvan]] and revenues flow to [[affiliation-tarmex|the Tarmeks Orzan]]. See [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian Currency]] for the full system.

## History {#history}

Taruzel's trading cities were among the first lands the Republic absorbed, and for most of the Empire's life Salkalon's harbors were its gateway to the eastern trade.

The coast has been raided from the north once. The fleets of the north went south every summer from 519 to 530 against the Empire's western provinces ([[lore-southrnfleets|The Southern Fleets]]), and in 523 one Malagna crew sailed a captured galley through the western strait under imperial colors and came ashore at a fishing village on a deep bay of this coast ([[lore-bankedfire|The Winter of the Banked Fire]]). A squadron from [[place-castamar|Kastamar]] fired the galley at her moorings, and the fire ran up the beach into the village's forty-one boats and its net-lofts. The crew wintered on the headland. In 524 the _therkos_, Zenothas Varelis, gave the survivors seven years at the oars of the Kastamar fleet, and let three of them stay in the village. The bay is [[place-braldvik|Bralvik]] now, the one Nordmal name on the coast, and its bill for the boats goes to the admiralty every autumn.

The war that cost the province most was Taruzel's. The islands rose in 707 ([[lore-taruzelwar|The Taruzelian War of Independence]]), and in 708 the largest squadron the Empire had ever sent into the eastern sea, some forty warships out of Kastamar, was destroyed in the Tamzir Shoals ([[lore-tamzirbattle|The Battle of Tamzir Shoals]]). About 709 the Empire recognized the Confederation. Taruzelian ships have competed with Salkalon's directly since, the merchants of [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] want the trade the admirals of Kastamar want to fight for, and the names of the drowned are cut on the Kastamar mole.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The petition.** Ornetha Venthelis hires a party to carry the houses' treaty petition to Orzara rather than trust the post.

- **The empty quay.** A Delalara house offers to hire three of [[place-castamar|Kastamar]]'s laid-up warships with their crews, and the admiral refuses to lend the fleet to merchants.
