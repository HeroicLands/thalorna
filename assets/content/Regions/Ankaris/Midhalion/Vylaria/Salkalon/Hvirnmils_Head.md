## Overview {#overview}

Hvirnmil's Head is the north headland of the bay at [[place-braldvik|Bralvik]], a blunt shoulder of turf and gray rock that stands between the town's beach and the open sea. It is the first thing a boat sees of Bralvik coming in from the fishing grounds and the last thing it loses going out. The town named it for [Hvirnmil]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Malagna captain whose crew dug in on its top after the bay burned, and it is the reason the town carries a northern name at all ([[lore-bankedfire|The Winter of the Banked Fire]]).

A net-mender sitting at the foot of the path tells a stranger how to climb it: "Keep to the left of the gully, where the turf is cut into steps. The right side looks easier and it drops into the sea. When you get to the top, the hollow is where they had the fire. The stones are where they put the nine who died. Do not sit on the stones, and do not take anything off the Head. It all belongs to somebody."

## The Climb and the Top

A path climbs the landward slope from the end of the town's shingle, cut into turf steps on the steep pitches and worn smooth by two centuries of feet. It takes a quarter of an hour at a walk. The pickets of the _felzos_ (legate) stood at the foot of it all that winter, where a low ridge of earth still marks the line of their ditch, and the baskets of fish went up and down past them.

The top is a broad, sloping table of turf with the sea on three sides and the wind always on one of them. In the middle of it is a hollow about the size of a fishing boat, a man's depth at the center, its edges grassed over and its floor black with old ash where the turf will not take. That is the pit-house where the crew kept the fire banked under a smoke-rack every night of the winter, the only light on that stretch of coast. Nothing of the house stands. A ring of post-holes shows where its roof was, and the town children are told that the ash at the bottom is still warm in the right weather, which it is not.

On the seaward slope below the hollow, in a row facing north, stand nine stones, each about knee-high and each cut with a single line of northern runes. Nine of the crew died before spring, and these are their graves. Nobody in Bralvik can read the runes, any more than they can read the brand on the town's barrels, and nobody has moved a stone. The Salken family, who keep the [[miscgear-oldcoal|Old Coal]], clear the turf from around them every autumn before [Coal Night]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}.

## What the Head Is Used For

The Head is Bralvik's sea-mark and its lookout. A fire lit in the hollow means a boat is overdue and the town is watching for it, and the boats coming home at dusk steer for the gap between the Head and the bay's southern point. On a clear day a watcher on the top can count the fishing fleet on the near grounds and see the masts of a [[place-castamar|Kastamar]] squadron long before it rounds the point, and in a town that sends the Kastamar admiralty a bill every autumn ([Boat-Bill]{.name data-type="lore"}) that second use is not forgotten.

The town has no law about the Head and needs none. Children dare each other to spend a night in the hollow, courting couples go up on summer evenings, and the old men go up to watch the weather. A stranger who digs on the Head, or pockets a stone, is turned off the town's beach and finds no boat in Bralvik willing to carry him.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The Head is the town's sea-mark and the Salken family's charge, and strangers are taking an interest in it. A man from [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] has been seen on the Head at night with a lantern and a spade, and the Salken want him found before the town finds him. The fire in the hollow is still lit for every overdue Bralvik boat.

## Trouble on the Head {#hooks}

- **The digger.** A man from [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] has been seen on the Head at night with a lantern and a spade, and the Salken family wants him found before the town finds him first.
- **The runes read.** A northern sailor ashore from a merchant ship climbs the Head and reads the nine stones aloud. Each names a man of the crew and his hall, and the last of them adds that his share lies with the fire.
- **The overdue fire.** The fire is lit in the hollow for a Bralvik boat three days overdue, and on the second night a light answers it from far out to sea, where no boat of the town should be.

# The Truth of the Head {#truth .secret}

The crew carried more up the headland than food. When the galley burned at her moorings the men on watch saved what they could reach, and what they could reach was the galley's strongbox: the Provénzian owner's coin for the voyage and the silver the crew had taken in the [[place-batarengulf|Gulf of Bataren]]. They buried it under the floor of the pit-house in the first week, and when the _therkos_ (governor) offered terms in the spring nobody mentioned it. Hvirnmil meant to come back for it at the end of his seven years. About eighteen of the rowers came home to [[place-malagna|Malagna]] in 532 AF, and none of them has been back to the Salkalon coast.

The box is still there, under the black floor of the hollow, at about the depth of a man's arm below the ash. The ninth stone says as much in its runes, and the man with the lantern has bought a copy of a Malagna skald's verse about the Door-Faring that says it too, in a line about silver sleeping under the fire. Dvilgvir knew, and never dug, and nobody in Bralvik has ever had the stones read.
