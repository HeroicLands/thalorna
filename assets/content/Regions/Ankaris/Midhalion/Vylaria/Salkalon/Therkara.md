## Overview

Therkara lies in [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]]'s inland country, where the coastal province turns to farmland, a market town of about four thousand among the agrarian estates that feed the port cities. It supplies grain, leather and wool to the harbors rather than shipping them abroad directly, and it is the quiet, landward face of an otherwise seafaring province.

An estate steward from the interior, in town for market, tells a coast-bred clerk where Therkara's loyalties lie: "Our lords have more in common with the gentry of [[place-hylen|Hilen]] than with the shipping houses of the coast. The sea is somebody else's business, and we sell it what it eats." Its estate-lords look inland, toward Hilen, rather than out to sea.

## Arriving

The road inland from [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] leaves the coast behind in half a day and climbs into rolling country of wheat, pasture and oak woods, with the villas of the estates on the low ridges and their tenants' villages in the valleys below them. Therkara stands where three estate roads meet the provincial road, a walled town of yellow stone on a hill above a river ford, with its market square inside the gate and the tanneries outside it, downstream, where the smell can go with the water.

The square is the town. On its north side stands the [Grain Hall]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, a long stone barn on pillars with a weighing floor under its arches, where the estates' grain is measured and sold by the sack to the coast's buyers. On its south side is the small provincial temple of the [[affiliation-imperialcult|Imperial Cult]], and between them, along the east side, the townhouses of the estate families, each with its name cut over the door and its mask-cupboard just inside. A stone pillar in the middle of the square carries the names of the town's men who died in the war with Taruzel, and there are more of them than a town of four thousand should have, because the estate-families buy their sons commissions in the marines.

## What Therkara Lives By

Market day is every eighth day, and the estates send in what the coast needs: grain above all, since the coast grows little of its own, and wool, hides, cheese and salted pork. The buyers come up from Delalara, [[place-castamar|Kastamar]] and the fishing towns, the fleet's victuallers among them, and the grain goes down to the coast in wagons the next morning. Outside the gate the tanners cure the hides of the estates' cattle, and the leather goes to the coast for the harness, buckets and bindings of a seafaring province. In the weeks after harvest the town is crowded with wagons and the Grain Hall weighs from dawn to dark. In winter the estate-families come in from their villas, and the town has dinners.

The estate country has its own temper, which is the temper of Hilen. Its families are old provincial houses named for their estates, they hold to the cult and to the old forms, and they think the coast's merchants are too friendly with the islands. They sent their sons to the marines in the war, and they lost them at the [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]], and when the admirals at Kastamar say that the war should be resumed, the estate-lords of Therkara are the first in the province to agree.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order

Therkara is a chartered town under the [[affiliation-provincvld|Province of Salkalon]], with two _tavemoi_ (magistrates) and a council of _kurthaloi_ (councilors) who answer in their own persons for its taxes. The estate-families fill the council through their stewards, and the masters sit in it themselves in winter. The first of them is [Teremos Kalmethen]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, master of the [Kalmethen]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} and of their estate of [Kalmethenna]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, the largest in the district, who came home from the war with a scar across his face and has spoken for its renewal at every provincial audience since.

The legion stationed in the province keeps its camp on the provincial road an hour below the town, and its quartermasters buy at the Grain Hall. Order in the town rests on the estates: a tenant in trouble goes to his lord's steward, and a stranger goes to a _tavemos_. A theft at the Grain Hall is judged in the square on market day.

## Where a Party Lodges

The [Sheaf and Sickle]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, inside the gate, is the inn of the coast's buyers, and on the night before market it is full of factors and victuallers comparing prices. A party with a letter to one of the estate-families is lodged at that family's villa, and finds that its host wants to know its views on the islands before the second course.

## Hooks

- **The victualler's wagons.** The fleet's victuallers have bought half the grain at the last three markets and paid for it in the admiralty's notes, which the estates do not want. The stewards hire a party to find a moneylender in Delalara who will buy them at less than a ruinous discount.
- **The pillar.** Someone has chiseled a name off the war pillar in the night. The families want to know whose name it was and who wanted it gone.
- **The old servant.** An old woman who was a nurse in the Kalmethen household has left the estate and come to the town, and she asks a party to carry a letter for her to the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]] in Orzara.

# The Truth of Therkara {#truth .secret}

The master of the Kalmethen is a slave. The heir, Teremos Kalmethen, went to the war as a junior officer of marines with his body-servant [Zetimis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a slave of the household his own age and much his own build, who had been raised beside him. Teremos was killed at the Tamzir Shoals. Zetimis was taken from the water by a Taruzelian boat and held on the islands for two years with the other prisoners, and in that time he became, to the Taruzelians and then to the marines who were held with him, the officer Teremos Kalmethen, whose seal-ring he had taken from his master's hand. When the prisoners were released he came home under that name, with a scar that had changed his face.

His master's mother, [Lizelen Kalmethen]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, knew him at once, at the gate, and embraced him as her son. She had no other child, and a house with no heir passes to a cousin she despised. The steward knew him too, and so did the old nurse, and the household has kept the secret for ten years. Zetimis has run the estate better than the heir would have, married well and fathered a son. He speaks for the war at every audience because Teremos would have, and because he has come to mean it.

The truth is on a roll in Orzara. The Levies desk of the Imperial Offices keeps the musters of the marines who sailed for the Shoals, and it records the officer Teremos Kalmethen lost with his ship and a slave of his household taken prisoner. Nobody has ever compared that entry with the release lists. The old nurse's letter asks the desk to compare them. She nursed the heir, Lizelen is dying, and the nurse has decided that her boy should be mourned under his own name while one of the women who raised him is alive to do it.
