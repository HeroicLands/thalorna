## Overview {#overview}

The Old Coal is the fire of [[place-braldvik|Bralvik]]. It lives banked in the Salken smokehouse, and the town holds that it has not gone out since [Dvilgvir]{.name data-type="being/npc"} first banked it on [[place-hvirnmilhd|Hvirnmil's Head]] in the winter of 523. On Coal Night, the night before 1 [Karnavar]{.name data-type="lore/calendar"}, its keeper carries it through the town in an iron helm, and every smokehouse in Bralvik lights its autumn fire from it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The keeper is [Sorima Salken]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a smokehouse mistress of sixty and the last of her line to carry the Salken name. She banks the coal in the family smokehouse under a hood of turf and ash, and she carries it on Coal Night in Hvirnmil's helm, the iron gone black with two centuries of embers. She also keeps a second coal banked in a cellar, against the night someone tries to put the first one out, and she does not say which cellar.

## Its Story {#its-story}

The town tells it as the story of its name ([[lore-bankedfire|The Winter of the Banked Fire]]). In 523 a raiding crew out of [[place-malagna|Malagna]], their galley burned, held the headland above a village the fleet had burned, and the imperial _felzos_ chose to starve them both. The crew kept a fire banked under a smoke-rack every night, as northern halls keep a winter's fish, and the widow [Ilthera Salken]{.name data-type="being/npc"} carried a basket of fish up the slope and brought it down smoked. The trade went on all winter under the pickets' eyes.

In the spring the crew went to the oars of the Kastamar fleet. Dvilgvir, who had kept the fire, stayed by the governor's leave and married Ilthera, and the captain gave him his helm on the beach. Dvilgvir carried the fire down into the village, branded the first barrels of smoke-fish with a line of runes, and sold them in [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] as fish from the Northmen's fire. The Salken smokehouse has kept the coal since, and the town's word for itself is the crew's word for the bay.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

The coal lights the smokehouses, and through them it holds the town's trade. A smokehouse lit from any other fire may smoke what it likes, but it may not burn the rune brand on its barrels, and the brand is what the Delalara markets pay for. Whoever keeps the Old Coal decides which fires in Bralvik are Bralvik's. Nothing else is claimed for it: the town calls it luck and custom, never a working.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **A Delalara shipping house** has bought two smokehouses in [[place-tholkezora|Tholkezora]] and wants the rune brand for its own barrels. The cleanest way to break the Salken hold on the brand is a coal that dies on Coal Night, and Sorima's cellar coal is the second thing the house's men look for.
- **Hvirnmil's own hall** in Malagna has sent a man with the hall's tally of the crew to ask for the captain's helm. The town carries its fire in it, and nobody in Bralvik has ever asked whether it was a gift or a pledge.
- **The town** wants it kept exactly as it is, by a Salken, and Sorima has no child to carry the name after her.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- Sorima Salken takes a fever a week before Coal Night. She asks a party to carry the coal through the town in her place, and to tell nobody where she has sent them for the cellar coal.
- A Delalara factor offers a party a year's wages to be in the Salken smokehouse when the coal is banked for the night, and to bank it badly.
- The man from Malagna presses his claim to the helm on the eve of Coal Night, the one night the town cannot do without it.

:::secret {title="What the first fire stood on"}
Dvilgvir first banked the fire in the hollow on the headland, and the crew's strongbox lies under that floor: the Provenzian owner's coin for the voyage and the silver taken in the [[place-batarengulf|Gulf of Bataren]], buried in the first week and never mentioned at the surrender. It is still there, a man's arm below the ash, and the ninth rune-stone on the slope says so. Dvilgvir knew and never dug. Anyone who has the stones read learns that the Old Coal's first hearth is a treasure's lid ([[place-hvirnmilhd|Hvirnmil's Head]]).
:::
