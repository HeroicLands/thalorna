## Overview

Tholkezora is the last of [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]]'s four principal fishing towns, perched with its seven thousand people on the rocky headland its name describes, over deep water. Its crews fish the far grounds and, in season, hunt the great sea-beasts whose oil lights lamps across the province. That is a harder, more dangerous trade than the inshore netting of its sister-towns.

Tholkezora men have a name up and down the coast for being half-wild and wholly fearless. A [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] sailing master, hiring a crew, tells his mate what the name means on a dock: "They will go where you will not, and they will not always tell the customs-house where they went." Its captains are as comfortable smuggling as fishing when the imperial customs press too hard.

## Arriving

The headland stands out into the sea like a fist, a mass of dark rock with the town packed on its top and down its landward side, and the coast road reaches it over a neck of land so narrow that a cart and a man can barely pass. The houses are of the same dark stone as the rock, low and thick-walled against the winter gales, and their roofs are weighted with stones on ropes. There is no harbor on the seaward side, only cliffs and the sound of the swell in the caves under them. The harbor is on the lee side, a deep cove under the headland where the water is black and still, and the boats lie at moorings in it: long, decked, high-bowed boats built for the open sea, with a lance-rack along each gunwale.

On the beach at the head of the cove stand the [Try-Works]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, a row of brick furnaces under iron pots where the blubber of the sea-beasts is boiled down for oil. When a beast has been towed in the whole town smells of it for a week, the smoke is black, and the beach is red. In the cliff above the cove the caves open, and the town uses the lower ones as cellars, and says so.

## What Tholkezora Lives By

The far grounds lie a day and a night out, over deep water where the fish run big, and a Tholkezora boat stays out a week at a time with lines and salt in her hold. In late summer the sea-beasts pass the coast, and the boats go out together to hunt them with lances and lines, and tow the dead beasts home to the Try-Works. One beast fills two hundred casks with oil, and the oil lights the lamps of the province and the lighthouses of the coast. A beast can also stave in a boat with a blow of its tail, and every year one crew or two does not come home. The town keeps their names on the wall of the temple of [[affiliation-brazemas|Brazemas]] above the cove, and the wall is long.

The other trade is the one the sailing master means. A boat a week out on the far grounds can meet another boat, and what she brings home under her salt fish pays no duty. Tholkezora boats carry wine and steel out and bring back spices, cloth and silver, and the caves under the headland hold what the customs would like to see. Bralvik crews sail with Tholkezora captains when the money is good, and the town's grandmothers can name every customs cutter on the coast by her rig.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order

Tholkezora is a chartered town under the [[affiliation-provincvld|Province of Salkalon]], with two _tavemoi_ (magistrates) and a council of _kurthaloi_ (councilors) who answer in their own persons for its taxes. The council is the captains, who own the decked boats, and the first of them is [Revothos Linthimis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a hunter of sea-beasts for thirty years, who has lost two boats and three fingers and has never been caught with anything he should not have carried.

The town keeps its own order, and it keeps it hard. A crew settles its own quarrels at sea, and a captain is master of his boat under the old law of the coast. In the town a quarrel between crews goes to the captains in council, and a man who informs to the customs is put ashore on the far side of the neck and not let back. The tax-farmer's clerks come to Tholkezora in pairs and leave before dark.

## The Present {#present}

The customs press harder this year, and the captains push back. A new customs cutter with a captain who cannot be bought has taken two Tholkezora boats in a month, and the captains under Revothos Linthimis want her captain persuaded, warned or wrecked. A boat towed in a sea-beast with a Taruzelian harpoon in its back, and a Delalara factor wants to know how far out the boats go. The same factor's house has bought two smokehouses in the town and wants the use of a cave the captains will not give.

## Where a Party Lodges

The [Lance]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, on the cove, is the town's only inn, and a stranger who takes a room there is asked his business by three people before supper. The rooms are clean, the oil-lamps burn bright, and the keeper's prices rise for anyone who asks too many questions about the caves. A party that comes with a captain's word is lodged in the captain's house instead, and is treated as one of his crew for as long as it stays.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The beast that came in.** A boat has towed in a sea-beast with a Taruzelian harpoon in its back, and nobody in the town will say where she found it. A factor of Delalara pays a party to find out how far out the Tholkezora boats are going.
- **The cutter.** A new customs cutter has come to the coast with a captain who cannot be bought, and she has taken two Tholkezora boats in a month. The captains want her captain persuaded, warned or wrecked, and do not mind which.
- **The cellar.** A Delalara shipping house has bought two smokehouses in Tholkezora and wants the use of a cave. The captains refuse. The house hires a party to find out what is in the cave that it cannot have.

# The Truth of Tholkezora {#truth .secret}

The boats that meet Tholkezora on the far grounds are Taruzelian, and their cargoes are stolen. The outlaw captains of the islands, whom the Taruzelian guilds call pirates, take guild shipping in the channels and need somewhere to sell what they take that no guild factor will see. For eight years Revothos Linthimis and four other captains have bought their plunder at sea, a week out, at a third of its worth, carried it home under salt fish, and sold it through the caves to buyers in Delalara who ask no questions about Taruzelian cloth at Vylarian prices. The outlaw captains are paid in Vylarian silver, and the guilds lose twice: once in the channels and once in the price of their own goods in Delalara.

The admiralty knows. The captain of the last customs cutter on this coast reported it to Kastamar four years ago, and the admiral's officers told the customs to leave Tholkezora alone, because every bale of guild cloth sold in Delalara is a bale the islands lose, and because the Tholkezora captains bring the fleet news of the outlaw captains' strength and the guilds' convoys. The new cutter's captain was sent by the tax-farmer at Delalara, who was told nothing.

The [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]] has begun to trace the cloth. Its assessor [Termuza Ezzinu]{.name data-type="being/npc"} has bought three bales in Delalara bearing the marks of guild houses that reported them taken in the channels, and she has come to Tholkezora as a buyer of oil, to learn which captains sail to the far grounds and come back heavier than fish would make them.
