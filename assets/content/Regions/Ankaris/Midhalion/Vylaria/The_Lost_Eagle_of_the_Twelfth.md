## Overview {#overview}

Every legion of the [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|Vilérian Military]] marches behind its _orkaima_, its eagle, and the eagle of the [Doran Hilvan]{.name data-type="affiliation"}, the Twelfth, is the one the army does not have. The legion went up into the [[place-kervaron|Kervaron Spine]] about 680 to read the boundary-stones to the clans, ran out of road when the snow came, and was struck from the rolls as destroyed. Its eagle was never returned. It may lie in [[place-twelfthcamp|The Twelfth's Last Camp]], the ruined fort below the passes where the legion's rear cohorts wintered, and nobody who says so can show it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody is known to. The army's record says the eagle is lost with the legion. The shrine of the _orkaima_ in the Last Camp stands intact with its niche empty, and the border patrol that cleared the fort of the undead reported finding no eagle. The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] watch the fort from the slope and say nothing to outsiders, and the clans' own account of the year says only that the mountain took back what climbed it.

## Its Story {#its-story}

An eagle of the legions is a figure of gilded bronze with raised wings, set on a staff and carried by the _orkaimos_, the eagle-bearer, and it is the most sacred object in the army. The military manual opens with the formula every Vilérian schoolchild learns, _Zel orkaima thalentor uthrime_, "Beneath the eagle stand the borders", and it requires a legion that loses its eagle to be struck from the rolls.

About 680, under a regency, the regent's council wanted a victory it could show [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] and sent the Twelfth to enforce the boundary-stones the western expeditions had planted at the passes ([[lore-twelfthloss|The Loss of the Twelfth]]). The [[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan clans]] let the legion climb, closed the passes behind it and did not give battle. The legion left its rear cohorts in a fort at the foot of the passes and took the rest up through the summer. The eagle never came back, and no report says whether it went up the passes or stayed in the fort. The army disbanded the legion on the record, as the manual requires, and the men of the Twelfth who were not in the range were cashiered with it.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

The eagle does nothing a smith could not make. What it does is in what the army holds of it. A legion stands beneath its eagle, and a legion without one does not stand at all: the Twelfth's name is gone from the rolls because its eagle is gone, and the families of the cashiered carry that disgrace. The manual provides for a legion that loses its eagle and says nothing of one whose eagle comes home, and every party to the matter reads that silence its own way.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **The families of the cashiered** want it found. They hold that an eagle brought home undoes the striking of the legion, and with it the disgrace of the sons who were cashiered in the disbanding.
- **The legions raised in the Twelfth's place** want it left where it is. A Twelfth restored to the rolls takes back its number, its honors and its share of a shrinking army.
- **Salvagers** who work the fort for its iron want any gilt that comes out of it, and know that more than one buyer is waiting.
- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|The Arcane Order]]** has a standing interest in the undead, and has not been told what the patrol found in the fort's barracks.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- The niche in the Last Camp's shrine has been scraped clean of dust in a pattern that fits an eagle's base, and the scraping is recent ([[place-twelfthcamp|The Twelfth's Last Camp]]).
- A salvager's cart comes down from the fort with a gilt fragment that may be part of an eagle, and three parties offer to buy it before it is cleaned.
- A legate of the legions with an eye on the succession lets it be known that he would pay well for the Twelfth's eagle, carried into [[place-orzara|Orzara]] at the head of his column. The legions raised in its place hear of the offer the same week.
