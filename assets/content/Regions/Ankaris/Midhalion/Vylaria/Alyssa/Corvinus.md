## Overview

Corvinus is the garrison town of [[place-alyssa|Alyssa]], a town of about 9,000 anchoring the interior of a province where towns, villas and senatorial estates form a near-continuous quilt around the imperial capital. The [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] holds its enemies at distant frontiers, so a garrison in the middle of Alyssa watches no border. It anchors the interior.

A drover who has delivered into Corvinus for twenty years tells the lads new to the road where to take their meals: "Eat where the soldiers eat, and you will be served quickly. Corvinus keeps the garrison's hours, and a stranger learns them by missing a meal."
