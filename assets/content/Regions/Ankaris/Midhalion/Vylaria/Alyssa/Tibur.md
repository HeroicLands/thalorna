## Overview

Tibur is a busy market town on one of the great roads into the capital, where the produce of the surrounding estates is gathered and sold on toward [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]]. Its inns and warehouses serve the constant traffic of carters and drovers, and its weekly market is among the largest in the heartland.
