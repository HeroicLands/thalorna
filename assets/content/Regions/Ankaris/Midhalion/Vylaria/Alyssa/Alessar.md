## Overview

Alêssar is the capital of a province whose greatest city it does not govern. About 45,000 people live here, and it is the administrative seat of [[place-alyssa|Alêssâ]] proper: the [[affiliation-provinclys|Province of Alêssâ]] is held from Alêssar by the _thêrkhôs_ (governor) the emperor appoints, while [[place-orzara|Ôrzâra]], which stands within the province and is counted with it, is kept as a thing of its own.

A clerk on the _thêrkhôs_'s staff explains the arrangement to a newcomer sent to deliver a petition: "Everyone who writes to us wants the capital, and the capital is not ours. What is ours is everything else in Alêssâ—the towns, the roads, the estates, the tax rolls—and that is charge enough for one _thêrkhôs_."
