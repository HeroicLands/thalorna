## Overview

Almost nothing in Alessa is more than a day's ride from a temple, a magistrate and a tax-collector. This is the land of the [[affiliation-provinclys|Province of Alessa]], the heartland of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] within the [[place-vylariargn|Vylaria Region]]: seven million people in a near-continuous quilt of towns, villas and senatorial estates around the imperial capital, knit together by the oldest and best-kept stretch of the Vylarian road network. Its provincial capital is [[place-alyssar|Alessar]].

A porter in one of the gate-yards outside [[place-orzara|Orzara]], where the teams are unhitched and the cargo is broken down onto handcarts, teaches a new hand to read the carts by their roads. "Barge-rope on the axles means [[place-ostenna|Ostenna]], and the grain is in sacks. A cart of fruit and wine with a steward walking beside it comes down from a villa in the hills around [[place-vennara|Vennara]], and you carry that one carefully. Straw in the bed and a driver asleep on the load means a night on one of the great roads, and most likely [[place-delara|Delara]]. Everything else is grain, and you will carry it."

## Character

The capital dominates everything around it. Its senatorial estates, bureaucratic offices, temples and markets spread across the province, and no corner is far enough from them to count as wilderness. Adventure here starts with people rather than terrain: a magistrate who is always near, a road that is always busy, and a country house whose owner has interests reaching back to the capital.

## Economy

Alessa runs on government, trade and the crafts that serve the wealthy. It grows little surplus food of its own and depends on [[affiliation-provnchyln|Hilen]] for grain and [[affiliation-provincvld|Salkalon]] for seafood and imports, which is why the gate-yards never empty. What Alessa produces is influence, law and [Vylarian Steel]{.name data-type="miscgear"}. The capital's metalworking guilds are the finest on the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]], and access to the production of Vylarian Steel is one of the empire's most closely guarded advantages.

## Notable Features

- **The imperial capital**, seat of the Orzas (the emperor), [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] (the Senate) and [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]].
- **The [Platan of Laws]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}**, the empire's supreme judicial institution.
- **The greatest concentration of Aureldian temples in the world.**
- **The Thordimalon headquarters** and its academy for arcane training.

## Settlements

Alessa is the most densely settled of the provinces. A carter can leave one town after breakfast and reach the next by evening on paved road, and the countryside between is farms and villas the whole way.

**The cities.**

[[place-orzara|Orzara]] (something under 1,000,000)
: the imperial capital, seat of the Orzas, the Senate and the Thordimalon (the Arcane Order), so vast it is kept as its own thing rather than filed under the province. See [[place-arvon|Arvon]] and [[place-pnthnrldnm|Sarkena Dorekis]].

[[place-alyssar|Alessar]] (about 45,000)
: the provincial capital and administrative seat of Alessa proper.

[[place-lentora|Lentora]] (about 32,000)
: the province's second city, market and governing hub of the inner farm-and-villa country.

[[place-belekos|Belekos]] (about 25,000)
: a merchant city of the river valleys, grown rich on agricultural surplus and trade.

[[place-velysara|Velisara]] (about 20,000)
: a secondary provincial city of columned forums and faded imperial grandeur.

**Towns.**

[[place-delara|Delara]] (about 12,000)
: a market town on a great road into the capital.

[[place-corvinus|Korvenara]] (about 9,000)
: a garrison town anchoring the province's interior.

[[place-calvaris|Kalvaris]] (about 9,000)
: a temple-and-market town around an ancient hilltop precinct.

[[place-ostenna|Ostenna]] (about 7,000)
: a river-port town transshipping grain by barge.

[[place-vennara|Vennara]] (about 6,000)
: a villa-town in the hills, favored for senatorial country estates.

**Villages and villa-estates.** Beyond the towns the heartland is a near-unbroken quilt of villages and country villas of 300–1,500 souls, among them [Aventis]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Caldera]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Tibrenna]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Sorrenza]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Marvale]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Corennis]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Vellano]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Pradio]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Sennara]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Olivetta]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Tarsenna]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Quirnis]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Belluno]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Castellis]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Florenza]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Nervia]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Ostrella]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} and [Aravenna]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}. Most lie within a half-day's road of a market town and a magistrate. These are only the named places; the great mass of Alessa's people live in countless smaller hamlets and farms between them.
