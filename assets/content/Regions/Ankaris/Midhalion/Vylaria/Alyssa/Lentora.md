## Overview

Lentora is the second city of [[affiliation-provinclys|Alyssa]] after the capital, an old inland city of about 32,000 with forums, baths and guildhalls, and the market and administrative hub of the populous farm-and-villa country of the inner province. Beside [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]] it is wealthy and unglamorous, which suits the people who keep their seats here: the lesser senatorial houses and the prosperous equestrian families.

A villa steward who rides in at the turn of each season to settle the household's accounts and attend its suits describes the city to the new under-steward: "Do not look for the Senate here. Look for the guildhalls and the magistrates' benches. The capital is busy with the empire, and so a great deal of what Alyssa actually needs done, the leases, the roads, the disputes between neighbors, gets done in Lentora, by people nobody in the capital has heard of."
