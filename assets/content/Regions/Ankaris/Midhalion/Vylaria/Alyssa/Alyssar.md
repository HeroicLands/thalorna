## Overview

Alyssar is the capital of a province whose greatest city it does not govern. About 45,000 people live here, and it is the administrative seat of [[place-alyssa|Alyssa]] proper: the [[affiliation-provinclys|Province of Alyssa]] is held from Alyssar by the governor the emperor appoints, while [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]], which stands within the province and is counted with it, is kept as a thing of its own.

A clerk on the governor's staff explains the arrangement to a newcomer sent to deliver a petition: "Everyone who writes to us wants the capital, and the capital is not ours. What is ours is everything else in Alyssa—the towns, the roads, the estates, the tax rolls—and that is charge enough for one governor."
