Alyssar is the provincial capital of [[place-alyssa|Alyssa]], the heartland of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] and a near-continuous quilt of towns, villas and senatorial estates around the imperial capital. It is the seat of the [[affiliation-provinclys|Province of Alyssa]], the urban, political and institutional core of the Empire.
