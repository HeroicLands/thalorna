## Overview

Calvaris is a market town grown up around an ancient hilltop temple precinct of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían]] faith. Pilgrims and traders mingle in its streets, and its fortunes rest on the festivals that fill its market squares several times a year, when the roads from the surrounding country choke with the devout and the merchants who follow them.
