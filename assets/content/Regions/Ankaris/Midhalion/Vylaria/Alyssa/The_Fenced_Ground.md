## Overview

A day's ride from [[place-velysara|Velysâra]] there is a tract of good farmland that nobody farms. It is the ground where [[lore-recensio|the Recensio of Alyssa]] ended in 75 BF, and nearly eight centuries on it is still bare. A fence runs round it, kept in repair, and the grass inside the fence is thin and does not take.

A farmer whose holding adjoins the fence gives carters asking for a short cut across it the local rule: "Go round by the road. The Ordo's patrol will ask you to turn back once, and it does not ask twice."

## What Is There

The fence encloses a tract of the Alyssan plain. Inside it the soil is the same black loam as the farms on every side, and nothing has taken root on it that the Ordo's crews do not clear at the Ordo's charge. There are no buildings and no ruins visible from the road. The Ordo has never dug the ground, and it stops anyone who tries.

## Who Comes Here

- **Quaesitores**, on the first commission the Quaesitorium holds: to establish what happened at Alyssa, which has never been closed.
- **Scholars of Velysâra**, to look at the fence.
- **Treasure-seekers**, whom the Ordo's patrols stop.

## Hooks

- A tenant on the farm beside the fence turns up something with his plow at the fence line, and the Ordo's patrol is at his door before he has finished cleaning it.
- A clerk reconciling the inventory taken after the Recensio finds the count comes up one short, as the first inventory did, and cannot find the copy that would say of what.
