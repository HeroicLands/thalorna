## Overview

A day's ride from [[place-velysara|Velysâra]] there is a tract of good farmland that nobody farms. It is the ground where [[lore-recensio|the Recensio of Alêssâ]] ended in 75 BF, and nearly eight centuries on it is still bare. A fence runs round it, kept in repair, and the grass inside the fence is thin and does not take.

A farmer whose holding adjoins the fence gives carters asking for a short cut across it the local rule: "Go round by the road. The Order's patrol will ask you to turn back once, and it does not ask twice."

## What Is There

The fence encloses a tract of the Alêssan plain. Inside it the soil is the same black loam as the farms on every side, and nothing has taken root on it that the Order's crews do not clear at the Order's charge. There are no buildings and no ruins visible from the road. The Order has never dug the ground, and it stops anyone who tries.

## Who Comes Here

- **Sêkhêrândoi**, on the first commission the Sekran holds: to establish what happened at Alêssâ, which has never been closed.
- **Scholars of Velysâra**, to look at the fence.
- **Treasure-seekers**, whom the Order's patrols stop.
