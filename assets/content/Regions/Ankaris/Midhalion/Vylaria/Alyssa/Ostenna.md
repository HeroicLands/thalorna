## Overview

Ostenna sits where a navigable river meets the road network, a small river-port town of about 7,000 that transships grain and goods by barge toward the capital. Its boatmen, warehousemen and toll-clerks form a tight and prosperous community, and the river trade gives the town a reach and a worldliness out of proportion to its size.

A toll-clerk at the landing, teaching a boy to read the barges, starts with the one fact that matters: "A great many of the sacks that go to [[place-orzara|Orzara]] by water pass through the hands of this town. The boatman knows the river, the warehouseman knows what is stored, and I know what has paid. When all three of us agree, the barge goes. When we do not, it waits, and a waiting barge is the loudest thing on the river."

## Arriving

The river is the [[place-vylarisrvr|River Vylaris]], and Ostenna stands on a low bluff above it, upstream of the capital, with the road coming down to the water through a cutting. Below the bluff the [Landing]{.name data-type="place/site"} runs along the bank: stone quays, a row of tall warehouses with hoists jutting from their upper doors, and the [Toll House]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, the one handsome building in the town, with a colonnade on the water side and the tariff of the river cut into a bronze board beside its door. Barges lie two and three deep along the quays, and more wait in a line in midstream for a berth. On a still evening the smell of wet grain carries up to the town.

The upper quay is busy. The lower one has silted, and its warehouses have bricked up their river doors and load from the road instead. The bronze tariff board was cut for coins that are no longer struck, and a clerk has chalked the present charges beside it.

## What Ostenna Lives By

Carts bring the produce of the inland estates down the road to the Landing, and the warehousemen store it, sack it and load it onto barges for the run down to the capital's wharves. Teams of oxen walk the towpath back upstream with the empties. Most of the cargo is grain, and a good part of that is bound for the state granaries that feed the capital's dole; the factors of the great grain houses, [[affiliation-mardovanes|House Mardovanes]] among them, keep agents at the Landing who watch their sacks from cart to barge. The town also makes rope, mends sacks, builds and caulks barges, and feeds the boatmen.

The three trades of the toll-clerk's lesson run the town between them. The boatmen belong to a fraternity that keeps its own hall above the Landing, settles its members' quarrels and buries their drowned. The warehousemen are a handful of families who have held the same warehouses for generations. The toll-clerks are the emperor's men, and they are the only people in Ostenna who were not born there.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order

Ostenna is a chartered town under the [[affiliation-provinclys|Province of Alessa]], with two _tavemoi_ elected each year and a council of _kurthaloi_ who answer for its taxes; by custom one _tavemos_ is a boatman and the other a warehouseman. The Toll House is not the town's. Its clerks collect the river toll for the _karezos_ (imperial steward) at [[place-alyssar|Alessar]], and no barge leaves the Landing without their stamp on its manifest. Order on the water belongs to the fraternity, and order on the quays to whoever is loading: a thief caught on the Landing is dealt with there, and the _tavemoi_ hear about it afterward if at all.

## Where a Party Lodges

The boatmen's inns stand at the top of the cutting, where the road reaches the bluff, and are loud, cheap and full of men waiting for a berth. The town's one decent inn faces the Toll House across the Landing and serves the factors and the clerks. A party wanting passage downstream asks at the fraternity's hall, where a boatman with room on his barge names a price and a day; the day is the boatman's best guess and nothing more.

## Hooks

- **The waiting barge.** A party's employer has a barge in the midstream line that has waited eleven days for a berth while later barges were loaded. Somebody wants it to wait, and the cargo will not keep much longer.
- **The drowned clerk.** A toll-clerk is pulled from the river below the Landing. The fraternity buries him, and the _karezos_ sends a man from Alessar to ask why. Nobody at the Landing will talk to him, and he hires a party to ask for him.
- **The sack that rattles.** A warehouseman's boy finds something in a grain sack that is not grain and brings it to the first strangers he meets, because he knows better than to bring it to anyone in the town.

# The Truth of Ostenna {#truth .secret}

The waiting is a business. Grain brokers in the capital pay the three trades of Ostenna to slow the river in the weeks before the dole is drawn, when the state granaries are low and every sack in the city is worth more for being late. The boatmen find reasons not to sail, the warehousemen find reasons not to load, and the toll-clerks find faults in manifests, and the price in Orzara climbs while the barges sit. The payments come through one broker's agent at the Landing, who also sells the timing of each slowdown to a second broker, so that the capital's grain market is being played twice from one quay.

The drowned clerk had begun to keep a list of the manifests he was told to refuse, with the dates. The list is in the Toll House, in the bronze tariff board's hollow back. The fraternity did not drown him and does not know who did, and it wants to.
