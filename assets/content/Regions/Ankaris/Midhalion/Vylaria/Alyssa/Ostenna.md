## Overview

Ostenna sits where a navigable river meets the road network, a small river-port town of about 7,000 that transships grain and goods by barge toward the capital. Its boatmen, warehousemen and toll-clerks form a tight and prosperous community, and the river trade gives the town a reach and a worldliness out of proportion to its size.

A toll-clerk at the landing, teaching a boy to read the barges, starts with the one fact that matters: "A great many of the sacks that go to [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]] by water pass through the hands of this town. The boatman knows the river, the warehouseman knows what is stored, and I know what has paid. When all three of us agree, the barge goes. When we do not, it waits, and a waiting barge is the loudest thing on the river."
