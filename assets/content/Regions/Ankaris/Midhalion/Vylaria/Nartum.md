Nartûm is the holy city of the [[place-nartumkngdm|Nartûm Kingdom]], the independent Vylari-speaking realm ruled under its priestly aristocracy. It is the seat of the [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartûm]] and of the Holy Mother, a sanctuary city where the sacred and the secular intertwine under priestly rule, and it is among the largest cities of the region.
