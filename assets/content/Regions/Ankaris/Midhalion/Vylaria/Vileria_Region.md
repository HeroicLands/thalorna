## Overview {#overview}

Viléria is the heartland of an empire that is smaller than its maps: a warm peninsula on the northern shore of the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]], within the [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión Region]], that once stood at the summit of civilization and still governs as though it did. It is the land of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] and of the independent [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]]. An outsider remembers it for three things: its steel, its roads, and its capital, [[place-orzara|Orzara]].

Viléria lies east of [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]], west of [[place-heladrgn|Hellád]], south of [[place-velanthrgn|Velanthia]], and southeast of [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]].

The imperial capital, Orzara, with something under a million people, stands in [[place-alyssa|Alessa]] and is counted with it.

## Geography

Viléria proper is a broad peninsula and the coastal country around it. The coast is sun-drenched lowland; the hills behind it are terraced for olive groves and vineyards; and the interior climbs into mountains that rise toward the forests of Vristwald. Summers are warm and dry and winters mild, and the sea tempers the climate all year.

The **Vilérian roads** still join the great cities and run on into provinces the empire no longer holds. Many are in disrepair. The engineering still shows: they run straight, they are paved, and they cross rivers and gorges on arches of cut stone. They remain the main overland trade routes of the region, and a carter who has driven one learns to read the empire's fortunes by where the paving has been patched and where it has simply been left.

### The Kervaron Spine {#the-kervaron-spine}

The empire's western frontier rises into the [[place-kervaron|Kervaron Spine]], a great arc of high mountains between the Vilérian peninsula and the eastern reaches of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] and the northeastern marches of [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]]. Its dozen narrow passes are the only practicable crossings from east to west. [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]], the Senate, lists the range as imperial territory on the strength of the boundary-stones its legions planted at the passes in [[lore-westernwars|the Western Wars]], and the empire has never governed a valley of it. The Kervaron is the sacred country of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]], who hold its peaks to be the dwellings of [[affiliation-firstgods|the First Gods]], and Vilérian merchants cross it by paying the clans a passage-gift at the first camp below each pass. Since the [Doran Hilvan]{.name data-type="affiliation"} was lost in the range ([[lore-twelfthloss|The Loss of the Twelfth]]), no merchant questions the gift and the chancery has not raised the range with the clans.

### The River Vilaris

The [[place-vylarisrvr|River Vilaris]] crosses the heartland from the interior hills to the sea. Grain comes down it from the inland landings to the capital, and the capital's sea cargoes are hauled up it from [[place-aelissium|Elissara]], the chartered city at its mouth, where the river reaches the sea through a fan of reed-marsh channels called the [Mouths]{.name data-type="place/feature"}.

## Current Situation {#present}

The heartland lives on borrowed money and waits on an old man's death. The [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]'s throne pays its veterans with loans it rolls over each quarter, the capital holds about a week of grain, and the Orzas has adopted nobody. Each province shows the strain its own way. The governor of [[place-alyssa|Alessa]] has stayed in [[place-vennara|Vennara]] since spring, and the province’s old cities sell their stone to the capital. [[place-hylen|Hilen]]'s estates take their tenants' land and fear civil war more than anything. [[place-moktur|Moktur]] is a province the capital can neither lose nor hold. [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]]'s merchants live on the strait trade the capital calls collaboration, beside a fleet that cannot pay its oarsmen. In the [[place-nartumkngdm|Nartun Kingdom]] the high priesthood moves to judge its king while the forest dies.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The week of bread.** A grain house of [[place-orzara|Orzara]] hires a party to bring a string of grounded barges down the [[place-vylarisrvr|River Vilaris]] before the granaries run low.
- **The empty chair.** A family that lost a case in [[place-alyssa|Alessa]] this year wants to know who sealed the judgment.
