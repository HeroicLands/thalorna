## Overview

Saltholm is one of [[affiliation-provincvld|Vald]]'s four principal fishing towns, a town of about six thousand built beside the broad coastal salt-pans that give it its name. Salt and salt-fish are its livelihood. It cures the catch of half the lesser villages along this stretch of coast, and its salt feeds the curing-sheds from [[place-ostvar|Ostvar]] to [[place-brennvik|Brennvik]].

A woman who has worked the curing-houses since girlhood shows a new hand the line of barrels and says why the work comes to Saltholm: "Every village on this coast has fish. We have the salt, so they bring the fish to us." Control of the salt-pans makes Saltholm's handful of pan-owning families quietly powerful far beyond the town's modest size.
