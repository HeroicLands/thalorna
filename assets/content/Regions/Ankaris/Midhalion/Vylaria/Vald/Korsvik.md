## Overview

Korsvik is the last of [[affiliation-provincvld|Vald]]'s four principal fishing towns, perched with its seven thousand people on a rocky headland over deep water. Its crews fish the far grounds and, in season, hunt the great sea-beasts whose oil lights lamps across the province. That is a harder, more dangerous trade than the inshore netting of its sister-towns.

Korsvik men have a name up and down the coast for being half-wild and wholly fearless. A Mercavia sailing master, hiring a crew, tells his mate what the name means on a dock: "They will go where you will not, and they will not always tell the customs-house where they went." Its captains are as comfortable smuggling as fishing when the imperial customs press too hard.
