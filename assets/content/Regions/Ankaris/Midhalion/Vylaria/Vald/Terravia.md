## Overview

Terravia lies in [[affiliation-provincvld|Vald]]'s inland country, where the coastal province turns to farmland, a market town of about four thousand among the agrarian estates that feed the port cities. It supplies grain, leather and wool to the harbors rather than shipping them abroad directly, and it is the quiet, landward face of an otherwise seafaring province.

An estate steward from the interior, in town for market, tells a coast-bred clerk where Terravia's loyalties lie: "Our lords have more in common with the gentry of Hylen than with the shipping houses of the coast. The sea is somebody else's business, and we sell it what it eats." Its estate-lords look inland, toward [[affiliation-provnchyln|Hylen]], rather than out to sea.
