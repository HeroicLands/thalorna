## Overview

Ostvar is the largest of [[affiliation-provincvld|Vald]]'s four great fishing towns, its harbor crowded with the boats and the curing-sheds that supply fresh and salted fish to the capital and the interior provinces. It lives by the catch and the fickle sea; its people keep to the old coastal customs and a wary devotion to the sea's moods. In a hard winter the price of Ostvar's salt fish is felt as far inland as the capital's markets, and the town's net-masters and curing-houses wield quiet weight in the provincial fish trade.
