## Overview

Brennvik is one of [[affiliation-provincvld|Vald]]'s four principal fishing towns, set on a deep bay whose boats work the rich offshore grounds. It is a town of net-lofts, smokehouses, and the boatwrights who keep the fishing fleet afloat — humbler craft than the warships of [[place-castamar|Castamar]], but more numerous. Its catch is smoked rather than salted, and Brennvik smoke-fish is a recognized article of trade in the markets of [[place-mercavia|Mercavia]] and beyond.
