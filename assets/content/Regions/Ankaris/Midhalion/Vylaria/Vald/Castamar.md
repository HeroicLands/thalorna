## Overview

Castamar is the empire's sword at sea, the great naval city of [[affiliation-provincvld|Vald]] and the home of the imperial shipyards and of the command that keeps what remains of Vylaria's sea power. About twenty thousand people live here, and its harbor is a working war-port: dry-docks and slipways, cordage-walks and timber-yards, marine barracks, and the offices of the officers of the Classar, as the fleet is called in the [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|Vylarian military]].

A shipwright of the yards, showing a new apprentice the slips, makes the point about how the city lives: "Warships go down these ways when the Senate votes the money, and not before. When the money is late, the yard is quiet and so is every tavern in Castamar." Where its sister-city [[place-mercavia|Mercavia]] up the coast lives by trade, Castamar lives by the fleet. Its fortunes rise and fall with imperial naval spending, and since the loss of Harad broke Vylarian supremacy in the eastern sea, that spending has been anxious and political. Discipline sets the city's tone, and a third of its people draw their bread, directly or not, from the navy.
