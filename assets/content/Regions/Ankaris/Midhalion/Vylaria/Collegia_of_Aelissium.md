The **Collegia of Aelissium** are the trade collegia of [[place-aelissium|Aelissium]]: the potters' collegium, whose master's seal outvalues the bowl it marks, and the Seamen's Collegium, on whose roll the city's sailors are hired by the voyage. The chapterhouse of the [[affiliation-clgmrgntrrm|Collegium Argentariorum]] stands beside them.
