## Overview {#overview}

Three marks tell you whose city Elissara is: the master's seal of the potters' guild on a bowl, the blue anchor of the [[affiliation-seamensgld|Seamen's Guild of Elissara]] on a sailor's right arm, and the bronze doors of the chapterhouse of [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]]. None of them is the Orzas's. Elissara is a chartered free city of about forty thousand people at the mouth of the [[place-vylarisrvr|River Vilaris]], one of the larger cities of [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión]], and it belongs to no province. Its trade guilds govern it as the [[affiliation-tevranaelissium|Tevran of Elissara]], under a charter of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]].

A supercargo who has made landfall here more times than he can count briefs a young partner on his first call: "Keep your eyes on the quays. Everything the capital buys over the sea comes in here, and goes up the river on a barge, so half the cargoes you see are not for sale and the other half are spoken for. The men who sail the ships are on the roll of the Seamen's Guild, so you hire by the voyage, from the roll. Behind the quays is the market, and the market is full of pots. A master's seal on a bowl is worth more than the bowl. When you need a draft cashed, the Danvar keep one of their largest chapterhouses here. Say who you are when you walk in, and remember there is no governor to complain to. The guilds are the government."

## Arriving {#arriving}

[[place-orzara|Orzara]] lies five days north by road.

Ships come in from the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] past a stone mole with a light at its end and anchor in the [Sea Basin]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, a broad pool where the river's brown water meets the sea's green. The city stands on the low ground behind the basin, inside a brick wall. Its sea entrance is the [Sea Gate]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, a tall double arch on the quay, where the chapter of [[affiliation-twinbldrdr|the Twinblade Order]] keeps its house and its knights stand the threshold beside the guild watch. A traveler from the land side comes by the coast road through the estate country of [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]] and finds the province ending at a ditch and a toll-house a mile outside the walls, where the city's own clerks take the city's own dues.

From the Sea Gate the [Long Quay]{.name data-type="place/feature"} runs upstream along the river for half a mile, lined with warehouses, cranes and the halls of the guilds that work it. At its upper end is the [Barge Basin]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, where the river barges lie three deep waiting for their loads. Between the two basins every cargo for the capital is lifted out of a sea ship, carried along the quay or rafted across, and stowed in a barge to go upriver to Orzara. The quay is loud from dawn to dark with cranes, carts, tally-men calling numbers and gulls.

Behind the quay the streets climb a little to the market square, where the [Charter Hall]{.name data-type="place/structure"} of the Tevran stands on one side and the Danvar chapterhouse on the other, and the potters' streets begin. The kilns are outside the wall on the landward side, and on a still day their smoke lies over the city.

## What Elissara Lives By

Elissara lives by the river mouth. The capital's grain from over the sea, its oil, its timber and its foreign goods all come in here, and none of them can reach Orzara without passing from ship to barge on the Long Quay. The charter lets the capital's cargoes through free of the city's dues, so the city earns its living from everything else: the warehousing and the lifting, the ships' stores and repairs, the dues on every cargo that is not the capital's, and the wages of three thousand seamen who spend them ashore.

The second trade is pottery. Elissara's potters make the plain crockery of half the coast and the patterned ware that sells in the capital, and the potters' guild seals its masters' work. [[being-deklbrmzts|Dekela Baremzates]] holds the master's seal her parents held before her, and her green-glazed bowls are on the tables of houses in Orzara that have never heard her name. The third is money. The Danvar chapterhouse clears the notes of half the merchants on the sea, and the city's spring payment to the treasury goes up the river as a Danvar draft.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order

The Tevran of Elissara governs the city. Each of its nineteen guilds sends one _kelros_ (guild's council member) to the council, and the council chooses a _holkelros_ (first of the council) to preside for a year. The present _holkelros_ is [[being-znthnlnthms|Zenothen Linthimis]], master of the shipwrights, in his third year. There is no _therkos_ (governor), no legion and no imperial tax-farmer inside the walls; the city pays the treasury a fixed sum instead and keeps the river mouth lit and dredged.

Each guild judges its own members, and a stranger with a grievance goes to the hall of the guild whose member wronged him. A theft on the water or the quays goes to the [Roll House]{.name data-type="place/structure"} of the Seamen's Guild, whose hands keep the quays every night. A man of no guild has no court of his own and is wise to find a guildsman to speak for him.

## The People

The city's people can be seen in its trades. [[being-arklrdsklmkns|Arkalordas Kalmakanes]] went to sea from Elissara at seventeen and is the hand its captains call by name. [[being-hrphsrchrns|Harfos Orkarenis]] is a chandler whose candles go up to the stewards of the capital's great houses. [[being-chgnzrznds|Kegan Zerazendis]] keeps the [Zerazendis Inn]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, which her parents kept before her.

## Politics {#present}

Elissara's freedom rests on a charter that every Orzas seals anew, and the old Orzas is dying. The province of [[affiliation-provincvld|Salkalon]], whose land surrounds the city on three sides, holds the charter to be a privilege the province merely tolerates. An advocate from [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] has taken rooms in the city and asks every guild for a copy, saying he is writing a history. The [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]] lends to the throne against the customs of Elissara, and the League's own clerks collect them at a customs house at the river mouth, outside the walls. The fleet, short of seamen, is paying the Seamen's Guild for copies of its roll. Inside the walls the vote is the standing quarrel: the seamen and the bargemen are the largest guilds, the shipowners and merchants hold the seats, and Zenothen Linthimis presides in his third year, two more than custom expects.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The Zerazendis Inn, two streets back from the Long Quay, is the inn for travelers and for the farmers who bring produce down from the estates, and its kitchen is the best in the lower town. Seamen between voyages sleep at the Roll House, and a party that has signed on a ship may be offered a bench there.

## History {#history}

No account dates Elissara's founding or the first grant of its charter, and the city's history is told by its charter's custom: every Orzas seals it anew, and the [[affiliation-tevranaelissium|Tevran of Elissara]] governs under it with no _therkos_ inside the walls. The city's place in the Empire has been the river mouth's. Whatever the capital bought over the sea came up the Vilaris from Elissara's quays, and the city lived on everything else that passed them.

Between about 418 and 425 the Orzas Arzanas founded [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]], the college that charters each of the city's guilds, and made the Tarmeks the central bank of the note system ([[lore-arzanasedict|The Edicts of Arzanas]]); the Danvar, its chartered moneylenders, keep one of their largest chapterhouses beside the Charter Hall. Through the decline that followed, the throne came to pay the dole with borrowed money ([[lore-empirdclne|The Decline of the Vilérian Empire]]), and the customs of Elissara are now among what it borrows against.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The seal.** Bowls carrying Dekela Baremzates's master's seal are selling in the capital, and she did not make them. The potters' guild wants the forger found inside the guild before the council hears of it.
- **The advocate.** An advocate from [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] has taken rooms in the city and is asking every guild for a copy of the charter. He says he is writing a history.
- **The navy's need.** The fleet is short of seamen and is paying the Seamen's Guild for copies of its roll. A hand whose name is on one wants to know what the fleet means to do with it.

# The Truth of Elissara {#truth .secret}

The charter in the chest behind the presiding chair carries the seal of the last Orzas, not the present one. Every Orzas seals Elissara's charter anew in the first year of his reign, and the copy the city sent up for the reigning Orzas's seal lies in the chancery at Orzara, unsealed and unrefused. Zenothen Linthimis pays a clerk of the chancery every spring to keep it at the bottom of the pile, because he believes the Orzas's councilors would refuse it if it came before them and sell the city to the [[affiliation-provincvld|Province of Salkalon]] for the price of a loan.

That is why the council keeps choosing him. The other _kelroi_ know the charter is unsealed, and none of them wants to be the _holkelros_ the day it is found out. The advocate from Delalara is not writing a history. He is the province's, and he has heard that the seal on the city's charter is a dead man's. If he can show it, the province's lawyers will argue that the charter died with the Orzas who sealed it, and the _therkos_ of Salkalon will hold Elissara's harbor dues.

The succession is the city's best hope and its gravest danger. A new Orzas seals every charter brought to him in his first year, and the council means to have Elissara's on his table the first morning. The Province of Salkalon means to have its petition there first.
