## Overview

The coast of [[place-vylariargn|Vylaría Region]] along the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]] is a working shore of shipyards, naval bases and fishing towns, with agrarian estate-country behind it. This is Sâlkhâlon, the whole shore, the land of the [[affiliation-provincvld|Province of Sâlkhâlon]] of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], and five million people live here. Its seat is [[place-mercavia|Mercavia]].

A Mercavia sailing master, taking a new mate along the quays on his first morning, gives him the lay of the province in one pass: "Warships that way, at Castamar. Merchantmen here. Fishing boats everywhere in between, and every one of them is feeding somebody in Ôrzâra who has never seen the sea. Learn which harbor you're in before you talk to anyone, because the three of them want different things from you."

## Economy

Maritime trade, shipbuilding and fishing drive Sâlkhâlon's economy. The imperial shipyards, building both warships and merchant vessels, are the province's largest employer. The port cities also serve as manufacturing centers, processing imported raw materials into finished goods. Sâlkhâlon's fishing fleets supply fresh seafood to the capital and the interior provinces, while the agrarian estates of the inland country grow the grain, wool and leather that feed the ports.

## Notable Features

- The imperial shipyards, the empire's naval construction and repair facilities
- Cosmopolitan port cities with foreign merchant quarters
- Naval bases maintaining the empire's remaining sea power

## Settlements

Geography sets Sâlkhâlon's pattern: two great cities and a working coast of fishing towns and villages, with quieter agrarian estate-country in the interior that feeds them.

**The two cities.**

- [[place-mercavia|Mercavia]] (~50,000)—the provincial capital and economic hub; the empire's busiest commercial harbor, its merchant houses, and its foreign quarters.
- [[place-castamar|Castamar]] (~20,000)—the naval and military hub; the imperial shipyards and the seat of the fleet command. It is not the capital, but it is the empire's sword at sea.

**The four great fishing towns** (each ~5,000–10,000), strung along the coast and supplying fish to the capital and the interior:

- [[place-volkhemara|Vôlkhêmâra]] (~8,000)—salt-fish and curing.
- [[place-braldvik|Braldvík]] (~9,000)—the largest, deep-bay fishing and smoke-fish, and the one Nordmal name on the coast, left by a raiding crew stranded there in 523.
- [[place-tholkezora|Thôlkezôra]] (~7,000)—deep-water crews and sea-beast oil.
- [[place-thremuzara|Thrêmûzâra]] (~6,000)—salt-pans and the curing of the lesser villages' catch.

**The lesser fishing villages.** Some dozen small communities of 500–1,500 people line the coast between the great towns, each living by its boats, its nets, and the curing-houses of [[place-thremuzara|Thrêmûzâra]] and [[place-volkhemara|Vôlkhêmâra]]. Among them are **Nîzêlenna**, **Sathêrenna**, **Zâmbêlenna**, **Thêrsôlenna**, **Thôlmârenna**, **Talôthenna**, **Ôrnêthenna**, **Pâlgezenna**, **Hûrâdenna**, **Khêdrîlenna**, **Lênthizenna** and **Ilthêrenna**.

**The inland country.**

- [[place-terravia|Terravia]]—a market town among the agrarian estates of Sâlkhâlon's interior, the province's landward face.
- [[place-kurzalora|Kûrzâlôra]]—a coastal pilgrimage town.
