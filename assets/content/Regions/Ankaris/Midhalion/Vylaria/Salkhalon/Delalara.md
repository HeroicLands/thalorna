## Overview

Dêlâlâra is the busiest commercial harbor still under imperial control. With about fifty thousand people it is the largest city of [[affiliation-provincvld|Sâlkhâlon]] and its provincial capital, the trading face the province shows to the sea.

A Tarûzelian factor who has kept a counting-room in its foreign quarter for twenty years describes the quays to a newly arrived partner: "Merchantmen two deep along the wharves, warehouses full of goods bound inland and abroad, and our quarter and the Heliónite one a short walk apart. The shipping houses of Sâlkhâlon keep their counting-rooms here, and since Tarûzel's secession squeezed their eastern trade they have been loud about wanting it back."

Dêlâlâra is the most cosmopolitan city outside the capital, and the loudest voice in the empire for engagement with [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Tarûzel]] rather than enmity. Its foreign quarters hold Tarûzelian and [[place-helionis|Heliónite]] factors. The provincial _thêrkhôs_ (governor) sits here, but real power is shared with the merchant houses whose money the province runs on.
