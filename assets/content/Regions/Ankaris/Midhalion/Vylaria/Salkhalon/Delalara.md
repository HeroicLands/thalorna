## Overview

Delalara is the busiest commercial harbor still under imperial control. With about fifty thousand people it is the largest city of the [[affiliation-provincvld|Province of Salkalon]] and its provincial capital, the trading face the province shows to the sea.

A Taruzelian factor who has kept a counting-room in its foreign quarter for twenty years describes the quays to a newly arrived partner: "Merchantmen two deep along the wharves, warehouses full of goods bound inland and abroad, and our quarter and the Heliónite one a short walk apart. The shipping houses of Salkalon keep their counting-rooms here, and since the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]]' secession squeezed their eastern trade they have been loud about wanting it back."

Delalara is the most cosmopolitan city outside the capital, and the loudest voice in the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] for engagement with [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Taruzel]] rather than enmity. Its foreign quarters hold Taruzelian and [[place-helionis|Heliónite]] factors. The provincial _therkos_ (governor) sits here, but real power is shared with the merchant houses whose money the province runs on.
