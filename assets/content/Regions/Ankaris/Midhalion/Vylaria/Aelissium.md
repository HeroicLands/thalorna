## Overview

Three marks tell you whose city Aelissium is: the master's seal of the potters' collegium, the mark of the Seamen's Collegium on a sailor's right arm, and the chapterhouse of the [[affiliation-clgmrgntrrm|Collegium Argentariorum]]. It is a trade city of about 40,000 in [[place-vylariargn|Vylaría]], one of the larger cities of [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]], and a working city of ships, kilns and counting rooms.

A supercargo who has made landfall here more times than he can count briefs a young partner on his first call: "Keep your eyes on the quays. The ships out of Aelissium run grain to the capital, and the men who sail them are on the roll of the Seamen's Collegium, so you hire by the voyage, from the roll. Behind the quays is the market, and the market is full of pots. The potters have a collegium of their own, and a master's seal on a bowl is worth more than the bowl. And when you need a draft cashed, the Argentariorum keep one of their largest chapterhouses in the city. Say who you are when you walk in."

The city's people can be seen in its trades. [[being-arklrdsklmkns|Arkálòrdâs Kâlmâkanês]] went to sea from Aelissium at seventeen and is the hand its captains ask for by name. [[being-deklbrmzts|Dékêla Bàremzâtês]] holds the potters' master's seal, which her parents held before her.
