## Overview

Soraks is a city of thirty thousand with four thousand people living in it. It stands in northern [[place-alyssa|Alessa]], in the [[affiliation-provinclys|Province of Alessa]] of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]], on the old north road from [[place-orzara|Orzara]], and it was built to the measure of that road: a mile of colonnaded street, forums, baths and a council house for a hundred. The road still runs through it, paved and straight, and on its far side, past the river, it runs on to nowhere a cart can reach. The bridge is down, the traffic goes by another road a day to the east, and Soraks is the end of its road, not a stage on it.

A stone-carter who has hauled out of Soraks for twenty years teaches his new boy on the first morning: "Count the columns as we go in. A rope round one means it is sold. A red mark means it is the council's, and you do not touch it. The rest belong to whoever finds the owner first, and the owners are in the tombs along the road, so mostly it is the council. We load from the top down, the carved head first, then the drums, and you keep your feet out from under them. There is more stone in this town than there are people, and we are taking it to [[place-vennara|Vennara]] one cart at a time."

## Arriving

From the south the north road comes across farmland, straight as a rule, with grass growing in the joints of its paving. Its milestones still stand, cut to the pattern of the _pozerus_ (warden of the roads), and the post-house at the last mile is shut, its stable roofless and its trough full of leaves. The tombs along the last mile are old and handsome and nobody's newest; the families who built them live in Alessar and Lentora now, or nowhere.

The road enters by a gate arch carrying the city's name in letters a man's height, and becomes the [Long Colonnade]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, a mile of covered porticoes on both sides of the street, built for shops. The southern quarter of it is alive: shops, a cookhouse, a cobbler, a copyist at a table, washing hung between the columns. North of that the porticoes are empty, their back rooms without doors, their floors grazed by the town's goats. Further north still the columns themselves begin to go. Some have ropes round them, some stand bare against the sky with their roof gone, and some are stumps, with the drums lying in the street waiting for the carts.

The living town is gathered round the [Bench Square]{.name data-type="place/feature"} at the southern end of the colonnade. On its north side stands the court-house of the [Northern Bench]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, the provincial court that sits here, with the registry behind it; on the east side the council house, too big for its council; and on the west a shrine of [[affiliation-pozeras|Pozeras]], the Wayfarer, whose priest keeps the lamps lit for travelers who come mostly to go to law. The house with new shutters at the corner of the square is the one strangers are told about.

North of the town the road runs down to the river and the [Broken Bridge]{.name data-type="place/structure"}. Three of its arches stand. The middle one lies in the water in great blocks the river breaks white over, and the road climbs out on the far bank, still paved, into empty country.

## What Soraks Lives By

Soraks lives by the province's paperwork and by its own stone. The Northern Bench hears the land suits of the estates of northern Alessa, and their deeds are kept in the registry behind it. A suit takes weeks, so litigants, their stewards and their witnesses lodge in the town, and the advocates, copyists and notaries who serve them are the town's best-paid people. Every one of them came up the north road, and every one of them complains about it.

The stone is the town's second living. The council sells the empty quarters a column at a time, and the carters take them south to the builders of villas in the hills round Vennara and the country houses of the senatorial families, who pay well for old marble with the city's mason-marks on it. The council licenses each column, takes a share of its price, and pays the town's taxes with it. The rest of the town lives as a country town does: a tannery by the river, market gardens inside the walls where the eastern forum was, and sheep on the grass of the baths.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order

Soraks is a chartered town of the Province of Alessa, with two _tavemoi_ (magistrates) elected every year and a council of _kurthaloi_ (councilors) who answer in their own persons for the taxes it owes. Twelve of them sit in the front rows of a council house built for a hundred. The province assesses the town at a figure fixed for a much larger city, and the _kurthaloi_ meet it by selling columns. The senior _tavemos_ is [[being-iltherosguri|Iltheros Gurimandis]], a cloth-merchant who has spent his life trying to get the bridge mended, and he signs every column sale himself, so he knows the price of each.

The Northern Bench is the province's, not the town's. Its judge is appointed from Alessar, and its clerks and registrars are provincial men who answer to the Hall there; a clerk of the registry outranks a _tavemos_ in the town's own street, and both of them know it. Order in the town rests on the square: a theft goes to a _tavemos_, a quarrel over land goes to the Bench, and a quarrel over a column goes to Iltheros, who has the ledger.

## The Old Houses

Soraks keeps a handful of old families who measure themselves against one another and against what the town once was. The clearest example is [[affiliation-zarkedis|House Zarkedis]]. The house with the new shutters on the square is its seat in name only: the creditors sold it with everything in it, the masks in the cupboard by the door included, and the disgraced master of the house lives in two rented rooms behind the tannery. Its heir, [[being-klstrtsxrchds|Kalestratos Zarkedis]], is a [[affiliation-pirethos|Pirethos]] mage of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] at [[place-velysara|Velisara]], and the fall of his house is what he says when he is asked why he wants what he wants.

## The Present {#present}

Soraks pays a large city's assessment and sells its colonnades to meet it. Iltheros Gurimandis, the senior _tavemos_, signs every column sale himself and has spent his life trying to get the bridge mended. A villa-builder's agent and a second agent each hold a bill for the same twelve columns of the western portico, sealed a month apart. The registry's page naming the buyer of the [[affiliation-zarkedis|Zarkedis]] house at the creditors' sale has been cut from the book. An antiquary's monograph, drawn from a chancery docket of the Republic, is on order with the copyists of Alessar, and two men from the capital have come up the north road to ask him to withdraw it.

## Where a Party Lodges

The [Last Mile]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, on the Bench Square opposite the court-house, is full of litigants and lets its rooms by the week, not the night. It is clean, slow and dear. The old post-house inns along the colonnade are shut, but the cookhouse by the gate lets a loft to travelers who arrive after the Last Mile has closed its door.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The column sold twice.** A villa-builder's agent has come up from Vennara with a bill of sale for twelve columns of the western portico, sealed by the council. A second agent holds a bill for the same twelve, sealed by the council a month later, and Iltheros Gurimandis's ledger shows one sale.
- **The cut page.** The creditors' sale of the Zarkedis house was recorded in the registry, and the page that names the buyer has been cut from the book. The clerk who cut it still works behind the court-house.
- **The monograph.** An antiquary of Soraks has read, in the registry's oldest boxes, a docket of the chancery of the [Vilérian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} that names something kept for the Republic as an egg, and has written a monograph saying what it was. Copyists in Alessar have it on order, and two men from the capital have come up the north road to ask him to withdraw it.
- **The bridge.** A Lentora merchant says the bridge could be mended in a summer if someone found the stone, and Soraks has more stone than anything else. He wants a party to cross the river and see what the far road is like now.

# The Truth of Soraks {#truth .secret}

The Broken Bridge is in the province's accounts as under repair. The _pozerus_ at Alessar assesses the levy for it on the estates of northern Alessa every year, and the clerks enter it as spent on the bridge. It is spent on the roads nearer the capital, where the senators drive, and the only work done at the bridge is a survey every few years that finds the arch too costly to raise. Iltheros Gurimandis has asked to see the accounts four times and been refused each time by letter.

The worse news for Soraks lies in the Hall at Alessar. An act moving the Northern Bench and its registry south to [[place-lentora|Lentora]], where the roads are good, is written, sealed and waiting to be read from the steps. Without the Bench the litigants stop coming, and without them Soraks is a quarry with a council. Nobody in Soraks has seen the act. The clerk who cut the page from the Zarkedis ledger has heard of it, and he is hoping to go to Lentora with the registry.
