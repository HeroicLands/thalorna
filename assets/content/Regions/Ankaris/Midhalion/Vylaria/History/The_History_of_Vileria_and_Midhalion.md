An old pensioner on the steps of a Circle in [[place-orzara|Orzara City]], waiting with his token for the month's pension, is asked by a foreigner in the queue what the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] is, and gives its history in four sentences. "The first man of my family earned his citizenship with twenty years under the eagles, and I earned this token with twenty more. Every Orzas pays the pension before he pays anything else, because the one year it went unpaid the city burned. The money that paid it came from the provinces, and the provinces have gone. Now it comes from the bankers, and so do the Orzoi." It is a fair summary of seven centuries, and the ages below are the long form of it.

The lands of the [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión Region]] that this history follows are three: the [[place-vylariargn|Viléria Region]], the peninsula of the [[place-vylarisrvr|River Vylaris]] where the Empire began and where it has drawn back to; the [[place-nartumkngdm|Nartun Kingdom]] in its forested north-east, which left the Empire over a question of faith; and the [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]], the islands of the eastern sea that left it over money twelve years ago. Their history is told here in the ages that Viléria's historians count ([[lore-vylariages|The Vilérian Ages]]), and every year is a year of [[lore-commoncal|the Vilérian Calendar]]. Each age closes on what it left behind, because a party arriving in any of these lands walks through what the ages built, burned and owe.

## The Kings and the Republic

Of the kings who ruled the city on the Vylaris nothing is recorded but their end. About 650 BF the patrician families overthrew the last of them and set up a Republic governed by a Senate of landed men ([[lore-vylarirpbl|The Establishment of the Vilérian Republic]]). In two and a half centuries the [Vilérian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} grew from a city-state into the power of the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]]. Taruzel's trading cities were among the first lands it absorbed, and they gave the Republic the trade of the eastern sea.

The Republic's great war was fought against [[place-helionis|Helionis]], whose philosophers had joined their schools in [[affiliation-panepistmm|the Epistémion]] about 400 BF ([[lore-panepistfnd|The Founding of the Epistémion]]). From 335 to 312 BF the legions took the [[affiliation-ctysttshlns|Helionite city-states]] one at a time, and the cities answered with their mages in the worst magical warfare the western part of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]] has seen ([[lore-helionscnq|The Conquest of Helionis]]). The Republic answered with the [Dragon Riders]{.name data-type="affiliation"}, near whom a working turns back on the one who makes it. In 330 BF it took [[place-theradon2|Theradon]] and the workshop of its mage-warlord ([[lore-dragondead|The Dragon Dead at Theradon]]), and what the Republic gave the dragons for their part in the war is written in no account of it ([[lore-thebargain|The Bargain]]). In the year the last city fell, the Senate cut the College of Arcane Philosophy out of the Epistémion and placed its arcanists under a magistracy, [[affiliation-kandral|The Arcane Magistracy]] (the Kandral), which kept them on a register as state property.

The register ended in fire. In 75 BF the Kandral ordered every registered practitioner in the Republic to bring their working-books to one place in the country of [[place-alyssa|Alessa]] to be copied, and in the fourth month an unchanneled release burned the books and killed most of the Republic's senior arcanists ([[lore-recensio|The Therona of Alessa]]). Whether it was accident or act has never been established. In 73 BF the surviving masters, a few score of them, drafted their own terms, and the Senate took them because there was nobody else to ask ([[lore-kandrol|The Kandrol]]). The charter made [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Thordimalon]] (the Arcane Order) the only lawful practice of the arcane in the Republic's lands, and constituted the [Sekran]{.name data-type="affiliation"} a clause earlier to enforce it. For a century the Sekran enrolled, imprisoned or killed every practitioner outside the register, with legions lent for the work ([[lore-huntingyears|The Hunting Years]]).

About 45 BF the standing lease of the dragons was not renewed. On the Order's counsel the Republic ended the term, the killing followed, the Dragon Riders were disbanded, and the Eyrie on [[place-arvel|Arvel]] was sealed ([[lore-ridersdisb|The Lease Refused]]). The reason the Republic gave was the dragons' effect on magic, and the records it suppressed were the records of the terms. In the west the Republic had already planted a garrison colony among the kings of the Provenzal shore, about 110 BF, and when it withdrew the garrison the colony stayed ([[lore-aurelianefound|The Founding of Aurelane]]).

The late Republic was a quarrel of generals and senators, and in Year 1 a _kurthos_ (senator) with loyal legions and the Arcane Order behind him took supreme power and called himself Emperor ([[lore-thefounding|The Founding]]). No record gives his name. The Senate survived as an advisory body, and western Ankaris counts its years from that day.

**What it left.** The calendar every clerk from Midhalión to [[place-aelwyth|Aelwyth]] writes in. The Thordimalon's monopoly, its register and the Sekran's prisons. A fenced tract in the Alessan country that has never been dug. A sealed hill under the capital, and a quarrel with the dragons whose terms the chancery never wrote down.

## The First Orzoi, 1 to 95

The Founder's line built the Empire's first institutions in a century. The first Orzas walled the crown of Arvel above the sealed galleries, built the Senate house and the Guard Camp inside the new wall, and moved [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] (the Senate) up from its old hall on [[place-antel|the Old Hill]] ([[lore-eaglecityrse|The Raising of the Eagle City]]). He walked and consecrated the thrima, the sacred boundary, around the whole of the [[place-sixhillsvyl|Six Hills]], and gave the city the name Orzara. The Republic's capital became the Old Hill, and the hill above it became the throne's.

The second Orzas, [Arkanamos]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, made the citizenship a reward for twenty years under the eagles about 34 AF, and tied to it the dole, a pension for life graded by a veteran's last rank, and the right to live inside the thrima ([[lore-veteranslaw|The Veterans' Law]]). The nobility were left the only citizens by birth. In one statute he recruited his legions and bound the men who filled them to his throne, and while conquest paid for the pensions nobody counted what they would cost when it stopped. The line ended after ninety-five years when [Halanor]{.name data-type="being/npc"} died without a son and named a _felzos_ (legate) of [[affiliation-varkalenis|House Varkalenis]] to follow him.

**What it left.** The [Inner Wall]{.name data-type="place/structure"} on Arvel, the only wall Orzara has, and the [[place-palvar|Palvar]] standing on galleries [[affiliation-sorvan|the Sorvan]] (the Imperial Guard) keeps shut. A capital whose citizen body is at its core a veterans' class. The dole, which is the largest standing charge on the Empire and the one no Orzas dares miss. The resident's waiver to sleep inside the thrima, and the clerks who sell it.

## The Age of Conquest, 96 to 388

For three centuries the legions pushed every frontier. From about 140 they fought west and north-west ([[lore-westernwars|The Western Wars]]): they took the lowlands of the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]] and occupied [[place-leonrik|Lienrik]], failed four times in the mountains of the [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]], made the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]] a province and the [[place-elavendre|Élavendre Region]] a tributary, fortified the western strait from its northern shore, and planted boundary-stones at the passes of the [[place-kervaron|Kervaron Spine]], which the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] clans closed behind every expedition. Between about 209 and 214 the Orzas [Karomfos]{.name data-type="being/npc"} turned east as well, and a fleet from the Vilérian coast took the harbors of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]] in a season ([[lore-heladeastwar|The Eastern War]]).

The same Orzas made the break with Nartun. About 220 the faith of [[lore-ustarondty|Ústaron]], the Forge-Lord, split into [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] and [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] ([[lore-ustaranschism|The Ústaran Schism]]). The orthodox clergy asked Karomfos to proscribe the new faith, and he declined, because it had temples in his capital and officers in his legions. About 224 the high priesthood of [[place-nartum|Nartun]], the oldest sacred city of the heartland, withdrew from imperial obedience, anointed a king of its own, [Daronos]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, and raised [[affiliation-sacredflame|the Order of the Sacred Flame]] to hold its walls ([[lore-nartumrefuse|Nartun Refuses the Empire]]). The legions sent to bring the city back found its gates shut, and they turned back at the first winter. The kingdom says the Empire strayed and Nartun kept the path; the Empire says Nartun is a wayward province that will come back. No Orzas has sent a second army.

The east brought the Empire its next war secondhand. About 315 a steppe horde rode round the western end of [[place-graznmntns|the Grazian Mountains]], crossed the [[place-dunharargn|Dunhara Region]] and broke up at the walls of the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]] ([[lore-hndrdbnnrs|The Storm of the Hundred Banners]]). The eastern legions expected its scattered bands to cross the strait to Taruzel, and from about 316 to 330 they built a line of hill forts and a military road across the interior of the larger islands, garrisoned by hill horsemen on the Empire's pay ([[lore-easternline|The Eastern Line]]). Whether any band ever crossed is not known. In the same years, about 318, the imperial archive on Arvel burned with most of three centuries of edicts and the greater part of the Republic's rolls ([[lore-archivefire|The Archive Fire]]). The [[affiliation-imprlscrblgld|Imperial Scribal Guild]] took custody of everything written after it, and nobody asked aloud whether the fire was set.

The age ended with its greatest adventure. In 377 the Orzas [Barnakis]{.name data-type="being/npc"} sent the strongest field army the Empire had across the sea to the delta of the Khelathi river, where it broke the chariot corps at [[place-khelaga|Khelaga]] ([[lore-deltaexped|The Delta Expedition]]). The river's priesthood crowned its commander under the throne name [Zab'el'Qar]{.name data-type="being/npc"}. He never came home, and the chancery entered him as a traitor and stopped writing his name.

**What it left.** The [Paldor]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, Orzara's avenue of conquest trophies. Lienrik's watchtowers, the strait forts and Provénzia's Vilérian law, all now held by others. The boundary-stones on the Kervaron, honored by the clans on the clans' terms. The [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]] in the Empire's own heartland, which speaks an older Vilari and admits no Arcane Order. The open temples of the Black Flame in Orzara and Alessa. Ruined hill forts on Taruzel's islands and a tradition of hill tribes serving whoever pays. An archive whose originals begin in 318, and a senatorial house that will not speak of one of its sons.

## The Years of Three Eagles, 389 to 405

Barnakis died in 389 with no heir named and his best army in the Khelathi delta ([[lore-brkensuccsn|The Broken Succession]]). The Sorvan opened the Guard Camp to its own candidate, [Kadelos]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, within the day. The Senate acclaimed [Sylarion]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a senator of an old house with precedent and no army. The legions of [[place-moktur|Moktur]] acclaimed their legate, [Makandas]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, and marched. Each man carried an eagle and held a part of the Empire's revenue, and for sixteen years the Empire had three Orzoi.

The claims were settled over bread. [[place-hylen|Hilen]]'s estates grow the grain that is milled into the capital's bread, and about 401 the Senate's army and the Moktur legions met on the Olkassa estate there, legions against legions under the same eagle, with no quarter given ([[lore-olcassabtl|The Battle of Olkassa]]). Makandas won the field and lost half his army on it, and the harvest was lost with the granaries. About 403 the bread ran short in Orzara and the dole went unpaid. The Circles rioted, the Watch lost the valley wards, and fire ran from the [[place-warrensorz|Warrens]] over Antel for six days ([[lore-greatburnng|The Great Burning]]). The families of the Old Hill moved to the newer slopes of [[place-selvin|the Laurel]] and never went back.

**What it left.** The [[place-olcassafld|Blood-field of Olkassa]], a harvest field that grows the best wheat in Hilen and that the reapers cut round. Hilen's great houses' horror of any claimant who marches on another Vilérian army. The Guard Camp's knowledge that its gates make reigns, the Senate's habit of acclaiming a man of its own whenever a throne falls vacant, and Moktur's pride in having made the reigning house. The Old Hill half-empty, the Laurel full, and the district laws that carve the city into wards a fire can gut without leaping. Above all, the fear of a missed dole.

## The Restoration, 406 to 511

About 406 Makandas took the city from the north ([[lore-restorehouse|The Restoration]]). The Senate acclaimed him within the week, the Sorvan handed over Kadelos, and Sylarion died in the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]] in a room whose door was not opened for two days. Makandas forgave the legions that had fought him and paid them all, his own and the Senate's, with the largest donative the Empire had given. He founded [House Helarkales]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} and reigned twelve years, and his house's name is cut into the colonnades and aqueducts it paid to rebuild.

His successor, [Arzanas]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, gave the Empire the machinery it runs on today between about 418 and 425 ([[lore-arzanasedict|The Edicts of Arzanas]]). He closed the western conquests by edict and founded [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]] to put every conquered city's guilds under one charter. He constituted [[affiliation-tolvan|the Tolvan]] and its tax-farmers, made [[affiliation-tarmex|the Tarmeks Orzan]] the central bank of the paper-note system and built its strongroom, rebuilt the Palvar, and lined the Paldor with three centuries of trophies. For a century the Empire reached from the Élavendren border to the coast of Taruzel, and the pensions were paid from tribute.

**What it left.** Nearly every institution a party deals with in Viléria: the guild charter, the tax-farmers, the central bank and its paper. "Three centuries ago" is the phrase Vilérian notes give for all of them. The donative an Orzas owes the legions and the Guard at his accession. The line "restored by Makandas" on every monument in the capital, and the Helarkales who still sit the throne.

## The Drift, 512 to 619

The Drift began in the north. From 512 to 514 three winters ran together with no summer between them across the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands Region]] and the forest south of them ([[lore-threewinters|The Three Winters]]), and from 515 the hunger sent [[lore-varokhiclt|Varokh]] war-bands south over the passes of [[place-vandstein|Vandstein]] into northern Moktur in numbers the province had never met ([[lore-hungerraids|The Hunger Raids]]). The valleys emptied, and [[place-dolvar|Dolvar]] was raised to a legion fortress. In 518 a war-band burned the camp of the [Pelsan Hilvan]{.name data-type="affiliation"} and carried off its eagle. In the spring of 520 the host that carried it came south again, the largest the raids had sent, and the [Karsan Soldan Korvan]{.name data-type="affiliation"} closed the valley above it and drove it down into the marsh at the valley's foot, where it died to the last man ([[lore-blackmarshbtl|The Battle of the Black Marsh]]). The legion took the eagle out of the reeds and has carried a gold band on its name since.

The west was struck from the sea. From 519 to 530 the fleets of the [[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Kingdom of Nordheim]] went south every summer against the Empire's western provinces ([[lore-southrnfleets|The Southern Fleets]]). In 523 a crew from the [[affiliation-kingdomlgn|Kingdom of Malagna]] sailed a captured galley through the western strait under imperial colors and came ashore on the [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]] coast ([[lore-bankedfire|The Winter of the Banked Fire]]). A squadron from [[place-castamar|Kastamar]] burned the galley, and the fire took the fishing village's boats with it. The crew wintered on the headland, and in 524 the survivors took terms: seven years at the oars of the Kastamar fleet. A generation later, about 550, the Empire's frontier commanders were hiring Nordmen by the season to hold what the raids had loosened ([[lore-compactsignd|The Signing of the Compact]]).

Meanwhile the far provinces stopped obeying one at a time ([[lore-empirdclne|The Decline of the Vilérian Empire]]). From about 538 Tarvénia's lowlands stopped paying and its mountain lords elected a High King to drive the garrisons out ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]). About 571 the five cities of the Bizarian League re-swore their old Compact and paid the frontier garrisons themselves ([[lore-heladreswear|The Re-swearing of the Compact]]). From about 609 to 618 Provénzia's estates fought the _therkos_'s (governor's) legion for nine years and won a Charter of Rights held by a king of their choosing ([[lore-charterwar|The War of the Charter]]). About 619 the Orzas [Azénales]{.name data-type="being/npc"} received the envoys of the Aureldian crowns and the League and settled what the Empire still was ([[lore-crownssettle|The Settlement of the Crowns]]). The crowns and the League went free of imperial governance and tax and stayed inside the note system at a two-percent fee to the Tarmeks; Helionis stayed a province on the chancery's roll; the far forts' commissions went unpaid.

**What it left.** The watchtowers of the northern march, where the sergeants teach recruits to count the smoke and not to look at the marsh. The [[place-blackmarshfld|Blood-field of the Black Marsh]] below them. [[place-braldvik|Bralvik]], whose smokehouses are lit from a fire a Nordman banked, and whose bill for forty-one boats goes to the Kastamar admiralty every autumn unpaid. Nordmen companies in the Empire's pay. The two-percent fee, the Empire's largest revenue from lands it does not govern, which the crowns now want renegotiated. Hereditary commanders in [[place-unpaidfort|The Unpaid Fort]] and its like, who file a yearly report and wait for an answer.

## The Core, 620 to 707

After the Settlement the Empire was Viléria, Helionis on paper, and Taruzel. It kept the forms of a great power, stopped paying for what it could not hold, and looked for revenue in the one rich province it still governed.

About 680, under a regency, a regent's council wanted a victory to show the Senate, and it chose the one boundary the Empire had claimed and never held ([[lore-twelfthloss|The Loss of the Twelfth]]). It sent the [Doran Hilvan]{.name data-type="affiliation"} into the Kervaron to read the boundary-stones to the clans, without asking the chancery. The Áelendan let the legion climb, closed the passes behind it and waited for the snow. The army struck the Twelfth from the rolls as destroyed, and its eagle never came back.

In the east the court answered Taruzel's wealth with tariffs. The trading families of [[place-besikar2|Besikar]], [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]] and [[place-bazunel2|Bazunel]] had grown rich as the Empire's middlemen in the eastern trade, and through the Core their exports bore new tariffs, their harbor fees rose, and their _therkoi_ were harder men sent to bring them to heel. They had no seat in the Senate and could contest none of it.

**What it left.** The passage-gift every merchant pays at the first camp below the Kervaron passes without haggling. [[place-twelfthcamp|The Twelfth's Last Camp]], a ruin on the Vilérian side of the range. The Twelfth's eagle, which the disbanded legion's families want found and the legions raised in its place want left where it lies ([[miscgear-twelftheagle|The Lost Eagle of the Twelfth]]). And a province of merchants who had decided the Empire cost more than it was worth.

## After the Shoals, 708 to the Present

The Taruzelian merchant princes began with petitions and went on to slow the harbors, and the Empire answered with arrests and more legions. In the winter before 707 they printed broadsheets at guild expense, signed "the people of Taruzel", with a broken chain and a rising sun on them, and in 707 the harbors, the fishing villages and the hill tribes rose ([[lore-taruzelwar|The Taruzelian War of Independence]]). The legions won on land: they besieged Besikar and routed the militias in three engagements on the coastal plains. The hill horsemen of the old Eastern Line rode out of their forts and joined their own tribes. The [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]] sent gold, steel, naval stores and advisors under the name of commerce, and Amradad helped across the strait. A flotilla of armed traders, fishing boats with rams spiked to their bows and harbor boats full of archers closed the island channels to imperial convoys.

On the thirteenth and fourteenth of [Brazemandi]{.name data-type="lore/calendar"} in 708 the largest squadron the Empire had ever sent into the eastern sea, some forty warships out of Kastamar, followed the flotilla into the [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]] and was destroyed there in two days and a night ([[lore-tamzirbattle|The Battle of Tamzir Shoals]]). The island garrisons fell within months, the fortress over the narrows at Yamuzel was given up by treaty, and about 709 the Empire recognized the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]]. Kastamar holds that Taruzelian pilots steered the leading ships onto the sand. The Confederation holds that its captains chose the ground.

The guilds who paid for the war wrote the peace ([[lore-taruzelbetrayal|The Betrayal of the Revolution]]). The charter of about 709 counts every vote of the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]] by heads and again by each member's deposits with the treasury. The veterans had been promised land, guild places, pensions and a voice, and they were discharged with a small purse of silver. The [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]], founded by the captains of the Shoals, passed to the shipowners. About 710 the captains and militia leaders who would not be quiet were outlawed by acts of the Grand Council, and took to the channels as [[affiliation-freecaptains|the Free Captains]] and [[affiliation-shoalsbrthd|the Shoals Brotherhood]], with [[affiliation-unchained|the Unchained]] ashore. Since 709 Yamuzel has held the [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]] every year on the day of the victory.

In Orzara the defeat produced a report. The Dukran's account of the war, which blamed purchased commissions for engagements the Empire should have won, leaked to the senatorial houses, and about 710 a compromise was brokered ([[lore-commissioncmp|The Commission Compromise]]). A candidate raised from the commoner track to the rank of Vallar now sits an examination, the Reformist Faction sits on the Tarmeks's audit, and noble commissions are bought as before. The Orzas keeps the question "under continuing review".

**What it left.** The wrecks of the squadron on the Shoals with their bronze rams still on them, and a captured warship in a dry-dock at Besikar. The names of the drowned on the eastern mole at Kastamar. The broadsheets on the walls of the veterans' halls, the tenements below and the guild houses above. A Khelathi debt the Confederation denies in public and repays in harbor dues. A war indemnity the Empire owes and will not pay. Career centurions with a grievance, and a navy being rebuilt.

## What It Leaves Today {#what-it-left}

The year is 720. Every account above ends in something still owed, and the present is the reckoning of those debts.

The throne of Viléria pays the dole that Arkanamos tied to the citizenship with money borrowed from the great houses of [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]], rolls the loans over each quarter and puts nothing by, and the capital's granaries hold about a week. The old Orzas, [[being-zrmlshlrkls|Zarmelas Helarkales]], is seventy-eight and failing, and he has adopted nobody. The court names three men. [[being-klvnsmrdvns|Kolvenas Mardovanes]], of the house that gave the Empire Azénales, is the Senate's and the grain's choice. [[being-hlzrsvndrn|Halzoras Vendaren]], a legate of the legions, is the Guard's favorite. [[being-dlmrsvrklns|Dalmeras Varkalenis]], of the house Halanor named, is poor, pious and the old houses' man. The Sorvan learned in the Years of Three Eagles that its gates make reigns, and it expects a donative from whoever is adopted. The expected man has promised one and paid nothing on it, and a rival house offers the Guard's officers a larger sum now, before anyone is adopted. The watches are weighing the promise against the coin.

In Taruzel the veterans of the Shoals are growing old, and their children have inherited the purse and the grievance. The halls of Yamuzel and the Free Captains talk of a rising, and the whole coast wants [[being-zbzmjkdn|Zabuzum Jakuduni]] to lead it; he has answered nobody. The Fleet Review brings the whole coast to the walls of Yamuzel in early summer, and the Grand Council's summer session follows it a few weeks later with the veterans' petition on its order of business again.

Between the two, the old quarrels keep their shape. Viléria refuses the indemnity, its war party wants the islands back, and the treasury cannot pay for a war. Au'Khelathu, whose gold won Taruzel its freedom, offers grain on credit to the throne it humbled. Nartun's high priesthood moves to judge its own king while the forest of its uplands dies, and the High Flame-Warden of the Black Flame that Karomfos would not close has drafted a petition for the Forge-Lord's niche in Orzara's [[place-pnthnrldnm|Temple of the Twelve]]. None of these is settled, and each is a place for a party to stand.
