## Overview {#overview}

The River Vilaris gave the heartland its name, and it runs through it. It rises in the hills of the interior, crosses the [[affiliation-provinclys|Province of Alessa]], flows past the western flank of [[place-orzara|Orzara]], and reaches the sea on the coast of the [[affiliation-provincvld|Province of Salkalon]] at [[place-aelissium|Elissara]], a chartered city that belongs to no province. For a land that lives on hauled goods, it is the one road that carries a cargo without a team.

A barge-master on the lower river puts it to the passengers who come aboard at the capital: "Every sack you saw in the gate-yards this morning came the long way round, by water or by road. We are the water. We bring the grain in to the city's wharves under [Nerval]{.name data-type="place/feature"} in the dark, so the bakers have flour at dawn, and we take the empties back. The river does not care about [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]], the Senate. It cares about the rains in the hills."

Orzara stands on the river's eastern bank, beneath the [[place-sixhillsvyl|Six Hills]], and its [[place-wharvesorz|Wharves]] and the [River Circle]{.name data-type="place/site"} lie along the water.

## The Name

In the [[skill-vylarilng|Vilari Language]] the river is Vilaras, and it carries the hallowed ending a god's name takes. It is the only river in the language that does. The empire was born beside it, and the land and its people take their name from it, so a Vilérian swearing by the river swears by something nearer a god than a road. A boatman's oath on a bargain is sworn with a hand in the water, and a boatman who breaks one is not hired again on any landing between the hills and the sea.

## The Upper River

The river comes down out of the interior hills fast and cold, and it is navigable from the point where the road network meets it, at the landing of [[place-ostenna|Ostenna]] on its bluff. Above Ostenna it is a hill river, shallow over gravel and good only for rafts of timber in the spring flood. Below it the barges begin. The inland estates send their grain down the road to the Landing at Ostenna, and the barges carry it downstream on the current to the capital, in strings of six and eight with a steersman on each and a pilot in the first. Teams of oxen walk the towpath back upstream with the empties.

The river rises with the spring rains in the hills and falls through the summer. By late summer the shoals above the capital are a hand's breadth under the keel of a loaded barge, and the boatmen lighten their loads, wait for rain or pay a gang to drag them over. The weeks of low water fall just before the new harvest comes down the river, when the state granaries in the capital are at their lowest, and the price of a sack in Orzara follows the river's height as closely as any gauge.

## At the Capital

The river passes Orzara along the city's western flank, under the slope of Nerval, the River Hill, where the wharves and the River Circle line the bank. The barges from upstream come in there by night and the barges from the sea by day, and the quays are never empty. Outside the city the water-meadows of the [Vilarpolthamis]{.name data-type="place/site"} run along the bank, and below everything the city drinks stand the tanneries, dye-works and fulling-mills of the outskirts, which empty their vats into the river.

## The Lower River

Below the capital the Vilaris is broad, brown and slow, winding between levees through the flat estate country toward the sea. Everything the capital buys over the sea comes up this stretch. Ships cannot pass far above the mouth, so the cargoes go from ship to barge on the quays of Elissara, and the barges are hauled upstream by ox teams on the towpath, a string at a time, for several days against the current. The towpath is the busiest road in the empire that has no paving. Its inns stand a day's haul apart, each with a yard for oxen and a landing for a string, and the teamsters who walk it belong to the boatmen's fraternities of the landings they start from.

## The Mouths

The last miles of the river are not a river at all. Below the estates it splits into the [Mouths]{.name data-type="place/feature"} (Vilari Kozmai), a fan of channels, reed-marsh, mudflats and salt-meadow that spreads along the coast wider than the capital and changes its shape with every winter's flood. Only one channel is kept open for ships: the main channel, which runs past the stone mole and its light into Elissara's harbor, and which the city dredges, stakes and lights at its own charge under its charter.

The rest of the Mouths silts as it pleases. The largest of the other channels is the [Reed Mouth]{.name data-type="place/feature"} (Vilari Sulkozma), which carried ships within living memory of the oldest boatmen and now carries a laden skiff at high water and nothing at low. Eel-fishers and fowlers work the marsh in season from reed huts on stilts, cattle graze the salt-meadows in summer, and the reed is cut every winter for thatch and sold up the river. The marsh breeds fever in the hot months, and the fishers leave it then.

The side channels are also the oldest smuggling road on the coast. A cargo landed from a small boat in the reeds and carried inland by night pays neither Elissara's dues nor the imperial customs, and the boatmen of [[place-tholkezora|Tholkezora]] know the channels better than the customs men do.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The river carries the capital's bread, and the capital has about a week of it in store. Every barge of Hilen grain that lies aground or waits at a landing raises the price on the River Circle, and the grain houses, the brokers, the boatmen's fraternities and the customs all read the water differently. This season the river is low. A string of eight grain barges is aground on the shoals above the capital while the state granaries are at their lowest, the grain houses want it dragged over, and a broker in the capital pays better to have it left. On the lower towpath someone is laming the ox teams of one fraternity by night. At the mouth, where the chartered city of [[place-aelissium|Elissara]] passes the capital's cargoes upriver, a customs officer of Salkalon has seized a skiff of Taruzelian silk in the Reed Mouth.

## Trouble on the River {#hooks}

- **Low water.** A string of eight grain barges is aground on the shoals above the capital while the state granaries in the capital are at their lowest. The grain houses want it dragged over; a broker in the capital wants it left where it is, and pays better.
- **The towpath.** Ox teams are being lamed on a stretch of the lower towpath between two inns, always at night and always the teams of the same fraternity. The fraternity's elder wants to know who is doing it before his men settle it themselves.
- **The reed boat.** A customs officer of Salkalon's shore has seized a skiff in the Reed Mouth with a cargo of Taruzelian silk and a letter in a merchant's cipher, and wants a party to go back into the reeds with him to find where it was going.

# The Truth of the River {#truth .secret}

The Reed Mouth is silting because Elissara wants it to. Every few years the city's council pays its dredge-master to drop the spoil from the main channel into the head of the Reed Mouth instead of out at sea, and to do it in the autumn, so the winter flood spreads it. A ship that could come up the Reed Mouth would land its cargo on the provincial shore, a day's haul nearer the capital, and pay its dues to the _therkos_ (governor) of Salkalon and none to the city. The province's surveyors have sounded the channel twice and found it shallower each time, and the _therkos_'s lawyers would give a great deal for one of the dredge-master's tally-books, which record each load dropped, the date and the payment.
