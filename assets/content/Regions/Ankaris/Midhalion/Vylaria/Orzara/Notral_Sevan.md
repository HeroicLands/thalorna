A senior scholar of the Ombalon, asked by a young reader of the Epistémion how to get into the Notral Sevan, gives him the answer every reader gets: "Write to the throne. Say what you want to read and why. If the throne says yes, a keeper will take you down, stand beside you while you read, and take you up again. If the throne says nothing, that is the answer. Most readers in this city have died waiting for one."

## Overview

The Notral Sevan is the warded library of [[affiliation-panepistmm|The Academy]], the Epistémion, kept beneath the [[place-aulpnpstmm|Court of the Academy]], the Ombalon, in [[place-arvon|Arvon]], the Inner City of [[place-orzara|Orzara City]]. It holds the rarest knowledge the Epistémion possesses: singular manuscripts that exist nowhere else, the captured archives of conquered peoples, and arcane artifacts too valuable or too dangerous to leave in open hands. It is not a library one visits. Entry is by the throne's leave alone, behind the wards of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Thordimalon]] that guard the imperial precincts, and what is kept inside is known in full to very few.

## Inside {#inside}

The library lies below the Ombalon, reached by a single stair from a door in the Ombalon's inner court. The door is bronze and bears no inscription, and the wards on it are the Thordimalon's, the same that guard the palace. A keeper of the Ombalon stands at it by day, and nobody stands at it by night, because nobody needs to.

The stair goes down to a vaulted hall where the readers sit, lit by the Order's cold-fire lamps, because no flame is brought below. Beyond the hall run the stacks: long, low rooms cut into the rock of [[place-arvel|Arvel]], lined with cedar presses and closed chests. The presses hold the manuscripts and the archives, each chest sealed with the Ombalon's seal and entered in the keepers' catalog. The artifacts are kept in the farthest rooms, behind a second warded door, and the catalog for those rooms is not kept in the reading hall.

A reader who has the throne's leave sits at a table in the hall. A keeper fetches what the leave names, stands beside him while he reads, counts the leaves when he has finished and takes it back. Nothing leaves the Notral Sevan. A copy may be made only by a scribe of the Ombalon, at the throne's order, and the Ombalon keeps a copy of the copy.

## Who Is There {#who-is-there}

The **keepers** of the Notral Sevan are scholars of the Ombalon, the standing body of the Epistémion's most senior scholars who answer the throne's questions. They catalog the library, fetch for the readers and answer for every leaf. When the Ombalon is asked to settle a point of law, a history or a precedent, the keepers go down for the documents, and the Ombalon's answer often rests on a manuscript nobody else has read.

The **Thordimalon** keeps the wards. Its wardens renew them on a schedule the Order does not publish, and a warden of the Order may go down the stair without the throne's leave to see to them.

The **readers** are few. The throne's leave goes to an Ombalon scholar on the throne's own business, to a senior scholar of the Epistémion with a powerful patron, and now and then to a foreign envoy whose master the throne wishes to please.

## The Present {#present}

The succession has brought readers to the bronze door. The reigning Orzas is old and has adopted nobody, and when an Orzas dies without naming a successor, the law of the throne is whatever the precedents say it is. The precedents lie in the Notral Sevan: the records of past accessions, past adoptions and the throne's past disputes with the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]]. Every house that expects to take a side has asked the throne for leave to read them, and the throne has granted none. The keepers expect the Ombalon to be asked for an opinion on the night the Orzas dies, and they are preparing the documents now, without being told which answer is wanted.

The Thordimalon wants the library kept closed. The Order's wards guard it, and the Order prefers that what it guards stay unread. Its masters say so in the Ombalon whenever a leave is asked.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The leave.** A house has the throne's leave for one afternoon, and it needs a reader who can find what it wants in that time and remember it after the keeper counts the leaves.
- **The copy.** A scholar of the Ombalon has made a copy of an accession record at the throne's order, and a second copy of it has been offered for sale on Mernel.

# The Truth of the Notral Sevan {#truth .secret}

The rumors in the city are right on all three counts. The presses hold books the Empire has officially burned, copied before the burning on the throne's order, and the chests hold the only surviving records of peoples the Empire erased. The farthest rooms hold relics the Thordimalon would rather no rival studied, some of them put there by the Order itself.

The wards answer to the Thordimalon before they answer to the throne. The throne's leave is a paper the keepers honor, but the wards let a warden of the Order through without it, and the Order's wardens go down the stair more often than the schedule needs. The keepers have noticed. They have never been able to tell from the catalog what the wardens read, because the wardens do not ask the keepers to fetch anything.
