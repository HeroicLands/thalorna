## Overview {#overview}

Arvel, the Eagle Hill, is the highest of the [[place-sixhillsvyl|Six Hills]] on which [[place-orzara|Orzara City]] rises, and it looks down on all the rest. Its crown is walled: behind its own ancient ring of walls stands [[place-arvon|Inner City]], the sacred and administrative core of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]], and over the summit rises [[place-palvar|The Grand Palace]], the largest single structure in the known western world. Below it, in the central saddle, lies the [[place-platandstr|Platan District]] around the [Circle of Árdavon]{.name data-type="place/site"}.

A newcomer to Orzara learns the hill by what is closed to them. A Watch sergeant who walks recruits up the [Paldor]{.name data-type="place/feature"} on their first day stops them at the foot of the Founder's statue and turns them round: "Look back down the way you came. Everything you can see from here is yours to walk in. Everything behind that gate belongs to the Guard, and the Guard does not think much of us. You stop here, you hand over what you are carrying, and you come back down."

## The Hill {#the-land}

Arvel stands at the center of the six hills, steep on its north face and climbed on its south by the Paldor, the Triumphal Way, which runs up from the [Paldor Gate]{.name data-type="place/structure"} through the Platan District to the [Orzas's Circle]{.name data-type="place/site"} before the Inner City's gate. The colossus of the Founder stands in that Circle, and every accession and triumph passes it. Above the Circle the [Inner Wall]{.name data-type="place/structure"} rings the crown, the only wall Orzara has.

Inside the wall the hill is built over to the rock. The domes and pylons of the [Vanor]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the Temple District, fill the lower ground with [[place-pnthnrldnm|Sarkena Dorekis]] (the Temple of the Twelve) at their heart. Beside them stand the Senate's palace, [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]], the counting-houses and archives of the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]], [[place-thaurzurgalis|The Guard Camp]] and the warded seat of the [[place-aulpnpstmm|Ombalon]], and the palace climbs over the summit in terraces until the eye cannot tell masonry from rock. The [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]] keeps its small inner compound and its restricted library on the summit, within the citadel. Look up from the Platan on a clear morning and the palace seems to grow out of the hill itself.

Under the crown lie the sealed galleries of the [[place-eyriearvel|Eyrie of the Dragon Riders]]. Their one known door is a bronze-leaved arch in the north face, inside the wall and below the palace terraces, with the Republic's chancery seal still whole across it and two guardsmen of [[affiliation-sorvan|The Imperial Guard]] at the mouth of its court.

## Who Climbs It {#who-uses-it}

The Inner City is the one quarter shut to the common crowd. It is given over to priests, _kurthoi_ (senators), soldiers and magistrates, and the people who pass its gate each day are the ones who serve them: the palace household of some three thousand, the thousands of clerks, scribes and tax-assessors of the Offices, the priesthoods of the Vanor, and the guardsmen. Senators come up from the [[place-heightsorz|Heights]] each morning to sit in the Seldan, and petitioners from the provinces climb the Paldor with letters they hope a chamberlain will take.

## What the Gate Asks {#dangers}

The Sorvan holds the gate and the wall, and its men do not explain themselves. A stranger without business inside is turned back in the Orzas's Circle, and one who argues is held in the gatehouse until a magistrate or his patron vouches for him. Inside the wall the Guard keeps order by its own lights, and a man the Guard detains is not always produced before the imperial tribunals. The [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|City Watch]] stops at the Circle, and its captains and the Guard's officers keep the line between them with open contempt on one side and resentment on the other.

## What the City Tells {#what-is-told}

The city tells more stories about Arvel than about any other ground in Orzara. The commonest holds that a dragon's egg lies sealed beneath the palace and will hatch on the day the empire falls. The palace servants say that the last mount of the corps sleeps in the rock, and that is why the lowest kitchens are warm in winter. The petitioners on the Paldor say that the Orzas can see the whole city from his terrace and counts the smoke from the Warrens each morning to know how many are eating.

## The Hill Waits {#present}

The reigning Orzas is old and has adopted nobody, and every power on the hill is counting what that will cost.

- **The Sorvan** expects a donative from whoever is named. The man the court expects to be named has promised one and paid nothing on it, a rival house is offering the officers more now, and a handful of watch-captains hold the gates of the Guard Camp.
- **The Seldan** cannot name an heir, and its houses can only back one. Its old families on Torvel and its rising ones on [[place-selvin|Selvin]] dine together every night, and the succession is discussed at every dinner without the Orzas being named.
- **The Imperial Offices** pass to the new reign with their seals. A new Orzas appoints a new Keeper of the Seal, and the houses that want the seal for one of their own freedmen are already courting the claimants.
- **The treasury** of [[affiliation-tarmex|the Tarmeks Orzan]] pays the veterans' pension with money borrowed from the great houses of [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]], and every lender wants the next Orzas in its debt before he is named.

None of them wants the city broken. Each is taking what it can from a throne it needs to keep standing, and each is sure the others will hold the line.

## History {#history}

The [Vilérian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} never walled the Eagle Hill. Its city stood on [[place-antel|Antel]], the Old Hill, and Arvel rose beside it, higher and emptier, with the galleries of the [Dragon Riders]{.name data-type="affiliation"} cut under its crown. About 45 BF the Republic did not renew its standing lease with the dragons ([[lore-ridersdisb|The Lease Refused]]), the Riders were disbanded and the Eyrie was sealed. What passed between the Republic and the dragons for the Riders' service is in no account of the war ([[lore-thebargain|The Bargain]]).

In the first dozen years of his reign the first Orzas walled the crown, built the Seldan and the Guard Camp inside the wall, founded the palace over the sealed galleries, and moved the Senate up from its hall on Antel ([[lore-eaglecityrse|The Raising of the Eagle City]]). Every reign since has built on the hill, and every accession has climbed the Paldor to the gate.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The donative.** A watch-captain of [[affiliation-sorvan|the Sorvan]] wants a party to learn, before his officers vote with their gates, whether the promised donative exists anywhere but in the promise.
- **The vouching.** A petitioner from the provinces has been held in the gatehouse for three days, and his patron on the Heights will not vouch for him. His wife hires a party to find out what the patron is afraid of.
- **The rollover.** A factor of a great house of [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]] carries his house's terms for the throne's next loan up the Paldor this week, and a rival house hires a party to learn them before the chamberlain does.
