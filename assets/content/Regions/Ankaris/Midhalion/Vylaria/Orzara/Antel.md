## Overview

A city guide who takes visitors round the Six Hills in a day starts at Antel and gives the reason: "Everything else you will see is a reply to this one." The Old Hill is where the Republic lived. Its Senate sat here in a hall of its own, its patricians built their houses here, and its first monuments went up here, long before the Founder walled [[place-arvel|Arvel]] and moved the Senate uphill ([[lore-eaglecityrse|The Raising of the Eagle City]]). The hill was once second only to the Eagle Hill. It is now the plainest face of the city's decline.

## The Hill Today

The early monuments weather unrepaired. The great families began moving to [[place-selvin|Selvin]] after the Senate's move, and the six days of fire that ran over the hill about 403 finished the migration ([[lore-greatburnng|The Great Burning]]). The hill's once-proud Circle has gone shabby, dignified still and visibly fading. A few old houses are kept up by families too proud or too poor to leave. The rest are let by the room, and the Old Hill's lanes hold the city's antiquarians, its retired clerks and a growing number of squatters in the houses nobody claims.

The old Senate hall stands on the crown of the hill, empty. The Seldan's clerks sweep it on the Republic's feast days and lock it again, and the cellars below it have not been opened in living memory.

## Who Comes Here

- **Antiquarians**, who come for the Republic's early monuments and are the hill's only steady custom.
- **Squatters**, in the abandoned houses on the lower slopes.
- **The Scribal Guild**, which has asked the Seldan more than once for leave to search the old hall's cellars.

## Hooks
