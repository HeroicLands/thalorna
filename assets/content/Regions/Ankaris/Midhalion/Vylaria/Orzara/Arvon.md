## Arvon—the Inner City of Orzara

At the summit of [[place-arvel|Arvel]], the highest of the six hills of [[place-orzara|Orzara]], behind its own ancient ring of walls, stands **Arvon**—the Inner City, the sacred and administrative core of the Empire. The name is old and freighted with glory: _Arvon_, the **Eagle City**, is the walled crown of the Eagle Hill, raised by the first Orzas in the Empire's founding age ([[lore-eaglecityrse|The Raising of the Eagle City]]) above the Republic's older city on [[place-antel|Antel]]—and it takes its name from Arvel beneath it and from the imperial orkaima whose wings shadow the throne. To a Vylarian it means simply _the heart of the world_; in daily speech it is clipped to **the Orkaim**, or merely **the Veramis**. Entry is controlled; common citizens may pass through certain precincts on feast days or to petition the bureaucracy, but much of it is the preserve of priests, _kurthoi_ (senators), soldiers, magistrates, and the great families. Within it lie the temples of the state, the seats of its government, the imperial guard, the engine of its bureaucracy, and the Grand Palace itself.

An usher of the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] who has walked petitioners up from the gate for twenty years gives the rule to every new face: "You may come up on a feast day, or you may come up with a petition for the bureaucracy. If you have neither, you will be turned at the gate, and the guard will not argue it. Keep to the precinct you were let into. The hill is not a street."

### Vanor—The Temple District {#pelthra-sarkhena-the-temple-district}

The [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian]] pantheon is the state religion of the empire, and the Vanor holds its grandest expression anywhere in the world. But the City rules a continent, and into it flow the gods of every people Vylaria has conquered, courted, or traded with. The result is the most crowded sacred precinct on Thalorna: a forest of domes, pylons, spires, and timber halls where, by ancient toleration-edict, nearly every faith of the known world keeps a house. Vylarian law outlaws no religion whatsoever—but it expects every faith to behave itself within the City. Open conflict between religious groups—riot, arson, the public spilling of a rival's blood—is forbidden absolutely and punished without mercy. Covert conflict is abundant: the Vanor seethes with quiet feuds, poisonings dressed as fevers, bribed magistrates, and slanders whispered into senatorial ears.

The rule of the district mirrors the rule of the **Palor** beside it. A major or wealthy religion raises its own temple; a minor or poor one is granted space in a shared compound alongside others of compatible character, sometimes dividing a single sanctuary across a roster of holy days.

**The Aureldian establishment.** The heart of the district belongs to _the Twelve_. The greatest of all is [[place-pnthnrldnm|Sarkena Dorekis]], the Temple of the Twelve, a single colossal sanctuary honoring the whole [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] at once beneath a dome so wide that visiting architects refuse to believe it stands without [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|arcane]] assistance—and they are not entirely wrong. The main temple is attended by the priests of Árdavon. Around it cluster the individual great temples: [[affiliation-ardavon|Árdavon]] of the Gates, fittingly enormous in the capital of order; wise [[affiliation-marendas|Marendas]]; bountiful [[affiliation-olvenia|Ólvenia]]; [[affiliation-brazemas|Brazemas]] the Thunderer; [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]] the Silent Judge; and the rest, each with its own priesthood, endowments, and feast-day processions. The forge-fires of [[affiliation-sacredforge|Ústaron's Sacred Forge]] burn here too. But not so the [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]]: eschewed by the other faiths of the pantheon, the Black Flame have their own magnificent temple within the district, and are amply attended, for its creed—purification through fire, the rightful dominance of the strong over the weak—sits closer to the Vylarian imperial temper than the Aureldian's gentler priests care to admit. Sacred Forge and Black Flame loathe one another across the district, but their war is waged in the senate and the shadows, never in the streets.

**The foreign great temples.** Two foreign faiths are wealthy and ancient enough to rival the Aureldian houses on their own ground. The [[affiliation-khelathpnthn|Khelathi Pantheon]] temple-compound—serving the gods of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]], whose civilization was old before the Aureldian names were spoken—is a walled precinct unto itself, its painted pylons and the smoke of its mortuary rites a dignified, deliberate reproach to Vylarian pretension. The [[affiliation-asguardian|Asguardian]] faithful of the north keep a great timber **hov**, smoke-darkened and carved with runes, where the merchants, mercenaries, and envoys of [[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Nordheim]] swear their binding oaths and pour mead to the defending gods. And even **Nahild**—pariah to her own kin across the Asguardian north—is no outcast in Orzara. Her clergy are quietly powerful here, for they share with the Aureldian priests of [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]] the keeping of the dead, and the dead of a city this vast are a charge of enormous weight, wealth, and influence.

**The lesser and shared houses.** Beyond the great temples spreads a denser warren of smaller shrines, where the minor and provincial faiths keep what they can afford. The fire-and-light worship of the eastern [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaians]], the cyclic devotions of [[affiliation-varakpnthn|Varnuka]] pilgrims from Vedyara, the bureaucratic celestial rites of [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Téngwou Rong Lei]] carried in by Tantsou traders, and a hundred smaller cults from the provinces and beyond all maintain modest sanctuaries—many co-located in shared compounds, dividing the calendar and the altar-space between faiths judged compatible enough to share a roof. No faith here is barred from worship; the City taxes them all, tolerates them all, and watches them all—stepping in only when a quarrel between them threatens to break into the open.

### Palor—The Palace District

The Palace District is the Empire's diplomatic heart: its principal purpose is to house the **embassies** of the powers that must treat with Vylaria. Foreign **polities** keep their legations here—the great realms of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]], [[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Nordheim]], the eastern empires, the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Taruzelian states]], and dozens of lesser kingdoms, princedoms, and city-states—but so do foreign **organizations** with standing business before the throne: merchant leagues and trading houses, craft and banking guilds, mercenary companies, temple-states, and chartered orders, all of which need a permanent voice in the City.

As in the Temple District, the rule is wealth and standing. A great power or a rich league raises its **own embassy**—a palatial compound behind its own walls, with gardens, guard, and private quarters, flying its own colors like a small sovereign island within the City. Smaller and poorer delegations are **co-located in shared compounds**, where a clutch of minor realms or modest guilds keep neighboring suites of offices and apartments under one roof, sharing a courtyard, a gate, and the watchful attention of the imperial protocol-officers. Interspersed among the embassies stand the city palaces of the greatest senatorial families and priestly houses, for whom an address in the diplomatic quarter is itself a statement of reach.

In addition to the embassies, this district also houses the **ceremonial palace of the [[affiliation-tevran|Tevran Maldor]]**—the seat of the Council of the Tevran and the face the guild-of-guilds turns toward the throne. This is ceremonial quarters only: the working guild-halls of its constituent trades keep no place inside the Inner City. Those stand out in the city proper, each lodged within or beside the ward where its trade is practiced, under the same district law that governs every other craft.

### Seldan—The Palace of the Senate

The [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]], seat of the Vylarian Senate. Beneath its great bronze doors and within its tiered semicircular chamber, the patrician class debates, postures, conspires, and—in theory—advises the Orzas. In practice its real power waxes and wanes with the strength of whoever wears the imperial diadem.

### Thaurzurgalis—The Imperial Barracks

The [[place-thaurzurgalis|Thaurzurgalis]]—fortified barracks of the [[affiliation-sorvan|Sorvan]], the imperial guard: the Orzas's personal bodyguard and the keepers of order and justice within the Inner City itself. From here the throne's grip on its own heart is enforced. The tramp of drilling soldiers is a constant background rhythm of the precinct, and the sight of the guard's polished armor is meant to reassure the loyal and unsettle the ambitious. The policing of the city _beyond_ the **Inner Wall** is no part of their charge; that falls to the [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|City Watch]].

### The Imperial Offices—The Imperial Bureaucracy

The whole administrative apparatus of the Empire is gathered into a single walled compound of the Inner City: the **Imperial Offices**—_the Imperial Offices_, known to everyone simply as the **Offices**. It is a sprawling complex of **administrative halls, archives, and counting-houses** that sets the great organs of imperial governance side by side: the [[affiliation-tarmex|Tarmeks Orzan]] (the imperial treasury and central bank), the [[affiliation-tolvan|Tolvan]] (tax collection and administration), the **imperial tribunals** (the throne's own courts—see [[affiliation-verdanorzara|Verdan Orzara]]), and the central imperial archives. It is the true engine of empire. Here thousands of clerks, scribes, tax-assessors, _theronoi_ (censors), and magistrates process the endless paper of governing a continent: census rolls, tax ledgers, military levies, grain dole records, court judgments, and the correspondence of a hundred provinces. The archives are said to be so vast that documents are lost in them for generations, and that a man with the right friends can make an inconvenient law, debt, or birth-record simply _disappear_.

### Ombalon—The Court of the Academy of Knowledge

The [[place-aulpnpstmm|Ombalon]] is the ceremonial headquarters of the sprawling Epistémion (the Academy of Knowledge), the Academy of Knowledge; the small, rarefied counterpart to the great campus that lies beyond the Inner Wall (the Scholars' District; see [[place-orzara|Orzara]]). This is no place of teaching. It is a palace of counsel and of keeping: a standing body of the institution's most senior scholars who answer, at the throne's summons, the questions of the Orzas, the [[affiliation-seldan|Senate]], and the great houses—a point of law construed, a history settled, a treaty's precedent traced, a portent weighed. Its treasure is the **Notral Sevan**, a deep and warded library of the rarest knowledge the Epistémion holds: singular manuscripts, the archives of conquered peoples, and arcane artifacts too valuable—or too dangerous—to keep in open hands. Entry is by the throne's leave, behind the same Arcane Order wards that guard the imperial precincts. That the Order already works openly in the Grand Palace makes the arrangement natural: the Epistémion's quiet inner seat and the Arcane Order's brilliant displays share one hill, while the bustle of the university proper is kept, by design, beyond the walls of the Arvon.

### Palvar—The Grand Palace

Rising above all of it is the [[place-palvar|Palvar]]—the largest and grandest single structure in Orzara, and therefore in the known western world. It is less a building than a small city of its own: tier upon tier of marble halls, colonnaded courtyards, hanging gardens, private baths, throne rooms, and audience chambers, all rising in terraces up and over the crown of the hill. Gold leaf, porphyry, and rare colored marbles from every conquered land sheathe its surfaces. Its private gardens are watered by dedicated aqueducts; its great bronze gates are taller than three men.

It is here, within the Palvar, that the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] is permitted—_encouraged_, even—to show off (see the Hand of the Thordimalon (the Arcane Order), and the Mechanical Court, in the [[place-orzara|Orzara]] note).
