## Overview

Arvel, the Eagle Hill, is the highest of the [[place-sixhillsvyl|Six Hills]] on which [[place-orzara|Orzara]] rises, and it looks down on all the rest. Its crown is walled: behind its own ancient ring of walls stands [[place-arvon|Arvon]], the Inner City and the sacred and administrative core of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|empire]], and over the summit rises the [[place-palvar|Palvar]], the largest single structure in the known western world. Below it, in the central saddle, lies the [Platan District]{.name data-type="place/site"} around the [Circle of Árdavon]{.name data-type="place/site"}.

## The Hill and the Empire

A newcomer to Orzara learns the hill by what is closed to them. The Inner City is the one quarter shut to the common crowd, given over to priests, _kurthoi_ (senators), soldiers and magistrates, and the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]] keeps its small inner compound and its restricted library on the summit, within the citadel. Look up from the Platan District on a clear morning and the palace seems to grow out of the rock itself. The first Orzas walled the crown in the Empire's founding age and moved [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] (the Senate) up to it from [[place-antel|Antel]] ([[lore-eaglecityrse|The Raising of the Eagle City]]).

## The Sealed Eyrie

Under the crown of the hill lie the sealed galleries of the [[place-eyriearvel|Eyrie of the Dragon Riders]] (the Eyrie of Arvel), where the [Dragon Riders]{.name data-type="affiliation"} of the [Vylarian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} were quartered. Around 45 BF the Republic did not renew its standing lease with the dragons ([[lore-ridersdisb|The Lease Refused]]), the Riders were disbanded, and the Eyrie was sealed. Its one known door is a bronze-leaved arch in the hill's north face, inside the Inner City and below the palace terraces, with the Republic's chancery seal still whole across it and two guardsmen of [[affiliation-sorvan|The Imperial Guard]] at the mouth of its court. What passed between the Republic and the dragons for the Riders' service is in no account of the war; see [[lore-thebargain|the Bargain]].

The city tells more stories about the hill than about any other ground in Orzara. The commonest holds that a dragon's egg lies sealed beneath the palace and will hatch on the day the empire falls; the palace servants say that the last mount of the corps sleeps in the rock, and that is why the lowest kitchens are warm in winter.
