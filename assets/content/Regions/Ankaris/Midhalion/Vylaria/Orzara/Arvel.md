## Overview

Arvel, the Eagle Hill, is the highest of the [[place-sixhillsvyl|Six Hills]] on which [[place-orzara|Ôrzâra]] rises, and it looks down on all the rest. Its crown is walled: behind its own ancient ring of walls stands [[place-arvon|Arvon]], the Inner City and the sacred and administrative core of the empire, and over the summit rises the [[place-palvar|Palvar]], the largest single structure in the known western world. Below it, in the central saddle, lies the Platan District around the **Circle of Árdavon**.

## The Hill and the Empire

A newcomer to Ôrzâra learns the hill by what is closed to them. The Inner City is the one quarter shut to the common crowd, given over to priests, senators, soldiers and magistrates, and the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Panepistemium]] keeps its small inner compound and its restricted library on the summit, within the citadel. Look up from the Platan District on a clear morning and the palace seems to grow out of the rock itself. The first Ôrzâs walled the crown in the Empire's founding age and moved the Senate up to it from [[place-antel|Antel]] ([[lore-eaglecityrse|The Raising of the Eagle City]]).

## The Sealed Eyrie

Under the hill lie the galleries where the dragon mounts of the **Dragon Riders** were housed, and **the Eyrie of Arvel** that held them. Around 45 BF the Republic did not renew its standing lease with the dragons ([[lore-ridersdisb|The Lease Refused]]), the Riders were disbanded, and the Eyrie was sealed. The galleries under Arvel remain shut. What passed between the Republic and the dragons for the Riders' service is in no account of the war; see [[lore-thebargain|The Bargain]].
