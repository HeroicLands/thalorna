## Ombalon—The Court of the Academy of Knowledge

Within [[place-arvon|Inner City]] of [[place-orzara|Orzara City]], sharing the palace hill with the throne itself, stands the **Ombalon**—the ceremonial headquarters of [[affiliation-panepistmm|The Academy]], the Academy of Knowledge. It is the small, rarefied counterpart to the great campus that lies beyond the [Inner Wall]{.name data-type="place/structure"} in the [[place-scholarsdstr|Scholars' District]] (see [[place-orzara|Orzara]]). The bustle of the university proper—the lecture halls, the wrangling students, the chapterhouse traffic—is kept, by deliberate design, outside the Inner Wall of Arvon. What remains within is something quieter and far more select.

### A Palace of Counsel

The Ombalon (the Court of the Academy) is no place of teaching. It is a palace of counsel and of keeping: the seat of a standing body of the Epistémion's most senior scholars, who answer, at the throne's summons, the questions of the Orzas, the emperor, [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] (the Senate), and the great houses. A point of law to be construed, a contested history to be settled, the precedent of a treaty to be traced, a portent to be weighed—these are the matters carried up to the Ombalon. Its scholars do not lecture; they advise, and their advice can decide an inheritance, justify a war, or quietly bury a claim. To be called to the Ombalon is, for a scholar of the Epistémion (the Academy of Knowledge), the summit of the institution's honors; to be consulted by it is, for the great, a mark of standing in itself.

### The Notral Sevan

The Ombalon's true treasure is the [[place-notralsevan|Notral Sevan]]—a deep and warded library holding the rarest knowledge the Epistémion possesses: singular manuscripts that exist nowhere else, the captured archives of conquered peoples, and arcane artifacts too valuable, or too dangerous, to leave in open hands. It is not a library one visits. Entry is by the throne's leave alone, behind the same [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Arcane Order wards]] that guard the imperial precincts, and what is kept within is known in full to very few. Rumor in the City holds that the Notral Sevan contains books the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Empire]] has officially burned, the only surviving records of nations Viléria erased, and relics the Thordimalon (the Arcane Order) would rather no rival ever study.

### Two Faces on One Hill

That the Ombalon and the Thordimalon share a single hill is no accident but a designed arrangement. The Arcane Order already works openly in the [[place-palvar|Palvar]], turning out the brilliant displays the throne wants seen; the Epistémion keeps its quiet inner seat in the Ombalon, turning the same proximity to the opposite use. The Order's marvels and the Academy's secrets are two faces of one strategy—knowledge as the ornament of power, and knowledge as the hoarded reserve of it. The warded calm of the Notral Sevan and the gleaming spectacle of the [[place-magngndorery|Grand Orrery]] (the Mechanical Court) are, in the end, the same instrument held by the same hand.

## The Present {#present}

The Ombalon answers the throne's questions, and this year the great houses bring theirs too. The precedents carried up to its scholars are the succession's: what an Orzas who dies having adopted nobody leaves behind, and which precedents bind his successor. Entry to the [[place-notralsevan|Notral Sevan]] is by the throne's leave alone, and with the Orzas failing the leave is his to give for a short while longer. The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]'s wards stand on the same hill, and the Order argues within itself whether it should crown and uncrown.
