## Overview {#overview}

Selvin is the hill a family builds on when it is rising. The Laurel is one of the [[place-sixhillsvyl|Six Hills]] of [[place-orzara|Orzara City]], and with [Torvel]{.name data-type="place/feature"} (the Coronal) it makes **the [[place-heightsorz|Heights]]**: the twin patrician hills of walled villas, terraced gardens and city-houses of _kurthoi_ (senators) and great families who do not lodge in [[place-arvon|Inner City]]. Both are prized for their air and their views, and both are reached only on foot.

A mask-maker of the [[place-gildedqrtr|Gilded Quarter]], who carries his work up the hill himself, tells his apprentice how to read it: "On Torvel a house orders a mask when somebody dies. On Selvin a house orders a whole cupboard at once, because it has just been given ancestors. Never ask whose faces they are. Ask when they want them."

## The Newer Hill {#the-land}

Torvel is the older and prouder of the two, ringed with the ancestral villas of the senatorial houses and facing the palace on [[place-arvel|Eagle Hill]] across the central saddle. Selvin is greener and newer. Its slopes are gentler, its lanes wind up through the Gilded Quarter instead of climbing in stairs, and the newest houses have paved them at their own expense. The houses on its crown stand in gardens of cypress, box and roses that step down the slope in terraces, each with its own cistern, because Selvin's houses were built after the city learned what a dry summer does to a hill that shares its water.

On its lower slopes lies the Gilded Quarter, where goldsmiths, jewelers, perfumers, fine tailors, apothecaries and instrument-makers make and sell to a wealthy clientele, with the physicians, scriveners and better baths and inns beside them. The higher a visitor climbs, the quieter and the newer the walls.

## Who Lives on the Laurel {#who-uses-it}

The rising houses live on Selvin: families raised to the senatorial order within living memory, rich from grain, banking and the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]]. The greatest of them is [[affiliation-mardovanes|House Mardovanes]], whose house near the crown keeps a gate lit all night and the best table in the city. Below the houses of rank live the households that serve them, the craftsmen of the Gilded Quarter, and the clients who climb the hill each dawn for the morning call.

## What the Hill Asks {#dangers}

A house on Selvin is new, and newness is watched. Torvel's old houses count Selvin's ancestors and know which masks were made last year. A house that rose by the throne's favor can fall by the throne's displeasure, and a name posted for proscription on the boards of the [[place-platandstr|Platan District]] ends a Selvin house faster than an old one, because a new house has fewer friends to vouch for it.

## What Is Told {#what-is-told}

The hill takes its name from the laurels that grew on it before it was built over, and the city says that every house on Selvin keeps one laurel in its garden for luck. The older families on Torvel say the laurel is what Selvin's houses are crowned with because they cannot be crowned with anything older.

## Titles for Sale {#present}

Selvin is building faster than at any time since the fire, and the money comes from the throne's need of it.

- **The throne's sales.** The Orzas has no cash to spare and sells offices, titles and grants quietly, for coin now. The buyers build on Selvin. New houses are going up on the hill's upper slopes, and their masters order cupboards of ancestor masks from the Gilded Quarter before the walls are roofed. Torvel's old houses resent every one of them and lend to several.
- **House Mardovanes's table.** [[being-nthrsmrdvns|Nethoras Mardovanes]], master of [[affiliation-mardovanes|House Mardovanes]], hosts the best dinners in the city, and the succession is discussed at every one of them without the old Orzas ever being named. The house wants a temple voted for its ancestor the Orzas Azénales, and it wants to stand behind whichever claimant wins, with Hilen's grain in its granaries when he does. Its daughter is betrothed to the heir of the Olkassa estate, and the wedding is the hill's event of the season.
- **The succession.** The reigning Orzas is old and has adopted nobody. The houses on Selvin owe their rise to the present reign, and none of them knows whether the next will honor what this one sold.

## History {#history}

Selvin was a hill of gardens while the [Vilérian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} lived on [[place-antel|The Old Hill]]. When the first Orzas walled the crown of Arvel and moved the Senate up from Antel ([[lore-eaglecityrse|The Raising of the Eagle City]]), the great families began to follow the Senate across the saddle, and Selvin, close to Arvel and still open, took the first of them. The six days of fire that ran from the [[place-warrensorz|Warrens]] over Antel about 403 sent most of the rest ([[lore-greatburnng|The Great Burning]]), and they rebuilt on Selvin rather than go back. The hill has been the address of the rising since. House Mardovanes built near its crown, and the house gave the Empire the Orzas Azénales, who reigned from about 604 to 619.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The new neighbor.** A new house is going up on the slope above an old Selvin house, and its terraces will take the old house's view of the palace. The old house hires a party to learn what title the newcomer bought, and what he paid for it.
- **The votes.** Nethoras Mardovanes wants to know which _kurthoi_ on Selvin would vote his ancestor a temple, and which of them already owe the house that would vote against it.
- **The seating.** The steward of [[affiliation-mardovanes|House Mardovanes]] must seat the Olkassa wedding feast, and the friends of every claimant expect a place near the high table. He hires a party to learn, before the invitations go out, which guests will not sit beside which.
