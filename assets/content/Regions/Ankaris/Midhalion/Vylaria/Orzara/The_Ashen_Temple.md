The house of the Twelve has a fire too, in the Forge-Lord's niche, and nobody in that house has ever been burned by it. That is the whole difference between us. ## Overview

The Ashen Temple, Sarkena Elzen in [[skill-vylarilng|Vylari]], "the ash-gray temple", is the great temple of [[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]] in [[place-orzara|Orzara City]], and the largest house of the faith in the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]. It stands in the [Vanor]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the Temple District of [[place-arvon|Arvon]], the walled Inner City, apart from [[place-pnthnrldnm|The Temple of the Twelve]], which keeps the niche of [[lore-ustarondty|Ústaron]], the Forge-Lord, for [[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]] and gives the Black Flame no altar. It is the seat of the **High Flame-Warden of Vylaria**, who oversees the faith's temples in the Empire, and the house and household he keeps here are called the Flame-Wardenate.

The temple is amply attended. Its creed of purification by fire and the rightful rule of the strong sits closer to the Vylarian imperial temper than the Twelve's gentler priests care to admit, and among its faithful are _kurthoi_ (senators), officers of the legions and the sons of houses who will never inherit. Its preachers speak on the Circles below the hill, the Spire Circle of the [[place-warrensorz|Warrens]] above all, and they find more listeners every year.

## Founding {#founding}

The Black Flame has kept temples in Orzara since the [[lore-ustaranschism|Ústaran Schism]] split the Forge-Lord's faith, about 220 AF. When the orthodox clergy of the Empire asked the Orzas [Karomfos]{.name data-type="being/npc"} to proscribe the new faith, he refused: it had temples in his capital and officers in his legions, and the Empire's law permits every faith that does not threaten the stability of the state. The high priesthood of [[place-nartum|Nartun]] broke with the Empire over that refusal about 224 ([[lore-nartumrefuse|Nartun Refuses the Empire]]). The Ashen Temple stands in the Vanor beside the temples of the pantheon whose clergy asked the throne to close it, and it has stood there for five centuries.

## The Present {#present}

The niche is the Ashen Temple's quarrel. The Temple of the Twelve keeps the Forge-Lord's fire for the Sacred Forge, and its keepers, the priests of [[affiliation-ardavon|Árdavon]], count the Black Flame a cosmic enemy and mean to keep it out. The Ashen Temple does not ask for the altar. Its priests say that what the Sacred Forge tends there is not Ústaron at all. Its patrons in [[affiliation-seldan|The Vylarian Senate]] have never forgiven the house of the Twelve for shutting their god's truest face outside its doors, and they raise the matter whenever the Seldan votes the Temple of the Twelve its endowment.

The succession sharpens it. The reigning Orzas is old and has adopted nobody, and an Orzas's sympathies decide how warmly the throne treats the Ashen Temple. The High Flame-Warden wants a claimant who will press the Árdavan keepers on the niche, or at the least one who will not press the Ashen Temple, and the faith's officers in the legions and its patrons in the Seldan are weighing every claimant on that question.

The war between the Ashen Temple and the Sacred Forge is waged in the Seldan and in the shadows, never in the streets, because the Vanor's law punishes open conflict between faiths without mercy. An archimandrite of the Sacred Forge in the capital, [[being-ankhelossath|Ankelos Satheris]], presses the city's magistrates to restrict the Black Flame's preaching on the Circles and pays for counter-sermons, and the law does not let him do more.

## Who Rules It {#who-rules-it}

The **High Flame-Warden of Vylaria** rules the Ashen Temple and, from it, the Black Flame's temples across the Empire. The office is one of considerable power and prestige, and it is held as the faith holds every authority: by strength. The faith recognizes no supreme authority even in Vylaria, and the High Flame-Warden keeps order among the Flame-Wardens of the provincial temples by the weight of his own standing, his patrons and his Scorchmasters, not by any rank above theirs. A Flame-Warden who thinks himself stronger may challenge him.

The Flame-Wardenate is his household in the temple: the Scorchmasters who serve as his lieutenants and train the Aspirants, the priests registered with the imperial authorities as the temple's clergy, and the Aspirants themselves. The Black Flame's priests in Vylaria are registered as every faith's priests are, and the Ashen Temple's register is the longest of the faith's.

## Lands and Income {#lands-and-income}

The Ashen Temple holds no estates of note. It lives on its patrons: senators, officers and the sons of great houses, who give to it as the Seldan's families give to every temple they use as a political tool, and more freely than most. The faithful give at the Night of Ashes, and officers give before a campaign. Like every faith in the Vanor, the temple pays the city's tax on its house.

## Visiting the House {#visiting}

The Vanor lies inside the Inner Wall, and a common citizen comes up the hill only on a feast day or with a petition. The Ashen Temple's faithful from the city below come up on the faith's own feast days, which the Vanor's law counts like any other, and on those days the gate of the Inner City admits them. The temple's officers and patrons come and go as they please.

A visitor sees a compound of dark stone, plain where the Twelve's temples are gilded, around a paved court with a great fire-basin at its center. The basin is never cold. Most of the faithful who come to the temple show a burn scar somewhere, and many show the [Mark of the Fist]{.name data-type="lore"}, the brand of a clenched gauntlet on the shoulder or the chest.

The faith's great festival is the [Night of Ashes]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}, at the dark of the new moon, and in Orzara it is public. The faithful gather in the court and on the Circle below the Vanor's gate, a bonfire is lit in the basin, and they throw into it what they mean to be rid of: captured weapons, broken chains, and grievances written on wax and slate. Officers burn the banners of enemies their legions have beaten. The City Watch stands its engines at the foot of the hill until the fire is out.

## The Grounds {#the-grounds}

The temple compound is a square of dark stone walls around three spaces. The **court** holds the fire-basin, where the Night of Ashes burns and where the [Ordeal of Flame]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} is undergone. The **sanctuary** behind it holds the god's image, a mailed fist wrapped in black flame, and the altar where the [Blessing of Battle]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} is said over men going to war. The **Flame-Wardenate**, the High Flame-Warden's house, stands at the back of the compound against the Vanor's inner wall, with the rooms of the Scorchmasters and the Aspirants' hall beside it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The endowment vote.** A senator who patronizes the Ashen Temple wants proof that the Árdavan keepers of the Temple of the Twelve misspend the endowment the Seldan votes them, before the vote.
- **The challenger.** A provincial Flame-Warden has come to Orzara with his Scorchmasters, and the Ashen Temple wants to know whether he has come to pray or to challenge.
- **The preacher on the Spire Circle.** A preacher of the Black Flame on the Spire Circle draws a bigger crowd each week, and a watch-house captain wants to know who pays him.

# The Truth of the Ashen Temple {#truth .secret}

The grievances are read before they burn. On the Night of Ashes the faithful throw their written grievances into the fire, and the Scorchmasters who tend the basin take the wax and slate from the crowd's hands to throw in for them. The best of them, the grievances of officers, senators' clients, clerks of the Offices and men of the Watch, go into a sack and not into the fire. The Flame-Wardenate reads them afterward. Over the years it has built a ledger of the city's grudges: who hates whom in the Seldan, which officers blame which house for Taruzel, which clerks hate their usos. The High Flame-Warden uses the ledger to choose his patrons and to know which men in the legions will listen to the faith. Nobody outside the Flame-Wardenate knows the ledger exists.

The faith's quarrel reaches past the Vanor. Knights of Nartun's [[affiliation-sacredflame|Order of the Sacred Flame]] carry worshippers of the Black Flame out of the villages of the Hilen march across the border to be judged in the holy city, and the High Flame-Warden has the names of the households taken. His patrons in the Seldan have not raised it, because to raise it would ask the throne to do something about Nartun, and no Orzas has moved against Nartun since the legions turned back from its walls.
