## Overview {#overview}

Everything in [[place-orzara|Orzara]] is carried up or down one of six hills, and a bearer learns the names before he learns the streets. The Six Hills stand in a cluster on the eastern bank of the [[place-vylarisrvr|River Vilaris]], and the city's bounds follow their contours rather than any drawn circle: an irregular sprawl, kinked along the river and notched and bulging wherever the high ground runs.

A bearer who has carried firewood, water and wine up the hill-lanes for thirty years teaches an apprentice by the slope: "The high ground is the good ground. Better air, better view, and nobody's cart-wheels in your ear. But nothing up there comes to you. You carry it, one load at a time, and that is how the great houses know what an address costs."

## The Hills {#the-land}

- [[place-arvel|Arvel]], the Eagle Hill, is the highest. The walled [[place-arvon|Arvon]] (the Inner City) and [[place-palvar|Palvar]] (the Grand Palace) crown it.
- [Torvel]{.name data-type="place/feature"} (the Coronal) and [[place-selvin|the Laurel]] are the twin patrician hills that together make **the [[place-heightsorz|Heights]]**. Torvel is the older and prouder, ringed with the ancestral villas of the senatorial houses; Selvin is greener and newer, and the [[place-gildedqrtr|Gilded Quarter]] climbs its lower slopes.
- [Mernel]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the Scholars' Hill, carries the [[place-scholarsdstr|Scholars' District]], the campus of [[affiliation-panepistmm|The Academy]], and the [Circle of Marendas]{.name data-type="place/site"}.
- [[place-antel|Antel]], the Old Hill, was the [Vilérian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}'s city before the Founder walled the Eagle Hill, and was once second only to it. Its early monuments weather unrepaired, most of its great families have moved to the Laurel, and its once-proud Circle has gone shabby, dignified still but visibly fading.
- [Nerval]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the River Hill, is the crowded working height above the wharves, its slopes packed with the densest of the [[place-warrensorz|Warrens]].

The saddles, valleys and riverside flats between the hills carry the city's daily business: the [[place-grandmarketorz|Grand Market]] on the central flats, the [[place-wharvesorz|Wharves]] beneath Nerval, and [[place-rathaunamelvar|The Racecourse]] along the valley between Antel and Nerval. The central saddle between Arvel and Torvel holds the [[place-platandstr|Platan District]], the civic heart, around the [Circle of Árdavon]{.name data-type="place/site"}. Beyond the hills and the Clearance that rings them, the [[place-outskirtsorz|Outskirts]] spread across the lower ground, and the foundries and foul trades of the [Smoke]{.name data-type="place/site"} lie south of the city, downwind and downriver of the hills.

The hills explain where the city's twelve Circles fall. A Circle is three hundred yards of level pavement, and level ground is what a hilly city is short of, so the Circles sit on the broader hilltops, in the saddles and on the river flats, never on the grades. The avenues that string them together run to irregular lengths and bearings as they climb and descend.

## Who Lives Where {#who-uses-it}

As a rule the high ground is prized and the low ground is dense, busy and poorer, except where the river makes the wharves rich. The houses of rank sit on the crowns and the upper slopes, the craftsmen who serve them on the lower slopes, and the veteran-citizens in the _tengali_ (tenements) that fill the valleys and climb Nerval. An Orzaran gives his address by hill before he gives it by street, and the hill tells a listener most of what he needs to know.

Nothing on the steep lanes comes by cart. The bearers' guild carries the upper slopes' water, wood, wine and furniture on its members' backs, and keeps its hall at the foot of the [Torvel Stairs]{.name data-type="place/feature"}. Water comes to the hills by the aqueduct, whose branches climb them through pressure-towers to the houses' allotted pipes, and the Veramus of Works and Waters, who sits for [[affiliation-verdanorzara|Orzara Courts]], sets every allotment.

## What the Slopes Do {#dangers}

Fire runs uphill and water runs down. A blaze in the timber upper floors of a tenement on Nerval climbs the slope faster than the Watch's hand-engines can follow, and the blank fire-walls that cross the lower wards each mark a fire that did ([[lore-greatburnng|The Great Burning]]). The spring rains bring down whatever the slopes hold: a lane's paving, a garden wall, now and then a tenement built too high on a bad foundation. In a dry summer the pressure-towers cannot lift enough water to the crowns, and the oldest cisterns on Torvel run low first.

## What Is Told {#what-is-told}

The city's guides say the hills were chosen by the eagle that gave Arvel its name, which circled them six times before it settled on the highest. The Warrens say that the hills are worth exactly what it costs to carry a jar of water to the top of each, and price them from Nerval upward.

## Water and Height {#present}

The Six Hills quarrel over water, over height and over the proclamation every one of them is waiting for.

- **The water.** The Veramus of Works and Waters cannot lift more water up the hills than the towers carry, and the houses at the top of Torvel and of [[place-selvin|Selvin]] want more than their allotments. A house that widens its pipe takes water from the house below, and every dry summer the Veramus's clerks are offered more for an allotment than the law fines for exceeding one.
- **The height.** The landlords of Nerval build higher than the district law's seven stories and pay the _peltros_ (ward magistrate) not to see it. The tenants on the top floors fear fire, and the ward's watch-house cannot reach an eighth story with its ladders.
- **The day of the proclamation.** The reigning Orzas is old and has adopted nobody. Every hill knows that the news of his death will come down from Arvel to the Platan and spread to the Circles within the hour, and every hill counts what its own Circle will do when it arrives.

## History {#history}

The [Vilérian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} built its city on Antel and left Arvel bare above the sealed galleries of the [[place-eyriearvel|Eyrie of the Dragon Riders]]. The first Orzas walled Arvel's crown, moved the Senate up from Antel and consecrated the thrima around all six hills, and from then the name Orzara covered everything the line enclosed ([[lore-eaglecityrse|The Raising of the Eagle City]]). Antel lost the Senate and, over a few generations, most of its great families, and the six days of fire that ran from the Warrens over Antel about 403 sent most of the rest to Selvin ([[lore-greatburnng|The Great Burning]]). The fire-walls, the Clearance around the city and the district law that keeps forges and foul trades beyond the line are all the rebuilding's statutes.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The allotment.** A clerk of the Veramus of Works and Waters, who sits for [[affiliation-verdanorzara|Orzara Courts]], has been offered a summer's wages to move a valve on Torvel. He wants a party to learn which house is paying before he decides.
- **The ladders.** The captain of the watch-house on the [Spire Circle]{.name data-type="place/site"} wants longer ladders before winter, and the ward's _peltros_ will not pay for them. He hires a party to count the stories on Nerval that his ladders cannot reach.
- **The first Circle.** A ward captain of [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|the City Watch]] wants to know which hill's Circle will fill first when the Orzas dies, and hires a party to listen in the taverns of each.
