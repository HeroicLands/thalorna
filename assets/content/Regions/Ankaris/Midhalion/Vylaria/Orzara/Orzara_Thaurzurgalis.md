## Thaurzurgalis—The Imperial Barracks

Within the walls of [[place-arvon|Arvon]], hard against the flank of the palace hill, stands the **Thaurzurgalis**—the fortified barracks of [[affiliation-sorvan|the Sorvan]], the imperial guard. It is the fortress of the Orzas, the emperor, at the center of his own capital: the strongpoint from which the throne's grip on the Inner City is enforced, and the only true camp raised inside the [Inner Wall]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, where no field legion is ever quartered.

### The Fortress

The Guard Camp is built to the legionary pattern but turned inward, a compact stone fastness rather than a marching camp—high curtain walls, a single heavily-warded gate, drill yards, armories, stables, granaries, and barrack-blocks enough to house the guard at strength with room to spare. Its walls look not only outward at the city but across the short distance to the [[place-palvar|Palvar]] itself, for the guard's first charge is the imperial person, and the Guard Camp is sited so that armed men can reach the palace gates within minutes of an alarm. The tramp of drilling soldiers is a constant background rhythm of the precinct, and the gleam of the guard's lavish armor on the walls is meant to do two things at once: reassure the loyal, and unsettle the ambitious.

### The Armed Heart of the Court

To hold the Guard Camp is, in a real sense, to hold the capital. The body best placed to defend an Orzas is also the body best placed to unmake one, and more than one reign in Vylarian history has turned on which way the gates of the Guard Camp opened on a decisive night, the first of them in 389 ([[lore-brkensuccsn|The Broken Succession]]). Every reigning Orzas therefore courts the guard with care—with donatives, with privileges, with the careful cultivation of the officers who command the watches—and a wise throne keeps a close and personal eye on who rises within these walls. The Guard Camp's politics are the court's politics in their most concentrated and dangerous form.

The guard's writ, and the Guard Camp's reach, stop at the Inner Wall. The policing of the city beyond is no part of their charge; that falls to the [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|City Watch]], whose watch-houses ring the Twelve Circles below and whose watchmen the guardsmen of the Guard Camp regard with lordly contempt. A veteran of the Watch, who has spent his working life on the street side of that gate, puts the arrangement in a sentence: "The Guard Camp's gate does not open for us, and we do not knock." Within the Inner City, however, order and justice are the guard's to keep, and the Guard Camp is where that authority is mustered, drilled, and—when the throne requires it—unleashed.
