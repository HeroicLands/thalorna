## Overview {#overview}

A day's ride from [[place-velysara|Velisara]] there is a tract of good farmland that nobody farms. It is the ground where [[lore-recensio|the Therona of Alessa]] ended in 75 BF, and nearly eight centuries on it is still bare. A fence runs round it, kept in repair, and the grass inside the fence is thin and does not take.

A farmer whose holding adjoins the fence gives carters asking for a short cut across it the local rule about [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]'s patrol: "Go round by the road. The Order's patrol will ask you to turn back once, and it does not ask twice."

## What Is There

The fence encloses a tract of the Alessan plain. Inside it the soil is the same black loam as the farms on every side, and nothing has taken root on it that the Order's crews do not clear at the Order's charge. There are no buildings and no ruins visible from the road. The Order has never dug the ground, and it stops anyone who tries.

## Who Comes Here

- **Sekeroi**, on the first commission [[affiliation-sekran|The Arcane Inquisition]] (the Sekran) holds: to establish what happened at [[place-alyssa|Alessa]], which has never been closed.
- **Scholars of Velisara**, to look at the fence.
- **Treasure-seekers**, whom the Order's patrols stop.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The Order keeps the ground fenced, bare and undug at its own charge, and its patrols stop anyone who tries to dig it. The [[affiliation-sekran|Sekran]]'s first commission, to establish what happened at the Therona, has never been closed, and its _sekeroi_ still come. Scholars of [[place-velysara|Velisara]] come to look at the fence, and treasure-seekers come for what they believe is under it. A tenant whose farm runs to the fence line has turned something up with his plow, and a clerk reconciling the inventory taken after the Therona finds it one short.
