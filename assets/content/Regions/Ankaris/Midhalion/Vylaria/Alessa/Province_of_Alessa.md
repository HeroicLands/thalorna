## Overview

When a Vilérian says "the empire," he means Alessa first and everything else second. The Province of Alessa is the heartland of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]: the province that contains the imperial capital, [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] (the Senate) and the oldest institutions of Vilérian civilization, and the most urbanized, politically sophisticated and culturally self-assured of the four. It holds the land of [[place-alyssa|Alessa]], seven million people governed from the provincial capital, [[place-alyssar|Alessar]], by a _therkos_ (governor) the emperor appoints.

A _kurthalos_ (council member) of a chartered town, seated on its council and liable in his own person for the taxes the town owes, gives his nephew the same advice on the day the young man takes his place: "Learn who writes to whom. The _therkos_ answers to the Orzas, the emperor, and to nobody else. The _karezos_ (imperial steward) collects the emperor's revenue and reports past the _therkos_, so he does not need to like him. The _felzos_ (legate) holds the soldiers. And every one of them has a cousin in [[place-orzara|Orzara]]. Whatever you decide here, somebody in the capital will hear it before the week is out."

## Character

The oldest families, those who trace their lineage to the empire's founding, hold court here, and the politics of Alessa are the politics of the empire. Every major institution has its headquarters in the capital: the Senate, the Imperial Court, the high priesthoods of the faiths of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] and, most significant of all, the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]].

Alessan society is intensely political. Every conversation carries subtext and every dinner party is a negotiation. The senatorial families compete for influence through patronage networks, strategic marriages and control of religious offices. The common people of the capital are more politically aware than their counterparts elsewhere in the empire, and public opinion, expressed through street demonstrations, pamphlets and the occasional riot, is a force that even _kurthoi_ (senators) must reckon with.

## Life in the Province

Alessa is the empire at its most cultivated and its most political: paved roads, aqueducts, market towns and great estates, where almost nothing is more than a day's ride from a temple, a magistrate and a tax-collector. Adventure here is rarely about wilderness. It is about the press of people and power: senatorial intrigue, Arcane Order surveillance, religious patronage-fights, debt and inheritance suits, and the patronage networks that decide everything.

What is scarce is food, which arrives from the [[affiliation-provnchyln|Province of Hilen]] and the [[affiliation-provincvld|Province of Salkalon]], and privacy. What is abundant is law, rumor, money and witnesses. A settlement seeded here can be assumed to have roads, an inn, a shrine, a resident magistrate, and at least one great family whose interests reach back to the capital.

## Current Situation {#present}

The province waits on two deaths. The _therkos_, [[being-kldrmslthzs|Kaldremas Elthezis]], is old and has kept to his villa at [[place-vennara|Vennara]] since the winter, and his freedman secretary, Nethoren, reads his sealed acts from the steps of the Governor's House at [[place-alyssar|Alessar]]. The old Orzas, [[being-zrmlshlrkls|Zarmelas Helarkales]], has adopted nobody, and in Alessa, where every officer has a cousin in the capital, the succession is the province's own politics.

- **The cities and the capital.** The old provincial cities pay for the capital's roads and houses with what they have. The north road's levy is assessed every year while its bridge beyond [[place-skoraz|Soraks]] lies broken, builders from Orzara work by night with cranes in [[place-velysara|Velisara]]'s forums, and the great houses of [[place-belekos|Belekos]] move their money out of the empire.
- **The debts.** A factor who will not say whose he is has bought the notes of several lesser houses on [[place-lentora|Lentora]]'s council, and its seats are falling empty because a _kurthalos_ answers for the city's taxes in his own person.
- **The legion.** An officer is coming from Orzara to count the legion at [[place-corvinus|Korvenara]], whose _vandraks_ spends most of the year at court.
- **The street.** The capital's people read pamphlets and riot, and the senatorial families reckon with them. The province's grain comes from Hilen and Salkalon, and the price at [[place-ostenna|Ostenna]]'s Landing is the one every household watches.

## Commerce and Currency

Alessa uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian currency]] (Solden, Ardin and Ogdel) as its standard, with full access to the paper-script system through [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]], the moneylenders chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]]. [[affiliation-tolvan|The Tolvan]] administers imperial taxation, and the revenues flow to [[affiliation-tarmex|the Tarmeks Orzan]]. The full system is set out in [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian Currency]].

## History {#history}

The Vilérian state began in this province, and every age of it has left something here. About 650 BF the patrician families of the city on the Vilaris overthrew the last of its kings and set up the Republic and its Senate ([[lore-vylarirpbl|The Establishment of the Vilérian Republic]]). In 75 BF the Kandral ordered every registered practitioner in the Republic to bring their working-books to one place in the Alessan country to be copied, and in the fourth month an unchanneled release burned the books and killed most of the Republic's senior arcanists ([[lore-recensio|The Therona of Alessa]]). The ground is still bare and fenced a day's ride from [[place-velysara|Velisara]] ([[place-recensiogrnd|The Fenced Ground]]). In 73 BF the survivors' charter made the Thordimalon the only lawful practice of the arcane ([[lore-kandrol|The Kandrol]]), and the Sekran hunted everyone outside the register into the first reigns of the Empire ([[lore-huntingyears|The Hunting Years]]).

In Year 1 a _kurthos_ with the legions and the Arcane Order behind him made himself Orzas ([[lore-thefounding|The Founding]]). He walled the crown of Arvel, moved the Senate up from the Old Hill and named the city Orzara ([[lore-eaglecityrse|The Raising of the Eagle City]]), and about 34 his successor made the citizenship a soldier's reward and tied the dole to it ([[lore-veteranslaw|The Veterans' Law]]). The capital the province holds has been a veterans' city since.

About 220 the faith of the Forge-Lord split ([[lore-ustaranschism|The Ústaran Schism]]). The Orzas Karomfos would not proscribe [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]], which had temples in his capital, and about 224 Nartun's high priesthood left the Empire over it ([[lore-nartumrefuse|Nartun Refuses the Empire]]). The Black Flame has kept open temples in Orzara and in the province's commercial cities ever since, and the Night of Ashes is a public festival in them. About 318 the imperial archive on Arvel burned ([[lore-archivefire|The Archive Fire]]).

The province's worst years followed the death of Barnakis in 389, when three men claimed the throne for sixteen years ([[lore-brkensuccsn|The Broken Succession]]). The war cost Hilen its harvest, the dole went unpaid about 403, and fire ran over the capital's Old Hill for six days ([[lore-greatburnng|The Great Burning]]). About 406 Makandas took the city and founded House Helarkales ([[lore-restorehouse|The Restoration]]). Between about 418 and 425 his successor Arzanas founded the Tevran Maldor, constituted the Tolvan and made the Tarmeks the central bank of the paper the province's moneylenders clear ([[lore-arzanasedict|The Edicts of Arzanas]]).

Through the two centuries of decline the far provinces stopped paying ([[lore-empirdclne|The Decline of the Vilérian Empire]]), and about 709, after the Shoals, the Empire recognized Taruzel's independence ([[lore-tamzirbattle|The Battle of Tamzir Shoals]]). The heartland carries what the lost provinces paid. Its old cities pay for the capital's roads and houses with what they have left, and the province still eats grain from Hilen and Salkalon.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The physician's errand.** The _therkos_'s physician wants a remedy fetched from Orzara quickly, and nobody in [[place-alyssar|Alessar]] told who it is for.
- **The count.** The Vallar at [[place-corvinus|Korvenara]] wants the inspecting officer delayed on the road for three days.
- **The empty seat.** [[place-lentora|Lentora]]'s magistrates mean to name a party's patron to the council while he is away.
