## Overview

Lentora is the second city of [[affiliation-provinclys|Alessa]] after the capital, an old inland city of about 32,000 with forums, baths and guildhalls, and the market and administrative hub of the populous farm-and-villa country of the inner province. Beside [[place-orzara|Orzara]] it is wealthy and unglamorous, which suits the people who keep their seats here: the lesser senatorial houses and the prosperous _gulmen_ (equestrian) families.

A villa steward who rides in at the turn of each season to settle the household's accounts and attend its suits describes the city to the new under-steward: "Do not look for [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]], the Senate, here. Look for the guildhalls and the magistrates' benches. The capital is busy with the empire, and so a great deal of what Alessa actually needs done, the leases, the roads, the disputes between neighbors, gets done in Lentora, by people nobody in the capital has heard of."

## Arriving

Lentora sits in open farm country with no hill to make it grand, and it announces itself by its walls, which are old, whole and kept in repair, a rare thing in the heartland. Inside the gate the main street runs straight to the [Great Forum]{.name data-type="place/site"}, a paved square larger than the city needs, with the guildhalls on three sides and the magistrates' basilica on the fourth. Every guild in the inner province keeps a hall there, each with its emblem over the door and a bench outside where its members wait for business: the surveyors with their chains, the notaries, the builders, the oil-pressers, the wool merchants and a dozen more.

The city's pride is the [Lentora Baths]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, the largest outside the capital, whose furnaces have been kept burning without a break for longer than the oldest guild can show in its books. Lentorans bathe every afternoon, and the business of the morning is finished in the hot rooms. The city's other public buildings have done less well. The theater beside the baths has been shut for two generations and is a timber yard, and in the streets behind the forum the townhouses of the lesser senatorial families have been divided into apartments, with the family on the first floor and tenants above.

## What Lentora Lives By

Lentora lives by the ordinary business of the farm-and-villa country. A tenant renewing his lease, a family dividing an estate, two neighbors quarreling over a boundary wall or a water channel, a steward hiring hands for the harvest: each comes to Lentora, finds the guild and the magistrate he needs on the forum, and is home by evening. The surveyors' hall is the busiest in the city, and its members are the most trusted men in it, since half the suits before the magistrates turn on where a stone was set.

The country's produce passes through as well. Oil, wine, wool and grain are sold by sample in the guildhalls and delivered by road to the capital, so Lentora's markets show little of the goods and a great deal of the paper. The money stays: the families of the inner province bank it with the guilds, spend it in the city's shops and baths, and marry their children to one another at the [Feast of Thresholds]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}, when contracts of every kind are renewed before [[lore-ardavondty|Árdavon]]'s priests.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order

Lentora is a chartered town under the Province of Alessa, governed by two _tavemoi_ elected each year and a council of _kurthaloi_. The magistrates sit in the basilica on the forum and hear the city's suits, with appeal to the _therkos_'s court at [[place-alyssar|Alessar]]. The council and the guilds run the province's ordinary business, the leases, the roads and the boundary suits, and Alessar's part is to seal it. The city keeps its own watch, a company of veterans paid by the council, which locks the gates at dark.

A seat on the council is the city's great honor and its great danger. Each _kurthalos_ answers in his own person for the taxes Lentora owes, and when the harvest is poor and the city's collections fall short, the council makes up the difference from its members' purses. The _gulmen_ families compete for the honor in good years and send their sons to the capital in bad ones, so that nobody can be named to an empty seat. Lately the council has had more empty seats than it can fill. A factor who gives his name as a buyer of debts and will not say whose he is has bought up the notes of several of the lesser houses on the council, and the guilds talk of little else in the baths.

## Politics {#present}

Lentora's council is falling into a stranger's hands. A factor who gives his name as a buyer of debts, and will not say whose he is, has bought the notes of several lesser houses that sit on the council, and the guilds talk of little else in the baths. The seats are a danger in a poor year, because each _kurthalos_ makes up the city's shortfall from his own purse, and the _gulmen_ families send their sons to the capital so they cannot be named. The _tavemoi_ mean to name an absent man to an empty seat before he can refuse. Someone is trying to put out the furnaces of the baths.

## Where a Party Lodges

Strangers lodge around the forum, where the inns serve the people who have come in on business, or in the hostels the guilds keep for their own members from out of town, which take paying guests when they have beds. An inn on the forum clears its tables at the hour the baths open and fills them again at dusk. Lentora's people are polite to strangers and incurious about them; a newcomer who wants to be noticed has to have a suit, a lease or a letter.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The stone.** A boundary stone between two estates has moved a hundred paces in the night, and the surveyor who set it is the party's employer's cousin. Both estates have hired advocates, and the case is heard in ten days.
- **The empty seat.** The _tavemoi_ mean to name a party's patron to the council while he is away from the city. His steward hires the party to reach him first, so that he can refuse in person.
- **The furnace.** Someone is trying to put out the furnaces of the baths. The city regards this as a threat to everything it is.

# The Truth of Lentora {#truth .secret}

The factor buys for [[affiliation-thalurates|House Thalurates]], the house raised from the clerks of the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]], which is buying the debts of old houses everywhere. In Lentora it is buying a council. The notes the factor holds belong to seven of the city's _kurthaloi_, and in a poor year each of them must find his share of the city's taxes or default. The house means to forgive the notes one at a time, each in return for a vote, until Lentora's council does what the Thalurates ask, and the Thalurates mean to ask for the city's lease of the imperial estates around it, which come up for renewal at the next Thresholds.

The man trying to put out the furnaces is a _kurthalos_ who has worked this out. A city with cold baths has an emergency, an emergency calls the council into open session in the basilica, and in open session the debts come out.
