## Overview {#overview}

**The Linden Walk** lies in the north of the imperial game-forest of [[place-hylen|Hilen]], a long day's ride inside the paling from [[place-cervalia|Kervalara]], where the reserve climbs into low wooded hills. Two rows of lindens lean together over a path, and their limbs are braided into one another overhead so closely that the path runs under a green roof for a quarter of a mile, winding up the hillside to a round glade at the top. The wardens of the forest know it, keep the deer rides clear of it, and cut no wood there. In early summer, when the lindens flower, the walk hums with bees from end to end and the scent of it carries to the nearest ride.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The walk is the crown's, as every tree inside the paling is, and the wardens keep it for the household of the Orzas with the rest of the forest. Nobody may enter the reserve without the wardens' leave, and a stranger taken inside the paling goes before the forest court.

## Layers {#history}

The wardens say the walk was there when the first paling was set, and that the first wardens found it already old. Their own account is that a gardener of some forgotten emperor planted it for a hunting lodge that was never built. The oldest of the houndsmen say that no warden in their memory has cut a linden in the walk, and that none of them knows who first forbade it.

## What Is There Now {#present}

A scholar of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] has come to Kervalara from the capital and asked the wardens for leave to enter the forest and study the walk. He holds that it is a work of the Elder Folk, and that a walk of living trees grown to a plan is the proof the Arcane Order has never had that they exist. The wardens refused him. Hilen has no love for the Arcane Order, the court comes to hunt this autumn, and the wardens want no stranger walking the rides while the deer are being brought on.

The scholar has not gone home. He lodges at the [Paling]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, the inn at the far end of Kervalara's street, he has money, and he has let it be known that he pays well for a guide who knows the forest and does not mind the forest court.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The wardens are the danger: some two hundred wardens and houndsmen, armed, mounted on the rides and paid to take trespassers, and the forest court in the Lodge does not care what a trespasser was looking for. Boar lie up in the thickets of the northern hills and do not give way to a man on foot. In the flowering, the bees of the walk swarm at anyone who disturbs the hollow trees.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A green tunnel under braided linden limbs, the trunks on either side leaning in at the same angle along its whole length, the path between them soft with old leaves. It climbs the hill in long bends and comes out into a glade. Around the edge of the glade stands a ring of linden stools, each a low mass of old wood wider than a cart wheel with a crowd of trunks rising from it, every trunk as thick as a man's body. Each stool was cut back to the ground many times and has grown on uncut since, and no warden cuts there. In the middle of the glade stands one linden older than all the rest, its trunk hollow, with an opening on its south side the height of a man and a room inside it where four could sit. Below the glade on its downhill side, a hollow holds standing water among sedge, with flat stones laid along its edge.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The scholar's guide.** The scholar pays a party to take him in by night, to the glade and back, without the wardens' knowledge, and he means to bring back cuttings, drawings and the measure of the hollow tree.
- **The wardens' trespasser.** The wardens have found tracks on the northern rides that are not theirs, and the head warden hires a party to find who is walking the forest before the court arrives.
- **The court's table.** The household of the Orzas wants a table laid in the glade for the court on the last day of the hunt, and the wardens want a party to keep the court's servants from breaking anything there.

# The Truth of the Linden Walk {#truth .secret}

**The camp.** The glade is **Nelpusto**, "the place of rope", an early-summer camp of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]], abandoned. When the lindens flowered and their bark slipped from the wood, a company of Senali came to the glade, cut the poles from the ring of stools, stripped the inner bark from them and laid it in the hollow below to soften, then twisted it into cord and rope on the glade and dried it on the walk. The hollow tree was their store. They led the walk's limbs together to roof the way up from the low ground and never cut a tree of the walk, only the stools of the ring, which grow back from the cut.

**The leaving.** The camp emptied as the Senali drew back from the peninsula into remote forests, long before the empire. The crown's forest kept the plow from the hill, and the wardens' rule against cutting in the walk kept the rest, so the walk and the stools grew on untended.

**What the scholar wants.** Nothing in the walk is magic, and nothing in it answers the Arcane Order's question. A party that takes the scholar in brings him to trees, and the trees prove only that someone led them.

> [!TIP]
> The scholar is right and cannot prove it, and the forest court keeps the rope for deer and fines or flogs a trespasser. The bark-stripping, the retting hollow, the hollow tree as a store and the camp's leaving are this note's own account; a game master may change them.
