## Overview {#overview}

Grain, wine and olive oil: the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|empire]] lives on what Hilen grows. It is the territory of the [[affiliation-provnchyln|Province of Hilen]], seven million people on the fertile lowlands and terraced hillsides of the [[place-vylariargn|Viléria Region]], where a few aristocratic families hold estates as large as valleys and grow the grain, wine and olive oil that the empire lives on. Its seat is [[place-aravantia|Aravantara]]. Only a small share of the seven million live in its towns; the rest live in the villages of the great estates, as tenants of the manor houses.

Hilen borders [[place-alyssa|Alessa]] to the west and [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]] to the south, with [[place-nartumkngdm|Nartun]] to the north. It shares no border with [[place-moktur|Moktur]].

An estate steward, riding the boundary with a newly hired bailiff on his first morning, gives the whole lesson in a few sentences: "Everything between this road and that ridge belongs to the house. Everything past the ridge belongs to the neighbors, and they sit in [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] (the Senate) too. Between us we send the capital its bread, and in all my years nobody in [[place-orzara|Orzara]] has asked how it gets there."

## Character

Where [[place-alyssa|Alessa]] runs on politics and intrigue, Hilen runs on land and harvests. Each great estate is a self-contained world: a manor house at its center, surrounded by fields, vineyards and orchards, and by the villages of the tenant farmers and laborers who work them. A traveler here passes from one world to the next by crossing a boundary stone, not by passing a town gate.

## Economy

Agriculture dominates. Hilen's grain feeds the capital and the legions, its wine and olive oil are exported across the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]], and its estates add wool, linen and leather. Almost nothing is manufactured here. The lords prefer to sell raw goods and buy finished ones from Alessa and [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]], so the province sends out what it grows and brings in what it wears and uses.

## Notable Features

- The great aristocratic estates, some encompassing entire valleys
- The breadbasket of the empire, which controls the food supply
- The imperial game-forests, woodland reserved by the crown for the hunt

## Current Situation {#present}

Hilen's quarrel is over land and who carries its debt. The great estates grow by taking it from the people who work it. At the harvest settling in [[place-messora|Messora]] the houses' factors buy out free farmers and carry their tenants' debts into the next year, and at [[place-cervalia|Kervalara]] the paling of the imperial game-forest moves outward into tenant fields every few years, where the forest magistrate hangs the poachers. The land tax is farmed, and [[being-ylzmsmlvrts|Yalzemas Molvarates]], who holds its contracts a year at a time, offers the throne three years' silver now for the right to collect for ten.

Above the land the houses watch the succession. The old Orzas, [[being-zrmlshlrkls|Zarmelas Helarkales]], has adopted nobody, the capital eats what Hilen grows, and the estates that fed both armies at [[place-olcassafld|Olkassa]] fear civil war more than any province does. The house that sells the capital its bread, [[affiliation-mardovanes|House Mardovanes]], is marrying its master's daughter to the heir of the Olkassa estate, and the man the court expects to be named is a Mardovanes kinsman.

## Settlements {#settlements}

Hilen is a country of estates and market-towns: great aristocratic domains, each a small world of manor, fields, vineyards and tenant villages, linked by the towns where their produce is weighed and sold on.

**Towns.**

[[place-aravantia|Aravantara]] (~28,000)
: the provincial capital and the great grain-and-wine market of the breadbasket.

[[place-granaria|Kelborara]] (~7,000)
: the chief grain-market of the central province, all granaries and weighing-yards.

[[place-vindara|Vindara]] (~6,000)
: a prosperous town of the terraced wine country.

[[place-olivenna|Olivenna]] (~5,000)
: a town of presses and oil-merchants in the olive country.

[[place-polthara|Polthara]] (~5,000)
: a market town of the estate country.

[[place-messora|Messora]] (~4,000)
: a harvest-market town of the southern estates.

[[place-ashenmere|Ashenmere]] (~4,000)
: a border town on the province's march.

[[place-cervalia|Kervalara]] (~2,500)
: the forest town at the edge of the imperial game-reserve.

**The great estates and manorial holdings.** The countryside belongs to vast aristocratic domains, not to free villages. Each is a manor with its tenant village, chapel and labor force of 200–1,000 souls. The named holdings include [Kaldóvia]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Sennfeld]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Aurvale]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, **Olkassa** ([[place-olcassafld|The Blood-field of Olkassa]], the field of [[lore-olcassabtl|the Battle of Olkassa]]), [Brennfeld]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Granholt]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Vellora]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Kalvenna]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Marenna]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Tallenza]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Oksenna]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Harvenna]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Winnara]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} and [Stonemere]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}. Several are the seats of senatorial families whose word is effectively law within their own bounds. These are only the great houses; the province holds hundreds of lesser estates and tenant villages besides.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The ten-year bid.** A master of an old house wants to know what [[being-ylzmsmlvrts|Yalzemas Molvarates]] means to collect from his tenants if she wins ten years of the land tax, and hires a party to ride her collectors' last circuit.
- **The new line.** A village outside [[place-cervalia|Kervalara]] hears that the paling is to move again, and its headman wants witnesses to how much ground the wardens take.
