## Overview {#overview}

The Province of Hilen holds the land of [[place-hylen|Hilen]], and its landed aristocracy is the most conservative faction in Vilérian politics: traditionalist, suspicious of change, and deeply invested in the old social order. The _therkos_ (governor) sits at [[place-aravantia|Aravantara]].

A steward of one of the great estates, asked by a visiting _gulmen_ (equestrian) what Hilen makes of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] and its growing weight at court, does not trouble to be polite: "The [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|empire]] was built on soldiers and farmers. The mages and the money-changers came afterward, and they will be gone before the vines are."

## Character

Most of the province's people live outside its towns, in the villages that belong to the great estates, working the land as tenants of the lords whose manor houses stand at their centers.

The estate lords live well, though not with the ostentatious wealth of the capital. Theirs is the quiet confidence of people who know that everyone else depends on what they grow.

They resist the growing influence of the merchant class and of the Arcane Order alike, and they see themselves as the true guardians of Vilérian tradition. That makes Hilen a bastion of the [[affiliation-imperialcult|Imperial Cult]] and of the traditional practices of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. The estates have a horror of civil war that the Empire's other provinces do not share, because the one battle the Empire fought between its own legions was fought on an estate of this province ([[lore-olcassabtl|The Battle of Olkassa]]). The field itself is [[place-olcassafld|the Blood-field of Olkassa]].

## Notable Features

- The conservative stronghold of traditional Vilérian values
- A minimal Thordimalon (the Arcane Order) presence, because the aristocracy keeps the Arcane Order at arm's length

## Life in the Province

Hilen is settled but not urban. It is a managed countryside of great estates, vineyards, olive groves and the imperial game-forests that the crown reserves from the plow. Power belongs to the old landed families, not to merchants or the Arcane Order, and the prevailing temper is conservative, pious and slow to change. A settlement here sits on or beside a great estate, and its real lord is whichever family owns the land.

Trouble in Hilen is trouble over land: estate and inheritance disputes, tenant grievances and the occasional rising, banditry in the wooded margins, and the quiet feuds of neighboring great houses. Poaching is the sharpest of them. A game-warden at [[place-cervalia|Kervalara]] tells each new forester the rule before he is handed a bow: "A hungry man who takes the emperor's deer is a hanged man, and it does not matter whose tenant he was. Do not argue the case with him in the forest. Argue it with the magistrate, after."

What is abundant is food, wine and labor. What is scarce is hard coin, news, and anyone who welcomes change.

## Current Situation {#present}

The province is governed by an outsider and run by its houses. The _therkos_ (governor) at [[place-aravantia|Aravantara]], [[being-lnthmsrvls|Linthimas Orvelis]], is the first in a generation with no estate in Hilen, and the masters of the great houses settle among themselves what they will ask of him before his audience opens. Four things press on them now.

- **The succession.** The old Orzas, [[being-zrmlshlrkls|Zarmelas Helarkales]], has adopted nobody, and the capital lives on Hilen's grain. Every master remembers that the one battle legion fought against legion was fought on a Hilen estate. [[affiliation-mardovanes|House Mardovanes]], the Grain House of [[being-nthrsmrdvns|Nethoras Mardovanes]], is marrying his daughter to the heir of [[affiliation-ganothandis|House Ganothandis]]'s Olkassa estate, and the man the court expects to be named is a Mardovanes kinsman.
- **The land.** The estates buy out the free farmers at the [[place-messora|Messora]] settling and carry their tenants' debts from year to year. The crown's paling at [[place-cervalia|Kervalara]] takes tenant fields for the deer, and this year several villages refused to beat for the court's hunt.
- **The tax.** [[being-ylzmsmlvrts|Yalzemas Molvarates]], whose house farms the land tax a year at a time, offers the throne three years' silver now for a ten-year contract. The houses that sit in the Seldan argue against it, because a farmer who has paid ten years in advance collects ten years' worth.
- **The march.** At [[place-ashenmere|Ashenmere]] the forge-shrine of the Sacred Forge and the chapel of Nartun's knights face each other across the border stream, Nartunese priests come down to preach on the estates, and tenant households on the march have left their cottages in the night with nothing taken.

## Commerce and Currency {#commerce}

The Province of Hilen uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian currency]] (Solden / Ardin / Ogdel) as its standard currency, with full access to the paper-script system through [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]] moneylenders chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]]. Imperial taxation is administered by [[affiliation-tolvan|the Tolvan]] and revenues flow to [[affiliation-tarmex|the Tarmeks Orzan]]. See [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian Currency]] for the full system.

## History {#history}

Hilen's estates grow the grain that is milled into the capital's bread, and the province's history turns on the one war that was fought for it.

About 224 the high priesthood of [[place-nartum|Nartun]] withdrew from imperial obedience and anointed a king of its own ([[lore-nartumrefuse|Nartun Refuses the Empire]]). Since then Hilen's north-eastern march has been the Empire's border with the [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]], at the stream below [[place-ashenmere|Ashenmere]] where a chapel of the Order of the Sacred Flame faces a forge-shrine of the Sacred Forge.

When the Orzas Barnakis died in 389 with no heir named, the Guard, the Senate and the legions of Moktur each acclaimed a man of their own ([[lore-brkensuccsn|The Broken Succession]]). Whoever held Hilen's granaries held the capital's bread, and about 401 the Senate's army and the Moktur legions met on the Olkassa estate, legions against legions under the same eagle with no quarter given ([[lore-olcassabtl|The Battle of Olkassa]]). Makandas won the field and lost half his army on it, and the harvest was lost with the granaries. About 403 the bread ran short in Orzara, the dole went unpaid and the capital burned for six days ([[lore-greatburnng|The Great Burning]]). About 406 Makandas took the city and founded the house that still reigns ([[lore-restorehouse|The Restoration]]).

The field is farmed again, and the best wheat in Hilen comes off [[place-olcassafld|the Blood-field of Olkassa]], which the reapers cut round. The great houses took the war as a family's worst memory. A Hilen house does not applaud a general who marches on another Vilérian army, and the province has a horror of civil war that the Empire's other provinces do not share.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The audience.** Linthimas Orvelis believes the petitions he hears are agreed before they reach him, and wants a party with no house behind it at the tables of the Street of the Houses in [[place-aravantia|Aravantara]].
- **The contract.** [[being-ylzmsmlvrts|Yalzemas Molvarates]] wants a party to carry her bid to the capital before the Seldan sits on it, and a house of the province wants the same party to arrive late.
- **The beaters.** The head warden at [[place-cervalia|Kervalara]] is short of beaters for the court's hunt and pays a party to find out why the villages refused.
