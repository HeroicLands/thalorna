## Overview

Vindara sits among the terraced hillsides of [[place-hylen|Hilen]]'s wine country, a prosperous town of six thousand vintners, coopers and the estate-houses whose vintages cross the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]]. The slopes around it are quilted with vineyards worked by tenant families, and the town's fortunes rise and fall with the vintage.

Its merchants deal in the casks and the contracts that carry Hilen's wine to the ports of [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]] and beyond. A cooper's apprentice learns early that a good year fills his master's yard with orders, and a bad one leaves him sweeping it.

## Arriving

The road climbs to Vindara in long turns between terrace walls, and for the last hour a traveler sees nothing but vines above him and vines below. The town is built across the face of the hill on three terraces of its own, joined by stairs, with the cooperages on the lowest, the houses and the market on the middle one, and the temple of [[lore-olveniadty|Ólvenia]], the Bountiful One, alone on the highest. Smoke rises all day from the cooperage yards, where the coopers toast their staves over fires of vine-cuttings, and the smell of charred oak and old wine hangs over the whole hill.

Behind the houses the hill is hollow. The cellars of Vindara are cut back into the slope from every house and street, and the oldest run so far that their owners keep maps of them. In summer they are the coolest places in the province, and the wine merchants do their tasting and their bargaining underground.

Above the town, where the road tops the ridge, the terraces of two estates meet along a wall of dry stone. A stretch of it some forty paces long lies in a slide of rubble across the slope, and the vines on the slide grow at an angle no vintner would plant them. Each house has posted a boundary stone at the top of the slide and another at the foot, and the two pairs of stones disagree by the width of the terrace.

## What Vindara Lives By

Vindara lives by the vintage. In early autumn the tenant families pick the terraces, carry the grapes down in baskets on their backs and tread them in the estates' presses, and for three weeks the town does nothing else. The new wine goes into casks from the cooperages, rests in the cellars through the winter, and goes down the road in spring by wagon to the ports of Salkalon, chiefly [[place-mercavia|Delalara]], where the merchants of the coast ship it across the sea. The best of it goes the other way, to the tables of the capital.

The coopers are the town's guild of note, and the master coopers sit on the council beside the vintners. A Vindara cask is branded with its maker's mark and the year, and the merchants of Salkalon pay more for a Vindara-marked cask than for the same wine in another, since a Vindara cask does not leak on a long voyage.

The great vineyard estates belong to provincial families of standing, named for their estates in the provincial way. The two greatest are the [Ronalen]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, whose estate of [Ronalenna]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} holds the ridge above the town, and the [Kliven]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, whose estate of [Klivenna]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} holds the slope below it. Between them they own half the terraces in sight of the town, and the fallen wall is where their land meets.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order

Vindara is a chartered town under the [[affiliation-provnchyln|Province of Hilen]], with two _tavemoi_ (magistrates) elected each year and a council of _kurthaloi_ (councilors) who answer in their own persons for its taxes. By long custom one _tavemos_ is a vintner's man and the other a master cooper, and the council's seats are divided among the vintners, the coopers and the stewards of the estates.

The feud runs through all of it. Eleven winters ago a season of rain brought the wall down, and a terrace of Ronalen vines slid onto Kliven ground. Both houses claimed the vines that went on growing there, and at the next vintage the pickers of the two estates fought on the slide, and a Kliven steward was killed with a pruning-knife. The case has been before the _therkos_ (governor) at [[place-aravantia|Aravantara]] four times and has come back each time unsettled. Every vintner and cooper in Vindara sells to one house or the other, every council vote is counted by house, and in vintage weeks the _tavemoi_ post men of the town at the slide to keep the two sets of pickers apart.

## The Present {#present}

The feud of the slide is the town's present and its politics. The Ronalen and the Kliven still both claim the terrace that slid eleven winters ago, the _therkos_ has sent the case back unsettled four times, every council vote is counted by house, and the town's men stand between the two sets of pickers at the vintage. The Kliven master has betrothed his daughter to a Salkalon shipping family, to be sealed at Delalara this autumn, to bind his wine to their hulls. Vindara-marked casks of fake brand are reaching ships at sea, and the coopers' name pays for them.

## Where a Party Lodges

The [Vine Stair]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, the inn at the head of the stairs on the middle terrace, takes merchants and travelers and serves the town's own wine at a price that would not buy it in the capital. The carters who take the casks to the coast sleep in the cheaper inns by the cooperages, and in vintage weeks every inn is full of pickers' agents hiring hands. A guest at the Vine Stair is asked, politely and on the first evening, whose wine he drinks, and the answer is remembered.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The slide.** At the vintage the Ronalen pickers find a Kliven boundary stone moved ten paces up the slide overnight. Both houses want a party to stand guard on the slope for the three weeks of the picking, and both offer to pay for it.
- **The betrothal.** The master of the Kliven has betrothed his daughter to the son of a Salkalon shipping family, to bind his wine to their ships. A party is hired to escort her to the betrothal at Delalara, and she asks them, on the road, how fast they can ride.
- **The bad cask.** A shipment of Vindara-marked casks has leaked its whole cargo into a ship's hold at sea. The merchant blames the cooper, the cooper blames the brand, and the brand turns out to be a fake.

# The Truth of Vindara {#truth .secret}

The heirs of the two houses are married. [Volkemas Ronalen]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Ronalen heir, and [[being-gurimaskliv|Gurimas Kliven]], the daughter of the Kliven master, met at Delalara three springs ago when both were sent down with their houses' wine, and last spring, in the same city, they married by contract before the priests of [[affiliation-ardavon|the Faith of Árdavon]], who witness and record contracts for a fee and ask nothing about the parties' fathers. Both are of age. The contract is written, witnessed and binding at law. A father may disown a child for such a marriage, and neither father can undo it.

Neither father knows. Gurimas's father has betrothed her to the Salkalon shipping family, and the betrothal is to be sealed at Delalara this autumn, where the priests of Árdavon hold the record of her first contract in the same temple. Volkemas has told his own father nothing, because the Ronalen master swore at the steward's funeral that no Kliven would ever eat at his table.

The marriage changes the feud. If it is known and stands, the disputed terrace belongs to one family, and so in time do both estates. The pickers on the slide have been fighting for eleven years over land their masters' children have already joined. The Kliven boundary stone that moved in the night was moved by Volkemas himself, who wanted a quarrel loud enough to keep both fathers at Vindara and away from Delalara until he and his wife can decide how to tell them.
