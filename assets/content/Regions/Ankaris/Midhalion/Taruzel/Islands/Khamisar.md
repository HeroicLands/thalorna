## Overview

Every ship that goes through the heart of [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel]] passes between two capes three miles apart: on one hand the beacon of [[place-sharimun|Sharimun]] on the south-western cape of [[place-qalirel|Qalirel]], and on the other the cliffs of Khamisar and the walls of [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]] above them. Khamisar's name is the narrows' purpose: _khamis-_, to close the sea to others, with _-ar_, where the thing is kept. The island is about twenty-five miles long, high and steep, and with the smaller island of [[place-milazun|Milazun]] to the south it makes the country of the city-state of [[affiliation-yamuzel|Yamuzel]], some two million people on Khamisar itself.

Khamisar is a navy's island. Yamuzel's [[place-straitfortress|Strait Fortress]] commands the narrows, its [Naval Yards]{.name data-type="place/site"} build the warships of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]], and the rest of the island is arranged around keeping both supplied, manned and watched.

## The Land

The island rises steeply out of the sea on every side, and its eastern face, toward the narrows, is cliff for most of its length. The highest ground is a ridge running the length of the island, and on every summit of it stands a signal tower of stone, close enough to the next that a fire on one is seen on two others. The towers pass word of a sail from the western channels to the Strait Fortress faster than a ship can sail the distance.

The southern slopes, in the lee of the ridge, are terraced from the shore to the crest in vines, grain and orchards. The northern slopes are rougher, grazed by sheep and cut for the timber the yards can use; the long timbers for keels and masts come in by sea from [[place-visizel|Visizel]]. Rope-walks, sail lofts, tar kilns and smithies line the shore road below the city.

## Harbors and Roads

- **Yamuzel and the narrows.** The city's harbor lies under the Strait Fortress on the narrows themselves. Merchant ships bound through the archipelago gather in the roads below the fortress to wait for a convoy and an escort through the channels.
- **The western shore.** Khamisar's western side faces the channels and the [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]]. Its coves hold marine posts and the watch-boats that patrol the channels, and nothing else is allowed to anchor there.
- **The ridge road.** A paved road built by the navy runs along the ridge from tower to tower, wide enough for a cart and patrolled by marines. A shore road along the southern terraces carries the farm traffic to the city.
- **To Milazun.** Small boats cross daily from the southern shore to Milazun, a few hours' sail.

The farm villages of the southern terraces and the fishing harbors of the northern coves make up the rest of the island.

## Crossing Khamisar

Nobody walks Khamisar unseen. A party landing anywhere but Yamuzel's harbor is met by a marine post within the hour, asked its business and its ship, and sent on to the city with a marine to walk beside it. The ridge road is the quickest way along the island, and the towers on it log every traveler who passes, by name, ship and errand, in books the Strait Fortress collects at the end of each month. The southern terraces are friendlier ground: the farm villages there sell wine and bread to anyone, and many of the farmers are veterans of the war who served in the fleet and talk freely about it to anyone who stands them a cup.

## The Stones on the South Slope

On a long stretch of the southern terraces above the shore, someone has set up rows of white-painted marker stones, each with a number cut into it, dividing the land into small plots. This spring veterans from the Veterans' Quarter began to plough them, a family to a plot, saying the plots are theirs, marked out for them during the war as the land they were promised. The land is leased to the [[affiliation-tanaduni|Tanaduni]], an admirals' family of Yamuzel, whose stewards say it was never surveyed for anyone and that the stones are the veterans' own work. The stewards have asked the Admiral's Council for marines to clear the plots, and the marines are veterans' sons. The [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]] is a month off, and both sides want the matter settled before the whole city turns out on the walls.

A veteran who ploughs one of the plots hires a party to find the proof that the plots were surveyed. The stewards of the admirals' family would pay a party better to find that they were not.

## Current Situation {#present}

Khamisar is a navy's island waiting for its festival with a quarrel on its south slope. The [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]] is a month off, the whole of Yamuzel will be on the walls, and the veterans ploughing the marked plots on the southern terraces will not leave them. The [[affiliation-tanaduni|Tanaduni]], the admirals' family that leases the land, want them cleared first, the marines who would clear them are veterans' sons, and [[being-rzznpddn|Razzina Padduni]] of the Veterans' Quarter wants witnesses on the terraces when they come. On the ridge the signal towers log every traveler as they always do, and on the height of Yamuzel [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]] counts votes for a League toll on the narrows.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The witnesses.** [[being-rzznpddn|Razzina Padduni]] wants a party with no stake in the plots on the terraces the morning the marines come, so that the Council hears an account it will believe.
- **The stewards' price.** The stewards of the [[affiliation-tanaduni|Tanaduni]], who lease the slope, offer a party better pay than any veteran can to show that the plots were never surveyed for anyone.

# The Truth of the South Slope {#truth .secret}

**The plots were surveyed.** In the second year of the war, when Yamuzel needed men for the fleet, its admirals had the south slope surveyed into plots and promised one to every man who served through the war. A surveyor of the Strait Fortress set the stones, and the plots were entered by number in a survey book with a list of the men who signed for them. The stones were never painted then; the veterans painted them this year, to make them easy to see.

**Where the book is.** The survey book is in the Strait Fortress, in the archive where the towers' monthly logs are kept, filed under the year of the survey with the fleet's muster rolls. The archive's keeper is an old navy clerk who knows exactly what the book is and has told nobody, because he was told to file it and forget it. A copy of the list of names exists as well, folded inside the cover of a veteran's discharge papers in the Veterans' Quarter, though its owner died last winter and his widow does not know what it is.

**What follows.** The book in the veterans' hands proves the promise and starts a fight with the admirals' family in the Admiral's Council, at the moment the city is about to celebrate the war. The family's stewards want the book quietly destroyed, and they have offered the archive's keeper a pension to do it before the Review. He has not decided.
