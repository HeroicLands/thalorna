## Overview

The astronomers of [[place-bazunel2|Bazunel]] work on the roofs of the city, and on most nights of the year they have no cloud to complain of. Tashilar is named for that sky: _tashil-_, clear weather, with _-ar_, where the thing is kept. It is the southernmost of the islands of [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel]], about thirty miles long, lying south of the narrows with the small island of [[place-milazun|Milazun]] between it and [[place-khamisar|Khamisar]], and its two million people make up the city-state of [[affiliation-bazunel|Bazunel]] of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]].

Its southern shore faces the open sea, and beyond it, out of sight, the coast of [[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu]]. Khelathi ships make Bazunel their first Taruzelian port, and the city keeps its windows open to them.

## The Land

Tashilar is lower and gentler than the islands to its north, and they shelter it. In summer the north wind comes over Qalirel and Khamisar dry and steady, sweeps the haze off the water and leaves the nights cloudless; in winter the rain clouds come in from the west and break on Visizel's hills before they reach it. The island has no hill country to speak of. Its hills are farmed to the top in terraced vines, grain, almonds and figs, and its villages stand on the hilltops, painted in the strong colors Bazunel paints its waterfront in, ochre, blue and red.

The water is the island's difficulty. Tashilar has no river and few springs, and its farms live on cisterns and on a network of rock-cut channels that carry the winter rain from the hilltops down to them.

## Harbors and Roads

- **Bazunel.** The city's harbor on the southern shore is the [[place-paintedharbor|Painted Harbor]], lined with colored buildings and open-air theaters, and the [[place-designyards|Design Yards]] stand along its western arm.
- **The northern coves.** The island's northern shore is a string of small coves with fishing harbors, facing Milazun across a channel that boats cross in an hour or two.
- **The ridge road.** A road runs the length of the island along the hilltops from village to village, and from its highest point a traveler sees the whole of the archipelago's southern half on a clear day.
- **The southern sea.** Ships for Au'Khelathu and the southern ports leave from Bazunel, and the Academy's navigators teach their pupils on those passages.

## Crossing Tashilar

Tashilar is the easiest island in Taruzel to walk across, and the most pleasant. The ridge road is good, the villages on it keep inns and wine-shops, and the island's people talk to strangers. A party crossing it from the northern coves to Bazunel goes in a day and a half through vineyards and terraces, past villages whose squares hold small theaters of their own, wooden stages where traveling companies from Bazunel play in summer. The [Academy of the Tides]{.name data-type="affiliation"} keeps a viewing station on the highest hill of the ridge, a walled yard with stone sighting-pillars and a keeper's house, and its students walk up from the city in pairs to keep the night watch on the sky.

Bazunel's reputation as a refuge reaches the hill villages too. A stranger who keeps to himself and pays in coin is not asked where he came from, and the hunters of debtors and fugitives from the other cities find the villagers slow to remember faces.

## The Academy's Chart

A cartographer of the Academy of the Tides has spent three summers sounding the channels between the islands from a hired fishing boat, and this winter she finished her chart. Before it had been copied, it was stolen from her rooms in Bazunel. She says it is a chart of the channels for merchant captains, more accurate than any in use, and that its theft is a matter for the city. Three parties have since asked after it in Bazunel's harbor taverns: a factor of the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]], a broker known to deal for the [[affiliation-mrchntryvl|Merchantry of the Veil]], and a quiet Helionite merchant who pays in Vylarian coin. The cartographer offers a party her fee for the next survey to get the chart back before any of them does.

# The Truth of the Chart {#truth .secret}

**What the chart shows.** Among its soundings the chart shows a passage through the [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]] deep enough for a warship of the largest draft, which no chart in use shows and which the pilots of Yamuzel keep to themselves. The cartographer found it by accident and sounded it twice to be sure. She knows what it means: with her chart, a deep-keeled fleet could do in the Shoals what the Vylarian squadron in the war could not.

**Who has it.** The thief was her own assistant, a student of the Academy who owes money to a Bazunel moneylender, and he still has the chart, hidden in the loft of a theater on the Painted Harbor where he works as a scene-painter. He has not sold it, because he has three buyers and is trying to make them bid.

**The buyers.** The Corsair League wants the chart burned, because the deep passage is the League's own advantage in the channels. The Merchantry of the Veil wants to own it and sell its existence, quietly, to whoever pays most. The Helionite merchant is buying for a Vylarian agent. Each of them believes one of the others already has it.
