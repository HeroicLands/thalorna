## Overview

Twice a day the sea comes up the mouth of the [[place-alzriver|Alz River]] and lifts every hull at the quays of [[place-sazumun2|Sazumun]], and twice a day it goes out again and takes the outbound ships with it. The island is named for that motion: _visiz-_, to rise and fall, with _-el_, the ground of it, the ground of the tide. Visizel is the westernmost of the islands of [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel]], some eighty miles from the Amradi shore and about forty miles long, and its two and a half million people make up the city-state of [[affiliation-sazumun|Sazumun]] of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]].

It is the island that faces the west. The desert goods that cross the strait come through the channels to its harbor, and they leave it on the sea routes to [[place-helionis|Helionis]], [[place-vylariargn|Vylaria]] and the western realms.

## The Land

Visizel catches more rain than any other island, because the winter weather comes in from the west and the island's hills meet it first. Its western side is green from autumn to early summer: a coastal plain of grain, orchards and vegetable gardens, watered from the river and from springs. The Alz runs down to that plain from the hills of the interior through a long valley, and the upper valley is wooded in oak, pine and chestnut, the **Alz woods**, where timberwright families cut the trees that become the ships of Sazumun and the masts and keels of the Naval Yards at [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]]. Felled trunks come down the river on the winter flood, chained into rafts.

The interior is high, broken hill country, wooded on its western slopes and bare scrub on its eastern ones, and it belongs to the [[affiliation-haduruni|Haduruni]], the island's hill tribe, who sell the timberwrights the right to cut on their side of the valley and watch the cutting. The eastern side of the island, in the rain shadow, is drier, poorer and thinly settled, a coast of fishing harbors looking out over the channels.

## Harbors and Roads

- **Sazumun and the Alz mouth.** The city spreads along the natural harbor at the river's mouth on the western coast. A bar of sand and gravel lies across the mouth, and ships cross it on the flood tide by marked channels, with a harbor pilot aboard.
- **The western coast.** North of Sazumun the coast runs in low headlands and sandy coves, and [[place-hitusel|Hitusel]] stands on one of them, a fishing village with tanning pits below it.
- **The eastern coast.** The fishing harbors on the channel side trade with [[place-khamisar|Khamisar]] and look out over the [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]].
- **The valley road.** A road runs up the Alz valley from Sazumun to the woods, built for timber carts and ox teams, with toll posts of the city's guilds at the bridges.
- **The coast road.** A cart road runs north along the western coast from Sazumun to Hitusel and the fishing coves, and buyers from the city ride it for Hitusel's hides.

## Crossing Visizel

The road up the Alz is the island's main way inland, and a party on it walks with the timber trade. In the lower valley the road passes mills, orchards and the brick villages of the plain, and the guilds' toll posts stop every cart at every bridge and ask its owner's name. Above the last bridge the woods close in, and the road becomes a track of rutted clay between felling camps, where the timberwrights live in the summer in huts of bark and boards. The camps welcome a traveler who brings news and wine. Above the woods the track gives out at the tribes' boundary, marked by a stone heap the timberwrights do not pass without leave, and the hills beyond it are crossed only with a hill guide.

The coast road north to Hitusel is easier going, through sandy country of fishing coves and smokehouses, and a traveler smells Hitusel's tanning pits a mile before reaching the village.

## The Bar at the Alz Mouth

Three ships have stuck on the Alz bar since the spring, every one of them a foreign merchantman crossing on the flood tide by the marked channel with a Sazumun harbor pilot aboard. Each pilot swore the ship struck where no sand was before. The harbor guild has fined the pilots, charged the ships for their salvage and towage, and insists the marked channel is sound, and the guild's own ships cross the bar every day without trouble. A foreign captain who lost a cargo on the bar, and who believes the pilot was bought, offers a party half of whatever it recovers if it can prove the harbor guild is lying.

## Current Situation {#present}

Visizel's guilds and woods are both under strain. Three foreign merchantmen have stuck on the Alz bar since spring with Sazumun's harbor pilots aboard, the harbor guild has fined the pilots and charged the ships for salvage, and the guild's own ships cross every day without trouble. In the Alz woods the timberwright [[being-zttyblnn|Zattuya Balinuni]] has been asked to mark next winter's whole cut of keel timber for a single buyer, to the lengths of the League's galleys, and she has found the best oak she has seen in years in a stand on the [[affiliation-haduruni|Haduruni]] side of the boundary. Sazumun's charters to the timberwrights say nothing about the tribe. In [[place-hitusel|Hitusel]], [[being-dpmnjkdn|Dapuman Jakuduni]]'s pits have no one to inherit them.

# The Truth of the Bar {#truth .secret}

**The bar has moved.** The winter flood brought down more gravel than usual and cut a new channel through the bar to the south of the old one, and the old marked channel is silting up. The harbor guild learned it in the spring from its own pilots' soundings. It moved its own ships to the new channel at once, sounded and marked by the guild's pilots in their heads and not with buoys, and it left the buoys on the old channel where they were.

**Why.** A foreign ship that sticks on the old channel pays the guild for salvage and towage at the guild's rates, and pays its port dues while it waits for repairs. Three groundings have brought the guild more than a season's pilotage, and the guild's council means to move the buoys at the end of the sailing season and record the change as the result of the autumn storms.

**The pilots.** The pilots who were aboard the grounded ships were not bought. They were never told of the new channel, because the guild council told only the pilots who take the guild's own ships across, and they were fined for wrecks the council arranged. One of them, fined a year's wages, knows something is wrong and has begun sounding the bar by night from a rowing boat. A party that finds him has a witness and a set of soundings, and the guild council has noticed his boat.
