## Overview

The colorful buildings and open-air theaters of the **Painted Harbor** line Bazûnel's waterfront, and a ship coming in sees the city's character before she ties up. Bazûnel is a city of 150,000 on the southern coast of [[place-taruzelrgn|Tarûzel]] and the cultural heart of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Tarûzelian States]]: smaller and less wealthy than [[place-besikar2|Besîkar]], less martial than [[place-yamuzel2|Yamûzel]] and less cosmopolitan than [[place-qudumel2|Qudûmel]], and unlike all three in the creative energy of its scholars, artists, shipwrights and dreamers. Tarûzelian art, music, theater and literature flourish here, and the Confederation's finest ships are designed here, if not always built here.

## The Yards and the Academy

The **Design Yards** are where Bazûnel's ships are conceived. The shipwright guilds work more like artists' studios than industrial workshops, the designers are counted artists as much as engineers, and a vessel drawn in Bazûnel is recognizable by its elegant lines and inventive rigging wherever she was built.

The **Academy of the Tides** teaches navigation, astronomy and natural philosophy, and its character is practical: it turns out good navigators, cartographers and physicians, and the best astronomers in the Confederation. Its charts are prized by captains across the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]].

## Who Governs

A council of merchant-princes governs Bazûnel, and it is the least guild-dominated of the major Tarûzelian cities. The [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]] and the other great guilds keep agents here, but the council is more balanced, with real representation from the craftsmen's guilds, the academies and the old noble families. That makes the city a natural haven for people who have fallen afoul of guild politics elsewhere: artists, free-thinkers and the occasional fugitive.

## Money

Bazûnel uses the Tarûzelian currency, backed by Tarûzelian reserves, and the Vylarian script is not honored here. See [[lore-trzlncrncy|Tarûzelian Currency]].
