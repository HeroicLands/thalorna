## Overview

The colorful buildings and open-air theaters of the [Painted Harbor]{.name data-type="place/site"} line Bazunel's waterfront, and a ship coming in sees the city's character before she ties up. Bazunel is a city of 150,000 on the southern coast of [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel]] and the cultural heart of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]]: smaller and less wealthy than [[place-besikar2|Besikar]], less martial than [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]] and less cosmopolitan than [[place-qudumel2|Kudumel]], and unlike all three in the creative energy of its scholars, artists, shipwrights and dreamers. Taruzelian art, music, theater and literature flourish here, and the Confederation's finest ships are designed here, if not always built here.

## The Yards and the Academy

The [Design Yards]{.name data-type="place/site"} are where Bazunel's ships are conceived. The shipwright guilds work more like artists' studios than industrial workshops, the designers are counted artists as much as engineers, and a vessel drawn in Bazunel is recognizable by its elegant lines and inventive rigging wherever she was built.

The [Academy of the Tides]{.name data-type="affiliation"} teaches navigation, astronomy and natural philosophy, and its character is practical: it turns out good navigators, cartographers and physicians, and the best astronomers in the Confederation. Its charts are prized by captains across the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]].

## Who Governs

A council of merchant-princes governs Bazunel, and it is the least guild-dominated of the major Taruzelian cities. The [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]] and the other great guilds keep agents here, but the council is more balanced, with real representation from the craftsmen's guilds, the academies and the old noble families. That makes the city a natural haven for people who have fallen afoul of guild politics elsewhere: artists, free-thinkers and the occasional fugitive.

## Money

Bazunel uses the [[lore-trzlncrncy|Taruzelian currency]], backed by Taruzelian reserves, and the Vylarian script is not honored here. See [[lore-trzlncrncy|Taruzelian Currency]].
