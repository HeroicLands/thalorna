On the first morning of a session, before the doors of the [[place-grandcnclhall|Grand Council Hall]] in [[place-besikar2|Besikar]] are opened to the public, a clerk of the treasury stands at the lectern with a ledger and reads five figures aloud. They are the weights of the five cities: the bullion and deposits that each city's houses hold with the [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]], as the treasury's register gives them that morning. The twenty-five councilors write the figures down. Everything else the session does is counted against them.

The Grand Council is the federal council of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]], and its charter makes every member city equal. Each of [[affiliation-besikar|Besikar]], [[affiliation-yamuzel|Yamuzel]], [[affiliation-qudumel|Kudumel]], [[affiliation-bazunel|Bazunel]] and [[affiliation-sazumun|Sazumun]] sends five councilors, and each councilor has one vote. The same charter counts every vote twice.

## The Two Counts

A measure before the Council passes on two counts at once. The first is the count of heads: thirteen of the twenty-five councilors. The second is the count of weight: each councilor carries one fifth of his city's weight, and a measure needs more than half the weight of the whole Council. Besikar's weight is nearly half of the whole by itself, so a measure Besikar opposes cannot pass, and a measure Besikar wants passes with the weight of any one other city behind it. The heads decide only what Besikar does not care about.

A councilor is named by the council of his own city and votes as that council instructs him, and the councils of the cities are seated by the standing of their houses. So the votes in the Grand Council Hall are the votes of the great houses, counted by what they hold. A clerk of the Council puts it to every new arbitrator the same way: "Every city has an equal voice in the Council. Count the votes by property and see whose voice carries."

## What the Council Decides

The Council sits twice a year, at the opening and at the close of the sailing season, and between sessions its acts are kept by the Clerk and its seal by the Arch-Consul. It decides what the cities must decide together: war and peace, treaties with the realms around the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]], the tariffs one member city may lay on another's goods, and the charter by which the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]] serves as the Confederation's navy, which the Council threatens to revoke whenever the League's captains read it too widely. It is the patron of the [[affiliation-vezakuni|Vezakuni]], it confirms the Kubasum who keeps the Merazuni, and it authorizes every addition to the treasury's reserves.

The first business of every session is the debt. The Confederation owes the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]] for the war, and the interest on the loan is read and voted before any other measure. It always passes.

## The Arch-Consul

The office of Arch-Consul passes each year to the next city in the order the cities signed the charter, Besikar, Yamuzel, Kudumel, Bazunel and Sazumun, and the city whose year it is names one of its councilors to hold it. The Arch-Consul presides, holds the Confederation's seal, receives envoys and signs the Council's acts, and for the year he keeps a residence in Besikar beside the Hall. He has no more vote than any other councilor, and he cannot make a measure pass.

What he can do is set the order of business. A session lasts as long as the councilors will stay, and a measure read on the last afternoon is a measure that is not counted. The Arch-Consul decides which measures are read first and which are read last.

This year's Arch-Consul is Danashu Kumuni of Kudumel, a round, soft-spoken coffee merchant in his sixties who keeps the most generous table the residence has seen and greets every petitioner as an old friend. Kudumel's council named him on the votes of the [[affiliation-balinuni|Balinuni]], the caravan house of the city, and he owes them the office.

## Petitions

Any freeman of a member city may petition the Council through his city's councilors, and the Clerk reads every petition that a councilor sponsors. The veterans of the war petition at every session for the land, the guild membership, the pensions and the voice they were promised, and a councilor of Yamuzel sponsors the petition every time. It is read on the last afternoon, every time.

:::secret
**For the GM:** The weight of every count comes from the Merazuni's register, and the Reckoner of the Weight is a treasury clerk seconded by the Kubasum. Nobody outside the treasury checks the figures he reads. [[being-yshdyjkdn|Yushuduya Jakuduni]], the Kubasum, has never been known to bend them, and the houses that would like the treasury in their own hands, the [[affiliation-qizikuni|Qizikuni]] of Besikar first among them, know that whoever keeps the Merazuni's register also keeps the Council's scales.

Danashu Kumuni wants to be remembered for something. A party that brings him a reason to read the veterans' petition first, or the hill tribes' grievance against the Balinuni, may find him willing. If the Balinuni learn of it before the session opens, they will call in what he owes them.
:::

## What It Is Fighting About {#present}

The summer session is a fight over its order of business, and [[being-dnshkmn|Danashu Kumuni]] holds the list. It opens a few weeks after the [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]], with the debt's interest read first as always, and every party that matters wants the next place:

- **The veterans' petition**, which [[being-zlmrzzn|Zilmar Ezzinu]] of the Besikar halls brings to the residence before every session and a councilor of Yamuzel sponsors. It has been read on the last afternoon of every session since the outlawing, and the halls are talking of a rising.
- **The toll.** [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]] wants a League toll on every hull through the narrows, collected by League galleys, and the League's charter is on the order beside it.
- **The audit.** [[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]] wants the Council to order an audit of the [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]]'s strongroom, and has a candidate of her own for [[being-yshdyjkdn|Yushuduya Jakuduni]]'s chair.
- **The indemnity.** Viléria refuses the war indemnity, and the Council's claim to it stands in the petitions again.

Behind the list the houses talk of something larger. Tirmana Qizikuni has sounded the heads of the other four houses on council seats that pass from a head to an heir. Nobody has put it on the order.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The petition's friends.** [[being-zlmrzzn|Zilmar Ezzinu]] wants a party with standing in Besikar to stand behind him when he brings the veterans' petition to the residence.
- **The visitors from Yamuzel.** [[being-llmnblnn|Ellamuna Balinuni]] wants to know what Danashu's late-evening visitors from Yamuzel asked him and what he answered.
- **The closed roll.** [[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]] sends a party with sealed letters to the heads of two other houses, and each head asks the party what the others answered.
