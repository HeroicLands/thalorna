## Overview

A traveler sailing east through the islands of the [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]] notices the walls change first. The pale stone of the western harbor cities gives way in Kudumel to mudbrick and tile, the roofs are flat and crowded with cisterns, and the bazaars smell of desert spice and camel. Kudumel, a city of about 220,000 on the eastern shore of [[place-nishurel|Nishurel]], is the easternmost of the major cities of Taruzel and the seat of the city-state of [[affiliation-qudumel|Kudumel]] in the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]]. Its name is its trade: _kudum-_, to come, with _-el_, the ground of it, the ground of arrival. The caravan roads of the [[place-dunharargn|Dunhara Region]] and the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn Desert Region]] reach the sea on the Amradi shore forty miles across the strait, and their goods come over to Kudumel on the ferries.

## Arriving

Most of Kudumel's visitors arrive from the east, on the broad-beamed ferries that cross from the Amradi coast in a day with a fair wind, and they land at the [[place-caravangate|Caravan Gate]]. The ferries come in low in the water and loud with camels. A clerk of the [[affiliation-balinuni|Balinuni]], the house that keeps them, walks down the bales with a tally-board before anything is unloaded. The animals go down stone ramps into the watering pens, the goods go to the weigh-house, the people go to the dues hall, and from the dues hall they pass under the great arch of the Gate into the eastern market.

Hulls from the western cities come into the harbor north of the Gate, a smaller basin behind a mole, where the Harbormaster's clerks deal with them in the ordinary Taruzelian way. A party crossing Nishurel from the fishing harbors of the western shore comes in by the hill roads in two days, with a hired guide, and enters by the west gate, where the hill tribes' herds come down to the city's wells.

In the last weeks of summer the east wind comes off the mainland hot and full of grit, the sky goes yellow, and the Harbormaster closes the quays at noon. A ship that arrives in the afternoon of a yellow day lies in the roads until morning.

## The Market and the Caravansaries

The eastern market fills the square inside the Gate and the streets that open off it, and it is the greatest market for eastern goods in the islands. Spice, dates, salt, dyes, horses, camels, carpets, copper and the cut stones of the desert are sold there by the bale and by the handful, under awnings, from the backs of animals and from the doorways of the houses. A sale is settled in [[lore-trzlncrncy|Taruzelian Currency]] at the money-changers' benches along the Gate's inner wall, and a price is settled over tea first: every bargain of size has its _pazirum_ (go-between), who keeps the tea and the peace and takes a fee from both sides.

South of the market lies the [Caravansary Quarter]{.name data-type="place/site"}, built to serve the caravan trade: courtyard hostels two floors high round a well, with stables below and lodgings above, warehouses with barred doors, farriers, saddlers, water-sellers and cookshops. Each caravansary keeps to one road and one people. The Dunhari trains lodge in the courts nearest the Gate, the Khazryn caravan masters in the long courts along the southern wall, and the factors who pool goods for the desert trails in the warehouses between. The Balinuni keep the largest warehouses of the quarter and lend from a counting house above them.

The hedge practitioners of the eastern traditions work in the back courts of the caravansaries, openly: fire-priests who read the flame at sunrise and sunset, sand-diviners who cast for a caravan's road, and teachers of the meditations brought from the [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara Region]]. A caravan master consults one before a crossing as a matter of course. The chapter of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Order]] (Thordimalon) in Kudumel is small, keeps a house near the harbor, and has given up asking the council to close the back courts.

## Who Lives Here

Kudumel is the most mixed city of the Confederation. Its merchant houses are Taruzelian, and they live on the hill above the harbor in courtyard houses with blank walls to the street. The Dunhari who have settled into city life hold the streets west of the market, where their families keep the old tent-cloth hangings over their doors. The Khazryn caravan masters live in the city as Residents, under its protection and without its freedom, and keep to the southern courts between crossings. Kaliharan factors and sailors work out of the harbor, and the caravan guard [[being-tvnrthr|Tavonei Reithuri]] is one of the Kaliharans who has made the city home. Now and then traders from as far as Vedyara and the [[place-byzariargn|Bizaria Region]] come through, and their goods sell for the highest prices in the market.

The faith of the city is the faith of [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|the Twelve of the Flame]] (the Ashaian Pantheon): every house keeps the fire-rite at sunrise and sunset, and the fire-shrines stand on the hills behind the city. The temples of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] stand too, nearer the harbor, and in the [Temple of Two Fires]{.name data-type="place/structure"} on the market's north side the rites of both traditions are practiced under one roof.

When the council sits, the hill tribes' councilors come down to the city with their households and camp outside the west gate by the wells. They do not lodge inside the walls.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order

Kudumel is governed by a council of its merchants with seats kept for the hill tribes. A First of the Council presides and holds the seal for his term. The Tribal Councilors sit beside the merchants for peoples who do not recognize guild authority, and need not live in the city. A Treasurer keeps the customs and tolls of the sea trade and of the caravans landed at the Gate. The Warden of the Weights inspects the measures, the coin and the quality of goods in the markets, in whatever language the bargain is struck, and his measurers, the _ashurum_, weigh every bale that passes the Gate. The great guilds keep houses in Kudumel and hold less sway here than anywhere in the Confederation. The council answers to the city's own merchant networks and to the tribes as much as to them.

The guilds' enforcers are not welcome in Kudumel, because the caravan peoples will not deal with them, and debts are collected in the city by other means. A caravan master who owes the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]] owes the Balinuni, who keep the Compact's counting house in the city, and a partner who will not pay finds the ferries full, the warehouses full and every other partner at the Gate informed. The council's watchmen keep the gates, the market and the harbor. A shopkeeper robbed in the night goes to the watch at the nearest gate, and a merchant cheated in a bargain goes to the Warden of the Weights, who hears complaints of short measure at the weigh-house door every morning. On the hill roads the tribes keep their own wells and their own law.

The present Arch-Consul of the Confederation, [[being-dnshkmn|Danashu Kumuni]], is a coffee merchant of Kudumel, named to the office by the city's council on the votes of the Balinuni. He keeps his residence in [[place-besikar2|Besikar]] for the year, and his warehouse on the market is kept by his sons.

## Where a Party Lodges

A party that comes in with a caravan lodges in its caravansary, eats at the common fire and pays for its animals' water by the skin. The harbor inns take western travelers and charge western prices. A party with a letter to a merchant house lodges behind that house's blank wall, where the courtyard is cool, the coffee is the best in the city and the house's guests are not expected in the Caravansary Quarter after dark.

## Hooks

- **The cheap goods.** Someone is selling caravan goods in Kudumel's bazaars under the price of goods that paid the Gate's dues, and the Gate's takings are down. A merchant losing sales to them hires a party to find where the goods come from.
- **The cast.** A sand-diviner in the back courts has refused, three caravans running, to cast for any train bound by the southern trail, and the caravan masters want to know what he has seen. The Thordimalon's chapter would like to know who taught him.
- **The councilor's camp.** A Tribal Councilor has not come down to the session, and his household has camped by the west gate without him.

# The Truth of Kudumel {#truth .secret}

**The underground is three circles, and they talk.** The fire-priests, the sand-diviners and the meditation teachers of the back courts each keep a circle of pupils, and the three circles meet. Once a month, on the night of the new moon, their senior members share a meal in a caravansary near the southern wall whose keeper is a fire-priest's brother, and trade pupils, warnings and what each has learned of the Thordimalon. Some of the hill guides who take caravans across Nishurel are pupils of the sand-diviners. The Thordimalon's chapter pays an informer in the Caravansary Quarter for the names of anyone teaching a practice the Order would claim. The informer is a pupil of the fire-priests, and the names he sells are the names the circles give him.

**The diviner's refusal** is no casting. The southern trail's wells are held this season by a band that takes the trains that pass them, and the diviner's nephew rode with the last train that went that way and came back alone. The diviner will not cast for a road that took his kin, and he will not say why to a Taruzelian.

**The cheap goods** come over the strait by night to Nishurel's north beaches and go round the Gate; the truth of it, and of the two councilors who take a share, is in the note on [[place-nishurel|Nishurel]]. The missing Tribal Councilor has gone up into the hills to learn what his people's guides are paid by the bale.
