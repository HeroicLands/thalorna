A traveler sailing east through the Taruzelian islands notices the walls change first: the Vylarian stone of the harbor cities gives way in Kudumel to mudbrick and tile, and the bazaars smell of desert spice and camel. Kudumel, a city of about 220,000, is the easternmost of the [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]]'s major cities and the seat of the city-state of [[affiliation-qudumel|Kudumel]] in the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]]. It stands on the island nearest the mainland, at the crossroads where the sea routes meet the overland caravan trails from the [[place-dunharargn|Dunhara Region]] and the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn Desert Region]], whose goods cross the strait from the Amradi shore.

## The Gate and the Caravansaries

The overland trade comes over the strait on the ferries and lands at the [Caravan Gate]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the great eastern market, and spreads from there into the [Caravansary Quarter]{.name data-type="place/site"}, a vast complex of hostels, stables and warehouses built to serve it. A caravan master who has crossed the desert brings his goods and animals over from the Amradi shore, unloads, waters his animals and sells in the same quarter, and an island merchant who wants eastern goods first-hand goes to the Gate to meet it.

## Two Fires

Kudumel feels different from the other port cities, and its faith is the clearest sign: the dominant religion here is the faith of the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]], rather than the faiths of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. In the [Temple of Two Fires]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, a syncretic shrine, the rites of both traditions are practiced side by side under one roof.
