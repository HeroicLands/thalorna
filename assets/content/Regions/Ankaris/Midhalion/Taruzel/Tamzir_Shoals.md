## Overview

The Tamzir Shoals lie in the channels of the **Taruzelian archipelago**, the islands of [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]] that lie across a strait from the Amradi coast, and every pilot on the coast gives a newcomer the same first warning about them: do not take a deep-keeled ship into those waters without someone aboard who knows them. The Shoals are a maze of sandbars, submerged rocks and hard currents in the channels of [[place-zupumun|Zupumun]], filling the water between the western shore of [[place-khamisar|Khamisar]] and the eastern coast of [[place-visizel|Visizel]], some twelve miles from north to south and eight across. A ship that does not know the channels finds the sand.

## The Water

From the cliffs of Khamisar's western shore the Shoals look like a calm sea with its colors laid out on it: pale green over sand, brown where weed grows on the rocks, dark blue in the channels, and white in long lines where the swell breaks on a bar. At low water the bars come up out of the sea as banks of white sand, some of them a mile long, and the birds of the whole archipelago stand on them in thousands. At high water most of them vanish, and a boat can sail over a bar it walked on six hours before.

The channels between the bars are deep and narrow, and they move. A winter storm can close a channel that was open in the autumn and cut a new one a cable away, so a pilot learns the Shoals again every spring, by sounding them from a small boat. The tide runs through them hard, faster on the ebb than on the flood, and a ship that loses steerage way in a channel is carried onto the sand on whichever side the current chooses. Yamuzel's watch-boats patrol the edges of the Shoals from the marine posts on Khamisar, and few of them go far in.

## The Battle of Tamzir Shoals

The pilots' warning won Taruzel its independence. In the early summer of 708 a Vylarian squadron, the largest the empire had ever gathered in the eastern sea, followed the war's flotilla into the Shoals to break the blockade of the island channels. The deep-drafted imperial warships took the sand on the falling tide, the lighter Taruzelian boats worked the channels around them like wolves around elk, and in two days and a night the squadron was burned, captured or wrecked, and its admiral killed.

**[[lore-tamzirbattle|The Battle of Tamzir Shoals]]** broke the empire's power in Taruzelian waters. Its land garrisons, cut off from supply, fell one by one, and Vylaria recognized Taruzelian sovereignty within the year.

## The Wrecks

Most of the squadron is still in the Shoals. At low water on a spring tide the stern-posts and ribs of a dozen warships show above the sand along the main channel, black and furred with weed, and on a calm day a boat over the deeper channels can see more of them below: long hulls lying on their sides, oar-ports open, half buried in the bottom. The divers of the Shoals bring up their nails, chain, anchors and bronze fittings, and sell the metal by weight across the strait.

The rams are still on the wrecks. Each of the squadron's heavy warships carried a ram at her bow, a beak of cast bronze longer than a man and heavier than a cart can carry, and only a few have ever been seen above water. They are the most valuable thing in the Shoals, and three parties want them:

- **The admirals of Yamuzel** want a column of them raised at the Strait Fortress, as the empire raises the rams of its enemies on the columns of the [Paldor]{.name data-type="place/feature"} in [[place-orzara|Orzara City]].
- **The empire** wants them home. Its factors in the eastern harbors say that the navy pays well for any ram returned to [[place-castamar|Kastamar]], where the names of the drowned are cut on the mole.
- **The [[affiliation-shoalsbrthd|Shoals Brotherhood]]**, which lives among the wrecks, says the rams belong to the Shoals, and its code forbids any Brother to sell one.

## Sulimar

At the heart of the Shoals, out of sight of Khamisar and Visizel, a ring of sandbars encloses a lagoon of still water about a mile across, with a low island of dunes in its middle. This is [Sulimar]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the quiet place (_sulim-_, to be quiet, with _-ar_, where the thing is kept), and it is the home of the Shoals Brotherhood. Huts of driftwood and ship's timber stand on the island, boats lie drawn up on its beaches, and nets dry on poles cut from the masts of imperial warships. The way in is a gap in the ring that moves with the storms, and only the Brotherhood's pilots know where it is this year.

## What the Shoals Mean Now

The battle is the founding myth of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]]. [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]], whose yards built the fleet, holds an annual [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]] in its memory, and [[place-besikar2|Besikar]] keeps a captured Vylarian warship in dry-dock as **the [[place-tamzirmonument|Tamzir Monument]]**. The [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]] grew out of the captains who fought there. The veterans remember the Shoals differently: the dock workers, fishermen and tribal warriors who sailed into the imperial fleet were promised land, guild membership and a voice in the new government, and received a discharge and a small purse of silver instead. Their children have inherited the grievance along with the story. One trading vessel on the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]], [[affiliation-thetamzir|the Tamzir]], carries the battle's name.

## Converging Interests {#present}

Three parties want the rams, and the water keeps them. The admirals of Yamuzel want a column of them at the Strait Fortress, the empire's factors in the eastern harbors offer silver for any ram returned to Kastamar, and the [[affiliation-shoalsbrthd|Shoals Brotherhood]] holds that the rams belong to the Shoals. The League's patrols hunt the channels round the Shoals and follow no boat inside them, and some League masters hire the Brotherhood's pilots on the quiet. The [[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captains]] lie in Sulimar when the League's galleys are out, and the halls of Yamuzel that supply the lagoon talk of a rising. The [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]] keeps the battle's anniversary in the narrows, and the veterans' boats that close its line were the boats that fought here.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The factor's offer.** A Vylarian factor in the eastern harbors offers a party good silver to bring him word of any ram raised from the Shoals, and the admirals of Yamuzel offer the same party more to bring the ram to them.
- **The pilot.** A League master bound through the channels west of Khamisar wants a Brotherhood pilot without his owners hearing of it, and asks a party to meet the boat that comes out at dusk.

# The Truth of the Shoals {#truth .secret}

**The admiral's ship is in Sulimar.** The imperial flagship did not burn and was not taken. On the second day of the battle she was driven through the gap in the ring on the morning flood, holed by a fishing boat's ram, and she sank in the lagoon in four fathoms of still water, where she lies upright with her ram and her stern-post standing clear of the mud. The Brotherhood's divers have been in her many times. They left the admiral's bones on her deck, where he fell, and brought up his sword, which the Elders keep, and the squadron's pay chest, which paid for the Brotherhood's first boats. Only the Brotherhood knows where she is, and the veterans who say the warship at Besikar was the flagship have never asked it.

**The rams are counted.** The Brotherhood has raised a few of the squadron's rams over the years and sunk them again in the shallows of Sulimar, where its Keeper of the Lots can count them from a boat. The rest are still on the wrecks. Any ram raised and taken out of the Shoals would be known for what it is in any harbor of the sea, because each carries the cast badge of the ship that bore it, and the navy at Kastamar keeps the list of badges.
