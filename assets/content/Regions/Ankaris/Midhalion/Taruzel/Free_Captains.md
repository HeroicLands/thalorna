A ship in the island channels after dark that shows two lanterns at her masthead, one above the other, is asking for a friend, and she is answered the same way. The signal is the war's: the flotilla used it to find its own boats among the islands at night, and the Free Captains still use it, because every one of them sailed in the flotilla or learned the trade from someone who did. The Free Captains are the outlawed masters of [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]], about thirty hulls and nine hundred men and women, who sail against the cargoes of the guilds that outlawed them. Nobody has counted them exactly, the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]] included.

## Who They Are

The first Free Captains were the masters of the war's flotilla who would not be bought off or quieted after the peace, and whom the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]] declared outlaws on charges of piracy ([[lore-taruzelbetrayal|The Betrayal of the Revolution]]). They took their hulls with them: armed traders, a few of the merchant warships, and the fast coasters the flotilla had used as scouts. Since then others have joined them, captains ruined by the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]]'s calls on their debts, captains struck from the League, and the sons and daughters of the first ones, who were born to the work. About half the hulls are commanded by women.

Each captain owns her hull, or owes for it, and commands it as she likes. The Free Captains have no chief, no charter and no common treasury. When a matter touches them all, a League squadron in the channels, a captain taken, a cargo too large for one hull, the captains who can be reached meet aboard one ship in some cove, choose a Speaker for the night, and decide by voice. A captain who disagrees sails home and is not bound.

## What They Do

The Free Captains take cargoes, and they choose them. The Compact's cargoes come first, then those of the houses that sit in the Grand Council, then any ship sailing under the League's protection. They let fishing boats, island ferries and small traders go, and a master who shows the papers of a veterans' hall is let go with her hold untouched. A cargo taken is sold across the strait on the Amradi shore, or in harbors where nobody asks for a Taruzelian seal, and the crew shares the price.

They are also the islands' best smugglers, because they have nothing to lose by it. A Free Captain carries what the guilds tax most, to the cities whose laws disagree, and carries people the guilds want: debtors, witnesses, runaway bondsmen sent down to the boats by the [[affiliation-unchained|Unchained]].

## The Receipts

During the war the captains of the flotilla stored their prize cargoes in the bonded warehouses of the [[affiliation-hitusuni|Hitusuni]] of [[place-sazumun2|Sazumun]], under the house's seal, to be sold when the war was won. When the captains were outlawed, the house kept the cargoes. The Free Captains still hold the house's receipts, a bundle of sealed papers that the Keeper of the Receipts carries aboard her own ship and never ashore, and they regard them as the one honest account of the war anyone gave them. They have never presented one. A receipt presented in an outlaw's hand would be taken from her with the outlaw.

## The Banker

The Free Captains borrow, as every Taruzelian captain does. Their lender is [Yazila Ilammuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who keeps a factor's counting-room in [[place-pashinar|Pashinar]], above the ferry quays where the caravan goods cross from the mainland. Her husband's house fitted out three hulls of the flotilla at its own cost and was ruined when the Compact called the loans it had taken to do it, and she lends the Captains money against their next takings at a rate no guild lender would offer an outlaw. She sells their cargoes to the caravan factors, and her ledger is the nearest thing the Free Captains have to a treasury.

## Relations

- **The [[affiliation-shoalsbrthd|Shoals Brotherhood]]** pilots their hulls through the [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]] and shelters them in its lagoon. The Captains pay for both with a share of what they take.
- **The Unchained** find them crews, surgeons and a bed ashore in the veterans' quarters of the port cities.
- **The Corsair League** hunts them, and some of its captains warn them first. Several League admirals would sooner hire them than hunt them, and have said so to the Grand Council.
- **The Hitusuni** owe them what the receipts say, and know it.
- **[[affiliation-thetamzir|The Tamzir]]** sails under no flag. The Free Captains count her captain one of theirs, and he declines the honor.

## Meeting the Free Captains

A party aboard a merchantman taken by a Free Captain is asked whose cargo it is, and the answer decides the rest of the day. A party with something to carry that the guilds would stop is told, in any veterans' hall, which cove to wait in and which night to show two lanterns. A party with a grievance against the Compact can find a Free Captain willing to listen, for a price, and a Free Captain who offers a fair price for a manifest the League would kill to keep is offering the party a share of a war.

## The Present {#present}

The Free Captains are closer to the halls ashore this year than at any time since the outlawing, and the halls are talking of a rising. The captains would be on the water for it, and they have no more chosen a leader for it than the halls have. They count [[being-zbzmjkdn|Zabuzum Jakuduni]] one of theirs, and he still declines the honor. The [[affiliation-hitusuni|Hitusuni]] still hold the war's prize cargoes against the receipts that [[being-nzzntrshn|Nazzuna Tarshuni]] carries on her own hull. The League's sweeps of the outer channels go on, and the warnings that used to come before them have stopped coming.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The letter ashore.** [[being-nzzntrshn|Nazzuna Tarshuni]] takes a Compact merchantman with a party aboard, and offers it passage to any cove it likes in exchange for carrying a letter to a veterans' hall in Yamuzel.
- **The silent fishing boat.** The fishing boat that warns the coves before a League sweep has not come this season. Nazzuna wants to know what its crew has told the marines at Yamuzel.

# The Truth of the Free Captains {#truth .secret}

**The ledger is a warrant.** Yazila Ilammuni's ledger is her husband's, and it begins in the war. Among its first pages is a copy, under the seal of the rebels' council, of the promise that council made the flotilla's captains: the prize money of every imperial ship and cargo taken in the war, to be paid by the Confederation once it stood. Six warships were taken at the Shoals alone. Nothing was ever paid, and the councilors who sealed the promise sit in the Grand Council now. The Compact has learned that a copy exists and has not learned where it is, and its enforcers have begun to call on factors in Pashinar.

The ledger and the receipts together are a claim on the Confederation's treasury by the men it outlawed, signed by the men who outlawed them. Presented to the Grand Council by a freeman, they would be read; presented by a Free Captain, they would be burned with her. A party that carries them to Besikar on the Captains' behalf will be the most wanted party in Taruzel before the session opens.
