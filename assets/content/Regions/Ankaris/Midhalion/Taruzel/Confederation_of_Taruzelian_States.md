Pinned to the wall of every veterans' hall on the Taruzelian coast is a broadsheet gone brown at the edges: a broken chain, a rising sun, a ship under a Taruzelian flag, and the words _we bleed gold for an empire that gives us nothing but chains_. The guilds printed it twelve years ago to raise a war. The men who keep it on the wall now read it as a promise the guilds made and did not keep, and that reading is the shortest true account of the Confederation of Taruzelian States.

The Confederation is a league of five merchant city-states on the islands of an archipelago in the eastern [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]], off the coast of the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]. It is the leading naval power of the eastern sea, it is rich, and its politics are as cutthroat as they ever were under imperial rule. Roughly twelve years ago Taruzel fought and won a war of independence from the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]; the merchant oligarchs who paid for that war now hold the freedom its veterans bled for. The island shores are crowded with wealthy trading cities, and the hill country of the larger islands belongs to tribal communities and ancient ruins.

## The Islands

[[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel]] is an archipelago of half a dozen islands in the eastern Vylarian Sea, lying forty to eighty miles off the Amradi coast, and it holds no shore on the mainland. The islands' coastlines are deeply indented, and their natural harbors have made Taruzel the premier maritime region of the western part of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]]. On the larger islands the ground rises from narrow coastal plains through rolling hills to arid scrub. The climate is warm and dry, and the hot summers are tempered by the sea breeze. Across the strait to the east, the caravan roads of the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn Desert Region]] and the [[place-dunharargn|Dunhara Region]] reach the sea on the Amradi shore, and their goods cross to the islands by ship.

The channels between the islands are the other half of the country. Whoever holds them controls the trade routes of the eastern sea, and the Confederation holds them: the islands carry its naval bases and its trading entrepôts, and, for those who know the passages, its smuggling havens.

## History

Taruzel was a Vylarian province for centuries, among the first lands the [Vylarian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} absorbed and by the end the empire's wealthiest in the east. Its merchant princes grew rich as the empire's intermediaries in the eastern trade, and when the imperial court answered their wealth with tariffs, harbor fees and harder _therkoi_ (governors), and gave them no seat in [[affiliation-seldan|The Vylarian Senate]] (Seldan), they paid for a revolution.

The war began in 707 with broadsheets printed at guild expense, and it went badly on land: Besikar was besieged and the militias were routed on the coastal plains. the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]]'s gold kept the rebellion solvent, the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]] helped across the strait, and a flotilla of armed traders and fishing boats closed the island channels to imperial shipping ([[lore-taruzelwar|The Taruzelian War of Independence]]). In the early summer of 708 the flotilla destroyed the empire's largest squadron at the [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]], the island garrisons fell within months, and Vylaria recognized the Confederation within the year ([[lore-tamzirbattle|The Battle of Tamzir Shoals]]).

The merchant guilds wrote the Confederation's charter for themselves. The veterans were discharged with a small purse of silver in place of the land, guild places, pensions and voice they had been promised, the Corsair League was taken over by the shipowners, and the captains and militia leaders who would not be quiet were declared outlaws. Within two years of independence the Confederation was an oligarchy in all but name, and the outlaws had taken to the channels as the [[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captains]] and the [[affiliation-shoalsbrthd|Shoals Brotherhood]], with the [[affiliation-unchained|Unchained]] ashore ([[lore-taruzelbetrayal|The Betrayal of the Revolution]]).

## The Taruzel of Today

The undercurrent of rebellion has never gone away. It rises—a harbor strike in Yamuzel, a tax riot in Besikar's dock districts, a tribal raid on a guild caravan—and each time it is put down hard. The guilds learned from the empire's mistakes and send no legions, because legions make martyrs. They use economic pressure instead (revoked licenses, called-in debts, blacklisted families), quiet enforcement by guild enforcers, and the steady weight of an economy built to keep the powerless where they are.

The anger accumulates. The veterans of the Shoals are growing old, and their children have inherited the betrayal. The dock workers and fishermen know what was promised and what was delivered, and the hill tribes remember everything. In the taverns, the veterans' halls and the hidden anchorages of the archipelago, people still pass around the old broadsheets and read them with a bitterness their authors never intended.

So the Confederation stands free, prosperous and seething. The merchant guilds rule through money and the institutions they control. The veterans and their children nurse the betrayal, the outlawed captains raid from the island channels, and the hill tribes trust no one. Everywhere—in the dock districts, the fishing villages and the tribal camps—people remember the words that recruited them and wait for someone to mean them.

Into this sails [[affiliation-thetamzir|the Tamzir]]. Her captain fought at the Shoals, believed the promises and watched the merchant princes take the victory. He now runs cargo on the margins of a Confederation that would rather he did not exist: too famous to ignore, too dangerous to tolerate, and too stubborn to kill quietly. The guilds want him brought to heel, the veterans want him to raise the flag again, and he wants to keep his crew alive and his ship moving. Nobody involved expects that position to hold.

## Government

A clerk of the Grand Council puts the constitution to a new arbitrator in two sentences: "Every city has an equal voice in the Council. Count the votes by property and see whose voice carries." The [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]] is the federal council of the five cities. Each city-state sends five councilors, named by its own council, and the Council sits twice a year in the [[place-grandcnclhall|Grand Council Hall]] at Besikar. Every measure is counted twice: once by heads, and once by weight, which is each city's deposits with the treasury. Besikar's weight is nearly half of the whole, so nothing passes that Besikar opposes. The office of Arch-Consul passes each year to the next city in the order of the charter; it presides, holds the seal and sets the order of business, and this year it is held by [Danashu Kumuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Kudumel.

The Council is where the great houses vote, because the councils that name its members are seated by the standing of their houses. Each city has one house above the rest, and each of the five is bound to one of the three great guilds:

[[affiliation-qizikuni|Qizikuni]] of Besikar
: bankers and underwriters, the heaviest house of the Auric Compact and the first lenders of the war

[[affiliation-tanaduni|Tanaduni]] of Yamuzel
: an admirals' house of war-galleys and convoys in the Corsair League, under an admiral of the Shoals

[[affiliation-balinuni|Balinuni]] of Kudumel
: caravan partners and ferry-keepers, the Auric Compact's door into the city that will not deal with its enforcers

[[affiliation-rusakuni|Rusakuni]] of Bazunel
: ship designers whose lines every new League galley is built to

[[affiliation-hitusuni|Hitusuni]] of Sazumun
: keepers of the bonded warehouses, and the Merchantry of the Veil's house on the quays

The Confederation's treasury and mint are the [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]], kept by the Kubasum of the [[affiliation-vezakuni|Vezakuni]], and its register of deposits is what weighs the Council's votes. The real decisions are made before a session opens, in the back rooms of these houses and of the guilds they serve.

Each city-state keeps its own internal government, militia and laws, and the patchwork of regulations between them bewilders outsiders. Smuggling and legal arbitrage—profiting from the differences between one city's laws and another's—are thriving trades.

## Society

Taruzelian culture is mercantile at its core: wealth, reputation and the ability to strike a profitable deal are its highest virtues (see [[lore-taruzelianclt|Taruzelian]]). The great trading houses, sprawling family enterprises that combine banking, shipping, warehousing and political influence, are the true power in Taruzel. Their warehouses line every harbor, their agents work in every port of the Vylarian Sea, and their influence reaches into the courts of Vylaria, the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provenzia]] and the [[affiliation-ctysttshlns|City-States of Helionis]].

Society divides sharply between the guild elite and everyone else. The guild families live in palatial urban estates, patronize the arts and educate their children in rhetoric, mathematics and mercantile law. Below them a large middle class of independent merchants, skilled craftspeople and ship captains forms the economic backbone. At the bottom, dock workers, sailors and the urban poor live in cramped harbor districts and grow more restless every year. The tribal communities of the island hills stand apart from the port cities: they keep older traditions, speak archaic dialects and resent the coastal cities' dominance, and they supplied many of the foot soldiers of the war of independence and have seen little benefit from it.

Taruzelian cooking is famous across [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]], built on spiced meats, flatbreads, honeyed pastries and strong coffee.

## Religion

Taruzelian faith is syncretic. The faiths of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]], imposed in the Vylarian period, still dominate the coastal cities, where the merchant class favors [[affiliation-olvenia|Ólvenia]] for prosperity and [[affiliation-marendas|Marendas]] for knowledge, and sailors and naval officers honor [[affiliation-brazemas|Brazemas]] of the storms. The eastern states and the hill tribes keep strong ties to the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]], a tradition older than the Vylarian conquest; [[affiliation-ahurdaen|Ahurdaen]] (order and harmony), [[affiliation-korsadra|Khórsadra]] (knowledge and eternal light) and [[affiliation-aravesta|Aravesta]] (prosperity and hedonism) have large followings. In many Taruzelian cities the Aureldian and Ashaian temples stand side by side, and a single family often patronizes both.

## Magic

The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] kept a strong presence in Taruzel through the Vylarian period, and it is still active in the major cities, on worse terms. The merchant guilds regard it as a possible tool of Vylarian influence and have restricted its work. Some guild leaders would expel it outright; others see its uses and would rather co-opt it.

Independent practitioners—hedge mages, alchemists and fortune-tellers—are more common in Taruzel than in Vylaria proper, partly because the Arcane Order's grip is looser here. The Arcane Order resents it and keeps pressing for its monopoly to be enforced, sometimes using Vylarian imperial agents as proxies.

## Relations

Relations with Vylaria are officially correct and privately hostile. The empire has not forgiven the loss of Taruzel, and Vylarian agents, often working with the Thordimalon (the Arcane Order), operate in the Taruzelian cities, gathering intelligence and cultivating sympathizers. Taruzelian merchants, for their part, are pushing hard into markets the empire once dominated.

Helionis, to the north-west, is Taruzel's natural trading partner, and the two share cultural and intellectual ties. Au'Khelathu, to the south, is its most important long-distance partner, and that relationship runs deeper than commerce. Khelathi gold, weapons and military advisors were instrumental in the war of independence, a debt the merchant princes deny in public and honor in private, through preferential trade agreements, reduced harbor fees for Khelathi vessels and quiet diplomatic coordination that favors Khelathi interests over Vylarian ones. The surge in Taruzelian–Khelathi commerce since independence is one of the largest economic shifts on the Vylarian Sea: trade that once passed through Vylarian intermediaries now runs directly between Taruzelian and Khelathi ports, enriching both and further weakening the empire's commercial position. For Au'Khelathu the investment in Taruzelian independence has paid for itself many times over.

The Kingdom of Amradad holds the mainland across the strait, and relations with it are cordial: the Kingdom, long antagonistic to Vylaria, was among those who helped Taruzel win its independence. Beyond its shore, relations with the nomadic tribes of Dunhara are complicated: the desert peoples hold the overland trade routes the island cities depend on, which makes the relationship profitable to both and often tense.

## Commerce and Currency

The Confederation pays in the [[lore-trzlncrncy|Taruzelian currency]], the system it built after secession and administers through the Merazuni, the treasury of the [[affiliation-vezakuni|Vezakuni]]. Its denominations are the Vylarian ones (Solden, Ardin and Bit), but they are backed by Taruzelian reserves rather than by the Vylarian reserves of [[affiliation-tarmex|the Tarmeks Orzan]], and the Taruzelian Solden is slightly underweight by the Vylarian standard. A network of Merazuni-affiliated moneylenders carries the banking. Vylarian script is not honored in Taruzel, and Taruzelian script is not honored in Vylarian territory.
