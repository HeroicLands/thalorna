Below Hitûsel are the tanning pits, and the smell of them is the first thing a visitor learns about the place. Hitûsel is a fishing village of about 2,000 on the coast of the [[place-taruzelrgn|Tarûzel Region]], within [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]]. Its fishermen wear heavy leather jerkins against the wind, and the hideworker [[being-dpmnjkdn|Dapûman Jakûdûnî]] makes them the old way, in the pits his father worked before him; buyers come up from [[place-sazumun2|Sazûmun]] for his hides because the city tanneries' leather cracks in two winters.
