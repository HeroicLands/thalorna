The **Guild Board of Shamular** is the body of the guild families of [[place-shamular|Shamular]], the merchant town of [[place-milazun|Milazun]] in the [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]]. It meets in the guild hall on the market square, a cool stone building behind a garden of plane trees, and its business is the scent trade of the whole island: who may sell attar on the square, what a flask pays at the quay, and whether a seal is what it claims to be.

## The Members

Nine members sit on the board, elected every four years by the licensed houses of the town's trades. The perfumers hold three seats, the still-house factors two, the honey merchants two and the shipping houses two. One of the perfumers' seats goes with the oldest perfumer's license on the square, and the holder of that license sits on the board without an election: this term it is [[being-kdmyqbsn|Kadumuya Kubasuni]], who attends when she remembers. The First of the Board is [Lamiran Dammuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a smooth, unhurried moneylender of fifty-five who sits for the still-house factors, because his house lends to the still-houses against the coming harvest and is counted among them. The Warden of the Seals, [Meraza Latari]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, is an officer of the board and not a member of it, and she has no vote.

## What the Board Does

The board licenses the perfumers, the attar sellers and the honey merchants of the square, sets the dues at the quay by the flask and the jar, and keeps the town's register. It answers to [[affiliation-yamuzel|Yamuzel]] for the city-state's dues and sends them across to Khamisar twice a year. Through its Warden of the Seals it keeps an impression of every still-house's seal on the island, and the board's ruling on a broken or false seal is the ruling every buyer in the islands accepts: a seal-breaker it finds is put out of the town and struck from its register.
