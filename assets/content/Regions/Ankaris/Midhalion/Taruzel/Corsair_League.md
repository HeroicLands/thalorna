A Corsair League captain tells a newly made master about the [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]]: "Under the League's flag you can carry a sword on any quay in Taruzel and watch the customs house wave your cargo through. Out from under it, every harbor is slow to find you a berth, and some night a ship you have never seen will mistake you for a pirate." The Corsair League is Taruzel's maritime guild, and it blurs the line between legitimate shipping, naval power and outright piracy according to the political weather. The captains and admirals who won [[lore-tamzirbattle|The Battle of Tamzir Shoals]] founded it as a fraternity of war veterans; it is now a powerful shipping cartel that controls much of Taruzel's merchant marine and serves the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]] as its de facto navy.

## Members and Privileges

The League's members are ship captains, fleet owners and naval officers who count themselves the heirs of the independence fleet, and its identity is half military and half mercantile. It controls the convoy routes, negotiates with pirates or hunts them, and hires out its warships to the Confederation when naval force is needed. In exchange its members enjoy preferential access to harbor facilities, customs exemptions and the right to carry weapons in Taruzelian ports. Its roots are deepest in [[affiliation-yamuzel|Yamuzel]].

## From Brotherhood to Cartel

The League was founded in the months after the [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]] by the captains of the war's flotilla, who swore to keep their ships together in peace as they had in the war and to share the convoys and the prizes between them. Its first charter from the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]] gave it the escort of Taruzelian shipping and the privileges that still go with it. Within two years the shipowners who held the largest hulls had taken over its councils, and some of the captains who founded it voted with them. The League's members became fleet owners and the captains who sail for them, and the captains who would not accept the change were outlawed ([[lore-taruzelbetrayal|The Betrayal of the Revolution]]).

## The League and the Outlaws

The League's patrol galleys hunt the outlawed captains in the island channels, and the League collects the Compact's bounties on the ones it brings in. The hunting is uneven. The [[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captains]] sailed in the flotilla beside the League's own admirals, some League captains warn them before a sweep, and several admirals would sooner hire them than hunt them and have said so to the Grand Council. No League galley has ever followed the [[affiliation-shoalsbrthd|Shoals Brotherhood]] into the Tamzir Shoals, and League masters bound through the channels west of [[place-khamisar|Khamisar]] hire its pilots on the quiet.

## The Fleet Review

Every year on the fourteenth of Brazemandi the League's galleys lead the [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]] through the narrows under the walls of Yamuzel. Each captain calls the name of his galley and the number of her oars as she passes the Grand Council's councilors on the wall of the Strait Fortress, and at the end the League's admiral calls the total. The Review falls a few weeks before the Council's summer session, where the League's privileges are always on the order of business, and the count is the League's argument.

## The League and the Compact

The League and the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]] need each other and distrust each other. The Compact finances voyages and the League protects them. The League resents the Compact's financial dominance, and the Compact distrusts the League's military power and its romantic attachment to the war's revolutionary ideals.

## The Privateer Problem

The League's charter gives its captains broad authority to "protect Taruzelian commerce," and they read that language widely: it has covered attacking competitors' ships in disputed waters, seizing cargoes from vessels trading outside guild agreements, and occasional outright piracy against targets of opportunity. The [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]] periodically threatens to revoke these privileges, and the League reminds the Council each time that it is the only naval force standing between Taruzel and a [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian]] reconquest.

Independent operators such as [[affiliation-thetamzir|the Tamzir]] live in a gray zone. The League wants every Taruzelian-flagged vessel under its umbrella, and captains who refuse meet harassment, port restrictions and the occasional "mistaken" confrontation at sea.
