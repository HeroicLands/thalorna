## Overview

The Strait Fortress stands on the eastern cliffs of [[place-khamisar|Khamisar]] above the city of [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]], where the narrows between Khamisar and [[place-qalirel|Qalirel]] close to three miles, and no hull goes through the heart of the islands of the [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]] without passing under its walls. Vylarian engineers built it, and Taruzelian marines hold it. In a sailor's mouth "the strait" means these narrows, and the fortress gave the word that meaning ([[place-zupumun|Zupumun]]).

## What Is There

From the water the fortress is a wall of pale dressed stone rising straight out of the cliff, so that rock and masonry are hard to tell apart for the first fifty feet. Above them run two lines of wall, one inside the other, with eleven towers on the outer line, and the seaward towers are cut with wide, low embrasures for the engines that cover the channel. The great tower at the northern end, the keep, stands higher than the rest, and a beacon-basket on its roof answers the signal towers along the island's ridge. On a clear night the beacon of [[place-sharimun|Sharimun]] burns opposite it across the narrows.

The landward gate faces the admirals' street on the height of Yamuzel, and it is the only way in on foot. Inside the outer wall lie the marines' barracks, the cisterns cut in the rock, the stores and the engine-sheds. Inside the inner wall stand the keep, the Warden's house and the archive, a long vaulted room where the logbooks of the signal towers are brought at the end of each month and shelved with the fleet's muster rolls.

A sea stair, cut in the cliff and closed by an iron gate at each end, runs down from the outer wall to the guard-boat's landing at the cliff foot. The guard-boat goes out from it to meet every hull that enters the narrows.

## What It Does

The Warden of the Strait Fortress, an admiral named by the [Admiral's Council]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, commands it. Its work is the narrows. The guard-boat's officer takes the name, master, cargo and port of every hull entering them, and the fortress's clerks enter each in the day-book. A hull bound through the archipelago anchors in the roads below the walls to wait for a convoy of the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]], and the fortress decides when a convoy may go. A hull that runs the narrows without stopping is fired on from the seaward towers, and the marines say no hull has done it twice.

The fortress also keeps the island's watch. The signal towers on Khamisar's ridge pass word of a sail from the western channels to the keep faster than a ship can sail the distance, and every traveler they log ends up in the archive.

A party with business inside comes to the landward gate with a letter from an admirals' house or from the Warden's own office, and is walked to wherever it is going by a marine and walked out again.

## What Is Said of It

The marines say the fortress has never been stormed: its Vylarian garrison gave it up by treaty in the months after the Shoals, and the first marines to march in found its stores full and its engines oiled. The old men of the Veterans' Quarter say the imperial garrison left something behind besides its stores, and do not agree on what. The admirals' houses say the Vylarians who built it would like it back, and that they still have the drawings.

## The Present {#present}

The fortress is preparing for the [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]], when the councilors of the Grand Council will stand on its wall to take the salute. The Commander of the Marines has been told to catch the Review's last boat this year, and his sergeants grew up in the Veterans' Quarter. [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]] wants the Council to grant the Corsair League a toll on every hull that passes under the walls. The foreign masters waiting in the roads for a convoy have heard of the toll and mean to fight it. The marine captain who holds the second key of the Merazuni's strongroom at Besikar is to be replaced, and the Admiral's Council has put forward a man of its own.

## History {#history}

Vylarian engineers built the fortress to close the narrows to the empire's enemies, and an imperial garrison held it until the war. By the marines' account that garrison gave it up by treaty in the months after the Tamzir Shoals, and the first Taruzelian marines to march in found its stores full and its engines oiled. Since then it has been Yamuzel's, under a Warden named by the Admiral's Council, and its wall has been the councilors' stand for every Fleet Review since the first in 709.

# The Truth of the Fortress {#truth .secret}

**The drawings exist.** A copy of the engineers' plans of the fortress lies in the naval offices of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], and a Vylarian naval officer has been studying it since the war. The plans show what the marines have never found: a second sea door, cut through the rock under the keep, opening on a cave at the waterline that a boat can enter at low tide. Inside the cave a stair climbs to a storeroom under the keep, and the storeroom's inner door was bricked over by the imperial garrison before it marched out and plastered to match the wall. The marines use the storeroom for old sails. A party that knows where to knock finds the wall hollow.

**The archive holds more than logs.** Filed among the muster rolls of the war's second year is a survey book of plots on Khamisar's southern terraces, with the names of the men who signed for them (see [[place-khamisar|Khamisar]]). The old navy clerk who keeps the archive was told to file it and forget it, and he has been offered a pension to burn it.

**The toll.** The admiral [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]] wants the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]] to grant the Corsair League a toll on every hull that passes under the fortress. The present Warden is not a Tanaduni man, and the day-book is the only honest count of what such a toll would raise. Both the League and the toll's opponents would pay for a copy of it.
