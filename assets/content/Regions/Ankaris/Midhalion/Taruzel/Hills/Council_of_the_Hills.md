## Overview

The Council of the Hills is the council of the hill tribes of the [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]], and it is the nearest thing to a government the hill country has. Once a year, in the autumn when the flocks come down from the summer grounds, the elders of the camps of the [[affiliation-qazishuni|Qazishuni]], the [[affiliation-saliruni|Saliruni]] and the [[affiliation-haduruni|Haduruni]] cross to [[place-qalirel|Qalirel]] and climb to the ruin of [[place-ralumar|Ralumar]] on its spine. They sit on the stone benches round its paved court for as long as the business takes, usually five or six days, and then they go home and carry what was decided to their camps.

The council does four things. It settles quarrels between the tribes, chiefly over grazing, water and blood-price. It sets the year's price of water at the wells and cisterns of the hill roads, and of passage for a caravan over each tribe's ground, so that a caravan master pays the same at every well of a tribe. It names the hill tribes' councilors to the council of [[affiliation-qudumel|Kudumel]], the one coastal government that seats them. And it answers the coast, when the coast asks the hills anything at all.

The Lawspeaker presides. The present one is [Qatush Qazishuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, an old Qazishuni elder who served as a young man in the forts of [[lore-easternline|The Eastern Line]] on imperial pay and carried a spear in the war of independence. He holds the law of the hills by heart and speaks it at the opening of every sitting: the price of a life, the rule of the well and the cup, the duties of a guest and a host, and the terms on which one tribe crosses another's ground. The hill word for him is _mugilum_ (lawspeaker), and the law itself is _mugilu_, the custom set down, although nobody in the hills has set it down anywhere but in memory.

## How the Council Sits

A matter comes to the council through an elder. A herder whose flock has been driven off a spring tells the elder of his camp. The elder takes it first to the other elders of his own tribe, and only if it lies between tribes does he carry it to Ralumar. A coast man with business for the council has to find a hill elder willing to carry it, and the elders carry very little for the coast.

On the first morning of the sitting the Keeper of the Doors lights a fire in the middle of the court from the Qazishuni's hilltop fire, and the Lawspeaker stands by it and speaks the law aloud, from beginning to end, which takes most of the morning. Then the matters are heard one at a time. The two sides speak, their kin speak for them, and anyone of the hills may speak when the Lawspeaker gives leave. The elders argue in the open. A decision stands when the elders of all three tribes have each accepted it aloud, so that any one tribe's elders can stop it, and a matter they cannot agree on waits a year.

Nothing is written. The decisions are spoken into the memory of every elder present, and each carries them home and repeats them to his camp. A hill man who wants to know what the council decided three years ago asks an elder who was there.

## Water and Passage

The council's prices are the part of its work the coast meets. Each autumn it sets what a skin of water costs at a Saliruni well, what a mule train pays to cross the Qazishuni's ground on Qalirel's pass road, and what the timberwrights of the Alz pay the Haduruni for each tree felled on their side of the valley. The prices hold for a year on every tribe's ground, and the caravan houses of Kudumel and [[place-pashinar|Pashinar]] send riders up after the sitting to learn them. A tribe that charges more than the council's price on its ground answers for it at the next sitting.

## The Seats at Kudumel

The council names the councilors who take the seats Kudumel's council keeps for the hill tribes. They are hill elders, who need not live in the city and mostly do not. They carry the hills' word to the coast and bring the coast's decisions back to Ralumar. No other city of the Confederation seats them. [[affiliation-besikar|Besikar]], whose island Qalirel is, and [[affiliation-sazumun|Sazumun]], whose island [[place-visizel|Visizel]] is, count the hill country as their own country and have never asked the council anything.

## Relations

The three tribes are the council, and no tribe takes a quarrel with another anywhere else. Kudumel is the council's one friend on the coast, because Kudumel lives by the hill roads and keeps the hills' seats on its council. The house of [[affiliation-balinuni|Balinuni]] owes the tribes the share of the hill-road tolls promised in the war, by clasped hands and never in writing, and the Saliruni's elders raise the debt at every sitting. [[affiliation-vezakuni|The Council of the Guild-Wardens]] (the Vezakuni), whose Recovered Compacts want the cut lintels of Ralumar, have asked the council's leave to copy them, and the council has named a price the Vezakuni have not paid.

## Commerce and Currency

The council takes no tax and keeps no treasury. Its prices are paid to the tribe that keeps the well or the ground, in silver weighed on the tribe's scales or in goats. The hills take the coast's [[lore-trzlncrncy|Taruzelian Currency]] by weight and not by face, and refuse the Confederation's paper script entirely.

## What It Is Fighting About {#present}

The council's quarrel is with the coast, and the coast will not come to it. The Saliruni's elders raise the [[affiliation-balinuni|Balinuni]] debt at every sitting, and the tribes' councilors at Kudumel have stopped asking the house for it. The Grand Council at Besikar, where [[being-dnshkmn|Danashu Kumuni]] of Kudumel sets the order of business this year, is the one body on the coast that could make the house pay. A gate the hills did not build hangs on Qalirel's pass road in their name. The [[affiliation-vezakuni|Vezakuni]] have not paid the price the council named for copying Ralumar's lintels, and the factors of the great houses come up to the Doors in pairs to offer for the stones.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The message to the coast.** The Keeper of the Doors asks a party that has been let into Ralumar to carry the council's word to the coast and speak it once, to [[being-dnshkmn|Danashu Kumuni]].
- **The grievance.** The tribes' councilors at Kudumel want the hills' grievance against the Balinuni read in the Grand Council, and ask a party with standing in Besikar to find them a councilor who will sponsor it.
