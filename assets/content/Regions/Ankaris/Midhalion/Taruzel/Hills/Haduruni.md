The timber track up the Alz valley ends at a heap of stones as tall as a man, with a peeled staff standing in the top of it. The timberwrights' carts turn round there. Beyond it the woods belong to the Haduruni, and a timberwright who wants to fell a tree on that side of the line comes back to the heap with silver and waits for someone to come down out of the trees.

The Haduruni (from _hadur-_, to close an opening, the company of the closed way) are the hill tribe of [[place-visizel|Visizel]], some 50,000 people in the island's high interior. Their ground has two faces. The western slopes, which take the winter rain, are oak, pine and chestnut down into the upper valley of the [[place-alzriver|Alz River]], and the camps there hunt deer and boar, burn charcoal and run pigs in the woods. The eastern slopes, in the rain shadow, are scrub and stone, and the camps there herd goats like every other hill tribe.

## The Boundary

A line of stone heaps runs along Visizel's hills above the timber camps of the Alz, each in sight of the next, and it is the edge of the tribe's ground. The Keeper of the Boundary gives a stranger leave to pass it or refuses. A stranger found above the line without leave loses whatever animal he brought, and a second time loses his boots, and walks down. Hunters and charcoal-burners of the plain come up to the heaps to trade, and the tribe's guides take a traveler with leave across the hills to the island's eastern shore in two days.

## Trees Sold

The Haduruni's living from the coast is timber. The timberwright families of the Alz woods buy the right to fell on the tribe's side of the valley, tree by tree. The tribe's elders decide which trees may be sold, at the price fixed for the year by the [[affiliation-hillcouncil|Council of the Hills]]. A Marker of Trees walks the approved stand with the timberwright and cuts the tribe's mark into the bark of each tree sold: a closed square, the sign of the closed way. A tree felled without the mark is a theft. The tribe sends a party down to the timber camp it came from, and the camp pays twice the price or loses its next season's cutting.

The great timber of the island goes down the Alz to the yards of [[place-sazumun2|Sazumun]] and the Naval Yards of [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]]. [[affiliation-sazumun|Sazumun]], whose island Visizel is, grants the timberwrights their charters, and the charters say nothing about the tribe. The timberwright [[being-zttyblnn|Zattuya Balinuni]] has found the best oak she has seen in years in a stand on the Haduruni side of the line, and the shipwrights of Sazumun want it.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

The Haduruni's quarrel is with a charter they were never asked about. [[affiliation-sazumun|Sazumun]] grants the timberwrights of the Alz their charters, the charters say nothing of the tribe, and the tribe's mark on every tree felled above the line is the only law the cutting answers to on their side. [[being-zttyblnn|Zattuya Balinuni]] has found the best oak she has seen in years in a stand above the boundary, the shipwrights of Sazumun want it, and she has been asked to mark the whole of next winter's keel cut for a single buyer. The Keeper of the Boundary has not yet come down to the stone heap to hear her request for the stand.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The stand above the line.** [[being-zttyblnn|Zattuya Balinuni]] asks a party to wait with her at the stone heap with silver, and to speak for her when the Keeper of the Boundary comes down.
- **The unmarked trunk.** A trunk without the closed square has come down the Alz on the winter flood, and the Haduruni send to the timber camp it came from for twice its price.
