A ship bound for Besikar passes between fortified islands and long breakwaters before the city opens in front of her, and inside them lies the **[Grand Harbor]{.name data-type="place/feature"}**, a deep natural bay that can shelter hundreds of vessels at once. It is the finest anchorage on the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]] and the largest and best protected. Besikar, a city of about 400,000 on the coast of the [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]], is the seat of the city-state of [[affiliation-besikar|Besikar]], the largest and wealthiest of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]], and the place where the Confederation's [Grand Council]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} meets.

## The Harbor

The harbor has two arms with two trades. The naval yards on the northern arm build warships and merchantmen; the southern arm belongs to the fishing fleet and the smaller traders. In a dry-dock on the waterfront stands the **[Tamzir Monument]{.name data-type="place/site"}**, a captured Vylarian warship kept as a memorial to the [Battle of Tamzir Shoals]{.name data-type="lore/history"} ([[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]]).

## Two Cities in One

Besikar is a city of staggering contrasts. Along the harbor runs the **[Guild Quarter]{.name data-type="place/site"}**, a canyon of marble-faced warehouses, counting houses and palatial guild halls where the major merchant guilds keep their headquarters and fortunes are made and lost over a single shipment of eastern spice. The **[Grand Council Hall]{.name data-type="place/structure"}**, where the Confederation's governing body meets, stands among them.

Behind the harbor the old city climbs in a maze of narrow streets, covered bazaars and tenement blocks. The dock workers, sailors and craftspeople who live there work in the shadow of wealth they will never share, and a visitor who leaves the marble for the bazaars crosses from one Besikar to the other in the length of a street.
