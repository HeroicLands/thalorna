The bronze doors of the Qizikuni stand open from first light to the evening gun, and a captain who walks in off the [Guild Quarter]{.name data-type="place/site"} of [[place-besikar2|Besikar]] is handed a cup of coffee before anyone asks his business. The counting room behind them is long, cool and quiet: a floor of green marble, a clerk at every table, a bullion scale under glass, and at the far end a wall of pigeonholes, one for every cargo the house has insured that season. When a ship comes home, a boy runs to take her slip out of its hole. When a ship is lost, the slip goes to the table at the very end of the room, where the head of the house pays.

The Qizikuni (from _qizik-_, to inherit) are the company of the inheritance, and the name is apt: the house counts back further than any other on the quay, it has outlasted every man and woman who headed it, and it expects to outlast the Confederation.

## What the House Does

The Qizikuni insure cargoes and lend against them. A captain who wants a voyage financed borrows from the house against the cargo he will carry, and pays a bond that insures the hull and the goods. If he comes home, the house takes its interest. If the sea takes him, the house pays his lenders and takes the loss. More of Besikar's outward cargoes sail under a Qizikuni bond than under any other, and a captain who carries one is taken more seriously at every harbor he enters.

The house also holds bullion, and lends it. When the merchant princes needed gold to raise the war, the first gold of the Auric Compact's loan came out of the Qizikuni strongroom, so a large share of what the Confederation owes the Compact it owes, in the end, to this house. The Qizikuni keep litigants of their own name in other cities to see that such debts are honored: [[being-pshtnqzkn|Pushutan Qizikuni]] argues the house's trade disputes in Sharimun, and [[being-ltryqzkn|Lataruya Qizikuni]] its inheritance cases in Sazumun.

## Tirmana Qizikuni

The head of the house is a small, plain-dressed woman of sixty-one who wears no rings, does her own sums and keeps the table at the end of the counting room for herself. She sits for the house among the masters of the Compact and on the council of Besikar, and she is courteous to everyone, including the people she is ruining. She has never lost an argument over a ledger.

## Debt and Appetite {#present}

The Qizikuni are owed more than any house in Taruzel, and they are owed it by the Confederation, which pays the interest on the war loan before it pays anything else. What Tirmana wants is the treasury that collects it. The [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]] keeps the Confederation's reserves, strikes its coin and clears its script, and its Kubasum answers to the [[affiliation-vezakuni|Vezakuni]], not to the Compact. Tirmana says in the Compact's councils that a treasury with small reserves should be kept by the people who hold its debt. She is pressing the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]] to order an audit of the Merazuni's strongroom, and she has a candidate of her own for the Kubasum's table.

This season the house is collecting. The Compact's calls on what the cities and harbor towns owe come sooner and dearer, and the Qizikuni hold more of that paper than any other house. Tirmana's audit is on the summer session's order if [[being-dnshkmn|Danashu Kumuni]] gives it a place, and her candidate for the Kubasum's table is a Qizikuni clerk of the treasury's own ledger. She also means to sound the heads of the other four great houses, by sealed letter, on council seats that would pass from a head to an heir, so that the houses which sit in the councils now would sit there for good. Nobody has moved it in any council. She wants the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] out of Besikar as Vylaria's instrument, and a mage of the Order has leased a house in the city through a broker of the Merchantry.

## Meeting the House

A party meets the Qizikuni through [Zarutan Qizikuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the heir (_qizikum_), a broad, laughing man of twenty-six who runs the underwriting desk on the quay. He writes a cheap bond for a captain he likes, a dear one for a captain he does not, and he remembers every captain either way. He stands drinks at the harbor taverns, he stands surety for strangers when it amuses him, and he is betrothed to [Katira Tanaduni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the convoy master of the [[affiliation-tanaduni|Tanaduni]] of Yamuzel. The marriage (_teshudush_) will bind a Compact house to a house of the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]], two guilds that need each other and trust nothing about each other.

:::secret
**For the GM:** The gold the Qizikuni lent the war was not all their own. When the house's strongroom ran low in the first months of the rising, Tirmana borrowed from Khelathi lenders through a broker of the [[affiliation-mrchntryvl|Merchantry of the Veil]], on notes signed in the house's name and entered in none of its books. The notes lie in a Khelathi factor's strongbox in Besikar. Nobody in [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Au'Khelathu]] wants them presented, because Au'Khelathu never acknowledged its aid to the war; they are worth more as a hold over the house than as gold, and the factor uses them as one. Zarutan does not know they exist.

A marriage merges the debts of two houses, and Katira Tanaduni has asked to read the Qizikuni books before she signs. A party can be hired by Katira to learn what the Qizikuni owe and to whom, by Tirmana to see that the factor's strongbox is empty before the wedding, or by the factor himself to remind the house, quietly, that the notes are still there.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The candidate.** [[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]] hires a party to learn which of the Kubasum's keepers would serve under her candidate, and which would have to be bought.
- **The broadsheet.** A broadsheet pasted on the bronze doors overnight names the Qizikuni as the lender that took the veterans' land. Tirmana wants the printer found and the sheet left up until she knows who reads it.
- **The answers.** Tirmana sends a party to Yamuzel and Bazunel with her letters on the hereditary seats, and [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]] and [[being-brshrskn|Abarash Rusakuni]] each ask the party what the other said.
