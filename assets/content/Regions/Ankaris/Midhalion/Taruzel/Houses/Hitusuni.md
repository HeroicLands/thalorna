Nearly every cargo that comes ashore at [[place-sazumun2|Sazumun]] spends a night or a season in a Hitusuni warehouse. The house's warehouses line the quays below the [House of Factors]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, white-walled like the rest of the waterfront and marked over each door with a blue wave breaking on a rock, and a cargo that enters one is sealed in wax with the house's mark until its tariff is paid to the guilds. The porters sing as they carry. The wardens at the doors are polite to everyone, and they write down who comes and goes.

The Hitusuni (from _hitus-_, to lie hidden under water) take their name from the root that gives _hitusu_, a reef, and _hitusush_, a secret. Sazumun reads the name both ways.

## What the House Does

Sazumun's guilds tax what passes through the city with an elaborate system of tariffs, and the tariff falls due when the goods leave the quay, not when they land. Between landing and leaving, the goods lie in a bonded warehouse under seal, and most of the bonded warehouses are the Hitusuni's. The house charges for the space, lends against the goods while they wait, and knows to the bale what every merchant in the city has in store. That knowledge is what binds the house to the Merchantry of the Veil, which trades in information as much as in silk: the Hitusuni tell the Merchantry what lies in Sazumun's warehouses, and the Merchantry tells the Hitusuni what is coming.

Some cargoes leave the warehouses with their seals unbroken and their tariffs unpaid. They are the Merchantry's cargoes, the ones the other guilds will not touch, and they go out by night in boats that do not fly the house's mark.

## Ashuraz Hitusuni

The head of the house is a heavy, soft-spoken man of about fifty with a black beard going gray and a habit of asking after the health of everyone's children. He sits for the house in the House of Factors, he is intelligent and well connected, and his dealings are complex enough that no court has made a charge against him stick. A warehouse guard named [[being-pzsnkhdzm|Pazasan Khidizum]] has spent years writing down what leaves the house's doors without a tariff, and Ashuraz has begun answering him the Taruzelian way: with accusations that Pazasan is corrupt.

## Debt and Appetite

During the war the captains of the flotilla stored their prize cargoes in Hitusuni warehouses, under the house's seal, to be sold when the war was won. When the guilds declared the most stubborn of those captains outlaws, the house kept the cargoes, and the seals on them. Some of the outlaw captains who now call themselves the [Free Captains]{.name data-type="affiliation"} still hold the house's receipts, and the house owes them what is written on the receipts.

What Ashuraz wants is a moneylender's charter. A moneylender house of the [Treasurers' Guild]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"} deposits bullion with the [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]] and draws script against it, and script cleared by the Merazuni is good anywhere in the Confederation. A Hitusuni charter would let the Merchantry's money come out of the warehouses as clean Merazuni script, and Ashuraz has been courting the Kubasum's clerks for it with great patience and very good dinners.

## Meeting the House

A party meets the Hitusuni at the counter of [[being-mglshhtsn|Mugilush Hitusuni]], the house's cousin, an apothecary who keeps a narrow shop in the lane behind the warehouses. He sells rare herbs and remedies, he sells poisons when he must, and he trusts nobody. The rare alchemical goods that come through the Hitusuni warehouses often pass across his counter on the way to their buyers, and Mugilush keeps out of the house's business so carefully that he knows a great deal of it.

The house has rivals on the same quays. The [Visizuni]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, a shipping house of Sazumun, carry many of the cargoes the Hitusuni store, and each house keeps a careful account of the other.

:::secret
**For the GM:** Two people want the Hitusuni brought down, and neither can do it alone. Pazasan Khidizum has the record of what has left the warehouses unsealed, but no court in Sazumun will hear him while Ashuraz sits in the House of Factors. One of the Free Captains has a receipt for a cargo the house sealed during the war and still holds: if she can prove the cargo is still in its warehouse, the house is a thief on its own seal, which no house in Taruzel survives.

A party can carry the receipt to Pazasan, or Pazasan's record to the captain; Ashuraz will pay handsomely for either to be lost. If the moneylender's charter comes through first, the house will have the Merazuni's script on its side, and both the record and the receipt will be worth much less.
:::
