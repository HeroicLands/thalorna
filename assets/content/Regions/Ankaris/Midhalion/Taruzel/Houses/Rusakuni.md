The loft of the Rusakuni is the long whitewashed room above the slipways of the [[place-designyards|Design Yards]] of [[place-bazunel2|Bazunel]], and its floor is the largest drawing in Taruzel. A new hull is drawn there full size in chalk, every rib and every curve of it, by draughtsmen on their knees with lengths of cord and pegs. The walls carry the sail-plans, the shelves carry half-models of every hull the house has drawn, and in the afternoons visitors climb up from the [[place-paintedharbor|Painted Harbor]] to watch the draughtsmen work and buy a painted model to take home.

The Rusakuni (from _rusak-_, to rig, which gives _rusakush_, a rope) are the company of the rigging. A hull drawn in Bazunel is known on any sea by its long lines and its clever rigging, and a large share of the hulls drawn in Bazunel are drawn in this loft.

## What the House Does

The Rusakuni sell drawings, not ships. A shipyard that wants to build a hull to Rusakuni lines pays the house for the drawings and a fee for every hull it builds from them, and the house sends a master to see the first one laid down. Every new galley of the Corsair League is built to Rusakuni lines at the [Naval Yards]{.name data-type="place/site"} of Yamuzel, and the League pays the house a fee on each, when it pays.

The house's masters are also the heart of the [Taruzelian Shipwrights' Guild]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"} in Bazunel, and it is their votes that keep the guild closed to women. Bazunel is the most open city of the Confederation in nearly everything else, and the Rusakuni are a large part of why shipbuilding is the exception. The house has kin in other harbors: [Jiraman Rusakuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"} is a shipwright in [[place-pashinar|Pashinar]], and his daughter [[being-blgnrskn|Belgina Rusakuni]], who learned the craft in his yard without ever being apprenticed, is the shipwright of [[affiliation-thetamzir|the Tamzir]].

## Abarash Rusakuni

The master designer is a lean, bright-eyed man of sixty-six who wears silk spattered with chalk and paint and has not drawn a line on paper in ten years: he draws on the loft floor, on his knees, with the young draughtsmen. He sits for the shipwrights on the council of Bazunel, where a master shipwright's vote carries real weight, and he is the city's most generous patron of its theaters. He talks about hulls with more warmth than about anything else alive, and about the League as he talks about bad weather.

## Debt and Appetite

The League owes the house its fees on several years of galleys, because the Confederation is slow to pay the League and the League is slower to pay its designers. Abarash has borrowed against what he is owed, and the lender is a broker of the [[affiliation-mrchntryvl|Merchantry of the Veil]] who takes part of the interest in a currency the Merchantry values more than coin: copies of the drawings.

What Abarash wants is to be free of the League. A house bound to the League draws for the League, and the League says who else may build to its lines. He wants a charter of the [[affiliation-vezakuni|Vezakuni]] that makes the Design Yards a trade of their own, so that the house can sell its lines to any yard on the sea that pays: in [[place-helionis|Helionis]], in [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Au'Khelathu]], or anywhere else.

## Meeting the House

A party meets the Rusakuni in the loft or in the theaters below it, through [Lushar Rusakuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Abarash's nephew and the house's keeper of the drawings. He is thirty-one, handsome, idle in the evenings and very good at his work in the mornings, and he spends the evenings at the theaters of the [[place-paintedharbor|Painted Harbor]] and at their card tables. He shows visitors the half-models, charms them into commissions and remembers which of them own ships.

:::secret
**For the GM:** Lushar is the one who carries the drawings to the Merchantry's broker, on his uncle's order, and he has begun carrying more than his uncle orders, to pay his own debts at the card tables. Among the drawings now in the broker's hands is the set for the League's newest galley, and the broker has a buyer for it in the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]], Taruzel's great rival at sea.

When the League's captains learn that a Bizarian yard is building to Rusakuni lines, they will want to know who sold them, and the answer will end the house's bond to the League. A party can be hired by Abarash to recover the drawings before they sail, by the broker to see them safely aboard, or by the League to find out who in Bazunel is selling its galleys. Belgina is the house's other trouble. When the shipwrights' guild leans on Jiraman to recall his daughter from the Tamzir, the masters who do the leaning are Rusakuni.
:::
