A letter of credit under the Auric Compact's seal is accepted as currency across [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión]], and a contract that carries the seal binds as tightly as any court order. That seal is how most people meet the most powerful institution in the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]]—more powerful, in practice, than the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]] it nominally serves. The Compact is a cartel of banking houses, trading firms and commodity brokers, and it controls the flow of capital that Taruzelian commerce runs on. Ships sail because the Compact finances them, cargoes move because it insures them, and governments function because it lends to them.

## Counting Houses and Agents

The Compact works through counting houses in every major Taruzelian city and most of the major ports of the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]]. Its agents—factors, assessors and debt collectors—are a familiar sight in harbors from [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]] to [[place-helionis|Helionis]] to the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]].

## The Debt That Rules Taruzel

The Compact financed [[lore-taruzelwar|The Taruzelian War of Independence]]. It was a calculated investment, and it paid off spectacularly when the post-war government fell under guild control. The Compact is the Confederation's creditor; the treasury and the mint belong to the [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]] of [[affiliation-vezakuni|The Council of the Guild-Wardens]] (the Vezakuni), and the Compact holds only the debt. It uses that leverage to make government policy serve commercial interest: taxes stay low on guild operations, tariffs protect guild monopolies, and the courts enforce guild contracts with enthusiasm.

## Methods

The Compact prefers economic pressure to violence and is not above either. A merchant who defaults on a Compact loan finds his credit revoked in every port of the Confederation. A captain who carries cargo for the Compact's rivals finds her vessel refused harbor services. When the softer methods fail, the Compact sends enforcers—discreet, professional and ruthless—who collect through intimidation and, when they must, by force.

Those enforcers are the people most likely to cross paths with the crew of [[affiliation-thetamzir|the Tamzir]]. Old debts from the war, broken contracts and the captain's refusal to work inside the guild system make the ship a persistent irritant to the Compact's sense of order.

## The Present {#present}

The Compact is collecting. This season its counting houses call in what the cities and their harbor towns owe sooner than the notes require and at dearer renewals, and the calls fall on shopkeepers, fishermen and the members of the veterans' halls, who have nothing left to pledge. Its answer to the anger that follows is the answer it has always given: revoked licenses, blacklists and enforcers. It would crush the outlaw captains and enforce every debt they owe, and it pays the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]] bounties for the captains its galleys bring in.

Its heaviest house, the [[affiliation-qizikuni|Qizikuni]], wants more than the interest. [[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]] presses the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]] for an audit of the [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]]'s strongroom, and sounds the other houses on council seats that pass from head to heir. The Compact wants the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] expelled from the islands as Viléria's instrument, and it finds the [[affiliation-mrchntryvl|Merchantry]] hiring the Order's mages and the League's admirals wanting their battle-magic. In Besikar its factors keep petitioning to have the [[place-tamzirmonument|Tamzir Monument]] broken up, and the veterans sit up in her dock at night.

:::secret {title="Why the calls come dearer"}
The Qizikuni owe Khelathi lenders for gold borrowed in the war on notes no ledger records, and the head factor of the Khelathi factorage in Besikar now asks when the house means to pay something down. Tirmana pays a little each quarter, through a Merchantry broker, and finds the coin by calling in the cities' debts to the Compact. The Compact's enforcers pay a Keeper of a Besikar veterans' hall, [[being-zlmrzzn|Zilmar Ezzinu]], for the names of the Breakers who empty its debtors' strongrooms, and they have learned that a copy of the rebels' promise of prize money to the Free Captains exists. The Compact means to trade the Tamzir Monument back to Viléria for lower tariffs once her hull is condemned.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The renewal.** A Besikar fishing-boat owner whose note the Compact has called asks a party to learn why the house that holds it will not renew, and [[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]]'s clerk is the one who refused.
- **The Breaker's name.** The Compact's enforcers want a Breaker of the [[affiliation-unchained|Unchained]] taken before the next strongroom night, and hire a party the halls of Besikar do not know by sight. The first hall the party drinks in is [[being-zlmrzzn|Zilmar Ezzinu]]'s, and the elders there ask the party's business before the second cup.
- **The bounty.** The Compact posts its largest bounty of the season on a Free Captain's hull, and [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]]'s galleys are slow to look for her.
