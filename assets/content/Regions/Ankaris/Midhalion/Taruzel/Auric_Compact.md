A letter of credit under the Auric Compact's seal is accepted as currency across the [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión Region]], and a contract that carries the seal binds as tightly as any court order. That seal is how most people meet the most powerful institution in the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]]—more powerful, in practice, than the [Grand Council]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} it nominally serves. The Compact is a cartel of banking houses, trading firms and commodity brokers, and it controls the flow of capital that Taruzelian commerce runs on. Ships sail because the Compact finances them, cargoes move because it insures them, and governments function because it lends to them.

## Counting Houses and Agents

The Compact works through counting houses in every major Taruzelian city and most of the major ports of the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]]. Its agents—factors, assessors and debt collectors—are a familiar sight in harbors from [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]] to [[place-helionis|Helionis]] to the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]].

## The Debt That Rules Taruzel

The Compact financed the [Taruzelian War of Independence]{.name data-type="lore/history"}. It was a calculated investment, and it paid off spectacularly when the post-war government fell under guild control. The Compact is the Confederation's creditor; the treasury and the mint belong to the [Merazuni]{.name data-type="affiliation"} of the [[affiliation-vezakuni|Vezakuni]], and the Compact holds only the debt. It uses that leverage to make government policy serve commercial interest: taxes stay low on guild operations, tariffs protect guild monopolies, and the courts enforce guild contracts with enthusiasm.

## Methods

The Compact prefers economic pressure to violence and is not above either. A merchant who defaults on a Compact loan finds his credit revoked in every port of the Confederation. A captain who carries cargo for the Compact's rivals finds her vessel refused harbor services. When the softer methods fail, the Compact sends enforcers—discreet, professional and ruthless—who collect through intimidation and, when they must, by force.

Those enforcers are the people most likely to cross paths with the crew of [[affiliation-thetamzir|The Tamzir]]. Old debts from the war, broken contracts and the captain's refusal to work inside the guild system make the ship a persistent irritant to the Compact's sense of order.
