## Overview

The Alz River runs down from the hills of one of the Tarûzelian islands and reaches the sea at [[place-sazumun2|Sazûmun]], and the natural harbor at its mouth is the reason that city-state stands where it does. The harbor is the conduit between the [[place-dunharargn|Dunhari]] desert trade and the western sea routes: goods brought over the strait fill the white-walled warehouses on the quays and go out from there on every tide.

## The Alz Valley

Upriver the valley climbs into hill woods, and the trees of the **Alz woods** are what Sazûmun's shipwrights build with. Timberwright families have cut there for generations. [[being-zttyblnn|Zattûya Balînûnî]], born to such a family up the valley, now chooses the standing trees that become Sazûmun's ships and furniture, reading the lean of a trunk and the run of its grain before anyone lifts an axe. A keel that sails out of Sazûmun usually grew on the Alz.
