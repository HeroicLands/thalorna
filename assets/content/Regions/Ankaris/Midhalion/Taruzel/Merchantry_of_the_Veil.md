The agent wears a ring with a sigil cut into it and says a phrase that sounds like small talk, and if you know the answering phrase, you are doing business with the Merchantry of the Veil. It is the least understood and most whispered-about of the great guilds of the [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]]. Officially it is a trade association of dealers in luxury goods: silks, spices, rare alchemical ingredients, exotic animals and fine art. Unofficially it deals in something worth more, which is information, and where the information trade meets the goods trade it will move cargo the other guilds will not touch.

## The Veil

The Merchantry cultivates mystery. Its membership rolls are not public, its meetings are private, and its agents identify themselves by sigil rings and coded phrases instead of guild badges. The secrecy is partly affectation, since the luxury trade has always traded on exclusivity, and partly necessity. The Merchantry's real business is brokering connections between people who cannot afford to be seen dealing with each other.

Its clients show what that means. A Vylarian _kurthos_ (senator) who needs a message carried to a Taruzelian rival without either government knowing comes to the Merchantry. So does a [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] mage who needs alchemical ingredients banned under imperial law, and so does a fugitive who needs passage out of a Taruzelian port with no questions asked: the Merchantry knows which captains do not check manifests.

## Agents

The Merchantry works through agents placed in every port city, caravan stop and aristocratic court in [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión]]. Most are legitimate merchants who do the Merchantry's business alongside their own: a spice trader who also carries sealed letters, a jeweler who also appraises stolen goods, a courtesan who also listens.

## The Other Guilds

The other great guilds live with the Merchantry as a tolerated parasite. The [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]] officially disapproves of its shadowy dealings and quietly buys its information. The [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]] calls it a pack of spies and smugglers and accepts that some cargoes are more profitably moved in the dark.

Of the three, the Merchantry is the one most likely to approach the crew of [[affiliation-thetamzir|the Tamzir]] with an offer of work instead of a threat, because a vessel that sails outside the normal guild channels is exactly what its less conventional shipments need.

## The Present {#present}

The Merchantry trades with every side of the trouble coming, and says nothing about any of it. It buys the [[affiliation-rusakuni|Rusakuni]]'s drawings as interest on [[being-brshrskn|Abarash Rusakuni]]'s debts, and its cargoes go out of the [[affiliation-hitusuni|Hitusuni]] warehouses of Sazumun with their seals unbroken. It hires the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]'s mages that the Compact would expel, and a Merchantry broker leased a house in Besikar to one of them this winter. [[being-ashurazhitus|Ashuraz Hitusuni]], its merchant prince in Sazumun, wants a moneylender's charter from the [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]], and the Merchantry's agents have offered [[being-zbzmjkdn|Zabuzum Jakuduni]] work, which he has not taken.

:::secret {title="What the Merchantry is selling"}
Since the winter, crates of crossbows, boarding pikes and Vylarian silver have gone out of a Hitusuni warehouse under a Merchantry seal, paid for by an agent of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], to the coves of the [[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captains]] and the veterans' halls of Yamuzel. Ashuraz has sold the dates of three of those deliveries to an enforcer of the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]]. A Merchantry broker carries the Qizikuni's quarterly payments to the Khelathi factorage in Besikar, and another holds the Rusakuni drawings of the League's newest galley, with a buyer waiting in a yard of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]].
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The silk for the Amradi shore.** [[being-ashurazhitus|Ashuraz Hitusuni]] wants an offer of Merchantry silk carried to the captain of the Tamzir, because the captain will not come to Sazumun to hear it.
- **The mage's lease.** [[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]] wants the lease of the Thordimalon mage's house in Besikar broken by its owner, and the Merchantry broker who arranged it wants to know who is asking.
- **The ring.** A party that has done the Merchantry a favor is given a sigil ring and a phrase, and the first person who answers the phrase asks it to carry a sealed letter to [[being-brshrskn|Abarash Rusakuni]]'s loft.
