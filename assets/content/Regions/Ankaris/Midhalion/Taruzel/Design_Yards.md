## Overview

The Design Yards line the western arm of the bay of [[place-bazunel2|Bazunel]], below the point where ships from the channels round into the harbor. They are where the Confederation's best ships are conceived. Few of those ships are built here. The yards build the first hull of each new line, and the yards of [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]], [[place-besikar2|Besikar]] and harbors across the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] build the rest to drawings bought from Bazunel. A hull drawn here is known on any sea by its long, clean lines and its inventive rigging.

## What Is There

**The slips.** Four slipways are cut into the rock of the shore, each a long stone ramp running down into the water, with a tiled shed over its upper end on timber posts. Two or three of them usually carry a hull, and a hull on a Bazunel slip stands in its frames for a whole season, because the yards build slowly and change their minds.

**The timber yard.** Behind the slips lies the timber yard, where keel timbers from the Alz woods of [[place-visizel|Visizel]] and southern hardwoods off the [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelathi]] ships season under open roofs. Tashilar grows no ship timber of its own, and every plank in the yards came by sea. The steam-boxes for bending planks stand along the yard wall, and on a working day their smoke drifts over the harbor.

**The loft.** Above the longest slip stands the loft of the [[affiliation-rusakuni|Rusakuni]], a long whitewashed room under a roof of skylights, whose floor is the largest drawing in Taruzel. A hull's lines are chalked out on it at full size, every frame and every curve, and the shipwrights lift their patterns straight off the floor. When a line is finished the master who drew it walks it end to end in his bare feet before he signs it. The floor is scrubbed white for each new line, and the old lines are kept as drawings in the house's strongroom.

**The model room.** At the foot of the loft stair, a long room is hung with half-models, carved half-hulls mounted on boards, one for every line the yards have sold. Buyers are welcome in the model room, and it is where a captain or a yard-master comes to choose a ship. The loft above it is closed to anyone the house has not invited.

## Who Works There

The yards belong to the shipwrights of Bazunel, and the [Taruzelian Shipwrights' Guild]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"} keeps them closed to women: its masters, the Rusakuni first among them, vote it so every year. Draughtsmen, shipwrights, sawyers, caulkers, riggers and their apprentices work the slips and the sheds. The guild's master shipwrights are counted artists in Bazunel as much as engineers, and one of them always sits on the city's council as Councilor of the Crafts. A launch is a public day. The hull goes down the slip on greased timbers with the harbor watching from the quays, and the yards stand the whole waterfront a drink.

## What Is Said of It

The shipwrights say a master who walks a line barefoot can feel a bad curve through his soles before his eye finds it. The apprentices say the old lines under the whitewash show through the floor on damp mornings, and that a draughtsman who draws over one of them draws a ship that sails like the old one. The riggers of Yamuzel say a Bazunel hull is a beautiful thing until something carries away, and then it is a puzzle.

## Layers {#history}

The yards build only the first hull of each new line. The loft above the longest slip belongs to the [[affiliation-rusakuni|Rusakuni]], whose floor is scrubbed white for each new line and whose strongroom keeps every old one as drawings. Every new galley of the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]] since its founding after the war has been built at Yamuzel to lines drawn on this floor, and the house is bound to the League. The shipwrights' guild that holds the yards has voted them closed to women every year.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The yards are busy and owed. The League owes the Rusakuni their fees on several years of galleys and offers privileges in place of coin, and [[being-brshrskn|Abarash Rusakuni]] wants a charter from the [[affiliation-vezakuni|Vezakuni]] that makes the yards a trade of their own, so that the house can sell its lines to any yard on the sea. A yard on the Amradi shore is said to be building a coaster to an old Bazunel line the house sold to nobody there. The guild's masters, the Rusakuni first among them, keep the yards closed to women, and the shipwright of the Tamzir, [[being-blgnrskn|Belgina Rusakuni]], learned the craft in her father's yard in Pashinar without ever being apprenticed.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The coaster on the slip.** [[being-brshrskn|Abarash Rusakuni]] pays a party to cross the strait and tell him whose drawing the Amradi coaster was built from.
- **The galley's fee.** Abarash hires a party to learn what the League's privileges are worth on the Painted Harbor before he takes them in place of coin.

# The Truth of the Lines {#truth .secret}

**The last two lines the loft accepted from a journeyman were drawn by his sister.** [Pazasu Rasuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a journeyman draughtsman of the yards, is a steady hand of no great gift. His sister [Helaruya Rasuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who keeps his rooms and has never been apprenticed, draws better than anyone in the loft but its master. She learned from watching her brother's work and correcting it, and for two years she has drawn by night on the floor of a rented warehouse in [Zatturel]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the newcomers' quarter, and her brother has carried her lines into the loft by day as his own. Both lines were accepted and sold. One of them is a fast merchantman now building in three yards at once.

**Who knows.** [[being-brshrskn|Abarash Rusakuni]], the master of the loft, knows. He watched Pazasu chalk out the second line on the loft floor and saw a man copying a drawing he did not understand. He has said nothing, because the guild's masters would strike the brother from the yards and Abarash wants the lines. Helaruya does not know that he knows.

**What she wants.** She wants one line with her own name on it. A party she trusts can help her get a line into the model room under a name the guild cannot refuse, or into a foreign yard that will build it as hers. The guild's masters, if they learn of her, will want the lines withdrawn and the brother expelled. Abarash, if he is pressed, will want her inside the loft and her name kept off the drawings.
