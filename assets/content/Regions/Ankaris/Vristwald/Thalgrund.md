## Overview

**Thalgrund** (230 people) occupies the dale bottom in [[place-edriwald|Edriwald]]. A wet dale narrows the eastern bay path to one ford. The Other Chief allocates its raised fields among households and claims a share of each harvest for winter stores. The War Chief calls that share a tax and has promised the younger families new clearings beyond the ford. There is room for a daughter village, but the two sides disagree about who owns the stores that would keep it alive.

The Other Chief holds the strongest of the village's three seats, but the other two elders retain their own authority. The Shaman keeps the [[lore-badgerttm|badger]] as the village’s single totem. The neighboring villages are reached by narrow forest tracks, used by messengers and small parties; no village commands the next one along the path.
