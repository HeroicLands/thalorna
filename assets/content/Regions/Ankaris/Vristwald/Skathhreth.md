## Overview

**Skathhreth**, the harm ford, is a broad gravel crossing of the [[place-moldunriver|Moldún]] in the central forest of [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]], and no village stands within a day's walk of it. In low water a man can wade it with the river at his knees. On the day in 291 AF when a Vylarian legion tried to cross it in the rain, the river was at a man's chest, and the muster that came out of the trees on both banks killed almost all of it ([[lore-skathhrethlss|The Loss of the Legion at Skathhreth]]). Several thousand men died there in an afternoon, far from their gods' ground and many of them in the water, and nobody judged them before the god [[lore-selmorosdty|Sélmoros]], as the [[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]] teaches, or called them home to anything. The ford has been a [[lore-bloodfield|blood-field]] since, and it is the thinnest one in the forest.

## What Crosses

The field is a recurrence. In the week of the battle, at dusk, the column is seen on the water: a long file of men wading north in the rain whether or not it is raining, their shields held high, never reaching the far bank. The Varokh say it is the legion still looking for a road home, and the Vylarian loggers upstream, who have heard nothing of the battle, call it fog.

In the thaw the river runs high again, as it did on the day, and the _hrag_ (hungry spirits) gather at the ford. On a thin field they cross into the world in a half-made shape, and they take what they find. Every village near the Moldún can name somebody who went down to the ford in the thaw-week and was found on the gravel in the morning.

## How the Varokh Keep Their Distance

The ford is crossed by day or not at all, and never in the thaw-week. Each spring the Shamans of the nearest villages walk to the north bank together and lay a line of ash along it from the trees to the water, which is the only thing any Varokh does at Skathhreth on purpose. The line marks the field; it does not hold anything back, and the Shamans say so. None of them has ever offered to call the dead home, because the dead are Vylarian and have no totem to be called to.

## Who Goes There

- **Treasure-hunters** after legion iron, which the river still gives up after a flood: a buckle, a blade, a helmet with the cheek-pieces rusted shut.
- **The loggers.** A Vylarian logging camp has set itself up on the Moldún upstream, felling trees the Varokh hold sacred within sight of the [Garskald]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}'s boundary markers, and its men water their oxen below the ford.
- **Mokturan officers** who want the eagle back. It went into the river with the men around it, and a legion that recovers an eagle lost in an earlier defeat carries a gold band in its name forever.

## Hooks

- Men from the logging camp begin to vanish at night, and the camp's master blames the Garskald. The Garskald's War Chief, who wants the camp gone, does not deny it and did not do it.
- A Mokturan _Lusar_ hires a party to raise the eagle from the riverbed. Raising it brings the column up the bank, and the column has been looking for its eagle for four hundred years.
- A Shaman laying the ash line finds it already laid, by somebody who did not know the words.
