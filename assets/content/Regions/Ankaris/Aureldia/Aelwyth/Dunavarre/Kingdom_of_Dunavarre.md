## Overview

Dúnavarre is the most powerful and diplomatically active kingdom on [[place-aelwyth|Aelwyth]], occupying the southeastern coast of the island where the best natural harbors face the open sea toward [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] and the mainland beyond. Where other Aelwythan polities look inward—to the mysteries of the great central lake or the feudal quarrels of the lowlands—Dúnavarre looks outward, maintaining trade and diplomatic ties with the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]], the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provenzia]], and even the courts of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]. The kingdom holds the land of [[place-dunavarre|Dúnavarre]].

## Government

Dúnavarre is unique in the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]]—and possibly in the known world—as a kingdom ruled directly by a synod of mages belonging to the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]. Where elsewhere the Arcane Order operates through influence, court intrigue, and institutional leverage, in Dúnavarre the mages simply govern. The [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]] sets policy, administers justice, directs diplomacy, and commands the kingdom's military forces through appointed officers.

How the Arcane Order came to rule Dúnavarre outright is a matter of historical debate. The kingdom's own chronicles present it as a natural evolution—generations of mage-advisors gradually assuming the responsibilities that incompetent kings could not handle, until the crown became ceremonial and was eventually set aside altogether. The last king, [Yenral]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, was set aside about 490 AF ([[lore-dunavarresynod|the Mages Take Dúnavarre]]). Critics (particularly in the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]] and the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]) tell a darker story of magical coercion and political manipulation. The truth is likely somewhere between.

The Synod's rule gives Dúnavarre a character unlike any other Aelwythan polity: orderly, learned, and efficient, but also secretive, paternalistic, and intolerant of dissent. The mages govern well by most material measures—Dúnavarre's roads are maintained, its laws enforced, its commerce thriving—but the Synod's decisions are made behind closed doors, and ordinary citizens have no voice in them.

## Character

Dúnavarre's culture is a blend of Aelwythan mysticism and Élavendren refinement, filtered through the Arcane Order's scholarly discipline. The kingdom has absorbed considerable cultural influence from [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] through centuries of close contact, giving its aristocracy a polish that contrasts with the rougher manners of Aldorath's feudal lords or the Peshtar barbarians. Education and literacy are prized—the Synod encourages learning as a matter of policy, and Dúnavarre's schools and libraries are among the finest on the island.

The kingdom is devout in its worship of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]], with the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]] (whose god represents order, justice, and honorable struggle) holding particular prominence—a natural fit for a realm that values structure and discipline above all. The militant [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]], an Árdavonian knightly order, serves as the kingdom's military arm and moral authority. Its Knight-Captains answer to the Synod but command enormous respect in their own right. The Synod sent the Order against the [[affiliation-saelvri|Saelvri]] between about 694 and 703 AF ([[lore-saelvricrusade|the Saelvri Crusade]]).

## Relations

Dúnavarre enjoys **very cordial** relations with [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], with whom it shares cultural roots and active trade. Its travelled and educated people commonly speak [[skill-elvndrlng|Élavendri]] as well as [[skill-aelwthlng|Aelwythi]], and Élavendri above all at court. Dúnavarre sits on Aelwyth's southeastern coast, whose harbors face Élavendre across some two hundred and fifty sea-miles, and the two are each other's nearest civilized neighbor across that water. The tie is personal as well as commercial: the **Synod** maintains close relations with the Élavendren royal house, and has done for generations. This is a notable diplomatic achievement given how carefully Élavendre holds the Thordimalon at arm's length. Élavendre **permits** the Arcane Order and **limits its access**: it may keep its houses and teach in the cities—the [University of Péravel]{.name data-type="affiliation"} is its largest campus outside the Empire—and it may not reach past them into the tribal lands, the sacred sites or the deep forest, where the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] enforce a boundary the crown declines to move. That an Arcane Order-governed kingdom is nonetheless welcome at [[place-beravel|Péravel]] says a good deal about both courts. That Dúnavarre—an Arcane Order-governed kingdom—maintains warm ties with Élavendre speaks to the pragmatism of both parties, and perhaps to the fact that the Dúnavarren Synod is more flexible and less doctrinaire than the Arcane Order's mainland hierarchy.

Relations with [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Aldorath]] are competitive—the two realms have long vied for influence over the island's interior, and Aldorath's feudal aristocracy views Dúnavarre's mage-rule with a mix of envy and contempt. The kingdom maintains connections to the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]] through coastal trade routes.

Most recently, Dúnavarre has launched an ambitious diplomatic initiative to establish independent relations with the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], seeking to bypass the Vylarian Empire's long-standing monopoly as intermediary for east-west diplomacy. This mission, commissioned by the Synod and led by Sir [[being-arancelavren|Aran Kélavren]], represents a bold assertion of Dúnavarren independence on the international stage.

## Notable Institutions

The Synod
: The governing body of Dúnavarre, composed of senior mages of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]. The Synod functions as both legislature and executive, with individual members overseeing portfolios of governance (diplomacy, defense, commerce, justice).

The Order of the Just Blade
: A militant knightly order dedicated to the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]], whose god represents order and justice. The Order serves as Dúnavarre's military arm, its Knight-Captains commanding the kingdom's forces under the Synod's authority.

The Umbral Court
: The Synod's position is that there is no such organization. See [[affiliation-umbralcourt|the Umbral Court]].

## Why the Arcane Order Is Here

The Arcane Order governs no other place on earth, and the usual explanations are Dúnavarren—competent
mage-advisors, incompetent kings, a crown set aside. Those explain the _form_. They do not explain why
the Arcane Order wanted it.

The answer is the rest of Aelwyth. **This is where the Arcane Order can study the fae without constraint.**
In [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]]—richer fae country by far—it is permitted and limited, held out of
the sacred sites by Wardens the crown will not overrule. Aelwyth has real fae
sites and nothing of the kind guarding them: the country belongs to scattered tribes with no common
council and no way to compel anyone.

Dúnavarre is therefore not an anomaly in the Arcane Order's history. It is the Arcane Order's **laboratory**, and the
Synod governs a kingdom chiefly because governing one is the price of unrestricted access to an island.

The Synod holds that access by an instrument nobody argues with: **every ship crossing between the
mainland and Aelwyth must call first at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]]**, inbound and outbound alike, to be inspected. Prohibited cargo is the stated purpose; anything magical is the real one, and is recorded, examined and often detained. No relic has left the island in living memory without the Arcane Order seeing it first.

The Synod also knows exactly how small and provincial Aelwyth is—being the only people on it who
correspond with the mainland as equals—and has never corrected a single neighbor's illusions on the
subject. An incurious island with a frightening reputation is a well-fenced research ground.

## Population and Settlement

Dúnavarre holds about **75,000** people—the smallest of Aelwyth's four settled realms, and the one the
other three are careful with. [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Aldorath]] has nearly three times as many
subjects and minds Dúnavarren opinion a great deal, which looks absurd until one remembers what the
Synod is: the Arcane Order's **central chantry for the island**, and therefore the body that decides whether a
neighboring court keeps the advisors it cannot govern without.

The remaining
sixty-nine thousand live on **manorial estates** through the coastal plains and the river valleys—demesne
and tenantry in the ordinary way, but administered for the Synod by appointed stewards rather than held
by a landed aristocracy with claims of its own. That is one of the quieter ways mage-rule differs from
the feudal kingdoms across the water, and one of the reasons the Synod's writ runs as evenly as it does.

## Commerce and Currency

Kingdom of Dúnavarre uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vylarian currency]] (Solden / Ardin / Ogdel) as its standard currency, with full access to the paper-script system through moneylenders belonging to [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]] and chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]]. Kingdom of Dúnavarre is a post-imperial commonwealth member—it participates fully in the trade and banking infrastructure of the Vylarian system but is not subject to imperial governance or imperial taxation. See [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vylarian Currency]] for the full system.
