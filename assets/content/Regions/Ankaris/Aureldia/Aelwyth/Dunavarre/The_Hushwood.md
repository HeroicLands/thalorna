In the wooded foothills where the Ironfells break up above [[place-dunavarre|Dúnavarre]] lies the **Hushwood**, a stand of old forest in which nothing sings. It is the newest of Aurèldía's [[lore-bloodfield|blood-fields]], made in three days at the turn of the year about 703 AF, and the only one in living memory.

## A Silence

The Hushwood is a field of the silent kind. The birds that fill every other wood on the island are absent from it, summer insects do not stir, and a traveler who enters hears only his own breath and the weather, and a lantern burns low and small. The field is thin. By day it is merely wrong; after dark things come out of it that harm the living, and the Order of the Just Blade's patrols have lost men on its edge.

At the edge of hearing, in the stillest hours, there are voices. Those who have listened describe a speech of a kind none of them knew, spoken at a distance and in numbers, never close enough to answer. The Saelvri's tongue is not spoken by anyone alive. Beyond the voices, the predators of the spirit realm gather where souls are unreceived, and the Hushwood has not stopped drawing them since the killing.

## Whose Dead

The dead of the Hushwood are the [[affiliation-saelvri|Saelvri]], a forest people the [[lore-saelvricrusade|Synod's crusade]] hunted out of the south-east of the island and killed, several thousand of them with their children, in the wood they had kept. They went to their deaths without the rite their own people keep, and the rite itself is lost with the people who knew it. The clergy of [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]] settle a field by placing in the god's keeping the dead who were his, and the Saelvri were not. Nobody living has a rite that can close this field.

## How the Place Is Treated

[[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Dúnavarre]] forbids entry. The Synod posts the paths on its side of the wood, and the forest peoples of the south-east do not need to be told. Neither the Order nor the tribes will give a reason. The Order's patrols keep to the margin and lose men there regardless.

## Hooks

- **The child.** A rumor on the forest edge says one Saelvri child was carried out of the wood alive. The Synod would like to find her.
- **The voices.** Someone has learned to follow them. What they say is not known, and what they say is wanted.
- **A knight's conscience.** A Knight-Captain of the Order who fought in the crusade comes to the edge by night and asks to be taken in.
