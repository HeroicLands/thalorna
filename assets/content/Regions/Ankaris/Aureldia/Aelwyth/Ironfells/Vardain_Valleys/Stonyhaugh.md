**Stonyhaugh** is the quarry, and the masons. Four hundred and sixty people cutting the gray stone that
the whole district is built and walled with.

The **terrace walls** are Stonyhaugh's real monument – mile upon mile of dry-stone terracing up valley
sides that would otherwise grow nothing, laid over centuries and maintained by a rota every household in
the valleys contributes to. Visitors invariably assume the Khazári built them. They did not. They taught
the first courses of it a very long time ago and have not touched a wall since.

The village keeps the rota, judges the disputes it generates, and regards the annual walling season as
the single most important thing that happens in the Vardain year – more important, they will tell you,
than anything that goes up the mountain.
