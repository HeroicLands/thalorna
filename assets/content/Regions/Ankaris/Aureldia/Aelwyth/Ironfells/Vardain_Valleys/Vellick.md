**Vellick** sits where the beck drops enough to drive hammers, and it is where the
[[lore-vardain|Vardain]] work metal. Five hundred and forty people, most of them in the trade or
married into it.

Vellick's smiths are the best human smiths in Aelwyth by a distance nobody bothers to dispute, and the
reason is not secret: some part of Khazári technique has been taught here, deliberately, over a very
long time. Not the deep craft – the Vardain are clear that what they have is the shallow end of it –
but enough that a Vellick blade or a Vellick spring is a different order of object from anything made at
[[place-dunmere|Dunmere]].

It comes with a condition, and the condition is absolute: **it is not taught onward**. No apprentice is
taken from outside the valleys, no Vellick smith has ever set up in a lowland town, and mainland offers –
which arrive regularly and are enormous – are refused without discussion.
