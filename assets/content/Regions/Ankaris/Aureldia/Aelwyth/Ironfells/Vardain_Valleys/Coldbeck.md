**Koldbek** stands on the fastest water in the valleys and uses all of it. Five hundred
people, four mills—two grinding, one fulling, one driving a set of trip-hammers that
[[place-vellick|Vellik]] built and Koldbek maintains.

The village mills for everybody and takes its multure in kind, which makes it modestly wealthy and
universally grumbled about, exactly as millers are everywhere. The difference here is that the Vardain
grumble with precision: Koldbek's tolls are fixed, published, unaltered in eighty years, and complained
about anyway.

Its other trade is the valleys' cloth—coarse, dense, weatherproof stuff, fulled at Koldbek and worn
by everyone in the Ironfells including, it is said, those who do not need to worry about weather.
