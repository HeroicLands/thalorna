**Barakth** lies highest of the inhabited valleys but one, closest to the road up to
[[place-vorgald|Vorgald]], and it is the oldest Vardain settlement – old enough that its name is
Khazári and nobody can say what it means.

Six hundred and twenty people, and the **tallies**. Every delivery owed up the mountain and every
consignment coming down is reckoned here before it reaches [[place-vargate|Vargate]], on tally-sticks
and in ledgers kept by four families who have kept them for longer than Aldorath has had kings. The
system is exact to a degree outsiders find unnerving, and the Khazári have never once queried a figure.

Barakth is also where a Vardain goes when a matter must be put to the lords. The tally-keepers do not
claim to speak for anyone; they simply know how to send a question up and are the only people who do.
