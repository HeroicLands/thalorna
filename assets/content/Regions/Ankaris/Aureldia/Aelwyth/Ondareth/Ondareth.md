## Overview

Ondareth is the land of the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]], along the western coast of [[place-aelwyth|Aelwyth]].

## Extent

The coast is good for fishing and moderate for farming, and the republic's wealth comes chiefly from
carrying trade along the western shore and from what it can charge others for access to it.
