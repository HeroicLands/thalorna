## Overview

Far into the interior forest, in the folds where the Peshtar country gives way to the wooded settlements of the more warlike tribes, a ring of enormous oaks surrounds a round dell sunk twenty feet below the forest floor. The tribes of that country call it **Nethermoot**. It was a meeting-place of the clans of the nine fords, where disputes of inheritance and bloodprice were heard under the oaks by the druids and war-chiefs together, and where the clans' dead were buried at the foot of the trees that now stand about the dell.

Nobody moots there. A thatched cottage of black timber sits against the dell's lower wall with a thread of smoke going up from its roof, and the paths into the hollow are kept clear. The trees are wrong. The oaks lean inward, and in the right light their bark shows a knot that is a face, then a second, then a ring of them.

## The Witch

**Nanwyth Crake** is a woman who looks sixty and has looked sixty longer than the clan elders have lived. She was a druid of the nine fords, trained in the mysteries of the stones and trackways and expelled from her clan for a binding she would not undo. She lives in the hollow with a black cat, a loom and a long table of jars. She sells cures, curses and the finding of lost things, and she is fair in her dealings: she says what each costs and takes only that.

What she takes is **years**. A cure for a fever costs a month, a curse on a rival costs a year, the finding of a lost child costs five. She takes them from the buyer or, if the buyer is generous, from a third party the buyer names. The years go into the oaks. The old dead of the clan, rooted in the trees since she bound them, are fed by them, and in return they answer her call. A dozen are warriors still. They stand at the dell's rim among the oaks, grey and silent, and they put the intruder to the question.

## What the Country Says

- **The clans of the nine fords.** Their war-chief, **Haldrec Vane**, says the witch is a thing of the old woods and that no one in the hollow should be troubled. His warriors leave her jars unpicked and go around the dell on the north side.
- **The border steadings of Aldorath.** Their shepherds say the witch can cure anything. The sick are carried there in the dark of the moon, and the carriers do not discuss the price.
- **The druids of the stone circles.** The druids of neighboring clans say Nanwyth Crake broke the oldest rule of their order, that the dead are given to the earth and no one else, and that her work will draw something worse than a witch to the hollow.
- **Sélmoros's few priests in the interior.** They say the dead in the trees are not at rest and that no rite exists for them, because the clan's rite was never done.

## Reaching It

The hollow lies a hard three days' walk into the interior forest from the nearest Aldorathi steading, along trackstone paths that are older than the clans. The Grukar hunt the same country, and a party keeps to the trackstones, which the Grukar are said to avoid. The oaks give no sign until the traveler is among them.
