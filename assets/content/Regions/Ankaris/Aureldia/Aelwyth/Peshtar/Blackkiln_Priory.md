## Overview

At Blackkiln Priory, on the south-eastern fringe of the [[place-pshtrwldrns|Peshtar Wilderness]], where the pine forest reaches the burned-over farmland of [[place-ondareth|Ondareth]]'s border, a ring of low stone kilns stands in a clearing with the shell of a chapel built against them. The charcoal-burners who first cut the clearing called the place **Blackkiln**, for the pits that were already black when they came. Their huts are fallen. The chapel's roof is gone, its east wall is split from foundation to gable, and the kilns are cold to the eye.

They are not cold. At the center of the largest kiln a fire burns without fuel, without smoke and without light, and the air above it ripples as over a forge. A hand held near it comes away blistered and a torch is put out by it. The pines within a bowshot are dead.

## The Cell

Two generations ago a **Flame-Warden** of [[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]], **[Gerolt Sarne]{.name data-type="being/npc"}**, built a priory at the kilns with the help of Ondarethan money. He promised the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]'s border magistrates that the fire would clear the Peshtar raiders from the pine forest, and he and his Scorchmasters burned the first raiding party they caught in the largest kiln. The fire took the offering, grew hotter and darker, and burned without charcoal.

The clans of the border came with the winter, in strength, and razed the priory. Sarne was taken alive and thrown into his own kiln. The fire did not go out. His Aspirants, the Acolytes of Ash, died in the chapel where the clans found them, and none were given the rites of the [[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]] or any rite at all.

[[lore-theshadow|The Shadow]] came into the kilns with the offering, and the fire is its door. The dead of the cell walk the clearing at night and tend it. The Black Flame's priesthood acknowledges no supreme authority, and no Flame-Warden has come to claim the place or to deny it.

## What the Country Says

- **The Ondarethan border farms.** The farmers say the kilns are haunted and keep their children indoors on the autumn nights. The Senate's border magistrates say nothing of Ondareth's money in the priory.
- **The Peshtar clans.** The clans that burned the priory say it should be left burning, and that the war-chief who disturbs it will lose his clan.
- **[[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]].** A smith-priest of the Sacred Forge at the nearest Ondarethan temple says the kilns were once a true forge of a mountain order, and that the first fire in them was a holy one. He asks that no one go there, and will say no more.
- **The charcoal-burners.** The few who still work the fringe say that the fire speaks at night, in a voice like a bellows, and that it knows a name.

## Reaching It

The clearing lies a day's walk into the pines from the Ondarethan border posts, along a charcoal road that the Peshtar do not use. The road is rutted, overgrown and marked every furlong with a stone cairn. Past the dead pines, the clearing opens and the smell of hot iron reaches the road before the chapel comes into view.
