**Wraymouth** stands where the Wray comes down to the sea on Aelwyth's south-eastern coast, and is the
second city of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]] at about fifteen thousand people.

[[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]] is the seat and holds the Synod; Wraymouth does the work. These are the
best natural harbours on the island, facing open water toward [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] and the
mainland beyond, and effectively all of Dúnavarre's trade — and a good share of Aelwyth's — passes over
its quays.

## The Port

Wraymouth handles the outbound wool, hides, salt-fish and worked goods of the kingdom's estates, and
takes in grain, wine, timber and manufactures from the mainland. Its shipping is unromantic and
extremely regular: Dúnavarre is an orderly realm and its port reflects it, with a harbour-master's
office that keeps records the Synod actually reads.

The city is also where Dúnavarre's diplomacy physically happens. Embassies to and from Élavendre,
Provènzia and the Vylarian ports land here, and the mission to Tānvür sailed from these quays.

## Character

Wraymouth is more mixed and considerably less decorous than Ravenmoor. Its harbour quarter speaks
several languages and a good deal of trade-creole, its shipping houses are commercial rather than
courtly, and the Synod's administration sits more lightly here than anywhere else in the kingdom — not
from weakness, but because a port that is generating the realm's revenue is granted a certain latitude.
