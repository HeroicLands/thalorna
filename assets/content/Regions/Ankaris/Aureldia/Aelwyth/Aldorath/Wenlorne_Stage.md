## Overview

The Crown Road that runs south from [[place-wyndmarch|The Wyndmarch]] toward [[place-hallowwood|The Hallowwood]] is whole for most of a day's walk beyond the last Aldorathi steading. Its fitted blocks are as true as when the Khazari masons of [[lore-jointcrown|The Joint Crown of Aelwyth]] cut them, and the oaks and beeches the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale Folk]] planted along it have grown into a roofed lane. About halfway down the whole stretch stands **Wenlorne Stage**, a square stone relay house with a gate-arch, a stable court and a tower at the north-west corner for the road-horn. The crown kept such houses a day apart so that a rider with a royal writ changed horses at each one and a traveler slept behind a barred door.

Nothing lives at Wenlorne. The roof is sound and the gate stands open. At dusk the iron lamp-posts that line the last mile of road are lit, one after another, from the stage outward, and burn with a steady pale light until dawn. Nobody has been seen to light them.

## The Reeve

Every stage had a **road-reeve**, an officer who answered to the crown for the safety of the road and the roll of those who slept under his roof. The reeve of Wenlorne held the stage to the last days of the [Great War]{.name data-type="lore/history"}, when the king was dead at [[place-threesisters|The Three Sisters]] and the crown's subjects were fleeing along the road in the dark: Vardain farmers, Khazari masons and the small Pelwar parties who had come over to the crown. The reeve took in everyone, shut the gate against the war, and swore by the crown that no one under his roof would come to harm before the road was safe. The road was not safe the following morning, or the one after. What happened inside the gate is not recorded. The stage was found empty and the gate closed, and a reeve's oath of that kind does not lapse at his death.

The oath was caught by [[lore-theshadow|The Shadow]]. What keeps the stage now is a bound dead thing in a reeve's grey coat, with the keys at its belt and the lamps in its keeping, and it keeps its oath as the Shadow reads it: no guest leaves while the road is unsafe, and the road is never safe. It lights the lamps to bring travelers in. Around it move the **porters**, the dead of the stage, who take coats and carry baggage and set out supper, and who believe the war is still going on.

## What the Countryside Says

- **The Wyndmarch drovers.** Carters who work the last Aldorathi steadings say there is a lit house down the old road that never charges for a bed. Two of them have gone to see and one has come back. He does not say what he saw.
- **The garrison at [[place-caerwynd|Kaerwynd]].** The Wyndmarch's soldiers count nine patrols that have taken the old road south and not returned. The commander has stopped sending them. He keeps the roll anyway.
- **[[lore-vardain|The Vardain]].** A Vardain drover who knows the Crown Roads says only that a stage should never be lit, since the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]] put out the lamps when they left a house.
- **The Hallowwood's edge.** The Sinale do not discuss the stage. Travelers who reach the wood's edge from the north by the road are turned, politely and without conversation, and sent back the way they came.

## Reaching It

From the Wyndmarch the old road starts at a ford below the last royal-forest lodge and runs south under the trees. A party on foot reaches Wenlorne in a long day. The road is the surest footing in the forest, and the forest on either side of it is wet, dense and held by Grukar, so the road is where a party stays. After dark the lamps begin.
