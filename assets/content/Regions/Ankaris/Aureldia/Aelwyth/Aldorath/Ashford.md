**Ashford** grew where the drove roads out of Aldorath's manorial country meet the ford, and it is the
kingdom's principal market for what that country produces.

Wool above all – Aldorath's estates run enormous flocks, and the clip is gathered, graded and sold at
Ashford before it goes to the coast – and with it cattle, hides, tallow and horn. The autumn drove fair
is the largest gathering of people in the realm outside [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]]'s court terms, and the
one occasion on which a serf may lawfully be a considerable distance from his manor.

## The Ford and the Bridge

There has been a bridge for two hundred years and the ford is still used, because the bridge tolls are
the town's second income and the drovers resent them. Disputes over this are the staple business of
Ashford's court and have generated a body of local custom of quite disproportionate complexity.

## Character

Three thousand two hundred people, more in fair-time, and a merchant class that is prosperous, entirely
unlanded and therefore of no account whatever in a kingdom where standing is land. Ashford's leading
families have money and no voice, which they mind, and which occasionally makes them interesting to
outsiders looking for a foothold.
