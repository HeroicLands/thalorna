**Dunmere** is the seat of the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]] and, at some twenty
thousand people, the only place in the realm that is a city rather than a market town. It stands inland
on the eastern side of Aelwyth, under a castle that has been extended by every king who could afford to.

Everything a feudal kingdom does is done here: the king's court and household, the assize, the exchequer
such as it is, the great fairs, and the perpetual attendance of lords who must be seen at court often
enough to keep what they hold. Dunmere's permanent population is swollen for months of the year by
retinues.

## The Court

Aldorath's aristocracy is landed, hereditary and genuinely powerful, and Dunmere is where that power is
negotiated. A king who wants anything done must obtain it from men whose estates are their real
interest, and the bargaining is conducted in hall, at table and in the yards outside – slowly, publicly,
and with an attention to precedence that visitors from [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Dúnavarre]] find
exhausting and revealing.

It is also, in the manner of Aelwyth, conducted with the entire seriousness of a great power. Nobody at
Dunmere has any working sense of how small Aldorath is.

## The Town

Below the castle: the market, the guild halls of trades that would be modest anywhere else, the wool and
hide staples that carry the kingdom's trade, and the cathedral of the
[[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Twelve]]. Beyond the walls, in every direction, the manorial country that
holds four in five of Aldorath's people.
