A carter bringing a load down the Tribute Road stops at the last imperial milestone outside [[place-valdun|Valdun]] to water his team, and a traveler from the south asks him what [[place-aureldirgn|Aureldia]] is. He answers in four sentences. "The Empire counted this road to the stone you are leaning on and not a mile past it. My kingdom keeps its own law and its own line of kings, and above the tree line it keeps nothing the Lodge has not allowed. Tarvénia elects its king, and not quietly, and Provénzia has a charter because it fought nine years for one. Eilwìth is one island and several realms, and Kalipsa pays its tax to nobody." It is a fair summary of the last five centuries, and the ages below are the long form of it, carried back to the years before there were crowns.

The lands this history follows are five. [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]] is the southern peninsula, three petty kingdoms, a free city and a free territory under an elected High King. [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] is the Pelwar coast, a lowland kingdom beside the tribal country of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] and their forests. [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia]] is the cultured southern coast, which is also a part of Midhalión and whose Vilérian side is told in [[lore-hstrymdhln|The History of Viléria and Midhalión]]. [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]] is the Misty Isle, and [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]] is the free port on its own island off Élavendre's north coast. Every year is a year of [[lore-commoncal|the Vilérian Calendar]], and the ages are the ones [[place-aureldirgn|the Aureldia Region]] counts. Where an event belongs to more than one land, or to the world, it is named here and linked, and its own note tells it. Each age closes on what it left, because a party arriving in any of these lands walks through what the ages built, closed and owe.

## The Elder Ground, before about 1400 BF

Before any people the histories name, the western woods were the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]]'s, and the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] kept halls cut into the high mountains. Around 3100 BF they cut [[place-vorgald|Vorgald]] into [[place-ironfells|the Ironfells]] of Eilwìth, the westernmost hold they ever placed and the only one reached across open sea, and brought across the farmers beneath it who became [[lore-vardain|the Vardain]] ([[lore-vorgaldcut|The Cutting of Vorgald]]). In 2427 BF the fall of Khazartúrn ended all dealing between the two elder peoples everywhere in the world ([[lore-fallkhazturn|The Fall of Khazartúrn]]), and Eilwìth was the exception: Senali and Khazari there still shared one island, and no other pair of their peoples shared a country at all.

They tried to mend what the breach had cost. In 1919 BF the Convoy of Six sailed from Élavendre to put the Second Voice into their joint keeping and was never seen again ([[lore-thecrossing|The Crossing]]). A little over three centuries later, about 1585 BF, the two peoples raised one crown over the island, held by a Senali king and governed by both ([[lore-jointcrown|The Joint Crown of Eilwìth]]). The Khazari cut roadbeds, bridge piers and the footings of towns, and the Senali raised avenues of trained oak and beech along them, so that neither people's work reads without the other's. How the offices were divided is not recorded, and no capital has been found. The crown lasted six centuries. On the mainland the stone circles, passage graves and trackstones belong to peoples nobody now names, and the ages do not try to name them.

**What it left.** Vorgald and the Ironfells, held for the Vardain. A straight paved road that ends at a bank of old oaks. Fitted stone and grown avenues on ground their makers no longer keep.

## The Coming Over, about 1400 to 984 BF

The Elder Ground gave way to people pushed from behind. [[lore-pelwarmigr|The Pelwar Migration]] drove whole peoples west across half a continent for three centuries until the land ran out at the western sea, and the bands it shed arrived in Aureldia as refugees. One band, led by the herdsman [Dhargan]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, came over the eastern pass of [[place-nelgan|Nelgan]] in snow about 1380 BF and starved through a winter at the edge of the Silver Forests that the people still call the Lean Year, watched by the Senali the whole time and welcomed by none ([[lore-nelgancross|The Crossing of Nelgan]]). Dhargan came back to the wood's edge with empty hands and sat until the Senali answered, and about 1350 BF the band was taught the rules of [[place-longstair|the Long Stair]], made its first pact with the river-mother [[lore-salendaspr|Sálenda]] at the lowest pool, and set the watch on the fog-hollow that became the Wardens ([[lore-lowestpoolpct|The Pact at the Lowest Pool]]).

The pact was tested about half a century later. About 1300 BF, in a killing winter, the chief [Ghalmir]{.name data-type="being/npc"} cut a stand of silver birch for fuel and pulled the watch off the hollow for the hands to haul it, and something came through. The hall made him Kinless, and his sister led the people back to the wood's edge to ask again ([[lore-blacksnow|The Black Snow]]). The asking was needed once more. About 1250 BF a war-band of the host that was taking the forest east of the mountains ([[lore-takingwood|The Taking of the Wood]]) came over Nelgan with brands, a second Dhargan stood the Áelendan at the trees and asked the Senali to take the children in, and the Senali walked out through the fire. The wood was given over to the Áelendan to keep, and the Wardens' charge widened from the groves to the passes ([[lore-firesnelgan|The Fires on Nelgan]]). The host that sent the war-band is dated across about 1250 to 1150 BF, and the fires are placed at its opening.

About 1200 BF refugees of the same migration reached Eilwìth and brought on the Great War with the elder kingdom, two centuries of arrival and reprisal. In about 984 BF the Senali king was killed at [[place-threesisters|the Three Sisters]], and the Senali left the field and the crown ([[lore-threesistrs|The Battle of the Three Sisters]]).

**What it left.** The Áelendan's reckoning of their people from a crossing and a pact, and the law that no standing silver birch is felled in their country. The sentence of Kinlessness, still the worst a tribe's hall can pass. The Wardens, whose charge covers the hollow, the groves and the passes. The Tradition Keepers, who hold the lore in one child's mouth and never in writing. A refugee people settled on the elder kingdom's island, and the Senali withdrawn into Seirasto.

## The Old Crowns, 984 BF to about 140 AF

With the Senali gone, the Khazari finished the Great War in a few seasons, about 980 BF, and did not take the island. They kept the Ironfells and the north-eastern plain for the Vardain who farmed it, and left everything else to the Pelwar they had fought, with no oath sworn and no line drawn. Nothing was written down, so the ending is reconstructed from where the lines came to lie ([[lore-endgreatwar|The Ending of the Great War]]). The works of the Joint Crown stood on that ground with no keepers. The Pelwar settled beside the roads, quarried the footings for their first walls and forgot who had laid them, and the realms that rose on the island afterward stand on that settlement.

On the mainland the first crown came from the lowland houses of the Berav basin, who were Áelendan by descent and had stopped being Áelendan by habit. About 560 BF they acclaimed [Táldoren]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the house of [Orethan]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, and before he held a court he went up to [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa]] and swore that the crown's writ stopped where the tribes' ground began ([[lore-firstcrown|The First Crowning of Élavendre]]). Every Élavendren monarch has sworn it since. In the south the [[lore-vylarirpbl|Vilérian Republic]] planted a garrison colony on the great road above the Provenzal shore about 110 BF, the Tanevar kings traded with it and sent their sons to learn its law, and when the Republic drew the garrison out about 80 BF the colony stayed and married into the country ([[lore-aurelianefound|The Founding of Aurelane]]). More than two centuries before the legions came, the west had a Vilérian town with a square grid in a land of river roads.

On Eilwìth a faction shed by a northern Grukar tribe crossed in the holds of trading ships about 20 BF, too few to be noticed ([[lore-grukarcomes|The Grukar Come to Eilwìth]]; the Grukar's first record anywhere is [[lore-grukarapp|The Appearing of the Grukar]]). Within a century they held the unfarmed third of the island, forest, bog and moor, and the forest peoples were at war with them when the eastern barons, who wanted a war-leader and a judge, crowned Drethar of Kingshollow in the chapel beside his hall about 120 AF ([[lore-kingshlwcrwn|The Crowning at Kingshollow]]). Aldorath's kings are still crowned there.

**What it left.** The Tree-Line oath, sworn at every crowning and the reason no royal magistrate enters Áelendan country unasked. Aurelane's grid and the Vilérian names that Provénzian great houses trace their descent to. A Grukar third of Eilwìth that the settled realms treat as routine loss. A chapel and a burial ground at Kingshollow, and a crown that rules by the consent of barons who are older than it.

## The Eagles in the West, about 140 to 418 AF

The legions came west from about 140 ([[lore-westernwars|The Western Wars]]). Between about 140 and 160 they took Tarvénia's southern coast and central lowlands, made [[place-valthari|Valthari]] an imperial port and held [[place-leonrik|Lyenrik]] as an occupied march watched by a chain of towers ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]). A governor who tried to rule the high meadows of [[place-eskarath|Eskarath]] directly lost his escort there, and the Empire did not replace him. The mountains were the next prize. About 143, in the same years as the occupation, the fourth expedition into Asturath was let into the gorge below [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] by the lord [Tórsaro]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who closed both ends, and the legion died there in two days; Viléria sent no fifth ([[lore-horngorgebtl|The Battle of the Horn Gorge]]). The Empire held the lowland approaches and left the mountains alone.

In the south the legions landed below Aurelane about 152, used the colony as a landing, a road and a gate, and broke the Tanevar kings on the shore of [[place-tiravellake|Lake Tiravel]]. The province of the West was made, with Aurelane its capital, and the Tanevar country was left its villages and the memory of governing itself ([[lore-tiravlenbattle|The Breaking of the Tanevar Kings]]). In the north a legion struck into the hills of Áuldalon between about 166 and 171, was harried to the forest margin and lost a cohort, and Queen [Réidane]{.name data-type="being/npc"} treated for Élavendre at Valdun: the kingdom kept its law and its line, paid a yearly tribute in silver, and took a Vilérian legate into a house at Péravel ([[lore-elvtribute|The Tribute of Élavendre]]). The Áelendan were no party to the treaty. The legate's door let the Arcane Order in, first as clerks and then as a school, and the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] was chartered about 230.

Faith split in the same century. About 220 the clergy of [[lore-ustarondty|Ústaron]] divided over whether the Forge-Lord's true nature was creative or destructive, and [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] and [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] have been enemies since ([[lore-ustaranschism|The Ústaran Schism]]). In the province of the West the governors allowed both, and about 248 the Flame's war-bands under the Ashlord [Hazarak Bloodhand]{.name data-type="being/npc"} burned the Forge's temples along the Tarvénian frontier and met the Forge's knights in the hills east of Keravel. Hazarak fell, a storm ended the fighting, and the Forge carried a grievance against an Empire that would not choose between the two faiths ([[lore-bloodhillsbtl|The Battle of the Blood Hills]]).

The tribute became a burden. About 320, after a run of bad harvests, King [Hóuradel]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Élavendre opened silver workings above the tree line in Sórvanda to meet it, in the mountains where his oath gave him no writ. The Wardens closed them in a single season, the tribes withdrew their guides from the whole interior, and the king came down and renewed the oath on the Lodge's terms. Every noble house then set the Bardic Colleges to prove its descent from the old blood. The closing is known from the Colleges' genealogies and the Lodge's account, not from a record of the year ([[lore-sorvandaclose|The Sórvanda Closing]]).

Before anything Kalipsa can date, the island's own tradition places two beginnings. It says refugees from a forgotten Helionite colony laid the stones of [[place-oldmole|the Old Mole]], and nobody can date them. Nordmen fishers and traders settled the harbor and gave the old city its tongue, and the oldest houses count their lines from them, [[affiliation-hsetvarnidrok|Tvarnidrok]] first, though nobody can say when. Only the next event has a place in the age: during the western conquests the port took the guild charter of [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]], paid the Empire its dues and kept its own Council. The age ended with the edicts that closed the conquests about 418 ([[lore-arzanasedict|The Edicts of Arzanas]]).

**What it left.** Lyenrik's towers, law and sense of precedence, and Valthari's harbor. The gorge below Lezkora, a blood-field, and the reason the High Kings sit there. The province of the West and Aurelane as its capital, and the milestones that stop at Valdun. A felzos's house that is now a University court, and the Order's foothold in Péravel. The enmity of Forge and Flame, and Provénzia's blood-fields, which the kingdom names by their ground and not by the war that made them ([[lore-bloodfields|The Blood-fields of Provénzia]]). Sealed adits above the tree line, and genealogies the Colleges keep. A Kalipsa that keeps its mole and its first settlers without dates.

## The Tribute Peace, about 418 to 512 AF

The west paid and was left alone. The Edicts also closed the Empire's willingness to be the court of last appeal, and the priesthood of [[lore-ardavondty|Árdavon]], the god of oaths and gates, saw the gap before the crowns did. About 421 it chartered a sworn order at [[place-olvestria|Ólvestra]] whose brothers ride to where a binding oath has been broken, hear the matter and compel the remedy ([[lore-sealedwordfnd|The Founding of the Sealed Word]]). The first petition it accepted was a boundary oath between two Tarvénian lords, and the brother who rode to hear it ruled in a day and was obeyed against the stronger.

About 440 the captains of Ondareth rose against their king. Tralkar, the last king of the western coast, taxed the carrying trade until it could not pay and hanged the captains who said so, and the levies under [Zalmen]{.name data-type="being/npc"} took Thornburi and threw his castle down ([[lore-ondarethfall|The Fall of the Last King of Ondareth]]). The captains needed a government that was not a king, and the only model they had was the [[lore-vylarirpbl|Vilérian Republic]] as shipmasters and factors had described it, a Senate of landholders and officers who held command for a year. They built it as it had been told, with mantles and Vilérian magistracies and a calendar of processions assembled from memory, and Ondareth has performed the forms in earnest since. The Republic they copied had been founded more than a thousand years before, and the link between it and the captains is a model and not a sequence.

Dúnavarre lost its crown more quietly. From about 450 until about 490 the advisors of the Arcane Order at Ravenmoor took the offices from the kings one by one, the treasury from the first, the courts from the second, and the harbors and the host from the third. The last king, Yenral, was set aside without a blow and the Synod governed as it had governed the day before ([[lore-dunavarresynod|The Mages Take Dúnavarre]]). Dúnavarre's chronicles tell this as natural evolution, Aldorath and Ondareth tell it as coercion, and the Synod has never been asked to open its records.

In the middle of these years, about 470, Kalipsa fought itself. A Harbormaster who would not give up the office brought on a civil war among the houses, fought in the harbor and the streets of the old city. The houses that won it gave the Harbormaster a single term with no second to follow, built the Sea-Law Hall so that the Sea-Law would be heard under a roof that belonged to no house, and have honored the rule since without exception. Tvarnidrok fought on the winning side ([[lore-calypsacivilwar|The Civil War of Kalipsa]]). No account gives the Harbormaster's name or the length of the fighting. The Tribute Peace left the west alone, and Kalipsa's war was a war among its own houses.

**What it left.** The master register at Ólvestra and the Council of the Seal, a license each crown is reluctant to confirm and unwilling to refuse. Ondareth's Senate, which has never been corrected against the original. Ravenmoor's empty chair and the Synod behind it. The single term, the Sea-Law Hall and its unbroken roll of hearings.

## The Falling-Away, 512 to about 619 AF

This age is counted from 512, the year [[lore-threewinters|the Three Winters]] began in the north, and not from the Empire's own decline, which the spine dates about 520 ([[lore-empirdclne|The Decline of the Vilérian Empire]]). The hunger drew the legions toward the northern raids and cut the pay of the soldiers left behind. From 519 to 530 the fleets of Nordheim went south each summer, to Provénzia's coast and to Élavendre's north shore ([[lore-southrnfleets|The Southern Fleets]]). On the north shore the Nordmen came in numbers and stayed to burn. [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]] burned more than once and was rebuilt on a firmer foundation each time, and King [Thélmoren]{.name data-type="being/npc"} swore that the crown would make no treaty with them. In the years after, about 535, the crown raised the fort at [[place-durmelen|Durmelen]] on the headland where the earliest landings struck, and the Great Fire on its tower led the chain of lamps that the shore's families had already begun to keep ([[lore-northshorefire|The Burning of the North Shore]]). Kalipsa met the same fleets differently. The houses built the navy on the tonnage levy, hung the harbor chain from the mole's head and raised the Lantern's tower, and the fleets that burned the shore passed the island by.

The Empire's lowland garrisons were under-strength and underpaid, and the lowlands stopped paying them. About 538 the occupied lowlands of Tarvénia stopped paying the Empire, the mountain lords met at Lezkora and elected [Garvaneth]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Asturath the first High King to drive out the garrisons, and the garrisons were out within seven years. He had no silver to pay the lords who had elected him, and he sold Valthari a charter of self-government for a yearly tribute ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]). The Liénriki turned their watchtowers from the highlands to the coast. About 556 the second High King, [Mórezan]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, led a host down the pass into the thinned province of the West, and the provincial levy held the road at Malnebris for a day and a night and broke ([[lore-malnebris|The First Great Raid]]). The border families on both sides took it as the beginning of a war nobody declared. The Western March calls it the Summer War, because it is fought only while the passes are open, and counts it from that raid ([[lore-summerwar|The Summer War]]).

The raids had shown how little the governors could defend the province. By about 609 it paid for a frontier it could not see in levies and taxes that rose every year, and its estates refused the governor a levy and then refused his legion. The war ran nine years, to about 618, and the Tarvénian hosts came down the passes every season of it. The Forge preached the cause from every temple, still angry over the Blood Hills, and the port cities held the coast. The peace gave the estates a king of their own choosing, [Ilarven Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, bound to a Charter of Rights, a Council of Peers and a levy only by consent ([[lore-charterwar|The War of the Charter]]). Nobody declared a kingdom, and the Provénzians date the Charter. About 619 the Empire recognized it and freed the crowns of Aureldia from its governors and its tax ([[lore-crownssettle|The Settlement of the Crowns]]). Élavendre's tribute lapsed with it, and Kalipsa has paid tax to nobody since. In the early seventh century [Hlurthann II]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of [[affiliation-kingdomlgn|Malagna]] married his crown's daughters into the island families, and the old houses have had the run of the Malagnan yards since.

**What it left.** Élavendre's oath of no treaty, retaken by each Castellan of Durmelen, and the chain of lamps from one fire to three. The elected High Kingship, a crown with no army and a vote that is rarely quiet. Valthari's bought charter, Lyenrik's towers turned to the coast, and Kemthoza at the head of the war pass, with Durvalis raised against it. The Charter and its Council. Kalipsa's navy, its chain, its Lantern and its tonnage levy.

## The Charter Century, about 619 AF to the Present

The first test of the Charter's crown came in 653, when Padran Lédravren died with no named heir and the Charter gave the Council no word on the succession. Two princes of the blood each held a party, one backed by the centralizing houses of the coast and one by the Tanevar country and the lords who had never loved the Charter. For two years the war was fought in [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]], on the road from the east. The Tarvénian host that came down the passes in the second year went through the vale unopposed, because there was no army to oppose it. [House Dorgal]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} chose the wrong prince and was broken. The moderate houses found a third man in the royal house, Dalenar Lédravren, confirmed the Charter and left the succession where it had been ([[lore-warprinces|The War of the Princes]]). Provénzia raised the fortress town of [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] below the passes to hold the road the host had used.

Every king after the war worked to strengthen the crown, and the last turn of that came with [[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]]. He took the crown in 688 and in the first months of his reign resumed the crown lands that the princes of Gefavel held by grant and took the great seal from the Gefavel prince, who was then Chancellor. The court calls it the Resumption, and Gefavel calls it the Taking. He died in 709 without a son, and his nephew [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] succeeded without a quarrel, the Peers, the cities and the great officers sending their oaths within the month ([[lore-velarikreign|The Reign of Vélarik III]]). Whether the writ of resumption went to the Council of Peers, as the Charter requires, is the question that remains open. In the same year, about 709, the hearth of the High Hall at Lezkora went out. The lords of the three Tarvénian kingdoms met to elect a High King, and each of the greater candidates could block the others and none could gather two voices in three. They gave their voices at last to [[being-ularrekgarr|Ularrek Garrundeta]], an old lord of Asturath's high valleys, whose house was known to keep an oath ([[lore-ularrekelect|The Election of Ularrek]]).

Beyond the Charter's reach, Aureldia's other lands met the Nordmen and the Empire's edges. In 660 the fleets of Nordheim took the Vardain plain in Eilwìth's north in one season, and the Vardain's old friendship with the Khazari of Vorgald was destroyed ([[lore-stormveldtkn|The Taking of Stormveld]]). About 680 a regent's council sent the Doran Hilvan into the Kervaron to enforce the boundary stones on the Áelendan clans, and the clans let the legion climb and closed the passes behind it ([[lore-twelfthloss|The Loss of the Twelfth]]).

The war on the island is the one still being fought. About 694 the Synod of Dúnavarre declared the Seilvri, a forest people of the western woods, a danger to the island, and the Order of the Just Blade took the field. It drove them out valley by valley, burning steadings and taking the passes, and about 703 several thousand of them, with their children, were gathered in the oldest of their woods and killed in three days. The ground is [[place-hushwood|the Hushwood]]. The survivors went west and up into [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]], and the war went with them ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]). The Synod calls it a necessary campaign against raiders, and the Seilvri call it the Hunting.

## What It Leaves Today {#what-it-left}

The year is 720, and each land is deciding something this year.

In Kalipsa the election is the quarrel. [[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]] has held the Harbormaster's office since the spring of 717, and her term ends at the spring sitting, where she may stand for nothing. [[being-knilthtvrn|Knilthorv Tvarnidrok]] leads the old houses and has the voices to win on last year's census. [[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]] leads the newer houses and has bought and ordered hulls all winter to take them away. The loss of three Norodari hulls in the autumn fog is before [[place-sealawhall|the Sea-Law Hall]], and whether [[affiliation-bondsyndcts|the Bond Syndicates]] pay their bonds decides whether the Norodari come to the census strong or short.

In Tarvénia the High King is old. Ularrek holds his court in the High Hall as the guest of the king of Asturath, [[being-alzemozold|Alzemo Zolduregi]], and the king of Lyenrik, [[being-zenotholtr|Zenoth Oltris]], means to stand at the next election while the lowland lords send their men up the road to Lezkora one and two at a time. The Western March goes down the passes into the Talote each season the snow allows.

In Provénzia [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] reigns over a Council of Peers that cannot find a majority. [[being-itrolaldrvn|Itrola Lédravren]], Princess of Gefavel, has a petition before the Council for her branch's grants and the great seal. The king keeps the resumed lands because they pay for his household, and he has no child. The Earl of Degavel and the Count of Atavel measure themselves by each other, and the Earl of Hárivren, who keeps the Black Flame openly, belongs to neither party and is being bid for by both.

In Élavendre the Arcane Order's chapter houses at the University of Péravel hold an influence over the courts and the schools that equals or exceeds the Bardic Circle's and the Grove Council's, and the Áelendan count the Order's welcome against the crown. Each Castellan of Durmelen takes the oath of no treaty with the Nordmen on the day he takes the fort, and the signal of three fires has not been lit in the present Castellan's lifetime.

In Eilwìth the Order of the Just Blade cuts its road deeper into Teilvari each summer under the Knight-Captain of the West, [[being-merraddovvrn|Sir Merrad Dovavren]], and the Seilvri bowmen take back what they can of the forest's edge each winter. About two thousand of the people are left. In Aldorath [[being-aldrikdrthng|King Aldrik]] asks the council of barons to accept his son as king-to-be, and the barons hold their levies back.
