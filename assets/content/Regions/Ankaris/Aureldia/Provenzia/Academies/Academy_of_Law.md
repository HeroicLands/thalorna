## Overview {#overview}

The **Academy of Law** is one of the three academies of [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]], with the [[affiliation-acadrhetrc|Academy of Philosophy and Rhetoric]] and the [[affiliation-acadmedcn|Academy of Medicine and Manuscripts]]. It sits beside the high courts, which keep the old imperial basilica, and it teaches the law those courts make: Vilérian law as received, amended and argued over at Aurelane. Every advocate who pleads before the high courts is licensed by it, and most of the justices were its students.

## What It Controls {#what-it-controls}

The academy controls the right to plead before the high courts. A party may speak for himself; anyone who speaks for another needs the academy's license, and the masters examine for it and withdraw it for a false pleading. The academy holds a charter of the crown of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]] that lets its masters choose their own Rector and license their own advocates. The crown names no master and licenses no advocate.

The academy has no say in a judgment. When a question is new, the [[being-solkimervtn|Justiciar]] may ask the masters for the academy's opinion, and the high courts weigh it as they weigh a learned advocate's. The [[lore-chrtrrghts|Charter of Rights]] is argued before the Justiciar whenever a party claims the crown has broken it, and the academy has taught the Charter as law since the peace that made it. A city's own court, a lord's court and the [[affiliation-wntrassize|Winter Assize]] in the march all have advocates the academy licensed.

## Ranks {#ranks}

A **student** is enrolled on the roll to hear the masters and to sit in the high courts as a listener. An **advocate** is a student the masters have examined and licensed to plead and to draw opinions for a fee. A **master of law** is an advocate the masters have voted into their number; he teaches, gives the academy's opinion when the justices ask for it and votes in the choice of the Rector. An advocate whose license is withdrawn is **sent down**, and he may not plead before the high courts again.

## Masters {#masters}

Rector
: [[being-kolmetgrdl|Kolmete Gredalis]], chosen by the masters for life. He keeps the roll and the charter, licenses advocates on the masters' vote and carries the academy's opinions to the Justiciar.

## History {#history}

The academy grew up around the provincial courts. When the Empire made Aurelane the capital of the province of the West after [[lore-tiravlenbattle|Tiravlen]], about 152 AF, the _therkoi_ tried the province's great cases in the basilica, and the advocates who pleaded there taught the next ones in its porches. In [[lore-charterwar|the War of the Charter]] the peace kept Aurelane's courts and academies and gave the crown to the coast. The academy has taught the Charter of Rights as the kingdom's first law since, and the high courts have never sat anywhere else.

## The Present Quarrel {#present}

**The salt loan.** The crown's loan against the salt revenue is before the high courts: whether a levy the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]] never voted can be called something else. The Justiciar has asked the masters for the academy's opinion, and the masters have given three. The advocates of the crown and of the Peers are drawing opinions for whoever pays, and Kolmete must carry one opinion to the Justiciar or tell him the academy has none.

**The recited record.** An inheritance suit between two old houses has reached the high courts, and one party has asked that the Nordland skald [[being-bldnstylgn|Balden Estilgon]], who keeps the family's recited record, be heard in evidence. No Provénzian court has taken a recitation as evidence. The masters are divided on whether a record nobody wrote can be read into one, and the other party has hired the academy's best advocate to argue that it cannot.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The skald's safety.** The party that does not want Balden heard would be glad if he left Aurelane before the hearing, and the advocate who wants him heard hires a party to see that he does not.
- **The missing opinion.** A master's draft opinion on the salt loan has gone from his rooms before it reached the Rector, and a copy is said to be at Jalnasdal.
