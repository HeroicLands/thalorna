## Overview {#overview}

The **Academy of Medicine and Manuscripts** is one of the three academies of [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]], with the [[affiliation-acadrhetrc|Academy of Philosophy and Rhetoric]] and the [[affiliation-acadlaw|Academy of Law]]. It teaches medicine, and it keeps the scriptorium where the manuscript and illumination trades grew up around the academies' need for books. Its physicians are licensed to practice across the kingdom, and its copyists make the books the other two academies teach from.

## What It Controls {#what-it-controls}

The academy controls two licenses and one archive. A physician with its license is admitted to practice in any chartered city, and a city's council asks for that license before it pays a physician to keep its poor. A copyist or illuminator admitted to the scriptorium works for the academy's commissions and is paid by the page. The archive keeps the academies' books and the copies of every opinion and oration the other two academies have sent to be copied.

The academy holds a charter of the crown of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]] that lets its masters choose their own Rector and license their own physicians and copyists. It copies for anyone who pays, and the crown, the Peers and the cities' councils are all its customers. The scriptorium keeps the [Dedication of the Apprentice]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} of [[affiliation-marendas|Marendas]] each autumn, when its new students are blessed into the trade.

## Ranks {#ranks}

A **student** is enrolled to hear the physicians or to learn the copyist's and illuminator's trades. A **licentiate** is a physician licensed to practice, or a copyist or illuminator admitted to the scriptorium for pay. A **master** is a licentiate the masters have voted into their number; masters teach, keep a part of the archive and vote in the choice of the Rector. One the Rector **sends down** may not practice or copy under the academy's license.

## Masters {#masters}

Rector
: [[being-cholsamtlm|Cholsama Tulemis]], chosen by the masters for life. She keeps the roll, the charter and the keys of the archive, licenses on the masters' vote and takes the scriptorium's commissions.

## History {#history}

The academy began as the physicians' school of the provincial capital. After [[lore-tiravlenbattle|Tiravlen]], about 152 AF, the Empire governed the province of the West from Aurelane, and its legion and its officials needed physicians, and its courts and schools needed books. The copyists who served the schools of rhetoric and of law set up beside the physicians, who had the only rooms in the basilica quarter dry enough for a library, and the two trades have kept one academy since. In [[lore-charterwar|the War of the Charter]] the peace kept Aurelane's academies, and the scriptorium copied the Charter of Rights for every chartered city.

## The Present Quarrel {#present}

**The salt loan.** Every opinion and oration on the crown's loan against the salt revenue comes to the scriptorium to be copied fair, for the crown's lawyers, for the Peers' and for every chartered city that wants to know what is being argued. The copyists are working by lamplight, and Cholsama has refused to give either side a sight of the other's copies before they are collected.

**The account of Sirmenne.** A scholar's account of [[place-sirmenne|Sirmenne]] has gone the rounds of the academies in the scriptorium's copies, and the treasure-seekers in the borderland woods carry them. The commission came through a factor, and it paid for more copies than the academies could read. Cholsama wants to know who wanted the account read so widely, and why ([[scenario-engmsrmnn|The Enigma of Sirmenne]]).

:::secret {title="Who paid for the copies"}
The factor is the Bataren bookseller who keeps the shop below [[being-zdmdrvs|Zedime Diravis]]'s rooms, and he bought the copies for Zedime, the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]'s magister at Bataren, who let the account circulate to bring treasure-seekers into the woods while two of the Order's men went into the ruin by night. The scriptorium's commission book names the factor, and the factor's own book names his client.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The commission book.** Cholsama wants a party to find the factor who paid for the copies of the account of Sirmenne, and to learn who he paid for.
- **A physician for the march.** The Warden of the Talote has asked the academy for a physician to keep the garrison at [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] through the raiding season. No licentiate has volunteered, and the Rector needs one escorted there who will go.
