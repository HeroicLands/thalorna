## Overview

Atavel is a county of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]] in the western basins of the [[place-talothe|Talote]], below the mountains and behind the frontier, with some two hundred thousand people. Its count, [[being-velzoreaitv|Velzore Aitevis]], is Chancellor of the realm and a politically embattled man. He fights on two fronts at once: inside his own family, where his brother disputes the inheritance, and at court, where he is the Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]]'s great rival.

## The Land {#the-land}

The county is basin country: broad, well-watered farmland and market villages in the lower ground of the march, sheltered by the uplands to the east and far enough behind the passes that the raiding season seldom reaches it. Its eastern edge meets [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]], and its trade goes up the vale's roads to the frontier and down to the south. It feeds more of the march than any other county, and the levy that musters at [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] eats its grain.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The count holds Atavel of the crown directly and sits for it in the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]]. He governs it from [[place-atavelcstl|Atavel Castle]] through his steward and his manor courts, and he is seldom there himself: the seal keeps him at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] for most of the year. Under their father's will his younger brother holds the county's richest manors, and the brother's household is the county's second court.

## Current Situation {#present}

The count is losing ground at home while he holds it at court. His kin dispute the inheritance among themselves, his brother has hired a notary at [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]] to draw a case on their father's will, and each faction in the county looks for friends in the Council of Peers, where the Earl of Degavel is glad to be one. In Council the count presses the [[affiliation-hsepelanvri|Pélanvri]] over the bandits in [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]], because Atavel's caravans are among those taken there and every one is a failure of the earl's brother, the Marshal. Each man needs the other to fall first.

## History {#history}

[[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]] gave the great seal to Velzore Aitevis in 704 AF, and from then the rivalry between Atavel and Degavel took the shape it has in the Council now ([[lore-velarikreign|The Reign of Vélarik III]]). Velzore's father divided the county by his will, and the quarrel between his sons began at his death.
