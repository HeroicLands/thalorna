## Overview

Torvila Atavel is the castle town of [[place-athavel|Atavel]], a county of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]] in the western basins of the [[place-talothe|Talote]]. Some eighteen hundred people live below the castle on the county's market road, and the town lives on the grain of the basins and the carts that take it to the march. It is the seat of [[being-velzoreaitv|Velzore Aitevis]], Count of Atavel and Chancellor of the realm, who is seldom in it.

## History {#history}

The castle is the counts' seat and the place the county's courts sit. Velzore's father divided the county by his will, leaving the richest manors to his younger son, and since his death the town has had two courts: the count's, kept in the castle by his steward, and his brother's, kept in the brother's manor house across the market. [[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]] gave Velzore the great seal in 704 AF, and from then the count has governed the county from [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] by letter.

## Current Situation {#present}

The town is choosing sides. The brother's notary at [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]] is drawing a case on the will, and the brother's household is in the market every day buying goodwill with the manors' revenue. The count's steward holds the castle, the courts and the county's records, and he answers to a count who reads his letters twice and comes home only at harvest. Every family of standing in the town has been asked by one side or the other which heir it will swear to.

## The Holder {#holder}

The count holds the castle and the county of the crown directly. His steward keeps both in his absence, holds the manor courts in his name and sends the county's accounts to Jalnasdal every quarter.

## Within the Walls {#within-the-walls}

The castle's hall holds the county court, its muniment room the county's charters and the father's will, and its granary the count's share of the basins' grain, bought every autumn by the victualers of [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]].

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The will in the muniment room.** Both heirs want a sight of their father's will, and the steward will show it to neither without the count's order.
- **The brother's notary.** The count wants the notary's draft before it reaches the high courts at Aurelane.
