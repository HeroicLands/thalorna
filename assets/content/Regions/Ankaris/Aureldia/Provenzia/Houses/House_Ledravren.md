## Overview {#overview}

**House Lédravren** is the royal house of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]. It was a house of the [[place-korvathis|Korvatis]] with its lands on the southern coast when the estates chose one of its sons, [Ilarven Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, to hold the [[lore-chrtrrghts|Charter of Rights]] at the end of [[lore-charterwar|the War of the Charter]], and it has held the crown under the Charter since. The house reigns at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]], where Ilarven settled his court because the money was there.

The crown is the house's, and the Charter is the crown's limit. Every king of the house has reigned under it, and every king since [[lore-warprinces|the War of the Princes]] has worked to make the crown stronger inside its forms.

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

The head of the house is the king, [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]], who succeeded his uncle [[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]] in 709 AF. He is the son of Vélarik's younger brother and was raised a prince of the blood with no expectation of the crown. He has no child and has named no heir.

### The Gefavel Branch {#gefavel-branch}

The princes of Gefavel descend from a younger son of the house. They held crown lands by grant and, in the last prince's time, the great seal as Chancellor, and they hold Gefavel in the [[place-sathvothe|Satvote]] from [[place-gefavelcstl|Gefavel Castle]]. In 688 AF Vélarik III resumed the grants and took the seal, and the branch lost its standing at court ([[lore-velarikreign|The Reign of Vélarik III]]). The court calls it the Resumption, and Gefavel calls it the Taking.

The branch's head is [[being-itrolaldrvn|Itrola Lédravren]], Princess of Gefavel, who has held it since her father died without recovering either the lands or the seal. She sits in the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]] by right of her blood and keeps a house at Jalnasdal for its sittings.

## Holdings {#holdings}

- **The crown lands**, which pay for the king's household, the lands resumed from the Gefavel branch among them.
- **The house's own lands on the southern coast of the Korvatis**, which it held before it held the crown.
- **The court at Jalnasdal**, where the king reigns and the Council of Peers sits with him.
- **Gefavel and Gefavel Castle**, held by the Gefavel branch of the crown directly.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

**The Taking.** Itrola Lédravren's petition to the Council of Peers holds that Vélarik III resumed her branch's grants by writ without the Council's counsel, which the Charter requires for the crown's grants. Kralen keeps the lands because they pay for his household, and he keeps the Chancellor his uncle appointed, [[being-velzoreaitv|Velzore Aitevis]], who holds the seal the branch lost. The house's quarrel with itself is now the Council's business.

**The succession.** The king has no child, and the Charter gives the Council no ruling on who follows him. The last king of the house who died without naming an heir left the question to two princes of the blood, and they fought [[lore-warprinces|the War of the Princes]] over it. The Gefavel branch is of the blood, and its head sits in the Council that would have to settle the question.

**The money.** The frontier, the ships and the court cost more than the crown takes. The king would rather borrow against the salt revenue of [[place-lizela|Lizela]] than ask the Council for a levy, and every loan the house takes from the coastal houses is a debt it owes outside the Council's reach.

## History as Told {#history}

The estates fought the _therkos_ of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] for nine years, and the peace gave them a Charter that needed a holder. They chose [Ilarven Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"} from among themselves, and he took the crown about 618 AF on the Charter's terms. He kept the estates together by letting each keep its faith and by sharing power between the great houses and the local lords, and the house has held to that tolerance since.

[Padran Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"} reigned from 647 and died in 653 without naming an heir. Two princes of the blood each held a party, one backed by the centralizing houses of the coast and one by the Tanevar country and the regional lords, and the house fought itself across [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] for two years. The moderate houses ended it by crowning a third man of the house, [Dalenar Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who confirmed the Charter and left the succession where it had been.

From Dalenar on, each king of the house strengthened the crown inside the Charter's forms, through offices, marriages, loans and the placing of relatives. [[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]] came to the crown in 688 and took the grants and the seal from his own cadet branch in his first months. He died in 709 without a son, and his nephew Kralen succeeded him without a quarrel.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The nearest blood.** A Peer of the Atavel party wants a party to find out, quietly, which princes of the blood stand nearest the crown, and who among them is in debt to whom.
- **The writ.** [[being-itrolaldrvn|The Princess of Gefavel]] needs a sight of Vélarik III's writ of resumption, which the Chancellor keeps at Jalnasdal, before her petition is heard.
