## Overview {#overview}

**House Kórisvar** is a minor noble house of [[place-lizela|Lizela]], the salt town on the southern coast of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]. Its wealth rests on coastal trade and the salt pans east of the town rather than on broad land, which puts it in an awkward rank: rich enough to matter in the district and not landed enough to matter at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]]. It survived the long contest between the crown and the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]] by declining to be interesting to either.

The house has sent its younger children into service as heralds and secretaries for generations, and its cadet lines live in the towns of the march as well as on the coast.

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

The head of the house is [[being-arnelkrsvr|Arnela Kórisvar]], who inherited in 704 AF from her father, [[being-lordhemekoris|Dolvere Kórisvar]], a cautious man who left her an estate that was solvent and unremarkable. She sits on Lizela's charter council, chairs its harbor commission and keeps a small school of navigation, natural philosophy and commercial law, and she has made a house that spent generations avoiding notice interesting to everyone. She has never married, and the main line has no heir.

The cadet lines serve. [[being-arislkrsvr|Arisla Kórisvar]] is the herald of the Warden of the Talote at [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]], and her cousin [[being-chynrkrsvr|Chinara Kórisvar]] salts the garrison's meat and fish in a salting-house inside the walls there.

## Holdings {#holdings}

- **The manor on the hill above the harbor** at Lizela, with a handful of tenant farms.
- **Fishing rights along the coast**, and Lizela's share of the fishing compact with [[place-kalveza|Kalveza]] that Arnela brokered.
- **A share of the salt pans** east of the town.
- **A seat on Lizela's charter council**, which nobody coveted until Arnela held it.
- **The school**, small and practical, which Arnela founded out of the estate's income.
- **A modest townhouse at Jalnasdal**, where Arnela stays when she represents Lizela at court.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

**The salt loan.** The crown's Treasurer has asked the coastal houses for a loan against the salt revenue, and Lizela's pans are the salt revenue. A loan the Council never voted is a levy by another name under the [[lore-chrtrrghts|Charter of Rights]], and Lizela's answer places the house in the contest it has always kept out of. Lord [[being-aurentheorth|Aurente Ortemandis]], head of the oldest family in the town, means to answer whichever way Arnela does not.

**The father's debts.** A merchant from [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel]] has come to Lizela with papers saying Dolvere Kórisvar borrowed heavily near the end of his life to cover losses his daughter never knew of. He wants payment or the estate, and the papers may be genuine or forged.

**The patron.** [[being-kiramailthor|Kirama Iltoris]], the reformist countess, is the house's patron in the Council, and she uses Arnela to carry what she cannot champion herself. The chartering of two more towns is the next thing she has asked for.

**The compact.** Kalveza's charter council has retained an advocate to challenge the fishing compact before the high courts at [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]].

**The march.** Chinara's barrels in the garrison's winter store at Durvalis have begun to spoil, carrying a mark she says is forged, and Arisla carries the Warden's word over a pass where a ransom is still unanswered ([[scenario-hndsrdngssn|Hands for the Raiding Season]]).

## History as Told {#history}

The house built its wealth on coastal trade and the pans and kept it by keeping its head down. Through the long maneuver between the crown and the Council it stood aside and sent its younger children into the households of greater lords as heralds and secretaries. Dolvere Kórisvar was the last head to keep that course, and he died at fifty-nine with the house intact and unnoticed. His daughter used the estate's modest income within a year to found the school, and the house has been noticed since.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The answer to the Treasurer.** Arnela must give Lizela's answer to the crown's loan before the council votes, and she wants a party to find out which of her council colleagues is selling her letters to Aurente.
- **Debts of the father.** The Taruzel merchant's papers must be proved or broken before the house's enemies on the council learn of them.
