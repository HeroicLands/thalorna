## Overview {#overview}

**The Pélanvri** are the house of the Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]] and the greatest of the houses that hold the country between the cities of the south of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]. They have held Degavel and [[place-degavelcstl|Degavel Castle]] of the crown for generations, and they carry the frontier as well as the earldom: the house's sons go from the castle to the Western Army. Their colors are worn at Degavel, at [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]] and now at [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]].

A Pélanvri who holds land of a kinsman writes his seat after the house's name, so the Marshal is _al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel_, of the Pélanvri at Torvila Keravel.

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

The head of the house is [[being-rutalplnvr|Rutal Pélanvri]], Earl of Degavel and First Peer of the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]], who spends the Council's sittings at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] and the seasons between at Degavel Castle.

His younger brother, [[being-jarvelplnvr|Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]], is Marshal of the Western Army and liege of the lesser lords of [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]]. Their kinsman [[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren]] holds Belsaren of the Marshal, given him two summers ago when the Marshal deposed its Tanevar house.

The house's clients reach the coast. [[being-lrrmtlms|Lorrame Tulemis]] of [[place-valarencity|Válaren City]] heads a merchant house that has prospered for two generations on the Pélanvri custom.

## Holdings {#holdings}

- **The earldom of Degavel** and **Degavel Castle**, held of the crown directly, where the southern levy musters.
- **The command of the Western Army** at Torvila Keravel, which the crown gives and can take away.
- **The lordship of Belsaren** in Keravel Vale, held of the Marshal, with its timber and ore.
- **The office of First Peer**, which the Peers give for a year at a time.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

**Atavel.** The Count of [[place-athavel|Atavel]], [[being-velzoreaitv|Velzore Aitevis]], holds the great seal as Chancellor and heads the party that would take the office of First Peer from the earl. He needs one more voice at the year's first sitting, and the [[being-avaarlzlvrn|Earl of Hárivren]] has not declared. Atavel's agents ask in Council why the Pélanvri cannot keep their own roads, and ask in Degavel's market which of the earl's tenants have complaints.

**The vale.** The bandits of Keravel Vale strike hardest at Belsaren, the lordship the Marshal gave to a kinsman. The Marshal is answerable for the vale and Tarlas must end the raids before autumn, and every raid is a speech for Atavel in the Council.

**The deposed house.** The heir of the Tanevar house of Belsaren, [[being-diravekldrm|Dirave al Kaldremis]], leads the vale's outlaws against the Pélanvri who took his lordship, and he says the treason charge that deposed his house was a lie.

:::secret {title="The Belsaren charge"}
The treason charge against Dirave's house was fabricated. The Marshal brought it to put Belsaren's timber and ore in Pélanvri hands, and it would not survive an honest hearing ([[scenario-bldshscrvlvl|Blood and Ashes in Keravel Vale]]).
:::

## History as Told {#history}

The Pélanvri tell their history as a list of what they have held: Degavel and its castle, of the crown, for generations; a son at the frontier in every generation; and the First Peer's office whenever the Council would give it. The rivalry with Atavel took its present shape in 704 AF, when [[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]] gave the great seal to the Count of Atavel and not to a Pélanvri ([[lore-velarikreign|The Reign of Vélarik III]]). The house has spent the years since making the Council the place where the seal is answered.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The courier from the vale.** A letter from the Marshal to the earl has not reached Degavel Castle, and the household wants to know who on the road took it.
- **The tenants' complaints.** Atavel's agents are gathering grievances against the earl among his own tenants, and the earl's steward wants them found.
