# Appearance {#appearance}

Vélarik III was a heavy, broad man of 5'9", olive-skinned and ruddy, with gray eyes, graying brown hair and a heavy jaw. In his last years an illness left his left eyelid drooping, and the court portraits painted after it show him in three-quarter face. He died at sixty-four.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Died 709 AF, aged 64. King of Provénzia from 688. Wanted a crown that answered to its own revenue and not to its cousins.**

Vélarik III of [[affiliation-hseledravren|House Lédravren]] reigned over the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]] from 688 AF until his death in 709, from [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]]. He came to the crown at forty-three, a grown man with a settled opinion of the great houses, and he spent his reign making the crown less dependent on them.

His first act set the reign's course. In 688 he resumed the crown lands that the princes of [[place-gwefavel|Gefavel]], a cadet branch of his own house, held by grant, and he took the great seal from the Gefavel prince who held it as Chancellor ([[lore-velarikreign|The Reign of Vélarik III]]). The lands paid for his household for twenty years, and the Gefavel branch lost its standing at court. The prince died without recovering either, and his daughter [[being-itrolaldrvn|Itrola Lédravren]] holds Gefavel now and wants both back.

Vélarik had no son. His nephew [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] succeeded him in 709 without a quarrel and kept the resumed lands.

## Psyche

### Personality

Vélarik was blunt for a Provénzian king, and the court remembered it. He read every account himself and asked for the figures before the argument, and he let a petitioner finish and then said no without softening it.

### Motivation

He wanted the crown to pay its own way. Each king since [[lore-warprinces|the War of the Princes]] had worked to strengthen the crown inside the [[lore-chrtrrghts|Charter of Rights]], and Vélarik did it with land and offices rather than with argument in the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]].

### Strengths

He understood revenue, and he was patient with the Council: he never asked it for a levy he could do without, so it had little to refuse him.

### Weaknesses

He made enemies of his own blood to do it, and he left no son. The Gefavel grievance and an open succession are both his legacy.

## Social

### Companions

He raised his nephew Kralen at court in his last years and gave him a place on every commission that mattered.

### Patrons

He answered to the Charter and to no patron.

### Enemies

The princes of Gefavel never forgave the resumption, and Itrola Lédravren's petition to the Council for the lands and the seal is the quarrel he left behind.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Vélarik held the crown as King from 688 AF until his death in 709.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The writ of resumption.** The sealed writ by which Vélarik resumed the Gefavel grants lies in the Chancellor's archive at Jalnasdal. The Princess of Gefavel's lawyers want a sight of it, because she believes it was issued without the Council's counsel, and a writ the Charter required counsel for may not stand.
2. **The king's own accounts.** Vélarik kept his own books of the crown's revenue. A clerk of the late king's chamber has them, and both the Treasurer and the Earl of Degavel want them for opposite reasons.
