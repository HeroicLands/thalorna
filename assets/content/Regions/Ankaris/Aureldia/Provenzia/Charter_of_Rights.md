## Overview

The Charter of Rights is the constitution of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]. The estates of the old province of the West won it in [[lore-charterwar|the War of the Charter]], which ended about 618 AF, and it was a settlement rather than a victory: the estates agreed to a king so that the Charter would have a holder, and chose one from among themselves, [Ilarven Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}. The [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] recognized it, and its chancery stopped sending _therkoi_ to the province ([[lore-crownssettle|The Settlement of the Crowns]]). Nobody declared a kingdom. The Provénzians do not date one; they date the Charter.

Every king since Ilarven has held the crown under it, and [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] holds it now. A Provénzian names his city before his family and the Charter before his king.

## The Clauses

The Charter is short, and its force lies in what each clause forbids the king to do alone.

**Counsel.** The king takes the counsel of a [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]], drawn from the greater nobility who hold of the crown directly. In practice its counsel is required on war, on the great offices and on the crown's grants of land and office, and an act the Charter puts to the Council is void without it.

**Consent to levy.** The king levies money and men only by the estates' consent. The crown asks for a levy in the Council of Peers, where the deputies of the chartered cities sit with the Peers to give or refuse it. The king's own estates, tolls and dues are his without asking.

**The cities' law.** The cities keep their own law. Each holds its charter, the _daktela_, a grant written and sealed, which gives it its chartered rights and its self-governance on local matters, and the crown owes each city the Charter in return for its levy and its tolls.

**The received law.** The law of the realm is the law of the province as received, and the high courts that kept it at [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]] keep it still. A claim that the crown has broken the Charter is heard there, before the Justiciar.

## What It Does Not Say

The Charter says nothing about the succession. It made a king for the estates and gave his house the crown, and it left the question of who follows a king who dies without a clear heir to nobody. That silence was fought over in [[lore-warprinces|the War of the Princes]], from 653 to 655 AF, and the compromise king who ended the war confirmed the Charter and left the question where it was. Kralen Lédravren has no child.

The Charter also does not speak for the country. The Tanevar of the old valleys hold that it was the cities' charter and that the country was not asked, and they keep that grievance in every generation ([[lore-provenzianclt|Provénzian]]).

## How It Is Kept

The sealed charter is kept in the archive of the high courts at Aurelane, and the Chancellor keeps a sealed copy with the crown's writs at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]]. Every king swears to it at his crowning, and every city keeps its own _daktela_ beside a copy of it. Lawyers argue its clauses before the Justiciar, and Peers quote them in the Council. The [[being-itrolaldrvn|Princess of Gefavel]]'s petition against the resumption of her branch's grants is an argument from the clause on counsel.
