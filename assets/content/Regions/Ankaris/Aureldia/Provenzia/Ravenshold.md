## Overview {#overview}

Ravenshold is a county of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]] in the [[place-korvathis|Korvatis]], with some hundred and fifty thousand people, and its lord, **[[being-melvrelvns|Melvare Elvanis]], the Count of Ravenshold**, is a noble of considerable influence. The county gives him wealth enough to be generous and standing enough that his private matters are worth protecting. He is a patron of the theater and of performers, as a great Provénzian is expected to be, and is seen at the performances of [[affiliation-prvnzthtrclctv|the Provénzia Theater Collective]] in the season.

## The Land {#the-land}

The county lies in the warm country behind the southern coast, inland of [[place-lizela|Lizela]] and its salt flats, with the earldom of [[place-degavel|Degavel]] to the east. It is good land, of vineyards, orchards and horse pasture, and it is the reason the count is rich.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The count holds Ravenshold of the crown directly and sits for it in the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]]. He lives at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] for most of the year, and his steward keeps the county from [[place-ravensholdcstl|Ravenshold Castle]].

## Current Situation {#present}

The count's standing at court is his chief asset, and he guards it. He holds aloof from the quarrel between the Earl of Degavel and the Count of [[place-athavel|Atavel]], and both men court him for it. A count who can be relied on by neither side is valuable to both, and his discretion is what makes him so. Envoys of both parties call at the castle as well as at Jalnasdal, and the steward gives them all the same answer.

:::secret {title="The count's arrangement"}
For four years the count has kept an arrangement with [[being-kelnstylgn|Kelena Estilgon]], a courtesan of [[place-pirnebris|Pirnebris]]. It is more transaction than intimacy, though he is fond of her: he provides substantial financial support, and she provides her company and her discretion about his private affairs. What she knows of those affairs would cost him his place between Degavel and Atavel if either learned it.
:::

## History {#history}

The counts of Ravenshold hold the county of the crown. The present count inherited it young and has held it for more than twenty years, through the last of [[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]]'s reign and the whole of [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]]'s, without once committing his vote to a party.
