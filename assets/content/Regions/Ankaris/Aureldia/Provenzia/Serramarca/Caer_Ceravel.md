## Overview

Caêr Céravel is the castle town that anchors [[place-ceravelvale|Céravel Vale]], the place every road in the vale eventually leads to and every lord in it answers to. Its castle is the command of **Khiráta Jârvel al Pélanvri nâ Caêr Céravel**, the Élgâr of Céravel Castle and **Marshal of the Western Army** of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provènzia]], and a petitioner who wants the vale's justice comes here to get it.

## The Marshal's Command

Jârvel is brother to the Earl of [[place-degavel|Dégavel]], and his charge is the kingdom's frontier with [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]]: he manages the defenses against Tarvénian incursion and the banditry that thrives along a contested border. He is also the liege of the vale's lesser lords, and he uses that power freely. He deposed the Thânevar family that held [[place-belsaren|Bélsaren]] on a charge of treason and installed **Târlas al Pélanvri** in its place, and Târlas's future there depends on whether he can end the bandit trouble before the Marshal decides someone else would do it better.
