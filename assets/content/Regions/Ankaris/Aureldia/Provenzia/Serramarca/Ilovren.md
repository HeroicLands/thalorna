## Overview

Ilóvren is a lordship of [[place-ceravelvale|Céravel Vale]] and the seat of **Nâlor Kílan alrí Ilóvrel nâ Ilóvren**, a calculating noble with a fortified stronghold and ambitions that reach well past his own borders. To the rest of the vale he is a local lord like any other.

## Its Lord's Game

Kílan is a secret sympathizer of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]]. He has been manipulating the quarrel between the deposed lord of [[place-belsaren|Bélsaren]] and the new one, supplying the outlaw Thárion with resources and false intelligence to keep the raids going, so that the Marshal will remove Bélsaren's lord and accept Kílan as the man who can restore order. What he wants from Bélsaren is its timber, its minerals and its road into the **Blood Hills**, where he believes a Black Flame artifact lies hidden. His ally is **Kâldin al Lénavel nâ Léravren**, lord of [[place-leravren|Léravren]], who has supplied him with Black Flame soldiers.

A message that asks a stranger to come alone to the grove near Ilóvren on the night of the new moon, offering proof of Kílan's lies, is the kind of message the vale's politics produce. Whether it is an offer or a trap depends on who sent it.
