## Overview {#overview}

**Darvena**, the deep wood, is the forest that closes the western sixth of the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]]'s northern edge. East of it the frontier is mountain all the way to the Tarvénian corner. Here it is trees: old oak and beech on low, wet ground, from the cliffs of the cold ocean inland to where the ground rises into the northern mountains. On the far side the forest gives way to the worked woodland of [[place-lirve|Lirve]] around [[place-liranel|Liranel]] in the [[place-elavendre|Élavendre Region]].

One road crosses it, [[place-darvenaroad|the Darvena Road]], from [[place-batarencity|Bataren City]] to Liranel. It is the chief trade road between the two kingdoms.

## The Land {#the-land}

The forest is three days deep on the road. Its floor is leaf-mould and standing water, and its streams run west to the ocean in brown, slow channels. The trees stand close, and under them the light is green in summer and dim the rest of the year. Away from the road there are no paths a stranger can follow, only the tracks of deer and pigs and the rides the charcoal-burners cut for one season and let close.

## Who Uses It {#who-uses-it}

**The road's traffic.** Carters, drovers, pack-trains and the merchants who travel with them, going north with salt, wine and oil and south with Élavendren manufactures, furs and timber.

**The woodmen of Bataren's hinterland.** Charcoal-burners, pig-keepers and timber-cutters work the southern margin under the crown's leave, and the timber goes down to Bataren's yards.

**The border families.** Élavendren settlers of the borderland live in clearings along the southern edge, and their kin live on the far side.

## Dangers {#dangers}

**Losing the road.** The forest looks the same in every direction once the road is out of sight, and a traveler who leaves it to hunt or to find water can walk in circles for days.

**The wet.** In autumn and spring the low ground floods, the road goes to mud between its causeways, and a loaded cart can stand a day in one place.

**Men.** A pack-train carries goods worth stealing through three days of trees, and the forest hides those who would steal them.

## Converging Interests {#present}

More freight is going overland this season. Since the pilots of Bataren doubled their fees over the ghost ships in the [[place-batarengulf|Gulf of Bataren]], merchants who would have shipped north are sending their goods by the Darvena Road instead, and the road is busier than it has been in years. The toll-hut in the forest is taking more than it ever has, the woodmen of the margin are selling fodder at summer prices, and the Élavendren and Provénzian carrying houses of Bataren are each trying to take the new trade before the other can.
