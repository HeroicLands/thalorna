## Overview {#overview}

**The Darvena Road** is the road between the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]] and the [[place-elavendre|Élavendre Region]], and the busiest of all the ways between them. It leaves the landward gate of [[place-batarencity|Bataren City]], crosses the farms of the gulf's hinterland and enters [[place-darvena|Darvena]], the deep wood that closes the western sixth of the northern frontier. Three days later it comes out of the trees into the worked forest of [[place-lirve|Lirve]] and ends at [[place-liranel|Liranel]], where it meets the coast road of Lirve. From gate to gate is about five days on foot.

East of the forest the frontier is mountain, crossed only by the shepherds' trails over [[place-hidralza|Hidralza]] and [[place-ronalza|Ronalza]]. The forest road is level, open in every season and wide enough for a cart, and the overland trade of the two kingdoms goes by it.

## What Moves on It {#what-moves-on-it}

**North.** Salt from the southern pans, wine and oil from the Korvatis, Vilérian cloth and glass from the southern ports, all carted up from the south to Bataren and on.

**South.** The manufactures of [[place-ellive|Éllive]]: illuminated books, instruments, fine cloth and the dyes of the Lirve coast. Furs from the north, and licensed timber from Liranel's yards.

**People.** Scholars and students going to [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]] and to [[place-beravel|Péravel]], síladors between engagements, the couriers of the two courts, and Élavendren families of Bataren visiting kin across the border.

**Cattle and pigs.** Drovers bring herds down through the forest to the autumn markets at Bataren.

## Logistics {#logistics}

The road is laid on a raised bed of earth and gravel through the forest, with log causeways over the wet hollows and plank bridges over the streams. Two carts can pass on it almost everywhere. It keeps straight where the ground allows and bends round the worst of the standing water.

There are no towns in the wood. Stage-houses stand about a day apart, each a walled yard with a well, a stable and a common room, kept by families who sell fodder and bread at the prices the road will bear. A pack-train sleeps in a stage-house or in the open by the road, and nobody camps out of sight of it.

## The Stations {#stations}

**Bataren** is the southern end, and its landward gate is the crown's toll. **The forest edge** is half a day north, where the last fields stop and the trees begin. **The border post** stands in the middle of the wood, where a Provénzian toll-hut and an Élavendren guard-house face each other across the road a bowshot apart. **Liranel** is the northern end, where the road comes out of the forest and meets the coast road of Lirve.

## The Year on the Road {#year-on-the-road}

**Spring** floods the low ground, and the causeways carry the road through it. **Summer** is the heavy season, when the carters of both kingdoms run the road end to end. **Autumn** brings the drovers and the last wine of the year. **Winter** slows the road and does not close it. The northern passes shut with the first snow, but the forest road stays open, and through the winter months it is the one land way between the two kingdoms.

## Who Patrols and Taxes It {#present}

The two crowns divide the road at the border post. On the Provénzian side the crown's officers take the toll at Bataren's gate, and the crown keeps a few riders on the road. On the Élavendren side [[affiliation-brdrmltia|the Border Militia]] holds the guard-house and patrols the road to Liranel, and the crown's forest court at Liranel tallies the timber that comes out of the forest. Relations between the crowns are cordial, and the officers of the two posts drink together and share what they hear.

The road is busier this season than it has been in years. The pilots' fees at Bataren have doubled, and freight that would have gone north by sea is going by the forest instead. The carrying houses of Bataren, Élavendren and Provénzian, are each trying to take the new trade before the other can.

## Dangers {#dangers}

**Thieves.** Three days of trees hide those who would rob a pack-train, and a train that falls behind the others is the one they choose.

**The mud.** In a wet spring or autumn a cart off the causeway can sink to the axle, and a train can lose a day digging it out.

**The quarrel.** The quarrel between Bataren's Élavendren and Provénzian houses travels with their carters, and a dispute at a stage-house can turn into a fight.
