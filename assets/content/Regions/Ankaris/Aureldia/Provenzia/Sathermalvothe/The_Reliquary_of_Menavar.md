## Overview {#overview}

**The Reliquary of Ménavar** is a reliquary of beaten silver the size of a loaf, holding the bones of the hand of the founder of [[place-menavarabbey|Ménavar Abbey]], a healer of the [[affiliation-lizelas|Faith of Lizelas]]. The brothers of the abbey credited it with cures.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody holds it. It was not carried out when the Nordheim fleets gutted the abbey about 520 AF, and it lies somewhere in the ruins on the low country of [[place-sathvothe|Satvote]], a day's march from the [[place-batarengulf|Gulf of Bataren]]. The abbey's own plans survive in three versions that disagree exactly where it matters. The ruins are said to be haunted by the brothers who died defending them, and at least one expedition after the relic has failed.

:::secret {title="Where it is"}
The reliquary is behind the east wall of the chapter house, in a niche the brothers bricked up the night before the abbey fell, wrapped in a rotten cloth with a brother's note in the old hand asking the finder to carry it where it is needed. All three plans draw that wall as solid, and it is the one thing they agree on. The dead brothers in the cloister turn back anyone who comes armed or digging, and let through a party that comes unarmed, at dusk, and asks.
:::

## Its Story {#its-story}

The abbey was a great house of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]], with a library, a school and an endowment from the counts of [[place-batarencity|Bataren]]. As the fleets came in, the brothers drew the plans themselves and gave them to three houses of the faith in the south ([[lore-southrnfleets|The Southern Fleets]]). The abbey was never refounded, and every attempt to reoccupy it was abandoned within a season.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

Whether it heals anyone is not settled. What has changed is the need: the country round [[place-bldfldyharvalen|Yarvalen]] is withering a day's march from the abbey, and a healing relic so near has become a question of politics as well as faith.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **The church.** [[being-ortmkrms|Ortema Kiramis]], prioress of the house of Lizelas at Bataren City, holds that the relic is the faith's and has never been relinquished, and wants it carried to the withering country and used there. She holds one of the three plans.
- **The Order.** [[being-zdmdrvs|Zedime Diravis]], the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]'s magister at Bataren, wants to _study_ it, and holds a copy of the second plan.
- **The house.** The counts of Bataren endowed the abbey and claim custody of its relic, a claim that is probably genuine.
- **The collector.** A dealer from Válaren offers more than any of the others, for a client he will not name.

:::secret {title="The house and the collector"}
The house's claim is pressed by the steward of the present countess, [[being-kiramailthor|Kirama Iltoris]], without asking her, and Kirama would rather not be seen to grab a healing relic in a dying land. The dealer's client is [[being-jntzgmls|Jentize Gaimelis]], who leads the [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]]'s working at Yarvalen. A relic that might heal the country around the henge is a threat to that working, and he wants it carried south and would as soon it were melted down.
:::
