Satvote—_beyond the mountains_, and also the name of the cold wind that comes over them.

The Satvote is the [[place-provenzrgn|Provenzia Region]]'s frontier with [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] and with the northern
sea: some three-quarters of a million people on the coastal plain and in the valleys, and above them a
great deal of mountain that is not, in any sense that matters, governed.

Relations with the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]] are **very cordial** and the land border is open. That does not make the march
simple. It is the most mixed country in the kingdom, its coast trades north instead of south, and its
uplands belong to people who acknowledge no crown at all.

## The Áelendan Country

The high ground belongs to the [[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]]—the tribal kin of the
[[lore-elavendrnppl|Élavendren People]], holding the old ways in the high valleys and entirely indifferent to
whose map covers them. They do not dispute sovereignty and will not be drawn into anyone's politics.
They will involve themselves the instant a sacred site is disturbed, and they do not distinguish
between a trespasser, a licensed antiquarian and an army.

[[place-hydravenmnt|Mount Hidraven]], the source of the kingdom's water, is theirs.
[[place-sirmenne|Sirmenne]]—shared with the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale Folk]]—is theirs.
[Úkua-Arálwen]{.name data-type="place"}, which straddles the frontier, is held by the
[[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] and administered by neither kingdom.

## The Sea-Frontier

There is no land border with the [[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Kingdom of Nordheim]]; there is open water,
and the water has never been much of an obstacle. The [[place-batarengulf|Gulf of Bataren]] is the
one real harbor on an ocean coast otherwise made of cliffs, which makes it both the march's fortune
and its standing vulnerability.

Two centuries ago a fleet used it ([[lore-southrnfleets|The Southern Fleets]]). The raiders threw down
[[place-yharvalenhenge|Yarvalen Henge]] and left the
[[place-bldfldyharvalen|Blood-field of Yarvalen]] that is still killing the country around it.

[[place-menavarabbey|Ménavar Abbey]] was gutted in the same raids and never refounded.

## Its Settlements

- [[place-batarencity|Bataren City]]—the ocean port, substantially Élavendren, trading north
  rather than south, and quietly grinding over rights both crowns treat as domestic.
