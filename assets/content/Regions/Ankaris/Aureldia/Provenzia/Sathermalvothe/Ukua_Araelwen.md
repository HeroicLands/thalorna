## Overview {#overview}

**Úkua-Araelwen** is a stretch of the high forest of [[place-sathvothe|Satvote]], on the shoulders of the Áelendan mountains, where the ancient trees stand closer and larger than anywhere else on the range and time runs slow. It is an [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] of the kind that disturbs time, a place of deep [Terrae]{.name data-type="lore/arcana"} resonance, the elemental way of earth, stone and growth. A day inside can be a month outside, and few who go in come out.

It lies across the frontier between [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] and [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia]], and neither crown administers it. At its heart, beyond the [[place-mazeofechos|Maze of Echoes]], lies [[miscgear-heartofdhirikri|the Heart of Dirikri]].

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] hold it, from outside its bounds. They do not go in, and a place that disturbs time is kept by a watch that turns people away: a line of posts and fires round the margin of the forest, manned in pairs and relieved by turns, set where the ancient trees begin. The Áelendan do not camp in it, and they do not let a sick person or a child be carried into it.

The law is the same at Úkua-Araelwen as everywhere. It is forbidden to every outsider, the Wardens warn once, and the courts of neither kingdom hear a complaint from anyone hurt in it. A device of the art carried inside, such as [[miscgear-temprlcmpss|the Temporal Compass]], counts as a working, and a working there as desecration.

## Layers {#history}

What the forest is, and why its time is slow, is not known to anyone who has come back to say.

**The Wardens' account.** The forest is an Araelwen, and the Heart is its charge. Nothing in the Pact permits anyone to carry it out, and the Wardens believe that removing it would unbalance everything that grows in the forest.

**The Order's reading.** The theorists of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] hold that the Heart is the reason the forest is slow, and that a thing which bends time is the most useful object in the west. A scroll in poor condition, held at court, places the Heart at the center and implies that sound has something to do with it.

**The crowns' account.** Neither kingdom claims it. The line on the map of Provénzia's border and Élavendre's passes through it, and both maps leave it blank.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The Royal Loremaster [[being-aelithraenewyn|Áelithra Énevin]] holds the scroll and wants a party to carry a Temporal Compass into the forest and bring the Heart out. The Wardens know she wants it. The posts on the Provénzia side have been doubled this season, and a pair of Order agents is camped at the margin on the Élavendre side with a map they bought.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbours}

- **The Wardens.** They warn once at the margin and do not warn again. They count the Compass as a working.
- **The time.** A party that comes out finds its kin older, its debts due and its news stale. A day inside can be a month outside.
- **The dryads.** The ancient trees are held by dryads who defend their domain, and who may be negotiated with by someone who knows the customs of earth-bound spirits.
- **The flora.** The trees are not merely large. Some move, and some of those that move are hostile.
- **The Maze.** It shifts and does not always let a party retrace a wrong turn.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

**The margin.** A line of mark-stones, a fire-pit and a turf hut at the edge of the ancient trees. Two Wardens sit at the post. A dog lies at their feet and does not look up.

**The first trees.** Vast trunks set close, their bark furrowed and mossed, the light under them green and slow. Nothing moves, and the air tastes of stone.

**The dryad glade.** A clearing among the largest trees, ringed by trunks that stand as people stand. A party that comes in with a blade drawn finds the trees nearer when it looks again.

**The Maze of Echoes.** A labyrinth of living wood and vine, shifting, that answers to sound. The correct way returns a clear, crystalline resonance. Crystals set through it must be struck in order, and wrong notes lead to dead ends.

**The Heart.** A crystal at the center, behind the Maze. A Temporal Compass vibrates more strongly the nearer it comes.

:::secret {title="The truth of Úkua-Araelwen"}
The ratio is not fixed. A day at the margin is a few days outside, a day among the dryads is a month, and a day in the Maze is longer than that. The Compass does not stop the slowing. It holds the bearer's own time steady, so the party ages as it expects while the world outside runs ahead, and the longer it stays the greater the debt when it comes out.

The Heart is why the forest is slow. If it is carried out of the Maze, the slowing ends, and everything the slow days have held back comes due on the forest in a season: the trees near the margin shoot up, the dryads wake to a world that has gone on without them, and the Wardens' watch is not equal to what comes out. The Wardens do not know the whole of this. They know enough to forbid it.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The Heart.** Áelithra Énevin wants a party to carry the Compass in and bring the Heart out, and the Wardens know she wants it.
- **The doubled posts.** A Warden of the Provénzia side asks the party to carry a warning to the Order's agents at the margin, so that no guard can say he was not told.
- **The missing month.** A hunter of Satvote went past the margin after a wounded stag and came out in a night, a month late. His family wants the party to learn where he went before the Wardens decide what he is.
