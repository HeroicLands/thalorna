A maze encountered within [[place-ukuaraelwen|Úkua-Araelwen]], where sound does not behave as it should—the
obstacle that stands between a party and the [[miscgear-heartofdhirikri|Heart of Dirikri]].

## Where Úkua-Araelwen Is

The Araelwen lies in the **high forest of [[place-sathvothe|Satvote]]**—the deep wooded country on the shoulders
of the Áelendan mountains, straddling the frontier between the
[[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]] and [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]].

That it lies across the border is the important part, and explains nearly everything about how it is
treated. Neither crown administers it; neither crown has ever seriously tried. The ground is held by the
[[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]], who guard the Araelwen as a matter of duty rather than of
sovereignty and who are entirely indifferent to which kingdom's map the trespasser is carrying.

Úkua-Araelwen is a **slow** Araelwen of deep [Terrae]{.name data-type="lore/arcana"} resonance—ancient trees, dryads, flora both gigantic
and occasionally mobile—and it is temporally unreliable enough that few of the humans who enter it
return. A [[miscgear-temprlcmpss|Temporal Compass]] makes the passage survivable and does nothing about
anything else.

## The Maze

The Maze itself is a labyrinth of living wood and vine that shifts and rearranges, and it answers to
**sound**. Echoes reveal the paths: the correct way returns a crystalline resonance, resonant crystals
set through the maze must be struck in sequence, and a party that listens carefully to how the echoes
change can tell when it is nearing the Heart—or nearing a trap.

Wrong notes lead to dead ends, and the maze does not always let a party retrace one.

## Layers {#history}

Nobody who has come back says who made the Maze or when, and the accounts of it follow the accounts of [[place-ukuaraelwen|Úkua-Araelwen]] itself.

**The Wardens' account.** The Maze is the forest's own, grown round the [[miscgear-heartofdhirikri|Heart of Dirikri]] as a ward, and no hand set it there. The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] hold that it is the Araelwen's charge to keep and the Wardens' to leave alone.

**The Order's reading.** The theorists of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] take it for a lock and the echoes for its key. A scroll in poor condition, held at court by the Royal Loremaster [[being-aelithraenewyn|Áelithra Énevin]], places the Heart at the center and implies that sound has something to do with reaching it.

**The crowns' account.** Neither kingdom claims the Maze, because neither claims the forest. Both maps leave the ground blank.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The Maze still shifts and still answers to sound. The correct way returns a crystalline resonance, the crystals set through it must be struck in order, and a wrong note ends in a dead end that does not always let a party back out.

No one stands inside it. The Wardens keep their posts at the margin of the forest and do not enter, and a party reaches the Maze only by passing the posts, the ancient trees and the dryads' glade. Time runs slow all the way in, so every hour spent finding the way is paid for outside, and a [[miscgear-temprlcmpss|Temporal Compass]] carried in counts to the Wardens as a working and the working as desecration.

The Maze is the reason the Heart is still where it is. Áelithra Énevin wants a party to carry the Compass in, solve it and bring the Heart out, and the Wardens, who know she wants it, have doubled the posts on the Provénzia side.
