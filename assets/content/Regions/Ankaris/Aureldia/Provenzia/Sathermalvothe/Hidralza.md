## Overview {#overview}

**Hidralza** is the western of the two passes over the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]]'s northern mountains. Its name is the mountain's and _ralzena_ (a mountain pass) run together, the Hidraven pass. It leaves the valleys of [[place-gwefavel|Gefavel]] above [[place-gefavelcstl|Gefavel Castle]], climbs the summer pastures on the shoulder of [[place-hydravenmnt|Mount Hidraven]], crosses a high saddle west of the peak and goes down into the remote Áelendan country of [[place-sorvanda|Sórvanda]] in the [[place-elavendre|Élavendre Region]]. From the castle to the first Áelendan valleys on the far side is about five days on foot.

It is a trail, not a road. Nobody brings a cart over it, and the trade of the two kingdoms goes round by [[place-darvenaroad|the Darvena Road]] through the western forest.

## What Moves on It {#what-moves-on-it}

**Shepherds.** The flocks of Gefavel go up to the pastures on Hidraven's shoulder after the thaw, by arrangements with the tribes older than the kingdom, and come down before the snow.

**Pilgrims.** Pilgrims who come to the mountain the customary way walk the lower pass to the springs and turn back, and a few go on over the saddle.

**The Áelendan.** The tribes' own people cross by it between their kin on both sides of the range, and bring the bodies the thaw uncovers down to the nearest Provénzian village.

## Logistics {#logistics}

The trail is a mule's width, marked by cairns above the tree line and by blazes below it. It climbs from the valley farms through forest to the pastures in a day, crosses the saddle on the second, and drops through steep woods into Sórvanda over three more. There is no shelter above the shepherds' huts on the pastures, and water is plentiful from the springs until late summer.

## The Year on the Road {#year-on-the-road}

**Winter** shuts the pass from the first snow to late spring, and the tribes do not use it then either. **The thaw** brings the flocks up and the first pilgrims. **Summer** is the open season. **Autumn** brings the flocks down, and a party still on the saddle when the snow comes may not come down at all.

## Who Patrols and Taxes It {#present}

Nobody taxes the pass. The Princess of Gefavel's writ runs to the last valley farm and no further. Above it the mountain is the ground of the [[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]], and the tribes' watchers see who comes up. They let shepherds and pilgrims by, and they turn back anyone with instruments or with an interest in the springs. On the far side the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] keep the way down into Sórvanda.

This season the tribes have set a closer watch on the paths up to the springs. The shepherds tell of bad dreams on the high ground, two flocks have sickened, and a pilgrim who went up came down without speaking ([[scenario-crsmnthydrvn|The Curse of Mount Hidraven]]).

## Dangers {#dangers}

**The cold.** The weather on the saddle turns in an hour in any month, and the cloud hides the cairns.

**The tribes' law.** A party that leaves the trail for the springs, or carries a survey instrument, is warned once.

**The way down.** On the Élavendren side the trail runs into Áelendan country where the crown's writ does not run, and a stranger who goes on past the first valleys without a guide goes on alone.
