The Blood-field of Yarvalen is a battlefield of the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]] remembered as a blood-field—ground where an engagement was fought at such
cost that the name stuck to the place rather than to the battle. Near
[[place-yharvalenhenge|Yarvalen Henge]].

It lies in [[place-sathvothe|Satvote]], on the low country inland of the [[place-batarengulf|Gulf of Bataren]]—a day's march from where a fleet can put an army ashore, which is why an army was ever
brought to meet one there.

It is the worst of Provénzia's [[lore-bloodfields|blood-fields]], and the one every Provénzian can
name.

## A Withering

Most blood-fields are frightening and harmless. Yarvalen is neither. It is a **withering**: the land
does not merely remember the battle, it dies of it. Crops fail, beasts sicken, the soil turns sour,
and—unlike every other resonance recorded—the harm does not stay on the field. It spreads.

For two centuries it lay dormant, which the districts around it mistook for healing and which was in fact
only quiet. That has ended.

## Layers {#history}

**The henge.** Yarvalen is unusual in that the killing was not the whole of the injury. [[lore-southrnfleets|The Southern Fleets]], the Nordheim raids that swept
through northern Provénzia from 519 to 530 AF, **threw down the henge** about 520 as well as killing the men who stood on it, and
the standing opinion among those who study such things is that a blood-field made on the ruins of a
sacred circle is a different and worse proposition than one made in an open field—that the boundary
there was already answerable to the stones, and that breaking both at once left nothing to hold it.

Whether that is true is not established. It is, however, the only account that explains why this field
withers and the others do not.

**The quiet.** For two centuries the field lay still, and the district learned to farm at a distance from it and not to talk about why.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The field is awake and the withering is spreading outward at a pace that has stopped being deniable. The farms nearest it are being given up, and the families who leave them go to [[place-batarencity|Bataren]] with nothing to sell. Nobody in the district can say what woke it. [[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]] says the cult of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] is rousing it on purpose, to draw power from the dying, and says so from every pulpit in the north; the Forge is an interested party, the Earl of Hárivren is a Flame lord nobody can touch, and nobody with authority in Provénzia has taken the charge up. The priests of [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]] have not attempted the rite of settlement on a withering, and the [[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan]] are watching the broken henge from the mountains.

:::secret {title="What woke Yarvalen"}
The curse was awakened on purpose. The cult of the Black Flame has been rousing and magnifying it, drawing power from the destruction—a blood-field being a permanent reservoir of exactly what that faith holds sacred. The dying of the surrounding country is not incidental to their working. It is the working.
:::
