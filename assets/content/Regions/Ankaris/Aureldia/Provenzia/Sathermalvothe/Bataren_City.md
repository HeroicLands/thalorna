Bataren stands on the [[place-batarengulf|Gulf of Bataren]] in the northwest, and is [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Provénzia]]'s
window on the cold ocean and the northern sea-roads. It is the kingdom's second harbor and its most
awkward city.

## A City That Faces the Wrong Way

Provénzia's wealth runs along the warm southern coast, in and out of the Vilérian world, and the
southern ports are the kingdom as foreigners picture it: sunlit, rich, sophisticated, Vilérian in
manner if no longer in law.

Bataren is none of that. It faces the **open ocean**, trades with [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]],
[[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]] and the northern seas beyond, and takes its money from fish, timber, northern
furs and freight the south has no interest in. Its weather is gray where the south is bright. Its
building is heavy and low. A Provénzian from [[place-valarencity|Válaren]] finds Bataren cold in every sense and says so.

## The Élavendren

A substantial part of the city's population belongs to the [[lore-elavendrnppl|Élavendren People]]—descendants of settlers
who came down from Élavendre into these borderlands centuries ago, and who have been here longer than
the present arrangement of crowns. They are Provénzian subjects. They are not, in any way that matters
locally, Provénzian.

Provénzian rulers and the settled Élavendren population have real and unresolved quarrels over **land,
rights and tradition**—over inheritance customs the Crown does not recognize, over which observances
are permitted, over who holds what and on whose authority. Nothing about it is settled, and very
little about it is violent. It grinds.

Both crowns are careful to treat this as a Provénzian domestic matter, because relations between
Provénzia and [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Élavendre]] are otherwise **very cordial** and neither court wishes to test whether they
would survive being made about Bataren. That care is itself a pressure: everyone in the city
understands that the two kingdoms have agreed not to look closely, which is a great deal of latitude
for anyone who would rather they did.
