## Overview {#overview}

The Temporal Compass is an ornate enchanted instrument made for one purpose: to get a living person
through [[place-ukuaraelwen|Úkua-Araelwen]] and out again in something close to the time they expected.

Úkua-Araelwen is a _slow_ zone of deep [Terrae]{.name data-type="lore/arcana"} resonance, and its temporal anomalies are the reason so
few who enter it return. A day inside is not a day outside, the discrepancy is not constant, and a
traveler who simply walks in and walks out may emerge to find that the season has changed or that
years have. The Compass does not remove this. It **stabilizes time in a slight field around the
bearer**, which is enough to make the passage survivable and no more.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The Royal Loremaster of Élavendre, [[being-aelithraenewyn|Áelithra Énevin]], owns it, and it is the one instrument that can take a living party through Úkua-Araelwen in something like the time it expects. She keeps it at [[place-beravel|Péravel]] and carries it at court in her writing-case. She means to lend it to the party she sends into the Araelwen after the [[miscgear-heartofdhirikri|Heart of Dirikri]] ([[scenario-embsydnvrtantsou|Embassy from Dunavarre to Tantsou]]).

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

- It **glows** when pointed along the safest direction, which is not always the shortest one.
- It **vibrates** as it nears the [[miscgear-heartofdhirikri|Heart of Dirikri]], more strongly the
  closer it comes.
- Its field covers the bearer and those close by. Straying out of it during a passage is how parties
  become separated by more than distance.

It is an aid to navigation and not a license. Nothing about the Compass addresses the dryads, the
flora, or the [[place-mazeofechos|Maze of Echoes]].

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] guard the Araelwen and will attempt to seize the
Compass once they learn of it, both to deny the intrusion and because an instrument that manipulates time
inside an Araelwen is precisely the sort of thing they exist to prevent. They count it, carried inside, as a working, and a working there as desecration, and they know the Loremaster wants the Heart. A party carrying it should assume the Wardens will know.

The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] prizes instruments that work in the thin places, and this one above the rest ([[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]]).

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The compass comes back alone.** A party that took the Compass into the Araelwen has not come out, and the Compass has turned up on a stall in Péravel's book quarter. Áelithra wants to know who brought it out, and how long they were inside.
