Satvote—_beyond the mountains_, and also the name of the cold wind that comes over them.

The Satvote is the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]]'s frontier with [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] and with the northern
sea: some three-quarters of a million people on the coastal plain and in the valleys, and above them a
great deal of mountain that is not, in any sense that matters, governed.

Relations with [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Élavendre]] are **very cordial** and the land border is open. That does not make the march
simple. It is the most mixed country in the kingdom, its coast trades north instead of south, and its
uplands belong to people who acknowledge no crown at all.

## The Áelendan Country

The high ground belongs to the [[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]]—the tribal kin of the
[[lore-elavendrnppl|Élavendren]], holding the old ways in the high valleys and entirely indifferent to
whose map covers them. They do not dispute sovereignty and will not be drawn into anyone's politics.
They will involve themselves the instant a sacred site is disturbed, and they do not distinguish
between a trespasser, a licensed antiquarian and an army.

[[place-hydravenmnt|Mount Hidraven]], the source of the kingdom's water, is theirs.
[[place-sirmenne|Sirmenne]]—shared with the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale]]—is theirs.
[[place-ukuaraelwen|Úkua-Araelwen]], which straddles the frontier, is held by their [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] and administered by neither kingdom.

## The Sea-Frontier

There is no land border with the [[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Kingdom of Nordheim]]; there is open water,
and the water has never been much of an obstacle. The [[place-batarengulf|Gulf of Bataren]] is the
one deep harbor on an ocean coast otherwise made of cliffs, where [[place-volsather|Volsater]] and [[place-tremuzel|Tremuzel]] in Zuravlen are clefts in the rock, and that makes it both the march's fortune
and its standing vulnerability.

Two centuries ago a fleet used it ([[lore-southrnfleets|the Southern Fleets]]). The raiders threw down
[[place-yharvalenhenge|Yarvalen Henge]] and left the
[[place-bldfldyharvalen|blood-field]] that is still killing the country around it.

[[place-menavarabbey|Ménavar Abbey]] was gutted in the same raids and never refounded.

## Its Settlements

[[place-gwefavel|Gefavel]]
: the lands of the Princess of Gefavel in the valleys below the mountains, held by a cadet branch of the royal house.

[[place-batarencity|Bataren City]]
: the ocean port, substantially Élavendren, trading north rather than south, and quietly grinding over rights both crowns treat as domestic.

## Current Situation {#present}

The march is quiet on the surface. The border is open, the crowns are cordial, and the Áelendan keep to their mountains. What a traveler meets is the weight of what is sacred in the high forest.

[[place-ukuaraelwen|Úkua-Araelwen]], a slow [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] in the high forest, lies across the frontier. A day inside it can be a month outside. Anyone who goes past the margin comes out to find kin older, debts due and news stale, and many do not come out. No road crosses it, and a traveler who strays toward the ancient trees finds the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]]' posts first, manned in pairs. They warn once and do not warn again. Neither crown's courts hear a complaint from anyone hurt in it.

The posts on the Provénzia side have been doubled this season, and agents of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] are camped at the edge of the forest with a bought map. The Order wants the [[miscgear-heartofdhirikri|Heart of Dirikri]] that lies beyond the [[place-mazeofechos|Maze of Echoes]], and the Wardens know it. A traveler on the roads of the high valleys is likely to be asked by one side or the other where he is bound and what he carries. A [[miscgear-temprlcmpss|Temporal Compass]] in a pack is read as a declaration of purpose.

On the low country by the gulf the trouble is older and worse. The [[place-bldfldyharvalen|Blood-field of Yarvalen]] has woken from its long quiet, and the withering spreads outward from the broken henge every season: the farms nearest it are being abandoned, and the families who leave them carry their grievance to [[place-batarencity|Bataren]]. [[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]] says the Black Flame is rousing the field and is not believed, and the Áelendan watch the henge from the mountains. In Bataren itself the Élavendren quarrel with the crown's officers grinds on, and both crowns go on not looking closely.
