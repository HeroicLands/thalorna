## Overview

Ravenshold Castle is the seat of the county of [[place-ravenshold|Ravenshold]] in the [[place-korvathis|Korvatis]], and of its count, [[being-melvrelvns|Melvare Elvanis]]. Some sixteen hundred people live in the town below it, which is rich for its size, as the county is. The count lives at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] for most of the year, and the town is kept for him by his steward.

## History {#history}

The counts of Ravenshold have held the castle and the county of the crown, and the present count inherited it young. He has held it for more than twenty years, and in that time he has spent more on the theater than on the castle.

## Current Situation {#present}

The town is prosperous and quiet, and its quiet is the count's policy. The envoys of Degavel and Atavel who want the count's vote call at the castle as well as at Jalnasdal, and the steward entertains them all with the same courtesy and the same answer. Companies of [[affiliation-prvnzthtrclctv|the Provénzia Theater Collective]] that want a new piece staged without trouble come to the steward first, and the castle's hall is lent for their rehearsals in the winter.

## The Holder {#holder}

The count holds the castle and county of the crown and sits for them in the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]]. His steward keeps his accounts and his door, here and at Jalnasdal.

## Within the Walls {#within-the-walls}

The hall is hung for the players more often than for the court. The count's stables below the walls keep his horses when they are not at the inn above [[place-lizela|Lizela]].

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The envoy who stayed.** An envoy of the Atavel party has stayed at the castle a week past his welcome, and the steward wants to know what he is waiting for.
- **The rehearsal.** A company rehearsing in the hall has a comedy of a magnate who keeps two households, and the steward wants to know whether his master has read it.
