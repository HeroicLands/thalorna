## Overview

Gefavel is the domain of the **Princess of Gefavel**, [[being-itrolaldrvn|Itrola Lédravren]], one of the powerful magnates of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]], and some hundred and twenty thousand people live in it. She is of the royal blood, of a cadet branch of [[affiliation-hseledravren|House Lédravren]], and holds the rank that blood gives. From her stronghold she exerts considerable influence over the country around it, and she uses that influence with a purpose.

## The Land {#the-land}

Gefavel lies in the valleys of the [[place-sathvothe|Satvote]], the kingdom's northern quarter, on the settled ground below the mountains the [[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]] hold. It is farming and grazing country, cooler and poorer than the south, and it was poorer still once the crown took back the lands its princes held by grant.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The princess holds Gefavel of the crown by right of her blood and sits in the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]]. She governs it from [[place-gefavelcstl|Gefavel Castle]] in the seasons between the Council's sittings and keeps a house at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] for them.

## Current Situation {#present}

The princess is back at court and means to be counted. [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] is her kinsman, and she has made it plain that a cadet branch that lost its standing under his uncle expects it returned under him. Her petition to the Council for her branch's grants and the great seal stands on the Charter. She courts the Peers whom neither the Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]] nor the Count of [[place-athavel|Atavel]] can hold, and with them she can make or break either man's majority. The king needs her goodwill and cannot give her what she asks without offending the houses that rose while hers fell. At home Gefavel pays less than her state costs, and she has borrowed to keep it.

## History {#history}

The princes of Gefavel descend from a younger son of House Lédravren. They held crown lands by grant and, in the last prince's time, the great seal as Chancellor. In 688 AF [[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]] resumed the grants and took the seal, and the branch lost its standing at court ([[lore-velarikreign|The Reign of Vélarik III]]). The court calls it the Resumption, and Gefavel calls it the Taking. The prince died without recovering either, and Itrola has held Gefavel since she was seventeen.
