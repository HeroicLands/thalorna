## Overview {#overview}

The **Order of the Burning Brand** is a sworn order of knights and lords of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]] that stands with [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]]. Its knights swear before a Hammer-Priest of the Forge to defend the Forge's faithful and to oppose [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] in the kingdom, and its brand is the Forge's doctrine carried in a fist: fire held upright, as a tool and a light, never thrown.

It is not one of the Forge's own knightly orders. The [Knights of the Sacred Forge]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"} are warrior-priests of the faith; the Burning Brand is an order of the kingdom's lay nobility, and its weapon is as often a voice in the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]] as a sword. It keeps a chapter house at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]], close enough to the Peers' hall for its knights to wait in the same anterooms as everyone else.

## Membership {#membership}

A Knight of the Brand is a knight or a lord's son who has sworn the order's vow and wears its badge, a brand in a mailed fist. The knights ride at the Master's call to guard a shrine, escort a preacher or stand in a lord's hall while the Forge's case is heard.

The order's weight is its sworn friends: lords who back it without bearing its arms and give it silver, men and their voices. The firmest of them is the line of [[being-selvarezrvl|Selvare Zuravel]], Baron of [[affiliation-brnyzrvln|Zuravlen]], and that support gives a small agricultural barony more weight in Provénzian affairs than its size suggests.

The order does not count the [Zuravel]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} clan as its own. The clan is divided on the kingdom's faith quarrel: Selvare's line backs the order, and the Lénavel branch of [[being-kaldinlnvl|Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren]] keeps the Black Flame at [[place-leravren|Leravren]] in the eastern march.

## Leaders {#leaders}

Master of the Brand
: [[being-ormelazrvl|Ormela Zuravel]], the baron's younger sister, chosen by the knights for life. She keeps the chapter house and the roll, calls the knights out and speaks for the order to the Forge's bishops and to the Peers.

## What It Wants Now {#present}

**The western march.** The Forge's bishops have petitioned the crown to take the western march from [[being-avaarlzlvrn|Avaarl Zilavren]], Earl of [[place-harivrencity|Hárivren]] and the most senior lord of the Black Flame in the kingdom, and the petition lies before the Council of Peers. The order presses it in every anteroom at Jalnasdal. The Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]] and the Count of [[place-athavel|Atavel]] each wait to see which way the other moves, and both are bidding for the earl's voice.

**The baron's position.** The order's firmest backer holds his barony of the earl it wants removed. Selvare's fruit pays the earl's tolls on its way south, and earl and baron are civil in public. The order needs the baron's line to stay its friend without the baron's overlord making him pay for it.

**Kivarel.** The baron's best land, the [[place-bldfldkyvarel|Blood-field of Kivarel]], is a quickening its tenants will not let the priests settle. A Burning Brand baron whose best fruit grows on a blood-field would answer the Forge's charges against the earl with laughter, and the earl's people mean to see that the field's name reaches Jalnasdal ([[scenario-kvrlfld|The Field at Kivarel]]).

**Leravren.** Kaldin of Leravren keeps the Flame openly on the road the march needs, and he is the baron's kinsman. The order would rather the Marshal dealt with Leravren than that its own knights did.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

The chapter house at Jalnasdal takes letters and hires escorts, and the Master hears anyone who brings word of the Flame. A party meets the order's knights on the roads of the march, riding as escort to a Forge preacher, and its friends in the halls of the lords who back it, the baron's hall above [[place-elthrembis|Eltrembis]] first among them. The order pays for proof it can read to the Peers, and it does not pay for rumor.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The letter to Jalnasdal.** The earl's factor at Eltrembis has written to a secretary of the earl at Jalnasdal, describing the field at Kivarel and the baron's patronage of the order side by side. The Master wants the letter before it is read.
- **An escort for a preacher.** A Forge preacher means to preach in Hárivren City during the Council's sitting, and the order wants knights it can deny are its own to see her in and out.
- **A kinsman's hall.** The Master wants to know who visits Kaldin's hall at [[place-leravrncstl|Leravren Castle]], and she cannot send a Zuravel to find out.
