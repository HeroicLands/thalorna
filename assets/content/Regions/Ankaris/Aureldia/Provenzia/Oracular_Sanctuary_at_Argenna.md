## Overview

Argenna is the site of **the Oracular Sanctuary at Argenna**, a shrine of the [[affiliation-zunelas|Faith of Zunelas]] in the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]] whose omens are renowned across kingdoms. Pilgrims come to it from across the continent because they trust that the dreams received there come from the goddess herself.

## Layers {#history}

The sanctuary is older than the kingdom, and the priestesses say it is older than the province, though they keep no record that reaches so far. Its renown is the record: kings, merchants and the sick have slept in its precinct for generations, and the dreams they carried away are told in their families as the reasons for marriages, voyages and wars.

## Sleeping at the Sanctuary

A priestess explains the practice to newcomers before anything else: you come to the sanctuary with a question, you perform the purification rites, and then you sleep in the sacred precinct. In the morning you tell the priestess-oracles what you dreamed, and they interpret it for you. The sanctuary is built for rest—its chambers are hung in midnight and silver fabrics, the air is perfumed with herbs that bring sleep, and the architecture is made to quiet the waking mind—and the sick and troubled may stay for nights or weeks under the priestesses' care. Offerings are small and personal: a lock of hair bound in silver thread, a cup of milk left before sleep, a sealed prayer buried beneath a sacred tree.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The precinct is crowded beyond its custom. Pilgrims from the withering country of the north come to ask whether their land can be saved, and courtiers from [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] come to ask which way the Council of Peers will break. The priestess-oracles are asked by great houses for dreams that favor them, and they refuse; the houses that are refused say the oracles have taken a side.
