## Overview

Provénzia is the land of the chartered city: a kingdom whose king rules under a Charter of Rights, whose great cities govern themselves by charter, and whose wealth comes off the warm [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]]. It lies in the southwestern part of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]] and is one of its richest and most learned countries. Its terrain runs from coastal plains and vineyards to rolling hills, forest and mountain, and its cities are centers of art, philosophy and law.

## Geography

Provénzia occupies the southwestern corner of Ankaris, and each of its four edges is a different frontier. To the west is the open ocean. To the south is the Vilérian Sea. To the east an inlet of the same sea divides it from the [[place-galvaren|Galvaren]] coast of the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]], beyond which lies the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]. Mountains meet Tarvénia only at the north-east corner, and the Tarvénian passes cross them below [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]]. To the north lies the [[place-elavendre|Élavendre Region]]. All but the western sixth of that northern edge is mountain, crossed by two passes, [[place-hidralza|Hidralza]] and [[place-ronalza|Ronalza]], whose trails lead into the remote Áelendan country of Élavendre. The western sixth is deep forest, [[place-darvena|Darvena]], and one road crosses it: [[place-darvenaroad|the Darvena Road]] from [[place-batarencity|Bataren City]] to [[place-liranel|Liranel]], the chief trade road between the two kingdoms.

It has two coasts and they are nothing alike. The Vilérian Sea forms its southern and eastern shores—warm, sheltered, fringed with long sandy beaches, and the highway of nearly all its trade—while its western shore faces the **open ocean**, a vastly larger and far colder water, where craggy cliffs stand above deep gray swell and the wind comes ashore unbroken. Inland the ground rises: the north of the region is hilly and then frankly mountainous, and the uplands of its north-east corner carry the Tarvénian frontier. Only the northwest stays low, where the hinterland of the gulf runs into the forest.

The whole region is **temperate**, but not uniformly so. The southern reaches along the Vilérian Sea
are the warmest and grow what warmth allows; the western seaboard is cooler and grayer than its
latitude suggests, the cold ocean seeing to that; and the northern mountains are genuinely
cold and **very nearly impassable in winter**, when the passes close and the high valleys are left to
themselves for months at a time.

That closure is the single most important fact about Provénzia's eastern frontier: the skirmishing
with [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] has a season. Raiding begins when the passes open and ends when they shut, both sides
plan their year around it, and a winter crossing is either a disaster or a deliberate outrage.

Between the coasts and the uplands lie the fertile river valleys that feed the kingdom and carry its trade, with riverine market towns that tie the interior to the ports and, through them, to the neighboring realms. [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] on the coast is the royal and commercial capital, and [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]] inland keeps the law and the learning.

The two coasts give the kingdom two quite different peoples. The south is where Provénzia's wealth, its ports and its reputation live. The west is poorer, harder, better at fishing, and inclined to regard the south as soft.

### The Blood-fields

Provénzia has been fought over for as long as anyone can remember, and it carries the record in its
ground. Scattered across the kingdom are the [[lore-bloodfields|blood-fields]]—places where so
many died at once that the boundary with the spirit world was torn rather than worn thin, and did not
close afterward. Most replay the battle harmlessly; some grow too well; one is killing the country
around it.

Characteristically, Provénzia names the ground rather than the battle. Provénzians call a field a
blood-field long before they can say which war made it, and for many of them nobody
can. Three of the named fields have a battle on record: [[place-bldfldtiravlen|Blood-field of Tiravlen]], [[place-bldfldmalnebris|Blood-field of Malnebris]] and [[place-bloodhills|the Blood Hills]].

## Culture and Society

Provénzian society blends Vilérian sophistication with Élavendren mysticism. The cities are architectural marvels—marble buildings, grand amphitheaters, intricate aqueducts and soaring towers—that show both the legacy of the Vilérian Empire and the Provénzians' own taste.

The cities are not states. Each is a chartered city of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]], holding a city charter (_daktela_) that the crown has written and sealed, and governing itself on local matters under it: its own courts, its own treasurer, its own council of citizens and guild masters, and the crown's officers beside them for everything the charter does not grant. Above the cities sits the crown, bound by the [[lore-chrtrrghts|Charter of Rights]] won in [[lore-charterwar|the War of the Charter]] to take counsel of a [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]] and to levy only by consent. Power is shared among the three, and the share is always being argued. A Provénzian names his city before his family, and he takes his city's charter for a birthright.

The Provénzians are known across the [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión Region]] for their intellectual pursuits and artistic achievements. Philosophy, art, architecture, music, and theater thrive here. The region produces acclaimed painters, sculptors, playwrights, and natural philosophers. The Provénzian tradition of courtly love—an elaborate social code governing romantic relationships, patronage, and artistic expression—has influenced manners and literature across the Vilérian Sea region.

Education is highly valued. The three academies of [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]], the [[affiliation-acadrhetrc|Academy of Philosophy and Rhetoric]], the [[affiliation-acadlaw|Academy of Law]] and the [[affiliation-acadmedcn|Academy of Medicine and Manuscripts]], draw students from across Ankaris.

## Religion

The twelve faiths of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] dominate religious life. [[affiliation-marendas|Marendas]] (knowledge) and [[affiliation-olvenia|Ólvenia]] (prosperity and seduction) are especially popular, reflecting Provénzian priorities of learning and sensual appreciation of life. [[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]] (fire and the forge) is honored by the region's many skilled craftsmen.

Temples in Provénzia tend toward the grand and beautiful, doubling as galleries, concert halls, and centers of community life. Religious festivals are elaborate affairs combining solemn ritual with carnival-like celebration.

## Magic

Provénzia embraces the arcane arts more openly than most regions of western Ankaris. The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] has a strong institutional presence here, teaching the magical arts in its own chapterhouses, but the relationship is more collaborative than controlling. Provénzian society views magic as another form of art and scholarship—something to be studied, refined, and celebrated. This makes Provénzia something of a haven for mages and magical researchers, though the Arcane Order still keeps a watchful eye on independent practitioners.

The Élavendren influence is strong, particularly in the use of fae magic and nature-based arcane traditions. Many Provénzian artists and scholars are drawn to the mystical, and the border between artistic inspiration and genuine magical practice is often blurred.

## Relations

Provénzia was once a part of the Vilérian Empire: a colony on the great road ([[lore-aurelianefound|The Founding of Aurelane]]), a province from the breaking of the Tanevar kings at [[lore-tiravlenbattle|Tiravlen]], and a kingdom since [[lore-charterwar|the War of the Charter]]. While it is no longer formally under Vilérian control, the Vilérian influence remains deeply ingrained in its political and cultural life. The two regions maintain close economic ties, and many Provénzian noble families have Vilérian ancestry. However, [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Provénzia]] jealously guards its independence and has no desire to return to imperial subjection.

[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Élavendre]] to the north is a cultural cousin and Provénzia's closest friend among the kingdoms—the two share Pelwar heritage and a love of art and magic, though the Élavendren lean more toward the mystical while the Provénzians favor the intellectual. **Relations between the two crowns are very cordial**, and have been for long enough that neither court treats the other as a contingency: the border is open, the trade is heavy and goes overland by [[place-darvenaroad|the Darvena Road]] and by sea from Bataren, scholars and artists move freely in both directions, and the two have repeatedly taken the same side in Vilérian disputes.

That cordiality is between kingdoms, and should not be mistaken for ease everywhere along the line. In the borderlands—and conspicuously in [[place-batarencity|Bataren]]—Provénzian rulers and the settled [[lore-elavendrnppl|Élavendren]] population have real and unresolved quarrels over land, rights and tradition. Both crowns treat these as domestic matters and are careful not to let them become anything else, which is itself a measure of how much both value the friendship.

The northern mountains and the high forest of [[place-sathvothe|Satvote]] are, in the way that matters, neither kingdom's. They are
[[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan]] country—the tribal kin of the
[[lore-elavendrnppl|Élavendren]], holding to the old ways in the high valleys—and the Áelendan are
entirely indifferent to which crown's map colors the ground they live on. They do not petition, they
do not dispute sovereignty, and they will not be drawn into anyone's politics.

They will, however, **involve themselves the moment a sacred site is disturbed**, and they do not
distinguish between a trespassing shepherd, a licensed antiquarian and an army. Both Provénzia and
Élavendre understand this perfectly well, and both go to some trouble to keep relations with the
tribes cordial—which in practice means restraining their own subjects rather than negotiating with
the Áelendan, who have very little they want and nothing they will trade for. It is one of the few
questions on which the two crowns have never needed to be persuaded to agree.

Provénzia's tolerance of the tribes is policy, not sympathy. The sacred sites lie in the mountains and
the remote forest, where the crown loses little by leaving them alone, and tolerating their defense
keeps relations with Élavendre smooth. Tarvénia and Viléria are considerably less
tolerant ([[lore-aelendanclt#land-that-is-not-owned|Land That Is Not Owned]]).

Relations with Tarvénia are the opposite in every respect: competitive, mistrustful, and violent at the margins. Skirmishing along the mountain frontier of the north-east corner is effectively constant—raid and counter-raid over pasture, passes and precedence, kept below the level at which either crown must formally acknowledge a war. The border families on both sides have been fighting the same quarrel for so long that it functions less as a conflict than as a way of life.

## Current Situation {#present}

The crown is strong enough to hold the kingdom and not strong enough to settle it. [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] reigns from [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] over a Council of Peers that cannot find a majority while the Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]] and the Count of [[place-athavel|Atavel]] each measure themselves by the other, and over a Princess of [[place-gwefavel|Gefavel]] who means to win back the standing her line lost under [[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]]. The chartered cities press their charters against the crown's officers, and the Tanevar country holds that the Charter was the cities' and not its own.

Each quarter has its own trouble. In the [[place-talothe|Talote]] the raiding season is open, and in [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] bandits strike the roads with a precision nobody can explain. In [[place-sathvothe|Satvote]] the [[place-bldfldyharvalen|Blood-field of Yarvalen]] is withering the country around it faster every year, and [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] blames [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]], whose most senior lord in the kingdom, the Earl of [[place-harivrencity|Hárivren]], keeps his faith openly and holds the western march for the crown. On the southern coast [[place-valarencity|Válaren]] has not forgotten its last blockade, and the merchant houses lend to the crown with one eye on the salt revenue.

## Notable Features

The Academies of Aurelane
: The three academies of the old provincial capital, of philosophy and rhetoric, of law, and of medicine and manuscripts. They attract students from across Ankaris and are a major source of Provénzia's cultural influence.

The Courtly Tradition
: Provénzia's elaborate social code of [[lore-courtlylove|courtly love]], patronage, and artistic expression, which has influenced culture across the Vilérian Sea region.

The Vineyards
: Provénzia's wines are famous across Midhalión, and the wine trade is a cornerstone of the regional economy.
