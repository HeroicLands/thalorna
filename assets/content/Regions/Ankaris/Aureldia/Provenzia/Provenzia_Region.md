## Overview

Provènzia, located in the southwestern part of [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]], is a land of beauty, culture, and ancient magic. Strongly influenced by the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]] to the south, it is one of the most culturally advanced and prosperous regions in Ankaris. Its terrain ranges from coastal plains and vineyards to rolling hills and lush forests, and its cities are centers of art, philosophy, and learning.

## Geography

Provènzia occupies the southwestern corner of Ankaris, bounded by [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] to the north and [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]] to the east, beyond which lies the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]. It has two coasts and they are nothing alike. The [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]] forms its southern border — warm, sheltered, fringed with long sandy beaches, and the highway of nearly all its trade — while its western shore faces the **open ocean**, a vastly larger and far colder water, where craggy cliffs stand above deep grey swell and the wind comes ashore unbroken. Inland the ground rises: the north and east of the region are hilly and then frankly mountainous, and those eastern uplands carry the Tarvénian frontier.

The whole region is **temperate**, but not uniformly so. The southern reaches along the Vylarian Sea
are the warmest and grow what warmth allows; the western seaboard is cooler and greyer than its
latitude suggests, the cold ocean seeing to that; and the northern and eastern mountains are genuinely
cold and **very nearly impassable in winter**, when the passes close and the high valleys are left to
themselves for months at a time.

That closure is the single most important fact about Provènzia's eastern frontier: the skirmishing
with Tarvénia has a season. Raiding begins when the passes open and ends when they shut, both sides
plan their year around it, and a winter crossing is either a disaster or a deliberate outrage.

The two coasts give the kingdom two quite different peoples. The south is where Provènzia's wealth, its ports and its reputation live. The west is poorer, harder, better at fishing, and inclined to regard the south as soft.

### The Blood-fields

Provènzia has been fought over for as long as anyone can remember, and it carries the record in its
ground. Scattered across the kingdom are the **[[lore-bloodfields|blood-fields]]** — places where so
many died at once that the boundary with the spirit world was torn rather than worn thin, and did not
close afterwards. Most replay the battle harmlessly; some grow too well; one is killing the country
around it.

Characteristically, Provènzia names the ground rather than the battle. A Provenzian can tell you a
field is a blood-field long before they can tell you which war made it, and for many of them nobody
can.

## Culture and Society

Provenzian society blends Vylarian sophistication with Élavendren mysticism. The cities are architectural marvels — marble buildings, grand amphitheaters, intricate aqueducts, and soaring towers that reflect both the legacy of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] and the aesthetic sensibilities of the Provenzians themselves. The cities function as independent or semi-independent polities, each with its own character: some are ruled by hereditary nobility, others by elected councils of wealthy citizens, and a few by powerful guilds of merchants or artisans.

The Provenzians are known across [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión Region]] for their intellectual pursuits and artistic achievements. Philosophy, art, architecture, music, and theater thrive here. The region produces acclaimed painters, sculptors, playwrights, and natural philosophers. The Provenzian tradition of courtly love — an elaborate social code governing romantic relationships, patronage, and artistic expression — has influenced manners and literature across the Vylarian Sea region.

Education is highly valued. Provènzia is home to several renowned academies and schools of learning that attract students from across Ankaris, covering subjects from natural philosophy and mathematics to rhetoric, law, and the magical arts.

## Religion

The [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]] is the dominant faith. [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Ménérva]] (knowledge) and [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Vénusia]] (prosperity and seduction) are especially popular, reflecting Provenzian priorities of learning and sensual appreciation of life. [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|The Sacred Forge]] (fire and the forge) is honored by the region's many skilled craftsmen.

Temples in Provènzia tend toward the grand and beautiful, doubling as galleries, concert halls, and centers of community life. Religious festivals are elaborate affairs combining solemn ritual with carnival-like celebration.

## Magic

Provènzia embraces the arcane arts more openly than most regions of western Ankaris. The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] has a strong institutional presence here, running several academies of magical study, but the relationship is more collaborative than controlling. Provenzian society views magic as another form of art and scholarship — something to be studied, refined, and celebrated. This makes Provènzia something of a haven for mages and magical researchers, though the Ordo still keeps a watchful eye on independent practitioners.

The Élavendren influence is strong, particularly in the use of fae magic and nature-based arcane traditions. Many Provenzian artists and scholars are drawn to the mystical, and the border between artistic inspiration and genuine magical practice is often blurred.

## Relations

Provènzia was once a part of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], and while it is no longer formally under Vylarian control, the Vylarian influence remains deeply ingrained in its political and cultural life. The two regions maintain close economic ties, and many Provenzian noble families have Vylarian ancestry. However, Provènzia jealously guards its independence and has no desire to return to imperial subjection.

[[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] to the north is a cultural cousin and Provènzia's closest friend among the kingdoms — the two share Pelwar heritage and a love of art and magic, though the Élavendren lean more toward the mystical while the Provenzians favor the intellectual. **Relations between the two crowns are very cordial**, and have been for long enough that neither court treats the other as a contingency: the border is open, the trade is heavy, scholars and artists move freely in both directions, and the two have repeatedly taken the same side in Vylarian disputes.

That cordiality is between kingdoms, and should not be mistaken for ease everywhere along the line. In the borderlands — and conspicuously in Batáren — Provenzian rulers and the settled [[lore-elavendrnppl|Élavendren]] population have real and unresolved quarrels over land, rights and tradition. Both crowns treat these as domestic matters and are careful not to let them become anything else, which is itself a measure of how much both value the friendship.

The northern mountains are, in the way that matters, neither kingdom's. They are
[[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan]] country — the tribal kin of the
[[lore-elavendrnppl|Élavendren]], holding to the old ways in the high valleys — and the Áelendan are
entirely indifferent to which crown's map colours the ground they live on. They do not petition, they
do not dispute sovereignty, and they will not be drawn into anyone's politics.

They will, however, **involve themselves the moment a sacred site is disturbed**, and they do not
distinguish between a trespassing shepherd, a licensed antiquarian and an army. Both Provènzia and
Élavendre understand this perfectly well, and both go to some trouble to keep relations with the
tribes cordial — which in practice means restraining their own subjects rather than negotiating with
the Áelendan, who have very little they want and nothing they will trade for. It is one of the few
questions on which the two crowns have never needed to be persuaded to agree.

Relations with [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]] are the opposite in every respect: competitive, mistrustful, and violent at the margins. Skirmishing along the mountainous eastern frontier is effectively constant — raid and counter-raid over pasture, passes and precedence, kept below the level at which either crown must formally acknowledge a war. The border families on both sides have been fighting the same quarrel for so long that it functions less as a conflict than as a way of life.

## Notable Features

- **The Academies:** Renowned institutions of learning covering philosophy, art, law, and magic. They attract students from across Ankaris and are a major source of Provènzia's cultural influence.
- **The Courtly Tradition:** Provènzia's elaborate social code of courtly love, patronage, and artistic expression, which has influenced culture across the Vylarian Sea region.
- **The Vineyards:** Provènzia's wines are famous across Mídhalión, and the wine trade is a cornerstone of the regional economy.
