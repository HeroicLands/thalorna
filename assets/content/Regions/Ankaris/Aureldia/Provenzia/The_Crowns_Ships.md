## Overview {#overview}

**The Crown's Ships** are the navy of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]: the galleys the crown keeps, their crews, and the Admiral who commands them from [[place-valarencity|Válaren City]]. They keep the coasts for the crown, and there are fewer of them than the coasts need.

The navy is the crown's, and the crown pays for it. A levy needs the estates' consent through the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]], and the Council has never voted one for ships, so the galleys are paid out of the crown's own revenue, by the Treasurer, after the frontier and the court.

## Who Serves {#who-sits}

Each galley has a captain, a master and a crew of oarsmen, sailors and marines enrolled on its roll. The captains hold the Admiral's commission. Many of them came up through the ships of the Válaren merchant houses, as the Admiral did.

Admiral
: [[being-jaltezamlvt|Jalteza Malvotis]], who keeps her flag at Válaren City and holds a knight's fee for the office.

In a bad year the merchant captains of Válaren run their own ships under the Admiral's flag, and the merchant houses that remember the last blockade lend the Admiral what the crown will not.

## How It Is Commanded {#how-it-decides}

The King appoints the Admiral, and the Admiral commissions the captains and sends the galleys where she judges the coasts need them. The Treasurer, [[being-gredalekalv|Gredale Kalvezis]], pays the crews and the stores, and a galley he does not pay for stays in harbor. The Admiral's court at Válaren tries the crews' quarrels, desertion and the prizes the galleys take.

The galleys keep two stations: the salt route along the southern coast from [[place-lizela|Lizela]], and the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénian]] coast. Every hull is on one of the two.

## What It Lacks {#present}

**Ships.** The Admiral wants ships, crews and the silver to keep them at sea in winter, and she wants the coasts of the south and the west both guarded, not only the rich one. The crown keeps no ships in [[place-batarengulf|the Gulf of Bataren]], and she has asked the Treasurer for a northern squadron for three years and been refused ([[scenario-ghstshpsglfbtrn|The Ghost Ships of the Gulf of Bataren]]).

**Válaren's harbor mouth.** Three hulls without colors have been seen standing off Válaren, never together, and the merchant houses read them as the first move of a blockade. The Admiral would clear the harbor mouth in a day with two galleys, and every galley she has is on station elsewhere ([[scenario-siegevalrn|The Siege of Válaren]]).

**The Treasurer.** Gredale Kalvezis answers every request for ships with a page of figures. He wants the books to balance and the king never to need the Council for money, and ships cost silver every month.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

The Admiral keeps a house on the harbor at Válaren, and her captains hire pilots, marines and agents along the coast when they have the silver. A party meets the navy as a crew short of hands, as a captain who needs a cove searched, or as the Admiral's agent in a port where she has no ship. She pays for news of hulls nobody owns, and she pays best for proof she can put in front of the Treasurer.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **Ships nobody owns.** The Admiral wants to know whose the hulls standing off Válaren are before the merchant houses start hoarding grain.
- **A captain's loan.** Jalteza wants a Válaren house to fit out two ships at its own cost for a share of the prizes, and she needs someone the Treasurer does not know to carry the offer.
