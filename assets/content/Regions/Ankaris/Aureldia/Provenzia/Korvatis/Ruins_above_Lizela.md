## Overview

The **Ruins above Lizela** stand on a hilltop in the hills above [[place-lizela|Lizela]], in the [[place-korvathis|Korvatis]], within sight of the great crossroads where the coast road meets the road inland. From the town they are a line of gray on the skyline above the salt pans, and the town calls them the Old Walls.

They are older than the Vilérians. The town's courteous word for a ruin, _umeltandar_, resting, belongs to the empire's buildings, and Lizela does not use it of these.

## What Stands There

Walls of fitted gray stone, laid without mortar, run round the crown of the hill and are broken to knee height in most places and to twice a man's height in a few. Inside them the ground is rubble and thin turf, and on the seaward slope a run of terraces steps down toward the coast. A passage is cut into the hill on its landward side and runs back into the dark until fallen stone closes it. No mark is cut on any stone of it.

Shepherds of the hills graze the terraces in spring and do not pen their flocks inside the walls. The town's children go up on a dare and come down before dark.

## Layers {#history}

Nobody in Lizela knows who built the walls, and every account in the town is told with confidence.

**The town's account.** The walls are the hall of a Tanevar king of the old country, broken at [[lore-tiravlenbattle|Tiravlen]] with the rest, and the passage is his treasury, robbed before the empire came. Lizela tells this to every visitor and believes it.

**The fishermen's verse.** An old song of the salt-pan boats names the hill and names a door in it. The fishermen sing it as they sing everything, and they do not say what the door opens on.

**The school's reading.** An instructor of [[being-arnelkrsvr|Arnela Kórisvar]]'s school held that the walls are older than the Tanevar kings as well as the Vilérians, and went up to study them. That is the last thing the school can say of his work with confidence.

## The Present {#present}

The instructor has vanished. He went up to the hill with his notes, and he has not come back to the school or to his rooms in the town. Arnela wants him found, and found before [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Thordimalon]], whose enforcers, [[affiliation-sekran|the Sekran]], take an interest in anyone who studies a thing older than the Vilérians, and whose attention his work may have drawn.

The ruins have drawn a second visitor. [[being-brnklnthrk|Barneke Lantrike]], the sílador who sings at the tables of Lizela's houses, has heard the fishermen's verse and wants to find the door before he makes a song of it, and he wants company on the hill.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The missing instructor.** Arnela Kórisvar hires a party to find her instructor and his notes before the Sekran do.
- **The door in the verse.** Barneke Lantrike wants company to look for the door the fishermen's verse names, and he will share the song.
