## Overview {#overview}

Torvila Degavel is the castle town of [[place-degavel|Degavel]], the earldom of the [[place-korvathis|Korvatis]], and the seat of [[being-rutalplnvr|Rutal Pélanvri]], Earl of Degavel and First Peer of the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]]. Some twenty-five hundred people live below the castle, in a market town that serves the earldom's farms and the levy's musters. It is the house of the [[affiliation-hsepelanvri|Pélanvri]], and the family's sons go from it to the frontier.

## History {#history}

The Pélanvri have held Degavel and its castle of the crown for generations, and they are the greatest of the houses that hold the country between the cities of the south. The family carries the frontier as well as the earldom: the earl's brother, [[being-jarvelplnvr|Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]], went from here to command the Western Army at [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]], and two summers ago their kinsman [[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren]] was given [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] in [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]].

## Current Situation {#present}

The castle is waiting on the vale. Letters come from Torvila Keravel by every courier, and the earl, who spends the Council's sittings at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]], has his household send them on to him the day they arrive. The southern levy is called to muster here if the crown's levy for the Talote is granted, and the town's victualers are already buying against it. The Count of [[place-athavel|Atavel]]'s people have been seen in the market asking which of the earl's tenants have complaints.

## The Holder {#holder}

The earl holds the castle and the earldom of the crown directly. He lives at Jalnasdal through the Council's sittings and at Torvila Degavel in the seasons between, and he keeps the castle as a soldier keeps a camp.

## Garrison {#garrison}

The earl's household guard holds the castle, and the southern levy musters on the field below the walls when the crown calls it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The courier from the vale.** A letter from the Marshal to the earl has not arrived, and the household wants to know who on the road took it.
- **The tenants' complaints.** The Count of Atavel's agents are gathering grievances against the earl among his own tenants, and the earl's steward wants them found.
