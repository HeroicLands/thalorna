The **Túravel Hills** roll across the south of the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]]—low,
wooded, thinly settled, and mapped with a vagueness unusual in so well-surveyed a kingdom. Roads skirt
them. The villages at their edges are small, old and inward, and the shepherds who take flocks onto the
higher ground keep to paths that are not written down anywhere.

There is nothing forbidding about the country itself. It is good pasture and fair timber, and no
authority has ever declared it closed. It is simply that Provénzians who live near the Túravel do not
go far into them without a reason, and give reasons that do not survive much questioning.

## The Older Faith

The hills were a country of [[affiliation-theoldway|the Old Way]] before the
[[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] was worshipped here, and they were never really converted so much as
left alone. Provénzian church records treat the district as settled and orthodox. Provénzian _songs_
do not, and the ballads collected from the hill villages are full of observances no priest of the
Twelve would recognize and place-names that appear on no map.

Antiquarians have been arguing for a century about how much of this is genuine survival and how much
is romantic invention by collectors. The people of the hills decline to help them settle it.

## Character

- **Thinly held.** No town, no castle, no great manor; a scatter of villages holding old customs and
  a few isolated steadings.
- **Poorly mapped.** Every attempt to survey the hills has produced a chart that disagrees with the
  last one, which cartographers blame on the terrain and locals do not blame on anything.
- **Fae-touched, by reputation.** The stories are consistent about there being something in the deep
  hills, and inconsistent about everything else concerning it.
- **Of interest to the wrong people.** The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] claims authority
  over arcane sites, nobles have sought power in old practices before, and the district's obscurity is
  exactly what makes it attractive.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The hills are more crowded than they have been in a century. Scholars and antiquarians outfit at [[place-palvothe|Palvote]] and come in by the shepherds' paths, agents of the Thordimalon come in behind them with the Order's authority over arcane sites, and at least one noble's men have been asking in the hill villages about observances no priest of the Twelve keeps. All of them want the forgotten shrine. The hill families want them gone, and some of the younger shepherds have found that a stranger pays well for a path that is not written down.
