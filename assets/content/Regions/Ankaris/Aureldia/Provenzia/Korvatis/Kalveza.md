**Kalveza** stands on the warm southern shore and is, with [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]], one of the
two cities whose speech is [Cotiere]{.name data-type="skill/language"}—the prestige dialect of [[skill-provnzlng|Provenzal]], the form used in literature
and formal commerce, and the one carrying the deepest Vilérian influence.

Where Jalnasdal is a capital that happens to have a harbor, Kalveza is a harbor and nothing else,
and it is the better port for it. Eighteen thousand people live off the water and the warehouses.

## The Working Port

Kalveza handles what the kingdom actually ships in quantity: grain, oil, wine, salt out of
[[place-lizela|Lizela]], wool from the western march, timber, and the enormous re-export trade in
Vilérian manufactures moving inland. [[place-valarencity|Válaren]] takes the glamour and the luxury
freight; Kalveza takes the tonnage.

Its merchant houses are consequently less grand and considerably more liquid than Válaren's, and the
two cities' factors regard each other with a rivalry that is entirely genuine and entirely commercial.
When credit tightens anywhere in the southern part of the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]] it tightens at Kalveza first, because Kalveza is
where the goods are.

## The Port Dialect

The **Volchetarla**, the harbor tongue, a register of [[skill-provnzlng|Provenzal]] that multilingual merchants use among themselves, is heard here more than anywhere else in the kingdom. It is pared down almost to a pidgin and mixes [[skill-vylarilng|Vilari Language]] and [[skill-elvndrlng|Élavendri Language]] freely, and a good deal of Kalveza's daily business is conducted in a speech that is nobody's mother tongue.

## The Present {#present}

Kalveza is busy on Válaren's fear. With hulls without colors seen off the southern coast, Vilérian masters who would have put in at [[place-valarencity|Válaren]] are putting in here, and the warehouses are full to the roofs. Válaren's houses say that Kalveza's factors are glad of the trouble and may be paying for it, and Kalveza's factors answer that Válaren never minded being the only door while it was. Credit is tight in both cities, and it is tightest where Kalveza's warehouses hold goods bought on Válaren paper.
