## Overview

Degavel is an earldom of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]] in the kingdom's southern country, the [[place-korvathis|Korvatis]], with some three hundred thousand people. Its earl is one of the magnates who decide how Provénzia's court leans. The present **Earl of Degavel**, [[being-rutalplnvr|Rutal Pélanvri]], is the leading lord of the south and First Peer of the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]], and his leadership in the south gives him real weight in both court politics and military strategy.

## The Land {#the-land}

The earldom lies in the country behind the southern coast, between the chartered cities, where the great houses hold the land the cities' charters do not. It is farmed country of manors and market villages, and its levy is the largest in the south. Its western neighbor is the county of [[place-ravenshold|Ravenshold]].

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The earldom is a family affair. The earl holds it of the crown directly from [[place-degavelcstl|Degavel Castle]]. His brother, [[being-jarvelplnvr|Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]], is Marshal of the Western Army and holds [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]] on the Tarvénian frontier, and another of the [[affiliation-hsepelanvri|Pélanvri]] holds [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] under him. The earl's great rival is the Count of [[place-athavel|Atavel]]. Their clashes are one of the long-standing magnate rivalries that leave the crown's council gridlocked, and they weaken the kingdom's cohesion exactly when its frontier most needs it.

## Current Situation {#present}

The earl's family carries the frontier, and the frontier is going badly for it. His brother the Marshal is answerable for [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]], where the bandits strike harder every season, and his kinsman [[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren]] holds the lordship they strike hardest. At court the Count of Atavel asks in Council why the Pélanvri cannot keep their own roads. The earl needs the vale quiet before the Council sits again, and he leans on the Marshal, who leans on Tarlas.

## History {#history}

The Pélanvri are the greatest of the houses that hold the country between the cities of the south. The rivalry between Degavel and Atavel took the shape it has in the Council now after [[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]] gave the great seal to the Count of Atavel in 704 AF ([[lore-velarikreign|The Reign of Vélarik III]]). Two summers ago the Marshal deposed the Tanevar house of Belsaren and gave the lordship to his kinsman, and the family's present trouble on the frontier runs from that day.
