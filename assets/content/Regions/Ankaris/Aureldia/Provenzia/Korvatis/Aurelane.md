**Aurelane** stands inland in the south, on the great road, and is the oldest city in [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia]]. It
was founded under the [Vilérian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} as a [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian]] colony ([[lore-aurelianefound|The Founding of Aurelane]]), laid out on the Vilérian grid, and after [[lore-tiravlenbattle|Tiravlen]] served
for centuries as the provincial capital of everything the empire held west of the sea.

It is not the capital now. When the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]] was established in its own right ([[lore-charterwar|the War of the Charter]]) the court settled at
[[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] on the coast, where the money was, and Aurelane has spent the
century since being magnificent about it.

## Law and Learning

What the city kept was the institutions, and they turned out to matter more than the court did.

Provénzian **law** is Vilérian law as received, amended and argued over at Aurelane, and the high
courts still sit here rather than at Jalnasdal, in the old basilica. So do the three **academies**: the
[[affiliation-acadrhetrc|Academy of Philosophy and Rhetoric]]; the [[affiliation-acadlaw|Academy of Law]],
beside the high courts, which licenses the advocates who plead before them; and the
[[affiliation-acadmedcn|Academy of Medicine and Manuscripts]], whose scriptorium keeps the manuscript and
illumination trades that grew up around the other two. That is why a Provénzian sends a promising son to
Aurelane and an ambitious one to the capital.

The distinction is felt keenly in both directions. Aurelane regards Jalnasdal as a counting-house that
has acquired a throne. Jalnasdal regards Aurelane as a very expensive library.

## The Stones

The city is built of and on the empire. Its aqueduct still runs; its grid still governs streets laid
out for a garrison that left eight hundred years ago; its forum is a market and its basilica is a
court, both still doing approximately what they were built for.

This continuity is Provénzia's strongest claim to being the true heir of Vilérian civilization in the
west, and Aurelane makes it at every opportunity.

## Politics {#present}

The high courts are the city's power, and they are full. The crown's loan against the salt revenue has raised the Charter's oldest question, whether a levy the Council of Peers never voted can be called something else, and both the crown's lawyers and the Peers' have brought it before the high courts. The justices are Aurelane men, and the city is enjoying being needed by Jalnasdal. The academies are divided on the question as they are on everything: the masters of law have given the Justiciar three opinions, the masters of rhetoric are writing orations for whichever side pays, and the scriptorium copies them all.

At the same time an inheritance suit between two old houses has reached the court, and one party has asked that the Nordland skald [[being-bldnstylgn|Balden Estilgon]], who keeps the family's recited record, be heard in evidence. No Provénzian court has taken a recitation as evidence, and the other party knows what the record says.

## History {#history}

The Vilérian Republic planted the colony on the great road about 110 BF ([[lore-aurelianefound|The Founding of Aurelane]]), withdrew its garrison about 80 BF, and left a town of settlers who married into Tanevar houses. When the legions came ashore below it about 152 AF and broke the Tanevar kings at [[lore-tiravlenbattle|Tiravlen]], the Empire made Aurelane the capital of the province of the West, and for four and a half centuries the _therkoi_ governed the west from its basilica.

In [[lore-charterwar|the War of the Charter]] the _therkos_'s legion held the great road and Aurelane, and the city was the last place in the west where the Empire's writ ran. The peace kept its courts and its academies and gave the crown to the coast. Aurelane accepted the first and has never accepted the second, and it has kept the law of the kingdom since as a consolation it takes very seriously.
