**Jalnasdal** is the capital of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]] and, with some
seventy thousand people, its largest city. It stands on the warm southern coast, on the
[[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]]—a working port as well as a court, which is unusual among the
capitals of [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris]] and explains a good deal about how Provénzia is governed.

Its speech, [Cotiere]{.name data-type="skill/language"}, is the prestige dialect of the kingdom: the most standardized form of
[[skill-provnzlng|Provenzal]], the one used in literature and formal commerce, and the one that shows
the deepest Vilérian influence. To speak Cotiere is to be understood everywhere in Provénzia and to be
placed instantly.

## Crown and Council

Jalnasdal is where the kingdom's central political argument is carried on, and it is not a quiet one.
The Crown sits here, as it has since the first king under the [[lore-chrtrrghts|Charter of Rights]] settled his court on the coast ([[lore-charterwar|the War of the Charter]]); so does the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]], and the last several decades have been a long
maneuver between them over prerogative, taxation and appointment. Neither has won. Great houses have
spent generations positioning themselves in that gap, and cautious ones—like the [[affiliation-hsekorisvar|Kórisvars]] of
[[place-lizela|Lizela]]—have spent the same generations keeping their heads down in it.

Nothing about the contest is revolutionary. It is conducted through offices, marriages, loans and the
placing of relatives, and its participants would be genuinely shocked to be told they were doing
anything but serving the realm.

## The City

A capital that is also a port is a capital that cannot fully separate itself from trade, and Jalnasdal
does not try. Its court and its counting-houses occupy the same streets; ambassadors and factors wait
in the same anterooms; and a great deal of what passes for foreign policy is decided by which
merchants' interests happen to be represented at court that season.

The city is old, dense and magnificent in a slightly overbuilt way, having been added to by every
reign that could afford to and several that could not.

## Politics {#present}

The contest between the Crown and the Council of Peers is the city's weather, and this season it is about money. [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]]'s treasurer has asked the coastal houses for a loan against the salt revenue, and a loan the Council did not consent to is a levy by another name under the Charter. The Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]] and the Count of [[place-athavel|Atavel]] each hold enough of the Council to block the other and not enough to carry it, so the Council has answered nothing. The merchant houses are being asked by both sides to choose, and the cautious ones are discovering that declining to choose is itself an answer.

In the harbor quarter the landlords have doubled the rents on the strength of the court's spending, and the small workshops, among them that of the toymaker [[being-jaranlyskl|Jaren Liskal]], are being squeezed out toward the walls.

## History {#history}

Jalnasdal was a harbor town of the southern shore before the Empire's province was made, and it grew under the province as the port where the coast's wine and oil met Vilérian shipping. [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]] governed; Jalnasdal traded.

In [[lore-charterwar|the War of the Charter]] the port cities held the coast for the estates, and Jalnasdal held it longest. When the estates chose [Ilarven Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, whose house's lands lay on the southern coast, to hold the Charter of Rights, he settled his court in the port rather than in Aurelane, and the Council of Peers sat where the king did. The capital has been a court and a counting-house in the same streets ever since.

In [[lore-warprinces|the War of the Princes]] the coast houses backed the prince who promised a crown strong enough to bind the provinces, and the compromise that ended the war left them richer and the crown no stronger. Every reign since has added to the palace and the harbor, and several have borrowed to do it.
