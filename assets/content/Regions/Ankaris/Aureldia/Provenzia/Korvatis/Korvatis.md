**Korvatis**—_the noon country_; the south, and by long implication the warm and prosperous part.

The **Korvatis** is [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia]] as the world pictures it, and as half of Provénzia actually lives:
some two and a half million people along the warm, sheltered shore of the
[[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] and in the country behind it. It carries the overwhelming majority of
the kingdom's trade, wealth and population, and every other quarter of the realm is in some measure
organized around getting its produce here.

Long sandy beaches, easy anchorages and a climate that is the warmest in the kingdom make this the
natural front door to the Vilérian world—which is exactly what it has been since the empire founded
[[place-aureliane|Aurelane]] on the inland road.

## Its Cities

[[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]]
: the capital and the Crown's seat; a court that is also a port, and where the long contest with the Council of Peers is conducted.

[[place-valarencity|Válaren City]]
: the great luxury port, polished and rich, and unable to survive a blockade.

[[place-kalveza|Kalveza]]
: the working harbor that moves the tonnage while Válaren takes the glamour.

[[place-aureliane|Aurelane]]
: the old Vilérian provincial capital inland, still holding the high courts and the three academies, the [[affiliation-acadrhetrc|Academy of Philosophy and Rhetoric]], the [[affiliation-acadlaw|Academy of Law]] and the [[affiliation-acadmedcn|Academy of Medicine and Manuscripts]].

[[place-lizela|Lizela]]
: a modest coastal town on the kingdom's largest salt flats.

[[place-palvothe|Palvote]]
: the green hill town where the gentry escape the summer, and the last market before the old country.

## Its Interior

Behind the coastal plain the ground rises into the [[place-thuravelhills|Túravel Hills]], which
are the exception to everything above: thinly held, badly mapped, and never really converted from [[affiliation-theoldway|the older faith]]. The
forgotten [[place-shrinethuravelhills|shrine]] is somewhere in them.

The [[place-bldfldcalvenza|Blood-field of Kalvenza]] lies above the shore, a hundred silent acres
in the middle of the most comfortable country in Provénzia.

[[place-thornwood|Thornwood]] stands near the great crossroads of the south, close enough to the roads for a band of brigands to strike them and be under its trees again by dark.

In the hills above Lizela stand the [[place-ruinslizela|Ruins above Lizela]], broken walls older than the Vilérians that the town calls a Tanevar king's hall.

## Its Lords

The earldom of [[place-degavel|Degavel]] lies in the Korvatis, and its earl, [[being-rutalplnvr|Rutal Pélanvri]], is the leading lord of the south. The cities hold of the crown by their charters; the country between them is held by the great houses, of which the [[affiliation-hsepelanvri|Pélanvri]] are the greatest here. West of Degavel lies the county of [[place-ravenshold|Ravenshold]], whose count holds aloof from the quarrels of the south.

## Current Situation {#present}

The south is rich and nervous. The crown's treasurer has asked the coastal houses for a loan against the salt revenue, and every house from [[place-lizela|Lizela]] to [[place-valarencity|Válaren]] is weighing what an answer either way would cost it with the Council of Peers. At [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] the Earl of Degavel leads the lords of the south against the Count of [[place-athavel|Atavel]], and neither has a majority. Válaren's merchant houses still lend short and keep their partners close, because the last blockade broke the trust that held them together. In the hills behind the coast, scholars and agents of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] outfit at [[place-palvothe|Palvote]] for the [[place-thuravelhills|Túravel Hills]], and the hill families answer their questions less helpfully every season.
