## Overview

Degavèl is an earldom of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provènzia]] in the kingdom's southern country, the [[place-korvathis|Korvathìs]], and its earl is one of the magnates who decide how Provènzia's court leans. The present **Earl of Degavèl**, **Rútal alrí Pélanvri**, is prominent and influential, and his leadership in the south gives him real weight in both court politics and military strategy.

## The Pélanvri and Their Rival

The earldom is a family affair. The earl's brother, **Jârvel al Pélanvri**, is Marshal of the Western Army and holds [[place-caerceravel|Torvìla Keravèl]] on the Tarvénian frontier, and another of the Pélanvri holds [[place-belsaren|Belsarèn]] under him. The earl's great rival is the Count of [[place-athavel|Athavèl]]. Their clashes are one of the long-standing magnate rivalries that leave the crown's council gridlocked, and they weaken the kingdom's cohesion exactly when its frontier most needs it.
