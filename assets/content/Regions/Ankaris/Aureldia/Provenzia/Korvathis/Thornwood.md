## Overview

Thornwood is a stretch of woodland in the [[place-korvathis|Korvathìs]], close enough to the great crossroads where the roads from Provènzia, the Merchant Cities and the coastal provinces meet that a band of brigands can strike the road and be back under its trees by dark. That band is **the Road Bandits of Thornwood**, and their captain, the cruel **Captain Kalzòthe**, has a taste for horses.

## The Horses of the Traveler's Rest

The **Traveler's Rest Inn** at the crossroads stables some of the most valuable horses on the southern roads, because merchants and nobles ask for its chief ostler by name, and Kalzòthe's men have tried more than once to steal them. [[being-kjrvngjrln|Chorvàn Jarlèn]], that ostler, has helped recover the stolen animals, which makes him the bandits' enemy and every horse-owner's friend. A traveler who means to ride past Thornwood with a good horse is well advised to ask at the inn first.
