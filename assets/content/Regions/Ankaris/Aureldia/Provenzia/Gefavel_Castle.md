## Overview

Torvila Gefavel is the stronghold of the Princess of [[place-gwefavel|Gefavel]], [[being-itrolaldrvn|Itrola Lédravren]], in the valleys of the [[place-sathvothe|Satvote]]. Some eleven hundred people live in the town below it. It is the seat of a cadet branch of [[affiliation-hseledravren|House Lédravren]], and it is a poorer seat than it was. The trail over [[place-hidralza|Hidralza]] starts in the valley above the town, and the flocks go up it after the thaw.

## History {#history}

The princes of Gefavel held their stronghold and its lands by inheritance and held crown lands besides by grant. In 688 AF [[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]] resumed the grants and took the great seal from the Gefavel prince, and the stronghold lost the revenue that had kept its state ([[lore-velarikreign|The Reign of Vélarik III]]). The prince died without recovering either, and his daughter has held Gefavel since she was seventeen. The town calls the Resumption the Taking.

## Current Situation {#present}

The stronghold is keeping up appearances on borrowed money. The princess holds her court here between the Council's sittings and keeps a house at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] for them, and both cost more than Gefavel pays. Her lawyers come and go from [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]] with drafts of her petition, and a moneylender's agent from Jalnasdal has begun asking the steward about the stronghold's revenue.

## The Holder {#holder}

The princess holds the stronghold and her branch's lands of the crown by right of her blood. Her household is small and loyal, and her steward keeps the town's courts.

## Within the Walls {#within-the-walls}

The hall keeps the branch's charters and the copies of every grant the Gefavel princes held before the Resumption. The princess has the original grants read to her guests.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The grant rolls.** The princess wants the stronghold's old grant rolls compared with the crown's copies at Jalnasdal, and she needs someone the Chancellor's clerks will not recognize.
- **The lender's agent.** The moneylender's man wants to see the stronghold's accounts, and the steward wants him to see a version of them.
