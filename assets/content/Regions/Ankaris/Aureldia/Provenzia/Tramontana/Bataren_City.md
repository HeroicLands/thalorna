**Batáren** stands on the [[place-batarengulf|Gulf of Batáren]] in the northwest, and is Provènzia's
window on the cold ocean and the northern sea-roads. It is the kingdom's second harbour and its most
awkward city.

## A City That Faces the Wrong Way

Provènzia's wealth runs along the warm southern coast, in and out of the Vylarian world, and the
southern ports are the kingdom as foreigners picture it: sunlit, rich, sophisticated, Vylarian in
manner if no longer in law.

Batáren is none of that. It faces the **open ocean**, trades with [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]],
[[place-aelwyth|Aelwyth]] and the northern seas beyond, and takes its money from fish, timber, northern
furs and freight the south has no interest in. Its weather is grey where the south is bright. Its
building is heavy and low. A Provenzian from Válaren finds Batáren cold in every sense and says so.

## The Élavendren

The city's population is substantially **[[lore-elavendrnppl|Élavendren]]** — descendants of settlers
who came down from Élavendre into these borderlands centuries ago, and who have been here longer than
the present arrangement of crowns. They are Provenzian subjects. They are not, in any way that matters
locally, Provenzian.

Provenzian rulers and the settled Élavendren population have real and unresolved quarrels over **land,
rights and tradition** — over inheritance customs the Crown does not recognise, over which observances
are permitted, over who holds what and on whose authority. Nothing about it is settled, and very
little about it is violent. It grinds.

Both crowns are careful to treat this as a Provenzian domestic matter, because relations between
Provènzia and Élavendre are otherwise **very cordial** and neither court wishes to test whether they
would survive being made about Batáren. That care is itself a pressure: everyone in the city
understands that the two kingdoms have agreed not to look closely, which is a great deal of latitude
for anyone who would rather they did.
