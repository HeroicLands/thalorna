The **Temporal Compass** is an ornate enchanted instrument made for one purpose: to get a living person
through [[lore-aralwen|Úqua-Arálwen]] and out again in something close to the time they expected.

Úqua-Arálwen is a _slow_ zone of deep Terrae resonance, and its temporal anomalies are the reason so
few who enter it return. A day inside is not a day outside, the discrepancy is not constant, and a
traveler who simply walks in and walks out may emerge to find that the season has changed or that
years have. The Compass does not remove this. It **stabilizes time in a slight field around the
bearer**, which is enough to make the passage survivable and no more.

## Using It

- It **glows** when pointed along the safest direction, which is not always the shortest one.
- It **vibrates** as it nears the [[miscgear-heartofdhirikri|Heart of Dhiríkri]], more strongly the
  closer it comes.
- Its field covers the bearer and those close by. Straying out of it during a passage is how parties
  become separated by more than distance.

It is an aid to navigation and not a license. Nothing about the Compass addresses the dryads, the
flora, or the [[place-mazeofechos|Maze of Echoes]].

## Who Wants It

The **[[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]]** guard the arálwain and will attempt to seize any
Compass they learn of – both to deny the intrusion and because an instrument that manipulates time
inside an arálwen is precisely the sort of thing they exist to prevent. A party carrying one should
assume the Wardens will know.
