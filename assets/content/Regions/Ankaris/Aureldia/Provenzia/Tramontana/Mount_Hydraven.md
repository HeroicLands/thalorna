**Hýdraven** is the highest of the northern mountains, standing where Provènzia's cold uplands run up
toward [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], and it is the source of the kingdom's water.

The great springs rise on its shoulders. The rivers that water the southern plain, fill
[[place-tiravellake|Lake Tiravel]] and turn every mill in the north begin as meltwater and springwater
off Hýdraven, and Provenzians who have never been within fifty miles of the mountain know its name for
that reason. It is on the coins of two reigns.

## Áelendan Ground

The mountain is [[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan]] country, and more than that: it is one of the
places the tribes hold sacred, and the one they are least flexible about.

They do not forbid the mountain. Shepherds use the lower pastures by arrangements older than the
kingdom, and pilgrims who come the customary way are let alone. What is not tolerated is **survey** –
anyone arriving with instruments, or with an interest in the springs themselves, or with authority
from a crown. Three separate Provenzian attempts to map the watershed have been turned back without a
blow struck and without any possibility of misunderstanding.

Provènzia has never pressed the point, for reasons that are entirely practical. Every argument the
Crown could make about sovereignty is worth less than the water.

## The Cold

Hýdraven is genuinely dangerous ground from autumn to late spring. The high routes close, and the
tribes' own people do not use them in winter either. Bodies come down with the thaw most years, and the
Áelendan bring them to the nearest Provenzian village without comment, which is the whole of the
relationship in one gesture.
