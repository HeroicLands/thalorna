## Overview

Gwéfavel is the stronghold and domain of the **Princess of Gwéfavel**, one of the powerful magnates of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provènzia]]. From it she exerts considerable influence over the region around it, and she uses that influence with a purpose.

## A Clan Restoring Itself

Her clan's influence was diminished under the previous king, and the princess resents the failures that cost it. She has set herself to restoring her family's prestige, and Gwéfavel is the base she works from. She does it in a court already divided by rivalries such as the one between [[place-degavel|Dégavel]] and [[place-athavel|Áthavel]].
