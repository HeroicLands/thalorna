## Overview

Kivarèl is a barony in the western valleys of the [[place-zuravlenrgn|Zuravlèn]] seaboard, and it is held together with the barony of Zuravlèn by **Álegar alrí Zûravel**, who sits at [[place-elthrembis|Elthrembìs]]. Its people are farmers and fruit-growers, and they will talk to any factor about their yields.

## The Field Nobody Discusses

The best land in the barony is the [[place-bldfldkyvarel|Blood-field of Kivarèl]], a quickening where the ground grows too well: grass comes weeks early, grain comes in heavy and first, and fruit sets in years when the rest of the district loses its blossom to frost. Tenants who inherited the holdings farm it thoroughly, and its produce is sold across Provènzia under no particular name. What Kivarèl's people will not discuss is the field itself. A stranger who raises it at table is answered politely once and not answered again.

## Its Baron

Álegar is a prominent supporter of the **Order of the Burning Brand**, and his clan's alignment with that religious faction gives a small agricultural barony more weight in Provenzian affairs than its size suggests. His overlord is **Avaârl alrí Zylávren**, Earl of [[place-harivrencity|Hárivren]].
