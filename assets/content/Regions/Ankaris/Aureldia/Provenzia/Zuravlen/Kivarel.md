## Overview

Kivarel is a barony in the western valleys of the [[place-zuravlenrgn|Zuravlen]] seaboard, with some seven thousand people, and it is held together with the [[affiliation-brnyzrvln|barony of Zuravlen]] by [[being-selvarezrvl|Selvare Zuravel]], who sits at [[place-elthrembis|Eltrembis]]. Its people are farmers and fruit-growers, and they will talk to any factor about their yields.

## The Land {#the-land}

Kivarel is valley country beside the Zuravlen valley, farmed in grain, orchard and pasture, with none of the terraced peach benches that make its neighbor rich. Its markets and the baron's drying yards are at Eltrembis, where its crop goes to be dried and sold, and it has no town of its own. Its one famous ground is the [[place-bldfldkyvarel|Blood-field of Kivarel]].

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Selvare holds Kivarel of [[being-avaarlzlvrn|Avaarl Zilavren]], Earl of Hárivren, and through him of the crown, as he holds Zuravlen, and he governs both from his hall above Eltrembis. Selvare is a prominent supporter of the [[affiliation-brnngbrnd|Order of the Burning Brand]], an order that stands with [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]], and his line's alignment with it gives a small agricultural barony more weight in Provénzian affairs than its size suggests. His clan is divided: his kinsman [[being-kaldinlnvl|Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren]]'s branch keeps the Black Flame, as his overlord the earl does.

## Current Situation {#present}

The field's silence is under strain. A factor from the southern ports has traced the barony's earliest and largest fruit to the field and means to sell it under a name, and the priests of [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]] have asked a third time to settle it. The tenants want neither. Selvare's people want the question kept quiet, because a baron whose best land is a battlefield is a gift to his rivals at Hárivren. The tenants' spokeswoman, [[being-trlmzdms|Tarlema Zedimis]], keeps their lodging at Eltrembis through the harvest and speaks for them to the baron's steward.

## History {#history}

The best land in the barony is the Blood-field of Kivarel, a quickening where the ground grows too well: grass comes weeks early, grain comes in heavy and first, and fruit sets in years when the rest of the district loses its blossom to frost. Which battle made it is not known, for the western valleys were fought over before any record was kept of them. Tenants who inherited the holdings farm it thoroughly, and its produce is sold across [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia]] under no particular name. What Kivarel's people will not discuss is the field itself. A stranger who raises it at table is answered politely once and not answered again.
