In the western valleys, in the barony of Kývarel – held with [[place-zuravlenrgn|Zûravlen]] by Álegar
alrí Zûravel – lies a **quickening**: a [[lore-bloodfields|blood-field]] where the ground grows too
well.

## The Quickening

Grass comes weeks early. Grain comes in heavy and comes in first. Fruit sets large, sets reliably, and
sets in years when the rest of the district loses the blossom to frost.

It is the best land in the barony and it is farmed, thoroughly, by tenants who inherited the holdings
and did not choose them. What is not done is _talked about_. Kývarel's people will discuss the yields
with any factor who asks and will not discuss the field with anyone, and a stranger who raises it at
table is answered politely and not answered again.

## The Difficulty

The awkwardness is not superstition, and everyone involved knows exactly what it is. The produce is
excellent, it is sold across Provènzia under no particular name, and a good deal of it goes into the
preserving trades of Zûravlen – which is to say that some meaningful share of the finest fruit
conserves in Ankaris is grown on a battlefield.

The clergy of [[affiliation-thanatos|Thánatos]] have twice offered to perform the rite of settlement
here. They were refused both times, courteously, by the tenants rather than the lord – on the grounds
that nobody knows whether settling a quickened field would end the quickening, and that the district
cannot afford to find out.
