## Overview

The **Earldom of Hárivren** is the western march of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Crown of Provénzia]]: the long cliff coast of [[place-zuravlenrgn|the Zuravlen Region]] on the cold ocean and the valleys behind it, some three-quarters of a million people. It is held for the crown by [[being-avaarlzlvrn|Avaarl Zilavren]], Earl of Hárivren, from [[place-harivrencity|Hárivren City]], where the roads out of the coastal valleys meet the highway south. Among the Earl's vassals is [[being-selvarezrvl|Selvare Zuravel]], Baron of [[affiliation-brnyzrvln|Zuravlen]], whose fruit and wine pass through the Earl's city and pay his tolls.

The Earl is also the most senior noble of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] in Provénzia, openly and with the crown's knowledge, and the march's present turns on that and on his vote in the Council of Peers.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The Earl rules from Hárivren City. Everything the west produces, wool, salt-fish, timber, hides and the fruit and wine of Zuravlen, is carried inland to the city, sorted, taxed and sold there, and goes south from there; the tolls are the Earl's, and they are the earldom's wealth. The barons of the march, the Baron of Zuravlen and [[place-kyvarel|Kivarel]] among them, hold of the Earl, keep their own courts and bring their levies to him. The Earl holds the march of the crown directly and answers for it, and for his barons, in the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]] at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]].

## Current Situation {#present}

The march is quiet, well governed and pays, and that is the case Jalnasdal makes for leaving it alone. [[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]] has protested about the Earl for thirty years and been thanked for its concern, and every fifth year it renews a complaint about the fire ceremonies that frame the [Lordeme]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}, the Grand Games the city hosts, and loses.

The Earl's vote has become the most valuable in the Council. Avaarl belongs to neither the Earl of Degavel's party nor the Count of Atavel's, and the Peers choose the First Peer again at the year's first sitting. Envoys from both parties have been to Hárivren. Meanwhile the Forge's bishops have petitioned the crown to take the western march from a Flame earl, the petition lies before the Council and wants Peers to second it, and any Peer who wants Hárivren's vote must decide what to say about the petition first.

## Law {#law}

The Earl's courts at Hárivren hear the pleas of the city and of the march beyond the barons' courts, and appeals go from them to the high courts at Aurelane. The Earl's tolls are enforced at the city's gates and bridges, and goods that evade them are forfeit.

## Arms {#arms}

The barons of the march bring their levies to the Earl, and the Earl brings the march's levy to the crown when the estates grant one. The city keeps its walls and its watch. The western coast has almost no harbors worth the name, and the march's sea defense is its cliffs.

## Commerce and Currency {#commerce}

The march lives on fishing, salt, wool, timber and small mixed farming, and on the fruit and wine of the Zuravlen valley, the one product of the western coast known in every market. All of it passes through Hárivren and pays the Earl's tolls. The months around the Lordeme triple the city and bring enormous sums in wagers.

## Faith {#faith}

The march follows the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] like the rest of the kingdom, and its Earl keeps the Black Flame openly with his household. The Black Flame's place at Hárivren is the most open it has anywhere in the Aureldian world, and its activity in the west is harder to act against here than it would be elsewhere.

## History {#history}

Hárivren is older than Provénzia. It was a stronghold on its river crossing long before any crown claimed it, and its walls follow the line the first ones did. The march became an earldom under the crown, and the Zilavren family has held it for at least four generations, shaping the ceremonies of the Lordeme toward fire throughout. Avaarl succeeded his father as Earl in 690 AF, and the Forge's protests began with him, because he was the first Earl to keep the Flame without concealing it.
