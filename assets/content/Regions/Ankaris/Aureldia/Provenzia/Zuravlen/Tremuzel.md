## Overview {#overview}

Tremuzel is a salt and fishing port on the cliff coast of the [[place-zuravlenrgn|Zuravlen Region]], a day's sail north of [[place-volsather|Volsater]] on the cold ocean. Some five hundred people live in a cleft where a stream has cut the cliff down to a shingle beach, and the cleft is the whole of the harbor. It lies outside the barony of Zuravlen, on land the Earl of [[place-harivrencity|Hárivren]] holds himself, and it is the lesser of the two harbors on the Zuravlen coast. Its name is the Provenzal word for a salt rake.

## Arriving {#arriving}

From the land, Tremuzel is reached by a cart track off the coast road that drops through the cleft in a series of turns too tight for a team of more than two. From the sea it is a gap in the cliff that a master must know to find, with a stone mole on the north side of the beach that breaks the swell. Boats are hauled up the shingle at night and in every blow. The town stands above the beach on the cleft's south wall, low stone houses roofed with slabs against the wind, and the salt pans lie above it on the flat top of the cliff.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

**Salt.** The pans on the clifftop are older and larger than Volsater's, and they are the town's living. Sun and the wind off the ocean dry them through the summer, and the crop goes inland by cart to [[place-harivrencity|Hárivren City]], where the earl's toll is taken on it, and on to the preserving trades of the valleys.

**Fish.** The boats work the cold water off the cliffs and come back loaded, and the catch is salted and dried on the beach. Tremuzel has no fast boats, because no peach reaches it fresh: it lies too far from the valley, and the cleft's track is too slow for fruit.

**Eggs and down.** The cliffs on either side of the cleft are full of seabirds, and in spring the egg-gatherers go down them on ropes for eggs and down. The cliffs also hold [[being-crgclmbr|cragclimbers]], and each gatherer knows which stretch of rock is a cragclimber's and keeps off it.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The town and its pans belong to the earl, and his reeve, [[being-iltrgmls|Iltera Gaimelis]], keeps them for him. She measures the salt, takes the earl's share at the pans before it ever reaches the toll at Hárivren, sets the order in which the pans are opened, and settles the beach's quarrels over boat-places and nets. A dispute she cannot settle goes to the earl's court at Hárivren City, two days inland, which is far enough that most are settled.

## The Present {#present}

The salt has never been worth more, and Volsater wants it. The preserving trades inland buy salt from Volsater's pans as well as Tremuzel's, and the barony of [[affiliation-brnyzrvln|Zuravlen]] would rather pay its own baron's harbor than the earl's toll. This summer Volsater's masters have begun cutting the price of their salt at the valley markets, and the reeve suspects the baron's steward of helping them. Iltera has asked the earl's court for leave to sell Tremuzel's salt below the toll to win the valley back, and she is waiting on an answer from Hárivren while the pans fill.

The beach has a quarrel of its own. A boat went out in the spring fog and did not come in, and its master's family say the egg-gatherers saw it under the cliffs and did not call it.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

There is no inn. The reeve's house keeps a room for the earl's officers and lets it to others when none are in town, and the salt-carters sleep in the loft over the weighing shed in the season.

## History {#history}

The pans on the clifftop have been worked for as long as anyone in the town can say, and the mole is older than the houses. The earls of Hárivren took the town into their own hand long ago, because its salt is worth more to them than any baron's service for it. Its fortune has run with the salt: the town is larger in a year when the preserving trades buy heavily and smaller when they do not, and Volsater's rise as the barony's harbor has turned the two ports into rivals for the valley's custom.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The boat that did not come in.** The lost master's family want to know whether the egg-gatherers left him to the rocks, and the gatherers will say only that the fog was thick.
- **Salt below the toll.** If the earl's court gives Iltera leave, a cart train of cheap salt goes up the cleft and into the barony's markets, and the baron's steward means to stop it on the road.
- **The cliff below the north pans.** A cragclimber has moved onto the stretch of cliff the gatherers work most, and Iltera will pay a party to move it off before the gatherers stop going down.
