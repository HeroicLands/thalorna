**Vergéval** holds the best of the upper terraces, and is the wealthiest manor in
[[place-zuravlenrgn|Zûravlen]] and the most precarious. Nearly its whole demesne is under peach. In a
good year its lord out-earns the baron; in a frost year he borrows, and everyone knows it.

Use Vergéval as the model of a **manor run for a single high-value crop**.

## The Manor

- **Demesne.** Some forty hectares of terrace, almost all orchard, worked by labor services and—at
  harvest—by hired hands the manor cannot do without.
- **Tenancies.** Around fifty holdings, most of them small terrace strips. Rent is paid in fruit,
  reckoned at the grading table's marks, which means a tenant's rent depends on a stranger's judgment
  in Mercadaure. This is the manor's standing grievance.
- **Water.** Vergéval holds the earliest water-turns on the _règa_, granted before living memory and
  challenged roughly once a generation. The manor's charter is kept in the church rather than the hall,
  for reasons the family does not discuss.
- **Banalities.** The lord's press and the lord's oven. Vergéval has its **own drying yard**—one of
  only three in the barony—so it does not pay the baron's share, and it takes a share from the
  holdings around it that use the yard instead.
- **The graft-house.** A heated shed where winter grafting is done. The manor's grafters are the best
  in Provènzia and are lent out, at a price, as far as the coast.

## Officers and Custom

A **steward** who runs the estate, a **bailiff** for the demesne, and an elected **reeve** who speaks
for the tenants and is nobody's friend by Michaelmas. The **court baron** sits four times a year and
oftener in harvest, and its rolls are mostly water, folding and fruit-theft.

The manor's custom obliges every tenant to keep the **frost vigil**—to attend with prunings and
straw when the bell rings, at any hour, on pain of a fine that has never once been remitted.

## The Difficulty

Vergéval has no grain to speak of and buys its bread. It has too few sheep to dung its own terraces and
must contract for folding with [[place-estivren|Estivren]], whose shepherds know exactly how badly it
is needed. And it is the manor a late frost ruins first, being highest.

It is, in short, rich, hungry, dependent and frightened—which is why its lord is the most politically
active man in the barony after the baron himself.
