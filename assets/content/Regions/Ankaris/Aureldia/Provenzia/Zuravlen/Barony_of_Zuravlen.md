## Overview

The **Barony of Zuravlen** is the valley holding of Baron [[being-selvarezrvl|Selvare Zuravel]], who holds it for the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Crown of Provénzia]] through his overlord, [[being-avaarlzlvrn|Avaarl Zilavren]], the Earl of Hárivren ([[affiliation-erldmhrvrn|Earldom of Hárivren]]). It is one valley of [[place-zuravlenrgn|the Zuravlen Region]], some twelve thousand souls, and it grows the finest peaches on the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]] and some of its better wine. Its seat is [[place-elthrembis|Eltrembis]], the market town where the grading records, water-rolls and manor customs are kept. Selvare is also Baron of [[place-kyvarel|Kivarel]] in the western valleys, which he holds with Zuravlen.

Its present is a quarrel between the baron's drying yards and the fast boats of his own harbor.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The baron rules from his hall above Eltrembis. He keeps the barony's court, the water-rolls that record every family's water-turn on the _reva_ channels, and the manor customs, and he appoints the sworn graders who sort every basket of fruit at the grading table into three marks. A grader holds office for three years and may not own an orchard. The manors of the valley, [[place-palnebris|Palnebris]], [[place-blavera|Blavera]] and [[place-estivren|Estivren]] among them, answer to lords of their own who hold of the baron, and their manor courts hold their most crowded sessions during the autumn fair. The harbor of [[place-volsather|Volsater]] is the baron's.

The baron answers to the Earl of Hárivren for the barony's levy and its peace, and through the Earl to the crown. He does not sit in the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]], where the Earl answers for him.

## Current Situation {#present}

The drying yards south of Eltrembis belong to the baron. Every holding without a yard of its own must dry its crop there and pay him a share, which is his largest single income and the barony's most resented charge. The fast boats of Volsater carry fresh fruit to tables that had only ever seen it dried, and every basket that leaves fresh is a basket that pays no share. The harbor and the yards are in competition, and Selvare owns both.

The smallholders of the upper valley have built drying racks of their own this year and refuse the yard share. Somebody is selling first marks at the grading table, and the factors of [[place-batarencity|Bataren]] who buy at the fair have begun to notice. In Kivarel the best land is the [[place-bldfldkyvarel|Blood-field of Kivarel]], a quickening whose fruit sets in years when the rest of the district loses its blossom, and the baron wants it farmed through another harvest without questions.

## Law {#law}

The baron's court hears the barony's pleas, and the most litigated property in Zuravlen is the water-turn, which is worth more than a house. The manor courts hear their own tenants. A grader who takes a bribe loses his office and is fined, and selling fruit as first mark that the table has not graded is fraud before the baron's court. The heaviest penalty the court gives is outlawry from the barony.

## Commerce and Currency {#commerce}

The barony lives on peaches and wine from the sheltered middle valley, salt and fish from the coast at Volsater, and wool and cheese from the uplands. The autumn fair at Eltrembis runs eight days after the harvest, and factors from Bataren and the coastal ports buy the dried fruit and the honey-crocks there. The fruit and wine go inland to [[place-harivrencity|Hárivren City]] and pay the Earl's tolls there.

## Faith {#faith}

Zuravlen's devotion falls to [[affiliation-olvenia|Ólvenia]], who receives the first-picked basket from every orchard at the shrine in Eltrembis, and to [[affiliation-lizelas|Lizelas]], to whom the blossom and the frost vigil are dedicated. The baron's line is a prominent supporter of the [[affiliation-brnngbrnd|Order of the Burning Brand]], which stands with [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]], and that gives a small agricultural barony more weight in Provénzian affairs than its size suggests. His [Zuravel]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} clan is divided on the kingdom's faith quarrel, and its Lénavel branch at [[place-leravren|Leravren]] keeps [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]].

## History {#history}

The valley has been terraced and watered for some four hundred years, and the water-turns recorded at Eltrembis are older than the barony's present lords. The barony has been held of the Earls of Hárivren since the western march was an earldom, and its fruit has paid their tolls for as long. Three generations ago a scandal at the grading table produced the rule that no grader may own an orchard, and the fast boats of Volsater are the newest thing in it: within living memory fresh Zuravlen fruit was unknown outside the valley, and now it reaches the southern tables before it spoils.
