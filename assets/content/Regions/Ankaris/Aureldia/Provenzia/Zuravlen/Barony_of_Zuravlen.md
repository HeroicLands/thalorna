The **Barony of Zûravlen** is the valley holding of Baron **Álegar alrí Zûravel**, who holds it of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Crown of Provènzia]] and sits at [[place-elthrembis|Elthrembìs]], the market town where the grading records, water-rolls and manor customs are kept. His overlord in the western march is the Earl of [[place-harivrencity|Hárivren]]. The barony governs [[place-elthrembis|Elthrembìs]] and the harbor of [[place-volsather|Volsathèr]]; the manors of the valley answer to lords of their own.
