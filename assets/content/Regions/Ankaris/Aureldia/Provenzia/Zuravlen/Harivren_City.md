**Hárivren City** is the great city of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provenzia]]'s **western march**—inland, on the river that gathers the
coastal valleys before they run down to the sea, at the point where the roads out of
the [[place-zuravlenrgn|Zuravlen Region]] and the northern coast meet the highway south.

It is not a port and never has been; the western coast has almost no harbors worth the name. What
Hárivren has instead is _convergence_. Everything the west produces—wool, salt-fish, timber, hides,
and the fruit and wine of the Zuravlen valley—is carried inland to Hárivren, sorted, taxed and sold
there, and goes south from there to the rich Vylarian coast. Some thirty-four thousand people live on
the proceeds.

The name belongs to the older stratum, like [[place-yharvalenhenge|Yarvalen Henge]], and the city is older than
Provenzia. It was a stronghold on that river crossing long before any crown claimed it, and the walls
still follow the line the first ones did.

## The Earl

Hárivren is the seat of **[Avaarl Zylavren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Earl of Hárivren**, who holds the western march for the
Crown and commands great respect and considerable power. Among his vassals is **[Selvare Zuravel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}**,
Baron of the [[affiliation-brnyzrvln|Barony of Zuravlen]] and [[place-kyvarel|Kivarel]]; the fruit and wine that make Zuravlen famous pass through Avaarl's city
and pay his tolls.

He is also **the most senior noble of [[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]] in Provenzia**, and he
does not conceal it.

## The Flame at Court

This is the fact about Hárivren that everyone outside Provenzia finds difficult to believe.

The Black Flame is suppressed across most of the Aureldian world and works through secretive orders and
hidden shrines. In Provenzia it has an earl—openly, at the head of a march, with the Crown's
knowledge. Nothing about Avaarl's devotion is secret; his household observes it; [[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]] has protested about it for thirty years and been thanked for
its concern.

The usual explanation given at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] is that a Flame lord who can be seen is worth more than a
dozen who cannot, and that the western march is quiet, well governed and pays. Whether that is
statecraft or an accommodation nobody has the standing to refuse is a question Provenzians ask each
other and not strangers. It is, in any case, why the cult's activity in Provenzia—at
Yarvalen above all—is so much harder to act against here than it would be
anywhere else.

## The Lordeme

Every fifth year Hárivren hosts the **[Lordeme]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}**, the Provenzian Grand Games,
also called the Grand Games: the realm's greatest festival of martial prowess and athletic excellence,
drawing competitors and spectators from across Provenzia and well beyond it. A season of Provenzia's
politics arranges itself around the Games.

For the months around the Games the city triples. Nobles negotiate alliances, debts and revenge in the
open; enormous sums are wagered; the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] keeps an official
presence to watch for magical interference, and finds enough of it to justify returning. Rumors of
poison and arranged defeat circulate every cycle and are occasionally true.

The Games are the earldom's chief instrument of prestige, and the ceremonies that frame them—which
involve a great deal of fire, and which the Earl's family has been quietly shaping for four
generations—are the subject of a complaint the Sacred Forge renews every five years, and loses.
