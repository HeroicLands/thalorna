## Overview

Áthavel is a county of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provènzia]], and its count is a politically embattled man. He fights on two fronts at once: inside his own family, where disputes divide his house, and at court, where he is the Earl of [[place-degavel|Dégavel]]'s great rival.

## A Rivalry the Kingdom Pays For

The quarrel between Áthavel and Dégavel is one of the long-standing rivalries among Provènzia's magnates, and it is a large part of why the crown's council so often cannot act. Each lord measures his own standing by the other's, and the kingdom's cohesion suffers for it at the moment external threat is greatest.
