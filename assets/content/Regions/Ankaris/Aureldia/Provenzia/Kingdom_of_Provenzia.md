## Overview

The Kingdom of Provenzia is a prosperous, cultured realm that won its independence from the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] roughly a century ago, in [[lore-charterwar|the War of the Charter]], fought for the estates' rights against a _therkos_ (governor) rather than for a crown against an emperor—long enough that the memory of imperial rule has faded into a sense of cultural inheritance rather than resentment. Provenzia took the best of Vylarian civilization—its law, its learning, its architecture—and married it to a native tradition of artistic expression and courtly refinement. The kingdom holds the land of the [[place-provenzrgn|Provenzia Region]].

## Government

The kingdom is ruled by a monarch whose power is constrained by a [Charter of Rights]{.name data-type="lore/law"} won in the War of the Charter. Its first king, [Ylarven Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of [House Lédravren]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, was chosen by the estates to hold it, and his house has held the crown since; the one succession the Charter did not settle was fought out in [[lore-warprinces|the War of the Princes]]. The nobility holds significant authority through a [Council of Peers]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, and the major cities maintain chartered rights including self-governance on local matters. This produces a political culture of negotiation and consensus that is more stable than Vylaria's factional scheming, though no less intricate.

The crown's relationship with the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] is distinctive: Provenzia welcomes the Arcane Order but insists on royal oversight of its activities. Mages in Provenzia answer to the crown as well as to the Arcane Order, a compromise that irritates the Arcane Order's leadership but has produced a more open magical culture than exists anywhere else in the [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión Region]].

## The Crown and the Magnates

[[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]] was succeeded by [Kralen Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who reigns now. Kralen is a pragmatic king whose work is to hold together a kingdom that the Charter, the Council of Peers and the great houses all pull on at once. Each king since [[lore-warprinces|the War of the Princes]] has worked to strengthen the crown, and the balance between the crown, the nobles and the local leaders remains precarious.

## The Two Peoples

The kingdom holds two peoples who share a Charter and not much else. The **Tanevar** are the old people of the country. They remember their petty kings, broken at [[lore-tiravlenbattle|Tiravlen]], and the habit of governing themselves in their own valleys. The **Provenzians** are the city-dwellers, the heirs of the old Vylarian province ([[lore-aurelianefound|The Founding of Aurelane]]), and the crown governs from their cities. The Tanevar hold that the Charter was the cities' and that the country was not asked ([[lore-charterwar|the War of the Charter]]).

The quarrel is about land, local government and whose kingdom this is. Rural Tanevar resist the cities' officers in a hundred small ways and now and then rise in open rebellion. [[lore-warprinces|The War of the Princes]] drew the line between the two peoples where it runs today.

## Faith and the Crown

The first king, Ylarven, kept the estates together by letting each keep its faith, and his successors held to that tolerance and to power-sharing between the great houses and the local lords. The twelve faiths of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] are the religions of the kingdom, and [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] is officially favored and politically powerful. [[affiliation-brazemas|The Faith of Brazemas]] and [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] live beside it uneasily.

The Forge grows more dominant every year, and the friction is worst where an older or smaller faith still holds a district, as the Black Flame does in Leravren and Hárivren ([[place-leravren|Leravren]]).

## Pressures

The crown is pressed from within and without, and each pressure feeds the others:

- **The magnates.** Their rivalries leave the Council gridlocked, and lords distracted by personal quarrels cannot present a united front to a foreign threat.
- **The towns.** Factional disputes and a thin economy produce unrest that erodes the crown's control.
- **The roads and coasts.** Banditry and piracy disrupt the trade the kingdom lives on and strain the lords who are meant to keep order.
- **Tarvénia.** The crown of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] presses territorial claims and uses every sign of weakness in Provenzia's court. The skirmishing on the eastern uplands of [[place-talothe|the Talote]] drains the kingdom's men and money and tests its unity.

The kingdom's place as a crossroads of trade, with mineral wealth in the eastern uplands, makes it a prize as well as a power.

## Character

Provenzia's identity is built on beauty, learning, and refinement. Its cities are famous for their academies, where philosophy, natural science, magical theory, and the arts are studied side by side. The Provenzian court is the most elegant in the western part of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]], and its fashions, music, and literary forms set the standard for aristocratic culture across the region.

This emphasis on culture is not mere decoration. Provenzians believe that civilization's highest expression is artistic and intellectual achievement, and they invest accordingly. Patronage of scholars, artists, and musicians is not just expected of the nobility—it is the primary measure of a noble house's prestige.

## Commerce and Currency

Kingdom of Provenzia uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vylarian currency]] (Solden / Ardin / Ogdel) as its standard currency, with full access to the paper-script system through [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]]-member moneylenders chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]]. Kingdom of Provenzia is a post-imperial commonwealth member—it participates fully in the trade and banking infrastructure of the Vylarian system but is not subject to imperial governance or imperial taxation. See [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vylarian Currency]] for the full system.
