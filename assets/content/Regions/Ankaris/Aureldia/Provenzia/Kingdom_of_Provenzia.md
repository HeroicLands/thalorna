## Overview

The Kingdom of Provénzia is a prosperous, cultured realm in the southwest of [[place-aureldirgn|Aureldia]], and it holds the land of the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]]. Its king, [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] of [[affiliation-hseledravren|House Lédravren]], reigns from [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] on the warm southern coast over some five million people, Provénzian city-dwellers and the Tanevar of the old country. He reigns under the [[lore-chrtrrghts|Charter of Rights]], which binds him to take counsel of the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]], to levy only by the estates' consent and to leave the chartered cities their own law.

The kingdom won its independence from the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] roughly a century ago, in [[lore-charterwar|the War of the Charter]], fought for the estates' rights against a _therkos_ (governor) rather than for a crown against an emperor. That is long enough that the memory of imperial rule has faded into a sense of cultural inheritance rather than resentment. Provénzia took the best of Vilérian civilization, its law, its learning and its architecture, and married it to a native tradition of artistic expression and courtly refinement.

The tension that defines its present is the oldest one in it: a crown that wants to govern and a Council that makes it pay for every act, with the magnates' rivalries deciding which side the Council takes.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The king decides, and the Charter decides what he may decide alone. He appoints and dismisses the great officers, grants land and office, makes war and peace and keeps the high justice. He takes counsel of the Council of Peers on war, the great offices and the crown's grants, and a levy needs the estates' consent, which the crown asks for in the Council. The major cities keep chartered rights, among them self-governance on local matters, under their own councils, and the crown's officers do not govern inside their walls. This produces a political culture of negotiation and consensus that is more stable than Viléria's factional scheming, though no less intricate.

The crown's relationship with the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] is distinctive: Provénzia welcomes the Arcane Order but insists on royal oversight of its activities. Mages in Provénzia answer to the crown as well as to the Arcane Order, a compromise that irritates the Arcane Order's leadership but has produced a more open magical culture than exists anywhere else in the [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión Region]].

## Current Situation {#present}

Kralen Lédravren has reigned since 709 AF, when his uncle Vélarik III died without a son and the succession passed to him without a quarrel ([[lore-velarikreign|The Reign of Vélarik III]]). He is a pragmatic king whose work is to hold together a kingdom that the Charter, the Council of Peers and the great houses all pull on at once. Each king since [[lore-warprinces|the War of the Princes]] has worked to strengthen the crown, and the balance between the crown, the nobles and the local leaders remains precarious.

- **The Council.** The Earl of Degavel holds the Council as First Peer, and the Count of Atavel holds the seal. The Peers choose the First Peer again at the first sitting of each year, and the Atavel party needs one more voice to take the office. The Earl of Hárivren belongs to neither party, and both are bidding for him.
- **Gefavel.** Itrola Lédravren's petition for her branch's grants and the great seal stands before the Council. The king keeps the resumed lands because they pay for his household, and he has no child; the Charter gives the Council no ruling on who follows him.
- **The treasury.** The frontier, the navy and the court cost more than the crown takes. The Treasurer has asked the coastal houses for a loan against the salt revenue of [[place-lizela|Lizela]] rather than ask the Council for a levy, and every house that answers takes a side.
- **The towns.** Factional disputes and a thin economy produce unrest that erodes the crown's control, and banditry and piracy disrupt the trade the kingdom lives on.
- **Tarvénia.** The crown of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] presses territorial claims and uses every sign of weakness in Provénzia's court. The skirmishing in the eastern uplands of [[place-talothe|the Talote]] drains the kingdom's men and money, and the Marshal and the Warden there send the king accounts of the same raids that do not agree.

The kingdom's place as a crossroads of trade, with mineral wealth in the eastern uplands, makes it a prize as well as a power.

## The Two Peoples

The kingdom holds two peoples who share a Charter and not much else. The **Tanevar** are the old people of the country. They remember their petty kings, broken at [[lore-tiravlenbattle|Tiravlen]], and the habit of governing themselves in their own valleys. The **Provénzians** are the city-dwellers, the heirs of the old Vilérian province ([[lore-aurelianefound|The Founding of Aurelane]]), and the crown governs from their cities. The Tanevar hold that the Charter was the cities' and that the country was not asked ([[lore-charterwar|the War of the Charter]]).

The quarrel is about land, local government and whose kingdom this is. Rural Tanevar resist the cities' officers in a hundred small ways and now and then rise in open rebellion. [[lore-warprinces|The War of the Princes]] drew the line between the two peoples where it runs today.

## Law {#law}

Provénzian law is Vilérian law as received, amended and argued over at Aurelane, where the high courts sit under the Justiciar and the Academy of Law licenses the advocates who plead before them. A city's own court tries what happens inside its walls under its charter, a lord's court tries his manors, and the high courts hear appeals and every claim that the crown has broken the Charter. The march has its own court, the Winter Assize, which sits at Durvalis while the passes are closed. A contract is an act when a notary has sealed it.

The penalties run from a fine and a lost patron up to banishment from a city and the unnaming, which takes a name from the roll. Treason against the crown is tried before the king's justices and forfeits land and life. Mages answer to the crown's oversight as well as to the Arcane Order, and a working the crown has not licensed is tried as a crime.

## Arms {#arms}

The crown has two standing forces and calls a levy for the rest. The Western Army holds the Tarvénian frontier from Torvila Keravel under its Marshal, and the garrison of Durvalis holds the passes under the Warden of the Talote, who musters the march's levy every spring and disperses it every autumn. The Admiral's ships, [[affiliation-crownships|the Crown's Ships]], keep the coasts, and they are fewer than she asks for. A levy beyond the march needs the estates' consent, and a city's wall, watch and guard are its own.

## Commerce and Currency {#commerce}

Kingdom of Provénzia uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian currency]] (Solden / Ardin / Ogdel) as its standard currency, with full access to the paper-script system through [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]]-member moneylenders chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]]. Kingdom of Provénzia is a post-imperial commonwealth member—it participates fully in the trade and banking infrastructure of the Vilérian system but is not subject to imperial governance or imperial taxation. See [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian Currency]] for the full system.

The crown lives on its own estates, on the tolls of its roads and ports, and on the salt of the southern coast, and it borrows from the coastal houses when those fall short.

## Faith {#faith}

The first king, Ilarven, kept the estates together by letting each keep its faith, and his successors held to that tolerance and to power-sharing between the great houses and the local lords. The twelve faiths of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] are the religions of the kingdom, and [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] is officially favored and politically powerful. [[affiliation-brazemas|The Faith of Brazemas]] and [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] live beside it uneasily.

The Forge grows more dominant every year, and the friction is worst where an older or smaller faith still holds a district, as the Black Flame does in Leravren and Hárivren ([[place-leravren|Leravren]]).

## Character

Provénzia's identity is built on beauty, learning, and refinement. Its learning is kept at [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]], in three academies: the [[affiliation-acadrhetrc|Academy of Philosophy and Rhetoric]], the [[affiliation-acadlaw|Academy of Law]] and the [[affiliation-acadmedcn|Academy of Medicine and Manuscripts]]. The arts are the patrons' and the cities', and the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] teaches magical theory under the crown's oversight. The Provénzian court is the most elegant in the western part of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]], and its fashions, music, and literary forms set the standard for aristocratic culture across the region.

This emphasis on culture is not mere decoration. Provénzians believe that civilization's highest expression is artistic and intellectual achievement, and they invest accordingly. Patronage of scholars, artists, and musicians is not just expected of the nobility—it is the primary measure of a noble house's prestige.

## History {#history}

### The Province of the West (to about 609 AF)

For nearly five centuries the land was the Empire's province of the West, governed by _therkoi_ from Aurelane with the advice of the estates, the assemblies of nobles, towns and guilds. The Tanevar kings of the country had been broken at Tiravlen long before ([[lore-tiravlenbattle|The Breaking of the Tanevar Kings]]), and the Nordheim fleets that struck the coast from 519 to 530 AF showed how little the _therkoi_ could defend it ([[lore-southrnfleets|The Southern Fleets]]).

### The War of the Charter (about 609 to 618 AF)

The estates refused the _therkos_ a levy, then refused his legion, and fought it for nine years. The peace gave them the Charter of Rights, held by a king they chose from among themselves, [Ilarven Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the Korvatis House of Lédravren, and the Empire stopped sending _therkoi_ ([[lore-charterwar|The War of the Charter]]). Ilarven settled his court at Jalnasdal, where the money was, and left the law and the learning at Aurelane.

### The Charter Kings (618 to 653 AF)

Ilarven's house held the crown and the Charter held the house. The first kings kept the estates together by letting each keep its faith and by sharing power between the great houses and the local lords. [Padran Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"} reigned from 647 and died in 653 without naming an heir.

### The War of the Princes (653 to 655 AF)

Two princes of the blood fought for the crown, one backed by the centralizing houses of the coast and one by the Tanevar country and the regional lords, and Keravel Vale was the battlefield. House Dorgal was broken, and a Tarvénian host walked the eastern road unopposed. The moderate houses ended it by crowning a third man, [Dalenar Lédravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who confirmed the Charter and left the succession where it had been ([[lore-warprinces|The War of the Princes]]). Durvalis was built after the war to hold that road.

### The Strengthening Crown (655 to 688 AF)

From Dalenar on, each king worked to strengthen the crown inside the Charter's forms, and the crown and the Council of Peers began the long contest over prerogative, taxation and appointment that the court has not finished. It was conducted through offices, marriages, loans and the placing of relatives.

### Vélarik III (688 to 709 AF)

[[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]] took the crown in 688 and at once resumed the crown lands the princes of Gefavel held by grant and took the great seal from their prince. The lands paid for his household for twenty years, and the Gefavel branch lost its standing. He gave the seal in 704 to Velzore Aitevis, and he died in 709 without a son ([[lore-velarikreign|The Reign of Vélarik III]]).

### Kralen (from 709 AF)

Vélarik's nephew Kralen Lédravren succeeded him without a quarrel, kept the resumed lands and the Chancellor, and reigns now.
