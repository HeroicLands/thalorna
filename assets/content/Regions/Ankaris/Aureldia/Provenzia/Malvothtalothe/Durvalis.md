**Durvalis**—the bright castle—commands the road below the Tarvénian passes, and is the
military seat of [[place-talothe|Talote]], the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]]'s eastern march.

It is not an old town, which distinguishes it from almost everything else on this frontier. It was
built after [[lore-warprinces|the War of the Princes]], deliberately and at considerable expense, to hold a road that had been raided along since [[lore-malnebris|Malnebris]] and that a Tarvénian host had walked unopposed in the war's second year. Its walls are the newest of any consequence in the kingdom: high, thick, unromantic,
and laid out by somebody who had read the Vilérian manuals and believed them.

## History {#history}

The road below the passes has been raided since the first great raid came down it at [[lore-malnebris|Malnebris]] about 556 AF, and for a century the province and then the kingdom held it with the levy and a few towers. In the second year of [[lore-warprinces|the War of the Princes]] a Tarvénian host walked down it unopposed, because every man who might have stopped it was fighting for one prince or the other in [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]].

The crown that ended the war resolved that it would not happen again. Durvalis was laid out after the peace on open ground below the passes, walled to the Vilérian manuals, and given a permanent garrison and the march's muster. The Winter Assize came to sit in it because the march's officers wintered there, and the town has been the frontier's seat since.

## The Muster

Durvalis is where the march assembles. The permanent garrison is modest; what matters is that the
town is the point every levy, every remount, every cart of the eastern frontier passes through, and
the place from which any response to a raid is actually launched.

Its year is the campaign year. Through the winter, when the passes close and the mountains are
impassable, the town is shut in and idle and its population halves. From the thaw it fills: soldiers,
carters, farriers, victualers, and the trade that follows them. Provisioning comes up from
[[place-tiravellake|Lake Tiravel]] and through [[place-pirnebris|Pirnebris]], and Durvalis eats
very well for a frontier post.

## The Winter Assize

Because nothing can be fought in winter, winter is when the march does its law. The [[affiliation-wntrassize|Winter Assize]]
sits at Durvalis from the closing of the passes to the thaw, and hears everything the campaign
season has had no time for—claims over stolen stock, disputed ransoms, the standing quarrels of
border families, and the occasional charge against an officer that could not safely be brought while
he was needed.

It is the most honest court in Provénzia, in the estimation of the people who appear before it, largely
because everyone involved is snowed in together and will be until spring.

## Current Situation {#present}

The campaign season is at its height and the town is full. The levy is out along the road, the remount lines run to the walls, and the victualers are already laying in for a winter when the passes shut and nothing comes up from the lake for months. That store is the town's present quarrel. Barrels in the garrison's winter store have begun to spoil, and they carry the mark of the salter [[being-chynrkrsvr|Chinara Kórisvar]], who says the mark is forged by a rival victualer. The quartermaster must have a sound store before the snow, and the Winter Assize must hear the claim and counterclaim when it sits, with both parties shut in with the court until spring.
