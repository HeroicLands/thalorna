East of [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]], the hills rise in long bare ridges grazed by nobody. [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]]'s shepherds drive their flocks the long way round them, and the road that skirts the foot of the slope is one [[affiliation-twinbldrdr|the Twinblade Order]] holds.

## Layers {#history}

**The battle.** About 248 AF the Cohorts of Gashang and the Knights of the Sacred Forge destroyed each other on these slopes, a storm ended the fighting, and the Black Flame's Ashlord fell with his maul beside him ([[lore-bloodhillsbtl|The Battle of the Blood Hills]]).

**The shrine.** The Flame's loyalists are said to have carried the maul to a cave shrine nearby and set traps around it for a worthy successor. The Flame has kept a shrine in the hills on the night of the battle since.

**The vigil.** The Forge made the battle its victory, and its knights have kept the battle-night vigil at the foot of the slope for as long as either faith has had a presence in the vale.

## The Field

The **Blood Hills** are a [[lore-bloodfield|blood-field]] made by [[lore-bloodhillsbtl|the battle]] in which the [Cohorts of Gashang]{.name data-type="affiliation"} of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] and the [Knights of the Sacred Forge]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"} destroyed each other about 248 AF. Its kind is a **dread**: the field returns what was felt on it, and what was felt on these slopes was the unease of a line that knew it was losing and could not leave.

The field is moderately thin. A traveler who lingers on the ridges feels ranks drawn up on the slope above him and does not see them, and by dusk the feeling has become a conviction that the line will break in his direction. On the slope the legend gives the [[scenario-maulgashng|maul]], ember-lights show after dark, flickering where nothing burns. Around the cave shrine, where the Flame still keeps the night of the battle, the field is thin enough that the lights have been followed.

## How the Place Is Treated

Keravel's shepherds do not graze it. The Knights of the Forge keep a vigil at the foot of the slope on the night of the battle, and the Twinblades hold the road beside it, as they hold a threshold at a few battlefields, to guard it and not to keep it. The Black Flame keeps the shrine. No rite of settlement has been performed here, because the priests of [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]] will not go where the Black Flame keeps a shrine.

## Who Goes

- **Bandits**, who use the hills because pursuers will not follow.
- **Knights of the Forge**, on the night of the battle.
- **Black Flame seekers**, who come for the shrine and the relic in it.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The hills have more visitors than they want. The outlaws of Keravel Vale shelter on the ridges because pursuers will not follow them there, and the riders sent after them turn back at the foot of the slope. The Knights of the Forge expect trouble at this year's vigil, with a Black Flame lord at [[place-leravren|Leravren]] beyond the vale and the Flame's keepers more numerous at the shrine than the knights can remember. Seekers come for the relic the legend puts in the cave, and the Twinblades on the road below let them pass and count them.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The bandits' ground.** The outlaws of Keravel Vale shelter in the hills. A party sent after them must decide whether to follow.
- **The map.** A party carrying the lord of Ilovren's map climbs into the field toward the shrine ([[scenario-firsbldhls|The Fires of the Blood Hills]]).
- **The shrine.** The cave's gate, its fire pits, its altar and its guardian stand between a seeker and the maul ([[scenario-maulgashng|Maul of Gashang]]).
- **The campaign.** The hills are the end of the vale's raiding season ([[scenario-bldshscrvlvl|Blood and Ashes in Keravel Vale]]).
- **The second vigil.** A Forge knight keeping the battle-night vigil is joined by something that keeps it with him. He asks for another knight to stand beside him.
- **The maul.** Someone has found a way through the cave's traps without the blood the first gate demands.
