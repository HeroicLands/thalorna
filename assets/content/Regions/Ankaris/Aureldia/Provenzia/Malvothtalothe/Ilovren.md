## Overview

Ilovren is a lordship of [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] and the seat of **Nalor [Kilan Ilovrel na Ilovren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}**, a calculating noble with a fortified stronghold and ambitions that reach well past his own borders. To the rest of the vale he is a local lord like any other.

## Its Lord's Game

Kilan is a secret sympathizer of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]]. He has been manipulating the quarrel between the deposed lord of [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] and the new one, supplying the outlaw [Thárion al Dracaen]{.name data-type="being/npc"} with resources and false intelligence to keep the raids going, so that the Marshal will remove Belsaren's lord and accept Kilan as the man who can restore order. What he wants from Belsaren is its timber, its minerals and its road into **[[place-bloodhills|the Blood Hills]]**, where he believes a Black Flame artifact lies hidden. His ally is [Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, lord of [[place-leravren|Leravren]], who has supplied him with Black Flame soldiers.

A message that asks a stranger to come alone to the grove near Ilovren on the night of the new moon, offering proof of Kilan's lies, is the kind of message the vale's politics produce. Whether it is an offer or a trap depends on who sent it.
