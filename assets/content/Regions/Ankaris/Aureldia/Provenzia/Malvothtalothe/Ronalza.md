## Overview {#overview}

**Ronalza**, the ridge pass, is the eastern of the two passes over the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]]'s northern mountains. Its name is _ronal_ (a ridge) and _ralzena_ (a mountain pass) run together, for the long bare ridge the trail follows to the saddle. Snow lies on the saddle into midsummer and comes back before the harvest.

It begins where [[place-leravren|Leravren]]'s mountain road ends. Above [[place-leravrncstl|Leravren Castle]] the road climbs through the forest to the high valleys where the miners work, and past the last of the mining camps it becomes a trail. The trail crosses the saddle and goes down into the [[place-alndntrblnds|Áelendan Tribal Lands]] of the [[place-elavendre|Élavendre Region]], remote country where no Élavendren road reaches. From the castle to the first Áelendan camps on the far side is about five days on foot.

No trade crosses it. The ore and timber of the high valleys go down to Leravren, and what passes between the two kingdoms goes round by [[place-darvenaroad|the Darvena Road]] in the west.

## What Moves on It {#what-moves-on-it}

**Miners and their mules.** The mining camps of the high valleys sit on the lower part of the trail, and their ore goes down it to Leravren's toll and the smiths below.

**Shepherds.** The summer pastures below the saddle are grazed from both sides of the range.

**The Áelendan.** The tribes cross between their camps on both sides, and their traders bring herbs and hides down to the mining camps for iron.

## Logistics {#logistics}

The mountain road is a cart road as far as the highest mining camp, a day above the castle. Above it the trail is a mule path, then a footpath over loose rock to the saddle, marked by cairns. On the far side it drops through birch and old forest into the Tribal Lands over three days, with no shelter but the Áelendan summer camps.

## The Year on the Road {#year-on-the-road}

**Winter and spring** shut the saddle, and the mining camps below it are snowed in with their stores. **High summer** opens the trail for a few weeks, and the shepherds of both sides go up. **Autumn** closes it early, and the miners come down to Leravren for the winter or stay where they are.

## Who Patrols and Taxes It {#present}

The lord of Leravren, [[being-kaldinlnvl|Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren]], takes his toll on the mountain road at his castle gate, on the ore and timber that come down. Nobody taxes the trail above the mining camps. Beyond the saddle the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] keep the way into the Tribal Lands, and they turn back anyone who comes over without leave.

In the raiding season the mountain road is watched from both sides of the Tarvénian passes, because it is the march's back door, and Kaldin's men ask every stranger on it where he is bound.

## Dangers {#dangers}

**Snow.** The saddle can close in any month, and a party caught on it in cloud loses the cairns.

**The mining camps.** The high valleys keep to themselves, and a stranger who asks questions about their veins is not welcome in them.

**The Wardens.** On the far side a party without leave is warned once.
