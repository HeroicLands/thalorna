## Overview

Torvila Keravel is the castle town that anchors [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]], the place every road in the vale eventually leads to and every lord in it answers to. Its castle is the command of **Khirata [Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}**, the Elgar of Keravel Castle and **Marshal of the Western Army** of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provenzia]], and a petitioner who wants the vale's justice comes here to get it.

## The Marshal's Command

Jarvel is brother to the Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]], and his charge is the kingdom's frontier with the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]]: he manages the defenses against Tarvénian incursion and the banditry that thrives along a contested border. He is also the liege of the vale's lesser lords, and he uses that power freely. He deposed the Tanevar family that held [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] on a charge of treason and installed **[Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}** in its place, and Tarlas's future there depends on whether he can end the bandit trouble before the Marshal decides someone else would do it better.
