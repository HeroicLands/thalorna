## Overview

Torvila Leravren is the castle town of [[place-leravren|Leravren]], the lordship of the [[place-talothe|Talote]] that holds the road into the mountains, and the hall of [[being-kaldinlnvl|Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren]]. Some twelve hundred people live below the castle where the mountain road comes down out of the forest toward [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]]. The town lives on the road: the toll house at its gate, the carters' yards, the timber stacks and the smiths who shoe the ore teams. Above the town the road climbs to the mining valleys and becomes the trail over [[place-ronalza|Ronalza]] into Élavendre. Its lord keeps the fire of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] in his hall, in the open.

## History {#history}

The Lénavel branch of the [Zuravel]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} clan has held the castle and the toll on the mountain road for as long as the vale has been settled, and the town grew up below the castle to serve the road. The lords of Leravren kept the Flame there while the clan's line in the west turned to the Forge, and the Battle of the Blood Hills, fought on the lordship's edge about 248 AF ([[lore-bloodhillsbtl|The Battle of the Blood Hills]]), brought the Forge's knights to the edge of their land, where they have kept a vigil every year since.

## Current Situation {#present}

The toll is rising. The lords of Keravel Vale say it rises every time the Forge's knights ride through Leravren to their vigil at [[place-bloodhills|the Blood Hills]], and the carters who bring ore down the road pay what the toll house asks this month. Kaldin has demanded that the knights take an escort of his men through his land, and they refuse.

## The Holder {#holder}

Kaldin holds the castle and the lordship of the crown through the Marshal of the Western Army at [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]]. He lives in the castle all year, rides armed on his own land, and keeps the priests of the Flame in his hall.

## Garrison {#garrison}

Kaldin's household guard holds the castle and the toll house, soldiers who share his faith.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The toll on the ore road.** A carters' company hires a party to take its ore down the road through the town and learn what the toll is this month, and why.
- **The vigil's escort.** The Forge's knights riding to the Blood Hills vigil refuse Kaldin's escort, and both sides want a neutral party on the road through the town.
- **Who visits the hall.** The Master of the [[affiliation-brnngbrnd|Order of the Burning Brand]] wants to know who visits Kaldin's hall, and she cannot send a Zuravel to find out.
