## Overview

Torvila Keravel is the castle town that anchors [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]], the place every road in the vale eventually leads to and every lord in it answers to. Its castle is the command of **[[being-jarvelplnvr|Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]]**, lord of Keravel Castle and **Marshal of the Western Army** of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]], and a petitioner who wants the vale's justice comes here to get it.

## The Marshal's Command

The Western Army is named for the old province of the West that the kingdom grew from, and not for where it stands. Jarvel is brother to the Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]], and his charge is the kingdom's frontier with [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]]: he manages the defenses against Tarvénian incursion and the banditry that thrives along a contested border. He is also the liege of the vale's lesser lords, and he uses that power freely. He deposed the Tanevar family that held [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] on a charge of treason and installed [[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren]] in its place, and Tarlas's future there depends on whether he can end the bandit trouble before the Marshal decides someone else would do it better.

## History {#history}

The Western Army takes its name from the old province of the West, and its Marshals command the frontier from Torvila Keravel and hold the liege of the vale's lesser lords. In [[lore-warprinces|the War of the Princes]] the vale was the battlefield, remembered here as the Kéravian Rebellion: [House Dorgal]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, a minor family of the vale, was broken for choosing the wrong prince, and a Tarvénian host walked the eastern road while the vale's men fought each other. Two summers ago the present Marshal deposed the Tanevar house of [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] on a charge of treason and gave the lordship to his kinsman, and the vale's present trouble runs from that day.

## The Present {#present}

The Marshal's command is stretched between two enemies. The raiding season has his riders on the frontier road, and the bandits of the vale strike behind them, so that every troop he sends after Dirave is a troop the passes do not have. His brother the Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]] wants the vale quiet before the Council of Peers sits, and the Count of [[place-athavel|Atavel]] asks in Council why the Marshal cannot hold his own roads. The castle's hall is full of petitioners: merchants who have lost caravans, Tanevar villagers whose sons have been hanged at Belsaren, and the lord of [[place-ilovren|Ilovren]], who offers his men and his advice on who should hold Belsaren.
