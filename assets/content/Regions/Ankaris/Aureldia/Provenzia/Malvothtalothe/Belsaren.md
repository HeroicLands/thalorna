## Overview

Belsarèn is a lordship and village of [[place-ceravelvale|Keravèl Vale]], and when the vale's bandits strike, they strike here hardest. Its lord keeps a map of Belsarèn spread on the table of his hall, and the marks on it are raids. The land is worth fighting over: Belsarèn has valuable timber and minerals, and east of it rise the [[place-bloodhills|Blood Hills]], where the Black Flame fought its most infamous battle ([[lore-bloodhillsbtl|about 248 AF]]) and where something of the Black Flame's is said to lie hidden still.

## Two Lords

Belsarèn has two lords and the trouble is that both are alive. The family of **Thárion al Dracáen**, Thânevar, held Belsarèn and was content to be held by it; the villagers were long happy under a Thânevar lord. When the Marshal at [[place-caerceravel|Torvìla Keravèl]] accused Thárion's family of treason and deposed it, he installed **Élgâr Târlas al Pélanvri nâ Belsarèn** in its place. Târlas is a harsh ruler who holds the Thânevar in open contempt, and his treatment of them has driven many to support Thárion, who now leads the bandits from a hidden lair in the vale and promises to restore justice.

The village is divided accordingly. Every peasant of Belsarèn supports Târlas openly; the Thânevar majority supports Thárion in secret, and the Provenzian minority stays loyal to the lord. A stranger asking questions in Belsarèn hears one answer in the lane and another at the hearth.

## The Hand Behind It

The quarrel between the two lords serves a third. **Kílan alrí Ilóvrel**, lord of neighboring [[place-ilovren|Ilovrèn]], wants Belsarèn's timber, its minerals and its road into the Blood Hills, and he has been feeding Thárion resources and false intelligence to keep the raids going until the Marshal loses patience with Târlas. Neither lord of Belsarèn knows it.
