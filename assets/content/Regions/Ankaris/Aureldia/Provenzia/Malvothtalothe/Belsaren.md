## Overview

Belsaren is a lordship and village of [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]], and when the vale's bandits strike, they strike here hardest. Its lord keeps a map of Belsaren spread on the table of his hall, and the marks on it are raids. The land is worth fighting over: Belsaren has valuable timber and minerals, and east of it rise [[place-bloodhills|the Blood Hills]], where [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] fought its most infamous battle, [[lore-bloodhillsbtl|the Battle of the Blood Hills]], about 248 AF and where something of the Black Flame's is said to lie hidden still.

## Two Lords

Belsaren has two lords and the trouble is that both are alive. The family of [[being-diravekldrm|Dirave al Kaldremis]], Tanevar, held Belsaren and was content to be held by it; the villagers were long happy under a Tanevar lord. When the Marshal at [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]] accused Dirave's family of treason and deposed it, he installed **[[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren]]** in its place. Tarlas is a harsh ruler who holds the Tanevar in open contempt, and his treatment of them has driven many to support Dirave, who now leads the bandits from a hidden lair in the vale and promises to restore justice.

The village is divided accordingly. In the lane every peasant of Belsaren supports Tarlas; at the hearth the Tanevar majority supports Dirave, and only the Provénzian minority means its loyalty to the lord. A stranger asking questions in Belsaren hears one answer in the lane and another at the hearth.

## The Present {#present}

The raids are the lordship's whole life this season. Dirave's men strike the timber carts and the caravans on the Belsaren road, and they strike where the escorts are thinnest, as though they knew the escorts' orders. Tarlas answers with patrols and hangings, and each hanging sends another Tanevar family's sons to the outlaws. The Marshal at [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]] has told Tarlas to end it, and the lord of neighboring [[place-ilovren|Ilovren]], [[being-kilanilvrl|Kilan Ilovrel na Ilovren]], has let it be known that he could. Neither the lord nor the outlaw can say who tells the bandits where to strike.

:::secret {title="The hand behind it"}
The quarrel between the two lords serves a third. Kilan Ilovrel wants Belsaren's timber, its minerals and its road into the Blood Hills, and he has been feeding Dirave resources and false intelligence to keep the raids going until the Marshal loses patience with Tarlas and gives Belsaren to the man who can restore order. Neither lord of Belsaren knows it.
:::

- **The road east.** Belsaren's road is the way into the Blood Hills ([[scenario-firsbldhls|The Fires of the Blood Hills]]).
