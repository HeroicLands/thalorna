**Talote**—_the mountain march_.

The **Talote** is the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]]'s frontier with the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]]: about a
million people in the uplands of the kingdom's north-east corner and the basins below them, under mountains that are genuinely
cold and very nearly impassable from the first snow to the thaw.

Skirmishing along the frontier is effectively constant, and has been for as long as the records run—raid and counter-raid over pasture, passes and precedence, kept carefully below the level at which
either crown must formally acknowledge a war.

## The Season

Because the passes close, the war has a calendar, and everything in the march is arranged around it.
Raiding begins when the snow clears and ends when it returns; the levy musters in spring and disperses
in autumn; the courts sit in winter because nothing else can be done then. A winter crossing is either
a disaster or a deliberate outrage.

The border families on both sides have been fighting the same quarrel for so long that it functions
less as a conflict than as a way of life—and a good deal of the march's prosperity depends on it
continuing. Nobody in [[place-pirnebris|Pirnebris]], which sells the frontier its boots and carts,
imagines a lasting peace would be good news.

## Ore and Timber

The mountains are the kingdom's mineral country. Their veins carry precious metals and some gemstones, and small mining communities work the high valleys and keep to themselves. Timber and ore are what [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] and [[place-leravren|Leravren]] hold, which is why lords fight over them.

## Current Situation {#present}

The raiding season is open and the march is fighting on two fronts. Tarvénian riders are over the passes as they are every summer, and the levy that mustered at [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] in spring is spread along the road below them. Behind that line, in [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]], the bandits strike the roads with a precision the Marshal of the Western Army cannot account for, and the men he needs on the frontier are riding after outlaws instead. The Tanevar villages of the vale shelter the outlaws and the Provénzian lords hang the ones they catch. The Black Flame lord of [[place-leravren|Leravren]] and the Forge's knights watch each other across the vale, and the [[affiliation-wntrassize|Winter Assize]] has more charges waiting for the snow than it has heard in years.
