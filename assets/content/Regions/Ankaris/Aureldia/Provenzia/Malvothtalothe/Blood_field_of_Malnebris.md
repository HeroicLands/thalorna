High on the Tarvénian frontier, where the passes come down out of the cold mountains, lies the
**Blood-field of Malnebris**, made by the first great raid on the province in about 556 AF ([[lore-malnebris|Malnebris]])—and it is the only one of the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]]'s [[lore-bloodfields|blood-fields]]
that is not finished.

## Layers {#history}

**The first great raid.** About 556 AF the second High King of Tarvénia brought a host down the pass, and the provincial levy held the road at Malnebris for a day and a night before it broke ([[lore-malnebris|Malnebris]]). Both sides left more dead on the road than either could carry.

**The undeclared war.** Every raid since has come by the same road, and the border families have fought and died on the same ground in every generation.

## A Recurrence

Malnebris replays. On still nights through the campaign season the sounds come: horses, orders in two
languages, the particular noise of a shield-line giving way. Lights move along the pass. Nothing has
ever been found in the morning.

The border families on both sides have lived beside it for so long that they use it. Malnebris is
audible for miles, and a night when the field is loud is taken as a warning—not a supernatural one,
merely a reliable one, since the weather that carries the sound is the weather that brings raiders.

## Still Being Made

The frontier is fought over every year the passes are open, and men die at Malnebris with some
regularity because it is where the road is. Whether the new dead join the recurrence is a matter of
genuine dispute: the old say the sounds have grown, the priests of [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]]
say the field was made once and is not being remade, and nobody can settle it because no one can count
a noise.

What is certain is that no rite of settlement has ever been attempted here. It would have to be
performed in the campaign season, on ground both crowns claim, and neither will guarantee the other's
priests safe conduct to do it.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The field is loud this season, and the frontier is listening. The levy watches the pass on every still night, because a loud night has brought raiders more often than not, and the Tarvénian border families read it the same way from the other side. Two men of the levy died on the road below the field at midsummer, and the old men of the march say the sounds have grown since. A priest of [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]] at [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] wants to attempt the rite of settlement while the field can still be settled, and needs a safe conduct from both crowns that neither will give.
