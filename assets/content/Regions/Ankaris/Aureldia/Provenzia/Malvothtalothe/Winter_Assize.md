## Overview

The Winter Assize is the court of [[place-talothe|the Talote]], the eastern march of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]], and it sits at [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] from the closing of the passes to the thaw. Nothing can be fought in winter, so winter is when the march does its law. The Assize hears everything the campaign season has had no time for: claims over stolen stock, disputed ransoms, the standing quarrels of border families, and the occasional charge against an officer that could not safely be brought while he was needed.

It is the most honest court in Provénzia in the estimation of the people who appear before it, largely because everyone involved is snowed in together and will be until spring. Its present is a charge the Marshal of the Western Army means to bring against one of the Warden's own captains.

## Who Sits {#who-sits}

Justice of the Winter Assize
: [[being-dilmaravlch|Dilmara Volchemis]], an advocate of the high courts at [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]], named by the Justiciar, [[being-solkimervtn|Solkime Revotandis]], for several winters running. She lives in Durvalis the year round.

The clerk of the Assize keeps its rolls from one winter to the next, and the court's sworn men keep its door and carry its summonses into the high valleys while the roads allow. The [[being-talvenekmrl|Warden of the Talote]] keeps the peace of the town while the Assize sits and does not sit in it.

## How It Decides {#how-it-decides}

The Assize opens when the passes close, and the justice proclaims its peace in the square: from that day no blade is drawn over a quarrel that has been brought to the court. A plea is spoken before the justice, the other party answers it, and each brings witnesses. The justice hears them, asks the clerk what the rolls say of earlier pleas between the same families, and gives judgment. The penalty is usually a payment in stock or silver, set by the march's customs where they speak and by the received law where they do not. A judgment of the Assize stands as a judgment of the high courts does, and an appeal goes to Aurelane in spring.

A charge against an officer is heard like any other plea. That is the court's reputation and its danger, because the officer is still an officer when the thaw comes.

## What It Is Fighting About {#present}

**The Marshal's charge.** One of the Warden's captains refused an order from the Marshal of the Western Army, [[being-jarvelplnvr|Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]], during a raid, and the Marshal means to charge him before the Assize this winter. The question underneath it, whose orders a captain of the march obeys, is one nobody has written down, and Jalnasdal reads whatever Dilmara Volchemis decides as an answer to it.

**Ransoms across the pass.** Border families on both sides of the frontier take prisoners for ransom, and a disputed ransom with a Tarvénian family comes before the Assize most winters. The court can bind a Provénzian family; it can only send word to the other side.

**The Justiciar's choice.** The Warden has asked the Justiciar not to send the same justice again, and the border families have asked him to send nobody else.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

A plea must reach Durvalis before the passes close, and its witnesses with it. A party meets the Assize as suitors with a claim of their own, as witnesses brought down from a high valley before the snow, or as hired hands for a family that wants its plea heard. Anyone in Durvalis for the winter is in the Assize's peace, and the court is the one place in the march where a Tarvénian with a grievance can be heard without a raid.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The captain's account.** The [[being-talvenekmrl|Warden]] wants his captain's story proved before the Marshal's witnesses arrive.
- **The last witness.** [[being-dilmaravlch|The Justice]] needs a witness brought down from a high valley before the snow closes it.
