## Overview {#overview}

Keravel Vale is a frontier region of the [[place-talothe|Talote]], [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Provénzia]]'s mountain march against [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]]: a rugged, isolated vale of manorial estates and villages anchored by its castle town, [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]]. It sits near the kingdom's eastern border, which makes it a frequent flashpoint, and its lords spend their years balancing defense, governance and trade against one another. In a good year the vale is prosperous. In most years it is also dangerous.

## Who Holds the Vale

The vale is commanded from Torvila Keravel by **[[being-jarvelplnvr|Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]]**, brother to the Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]] and **Marshal of the Western Army** of Provénzia, whose charge is the defense of the frontier against Tarvénia and the banditry that feeds on it. Beneath him the vale's lordships are held by his own appointees and by older families: [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]], whose new lord **[[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren]]** he installed after deposing the Tanevar family that held it; [[place-ilovren|Ilovren]], held by the ambitious **[[being-kilanilvrl|Kilan Ilovrel na Ilovren]]**; and, beyond the vale to the northeast, [[place-leravren|Leravren]], the gateway to the mountains. West of the vale, in the basins below the uplands, lies the county of [[place-athavel|Atavel]], whose trade comes up the vale's roads and whose count asks in Council why the Marshal cannot keep them.

The vale's people are divided the way the kingdom is: a Tanevar peasantry who remember governing themselves, and a Provénzian minority loyal to the lords the crown sets over them. A Tanevar lord deposed on a charge of treason became [[being-diravekldrm|Dirave al Kaldremis]], bandit, and the vale's roads have been treacherous since.

## The Roads and the Hills

Bandits have harried the roads of Keravel Vale for years, striking caravans and travelers throughout the vale and worst of all around Belsaren, and lately they strike with a precision that suggests someone is organizing them. East of Belsaren rise [[place-bloodhills|the Blood Hills]], where [[lore-bloodhillsbtl|the Battle of Keravel's Blood Hills]] was fought about 248 AF between the [Cohorts of Gashang]{.name data-type="affiliation"} of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] and the [Knights of the Sacred Forge]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"}, and where the [[scenario-maulgashng|Maul of Gashang]] is rumored to lie hidden still. The kingdom's great civil war, [[lore-warprinces|the War of the Princes]], is remembered here as the **Kéravian Rebellion**, and the vale was its battleground.

## House Dorgal's Seat

On a rise above the vale's second river stands the burned shell of the manor of [House Dorgal]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, a minor family that chose the wrong prince in the war of 653 to 655 AF. The roof is gone and the hall's walls stand to the eaves. Its lands were given to a rival house, whose bailiff keeps the fields and does not go into the ruin.

The vale says the family walled its deeds up in the cellar before the manor was burned, and that the rival's claim holds only for as long as nobody finds them. [[being-branwldrgr|Branval Dorgal]], captain of [[affiliation-slntlncmpny|the Silent Talon]], comes from the family, and the lands are disputed between two lords. Whoever wants to settle the dispute wants the cellar searched.

:::secret {title="The Dorgal deeds"}
The story is true. The family's deeds lie walled up in the cellar of the burned manor, where the family put them before the burning. They prove the family's title to the lands, and the rival house's claim does not survive them.
:::

## Current Situation {#present}

The vale is losing a war it has not declared. The bandits strike the Belsaren roads every week of the season, and the Marshal's patience with [[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren]] is running out: Tarlas must end the raids before the Marshal decides another man would do it better, and every hanging he orders drives more of the Tanevar villages to the outlaws. Dirave promises the Tanevar their old lord's justice and takes the caravans the vale lives on. The lord of Ilovren offers the Marshal his help, and the Forge's knights and the Black Flame lord of [[place-leravren|Leravren]] each read the trouble as the other's work.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The raiding season.** The new lord of Belsaren hires strangers to break the outlaws, and the trail runs from his village to the lord of Ilovren and on into the Blood Hills ([[scenario-bldshscrvlvl|Blood and Ashes in Keravel Vale]]).
- **The Dorgal cellar.** A lord who wants the disputed Dorgal lands settled pays a party to search the burned manor's cellar before the rival house's bailiff hears of it.
