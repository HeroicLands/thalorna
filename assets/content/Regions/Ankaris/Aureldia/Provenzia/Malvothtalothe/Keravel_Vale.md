## Overview

Keravèl Vale is a frontier region of the [[place-talothe|Talòthe]], Provènzia's mountain march against [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]]: a rugged, isolated vale of manorial estates and villages anchored by its castle town, [[place-caerceravel|Torvìla Keravèl]]. It sits near the kingdom's southeastern border, which makes it a frequent flashpoint, and its lords spend their years balancing defense, governance and trade against one another. In a good year the vale is prosperous. In most years it is also dangerous.

## Who Holds the Vale

The vale is commanded from Torvìla Keravèl by **Khiráta Jârvel al Pélanvri nâ Torvìla Keravèl**, brother to the Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavèl]] and **Marshal of the Western Army** of Provènzia, whose charge is the defense of the frontier against Tarvénia and the banditry that feeds on it. Beneath him the vale's lordships are held by his own appointees and by older families: [[place-belsaren|Belsarèn]], whose new lord **Élgâr Târlas al Pélanvri nâ Belsarèn** he installed after deposing the Thânevar family that held it; [[place-ilovren|Ilovrèn]], held by the ambitious **Nâlor Kílan alrí Ilóvrel nâ Ilovrèn**; and, beyond the vale to the northeast, [[place-leravren|Leravrèn]], the gateway to the mountains.

The vale's people are divided the way the kingdom is: a Thânevar peasantry who remember governing themselves, and a Provenzian minority loyal to the lords the crown sets over them. A Thânevar lord deposed on a charge of treason became **Thárion al Dracáen**, bandit, and the vale's roads have been treacherous since.

## The Roads and the Hills

Bandits have harried the roads of Keravèl Vale for years, striking caravans and travelers throughout the vale and worst of all around Belsarèn, and lately they strike with a precision that suggests someone is organizing them. East of Belsarèn rise the [[place-bloodhills|Blood Hills]], where the [[lore-bloodhillsbtl|Battle of Keravèl's Blood Hills]] was fought about 248 AF between the **Cohorts of Gashang** of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] and the **Knights of the Sacred Forge**, and where the [[scenario-maulgashng|Maul of Gashang]] is rumored to lie hidden still. The kingdom's great civil war, [[lore-warprinces|the War of the Princes]], is remembered here as the **Céravian Rebellion**, and the vale was its battleground.

## House Dorgàl's Seat

On a rise above the vale's second river stands the burned shell of the manor of **House Dorgàl**, a minor family that chose the wrong prince in the war of 653 to 655 AF. The roof is gone and the hall's walls stand to the eaves. Its lands were given to a rival house, whose bailiff keeps the fields and does not go into the ruin.

The family's deeds were walled up in the cellar before the manor was burned, and the rival's claim has held for as long as nobody has found them. [[being-branwldrgr|Branvàl Dorgàl]], captain of the Silent Talon, comes from the family, and the lands are disputed between two lords. Anyone who wants to settle the dispute wants the cellar.
