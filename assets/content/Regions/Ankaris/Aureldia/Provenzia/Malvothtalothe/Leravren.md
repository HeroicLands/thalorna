## Overview

Leravren lies northeast of [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] in the [[place-talothe|Talote]], and it is the gateway to the mountains and forests whose timber, ore and passes the march lives on. Some six thousand people live in its valleys, in timber camps, small mining settlements in the high valleys and farms on the lower ground. Whoever holds Leravren holds a road the rest of the frontier needs.

## The Land {#the-land}

The lordship climbs from the head of Keravel Vale into forested mountain. Its lower valleys are farmed; above them the forest runs up to the high valleys where the mining communities work the veins and keep to themselves. Its one great road comes down out of the mountains, through Leravren and into the vale, and the timber and ore of the high country come down it. [[place-bloodhills|The Blood Hills]] lie on the edge of its land toward the vale.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Its lord is [[being-kaldinlnvl|Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren]], of the Lénavel branch of the [Zuravel]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} clan, who holds the lordship of the crown of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]] through the Marshal of the Western Army at [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]] and keeps his hall at [[place-leravrncstl|Leravren Castle]]. He is a fierce adherent of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]], and through him the region is closely associated with the faith. He keeps his devotion openly, as the Earl of Hárivren does.

The Zuravel clan is divided on the kingdom's faith quarrel. The line of [[being-selvarezrvl|Selvare Zuravel]], Baron of Zuravlen, backs the [[affiliation-brnngbrnd|Order of the Burning Brand]], which stands with [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]]. Kaldin's branch keeps the Flame, and it is his branch, not the clan, that links the march's local quarrels to the Flame's organized power in the kingdom. In a kingdom where the Forge grows more dominant every year, Leravren is one of the places its rivals still hold.

## Current Situation {#present}

Leravren holds the road the rest of the march needs, and Kaldin knows its price. The timber and ore of the mountains come down through his land, and the lords of [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] pay his tolls and resent his faith. The Knights of the Sacred Forge keep a vigil on the edge of his land at the Blood Hills every year, and every year he asks the Marshal at Torvila Keravel why the Forge's knights ride armed through a lordship that is not theirs. He and [[being-kilanilvrl|Kilan Ilovrel na Ilovren]] of [[place-ilovren|Ilovren]] are on good terms, which is noticed in a vale where few lords are on good terms with anyone.

:::secret {title="Kaldin's soldiers"}
Kaldin is Kilan's ally in Kilan's game against Belsaren. He has supplied Kilan with loyal Black Flame soldiers, who serve in Kilan's household as his own men and are ready to hold Belsaren once the Marshal removes its lord. Kaldin wants the road into the Blood Hills in friendly hands and the relic the Flame believes lies there in the faith's keeping.
:::

## Dangers {#dangers}

The mountain road is the frontier's back door, and in the raiding season it is watched from both sides of the passes. The Blood Hills on the lordship's edge return the dread of a losing line to anyone who climbs into them, and the outlaws of the vale shelter on their ridges.

## History {#history}

The road through Leravren is the march's oldest way into the mountains, and its lords have taken toll on it for as long as the vale has been settled. The Lénavel hold it as a branch of the Zuravel clan, the western seaboard's house, and they have kept the Flame there while the clan's line in the west turned to the Order and the Forge. The Battle of the Blood Hills was fought on the lordship's edge about 248 AF ([[lore-bloodhillsbtl|The Battle of the Blood Hills]]), and the Forge's knights have kept their vigil there since.
