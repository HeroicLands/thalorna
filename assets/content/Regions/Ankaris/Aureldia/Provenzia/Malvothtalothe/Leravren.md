## Overview

Leravrèn lies northeast of [[place-ceravelvale|Keravèl Vale]] in the [[place-talothe|Talòthe]], and it is the gateway to the mountains and forests whose timber, ore and passes the march lives on. Whoever holds Leravrèn holds a road the rest of the frontier needs.

## Its Lord and His Faith

That lord is **Kâldin al Lénavel nâ Leravrèn**, a member of the Zûravel clan and a fierce adherent of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]], and through him the region is closely associated with the faith. Kâldin is a key player in the frontier's political and religious conflicts, and his ties to the Zûravel clan make him the link between local intrigue and organized Black Flame power: he is the ally of **Kílan alrí Ilóvrel** of [[place-ilovren|Ilovrèn]] and has supplied him with loyal Black Flame soldiers. In a kingdom where the Church of the [[affiliation-sacredforge|Sacred Forge]] grows more dominant every year, Leravrèn is one of the places its rivals still hold.
