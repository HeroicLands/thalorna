**Aurèliane** stands inland in the south, on the great road, and is the oldest city in Provènzia. It
was founded as a [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian]] colony, laid out on the imperial grid, and served
for centuries as the provincial capital of everything the empire held west of the sea.

It is not the capital now. When Provènzia became a kingdom in its own right the court settled at
[[place-lunacorte|Lunacòrte]] on the coast, where the money was, and Aurèliane has spent the
intervening centuries being magnificent about it.

## Law and Learning

What the city kept was the institutions, and they turned out to matter more than the court did.

Provenzian **law** is Vylarian law as received, amended and argued over at Aurèliane, and the high
courts still sit here rather than at Lunacòrte. So do the **academies**—philosophy, rhetoric,
medicine, and the manuscript and illumination trades that grew up around them—which is why a
Provenzian sends a promising son to Aurèliane and an ambitious one to the capital.

The distinction is felt keenly in both directions. Aurèliane regards Lunacòrte as a counting-house that
has acquired a throne. Lunacòrte regards Aurèliane as a very expensive library.

## The Stones

The city is built of and on the empire. Its aqueduct still runs; its grid still governs streets laid
out for a garrison that left eight hundred years ago; its forum is a market and its basilica is a
court, both still doing approximately what they were built for.

This continuity is Provènzia's strongest claim to being the true heir of Vylarian civilization in the
west, and Aurèliane makes it at every opportunity.
