**Válaren** stands on the warm southern shore, on the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]], and is the
city outsiders mean when they say Provènzia: some forty-five thousand people, white stone and shuttered
galleries, a harbor crowded with Vylarian shipping, and a manner so polished that other Provenzians
find it exhausting.

It is a crucial trading port and it lives entirely by that trade. Everything that reaches Provènzia
from the Vylarian world arrives here or at [[place-belporte|Belpòrte]], and Válaren's merchant houses
have been the intermediaries for so long that the arrangement is mistaken locally for a law of nature.

## What It Is Like

Wealth, and the display of wealth, is the city's organizing principle. Its houses are old, its money is
older, and its enthusiasms are the Provenzian ones taken to their limit – art, philosophy, glass,
manuscripts, and an appetite for the rare and perishable that supports whole trades elsewhere. Fresh
**Zûravlen peaches** command absurd prices on Válaren tables, and always have; the fast boats out of
[[place-portvent|Portvent]] exist because Válaren will pay.

A Válaren merchant regards [[place-batarencity|Batáren]] as a cold northern outpost of no consequence
and the western march as a place wool comes from. Both opinions are returned with interest.

## The Vulnerability

Everything Válaren is rests on open water, and the city has no other resource – no great hinterland of
its own, no manufacture that matters, no walls that would signify against a serious army and no need of
them. Nobody has ever taken Válaren by assault.

**Blockade** is another matter. Close the harbor and the city begins to fail within weeks: prices go
first, then credit, then the arrangements that hold the merchant houses to one another. It has happened
before, and the last time forces unknown held the roads and the water for a quarter of a year, the
damage done to Válaren's standing outlasted the siege by a generation. See
[[scenario-siegevalrn|The Siege of Válaren]].
