## Overview {#overview}

**The Durrask** (_DOOR-rask_) are a hill people of [[place-thornwold|the Thornwold]], the rough upland between the south-western shore of [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]] and the edge of [[place-hallowwood|Seirasto]]. About nine thousand of them live in walled steads of a few households on the ridges, herd cattle and pigs in the oak copses, hunt, and raid. They are of the same forest stock as [[lore-peshtarclans|the Peshtar]] and have no dealings with them.

They are at war with every realm they can reach, and they always have been. Their raiders take cattle, horses and grain from [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Aldorath]]'s lake-shore manors, from the eastern upland grazing of [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Ondareth]] and the horse-runs of the Highlands, and from [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Dúnavarre]]'s western estates, and every killing a realm answers with goes into the Durrask's count.

They leave the people of Seirasto alone.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

Each stead is ruled by its **stead-head**, who keeps its stores and its spears. Each autumn the spears of all the steads meet at the autumn fire on the largest of the hill ramparts, and swear to one **War-Leader** for the year's raiding. A War-Leader whose year went badly is not sworn to again.

The War-Leader is [[being-kadrum|Kadrum]], sworn at the autumn fire of 719 for the third year running, which few War-Leaders reach.

The **Grey Heads**, the old of the steads, keep **the count**: every Durrask killed by a lowlander, by name, by stead and by the realm that owes the death. They recite it at the autumn fire, and a death comes off the count only when a death of that realm's people is set against it. The realms answer raids with hangings and burnings, each answer adds to the count, and the count has never stood at nothing.

## Range, Wells and Grounds {#range}

The Thornwold is gorse and thorn on the ridges, oak in the folds and bog in the bottoms, and the steads stand on the hilltops inside old earth ramparts. The raiding runs out of it in three directions: north-east, past the lake's south-eastern tip, to the manors and mills of Aldorath's lower shore, a night's ride from the nearest rampart; west to the horse-grazing of the Central Highlands; and east along Seirasto's northern edge to Dúnavarre's western estates. The Durrask do not raid south, into the forest.

## Relations {#relations}

- **[[affiliation-kngdmldrth|The Kingdom of Aldorath]].** The nearest enemy and the oldest. The kingdom's southern manors have cut their fields out of the Thornwold's edge for centuries, and the Durrask have burned them back for as long.
- **[[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|The Kingdom of Dúnavarre]].** The richest of the three to raid, and the hardest, since the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]] keeps its roads. The Order hangs every raider it takes.
- **[[affiliation-repblcndrth|The Republic of Ondareth]].** The Durrask take horses from its eastern upland grazing, and its herdsmen ride after them.
- **[[place-hallowwood|Seirasto]].** Left alone.
- **[[lore-grukaraelwyth|The Grukar]].** Fought wherever met in the copses, as the forest peoples of the island all fight them. The nest in the old rampart of Gorsering lies among the steads, [[place-aldermire|Aldermire]]'s in the bog to the north-west, and Brambledene's on the raiders' road east. The count holds only deaths a realm owes, and the steads' dead to the Grukar go on no count.

## History {#history}

The Durrask descend from the [[lore-pelwarpepl|Pelwar]], as nearly every human on the island does, and their steads have stood in the Thornwold since before the kings of Aldorath were crowned at [[place-kingsholow|Kingshollow]]. The kingdom's southern manors have pressed into the upland's edge ever since, field by field, and the Durrask have answered every field with a raid. The Grey Heads' count goes back further than any of them can recite at one fire, and they recite only the deaths of the last three generations.

Kadrum was first sworn War-Leader at the autumn fire of 717.

## The Present Pressure {#present}

Kadrum's spears are raiding all three realms at once.

- **Aldorath: the shore mills.** In the autumn of 719 Kadrum's raiders burned a mill on Aldorath's shore of Lake Eilìssa, a morning's ride from [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]], and drove off the plough-oxen of three manors. The barons of the shore have asked the king for men, and the king's men are on the Wiindmarch, short already.
- **Dúnavarre: the five hanged men.** Knights of the Order of the Just Blade took five Durrask raiders on a western estate in the summer and hanged them at the roadside. The Grey Heads have entered five deaths against Dúnavarre, and Kadrum's spears have raided the western estates twice this winter.
- **Ondareth: the mares.** In the summer of 719 the Durrask drove forty brood mares off the southern grazing of [[place-cntrlhghlnds|the Central Highlands]], and the herdsmen who rode after them killed Kadrum's brother at a ford. The Senate's levy has taken the Highlands' young men north to the frontier, and the southern breeders say there is no one left to ride after the next raid.

## The Gathering Place {#gathering-place}

The steads gather each autumn at the largest of the hill ramparts, a ring of earth banks on the Thornwold's highest ridge, around a fire that is lit at dusk and kept until the count has been recited. The spears swear to the War-Leader there, and the Grey Heads recite the count by the firelight.

## The Names of the People

Durrask speech is its own, and no [[skill-aelwthlng|Eilwìthi]] element builds its names. Its words are short and hard: mostly one or two syllables, closed on _k_, _d_, _m_, _r_ or _sk_. The vowels _u_ and _a_ lead and _o_ follows, and _e_ and _i_ are rare. The consonants it favors are _d_, _r_, _k_, _s_, _h_ and _m_, often doubled. Men's names end in _-um_, _-ud_ or _-osk_, women's in _-ka_ or _-ra_, and the people's own name in _-ask_. It leaves out the soft _dh_ and _gh_ and the _-ar_ endings of the Peshtar, and _v_, _w_, _z_, _th_ and the glides. A Durrask carries one name and is known among strangers by the stead.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The count.** A Grey Head will take a stranger to the autumn fire to hear the count, and will name what any one death on it would cost to settle.
- **The mares.** A Stoniford breeder pays for the return of her brood mares and does not mind who she pays.
- **The brother.** Kadrum wants the names of the herdsmen who killed his brother at the ford, and he knows they are on the southern grazing still.
