## Overview {#overview}

**The Crown Road** runs from [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]], at the south-eastern tip of [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]], south through the eastern oak woods of [[place-thornwold|the Thornwold]] to the north-eastern edge of [[place-hallowwood|Seirasto]]. It starts at a ford below the last foresters' lodge and passes the Grukar nest at [[place-fernbank|Fernbank]] and the relay house of [[place-wenlornestage|Wenlorne Stage]] on the way.

It is one of the Crown Roads of [[lore-jointcrown|the Joint Crown]], laid by [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] masons in fitted blocks no later mason on the island can match ([[place-eilwith#the-crown-roads|the Crown Roads]]). The road is whole for most of a day's walk beyond the last Aldorathi steading, its blocks as true as when they were cut, and the oaks and beeches the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] planted along it have grown into a roofed lane.

## What Moves on It {#what-moves-on-it}

Very little. The carters of the last steadings take the lit house at Wenlorne for a free bed, and each season one or two of them go down the road to see it. The king's foresters out of Dunmere send no patrols down it. Travelers who reach Seirasto's edge by the road are turned there, politely and without conversation, and sent back the way they came.

## Logistics {#logistics}

A party on foot reaches Wenlorne Stage from Dunmere in a long day. The road is the surest footing in the forest, and the forest on either side of it is wet, dense and held by [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]], so the road is where a party stays.

## The Stations {#stations}

[[place-fernbank|Fernbank]]
: Not a station but a nest. A Grukar nest lives in caves under a high, fern-grown bank beside the road, a few hours' walk south of the ford.

[[place-wenlornestage|Wenlorne Stage]]
: About halfway along the road, a square stone relay house with a gate-arch, a stable court and a tower at the north-west corner for the road-horn. The crown kept such houses a day apart, so that a rider with a royal writ changed horses at each one and a traveler slept behind a barred door. Iron lamp-posts line the last mile of road to it, and at dusk they are lit one after another from the stage outward and burn with a steady pale light until dawn. Nobody has been seen to light them.

## Who Patrols and Taxes It {#present}

No realm holds the Thornwold, and nobody keeps the road. The king's foresters of the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]] keep lodges above the ford where it starts. They have stopped sending patrols down it after the ninth failed to return, and their captain keeps the names of the lost on a roll.

At the southern end the wardens of the [[affiliation-nclvhllwwd|Enclave of Seirasto]] watch the road from the forest's northern edge. [[being-selkitteilvnt|Selkitte Ilevanto]], who keeps the northern watch, turns back the carters who come down the road to see the lit house and reach the trees instead.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **The Fernbank nest.** It holds the forest on both sides of the road from the ford to well south of Wenlorne Stage. It does not hold the road: its raiders cross the fitted stone quickly and never walk along it, and they keep clear of the lit mile around the stage. It takes the carters who step off the stone, and it raids north to the ford, the lodge and the stock of the last steadings. What it covets is the mast of the avenue and the deer and swine the mast draws every autumn.
- **The wardens' war.** The wardens of Seirasto hunt the Grukar along the Dunmere road above all, and a party that reaches the trees by the road has fought its way there.
- **The lit house.** Two carters of the last steadings have gone down the road to see the house, and one has come back. He does not say what he saw.

## History {#history}

The forest was the Joint Crown's, and the road ran through it to Seirasto. Every stage on it had a road-reeve, an officer who answered to the crown for the safety of the road and the roll of those who slept under his roof. In the last days of the [Great War]{.name data-type="lore/history"} the crown's subjects fled along the road in the dark, and Wenlorne Stage was found afterward empty, with its gate closed. When the crown fell the Senali gave up the avenues they had planted along its roads, the forest was left to nobody, and the Grukar came into it within a century of their landing.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The felled avenue.** Someone has begun felling the old avenue along the road from Dunmere, and [[being-solmerivonvnt|Solmerivo Nuuvanto]] of Seirasto wants to know who.
- **The road beyond the stage.** [[being-wnfrdlvrtn|Winrith Alverton]] of Dunmere needs an escort to compare the road with her sketches as far as Seirasto's edge.
- **The roll.** The captain of the king's foresters at Dunmere would like to know how many of the nine lost patrols the Grukar took, and how many the road did.
