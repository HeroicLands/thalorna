## Overview {#overview}

**Aldermire** is a bog in a wide wooded fold of the northern [[place-thornwold|Thornwold]], grown over with alder and willow, a short walk above the south-western shore of [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]] near its south-eastern tip. It lies between the horse-grazing of [[place-cntrlhghlnds|the Central Highlands]] to the west and the manors of [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Aldorath]]'s lower shore across the tip to the east. A [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] nest lives on the islands of firm ground in the middle of it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The nest holds the bog, the alder woods round it, and the shore below. What it guards is the islands themselves: no horse can cross the bog to them and no army can stand on it. Its raiders go out on paths of sunken logs only they know, and come back the same way.

## Layers {#history}

The bog has always been there, and the nest on its islands has been there as long as the manors of the lower shore have kept tallies of their losses. The [[affiliation-durrask|Durrask]] steads nearest it have fought it in the copses for as long, and the barons of the shore have twice sent men in after it and lost them in the bog.

## What Is There Now {#present}

In the autumn the Durrask burned a mill on Aldorath's lower shore, and since then the shore barons have armed their tenants and keep a watch along the shore by night. The nest's raiders have met the watch and lost to it. So the spawner has turned them west, onto the horse-runs of the Highlands, where the Ondarethan breeders graze their stock, the levy has taken their young men north, and no one is left to ride after a raid. The breeders blame the Durrask.

## Dangerous Neighbours {#dangerous-neighbours}

The Durrask, who fight the nest wherever they meet it and fight strangers in the Thornwold just as readily. The shore barons' night watch, which takes anyone on the shore after dark for a raider.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

Black water under alder and willow, and reed and moss over the soft ground. The log paths lie a hand's depth under the surface, and the way to find them is to follow the nest's own tracks. The islands are low hummocks of firm ground, dug through with dens, and the spawn-chamber is dug into the largest, under a stand of old alder. The bog keeps what falls into it, and the nest's refuse lies in it with older things: a baron's helm, a cart-wheel, an iron-bound chest.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The watch.** A shore baron pays for raiders' heads, and does not much mind whether they are Grukar or Durrask.
- **The mares.** A Highlands breeder who has lost foals to the west of the lake pays for them back, and believes the Durrask have them.
- **The chest.** The iron-bound chest in the bog is older than the nest, and a party that gets to the islands can get to it.
