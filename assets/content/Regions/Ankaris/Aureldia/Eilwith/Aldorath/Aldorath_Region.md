## Overview {#overview}

The Aldorath Region is the heartland of [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]]: the lowlands and hills east of the
great central lake, bounded by the mountains that separate them from the
[[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] to the north-west and by [[place-ironfells|the Ironfells]] to the north, and running
south to the border of [[place-dunavarre|Dúnavarre]]. Some two hundred thousand people live in it, under the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]], and four in five of them are serfs on manorial estates.

## Character

Aldorath's proximity to the great central lake gives it a mystical character that sets it apart from the more outward-looking [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Dúnavarre]]. The lake's mists sometimes part to reveal paths to places that cannot be found by ordinary means, and the kingdom's chronicles are full of accounts of knights who rode into the fog and returned changed—or did not return at all.

## The Land {#the-land}

The kingdom holds the eastern shore of [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]], the great central lake, from its northern end to its south-eastern tip, where the farmland climbs to the water; the lake's western shore is the upland of [[place-cntrlhghlnds|the Central Highlands]], which no realm holds. From the lake the land falls east through the grain country of the Aldorvale toward the sea, with wool uplands to the north and west of it by the lake's northern end, vine slopes in the lee of the Ironfells, tidal levels where the river reaches the sea on the Dúnavarre border, and a wooded march under the mountains along the kingdom's north. Dúnavarre lies to the south, and the Stormveld passes over [[place-sunderfells|the Sunderfells]] to the north-west.

## Current Situation {#present}

The kingdom is quarreling with itself. [[being-aldrikdrthng|King Aldrik]] wants the council of barons to accept his young son as king-to-be in his own lifetime, and [[being-godwaldstrl|Godwald Storlei]], the Elder of the Council, holds the barons against it, because the crown has always been theirs to give at each vacancy. The barons' levies reach the Wiindmarch late and short while the quarrel lasts, and the march holds the Stormveld passes and the forest margin with fewer men each season. On the lake the barons of the eastern shore and the free village of [[place-koldmere|Koldmere]] dispute the reed-beds at its northern end, and on the coast every hull out of Greiwater still calls at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]] before it may sail for the mainland.
