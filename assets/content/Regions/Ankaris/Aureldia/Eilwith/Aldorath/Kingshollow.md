## Overview {#overview}

**Kingshollow** is a village of perhaps fourteen hundred in a sheltered fold of the [[place-aldorathrgn|Aldorath Region]]'s eastern
country, and it would be of no consequence at all except for what stands in it. It is the crown's second seat: the crowning chapel, the old hall of the first kings and the royal graves are here, and the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]] crowns, buries and keeps midwinter with its kings at Kingshollow while it governs from [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]].

## The Old Seat

The kings of [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Aldorath]] came from here: the first of them, [Drethar of Kingshollow]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, was crowned in the chapel about 120 AF ([[lore-kingshlwcrwn|the Crowning at Kingshollow]]). The hall at Kingshollow is the dynasty's original seat, kept in
repair for six centuries, and though the court moved to [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]] long ago the hall has
never been given up. Kings are still crowned in the chapel beside it, still keep the midwinter feast
there when they can, and are still buried in the ground behind it.

The result is a working village with a royal household attached: a bailiff, a small permanent staff, a
chapel endowed out of all proportion to the parish, and periodic descents by the entire court which the
villagers dread and profit from in roughly equal measure.

## Who Leads It {#who-leads}

The hall's bailiff keeps the village and the royal manor for the crown under [[being-aldrithwlfl|Aldrith Wolfli]], the Steward of the household, and [[being-dunrikbrndw|Dunrik Brandwik]], the Forester, keeps the royal forest above it from the forest lodge and holds its forest court.

## The Hunting

The wood above Kingshollow is royal forest under forest law, and the kings hunt it. That is the
village's other defining fact—the forest courts, the restrictions on grazing and firewood, the
poaching, and the long unresolved grievance of tenants living beside timber they may not cut. In a
year the king keeps the midwinter feast here, the tenants sing the grievance at the hall door in the
burden of [[lore-kingswassail|the Wassail at Kingshollow]]:
[Wassail, wassail, and open the door!/The king hath the forest, the hollow is poor;/Then wassail the king, and wassail the tree,/And a fire in the hollow for thee and for me.]{.verse}

## The Quarrel {#present}

The king has not kept midwinter at Kingshollow for three years. Each year the Steward has come down from Dunmere in his place, given the tenants the tree the Forester marked, and heard the verse the village sings only when the king is absent. The tenants take the king's absence as a sign that he has stopped listening, and the poaching in the royal forest has grown with each winter, while the forest court's fines go unpaid. This year the tenants have asked the Forester to mark one oak in particular, and the king has asked the council of barons to come to the chapel and accept his son as king-to-be, which no council has done in a king's lifetime.
