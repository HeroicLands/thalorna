The **Aldorvale** is the broad, lush, well-watered heart of [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Aldorath]], and the
reason the kingdom is the largest on [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]]. Ninety-five thousand people live in it—half of
Aldorath's total—on some of the best arable ground in the Aureldian west.

## The Breadbasket

The vale grows grain, and grows it in quantity: wheat where the drainage allows, barley and oats
everywhere else, on deep soil under the mild wet weather that suits them. It feeds Aldorath, and its
surplus feeds a good deal of the rest of the island—[[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Dúnavarre]] buys grain
here every year, [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Ondareth]] buys it in bad ones, and even
[[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Stormveld]] has bought it over the passes when the northern harvest failed,
through intermediaries, at prices nobody enjoyed.

Being fed by Aldorath is a fact of Eilwìthan politics that Aldorath's kings understand very well and
mention perhaps too often.

## The Manors

This is manorial country in its purest form. Demesne and tenantry, labor service, the lord's mill and
the lord's oven, courts baron sitting four times a year—the whole apparatus, densely settled and
long-established, and four in five of the people bound to it.

[[place-dunmere|Dunmere]] sits at the vale's head, on the shore of [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]] where the river leaves the lake, and [[place-ashford|Ashford]] at its crossing;
between them run the roads that carry everything the kingdom sells.

## Current Situation {#present}

The vale's barons are the council's core, and the council is divided. [[being-godwaldstrl|Godwald Storli]], the Elder of the Council, holds manors here and holds most of the vale's barons against the king's wish to have his son accepted as king-to-be, while the king offers the younger barons grants of royal forest and good marriages for their voices before the council sits at midsummer. The quarrel reaches the manors as levies: the barons send their men to the Wiindmarch late and few, and keep their tenants at home for the harvest.
