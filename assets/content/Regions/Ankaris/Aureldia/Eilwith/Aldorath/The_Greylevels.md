The **Greilevels** are the low, wet, tidal country where the eastern part of the [[place-aldorathrgn|Aldorath Region]] drains to the sea: flat
grazing, drained marsh, winding channels and extensive mudflats, with [[place-greywater|Greiwater]] at
the head of navigation. Twenty-four thousand people live on them, most within sight of water.

## The Outlet

The Levels are Aldorath's connection to everywhere else. Everything the kingdom sells—grain from the
[[place-aldorvale|Aldorvale]], wool off [[place-brynwold|Brinwold]], the wine of
[[place-sunnerslade|Sunnerslade]]—comes down the river and out through Greiwater, and everything the
kingdom buys comes back the same way.

Which means, since the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]]'s requirement, that all of it calls at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]] first.
The Levels' shipmasters have views about this that they express freely at home and never in writing.

## The Ground

Reclaimed and reclaimable in roughly equal measure. Generations of drainage have turned a great deal of
marsh into excellent grazing and some of it into arable, and the works are maintained by commissions of
sewers whose disputes are the most tedious and most bitterly fought litigation in the kingdom.

The flats below the town are dangerous and change; pilotage into Greiwater is hereditary, jealously
guarded, and the Levels' one genuinely irreplaceable trade.

## Current Situation {#present}

Ships are being lost on the bar below [[place-greywater|Greiwater]] in weather that should not sink them, and the town's harbor guard suspects smuggling or worse. The commission of sewers is fighting another of its disputes, over the endowment it owes [[place-threnhallow|Threnhallow]] and the chest it left on the mound, and the fowlers of the Levels say the drowned house's bell has rung with no body brought in. Every hull the Levels send to the mainland still calls at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]] first.
