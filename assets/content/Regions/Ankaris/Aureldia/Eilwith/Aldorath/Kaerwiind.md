## Overview {#overview}

**Kaerwiind** stands where the [[place-aldorathrgn|Aldorath Region]]'s settled country runs out and the interior forest begins, and it is a
fortress before it is anything else: a stone keep, a walled bailey, a garrison maintained at the crown's
expense, and a town that exists because the garrison does.

## History {#history}

The crown raised Kaerwiind as the march's fortress when the kings first went to war with the forest, and it has been the [[place-wyndmarch|Wiindmarch]]'s keep and muster since. Since the Nordmen took the Stormplain it has watched the Stormveld passes as well as the forest. The march's wake tale, [[lore-coldfordhead|the Head at Koldford]], is set at the board of a lord of Kaerwiind and at the fords below it.

## Current Situation {#present}

[[being-kenwaldhghd|Kenwald Highden]], the Warden of the Wiindmarch, holds Kaerwiind for the crown with a garrison that is short of men. The barons' levies come late and short while the council quarrels with the king, and the smugglers over the Sunderfells grow bolder.

## The Watch

The forest beyond holds tribal communities that the lowland realms call barbarians and that are markedly
more warlike than the northern tribes of the [[place-pshtrwldrns|Peshtar Wilderness]]. They raid, they
are raided, and they have long memories about which is owed. Kaerwiind is where Aldorath's answer to that
is organized.

It is also the kingdom's forward position in its long rivalry with
[[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Dúnavarre]] over the interior—a rivalry that has never come to open war and
is conducted, on this side, largely by the placing of garrisons in country neither realm actually
controls.

## The Town

Two thousand four hundred people, most of them provisioning, arming, shoeing or feeding the garrison.
Its market deals in the goods of the forest margin: timber, charcoal, furs, and the trade with such
forest communities as are on speaking terms this decade. That trade is illegal in strict law and
universal in practice.
