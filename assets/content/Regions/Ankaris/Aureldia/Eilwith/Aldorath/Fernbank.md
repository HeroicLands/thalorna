## Overview {#overview}

**Fernbank** is a high bank of earth and rock, grown over with fern, in the wet oak woods of [[place-thornwold|the Thornwold]] beside the old Crown Road that runs south from [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]], at the lake's south-eastern tip, toward [[place-hallowwood|Seirasto]]. A [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] nest lives in caves under it, a few hours' walk south of the ford below the last foresters' lodge.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The nest holds the forest on both sides of the road from the ford to well south of [[place-wenlornestage|Wenlorne Stage]]. It does not hold the road. Its raiders cross the fitted stone quickly and never walk along it, and they keep clear of the lit mile around the stage. What it covets is the mast of the oak and beech avenue the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] planted along the road under [[lore-jointcrown|the Joint Crown]], and the deer and swine the mast draws every autumn.

## Layers {#history}

The forest was the Joint Crown's, and the road ran through it to Seirasto. When the crown fell the forest was left to nobody, and the Grukar came into it within a century of their landing. The nest at the bank has budded and been rebuilt more than once, and the bank has held a nest for as long as the king's foresters have kept a roll of their losses.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The king's foresters out of Dunmere send no patrols down the road, and the nest takes no patrols at the forest's edge. To the south the Senali kill every raider that crosses into Seirasto. So the nest raids north: up to the ford and the lodge, and into the stock of the last steadings, and it takes the carters who go down the road to see the lit house and step off the stone.

## Dangerous Neighbours {#dangerous-neighbours}

Wenlorne Stage, whose lamps the nest does not go near. The Senali at the forest's edge, who turn travelers back politely and kill Grukar without a word. The [[affiliation-durrask|Durrask]], who fight it in the oak copses wherever they meet it.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

From the road, nothing but a fern-grown bank under oaks, and a smell on a still day. Behind the fern the bank is holed with cave mouths, and the caves run back to a chamber under its highest point. A carter's cart lies on its side in a hollow nearby, with the harness cut.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The carters.** A steading below the ford has lost a son down the road, and does not know whether to blame the Grukar or the lit house.
- **The roll.** The captain of the king's foresters at Dunmere would like to know how many of the nine lost patrols the Grukar took, and how many the road did.
