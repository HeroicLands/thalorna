## Overview {#overview}

**Wolfgate** is a line of caves in a crag of the eastern [[place-sunderfells|Sunderfells]], above the path that climbs east from the summit of Sundergate toward the high valleys of [[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]]. Its name is the smugglers', for the narrows below the crag where the path squeezes between rock and drop. A [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] nest lives in the caves.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The nest holds the crag and the narrows, and everything that crosses them is its prey: the smugglers' pony trains, the jarl's men and [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Aldorath]]'s when they skirmish at the pass-head, and the thralls who run from [[place-stormplain|the Stormplain]] over Sundergate and east to the [[place-vardainvalleys|Vardain Valleys]], a few in every open season.

## Layers {#history}

The path was a Vardain path before the Nordmen took the plain, and the Khazari kept watch over it from the heights. When [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Stormveld]] took the plain in 660 AF the runaways began, and the path filled with people traveling light, alone and in the dark. The nest at the crag is older than that, but the runaways have made it rich. The smugglers have lost ponies at the narrows for as long as they have run goods over the pass, and they cross it by day and in numbers.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The snow is on the pass, and nothing crosses it. The nest dug in for the winter on short stores, after a season in which the garrisons at both pass-heads killed more of its raiders than usual. Its spawner has laid Uk, which hatch quickest and eat least, and it waits for the thaw. When the snow goes, the first to cross are the runaways who have waited out the winter on the plain, and the nest is waiting for them hungry.

## Dangerous Neighbours {#dangerous-neighbours}

The jarl's men at the northern pass-head and the garrison of [[place-caerwynd|Kaerwiind]] at the southern skirmish whenever the snow allows. Below the southern approach, the post at [[place-sunderwynd|Sunderwiind]] holds something worse than Grukar, and the nest's raiders do not go near it.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

The narrows: a ledge path under an overhang, with a drop on the other side and the crag above. Bones of ponies lie at the foot of the drop. The cave mouths are high in the crag, reached by a cleft the raiders climb, and the caves run back into a warm, deep chamber where the spawner lies. Pack-saddles, smugglers' bales and the iron collars of runaway thralls are piled in the outer caves.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The first crossing.** The Reeve of the Valleys wants the narrows cleared before the thaw, so that the runaways reach the valleys alive.
- **The bales.** A smuggler of the Wiindmarch lost a pony train at the narrows in the autumn, and pays for whatever of its cargo can be brought out.
