## Overview {#overview}

**Vorgald Hold** is the hold of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]] at [[place-vorgald|Vorgald]], in the eastern heights of [[place-ironfells|the Ironfells]], and the [[place-vardainvalleys|Vardain Valleys]] beneath it. The Khazari call it _Varíg_ (_vah-REEG_), "farthest". About two thousand Khazari live in the rock and about eight thousand five hundred [[lore-vardain|Vardain]] in the valleys below. It holds no relationship with any realm on [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]].

The hold holds the valleys: the Vardain there are admitted as refugees, protected and fed from the granaries the hold keeps, and they farm for it and serve as its factors with everyone else. Neither people could live in this country without the other.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

**Inside the rock** the hold is governed as every Khazari hold is, by the council of the elders of its houses, sitting together with no one first among them. The council judges the hold's quarrels from its archive, apportions its stores and sends its people to their work. It sends one of its elders to the Gate as **Speaker**, to hear what the valleys bring up and to give the hold's answer. The Vardain and the lowland realms call the Speaker the lord of the hold, and the Khazari let them. A second elder is **Master of Works**, who keeps the water, the flues, the terraces and the granaries of the valleys and sends the engineers down to mend them.

**In the valleys** the Vardain choose their own officers. At the autumn tally the elders of the nine settlements choose a **Reeve of the Valleys**, who speaks for them at the Weighing Yard of [[place-vargate|Vargate]] and answers to the Speaker. The four tally families of [[place-barakth|Hudakal]] give the valleys a **Tally-Keeper**, who keeps the reckoning of everything owed up the mountain and is the one who knows how to send a question up. The highest village, [[place-dorrin|Dorrin]], keeps a **Watch-Warden** over the eastern approaches, and the burial valley of [[place-threndale|Threndale]] a **Keeper of the Dead**.

## Current Situation {#present}

- **The count of mouths.** People still come over [[place-sunderfells|the Sunderfells]] from the thrall-fields of [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Stormveld]], a few in every open season, and the valleys still take them in. At the autumn tally the Speaker asked the Reeve how many more mouths the granaries will carry through a snowbound winter, and the Reeve gave a number. The Tally-Keeper has not said whether her tallies agree with it.
- **The outer workings.** In the autumn a band of [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] from the nest at [[place-hornhall|Hornhall]], an abandoned hunting hall of the hold's in the heights to the north, reached the outer workings above Dorrin and was killed there. The Watch-Warden's people found its tracks the day before and do not know where it came from.
- **The conduits.** A conduit above [[place-kalm|Kalm]] failed in the autumn frost and the Master of Works sent engineers down within the week. They mended it and went on to recut a stretch of the old channel no Vardain alive has seen them touch.
- **The Synod.** The [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]] of [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Dúnavarre]] sent another letter up with the autumn caravan to Vargate, as it does most years, and the Reeve sent it up to the Gate. The hold has not answered.

## History {#history}

The Khazari cut Vorgald about 3100 BF, before the Fall of Khazartúrn, at the far western end of the city's reach, and taught the people beneath it to farm ([[place-ironfells|The Ironfells]]). From about 1585 BF the hold shared [[lore-jointcrown|the Joint Crown]] of the island with the Senali, until the Senali king was killed at the Three Sisters in 984 BF and the Senali withdrew into the forest. The Khazari finished the war alone, fixed the bounds of [[place-vardanreach|Vardanreach]] for the Vardain, and kept that nation's friendship for more than sixteen centuries ([[lore-endgreatwar|the Ending of the Great War]]).

In 660 AF the Nordmen took the plain and enslaved most of the Vardain ([[lore-stormveldtkn|the Taking of Stormveld]]). The hold did not intervene, and has not said why. It admitted those who reached the passes to the valleys beneath it, and has protected them since.

## Relations {#relations}

The hold has no treaty, embassy or correspondence with any realm. A handful of times in a century it deals directly with [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Aldorath]] or Dúnavarre, and every other dealing goes through Vardain hands at Vargate. It is at war with the Grukar, a war that has no end, and kills them wherever it finds them. The Nordmen of Stormveld covet the Ironfells passes the hold overlooks, and no war-band has raided the valleys in memory.
