## Overview {#overview}

**Staghall** is a hunting hall the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] cut into a crag on a wooded ridge in [[place-dunavarre|Dúnavarre]]'s western forest, over the valley heads of [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]] near the edge of [[place-hallowwood|Seirasto]]. A stag cut in relief over its door gives it its name. A [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] nest holds it, above the forest where the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]] and the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]] are at war.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The nest holds the ridge and the upper valleys below it, and hunts the deer of the valley heads. Neither side of the war below claims the ridge: the Seilvri do not go up to the bare crag, and the Order's road has not reached that far.

## Layers {#history}

The hall was cut by [[place-vorgald|Vorgald]]'s hunters, as [[place-hornhall|Hornhall]] was, and used until the end of the Great War, when the Khazari left their outer halls. It is far from the hold, and no record of a Khazari company coming to clear it has reached the valleys. The Seilvri have known a nest was on the ridge for as long as they have kept the forest, and their bowmen have fought its raiders in the valley heads for generations.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The Order's road climbs a few miles deeper into Teilvari every summer, and the war around it drives the deer out of the valley heads. The nest's hunters come back with less each season. It has turned to the people below: its raiders take sentries from the Order's road camps by night, and gatherers from the Seilvri clearings, which the households have moved higher and closer to the ridge.

The Order counts every sentry it loses as a Seilvri killing and hangs Seilvri for them. The Seilvri count every gatherer lost as the Order's work.

## Dangerous Neighbours {#dangerous-neighbours}

The Order's patrols ride the road by day and hang anyone they find carrying food into the wood. The Seilvri bowmen hold the valley heads and take any stranger for the Order's scout.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A square door in the crag's rock face, under a carved stag with one antler broken off. A hall with a hearth-pit and a smoke shaft, meat racks cut in the walls and a passage of small rooms behind. The nest has packed the passage with rubble and turf and lies in the inner rooms. Under its litter are Order mail rings, a road-camp kettle and Seilvri arrowheads, all taken in the last season.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The sentries.** A knight of the Order wants to know who is taking his road camp's sentries, and expects the answer to be a Seilvri bowman.
- **The gatherers.** A Seilvri household has lost a daughter gathering above the clearings, and its elder offers to guide a party through Teilvari to anyone who goes up the ridge.
- **The proof.** Order mail in a Grukar nest is proof the Order hangs Seilvri for deaths they did not cause, and both sides would do something with it.
