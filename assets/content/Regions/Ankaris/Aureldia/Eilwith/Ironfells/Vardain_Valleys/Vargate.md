**Vargate** stands at the foot of the road that climbs to [[place-vorgald|Vorgald]], and it is the only
reason anyone outside [[place-vardainvalleys|the valleys]] has heard of
[[lore-vardain|the Vardain]]. Fifteen hundred people—much the largest settlement in [[place-ironfells|the Ironfells]].

## The Gate

The town's name is its function. **Everything the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] send down comes through Vargate, and
everything they receive goes up from it**, and there is no second route.

The exchange happens at the [Weighing Yard]{.name data-type="place/site"} at the top of the town: Vardain factors bring up what has
been contracted—grain, cheese, fodder, fuel, worked timber—and take down what has been made. No
outsider is admitted to the yard and no Khazár is seen below it. What passes between the yard and the
Gate above is Vardain business, conducted by a small number of families who have done it for
generations and who do not discuss the details with anyone, including other Vardain.

## The Caravan Road

Vargate is the **end of the road**, and that is the only reason outsiders know the name.

The road up is nearly six thousand feet of climb from the lowlands and is **shut by snow for a third of
the year**, so the trade has a season and everybody plans around it. Trading caravans come up for
Khazari goods—two or three times in the open months, from
[[place-ashford|Ashford]] and [[place-greywater|Greiwater]], less often from further—and Vargate is
where they stop. There is no arrangement by which a caravan proceeds further, no fee that buys it, and
no exception on record. **No caravan has ever been allowed into the hold**, and no outsider has been up
the road above the Weighing Yard.

Caravan masters accept this because the alternative is not trading. They corral below the town, buy over
several days, and go back down, and the more experienced of them have stopped asking questions that
Vardain factors answer with the same three sentences every year.

## The Market

Below the yard is an ordinary and rather good mountain market, and this is where outsiders come. Buyers
from Ashford and Greiwater climb up two or three times a year for
the Khazari goods—instruments, fine tools, wire, springs, worked stone, and metalwork of a quality
that makes mainland smiths ask uncomfortable questions about who really made it.

The Vardain sell it as their own work. Nobody believes this and nobody says so, because the polite
fiction is a condition of the trade and everyone would rather have the goods.

## For Visitors

Vargate is as far as you get. Strangers are received courteously, housed, fed, charged fairly and
watched constantly, and the road above the Weighing Yard is not walked by outsiders. People who have
tried have been escorted down, once, politely.

There is no second time, and the Vardain will not say what the second time consists of.

## The Present {#present}

The Reeve of the Valleys, [[being-davrimhssk|Davrim Hessek]], is of a Vargate factor family and speaks for the valleys at the Weighing Yard. The newest runaways from the plain are lodged in the town until the elders find them ground, and the Reeve is buying grain from the lowland caravans in quantities the market has noticed. A Greiwater buyer has begun telling the Ashford market which goods at Vargate are Khazari work, and the factors would like him to stop.
