The **Ironfells** are the spine of north-eastern [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]]. They rise at the south-eastern corner of
[[place-stormveld|Stormveld]], where they join the Sunderfells, and run **east to the sea**, with
[[place-aldorathrgn|Aldorath]] along their southern side. No realm holds them.

They are not high by continental standards and they do not need to be. Eilwìth is a **cool, wet
island**, and the Ironfells are steep, sodden, thickly weathered and snow-covered for a third of the
year—country that is crossed at two places and otherwise gone round.

## The Two Passes

Only **two passes** carry traffic between the Stormveld plain and [[place-aldorathrgn|Aldorath]], and both run through deep
wilderness on either approach. **Irongate**, the western, climbs out of the plain's south-western corner
and comes down into the [[place-wyndmarch|Wiindmarch]] under [[place-caerwynd|Kaerwiind]]. **Sundergate**,
the eastern, crosses [[place-sunderfells|the Sunderfells]] by the gorge that [[place-sunderwynd|Sunderwiind]]
watches on its southern approach, and from its summit a path climbs east toward the high valleys. They are the reason the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]]'s expansion southward has never happened
and the reason [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Aldorath]] has never seriously attempted to take the north back: an army can get
through, but only in single file, only in summer, and only if nobody is holding the far end.

The passes are watched from the south at [[place-caerwynd|Kaerwiind]] and from the north by whichever
of the jarl's men has currently displeased him.

## The Rain-Shadow

The range's other consequence is agricultural and out of all proportion to its size. The prevailing
weather comes off the western ocean, wet and cold; by the time it has crossed the island and struck the
Ironfells it has little left to give, and the **western slopes and the country in their lee are the
driest and warmest ground on Eilwìth**.

That is why [[place-sunnerslade|Sunnerslade]] grows vines at a latitude that has no business growing
them, and why Aldorath's wine is worth carrying to the mainland.

## The Khazari

Somewhere in the eastern heights, where the range meets the sea cliffs, is
[[place-vorgald|Vorgald]]—a hold of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]], the Deep Folk. That it exists
is known. Very little else is.

They came to this range **around 3100 BF**, before [[lore-khazarturn|the Fall of Khazartúrn]], when the city's
people were still carrying holds outward from it. Vorgald is the far western end of that reach: a hold
on an island, across open water, at the edge of the country its founders knew. A hold cannot import
what it eats, so making one here meant first making farmers beneath it, and [[lore-vardain|the Vardain]]
of [[place-vardainvalleys|the valleys]] below have fed this mountain ever since.

The date is approximate and it is the Khazari's; no Eilwìthan record goes anywhere near it. What it
settles is the ground everything else in the island's elder history stands on. Khazartúrn fell seven
centuries later and the two elder races stopped dealing with one another everywhere in the world—except here, where they had been living on the same ground for seven hundred years already. That is why
[[miscgear-secondvoice|the Second Voice]] was to be held in trust on Eilwìth by both races together,
and why the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] and Khazari of this island attempted a joint kingdom that no
others of their kind have ever tried.

## Converging Interests {#present}

- **The passes.** The southern jarls of [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Stormveld]] send raiders through Irongate and Sundergate into Aldorath whenever the snow allows, and Kaerwiind has fewer men each season to answer them.
- **The runaways.** Thralls who run from the Stormplain take Sundergate and the path east to the valleys of [[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]], a few in every open season, and the valleys still take them in.
- **The Grukar.** The [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] and the Khazari compete for the range's caves, and the hold kills every Grukar it finds. A band from [[place-hornhall|Hornhall]], an abandoned Khazari hunting hall in the heights north of the valleys, reached Vorgald's outer workings in the autumn and was killed there, and the Vardain watch at [[place-dorrin|Dorrin]] does not know how it came. Another of the hold's abandoned halls, [[place-staghall|Staghall]], far off in the forest above Teilvari, holds a nest; [[place-wolfgate|Wolfgate]] waits on the Sundergate path for the thaw; and the nest at Hartfell hunts the royal forest of the Wiindmarch.
