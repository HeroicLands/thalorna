## Overview {#overview}

**Hornhall** is a hunting hall the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] of [[place-vorgald|Vorgald]] cut into a crag in the heights of [[place-ironfells|the Ironfells]], north of the basin of [[place-vardainvalleys|the Vardain Valleys]] and above the high grazing of [[place-highfold|Highfold]]. Its name is the Vardain's, for the antlers carved over its door. It is a [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] nest, the nearest to the hold, and the hold has never let one stand there for long.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

A Grukar nest holds it, around its spawner in the hall's innermost room. The hall's ground is the crags and high corries above the basin, where the deer summer, and the nest hunts them as the Khazari once did. Its enemy is [[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]], which kills every Grukar it finds and wants the hall empty.

## Layers {#history}

The hold cut the hall in its early centuries, one of several it kept in the range's empty heights, so that its hunters could stay out through a season and bring venison and hides home to a people that grows nothing. The hunters used it until the end of the Great War in 984 BF, when the Khazari went back inside the mountain and left their outer halls as they stood.

The Grukar reached the Ironfells within a century or two of their landing, and the halls were the best ground in the range for them: cut, dry, defensible and empty. The hold's archive records clearing Hornhall more than once. Each time the hold's company killed every Grukar in it, and each time a faction budded from some nest farther off found the hall empty and took it. The nest in it now is the latest.

## What Is There Now {#present}

In the autumn the nest sent a band into the valleys. It crossed the high grazing above Highfold and [[place-dorrin|Dorrin]], took sheep, and reached the hold's outer workings above Dorrin, where the Khazari killed it. The Watch-Warden of Dorrin, [[being-vadrasnk|Vadra Senek]], found its tracks and does not know how it passed her watch.

The nest lost its best raiders with that band. Its spawner is laying to replace them, and its Sha, [[being-fellwatch|Fellwatch]], is watching the high grazing for a way into the valleys that the hold does not guard. The Highfold herders have seen a thin figure on the crest above the summer pastures twice this winter.

:::secret {title="The hunters' road"}
The band came down a hunters' road the Khazari cut along the crest from Hornhall to the high grazing, a ledge path on the north side of the ridge, out of sight of Dorrin, which watches east and toward the sea. The hold's archive records the hall and the road. The council knows where the band came from and means to send a company to clear the hall when the snow goes off the crest. It has told the valleys nothing, and the Speaker at the Gate, [[being-valdantnvgrz|Valdan Tanv-guráz]], has not answered the Watch-Warden's question.
:::

## Dangerous Neighbours {#dangerous-neighbours}

The hold, above all. A Khazari company that comes to clear the hall kills everything in it and does not ask who else is there. The crags are steep and iced for half the year, and the corries below hold the range's wolves.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A door cut square in the crag face, with a pair of stag's antlers carved in the rock above it and worn almost smooth. Behind it a long hall with a hearth-pit and a smoke shaft, racks cut in the walls for drying meat, and sleeping rooms off a passage at the back. The nest has walled the passage into chambers with rubble, and the innermost room, warm and close, holds the spawner and its guard. Old Khazari iron, hooks and a broken spit, lies under the nest's litter.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The way in.** Vadra Senek wants strangers who can climb to find how the band came down past her watch.
- **The thin figure.** The Highfold herders pay in cheese and wool for whatever is watching them from the crest to be driven off or killed.
- **The hall.** A scholar who knows the Khazari kept outer halls would give a great deal for a drawing of one, and Hornhall is the only one any human has found.
