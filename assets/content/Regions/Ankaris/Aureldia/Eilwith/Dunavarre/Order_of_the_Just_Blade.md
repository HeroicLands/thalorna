## Overview {#overview}

The **Order of the Just Blade** is a knightly order of the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]], whose god stands for order, justice and honorable struggle, and it is the army of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]]. Its knights swear before a priest of Árdavon to keep his law, to defend the weak and to serve the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]], and its badge is a sword upright between the two faces of the god's mask.

Dúnavarre honors it above every other body in the realm, the Synod included. Its knights keep the roads, ride escort for anyone the law protects, sit beside Árdavon's priests when a temple court hears a case of arms, and are held to their oath in public. The island's other courts measure their chivalry against it. Its commandery is at [[place-kelivren|Kelivren]], where it holds the [[lore-kelivrentrny|Royal Kelivren Tournament of Chivalry]] every spring.

It is also the instrument of the Synod's war on the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]], which it has waged since about 694 AF ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]).

## Membership {#membership}

A **Knight of the Blade** is sworn at the [Sanctification of Swords]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} each spring, before the Order's chaplain, and receives the sword-badge. Most are the sons and daughters of the Synod's stewards and of the kingdom's old families, and a knight who comes up from the sergeants is honored for it. **Sworn Sergeants** fight under the knights and keep the Order's houses and its roads. The **Knight-Captains** command its companies and its charges, among them [[being-toradhtaravren|Dame Torad Taravren]], who rides with the Synod's embassy to Tantsou.

The Order answers to the Synod for its service and to Árdavon for its oath, and its knights hold that the two never disagree.

## What It Wants Now {#present}

**The war in the west.** The Order means to finish the war on the Seilvri, and it is winning. Each spring its knights muster at Kelivren after the tournament, and each summer Sir Merrad cuts the road a few miles deeper into Teilvari and burns the clearings it reaches. About two thousand Seilvri are left. The Order offers terms: a Seilvri who comes out of the woods and kneels at [[lore-ardavondty|Árdavon]]'s threshold is spared and settled as an unfree tenant on an estate. A few hundred have accepted, and those who do not kneel are killed where they are found.

**Two names for one war.** The Order calls the war a crusade. Its chaplain preaches it as the light of Árdavon carried to a people outside every oath and law, and the knights who ride west believe it. The Seilvri call it the Hunting, and on the western estates and in [[place-wraymouth|Wraymouth]] many of the same people who honor the Order call the war a murder. The Order's chivalry and its war are the same knights in the same season.

**The cost.** The road eats men, oxen and silver, and the Synod's [[being-halvrasenvl|Bursar]] has asked the Knight-Marshal how many more summers it needs. The Order wants a larger muster this year and a final campaign into the heart of Teilvari.

**The Umbral Court.** The Order hunts [[affiliation-umbralcourt|the Umbral Court]] and has sympathizers of the Court in its own ranks.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

The Order's knights are on every road in Dúnavarre, and a party traveling the kingdom meets a patrol before it meets a steward. The commandery at Kelivren hires guides, scouts, axemen and drovers for the summer road, and the tournament each spring draws knights from every court on the island. Anyone who brings word of the Seilvri to Kelivren is heard and paid.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The muster.** The Knight-Marshal is hiring scouts who know the forest edge for this summer's road, and pays well.
- **The tournament.** A foreign knight means to ride in this spring's tournament and to ride west with the Order after it, and her house wants someone to bring her home.
- **The terms.** A Seilvri family that came out of Teilvari under the Order's terms has been taken from its steward's estate by night, and the steward wants it found.
