**Wreimouth** stands where the [Wrei]{.name data-type="place/feature"} comes down to the sea on [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]]'s south-eastern coast, and is the
second city of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]] at about twenty-five hundred people.

[[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]] is the seat and holds the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]]; Wreimouth does the work. These are the
best natural harbors on the island, facing open water toward [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] and the
mainland beyond, and effectively all of Dúnavarre's trade—and a good share of Eilwìth's—passes over
its quays.

## The Port

Wreimouth handles the outbound wool, hides, salt-fish and worked goods of the kingdom's estates, and
takes in grain, wine, timber and manufactures from the mainland. Its shipping is unromantic and
extremely regular: Dúnavarre is an orderly realm and its port reflects it, with a harbor-master's
office that keeps records the Synod actually reads.

The city is also where Dúnavarre's diplomacy physically happens. Embassies to and from Élavendre,
[[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia]] and the Vilérian ports land here, and the mission to [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Tantsou]] sailed from these quays.

## The Present {#present}

The harbor-master, [[being-alfrithbrnly|Alfrith Brinli]], runs the busiest quays on the island, and this spring they are busy with the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]]'s business: iron, oxen and hired men coming in for the summer's road into [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]]. The Order wants the outbound ships searched for Seilvri furs and wax, which come down to the coast from the forest edge and go out to the mainland in Wreimouth's holds. Alfrith answers to the Warden of the Ports at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]], and the shipping houses she answers for pay the realm's revenue. Wreimouth's taverns are where the war in the west is called a murder most openly, and the Order has begun to send knights to drink in them ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]).

## Character

Wreimouth is more mixed and considerably less decorous than Ravenmoor. Its harbor quarter speaks
several languages and a good deal of trade pidgin, its shipping houses are commercial rather than
courtly, and the Synod's administration sits more lightly here than anywhere else in the kingdom—not
from weakness, but because a port that is generating the realm's revenue is granted a certain latitude.
