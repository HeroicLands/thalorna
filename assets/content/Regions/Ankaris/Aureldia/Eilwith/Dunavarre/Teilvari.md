## Overview {#overview}

**Teilvari** (_tail-VAH-ree_) is the forest the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]] still hold: old oak and ash in the steep wooded valleys of [[place-dunavarre|Dúnavarre]]'s west, where the kingdom's forest runs up above its western estates to the edge of [[place-hallowwood|Seirasto]]. It is the deepest of their woods and the last. About two thousand of the people live in it, in household clearings a few miles apart.

## The Land {#the-land}

The ground climbs from the edge of the estates through wooded valleys, each with a stream, to the bare ridges along Seirasto's edge. The canopy is close, the undergrowth thick in the valley bottoms, and the ridges between the valleys are the only easy going. Rain comes off the sea most days. There is no road through it but the Order's, which runs up the southernmost valley from [[place-kelivren|Kelivren]] and stops where the last summer's work stopped.

## Current Situation {#present}

Teilvari is a war front. The Order's road, its camps and the burned clearings beside it are on the forest's southern side; [[being-arvanir|Arvanir]]'s bowmen hold the valley heads above them. The Order's patrols ride the road by day, and its camps lose sentries by night. The households have moved their clearings higher and closer together than the forest can feed, and they go hungry in the late winter. [[being-neilwa|Neilwa]] keeps the gathering in the heart of the forest, and its place is moved whenever the road comes near.

## Dangers {#dangers}

A stranger in Teilvari is taken for the Order's scout by the Seilvri and for a Seilvri friend by the Order. The ridges carry tripwires and the valley bottoms pits. The Order hangs anyone it finds carrying food into the wood.
