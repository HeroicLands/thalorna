## Overview {#overview}

The **Seilvri** (_SAIL-vree_) are a forest people of [[place-dunavarre|Dúnavarre]]'s west, perhaps two thousand of them, living in the old woods of [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]] where Dúnavarre's forest runs up to the edge of [[place-hallowwood|Seirasto]]. They hunt, gather, keep pigs and small gardens in their clearings, and trade honey, furs and wax at the forest edge. They have their own speech and their own rites for the dead, and they keep no lords.

They are losing a war. Since about 694 AF the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]] has fought them at the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]]'s command, and the war has taken their valleys, the wood where their dead lie, and more than half their people ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]). They fight back with the bow, in the woods they know, against knights in mail with roads, fire and numbers behind them.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

Every household keeps its own clearing and its own fire. The households meet at a gathering, and the gathering chooses an **Elder** for life, who speaks for the people, keeps the rites of the dead and the names of the woods, and settles quarrels between households. The Elder is [[being-neilwa|Neilwa]].

The fighters choose a **War-Leader** and follow for as long as they choose. The war-leader answers to the Elder at the gathering and nowhere else. The war-leader is [[being-arvanir|Arvanir]], who was a boy at the Hushwood.

## Range {#range}

Before the war the Seilvri held the wooded valleys between Dúnavarre's estates and Seirasto. The Order has taken the valleys and the Synod has granted them to estates. What is left to the people is Teilvari, the deepest of their woods, high against Seirasto's edge and a hard week from any road.

## Relations {#relations}

- **The [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]].** Its registers name the Seilvri its nemesis. Its Synod ordered the war and its estates hold the people's valleys.
- **The [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]].** The enemy in the field. The Order's terms are that a Seilvri who comes out of the woods and kneels at [[lore-ardavondty|Árdavon]]'s threshold is spared and settled on an estate. A few hundred have accepted.
- **The forest edge.** Charcoal-burners, swineherds and the tenants of the western estates trade with the Seilvri, shelter them now and then, and are hanged for it when the Order learns of it.

## History {#history}

The Seilvri held their valleys before Dúnavarre's first estate was cut from them, by their own telling and by the kingdom's oldest charters, which grant land "to the edge of the Seilvri woods." Dúnavarre traded with them for centuries. In about 694 AF the Synod declared them a danger to the island, and the Order took the field. At the turn of the year about 703 the Order trapped several thousand of the people in [[place-hushwood|the Hushwood]], the oldest of their woods and the ground of their dead, and killed them in three days. The survivors went west into Teilvari, and the Order has followed them there.

## The Present Pressure {#present}

Each summer the Knight-Captain of the West, [[being-merraddovvrn|Sir Merrad Dovavren]], rides out of [[place-kelivren|Kelivren]] with knights fresh from the tournament, cuts the road a few miles deeper into Teilvari and burns the clearings it reaches. Each winter the Seilvri take back what they can of the forest's edge. The road goes deeper every year. Arvanir's bowmen hold the passes into the deep wood, and they lose men they cannot replace.

The people are divided over what the war is for. Neilwa holds that the people's first duty is to their dead, who lie unsettled in the Hushwood because no Seilvri has been able to reach them and keep the rite since the killing. Arvanir holds that the living must come first, and that a people that spends its fighters on a wood the Order has posted will have no one left to keep anything.

## The Gathering Place {#gathering-place}

The households gather at the turn of each season in a clearing in the heart of Teilvari, around a fire that is not let go out while the gathering sits. Its place is moved whenever the Order's road comes near it.

## The Names of the People

Seilvri names are their own, and no Eilwìthi element builds them. They run to two or three syllables and favor _v_, _l_, _r_, _n_, _w_ and _s_, the vowel groups _ei_ and _ai_, and the clusters _lv_, _lw_ and _vr_ inside a word. Women's names end in _-a_, men's in _-ir_ or _-an_, and the names of woods and places in _-ri_. They avoid _th_, _k_, _g_ and _sh_, and the endings _-e_ and _-el_ of the Élavendri the kingdom's court speaks. A Seilvri carries one name and is known, among strangers, by the clearing of their household.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The rite.** Neilwa wants an escort into the Hushwood for the three nights the rite of the dead needs, past the Synod's posts and the Order's patrols.
- **The ones who came out.** A Seilvri woman settled on an estate near Kelivren asks a party to carry a message into Teilvari for her, and the steward who holds her bond is paid to watch for exactly that.
- **The road.** The Order is buying axes, oxen and labor for this summer's stretch of road, and pays well for scouts who know the forest edge.
