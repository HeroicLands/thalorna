In the old forest of [[place-dunavarre|Dúnavarre]]'s west, between the estates and the deeper woods along Seirasto's edge, lies **the Hushwood**, a stand of old forest in which nothing sings. It is the newest of the [[place-aureldirgn|Aureldia Region]]'s [[lore-bloodfield|blood-fields]], made in three days at the turn of the year about 703 AF, and the only one in living memory.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

[[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Dúnavarre]] forbids entry. The [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]] posts the paths on the kingdom's side of the wood, and the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]] patrols its margin. The [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]], whose wood it was, hold [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]] to the west and cannot reach it.

## Layers {#history}

The Hushwood was the oldest of the Seilvri woods and the ground where they laid their dead. In the crusade's ninth year the Order drove several thousand of the people into it, with their children, and killed them there in three days ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]). The survivors went west into Teilvari. No Seilvri has entered the wood since.

## A Silence

The Hushwood is a field of the silent kind. The birds that fill every other wood on the island are absent from it, summer insects do not stir, and a traveler who enters hears only his own breath and the weather, and a lantern burns low and small. The field is thin. By day it is merely wrong; after dark things come out of it that harm the living, and the Order's patrols have lost men on its edge.

At the edge of hearing, in the stillest hours, there are voices. Those who have listened describe a speech of a kind none of them knew, spoken at a distance and in numbers, never close enough to answer. It is the Seilvri tongue, which the people in Teilvari still speak.

:::secret {title="The unreceived dead"}
Beyond the voices, the predators of the spirit realm gather where souls are unreceived, and the Hushwood has not stopped drawing them since the killing.
:::

## Whose Dead

The dead of the Hushwood are the Seilvri, killed several thousand together with their children in the wood they had kept. They went to their deaths without the rite their own people keep, and the people who keep it are alive in Teilvari and cannot come to them.

:::secret {title="The rite that closes the field"}
The clergy of [[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]] settle a field through a rite understood as placing in the god's keeping the dead who were his, and the Seilvri were not. Only a Seilvri rite can close this field, and the Synod's posts stand between the people and their dead.
:::

## What Is There Now {#present}

The Synod's posts stand on the paths from the estates, and the Order's patrols keep to the margin and lose men there regardless. The forest peoples of the west do not need to be told to keep away. [[being-neilwa|Neilwa]], the Seilvri elder, means to keep the rite in the wood, which takes three nights, and the Order knows the people want their dead.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The child.** A rumor on the forest edge says one Seilvri child was carried out of the wood alive. The Synod would like to find her.
- **The voices.** Someone has learned to follow them. What they say is not known, and what they say is wanted.
- **The rite.** Neilwa needs three nights in the wood and someone to see her past the posts.
- **A knight's conscience.** A Knight-Captain of the Order who fought in the crusade comes to the edge by night and asks to be taken in.
