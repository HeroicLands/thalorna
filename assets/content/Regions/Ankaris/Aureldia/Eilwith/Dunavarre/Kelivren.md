## Overview {#overview}

**Kelivren** (_keh-LIV-ren_) is the commandery of the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]], a walled house of grey stone on a spur at the foot of [[place-dunavarre|Dúnavarre]]'s western hills, with a village of about six hundred people in the valley beneath it. Below the walls lies the tiltyard of the [[lore-kelivrentrny|Royal Kelivren Tournament of Chivalry]], the greatest martial gathering on [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]]. Behind it, a day's ride up the valley, the Order's road goes into [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]].

## History {#history}

The kings of Dúnavarre built Kelivren as a hunting lodge and a fort against the hill country, and the first king to hold a tournament there gave it the name it keeps. When the crown was set aside about 490 AF the Synod granted the house to the Order, which made it a commandery and kept the tournament ([[lore-dunavarresynod|The Mages Take Dúnavarre]]). Since about 694 the war on the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]] has been mustered here, and the valleys above the village are the first the Order took from them ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]).

## Current Situation {#present}

Spring at Kelivren is the tournament and summer is the war. The knights who ride the lists in the spring ride west with [[being-merraddovvrn|Sir Merrad Dovavren]] after it, and the village fills with drovers, axemen and carters hired for the season's stretch of road. This year the Order wants its largest muster since the Hushwood, and the Knight-Marshal has written to every house in the kingdom that owes the Order a knight. The Seilvri tenants on the estates of the taken valleys watch the road, and the Order watches them.

## The Holder {#holder}

The commandery is the Order's, and the Knight-Marshal, [[being-orvandkthvl|Sir Orvand Kéthavel]], keeps it. The Knight-Captain of the West, Sir Merrad, keeps his company there between campaigns, and the Order's chaplain, [[being-edrintalvvl|Edrin Talvavel]], keeps its chapel.

## Garrison {#garrison}

Two companies of knights and their sworn sergeants winter at Kelivren, about three hundred fighting men and women, and the summer muster doubles it. The walls are high on the valley side and low on the hill side, where the spur falls away.

## Government, Faith and Trade {#government-faith-trade}

The village is the Order's tenantry. The Knight-Marshal's steward holds its court, and the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]] keeps the chapel inside the walls where the knights are sworn at the [Sanctification of Swords]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}. The village lives by the commandery: horses, harness, arrows, ale and the hire of men for the road. Furs, wax and honey from the forest edge still come down the valley, by roads the Order does not watch closely.

## Within the Walls {#within-the-walls}

A hall, a chapel, stables for three hundred horses, an armory and the roll-room, where the name of every knight sworn since the house became a commandery is written on the wall. The tiltyard and its stands lie outside the gate on the valley floor.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The muster.** The Order hires scouts, guides and axemen for the road, and asks no questions of anyone who knows the forest.
- **The furs.** A trader in the village buys Seilvri furs, and the Order has begun to ask who sells them to him.
- **The roll.** A name has been scraped from the roll-room wall, and the Knight-Marshal wants to know whose and why.
