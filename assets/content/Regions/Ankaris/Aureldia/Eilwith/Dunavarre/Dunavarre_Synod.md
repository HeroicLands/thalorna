## Overview {#overview}

The **Dúnavarre Synod**, called simply the Synod, is the governing council of mages of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]]. Its members are mages of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]], and it sits at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]] in the hall of the last king, whose chair stands empty at the head of the room ([[lore-dunavarresynod|The Mages Take Dúnavarre]]). It is also the Arcane Order's central chantry for [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]], and so it decides whether every other court on the island keeps its mage advisors.

## Who Sits {#who-sits}

The Synod's decisions are made by the **Elder Marendos** in closed session. Below them the **Marendos** hold portfolios of state, a district, a court, a revenue or a garrison, and the **Galethoi** and **Kosthemoi** learn the Synod's business and do its writing. The Synod issues every writ under its seal and none under a name, and Ravenmoor knows who holds its offices:

Prime Marendos
: [[being-velisndorrvl|Velisande Orravel]], who presides in the closed session and is the realm's head of state.

Sekeros
: [[being-torvinasmvrn|Torvin Asmavren]], who investigates unlicensed practice and the conduct of the Synod's own members.

Chancellor of the Synod
: [[being-ithrelildvl|Ithrel Ildavel]], who keeps the seal and is also the realm's Archivist.

Master of Kosthemoi
: [[being-nerisekvlrn|Nerise Kavalren]], who governs the Synod's students.

Master of Galethoi
: [[being-dorvanmlthvl|Dorvan Melthavel]], who governs the Galethoi and posts them to the realm's offices and the island's courts.

## How It Decides {#how-it-decides}

The Elder Marendos meet behind a closed door and decide by agreement, and the Prime speaks last. What they decide goes out under the seal as a writ, a charter or a license, and the realm's appointed officers carry it out: the Captain-General, the Justiciar, the Bursar, the Warden of the Ports, the Archivist and the Ambassador. Nobody outside the session is told how the decision was reached, and the session has never been asked.

## What It Is Fighting About {#present}

**The war in the west.** The Synod ordered the war on the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]] in about 694 and has never revoked it ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]). The [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]] asks for a larger muster and a final campaign into [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]]. The Bursar, [[being-halvrasenvl|Halvra Senavel]], asks how many more summers the road needs and what the forest is worth when it is taken. The Sekeros wants the Seilvri elder brought to Ravenmoor alive. The session has not yet decided between them.

**The Umbral Court.** The Synod's position is that [[affiliation-umbralcourt|the Umbral Court]] does not exist. Torvin Asmavren has spent six years trying to prove otherwise to the session.

**The embassy.** The mission to the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] under [[being-arancelavren|Aran Kélavren]] is the Synod's boldest act abroad, and the Elder Marendos who opposed it are waiting for it to fail.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

Every ship between the mainland and Eilwìth calls first at Ravenmoor, and the Synod's officers meet every mainland party there before anyone else on the island does. A party with a petition is shown the empty chair on its way in and answered under the seal. A party with a relic meets the Warden of the Ports.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The elder.** The Sekeros is paying for word of where the Seilvri gathering sits, and for the elder, alive.
- **A writ without a name.** A writ under the seal has reached a party's patron ordering something the Prime denies the session decided.
- **The count.** The Bursar wants an honest count of the Order's dead on the road, and the Order will not give her one.
