## Overview {#overview}

**The Kelgar** (_KEL-gar_) are the easternmost of [[lore-peshtarclans|the Peshtar's three tribes]], about twenty-two thousand people in some forty clans in [[place-stormwood|the Stormwood]], the forest between [[place-stormplain|the Stormplain]] and the western [[place-nordfells|Nordfells]]. They are cattle people before they are anything else. Their wealth is the herds they drive into the forest glens each summer and back to the sheltered valleys each winter, and their shamans hold [Kalmara]{.name data-type="lore/deity"}, the mother of herds, first of the gods.

They are at war with [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|the Jarldom of Stormveld]]. Two of its jarls raided their pastures in 718, and in 719 the Kelgar burned four of the High Jarl's steadings in the Nordfells.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

Each clan is ruled by its **war-chief** and its **shaman**. The shamans of the forty clans meet at the tribe's stone circle at the turns of the year, and in war they name one war-chief the **War-Leader** of the Kelgar, whom the other clans follow while the war goes well.

The War-Leader is [[being-bronnakh|Bronnakh]], war-chief of the clan whose glen lay nearest the plain and whose herds the jarls took. The shamans named him at the midsummer gathering of 718, after the raid.

## Range, Wells and Grounds {#range}

The Stormwood is oak and hazel in the low ground and pine on the ridges, with long open glens where the herds summer. Its eastern edge runs against the grain land of the Stormplain and the steadings of [[place-vuldstead|Vuldstead]], and its northern edge climbs into the western Nordfells. To the west lie the high pastures the Kelgar share, unwillingly, with [[affiliation-bradhar|the Bradhar]]. The glens nearest the plain have stood empty since the raid, and their clans have driven their herds west into the deeper forest.

## Relations {#relations}

- **[[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|The Jarldom of Stormveld]].** The enemy. The forest-edge steadings and the Kelgar traded cattle and raided each other's stock from the taking of the plain until the jarls of Vuldstead and [[place-drasknes|Drasknes]] swore their pact against the tribe in 718. Thirty Kelgar taken in that raid are thralls at Vuldstead and Drasknes.
- **[[affiliation-bradhar|The Bradhar]].** Rivals for the high pastures between their countries. The Kelgar clans driven west from the plain's edge now graze ground the Bradhar call theirs, and there has been blood over it twice since the raid.
- **[[lore-grukaraelwyth|The Grukar]].** Fought without pause. The nest at Hazelbrake raids the herds driven west, and a young nest has taken the emptied glen nearest the plain, [[place-kineglen|Kineglen]], whose clan wants it back.
- **[[affiliation-lonagar|The Lonagar]].** Trading partners and nothing more. Kelgar hides and cattle go north to the Lonagar inlets, and Lonagar salt and fish come south.

## History {#history}

The Kelgar have grazed the Stormwood glens since the [[lore-pelwarpepl|Pelwar]] came to the island. When the Nordmen took the plain in 660 AF the forest edge became Stormveld's frontier, and for two generations the steadings and the clans raided each other's stock, traded cattle at the forest edge and paid for killings in cattle by an understanding older than any jarl.

In the spring of 718 [[being-vrithorvmrvd|Vrithorv Marvidrok]] of Vuldstead and [[being-draskmilglrv|Draskmil Glarvendik]] of Drasknes swore a pact against the tribe, and that summer their men raided the pastures of the eastern glens together. They took some five hundred cattle and thirty captives. In the spring of 719 Bronnakh led the Kelgar's answer, not at Vuldstead but across the forest into the western Nordfells, and burned four steadings held by the High Jarl's sworn men. At the Midsomar moot the Lawspeaker ruled that the pact bound only its two jarls and that the burned steadings were not on their ground, and the moot awarded the High Jarl nothing.

## The Present Pressure {#present}

The war has not ended and no one has offered to end it. The forest-edge district under Vuldstead has stood to arms since the burning, and its men watch the trees for the blow they expect at their own steadings. Bronnakh's clans have taken back none of their cattle and none of their thirty people.

The shamans are divided. Some hold that the Kelgar should strike Vuldstead itself before the summer moot, while the jarls quarrel over the High Jarl's seat. Others hold that the clans driven west cannot fight Stormveld and the Bradhar at once, and that the high pastures must be settled first. Bronnakh has said he will take the war to Vuldstead, and he has also sent a shaman to the Lonagar inlets to ask what a Nordman hall pays for its own people when they are taken.

## The Gathering Place {#gathering-place}

The Kelgar gather at a ring of standing stones in the largest of the Stormwood glens, a day's walk west of the plain. The clans drive their herds through the ring at the turn of each summer for Kalmara's blessing, and the war-chiefs meet in it when there is war. No Kelgar carries a blade inside the ring.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The thirty.** A Kelgar clan wants its people back from the thrall-halls of Vuldstead and Drasknes, and will pay in cattle a party that can bring even one of them home.
- **The pastures.** The Bradhar have killed a Kelgar herdsman on the high pastures, and both tribes want a stranger to carry word between them before the clans settle it themselves.
- **A peace in cattle.** A hersvald of Vuldstead's forest edge has let it be known that his district would trade its share of the raided herd for an end to the watch, and the Kelgar want to know whether he can speak for anyone but himself.
