**The Nordmoor** is the north and north-west of [[place-pshtrwldrns|the Peshtar Wilderness]]: open moor and bog behind a cliff coast, broken by a few rocky inlets, with the forest in the sheltered folds and fog on all of it most days of the year. It runs east along the northern coast until the moor rises into the western [[place-nordfells|Nordfells]].

It is the country of [[affiliation-lonagar|the Lonagar]], about seventeen thousand people in some twenty-five clans, who herd sheep and black cattle on the moor and fish from skin boats in the inlets. Their stone circle stands on a headland above the largest inlet, where the coasters put in to trade.

## Current Situation {#present}

Stormveld's ships close the inlets to the coasters, and the clans fish and fowl the cliffs harder for it, on ground the [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] nest in the sea caves of Gullstone holds. The nest takes fowlers off their ropes and boats off the shingle.
