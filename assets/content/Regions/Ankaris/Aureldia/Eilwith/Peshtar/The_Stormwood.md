**The Stormwood** is the eastern forest of [[place-pshtrwldrns|the Peshtar Wilderness]]: oak and hazel on the low ground, pine on the ridges, and long grassy glens between them where the cattle summer. It runs from the western edge of [[place-stormplain|the Stormplain]] north to the western [[place-nordfells|Nordfells]], and the Stormveld steadings of [[place-vuldstead|Vuldstead]] stand against its eastern margin.

It is the country of [[affiliation-kelgar|the Kelgar]], about twenty-two thousand people in some forty clans. Their stone circle stands in the largest glen, a day's walk from the plain. The lowland name is Stormveld's, for the forest at the plain's edge; the Kelgar call it by the names of its glens.

The glens nearest the plain have been raided and burned since the jarls' pact of 718, and the clans that held them have taken their herds west into the deeper forest.

## Current Situation {#present}

The glen nearest the plain is held now by a young [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] nest, [[place-kineglen|Kineglen]], which raids Vuldstead's forest edge. Deeper in, the nest at Hazelbrake raids the Kelgar herds driven west into its range.
