## Overview {#overview}

**The Lonagar** (_loh-NAH-gar_) are the smallest of [[lore-peshtarclans|the Peshtar's three tribes]], about seventeen thousand people in some twenty-five clans on [[place-nordmoor|the Nordmoor]], the open moor and cliff coast of the [[place-pshtrwldrns|Peshtar Wilderness]]'s north and north-west. They herd sheep and black cattle on the moor, fish the inlets from skin boats, and trade. Their shamans hold [Veirael]{.name data-type="lore/deity"}, the walker in the mist, first of the gods, and read the fog for the boats.

Of the three tribes they deal most with the lowlands, and most peaceably. The coasters of [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Ondareth]] and of the north shore put in at their inlets with salt, iron and cloth and take away wool, hides and salted fish. Since the autumn of 719 the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]]'s ships have closed that water, and the Lonagar have a War-Leader for the first time in a generation.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

Each clan is ruled by its **war-chief** and its **shaman**, and a Lonagar war-chief makes the clan's bargains with the coasters as well as leading its fighters. The shamans gather on the headland above the largest inlet, and in war they name a **War-Leader**.

The War-Leader is [[being-sennog|Sennog]], the oldest war-chief of the tribe, named at the midwinter gathering of 719 after the jarls' ships burned his clan's boats on its strand.

## Range, Wells and Grounds {#range}

The Nordmoor is bog and heather behind a cliff coast, with forest only in the sheltered folds and fog on most of it most of the year. A handful of rocky inlets break the cliffs, and each has its clan, its strand of skin boats and its trading place above the tide. The moor runs east along the northern coast until it rises into the western [[place-nordfells|Nordfells]], where Stormveld's steadings begin.

## Relations {#relations}

- **[[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|The Jarldom of Stormveld]].** The enemy since the autumn. The High Jarl and the jarl of [[place-vuldstead|Vuldstead]] swore a pact to close the northern inlets to the coasters that do not call at [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]], and the Lonagar inlets are where those coasters were going.
- **[[affiliation-repblcndrth|The Republic of Ondareth]].** Its coasters are the Lonagar's best customers. The republic's legion fights the Bradhar in the south, and the Lonagar keep out of that war and out of the legion's way.
- **[[affiliation-kelgar|The Kelgar]] and [[affiliation-bradhar|the Bradhar]].** Kin by marriage and partners in trade: Lonagar salt and fish go south, and cattle and hides come north for the coasters.

## History {#history}

The Lonagar have fished the northern inlets and grazed the moor since the [[lore-pelwarpepl|Pelwar]] came to the island. They traded with the coasters of the western coast before the republic was raised, and with the north-shore coasters since Stormveld's harbors were built. The trade was small and steady, and the Lonagar kept out of the wars of the other two tribes.

In the autumn of 719 the High Jarl, [[being-ulthorvhrnd|Ulthorv Hrindaruk]], and Vrithorv Marvidrok of Vuldstead swore their pact against the coasters, and within the month their ships took a Thornburi coaster off the Nordfells with the salt the Lonagar had bought for the winter. Before the snow the pact's ships put men ashore at the easternmost inlet and burned Sennog's clan's boats on their strand. The shamans named him War-Leader at midwinter.

## The Present Pressure {#present}

The Lonagar have no ships to meet the jarls on the water, and the coasters are not coming. The clans are short of salt to cure their catch, and a moor people without salt eats its stock before spring. Sennog has sent his fighters east along the cliffs to watch for the pact's ships and to kill the crews of any that land, and he has sent word south to the Kelgar, who are at war with the same Jarldom and want the same thing.

The clans of the western inlets, which the jarls' ships have not reached, want no war at all. They have begun to trade with the few coasters that still come round the western cape, and they keep their bargains to themselves.

## The Gathering Place {#gathering-place}

The Lonagar gather on the headland above the largest inlet, inside a ring of stones that looks out on the sea. The shamans keep the midwinter gathering there for Veirael, when the fog is thickest, and the coasters' masters who come at that season are welcome at its fires and know not to go inside the ring.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **Salt.** A Lonagar clan will pay in hides and fish for a cargo of salt landed at its inlet before the spring, and a coaster's master in Thornburi will carry it if someone will sail with him past the Nordfells.
- **The burned strand.** Sennog wants to know which of the pact's ships burned his clan's boats, and he will guide anyone who can tell him through the moor to the Nordfells and back.
- **The western cape.** A clan of the western inlets is trading with a coaster that comes round the cape, and its master is asking questions about the Nordmoor's inlets that have nothing to do with salt.
