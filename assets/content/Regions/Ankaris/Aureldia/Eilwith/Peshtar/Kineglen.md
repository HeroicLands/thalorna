## Overview {#overview}

**Kineglen** is the easternmost glen of [[place-stormwood|the Stormwood]], the one nearest the grain land of [[place-stormplain|the Stormplain]] and the forest-edge steadings of [[place-vuldstead|Vuldstead]]. Its lowland name is the cattle glen. A [[affiliation-kelgar|Kelgar]] clan kept its herds there until the jarls' raid of 718, and a young [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] nest holds it now.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The nest holds the glen, its grazing, and the forest edge below it. It lives in a cave in the glen side behind the deserted byres, and uses the byres as its outer chambers. It is the first ground the nest has ever held, and it guards the byres and the grazing as the whole of what it has.

## Layers {#history}

The glen was Kelgar grazing from the time the [[lore-pelwarpepl|Pelwar]] came to the island. In the summer of 718 the jarls of Vuldstead and [[place-drasknes|Drasknes]] raided it under their pact, took the herds and thirty of the clan's people, and burned the shielings. The clan drove what was left of its herds west into the deeper forest, and the glen stood empty.

In 719 a faction broke from the nest at Hazelbrake, on a ridge deeper in the Stormwood, with a reserve spawner stolen from the chamber door, and ran east until the reserve woke fertile in the empty glen. The new nest dug in behind the byres. It is the youngest on the island.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The nest is short of Hushk and short of food, and a Hushk clutch costs more provision than any other laying. Its raiders take cattle from Vuldstead's forest edge, which has stood to arms since the Kelgar burned the High Jarl's steadings and watches the trees for the Kelgar. The district counts every loss as a Kelgar raid, and the jarl's men answer with raids into the Stormwood that fall on the Kelgar.

The clan whose glen it was wants it back, and its war-chief has asked the tribe's War-Leader, [[being-bronnakh|Bronnakh]], for spears to take it. Bronnakh needs every spear against Stormveld.

## Dangerous Neighbours {#dangerous-neighbours}

Vuldstead's forest-edge watch, which shoots at anything in the trees. The Kelgar clan that wants its glen back. The nest at Hazelbrake, whose spawner cannot follow its stolen reserve and does not forget it either.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A long grassy glen with the burned rings of shielings at its mouth and a row of stone byres under the glen side, roofless, with their doorways walled up with turf. The nest's stench fills the glen on a still day. Behind the last byre a cave runs back into the hill, and the spawn-chamber is at its end, warm and close, with the reserve that woke there lying in it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The glen.** The Kelgar clan pays in cattle any party that clears the glen, and promises its byres to nobody else.
- **The watch.** A hersvald of Vuldstead's forest edge suspects the losses are not Kelgar work, and would like proof before the jarl burns another Kelgar glen.
