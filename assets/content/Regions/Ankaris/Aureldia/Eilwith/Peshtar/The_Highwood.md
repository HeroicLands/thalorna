**The Highwood** is the southern highland of [[place-pshtrwldrns|the Peshtar Wilderness]]: granite ridges under pine, cold rivers in steep glens, and high moor above the trees where the deer and the clans' sheep summer. Its southern edge comes down in wooded ridges to the burned ground north of [[place-kaermouth|Kaermouth]], where the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]] keeps its frontier, and its south-eastern fringe holds the dead pines of [[place-blackkilnpriory|Blackkiln Priory]].

It is the country of [[affiliation-bradhar|the Bradhar]], about twenty-four thousand people in some forty clans, and the largest of the three Peshtar countries. Their stone circle stands on a bare shoulder of moor, and from it the sea and the legion's camp can both be seen on a clear day.

## Current Situation {#present}

The [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] nest at Mossglen, in a granite glen under the high moor, takes the hill clans' sheep while their fighters face the legion, and the nest at Pinehollow in the northern pines kills herdsmen on the high pastures.
