**The Peshtar** (_PESH-tar_) are the clans of [[place-pshtrwldrns|the Peshtar Wilderness]], the forest, moor and cliff country of [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]]'s north-west. Some sixty-three thousand of them live there in about a hundred clans, and the clans fall into **three tribes**. A tribe is the clans that keep one gathering at one stone circle, share one name and follow one war-leader when a war is on them. The three tribes share a speech, the gods and the old stones, and they make war on one another as readily as on anyone else.

## The Three Tribes

[[affiliation-kelgar|The Kelgar]] (_KEL-gar_)
: about twenty-two thousand, the cattle clans of [[place-stormwood|the Stormwood]] in the east, where the forest meets the grain land of [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Stormveld]]. The jarls raided their pastures in 718, and they burned the High Jarl's steadings in 719.

[[affiliation-bradhar|The Bradhar]] (_BRAH-dhar_)
: about twenty-four thousand, the hunting and herding clans of [[place-highwood|the Highwood]], the pine highlands in the south above [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Ondareth]]'s frontier. They burned the watch-burgs north of [[place-kaermouth|Kaermouth]] in the autumn of 719.

[[affiliation-lonagar|The Lonagar]] (_loh-NAH-gar_)
: about seventeen thousand, the herders and fishers of [[place-nordmoor|the Nordmoor]], the cliff coast and moor of the north and north-west. They trade with the coasters that put in at their inlets, and Stormveld's ships now close the inlets.

## Clan and Tribe

A clan is a few hundred people of one descent with its own valleys, herds and summer pastures. It is ruled by its **war-chief** and its **shaman**, as the wilderness note sets out, and it owes its tribe nothing but attendance at the gathering and a share in the tribe's wars.

A tribe has no ruler. When a war comes on it, the war-chiefs of its clans meet at the tribe's stone circle, and the shamans name a **war-leader** from among them, a war-chief the other clans will follow for that war. The clans follow her or him while the war goes well and leave when it does not. A tribe at peace has no war-leader at all. All three tribes have one now.

Between tribes there is no law but the gathering of shamans at the great seasonal turns. A clan that crosses into another tribe's valleys pays in cattle or in blood. The Kelgar and the Bradhar have fought over the high pastures between them for as long as either has kept a war-song, and the Lonagar trade with both and trust neither.

## The Gods

The shamans of all three tribes call on three gods by name, beneath and above the spirits of the rivers, groves and stones.

[Kalmara]{.name data-type="lore/deity"}
: the mother of herds and the green year, who brings the calves and the grass. The cattle clans of the Kelgar hold her first. Aureldian clergy who meet her rites take her for [[lore-lizelasdty|Lizelas]].

[Veirael]{.name data-type="lore/deity"}
: the walker in the mist, who keeps the ways between the living country and the others and leads the dead along them. The Lonagar, whose coast is fog most of the year, hold him first.

[Vharoth]{.name data-type="lore/deity"}
: the hunter of the high moor and lord of the winter kill, who takes what the year cannot feed. The Bradhar hold him first, and their war-leaders swear by him.

The three are sung together at every gathering, and a shaman of any tribe may call on any of them. The tribes do not agree on which comes first, and the gathering of shamans has never settled it.

## The Peshtar Tongue and Its Names

The Peshtar speak a language of their own, with three ways of speaking it, one to a tribe, and they write none of it. Its words and names are their own, and no [[skill-aelwthlng|Eilwìthi]] element builds them. Lowlanders write them in the island's roman letters as the clans say them.

The tongue's sound is a cattle people's speech of the western sea. Most words have one or two syllables, closed on a single consonant, and few run past three. The vowels _a_ and _o_ lead, _e_ follows, and _i_ and _u_ are rare; the glides _ai_ and _ou_ are common. The consonants it favors are _d_, _r_, _n_, _l_, _g_, _k_, _b_, _s_ and _sh_, with the soft _dh_ and _gh_ inside a word and _kh_ at its end. A word may open on _br_, _dr_, _gr_, _kr_, _sk_ or _sl_ and on no other pair of consonants.

- **Folk and tribe names** end in _-ar_: Peshtar, Kelgar, Bradhar, Lonagar.
- **Men's names** end in _-akh_, _-og_ or _-en_: Bronnakh, Sennog.
- **Women's names** end in _-a_, _-et_ or _-ain_: Taskain.
- **A Peshtar carries one name**, and among strangers is known by the clan.

The tongue leaves out _w_, _z_, _j_, _q_, _ch_ and _ts_, the catch and every mark, and _th_ comes only in the old names of the gods. It avoids the endings of Eilwìthi names, _-wald_, _-rik_, _-mund_, _-ith_ and _-rith_, and the Seilvri endings _-ir_, _-an_ and _-ri_.
