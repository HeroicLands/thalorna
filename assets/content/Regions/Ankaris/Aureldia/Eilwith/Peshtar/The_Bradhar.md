## Overview {#overview}

**The Bradhar** (_BRAH-dhar_) are the largest of [[lore-peshtarclans|the Peshtar's three tribes]], about twenty-four thousand people in some forty clans in [[place-highwood|the Highwood]], the pine highlands of the southern [[place-pshtrwldrns|Peshtar Wilderness]]. They hunt deer and boar in the pines, run sheep and goats on the high moor, and keep a few cattle in the sheltered glens. Their shamans hold [Vharoth]{.name data-type="lore/deity"}, the hunter of the high moor, first of the gods, and their war-leaders swear by him.

Their country comes down to the frontier of the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]] at [[place-kaermouth|Kaermouth]], and the Bradhar have raided across it for as long as the republic has stood.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

Each clan is ruled by its **war-chief** and its **shaman**. When a war comes, the shamans meet at the tribe's stone circle and name a **War-Leader** from among the war-chiefs, and the clans that agree follow. A clan that does not agree goes home, and the Bradhar have rarely gone to war with every clan behind them.

The War-Leader is [[being-taskain|Taskain]], war-chief of a hill clan of the high moor, named at the autumn gathering of 719 after she burned the legion's watch-burgs. The clans of the river glens nearest Kaermouth, which trade at the forest edge, have not sent their fighters to her.

## Range, Wells and Grounds {#range}

The Highwood is granite and pine, cut by cold rivers in steep glens, with open moor above the trees where the sheep summer and the deer go in the heat. Its southern edge falls in wooded ridges to the burned ground north of Kaermouth. Its south-eastern fringe holds the dead pines of [[place-blackkilnpriory|Blackkiln Priory]], which no Bradhar enters. North and east of it lie the high pastures it shares, by force, with [[affiliation-kelgar|the Kelgar]].

## Relations {#relations}

- **[[affiliation-repblcndrth|The Republic of Ondareth]].** The old enemy. The republic's legion has held the frontier from its camp at Kaermouth for some two hundred and fifty years, and its patrols, gallows-posts and burned ground are what the Bradhar know of it. The merchant houses' factors at Kaermouth trade salt and iron for Bradhar cattle with the river clans under old terms, and the legion watches them.
- **[[affiliation-kelgar|The Kelgar]].** Rivals for the high pastures. Since the Stormveld raid drove Kelgar clans west, Kelgar herds graze ground the Bradhar call theirs, and Bradhar spears have killed Kelgar herdsmen over it.
- **[[affiliation-lonagar|The Lonagar]].** Kin by many marriages, and trading partners for salt and fish.
- **[[lore-grukaraelwyth|The Grukar]].** Fought without pause. The nest at Mossglen raids the hill clans' flocks on the high moor, and the nest at Pinehollow kills herdsmen on the high pastures.
- **[[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]].** Two generations ago a cell of the Black Flame raised a priory in the Highwood's eastern pines with Ondarethan money, to burn out Bradhar raiders. The Bradhar clans of the border razed it that winter and threw its Flame-Warden into his own kiln, and they hold that its fire should be left burning.

## History {#history}

The Bradhar have held the Highwood since the [[lore-pelwarpepl|Pelwar]] came to the island, and they raided the western coast under its kings as they raid it under its Senate. When the republic raised its standing legion at Kaermouth, a generation after the [[lore-ondarethfall|Fall of the Last King]], the raids became a frontier: the legion burned the ground north of the river into a two-mile strip of ash and stumps, and the clans learned to cross it at night.

In the summer of 719 the legion's patrols burned the forest edge a further mile north, across the lower end of a glen where the river clans winter their cattle. In the autumn Taskain's clan and two others crossed the ash, took two watch-burgs at night and killed their crews, and burned them. The shamans named her War-Leader at the autumn gathering.

## The Present Pressure {#present}

The legion is coming. [[being-godstanhrdl|Godstan Hardley]], the Vandraks at Kaermouth, has sent patrols into the trees all winter and wants another legion to carry the war into the Highwood in the spring. The Senate has voted him the levies and not the money, and the highland recruits coming north are going hungry in his camp. [[being-alfmundkrtn|Alfmund Kaerton]], who holds the spring field command, wants to know whether the burnings can be paid for rather than fought for.

Taskain means to meet the legion in the pines, where it cannot form, and she needs the river clans to do it. The river clans live by the Kaermouth trade and have no wish to lose it, and their shamans have not agreed to follow her. Meanwhile the Kelgar push into the high pastures behind her, and the hill clans want to know why their War-Leader faces south while the Kelgar take their summer grazing.

## The Gathering Place {#gathering-place}

The Bradhar gather on a bare shoulder of moor above the pines, at a ring of stones from which the sea and the legion's camp at Kaermouth can both be seen on a clear day. The shamans keep the autumn gathering there at the turn of the hunting year, and they name a War-Leader there when the clans need one.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The river clans.** A shaman of the river clans wants a party to carry a question to Kaermouth for her: whether the legion would leave the river glens alone if the river clans stayed out of Taskain's war.
- **The burned glen.** A Bradhar clan offers guidance through the Highwood to anyone who will bring back the bones of a war-chief killed in the legion's burning, which lie in the ash north of the river.
- **The pines.** Godstan Hardley pays well for guides who know the Highwood's glens, and the clans have a word for anyone who takes his money.
