## Overview {#overview}

The **Enclave of Seirasto** is the community of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] of
[[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]], about four thousand of them, who reside in [[place-hallowwood|Seirasto]]. It is the largest Senali community any human scholar knows of, and one of the most
closed. Senali have kings, queens and lords, but these do not work as human ones do, and this enclave
has raised no king since its last one was killed at [[place-threesisters|the Three Sisters]] in
984 BF. It does not answer to [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Dúnavarre]], which sends embassies that are
received and answered briefly.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

Every Senalo of Seirasto belongs to a hearth, and the hearths are scattered through the wood in
clearings and in dwellings grown into living trees. When there is something to decide, the hearths
gather at _lonkwolmarna_, the heart of the deep wood, and nothing is decided there until every voice
has been heard.

**The Keeper of the Wood.** The head of the enclave is the **Lonkunno** (_lon-KOON-no_), the one
who keeps the deep wood, raised by every voice of the hearths. The Keeper holds the wood and nothing
beyond it: no crown, no claim on the island, no say over any realm. The Keeper drives out the
outlaw, decides which embassies the wood receives, and gives the answers they carry away. The office
has stood since the withdrawal, and the present Keeper is
[[being-oltessalasslnt|Oltessala Esselinto]].

**The lords.** A Senali lord is raised for things done, by every voice, exactly as the Keeper is,
and holds nothing by it: no land, no hearth but the one that keeps them, no office. Lordship is standing
and respect. The hearths raise a lord once for a deed, and such a lord is _tevannisa_ (_teh-van-NEE-sa_),
raised for a deed. A lord whose deeds are many enough that the hearths make a praise-song of them is
_tellamesa_ (_tel-la-MEH-sa_), praise-sung, which is the highest standing the wood gives. A
praise-song praises the deed and withholds the doer, so the deeds of the wood's lords are sung in the
wood and their names are not.

**The edge.** The Keeper names one of the lords **Issaltenno** (_is-sal-TEN-no_), the one of the edge,
who meets the wood's guests. The present Issaltenno is
[[being-kelkasivotlknt|Kelkasivo Tellakinto]]. One the Keeper drives out is _vyerka_, an outlaw, given
no standing or shelter.

## Current Situation {#present}

The enclave is turned north and inward. Word came down from [[place-haulonna|Haulonna]] this
leaf-fall with [[being-lovattahssnt|Lovatta Hìssente]], who went up alone in the spring, and the
hearths have sung [[lore-lamenthaulonna|the Lament of Haulonna]] over it: the valley stands as it
stood, and its dead are not gathered. Among the five the lament calls is one of the wood's own hearth
of Ilevanto.

An envoy of the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]] has waited at the eastern edge since midsummer with
the Synod's old question about the Convoy of Six, and the Issaltenno has not fetched her in. The
wardens of the northern edge watch the raiders' country toward [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]] and the
Crown Road down from [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]]. The Keeper has said nothing about either, and
the hearths have not been called to the heart of the wood since the lament.

The war with the Grukar goes on at every edge, as it does every year. The wardens have hunted bands
off the northern and eastern edges since the thaw, and along the Dunmere road they fight in woods
that is the Grukar's own.

## Arms {#arms}

Walls and armies do not protect Seirasto against travelers. The wood does: a path that leads
into it uninvited leads somewhere else. The enclave keeps **wardens** (_ortinno_) along its edges, who
see a party coming long before it sees them and turn it, politely and without conversation.

The paths do not turn the Grukar, and against them the wood fights. The Senali are in perpetual war
with [[lore-grukaraelwyth|the Grukar of Eilwìth]], as the Khazari are, and the wardens are its hunters:
bowmen who meet the bands at the edges, track them back through the forest and the hills, and carry
the war to the nests. The Senali of Seirasto have not stood on an open field beside any other
people since the Three Sisters, and they have not stopped fighting.

## Faith {#faith}

The hearths keep the rite of [[lore-goddreams|the God of Dreams]], whom the Senali call Aulveira, in
a form once held in common with the Khazari ([[lore-oldcompact|the Old Compact]]). The two
traditions have diverged so far that neither people would recognize the other's. Every lament the
hearths sing opens on the god and closes on the line that says the dead are gathered, and the Lament
of Haulonna leaves that line unsung. The rite is in the keeping of the seer
[[being-unturovokilpnt|Unturovo Kilpente]].

## History {#history}

The Senali of the southern forest have kept hearths on Eilwìth since before [[lore-khazarturn|the fall of Khazartúrn]] in 2427 BF. With the Khazari of the Ironfells they accepted the trust of
[[miscgear-secondvoice|the Second Voice]] in 1919 BF, which never arrived
([[lore-thecrossing|the Crossing]]), and about 1585 BF they raised with them one crown over the whole
island, held by a Senali king ([[lore-jointcrown|the Joint Crown]]).

In 984 BF the king was killed at the Three Sisters, in the decisive battle of the
[Great War]{.name data-type="lore/history"} ([[lore-threesistrs|the Battle of the Three Sisters]]).
The Senali gave up the crown and every claim outside Seirasto, came into the wood, and left the
Khazari to finish the war. The hearths raised the first Keeper of the Wood that year, and they have
raised no king since. The Khazari have not forgiven the withdrawal, and the two peoples have had
nothing to do with one another since.

## Relations {#relations}

- **[[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Dúnavarre]]** is tolerated. Its embassies are fetched in from the eastern
  edge, heard and answered briefly.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Élavendre]]** is welcome, for the Senali's relationship with that kingdom.
  Its embassies come by sea to the southern shore.
- **The [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]]** are received above all, and are given songs.
- **The [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]]** of [[place-vorgald|Vorgald]] and **[[lore-vardain|the Vardain]]**,
  who are the Khazari's people, are not received at all.
- **The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Arcane Order]]** is turned at the edge like anyone else, and has
  learned almost nothing here.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The envoy at the edge.** The Synod's envoy offers a party a great deal to carry a letter in to the
  Issaltenno, who has not fetched her.
- **Across the water.** An Áelendan embassy has come ashore on the southern shore and waits there to
  be met, and its elders ask a party to keep the coast road's curious, and the Synod's watchers, away
  from the beach.
