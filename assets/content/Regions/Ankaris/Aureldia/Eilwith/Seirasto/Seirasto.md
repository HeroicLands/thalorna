## Overview {#overview}

**Seirasto**, "the hallowed place", which the Eilwìthans call the Hallowwood, covers the middle of [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]]'s south coast, between the land of
[[place-ondareth|Ondareth]] to the west and the lowlands of [[place-dunavarre|Dúnavarre]] to the
east, with the Thornwold's hills to the north: old, deep, unbroken forest, part of the third of the island that no realm has ever plowed.

It is where the **[[lore-flksenali|Senali]] of Eilwìth reside**, about four thousand of them, the
largest gathering of the Senali known to any human scholar. It is also the reason the island's
reputation abroad is not entirely nonsense. A party can walk to its edge from any realm on the
island in a few days. Going in is another matter, and most who try come out again somewhere they
did not intend.

## The Land {#the-land}

The wood is oak and beech on the low ground and on the long ridges that run down toward the south
coast, with holly beneath, and yew in the dark of the valleys. It is old in the way few forests are
anywhere: trees that have fallen are left where they fall, the next generation stands up out of the
last, and in the deepest valleys the floor is a soft, moss-covered ruin of trees older than any human
kingdom. Streams run south out of it to the sea, clear and cold, and no mill stands on any of them.

There are no roads in it. There are paths, and they belong to the wood. A path the Senali walk is
plain and dry and goes where it is meant to go. A path an uninvited traveler follows is just as plain,
turns gently, and brings him out at the edge he came in by, or another edge, or the same edge a day
later than he can account for. Nobody has been harmed by it, and nobody has got past it.

Deer, boar, badger and fox live here as they do in any great wood, in greater numbers than the
hunted woods of the realms keep. The fae presence is stronger here than anywhere else on the island:
the light under the canopy changes in ways the hour does not explain, the distances between two
trees are not always the same, and the creatures that humans call fae are seen at the edges more
often than anywhere else on Eilwìth. The Senali live among them as neighbors.

### The Edges

- **The north.** Seirasto's northern edge faces open country toward
  [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]], and the forest people who hold that country are warlike. They raid
  the farms beyond them, and they do not come under Seirasto's trees or deal with those who
  live there.
- **The north-east.** A [Crown Road]{.name data-type="place/feature"} runs south from
  [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]], at the lake's south-eastern tip, through the Thornwold's oak woods, held by [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]], past
  the lit house of [[place-wenlornestage|Wenlorne Stage]], to Seirasto's edge. Travelers who
  reach the edge by it are turned, politely and without conversation, and sent back the way they came.
- **The east.** The forest of [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]] comes up to the trees, with Dúnavarre's lowlands beyond it. This is the edge the
  [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]]'s embassies come to, and they wait there to be fetched.
- **The west.** One stretch of Crown Road runs straight from the highland grazing to the forest and
  stops at a bank of oaks, with no gate, no marker and no road beyond.
- **The south.** The wood comes down its ridges toward the sea, and Dúnavarre's coast road runs west
  between the last trees and the shore toward [[place-veldareth|Veldareth]]. Travelers on that road keep
  to it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The wood is held by the [[affiliation-nclvhllwwd|Enclave of Seirasto]], the Senali of Eilwìth,
who live in hearths scattered through it and gather at the heart of the wood when they have something
to decide. They are governed by the **Lonkunno**, the Keeper of the Wood, a lord raised by every voice
of the hearths. The present Keeper is [[being-oltessalasslnt|Oltessala Esselinto]]. No realm on the
island claims the forest, and none governs anything inside it.

## Not Welcome

The Senali here do not receive outsiders. There is no arrangement of the kind
[[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] enjoys, no tradition of contact for Aldorathi or Ondarethan to appeal
to, and no human who lives in the wood. People who go into Seirasto uninvited find the paths
unhelpful.

Three exceptions are recognized, and they are exceptions of person rather than of policy:

- [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Dúnavarre]] is _tolerated_. Its embassies come to the eastern edge, are
  fetched in, heard, and answered briefly, by the lord who meets the wood's guests,
  [[being-kelkasivotlknt|Kelkasivo Tellakinto]]. The reception is not warm, and the
  [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]] is under no illusion about the difference between being tolerated
  and being welcome.
- [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Élavendre]] is genuinely welcome, on the strength of a relationship the
  Senali hold with that kingdom and not with this island. Its embassies come by sea and are met on the
  southern shore.
- The [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] most of all. An Áelendan embassy from across the water is
  received as no human party on Eilwìth has ever been received, which every Eilwìthan realm knows and
  none has been able to turn to any use. The Áelendan are given songs: [[lore-lamenthaulonna|the Lament of Haulonna]] reached their Tradition Keepers as a gift to one such embassy.

The one people the wood has never received at all is [[lore-vardain|the Vardain]], who are the
Khazari's people.

## The Arcane Order Problem

This is the flaw in the Synod's arrangement. Eilwìth is [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]'s
unconstrained research ground precisely because its fae country has no
[[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]], and the single greatest concentration of fae presence on the
island is the one place that does have a keeper. That keeper does not answer to Dúnavarre, and it
tolerates the Synod's envoys the way one tolerates weather.

The Arcane Order has learned a great deal on Eilwìth. It has learned almost nothing here.

## Current Situation {#present}

Word came down from the north this leaf-fall. [[being-lovattahssnt|Lovatta Hìssente]] went up alone
to [[place-haulonna|Haulonna]] in the spring and came back to say that the valley stands as it stood,
and the hearths have sung the Lament of Haulonna again, as they sing it every time word comes and it
is not the word.

At the eastern edge an envoy of the Synod has waited since midsummer to be fetched in, with a question
the Synod has asked before about the Convoy of Six, and Kelkasivo Tellakinto has not sent for her.
On the northern edge the wardens watch the raiders' country and the Crown Road from Dunmere,
where the lamps of Wenlorne are lit every dusk and the king's foresters at Dunmere have
stopped sending patrols. [[being-selkitteilvnt|Selkitte Ilevanto]], who keeps the northern watch,
turns back the carters who come down the road to see the lit house and reach the trees instead.

The wood is at war, as it always is. The Senali are in perpetual war with
[[lore-grukaraelwyth|the Grukar of Eilwìth]], as the Khazari are, and the nests in the forest and
mountain country of the island send their bands against every edge of the wood. The wardens hunt them
at the edges and beyond them, along the Dunmere road above all, where the forest is the Grukar's.
No band has reached the heart of the wood, and the wardens do not let a summer pass without carrying
the war to the nearest nest.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **The paths.** An uninvited traveler is turned, not hurt, but the wood does not hurry, and a party
  that pushes on may lose days it cannot account for and come out short of food on the wrong side of
  the island.
- **The Grukar.** The Senali's war with the [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] never stops, and a party
  near the edges is likely to meet a Grukar band, or the wardens hunting one. The oak woods along the
  Dunmere road are held by Grukar, and a party that reaches the trees by that road has fought its way
  there.
- **The edges.** The country beyond the northern edge is raided and raiding. The danger of
  Seirasto is mostly the danger of reaching it.
- **The fae.** The creatures of the wood are not the Senali's servants, and they keep their own
  manners. A traveler who takes from the wood without asking may meet them before he meets a Senalo.

## History {#history}

The Senali have lived in the southern forest of Eilwìth for as long as there are records, and before
the war they lived far beyond it as well.

After [[lore-khazarturn|Khazartúrn fell]] in 2427 BF and the two elder races stopped speaking, the
Senali and [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] **of this island** did something no others of their kind have
ever attempted: they tried to repair it. In 1919 BF the [[miscgear-secondvoice|Second Voice]] was to
be put into their joint keeping here, and it never arrived ([[lore-thecrossing|the Crossing]]). About
1585 BF they tried again with something larger, and united Eilwìth under a **single crown**, held by
a Senali king, and governed the island together for **six hundred years**
([[lore-jointcrown|the Joint Crown]]). The Senali of the southern forest gave the crown its kings and
planted the avenues along its roads, and their hearths stood across the island.

Then, around **1200 BF**, the **[[lore-pelwarpepl|Pelwar]]** arrived: refugees, driven off the
mainland by the [Varkhad]{.name data-type="lore/folk"} in the great displacement
([[lore-varkhadcnq|The Varkhad Conquest]]), with nowhere else to go. Two centuries of pressure and
reprisal became the [Great War]{.name data-type="lore/history"}, and in **984 BF** the king was killed
by the Varkhad among them at [[place-threesisters|the Three Sisters]]
([[lore-threesistrs|the Battle of the Three Sisters]]).

The surviving Senali gave up the crown, the settled country, the coasts and every claim outside this
forest, came into it and stayed, and left the Khazari to finish the war without them. The Khazari
finished it within a few seasons ([[lore-endgreatwar|the Ending of the Great War]]). The paths of the
wood have turned outsiders aside since that year, and the hearths have raised no king since. They
raise a Keeper of the Wood instead, and the Keeper holds the wood and nothing beyond it.

That is the second time the Senali have walked away from the Khazari in a crisis, and the Khazari have
never forgiven either. The two elder races of Eilwìth have had nothing to do with one another since,
and the Senali of Seirasto will not discuss it: not with the Synod, not with Élavendre, and not
with the Vardain. What the withdrawal was—rout, grief, judgment, or something for which no human word
has been offered—is not known. That it ended six centuries of deliberate reconciliation, and that
nothing has been attempted since, is not in doubt.

### The Trust

Before the crown there was an agreement, and it is the only one the two elder races have made since
the sundering. The Second Voice—the relic a Senali outlaw used to destroy Khazartúrn—was to be **held
in trust on Eilwìth by both races together**, because this island was the one place they still shared
a country and neither would be trusted to hold it alone. A convoy of six ships sailed from Élavendre
carrying it, crewed by people chosen for being beyond reproach.

It never arrived, and nothing of it was ever found.

The Senali of Seirasto do not discuss this and never have. Whether they know more than the Khazari
do is precisely the question, and it is one of the reasons an embassy from Dúnavarre is _tolerated_
here and nothing warmer.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **Fetched in.** The Synod's envoy at the eastern edge has run short of patience and of food, and
  hires a party to see her through the wood unfetched. The paths will turn them; what turns them
  first may be Selkitte Ilevanto.
- **Word from the north.** A traveler from Nordheim reaches Veldareth with news of
  [[place-haulonna|Haulonna]] and asks to be taken to Seirasto's southern shore, where the Senali
  meet those who come by sea.
- **The Wenlorne ledgers.** A party that carries the last ledger of [[place-wenlornestage|Wenlorne Stage]] to the wood's edge hopes to trade it for a hearing.

:::secret {title="The ledger at the wood's edge"}
The ledger's last entry records the Senali envoy who came for the king's body and was turned away at the gate. It is the one thing the wood least wants to see written down.
:::
