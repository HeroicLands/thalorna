## Overview {#overview}

Ondareth is the land of the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]], the west and south-west of [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]]: the fog-bound western coast and the broad farmland and upland behind it, running east to the edge of [[place-cntrlhghlnds|the Central Highlands]]. All the republic's hundred and fifty thousand people live on it.

The coast is the republic's money and its government. The Senate sits at [[place-thornbury|Thornbury]], the harbors carry the trade, and the legion holds the northern end against the forest.

## The Land {#the-land}

The coast runs from the [[place-pshtrwldrns|Peshtar Wilderness]], which comes down to the sea in wooded ridges at its northern end, south and then south-east along the island's south-western shore toward [[place-veldareth|Veldareth]]. Behind it lies a strip of low farmland a day's walk deep, cut by short rivers, with the land rising behind it in long green slopes to a broad wooded upland that runs east for days to the republic's edge by the Highlands.

This is the worst weather on Eilwìth. The wind comes off the open ocean unbroken, fog lies on the shore for days together, and the fields are wet the year round. Oats, barley, cattle and fish feed the coast, and the farms are moderate at best. A traveler who climbs inland for an hour comes up out of the fog into the upland's sun and looks back on a white sea covering the coast. The upland is the republic's horse and cattle country, with the horse fair at [[place-stonyford|Stonyford]] at its eastern edge, and the senators keep their summer houses there.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The republic holds the whole land directly, and no lord stands between its farms and the Senate. The land belongs to citizens, the great estates to senators and the _gulmenoi_, the propertied order, and the harbors to the shipping houses whose masters raised the republic. A district's levy musters for the legion, and the Senate's magistrates judge its suits at Thornbury.

## Current Situation {#present}

- **The frontier.** Tribes of the Peshtar forest burned two of the watch-burgs north of [[place-kaermouth|Kaermouth]] in the autumn, and the legion's commander, [[being-godstanhrdl|Godstan Hardli]], wants another legion raised and sent north for the spring. The Senate has voted him the men and not the money.
- **The northern water.** Stormveld's jarls took an Ondarethan coaster off the Nordfells in the autumn, cargo and crew, and the shipping houses of Thornbury want the fleet sent north ([[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]]).
- **The call.** Every Ondarethan hull bound for the mainland still calls first at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]], and the shipping houses still complain of it to the Senate, which still sends envoys to [[place-veldareth|Veldareth]] about it.

## Roads and Trade {#roads-and-trade}

The coast road runs the length of the shore from Kaermouth through Thornbury to the south coast, where it meets the road west from [[place-dunavarre|Dúnavarre]] at Veldareth. Short roads climb from each river mouth to the upland, and the busiest of them goes up from Thornbury across the republic to Stonyford and on along a stretch of the old Crown Road toward the lake.

The coast lives by carrying. Ondarethan coasters work the island's shores from the Nordfells to Wraimouth, and the larger hulls cross to the mainland by way of Ravenmoor. Fish, hides, highland horses and wool go out; iron, wine, cloth and coin come in. A few merchant houses hold old terms with Peshtar clans and trade salt and metal for cattle at the forest edge.

## Dangers {#dangers}

The fog is the coast's first danger. Ships miss the harbor mouths in it, and travelers on the coast road lose the road where it crosses the river flats. North of Kaermouth the danger is the forest: the burned country between the last watch-burg and the trees belongs to nobody, and raiding parties cross it in both directions.
