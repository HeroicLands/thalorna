## Overview {#overview}

**Kaermouth** stands at the mouth of the last river on [[place-ondareth|Ondareth]]'s coast before the [[place-pshtrwldrns|Peshtar Wilderness]]. Its name is the fort at the river mouth. The [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]] keeps its frontier legion in a standing camp on the bluff above the harbor, and the town of about three and a half thousand lives on the camp, the fishing and the frontier.

## Arriving {#arriving}

The coast road comes in from the south past ditched fields and a line of gallows-posts, and ends at the camp gate. By sea a ship comes in over a sand bar that shifts with every winter storm, and a local pilot is the custom and the sense. North of the river the land is burned and empty for two miles to the first trees, with watch-burgs on the rises and the forest beyond.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The legion buys everything the town makes: bread, beer, boots, rope, washing and the work of its smiths. The harbor lands fish for the camp and for Thornburi, and the Senate's ships put in to take on water and to carry the legion's sick south. A few merchant houses keep factors here who meet Peshtar clans at the forest edge under old terms and trade salt and iron for cattle, and the legion keeps an eye on them.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The commander of the legion, the Vandraks, rules Kaermouth in all but name. The town has no magistrate of its own, and the camp's provosts keep its order. [[being-godstanhrdl|Godstan Hardli]] holds the command, and his word is the town's law.

## The Present {#present}

The tribes of the forest burned two watch-burgs north of the river in the autumn and killed their crews. Godstan Hardli has answered with patrols into the trees and wants another legion sent north for the spring. The Senate has voted him the levy and not the pay, and highland recruits are coming down to a camp that has no money to feed them.

One of the two tavoi of the year, [[being-alfmundkrtn|Alfmund Kaerton]], has come north to take the field command for the spring, and he and Godstan disagree about what the patrols are for.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

**The Bar**, on the harbor, lodges ships' crews and anyone else with coin, and the legion's provosts drink there. Merchants waiting for a meeting at the forest edge take rooms over the factors' warehouses.

## History {#history}

The republic raised the camp a generation after the rising at Thornburi, when the levies that had served Zalmen could no longer hold the frontier as seasonal musters and the Senate voted a legion that would not go home ([[lore-ondarethfall|the Fall of the Last King of Ondareth]]). The town grew up around the camp.
