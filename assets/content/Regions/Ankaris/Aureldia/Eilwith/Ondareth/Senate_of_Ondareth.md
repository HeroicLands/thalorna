## Overview {#overview}

The **Senate of Ondareth** is the governing council of the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]. It sits in the Senate house at [[place-thornbury|Thornburi]] under the portico, its members wear the bordered mantle, and it governs a hundred and fifty thousand people with the ceremony of an empire. It votes the republic's laws, its money, its levies and its wars, and every magistrate of the republic is chosen in it or answers to it.

## Who Sits {#who-sits}

Some three hundred senators sit for life, drawn from the _gulmenoi_, the propertied order that meets the census for office. A seat is elective in form and bought in practice, and the theronos, who keeps the roll, may strike a senator from it. A senator who has held the year as tavos is _tavalen_ ever after and speaks before every senator who has not.

About a dozen senators of the first rank decide what the chamber does, and the rest follow one of them. The First Senator presides, and the Clerk keeps the record:

First Senator
: [[being-harwingrymr|Harwin Greimore]], a shipping-house senator of Thornburi, in the last year of his term.

Clerk of the Senate
: [[being-osrithgrydn|Osrith Greiden]], who keeps the roll and the decrees and reads each decree on the steps.

The republic's magistrates sit in the chamber as senators and answer to it for their year: the two _tavoi_ [[being-kuthwldashdn|Kuthwald Ashden]] and [[being-alfmundkrtn|Alfmund Kaerton]], the _vornos_ [[being-mildrikhshwl|Mildrik Hushwell]], the _theronos_ [[being-winiwldbrnl|Winiwald Barnli]] and the _tarkelos_ [[being-leofmndstrmd|Leofmund Stormden]].

## How It Decides {#how-it-decides}

The First Senator calls the chamber and sets its business, and speaks first. The _tavalen_ speak next in order of their years, then the sitting magistrates, then the rest. Every question goes to a vote by division, the senators walking to one side of the chamber or the other, and the Clerk counts them.

The vote is the end of the matter and seldom where it is decided. The men of the first rank settle the business in their houses the night before, and the chamber's debate is the performance of what they settled. A question none of them wants decided is put off, and stays put off.

## What It Is Fighting About {#present}

**The succession to the chair.** Harwin Greimore's term ends this year, and he wants a second term, which the Senate has never given. Kuthwald Ashden wants the chair for himself and has put the question of the Tyrant's Rock above the harbor before the chamber again, because the houses at its foot have been bought in the name of one of Harwin's freedmen.

**The northern water.** Stormveld's jarls have taken a Thornburi coaster off the Nordfells, crew and cargo. The shipping houses want the fleet sent north, the Prefect of the Fleet, [[being-edstanbrnwk|Edstan Brinwik]], wants to take it, and the Tarkelos says the treasury cannot pay for the fleet and the frontier both.

**The frontier.** The Senate has voted the Vandraks at [[place-kaermouth|Kaermouth]], [[being-godstanhrdl|Godstan Hardli]], another legion's worth of levies and no money to feed them. The Lusos, [[being-wilrikbrnwl|Wilrik Brownwell]], speaks against the levy in the assembly, and the citizens of the coast cheer him.

**Veldareth.** The Senate's Ambassador, [[being-aldrunsmrl|Aldrun Somerli]], is instructed to learn whether the Speaker of [[place-veldareth|Veldareth]]'s Table answers to the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Dúnavarre Synod]], and to buy his letters if she cannot.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

A party with business before the Senate needs a senator to put it there, and a senator's help is bought. The Clerk takes petitions on the Senate house steps each morning, and a petition that comes with a senator's name is read; the rest are filed. A party that has something a man of the first rank wants is asked to dinner instead.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The houses below the rock.** Kuthwald Ashden wants proof that the freedman who bought the houses at the foot of the Tyrant's Rock bought them with Harwin Greimore's money, before the Senate debates the rock.
- **A captured crew.** The shipping house that owned the coaster taken off the Nordfells wants its crew bought back from Stormveld before the Senate decides anything.
- **The levy money.** Godstan Hardli wants the frontier legion paid, and he will owe a party that finds the money the Senate did not vote.
