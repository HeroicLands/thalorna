## Overview {#overview}

**Stoniford** is the market town of [[place-ondareth|Ondareth]]'s eastern upland, at the edge of [[place-cntrlhghlnds|the Central Highlands]], three days' ride up from [[place-thornbury|Thornburi]] and a day short of [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]]. About two thousand eight hundred people live in it. It stands where the road from the coast crosses a fast highland river at a ford paved with fitted stone, and it is where [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]] buys its riding horses.

## Arriving {#arriving}

The road climbs out of the coastal fog an hour above the coast, crosses the republic's farmland and upland, and comes to Stoniford across open grazing in sunlight. The last mile into the town runs on a stretch of the old Crown Road of the Joint Crown, laid in blocks no later mason on the island can match, and the ford that gives the town its name is the same stone carried under the water. The town lies along the east bank: one long street of inns, forges and saddlers, the horse-market on the common behind it, and the stone pens where the herds are held for the fair.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Horses. The breeders of the republic's upland and the free steadings of the Highlands bring their young stock to the spring and autumn fairs, and buyers come from every realm on the island, Aldorathi knights and Dúnavarre's Order among them. Between the fairs the town lives by wool, hides and the custom of the senators who ride up from Thornburi to their summer houses.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

Stoniford has no magistrate of its own. The great breeders of the district are senators, and they keep its peace through their stewards and its fair-court, which sits on the common during the fairs and settles disputes over a sale before the buyer leaves. The Senate's officers come up from Thornburi when the levy is taken.

## The Present {#present}

The recruiting officers of the frontier legion are at the spring fair, taking highland men for the levy the Senate has voted [[place-kaermouth|Kaermouth]]. The breeders want their herdsmen and grooms left to them. The district's greatest breeder, [[being-alfmundkrtn|Alfmund Kaerton]], is one of the year's two _tavoi_, and he is taking the levy north himself, so the breeders cannot appeal to him against it.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

**The Ford**, at the foot of the street, keeps a long room for buyers in fair-time and a quiet one the rest of the year. Drovers sleep in the lofts over the pens.
