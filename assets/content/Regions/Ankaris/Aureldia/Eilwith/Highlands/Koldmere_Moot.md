## Overview {#overview}

The moot is the whole government of [[place-koldmere|Koldmere]], the free village on the north-western shore of [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]]. It has no lord over it, and no realm holds the village.

## Who Sits {#who-sits}

The village governs itself in moot.

Reed-Reeve
: Chosen by the moot each spring. Sets the cutting and keeps the boats, and speaks for the village to anyone who comes asking, and to the Ondarethan senators at [[place-stonyford|Stoniford]], who buy its fish and its reed. [[being-elfithwgdn|Elfith Wigden]] has been chosen four springs running.

## What It Is Fighting About {#present}

The best reed-beds lie east of the village, across the lake's northern end under the Sunderfells, where the hill tenants of [[place-brynwold|Brinwold]] in [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Aldorath]] claim them too. This winter Brinwold's men took two Koldmere boats and the reed in them, and the reed-reeve has asked the Stoniford senators to put the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]'s weight behind the village.
