## Overview {#overview}

**Kingsdelf** is the old quarry on the north face of the northernmost of [[place-threesisters|the Three Sisters]], in the rocky ground above the open grazing of [[place-cntrlhghlnds|the Central Highlands]]. Its name is the king's quarry. [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] masons cut the blocks of the Crown Road from it, and the galleries they left in the hill hold a [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] nest, the strongest of the Highlands.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The nest holds it, around its spawner in the deepest gallery. The breeders of [[place-stonyford|Stoniford]] run their horses on the saddle between the peaks below it, and the nest's raiders take foals from the runs and herders from the folds. They do not go near the senators' summer houses within sight of the peaks, which are guarded and would bring the Senate's men up after them.

## Layers {#history}

Under [[lore-jointcrown|the Joint Crown]] the Khazari cut the north face back in square terraces and drove galleries into the hill to follow the best stone. The blocks went down to the road that runs from the coast through Stoniford toward the lake, on sledges along a ramp that still shows in the turf. When the Senali king fell beneath the peaks in 984 BF and the Khazari went back inside the mountain, the quarry was left as it stood.

The Grukar came into the galleries long before the republic, and the Stoniford breeders' tallies have never run back to a time without them. The galleries are dry, deep and easy to hold, and every nest that has lived there has outlasted its neighbors.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The levy for the frontier is taking the herders north, and fewer of them are on the saddle this spring. The nest's raiders have taken foals off the runs all winter and killed a herder at the spring-head below the north face, and the spawner has laid Hushk to take more once the grass comes. The breeders know the raiding party by its leader, an old Hushk they call [[being-greytusk|Greytusk]], and the fair-court at Stoniford has posted a horse's price for its head for years.

A digger paid by the scholar of the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]] who buys the iron turned up on the saddle went up to the north face in the winter to search the quarry ramp for worked stone, and has not come back.

## Dangerous Neighbours {#dangerous-neighbours}

The nest at Ravenkeld, in the gorges of the Highlands' northern edge, raids the same steadings from the other side, and the two nests fight each other where their raiders meet. The senators' stewards want the peaks quiet and their own houses safe, and they will not pay for a party to stir the quarry up.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

Square-cut terraces climb the north face, each with a gallery mouth, and the ramp runs down from the lowest. Inside, the galleries are tall, dry and straight, cut with the Khazari's flat tool-marks, and the nest has walled them into chambers with stone and turf. The warmest and best-guarded chamber is at the far end of the deepest gallery, where the Khazari cut a cistern that still holds water. Blocks the masons squared and never sent down stand in the side galleries, each with a mason's mark.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The bounty.** The Stoniford fair-court pays a horse's price for Greytusk's head, and the breeders add to it with every foal they lose.
- **The digger.** The Epistémion scholar wants her digger found, alive or dead, and what he found in the quarry with him.
- **The marks.** The marks on the unsent blocks are the same as the marks on the Crown Road's stones, and a scholar at Thornbury would pay to have them copied.
