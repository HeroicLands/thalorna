## Overview {#overview}

**Veldareth** is a free city on the southern coast of [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]], at the south-eastern end of the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]'s shore, where the coast road from the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]] comes west along the edge of [[place-hallowwood|Seirasto]], and belonging to neither. About fifteen hundred people live inside its walls and on the farms of its small valley. It makes its living as the place where its two neighbors meet without either being at home, and it keeps one of the five [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]] chapterhouses on the island, with those at [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]], [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]], [[place-thornbury|Thornbury]] and [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]].

## Arriving {#arriving}

The town sits at the head of a narrow bay, walled on the landward side, with a stone quay that takes coasters and the envoys' ships of both realms. A visitor by sea comes in under the chapterhouse, which stands on the height above the quay with its library tower facing the water. By land, the coast road from Dúnavarre, which runs west between Seirasto's trees and the sea, and the road from Ondareth meet at the town's one gate.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Veldareth lives by hosting. Its inns lodge envoys, its notaries draw up the agreements its guests sign, and its market sells to both realms without the tolls either would charge. Coasters work its quay between Ondareth's ports and [[place-wraymouth|Wraymouth]]. The chapterhouse teaches the children of both realms' gentry, and its students fill a quarter of the town in term.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The town is governed by the **Table**, the council of its burgesses, under the charter of the [[affiliation-freeveldareth|Free City of Veldareth]]. By that charter the Speaker of the Table is the master of the chapterhouse, because a scholar belongs to neither realm. The Speaker is [[being-leofwldchlwl|Leofwald Chilwell]]. The Table hires a small watch and keeps the gate. Envoys of both realms sit at the same table in the chapterhouse hall when they meet, and neither brings soldiers inside the walls.

## The Chapterhouse

The **chapterhouse of Veldareth** is among the most respected on the island. It keeps a library of law and treaty that both realms consult, teaches letters, law, logic and natural philosophy, and hosts sages of every discipline. Its master answers, as every chapterhouse master does, to the hierarchy of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]], and on Eilwìth that hierarchy is the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]] at Ravenmoor.

## The Present {#present}

Ondareth's envoys have begun to ask whose Speaker the Table's Speaker is. The chapterhouse's master answers to the Synod's hierarchy, and the Synod is the government of one of the two realms the town is neutral between. Leofwald Chilwell holds that the chapterhouse's scholarship and the Arcane Order's hierarchy are different things, and he has kept both realms at his table for eleven years. This spring Dúnavarre has asked to bring the matter of Ondareth's shipping and the compulsory call to the Table, and Ondareth has asked for the Table to meet without the Speaker.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The **Two Doors**, on the quay, keeps one entrance on the Dúnavarre side of the street and one on the Ondareth side, and guests of either realm come in by their own. Students and scholars lodge in the chapterhouse's guest rooms for a lecture or a copied page.

## History {#history}

Veldareth's charter is older than the Synod's rule. The town bought its freedom from the last kings of Dúnavarre and from Ondareth's Senate in the same generation, and has kept it by being more useful free than taken. The chapterhouse came with an Epistémion scholar who tutored the town's notaries, and the Table gave the Speaker's chair to its master when the realms first met there.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The Speaker's letters.** Ondareth wants copies of the Speaker's correspondence with Ravenmoor, and pays for them.
- **The library.** A treaty in the chapterhouse's library sets each realm's rights on the coast road along Seirasto's shore, and one of them wants the page.
- **A Seilvri at the Table.** A Seilvri envoy has come to Veldareth to ask the Table to hear the people's case, and the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]] wants her before she is heard.
