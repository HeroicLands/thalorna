## Overview {#overview}

Vardanreach is what the Eilwìthans called the north of their island while [[lore-vardain|the Vardain]] held it: the great plain, [[place-nordfells|the Nordfells]] and [[place-sunderfells|the Sunderfells]] above it, and the high valleys within them, something like an eighth of [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]]'s land, whose bounds the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]] fixed in [[lore-endgreatwar|the Ending of the Great War]]. It was a settled, literate, farming nation, and for thousands of years it kept a friendship no other human people has kept: a standing, mutually valued relationship with the Khazari of [[place-vorgald|Vorgald]].

## What Vardanreach Was

A Khazari hold grows nothing and will not be supplied from outside, so it has always needed farmers at its gate. Vardanreach was those farmers for Vorgald, and Vorgald fed off it for thousands of years; in return the Deep Folk gave the Vardain a friendship that was real, mutually useful and mutually respected.

## What Became of It

Sixty years ago the Nordmen took the plain. The lowland Vardain were not driven out but enslaved, and about a hundred and twenty thousand of them are thralls today in the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]], working ground their families held as free tenants within living memory. The conquerors renamed the country: the plain is [[place-stormplain|the Stormplain]] and the whole land is [[place-stormveld|Stormveld]].

What is left free of Vardanreach is [[place-vardainvalleys|the Vardain Valleys]] under Vorgald, where some eight thousand five hundred Vardain live under the protection of the Khazari. They belong to no realm, and the valley of [[place-threndale|Threndale]], where the dead of the whole district are buried, is still adding graves to the ground the Vardain call the new ground.

## Current Situation {#present}

Vardanreach is the historical name of the land, and no realm holds it under that name. The country it named is [[place-stormveld|Stormveld]], held by the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]], and everything a traveler meets there now is described under that name. The thralls of [[place-stormplain|the Stormplain]] still call the plain by the old name among themselves, and the free Vardain of the high valleys keep it in their count of the enslaved.
