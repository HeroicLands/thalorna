**Stormveil** is not a city and does not aspire to be one. It is the seat of the
[[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]], on the northern coast of [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]], facing the water
the Nordmen came over sixty years ago and still use, and its six thousand people fall into four
places: the High Jarl's hall on the height, the houses of his sworn men about it, the harbor below, and—down
the slope and set apart—the quarters of the thralls who do the work.

## The Hall and the Harbor

Everything that matters in Stormveld is decided in the hall, and the hall is a Nordmen institution
conducted in the Nordmen manner: the High Jarl, his sworn men, the drinking and the giving, disputes settled
in front of everybody. Eilwìthan forms have not been adopted and are not admired.

The harbor is the Jarldom's connection to the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]]—which is to say to the
world it actually belongs to. Timber, hides and thrall-worked produce go north; iron, ships, men and
quarrels come south. Nothing calls at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]] and nothing is inspected, the **[[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]]**
having no interest in northern traffic.

## The Thralls

Four in five people in the Jarldom are the property of the remaining fifth, and at Stormveil the
arrangement is legible in the layout of the town. The thralls of the seat are better fed and more
closely watched than those on the land, and Stormveil is where both of the Jarldom's risings began.

Neither reached its second year. The jarls have taken the lesson to be about vigilance.

## The Present {#present}

The common moot meets below the hall at Midsomar, and this year [[being-draskmilglrv|Draskmil Glarvendik]] of [[place-drasknes|Drasknes]] means to ask it for a new High Jarl. [[being-ulthorvhrnd|Ulthorv Hrindaruk]] holds the hall with his household men under his son, [[being-althmilhrnd|Althmil Hrindaruk]], and the Truce-Warden, [[being-gnarthrvdvlg|Gnarthorv Dvalgidrok]], has said he will hold the moot's peace against any hall.

The crew of a Thornburi coaster taken off the Nordfells in the autumn is held in the harbor-reeve's sheds below the hall, waiting for their shipping house to pay. The harbor-reeve, [[being-thalmthrvrld|Thalmthira Vruldaruk]], feeds them and counts the cost against the ransom.

## History {#history}

The fleets of Nordheim, Norgaad and Vithgard took the Vardain plain in one season in 660 AF ([[lore-stormveldtkn|the Taking of Stormveld]]), and the jarls who took it hold it under a High Jarl they elect among themselves. The Vardain were enslaved where they stood, and the thralls have risen twice; both risings began at Stormveil, and neither reached its second year. [[being-ulthorvhrnd|Ulthorv Hrindaruk]], born on the plain the year after the taking, was elected High Jarl at the Midsomar moot of 709 AF, after an election his men won by holding the moot-field.
