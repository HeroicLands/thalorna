## Overview {#overview}

"The [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands Region]] is where my grandfather's boat came from," a [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]] harbor pilot says, bringing a Nordheim hull in past the headland, "and the plain is where I was born, and I have never been asked which one is home." He grins, and then does not. "Mind the channel. It shifts."

Stormveld is the land of the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]], the north of [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]], at the middle of its northern coast: [[place-stormplain|the Stormplain]] between [[place-nordfells|the Nordfells]] and [[place-sunderfells|the Sunderfells]]. It occupies about an eighth of the island's land area, much of it mountain, and about 150,000 people live in it.

The northern coast of Eilwìth, with its fjord-like inlets and rocky headlands, was taken by Nordmen about sixty years ago, seafarers from the [[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Kingdom of Nordheim]], and they hold it still.

## Character

Fortified longhouses and harbors stand where the inlets give shelter, built to weather the storms of the northern coast. The plain and its farms are what keep the Jarldom alive: oats, barley and rye, cattle and turf, worked by thralls.

## The Conquest

All of Stormveld was [[place-vardanreach|Vardanreach]] before the conquest: the plain, the Nordfells, the Sunderfells and the high valleys within them, held by [[lore-vardain|the Vardain]] for thousands of years. The Nordmen who took it call it Stormveld.

## Facing North

Timber, thrall-worked produce and hides go north; iron, ships, men and quarrels come south.

## Current Situation {#present}

- **The pacts.** The jarls of the plain's edges have taken to swearing pacts with one another against the weak. [[being-vrithorvmrvd|Vrithorv Marvidrok]] of [[place-vuldstead|Vuldstead]] and [[being-draskmilglrv|Draskmil Glarvendik]] of [[place-drasknes|Drasknes]] raided a forest tribe together in 718 and divided its cattle and captives, and the tribe's answer burned four of the High Jarl's steadings in the western Nordfells the next spring. The High Jarl, [[being-ulthorvhrnd|Ulthorv Hrindaruk]], then swore a pact with Vrithorv to close the northern inlets to the free coasters, and their ships took a Thornburi coaster in the autumn.
- **The seat.** Draskmil Glarvendik, whose harbor lives on those coasters, has called for the High Jarl's seat to be put to the jarls at the Midsomar moot at [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]].
- **The thralls.** Four in five people on the land are thralls, and the halls watch them as closely as ever.
