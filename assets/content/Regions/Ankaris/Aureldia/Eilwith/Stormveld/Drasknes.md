## Overview {#overview}

**Drasknes** is the harbor of [[place-nordfells|the Nordfells]], on the northern coast of [[place-stormveld|Stormveld]], and the seat of its jarl, [[being-draskmilglrv|Draskmil Glarvendik]]. Its name is the headland of the kept light. About eighteen hundred people live there, Nordmen and thralls, under a hall on the headland and a fire that is kept burning on the point at night.

## Arriving {#arriving}

A ship comes to Drasknes along a coast of cliffs and narrow inlets, steering for the beacon. The inlet behind the headland is deep and sheltered, and a hull can lie there in any wind. By land the way is a track over the fells from the Stormplain, three days from [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]] in summer and closed for most of the winter.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Coasters. Small ships from Ondareth, from Nordheim and from the fells' own inlets put in at Drasknes to trade without calling at Stormveil, and the jarl takes his toll from them and asks them nothing else. Wool, hides, dried fish and seal-oil go out; salt, iron, cloth and news come in. The fells' steadings send their surplus down to the harbor, and the thralls of the town salt the fish and render the oil.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The jarl's hall rules. Draskmil Glarvendik keeps his own moot, his own levy and his own ship under its master, [[being-thraskvrsvlt|Thraskvir Svalthendik]], and his skald, [[being-skraldrnnlth|Skraldrinna Nulthumak]], keeps the hall's memory and makes its scorn.

## The Present {#present}

In the autumn the High Jarl, [[being-ulthorvhrnd|Ulthorv Hrindaruk]], and the jarl of [[place-vuldstead|Vuldstead]] swore a pact to close the northern inlets to coasters that do not call at Stormveil, and their ships took a Thornbury coaster off the fells within the month. The coasters are Drasknes's living, and none has put in since. Two summers ago Draskmil raided a forest tribe beside the same jarl of Vuldstead under a pact of their own, and he takes the new pact for a betrayal. He means to ask the moot at Midsomar for a new High Jarl, and his skald is making verses about Ulthorv for every hall that will hear them.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

Strangers sleep in the jarl's hall, as the Nordmen's custom requires of a hall with a guest at its door, and eat at the lower end of its board. Ships' crews sleep aboard.
