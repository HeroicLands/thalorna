The **Sunderfells** are [[place-stormveld|Stormveld]]'s southern march: the northern arm of [[place-ironfells|the Ironfells]]
where the range walls the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] off from the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]]. Sixteen thousand people
in high, wet, difficult country, and the most strategically important ground on [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]].

## The Passes

_Two passes_ cross the range, and they are the only routes between the Jarldom and the realms to the south
that do not involve a sea voyage or the Peshtar forest. Both are approached through deep wilderness on either side,
both are shut by snow for months, and both can be held at the top by a force far smaller than one
attempting to cross.

This is the whole reason the political map of Eilwìth looks as it does. The Jarldom has never pushed
south because pushing south means the passes; Aldorath has never seriously tried to retake the
north for the same reason. Sixty years of mutual hostility have produced skirmishes at the pass-heads
and nothing else whatever.

The southern ends are watched from [[place-caerwynd|Kaerwiind]]; the northern ends are garrisoned by
whichever of the jarl's men is currently out of favor, which tells you what the posting is thought
to be worth.

## The Wilderness

The approaches on both sides are wild—steep forest, no roads worth the name, and communities
in the folds of it that answer to neither realm and trade with both. Smuggling over the Sunderfells is
constant, seasonal and impossible to prevent, and a good deal of what Stormveld's thralls know about the
outside world comes over these mountains on a pack-pony.

## Current Situation {#present}

The snow is still on both passes. When it goes, the jarls' men at the northern pass-heads and [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Aldorath]]'s garrison out of [[place-caerwynd|Kaerwiind]] resume their skirmishing, and Aldorath's Warden, [[being-kenwaldhghd|Kenwald Highden]], has fewer men to send up than he had last year. The jarls' quarrel over the High Jarl's seat keeps them looking north, and the smugglers on both slopes are counting on it. Above the path that runs east from Sundergate's summit to the Vardain valleys, the [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] of [[place-wolfgate|Wolfgate]] wait for the thaw and the first runaways from the plain.
