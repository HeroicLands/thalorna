The **Nordfells** run along the whole northern edge of [[place-stormveld|Stormveld]], from the
[[place-pshtrwldrns|Peshtar forests]] in the west to the sea in the east. They are perhaps half the
[[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]]'s territory and hold about twenty-two thousand of its people—which is to say they are nearly
empty.

The name is a Nordmen one, and recent. What the Eilwìthans called them before the conquest is recorded in
places and is not used.

## The Country

Cold, wet, and higher than [[place-ironfells|the Ironfells]] to the south. Snow lies on the tops well
into what passes for summer on [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]], the valleys are narrow and boggy, and the whole range drops
sharply to the northern coast in cliffs and inlets.

Those inlets are the Nordfells' importance. Small craft can put in along the whole northern shore
without going near [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]], which suits a great many people—Nordmen coming and
going on business the jarl need not hear about, and a persistent traffic in thralls attempting to leave
that the Jarldom has never managed to stop.

## Its People

Shepherds, turf-cutters and a scatter of hard upland steadings, Nordmen and thrall alike, plus the
Jarldom's hunters. The fells also hold a number of communities that acknowledge nobody—Eilwìthan
uplanders who withdrew after the conquest and have not come down since, and whom the jarls treat as
weather rather than as rebels.

## The Other Occupants

The fells hold [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]], as the empty country of this island has for some seven
hundred years—long before there was a Jarldom to be troubled by them.

They are in the high folds and the old workings, and Stormveld loses upland steadings to them most
years. The Jarldom books these losses as raiding by the Eilwìthan uplanders who withdrew after the
conquest, which is convenient, occasionally accurate, and not believed by the men who patrol up there.

## Current Situation {#present}

The inlets are closed, by the pact the High Jarl swore with the jarl of [[place-vuldstead|Vuldstead]] in the autumn. Their ships watch the north shore for coasters that put in without calling at [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]], and have taken one already. [[place-drasknes|Drasknes]], the one harbor of the fells, lives on that coasting trade, and its jarl, [[being-draskmilglrv|Draskmil Glarvendik]], takes the pact for an attack on his hall. In the west of the fells, four steadings of the High Jarl's sworn men are burned shells since a forest tribe came over the ridges in the spring of 719, and the [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] of Koldfell, in the crags above them, take whoever comes to rebuild. In the central fells the nest in the old workings at Grimfold raids farther down toward the plain after a winter that killed the fell sheep.
