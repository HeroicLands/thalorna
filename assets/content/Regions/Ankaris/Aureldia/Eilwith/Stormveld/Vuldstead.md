## Overview {#overview}

**Vuldstead** is the hall-town of the western [[place-stormplain|Stormplain]], where the plain's grain land runs up to the edge of the [[place-pshtrwldrns|Peshtar forest]], and the seat of its jarl, [[being-vrithorvmrvd|Vrithorv Marvidrok]]. Its name is the grain-stead. About two and a half thousand people live there, and four in five of them are thralls.

## Arriving {#arriving}

The road west from [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]] crosses the plain for three days through barley and rye and comes to Vuldstead on a low rise: a great hall inside a ditch and palisade, the barns and granaries of the jarl's own land around it, and the thrall rows below. West of the town the fields end at a cleared strip and the forest begins.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Grain. The western plain is the best of the Jarldom's barley land, and Vuldstead's granaries send their surplus east to Stormveil and north over the sea. The town also lives by the forest edge: timber, charcoal and the cattle and captives its jarl's men take from the tribes.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The jarl rules from his hall and keeps his own moot. The men of the forest-edge district muster under their hersvald, [[being-anthorvrlthr|Anthorv Rultharuk]], who answers for the steadings nearest the trees.

## The Present {#present}

Vrithorv Marvidrok has sworn two pacts in two years. The first, in 718, with the jarl of [[place-drasknes|Drasknes]], took five hundred cattle and thirty captives from a forest tribe, and the tribe's answer fell on the High Jarl's steadings in the Nordfells rather than on Vuldstead's. The second, in the autumn of 719, with the High Jarl himself, closed the northern inlets to the free coasters and turned the jarl of Drasknes against them both. Vrithorv keeps his steadings armed for the tribe's next answer, and Anthorv Rultharuk has asked him how long the district must stand to arms for cattle the hall has already eaten.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

A stranger sleeps in the jarl's hall and is asked his business at the board. Drovers and grain-buyers sleep in the granary lofts at harvest.
