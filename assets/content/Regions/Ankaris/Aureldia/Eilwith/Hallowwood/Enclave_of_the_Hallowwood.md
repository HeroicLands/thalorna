The **Enclave of the Hallowwood** is the community of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] of [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]], who reside in [[place-hallowwood|the Hallowwood]]. Senali have kings, queens and lords, but these do not work as human ones do, and the enclave is governed by its lord. It does not answer to [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Dúnavarre]], which sends embassies that are received and answered briefly.
