## Overview {#overview}

**The Legate's Roll**, the _legrotherna_, is the roll the imperial legate of the occupied march kept at [[place-ronetho|Ornetho]] through the four centuries of the occupation ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]). It is a book of calfskin leaves sewn into boards, written in [[skill-vylarilng|Vilari]] in many hands, one legate after another. It records every house of the lowlands that swore to the Empire and what each owed the garrison in men and in grain. When the garrisons were driven out ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]), the new kings of [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Lyenrik]] took the city whole, and the roll was given into the keeping of [[lore-ardavondty|Árdavon]]'s temple at the north gate, because an oath belongs to the god of oaths. It was taken from the temple in 680 AF, and [[being-kotsudekanar|Kotsude Kanarris]] has been looking for it since.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody is known to. The priests of the north gate say it was taken from the temple's keeping in one night, on a watch Kotsude stood as a young knight sworn to the temple, and never seen again. Kotsude believes the man who took it kept a record of where it went, and that the record lies in the vaults under the old barracks, which the castellans of Ornetho have kept locked since the occupation ended.

## Its Story {#its-story}

The legates kept the roll as the Empire kept every march: a house that swore was written in, with its holding, its levy and its dues, and a house that refused was written in too. Each legate added his own notes, and the last of them wrote through the years when the lowlands stopped paying and the mountain lords elected a High King to drive the garrisons out. The garrisons left the roll behind with the rest of the march's records.

The kings of Lyenrik had no use for a list of their lords' oaths to the Empire, and no wish to burn a sworn record either. They gave it to the north-gate temple, and the priests sealed it and kept it beside the oaths the temple witnesses for the companies that march out under its blessing. In 680 the seal was found broken and the roll gone. Kotsude, who had the watch, swore before the altar to bring it back.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

The roll does nothing a scribe could not do. What it holds is the oaths. The Roll of Houses at [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]] ranks the houses of Lyenrik by their descent and their deeds, and a house's place on it is argued from records. The legate's roll is the oldest record of the lowland houses there is, and it names houses that now sit in the High Hall as electors, in more than one kingdom of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]].

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **Kotsude Kanarris** wants it back in the temple's keeping, sealed, before it reaches anyone who would use it.
- **The priests of the north gate** want the oath their knight swore kept, and the roll returned to the god's keeping.
- **The houses that want a higher place on the Roll of Houses** want a record that would prove their descent, and some of them have heard that the legate's roll would.
- **The houses that fear it** want it found first, and burned.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- Kotsude asks a party to find a way into the vaults under the old barracks, where he believes the thief kept his record ([[being-kotsudekanar|Kotsude Kanarris]]).
- A house of Lyenrik has put a claim before the Keeper of the Roll of Houses that stands on a record nobody has seen, and the claim names an oath sworn to a legate.

:::secret {title="Where the roll lies"}
The roll never left Ornetho. The castellan of 680, a brother of the king of that day, had it taken from the temple in the night and put back among the garrison's registers in the vaults under the old barracks, under a garrison shelf-mark, where it still lies. He told nobody why, and no record of it was kept. The roll names houses of the Roll of Houses, lords of the Galvaren coast and lords of Asturath's lowland approaches who swore to the Empire. Against several of them the last legates wrote what each was paid for word of the rising. Any of those houses would have paid the castellan to make it disappear.

The present castellan, [[being-hiltheroltr|Hilther Oltris]], does not know it is there. Kotsude's "record of the thief" is the roll itself. A party that reaches the vaults finds the march's archive and, among its registers, a book that is not a register.
:::
