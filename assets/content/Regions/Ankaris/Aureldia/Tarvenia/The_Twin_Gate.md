## Overview {#overview}

The Twin Gate is the gate of [[place-olvestria|Ólvestra]] in [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]], and the threshold every traveler to the city crosses first. It is one of the most consequential thresholds in all of [[place-aureldirgn|Aureldia]], and [[affiliation-twinbldrdr|the Twinblade Order]] keeps its mother-house here. The order takes the title of its chair from this gate: the Voice of the Twin Gate.

The city's gate-guards keep the passage. A Twinblade on station keeps the threshold. When the guards admit or refuse a traveler and the matter is contested, the Twinblade confirms the admission or the refusal, and a noble's blood and rank do not override that word.

## Inside {#inside}

- **[1] The Gate:** the passage into Ólvestra, kept by the city's gate-guards and by a Sworn Twinblade on station. The Twinblade's combat is fought at the threshold itself and not beyond it; past the gate, a Twinblade is a private knight.
- **[2] The Twin Gate Temple:** the temple of [[affiliation-ardavon|Árdavon]] at the gate, where the order's senior elevations are conducted in temple rite. A Senior Twinblade receives the pin of the open gate here.
- **[3] The Mother-House:** the Twinblade chapter house at the gate, where the Marendos of the Gate presides over the chapter and its stations. The order's ceremonial cadre lives here, and so do the candidates in their two to four years of training in arms, threshold-guarding, challenge and parley, and the paired-weapon discipline every chapter house teaches.

## Who Is There {#who-is-there}

- **The Marendos of the Gate** presides over the mother-house. The present Voice of the Twin Gate held this post before the assembled _marendoi_ (masters) of the order elevated her.
- **The Twinblades on station**, each in the pale gray surcoat with the two-faced head and crossed gold-and-silver blades at the breast, a longsword in the right scabbard and a shorter, more ornate blade in the left.
- **The ceremonial cadre**, the Twinblades the order sends to the coronations, treaty-signings and embassies of Aureldia that require a Twinblade of the right rank.
- **Brothers of [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|the Order of the Sealed Word]]**, whose own mother-house is in the city. The two orders coordinate constantly: the Sealed Word convenes oath-hearings, and the Twinblades provide the threshold-presence at formal proceedings.

## The Present {#present}

The largest cell of [[affiliation-ordershnvw|the Order of the Ashen Vow]], [[affiliation-olvstrncell|the Ólvestran Cell]], works from the hill-baronies around the city, under the barons' protection. The Ashen Vow is the Twinblades' sworn enemy, and the Árdavon orders keep standing arrangements with [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] to suppress it. A Black Flame operative who tries to cross a threshold the Twinblades guard is refused, and the refusal is sometimes contested by combat. The order's own doctrine sets the limit of that war. The Twinblades hold the gate, and they do not ride out after what withdraws from it into the hills, except by chapter order.

The Ólvestran chapter of the Sealed Word lost its Marendos seven years ago, three days before he was to issue a major writ against a baron sympathetic to [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]]. His death was ruled natural, and the city still talks about it. A hearing the Sealed Word convenes against a baron of the hills needs the Twinblades at its threshold.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The refusal at the gate.** A baron of the hills rides up to the Twin Gate with his retinue, and the gate is formally closed to him. The Twinblade on station refuses him, and the baron's men contest the refusal by combat at the threshold.
- **The cadre.** The ceremonial cadre sets out from the mother-house for a coronation, and an agent of a rival claimant has offered its commander a sum to allow one protocol violation in the ceremony ([[affiliation-twinbldrdr|the Twinblade Order]]).
