## Overview

The Spirits of Eskarath is the faith the Eskárathi of the [[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]] keep under their worship of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. They honor the Aureldian gods in name. In practice that worship lies over a much older tradition of the highlands, and the highland people venerate mountain spirits, forest guardians and ancestral figures alongside the gods or instead of them.

## Belief {#belief}

The tradition knows three kinds of power. The mountain spirits are the spirits of the peaks and passes, the forest guardians those of the woods and the old oaks, and the ancestral figures are the forebears of the clans, the _aitsen_. Each place has its own spirit, its _aidar_, and the shrines are kept for the spirits of the places where they stand.

## Worship {#worship}

Shrines to the local spirits stand at the mountain passes, at the springs and in the ancient oak groves of [[place-eskarath|Eskarath]]. Among the highland villages, [[place-leparrun|Leparrun]] keeps the shrine on its pass, [[place-ibotsain|Ibotsain]] the shrine at its spring and [[place-gerdoibun|Gerdoibun]] the shrine in its oak grove.

The Eskárathi have magical traditions of their own: herb-lore, weather-reading and a form of ancestral communion. They practice them openly.

## Politics {#present}

The folk practices of the Eskárathi have more in common with the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] traditions across the border in [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], and with [[affiliation-theoldway|the Old Way]] they keep, than with anything in lowland Tarvénia. That affinity, and the Eskárathi's distinctness from the Tarveni, leaves them belonging fully to neither Tarvénia nor Élavendre.

The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] has never established a presence in Eskarath. The few of its agents who have ventured into the highlands report the herb-lore, the weather-reading and the ancestral communion, and the Eskárathi refuse to submit any of it to outside regulation. The [[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]] holds to that refusal.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

A traveler in the highlands passes the shrines on the passes, waters at the springs that have them and walks through the oak groves where they stand. An Eskárathi who reads the weather or treats a wound with herbs does it in plain sight.
