## Overview {#overview}

Mirgon Lodge is the winter lodge of the Mirindi, a clan of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] in the western arm of the [[place-toravarrange|Toravar Range]]. It stands in a fold of fir wood a day's climb above the valley of [[place-kemthoza|Kemthoza]], and from the ridge over it a herder can see the fort, the yard where the [[place-westernmrch|Western March]]'s hosts gather and the saddle of [[place-grumagando|Grumagando]]. Its name is the Áelendi _mirgon_, the boundary cairn where a valley's ground begins. The cairn stands on the path above the lodge, and past it the high country is the clan's.

The lodge sits where two peoples' years cross. The Mirindi go up to their summer pastures when the snow goes off the high ground, and in the same weeks the barons of the March go down the war pass into [[place-talothe|the Talote]]. Every spring the clan's herds and the March's hosts want the thaw at the same time, and the shepherds' way from the March to the high pastures passes the cairn.

## The Village {#the-village}

About two hundred and forty people live at the lodge through the winter, in some forty households. The halls are long and low, timber on stone footings, with roofs of split shakes laid over with moss, and each hall holds three or four hearths under one ridge. The great hall stands at the head of the fold, and its kept fire, the _kárathon_, is banked at night and never let die. Before its door lies the _ghalnis_, the trodden gathering ground where the clan meets, and below the halls the walled fields, the _ilmis_, grow rye and turnips in a short summer.

The Mirindi keep sheep and goats, hunt the deer of the fir wood and trap marten for their fur. They make felt, cheese and horn ware, and they buy salt, iron and cloth in the valleys below. The greeting at the door at first light is _trénkira_, and a stranger who arrives after dark is fed and asked his business in the morning.

## The Holding {#the-holding}

The Mirindi claim no land and own none ([[lore-aelendanclt#land-that-is-not-owned|Land That Is Not Owned]]). They keep ground, and they answer for it under the [Kindred Pact]{.name data-type="lore/law"}. Their ground begins at the cairn and runs up to the bare rock: the fir wood above the lodge, the high pasture they call their _mirla_, and the summer steadings, the _tirna_, where the herders live from the summer move to the first snow.

At the head of the high pasture, in a corrie under the ridge, a stand of old pines is the clan's hallowed grove. The clan sings the Pact there in spring before the herds go up. Nothing is cut in it, nothing grazes it, and no stranger enters it.

## Who Leads It {#who-leads}

The lodge's Council of Elders is the head of each household, the _sorkhand_ who speaks for a hearth, and it meets on the gathering ground. Its eldest voice is [[being-randiramiri|Rándira Mirindi]], who has spoken for her hearth for thirty years and whose word the council waits for. The clan's Warden is [[being-tergadanmiri|Térgadan Mirindi]], who keeps the grove and the clan's ground and meets the barons below. He answers to the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] and to the Pact, and the council does not command him.

The Mirindi do not seat delegates at the common council at [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]]. They are the same people as the tribes of the interior ([[place-alndntrblnds#the-tarvenan-diaspora|the Tarvenan diaspora]]), and the Wardens' runners carry word between the Toravar and the interior when the Pact calls. No Tarvenan crown claims the lodge or the ground above it.

## The Spring at the Cairn {#present}

The snow is going off the high pasture, and the herders are waiting on the elders' word for the summer move. Two quarrels with the valleys below stand open.

The first is last autumn's. A herder of [[being-kaldrokanar|Kaldro Kanarris]]'s valley drove a flock past the cairn and into the grove before the first snow, and the clan's young men turned the sheep out with dogs and stones. Térgadan has asked the baron for the herder, so that the man can answer at the cairn, and Kaldro has not given him up.

The second is this spring's. Men from a valley of the March have come up the shepherds' way twice since the thaw began, past the cairn and onto the high pasture below the grove, and gone down again without coming to the lodge door. Térgadan has seen their tracks. The young men want to stand at the cairn with spears, and Rándira has told them to wait until the Warden has spoken with the barons.

## What Is Seen from It {#what-is-seen}

From the ridge above the lodge the March lies open below: the valleys climbing west, the tower of Kemthoza over its road and, in the season, the hosts filing through its gate. The saddle of Grumagando is in plain view, and so is the shepherds' way, a pale thread up the slope north of it that climbs to the clan's pasture and goes over the ridge beyond. On a clear day the herders on the ridge count the March's banners before Kemthoza does.

## The Cost of Reaching It {#cost-of-reaching-it}

The way up from Kemthoza's valley is the herders' path, a day's climb through beech and then fir, steep in its upper half and wet through the thaw. The snow closes it from the first heavy fall to the spring, and a winter climb is made on snowshoes or not at all. At the cairn a stranger stops and calls out, and waits until someone comes up from the lodge below. A party that brings nothing cut from the grove, and asks before it goes higher, is fed. One that walks past the cairn uncalled has started a quarrel.

## History {#history}

The Mirindi's Tradition Keepers hold the clan's line back to the people who came over the eastern pass in the snow ([[lore-nelgancross|The Crossing of Nelgan]]), and they say the clan has wintered in this fold for as long as the line is kept. The barons' valleys below are younger than the lodge. Since about 556 AF the March's hosts have gone down Grumagando every season the snow allows ([[lore-summerwar|The Summer War]]), and the clan has watched them go from the ridge.

The clan has had one mountain-war in living memory. In the autumn of 694 a baron of the March sent loggers past the cairn into the fir wood under the grove, the young men burned the loggers' camp, and the baron's valley and the clan fought through one autumn on the slopes between. The crown of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] settled it before the snow through Élavendri intermediaries, as it settles every such war, and the cairn was restacked where it had stood.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The herder at the cairn.** Térgadan Mirindi wants Kaldro Kanarris's herder brought up to answer at the cairn before the summer move, and needs someone the baron will listen to.
- **The tracks below the grove.** Térgadan wants to know whose men have been up the shepherds' way and why they did not come to the door.
- **The young men's spears.** Rándira Mirindi has held the young men back once. She asks a party to carry the clan's word to the barons before she has to hold them back again.
- **The war pass.** [[scenario-greenbough|The Green Bough]] begins in the weeks when the snow goes off the high pasture and the saddle at once.
