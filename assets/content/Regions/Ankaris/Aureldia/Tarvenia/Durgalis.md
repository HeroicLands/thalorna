## Overview {#overview}

Dúrgalis is a castle town of about 3,000 in the hill country of the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]], and in Tarvénia its valley is known for the hail. The castle stands above the town and its square, and below them the hill farms of the valley grow barley up to a pass where the summer storms come over. The farms tell [[lore-adepthail|the Adept and the Hail]] at their hearths in winter, the comic tale of the adept of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] who sold the valley a contract against the hail and was paid exactly what it said. The high valleys hold that the hail is the Mother's and comes for a lie ([[lore-motheryesno|the Mother's Yes and No]]).

## History {#history}

As the valley tells it, an adept of the Arcane Order once came up the road from a city chapter and offered the assembly a contract to turn every hail-cloud that came over the pass. The wise-woman [Beruzta]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Chestnut, told the assembly to buy it on her terms: a silver to the adept at the autumn fair for every field the hail spared, a silver from him for every field it struck, and a plain account from him every week on the steps of the well. The castle's notary wrote the terms and sealed them beside the order's seal, and the adept and the valley swore them at the stone in the square. The adept turned every storm at the pass. The hail still struck twenty-three of the valley's two hundred and twelve fields, each one out of a clear sky on the day after its owner lied, and the valley paid the adept a hundred and sixty-six silver in the square, counted out on the steps of the well. It has bought no contract against the hail since.

## Current Situation {#present}

Every autumn fair the [[affiliation-cncldrgls|Council of Dúrgalis]], the assembly of the valley's free men, stands in the square and says aloud how many fields the hail took that summer.

The trouble in Dúrgalis this year is a debt owed on the coast. The house of the pilot [[being-dalenavlrn|Dálena Valaron]] holds a small lordship at Dúrgalis, and the family's cargo ship out of [[place-valthari|Valthari]] has paid for that lordship for two generations. Her father has borrowed against the ship to hold the lordship, and the lender is a house on the ship-owners' council of Valthari. That council has asked Dálena, whose crew is loyal to her, to sail while the seamen of the port refuse to sign, and so break their refusal. She wants her seamen paid and fed as they should be, and the owners' council has told her that her house's standing will not survive her taking their side.

Bandits raid the countryside around the valley, and the yeoman [[being-trsrthldrn|Tórsaro Thalidran]] stands against them in the local militia under a militia leader who saw promise in him.

## The Holder {#holder}

A lord holds the castle and governs the town from it, as a Tarvenan lord governs from a hilltop castle. His steward speaks for him in the assembly, and his notary writes and seals what the assembly agrees. The valley's tale names neither lord nor steward. The Valaron house holds its small lordship at Dúrgalis beside him, on the money its ship brings home from the sea.

## Government, Faith and Trade {#government-faith-trade}

The valley's common matters belong to its assembly, the [[affiliation-cncldrgls|Council of Dúrgalis]], which meets on its assembly day in the square under the castle. A matter between neighbors goes to the assembly or the lord, and a contract the valley makes is sworn at the stone in the square and sealed by the castle's notary.

The valley lives by its barley and its autumn fair. Its fields run up to the pass, a miller keeps a weir on the stream, and the inn on the square takes in the travelers who come up the road. The oath stone (_goitsa_) stands in the square, older than any shrine, and the valley's wise-woman is a neighbor who sets bones and delivers children there.

## The Square {#within-the-walls}

The square under the castle is the whole public life of Dúrgalis. The assembly sits there, a speaker stands on the steps of the well to be heard, and contracts and debts are counted out on the same steps in front of the stone. The inn stands on the square with its own field of barley behind it, and from the castle wall the lord can watch the square and the home field in front of the castle.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The note on the ship.** [[being-dalenavlrn|Dálena Valaron]] wants to know whether the Valthari house that holds her father's note means to call it if she refuses to break the seamen's refusal, and she will pay a party to find out before she answers the owners' council.
- **The day after.** Hail has come out of a clear sky onto a single field of the valley, and its owner swore in the square the day before. The assembly wants the lie found and answered before the autumn count, and the owner says the hail fell on the wrong field.
- **The raiders on the pass road.** The militia leader who took [[being-trsrthldrn|Tórsaro Thalidran]] into the militia wants the bandits who raid the valley's farms followed to wherever they winter, and he has more young men than experienced ones.
