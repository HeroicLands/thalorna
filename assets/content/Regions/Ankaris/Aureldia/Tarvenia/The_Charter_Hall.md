## Overview {#overview}

The Charter Hall is the hall of the courts of [[place-valthari|Valthari]], which the city's royal charter guarantees it. It stands on the open square where the [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]] meets, so that the council sits in the square in front of it in fair weather and inside it in foul. It is a long building of gray stone bound with black iron, in the city's way, with a Provénzian arcade along its front where advocates and clerks wait for business. The Chancellor keeps the charter in it, and the Arbitrators, the city court and the Warden of the Weights all sit under its roof. A stranger's quarrel with a house of the city is heard here.

## Inside {#inside}

- **[1] The arcade.** The covered walk along the square, where advocates offer their services to anyone with a case and the city's notices are posted.
- **[2] The Arbitrators' bench.** The room where the Arbitrators hear disputes between houses under the commercial custom: contracts, cargoes, partnerships and debts. The council enforces what they rule.
- **[3] The city court.** The court of the [[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]] for every other case, freeman's or foreigner's.
- **[4] The weighing room.** The Warden of the Weights keeps the city's standard measures, weights and assay here, and a merchant who disputes a measure brings it to be tried against them.
- **[5] The charter room.** A locked room where the Chancellor keeps the charter, the city's treaties and the copies of the terms each High King has sworn.
- **[6] The cells.** A few cells under the hall, where prisoners wait for the court.

## Who Is There {#who-is-there}

- **The Arbitrators**, professional settlers of disputes between houses, sworn at the hall.
- **The Chancellor**, who keeps the charter room and the city's letters.
- **The Warden of the Weights** and his inspectors, who fine short weight and false coin in the markets.
- **The advocates and clerks** of the arcade, who take a case for a fee and find a stranger a councilor willing to put his petition.

## The Present {#present}

The Arbitrators' bench is crowded with cargoes that have not sailed. The seamen of Valthari refuse to sign on until the ship-owning houses raise the crew's share, and the houses whose contracts fall due are suing one another for late delivery. The Arbitrators must decide whether a seamen's refusal excuses a house that could not sail, and every house in the city is waiting to learn which way they rule, because the same ruling binds every house that could not sail.

In the charter room the Chancellor is having a fair copy of the charter made for the councilor who carries it to [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] when the next High King swears the terms of the crown.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The first ruling.** A house facing a suit for a late cargo hires a party to find a precedent in the hall's own rolls that a refusal of the seamen excuses delivery, before its case is called.
- **The copy.** A councilor who expects to carry the charter to Lezkora trusts neither the Chancellor nor his clerks, and wants a reader from outside the city to compare the fair copy with the charter clause by clause before he signs for it.
