## Overview

The Kingdom of Tarvénia is less a unified state than a political idea—a loose confederation of fiercely independent petty kingdoms, city-states, and frontier territories held together by a shared language, a common warrior culture, and periodic external threats. The High King of Tarvénia claims sovereignty over all Tarvéni-speaking lands, but in practice each local lord, town council, and clan chief governs their own territory with minimal interference.

## Government

The High King is elected by a council of the major lords—a process that produces as much warfare as governance. The first election, about 540 AF, was held to drive out the Vilérian garrisons ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]). The crown carries the right to call the ban (a general military levy) and to adjudicate disputes between lords, but the High King has no standing army, no central bureaucracy, and limited revenue beyond his own estates. Attempts to centralize power have historically ended with the ambitious king deposed, exiled, or dead.

The crown's officers are few. The Keeper of the Hall lives at [[place-lezkorahall|the High Hall]] at Lezkora, keeps it and its hearth, and opens it to the lords, and the crown's heralds carry the High King's word to them. The gate the electors climb through belongs to the [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]], whose Warden of the Plateau Gate counts them through, and the hall's roll is kept by the [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|Order of the Sealed Word]].

### Electing a High King

The High King is elected at [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]], in [[place-lezkorahall|the High Hall]] on the lip of its plateau, by every lord of the three kingdoms who brings his own banner to the ban of Tarvénia: the lords of Asturath, Galvaren and Lyenrik, about a hundred in all. An election has been over in a morning and has taken most of a summer, and it runs the same way every time.

1. **The hearth goes out.** When a High King dies, the Keeper of the Hall puts out the fire on the hall's hearth, and the crown's heralds ride to every lord who has a seat in the hall. The lords meet as soon as the plateau road is open, which after a winter death is not before late spring.
2. **The climb.** Each lord comes up to the plateau with twelve sworn men and no more, and the Warden of the Plateau Gate counts them through. The rest of a lord's following waits below at [[place-ardalis|Ardalis]]. The rule keeps any one lord from taking the plateau by force while the throne is empty, and it puts every elector inside a gate kept by the kingdom of Asturath.
3. **The naming.** On the first morning any lord may stand at the hall's oath stone and name a candidate, who must hold land and bring a banner to the ban. A lord may name himself.
4. **The voices.** Each lord in turn stands at the stone and names his choice aloud, and a brother of the [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|Order of the Sealed Word]] writes it in the hall's roll. A candidate with two voices in every three is High King. If nobody has them, the lords go back into the city to bargain and return the next morning to vote again. Buying a lord's voice is ordinary and nobody hides it. A voice promised to one candidate and given to another is a broken word before witnesses, and the lord who breaks it answers for it in the hall.
5. **The terms.** The elected lord swears the terms of the crown at the stone: he calls the ban only against an enemy from outside Tarvénia, judges between lords who bring him their quarrel, keeps the charter of the [[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]] and the old arrangement with the [[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]], and takes nothing from any lord's land that the terms do not allow. Each lord then grips the new king's forearm and says the ban, the promise that his banner will come when it is called.
6. **The fire.** The new High King lights the hall's hearth with fire carried from the temple of [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] in the city, and the election is over.

The free city and the free territory have no voice in the hall. Valthari sends one of its councilors to hear the terms sworn, because its charter is one of them, and pays the new king the year's tribute; Eskarath sends the Speaker of its council for the same reason. A High King who breaks the terms is answered the way any lord who claims more than was agreed is answered, and the lords stop obeying him. If enough of them come up to Lezkora, they can put out the hearth and elect another, and the crown has been lost that way more than once.

Local governance varies wildly. Some territories are ruled by hereditary lords in fortified hilltop castles. Others are governed by town councils of merchants and master craftsmen. The councils of four such towns answer to the crown, [[affiliation-cncldrgls|Dúrgalis]] through the lord of its castle and [[affiliation-cnclhrthm|Herthema]], [[affiliation-cnclnbrls|Nebralis]] and [[affiliation-cnclthrgl|Thórgila]] directly. The mountain clans in the interior follow their own ancient customs and acknowledge the High King only when it suits them.

## Current Situation {#present}

The High King is old. The electors chose [[being-ularrekgarr|Ularrek Garrundeta]], a lord of Asturath's high valleys, because each of the greater candidates could block the others. He holds his court in the High Hall as the guest of the king of Asturath, [[being-alzemozold|Alzemo Zolduregi]], and passes through that king's gate. The king of Lyenrik, [[being-zenotholtr|Zenoth Oltris]], means to stand at the next election, and the lowland lords have begun sending their men up the road to Lezkora one and two at a time.

## The Five Regions

The kingdom is divided into five semi-autonomous regions, each with its own government and character. All owe nominal allegiance to the High King, but in practice they run their own affairs and cooperate (or quarrel) as circumstances demand.

### The Three Kingdoms

[[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]]
: The mountainous heartland of Tarvénia, seat of the High King, and the spiritual core of Tarvenan independence. Asturath's mountain fortresses were never conquered by the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]], and the kingdom's lords consider themselves the truest heirs of the ancient Tarveni warrior tradition. The High King is traditionally (though not always) drawn from the Astúrathi nobility.

[[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]]
: The green, mist-shrouded south-western shore. Galvaren's people are seafarers, fishermen, and dreamers, with a reputation for mysticism that sits uneasily with mainstream Tarvenan skepticism of the arcane. The kingdom's fishing fleets are the largest in the western part of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]], and its sailors are legendarily fearless—or foolhardy, depending on who you ask.

[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]
: The largest and most fertile of the three kingdoms, occupying the central-southern lowlands. Lyenrik has the most conventional feudal structure, the richest farmland, and the largest population. Its lords are wealthier and more politically ambitious than their mountain and coastal counterparts, and Liénriki nobles have contested the High Kingship more often than anyone.

### The Free City

[[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]
: A prosperous port city-state on the southern coast, governed by a council of merchant families. Valthari is the richest single settlement in Tarvénia and the kingdom's window onto the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] trade. The city jealously guards its independence, paying tribute to the High King in exchange for a charter that guarantees self-governance. Its merchant-captains and naval power give it political leverage far beyond its modest territory.

### The Free Territory

[[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]
: The highland territory along the northern border with [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], home to an ancient people who predate the Tarveni migration. The Eskárathi speak their own language alongside [[skill-tarvenlng|Tarveni]], maintain their own customs and legal traditions, and acknowledge the High King only in the loosest sense. They are fiercely independent even by Tarvenan standards—which is saying something.

### The Royal Cities

Two cities lie under none of the three kingdoms and hold their government by the High King's charter.

[[affiliation-cnclolvstr|Council of Ólvestra]]
: The chartered council of the merchant and moneylending houses of [[place-olvestria|Ólvestra]], the moneylenders' city behind the Twin Gate and the seat of the Order of the Sealed Word.

[[affiliation-tmplplthms|Temple City of Polthamis]]
: [[place-polthamis|Polthamis]], ruled by the prelate of the Great Temple of Árdavon and his chapter.

## Character

Tarvénia's culture prizes individual courage, personal honor, and independence above all else. The Tarvenan mercenary tradition is famous across Ankaris—Tarveni swordsmen and crossbowmen serve in armies from [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Viléria]] to [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Taruzel]], and mercenary service is considered an honorable career rather than a mark of desperation.

The Tarvenans are deeply skeptical of magic and of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] in particular. The Arcane Order maintains a few chapters in the larger cities but operates cautiously; mages who throw their weight around in Tarvénia tend to end up with a knife between their ribs. Folk healers, hedge witches, and village wise-women are tolerated and even respected, but institutionalized arcane power is viewed as just another form of tyranny.

## Commerce and Currency

The Kingdom of Tarvénia uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian currency]] (Solden / Ardin / Ogdel) as its standard currency, with full access to the paper-script system through [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]]-member moneylenders chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]]. The kingdom is a post-imperial commonwealth member—it participates fully in the trade and banking infrastructure of the Vilérian system but is not subject to imperial governance or imperial taxation. See [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian Currency]] for the full system.

## History {#history}

### The Occupation (about 140 to 538 AF)

Between about 140 and 160 AF the legions of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] took Tarvénia's southern coast and central lowlands, made Valthari an imperial port and held Lyenrik as an occupied march ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]). The mountains of Asturath were never taken, and after the legion lost in [[lore-horngorgebtl|the Horn Gorge]] about 143 AF the Empire held the lowland approaches and left the mountains alone.

### The First High Kings (about 538 to 605 AF)

From about 538 AF the occupied lowlands stopped paying the Empire, and about 540 the mountain lords met at Lezkora and elected [Garvaneth]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Asturath the first High King to drive the garrisons out ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]). The garrisons were out within seven years. Garvaneth had no silver to pay the lords who had elected him, and he sold Valthari a charter of self-government for a yearly tribute. About 556 AF the second High King, [Mórezan]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, took a host down the western pass into Provénzia ([[lore-malnebris|The First Great Raid]]), and the High Kings and the lords of the Western March have gone down the passes every season the snow allows since ([[lore-summerwar|The Summer War]]).

### The Contested Crown (from about 605 AF)

The lords of Lyenrik have contested the crown more often than anyone, and [Estaril]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Lyenrik held it from about 605 AF, the first of several Liénriki High Kings. The High King has still most often been an Astúrathi lord, and the present one is.

### The Present Reign (from about 709 AF)

About 709 AF the hearth of the High Hall went out, and the greater candidates, the king of Lyenrik, [[being-zenotholtr|Zenoth Oltris]], among them, could each block the others. The lords gave their voices instead to [[being-ularrekgarr|Ularrek Garrundeta]], an old lord of Asturath's high valleys, who swore the terms and lit the hearth ([[lore-ularrekelect|The Election of Ularrek]]). He holds the crown still, as the guest of the king of Asturath at Lezkora, and the next election is already being bought.
