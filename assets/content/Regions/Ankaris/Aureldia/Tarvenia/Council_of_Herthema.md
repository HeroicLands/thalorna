## Overview {#overview}

The **Council of Herthema** governs [[place-herthema|Herthema]], a walled town on the coast of [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]]. It is a council of merchants and master craftsmen, the kind of council that governs a free city or a coastal town in Tarvénia, and it sits inside the walls.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The merchants of the town and the masters of its crafts hold the council's seats, and the council chooses a First of the Council from among them to preside and to speak for the town. It governs Herthema inside its walls and keeps the town's sellswords' roll: a man on the roll may take hire in Herthema lawfully, and a man the Keeper of the Roll strikes from it may not.

## Current Situation {#present}

The council's merchants are the town's new money, and they are pressing Herthema's old trades to change. A reform faction among them wants shipbuilding standardized and the shipwright [[being-domthgrvth|Dometh Graveth]] discredited, and the younger glassworkers who make figured glass for the merchants' houses are taking the commissions the guild's old masters held. The priest [[being-gaimelkorvat|Gaimel Korvathandis]] is agitating for stricter oversight of the temple of [[affiliation-olvenia|Ólvenia]]'s work in the town, and the woman he means to curb, [[being-palnsnrcth|Pálina Sánriseth]], has the town's poor behind her.

## History {#history}

Herthema has been a fortified town with a garrison for as long as its oldest masters remember: the master who trained the glassworker [[being-vlmrjthrn|Velmar Gútharen]] more than thirty years ago made plain ware for the town's needs, panes for the garrison's windows among them. The sellswords' roll answers to the trade the town breeds, since a Tarvenan expects to go abroad under contract, and the roll says which men may be hired at home.
