## Overview

The Free Territory of Eskarath holds the land of [[place-eskarath|Eskarath]]. The Eskárathi speak their own language—[[skill-eskarilng|Eskari]], a linguistic isolate with no known relatives—maintain their own legal customs, and acknowledge the High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] only in the loosest possible sense. They are fiercely independent even by Tarvenan standards, which is saying a great deal.

## Character

The Eskárathi are not Tarveni. They know it, the Tarveni know it, and both sides have long since agreed to a mutual arrangement: the Eskárathi provide fearsome mountain warriors when the High King calls the ban against a foreign threat, and in return, the rest of Tarvénia leaves them alone. This arrangement has held for centuries, with occasional interruptions when an ambitious High King or Vilérian _therkos_ (governor) tries to impose direct rule—attempts that have invariably ended badly for the outsider; the first Vilérian _therkos_ to try it lost his escort in the high meadows and was not replaced ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]).

Eskari culture is ancient, insular, and deeply rooted in the highland landscape. The people live in scattered mountain communities governed by clan elders, and major decisions affecting the territory as a whole are made by the [[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]]—a council of elders that meets in the high mountain meadow of [[place-gaztorai|Gaztorai]] at the summer solstice. The Biltzarrath has no hall and no seat but the meadow, no bureaucracy, and no written constitution; it operates on oral tradition and consensus, and its decisions carry the weight of centuries of custom.

The Eskárathi nominally honor the gods of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]], but in practice that worship is layered over a much older animistic tradition, [[affiliation-eskrthsprts|The Spirits of Eskarath]]. The highland people venerate mountain spirits, forest guardians, and ancestral figures alongside (or instead of) the Aureldian gods. Shrines to local spirits are found at mountain passes, springs, and ancient oak groves, and the folk practices of the Eskárathi have more in common with the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan traditions]] across the border in [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] than with anything in lowland Tarvénia. This cultural affinity with the Élavendren highlands—and the ethnic distinctiveness from the Tarveni—gives the Eskárathi a liminal quality, belonging fully to neither Tarvénia nor Élavendre.

The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] has never established a presence in Eskarath. The few Arcane Order agents who have ventured into the highlands report that the Eskárathi have their own magical traditions—herb-lore, weather-reading, and a form of ancestral communion—that they practice openly and refuse to submit to outside regulation.

## Current Situation {#present}

The arrangement with the crown stands. When the lords of Tarvénia elect a High King, the Speaker of the Biltzarrath goes up to Lezkora to hear the terms of the crown sworn, because the old arrangement with Eskarath is one of them ([[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]). The terms allow the ban only against an enemy from outside Tarvénia, and the Biltzarrath weighs every call against them before its Host-Caller summons a single warrior. The Thordimalon, which catalogues and licenses magic where it is established, has never established itself here, and the Eskárathi refuse to submit their own traditions to it.

## How It Is Held {#how-it-is-held}

A household holds its own flock and its own people, and its head speaks for them in the valley. The elders of a clan settle what the young dispute, and send some of their number to the Biltzarrath as delegates. Four posts are held from among them: delegates hold the Speaker of the Council, the Host-Caller and the Truce-Warden, and an elder holds the Water-Warden, who keeps the peace between clans over a shared spring.

## Commerce and Currency

Free Territory of Eskarath uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian currency]] (Solden / Ardin / Ogdel) as its standard currency, with full access to the paper-script system through [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]]-member moneylenders chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]]. Free Territory of Eskarath is a post-imperial commonwealth member—it participates fully in the trade and banking infrastructure of the Vilérian system but is not subject to imperial governance or imperial taxation. See [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian Currency]] for the full system.

## Holdings {#holdings}

The clans hold their own valleys, under their own elders. The Biltzarrath sits at [[place-gaztorai|Gaztorai]], a high meadow. The territory's villages include [[place-ibotsain|Ibotsain]] at its spring, [[place-leparrun|Leparrun]] below its pass and [[place-gerdoibun|Gerdoibun]] in its valley of oaks.

## History {#history}

The Eskárathi were in the highlands centuries before the Tarveni came. When the Empire took Tarvénia's lowlands, a _therkos_ sent to rule Eskarath directly lost his escort in the high meadows and was not replaced, and the arrangement the Eskárathi reached afterward with whoever held the lowlands was the one they had always kept ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]). It has held for centuries.
