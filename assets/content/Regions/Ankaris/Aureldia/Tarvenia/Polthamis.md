## Overview {#overview}

Polthamis is a city of [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]] and the home of [[place-grttmplrdvn|the Great Temple of Árdavon]], where the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]] trains the priests it sends out across the kingdom. Tarvenans keep their gods without much clerical supervision and are cool toward the grand temple establishments. The Great Temple is two things to them: the school that makes the faith's priests, and the seal that stands behind those priests' judgments.

## The Great Temple

A boy who comes to the Great Temple as an acolyte studies theology, law and combat together, because the Árdavonian faith expects its priests to be both protectors and spiritual guides. He leaves it ordained to serve Árdavon on the battlefield and in the courts. The temple sends its priests out to mediate disputes and to bring corrupt officials to judgment.

A village priest in Tarvénia is usually a local man, and his village resists any attempt to regularize its shrine, its priest or its festivals. The priests the Great Temple sends out are traveling priests, whose arbitrations carry its seal.

## The Prelate's Seal

The temple's reach outlasts the training. The Árdavonian prelate at Polthamis, Archbishop [[being-thalurozenot|Thaluro Zenothis]], confirms the arbitrations of the faith's traveling priests under seal, and the commissions that send them into cases of noble corruption carry that seal too, along with the protection those cases need from the complaints they draw.

## The Present {#present}

The protection is conditional: it holds only while the work never reaches the royal court. [[being-frstrndrvs|Father Ostaran Dorvás]] trained at the Great Temple and meets the prelate on the register four times a year. His commission against Lord [[being-nizelganotis|Nizel Ganotis]], a noble accused of seizing land unlawfully and oppressing the peasantry, is uncovering a web of corruption that reaches past Lord Nizel, and the further it reaches the nearer it comes to the court where the seal of Polthamis stops.

The prelate is the brother of [[being-zedimazenoth|Zédima Zenothis]], Ostaran's mother. Neither of them states the kinship.
