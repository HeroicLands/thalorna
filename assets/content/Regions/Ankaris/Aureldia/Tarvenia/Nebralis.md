## Overview {#overview}

Nebralis is a port town of about 6,000 on the coast of the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]], growing richer every season on its harbor, and the question its people argue is who the wealth belongs to. The merchant houses that control the harbor trade are taking hold of the town's council and want its institutions made orderly and accountable. The fishing families, the farmers of the country above the town and the priestess who serves them all hold to the older way, in which the free men of the town and its country meet in an assembly and decide what concerns them in common.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The harbor is the town's living. Cargoes come over the quays into the warehouses and go up to the market by cart, and the carters of the [Teamsters' Guild of Nebralis]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"}, chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]], share out the harbor's work among themselves. Ship-victualers supply the vessels in port, and a moneylender in the town holds the notes of farmers and tradesmen alike.

The fishing families work the water off Nebralis and the villages that surround it, and the common fishermen are represented by a fishing cooperative. On the slope above the town, freemen farmers grow wheat and barley for the town's millers on land their families have held past any record. The salter [[being-alvrngrvth|Alvaren Graveth]] runs a large saltworks that supplies salt to several towns of the coast.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The [[affiliation-cnclnbrls|Council of Nebralis]] governs the town, and its aldermen are more and more the men of the harbor's merchant houses. One alderman, [[being-dorandaroort|Dorándaro Orthemandis]], leads the fishing cooperative and speaks for the common fishermen. Beside the council, the free men of the town and its country meet in the [Freemen's Assembly of Nebralis]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, the communal assembly in which they decide matters of common concern, and the farmer [[being-alvrkrnthl|Alvarik Árenthar]] has a voice there and uses it.

The priestess [[being-alethraornet|Alethra Ornetis]] of [[affiliation-lizelas|Lizelas]] holds no office, and her place in Nebralis goes well beyond her rank in the priesthood. She blesses the nets before the season, attends births, prepares the dead and mediates when two families quarrel over water or a boundary, and she is one of the few people in the town whom both the fishing families and the merchant houses respect. The assembly listens when she speaks.

## The Present {#present}

The merchant houses are consolidating their hold on Nebralis, and the old arrangements are giving way under them one by one.

- **The harbor.** A merchant-captain's family has been gathering the harbor trade into its own hands, and he works quietly to undermine Alethra Ornetis, whose standing with the common people stands in the way of the changes he wants.
- **The temple.** A bishop of the formal priesthood, [[being-ornethoilthe|Ornetho Iltheris]], visited Nebralis and was scandalized by Alethra's unstructured way with her duties. He has recommended that she be placed under closer oversight and her temple's funding redirected to formally trained clergy, and the merchant leaders who find her mediation slower than a magistrate's would welcome it.
- **The guild.** The older teamsters have proposed at their guild that a seat may not pass to a daughter, and the vote is next month. If it carries, the teamster [[being-estrsnrcth|Estara Sánriseth]] loses the seat she took on her father's death, her license and the harbor work together.
- **The notes.** The lord of the manor beside Alvarik Árenthar's land disputes his boundary and his water-course, and the lord's steward has bought Alvarik's notes from the Nebralis moneylender. They fall due after harvest.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The bishop's return.** [[being-ornethoilthe|Ornetho Iltheris]] is coming back to Nebralis with orders to reform the temple's work, and [[being-dorandaroort|Dorándaro Orthemandis]] wants a party to learn which merchant houses asked for him before the assembly meets.
- **The cut trace.** [[being-estrsnrcth|Estara Sánriseth]]'s best team bolted on the quay road with a cut trace a month before the guild's vote, and she wants the hand that cut it found and named before the guild.
- **The steward's notes.** [[being-alvrkrnthl|Alvarik Árenthar]] needs his harvest in and sold before the lord's steward can call his notes, and the steward's men have been seen at his water-course.
