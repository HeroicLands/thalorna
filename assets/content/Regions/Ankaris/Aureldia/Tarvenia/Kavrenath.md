Kávrenath is the frontier town of [[place-asturath|Asturath]], ten thousand people behind a wall at the eastern edge of the kingdom, where the last valley road leaves the mountains of [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]] and climbs toward the [[place-kervaron|Kervaron Spine]]. Beyond the Kervaron lies the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]], and everything that crosses between the Empire and Asturath by land comes through Kávrenath or goes round it in the dark. The town is famous for two things: its fortress, and the crossbow companies of the valleys above it.

## The Town

Kávrenath stands where three valleys open into a fourth, on a spur above the meeting of their streams. The town wall runs along the spur's edge, and the fortress holds its eastern end, a square keep and a ring of towers facing the road east. The valleys above the town are narrow and high, with stone hamlets strung along their streams and beech woods on their slopes. The road east follows the largest stream out of the town and climbs for three days toward the Kervaron passes, through country where the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] clans graze their herds and expect a gift from anyone going through.

The town is a market for the valleys and a waystation for the road. Its square holds a weekly market, its inns serve mule trains and companies, and its smiths repair what the road breaks. The valleys send down wool, cheese, chestnuts and men.

## The Fortress and the March

The fortress is the seat of the Warden of the March, the king of Asturath's officer for the eastern frontier, who holds powers of war and justice along the road that the interior does not allow any lord. The present Warden is [Estaril Dómivar]{.name data-type="being/npc"}. The garrison holds the road, the town gate and a chain of watch-posts up the valley toward the passes.

The garrison's work is the traffic over the frontier. Some of it is lawful: Vilérian merchants with steel, glass and cloth who have paid their passage-gifts to the clans in the Kervaron and their toll at Kávrenath's gate. Much of it is not. Smugglers bring goods round the gate by the valley paths. Imperial deserters come over the passes to sell their swords to the companies. In a bad year on the far side of the mountains, refugees come down the road in hundreds, and the Warden must decide what the kingdom owes them before the king has told him.

## The Companies of the Valleys

The valleys above Kávrenath raise crossbow companies, and their men have gone abroad under contract for as long as anyone there remembers. A man of the valleys goes down to the coast in spring by the short way, over the high pass of [[place-zurgando|Zurgando]] at the head of the valleys, and comes home the same way in autumn. Nearly every house in the valleys has a man abroad or has had one.

The valleys keep the dead close. On the night of the lanterns every house sets an _ezkobe_ (lantern for the dead) on its threshold, and the valleys tell [[lore-lightatfront|The Light at the Front]] after the lanterns are lit. When Kávrenath sings [[lore-lowerendsong|The Song of the Lower End]] at its fires, it sings the asking at the plateau gate twice and holds the third for the end of the evening, a habit the town keeps against every other valley in Asturath.

## Who Rules

Kávrenath belongs to the [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]]. The Warden of the March governs the fortress, the road and the frontier. The town has an assembly of its householders that keeps the wall, the market and the streets, and each valley above the town has a mountain lord who holds it and judges in it. Where the Warden's law of the road and a valley lord's law of his valley meet, they quarrel, and the quarrel goes to the king.

## Hooks

- **The deserters' price.** A band of imperial deserters has come over the passes with good armor and a captain, and they want to sign on with a valley company that already has one.
- **The refugee road.** Two hundred people are camped below the town gate. The Warden has orders to send them back over the Kervaron, and the orders are a month old.
- **A toll unpaid.** A Vilérian merchant swears he paid his gift to the clans and his toll at the gate. The clans say he paid neither, and the Warden's clerk has a receipt.
