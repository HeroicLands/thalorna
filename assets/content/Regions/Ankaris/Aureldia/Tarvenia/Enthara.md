## Overview

Enthara is a town of terraces. It climbs the south face of the eastern hills above the [Thalsira]{.name data-type="place/feature"} valley in tiers of vineyard walls, each a man's height of dry stone holding up a strip of vines, and the town itself sits halfway up on the one shelf wide enough to build on, with the press-house of its lord at the top of the street and the market square below it. About 4,000 people live there, and its name is the word for what they make: _entho_ is wine.

It is the wine town of the [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]] and the largest of the market towns of the eastern hills, a day's cart-ride above [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]] by the hill road. The soft yellow rock of the hill is easy to cut, and under the town lie the cellars, tunnel after tunnel of them, some older than the town above. A house's wealth in Enthara is reckoned by how far back into the hill its cellar runs.

## The Year of the Vine

The vintage in early autumn sets the town's calendar. For three weeks every hand in the hills picks, from children to the old men who come down from the hill farms, and the carts of grapes go up the terraces to the press-houses in a line that does not stop from dawn to dark. The town's priest blesses the first press of the year before anything is trodden, and the new wine is in the cellars before the carts go down to the autumn wine market at Torvaleth.

The rest of the year belongs to the terraces. A vineyard wall that falls in the winter rains takes its strip of vines down the hill with it, and the work of rebuilding walls through the wet months is the work that keeps a grower's family on its terraces. The weekly market in the square trades the hill's other goods, oil, honey, chestnuts and goats, and in a lean year it is the place where growers sell cellar room to their neighbors.

## The Vintage Sold on the Vine

What Torvaleth's wine market does not take of Enthara's wine goes down to the river and by barge to [[place-bruthena|Bruthena]], and from there by cart to [[place-valthari|Valthari]], where it is shipped across the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]]. The merchant houses of Valthari buy most of it before it is picked. Their factors come up the hill road in spring, when the growers have least money and most wall to rebuild, and pay for the coming vintage in advance, at a price struck in the square and witnessed before the priest. In a good year the grower is paid twice, once in spring and once in the sale of what the advance did not cover. In a bad year, when the hail or the rains take the vintage, he owes Valthari wine he does not have, and the debt rolls over to the next year at interest.

Half the terraces of Enthara are in debt to one Valthari house or another, and the town is bitter about it. The growers call the advance "the Valthari chain," and they say so in the square in front of the factors, who have heard it before.

## Who Governs {#who-rules}

Enthara is held of the king by a lord whose house has held it since the kingdom was founded. The present lord has been under contract with a company in the east for six years, and his wife, [[being-atsekavolke|Átseka Volkemis]], runs the town in his name: she holds the market charter, sits at the press-house when the vintage comes in, and answers to the crown's bailiff for the dues. Every grower in Enthara knows whose word settles a dispute over a boundary wall, and the documents all carry her husband's name. The crown's share of the vintage is taken at the press-houses in wine, and the lord's at the same time; the factor of a Valthari house is usually standing in the yard while both are measured.

:::secret
**For the GM:** The lady of Enthara has been quietly buying the debts of the poorest terraces from the Valthari houses at a discount, with money her husband sends home from his contract. If she holds enough of them she can break the advance in Enthara by setting her own price at the next spring, and the Valthari houses that notice it first are already asking who in the town is selling to her.
:::

## The Present {#present}

The advance came up the hill road this spring as it does every spring, and this year the wine has nowhere to go. The seamen of [[place-valthari|Valthari]] refuse to sign on until the ship-owning houses raise the crew's share, the port's ships lie idle, and the wine that went down to Bruthena by barge waits there and at the port for a hull. The factors still want the vintage the advance bought. The growers say a house that cannot ship the wine it holds has no business buying more of it, and the terraces that owe the most are the ones that cannot refuse.
