## Overview

The Kingdom of Galvaren holds the land of [[place-galvaren|Galvaren]], the fog-bound south-western shore of [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]]. It is a kingdom of fishermen governed half by fishermen: its king rules from the castle above the harbor of [[place-durvolka|Durvolka]], and he takes his counsel at a table where the shipmasters of the harbors sit facing the lords of the inland valleys. The Galváreni are seafarers, fishermen and storytellers, with a reputation for mysticism that sets them apart from the more pragmatic Tarvenans of the interior.

## The King and the Long Table

The crown belongs to the house of Ervothandis and passes to the late king's eldest son, or failing sons to his eldest daughter. The present king, [[being-ganothervo|Ganoth Ervothandis]], came to the throne in 712 AF. He owes the High King of Tarvénia his presence at an election and his levy when the ban is called, and he owes him nothing else. The office of Queen is held only when the crown has passed to a daughter, and it stands empty while a king reigns.

The king governs through the [Long Table]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, the royal council, which meets in the hall of the Headland at Durvolka four times a year and whenever the king summons it. The landed lords of the valleys sit along one side by right of their lordships. The shipmasters sit along the other, elected each autumn by the rolls of masters of the harbors: three from Durvolka, two from [[place-ulhera|Ulhera]], and one from each lesser harbor, about a dozen in all. A master keeps his seat for the year and stands again if he wants it.

The Table advises, and the king decides, but a king who decides against both sides of the Table at once seldom does it twice. The two sides want different things. The lords want the crown's attention for their valleys, its justice for their tenants and its peace with the [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]] for their cattle trade. The masters want a lower price for salt, a lighter due on the catch and a king who will send galleys against the pirates on the southern salt route. The king's own revenue comes from the masters' side of the Table: the dues on hulls and catch, the lamp-due, and above all the salt, which the crown buys by sea and sells to the curing houses at its own price. So the masters pay for the crown and the lords hold its land, and every king of Galvaren governs by keeping the two sides arguing with each other instead of with him.

## Officers of the Crown

The great officers hold their posts at the king's pleasure. The Keeper of the Seal issues the king's writs and charters. The Keeper of the Salt buys and sells the kingdom's salt, and is the most resented man on the coast. The Master of the Fleet commands the king's six galleys at Durvolka and, in war, every fishing boat the coast can put to sea. The Marshal of the Ban musters the valleys when the High King calls the ban, and a Galvaren levy is mostly valley freemen with spears and coastal men with crossbows and boat-axes.

On the coast the crown's officers are harbor officers. The Warden of the Strand holds Durvolka's harbor and judges its waterfront; a Harbor Reeve holds each lesser harbor for the king; the Warden of Wrecks claims for the crown what the sea brings ashore unclaimed; and the Keeper of the Lamp keeps the Long Lamp at [[place-ezkoba|Ezkoba]] on a due paid by every boat between Ezkoba and Durvolka. The Warden of Wrecks is the officer who most often quarrels with the harbor tellers of the Drowned Road, because a wreck's goods go to the crown only once its crew is counted dead, and the teller decides when that is.

## Character

Where the [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]] looks inward to its mountains and Lyenrik to its fields, Galvaren looks outward to the sea. Galváreni sailors are legendarily fearless or foolhardy, by the account of whoever is telling it, and the kingdom produces a disproportionate number of the navigators, sea captains and explorers of the Vilérian Sea. Most of them learned their trade on an Ulhera cod boat, where a master's book of soundings, currents and landmarks lets him sail out of sight of land for days and find the same bank, or the same unknown island, again.

Its coast keeps the Drowned Road beside the pantheon's temples, and its courts take a widow's word from a harbor teller. The coast is crowded with standing stones, stone rings and passage graves older than the Tarveni migration, and the Drowned Road holds that the sea keeps its drowned, that they come home in the fog to those graves, and that a lamp in the seaward window shows them the door. Ghost stories and tales of drowned cities are taken more seriously here than anywhere else in Tarvénia, and the priests of the faiths of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] tolerate the coast's customs with varying degrees of patience. No other court in Tarvénia accepts a teller's calling-in as proof that a man is dead.

## Relations

Galvaren and Lyenrik are rivals and each other's best customers. Lyenrik's grain and cattle feed the coast, Galvaren's salt fish feeds Lyenrik's villages through the winter, and the two crowns quarrel every few years over the tolls on the one road between them. Galvaren depends on the southern coast for its salt, which makes the salt ships and the [[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]] that sells to them the kingdom's most important foreign concern. Asturath is distant and mountainous, and the two kingdoms have little trade and few quarrels.

## Current Situation {#present}

[[being-ganothervo|Ganoth Ervothandis]] has held the crown since 712 AF, and the Long Table is divided over salt. The Keeper of the Salt has raised the crown's price for the second year running, the curing-house mistresses of Durvolka's Strand have begun buying from a [[place-valthari|Valthari]] ship that anchors outside the bay at night, and [[being-korvaththal|Korvath Thaluris]] of [[place-ulhera|Ulhera]] is the loudest voice at the Table against the price. [[being-brazemabetsu|Brazema Betsuris]], the Master of the Fleet, wants her galleys sent against the pirates on the southern salt route, and the king, who is careful with the crown's money, has not given the order.

At [[place-ezkoba|Ezkoba]], [[being-nelmaular|Nelma Ularris]], the senior harbor teller of the [[affiliation-drownedroad|Drowned Road]], is failing and has not named who will keep the coast's rolls after her. The courts of the kingdom take a teller's calling-in as proof of death, so the choice of her successor is also a choice about who is a widow.

## Commerce and Currency

The Kingdom of Galvaren uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian currency]] (Solden / Ardin / Ogdel) as its standard currency, with full access to the paper-script system through [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]]-member moneylenders chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]]. The Kingdom of Galvaren is a post-imperial commonwealth member—it participates fully in the trade and banking infrastructure of the Vilérian system but is not subject to imperial governance or imperial taxation. See [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian Currency]] for the full system.

## History {#history}

### The Old Stones

The standing stones, stone rings and passage graves of the coast are older than the Tarveni migration, and nobody on the coast knows who raised them. The beliefs the coast built around them, that the drowned come home in the fog to the graves and that a lamp in the seaward window shows them the door, became the [[affiliation-drownedroad|Drowned Road]], which the coast has kept beside the temples of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] since.

### The Crown and the Table

The house of Ervothandis holds the crown, and it passes to the late king's eldest son, or failing sons to his eldest daughter. The Long Table puts the landed lords of the valleys on one side and the elected shipmasters of the harbors on the other, and every king of Galvaren has governed by keeping the two sides arguing with each other. When the occupied lowlands stopped paying the Empire and the mountain lords elected the first High King ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]), the electors of the High King came to be the lords of the three kingdoms, and Galvaren owes an election its presence and the High King its levy when the ban is called.

### The Present Reign (from 712 AF)

Ganoth Ervothandis came to the throne in 712 AF. His reign has been a careful one, spent in keeping the crown solvent on the price of salt.
