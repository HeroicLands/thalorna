## Overview {#overview}

Ornetho is the gray city in a red country. Every other town of [[place-leonrik|Lyenrik]] is built of the brick the lowland clay makes, and Ornetho is built of cut stone hauled down from the mountains it stands under, in a ring of walls so tall and so straight that they show as a gray line across the green for half a day's ride up the [Thalsira]{.name data-type="place/feature"} valley. The city takes its name from what stands behind it: _orneth_ is a mountain as it is seen from below, and the peaks of [[place-asturath|Asturath]] fill the sky over its north wall.

About 30,000 people live inside and around those walls, which makes Ornetho the second city of the [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]] after [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]]. It stands where the Thalsira comes out of its gorge onto the plain and where the old imperial road leaves the plain to climb toward the passes, so everything that goes between the mountains and the lowlands goes through one of its gates.

## The Legions' City

The [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] built Ornetho to hold the lowlands it had taken ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]), and it built it against the mountains. The garrison of the march lived here for four centuries, and the city still has the Empire's plan: two straight streets that cross at the _emba_ (town square), a grid of blocks between them, the old barracks along the north wall, an _ulmerpodera_ (aqueduct) that brings water down from a spring in the gorge, and a _thubla_ (bathhouse) the city still uses. The first tower of the chain of watchtowers that runs down the Thalsira to the sea stands over the north gate. It was built to warn the plain of a rising in the mountains; when the garrisons were driven out ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]) the Liénriki turned the rest of the chain to face the coast, and left this one looking at the mountains.

The Astúrathi have not forgotten whom the walls were built to keep out, and the city has not forgotten either. One rule of the occupation is still kept at every gate: no armed company of more than twelve passes the walls. A gate captain of long service puts it to a mountain lord's men at the north gate in the same words every time: "Twelve of you may come in, with your swords, and the rest may camp in the meadow by the river. The legions made the rule and we kept it, and it holds for the King of Lyenrik's own companies the same as for yours."

## Where the Mountains Meet the Plain

Ornetho lives by the exchange of two countries. Down the road from the passes come Astúrathi wool and fleeces, hard mountain _betsur_ (cheese), hides, and iron ore and bar iron from the mountain workings; up it go Liénriki grain, wine, olive oil and cloth. The wool merchants and the iron merchants are the two great guilds of the city and its two great rivals, and their halls face each other across the square.

The iron is worked here. The smiths of Ornetho make most of the crossbows, mail and blades that Lyenrik's lords buy for their companies, and the forges along the river below the south wall keep [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]]'s festival of the hearth with more ceremony than any temple of the plain. A company signing men in the lowlands sends to Ornetho for its arms, and many young men of the plain see their first mountain from the yard of an Ornetho smith, waiting for a crossbow they will carry abroad.

Ornetho is also the kingdom's door to the mountains for people. Drovers bring down the lean cattle that the meadows around Torvaleth fatten; Astúrathi second sons come down looking for a company; shepherds bring the flocks down in autumn to winter on the stubble of the plain. The meadow by the river where armed companies camp is the city's busiest ground in spring and autumn, and the inns along it take the mountain trade.

## Who Governs {#present}

Ornetho is a royal city, ruled by the crown through the Castellan of Ornetho, who holds the walls, the gates and their tolls, and keeps the garrison. By custom the castellancy goes to a son or a brother of the reigning king, and it is the post a king of Lyenrik gives the kinsman he most wants to keep busy and away from his own court. The present castellan is the king's brother, [[being-hiltheroltr|Hilther Oltris]], who has held the walls for twelve years. The castellan's court sits in the old barracks. The guilds govern their own trades and the market's daily business, and the quarrel between the wool hall and the iron hall over who sits nearer the castellan at the feasts is older than anyone now living in either.

The gates are the city's quarrel now. The senior watch commander of the gates, [[being-hldndrgn|Haldena Durgana]], has seized the contraband of the carter and smuggler [[being-garrutsagalt|Garrutsa Galtsuregi]] again and again, and every time his goods come back to him on appeal through the courts at [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]]. He has offered her a bribe himself, and his carts still come through a gate she does not hold. At the north gate an old knight-errant, [[being-kotsudekanar|Kotsude Kanarris]], watches the companies march out, and asks after a stolen thing he will not describe ([[miscgear-legatesroll|The Legate's Roll]]).

The city's faith keeps a soldier's shape. [[lore-ardavondty|Árdavon]] of gates and oaths has his largest temple of the kingdom at the north gate, built into the wall beside the tower, and every company that marches out through it stops for the priest's blessing on the contract.

:::secret
**For the GM:** The vaults under the old barracks still hold the archive of the imperial march: the garrison's registers and the records of the Empire's courts, including the lists of the Liénriki lords who took the Empire's offices and those who refused them. The castellans have kept the vaults locked since the occupation ended, and the [Court of Pleas]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} at Torvaleth does not know how much survives. A record from these vaults could raise a house on the [Roll of Houses]{.name data-type="lore"} or ruin one, and several houses would pay a great deal to know what is in them before anyone else does.
:::

## History {#history}

The Empire built Ornetho after it took the lowlands about 140 AF ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]), and it was the garrison city of the occupied march for four centuries, the seat from which the Empire governed Lyenrik and watched the mountains. When the lowlands stopped paying and the garrisons were driven out ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]), the new kings of Lyenrik took the city whole, kept the legions' rule of twelve at its gates and turned every tower of the chain but the north gate's to face the coast. The castellancy has gone to a son or a brother of the king since the kingdom was founded.
