## Overview {#overview}

Ithrathorga is the chain of watchtowers that runs the length of the [Thalsira]{.name data-type="place/feature"} in [[place-leonrik|Lyenrik]], from the tower over [[place-ronetho|Ornetho]]'s north gate to the castle tower at [[place-bruthena|Bruthena]]. Its name is _ithra_ (a lamp) and _thorga_ (a tower), the lamp towers, for the lamps that burn in their windows. There are some twenty towers, each within sight of the next and each with a beacon on its roof, and a fire lit at Bruthena passes up the whole chain to Ornetho in a single night.

The [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] built the towers to give warning of a rising in the mountains. When the garrisons were driven out, the first kings of [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Lyenrik]] turned them to face the coast, against an empire that might come back by sea. From almost any field in Lyenrik a gray tower stands against the sky.

## Inside {#inside}

**A tower.** Each is a square tower of gray imperial stone three floors high, on a bluff, a hillock or a bend of the river where the next tower can be seen. The door is on the first floor and reached by a ladder the keeper draws up at night. The ground floor is a storeroom for grain and wood; the first floor is the keeper's living room, with a hearth; the top floor is the watch-room, with a window on each face and the lamp in the window toward the river. On the roof stands the beacon: an iron basket on a stone plinth, with a stack of dry wood and pitch beside it under a hide.

**The chain**, from the mountains to the sea:

- **[1] The North Gate Tower** over Ornetho's north gate, the head of the chain under the mountains, kept by the castellan's men.
- **[2] The towers of the upper river**, from the mouth of the gorge to [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]], along the Tower Road, where the river runs between hedged fields.
- **[3] The Torvaleth towers**, two, one on each bank at the ford of the capital.
- **[4] The towers of the lower river**, from Torvaleth to the marsh, on the bluffs above the barge channel and the reed channels. One of them is kept by [[being-kaldralizel|Kaldra Lizelis]], whose window looks over the reed channels below the bridge.
- **[5] The Castle Tower** at Bruthena, on the south bank, the foot of the chain and the tower that lights first.

## Who Is There {#who-is-there}

- **The Warden of the Towers**, [[being-orthemoulmis|Orthemo Ulmis]], castellan of Bruthena and its bridge, who commands the chain from the castle tower and answers to the king.
- **The Towerkeepers**, one family to each tower, holding it by inheritance. They keep the beacon ready and the lamp lit, keep the watch through the night and take the light-due, a measure of grain from every village in sight of their light. The Warden cannot remove a Towerkeeper whose tower is kept.
- **The villages in sight of a light**, which pay the light-due and grumble about it.

## The Lamps and the Due {#present}

The beacons have one message, which is that the kingdom is called out. A fire on any tower calls every lord of Lyenrik to the muster, and no fire has been lit on the chain in living memory. What the towers watch from night to night is the river: the barges, the reed channels below the bridge where the marsh smugglers move goods past the toll, and the coast road.

The light-due is the chain's standing quarrel. The villages call it a tax, the Towerkeepers call it a payment for being warned, and a village that pays late says its tower's lamp was dark on some night or other. This spring the quarrel has a second side. The grain and wine of the plain lie in the warehouses at Bruthena while [[place-valthari|Valthari]]'s seamen refuse to sail, the villages have less to spare, and the Warden is short of the toll the bridge would bring.

King [[being-zenotholtr|Zenoth Oltris]] means to stand at the next election of a High King, and the old High King is at [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]], in the mountains up the road from Ornetho. The king has asked the Warden whether the chain could carry word of the High King's death down from Ornetho in a night. Orthemo Ulmis has answered that a fire on the chain calls out the kingdom and says nothing else.

## History {#history}

The legions built the chain while they held the lowlands ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]), from about 140 AF, with the walled towns and the bridge at Bruthena. Their towers faced north, and their beacons were meant to bring the garrisons out against the mountains. When the lowlands stopped paying the Empire from about 538 AF and the mountain lords elected a High King to drive the garrisons out ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]), the Liénriki took the towers whole and turned them to face the coast. The first kings of Lyenrik gave each tower to a family to keep by inheritance, with the light-due for its keep, and raised the castle at Bruthena to hold the chain's foot and the bridge.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **A dark night.** A village in sight of Kaldra Lizelis's tower has paid no grain and says the beacon was dark the night the marsh flooded. Kaldra wants to know who told it so.
- **The second message.** Orthemo Ulmis wants to know who in the king's household first asked whether the chain could carry news.
- **A boat in the reeds.** A boat lies sunk in the channel under a lower-river tower with its cargo still in it, and its Towerkeeper wants it raised before the Warden's boatmen find it.
