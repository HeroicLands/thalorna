## Overview {#overview}

Kemthoza is the fort that holds the head of [Grumagando]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the war pass, on the side of Tarvénia's [[place-westernmrch|Western March]]. Its name means "the walling", from _kemtho_ (a wall). It is the last Tarvenan roof before the saddle, and the road the March's hosts take down into [[place-talothe|the Talote]] runs under its gate. The baron of the valley below keeps it, and in the season it is where the March's hosts gather before they go down.

## Inside {#inside}

- **[1] The Gate Tower:** a square tower over the road, the only way through the fort's wall. The road passes under it, and a host going down to the pass files through it one rank at a time.
- **[2] The Yard:** the walled ground behind the gate, wide enough to hold a valley's host with its mules. Stairs from the yard climb to the wall-walk on the pass side.
- **[3] The Wall-Walk:** the wall facing the saddle, from which the watch sees the snow on the pass and, on still nights in the season, hears [[place-bldfldmalnebris|Malnebris]] on the road below the far side.
- **[4] The Hall:** a long stone room against the back wall, where the garrison eats and sleeps through the winter, and where the barons meet before the season opens. Its door opens on the yard.
- **[5] The Oath Stone:** a _goitsa_ (oath stone) in the yard, older than the walls, at which a host swears before it goes down and answers for its dead when it comes back.

## Who Is There {#who-is-there}

- **The baron's men**, a handful who hold the fort through the winter, when the passes are shut and the fort is snowed in with them.
- **The hosts of the March**, from the snow's going to its return, gathering in the yard before they go over and resting there when they come back.
- **A village priest from the valley below**, who comes up to say the rites for the dead a host brings home.

## The Present {#present}

The fort watches the saddle for the snow's going, because the first host over Grumagando sets the terms of the season. On the far side the walls of [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] hold the road down from the pass, and the Talote's muster gathers there when the snow clears. The dead of the first raid lie on that road unreceived, and every host that goes down from Kemthoza passes them.
