## Overview {#overview}

**Valthari** is everything the mountain lords of [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Asturath]] distrust: wealthy, cosmopolitan and thoroughly mercantile. This free city of 45,000 on the sandy southern coast of the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]] is the richest single settlement in the kingdom and Tarvénia's window onto distant trade. Its wealth comes from its position as the gateway for seaborne trade into Tarvénia. Wine, olive oil, grain, wool and iron flow out; spices, silk, luxury goods and coin flow in.

The [[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]] governs it through the [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]], the council of its great merchant houses, under a charter bought from the High King. The question the city lives with is the price of that charter, which every new High King swears to keep, and the present one has not come down to his hall for a month.

## Arriving {#arriving}

Most people come to Valthari by sea, and the sea makes them wait. The coast is sand, and a bar lies across the harbor mouth that shifts with every winter's storms. No master takes a ship over it without a pilot of the [Pilots' Guild of Valthari]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"}, which examines every pilot who works the bar, and in the month of the worst winter weather the pilots do not sail at all. Deep-laden ships anchor in the roads outside and send their cargo in by lighter. Every hull that crosses the bar pays the Harbormaster's dues.

By land the road comes down from [[place-bruthena|Bruthena]], a day's ride north across the edge of the salt marsh, the last stretch of the old imperial road from the lowlands of [[place-leonrik|Lyenrik]].

The city is loud and all its tongues talk at once. [[skill-provnzlng|Provenzal]] is spoken widely beside [[skill-tarvenlng|Tarveni]], and on the quays everybody speaks the harbor pidgin [[skill-valtarlng|Valtari]], which is named for this port. A stranger's quarrel with a house is heard at [[place-charterhall|the Charter Hall]].

## Politics {#present}

The Konsezarath is drawn from the dozen or so merchant dynasties that control the city's trading houses, [[place-vlthrshpyrds|shipyards]] and banks, and its seats are fiercely contested: Valthari politics is a dance of commercial alliances, marriage pacts and, occasionally, discreet assassination. Its First this term is [[being-orthemaiken|Orthema Ikentsis]], whose house ships wine and oil to the Taruzelian ports. Three quarrels fill the square this season.

- **The charter.** If the High King dies, his successor swears the terms of the crown at [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]], the city's charter among them, and is paid the year's tribute. The council cannot agree whether to pay that and no more or to back the likeliest lord, and every rider up the road from the city is watched.
- **The share.** The seamen of Valthari refuse to sign on until the ship-owning houses raise the crew's share. The owners' council, the houses that own ships meeting apart, has asked the pilot [[being-dalenavlrn|Dálena Valaron]] to sail with her loyal crew and break the refusal. Her house's own ship is mortgaged to one of those houses ([[place-durgalis|Dúrgalis]]).
- **The channel.** A Valthari house has again proposed cutting a channel through the salt marsh deep enough for barges to reach the port without passing the King of Lyenrik's toll at Bruthena, and the king has again declined to allow the digging on his side.

## Order and Law {#order-and-law}

The watch, the _zórala_, keeps the streets and the quays under the Captain of the Watch, and the chartered trades raise militia for the walls. The city keeps a small, professional navy to guard its shipping lanes, and its marine soldiers are among the best equipped in Tarvénia. The charter gives the city its own courts, and they sit at the Charter Hall: the Arbitrators settle disputes between houses under the commercial custom, and the Warden of the Weights fines short weight and false coin in the markets. The heaviest sentence the city passes is to strike a man from its roll, which leaves him no standing in its courts and voids every contract he holds.

## What the City Lives By {#what-the-city-lives-by}

Valthari buys most of what [[place-leonrik|Lyenrik]] sells to the world, at prices Valthari sets: the grain and the cattle of the plain and the wine of [[place-enthara|Enthara]], which the houses buy on the vine. Galváreni ships bring salt fish to the city and carry salt home, and the salt comes from the city's own pans at [[place-threskon|Threskon]]. The houses sell all of it across the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] to [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia]], [[place-helionis|Helionis]] and the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]], and the dockside markets trade in goods from all over [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión]].

The city also sells men. The hiring yards behind the quays are where the Tarvenan companies sign on for service abroad, and a captain from the high valleys is "of the Gray Highlands" in them ([[place-grayhghlnds|Gray Highlands]]). Its banks are the [[affiliation-danvar|Danvar]]-member moneylenders chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]], and the paper script of the Vilérian system passes in its counting-houses.

## Quarters {#quarters}

- **The quays** run along the harbor wall: the customs house, the warehouses of gray stone bound with black iron, the cookshops and the lighters. The great houses keep their counting-houses on the streets behind.
- **[[place-vlthrtrzlqrtr|The Taruzeli Quarter]]** is where the Taruzeli merchants keep their own warehouses, their temple and their factors' hall.
- **[[place-vlthrshpyrds|The Shipyards]]** stand along the inner harbor, where the houses' hulls and the city's galleys are built.
- **The square and [[place-charterhall|the Charter Hall]]** are the city's government: the Konsezarath meets in the open square, and the courts sit in the hall.

The city's architecture blends Tarvenan stone-and-iron practicality with Provénzian elegance.

## Faith and Festivals {#faith-and-festivals}

The city keeps the faiths of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. Its seamen keep the [[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pozeras]] of voyages and the [[affiliation-brazemas|Faith of Brazemas]] of storms, and the Taruzeli keep their own temples in their quarter.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The inns of the quay streets are many, loud and cheap, and full of seamen and men waiting for a contract. Merchants lodge with the factors of the houses they deal with, and a Taruzeli traveler lodges in the Taruzeli Quarter.

## History {#history}

The legions took the southern coast about 140 AF and made Valthari an imperial port, and its harbor and its crowd of tongues became its fortune ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]). The harbor pidgin of the whole inner sea took its first fixed form on its quays. When the lowlands fell away from the Empire, about 538 to 545, the first High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] had no silver to pay the lords who had elected him and sold the port a charter of self-government for a yearly tribute ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]). The Konsezarath has governed it under that charter ever since.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The strike-breaker.** [[being-dalenavlrn|Dálena Valaron]] wants to know whether the house that holds her father's note will call it if she refuses the owners' council, and the seamen want to know which way she will sail.
- **The quiet factor.** A factor of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] in Valthari hires guards who will not ask why the town has gone quiet around him.
- **The marsh survey.** The house that wants the channel hires a party to survey a line through the marsh, and the marsh people who carry the smugglers' goods do not want it surveyed ([[place-bruthena|Bruthena]]).
