## Overview {#overview}

The shipyards of [[place-valthari|Valthari]] stand along the inner harbor, out of the swell, on the sand behind the harbor wall: a row of slipways, sawpits, rope-walks and timber stacks, with the smell of tar and new oak over all of it. The great merchant houses build their hulls here, and the city builds the galleys of its small navy. A ship launched here is built to cross the harbor bar, broad and shallow in the hull, so that it can be beached on the sand of the coast and floated off again on the next tide.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The yards belong to the houses. Each great house of the [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]] that owns ships owns or leases a slipway, and a house's yard is part of its fortune, held as closely as its counting-house. The city's galleys are built on slips the council leases from the houses. The shipwrights are a chartered trade of the city, and their Guild Master speaks for them before the council.

## The Yards' Layers {#history}

The yards are as old as the harbor, and nobody in the city dates the oldest slipway. They built for the Empire while Valthari was an imperial port ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]), and for the houses since the city bought its charter ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]).

## What Is There Now {#present}

Half the slips are idle. The seamen of Valthari refuse to sign on until the ship-owning houses raise the crew's share, the ships already built lie at anchor, and the houses have stopped laying down new keels they cannot crew. The shipwrights are not part of the refusal and are losing a season's wages to it, and their Guild Master is pressing the houses and the seamen alike to settle.

One house is still building. On its slip is a fast hull with long lines and clever rigging, of a kind the Taruzelian design house of [[affiliation-rusakuni|the Rusakuni]] draws and sells: a yard that builds to Rusakuni lines buys the drawings and pays the house a fee for every hull, and the Rusakuni send a master to see the first one laid down. Nobody in Valthari remembers that house buying drawings.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

- **[1] The slipways.** A row of them running down into the inner harbor, each a house's, with the hulls on them in every stage from keel to fitting-out.
- **[2] The timber yard.** Seasoning oak and pine stacked under thatch, bought by sea, and the sawpits beside it.
- **[3] The rope-walk.** A long shed where the ropemakers walk the strands out and twist them into cable.
- **[4] The galley slips.** The city's own, where the navy's galleys are hauled up for the winter and its marines drill on the sand in front of them.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The copied hull.** The Rusakuni master in [[place-vlthrtrzlqrtr|the Taruzeli Quarter]] wants proof that the hull on the house's slip is built to his house's lines, and the house wants whoever is asking stopped.
- **The idle season.** The shipwrights' Guild Master hires a party to find out which of the ship-owning houses would settle with the seamen first, if it were not afraid of the others.
