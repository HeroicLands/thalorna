## Overview

The Biltzarrath is the common council of the [[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]: a council of elders drawn from the highland clans, which decides the matters that touch the whole territory. It meets once a year, at the summer solstice, in the high meadow of [[place-gaztorai|Gaztorai]]. It has no permanent seat beyond the meadow, no hall, no clerks and no written constitution. It works by oral tradition and by consensus, and what it decides carries the weight of centuries of custom.

## Who Sits {#who-sits}

Each clan sends its elders to the meadow as delegates. A delegate may argue for his clan and may not bind it.

Speaker of the Council
: Presides over the sitting and keeps the order of speaking. When the lords of Tarvénia elect a High King, the Speaker goes up to Lezkora to hear the terms of the crown sworn, because the old arrangement with Eskarath is one of them ([[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]).

Lawkeeper
: Recites the customs the clans hold in common, and gives the sitting the answer where two clans' customs differ.

Truce-Warden
: Holds the peace of the meadow while the council sits, and enforces it.

Keeper of the Feud
: Reckons the blood owed and the blood paid between kindreds. Without his count no feud can be settled.

Host-Caller
: Summons the common muster when the council agrees that a threat concerns every clan.

Envoy
: Carries the council's word to a settled power, and is protected by custom while he carries it.

## How It Decides {#how-it-decides}

The council sits in the open meadow under the Truce-Warden's peace. The Speaker gives each delegate his turn, and the Lawkeeper is heard whenever custom is in question. A matter is settled when the delegates agree; there is no vote and no count. A delegate cannot bind his clan, so each carries the decision home, and it holds there because custom holds it.

The council takes up what no one clan can settle alone: the muster when the High King calls the ban, feuds between kindreds that their own elders have not ended, a difference of custom between two clans, and whatever word is to be sent to a settled power.

## What It Is Fighting About {#present}

**The ban.** The Eskárathi send their warriors when the High King calls the ban against a foreign threat, and the rest of Tarvénia leaves them alone in return. The terms a High King swears allow the ban only against an enemy from outside Tarvénia. Every call that reaches the meadow is weighed against those terms before the Host-Caller summons anyone, and a High King who reaches for direct rule over the highlands meets a council that has refused every one of his predecessors who tried.

**The Thordimalon.** The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] has never established a presence in Eskarath, and its agents in the highlands find the Eskárathi practicing their herb-lore, weather-reading and ancestral communion in the open. The council refuses to submit any of it to outside regulation ([[affiliation-eskrthsprts|The Spirits of Eskarath]]).

**Blood owed.** The feuds between kindreds come to the meadow when their own elders cannot end them, and the sitting does not close until the Keeper of the Feud has given his count.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

A stranger who comes up to Gaztorai at the solstice comes under the Truce-Warden's peace, and a petition reaches the council through a delegate who will speak it. The Envoy meets a party wherever the council has business with a settled power, and the Speaker can be found at Lezkora when a High King is made.
