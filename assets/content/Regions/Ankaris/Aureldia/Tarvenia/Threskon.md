## Overview {#overview}

Threskon is the salt town of the [[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]], a harbor of about 2,500 on the sandy southern coast of the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]], a day's sail along the coast from [[place-valthari|Valthari]]. Its name is the salt shore. A long line of dunes stands between the sea and a shallow lagoon, and on the flats around the lagoon the town's pans make the salt that [[place-galvaren|Galvaren]] cannot make for itself, because its sun is too weak. Every cask of Galváreni herring is salted from somewhere on this coast, and most of it from Threskon. That makes the price of salt the town's whole politics: the crown of Galvaren buys it and sells it on to the coast's curing houses at the crown's price, and the curing houses have started buying it without the crown.

## Arriving {#arriving}

A ship comes into Threskon through a channel cut across the dunes, which the town digs out again every spring after the winter storms have half filled it, and lies in the lagoon on a flat calm. The town stands on the lagoon side of the dunes: the salt sheds, the quay, a temple of [[affiliation-pozeras|Pozeras]], and the low whitewashed houses of the panmen. Beyond it the pans run out across the flats in squares, gray water in spring and white in late summer, and the road to Valthari runs along the back of the dunes.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Salt, _thres_. The lagoon water is let into the shallow pans in spring and left to the sun, moved from pan to pan as it thickens, and raked off as salt from midsummer to the first autumn rains. The great houses of Valthari own the pans, and the panmen who work them are residents of the city's register, not freemen of it. The salt goes out in two ways: to Valthari's warehouses on carts along the dune road, and by sea in the salt ships that sail for Galvaren in late summer and come back with salt fish.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]] governs Threskon through the Reeve of Threskon, the head of one of the houses that own pans, whom the council names each year. The Reeve keeps the town's order, takes its dues for the city, and settles disputes over pans and water; anything larger goes to the courts at [[place-charterhall|the Charter Hall]]. The town has no council of its own and no walls, and the city's watch keeps a small post on the quay.

## The Present {#present}

The salt price is the town's quarrel this year. The Keeper of the Salt at [[place-durvolka|Durvolka]] has raised the crown of Galvaren's price for the second year running, and the curing-house mistresses there have begun to buy from a salt ship that anchors outside their bay at night and sells to them directly. The crown's buyers have complained to the Konsezarath that a Valthari ship is selling past them, and the Reeve has been told to find out whose. The masters of Threskon's salt ships say they sell only at the crown's quay, and the Reeve has no way to prove otherwise from a lagoon a long sail from Durvolka.

:::secret {title="The night ship"}
The salt ship that anchors outside Durvolka's bay sails from Threskon, and it carries the salt of the Reeve's own house. The Reeve asks the town's masters the council's question in public and keeps his own house's master out of his questions.
:::

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The carters who take the salt to Valthari keep an inn at the dune road's end, plain and cheap, with a yard for their teams. A ship's crew sleeps aboard in the lagoon.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The night ship.** The crown of Galvaren's buyers offer a party a share of the fines if it can name the ship that sells salt outside Durvolka's bay, and the Reeve offers the same party more to bring the name to him first.
- **The channel.** The spring storms have filled the channel through the dunes early, and the houses want it dug out before the salt ships are due, at a cost each wants another house to pay.
