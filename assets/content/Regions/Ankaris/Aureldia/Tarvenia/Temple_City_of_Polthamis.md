## Overview {#overview}

The **Temple City of Polthamis** is the government of [[place-polthamis|Polthamis]], the city of [[place-grttmplrdvn|the Great Temple of Árdavon]] in [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]]. The city lies under none of the three kingdoms. The High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] has chartered it to the temple, and the prelate of the Great Temple rules it with his chapter.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The prelate is the archbishop of the Great Temple, elected for life by the chapter from among its canons, the senior priests who teach and judge there. He presides over the chapter, judges the city's cases in the temple's court and keeps its seal. The present prelate is Archbishop [[being-thalurozenot|Thaluro Zenothis]]. The chapter governs the city's streets, markets and lands as the temple's estate, and the townspeople bring their cases to the prelate's court as they would to a lord's.

The same seal stands behind the arbitrations of the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]]'s traveling priests across Tarvénia, and behind the commissions that send them into cases of noble corruption.

## Current Situation {#present}

The seal's protection holds only while the work it covers never reaches the royal court. A commission under it, carried by [[being-frstrndrvs|Father Ostaran Dorvás]] against Lord [[being-nizelganotis|Nizel Ganotis]], is following accusations that reach beyond Lord Nizel, and the further it reaches the nearer it comes to a court the temple's charter does not touch.

## History {#history}

The city's government is a High King's charter, so it dates from the High Kingship, which the lords of Tarvénia elected about 540 AF to drive the imperial garrisons out of the lowlands ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]). The prelates of [[place-grttmplrdvn|the Great Temple]] have ruled the city under that charter since, each elected for life by the chapter from its canons, and the seal they keep has stood behind the arbitrations of the faith's traveling priests. The present prelate is [[being-thalurozenot|Thaluro Zenothis]].
