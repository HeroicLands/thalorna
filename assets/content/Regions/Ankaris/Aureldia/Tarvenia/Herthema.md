## Overview {#overview}

Herthema is a walled town of about 4,000 on the coast of the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]], and a town that breeds sellswords. Its back streets send young men out under contract, its shipwrights build hulls that sail for generations, and its glassworkers blow the jars, bottles and window-glass the wine trade needs. The warrior traditions of Tarvénia hold here even as merchant wealth reshapes the town, and the quarrel of the moment, in the shipyards, the furnace-houses and the temple alike, is whether the old standard should give way to the new.

## History {#history}

Herthema's trades are old ones, handed down master to apprentice. The glassworker [[being-vlmrjthrn|Velmar Gútharen]] was apprenticed more than thirty years ago to a master who made plain ware for a fortified town that needed it: jars that seal, bottles that survive a cart, and panes that keep the wind out of a garrison's windows. The shipwright Master [Toren Graveth]{.name data-type="being/npc"} was counted the finest builder of ocean-going vessels in the kingdom until he drowned at sea. His son [[being-domthgrvth|Dometh Graveth]] has kept the workshop for the two decades since, taking only the commissions that meet his standard, and captains wait years for a Graveth hull.

## Current Situation {#present}

The new money of Herthema wants things made faster, cheaper and finer, and the town's old masters will not make them that way.

- **In the furnace-houses**, the younger masters of the [Glassworkers' Guild of Herthema]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"} make colored and figured glass for the merchants' houses. One of them, trained in [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia]], is taking the commissions [[being-vlmrjthrn|Velmar Gútharen]] held and calling his work peasant ware, and Velmar has sat on the guild's bench for a decade.
- **In the shipyards**, the rival shipwright [Oztuk Kanarris]{.name data-type="being/npc"} builds fast, cheap hulls and spreads the word that Graveth vessels are overpriced relics, and a reform faction among the merchants wants shipbuilding standardized and Dometh Graveth discredited. Dometh's hands have begun to tremble.
- **In the temple of [[affiliation-olvenia|Ólvenia]]**, the austere priest [[being-gaimelkorvat|Gaimel Korvathandis]] is agitating for stricter oversight of the temple's work in Herthema. His target is the priest [[being-palnsnrcth|Pálina Sánriseth]], whose work among the poor brings the temple little money and many complications, and whom the common people of Herthema hold close to a saint.

## Garrison {#garrison}

Herthema's walls keep a garrison, and the town keeps a roll of sellswords: a man on the roll may take hire in Herthema lawfully, and a man off it may not. The bodyguard [[being-banrkcrsld|Banrik Káriselda]] is on the roll and belongs to no company, and the bigger bodyguards of the town, who have lost work to him, say a man who cannot lift a shield has no business in the trade.

## Government, Faith and Trade {#government-faith-trade}

The [[affiliation-cnclhrthm|Council of Herthema]], the merchants and master craftsmen of the town, governs Herthema and keeps the sellswords' roll, and its First of the Council is the wine merchant [[being-brazemotholm|Brazemo Tholmaris]]. The merchants and minor nobility of the town are the employers of its bodyguards and the buyers of its glass, and its vintners and wine merchants need ware that holds above all.

The temple of [[affiliation-olvenia|Ólvenia]] is the town's great house of faith. Its priestess-keeper, [[being-thelvaravolk|Thélvara Volkemis]], holds Dometh Graveth's work to be a form of worship and sees that the temple's ships are always commissioned from him. Fishing families seek Pálina Sánriseth's blessing before long voyages, and the wives of absent sailors, widows and women with nowhere else to go know they can come to her.

## Within the Walls {#within-the-walls}

Below the walls lie the lanes of the back streets, where the sellswords and their bodyguards grow up. The furnace-houses of the glassworkers and the Graveth workshop stand in the town, and the temple of Ólvenia is the place the poor come to Pálina. A traveler lodges at the [Ornethran Inn]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, kept since her husband's death by the widow [[being-ilmrrnthrn|Ilmara Ornethran]], who runs it single-handed and is known for her stews.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The figured glass.** A wine merchant of Herthema wants to know whether the Provénzian-trained glassworker's figured ware holds as well as [[being-vlmrjthrn|Velmar Gútharen]]'s plain ware before he moves his whole vintage into it, and he will pay a party to carry both down a bad road and see which arrives whole.
- **The reform faction.** The merchants who want [[being-domthgrvth|Dometh Graveth]] discredited are gathering stories of Graveth hulls lost at sea. Dometh suspects the stories, and the wrecks, are not what they seem, and he cannot ask the questions himself.
- **The oversight.** [[being-gaimelkorvat|Gaimel Korvathandis]] wants witnesses to what [[being-palnsnrcth|Pálina Sánriseth]] does among the poor, and he will pay strangers to watch her.
