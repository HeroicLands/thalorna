## Overview {#overview}

**Grumagando** is the war pass, the chief of the passes over the western arm of the [[place-toravarrange|Toravar Range]] and the road [Mórezan]{.name data-type="being/npc"}'s host came down about 556 AF ([[lore-malnebris|The First Great Raid]]). Its name is plain Tarveni for what it is. It leaves the valleys of Tarvénia's [[place-westernmrch|Western March]] under the gate of [[place-kemthoza|Kemthoza]], climbs to a high saddle, and goes down the far side into [[place-talothe|the Talote]], past the field of [[place-bldfldmalnebris|Malnebris]] to the walls of [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]]. From the fort to the walls is about three days on foot.

It is the road of [[lore-summerwar|The Summer War]]. The March's hosts go down it when the snow clears and come home up it when the snow returns, and the border families of the Talote come up it the other way for stock and ransom. Nothing else uses it in the season that can go round.

## What Moves on It {#what-moves-on-it}

**The hosts.** In spring the barons of the March gather their men in Kemthoza's yard and go down the pass under their own banners, a valley at a time, and in autumn what is left of them comes back up. The first host over the saddle sets the terms of the season.

**The raiders.** The Talote's border families counter-raid up the pass for cattle, sheep and men worth a ransom, and drive what they take down it at night.

**The flocks.** The highest summer pastures lie on both sides of the saddle, and shepherds of both sides drive their flocks up to them after the thaw. Pasture is one of the three things the war is fought over.

**The ransoms.** Between the hosts, men go up and down the pass under a green bough to carry ransom and bring back the ransomed. Each side lets them pass, and each side counts them.

**The dead.** A host that goes down from Kemthoza brings its dead home up the pass for the rites, as far as it can carry them.

## Logistics {#logistics}

The road is the old track of the shepherds, widened by six generations of hosts: a stony way wide enough for two mules abreast on the Tarvenan side and narrower on the descent. It climbs from Kemthoza to the saddle in half a day, by switchbacks cut into the slope. On the far side it drops more steeply and, at Malnebris, runs narrow between a ridge and a stream. Below the field the valley opens and the road runs a day on to Durvalis.

Water is plentiful from the snowmelt and bitter cold until midsummer. There is no fodder above the pastures and no shelter on the saddle, and a host carries its feed and its bread from the valleys. Neither side builds on the pass, and nobody keeps an inn on it. A party on foot makes the crossing in three days in good weather and in five when the cloud comes down.

## The Stations {#stations}

**Kemthoza** is the head of the pass on the Tarvenan side, the last roof before the saddle, and its gate is the road. **The saddle** is half a day above it: bare rock and old snow, marked by the cairns the hosts raise there on the way down. **Malnebris** is a day down the far side, where the road narrows between the ridge and the stream and the dead of the first raid still lie. **Durvalis** stands a day below the field, the fortress town where the Talote musters and where the road comes out of the mountains.

## The Year on the Road {#year-on-the-road}

**Winter** shuts the pass from the first snow to the thaw, and a crossing then is either a disaster or a deliberate outrage. **The thaw** is the watched season: the fort on one side and the muster on the other watch the snow go off the saddle, because the first host over has the season's advantage. **Summer** is the war, the flocks and the ransoms all at once, and on still nights the field at Malnebris is loud for miles. **Autumn** brings the hosts home before the snow, and a host caught on the far side by an early fall winters in enemy country or dies trying to come back.

## Who Patrols and Taxes It {#present}

Nobody taxes the war pass, and no crown holds the whole of it. Baron [[being-kaldrokanar|Kaldro Kanarris]] keeps the head from Kemthoza, and his men watch the saddle from the wall-walk through the winter. On the far side the garrison of Durvalis holds the foot of the road and sends the Talote's muster up it when the snow clears, under [Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Marshal of the Western Army. Between the two the pass belongs to whoever is on it. The High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] claims the March and has declared no war, and the crown of [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Provénzia]] holds the road below the pass and has declared none either.

This spring the fort is watching the snow go off the saddle, and two barons want the first crossing. The dead of the first raid still lie unreceived on the road below the pass. No rite of settlement has been attempted there, because it would have to be said in the campaign season on ground both crowns claim, and neither crown will grant the other's priests safe conduct.

## Dangers {#dangers}

**The hosts and the raiders.** A party on Grumagando between the thaw and the first snow is on the road of a war, and nobody on it asks a stranger his business before deciding what to do with him.

**The snow.** It closes the pass without warning in autumn, and the cloud can come down on the saddle in any month and hide the cairns.

**Malnebris.** On still nights in the season the field replays the first raid: horses, orders in two languages and a shield-line giving way, with lights moving along the road. Nothing has ever been found there in the morning. The border families of both sides take a loud night as a warning, because the weather that carries the sound is the weather that brings raiders.

**The clans' ground.** Above the saddle the range is [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] country, and a party that leaves the road for the high reaches answers to the clan whose ground it is.

## History {#history}

Mórezan, the second High King, led the first host down the pass about 556 AF, and the Talote's levy held the road at Malnebris for a day and a night before it broke ([[lore-malnebris|The First Great Raid]]). The March has gone down the pass every season the snow allows since. In the second year of [[lore-warprinces|The War of the Princes]], in 654, a host came down it and went through [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] unopposed, and afterward Provénzia raised Durvalis at the foot of the road to hold it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The first crossing.** The snow is going off the saddle, and two barons want to take their hosts over first. Kaldro Kanarris wants a party to learn which of them means to go before the custom allows.
- **The green bough.** A ransom-carrier who went down the pass under the bough has not come back up, and the family of the man he went to ransom wants to know whether he reached Durvalis.
- **The dead below the pass.** The village priest of Kemthoza's valley wants the dead of the first raid received, and needs someone to find a way to say the rites on ground both crowns claim.
