## Overview {#overview}

The Great Temple of Árdavon stands in [[place-polthamis|Polthamis]] in [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]], and it is where the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]] trains the priests it sends out across the kingdom. A boy comes to it as an acolyte and studies theology, law and combat together, because the faith expects its priests to be both protectors and spiritual guides. He leaves it ordained to serve [[lore-ardavondty|Árdavon]] on the battlefield and in the courts, to mediate disputes and to bring corrupt officials to judgment.

The temple's reach outlasts the training. Its prelate, Archbishop [[being-thalurozenot|Thaluro Zenothis]], confirms the arbitrations of the faith's traveling priests under seal, and the commissions that send them into cases of noble corruption carry that seal too.

## The Present {#present}

The temple's commissions into noble corruption carry the prelate's seal, and so does the protection from the complaints those cases draw. That protection holds only while the work never reaches the royal court. [[being-frstrndrvs|Father Ostaran Dorvás]], trained here and ordained at twenty, is investigating Lord [[being-nizelganotis|Nizel Ganotis]], a noble accused of seizing land unlawfully and oppressing the peasantry, and the evidence he is gathering reaches past Lord Nizel to others. If it reaches the court, the seal that covers him from Polthamis stops covering him.

The archbishop is also the brother of [[being-zedimazenoth|Zédima Zenothis]], Ostaran's mother. Neither of them states the kinship.

## Who Rules It {#who-rules-it}

Archbishop Thaluro is the prelate at Polthamis. He confirms under seal the arbitrations of the priests the temple sends out, and Father Ostaran meets him on the register four times a year. The prelate answers to the faith's hierarchy above him: the Sarnal of the Temple Judges and the Volsarnor, the High Judge, who keeps his seat in [[place-provenzrgn|Provenzia]].

## Lands and Income {#lands-and-income}

A temple of Árdavon is paid as the faith is paid: by royal support, by the fees charged for witnessing oaths and contracts, and by the endowments of pious nobles. The faith's temples are courts of law and archives of recorded oaths and contracts as well as houses of worship, and the Great Temple is all three.

## Visiting the House {#visiting}

The Great Temple keeps the faith's two great observances. At the [Feast of Thresholds]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}, four days at the turning of the year, lords and vassals reaffirm their bonds before its priests, merchants renew their contracts and spouses their vows, and the priests witness and record each oath. At the [Sanctification of Swords]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} each spring, knights and soldiers newly invested present themselves at the altar, and the priests bless their weapons and armor with holy oils.

## The Grounds {#the-grounds}

- **[1] The Altar:** where oaths are witnessed at the Feast of Thresholds and weapons are blessed at the Sanctification of Swords.
- **[2] The School:** where the Acolytes of the Threshold study theology, law and the sword. The course is long, and only a fraction of those who begin it are ordained. Many who leave partway become scribes, notaries and lawyers who draft binding contracts.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The evidence.** Father Ostaran needs the evidence against Lord Nizel gathered and carried safely to Polthamis, and Lord Nizel is ready to stop the investigation by bribery, manipulation or violence.
- **The duel.** Lord Nizel answers the commission by challenging Father Ostaran to a duel, and the priest must prove with the sword what the Great Temple taught him.

:::secret {title="The court"}
[[being-zenothaulmis|Lady Zenotha Ulmis]] publicly supports the Árdavonian faith and is secretly allied with the corrupt nobles Father Ostaran is pursuing. She is working quietly against him at the royal court, spreading rumors and spending her influence to undermine his reputation, which is the one place the prelate's protection does not reach.
:::
