## Overview {#overview}

The Taruzeli Quarter is the part of [[place-valthari|Valthari]] where the merchants of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]] keep their own warehouses, their own temple and their own hall. It lies along the quays, and from its warehouse doors to the water is a short walk with a cart. The quarter speaks [[skill-taruzelilng|Taruzeli]] at home and [[skill-valtarlng|Valtari]] on the quay, and its factors bargain in both, which is how most of Valthari meets it: a Taruzeli factor at a warehouse door, selling in one tongue and arguing with his clerk in the other. Anyone who trades east goes there.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The quarter is the city's, and its people live in it under the city's charter. The Taruzeli houses own or lease their warehouses from Valthari houses and pay the city's dues like anyone else, and most of the quarter's people are residents (_palvo_) of the city's register rather than freemen of it. The factors of the Taruzeli houses settle their quarrels among themselves in their own hall, by the custom of their home ports, and take a quarrel with a Valthari house to the Arbitrators at [[place-charterhall|the Charter Hall]] like anyone else.

## The Quarter's Layers {#history}

The quarter is as old as the port's crowd of tongues. Its oldest warehouses are imperial work, built when Valthari was an imperial port and Taruzel an imperial province ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]), and the Taruzeli who traded through the port then settled beside its quays. When the city bought its charter ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]) the quarter's people stayed, and they have kept their place in the city since, under the city's law and by their own custom among themselves.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The quarter is waiting on news from the east. At [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]] the Taruzelian admiral [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]] presses the Grand Council for a toll on every hull through the narrows of the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]]. If it passes, every Valthari ship trading to the Taruzelian ports pays it, and the Valthari houses that trade east blame the quarter for a decision made a sea away. The [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]] is divided over it, and its First, whose own house trades east, has been seen at the factors' hall more often this season than in any season before it.

The city's other quarrels reach the quarter too. Its warehouses are full, because the seamen of Valthari refuse to sign on and the cargoes are not moving.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

- **[1] The warehouses.** Long stone buildings on the quay street, three floors high, with iron-barred doors and a seal on every lock, in the manner of the bonded warehouses of [[affiliation-sazumun|Sazumun]]. A cargo stored there is sold on the seal, without the buyer seeing it.
- **[2] The temple of Ólvenia.** The Taruzeli merchant class favors the [[affiliation-olvenia|Faith of Ólvenia]] for prosperity, and the quarter's temple is the only one of hers in the city. Taruzeli contracts are sworn before her altar.
- **[3] The factors' hall.** The hall where the Taruzeli houses' factors meet, settle their quarrels and read the letters that come with every ship from the east. A Valthari merchant comes in by invitation.
- **[4] The quay steps.** Where the Taruzeli ships unload, and where the news from Yamuzel arrives first.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The letter from Yamuzel.** A Taruzeli factor wants a sealed letter carried to the First of the Council's counting-house without any other house learning that it came, and two houses have already asked the quay boys to watch for it.
- **The copied lines.** A master of the Taruzelian design house of [[affiliation-rusakuni|the Rusakuni]] is lodging in the quarter, and he wants proof that a hull on the slips of [[place-vlthrshpyrds|the shipyards]] has been built to his house's lines without its fee.
