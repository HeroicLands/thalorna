## Overview {#overview}

Ólvestra is one of the great commercial cities of the west, named in the same breath as [[place-orzara|Orzara City]] and the [[place-byzariargn|Bizaria Region]] when merchants talk about where goods fetch the highest prices. It stands in [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]], and it is two things at once: a moneylenders' city, and the city where [[place-aureldirgn|Aureldia]]'s sworn word is recorded and its thresholds are guarded. Two orders of [[affiliation-ardavon|Árdavon]] keep their mother-houses here. Its live trouble lies in the hills around it, where the barons shelter what both orders are sworn against.

## Arriving {#arriving}

What a traveler meets first is the gate. [[place-twingate|The Twin Gate]] is one of the most consequential thresholds in all of Aureldia, and [[affiliation-twinbldrdr|the Twinblade Order]], whose whole calling is the keeping of thresholds, keeps it. The city's gate-guards admit and refuse. Where an admission or a refusal is contested, the Twinblade on station confirms it, and no noble's rank overrides that word. A Black Flame operative who tries the gate is refused, and the refusal is sometimes contested by combat.

Prices inside run higher for premium goods than almost anywhere in the west, and a traveler who pays in [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vylarian]] coin pays more here for the same thing than in a provincial town.

## Politics {#present}

Influence in Ólvestra runs through its moneylenders and its two orders.

The great houses of [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]], the moneylenders' guild, keep offices here, the [House of Peleris]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"} among them, and an Ólvestran house's notes travel far. The imperial treasury, [[affiliation-tarmex|the Tarmeks]], has suspended one of the city's major moneylender houses for political reasons: its senior partners made the wrong enemy at court. Notes that other houses cashed and forwarded to the suspended house's Tarmeks account cannot be redeemed, the houses that cashed them are exposed, and the wariness is spreading from Ólvestra across Aureldia.

[[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|The Order of the Sealed Word]] keeps its mother-house here, and its [Council of the Seal]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, twelve Senior Brothers drawn from the five kingdoms, meets in the city every quarter. The order works under each crown's license, and the renewal has become a political question, since its rulings against powerful nobles make some crowns reluctant to confirm it.

The hills are the trouble. The hill-baronies around Ólvestra have, for political reasons, declined to suppress [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]], and [[affiliation-olvstrncell|the Ólvestran Cell]] of [[affiliation-ordershnvw|the Order of the Ashen Vow]], that order's largest, works under their protection. Seven years ago the Ólvestran Marendos of the Sealed Word died three days before he was to issue a major writ against a baron sympathetic to the Black Flame. His death was ruled natural, and it is still talked about as something less than natural.

## Order and Law {#order-and-law}

A broken oath that the civil courts cannot reach goes to the Sealed Word. A petition is presented at the mother-house, examined for substance, and accepted or refused with reasons; an accepted petition sends a brother riding to the oath-breaker with the order's writ, sealed in red wax, to convene a hearing. The ruling is given in Árdavon's name and recorded. It binds no court in law, and it is honored in practice, because the order refuses petitions it cannot substantiate and rules without favor on those it can. The Twinblades provide the threshold-presence at formal proceedings, and a combatant when trial by combat is a matter of threshold-violation.

Every Aureldian crown keeps standing orders against the Ashen Vow, and the Árdavon orders keep standing arrangements with [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] for its suppression. The hill-baronies are where those orders stop.

## What the City Lives By {#what-the-city-lives-by}

Ólvestra lives by money. The Danvar's chapterhouse here is one of its largest, with a permanent staff in the dozens: clerks, auditors, guards and the household that hosts visiting members of standing. The great houses lend, and cash and forward one another's notes.

The Tarvenan companies hire here. [[being-zedmogltsrg|Zedmo Galtsuregi]], captain of [[affiliation-goitsathal|Goitsa Thal]], met the captain of [[affiliation-irnwlvscmpny|the Iron Wolves Company]], [Filazur Zighidek]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, once, at a hiring fair in Ólvestra.

## The Master Register

The Sealed Word's mother-house holds the order's archives, and the heart of them is the master register of every contested oath the order has ruled on in its three centuries. It opens with a boundary oath between two Tarvénian lords ([[lore-sealedwordfnd|The Founding of the Sealed Word]]) and has not stopped growing. It is the nearest thing Tarvénia has to a national archive. Each Senior Brother wears a small bronze key at the throat, for Árdavon's gates and for the keys of the archive.

## Faith and Festivals {#faith-and-festivals}

[[lore-ardavondty|Árdavon]], god of oaths and gates, is the patron of both of the city's orders. The Twin Gate temple is where the Twinblades conduct their senior elevations in temple rite. The Twinblades keep their mother-house at the gate, with a ceremonial cadre for the coronations, treaty-signings and embassies of Aureldia that require a Twinblade's presence. The Sealed Word's brothers wear the deep blue surcoat with the Árdavon sigil, a two-faced head over a sealed scroll, in gold at the breast; the Twinblades wear pale gray with the two faces over crossed gold-and-silver blades.

## History {#history}

About 421 AF, when the Edicts of Arzanas closed the western wars and the crowns of Aureldia lost the legions that had kept oaths between them, Árdavon's priesthood chartered a sworn order at Ólvestra to ride to broken oaths, hear them and compel the remedy ([[lore-sealedwordfnd|The Founding of the Sealed Word]]). The Order of the Sealed Word keeps its mother-house and its register here. Seven years ago the order's Ólvestran Marendos died before he could issue his writ against a baron of the hills.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The hearing.** A brother of the Sealed Word needs riders to bring an oath-breaker to a hearing, and the oath-breaker is a baron with friends in the hills around Ólvestra ([[affiliation-olvstrncell|the Ólvestran Cell]]).
- **The frozen note.** A merchant holds a chest of notes cashed against the suspended Ólvestran house and cannot redeem them, and he needs his petition carried to the Imperial _Tarkelos_ in Orzara ([[affiliation-tarmex|the Tarmeks]]).
- **The refusal at the gate.** A baron of the hills rides up to the Twin Gate, and the Twinblade on station refuses him ([[place-twingate|the Twin Gate]]).
