## Overview {#overview}

Thórgila is a hill town of about 2,500 in the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]], with farms on the slopes above it and no sea in sight. It is the town of the Thórgila valleys, and once a year it holds the horse fair from which the fairs of those valleys take their measure. Its men go abroad under contract and come home with money in hand, and while they are away their wives run the farms.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Thórgila lives by its farms and its horses. The farms on the slopes above the town are run through the men's long contracts by the women who manage them, and the valleys bring their horses to the autumn horse fair, the _arthauna_, where the races and the trading are the occasion.

The fair has its horse-judge, the _kornazdo_, [[being-thelvarmozk|Thelvar Mozkarreta]], who reads the horses' teeth and says their ages, and his word settles a sale. He also gives the themes at the fair's verse-duel, the _gortsada_, in which two singers make verse against each other on the spot and the fair judges them by stamping. [[lore-fairverses|The Verses of the Thórgila Fair]] are what the town kept of one such duel, between a woman of the third farm above the town and a crossbowman home from six years under contract, and the valleys sing them at the hearth in winter. The town and the upper farms sing one line of them differently, and both versions are kept.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The [[affiliation-cnclthrgl|Council of Thórgila]] governs the town. Its free men meet in it and decide matters of common concern, and a matter between neighbors goes to it. At the fair, a sale is sworn at the oath stone, and a seller is held to the word he gave there.

## The Present {#present}

The autumn fair is the event the town's year turns on, and this year it falls in the middle of other people's quarrels.

- **The priest's inquiry.** [[being-frstrndrvs|Father Ostaran Dorvás]], a priest of [[affiliation-ardavon|Árdavon]] who makes his home in Thórgila, is investigating the lord [[being-nizelganotis|Nizel Ganotis]], who is accused of seizing land unlawfully and oppressing his peasantry. Lord Nizel sees the investigation as a direct threat to his power, and he would use bribery, manipulation and even violence to stop it.
- **The herald's house.** The herald [[being-carengrvth|Kárenna Graveth]], whose home is in Thórgila, serves the royal household as one of the kingdom's principal emissaries, and the quarrels of the court follow her home.
- **The farms and the sea.** The seaman [[being-rithardmvr|Ríthara Dómivar]] left Thórgila at seventeen to find a ship, against her family's wish that she marry a hill man. Her father is ill, and her mother has sent for her to come home and take up the hill life she left.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The lord's riders.** Riders of [[being-nizelganotis|Nizel Ganotis]] come to the autumn fair asking where [[being-frstrndrvs|Father Ostaran Dorvás]] keeps his papers, and they are buying horses at prices that buy friends.
- **A buyer at the stone.** A horse-seller at the fair swears a horse's age at the oath stone, and the horse-judge reads its teeth and says otherwise. The buyer wants the seller answered before the same witnesses, and the seller has friends among the crossbowmen home from contract.
- **The daughter sent for.** [[being-rithardmvr|Ríthara Dómivar]]'s mother wants a party to carry word to the coast that her daughter must come home, and to bring Ríthara back with them if she will come.
