## Overview {#overview}

The Western March is [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]]'s side of its frontier with [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia]]: the high valleys under the western arm of the [[place-toravarrange|Toravar Range]], where the passes go over into [[place-talothe|the Talote]], Provénzia's eastern march. Its barons have fought the same quarrel with the border families on the far side of the passes since about 556 AF, when [Mórezan]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the second High King, took a host down the pass ([[lore-malnebris|The First Great Raid]]). The March calls that quarrel [[lore-summerwar|The Summer War]]. It has a season, because the passes close: the hosts go down when the snow clears and come home when it returns.

## The Land {#the-land}

The March is mountain country. Its valleys climb west toward the range, and the passes at their heads are cold and very nearly impassable from the first snow to the thaw. Above the valley fields lie the summer pastures, and the highest of them lie on both sides of the passes. Pasture, the passes and precedence are what the war is fought over.

The year turns on the snow. In winter the passes are shut, the valleys keep to themselves, and a crossing is either a disaster or a deliberate outrage. In spring the March watches the snow go off the saddles, because the first host over sets the terms of the season.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Each valley is a baron's, held from a tower or a fortified house, and the baron brings its men down the pass under his own banner. About 11,000 people live in the March's valleys. No crown governs the March as a whole. The High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] claims it as he claims every Tarveni-speaking land, and the crown presses its claims on the Talote's uplands whenever the court of [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Provénzia]] is weak.

The March's houses raise knights for the border. [[being-srdlvnthrvkknghtwstrnmrchs|Sir Dalven Thorvek]], the youngest son of a prominent house of the March, made his name in its campaigns and is known through the valleys as the Knight of the Western Marches.

## Current Situation {#present}

The Summer War goes on, season by season, below the level at which either crown must call it a war. Across the passes [Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Marshal of the Western Army, holds the frontier from [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]], and the walls of Durvalis stand on the road the March's hosts use. The March goes down regardless.

The dead of the first raid still lie on the road below Grumagando. A Tarvenan holds that a field where the dead were left unreceived is a danger to everyone who passes it, and the March sends its men past Malnebris every summer. No rite of settlement has ever been attempted there, because it would have to be said in the campaign season on ground both crowns claim, and neither crown will grant the other's priests safe conduct.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **The passes out of season.** Snow closes them without warning in autumn, and a host caught on the far side winters in enemy country or dies in the attempt to come home.
- **Malnebris.** On still nights in the season the field is loud for miles. The weather that carries the sound is the weather that brings raiders, and both sides of the passes take a loud night as a warning.
- **The raiders from the far side.** The border families of the Talote counter-raid for stock and ransom, and a valley that sent its men down the pass can find its own pastures stripped.
- **The high country.** Above the March the range is [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] country, and a baron who pushes his herds or his loggers up into it can start a mountain war with a clan.

## History {#history}

The war began with Mórezan's raid about 556 AF ([[lore-malnebris|The First Great Raid]]). Tarvénian hosts pressed the march while the province fought its _therkos_ from about 609 to 618 ([[lore-charterwar|The War of the Charter]]), and in 654 a host went through [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] unopposed ([[lore-warprinces|The War of the Princes]]), after which Provénzia raised Durvalis to hold the road. [[lore-summerwar|The Summer War]] tells the quarrel from the March's side.
