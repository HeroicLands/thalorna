## Overview {#overview}

The **Council of Nebralis** governs [[place-nebralis|Nebralis]], a port town on the coast of [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]]. It is a council of aldermen, and as the town grows rich on its harbor, the merchant houses that control the harbor trade hold more of its seats every year.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The aldermen govern the town and choose a First of the Council from among themselves to preside over it. The present First is the merchant-captain [[being-gulmalenothel|Gulmaleno Thelvaris]], whose family has been gathering the harbor trade into its own hands. Most of the aldermen are men of the merchant houses. One seat belongs to the fishing cooperative, whose leader, the alderman [[being-dorandaroort|Dorándaro Orthemandis]], speaks for the common fishermen of Nebralis. Beside the council stands the older body, the [Freemen's Assembly of Nebralis]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, the communal assembly of the town and its country, in which every free man has a voice and matters of common concern are decided. The council governs the harbor town day to day, and the assembly decides what the town and its country share, and what is shared in a lean year. No lord holds the town, and the council answers to the crown of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] directly.

## Current Situation {#present}

The merchant oligarchy is consolidating its control of Nebralis, and its leaders want the town's institutions made more orderly and accountable: formal magistrates in place of mediation, and trained clergy in place of a priestess who works in the fields and on the boats. Dorándaro Orthemandis has sat beside [[being-alethraornet|Alethra Ornetis]] in the assembly for six years and trusts her absolutely, and he and the fishing families are the council's counterweight. The bishop [[being-ornethoilthe|Ornetho Iltheris]] has recommended that Alethra be placed under closer oversight, and the merchant aldermen who want her work made orderly have taken his side.

## History {#history}

The Freemen's Assembly is the older of the town's two bodies, the Tarvenan tradition of communal assembly kept in a port. When a run of harsh years brought drought and hunger to Nebralis, the assembly decided what was shared, and Alethra Ornetis fasted with the town and nearly died of it. The council has grown with the harbor, and the harbor has made the merchant houses rich.
