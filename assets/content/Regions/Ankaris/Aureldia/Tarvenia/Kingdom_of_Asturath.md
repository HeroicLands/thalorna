## Overview

The Kingdom of Asturath is the mountain kingdom at the heart of the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]], a realm of valley lords under a king who is the first of them, and the one part of Tarvénia that the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] never held. It is a mountain people's freedom kept by their own arms: Outsiders know it for two things: the crossbowmen its valleys send to other people's wars, and the plateau city of [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]], where the kingdom's king and the High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] both keep their courts. The kingdom holds the land of [[place-asturath|Asturath]].

## The King and the Lords

A mountain lord of Asturath holds a valley because his house has always held it, and he holds it of nobody. He judges in it, takes its rents and its dues, keeps its towers and brings its men to the ban under his own banner. The king of Asturath is a mountain lord too, with valleys of his own whose rents and flocks are the crown's revenue, and over the other lords he has three rights: to call the ban, to judge a quarrel between two lords, and to hold the crown's offices at the plateau gate, the iron hill, the drove roads and the eastern march. Beyond those, a lord owes the king what was agreed and nothing more.

The crown passes by blood. The king's heir is his eldest child, and failing children the nearest kinsman of the royal house, but the heir is king only once the council of mountain lords has acknowledged them. Each lord grips the heir's forearm at the council's oath stone and says the ban—that his banner will come when the king calls it—and a lord who does not grip it owes the new king nothing until he does. The council has never refused an heir outright. It has more than once let an heir wait at the stone for a day while the lords settled what the new reign would cost them.

The present king is [[being-alzemozold|Alzemo Zolduregi]], who holds his court in the castle at the north end of Lezkora.

## The Council of Mountain Lords

The council of mountain lords is every lord who holds a valley of the kingdom, about forty in all, meeting in the king's castle at Lezkora. It meets every autumn after the flocks have come down from the meadows and before the snow closes the roads, and at any other time the king calls it. Its presiding officer is the Keeper of the Stone, a lord the council chooses for each meeting.

The council does three things. It acknowledges a new king. It hears a quarrel between lords that the lords cannot settle between themselves, and the king judges it with the council's advice. And it hears the king's call to the ban, because a ban the lords have heard called before the council is a ban none of them can say he was not told of. The council makes no laws, raises no tax and keeps no treasury. The laws of a valley are its custom, and its lord keeps them.

When the High Kingship falls vacant, the lords of all Tarvénia meet at Lezkora to elect a new High King, and the mountain lords of Asturath and the lords of Lyenrik are the two great parties in [[place-lezkorahall|the High Hall]]. That election is the crown of Tarvénia's business. The council of mountain lords is the kingdom's own.

## Justice

A quarrel in Asturath goes to the nearest body that can end it. Neighbors take theirs to the village assembly, where the free householders of the village hear both sides and decide. A matter the assembly cannot settle, or one that touches the lord's rights, goes to the lord, who judges it in his hall before witnesses. A quarrel between two lords goes to the king and the council. Along the eastern road the Warden of the March judges in the king's name, and the Warden's justice is quicker and harder than any valley lord's. A broken oath that no lord can remedy goes to the [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|Order of the Sealed Word]], as everywhere in Tarvénia.

The heaviest sentence a lord can pass is _ventha_ (banishment), which puts the banished outside every valley's shelter. A man banished from one valley is banished from them all, because no lord shelters another lord's banished.

## The Ban

Every free man of Asturath is expected to bear arms, and every valley owes the ban. The kingdom's muster roll, kept by the Keeper of the Roll, records what each valley owes in men and arms, and the king's heralds carry the call up the valleys when he makes it. The lords bring their own men under their own banners, and the king names a Captain of the Ban to command them for the campaign. The muster is slow, because the valleys are far apart and the paths are hard, and it is fierce once it has gathered.

The ban is for defense. When the High King calls the ban of Tarvénia against a foreign enemy, the king of Asturath calls his own lords and brings them, and the Astúrathi are the last to go home. A war abroad is a company's business, under contract, and the ban has nothing to do with it.

## Character

Asturath is the most martial and conservative of the Tarvenan regions. Its people are mountain folk—tough, proud and attached to the old ways—and its social order rests on bonds between lord and sworn man, kept in person. Most of the kingdom's coin comes home in the purses of men who went abroad under contract, and nearly every household has had a man in a company. Asturath gives the companies their reputation, and the companies give Asturath its silver.

Invading armies that have ventured into Asturath's mountains have regretted it. The Vilérian legions, after several disastrous campaigns, the worst of them lost in [[lore-horngorgebtl|the Horn Gorge]] about 143 AF, settled for holding the lowland approaches and leaving the mountains alone, and the Astúrathi have never tired of celebrating it. The first High King, [Garvaneth]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Asturath, was elected at Lezkora about 540 AF to drive the garrisons out of the lowlands ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]), and the High King has most often been an Astúrathi lord since.

## Asturath and the Crown

The High King of Tarvénia is often the king of Asturath, and he is not at present. The electing council chose [[being-ularrekgarr|Ularrek Garrundeta]], an old lord of the high valleys, one of the king of Asturath's own mountain lords, because each of the greater candidates could block the others. The High King now holds his court in the High Hall in the king of Asturath's city and passes through the king of Asturath's gate. The two courts are courteous, and every lord in the kingdom knows that the High King is old.

## Offices

The king's officers are few, and each holds one thing for him. The Warden of the Plateau Gate holds Lezkora's gate and road for life, the Warden of the March holds the eastern frontier from Kávrenath, the Warden of the Iron counts the king's share at [[place-ardalis|Ardalis]], and the Drove-Warden keeps the drove roads and the king's meadows from [[place-tsaberis|Tsaberis]]. The Keeper of the Roll and the Steward of the King's Valleys serve in the king's household, the heralds carry his word, and castellans hold towers for the lords who appoint them. The Captain of the Ban serves for one campaign and goes home, and no ban is called at present, so the office has no holder. The Keeper of the Stone is chosen for each meeting of the council of mountain lords and holds nothing between meetings, so the office stands empty from the autumn council until the next.

## Current Situation {#present}

Lezkora holds two courts. The High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]], [[being-ularrekgarr|Ularrek Garrundeta]], is one of the king's own mountain lords, chosen because each of the greater candidates could block the others, and he is old and has not come down to the High Hall for a month. The king, [[being-alzemozold|Alzemo Zolduregi]], keeps his court in the castle at the north end of the same city, and the High King eats bread carried up the king's road and passes through the king's gate.

The lords of the lowlands, whose king [[being-zenotholtr|Zenoth Oltris]] means to stand at the next election, have begun sending their men up the road one and two at a time. At the eastern edge the Warden of the March, [[being-estarildomi|Estaril Dómivar]], holds the road at [[place-kavrenath|Kávrenath]] against smugglers, deserters and refugees, and in the high valleys the companies are abroad under contract.

## Commerce and Currency

The Kingdom of Asturath uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian currency]] (Solden / Ardin / Ogdel) as its standard currency, with full access to the paper-script system through [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]]-member moneylenders chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]]. It is a post-imperial commonwealth member—it takes part fully in the trade and banking of the Vilérian system but is not subject to imperial governance or imperial taxation. See [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian Currency]] for the full system.

## History {#history}

### The Mountains Never Taken (about 143 AF)

The legions of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] took the lowlands about 140 AF and sent expeditions into the mountains. The fourth, the worst of them, was let into the gorge below Lezkora by the mountain lord [Tórsaro]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who closed both ends of it, and the legion died there in two days ([[lore-horngorgebtl|The Battle of the Horn Gorge]]). The Empire held the lowland approaches thereafter and left the mountains alone.

### The First High King (about 538 to 556 AF)

With the lowlands in revolt against the Empire, the mountain lords met at Lezkora about 540 AF and elected [Garvaneth]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Asturath the first High King to drive the garrisons out ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]). His successor [Mórezan]{.name data-type="being/npc"} took a host down the western pass about 556 AF, and the lords of Tarvénia have gone down the passes into Provénzia every season the snow allows since ([[lore-summerwar|The Summer War]]).

### The Crown at Lezkora (from about 605 AF)

The lords of Lyenrik took the High Kingship from about 605 AF and have contested it at every election since. The High King has still most often been an Astúrathi lord, and the present one is.
