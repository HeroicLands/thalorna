## Overview

Tarvénia lies east of the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]] and west of the [[place-vylariargn|Viléria Region]], a land of rugged mountains, rich coastal areas, and fiercely independent peoples. The landscape is as varied as its people—dense forests, golden beaches, and high, windswept peaks are home to small, isolated villages and proud city-states. The Tarvenans trace their lineage back to ancient warrior tribes, and their culture prizes strength, resilience, and an unshakeable sense of freedom.

## Geography {#the-land}

Tarvénia is wine hills between two mountain walls. On the north and west the [[place-toravarrange|Toravar Range]] closes it: the northern arm runs east and west along the border with [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], and the western arm runs down the frontier with [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia]], where the passes of the [[place-westernmrch|Western March]] go over into [[place-talothe|the Talote]]. On the east the [[place-kervaron|Kervaron Spine]] divides it from the [[place-vylariargn|Viléria Region]], and the road over it comes down to [[place-kavrenath|Kávrenath]]. The south is the coast of the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]].

Between the two walls the land falls from the mountains to the sea. The mountain heartland of [[place-asturath|Asturath]] fills the interior, ridge on ridge of gray limestone with narrow valleys between, and the highlands of [[place-eskarath|Eskarath]] lie under the northern arm. Below the mountains the hills begin, with the barley farms and horse fairs of the hill towns, and the eastern hills of [[place-leonrik|Lienrik]], whose south faces carry the vineyards of [[place-enthara|Enthara]]. Below the hills the lowlands of Lienrik open along the [Thalsira]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the richest farmland in the west. In the south-west the hills climb into the forests of [[place-galvaren|Galvaren]], whose wet coast of drowned valleys and black cliffs looks out over the western reaches of the same sea.

The southern coast is long and sandy, warm and dry, and its harbors are on the sea: [[place-valthari|Valthari]], the richest port in the kingdom, and the harbor towns of [[place-nebralis|Nebralis]] and [[place-herthema|Herthema]]. Where the Thalsira comes down below [[place-bruthena|Bruthena]], the shore breaks into salt marsh, mudflat and shingle.

The climate is dominated by the Vilérian Sea—long, dry summers and mild winters in the south, cooler and wetter conditions in the north, and rain on most days on the Galvaren coast. The land is ideal for agriculture, particularly vineyards, olive groves, and grain fields. Tarvénia is a major producer of trade goods, including high-quality wines, olive oil, and textiles.

## Culture and Society

The Tarvenans are fiercely independent and proud of their heritage. In contrast to the more refined and cultured people of Provénzia, they are known for their strength, resilience, and deeply entrenched sense of personal and communal freedom. Many of the great warriors and military leaders of [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris]] come from Tarvénia, and the region is home to skilled tacticians, mercenaries, and raiders.

Politically, Tarvénia is a patchwork of semi-autonomous regions united under the loose authority of the [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]. Three petty kingdoms—[[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Asturath]] (the mountainous heartland), [[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Galvaren]] (the misty south-western shore), and [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Lienrik]] (the fertile central lowlands)—share power with the [[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]] (a merchant port on the southern coast) and the [[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]] (an ancient highland people on the Élavendren border). They share a common language and a history of both collaboration and rivalry. The people of Tarvénia are united by their warrior culture and their defiance of outside rule—a trait that has made them difficult subjects for every empire that has tried to conquer them. Even the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] at its height struggled to hold Tarvénia: it took the lowlands about 140 AF ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]), lost a legion in the mountains ([[lore-horngorgebtl|The Horn Gorge]]), and was driven out of the lowlands by the first High King, elected for the purpose ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]).

Social structure varies by region. Coastal cities tend toward merchant oligarchies, while inland kingdoms are more traditionally feudal. Across Tarvénia, however, there is a strong tradition of communal assembly where freemen have a voice in local governance.

## Religion

The distinct faiths of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] are the formal religions, but Tarvenan practice is inflected with strong local folk traditions. [[affiliation-ardavon|Árdavon]] (order and honorable struggle) is especially prominent among the warrior class, while [[affiliation-lusinia|Lusinia]] (creation and earth) is honored by farmers and rural communities. [[affiliation-brazemas|Brazemas]] (storms) has a significant following among seafarers and mountain communities.

The Tarvenans have a complex relationship with magic and the divine. While Provénzia embraces arcane arts and mystical studies, Tarvénia has traditionally been more skeptical of magic, viewing it with suspicion or as a tool of the elite. That said, many cities hold ancient shrines to local gods and spirits, and the connection to the divine remains a vital part of the culture at the folk level. Hermit holy-men, wandering mendicants, and local shrine-keepers are more common than formal temple priests.

## Magic

The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] has a limited presence in Tarvénia. The Tarvenan distrust of centralized authority extends to magical authority, and the Arcane Order's attempts to regulate arcane practice have been met with resistance ranging from polite refusal to outright hostility. Local magical traditions—hedge magic, herbal lore, and protective charms—persist alongside the Arcane Order's more formal practices, and many Tarvenan communities would sooner consult a village wise-woman than an Arcane Order-sanctioned mage.

## Relations

Tarvénia has begun to forge closer ties with the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]] in recent years, especially in trade, but the two regions remain competitive rivals for dominance over the southern trade routes. Relations with the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] are distant—the Tarvenans broke free of Vilérian control long ago ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]) and have no desire to renew the relationship. [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] to the north is viewed with a mix of respect and wariness; the Élavendren are admired for their arts but considered overly mystical and soft.

Within Tarvénia itself, the [[place-alndntrblnds#the-tarvenan-diaspora|Áelendan tribal enclaves]] of the Toravar Range and the northern forested uplands are the most distinctive non-Tarvénan presence on the peninsula. Áelendan communities—Pelwar forest-dwellers who recognize [[affiliation-theoldway|the Old Way]] of [[affiliation-firstgods|the First Gods]] rather than the Aureldian Twelve—occupy the high mountain country and the deepest forests, generally in territory the Tarvenan crown and the lowland feudal nobility have no use for. Relations are mostly cordial: the Áelendan leave the lowlands alone, the Tarvenans leave the high country alone, and a long-established practice of local accommodation between [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] and Tarvenan barons handles the periodic frictions over straying herds, logging concessions, road-surveys near sacred groves, and the occasional Aureldian missionary who needs to be politely but firmly escorted out. Roughly once a generation a particularly stubborn baron meets a particularly unyielding clan and the result is a brief mountain-war that the crown of Tarvénia prefers to settle quickly through Élavendri diplomatic intermediaries. No Tarvenan king has ever attempted to bring the Áelendan enclaves under formal authority, and none is expected to. The crown is considerably less tolerant than Provénzia's of the tribes' defense of their sacred sites, and what accommodation exists is the barons' and the Wardens', not royal policy.

Tarvenan sailors and mercenaries are found across the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] and beyond. Their reputation as fierce fighters and skilled seafarers makes them valued (if sometimes unpredictable) allies and employees.

## Current Situation {#present}

The High King is old. The electing council chose [Ularrek Garrundeta]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a lord of Asturath's high valleys, because each of the greater candidates could block the others, and he holds his court at [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] as the guest of the king of Asturath, [Alzemo Zolduregi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}. The king of Lienrik, [Zenoth Oltris]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, means to stand at the next election, and the lowland lords have begun sending their men up the road to Lezkora one and two at a time.

On the western frontier the [[lore-summerwar|Summer War]] goes on. The barons of the Western March go down the passes into the Talote every season the snow allows, and Provénzia's walls at [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] stand on the road they use. On the eastern frontier the Warden of the March at Kávrenath holds the road over the Kervaron against smugglers and imperial deserters, and in a bad year beyond the mountains, against refugees.

Valthari stands between the kingdoms and the sea trade. Lienrik pays Valthari's harbor for every cargo it ships, and Galvaren cures its catch with salt that the salt ships bring from the southern coast, which its crown sells to the curing houses at a price the shipmasters resent. In the hills around [[place-olvestria|Ólvestra]] the barons shelter [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]], and the license of [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|the Order of the Sealed Word]] is a political question at the crowns that must renew it.

## Notable Features

The Mountain Kingdoms
: Isolated highland realms—especially [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Asturath]]—where ancient traditions survive largely unchanged, and where the fiercest warriors are bred.

The Port Cities
: Prosperous trading hubs along the southern coast, above all the [[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]], where goods from across [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión]] and beyond change hands.

The Mercenary Tradition
: Tarvenan sellswords are found in armies across Ankaris. Service abroad is seen as a rite of passage for young warriors.

The Eskárathi Highlands
: The [[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]], which holds [[place-eskarath|Eskarath]], preserves a culture older than the Tarveni migration itself, with its own language, laws, and fierce mountain traditions.
