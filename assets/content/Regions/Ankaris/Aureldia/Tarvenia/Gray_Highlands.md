## Overview {#overview}

The Gray Highlands are the high country of [[place-asturath|Asturath]]: the ridges of gray limestone and darker rock above the kingdom's valley floors, the high meadows of the summer grazing, and the passes the crossbow companies take when they go abroad. A man of the high valleys names them as his country when he signs on, so that a captain is "of the Gray Highlands" in the hiring yards of [[place-valthari|Valthari]] and abroad. Their people, about 150,000 of them, live in the high valleys below the ridges and come up with the flocks in the summer drive.

## The Land {#the-land}

The ridges are broken and steep, with peaks that keep snow into summer. Beech and chestnut cover the lower slopes, and above them lie the high meadows, the _galtsur_, and above those the scree and the bare rock. The _ozkur_ (wind off the peaks) blows cold in every season, and in autumn the _durka_ (fog in the passes) sits on the saddles for days at a time. Snow closes the highlands from late autumn to spring.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]] holds the highlands, valley by valley. A mountain lord holds a valley and its share of the high meadows because his house has always held them, and he holds them of nobody. The [[lore-dilkorla|village assembly]] settles what concerns the whole village, and the shepherds' council orders the flocks at [[place-tsaberis|Tsaberis]] for the drive.

## Current Situation {#present}

The High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] is a man of the highlands. The electing council chose [[being-ularrekgarr|Ularrek Garrundeta]], an old lord of the high valleys and one of the king of Asturath's own mountain lords, because each of the greater candidates could block the others, and every lord in the kingdom knows that the High King is old. When he dies the lords meet again at [[place-lezkorahall|the High Hall]] to elect his successor.

Each spring the highlands send their young men down to the coast under contract. The cairns on Zurgando grow in the years of a long war abroad and shrink in a peace, and their height in late autumn is the count of the valleys' men still away.

## Faiths {#faiths}

The highland villages keep the Aureldian faiths in their village shrines and keep [[affiliation-menutsa|the Mother]] in the caves above them. On the night of the lanterns the valleys set an _ezkobe_ (lantern for the dead) on every threshold. Smaller [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] enclaves keep their sacred springs and groves in the highland interior, in country the mountain lords have no use for.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **The fog on the saddles.** A traveler who leaves the line of cairns on Zurgando in the _durka_ does not find it again, and the valleys keep stone huts on the passes for whoever must wait the fog out.
- **The snowmelt.** In spring the streams flood below the passes, and the paths along them go under.
- **The winter.** A party caught in the highlands by the first heavy snow is shut in until the thaw.
