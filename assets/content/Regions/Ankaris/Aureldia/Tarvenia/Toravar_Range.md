## Overview {#overview}

The Toravar Range is the great forested mountain belt of [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]]'s upper country. It has two arms. The northern arm runs east and west across the north and marks the border with [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]]. The western arm runs along Tarvénia's west and divides it from [[place-provenzrgn|Provenzia]]'s eastern march, [[place-talothe|the Talote]]. On a map the range is a frontier twice over. On the ground its high reaches are Áelendan country, among the most densely Áelendan land outside the [[place-alndntrblnds|Tribal Lands]] themselves.

## The Land {#the-land}

**The high reaches.** Above the forests the range is peak, high pasture and bare rock. The [[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan]] hold it as sacred high country, in the same way they hold the [[place-kervaron|Kervaron Spine]] to the east, and the great forested uplands that flank the range on both sides are Áelendan too.

**The northern arm.** Along the Élavendre border the range is a forested belt, and under it lie the highlands of [[place-eskarath|Eskarath]], whose people are older than the Tarveni.

**The western arm.** Along the Provenzian frontier the range is cold, and its passes are very nearly impassable from the first snow to the thaw. Under it lie the valleys of the [[place-westernmrch|Western March]]. Its passes go over into the Talote, and the chief of them is [Grumagando]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the war pass, which the March's hosts take down to the road at [[place-bldfldmalnebris|Malnebris]]. [[place-kemthoza|Kemthoza]] holds its head on the Tarvenan side.

## Who Uses It {#who-uses-it}

- **The Áelendan of the Toravar**, who are the same people as the clans of the Tribal Lands: they hold the same [Kindred Pact]{.name data-type="lore/law"}, recognize the same [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] and keep the same religious calendar, and they live in the high mountains because the high mountains are where they have always lived.
- **The Tarvenan barons** below the range, whose herds graze toward its pastures and whose loggers work its lower forests.
- **The hosts of the Western March**, which go down the western passes every season the snow allows ([[lore-summerwar|The Summer War]]), and the border families of the Talote, which come up them.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **The western passes in the season.** A party on Grumagando between the thaw and the first snow is on the road of a war, and the hosts and raiders of both sides use it.
- **The western passes out of it.** Snow closes them without warning in autumn, and a winter crossing is either a disaster or a deliberate outrage.
- **The clans' ground.** A party that cuts timber or grazes stock in the high reaches without the Wardens' leave answers to the clan whose ground it is.

## What Is Told {#what-is-told}

To the Áelendan reckoning [[place-drenavrspn|the Drenavar]], the Kervaron and the Toravar are not three ranges but one continuous sacred uplift, the high spine of the western world: the Drenavar is its heart, and the Kervaron and the Toravar are its eastern and southern arms. Outsiders distinguish them; the Áelendan distinguish them only when speaking to outsiders.

## Barons and Clans

The range is where Tarvenan barons and Áelendan clans meet. Frictions over straying herds, logging concessions and road-surveys near sacred groves are handled by the local Wardens with the local baron, usually without reaching the crown. The arrangement is the barons' and the Wardens', not royal policy. No Tarvenan king has tried to extend formal authority over the Áelendan of the Toravar, and the crown of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] is considerably less tolerant than Provenzia's of the clans' defense of their sacred sites.

Roughly once a generation a stubborn baron meets an unyielding clan. The baron pushes his herds or his loggers up into ground the clan holds sacred, the clan answers, and the Wardens and the baron cannot settle it between them. The result is a brief, ugly mountain-war between the baron's valley and the clan. The crown settles it quickly and quietly through Élavendri intermediaries.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The clans hold the high reaches, and no Tarvenan crown claims them. The barons below graze and log to the edge of the clans' ground, and the Wardens settle each quarrel with them one valley at a time. Every baron who pushes his herds or his loggers up into the high country risks a mountain-war, and the crown that settles one does it through Élavendri hands. In the western arm the passes are the March's road to the Summer War, and the Talote's walls at [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] wait at the bottom of them.
