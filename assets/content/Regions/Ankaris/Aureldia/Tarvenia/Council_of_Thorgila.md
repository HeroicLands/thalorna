## Overview {#overview}

The **Council of Thórgila** governs [[place-thorgila|Thórgila]], a hill town in [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]]. It is the town's meeting of its free men, after the Tarvenan tradition of [[lore-dilkorla|communal assembly]], and its one office of note is the horse-judge of the autumn fair.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The free men of Thórgila meet in council to decide what concerns the town in common, and a matter between neighbors goes to the council. Many of the town's men are away under contract for years at a time, and their wives run the farms and hold the contracts in their husbands' names. A sale at the fair is sworn at the oath stone. No lord holds the town, and the council answers to the crown of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] directly. Once a year the town holds its autumn horse fair, the _arthauna_, and the horse-judge, the _kornazdo_, sits over it. The present horse-judge is [[being-thelvarmozk|Thelvar Mozkarreta]]. His word on a horse's age settles a sale, and at the fair's verse-duel he sets each bout and says which way the stamping went, for the fair itself is the judge.

## Current Situation {#present}

The council has the autumn fair to keep, and the quarrels its notable residents carry home with them to keep out of it. The Árdavonian priest [[being-frstrndrvs|Father Ostaran Dorvás]], who lives in Thórgila, is investigating the lord [[being-nizelganotis|Nizel Ganotis]], and Lord Nizel would use bribery or violence to stop him. The fair brings buyers and sellers from every valley, and the lord's men can come to it as easily as anyone.

## History {#history}

The council's fair keeps the measure of the Thórgila valleys. A verse-duel sung at one autumn fair, between the woman the fairs called [Lírkeza]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Sling, and the crossbowman they called [Ozkarre]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Hail, is what the town sings back as [[lore-fairverses|the Verses of the Thórgila Fair]], and the fairs of the valleys take their measure from it.
