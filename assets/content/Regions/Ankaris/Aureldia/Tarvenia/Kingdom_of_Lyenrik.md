## Overview

The Kingdom of Lyenrik holds the land of [[place-leonrik|Lyenrik]]. Its rich farmland, productive vineyards, and thriving market towns make it the agricultural backbone of the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]]—and its ambitious nobility makes it the kingdom most likely to challenge the others for dominance.

## Character

Lyenrik has the most conventional feudal structure of any Tarvenan region. Its lords hold their lands from the king, who in turn owes nominal allegiance to the High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]]. In practice, Liénriki nobles are among the most politically active in the kingdom—their wealth gives them ambitions that the mountain lords of [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Asturath]] and the shipmasters of [[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Galvaren]] cannot easily match. Liénriki lords have contested the High Kingship more often than anyone, and several have worn the crown; the first, [Estaril]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, held it from about 605 AF, while [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia]] fought its _therkos_ (governor) and the Tarvénian hosts pressed west.

Lyenrik bore the brunt of Vilérian occupation during the imperial era ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]), and its architecture reflects this: fortified manor houses, walled towns, and a network of watchtowers that served as an early-warning system against risings and, once the garrisons were driven out, were turned to face the coast ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]). The families who keep the towers charge for the light. The occupation left a lasting mark on Liénriki culture. The kingdom's administrative traditions are more sophisticated than those of its neighbors, its legal codes more formalized, and its nobility more conscious of lineage and precedence—all legacies of Vilérian influence.

## The Crown and Its Court

The crown of Lyenrik descends to the king's eldest son, and in default of sons to his nearest male kinsman; a daughter carries a claim to her sons and holds none herself. The house of [Oltris]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} has held it since the kingdom was founded at the Falling Away, and the present king, [[being-zenotholtr|Zenoth Oltris]], keeps his court in the king's hall at [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]]. His heir sits in the [Court of Pleas]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} from his sixteenth year, to learn the kingdom's law where the kingdom's lords argue it; the present heir is the king's son [[being-zedmooltris|Zedmo Oltris]]. His council is made up of the Crown Councilors, a dozen great lords sworn to it, one of whom the king names Master of Commerce to keep the kingdom's trade and its dealings with Valthari; the present Master is [[being-hiltheratsol|Hilthera Tsoluregi]]. The question it argues most is the High Kingship of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]]: King Zenoth means to stand when it next falls vacant, and every lord on his council has an opinion on what his support will cost the king.

Lyenrik governs through its courts. The kingdom is divided into sixteen districts, each under a Bailiff who holds its court, raises its watch and answers for its dues. A matter between neighbors goes first to the village and the lord of the manor; a matter the lord cannot settle, or one against the lord, goes to the bailiff's court; and a matter between lords, or an appeal from a bailiff, goes to the Court of Pleas at Torvaleth under the Master of the Pleas. Every one of these courts writes its judgments down, and the plea rolls at Torvaleth hold every case the kingdom has heard. A Liénriki lord who loses a case goes looking in the rolls for an older judgment that would have given it to him, and the _lozo_ (advocate) who knows his way through the rolls lives well in Torvaleth on the search.

The other book the court keeps is the [Roll of Houses]{.name data-type="lore"}, the written order of precedence of every noble house of Lyenrik. A house's place on it decides where its head sits at the king's table, who walks before whom at a feast, whose banner leads in the host and whose daughter is a fit match for whose son. The Keeper of the Roll of Houses hears the claims of houses that think they deserve a higher place, and a claim is won by records: a charter, a grant, a judgment of the old imperial courts. That last kind is the sore point. Many of the oldest places on the Roll were won under the Empire, by houses that took its offices, and the houses that refused them and were passed over have been trying to prove it for nearly two centuries.

## The Towers and the Host

The kingdom's defense rests on [[place-ithrathorga|Ithrathorga]], the chain of watchtowers down the [Thalsira]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, and on the host its lords raise. The Warden of the Towers commands the chain from the castle at [[place-bruthena|Bruthena]], and the Towerkeepers who hold each tower by inheritance keep its beacon lit and its watch kept. A fire on any tower calls out the kingdom.

The host is the lords' muster. Every lord who holds land of the crown owes the king a number of mounted men for the length of a campaign season, set down for his fee in the Marshal's roll, and the Marshal of Lyenrik calls the musters and commands in the field. The host of Lyenrik is the largest in Tarvénia, and the richest; it is also the slowest to gather, because its lords argue precedence at the muster before they argue anything else. When the High King calls the ban the Liénriki lords come with their full musters, and they expect the High King to remember that they did.

The cities have their own garrisons. [[place-ronetho|Ornetho]] is held for the crown by its Castellan, by custom a son or brother of the king and at present the king's brother [[being-hiltheroltr|Hilther Oltris]], and Bruthena by the Warden of the Towers.

## Current Situation {#present}

King [[being-zenotholtr|Zenoth Oltris]] means to stand at the next election of a High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]], and the old High King, [[being-ularrekgarr|Ularrek Garrundeta]] of Asturath, is old. The lowland lords have begun sending their men up the road to [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] one and two at a time, and the king has not said who is paying for the climb. The question the Crown Councilors argue most is what each lord's support will cost.

[[being-iltherozedim|Ilthero Zedimis]], the oldest of the Crown Councilors, keeps the courtesies that Lyenrik's claim turns on: those owed to the king of [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Asturath]], who keeps the High King as his guest in the High Hall and holds the gate every elector climbs through, and those owed to the king of [[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Galvaren]]. The Keeper of the Roll of Houses meanwhile hears the claims of houses that think they deserve a higher place on the Roll, and the houses passed over for refusing imperial offices are still trying to prove it.

On the plain the Towerkeepers keep their beacons lit and take the light-due. The crown of Lyenrik and the crown of Galvaren quarrel over the tolls on the one road between them, and each is the other's best customer.

## Commerce and Currency

The Kingdom of Lyenrik uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian currency]] (Solden / Ardin / Ogdel) as its standard currency, with full access to the paper-script system through [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]]-member moneylenders chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]]. The kingdom is a post-imperial commonwealth member—it participates fully in the trade and banking infrastructure of the Vilérian system but is not subject to imperial governance or imperial taxation. See [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian Currency]] for the full system.

## History {#history}

### The Occupation (about 140 to 538 AF)

About 140 AF the legions of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] took the central lowlands and held Lyenrik as an occupied march ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]). The lords were watched from a chain of watchtowers and walled towns rather than dispossessed. They built fortified manors, learned the Empire's law in its courts, and took from it a precedence that the Roll of Houses still keeps. Some took the Empire's offices and some refused them, and which houses did which is still disputed. The legion lost in [[lore-horngorgebtl|the Horn Gorge]] about 143 AF stopped the Empire at the foot of the mountains.

### The Falling Away (about 538 to 545 AF)

From about 538 AF, with the legions drawn north, the lowlands stopped paying the Empire. The mountain lords elected a High King at Lezkora to drive the garrisons out, and the Liénriki turned the Empire's watchtowers from the highlands to the coast ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]). The kingdom of Lyenrik was founded in these years, and the house of Oltris has held its crown since.

### The Contested Crown (from about 605 AF)

[Estaril]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Lyenrik held the High Kingship from about 605 AF, the first of several Liénriki High Kings, and the lords of Lyenrik have contested the crown at every election since. The High King has still most often been an Astúrathi lord, and the present one is.
