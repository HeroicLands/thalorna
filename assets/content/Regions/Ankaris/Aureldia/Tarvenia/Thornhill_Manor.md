## Overview {#overview}

Thornhill Manor is the seat of **Lord [[being-zurgadeugarr|Zurgade]] of Thornhill Manor**, a Tarvenan lord who holds it of the crown of [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Lyenrik]]. It lies at the southern end of the [[place-leonrik|Lyenrik]] plain, off the road from [[place-bruthena|Bruthena]], a short ride north of [[place-valthari|Valthari]]: a fortified manor house, one village of about three hundred, its fields and vines, and the low hill of thorn and scrub oak that gives the manor its name. Its market is Valthari's. The manor is known to its neighbors for its hunting. The lord hunts often and with guests, and those guests include greater lords than he is.

## Current Events {#present}

At the last hunt of the autumn one of Lord Zurgade's guests shot a stag the lord had reserved for himself, and nobody has owned the arrow. The guests were greater men than their host, and the lord cannot ask them which of them did it. The huntswoman [[being-zoriladmvr|Zórila Dómivar]] was the only one who saw who loosed, and she has said nothing to anyone.

Her silence is her standing. Her place at the manor rests on the lord's standing, and his rests on Tarvénian politics: the High King has not come down to his hall at [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] for a month, and a change at court that threatens Lord Zurgade threatens everyone who holds a place at Thornhill because of him.

## Land and Livelihood {#land-and-livelihood}

The manor's land is the southern edge of the plain, where the wheat country runs down toward the salt marsh and the sand. Most of it is under wheat and barley in strips, worked by the village's tenants, with vines on the drier ground and hay meadow along a stream. The demesne, the lord's own land, takes about a third of the fields, and the tenants owe him labor on it and a share of their harvest. The grain is sold in Valthari to the merchant houses, at the houses' prices.

The thorn hill is the manor's pride. It rises a little above the plain, thick with thorn, scrub oak and bramble, and below it toward the coast a belt of pine and heath runs to the dunes. Both are kept for the hunt: deer and boar in the covert and the pines, hares and fowl on the heath, and the tenants may not cut, graze or snare in either.

## The Lord's Household {#lords-household}

Lord Zurgade is a heavy, weathered man in his thirties, a keen hunter and a generous host. His family lives in the manor house with him, with his steward, his servants and his retinue. The retinue counts the huntsmen and kennel men who keep the hunt, under Zórila Dómivar, who is often invited to hunt beside him and his guests and on whom he relies heavily in the hunting season.

## The Court and the Year {#court-and-year}

The lord holds his manor court in the hall of the manor house, as every lord of Lyenrik does, for matters between the tenants and quarrels over strips, labor and the hunt's rules. A matter against the lord goes to the bailiff's court of his district, and the court writes its judgments down, as Lyenrik's courts do.

The year turns on the fields and the hunt. The plain is sown in autumn and spring and harvested in early summer, and the vines are picked at the end of summer. When the High King or the King of Lyenrik calls the host, the lord owes his mounted men for the campaign season. The hunting season runs from the end of the vintage into late winter, and it is the season the manor is known for: the guests come then, and the house fills.

## Households {#households}

- **The manor house.** The lord, his family, his steward and his servants.
- **The kennels.** The huntsmen and kennel men, and the hounds. Zórila keeps a house in Valthari and lodges here in the hunting season. One of the older huntsmen has never accepted a woman in her post and is working to have her replaced.
- **The village.** About fifty tenant households under the lord, each holding its strips of the open fields and owing labor on the demesne.
- **The vine-dressers.** A few households on the drier ground who work the lord's vines and their own.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The arrow.** [[being-zoriladmvr|Zórila Dómivar]] knows which guest shot the lord's stag, and the guest has begun to show her a great deal of courtesy.
- **The kinsman.** A kinsman from the hunting family Zórila grew up in comes to Thornhill asking for a place in the retinue on the strength of her name, and the old huntsman who wants her gone takes him under his wing.
- **The offer.** A greater lord who hunted as Lord Zurgade's guest offers Zórila a post in his own household, and leaving Thornhill would cost her the trust she has built there.
