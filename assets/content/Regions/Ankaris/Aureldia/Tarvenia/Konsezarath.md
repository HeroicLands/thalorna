## Overview {#overview}

The **Konsezarath** is the council of the great merchant houses that governs the [[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]. It governs under the royal charter, the _zaktela_, that the first High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] sold the city for a yearly tribute ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]): its own courts, its own council and freedom from feudal levies. The council sits in [[place-valthari|Valthari]] itself, in the open, in the square before [[place-charterhall|the Charter Hall]], because a Tarvenan free city's council meets in the open. In foul weather it goes inside the hall.

The council decides what the city does: the tribute it pays and the terms it asks, the dues of the harbor, the coin the mint strikes, the officers who run the port, and the city's quarrels with its neighbors. It names the city's officers ([[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]) and enforces the rulings of its arbitrators. What it cannot do is sit in the High King's hall: the free city has no voice there.

## Who Sits {#who-sits}

The council is drawn from the dozen or so merchant dynasties that control the city's great trading houses, its [[place-vlthrshpyrds|shipyards]] and its banks. Each of them is written on the Roll of Great Houses, and the head of each house on the roll votes when a seat falls vacant. A seat goes to the House Head who can gather the votes, and seats are fiercely contested: a seat is won by commercial alliance and marriage pact, and occasionally by a discreet assassination that leaves one open.

First of the Council
: Presides over the council and holds the city's seal for a fixed term. The present First is [[being-orthemaiken|Orthema Ikentsis]], head of a house that ships wine and oil to the Taruzelian ports.

Keeper of the Roll
: Keeps the Roll of Great Houses. A house written onto it can vote; a house struck from it has lost its standing in the city's government, whatever its money.

## How It Decides {#how-it-decides}

The councilors sit in the open square, and anyone may stand at its edge and listen. A matter is settled by vote, _dil_, each councilor having one, and the First breaks a tie. The council's business is mostly money: the tribute, the harbor dues, the price of a charter it grants to a guild, and the loans it raises against the customs. A vote that touches a great house's trade is settled in the counting-houses before it is taken in the square, and the square knows it.

## What It Is Fighting About {#present}

**The charter and the crown.** The charter is one of the terms every High King swears at his election, and the new king is paid the year's tribute when he swears it ([[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]). The present High King has not come down to his hall at [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] for a month. The council is divided over what to send up the road when the hearth goes out: the tribute as it stands, and a councilor to hear the terms, or a larger sum to the lord most likely to win, with the risk of backing the wrong man.

**The narrows toll.** At [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]] the Taruzelian admiral [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]] presses the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation]]'s Grand Council for a toll on every hull through the narrows of the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]]. The houses that trade east to the Taruzelian ports would pay it, and the houses whose ships go no farther than the Provénzian and Galváreni coasts would not. The First's own house trades east, and the houses that do not say she cannot be trusted to argue the city's case.

**The share.** The seamen of Valthari have refused to sign on until the ship-owning houses raise the crew's share. The owning houses meet as the owners' council and have asked the pilot [[being-dalenavlrn|Dálena Valaron]], whose crew is loyal, to sail and break the refusal. The Konsezarath has not taken the matter up, and every house in it owns ships.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

A party meets the council standing at the edge of the square on a day it sits, or in a councilor's counting-house, where its business is really done. A petition reaches the council through a councilor who will put it; a stranger without one hires an advocate at the Charter Hall to find him. A quarrel between a stranger and a house goes first to the arbitrators, and the council enforces what they rule.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The road to Lezkora.** The First of the Council wants a party to ride to Lezkora and send word the day the High King's hearth goes out, before any other house's rider brings it, so that the council can choose its man first.
- **The struck house.** The Keeper of the Roll has been asked to strike a failing house from the roll, and the house's head offers a party good money to find out which councilor bought its debts.
