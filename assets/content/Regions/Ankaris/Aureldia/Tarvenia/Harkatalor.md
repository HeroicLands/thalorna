## Overview {#overview}

Harkatalor is the castle of the kings of [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Asturath]], the Astúrathi fortress that holds the north end of [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] on a knob of rock with its own wall. Its name is _harka_ (a king's hall) and _talor_ (stone), the stone hall, and it is the oldest building on the plateau. It is the seat of the kingdom and of its present king, [[being-alzemozold|Alzemo Zolduregi]]. The council of mountain lords meets in its hall every autumn, and every king of Asturath has been acknowledged at the oath stone in its yard.

The castle is a fortress inside a fortress. The city's own defense is the cliff and the plateau gate, and no army has ever reached the top. Harkatalor was built to hold out if one did: it has its own wall across the neck of its rock, its own gate and its own cisterns, and on three sides its walls stand on the cliff edge.

## Inside {#inside}

- **[1] The Neck Gate:** the castle's one gate, in a squat tower across the neck of rock that joins the knob to the city. A lane climbs to it from the city's square, and a ditch cut in the rock lies before it under a timber bridge that the gate's men can draw back. The gate is kept by the king's sworn men, and a lord's men wait in the lane while he goes in.
- **[2] The Yard:** an uneven court of bare rock inside the gate, with the castle's buildings round it. In its middle stands the council's _goitsa_ (oath stone), a gray standing stone older than the walls. Each lord grips a new king's forearm beside it and says the ban, and the stone is worn smooth at the height of a man's hand.
- **[3] The King's Hall:** the long hall on the yard's west side, roofed with stone slabs on stone arches, where the king keeps his court and the council of mountain lords sits. The lords sit on benches by valley in an order older than any of their houses, and the king's chair stands at the north end on a step of the living rock.
- **[4] The Shelter:** a cave under the north tower, the rock shelter the city is named for, with a hearth older than the castle. The household's tellings call it the first hall of the kings, and the castle's stores are kept in its inner end.
- **[5] The North Tower:** the tallest of the castle's towers, over the shelter on the cliff edge, from whose roof the watch can see the high valleys to [[place-tsaberis|Tsaberis]] and the lip of [[place-lezkorahall|the High Hall]] across the city.
- **[6] The Roll Room:** a vaulted room off the hall where the Keeper of the Roll keeps the kingdom's muster roll in chests, valley by valley.
- **[7] The Cisterns:** three, cut in the rock under the yard and filled from the roofs. The Astúrathi say the castle can hold a year's siege on them and its stores, and nobody has tested the claim.

## Who Is There {#who-is-there}

- **[[being-alzemozold|Alzemo Zolduregi]]**, king of Asturath, who holds his court in the hall.
- **[[being-ganothmozk|Ganoth Mozkarreta]]**, knight of the household, who keeps the muster roll as Keeper of the Roll and the king's own valleys as Steward of the King's Valleys.
- **[[being-garrutsaugar|Garrutsa Ugarreta]]** and the king's other heralds, who carry his word up the valleys and to other crowns.
- **The king's sworn men**, a few dozen, who keep the neck gate and the walls. The city's gate below is the Warden of the Plateau Gate's, not theirs.
- **The household**: stewards, grooms, cooks and the servants of the king's family.
- **The council of mountain lords**, about forty lords of the kingdom's valleys, for some weeks each autumn and whenever the king calls it.

## Two Courts and One Rock {#present}

Harkatalor looks across the city at the High Hall, where the High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]], [[being-ularrekgarr|Ularrek Garrundeta]], keeps his court as the king's guest. He is old and has not come down to his hall for a month. The king of Asturath is an elector, the lords of the lowlands have begun sending their men up the road one and two at a time, and the king of [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Lyenrik]] means to stand at the next election.

Alzemo has asked his Keeper of the Roll for a count of every valley's men, at home and abroad under contract, and Ganoth Mozkarreta is reading the roll through in the vaulted room. The heralds go up and down the plateau road more often than in any spring since the last election. The council of mountain lords is not due until the autumn, and the king has not said whether he means to call it sooner.

## History {#history}

The shelter under the north tower is older than anything written about Lezkora, and the castle grew over it. The kings of Asturath held it through the occupation, when the legions took the lowlands and left the mountains alone after [[lore-horngorgebtl|The Battle of the Horn Gorge]] about 143 AF. About 540 AF the mountain lords met on the plateau to elect [Garvaneth]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Asturath the first High King ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]), and the High King has kept his court in the High Hall since, in the castle's city. Every king of Asturath since the kingdom's own record begins has waited at the oath stone in the yard for the council's grip, some of them for a day.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The count in the roll room.** Ganoth Mozkarreta wants a valley's men counted where they live, because the roll and the valley disagree, and the king wants the number before the lords do.
- **The herald's road.** Garrutsa Ugarreta carries the king's word up the valleys on a road where lowland lords' men are also riding, and wants companions.
- **The guest across the city.** Alzemo Zolduregi wants to know who in the old High King's house is making ready for his death.
