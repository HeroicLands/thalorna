## Overview

Eskarath is the land of the [[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]], the highlands along the northern edge of the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]] where it borders [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]]. Its people, the Eskárathi, predate the Tarveni migration by centuries, and they keep their own tongue, their own law and their own spirits.

## The Land {#the-land}

Eskarath is mountain country: peaks and passes, springs, ancient oak groves and high meadows. Its people live in scattered mountain communities, each governed by the elders of its clans.

## Peoples {#peoples}

The Eskárathi are not Tarveni, and both peoples know it. They speak [[skill-eskarilng|Eskari]], a tongue related to no other, and [[skill-tarvenlng|Tarveni]] as their second language. Their folk practices have more in common with the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] across the border than with anything in lowland Tarvénia.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The clans hold their own valleys, and their elders govern them. What touches the whole territory goes to the [[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]], the council of elders that meets at the summer solstice in the high meadow of [[place-gaztorai|Gaztorai]]. The territory acknowledges the High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] in the loosest sense the word allows.

## Places {#places}

- [[place-gaztorai|Gaztorai]]: the high meadow where the Biltzarrath sits
- [[place-ibotsain|Ibotsain]]: a village round a spring and its shrine
- [[place-leparrun|Leparrun]]: a village below a pass, with a shrine on the pass
- [[place-gerdoibun|Gerdoibun]]: a village in a valley of oaks, with a shrine in its grove

## Current Situation {#present}

The old arrangement holds: the Eskárathi send their warriors when the High King calls the ban against a foreign threat, and the rest of Tarvénia leaves them alone. The Biltzarrath weighs every call against the terms the High King swore. Every attempt by an ambitious High King or a Vilérian _therkos_ to rule the highlands directly has ended badly for the outsider. The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] has no presence here, and the Eskárathi refuse it any say over their herb-lore, their weather-reading and their ancestral communion.

## Faiths {#faiths}

The Eskárathi honor the gods of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] in name, over an older tradition of mountain spirits, forest guardians and ancestral figures ([[affiliation-eskrthsprts|The Spirits of Eskarath]]). The tradition's shrines stand at the passes, the springs and the oak groves.

## History {#history}

When the Empire took the lowlands, a _therkos_ sent to rule Eskarath directly lost his escort in the high meadows and was not replaced ([[lore-lowlandstaken|The Lowlands Taken]]).
