## Overview

A harbor pilot at **Calypsa** will tell you the rule of the place before you are tied up: the city is nobody's vassal. It stands on a small, steep-sided island off the northern coast of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], barely large enough to hold the city proper, its terraced vineyards and a scattering of fishing villages, and its harbor is one of the best-sheltered anchorages in northern Ankaris. It pays no tribute to the Nordmen kingdoms, owes no fealty to Élavendre, and answers to no [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo]] authority beyond what its Captains' Council chooses to tolerate. That freedom is its principal product.

## The Harbor

Nordmal is the working language of the docks and the old city, and Valtari, a trade creole with Helionite roots, is spoken in the markets. A traveler from [[place-provenzrgn|Provènzia]] or the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Byzarian League]] is understood without much difficulty anywhere on the island. Weddings mix Nordmen drinking-songs with Aurèldían hymns, and the kitchens combine northern smoked fish with southern spice and olive oil. The local wine is, by the admission of its drinkers, indifferent.

Three landmarks anchor the waterfront. The Old Mole is the original stone harbor mole, built, local tradition says, by refugees from a forgotten Helionite colony; several of its stones carry characters no living scholar can read. The Lantern of Calypsa is a seamark tower on the northern headland, whose olive-oil beacon is relit every night, and whose lantern-keeper holds a hereditary office of real civic prestige. The Sea-Law Hall is where the city's professional arbitrators hear commercial disputes under the Sea-Law, and captains honor its verdicts across half the northern shipping world because none can afford the cost of defying them.

## Who Governs

The Captains' Council governs: the heads of the seafaring houses, with seats weighted by the tonnage each house commands. It elects a Harbourmaster for a single three-year term, and he may not serve again immediately. The Harbourmaster answers for the harbor, the fortifications, the customs revenue and the standing navy, so a visitor who arrives with cargo meets his office first. Family fortunes rise and fall with fleets: a family that loses three ships in a season may be elbowed from the Council within the year, and one that opens a new route may enter it. Guilds of shipwrights, chandlers, ropemakers and pilots run their own affairs under charters from the Council.

## Making a Living

The harbor is the economy. Calypsa levies modest tariffs on cargo landed, serious tariffs on cargo transshipped and crushing tariffs on cargo warehoused longer than a season, which forces capital through the city instead of letting it sit. The island itself produces almost nothing. It is steep, thin-soiled and short of fresh water, so Calypsa eats what it buys: grain from the Élavendri mainland, timber and furs from the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]], cloth from Provènzia, metalwork from [[affiliation-karatas|Karataş]]. The shipping houses jointly maintain a navy out of all proportion to the city's size, built to make raiding Calypsa or its sea-lanes cost more than it returns, and it is the reason the city has stayed free.
