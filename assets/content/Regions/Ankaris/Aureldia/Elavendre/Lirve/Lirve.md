**Lirve**—_the shore_—is [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]]'s western coast: some four hundred and twenty
thousand people along a seaboard of cliffs, coves and drowned river-mouths, with ancient forest running
down almost to the tideline.

The coast faces the open western ocean and is worked by the same cold, heavy swell that batters
[[place-provenzrgn|Provenzia]] further south. It is craggy, and the cliffs are real, but the country softens northward
toward [[place-lisere|Lísere]], where the cliffs give way to long shingle and sheltered inlets.

## The Sinale Coast

Lirve is the reason Élavendre is what it is. The [[lore-flksinale|Sinale]] keep their enclaves in
the deep forests here, and have since thousands of years before any human settlement—a fact the
kingdom lives with rather than administers. Outside [[place-sorvanda|Sórvanda]] this is where the
[[lore-aralwen|aralwain]] lie thickest: thin places, shifting paths, lights among the trees, and the
[[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] patrolling ground that is nominally the crown's.

Nothing is taken from the deep forest without a license, and the license is not the crown's to give
alone.

## The Economy

- **Fish and salt.** The cold western water is rich, and the coastal towns live on it. Salt-fish is the
  region's bulk export and the least interesting thing about it.
- **Ship timber and fine timber.** Lirve's oak and silver birch are the best in [[place-aureldirgn|Aureldia]], and cutting
  is **licensed, limited and watched**. What is released reaches shipyards as far as the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]]
  and commands prices that reflect the difficulty rather than the supply.
- **Dyestuffs**—coastal lichens, shellfish purple and woad from the cleared ground behind the cliffs.
  These feed the [[place-ellive|Éllive]] textile trade and are worth more by weight than most metals.
- **Pilgrim and scholar traffic.** People come to see the thin places. Some of them pay very well, and a
  quiet local trade exists in guiding them—legally to the edges, and otherwise past them.
