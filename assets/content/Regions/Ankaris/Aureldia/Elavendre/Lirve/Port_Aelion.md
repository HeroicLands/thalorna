## Overview {#overview}

**Port Aelion** stands on the western coast of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], where the great cliffs of [[place-lirve|Lirve]] yield to gentler slopes and a run of natural harbors gives shelter to merchant hulls and fishing boats alike. About 9,000 people live there, and it is the kingdom's chief harbor for sea trade: the timber of [[place-liranel|Liranel]] and the salt-fish of the coast go out over its wharves, and ships from distant realms come in. Its stone houses carry fae motifs in their carving, and its foundation stones are said to have been blessed by [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] who once walked the coastline.

The town is more cosmopolitan than any inland Élavendren town, and it is also stranger. Its people treat as plain fact what outsiders call sailors' tales: tides that sometimes keep their own hours, independent of any reckoning, and travelers who walk into the mist and come back days later saying that no time has passed.

## Arriving {#arriving}

A ship comes in under the headland, where the swell drops away, and is given a berth by the Keeper of the Wharves or his clerks before a rope is thrown. A traveler by land comes down from the coast road through orchards to the upper town, where the shrine of the patron hero stands at the highest point. The crown's law runs in Port Aelion, and a stranger's case for the crown goes to the royal court; a quarrel on the wharves is settled by the [[affiliation-twncnclprtaln|Town Council]].

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

**Timber.** Squared oak and birch come down from Liranel's yards, already measured at its tally-house, and the town's wharves load them without measuring them again. The shipwrights build from the same timber, cut under license by woodcutters such as [[being-thalrkhlrn|Thalrik Halren]], and longshorewomen such as [[being-porenadhrl|Pórena Darel]] load the holds.

**Salt-fish and dye.** The coast's fish and its lichen and shellfish dyes go out by sea to the realms that pay most for them.

**The Bataren run.** Ships from [[place-batarencity|Bataren City]], Provénzia's ocean port, coast up in two days with southern wine, oil and salt and go back with Élavendren manufactures.

**Envoys.** [[affiliation-clgenvoys|The Envoys' College]] of [[affiliation-bardicolgs|the Bardic Colleges]] stands in the town, where every foreign word comes ashore, and sends its bards out as envoys to houses and foreign courts. Its students listen in the public gallery of the council for a year before they are allowed to speak.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The [[affiliation-twncnclprtaln|Town Council of Port Aelion]] governs the town. The freemen choose some of its Councilors for the trades, the heads of the older families sit by custom, and the town says those families have fae blood. The merchant prince [[being-naldorkardet|Naldor Kardeth]] is its Keeper of the Wharves. The crown's Admiral, [[being-tenralsaltha|Tenral Salthar]], keeps his flag in the harbor and commands the crown's ships and coast-cutters from it, and the harbor's captains, among them [[being-tralkarherse|Tralkar Hérselnde]], sit together in a mariners' guild that the council hears on every question of the wharves.

## The Present {#present}

The harbor is arguing about the weather. The astrologer [[being-tarelblthl|Tárella Pelthal]] reads the sky for the Admiral and for Naldor Kardeth, whose fortune has followed her advice, and Tralkar Hérselnde, whose ship ignored her warning and was lost with its crew in the mist, tells every wharf that her readings cannot be trusted. An inquiry cleared her. The captains have not.

The [[affiliation-hsekardeth|House of Kardeth]] has its own trouble. Lord [[being-kenrelkardet|Kenrel Kardeth]] was killed on the road to [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]] three years ago in what the garrison there called a bandit raid, and his cousin [[being-heltankardet|Heltan Kardeth]], home from service abroad, is asking at every gathering in the town how bandits came to know which rider to take. His questions trouble the widow, [[being-glyencrdth|Lyenna Kardeth]], who heads the house.

## Faith {#faith}

The town's patron hero is [Aelion]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a half-legendary Élavendren hero said to have kept the peace between human and fae communities centuries ago. Her shrine stands at the highest point of the town, and captains make an offering there before they sail. The faiths of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] keep their houses below it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The lost crew.** Tralkar Hérselnde wants proof that Tárella's reading was wrong, and Tárella wants proof that the mist that took his ship was not weather.
- **A question at a gathering.** Heltan Kardeth asks the party to carry a copy of the Elanmere garrison's report to someone who can read it without the family's manners.
- **The College's report.** A foreign envoy in the harbor says the Envoys' College sold its report on his court before it was delivered.
