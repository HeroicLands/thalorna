## Overview {#overview}

**Siralonke**, the sea wood, is the deep forest of [[place-lirve|Lirve]]: old oak and silver birch running from the licensed coppice behind [[place-liranel|Liranel]] back toward the enclave of [[place-serenthale|Serenthale]]. Its margins are cut under the crown's license. Its interior is sacred ground of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]], far from their mountains, and the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] keep it as strictly as any ground on the Spine.

## The Land {#the-land}

The coppice behind Liranel is cut in blocks, each block numbered on the roll of the crown's forest court and each stem measured at its tally-house. Past the last numbered block the oaks stand broad and far apart, with birch in the gaps and bracken to the waist, and the light under them is green in summer and gray from the sea in winter. Rides cut by the Wardens run through it, and they do not run where a stranger expects.

The wood holds more than one [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]]. The best known is [[place-wrghthllw|the Wrought Hollow]], out of which made things walk by night. The [[being-blinkfox|blinkfoxes]] of the coast hunt round the thin places.

## Who Uses It {#who-uses-it}

- **The woodcutters of Liranel,** in the licensed belt, under a license from the forest court.
- **The guides of Liranel,** who take pilgrims and scholars to the margins and, for more money, past them.
- **The Wardens of Siralonke,** among them [[being-kelpanedhar|Kélpane]] of the Dhárgandi and [[being-thandreltirin|Thandrel]] of the Tírindi, who patrol the interior and turn back whoever comes in without leave.
- **The groves.** The [[affiliation-groveconcl|Grove Council]] keeps groves at the margin, and the Áelendan keep their own.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **The Wardens.** A trespasser is warned once and turned out. One who comes back, or who takes anything from an Araelwen, is dealt with finally, and no royal court hears his side.
- **The paths.** A ride that led in does not always lead out, and a guide who knows the margins does not know the interior.
- **The made things.** They pay no heed to watchers who keep still.

## What Is Told {#what-is-told}

The woodcutters say that stems found felled and stacked overnight in the deep wood are not counted against a license, and that a man who takes one is followed home. The guides say that the Wardens can count a party from a mile off and know which of its members has been warned before. The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] keeps one made thing out of Siralonke, folded and silent, in a chapterhouse collection at [[place-beravel|Péravel]].

## Converging Interests {#present}

An Order fellow, [[being-ivelinnareth|Ivelin Nareth]], is hiring sellswords at Liranel for a night in Siralonke and a second made thing that still walks ([[scenario-crpntrsnght|The Carpenter's Night]]). A licensed block at the margin has been cut down to a ring of great oaks that a grove-elder of the Council, [[being-evranetolsel|Evrane Tolsel]], swears was a Council grove, and the Wardens have turned the cutters' carts back once ([[scenario-grvthtsntrs|The Grove That Is Not Ours]]). The forest court wants its licenses honored, the Wardens want the interior left alone, and the inns of Liranel are full of guards waiting for a scholar who names no destination.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The night in the wood.** Ivelin Nareth hires the party to guard his cart and nets for one night in Siralonke and a fast drive out of it.
- **The felled ring.** A grove-elder asks the party to learn whose ground the felled oaks stood on before the Wardens act.
- **A lost axe.** A Liranel woodcutter lost his grandfather's axe in the wood and has heard where lost things there are found.
