## Overview {#overview}

**The Wrought Hollow** is a steep-sided hollow in [[place-siralonke|Siralonke]], the old oak and silver birch forest of [[place-lirve|Lirve]] that runs from behind [[place-liranel|Liranel]] toward the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale]] country. Out of it, at night, walk things that were made rather than born: figures of root and wood, of stone bound with root, of antler and bark, from the size of a hound to twice the height of a man. They go about the forest on errands of their own and go back into the hollow before light. It is an [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] whose door lets in the fae as constructs, and it is sacred ground of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]].

People go to it for what the made things are. The woodcutters of the licensed coppice find their tracks, and a few have seen one cross a ride in the moonlight. The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] has one of them, and wants another.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] who patrol Lirve's deep forest hold the hollow, on ground that is nominally the crown's. Liranel's forest court licenses no cutting within a long way of it, and its tally-clerks know the bounds without having seen them. The Wardens keep no watch at the lip, because what comes out of the hollow goes back into it. They keep a watch on the people who come looking.

## Layers {#history}

Who made the things that walk out of the hollow, and why, is not known, and the Áelendan do not guess.

**The Áelendan account.** The Wardens say the made things are of the far side, that the hollow is their door, and that they are fae as the trees of the Silver Forests are fae. They are not to be hindered, followed or touched.

**Liranel's account.** The woodcutters of Liranel call them the forest's carpenters, and say that a tree found felled and stacked overnight in the deep wood was felled by them. A woodcutter who finds such a stack leaves it.

**The Order's reading.** Order theorists hold that the made things are workings left running by an older art, without minds of their own, and that a working can be read, copied and stopped. The one in the Order's keeping has been examined for years on that ground.

## What Is There Now {#present}

A construct of root and stone, as tall as a man and folded on itself, stands in a chapterhouse collection at [[place-beravel|Péravel]], brought out of Siralonke by an expedition that did not stop at its guides' margins. It does not move. An Order fellow has hired guards at Liranel to bring out a second, one that still walks, and the crown's guides are not going with him. Liranel's other guides, who take pilgrims past the margins for pay, have been asked to show him the way.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbours}

- **The made things,** which defend what they carry and do not tolerate being held.
- **Blinkfoxes,** the small dreadspawn of Lirve's deep forest, which hunt round the thin places and step out of the world to bite ([[being-blinkfox|Blinkfox]]).
- **The Wardens,** who know Siralonke better than any guide out of Liranel.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

**The rides.** The licensed coppice gives way to old oak and silver birch, and the rides the woodcutters use run out. Here and there across the forest floor runs a track of round prints, deep and evenly spaced, that ends at a stream or a rock face.

**The lip.** A ring of old oak round a hollow a long stone's throw across. The slope inside is bare earth, trodden hard.

**The floor.** A level of packed earth and leaf at the bottom, with a heap of stones in the middle of it, laid without mortar into a shape too regular to be a cairn. Around it lie things the made things have carried in: a stacked log, a dead deer laid out straight, a woodcutter's lost axe, a lantern.

**The made things.** On a moonlit night, between midnight and the gray, a party at the lip sees them come up out of the floor past the stones, one or two at a time, and go off into Siralonke. They pay no heed to watchers who keep still.

:::secret {title="The truth of the Wrought Hollow"}
The made things carry what they find down into the hollow, and nothing carried in is ever carried out. A thing taken from the floor is followed: the made things come for it the next night and every night after, and they take it back from whoever holds it, along with whatever is in the way. They do not pursue past the edge of Siralonke, and a thing carried out of the forest is not followed farther.

The construct at Péravel stopped when it was carried out of the forest. Last winter, when it was moved within its case, a chapterhouse servant saw its hand close, and the chapterhouse has told nobody. It is not dead. Brought back within Siralonke, it walks to the hollow.

The Order fellow's party means to take one on a moonlit night with nets and a cart, and to be out of the forest before dawn. The road to Liranel is a night's walk.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The cart and the nets.** The Order fellow at Liranel hires the party as guards for one night in Siralonke and a fast drive out of it.
- **A woodcutter's axe.** A Liranel woodcutter lost his grandfather's axe in Siralonke and has heard where lost things in that wood are found. He wants it back.
- **The guide who went past the margin.** A Liranel guide took the Order fellow's scouts to the lip and has not come home, and his wife asks the party to look for him before the Wardens do.
