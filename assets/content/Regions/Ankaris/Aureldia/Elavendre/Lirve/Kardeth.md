## Overview {#overview}

Kardeth is a village of about 250 and a noble seat in [[place-lirve|Lirve]], on the western coast of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], within reach of the forest town of [[place-liranel|Liranel]]. It is the old seat of the [[affiliation-hsekardeth|House of Kardeth]], and it is held for the house by **Lady [[being-auliranekard|Áulirane Kardeth]]**, a noblewoman of twenty-one with ancestry among the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan People]]. Kardeth is an Élavendri name, and it is also borne as a family name by Élavendri households as far apart as [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]] and [[place-sourimael|Sóurimel]].

## The Holding {#the-holding}

Kardeth belongs to the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]], and its lady holds the seat under the crown. Among the [[lore-elavendriclt|Élavendri]] the land in a lord's keeping is a duty and not a possession. A household turns first to its own house, second to its lord and third to the village healer, and a lord whose people go hungry has failed the duty the rank exists for. In a bad year the lady's stewardship is the first thing Kardeth draws on.

The country around Liranel is licensed forest. The Crown's forest court at Liranel issues the licenses and its tally-house measures every stem cut, and past the licensed belt the old forest is the country of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]], patrolled by the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]].

## Who Leads It {#who-leads}

Lady Áulirane is Kardeth's lord. A quarrel in the village goes to the grove-elder, who tries cases in the villages of Élavendre, and a case for the crown goes to the royal courts of the shire; the crown's sheriffs collect what Kardeth owes. The lady is intelligent and ambitious, and she shares a certain pragmatism about the gap between public virtue and private necessity.

## The Lady's Remedies {#present}

Lady Áulirane has begun seeking out [[being-belrkflwyn|Melrik Salvin]], the alchemist of Liranel, for discreet remedies, and her patronage has become important to him. He knows it to be dangerous as well. Noble favor in Lirve can be withdrawn as quickly as it is given, what a lady asks for in private is exactly what she denies in public, and Melrik suspects that she may be testing his loyalty for [[affiliation-bardicolgs|the Bardic Circle]]. What the remedies are for, the lady has not said.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The test.** [[being-belrkflwyn|Melrik Salvin]] wants to know whether Lady Áulirane is testing him for the Bardic Circle before he makes her next remedy, and he cannot ask her. He pays strangers to spend a few days at Kardeth and learn whom the lady answers to, if anyone.
