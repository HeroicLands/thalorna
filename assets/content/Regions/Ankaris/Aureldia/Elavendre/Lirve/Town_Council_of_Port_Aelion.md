## Overview {#overview}

The **Town Council of Port Aelion** governs [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]], a town of about 9,000 on the western coast of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] and the kingdom's chief harbor, where the cliffs of [[place-lirve|Lirve]] give way to gentler slopes and natural harbors. It blends traditional human governance with the counsel of older families rumored to possess fae ancestry, and its decisions are made by principles written in no law code.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

Two kinds of Councilor sit together. The freemen of the town choose some for its trades, and the heads of Port Aelion's older families sit by custom. The balance between them is kept by compromise and mutual respect rather than by a written rule, and visitors sense that the council decides by principles no law code holds.

The council chooses a **Speaker of the Council** from its Councilors to preside over its sittings and to speak for the town to the crown's officers, and the Speaker is [[being-lamaineolnar|Lámaine Ólnarnde]], head of one of the older families; and it names a **Keeper of the Wharves** to keep the town's wharves and give their berths. Outlawry is the heaviest thing it does: one it puts out of Port Aelion loses the protection of its law and is unwelcome inside its walls.

## Current Situation {#present}

Port Aelion is more cosmopolitan than any inland Élavendren town, and the council governs its merchants from distant realms by principles they cannot look up. This winter it is hearing the harbor's quarrel over the astrologer [[being-tarelblthl|Tárella Pelthal]], whom the merchant captain [[being-tralkarherse|Tralkar Hérselnde]] blames for the ship and crew he lost in the mist, and whom an inquiry has already cleared. The Keeper of the Wharves, [[being-naldorkardet|Naldor Kardeth]], follows her readings and has prospered by them, and half the captains count him her accomplice.

The timber that crosses the Keeper's wharves comes down from [[place-liranel|Liranel]], where the Crown's forest court licenses every stem and the licenses are disputed and, often enough, quietly exceeded. What reaches Port Aelion has been measured at Liranel's tally-house, and the council's wharves load it without measuring it again.

## Law {#law}

The crown's law runs in Port Aelion, and a case for the crown goes to the royal courts. The council settles the town's own business, and it settles it as the town has always settled it rather than by any written rule.

## Commerce and Currency {#commerce}

Port Aelion is Élavendre's gateway for sea trade, and its wharves carry commerce between the kingdom and distant realms. Timber from Liranel goes out from them, and so does the salt-fish of the coast. Its markets honor both the traditional crafts and the subtle magics of Élavendre. The harbor's captains sit together in a mariners' guild that the council hears on every question of the wharves; the shipwrights and carpenters build from timber that woodcutters such as [[being-thalrkhlrn|Thalrik Halren]] cut under license; and longshorewomen such as [[being-porenadhrl|Pórena Darel]] load the cargo.

## Faith {#faith}

The town's patron hero is [Aelion]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a half-legendary Élavendren hero said to have kept the peace between human and fae communities centuries ago. Her shrine stands at the highest point of the town, and captains make offerings there before setting sail.

## History {#history}

Port Aelion's foundation stones are said to have been blessed by members of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]] who once walked the coastline. The council's blend of the town's freemen and its older families is a balance kept through centuries of compromise and mutual respect, and the town grew into the kingdom's chief harbor under it.

## Relations {#relations}

Port Aelion belongs to the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]], whose crown keeps its law and its courts. Its timber comes through the Crown's forest court at Liranel, and the old forest above the licensed belt is the country the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] patrol and nobody cuts.
