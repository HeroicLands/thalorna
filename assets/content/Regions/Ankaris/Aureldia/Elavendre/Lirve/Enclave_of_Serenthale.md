## Overview {#overview}

The **Enclave of Serenthale** is the community of about ninety of the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale Folk]] at [[place-serenthale|Serenthale]], which the Sinale call _Serentale_. It lies somewhere in the old forest of [[place-lirve|Lirve]], between the coast cliffs and the hill line, where the deep wood runs almost to the sea. It faces west, and it is by a wide margin the most withdrawn of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]]'s three enclaves. The Sinale have kings, queens and lords of their own, who do not work as human ones do, and the enclave is governed by its lord.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

A Sinalo of Serenthale belongs to a hearth the enclave keeps. The [[lore-sinaleclt|Sinale]] raise a lord for things done, by every voice, and no human record names the lord of Serenthale. One the lord drives out is _vierka_, an outlaw, given no standing or shelter.

Serenthale keeps to itself even from the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]]. Its contact with them is real, respectful and rare, and it happens at the enclave's initiative and never at anyone else's. No Áelendan guides anyone to it.

## Current Situation {#present}

Every [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Arcane Order]] expedition that comes to Lirve looking for a Sinale enclave is looking, whether it knows the name or not, for Serenthale, and none has been taken anywhere near it. The crown's guides out of [[place-liranel|Liranel]] conduct the expeditions with courtesy and inexhaustible resource and bring them back having seen a great deal and found nothing. The Áelendan who could find the enclave would not, for the crown or for anyone. The guides for hire in Liranel do not stop at the margins, and they account for a respectable share of the people who do not come back out of the wood.

Nothing is taken from Lirve's deep forest without a license, and the license is not the crown's to give alone.

## Defense {#arms}

The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] patrol the approaches as they patrol every sacred ground, and at Serenthale the arrangement differs in one respect: the Wardens do not know where the enclave is. They know the country it is in, they turn back anyone who goes too far into it, and they have never asked for more than that. Past them, the paths that lead to a Sinale enclave lead elsewhere.

## The Sea {#holdings}

The Sinale of Serenthale are held, by other Sinale, to be concerned with the ocean in some way that the mountain enclaves are not. That is the only opinion on the matter anyone has recorded, and what the concern is has never been explained to a human.

## History {#history}

The Sinale kept their enclaves in the deep forests of Lirve for thousands of years before any human settled the coast, and the kingdom lives with that fact rather than administers it. Nothing of Serenthale's own past has come out of the enclave.

## Relations {#relations}

- **The Áelendan** have rare contact with it, at its choosing.
- **The crown of Élavendre** holds the formal relation with the enclaves, and its guides at Liranel keep the Arcane Order away from this one.
- **The Arcane Order** looks for it and does not find it.
- **[[affiliation-nclvthlss|Ethalosse]]** is the door through which the Sinale deal with human powers.
