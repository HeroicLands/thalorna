The **Enclave of Serenthalë** is the Sinalë community of [[place-serenthale|Serenthalë]], in the old forest of [[place-lirve|Lírvë]]. Sinalë have kings, queens and lords, but these do not work as human ones do, and the enclave is governed by its lord. It keeps to itself even from the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]]; the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] patrol the approaches and do not know where the enclave lies.
