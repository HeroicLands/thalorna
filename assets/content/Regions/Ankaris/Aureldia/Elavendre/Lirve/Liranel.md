**Liranel** stands a few miles inland of the [[place-lirve|Lirve]] cliffs, in the belt of worked
forest between the coast road and the deep wood. Some three thousand four hundred people live there,
and its business is timber and everything that follows from timber.

## The Edge

Liranel matters because of where it stops. East and north of the town the managed coppice and oak give
way to the old forest, and the old forest is the country of the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale Folk]] and
patrolled by the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]]. Liranel is where the licenses to cut are issued, recorded,
disputed and—often enough—quietly exceeded.

The town holds the Crown's forest court, the tally-house where every licensed stem is measured, and the
yards where the timber is squared before it goes down to [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]] for shipping.
It also holds a standing population of people whose living depends on knowing exactly where the line
is: hauliers, foresters, tally-clerks, and guides.

## The Guides

The other trade. People come to Lirve to see the thin places, and Liranel is where they hire someone
to take them. The legitimate version escorts scholars and pilgrims to the margins, which the Wardens
tolerate. The other version does not stop at the margins, is very well paid, and accounts for a
respectable share of the people who do not come back out of the wood.

Liranel is also where the **crown's** guides are found—the courteous, capable, officially provided
escorts who conduct [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Arcane Order]] expeditions into the interior and see to it that
the expedition returns having seen a great deal and found nothing. Half the town knows which of its
guides are the crown's and which are for hire, and nobody explains the difference to visitors.

The town's magistrates take a dim public view and a pragmatic private one.

## The Present {#present}

Liranel is busy with the wrong kind of hiring. An [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] fellow is taking on guards for a moonlit night at [[place-wrghthllw|the Wrought Hollow]] with nets and a cart, and the crown's guide [[being-lenirasrdln|Lénira Sérdalnde]], who has brought every party she led home alive, will not go with him. The hired guide [[being-berinsalth|Berin Salthar]] took a second party past the margin and has not come back, and his wife [[being-sedrasalth|Sedra]] waits for him on the bench in the herb garden of the temple of [[affiliation-lizelas|Lizelas]], where the priestess [[being-delithakelva|Délitha Kélvasende]] has no answer for the families who wait.

The forest court has a quarrel of its own. A ring of old oak inside the block that the timber factor [[being-garrelpelth|Garrel Pelthal]] holds under license has been felled, the grove-elder [[being-evranetolsel|Evrane Tolsel]] says it stood inside her grove's bounds, and the Wardens say it was Áelendan ground and that someone moved the bounds on the tally-house roll ([[scenario-grvthtsntrs|The Grove That Is Not Ours]]).

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The cart and the nets.** The Order fellow hires the party to guard his night at the Wrought Hollow, and Lénira tells them plainly what she thinks of it.
- **The missing guide.** Sedra asks the party to learn where her husband is, and the only people who know are the Wardens.
- **The roll.** A tally-clerk at the forest court wants to be taken to Péravel before anyone asks him about the bounds of a licensed block.
