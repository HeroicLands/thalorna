## Overview {#overview}

Hélithron is a village of about 300 in [[place-lirve|Lirve]], on the western coast of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], in the country around the forest town of [[place-liranel|Liranel]] where the alchemist [[being-sevnmyrkls|Sevren Mírkelis]] does his work. It is an ordinary village with an unordinary watch on it: the men of [Úrthaven Tárvenel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a captain of hired guards out of Liranel who takes paying parties past the margin, have been seen watching the road through Hélithron.

## The Village {#the-village}

Hélithron lies in the licensed forest of the inner coast. The Crown's forest court at Liranel issues the licenses to cut, the tally-house there measures every licensed stem, and the timber goes down to [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]] for shipping. Past the licensed belt the old forest is the country of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]], and the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] turn back anyone who goes too far into it.

## Who Leads It {#who-leads}

Hélithron belongs to the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]], and its reeve, [[being-kelvasehalrn|Kélvase Halren]], elected each spring, keeps its coppice rights, its hauling roster and its tally against the forest court. A quarrel in the village goes to the grove-elder, as in every [[lore-elavendriclt|Élavendri]] village, a case for the crown goes to the royal courts of the shire, and the crown's sheriffs collect what the village owes.

## Úrthaven's Watch {#present}

Úrthaven once asked Sevren for an elixir that would make his men fearless in a fight with a rival company of guides, and Sevren refused to sell it. That refusal made Úrthaven a dangerous enemy. Sevren avoids any direct confrontation, and he reads the men at Hélithron as a sign that retaliation is coming. Nobody has asked the men what they are waiting for, and anyone who travels with Sevren through the village travels inside Úrthaven's grudge. The hauling season begins at the thaw, the village's carts go down that road to the yards at Liranel every day of it, and the reeve wants the road clear before the first of them.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The road through.** [[being-sevnmyrkls|Sevren Mírkelis]] has to pass through Hélithron and wants company that can fight. He does not want to know whether the men are there for him until he is past them.
- **What they want.** Sevren pays strangers to go to Hélithron ahead of him and learn what Úrthaven's men are waiting for, without letting the men learn who sent them.
