## Overview

Hélithron is a settlement of [[place-lirve|Lírvë]], in the country around [[place-liranel|Liranel]] where the alchemist [[being-sevnmyrkls|Sévren Mírkelis]] does his work. It is an ordinary place with an unordinary watch on it: the men of **Jarl Kervan Redblade**, a notorious warlord and privateer, have been seen lurking around Hélithron.

## Why They Are Watching

Kervan once asked Sévren for a dangerous elixir that heightens a fighter in combat, meaning to use it to win a bloody power struggle, and Sévren refused to sell it. That refusal made Kervan a dangerous enemy. Sévren avoids any direct confrontation, but he reads the men at Hélithron as a sign that retaliation is coming, and anyone who travels with him through the settlement travels inside Kervan's grudge.
