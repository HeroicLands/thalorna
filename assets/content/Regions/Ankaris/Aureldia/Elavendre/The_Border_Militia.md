## Overview {#overview}

The **Border Militia** is the crown's standing arm. It is a few companies of sworn soldiers posted where the kingdom is entered: the fort at [[place-durmelen|Durmelen]], the walled town of [[place-valdun|Valdun]], the road posts of the [[affiliation-morthalwtch|Morthal Watch]] and the forest patrols along the edge of the tribal country. Beside it stands the **levy**, the men of the shires and the hills who come up by turns and go home again, and with the levy the knights who owe their muster in person.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The crown names the **Captain of the Border Militia**, [[being-elenorthaurn|Captain Élenor Thaurnde]], a professional soldier who commands the sworn companies and moves their serjeants from post to post. Each post has its own Captain or Castellan, and each answers to the crown for its ground. The Captain of the Border Militia answers for the whole.

The levy is owed by rank. A **tenant** owes it with his holding, and a **knight** owes his muster in person. A **Muster-Serjeant** keeps the rolls of a district and calls each village's turn, so that a village sends its men by weeks and not all at once. The **Hill-Captain of Valdun** calls the hill levy, who are drovers, foresters and smiths on Áuldalon ponies, and leads it himself. The hill levy's officers are local men.

A man who deserts his post is struck from the rolls, and in a village that name follows him home.

## Current Situation {#present}

The militia is thin everywhere and says so. The Castellan of Durmelen has asked [[place-beravel|Péravel]] for sixty more men and been told the matter is under consideration. The Order hires guards at [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]] and [[place-liranel|Liranel]] at wages the crown does not match, and the Muster-Serjeants find their rolls full of names that do not come up. Captain Élenor has kept the sworn companies at strength by moving serjeants from quiet posts to loud ones, and the quiet posts have noticed.

## Arms {#arms}

The sworn man carries a spear, a shield and a short sword, and the Rider of the hills has a short bow besides. The levyman carries what he owns, usually a spear and an ash-wood shield, and brings meal for his weeks. The forest patrols go in pairs or threes, bow in hand and lightly clad, and know the paths along the tribal country's edge better than the roads. [[being-belrktrvnl|Melrik Tárvenel]] is one of them.

The crown's ships and coast-cutters are the Admiral's, not the militia's. A fire on the tower at Durmelen calls both, and the Admiral's cutters answer it at Sarmelis.

The militia does not enter the tribal country. The crown's oath stops at the tree line, and the Captain of the Border Militia puts it to every serjeant on the day he is given a post.

## History {#history}

The levy is the older of the two bodies. The hill levy was the force that harried a Vilérian legion into the forest margin without giving it battle ([[lore-elvtribute|The Tribute of Élavendre]]), and the shore's levy is older than the stone at Durmelen. The sworn companies were added after the Nordmen fleets burned the north shore, when the crown held the head of the bay with stone and found that its levy could not be raised in the time a fleet took to come and go ([[lore-northshorefire|The Burning of the North Shore]]).

## Relations {#relations}

The militia answers to the crown and has no quarrel with the Áelendan, who keep their own watches and their own count. It does not treat with the Nordmen and takes the crown's oath against them. Its relations with the hill levy are cordial in the field and cool in the hall, because the hills think the court has never come in time. The Order is a rival for the militia's men and not for its ground.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The roll.** A Muster-Serjeant's rolls show a village that has sent no one in three winters, and the village says nobody asked.
- **The patrol.** A forest patrol of three has come back with two and a story about the tribal country's edge that the Captain does not want repeated.
