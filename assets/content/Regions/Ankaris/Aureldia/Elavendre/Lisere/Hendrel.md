## Overview {#overview}

**Hendrel** is a village of about 190 people, a mile back from the shingle on the [[place-coastroad|Coast Road]], five days' walk from [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]]. Its name is built on _henda_, the Élavendri word for fire. The village is a beacon village, one of the families on the [[place-lisere|Lísere]] shore whose first work is a light on a headland. Its beacon-house stands half a mile away on the point, and no night passes without a watch in it.

## The Village {#the-village}

The houses sit in a hollow of the dunes with their backs to the sea wind, long and low, roofed in turf over tarred planks, and built close together so that the wind finds no gap. Between the houses and the shore lies a belt of gorse and thorn. The boats are not kept at Hendrel at all but at a cove a mile along, drawn up where they can be launched in an hour and abandoned in a minute.

A path of flat shingle-stones leads from the village to the beacon-house. Every household has its stones, and a child learns the path in the dark before it learns to read the weather.

## The Holding {#the-holding}

The village holds a strip of shore, a drove of black cattle on the dune pasture, and a right of fuel from the coastal forest behind it: split pine and a measure of pitch for every night the beacon is lit. In return it gives the crown the beacon-watch and the levy. Fish are caught from the cove, and the village sells salt-fish to Sarmelis through the council's quay.

A gap in the reeds beside the village is where the old drove track leaves the road for the river that the [[place-dskfrd|Dusk Ford]] crosses, a day's walk inland. The cattle are kept off the track, and so are the children.

## Who Leads It {#who-leads}

Hendrel is led by the head of its oldest line, [[being-taldorpardal|Taldor Párdalen]], who keeps the beacon-house and sits on the council of the [[affiliation-beaconfamlys|Beacon Families]]. The village judges its own quarrels in the beacon-house, and the grove-elder who tries cases in the villages of this coast comes down twice a year. The crown's sheriff collects what Hendrel owes through a clerk who comes in the autumn and does not stay the night.

## The Failing or the Quarrel {#present}

A beacon-station that keeps its light on the stretch behind the Ford is raided less than its neighbors, and Hendrel loses fewer people to raiders than any village on the shore. The village treats that as a matter of being careful and as nothing else. A scholar from the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] has passed through, asking about the Ford and then about the families' kin, and has been told the road is open and the Ford is shut after dusk.

Taldor's quarrel is nearer home: his daughter has taken up with a deckhand from Sarmelis and means to go with the Order scholar's guards, who are hired for a fortnight at [[place-fndr|the Fen Door]], where she has no business.

:::secret {title="The line that asked"}
Taldor's oldest line is the line of the beacon-keeper who asked the Toll-Keeper to stand between the raiders and the land. Its kin cross the Ford free by day or by dusk, and they carry a knotted cord at the belt that the Toll-Keeper knows. The line keeps the knowledge from the village's younger families and from the crown, because a bargain that favors one line would set the coast against it. Taldor knows what the Ford has taken from raiders. Nobody in the line knows what the Toll-Keeper asks of it when the pact comes due.
:::

## What Is Seen from It {#what-is-seen}

From the beacon-house on the point the watch sees the neighboring headlands of the bay's southern shore. The next stations to the northeast and southwest answer its fire within the minute; the stations to the northeast relay the signal to [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]]'s Lamp Tower. What the watch sees on the water is small boats, fishing luggers and, some nights, a darker shape that does not carry lamps.

## The Cost of Reaching It {#cost-of-reaching-it}

Hendrel lies on the Coast Road, five days from Sarmelis, and the road is easy: dry shingle, then packed sand. The cost is at the village. A stranger is received courteously at the first house and watched at every house after it. A party that asks for the Ford gets directions to the gap in the reeds and a warning about the hour. A party that asks to cross it gets a long silence.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The daughter.** Taldor asks the party to bring his daughter home from the Order's guards before they set out for the Fen Door, and will not say why the Fen Door worries him.
- **The dark shape.** The beacon-watch has seen a darker shape on the water for three nights running, carrying no lamp, and Taldor does not want to light the fire until someone else has seen it too.
- **The knotted cord.** A cord of knotted reed has washed up at the cove, and the oldest woman in the village wants it back before anyone sees it.
