## Overview {#overview}

**Olvalonke**, the evening wood, is the wet forest behind the [[place-lisere|Lísere]] shore: alder, oak and birch over bog and black water, running back from the shingle toward the hills. Its edges are the beacon villages' woodlots. Its depths are sacred ground of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]], and in them stands [[place-fndr|the Fen Door]], the one [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] keep this far from the mountains.

## The Land {#the-land}

The wood begins a few hundred paces behind the [[place-coastroad|Coast Road]] and thickens fast. Alders stand in the wet, oaks on the low rises between, and birch wherever a tree has fallen and let in the light. The ground is black water under moss, and a path that held a man in the autumn does not hold him in the thaw. On still nights a line of mist stands over the wood and does not drift.

## Who Uses It {#who-uses-it}

- **The beacon villages,** who cut fuel and alder for the lamps at the edge and go no deeper.
- **The watch at the Fen Door,** two Wardens who come down from the mountains by turns, keep a turf hut on dry ground by the Door, and buy their bread at [[place-harnasel|Harnasel]].
- **Smugglers,** a Kalipsan crew among them, who run tracks through the wood by night that the coast prefers not to know.

The crown's sheriffs of Lísere do not go into the deep part of the wood, and its foresters cut nothing there.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **The bog.** Black water under moss, and paths that change with the season.
- **The Wardens.** The deep wood is sacred ground. A trespasser is warned once and turned out, and the ground round the Door is not entered at all.
- **The dark nights.** Fell things come through the Door in the moonless weeks of winter, and the coast keeps its beacons lit against them.

## What Is Told {#what-is-told}

The beacon families say that on a dark night something comes out of Olvalonke to the shore and goes back before dawn, and that a beacon kept lit keeps it off. A fisherman says the mist over the wood holds a low, slow light on still nights, which goes out before dawn. Travelers on the Coast Road past Harnasel hear the dogs bark at nothing where the road comes nearest the trees.

## Converging Interests {#present}

This winter the watch at the Door is down to one Warden, [[being-keldiradhar|Kéldira]] of the Dhárgandi, and no relief can come down before the passes open. An [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] scholar has hired guards on the quay at [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]] to camp at the Door through the dark nights and see what comes through ([[scenario-brdfrthdr|Bread for the Door]]). Harnasel's beacon keeper, [[being-solmerekalme|Sólmere Kálmereth]], keeps the village's lamps lit every night and the watch's bread baked, and wants to know what the scholar's guards mean to do at the wood's edge.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The scholar at the Door.** The Order scholar hires the party to keep him at the Fen Door for a fortnight of dark nights, and the Warden there is alone.
- **The bread.** The Warden has not come out to the bake-house yard on the first of the month, and Harnasel asks the party to carry the sack in.
- **Smugglers' tracks.** The Kalipsan crew wants an escort past the part of the wood the coast avoids.
