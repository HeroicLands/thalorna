**Lísere Bay** bites deep into the northern coast of [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris]] between [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] and
[[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]], with Vristwald's long western arm for its northern shore. It opens **west** to the open sea. It is
roughly a hundred and sixty miles across and nearly three hundred from the mouth to its **head** in the
east, where the Élavendren shore meets the Vristwald border and runs on into Vristwald by land.

[[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] lies outside the mouth, some seventy-five miles off the Élavendren
shore to the north, and commands the approaches to the whole bay. [[place-kalipsarun|The Kalipsa Run]] crosses to it from Sarmelis in two days.

## What the Shape Means

The bay is sheltered, deep and superb for fishing, and it is the single reason the north of Élavendre is
difficult to hold.

Enclosed water is not a barrier. A shallow boat works round the bay's head along the shore, or
crosses in the long summer calms, in water that the enclosing arms keep far calmer than the open ocean
outside and in weather that would stop a proper ship. Varokh war-parties and [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] bands have been
coming over it for as long as there has been anyone on the southern shore to come for, and the northern
levy of [[place-lisere|Lísere]] exists for no other reason.

Trade crosses it too, in the same boats and often in the same season, which is a fact the crown at
[[place-beravel|Péravel]] finds easier to deplore than to prevent.

## The Open North

Beyond the mouth the water is no longer sheltered, and the threat changes character. **Nordmen**
raiders out of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] have worked this coast for centuries in seagoing
ships that need no sheltered water at all, and they do not stop at the bay. Élavendre's memory of the north is
long and unfriendly, and it is not chiefly about the Varokh.

## Converging Interests {#present}

This winter the bay has more watchers than usual. The beacon chain and the Great Fire at [[place-durmelen|Durmelen]] watch the head of the bay, and the Castellan there has a Varokh boat under a green branch beached in the lee of his point, carrying a woman who asks for him by name. At [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]] the boom was closed on a false fire, an [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] scholar up from Kalipsa is hiring the boats' best hands as guards, and the beacon-watch at [[place-hendrel|Hendrel]] has seen a darker shape on the water, carrying no lamp, three nights running.

## What Stands on It

[[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]] is the bay's one harbor for ships, on its southern shore behind a stone mole. [[place-durmelen|Durmelen]] stands at the head of the bay on the last headland of the Élavendren shore, beside the Vristwald border, with the Great Fire of the beacon chain on its tower, and the [[place-coastroad|Coast Road]] runs from the fort along the shore to the border of [[place-lirve|Lirve]]. The [[affiliation-beaconfamlys|Beacon Families]] keep a lamp on every headland between.
