## Overview {#overview}

The **Quay Council of Sarmelis** governs [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]], the harbor town of [[place-liserebay|Lísere Bay]]. It is a council of the boat-masters and quay-freemen who hold the harbor's berths, and it governs the quay, the fish market and the boom. The town is the crown's, and the crown's sheriff holds the pleas of the crown in it; the council holds everything else that a town of fishing boats and shipyards needs done every morning.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The boat-masters of the inner quay choose the Quay-Councilors each year at the turn of the herring season, and the councilors choose from among themselves a **Reeve of the Harbor** to preside. He gives the berths and speaks to the sheriff and the Admiral's lieutenant. The **Fish-Reeve** weighs the catch on the quay and keeps the tally from which the price of salt-fish is set, a price that the whole north follows. The **Keeper of the Boom** closes the chained logs across the harbor mouth when a fire is lit on the Lamp Tower and opens it again on the all-clear.

The heaviest thing the council does is to put a man off the quay. A man put off the quay finds no boat that will take him aboard, no stall that will sell to him and no berth for anything he owns, and in a town that lives on its quay the sentence is exile in all but name.

## Current Situation {#present}

The council is divided on the Kalipsa trade. The outer quay's Kalipsan captains pay well for berths, and the Quay-Councilors of the inner quay hold that every barrel entered at the tally-house in part is a barrel the council has not weighed. The Reeve of the Harbor, [[being-orlensalthar|Orlen Salthar]], a stout, shrewd boat-master of fifty, holds the middle: he gives the Kalipsan ships their berths and gives the sheriff's clerk a place to stand beside them.

An [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] scholar is hiring guards from the quay at wages the council cannot match, and the Fish-Reeve has proposed that no boat's crew may sign with him in the herring season. The motion is before the council and is not carried.

## Arms {#arms}

The council has no soldiers. It keeps the boom, the watch on the quay and a rota of the boat-crews who stand the Lamp Tower's fire in their turns. In a raid the town's men go to the boom first and to the sheriff's muster second.

## History {#history}

The council is as old as the first quay: the boat-masters who hauled their boats out under the shingle agreed who might use which stretch of beach, and the agreement grew a mole, a boom and a harbor. The Nordmen fleets burned the town more than once, and each rebuilding put the boom and the Keeper of the Boom's office on a firmer footing, until the council's authority over the harbor mouth was as plain as its authority over the berths.

## Relations {#relations}

Sarmelis belongs to the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]], and the crown's sheriff holds the crown's pleas and the road toll. The council's relation to the sheriff is courteous and wary, and its relation to the [[affiliation-beaconfamlys|Beacon Families]] is warm, since the families' sons make the best hands on the boats and the Lamp Tower is their chain's southern relay. The council has no dealings with the garrison at [[place-durmelen|Durmelen]] beyond the chandler's orders.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The motion.** The Fish-Reeve wants a party to learn whose money is behind the Order scholar's guards before the council votes.
- **The berth.** A Kalipsan captain has claimed a berth that belongs to a boat lost last winter, and the lost boat's master's widow has come to the council to contest it.
