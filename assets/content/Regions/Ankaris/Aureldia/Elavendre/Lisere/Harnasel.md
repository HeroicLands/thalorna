## Overview {#overview}

**Harnasel** is a village of about 260 people on the [[place-coastroad|Coast Road]], the last beacon village before the road leaves [[place-lisere|Lísere]] for the cliffs of [[place-lirve|Lirve]]. It stands at the edge of the forest. Behind it the alders thicken into [Olvalonke]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the wet wood whose deep parts are sacred ground of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]]. The name is built on _hárnathen_, the Élavendri verb for lighting a lamp.

## The Village {#the-village}

Harnasel is a village of two halves. The shore half is a row of boat-houses and net-lofts under the point, where the beacon-house stands. The forest half lies a quarter mile inland: bake-houses, a cooper's yard, a charcoal-burners' row and a timber store, all roofed with shingle split from the forest's oak. A cart-track joins the two, and it is the only street.

The village smells of woodsmoke and baking from before dawn, and the charcoal-burners' fires go on all night. A stranger sleeps badly for the first night, and well after.

## The Holding {#the-holding}

The village holds a belt of forest edge, a right of oak and pine for fuel and pitch, a strip of shore and its boat-cove, and the beacon. It pays the crown the watch and the levy. Its bread is sold along the road as far as [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]], and its charcoal goes to the smiths of Sarmelis and the fort.

One regular customer is not on the crown's books: the watch at the Fen Door. A Warden of the night watch kept by the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] comes out of Olvalonke to the end of the bake-house yard on the first day of every month with a sack and a list, pays in silver, and goes back by the path that the village does not walk. The flour, the salt and the bread for the watch go in that sack, and the village does not ask what the watch does with them.

## Who Leads It {#who-leads}

The village is led by [[being-solmerekalme|Sólmere Kálmereth]], the head of the oldest line and keeper of the beacon-house, who sits on the council of the [[affiliation-beaconfamlys|Beacon Families]]. She is a woman of sixty, broad and slow-spoken. She keeps the beacon-roster and the bake-house accounts in her head. The grove-elder of the coast tries the village's cases when he comes down, and he stays for the bread.

## The Failing or the Quarrel {#present}

The watch at [[place-fndr|the Fen Door]] is down to one Warden, and since midwinter she has come to the yard herself and left no message but the list. Sólmere has had her say twice, without being asked, that the Door wants hands, and that she will take them from anyone except the Order's. A dog and a child's pony have gone from the forest edge in the same weeks, and the village says it was wolves.

An [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] scholar's guards have begun buying bread in the village and asking the way to the Door. The village sells them the bread, and its people are divided on the rest. Half would send word up the path that the Order is coming. The other half remember that the Wardens forbid every stranger that path, and that the village's trade with the watch holds only so long as it keeps out of the Wardens' business.

## What Is Seen from It {#what-is-seen}

From the village's point the watch sees the bay's southern shore to the northeast, the next station along the road, and, behind it, a line of mist that stands over Olvalonke on still nights and does not drift. Sometimes the mist holds a light, low and slow, which goes out before dawn. The beacon is kept lit on those nights so that it is answered.

## The Cost of Reaching It {#cost-of-reaching-it}

Harnasel is a day's walk from Hendrel along the road, and the road is good until the last mile, where the forest comes down and the track turns to roots and standing water in a wet month. The cost is in what a party asks the village for. The Wardens' path begins at the end of the bake-house yard and nobody will point to it. A party that wants to go in by it hears the same sentence from every door: the woods are the Áelendan's.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The Warden's word.** Sólmere carries the lone Warden's message to any party that asks and is not the Order's: the watch needs hands for the dark nights, and she cannot offer a bed, a fire or an explanation.
- **The forest edge.** The charcoal-burners have lost a dog and a child's pony and want someone to sit a night at the end of the Wardens' path with a lantern and see what the wolves are.
- **The Order's bread.** The scholar's quartermaster buys a month's bread and asks for it to be delivered to the end of the path, where the guards will take it on.
