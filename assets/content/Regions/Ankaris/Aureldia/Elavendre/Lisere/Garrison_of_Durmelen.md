## Overview {#overview}

The **Garrison of Durmelen** is the company that holds [[place-durmelen|Durmelen]], the crown's fort at the head of [[place-liserebay|Lísere Bay]]. It is a standing body of sworn men under a Castellan, and beside it stand the levy of the shore's villages, who come up to the fort in their turns. It sees what comes round the head of the bay, lights the Great Fire so that the whole [[place-lisere|Lísere]] shore knows, and holds the muster until the levy is in.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The crown names the **Castellan**, who is a Captain of the garrison and answers to the crown for the wall, the stores and the fire. He judges the garrison's quarrels himself. Under him the **Muster-Serjeant** keeps the levy rolls of the shore's villages and calls each village's turn, and the **Keeper of the Great Fire** keeps the beacon on the tower laid and lit and answers for the signals it sends.

A **sworn man** serves a term for pay and a share of the stores. A **levyman** comes up from his village for his weeks, brings his own spear and a sack of meal, and stands the night watch beside the sworn men. The sentence the Castellan keeps for the worst offence is to strike a man from the roll: he may not enter the fort, bear its arms or draw its stores, and in a shore village that name follows him home.

## Current Situation {#present}

The garrison is short of men. The Castellan, [[being-morenothral|Moren Othral]], has asked [[place-beravel|Péravel]] for more and been told the matter is under consideration, and the best of the shore's young men are on the quay at [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]], hired by an [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] scholar at wages the crown does not match. The Keeper of the Great Fire has lost two of his four lamp-hands since midwinter, and one night in five the fire is stood by a single man.

A Varokh boat under a green branch has lain on the beach in the lee of the point for several days, and the garrison is divided on whether its people should be heard.

## Arms {#arms}

The garrison keeps four oared cutters in the lee and the iron basket of the Great Fire on the tower, laid with split pine and pitch. A watch of two stands the Fire every night. A fire on the tower is answered by every beacon along the shore, and the signal says what is seen: a single fire for boats on the bay, two for a landing, three for a fleet from the open sea. Three has not been lit in the Castellan's lifetime.

## History {#history}

The garrison begins with the crown's decision, after the Nordmen fleets burned the north shore, to hold the head of the bay with stone and not only with the shore's boats. The first Castellan took the fort under the oath that the crown would make no treaty with the Nordmen, and every Castellan since has taken it the same way. The levy's turns at the fort are older than the stone, and the sworn men were added to them.

## Relations {#relations}

The garrison answers to the crown, and its relations with the shore are practical. The [[affiliation-beaconfamlys|Beacon Families]] answer its fire and light their own, and the [[affiliation-sarmelisconcl|Quay Council of Sarmelis]] sells it pitch, rope and fish. It keeps the crown's oath against the Nordmen and has no dealings with them. It does what the Castellan judges the Varokh deserve, and the Castellan judges by the muster roll.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The roll.** The Muster-Serjeant finds that two levymen of Hendrel are on the roll for the winter turn and are in the Order's pay at Sarmelis, and asks the party to bring them up before the Castellan learns.
- **The fire.** A lamp-hand is found dead on the tower at dawn, with the Great Fire dark and the watch's lantern full.
