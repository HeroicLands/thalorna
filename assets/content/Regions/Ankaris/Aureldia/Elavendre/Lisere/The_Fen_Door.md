## Overview {#overview}

**The Fen Door** is a cleft in a low mound of peat and old stone, between two black alders, deep in [Olvalonke]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the wet, thick forest behind the [[place-lisere|Lísere]] shore. The ground round the mound is cold in every season, nothing grows within a few paces of the cleft, and the bog water near it does not freeze in the hardest winter. On dark nights, most often in the moonless weeks of winter, fell things come through it. It is an [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] whose door lets in demons and fell creatures, and it is sacred ground of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]], the farthest from the mountains that the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] keep.

People go to it because something has to be kept at it. The coast calls it the Door, and keeps out of that part of the wood. The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] wants to know what comes through.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The Wardens hold it, and they keep it with the oldest form of their keeping, the watch Dhargan set on [[place-talve|Talve]]. Two Wardens sit by the cleft through every night, with spear and dog and a banked fire, to turn what comes through by song, fire or stone, to retire when it does not turn, and to sing from shelter on the nights it wins. The watch comes down from the mountains by turns and is relieved at the turn of each season. It keeps a hut of turf on the dry ground a bowshot off, and the beacon families of the nearest stretch of shore sell it bread and do not ask.

The crown's sheriffs of Lísere do not go into that part of Olvalonke, and its foresters cut nothing there.

## Layers {#history}

What is on the far side of the Door is not known, and the Áelendan do not say what they believe of it.

**The Áelendan account.** The Tradition Keepers name the Door among the doors to fell things, with Talve, and sing it in the same breath. They hold that the watch at the Door is old, and that it has failed twice in the Keepers' memory, and they keep the names of the Wardens who died at it.

**The coast's account.** The beacon families say that on a dark night something comes out of Olvalonke to the shore and goes back before dawn, and that a beacon kept lit keeps it off. The families of the stretch nearest the Door keep their beacons lit every night of the winter, raid or no raid.

**The Order's reading.** An Order theorist of the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] holds that the cold of the mound is a bog vapor rising through a fault, and that what the coast sees on dark nights is marsh light.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The watch is short. One of the two Wardens who came down at the autumn turn was killed at the cleft at midwinter, and his partner keeps the Door alone with two dogs, sleeping by day in the turf hut. No relief can come down from the mountains until the passes open. An Order scholar, hearing at Kalipsa that the Door is thinly kept, has hired guards on the Lísere coast and means to camp at the mound and watch what comes through for himself.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbours}

- **What comes through.** The cleft itself on the dark nights, and whatever the watch fails to turn.
- **The bog.** Olvalonke is black water under moss, and a path that held a man in the autumn does not hold him in the thaw.
- **Raiders.** Varokh war-parties out of [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]] land on the Lísere shore, and some of them come inland through the wood.
- **Smugglers** of the [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] trade, who use the forest's tracks by night and do not want company.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

**The dry ground.** A low rise of firm earth among the alder and the moss, with the turf hut on it, a woodpile and a ring of stones round a fire that is never let go out.

**The mound.** A long, low hump of peat and gray stone, half grown with black moss. The air above it is cold, and breath smokes there in summer.

**The cleft.** A split in the mound's face between the roots of two black alders, a man's height and a shoulder's width, going down into the dark at a slope. The stone at its lip is scored with marks that are not tool marks.

**The bones.** A little way from the cleft, a heap of bones that are not a man's or a beast's that a party knows, which the watch has burned and which have not burned.

:::secret {title="The truth of the Fen Door"}
What came through at midwinter is still on this side. It killed the Warden who went at it alone with fire and the wrong song, as the thing in the fog killed a man of the Lodge in the Black Snow, and it did not go back. It is a long, low thing, black and wet like the bog, with too many joints and no face, and it moves under the moss as a man moves through water. By day it lies in the black water of Olvalonke. On dark nights it hunts along the edge of the forest toward the shore, and it does not come into the light of a beacon. The beacon families of the nearest stretch have lost a dog and a child's pony since midwinter and have not said why.

The Warden left at the Door, [[being-keldiradhar|Kéldira]] of the [Dhárgandi]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, cannot hunt it and keep the watch at once, and keeping the watch comes first. While she sits by the cleft, nothing else has come through. If the watch is left for a night, something else does.

The Order scholar's guards mean to keep the Warden away from the mound while he watches. If they do, the Door is unwatched.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The scholar's guards.** The Order scholar hires the party at the Lísere coast to keep him safe at the mound for a fortnight of dark nights, from the Wardens first.
- **The lone watch.** The Warden at the Door needs hands she would not otherwise accept, and the beacon families of the nearest stretch carry her message to anyone who comes asking.
- **The fallen Warden.** The Warden killed at midwinter lies under a cairn on the dry ground, and his kin in the mountains want his spear carried up to them before the thaw.
