## Overview {#overview}

**The Coast Road** is the one road of [[place-lisere|Lísere]]. It enters from [[place-lirve|Lirve]] at the southwest, where the cliffs give way to shingle, and runs northeast a short way behind the beach through [[place-harnasel|Harnasel]], [[place-hendrel|Hendrel]] and [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]], and on along the shore of [[place-liserebay|Lísere Bay]] to [[place-durmelen|Durmelen]] at the head of the bay. From the Lirve border to the fort is about four days on foot.

It keeps a short way behind the water on purpose. It is close enough to the shore to be reached in a hurry, and far enough back that a boat's crew coming over the shingle is seen from the nearest beacon before it reaches the road. The road is built for warning and for the levy's marching, and trade has made use of it.

## What Moves on It {#what-moves-on-it}

**Fish.** Barrels and dry-salted bales go southwest to the road's end at the Lirve border and on to [[place-beravel|Péravel]], by cart in the season and by pack-pony in the mud. This is the road's steadiest load.

**Timber, pitch and charcoal.** Charcoal and split pine come down from the forest edge at Harnasel. Pitch goes to the Sarmelis shipyards by the cartload.

**The levy.** In the dark months the shore's young men go northeast on the road in their turns to Durmelen, each with his own spear and a sack of meal.

**Furs and Kalipsan goods.** Bales go from the beach to the outer quay at Sarmelis without being entered, and they go by night, along the road's own edge.

**Wardens' errands.** The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] use the road as far as Harnasel and no farther, and a Warden on it is given the way and not questioned.

## Logistics {#logistics}

The road is a track of packed sand and gravel, wide enough for one cart and a pony beside it, laid on a bed of shingle where it crosses wet ground. Crossings are fords or log bridges kept by the villages. A party on foot makes about ten miles in a day, and a wagon about as much.

Water is plentiful and bad in the low places: the bog streams run brown, and the villages' wells are dug on the dune sand where the water is sweet. Fodder is thin and a pony's feed is carried. Inns exist only at Sarmelis. Between the towns a traveler sleeps in a village barn on the village's custom, and pays in bread or labor.

## The Stations {#stations}

**Harnasel** is the southwest end and the last village before the forest. **Hendrel** lies a day along, beside the gap in the reeds where the track to [[place-dskfrd|the Dusk Ford]] leaves the road. **Sarmelis** is a day on again, the road's one town and its single toll. **Durmelen** is two days beyond Sarmelis, at the head of the bay, and the road ends at its gate.

Between the villages the beacon stations stand on every headland, a day's walk or less apart: a stone hut, a pile of laid wood under an oiled hide and a pair of lamp-hands, with a path of flat stones to the road. They are not stops. A traveler who asks for water at one is given it and is not asked in.

## The Year on the Road {#year-on-the-road}

**The thaw** turns the low places to bog, and for two or three weeks the road is a chain of rafts and causeways kept by the villages for a toll of one coin a wheel. **Summer** is the season of the fish, when carts line the road to Sarmelis from before dawn. **Autumn** is the season of the raids that come before the weather shuts the bay, and the beacons are tested every week. **Winter** is the levy's season, the dark nights, and the season in which the Coast Road is least a road and most a line of lamps, with long gaps in which nothing else is lit.

## Who Patrols and Taxes It {#present}

The crown's sheriff of Lísere holds the road and sends riders along it by day. The road toll is one coin a wagon, taken at Sarmelis's gate, and one a pony-load, which the gatewarden usually forgets. The [[affiliation-beaconfamlys|Beacon Families]] hold it by night: the lamp-hands see the road from their huts and send word of anything on it that carries no lantern. The garrison at [[place-durmelen|Durmelen]] sends a patrol of sworn men down the road each week as far as the first beacon.

The crown's riders and the beacon families agree on the rule of the road and disagree on the Kalipsan carriers. The riders look the other way at the road toll and the families look the other way at the carriers, and the arrangement holds as long as neither says it aloud.

## Dangers {#dangers}

**Raiders.** A Varokh crew that lands between two beacons can reach the road before a lamp is lit, and a farm cart is a better prize than a village. A Nordmen fleet strikes anywhere and is gone before the levy is raised. The road runs through country the shore's families have fought over for centuries, and the old stones still show where a house was.

**The edge of the wood.** Where the road comes nearest Olvalonke, past Harnasel, travelers hear the dogs bark at nothing, and the villages keep the lamps lit. The road itself is safe by day.

**The reed-gap.** The gap beside Hendrel is open all day and shut after dusk, and a stranger on the road at the long dusk who turns into it meets whatever the Ford asks.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The convoy.** A Sarmelis boat-master hires a party to escort a convoy of salt-fish carts to Péravel through the thaw, with an Order scholar's agent in the third cart and nothing on the manifest to say what he carries.
- **The dark stretch.** A beacon on the road has gone out three nights running between Hendrel and Sarmelis, and the lamp-hands on either side say the other put it out.
- **The rider.** A sheriff's rider is found dead at the reed-gap with a knotted cord in his hand and his purse untouched.
