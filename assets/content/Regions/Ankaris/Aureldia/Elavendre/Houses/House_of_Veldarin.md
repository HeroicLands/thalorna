## Overview {#overview}

The **House of Véldarin** is a minor noble house of the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]], holding a hall and a few lake-shore farms at [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]] at the foot of [[place-sorvanda|Sórvanda]]. It is known for one thing: its sons and daughters go to the border garrisons young and come back officers, or do not come back. Its name carries weight with the crown's captains and very little at court.

Véldarin is also an ordinary Élavendri family name, and the Véldarins of Liranel and Kélpasi are no kin of the house.

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

The head of the house is [[being-halmesarveld|Hálmesar Véldarin]], who commanded on the border for thirty years and keeps the hall at Elanmere now, deeply traditional and done with soldiering. His son and heir, [[being-dharnvldrn|Daran]], is the captain of the crown's garrison at Elanmere: a capable officer whom his superiors have passed over twice for anything more, and who has been reprimanded twice and saved twice by his record. The house's military connections keep Daran's commission sound. Hálmesar has made it clear that the family's patience has limits.

## Holdings {#holdings}

- **The hall at Elanmere,** a timber hall on a stone foot above the lake shore, outside the town's market.
- **The lake-shore farms** below it, let to tenants.
- **The house's name** among the captains of the [[affiliation-brdrmltia|Border Militia]] and the crown's garrisons, which is worth more to it than the farms.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

**The garrison's name.** The grovekeeper [[being-lanthevileha|Lánthevile Halren]] has been mapping burned shrines and scorched clearings along the lake road, and the garrison under Daran has found nothing behind them. The [[affiliation-groveconcl|Grove Council]] deals with the garrison cordially and without warmth, and Hálmesar hears what the grove-elders say in the market.

**The Kardeth case.** Daran's garrison investigated the death of Lord [[being-kenrelkardet|Kenrel Kardeth]] on the road into Elanmere and named it a bandit raid. Kenrel's cousin, Lord [[being-heltankardet|Heltan Kardeth]], is asking how the bandits knew which rider to take, and every question about the report is a question about a Véldarin.

## History as Told {#history}

The house sings no long line. It sings its dead on the border: the grandfather who fell fighting raiders, the father who commanded, and the names before them who held a post and handed it on. The house's proof was made by the Colleges in the year every house proved its line, and it has added a name to it in each generation since, most of them with a garrison beside it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The father's word.** Hálmesar asks a party to ride the lake road and tell him, plainly and without the garrison's manners, what the burned shrines are.
- **A guard for the fair.** The garrison at Elanmere wants outsiders to guard a frightened lord through the autumn fair, and to report to Captain Véldarin.
