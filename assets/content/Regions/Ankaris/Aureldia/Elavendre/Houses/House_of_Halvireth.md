## Overview {#overview}

The **House of Hálvireth** is a lowland house of the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]] holding river-meadows in the basin of [[place-beravriver|the Berav]] below [[place-beravel|Péravel]]. It is a house of the middle rank, old enough to have had its line proved in the year the mountains closed, and known for one thing more than its land: its line, which its bard sings at the feast where a child of the house is named ([[lore-hlvrthline|The Line of the House of Hálvireth]]).

The house claims the old blood through [Sírtala]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a woman of the [Dhárgandi]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} who came down from the hills to the basin, married a river trader and kept the high tongue in her mouth all her life. Above her the line is in the high tongue, and the house sings no further up it than three names and hands the rest to the Tradition Keepers, whose it is.

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

The head of the house is [[being-taerenhalvir|Teiren]], who holds the river-meadows and whose people eat. His heir is his daughter [Sílvera]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, an infant, counted into the line at her naming feast in the hearing of everyone who might dispute it. Her other name, the shadow name, is not for the hall.

Teiren's father [Méldoreth]{.name data-type="being/npc"} followed the art at Péravel and came home, and kept a harper twenty years and paid him. A house that produces no artists and no mages across two generations stops being counted in the first rank, and the house of Hálvireth has kept a harper and sent its children to the art.

## Holdings {#holdings}

- **The river-meadows** of the Berav basin below the capital.
- **The harp-hall,** rebuilt in stone by [Sarhel]{.name data-type="being/npc"} after fire took it, where the house's feasts are given.
- **The living bridge over the mill-race,** built in the years the tribute stopped going east, which stands.
- **The oak at the ford-gate,** planted for the house's dead [Kálvorin]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, and **the birch in the low meadow**, planted by [Gálveren]{.name data-type="being/npc"} for his brother. Both stand, and both are sung.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

**The debt to Orethan.** [Lothel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, at the foot of the lowland line, was fostered in the house of [Orethan]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} and owed Orethan a fosterling in return. Orethan's line ran out before it was paid. A debt of honor outlasts the person who owed it and falls on the line, so the bard sings it at every feast, and it is owed to a house with nobody left to receive it.

**The claim.** The Dhárgandi who come down to the autumn fair at [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]] keep no daughter of [Ronken]{.name data-type="being/npc"} who went down to the basin. The house has sung Sírtala at the fair every autumn since [Velthar]{.name data-type="being/npc"} went up and sang the line there, and nobody at the fair has stood up. The house's standing rests on the old blood, and the old blood rests on a woman the Dhárgandi have never named. Every autumn the house's bard goes up to Elanmere and sings her again.

**The Order.** [Ílthera]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, an earlier head of the house, held a chair at court against [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Order]] and kept it, and the house still sings her for it. It sits a short ride below a capital where the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] educates the children of the nobility.

## History as Told {#history}

[Árnolen]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Lothel's son, set the Colleges to prove the line in the year the mountains closed ([[lore-sorvandaclose|The Sórvanda Closing]]), when every house did the same, and paid, and the College sang it in the house's hall. Árnolen's child [Nerrin]{.name data-type="being/npc"} was a harper of the Circle. The heads after him are sung each with one deed: a hall rebuilt, a line sung at Elanmere, a chair held against the Order, a satire lived down, a stand on the north shore in the years of the fleets, the stores unbarred in a bad year, and the living bridge built when the silver stopped going east.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The bard to the fair.** Teiren hires a party to escort the house's bard up to the Elanmere fair, where the claim to Sírtala must stand again before the Dhárgandi camped on the shore. This year a Dhárgandi elder has sent word down that he means to hear it.
- **A fosterling for Orethan.** A stranger at Péravel says he is of Orethan's blood through a daughter's line and asks the house for the fosterling it owes. If the Colleges prove him, the house can pay its oldest debt. If they do not, the house has been asked to pay a liar.
