## Overview {#overview}

The **House of Kardeth** is a noble house of [[place-lirve|Lirve]] in the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]], of moderate standing: old enough to command respect, and not prominent enough to draw unwanted scrutiny. It takes its name from its old seat, the village of [[place-cardeth|Kardeth]] near [[place-liranel|Liranel]], and its head lives at [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]], where the house is one of the older families and sits by custom on the [[affiliation-twncnclprtaln|Town Council]]. Its people wear the Kardeth green.

Kardeth is also an ordinary Élavendri family name, and the Kardeths of Sóurimel and Valdun are no kin of the house.

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

The head of the house is Lady [[being-glyencrdth|Lyenna Kardeth]], who took the house's affairs and its chair on the council when her husband, Lord [[being-kenrelkardet|Kenrel Kardeth]], was killed on the road to [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]] three years ago. The marriage had no child, and Lyenna has named no heir. She is building the house into a voice that counts on the council and beyond it.

The old seat is held for the house by Lady [[being-auliranekard|Áulirane Kardeth]], twenty-one, of a younger branch with ancestry among the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]], who wears a bronze Áelendan clasp with the Kardeth green. She keeps the village fed and her own counsel, and she has her own reasons for not wanting a bard of [[affiliation-clgline|the College of the Line]] to hear her branch's proof just now.

Kenrel's cousin, Lord [[being-heltankardet|Heltan Kardeth]], is home from service abroad. He is the nearest of Kenrel's blood, and he has no place in the house's affairs.

## Holdings {#holdings}

- **The estates** inland of Port Aelion, on the road toward Elanmere, which Kenrel held and Lyenna holds now.
- **The house at Port Aelion,** and the house's chair on the Town Council, held by its head by custom as one of the town's older families.
- **The village of Kardeth,** the old seat, about 250 people within reach of Liranel, held under the crown by the Keeper of Kardeth.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

**Kenrel's death.** The crown's garrison at Elanmere investigated under Captain [[being-dharnvldrn|Daran Véldarin]] and called it a bandit raid from beyond the border. Heltan has a copy of the garrison's report and asks at every gathering how the bandits knew which rider to take. Lyenna receives him with every courtesy. The family treats his question as bad manners.

**The heir.** Lyenna holds the house as Kenrel's widow and has named no heir. Heltan is Kenrel's blood, and Áulirane holds the seat the house is named for. A suitor from a neighboring district has begun to court Lyenna for the value of an alliance with the house.

**The proof.** A bard of the College of the Line has come to Kardeth to hear the younger branch's proof, and Áulirane would rather he did not.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The report.** Heltan asks a party to ride the road between the estates and Elanmere and tell him whether the narrow place is where the garrison's report puts it.
- **The suitor.** Lyenna asks a party to learn what her suitor's house owes, and to whom.
- **The bard at Kardeth.** Áulirane asks a party to keep the College's bard lodged and amused.
