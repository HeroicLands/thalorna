## Overview {#overview}

The **House of Kírethel** is the royal house of the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]], and it holds its court at [[place-beravel|Péravel]]. It is the second house to wear the crown. The first, the house of [Orethan]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, ran out without an heir, and [[affiliation-bardicolgs|the Bardic Circle]] chose a king from Kírethel. That king was [Hóuradel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who opened the workings above the tree line that the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] call the King's Cut ([[lore-sorvandaclose|The Sórvanda Closing]]), and the house has held the crown since.

Its right to rule is the right every Élavendren house claims, made larger: that it comes from the Áelendan, and that the Colleges have proved it. Its reputation is the one the region gives the crown. It is genuinely warm to the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] inside the walls and careful of the Áelendan beyond them, and it has kept close relations for generations with the mage [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]] of Dúnavarre across the water ([[place-elavendre|Élavendre]]).

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

The head of the house is [[being-aurenthekir|Queen Áurenthe]], fifty-one, who reigns in her own right. Her consort is dead. Her heir is her only son, [[being-ielaraskir|Prince Yélaras]], twenty-two, who was schooled at the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] as the children of the nobility are.

**The succession.** The crown passes to the monarch's eldest child, son or daughter. A queen reigns in her own right, as [Réidane]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Orethan did when she treated at [[place-valdun|Valdun]] ([[lore-elvtribute|The Tribute of Élavendre]]). Every monarch is crowned in the hall at Péravel, stands bareheaded through [[lore-treelinelay|the Lay of the Tree Line]], and swears with both hands open that the crown stops at the tree line ([[lore-firstcrown|The First Crowning of Élavendre]]). When a line ends without an heir, the Bardic Circle chooses the next monarch from a house whose descent the Colleges have proved, as it chose Hóuradel.

The law of succession is not in question. What is in question is the judgment the region names as the crown's ceiling: a monarch believed to have taken the Order's side faces no rebellion, only the quiet, unanswerable verdict that he has stopped being of the old blood. Yélaras is the next monarch by law, and he is the University's pupil.

## Holdings {#holdings}

- **The court at Péravel**, where the hall of the crowning stands and the court grew at the crossing of [[place-beravriver|the Berav]] ([[place-beravel|Péravel]]).
- **The toll on the river.** The crown's steadiest revenue is the toll on the traffic through the capital, where the kingdom's goods are worked and sold ([[place-beravel|Péravel]]).
- **The house's proof of descent**, kept by the Colleges with the proofs of every house the Cut set to proving its line ([[lore-sorvandaclose|The Sórvanda Closing]]).

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

**The Cut.** The founder of the house is the king the market singers lament. [[lore-roadsendlmnt|The Lament at the Road's End]] is sung at the autumn fair at [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]] on its first night, it is a satire against a crown, and it names Hóuradel of Kírethel, the king who could not pay. The law recognizes a bard's satire as a weapon, and no monarch of the house has tried to stop it.

**The balance.** Queen Áurenthe keeps the University, the scholars and the Vilérian goodwill, and keeps the Order's movement inward deferred, complicated and guided ([[place-elavendre|Élavendre]]). The crown's guides out of [[place-liranel|Liranel]] show the Order real country and nothing of consequence. The Áelendan know what the guides are for, and they tolerate the arrangement because the alternative is worse.

**The Sinale.** The crown's formal relation with the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale]] is carried day to day by Áelendan intermediaries, and it could be withdrawn without a word said to anyone at Péravel ([[place-ethalosse|Ethalosse]]). The queen has not been asked up to [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa]], and no monarch goes there unasked.

**The heir.** The prince's friends are fellows of the University, and he says in company that learning and the old blood are not enemies. The bards of the Circle have heard him say it. He means to answer them in the way the houses answer a doubted claim, by having his line sung at the Elanmere fair before people who remember the genealogy differently, and the fair is in the autumn.

## History as Told {#history}

The lowland houses of the Berav basin acclaimed [Táldoren]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Orethan as their first king about 560 BF, and his line held the crown through the Empire's wars and the treaty at Valdun. When it ended without an heir, the Bardic Circle chose Hóuradel of Kírethel. Hóuradel inherited the Empire's tribute in silver and a run of bad harvests, opened royal workings above the tree line in Sórvanda to pay it, and saw the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] close them in a single season about 320 AF. He went up to Ésalosa uninvited, was turned back at the outer ridge, closed the workings and renewed the Tree-Line oath on the Lodge's terms. Every noble house then set the Colleges to prove its descent, and the house of Kírethel proved its own.

The tribute lapsed with [[lore-crownssettle|the Settlement of the Crowns]] about 619 AF, and the house has reigned since in the balance the region describes, between the Order inside the walls and the Áelendan above the tree line. [[being-aurenthekir|Áurenthe]] was crowned about 698 AF, at thirty, on her father's death, and she has reigned since in the manner of her house.
