## Overview {#overview}

The **Heart of Dirikri** is a crystalline artifact believed to hold the essence of the powerful elemental spirits known as the **Dirikri**. It emanates an aura that quickens natural growth around it, and within the [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] where it lies it is said to bend time.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody holds it. It lies at the center of [[place-ukuaraelwen|Úkua-Araelwen]], the Araelwen on the frontier with Provénzia where a day inside can be a month outside, behind the [[place-mazeofechos|Maze of Echoes]], which answers to sound. The Araelwen is sacred ground of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]], forbidden to every outsider, and the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] keep it.

## Its Story {#its-story}

What is known of the Heart comes from one ancient scroll, recently found and now in the keeping of [[being-aelithraenewyn|Áelithra Énevin]], the Royal Loremaster at [[place-beravel|Péravel]]. The scroll is in poor condition and largely unreadable. It places the Heart within Úkua-Araelwen and implies that music or sound has something to do with the artifact itself, and not only with the maze that guards it. The Royal Loremaster reads it as the reason the place is a slow zone at all, rather than the Heart being merely a thing that happens to lie inside one. Nobody has yet been in a position to find out.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

Growth quickens near it, and time within its Araelwen runs slow. A [[miscgear-temprlcmpss|Temporal Compass]] vibrates as it approaches the Heart, more strongly the nearer it comes.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

The Royal Loremaster wants a party sent into Úkua-Araelwen to retrieve it, and at a party at Péravel in honor of the ambassador of Dúnavarre she has proposed exactly that ([[scenario-embsydnvrtantsou|Embassy from Dunavarre to Tantsou]]). The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] would study it. The Wardens forbid the Araelwen to every outsider, and a device of the art carried inside one counts with them as a working, and a working there as desecration.
