The **Áelendan People**—also known as the **People of the Pact**—are a sub-people of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], the ancestor tribes of the [[lore-elavendrnppl|Élavendri]] who never settled into the kingdom's cities and who have maintained the old ways since before the founding of the monarchy. They live in the mountainous interior of Élavendre, organized in tribal communities that follow traditions stretching back to the earliest [[lore-pelwarpepl|Pelwar People]] settlement of the region. They eschew the modern cities and the ways of the kingdom, living close to the land as their forebears did.

## The Kindred Pact

The name "People of the Pact" refers to the [Kindred Pact]{.name data-type="lore/law"}, the unwritten compact that the Áelendan believe binds them to the land itself, transmitted through oral tradition and enacted through the seasonal rituals that mark Áelendan life. The people came over the eastern pass in the snow ([[lore-nelgancross|The Crossing of Nelgan]]), and the pact was first sung at the lowest pool of [[place-longstair|the Long Stair]] in the generation that followed ([[lore-lowestpoolpct|The Pact at the Lowest Pool]]). What the Pact means to them, and the spiritual tradition it sits within, is in [[lore-aelendanclt#the-pact-and-the-old-way|the Áelendan culture note]].

## Culture and Society

Áelendan communities are organized tribally, with authority vested in councils of elders rather than hereditary nobility. Leadership is earned through demonstrated wisdom, skill in the old ways, and service to the community. The Áelendan are [[lore-aelendanclt|deeply conservative]], almost to the point of it defining them, and their beliefs, mores and obligations are described in [[lore-aelendanclt|the Áelendan culture note]].

The Áelendan speak [[skill-aelendlng|Áelendi]], a Proto-Pelwar tongue heavily influenced by Sinale, with many Sinale loanwords; they do not speak Sinale. They write it in the [[skill-clthndscrpt|Veldikko]], and carve in the [[skill-thrvrdhscrpt|Thurarth]] runes. See [[skill-aelendlng|Áelendi Language]] for linguistic details.

## Relationship with the Élavendri

The relationship between the Áelendan and the settled [[lore-elavendrnppl|Élavendri]] is one of mutual respect, and the crown of [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Élavendre]] protects Áelendan sacred ground by law ([[lore-aelendanclt#the-elavendri|the Élavendri]]).

## Relationship with the Sinale

The Áelendan maintain a uniquely close neighborly relationship with the enclaves of the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale Folk]] in the deep forests. This is not an alliance in any formal sense—the Sinale have kings, queens and lords of their own, who do not work as human ones do—but a relationship of shared land and mutual tolerance built over many generations. The Áelendan fae traditions are not superstition; they reflect genuine contact with the Sinale and with the other fae beings that inhabit the ancient forests. Áelendan druids practice forms of magic that bear a distant, diluted resemblance to Sinalean arts. The Sinale love the Áelendan and keep them apart from [[lore-flksinale#the-numinous|the numinous]]: the Áelendan are taught its outer fringes only. They have the concept and cannot fully apprehend it, because it is closed to humans, and no Áelendan practises the inner Sinale ways. Why the Sinale have opened themselves to the Áelendan in a way they do not to other peoples is a question the Sinale have never explained, and that the Áelendan do not discuss.

## Institutions

The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]]
: An ancient order tasked with protecting the sacred sites of the mountains and deep forests, particularly those connected to the fae. The Wardens patrol the boundary between the settled lowlands and the wild interior.

Council of Elders
: The local governing bodies of each Áelendan tribe, composed of recognized elders from the community's leading families and lineages.

Tradition Keepers
: Specialist memorizers who hold the unwritten Kindred Pact, the tribal songs, and the genealogies. Training to become a Tradition Keeper begins in childhood and continues across decades.

## Where They Live

The Áelendan live in the mountainous interior of Élavendre, most thickly around the sites sacred to the Kindred Pact. They claim no land and own none ([[lore-aelendanclt#land-that-is-not-owned|Land That Is Not Owned]]), and they defend the sacred sites fiercely:

## The Order

The Áelendan **do not trust the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] at all**, and do not pretend
to. Where the Élavendri court treats the Order as a rival institution to be balanced, licensed and
argued with, the Áelendan regard it as a body of meddlesome intruders—outsiders who arrive to
_study_ what the tribes exist to _keep_, who mistake a sacred site for a subject, and who have never
once asked permission in a form the tribes recognize.

The kingdom permits the Order and limits its access. The Áelendan did not agree to the first half and
enforce the second: the boundary the crown declines to move is the boundary the
[[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] hold. The grievance is not abstract. **The Order steals from the sacred sites**, and has done so for as long
as it has been in the kingdom. Expeditions arrive with licenses to survey and leave with what was
seated there; scholars admitted to the margins are found past them; and objects that belonged to a
grove or a spring have a way of reappearing in a chapterhouse collection at [[place-beravel|Péravel]], cataloged,
provenance unrecorded. The Order calls this recovery and study. The Áelendan call it what it is, and
have never accepted a single explanation offered.

An Order expedition turned back at the tree line is a routine
event. An Order expedition that does not turn back is a different kind of event, and the Order's own
records of the northern interior are notably thin on what became of several of them.
