Eight silver birch stand in a ring in a hollow of the [[place-silvrfrsts|Silver Forests]], half a day's walk east of [[place-sourimael|Sóurimel]] and nearer to [[place-aelirosse|Aelirosse]] than any lowland map allows. The [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] call the place **Khálpilonpu**, a birch stand within the deep wood. Nobody has cut a tree of it, and nobody has needed to: the ring keeps itself unreachable.

## The Ring

The trees are large and close-set, their bark the pale metal of the forest, and between them the ground is bare of undergrowth. Starlight lies on the bark after dark in a way ordinary light does not, and a visitor who has seen the [[place-silvrfrsts|Felled Stand]] knows what it is to see the same bark gone dull. Here it has stayed bright.

The ring is a place where the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale]] turn paths, and it is one of the places where they turn them hardest. A track that runs toward the hollow bends away from it a little at a time, so gently that a walker takes the bend for his own choice. A traveler who pushes against the turning finds the wood closing in front of him, distances stretching, and the daylight shifting in ways the sun does not account for. Nothing in the turning is a weapon. It is the wood declining to be walked, and it holds as long as the paths are left to turn.

## What Was Done Here

Within living memory a _marendos_ (master) of the Arcane Order's [[affiliation-sideros|Zulen Sideris]], the Fellowship of Metal, came to the hollow with a party of retainers and a plan. **Denrik Réncaris** held that a path is a bound thing and that a binding can be reversed with the right metal. He drove silver pegs into the ground in a circle among the trees, strung silver wire between them at the height of a man's knee, and worked a Straight Road upon the wire. The silver came from the spoil heaps of the [[lore-sorvandaclose|King's Cut]], bought from a Lísere carter who asked no questions.

The working succeeded and did not do what he meant. The paths hold straight now, and every straight line in the hollow ends at the ring. The wire draws a walker in, and the working takes his footing: he walks the circuit between the pegs and cannot stop or turn aside. Each dusk it takes another. The _marendos_ stands in the middle of the ring in the working's eye, kept alive by it and unable to unmake it without unmaking himself.

The Sinale have done nothing, because they do not fight. The paths around the ring turn outward harder than before, and the hollow is sealed on every side but the one the wire opens.

## The Walkers

The Áelendan name them the **Pegged**: the _marendos_'s retainers and the guide he bribed, and anyone since drawn along the wire. A Pegged walker paces the circuit in step with the others, pale, silent and unhurried. He is not dead and not one of the dead of the Shadow. The Wardens call him a lingering spirit held by an ill-made working, and take the point seriously. He tires, and he eats and drinks if food is put to his mouth, but he does not stop of his own will.

A Pegged walker does not strike. He walks, and a body that stands in his way is carried along by the press of the circuit. A stranger who keeps step with the walkers for the space of a hundred paces finds that he cannot stop either.

## Who Knows, and What They Say

- **The Wardens** know that something is wrong in the Silver Forests east of Sóurimel, because their own paths have turned them from the hollow three seasons running and the turning is not the old kind. They have not found the ring. A Warden asked about it says only that the wood is closed there and that a closed wood is not opened by force.
- **Sóurimel** lost a guide, a charcoal-burner's daughter named Alena Alveryn, who left with lowland strangers and did not return. The village keeps her hearth lit. Her mother tells anyone who stays long enough that a lamp was seen walking in a straight line through the trees on a night when no lamp could.
- **The Arcane Order** records the expedition as lost in the northern interior, which is what its records say of several. The chapterhouse at [[place-beravel|Péravel]] holds Réncaris's licence and not his survey notes.
- **The Bardic Colleges** have a verse about a man who asked the wood to go straight. It does not end.

## Reaching It

The ring is in sacred territory, and the Wardens turn back everyone who enters without leave. The wood turns back the rest. A party reaches the hollow in one of three ways: by Warden leave, which comes with a Warden at the elbow; by following the silver wire, which is the only line in the forest that does not bend; or by Sóurimel's quiet knowledge of the Silver Forests, which a villager gives a stranger on terms of her own. Following the wire is the way the working was built to be followed.

## Hooks

- **The silver.** A carter in Lísere sold a quantity of Sórvanda silver and a _marendos_ of Péravel paid in coin. Someone wants to know where the metal went.
- **A hearth kept lit.** Alena Alveryn's mother pays what she has to anyone who brings her daughter's name back, alive or not.
- **A Warden's quiet word.** The Wardens cannot reach the hollow. A lowlander who reaches it is useful to them in ways they do not name.
