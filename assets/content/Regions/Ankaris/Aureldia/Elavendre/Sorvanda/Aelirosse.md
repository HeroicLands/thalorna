**Aelirosse**, as it is called in [[skill-elvndrlng|Élavendri]], lies deep in [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]], within the
[[place-alndntrblnds|Áelendan Tribal Lands]] of [[place-sorvanda|Sórvanda]], not far from the
[[place-urontekhi|Uron Tekhi]]. Perhaps a hundred and twenty members of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]] live there, and
have for longer than there has been a [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|kingdom of Élavendre]] to be unaware of it.

It is not a settlement in any sense a human surveyor would accept. There are no streets and no walls;
there are dwellings worked into and around the living silver birch over the course of centuries, a
water-court, and a great deal of open ground that is plainly tended and not plainly cultivated. It is
protected the way every Senali enclave is protected—not by force, but because the paths that lead to
Aelirosse have a way of leading elsewhere.

## The Nearest Neighbors

Aelirosse is the closest that Senali and humans live to one another anywhere in Thalorna, and the
relationship with the surrounding [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] is **genuinely cordial**—not an alliance, which would
imply purposes held in common, but a long neighborliness between peoples who share a wood and have had
many centuries to work out how.

Áelendan come and go without ceremony. Senali attend some Áelendan observances and are attended at
some of their own. The songs [[affiliation-bardicolgs|the Bardic Colleges]] treasure as Senalian gifts
were, most of them, first heard here and carried out by Áelendan who had been given them freely.

One of them, [[lore-seventowers|The Seven Towers]], stands in the makers' halls and says what the Senali make instead:

: We Do Not Build

::: {.verse form=elegy lang=en work=aelirosse-1}
We do not build. We have never wished to; the forest builds itself,/
.and what we make we sing, and what we sing does not fall down./
But we stood a long while in those ways and said nothing,/
.and among us there is no higher praise than that.
:::

Whether the [[place-sourimael|Sóurimel]] villagers a half-day west understand what they live beside is a
matter on which the Senali have never been asked and the Áelendan decline to speculate.

## The Present {#present}

The birch law holds, and it is under more pressure than it has been in a generation. No standing silver birch in the Silver Forests is felled by anyone for any reason, and the Senali of Aelirosse, whose dwellings are worked into the living trees, are the other party to that law with the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] ([[lore-blacksnow|The Black Snow]]). This year a strip of green bark cut from [[place-wkngstnd|the Waking Stand]] lies in a chapterhouse collection at [[place-beravel|Péravel]], the Wardens of the Silver Forests want the man who cut it, and [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] parties come up the valleys past the crown's guides with hired guards. The enclave has said nothing of any of it through [[place-ethalosse|Ethalosse]].
