Nelgan is the eastern pass of the Áelendan country, a notch in the high ridge above [[place-ethalosse|Ethalosse]] that is blocked by snow for much of the year. The [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] call it the Snow-Gate. It is the way the people came into the country and the way the country has been entered from the east since.

## The Cairn

Near the top of the pass, on the west side, stands a cairn of grey stones over the ones who sat down to rest in the snow during [[lore-nelgancross|the Crossing]] and did not stand again. The Áelendan say they sit there still.

## The Watch-Post

A [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens' watch-post]] stands below the ridge on the western slope, built of the pass's own stone and kept by Wardens in pairs through the season the pass is open. The Wardens charge a **passage-gift** from the caravans that come over from the east, in goods and not coin, and in return they keep the road marked. The pass has been the Wardens' charge since [[lore-firesnelgan|the Fires on Nelgan]].

## The Scorched Rock

Below the cairn, on a shelf of rock out of the wind, the stone is black and shattered. A war-band from east of the mountains came over the pass with fire, and the rock marks where it camped and lit its brands. Iron from the raid turns up in the scree, mostly arrowheads and spear-heels, and now and then something larger.

## Who Goes

- **Wardens,** who keep the watch-post and walk the pass at the turn of the seasons.
- **Merchants and drovers from the east,** who pay the passage-gift and want to cross before the snow clears or after it closes.

## Hooks

- **A figure at the cairn.** A caravan caught in the snow finds someone sitting by the stones, who does not rise when the caravan leaves.
- **A raider's iron.** A piece dug out of the scree is a warrior's helm or a chief's spear-head, and the Wardens want it back.
- **A caravan master who will not wait.** A merchant who wants to cross in the dead of winter offers more than the passage-gift, and a young Warden is tempted.
