## Overview {#overview}

The **Enclave of Aelirosse** is the community of about a hundred and twenty of the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale Folk]] at [[place-aelirosse|Aelirosse]], deep in [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]] of [[place-sorvanda|Sórvanda]], not far from [[place-urontekhi|the Uron Tekhi]]. It is neither the crown's nor the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]]'. It is the closest that Sinale and humans live to one another anywhere in Thalorna, and its relationship with the surrounding [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] is cordial, not an alliance: a long neighborliness between peoples who share a wood. The Sinale have kings, queens and lords of their own, who do not work as human ones do, and the enclave is governed by its lord.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

A Sinalo of Aelirosse belongs to a hearth the enclave keeps. The [[lore-sinaleclt|Sinale]] raise a lord for things done, by every voice, and no human record names the lord of Aelirosse. One the lord drives out is _vyerka_, an outlaw, given no standing or shelter.

What passes between the enclave and a human authority goes, as all such business does, through [[affiliation-nclvthlss|Ethalosse]] and the Áelendan who carry. What passes between Aelirosse and its Áelendan neighbors needs no carrier. Áelendan come and go without ceremony, Sinale attend some Áelendan observances and are attended at some of their own, and marriage, fosterage and long friendship happen at that edge, rarely and quietly.

## Current Situation {#present}

The enclave and the Áelendan keep one law between them: no standing silver birch in the Silver Forests is felled, by anyone, for any reason. The Sinale of Aelirosse, whose dwellings are worked into the living birch, are the other party to it, and the village of [[place-sourimael|Sóurimel]] a half-day west keeps it too. Of the asking that restored the law after [[lore-blacksnow|the Black Snow]], the Sinale say that they said yes, and that they have not told the Áelendan the cost.

Whether Sóurimel's villagers understand what they live beside is a matter on which the Sinale have never been asked and the Áelendan decline to speculate.

## Defense {#arms}

The enclave is protected the way every Sinale enclave is protected: not by force, but because the paths that lead to Aelirosse have a way of leading elsewhere. Its Áelendan neighbors are the exception, and their coming and going is ordinary.

## Faith {#faith}

The enclave keeps its Oak-Wardens and its Oak-Pupils. [[lore-saunari|The Oak-Warden's Sayings]] are the counsel one Oak-Warden of Aelirosse, [Olmasitti Ropelinto]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, gave the Oak-Pupil he taught, in the grove, at the water-court, at the sickbed and once at the ford, and every Oak-Pupil of Aelirosse learns them. [[lore-ellevirsa|Guest-Right]] is sung over a guest at Aelirosse on the first night the guest sleeps there.

## The Enclave {#holdings}

Aelirosse is not a settlement in any sense a human surveyor would accept. There are no streets and no walls. There are dwellings worked into and around the living silver birch over the course of centuries, a water-court, and a great deal of open ground that is plainly tended and not plainly cultivated.

## History {#history}

The Sinale have lived at Aelirosse for longer than there has been a [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|kingdom of Élavendre]] to be unaware of them. About 1300 BF the Áelendan chief Ghalmir felled a stand of silver birch for fuel in a killing winter and took the watch off the fog-hollow, something came through, and his sister Mogha led the people back to the wood's edge to sit three days and ask to keep the wood again ([[lore-blacksnow|The Black Snow]]). The law against felling the birch stands from then.

Most of the songs [[affiliation-bardicolgs|the Bardic Colleges]] treasure as Sinalean gifts were first heard at Aelirosse and carried out by Áelendan who had been given them freely, [[lore-seventowers|The Seven Towers]] among them.

## Relations {#relations}

- **The Áelendan** of the Silver Forests are its neighbors of many centuries, cordial and not allied.
- **Sóurimel**, the village a half-day west, keeps the birch law beside it.
- **The crown and the Wardens** hold no authority over it.
- **[[affiliation-nclvthlss|Ethalosse]]** is the door through which its business with human powers passes.
