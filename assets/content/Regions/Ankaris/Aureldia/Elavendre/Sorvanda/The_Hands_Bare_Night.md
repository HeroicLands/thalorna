[[place-sourimael|Sóurimel]] keeps the old festivals rather than the Aureldian calendar, and the one that outsiders remember is the **Hands-Bare Night**. It is kept on the longest night of the year, at the edge of the birch wood above the village, and nobody in Sóurimel will say that it is a festival.

## The Night

At dusk the whole village goes up to the birches and sits among the standing trees in the cold, with hands bare of gloves and wraps, and does not light a fire. The villagers sing in the manner of [[lore-theasking|The Asking]], low and in answering lines, from the dusk to the first grey. The singers are the village's own. They hold what the village has of the song and add nothing to it.

The silver that left the bark on the night of the felling ([[lore-blacksnow|The Black Snow]]) came back to the standing birch slowly, over four lives, and the village does not forget what it cost. Sóurimel sits to ask to keep the wood, as the people asked at the wood's edge when they went back, and it does not say what it asks or what answer it waits for.

## The Law Behind It

No standing silver birch is felled at Sóurimel, ever, by anyone, for any reason. The night is the law's yearly sitting: the night is the village's way of asking again, as the chief's sister led the people back to the wood's edge to ask. The Sinale of [[place-aelirosse|Aelirosse]], whose dwellings are worked into living birch, are the other party to the law.

## Outsiders

A stranger who arrives in Sóurimel for the night is welcomed and watched. He is told where to sit and is asked to take off his gloves, and a stranger who cuts a twig for a fire is put out of the wood at once by the village. The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] who pass through Sóurimel do so that night without comment, as they do on every other.

## What a Party Finds

A party that comes up with the village sits where it is put. It hears the song and sees the starlight come up on the bark, birch by birch, until the whole wood stands pale in the dark. On a clouded night the bark stays dark and the village sits all the same. A guest whose hands are not bare is asked once.
