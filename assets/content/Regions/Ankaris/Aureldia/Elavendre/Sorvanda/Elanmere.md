**Elanmere** stands on the shore of the lake that gives it its name, in a glacial valley at the western
foot of [[place-sorvanda|Sórvanda]] where the mountains give way to the [[place-ellive|Éllive]] basin. Some
two thousand one hundred people live there year-round, and a great many more pass through.

## The Meeting Place

Elanmere is where the high country and the low country do business. It is the highest town an Élavendri
merchant will comfortably reach and the lowest an [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] will comfortably come
to, which makes it the natural market for everything Sórvanda sends down: mountain cheese, hides and furs,
wool, herbs, and the small, closely-watched quantity of worked silver and rough gemstone that the tribes
release by arrangement ([[lore-sorvandaclose|The Sórvanda Closing]]).

The autumn fair is the event of the year. For a fortnight the lake shore fills with tribal families who
will not enter a walled town, [[place-beravel|Péravel]] factors who have traveled a week to meet them, and a great deal
of careful, ceremonious, extremely slow negotiation.

## The Lake

Deep, cold and startlingly clear, fed from the snowfields above. It supports a good fishery and a small
trade in the reed and rush work the district is known for.

It is also understood to be _inhabited_, in the way most deep water in Sórvanda is understood to be
inhabited, and the boatmen keep observances about where one does and does not put down a net. Visitors
who make light of this are corrected once.
