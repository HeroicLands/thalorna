**Ethalosse**, as it is called in [[skill-elvndrlng|Élavendri]], sits at the eastern margin of the [[place-alndntrblnds|Áelendan Tribal Lands]], where the
high wood thins toward the passes. It is the smallest of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]]'s [[lore-flksenali|Senali enclaves]]—seventy or so—and the only one whose position looks, to an outsider who knew enough to notice,
**chosen for access rather than for seclusion**.

That is exactly what it is. Ethalosse is where the Senali conduct business with the world.

## The Intermediaries

The Senali do not treat with human powers directly. They have not for millennia, they decline to explain why,
and the handful of exceptions are all one exception: they will deal with the
[[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]], and the Áelendan will carry. Élavendre's royal family and aristocracy of the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]] hold the formal relation with the enclaves; the day-to-day contact runs through the Áelendan.

So when something must pass between a Senali enclave and a human authority—a warning, a refusal, a
question about a disturbed site, the return of something taken—it goes through Ethalosse, and out
again in the mouth of an Áelendan who has been asked to carry it. Elders come up to the enclave; they
are received; they go back down and speak to whoever needs speaking to.

The arrangement has two properties that matter enormously and are rarely stated:

- **The Áelendan carry; they do not represent.** No Áelendan has ever claimed to speak _for_ the Senali,
  and none would. What is carried is carried exactly, and an elder who was asked to embellish would
  decline and say so.
- **It makes the Áelendan indispensable to everyone.** The Élavendren crown's celebrated centuries-long formal relationship with the Senali is, in practice and in the overwhelming majority of instances, carried on through Áelendan intermediaries. The crown does not advertise this, for the obvious reason:
  it means the relationship is not the monarchy's to possess but the tribes' to grant, and it could be
  withdrawn without a word being said to anyone at [[place-beravel|Péravel]].

Every Élavendren monarch has understood this. It is the quiet floor beneath the whole of the kingdom's
careful management of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Arcane Order]]—and the reason no reign has ever been
willing to let the Áelendan be pushed too far.

The arrangement dates from the day the wood was given to the Áelendan in the court itself ([[lore-firesnelgan|The Fires on Nelgan]]).

## The Threshold

Ethalosse itself is spare by Senalian standards: a court of worked stone that predates the forest around it, a stone work the Senali keep, though they prefer to grow what they build, a few dwellings, and the long open way down which visitors are watched arriving for a considerable
time before they arrive. It is the one Senali settlement in Élavendre a human has a realistic chance of
being invited to, which is not the same as a realistic chance.

## The Present {#present}

The carriers are coming up more often than they used to. A warning, a refusal or a question about a disturbed site reaches the court of worked stone in the mouth of an Áelendan elder, and [[being-ansorulamasse|Ansorula Masserinto]], who receives the carriers, gives back exactly what the enclave has said. This season the questions are about the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]]'s camps above the tree line and the things its parties have carried down to [[place-beravel|Péravel]], and the enclave's answers go back down by the same mouths and are given to whoever needs them, the crown included, without a word added.
