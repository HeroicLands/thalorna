## Overview {#overview}

**Mallonna** is a deep pool in [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]], round and black and still, with a flat stone standing at its edge. In the Sinale tongue the name is the hearth of the deep pool. It is a thin place in water, an [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] whose door lets in the fae, and the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale]] [Lauva]{.name data-type="being/npc"} keeps it.

The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] keep its approaches, and the pool itself is Lauva's. The Áelendan name it in their oldest song as the place their first chief knelt.

## The Land {#the-land}

The pool lies in a fold of the forest where the birch gives way to older trees and the ground falls toward the water. The surface does not move in wind. The bank is steep and clean, with no weed, no reed and no shingle, and the water goes dark a stride from the edge. Nothing grows in the pool, and nothing floats on it. The stone at the edge is flat on top and worn smooth, large enough for one person to stand on.

The track to it is not a track. The Sinale lead outsiders by paths of their own, and a traveler who looks for the pool without them finds the forest declining to be walked.

## Who Uses It {#who-uses-it}

Lauva keeps it. Her care is water, and she has kept the pool as long as the Sinale have been at the wood. She is of the old stock of her people, and she does not speak to those who come to the pool unless she is spoken to well.

Nobody else uses it. The Áelendan kneel at the edge and leave bread, as the first of them did, and what is left on the stone is gone by morning. The Sinale of [[place-aelirosse|Aelirosse]] come for reasons of their own.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **What is under it.** The Sinale say it has limbs and no head. Nobody who has gone into the pool has come out to add to that.
- **The depth.** The water is cold and deep from the first stride, and the bank is too steep to climb from the water.
- **The door.** Whatever comes up through the pool comes through into the forest, and what the fae send up is as various as the fae.
- **The paths.** The forest turns away anyone the Sinale have not led.

## What Is Told {#what-is-told}

**The Sinale account.** The pool is thin, and Lauva keeps it. Hauletta told the first chief of the Áelendan not to go in, because what is under it has limbs and no head ([[lore-theasking|The Asking]]).

**The Áelendan account.** The chief knelt at the pool alone, took Lauva for the wood and laid bread before her feet. She went away without eating, and the wood went still, and he took the stillness for a yes. He told it so at the fire, and so it is sung.

**The other half.** The Sinale say Lauva ate the bread when he had gone and said it was well made. She came back to the others and told them what he had done, and the talk went on. They say it is good bread, still.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The Áelendan want the pool left as it is, and the bread on the stone. The Wardens of the Silver Forests want it kept out of every Order survey. The Order wants a sample of the water and a look at what is under it. Lauva wants what she has always wanted from those who come: to be spoken to well, and left alone.

A fellow of the [University of Péravel]{.name data-type="affiliation"} has asked the Order's chapterhouse for guards to carry a lead-lined vessel to the Silver Forests and bring it back full. The Wardens know he has asked.

## History {#history}

The pool is older than the Áelendan in the forest, and the Sinale say nothing of when it first thinned. It is the place where the first of the Áelendan knelt, which the Áelendan hold to be the beginning of their standing with the wood ([[lore-lowestpoolpct|The Pact at the Lowest Pool]]).

:::secret {title="What is under Mallonna"}
What is under the pool is asleep, and the pool holds it so. The name the Sinale give the place, the hearth of the deep pool, means what it says: a thing is kept at Mallonna by being returned to, as a hearth is kept, and the bread the Áelendan have left on the stone since the first chief is the returning. Lauva eats it, as she ate the first loaf, because the keeper must be seen to take what is given.

If the bread stops, the thing stirs. It does not wake in a night. The first sign is that the pool moves in wind.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The vessel.** The University's fellow hires guards to carry a lead-lined vessel to the pool, and the Wardens want it turned back before it reaches the stone.
- **Good bread.** A woman of Sóurimel bakes the loaf that is left on the stone, and nobody has asked her to, and her flour is short this year.
- **Lauva's word.** A messenger from Aelirosse brings the Lodge word that Lauva has spoken of the pool, and the Wardens want a lowlander to carry the reply.
