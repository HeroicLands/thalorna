## Overview {#overview}

The **Enclave of Ethalosse** is the community of about seventy of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]] at [[place-ethalosse|Ethalosse]], which the Senali call _Etalosse_. It lies at the eastern margin of the [[place-alndntrblnds|Áelendan Tribal Lands]], where the high wood thins below the pass of [[place-nelgan|Nelgan]]. It is the smallest of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]]'s three enclaves and the only one placed for access rather than seclusion: it is where the Senali conduct business with the world. The Senali have kings, queens and lords of their own, who do not work as human ones do, and the enclave is governed by its lord.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

A Senalo of Ethalosse belongs to a hearth the enclave keeps. The [[lore-senaliclt|Senali]] raise a lord for things done, by every voice, and a Senali praise-song praises the deed and withholds the doer; no human record names the lord of Ethalosse, who is [[being-raltilaoltes|Raltila Oltessanto]]. Beside the lord of the enclave stand other lords, Senali the enclave holds in respect for things done, among them [[being-aldessevolapu|Aldessevo Lapurinto]] and [[being-issaltelalise|Issaltela Liserinto]]. A lord's standing is a rank and not a holding. One the lord drives out is _vyerka_, an outlaw, given no standing or shelter.

The Senali do not treat with human powers directly, and they decline to explain why. They deal with the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]], and the Áelendan carry. A warning, a refusal, a question about a disturbed site or the return of something taken goes up to Ethalosse in the mouths of Áelendan elders who have been asked to carry it. The elders are received, and they go back down and speak to whoever needs speaking to. **The Áelendan carry; they do not represent.** What is carried is carried exactly, and no Áelendan claims to speak for the Senali.

## Current Situation {#present}

The royal family and the aristocracy of the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]] hold the formal relation with the enclaves, and day to day the contact runs through Ethalosse and the Áelendan who carry. The relationship is therefore not the monarchy's to possess but the tribes' to grant, and it could be withdrawn without a word being said to anyone at [[place-beravel|Péravel]]. The crown does not advertise this. It is the quiet floor beneath the kingdom's careful management of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Arcane Order]], and the reason no reign lets the Áelendan be pushed too far.

The court's last great question to Ethalosse stands unanswered. When Péravel asked, through two carriers, how the Senali hold that the world ends, what came down was [[lore-gatheringend|The Gathering on the End]], the account of a convocation that has never reached accord, with the gathering's standing as its answer: "We have not agreed. Ask again when you have a speech of your own."

## Defense {#arms}

Walls and armies do not protect a Senali enclave. A path that leads to one uninvited leads somewhere else. At Ethalosse the long open way to the court is watched, and visitors are seen arriving for a considerable time before they arrive. Ethalosse is the one Senali settlement in Élavendre a human has a realistic chance of being invited to, which is not the same as a realistic chance.

## The Court {#holdings}

Ethalosse is spare by Senalian standards: a court of worked stone older than the forest around it, a few dwellings, and the long open way. The court is where carriers are received.

## History {#history}

About 1380 BF a band of Áelendan came down from Nelgan in the dark and found below the pass a court of worked stone with grass in its cracks, older than stone should be, and nobody went in ([[lore-nelgancross|The Crossing of Nelgan]]). About 1250 BF raiders came over the same pass with brands, and the Senali walked out through the fire behind [Hauletta]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the eldest of them. The wounded Dhargan was laid in the court at Ethalosse, and there Hauletta gave the wood to the Áelendan to keep ([[lore-firesnelgan|The Fires on Nelgan]]). The carrying dates from that day.

## Relations {#relations}

- **The Áelendan** carry the enclave's words and receive its songs, and the bond is the closest between the Senali and any human people.
- **The crown of Élavendre** holds the formal relation and reaches the enclave only through the carriers.
- **The Arcane Order** is turned back at the tree line by the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]], who regard it as an intruder on what the tribes exist to keep.
- **The other enclaves**, [[affiliation-nclvlrss|Aelirosse]] and [[affiliation-nclvsrnthl|Serenthale]], deal with the world through Ethalosse when they deal with it at all.
