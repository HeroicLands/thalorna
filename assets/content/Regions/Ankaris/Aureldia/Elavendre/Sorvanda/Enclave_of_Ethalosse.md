The **Enclave of Ethalossë** is the Sinalë community of [[place-ethalosse|Ethalossë]], at the eastern margin of the [[place-alndntrblnds|Áelendan Tribal Lands]]. Sinalë have kings, queens and lords, but these do not work as human ones do, and the enclave is governed by its lord. It is the point through which the [[lore-flksinale|Sinalë]] deal with the rest of humanity, by way of [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] intermediaries who carry what is said and do not speak for the enclave.
