## Overview {#overview}

**Talve** is a bowl of ground at the edge of [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]], beyond [[place-fldstnd|the Felled Stand]] and within sound of [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]], where a white fog lies. It lies in every season, and in spring, when the cold goes out of the ground, it thickens and rises. The hollow is a door to fell things: an [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] that lets in what does not belong on this side and does not go back of itself. The Senali gave it its name, which means the low fog, and it is sacred ground of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]], the oldest they keep.

It is the first thing the Áelendan watched. [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|The Wardens]] began as the watch set on it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The Wardens hold it with the oldest form of their keeping, and the watch is never pulled. Two Wardens sit by the hollow through every night with spear and dog and a banked fire, turn what comes through by song, fire or stone, retire when it does not turn and sing from the hall on the nights it wins. By day one sits at the edge. On the nights the watch wins, the song is sung from the Lodge's hall, and the hall's people sing it with them.

Nobody goes in, the Wardens included.

## Layers {#history}

The first account of the hollow comes from the winter after the Áelendan reached the wood, and the tellings agree on that and on little else.

**The Áelendan account.** The band had come to the edge of the forest and was starving. A son of the chief's went in by day with fire to drive the deer, and the fog kept him. The chief set the watch the following spring, and the watch has not left the hollow since ([[lore-lowestpoolpct|The Pact at the Lowest Pool]]).

**The Senali account.** The hollow is thin when the cold goes out of the ground. [Hauletta]{.name data-type="being/npc"} told the chief how to keep it, and told him his son was not dead. The Senali do not say more of what keeps him.

**The Black Snow.** In the killing winter, the chief of the people pulled the watch from the hollow to haul and split wood, and in spring something of fog, shaped like a stag and without eyes, came through and killed at the Lodge. The people and the Senali sang together at the hollow, and the thin place took the thing back ([[lore-blacksnow|The Black Snow]]).

**The Order's reading.** A theorist of the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] holds that the hollow is a frost pocket where cold air pools and breeds fog, and that what has come out of it on spring nights is a deer in the mist.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The watch is whole, and it is young. Two of the four Wardens who sit the spring nights are in their second year, and they have been told what the hollow does at the thaw. The ground has begun to soften. The fog has come up to the Stand, and it lies on the stumps in the morning.

A fellow of the University has paid a guide of [[place-sourimael|Sóurimel]] to bring him to the margin by the Felled Stand, to see the fog in the right season. The Wardens of the Lodge know a stranger is coming. They have not yet decided what the right season means to a man who has come to see it.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbours}

- **What comes through.** A thing of fog in a stag's shape took a dog, then three children, then a man who went at it with fire and the wrong song.
- **The fog.** It hides the hollow's lip and the ground below the watch's fire, and it muffles song.
- **The watch.** Two Wardens by the hollow at night turn a stranger back once, and they do not carry him out a second time.
- **The call.** The hollow calls, and it hears when it is called back. A person who calls into it by day with a fire is answered.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

**The edge-post.** A bench of split oak at the lip, a spear-rack, a heap of river stones that the young Wardens carry up from the beck, and a dog's kennel of turf.

**The fire.** A banked hearth at the post, never allowed to go out. Beside it, a flat stone with a handful of ash and a few small things left on it by the people of the Lodge.

**The hollow.** A smooth bowl of ground a long bowshot across, grass-grown on its rim, its floor under white fog that does not move with the wind.

**The marks.** On the stones along the rim, cut by no tool a smith knows, a line of marks that the Wardens do not read aloud.

:::secret {title="The truth of Talve"}
The chief's son is alive in the fog. He was a young man in the first winter of the Áelendan and he is a young man now. He does not age, he does not eat, and he is not alone, because the fog keeps others it has taken since, and they are all as they were when it took them. The stag-thing of the Black Snow is not made by the hollow. It is what the hollow lets through when the watch is gone, and what answers the spring cold and the wrong song.

The right song is one the Tradition Keepers hold, and they teach it to the Wardens of the Talve watch and to nobody else. It was held whole by a Keeper in the Black Snow year, and she was not asked. The song turns what comes through. It does not close the door, and nothing the Áelendan know does.

A person who stays in the fog past the dusk of one day does not come out as the same age, or at the same time, as when he went in.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The watch needs a hand.** A pair of the Wardens at Talve is short a man for the thaw, and a Warden of the Lodge asks the party to sit the edge by day.
- **The guide's stranger.** The University fellow arrives at the Felled Stand with a guide of Sóurimel and a sketching frame, and wants to see the fog from the lip.
- **The voice.** A Warden of the watch hears his own name from the fog at the edge of dusk, and the pair that sits beside him does not hear it.
- **The unsung song.** The young Keeper who holds the song for the watch has never sung it at the hollow and is afraid to, and the Lodge wants a steady hand beside her at the thaw.
