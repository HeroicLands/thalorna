## Overview {#overview}

**The Wandering Ridge** is a long, high ridge of [[place-drenavrspn|the Drenavar Spine]], grassed on its flanks and bare rock along its crest, that divides two of the range's high valleys. It is alive. Its saddle is not where it was the season before; its springs run and stop; its gullies open and close; scree moves uphill on it in the night. When it lies right, a crossing of its saddle is the shortest way between the two valleys by two days, and when it does not, the crossing ends at a drop that was not there. It is an [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] whose door lets in the fae as living ground, and it is sacred ground of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]].

It is the plainest case on the Spine of what travelers say of the whole range: that paths which were obvious one day have shifted or vanished by the next. People go to it to cross it, and the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] goes to it to measure it twice and compare.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] of the Spine hold the ridge, and outsiders are not let onto it. The Áelendan of the two valleys cross it in summer by leave of the Wardens and by the protective songs for the ground a person must pass, which every child of those valleys learns. A crossing party sings the ridge's song from the foot to the far foot without a break. Nobody crosses after dark, and nobody leaves anything on it.

## Layers {#history}

What the ridge is, and how long it has been alive, is not known.

**The Áelendan account.** The Tradition Keepers hold that the ridge is fae and not [[lore-thekindred|Kindred]]: nothing is seated in it, it makes no bargain, and the song is not an offering but a courtesy, the asking of leave to walk on something that is alive. [[lore-urnelspr|Urnel]], the Kindred of the high corries, warns of avalanche elsewhere on the Spine; on the ridge the Áelendan do not look to him.

**The herders' account.** The herders of the two valleys say the ridge closes over what is left on it, and they count the sheep it has taken.

**The Order's reading.** Order surveyors have mapped the ridge in different seasons and kept every map. No two agree. The Order's theorists hold that the fault lies in the surveyors, that the range confuses the eye, and that a careful enough survey sets the matter to rest.

## What Is There Now {#present}

An Order survey under hired guards has set a line of marker cairns along the crest, one at every hundred paces, to measure how far the ridge moves between one season and the next. Since it was set the line has bent, and its far end runs out over a gully that the first surveyor did not draw. A guard is missing on the ridge. The Wardens of the Spine are taking the cairns down one by one, and the survey's scholar is camped in the lower valley, waiting for the guard and refusing to leave.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbours}

- **The ridge,** which opens and closes beneath a walker.
- **The weather of the Spine,** mist above all: a crossing in mist cannot see where the ridge has gone.
- **The Wardens,** who turn outsiders off the ridge at its foot and do not turn them a second time.
- **Shardbeaks,** which perch on the marker cairns in sunlight ([[being-shrdbk|Shardbeak]]).

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

**The foot.** The path from either valley climbs through grass and juniper to a slope of loose stone, where the old path stops being where it was and a party must find the ridge's way for itself.

**The saddle.** A long grassy saddle with a spring in it, or without one. A rough shelter of piled stone stands on one side, roofless and half buried.

**The crest.** Bare rock and the Order's cairns, a line that runs straight for half a mile and then curves.

**The gully.** A raw, new cleft below the end of the cairn line, its sides fresh earth and torn roots, with the last cairn lying at its foot.

:::secret {title="The truth of the Wandering Ridge"}
The ridge does not move under a song. While a crossing party sings the ridge's song without a break, the ground under them keeps still, and it moves again when the song stops. The Áelendan who cross it know only that the song brings them over safely.

It closes over what is left on it. A thing set down and walked away from, a cairn, a tent, a sleeping man, is under the turf by the next season. The missing guard lay down to sleep on the saddle two nights after the cairns were set, and the ground closed on him in the night. He is alive, held in a hollow under the saddle that is still open, and a party on the saddle at night hears him. He has a day or two of air.

The roofless shelter is an Áelendan shelter from the herders' grandfathers' time, built where the saddle then lay, and the saddle has walked half out from under it.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The missing guard.** The Order's scholar in the valley pays the party to find his guard on the ridge, and the Wardens have closed it.
- **The contract.** The missing guard's brother, a sellsword at [[place-liranel|Liranel]], wants to know whether anyone told the guards that the Order's survey was on forbidden ground, and what the contract they signed said about it.
- **The short way.** A herder needs a sick child carried over the ridge to a shaman in the far valley, and the long way round takes two days the child does not have.
