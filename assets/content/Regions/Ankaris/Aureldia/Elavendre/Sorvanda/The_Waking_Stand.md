## Overview {#overview}

**The Waking Stand** is a stand of very large trees in the deep of [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]], beyond [[place-sourimael|Sóurimel]] and well inside the sacred country. Their bark has the forests' sheen of metal in moonlight, but they are not birch, and no woodsman has put a name to their kind. Their branches move when there is no wind. The stand is not in the same shape from one visit to the next: a gap between two trunks is closed a season later, and a tree that stood at the edge stands in the middle. It is an [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] whose door lets in the fae as sentient trees, and it is sacred ground of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]].

People go to it to see trees that wake. The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] wants a cutting of one, and it is the one thing in the Silver Forests the Áelendan least want cut.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] of the Silver Forests hold the stand, and their patrols pass round it in daylight and never camp within sight of it. The [[lore-flksenali|Senali]], who share the Silver Forests with the Áelendan, have never said whether the stand is anything to them.

The birch law of the Áelendan forbids anyone to fell a standing silver birch for any reason ([[lore-blacksnow|The Black Snow]]). The trees of the stand are not birch, and the Wardens treat them as if the law ran twice over: nobody cuts, breaks or takes a branch, a leaf or a strip of bark, and nobody lights a fire within the stand.

## Layers {#history}

How old the stand is, and whether it grew where it stands or came there, is not known.

**The Áelendan account.** The Tradition Keepers sing that the stand came through the door and stayed, and that its trees are fae and not [[lore-thekindred|Kindred]]: they are not the seated spirits of the wood, and nothing in the Pact is owed them or asked of them. The Kindred grove-keepers of the Silver Forests are older than the tribes, the Keepers say, and the stand is older than the grove-keepers.

**Sóurimel's account.** The charcoal-burners of Sóurimel say the stand walks, a little each winter, and that it is walking toward the village. Nobody in Sóurimel has been to look.

**The Order's reading.** Order theorists hold that the trees are ordinary trees of an old kind, altered by long exposure to the thin place, and that their movement is growth too fast to be natural and too slow to be seen.

## What Is There Now {#present}

[[miscgear-grnbarkwkng|A strip of bark]] from one of the trees, cut by a poacher and sold in Lísere, lies in a chapterhouse collection at [[place-beravel|Péravel]], and the Order's fellows who keep it say that it is still green. The Order has licensed a fellow of the University to bring back a living cutting, and he is hiring guards. The Wardens of the Silver Forests know that bark was taken. They do not know who took it, and they are looking.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbours}

- **The trees** themselves, at night.
- **The Wardens,** who count a cut as desecration and the man who carries the cut wood as the man who made it.
- **The hunter of the stone ring** on the forests' outer margin, which takes whoever walks alone on its ground ([[place-hntrsrng|The Hunter's Ring]]).
- **The Silver Forests' turning paths,** which bend a traveler away from places the wood is not to be walked ([[place-rngtrndpths|The Ring of Turned Paths]]).

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

**The edge.** The ordinary silver birch of the forest stops a little short of the stand, and the ground between is bare of undergrowth and soft with old leaf. Nothing grazes there.

**The trees.** Trunks too broad for three people to circle with their arms, smooth bark gleaming dully in daylight and brightly under the moon, branches beginning high. Up close the bark is warm. Here and there a root lies across the ground in a furrow of turned earth, as a plow leaves it.

**The middle.** A clearing among the largest trees, its size different at every visit, with a stone in it that the roots have closed half round.

**The scar.** On the trunk nearest the clearing, a long strip of bark is gone, at the height of a man's chest, and the wood beneath has not darkened.

:::secret {title="The truth of the Waking Stand"}
The trees move when nobody is watching them. A tree under the eye of anyone awake does not move; a tree in the dark, or behind a sleeping camp, moves at the pace of a walking man, and its roots draw it through the ground. They do not move far, and they do not leave the stand.

They remember every cut. Whoever carries wood, bark or leaf taken from the stand is followed, and the trees close round him wherever he sleeps within the Silver Forests, first in a ring, and on the second night tighter. The poacher who cut the strip of bark sold it in Lísere because he slept one night under the stand and woke inside a ring of trunks with a hand's breadth between them, and he walked out of the forest without stopping.

The strip of bark at Péravel is green because the stand has not let it go. The trees cannot follow it out of the Silver Forests. A cutting carried out of the forests lives as long as its bearer keeps moving and is never left in the dark within them.

Sóurimel's account is true: the stand moves toward the village, a little each winter, because the poacher was a Sóurimel man.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The living cutting.** The Order's fellow hires the party to guard him through two nights in the Silver Forests while he takes a cutting, and pays half before they leave Péravel.
- **Who cut the bark.** A Warden of the Silver Forests asks the party to find out who sold the bark in Lísere, because the stand knows and the Wardens do not.
- **The walking wood.** A charcoal-burner of Sóurimel pays the party to go and look at the stand and tell the village how near it is.
