**Béravel** stands on the [[place-beravriver|Bérav]] some **fifteen miles up from the western sea**, at
the point where the tide stops and sea-going hulls must give way to barges. It is the capital of
[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Élavendre]], the largest city in the kingdom at around fifty-five thousand
people, and the reason [[place-tirwen|Tirwen]] is arranged the way it is.

The city takes its name from the river, not the other way about.

## The Site

Fifteen miles is a deliberate distance. Far enough up that no raider under sail arrives without warning
and without a pilot; near enough that the tide does the work of bringing ships to the quays. Béravel has
never been sacked from the sea, which distinguishes it from most of the coast north and south of it.

The city is built on both banks and on the islands between, joined by the bridges Élavendre is famous
for – pale stone, and in the older quarters grown over with the trained living vine that visitors
always mention and residents never do.

## The University

The **University of Béravel** is the largest [[affiliation-panepistmm|Panepistemium]] campus outside
the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], with several Sodalitas chapterhouses, and it makes the
capital the center of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]]'s presence in the kingdom.

It also makes Béravel the field on which the kingdom's central quarrel is fought. The
[[affiliation-bardicolgs|Bardic Colleges]], the Grove Council and the Áelendan tradition all keep
establishments here, all dispute the Ordo's authority over Élavendre's supernatural life, and all
conduct that dispute – in the capital – through patronage, appointment and the placing of students
rather than through anything worse. The city's booksellers do very well out of the argument.

## The Economy

Everything the kingdom produces passes through. Wool and iron come down from
[[place-brynavar|Brynavar]], the little metal and gemstone that [[place-ardwyn|Ardwyn]] releases comes
down with it, timber and dye come round the coast from [[place-glanmor|Glanmor]], and salt-fish comes
south from [[place-cilfor|Cilfor]].

What leaves is worth far more than what arrives, because Béravel is where it is worked: goldsmiths and
gem-cutters who have made an art of using almost no metal, instrument-makers, weavers, glasshouses, and
the copying and binding trades of the University quarter. The crown's steadiest revenue is the toll on
this traffic.
