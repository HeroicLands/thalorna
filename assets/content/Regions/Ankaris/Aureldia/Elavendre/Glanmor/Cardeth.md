## Overview

Cárdeth is a noble seat in [[place-glanmor|Glanmor]], within reach of the forest town of [[place-liranel|Liranel]], and it is held by **Lady Eadrun of Cárdeth**, a young noblewoman with Áelendan ancestry. Cárdeth is an Élavendri name, and it is also borne as a family name by Élavendri households as far apart as [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]] and [[place-silvain|Silvain]].

## The Lady and Her Physician

Lady Eadrun is intelligent and ambitious, and she shares a certain pragmatism about the gap between public virtue and private necessity. She has begun seeking out [[being-belrkflwyn|Bélrik Fálwyn]], the alchemist of Liranel, for discreet remedies, and her patronage has become important to him. He knows it to be potentially dangerous as well: noble favor in Glanmor can be withdrawn as quickly as it is given, and what a lady asks for in private is exactly what she will deny in public.
