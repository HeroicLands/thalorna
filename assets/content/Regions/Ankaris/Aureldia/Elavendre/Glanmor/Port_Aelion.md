## Overview

Port Aelion sits upon the western coast of Élavendre where the great cliffs yield to gentler slopes and natural harbors provide shelter for merchant vessels and fishing boats alike. The town has emerged as Élavendre's primary gateway for sea trade, its wharves bustling with commerce between the kingdom and distant realms. Yet despite its mercantile function, Port Aelion retains a character distinctly Élavendren—the stone buildings incorporate fae architectural motifs, the markets honor both traditional crafts and the subtle magics that characterize Élavendre's culture, and the town's foundational stones are said to have been blessed by Sinalë who once walked the coastline.

The population of Port Aelion is more cosmopolitan than inland Élavendren towns, yet visitors remark that something ineffable distinguishes it from typical trading ports. The light seems softer here, the boundaries between seasons less rigid. Local merchants and fishermen speak of how the tides sometimes follow their own rhythms, independent of astronomical calculation, and how travelers occasionally vanish into the mist only to reappear days later claiming no time has passed. These stories are dismissed as sailor's tales by outsiders, but Port Aelion's residents treat them as simple facts of living in a land touched by older, stranger magics.

The town's patron saint is Aelion, a half-legendary Élavendren hero said to have maintained peace between human and fae communities centuries ago. Her temple stands at the highest point of the town, and captains always make offerings there before setting sail. The town council blends traditional human governance with the counsel of older families rumored to possess fae ancestry, and visitors sense that decisions are made according to principles not written in any law code—a balance maintained through centuries of compromise and mutual respect.
