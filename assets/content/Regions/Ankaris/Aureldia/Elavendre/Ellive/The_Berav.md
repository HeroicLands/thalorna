The **Berav** is [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]]'s principal river and the axis of [[place-ellive|Éllive]]. It rises in the
northwestern uplands and runs southwest across the heartland basin to the western sea, gathering the
tributaries of the whole interior as it goes.

It is navigable a considerable way inland—comfortably past [[place-beravel|Péravel]], which
stands on its banks some fifteen miles up from the coast and takes its name from the water. Sea-going
hulls come up to the capital's quays on the tide; above that the traffic is barge and lighter, working
as far as the country towns of the upper basin some forty miles inland, [[place-ruthelmar|Ruthelmar]] the last of them. The orchard town of [[place-lamanis|Lámanis]] stands a day above the capital. Below Péravel the river braids through the reed beds of [[place-osselen|Ósselen]] to the pilots' village of [[place-sirlathen|Sirlathen]] at its mouth.

## Why It Matters

Every economy in Élavendre ends at the Berav.

Wool and iron out of [[place-auldalon|Áuldalon]] come west down the drove roads to the river. What
little metal and gemstone [[place-sorvanda|Sórvanda]] releases comes down the same way. Timber, salt-fish
and dye out of [[place-lirve|Lirve]] come up the coast and in through the mouth. All of it is
worked in Éllive and goes out again through Péravel, and the tolls on that traffic are the crown's
steadiest revenue.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The river's quarrels are about what comes down it. The crown takes its toll at Péravel on everything that passes, the pilots of [[place-sirlathen|Sirlathen]] take their fee on every hull that comes up, and the market towns of the upper basin sell to the capital's factors at the head of navigation. Sórvanda silver is coming down in larger lots than the tribes have released, and the objects the Order's expeditions carry out of the interior reach the chapterhouses at Péravel by the same barges, entered on no manifest. Below the capital the reed-cutters of [[place-osselen|Ósselen]] keep the channels and are the first to see what is landed at night.

## The Vulnerability

A river that carries a kingdom's trade and passes its capital fifteen miles from open water is also,
inevitably, the kingdom's chief vulnerability. It has never been forced. That it could be is the
standing argument of every Élavendren admiral who has ever wanted money.
