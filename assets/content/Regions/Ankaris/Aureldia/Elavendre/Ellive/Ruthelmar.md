## Overview {#overview}

**Ruthelmar** stands on [[place-beravriver|the Berav]] about forty miles inland, at the last reach where a laden barge can be poled and towed. Some four thousand eight hundred people live there, and on a market day twice that number are in the streets. The name is Élavendri, _ruthon_ "a ford" with _mirlar_ "town": the town grew where the river was crossed on foot before anyone built the bridge.

Everything the upper basin grows and raises comes here to be sold, and everything the high roads bring down is stacked on its quays for the barges. [[place-beravel|Péravel]]'s factors come up by river for the grain and the cattle, and they leave with a season's contracts in their satchels.

## Arriving {#arriving}

Visitors from the capital come up in a barge, a slow two days against the current, and step off at the stone quays below the bridge. Visitors from the hills come down the Gandelis road and cross by the bridge, the last in the basin that takes a loaded cart. A toll-house stands at each end of it, and the tolls are the crown's, collected by the sheriff's clerks in a ledger that has never once been late.

The town has a cattle ground on the east bank, a grain hall on the west, and between them the barge-yards, where the long flat hulls are built and caulked and broken up.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

**Grain and cattle.** The market runs every sixth day and swells at the autumn drove. Prices are cried from the steps of the grain hall, and the cry carries to Péravel faster than the barges do.

**The barges.** The bargemasters' fellowship owns the yards, sets the freight, and decides which of its members tows what. A barge leaves with a pilot who knows the shoals, and no cargo goes down without a tally cut on a stick and split between shipper and master.

**The carters.** Every load that does not go by water goes by the carters' road, and the carters, who are not members of anything, are the town's best source of news and its worst for accuracy.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The crown's sheriff holds the shire court in the grain hall and answers to Péravel for the tolls. Day to day the town keeps its own order through the bargemasters' fellowship, whose members settle freight quarrels at the quay without a magistrate and fine each other in barrels of cider. A theft from a barge is a quarrel of the fellowship. A theft from a carter is nobody's.

## The Present {#present}

A carter is stranded on the east bank with a sealed crate bound downriver to the University at Péravel, and no bargemaster will take it. The crate is warm to the hand on a cold morning. The lamp-oil in the lantern left beside it burned through a night in the carter's hut and stands as high in the flask as it did at dusk. The bargemasters will not say what they are afraid of, and the carter, who was paid in advance and in coin, will not say where the crate came from.

:::secret {title="The crate"}
It holds a billet of heartwood cut from a tree in a slow [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] in the forest above [[place-gandelis|Gandelis]], a place the Wardens have never named to an outsider. A guide of Gandelis, paid by a fellow of the University, went past the margin and cut it. Inside the crate a day is a month. The oil has not burned because the lantern burns in the crate's slow hour, and a person who sleeps against it ages the little it ages at that rate. The carter has slept against it for three nights and thinks he is merely rested.

The sealed schedule that came with it names the Fitter of Expeditions at the University as its receiver. The Wardens know that wood is missing from the forest and have not learned who carried it.
:::

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

**The Tally Stick** on the quay is the bargemasters' house, loud and clean, with a long room where the freight is settled. **The Ford Inn** on the west bank is a carters' house with a cold yard and a warm kitchen. Both will sell a party passage downriver, a mule, or silence at a fair price.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The warm crate.** The carter wants his crate carried to Péravel by someone who will not ask what is in it, and will pay what the bargemasters refuse to be paid.
- **The drove.** The cattle factors of the autumn drove need an escort for a herd going down the Gandelis road, which crosses ground where the Wardens are watching more closely than usual ([[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]]).
