## Overview {#overview}

**Gandelis** stands where the Berav basin's last farmland gives way to the foothill woods, on the edge of the [[place-alndntrblnds|Áelendan Tribal Lands]], at the end of a road the crown built and will not extend. About seventeen hundred people live there. The name is Élavendri, from _gandon_ "a gate", and the gate is real: a stone arch across the road at the town's upper end, with the crown's arms on one face and a plain staff cut into the other.

The town holds a royal charter, _záktela_ in Élavendri, which makes it a free market under the crown and obliges it to keep a gate that the crown's writ does not pass. Past the gate the road is a forest track, and the ground belongs to the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]].

## Arriving {#arriving}

The road from [[place-ruthelmar|Ruthelmar]] climbs for a day through hay meadows and then through the first stands of birch and oak, and the town appears all at once, a huddle of stone and shingle around a square. The gate stands at the top of the square, and a Warden is almost always sitting beneath it, on a bench worn smooth, whittling. He does not stop outsiders. He looks at them, and what he sees is entered in the memory of every Warden up the valley.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

**Outfitting.** Gandelis sells what a party needs for the forest: hobnailed boots, oiled cloaks, rope, salt, lamp-oil, and mules for as far as the mules will go. The outfitters know exactly which equipment keeps people alive, and which merely makes them feel safe.

**Hay and wool,** from the meadows below, sold to the Péravel factors in the autumn.

**Guides and guards.** Contracts for hired guards are written in Péravel, and companies come up the Ruthelmar road already hired. The town houses them for a night or a week, according to the weather and the scholar's patience. A good share of its inns' income comes from the companies of the University and the Order, and it is not a share anyone in the town speaks of at the gate.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The crown's charter-reeve, [Orlen Tárvenel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, holds the gate-house, collects the market dues and keeps the peace within the walls. A quarrel inside the gate goes to him, and a quarrel outside it goes to nobody: it is the Wardens' ground, and the Wardens' judgment is final. The town's magistrates take a dim public view of the companies that cross the line, and a pragmatic private one.

## The Present {#present}

An Order scholar and eight hired guards are in the largest inn, and they leave at first light. The Warden under the gate has watched them lodge, and has said to the reeve, in four words, that he would like to speak to the scholar. The scholar has declined. The guards, who were told in Péravel that the Wardens "warn once," have begun to ask each other whether that has already been done.

:::secret {title="The reeve's second ledger"}
Orlen Tárvenel is paid by the Fitter of Expeditions at the University for the name of every company he outfits, the route it asks about and the day it leaves. He tells himself that the money keeps the Order content with a town that would otherwise be refused, and that the Wardens already know where everyone goes. The Wardens do. They have not told him that they know he sells it, and they let him go on, because a ledger in the Order's hands tells them how much the Order knows.

The scholar in the inn carries a map that is better than it should be.
:::

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

**The Gate Inn** is the largest house in the square, expensive and loud, with a long table for companies. **The Hay Barn** is a loft inn behind the outfitters, cheap and cold, favored by guides. The reeve keeps rooms in the gate-house for anyone he wants to watch.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The last warning.** The Warden under the gate asks a party to carry a word to the scholar that he will not accept from a Warden: that the way he means to go is not a path.
- **The hired guards.** One of the eight guards, a woman from the south, wants to know what she has been hired to do before she crosses the line, and will pay a party to find out ([[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]]).
- **The cutter.** A guide of the town has not been seen since the autumn, and the Wardens are asking after him. His wife says he left for Péravel with a sealed crate and a purse.
