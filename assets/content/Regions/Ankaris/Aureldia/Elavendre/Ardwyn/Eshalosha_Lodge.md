**Controlled by:** [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]]

## Overview

**Eshálosha Lodge** is the principal settlement of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]]—a ceremonial, political, and residential center tucked into a sheltered valley within the [[place-drenavrspn|Drenavar Spine]]. It is not a city in the Élavendri sense, nor a fortress in the lowland sense. It is something older and harder to describe: a permanent gathering place that has been continuously inhabited and maintained since the earliest Pelwar settlement of the interior, and that serves as the center of Áelendan collective life.

## Character

Eshálosha is built in harmony with its mountain setting rather than in opposition to it. The Lodge's structures—long timber halls, stone-walled councils, residential compounds, and the great central ceremonial hall—follow the contours of the valley floor and the surrounding slopes. Roofs are steep and moss-laid; walls are built of local stone and shaped timber; paths are stone and packed earth, kept to the natural flow of the land. Gardens and orchards occupy the terraced slopes above the main compounds, and the valley's streams feed both the communal water supply and a series of sacred pools that figure in Áelendan ritual.

The Lodge does not fortify itself against outside attack. Its defense is the mountains themselves, the Wardens who patrol the approaches, and the long-standing understanding that the lowland kingdom will not permit anyone to assault sacred territory. This arrangement has held for as long as the kingdom has existed.

## Function

Eshálosha serves several interrelated functions for the Áelendan:

- **Council seat**—The periodic great councils of Áelendan elders convene at Eshálosha. These are not permanent governing bodies; the Áelendan have no centralized government. The councils meet to decide matters that affect multiple tribes, to resolve disputes that local elders cannot settle, and to coordinate responses to outside pressures (of which the expansion of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] is the most significant current example).
- **Ceremonial center**—The great seasonal rituals of the Kindred Pact are held at Eshálosha, drawing Áelendan from across the Drenavar Spine and the deeper forests. The ceremonial hall is the only structure in the Lodge large enough to hold the full gathered community, and it is used for little else.
- **Tradition-keeping**—The senior tradition-keepers—the memorizers who hold the Kindred Pact, the genealogies, and the ancient songs—gather at Eshálosha to teach, to consult, and to preserve the continuity of the oral tradition. The Lodge is effectively the living archive of Áelendan culture.
- **Warden coordination**—The senior [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] meet at Eshálosha to coordinate patrols across tribal territories, to share intelligence about intrusions, and to train new generations of Wardens. The Lodge is not the Wardens' headquarters in any formal sense, but it is the place where their leadership converges.
- **Hospitality to invited outsiders**—On rare occasions, the Áelendan extend invitations to outsiders—scholars of high reputation, envoys from the Sinalë, or persons with some specific claim on Áelendan trust. Such guests are received at Eshálosha, under protocols that the Áelendan set.

## Access

Eshálosha is not accessible to casual travelers. The approaches are patrolled by Wardens, and no outsider reaches the Lodge without Áelendan invitation and escort. The last recorded unsanctioned attempt to approach the Lodge—a small Ordo Arcanis survey expedition some decades ago—is said to have ended at the outer ridge, though no official account of its fate has been published.

Visitors who are invited are guided through the mountain passes by Warden escorts and received at the Lodge under protocols of hospitality that are both generous and exacting. Guests are expected to observe the Kindred Pact's basic requirements—no weapons in the ceremonial areas, no writing in certain rooms, no approach to particular sites—and violations, even inadvertent, terminate the visit and are not forgiven.

## The Lodge and the Crown

No Élavendren monarch has ever set foot in Eshálosha without Áelendan invitation, and only a small number of monarchs have ever received such an invitation. Those who have are remembered with particular honor in Áelendan song. The crown recognizes the Lodge as the political seat of the Áelendan for purposes of the limited diplomatic intercourse that the two peoples conduct, and royal envoys who need to communicate with the Áelendan send their messages to Eshálosha through agreed channels.
