## Overview

The **Drenavar Spine** is the central sacred mountain range of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], lying in the interior of the kingdom beyond the settled farmlands and accessible foothills. It is the heart of [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] territory—the land in which the Kindred Pact is most deeply rooted and in which the greatest concentration of sacred sites is found. The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] maintain their heaviest presence here, and the crown of Élavendre recognizes Áelendan sovereignty over the mountains by ancient law.

The Drenavar is part of a larger Áelendan sacred mountain country that extends east into the **[[place-vylariargn#the-cervaron-spine|Cervaron Spine]]**—the contested Vylarian frontier range—and south into the **[[place-alndntrblnds#the-tarvenan-diaspora|Toravar Range]]** that runs along the northern Tarvénan border. To Áelendan reckoning these are not three separate ranges but a single continuous sacred uplift, the high spine of the western world. The Drenavar is the heart; the Cervaron and Toravar are its eastern and southern arms. Outsiders distinguish them; the Áelendan distinguish them only when speaking to outsiders.

## Geography

The Drenavar rises steeply from the inland valleys of Élavendre, its peaks perpetually shrouded in mist and often capped in snow. The slopes support dense old-growth forest at lower elevations, yielding to alpine meadows and bare rock as altitude increases. The mountains are cut through by glacial valleys and clear lakes, and countless streams run down to feed the rivers of the lowlands. Passes through the range are few, often narrow, and—to outsiders attempting to cross without Áelendan escort—notoriously difficult to navigate. Travelers report that paths which appeared obvious on one day have shifted or vanished by the next, a phenomenon that the Áelendan do not bother to explain.

The mountains are home to unusual concentrations of wildlife—some common to Ankaris, some rare, and some that appear to exist only in these ranges and the deep forests that ring them. Among the Áelendan, it is understood that not all of the creatures encountered in the mountains are ordinary beasts.

## Sacred Sites

The Drenavar Spine contains many of the most significant sacred sites of the Áelendan tradition. The specific locations and protocols of these sites are not published, and outsiders are not permitted to seek them. What is known publicly is that the sites include:

- **Ancient grove-shrines**—Old-growth groves that the Áelendan regard as places where the Kindred Pact is most fully present. Some of these groves are believed to predate the Pelwar settlement by uncertain duration.
- **Mountain sanctuaries**—High-elevation sites on the peaks and ridges, often associated with particular fae beings or with specific Kindred Pact pledges.
- **Sacred springs and lakes**—Water sources held to be especially sacred, around which certain Áelendan rituals are performed.

The [[place-silvrfrsts|Silver Forests]] lie at the edge of the Drenavar range, in a transition zone where the mountains descend into the deepest forests of the interior.

## [[place-eshalshldg|Eshálosha Lodge]]

[[place-eshalshldg|Eshálosha Lodge]]—the principal settlement of the Áelendan and the center of their political and ceremonial life—is located in a sheltered valley within the Drenavar range. The Lodge is the seat of the tribal councils and the traditional gathering place for seasonal ceremonies.

## Access and Restrictions

Access to the Drenavar is governed by Áelendan custom and enforced by the Wardens. The lower approaches—the foothills and the outer valleys—are open to lowland travelers who behave respectfully, and Áelendan communities at the edge of their territory engage in limited trade and occasional hospitality with visitors. The deeper mountains are sacred territory, and entry requires Áelendan invitation or clear sanction from a tribal elder.

Trespassers who venture into sacred territory are warned once and escorted out. Those who persist, who return uninvited, or who desecrate a site are dealt with according to the Wardens' graduated doctrine of response (see [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]]). In recent decades, [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] agents have increasingly attempted to conduct clandestine research in the Drenavar range, with consequences that have become the subject of considerable private frustration within the Ordo.

## Relations with the Crown

The crown of Élavendre has recognized Áelendan sovereignty over the Drenavar Spine for as long as the kingdom has existed, and the recognition is not ceremonial. Royal magistrates will not intervene in matters that fall within Áelendan territory; royal troops will not enter the mountains without explicit Áelendan sanction; and royal taxation does not extend to the mountain communities. In exchange, the Áelendan accept the legitimacy of the crown in the lowlands and have, on a small number of historic occasions, contributed warriors to defend Élavendre against existential threats.
