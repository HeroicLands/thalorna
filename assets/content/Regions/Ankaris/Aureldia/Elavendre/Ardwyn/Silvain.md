**Silvain** lies inside the **[[place-silvrfrsts|Silver Forests]]** of [[place-ardwyn|Ardwyn]], which
makes it unusual: a settled, permanent, largely Élavendri community of some six hundred souls, standing
in country where such things are not normally permitted.

## On Sufferance

Silvain exists by an arrangement with the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] whose terms are known to
everyone in general and to nobody in particular. There is no charter. There is no grant recorded at
[[place-beravel|Béravel]]. There is a settlement, several centuries old, that the
[[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] pass through without comment.

What the villagers give in return is a matter of quiet local certainty and no evidence. The usual
account is that Silvain keeps something—a watch, an observance, a duty at the
[[place-ataanwyrd|At'aan Wyrd]]—and that the arrangement lapses the day it stops. Villagers asked
directly will say that they live there, which is true, and change the subject.

## Life

Charcoal, honey, hazel and withy, and the gathering of the herbs that the physicians of Béravel will
pay absurd prices for. No felling of standing silver birch, ever, by anyone, for any reason.

The village keeps the old festivals rather than the Aurèldían calendar, and outsiders who arrive for
one are made welcome and watched.
