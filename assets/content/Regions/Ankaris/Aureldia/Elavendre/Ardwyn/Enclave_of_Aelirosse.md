The **Enclave of Aelirossë** is the Sinalë community of [[place-aelirosse|Aelirossë]], in the [[place-silvrfrsts|Silver Forests]] of [[place-ardwyn|Ardwyn]]. Sinalë have kings, queens and lords, but these do not work as human ones do, and the enclave is governed by its lord. It is neither the crown's nor the Wardens': its relationship with the surrounding [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] is cordial, not an alliance. [[lore-flksinale|Sinalë]] grow and craft their dwellings rather than build them in stone.
