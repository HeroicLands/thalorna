**Ardwyn**—_the high land_—is the mountain quarter of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], running along
the kingdom's eastern and southern borders: perhaps a hundred and eighty thousand people in a country
that could hold far more and does not, because most of it is not the kingdom's to settle.

Mist-shrouded peaks, glacial valleys with lakes in them, high pasture, and the deep old woods below the
tree line. The **[[place-drenavrspn|Drenavar Spine]]** is its central range and its sacred heart.

## Áelendan Country

Ardwyn is where the **[[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]]** live—the ancestor people of the Élavendri, who
never came down into the cities and who keep the Old Way in tribal communities under their own elders.
The **[[place-alndntrblnds|Áelendan Tribal Lands]]** within Ardwyn are sovereign in practice and
protected by crown law in principle: the courts of Élavendre will act against anyone who encroaches, and
generally do not have to, because the **[[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]]** get there first.

This is the densest concentration of fae sites in Ankaris. The [[lore-aralwen|arálwain]], the sacred
groves, the seated spirits—the [[place-ataanwyrd|At'aan Wyrd]] in the
[[place-silvrfrsts|Silver Forests]], the river-mother **[[lore-aranwyspr|Aranwy]]** at the [[place-longstair|Long Stair]], the owl-spirit of the [[place-owlhollow|Owl Hollow]]—are all here,
and all watched.

## The Wealth That Cannot Be Taken

Ardwyn's mountains are **rich in minerals**, and unusually so: not merely iron and copper but silver,
gold, and gemstones in genuine abundance. On any ordinary reading Élavendre ought to be the wealthiest
kingdom in western Ankaris.

It is not, because the ore lies under Áelendan ground and the Áelendan do not permit mining on it. The
prohibition is not negotiable, it is not for sale, and four centuries of Élavendren crowns have tested
it exactly far enough to establish that it is real.

What extraction exists is therefore:

- **Marginal**—worked at the fringes, on ground the tribes have not claimed or have released by
  specific arrangement, usually in exchange for something that took a generation to negotiate.
- **Dangerous**—an unlicensed working that the Wardens find does not continue, and its people do not
  always come back down.
- **Ruinously expensive**—which is why Élavendren gold and gemwork are worth what they are. The
  scarcity is not geological. It is political, and everyone in the trade knows it.

Prospectors, speculators and agents of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] come anyway, in
numbers, every year—and the Ordo's people are the least welcome of the three. The Áelendan hold the
Ordo in flat contempt as meddlesome intruders who come to study what the tribes exist to keep, and a
Warden who finds a licensed surveyor above the line treats the license as an aggravation rather than a
defense.

## The Rest of the Economy

- **High pasture**—cattle, sheep, and the mountain cheeses that come down at the autumn fairs.
- **Furs and hides** from the tree line.
- **Herbs and simples**, gathered by Áelendan and traded down; the physicians of Béravel will pay for
  what only grows above the passes.
- **Timber**, sparingly and by arrangement.
