## Overview {#overview}

**The Green Bark** is a strip of bark as long as a forearm, cut from one of the trees of [[place-wkngstnd|the Waking Stand]] deep in [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]] beyond [[place-sourimael|Sóurimel]]. The trees of the Stand are not birch and are not still. Cut bark dries and curls in a season, and this strip has not dried.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] holds it. It lies on a shelf of the Fisera herbarium at the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]], and the catalog of the Keeper of the Collections, [[being-netharahalre|Néthara Halren]], names the strip and its shelf and not the hand that brought it in.

## Its Story {#its-story}

A poacher cut it in the autumn, carried it out of the forests without stopping and sold it in [[place-lisere|Lísere]], and from there it came to the University. Nobody at the University says who sold it to them. The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] of the Silver Forests know that bark was taken from the Stand, and they have asked Sóurimel, twice and courteously, who passed through that autumn.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

It stays green, and it is warm to the hand, as the bark of the Stand is warm. The Order has licensed a fellow of the University to go back to the Stand for a living cutting, because the strip proves that the Stand's wood lives apart from the tree.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

The Wardens want it returned to the Stand, and they want the man who cut it. The Order wants a living cutting to set beside it. The Grove Council has petitioned the court at Péravel about objects taken from groves, and the strip is one of the things the petition means.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The strip on the shelf.** A grove-elder asks a party to find out which shelf of the Fisera herbarium holds the Green Bark, and whether it can leave the University without the Keeper's catalog noticing.
- **The living cutting.** The Order fellow licensed to go back to the Stand is hiring guards in Péravel, and a Warden of the Silver Forests is waiting for him at the margin.
