**Brynavar** – _the hill country_ – is Élavendre's eastern march: some two hundred and forty thousand
people in rolling wooded hills between the [[place-tirwen|Tirwen]] basin and the borders beyond, rising
southward into the mountains of [[place-ardwyn|Ardwyn]].

It is good country and hard country at once. The hills are green, well watered and excellent for stock,
and they are also the road by which trouble arrives overland.

## The Raiding

Where [[place-cilfor|Cilfor]] is raided from the sea, Brynavar is raided by land. Bands of
**[[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]]** out of [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukarholm]], and Varokh war-parties ranging
south out of [[place-vrystwald|Vrystwald]], come down through the eastern hills – not as armies, and
not seasonally enough to plan around comfortably, but often enough that no generation of Brynavar has
been without it.

So the region is built for it. Towns are walled or ditched; steadings cluster; stock is grazed within a
day's drive of somewhere defensible; and the hill levy is the most experienced body of fighting men in
Élavendre and knows it. Brynavar's opinion of the court at Béravel is that it is charming, cultured, and
has never once arrived in time.

## The Economy

- **Wool and cattle**, the region's staple and its bulk export. The great drove roads run west from the
  hills down to the Tirwen markets, and the droving families are a power of their own.
- **Hill ponies** – small, sound and famously bad-tempered; Élavendre's baggage and border-patrol
  animal, sold across Aurèldía.
- **Iron.** Brynavar has no gold and no gems, and unlike [[place-ardwyn|Ardwyn]] it is permitted to dig.
  Its iron is ordinary, plentiful and entirely uncontested, and it arms the kingdom.
- **Charcoal and timber** off the lower slopes, feeding the bloomeries.
- **Hides, horn and tallow** – the unglamorous half of a stock economy, and steady money.

The hills also take a quiet cut of the trade that crosses them: pack-trains from the east pay for
escort whether or not they wanted one.
