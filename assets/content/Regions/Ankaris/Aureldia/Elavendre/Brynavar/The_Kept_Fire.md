Where the [[place-brynavar|Brynavar]] hills climb toward the mountains and the oak gives way to birch, a fold of ground holds a hill with a door in it. The door is fitted stone, cut square in the manner of the Vylarian legions, and behind it a hall is lit by a fire that has burned since the war at Valdûn. The hill-drovers name it **the Kept Fire**, in [[skill-elvndrlng|Élavendri]] _Káralson_, the hearth of a hall. They drive their flocks round it by a half-day's detour and do not say why.

## The Cohort

When the Vylarian legions came north-west into the hills and [[lore-elvtribute|the war at Valdûn]] was fought, a cohort that followed a path at the forest margin was not seen again. The hill levy tells it as a victory of the ground. The cohort's tribune, **Sôlkimôs**, led it on a winter night into the fold, and found a hall with a lit door.

He did not ask. A Vylarian tribune with frostbitten men at his back and a legion's name behind him demanded shelter for the Sovereign's soldiers. The one within, whom the Áelendan name _dáimela_, the Giver, answered in the manner of its kind: exactly. It gave a hall, a fire and bread. The tribune swore that his cohort would hold the place against all comers for as long as the Sovereign's name stood, and the Giver took him at his word.

The cohort holds it still. Its men are not dead and not of the Shadow. They are kept: they do not age, they do not sleep, they eat the hall's bread and drink its wine, and they cannot leave. The legion's name still stands.

## Guest-Right

The hall's rule is guest-right, and it runs both ways. A stranger at the door is received and fed, and anyone who eats or drinks at the fire is counted a guest of the hall. A guest of the Kept Fire owes the hall its watch. The roll is called each night, and the names called are those who ate.

The tribune enforces the rule as a soldier does: with courtesy, with force when needed, and with the plain conviction that the order he gave is still the order. He is a hard man who has held a door for centuries, and nobody has asked him once whether he wants to put it down.

## Who Knows, and What They Say

- **The drovers** of the southern hills say the hall is full of old soldiers and that you do not accept bread from them. The older ones add that they are the ones who stayed.
- **The Colleges** keep a bard's lament of a cohort lost in the forest margin, and the last verse is sung only to a bardic student who has asked to hear it.
- **The legate's house at Béravel** keeps a muster-roll showing the cohort as deserted, its banner unaccounted for and its pay-chest lost with it. A clerk there keeps the file open for reasons nobody cares to explain.
- **The Áelendan** call it _Dáimela's door_. They do not enter, and they do not forbid anyone else to.

## Reaching It

The fold lies a day's walk south of the last Brynavar drove road, where the ponies refuse the hill. The path to the door is stone, set in the Vylarian way, and it is swept. The sentry keeps the approach.

## Hooks

- **The muster-roll.** A clerk of the legate's house at Béravel wants proof the cohort held, not deserted. It is worth money to a family that carries the cohort's name.
- **A drover's brother.** A boy followed a lamp into the fold and has been counted at the door for three years.
- **The banner.** A Vylarian collector pays for a cohort's standard. A Bardic College pays for the lament's last verse.
