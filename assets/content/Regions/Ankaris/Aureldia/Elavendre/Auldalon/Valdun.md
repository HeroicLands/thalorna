**Valdun** stands in the eastern hills of [[place-auldalon|Áuldalon]], on the ridge road where the
drove ways out of the hill country come together before running west to the
[[place-ellive|Éllive]] markets. Five thousand two hundred people, a wall that has been rebuilt three
times, and the most experienced garrison in the kingdom.

## The Anchor

Valdun is what a border town looks like when the border has never been quiet. The wall encloses the
market as well as the houses; the stock pens are inside it; the gates are shut at dusk as a matter of
routine rather than alarm; and every outlying steading within a day's drive knows which road it runs
to.

The **hill levy** musters here under the Hill-Captain of Valdun ([[affiliation-brdrmltia|the Border Militia]]). It is not a noble force and does not pretend to be—drovers,
foresters, smiths and their sons, mounted on Áuldalon ponies, who have done this before and expect to
do it again. Its officers are local men. Its opinion of the court's cavalry is unprintable, and its
record against Varokh war-parties and [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] bands is better.

## The Market

Between raids, Valdun is a prosperous stock town. The great drove fairs bring cattle and wool down out
of the hills; the iron of the eastern bloomeries is traded here; and the pack-trains that cross the
march from the east pay their escort money in Valdun's yards, to men who will provide the escort and
who would in any case have been the ones to worry about.

## History {#history}

[[place-trbtrd|The road]] that reaches Valdun from the east is marked with imperial milestones, and the milestones stop at the gate. They were set when the legions marched into the hills and the queen treated with the _felzos_ (legate) in the town ([[lore-elvtribute|The Tribute of Élavendre]]), and the Empire laid no road beyond the treaty town. The town's first wall was raised in that year, and it has been rebuilt three times since.

Valdun has been the march's muster ever since. The hill levy that harried the legion into the forest margin mustered under its walls, the sworn company of [[affiliation-brdrmltia|the Border Militia]] was posted here when the crown found that a levy alone could not be raised in time, and the drove fairs grew up in the yards the soldiers left empty between raids.

## Current Situation {#present}

Valdun is short of men and long on opinions. [[being-elenorthaurn|Captain Élenor Thaurnde]] keeps the crown's sworn company in the town and wants the hill levy brought into one plan with it. [[being-jeldormorlin|Jeldor Mórlineth]], the Hill-Captain of Valdun, wants the levy led by men who know the ground, and the militia and the levy share the walls without sharing a plan. The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] hires guards for the interior in the town's inns, and the men who come back hurt from beyond the tree line bring their complaints to the royal court, where the magistrate [[being-ilseviletirv|Ílsevile Tírvalnde]] asks each of them first whether he was warned. [[being-vargenhalmes|Lord Vargen Hálmesende]], who keeps a house in the town, says in company that the tribes' ground should be opened to the crown's roads.

A hill hunter has brought in a helmet of an imperial pattern that has not rusted, and the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]'s antiquarians want to know where it was found.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The helmet.** The hunter will sell the helmet and nothing else, and the Order's antiquarians want his road to the woods.
- **One plan.** Captain Élenor asks the party to ride with the hill levy for a muster and tell her plainly how Jeldor's men fight.
- **A complaint the court does not hear.** A hired guard back from the interior with a Warden's arrow-wound wants a party to swear in Ílsevile's court that he was never warned.
