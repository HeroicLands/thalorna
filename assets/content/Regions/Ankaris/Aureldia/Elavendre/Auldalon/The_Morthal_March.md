## Overview {#overview}

The **Morthal March** is the crown's frontier at the head of [[place-liserebay|Lísere Bay]]. It lies east of [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]], where the low shingle shore of [[place-lisere|Lísere]] gives way to the northern hills of [[place-auldalon|Áuldalon]], and it carries the one road by which a traveler on foot gets from [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]] into Élavendre without crossing the water. The [[affiliation-morthalwtch|Morthal Watch]] holds the road under the Warden of Morthal, and the Watch is the crown's and nobody else's.

The name is Élavendri and the Watch's people use it for the whole strip, shore and hill together. Outside the March the road is called the bay road.

## The Land {#the-land}

The shore is a belt of salt meadow and bog behind a bar of shingle, cut by a few streams that run brown from the hills. Behind it the ground rises in wooded ridges, thick with alder and oak at the foot and pine higher up, and the ridges climb toward the northern edge of [[place-sorvanda|Sórvanda]], where the country is Áelendan and the law is the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]]'.

The road keeps to the firm ground between the bog and the first ridge. At one place a mile of it is a causeway of logs, and everything that comes round the bay on foot goes over the causeway in single file. The Watch keeps its posts where the road narrows.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The Watch holds the road, from the edge of Sarmelis's country to the first of the hill steadings. It counts what crosses, turns back what is armed and unannounced, and sends word along the [[affiliation-beaconfamlys|Beacon Families]]' chain and by rider when a war-party shows. The crown's sheriff at Sarmelis holds the crown's pleas in the March as he does on the shore, and the Watch does not try a case.

The heights above the road belong to the Wardens. The Watch may follow a raider uphill as far as the tree line and no farther, because the crown's oath stops there ([[lore-firstcrown|The First Crowning of Élavendre]]). Past the line the Wardens deal with whatever has come, in their own way and on their own count, and they do not report it.

## Current Situation {#present}

The March has two quarrels, and they meet at the tree line. The Watch's riders chase Varokh and Grukar raiders up the ridges and stop at the line with the raiders in sight, and the Warden of Morthal has put to the crown's Liaison to the Tribes that the Wardens should be asked to close the heights behind a raid. The Wardens answer that the heights are closed already and have been for as long as there have been Wardens.

The second quarrel is quieter. [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] agents who want the interior and have been guided to nothing at [[place-liranel|Liranel]] come to the March instead, because the Watch looks at the road and the hills above it are not looked at by anyone the crown pays. Three parties have been turned back at the line this season, and a fourth has not been found.

## Roads and Trade {#roads-and-trade}

The bay road is slower than the water and safer in a gale. Varokh who would rather trade than raid bring furs round the head of the bay on pack ponies, and the Watch lets them through at the tally-post for a toll and a count, which is the same arrangement the furs have by boat at [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]]. In the hill steadings the Watch's riders are the only law that comes up the valley more than once a year, and the steadings feed them for it.

## Dangers {#dangers}

**Raiders.** A war-party that comes round the bay arrives on foot, and the causeway is the place to stop it. A war-party that has been refused at the causeway goes up into the ridges and comes down on a steading three valleys away.

**The line.** A Watchman who follows a raider past the tree line is not rescued by the crown. The Wardens turn a man back once, and the Watch's serjeants say so to every recruit.

**The bog.** The salt meadow is firm in summer and takes a pony to the girth in the thaw.

## History {#history}

The road is older than the Watch. It was the way the earliest Varokh bands came into the hills, and the first posts on it were the hill steadings' own, kept by the families whose stock was taken. The crown took the posts into a single Watch after the Nordmen fleets burned the north shore ([[lore-northshorefire|The Burning of the North Shore]]), when it set stone at the narrows and a chain of lamps along the coast and found that a coast held on one side was entered on the other.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The causeway toll.** A Varokh headman with forty ponies of furs asks the party to vouch for him at the tally-post, because the Watch's serjeant knows his face from a raid.
- **The fourth party.** An Order scholar's clerk comes down off the ridges alone, without the rest of his party and without his notes, and asks the Watch to be taken to Sarmelis before dark.
- **The line.** A Rider of the Watch followed a raider past the tree line two nights ago and has not come back, and the Warden of Morthal wants someone who is not the crown's to ask the Wardens.
