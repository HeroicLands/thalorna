Where the [[place-auldalon|Áuldalon]] hills climb toward the mountains and the oak gives way to birch, a fold of ground holds a hill with a door in it. The door is fitted stone, cut square in the manner of the Vilérian legions, and behind it a hall is lit by a fire that has burned since the war at [[place-valdun|Valdun]]. The hill-drovers name it **the Kept Fire**, in [[skill-elvndrlng|Élavendri]] _Káralson_, the hearth of a hall. They drive their flocks round it by a half-day's detour and do not say why.

## Layers {#history}

When the Vilérian legions came north-west into the hills and the war at Valdun recounted in [[lore-elvtribute|the Tribute of Élavendre]] was fought, a cohort that followed a path at the forest margin was not seen again. The hill levy tells it as a victory of the ground. Hunters find the cohort's last ditched camp in the woods and cannot find it twice ([[place-auldalon|Áuldalon]]), and the drovers say the cohort went on from the camp to the hill, and that the men in the hall are the ones who stayed.

:::secret {title="The truth of the Kept Fire"}
The cohort's _Lusar_, [[being-solkimoslsr|Solkimos]], led it on a winter night into the fold, and found a hall with a lit door.

He did not ask. A Vilérian _Lusar_ with frostbitten men at his back and a legion's name behind him demanded shelter for the Sovereign's soldiers. The one within, whom the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] name _[Dáimela]{.name data-type="lore/spirit"}_, the Giver, answered in the manner of its kind: exactly. It gave a hall, a fire and bread. The _Lusar_ swore that his cohort would hold the place against all comers for as long as the Sovereign's name stood, and the Giver took him at his word.

The cohort holds it still. Its men are not dead and not of [[lore-theshadow|the Shadow]]. They are kept: they do not age, they do not sleep, they eat the hall's bread and drink its wine, and they cannot leave. The legion's name still stands.
:::

## Guest-Right

A stranger who comes to the door is received and offered bread and wine at the fire. The drovers' rule is never to accept it, and the people who have accepted are the ones who do not come home.

:::secret
The hall's rule is guest-right, and it runs both ways. Anyone who eats or drinks at the fire is counted a guest of the hall, and a guest of the Kept Fire owes the hall its watch. The roll is called each night, and the names called are those who ate.

The _Lusar_ enforces the rule as a soldier does: with courtesy, with force when needed, and with the plain conviction that the order he gave is still the order. He is a hard man who has held a door for centuries, and nobody has asked him once whether he wants to put it down.
:::

## What Is There Now {#present}

The door is kept, and the hills are uneasy about it this year. A hunter has brought an imperial helmet that has not rusted into [[place-valdun|Valdun]], and the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]]'s antiquarians in the town are paying for the place he found it. A drover's boy who followed a lamp into the fold three winters ago has not come home, and his kin say he is counted at the door each night.

- **The drovers** of the southern hills say the hall is full of old soldiers and that you do not accept bread from them. The older ones add that they are the ones who stayed.
- **[[affiliation-bardicolgs|The Colleges]]** keep a bard's lament of a cohort lost in the forest margin, and the last verse is sung only to a bardic student who has asked to hear it.
- **The felzos's house at [[place-beravel|Péravel]]** keeps a muster-roll showing the cohort as deserted, its banner unaccounted for and its pay-chest lost with it. A clerk there keeps the file open for reasons nobody cares to explain.
- **The Áelendan** call it _Dáimela's door_. They do not enter, and they do not forbid anyone else to.

## Reaching It

The fold lies a day's walk south of the last Áuldalon drove road, where the ponies refuse the hill. The path to the door is stone, set in the Vilérian way, and it is swept. The sentry keeps the approach.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The muster-roll.** A clerk of the _felzos_'s house at Péravel wants proof the cohort held, not deserted. It is worth money to a family that carries the cohort's name.
- **A drover's brother.** A boy followed a lamp into the fold and has been counted at the door for three years.
- **The banner.** A Vilérian collector pays for a cohort's standard, and [[miscgear-keptstandard|the Kept cohort's]] hangs in the hall. A Bardic College pays for the lament's last verse.
