## Overview {#overview}

The **Bardic Colleges** of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] are institutions of learning that combine musical training, oral history, magical study, and diplomacy. They are among the most prestigious centers of education in [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión]], and Élavendren bards—graduates of the Colleges—serve as ambassadors, historians, and, on occasion, spies across the western kingdoms.

The Colleges trace their origins to the earliest Pelwar bardic traditions carried into Élavendre by the ancestors of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]]. The institutional form is more recent—the named Colleges with their curricula, their masters, and their formal degrees date mostly to the early centuries of the Élavendren kingdom—but the substance of what they teach is ancient. A College bard trained in the most conservative tradition can recite genealogies and sagas whose roots lie in Proto-Pelwar oral poetry.

## What the Colleges Teach {#what-it-controls}

The Bardic curriculum is famously broad. A fully trained bard is expected to be competent in:

Music
: Composition, performance, improvisation, and mastery of multiple instruments. The harp is the principal bardic instrument, but the Colleges train bards on strings, winds, and percussion as the material demands.

Oral poetry and storytelling
: The preservation, composition, and performance of the long poetic forms that structure Élavendren cultural memory. Bards are expected to carry epics of many hours' length in memory and to perform them on demand.

Genealogy and history
: The noble houses of Élavendre set great store by accurate lineage, and the Colleges are the kingdom's definitive authority on the question of who is descended from whom ([[lore-sorvandaclose|The Sórvanda Closing]]).

Languages
: All Pelwar descendants are taught to some degree, along with the principal Ankarian trade languages. The most learned bards also study [[skill-aelendlng|Áelendi]] and, in rare cases, fragments of [[skill-senalilng|Senalian]].

Bardic magic
: The old magical traditions of Élavendre, distinct from the institutional arcana of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]. These include song-binding, memory-shaping, and various forms of inspiration and dispiriting magic that operate through voice and narrative. The Colleges do not teach these openly to outsiders, and their exact mechanisms are closely held.

Diplomacy and statecraft
: Many bards serve as envoys and advisors to noble houses and foreign courts. The Colleges train their students in protocol, persuasion, and the particular arts of gathering information without seeming to do so.

## The Colleges

Four Colleges teach the whole curriculum, each stressing one part of it, and each keeps its own library, its own archive of songs and genealogies and its own sayings. A bard is a bard of every College in the Circle's eyes, and a student who changes College keeps his degree.

[[affiliation-clgline|The College of the Line]]
: At [[place-beravel|Péravel]]. Genealogy, history and the proofs of descent; it holds the crown's proof and sings the Lay of the Tree Line at every crowning.

[[affiliation-clgtongues|The College of Tongues]]
: At [[place-liranel|Liranel]]. Áelendi, the carved rune-row and the fragments of Senalian; it licenses the interpreters who go up the valleys.

[[affiliation-clgenvoys|The Envoys' College]]
: At [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]]. Diplomacy, protocol and gathering information without seeming to; it sends envoys to houses and foreign courts and takes Order patronage.

[[affiliation-clgwoodsng|The College of the Wood-Song]]
: At [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]]. The old magic of voice and story, closely held; its older bards go up in the thaw to hear what the Áelendan will sing.

## Ranks {#ranks}

A **Student** learns in one College. A **Bard** is a graduate with a formal degree, fit to serve as envoy, historian or advisor to a house or a court. A **Bard of the Circle** is one of the most accomplished working bards, seated in the Circle that guides the Colleges. A student or bard whom a College strikes from its roll is **Cast Out**, and no College teaches or hears him afterward.

## The Bardic Circle {#masters}

The senior leadership of the Colleges—the masters, the archivists, and the most accomplished working bards—form a body known as the **Bardic Circle**. The Circle is one of the three traditional Élavendren institutions that together have shaped the kingdom's magical, cultural, and political life since before the monarchy was founded, the other two being **[[affiliation-groveconcl|the Grove Council]]** (the religious authority of the fae traditions) and the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] (whose sovereign authority over sacred territory predates all other institutions). The Circle chooses a new royal house when a line of kings ends, as it chose the house of [[affiliation-hsekirethel|Kírethel]] when the line of Orethan ran out.

The Circle is presently one of the primary organized forces resisting the expansion of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] influence in Élavendre. The Circle regards the Arcane Order's attempts to catalog and bureaucratize magic as fundamentally incompatible with the bardic tradition's understanding of magic as something that lives in voice, story, and relationship. In the cities, this resistance takes the form of civilized rivalry—competing chairs at court, contested pupils, reciprocal exclusion from certain venues. Beyond the cities, where the Circle's interests align with the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]], the resistance turns considerably less polite.

## The Present Quarrel {#present}

The Circle is not of one mind about the Order. The Envoys' College takes the Order's patronage and the other three refuse it, and the Colleges that refuse have not decided what to do about the one that does. The College of the Line has the heir's line to sing at the Elanmere fair, the College of Tongues has pupils on the road to the margin with Order parties, and the College of the Wood-Song has a petition before the [[affiliation-groveconcl|Grove Council]] that the Council has not ruled on. The Circle meets on all three, and none is settled.

## Relationship with the Crown

The Bardic Colleges operate with considerable institutional independence, but they are deeply woven into Élavendren political life. The crown consults the Circle on matters of historical precedent, diplomatic protocol, and questions of lineage; the Colleges in turn rely on royal patronage to sustain their libraries and their teaching. The relationship has had its tensions over the centuries—bards have been known to speak inconvenient truths in verse that no courtier would say in prose—but it has endured.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The Circle's split.** A Bard of the Circle from one of the refusing Colleges asks a party to learn what the Envoys' College was paid, because the Circle decides on it at its next sitting.
- **A student who changed College.** A student of the Line has gone to the Wood-Song, taking what he learned of the proofs, and the Archivist wants to know what he has told.
- **Cast Out.** A bard struck from every roll offers a party what he knows of the closed songs, in return for a name in the next house's line.
