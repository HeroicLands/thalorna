## Overview {#overview}

**The Standard of the Kept Cohort** is the banner of the Vilérian cohort that followed a path at the forest margin of [[place-auldalon|Áuldalon]] in the war that ended at [[place-valdun|Valdun]] and was not seen again ([[lore-elvtribute|The Tribute of Élavendre]]). It is a square of red wool on a crossbar, worked with the cohort's mark, on a staff shod in bronze. It hangs in the hall under the hill the drovers call [[place-kptfr|the Kept Fire]], and the cohort still musters under it.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The cohort holds it. It stands in the standard-rack of the alcove of its _Lusar_, [[being-solkimoslsr|Solkimos]], at the head of the hall, rotted at the edges and still legible, and the Kept stand to the roll beneath it each night.

## Its Story {#its-story}

The cohort carried it into the hall under the hill in the war, and it has not been carried out since. The _felzos_'s house at [[place-beravel|Péravel]] keeps the cohort's muster-roll, which shows the cohort as deserted, its banner unaccounted for and its pay-chest lost with it.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

It does nothing a weaver could not make. Carried out of the hall, it is the proof that the cohort held its post under its own colors and did not desert.

:::secret {title="The muster"}
The cohort carried the standard into the fold on the winter night its _Lusar_ demanded shelter of the Giver. Without it the cohort cannot muster, and a _Lusar_ whose standard is held by another must treat with whoever holds it.

**Hook: the muster.** A party that takes the standard from the rack while the Kept stand to the roll finds the _Lusar_ ready to treat with it.
:::

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

A Vilérian collector at Péravel pays for a cohort's standard and does not ask where it hung. The clerk of the _felzos_'s house wants proof that the cohort held, which is worth arrears to the families that carry its name. The cohort wants it where it is.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The collector.** A Vilérian collector in Péravel pays well for the standard of the cohort lost in the war at Valdun, and says the old soldiers who guard it are the only difficulty ([[scenario-drkptfr|The Roll Beneath the Hill]]).
