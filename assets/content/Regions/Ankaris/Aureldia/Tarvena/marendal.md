## Overview

In **Maréndal** the fog comes first and the boats come second. The capital of the Kingdom of Galváren is a harbor town of about 20,000, built along a natural bay on the green, mist-shrouded western coast of [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]], where fishing boats, merchant vessels and the occasional war galley crowd together. Galváren is a country of rain, fog, deep forests and rocky shores facing the open ocean, and its fishing fleets are the largest in western Ankaris, ranging far out for the seasonal runs of cod, herring and tuna.

## Who Governs

The kingdom is a hereditary monarchy with a council of coastal lords and shipmasters, and Maréndal is its seat. The shipmasters, the captains of the fishing and trading fleets, sit on the royal council beside the landed lords, which gives the people who work the water a voice in how the kingdom is run. The Kingdom of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] is Galváren's overlord in name.

## Character

Galváreni are seafarers, fishermen and storytellers, and the harbor is where the stories are told. The western coast is littered with ancient stone circles, standing stones and passage graves older than the Tarvéni migration, and local people keep a body of folk belief about the spirits of the dead, the sea and the places where the mortal world thins. Ghost stories and tales of drowned cities are taken more seriously here than anywhere else in Tarvénia, and the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían]] priests tolerate them with varying degrees of patience. Galváreni sailors are legendarily fearless, or foolhardy, depending on whom you ask, and the kingdom produces a disproportionate number of navigators, sea captains and explorers.
