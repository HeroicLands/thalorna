## Overview

Ólvestra is one of the great commercial cities of the west, named in the same breath as [[place-orzara|Ôrzâra]] and [[place-byzariargn|Byzaría]] when merchants talk about where goods fetch the highest prices. It stands in [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]], and what a traveler meets first is its gate: **the Twin Gate of Ólvestra**, one of the most consequential thresholds in all of Aurèldía, kept by knights whose whole order exists to keep gates.

## The City of Oaths and Gates

Two orders keep their mother-houses here, and between them they make Ólvestra the place where Aurèldía's sworn word is recorded and its thresholds are guarded.

- **The [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|Order of the Sealed Word]]**, chartered here about 421 AF ([[lore-sealedwordfnd|The Founding of the Sealed Word]]), keeps its mother-house and its archives at Ólvestra, including the master register of every contested oath the order has ruled on in its three-century history. Its **Council of the Seal**, twelve Senior Brothers drawn from the five kingdoms, meets here every quarter, and a Senior Brother wears a small bronze key at the throat for the keys of the archive.
- **The [[affiliation-twinbldrdr|Twinblade Order]]** keeps its mother-house at the Twin Gate, conducts its senior elevations in temple rite at the Twin Gate temple, and maintains a ceremonial cadre in the city for the coronations, treaty-signings and embassies that require a Twinblade's presence. Its present Voice, **Veronica Aldorina**, was Mârendândôs of the Ólvestran Twin Gate before her elevation.

## Money and Danger

Ólvestra is a moneylenders' city. The great houses of the [[affiliation-danvar|Danvar]] keep offices here—the **House of Calventian** among them—and the Danvar's chapterhouse is one of its largest, with permanent staff in the dozens. Prices for premium goods run higher here than almost anywhere in the west, and an Ólvestran house's notes travel far: if the imperial treasury suspends one for political reasons, cashing houses across the empire are left holding paper they cannot redeem.

The city has its shadows too. **The Ólvestran Cell** of the [[affiliation-ordershnvw|Order of the Ashen Vow]] is that order's largest, operating under the protection of the hill-baronies around the city, and the death of the Ólvestran Mârendândôs of the Sealed Word seven years ago—ruled natural, three days before he was to issue a major writ against a baron sympathetic to [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]]—is still talked about as something less than natural.
