## Overview

Ólvestria is one of the great commercial cities of the west, named in the same breath as [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]] and [[place-byzariargn|Byzaría]] when merchants talk about where goods fetch the highest prices. It stands in [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]], and what a traveler meets first is its gate: **the Twin Gate of Ólvestria**, one of the most consequential thresholds in all of Aurèldía, kept by knights whose whole order exists to keep gates.

## The City of Oaths and Gates

Two orders keep their mother-houses here, and between them they make Ólvestria the place where Aurèldía's sworn word is recorded and its thresholds are guarded.

- **The [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|Order of the Sealed Word]]** keeps its mother-house and its archives at Ólvestria, including the master register of every contested oath the order has ruled on in its three-century history. Its **Council of the Seal**, twelve Senior Brothers drawn from the five kingdoms, meets here every quarter, and a Senior Brother wears a small bronze key at the throat for the keys of the archive.
- **The [[affiliation-twinbldrdr|Twinblade Order]]** keeps its mother-house at the Twin Gate, conducts its senior elevations in temple rite at the Twin Gate temple, and maintains a ceremonial cadre in the city for the coronations, treaty-signings and embassies that require a Twinblade's presence. Its present Voice, **Veronica Aldorina**, was Magister of the Ólvestrian Twin Gate before her elevation.

## Money and Danger

Ólvestria is a moneylenders' city. The great houses of the [[affiliation-clgmrgntrrm|Collegium Argentariorum]] keep offices here—the **House of Calventian** among them—and the Collegium's chapterhouse is one of its largest, with permanent staff in the dozens. Prices for premium goods run higher here than almost anywhere in the west, and a Ólvestrian house's notes travel far: if the imperial treasury suspends one for political reasons, cashing houses across the empire are left holding paper they cannot redeem.

The city has its shadows too. **The Ólvestrian Cell** of the [[affiliation-ordershnvw|Order of the Ashen Vow]] is that order's largest, operating under the protection of the hill-baronies around the city, and the death of the Ólvestrian Magister of the Sealed Word seven years ago—ruled natural, three days before he was to issue a major writ against a baron sympathetic to [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]]—is still talked about as something less than natural.
