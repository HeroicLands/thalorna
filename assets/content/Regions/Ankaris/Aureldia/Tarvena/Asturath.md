## Overview

Asturath is the land of the [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]], the mountainous interior of the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]] and the home of a million people who have never been ruled from outside. Its ranges stopped the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], its plateau city of [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] is the seat of the High King, and its high valleys send crossbowmen to every war in the west.

## Character

Asturath is mountain country from end to end. Ranges of gray limestone and darker rock run through it in broken ridges, with peaks that keep snow into summer and narrow valleys between them, each with its stream, its strip of fields and its villages of stone houses roofed with stone. Beech and chestnut woods cover the lower slopes; above them are the high meadows, the summer grazing, and above those the scree and the bare rock. The valleys are deep and close, and two villages a mile apart as the crow flies may be a day's walk apart by the path.

The weather follows the height. Winters are long and hard, and snow closes the passes and the higher roads from late autumn to spring. The spring snowmelt floods the streams, summer is short and bright, and autumn brings the fog that sits in the passes for days at a time. The _ozkur_ (wind off the peaks) blows cold in every season.

## The Land and Its Roads

Lezkora stands near the middle of the kingdom on a plateau ringed by cliffs, and the plateau road climbs to it from the south. A day below the plateau lies [[place-ardalis|Ardalis]], the iron town, and below Ardalis the road goes down out of the mountains to the lowlands of [[place-leonrik|Lienrik]]. North of Lezkora the high valleys open toward the summer meadows, and their flocks gather at [[place-tsaberis|Tsaberis]] each spring for the drive. At the kingdom's eastern edge [[place-kavrenath|Kávrenath]] watches the road toward the [[place-kervaron|Kervaron Spine]] and the imperial frontier, and above its valleys the pass of [[place-zurgando|Zurgando]] gives the companies a short road to the coast. The gorge below the plateau, [[place-horngorge|the Horn Gorge]], is where the legion died, and nobody uses it as a way through.

Most of the kingdom's people live in villages of a few hundred. A valley is a lord's holding, its villages are his, and its people meet in a village assembly to settle what concerns them all. Roads between valleys are mule paths, and the only roads a cart can use are the plateau road and the valley road to Kávrenath.

## Livelihood

Asturath lives on its flocks, its iron and its men. Sheep and goats are the wealth of every valley: they go up to the high meadows in the summer drive, come down in autumn, and give the wool, the cheese and the mutton the valleys live on through the winter. The valley floors grow rye, barley and beans, and the woods give chestnuts, which feed a household when the grain runs short. Ardalis mines and forges the iron of its hill, and Ardalis steel goes into the crossbows of the companies.

The companies are the third harvest. A young man of the valleys goes abroad under contract as a matter of course, and the money that comes home from other people's wars buys the grain a valley cannot grow. The households that send men abroad are better supplied than the ones that do not, and a valley whose companies have a bad season has a hungry winter.

## Neighbors

The lowlands of Lienrik lie to the south, and the two kingdoms need each other and dislike it: Lienrik's grain feeds the mountains, and Asturath's iron and soldiers are bought by Lienrik. The highlands of [[place-eskarath|Eskarath]] lie to the north, a people older than the Tarveni, with their own tongue and their own law. To the east the Kervaron belongs to the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] clans in all but the Empire's paperwork, and beyond it lies the Empire.
