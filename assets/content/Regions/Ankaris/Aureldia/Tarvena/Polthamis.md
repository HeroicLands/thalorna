## Overview

Polthamis is a city of [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]] and the home of **the [Great Temple of Árdavon]{.name data-type="place/structure"}**, where the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]] trains the priests it sends out across the kingdom. A boy who comes to the Great Temple as an acolyte studies theology, law and combat together, because the Árdavonian faith expects its priests to be both protectors and spiritual guides, and he leaves it ordained to serve Árdavon on the battlefield and in the courts.

## The Prelate's Seal

The Temple's reach outlasts the training. **The Árdavonian prelate at Polthamis** confirms the arbitrations of the faith's traveling priests under seal, and the commissions that send them into cases of noble corruption carry that seal too, along with the protection those cases need from the complaints they draw. [[being-frstrndrvs|Father Ostaran Dorvás]] trained at the Great Temple and meets the prelate on the register four times a year. He knows the protection is conditional: it holds only while his work never reaches the royal court.
