## Overview

Eskárath is the land of [[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|The Free Territory of Eskárath]], the highlands along the northern edge of [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]].

Eskárath is the highland territory along Tarvénia's northern border with [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], home to a people who predate the Tarvéni migration by centuries.
