## Overview

**Valthari** is everything the mountain lords of [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Asturath]] distrust: wealthy, cosmopolitan and thoroughly mercantile. This free city of 45,000 on the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]]'s southern coast is the richest single settlement in the kingdom, and it is Tarvénia's window onto distant trade. Its wealth comes from its position as the gateway for seaborne trade into Tarvénia. Wine, olive oil, grain, wool and iron flow out; spices, silk, luxury goods and coin flow in.

## The Harbor

The harbor pidgin of the whole coast, [[skill-valtarlng|Valtari]], is named for this port, where its first fixed form grew up on the quays.

The harbor is crowded with ships from the [[place-provenzrgn|Provenzia Region]], the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]], [[place-helionis|Helionis]] and beyond, and the dockside markets trade in goods from across [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]]. [[skill-provnzlng|Provenzal]] is spoken widely beside [[skill-tarvenlng|Tarveni]], Taruzeli merchants keep their own quarter, and the city's architecture blends Tarvenan stone-and-iron practicality with Provenzian elegance. The mountain lords call Valthari soft and foreign-tainted, and Valthari calls them provincial and broke. Both assessments contain a grain of truth.

## Who Governs

The [Consejarath]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, a council of the great merchant houses, governs Valthari under a royal charter bought with tribute to the High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]], the first of whom sold it ([[lore-tarvfallsawy|Tarvénia Falls Away]]). The charter guarantees the city its self-government, its own courts and freedom from feudal levies. The council is drawn from the dozen or so merchant dynasties that control the major trading houses, shipyards and banks, and seats are fiercely contested: Valthari politics is a dance of commercial alliances, marriage pacts and, occasionally, discreet assassination. The city keeps a small, professional navy to guard its shipping lanes, and its marine soldiers are among the best equipped in Tarvénia.
