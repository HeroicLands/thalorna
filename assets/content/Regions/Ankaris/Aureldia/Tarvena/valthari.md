## Overview

**Valthári** is everything the mountain lords of [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Astúrath]] distrust: wealthy, cosmopolitan and thoroughly mercantile. This free city of 45,000 on Tarvénia's southern coast is the richest single settlement in the kingdom, and it is Tarvénia's window onto distant trade. Its wealth comes from its position as the gateway for seaborne trade into Tarvénia. Wine, olive oil, grain, wool and iron flow out; spices, silk, luxury goods and coin flow in.

## The Harbor

The harbor is crowded with ships from [[place-provenzrgn|Provènzia]], [[affiliation-cnfdrtnhrdnstts|Harad]], [[place-helionis|Heliónis]] and beyond, and the dockside markets trade in goods from across [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]]. Provenzal is spoken widely beside Tarvéni, Haradi merchants keep their own quarter, and the city's architecture blends Tarvénan stone-and-iron practicality with Provenzian elegance. The mountain lords call Valthári soft and foreign-tainted, and Valthári calls them provincial and broke. Both assessments contain a grain of truth.

## Who Governs

The Consejárath, a council of the great merchant houses, governs Valthári under a royal charter bought with tribute to the High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]]. The charter guarantees the city its self-government, its own courts and freedom from feudal levies. The council is drawn from the dozen or so merchant dynasties that control the major trading houses, shipyards and banks, and seats are fiercely contested: Valthári politics is a dance of commercial alliances, marriage pacts and, occasionally, discreet assassination. The city keeps a small, professional navy to guard its shipping lanes, and its marine soldiers are among the best equipped in Tarvénia.
