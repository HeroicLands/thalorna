## Overview

Galvaren is the land of the [[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]], the western coast of the [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]].

Galvaren occupies the green, mist-shrouded western coast of Tarvénia, where the land meets the open ocean. It is a kingdom of rain, fog, deep forests, and rocky coastlines—beautiful and melancholy in equal measure.

## Character

Its fishing fleets are the largest in the western part of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]], ranging far into the open ocean for the great seasonal runs of cod, herring, and tuna.

The capital, **[[place-ornethe|Ornethe]]**, is a harbor town built along a natural bay where fishing boats, merchant vessels, and the occasional war galley crowd together.
