## Overview

The Toravar Range is the great forested mountain belt that runs east–west across the upper country of [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]] and marks its northern border with [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]]. On a map it is a frontier. On the ground it is Áelendan country: the high reaches of the range, and the great forested uplands that flank it on both sides, are among the most densely Áelendan land outside the Tribal Lands themselves.

## Sacred High Country

The [[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan]] hold the Toravar as sacred high country, in the same way they hold the [[place-vylariargn#the-cervaron-spine|Cervaron Spine]] to the east. To their reckoning the [[place-drenavrspn|Drenavar]], the Cervaron and the Toravar are not three ranges but one continuous sacred uplift, the high spine of the western world: the Drenavar is its heart, and the Cervaron and the Toravar are its eastern and southern arms. Outsiders distinguish them; the Áelendan distinguish them only when speaking to outsiders.

The Áelendan of the Toravar are the same people as the clans of [[place-alndntrblnds|the Tribal Lands]]: they hold the same Kindred Pact, recognize the same Wardens and keep the same religious calendar, and they live in the high mountains because the high mountains are where they have always lived.

## Barons and Clans

The range is where Tarvénan barons and Áelendan clans meet. Frictions over straying herds, logging concessions and road-surveys near sacred groves are handled by the local Wardens with the local baron, usually without reaching the crown. Roughly once a generation a brittle baron meets a stubborn clan, and the result is a brief, ugly mountain-war that the crown of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] settles quickly and quietly through Élavendri intermediaries. No Tarvénan king has tried to extend formal authority over the Áelendan of the Toravar.
