## Overview

The Toravar Range is the great forested mountain belt that runs east–west across the upper country of [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]] and marks its northern border with [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]]. On a map it is a frontier. On the ground it is Áelendan country: the high reaches of the range, and the great forested uplands that flank it on both sides, are among the most densely Áelendan land outside the [[place-alndntrblnds|Tribal Lands]] themselves.

## Sacred High Country

The [[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan]] hold the Toravar as sacred high country, in the same way they hold the [[place-vylariargn#the-cervaron-spine|Kervaron Spine]] to the east. To their reckoning [[place-drenavrspn|the Drenavar]], the Kervaron and the Toravar are not three ranges but one continuous sacred uplift, the high spine of the western world: the Drenavar is its heart, and the Kervaron and the Toravar are its eastern and southern arms. Outsiders distinguish them; the Áelendan distinguish them only when speaking to outsiders.

The [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] of the Toravar are the same people as the clans of the Tribal Lands: they hold the same [Kindred Pact]{.name data-type="lore/law"}, recognize the same [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] and keep the same religious calendar, and they live in the high mountains because the high mountains are where they have always lived.

## Barons and Clans

The range is where Tarvenan barons and Áelendan clans meet. Frictions over straying herds, logging concessions and road-surveys near sacred groves are handled by the local Wardens with the local baron, usually without reaching the crown. Roughly once a generation a brittle baron meets a stubborn clan, and the result is a brief, ugly mountain-war that the crown of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]] settles quickly and quietly through Élavendri intermediaries. No Tarvenan king has tried to extend formal authority over the Áelendan of the Toravar.
