## Overview

Thornhill Manor is the seat of **Lord Varek of Thornhill Manor**, a Tarvénan lord in the country around [[place-valthari|Valthári]], and it is known to its neighbors for its hunting. The lord hunts often and with guests, and those guests include greater lords than he is.

## The Huntswoman

The manor's hunts are in the hands of its trusted huntswoman, [[being-zoriladmvr|Zórila Dómivar]], who grew up in a hunting family and is known for her skill with the bow and her ability to move silently through the woods. Lord Varek relies on her heavily in the hunting season and often invites her to hunt beside him. Her place at the manor rests on his standing, and his standing rests on Tarvénian politics: a change at court that threatens Lord Varek threatens everyone who holds a place at Thornhill because of him.
