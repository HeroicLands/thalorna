The Horn Gorge is a long cleft in the rock below [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]], too narrow for a road and too steep for a flock. It is not used as a way through, and the mountain people say so as a matter of course.

## The Field

The gorge is a [[lore-bloodfield|blood-field]] made by [[lore-horngorgebtl|The Battle of the Horn Gorge]] in which the Astúrathi closed both ends on a Vylarian legion about 143 AF. Its kind is a **recurrence**, and it is moderately thin. On autumn nights the legion's horns sound the recall from the open end of the gorge, and the echo comes back from the closed end as though a wall of stone had been thrown up behind the sound. A traveler who sleeps in the gorge dreams that both ends are closing.

The harm is the dream, and the dreams stay with the sleeper for weeks. By day the gorge is a cold, stony cleft with nothing in it.

## How the Place Is Treated

At the upper end the Astúrathi raised a cairn after the battle. They leave nothing there for the Vylarian dead and something for their own, and the cairn's stones are the ones the legion's baggage lay under. No priest of the [[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]], which teaches that the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]'s dead are judged before the god [[lore-selmorosdty|Sélmoros]], has been asked to settle the field, though the legion's dead were his in its teaching and the rite could reach them. The Astúrathi will not pay to settle an enemy's dead, and the Empire struck the legion off its rolls and has not asked for it since.

## Who Goes

- **Astúrathi shepherds**, by day and never to stay.
- **Vylarian antiquarians**, who want the legion's name and its standards.
- **Pilgrims of [[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]]**, who climb to Lezkora by the path above the gorge.

## Hooks

- **The paychest.** The legion's strongbox was never found, and nobody in [[place-asturath|Asturath]] will say whether it was looked for.
- **A name read at the cairn.** A Vylarian family wants a name read aloud there, which may be the beginning of a settlement or of something worse.
- **The recall.** Someone has begun to answer the horns, and the answer comes from higher up the gorge.
