## Overview {#overview}

**The Isle of Kalipsa** lies in the cold sea seventy-five miles off the northern coast of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], a day and a night's sail west of the mouth of [[place-liserebay|Lísere Bay]]. It is about fifteen miles across at its widest, from the cliffs of the west to the harbor on the east coast, and it rises steeply from the water to a bare gray ridge. Some forty-five thousand people live on it: twenty-eight thousand in the free port of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]], and seventeen thousand in the fishing villages and upland steadings of [[place-calypsashore|the South Shore]].

The island is the whole domain of the city-state of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]], and it pays no tribute and owes no fealty to anyone. It feeds itself by trade. Its harbor is one of the best-sheltered anchorages in the north of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]], and its squadrons sit across the approaches to Lísere Bay.

## The Land {#the-land}

Kalipsa is rock with a skin of soil. The western and northern coasts are cliff, white with seabirds in spring, and no boat lands there. The ridge along the island's spine, [Hlomfell]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, is bare turf and scree above the last walls, grazed by goats and a few sheep. On the eastern slopes, below the ridge and above the city, the vineyards climb in terraces of dry-laid stone. The southern shore is lower and kinder, a string of coves and shingle beaches between black headlands, and every cove that a boat can be hauled out of has a village in it.

Fresh water is the island's poverty. No river runs on Kalipsa, and the springs that rise along the southern shore run thin by late summer. Every roof in the city drains into a cistern, every village has its spring-house, and water is sold by the cask in a dry year.

The weather comes off the open ocean from the west. Winters are wet and windy rather than hard, and fog lies on the eastern approaches for days together in the autumn.

## Peoples {#peoples}

The Kalipsi are a harbor people made from everyone who has ever tied up there. The oldest houses are of [[lore-nordheimnclt|Nordmen]] stock, and [[skill-nordmalng|Nordmal]] is the tongue of the old city, the docks and the villages. [[skill-valtarlng|Valtari]], the trade pidgin of the inner sea, is the tongue of the markets and of the houses that came later. Élavendri younger sons, Provénzian factors, Vilérian moneylenders and Malagnan sailors settle in every generation, and their grandchildren are Kalipsi. The [[lore-kalipsanclt|Kalipsan]] culture note describes how they live.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The island and its waters are governed by the [[affiliation-calypsa|city-state of Kalipsa]] through its [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]], whose Harbormaster holds the harbor, the forts and the navy for a single three-year term. The city governs the villages as it governs its own wards, through its customs, its watch and the [[lore-sealaw|Sea-Law]], and the villages have no seats on the Council. Most of the island's land belongs to the shipping houses, which hold the vineyards and the upland grazing, or to the village families who have fished the same coves for centuries.

## Places {#places}

- **[[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]]**, the free port on the east coast, with [[place-oldmole|the Old Mole]] across its harbor mouth, [[place-lantrnklps|the Lantern of Kalipsa]] on the northern headland and [[place-sealawhall|the Sea-Law Hall]] on the quay.
- **[[place-calypsashore|The South Shore]]**, the fishing villages and steadings of the southern coves.
- **Hlomfell**, the ridge, where the herders' paths cross the island and the city's watchmen keep a fire-station that can see both coasts.
- **[The Hounds]{.name data-type="place/feature"}**, a line of rocks off the northern headland that every pilot is licensed to avoid.

## Current Situation {#present}

Kalipsa is rich, armed and anxious about money. The tonnage levy that pays for the navy has risen three years running, and the houses that pay most of it are the houses that want it lowest. The present Harbormaster, [[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]], has held it, and her term ends at the spring sitting of 720.

The election that follows is the island's quarrel. [[being-knilthtvrn|Knilthorv Tvarnidrok]], head of the oldest house on the Council, means to succeed her. [[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]], whose house has risen in a decade on the fur trade through [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]], means to stop him. The two are counting the same hulls: the Council's voices follow the tonnage census taken each spring, and three of the Norodari hulls went down on the Kalipsa Run in the autumn fogs. Whether the [[affiliation-bondsyndcts|Bond Syndicates]] pay for them, and whether Norodari can buy their replacements before the census, is before the arbitrators of the Sea-Law Hall.

On the South Shore the quarrel is water. A dry summer left the springs low, and the city's water-carts have been drawing from village spring-houses that the villages say are theirs.

## Roads and Trade {#roads-and-trade}

The island's road is the sea. [[place-kalipsarun|The Kalipsa Run]] crosses to Sarmelis on Lísere Bay in a day and a night, and the Malagnan hulls come down from [[place-gnarthborg|Gnarthborg]] and the north. On the island itself one cart road climbs from the city over the shoulder of Hlomfell to the South Shore, and herders' paths link the villages along the cliffs.

Everything the island eats comes in by ship: grain from Élavendre, timber and furs from the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]], cloth from [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia]], olive oil and wine from the south. What the island makes for itself is fish, shellfish dye from the southern rocks, wool and goat's cheese from the upland, and an indifferent wine.

## Faiths {#faiths}

The faiths of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] are the island's religions, and [[affiliation-pozeras|the Faith of Pozeras]], the Wayfarer's, keeps the busiest temple, on the quay. The Nordmen families also keep the gods of the north, and the [[affiliation-thrunvald|Faith of Thrunvald]] is honored at sea by crews who would not mention it ashore. The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] keeps a factor's house in the city and no chapterhouse.

## Settlements {#settlements}

**Kalipsa**, about 28,000, is the city and the harbor. **The South Shore**, about 17,000, is four villages of size, [Dvonvík]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Vrellnes]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, [Hvelmnes]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} and [Glervhavan]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, a dozen smaller coves and the steadings of the upland.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **The Hounds and the autumn fog.** The eastern approach is easy in clear weather and deadly in fog, and every year the fog takes a hull that would not wait for a pilot.
- **The cliffs.** The western and northern coasts give no landing, and a ship driven onto them in a westerly is lost with her crew.
- **Raiders.** Nordmen and [[lore-varokhiclt|Varokh]] boats work the coasts around Lísere Bay. Few come near the island, because its squadrons make the visit expensive, but a South Shore cove at night is not the city.

## History {#history}

The Old Mole's stones are the oldest thing on the island, and nobody can date them. The Nordmen fishers and traders who settled the harbor gave the old city its tongue, and the oldest houses count their lines from them. Under the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] the port took the guild charter of [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]] and paid its dues, and kept its own Council. A civil war among the houses about 470 AF gave the Harbormaster a single term. The houses built their navy on the tonnage levy in the years of [[lore-southrnfleets|the Southern Fleets]], and since [[lore-crownssettle|the Settlement of the Crowns]] the island has paid tax to nobody. The city-state's note tells the history at length.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The census.** [[being-hrundthrsk|The Harbormaster]] must certify the spring census, and both candidates want a witness to the count who is not in either house's pay.
- **The spring-houses.** A village of the South Shore has barred its spring-house to the city's water-carts, and the carters want an escort.
