## Overview {#overview}

**Norodari** is a house of the markets of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]], and its name is a [[skill-valtarlng|Valtari]] trade name: the house of the north, for the trade it lives by. It carries the furs that come across [[place-liserebay|Lísere Bay]] to the outer quay of [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]], and the Élavendren grain and salt-fish that go with them, on [[place-kalipsarun|the Kalipsa Run]]. A decade ago it sailed three hulls and had no seat on the [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]]. It now sails more than a dozen, and its head leads the newer houses there.

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

Kubasor Norodari heads the house, and he took its seat on the Council when its tonnage first passed the census floor. He means to deny the Harbormaster's office to [[affiliation-hsetvarnidrok|Tvarnidrok]] at the election of 720, and the newer houses follow him because a Tvarnidrok Harbormaster would hold the levy on the old houses' terms. The house keeps a counting-room at Sarmelis behind the outer quay and another on Kalipsa's lower quay, and its factors buy at both.

## Holdings {#holdings}

- **Its hulls**, about fourteen of its own, most of them coasters of the Run, and two building in the [[affiliation-hsethraskumak|Thraskumak]] yards.
- **Its counting-rooms** at Kalipsa and at Sarmelis.
- **The fur trade** of the outer quay, which it carries more of than any other house.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

**The lost hulls.** Three Norodari hulls went down in the autumn fog on the eastern approaches, and [the Syndicate of the Gull]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"} of [[affiliation-bondsyndcts|the Bond Syndicates]] has refused their bonds before a hearing at [[place-sealawhall|the Sea-Law Hall]]. The house borrowed against those bonds to order its new hulls, and without them it comes to the spring census short and in debt.

**The census rule.** Tvarnidrok wants hulls bought in the winter left off the spring roll until they have sailed, and Kubasor calls it a rule invented for the occasion.

**The furs.** The furs the house carries come across the bay to Sarmelis in small boats from [[lore-varokhiclt|Varokh]] hands, and a good part of them leaves the outer quay unentered at the crown's tally-house. The old houses say so in the Council when it suits them, and their own factors buy from the same boats.

## History as Told {#history}

The house began with Kubasor's mother, a factor at Sarmelis who bought furs from the boats when the old houses would not, and took the trade name the outer quay gave her. She bought her first hull on money borrowed from the city's moneylenders, and her son bought the rest.

## Arms {#arms}

The house has no arms. Its mark, a north-pointing arrow cut in a bale's wrapping, is on every bale it carries.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The pilots' boat.** Kubasor Norodari wants proof that the pilots' boat was off its station on the nights his hulls were lost.
