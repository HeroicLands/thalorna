## Overview {#overview}

**The Kalipsa Run** is the sea road between [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]], the harbor town at the head of [[place-liserebay|Lísere Bay]], and the free port of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]]. A hull leaves the outer quay of Sarmelis, works out of the bay past the narrows under [[place-durmelen|Durmelen]], and crosses the open sea westward to the [[place-calypsaisle|Isle of Kalipsa]], seventy-five miles off the Élavendren shore. With a fair wind the passage is a day and a night. Against a westerly it is two days of beating, or a wait at Sarmelis for the wind to change.

It is the busiest crossing in the north. More of the north's goods change hulls on it than anywhere else on the coast, and a good part of what crosses it is never entered at any tally-house. The smugglers of the Lísere shore say "the Kalipsa run" of their own trade, and the name has spread to the honest one.

## What Moves on It {#what-moves-on-it}

**Grain.** Kalipsa eats what it buys, and much of what it eats is Élavendren grain, loaded at Sarmelis in the autumn for the island's granaries.

**Salt-fish, oil and timber.** The bay's fish and fish oil, and the pine and pitch of the Lísere forest, go west to be shipped on.

**Furs.** Bales come across the bay to Sarmelis from [[lore-varokhiclt|Varokh]] hands in small boats and leave the outer quay with a Kalipsan mark. [[affiliation-hsenorodari|The Norodari]] carry most of them.

**Everything else.** Cloth, wine and oil from the south, Malagnan iron and amber, and the goods of the east go the other way, landed at Kalipsa from the deep-sea hulls and carried into the bay by the smaller coasters that the Sarmelis yards build for the trade.

## Logistics {#logistics}

The coasters of the Run are decked, single-masted hulls of about twenty to forty tons, built to work out of the bay against the wind and to sit low with grain. A hull carries water and bread for three days. There is no harbor between the two ends: a hull caught by weather in the open runs back for the bay or runs on for the island, and nothing in between offers a landing.

At the Kalipsa end every hull takes a pilot. The licensed pilots of the city's pilots' guild keep a boat at the edge of the approaches in daylight, and in clear weather a master who arrives at night lies off under the Lantern until morning. When fog lies on the approaches the boat keeps its station through the night as well, sounding a horn, because a master in fog cannot see the Lantern to lie off by it. The pilot's fee is fixed by the [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]], and so is the fine for entering without one. At Sarmelis the [[affiliation-sarmelisconcl|Quay Council of Sarmelis]] gives Kalipsan hulls their berths at the outer quay for a fee by the day.

## The Stations {#stations}

**The outer quay of Sarmelis** is the eastern end. Kalipsan factors keep counting-rooms behind it, and [The Hawser]{.name data-type="place/structure"} takes their captains.

**The narrows** under Durmelen are the end of the first day's sail down the bay, where the crown's fort watches the horn and the crown's three cutters out of Sarmelis may stop and board a hull.

**The open sea** beyond the narrows is the night of the day and the night. A master keeps the [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]] shore well to starboard for its raiders and steers for the Lantern.

**[[place-lantrnklps|The Lantern of Kalipsa]]** shows from the open sea long before the island does, a clear yellow light on the northern headland.

**[The Hounds]{.name data-type="place/feature"}**, the rocks off the headland, are where the pilot boards.

**The harbor of Kalipsa**, behind [[place-oldmole|the Old Mole]], is the western end, where the Harbormaster's customs officers board every hull before it berths.

## The Year on the Run {#year-on-the-road}

**Spring** opens the Run with the first steady easterlies, and the hulls that wintered at Kalipsa go into the bay for the new season's fish and timber. **Summer** is the season of the furs, when the bales come over from the Vristwald shore by night. **Autumn** is the grain season and the season of fog, the busiest and most dangerous months, when the hulls cross deep-laden and the fog lies on the eastern approaches for days together. **Winter** closes most of the Run: the westerly gales keep the coasters in harbor, and only the largest hulls cross, and only in a fair spell.

## Who Patrols and Taxes It {#present}

Kalipsa's squadrons patrol the approaches to the island and the mouth of the bay, and no raider has taken a hull on the Run within sight of the Lantern in living memory. The Harbormaster's customs take the island's dues on every cargo landed at Kalipsa. On the Élavendren side, the crown's sheriff of [[place-lisere|Lísere]] takes the duty on whatever is entered at the tally-house of Sarmelis, and the crown's cutters board where they please inside the bay. What leaves the outer quay unentered is a matter between the sheriff's clerks and the Kalipsan factors, and both prefer it so.

The Run's quarrel this year is the autumn's losses. Three Norodari hulls went down in the fog off the eastern approaches within a fortnight, with their crews and their grain. [[affiliation-bondsyndcts|The Bond Syndicates]] that wrote the hulls' bonds have refused to pay before the loss is heard at [[place-sealawhall|the Sea-Law Hall]], and the pilots' guild says that none of the three masters took a pilot. The Norodari say the pilots' boat was not on its station, and the hearing will decide who pays.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **The autumn fog** on the eastern approaches, where the Hounds lie a cable's length from the deep channel.
- **Westerly gales**, which drive a hull back into the bay or onto the island's cliffs.
- **Raiders** at the mouth of the bay, where Varokh and Nordmen boats wait for a hull that has fallen behind the others.
- **The crown's cutters**, for a master whose cargo would not bear entering at the tally-house.

## History {#history}

Hulls have crossed between the bay and the island for as long as there has been a harbor at either end. The Run became a crossing worth guarding when the houses built their navy, in the years of [[lore-southrnfleets|the Southern Fleets]], and the pilots' charter is as old as the navy.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The lost hulls.** The Norodari want a crew to go out on the eastern approaches in the next fog and see whether the pilots' boat keeps its station.
- **A hull for the bay.** A Kalipsan factor at the outer quay needs a cargo of furs carried to the island before the crown's cutters come down the bay, and his regular master is in the sheriff's hall.
