## Overview {#overview}

**The Old Mole** is the stone breakwater that closes the south side of the harbor of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]], running out from the foot of the old city into the deep water and curving north to leave the harbor mouth between its head and the northern headland. It is about four hundred paces long and wide enough on its back for two carts to pass. Its outer face takes the whole weight of the eastern sea in an autumn gale, and the harbor behind it lies still.

The stones are the thing people come to see. The lower courses are great squared blocks, each larger than a cart, laid without mortar and fitted so close that a knife blade does not go between them. Several of them are carved on their faces with lines of characters that no living scholar can read.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The mole belongs to the city, and the [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]] holds it through the Harbormaster, who answers for the harbor works. A standing order of the Council forbids anyone to lift, cut or move a stone of the old courses, and the city's masons repair the upper work and leave the old blocks as they lie.

## Layers {#history}

**The old courses.** Nobody knows who laid the lower blocks or when. The city's tradition says the mole was built by refugees from a forgotten Helionite colony who came over the sea and found the harbor empty, and that the carving on the stones is their writing. The tradition is old, and it is the city's only account. Whether any colony of the years when the cities of [[place-helionis|Helionis]] sent their sons over the sea lies behind it is a question that the histories of those years leave open ([[lore-daughtercts|The Daughter-Cities]]).

**The city's work.** The upper courses are the city's, laid and relaid by its masons over the old blocks after every great storm, in smaller stone and mortar. The quay along the mole's inner face, where the deep-sea hulls berth, is the city's work too, and so is the round tower at its head that carries the harbor chain.

**The chain.** In the years of [[lore-southrnfleets|the Southern Fleets]] the houses hung an iron chain from the mole's head to the headland, and it can be raised across the harbor mouth in an hour. It has been raised in earnest twice in living memory.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The winter's first great storm shifted a block of the outer old course near the mole's head. It did not fall, but it settled and turned, and at low water on a spring tide its underside now shows a carved face that nobody living had seen. The city’s master mason, [[being-ralthannrulth|Ralthann Rultharuk]], wants the block wedged and the upper work rebuilt over it before the next storm opens the gap further. [[being-marvindnalth|Marvinda Naltharuk]], [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|The Order]]’s factor in the city, has asked the Harbormaster for leave to take copies of the new face before it is covered, and has offered to pay the masons for the delay.

[[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]] has given neither leave. The Council's order forbids moving the stone, the master mason says the stone has moved itself, and a delay costs the harbor its safety for every day it lasts.

## Dangerous Neighbours {#dangerous-neighbours}

- **The outer face in a gale.** The sea breaks over the mole's back in an easterly storm, and the watch closes the mole to everyone but the chain-men.
- **The water at its head.** The tide runs hard round the head of the mole, and a swimmer or a small boat taken by it is carried out through the harbor mouth.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

The inner face of the mole is a working quay: deep-sea hulls berthed two deep, porters, the customs officers' boats, and the smell of tar and wet rope. The outer face is reached by a stair at the mole's root and a ledge along the old course, wet at high water and dry at low, and the carved stones are along that ledge. The carving is worn on the faces the sea strikes and sharp in the joints. The round tower at the head holds the chain's windlass and a guard of the watch.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The new face.** [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|The Order]]'s factor wants copies taken at the next low spring tide, quietly, before the masons cover it, and the watch at the mole's head does not let anyone onto the outer ledge at night.
- **The master mason's wedge.** The master mason wants the block wedged before the next storm, with or without the Harbormaster's leave, and he is a man short.
