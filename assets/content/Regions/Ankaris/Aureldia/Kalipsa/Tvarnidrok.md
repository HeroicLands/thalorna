## Overview {#overview}

**Tvarnidrok** is the oldest of the seafaring houses that sit on the [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]] of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]. It is of [[lore-nordheimnclt|Nordmen]] stock, and its name is [[skill-nordmalng|Nordmal]]: a line that can recite its forebears back to the name-giver who founded it, which Tvarnidrok does at every feast. Its hall stands at the top of the old city of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]], and its hulls berth along the inner quay of [[place-oldmole|the Old Mole]], in the deepest water in the harbor.

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

The head of the house is Knilthorv Tvarnidrok, who sits on the Council and leads the old houses' party there. He means to be Harbormaster at the election of 720, and on last year's census he has the voices to win. The house sails about a dozen hulls of its own and holds shares in as many more, most of them in the Malagnan trade: iron, amber and timber from [[place-gnarthborg|Gnarthborg]], and wine and cloth north in return.

## Holdings {#holdings}

- **The deep berths** on the inner quay of the Old Mole, which the house has held longer than the Council has kept a census roll.
- **Its hall** in the old city, and the vineyards and upland grazing below [Hlomfell]{.name data-type="place/feature"} that the South Shore families graze on shares.
- **The largest share of the book** of [the Syndicate of the Two Ships]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"}, the eldest of [[affiliation-bondsyndcts|the Bond Syndicates]].
- **The Malagnan marriage.** [Hlurthann II]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of [[affiliation-kingdomlgn|Malagna]] married one of the crown's daughters into the house in the early seventh century, and Tvarnidrok has had the run of the Gnarthborg yards since.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

**The office.** Knilthorv wants the Harbormaster's office, and [[affiliation-hsenorodari|the Norodari]] are buying hulls all winter to deny him the voices.

**The census rule.** Tvarnidrok holds that a hull bought in the winter should not count at the spring census until it has made a voyage for its house. The rule would leave the Norodari's new hulls off the roll, and Knilthorv has asked the Harbormaster to apply it.

**The Gull's loss.** The Syndicate of the Gull has asked the Two Ships to share its autumn losses, and Tvarnidrok, which holds the Two Ships' book, would rather the Gull paid nothing for the Norodari hulls at all.

## History as Told {#history}

The house recites its line to the Nordmen who first tied up in Kalipsa's harbor, and it says it was old when the Council first sat. It fought on the winning side of the civil war of about 470 AF that gave the Harbormaster a single term, and four of its heads have held the office since. Its Malagnan marriage brought it the northern trade, and the house has stayed out of every Nordmen quarrel since with the care of a house that has kin on both sides.

## Arms {#arms}

Two black ships on a gray field, keel to keel, for the two things the house counts as one.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The rule unwritten.** Knilthorv Tvarnidrok wants a master arbitrator to recite a verdict that supports the census rule, and needs someone to find one who remembers such a verdict.
