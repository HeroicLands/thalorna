## Overview

**The Bond Syndicates** are the underwriters of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]. A syndicate is a company of merchants who write bonds on hulls and cargoes: a house that sends a hull to sea pays a syndicate a premium, and if the hull or its cargo is lost at sea the syndicate pays the bond's full sum. The syndicates write bonds for any hull that sails from Kalipsa and for many that never touch it, on the coasts of the north, the bays of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] and [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia]] and the routes to the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]]. They are celebrated across the northern shipping world, and merchants as far away as [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Viléria]] buy shares of their bond books for the premiums.

The syndicates live by the [[lore-sealaw|Sea-Law]], which says when a bond is paid and when it is not, and their power is the reverse of that law: a house that defies a verdict of [[place-sealawhall|the Sea-Law Hall]] is barred by every syndicate at once, and a house whose hulls cannot be bonded cannot borrow against them.

## The Syndicates

There are usually five or six syndicates, each known by the sign painted over its counting-room on the quay. The eldest is [the Syndicate of the Two Ships]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"}, whose underwriters are drawn from the old houses and whose book [[affiliation-hsetvarnidrok|Tvarnidrok]] holds the largest share of. [The Syndicate of the Gull]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"} writes most of the bonds on [[place-kalipsarun|the Kalipsa Run]], and [the Syndicate of the Lamp]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"} writes the long voyages to the south. The others are younger and smaller, and a syndicate that pays out on a bad season more than its book can carry is wound up and its underwriters join another.

A bond is written on a slate in the counting-room and copied onto a sealed paper for the house, and the syndicate's sign and the underwriter's mark make it good. Its money is kept with the [[affiliation-danvar|Danvar]] moneylenders of the city, and a bond's sum is paid in [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian]] paper as readily as in coin.

## How It Is Run

Each syndicate answers for its own bonds and keeps its own book. The syndics meet together twice a year, after the spring census and after the autumn grain season, to fix the year's rates on the common routes. They act together on one other thing: when a house or a master defies a verdict of the Sea-Law Hall, the syndics strike the name from their roll, and no syndicate writes for it until the verdict is honored.

## The Present {#present}

The autumn has been the worst in years. Fog on the eastern approaches took five hulls in the grain season, three of them [[affiliation-hsenorodari|Norodari]] hulls bonded by the Syndicate of the Gull. The Gull has refused the Norodari bonds before the loss is heard at the Sea-Law Hall, on the rule that a bond is not paid where the master caused the loss by refusing a pilot, and its underwriters say that paying all five losses in full would wind the syndicate up.

The quarrel has a second edge. If the Gull pays, the Norodari can buy hulls before the spring census and carry their voices into the Harbormaster's election, and the old houses who hold the Two Ships' book would rather it did not. The Gull's syndic, [[being-vraldkrldd|Vraldselda Kruldidrok]], has asked the Two Ships to take a share of the loss, and the Two Ships has not answered.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The Gull's book.** The Gull's underwriters need to know how much of their book has been sold to buyers abroad before word of the losses reaches them, and how many of those buyers will sell.
- **The two bonds that were paid.** The Gull paid the bonds on the two other hulls lost in the fog without a hearing, and the Norodari want to know why.
