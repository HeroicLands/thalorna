## Overview {#overview}

The Captains' Council is the governing body of the city-state of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]], and it sits in the Council chamber above the customs house on the quay of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]]. Its members are the heads of the seafaring houses, and each house's weight in it is the tonnage of the hulls it brings to the census each spring. It elects the Harbormaster, decides war and peace, sets the tariffs and the tonnage levy that pays for the navy, charters the guilds, and holds the [[lore-sealaw|Sea-Law]] in the sense that it alone may change a rule the arbitrators keep.

Its present is the election of 720. [[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]]'s term as Harbormaster ends at the spring sitting, and the two houses that want the office are counting the same hulls.

## Who Sits {#who-sits}

Every house that brings enough tonnage to the spring census holds one seat, and its head sits in it. About a dozen great houses and twice as many lesser ones sit in a usual year. A house whose fleet falls below the census floor loses its seat at the next sitting, and a house that rises above it takes one. The city calls this its merit, and the merit it means is the talent to make a ship pay.

Harbormaster
: [[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]], head of [[affiliation-hsethraskumak|Thraskumak]], elected at the spring sitting of 717 for the three years to the spring of 720.

The Councilors who decide the Council's majority are these:

- [[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]], the Harbormaster, whose house builds as many hulls as it sails and belongs to neither party.
- [[being-knilthtvrn|Knilthorv Tvarnidrok]], head of [[affiliation-hsetvarnidrok|Tvarnidrok]], the oldest house on the Council and the head of the old houses' party, who wants the office next.
- [[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]], head of [[affiliation-hsenorodari|the Norodari]], the house that has risen furthest in a decade, who leads the newer houses and means to stop him.

The Past Harbormasters speak first after the Harbormaster on every question, and the eldest living, [[being-skruldhvrn|Skruldthann Hvarnumak]], Harbormaster from 714 to 717, speaks before the others. Their precedence is honor, and their voices are only their houses' voices.

Clerk of the Council
: [[being-mirvirnlth|Mirvir Nulthumak]], a salaried officer of the city-state and not a Councilor, who keeps the census roll, the record of every sitting, the Council's charters and its seal.

## How It Decides {#how-it-decides}

The Customs Officer takes the tonnage census each spring, by counting every hull a house owns or holds a share in at its berth or on the customs roll, and the Clerk of the Council enters the totals on the census roll. The Harbormaster certifies the roll at the spring sitting, and the voices of every question that year follow it. A house casts all its voices together, as its head decides.

The Harbormaster calls the sittings, keeps the order of business and puts the question; the Clerk counts. A question carries on a majority of the voices cast. The Harbormaster's own election is held at the spring sitting of a term's last year, after the census is certified, and the outgoing Harbormaster may stand for nothing. The single term and the bar on the next are the rule the houses took after the civil war of about 470 AF, and no Council since has set either aside.

Between sittings the Harbormaster holds the harbor, the forts, the customs and the navy and acts on the Council's standing orders. A question the standing orders do not cover waits for the Council, or the Harbormaster calls a sitting for it.

## What It Is Fighting About {#present}

**The office.** Knilthorv Tvarnidrok has the old houses behind him and the voices to win on last year's census. Kubasor Norodari has bought hulls all year to change that, and the newer houses follow him because a Tvarnidrok Harbormaster would hold the levy on the old houses' terms.

**The lost hulls.** Three Norodari hulls went down in the autumn fog on [[place-kalipsarun|the Kalipsa Run]]. If [[affiliation-bondsyndcts|the Bond Syndicates]] pay their bonds, Norodari can replace them before the census; if the arbitrators of [[place-sealawhall|the Sea-Law Hall]] find that the masters lost them by refusing a pilot, the syndicates pay nothing and Norodari comes to the census three hulls short. Both parties have taken counsel among the arbitrators, and both say the other has tried to buy one.

**The census rule.** The old houses hold that a hull bought in the winter should not be counted at a spring census until it has made a voyage for its house. The rule is unwritten, the Clerk's roll has never applied it, and the Harbormaster, who certifies the roll, has not said how she will count.

**The levy.** The tonnage levy has risen three years running to pay for the squadrons, and both parties promise to lower it. Hrundselda has told the Council that it cannot be lowered while the squadrons sail.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

Nobody outside the houses addresses the Council. A petition reaches it through a Councilor who will speak it, through the Clerk if it concerns a charter, or through the Harbormaster's office on the quay if it concerns the harbor, the customs or the navy. A party meets the Council in the long room of the customs house below the chamber, where captains wait to be called, factors wait for their captains, and the Clerk's men copy the census roll for anyone who pays the fee.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The count.** [[being-hrundthrsk|The Harbormaster]] wants a census taken by someone in neither party's pay, and the Customs Officer's tally-men are in both.
- **One arbitrator.** [[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]] wants proof that the old houses have bought an arbitrator before the hearing on the lost hulls.
