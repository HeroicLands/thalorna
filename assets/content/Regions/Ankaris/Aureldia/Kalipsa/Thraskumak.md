## Overview {#overview}

**Thraskumak** is the shipwrights' house of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]. Its name is [[skill-nordmalng|Nordmal]] for a clan with a craft that others come to it for, and the craft is the keel: its yards on the slips below the old city of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] build the hulls of the city's navy and many of its merchant hulls, and the house sails as many hulls as it builds. Its head, [[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]], is the present Harbormaster.

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

Hrundselda Thraskumak heads the house and holds the Harbormaster's office to the spring sitting of 720. The house belongs to neither of the Council's parties: it builds for the old houses and the new alike, and it builds the navy's hulls at the price the tonnage levy pays. Every Councilor owes the yards money or a hull, and the house is careful never to say so.

## Holdings {#holdings}

- **The yards**, four slips and a timber pond below the old city, with the ropewalk and the sail-lofts behind them.
- **The navy contract**, by which the yards build and refit the squadrons' hulls for the city at the levy's price.
- **Its hulls**, about eight of its own sailing for the house, and shares in the hulls it has built for others.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

**The Norodari order.** [[affiliation-hsenorodari|The Norodari]] have ordered two hulls from the yards for delivery before the spring census, and the Harbormaster who certifies the census is the head of the house that is building them. [[affiliation-hsetvarnidrok|Tvarnidrok]] says she will count her own hulls, and she says the yards build for anyone who pays.

**The levy.** The navy's refits are the yards' steadiest work, and a lower levy means fewer of them. The house's interest and the Harbormaster's insistence that the levy cannot be lowered are, for the old houses, one argument.

## History as Told {#history}

The house began as shipwrights to the old houses and took a seat on the Council when it began to sail what it built. It built the first hulls of the navy in the years of [[lore-southrnfleets|the Southern Fleets]], and it has built for the city ever since. Two of its heads have been Harbormaster before Hrundselda.

## Arms {#arms}

A keel in white on a blue field, its forefoot on a line of shingle.

## Hooks {#hooks}
