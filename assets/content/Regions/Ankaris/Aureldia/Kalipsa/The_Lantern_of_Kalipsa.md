## Overview {#overview}

**The Lantern of Kalipsa** is the seamark tower on the northern headland of the [[place-calypsaisle|Isle of Kalipsa]], above the harbor of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] and the rocks of [The Hounds]{.name data-type="place/feature"}. It is a square tower of gray island stone four stories high, with a lantern room of iron and thick green glass on its roof, and its beacon burns olive oil in a ring of brass lamps behind a polished mirror. The light is a clear, steady yellow, and it shows from the open sea long before the island does.

It has been relit every night for as long as anyone remembers. A master on [[place-kalipsarun|the Kalipsa Run]] steers for it, and a pilot reads the Hounds by where the light stands against the headland.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The tower and the light belong to the city, and the [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]] pays for the oil. The keeping of the light is hereditary in one family, [Druskaruk]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, whose name says the charge in [[skill-nordmalng|Nordmal]]. The lantern-keeper's office carries real civic weight: the keeper walks behind the Harbormaster at the spring sitting, and a keeper's word on what the light showed on a given night is taken at the [[place-sealawhall|Sea-Law Hall]] without a second witness.

The present keeper is [[being-snurvdrskr|Snurvinda Druskaruk]], who has kept it for twenty years with her two sons and a granddaughter learning the lamps.

## Layers {#history}

**The first light.** The city's tradition says the first light on the headland was a fire of driftwood kept by the families of the old harbor, and that the Druskaruk were the family who kept it. The tower's lowest course is older than its upper stories, and the masons say so.

**The tower.** The present tower was raised on the old base when the houses built their navy, in the years of [[lore-southrnfleets|the Southern Fleets]], and the lantern room was glazed then with glass brought from [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia]].

**The olive oil.** The beacon has burned olive oil from the south since the tower was raised, because it burns clear and steady and leaves the mirror clean. Fish oil burns redder and smokes the glass, and the keepers have refused it ever since.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The oil is the quarrel. Olive oil from the south has risen in price two years running, and the Council's treasurers want the cost of the light cut. [Glervhavan]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} on [[place-calypsashore|the South Shore]] renders lamp oil from fish and seal, and its elder, [[being-thruldsklf|Thruldmil Skulfendik]], has offered it to the city at a third of the price.

Snurvinda Druskaruk has refused it. A fish-oil flame burns redder than the Lantern's, and she holds that a master who has steered by the clear yellow light all his life might take a red light on the headland for a ship on fire, or for another headland, and put his hull on the Hounds. [[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]] has kept the olive oil coming at the old price out of the harbor revenue, and the treasurers have told the Council so.

## Dangerous Neighbours {#dangerous-neighbours}

- **The Hounds**, below the headland, which have taken more hulls than any raider.
- **The cliff path** to the tower, which is narrow, unrailed and wet, and is walked by the keepers in every weather.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

The tower stands at the end of a walled path along the headland from the old city's upper gate, a half-hour's climb. The keepers live in the two lower stories. The third holds the oil store, a cistern and the spare lamps, and the fourth the keepers' watch-room, with a ladder through the ceiling to the lantern room. The lamps are lit at sunset and trimmed every two hours through the night, and the mirror is cleaned every morning.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The oil cart.** The next oil cargo from the south is overdue, and Snurvinda Druskaruk wants to know whether it was delayed or bought up before it reached the quay.
- **The trial lamp.** The Glervhavan elder wants a cask of the village's oil carried up to the tower and burned beside the olive oil for one night, before witnesses from the Council, and the keeper will not have it in the lantern room.
