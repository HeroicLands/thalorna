## Overview {#overview}

**The Sea-Law Hall** stands on the quay of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] between the customs house and the root of [[place-oldmole|the Old Mole]], and it is where the arbitrators of the [[lore-sealaw|Sea-Law]] hear the city's disputes over ships, shares, cargoes, wages and bonds. It is a long hall of dressed gray stone with a slate roof and tall windows on the harbor side, and its doors stand open on every hearing day, so that anyone on the quay may come in and listen.

Its verdicts bind nobody outside the city. They are honored across half the northern shipping world all the same, because no captain or house can afford what defying one costs: no syndicate in Kalipsa writes its bonds again, and no Kalipsan factor carries its goods.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The hall belongs to the city, and the arbitrators keep it. The master arbitrators, the eldest of the caste, fix the order of hearings, examine the young arbitrators, and keep the hall's one written record, a roll of the parties and the dates of every hearing, with no word of the verdicts, which are kept by recitation. The [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]] pays for the building's repair and has no other say in what is done there.

## Layers {#history}

**The open quay.** The arbitrators heard cases on the quay itself before there was a hall, under an awning beside the customs house, and the hall's long shape follows the stretch of quay they used.

**The hall.** The present hall was built after [[lore-calypsacivilwar|the civil war]] of about 470 AF, when the houses that ended the war agreed that the Sea-Law would be heard under a roof that belonged to no house. The hall's three chairs, at the landward end, are older than the building and were carried in from the quay.

**The roll.** The roll of hearings runs back unbroken to the hall's building, and the master arbitrators can recite a verdict for almost every name on it.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The hall's next great hearing is the loss of the three Norodari hulls in the autumn fog on [[place-kalipsarun|the Kalipsa Run]]. [[affiliation-hsenorodari|The Norodari]] claim the full sum of the hulls' bonds from [[affiliation-bondsyndcts|the Bond Syndicates]]. The syndicates refuse, on the rule that a bond is not paid where the master caused the loss by refusing a pilot, and the pilots' guild under its Guild Master, [[being-knurvhnrvn|Knurvorv Hnirvendik]], says that none of the three masters took one. The Norodari say the pilots' boat was not on its station.

The hearing matters to more than the parties. If the syndicates pay, the Norodari can buy hulls before the spring census and carry their voices into the Harbormaster's election. Each party has chosen its arbitrator, and the two arbitrators have not agreed on the third. The city says that both great parties of the Council have tried to buy one, and the master arbitrators have said nothing.

## Dangerous Neighbours {#dangerous-neighbours}

- **The quay on a hearing day.** A great hearing fills the hall and the quay outside it with crews of both parties, and the watch stands at the doors.
- **A party's displeasure.** An arbitrator who gives a verdict a great house dislikes keeps his reputation and may lose his custom.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

The hall is one long room. The three chairs stand on a low step at the landward end under the window that looks up at the old city, the parties stand before them, and benches for listeners run along both walls to the harbor doors. A witness speaks from a stone set in the floor before the step, and anyone who speaks from it is held to have spoken under the Sea-Law. Behind the step a door leads to the master arbitrators' rooms and the chest where the roll is kept.

A stranger may come in and listen to any hearing, and a stranger who has a dispute with a Kalipsan house may bring it here, pay his half of the arbitrators' fee, and be heard as a citizen would be.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The third arbitrator.** The two chosen arbitrators cannot agree on the third, and each party wants to know which of the master arbitrators the other has approached.
- **A witness from the bay.** The Norodari need a witness from [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]] who saw the three hulls leave the outer quay and saw whether they asked for a pilot's mark, and the witness has not come over.
