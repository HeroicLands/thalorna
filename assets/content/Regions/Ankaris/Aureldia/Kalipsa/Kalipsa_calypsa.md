## Overview {#overview}

Kalipsa is a free city-state on the [[place-calypsaisle|Isle of Kalipsa]], a small, steep-sided island in the cold sea seventy-five miles off the northern coast of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], west of the mouth of [[place-liserebay|Lísere Bay]]. The island is about fifteen miles across at its widest, from the western cliffs to the harbor of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] on the east coast. It holds the city, its terraced vineyards, the upland grazing below the ridge, and the fishing villages of [[place-calypsashore|the South Shore]], and some forty-five thousand people. The island lies across the sea-lane between the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] and the coasts of Élavendre and the south, and its harbor is one of the best-sheltered anchorages in the north of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]]. The galleys that use it tie the north and west of the continent together more completely than any treaty could.

The city-state is nobody's vassal. It pays no tribute to the Nordmen kingdoms, owes no fealty to [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Élavendre]], and answers to no [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Arcane Order]] authority beyond what its [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]] chooses to tolerate. That freedom is its principal product, and every other good that passes through its warehouses is, in some sense, paying for it.

Its people are [[lore-kalipsanclt|Kalipsan]]. [[skill-nordmalng|Nordmal]] is the working language of the docks, the old city and the villages; [[skill-valtarlng|Valtari]], a trade pidgin with deep Helionite roots, is spoken in the markets and among the younger merchant families; and a traveler from the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]] or the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]] is understood without much difficulty anywhere on the island.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The **Captains' Council** governs: an assembly of the heads of the seafaring houses, each house's voices weighted by the tonnage it brings to the census each spring. The Council elects a **Harbormaster** from among its members for a single three-year term, and the Harbormaster may not serve the next. Between the Council's sittings the Harbormaster holds the harbor, the forts, the customs and the navy. The Master of the Watch commands the guard and the forts' garrisons under the Harbormaster, and the Customs Officer collects the dues on every hull and cargo under the Harbormaster, and the dues are the city's main revenue.

Kalipsi civic life is dominated by the shipping houses, perhaps a dozen great families and twice as many lesser ones, whose fortunes rise and fall with their fleets. A family that loses three ships in a season may be elbowed from the Council within the year, and a family that opens a new trade may enter it. The mobility gives Kalipsi politics an unusually meritocratic flavor for Ankaris, though merit here means, specifically, the talent to make a ship pay.

Outside the Council chamber, power is diffuse. Guilds of shipwrights, chandlers, ropemakers and pilots each run their own affairs under charters granted by the Council. The villages of the South Shore choose their own elders by custom and have no seat on the Council.

## Current Situation {#present}

[[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]] of [[affiliation-hsethraskumak|Thraskumak]] has held the Harbormaster's office since the spring of 717, and her term ends at the spring sitting of 720. The election is the city-state's quarrel. [[being-knilthtvrn|Knilthorv Tvarnidrok]] of [[affiliation-hsetvarnidrok|Tvarnidrok]] leads the old houses and has the voices to win on last year's census. [[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]] of [[affiliation-hsenorodari|the Norodari]] leads the newer houses and has bought and ordered hulls all winter to take them away. The loss of three Norodari hulls in the autumn fog is before [[place-sealawhall|the Sea-Law Hall]], and whether [[affiliation-bondsyndcts|the Bond Syndicates]] must pay their bonds decides whether the Norodari come to the census strong or short.

Under the election lies money. The tonnage levy that pays for the navy has risen three years running, and the houses that pay most of it want it lowered. The Harbormaster holds that it cannot be lowered while the squadrons sail. On the South Shore the villages and the city are at odds over the water of the village springs after a dry summer.

## Law {#law}

Disputes between merchant houses are settled by arbitration under the [[lore-sealaw|Sea-Law]], a body of commercial custom with no single textual source but a dense and consistent oral tradition maintained by the city's small, prickly caste of professional arbitrators. Its verdicts, given at the Sea-Law Hall, are not binding outside the city, and in practice they are honored across half the northern shipping world because no captain can afford what defying them costs. Only the Captains' Council may change a rule of it. Theft, violence and murder are tried by the city's courts under the Harbormaster's officers.

The people cultivate a reputation for cheerful amorality in commerce and a surprising severity in matters of personal honor, and the law follows them. A Kalipsi captain who breaks a contract pays under the Sea-Law and may be pitied; a wrong done to a guest is judged twice as heavily as the same wrong done to a citizen, and a captain who breaks a guest's trust is not welcome in the harbor again.

## The Fleet {#arms}

Kalipsa keeps a **navy out of all proportion to its size**, and it is the reason the city has stayed nobody's vassal.

The shipping houses maintain it jointly, on a levy assessed by tonnage, and it is commanded by officers who are professionals rather than gentlemen, a distinction Kalipsi make loudly and Aureldian nobles find offensive. The [[affiliation-hsethraskumak|Thraskumak]] yards build and refit its hulls at the levy's price. It is not built to hold a line against an empire and could not. It is built to make raiding Kalipsa, or the sea-lanes Kalipsa's trade uses, cost more than it returns.

Against the **Nordmen** and the raiders of the northern lands this has worked for a very long time. The city pays no tribute to any northern kingdom and is emphatic about the difference between not paying tribute and not being asked. The wider consequence matters to its neighbors: Kalipsa's squadrons sit across the approaches to Lísere Bay, and northern Élavendre enjoys a degree of protection that it neither pays for nor formally acknowledges, and would miss immediately.

Everything the city has rests on the fleet, and the fleet rests on trade paying for it. A bad decade of tariffs is a naval problem, and the shipping houses know it.

## Commerce and Currency {#commerce}

The harbor is the economy. Kalipsa levies modest tariffs on cargo landed, serious tariffs on cargo transshipped, and crushing tariffs on cargo warehoused for longer than a season. The last is deliberately punitive, to force capital through the city rather than letting it accumulate. The turnover underwrites the Council's treasury and the city's celebrated Bond Syndicates, whose bonds are bought by merchants as far away as [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Viléria]].

The island itself produces little. There is fishing, a valued trade in the purple-red dye gathered from shellfish on the rocks of the South Shore, and enough rough grazing on the upper slopes to keep a sparse stock of goats and sheep. The terraced vineyards above the city make a wine that, by the admission of its drinkers, is indifferent. The island is steep, thin-soiled and short of fresh water, and **Kalipsa eats what it buys**: grain from the Élavendri mainland, timber and furs from the Nordlands, fine cloth from Provénzia and the southern [[place-aureldirgn|Aureldian]] courts, metalwork from [[affiliation-orashion|Órashion]], and the luxury goods of the east that trickle north through [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian]] and Vilérian intermediaries.

Kalipsa uses the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian currency]] (Solden, Ardin and Ogdel) as its standard, with full access to the paper-script system through [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]]-member moneylenders chartered under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]]. It is a post-imperial commonwealth member: it takes part fully in the trade and banking of the Vilérian system and is subject to neither imperial governance nor imperial taxation.

## Faith {#faith}

The faiths of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] are the city-state's religions, and the temple of [[affiliation-pozeras|the Faith of Pozeras]], the Wayfarer's, on the quay is the one every master visits. Weddings mix Nordmen drinking-songs with Aureldian hymns. The old Nordmen families also keep the gods of the north, and crews at sea honor the [[affiliation-thrunvald|Faith of Thrunvald]].

**The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]** maintains a small factor's house on the island but no chapterhouse and no licensing authority. Kalipsa's official position is that the Arcane Order's writ stops at the waterline; in practice, Arcane Order agents who conduct themselves discreetly are tolerated, and those who do not are asked to leave.

## Holdings {#holdings}

The city-state's domain is the whole [[place-calypsaisle|Isle of Kalipsa]]:

- **[[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]]**, the city and harbor, about 28,000 people.
- **[[place-calypsashore|The South Shore]]**, the fishing villages and upland steadings, about 17,000.
- **[[place-oldmole|The Old Mole]]**, the stone harbor mole, older than any dating the city will admit to, carved on several stones with characters no living scholar can read.
- **[[place-lantrnklps|The Lantern of Kalipsa]]**, the seamark tower on the northern headland, whose olive-oil beacon is relit every night and whose keeper's office is hereditary.
- **[[place-sealawhall|The Sea-Law Hall]]**, where the arbitrators hear the Sea-Law.
- **[[place-kalipsarun|The Kalipsa Run]]**, the sea road to Sarmelis on Lísere Bay, which its squadrons patrol and its pilots bring home.

## History {#history}

**The mole and the old houses.** The Old Mole is the oldest work on the island, and the city's tradition says refugees from a forgotten Helionite colony built it. Nordmen fishers and traders settled the harbor and gave the old city its tongue, and the oldest houses, Tvarnidrok first among them, recite their lines to those settlers.

**The Empire.** Under the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] the port took the guild charter of the Tevran Maldor during the western conquests, paid the Empire its dues, and kept its own Council. Through the Tribute Peace, from about 418 to 512, the west paid and was left alone, and Kalipsa with it.

**The civil war.** About 470 AF a Harbormaster who would not give up the office brought on a civil war among the houses, fought in the harbor and the streets of the old city. The houses that won it gave the Harbormaster a single term with no second to follow it, built the Sea-Law Hall so that the Sea-Law would be heard under a roof that belonged to no house, and have honored the rule since without exception.

**The navy.** When [[lore-southrnfleets|the Southern Fleets]] of Nordheim went south every summer from 519 to 530, the houses built the navy on the tonnage levy, hung the harbor chain from the mole's head and raised the Lantern's tower. The fleets burned Élavendre's north shore and passed the island by.

**The free port.** [[lore-crownssettle|The Settlement of the Crowns]], about 619, left the Aureldian crowns free of imperial governance and tax and inside the Vilérian note system, and Kalipsa with them. [Hlurthann II]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of [[affiliation-kingdomlgn|Malagna]], who reigned from about 600 to 632, married the crown's daughters into the island families, and the old houses have had the run of the Malagnan yards since. Kalipsa has stayed out of every Nordmen dynastic quarrel of the last three centuries.

**The present Council.** The fur trade through [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]] raised the Norodari to a seat within the last decade, and the newer houses that rose with them now contest the old houses' hold on the Council. The Council elected Hrundselda Thraskumak, of neither party, Harbormaster in the spring of 717.

## Relations {#relations}

[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Élavendre]] is Kalipsa's nearest mainland neighbor and largest single trading partner. The relationship is warm, cordial and quietly transactional: the Élavendri crown has never seriously attempted to annex the island and Kalipsa has never seriously contemplated resisting if it did. In practice the two realms negotiate as peers, and a steady stream of Élavendri younger sons and restless merchant families settles on the island each generation. Much of Élavendre's northern trade passes through Kalipsa, including goods nobody wishes to declare at the crown's tally-house in Sarmelis.

**The Nordmen kingdoms**, [[affiliation-kingdomlgn|Malagna]] especially, provide many of Kalipsa's sailors, much of its shipbuilding timber, and a disproportionate share of its old-money families. Nordmal is still the working tongue of the docks for this reason. Relations with the Nordmen courts are friendly in trade and carefully neutral in politics.

**[[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]]**, the far island to the west, is a trading partner across open water, and the Malagnan hulls that sail to its ports call at Kalipsa too.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The census.** [[being-hrundthrsk|The Harbormaster]] wants a census of the spring's hulls taken by strangers in neither party's pay.
- **The hearing.** [[affiliation-hsenorodari|The Norodari]] need a witness from Sarmelis before the Sea-Law Hall hears the loss of their hulls.
- **The springs.** The city's water-carters want an escort to the locked spring-house of Vrellnes on the South Shore.
