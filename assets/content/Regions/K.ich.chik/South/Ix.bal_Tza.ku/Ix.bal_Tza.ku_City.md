## Overview

Come down the great southern white road from [[place-neokbaatergn|Ne'ok Ba'ate]] and the mountains of **Ix'bal Tza'ku** fill the horizon: a range running north to south, with snow on the peaks, fortress-cities and ceremonial centers on the heights, and jungle in the valleys below. The seat of the realm is a city of about 60,000, in the jewel of the southern K'ich'chik territories, a realm whose cities rival the great ones of [[affiliation-ixilankul|Ix'ilankul]] in grandeur and influence. The name means "Jaguar's House" in the high speech, and the people revere the spotted predator as an emblem of divine power.

## Who Governs

Ix'bal Tza'ku is the uppermost reach of the south, fully integrated into the unified [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni]] priesthood. The celestial calendar governs planting and harvest, pilgrimage and sacrifice, and a message from the Ch'alix Tq'an reaches the realm in three to four days along the highland road. The priesthood's control of trade is absolute: tribute flows constantly to the temples, no major transaction goes unwatched, and the priests take their religious levy from every one. The highland cities control the river systems, and the rulers of the river-junction city [[place-nepaxel|Ne' P'axel]] exact tribute from merchants passing through to the coast.

## What It Sells

The highlands export worked gold and silver, in ceremonial vessels, jewelry and temple adornments, along with copper implements and finished textiles. The valleys send cacao, fruit, jaguar pelts, medicinal herbs and the feathers of the quetzal and macaw, and rubber tapped from jungle trees. The most valuable cacao and the rarest feathers go north to the great pyramid cities. Rivers are sacred highways, and those who travel them make offerings to Xilan Ix'lan, god of voyages.
