## Overview

Southern K'ich'chik is where the ordered K'ich'chik world runs out. The realms of [[affiliation-ixbaltzaku|Ish'bal Tsa'ku]] and [[affiliation-kuxiixbal|K'ushi Ish'bal]] are fully part of K'ich'chik civilization, with cities, agricultural estates and the priesthood's _ish'lan ch'upul_ (white roads); beyond [[affiliation-qapteltzakl|Q'aptel Tsa'kul]], the road ends at the wastes of [[place-xelemixbalrgn|Shelem Ish'bal]]. About 3,500,000 people live in the south.

## Character

The south descends in four steps. Ish'bal Tsa'ku is mountains, river valleys and jungle, rich in metal, cacao and feathers. K'ushi Ish'bal is a thin-aired plateau of cold grasslands and a sacred lake, its cities garrison-like and its economy built around provisioning travelers. Q'aptel Tsa'kul is a poisoned threshold of fortress-temples where those bound south buy guides, provisions and blessings. Past it lie blasted rock, ash and ice, where no one rules and the priesthood only watches.

The Speaking Road thins as the land climbs and falls, and it falls silent beyond K'ushi Ish'bal: the last runner's cord is decoded at the frontier garrison city of [[place-qptlulneok|Q'aptel'ul Ne'ok]], and what lies further south depends on the courage of individual messengers. For warriors and priests the wastes are a proving ground, and surviving them is a mark of worth before the gods.
