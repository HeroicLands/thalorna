## Overview

Southern K'ich'chik is where the ordered K'ich'chik world runs out. The realms of [[affiliation-balamtzaku|Ba'alam Tza'ku]] and [[affiliation-kuxibalam|K'uxi Balam]] are fully part of K'ich'chik civilization, with cities, agricultural estates and the priesthood's sacbe roads; beyond [[affiliation-kawiltzakl|K'awiil Tza'kul]], the road ends at the wastes of [[place-tzikinbalmrgn|Tz'ikin Ba'alam]]. About 3,500,000 people live in the south.

## Character

The south descends in four steps. Ba'alam Tza'ku is mountains, river valleys and jungle, rich in metal, cacao and feathers. K'uxi Balam is a thin-aired plateau of cold grasslands and a sacred lake, its cities garrison-like and its economy built around provisioning travelers. K'awiil Tza'kul is a poisoned threshold of fortress-temples where those bound south buy guides, provisions and blessings. Past it lie blasted rock, ash and ice, where no one rules and the priesthood only watches.

The Speaking Road thins as the land climbs and falls, and it falls silent beyond K'uxi Balam: the last runner's cord is decoded at the frontier garrison city of [[place-kawilulkik|K'awi'il'ul Ki'ik]], and what lies further south depends on the courage of individual messengers. For warriors and priests the wastes are a proving ground, and surviving them is a mark of worth before the gods.
