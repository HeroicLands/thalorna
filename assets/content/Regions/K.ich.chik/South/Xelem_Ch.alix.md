## Overview

Shelem Ch’alish, "The House of the Dead Bird," is the final outpost of civilized [[place-kchchkcntnnt|K’ich’chik]] before the forsaken wastes of [[place-xelemixbalrgn|Shelem Ish’bal]]. The settlement is a temple-fortress of forbidding aspect, built to endure and to serve as both blessing-place and final supply depot for those venturing into the abyss. Few permanently live here save the priesthood and their essential servants; most inhabitants are transient—warriors, priests, and desperate individuals gathering supplies and spiritual fortification before departing southward.

## Location and Geography

Shelem Ch’alish stands at the boundary between [[affiliation-qapteltzakl|Q’aptel Tsa’kul]] and the true wastes, built on a high plateau with clear sight-lines in all directions toward the south. The architecture is entirely defensive and ritualistic—high walls, watchtowers, and a central pyramid-temple of imposing grandeur. The landscape around the city is already hostile; toxic mineral flats and geothermal vents mark the transition zone, and the priesthood uses these natural features as outer defenses.

## Religious and Functional Role

Shelem Ch’alish serves as the staging ground for all major expeditions into [[place-xelemixbalrgn|Shelem Ish’bal]]. The priesthood here performs elaborate blessing ceremonies, consults auguries and celestial omens, and supplies final provisions. Two deities are worshipped with particular intensity: [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Ts’uqil Ish’bal]], mistress of death, who receives blood offerings for the safe passage of the departed, and [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|P’uqil Ch’akun]], god of storms and chaos, whose favor is desperately sought before southern ventures.

## The Priesthood

The priesthood of Shelem Ch’alish is known to be among the most severe and uncompromising in [[place-kchchkcntnnt|K’ich’chik]]. These priests are accustomed to dealing with death, sacrifice, and the darkest aspects of the cosmic order. They are said to possess secret knowledge about the wastes and the ruins said to lie buried there, though they share this knowledge only with the most worthy (or the most desperate) of those seeking to venture south.
