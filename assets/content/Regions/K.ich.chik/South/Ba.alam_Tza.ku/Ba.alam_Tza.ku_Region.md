## Overview

Ba'alam Tza'ku Region is the land of [[affiliation-balamtzaku|Ba'alam Tza'ku]], and lies within [[place-sthrnkchchk|Southern K'ich'chik]] on the [[place-kchchkcntnnt|K'ich'chik Continent]].

Ba'alam Tza'ku is the jewel of the southern [[place-kchchkcntnnt|K'ich'chik]] territories—a vast realm of soaring mountains, verdant river valleys, and emerald jungle.

## Geography

The backbone of Ba'alam Tza'ku is a mighty mountain range running north-to-south, splitting the region into distinct highland and lowland zones. The high peaks rival those of the far north, crowned with perpetual snows that feed rushing rivers and deep gorges. The highlands support thriving agricultural terraces where quinoa-analog grains, tubers, and hardy maize grow in neat, engineered rows—a testament to centuries of Kichian engineering. These peaks are studded with fortress-cities and ceremonial centers, their temples climbing ever skyward to commune with the celestial powers.

Westward and eastward from the mountains lie vast river valleys, where the climate grows warm and humid. Here, the jungle thrives—dense, verdant, and teeming with life. The rivers themselves are arteries of trade and travel; great canoes laden with goods and tributary goods flow between highland and lowland, linked by well-maintained sacbe roads that cut straight through the jungle canopy. These lowland zones produce cacao, exotic fruits, medicinal herbs, and the rare feathers of precious birds used in priestly regalia. The rivers themselves are sacred highways, and those who travel them must make offerings to [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Xilan Ix'lan]], god of voyages.

The climate shifts from alpine cold in the high peaks to tropical warmth in the valleys. The region experiences two seasons clearly marked: the season of rains (aligned with the Water month of the celestial calendar) and the season of growth. Sudden cloudbursts are common in the lowlands, and the rivers swell with fury during the rainy months.

## Maritime Powers

Though Ba'alam Tza'ku is primarily a mountain and river kingdom, its western frontier opens to great ocean waters where coastal city-states maintain significant maritime interests. The great port city of [[place-tzalamha|Tz'alam Ha']] commands these waters and competes with other coastal settlements for trade dominance.

## Trade

Ba'alam Tza'ku is a nexus of wealth and commerce. The highlands export finely worked metals—gold and silver fashioned into ceremonial vessels, jewelry, and temple adornments—as well as copper implements and finished textiles of extraordinary quality. The valleys and lowlands produce cacao (bitter and precious), exotic fruits, jaguar and other fine pelts, medicinal herbs, and feathers of the Quetzal and macaw. Rubber tapped from jungle trees provides a sticky substance used in ritual and practical applications.

Trade flows in multiple directions: from the highlands down to the coasts, from the coasts back inland, and northward along the great sacbe roads toward [[affiliation-balamkul|Balamkul]] and the central heartland of [[place-kchchkcntnnt|K'ich'chik]]. The most valuable cacao and rarest feathers flow north to feed the appetites of the great pyramid cities. In return, the southern cities receive obsidian (worked by master craftspeople in the volcanic highlands of [[place-hakiahkul|Ha' Ki'ahkul]]), textiles from the northern looms, and the spiritual prestige that comes from access to the central priesthood's most sacred rites.

Markets in the great cities—particularly [[place-hakukul|Ha' K'ukul]] and [[place-tzalamha|Tz'alam Ha']]—bustle with merchant cries in a dozen dialects.
