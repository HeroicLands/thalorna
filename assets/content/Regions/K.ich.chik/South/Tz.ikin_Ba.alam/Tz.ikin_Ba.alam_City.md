## Overview

No one rules **Tz'ikin Ba'alam**. The Land of the Dead Jaguar is the forsaken realm at the southern edge of the continent: blasted rock, ash, fields of ice, geysers of superheated water, and ruins said to lie buried beneath ice and ash. The Itzáni priesthood watches over it from its threshold, at [[place-tzikinkul|Tz'ikin K'ul]], the last outpost. What passes through the wastes is not trade but transformation.

## What a Traveler Is Told

The wastes are a proving ground. Warriors and priests go in to test their worth, and most never return; those who do are marked by what they have learned and suffered, and the pyramid-cities elevate them to positions of great authority or spiritual significance. Water is scarce and often toxic, the plants are twisted and sometimes poisonous, and the fauna is worse. A handful of treasure-seekers loot the ruins of an age that came before the current one, and a glorious death in the wastes is held to open the way to the Eternal Garden of [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|P'iqal Kul'qat]], the creator god.

## The Settlement

The region has a central settlement. How many live in it, and who they are, no traveler has come back to say.
