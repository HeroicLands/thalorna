## Overview

Tz'ikin Ba'alam holds the land of [[place-tzikinbalmrgn|Tz'ikin Ba'alam Region]].

## Maritime Powers

The [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni]] priesthood does not truly rule these wastes—it merely maintains the single great temple-fortress of [[place-tzikinkul|Tz'ikin K'ul]] at the threshold, sending out occasional blessed expeditions and receiving the rare survivor who emerges from the south with knowledge or artifacts of power.

## Trade

The [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni]] priesthood tightly controls what little trade does exist: the selling of provisions and protective amulets to those heading south, and the acquisition of strange materials brought back by survivors—crystalline formations that glow with inner light, metals that do not rust or corrode, organic matter that defies classification. These items command astronomical prices in the northern markets, justified by their rarity and the blood-cost of their acquisition.
