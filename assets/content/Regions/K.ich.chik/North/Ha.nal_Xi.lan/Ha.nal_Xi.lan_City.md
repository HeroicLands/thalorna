## Overview

**Ha'nal Xi'lan** is where the goods of all five northern nations meet, and where K'ich'chik merchants come to buy them. The central settlement of the Great River Lands, about 1,200 people, stands in contested borderlands that belong to no single people: the Tz'okum, the Ha'ik and the Ch'aqun all hold ground there, and the region sees the highest concentration of inter-tribal violence on the frontier. Furs and hides from the No'kin, canoes and water-products from the Ha'ik, herbal medicines and poisons from the Ch'aqun, crafted goods from the Xak'nal and herd products from the Tz'okum all accumulate in its trading posts and enclaves.

## Why Merchants Come

The margins on trade through Ha'nal Xi'lan are large enough to attract merchant families willing to work in contested territory. Southern luxuries, obsidian, dyes, feathers and jade, go north from here and are distributed to the other nations by established traders; fish products, preserved meats and farm goods go south to K'ich'chik. The river system carries the bulk. The Ha'ik, master boatbuilders, move goods across the frontier with an efficiency land traders cannot match. A skilled canoeist can run from the far north to the far south on connected waterways.

## The Valley

The floods deposit rich sediment on the bottomlands, and in spring the bottomlands become shallow swamps thick with waterfowl and fish. In summer and fall the water recedes and leaves open meadows for hunting. Annual spawning runs draw peoples from every direction.
