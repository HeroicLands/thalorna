## Overview

Northern K'ich'chik is the part of the continent the K'ich'chik do not rule. Five tribal nations hold it: [[affiliation-balamkiahk|Ba'alam Ki'ahk]], the Eagle People of the western fjords and mountains; [[affiliation-kiximtzul|K'ixim Tzul]], the Bear People of the boreal forest; [[affiliation-haxaman|Ha' Xaman]], the Coyote People of the grasslands; [[affiliation-xikulchakl|Xik'ul Ch'akal]], the Otter People of the lakes; and [[affiliation-tzumanotun|Tz'uma No'tun]], the Serpent People of the swamps and eastern mountains. About 2,000,000 people live in it.

## Character

K'ich'chik names are all that the south has for these peoples. The nation names are exonyms, given by merchants and border scouts from the animals painted on war-standards and camp-markings, and what the nations call themselves is unknown. They tolerate K'ich'chik traders only at arm's length, at sites and on terms they choose, and they unite against any outside incursion, though they war among themselves. The distances make a conventional campaign nearly impossible.

Two places in the north belong to no nation. [[place-hanalxilanrgn|Ha'nal Xi'lan]] is a region of contested river valleys where the Coyote, Otter and Serpent peoples clash and where the goods of all five nations meet at trading posts. [[place-chakultzklrgn|Ch'akul Tza'kul]], the Thirsty Land, is a desert of sand seas, mesas and salt flats that forms the barrier between the tribal north and K'ich'chik civilization. The Speaking Road does not extend into the north, and furs, amber and freshwater pearls reach the south through irregular border trade.
