## Overview

Ts'ilil Ne'ts'ik, the Island of Swallows, is an island trading port of [[affiliation-neokbaate|Ne'ok Ba'ate]] and one of that realm's greatest city-states. It controls a wide archipelago of small islands and maintains its own small but efficient navy, and the merchant families who hold its port have become immensely wealthy from the traffic through it.

## A Port for Two Realms

The island's trade is not only its own realm's. [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]]'s ships trade through it—as a port Ne'ok Ba'ate holds rather than one K'ankul does—and between Ts'ilil Ne'ts'ik and the harbor of [[place-tzokilkul|Ts'okilkul]] an enormous volume of K'ankul cargo passes: salt fish, bird feathers, exotic woods and luxury resins coming in, books, instruments and carved jade going out. Ne'ok Ba'ate's own traders range further still, and it is through ports like this one that they have made the first contacts with peoples far to the south.
