## Overview

A Ch'ul'bal leading pilgrims up from the lowlands begins the lesson the same way each time: everything in K'ich'chik is measured from here. **Ix'ilankul**, the Seat of the Stars, takes its name from the observatories on its temple summits, and it is the seat of the paramount realm of the [[place-kchchkcntnnt|K'ich'chik]] continent, a city of about 150,000 on a highland plateau of volcanic peaks and terraced valleys, and the realm it governs is the cradle of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni]] faith and home to the K'ul Tq'an, the supreme high priest. The other realms of Central K'ich'chik send tribute here and send their young nobles to its temples for ritual training and priestly initiation.

## Two Authorities

Two kinds of power share the plateau, and a newcomer learns to tell them apart. The K'inmah, the Sun Lords, command the largest tribute networks and the tribute-armies; the K'ul Tq'an and the priesthood hold the calendar, the temples and the Speaking Road, the relay of runners that carries every message in the realm. A K'inmah receives no word from a distant province except through the priests' hands. The line between the two powers is fluid and often contested. An ambitious K'ul Tq'an may engineer the fall of a K'inmah who collects too little tribute or loses a battle, and a strong K'inmah may try to install a high priest he can manage. The struggles are conducted with ritual formality, because undermining the priesthood risks the anger of the gods.

A visitor meets the priesthood's reach in the market, because it levies a religious tribute on all trade.

## The Plateau

The plateau rises between 1,200 and 2,400 meters above the coastal lowlands. Roads of packed limestone, the _ix'lan ch'upul_ (white roads), connect its cities and glow pale in the moonlight. Terraced fields climb the hillsides, fed by seasonal rains from the fifth month to the tenth and by aqueducts, cisterns and canals carved from living stone, and the soil supports far more people than any other region of the heartland. The greatest cities of the plateau are Kul'taq'an, where the K'ul Tq'an traditionally resides and where the holiest precinct in K'ich'chik stands; Ixtal'hun, which trains captive-warriors; and Tza'max, a merchant republic of great market plazas. The plaza markets run on a rotating twenty-day cycle that follows the calendar.

## Trade

Ix'ilankul is the premier trading hub of the heartland. Obsidian from the volcanic slopes goes out as ritual blades, mirrors and ceremonial objects. Cacao from the southern highlands is processed and distributed by Ix'ilankul merchants, who guard the recipes for sacred preparations. Textiles dyed with cochineal and woven with the seals of the K'inmah command prices that only the nobles of tributary states can pay. The most prestigious good is the quetzal feather, worth more than jade or gold. Ix'ilankul's merchants keep a steady, limited supply flowing from the rainforests of [[place-tzikinrgn|Tz'ikin]].
