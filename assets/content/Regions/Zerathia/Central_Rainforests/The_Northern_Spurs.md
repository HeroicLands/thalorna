## Overview {#overview}

**The Northern Spurs** are the highland spurs that descend from the northern crescent toward the [[place-cntrlrnfrsts|Central Rainforests]] of [[place-xerathia|Zerathia]]. The canopy climbs their lower slopes and gives out below the cliffs, and the upper walls take snow and frost in the cold months. The rock is limestone, cave-riddled, and the ground shudders. Rivers cut gorges through the spurs on their way north to [[place-vylarianse|the Vilérian Sea]].

[[place-magarthubad|Magar-thubád]], the hold of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]], stands in one of the massifs of the range, above the forest margin and the cloud forest.
