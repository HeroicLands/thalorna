## Overview {#overview}

The Wenela Tribe is four bands of [[lore-yesomaclt|Yesoma]] in the misty country east of the interior's great rivers, where the canopy holds the morning mist until noon. The bands meet at [[place-wenelacamp|Wenela Camp]] in the honey season and camp apart for the rest of the year.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

The leader is [[being-hirawenela|Hira Wenela]], at whose fire the bands camp at the gatherings. She decides nothing alone. The Caller of the Sogola leads the band of song, and the bands follow the Caller on the nights of a failing season more readily than they follow anyone.

## Range {#range}

The tribe ranges over mist forest a few days' walk across. Its bands hunt with nets, gather nuts, fruit, mushrooms and grubs the year round, and abandon the camp where anyone dies, leaving it to the forest as a _hewo_ that no band camps on again. The dead person's name is not spoken until a child is born who is given it.

## Relations {#relations}

The Wenela are neighbors of the [[affiliation-ngotolatribe|Ngotola Tribe]] and share honey trees with it by an old agreement kept in the memory of the elders. They have no dealings with the river-tribes beyond a trade at the Lihola landing, and they send no gifts to the Court.

## History {#history}

The Wenela tell that the forest fell asleep in the grandparents' time and that the song band woke it, and that the song they sang then is the one they sing now.

## The Present Pressure {#present}

The song band has sung every night for a moon and the hunting has not come back. Hira has been asked at three fires whether the forest has stopped listening to this tribe, and some of the bands have started to camp nearer the Ngotola fire.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The sleeping forest.** The Caller of the Sogola wants a party to carry a gift of fish from the Lihola landing to the song camp, because none of the band may leave it while the song goes on.
- **The honey trees.** Two bands claim the same flowering stand, and the Keeper of the Honey Trees wants a witness from outside the tribe to hear both without a share in it.
- **The fire that moved.** Hira's fire has lost two bands to the Ngotola, and she asks a party to learn what the Ngotola gave them.
