## Overview {#overview}

Lihola Camp is the seat of the [[affiliation-liholatribe|Lihola Tribe]], a row of pole-and-thatch houses raised a little off the ground on the high bank of the [[place-luwabolu|Luwa Bolu]]. The landing below it is where the elders sit through the naming night, where weirs are mended and where a stranger the river brings is fed for three nights and asked nothing. The camp stays in one place for a few years and moves when the river moves, when the garden tires or when someone dies there. About a hundred and fifty people are counted in it in the fish run.
