## Overview {#overview}

The Lihola Tribe is five bands of [[lore-luwandoclt|Luwando]] river-keepers who hold a stretch of the upper [[place-luwabolu|Luwa Bolu]], a few days by dugout from end to end. Its seat is [[place-liholacamp|Lihola Camp]], on the high bank at the head of the reach. The tribe keeps the four standings of every forest tribe, and its place on the river makes it one of the forest's doors to the north.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

The leader is [[being-sogolihola|Sogo Lihola]], named by the elders at the landing and open to being unmade the same way. The elders sit a night, speak names, and the first name no one speaks against by dawn is the leader. The trade-keeper is [[being-kobololihola|Kobolu Lihola]], who is not the leader and does not choose one. The tribe watches any leader who also trades more closely than one who does not.

## Range {#range}

The reach begins a short way below where the river leaves the valley of [[place-lpdkngcrt|the Leopard King's Court]] and ends above the gorge where the [[affiliation-morolatribe|Morola Tribe]] hold the landings. The bands camp on the high banks and move when the river moves, when a garden tires or when someone dies. They fish with weirs, basket-traps and spears, and each band keeps a small garden of plantain and root behind thorn, which the forest elephants raid in the dry months.

## Relations {#relations}

The Lihola send smoked fish and river-salt to the Court, whose valley holds the river's head, and the Spotted Guard visits a tribe that stops. They trade fish and metal for honey and ivory with the [[affiliation-ngotolatribe|Ngotola Tribe]] at the landing, take their sick to the court of the [[affiliation-gbelelatribe|Gbelela Tribe]], and keep trade-friendships with the Morola at the gorge, through whom the Khelathi river-posts reach the forest's goods.

## History {#history}

The Lihola tell of a founding night at the landing, when the elders of five bands spoke one name and no one spoke against it, and they claim no older story ([[lore-rainforhist|the History of the Central Rainforests]]).

## The Present Pressure {#present}

Fewer boats come up from the northern river-posts than in other years, and the metal that came up with them is thin. The elders watch Sogo closely, because a leader under whom the river gives bad years seldom lasts, and the trade-keeper's store of knives and spear-heads is the first stock the bands count.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The thin metal.** Kobolu needs a trade-friendship token carried to the gorge landings and iron brought back, and he cannot go himself during the fish run.
- **The late gift.** Sogo wants to send the Court's smoked fish a few days late and needs someone from outside the tribe to carry the message to the Spotted Guard and be believed.
- **The fourth day.** A stranger the river brought has used up the three nights of guest-right, and the Keeper of the Landing wants a witness to hear what the stranger asks.
