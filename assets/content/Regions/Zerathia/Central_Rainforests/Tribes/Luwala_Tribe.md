## Overview {#overview}

The Luwala Tribe is four bands of [[lore-luwandoclt|Luwando]] who hold the lower reach of the [[place-luwagbasa|Luwa Gbasa]], the Dark River, below the drowned plain. Its seat is [[place-luwalacamp|Luwala Camp]], the last river camp on the black water. Every boat that goes up the river meets the Luwala first.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

The leader is [[being-moroluwala|Moro Luwala]], named by the elders at the landing. The elders unmake a leader more readily on this river than on any other, since the black water gives or withholds its fish with little warning. The Warden of the Forbidden Reach is an elder and holds no authority over the leader.

## Range {#range}

The reach runs from a long week by dugout below the drowned plain down to where the river spreads into flooded forest and swamp. The bands camp on pole houses over the bank and fish the dark water with weirs and spears. They hold the water above the camp forbidden, and the elders give a visitor no reason beyond the warning ([[place-drwndzgrt|the Drowned Ziggurat]]).

## Relations {#relations}

The Luwala are the nearest river-tribe to the [[affiliation-sewilatribe|Sewila Tribe]], whose court lies a few days from the water, and they take their sick there. They trade fish for metal with the [[affiliation-balolatribe|Balola Tribe]] at the southern edge, and they send word down the river when a boat comes up with salvage crews or strangers who ask for the way to the plain.

## History {#history}

The Luwala say the river took the dead of the court above them and gave them back as wheels, and that this is why the water above the camp is not fished ([[lore-rainforhist|the History of the Central Rainforests]]).

## The Present Pressure {#present}

Salvage crews from the northern river-ports have reached the Dark River in greater numbers than in other years, each with a story of a roof of green stone. The elders are divided on whether to bar the river entirely or to sell guides to the lower plain, and a bad fishing season has the oldest elders speaking against Moro's name.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The bar on the river.** A crew of salvagers has sent a woman ashore with iron to buy a guide, and the Warden wants her turned back without a fight.
- **The dead boat.** The Maker of the Ewudi needs bark from a tree that stands on a stretch of the forbidden bank, and wants an outsider to cut it so the tribe's hands stay clean.
- **A name spoken against.** An elder has spoken against Moro at the landing, and Moro wants a witness who owes the tribe nothing.
