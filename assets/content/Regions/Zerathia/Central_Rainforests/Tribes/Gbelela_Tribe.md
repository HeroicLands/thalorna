## Overview {#overview}

The Gbelela Tribe is three bands of [[lore-kubindoclt|Kubindo]] who keep [[place-kubigbele|Kubi Gbele]], the court of the tree, a giant hardwood in a stand of its own relations a day's walk west of the Luwa Bolu. The tribe hunts and forages around the tree, returns to it at the new moon and hangs its offerings on the lowest boughs.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

The leader is [[being-wenegbelela|Wene Gbelela]], whom the spirit of the tree answered when the tribe last sat the night. The kelendo keeps the court's manners and teaches them to any member who asks, and the Gbelela keep the kelendo from power on purpose.

## Range {#range}

The bands range through forest within a few days' walk of the tree. The court forbids the felling of any tree within sight of its crown, and the stand around it is the tribe's boundary. The sick of the river tribes are brought to the tree, laid under it for a night and asked what they did to offend.

## Relations {#relations}

The Gbelela send dyes and medicinal plants to [[place-lpdkngcrt|the Leopard King's Court]] when the Spotted Guard asks, and send their gift reluctantly. They turn hardwood buyers away from the tree's stand, and they take the sick of the [[affiliation-liholatribe|Lihola Tribe]] as a matter of custom.

## History {#history}

The Gbelela say that the spirit of the tree has answered the night-sitting every time it has been asked, and that a tribe which cut one of its kin would find the spirit silent.

## The Present Pressure {#present}

Buyers from the river landings are offering iron for the felled trunks of the stand around the court, and some members say the tree's kin are only trees. The offering on the lowest bough has been taken three nights in a row, and the Hanger of Offerings cannot say by what.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The buyers.** A Khelathi factor offers iron for ten trunks from the stand, and Wene wants a party to deliver a refusal the factor will not argue with.
- **The empty bough.** The Hanger of Offerings wants a party to watch the lowest bough through one night without touching it.
- **The gift for the Court.** Wene must send dyes to the Lower Terrace and wants an escort who will not be asked what the tree's tribe thinks of the King.
