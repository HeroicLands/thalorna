## Overview {#overview}

Kirola Camp is the seat of the [[affiliation-kirolatribe|Kirola Tribe]], a camp of pole houses on the forest side of the edge, with thorn-fenced gardens and watch platforms behind it. A line of carved grave-posts runs along the outer edge, each with a face that looks out of the forest. The camp has stood in one place for a generation. In the dry season the [[lore-okharinclt|Okharik]] herders bring their cattle to the pasture beyond the line and come to the camp to trade.
