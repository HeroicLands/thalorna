## Overview {#overview}

Wenela Camp is the gathering place of the [[affiliation-wenelatribe|Wenela Tribe]], a clearing where the canopy holds the morning mist until noon and the huts are built of bent saplings and wet leaves. The four bands come together there in the honey season. In a failing season the song band sits at its edge from dark until near dawn and sings the forest awake, and the women and children stay in the huts and listen.
