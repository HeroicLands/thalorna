## Overview {#overview}

The Morola Tribe is seven bands of gorge-folk who hold the landings where the [[place-luwabolu|Luwa Bolu]] cuts through the highland spurs. Its seat is [[place-morolalanding|Morola Landing]], on the bench above the first rapid. The tribe speaks the [[skill-ekpelulng|Ekpelu Language]] and keeps the forest's offerings and restraints, but it is not one of the four traditions: the [[lore-gorgfolkclt|Gorge-folk]] hold ground where the river-keepers pass through as guests.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

The leader is [[being-kelemorola|Kele Morola]], whom the members and elders agree has run the fairest portage for a few seasons. Nothing is conferred or inherited. A leader who cheats a landing is dropped without ceremony, since no canoe will come to a cheated landing, and nothing is placed above the tribe.

## Range {#range}

The tribe holds the benches above the rapids and the portage trails that run around them, with a landing at each end of the carry. The bands keep ridge gardens on the slopes, fish-traps in the white water and a bench at each landing where cargo waits. Garden crops, river fish and forest game give them a fuller diet than the foragers of the deep interior.

## Relations {#relations}

The Morola carry the cargoes of the Khelathi river-posts upstream and the forest's goods down, and take the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]]'s metal and cloth as part of their share. They deal with the [[affiliation-liholatribe|Lihola Tribe]] through the trade-friendships that bring the Luwando past the gorge as guests, and they owe the Court nothing.

## History {#history}

The Morola tell that the first landing was a flat rock where a woman set down a canoe too heavy to carry and charged nothing, and that every share since has been measured against her.

## The Present Pressure {#present}

The dry season has lowered the river, and the heavier canoes must be carried the full length of the gorge. The keepers of the two landings quarrel over the division of the shares, and a Khelathi river-post has asked to carry its own boats past the first rapid for a fixed fee.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **A season's price for the carry.** The post's factor wants the carry priced by the season, and Kele wants a witness who will say what the Morola charge a trader who pays fairly.
- **The two landings.** The keepers of the Upper and Lower Landings each ask a party to carry the same cargo past the other's bench.
- **A cheated landing.** A porter has been caught at the lower bench with a load lighter than he was paid to carry, and the members want a hearing.
