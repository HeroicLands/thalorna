## Overview {#overview}

The Ngotola Tribe is six bands of [[lore-yesomaclt|Yesoma]] who range the deep interior between the two great rivers, following the trails of the forest elephant. The bands keep no fixed camp, and the one place all of them know is [[place-ngotolacamp|Ngotola Camp]], the clearing by the honey trees where they gather each flowering.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

The leader is [[being-ranongotola|Rano Ngotola]], at whose fire the bands have camped for a number of honey seasons. No one named Rano. A Ngotola tribe often goes years without a leader and feels no lack, and the elders matter more: they hold the trails, the honey trees and the songs.

## Range {#range}

The tribe ranges over a stretch of forest a few days' walk across, on the old [[being-xrthnfrs|elephant]] trails and on paths of its own. A band lives in domed huts of bent saplings and leaves, built in an afternoon, and moves when the game thins, the honey season calls or the camp quarrels. Nothing is stored and nothing is planted.

## Relations {#relations}

The Ngotola trade honey and ivory for fish, salt and metal at the [[affiliation-liholatribe|Lihola]] landing, and think the river-keepers improvident with the forest's gifts. They avoid the court of the [[affiliation-gbelelatribe|Gbelela Tribe]] without a quarrel, and the bands that range near the valley of [[place-lpdkngcrt|the Leopard King's Court]] send honey there when the Spotted Guard asks.

## History {#history}

The Ngotola name no founder. A band remembers a leader as the fire it camped beside, and the fire's name is lost with the leader's.

## The Present Pressure {#present}

The elephants have moved their trails east, and the Keeper of the Trails says the old paths lead away from the honey trees. The song band has sung through the nights of one moon, and some of the young want to carry honey to the Lihola landing for metal and not wait for the forest to wake.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The lost trail.** The Keeper of the Trails wants a guide who can read elephant sign to find where the herd went, since no band will say its own paths aloud to an outsider.
- **The honey fire.** Rano needs the first comb of the season carried to the gathering before a rival band camps elsewhere, and the nearest flowering stand lies inside the Court's patrol range.
- **The song at night.** The sogola has sung for a moon and the hunting has not returned. A party that camps nearby hears it, and the Caller asks that no one carry the sound out of the forest.
