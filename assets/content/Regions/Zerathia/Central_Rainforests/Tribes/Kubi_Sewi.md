## Overview {#overview}

Kubi Sewi is the spirit-court of the [[affiliation-sewilatribe|Sewila Tribe]], a black pool under a lip of rock in a fold of the forest, held by one spirit and the manners owed there. A few huts stand back from the water, and no fire burns within sight of it. The whole tribe gathers at the pool on the night of each new moon, and the kelendo speaks to the tribe's dead, who are buried beside it. Between moons only the keeper's family stays.
