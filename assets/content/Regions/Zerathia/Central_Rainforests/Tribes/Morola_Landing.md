## Overview {#overview}

Morola Landing is the seat of the [[affiliation-morolatribe|Morola Tribe]], a village on the bench above the first rapid of the [[place-luwabolu|Luwa Bolu]], with ridge gardens on the slope behind it and fish-traps set in the white water. Canoes come ashore at the landing, the cargo is weighed and a share set before anything is lifted, and porters carry it down the portage trail to the lower landing. The Morola grade each trader by how he paid at the last landing, and the grade is known at the next.
