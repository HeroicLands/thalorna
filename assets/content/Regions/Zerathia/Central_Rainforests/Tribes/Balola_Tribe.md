## Overview {#overview}

The Balola Tribe is four bands of [[lore-pewayeclt|Pewaye]] who hold the southern edge of the Central Rainforests, where the flooded forest of the [[place-luwagbasa|Luwa Gbasa]] gives way to savannah. Its seat is [[place-balolacamp|Balola Camp]], a camp that has stood in one place for a generation.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

The leader is [[being-sepobalola|Sepo Balola]], whose gift of iron knives the members accepted. Every member chooses whether to take it, and a member who takes it owes the leader a voice in council. The Keeper of the Iron holds the store of metal and is not the leader.

## Range {#range}

The tribe holds a stretch of edge where trails come in from the grass and the flooded forest, and each family keeps a _tegbo_ of it from father to son. The bands garden on the forest side of the line, behind thorn and watch platforms, and bury their dead facing out under a _hokpe_.

## Relations {#relations}

The Balola trade with the [[affiliation-nylbtrblntn|Nyáluba Tribal Nation]] at the Sengala post, where the leopard-clan's traders bring iron, cloth and salt north across the grass. The Balola carry some of the iron to [[place-lpdkngcrt|the Leopard King's Court]] in exchange for ivory and hides, and are the Court's one door to the south.

## History {#history}

The Balola tell that their first leader brought iron to the edge before there was a trail, and that the Sengala came after the iron.

## The Present Pressure {#present}

The Sengala traders are late with iron this season because their clan is weighing which side to take in the war between the Ruwozi and the Kolembozi, and the Great Lubemo that decides it is gathering. The Keeper of the Iron has less to hand out than the tally expects, and the Court's gift-bearers have been asking why.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The road to the Sengala post.** Sepo wants a party to ride to the Sengala post and learn whether the traders are coming or whether the clan has closed the road.
- **A war at the edge.** A Ruwozi rider has come to the edge asking for a guide into the forest, and Sepo wants to know whether the Sengala have sent him.
- **The Court's tally.** The Receiver of Gifts has sent a bearer to count the Balola's iron, and the Keeper of the Iron wants a witness to the count.
