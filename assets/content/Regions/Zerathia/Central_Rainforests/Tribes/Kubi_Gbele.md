## Overview {#overview}

Kubi Gbele is the spirit-court of the [[affiliation-gbelelatribe|Gbelela Tribe]], a giant hardwood standing in a stand of its own relations. Strings of beads, bundles of dye-plants and bowls of smoked fish hang from the lowest boughs, and the Hanger of Offerings renews them at every new moon. The whole tribe gathers beneath the crown on that night, and the tribe's dead are buried among the roots. No tree is felled within sight of the crown.
