## Overview {#overview}

The Kirola Tribe is five bands of [[lore-pewayeclt|Pewaye]] who hold the northern edge of the Central Rainforests, where the forest breaks into the dry pasture that [[lore-okharinclt|Okharik]] herders use. Its seat is [[place-kirolacamp|Kirola Camp]], a camp that stays in one place for a generation.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

The leader is [[being-danokirola|Dano Kirola]], whose gift the members accepted. Every member chooses whether to take it, and a member who takes it owes the leader a voice in council. A leader whose door fills with returned gifts stops being leader, and the gifts do not stay long on a door where the bands are content.

## Range {#range}

The tribe holds a stretch of forest edge, each family a _tegbo_ of it that passes from father to son, and knows every trail that crosses. The bands keep small gardens on the forest side of the line, fenced with thorn against the elephants. The dead are buried sitting at the outer line of the family's tegbo, and a _hokpe_ is set over each grave: a post carved with a face that looks out of the forest.

## Relations {#relations}

The Kirola trade with the Okharik herders at the line and carry iron blades, glass beads and cloth into the forest. They think the [[affiliation-liholatribe|Lihola]] soft, and the Lihola think them rivals at the door. The deep tribes do not regard the Kirola as the forest's speaker, and the Kirola do not say that they are.

## History {#history}

The Kirola tell of a first member who went out past the edge and came back with a blade for every family, and each leader since has been measured against that gift.

## The Present Pressure {#present}

The herders bring their cattle deeper into the forest edge each dry season, and the elders say the dry season runs longer each year. Dano's gift of last season is still being weighed: three members have taken it, and one has left a blade at his door.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The cattle at the line.** A herders' headman wants a party to carry a gift of salt to the Kirola in return for a grazing the Kirola have not agreed to.
- **The returned blade.** Dano wants to know which member left a blade at his door and why, and asks a party that can ask a question of the herders.
- **The line of posts.** A hokpe has been cut down on the edge, and the Keeper of the Hokpe wants to know who is crossing the line at night.
