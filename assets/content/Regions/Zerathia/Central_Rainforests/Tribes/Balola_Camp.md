## Overview {#overview}

Balola Camp is the seat of the [[affiliation-balolatribe|Balola Tribe]], a camp of pole houses where the flooded forest gives way to grass. A post of poles stands at the edge for the traders, with a trade ground in front of it, and the grave-posts of the tribe's dead face out across the savannah. The [[affiliation-nylbtrblntn|Nyáluba]] bring iron, cloth and salt north to the post, and the Balola carry them into the forest. The tribe's iron store is kept in a pit under the Keeper's own house.
