## Overview {#overview}

Luwala Camp is the seat of the [[affiliation-luwalatribe|Luwala Tribe]], pole houses over the bank of the black water, with the landing where each boat bound upriver is stopped and asked its business. Bark boats stand drying under the eaves, each cut for one passenger, and one is pushed out on the current each time the tribe loses someone. About a hundred people live there, and the long week by dugout upriver to [[place-drwndzgrt|the Drowned Ziggurat]] begins at its landing.
