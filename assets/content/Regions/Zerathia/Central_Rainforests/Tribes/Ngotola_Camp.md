## Overview {#overview}

Ngotola Camp is the gathering place of the [[affiliation-ngotolatribe|Ngotola Tribe]], a clearing among tall honey trees where domed huts of bent saplings are built in an afternoon. The bands come in for the flowering, climb for the combs and eat little else for weeks, and the first comb of the season goes to the leader's fire. The huts are left standing when the gathering ends. A camp where someone has died is left to the forest and never camped on again, and this one has been kept clean of the dead.
