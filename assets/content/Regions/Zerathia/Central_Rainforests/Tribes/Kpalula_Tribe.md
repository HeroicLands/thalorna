## Overview {#overview}

The Kpalula Tribe is the leopard's band, the people of [[place-lpdkngcrt|the Leopard King's Court]], about four hundred in all: a large tribe by the forest's measure. Its bands follow one leader, a sorcerer who calls himself the Leopard King, and nothing stands above it. It takes no tribute and rules nothing; its neighbors bring it gifts because they fear its leader.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

The leader is [[being-lpdkngldr|the Leopard King]], recognized for as long as the neighboring tribes' gifts keep coming. The tribe stays while it is fed, and the title is his own: every other forest tribe has a leader and none has a king. [[being-dagbekpalula|Dagbe Kpalula]] is the Speaker, who gives his judgments in his name, and [[being-ndokokpalula|Ndoko Kpalula]], called Red-Spear, leads the Spotted Guard.

## Range {#range}

The tribe holds the valley where three rivers rise within a day's walk of each other, among them the [[place-luwabolu|Luwa Bolu]]. Its hunters range the ways between the rivers in leopard-skin cloaks and keep a ring of watch-posts in the trees. The neighboring tribes keep out of the valley unless they come with a gift.

## Relations {#relations}

The [[affiliation-liholatribe|Lihola Tribe]], the [[affiliation-gbelelatribe|Gbelela Tribe]] and some bands of the [[affiliation-ngotolatribe|Ngotola Tribe]] send gifts on their own schedules, and the Spotted Guard visits one that stops. The Court keeps iron, which it takes from the Sengala through the [[affiliation-balolatribe|Balola Tribe]] in exchange for ivory and hides, and hands out by measure.

## History {#history}

The Kpalula say the leader's rule began when the neighbors began to send gifts, and that it will end when they stop ([[lore-rainforhist|the History of the Central Rainforests]]).

## The Present Pressure {#present}

Gift-bearers report that no delegation has met the King in two seasons and that the Speaker gives his judgments. Red-Spear says in the Hall of Pelts that the Court should be led in the open, and the iron that comes through the south is late this season.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The gift-bearers' wait.** A caravan of gift-bearers takes on a party as porters, and at the Court the Speaker tests each newcomer with small questions.
- **Red-Spear's offer.** The Leader of the Spotted Guard looks for allies among visitors who will say what they saw on the Lower Terrace.
- **The tally and the iron.** The Receiver of Gifts needs word of why the Balola have sent less iron than the tally expects.
