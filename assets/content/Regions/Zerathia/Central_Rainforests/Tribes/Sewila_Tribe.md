## Overview {#overview}

The Sewila Tribe is three bands of [[lore-kubindoclt|Kubindo]] who keep [[place-kubisewi|Kubi Sewi]], the court of the serpent, a black pool in a fold of the forest a few days from the Dark River. The tribe forages and hunts in the country around the court and returns to it at every new moon.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

The leader is [[being-dibosewila|Dibo Sewila]], made by the spirit at the night-sitting. When the tribe needs a leader the elders name the members they think fit, each sits a night alone at the pool, and in the morning the kelendo [[being-mbekosewila|Mbeko Sewila]] decides whom the spirit answered. The kelendo can never be leader and can never be named, and the Sewila hold the rule hardest of all their rules.

## Range {#range}

The bands range through forest within a few days' walk of the pool and keep no gardens. The court's manners forbid the killing of any serpent and any fire within sight of the water. The sick of other tribes are laid at the pool for a night and asked what they did to offend, and the Sewila keep the sick of the [[affiliation-luwalatribe|Luwala Tribe]] as a matter of custom.

## Relations {#relations}

The Sewila tell the story of the keeper who became a leader and took helpers and built a house of stone for his serpent, and they tell it as their own warning ([[lore-rainforhist|the History of the Central Rainforests]]). They have no quarrel with the Luwala, who hold the black water below the drowned plain, and are polite to the Yesoma, whom they think careless of other spirits' houses.

## History {#history}

The Sewila hold that the serpent of this pool is not the serpent of the dead court, and that its keepers have never built it a house ([[place-drwndzgrt|the Drowned Ziggurat]]).

## The Present Pressure {#present}

Strangers have come to the pool asking the way to the drowned plain, and the Sewila have named the wrong river to every one. Mbeko is old, and the tribe has begun to ask who can keep the whole of the court's manners when he is gone.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The wrong river.** A salvager's guide has been told the wrong river and has found the pool, and Dibo asks a party to take him out of the forest before the kelendo sees him.
- **The next keeper.** Mbeko wants a member to learn the whole of the manners, and the member he picked wants an outsider's view of whether he can bear it.
- **A sick stranger.** A trader has been laid at the pool for a night and has told the Caller of the New Moon nothing of what he did to offend.
