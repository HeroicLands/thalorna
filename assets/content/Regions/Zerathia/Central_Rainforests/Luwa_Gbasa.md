## Overview {#overview}

The Luwa Gbasa is a river of black water, stained by the leaf-litter of the forest it drains, that flows south through the interior to the flooded forest and the grass beyond. Its current slackens into a drowned plain of standing trunks in the north, where [[place-drwndzgrt|the Drowned Ziggurat]] stands. Below that the [[affiliation-luwalatribe|Luwala Tribe]] hold the river, and at its southern end the [[affiliation-balolatribe|Balola Tribe]] hold the edge where the flooded forest meets the savannah.

## The Land {#the-land}

Above the Luwala the river is a maze of flooded trunks, and a boat that leaves the old channel is lost. Below the plain the black water runs deep and slow between banks of root and mud, and the pole houses of the Luwala stand over it. The river spreads at its southern end into seasonal swamp, and the Balola camp where the swamp gives way to grass. The [[place-kubisewi|Serpent Court]] of the Sewila lies a few days' walk from the water, in a fold of the forest.

## Who Uses It {#who-uses-it}

The Luwala fish it with weirs and spears. The Balola trade across its flooded lower reach with the [[affiliation-nylbtrblntn|Nyáluba]]. The Sewila lay the sick of the Luwala at their pool and say nothing of the water. Salvage crews from the northern river-ports come up it from the southern edge in hired boats, asking for a roof of green stone.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **The drowned plain.** A boat that leaves the old channel is lost among the trunks, and the elders hold the water above the last camp forbidden.
- **The black water.** Its fish come and go without warning, and a bad season turns the Luwala elders against their leader.
- **Strangers on the bar.** The Luwala turn back every boat bound for the plain, and a crew that will not turn is a danger to the camp.

## What Is Told {#what-is-told}

The Luwala say the river took the dead of the court above them and gave them back as wheels, and that this is why they do not fish the water above their last camp. The deep-forest tribes do not speak of it to outsiders and name the wrong river when asked the way ([[lore-rainforhist|the History of the Central Rainforests]]).

## Converging Interests {#present}

Salvage crews have reached the Dark River in greater numbers than in other years. The Luwala elders are divided on whether to bar the river or to sell guides to the lower plain, the Sewila have named the wrong river to every stranger who has found their pool, and the Balola have watched a Ruwozi rider ask for a guide where the swamp ends.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The roof of green stone.** A crew with iron and a map wants a guide past the bar, and the Luwala leader wants them stopped without bloodshed.
- **The wrong river.** A salvager's guide has found the Serpent Court by mistake, and the Sewila want him taken out of the forest.
- **The rider at the edge.** A Ruwozi rider has asked at the southern edge for a way into the forest, and the Balola want to know who sent him.
