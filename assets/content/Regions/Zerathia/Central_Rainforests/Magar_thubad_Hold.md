## Overview {#overview}

**Magar-thubád Hold** is the hold of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]] at [[place-magarthubad|Magar-thubád]], in a limestone range above [[place-cntrlrnfrsts|the Central Rainforests]] of [[place-xerathia|Zerathia]], and the [[lore-pewayeclt|Edge-people]] community of the Pewaye at its gate. About four hundred Khazari of nine houses live in the rock and about twelve hundred Pewaye garden, herd and fish below it. It holds no relationship with any realm, and no realm knows where it is.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

The hold is governed as every Khazari hold is, by the council of the elders of its houses, sitting together with no one first among them. The council seats the elders of nine houses, and the hold's record names five of them in the account of its water-line: [Tabar-suláth]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, whose craft is the water, [Ramk-sumád]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, [Khalv-gunáv]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, [Khard-vurán]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} and [Lakam-mulán]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}. The council judges from the archive, whose keeper brings up the records that bear on a case and reads them whole, and where a record and an elder's memory disagree, the record stands. It sends one elder to the gate as **Speaker**, to hear the community below and to answer it.

The Pewaye of the gate keep their own elders, as the margin peoples do, and bring the community's word to the gate.

## Current Situation {#present}

- **The iron downriver.** A Pewaye trader of the gate sold a machete of the hold's iron at an [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Au'Khelathu]] trading post last season, and the post's factor is asking every river trader where the Pewaye of that river buy their iron. The community has not told the hold, and the council does not know.
- **The rains.** The rains came heavy and late, the river has risen into the lowest gardens twice, and the council has sent the engineers down to raise the channel banks at the gate.

## The Tie

The Pewaye send up the gardens' yams and plantains, goats, fowl, eggs and river fish to the gate, and the hold sends down tools and iron, keeps the channels that water the gardens, and keeps the approaches up the gorge. In a hungry year the hold opens its caches to the community, and in a war it opens its refuge. The Pewaye know the hold's gate and its people closely and are never taken inside.

## History {#history}

The hold's archive counts its years from the finding of its cave. In the year of the finding 309 a quake took the hold's water, and the water-line [Dulkun of Khalv-gunáv]{.name data-type="being/npc"} cut along the grain to the spring-head saved it. The account is [[lore-longtunnel|The Long Tunnel]], and copies of it stand in the archives of other holds. The gate's people drew from the river through the whole of it, and the timber the house of Ramk-sumád had brought up was given to the gate.

## Relations {#relations}

The hold has no treaty, embassy or correspondence with any realm. Its masters go out to other holds when their work is in order, and its archive copies the records of other holds as theirs copy its own.
