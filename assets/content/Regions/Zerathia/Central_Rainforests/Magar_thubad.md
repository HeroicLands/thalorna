> **GM-only note.** The holds name Magar-thubád freely, and no record outside its own says where it is. Its place belongs in no public note.

## Overview {#overview}

**Magar-thubád**, "the following of the grain", is a lived-in hold of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]], the Khazari, about four hundred of them in nine houses, in a range of limestone that no record outside its own locates. The holds know it by its archive and its masters: [[lore-longtunnel|The Long Tunnel]], its keeper's account of the water-line cut along the grain after a quake took the hold's water, stands in the archives of other holds and is told in the workshops to apprentices who ask for the hammer. An [[lore-pewayeclt|Edge-people]] community, the Pewaye, of about twelve hundred farms and fishes the valley at its gate and feeds it, and the hold is governed by the council of its houses as [[affiliation-mgrthbdhld|Magar-thubád Hold]].

:::secret {title="Where it stands"}
Magar-thubád stands in [[place-xerathia|Zerathia]], in a high limestone massif above the northern edge of [[place-cntrlrnfrsts|the Central Rainforests]], where the canopy climbs the massif's lower slopes and gives out far below its cliffs, and the upper walls take snow and frost in the cold months. A river comes north through the spur in a gorge on its way to [[place-vylarianse|the Vilérian Sea]], and the hold's face looks down into that gorge from its eastern wall, two days' climb above the forest margin and above the cloud forest, where the rains of the warm months fall as snow in the cold ones, and hidden from the river by mist on most mornings of the year. The rock is cave-riddled and the ground shudders, and the hold's water comes from a spring-head inside the mountain above it, by the water-line the account tells of. The Pewaye of the gate live at the foot of the gorge, where the river leaves the massif, and draw their water from the river.
:::

## What the Margin Sees

From the forest margin the massif is a green wall under cloud, with white cliffs where the trees give out and snow above them in the cold months. The northern traders who come upriver know the forest's edge and not the massif, and Zerathian accounts put no more than a handful of mountain halls somewhere in the continent's interior highlands. The Pewaye of the gate garden, keep goats and fowl, and fish the river, and they hold the line between the forest and the outside as the margin peoples do. They take nobody up the gorge.

## The Present {#present}

A Pewaye trader of the gate went downriver last season with the community's surplus and sold, among the usual goods, a machete of iron so fine that the factor at an [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Au'Khelathu]] trading post sent it north to his house rather than to the market. The factor is asking every river trader who comes to the post where the Pewaye of that river buy their iron. The trader has told him nothing, and the community has not told the hold.

## History {#history}

The hold's archive counts its years from the finding of its cave, and its record is the only one that dates it. In the year of the finding 309 a quake brought down the roof of the passage that carries the spring to the cistern, and [Dulkun of Khalv-gunáv]{.name data-type="being/npc"} cut a new water-line along the bedding seam in a hundred and fifty-eight days, with no prop in it. The hold's keeper of the archive, [Lukum of Lakam-mulán]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, wrote the account of it, and the water-line is the first work the hold's record enters as a long tunnel that needs no prop. Dulkun went to another hold when his work there was in order, and the masters of Magar-thubád have gone out to other holds since, as masters do.
