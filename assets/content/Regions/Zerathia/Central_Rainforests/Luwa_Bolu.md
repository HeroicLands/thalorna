## Overview {#overview}

The Luwa Bolu is the great river of the northern forest, the one outsiders mean when they say the river. It is one of three rivers that rise within a day's walk of each other in the valley of [[place-lpdkngcrt|the Leopard King's Court]], and it runs north through the interior, past the high-bank camps of the [[affiliation-liholatribe|Lihola Tribe]], through the gorge where the [[affiliation-morolatribe|Morola Tribe]] hold the landings, and out of the forest at its northern edge.

## The Land {#the-land}

The upper river is a clear, fast stream under unbroken canopy. Below the Court's valley it widens, and the Lihola reach begins: high banks that the water does not climb in the rains, weirs of staked cane, and plantain gardens behind thorn. Farther north the spurs close in, and the river drops through a gorge of white water in three falls with a carry around each. Below the gorge it runs on between low banks to the forest's northern edge, past the river-posts where traders wait.

## Who Uses It {#who-uses-it}

The Lihola fish it and carry the forest's honey, ivory and hides down to the gorge landings in dugouts cut from single trunks. The Morola carry canoes and cargo around the rapids for a share. The [[affiliation-ngotolatribe|Ngotola Tribe]] trade honey at the Lihola landing for fish and metal, and the sick of the Lihola are taken west to the [[place-kubigbele|Kubi Gbele]], the Tree Court, of the Gbelela. The Court's neighbors send gifts down and up it.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **The falls.** A canoe that tries the gorge instead of the carry is lost, and the Morola take no responsibility for it.
- **The Court's reach.** The Spotted Guard visits a tribe whose gift is late, and a boat that carries a stranger past the head of the river is noticed.
- **Fever.** The low banks below the gorge breed fevers that outsiders seldom survive for long.

## What Is Told {#what-is-told}

The Luwando call the river _yobe_, the host, and a drowning is the host's displeasure. The first fish of every catch goes back to the water. The dead go downstream, and the stretch below a camp is not fished until the next rains.

## Converging Interests {#present}

Fewer boats come up from the northern river-posts than in other years, and the Lihola elders count the metal they have left. The river-post below the gorge wants to carry its own boats past the first rapid for a fixed fee, the Morola keepers of the two landings quarrel over the division of the shares in the low water, and the Court's gift-bearers walk the banks with smoked fish that the Lihola leader means to send late.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The river-post contract.** A factor at the river-post wants a witness to what the Morola charge a fair trader before he signs.
- **The cargo that stopped.** A load of iron is held at the upper landing while the keepers argue the share, and the buyer upriver wants it moved.
- **The head of the river.** A stranger asks for a guide to the valley where the river rises, and no Lihola will take the work.
