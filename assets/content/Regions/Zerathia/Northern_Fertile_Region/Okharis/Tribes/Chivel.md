## Overview {#overview}

**Chivel** (about 3,500 people) is a linen-weaving town on the river of [[place-okharisrgn|Okharis]], near [[place-zhikandevlg|Zhíkande Village]]. The Zhíkande flax-pools supply the weavers, and the grain that feeds the looms comes from the farms of the river plain.

Weavers sell their cloth to buyers from the temple-cities and the Bethuan coast, and dyers keep the lower town. A flax blight takes a season's thread, and a dye-vat fire can burn a street of workshops.
