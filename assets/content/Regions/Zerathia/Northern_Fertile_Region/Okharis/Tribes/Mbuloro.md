## Overview {#overview}

**Mbuloro** (about 2,800 people) is a timber-and-charcoal market town on the forest edge of [[place-okharisrgn|Okharis]], a day from [[place-dongaruvlg|Dóngaru Village]]. The Dóngaru bring hardwood and charcoal to its yards, and the town buys grain, dried fish and beer from the plains villages with what the yards earn.

Smiths' agents from the temple-cities contract here for the season's charcoal, and carters take it down the forest road. A charcoal stack that escapes its earth cover burns a street, and a dry season keeps the whole town on fire watch.
