## Overview {#overview}

**Kandoro** (about 2,600 people) is a crossroads market town of [[place-okharisrgn|Okharis]], a short ride from [[place-khamundavlg|Khamunda Village]], where the Khamunda meet the herders of the open plain. The farms around it grow the grain that feeds the traders, and the herders pay for it in cattle and hides.

Bargaining here runs on custom: a sale is settled by the elders of the two kindreds, and the elders' fee is part of the price. A feud that follows a herder into the market closes it for a day, and the town's elders hold the gates until the kinsmen are parted.
