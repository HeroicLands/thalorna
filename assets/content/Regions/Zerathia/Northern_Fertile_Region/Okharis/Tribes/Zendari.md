## Overview {#overview}

**Zendari** (about 3,000 people) is a horse-fair town on the grassland of [[place-okharisrgn|Okharis]], half a day from [[place-magaravlg|Magara Village]]. The Magara breed the horses, and the town lives on the buyers and grooms who come to choose them and on the grain that the farms to the north send to the paddocks.

Buyers from the temple-cities pay in silver and in iron, and the Magara sell their best stock last. A fair that draws a rival buyer from beyond the border can end in a challenge, and horse-thieves work the roads leaving it.
