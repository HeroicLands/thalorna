## Overview {#overview}

**Sembari** (about 5,000 people) is the grain-market town of [[place-okharisrgn|Okharis]], among the barley and sorghum fields near [[place-wambuchivlg|Wámbuchi Village]]. The Wámbuchi threshing floors and clay granaries feed it, and the surplus goes by cart to the temple-cities.

Measurers, bakers and granary-keepers keep its streets busy after the harvest. A year of locusts or a late rain empties the granaries, and the town buys its own bread back at the temple-cities' price.
