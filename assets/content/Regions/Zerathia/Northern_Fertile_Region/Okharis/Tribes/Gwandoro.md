## Overview {#overview}

**Gwandoro** (about 6,000 people) is the cattle-market town of [[place-okharisrgn|Okharis]], on the open plain near [[place-talumengvlg|Tálumeng Village]], and the largest of the market towns. The herds are its wealth, and the grain, beer and iron that the herders buy are carted in from the farms on every side of it.

Cattle-dealers from the temple-cities fill its pens each dry season, and brides' cattle change hands in its yards. A cattle sickness closes the pens at once, and a raid on the drovers' road makes dealers wait for an escort.
