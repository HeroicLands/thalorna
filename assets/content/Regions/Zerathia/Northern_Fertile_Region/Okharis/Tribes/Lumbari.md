## Overview {#overview}

**Lumbari** (about 4,000 people) is a date-and-fig market town on the river banks of [[place-okharisrgn|Okharis]], two days from [[place-kirofavlg|Kirofa Village]]. The Kirofa orchards feed it, and the barley and sorghum it needs come down the river from the plains.

Fruit-factors, drying-floor hands and boat-owners fill its landing, and the dried fruit goes on to the temple-cities in sealed baskets. A late river flood spoils the drying, and a fever season on the banks empties the orchards of pickers.
