## Overview {#overview}

**Bokaru** (about 2,100 people) is a honey-and-wax market town in the forest country of [[place-okharisrgn|Okharis]], upriver from [[place-shokumavlg|Shókuma Village]]. The Shókuma bring their wax and honey by canoe, and the town buys grain and salt from the plains with the sealed cakes of wax that the temple-cities need for candles.

Chandlers from the flame-cities buy here, and shamans come to consult the Shókuma communers. A hive-robber from outside the tribe is judged by the Shókuma elders and not by the town, and the judgment is hard.
