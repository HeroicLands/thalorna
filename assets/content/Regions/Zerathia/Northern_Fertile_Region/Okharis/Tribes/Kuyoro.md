## Overview {#overview}

**Kuyoro** (about 2,300 people) is a tanning-and-leather town on the forest margin of [[place-okharisrgn|Okharis]], a short walk from [[place-yeshirovlg|Yéshiro Village]]. Hides come in from the plains tribes and leave as leather, and the town's grain comes from the same plains in payment.

Saddlers, sandal-makers and shield-binders work in its yards downwind of everyone. Bark-gatherers who strip too many acacias are fined by the elders, and a bad batch of tanning liquor ruins a season's hides.
