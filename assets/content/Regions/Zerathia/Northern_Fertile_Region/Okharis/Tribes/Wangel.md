## Overview {#overview}

**Wangel** (about 3,200 people) is a fish-market town on a river of [[place-okharisrgn|Okharis]], below [[place-ngorashavlg|Ngorasha Village]]. The smoke-racks of the Ngorasha supply its stalls, and the grain and millet that it buys come upriver from the farms on the plain.

Drovers from the herding camps carry the dried fish inland, and the town's traders take cattle and hides in exchange. A poor run of fish thins the market in a month, and a flood that carries off the smoke-racks takes a year's trade.
