**meaning:** _The Golden Flame_

## Overview {#overview}

Kaljekor, the Golden Flame, is the harbor of [[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]] on its north coast, with about thirty-five thousand people and the busiest quays of Okharis. Its temple complex is a pyramid whose terraces are markets: merchants from [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]] and the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] sell under striped awnings on every stair, and the temple at the top takes its offering from each bargain. The Golden Flame is the flame of abundance and commerce, and the city is known for the gold work and the wealth of its priest-nobles. It trades the herds, the bronze and the worked metal of the kingdom for what the kingdom does not make, and it buys the clans' beasts with what the sea brings.

## Arriving {#arriving}

Ships come in from [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]] in two days and from [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]] in five, and the coast road brings caravans from [[place-bethura|Bethura]] in about twenty. Most Okharik arrive from the other side, walking behind a herd, with the red dust of the plain in the air ahead of them and the pyramid standing up out of the haze. At the landward gate the herd is counted and the drovers are sent to the cattle-yards outside the city, and at the quays the harbor-wardens read each ship's cargo against its master's tally and take the harbor due.

## The Present {#present}

Kaljekor is rich this year, and its wealth is part of the realm's quarrel. The Khelathi houses buy at its quays and send gifts and offers through the same merchants, and the council of priest-nobles, the [Ngolu Nkaring]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, hears each one. It does not answer them. The council reads the Oracle's silence as the absence of a prohibition: no one has forbidden the Golden Flame to trade with every neighbor, and an open harbor is its best argument that none will.

The village tribes see the same wealth from the other side. The tribute they carry up to the moots has risen, and the dues asked in the name of the Golden Flame are among the reasons. The chieftains count the gold on the quays against the herds they have given. The council rules itself, the King cannot make it hold back, and [[affiliation-crwntrdskhrs|The Crown Trades of Okharis]], which keep their accounts and correspondents here, take no side. Trade between the harbor and the clans goes on, and each season it goes on at a slightly worse temper.

## Order and Law {#order-and-law}

A council of priest-nobles, the [Ngolu Nkaring]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, rules the city, and no one outside it can order its trade or its civic affairs; its priesthood defers to the High Pyremant of [[place-zarhanis|The First Flame]] on doctrine and on nothing else. The Master of the Market, [[being-fumbarimakora|Fúmbari Mákora]], keeps [[miscgear-sclskaljekr|The Scales of Kaljekor]]; the Servants of Gold weigh offerings and goods on them, and the [Flameweavers]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"} keep the record of every oath sworn on the beam. The [Council of the Three-Flame Charters]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} of the Crown Trades sits here during [The Market of Flames]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}, chaired by the Steward of the Trades, a moneylender of [[place-vuthraka|The Wild Flame]] acceptable to all three cities. A merchant's bargain is weighed, sealed on the beam and written down, and a dispute over it is heard before the Master of the Market. An oath sworn falsely is held to ruin the swearer within a year and a day, and few traders at Kaljekor test the saying.

## What the City Lives By {#what-the-city-lives-by}

Kaljekor lives by trade and eats from the country behind it. Grain from the [[affiliation-wambuchitrb|Wámbuchi Tribe]] fields at [[place-sembari|Sembari]] travels the coast road and fills the temple granaries, cattle come on the hoof from [[place-gwandoro|Gwandoro]], and the [[affiliation-ngorashatrb|Ngorasha Tribe]] send smoked fish from [[place-wangel|Wangel]] to add to the catch of the city's own boats. The coast gives salt, which is among the city's cheapest goods and its most tightly weighed. The tribute that the clans carry up to the moots is part of this supply.

What goes out pays for what comes in. Cattle, hides from [[place-kuyoro|Kuyoro]], honey and wax from [[place-bokaru|Bokaru]], linen from [[place-chivel|Chivel]], horses from [[place-zendari|Zendari]], bronze, and worked metal leave by the same quays, and [The Royal Goldworkers]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"}, chartered here, make the gold pieces the Crown sends to neighboring courts. The Khelathi houses buy at the quays and pay in the weight-pieces of [[lore-aukhlthcrncy|Au'Khelathu Currency]], and [The Crown Moneylenders]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"} keep correspondent accounts with the [[affiliation-garhalzi|Gar-Hálzi]] and issue letters of credit against them. The rain-fed harvests of Okharis do not depend on the Khelathi flood, so the city is not hungry while the empire's granaries thin.

## Quarters {#quarters}

**The pyramid market** is the temple and the bazaar in one, the stairs lettered by trade. **The quays** run along the harbor under the harbor-wardens' tower, and the warehouses of the Khelathi houses stand behind them. **The Goldworkers' Lane** holds the Royal Goldworkers' shops and is among the best-guarded streets in the kingdom. **The moneylenders' court** holds the Crown Trades' hall, its strongrooms and the correspondents' desks. **The cattle-yards** lie outside the landward gate, where the herds wait for the market.

## Faith and Festivals {#faith-and-festivals}

Kaljekor is the Golden Flame, the gold fire of abundance. Its priests climb from Student of the Flame through Servant of Gold and Guardian of Wealth to Master of the Market, and the council rules above them. Merchants make an offering at the temple before their largest bargains. The great day is [The Market of Flames]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}, the yearly trade festival, when the year's bargains are bound by oath on the Scales, and the saying every trader repeats at a weighing is [Dry salt. Thirsty cow.]{.verse} Apprentices of the Crown Trades hear [[lore-scalesoath|the Oath at the Scales]] on the night before the market opens.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The caravanserais by the landward gate take anyone with money and a beast, the harbor inns take ship's crews and ask for the harbor-wardens' tally, and the Crown Trades' hall puts up chartered merchants. At the Market of Flames every bed in the city is let twice.

## History {#history}

Kaljekor is the city of the Golden Flame, one of the three flames that [[affiliation-nkaruthar|The Eternal Flame]] reads in the three cities ([[lore-okharishist|the History of Okharis]]). The Flameweavers' records of the oaths sworn on the Scales go back further than the newest stair of the pyramid. The count of years is [[lore-qettelgu|The Qet Telgu]], and the coin the Au'Khelathu Currency of the empire, which the harbor handles more than any other place in the kingdom.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The coin and the clan.** A clan's chieftain wants his tribute carried to the moot in a form that counts at the Scales for more than the Tribute-Warden's tally, and he will hire a party to bring the grain past the harbor-wardens.
- **The offers at the quay.** A Khelathi house asks the council for a favor in return for a gift, and the council has neither refused nor agreed. The house wants a party to find out what the Ngolu Nkaring meant by its silence before the next ship sails.
