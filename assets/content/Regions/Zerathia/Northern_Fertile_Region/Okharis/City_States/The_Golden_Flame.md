**meaning:** _The Golden Flame_

- The economic hub, Kaljekor is the harbor of Okharis on the north coast and sits at the crossroads of major trade routes. Its pyramidal temple complex doubles as a marketplace, bustling with merchants from [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Au'Khelathu]] and the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] and beyond.
- Kaljekor is ruled by a council of priest-nobles
- Known for its golden adornments and the wealth of its priest-nobles, Kaljekor’s influence extends far through trade alliances.
