## Overview {#overview}

The First Ember is the flame [[lore-nkaruthardty|Nkaru'thar]] is said to have kindled at the moment of creation. It burns in the deepest chamber of the temple of [[place-zarhanis|The First Flame]], the city the Okharik call Zarhanis, the room under the pyramid, and it has never been put out. The Okharik call it the first fire set down out of the great one, so that the fire had something before it that was itself and was not itself.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The temple of Zarhanis does. The High Pyremant alone may enter the chamber, and he comes out and does not say what he saw. The [Keepers of the First Ember]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"}, an inner circle of Zarhanis priests, tend the flame and guard the deepest chambers; they are sworn to silence about what they witness in its presence, and are said to receive visions directly from Nkaru'thar. It is the keystone of the faith of [[affiliation-nkaruthar|The Eternal Flame]].

## Its Story {#its-story}

[[lore-hymnthrfires|The Hymn of the Three Fires]] tells the ember as the first thing the fire made to look at. The same hymn tells its end: at the last the red fire eats all things, the pyramid at Zarhanis, [[miscgear-sclskaljekr|The Scales of Kaljekor]] and [[miscgear-brndwarden|The Warden's Brand]] among them, and the ember goes back into the fire it was set down from.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

Every fire in Okharis is reckoned descended from it, at a remove or at many removes. A temple lamp, a household's three-wick hearth and a herd-camp fire all count as the ember's descent, and a household's hearth is the household's own line from it.

At the summer solstice every temple fire in Okharis is put out and relit from embers carried out of the chamber, in the festival called [The Rekindling]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}. Pilgrims from every corner of the kingdom travel to the cities, and a carrier who takes the new fire home to a far camp carries the ember's descent with it.

A person put out of the Flame is a **shurem**, ash, to whom no temple gives an ashmark and whose fire is not reckoned descended from the ember. This is what the Flame takes from the cast-out.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **The temple of Zarhanis** guards it against every hand but the High Pyremant's, and the Overlord [[being-takorozanethar|Tákoro Zanethar]], who lives in Zarhanis beside the High Pyremant, answers for the realm that is lit from it.
- **The shurem** of [[place-shuremis|The Ash-Fold]] want the descent that the ember would give them and cannot have it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- A carrier bound home from Zarhanis at the Rekindling loses the ember to the rain, and the lineage's standing depends on whose fire it lights from instead ([[lore-emberroad|The Ember on the Road]]).
- A cold lamp is seen in the granary of the Ash Fold at dusk, and a fire is tended there that has no descent from the ember.
