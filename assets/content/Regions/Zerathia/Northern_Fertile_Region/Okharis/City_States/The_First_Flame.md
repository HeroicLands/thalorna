**meaning:** _The First Flame_

## Overview {#overview}

Zarhanis, the First Flame, is the spiritual capital of [[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]] and its largest city, with about forty thousand people on the crown lands inland of the coast. Its temple complex is the largest in the kingdom and is held to be the birthplace of [[affiliation-nkaruthar|The Eternal Flame]], the faith of [[lore-nkaruthardty|Nkaru'thar]]; murals and carvings on the great pyramid show the creation of the world. The King, [[being-takorozanethar|Tákoro Zanethar]], lives here beside the High Pyremant, who rules Zarhanis and its temple lands and advises the King, and the city holds the realm's first fire, its registers and its granaries. It makes less for sale than [[place-kaljekor|The Golden Flame]] (Kaljekor) and fights less than [[place-vuthraka|The Wild Flame]] (Vuthraka). What it supplies is legitimacy, and the rest of the kingdom pays for that in grain, cattle and riders.

## Arriving {#arriving}

Every road of the crown lands runs to the pyramid, which stands up over the barley fields long before the first house, and the herders' tracks from the grazing country east and south join the roads a day out. Pilgrim camps ring the city at festival time. At other seasons the gate that matters is the registry gate, where temple clerks ask a visitor for his lineage, his clan and his city and write all three in a book. A traveler who answers "none" is sent to the guest-houses of the outer court and given no more.

## Politics {#present}

The King, [[being-takorozanethar|Tákoro Zanethar]], lives at Zarhanis beside the High Pyremant, and the city is where the realm waits. The Oracle, who speaks the god's answer, has given none this year to the question the King put to it, whether he calls the Host, and the silence is read here first and hardest. The temple's priests hold that the Flame asks the realm to wait. The King may not call the Host, answer an embassy or set the tribute until the answer comes, and nobody at court proposes that he should.

[[miscgear-emberfirst|The First Ember]] burns in its chamber as it always has, and the festivals fill the city with pilgrims and with chieftains, who come up to the moots with the tribute they owe and a count of what they have been asked for. The Chief Scribe's registers hold what is owed and what is late, and the lateness has grown. Khelathi embassies, which the city treats as requests, reach Zarhanis for an audience and wait in its guest-houses for an answer the King cannot give. Meanwhile their gifts go on to the chieftains.

## Order and Law {#order-and-law}

The High Pyremant rules Zarhanis and its temple lands, advises the King and decides doctrine for all three cities. The King holds the court, and the Chief Scribe holds the registers: tribute owed, labor due and land held. A dispute between villagers or townsmen of the realm may be carried to the courts of the temple-cities, and the Zarhanis scribes can produce the count of a herd or a debt from the registers. A feud between two kindreds is settled in cattle, and the court's work is to see the cattle counted while the Keeper of the Feud records the blood owed and the blood paid. The shamans of [[affiliation-yenorzhrng|The Spirit Path]] (Yenor Zohiring) come and go without rank, and the temple court has no procedure for what happens when a priest and a shaman disagree about a matter in the city. The Tribute-Wardens ride out from Zarhanis with an escort and a scribe and return to it with the dues.

## What the City Lives By {#what-the-city-lives-by}

Zarhanis eats from the crown lands: the barley, sorghum, flax, dates and figs of the rain-fed country around it, and the grain and fruit that the tribes owe. The [[affiliation-wambuchitrb|Wámbuchi Tribe]] send barley and sorghum through the grain market at [[place-sembari|Sembari]], the [[affiliation-kirofatrb|Kirofa Tribe]] send dried dates and figs through [[place-lumbari|Lumbari]], and the [[affiliation-zhikandetrb|Zhíkande Tribe]] send linen woven at [[place-chivel|Chivel]]. The [[affiliation-khamundatrb|Khamunda Tribe]] fire the clay jars that stand in the temple granaries, so that the stores of the First Flame are kept in Khamunda ware. Meat arrives on the hoof from the [[affiliation-talumengtrb|Tálumeng Tribe]] herds by way of [[place-gwandoro|Gwandoro]]. The Granary-Keepers hold the stores on which the city lives between harvests, and the realm reckons their ledgers its truest measure of power.

The city sends out metal and the sanction of the Flame. [The King's Smiths]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"}, chartered here, make the tools, weapons and prestige metalwork that the clans cannot make for themselves; their charcoal comes from the forest edge through [[place-mbuloro|Mbuloro]]. [The Spice-Merchants]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"}, chartered here as well, send the savannah herbs and dried fruits that travel best. [[affiliation-crwntrdskhrs|The Crown Trades of Okharis]] keep a hall for their moneylenders, and credit for the court's undertakings runs through it.

## Quarters {#quarters}

**The pyramid precinct** holds the temple complex, the High Pyremant's house and, in the room under the pyramid, the chamber of [[miscgear-emberfirst|the First Ember]], which only the High Pyremant may enter. **The King's court** stands beside it, and the Chief Scribe's registry fills the court's western side. **The Granary Court** is a square of pits and jar-halls, kept by the Granary-Keepers and guarded by temple men. **Smiths' Row** runs along the city's leeward edge, where the King's Smiths work their forges. **The outer court** holds the guest-houses, where Khelathi embassies and the chieftains' parties wait. **The pilgrim ground** lies outside the houses and fills only for the festivals.

## Faith and Festivals {#faith-and-festivals}

Zarhanis is the Spirit Flame, the white fire of faith, wisdom and prophecy. Its priests climb from Student of the Flame through Scholar of the Fire and Guardian of Spirits to Prophet of Light, and the Prophet of Light, the Ambelari Zheking, holds communion with the ancestors on [The Night of Ashes]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} at midwinter. The [Keepers of the First Ember]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"} tend the flame and are sworn to silence about what they see. At the summer solstice every temple fire in the kingdom is put out and relit from the First Ember in [The Rekindling]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}, and carriers from every clan take the new fire home. A priest who seeks high office keeps three fires alight for three days, through storm and wind, in [The Trial of Three Flames]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The temple guest-houses of the outer court take pilgrims, embassies and anyone with a clan's introduction, and a stay is entered in the registry. At the Rekindling they are full, and a party camps on the pilgrim ground among the clans, where its place is settled by lineage. The Crown Trades' hall rents rooms to merchants with a charter.

## History {#history}

Zarhanis is believed to be the birthplace of the faith of the Eternal Flame ([[lore-okharishist|the History of Okharis]]), and its pyramid carries the story in its murals. The compact of the [Three-Flame Settlement]{.name data-type="lore/law"} puts the city's temple, registers and metalwork at the center of the realm's life, and the realm counts its years by [[lore-qettelgu|The Qet Telgu]] as the empire does.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The late count.** The Chief Scribe's registers show the tribute of three clans a season late and short. The Granary-Keepers want to know whether the clans are withholding it, holding it for a Khelathi offer or have none to give, and a party that can ride out and count herds is useful to them.
- **The offer past the gate.** A Khelathi envoy has waited in the outer court for an audience the King cannot give. He wants an honest guide to a chieftain's fold, and the court wants to know who goes with him.
