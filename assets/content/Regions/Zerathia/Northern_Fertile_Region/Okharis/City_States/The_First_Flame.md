**meaning:** _The First Flame_

- Known as the spiritual capital, Zarhanis houses the largest temple complex, believed to have been the birthplace of the kingdom’s religion.
- The temple is adorned with murals and carvings detailing the creation of the world by [[lore-nkaruthardty|Nkaru'thar]].
- Ruled by the **High Pyremant**, the spiritual leader of [[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]] and advisor to the overlord.

## Politics {#present}

The King, [[being-takorozanethar|Tákoro Zanethar]], lives at Zarhanis beside the High Pyremant, and the city is where the realm waits. The Oracle, who speaks the god's answer, has given none this year to the question the King put to it, whether he calls the Host, and the silence is read here first and hardest. The temple's priests hold that the Flame asks the realm to wait. The King may not call the Host, answer an embassy or set the tribute until the answer comes, and nobody at court proposes that he should.

The [[miscgear-emberfirst|First Ember]] burns in its chamber as it always has, and the festivals fill the city with pilgrims and with chieftains, who come up to the moots with the tribute they owe and a count of what they have been asked for. The Chief Scribe's registers hold what is owed and what is late, and the lateness has grown. Khelathi embassies, which the city treats as requests, reach Zarhanis for an audience and wait in its guest-houses for an answer the King cannot give. Meanwhile their gifts go on to the chieftains.

## History {#history}

Zarhanis is believed to be the birthplace of the faith of the Eternal Flame ([[lore-okharishist|the History of Okharis]]).
