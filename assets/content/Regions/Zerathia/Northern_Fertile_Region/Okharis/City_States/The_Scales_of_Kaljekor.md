## Overview {#overview}

The Scales of Kaljekor are a gilded balance of two pans, a beam, a post and gold set along the beam, standing in the temple-marketplace of [[place-kaljekor|The Golden Flame]], the city called Kaljekor. Offerings are weighed on them in the morning before the market opens, and after that a bargain to be sealed is brought to the beam.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The temple of Kaljekor does. [Ndumbari Zhabing]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Master of the Market, keeps the Scales on which trade oaths are sworn, and [the Servants of Gold]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"} weigh the offerings on them. The [Flameweavers]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"} keep the records of every oath sworn on the beam.

## Its Story {#its-story}

Okharik clerks tell [[lore-scalesoath|The Oath at the Scales]] to the apprentices of [[affiliation-crwntrdskhrs|The Crown Trades of Okharis]] on the night before the market opens. The Flameweavers' records of the oaths go back further than the pyramid's newest stair.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

An oath sworn on the beam is held unbreakable at [The Market of Flames]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}, the yearly trade festival. A buyer and a seller come to the beam with the goods, the goods go in one pan and the city's flame-cut weights in the other, and the Servant at the post says the weight aloud. Each party lays a right hand on the gold and swears.

The priests of the temple teach that a merchant who swears falsely on the Scales is ruined within a year and a day, and that a merchant who keeps his oath prospers. The Flameweavers write beside every oath what became of the man a year and a day later, and a trader may pay a clerk's fee to read the record.

The Scales weigh only what is put on the beam. The priests teach that the Flame, and not the beam, judges what was given.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **Every trader at the Market of Flames** wants the Scales to stand behind his bargain, and many want them to be seen standing behind it.
- **A merchant who swore falsely** and sees the year and a day running out wants the oath unsworn, and has no way to unswear it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- A merchant swore falsely on the Scales, the year and a day is nearly out, and his family hires someone to find out what is coming for him ([[doc-okharisadvguide|the Okharis guide]]).
- Two traders each swear a true weight at the beam and each cheat the other. The Flameweavers' record must say whether the oaths were false.
