**meaning:** _The Wild Flame_

## Overview {#overview}

Vuthraka, the Wild Flame, is the military stronghold of [[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]], a garrison town of nearly ten thousand people on the southern frontier, where the grassland meets the wall of the [[place-cntrlrnfrsts|Central Rainforests]]. Its temple statues of the demi-gods of war and chaos are believed to guard the frontier. The Wild Flame is the red fire of strength and protection.

## History {#history}

Vuthraka is one of the three flames of [[affiliation-nkaruthar|The Eternal Flame]], the flame of war and protection ([[lore-okharishist|the History of Okharis]]). The [Ashborn]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"} chant [[lore-ashbornsong|The Song of the Burnt Ground]] on the march out of the town, and the song tells how a company under [Otham of the Brand]{.name data-type="being/npc"} held the edge of a burn on [[affiliation-talumengtrb|Tálumeng Tribe]]'s grazing and how the Brand was laid on seven more arms that year.

## The Present {#present}

The Warden of the Flame holds Vuthraka as the frontier's guard and, in war, as commander of the armies of Okharis, and this year the office asks more of the realm for it. The Warden argues that the frontier cannot be held on less and reads the Oracle's silence as a reason to arm it.

The garrison's yearly draw of riders comes in late and short while the chieftains weigh the Khelathi offers. The King cannot call the Host and the Warden cannot call it without him, so the garrison drills and waits. Meanwhile a young warrior of a village tribe, who came for [The Trial of Flames]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, has been turned away: the Commander of Fire has not lit the sacred fire for him, and the town has been given no reason.

## The Holder {#holder}

The Warden of the Flame, Korthari Nkaring in the temple's tongue, has one holder at a time and joins the rule of the town to command of the realm's armies in war. Beside him the Commander of Fire, Zhonkari Nkaring, keeps [[miscgear-brndwarden|the Warden's Brand]]. The Warden defers to the High Pyremant of [[place-zarhanis|The First Flame]] in doctrine and otherwise acts with considerable autonomy.

## Garrison {#garrison}

The garrison is the Ashborn, warrior-priests who have passed the Trial and bear the scars of the Brand, and the Soldiers of Fire and Guardians of Battle, the ordained and senior warrior-priests of the temple. The riders of the Host come from the tribes under the Captain of the Host.

## Government, Faith and Trade {#government-faith-trade}

The town lives on what the realm sends: grain, cattle and riders from the tribes. The forest margin adds timber and charcoal from [[place-mbuloro|Mbuloro]], hides from [[place-kuyoro|Kuyoro]], and honey and wax from [[place-bokaru|Bokaru]]. [The River-Pilots]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"}, chartered at Vuthraka, run the seasonal rivers and the trade routes to the southern jungle margin, and the Crown Moneylender [Mekhar Saharil]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Steward of the Trades, is of this town.

## Within the Walls {#within-the-walls}

**The temple court** holds the war-statues and the red fire. **The Brand chamber**, where the Commander of Fire heats the Brand in sacred fire, lies behind it. **The barracks yards** hold the Ashborn's quarters. **The south gate** opens on the forest track and is shut at dusk.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The short draw.** The Captain of the Host wants to know which clan is holding its riders back for a Khelathi offer, and the Warden counts each absent rider against the clans.
- **The Commander's fire.** The young warrior refused the Trial needs a party to learn why the Commander of Fire will not light the fire for him.
