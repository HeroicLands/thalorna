**meaning:** _The Wild Flame_

- The military stronghold, Vuthraka guards the southern frontier of [[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]]. Its temple complex features intricate statues of the demi-gods of war and chaos, believed to protect the city from outside threats.
- The **Warden of the Flame**, its ruler, is a warrior-priest who commands Okharis’s armies during times of conflict.

## The Present {#present}

The Warden of the Flame holds Vuthraka as the frontier's guard and, in war, as commander of the armies of [[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]], and this year the office asks more of the realm for it. The grain, cattle and riders that the garrison draws from the tribes are what the tribes mean when they say the tribute is rising, and Vuthraka is where they say it. The Warden argues that a frontier at the edge of the rainforest wall cannot be held on less, and reads the Oracle's silence as a reason to arm it.

The Host that would march from here is the strength the Khelathi houses want. Its riders come from the tribes, and the Captain of the Host, who commands men drawn from the cities and the tribes together, finds the numbers uncertain: the garrison's yearly draw of riders comes in late and short while the chieftains weigh the Khelathi offers. The Warden counts each absent rider as a charge against the clans. The Commander of Fire keeps [[miscgear-brndwarden|the Warden's Brand]], and the [Ashborn]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"} who bear its scars stay at their posts. The King cannot call the Host and the Warden cannot call it without him, so the garrison drills and waits.
