## Overview {#overview}

The Warden's Brand is a ceremonial iron blade. It is kept at [[place-vuthraka|The Wild Flame]], the garrison town that guards the southern frontier of [[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]] and that the Okharik call Vuthraka, and it is heated in sacred fire to mark the warriors who have proved themselves in [The Trial of Three Flames]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}. The Okharik also call that rite the Trial of Flames.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The Commander of Fire at Vuthraka holds it, an office of [[affiliation-nkaruthar|The Eternal Flame]] that goes by the title Zhonkari Nkaring, and conducts the Trial. The Warden of the Flame, the warrior-priest who rules Vuthraka and commands the armies of Okharis in war, rules the town it lies in.

## Its Story {#its-story}

[[lore-hymnthrfires|The Hymn of the Three Fires]] names the Brand at Vuthraka among the things the red fire eats at the end. The [Ashborn]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"} chant [[lore-ashbornsong|The Song of the Burnt Ground]] on the march out of Vuthraka: a company under [Otham of the Brand]{.name data-type="being/npc"} held the edge of a burn on [[affiliation-talumengtrb|Tálumeng Tribe]]'s grazing, and the Brand was laid on seven more arms that year.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

The Brand marks a warrior who has passed the Trial, and what it leaves is a scar. The scars are worn with pride, and the faithful take them as proof of [[lore-nkaruthardty|Nkaru'thar]]'s favor. The warrior-priests of Vuthraka who have passed the Trial and bear the Brand are the Ashborn, and they are said to be immune to fear.

The Ashborn fight with the scarification visible on their arms and chest. The Brand is the instrument of the rite and is not carried to war.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **A candidate for the Trial** wants the Brand laid on his arm, and the Commander of Fire decides whether he has proved himself.
- **A foreign priesthood or a raider** who wants to humble Vuthraka wants the Brand stolen or the Trial broken, and the Ashborn treat both as an attack on the frontier.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- A young warrior of a village tribe comes to Vuthraka for the Trial and finds the Commander of Fire unwilling to light the sacred fire for him. He needs a party to learn why.
- A caravan guard in the city of [[place-kaljekor|The Golden Flame]] shows scars he says the Brand gave him, and the Commander of Fire wants to know who laid them.
