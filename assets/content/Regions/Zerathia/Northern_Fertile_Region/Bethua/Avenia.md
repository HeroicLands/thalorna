## Overview {#overview}

**Avenia** (about 3,700 people) is an olive-hill town on the western upland of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]], not far from [[place-ashira|Oshira]]. The groves feed it, and the oil is pressed and sold to the coast.

Press-owners, pickers and mule-drivers work in rhythm. A blight spreads from grove to grove, and a House that withholds the press stalls the harvest.
