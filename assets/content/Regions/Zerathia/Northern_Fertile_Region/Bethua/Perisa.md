## Overview {#overview}

**Perisa** (about 8,500 people) is the greatest of the aqueduct-head towns, where the main northern channel leaves the hills for the plain, a long day inland from [[place-bethura|Bethura]]. The irrigated strips below the head feed it, and the engineer-priestesses who keep the channel eat from them.

The engineering corps trains its surveyors here, and the town's market sells the plain's wheat to the coast. A breach in the channel above floods the lower town and drains the strips below, and the priestesses answer for it to the Council of Houses.
