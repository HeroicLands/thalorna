## Overview {#overview}

**Meliora** (about 6,400 people) is a wheat-market town on the central plain of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]], between [[place-perisa|Perisa]] and the coast road. The irrigated strips around it feed it, and its market sells their grain to the coastal cities.

Grain-factors, measurers and cart-owners keep its market-square busy from harvest to midwinter. A rigged measure costs a factor her license, and a lean harvest tempts the House to hold the grain for the capital.
