## Overview {#overview}

**Palisa** (about 4,800 people) is a wool-and-cheese town on the grassland margin of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]]. The irrigated strips along the stream feed it, and its flocks supply the coastal weavers.

Shepherds, fullers and dyers share its lower quarter. A drought thins the pasture, and a wolf-year sends the shepherds into the town for shelter.
