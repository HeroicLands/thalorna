Bethua has two histories, one before 615 AF and one after, and the second is built from the first.

## A Daughter of Pelagora

Helionite cities with more sons than land sent colonists over the sea in the centuries after the First Games. About 650 BF Pelagora planted Bethua on the southern shore of the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] and governed it through a council in Pelagora, which appointed the officers who kept its harbor and collected its dues ([[lore-daughtercts|the Daughter-Cities]]). The colonists brought the Helonik tongue and the faiths of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] with them. The matriarchal traditions, the Aureldian worship and the Helonik-descended language of the Matriarchy all date to this deep colonial past ([[lore-bethuanclt|the Bethuans]]).

The council's rule lasted twelve centuries. In 314 BF, when Pelagora's assembly voted to treat alone with the Vilérian Republic, the city kept its fleet, its walls and its overseas daughter-cities, Bethua among them, and held Bethua as an allied city's colony through the imperial age ([[lore-heladpelagtrty|the Pelagoran Treaty]]).

## The Separation

About 615 AF Bethua fought a war of separation from Pelagora, and won it with Khelathi gold, engineers and loans under the queen Meqes XI ([[lore-bethuasep|the Separation of Bethua]]). The war ended the colony and began a patronage: the court at Galezkara saw in a free Bethua a partner on the southern shore of the sea that kept Helonik and Vilérian ambitions in Midhalión divided, and it has cultivated the Matriarchy since with subsidy and loans.

## The Rebuilding

The peace was built in Khelathi forms, and [[lore-bethuasep|the Separation of Bethua]] tells how the treasury, the water corps and the accounts took them. What the war did not replace was the Helonik core of the realm: its tongue, its faiths and its sacred and clan institutions, which [[place-bethura|Bethura]] keeps beside the Khelathi offices ([[affiliation-mtrrchybth|the Matriarchy of Bethua]]).

## The Privateer War

The Matriarchy is a naval power, and the fleet wages a licensed privateer war on Vilérian shipping that the Khelathi court cannot be accused of ([[affiliation-bethuanflt|the Bethuan Fleet]]). Pelagora, which lost its colony and the right it held for twelve centuries, counts the loss as a debt, and Helionite captains hunt Bethuan privateers on sight.

## The Marches and the Frontier

Bethua claims the [[place-sthmrchsrgn|South Marches]], where herd-clans pay it tribute for grazing rights, and the frontier clans of the Okharik east pay tribute to its authorities on the same terms ([[lore-okharishist|the History of Okharis]]). The frontier with the empire was never precisely drawn.

## What It Left {#what-it-left}

- **The Helonik core beside the Khelathi offices.** Tongue, faiths and clan institutions stay Bethua's own.
- **The privateer war.** The fleet fights a licensed war on Vilérian shipping.
- **The open frontier.** The line with the empire and the tribute of the Marches are unsettled.
