## Overview {#overview}

**Ormira** (about 6,000 people) is an aqueduct-head town on the hill-edge of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]], where cisterns store the spring melt for the summer. Its fields and orchards feed it, and it sells fruit and legumes to the plain.

Cistern-keepers and terrace-farmers work under the head-priestess of the cisterns. A cracked cistern wall lets a year's water out in a night, and the town's House fines the keeper who missed the crack.
