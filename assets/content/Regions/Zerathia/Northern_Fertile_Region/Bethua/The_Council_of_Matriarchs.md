## Overview {#overview}

"Twice a year the Great Houses sit in one hall and agree to disagree in writing," a factor of the federation tells a foreign trader. The **Council of Matriarchs** is the governing body of the federation of the [[affiliation-bthntrdhss|Bethuan Trade-Houses]], the assembled matriarchs of the Great Houses, the _An'Bet_. It is a commercial federation and not an organ of the state, a third, de facto power beside the [[affiliation-sacredcollege|Sacred College]] and the [[affiliation-councilhouses|Council of Houses]]. It meets twice a year at the federation's principal hall in [[place-bethura|Bethura]].

## Who Sits {#who-sits}

There are perhaps a dozen Great Houses. Among them stand House Khalath-Mara, the oldest and most prestigious, whose ancestral seat is at [[place-ashira|Oshira]]; House Bera-Sehel, horse-breeders and suppliers to armies; and House Othris-Khanu, the principal shipping House. The matriarchs are themselves Dómissai who sit in the Council of Houses, and many sit in the Sacred College besides: the institutions are distinct and the women who staff them are not. Its senior voice by age and precedence is Matriarch [[being-aseliakhlthmr|Aselia of House Khalath-Mara]], who has held her seat for thirty-five years and is the dominant voice when she chooses to speak.

## How It Decides {#how-it-decides}

Each session the Council chooses a Speaker of the Council by consensus from the matriarchs present. She presides, frames the questions and announces the rulings, and the office rotates; the federation has no standing chief, and its members prefer it that way. The Council sets the standing protocols by which the Houses recognize each other's letters of credit, adjudicates commercial disputes between Houses, coordinates federation-wide diplomatic positions, and admits or expels member houses.

## The Present {#present}

The credit the federation rests on is under pressure. Viléria has persuaded enough of the Danvar to stop honoring the letters of credit of a major House, and the House is bleeding. The Houses that profited from the privateers and the Houses that carry the ledgers disagree about how hard to press the Purple Warrant, and the matriarchs are courted by the reformist priestesses of the [[affiliation-courtveil|Court of the Veil]].

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

A party meets the Council through a House's letter of credit, which another House's matriarch honors or refuses on the Council's protocol. A trader who wants a seat at the federation's table needs a House to speak for him.
