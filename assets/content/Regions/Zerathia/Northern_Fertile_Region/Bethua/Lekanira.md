## Overview {#overview}

**Lekanira** (about 4,300 people) is a legume-and-millet town in the dry interior of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]], at the foot of a qanat line. Its strip feeds it, and its beans are sold to the Fleet.

Bean-merchants, porters and cistern-wardens work the yards. A failed qanat condemns the strip in a season, and the town's women meet in council the same week.
