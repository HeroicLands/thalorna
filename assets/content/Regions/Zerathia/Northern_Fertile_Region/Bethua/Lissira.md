## Overview {#overview}

**Lissira** (about 5,200 people) is a salt-and-fishing port on the western coast of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]], a day's ride from [[place-ashira|Oshira]]. The pans and the fishing boats earn the coin that buys its bread from the inland strips.

Salters, net-makers and coasting skippers keep the harbor busy. A raiding galley from the Vilérian Sea makes the town man its walls, and a failed salt harvest puts its people in debt to the grain-sellers.
