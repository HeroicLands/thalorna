## Overview {#overview}

**Oshira** (about 7,000 people) is a town on [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]]'s western coast and the ancestral seat of [House Khalath-Mara]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, the oldest and most prestigious of the Great Houses. It lies on the coast road west of [[place-bethura|Bethura]], on a bay open to the north, with the olive uplands of [[place-avenia|Avenia]] at its back and the salt port of [[place-lissira|Lissira]] a day's ride along the shore. Bethuans call it a city when they speak of the House, and it is a town in size and a capital in how its House carries itself.

## Arriving {#arriving}

The coast road enters from the east through terraced vineyards, and the first thing a traveler sees is the House's press-yards on the slope above the bay. The harbor works are laid out along the _en-telekas_, the stone quay, with a customs-house at its head where the _et-degan_, the toll, is paid in Khelathi coin. The town has no wall. A [[affiliation-spearsisterhood|Spear-Sisterhood]] post at the road's eastern bend and the harbor's own watch keep order, and strangers are asked their House and their cargo before they are asked their name.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Oshira lives by wine and oil. The vineyards of the coastal hills and the groves of the western upland feed the House's presses, and the town's coopers, potters and jar-makers work for the press-yards. The strips of the interior send grain by wagon to feed the town, since the hills grow vines and olives rather than wheat. Every season the House fills the holds of the convoys that cross the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] to the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]], and the harbor's wine-houses sell the rest to the coast road's traders. The yards employ more of the town's women than any other work, and the House's factors keep the accounts in the Khelathi style.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The town stands in the coastal canton of the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethua]], and the Hégissa [[being-ibinethsufrs|Ibinetha of House Sufiris]] holds its courts and its levy in the Metrissa's name. In Oshira her word and the House's overlap, and a dispute between a ship's master and a factor of Khalath-Mara goes to a court whose judge dines at the House's table. Matriarch [[being-aseliakhlthmr|Aselia of House Khalath-Mara]], the senior voice of the [[affiliation-councilmatriarchs|Council of Matriarchs]], keeps her family seat here and is more often at Bethura than at home. [[affiliation-bethuanflt|The Bethuan Fleet]] holds the Harbor-Mistress answerable for the convoy berths. The scholar [[being-juthrjmthn|Júthara Jamithon]] makes her home in the town.

## The Present {#present}

Oshira's wealth does not depend on the flood. A low river leaves the Khelathi valley short of grain, and the Khelathi buyers across the sea are paying more for what it can get, wine and oil among it. The House's factors are filling the yards ahead of the season, and its holds are fuller than the convoy schedule can carry. The same factors watch the Metrissa's choice on the surplus grain. The [[affiliation-councilhouses|Council of Houses]] presses for a sale to the Khelathi patron, and Khalath-Mara's name carries weight in that room. Aselia has not said which way the House leans. A sale to the patron lifts the House's Khelathi accounts, and a refusal keeps the coastal cisterns full for the dry year that the [[affiliation-sacredcollege|Sacred College]] fears.

The Spear-Sisterhood's patrols are drawn east and south, and the coast road west of Oshira is thinly watched. Carters bound for the Okharik frontier ask the Harbor-Mistress for an escort and are told to wait for the next convoy.

## Where a Party Lodges {#lodging}

The House keeps a guest-hall for correspondents of other Houses, and a Great House's letter opens its doors. Everyone else lodges in the wine-houses along the quay, where a room above the cooperage costs a little more than a night in a Bethuan inn and carries the smell of oak and must. The jar-makers' quarter on the road to Avenia lets cheap pallets to carters and teamsters.
