## Overview {#overview}

The Bethua Region is the middle of the
[[place-nrthrnfrtlrgn|Northern Fertile Region]], between [[place-okharisrgn|Okharis]] to the west and
[[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu]] to the east—the coast and hinterland held
by the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethua]], and the part of [[place-xerathia|Zerathia]]'s
northern shore that faces most directly across the
[[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] toward [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris]].

## Geography

The **coastal strip** is the region's foundation, and it is here at its widest
and most reliable anywhere along the northern shore: sea-tempered winters and
moderate summers carrying citrus, grape, olive, fig, date-palm and
pomegranate. The coastal cities, the capital [[place-bethura|Bethura]] on its
bay among them, are among the oldest and most cosmopolitan in Zerathia, and have been the continent's principal point of contact with the
north for as long as the two continents have known one another.

**Inland**, rainfall falls away sharply and the interior is semi-arid
grassland, productive only where water is brought to it. Bethua's answer has
been millennia of hydraulic engineering—aqueducts, tunnel qanats, cisterns
and canal networks whose upkeep employs tens of thousands year-round, and
whose engineering corps is one of the most prestigious institutions of the
realm. Wheat, barley, millet and legumes grow inside the irrigated strips;
between them, open grassland pastures sheep, cattle and horses.

The interior is semi-arid savanna in the west and grows more arid eastward,
to chaparral at the border with Au'Khelathu.

Two roads cross the country. The **coast road** is the one land route that joins
the three countries of the northern shore: it runs west from [[place-gezazabu|Gezazabu]], the western delta port of
[[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu]], through [[place-bethura|Bethura]] to
[[place-kaljekor|Kaljekor]] in [[place-okharisrgn|Okharis]]. A second road runs
south from Bethura across the irrigated strips to the frontier posts and the
wells of the [[place-sthmrchsrgn|South Marches]]. By sea, Bethura is the
shortest crossing to the Tarvénian shore.

## Where the People Live

Three million people live in the region, and about one in twenty lives in a city or town. The coastal strip and the irrigated strips along the aqueducts and qanats hold nearly all of them: every town stands at a channel's head, on a harbor, or on the Marches road. Between the strips lies grassland with flocks and few people, and the dry interior holds almost no one beyond the qanat villages. The capital [[place-bethura|Bethura]] is the one city, with a second port at [[place-tharenia|Tharenia]], and the towns feed on the grain of the strips the engineer-priestesses keep alive.

## Peoples and Culture

The region is Bethuan, and its ruling class, priesthood and engineering corps
are all female. Its cultural gravity pulls north across the sea rather than
toward its immediate neighbors: its language carries ancient Pelwar-linked
roots shared with [[place-helionis|Helionis]], and it venerates the
[[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]] in a matriarchal rite rather
than any home-grown tradition.

## Politics and Power

The region is the territory of the Matriarchy of Bethua, which won its separation from its metropole, [[affiliation-pelagora|Pelagora]], about 615 AF with Khelathi backing ([[lore-bethuasep|the Separation of Bethua]]).
Along its western margins, Okharik frontier clans pay tribute to Bethuan
authorities in exchange for grazing rights; its eastern frontier with
Au'Khelathu Region is poorly defined and a recurring source of
friction.

## Current Situation {#present}

The Khelathi flood has come low two years running, and the valley to the east is short of grain. Bethua's strips do not depend on the flood. The [[affiliation-orderwaters|Order of the Waters]] waters them from aqueducts, tunnel qanats and cisterns that draw on the seasonal streams off the spurs and on deep wells, so the wheat, barley and millet of the irrigated country stand green while the black soil of [[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu Region]] goes to dust. That makes the strips' grain the one reliable surplus on the northern shore, and every granary on the coast and at the channel-heads is counted twice, once by the [[affiliation-councilhouses|Council of Houses]] and once by the [[affiliation-sacredcollege|Sacred College]].

The region's politics turn on what becomes of it. The Council of Houses presses the Metrissa to sell the surplus to Bethua's Khelathi patron, and the College wants it kept in the cisterns against a dry year of Bethua's own. The Metrissa has not chosen, and her standing with both councils turns on the choice ([[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethua]]).

Along the eastern frontier the strain shows first. Khelathi herders graze closer to the water in the dry years and push toward the irrigated strips. On the southern line, the posts of the southern cantons wait for the tribute of the [[place-sthmrchsrgn|South Marches]], which is late. The [[affiliation-spearsisterhood|Spear-Sisterhood]]'s patrols are stretched between the eastern frontier and those southern posts, and the towns on the Marches road feel it when a caravan arrives with no escort.
