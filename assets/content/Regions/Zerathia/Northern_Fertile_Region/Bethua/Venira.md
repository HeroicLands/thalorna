## Overview {#overview}

**Venira** (about 7,200 people) is an aqueduct-head town on the middle plain of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]], where a branch channel divides to feed three irrigated strips. The strips feed the town, and its granaries hold the strips' wheat for the coast.

Channel-wardens, gate-keepers and grain-measurers run it, and the head-priestess of the gate rules on disputes over water. A neighbor who steals water at night answers to the wardens, and a dry year sets the strips against each other.
