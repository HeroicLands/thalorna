## Overview {#overview}

**Tessira** (about 4,600 people) is a cistern town on the dry southwestern plain of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]], at the end of a long branch channel. A single irrigated strip feeds it, and its wells are the last reliable water for a day's ride.

Shepherds from the grassland bring their flocks to its troughs and pay in wool and cheese. A failing branch channel leaves it dry first, and the town's House lets the shepherds go before it lets the strips go.
