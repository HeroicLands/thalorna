## Overview {#overview}

**Zenira** (about 3,500 people) is the school-town of the engineering corps in [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]], where young women learn to survey a channel and sink a well. Its own strip and the corps' stipend feed it.

Student-engineers, instructors and surveyors' families make up its households. A pupil who botches a leveling can ruin a village's strip, and the corps' examiners are feared for it.
