## Overview {#overview}

**Tharenia** (about 9,900 people) is the second port of the Matriarchy, on a sheltered bay a day's sail east of [[place-bethura|Bethura]]. Fishing fleets and the olive and wine barges of the coast land at its quays, and the grain that feeds it comes down the coast road from the aqueduct-fed fields of the interior.

Shipwrights, rope-walkers and cargo-brokers crowd its waterfront, and the Fleet keeps a squadron in the harbor. Its pilots resent paying the capital's harbor dues, and a winter gale that closes the bay stops the coast's whole export trade.
