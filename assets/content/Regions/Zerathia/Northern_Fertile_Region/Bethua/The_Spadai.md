## Overview {#overview}

The **Spadai** (singular _Spados_, "the gelded swords") are the sworn cadre of eunuch-warriors of the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethua]]. They are the single great exception to the prohibition on armed men, and they are trusted with steel because they have been set outside the male threat the matriarchy fears. A Spados has no House to raise, no sons to set up as patriarchs and no stake in any male revolt.

## Membership {#membership}

The realm takes boys young, gelds them and binds them by oath into its service. A mother who puts a beautiful and gifted son forward does it with pride. The Spadai are praised in song and ceremony and are quartered and equipped at the crown's and the Houses' expense: the most honored men in Bethua, held up as the ideal of what a man may become once he has surrendered the things that make men dangerous. A cadre is sworn at the head of the great quay of [[place-bethura|Bethura]] to the singing of [[lore-dorashospraise|the Praise of Dorashos]] by the [[affiliation-lusinia|Faith of Lusinia]].

## Leaders {#leaders}

The Spadai are a small, elite corps under the command of the Stratégissa and her officers, serving like any other arm of the host. They fight alongside the women of the [[affiliation-spearsisterhood|Spear-Sisterhood]], and their men serve as marines on the fleet's war-galleys.

## What It Wants Now {#present}

The order is paid for by the men it holds and the women it casts out. Some of the Houseless in the south are the Spadai the realm unmade, now loose in the South Marches with a grievance the [[affiliation-courtveil|Court of the Veil]] would rather not admit it made. Cast-out Spadai ride in [[affiliation-jcklsthmrchs|the Jackals of the South Marches]], a warband of the Houseless under [[being-argyrirjckl|Argirira the Jackal]].

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

A party meets the Spadai on a quay or a galley, armed and unremarked: the one place in Bethuan life where a man carries steel and is celebrated for it. A foreign man who wears a sword is not one of them.
