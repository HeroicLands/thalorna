## Overview {#overview}

Sehela is a grain-village of about 650 people in the dry southern interior of the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethua]], and it exists because water is brought to it. A tunnel-well, the _katelun_, runs underground from a mother-well in the hills to the village's fields, and the green on one side of the last gate and the straw on the other is the line every Bethuan child learns. The village is the holding of [House Khalithon]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, and [[being-lmbhrpdhnn|Lámbira Pédhinion]] was born in it.

## The Village {#the-village}

Sehela is ordered the Bethuan way. A household turns on its women: the mothers and grandmothers hold what the family owns and govern its affairs, the men labor in the fields as the dependents they are under law, and the eldest daughter is raised from the first as the one who will inherit and answer for the household. Every free girl of the village is trained young in arms and in the [[skill-charissa|Kharessa]], and the women who train the village levy watch for the quick ones.

Mud-brick houses stand in rows along the channels, and a gate in each channel lets the water out to each field in its turn, so much for each household, written down. The wells that served the village before the line came are dry or brackish, and a few are still shown to visitors.

## The Holding {#the-holding}

The fields belong to House Khalithon, the water belongs to the [[affiliation-orderwaters|Order of the Waters]], and the _katelun_ belongs to the realm. The village grows barley and wheat on its tenants' strips, with millet on the poorer ground and a few rows of legumes between. The harvest is carted north on the Marches road to the strip towns, and the village keeps what its households need and sends the rest to the House's granary. The sisters of the Order keep the gate-roll and walk the line each season for cracks, and a tenant who opens a gate out of turn answers to them. [[lore-risakostest|The Testimony of Rísakos]] tells the story of the line's making and is read aloud at the head of every new mother-well. The village was dying when the line reached it, and it counts the first field in the water as the day it was saved.

## Who Leads It {#who-leads}

The Dómissa of House Khalithon, [Tázitha]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, holds the village and the cantons around it. Her steward lives in the village and keeps the House's roll of tenants and shares. The Order keeps a Hiora at the head gate who opens and closes the channels and answers to the Order, and the two offices seldom agree on whose word a gate answers to. The village levy drills under veterans of the Dómissa's guard, the women who trained [[being-lmbhrpdhnn|Lámbira]], and the [[affiliation-spearsisterhood|Spear-Sisterhood]] counts the levy among the hands it can call on for the southern posts.

## The Failing or the Quarrel {#present}

Sehela keeps its fields green while the Khelathi flood fails. Its grain comes from the _katelun_ and not from the river, so the village's harvest is part of the surplus that the [[affiliation-councilhouses|Council of Houses]] wants sold to Bethua's Khelathi patron and the [[affiliation-sacredcollege|Sacred College]] wants kept in the cisterns. Tázitha of Khalithon sits in the quarrel as a landholder. A sale takes grain her tenants would need in a dry year, and a refusal leaves her House out of the profit.

The southern cantons also feel the late tribute of the [[place-sthmrchsrgn|South Marches]]. The Hégissa of the southern cantons holds the frontier posts and waits for the stock, and the village levy is drawn on to man them while the Spear-Sisterhood's patrols are stretched between the eastern frontier and the southern line. The women who train the levy find fewer hands for the fields in the weeks before harvest. The Metrissa has not decided, and the village waits to learn whether the channel will carry grain out or hold it in.

## What Is Seen from It {#what-is-seen}

From the head of the village the Marches road shows as a pale line to the east, and the dust of caravans and herders on it is the village's news. The frontier posts lie a day or two south, past the last cisterns. On a clear evening the smoke of herd-clan camps rises beyond them, and the village does not light its own fires on the southern wall after dark.

## The Cost of Reaching It {#cost-of-reaching-it}

Sehela lies a few days' ride south of [[place-bethura|Bethura]] by the road to the Marches and a side track along the line of the _katelun_. The track is dry and carries no inn. A traveler brings water for the animals, since the gates are the Order's and are opened to travelers only with a House's letter or the Order's leave. A stranger without either camps outside the village wall.
