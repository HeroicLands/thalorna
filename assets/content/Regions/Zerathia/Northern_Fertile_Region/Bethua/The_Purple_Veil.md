## Overview {#overview}

The Purple Veil is the regalia of the Metrissa, a mantle and a face-veil dyed in the deep murex purple that Bethua has exported and worn as its royal color since colonial antiquity. It is worn in [[place-bethura|Bethura]], where the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethua]]'s two pillars meet, and the Metrissa's court takes its name from it: [the Court of the Veil]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}. The same purple dyes the sails of the fleet.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The reigning Metrissa wears it, and no one else may. She is [Amithena]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of [House Kervanion]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, raised to the Veil by election of [the Sacred College]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} and confirmed by the oracle of the [[affiliation-zunelas|Faith of Zunelas]].

The Veil is the throne itself, said as a phrase. To take the Veil is to be raised to the throne, and to set it down is to abdicate or die. It passes by election within the female line, from one consecrated priestess of the [[affiliation-lusinia|Faith of Lusinia]] of the sacred royal line to the next, and never by simple inheritance. A sovereign cannot name an heir, so the Veil has no one waiting for it.

## Its Story {#its-story}

Bethuans say the purple came with the colonists and became the realm's color before the realm had a throne. The tellings of the Houses differ on which Metrissa wore it first, and the Archivist's rolls, which set down every House's descent, do not settle the point.

## What It Does {#what-it-does}

The Veil makes the Metrissa. A woman wearing it speaks as queen of Bethua and as high priestess of the Faith of Lusinia at once, and the Sacred College and [the Council of Houses]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} treat her word accordingly. A Veil on any other head is a claim to the throne, and a claim the College has not consecrated is treason.

Its purple is the realm's standard. A Bethuan who sees it on a mantle, a sail or a banner reads whose authority is being invoked, and a murex dyer who sells that shade to anyone outside the license of the Court of the Veil answers for it.

## Who Wants It {#who-wants-it}

- **A priestess of the sacred royal line** who is not the Metrissa wants the Veil in the long game of succession, which is conducted in the College and the oracle's chamber and not with knives.
- **A Dómissa with a strong faction in the Council of Houses** can lift a rival to the Veil, and [[affiliation-bthntrdhss|The Bethuan Trade-Houses]] can fund one.
- **A foreign agent** who wants Bethua's sovereign discredited wants the Veil seen on the wrong shoulders.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- The oracle of Zunelas has refused a candidate before, and the memory of it keeps every faction careful. A party hired to carry a name to the oracle learns how many people want it carried slowly.
- A murex dyer in a northern orchard-port is selling the royal shade to a foreign captain, and the Court of the Veil wants to know whether he holds its license.
