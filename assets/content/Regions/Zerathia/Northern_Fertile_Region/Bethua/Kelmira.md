## Overview {#overview}

**Kelmira** (about 5,000 people) is a qanat town on the eastern plain of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]], close to the border with [[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu]]. The tunnel-channel that waters it feeds its fields, and the town's grain goes to the Fleet's granaries on the coast.

Border watchwomen share its walls with the engineers. A quarrel with a Khelathi neighbor over a boundary well can draw armed riders, and a low-water year puts the channel's flow in question.
