## Overview {#overview}

**Ishenia** (about 3,900 people) is a vine-and-barley town of the coastal hills of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]]. The terraced vines feed it, and the wine goes down to the harbors.

Vintners, coopers and carters keep to the pressing season's rhythm. A hailstorm can strip a vineyard overnight, and a House that holds the press can set the price of every cup.
