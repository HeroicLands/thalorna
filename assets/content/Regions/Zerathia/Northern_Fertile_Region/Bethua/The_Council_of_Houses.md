## Overview {#overview}

"A seat on the Council is what a House is for," a Dómissa's clerk tells a foreign factor who has asked what the Great Houses want. The **Council of Houses** (Bethuan: _the Sunedra_) is the governing body of the secular pillar of the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethua]]. It sits at [[place-bethura|Bethura]] and is the realm's secular legislature and high court: it ratifies law, votes the levies and taxes, adjudicates disputes between Houses and speaks for the temporal interests of the aristocracy against the temple.

## Who Sits {#who-sits}

The members are the Dómissai, the House-Mothers of the recognized clans, who hold the coastal villa-estates and the interior grain-lands, sit in judgment over their clansfolk and fill the offices of civil government. A seat is the reward of a Dómissa. The Metrissa presides as First of the Houses, and a Speaker of the Council of Houses presides in session and holds no vote beyond her own. The matriarchs of the great trading clans, the _An'Bet_, are themselves Dómissai who sit here, and many also sit in the [[affiliation-sacredcollege|Sacred College]]. The Stratégissa attends when war is in question and does not vote.

## How It Decides {#how-it-decides}

The Council sits in session at the capital and rules on the law, the levy and the quarrel between Houses. When a Dómissa takes her House's seal, the head of the roll is read aloud in the Council, and the Archivist reads her own House's line down from it until she reaches the woman standing there ([[lore-headofroll|the Head of the Roll]]). A House's standing in the Council rests on those rolls.

## The Present {#present}

The Council of Houses presses the Metrissa to sell the grain of the aqueduct stores to the Khelathi patron, and the choice is open. The Council is split over the privateer war. Viléria has persuaded enough of the Danvar to stop honoring the letters of credit of a major Bethuan House, and the House is bleeding. Its matriarch, who profited from the privateers, wants the Warrant reined in to save her ledgers, while the Houses still flush with prize-money want it pressed harder. The quarrel is about whether Bethua's future lies with its Khelathi patron or its Vilérian customers.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

A party meets the Council through a House: a Dómissa whose seal closes a warrant, a contract or a letter of credit, whose credit rests on that seal. A foreign man in Bethura is suffered and expected to defer.
