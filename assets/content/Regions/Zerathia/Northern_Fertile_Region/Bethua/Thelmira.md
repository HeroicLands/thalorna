## Overview {#overview}

**Thelmira** (about 4,000 people) is a citrus-and-fig town on the coastal strip of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]], where cisterns hold the winter rain for the summer groves. The orchards feed it, and the fruit is shipped from its small harbor to [[place-bethura|Bethura]].

Orchard-women, packers and boatmen keep the harbor lively in the picking weeks. A dry winter leaves the cisterns short, and the water-wardens ration the groves by the House's order.
