## Overview {#overview}

**Askelia** (about 3,200 people) is a waystation town on the Marches road, a day south of [[place-kolenara|Kolenara]]. A small irrigated strip feeds it, and the caravans that stop for water buy its bread.

Well-keepers, drovers and innkeepers earn from the traffic. A dry-season raid on the road makes caravans wait for an escort, and the town's watchwomen take the first blow.
