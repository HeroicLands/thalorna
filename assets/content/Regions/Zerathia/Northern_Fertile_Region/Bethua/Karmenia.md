## Overview {#overview}

**Karmenia** (about 4,100 people) is a pottery-and-olive harbor on the coast of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]], east of [[place-bethura|Bethura]]. The terraced groves behind it feed it, and the jars it fires carry the oil of half the coast.

Potters, oil-pressers and jar-stackers fill its lower quarter. A kiln fire can burn the whole row, and a blight in the groves shuts the presses for a year.
