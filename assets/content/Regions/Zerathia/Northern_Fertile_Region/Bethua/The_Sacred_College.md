## Overview {#overview}

"The College does not rule," a Hiora of a Bethura temple tells a pilgrim who asks who governs, "but nothing is ruled without it." The **Sacred College** (Bethuan: _the Hierossa_) is the governing body of the sacred pillar of the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethua]]. It sits at [[place-bethura|Bethura]] and is the realm's spiritual authority, its highest religious court and, decisively, its electoral body: the College elects each new Metrissa. Political and religious authority is reserved to the cults of three goddesses, [[affiliation-lusinia|Lusinia]], [[affiliation-zunelas|Zunelas]] and [[affiliation-olvenia|Ólvenia]], whose consecrated priestesshoods hold a co-equal share of governance. The many other cults, male and female, are honored and hold no share of rule.

## Who Sits {#who-sits}

The members are the Hiérissai, the high priestesses: the head of each of the three goddess-orders and the head of each great temple. The Metrissa presides as supreme priestess of Lusinia and First of the College. Her chief deputy, the Prohiérissa, runs the state cult day to day and is almost always the strongest candidate at the next election. The Izet-Mu, the chief engineer of the realm, sits by right of office, and the Stratégissa attends when war is in question and votes in no council. A Keeper of the Sacred College holds its rolls, its instruction and its elections.

## How It Decides {#how-it-decides}

When a Metrissa sets down the Veil, the College convenes as a conclave and chooses her successor from the eligible women of the sacred royal line, the priestesses of Lusinia who descend from past Metrissai. The Hiérissai weigh each candidate's fitness, faction and the balance of the two pillars, then carry the chosen name to the oracle of Zunelas, whose ratification, that the candidate's worth has been weighed and found true, makes the election sacred and final. The oracle has refused once or twice in the realm's history, and the College respects the veto and does not test it.

## The Present {#present}

The reigning Metrissa, [[being-amithenakrvn|Amithena of House Kervanion]], was raised to the Veil by election of the College and confirmed by the oracle. She was a consecrated priestess of Lusinia before her elevation, and she is reckoned a careful, conservative ruler who keeps the two pillars in balance by giving neither its way entirely. The College presses her to keep the grain in the cisterns while the [[affiliation-councilhouses|Council of Houses]] presses her to sell it to the patron, and the choice is open. The College and the Council keep that balance as a standing negotiation, and a grandee often sits in both.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

A party meets the College through a temple: a Hiora who keeps a sanctuary, a Parthena in her long education, or a Hiérissa whose blessing a House seeks for a contract or a voyage. The Order of Ólvenia consecrates the contracts and voyages of the Trade-Houses, and its clergy are the most worldly and best endowed of the three.
