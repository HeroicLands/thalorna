## Overview {#overview}

**Dasenia** (about 5,500 people) is a grain-and-flour town at the head of a canal on the plain of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]]. The canal waters the wheat that feeds it, and its mills grind for the coast and the Fleet.

Millers, bakers and canal-boatwomen make up its households. A fire in the mill-street spreads through the flour dust, and a siltup of the canal halts the mills within a week.
