## Overview {#overview}

**Soredia** (about 6,800 people) is a qanat town in the dry southern interior of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]], built above the mother-well of a tunnel-channel that runs for leagues underground. Barley, millet and beans from the strip the channel waters feed it.

Well-sinkers and tunnel-diggers live here with their families, and the work passes from mother to daughter. A tunnel collapse buries a crew, and a channel that runs dry condemns the whole strip below it.
