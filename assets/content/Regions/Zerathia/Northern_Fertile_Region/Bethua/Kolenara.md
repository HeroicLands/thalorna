## Overview {#overview}

**Kolenara** (about 6,700 people) is the market town on the Marches road, on the eastern edge of the cultivated strips of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]], where herders from the dry south bring wool, hides and cattle. Strip grain and imported iron feed and clothe the traders.

Caravan-masters, tribute-clerks and horse-dealers fill its inns. A late tribute from the southern clans leaves the clerks quarreling over arrears, and raiders watch the road at the town's edge.
