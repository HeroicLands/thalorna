## Overview {#overview}

**Anthira** (about 5,800 people) is an olive-and-barley town on the foothills of [[place-bethuargn|Bethua Region]], where the first channels leave the hills. The terraces feed it, and the presses send their oil to the coast.

Terrace-women, pressers and mule-drivers carry the work. A late frost kills the blossom, and a hill-slide can cut the road to the plain for a season.
