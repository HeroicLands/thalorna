## Overview {#overview}

"Everyone who walks the astrologers' road starts in the valley," a temple astrologer says, "and nobody who starts there is sure where the valley ends." **The Targa River Valley** is the valley of the Targa River in [[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu Region]]. In the middle of it stands [[place-anlaghura|Anlaghura]], the monolith that carries fragments of a lost prophecy in runes older than the empire's reading of them. The valley is where the astrologers' road begins.

## The Land {#the-land}

Nothing in the valley is named but the stone, which stands alone in the middle of it. The empire's astrologers can transliterate the runes and cannot entirely construe them. What they have construed points elsewhere: to a shrine in the highlands of [[place-chenosolis|Chenosolis]], and from there to the sands of [[place-lareshu|Lareshu]], where the road is held to end at [[place-lutzelutha|Lut-Zelutha]].

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The valley is part of the region, and the astrologers of the temple libraries keep the road in their charts, clue by clue. Nobody holds the stone.

## Current Situation {#present}

The valley is where a court astrologer begins. [[being-amqltzlmlglq|Amqelet-Zelemu Legulu’akun]] found fragments of the prophecy at Anlaghura and followed them to Chenosolis, on a pilgrimage through the empire's sacred sites to understand the event he calls the Convergence of the Nine Moons. Rival astrologers and priests who do not want his reading confirmed have sent agents after him. A party that goes to the valley finds the stone, and students of the temple libraries who can read its signs aloud sign by sign and cannot say what they mean.
