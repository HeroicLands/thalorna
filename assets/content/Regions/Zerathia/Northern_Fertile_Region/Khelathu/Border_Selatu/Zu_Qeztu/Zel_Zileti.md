## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Zileti** (about 3,500 people) is the horse-fair town of [[place-zuqeztunome|Zu-Qeztu Selat]], a half-day west of [[place-zileti|Zileti]]. The river meadows feed its stock, and the barley that the town's breeders buy comes from the valley farms.

Breeders, grooms and army buyers meet here after the spring foaling. Raiders from the western sand drive off herds from the outer pastures, and a lost stud herd ruins a breeder's house in a season.
