## Overview {#overview}

**Lut-Zileti** (about 2,800 people) is a stud-estate town in [[place-zuqeztunome|Zu-Qeztu Selat]], a short ride from [[place-zileti|Zileti]] on the canal that waters the pasture. Hay, barley and the estate's own beans feed it, and the stewards sell surplus to the horse-fair.

The estates breed mounts for the chariot corps, and their riders are the selat's first line against raiders. A foal sickness that runs through a stable burns a year's profit in a month.
