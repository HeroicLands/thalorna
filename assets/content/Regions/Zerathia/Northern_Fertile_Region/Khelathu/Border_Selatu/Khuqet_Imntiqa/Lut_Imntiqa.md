## Overview {#overview}

**Lut-Imntiqa** (about 2,800 people) is an estate-town of date and barley land in [[place-khuqetimntiqnome|Khuqet-Imntiqa Selat]], two days from [[place-khuqet|Khuqet]] along the oasis chain. The great temple estates of the selat hold the groves, and the town houses their stewards, tenants and tally-men.

The estates send dates and barley to the forts and buy cloth and iron in return. Sand drifts bury the outer fields each spring, and the town raises a labor levy every year to dig them out.
