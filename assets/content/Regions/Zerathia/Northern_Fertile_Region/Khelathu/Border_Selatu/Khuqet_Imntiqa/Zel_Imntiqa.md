## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Imntiqa** (about 3,500 people) is an oasis market town in [[place-khuqetimntiqnome|Khuqet-Imntiqa Selat]], a week of dry track west of the river and the nearest large place to [[place-khuqet|Khuqet]]. Date groves and irrigated barley fields feed it, and what they do not grow arrives with the caravans.

Drovers, salt-carriers and track-guides use its market to exchange news and goods. Its wells are its fortune and its weakness: a drought that drops the water table by a hand's breadth turns the oasis from a market into a refugee camp.
