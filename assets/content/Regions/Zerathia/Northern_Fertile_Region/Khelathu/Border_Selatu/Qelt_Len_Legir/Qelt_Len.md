## Overview {#overview}

**Qelt-Len** (about 5,300 people) is the capital of [[affiliation-seltqltlnlgr|the Selat of Qelt-Len-Legir]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]. It is the capital of the forward forts, and a posting to it is a posting to the hardest corner of the southern march.

The chief temple of [[affiliation-qeztu|Qeztu]] is the first building a new arrival is taken to see, though the busiest place in town is the convoy dock. "Sacks in, sacks out, and the number goes to the forts before they ask for it," a granary-keeper tells the boy sent to learn the tally. The Halzi'a's court and granaries stand near the temple, the scribal bureau keeps the tax rolls, and the markets serve the villages and estate-towns around.

## Arriving {#arriving}

A traveler reaches Qelt-Len along the cataract road or with a river convoy, and either way arrives under escort. The road beyond the town is the dangerous one: southern raiders strike the portage road in the dry season, and no escort makes it safe. The town itself is the last place on the march where a party can buy supplies and hire carters.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Qelt-Len is a town of the supply line. The granaries hold what the valley sends upriver in convoys, and the Halzi'a's Steward counts it out to the forts beyond the first cataract. [[place-zelqelt|Zel-Qelt]], at the head of the cataract road, carries the stores upriver and stores the army's barley. [[place-garlegir|Gar-Legir]], a day's march south, is the walled garrison town that stops the first southern raid. The villages and estate-towns of the selat farm the narrow cultivable ground and the temple estates, and their render fills the granaries here.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The Halzi'a of the selat governs from Qelt-Len as a hereditary military governor, the first soldier of the cult of Qeztu. Fort-Commanders hold the forward forts and answer to him, an Overseer of Scribes keeps the rolls and the pay, and the [[affiliation-garhalzi|Gar-Hálzi]] chapter attached to the chief temple attests the weight-pieces and keeps the granary accounts.

## The Present {#present}

Every convoy that ties up at the dock is counted twice, once by the Steward's clerks and once by the quartermasters of the forts who wait on the quay, and the second count is the shorter each season. The granaries are not empty, but the stacks stand lower than the walls were built for, and the carters hired for the road beyond the cataract go out with lighter loads. The garrisons at the cataract see each flood begin before the empire does, so two short rises surprised nobody there.

The Halzi'a's render goes north a season late, with the shortfall written off as stores issued to the forts. His officers hold that a throne which cannot keep the river should keep the garrisons at the cataract fed, and he sends that sentence north only in a form the capital's clerks can read as a request. A lost convoy on the portage road leaves a fort on half rations, and the dry-season raiders know it.
