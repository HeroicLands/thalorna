## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Qelt** (about 4,800 people) is the supply town below the forward forts of [[place-qeltlenlegirnome|Qelt-Len-Legir Selat]], at the head of the cataract road from [[place-qeltlen|Qelt-Len]]. Its barley fields and granaries are the army's stores, and the town lives on the carting and portage that move them upriver.

Porters, ox-drivers and storekeepers make up its population. Southern raiders strike the portage road in the dry season, and a lost convoy leaves the forts above on half rations.
