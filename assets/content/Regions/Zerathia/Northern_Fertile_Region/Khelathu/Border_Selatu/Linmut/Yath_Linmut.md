## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Linmut** (about 5,500 people) is the mainland landing town opposite the river-island fortress of [[place-linmut|Linmut]], in [[place-linmutselat|Linmut Selat]]. Grain and vegetables from the east-bank farms cross to the fortress by ferry, and the garrison's pay and the toll on every boat that stops feed the town.

Ferrymen, toll-clerks and boat-wrights hold the quays. The cataract's current kills a ferry now and then, and the town's livelihood stops the day the garrison closes the river to travelers. It is larger than the fortress it serves because the fortress has room only for the garrison, so the ferry trade lodges on the mainland bank.
