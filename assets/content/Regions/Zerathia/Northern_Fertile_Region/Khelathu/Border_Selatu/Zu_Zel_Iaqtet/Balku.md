## Overview {#overview}

**Balku** (about 22,000 people) is the capital of [[affiliation-selatzuzlqtt|the empire's Selat of Zu-Zel-Iaqtet]], a fortified hill-city at the mouth of the chief mining district where the desert roads come down out of the hills. The eastern gold is gathered here, weighed and sealed for the long guarded journey to the capital. The hills grow nothing, so the city buys its bread: grain carts from the river villages climb the eastern road to its granaries, under the same escorts that carry the gold down.

"Look at the walls and you know what the city is for," a mine overseer tells a newcomer riding in behind a convoy. "They face outward at the desert and inward at the strongroom, and the second is the harder to get past." The great temple of [[lore-psaqarudty|Saq'aru]] the smith presides over a city of mine-captains, smelters, engineers and hard frontier labor.

## Current Situation {#present}

Balku eats what the valley sends up the eastern road, and two low floods reach it as lighter carts and later ones. The granaries that feed the city and the camps behind it are drawn down, and the mine-captains have raised rations rather than lose their gangs, which shortens the stores further. The smelters are short of fuel and the quarry-roads are hard to hold, so the crown's share of the gold reaches the assay-house short and explained.

The Halzi'a keeps his court here, and so does the Commander of the Marches, so the one officer who could question the weights lives at the next table and does not. The capital asks for tribute and levies in the same letter, and the city answers that the gold is the tribute and the levies are needed on the walls.

## The Holder {#holder}

[[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|The Halzi'a]] of the selat is a hereditary military governor who holds the mines, the smelter-towns and the quarry-roads, and he sits at Balku with his Steward, who keeps the granaries and the corvée. Whoever holds it is a soldier first. Beside him sit the Commander of the Marches, a Halzi'a of another march whom the Gar-Au names to call the ten garrisons together, and the Overseer of the Mines, a Zelar who weighs what the smelters render.

## Garrison {#garrison}

The garrison barracks hold the troops who escort the gold-convoys and answer the desert raiders from the east. Fort-Commanders hold the guard-forts and water-stations along the quarry-roads, and each draws its stores through Balku. A fort whose cart does not arrive reports it to the Overseer of Scribes, who keeps the rolls and the pay, and a late roll is the first sign of trouble on a road.

## Government, Faith and Trade {#government-faith-trade}

Balku is the head of a producer-selat. The gold of the hill-mines, the copper of [[place-baliazalu|Balia-Zalu]], the turquoise of [[place-melgat|Melgat]] and the white stone of [[place-inululedju|Inulu-Ledju]] come here to be weighed, the convict-station of [[place-kheset|Kheset]] makes its demands on the grain-trains, and the water-caravans that supply every camp come up from the river. Saq'aru is the city's patron, and the faith of [[affiliation-qeztu|Qeztu]], the war-god, stands close behind among the soldiers.

## Within the Walls {#within-the-walls}

- The assay-house and treasury where the crown's gold is weighed and sealed
- The garrison barracks and the convoy yards
- The great temple of Saq'aru the smith
- The smelter-works and the mine-captains' quarters
