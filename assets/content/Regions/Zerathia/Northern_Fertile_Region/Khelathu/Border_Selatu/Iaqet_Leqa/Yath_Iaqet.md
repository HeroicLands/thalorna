## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Iaqet** (about 2,700 people) is a quarry-and-turquoise town in the hills of [[place-iaqetleqaslt|Iaqet-Leqa Selat]], north of [[place-iaqetleqa|Iaqet-Leqa]]. The pits grow no food, so the town buys its grain, oil and beer from the river villages and pays for them in cut stone and turquoise for the temples.

A quarry-master's word governs the town, and the cart-road to the valley is its only tie to bread. A collapsed gallery kills a crew in a day, and a delayed grain convoy starves the workings within the month.
