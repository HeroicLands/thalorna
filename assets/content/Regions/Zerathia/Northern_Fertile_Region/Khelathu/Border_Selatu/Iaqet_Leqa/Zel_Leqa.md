## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Leqa** (about 3,200 people) is a well-station town in [[place-iaqetleqaslt|Iaqet-Leqa Selat]], on the caravan track that leaves [[place-iaqetleqa|Iaqet-Leqa]] eastward into the dry country. Its wells are deep and sweet, and its fields are small irrigated plots that feed the drovers and the garrison; the rest of its grain comes up the track from the river.

Caravan masters pay for water, shade and a safe night, and the town keeps a toll on all three. Raiders from the sand-seas know every well, and the town lives with a standing watch and a standing fear of a poisoned cistern.
