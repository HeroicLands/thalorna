## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Zalu** (about 5,700 people) is a river-terrace town in [[place-zuzalulegulunome|Zu-Zalu-Legulu Selat]], where the valley narrows below the cataract country, a day south of [[place-balehen|Balehen]]. Terraced barley and onion fields on the narrow bank feed it, and the fortress buys its surplus.

Its people are farmers who also serve the fortress as laborers and carriers. A low flood leaves the terraces half-watered, and a hungry town on the border is a short step from a quarrel with the garrison over requisitions.
