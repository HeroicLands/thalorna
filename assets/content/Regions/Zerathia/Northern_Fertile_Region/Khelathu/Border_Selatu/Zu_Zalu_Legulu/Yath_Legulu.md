## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Legulu** (about 4,600 people) is the portage town at the foot of the cataract in [[place-zuzalulegulunome|Zu-Zalu-Legulu Selat]], where boats from the south unload to be carried around the rapids. Portage fees and the grain of the bank farms feed it.

Porters, rope-makers and boat-haulers keep the town busy in the sailing season. A southern boat that arrives with fever aboard threatens the whole town, and the harbor-wardens hold such a boat at the rapids until its crew is cleared.
