## Overview {#overview}

**Balehen** (about 5,700 people) is the strongest fortress the empire holds south of its capital and its gateway to the lands beyond. It is the great fortress-capital of [[affiliation-selatzuzllgl|the Selat of Zu-Zalu-Legulu]], a double-walled stronghold commanding the river at the first cataract, where the water breaks into rapids and the green valley fails.

"Stand on the wall at dawn and you can see the whole bargain," a priest of [[affiliation-qeztu|Qeztu]] tells a soldier newly posted to the garrison. "Below you is the river, loud with the cataract. Behind you is the last of the green. In front of you is a country the [[lore-garauu|Gar-Au]] can count on only as far as this wall. Everything that goes between here and there goes under it."

What passes under it is the gold, ivory, ebony and slaves of the southern trade, taxed and escorted through the toll-house and the tribute-store, and the embassies and war-parties of the southern tribes, watched from the battlements. Balehen is a hard, soldierly, mixed [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelathi]]-and-southern town, and the empire's writ here is exactly as strong as its garrison.

## Current Situation {#present}

Balehen's stores are the frontier's stores. The fortress eats the barley and onions of [[place-zelzalu|Zel-Zalu]], whose terraces stand half-watered after two low floods, and what the terraces cannot give it requisitions, so the town and the garrison are a short step from a quarrel. The tribal embassies that bring tribute up the river-road are fewer, the cattle-peoples beyond the cataract press harder on the border, and the toll-house collects less than the garrison spends.

The court knows what a Halzi'a with a full fortress and a short throne might decide, and the Halzi'a decides nothing in writing. He says that the border war that never quite ends is quiet, and that the quiet costs more than usual.

:::secret {title="The gold-road tally"}
The share sent to the capital is smaller than the road carries, and the Halzi'a keeps the remainder in Balehen.
:::

## The Holder {#holder}

The warden-[[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]] of the selat holds Balehen as a hereditary military governor and rules by the troops at his back. He commands the cataract forts, taxes and escorts the southern road, and watches the southern tribes, whose loyalty runs from grudging vassalage to open war. Fort-Commanders hold the river-forts beyond him, and a Caravan-Warden keeps the toll-house.

## Garrison {#garrison}

The garrison barracks hold the frontier army. [[place-zinhenzalu|Zinhen-Zalu]] holds the deepest stretch of the river-line beyond the cataract, and what passes upstream of it passes out of the empire's certain reach. Raids and relief-columns, more than harvests, mark the year.

## Government, Faith and Trade {#government-faith-trade}

The toll-house and tribute-store tax everything that comes up from the south. [[place-yathlegulu|Yath-Legulu]], the portage town at the foot of the cataract, unloads the southern boats and carries their cargo around the rapids to the fortress's scales, and a southern boat with fever aboard is held at the rapids until its crew is cleared. The soldiers' pay is the coin the local markets run on. The war-god's cult is stronger here than anywhere in the empire.

## Within the Walls {#within-the-walls}

- The toll-house and tribute-store of the southern gold-and-ivory trade
- The war-temple of Qeztu, the war-god
- The garrison barracks and the seat of the warden-Halzi'a
