## Overview {#overview}

**Gar-Wagaru** (about 3,600 people) is a caravan station in [[place-wagaruselat|Wagaru Selat]] where the gold road meets the tribute route from the south. The farms along the river beside it supply the stables and inns, and the station sells fodder and water to every train that halts.

Tribute collectors, drovers and factors fill its inns. A caravan master who cannot pay the toll waits for a clerk's decision, and a bad season on the southern tribute route thins the town's trade at once.
