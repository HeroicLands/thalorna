## Overview {#overview}

**Wagaru** (about 5,100 people) is the capital of [[affiliation-selatwagaru|the Selat of Wagaru]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]], and the town sits on the southern gold-road.

A priest of [[affiliation-psaqaru|Saq'aru]] in the chief temple tells the drivers who stop to give thanks for a safe stage: "Everything that comes up the road was in the ground first, and the ground is the god's workshop." Beside the temple stand the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and the drivers passing through.

## Arriving {#arriving}

Every load that passes the seat is counted, taxed and escorted, and the gate-house keeps them in that order. "Load, weight, escort," a Caravan-Warden tells a driver on his first run north. "I have never had a driver ask me to change the order." The garrison collects and guards in one body, so a party that comes in with a caravan comes in under its escort. Grain goes up the road and gold comes down, and each convoy on it draws raiders.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Wagaru lives by the road's traffic. The mines of [[place-yathwagaru|Yath-Wagaru]] send their gold down to be weighed here and buy their grain, beer and oil back from the river. [[place-garwagaru|Gar-Wagaru]], the caravan station where the gold road meets the tribute route from the south, sells fodder and water to every train and sends its tolls to the seat. The villages and estate-towns beside the road raise what the traffic eats, and the nearest customer for their grain is a caravan. The temple of Saq'aru keeps the assayers' ledger.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The Halzi'a of the selat rules from Wagaru as a hereditary military governor with the garrison at his back. Caravan-Wardens collect the tolls at the stations, Fort-Commanders keep the wells, an Overseer of the Mines sees the crown's share to the treasury, and the [[affiliation-garhalzi|Gar-Hálzi]] agents follow the garrisons and the caravans.

## The Present {#present}

The weighing-house is where a short flood shows. The Halzi'a's share and the crown's share of the gold go over the same scales, and the figure sent north to the capital's clerks is lower than a visitor counting the carts would reckon. The Halzi'a answers questions with the garrison's weights, and the ledger stays with the temple of Saq'aru.

Caravans keep arriving under escort, because the factions at court want gold above all else. The road is kept safe for all comers, and the mess-halls of Yath-Wagaru, fed by the grain carts, empty within a week if one delivery is skipped.

:::secret {title="The assayers' ledger"}
The ledger records the true weights, and the gap between the figure sent to the capital and the metal that remains in Wagaru's strong-room is wider than the road's traffic explains. The Halzi'a keeps the best of the road's cargo for himself until the throne decides who it is, and the priests of Saq'aru know the true figures.
:::
