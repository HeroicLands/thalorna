## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Wagaru** (about 4,300 people) is a gold-mining town in the hills of [[place-wagaruselat|Wagaru Selat]], on the road south from [[place-wagaru|Wagaru]]. The workings grow nothing, so the town buys grain, beer and oil from the river and pays in gold dust weighed at the assay-house.

Miners, assayers and the tax-clerks who watch them are its three classes. Each convoy that carries grain up and gold down is a target, and a skipped delivery empties the mess-halls before the week is out.
