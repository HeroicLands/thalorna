## Overview {#overview}

**Gar-Qeztu** (about 4,900 people) is the capital of [[affiliation-selatgarqezt|the Selat of Gar-Qeztu]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]], and every barracks in the empire knows it for the drill-grounds and officer schools beside it.

An instructor at the officer schools tells new cadets which buildings matter in their first week. "The temple, because the god of the corps lives there. The court, because that is where the Halzi'a sits. The bureau, because the rolls it keeps are what pay for you. The granaries, because they feed you. And the market, because that is where you will spend what is left." The chief temple of [[affiliation-qeztu|Qeztu]] is the first of them, with its Priest of the Chariot-Corps.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

The town is the corps' customer and its supplier. Carts of grain go in toward the drill-grounds and chariots come back out of them. [[place-zelqeztu|Zel-Qeztu]], a short ride away, is the remount-and-fodder market whose granaries are the corps' reserve, and the quartermasters buy its grain, straw and barley before the market opens. [[place-yathqeztu|Yath-Qeztu]], a day downstream on the canal, sends the timber, leather and axle-iron that the chariot-works consume. The villages and estate-towns behind them owe their share of the harvest and labor on the canals as anywhere in the empire, and here the grain they owe has an obvious customer.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The Halzi'a of the selat is a soldier before he is a noble, and he governs from Gar-Qeztu as a hereditary military governor, with the corps and its schools as his authority. An Overseer of Scribes keeps the rolls that pay the instructors, Fort-Commanders hold the drill-grounds' outposts, and the [[affiliation-garhalzi|Gar-Hálzi]] chapter at the chief temple attests the weight-pieces.

## The Present {#present}

The schools turn out the men who will command the empire's armies, and this year they train them for a throne whose standing is in doubt. Fodder for the chariot horses is short with the valley's grain, and the Halzi'a trims the drill-grounds' rolls before he trims the schools'. He keeps the selat's tax in the selat to pay the instructors.

Every faction at court wants a young officer's loyalty, and the cadets' families write from there. The Halzi'a sees which letters his graduates receive, and he lets them take the posts without saying whose they are. He has heard that Okharik horsemen are being courted, and the officers of his schools hold that riders bred for the saddle serve better than chariots anyway. A breached canal bank in the flood season strands the stores of Yath-Qeztu for a month, and the corps does not wait for the repair.

## History {#history}

The victory stela of [Kuz'Uqa]{.name data-type="being/npc"} at [[place-zelhalzi|Zel-Halzi]] counts the standard of [[affiliation-selatgarqezt|the Selat of Gar-Qeztu]] among the fourteen standards of the selatu taken up from the field below [[place-lenennezut|Lenen-Nezut]], where the [[affiliation-thaulgau|Tha'Ulgau]] destroyed the last army of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] in 1612 ST (499 BF) ([[lore-stelaquzuqa|The Stela of Kuz'Uqa at Zel-Halzi]]; [[lore-thaulgau|The Coming of the Tha'Ulgau]]).
