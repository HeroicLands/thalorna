## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Qeztu** (about 3,900 people) is a remount-and-fodder market town in [[place-garqeztuselt|Gar-Qeztu Selat]], a short ride from the drill-grounds at [[place-garqeztu|Gar-Qeztu]]. Its people live on the corps' purchases: the quartermasters buy grain, straw and barley from the farms around it, and the town's granaries are the corps' reserve.

Horse-dealers, wheelwrights and fodder contractors fill its streets. A short harvest empties its granaries first, because the corps takes its share before the market opens, and a levy for a new chariot wing sends prices up for a season.
