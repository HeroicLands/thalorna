## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Qeztu** (about 3,200 people) is a canal-mound town in [[place-garqeztuselt|Gar-Qeztu Selat]], a day downstream of [[place-garqeztu|Gar-Qeztu]]. The canal brings its grain from the estates upstream, and the town sends back the timber, leather and axle-iron that the chariot-works at the capital consume.

Carters and boat-poles outnumber farmers here, and the canal-wardens are its real magistrates. The danger is the canal itself: a breached bank in flood season strands the town's stores for a month, and the corps does not wait for the repair.
