## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Lenen** (about 6,100 people) is a horse-pasture town in [[place-lenenneztslt|Lenen-Nezut Selat]], on the dry plain behind the old capital of [[place-lenennezut|Lenen-Nezut]]. The meadows feed its herds, and the barley that its breeders buy arrives from the river villages.

Breeders, drovers and saddlers live on the plain. A horse sickness takes a stud in a week, and the old capital's proud families sell their mounts last. It is larger than the old capital because the open plain behind it has room for the herds and the breeders' households that the walled seat lacks.
