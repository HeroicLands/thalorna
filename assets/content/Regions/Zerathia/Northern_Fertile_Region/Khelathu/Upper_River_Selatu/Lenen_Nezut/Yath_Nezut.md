## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Nezut** (about 5,000 people) is a ram-shrine town in [[place-lenenneztslt|Lenen-Nezut Selat]], upstream of [[place-lenennezut|Lenen-Nezut]]. The shrine's herds graze the hillsides, and the shrine's tenants grow grain on the river terraces.

Herders, shearers and shrine-servants fill its lanes. A year of rams that will not breed is read as a sign, and the shrine's priests demand offerings until the sign is removed.
