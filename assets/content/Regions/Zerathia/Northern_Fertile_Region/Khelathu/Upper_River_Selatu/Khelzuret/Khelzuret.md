## Overview {#overview}

"Seventy days," an embalmer of Khelzuret tells a family that has brought its father upriver by barge. "That is the first grade: the body drawn, dried in natron, anointed with resin and wrapped in fine linen with an amulet at every joint. The second is a shorter course in cheaper oils. The third is a wash, a wrapping and a prayer. I name all three at the door because you are going to ask what the first one buys. It buys the first. The [Weighing]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} takes no payment."

Khelzuret is the empire's great temple-city of the dead and the capital of [[affiliation-selatkhelzrt|the Selat of Khelzuret]], and some 90,000 people live in it. It stands on the east bank, facing across the water the vast necropolis where the dead are laid, and the whole city lives on that traffic. The great temples of [[lore-wethurdty|Weth'ur]] and [[lore-hezmuiridty|Hezmu'iri]], lords of the passage into death, draw the empire's largest pilgrimage. The embalmers' and coffin-makers' quarters prepare the dead sent here from distant selatu, and the pilgrim-markets sell the amulets, funerary papyri and grave-goods that go across the river with them. The Thaz'Lekhau (high priests) of the mortuary temples command estates and labor-gangs to rival the [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]'s own, and the Halzi'a keeps his seat in the same city, sharing it with them uneasily. The streets are old and solemn, and mourners, pilgrims, priests and the craftsmen of death crowd them all year.

## Politics {#present}

The [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]] of Khelzuret shares the city uneasily with the Thaz'Lekhau of the mortuary temples, who hold more land and more coin than the throne's governor. The temples of Weth'ur and Hezmu'iri settle what the dead are owed, and the Halzi'a settles what the living owe. Two low floods have thinned the pilgrimage: mourners arrive fewer and poorer, and the embalmers' quarters fill more slowly. The walled company-village of [[place-zelqar|Zel-Qar]] across the water draws its rations from the selat's granaries, and its workers' complaints reach the Halzi'a before anyone else's. The temple priesthoods do not complain, since a short year sends them more of the dead, and the Halzi'a has no standing to ask them to share.

## What the City Lives By {#lives}

Khelzuret grows no grain, and about 90,000 people live in it on the strength of the dead. The farm and pilgrim villages of the east bank, [[place-garzekri|Gar-Zekri]], [[place-lutanpuqa|Lut-Anpuqa]], [[place-yathmulu|Yath-Mulu]] and [[place-zmagezer|Zma-Gezer]] among them, work the river margin and lodge the funeral parties, and the city buys the rest of its bread with the fees of embalming, the pilgrim-markets and the ferry-crossing. Trade here is the passage into death: linen, resin and natron brought in from other selatu, amulets and funerary papyri sent out. The city's danger lies across the water. The necropolis is built against thieves in layers, and the bound dead keep the deepest chambers.

## Notable Features

- The great temples of Weth'ur and Hezmu'iri, lords of the dead
- The pilgrim-markets and the trade in amulets, funerary papyri, and grave-goods
- The embalmers' and coffin-makers' quarters
- The ferry-crossing to the western necropolis
- The seat of the Halzi'a, sharing the city uneasily with the mortuary priesthoods

## History {#history}

The victory stela of [Kuz'Uqa]{.name data-type="being/npc"} at [[place-zelhalzi|Zel-Halzi]] counts the standard of [[affiliation-selatkhelzrt|the Selat of Khelzuret]] among the fourteen standards of the selatu taken up from the field below [[place-lenennezut|Lenen-Nezut]], where the [[affiliation-thaulgau|Tha'Ulgau]] destroyed the last army of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] in 1612 ST (499 BF) ([[lore-stelaquzuqa|The Stela of Kuz'Uqa at Zel-Halzi]]; [[lore-thaulgau|The Coming of the Tha'Ulgau]]).
