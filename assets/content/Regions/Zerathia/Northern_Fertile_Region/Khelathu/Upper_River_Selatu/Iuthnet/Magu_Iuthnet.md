## Overview {#overview}

**Magu-Iuthnet** (about 8,000 people) is a birth-shrine town in [[place-iuthnetselat|Iuthnet Selat]], in a garden ring around a shrine of the mother-goddess. Midwives and pilgrims from the whole river fill its inns, and the farms of the selat supply their meals.

Midwives, wet-nurses and amulet-sellers live here. A birth that goes wrong in the shrine's precinct brings the temple's inquiry on the midwife, and a mother's death is a public matter.
