## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Iuthnet** (about 6,600 people) is a healing-temple town in [[place-iuthnetselat|Iuthnet Selat]], upstream of [[place-iuthnet|Iuthnet]]. The patients who come to the temple eat the barley and beans of the farms nearby, and the town sells them its bread at temple prices.

Healers, apothecaries and bearers staff the wards. An outbreak inside the wards closes the gates for a month, and the town starves while the sick are held.
