## Overview {#overview}

**Gar-Iuthnet** (about 5,400 people) is a farm-and-market town of [[place-iuthnetselat|Iuthnet Selat]], on the river road, that supplies the shrines with flour, oil and honey. The fields around it are the selat's richest, and it sells its surplus to the larger towns.

Millers, bakers and honey-sellers work its yards. A late flood delays the planting and cuts the shrines' bread, and the shrines' demands do not fall.
