## Overview {#overview}

Zebt-Relepet is a city that does its work. It is the capital of [[affiliation-selatzbtrlpt|the Selat of Zebt-Relepet]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]], and about 28,000 people live here, among the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the chief temple of the [[affiliation-wethur|Faith of Weth'ur]], the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls and the markets that serve the villages around. The cavern-tombs lie in the cliffs behind, the natron comes in from the flats, and the city's trade is the plain, careful business of preparing the dead. The villages of the selat's plain render grain to the granaries, and the natron the city ships out pays for what the plain does not grow.

## Politics {#present}

The [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]] of Zebt-Relepet keeps his court and granaries beside the chief temple of Weth'ur, and the two stand closer than in most seats, because the natron the temple's workers dig is the selat's real wealth. Two low floods have cut the plain's harvest, and the mortuary trade has held steady through them: the neighboring selatu's estates pay less for their dead, and still pay. The Halzi'a sends the throne its natron in full, since natron keeps, and delays the grain. The estate-towns eat from the temple of Weth'ur's stores, which are better than the farmers' own, so the temple's steward is heard in the city as the Halzi'a is. The scribal bureau holds the tax rolls and keeps the natron weights.

## What the City Lives By {#lives}

About 28,000 people live in a city the plain could not feed alone. The cavern-tombs in the cliffs behind employ the embalmers, the coffin-makers and the guards; the natron-diggers and the cart-masters make up the rest. Each cart leaves the flats with a tally stamped at the diggings, and the weights-masters at the city gate check the load against it; a cart-master whose load runs light is fined the difference. The danger is in the trade's shape. A sealed cavern-tomb is a vault of goods, and thieves know which cliffs hold them. A natron cart that loses its way on the flats does not come back, and the selat's weights-masters answer for every load that leaves. The city has no market town beside it to share the work: its trade is the one trade, and the selat's few lesser settlements are farm villages that look to it for the market, the granary and the ferry. The temple of Weth'ur buries the city's poor in common caverns without fees.
