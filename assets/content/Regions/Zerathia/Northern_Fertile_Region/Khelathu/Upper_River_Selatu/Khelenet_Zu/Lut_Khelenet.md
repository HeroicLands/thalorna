## Overview {#overview}

**Lut-Khelenet** (about 7,900 people) is a land-survey town in [[place-khelenetzslt|Khelenet-Zu Selat]], where the scribes who remeasure the fields after each flood train and lodge. The farms around it feed the scribes' households, and the surveyors' fees keep the town's granary full.

Cord-stretchers, surveyors and boundary-stone setters make up its population. A boundary dispute that the surveyors cannot settle goes to the assize at [[place-khelenetzu|Khelenet-Zu]], and a bribe that is found out ends a career. It is larger than the capital because the surveyors' schools lodge their pupils and households here, away from the assize.
