## Overview {#overview}

**Gar-Zalu** (about 6,400 people) is an assize-market town of [[place-khelenetzslt|Khelenet-Zu Selat]] on the road to the capital. Litigants from the villages lodge and eat here while their cases wait, and the farms of the plain supply the inns.

Pleaders, scribes and process-servers fill its inns. A long suit drains a farmer's savings into the innkeepers' hands, and a lost suit leaves the farmer homeless.
