## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Legirusi** (about 7,000 people) is a quarry town in [[place-legirusiselt|Legirusi Selat]], below the white cliffs. Its workers eat the barley and beans carted up from the river farms, and the monument-carvers of [[place-legirusi|Legirusi]] buy its blocks.

Quarrymen, haulers and wedge-setters work in crews under a master. A rockfall kills a crew in an instant, and a wrong cut ruins a block worth a year's wages.
