## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Legirusi** (about 5,800 people) is a stone-carving town of [[place-legirusiselt|Legirusi Selat]] on the river bank, where the rough blocks are dressed and loaded. The workshops buy grain from the farms on the opposite bank by ferry.

Dressers, loaders and barge-builders crowd the wharves. An overloaded barge sinks with a god's statue aboard, and the carvers' guild pays the damages out of the crew's wages.
