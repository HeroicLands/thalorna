## Overview {#overview}

**Lut-Legirusi** (about 4,800 people) is a mason's estate-town in [[place-legirusiselt|Legirusi Selat]], with its own grain-lands and its own granary. It houses the master masons' families and the apprentices of the temple-workshops.

Masters, apprentices and their wives run its households. A master who dies without an heir breaks up his workshop, and the town loses its best hands to the capital.
