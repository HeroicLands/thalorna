## Overview {#overview}

**Zu-Pelgun** (about 4,300 people) is a well-and-pasture town of [[place-selatpelgunnome|Selat-Pelgun]] on the edge of the desert, where herders bring their flocks to water. The river farms feed it by cart.

Herders, well-keepers and shepherds' wives live here. A well that goes brackish in a dry year forces the herders to move on, and their departure takes the town's trade with them.
