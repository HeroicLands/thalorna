## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Pelgun** (about 5,300 people) is a harvest-festival town in [[place-selatpelgunnome|Selat-Pelgun]], on the river a day's ride from [[place-iqu|Iku]]. The festival crowds eat the town's stored grain, and the town's farms produce the beer and bread that the festival uses.

Brewers, bakers and shrine-keepers do their work in the festival weeks. A bad harvest cancels the festival and the town's trade with it, and the shrines' priests ask the farmers to give anyway.
