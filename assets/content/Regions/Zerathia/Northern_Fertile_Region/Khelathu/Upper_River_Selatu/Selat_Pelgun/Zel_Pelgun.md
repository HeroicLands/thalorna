## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Pelgun** (about 6,400 people) is a caravan-yard town in [[place-selatpelgunnome|Selat-Pelgun]], at the end of the eastern desert roads beside [[place-iqu|Iku]]. The caravans buy grain, water and fodder from the farms along the river before they set out, and the town's inns and yards live on the trade.

Caravan masters, camel-sellers and guides fill its yards. A caravan that does not return leaves a hole in the town's credit, and desert raiders know the departure dates.
