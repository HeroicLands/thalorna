## Overview {#overview}

**Gar-Gadju** (about 5,400 people) is a market town on the dry rim of [[place-gadjumuluslt|Gadju-Mulu Selat]]. The basin villages bring flax, fowl and fish to its market, and the grain of the rim farms feeds the traders who come to buy.

Linen-factors, fowlers and boat-sellers do their business at its quays. Fever from the standing water hits the market-day crowds hardest, and the town closes its gates to the basin when it spreads.
