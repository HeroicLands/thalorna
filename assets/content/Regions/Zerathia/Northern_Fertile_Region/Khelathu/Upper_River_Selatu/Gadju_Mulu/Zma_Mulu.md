## Overview {#overview}

**Zma-Mulu** (about 6,500 people) is a flax-and-waterfowl town in the floodbasin of [[place-gadjumuluslt|Gadju-Mulu Selat]], a short punt from [[place-gadjumulu|Gadju-Mulu]]. The flax-fields yield fiber and seed, and the traders who buy them bring grain back from the dry country.

Retters, spinners and bird-catchers live along the channels. A wet year rots the flax in the pits, and a dry one starves the basin of its water-birds. It is larger than the capital because the flax-fields of the floodbasin employ more households than the capital's offices need.
