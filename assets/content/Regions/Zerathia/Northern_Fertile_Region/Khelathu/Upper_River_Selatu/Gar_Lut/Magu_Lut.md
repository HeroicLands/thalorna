## Overview {#overview}

**Magu-Lut** (about 3,900 people) is a herb-garden town in [[place-garlutselat|Gar-Lut Selat]], a short walk from the healing-shrines of [[place-garlut|Gar-Lut]]. Its beds grow the simples that the shrines dispense and the traders carry to every selat, and its bread comes from the grain-fields to the south.

Gardeners, drug-sellers and herbalists fill its market. A blight in the herb-beds strips the shrine of its stock, and a mislabeled sack of herbs can poison a patient and ruin a house's name.
