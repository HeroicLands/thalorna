## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Lut** (about 3,200 people) is a spring-side town in [[place-garlutselat|Gar-Lut Selat]], built on a mound above a mineral spring that the healers prize. Pilgrims pay for baths and lodgings, and the farms behind the mound send grain to the lodging-houses.

Bath-keepers, masseurs and lodgers share the mound. A spring that runs dry or foul would end the town, and the keepers inspect it at every dawn.
