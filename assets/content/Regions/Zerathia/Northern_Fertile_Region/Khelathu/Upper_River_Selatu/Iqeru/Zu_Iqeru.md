## Overview {#overview}

**Zu-Iqeru** (about 6,800 people) is a canal-head town in [[place-iqeruselat|Iqeru Selat]] where the main irrigation canal leaves the river. The canal-wardens control the sluices, and the grain of the fields the canal waters feeds the town.

Wardens, diggers and sluice-keepers hold its offices. A farmer who steals water at night meets the wardens' flails, and a broken sluice floods a village or starves it.
