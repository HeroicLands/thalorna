## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Iqeru** (about 8,300 people) is a grain-market town in the wheatlands of [[place-iqeruselat|Iqeru Selat]], a day from [[place-iqeru|Iqeru]]. Its farms and the sun-temples' irrigated estates grow more grain than the district eats, and the market exports the rest by barge.

Grain-factors, measurers and barge-captains do their business at its granaries. A rigged measure ends a factor's license, and a rat plague in the granaries ends a season's profit. It is larger than the capital because the granaries stand at the head of the barge route, where the grain trade gathers.
