## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Ger** (about 7,400 people) is a mortuary-service town in [[place-zugernome|Zu-Ger Selat]], close to the old royal burial-ground beside [[place-chegu|Chegu]]. The priests, tomb-keepers and offering-bearers who tend the dead buy their bread from the valley farms with the temple's stipend.

Priests, offering-bearers and stone-cleaners keep the tombs. A tomb-robbery at the ancient burial-ground shames the whole selat, and the town's keepers answer for every missing object.
