## Overview {#overview}

"My grandfather plowed round the stones, and so do I," a farmer of Zu-Ger says, turning his team at the end of a furrow beside a row of old marker stones.

Zu-Ger is the land of [[affiliation-selatzuger|the Selat of Zu-Ger]], one of [[affiliation-upperrivrslt|the Upper River Selatu]] of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]], and lies within the [[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu Region]]. It is a different place from [[place-zugezer|Zu-Gezer]], the royal necropolis across the river from the capital.

## Character

The land is "the Great Land," an ancient royal selat and the old burial-ground of the first dynasties. Fields and villages fill the river strip between the canals and the dry uplands, and the burial-ground lies in the desert edge above them, with high desert and chaparral beyond. The villages are ordinary [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelathi]] villages and the estates are ordinary estates, working ground that is very old.

## Economy

Grain goes to the granaries, labor to the canals and a share of everything to the temples and the crown, as in every Khelathi selat. What the selat adds is its age and its dead: the old burial-ground of the first dynasties lies in its desert edge, and its patron is [[lore-hezmuiridty|Hezmu'iri]], the Keeper of Transitions.

## Notable Features

- [[place-chegu|Chegu]]—the selat capital and the Halzi'a's seat
- The chief temple of the [[affiliation-hezmuiri|Faith of Hezmu'iri]] and its estates
- The old burial-ground of the first dynasties, [[place-firsttablets|the Ground of the First Tablets]]

## Current Situation {#present}

The villages of Zu-Ger lie round the oldest burial-ground of the empire, and they keep the tombs. The throne's offerings to the first kings are paid in grain and oil, and the villages that carry them to the tombs report that the loads are smaller. The temple of Hezmu'iri at Chegu holds the rest. The estate-towns farm what the flood leaves.

## Settlements {#settlements}

Chegu (~7,600)
: the selat capital and the seat of the Halzi'a.

[[place-zelger|Zel-Ger]] (~7,400)
: a mortuary-service town in Zu-Ger Selat, close to the old royal burial-ground beside Chegu

[[place-lutger|Lut-Ger]] (~6,100)
: an estate-town of Zu-Ger Selat with a granary that the royal funerary estates fill

[[place-yathger|Yath-Ger]] (~5,000)
: a river-landing town of Zu-Ger Selat where the barges of the burial-ground unload stone and timber

The villages and estate-towns
: the ordinary settlements of the selat, from a few hundred to a few thousand each, clustered on the cultivable ground and the temple estates.
