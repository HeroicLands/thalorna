## Overview {#overview}

**Lut-Ger** (about 6,100 people) is an estate-town of [[place-zugernome|Zu-Ger Selat]] with a granary that the royal funerary estates fill. Its fields are among the oldest in the valley, and its tenants owe grain and labor to the dead kings' foundations.

Tenants, stewards and tally-clerks keep the foundations' accounts. A foundation whose dead king is forgotten loses its grain, and the town's tenants are dismissed with the king.
