## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Ger** (about 5,000 people) is a river-landing town of [[place-zugernome|Zu-Ger Selat]] where the barges of the burial-ground unload stone and timber. The landing's wages and the grain of the near farms feed it.

Barge-hands, stone-carters and rope-makers work its wharf. A barge that sinks with a king's sarcophagus costs the landing its contract, and the crew its lives.
