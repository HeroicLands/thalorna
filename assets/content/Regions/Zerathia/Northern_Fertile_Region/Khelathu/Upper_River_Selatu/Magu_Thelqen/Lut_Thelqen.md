## Overview {#overview}

**Lut-Thelqen** (about 4,300 people) is an estate-town of [[place-maguathenslt|Magu-Thelqen Selat]], at the edge of the old planned city. The temple's gardens still produce vegetables and figs, and the town sells them to the capital's markets.

Gardeners and fig-pickers work the terraces. A blight in the fig-orchards ruins the town, and the temple's stewards do not forgive a missed delivery.
