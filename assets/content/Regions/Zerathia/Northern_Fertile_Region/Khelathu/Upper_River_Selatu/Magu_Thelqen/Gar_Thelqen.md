## Overview {#overview}

**Gar-Thelqen** (about 5,200 people) is a half-empty temple town in [[place-maguathenslt|Magu-Thelqen Selat]], a short ride from [[place-maguathen|Magu-Thelqen]]. The wide avenues were laid out for a population that never arrived, and the people who live there farm the open lots and eat what the lots yield.

Gardeners, squatters and a few priests fill the empty houses. A squatter who occupies a house that has an owner may find the owner's agent at the door, and the empty quarter is a refuge for fugitives.
