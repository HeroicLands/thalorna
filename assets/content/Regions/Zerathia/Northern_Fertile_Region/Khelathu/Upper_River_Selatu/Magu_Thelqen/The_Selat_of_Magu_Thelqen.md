## Overview {#overview}

"The streets were laid for more people than we are, so we use the half that has roofs," a Legha'lutu whose estate runs up to the edge of the empty quarter tells a visitor from the capital.

The Selat of Magu-Thelqen is one of the eighteen provinces of [[affiliation-upperrivrslt|the Upper River Selatu]] of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]], and about 300,000 people live on its land, [[place-maguathenslt|Magu-Thelqen Selat]]. It is a planned temple-city of one zealous dynasty, half-abandoned and still inhabited. The patron is [[lore-uqaadty|Uqa'a]], the god of the sun, and the cult's chief temple and estates anchor the selat's religious life.

## Character

The estates are the working part of the selat. The [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]] governs through the Legha'lutu who rule them, each answerable to him for the manor, the fields and the village that belong to it, and in [[place-maguathen|Magu-Thelqen]] those estates carry the weight the empty city cannot. The city is the work of the house of [[affiliation-thelqenrelu|Thelqen'Relu]], the house of the sun, and it emptied when the temples of the [[affiliation-qearet|Faith of Qe'aret]] and the [[affiliation-rethsaar|Faith of Reth'Sa'ar]] refused the house's doctrine and the court went home to [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] ([[lore-sunhouse|the Sun-House at Magu-Thelqen]]). The seat is Magu-Thelqen, where the Halzi'a keeps his court and the chief temple of [[affiliation-uqaa|Uqa'a]] stands.

## Current Situation {#present}

Magu-Thelqen is a temple-city built by one zealous dynasty, half-abandoned and still inhabited, and its working part is the estates. The Halzi'a governs through the Legha'lutu who rule them, and in a short-flood year the estates are where grain is kept. The empty city gives the selat room: families from the dry villages of the neighboring selatu come to the empty quarters, and the estates take them on as bondsmen for their food. The Halzi'a's tribute is small. He sends the throne a tithe of the estates' grain.

:::secret {title="The estate rolls"}
The rolls of those who live under the Halzi'a's protection are larger than the rolls the capital holds. He keeps the labor, which is the wealth that the half-empty city was built to house.
:::

## Commerce and Currency {#commerce}

Magu-Thelqen uses the [[lore-aukhlthcrncy|Au'Khelathu Currency]], and the [[affiliation-garhalzi|Gar-Hálzi]] chapter at the chief temple attests the weight-pieces and holds the granary accounts. See [[lore-aukhlthcrncy|Au'Khelathu Currency]] for the full system.

## History {#history}

The house of [[affiliation-thelqenrelu|Thelqen'Relu]] laid out [[place-maguathen|Magu-Thelqen]] as a new capital in 1790 ST (321 BF) and ruled from it until 1822 ST (289 BF). The temples of [[affiliation-qearet|Qe'aret]] and [[affiliation-rethsaar|Reth'Sa'ar]] refused the house's doctrine, the court went home to Galezkara, and the city emptied ([[lore-sunhouse|The Sun House at Magu-Thelqen]]).
