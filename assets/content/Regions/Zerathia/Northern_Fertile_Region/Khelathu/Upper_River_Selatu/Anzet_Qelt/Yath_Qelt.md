## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Qelt** (about 4,600 people) is a tomb-guards' town at the edge of the necropolis in [[place-anzetqeltnome|Anzet-Qelt Selat]]. The guards and their families live on a ration of grain from the temple and on the barley that their small plots yield.

The guards patrol the cliff tombs against thieves who tunnel from the desert side. A guard who sells a secret to a robber loses his hands, and a guard who dies on watch is buried in the cliff beside the kings.
