## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Anzet** (about 5,700 people) is an embalmers' town at the desert edge in [[place-anzetqeltnome|Anzet-Qelt Selat]], close to [[place-anzet|Anzet]]. Its linen, natron and resin come from other selatu, and its own people buy bread from the farms of the flood-plain with the fees of the embalming-houses.

Embalmers, linen-wrappers and coffin-makers fill its workshops. A plague that fills the houses with bodies pays well, and the town's trade depends on deaths in cities far away.
