## Overview {#overview}

**Lut-Gekhenu** (about 6,100 people) is an estate-town of the library-temples in [[place-gekhenuselat|Gekhenu Selat]], two days from [[place-gekhenu|Gekhenu]]. The temples own its fields, and the grain that the tenants render feeds the scholars and the pilgrims who study at [[place-gekhenu|Gekhenu]].

Stewards, tenants and tally-clerks run the town. A tenant who withholds grain from the temple faces a scribe's inquiry, and an inquiry can last longer than a harvest.
