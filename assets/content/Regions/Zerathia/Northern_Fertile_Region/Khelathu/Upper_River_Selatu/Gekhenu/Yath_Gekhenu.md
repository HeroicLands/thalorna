## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Gekhenu** (about 7,400 people) is a paper-and-ink town in [[place-gekhenuselat|Gekhenu Selat]], downstream of [[place-gekhenu|Gekhenu]]. The library-temples buy its sheets, inks and reed-pens, and the barges that carry the stationery upriver return with grain.

Ink-makers, pen-cutters and binders work for the temples' scribes. A fire in the drying-sheds can destroy a year's stock, and a shortage of oak-gall closes the ink-works. It is larger than the capital because the paper and ink trades need drying-sheds and water, which the crowded capital lacks.
