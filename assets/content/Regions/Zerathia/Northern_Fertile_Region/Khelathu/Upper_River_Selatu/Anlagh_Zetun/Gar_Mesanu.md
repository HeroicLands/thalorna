## Overview {#overview}

Gar-Mesanu is a grain-village of 160 people in the flood-basin country of [[place-anlaghztnslt|Anlagh-Zetun Selat]]. Its fields share the annual inundation that feeds the selat's wheatlands, and its harvest goes up the chain to the granaries and the Halzi'a. The village lives by the water's rise and retreat and by the measure of grain it can render after the harvest.
