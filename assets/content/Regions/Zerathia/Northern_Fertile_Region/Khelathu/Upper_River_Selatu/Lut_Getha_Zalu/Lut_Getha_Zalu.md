## Overview {#overview}

You smell the smith-towns of Lut-Getha-Zalu before you see them: charcoal smoke lies along the bank for a mile, and on a still evening the hammering carries across the water.

Lut-Getha-Zalu is the land of [[affiliation-selatltgthzl|the Selat of Lut-Getha-Zalu]], one of [[affiliation-upperrivrslt|the Upper River Selatu]] of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]], and lies within the [[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu Region]].

## Character

The land is the river strip, with fields and villages between the canals and the dry uplands, and among the villages stand the smith-towns, each built around its forges and a craft-temple of the [[affiliation-psaqaru|Faith of Saq'aru]]. Farmers owe their share of the harvest and their labor on the canals as everywhere, and the scribes remain the ladder out of the fields. Beyond the cultivated strip lie the high desert and chaparral.

## Economy

The selat feeds its granaries from the flood like the rest of the valley. Its particular trade is metalwork: bronze and gold, and the ceremonial arms of the nobility, the khopesh, the mace of command and the collar of rank that a Halzi'a puts on for court.

## Notable Features

- [[place-lutgetha|Lut-Getha]]—the selat capital and the Halzi'a's seat
- The chief temple of the Faith of Saq'aru and its estates
- The smith-towns and craft-temples, where bronze and gold become the nobility's ceremonial arms

## Current Situation {#present}

The smith-towns of Lut-Getha-Zalu work bronze and gold, and the villages around them supply the charcoal and the reed-fuel. A short flood has raised the price of fuel, and the smiths work the ceremonial arms of the nobility at a slower rate. The estate-towns send their grain to feed the smiths, and the craft-temples keep a ledger of every order. The villages are poorer, but the smith-towns' trade is the best in the upper river.
