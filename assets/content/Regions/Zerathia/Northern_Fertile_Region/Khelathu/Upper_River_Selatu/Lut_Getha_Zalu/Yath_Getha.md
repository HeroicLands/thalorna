## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Getha** (about 4,500 people) is a charcoal-and-ore depot of [[place-lutgethazalunome|Lut-Getha-Zalu Selat]], on the mound where the hill-roads meet the river. Barges take its ore down to the smiths, and the barges return with grain.

Carters, ore-weighers and charcoal-burners work its yards. A mine collapse in the hills cuts the ore, and an ore-weigher who cheats is thrown into the river.
