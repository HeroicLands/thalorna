## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Getha** (about 5,500 people) is a gold-and-bronze craft town of [[place-lutgethazalunome|Lut-Getha-Zalu Selat]], on the river road. Its goldsmiths work for the temples, and the bread of the town comes from the estates that the temples hold nearby.

Goldsmiths, casters and engravers share its workshops. A robbery of a goldsmith's strongroom brings the inspectors in, and everyone in the street is a suspect until the gold turns up.
