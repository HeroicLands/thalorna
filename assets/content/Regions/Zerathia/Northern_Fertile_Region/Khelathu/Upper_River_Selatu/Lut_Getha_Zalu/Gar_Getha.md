## Overview {#overview}

**Gar-Getha** (about 6,700 people) is a smithing town in [[place-lutgethazalunome|Lut-Getha-Zalu Selat]], close to [[place-lutgetha|Lut-Getha]]. The smiths buy their charcoal and ore from the hills and their grain from the river farms, and the nobility buys the ceremonial arms they make.

Smiths, bellows-boys and charcoal-burners work in rows of furnaces. A fire in the charcoal-yard burns a street, and a smith who sells an unmarked blade loses his forge.
