"Walk your dead to the grave by daylight and be back on the ferry before dusk," a guard of the necropolis tells a family crossing for a burial. "After that the western fields belong to us."

**The Western Estates** is the name for the funerary estates that the great houses of [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Au'Khelathu]] endow on the western bank. Death is the organizing preoccupation of Khelathi civilization, and a house that can afford it does not leave its dead to the common necropolis: it endows a house of its own, in the desert margin where the black land stops, so that its dead are prepared, laid in their own ground and fed for as long as the endowment lasts. The west is the direction of the dead, and the estates take their name from it.

## What an Estate Is

An estate stands at the end of a canal under the cliffs: a walled house with a water-gate on a basin, a preparation hall, and a row of tomb doors cut into the cliff behind it. The dead come upriver by boat, pass the water-gate into the preparation hall and spend seventy days there in washing-salt and linen, then are walled into the cliff galleries with their tablets beside them. A household of embalmers, bondsmen and offering-priests lives in the house to do the work, and the estate's fields at the canal's edge feed them and the offering tables both. A steward, the _lemzabu_, keeps the accounts.

## How an Estate Fails

An estate lasts as long as its endowment. The restoration guilds keep the honored Ways of the royal necropolis immaculate, but decay creeps in wherever a house's endowment has lapsed, its cult dwindled or its line been forgotten, and the tomb-robbers work those reaches. A dwindled house leaves its steward in charge of dead it can no longer feed. [[place-lutithnet|Lut-Ithnet]], the western estate of the house of [Zu'Lemetu]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, is one: its last mistress is dead, her heirs live downriver and have never seen it, and the steward keeps its dead and its servants.

## The Trade Around Them

The mortuary trade of the western bank is the same work at the scale of a selat: the embalmers, tomb-builders and offering-priests of [[affiliation-selatkhelzrt|Khelzuret]] and [[affiliation-selatzbtrlpt|Zebt-Relepet]] live by the dead, the cliff tombs and the natron.
