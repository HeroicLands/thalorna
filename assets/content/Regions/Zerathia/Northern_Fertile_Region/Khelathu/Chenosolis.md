## Overview {#overview}

Chenosolis is highland country of [[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu]], and what draws anyone up into it is a mountain shrine where the oldest records of the empire's astrologers are kept. The shrine is one of the sacred places of the early astrologers of Au'Khelathu, and its oldest records were laid down in the age of [[lore-akhfirsttab|the First Tablets]]. An astrologer who wants to read the sky the way the first ones read it has to climb to it.

## The Shrine and Its Records

The way to Chenosolis is usually found before it is walked. [[being-amqltzlmlglq|Amqelet-Zelemu Legulu’akun]], court astrologer, found fragments of a lost prophecy at the [[place-anlaghura|Anlaghura]] in the [[place-targavalley|Targa River Valley]], and they pointed him to the shrine in the highlands of Chenosolis. He went on a pilgrimage through the empire's sacred sites to understand the event he calls [The Convergence of the Nine Moons]{.name data-type="lore/history"}, and the records at Chenosolis are among the oldest evidence he can consult. Rival astrologers and priests who do not want his reading confirmed have sent agents along the same road.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The shrine's records are wanted by readers who want opposite answers. Amqelet-Zelemu needs them to confirm his reading of the Convergence before the stars, as he reckons them, draw into alignment, and the road he follows runs on from here to the sands of [[place-lareshu|Lareshu]], where it is held to end at [[place-lutzelutha|Lut-Zelutha]]. The agents of his rivals travel the same road to stop his reading being confirmed, and others are said to want the celestial power for themselves.

## History {#history}

The earliest astronomers of the empire carried their records up to the shrine in the age of [[lore-akhfirsttab|the First Tablets]], and those records are cut in the sacred hand that the priesthood of Reth'Sa'ar fixed about 540 ST (1571 BF) ([[lore-sacredhand|the Fixing of the Sacred Hand]]).
