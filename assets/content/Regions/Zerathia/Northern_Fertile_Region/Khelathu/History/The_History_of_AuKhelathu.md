Au'Khelathu has been counting its years since 2110 BF, and its history is a list of houses. Three foreign houses have sat its throne, and each ended by speaking Khelathi, worshipping the Khelathi gods and entering its line in the king-list under a throne name.

## Before the Count

The temple will not date what came before the First Occasion, and says so: what cannot be counted is not history. The valley it calls chaos was a country of several polities, in the Khelathi phrase "the valley was many", with farming and water-works whose first teachers no human record remembers. Two things survive from it: the [[place-anlaghura|Anlaghura]] monolith and [[place-drownstones|the Drowned Stones]] under the southern marsh, both cut in a script older than Khelathi reading ([[lore-khelathclndr|the Khelathi Calendar]]).

## The First Tablets (1 to 1006 ST)

The count opens in 1 ST (2110 BF), when the first Gar-Au stands upon the mound of [[place-yathtelgu|Yath-Telgu]] and the many become one ([[lore-qettelgu|the Qet Telgu]], told as a story in [[lore-firstoccasion|the First Occasion]]). The [[lore-garauu|king-list]] gives this age eleven Gar-Auu in two houses and almost nothing else about them: a name, a length and the formula _his deeds are the god's_. The lengths are 150 years, 120, 100 and fewer, and they add end to end, as no real succession does; the Keepers of the King-Lists leave the arithmetic where it stands.

The age has one act the temples date. About 540 ST (1571 BF), under the fifth Gar-Au, the priesthood of [[affiliation-rethsaar|Reth'Sa'ar]] fixed the signs of the sacred hand and the classical register of the language, and every temple inscription since is cut in what that reign fixed ([[lore-sacredhand|the Fixing of the Sacred Hand]]). The second house, [[affiliation-azletkutu|Azlet'Kutu]], took the throne in 621 ST (1490 BF), and its reigns shortened until the last of them ran twenty-six years. Both houses kept their court in the Great Land of [[place-zugernome|Zu-Ger]] and lie there, at [[place-firsttablets|the Ground of the First Tablets]] ([[lore-akhfirsttab|The First Tablets]]).

## The Court at the Mound (1006 to 1390 ST)

In 1006 ST (1105 BF) the house of Azlet'Kutu closed and a Gar-Au stayed on the mound after his crowning. [Anlagh'el'Qar I]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of [Zu'Amqeletu]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} carried the court to [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]], laid the Sun's Road on the solstice line and proclaimed the law that nothing may stand in the sightline between the temple and the horizon ([[lore-crossmound|the Crossing to the Mound]]). The necropolis of [[place-zugezer|Zu-Gezer]] opened across the river for the houses that ruled from the mound.

Nine houses ruled from Galezkara in the next nearly four centuries, in forty-one reigns, and the list began to read like a real succession: the lengths were ones a life can hold, and a new Gar-Au was crowned in the year the old one died. The age closed in 1390 ST (721 BF) without a catastrophe, as houses rose upriver and the list passed to the seven of the next age ([[lore-akhsunroad|The Sun's Road]]).

## Two Banks and the Hill-Nomads (1390 to 1671 ST)

For most of two centuries the valley had a capital on the mound and a rival upstream. In 1462 ST (649 BF) the house of [[affiliation-gezeelanlaghu|Geze'el'Anlaghu]] was crowned in its own city, [[place-lenennezut|Lenen-Nezut]], and three of its Gar-Auu ruled and were buried there ([[lore-thronelnz|the Throne at Lenen-Nezut]]). The king-list counted that line as the legitimate one for sixty-nine years and gave no reign to the Galezkara claimants who held the delta.

The claimants fought back from 1525 to 1531 ST (586 to 580 BF), and the war ended in the wheatland of [[place-iqeru|Iqeru]] with the death of the upriver Gar-Au and the crowning of Kuz'el'Qar I at Galezkara ([[lore-twothrones|the War of the Two Thrones]]). The upper-river granaries were left half burned, and a valley that could not feed its army from its own fields was weak for eighty years. In the season of emergence of 1612 ST (499 BF) the hill-nomads of the western margins came down through the horse-country and destroyed the last Galezkara army on the floodplain below Lenen-Nezut. The ground is [[place-zelhalzi|Zel-Halzi]], the first blood-field in Khelathi memory, and the priesthood crowned the nomad chief ([[lore-thaulgau|the Coming of the Tha'Ulgau]]). The [[affiliation-thaulgau|Tha'Ulgau]] held the throne for four reigns and fifty-nine years. The fourth took a throne name no nomad would have chosen, and when he died in 1671 ST (440 BF) the priesthood closed the house and settled its kin on the western horse-pastures ([[lore-hillhouse|the Closing of the Hill House]], [[lore-akhdivriver|The Divided River]]).

## The Restored Crown (1671 to 1990 ST)

The new house took a throne name last used in the First Tablets, and for more than a century the list held nothing a scribe would stop to read. In 1790 ST (321 BF) the house of the sun, [[affiliation-thelqenrelu|Thelqen'Relu]], laid out a new capital at [[place-maguathen|Magu-Thelqen]] and pressed the doctrine that every temple's god is Uqa'a. The temples of Qe'aret and Reth'Sa'ar refused, and the house was gone by 1822 ST (289 BF) ([[lore-sunhouse|the Sun-House at Magu-Thelqen]]). In the same years the court sent an army and an embassy west across the [[place-sthmrchsrgn|South Marches]] to make the eastern temple-cities of [[place-okharisrgn|Okharis]] pay tribute, and the chain of forts it built along the wells was abandoned within a generation ([[lore-templetrib|the Tribute of the Temple-Cities]]).

The age ended by sea. In 1960 ST (151 BF) raiders from across the Vilérian Sea landed in the delta, burned the port of [[place-zelamqelu|Zel-Amqelu]] and marched on Galezkara, and the priesthood crowned their chief as the house of Malu'Thenoru ([[lore-searaiders|the Sea-Raiders' Throne]]). The house held the crown for three reigns. When the third died in 1990 ST (121 BF) the priesthood raised [Gezer'Kuru]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} ([[lore-seahouse|the Closing of the Sea House]], [[lore-akhrestcrown|The Restored Crown]]).

## The Count of Months (1990 to 2281 ST)

The first Gar-Au of Gezer'Kuru, Gez'Uzner I, was the first whose reign the list measured to the month. The habit came from the delta's rule that no cargo travels upriver unweighed, which taught the scribes to count what they handled and carried the counting to the throne. About 2030 ST (81 BF) the queen Gith'el'Retha I sanctioned Qalezu, the bureaus' alphabet of twenty-four signs reduced from the sacred hand ([[lore-qalezusanction|the Sanction of Qalezu]]). The tax rolls, land registers and counting-houses of [[place-garmedjatozo|Gar-Medjatozo]] were written in it from then on, and the oldest archives of months in the House of Records date from these reigns.

In 2111 ST a new empire began counting across the sea, and the Vilérians called that year 1 AF ([[lore-thefounding|the Founding]]). The Keepers entered nothing of it. The bureaus kept a clerk who could convert the year, by subtracting 2,110 ([[lore-akhcountmon|The Count of Months]]).

## The Struck House (2281 to 2378 ST)

In 2281 ST (171 AF) the priesthood crowned the house the necropolis calls [[affiliation-garzinelrelu|Gar-Zin'el'Relu]]. Its six reigns stand in the list as years under struck names, and its sixth Gar-Au, crowned in 2365 ST (255 AF), proclaimed himself [[lore-uqaadty|Uqa'a]] in the flesh, seized the [[place-greattempluqaa|Great Temple]] and its granaries and silenced the priests who named the blasphemy. Then the floods failed. For the rest of his reign the river rose short year after year, and he hid the famine by falsifying the reading of [[place-themeasure|The Measure]] and killing anyone who said the word _hunger_ ([[lore-failfloods|the Failing Floods]]).

The upper river broke him. In 2378 ST (268 AF) the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatnlghztn|Anlagh-Zetun]] raised the selat and the temple levies against the guard that held the granaries shut, and the fighting at the gates ended in a grave without rites ([[lore-sealedgran|the Battle of the Sealed Granaries]]). The Thaz'Lekhau of [[affiliation-uqaa|Uqa'a]] judged the god-king at the Great Temple, and he was sealed living into his tomb and struck from every wall ([[lore-judgfalseuq|the Judgment of the False Uqa'a]], [[lore-akhstruckhs|The Struck House]]). The only attempt to make the fiction of divinity true ended with the house's name removed from the record.

## The Priests' Thrones (2378 to 2580 ST)

The priesthood raised the next house in the year of the fall, and for two centuries it raised the houses after it. Children were crowned, regents stood beside them, and the regent was next to stand on the mound so often that the line recording it is the most worn in the list. Two shocks came from outside. In 2425 ST (315 AF) the steppe host of [[lore-hndrdbnnrs|the Storm of the Hundred Banners]] crossed [[place-dunharargn|Dunhara]] and struck the wells of the eastern road, and a frontier commander seized the throne to meet it and did not complete his year ([[lore-eastroad|the Storm on the Eastern Road]]). The forts of [[place-khuqetmiglet|Khuqet-Miglet]] were walled in the decades that followed.

In 2487 ST (377 AF) a Vilérian army met the chariot corps at the delta apex, held its line while the corps broke, and had its commander crowned Gar-Au ([[lore-vylconquest|the Vilérian Conquest]]). His son reigned as a Khelathi and died in the night in 2505 ST (395 AF), and no Vilérian claimed the throne after him ([[lore-foreignhs|the Closing of the Foreign House]]). About 398 AF the next house starved the army's garrisons out of the delta forts. The age ended with the child [Kuz'el'Retha III]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, crowned in 2579 ST (469 AF) with [[affiliation-zumeku|Zu'Meku]] standing beside him ([[lore-akhpriestthr|The Priests' Thrones]]).

## The House of Meqes (2580 ST to the present)

In 2580 ST (470 AF) the child went to the West in the night and Meqes I of Zu'Meku, who had stood beside him, was crowned in the child's second year ([[lore-childfloods|the Child of Three Floods]]). He kept his own name as his throne name, and every Gar-Au of the house has taken it since. The house is the longest of the Near Count and the one the empire dates every document by.

Under Meqes VI the empire reached the height of this age, and about 2650 ST (540 AF) the older houses were carried out from the river-front of Zu-Gezer to galleries farther out, and the reigning house raised its own Way at the head of the death-road ([[lore-greatremovl|the Great Removal]]). The house chartered the guilds that date themselves by it: [[affiliation-garnul|Gar'Nul]] under Meqes VII, crowned in 2665 ST (555 AF), and [[affiliation-garmelnu|Gar'Melnu]] under Meqes XI, crowned in 2710 ST (600 AF). The same queen underwrote Bethua's war of separation from its Helonik metropole about 615 AF with gold, engineers and loans ([[lore-bethuasep|the Separation of Bethua]]). The court of Meqes XV repeated the instruments in 707 and 708 AF, when covert aid carried [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Taruzel]] out of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] ([[lore-empirdclne|the Decline Begins]]).

Three reigns of the house were short or children's, and the house knows what a succession can cost: the year of Meqes III was not completed, Meqes VIII was a child of four floods with her uncle beside her, and Meqes XIV, a child of two floods, did not complete his year before the uncle beside him took the mound as Meqes XV. Meqes XVI, the daughter of Meqes XV, was crowned in 2819 ST (709 AF) ([[lore-akhhsmeqes|The House of Meqes]]).

## The Present Flood and the Struck House

The Struck House is the last time a throne and a short river met, and the empire cites it when the flood runs low. The sixth Gar-Au of the struck house hid his famine, while the short readings of the present flood are proclaimed to the city and the empire as custom requires. The present year belongs to [[affiliation-empireakhlth#present|the empire's present]].

## What It Left {#what-it-left}

- **The count.** Every year the empire writes is a year of the Qet Telgu, and every document is dated by the reigning Gar-Au's regnal year; the bureaus convert to the western reckoning by subtracting 2,110.
- **The habit of absorbing.** The hill-nomads, the sea-raiders and the Vilérian army each took the throne, and each was entered in the list as a house that spoke Khelathi within a generation or two.
- **Three blood-fields.** Zel-Halzi, [[place-eleventhgran|the Eleventh Granary]] and [[place-chariotfld|the Chariot Field]] are the ground three wars left ([[lore-bloodfield|Blood-fields]]).
- **The necropolis.** The houses that ruled from the mound lie at Zu-Gezer in the order of their raising, and the first two houses lie at Zu-Ger.
- **The patron's loans.** Bethua owes the court a debt that is never called, and Taruzel's independence rests on the court's covert aid.
