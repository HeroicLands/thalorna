## Overview {#overview}

Every cargo that reaches [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Au'Khelathu]] by water crosses one of these quays before it crosses anything else. **Amqel-Miglet**, the Delta Quays, is the name for the harbors of the northern selats of [[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu Region]], where the river's several mouths open onto the sea, and its business is a single process: goods are landed, weighed and taxed here, and until they have been, they travel upriver only as contraband.

"Weigh first and argue afterward," a weigh-house clerk tells a [[lore-taruzelianclt|Taruzelian]] shipmaster who is still shaking the tide out of his boots. "Nobody's cargo has ever been worth what he swore it was until the beam said so. After the beam it has a weight and a duty on it, and that is the only form in which it may go up the river." A merchant's fortune is made or broken at that scale, before the goods reach any market that will pay a fair price for them.

The quays are flat, brackish and cut by tidal channels where the river's fresh water gives way to the sea twice a day, so the harbors are built to ride a mudflat as much as a shoreline. Hulls lean on the silt at low water, and cargo comes ashore on whichever tide serves. The ports are crowded and polyglot, with the foreign merchant quarters and the Taruzelian factories of the delta along their docks.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

The ten selatu of [[affiliation-deltaselatu|the Delta Selatu]] keep their own harbor fees, and the quays answer to the **Overseer of the Sea-Gate** at [[place-garanlaghet|Gar-Anlaghet]], who collects the Gar-Au's customs duty at the delta, licenses the foreign factors and their warehouses and answers to the throne for the customs. The weigh-houses keep the beam, and a customs patrol of the weigh-houses calls at the creeks and landings between the quays.

## Layers {#history}

The weigh-house rule dates from the reign of [Gez'Uzner I]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the first Gar-Au after the sea-raiders' throne ([[lore-seahouse|the Closing of the Sea House]]). The raiders had come in over the river's mouths with nothing between them and the capital. Gez'Uzner I closed the mouths with paper: every cargo reaching the delta by water was to be landed at a quay, weighed at a weigh-house and taxed before it moved, and a cargo not yet weighed was contraband for as long as it stayed unweighed. A ship that unloaded elsewhere was a smuggler or a raider, and the same patrol dealt with both.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The quays are the empire's first line against a landing, and they are where the empire's trade with the Confederation of Taruzelian States and with [[place-midhalnrgn|Midhalión]] is counted. Smugglers keep landings behind the silt-mounds, such as the one at [[place-zelamqelu|Zel-Amqelu]], and a customs patrol finds nothing there on every visit. The grain contract with the Vilérian throne, which the temple-estates' factor offers on credit ([[lore-zelgeru|the Zelgeru]]), is a question for the weigh-houses as much as for the shipping houses, and a party with cargo or a contract to place meets the beam first.
