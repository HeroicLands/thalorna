## Overview {#overview}

You smell Gau before you see it: crushed olive and warm citrus peel carried inland on the sea breeze. The city is the capital of [[affiliation-selatgaulegr|the delta's Selat of Gau-Legiru]], comfortable and devout, prosperous in the unhurried way of a city that grows good things, about 45,000 strong, and set back a little from the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] among the orchards and vineyards.

"Start at the temple, because everybody does," a healer-priestess of [[affiliation-uznera|Uzn'era]] tells a pilgrim on the steps, and points across the square to the great temple behind her, where the birth-shrines and healing-halls draw worshippers from across the delta. Her goddess is the city's patron, and the sick and the expectant come to her before they come to the market.

## Politics {#present}

Gau lives on winter rain and sea air, not on the flood, so the garden coast is the one corner of the delta that two low floods have barely touched. The vintners' slopes and olive terraces bear as they did, and the villas of the delta's merchant-princes fill with relatives from the ports. The [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]] hosts them. What the selat lacks is grain, which it has always imported and now pays dear for. He trades wine and oil for bread-grain at prices that lighten the empire's tax, and he sends the throne a tribute in luxury goods instead of the grain it asked for. The gentry at the villas talk of the court's quarrel as a matter of marriage alliances, and the Halzi'a lets them. He has no reason to refuse the throne anything.

## Order and Law {#order-and-law}

The Halzi'a of Gau-Legiru is a hereditary governor and a Zabu, and the administration that seats him sits in the city, governing the gentry of villas around it. Estates are ruled by their Legha'lutu and Zabet'lutu, answerable to him, and the Overseer of Scribes keeps the selat's bureau. The temple of Uzn'era holds the birth-shrines and healing-halls. The city's dangers are the quiet ones of a rich country: villa rivalries and marriage alliances, inheritance and poison among the leisured, vintners' jealousies over the best slopes, and the smuggling of high-value wine and oil to dodge the customs. A fugitive courtier or a foreign agent hides more readily in a villa here than in a warehouse at [[place-garanlaghet|Gar-Anlaghet]].

## What the City Lives By {#what-the-city-lives-by}

Gau is the market of the garden coast. Its great produce-markets buy the harvest of wine, oil, citrus, figs and dates, its press-houses and warehouses gather the high-value crops, and its carters take the lot down to Gar-Anlaghet and the export trade. [[place-chezu|Chezu]] (about 9,900), the coastal wine-town among the western vineyards, makes the most prized vintages in the empire and ships them from its own harbor. The orchard villages of a few hundred people each, among them [[place-gariuget|Gar-Iuget]], [[place-lutlegiru|Lut-Legiru]], [[place-zmachezu|Zma-Chezu]], [[place-yathkhelenet|Yath-Khelenet]] and [[place-garshelu|Gar-Shelu]], tend the groves and vines and send oil, wine and fruit to the city. Its bread-grain comes down the river and is bought with oil and wine, so the city eats on the strength of its sales, and a household's wealth is counted in what it can trade for wheat.

## History {#history}

The victory stela of [Kuz'Uqa]{.name data-type="being/npc"} at [[place-zelhalzi|Zel-Halzi]] counts the standard of [[affiliation-selatgaulegr|the Selat of Gau-Legiru]] among the fourteen standards of the selatu taken up from the field below [[place-lenennezut|Lenen-Nezut]], where the [[affiliation-thaulgau|Tha'Ulgau]] destroyed the last army of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] in 1612 ST (499 BF) ([[lore-stelaquzuqa|The Stela of Kuz'Uqa at Zel-Halzi]]; [[lore-thaulgau|The Coming of the Tha'Ulgau]]).

A name from Gau is one of the five that families have brought to the stela since, for a man who marched with the army of Galezkara and did not come home.
