## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Telgu** (about 6,400 people) is a market town on the marsh-edge of [[place-garshebztslt|Gar-Shebazet Selat]], where the marsh villages sell fish and reeds and the river farms sell grain and beer. Its markets feed it, and its granary holds a season's bread for the marsh.

Traders from three channels meet on its quays. A fever season in the marsh sends the sick to its healing-house, and the sickness stays when the sick are gone.
