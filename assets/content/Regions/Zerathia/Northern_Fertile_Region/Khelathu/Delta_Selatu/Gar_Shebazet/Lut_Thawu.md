## Overview {#overview}

**Lut-Thawu** (about 7,800 people) is a papyrus-cutting town in [[place-garshebztslt|Gar-Shebazet Selat]], a day downstream of [[place-garshebazet|Gar-Shebazet]]. The temple scriptoria buy its paper, and the barges that deliver the paper's price carry the town's grain back.

Cutters, soakers and pressers work in damp sheds. Mildew ruins a season's sheets, and a scribal order that changes its paper standard strands a warehouse of stock.
