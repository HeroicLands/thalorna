## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Shebazet** (about 9,400 people) is a reed-and-fowling town in the papyrus marshes of [[place-garshebztslt|Gar-Shebazet Selat]], reached by punt from [[place-garshebazet|Gar-Shebazet]]. Fish, fowl and reed-bundles are its produce, and it trades them to the capital for grain.

Fowlers, reed-cutters and boat-builders live on raised mud platforms. A high flood floats the whole town's stores away, and crocodiles take a punter each season. It is larger than the capital because the marshes give a living to far more households than the capital's streets can hold.
