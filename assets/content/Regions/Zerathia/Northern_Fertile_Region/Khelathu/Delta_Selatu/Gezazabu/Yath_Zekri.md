## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Zekri** (about 9,100 people) is a harbor town of [[place-gezazabuselt|Gezazabu Selat]], south of the capital on a mud-bar coast. Fishing fleets land there, and the town lives by what the boats bring and by the grain that comes down from the valley in return.

Netmenders, boat-yards and salting-houses line its waterfront. Winter storms wreck its boats and drown its fishermen, and a bad season sends half its crews into the capital's debt.
