## Overview {#overview}

**Lut-Zechi** (about 8,600 people) is an estate-town of the salt-fish merchants in [[place-gezazabuselt|Gezazabu Selat]], inland from the curing-houses. Its fields grow the beans, onions and barley that the curers buy by the sack, and its granary serves the capital in a bad season.

Merchant stewards, field-hands and cart-drivers fill it. A single bad harvest ties it to the capital's merchants for years of debt.
