## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Megit** (about 9,900 people) is a ferry-and-curing town in [[place-gezazabuselt|Gezazabu Selat]], on the westernmost channel and the road that leads toward Bethua. Salt-fish and flat-bread feed it, and the barges from [[place-gezazabu|Gezazabu]] bring grain for the curing crews.

Ferrymen, curers and carters of salt-fish crowd its landing. A Bethuan grain buyer who arrives with coin sets the price for everyone, and a closed border puts a whole season's fish in the pits.
