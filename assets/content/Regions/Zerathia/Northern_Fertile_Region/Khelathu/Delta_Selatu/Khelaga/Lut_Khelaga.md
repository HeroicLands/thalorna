## Overview {#overview}

**Lut-Khelaga** (about 6,300 people) is a customs town at the delta apex in [[place-khelagaselat|Khelaga Selat]], a short sail from [[place-khelaga|Khelaga]]. Every boat that goes down to the sea pays at its station, and the toll-clerks, their guards and their families eat the grain that the estates around it grow.

Pilots, clerks and river-police share the quays. A clerk who cheats a shipper meets a magistrate who is paid by the shipper's guild, and a pilot who misjudges the channel strands a barge on the bar. It outgrows the capital because every boat bound for the sea must stop at its station, so the customs trade lodges here and not in the seat.
