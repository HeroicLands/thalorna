## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Khelaga** (about 5,200 people) is a shrine-and-estate town in [[place-khelagaselat|Khelaga Selat]], upstream of [[place-khelaga|Khelaga]] on the western channel. The temple of the apex holds its fields, and the pilgrims who come to the shrine buy the bread the fields produce.

Priests, pilgrims and field-tenants live in tight order. A year when the flood fails to reach the outer fields shrinks the temple's tithe, and the shrine's grain-doles shrink with it.
