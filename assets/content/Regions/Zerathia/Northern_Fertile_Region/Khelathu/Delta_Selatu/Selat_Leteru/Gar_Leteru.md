## Overview {#overview}

**Gar-Leteru** (about 3,500 people) is a pilgrims' lodging town on the mainland shore in [[place-selatleterunome|Selat-Leteru]], opposite the shrine-islands that [[place-yathleteru|Yath-Leteru]] serves. Its inns and shrine-gardens depend on the pilgrim traffic, and its bread arrives by ferry from the farms upstream.

Innkeepers, ferrymen and guides are its livelihood. A fever in the marsh keeps pilgrims away for a season, and the town's debts to the grain-ferries mount.
