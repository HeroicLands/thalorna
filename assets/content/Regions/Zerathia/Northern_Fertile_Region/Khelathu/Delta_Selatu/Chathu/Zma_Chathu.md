## Overview {#overview}

**Zma-Chathu** (about 6,100 people) is a fish-curing town on the sea-margin of [[place-chathuselat|Chathu Selat]], where the catch of the lesser coast villages is brought to be salted. The curing sheds buy their salt from the pans nearby, and the barges that carry the cured fish upriver return with the grain that feeds the town.

Curers, coopers and barrel-haulers fill its lanes, and the smell reaches the capital on a west wind. A spoiled batch ruins a curer's name with every buyer on the river, and a storm that keeps the boats in port stops the whole trade in a week.
