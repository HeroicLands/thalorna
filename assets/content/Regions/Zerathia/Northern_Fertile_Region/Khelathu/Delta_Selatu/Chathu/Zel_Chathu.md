## Overview {#overview}

**Zel-Chathu** (about 7,100 people) is a salt-town on the pans of [[place-chathuselat|Chathu Selat]], a short march inland from the sea-margin capital at [[place-chathu|Chathu]]. Salt, not grain, pays for it: the pan-masters sell to the river and buy their bread from the valley villages.

Rakers, brine-boilers and salt-carters work the flats in a blinding glare. A wet winter drowns the pans and halves the harvest, and the town's debts to the grain merchants run on regardless.
