## Overview {#overview}

"We dry the dead here, in a manner of speaking," says a priest of [[affiliation-wethur|Weth'ur]] to a visitor who has asked about the white flats at the edge of town. "The natron is ours, and the embalmers of the whole empire buy it." Chathu is the capital of [[affiliation-selatchathu|the Selat of Chathu]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]], a town of about 7,900 on the sea-margin of the delta.

It holds the selat's chief temple of Weth'ur, the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns.

## What the Town Lives By {#what-the-town-lives-by}

Chathu lives by salt and natron. The pans fill with brine at the sea's edge, the sun does the work, and rakers bring in white heaps that are weighed at the town's scales. [[place-zelchathu|Zel-Chathu]] (about 7,100) is the salt-town on the pans a short march inland, and [[place-yathchathu|Yath-Chathu]] (about 6,500) is the natron-works town on the white flats. [[place-zmachathu|Zma-Chathu]] (about 6,100) is the fish-curing port where the catch of the lesser coast villages is salted, and the seat buys its cured fish from there. The embalmers of the upper river buy the natron, and carters take it up the delta channels and return with grain. The town grows none of its own bread, and the grain it eats is the grain its salt has bought.

## Who Rules and Keeps Order {#who-rules}

The Halzi'a of Chathu governs from the seat, a hereditary office held by a Zabu that commands the selat's levies, collects its taxes and dispenses its justice. The Halzi'a's steward of revenue weighs the pans' yield against the rolls, and the Gar-Hálzi chapter of the temple settles weight disputes at its scales. A customs patrol of the weigh-houses of [[place-amqelmiglet|Amqel-Miglet]] watches the coast, including [[place-zelamqelu|Zel-Amqelu]], the port the sea-raiders burned, which lies in the reeds as a mound. The flats are the town's danger: a crust that holds a raker in dry weather breaks over wet brine, and the reed-grown ruin is a hiding place for smugglers who avoid the patrol.

## The Present {#present}

The flood has come low two years running, and for Chathu that is profit. Less fresh water reaches the margin, the brine thickens, and the pans yield more. The Halzi'a sells salt and natron upriver at a better price than he has had, and he sells them for grain, which he stores in the temple vaults of Weth'ur. The embalmers of the upper river need the natron more as the dead multiply. The lesser villages' catch is cured in the extra salt and carried up the channels, so the coast eats better than the fields inland do. The selat's tribute is paid in salt, and the accounts enter part of the pans' yield as damp spoilage. Few delta Halzi'a have grain in their own vaults, and he is one of them. The townsfolk say little about the flood, since the sea is not short.

## History {#history}

The sea-raiders landed on this coast in 1960 ST (151 BF) and burned the port that lies in the reeds as Zel-Amqelu ([[lore-searaiders|the Sea-Raiders' Throne]]).
