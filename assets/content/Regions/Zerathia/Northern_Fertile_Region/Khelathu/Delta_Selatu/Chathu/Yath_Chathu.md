## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Chathu** (about 6,500 people) is a natron-works town on the white flats of [[place-chathuselat|Chathu Selat]]. The mortuary temples and the glassmakers of the river buy the natron it digs, and the grain-barges that return up the coast bring its food.

Diggers, dryers and bagmen live in rows of mud-brick sheds. The dust inflames the eyes and lungs, and a death in the pits goes unremarked unless the overseer wants a replacement.
