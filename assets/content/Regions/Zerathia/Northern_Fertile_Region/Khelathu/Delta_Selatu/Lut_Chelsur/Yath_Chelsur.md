## Overview {#overview}

**Yath-Chelsur** (about 7,900 people) is a mound-town of the crocodile-keepers in [[place-lutchelsrslt|Lut-Chelsur Selat]], a half-day from [[place-lutchelsur|Lut-Chelsur]]. Fish for the sacred beasts and bread for their keepers arrive by barge from the valley.

Keepers, fish-carriers and temple-guards tend the pools. A keeper who loses a beast to fever is dismissed, and a keeper who is bitten by one is thought blessed. It is larger than the capital because the crocodile pools need keepers by the thousand, and the cult houses them beside the beasts.
