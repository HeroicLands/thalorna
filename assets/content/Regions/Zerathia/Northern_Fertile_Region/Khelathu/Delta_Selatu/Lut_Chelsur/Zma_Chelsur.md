## Overview {#overview}

**Zma-Chelsur** (about 9,700 people) is a hunters' landing in the eastern marsh of [[place-lutchelsrslt|Lut-Chelsur Selat]], reached by boat from [[place-lutchelsur|Lut-Chelsur]]. The marsh gives fish and fowl, and the town buys grain from the capital with the hides and the teeth that the hunters sell to the temple.

River-beast hunters, hide-curers and boat-keepers live here. The crocodiles the hunters kill are sacred to the cult, and a hunter who kills the wrong one pays the temple in coin or in service. It is larger than the capital because the marsh offers more hunters' work than the capital offers anything else.
