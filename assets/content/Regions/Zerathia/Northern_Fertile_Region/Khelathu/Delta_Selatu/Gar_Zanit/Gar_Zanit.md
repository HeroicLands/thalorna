## Overview {#overview}

Gar-Zanit is a city of looms. "Listen," says a master weaver, holding a hand up in the lane outside her workshop, "that is every house on this street at work. When it stops, somebody has died or a festival has started." The city is the capital of [[affiliation-selatgarzant|the Selat of Gar-Zanit]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]], home to about 34,000 people.

It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-psaqaru|Saq'aru]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns.

## Politics {#present}

The flax fields of Gar-Zanit depend on wet ground, and two low floods leave the retting pits and flax beds short of water. The cloth that clothes the empire is thinner, the weavers of the linen towns work for fewer wages, and the Halzi'a's tax in cloth comes in lighter than the rolls expect. He pays the throne in the cloth he has and enters the missing bolts as a failed crop. The temple of Saq'aru holds the weavers' guild-stores, and in a year of idle looms those stores feed the weavers' families. The funerary trades upriver are buying linen for their wrappings, and the Halzi'a sells to them first, since they pay in silver and the throne pays in promises. The city sells fewer bolts and more rope.

## Order and Law {#order-and-law}

The Halzi'a of Gar-Zanit is a hereditary governor and a Zabu who commands the levies, collects the taxes and dispenses justice. The Canal-Warden keeps the sluices that give the flax its days of water, the Overseer of Scribes keeps the tax bureau that counts the bolts, and the temple of Saq'aru, god of creation and craftsmanship, keeps the guild-stores and the weavers' name for the work of their hands. The danger is hunger behind idle looms. A weaver who is not paid cannot buy bread, and a house whose loom stops for want of fiber can fall into debt to an estate. The shuttered street of a city that normally never falls quiet is the first sign of it.

## What the City Lives By {#what-the-city-lives-by}

Gar-Zanit lives by flax and linen. The flax is grown on the selat's fields, retted in water, spun and woven in the city and its towns, and sold up and down the river and out through the delta ports. Cloth is also the delta's small coin: a village that has no flax pays its debts in whatever it can weave. The flax-farming villages around the city supply the fiber and take bread-grain and beer in payment, and the weavers eat what those villages grow. Away from the flax the villages and temple estates of [[place-garzanitselt|Gar-Zanit Selat]] grow grain on the usual terms, and the scribes are the one reliable ladder out of the fields. The chief temple and its estates anchor the weaving towns.
