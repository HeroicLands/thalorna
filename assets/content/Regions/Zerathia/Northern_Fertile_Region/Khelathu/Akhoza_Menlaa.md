"Every field in the valley has a line in some survey roll," a scribe of the spring surveys tells the apprentice walking beside her, "and every roll answers to a temple. **Akhoza-Menlaa** has no line in any of them."

Akhoza-Menlaa is not a place a map could show. Its site is kept out of every record the [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelathi]] keep, in a country that writes down everything from a ferry fare to a field boundary. The embalmers say that a circle works there against the balance the empire holds between the living and the dead, and that whoever finds the place without being shown is marked for the embalmer's table.

:::secret {title="What the circle does"}
The omission is the circle's own choice. What the circle does there is experiment on the dead and on the spirit-realm, against everything the empire holds about the balance between the two. What the circle's necromancers want above all is the unclaimed dead, and the largest store of them lies unwrapped in the silt of [[place-zelhalzi|Zel-Halzi]], where no Reading has been able to close a name.

Whoever finds the site without being shown is marked for the embalmer's table, which is the circle's whole answer to the question of discovery.
:::
