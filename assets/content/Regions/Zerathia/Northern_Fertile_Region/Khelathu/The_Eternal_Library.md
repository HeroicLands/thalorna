A schoolmaster of [[affiliation-rethsaar|Reth'Sa'ar]] ends the first lesson the same way every year: "Write down only what is so. The Library has it already, and he is reading over your shoulder."

**The Eternal Library** is the celestial archive of [[lore-rethsaardty|Reth'Sa'ar]], the Scribe of the Eternal Library. The [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelathi]] hold that it exists both within the world and beyond it. In its vaults the god inscribes every moment of creation with his ibis quill, so that nothing of importance is ever lost. No door in the world opens on it, and no temple claims to hold it: the library-temples of the empire, with their walls of scrolls and tablets, are the world's archives, and the Eternal Library is the one they stand beside.

## What It Holds

The Library holds what the temple archives cannot, which is what was never written: every word spoken and every deed unrecorded. The deeds of mortals and the workings of the cosmos are recorded in the same hand. The temple archives at [[place-khelunu|Khelunu]], the deepest in the empire, hold the king-lists and the priestly records, and the Library holds the rest.

## What It Is Used For

[[lore-qearetdty|Qe'aret]]'s reading of a dead person's account needs both the Library and the temple archives at Khelunu, because the account the archives hold covers only what was written down. The faith teaches that knowledge is the greatest treasure and the surest path to virtue, and an astrologer who reads the stars as the divine script written across the heavens takes her study as worship of the god who keeps it. A text said to be "written in the Library" is a text no archive in the world holds.
