## Overview {#overview}

"Stand here, at the foot of the bridge, and face the island," a young priest of [[affiliation-uqaa|Uqa'a]] tells a party of pilgrims at first light. "Behind you the city is born every morning. In front of you is the mound where the world began."

The **Bridge of the Coming-Forth** is the eastern of the two bridge-causeways of [[place-waluqaa|Wal-Uqa'a]], the Sun's Road. It leaves [[place-qeltge|Qelt-Ge]], the plaza where the road meets the river, and crosses to the eastern gate of [[place-yathtelgu|Yath-Telgu]], the Sacred Island. It is built of stone on a long ramped approach and stands above the greatest flood, so the processions that cross it do so dry-shod above the inundation. It takes its name from the midsummer dawn, which breaks straight down the eastern road.

## Inside {#inside}

The causeway carries the avenue its whole way, with the carved sphinxes and ram-statues of the Sun's Road along it. At the island end it meets the island's white wall and the gate the royal guard watches day and night. Lesser crossings in the city are pontoon bridges, floated out and drawn back with the water, and where those fail the city falls back on sacred ferrying; this bridge stays open through the flood.

## Who Is There {#who-is-there}

The royal guard holds the island end. It is a body apart from the city's [[affiliation-meglai|Meglai]], sworn to the [[lore-garauu|Gar-Au]]'s person and the holy ground, and its sergeants decide who goes on. "Everything inside it is spoken for," a sergeant tells a petitioner at the foot of the bridge.

## The Present {#present}

The bridge is the way in to everything the court holds. A party that wants an audience, a priest's hearing or a look at [[place-themeasure|The Measure]] queues at its foot, and a summons is the only thing that carries a stranger across. At the midsummer [Festival of the Coming-Forth]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} the whole capital turns out to watch the god born along his own road, and the image of Uqa'a goes across it to the island.

## History {#history}

The law that keeps the view from the temple to the horizon along the road clear is older than memory and has stood through every dynasty. The court has held the island since [[lore-crossmound|the Crossing to the Mound]].
