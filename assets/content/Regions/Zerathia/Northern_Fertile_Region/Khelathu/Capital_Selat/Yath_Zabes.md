## Overview {#overview}

"We answer to the capital for the harvest," says the headman of **Yath-Zabes**, "and for most else we answer to nobody."

The village is one of the farm-and-market villages of [[place-galezkaraslt|Galezkara Selat]], about 450 people feeding [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]]'s granaries. It draws its own living from the same traffic that carries its grain to the city.
