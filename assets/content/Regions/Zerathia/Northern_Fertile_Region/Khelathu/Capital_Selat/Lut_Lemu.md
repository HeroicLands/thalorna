"One kiln is a nuisance and ten kilns are a quarter," a faience-maker says, feeding her fire. "Light yours and see how long the others wait."

**Lut-Lemu** (the **Place of Craftsmen**) is the artisan quarter of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]]: coppersmiths, potters, faience-makers, weavers and woodworkers working loud and smoky trades. It is kept downstream and downwind of the rest of the city, which shows how the capital weighs its craftsmen's usefulness against their noise. A kiln lit here is answered within the hour by a dozen others, since no one trade in the quarter works in isolation from the rest. A fire is the quarter's danger: a blaze in the crowded lanes can run from workshop to workshop before the [[affiliation-meglai|Meglai]]'s fire-gangs reach it.

## Layers {#history}

The quarter belongs to the living city that has grown along and off the Sun's Road, unplanned, in the centuries since the court crossed to the mound in 1006 ST (1105 BF) ([[lore-crossmound|the Crossing to the Mound]], [[lore-akhsunroad|the Sun's Road]]). Its mudbrick is torn down and rebuilt every few generations on its own rubble, as the living city's is.
