## Overview {#overview}

"Beyond the island, across the second channel, is where the sun goes to die each evening, and where the kings went before it," a priest tells pilgrims, and the **Bridge of the Going-Down** is what he points at. It is the western of the two bridge-causeways of [[place-waluqaa|Wal-Uqa'a]], the Sun's Road. It leaves the western face of the [[place-greattempluqaa|Great Temple of Uqa'a]], carries the road over the second channel and up onto the desert shelf, where the road becomes the single avenue of the necropolis of [[place-zugezer|Zu-Gezer]]. Like its eastern sister it is built of stone on long ramped approaches and stands above the greatest flood.

## Inside {#inside}

The causeway runs level and straight from the temple's western forecourt to the desert shelf, and its far end is the head of the death-road, where nothing may stand in the line of the setting sun. The necropolis never floods: it sits beyond the water's reach, so the dead lie forever above the inundation and the living below it.

## Who Is There {#who-is-there}

The island end lies inside the royal guard's wall. The far end belongs to the necropolis guard under its Captain, a body separate from the army, the royal guard and the city's [[affiliation-meglai|Meglai]], which walks the necropolis and keeps its order.

## The Present {#present}

The bridge is the road to the dead. A party goes west across it by daylight to a tomb or the Ways of the necropolis, and the guard on the far shore decides what business the living have there. Funeral parties cross by the necropolis ferry. At the midwinter [Festival of the Going-Down]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} the sun sets straight down the death-road into the necropolis, and the whole capital watches the god die along his own road.
