## Overview {#overview}

Lombesa is the seat of the [[affiliation-lobwezi|Lobwezi]] paramount, a village-sized seat round an ancient baobab in one of the perennial-water districts of the [[place-sthrnsvnhs|Southern Savannahs]], in the country of the baobab-clan. Its fields of sorghum and millet lie round the tree, and its household folds are small.

The village is where [[lore-twodispersals|The Song of the Two Dispersals]] is sung when a child of Bwelende or Shendala is named, because Lombesa took in households of both when their baobabs died. Its griot is the custodian of the tree, and the paramount holds the clan's villages and their griots in order from here.
