## Overview {#overview}

Nkabewi is the seat of the [[affiliation-sengala|Sengala]] paramount, a village-sized great cattle-fold in the northern grass-and-tree belt of the [[place-sthrnsvnhs|Southern Savannahs]], where the grassland meets the southern margin of the [[place-cntrlrnfrsts|Central Rainforests]]. It is the leopard-clan's seat.

Traders from the forest tribes camp beside its fold in the season, and its trading houses keep their goods and their pacts here. The Heads of the Trading Houses hold the routes from here, and the paramount holds the clan's bargains with the rainforest tribes.
