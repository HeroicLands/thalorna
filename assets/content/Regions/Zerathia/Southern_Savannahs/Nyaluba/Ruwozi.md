## Overview {#overview}

The **Ruwozi** are the lion-clan of the [[affiliation-nylbtrblntn|Nyáluba Tribal Nation]], the cattle-herders of the central grasslands of the [[place-sthrnsvnhs|Southern Savannahs]] and the most numerous of the five clans, about eight hundred thousand people. They are first among the clans for one reason: the [Long Pact]{.name data-type="lore/law"} was sworn in their country, under a great baobab, by [Nyoli Ruwozi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Daughter of the Lion, and the lion was witness to it. Their wealth is cattle, their watchword is courage, and their humor is as dry as the dry season.

## The Clan Now {#the-house-now}

The paramount is the Nyoli Ruwozi, by custom a daughter of the senior lineage, and the title passes to a woman of that line as a name passes. The sixth to bear it is [[being-nzoburwz|Nzobu Ruwozi]], about fifty, who has presided over three Lubemos and is held across all five clans to know the totemic law as few do. As Convener she summons the [[affiliation-greatlubemo|Great Lubemo]] and speaks last in it, and the Ruwozi griot recites [[lore-braiddaughters|The Braid of the Daughters]] when she takes the convener's place at [[place-hetolubemo|Heto Lubemo]].

A Ruwozi man takes a single lion-tooth at adulthood, and the taking is the ordeal by which he enters the tradition. A Ruwozi woman braids her hair in patterns that record her lineage. No Ruwozi hunts a lion or eats lion flesh, and a beast lost to lions on the guide's ground is entered in the tally as a rendering and not a loss ([[affiliation-nyalblion|Lion Totem]]).

## Territory and Waters {#holdings}

The clan's country is the central grasslands: rolling open plains broken by rocky outcrops, lone baobabs and seasonal pans that flood in the wet season and dry to cracked clay in the dry. The great herds of antelope, wild cattle and zebra cross it twice a year, and the Ruwozi follow them with their own. The paramount's great cattle-fold is [[place-mbolewevlg|Mbolewe]], and the tree of the swearing stands in central Ruwozi country, where the Guardian of the Pact-Baobab says who may approach it.

The pans carry the herds through the wet months, and the dry season sends them to the rivers, which run through Kolembozi country before they reach the sea. West of the clan's range lie the dry-country tribes, who are not party to the Pact; the Ruwozi paramounts keep personal relations with their chieftains and no policy toward them.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

The Ruwozi are at war with the [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]], the first war between two clans of the Pact. The dry season sent the herds toward rivers that run through Kolembozi country, and a channel that fed the Ruwozi pans had been turned aside. The Ruwozi say the Kolembozi cut it. The Kolembozi say another hand cut it, and which account is true is not settled. A herd driven to a Kolembozi pool ended in a killing, the lineages on both sides took up the feud, and the feud became a war when the paramount ordered the muster.

The regiments stand mustered at [[place-mbolewevlg|Mbolewe]], and the Regiment-Caller keeps the cohorts at home while the paramount sits as Convener at [[place-hetolubemo|Heto Lubemo]]. The paramount travels there with her delegation, and the business of the sitting is her own. As Convener she keeps the order of speaking, and the order puts the war first and has her speak last, so she hears every other paramount before she says a word on a quarrel in which she is a party. The clan holds that the rivers are the herds' by the Pact's third term, which shares the rivers, and asks of the other three clans that they stand with the herds.

The paramount keeps her personal relations with the dry-country chieftains, who are not party to the Pact, and she has sent word to them since the muster.

## History as Told {#history}

The Ruwozi tell the swearing as their own. Nine generations ago, five mutually hostile clans fled a catastrophe and agreed under a baobab in Ruwozi country to stand together, and Nyoli Ruwozi answered allies who pressed her for a constitution with the words the lion cult keeps in [[lore-swearbaobab|The Swearing Under the Baobab]]: that the five were not building a kingdom, only agreeing not to destroy each other. Every woman who has borne the title since is the Daughter of the Lion again.

## Arms {#arms}

The clan keeps no standing force. Its regiments are age-set cohorts of young men and young women, who pass initiation together, take a regimental name and a totem, and stay bound for life. The paramount orders a muster, the Regiment-Caller names the cohorts, and several thousand trained fighters can be in the field within days. Ruwozi fighting is a herdsman's: mobile, in open country, and concentrated where the enemy is thin.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **A herd at the wrong river.** A Ruwozi household has driven its cattle to a pool in Kolembozi country and will not say whose leave it had. A party hired to bring the herd back learns that the pool is the one the diverted channel left dry.
- **The paramount's message.** The Ruwozi paramount wants word carried to a dry-country chieftain, outside the Lubemo and outside the other four paramounts' knowledge.
