## Overview {#overview}

Heto Lubemo is a dry-stone enclosure in the southeastern highlands of the [[place-sthrnsvnhs|Southern Savannahs]], one of the great [[place-oldhetos|Old Hetos]], and the meeting ground of the [[affiliation-greatlubemo|Great Lubemo]] of the [[affiliation-nylbtrblntn|Nyáluba Tribal Nation]]. The [[affiliation-ngonzi|Ngonzi]] keep it. For most of the four years between gatherings it is quiet stone; every fourth dry season it is the political center of five clans.

It is also the one Old Heto that outsiders may enter. The Ngonzi turn every outsider away from every other Old Heto, the Nyáluba of the other four clans included, and make one exception here, for the delegations of the five-clan council who sit within its enclosure under the Ngonzi who keep it.

## The Enclosure {#enclosure}

The enclosure is an oval of dry-stone walls, fitted without mortar and higher than a tall man, set on a raised platform at the edge of the escarpment where the highlands fall to the central plains. Lesser enclosures and platforms stand around it, and nobody living knows what they were for. The Nyáluba do not call it a ruin. Like every Old Heto it is held to be inhabited by the spirits of the stone-builders who raised it, and the Ngonzi, who alone claim descent from those builders, make the offerings that keep the Lubemo welcome there.

The highlands are cool and the rivers run all year, so the camps outside the wall have water. The delegations camp in grounds apportioned among the five clans, and several hundred people in support roles camp with them. The formal sessions are held inside the stone, and nobody is admitted to them but the delegations, with the guests and witnesses who come as part of a delegation, and the Ngonzi who keep the wall.

## Who Keeps It {#who-holds-it}

The Guardian of Heto Lubemo is an eagle-shaman of the [[affiliation-ngonzi|Ngonzi]] who holds this enclosure and no other. The Guardian makes the offerings, receives each delegation at the wall, and says who may pass. The paramount of the Ngonzi sits among the five paramounts inside and is host in everything else, and the apportioned grounds are the clan's to allot.

The Ngonzi's authority here is the authority of a host and a keeper, and not of a ruler. They convene nothing. The Convener summons the Lubemo, the Ngonzi receive it.

## The Great Lubemo {#the-great-lubemo}

The Great Lubemo is convened at Heto Lubemo by the [[affiliation-ruwozi|Ruwozi]] paramount and lasts about six weeks. Each of the five clans, Ruwozi, [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]], Ngonzi, [[affiliation-sengala|Sengala]] and [[affiliation-lobwezi|Lobwezi]], sends a delegation of the same size, thirty to fifty people: the paramount, the paramount's senior advisors and senior griot, the clan's most experienced shamans, and younger lineage representatives who come to watch and learn. The [[affiliation-ekunda|Ékunda]] and the [[affiliation-fenjara|Fénjara]] each send a delegate.

The order of business never changes: defense and any war begun since the last Lubemo; inter-clan disputes the paramounts could not settle; the state of the pastures, rivers and herds; questions of totemic law; and last, the renewal of the [Long Pact]{.name data-type="lore/law"}, conducted by the senior griots and witnessed by all. The renewal keeps the fourth term as [[lore-swearbaobab|The Swearing Under the Baobab]] counts it: [Fourth: the five come back in their own bodies/every fourth dry season, and say it again.]{.verse} The paramounts speak in turn, the Ruwozi last, and the Lubemo decides by their convergence, never by vote. A matter they cannot agree on waits four years for the next gathering.

Outsiders who watch the sessions and see only ceremony are mistaken. The clans have argued everything out at home in the months before, and Heto Lubemo is where their decisions become commitments of the Pact. The Lobwezi griots enter into the record which clan holds each claim.

## The Years Between {#the-years-between}

Between gatherings the enclosure is empty of everyone but the Guardian and the Ngonzi who tend it. The offerings continue, the walls are kept, and the grounds are left to grass. The paramounts consult by drum in the meantime, and nothing is decided here.

## History {#history}

The enclosure is one of the Old Hetos, raised by a people who built in stone before the Nyáluba, and the Great Lubemo meets in it every fourth dry season to renew the Long Pact ([[lore-nyalubahist|the History of the Nyáluba]]). Nobody living knows who raised the enclosure, or why the lesser platforms around it stand. The Ngonzi say the builders were their ancestors, and the Lobwezi griots say the builders may have been the ancestors of some other people. [Lusheya nu Kolembozi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the senior spirit-speaker of the Kolembozi, is said to have walked into an Old Heto in her initiation and come out speaking [[lore-stonetongue|the Stone Tongue]], the speech the Ngonzi elders say was the builders'. The Ngonzi confirm the report and the griots have entered it into the record of the Lubemo.

## The Present {#present}

The Lubemo is gathering at Heto Lubemo this year, and the Ngonzi are hosts of a council whose Convener is at war. The [[affiliation-ruwozi|Ruwozi]] paramount, who must summon the sitting and speak last, is a party to the war with the [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]] over the rivers. The delegations are arriving to the apportioned grounds, the Guardian is receiving them at the wall, and the three other clans have not said whom they will stand with.

The Guardian has ruled that no weapon passes the wall, and the Ngonzi paramount, as host, has placed the Ruwozi and the Kolembozi at opposite ends of the grounds. The Sengala delegation keeps close to the Kolembozi camp, the Lobwezi griots go between, and the [[affiliation-ekunda|Ékunda]] delegate camps apart, on neither clan's ground. The matter is the first business of the sitting, and if the paramounts cannot converge it waits for the next sitting.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

A party reaches the enclosure as a delegation's guest, a griot's witness or a trader's foreign guest, and not otherwise. It is stopped at the wall by the Guardian, who asks whose guest it is. A party inside sees the platform's stone and the sessions, hears the recital of the Pact, and is not admitted to any other Old Heto however long it stays.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The guest list.** A delegation's guest has been refused at the wall by the Guardian, and the delegation will pay for the refusal to be explained.
- **Outside the wall.** The support camps hold several hundred people who are not in the sessions, and what they say to each other reaches the paramounts before the paramounts speak.
