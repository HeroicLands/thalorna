## Overview {#overview}

Nyangulo is the seat of the [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]] paramount, a village-sized great cattle-fold at the edge of the eastern wetlands of the [[place-sthrnsvnhs|Southern Savannahs]], in the country of the crocodile-clan. Its fold stands on dry ground above the channels, and the landings below it hold the long reed canoes of the households that fish the reaches around.

The paramount's compound looks out over water the clan's spirit-speakers know by name. When the paramount goes to [[place-hetolubemo|Heto Lubemo]] to sit in the [[affiliation-greatlubemo|Great Lubemo]], the paramount leaves the Master of the Canoes to keep the landings.
