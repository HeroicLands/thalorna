## Overview {#overview}

Ndelushi is the seat of the [[affiliation-ngonzi|Ngonzi]] paramount, a village-sized great cattle-fold on the highland terraces of the southeastern [[place-sthrnsvnhs|Southern Savannahs]], in the country of the eagle-clan. Its fold lies behind a low dry-stone wall that the clan's own builders raised, with terraced fields below it and a river that runs all year.

The village is where the clan's goldwork is done: ingots panned from the highland rivers are worked here by hammer and bone tool before they travel north. The paramount receives here the Guardians of the Old Hetos, and goes from here to [[place-hetolubemo|Heto Lubemo]], which the clan keeps for the [[affiliation-greatlubemo|Great Lubemo]].
