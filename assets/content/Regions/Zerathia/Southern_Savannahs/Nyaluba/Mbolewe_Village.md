## Overview {#overview}

Mbolewe is the seat of the [[affiliation-ruwozi|Ruwozi]] paramount, a village-sized great cattle-fold on the open plains of the central grasslands of the [[place-sthrnsvnhs|Southern Savannahs]], in the country of the lion-clan. It is a ring of herding households round a great fold of thornbrush, with the paramount's compound at its head, and a mass of cattle at every season, since the paramount's herds and the cattle owed to the paramount are driven in to be counted and tallied.

The village is the seat of the Nyoli Ruwozi, and when she travels to [[place-hetolubemo|Heto Lubemo]] to convene the [[affiliation-greatlubemo|Great Lubemo]] she goes from here. It stands empty of its young men and women when the regiments muster.
