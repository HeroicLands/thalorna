## Overview {#overview}

**The Dry Reach** is a reach of channel in the country of the [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]], some days from [[place-hetolubemo|Heto Lubemo]]. The channel bed is cracked to the papyrus, a dry landing stands below it, and a dry pool lies at the foot of the landing. Above them a reach holds water in a new backwater.

The households of the dry landing fish, ferry and water their stock from the channel. The [[being-dryreachelder|Reach-Elder of the Dry Landing]] says the channel was diverted by hand.

## The Land {#the-land}

The channel runs through the eastern wetlands, where the rivers from the highlands spread and slow before the coast. The Kolembozi read the channels as other clans read a road. A landing is a place where a household beaches its canoes and keeps its weirs, and a reach is the stretch of channel its households hold together.

## Who Uses It {#who-uses-it}

The households of the dry landing use the channel below the cracked bed, and the households of the reach above use the water that now stands there. The [[affiliation-fenjara|Fénjara]] send trackers to read the ground when a clan asks for a witness.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **Canoe parties.** The reach above is held by boatmen with spears in their own water, wary of strangers.
- **Dry ground.** A pool and a landing with no water in them hold stranded fish, dust and thirst.

## What Is Told {#what-is-told}

The Reach-Elder of the dry landing says the channel was diverted by hand, and nobody has named the hand.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The dry landing wants the hand that cut the channel found and named. The reach above wants strangers to cross as guests or not at all. Both clans of the war, the [[affiliation-ruwozi|Ruwozi]] and the Kolembozi, have asked the Fénjara to read the channel.
