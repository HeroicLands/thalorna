## Overview {#overview}

A Lobwezi griot, asked where the Nyáluba keep their king, counts on one hand instead: five clans, one pact, no king. The **Great Lubemo** is the ruling body of the [[affiliation-nylbtrblntn|Nyáluba Tribal Nation]]: a _lubemo_, a sitting that talks until the objections stop, of the paramounts of the five clan-nations and their delegations. It meets every fourth dry season at [[place-hetolubemo|Heto Lubemo]], one of the Old Hetos, and settles what touches all five. It is a political body and not a spiritual one.

## Who Sits {#who-sits}

The five paramounts of the [[affiliation-ruwozi|Ruwozi]], [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]], [[affiliation-ngonzi|Ngonzi]], [[affiliation-sengala|Sengala]] and [[affiliation-lobwezi|Lobwezi]] sit with their delegations. The Ruwozi paramount, the Nyoli Ruwozi, the Daughter of the Lion, is the Convener: she summons the Lubemo, keeps its order of speaking and speaks last, because the Long Pact was sworn in Ruwozi country under a baobab. The Ngonzi keep the enclosure. The Lobwezi griots enter into the record of the Lubemo which clan holds each claim, and the senior griot recites the Pact's memory. The [[affiliation-ekunda|Ékunda]] and the [[affiliation-fenjara|Fénjara]] each send a delegate, who may speak for the tribe and may bind it only with the elders' agreement, and the clans listen because every one of them waters its cattle at Ékunda in a bad year.

## How It Decides {#how-it-decides}

The Lubemo does not vote. It talks until the objections stop, in an order fixed by custom with the senior speaker last, and the slowness is the method: a decision reached by convergence holds. The fixed order of business is defense and any war begun since the last Lubemo, inter-clan disputes the paramounts could not settle, the state of pastures and herds, questions of totemic law, and last the renewal of the Long Pact. A matter the paramounts cannot agree on waits four years. Between Lubemos the paramounts consult by drum, and drum-speakers carry messages across all five territories in about two days.

## The Present {#present}

The Lubemo is being convened this year, and the delegations are gathering at Heto Lubemo. The matter the paramounts could not settle is a war begun since the last Lubemo: the Ruwozi against the Kolembozi, the cattle-clan whose herds need the rivers the crocodile-clan controls, after a dry season and a diverted channel. The Convener is a party to the war, so the convergence is in doubt, and the other three clans must choose sides or hold the Pact by convergence. The water-keeping Ékunda, who stand between the clans, are drawn in.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

A party meets the Lubemo as a delegation's guest, a griot's witness or a trader's foreign guest, and is received at the enclosure by the Ngonzi who keep it.
