The Nyáluba keep their history in the memory of the Lobwezi griots, and what the griots hold begins with a stone people and ends with a pact. The dates are counts of generations.

## The Builders

The highlands of the southeast held a people who built in stone before the Nyáluba were a people. Their dry-stone walls, platforms and oval enclosures stand as [[place-oldhetos|the Old Hetos]], and the Nyáluba hold them to be inhabited by their builders' spirits. The Ngonzi alone claim descent from the builders and keep the Hetos. A Kolembozi spirit-speaker came out of one speaking the speech the Ngonzi elders say was the builders' ([[lore-stonetongue|the Stone Tongue]]), and the Lobwezi griots entered it into the record of the Lubemo.

## The Catastrophe

Before the Pact the five clans were mutual rivals and cattle-feuds were the custom of the country. By the griots' account the southward expansion of a militarist confederacy of the central savannahs, now extinct, took cattle, women and lives indiscriminately. The telling has smoothed the disaster until its nature rests on what the griots maintain.

## The Long Pact

Nine generations ago, perhaps two hundred and fifty years past and perhaps more, the five clans met under a great baobab in central Ruwozi country. [Nyoli Ruwozi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Daughter of the Lion, a Ruwozi paramount whose name every senior Ruwozi woman since has carried as a hereditary title, led the swearing. The terms are few: the clans will not raise arms against each other, will assist each other against any outside threat, will share the pastures, rivers and rains, will renew the Pact in person at the Great Lubemo every fourth dry season, and will amend it only by the unanimous consent of the five paramounts. It founds no central authority and raises no tax or levy beyond the Lubemo itself ([[lore-swearbaobab|the Swearing Under the Baobab]], [[affiliation-nylbtrblntn|the Nyáluba Tribal Nation]]).

## The Peace and the Wars Outside

The clans have not fought each other since the Pact until the present war. They fought outsiders: pre-Pact raiders from the central plains, Bethuan slaving parties along the northern margin, and the great drought-war of three generations ago, when famine pushed neighboring tribes into the Sengala country and the regiments had to be called out. They have not lost a war in living memory ([[affiliation-nylbtrblntn|the Nyáluba Tribal Nation]]).

## The Great Lubemo

The Pact is renewed in person every fourth dry season at [[place-hetolubemo|Heto Lubemo]], the one Old Heto the Ngonzi open, and the Lobwezi griots carry the memory of each renewal. The Lubemo decides by the convergence of the five paramounts and never by vote, and a matter on which they cannot agree waits four years ([[affiliation-greatlubemo|the Great Lubemo]]).

## The Pact in 720 AF

The Lubemo is being convened in 720 AF, and the matter the paramounts could not settle is a war begun since the last one: the Ruwozi against the Kolembozi. The convener is a party to it, and the Ngonzi, Sengala and Lobwezi must choose sides or hold the Pact by convergence ([[affiliation-greatlubemo#present|the Great Lubemo, The Present]]).

## What It Left {#what-it-left}

- **The Pact.** Five clans share a peace, a defensive alliance and a ritual calendar, and each stays sovereign at home.
- **The Lubemo.** The council meets every fourth dry season and has no vote.
- **The Old Hetos.** The stone enclosures belong to the Ngonzi, and Heto Lubemo is the one they open.
