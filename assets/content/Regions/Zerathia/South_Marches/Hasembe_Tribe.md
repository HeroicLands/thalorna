## Overview {#overview}

The Hasembe are the eastern tribe of the [[place-sthmrchsrgn|South Marches]], the marcher clans of the long forage belts that run east along [[place-marchesroad|the Marches Road]] toward the frontier of [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]]. They keep sheep, goats and small sturdy horses, and their sons are the light horse that the caravans and the free companies hire. Their seat is [[place-hasembecamp|Hasembe Camp]], a well-camp on the road.

They are of the same stock as the frontier clans of [[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]], and they and the [[affiliation-dorumbetribe|Dorumbe Tribe]] are one people with two grazings. A Hasembe says his tent first and his tribe second.

## How It Is Led {#how-it-is-led}

The headman is [[being-khandoruhsmb|Khandoru of the Hasembe]], who holds the tribe by descent and consent together, and the elders of the clans sit with him. The Elder of the Grazing assigns the forage belts season by season, and the Hirer of Riders runs the tribe's one business beyond herding. The head of a clan and of a household is a man, and stock, wells and grazing pass from father to son.

## Range, Wells and Grounds {#range}

The tribe grazes the eastern belts and waters at the wells of the eastern Marches ([[place-longwells|the Long Wells]]), among them the eastern stages of [[place-khuqetzalu|Khuqet-Zalu]], the walled Khelathi wells that stand a day apart along the road. Its country ends where the grazing selatu of [[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu Region]] begin, and the tribe and the [[affiliation-borderselatu|Border Selatu]] each keep a list of whose flocks have crossed. Most of the tribe camps in clan groups at the eastern wells, among them Tarembe Camp, Njeko Camp and Okhelo Camp, and gathers at Hasembe Camp for hiring and trade.

## Relations {#relations}

The Hasembe trade warily with the grazing selatu, selling horses and buying grain, and they send an envoy to the Halzi'a whenever a herd strays. They pay Bethua no tribute of their own; the Dorumbe carry it for the Marches. They hire out riders to every caravan that crosses their wells and to every free company that pays, which makes them useful to [[affiliation-jcklsthmrchs|the Jackals of the South Marches]] and watched by everyone else.

## History {#history}

The eastern stages of Khuqet-Zalu were the first forts the sun-house built on the road, and the Hasembe say that a Khelathi quartermaster taught their grandfathers to keep a cistern lidded ([[lore-templetrib|the Tribute of the Temple-Cities]]).

## The Present Pressure {#present}

The Marches' tribute is late, and the Hasembe are the ones the road feels it through. The Hirer of Riders has more caravans asking for escort than riders to give, because the Dorumbe have called their horsemen home for the autumn drive and Hasembe Camp contracts the Dorumbe riders as well as its own, and a caravan that finds no escort at the eastern wells pays a company that holds the next well instead. Khandoru does not want the Jackals to be that company.

## The Gathering Place {#gathering-place}

[[place-hasembecamp|Hasembe Camp]] is where a caravan master hires riders and where the Envoy to the Selatu is chosen.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The unescorted caravan.** A caravan master stands at the eastern wells with no riders, and the Hirer of Riders will name a price for a party that takes the contract.
- **The strayed herd.** A selat's herd has crossed into Hasembe grazing and the Envoy wants someone who can read a brand and speak to a Halzi'a.
- **The tribe's share.** A young horseman has come home from the Jackals' pay with more coin than a season's riding should earn.
