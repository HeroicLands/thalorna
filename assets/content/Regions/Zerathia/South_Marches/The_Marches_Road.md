## Overview {#overview}

**The Marches Road** is a track and not a built road. It leaves the Bethuan frontier at the last frontier posts, bends along the forage belts under the highland spurs, and runs east from well to well across the drylands toward the grazing selatu of [[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu Region]]. It is the way a caravan takes when the coast is closed to it and the way a fugitive takes who cannot use the northern ports. A caravan with a good guide takes about twenty days to cross.

There is no milestone on it, no paving and no ditch. What marks it is the line of wells, each a day from the next, and the hoof-bare ground between them.

## What Moves on It {#what-moves-on-it}

Caravans of Bethuan grain and cloth go east, and Khelathi gold, turquoise and copper come west when the sea routes are shut or too watched. Herds are driven along it from belt to belt, and so are the tribute stock for the frontier posts. A caravan that must avoid a House's customs takes it; so does a runaway bound-man and a woman stripped of her House.

## The Way {#logistics}

The road keeps to the forage belts, where the stock a caravan needs can graze, and to the wells, where it can drink. A caravan carries water for the dry stage and grain for its beasts. Dust, thorn and stone make the going slow, and the seasonal streambeds, dry for most of the year, can come down in a night.

## The Stations {#stations}

The stations are the wells, and most are the old Khelathi stages of [[place-khuqetzalu|Khuqet-Zalu]]. The chain of forts runs on west across the Marches toward the eastern temple-cities of [[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]], the end the sun-house built it for; the Bethuan frontier is the branch the caravans use. The western ones stand nearest Bethua; [[place-dorumbecamp|Dorumbe Camp]] lies a short way off the road at the foot of the spurs, and [[place-hasembecamp|Hasembe Camp]] stands on it in the east. Between them a caravan pays at every well that someone holds ([[place-longwells|the Long Wells]]).

## Who Patrols and Taxes It {#present}

Nobody patrols it and everybody taxes it. Whoever holds a well holds the road beside it, and the Marches' tolls are paid in coin, stock or water to the clan that owns the well or to the company that holds the fort. The western stages are held by [[affiliation-jcklsthmrchs|the Jackals of the South Marches]]; the clans of the [[affiliation-hasembetribe|Hasembe Tribe]] sell escort at the eastern wells; Bethuan patrols of the [[affiliation-spearsisterhood|Spear-Sisterhood]] look at the western stages from a distance and keep a list of the ones held.

The Marches' tribute to Bethua is late, the Bethuan patrols are watching the frontier posts, the Jackals hold the western stages with fewer eyes on them, and the lesser companies are taking the wells between.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **The companies.** A caravan that will not pay at a well is robbed at the next ([[affiliation-frcmpnssthmrchs|the Free Companies of the South Marches]]).
- **Thirst.** A caravan that misses a well because a company holds it has not enough water for the next stage.
- **The streambeds.** A caravan camped in a dry streambed can lose its beasts to a flood from rain it did not see fall.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The closed coast.** A merchant whose cargo cannot pass the Bethuan customs wants a guide for the whole twenty days.
- **Who holds the well.** The company at a western stage has changed, and a caravan that paid its toll last month is being asked again.
