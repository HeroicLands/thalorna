## Overview {#overview}

Hasembe Camp is the seat of the [[affiliation-hasembetribe|Hasembe Tribe]], a spread of tents and horse-lines along [[place-marchesroad|the Marches Road]] beside a walled well that the Khelathi dug. About twelve hundred people are counted there in the dry months, and the number halves when the herds go out to the far belts.

A caravan comes here to water, to buy a skin of water for the stage beyond and to hire riders. The Hirer of Riders sits in the largest tent by the horse-lines, and his price for an escort is settled over a bowl of soured milk before it is written anywhere.
