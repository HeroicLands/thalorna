## Overview {#overview}

Thumbel Camp is a clan camp of the [[affiliation-dorumbetribe|Dorumbe Tribe]], a ring of goat-hair tents and stock pens around a rock-cistern in the western belt of the [[place-sthmrchsrgn|South Marches]]. It has no walls and no market; the clan lives on its herds and the cistern's water. The tents move with the grass, and the cistern does not.

The clan's head, the [[being-thumbelhead|Clan Head of Thumbel]], keeps its claim on the cistern and rides with its young men. Herders mind the stock beside the water, among them the [[being-thumbelboy|Herder Boy of Thumbel]], and the pens stand where the stock is gathered before the autumn drive.

## The Present {#present}

The clan set aside its share of the tribute in a pen beside the cistern, and the pen stands empty. The clan's herders say riders came by night, and the clan wants someone to blame.
