## Overview

The Free Companies are the armed bands of the [[place-sthmrchsrgn|South Marches]]: mounted companies of ten riders to a hundred that belong to no clan, no House and no crown. Country that no realm holds breeds them, and no realm holds the Marches. Each company keeps its own captain and its own articles, sells its riders by the season or the job, and raids when nobody is buying.

They are marauders first. They take the herds, the caravans and the exposed villages of the frontier, Bethuan targets by preference, and they are gone before a House levy can muster. When the grass is good they escort caravans for the same wells they would otherwise rob.

## Wells and Lairs

A company lives where it can hold water. The abandoned Khelathi forts of [[place-khuqetzalu|Khuqet-Zalu]] stand on the wells of the Marches, and the strongest companies hold the best of them: a fort with a standing cistern decides which caravans cross and which clan's herds drink. A caravan that cannot avoid the Marches pays the company that holds the well or pays a second company to escort it past ([[place-longwells|the Long Wells]]).

## The Jackals and the Rest

[[affiliation-jcklsthmrchs|The Jackals of the South Marches]] are the most feared of them, a company of the Houseless under [[being-argyrirjckl|Argirira the Jackal]] and nothing like the others. The rest are bands of marcher men, riders cast out of or hired out by the marcher clans of the [[affiliation-dorumbetribe|Dorumbe Tribe]] and the [[affiliation-hasembetribe|Hasembe Tribe]]. They are tolerated, hired and given room by the clans, because they are useful against a shared northern enemy and because too many of the clans' own sons ride in them.

## The Present {#present}

The tribute from the clans to Bethua is late, and the companies are the ones who gain by it. Bethuan patrols are watching the frontier posts instead of the wells, and the clans' riders are home for the autumn drive, so the companies have the road and the wells with fewer eyes on them. Every caravan that wanted an escort at the eastern wells is bargaining with whichever company is nearest.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **A company for hire.** A caravan master wants a company's escort past the western stages and does not want it to be the Jackals.
- **The captain's articles.** A band's captain has broken his articles and his riders want a stranger to hear the case.
- **A herd taken.** The Dorumbe have lost stock to a company that none of their own sons ride in, and the Elder of the Wells wants to know whose.
