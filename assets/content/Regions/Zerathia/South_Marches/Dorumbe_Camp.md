## Overview {#overview}

Dorumbe Camp is the seat of the [[affiliation-dorumbetribe|Dorumbe Tribe]], a ring of black goat-hair tents and stock pens around a deep well and a rock-cistern at the foot of the highland spurs, a long ride south of the last Bethuan frontier post. The tents move with the grass, and the well does not: fifteen hundred people are counted there in the dry months, when the herds come in to water, and a few hundred in the wet weeks.

The elders sit at the well. A stranger asks the Elder of the Wells for leave to water before he lets his horse drink, and the tribute stock is gathered in the pens beside the cistern before it goes north.
