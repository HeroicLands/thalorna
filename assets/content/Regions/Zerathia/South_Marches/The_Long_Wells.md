## Overview {#overview}

**The Long Wells** is the drovers' name for the wells of the [[place-sthmrchsrgn|South Marches]] taken together: the deepened wells and rock-cisterns along the road, and the springs at the foot of the highland spurs. The Marches have no river. A seasonal streambed comes off the spurs and runs north, full for a season and dry the rest, and between the streambeds the whole country is held together by these wells.

Every one is known, named and claimed. A herdsman gives the name of the well his clan waters at before he gives his own.

## The Land {#the-land}

The wells lie a day apart along [[place-marchesroad|the Marches Road]], in the strips of tougher grass and browse that carry hardy stock through the dry months. A well is a shaft in stony ground with a rock-cistern beside it and a trough for the beasts. The oldest and deepest are the Khelathi stages of [[place-khuqetzalu|Khuqet-Zalu]], each with its broken tower, and the springs under the spurs run clear after the winter rains.

## Who Uses It {#who-uses-it}

The marcher clans use them first. The [[affiliation-dorumbetribe|Dorumbe Tribe]] hold the western wells and the [[affiliation-hasembetribe|Hasembe Tribe]] the eastern, and in each tribe the elders of the clans say who waters where and in what order. Caravans water at them for a price, and the companies hold the best forts for the same reason.

A stranger asks leave before he lets a beast drink, and a clan that has watered a traveler owes him no more than the water.

## Dangers {#dangers}

- **A dry well.** A well that gave water last season may not give it this one, and the caravan that planned on it has no other.
- **A held well.** A well that a company holds is a toll, and a toll refused is a raid.
- **Foul water.** A cistern that nobody has lidded breeds sickness, and the dead beast at the bottom of a shaft is a rumor every drover has heard.

## What Is Told {#what-is-told}

The drovers say that the wells are the forts, and the forts are whoever holds the wells this year. They say a clan's claim is older than any fort, and they name the clan that owns a well now as the builder of its fort.

## Converging Interests {#present}

The marcher clans want the wells held by clans and watered by custom. The free companies want them held by whoever can keep them. [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethua]] wants the western wells closed to the companies and the tribute paid. The tribute is late, the riders are home for the autumn drive, and the wells that the companies hold have no one watching them.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **A well that has gone dry.** The Elder of the Wells wants a stranger who can find water and has no clan to favor.
- **Who dug it.** A scholar of the empire wants the cisterns' inscriptions and the clan that owns the best-preserved fort wants to be paid.
