In the heart of the [[place-cntrlrnfrsts|Central Rainforests]], far from any river, the forest ends in a perfect circle. The **Silent Clearing** is some three hundred paces across, and the line of the trees around it is as sharp as a drawn edge. Inside, the soil is grey and bare. Nothing grows in it. No bird calls over it, and no insect crosses it. The air is still, and sound goes dead a few paces in.

## What It Is

The Clearing is a wound. The [[lore-theshadow|Shadow]] is chaos from outside creation, and it touched the world here. The Clearing is where the touch landed, and the forest around it has not healed. The cause is not agreed. The tribes who know the place tell of a star that fell silent, a sorcerer's rite that went wrong, and a god's folly. None of the accounts is verified, and the Shadow does not explain itself.

What is certain is that the dead come out of it. The undead that roam the forest, which the tribes distinguish from the bound dead of ruins and the restless dead of their own graves, trace to the circle. They rise from the grey soil at dusk. They are [[being-damut|damutu]], the driven dead, bound by the Shadow and given one purpose, and they walk outward along the game trails until the sun puts them down. The forest tribes call them the **Quiet Dead**, because they make no sound.

## What Lies There

At the edge of the circle, the forest bears the marks of generations of watchers. Bell-posts of dark wood stand at intervals around the rim, each hung with a bronze bell with its clapper cut away. A ring of burnt earth lies outside them, scorched each season. The paths into the Clearing are marked in ash and renewed by a hand that does not show itself.

At the centre, according to those few who have approached, there is a place where the grey ground is darker and the light dims. Nothing there reflects. A figure is said to sit at the centre, motionless, facing outward.

## Who Knows of It, and What They Say

- **The forest tribes** teach their children to turn back at the first silence. A hunter who loses his birdsong turns around, and he does not discuss the reason.
- **The Leopard King's Court** knows it best, and its hunters patrol the rim. The hunters carry torches and oil as readily as spears.
- **The [[lore-kubindoclt|Kubindo]] elders** who have been near it say the Clearing belongs to no spirit and has no manners at all. A place with no manners has no patron to bargain with, which they take as the mark of the Shadow.
- **Northern scholars** have heard of it from river-factors and file it with the other rumours of the interior.

## Reaching It

The Clearing lies a ten-day march from the nearest river, on no trail the tribes will use. The approach is marked by silence: first the birds stop, then the insects, then the forest floor loses its leaf-litter and the trees stand bare of vines. Travelers who follow the game trails against the flow of the Quiet Dead's tracks will find it.
