## Overview

The Venâria Sea lies on the pilgrim road of the astrologers of [[place-aukhelathrgq|Aû'Khelâthu]]: the sites sacred to the empire's earliest astrologers are scattered widely, and one of the ways between them goes across this water.

## The Crossing

[[being-amqltzlmlglq|Amqelet-Zelemu Legulu’aqun]] crossed the Venâria Sea on his pilgrimage to the ancient astrological sites, between the deserts of [[place-beshakan|Beshakan]] and the forgotten ruins where the earliest astronomers carved their star maps. Agents of the rival astrologers and priests who want his reading of the Convergence silenced follow the same roads.
