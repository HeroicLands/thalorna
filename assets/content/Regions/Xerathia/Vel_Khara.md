## Overview

Vel'Khara is a distant kingdom, and in [[place-aukhelathrgq|Aû'Khelâthu]] it is known less by what it is than by what its agents want to buy. The **Kingdom of Vel'Khara** sends buyers into the empire's timber markets looking for timbers of unprecedented rarity and size, and they pay astronomical prices for them on behalf of noble patrons they decline to name.

## What the Timber Is For

A timberwright who looks closely at the order learns what the payment is for: massive siege engines built to breach the walls of Aû'Khelâthu's allies. [[being-githrrthlkhl|Githara Reth'el'Khelensu]], a timberwright who values her neutral standing in the markets, is the kind of craftsperson Vel'Khara's agents approach, and the kind who then has to decide whether her craft comes before politics and whether telling the authorities would cost her that neutrality.
