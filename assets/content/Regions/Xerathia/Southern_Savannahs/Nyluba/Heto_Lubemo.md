## Overview

Heto Lubemo is an ancient stone enclosure in the southeastern highlands of the [[place-sthrnsvnhs|Southern Savannahs]], one of the great Old Hetos, and it is the Lubemo ground of the [[affiliation-nylbtrblntn|Nyáluba Tribal Nation]]. The Ngonzi clan maintains it. For most of the four years between gatherings it is quiet stone; every fourth dry season it is the political center of five clans.

The enclosure is an oval of dry-stone walls, fitted without mortar and higher than a tall man, set on a raised platform at the edge of the escarpment, with lesser enclosures and platforms around it whose purpose nobody living knows. The Nyáluba do not call it a ruin. Like every Old Heto it is held to be inhabited by the spirits of the stone-builders who raised it, and the Ngonzi, who alone claim descent from those builders, make the offerings that keep the Lubemo welcome there.

## The Great Lubemo

The **Great Lubemo** is convened at Heto Lubemo by the Ruwozi paramount and lasts about six weeks. Each of the five clans—Ruwozi, Kolembozi, Ngonzi, Sengala and Lobwezi—sends a delegation of the same size, thirty to fifty people: the paramount, the paramount's senior advisors and senior griot, the clan's most experienced shamans, and younger lineage representatives who come to watch and learn. The delegations camp in apportioned grounds outside the enclosure, several hundred people in support roles camp with them, and the formal sessions are held within the stone.

The order of business never changes: defense and any war begun since the last Lubemo; inter-clan disputes the paramounts could not settle; the state of the pastures, rivers and herds; questions of totemic law; and last, the renewal of the Pact, conducted by the senior griots and witnessed by all. The paramounts speak in turn, the Ruwozi last, and the Lubemo decides by their convergence, never by vote. A matter they cannot agree on waits four years for the next gathering. Outsiders who watch the sessions and see only ceremony are mistaken: the clans have argued everything out at home in the months before, and Heto Lubemo is where their decisions become commitments of the Pact.
