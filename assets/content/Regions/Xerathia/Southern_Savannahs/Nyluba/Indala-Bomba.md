## Overview

Indala-Bomba is an ancient stone enclosure in the southeastern highlands of the [[place-sthrnsvnhs|Southern Savannahs]], one of the great Old Kraals, and it is the Indaba ground of the [[affiliation-nylbtrblntn|Nyáluba Tribal Nation]]. The Ngonzi clan maintains it. For most of the four years between gatherings it is quiet stone; every fourth dry season it is the political center of five clans.

## The Great Indaba

The **Great Indaba** is convened at Indala-Bomba by the Mvuzi paramount and lasts about six weeks. Each of the five clans—Mvuzi, Kambezi, Ngonzi, Sengala and Bombwe—sends a delegation of the same size, thirty to fifty people: the paramount, the paramount's senior advisors and senior griot, the clan's most experienced shamans, and younger lineage representatives who come to watch and learn. The delegations camp in apportioned grounds outside the enclosure, several hundred people in support roles camp with them, and the formal sessions are held within the stone.

The order of business never changes: defense and any war begun since the last Indaba; inter-clan disputes the paramounts could not settle; the state of the pastures, rivers and herds; questions of totemic law; and last, the renewal of the Pact, conducted by the senior griots and witnessed by all. The paramounts speak in turn, the Mvuzi last, and the Indaba decides by their convergence rather than by vote. A matter they cannot agree on waits four years for the next gathering. Outsiders who watch the sessions and see only ceremony are mistaken: the clans have argued everything out at home in the months before, and Indala-Bomba is where their decisions become commitments of the Pact.
