Zu-Leri is the oldest and highest tell of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]], crowned by the Temple of [[lore-psaqarudty|Psaq'âru]], craft-god and patron of the capital, and ringed by the walled mansions of the priestly-noble houses. It stands above the rest of the city because the rest of the city was built on top of what came before it, layer upon layer, with Zu-Leri rising highest of all of them. The deepest excavation into its ground reaches further into the capital's past than any document in [[place-garmedjatozo|Gar-Medjatozo]]'s archives.
