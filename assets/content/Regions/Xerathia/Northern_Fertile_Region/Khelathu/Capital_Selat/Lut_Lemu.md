"One kiln is a nuisance and ten kilns are a quarter," a faience-maker says, feeding her fire. "Light yours and see how long the others wait."

**Lut-Lemu** (the Place of Craftsmen) is the artisan quarter of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]]: coppersmiths, potters, faience-makers, weavers and woodworkers working loud and smoky trades. It is kept downstream and downwind of the rest of the city, which shows how the capital weighs its craftsmen's usefulness against their noise. A kiln lit here is answered within the hour by a dozen others, since no one trade in the quarter works in isolation from the rest. A fire is the quarter's danger: a blaze in the crowded lanes can run from workshop to workshop before the Meglay's fire-gangs reach it.
