Lut-Lemu is the artisan quarter of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] — coppersmiths, potters, faience-makers, weavers and woodworkers working loud and smoky trades. It is kept downstream and downwind of the rest of the city, which says plainly how the capital weighs its craftsmen's usefulness against their noise. A kiln lit here is answered within the hour by a dozen others, since no one trade in the quarter works in isolation from the rest.
