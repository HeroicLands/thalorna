A liminal quarter of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] by the necropolis-ferry, named the Pure Place for what is done in it and shunned for the same reason. The embalmers, the linen-wrappers and the carriers of the dead keep their houses here, downwind of the living city, and the rents are low for reasons nobody has to explain twice.
