Amqel-Ger is the working riverfront of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]], downstream of [[place-qeltge|Qelt-Ge]]. It lives by trade in its plainest form — docks and warehouses, the Gár-Hálzi banking-houses, the Zebequ'Zut merchant halls, and the foreign shore where Haradian and Mídhalión factors keep their compounds. A cargo that never touches the ceremonial city upstream still passes through here before it counts as having reached Galezkara at all.
