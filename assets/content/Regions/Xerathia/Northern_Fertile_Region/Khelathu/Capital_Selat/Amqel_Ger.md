"Nothing has reached Galezkara until it has crossed this quay," says a tally-man on the Great Quay, ticking a sack off against his slate. "The ceremonial city upstream can have what is left."

**Amqel-Ger** (the Great Quay) is the working riverfront of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]], downstream of [[place-qeltge|Qelt-Ge]]. It lives by trade in its plainest form: docks and warehouses, the [[affiliation-garhalzi|Gár-Hálzi]] banking-houses, the [[affiliation-zebequzut|Zebequ'Zut]] merchant halls, and the foreign shore where Haradian and Mídhalión factors keep their compounds. A cargo that never touches the ceremonial city still passes through here before it counts as having reached Galezkara at all.
