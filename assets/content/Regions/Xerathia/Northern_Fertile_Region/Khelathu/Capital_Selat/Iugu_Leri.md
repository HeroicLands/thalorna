## Overview

Iugu-Leri is one of the scores of villages ringing [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] within [[place-galezkaraslt|Galezkara Selat]], half a day's barge from the capital's wharves. It renders grain, beer and labour up to the city and its temples, and takes back the custom of a market that never quite closes. A household here counts its year by both harvests — its own, and the city's.
