"Do not ask which office holds your record," a clerk of the bureaus tells a petitioner at the steps. "Ask which clerk can put it in your hand."

**Gar-Medjatozo** (the **House of Records**) is the bureau district of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]]: the treasuries, the council-hall of the [[affiliation-genzetpalu|Genzet'Palu]], the great scribal schools, and the archives where the empire's paperwork lives. The ministry of [[affiliation-garzeglar|Gar-Zeglar]] runs the civil administration of the city and the empire from its bureaus here. The district exists because an empire this old generates more record than any single office can hold, and someone has to be able to find a given sheet again. Its oldest archives of months date from the age when the king-list began to give a reign's months and the bureaus took up the people's hand ([[lore-akhcountmon|the Count of Months]]). A dispute anywhere in [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Aû'Khelâthu]] is settled by whichever clerk here can produce the right document first.
