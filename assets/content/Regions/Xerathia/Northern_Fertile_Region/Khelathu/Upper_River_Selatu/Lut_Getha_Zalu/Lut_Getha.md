## Overview

"The god shaped the world," a Lem'Nelgir (priest) of [[lore-psaqarudty|Psaq'âru]] tells an apprentice in the forecourt of a craft-temple, "and a smith does it again, small. Handle the metal accordingly."

**Lut-Getha** is the capital of the [[affiliation-selatltgthzl|Selat of Lut-Getha-Zalu]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]], who keeps his court and granaries beside the chief temple of [[affiliation-psaqaru|Faith of Psaq'âru]], the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the smith-towns and villages. About 36,000 people live here. The empire's smiths work steel as a matter of course, so it is bronze and gold that Lut-Getha is known for: the metal of ceremony.
