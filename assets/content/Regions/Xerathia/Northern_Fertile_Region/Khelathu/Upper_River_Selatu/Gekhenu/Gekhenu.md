## Overview

"Come up after the lamps are out," a library-temple astronomer says to a visiting scribe who has asked how the year is kept in **Gekhenu**. "The records are downstairs, and they are accurate. The sky is up here, and it is the thing the records are accurate about."

Gekhenu is the capital of the [[affiliation-selatgekhenu|Selat of Gekhenu]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]], a town of about 30,000 and the calendar-keeper of the middle river. It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-rethsaar|Faith of Reth'Sa'âr]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its library-temples and astronomers are the town's character, and they work under [[lore-rethsaardty|Reth'Sa'âr]], the ibis-headed scribe of the Eternal Library, whose domain includes the reckoning of time.
