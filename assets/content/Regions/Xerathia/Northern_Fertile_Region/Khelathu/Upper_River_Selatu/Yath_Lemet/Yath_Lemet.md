## Overview

Yath-Lemet is the smallest capital on the upper river, with about 6,000 people crowded between the cliff and the water. It is the seat of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]] of [[affiliation-selatyathlmt|the Selat of Yath-Lemet]], who keeps his court and granaries beside the chief temple of the [[affiliation-chelsur|Faith of Chelsur]], the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls and the markets that serve the gorge's few villages. The crocodile is sacred to the god and is also the beast that takes farmers and children along the river, and the temple teaches both facts together. When a beast takes someone, a culler licensed by [[affiliation-garnuw|Gar'Nuw]] answers, and the temple treats the cull as a religious act before it is a commercial one.
