## Overview

**Yath-Khelunu** lies within easy reach of [[place-khelunu|Khelunu]], on the farmland of [[place-garrethsaarnome|Gar-Reth'Sa'âr]]. Families who arrive with pupils bound for the great temple-schools lodge here, and the village draws most of its trade from that traffic. A house with a spare room is a house with an income, whether or not its own fields do well that year.
