## Overview

Yath-Khelunu farms the land of [[place-garrethsaarnome|Gar-Reth'Sa'âr]] within easy reach of [[place-khelunu|Khelunu]]. It lodges the families that come with pupils bound for the great temple-schools, and draws most of its trade from exactly that traffic. A house with a spare room here is a house with an income, whether or not its own fields do well that year.
