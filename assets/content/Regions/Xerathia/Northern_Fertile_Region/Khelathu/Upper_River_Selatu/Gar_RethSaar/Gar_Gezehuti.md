## Overview

Gar-Gezehuti stands among the fields of [[place-garrethsaarnome|Gar-Reth'Sa'âr]], grown prosperous on two trades that feed each other. It farms its own grain-land and lodges the families of pupils bound for the lesser temple-schools that in turn feed the great one at [[place-khelunu|Khelunu]]. A lean year for lodgers is still a good one for the village, because a school sends its hardest cases here before it sends them home.
