## Overview

"The water doesn't care whose dynasty built the road," a farmer of [[place-maguathen|Magu-Thelqen]] says, leaning on his hoe at the edge of a canal that runs straight for a mile beside an empty avenue.

Magu-Thelqen Selat is the land of [[affiliation-selatmaguthn|the Selat of Magu-Thelqen]], one of [[affiliation-upperrivrslt|the Upper River Selatu]] of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]], and lies within the [[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu Region]].

## Character

The land is ordinary valley country with an extraordinary town at its center. Flood-silt fields, canals and villages fill the strip between the river and the dry uplands, with high desert and chaparral beyond, and over it all stands a planned temple-city, built by one zealous dynasty and left half-abandoned. The farmers work their fields and keep the estates running while the city stands half empty. The city is the work of the house of [Thelqen'Relu]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, the house of the sun, and its half emptied when the temples refused the house's doctrine and the court returned to [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] ([[lore-sunhouse|the Sun-House at Magu-Thelqen]]).

## Economy

The selat runs on the flood, the harvest and the render like every [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelathi]] selat: grain to the granaries, labor to the canals and the works of the flood season, and a share of everything to the temples and the crown. The temple-city is still inhabited, and its chief temple of [[affiliation-uqaa|Uqa'a]] still holds its estates.

## Notable Features

- Magu-Thelqen—the selat capital and the Halzi'a's seat
- The chief temple of the Faith of Uqa'a and its estates
- The planned temple-city, half-abandoned and still inhabited
