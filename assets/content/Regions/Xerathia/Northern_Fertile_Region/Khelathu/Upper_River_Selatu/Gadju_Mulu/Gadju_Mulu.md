## Overview

Gadju-Mûlu is the capital of the [[affiliation-selatgadjuml|Selat of Gadju-Mûlu]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]. It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-thubai|Faith of Thubâ'i]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the selat's: "Green Waters"—marshy floodbasin, flax and waterfowl, the wettest of the upper selatu.
