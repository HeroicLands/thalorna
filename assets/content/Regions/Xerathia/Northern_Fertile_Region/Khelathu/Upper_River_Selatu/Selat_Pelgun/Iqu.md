## Overview

Iqu is the capital of the [[affiliation-selatsltplgn|Selat of Selat-Pelgun]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]. It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-pelgun|Faith of Pelgun]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the selat's: caravan-head for the eastern desert roads; harvest-festivals and virility cults.
