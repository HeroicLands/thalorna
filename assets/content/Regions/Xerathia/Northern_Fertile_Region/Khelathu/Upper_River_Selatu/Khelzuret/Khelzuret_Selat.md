## Overview

Khelzuret Selat is the land of [[affiliation-selatkhelzrt|The Selat of Khelzuret]], one of the [[affiliation-upperrivrslt|The Upper River Selatu]] of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Aû'Khelâthu]], and lies within the [[place-aukhelathrgq|Aû'Khelâthu Region]].

Built around the temple-city of [[place-khelzuret|Khelzuret]] on the river's east bank and the vast necropolis that faces it across the water on the west, the selat is the beating heart of the empire's funerary civilization: its embalmers, its tomb-builders, its funerary artists, and the priesthoods of [[affiliation-khelathpnthn|Khelâthi Pantheon]] and [[affiliation-khelathpnthn|Khelâthi Pantheon]], lords of the passage into death.

## Economy

The mortuary industry is the economy. Embalming and mummification, tomb construction and decoration, the manufacture of coffins, grave-goods, amulets, and funerary papyri, and the temple-pilgrimage trade together employ the overwhelming majority of the selat's people and draw wealth in from every corner of the empire—for the dead of distant selatu are sent here for proper preparation, and the rich of the whole realm pay to be buried, or at least commemorated, in sacred ground. Around this core the selat farms enough flood-silt grain to feed itself, quarries the stone its tomb-builders need from the western cliffs, and runs the natron-and-resin trades the embalmers depend on. It is a selat that exports the one service no one can finally do without.

## Notable Features

- The temple-city of [[place-khelzuret|Khelzuret]] and its great temples of Wethûr and Hezmuîri
- The western-bank necropolis—the empire's most sacred burial ground
- The embalmers' workshops and the tomb-builders' settlements of the west bank
- The natron flats and resin-stores that supply the mummification trade
- The pilgrim-road and the festival of the dead that draws the whole empire

## Settlements

- [[place-khelzuret|Khelzuret]] (~90,000)—the selat capital and the empire's great temple-city of the dead; the mortuary temples, the pilgrim-markets, the embalmers' and coffin-makers' quarters, and the seat of the Halzi'a in uneasy proximity to the Thâz'Lekhau.
- [[place-zelqar|Zel-Qar]] (~6,000)—"the Place of Truth," the walled tomb-workers' settlement on the west bank; a closed company-town of the master-craftsmen, draftsmen, and stonecutters who cut and paint the great tombs, hereditary in their trades and proud of their secrets.
- **The pilgrim and farm villages:** small communities of 500–2,500 line the east bank and the pilgrim-road—among them Gar-Zekri, Lut-Anpuqa, Yath-Mulu, and Zma-Gezer—lodging pilgrims, farming the river-margin, and serving the endless traffic of the dead and their mourners.
