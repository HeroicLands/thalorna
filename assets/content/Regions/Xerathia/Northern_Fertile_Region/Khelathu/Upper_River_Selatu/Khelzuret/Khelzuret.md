## Overview

Khelzuret is the great temple-city of the dead, capital of the [[affiliation-selatkhelzrt|Selat of Khelzuret]] and the heart of the empire's funerary civilization. It stands on the east bank of the river facing, across the water, the vast necropolis where the rich and the holy of the whole realm are laid to rest; and the city itself lives entirely for that traffic. Its great temples of [[lore-wethurdty|Wethûr]] and [[lore-hezmuiridty|Hezmuîri]], lords of the passage into death, draw the empire's largest pilgrimage; its embalmers' and coffin-makers' quarters prepare the dead sent here from distant selatu; and its pilgrim-markets sell the amulets, funerary papyri, and grave-goods that ease the soul's journey. The Thâz'Lekhau of the mortuary temples command estates and labor-gangs rivaling the [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]'s own, and the city's solemn, ancient streets are crowded year-round with mourners, pilgrims, priests, and the craftsmen of death.

## Notable Features

- The great temples of Wethûr and Hezmuîri, lords of the dead
- The pilgrim-markets and the trade in amulets, funerary papyri, and grave-goods
- The embalmers' and coffin-makers' quarters
- The ferry-crossing to the western necropolis
- The seat of the Halzi'a, sharing the city uneasily with the mortuary priesthoods
