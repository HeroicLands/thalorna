## Overview

"Seventy days," an embalmer of **Khelzuret** tells a family that has brought its father upriver by barge. "That is the first grade: the body drawn, dried in natron, anointed with resin and wrapped in fine linen with an amulet at every joint. The second is a shorter course in cheaper oils. The third is a wash, a wrapping and a prayer. I name all three at the door because you are going to ask what the first one buys. It buys the first. The **Weighing** takes no payment."

Khelzuret is the empire's great temple-city of the dead and the capital of the [[affiliation-selatkhelzrt|Selat of Khelzuret]], and some 90,000 people live in it. It stands on the east bank, facing across the water the vast necropolis where the dead are laid, and the whole city lives on that traffic. The great temples of [[lore-wethurdty|Wethûr]] and [[lore-hezmuiridty|Hezmuîri]], lords of the passage into death, draw the empire's largest pilgrimage. The embalmers' and coffin-makers' quarters prepare the dead sent here from distant selatu, and the pilgrim-markets sell the amulets, funerary papyri and grave-goods that go across the river with them. The Thâz'Lekhau (high priests) of the mortuary temples command estates and labor-gangs to rival the [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]'s own, and the Halzi'a keeps his seat in the same city, sharing it with them uneasily. The streets are old and solemn, and mourners, pilgrims, priests and the craftsmen of death crowd them all year.

## Notable Features

- The great temples of Wethûr and Hezmuîri, lords of the dead
- The pilgrim-markets and the trade in amulets, funerary papyri, and grave-goods
- The embalmers' and coffin-makers' quarters
- The ferry-crossing to the western necropolis
- The seat of the Halzi'a, sharing the city uneasily with the mortuary priesthoods
