## Overview

**Khelenet-Zu** is where the selat comes to argue. Farmers with a disputed furrow, stewards with a tenancy question and heirs who cannot agree on a share all end up here, because the town holds the old assize-courts, the chief temple of [[affiliation-qearet|Faith of Qe'âret]], and the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls and the survey records every judgment is measured against. It is the capital of the [[affiliation-selatkhelntz|Selat of Khelenet-Zu]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]], who keeps his court and granaries here. About 32,000 people live in its streets, and its markets feed the litigants and the villages around them.

If you come on business, find a scribe before you find an inn. The bureau holds the rolls, and little said in the courts outweighs what is written there.
