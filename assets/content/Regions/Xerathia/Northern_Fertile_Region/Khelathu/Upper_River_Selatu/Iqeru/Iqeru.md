## Overview

"We do not ask whether the sun will rise," a priest at the temple of [[lore-uqaadty|Uqa'a]] says to a pilgrim in Iqeru. "Every night he goes down into the underworld and fights [[lore-azuathisdty|Azu'athis]], the Devouring Shadow, and every morning he has won. We ask what we owe him for it."

Iqeru is the capital of [[affiliation-selatiqeru|The Selat of Iqeru]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]], a town of about 33,000 among irrigated wheatland. It holds the selat's chief temple of the [[affiliation-uqaa|Faith of Uqa'a]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the selat's own: sun-temples and wide irrigated wheatland.

Uqa'a's demand on a life is a hard one, clarity, purpose and righteous intensity, and his cult holds the throne itself, because the [[lore-garauu|Gar-Au]] is understood as his earthly manifestation. The [Great Temple of Uqa'a]{.name data-type="place/structure"} stands on [[place-yathtelgu|Yath-Telgu]] at the capital; Iqeru keeps the selat's own chief temple.

The wheat outside the town is the [Field at Iqeru]{.name data-type="place/site"}, where the war between [[place-lenennezut|Lenen-Nezut]] and [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] ended in 1531 ST (580 BF) with the death of the Gar-Au [Zab'el'Psaqa III]{.name data-type="being/npc"} ([[lore-twothrones|The War of the Two Thrones]]). It is an ordinary battlefield: the dead were carried home to their tombs and rites, and the field is farmed like any other.
