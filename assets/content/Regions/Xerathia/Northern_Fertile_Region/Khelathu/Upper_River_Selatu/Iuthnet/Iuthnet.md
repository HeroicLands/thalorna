## Overview

"Say what you came for, whether it is a child, a cure or only a blessing," a Wazu of the temple of [[lore-uzneradty|Uznêra]] tells a pilgrim at the door in **Iuthnet**, "and the first priestess you meet will know where to send you."

Iuthnet is the capital of the [[affiliation-selatiuthnet|Selat of Iuthnet]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]], a town of about 42,000. It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-uznera|Faith of Uznêra]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the selat's own: healing-temples and birth-shrines, the great cult of the mother-goddess.

Uznêra's priestesses practice medicine and hold office up to the highest. Behind them stands the doctrine that creation needs the balanced partnership of a masculine and a feminine divine principle, which is why [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelâthi]] women own property, initiate divorce and plead in the courts.
