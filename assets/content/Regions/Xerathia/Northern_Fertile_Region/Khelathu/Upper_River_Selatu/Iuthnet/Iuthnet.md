## Overview

Iuthnet is the capital of the [[affiliation-selatiuthnet|Selat of Iuthnet]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]. It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-uznera|Faith of Uznêra]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the selat's: healing-temples and birth-shrines; the great cult of the mother-goddess.
