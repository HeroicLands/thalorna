## Overview

Anlagh-Zetûn—"the living storehouse"—is the capital of the empire's [[affiliation-selatnlghztn|Selat of Anlagh-Zetûn]], a substantial inland city set among the richest wheatland in the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Aû'Khelâthu]]. It is a city built around grain: its skyline is a forest of granary-domes, its markets trade in wheat and barley by the boatload, and its busiest halls are the survey-courts and tally-bureaus where the flood-fields are measured and the harvests assessed. Here the [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]] holds court—a conservative, land-proud lord whose power rests on the bread he controls—and the great temple of [[lore-qearetdty|Qe'âret]], goddess of order and right measure, presides fittingly over a city whose whole life is survey, tally, and the honest filling of stores. It is not a glamorous place, but it is one of the most important in the empire: when the harvest fails elsewhere, it is Anlagh-Zetûn's granaries that decide who eats.

## Notable Features

- The great granaries that store the empire's reserve against famine
- The grain-markets and the survey-and-assize courts that measure the land
- The temple of Qe'âret, goddess of order and right measure
- The seat of the granary-Halzi'a, one of the empire's quiet kingmakers
