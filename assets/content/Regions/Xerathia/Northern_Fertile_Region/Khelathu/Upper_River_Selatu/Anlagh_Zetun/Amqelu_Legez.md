## Overview

Amqelu-Legez is the river-port of the [[affiliation-selatnlghztn|Selat of Anlagh-Zetûn]], the quayside town where the wheat of the great flood-basin is gathered, measured, and loaded for the journey downriver. Its long grain-wharves are the throat through which [[place-anlaghzetun|Anlagh-Zetûn]]'s harvest reaches the capital and the delta: barge after barge loads here through the harvest season, weighed and sealed by the tally-scribes, while the granary-keepers and grain-factors haggle on the docks. It is the busiest and most worldly place in an otherwise inward-looking selat—a town of boatmen, porters, measurers, and traders, where the river-traffic brings news and strangers that the farm-villages never see. Where the rest of the selat lives by the field, Amqelu-Legez lives by the river and the barge.
