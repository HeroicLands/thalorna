## Overview

**Yath-Thawu** stands in the dense farm-country of [[place-anlaghztnslt|Anlagh-Zetûn Selat]], the country that makes the selat one of the empire's granaries. Its year is the flood's year: planted the day the water begins to fall and reckoned by nothing else. A household here can name the day of the retreat going back further than it can name its own founding.
