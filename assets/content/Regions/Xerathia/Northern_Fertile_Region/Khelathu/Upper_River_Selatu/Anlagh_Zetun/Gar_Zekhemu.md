## Overview

**Gar-Zekhemu** is a grain-village of 160 people in the flood-basin country of [[place-anlaghztnslt|Anlagh-Zetûn Selat]]. Its fields share the annual inundation that feeds the selat's wheatlands, and its harvest goes up the chain to the granaries and the Halzi'a. The village lives by the water's rise and retreat and by the measure of grain it can render after the harvest.

It shares its name with [[place-garzekhemu|Gar-Zekhemu in Galezkara Selat]], and the clerks who enter consignments have learned to write which of the two sent each one. "Put the selat after the name, every time," a senior scribe tells a new hand, "or the tally credits the wrong granary." The joke of the two villages has outlasted several generations of scribes.
