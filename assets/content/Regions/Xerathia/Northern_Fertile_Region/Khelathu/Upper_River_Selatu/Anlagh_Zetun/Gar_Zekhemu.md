## Overview

Gar-Zekhemu is a grain-village of 160 people in the flood-basin country of [[place-anlaghztnslt|Anlagh-Zetûn Selat]]. Its fields share the annual inundation that sustains the selat's wheatlands, and its harvest goes up the chain to the granaries and the Halzi'a. The village lives by the water's rise and retreat and by the measure of grain it can render after the harvest.

It shares its name with a distinct [[place-garzekhemu|Gar-Zekhemu in Galezkara Selat]]. A scribe entering a consignment has to write which Gar-Zekhemu sent it, and the joke of the two has outlasted several generations of scribes.
