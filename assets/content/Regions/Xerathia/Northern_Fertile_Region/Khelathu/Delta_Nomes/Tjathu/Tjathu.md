## Overview

Tjathu is the capital of [[affiliation-selattjathu|The Selat of Tjathu]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]. It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-wethur|Faith of Wethûr]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the selat's: the sea-margin; salt-pans, natron flats, and the curing of the lesser villages' catch.
