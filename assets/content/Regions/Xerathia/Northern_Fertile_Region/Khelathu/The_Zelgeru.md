## Overview

The Zelgeru names the great temple-estates scattered through [[place-aukhelathrgq|Aû'Khelâthu Region]], each with its own granaries, workshops and tenant-villages. They live by the same grain and labour as any estate, but hold it as religious foundations rather than as a lord's demesne, which is what lets their combined wealth rival the [[lore-garauu|Gar-Aû]]'s own palace. A tenant-village bound to one of the Zelgeru answers to its temple before it answers to any secular official, and the distinction matters more than it might sound.

## Character

The Zelgeru holds no single tract of ground: its estates lie wherever the empire's irrigated grain-land will support a temple's granaries, from the delta marshes to the upper-river basins, each estate carved out of whichever selat surrounds it.
