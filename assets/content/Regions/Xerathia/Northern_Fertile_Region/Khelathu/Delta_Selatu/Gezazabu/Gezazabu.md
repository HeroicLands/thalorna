## Overview

"You will smell the curing-houses long before you see the harbor," says a carter who loads at them, hauling a strap tight over a stack of salted barrels. "By the second day you stop noticing, and then you are one of us." The town is the capital of the [[affiliation-selatgezazab|Selat of Gezazabu]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]], and its roughly 56,000 people live around a western delta port, its salt-fish and curing-houses, and the trade road to **Bethûa**.

It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-thubai|Thubâ'i]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns.
