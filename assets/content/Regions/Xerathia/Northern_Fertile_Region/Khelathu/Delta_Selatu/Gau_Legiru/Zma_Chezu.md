## Overview

Zma-Chezu is one of the orchard villages of the [[place-gaulegirunome|Gau-Legiru]] garden strip. It lives by its groves and its press like its neighbors along the strip, and sends on whatever the household does not keep for itself. A bad frost here costs a year of oil before it costs anything else.
