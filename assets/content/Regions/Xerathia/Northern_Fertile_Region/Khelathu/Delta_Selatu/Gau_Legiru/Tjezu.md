## Overview

"Taste it before you talk price," a Tjezu vintner tells a factor from the export houses, and pours the second cup before the first is finished. **Tjezu** is a coastal wine-town of about 12,000 among the western vineyards of the [[affiliation-selatgaulegr|Selat of Gau-Legiru]], and it is famous out of all proportion to its size for what comes out of its cellars.

Its presses and aging-cellars produce the most prized vintages in the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Aû'Khelâthu]], wines that grace the Gar-Aû's table and travel, sealed and named, to the markets of [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión Region]]. The town's little harbor ships them straight down the coast to [[place-garanlaghet|Gar-Anlaghet]] and the export trade, and the dock is never far from a cooper's yard.

The year turns on the vintage, and a good or bad season is felt in every household. Vintners, coopers and merchant-factors live here, prosperous and proud. The labels are jealously guarded, the best slopes are worth a fortune, and the wine-merchants reckon themselves a cut above ordinary orchard-folk.
