## Overview

Tjezu is a coastal wine-town among the western vineyards of the [[affiliation-selatgaulegr|Selat of Gau-Legiru]], famous out of all proportion to its size for what comes out of its cellars. Its presses and aging-cellars produce the most prized vintages in the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Aû'Khelâthu]]—wines that grace the Gar-Aû's table and travel, sealed and named, to the markets of [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión Region]]—and its little harbor ships them straight down the coast to [[place-garanlaghet|Gar-Anlaghet]] and the export trade. The town is a place of vintners, coopers, and merchant-factors, prosperous and proud, where the year turns on the vintage and a good or bad season is felt in every household. Its labels are jealously guarded, its best slopes worth a fortune, and its wine-merchants reckon themselves a cut above ordinary orchard-folk.
