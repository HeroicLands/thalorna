## Overview

**Gar-Shelu** is one of the orchard communities of the [[place-gaulegirunome|Gau-Legiru]] garden strip, a few hundred to a couple of thousand people tending grove and vine. Its fruit and oil go out through the selat's little harbors, because the water route is shorter and kinder to a cargo that bruises. A poor crush here is a poor year for every harbor it would otherwise have supplied.
