## Overview

"The first thing to learn is that the port is only the mouth," says a net-boat ferryman, backing his pole off a mudbank in the gray before sunrise. "Behind it, the water goes on for a day's poling in every direction, and most of what the city eats and drinks and loads comes through channels you cannot see from the quay."

**Gar-Anlaghet Selat** is the land of the [[affiliation-selatgrnlght|Selat of Gar-Anlaghet]], the first country of the [[affiliation-deltaselatu|Delta Selatu]] in the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Aû'Khelâthu]], and it lies within the [[place-aukhelathrgq|Aû'Khelâthu Region]]. It is built around the harbor-city of [[place-garanlaghet|Gar-Anlaghet]] on the westernmost and deepest of the river's mouths, and it handles the lion's share of the empire's seaborne commerce.

## Character

If the delta is the empire's purse, Gar-Anlaghet is the mouth of it. The selat is a country of quays and counting-houses, foreign quarters and bonded warehouses, a hundred languages on the dockside and a fortune changing hands every hour. It grows hardly any of its own bread and mints comparatively little of its own coin; its genius is moving other people's goods and taking a fee on every transaction.

Outward from the port go grain, papyrus, linen, gold and the empire's artisan work. Inward come timber, metals, spices, wine and the luxury goods of [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión Region]]. Beyond the quays, the marshes yield fish, fowl, salt and papyrus, and the selat's share of the garden coast grows wine and oil for export. West along the garden strip lie the villas of the merchant-princes, and the [[affiliation-garhalzi|Gár-Hálzi]] banking-houses and bonded warehouses of the waterfront keep the accounts of all of it.

## Settlements

- [[place-garanlaghet|Gar-Anlaghet]] (~120,000)—the selat capital and the empire's first port: the great harbor, the foreign quarters, the customs-house, and the counting-houses of the richest merchants in the realm.
- [[place-megitgar|Megit-Gar]] (~14,000)—a fishing-and-victualing town on a northern channel that supplies the great port with fish and crews and handles the lesser coasting-trade the big harbor cannot be bothered with.
- **The channel villages:** a scatter of small fishing and ferry communities of 500–2,000 thread the marsh-channels around the port, among them Tjautha-Megit, Gar-Irezu, Zma-Belgen and Gadju-Zetji, living by their boats, their nets and the custom of the great city.
