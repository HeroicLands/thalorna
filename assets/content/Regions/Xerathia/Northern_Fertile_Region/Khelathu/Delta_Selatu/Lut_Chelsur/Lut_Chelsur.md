## Overview

"You will have noticed there are no children playing at the water's edge," a priest of [[affiliation-chelsur|Chelsur]] says to a visitor on the temple steps. "That is the god's doing and the god's gift, and we have never agreed on which." Lut-Chelsur is the capital of [[affiliation-selatltchlsr|the Selat of Lut-Chelsur]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]], a town of about 38,000 in the eastern marsh.

It holds the selat's chief temple of Chelsur, the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. The town's life turns on the great crocodile cult and on the river-beast hunters who work the marsh around it. The priests of the god disagree whether the crocodile is the god or his creature, and the answer decides whether a cull is a sacrilege that needs a dispensation or a service that needs only a rite.
