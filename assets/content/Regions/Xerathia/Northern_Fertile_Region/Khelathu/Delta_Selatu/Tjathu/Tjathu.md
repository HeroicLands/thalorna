## Overview

"We dry the dead here, in a manner of speaking," says a priest of [[affiliation-wethur|Wethûr]] to a visitor who has asked about the white flats at the edge of town. "The natron is ours, and the embalmers of the whole empire buy it." **Tjathu** is the capital of the [[affiliation-selattjathu|Selat of Tjathu]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]], a town of about 41,000 on the sea-margin of the delta.

It holds the selat's chief temple of Wethûr, the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the selat's: salt-pans, natron flats, and the curing of the lesser villages' catch.

The selat's coast is where the sea-raiders landed in 1960 ST (151 BF); the port they burned lies in the reeds as [[place-zelamqelu|Zel-Amqelu]], and the town's customs patrol still watches that shore ([[lore-searaiders|the Sea-Raiders' Throne]]).
