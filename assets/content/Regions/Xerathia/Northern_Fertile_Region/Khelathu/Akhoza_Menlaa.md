Akhoza-Menlaa is a circle's hidden work rather than a place a map could show — its site is kept from every record the [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelâthi]] keep, by the plain choice of the people who use it. What it lives by is experiment on the dead and on the spirit-realm, against everything the empire holds about the balance between the two. Whoever finds it without being shown is marked for the embalmer's table, which is the circle's whole answer to the question of discovery.
