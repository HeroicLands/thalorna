"Read it aloud," an astrologer of the temple libraries tells a student kneeling at the foot of the monolith, and the student can, sign by sign. "Now tell me what it says." That is the whole lesson of **Anlaghura**.

The stone stands alone in the middle of the Târga River Valley and is older than anyone who reads it. Fragments of a lost prophecy are cut into its faces in runes the empire's astrologers can transliterate and cannot entirely construe, because the runes are older than the empire's reading of them. What they have construed points elsewhere: to a shrine in the highlands, and from there further still.

The astrologers hold the monolith to be the first stone of a road that ends in the sands of [[place-lareshu|Lareshu]], at [[place-lutzelutha|Lut-Zelutha]].
