An ancient monolithic stone standing in the middle of the Târga River Valley, older than anyone who now reads it. Fragments of a lost prophecy are cut into its faces in runes the empire's astrologers can transliterate and not entirely construe, and what they have construed points elsewhere — to a shrine in the highlands, and from there further still.
