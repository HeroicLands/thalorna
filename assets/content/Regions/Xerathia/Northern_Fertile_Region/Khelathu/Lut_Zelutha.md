An ancient sanctuary hidden deep within the sands of [[place-lareshu|Lareshu]], and the place a certain prophecy points to. What is kept there is supposed to be the last of a sequence of clues: the thing that says whether what the stars foretold can be stopped, or only survived. Nobody who claims to have reached it has come back able to say which.
