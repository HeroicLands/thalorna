## Overview

**Balehen** (about 24,000 people) is the strongest fortress the empire holds south of its capital and its gateway to the lands beyond. It is the great fortress-capital of the [[affiliation-selatzuzllgl|Selat of Zu-Zalu-Legulu]], a double-walled stronghold commanding the river at the first cataract, where the water breaks into rapids and the green valley fails.

"Stand on the wall at dawn and you can see the whole bargain," a priest of [[lore-qeztudty|Qeztu]] tells a soldier newly posted to the garrison. "Below you is the river, loud with the cataract. Behind you is the last of the green. In front of you is a country the Gar-Aû can count on only as far as this wall. Everything that goes between here and there goes under it."

What passes under it is the gold, ivory, ebony and slaves of the southern trade, taxed and escorted through the toll-house and the tribute-store, and the embassies and war-parties of the southern tribes, watched from the battlements. Inside the walls stand the garrison barracks of the frontier army, the seat of the warden-[[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]] who holds the south, and the war-temple of Qeztu, whose cult is stronger here than anywhere in the empire. Balehen is a hard, soldierly, mixed Khelâthi-and-southern town, and the empire's writ here is exactly as strong as its garrison.

## Notable Features

- The double-walled fortress commanding the first cataract
- The toll-house and tribute-store of the southern gold-and-ivory trade
- The war-temple of Qeztu, the war-god
- The garrison barracks and the seat of the warden-Halzi'a
