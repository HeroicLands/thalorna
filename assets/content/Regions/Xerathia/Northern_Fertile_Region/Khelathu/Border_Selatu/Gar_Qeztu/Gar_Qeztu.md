## Overview

Gar-Qeztu is the capital of the [[affiliation-selatgarqezt|Selat of Gar-Qeztu]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empireakhlth#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]. It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-qeztu|Faith of Qeztu]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the selat's: A garrison heartland; the chariot-corps drill-grounds and officer schools.
