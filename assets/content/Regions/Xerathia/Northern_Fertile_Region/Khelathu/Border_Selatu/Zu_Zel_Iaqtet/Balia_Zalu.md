Balia-Zalu stands in the copper country of [[place-zuzeliaqtetnome|Zu-Zel-Iaqtet]], a camp of miners and smelters clustered around its own workings and its guard-fort. It lives entirely by the ore, with nothing grown nearby to feed it — every sack of grain and jar of water comes in by caravan from the river. A camp that eats only what the caravan brings stops working within days of a caravan that does not arrive.
