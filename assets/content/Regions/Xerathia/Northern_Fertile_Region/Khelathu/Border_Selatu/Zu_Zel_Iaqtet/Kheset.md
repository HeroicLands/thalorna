## Overview

Kheset is the convict-station of [[place-zuzeliaqtetnome|Zu-Zel-Iaqtet]], one of the mining and quarry camps scattered through the selat's hills and desert. Like its neighbors—[[place-baliazalu|Balia-Zalu]] at the copper diggings, [[place-melgat|Melgat]] at the turquoise camps, [[place-inululedju|Inulu-Ledju]] at the white-stone quarry—it clusters around its workings and a guard-fort, and it drinks what the water-caravans bring up from the river.

## A Station of the Condemned

What sets Kheset apart is who does the work. The other camps are worked by miners and quarrymen; Kheset's labor is convicts. The quarry-roads that link the camps are guarded by forts and water-stations, and Kheset depends on that guard more than any of its neighbors: a convict who walks away from the station walks into a desert where the only water is at the stations the soldiers hold.
