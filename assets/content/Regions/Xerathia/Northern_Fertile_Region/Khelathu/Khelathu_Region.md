## Overview

Aû'Khelâthu is a single river and the land it waters: the [[place-zumeleshrvr|Zumélesh]] runs north out of the southern uplands for hundreds of leagues, through desert, to a delta on the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]], and nineteen million people live within reach of its water. Everyone in the [[place-nrthrnfrtlrgn|Northern Fertile Region]] knows the country by its flood. Once a year the river rises over the fields and goes down again leaving black silt behind, and that silt is why the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Aû'Khelâthu]] can feed more people on less ground than anywhere else in the known world.

"Stand on the bank and look west," a river pilot tells a passenger on the first morning above the delta. "Green for a few miles, then nothing. The line where the black soil stops is where the country stops. Everything you want is between the two lines, and so is everybody else."

The river makes Aû'Khelâthu the exception in its region. [[place-bethuargn|Bethûa]] and [[place-okharisrgn|Okháris]] draw their harvests from aqueducts and herds, and their ground is fertile only where somebody has engineered it to be. The Khelâthi valley is fed by the river itself.

## Character

The country changes three times between the sea and the cataracts, and a traveler going upriver meets each change in turn.

The **delta** shares the wet-coastal character of the whole northern shore: sea-tempered winters, moderate summers, and the orchard crops that go with them—citrus, olive, grape, fig and date. Its channels, marshes and silt-islands hold the ports, the papyrus beds and the salt-pans, and it is the part of Aû'Khelâthu a foreigner usually sees first.

Upstream, the **valley** is almost tropical. Its water is warm enough that hippopotami and crocodiles thrive in it and kill farmers every year, and on a summer evening the air on the bank feels nothing like the dry country a day's ride to either side.

Beyond the flood's reach is **desert**. West of the river, high desert and chaparral run out toward the Bethûan frontier and give way to sand dunes nearer the valley; east, the land rises into rugged, mineral-rich hills before it runs out into the semi-desert of [[place-khuqetmiglet|Khuqet-Miglet]] and the Dunhari sand seas. Canal and cistern carry water a little way into this country, and herds graze its margins; nothing else lives there by choice. The deep sands of [[place-lareshu|Lareshu]] lie out beyond the settled country altogether.

## Peoples and Culture

The region is [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelâthi]]: urban, literate and hierarchical, governed by a vast scribal bureaucracy beneath the divine office of the [[lore-garauu|Gar-Aû]], and organized around the temples of the [[affiliation-khelathpnthn|Khelâthi Pantheon]]. A visitor learns quickly that a Khelâthi asks two things of a stranger before anything else—whose house speaks for him, and which of the two hands he was taught to write.

Along the western margins, herding peoples graze the dry country under arrangements with the Khelâthi Halzi'a (provincial governors), of the kind found on the edges of the region's other realms.

## Politics and Power

The region is the heartland of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Aû'Khelâthu]] and is administered through its thirty-nine selatu (provinces), grouped as the [[affiliation-deltaselatu|Delta Selatu]], the [[affiliation-upperrivrslt|Upper River Selatu]], the [[affiliation-borderselatu|Border Selatu]] and the [[affiliation-capitalselat|Capital Selat]] around [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]]. The frontier with Bethûa to the west has never been precisely drawn, and the occasional friction along it is the region's most persistent quarrel with a neighbor.
