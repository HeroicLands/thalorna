A harbor clerk of the delta lays the beam across the quay and says what every clerk before him has said: "Nobody's cargo has ever been worth what he swore it was until the beam said so." The rule behind the sentence is the age of the house that made it.

## The Third Reign Ends

In 1990 ST (121 BF) the third Gar-Aû of the sea-raider house died. The list enters the year and gives no formula, and the priesthood did what it had done thirty years before: it chose a house. **Gezer'Quru** was raised at Galezkara, and its first Gar-Aû took the throne name **Gez'Uzner I**.

The raiders' line was not hunted down. The Khelâthi way with a conqueror's house is to close it and wait, and a generation or two after, its people were Khelâthi ([[lore-searaiders|the Sea-Raiders' Throne]]). What the new Gar-Aû wanted was not revenge but a delta that could not be landed on twice.

## The Weigh-House Rule

The raiders had come in over the mouths of the river with nothing between them and the capital. Gez'Uzner I closed the mouths with paper first. He ruled that every cargo reaching the delta by water was to be landed at a quay, weighed at a weigh-house and taxed before it moved, and that a cargo not yet weighed was contraband for as long as it stayed unweighed. A ship that unloaded elsewhere was a smuggler or a raider, and the same patrol dealt with both ([[place-amqelmiglet|Amqel-Miglet]]).

The rule is why the delta quays are a single business. The beam is the empire's first line against a landing: anything that comes ashore without being counted can be stopped without proving a motive.

## The Navy That Stays Home

Gez'Uzner I built a navy to enforce the rule, and gave it one task. The ships guard the river's mouths and the coast of the delta selatu, and they return to the quays at dusk. The apex of the delta, where the river first divides, gained its forts and customs-stations in the same reign, so that a force coming up past the mouths would meet a second line.

The navy grew large, and it stayed what the first reign made it. The [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]] is contested by older fleets, and the Khelâthi navy is among the largest of them and the least likely to leave its own shore ([[affiliation-bethuanflt|Bethuan Fleet]]). The empire leaves deep water to others, and has since the first reign of this house.

## What It Left

- **The weigh-house rule.** An unweighed cargo is contraband, and the quays are built to it.
- **A homebound navy.** Large, disciplined, and given one coast to keep.
- **The apex fortifications.** Forts and customs-stations where the river divides, held for the throne and not for any selat.
- **A closed house.** The list enters Malu'Thenoru as a house like any other and closes it like any other.
