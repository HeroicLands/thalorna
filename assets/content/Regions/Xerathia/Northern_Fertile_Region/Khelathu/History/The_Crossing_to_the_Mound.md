For the first thousand years of the count the kings were crowned on the mound and went home. In 1006 ST (1105 BF) a Gar-Aû stayed, and the valley has been ruled from the river-front of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] ever since.

## The Last of the Great Land

The First House and **Azlet'Qûtu** kept their court in [[place-zugernome|Zu-Ger]], the Great Land, and went downstream to the island of [[place-yathtelgu|Yath-Telgu]] to stand upon the mound at each crowning. The list gives their reigns as 150, 120 and 110 years and enters nothing else of them. The last reign of Azlet'Qûtu is **Lin'Uzner**'s twenty-six years, the first of a length a man can live, and the list enters his house as closed.

## The Crossing

The next house is **Zu'Amqeletu**, and its first Gar-Aû is **Anlagh'el'Qar I**. The list enters him by the formula for a crowning and no more. What he did is what the city shows.

He did not return to the Great Land. The court crossed to the mound and settled on the island where the first Gar-Aû had stood, beside the temple of [[lore-uqaadty|Uqa'â]]. He laid a road through them on the solstice line: from the eastern edge of the living city, across the water to the island, through the temple and out of its western face to the desert shelf ([[place-waluqaa|Wal-Uqa'â]], the Sun's Road). At midsummer dawn the sun comes up straight down the eastern road. At midwinter dusk it sets straight down the western one, into the ground that was about to become the city of the dead.

## The Law of the Sightline

He proclaimed that nothing may stand in the line between the temple and either horizon, and that anything built across it is pulled down. The law is cited in every dynasty's reign as older than the dynasty, and its first reign is the one that wrote it. No house since has been able to keep a building in the sightline, however great its owner.

## The Necropolis

The western shore became the burial-ground of the houses that ruled from the mound. Across the river, [[place-zugezer|Zu-Gezer]] opened with the first crossing-road, cut off the death-road at the head of the row nearest the water. By custom the Way of each later house lies farther toward the sunset, so the row reads from the oldest at the river outward. The first two houses were not moved. They lie in the Great Land, where the necropolis at Zu-Gezer does not count them.

## What It Left

- **The axis.** The capital hangs on the road, and the two festivals of the year, the Coming-Forth and the Going-Down, are held at its ends.
- **The sightline law.** A building that creeps into the line is pulled down without appeal.
- **The necropolis.** The row of Ways at Zu-Gezer is the list from 1006 ST with the First Tablets missing, and the order of the row is the order of the houses.
- **A split in the royal dead.** The two oldest houses lie in the Great Land, and the farmers there still plough round their marker stones ([[place-firsttablets|the Ground of the First Tablets]]).
- **The next age.** The Keepers date the Sun's Road from this year ([[lore-akhsunroad|the Sun's Road]]).
