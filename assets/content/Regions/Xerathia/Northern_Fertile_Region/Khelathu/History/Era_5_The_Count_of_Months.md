The **Count of Months** is the age in which the empire learns to write down how long things took. Before it, the king-list gives a reign in years and trusts the reader; from 1990 ST (121 BF) it gives the months beside the years, and a clerk who wants to know whether a flood was entered in the first reign or the second can finally find out.

## What Happened

The age opens where the sea-raiders' throne closes. The priesthood raises **Gezer'Quru**, and its first Gar-Aû, Gez'Uzner I, crowned 1990 ST (121 BF), is the first the list measures to the month ([[lore-seahouse|the Closing of the Sea House]]). The change is the Keepers' own doing and nothing a king ordered: the delta rule that no cargo travels upriver unweighed has taught the empire to count what it handles, and the scribes carry the habit to the throne.

The habit spreads through everything the scribes keep. About 2030 ST (81 BF) the queen **Gith'el'Retha I** of the house **Amqelu'Gatau** sanctions the bureaus' cursive, cut down from the seven hundred signs of the sacred hand to about a hundred ([[lore-peopleshand|the People's Hand]]). The counting-houses, the tax rolls and the land registers of [[place-garmedjatozo|Gar-Medjatozo]] are written in it from then on, and the oldest archives of months in the House of Records date from these reigns. Every land suit that reaches back past the struck house ends in them.

Two other changes pass with less notice. The temple-treasuries of the [[affiliation-garhalzi|Gár-Hálzi]] are older than reliable record already, and they take the new hand into their ledgers. Scholars cross between [[place-khelunu|Khelunu]] and the Helionite schools in both directions. And in 2111 ST, the year the Vylarians call 1 AF, a new empire begins counting across the sea ([[lore-thefounding|the Founding]]); the Keepers enter nothing of it. The Khelâthi notice it only as a conversion: the year in the western column is the count less 2,110, and the bureaus keep a clerk who can say it.

## Rulers

- **Gezer'Quru:** Gez'Uzner I, crowned 1990 ST (121 BF).
- **Amqelu'Gatau:** the queen Gith'el'Retha I, crowned 2025 ST (86 BF).
- The age holds fourteen houses in all. The Keepers give the rest as the list gives them: a house, a run of reigns, and the months of each.

## Its End

The age closes in 2281 ST (171 AF), when the priesthood crowns the house the necropolis calls [[affiliation-garzinelrelu|Gar-Zin'el'Rêlu]], the struck house. From that crowning the list gives every reign whole ([[lore-akhstruckhs|the Struck House]]).
