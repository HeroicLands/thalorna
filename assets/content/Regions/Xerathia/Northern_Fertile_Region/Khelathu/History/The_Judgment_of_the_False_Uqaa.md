"The heart of even a Gar-Aû is weighed," a priest of the Great Temple tells a boy who has asked why the road across the river is empty. "Go and look at it from the bridge. Then ask me again."

The road is the Way of the struck house at [[place-zugezer|Zu-Gezer]], and the **Judgment of the False Uqa'â** is how it came to be given to the god.

## The Crime

The charge was two crimes, and the temple sets them down in order. The first was blasphemy: the last Gar-Aû of [[affiliation-garzinelrelu|Gar-Zin'el'Rêlu]] had proclaimed himself [[lore-uqaadty|Uqa'â]] in the flesh, not in rite but in truth, and had silenced the priests who named it. The second was the hidden famine: he had falsified the Measure and killed the people who spoke the word _hunger_ ([[lore-failfloods|the Failing Floods]]). The old fiction of the throne says the Gar-Aû is divine and every Khelâthi knows nobody believes it. His offense was to remove the fiction by taking it at its word.

## The Judgment

The rising at Anlagh-Zetûn delivered him to the capital ([[lore-sealedgran|the Battle of the Sealed Granaries]]), and in 2378 ST (268 AF) the **Thâz'Lekhau** of Uqa'â, **Zinlegir,** judged him in the Great Temple on [[place-yathtelgu|Yath-Telgu]], the island where the god and the god-king were moored together. The high priest of Uqa'â has deposed or raised more Gar-Aûu than any army, and this judgment ended a house outright.

The sentence was given in the order of the crime. His wives and children were killed before his eyes. He was bound and embalmed living, which is a rite meant for the dead and was done to him alive, and sealed breathing into his tomb. He is the only Gar-Aû the Khelâthi say was sealed so. The sentence on the House was the other half. The name he reigned under and the name of his house were struck from every wall in the empire, the temples scraped them from the copies of the list and left the years, and the entry that stands where the house ends reads _the house is closed, and its name with it_. His Way at Zu-Gezer was given over to the god, pronounced cursed and left to ruin.

The next flood came full. The temple takes that as the answer, and the famine broke.

## What It Left

- **The cursed road.** The Way lies four crossing-roads back from the river-front, walled by dunes, with a warning stela at its mouth, and the necropolis guard kill anyone seen seeking it.
- **The struck name.** The formula _the name is struck; the years stand_ is the temple's heaviest sentence on a record, and a document of the house carries no name. A private contract that escaped the scraping is valid and dangerous to own.
- **Name Struck.** The lowest rank in the Khelâthi bodies that keep ranks, a person put out and struck from the record, carries the title of his sentence.
- **The whispered name.** The oldest servants of the necropolis keep what he was called and say it to no stranger. A scholar who wants it has to find the people the erasure never reached.
- **A sect that would wake him.** Now and again a cult comes to the sand, wanting to wake him, and the sand takes it.
