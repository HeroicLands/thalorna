The Keepers of the King-Lists say of the sea-raiders what they say of every conqueror: "He was entered like anyone else." The sentence is the fact of the case. The people who burned a port and took a throne in 1960 ST (151 BF) were entered in the list as a house, and nothing in the list says whose sons they were.

## The Landing

The raiders came from the north, over the Vylarian Sea, in numbers that the delta's watchers counted in ships and not in men. The Khelâthi record keeps no name for their country, and the sea-raiders' own name for themselves, if they had one, was not entered. What the record keeps is the place they landed: the silt-islands of the Selat of [[place-tjathu|Tjathu]], where the river's mouths spread into reed and natron flat.

The nearest port was **Zel-Amqelu**, a trading town on one of those islands. They burned it the day they landed. The port was never rebuilt, and its channel shifted soon after ([[place-zelamqelu|Zel-Amqelu]]). The Khelâthi river fleet of the day was a toll-boat fleet, built to meet traders at the mouths, and it did not meet the raiders.

## The March on Galezkara

The raiders did not stop to hold the delta. They took the river road upstream with the boats they had taken, and the delta selatu could not muster against them before they reached the capital. The Gar-Aû of the day did not survive the flood season, and by its end [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] was in their hands.

The priesthood did what it does. It crowned the raiders' chief under the throne name **Gul'el'Retha**, wrote his line into the list as **Malu'Thenoru**, and waited. The list holds the house for three reigns. The Keepers' abstract names the first and the dates of the house, and no more.

## What Followed

The raiders ruled as the Khelâthi priesthood let them, which was through the existing bureaus. The temples kept their rites and their estates, and a conqueror who left the Measure alone found the Measure left alone. The house lasted thirty years because no one needed to remove it. In 1990 ST (121 BF) its third Gar-Aû died, and the priesthood raised another house ([[lore-seahouse|the Closing of the Sea House]]).

## What It Left

- **A burned port.** Zel-Amqelu, never rebuilt, in the Selat of Tjathu.
- **The apex defenses.** The forts and customs-stations at the delta's apex date from the raiders' going, when the next house resolved that a landing would not reach the river again.
- **A shared alarm.** The three powers of the northern shore coordinate against sea-raids still.
- **A house in the list.** Malu'Thenoru, a house of the Khelâthi list whose people came from a country the record does not name.
