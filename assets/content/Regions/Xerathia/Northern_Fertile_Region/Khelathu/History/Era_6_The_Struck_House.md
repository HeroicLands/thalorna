The Struck House is the shortest age the Keepers teach, and the only one that ends with a sentence of the temple. A schoolroom in the valley gives it to the children last and says the least about it: the house the necropolis calls [[affiliation-garzinelrelu|Gar-Zin'el'Relu]], six reigns, and a name that is never spoken. The reigns are not missing from the list. The years stand in it, counted and unnamed, and the formulas around them show the shape of what the temple chose not to say.

## What Happened

The age opens in 2281 ST (171 AF) with the crowning of the first of the house, at the close of the age of the clerks ([[lore-akhcountmon|the Count of Months]]). The list keeps its six reigns as years under struck names: 31, 22, 4, 19, 13 and 14. The first five pass without an entry a scribe would remember. The sixth Gar-Au is crowned in 2365 ST (255 AF), and his reign is the reason the age is taught.

He proclaimed himself [[lore-uqaadty|Uqa'a]] in the flesh, seized the [Great Temple of Uqa'a]{.name data-type="place/structure"} and its granaries, and silenced the priests who named the blasphemy. Then the floods failed. For the rest of his reign the river rose short year upon year, and the Gar-Au hid the famine by falsifying the reading of [The Measure]{.name data-type="place/structure"} and by killing anyone who said the word _hunger_ aloud ([[lore-failfloods|the Failing Floods]]).

The upper river broke him. In 2378 ST (268 AF) the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatnlghztn|Anlagh-Zetun]] raised the selat and the temple levies against the guard that held the great granaries shut, and the fighting at the granary gates ended in a rite-less grave that the selat still keeps shut ([[lore-sealedgran|the Battle of the Sealed Granaries]]). The god-king was taken within the month, and the Thaz'Lekhau of [[affiliation-uqaa|Uqa'a]] judged him at the Great Temple ([[lore-judgfalseuq|the Judgment of the False Uqa'a]]).

## Rulers

- The house of Gar-Zin'el'Relu: six Gar-Auu, entered by years alone; the list gives no throne name for any of them and no name for the house.
- The [[being-falseuqaa|False Uqa'a]]: the sixth, crowned 2365 ST (255 AF), thrown down 2378 ST (268 AF). The records name him by the epithet and no other.
- [Zinlegir]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Thaz'Lekhau of Uqa'a, who judged him; [Gezanlaghur]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of [Anlaghet'Zaru]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, Halzi'a of Anlagh-Zetun, whose rising delivered him to the temple.

## Its End

The age closes in 2378 ST (268 AF) with the entry the list writes in place of a name: _the house is closed, and its name with it_. The priesthood of Uqa'a raises the next house the same year, and the Keepers date a new age from that crowning ([[lore-akhpriestthr|the Priests' Thrones]]).

What the age left on the ground is still there. The road to the False Uqa'a's tomb is cursed and guarded by an order to kill on sight. Beneath the warrens of [[place-garqethar|Gar-Qethar]] lie the famine pits. At [[place-anlaghzetun|Anlagh-Zetun]] stands a granary nobody opens ([[place-eleventhgran|the Eleventh Granary]]), the second blood-field in Khelathi memory.
