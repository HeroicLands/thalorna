Every contract, tax roll and temple receipt in [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Aû'Khelâthu]] is headed with the same name: Year 12 of Gar-Aû Meqes. The **House of Meqes** is the age the empire lives in, the longest house of the Near Count, and no clerk now alive has written any other.

## What Happened

In 2580 ST (470 AF) the child Quz'el'Retha III went to the West in the night, and Meqes of Zu'Mequ, who had stood beside him as regent, was crowned in the child's second year ([[lore-childfloods|the Child of Three Floods]]). He kept his own name as his throne name, and every Gar-Aû of his house has taken it since.

The house settled in. Under Meqes VI the empire reached the height of this age, and the Gar-Aû ordered the older houses carried out from the river-front of [[place-zugezer|Zu-Gezer]] and set the house's own Way at the head of the death-road ([[lore-greatremovl|the Great Removal]], about 2650 ST, 540 AF). The grievance of the removed houses' descendants has outlasted the endowments that paid for their new tombs.

The house chartered the guilds that now date themselves by it: [[affiliation-garnuw|Gar'Nuw]] under Meqes VII, the seventh of the name, and [[affiliation-garmelnu|Gar'Melnu]] under Meqes XI, the eleventh. Under Meqes XI the court also lent Bethûa gold, engineers and loans for its war of separation from the Helonic metropole ([[lore-bethuasep|the Separation of Bethûa]], about 2725 ST, 615 AF). The court of Meqes XVI used the same instruments a century later, in 708 AF, when covert aid carried Harad out of the Vylarian Empire ([[lore-empirdclne|the Decline Begins]]).

Three reigns of the house were short or children's. The year of Meqes III was not completed; Meqes VIII was a child of four floods with her uncle beside her; and Meqes XIV, a child of two floods, did not complete his year before the uncle beside him took the mound as Meqes XV. The house knows what a succession can cost.

## Rulers

- **Meqes I** (crowned 2580 ST, 470 AF), the founder.
- **Meqes VI** (2639 ST, 529 AF; twenty-seven years), who ordered the Great Removal.
- **Meqes VII** (2665 ST, 555 AF) and **Meqes XI** (2710 ST, 600 AF), under whom Gar'Nuw and Gar'Melnu hold their charters.
- **Meqes XVI** (2819 ST, 709 AF), the daughter of Meqes XV, who reigns now and has reached her twelfth year.
- Her nephew [[being-anlaghur|Anlaghur]] stands near enough the succession that the court asks the question seriously.

The [[lore-garauu|king-list]] gives every reign of the house with its months.

## Its End

The age has not ended. Its present is the twelfth year of [[being-garaumeqesxv|Meqes XVI]] (2830 ST, 720 AF), with a nephew near the succession and the peace of the eastern road growing thin.
