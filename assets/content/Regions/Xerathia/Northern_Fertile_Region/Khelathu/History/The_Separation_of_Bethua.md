A trade-house factor in Bethûra pays a bill with a Khelâthi weight-piece of silver and observes, to a newly arrived clerk who has never held one, that no coin of Bethûa has ever been struck. "Ask why," the factor says. "It is a long answer, and it begins with a war."

## The War

Bethûa began as a daughter-settlement of the Helonic peoples, planted on the southern shore of the Vylarian Sea and governed from across the water. About 2725 ST (615 AF) it fought a war of separation from that metropole. Bethûa did not fight alone, and could not have: the queen **Meqes XI** (2710 ST to 2738 ST) underwrote the war with Khelâthi gold, with engineers, and with loans.

The court at [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] had its reasons. A free Bethûa on the southern shore of the sea, aligned with the empire, would keep Helonic and Vylarian ambitions in [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]] divided and give the court a partner at sea without the cost of a navy. The instruments were the [[affiliation-garhalzi|Gár-Hálzi]] correspondents, the engineers of the temples, and the loans themselves.

## The Rebuilding

The war was won, and the peace was built in Khelâthi forms. Bethûa rebuilt its treasury on the model of a Khelâthi temple treasury, and its water-engineering corps on the model of a canal-warden's bureau. The two offices took Khelâthi titles that Bethûa uses to this day: the **Izet-Mû**, Overseer of the Waters, and the **Izet-Halzi**, Overseer of the Account. The kingdom strikes no coin of its own, and the weight-pieces of [[lore-aukhlthcrncy|Aû'Khelâthu Currency]] pass in every purse in the capital.

The same queen chartered the guild of [[affiliation-garmelnu|Gar'Melnu]], so the court that was lending to a foreign sea-power was also putting its own guilds in order.

## A Century Before Harad

The separation of Bethûa belongs to the age in which the Vylarian Empire and its neighbors began losing provinces ([[lore-empirdclne|the Decline Begins]]). Provènzia drifted out of its empire's hands without a war and became a realm in all but name; Bethûa, on the southern shore of the same sea, left its own metropole by war, and the Khelâthi court lent the gold openly, as a loan, with its engineers walking the capital's streets. About a century later the court repeated the arrangement for Harad, in secret and under the name of humanitarian commerce.

## What It Left

- **The Khelâthi machinery of the Matriarchy.** Treasury, water corps and court accounts speak Khelâthi, and the Izet-Mû and Izet-Halzi of [[place-bethura|Bethûra]] answer to the Mêtríssa.
- **The patron relationship.** Bethûa receives a Khelâthi envoy with a courtesy it gives no other foreign man, and the court at Galezkara cultivates the Matriarchy, for strategy rather than sentiment ([[affiliation-mtrrchybth|the Matriarchy of Bethûa]]).
- **The correspondent accounts.** The Great Houses of the [[affiliation-bthntrdhss|Bethûan Trade-Houses]] keep accounts at Gár-Hálzi chapters, and a Bethûan letter of credit is redeemed in Khelâthi weight.
- **A fleet.** The privateer war on Vylarian shipping that the court cannot be accused of is fought by [[affiliation-bethuanflt|the Bethûan Fleet]].
