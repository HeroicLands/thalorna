The **Priests' Thrones** are the two centuries after the judgment, and the Keepers name them for who did the crowning. The throne has a Gar-Aû on it throughout, and the list gives each reign in full, but the formulas change character. Houses close within a generation, children are crowned, and the regent who stood beside a child is the next to stand on the mound so often that the line recording it becomes the most worn in the list.

## What Happened

The priesthood of [[lore-uqaadty|Uqa'â]] raises the first house in the year of the fall, in 2378 ST (268 AF). **Qelt'Leru** gives three reigns, the last a child of five floods with **Gul'Zetu** standing beside him, and Gul'Zetu's own house takes the crown in 2401 ST (291 AF). The pattern is set: a house, a child, a regent, a new house.

In 2425 ST (315 AF) the age takes its first shock from outside. A steppe host that has crossed Dunhara ([[lore-hndrdbnnrs|the Storm of the Hundred Banners]]) comes onto the eastern road, the caravans stop, and a frontier commander of **Shethes'Râlu** seizes the throne to meet it. The list enters his end as _his year was not completed_, and the priesthood raises **Let'Thariu** in the same year ([[lore-eastroad|the Storm on the Eastern Road]]). The forts of [[place-khuqetmiglet|Khuqet-Miglet]] are walled in the decades that follow.

The second shock comes by sea. In 2487 ST (377 AF) a Vylarian army meets the chariot corps at the delta apex, the corps breaks, and the priesthood crowns the army's commander as Zab'el'Qar ([[lore-vylconquest|the Vylarian Conquest]]). His son reigns after him as a Khelâthi and goes to the West in the night in 2505 ST (395 AF) ([[lore-foreignhs|the Closing of the Foreign House]]). The house of **Anlagh'Zeketu** follows.

The age ends with the child of three floods. In 2579 ST (469 AF) the house of **Qelt'Gultau** crowns Quz'el'Retha III with **Zu'Mequ** standing beside him, and the list enters the child's death in the night.

## Rulers

- **Qelt'Leru:** Waz'el'Qar I, crowned 2378 ST (268 AF), then Waz'el'Qar II and the child Waz'el'Qar III. **Gul'Zetu** stood beside the child and was crowned Gez'el'Qar I.
- **Shethes'Râlu** and **Let'Thariu:** Quz'Uqa II, crowned 2425 ST (315 AF), whose year was not completed, and Legir'el'Retha I, crowned the same year and reigning twenty.
- **Zu'Letetu:** Zab'Uzner II and Zab'Uzner III, the second of whom died on the field at the delta apex.
- **Amralo'Methu,** the Vylarian house: Zab'el'Qar I, crowned 2487 ST (377 AF), and Zab'el'Qar II.
- **Qelt'Gultau:** Quz'el'Retha I, II and III.

## Its End

The age closes in 2580 ST (470 AF) when **Meqes** of Zu'Mequ, who stood beside the child, is crowned in the child's second year ([[lore-akhhsmeqes|the House of Meqes]]). The list enters _the throne passed to the one who stood beside it_.

The age leaves three things on the ground. The Chariot Field at Khelaga is the third blood-field in Khelâthi memory and the only thin one. The forts of Khuqet-Miglet hold the eastern wells. A short foreign Way at Zu-Gezer holds two tombs and bare ground.
