The Priests' Thrones are the two centuries after the judgment, and the Keepers name them for who did the crowning. The throne has a Gar-Au on it throughout, and the list gives each reign in full, but the formulas change character. Houses close within a generation, children are crowned, and the regent who stood beside a child is the next to stand on the mound so often that the line recording it becomes the most worn in the list.

## What Happened

The priesthood of the [[affiliation-uqaa|Faith of Uqa'a]] raises the first house in the year of the fall, in 2378 ST (268 AF). [Qelt'Leru]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} gives three reigns, the last a child of five floods with [Gul'Zetu]{.name data-type="being/npc"} standing beside him, and Gul'Zetu's own house takes the crown in 2401 ST (291 AF). The pattern is set: a house, a child, a regent, a new house.

In 2425 ST (315 AF) the age takes its first shock from outside. A steppe host that has crossed the [[place-dunharargn|Dunhara Region]] ([[lore-hndrdbnnrs|The Storm of the Hundred Banners]]) comes onto the eastern road, the caravans stop, and a frontier commander of [Shethes'Ralu]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} seizes the throne to meet it. The list enters his end as _his year was not completed_, and the priesthood raises [Let'Thariu]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} in the same year ([[lore-eastroad|The Storm on the Eastern Road]]). The forts of [[place-khuqetmiglet|Khuqet-Miglet]] are walled in the decades that follow.

The second shock comes by sea. In 2487 ST (377 AF) a Vylarian army meets the chariot corps at the delta apex, the corps breaks, and the priesthood crowns the army's commander as [Zab'el'Qar I]{.name data-type="being/npc"} ([[lore-vylconquest|The Vylarian Conquest]]). His son reigns after him as a Khelathi and goes to the West in the night in 2505 ST (395 AF) ([[lore-foreignhs|The Closing of the Foreign House]]). The house of [Anlagh'Zeketu]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} follows.

The age ends with the child of three floods. In 2579 ST (469 AF) the house of [Qelt'Gultau]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} crowns [Kuz'el'Retha III]{.name data-type="being/npc"} with [Zu'Meku]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} standing beside him, and the list enters the child's death in the night.

## Rulers

- Qelt'Leru: [Waz'el'Qar I]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, crowned 2378 ST (268 AF), then [Waz'el'Qar II]{.name data-type="being/npc"} and the child [Waz'el'Qar III]{.name data-type="being/npc"}. Gul'Zetu stood beside the child and was crowned [Gez'el'Qar I]{.name data-type="being/npc"}.
- Shethes'Ralu and Let'Thariu: [Kuz'Uqa II]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, crowned 2425 ST (315 AF), whose year was not completed, and [Legir'el'Retha I]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, crowned the same year and reigning twenty.
- [Zu'Letetu]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}: [Zab'Uzner II]{.name data-type="being/npc"} and [Zab'Uzner III]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the second of whom died on the field at the delta apex.
- [Amralo'Methu]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, the Vylarian house: Zab'el'Qar I, crowned 2487 ST (377 AF), and [Zab'el'Qar II]{.name data-type="being/npc"}.
- Qelt'Gultau: [Kuz'el'Retha I]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, [Kuz'el'Retha II]{.name data-type="being/npc"} and III.

## Its End

The age closes in 2580 ST (470 AF) when [Meqes I]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Zu'Meku, who stood beside the child, is crowned in the child's second year ([[lore-akhhsmeqes|The House of Meqes]]). The list enters _the throne passed to the one who stood beside it_.

The age leaves three things on the ground. [[place-chariotfld|The Chariot Field]] at [[place-khelaga|Khelaga]] is the third blood-field in Khelathi memory and the only thin one. The forts of Khuqet-Miglet hold the eastern wells. A short foreign Way at [[place-zugezer|Zu-Gezer]] holds two tombs and bare ground.
