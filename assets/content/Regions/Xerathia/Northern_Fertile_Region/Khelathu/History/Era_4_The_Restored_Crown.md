The **Restored Crown** is the Keepers' name for the years in which the throne sat at [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] with no rival and no conqueror on it, apart from thirty years at the very end. A schoolroom in the valley teaches it as the age of tidy lists: reign follows reign, the list gives lengths a human life can hold, and the priesthood has no need to explain a crowning.

## What Happened

The age opens in 1671 ST (440 BF), when the last of the hill-nomad house dies and the priesthood raises **Azlet'Râlu** ([[lore-hillhouse|the Closing of the Hill House]]). Its first Gar-Aû takes the throne name Gez'el'Retha III, the first use of that name since the First Tablets, and the choice is a statement: the crown is the old crown again.

For more than a century the list holds nothing a scribe would stop to read. Then, in 1790 ST (321 BF), the house of the sun, **Âthen'Rêlu**, takes the throne, and its first Gar-Aû lays out a new capital at [[place-maguathen|Magu-Athen]] ([[lore-sunhouse|the Sun-House at Magu-Athen]]). The house rules from that city for a generation, presses a doctrine the great temples refuse, and is gone by 1822 ST (289 BF). In the same years the court sends an army and an embassy west across the South Marches to bring the eastern temple-cities of Okháris into tribute ([[lore-templetrib|the Tribute of the Temple-Cities]]), and the chain of forts it builds on the way is abandoned within a generation.

The age ends in two strokes. In 1960 ST (151 BF) raiders from across the sea burn the port of Zel-Amqelu and march upriver, and the priesthood crowns their chief ([[lore-searaiders|the Sea-Raiders' Throne]]). Their house, **Malu'Thenoru**, holds the crown for three reigns. When the third dies in 1990 ST (121 BF), the priesthood raises **Gezer'Quru** and the delta takes the shape it keeps ([[lore-seahouse|the Closing of the Sea House]]).

## Rulers

- **Azlet'Râlu:** Gez'el'Retha III, crowned 1671 ST (440 BF).
- **Âthen'Rêlu**, the house of the sun: Anleth'Uqa I, crowned 1790 ST (321 BF), and his son Anleth'Uqa II, crowned 1808 ST (303 BF).
- **Malu'Thenoru**, the sea-raiders: Gul'el'Retha, crowned 1960 ST (151 BF), and two reigns after him that the Keepers' abstract does not name.
- The other houses of the age rule from Galezkara, and the list gives them as it gives all the Middle Count: a house, a run of reigns and a closing formula. The royal necropolis at [[place-zugezer|Zu-Gezer]] holds their Ways, but not the sun-house's, which lies in its own city.

## Its End

The age closes with the sea house in 1990 ST (121 BF). The list then begins to enter a reign's months beside its years, and the Keepers date a new age from that change ([[lore-akhcountmon|the Count of Months]]).
