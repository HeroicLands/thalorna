## Overview

Linmut is the capital of [[affiliation-nomesenmut|the Nome of Linmut]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]. It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-seker|Wethûr]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the selat's: A river-island fortress-selat at the second cataract; gateway and toll-gate.
