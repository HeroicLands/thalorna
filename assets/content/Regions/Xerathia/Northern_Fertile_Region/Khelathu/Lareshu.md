**Lareshu** is a waste of deep sand beyond the settled country, crossed by nobody who has a choice and by few who have an errand. The canals and cisterns of the valley carry water a little way into the dry country; they do not carry it here.

What draws the rare traveler is what lies hidden in the sand. "We have the road in our charts, clue by clue," an astrologer of the temple libraries says, "and the last clue is the one the desert keeps." The road begins with a prophecy and is held to end at an ancient sanctuary, [[place-lutzelutha|Lut-Zelutha]], which the empire's astrologers have sought without finding.
