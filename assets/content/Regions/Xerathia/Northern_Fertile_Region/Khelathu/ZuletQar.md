**Zulet'Qar** holds the largest wells on the desert road through [[place-khuqetmiglet|Khuqet-Miglet]]. It exists for the water and for nothing else: there is no farming here and no permanent household, only the wells and the ground around them.

"Put your sword up, because nobody sheds blood at Zulet'Qar," a [[lore-dunhariclt|Dunhari]] guide tells a caravan driver on his first crossing. By long custom a Dunhari who has eaten [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelâthi]] salt here is a guest and not a prisoner, and the empire's garrison and the desert clans meet on this ground to deal with one another. The caravan-tolls are paid here and the guides are hired; the scribes of the Overseer of the March argue a stolen herd or a closed well with the sheikhs' poets; and each year the peace is renewed with gifts. "The Khelâthi write the terms down and we recite them," the guide adds. "When the two versions differ, there is a raid."

Water is the one resource neither side can afford to let the other poison, and the truce around Zulet'Qar has outlasted every quarrel that has tried to break it.
