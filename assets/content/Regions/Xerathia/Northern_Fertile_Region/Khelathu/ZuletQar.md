Zulet'Qar holds the largest wells on the desert road through [[place-khuqetmiglet|Khuqet-Miglet]]. It exists for the water, and for nothing else — there is no farming here and no permanent household, only the wells and the ground around them. That ground is where the empire's garrison and the desert tribes meet to deal with one another, since water is the one resource neither side can afford to let the other poison, and the truce around Zulet'Qar has outlasted every quarrel that has tried to break it.
