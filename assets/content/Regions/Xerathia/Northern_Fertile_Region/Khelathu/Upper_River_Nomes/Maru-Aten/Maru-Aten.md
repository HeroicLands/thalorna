## Overview

Magu-Aten is the capital of [[affiliation-nomemaruat|the Nome of Magu-Aten]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]. It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-ra|Uqa'â]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the selat's: A planned temple-city of one zealous dynasty, half-abandoned, still inhabited.
