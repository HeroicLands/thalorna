## Overview

The Nome of Ankh-Setûn holds the land of [[place-ankhsetunnome|Ankh-Setûn Nome]]. Its [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]] is the very type of the [[affiliation-uperivrnms|upper-river]] noble—conservative, hereditary, land-proud, and powerful precisely because the empire cannot do without his grain.

## Character

Its great families have farmed the same flood-basins for longer than the current dynasty has existed, and they guard their land, their grain-rents, and their ancient privileges with the patience of men who measure time in floods rather than reigns. The selat's patron is [[affiliation-kemtnpnthn|Qe'âret]], goddess of order, truth, and right measure—fitting for a country whose whole life turns on the survey of fields, the assessment of harvests, and the honest filling of granaries.

## For the Worldbuilder

Ankh-Setûn is the empire's breadbasket, and its importance is exactly its grain—control the granaries here and you have a grip on the capital's throat. Run it as deep, conservative farm-country where the stakes hide behind a placid surface: the politics of the harvest-assessment and the grain-tax, the survey-fraud and boundary-disputes that follow every flood, the granary-keepers who can quietly starve or feed a region, the hereditary feuds among landowning houses, and the perennial dread of flood-failure and famine. In a weak reign the Halzi'a who holds the empire's grain is a kingmaker; in a hungry year the selat is where rebellion or relief begins. Adventure here is the intrigue of land and bread: rigged surveys, stolen grain-tallies, a hoarding Halzi'a, a sabotaged dike, a famine that someone is profiting from. What is abundant here is grain, order, and tradition; what is scarce is novelty, coin, and any patience for the delta's ways.

## Commerce and Currency

Ankh-Setûn uses the [[lore-tkhprcrncy|Aû'Khelâthu currency system]], but its economy runs more on grain-render and granary-account than on struck metal: taxes are paid and wealth is held in stored grain as much as in silver, reckoned and warehoused through the temple-treasuries and the [[affiliation-perhati|Gár-Hálzi]] houses. The river-port of Meru-Hotep is where the selat touches the coined economy most. See [[lore-tkhprcrncy|Aû'Khelâthu Currency]] for the full system.
