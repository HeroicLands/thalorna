## Overview

Anzet is the capital of [[affiliation-nomeanptkh|the Nome of Anzet-Khent]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]. It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-anubis|Hezmuîri]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the selat's: jackal-cult and desert-edge tombs; embalmers and tomb-guards.
