## Overview

Gar-Reth'Sa'âr is the land of [[affiliation-nomeprthth|The Nome of Gar-Reth'Sa'âr]], one of the [[affiliation-uperivrnms|Upper River Nomes]] of the [[affiliation-empirtkhpr|Empire of Aû'Khelâthu]], and lies within the [[place-takheperurgn|Aû'Khelâthu Region]].

At its center stands [[place-khemenu|Khelunu]], the foremost temple-city of [[affiliation-kemtnpnthn|Reth'Sa'âr]], keeper of sacred wisdom, whose great library-temple holds the deepest archive in [[affiliation-empirtkhpr|The Empire of Aû'Khelâthu]] and whose priest-scribes maintain the meticulous king-lists that stretch back, unbroken, to the [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#the-kheperi-calendar-and-dating|Qet Telgu]]. The selat's worldly produce is ordinary upper-river grain; its real export is literate, numerate, and learned men, and the records by which the empire knows itself.

## Economy

The selat farms its share of upper-river grain like any other, but its distinctive wealth is intellectual and institutional. The temple-schools draw fee-paying pupils and temple-endowments from across the empire; the scriptoria copy and sell the texts—legal, medical, astronomical, ritual—that the rest of the realm depends on; the astronomers' calendar-service is a quiet monopoly that every temple and farmer relies on; and the certification of scribes is itself a source of fees and influence. Around this learned core the selat supports the ordinary trades of a settled river-country, but its true balance of trade is in trained minds: Gar-Reth'Sa'âr imports raw talent and exports educated men, and grows comfortably rich on the difference.

## Notable Features

- The great library-temple of [[affiliation-kemtnpnthn|Reth'Sa'âr]] at [[place-khemenu|Khelunu]]—the deepest archive in the empire
- The astronomers' observatory and the calendar-service that keeps the realm's time

## Settlements

- **[[place-khemenu|Khelunu]]** (~85,000)—the selat capital and the empire's great temple-city of learning; the library-temple of Reth'Sa'âr, the scribal schools, the observatory, the archives, and the bustling pupil-and-scholar quarters that surround them, alongside the seat of the Halzi'a.
- **The school-and-farm villages:** the selat's grain-country holds the usual scatter of villages of 500–2,500—among them Gar-Djehuti, Lut-Sesh, Yath-Khelunu, and Sma-Thoth—several of them grown prosperous as lodging-towns for the families of pupils and the lesser temple-schools that feed the great one at Khelunu.
