The priesthood of [[lore-rethsaardty|Reth'Sa'âr]] keeps the temple chronicle of the [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelâthi]] in its precinct beside the Great Temple of [[lore-uqaadty|Uqa'â]] on [[place-yathtelgu|Yath-Telgu]], where the master copies of the king-lists are held, and the chronicle opens with the [[lore-qettelgu|Qet Telgu]]. This is that opening as the temple tells it, in the terse voice of its scribes: entries, names and outcomes, each in its place. It gives the first Gar-Aû by title alone and stops where the list of the [[lore-garauu|Gar-Aûu]] begins, which is the Keeper of the King-Lists' charge. It dates nothing before the First Occasion and accounts for nothing older than it; the age of the world and the lineages that predate the valley's reckoning lie outside it, and the chronicle does not raise them.

: The First Occasion {#chronicle}

:::
Year 1 of the Qet Telgu, which is year 1 of the first Gar-Aû. Entered by the house of Reth'Sa'âr, from the first of its tablets, in the order the tablets keep.

Before this year nothing is entered, because there was no year to enter it in. There was the dark, and in the dark the serpent whose name is not written at the head of a chronicle, and there was water without a bank. Nothing in it stood, so nothing in it could be counted, and what cannot be counted is not history. This house does not date it. It is chaos, and the whole account of chaos is that it was.

Qe'âret laid order on the dark. Law was set over the water before there was ground to set it on. That is the first thing, and this house enters it first, though it has no year of its own.

Then the mound lifted from the waters. It is the mound in the middle of the river at Galezkara, Yath-Telgu, the First Mound, and it is the first ground; the world begins on it. The fire of Uqa'â rose over it, and the first light fell on the first ground.

The gods established the order of civilization upon that ground. Qe'âret gave it law. Psaq'âru shaped it. Uznêra gave it the river that goes and comes again. Reth'Sa'âr set it down, so that it would be kept. This house keeps what he set down, and copies from it.

In that year the first Gar-Aû stood upon the mound. The valley was many, and he made it one. There was one crown and it was his. Aû'Khelâthu was founded on its river. The fire of Uqa'â was in him, and the god and the god-king stood together on the first ground, as they stand there now.

From this year every year is numbered. The first year is this one, and every year after it is entered in its place. None is missing.

The house notes that the earliest reigns are long, and that foreigners count them on their fingers and frown. The house has entered them as the tablets give them. The tablets are the god's, and the house does not correct the god.

This is the First Occasion. What came before was chaos. What comes after is entered.
:::
