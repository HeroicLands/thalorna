## Overview

Hut-Sobek is the capital of [[affiliation-nomehutsob|the Nome of Hut-Sobek]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#titles-and-forms-of-address|Haty'a]]. It holds the nome's chief temple of [[affiliation-sobek|Sobek]], the Haty'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the nome's: eastern marsh; the great crocodile cult and the river-beast hunters.
