## Overview

Lut-Tjelsuk is the capital of [[affiliation-nomehutsob|the Nome of Lut-Tjelsuk]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]. It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-sobek|Tjelsuk]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the selat's: eastern marsh; the great crocodile cult and the river-beast hunters.
