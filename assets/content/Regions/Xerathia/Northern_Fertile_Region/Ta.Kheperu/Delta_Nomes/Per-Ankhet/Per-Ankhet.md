## Overview

Gar-Ankhet is the great port of [[affiliation-empirtkhpr|The Empire of Aû'Khelâthu]] and the capital of [[affiliation-nomeprnkht|its selat]]—the empire's principal sea-gate, set on the westernmost and deepest of the river's mouths where the channel runs broad enough to take the largest ships. It is the richest and most cosmopolitan city in the empire after the capital, a crowded sprawl of quays, bonded warehouses, counting-houses, and foreign quarters where the wealth of forty nomes is loaded out and the goods of the wider world brought in. The harbor never sleeps: grain, papyrus, linen, gold, and exquisite artisan-work go down to the ships, and timber, metals, spices, wine, and the luxuries of [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión Region]] come up the quays, every cargo taxed at the Gar-Aû's customs-house and every fortune banked through the [[affiliation-perhati|Gár-Hálzi]] houses of the waterfront.

The city's temper is mercantile, polyglot, and pleasure-loving. [[affiliation-cnfdrtnhrdnstts|Haradian]] and Mídhalión merchant factories keep permanent quarters here; a dozen tongues sound on the dockside; and the cult of [[affiliation-kemtnpnthn|Hápi]], lord of abundance, is worshipped with the easy confidence of the very rich. The merchant-Halzi'a who rules from Gar-Ankhet is reckoned the wealthiest noble in the empire outside the royal house—and the most worldly, balancing his profit against the capital's standing suspicion that the delta would sell the realm for a good freight-rate.

## Notable Features

- The great harbor—the empire's principal deep-water sea-gate
- The foreign quarters, with permanent Haradian and Mídhalión merchant factories
- The Gar-Aû's chief customs-house and the bonded warehouses of the waterfront
- The Gár-Hálzi banking-houses, hub of the empire's foreign-exchange and credit trade
- The great temple of Hápi and the festival-life of a port that loves its wealth
