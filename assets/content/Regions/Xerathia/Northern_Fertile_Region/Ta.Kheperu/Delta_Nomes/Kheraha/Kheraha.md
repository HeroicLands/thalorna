## Overview

Kheraha is the capital of [[affiliation-nomekherah|the Nome of Kheraha]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#titles-and-forms-of-address|Haty'a]]. It holds the nome's chief temple of [[affiliation-ra|Rā]], the Haty'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the nome's: the delta apex, where the river first divides; old shrines and customs-stations.
