## Overview

Pekhenu is the capital of [[affiliation-nomepekhen|the Nome of Pekhenu]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]. It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-thoth|Reth'Sa'âr]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the selat's: library-temples and astronomers; the calendar-keepers of the middle river.
