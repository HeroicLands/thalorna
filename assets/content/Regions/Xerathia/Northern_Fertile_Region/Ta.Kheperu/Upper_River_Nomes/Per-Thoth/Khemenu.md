## Overview

Khemenu is the empire's city of learning, capital of [[affiliation-nomeprthth|the scribal nome]] and seat of the foremost temple of [[affiliation-kemtnpnthn|Thōth]], keeper of sacred knowledge. Its great library-temple holds the deepest archive in [[affiliation-empirtkhpr|The Empire of Ta'Kheperu]]—the priestly records, the medical and astronomical and legal texts, and the meticulous king-lists that reach back, unbroken, to the [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#the-kheperi-calendar-and-dating|Sep Tepy]]. Around it cluster the finest scribal schools in the empire, drawing fee-paying pupils from every nome; the observatory whose astronomers keep the realm's calendar; the scriptoria that copy and sell the texts the empire runs on; and the bustling quarters of scholars, pupils, and lodging-houses that make Khemenu feel less like a provincial city than a vast and ancient university. The Wer'Hekau of Thōth, less wealthy than the great solar or mortuary cults but more influential than his purse, governs the word and the number here—and in a civilization that runs on records, that is power enough.

## Notable Features

- The great library-temple of Thōth—the deepest archive in the empire
- The scribal schools—the gold standard of literate training
- The observatory and the calendar-service that keeps the realm's time
- The priestly archives and the unbroken king-lists since the Sep Tepy
- The scriptoria and the medical, legal, and mathematical learning of the temple
