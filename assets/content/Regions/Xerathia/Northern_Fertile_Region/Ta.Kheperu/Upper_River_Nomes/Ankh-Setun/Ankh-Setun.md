## Overview

Ankh-Setûn — "the living storehouse" — is the capital of the empire's [[affiliation-nomenkhstn|great granary nome]], a substantial inland city set among the richest wheatland in [[affiliation-empirtkhpr|The Empire of Ta'Kheperu]]. It is a city built around grain: its skyline is a forest of granary-domes, its markets trade in wheat and barley by the boatload, and its busiest halls are the survey-courts and tally-bureaus where the flood-fields are measured and the harvests assessed. Here the [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#titles-and-forms-of-address|Haty'a]] holds court — a conservative, land-proud lord whose power rests on the bread he controls — and the great temple of [[affiliation-kemtnpnthn|Má'át]], goddess of order and right measure, presides fittingly over a city whose whole life is survey, tally, and the honest filling of stores. It is not a glamorous place, but it is one of the most important in the empire: when the harvest fails elsewhere, it is Ankh-Setûn's granaries that decide who eats.

## Notable Features

- The great granaries that store the empire's reserve against famine
- The grain-markets and the survey-and-assize courts that measure the land
- The temple of Má'át, goddess of order and right measure
- The seat of the granary-Haty'a, one of the empire's quiet kingmakers
