## Overview

Sebt-Renpet is the capital of [[affiliation-nomesbtrnp|the Nome of Sebt-Renpet]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#titles-and-forms-of-address|Haty'a]]. It holds the nome's chief temple of [[affiliation-seker|Sēker]], the Haty'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the nome's: cavern-tombs and natron; a workmanlike mortuary nome.
