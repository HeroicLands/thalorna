## Overview

Wagaru is the capital of [[affiliation-nomewawat|the Nome of Wagaru]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#titles-and-forms-of-address|Halzi'a]]. It holds the selat's chief temple of [[affiliation-ptah|Psaq'âru]], the Halzi'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the selat's: the southern gold-road; mines, caravan-stations, and tribute-collection.
