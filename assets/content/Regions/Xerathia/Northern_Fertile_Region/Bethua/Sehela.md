## Overview

Sehéla is a small grain-village in the dry southern interior of the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethûa]], and it exists because water is brought to it. Its fields are kept alive only by the qanats and channels the engineer-priestesses maintain; let the channels fail for a season and the grain fails with them. The village's peasants work land held by the local House, and the House that holds Sehéla and the cantons around it is **House Khalîthôn**, under its clan-matriarch, **Dómissa Tázîtha**.

## A Bethûan Village

Sehéla is ordered the Bethûan way. A household turns on its women: the mothers and grandmothers hold what the family owns and govern its affairs, the men labor in the fields as the dependents they are under law, and the eldest daughter is raised from the first as the one who will inherit and answer for the household. Every free girl of the village is trained young in arms and in the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth#the-martial-bridge-the-spear-sisterhood-and-the-fleet|Charíssa]], and the women who train the village levy watch for the quick ones.

The village's best-known daughter is [[being-zahirmlkht|Zahira Malkhet]], a peasant's eldest daughter who outfought girls years older by the age of ten, was taken into the Dómissa's own household guard, and left it for a path that answered to her own judgment.
