## Overview

Bethûra is the capital of the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethûa]], a city of some thirty-five thousand in the [[place-bethuargn|Bethûa Region]] on Xerathia's northern shore. It is the seat of both pillars of Bethûan government: the Sacred College of the high priestesses and the Council of Houses of the clan-matriarchs sit here, and the Mêtríssa, Queen and High Priestess, presides over both.

Bethûra is not one of the realm's great harbors. It stands inland of the orchard-ports of the coast, in the irrigated interior, and the roads north from it to the ports of the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]] are the ones the Court of the Veil watches most closely.

## The Seat of the Two Pillars

The **Sacred College** (_the Hieróssa_) is the assembly of the Hiérissai, the high priestesses of the three goddess-orders of Lúsinía, Aethería and Vénusia and of the great temples. In Bethûra it sits as the realm's spiritual authority and highest religious court, and as the conclave that elects each new Mêtríssa from the priestesses of the royal line. The Izet-Mû, chief of the engineer-priestesses of the Order of the Waters, sits among them by right of office.

The **Council of Houses** (_the Sünédra_) is the assembly of the Dómissai, the matriarchs of the recognized clans. In Bethûra it ratifies law, votes the levies and taxes, and judges disputes between Houses.

The Mêtríssa sits where the two meet. The reigning sovereign is **Mêtríssa Amîthéna of House Thamîrîon**. The Stratégissa, the supreme war-leader, attends both councils when war is in question and votes in neither.

## The Trade-Houses

The [[affiliation-bthntrdhss|Bethûan Trade-Houses]] keep the principal hall of their federation in Bethûra. Their **Council of Matriarchs**, the matriarchs of the Great Houses, meets there twice a year to set the protocols by which the Houses honor each other's letters of credit, to settle disputes between Houses, and to admit or expel members. Many of the same women sit in the Council of Houses, and some in the Sacred College besides, so a session of the federation and a session of the realm draw on the same grandees.

## The Houses of the City

Not every House of Bethûra holds great estates. The city's lettered Houses supply the literate functionaries a realm of clerks and qanats depends on: House Safîrôn, a middling House of Bethûra, has served the realm's offices for generations, and [[being-rshrhssfrn|Rashîra of House Safîrôn]] rose from it to keep the sealed rolls of the Purple Warrant before she fled south with them. [[being-laylamarut|Layla Marut]], heir-presumptive of House Marut, a Lesser House of the Trade-Houses, makes her home here. The thief-taker [[being-zahirmlkht|Zahira Malkhet]] works out of Bethûra, hiring her tracking and her Charíssa to the Houses, the Trade-Houses, the cantonal governesses and the agents of the capital itself.
