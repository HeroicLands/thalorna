## Overview

**Kirófa Village** is the seat of the [[affiliation-kirofatrb|Kirófa tribe]], which owes fealty to
[[affiliation-okharis|Okháris]]. It is one of the settled villages of
[[place-okharisrgn|Okháris]].

Date palms shade the village lanes down to the water, and its drying floors are covered with fruit through the end of the hot season.
