## Overview

Dóngaru Village is the seat of the [[affiliation-dongarutrb|Dóngaru Tribe]], which owes fealty to
[[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]]. It is one of the settled villages of
the [[place-okharisrgn|Okharis Region]].

Charcoal stacks smoulder in the clearings beyond the village, and its timber and charcoal go by cart to the forges of the temple-cities.
