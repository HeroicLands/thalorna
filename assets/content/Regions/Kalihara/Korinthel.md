## Overview

Korinthel is the port a foreign captain reaches with a letter and not without one. It lies a day's sail along the eastern coast of [[place-klhrcntnnt|Kalihara]] from [[place-qasirah|Qasirah]], a town of nine thousand on a river mouth under the same green ridgeline, and its harbor belongs first to the island's own coasters: the broad-beamed boats that carry terrace produce, timber and dyestuff between the ports and up the coast. A foreign hull is admitted on a factor's letter from Qasirah, lands in a quarter a fraction the size of the great port's, and loads under a warden who has fewer ships to watch and watches them more closely.

The town's merchant houses are the rivals of Qasirah's three great families, and the rivalry is the nearest thing the island has to politics a foreigner can see. Korinthel's factors hold that the gem trade is theirs by right of the rivers behind them, which bring the stones down from the highlands, and that Qasirah's houses took it by being nearer the strait. The wardens of both ports answer to the same councils and keep the quarrel to prices and letters.
