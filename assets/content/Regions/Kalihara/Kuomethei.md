## Overview

Kuomethei stands high on the southern crest of [[place-klhrring|the Ring]], below the crest line and above the cloud, on a bench of forested hillside facing north into [[place-klhrbowl|the Bowl]]. The Kaliharans call the mountains _kuomanei_ (sleeping mountain), and _kuomethei_ is the name of the place on them where the custodians of the crest go to ground. The lineage Kuomanei that holds this stretch of the Ring keeps the village and the paths along the crest to the passes either side of it.

## The Holding

The custodians who tend the wind-stones along the crest, and the pass lineages who walk them, winter at Kuomethei, since the high paths are closed in Deepwater and the village sits in reach of the whole southern crest. The custodians of the dark works live here too, in an office the Council does not name, with no work left to tend. The village is the lineage's, and the custodians are its guests.

Above the houses a grove stands over a smooth swelling out of the hillside, a work of the [[lore-theithari|Ithari]] about the height of a kneeling woman, cold in the hottest month. The lineage tends it as a grove-stone for as long as it has held the slope. The grove fruits no better than any other, and in the Deepwater rains of four centuries every hillside round Kuomethei has come down at least once, and this one has stayed.

## The Failing

Of the wind-stones along the southern crest, one stands bone-white and hard, a dead work, to be seen from the head of the stair, and another is intermittent. Storms the stones once broke now reach the southern slopes whole, and the lineage has lost terrace walls in Deepwater that stood a hundred years. The custodians of the dark works have little to do but keep the count.

## What Is Seen from It

North, down the inner face, the southern shore of [[place-klhrlake|the Bowl Lake]] and the cleared land beside it; south, the long ridges falling to the coast under canopy. Along the crest to the west a bone-white wind-stone shows on a clear day, as a pale mark on the skyline.

## The Cost of Reaching It

The village lies a day's climb from the head of [[place-klhrsthstr|the Southern Stair]] along the crest paths, which belong to the lineage Kuomanei and are walked with one of its households. In Deepwater the paths are closed and the village is cut off until the rains end.
