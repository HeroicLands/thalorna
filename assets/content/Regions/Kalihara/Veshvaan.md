## Overview

Veshvaan is the oldest and largest of the sanctuaries of the [[affiliation-tharivaan|Ithakan]], the custodians of [[place-klhrcntnnt|Kalihara]]. It stands on a shoulder of [[place-klhrmassif|the Massif]], the mount in the middle of [[place-klhrlake|the Bowl Lake]], below the summit where the Warm stands. Like every sanctuary it is seminary, archive, workshop and working site at once, and it holds the most extensive archives of inherited procedure on the island. Its deepest rooms hold the oldest of the first records: the carved slabs and inscribed plaques that the earliest Kaliharans cut to set down what they had been given. Only the silkan and the most senior kaan'thari see them.

Veshvaan also keeps the island's count of years. Each sanctuary counts in Tenth-Year Walks, and the count since the departure of the [[lore-theithari|Ithari]] is kept here.

## The Holding

The Massif and its shores are held by the lineage Marivoel (warm stone), named for the mount, whose households keep the landings, the boats and the gardens below the sanctuary. The sanctuary itself is the custodians'. The kaan'sili in training at Veshvaan come from every lineage of the island, and a child chosen for it crosses the lake and lives on the Massif for years.

## What Is Seen from It

From the sanctuary's terraces the whole of [[place-klhrbowl|the Bowl]] lies round about: the lake on every side, [[place-klhrestrrc|the Eastern Terraces]] climbing the far shore to the east and south, [[place-klhrdpfrst|the Deep Forest]] dark to the west and north, and beyond them the crest of [[place-klhrring|the Ring]] closing the view all the way round, with the passes as notches in it.

## The Cost of Reaching It

Veshvaan is reached only by water, half a day by boat from the eastern shore, and the boats are the Marivoel's. Nobody lands without the custodians' leave, and the custodians give it for teaching, for the councils they convene and for little else. No foreigner has visited Veshvaan, and most Kaliharans see it only across the water.
