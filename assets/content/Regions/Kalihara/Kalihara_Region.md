## Overview

The Kalihara Region is the inhabited country of the island of the [[place-klhrcntnnt|Kalihara Continent]], which is to say very nearly the whole of it. Every other great landmass of [[place-worldthlrn|The World of Thalorna]] is a mosaic of regions and rival powers; Kalihara carries one continuous civilization, one people, one language family and one philosophical tradition, so the island and its region are the same country under two names. The distinction is the one Kaliharans draw themselves: the region is the land as it is lived in and governed by [[affiliation-kalihara|Kalihara]], the one polity of its lineages and its ports, and the island is the landmass and the ocean approaches that guard it.

Three million people live here, and a foreigner has met perhaps thirty thousand of them, because the ports are as far as a foreigner almost ever goes. What an outsider remembers Kalihara for is the spice and gem trade of [[place-ilovanu|Ilovanu]] and the pilot who comes aboard to thread the approaches. What a Kaliharan would name first is the terraces, the custodians and the works [[lore-theithari|The Ithari]] left standing in the interior, none of which a foreigner has seen.

## Geography

The region climbs from the coast to the center of the island in steps. The coastal lowlands are jungle, mangrove and gardened village, and the four ports stand on the eastern shore and its islets. Behind them steep ridgelines under unbroken canopy close off the interior to anyone who does not know the paths. The river valleys that run down toward the sea are terraced on volcanic soil, broadest on the eastern side, and above them stands [[place-klhrring|The Ring]], a circle of tall volcanic mountains that closes the island on every side, forested to the treeline and bare along the crest, its peaks visible from the sea and effectively unreachable without Kaliharan guidance. Three passes cross it: [[place-klhrcldpss|The Cloud Pass]], [[place-klhrwstsddl|The Western Saddle]] and [[place-klhrsthstr|The Southern Stair]]. In one place [[place-klhrkthrgap|The Kuethori Gap]] breaks it, where the water of the lake goes out to the sea.

Inside the Ring lies [[place-klhrbowl|The Great Bowl]], a great bowl at the heart of the island, forested over most of its floor and terraced on the inner slopes of the mountains, with [[place-klhrlake|The Bowl Lake]] at its center and [[place-klhrmassif|The Massif]] rising from the water as a single mount. The Bowl's cleared land, [[place-klhrestrrc|The Eastern Terraces]], lies along the eastern and southern shores of the lake; its western and northern reaches are [[place-klhrdpfrst|The Deep Forest]], unbroken from the shore to the Ring. Forest covers most of the island; the cleared land is the terraces and the gardened villages.

Every stretch of the interior is held by a lineage, and a lineage takes its name from the feature it holds and tends: the kindred named for a spring keeps that spring, and the kindred named for a pass keeps the pass. A Kaliharan's lineage name says which part of the island is his people's.

The climate is hot and humid year-round, cooler on the heights, and the monsoon's four turnings order every calendar on the island. The jungle holds plants and animals found nowhere else on Thalorna, among them the [[being-mbrydct|Ember-eyed Cat]], the [[being-cnpyglr|Canopy Glider]], the [[being-slndtrtl|Island Turtle]] and the [[being-mngbhmth|Mangrove Behemoth]] of the eastern shore, with the trees and blooms of its [[lore-klhrflora|flora]], and the terraces, worked by the same lineages for thirty generations, feed the whole population without any trade in staple goods. The surrounding waters run with currents that experienced sailors call uncanny, and the Kaliharans hold that the works of the interior have something to do with them.

Beyond its mountains, its bowl and its lake, the named places of the interior are the custodians' sanctuaries, of which there are about two dozen. [[place-veshvaan|Veshvaan]], on the Massif in the middle of the lake, is the oldest; [[place-thalari|Thal'ari]] stands on the western slopes of the Ring, facing the sea, and [[place-mirthaan|Mirthaan]] in the eastern river valleys of the Bowl. No foreigner has visited any of them.

## Peoples and Culture

The Kaliharans are one people and two kinds. The Kaliharan of the interior keeps the [Three Observances]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} of [[affiliation-tharivaan|Thári'vaan]], tends a terrace, grove or stretch of shore that is his, and has probably never met a foreigner. The Kaliharan of the ports speaks three trade tongues, lives beside Vedyaran and Taruzelian families, and is almost never admitted inland either. Both hold that the arrangement is the cost of touching the world without letting it in. The culture note [[lore-kaliharanclt|Kaliharan]] sets out what they believe and what a person owes; the language is [[skill-kalihrlng|Kalihari Language]], with the harbor pidgin [[skill-valtarlng|Valtari Language]] in the ports.

## Politics and Power

The region is held by Kalihara, one polity of lineages and ports under [[affiliation-councilisland|The Council of the Island]], a single Council seated at Ilovanu and governed by layered councils whose composition no foreigner has been told. The custodians of Thári'vaan carry great influence and do not rule. There is no rival power on the island and never has been: Kalihara has never had an empire, never fought a war of conquest, and never admitted a settler it did not choose.
