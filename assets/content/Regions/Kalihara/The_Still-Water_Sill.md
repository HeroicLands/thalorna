## Overview

The Still-Water Sill lies across the bed of the river at the head of [[place-klhrkthrgap|the Kuethori Gap]], where the water of [[place-klhrlake|the Bowl Lake]] leaves the interior of [[place-klhrcntnnt|Kalihara]] and enters the gorge. It is a long, low swell of [[lore-itharisul|ithári'sul]] from bank to bank, gray and without a seam, and the river slides over it smooth. The custodians call it _ithavanu'sul_ (still-water stone), for the lake it keeps.

## What It Does

While the sill does what it does, the lake holds its level through Deepwater and does not drain in the dry months, and the terraces on its shores neither drown nor dry. Every terrace wall on the lake was built to the level the sill has held.

## The Holding

The lineage Kuethori holds the sill, and has held it since the lineage's memory begins; its village stands on pilings at the lip of the gorge, along the last quiet water above the work. The custodians of [[place-mirthaan|Mirthaan]] tend the sill jointly with the Kuethori, who walk it with them and keep the marks by which the lake's level is read.

## The Failing

The lake stands three hands above the Kuethori's marks, and the lowest terraces of [[place-koathevoi|Koathevoi]], on the eastern shore, are under water. The sill is as gray, and gives as much under the hand, as in the oldest account anyone has of it, and the river still slides over it smooth.

The Rite of Last Tending is the office for a dead work, and the custodians do not perform it over a work that still does something. So the sill is tended every morning as it always has been, with the lake three hands above the marks. The Kuethori argue, openly and often, whether a live work can be given up, and whether the lineage should be the first to say that it has. Their Elder has said she will treat with anyone who can say what the lake will do.

## What Is Seen from It

Upstream the lake lies open and still, with [[place-klhrmassif|the Massif]] standing in it. Downstream the gorge walls close in, wet and hung with fern, and a slot of sea and sky shows at the far end of the gap.

## The Cost of Reaching It

The sill is a day along the southern shore of the lake from the terrace country, at the Kuethori's own village. Below it the only way down the gorge is the ledge road, which nobody uses without a Kuethori on it, and in Deepwater the lower ledge goes under.
