## Overview

The Ring is the first thing of Kalihara a ship raises and the last thing a foreigner understands about it. Tall mountains stand all the way round the island, and their peaks, wrapped in cloud for most of the year, show from far out at sea. Nothing a foreigner sees from the water or from the quays of the four ports lies beyond them.

## The Mountains

The Ring's outer slopes fall in steep ridgelines under unbroken canopy to the coastal lowlands, and the rivers of the island rise in them and run down to the sea on every side. The mountains are volcanic, as is the island's soil. Their heights are cooler than the rest of the island, with sheer cliffs, high slopes above the cloud line and a few high meadows, where small herds of the [[being-cldgt|Cloud Goat]] keep to faces a person cannot cross. The inner face of the Ring looks down into [[place-klhrbowl|the Bowl]].

Overland travel across the Ring is impractical for anyone who does not know the paths, and the paths are the Kaliharans' to show or withhold. Almost no foreigner has crossed it, and the few the island has admitted were walked over [[place-klhrcldpss|the Cloud Pass]] blindfolded.

## The Warm

The custodians hold that the Warm keeps the Ring's mountains asleep. Nothing confirms it.
