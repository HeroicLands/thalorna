## Overview

The Eastern Terraces are the cleared land of [[place-klhrbowl|the Bowl]] of [[place-klhrcntnnt|Kalihara]]. They run along the eastern and southern shores of [[place-klhrlake|the lake]] and climb the inner face of [[place-klhrring|the Ring]] in steps of volcanic soil, wall above wall, up the river valleys that come down to the lake from the east. Most of the food the interior eats grows here, and much of the spice the ports sell.

## The Lineages

Every terrace is held by a lineage, and a hillside is worked by the same lineage for thirty generations or more. The lineage Koathevoi (dark soil) holds the largest share, along the eastern shore; the lineages above it hold the upper valleys up to the ground of the Avoluthei below the crest. The Master of the Terraces, an office of [[affiliation-kalihara|Kalihara]], answers for the terraces as a whole. The sanctuary of [[place-mirthaan|Mirthaan]] stands in one of the eastern valleys, and its custodians tend the works the terraces depend on: the wind-stones on the crest above, which send the storms down as rain, and the work at the head of [[place-klhrkthrgap|the Kuethori Gap]], which holds the lake to its level.

The lake stands higher than the terraces were built for, and the lowest of them along the eastern shore are under water.

## What Is Seen from It

From any terrace the view runs west, across the water to [[place-klhrmassif|the Massif]] standing in the middle of the lake and the dark line of [[place-klhrdpfrst|the Deep Forest]] on the far shore. Behind and above, the crest of the Ring closes the view, and [[place-klhrcldpss|the Cloud Pass]] shows as a notch in it under its cloud.

## The Cost of Crossing

The terraces are the easiest country in the interior to cross, a day or two along the shore by the paths on the terrace walls. They are also the most watched: every terrace belongs to a household, and a stranger on its wall is noticed and asked his lineage before he has gone a mile. In Deepwater the paths stay open but the walls run with water, and nobody travels who does not have to.
