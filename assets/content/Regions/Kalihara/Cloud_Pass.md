## Overview

The Cloud Pass carries the road from the eastern coast of [[place-klhrcntnnt|Kalihara]] over [[place-klhrring|the Ring]] and into [[place-klhrbowl|the Bowl]]. It climbs from the paths behind [[place-ilovanu|Ilovanu]] that a foreigner is not shown, crosses the crest in a high notch that stands in cloud for most of the year, and comes down the inner face to [[place-klhrestrrc|the Eastern Terraces]]. The Kaliharans call the pass _avoluthei_ (cloud pass), and the lineage Avoluthei that holds it is named for it. It is the busiest of the three passes, with the principal port on one side of it and the terrace country on the other.

## The Lineage and the Road

The Avoluthei keep the road: its cut steps, its rope bridges over the ravines and its shelters below the crest. They walk every traveler over the top, and they turn back anyone who comes up from the coast without a lineage's word for him. A port Kaliharan seldom goes up the road, and a foreigner almost never does.

A wind-stone stands at the crest, one of the works of the [[lore-theithari|Ithari]] along the top of the Ring: a smooth gray swelling out of the wet rock, without a seam or a mark on it. Storms that come in from the sea break on the crest above the pass and reach the Bowl as rain. Custodians out of [[place-mirthaan|Mirthaan]] tend the stone, and the Avoluthei walk it with them.

## The Blindfolded Walk

The Cloud Pass is the one way an outsider is ever brought inland. The custodians give that leave rarely, and only they give it. When they do, the Avoluthei meet the outsider at the foot of the outer face, bind his eyes with a woven band and walk him over the pass, leading him step by step from the last coastal valley to the terraces below the inner face. He wears the band until the road is behind him, and so he sees the Bowl without ever seeing the way into it. The band, the walk and the choice of who leads are the Avoluthei's custom, and no other lineage walks an outsider over the Ring.

## What Is Seen from It

Most days the crest shows nothing but cloud. On a clear morning in the dry months, the ridges fall away eastward under unbroken canopy to the coast, the harbor of Ilovanu and the sea as far as the reefs off [[place-telvari|Telvari]]. Westward the whole Bowl opens below: the terraces stepping down to [[place-klhrlake|the lake]], and [[place-klhrmassif|the Massif]] standing in the water. Herds of [[being-cldgt|Cloud Goats]] keep to the cliffs on either side of the notch.

## The Cost of Crossing

From the last coastal valley the climb to the crest takes two days and the descent to the terraces a third. The cloud wets everything, the steps are slick, and the crest is cold for people raised on the coast. In Deepwater the road is closed, and the Avoluthei stay in their villages until the rains end.
