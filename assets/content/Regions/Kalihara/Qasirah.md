## Overview

Qasirah perches on the eastern coast of the central Kaliharan islands, its harbors sheltered by natural breakwaters and blessed with trade winds that make it the primary entrepôt for spice merchants and gem traders moving goods between Vedyara, Harad, and the western realms. The city is home to three great merchant families whose warehouses overflow with cardamom, cloves, and precious stones—goods that command extraordinary prices in distant markets. Qasirah's merchant class has grown wealthy beyond measure, and their influence extends far beyond the islands, reaching into trading houses throughout the known world.

The architecture of Qasirah reflects its cosmopolitan character: whitewashed warehouses stand beside elaborate merchant palaces with courtyards designed to showcase exotic goods and host diplomatic negotiations. The city's bazaars are famous for their density of spice vendors and gem dealers, each stall carefully positioned to catch the eye of merchants seeking bulk purchases. Qasirah maintains neutral ground in all the disputes that roil the surrounding realms—a merchant city profits when all are trading, regardless of their allegiances.

The population is diverse: native Kaliharan families, long-established Vedyaran traders, Haradian ship captains, and adventurers from every realm drawn by opportunity and profit. The temples of the Aurèldían pantheon are well-maintained but unobtrusive; the true power here flows through the merchant councils that meet in the House of Factors. Qasirah's motto, carved above the harbor master's office, reads simply: "Trade, Honor, and Profit"—the three pillars upon which the city's peace and prosperity rest.
