## Overview

Mirthaan is the sanctuary of the [[affiliation-tharivaan|Ithakan]] in the eastern river valleys of [[place-klhrbowl|the Bowl]], among [[place-klhrestrrc|the Eastern Terraces]] and below [[place-klhrcldpss|the Cloud Pass]]. It trains the custodians of the works the island's food depends on: the village works of the terraces, the wind-stones along the crest of [[place-klhrring|the Ring]], on which the storms from the sea break and come down on the Bowl as rain, and the work at the head of [[place-klhrkthrgap|the Kuethori Gap]], which holds [[place-klhrlake|the lake]] to its level. The custodians of the terraces serve the Master of the Terraces, and they go out from Mirthaan to every part of the Bowl where a terrace or a stone needs them.

## The Holding

The valley is held by the terrace lineages, the Koathevoi chief among them, whose walls run up to the sanctuary's own; the sanctuary itself is the custodians'. Its kaan'sili come from every lineage of the island, and the terrace households see more of its custodians than any other Kaliharans of the interior do.

## What Is Seen from It

Mirthaan looks down its valley to the west: terrace below terrace to the shore of the lake, the drowned lowest walls showing under the water, and [[place-klhrmassif|the Massif]] standing in the middle distance. Above and behind it the crest of the Ring closes the east, and on a clear day the wind-stone of the Cloud Pass can be picked out at the top of the notch.

## The Cost of Reaching It

Mirthaan is a day's walk down from the crest of the Cloud Pass, and a day along the terrace paths from the lakeshore. The way to it is open to any Kaliharan of the interior on lawful business, and the terrace households watch it closely. No foreigner has visited Mirthaan.
