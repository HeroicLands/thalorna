## Overview

Telvari is the first Kaliharan ground a foreign ship raises and the last she is likely to set foot on. The town stands on a low islet off the eastern approaches to [[place-klhrcntnnt|Kalihara]], twelve hundred people in houses of palm thatch and whitewashed stone around a boat harbor, and its business is the sea between the islet and the coast: the reefs that show nothing on the surface and the currents that set across the wind. The pilots who take foreign hulls into [[place-ilovanu|Ilovanu]] and the other ports keep their boats here, and a ship bound for the island heaves to off Telvari and waits for one to come out to her.

The pilots answer to the Warden of the Approaches and not to any merchant, and a captain who tries to buy his way past them has made his last voyage to Kalihara. The islet has no foreign quarter and no trade of its own worth the name. Its fishermen work the reef, net round the shoals of [[being-rflntrn|Reef Lantern]]s that glow over it on dark nights, and know the [[being-slndtrtl|Island Turtle]]s that circle the island by their shells, its households keep their [sílhari]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} accounts like every household on the island, and the wardens who watch the approaches from Telvari's shore see every sail long before it sees them.
