## Overview

Kuethori is the village at the head of [[place-klhrkthrgap|the Kuethori Gap]], where the water of [[place-klhrlake|the Bowl Lake]] leaves the interior. It stands on the southern lakeshore at the lip of the gorge, a line of stone houses on pilings along the last quiet water, with the gorge wall closing the view at the far end. The lineage Kuethori (swift current) that holds the gap lives here, and the village carries the lineage's name.

## The Holding

The Kuethori hold the gorge from the outlet to the foot of the Ring, with the ledge road along its wall. At the head of the gap a work of the [[lore-theithari|Ithari]] lies across the riverbed, a long low swell of gray stone over which the water slides smooth, and the Kuethori tend it jointly with the custodians of [[place-mirthaan|Mirthaan]]. The work holds the lake to its level, and the Kuethori have held the work since the lineage's memory begins. Below the gorge the river reaches the sea through mangrove and marsh, at a mouth where no port stands and none has stood, and the lineage does not go down it.

## The Failing

Of the great lineages of the island, the Kuethori stand to lose the most by the failing. The lake has risen three hands above the lineage's marks, and the lowest terraces of [[place-koathevoi|Koathevoi]] are under water. The work across the river is as gray, and gives as much under the hand, as in the oldest account anyone has of it, and the custodians do not hold the Rite of Last Tending for a work that is not dark. The village argues, openly and often, whether a live work can be given up, and whether the lineage should be the first to say that it has. The Elder has said she will treat with anyone who can say what the lake will do.

## What Is Seen from It

The lake lies behind the village, open and still, with [[place-klhrmassif|the Massif]] in it. Ahead the gorge walls close in, wet and hung with fern, and a slot of sea and sky shows at the far end.

## The Cost of Reaching It

The village is a day along the southern shore from the terrace country, and no road goes down the gorge but the ledge, which nobody uses without a Kuethori on it. In Deepwater the lower ledge goes under and the gap is closed.
