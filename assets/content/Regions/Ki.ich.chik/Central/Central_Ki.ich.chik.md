## Overview

Central Ki'ich'chik is the heartland of Ki'ich'chik civilization, home to some 28,000,000 people, and the place where the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani priesthood]] holds its greatest influence. The fertile civilization extends southward through forests and river basins. Four realms divide the center, and each is known for one thing an outsider remembers: [[affiliation-ixilankul|Ish'ilankul]] for its plateau of temples and its high priest, [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]] for its astronomers and scribes, [[affiliation-neokbaate|Ne'ok Ba'ate]] for the isthmus that is the bridge to the south, and [[affiliation-xelem|Shelem]] for the feathers and furs of its rainforests.

## Character

No one sovereign rules the four. Each is a group of competing city-states under its own Ts'enmah, the Sun Lord, and the priesthood binds them together: one hierarchy, one calendar, one relay of runners, the [[affiliation-chumishlan|Ch'um Ish'lan]], that carries messages along the _ish'lan ch'upul_ (white roads) and across the whole region in days. Ish'ilankul sits at the center of that network, and a message from its dispatch reaches K'ankul in two days, Ne'ok Ba'ate in two to three, and Shelem in three to four.

The land changes with the realm. Ish'ilankul is volcanic highland with terraced valleys; K'ankul is limestone lowland of sacred pools, jungle and coral coast; Ne'ok Ba'ate is a narrow volcanic isthmus with two very different coasts; Shelem is river-bound jungle, cloud forest and flat-topped mountains that rise above the canopy. What moves between them is trade: cacao, jade, cotton, tropical hardwoods and exotic feathers, obsidian for the temples' blades and mirrors, and iron and steel from the southern forges, carried on the white roads and by coastal canoe.

## The Country's Quarrel

The four realms quarrel over who stands first among them, and the quarrel comes to a head once in every round of fifty-two years. When the round turns, the Presiding Ch'alish Tq'an of every city carries a coal of his city's new fire to [[place-kultaqan|Kul'taq'an]], and together they choose the Q'emul Tq'an, the head of [[affiliation-itsanihierarchy|The Itsani Hierarchy]]. The high priests of Ish'ilankul's cities have been chosen at every conclave, and Ish'ilankul's Sun Lords treat the Q'emul Tq'an as their own. The other three realms answer with what the plateau cannot do without. K'ankul computes the day-tables that every city works by, Ne'ok Ba'ate holds the one road south, and Shelem holds the feathers that every Sun Lord needs to dress his warriors for the rites. The round is near its turning, and each realm is spending what it holds to change the choice or to keep it.

## Hooks

- **The slow letter.** [Ts'ultel Ishkul K'ushi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Ts'enmah of [[place-kankul2|K'ankul]], wants a letter carried to [Ishkul Ne'ok Ts'ultel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Ts'enmah of [[place-neokbaate2|Ne'ok Ba'ate]], by any road but the fast net of the Speaking Road, because he believes the dispatch at Kul'taq'an reads more than it carries. He wants a party with no city of its own, and he pays it to say nothing of where it is going.
- **Plumes before the fire.** [Toshel Ishtem Ts'ultel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Ts'enmah of Kul'taq'an, needs new feather regalia for his warriors before the round turns, and the price of plumes is already climbing. He sends a party down the rivers to [[place-xelem2|Shelem]] with cacao, to buy quetzal feathers at the landing price before the merchant families learn who is buying.
- **The coal on the road.** The Presiding Ch'alish Tq'an of [[place-nechakalkul|Ne' Ch'akalkul]] will carry his city's coal up the white road to the conclave in a covered jar, and the cities that would rather he arrived late know which road he takes. He hires a party to walk beside his litter from the lowlands to the plateau.
