# terran_analog: the walled ceremonial precinct of Tenochtitlan and the pilgrim hostels of the Andean shrine at Pachacamac
---

## Overview

The pilgrims smell the precinct before they reach its gate. A faint warm breath, damp and sharp like struck flint, drifts down the streets of [[place-kultaqan|Kul'taq'an]] on still mornings, and the crowd moving toward the center of the city goes quiet when it meets it. Ts'omal Ch'alish, the Sacred Foundation, is the walled precinct at the heart of Kul'taq'an in [[affiliation-ixilankul|Ish'ilankul]], built around an ancient volcanic vent from which warm, mineral-laden vapor still rises. It is the holiest site on the [[place-kiichchik|Ki'ich'chik Continent]] and the supreme destination of its pilgrims. The priesthood teaches that the vapor is the living breath of [[lore-piqalkulqatdty|P'iqal Kul'qat]], the creator, who first breathed the world into existence here at the dawn of the [First Age]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"}.

## Inside the Wall

The precinct wall is plastered white and painted along its top with a band of red, and it is broken by four gates, one to each of the city's quarters. Inside lies a paved court large enough to hold a city's whole levy, with the [[place-tsultelchalish|Ts'ultel Ch'alish]], the Sacred Mountain, rising in nine tiers at its center over the vent. The court around it holds the temples of the Ten, each on its own platform and each kept by its own branch of the priesthood; the house of the Q'emul Tq'an, a long, low palace of plain stone set against the western wall; and, at the foot of the great stair, the hearth of the precinct, on which the coals of every city's new fire are laid after each [[lore-itsaniclndr#round|Living Flame]].

Only the Q'emul Tq'an, the Ch'alish Tq'an of the great temples and the most senior Ch'alishbal climb the Sacred Mountain, and the supreme rites of the [Celestial Recurrence]{.name data-type="lore/theology"} are performed at its summit. Everyone else stands in the court below and breathes what drifts down.

## The Seat of the Hierarchy

The precinct is the seat of [[affiliation-itsanihierarchy|the Itsani Hierarchy]]. The Q'emul Tq'an, the Supreme Boundary, lives within its walls with his household and his inner circle, and consecrates here every Presiding Ch'alish Tq'an of every city the cords reach. The **Keeper of the Conclave** keeps a house beside the hearth, and the **Envoy to Kul'taq'an** of [[affiliation-ishilanshulum|the Star House]] lives in another and brings the Q'emul Tq'an the house's tables.

The **Steward of the Sacred Foundation** keeps the estates of the precinct: the fields in the valleys around Kul'taq'an, the hostels on the terraces outside the wall, and the storehouses that feed both the Q'emul Tq'an's household and the pilgrims. The estates owe nothing to the city's Ts'enmah, and the Steward collects no tribute beyond them.

## The Pilgrimage

Every Ki'ich'chik person, from the highest Ts'enmah to the lowest bound laborer, is expected to make the pilgrimage to Ts'omal Ch'alish at least once. A pilgrim arrives at a hostel, is fed from the Steward's stores for three days, and on the third morning goes in through the eastern gate with an offering of cloth, cacao or blood and stands in the court at the foot of the stair. The priests at the gate tell each newcomer what is at stake: to have breathed the breath of creation in the shadow of the Ts'ultel Ch'alish is to have touched the divine, and to die without making the journey is to face the gods with an incomplete soul.

The great festivals of the calendar fill Kul'taq'an to the horizon, and the Living Flame fills it most of all. The next falls at the winter solstice of 722, and the Steward is raising new hostels on the lower slopes for the hundreds of thousands of pilgrims expected for it.

:::secret {title="The maize on credit"}
The estates of the Sacred Foundation cannot feed the pilgrims of 722. The Steward has known it since he counted the hostels he needs. He has bought the maize he lacks from the trading houses of [[place-tsamash|Tsa'mash]], on credit, to be paid from the offerings the pilgrims bring, and the knotted cords that record the debt are held by [P'enqal Ts'alam K'ushi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, eldest of the merchant guild's elders there. If the pilgrims bring less than he expects, the holiest precinct on the continent owes its maize to a merchant of Tsa'mash. The Q'emul Tq'an has not been told.
:::
