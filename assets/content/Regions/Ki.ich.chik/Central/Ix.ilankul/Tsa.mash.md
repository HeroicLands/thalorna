## Overview

In the middle of the market plaza of Tsa'mash stands a rock. It is a dome of dark gray stone, smooth and rounded, as tall as a house of three stories, and the city has never moved it. The market is laid out around it in rings, and every trader in the city gives directions from it. Tsa'mash, the Stone Heart, takes its name from the rock. It is a city of about 70,000 on the southern edge of the [[place-ixilankulrgn|Ish'ilankul Region]], one of the city-states of [[affiliation-ixilankul|Ish'ilankul]], and it is the greatest market on the plateau.

## The Market

The market meets every day, and on the market days of the twenty-day cycle, which it shares in turn with [[place-kultaqan|Kul'taq'an]], it takes over the whole plaza and the streets around it. Each ring around the rock sells one kind of thing. The innermost ring sells jade and feathers. Then come cloth, obsidian from [[place-ishtalhun|Ishtal'hun]], salt, pottery, maize and beans, and at the outer edge slaves and dogs. A trader who sets out his goods in the wrong ring is moved, and a trader who argues is fined.

Cacao is money in Tsa'mash. A load of cloth or a jar of honey is priced in beans and paid in beans, counted out by the hundred on a mat, and small change is a handful of them. The merchant guild judges its own members, and its judges sit on a platform cut into the side of the rock, where any buyer may bring a complaint against a seller. They hear it at once, standing, and their sentence is carried out before the market closes. A seller who gives short measure loses his goods. A seller who passes false beans loses his place in the market for a year.

## The Cacao Groves

South of the city the plateau falls away in ravines toward the warmer country, and in their shaded bottoms grow the cacao groves that make Tsa'mash rich. The groves belong to the city's trading houses. Their stewards live among them, and in the harvest months strings of porters climb the ravine paths with the beans in baskets on their backs, a hundred to a string. Most of the cacao drunk on the plateau, and most of the cacao spent in its markets, comes up those paths.

## Who Governs

Tsa'mash is ruled by a Ts'enmah chosen from its royal line by four P'eshalmah and confirmed by its Presiding Ch'alish Tq'an, as every city of the plateau is. Here, though, the four lords of the quarters are all heads of trading houses and Ch'eshmah of the merchant guild, and they choose the prince who will trouble them least. The other cities call Tsa'mash the merchant republic, and the merchants do not mind. The eldest of the Ch'eshmah is [P'enqal Ts'alam K'ushi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a woman of sixty who owns more cacao groves than anyone alive, sits in the shade on the judges' platform every market day and judges nobody, because the judges all owe her money.

:::secret {title="The false beans"}
False cacao is moving through the market in quantity. It is made in a workshop in the potters' ward: good beans are opened, emptied, packed with gray clay and closed with a smear of wax, so that they look and weigh almost right. The workshop belongs to a trading house of P'enqal's own quarter, and two of the market judges have been paid to look at the beans in a complaint and find them sound. A buyer who breaks a bean open with a thumbnail can see the clay.
:::
