## Overview

Ts'okilkul, the Walled City, is a coastal fortress on the sea-cliffs of [[place-kankulrgn|K'ankul]], and its ramparts are placed to command two things at once: the sea trade along the coast and the overland routes that run from it into the interior. It is one of the great city-states of [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]].

## The Harbor

Ts'okilkul's harbor is one of the two through which K'ankul's sea trade flows; the other is [[place-tzililnetzik|Ts'ilil Ne'ts'ik]], the Island of Swallows, a [[affiliation-neokbaate|Ne'ok Ba'ate]] port. Between them they handle an enormous volume of K'ankul cargo, with merchant vessels arriving with salt fish, bird feathers, exotic woods and luxury resins from throughout the Ki'ich'chik world, and K'ankul's own trade in rare books, astronomical instruments and fine carved jade going out.
