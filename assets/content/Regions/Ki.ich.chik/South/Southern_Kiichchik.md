## Overview

Southern Ki'ich'chik includes the fertile forests and river basin through which Ki'ich'chik civilization extends southward. The realms of [[affiliation-ixbaltzaku|Ish'bal Tsa'ku]] and [[affiliation-kuxiixbal|K'ushi Ish'bal]] are fully part of that civilization, with cities, agricultural estates and the priesthood's _ish'lan ch'upul_ (white roads). Farther south, beyond the basin and [[affiliation-qapteltzakl|Q'aptel Tsa'kul]], the land gives way to the wastes of [[place-xelemixbalrgn|Shelem Ish'bal]]. About 3,500,000 people live in the south.

## Character

The south descends in four steps. Ish'bal Tsa'ku is mountains, river valleys and jungle, rich in iron and precious metals, cacao and feathers, and its forges make much of the steel that Ki'ich'chik warriors carry. K'ushi Ish'bal is a thin-aired plateau of cold grasslands and a sacred lake, its cities garrison-like and its economy built around provisioning travelers. Q'aptel Tsa'kul is a poisoned threshold of fortress-temples where those bound south buy guides, provisions and blessings. Past it lie blasted rock, ash and ice, where no one rules and the priesthood only watches.

The [[affiliation-chumishlan|Ch'um Ish'lan]] thins as the land climbs and falls, and it falls silent beyond K'ushi Ish'bal: the last runner's cord is decoded at the frontier garrison city of [[place-qptlulneok|Q'aptel'ul Ne'ok]], and what lies further south depends on the courage of individual messengers. For warriors and priests the wastes are a proving ground, and surviving them is a mark of worth before the gods.

## The Road South

A traveler going south from the heartland meets the realms in order, and each step is poorer, colder and more watchful than the last.

In [[affiliation-ixbaltzaku|Ish'bal Tsa'ku]] the road climbs from the jungle rivers into the mountains. The market town of [[place-nepaxel|Ne' P'ashel]] tolls every cargo at the river junction, the forges of [[place-nekiahkul|Ne' Ki'ahkul]] make the realm's steel, the port of [[place-tzalamne|Ts'alam Ne']] sends its ships along the line of shore shrines, and the fortress of [[place-tzalamtzltl|Ts'alam Ts'ultel]] counts every load that crosses the high pass.

On the plateau of [[affiliation-kuxiixbal|K'ushi Ish'bal]] the air thins and the herds belong to the temples. The temple-city of [[place-ixkulchalix|Ishkul Ch'alish]] owns the sacred lake, and the garrison town of [[place-qptlulneok|Q'aptel'ul Ne'ok]] outfits the expeditions and reads the last cord of the Speaking Road.

In [[affiliation-qapteltzakl|Q'aptel Tsa'kul]] there are only the fortress-temples. The seat sets the fee, and the gate of [[place-xelemchalix|Shelem Ch'alish]] sends each party out alone on an opened day, after its funeral has been recited to it.

## Government

The three realms share the shape of their rule. Every town is two halves, upper and lower, each with its own lord and its own kin groups; households are counted in twenties and in four hundreds; cord-keepers knot the count, and the storehouses above each town hold its stores. Ish'bal Tsa'ku and K'ushi Ish'bal are ruled by Sun Lords who are ordained priests, and in K'ushi Ish'bal the priesthood's word prevails over the Sun Lord's. Q'aptel Tsa'kul has no Sun Lord at all, and its high priest rules it.

## The Country's Quarrel

The south quarrels with the heartland over what it pays and what it is given back. Its steel, gold, cacao and feathers go north on the white roads, its warriors and priests go south into the waste, and the priesthood levies every cargo in between. In return it has the white roads, the rites and a Speaking Road that grows slower with every stage. Ish'bal Tsa'ku's cities rival the great ones of [[affiliation-ixilankul|Ish'ilankul]], and at every conclave its high priest goes north to press for a Q'emul Tq'an of [[affiliation-itsanihierarchy|The Itsani Hierarchy]] from the south, and at every conclave the heartland chooses one of its own. The heartland also sends the south what it does not want. Debtors, failed heirs, the kin of the executed and the Blood-Denied come up the road to the garrison towns and the gate, where nobody asks who they were, and the houses they left keep asking after them.

## Hooks

- **Letters for the conclave.** The Ts'enmah of Ish'bal Tsa'ku wants the high priests of K'ushi Ish'bal and Q'aptel Tsa'kul to stand with his own at the next conclave. He sends a party to their seats with gifts, and with letters that travel by hand and not by the Speaking Road, because the dispatch at [[place-kultaqan|Kul'taq'an]] reads the cords.
- **The bill from the field.** The Keeper of the Field Ledger of [[affiliation-tsumulob|The Blood-Workers]] has brought the Presiding Ch'alish Tq'an of K'ushi Ish'bal the account of what a band spent from the city's blood on its last march south, and it is more than the city can pay this year. The high priest asks a party to find the band's survivors at Q'aptel'ul Ne'ok and learn what was spent, and on what.
- **A name set aside.** A lord-house of Ish'ilankul wants back a son who fled a debt and was last seen on the road south. It hires a party to bring him home, and the trail ends at the foot of the wall at Q'aptel'ul Ne'ok, where the Keeper of the Wall-Names of [[affiliation-wallsworn|The Wall-Sworn]] will say whether a man of that description took the wall and never which wall-name he took.
