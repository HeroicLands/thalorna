## Overview

The Drowned Pyramid, Ch'al Ne', stands in the reed beds of [[place-deadwater|the Dead Water]], weeks into [[place-xelemixbalrgn|Shelem Ish'bal Region]], where three slow channels meet in a broad pool. It is round, not square: a stack of stone rings, each narrower than the one below, like a great stepped cone. Four rings show above the water, and the brown water hides how many more go down. On the topmost ring stands a round temple of stone with a pointed roof of stone slabs, and it is the only dry roof for days in any direction.

## The Rings

Each ring is a step as tall as a man, built of blocks bigger than any raft in the Dead Water could carry. The face of every ring is carved with faces, one beside the next all the way round, wide-mouthed and staring, with their eyes cut deep. When the water clears on a still day, the faces of the drowned rings show beneath it, row below row, green with slime, until the brown takes them. A narrow stair climbs the rings on the side that faces the meeting of the channels, and runs down into the water as well as up.

## The Temple

The temple on the top is a single round room, its doorway facing the stair. Its walls are bare, and its floor is a single disc of polished dark stone, cut with a spiral that runs from the wall to a round hole in the center no wider than a hand. Water stands in the hole and does not fall or rise with the swamp. The room is dry, and cool, and the swarms that rise off the water at dusk do not come into it.

## Dangers

By day the rings belong to the swamp's beasts. The massive reptilian things of the Dead Water haul themselves out of the water onto the sunny side of the rings, a dozen at a time, and lie there with their mouths open. They are nothing like as slow as they look. At dusk they slide back into the water, and the rings and the temple are empty until the sun is well up.

A party that comes to the pyramid comes at dusk, poles in close after the last beast is in the water, climbs to the temple and leaves before the sun is on the rings. The stair is slimed where the beasts lie, and a slip from it is a fall into their water. The returners say not to sleep on the rings outside the temple.

## What Is Found There

The temple holds nothing a party can carry. The flooded rings hold doorways, seen when the water clears, low and square, opening into the pyramid below the waterline. Returners who have dived to them speak of rooms full of black water, and of something on the floor of the first room that glints.

# The Truth of the Drowned Pyramid {#truth .secret}

The beasts keep to their hours. A party on the top ring by night is safe from them, and a party still on the stair when the sun reaches it is not.

The first flooded doorway lies two rings below the surface, and it opens on a room whose floor is covered with small disks of hammered gold, hundreds of them, each pierced to be sewn to cloth, lying where the cloth rotted. A diver can bring up a handful a breath. Further in, the rooms go on and down in the dark, and the beasts den in them.

> [!WARNING]
> The pool round the pyramid is never empty, even by night. Every dive into it is a chance that something in the deep channels takes notice, and a party that dives more than a few times in a night meets it.

The water in the hole of the temple floor is clean, sweet and safe to drink, and there is never more than a cupful of it. A cup drawn out is full again by the next morning.
