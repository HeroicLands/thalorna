## Overview

The Walking Groves, Shik'tel Shilan (the groves that walk), are the kindest places in [[place-thethirst|the Thirst]]. Each is a ring of broad, hollow-trunked trees around a spring of clear water, with grass under them and shade enough for a whole party, and a traveler comes on one in the middle of the gravel with nothing to warn him of it. No grove is where the last party found it. The returners' cords give the place of every spring in the Thirst, and every account says the same of the groves: they are somewhere else.

## The Keepers

Every grove has its keepers. They are small, the height of a child, made of something like bark and something like moss, and they live in the hollow trunks and come out to look at whoever arrives. They speak in a voice like dry leaves, in the priests' tongue and sometimes in others. The returners' advice is plain: greet the keepers before drinking, leave something at the foot of the largest tree, and do not cut the wood.

Keepers are rude to the rude. A party that drinks without asking finds its water jars cracked in the morning, its rope knotted beyond untying, or its way out of the grove leading back in. A party that leaves an offering, food or a bead or a song, finds its jars full and its rope coiled, and sometimes the keepers tell it the way.

## The Directions

The keepers' directions are the only directions in the waste that hold. A keeper asked the way to a spring, a range or a place in the Thirst points, says how many days, and is right, whatever the land has done to the party's own count. The returners prize nothing in the waste more than a keeper's directions, and the gate's storehouses pay more for an account that holds them than for any crystal.

The groves do not lie on the road to anything. Every party that has found one found it by chance, usually on the day it would have died of thirst.

# The Truth of the Walking Groves {#truth .secret}

The groves move. A grove stands for a season or two around its spring and then is gone, spring and all, and stands somewhere else in the Thirst. The keepers know the whole of the Thirst and tell the truth about it, though they answer only the question asked.

> [!TIP]
> A party near death from thirst in the Thirst may find a grove on a good roll, once in a journey. Play the keepers as small, proud and fussy hosts. A party that offends them is not harmed, only hindered, and a party that pleases them leaves with one true answer about the Thirst.
