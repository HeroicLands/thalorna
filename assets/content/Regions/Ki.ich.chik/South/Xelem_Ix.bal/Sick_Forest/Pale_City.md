## Overview

The Pale City, Kulal Ch'upul, lies deep in [[place-sickforest|the Sick Forest]] of [[place-xelemixbalrgn|Shelem Ish'bal Region]], on a low rise where the great fungi thin out and the trees stand back. It is a city of stone: broad plazas, raised causeways running between them, terraced platforms, and at its center a stepped pyramid of six tiers with a temple on top. Every stone of it is wrapped in white. A thick, soft growth of fungus covers the whole city like felt, smoothing every edge, so that the pyramid looks like a hill of snow and the causeways like paths through snow running off into the green.

## The City

Under the white, the stone is carved. Where the felt has torn, or been cut away, the walls show rows of figures with tall feathered backs, coiled shapes, and blocks of interlocking squares. The felt grows over the carving so closely that its shapes show through it, softened, the way a body shows under a sheet, and on a still day the whole city looks carved of something pale and soft.

Four causeways run from the plazas into the forest, straight as cords, one toward each quarter, and are lost in the trees within a mile. The plazas are wide and flat, and in the middle of each is a square sunken court, its steps and floor felted over. The pyramid's stair climbs its eastern face.

## Dangers

The felt is a fungus like the forest's others, and it releases its spores when it is cut or trodden hard. A party that walks the causeways carefully raises a little haze about its feet. A party that cuts the felt to see the carving, or runs, raises a cloud.

The felt also grows. It grows fast enough that a party's tracks across a plaza are gone by the next morning, and a mark cut into it at dusk is smooth again at dawn. The four causeways look alike under it, and a party that came in along one finds it hard to say which. The sunken courts are the third danger: the felt stretches across some of them like a drumskin, and a party crossing a plaza in the half-light of the forest walks out onto it and falls through.

## What Is Found There

The temple on the top of the pyramid is the one room in the city with no felt in it. Its walls are bare carved stone, gray and dry, and the felt stops at its doorway as if at a line drawn on the floor. Inside lie plates of a pale green stone, carved with figures and pierced to hang, and cups and bowls of a dull gray metal that has not tarnished in the wet. Returners who have reached the temple bring out what they carry, and the gate's storehouses buy it before it reaches a northern market.

The returners say the temple is the only place in the Sick Forest where a party can sleep safely. They also say it is a long way down the stair in the morning, and that a party should mark its causeway before it climbs.

# The Truth of the Pale City {#truth .secret}

The returners are right about the temple. Nothing grows on its stone, and no spore settles there; the air inside is clean, and a party can sleep in it without masks. Why the felt stops at its doorway is not something a party can learn.

A cut in the felt closes within a night, but a mark of ash, charcoal or blood laid on top of the felt stays, and the felt grows round it. A party that marks its causeway that way finds it again.

> [!WARNING]
> The sunken court in the northern plaza is roofed over entirely by felt and holds deep standing water. A character who falls through it falls into water in the dark, under a skin of fungus that closes behind him and sheds spores every time he strikes it.
