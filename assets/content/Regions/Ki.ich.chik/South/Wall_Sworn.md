## The Wall-Sworn

A man who comes up the road to [[place-qptlulneok|Q'aptel'ul Ne'ok]] and asks for the wall is taken to its foot, where the order's dead are buried, and asked one question: whether he will hold it. He is not asked his name. If he says yes, the **Keeper of the Wall-Names** gives him a new one, a short name of the wall's own, and from that day the order calls him by nothing else. What he was in the north stays in the north.

The **Wall-Sworn**, _Sh'ulakbal_ in Ki'i'chek, those who serve the wall, are the sworn brotherhood of the secular garrison companies on the southern edge of [[affiliation-kuxiixbal|K'ushi Ish'bal]]. The companies are the garrison lord's, levied from the kin groups the Ts'enmah settles on the plateau and filled out with men from everywhere. The Wall-Sworn are the men among them who have sworn to the wall for life. They hold the towers of the garrison towns, march with the companies, and guide the expeditions that leave Q'aptel'ul Ne'ok down across the flats of [[place-qapteltzaklrgn|Q'aptel Tsa'kul Region]] to the gate of [[place-xelemchalix|Shelem Ch'alish]].

## Membership

Anyone may swear. Most of the Wall-Sworn are younger sons of the settled kin groups, who would otherwise wait their whole lives for a terrace below the walls. The rest have come from everywhere: debtors, men whose kin were executed, deserters from the armies of the north, and the Spent whom [[affiliation-tsumulob|the Blood-Workers]] have put out of their order. The Blood-Denied, whom [[affiliation-shaqalob|the Severers]] have barred from the blood and the calendar, are taken on the same terms as everyone else.

A new brother is **Wall-Bound** for a year. He serves on the walls and in the storehouses, carries stone and counts cloaks, and he is not let outside the town. At the year's end the captains decide whether he is Wall-Sworn. A brother who deserts his wall, or turns back from an escort and leaves a party on the flats, is **Off the Wall**: his wall-name is taken back, and the order tells the north which name he set aside.

The order buries its own. Every brother is owed a grave at the foot of the wall, and the order carries its dead back from the flats when it can. A death-walker of the town recites over a brother who may be recited for. Over a Blood-Denied brother, whom no priest recites for, the Wall-Sworn say his wall-name together at the grave, all of them at once, and that is the whole rite.

## Standing

On the plateau the Wall-Sworn are the soldiers nobody asks after. The settled kin groups keep their distance, the priests of the garrison towns see too many of the Blood-Denied among them to be easy, and the lords of the north count them among the garrison's stores. On the flats they are the most experienced people alive. They know the sinkholes, the boiling pools, the springs that are safe in one season and poison the next, and the paths the gate's own guides use for the last stretch. The **Sh'ukelmah** of Q'aptel'ul Ne'ok, [Tsul Ishkul]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who can say how many cloaks are in each storehouse, can also say how many of his Wall-Sworn have crossed the flats, because he counts those as stores too.

The First of the Wall is [Shelem Ishtem Tsul]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a small, bow-legged man with a voice like gravel, who has guided more expeditions to the gate than any brother living. Nobody in the order knows the name he set aside, and the Keeper of the Wall-Names says he has forgotten it.

## Rivals

**The Blood-Workers.** The order's southern garrison and the Wall-Sworn hold the same walls and march with the same expeditions, and they do not eat together. The Blood-Workers do not ask after their own Spent, and the Wall-Sworn do not tell them which brothers were.

**The Severers.** The Severers wait at the gate of Q'aptel'ul Ne'ok for every returning band's ledger, and they look hard at the Wall-Sworn who come in with it. A Blood-Denied brother may hold the wall, but the Severers hold that the sentence still stands on him, and the order holds that the wall-name stands on him instead.

**The outfitters.** The merchants who sell to the expeditions sell guides of their own as well, men who have crossed the flats once or twice. The Wall-Sworn call them the quickest way onto the flats and the slowest way back.

## What a Party Can Ask of Them

- **An escort to the gate.** A Captain of the Wall names a **Guide of the Flats** and a few brothers to take a party from Q'aptel'ul Ne'ok to the north gate of Shelem Ch'alish, for a fee paid to the Sh'ukelmah's storehouse and a gift to the order's graves.
- **Swear.** Anyone may take the wall. A party member who needs to stop being who he was can stop at Q'aptel'ul Ne'ok, and the order never asks.
- **Find a man.** A party hunting someone who came south may ask the Keeper of the Wall-Names. He will say whether a man of that description took the wall, and he will not say which wall-name he took.
- **News of the flats.** The Guides know which springs have turned, which paths have fallen in and which parties have not come back, and they tell it to a party they are about to walk with.
