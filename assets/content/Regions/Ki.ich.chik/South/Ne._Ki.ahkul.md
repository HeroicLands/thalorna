## Overview

Ne’ Ki’ahkul, "The Water House of Fire," is named for the volcanic activity that dominates the landscape and defines the city’s purpose. Built on the flanks of a semi-active volcano, Ne’ Ki’ahkul is the undisputed center of metalworking and obsidian craftsmanship in [[affiliation-ixbaltzaku|Ish'bal Tsa'ku]]. The heat and mineral richness of the volcanic earth provide the resources and energy that master smiths and craftspeople use to create items of legendary quality—from functional tools to ceremonial vessels of stunning artistry.

## Location and Geography

The city clings to the steep volcanic slopes, its architecture ingeniously adapted to the terrain. Buildings are constructed from local stone, often incorporating naturally occurring volcanic rock into their foundations. The volcanic heat is harnessed in workshops and forges; skilled craftspeople tap geothermal warmth for their work. Obsidian flows like dark glass in certain valleys and canyons near the city, making this one of the richest sources of the precious volcanic stone in [[place-kiichchik|Ki'ich'chik]].

## Craftsmanship and Economy

Ne’ Ki’ahkul is a city of artisans and craftspeople. The city's _kulishbal_, its smiths, forge iron and steel into the blades, spearheads and breastplates that Ki'ich'chik warriors take to war, and work copper, gold and silver into ceremonial objects and jewelry of breathtaking quality. Master stoneworkers and craftspeople carve obsidian into the priests' ritual blades, sharp enough to cut bone, mirrors that show a warrior’s true face, and decorative masks for priestly regalia. The guild system is strong here; master craftspeople train apprentices over many years, passing down closely guarded secrets. The priesthood maintains tight control, demanding that a portion of the finest work be surrendered as tribute.

## People and Culture

Ne’ Ki’ahkul is less cosmopolitan than [[place-nepaxel|Ne’ P’ashel]], more focused and inward. The population is primarily concentrated among craftspeople families—guilds that have worked certain trades for generations. Social status is determined largely by skill and guild rank. The city has a reputation for producing artisans of exceptional ability, and master craftspeople from Ne’ Ki’ahkul are sought throughout Ki'ich'chik.
