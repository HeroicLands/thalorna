## Overview

K'ushi Ish'bal Region is the land of [[affiliation-kuxiixbal|K'ushi Ish'bal]], and lies within [[place-southkiichchik|Southern Ki'ich'chik]] on the [[place-kiichchik|Ki'ich'chik Continent]].

K'ushi Ish'bal, the "Second Jaguar Land," sits at a threshold—one foot planted firmly in the heights and civilization of the upper southern territories, the other pointing toward the harsh, forsaken wastes that lie beyond. The region is a frontier realm, vast and relatively thinly settled compared to the dense city-states of [[affiliation-ixbaltzaku|Ish'bal Tsa'ku]]. Here, the grasslands stretch in endless waves, broken by isolated highlands and the scattered outpost-cities of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani priesthood]].

This is a land of transition and warning. The great ceremonial roads that bind Ki'ich'chik together grow less certain here, their magnificent stones sometimes cracked by forces that priests attribute to the anger of [[lore-tzuqilixbaldty|Ts'uqil Ish'bal]], goddess of death, or the chaotic wrath of [[lore-puqilchaqundty|P'uqil Ch'akun]], master of storms.

## Geography

The dominant feature of K'ushi Ish'bal is an immense high plateau that dominates the landscape—the "altiplano," where cold winds sweep across sparse grasslands dotted with hardy shrubs and low vegetation. The air grows thin here; those from the lowlands gasp for breath on their first ascent. The plateau sits beneath an open sky where the heavens seem closer, and shamans and priests travel here to commune with celestial forces.

Vast herds of llama-analogs and alpaca-analogs roam these grasslands, tended by pastoral peoples who have adapted to the high-altitude existence. Deep in the plateau lie hidden valleys where settlements cluster near freshwater springs and sheltered gorges. The great lake of [[place-ixkulchalix|Ishkul Ch’alish]] dominates the region's eastern reaches—a vast, sacred body of water said to be the eye of the world, where the boundary between the mortal and divine grows thin.

To the south and west, the plateau gradually descends, the grasses thicken into scrubland, and the temperature begins to rise. The transition is slow and uneasy—the land does not offer gentle gradations here, but rather abrupt drops, twisted rock formations, and increasing signs of the cosmic wound that has soured the very earth farther south.

## Maritime Powers

K'ushi Ish'bal holds no maritime territories in the traditional sense, but the great sacred lake of Ishkul Ch'alish is its heart and spiritual center.

The plateau itself is bordered to the north by Ish'bal Tsa'ku and to the south by the increasingly hostile terrain of [[place-qapteltzaklrgn|Q'aptel Tsa'kul]].

## Trade

Trade in K'ushi Ish'bal flows primarily north and south along the sacred roads, though the volume is far less than in the wealthier regions to the north. The pastoralist peoples herd llama and alpaca, producing fine wool that is woven into textiles and exported northward. The high grasslands produce hardy grain analogs, freeze-dried tubers, and medicinal plants said to cure altitude sickness and sharpen the mind.

The main trade goods flowing through K'ushi Ish'bal are provisions for travelers heading to and from the far south—dried meats, cold-weather cloaks, strong cords, and the dried leaf that climbers and travelers at altitude chew to breathe more easily. The great fortress-city of [[place-qptlulneok|Q’aptel’ul Ne’ok]] stands as the primary staging point for expeditions into the wastes, and merchants there profit enormously from outfitting southern-bound adventurers, priests, and warriors.
