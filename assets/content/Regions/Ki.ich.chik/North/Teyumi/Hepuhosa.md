## Overview

Every other great house of the [[affiliation-teyumi|Turquoise People]] shows a stranger a blank outer wall and a ladder he must wait to have let down. Hepuhosa, the Door House, shows him a door. It stands across the [[place-hosaoyalargn|Canyon of Houses]] from [[place-yaahosa|Yaahosa]], a block of some three hundred rooms against the south cliff, and at the foot of its outer wall is a doorway as tall as a man, cut through the masonry and never closed. About 700 people live in it and in the small houses around it.

## The Door

The door was cut at the end of the [Year of Shut Doors]{.name data-type="lore/history"}. [Hepuluma]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Rain at the Door, was this house's head. She let down its ladders and fed everyone below the walls from its storerooms, and when the rain came the next summer her kin cut the doorway so that the house would never again need its ladders let down to feed anyone.

The doorway has no door in it. When the signal fires call a southern war party, the house fills it with loose stone like any other gap in a wall, and pulls the stone out again on the morning the ladders of the other houses come down. It is the first thing the house does after a war.

Many house heads come to the Door House to swear their oath, standing in the doorway with their backs to the storerooms and their faces to the canyon, and say that their storerooms open before their chambers close. A house head may swear in her own plaza, and those of the far towns usually do. Those of the canyon, and those who want the oath remembered, walk here.

## The Hungry Coming In

The house head is [Teyuhesi]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the Corn clan, a woman of forty of Hepuluma's line. Three short summers have sent families down the roads from the [[place-wahaulahuvorgn|Cave Mesas]] and the dry towns of the west, and they come to the Door House because it is the door they have heard of. Teyuhesi feeds them, as the oath says she must. Her storerooms are half empty in the autumn, and she has begun sending her own kinsmen to the Grandmother House to ask for corn.

The Grandmother House has given it twice. Its rain chief, [Waimahalu]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, has said that the Door House is gathering every dry heart in the north into one canyon, and that the rain will not come until somebody looks at who is eating there. Teyuhesi has answered him in the one way the canyon respects, which is by saying nothing and opening another storeroom. Everyone in the canyon expects the two houses to meet over this at the canyon feast.
