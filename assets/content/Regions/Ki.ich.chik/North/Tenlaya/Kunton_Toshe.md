# terran_analog: Head-Smashed-In buffalo jump in the Porcupine Hills of Alberta, with its gathering basin, drive lanes of stone cairns, kill site and processing camp below the cliff
---

## Overview

From the camp below the cliff you hear the herd before you see it: a low rumble out on the plain above, growing louder, and then the shouting, and then the first animals come over the edge against the sky and fall thirty feet onto the bones of every herd before them. Kunton Toshe, Thunder Cliff, takes its name from that sound. It is the greatest drive on the [[place-wehioshtergn|Cairn Plain]] and the greatest on the plains of the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]]: a sandstone cliff at the head of a river valley in the foothills, where four drive lanes meet. For a month each autumn about 2,500 people from four or five bands camp in the valley below it, and the rest of the year nobody lives there at all.

## The Drive

Above the cliff, the plain rises in a long, shallow basin of good grass, where the herds come to graze in autumn, and four lanes of cairns (_wehi_) run across the basin toward the drop, closing on it like the fingers of a hand. The bands that drive together spend days easing a herd into the basin from downwind, with young men in calf skins bawling at its front. When it is deep enough between the lanes, the people hidden behind the cairns stand up in turn, the herd breaks into a run, and the hunt chief (_yuchanka_) on the last rise drops his robe as the signal for everyone to shout. A herd in full run cannot stop at the edge. A good drive at Thunder Cliff kills five hundred animals in the time it takes the dust to settle.

Below the cliff, the men finish the animals that lived through the fall, and then the women take over. The camp in the valley is a city of hide lodges for a month: racks of meat drying in strips over every fire, hides pegged out across the grass, pits where fat is boiled out of the bones, and pemmican pounded in bowls of rock. The bone bed under the cliff is deeper than a man is tall, and the bands still argue about how many herds lie in it.

## Who Drives Here

Thunder Cliff belongs to no band. The bands that want to drive here agree at the summer gathering, at the [Cottonwood Bend]{.name data-type="place/site"}, and name one hunt chief among them for the drive. This autumn's hunt chief is [Toshewiwan]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Cliff Stands, of the Cliff band, a heavy, slow-spoken man in his forties who has led the drive here twice before. The Cairn band keeps the drive lanes, restacks their fallen cairns each autumn and leads the singing over the bundle before a drive, and its bundle keeper (_chekanka_), [Mahintayu]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Sky Dream, opens the bundle at the top of the cliff on the morning of the drive.

The warrior societies of every band at the drive take turns policing it. While the herd is in the basin, nobody hunts, and a man caught stalking a bison for his own lodge has his bow broken on the spot.

## Last Autumn's Drive

Last autumn the drive here went too well. The herd that came into the basin was the largest anyone could remember, the hunt chief let too much of it into the lanes, and well over a thousand animals went over the cliff in one run. The bands could not butcher them all before the weather turned warm again, and for a week the valley below the cliff stank of rotting meat, which the wolves and the ravens took. The year-robe keepers of three bands painted the winter as the [Winter the Meat Spoiled]{.name data-type="lore/history"}.

The herds have been thin this year. Mahintayu has said that the herd went back under the earth because it was wasted, and that it will not come to Thunder Cliff this autumn unless the bands give away a robe for every animal that rotted, and she will not open the bundle on the cliff until they do. Toshewiwan has said that the herds are thin across the whole west, and that the bands that drove here last year should not be made to pay for a dry summer. Neither will drive against the other, and the bands are already gathering in the valley.

## The Contrary

Among the Cairn band's lodges this autumn is a contrary (_kewitaka_), [Kuntonsawi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Thunder Runs, a young man who dreamed of the Thunders in the spring. He says yes for no and no for yes, washes in dust and dries himself in the river, walks backward into the camp and shivers in the sun. Everyone at the drive watches him, because a contrary is one of the Thunders' people, and when he said loudly, on the first day in the valley, that the herd would certainly come and the drive would be a great success, nobody knew how to take it.
