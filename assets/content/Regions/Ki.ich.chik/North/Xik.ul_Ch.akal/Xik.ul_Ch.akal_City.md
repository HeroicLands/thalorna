## Overview

A visitor to Shik'ul Ch'akal who wants an answer from the League waits, sometimes for days, while the council talks. The village is the one the south counts as the seat of the [[affiliation-xikulchakl|Woodland League]], because the League's great council meets here: about 2,000 people behind a double palisade on a rise above a lake, in the lake-and-river country of the northeast.

## The Village

Inside the palisade stand some forty longhouses, each the home of one clan's women, their husbands and their children, with a fire for every two families down the middle and the clan's emblem carved over the door. Outside lie the fields of maize, beans and squash, and beyond them the forest the village has been cutting for firewood since it moved here. The canoe landing below the rise is never empty: canoes come in from the portages with copper, furs and dried fish, and go out again.

The village will move. When the fields wear out and the firewood is a long walk away, the clan mothers and the council choose a new site, and the whole village builds again there. The great council goes with it.

## The Council

When the great council sits, the chiefs of the League's nations and their speakers fill the council house, and the clan mothers sit where they can hear. Nothing is decided until everyone has spoken and been answered. A visitor who presses for a quick answer is told, politely, that the council has not finished talking.
