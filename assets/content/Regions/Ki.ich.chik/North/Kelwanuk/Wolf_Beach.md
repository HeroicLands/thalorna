# terran_analog: a Nuxalk village at the head of the Bella Coola inlet, where the grease trail began, with a Nisga'a eulachon fishery
---

## Overview

The smell reaches a canoe an hour before the village does. Mukwasis, Wolf Beach, stands at the head of the longest of the [[place-hapasrgn|Long Inlets]], where a river full of eulachon comes out of the mountains, and at the end of every winter its beach is lined with rendering pits where the small oil fish rot and are boiled into grease. About 1,200 [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Kelwanuk]] of the Wolf House live here in seven plank houses. Behind the village the valley climbs toward a pass, and up the valley runs the [Grease Trail]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the one road the Cedar People keep over the mountains.

## The Grease

The eulachon (_hulis_) come into the river at the end of winter in such numbers that the water turns dark with them. The village rakes them out into canoes, piles them in pits to ripen for ten days, then boils them in boxes with hot stones and skims off the grease (_melum_). The grease is poured into bentwood boxes, and a good year fills hundreds. Most of it goes down the inlet to the houses of the coast, who must give grease at every feast. The rest goes up the trail.

## The Grease Trail

In early summer, once the snow is off the pass, the carriers of Mukwasis go over the mountains with boxes of grease on their backs: twenty days' walk to the dry country of the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]] beyond, and the great salmon falls of the western bands at [Falling Water]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}. They come back before the snow with robes, dried roots and, now and then, copper. The trail is the Wolf House's: other houses' traders may walk it, but they pay the Wolf House a box in ten.

The carriers are led by [Melumniksala]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Carries the Grease, a noble of the Wolf House who has walked the trail twenty-two times and speaks enough of the Grass People's tongue to bargain without signs. The noble of the Eagle House who holds the coast's seat at the [[affiliation-redstncmpct|Red Stone Compact]] goes over the mountains with her each summer, because hers is the only road from the coast toward the Red Quarry.

## The Captive

This summer the carriers took something other than grease over the pass. The Bear House of [[place-lamekis|Lamekis]] sent a captive girl of about twelve up the inlets to Wolf Beach, taken in the oak valleys behind the Shell-Bead coast, and asked the Wolf House to sell her at Falling Water for robes. No captive has ever gone over the Grease Trail for sale. The Grass People adopt their captives and do not buy or sell people, and the Bear House wants to know whether they can be taught to.

The chief of the Wolf House, [Ulsemhakwa]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Lifts the Fire, agreed, because the Bear House is rich and its feasts are coming. Melumniksala took the girl over the pass, and she is at Falling Water now, with a price of twenty robes on her that nobody there has yet paid. Melumniksala has said at the Wolf House's fire that if the plains do not buy her, she will not carry the girl back over the pass. She has not said what she will do instead.
