## Overview

Ishochi sits on a long sand ridge at the edge of [Ne' P'uqan]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the Black Water, with the cypress swamp on one side and the pine flats rising toward the [[place-poktaskorgn|Old Ranges]] on the other. About 1,500 people live in it, the largest of the swamp-edge towns of the [[place-wikopihirgn|Dark Water]], and it is a town of [[affiliation-tokchiaska|Tokchi Aska]], the One Fire, by its own account and by the Great Sun's. The name means Ash, which is what is left in a hearth when the fire has gone out.

## The Town of the Put-Out

Ishochi takes in the households nobody else will. Putting out is the gravest punishment of the One Fire short of death: the household's fire is put out, and no town may give it a coal. A household put out in the valley or the ranges has two roads, out of the nation or into servitude, and a third that the law does not name, which is down the black-water rivers to Ishochi. The town takes the household in, gives it a coal from its own hearth, and asks it no questions until the next New Fire, when the household's head stands in the plaza and says what was done.

The heartland knows. The Great Sun tolerates the town because its scouts hold the swamp edge for him, its hunters send up margrush hides and alligator teeth in tribute, and a put-out household in Ishochi is a household that is not raiding the ranges. The Keepers call it a sin against the fire and send a Keeper every year to say so.

## The Town

The town is poor and proud of it. Its mound is low, its palisade is cypress posts carved from top to bottom with coiled serpents, and its houses stand on posts against the floods. Its people fish the black-water channels, hunt the swamp islands and farm a little maize on the ridge. Its carvers make the posts, masks and paddles that the heartland's priests buy and argue over.

The Mound Chief, [Ikoho]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, is a Noble of the heartland who was sent here after a quarrel with the Keepers and has decided he likes it. The town's real authority is [Kelkawa]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the eldest woman of its largest clan, who decides which put-out households stay, and the town's _iklilo_ (knowers), who heal, dream and read the swamp, and whom the heartland's Keepers distrust.

## Pakalo's Town

The town says it was founded by the coast women whom [Pakalo]{.name data-type="being/npc"} took down the river the night before the [Funeral of the Hundred]{.name data-type="lore/history"}. The Keepers recite that only three of the nine were caught, and Ishochi says the other six came here, down channels no heartland canoe could follow, and that every old family in the town descends from one of them. The town tells the story every New Fire, after the coal is lit. The shell towns send a canoe to hear it.

:::secret {title="Pakalo's grave"}

Pakalo is buried in Ishochi's mound. He came to the town with the coast women, lived there twenty years, and was buried high in the mound's flank, which the Keepers would call a sacrilege: a traitor sent up with the sun. His bones are in a cedar box under the Mound Chief's house, and Kelkawa and three other clan mothers know which post marks it. If the Keepers learned of it, they would demand the bones, and the shell towns would go to war to stop them.

:::
