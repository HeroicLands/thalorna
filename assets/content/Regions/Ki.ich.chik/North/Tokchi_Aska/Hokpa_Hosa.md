## Overview

Hokpa Hosa stands where two rivers meet: the great river coming down from the north, wide and brown, and the clearer eastern river coming in from [[place-hanalxilanrgn|Ha'nal Shi'lan]], whose water runs beside the brown for a mile before the two mix. The name means Two Rivers. About 6,000 people live in the town, on a bluff above the meeting of the waters, and it is the northern gate of the [[place-hokpargn|River Valley]]: anything that comes into the heartland from the north, by canoe or on foot, passes under its palisade.

The town belongs to [[affiliation-tokchiaska|Tokchi Aska]], the One Fire, and its people take the Great Sun's coal at every New Fire. They also live closer to the One Fire's enemies than any other town of the heartland, trade with them every summer and fight them most autumns.

## The Copper Landing

Below the bluff a long beach serves as the landing, and in summer it is the busiest shore in the north. Canoes of the [[affiliation-osetakona|Maple People]] come down the eastern river under the red pipe with copper, furs, maple sugar and dried fish, and the town's traders meet them with shell, maize, pots and engraved gorgets. Copper is the reason for all of it. The heartland's smiths need it, the Maple People's lake country has it, and Hokpa Hosa takes a share of every sheet that passes. The Mound Chief's own factor weighs the copper on the beach against stones of known weight, and his count is the price.

## The Town

The town's mound stands at the bluff's edge, high enough to see both rivers for miles, with the Mound Chief's house on its summit and a signal fire laid ready beside it. A double palisade with bastions encloses the mound, the plaza and the granaries, and the commoners' houses spread along the bluff top outside it among the fields. The rolling-stone yard is the longest outside [[place-hayopokta|Hayo Pokta]], and the town's champions are counted among the best in the nation.

## The Mound Chief

The Mound Chief, [Liyahi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, is a Noble of about forty who has smoked the red pipe with more Maple People than any man of the heartland. He collects the town's maize and earth, judges its clans, leads its warriors and keeps the peace of the landing. Every summer he sees off the One Fire's delegates to the [[affiliation-redstncmpct|Red Stone Compact]], who go up the great river from his landing toward the [Red Quarry]{.name data-type="place/site"}, and every autumn he sends war parties up the eastern valleys against the Maple People's new villages. He sees no contradiction in either.

## What the Town Argues About

Hokpa Hosa lives on trade with a people it is at war with, and its households argue about which matters more. The traders on the landing want the valleys quiet. The young men want war names, and the only place to earn them in the north is up the eastern river. The Keepers' house on the mound wants captives for the next Sun's funeral, and says so to nobody outside it.

:::secret {title="Raiding under the pipe"}

The Maple People accuse the One Fire of raiding Ha'nal Shi'lan under cover of pipe kinship, and in Hokpa Hosa the charge is true. Liyahi's war parties go up the valleys while their pipe kinship still runs, on the orders of the Falcon Chief, [Naksochi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who wants a war with the Maple People before the Great Sun dies. A war would bring captives for the funeral the Keepers want and a victory for the man who hopes the Sun Mother names him heir. Liyahi has worked out that a war would also close his landing, and he has not decided which of the two to betray.

:::
