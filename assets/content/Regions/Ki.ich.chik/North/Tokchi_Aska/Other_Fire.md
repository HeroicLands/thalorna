## Overview

Tokchi Hinchaka stands in the widest valley of the [[place-poktaskorgn|Old Ranges]], on a terrace above a fast green river, with the ridges close on both sides. About 7,000 people live in it and its hamlets, which makes it the largest town of [[affiliation-tokchiaska|Tokchi Aska]], the One Fire, outside the [[place-hokpargn|River Valley]]. Its name means the Other Fire. The heartland gave it that name during the Scattering, when the town kept a rival Sun's fire for three lifetimes, and the town took the name up as its own and has never let it go.

## The Town

The great mound rises in three terraces, the second-largest mound of the nation, with the Mound Chief's house on the top terrace and a temple on the middle one. A moat cut from the river runs round three sides of the town inside the palisade, and the fourth side is the river itself. The craft quarter beside the plaza is the busiest outside [[place-hayopokta|Hayo Pokta]]: smiths emboss copper with the heavy line the hill towns are known for, mica-cutters split the glittering stone of the streams into sheets and cut it into hands and falcons, and soapstone carvers make the bowls and everyday pipes the town trades down to the great river.

## The Rival Fire

At the end of the Second Gathering, when the valley towns were still burying [Lapali]{.name data-type="being/npc"} and the shell towns had stopped paying, a Sun of a lesser line, [Apiyo]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, came over the ridges with his sister and lit a fire on this mound. The hill towns took their coals from him, and for three lifetimes the Old Ranges were a nation of their own, with their own Suns, their own New Fire and their own wars against the valley. The town remembers that its Eastern Suns fed it better than Hayo Pokta had.

The Third Gathering won the town back without a war. [Tamoska]{.name data-type="being/npc"} the Gatherer made the town one concession that no other town has: at the New Fire, its coal is carried from the great mound by the town's own Keeper, walking, and never by the Great Sun's runners. The town holds that concession dearer than anything it got from the Scattering.

## The Eastern Mothers

The Mound Chief is [Lofamme]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a cautious Noble who keeps the town's tribute exact and its warriors home. The voice the town listens to belongs to [Tinkapi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, eldest woman of the house descended from Apiyo's sister. The heartland ranks her line as Nobles. In Tokchi Hinchaka her household is called the Eastern Mothers, she is served at feasts before the Mound Chief, and the town's clan mothers bring their quarrels to her before they bring them to anyone else.

:::secret {title="The fire in the hills"}

The Other Fire was never put out. A day's walk up a side valley, in a house that looks like any hill farmer's, a woman of Tinkapi's household keeps a coal of Apiyo's fire alive in a clay pot, fed every day since the Scattering, and it passes from mother to daughter. Tinkapi does not mean to use it. She means to have it if the old Great Sun's funeral splits the nation, and the hill towns' clan mothers know where it is.

:::
