## Overview

Koyalo Lintako is the last town of the [[place-koyalorgn|Shell Coast]] before the open gulf. It stands on a cluster of shell heaps among hundreds of small mangrove islands, where the coast breaks up into a maze of channels that only its own people can paddle without getting lost. The name means the Last Shore. About 2,000 people live in it, the southernmost subjects of [[affiliation-tokchiaska|Tokchi Aska]], the One Fire, and the farthest from the fire on the great mound: the Coal Bearer who brings the New Fire's coal needs eleven days by river and canoe to reach it.

## The Town

The town is a smaller Great Shell, with canals between its heaps, a summit house for its Shell Chief and a carvers' quarter, and its people live as the rest of the coast does, on oysters, clams, mullet and sea turtle. What sets it apart is its canoes. The Last Shore builds the largest dugouts on the coast, cut from whole cypress trunks hauled down from the mainland, and lashes two of them side by side under a platform of poles to make a craft that can live on the open gulf. Its captains are the only people in the north who go out of sight of land on purpose.

## The Captains

The captains of the Last Shore go south every spring, well before the storm season, following the coast and the islands farther than any heartland canoe has gone, and come back in early summer with shell, turtle, dried fish and stories. The stories are the town's chief export. Its captains tell of islands with no people on them, of towns of strangers who paint their faces blue, and of a coast farther south where the people have seen men from the south itself.

The Shell Chief, [Tiloho]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, is a former captain with one hand, who lost the other to a shark and talks about it freely. The town's best-known captain is [Chasise]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a lean, quiet man who has made the southern run nine times and tells fewer stories than anyone.

## What the Town Owes

The Last Shore pays its tribute to [[place-koyohasko|Koyo Hasko]], which sends it on up the river, and it pays in canoes as much as in shell. The great double canoes of the One Fire's war fleet on the gulf are built here, and a Great Sun who wants to move warriors by sea asks the Last Shore for them.

:::secret {title="The bell"}

Chasise has made the voyage both ways. Two years ago he followed the coast south for forty days to a river mouth where a town of fishers traded with southern merchants, and he came home with a cast copper bell of southern make, a bolt of dyed cotton and the knowledge that the southern merchants pay well for shell, feathers and slaves. Tiloho and Otoki of Koyo Hasko have kept the voyage quiet. They mean to sell the route to the next Great Sun, and the Keepers of the Fire at Hayo Pokta do not know that it exists.

:::
