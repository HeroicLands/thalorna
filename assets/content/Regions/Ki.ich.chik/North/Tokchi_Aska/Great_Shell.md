## Overview

A canoe coming in from the gulf sees Koyo Hasko as a long white hill rising out of the mangroves: an island of shell heaped up over generations until its summit stands higher than the trees, with houses on its terraces and smoke along its ridge. The name means the Great Shell. About 5,000 people live on it and on the smaller heaps around it, the largest town of the [[place-koyalorgn|Shell Coast]], and its Shell Chief is first among the chiefs of the coast of [[affiliation-tokchiaska|Tokchi Aska]], the One Fire.

## The Heap and the Canals

Every part of the town is made of what its people ate. The great heap is shaped into ramps and terraces, with the Shell Chief's house on its summit, a temple beside it, and the houses of the town's leading families on the terraces below. A canal wide enough for two dugouts abreast cuts straight through the island from the bay to the open water, and smaller canals branch from it to every quarter, with walled basins of shell where canoes tie up and fish are kept alive in the water until they are eaten. The town's children learn to paddle before they learn to walk far.

The carvers' quarter lies along the main canal. Its craftsmen cut masks, animal heads and canoe figureheads from cypress, paint them and inlay their eyes with shell, and no one else in the north carves wood as well.

## The Shell Chief

The Shell Chief is [Otoki]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a broad, slow-spoken man of about fifty whose family has held the summit house for as long as the coast remembers. Thirty smaller towns send him tribute, and he sends part of it up the river to [[place-hayopokta|Hayo Pokta]] in shell, dried fish and canoes. At every New Fire he stands on the summit before the town and recites the [Shell Promise]{.name data-type="lore/law"} word for word before he takes the coal, with a Keeper sent down from the great mound standing beside him to say that the promise is kept. Otoki recites it louder than any Shell Chief on the coast.

## The Sun's Pledge

A house on the second terrace belongs to the Suns. Since the Shell Promise was made, the Sun Mother has sent one boy of the Suns to be raised in Koyo Hasko as the pledge of it, and he lives there from his seventh year to his twentieth, eats the coast's fish and learns to handle a canoe on the gulf. The pledge before the present boy, [Sohiyo]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, is now a man of thirty and back on the great mound, a son of the Sun Mother's sister and one of the Suns she can choose from. The coast calls him its own Sun, and he speaks Tokchili with a salt accent.

## The Shell Fair

In late winter the heartland's traders come down the river with maize, copper and pottery, and the coast's canoes bring shell, dried fish, carved wood and turtle shell to Koyo Hasko for the [Shell Fair]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}. For a month the canals are jammed with canoes, the heaps are covered with traders' shelters, and the shell towns' chiefs meet in the summit house to settle their quarrels and decide what they tell the Great Sun.

:::secret {title="The coast's candidate"}

The Shell Chiefs mean to make Sohiyo the next Great Sun. They have told the Sun Mother through his mother that the coast will keep taking the coal if he is chosen, and that it will take the coal from nobody if the old Great Sun is buried with a single coastal attendant. Otoki also knows what the captains of [[place-koyalolint|Koyalo Lintako]] found down the gulf, and he means to offer that route to Sohiyo, and to no one else, for a lighter tribute.

:::
