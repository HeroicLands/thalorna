## Overview

Kiyo Pokta is the town on the western bank that faces the grass. About 5,000 people live inside and around its two palisades, on a terrace above the [[place-hokpargn|River Valley]] where the bluffs give way to prairie, and from the summit of its mound a watchman can see the smoke of a plains camp a day before its warriors arrive. The name means the Falcon Mound. It is the muster town of [[affiliation-tokchiaska|Tokchi Aska]], the One Fire, against the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]], and the home of the falcon dancers.

## The Walls

Kiyo Pokta is the only town of the One Fire with two palisades, one inside the other, with a dry ditch between them and square bastions every bowshot along both. The inner wall encloses the mound, the plaza, the granaries and the falcon house; the outer wall encloses the commoners' houses and the nearer fields. A plains war party on foot can burn the outer fields and drive off anyone caught in them, and in most years one does. It has never taken the inner wall.

## The Falcon House

On the plaza's west side, facing the grass, stands the falcon house: a long council house with a copper falcon over its door, where the falcon dancers keep their headdresses and cloaks and young men of the western towns come to learn the dance and the war club. A boy who has taken his first captive is brought here by his mother's brother, painted, and taught the steps of the bird-man who strikes down the enemies of the sun. Before a war the dancers go out through the plaza in copper-plated headdresses and falcon-feather cloaks, and the whole town comes to watch.

When the Great Sun calls a war against the plains, the Falcon Chief, the _Kiyo Imala_, comes to Kiyo Pokta to raise it. The present Falcon Chief is [Naksochi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Great Sun's sister's son, a hard, popular man of about forty-five who has led three wars west and spends more of the year here than at [[place-hayopokta|Hayo Pokta]].

## Trade and Raids

The town fights the plains and feeds on them. Every autumn, under the red pipe, the plains bands bring robes, dried meat, fat and hides to a trading ground below the outer wall, and the town's women trade maize, beans, pots and tobacco for them. Every spring and summer, when the pipe kinship has lapsed, young men of the bands come back to take maize, captives and names. The town's own young men go out the other way for the same reasons. Captives cross the grass in both directions, and some of the town's households have a grandmother who was born in a plains lodge.

The Mound Chief, [Ikinko]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, is an old warrior who knows the plains band leaders by name and wants the autumn trade more than another war. He and Naksochi are civil to each other in the falcon house.

:::secret {title="The Falcon Chief's war"}

Naksochi wants a war, and wants it before the Great Sun dies. A victory would make him the obvious heir when the Sun Mother chooses, and captives from it would give the Keepers the following they want for the old Great Sun's funeral. He is pressing a war on two fronts: west against the plains, which Ikinko is quietly resisting by warning the band leaders he trades with, and north into Ha'nal Shi'lan through the war parties of [[place-hokpahosa|Hokpa Hosa]]. If either war starts, the shell towns read it as a funeral being stocked.

:::
