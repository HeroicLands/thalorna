## Overview

The Shell Coast (_koyalo koyoli_) is the gulf shore of [[affiliation-tokchiaska|Tokchi Aska]], the One Fire, at the southern end of the [[place-tokchiaskargn|Tokchi Aska Region]]. About 220,000 people live on it, in shell towns on the islands and the estuary shores, and they have never farmed. They are the _koyoli_, the people of the shell: carvers, fishers and the north's only seafarers on the gulf.

The coast belongs to the One Fire on terms. Its towns take the Great Sun's coal at the New Fire and send tribute up the river, in shell, dried fish and canoes and never in people. That last condition is the [Shell Promise]{.name data-type="lore/law"}, and the coast's Shell Chiefs recite it at every New Fire before they take the coal.

## Geography

The land runs out gradually. South of the [[place-hokpargn|River Valley]] the great river breaks into a delta of channels and marsh, and beyond the delta the shore runs on for weeks of paddling: salt marsh and mangrove, broad shallow bays, and a chain of low barrier islands with the open gulf beyond them. The water inside the islands is warm, shallow and astonishingly rich. Oyster reefs, clam beds and seagrass meadows feed mullet, sea trout, sheepshead and sea turtles, and the seabirds nest on the islands in tens of thousands.

Every shell town stands on its own leavings. Generation after generation has eaten oysters and clams and thrown the shells on the heap, and the heaps have grown into islands of shell, some of them higher than a heartland town's mound. On the largest, the towns have shaped the heaps on purpose, with ramps, terraces, flat summits for the chiefs' houses and canals dug between the heaps, so that a canoe can reach every house from the open water.

## Storms

The gulf is calm most of the year and deadly in late summer. The storms that come off it can flatten a shell town's houses in a night and drive the sea over the low islands, and the towns build their chiefs' houses and their storehouses on the summits of the heaps for that reason. The shell towns read the sky and the birds for warning, and a canoe captain who is caught on the open water in a storm season is not expected back.

## The Towns

The coast has some fifty shell towns of a few hundred to a few thousand people, each under its Shell Chief, the _Koyo Imala_, and the greater chiefs collect tribute from the lesser. The greatest of them is [[place-koyohasko|Koyo Hasko]], the Great Shell, where the [Shell Fair]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} is held every late winter and where the coast's chiefs meet. The farthest south is [[place-koyalolint|Koyalo Lintako]], the Last Shore, whose captains say they have sailed farther down the gulf than anyone.

## What Moves Along the Coast

The coast's canoes are its roads. Heavy dugouts, some lashed in pairs under a platform, carry dried fish, shell and carved wood between the towns, and the larger ones go up the delta and the great river to the heartland. Whelk and conch shell is the coast's great export: the heartland's engravers cut it into gorgets and cups, and a Noble's chest is measured in it. In late winter the heartland's traders come down the river with maize, copper and pottery to the Shell Fair. The south lies far down the gulf beyond the Last Shore, and the coast's captains argue over how far.
