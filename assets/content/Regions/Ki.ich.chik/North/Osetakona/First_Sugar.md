# terran_analog: an Onondaga or Oneida village of the sixteenth century, set on the upper St. Lawrence rapids

## Overview

First Sugar has moved six times in eleven generations, and it has never moved out of sight of one stand of maples. The village is the chief village of the Rapids People, about 1,800 people behind a palisade on a terrace above the rapids of the [Great River]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, in the [[place-kahsiotargn|Falls Country]]. Its name (_tsinikwen kasiwa_) is the name of the sugar made in that stand in the thaw when the Grove began, and whichever clearing the village moves to, it keeps the name and keeps within a walk of the trees.

## The First Grove

The stand is the [First Grove]{.name data-type="place/site"} (_tsinikwen osetaten_), a few hundred sugar maples on a slope above the river, some of them so old that three men cannot reach around them. The rememberers say that [Siwenkatiro]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Sweet Words, carried his kettle of the Falls People's sugar into this stand and set it down before the Rapids People's clan mothers, and that the oldest trees here were tapped that spring.

The First Grove is tapped every thaw by the Rapids People and the Falls People together, the only sugar bush in the Grove that two nations share. The clan mothers of both tap the first tree on the same morning, the first sap is boiled in one kettle, and the sugar from it is carried to the Kettle for the opening of the great council. No village is ever built in the stand, and nobody cuts a tree in it for any reason.

## The Village

The village stands above the rapids, and the rapids are its living. Every canoe going up or down the Great River has to be walked down the white water on lines, or carried around it on the portage path along the bank, and the young men of First Sugar do the carrying. They are fed for it and given gifts, which the Rapids People do not call payment. In return the village sees every canoe on the river and hears every piece of news before the Kettle does.

Inside the palisade stand thirty-five longhouses and a council house. Below the village, at the foot of the rapids, the Rapids People keep eel weirs and fishing stages, and in the autumn run the whole village goes down to the river.

The village's rememberer is [Ohnakensakwenta]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Remembers the Corn, an old blind man of the Crane clan who is said to know the Founding better than any rememberer at the Kettle. The other rememberers come to First Sugar to check their recitations against his, and he sends them home corrected.

## What the Village Wants

The Rapids People are the Grove's second nation, and they are tired of being second. The great council has sat in the Falls People's villages for most of eleven generations, and the Kettle is about to move again. The Dawn People want it moved to the sea, the Falls People want it to stay among their own villages, and the clan mother [Osetarahion]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of First Sugar has offered her village, which stands between the two, beside the First Grove where the Grove began. She has sent sugar from the First Grove to every clan mother of the Falls People with the offer. None has answered, and the Rapids People know that a Falls clan mother who does not answer has not said no.
