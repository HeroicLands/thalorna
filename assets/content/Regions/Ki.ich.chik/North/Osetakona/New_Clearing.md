## Overview

New Clearing is the farthest south any village of the Grove has ever stood. It sits on a bend of a river in the [[place-tionhsekargn|Southern Rivers]], where the valley opens into the bottomlands of [[place-hanalxilanrgn|Ha'nal Shi'lan]], a few days' paddle from the northern towns of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]]. About 600 people of the River People live in it. The name (_tsiontaha tahsiren_) means only what it says: the clearing was new when the village moved here five years ago. Two autumns ago a war party of the One Fire burned it, and the River People rebuilt it on the same ground the next spring, and kept the name.

## The Village

New Clearing is built for war. Its palisade is double, with an earth bank between the rows and bastions at the corners where archers can shoot along the walls. Its eighteen longhouses stand close together inside, and its storage pits are inside the palisade as well, where most villages of the Grove keep them outside. The fields outside are the best soil any River People village has farmed, black bottomland that grows maize taller than a man, and the women work them with the young men posted on the ridges above.

The burn still shows. The palisade stakes on the south side are new, the council house is new, and the ground where four longhouses stood is a row of fresh graves. The village lost fourteen people in the raid and three more were carried south as captives.

## The War Leader and the Clan Mother

The village's war leader is [Tiahwentahkon]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Otter Comes, a man of thirty who led the party that followed the raiders south and brought back two of the three captives. He wants a mourning war for the dead, a large one, raised from every River People village, and he has asked the River People's council for it twice.

The clan mother of the Sturgeon clan, [Otsitsentiotenha]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Sturgeon Waits, has refused to consent, and without the consent of the clan mothers whose names it is meant to raise, no mourning war goes out. She says the village came too far south, and that the clan mothers who chose this site chose it for the soil and forgot whose land it was beside. Tiahwentahkon answers that she chose it herself. Both are right, and the village knows it.

## What the Village Argues About

The raiders came in autumn, after the pipe kinship of the summer had lapsed, and the One Fire calls the raid a fair act of war against a village built on its land. The River People call it a raid under cover of the pipe, because the war party's men had traded at New Clearing's landing that summer and seen its walls. Both accounts are told at every council of the [[affiliation-redstncmpct|Red Stone Compact]].

Inside the Grove the village has a second grievance. The war clubs that burned New Clearing were headed with copper from [[place-kahnekwaroten|Hammerstone]], and the River People's speakers have asked the great council to stop the copper going south. The Copper People have refused.

:::secret {title="The third captive"}

The third captive taken from New Clearing, a girl of twelve of the Sturgeon clan, was not brought back because she would not come. She is Otsitsentiotenha's sister's daughter. A household of the One Fire's northern landing has taken her in as a daughter, and she has sent word north with a trader that she is well and means to stay. Otsitsentiotenha knows this and has told no one, because a mourning war would be fought against the town where her sister's daughter lives.

:::
