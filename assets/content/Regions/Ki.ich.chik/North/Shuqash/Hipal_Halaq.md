## Overview

Where the Bead Coast turns north, a high headland of dark rock runs out into the open ocean, and the wind never stops. On the sheltered side of it, above a small cove, stands Hipal Halaq, the Crossing Shore, a town of about 600 people. Every one of the [[affiliation-shuqash|Shuqash]], the Shell-Bead People, expects to pass through it once, three days after death, on the way to the Far Island.

## The Point

A path runs from the town along the top of the headland to its tip. Halfway along it is a pool in the rocks where the souls are said to bathe, and at the tip is a flat ledge above the surf, the place where the souls go down to the canoe of the dead. Sea lions haul out on the rocks below the ledge and bark day and night. The people of the [[place-shuqhalaqrgn|Bead Coast]] hold that they are the souls who could not pay their fare, and nobody hunts them here.

The living go out to the ledge for the [Feast of Strands]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}. Families from every town bring beads, the astronomer-priests sing the names of the dead, and for each name they were paid to send they throw a strand from the ledge into the surf. The town feeds and houses the mourners, and the mourners pay for it.

## The Town

Hipal Halaq is a fishing town that lives on the dead. Its chief, [Tiyuplesh]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Sky Running, keeps the path, the pool and the ledge, and takes a bead in ten of every mourner's gift, as any chief takes from a cargo landed on his beach. The astronomer-priests keep a house at the landward end of the path, and the town's best houses stand nearest it.

:::secret {title="The strands in the surf"}
The strands thrown from the ledge land on a rock shelf that the lowest tides of the year uncover. Every winter the town's divers bring up what the surf has not scattered, and the chief and the astronomer-priests of the house divide it. Tiyuplesh holds that the strands have already paid for the crossing by the time they reach the shelf, and that what the sea leaves is the town's. The Sky Elder knows of the practice and has not stopped it. The dives are made at night, and the secret is the town's most valuable possession: the Feast of Strands, and the priests' fees for it, depend on every family believing that its strands went across with its dead.
:::
