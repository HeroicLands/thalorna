## Overview

A traveler coming down from the pass in autumn hears Tulishwil before seeing it: the beat of stone pestles from a granite outcrop at the edge of town, where thirty women sit at once over the mortar holes worn into the rock and pound acorns. Tulishwil, Oak Place, is the largest town of the [[place-tulishlumuqrgn|Oak Valleys]], about 1,500 people at the mouth of a canyon where a creek leaves the foothills, with oaks all around it and the reed marshes of the valley floor below. It is the first town the coast reaches over the pass, and the one where the [[affiliation-shuqash|Shuqash]] of the coast and of the valleys meet.

## The Granaries

The town's wealth stands on legs. Every household keeps one or two acorn granaries, woven bins as tall as a man on stilts beside the house, and the headman keeps a dozen. In a good year the granaries hold two winters of acorns, and in a bad year the town still eats. The town's women weave the watertight baskets the coast prizes, coiled tight and patterned in black and red, and the best of them sell on the coast for a strand each.

Tulishwil has no rich chief. Its headman, [Ayullesh]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Moon Running, holds his place because his family owns the best oak groves on the canyon slopes and because he speaks for the valleys at the gathering. His storehouse holds acorns, deer meat and baskets, and few beads.

## The Acorn Gathering

In autumn, when the acorns fall, the coast families come over the pass with beads, dried fish, shell and tar, and the town fills with their camps for a month. The [Acorn Gathering]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} is a market, a festival and a marriage fair at once: acorns and baskets go to the coast, beads come to the valley, the young men play the hoop-and-pole game for strands on the flat by the creek, and the families of coast and valley arrange their marriages. The coast sets the prices, and the valley grumbles about them every year.

## The Painted Cave

An hour's climb up the canyon, under an overhang of sandstone, is [Tishal Hapuq]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the Painted Cave. Its walls and ceiling are covered with the astronomer-priests' paintings, wheels, suns and sky beings in red, black and white. The priests come up from the coast at midwinter to paint and to take the dream drink there, and the town feeds them. Nobody from the town goes into the cave, and the priests pay the headman a strand each winter for the climb.

On the ridge across the canyon stands the first watch fire that [Kowiqnaq]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Acorn Seed, built after the [Pointing]{.name data-type="lore/history"}. A boy of the town keeps it ready from spring to autumn.
