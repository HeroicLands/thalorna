## Overview

Ha'nal Shi'lan is where the goods of the eastern north change hands. The largest settlement of the [[place-hanalxilanrgn|Ha'nal Shi'lan Region]], about 1,200 people, stands on a bluff where two rivers meet, in a frontier that the [[affiliation-xikulchakl|Woodland League]] and the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]] both claim and neither holds. Neither nation governs it: the [[affiliation-hanalxilanmktcncl|Ha'nal Shi'lan Market Council]], made of its trading families and the elders of its quarters, does. Its families are a mixture of both, with adopted captives, plains traders who stayed and people whose villages were burned, and they keep the place alive by being useful to everyone.

## Why Traders Come

League canoes come down the rivers with copper, furs, maple sugar and dried fish. One Fire traders come up from the south with shell, engraved copper plates and fine pottery, and Grass People bring hides and robes overland from the west. The settlement's families feed them, store their goods, broker their bargains and take a share. The rare southern good that has come this far, a cacao bean or a macaw feather passed from hand to hand out of the Mesa Towns, fetches more here than anywhere else in the east.

## The Valley

The floods leave rich silt on the bottomlands, and in spring the bottoms become shallow swamps thick with waterfowl and fish. In summer and autumn the water recedes and leaves open meadow and maize fields. The spawning runs bring people from every direction, and the war parties of both claimants come with them.
