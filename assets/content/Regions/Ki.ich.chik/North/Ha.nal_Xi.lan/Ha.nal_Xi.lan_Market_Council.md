The **Ha'nal Shi'lan Market Council** governs [[place-hanalxilan2|Ha'nal Shi'lan]], the river town that the [[affiliation-osetakona|Maple People]] and the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]] both claim. Its seats belong to the heads of the trading families and the elders of the town's quarters, and nobody sits on it for either nation. The town's own people are a mixture of both, with plains traders who stayed and people whose villages were burned. The Maple People and the One Fire each send gifts and envoys and each hopes to be the town's patron, and neither owns it. Southern merchants trade in the market and sit on no council.

## A Market Day

The Weigher sets up his scale at dawn on the landing, and the Market Warden's watchmen take every knife and war club into keeping until sunset. The Family Heads assign the stalls and the storage sheds, settle who owes the landing fee, and hear the small complaints of the morning: a short measure, a spoiled bale, a canoe moored across another's. A Quarter Elder settles what her own people can. Whatever she cannot goes to the council that evening.

## Lake and River Traders

When a canoeman of the Maple People and a One Fire factor quarrel over a bargain, the council seats no one from either people. Three Family Heads whose families have no stake in the goods hear both men, the Weigher gives his count, and the heads award the price. The loser pays in goods, and the town stands surety so the winner cannot be cheated on the road home. A trader who ignores the ruling finds every shed in the town closed to him. Both nations accept this, because a market that cannot be trusted would empty within a season.

## What It Fears

The council fears the day a war party arrives with a claim to the town. The Market Warden's watchmen are few, and the town has no walls worth the name. It also fears a patron: the nation that gets to pay the Weigher and the watchmen will soon give the orders, and the other will burn the town to take it back.
