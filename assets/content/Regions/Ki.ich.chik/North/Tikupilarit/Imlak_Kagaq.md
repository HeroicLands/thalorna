## Overview

Imlak Kagaq, Whale Point, stands on a long gravel spit at the northern tip of the [[place-tikumanikrgn|Ice Coast]], where the land turns east along the northern ocean. About 450 people live in it, in sod houses framed with whale jaws and ribs, around two men's houses, the only village on the coast with more than one. A row of whale skulls lines the beach, one for every whale the village has taken in living memory, and the cemetery behind the village is fenced with jawbones.

## The Whale

In spring, when leads open in the ice off the point, the great whales pass north and east along them. Whale Point's skin boats wait at the ice edge with their crews of eight, and when a whale surfaces in a lead the boats go after it, harpoon it with floats, and kill it with lances. A whale feeds the village for a year. Its meat and blubber are shared by fixed rules: each crew that struck it takes its share, then every house in the village, then the widows and orphans of the villages along the coast, who send sleds for it. The boat-holder's wife gives the whale its drink of fresh water on the ice before it is cut.

The boat-holders (_kalviaqtiq_) of Whale Point are the richest men on the coast, and the men's houses here are each the house of a few of them and their crews. The talk in them is less equal than anywhere else on the coast.

## The East

Whale Point is where the East People came from. Its crews have followed the whales east along the northern ice every spring for three generations, and many have not come home: they winter in the east and stay. The village's boat-holders count it a blessing, proof that the whales chose them. Its leading boat-holder, [Tikumalu]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Ice Running, is a great-grandson of [Imlakaklu]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Whale Diving, the boat-holder of the [Taking]{.name data-type="lore/history"}. He sends two boats east every spring, and the Asking of his men's house dances Imlakaklu's mask.

The descendants of the two children [Kilnaqivi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Star Giving Drink, brought back from the east sleep in the other men's house. They do not dance Imlakaklu's mask, and at the Asking their men's house is silent while the other one sings it.
