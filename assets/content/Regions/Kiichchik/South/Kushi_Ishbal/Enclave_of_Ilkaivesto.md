## Overview {#overview}

The **Enclave of Ilkaivesto** is the community of about a hundred and ten of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]] at [[place-ilkaivesto|Ilkaivesto]], the southernmost of the three conclaves they keep in [[place-kiichchik|Kì'ìch'chik]]. It lies in the great swamp of the lowland below the plateau of [[affiliation-kuxiixbal|K'ushi Ish'bal]], the last green country before the waste of the [[place-xelemixbalrgn|Shelem Ish'bal Region]]. Neither the Ts'enmah nor the temples of the plateau have ever sent anyone to it, and it has never come up to the plateau.

## How It Is Ruled {#how-it-is-ruled}

A Senalo of Ilkaivesto belongs to a hearth the conclave keeps. The [[lore-senaliclt|Senali]] raise a lord for things done, by every voice, and the conclave raises from among its lords a Keeper of the Marsh, the _hemmikanno_, who holds the deep swamp and answers for its herons. A lord's standing is a rank and not a holding. One the Keeper drives out is _vyerka_, an outlaw, given no standing or shelter.

The conclave has no dealings with the plateau to govern. What the Keeper rules on is what the swamp does with the people who come into it.

## Current Situation {#present}

Traffic toward the waste presses on the swamp's edge. Expeditions look for a way around the fee at the gate of [[place-xelemchalix|Shelem Ch'alish]], and a feather-trader's price for white heron plumes sends the rim hunters into the deep swamp, where the best plumes grow.

:::secret {title="What the Keeper has not decided"}
The Hemmikanno has had one rim hunter in the conclave's keeping since he followed the plumes in, fed and left to sleep until the price in his head has faded. He is set down at a channel the rim hunters know, days late and empty-handed, as the others have been. The Keeper has not decided what to do about the trader, and the hearths that hold the herons as kin want more from him than the plumes back.
:::

## Defense {#arms}

Walls and armies do not protect a Senali conclave. The land does. A canoe that turns toward the deep swamp comes out of the reeds into a lagoon it has already crossed, and nobody has seen a dwelling there. The herons rise from it at evening in flights larger than anywhere else on the lowland.

## The Deep Swamp {#holdings}

The conclave's country is the deep of the swamp east of the plateau, where the rivers spread in the wet season into one sheet of water broken by islands of trees and floating meadows of weed. The Senali build there as they build everywhere, by singing and growing what they need and raising nothing in stone.

## History {#history}

The Senali kept the deep swamp before the plateau's people reached its rim. The rim hunters say that it belongs to the tall people and that a hunter who follows the herons inward comes home empty-handed and days late, and that nothing worse happens to him.

## Relations {#relations}

- **[[affiliation-kuxiixbal|K'ushi Ish'bal]]** leaves the deep swamp alone. The water priestesses of [[place-ixkulchalix|Ishkul Ch'alish]] count it among the lowland's waters and hold no rite in it.
- **The rim hunters** go down in the dry season for feathers, hides and fish, keep to the swamp's edges, and say the deep belongs to the tall people.
- **[[affiliation-nclvlrnst|Lirnasto]]** and **[[affiliation-nclvhrsllnk|Hersellonku]]**, the conclaves of the centre and the north, keep their own counsel, and the three take no part in one another's affairs.
