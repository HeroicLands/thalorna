**Ilkaivesto**, the place of herons in the [[skill-senalilng|Senali language]], is the southernmost of the three conclaves the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]] keep in [[place-kiichchik|Kì'ìch'chik Continent]]. It lies in the [[place-kuxiixbalrgn|K'ushi Ish'bal Region]], in the great swamp of the lowland east of the plateau, where the land falls away below the sacred lake into slow rivers and standing water. South of the swamp the waste of [[place-xelemixbalrgn|Shelem Ish'bal Region]] begins. Nobody outside it has counted the Senali there. They form the [[affiliation-nclvlkvst|Enclave of Ilkaivesto]].

## What a Traveler Finds

The swamp is the last green country before the waste, and it is green to excess. In the wet season the rivers spread over the lowland until it is one sheet of water broken by islands of trees, floating meadows of weed and channels deep enough for a canoe. In the dry season the water draws back into lagoons and the herons, storks and spoonbills crowd onto the mud in thousands. The water here is good to drink, and the swamp's beasts are the ordinary caiman, snakes and fish of any lowland river.

A traveler can pole a canoe along the swamp's western channels for days. The Senali are somewhere in the deep of it, and a canoe that turns toward them comes out of the reeds into a lagoon it has already crossed. Nobody has seen a dwelling there. The plateau's people know the deep swamp by its herons, which rise from it at evening in flights larger than anywhere else on the lowland.

## The Plateau's People

The people nearest the swamp are the hunters and fishers of the plateau's eastern rim, who go down in the dry season for feathers, hides and fish and keep to the swamp's edges. They say that the deep swamp belongs to the tall people, that a hunter who follows the herons inward comes home empty-handed and days late, and that nothing worse happens to him. They leave the deep swamp alone. The water priestesses of [[place-ixkulchalix|Ishkul Ch'alish]] count the swamp among the lowland's waters and hold no rite in it. Neither the Ts'enmah of [[affiliation-kuxiixbal|K'ushi Ish'bal]] nor the temples have ever sent anyone to the Senali, and the Senali have never come up to the plateau.

## The Present {#present}

Traffic toward the waste presses on the swamp's edge. Expeditions outfitting at the plateau look for a way around the fee at the gate of Shelem Ch'alish, and a feather-trader's double price for white heron plumes sends the rim hunters farther into the deep swamp than they would otherwise go. The rim hunters take the plumes from the edges, and the best plumes grow in the deep.

The swamp keeps its rule for those who go in after them: a hunter who follows the herons inward comes home empty-handed and days late, and nothing worse happens to him. The rim hunters say so with some pride, and a few of them have begun to say it with doubt.

:::secret {title="The hearths among the herons"}
About a hundred and ten Senali live in the deep swamp, in dwellings raised on the tree islands and woven of living reed, around a lagoon where the herons roost in the thousands. The Hemmikanno, the Keeper of the Marsh, governs the conclave and answers for the herons, which the Senali hold in a kind of kinship that they will not explain.

The man who followed the trader's plumes is in the conclave's keeping. He walked into a hearth's lagoon at dusk behind a white heron, and the Keeper has had him fed and left to sleep until the price in his head has faded. He will be set down at a channel the rim hunters know, days late and with nothing in his hands, and will say, as the others have, that nothing happened to him. The Keeper has not decided what to do about the trader.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The way around the gate.** An expedition outfitting at [[place-qptlulneok|Q'aptel'ul Ne'ok]] wants to reach the waste without paying the fee at the gate of [[place-xelemchalix|Shelem Ch'alish]], by going down into the eastern lowland and south through the swamp. It hires a party to find a guide among the rim hunters, and every hunter it asks will take it to the swamp's edge and no farther.
- **The heron feathers.** A feather-trader of the north has been paying the rim hunters double for white heron plumes, and the plumes he wants are the ones from the deep swamp. A hunter who took the trader's price went in after them three weeks ago, and his wife asks a party to bring him out.
