## Overview {#overview}

The Green Moonlight, Ts'uqal Ha'nak, is a chain of valleys deep in [[place-sickforest|the Sick Forest]] whose floors are carpeted from wall to wall with luminous fungi. By day the carpet is a pale, soft gray. At dusk it begins to glow, and by full dark each valley is lit from below with a cold green light, bright enough to read a cord by, while the forest on the ridges above stands black. The returners who have seen it call it the most beautiful thing in the waste.

## The Valleys

The valleys are quiet. The great fungi of the forest do not grow on their floors, the air is cooler than on the ridges, and the beasts of the forest do not come down into the light. A party that has walked for days through the rot looks down into a valley of soft green light, with no spores hanging in it and nothing moving, and wants very much to sleep there.

The floors are full of sleepers. They lie on the glowing carpet singly and in groups, travelers with their packs beside them, and the fungus has grown over them so evenly that they glow too. Some have lain there long enough to be only a shape under the light. The returners' rule is to cross the valleys by day, when the carpet does not glow, and to be on the ridge by dusk.

# The Truth of the Green Moonlight {#truth .secret}

The glow makes a traveler drowsy, and the longer he looks at it the drowsier he grows. A traveler who lies down on the lit carpet sleeps, and does not wake while the light lasts; by dawn the fungus has begun to grow over him, and a sleeper who lies through a second night does not wake at all.

> [!TIP]
> A character who looks at the valleys by night makes a test of will each hour or wants to lie down. A sleeper can be woken only by being carried off the carpet into darkness.
