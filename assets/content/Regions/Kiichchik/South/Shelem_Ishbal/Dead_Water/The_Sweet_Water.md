## Overview {#overview}

The Sweet Water, Ne' Nal'ishik, is a lake in the heart of [[place-deadwater|the Dead Water]], and after days of brown channels it looks like rescue. Its water is clear to the bottom, its shores are firm white sand, and no reeds grow in it. The water tastes clean and faintly sweet, like rain caught in a new jar. Every account at the gate that mentions it says the same thing first: do not drink it.

## The Lake

The lake is round and shallow, a few hours' poling across, and its bottom is white sand, with the bones of animals scattered over it plainly visible through the water. Nothing lives in it. No fish, no weed, no leech, and none of the swamp's reptilian things will enter it, and the swarms that rise off every other water in the Dead Water at dusk do not rise off this one. The sand of the shore is clean, and the ground is dry for the first time in days.

## Those Who Drink

A traveler who drinks from the Sweet Water feels well for a day, better than he has felt since the gate. Then it takes him one of two ways. Some go mad: they grow quiet, then cheerful, and walk into the swamp in the night, smiling. The rest waste away over weeks, eating and drinking as much as they can hold and growing thinner with every day, until they die looking like men who have starved. The returners' accounts tell of whole parties found on the white shore, with their water jars full of the lake.

# The Truth of the Sweet Water {#truth .secret}

The water is what the returners say it is. A traveler who drinks it makes a test of endurance: on a failure he is mad within a day, and on a success he begins to waste, losing strength every week until a healer of great skill treats him or he dies. Boiling the water changes nothing, and neither does any filter a party carries.

> [!WARNING]
> The shore of the Sweet Water is the safest camp in the Dead Water, and it is the camp where parties die, because a party camped there with its own water running low drinks.
