## Overview {#overview}

K'ankul holds the land of the [[place-kankulrgn|K'ankul Region]]. Where [[affiliation-ixilankul|Ish'ilankul]] is the militant heart and the seat of spiritual authority, K'ankul is the realm of knowledge and precision. Here, the Tq'an'ik (priestly initiates) labor for decades to perfect the astronomical tables carved into stone, calculating with near-perfect accuracy the cycles of [K'inish'ilan]{.name data-type="place/celestial"}, the star of dawn and dusk, the precession of the equinoxes, and the great wheel of days that turns once through the ages of the world.

K'ankul's political independence from Ish'ilankul is carefully balanced. While the Ch'alish Tq'an and the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani priesthood]] maintain ultimate religious authority, K'ankul's greatest city-states have cultivated sufficient wealth and cultural prestige that they are treated as partners in the management of the faith rather than as mere tributaries. K'ankul's merchants control crucial sea routes along both coasts, and their mathematical and astronomical expertise is essential to the priestly calendar-keepers.

The [[affiliation-chumishlan|Ch'um Ish'lan]] relay network connects K'ankul's major city-states to Ish'ilankul within two days, and the priesthood-controlled coastal white road allows messages to circulate between K'ankul's own cities in a day or two. The Ch'umbal way-stations here are well-maintained and generously staffed—K'ankul's scribal tradition means that the priestly decoding of k'otsel is exceptionally swift, and K'ankul's astronomers frequently use the Speaking Road to transmit time-sensitive observational data to the central dispatch at [[place-kultaqan|Kul'taq'an]].

## City-States and Realms

K'ankul's political landscape is dominated by several great city-states, each controlling a network of smaller settlements and agricultural zones.

The K'ankul city-states are intensely competitive in intellectual and cultural achievement. Rather than fielding massive armies, they compete through astronomical discoveries, architectural innovation, and the training of scribes and astronomers. A city-state that announces a new calculation or a new architectural technique becomes the center of pilgrimage and study for scholars from across [[place-kiichchik|Kì'ìch'chik]].

The pool-centered spirituality of K'ankul has produced a distinct tradition within the Itsani faith. The Ch'alishbal (ordained priests) of K'ankul place special emphasis on watery sacrifice and on divination through the reading of reflections in the water of a sacred pool. Some of the most powerful priestly families maintain personal relationships with specific sacred pools, regarding them as sacred entities with individual personalities and preferences. The boundary between the practical (managing water resources, maintaining agricultural canals) and the sacred (interpreting pool omens, timing rituals) is intentionally blurred in K'ankul thought.

## The Understanding

K'ankul keeps an understanding with the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]] of [[place-lirnasto|Lirnasto]], the conclave in the high jungle of the platform's interior: the realm's people stay out of that jungle and do not disturb those who live there. Every Ts'enmah holds to it, and the Ha'nalmah of the towns along the forest's edge turn back hunters, rubber-tappers and gatherers where the old trees begin. The realm's books do not say when or how the understanding was made, and the Senali take no other part in the realm's affairs.
