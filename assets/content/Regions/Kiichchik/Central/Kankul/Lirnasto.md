**Lirnasto**, the place of vines in the [[skill-senalilng|Senali language]], is the conclave the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]] keep in the middle of [[place-kiichchik|Kì'ìch'chik Continent]]. It lies in the [[place-kankulrgn|K'ankul Region]], in the high jungle of the interior of the limestone platform, a long way from the coast and from every city of the realm. It is the one place in Kì'ìch'chik where the Senali and a human realm hold to an understanding. Nobody outside it has counted the Senali there. They form the [[affiliation-nclvlrnst|Enclave of Lirnasto]].

## What a Traveler Finds

The jungle of K'ankul is a worked land of maize plots, regrowing forest and hunting grounds, and at the edge of Lirnasto's country the working stops. The trees beyond are older and taller than any the lords' foresters keep, hung with vines from crown to root, and no plot has ever been burned among them. Sacred pools open in the limestone there as they do everywhere on the platform, and no priest has poured an offering into them.

Nobody in K'ankul goes in, so a traveler has only the edge to describe: the wall of old trees, birds in a number and a noise the worked forest does not hold, and the white causeways of the realm stopping short of it on every side.

## The Understanding

The realm of [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]] keeps an understanding with the Senali of Lirnasto: its people stay out of the old jungle and do not disturb those who live there. Every Ts'enmah of the realm holds to it, and the Ha'nalmah of the towns along the forest's edge enforce it on their people. Hunters, rubber-tappers and gatherers of medicinal plants turn back where the old trees begin, and a man who goes in is answerable to his Ha'nalmah when he comes out. No causeway is laid toward the forest and no plot is burned into it. The day-tables of [[affiliation-ishilanshulum|the Star House]] mark no rite to be held there.

When the understanding was made, and by whom, the realm's books do not say. The Senali have never come out of the forest to speak of it. They take no part in the realm's trade, its rites or its quarrel with [[affiliation-ixilankul|Ish'ilankul]], and the realm asks nothing of them beyond what the understanding already holds.

## The Present {#present}

The understanding holds, and the realm's hunger for maize land leans on it every dry year. The towns along the forest's edge press close, and the Ha'nalmah who keep the line have more households to turn back each season. A Kulmah's foresters mark a stand for burning, the Ts'enmah sends a man to stop them, and the old trees stand. Nobody in the realm has seen the other side of the understanding keep it, and nobody doubts that it is kept.

Lirnasto asks nothing of the realm. The Ts'enmah read that as patience, and the Ha'nalmah of the edge towns read it as a debt that the realm is slow to count.

:::secret {title="The conclave behind the trees"}
The Senali of Lirnasto keep their hearths in the crowns and among the buttress roots of the oldest trees, around a cenote the realm's priests have never found, and they do not build in stone. About a hundred and sixty live there under the Lirnanno, the Keeper of the Vines, who raises the conclave's word and holds the understanding on its behalf. The Keeper does not need the Ha'nalmah to enforce it. The paths at the forest's edge turn a trespasser back by themselves, and what the Ha'nalmah's patrols do is give the turning a human reason.

The Keeper has watched the edge towns grow and has marked, in the long reckoning the Senali keep, which stands the Kulmah's foresters have measured for burning. A fire in the old trees is the one act the understanding does not forgive, and the conclave has not decided what it would do in answer. The Keeper has told no one.
:::

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The man who went in.** A thief condemned in a town on the forest's edge has fled into the old jungle, and an Inspector of [[affiliation-shaqalob|the Severers]] demands him. The town's Ha'nalmah will send none of his own people after him and will not stop outsiders who choose to go, and he wants it understood that whatever they disturb is theirs to answer for.
- **A lord's new field.** A Kulmah whose seat borders the forest has had his foresters mark a stand of the old trees for burning, to make room for maize in a hungry year. His Ha'nalmah has asked the Ts'enmah to stop him, and the Ts'enmah wants a party to reach the Kulmah's seat with his word before the first fire is lit.
