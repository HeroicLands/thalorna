## Overview {#overview}

From the sea, Ts'okilkul is a line of white on top of a gray cliff, with a temple at the cliff's edge and a small beach below it. From the land it is a wall. The wall runs in a great bracket from the cliff edge inland and back to the cliff again, as tall as two men and thick enough to walk along, with five narrow gates and a watchtower at each corner, and the town fills the space inside it. Ts'okilkul, the Walled City, is a harbor town of about 20,000 people on the coast of the [[place-kankulrgn|K'ankul Region]] and one of the great cities of the realm of [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]]. Its wall commands the overland roads that run from the coast into the interior, and its harbor commands the sea trade along the coast.

## The Reef and the Lamp

A coral reef runs along the whole coast here a little way out from the shore, and a canoe that strikes it in a swell breaks up. There is one gap in the reef in front of Ts'okilkul, and the temple on the cliff marks it. The temple has two small windows in its seaward wall, and after dark a lamp burns behind each. A canoe-master coming in at night keeps the two lights lined up one above the other and steers straight through the gap onto the beach below the cliff. The keepers of the lamp are road priests of [[affiliation-xilanixlan|Faith of Shilan Ish'lan]], and every canoe that lands pays them a share of its cargo.

The beach is the harbor. The canoes are drawn up on the sand in rows, and a stair cut in the cliff climbs from the beach to the town through a gate of its own. Long trading canoes come in from the other coasts of the continent with salt fish, bird feathers, exotic woods and luxury resins, and go out again with K'ankul's rare books, astronomical instruments and fine carved jade.

## The Town

Inside the wall the town is built close. The houses of the lords stand on low platforms along a single paved street that runs from the main gate to the temple on the cliff, the storehouses of the merchant families stand behind them, and the houses of the canoe-men and the porters fill every space that is left. Water comes from a sacred pool on the town's northern edge, inside the wall, and the town has no other water.

## Who Governs

Ts'okilkul has its own Ts'enmah, who rules by descent and names his heir as a child in the manner of every city of K'ankul, but the harbor belongs to the Ilkanmah, the lord of the shore, who holds it for him. The present Ilkanmah is [Tsul Q'aptel Shelem]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a broad, cheerful man of forty who was a canoe-master himself and who takes the lord's share of every cargo landed on the beach.

Ts'okilkul is one of the two harbors through which most of K'ankul's sea trade passes. The other is [[place-tzililnetzik|the Island of Swallows]] (Ts'ilil Ne'ts'ik), a port that the realm of [[affiliation-neokbaate|Ne'ok Ba'ate]] holds, and the two compete for every K'ankul cargo. The Ilkanmah would like all of it.

:::secret {title="The third window"}
The temple on the cliff has a third window, low in its northern wall and shuttered by day. A lamp set in it lines up with the lamp in the upper seaward window at a point a few hundred paces north of the true gap, where the reef lies just under the surface. Twice in the past two years, on moonless nights, the Ilkanmah has had the true lamps covered and the third window lit when a canoe of Ts'ilil Ne'ts'ik was expected, and both canoes broke on the reef. The cargo that washed ashore is in his storehouses. One of the lamp-keepers took his share and the other refused it, and the one who refused has asked to be sent to another temple.
:::
