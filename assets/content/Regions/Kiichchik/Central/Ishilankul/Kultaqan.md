## Overview

A pilgrim on the white road sees Kul'taq'an a day before he reaches it. On a cold morning a thread of white stands over the hills ahead, straight up from the summit of the [[place-tsultelchalish|Ts'ultel Ch'alish]], where the warm breath of a volcanic vent comes out of the greatest pyramid on the continent. Kul'taq'an, the Seat of the Priests, is a city of about 110,000 people in the high valleys of the [[place-ixilankulrgn|Ish'ilankul Region]], one of the city-states of [[affiliation-ixilankul|Ish'ilankul]]. It holds three things no other city has: [[place-tzomalchalix|The Sacred Foundation]] (Ts'omal Ch'alish), where the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]] teaches that the creator first breathed the world into being; the seat of the Q'emul Tq'an, the supreme high priest; and the central dispatch of the [[affiliation-chumishlan|Ch'um Ish'lan]], the Speaking Road.

## The City

The white road comes in from the west through a gate of plain dressed stone, low and broad. The city's people say a gate on the plateau needs no height, because the wind does the work of a wall. Past it the city spreads over four hillsides, one to each of its four quarters, and every quarter climbs its hill in terraces of white-plastered houses, each ward with its own small temple platform and its house of youths. Below the precinct, where the four quarters meet, lies the great plaza. The market fills it every day, and on the market days of the twenty-day cycle, which Kul'taq'an shares with [[place-tsamash|Tsa'mash]] in turn, it fills to the walls with traders and tribute-bearers from the whole plateau.

The great plaza is also where the city performs. On the first day of Shok'alal, the war month, two dancers in the feather-and-leather armor of the captive-hunts dance [[lore-housename|The House Name]] there to a slit drum and two shell trumpets, and the young nobles who will go out on their first campaign stand at the front to learn how a captive speaks.

## The Sacred Foundation and the Priests

The walled precinct of Ts'omal Ch'alish stands at the heart of the city, and the Ts'ultel Ch'alish rises inside it in nine tiers. Around the precinct, on terraces of their own, stand the hostels where pilgrims sleep. They and the fields that feed them belong to the estates of the Sacred Foundation, which the **Steward of the Sacred Foundation** keeps for the Q'emul Tq'an, and they owe nothing to the city's lords.

Kul'taq'an has more priests than any other city on the continent. The Q'emul Tq'an's household lives within the precinct walls. The city's own priesthood answers to its Presiding Ch'alish Tq'an, who fixes Kul'taq'an's calendar like the hierarch of any city, and who holds the Q'emul Tq'an's seat if the Q'emul Tq'an dies within a round. Envoys of [[affiliation-ishilanshulum|the Star House]] and the other bodies of [[affiliation-itsanihierarchy|the Itsani Hierarchy]] keep houses in the streets around the plaza. Outside the priesthood, the cities call the Q'emul Tq'an simply the Ch'alish Tq'an, and a pilgrim who asks for the high priest is sent here.

## The Dispatch

At the foot of the precinct's eastern wall stands the central dispatch: a long, low hall with a door at each end, and a runner coming in or going out of one of them at every hour of the day and night. The Ch'umbal of the Speaking Road run the white roads in relays, and every cord carried anywhere on the continent passes through this hall sooner or later. Inside, priestly scribes untie the _k'otsel_ (knotted cords), read them, copy them and send the copies on. A message crosses the plateau in a day, and the astronomers of [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]] send their observations here in two.

No lord commands a runner, and no Ts'enmah reads a cord before the priests have read it. The dispatch is the reason a Sun Lord of the plateau keeps a kinsman in Kul'taq'an, whatever city he rules.

:::secret {title="The extra copy"}
Every cord that passes through the dispatch is copied once more than its sender knows. The extra copy goes down a stair under the hall to a sealed archive kept by the Q'emul Tq'an's inner circle, and the archive holds every message the Speaking Road has carried in the present round. This year the inner circle is reading the cords that the Presiding Ch'alish Tq'an of the other cities send one another about the coming conclave, and it knows which of them mean to choose whom.
:::

## Who Governs

Kul'taq'an has its own Ts'enmah, chosen from its royal line by the four P'eshalmah, the lords of its quarters, and confirmed by its Presiding Ch'alish Tq'an, with a Ch'ikalmah who holds the city when he is in the field. The present Ts'enmah is [[being-tshlishtmtsl|Toshel Ishtem Ts'ultel]], a lean man in his forties who has led the city's captive-hunts against [[place-qokulqat|Q'o Kul'qat]] in nine war months and taken eleven captives himself. He holds the quarters, their wards and levies, and the fees of the great plaza. He does not hold the precinct, the dispatch or the hostels, and he collects no tribute from their estates. The Q'emulmah, first of the Sun Lords, sits at [[place-ixilankul2|Ish'ilankul]], and the Ts'enmah of Kul'taq'an rules the holiest city on the continent as one Sun Lord among the others.

## The Coming Fire

The present year is 4158 of the Fifth Age, Eleven Death, and the next [[lore-itsaniclndr#round|Living Flame]] falls at the winter solstice of 722. When the fire has been drawn, the Presiding Ch'alish Tq'an of every city come to Kul'taq'an, each carrying a coal of his own city's new fire in a covered jar, and choose the Q'emul Tq'an for the next fifty-two years. The **Keeper of the Conclave** is already sending to every city the cords reach, to learn whose hierarch will come and in what order they will speak. The Steward is raising new hostels on the lower slopes, and the wards are called out by turns to carry stone for them. Envoys of a dozen cities have taken houses near the plaza for the two years ahead, and in the evenings they visit one another.
