## Overview

Coming up the valley through the maize fields, a traveler sees two pyramids long before the city, side by side on one platform, with the green wall of the jade mountains behind them. They look like a pair, but the right-hand one stands a hand's width higher, and the city has made that hand's width its pride. Q'o Kul'qat, the Twin Pyramid, is a city of about 35,000 people in a highland valley of [[place-neokbaatergn|Ne'ok Ba'ate Region]], one of the greatest city-states of the realm of [[affiliation-neokbaate|Ne'ok Ba'ate]] and the proudest of them, and it is known everywhere for two things: its carved stones, and its independence.

## The Two Pyramids

The first pyramid was raised by the royal house, and the Ts'enmah climbs it to bleed for the city at the turns of the calendar. The second, beside it on the same platform, belongs to the lord-house that raised it. The head of that house sits at the left hand of the Ts'enmah at court, and its sons are the city's most famous captive-takers. The captives of the war month go up the second pyramid to the sun.

The city's gate is high and narrow, built of the same green-gray stone as the platform, with a lintel carved with the two pyramids. The people of Q'o Kul'qat say it is higher than any gate on the plateau, and the people of [[place-kultaqan|Kul'taq'an]] say a gate on the plateau needs no height.

## The Carvers

The Kulumbal of Q'o Kul'qat, the keepers of the stones, raise a new stone at every turn of the count, and the carvers' ward cuts them in relief so deep that the figures of the Ts'enmah and his ancestors stand almost free of the stone, with their feathers, their jewels and the knots of their sandals cut to the thickness of a finger. Carvers come from the other cities of the realm and from [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]] to learn the work, and the ward takes their fees and teaches them everything except how it sharpens its chisels.

## The Jade

The mountains behind the city hold the green stone that makes Q'o Kul'qat rich. Its miners follow the veins into the hillsides with fire and water, cracking the rock around the jade and carrying the boulders down whole, and its lapidaries saw them with cord and wet sand. The green of Q'o Kul'qat is the color the plateau's nobles want for their ear-spools and the priests for their offerings. When the city sends a ransom or a gift, it sends jade.

:::secret {title="The green vein"}
The great vein behind the city is nearly worked out. The miners have followed it so far into the mountain that the last face yields a few boulders a year, and the green of the newer stone is paler. For several years the Ts'enmah has been buying jade quietly from the highland villages of the interior and from traders who carry it up from the south, and sending it out as the green of Q'o Kul'qat. The head of the house of the second pyramid has found out.
:::

## Who Governs

A Ts'enmah rules the city by descent, and names his heir in a public rite while the heir is still a child. Q'o Kul'qat pays tribute to [[affiliation-ixilankul|Ish'ilankul]], in jade, on its own day and by its own envoy, and it admits no steward from any other city to count it. It makes its own wars and its own peace. Its chosen enemy is Kul'taq'an. In the war month the war-leaders of Kul'taq'an come down off the plateau to take captives in the city's fields, and the captive-takers of the second pyramid go up to take theirs, and each city tries to ransom its nobles from the other and is refused.
