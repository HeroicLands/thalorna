## Overview

The Ha'nal Ts'uma, the Misty River, is the larger of the two great rivers of [[place-xelemrgn|Shelem]], and it is named for the fog that lifts off its flooded forest at dawn. It gathers so many tributaries that its network of waterways winds for thousands of kilometers through the landscape, and in Shelem that network is the road: the realm is built on water, and the river matters more than any road or temple.

## The Flooded Forest

Along the Ha'nal Ts'uma the forest floods each year and drains again, and between the flooded stands run navigable channels that join communities too far apart to reach on foot. The villages stand on the higher ground along the banks. The city of [[place-xelem2|Shelem]] itself lies in the river-bound jungle where the Ha'nal Ts'uma, the [[place-hanalxilik|Ha'nal Shilik]] and a thousand smaller waterways meet. A traveler who wants to go anywhere in Shelem asks first which channel, and in which season.
