## Overview {#overview}

**The Stone Rooms** are a row of doorways in a cliff of dark rock in the hills above the [Copper River]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, on the barren grounds of the [[place-kawiskakrgn|Kawiskak Region]], weeks of travel beyond the treeline. The doorways are square, each the height of a tall man, and behind them rooms run back into the rock, dry and cold in every season. Below the cliff a river narrows between two gravel ridges, and the caribou herds cross there on their way north in spring and south in autumn. The [Barren-Ground People]{.name data-type="lore/culture"} who hunt the crossing keep their meat in the rooms through the winter, and the forest bands of the [[lore-kawiskakclt|Kawiskak]], the Spruce People, know the place only from what the Barren-Ground hunters say of it at the treeline.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

A Barren-Ground band winters on the gravel ridges below the cliff and holds the crossing and the rooms as its own. It sleeps in its half-sunken houses on the ridges and not in the rooms, which it uses for meat, hides and the season's copper. Nobody else has a claim the band recognizes.

## Layers {#history}

The Barren-Ground hunters say the rooms were there before their own people came onto the barrens, and that nobody they know of has ever lived in them. They say that whoever cut them knew the rock: the walls are straight, the floors are level and dry, and the rooms do not drip in the thaw.

## What Is There Now {#present}

The crossing is the trouble. Last spring a party of hunters of the [[affiliation-tikupilarit|Ice-Edge People]]'s eastern kin came inland from the coast with their dogs and took caribou at the crossing in the spring run, before the band had its own kill. They went back to the coast, and the band expects them again at the thaw, in greater numbers. If they take the crossing, the band loses its winter meat and the rooms with it.

The band did not come to the treeline this year to trade its copper. [Tapiskostin]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the Tapiskak, the Copper band of the Spruce People, has met it at the edge of the trees every late summer of his life, and he went north to look for it and found its tracks going back toward the crossing. He has the band's copper to lose and no wish to go onto the barrens alone.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

The barrens kill by weather: wind that never stops in winter, fog off the gravel in summer, and flies that drive men as they drive the caribou. A bear of the barrens dens in the inner rooms some winters and takes the meat, and the band has lost a hunter to one in living memory. The Ice-Edge kin's hunters are armed, many and new to the country.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

Six doorways in a row along a ledge a short climb above the scree, each opening on a square room cut level, the walls smoothed, the floors swept by the wind. Meat hangs in the outer rooms on racks of caribou antler, and hides lie folded on the floors. A passage leads back out of the third room into the dark, and cold air comes up it in every season. The band does not go past the mouth of that passage.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The missing band.** Tapiskostin wants companions to go north beyond the treeline with him at the end of the thaw, find the band at the crossing and bring the copper trade back.
- **The spring run.** The band wants hunters and fighters at the crossing when the Ice-Edge kin come back, and pays in copper and furs for anyone who stands with it.
- **The bear.** The band wants whatever dens in the inner rooms killed or driven out before it takes another winter's meat.
- **The passage.** Nobody of the band goes past the mouth of the passage in the third room, and the cold air coming up it says the rooms go much further back than any of them has seen.

# The Truth of the Stone Rooms {#truth .secret}

**The hold.** The cliff is the face of **Balg-gunáv**, "the delving of copper", an abandoned hold of the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]], cut where the copper of the river country lies in the rock as well as in the gravels. The six rooms are the outermost homes of the face, and the passage out of the third room is the road into the hold: down to its halls, its forges, its archive chambers and the deep where its dead lie, with their names cut over them. The hold stands in the record of the holds, and every hold's archive keeps where it lies.

**Its making and its leaving.** Balg-gunáv was cut in the centuries [[place-khazarturn|Khazartúrn]] stood, by houses that crossed the water in the ships of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]], as the party for the far edge did ([[lore-vargfukar|The Finding at the Far Edge]]), and it was fed by the hunters of the barrens, who brought caribou, fish and hides to its gate. It was left before the city fell. Its count was falling, as the counts of holds were falling across the world while the Khazari dwindled, and its elders found at the table that the hold go back over the water while the forest folk's ships could still carry its people and its record. They swept the halls, laid the hearths cold and took the record with them. After [[lore-khazarturn|the Fall of Khazartúrn]] the Khazari spoke to the Senali no more, and no Khazár has crossed to Balg-gunáv since.

**What is left.** The halls are empty and in order. The forges are laid, the racks are bare, and the caches are open and swept. Nothing in the hold is valuable to the holds, which left nothing they needed, but a Khazari tool or fitting left in a rack is worth more to a human scholar than anything on the barrens.

> [!TIP]
> The passage out of the third room is where the rooms stop being a meat store. Cold air coming up it means the hold breathes through its own shafts, and a party with a lamp finds dressed stairs going down within a hundred paces.
