## Overview {#overview}

The Salt House, Sh'ulum Ne'shik, stands on an island of dark rock two days out on the salt flats of southern [[place-chakultzklrgn|Ch'akul Tsa'kul Region]]. From the shore of the flat it is a white glitter on the horizon, shaking in the heat. Closer, it is a building of three broad terraces climbing the island's back, each terrace a row of rooms, and every wall, step and lintel is grown over with salt crystal, white and glittering, so that the whole house looks carved out of salt.

## The Crossing

The flat around the island is a white crust over brine mud, and the crossing is the worst of the Salt House. The crust holds a walker on some ground and breaks under him on other ground that looks the same, and the mud beneath it is soft, deep and bitter. The glare off the salt blinds a traveler who walks it with his eyes uncovered, and the heat comes up from below as well as down from the sun. There is no shade between the shore and the island, and no water that is not brine.

## The House

The rooms are small and square, with low doorways and flat roofs of stone beams, and most of the roofs still stand. The salt has grown over everything inside as it has outside, in crusts and needles and branching white growths like coral, and it has grown over what the rooms hold, so that every object has a skin of crystal and keeps its shape under it. A touch breaks the skin, and under it the things are as they were: baskets with their weave and color, rolls of cotton cloth dyed blue and yellow, wooden bowls, cord, jars with their stoppers in.

In the inner rooms of the top terrace, the dead sit along the walls. Each is wrapped in cloth and seated with knees drawn up, dried to leather by the salt, under its own crust of crystal. There are dozens in each room. Some wear ornaments of shell and copper over their wrappings, and the salt has grown over those too.

## Dangers

The flat is the great danger, going and coming. The house adds its brine: the floors of the lower rooms hold pools of it, clear and still and so salt that it burns the skin, and the crust that covers the pools looks like any floor. The salt needles cut, and a cut from them does not close cleanly. Inside the rooms it is cool, and a party that shelters there through the heat of the day breathes salt dust that dries the throat and swells the tongue.

## What Is Found There

What the salt keeps is the treasure. Cloth that has kept its color sells in the south as a wonder, and the baskets, bowls and cord are like nothing woven in [[place-kiichchik|Kì'ìch'chik Continent]]. The traders who know the house carry out what they can, and take nothing from the seated dead; they leave the ornaments where the salt has grown over them.

# The Truth of the Salt House {#truth .secret}

The crust of the flat is firm wherever it is ridged and gray, and soft wherever it is smooth and white. A party that learns this, or brings someone who knows it, crosses safely in two days; a party that walks a straight line toward the island breaks through within the first morning.

The seated dead are only dead. Robbing them carries no curse. What it does carry is the traders' anger: the traders who cross the flat hold the dead of the Salt House as their own luck, and a party known to have stripped them finds no trader on the desert's southern edge who will guide it, sell to it or carry for it.

> [!TIP]
> Let a party see, in the first room it enters, a seated body whose salt crust has been broken and its ornaments taken, and an empty space on the wall beside it where the next body sat. Somebody else has been here, and not long ago.
