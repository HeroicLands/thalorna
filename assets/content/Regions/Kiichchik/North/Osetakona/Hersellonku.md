**Hersellonku**, the pine deep-wood in the [[skill-senalilng|Senali language]], is the northernmost of the three conclaves the [[lore-flksenali|Senali Folk]] keep in [[place-kiichchik|Kì'ìch'chik Continent]]. It lies in the [[place-tsitarargn|Pine Country]], north and west of the Pine People's six villages, where the ridges of rock and the cold lakes run on into country with no villages at all. Nobody outside it has counted the Senali there.

## What a Traveler Finds

The forest thickens as it goes north. The pines on the ridges grow taller and closer, white and red pine older than any the canoe makers cut, and the ground beneath them is a deep floor of needles and moss with almost nothing growing on it. The lakes are clear to the bottom. Sound carries a long way, and there is little of it.

A traveler who walks on into this wood finds pine and lakes and nothing else. Paths that seem to lead deeper bend back to a lake already passed, and a party that set out to cross a ridge comes down on the side it started from. Nobody who has gone looking for the Senali here has found a dwelling, a fire or a trail.

## The Pine People

The Pine People know the wood by the trees. Their canoe makers come north for the tallest pines in the Grove, and they take them from the near ridges and leave the far ones standing, because a cutter who goes on toward the far ridges comes home a day later than he meant, or by a lake he did not set out from, with no tree cut. Some summers a hunter sees a tall figure on a far shore at dusk, standing as still as the trees, and it is gone when he looks again. The Pine People say that the far ridges belong to the tall people, and they hunt elsewhere. Their speaker at [Snow Place]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} sends nobody into the wood and asks nothing of it.

The Senali ask nothing of the Pine People either. No gift has passed in either direction, and the conclave takes no part in the quarrel over the hunting bands, in the [[lore-tiatorenhsa|Answering]], or in anything else of the Grove's.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **Days lost in the pines.** Hunters of a band driven south from the far woods have been following moose onto the far ridges, and two of them came back after nine days believing they had been gone for one. The band's spirit-doctor wants a party to bring back the third, who has not come back, and the Pine People's clan mothers want the band told to hunt elsewhere before the young men use this as their reason to drive it off.
- **The tallest tree.** A canoe maker of the Falls Country has been promised a hull for the great council's next voyage and means to cut it from a pine he saw on a far ridge. He hires a party to clear the way and carry the trunk out, and the Pine People who would usually guide him say no.
