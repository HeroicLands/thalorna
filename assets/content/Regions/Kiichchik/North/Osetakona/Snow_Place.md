## Overview {#overview}

Kenrisaten means the Snow Place. It is the largest of the six palisaded villages of the Pine People, about 900 people on a river below a falls in the [[place-tsitarargn|Pine Country]], and the seat of the nation's council. The falls is the village's mill and its larder: the fish run up to the foot of it in spring, and the people take them there with weirs and spears.

## The Village {#the-village}

The palisade is single and old, with bark longhouses inside it and a council house in the middle that is the biggest building the Pine People own. The maize patches lie on the deep soil along the river, and the village hopes each year for a late frost. Outside the palisade, along the river, stand the birchbark lodges of the hunting bands, a few dozen families at a time.

## The Failing or the Quarrel {#present}

Snow Place is where the Pine People's divisions sit down together. Some of its clan mothers want to adopt the bands family by family, some want to trade with them and keep them outside the palisade, and the young men want them driven off before they are too many. [[being-okwiahasterio|Okwiahasterio]], the nation's speaker, has taken a band's spirit-doctor into the village and let him raise a shaking lodge outside the palisade, and goes to hear him. The healers of the Grove call it witchcraft, and the question has gone to the great council.

The village sends nobody into the far wood to the north and west, and asks nothing of it. The hunters of Snow Place hunt the near hills.
