## Overview {#overview}

**The High Floors** are three cedars standing close together in the forest above the [Grease Trail]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, a day's climb out of [[place-mukwasis|Wolf Beach]] at the head of the longest of the [[place-hapasrgn|Long Inlets]]. Each is so broad at the foot that ten men with their arms spread cannot ring it, and at three heights their limbs reach across the gaps between them and grow into one another, so that the three trees carry floors of living wood one above another, the lowest of them higher than a war canoe is long. The [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Cedar People]] live on the beaches and the rivers and leave the forest to itself, and the carriers of Wolf Beach see the floors from the trail, a long way up the slope through the twilight under the canopy, and walk on.

## Who Holds It {#who-holds-it}

Nobody holds it. The trail belongs to the Wolf House by the right of the carriers who walk it, and the Wolf House claims nothing off the trail. No crest of any house on the coast carries the three trees.

## Layers {#history}

The carriers tell new carriers that the floors were there when the first carriers went over the pass, and that the trees grew that way because something asked them to. Nobody of Wolf Beach has climbed to the floors, and no story of the house says that anyone ever has. A carrier who falls sick on the trail is carried past the slope below them at a quicker pace.

## What Is There Now {#present}

Two slaves of the Wolf House, a man and a woman taken in a northern raid, ran from Wolf Beach at the end of the rendering, when the village was busy at the grease pits, and went up the trail. The carriers sent up to clear the trail of winter fall have seen smoke over the high floors in the evenings and have not gone up after it. The Wolf House wants the two back before the carriers go over the pass with the grease, because a slave who is let go teaches the rest of the house's slaves that the forest will hide them, and the carriers will not climb.

The two cannot stay. The floors are cold, the forest gives them little to eat that they know how to take, and once the snow is off the pass the carriers come up the trail every day for a month.

## Dangerous Neighbors {#dangerous-neighbors}

Bears come down to the river below the slope when the salmon run, and a bear in the forest at that season does not give way. The forest floor under the canopy is a tangle of fallen trunks and devil's club, slow to cross and quick to lose a man in. The floors are high enough that a fall from the lowest kills. The Wolf House sends armed men after its runaways and does not ask whose they were before.

## What a Party Finds {#what-a-party-finds}

Three trunks rising out of a mound of their own roots, each broader than ten men can ring. On the uphill trunk the stubs of old limbs stand out from the bark one above another in a slow turn, each the right height above the last for a foot, and they climb to the first floor. The floors are limbs as thick as a man's body, grown across the gaps and into the far trunks so that no join shows, with lesser limbs grown flat between them and roofed by the canopy above. The lowest floor is the widest and could sleep twenty. The second is smaller, and the third, under the crowns, is a ring of limbs around open air. Nothing on any floor is carved, tied or nailed. On the first floor, where the two runaways sleep, are a fire they have built on a slab of bark and the bones of what they have caught.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The runaways.** The Wolf House pays in blankets and grease for the two brought down alive, and more if a party brings them down before the carriers set out.
- **The other way.** The two runaways want help to cross the pass ahead of the carriers and reach the country beyond the mountains, where no house of the coast can follow.
- **The count.** The Orca House of [[place-malkisis|Orca Beach]] keeps the count of the coast and frees slaves. A party that carries word of the two to the Orca House can try to buy them free before the Wolf House takes them back.

# The Truth of the High Floors {#truth .secret}

**The camp.** The three cedars are **Ivarrasto**, "the place of hope", the waiting that expects: a camp of the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]], abandoned. Senali crossing the mountain spine between the coast forests and the country beyond came up from the inlet in late spring and lived on the floors until the snow left the pass, then went over. The floors kept them above the forest floor and the bears, dry under the canopy, and in sight of the snowfields. They grew the floors by leading the limbs across while the trees were young enough to be led, and the stair is limbs they let fall so that the stubs stayed.

**The leaving.** The crossings ended when the Senali drew back into the few deep forests where they keep their sanctuaries, long before any carrier walked the trail.

**What is there.** The floors are only floors. The trees are sound and grow as other cedars grow. No Senalo has come to them in the lifetime of any house on the coast, and nothing in the camp watches or wards it.

> [!TIP]
> The floors make a fair fort for two people and a poor larder. A party that climbs to them finds two frightened people who know the forest less well than the Wolf House's men do. The crossing, the season and the leading of the limbs are this note's own account of the camp; a game master may change them.
