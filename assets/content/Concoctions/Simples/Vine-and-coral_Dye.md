Vine-and-coral dye is sold as a pair of cakes. The green is steeped from vine leaf, and the red is the root of the ember bloom (_kalovael_), lifted from each shrub only every few years. Together they are the two colors of the ring [[affiliation-tharivaan|Thári'vaan]] takes for its sign, woven of green vine and coral branch.

## What It Does

Cloth dyed with the pair keeps its color through the wet seasons, and the red does not fade. The dyers of [[place-nalurithan|Nalurithan]] and [[place-ilovanu|Ilovanu]] are craftsmen under the island's ranks, and the dye is one of the few staples that leaves the island as cloth and not as plant. A bolt of it dyed at Nalurithan is carried to the foreign quarter and sold there.
