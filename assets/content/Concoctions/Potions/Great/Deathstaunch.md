Nearly black, hard as resin when cool but with a crystalline sheen that catches light strangely, this legendary coagulant looks like no ordinary preparation. The scent is almost metallic – pure iron, rendered to its essence, mixed with herbal compounds so rare and potent that breathing the air around an opened vial makes the head swim slightly. When warmed and pressed against a wound, the paste seems to hunt for the bleeding source actively, drawn toward flowing blood as iron to lodestone, and seals it with extraordinary finality. The flesh around the application visibly drains of color within heartbeats, the body's blood traffic diverted completely away from the wound and back toward vital organs.

Pressed onto a single bleeding injury, the great coagulant applies a quadrupled SV bonus – SV ×4 – to Bleeding Rolls. The effect persists for the full ten-minute window, during which the most catastrophic wounds stop bleeding. This is the preparation that allows someone to survive injuries that should be immediately and fatally terminal – an arterial gash that would empty the body in minutes becomes manageable; a wound that has been bleeding uncontrollably for rounds becomes sealed. Legendary battle-surgeons and field herbalists of the highest rank carry the great coagulant as a last resort, the preparation that separates a death that might have been prevented from a death that absolutely could not have been.

The great coagulant is so expensive that only the wealthiest mercenary companies, the most elite royal guards, and the highest-ranking adventuring leagues maintain supplies. Its existence signals that someone is serious about treating battlefield trauma at the most extreme level. When a great coagulant is called for, everyone present knows that the injured character has entered the razor-thin space between salvation and the pyre.
