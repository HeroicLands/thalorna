Nearly black-green, this preparation glimmers with an inner iridescence and has the stiffness of rendered wax. The smell is extraordinary – every healing herb known to the Thalorna apothecaries layered together, yet somehow harmonious rather than overwhelming, with hints of minerals and something nearly alchemical beneath the herbalism. When applied to an injury, the paste seems to hunt actively for damaged tissue, flowing slightly to cover the entire wound area even as it hardens into a protective seal. Patients watch their injuries visibly reverse their decay within the first hours of application – swelling collapses, infection retreats, dead tissue seems to slough away and be replaced by new, healthy growth.

Applied to a single injury, the great curative provides a quadrupled SV bonus – SV ×4 – to all Healing and Infection Rolls throughout the recovery period. This is the preparation that allows a previously life-threatening injury to become recoverable, the one that can take an infection that was preparing the body for amputation and turn it toward clean healing. Injuries that would ordinarily require months of recovery show visible improvement within days, and full recovery comes within weeks rather than seasons. Only the most elite physicians, the richest temples, and the most prestigious healers ever possess supplies of great curative, and they guard their sources jealously.

The great curative is worth its weight in gold and more besides. Its mere existence in someone's medical kit signals that money and access are no object, and that the injured character's survival and full recovery have been prioritized above nearly any cost. When great curative is applied, everyone present understands that the most powerful healing magic short of divine intervention has been deployed.
