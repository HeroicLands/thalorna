This is blackened liquid, thick and almost tar-like, that seems to absorb light rather than reflect it. The smell alone can kill small animals—a nauseating, choking miasma that penetrates cloth and lingering in the nostrils for hours after exposure. The vial must be sealed with multiple layers of cork and wax, and even then, a careless whiff can drop a person to their knees in agony. The herbalist who dares prepare this works in isolation, often wearing a leather mask filled with herbs to shield against the vapors. This is not merely poison—this is death given liquid form.

When ingested or injected through a wound, this acts as a HR2 virulence toxin that kills with terrifying speed. Death or recovery occurs in roughly fifteen minus the preparation's Success Value rounds—in other words, within moments. The victim experiences apocalyptic suffering: the body burns from within, vision fails, organs fail. There is no time to flee, no time to seek aid, rarely even time to scream properly. When applied as paste to a weapon, the +20 bonus to resist is almost meaningless given the speed of onset. A victim struck has perhaps one minute to find a healer with potent counteragents, and most healers lack the power to save those at this stage.

This poison is an act of pure malice. Few nations permit its creation, and those who brew it are often hunted as much as those who employ it.
