This is liquid darkness itself—a black potion so opaque that light dies against its surface. The scent is overpowering, almost choking: a suffocating sweetness layered with burned sugar and something mineral, almost like smelling a cold stone tomb. Even unwrapping the cork releases fumes that make the eyes water. The herbalist who prepares this works with their face wrapped against the vapors. A single drop tastes like the distilled essence of oblivion.

Within a single round, the narcotic floods the imbiber's body. Pain becomes a foreign concept—something that once mattered but no longer reaches the consciousness. The user gains an SV × 4 bonus to Shock Rolls and suffers twice that amount (SV × 2) as a penalty to Move, Agility, and Agility-based skills. The limbs move as though weighed down by great stones. Speech becomes slurred. Many who take this potion report that the four-hour window of its effects becomes a blur of half-memories and dream-like images, entire conversations happening without their participation.

This strength is reserved for the dying, the tortured, and those beyond saving. Some temples use it as a tool of mercy; some use it as a tool of control. Few who drink this willingly are ever quite the same.
