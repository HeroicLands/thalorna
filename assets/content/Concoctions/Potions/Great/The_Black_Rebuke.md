Nearly black, with the opacity of tar and the fluidity of mercury, this legendary emetic exudes an aroma so potently repugnant that bottles are often kept in sealed leather cases rather than exposed even to air. The mere sight of the stuff—much less the smell—is enough to make the experienced drinker's entire body rebel. To consume the great emetic is to enter a state of deliberate and total bodily rejection, a calculated assault on the stomach and bowels in service of survival. Those who have had to administer or consume the great emetic often refuse to speak of the specific details of the experience, acknowledging only that it worked and that they lived.

Within a single round of ingestion—barely the space to regret the decision—the great emetic forces a Shock Roll against Shock Index 7 with a quadrupled penalty—SV ×4 crushing any resistance the body might offer. The result is violent beyond ordinary description; the body expels everything and then continues trying to expel things that were never there. Bones ache; the world spins; rational thought becomes difficult. Those subjected to the great emetic are incapacitated for the duration of the effect, able only to lie still and endure. Yet the result is absolute: every particle of ingested poison, every trace of toxin, every drop of venom is violently expelled, and the body, however traumatized, is clean.

The great emetic exists at the boundary between medicine and torture. It is prepared only by the most specialized apothecaries and is found exclusively in the hands of temple physicians who serve rulers, legendary herbalists with life-or-death practice, and the rarest adventuring parties. Its very existence is a testament to the commitment to survive at any cost.
