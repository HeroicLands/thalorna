A hazy, opalescent liquid that catches the light in unsettling ways – sometimes amber, sometimes violet, shifting as the vial is tilted. The herbalist prepares it by steeping certain night-blooming flowers, dried mandrake root, and the crystallized tears of creatures said to inhabit the fever dreams between waking and sleep. When swallowed, the taste turns bitter-sweet, almost medicinal, before fading into a numbness that creeps across the tongue.

About ten minutes after ingestion, the imbiber begins to sense a softening of the world's edges. Colors grow richer and more fluid. The familiar blurs into the strange. Those trained in meditation or trance work report that the Veil – that gossamer boundary between the mundane and the luminous – becomes perceptibly thinner. For those already dwelling in a Trance, this potion lifts the deepest part of the Veil in roughly fifteen minutes minus the preparation's Success Value, granting an SV bonus to Transcendence Rolls and deepening connection to the spirit realm.

Some cultures view this potion as sacred medicine, reserved for shamanic rites and healing visions. Others regard it with suspicion, as a tool of madness. Regardless, a single dose lingers in the blood for several hours, gradually releasing its hold on perception and sensation. The experience leaves many users exhausted yet strangely exhilarated, changed in small ways that are difficult to articulate.
