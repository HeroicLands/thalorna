A deep amber liquid that seems almost golden when held to candlelight, thick enough that it clings to the sides of its container. The scent is far more potent than the mild variety—sweet and heavy, with underlayers of bitter almond and something faintly metallic. A small sip induces immediate numbness in the mouth, and the herbalist who handles it too carelessly may find themselves drowsy for hours. This draft represents a serious intervention, not a simple palliative.

Within a single minute, the narcotic's potency engulfs the imbiber's entire body. Pain dissolves into an almost dreamy absence. The user receives an SV × 2 bonus to Shock Rolls and suffers half that amount (SV × 1) as a penalty to Move, Agility, and Agility-based skills. Limbs become heavy and sluggish, as though moving through deep water. The four-hour longevity of the strong potion makes it dangerous for those who must move swiftly or dodge threats. Many warriors report feeling invulnerable on this draft, a false confidence that has claimed countless lives.

Users speak of euphoria, a disconnection from the world almost as profound as dreaming, combined with a dangerous inability to accurately gauge threats to their bodies.
