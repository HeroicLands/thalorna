This draft is clouded and murky, its pale greenish color barely visible through swirls of suspended precipitate. The odor is sharp and acrid, burning the nostrils even from a distance. Those who have handled it report that the cold sensation penetrates deeper than its mild counterpart, as though the potion itself is drawing warmth from the air around it. The vial may frost over in warm weather. A taste induces complete numbness of the mouth and tongue within seconds, followed by a terrible cold that spreads down into the chest.

Within one minute of ingestion, the paralytic seizes control. The victim must make an Endurance test with a penalty of SV × 2 – a crushing disadvantage. Failure locks every muscle in rigid immobility for one hour. The imbiber remains conscious and aware throughout, able only to watch and listen. When applied as paste to a blade, the victim receives a +20 bonus to resist, which may be the only thing standing between them and absolute helplessness.

This potency is used by those who wish their captives not merely restrained but psychologically devastated. One hour of complete paralysis often breaks the spirit in ways that physical chains never could.
