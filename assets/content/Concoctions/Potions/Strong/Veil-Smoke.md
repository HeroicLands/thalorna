This draft is darker than its mild cousin, an almost indigo suspension in which specks of finely ground plant matter hover like suspended stars. The scent is potent—acrid, almost metallic, with an underlying sweetness that nauseates some on first inhalation. The herbalist who brews it must steady their hands against the fumes alone. One swallow coats the mouth with an astringent taste that lingers for hours.

Within mere moments—less than a minute—the imbiber's perception fractures like glass struck by a hammer. The world becomes a symphony of simultaneous, overlapping impressions: colors emit sounds, surfaces breathe, and the boundaries between thought and sensation collapse entirely. If the user is already in Trance, the potion tears through the Veil with remarkable swiftness, dropping it in roughly fifteen minutes minus twice the Success Value. The doubled potency of this draft grants an SV × 2 bonus to Transcendence Rolls, lending profound insight and an almost overwhelming sense of communion with the spirit world.

This strength of hallucinogenic is prized by mystics and dangerous in untrained hands. The vision it bestows can be illuminating or shattering, and some who drink it find themselves unable to fully return to the everyday world afterward.
