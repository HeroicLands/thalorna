This is a vibrant, almost lurid red or orange liquid, sometimes darkening to a deep crimson, laden with visible particles of crushed stimulant botanicals. The aroma hits like a blow – sharp, aggressive, and overwhelming, filling the nostrils with spice and burning sensation. Many describe the smell as almost electric. A single swallow induces intense tingling that spreads from the mouth to the fingertips, along with a racing sensation in the chest. Those unused to this potency sometimes report heart palpitations that cause alarm, though they pass quickly.

Within a single minute, the stimulant floods the bloodstream entirely. The imbiber becomes restless with energy, almost hyperalert. Eyes sharpen; ears strain to catch distant sounds; every sense sharpens to an almost painful degree. One receives an SV × 2 bonus to Initiative and Awareness, gifts that persist for roughly one hour before the potion's effects fade. The subsequent crash is steeper and more disorienting than with the mild variant – some users become nearly catatonic for hours afterward, and many develop a psychological dependency on the heightened state.

This strength is favored by soldiers before battle and by performers before high-stakes displays. Some use it to work through nights without rest. The cost to the body is steep.
