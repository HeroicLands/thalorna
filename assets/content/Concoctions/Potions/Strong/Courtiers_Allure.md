Jewel-toned amber, as though the preparation has imprisoned sunlight itself, this concentrated aromatic announces its presence long before the vial is opened. The fragrance is extraordinary – rich florals layered with exotic spices, a hint of precious woods, and the ineffable sweetness of rare compounds that cost as much as their weight in silver. A single drop applied to the neck, wrist, or collarbone spreads through the surrounding air like thrown water, intoxicating everyone within ten feet with a scent that seems designed to trigger memory and desire simultaneously. Those nearby find themselves unable to focus on anything but the wearer, their attention drawn back again and again to this source of pleasure.

During the four-hour duration, the strong aromatic provides a doubled bonus – SV ×2 – to the wearer's Charm rolls. The effect is decidedly obvious to observers; people visibly brighten in the wearer's presence, their eyes tracking the wearer's movements, their voices softening and slowing into patterns of trust and cooperation. Negotiations that might otherwise be contentious become remarkably smooth; offers that would normally be refused are reconsidered; even accusations and suspicions seem to lose their edge. The aromatic does not lie or promise; it simply makes the wearer seem more worthy of belief and affection.

Strong aromatics are associated with courtesans of the highest rank, merchants dealing in gemstones and fine art, and nobility whose political fortunes depend on personal magnetism. A wearer of strong aromatic becomes instantly identifiable as someone of considerable means and social ambition, and the scent is often as effective as a calling card in announcing their presence and importance.
