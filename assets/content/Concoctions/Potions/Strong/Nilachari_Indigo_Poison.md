Distilled down from the spent mordant and fermentation residue of indigo vats in [[place-vyalendraland|Vyalendra Land]]'s Nilachari valleys, this poison keeps the dye's own deep blue-black color, which is both its signature and its weakness—a taster who knows to look for the stain under a fingernail or at the rim of a cup has half the warning he needs. What makes it valuable to the trade that seeks it out is the other half: worked correctly, its onset mimics an ordinary wasting fever closely enough that a physician who is not looking for poison rarely finds one.

Among the apothecaries of the [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara Region]], the shrena halls connect the preparation, in low voices, with [[being-chndrtrmkhyshvr|Chandratar Mukhyeshvara]], whose legitimate skill with dye-chemistry is the reason they give.
