Deep crimson as fresh blood mixed with wine, this concentrated preparation seems to pulse with vitality in its crystal vial. The perfume that rises from the stopper is intoxicating—night-blooming jasmine, sweet amber, and the exotic warmth of distant spices all layered into a single breath-stealing note. Even a small sip floods the mouth with silken heat; the sensation spreads with startling speed through the body, raising the skin's temperature and quickening the heartbeat. The drinker's pupils dilate, and an almost visible aura of magnetism seems to gather around them.

Throughout the four-hour window of efficacy, the strong aphrodisiac's doubled penalty—applying SV ×2 to Charm resistance—overwhelms ordinary reluctance and creates an almost palpable magnetism. Those who encounter the potion's user find their skepticism and reserve undermined far more readily than by any mild preparation; even genuine disinterest struggles to compete with such chemically-amplified allure. Experienced seducers prize this strength for negotiations, courtships, and situations where ordinary charm simply will not suffice. The effect is heady and obvious to anyone watching carefully—the imbiber glows with an almost feverish desirability.

The strong aphrodisiac is a luxury good, affordable primarily to merchants of consequence, nobles seeking conquest, and those with reason to invest heavily in persuasion. Its use carries whispered accusations of unfair advantage in matters of the heart and is barred outright in some jurisdictions, deemed an assault on free will no different than poisons or binding spells.
