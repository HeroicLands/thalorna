A Passion Elixir roils with deep reds and violets, crackling with barely suppressed emotion, and tastes of fire and bitter fruit. This elixir implants a psychic seed that blossoms into a chosen mental state—the alchemist specifies during preparation whether the imbiber acquires a psyche or sunsign trait Disorder, flooding the mind with compulsive impulses and conflicting desires.

The effect is deliberate and precise, though its expression is far from predictable. The elixir itself generates a random Impulse psyche trait (with a PSY value equal to half the base AS), which may work with or against the intended disorder. Some alchemists use Passion Elixirs as tools of enslavement or punishment, while others craft them as spiritual medicines for those who have grown numb to feeling. The cost is immediate: the imbiber suffers an Aural Shock penalty of 1 AS per use, consumed via monthly Decay.

Passion Elixirs are restricted by many temples and city orders, regarded as instruments of violation—yet spymasters, cult leaders, and dark bargainers keep them close.
