Keeper's Vow appears as a shimmering, pearl-like suspension that casts a faint luminescence, as though defensive power itself has been trapped in liquid form. When poured upon an object weighing no more than AS × 5 pounds, the elixir bonds with the item and fortifies it against arcane meddling. Any attempt by an attune or invoker to establish a psychometric connection to the object incurs a d10 roll against AS; success decreases the would-be connector's test result by one level of success and reduces the elixir's AS by 1.

The process is incremental but relentless. Each failed attempt to breach the aegis leaves the object more stubbornly guarded, consuming the elixir's charge via the monthly Decay Roll. When AS reaches zero, the protective aura dissipates entirely. This elixir is applied, not swallowed – drinking it inflicts Alchemical Dissolution (page 267), with Crucial Dissolution triggered on certain d100 rolls.

The elixir draws from the Principle of Substance (Sidéros). It serves as a guardian for reliquaries, merchant's safes, and arcane tomes that must never fall under enemy control.
