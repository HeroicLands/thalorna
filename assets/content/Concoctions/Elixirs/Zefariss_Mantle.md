Zefaris's Mantle is a pale, frosted blue elixir that freezes at the edges of the vial even in mild temperatures, its cold radiating through layers of wrapping, bound to the Principle of Cold ([[affiliation-zefaris|Zefaris]]). When drunk, the imbiber's body becomes resistant to heat and flame—they receive a bonus to their Armor Value against Heat impact in all body locations, including area-effect fire damage. This bonus equals half the Aural Shock multiplied by five and helps the imbiber withstand even the searing grip of supernatural fire.

Beyond passive defense, the elixir grants a bonus equal to half Aural Shock multiplied by five to Heat Shock and Exposure Advance tests, as the imbiber's inner chill fortifies them against scorching air. Yet this resistance exacts a price: the imbiber accrues weariness fatigue equal to base Aural Shock multiplied by five, a creeping exhaustion as if their body has spent its reserves to hold the fire at bay.

Smiths at the forge, soldiers storming a burning keep, and those who cross deserts or face supernatural flame depend on Iceshield. Some survivors of terrible heat claim that the elixir forever changes how they experience temperature, leaving them perpetually cool even beside a roaring hearth.
