A Soulbuffer Elixir appears as a pearlescent, opalescent draft that shifts hue with each angle of light, radiating a profound sense of protection. This elixir works silently, drawn from the Principle of Aura (Pneuménos), absorbing Aural Shock that would otherwise tear at the imbiber's dormant soul. Each instance of Aural Shock the imbiber would suffer is instead absorbed by the elixir, with the Soulbuffer's AS decreasing by an amount equal to the shock level absorbed.

Critically, the elixir also protects pre-existing Aural Shock that accumulated before the drink was imbibed, offering retroactive defense against accumulated spiritual damage. The protection does not extend to shock suffered while the imbiber inhabits their soul body – only while they are anchored to flesh. This makes Soulbuffer invaluable for those who venture into the spirit world or face enemies skilled in Aural Shock attacks. The cost is severe: weakness fatigue equal to AS × 10 accumulates, requiring the imbiber to rest heavily. Each daily action drains 1 AS via the Decay Roll.
