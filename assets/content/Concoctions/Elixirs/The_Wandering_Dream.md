A translucent, opalescent liquid that shifts between colors as it moves – lavender, rose, silver – never settling into a single hue. Bound to the Principle of Dimension (Neutral), this elixir performs one of the most unnerving feats in alchemy: it divorces the drinker's soul from their flesh. Upon drinking, the imbiber's body falls into a deep coma while their active soul transfers to the spirit world, a shift in perspective from the physical realm to the astralscape beyond. The body remains corporeal and unharmed, left for companions to guard.

The soul can inhabit the spirit world for a duration equal to the Aural Shock in rounds before the imbiber must make a Reembodiment Roll to return to flesh. This journey exacts a high price: the imbiber suffers Aural Shock 3 after completing the Reembodiment Roll. Those who engage the spirit world through Dreamwalk report visions of profound strangeness – conversations with entities that wear the forms of living people, journeys through impossible geometries, revelations that linger long after waking.

Shamans, mystics, and those seeking communion with the spirit world value Dreamwalk above many other elixirs. Yet the experience is not without peril: a disrupted journey or forced reembodiment can leave the soul shattered.
