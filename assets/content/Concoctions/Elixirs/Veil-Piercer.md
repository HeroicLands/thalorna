A pearlescent, opalescent draft that seems to contain wisps of smoke or mist trapped in liquid, bound to the Principle of Incorporeality (Hydälis). When drunk, Ghostsight grants the imbiber a profound shift in perception: they can now take Spirit Sensing actions while remaining fully incarnate in their flesh. As a free action on their turn, the drinker may shift their perspective from the physical landscape to the astralscape of the spirit world, or back again. Only one perspective can be maintained at a time; the imbiber cannot see both realms simultaneously.

While inhabiting the spirit world's perspective, the imbiber can perceive ethereal entities, spectral forms, and the spiritual resonance of places, but their movement through the astral world is limited: they can maintain perspective there for no longer than half their Aural Shock in rounds per shift (minimum one round). Each shift back to physical perception costs Aural Shock 1, in addition to monthly Decay Roll losses. The imbiber suffers Aural Shock 3 as the base ill effect.

Shamans, ghost-touched warriors, and those who commune with the restless dead value Ghostsight for investigating haunted places. Some claim the elixir opens the third eye, allowing sight that persists in dreams long after the effect fades.
