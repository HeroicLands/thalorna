A chalky white draft that tastes of limestone and chalk dust, bound to the Principle of Dryness (Pyréthos). When the imbiber exhales after drinking, they release an aura of absolute dryness in a five-foot radius that lasts for a number of minutes equal to their Aural Shock and follows their movement. Within this zone, all water transforms into ethereal water – a substance as insubstantial as mist, unable to quench or conduct, harmless to those within. The drinker's own body remains unaffected; the elixir does not strip moisture from flesh, only from the environment itself.

Each exhalation of this dryness power costs Aural Shock 1, in addition to the monthly Decay Roll loss. The effect is controllable: the imbiber may activate the drying aura as a one-turn action and dismiss it at will, though once dismissed it must be reactivated if needed again, consuming fresh Aural Shock.

Alchemists prize Exsiccation for extinguishing fires, spoiling supplies, and purifying water-logged ruins. In siege warfare, a single dose can ruin an enemy's water supply if deployed strategically. Those who drink it report a strange aftereffect: their skin and lips feel perpetually parched for hours, no matter how much water they consume.
