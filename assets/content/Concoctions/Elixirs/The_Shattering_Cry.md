A Shout Elixir vibrates in its vial like a plucked harpstring, golden and thrumming with barely contained power. When the imbiber speaks, they may unleash the full might of this elixir as a 1-turn action—a bone-shattering cry that reverberates across thirty feet, deafening all creatures within range. Those struck must make a Shock Roll against SHK 6; the resulting deafness carries a Healing Rate of 5 and lasts approximately ten minutes.

Each shout consumes 1 AS and triggers monthly Decay. The elixir draws from the Principle of Sound (Hydälis), though the toll on the imbiber's senses is sharp: a half AS × 5 penalty applies to Perception and Awareness tests that rely on hearing, as a persistent ringing in the ears overwhelms subtle sounds (Healing Rate 5, duration d6 days). Commanders use Shout Elixirs to break sieges, bards employ them to silence crowds, and desperate warriors make their final stand with a cry that carries the force of thunder.
