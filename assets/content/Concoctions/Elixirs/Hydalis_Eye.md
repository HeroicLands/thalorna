A Verity Elixir is perfectly transparent, yet seems to glow from within with clarifying light, and leaves the drinker's vision achingly sharp. Drawn from the Principle of Illusion (Hydälis), this elixir pierces deception and phantasm, granting the imbiber sight beyond sight. Any illusion directly affecting the drinker or appearing visually within sixty feet is revealed in full, stripped of its masking magic. The effect is immediate and automatic upon imbibing, requiring no action or test.

The elixir carries a minimal cost: only 1 AS consumed per use through standard Decay. This makes Verity among the most efficient of alchemical preparations. Warriors employ it to cut through enemy camouflage, investigators use it to see through forgeries and hidden evidence, and the paranoid keep a vial against court intrigue and magical deception. The cost is slight – a mild Aural Shock of 1 – but the psychological toll of seeing through illusion can be profound, as reality appears starker and harsher than the comforting lies that normally veil the world.
