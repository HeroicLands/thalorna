A pale, frosted blue elixir that freezes at the edges of the vial even in mild temperatures, its cold radiating through layers of wrapping, bound to the Principle of Cold (Zephäris). When drunk, the imbiber's body becomes resistant to frost and freezing – they receive a bonus to their Armor Value against Frost impact in all body locations, including area-effect frost damage. This bonus equals half the Aural Shock multiplied by five and helps the imbiber resist even the numbing grip of supernatural cold.

Beyond passive defense, the elixir grants a bonus equal to half Aural Shock multiplied by five to Cold Shock and Exposure Advance tests, as the imbiber's internal warmth fortifies them against the elements. Yet this resistance exacts a price: the imbiber accrues weariness fatigue equal to base Aural Shock multiplied by five, a creeping exhaustion as if their body has burned through reserves of vital heat to achieve protection.

Arctic explorers, mountaineers, and those who face winter blizzards or supernatural freezing depend on Iceshield. Some survivors of terrible cold claim that the elixir forever changes how they experience temperature, leaving them perpetually cool even by warm hearths.
