# Appearance {#appearance}

A Dryad is encountered among the ancient trees of [[place-ukuaraelwen|Úkua-Araelwen]]. In the glade, trunks stand with the spacing and posture of people; a party entering with drawn blades finds the trees closer when it looks again. The forest’s growth is immense, and some of it moves.

# Dossier {#dossier}

A Dryad is a tree-bound spirit of [[place-ukuaraelwen|Úkua-Araelwen]], defending the ancient-tree glade on the approach to [[place-mazeofechos|Maze of Echoes]].

## Presentation

Trunks stand with the spacing and posture of people. A visitor’s drawn blade changes the room between them; a sheathed weapon and a question give the encounter time to become a negotiation.

## In the Land

The glade lies within a slow [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] in the high forest of [[place-sathvothe|Satvote]]. Its trees are immense and some of its growth moves. The dryads keep that domain and can be approached through the customs of earth-bound spirits.

## Key Behaviors

The guardians defend the grove against damage. They respond to an approach and to what an intruder does among their trees. An offer to leave the grove unharmed gives a party a way to ask for passage.

## What the Wardens Do

The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] keep outsiders away from the forest’s margin. They do not enter the slow ground themselves. A party passing their watch still has to deal with the grove’s guardians.

## Signs

- Ancient trunks standing close enough to hem a path.
- A clearing whose space narrows after weapons are drawn.
- Growth changing position while attention is elsewhere.

## Combat Strategy

The guardians’ defense uses the grove’s close terrain and moving growth. A party can retreat, force its way at a cost or renew a negotiation. [[scenario-embsydnvrtantsou|Embassy from Dunavarre to Tantsou]] describes the glade’s encounter.

## Attack Methods

Use the glade’s obstacles and the encounter’s choices when the guardians resist passage.

## Special Abilities

### The guarded grove

The surrounding growth can block and narrow an intruder’s route. The [[miscgear-temprlcmpss|Temporal Compass]] steadies its bearer’s time, but grants no authority over the dryads or their ground.

## Attributes

The encounter’s visible scale is that of the ancient trees around the clearing. Its changing spaces make the guardians’ attention apparent before a traveler is hemmed in.
