## Overview

"I can name what they do," a temple-inquisitor says of the **Zeghet'Zulun** (the **Bloodlust Cult**). "I cannot name what they write, because they write nothing." The society is secret and oath-bound. It takes exotic beasts and forces them to fight for spectacle, and anyone who trains animals for any other purpose is an obstruction to be removed. A beast-trainer who is offered work by strangers should ask who the strangers are.

The society takes oaths and keeps no book. What a member owes is sworn in front of the others and held by them, in the way a household holds what it is owed, which leaves the priests without the one charge that would let them move. Whether that is caution or accident is disputed by the people who would know.
