A network of corrupt officials and merchant-lords who have lost a great deal to honest auditing, and who keep a quiet bounty on those who did the auditing.

The network runs on a book, because a network must. Its members are strangers to one another by design—an official in one selat, a merchant-lord in another, introduced by a third who is paid to forget it—and what each is owed for what is written down and enforced. That is the temple's office performed by somebody else, and the priesthood pursues the Gar-Gezru as a heresy while the magistrates pursue it as embezzlement. The two investigations do not cooperate.
