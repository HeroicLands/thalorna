## Overview {#overview}

Kraveldo, "the Crow", is a Tarvenan crossbow company homed in the back streets of Herthema, the walled coast town that breeds sellswords. It is a _thramo_ (company) in the Tarvenan sense, a captain and the men sworn to her, and its captain is Prámiza Mozkarreta. The captains of the valley companies named it: a crow follows any army that will feed it, and Prámiza's company takes contracts the valley companies refuse. Prámiza took the name and had it cut on the company's pavises.

The company differs from a valley company in where it comes from and what it signs. A valley company is the young men of one valley, sworn at their own stone, and it goes home to that valley. Kraveldo is filled from Herthema's roll: a man on the town's roll may take hire lawfully, and Prámiza takes the roll's best and the young men who hope to be put on it. It has no valley to go home to and no oath stone of its own. It swears at whatever stone stands nearest the paymaster, and the valley captains hold that to be the root of everything wrong with it.

## What the Company Keeps

The company keeps its book and its silver. The clerk's book holds every contract the company has sworn, word for word, with the names of the men sworn to it and the day each was paid. Prámiza holds the silver, and she pays on the day the contract names. In a trade where companies go unpaid for a quarter and argue it at the stone, a company that is never paid late, because its captain carries the pay, fills its ranks easily.

It keeps no long poem and no muster roll in a captain's head. When a contract ends the men are paid off, and the ones Prámiza wants again are told so. A man taken on through three contracts is an Old Crow, and the Old Crows are heard before she signs.

## The Contracts

Every contract is read aloud by the clerk to the whole company before it is sworn, clause by clause, and read again at each clause's day. Prámiza reads every contract twice before that. What the valley captains object to is what she will sign: a clause binding the company to arrest whomever the client names, a contract to hold a merchant's warehouse against his own creditors, a season's hire with a lord against his neighbor. A valley captain refuses a contract to burn a village before he signs it, and Prámiza has never signed one either. Where the valley companies draw a line at what a contract asks, she draws it at what a contract says. She keeps every word of it, and nothing it does not say.

## The Present {#present}

This spring Kraveldo has taken a season's contract with [[being-tharozozurg|Tharozo Zurgaregi]], a baron of the Western March, to go down the war pass with his valley's host in the season of [[lore-summerwar|The Summer War]] and come home with it. The company marched up from the coast before the thaw and is camped in the baron's valley under the [[place-toravarrange|Toravar]]. The barons of the March bring their own valleys' men to the war, and none of them has hired a coast company for it before. At [[place-kemthoza|Kemthoza]] the other barons' men say so in the yard.

At Herthema the company's quarrels go on without it. The Keeper of the Roll of the [[affiliation-cnclhrthm|Council of Herthema]] struck one of Prámiza's best file-leaders from the roll over the winter, and the man says he was betrayed. The bodyguard [[being-banrkcrsld|Banrik Káriselda]] is on the roll and belongs to no company, and he has refused Prámiza's offer once.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The coast company in the March.** Prámiza Mozkarreta hires a party to ride the March's valleys ahead of the season and tell her what her baron's neighbors think of her contract.
- **The struck file-leader.** Prámiza wants to know who in the Council of Herthema arranged the striking, and what they gain.
- **The war pass.** [[scenario-greenbough|The Green Bough]] turns in part on what Kraveldo's contract with its baron says.
