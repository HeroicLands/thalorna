"The contract is the road from the step to the first camp, and nothing else," [[being-zedmogltsrg|Zedmo Galtsuregi]] tells every imperial officer who rides up to the fort with a new demand. "Read it. I had it read to me at the stone." The captain of Goitsa Thal says the same thing in the same words to tribunes, tax clerks and the agents of men who would be Orzas, and the company's long poem already has a verse for it.

## Overview

Goitsa Thal, "the Red Stone", is a company of the high valleys of [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]], named for the standing stone of red rock its men swear on. It is a _thramo_ (company) in the [[lore-tarvenanclt|Tarvenan]] sense, a captain and the men who have sworn to him for a season, and it has sold its crossbows abroad for four generations. For the last nine years it has held the imperial road where the paving ends below the Kervaron passes, a posting the legions held themselves until they were thinned.

The company is what the empire buys when it cannot raise a cohort: a hundred and eighty men who fight well, cost nothing once the contract ends, and keep their word to the letter. The letter is the difficulty. A Tarvenan company does what its _semna_ (contract) says and nothing it does not, and every officer who has dealt with Goitsa Thal has learned where the contract stops.

## What the Company Keeps

The red stone stands in a meadow at the head of the valley above Kávrenath, a day's walk below the pass of [[place-zurgando|Zurgando]]. It is the company's _goitsa_ (oath stone), older than any shrine in the valley, and a man becomes a _daro_ (sworn man) of Goitsa Thal by putting his hand on it and saying the contract after the captain. The company's other possessions are the ones every Tarvenan company keeps: its _therna_ (muster roll), with the empty places of the struck names kept on it; its _oldemo_ (long poem), sung after supper by the man who was there; and its name, which carries the _damla_ (stain) of any broken contract along with its honors.

The company is a crossbow company first. Its men carry steel-bowed crossbows made in the iron town of [[place-ardalis|Ardalis]], and each man buys his own bolts and is paid back for every one he can show was spent on the contract's business. The forty men who are not crossbowmen are spear-and-pavise men who hold the line while the bows reload. They are men of the valleys and their sons, with a few men from Kávrenath and, every year, a few young men from farther down the kingdom who walked up to the stone and asked.

The company goes home in halves. The Kervaron passes close with the first heavy snow of autumn, and half the company crosses home before they close and comes back over when they open. The half at home says [[lore-thresholdroll|The Roll at the Thresholds]] at the stone on the first morning of the [Feast of Thresholds]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}. The half at the fort says it on the same morning in the fort's yard, facing west toward the pass, and the captain answers for the stone from there.

## The Articles

The company's law is the contract, and its order is short. Every man is a Sworn Man for the year he swore to. A man kept on through more than one contract is given a _kiramharlen_ (earned name) by his companions and becomes a Named Man, and the Named Men are heard when the captain proposes a new contract. A captain who swears the company to a contract the Named Men would not have sworn finds he has a company of eighty in the spring.

The captain, the _thrumar_, is the man the company swears to, and he holds the contract with the paymaster, the _daima_, whoever that is in a given year. Beside him rides his _holvo_ (companion), who holds the company when he is away. Each file of twenty has a _takzo_ (sergeant). The purser keeps the roll and the pay, the singer keeps the poem, and the surgeon keeps the wounded.

The worst thing a man of the company can do is break the contract, and the penalty is the _neharlo_ (unnaming). The company strikes his name from the roll, keeps his place on it empty, and does not say the name again. A man who runs from a fight is beaten and kept; a man who sells the company's word is unnamed.

## The Contract and the Fort

The imperial road west out of [[place-alyssa|Alessa]] climbs into the foothills and stops at the last boundary-post, where the paving ends in a single worn step. The fort above it is called the [Step]{.name data-type="place/structure"} by everyone who has served in it: a square of dry stone and timber for two hundred men, with a gate-tower over the road, a cistern, stables for the mule trains and a signal platform facing east. Below it a chain of four posts runs down the road a day apart.

The company's contract with the empire is sealed by the war council of [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|The Vylarian Military]] and runs from thaw to thaw. The war council's clerk came over the mountains to Kávrenath the first spring to swear it, and climbed to the stone with the captain, because a Tarvenan company swears nowhere else. The contract binds Goitsa Thal to four things: to hold the Step and the posts, to keep the road open from the Step down to the foothills, to escort imperial trains from the Step to the first camp of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan People]] below the pass, and to report by the road whatever comes over the passes in arms. It binds the empire to pay the company by the quarter.

The Áelendan clans that hold the passes know the company well. Tarvenans go home over the Kervaron and pay the clans' passage-gift as any traveler does, and the company's men have been paying it for four generations. The clans treat Goitsa Thal as travelers who happen to be armed. The legion's cohorts that held the Step before them never had that footing with the clans.

## Pay

The empire pays a sworn man of the company four Ardin a day, a Named Man five, and the captain a fixed sum by the quarter, with bolts and remounts on account. A legionary draws less by the day. He also draws the dole and the citizenship at the end of twenty years ([[doc-vylrnctznshp|Vylarian Citizenship]]), and a Tarvenan draws neither. Over a man's life the company is the cheaper soldier, and the war council knows it.

The pay comes as traveler's notes drawn on [[affiliation-tarmex|The Tarmeks Orzan]], and the notes are drawn against money the throne has borrowed. The purser rides down to [[place-orzara|Orzara City]] each quarter to collect them, and carries them to a chapterhouse of [[affiliation-danvar|The Bankers' Guild]] to be changed into silver, because the valleys do not take paper. He changes them at the [House of Aldovardian]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"}, the great house of the guild that began in Tarvénia, when he can, and he watches the discount the moneylenders take, which has risen two years running.

**An unpaid month breaks the contract on the empire's side.** The contract says so in a clause every Tarvenan contract carries: pay more than a month late releases the company. The captain then says the breach aloud before witnesses at the Step, and the company marches home over the next open pass with the contract in the purser's satchel and the breach written on it. It does not sell itself to another paymaster in the empire, because a company that changes masters in the middle of a posting carries the stain of it. It does not hold the road either. If the passes are closed, the company sits at the Step until the thaw as guests, keeping its own gate and nobody else's. The war council knows the date of every quarter-day and what each one would cost.

## The Legion's Officers

The legion's officers dislike Goitsa Thal for reasons each of them states differently. The Step was a cohort's posting until the legions were thinned after the war in which the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]] won its independence, and the cohort's withdrawal is remembered by its officers as a disgrace bought cheaply. The officers who blame the Taruzelian war on the purchased commissions resent the company less than the officers who hold them, who hear in every Tarvenan contract the argument that a soldier can be bought by the year.

The real quarrel is deserters. Men who run from the legions go west over the passes to sell their swords to the Tarvenan companies, as Kávrenath knows well, and every one of them comes up the road past the Step. The legion's officers hold that a company in imperial pay should stop them and send them back in irons. The captain holds that the contract says nothing about deserters and that he will not do unpaid work for an army that is not his paymaster. The war council has been asked twice to write a clause about deserters into the next contract, and has not, because the clause would raise the price.

## The City Watch

The company rarely sees the capital, and the capital's veterans see it only when the purser comes down for the pay. The City Watch, staffed by veteran-citizens, keeps the gate-yards where the purser stables his mule and the gate where he must unwrap his sword. Its watchmen call Tarvenans the empire's runaway sons, since Tarvénia was a province once, and they count the company among the men drawing imperial silver without the twenty years. A purser of Goitsa Thal drinks in the gate-yard of the [Paldor Gate]{.name data-type="place/structure"} and pays for his own wine, and a watchman who calls him a deserter's fence answers for it on the spot. The company has had two men fined by the Watch for brawling in nine years and holds the fines to be a fair price.

## Other Companies

The company will not share a camp with [[affiliation-irnwlvscmpny|The Iron Wolves Company]]. A Tarvenan grants that the Iron Wolves keep their contracts. The quarrel is about which contracts a company may swear. A Tarvenan captain refuses a contract to burn a village before he signs it, and the Iron Wolves sign it. Zedmo Galtsuregi met the Iron Wolves' captain, [Filazur Zighidek]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, once, at a hiring fair in [[place-olvestria|Ólvestra]], and the poem gives the meeting four lines, all of them plain.

[[affiliation-slntlncmpny|The Silent Talon Company]] is Provénzian, and Tarvenans and Provénzians understand each other's speech and seldom each other's trade. A Tarvenan says what he means, and the Silent Talon sells concealment, which a Tarvenan regards as a lie told for money. The Talon's scouts have crossed the Kervaron twice in three years for clients they did not name, and both times the Step logged them and let them pass, because nothing in the contract forbade it.

## The Present {#present}

The quarter's notes came to Orzara eleven days late this spring, and the purser wrote the date in the roll. Eleven days is inside the month, and the captain said so at the stone when the company swore the year's contract.

The contract names the reigning Orzas by his name, as every imperial contract does, and the reigning Orzas is failing. In imperial law the contract is with the throne. In Tarvenan reading a contract is with the man named in it, and a company sworn to a dead man is free. The captain has said neither thing aloud. Two men have ridden up to the Step this year with letters from houses that expect to matter after the funeral, and both rode down again with the same answer: that the company will read its contract when it has to. The legion's officers on the road, meanwhile, have heard that a company of foreigners holding the western road-head means to decide for itself whom it serves.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The deserter at the gate.** A legionary in a torn tunic reaches the Step at dusk two hours ahead of a mounted patrol of his own legion. The patrol's officer demands him. The captain asks what the contract says and sends a runner to find out whether the man is anything worse than a deserter.
- **The dead man's contract.** The Orzas dies in the middle of the quarter. A party is in Orzara with the company's purser when the news comes, and the purser has the contract, the notes and a question for the war council that nobody on the council wants to answer in writing.
- **The fourth post.** The lowest post on the road has stopped answering the signal from the Step. The road below it runs through country that belongs to no lord the company knows, and the captain hires a party to ride down ahead of the relief.

:::secret {title="What goes over the pass"}
The captain's companion, [Oltremo Ugarreta]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, sells the deserters their passage. A runaway legionary who reaches the Step with silver is taken over the pass with the company's half going home, in the company's cloak, and is put into a valley company's hands at Kávrenath with a word in the right ear. Zedmo knows that it happens and has never asked how much it pays. It is not in the contract, so in his reading it is not the company's business, and the money goes into the roll as gifts from travelers. The legion's officers suspect it and cannot prove it. If they could, they would have a breach of the empire's peace by a company in its pay, and the war council would have to choose between its cheapest garrison and its officers.
:::
