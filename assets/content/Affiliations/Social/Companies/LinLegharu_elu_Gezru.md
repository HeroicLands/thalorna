## Overview

Lin'Legharu elu Gezru, the Desert Consortium, is a company of merchants who take rare plants out of the red land around **Gezru** for quick profit rather than for any longer-term stake in the trade. Members harvest what sells fastest and move on, treating the land's capacity to keep producing as someone else's problem. The consortium regards anyone conserving those plants as a threat to the trade itself, so a conservation-minded harvester gets no quarter from the consortium's own members, let alone its competitors.
