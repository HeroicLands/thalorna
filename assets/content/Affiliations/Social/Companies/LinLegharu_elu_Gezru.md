## Overview

Lin'Legharu elu Gezru, the Desert Consortium, is a company of merchants who take rare plants out of the red land around **Gezru** for quick profit rather than for any longer-term stake in the trade. Members harvest what sells fastest and move on, treating the land's capacity to keep producing as someone else's problem. The consortium regards anyone conserving those plants as a threat to the trade itself, so a conservation-minded harvester gets no quarter from the consortium's own members, let alone its competitors.

## Character

A temple herbalist, asked about the rare plants of the red land, shuts her ledger before she answers. Whatever she says next is a complaint.

The consortium's members are short-sighted traders who harvest what sells fastest and move on. They treat conservation as a threat to the trade, which makes an herbalist who counts what is left in the ground their natural enemy. The herbalist speaks for the plants, so weigh her account accordingly; the traders' account would be about prices.

## Relations

The consortium is a loose association of merchants working the red land around Gezru. It has no master and no seat, only a shared interest in the harvest, and it gives no quarter to anyone conserving the plants it takes. A harvester who tries to leave a stand to regrow finds the other members already working it.

## Commerce and Currency

Its trade is rare plants taken out of the red land for quick profit, with no stake in the land's capacity to keep producing. Whatever sells fastest is what gets harvested first.

If you are hired to gather for the consortium, ask how many seasons the stand has left. They will not know, and the question is the useful part of the answer.
