One of the two hunt-patrons of Aû'Khelâthu, whose concern is understood to extend past the taking of the beast to the conduct of the taker. Gar'Nuw, the Gar-Aû's chartered guild of hunters, holds this cult and Linqur's as its religious foundation, and conducts its senior elevations as rites at both temples performed in tandem.
