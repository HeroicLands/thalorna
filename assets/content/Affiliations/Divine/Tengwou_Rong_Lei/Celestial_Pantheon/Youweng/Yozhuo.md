## Yozhuo—The Custodian of Lost Seals

In the Yozhuo cultus, [[lore-yozhuodty|Yozhuo]] occupies an anomalous position within the [[affiliation-youweng|Youwéng]] hierarchy: neither a Demon Lord in the full sense nor a mere lesser demon, but something closer to a provincial administrator of the Infernal Realms. Once a functionary scribe in [[affiliation-wantou|Wántou]]'s Bureau of All Records (Chou Sei), Yozhuo's duties involved the manufacture and distribution of seals—the celestial stamps by which directives became binding, petitions became actionable, and bureaucratic authority was made manifest across the classified cosmos.

At some point—the celestial record is conspicuously vague on the circumstances—Yozhuo began producing counterfeit seals. Not crude forgeries, but meticulous copies indistinguishable from originals to all but the most careful inspection. These counterfeits bore the authorized marks of bureau overseers and celestial marshals, yet when traced through the archive, the directives they represented could not be found. Yozhuo had not merely stolen existing authority; it had fabricated authority that the system could not deny _or_ refute. For an undetermined span of time, orders bearing Yozhuo's false seals were executed by lower functionaries who saw no reason to doubt their legitimacy.

The theft was eventually discovered, though the full extent of Yozhuo's counterfeiting operation remains unknown. What is documented is the escape: Yozhuo fled into the Youwéng with an archive of counterfeit seals and the knowledge of how to produce more—knowledge it zealously guards and occasionally deploys.

### Authority in Exile

In this account, Yozhuo now maintains a peculiar dominion within the Infernal Realms as an administrator of documents and authentication. It is not appointed to this role by any Demon Lord—there is no official structure to grant appointments in the Youwéng—yet by common tacit understanding among the lesser demons, Yozhuo maintains custody over the [Infernal Archive]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the repository of stolen records, false documents, and bureaucratic tools that have leaked or been absconded with from the celestial bureaucracy.

The exact nature of Yozhuo's relationship to the five named Demon Lords is unclear. The entity appears to operate with a degree of autonomy unusual for one of sub-Lord status, yet is occasionally a resource to them: [[lore-watsaidty|Watsai]] consults Yozhuo when seeking records of particularly obscure falsifications; [[lore-nuohodty|Nuoho]] has employed Yozhuo's seals to authorize destructions that would otherwise require higher celestial oversight.

Yozhuo does not issue commands but instead operates as a gatekeeper: mortals or lesser demons seeking access to the Infernal Archive, or requiring documents that bear the weight of false authority, must petition Yozhuo directly. This has made the Custodian of Lost Seals one of the more actively accessible powers within the Youwéng for those willing to engage the Infernal Realms through intermediaries.

### Mortal Engagement

Yozhuo is said to deal directly with mortals only rarely. Its transactions are reserved for those with sufficient cosmological understanding or ambition to recognize what a stolen or forged celestial seal might accomplish. A nobleman seeking to manufacture a reclassification order, a scholar attempting to recover a destroyed record, a cult operative needing documentation that will survive celestial audit—these are the mortals who might successfully petition Yozhuo.

The price is invariably specific: bring Yozhuo a document from the celestial bureaucracy, preferably something that bears an actual seal. Yozhuo collects such items, studying their composition and authentication marks in its repository. In return, the petitioner receives access to the Infernal Archive or a single seal that can be affixed to a mortal-crafted document to grant it temporary authority within the classified cosmos. Such authority is fragile—celestial inspectors have means of detecting counterfeit seals—but brief windows of legitimacy have proven sufficient for acts of significant consequence.

The [[affiliation-nuotsourui|Nuotsou Rui]] maintains discrete contact attributed to Yozhuo through a single trusted intermediary, a former records clerk who understands the protocols of celestial authentication. The cult's archive corruptions and falsified directives often carry Yozhuo's seals, a partnership of mutual interest: the cult provides Yozhuo with fresh celestial documents to study and copy, while Yozhuo provides the cult with the tools to conduct its bureaucratic sabotage.

## Outside the Ladder

A person the Bureau of All Records has noticed has a file opened and the forgery known, and the cult is finished with them. Where an orthodox clerk fears being struck from the rolls, here the catastrophe is being entered on them.
