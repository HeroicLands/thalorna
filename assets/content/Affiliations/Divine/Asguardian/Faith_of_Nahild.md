No Nordlands hof of the Ten will take Náhild's offering, and no kingdom lets her clergy stand in the open, so nobody can tell you where one of her hofs is. A landvördr who has ridden the dues circuit for twenty winters gives a new clerk the one rule she has about them: "You will never be shown a hof of hers, and nobody who has stood in one will tell you where. If a hof will not say where it stands or who keeps it, ask what the secrecy is for. Her faith is the only one of the Ten the other nine call a heresy, and it survives because the hungers it feeds on survive in every generation: rage, grief nobody admits to, envy nobody settled."

What follows is what the cult's own accounts claim for it.

## The Goddess and the Eye

[[lore-nahilddty|Náhild]] is the goddess of the dead and of [[place-nulthey|Nulthey]], and she holds a deep contempt for life. She counts existence a blight on the cosmos and works to bring all creation to chaos and ruin. The other faiths teach that she does not torment the unworthy dead who dwindle on Nulthey but is simply indifferent to them; her contempt is for the living.

She draws on the [[lore-ginnauga|Eye of the Void]], Ginnauga, a primordial artifact of raw corruption from which flows [[lore-theshadow|the Shadow]], a chaos from outside creation that corrupts whatever it touches, mortal or divine. The need to gaze into the Eye drives her further into madness, and its influence corrupts everything near it, releasing a caustic shadow of dread and hopelessness into the mortal world. Images show her as a ghastly figure with eyes burning with a baleful light and a morbid grin fixed on her face, with the Eye hung around her neck, the source of her strength and the symbol of her hatred of the living.

## What Her Followers Do

Her followers are devoted to spreading death and chaos. Her priests perform ritual human sacrifices, believing the offerings increase chaos and Náhild's power and hasten the end of all life, and through those ceremonies they seek her favor and dominion in the ruined world she envisions.

She commands an army of the undead, reanimated by her dark magic. [[lore-nagengir|Nágengir]] ("the favored and terrible") are her elite warriors and sorcerers, guardians of her temples and leaders of her undead armies. [[lore-haugverdir|Hrúmverdir]] are the corpses of the weak and the dishonored, mindless servants and soldiers in her legions.

A worshipper carries an obsidian shard for darkness and the void, a bone fragment as a reminder of mortality and of power over the dead, or a black candle used to honor her dominion over the dead. Two relics belong to the faith. **The Eye of the Void** is unique, a cursed artifact said to amplify the powers of death and chaos and venerated in every temple of Náhild. **Nightblade** is a ceremonial dagger used in ritual sacrifices, believed able to sever the soul from the body.

## The Clergy

The cult confers standing by teaching, and it teaches in secret:

- The Betrayer: named a hidden hof, an initiate or a godi to outsiders, and loses the shelter the cult's secrecy owed him.
- Her Offerer: turns to Náhild and brings a hidden hof's offering, trusted enough to share its secrecy but not yet taught its observances.
- Her Initiate: taught the cult's secret observances and the location of a hidden hof.
- Her Godi / Gydja: takes the sacrifices of a hidden hof and teaches the observances that raise an offerer to an initiate.

Embalming is the one skill the faith teaches.

## Ceremonies and Festivals

The cult's low ceremonies are held in the presence of the clergy. At the **Night Vigil**, a monthly gathering, followers meditate on the inevitability of death and decay. At the **Offering of Shadows** they give a piece of their essence, blood or hair, to Náhild, seeking to spread chaos in the world.

The high ceremonies are secret. The **Rite of Eternal Darkness** is performed by the clergy to call down power from the Eye of the Void and deepen their connection to Náhild. In the **Binding of Souls** high-ranking clergy create Helspawn, the [[lore-undead|undead]], by sacrificing folk victims to serve Náhild in the afterlife.

Two festivals mark the year. **Night of the Dead** falls on the longest night and celebrates Náhild's dominion over the dead, with processions that include elaborate sacrifices assisted by the nágengir. The **Veil of Shadows** is an annual gathering at which the faithful witness the temporary merging of the mortal world with Náhild's domain, causing widespread fear and chaos.

## Ordeals for Favor

Five ordeals win her favor, and every one of them works against the living. In the **Walk of the Dead** the faithful cross a battlefield with the help of an unholy relic, summoning, confronting and surviving the spirits of the courageous fallen who would otherwise go to [[place-valsal|Valsal]]. The ordeal turns their souls onto Nulthey instead, to wander aimless and unfulfilled among the unworthy until they fade to nothing. In the **Blood Oath** the participant pledges their soul to Náhild in a binding pact made in blood, in exchange for dark powers. The **Grave Descent** is a trial in which the faithful spend a night buried alive, relying only on their faith in Náhild to survive. In the **Bone Ritual** participants fashion weapons from the bones of the dead and use them successfully in combat. In the **Shadow Hunt** the faithful track and capture a living sacrifice under cover of darkness and offer it to Náhild at dawn.
