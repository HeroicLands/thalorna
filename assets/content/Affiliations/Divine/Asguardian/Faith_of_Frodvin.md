The first thing a hofsmadr learns at a hov of the Faith of Frodvin is to carry water. Nobody explains it; you carry it to the herb beds in the morning, to the sickroom at noon and out to the field benches when the [Blessing of the Fields]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} comes round, and sometime in the second year you notice that all three trips are the same prayer. A young hofsmadr bent over a drying rack of yarrow says it better: "People come here wanting to be told the god is kind. He is. But what he does with his kindness is hand you a bucket."

[[lore-frodvindty|Frodvin]] is the compassionate god of fertility, agriculture, peace and healing, and the faith holds a special place among farmers and rural folk, who believe their harvests and herds depend on his blessing. Temples of Frodvin are rare, and they are invariably attached to hospitals: they serve the sick first. The clergy are mendicant, humble in dress and in habit, and are most often found working beside their flock in fields and glens. They hold their services outdoors, under the open sky, so that worshippers seek to meet the god directly through the earth and what it yields.

## The God of Peace and Healing

In its teaching, Frodvin is also a god of peace, a divine mediator who strives to resolve conflicts and promote understanding, and his influence keeps a community together and prevents discord. He is shown as a serene, nurturing figure who radiates kindness and warmth, usually with sheaves of wheat or fruits, in simple dress that reflects his closeness to humble rural life.

A worshipper carries a sheaf of wheat for fertility and agriculture, an oak leaf for strength and growth in nature, a healing herb bundle for the rites of healing and blessing, or a river stone for the flow of life and the nurturing aspect of water. Two relics belong to the faith. **[The Staff of Growth]{.name data-type="miscgear"}** is unique, a divine staff said to carry Frodvin's power and to make barren land fertile. The **[Healing Chalice]{.name data-type="miscgear"}** is a sacred cup used in rituals, believed to hold the power of healing when it is filled with blessed water.

## The Clergy

The ladder is the one every blet-keeping faith of the Ten shares, and in Frodvin's faith each rung belongs to a valley infirmary's hov:

Refused the Blet
: denied the offering at a valley infirmary's hov, and cut from the clergy and from the care it extends to the sick.

Bletmadr
: attends the blet at a valley infirmary's hov and shares its harvest-offering.

Hofsmadr
: given to a valley infirmary's hov young, laboring at its herb-lore and its sick before any claim to the priesthood.

Gudi / Gudja
: ordained to keep a valley infirmary's rites and tend its sick.

Hofgudi / Hofgudja
: consecrates valley infirmaries' hovs, ordains their gudar, and performs the great blet at a season's turning.

The skills the faith teaches are herbalism, the physician's art, survival, animalcraft and agriculture. Two kinds of servant belong to the god: the **Sylvans**, forest spirits who aid the growth and protection of the natural world, and the **Healing Wisps**, ethereal beings that guide the hands of Frodvin's priests in the healing arts.

## Ceremonies and Festivals

The low ceremony is the **Blessing of the Fields**, a monthly ritual, held preferably outdoors, at which the faithful bless their crops to ensure a bountiful harvest and the sick and wounded receive the priests' blessing. There are two high ceremonies. At the **[Rite of Renewal]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** the clergy renew the earth's fertility, often by planting sacred seeds. At the **[Grove's Embrace]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** high priests enter a sacred grove to commune with Frodvin and seek his guidance for the coming seasons.

Two festivals mark the year. The **[Festival of the First Harvest]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}** opens the harvest season with feasts, music and communal blessings. The **[Day of Peace]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}** is an annual event given to harmony among communities, with rites of conflict resolution and reconciliation.

## Ordeals for Favor

Five ordeals are held to win Frodvin's favor, and each asks for something a field or a sickbed would ask. The **[Pilgrimage of Growth]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** is a journey to sacred sites where the faithful plant trees and restore life to barren land. In the **[Healing Trial]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** a participant must heal a grievously injured creature using only the natural remedies attributed to Frodvin. The **[Peacewalk]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** asks the faithful to mediate a dispute between feuding families or tribes and bring them to peace. In the **[Blessing of the Wild]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** a participant survives alone in the wilderness, relying on the connection to nature and on Frodvin's guidance. In the **[Seed of Life]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** the faithful nurture a seedling through harsh conditions until it grows into a strong, healthy tree.
