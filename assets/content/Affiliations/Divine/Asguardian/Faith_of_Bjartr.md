The Faith of Bjart gathers under trees rather than in a hov. At the **[Blessing of Light]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}**, a priest stands a worshipper in a forest clearing at dusk and asks that the light come between that person and despair, and for most of Bjart's followers that one ceremony is the whole of their devotion. A pilgrim who walks to a northern grove each autumn puts it plainly: "I will never be a priest, and nobody there expects it of me. I take the blessing, I sleep easier for a season, and I gho home. The faith asks nothing more than that I came."

[[lore-bjartrdty|Bjart]] is the Asguardian name for [[lore-goddreams|the God of Dreams]], a deity older than any human pantheon, honored by the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale Folk]] and the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]] since before humankind existed. The Aureldian peoples know the same god as [[lore-zunelasdty|Zunelas]]. Among northern humans the faith draws its worshippers through the god's dream aspect, understood as the power to guide, inspire and foretell through the landscapes of sleep. That makes it unusual within the [[affiliation-asguardian|Asguardian Pantheon]] (the Ten): where most northern faiths are led by warriors, jarls and seafarers, Bjart's human followers tend to be seers, healers and people who look for wisdom in vision rather than in the sword.

## What the Light Means

Ask the pilgrim what Bjart's light is and the answer is wider than the sun. It is clarity and hope and the truth that drives off shadow, and the faithful hold that it feeds living things and lets them grow in step with the natural world. Images of the god show an aura of radiance around a serene figure of boundless compassion, ringed by the things that carry the elder races' bond with the world: ancient trees, intricate runes, luminous crystals. That bond is older than human memory.

A worshipper carries one of four objects. A sunstone stands for light and clarity, an owl's feather for wisdom and guidance in dreams, a silver leaf for purity and the tie to living growth, and a crystal dewdrop for the ethereal nature of dreams. The faith's one named relic is **[The Lightstone]{.name data-type="miscgear"}**, a radiant gem believed to be a fragment of Bjart's divine essence.

## Who Keeps the Rites

Human adherents of Bjart have taken the Sinalean clergy structure, the only faith of the Ten to do so; [[lore-goddreams|the God of Dreams]] sets out the whole of it. The structure is nearly flat. The faithful come to the rites and take the priests' blessing. An [[lore-tindesarrnk|Ilthorin]] ("seer") is newly called and learns dream-reading, healing and the tending of groves under an [[lore-calathirrnk|Ansorun]]'s guidance, in a bond closer to apprentice and mentor than to subordinate and superior. Every Ansorun ("long-watcher") keeps the rites, cares for the faithful and stewards the sacred places, and every Ansorun stands equal to every other. Any one of them may call the **[Aulirarno]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}** ("council of radiance"), an open convocation with no presiding officer that decides what touches the whole faith by deliberation rather than decree.

The faith's skills are the ones a seer and a grove-keeper need: dream interpretation, archery, survival, tracking, trance, communication with the elder races and prophecy. Two kinds of servant belong to the god. The **Luminae**, beings of pure light, carry his messages, and the **Dreamwardens** are spirits who roam the spirit realm and guide souls through their dreams.

## Ceremonies and Festivals

The Blessing of Light is the faith's low ceremony, held by preference in a forest, where the faithful receive a blessing to inspire their connection to light and nature and to ward off darkness and despair. The high ceremonies belong to the clergy. At the **[Rite of Illumination]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** the clergy gather to deepen their connection with Bjart's light, and the gathering often brings visions or inspiration; at the **[Weaving of Dreams]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** the high-ranking priests raise protective wards and dreamscapes over the community, so that its sleep is peaceful and its dreams useful.

Two festivals mark the year. The **[Festival of the Radiant Dawn]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}** falls at the summer solstice, the season of [[lore-midsumar|Midsomar]], and honors Bjart's light with grand processions, music and the lighting of massive bonfires. The **[Night of Dreams]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}** is the annual gathering where the faithful share their dreams and visions and look to Bjart for wisdom and guidance. Of the two, the Night of Dreams is the one a newcomer should attend first: it asks only that you listen.

## Ordeals for Favor

Four ordeals are held to win Bjart's particular favor, and each tests a different part of a seer. In the **[Dreamwalk]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** the faithful travel through the spirit realm, facing their fears and desires until they emerge enlightened. The **[Elder's Blessing]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** is a pilgrimage to the ancient groves or halls of the elder races, seeking their wisdom and Bjart's favor. The **[Luminous Quest]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** sends the faithful to retrieve a lost artifact of light from a dark and dangerous place. In the **[Rite of Pure Vision]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}** a participant endures a period of sensory deprivation and comes out, if the rite works, with true vision and clarity.
