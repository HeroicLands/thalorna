## Overview

Lem'Thubâ'i is the sworn priesthood of [[lore-thubaidty|Thubâ'i]], god of the flood and of prosperity, drawn from those trained to read the river and the calendar together. Its priests keep the flood-records that set the planting season and perform the rites by which the year's prosperity is formally asked for. A poor reading from this order unsettles granary stores and tax assessments alike, since both are set against its forecast.

## Character

The order works to the rhythm of the river and the calendar. It is answerable for its forecast, since the planting season, the granary stores and the tax assessments are all set against its flood-records. Its rites ask for the year's prosperity in the form its priests keep.

## Relations

- [[affiliation-lutthubai|Lut-Thubâ'i]]—The temple of the same god, whose priests read the river's rise.
- The granaries and the tax assessments—Both are set against the order's forecast.
