A frontier cult, and deliberately so: Qeztu is patron of four of the ten border selatu and rare in the gentler valley, his worship strongest where the empire's writ is thinnest. His great war-temple stands at Balehen, the double-walled fortress-capital commanding the river at the first cataract.
