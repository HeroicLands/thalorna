"Nobody asks for Qeztu in the valley," a **Priest of the Frontier Garrisons** tells a conscript on his first night at a fort beyond the first cataract. "The valley likes gentler gods. Out here you will."

**Qeztu**, the war-god, keeps a frontier cult, and deliberately so. He is patron of four of the ten border selatu and rare in the valley, and his worship is strongest where the empire's writ is thinnest. The four are the [[affiliation-selatzuzllgl|Selat of Zu-Zalu-Legulu]], the [[affiliation-seltqltlnlgr|Selat of Qelt-Len-Legir]], the [[affiliation-selatgarqezt|Selat of Gar-Qeztu]] and the [[affiliation-selatbelgnzl|Selat of Belgen-Zalu]]. The name of a selat is no guide: the [[affiliation-selatzuqeztu|Selat of Zu-Qeztu]] belongs to [[lore-uqaadty|Uqa'â]].

## Where the Cult Serves

His great war-temple stands at [[place-balehen|Balehen]], the double-walled fortress-capital that commands the river at the first cataract. The **Keeper of the War-Temple** holds it, and it is the house the southern march measures itself by.

The **Priest of the Frontier Garrisons** serves the forward forts beyond the first cataract, the empire's hardest postings, where the cult is stronger than anywhere else in the realm. The **Priest of the Chariot-Corps** serves the garrison heartland of Gar-Qeztu, where the chariot-corps drill-grounds and the officer schools are.

The cult keeps the shared temple ladder of Wazu, Lem'Nelgir and Thâz'Lekhau. A selat god's Thâz'Lekhau carries real weight inside the selat and very little outside it.
