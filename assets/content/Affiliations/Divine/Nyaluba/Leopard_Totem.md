The **Leopard Totem** is the guide of the [[affiliation-nylbtrblntn|Sengala]], the northern trade-tribe—stealth, patience, and solitude. The Sengala are the outward-facing clan: intermediaries with the rainforest peoples to the north and, through them, with civilizations the other four clans know only as rumor. They are the most polyglot of the Nyáluba and the most stratified, with a class of professional traders whose standing approaches a hereditary specialist caste, an arrangement the other clans regard with a mixture of admiration and unease.

The leopard suits them, and they say so. It is the guide that works alone, moves without being seen, waits as long as waiting takes, and is present in country it does not obviously hold. A Sengala trader far from the savannah is understood to be doing exactly what the guide does.

## Veneration

Because Sengala parties travel, the leopard is the Nyáluba guide most often addressed outside Nyáluba territory—and this raises a question the tradition otherwise does not face, since spirits are territorial and local by nature. The Sengala answer is that the guide accompanies the clan rather than the country, and that the ordeal binds the person, not the ground. Shamans of the other four clans consider this convenient, and do not accept it.
