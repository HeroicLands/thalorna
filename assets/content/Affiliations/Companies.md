There are various groups of individuals of note in the world of Thalorna. The following is a short and incomplete list:

- [[affiliation-blckpnwlvs|The Blackpine Wolves]]: A volatile band of road bandits operating in the Vrystwald, preying on merchant caravans along the old Vylarian trade road. Led by the ruthless former serf Dágulf Véthar, the six outlaws are held together only by desperation – and riddled with internal tensions that threaten to tear them apart.
- [[affiliation-slntlncmpny|Silent Talon]]: A mercenary company based out of Provènzia, specializing in both battlefield operations and covert missions. Led by Captain Brànwâal Dôrgaar, a dispossessed noble seeking to reclaim his family's lost lands, the five-member band has earned a reputation for discretion and precision in morally gray contracts.
- [[affiliation-thetamzir|The Crew of the Tamzîr]]: The crew of a weathered coastal trader plying the Vylarian Sea under Captain Eshârum Tarûzî, a disillusioned war veteran. They juggle legitimate trade with smuggling and contract work that respectable captains refuse – all while harboring the fugitive Serentia siblings, one of whom possesses extraordinary and dangerous arcane power.
- [[affiliation-tghsvrnzrnsh|Tîgh'Savâran of the Zarnûsh]]: An elite warband of twelve mounted horse-archers and lancers from the Khazryn steppe, sworn to the Zarnûsh tribe. Led by the scarred veteran Bahrazad Darûsh, they face an existential crisis: drought, rival raids, and the kidnapping of women and children by the Shirzâri – with one season to set things right or watch their tribe fragment.
- [[affiliation-wldjgrrndgrskld|Wuldjagâr of the Gárskald]]: A tribal hunting company of seven skilled hunters from the central Vrystwald, tasked with feeding the Gárskald clan of two hundred. Ecological collapse – shifting elk migrations and a mysterious boar plague – is driving them beyond the Blackthorn Ridge into wild, uncharted territory where the elders warn of things that are "not animals."
- [[affiliation-irnwlvscmpny|The Iron Wolves Company]]: A Byzarian free company that takes the contracts other companies refuse – village suppressions, unlawful concerns, broken strikes – and charges a premium for never flinching. Captain Lukás Themiras runs it as a business; the massacre at Trianthion, twenty-six years past, is the reputation he trades on and the wound the company has never closed.
- [[affiliation-sekhetneru|Sekhet'Neru]]: A hereditary order of hunter-wardens from the Medjay caste, charged under a seven-generation Per-Aá's charter with conducting sacred ritual hunts of hippopotami and crocodiles along the Upper Taméresh River. With herds surging to unprecedented levels and the temple priesthood questioning their worthiness, Steersman Amûnmat Khâfirah has called for the most ambitious hunt in a generation to save their charter – and their legacy.
