Companies are among the groups of individuals of note in [[place-worldthlrn|the world of Thalorna]]. The following is a short and incomplete list:

[[affiliation-blckpnwlvs|The Blackpine Wolves]]
: A volatile band of road bandits operating in the Vristwald, preying on merchant caravans along the old Vylarian trade road. Led by the ruthless former serf Dagbán yra Vethar wur Thrareth, the six outlaws are held together only by desperation—and riddled with internal tensions that threaten to tear them apart.

[[affiliation-slntlncmpny|Silent Talon]]
: A mercenary company based out of Provenzia, specializing in both battlefield operations and covert missions. Led by Captain Branval Dorgal, a dispossessed noble seeking to reclaim his family's lost lands, the five-member band has earned a reputation for discretion and precision in morally gray contracts.

[[affiliation-thetamzir|The Crew of the Tamzir]]
: The crew of a weathered coastal trader plying the Vylarian Sea under Captain Zabuzum Jakuduni, a disillusioned war veteran. They juggle legitimate trade with smuggling and contract work that respectable captains refuse—all while harboring the fugitive Serenthis siblings, one of whom possesses extraordinary and dangerous arcane power.

[[affiliation-tghsvrnzrnsh|Tigh'Savaran of the Zarnush]]
: An elite warband of twelve mounted horse-archers and lancers from the Khazryn steppe, sworn to the Zarnush tribe. Led by the scarred veteran Bahrazad Darush, they face an existential crisis: drought, rival raids, and the kidnapping of women and children by the Shirzari—with one season to set things right or watch their tribe fragment.

[[affiliation-wldjgrrndgrskld|Wuljagar of the Garskald]]
: A tribal hunting company of seven skilled hunters from the central Vristwald, tasked with feeding the Garskald clan of two hundred. Ecological collapse—shifting elk migrations and a mysterious boar plague—is driving them beyond the [[place-blackthornrdg|Blackthorn Ridge]] into wild, uncharted territory where the elders warn of things that are "not animals."

[[affiliation-irnwlvscmpny|The Iron Wolves Company]]
: A Bizarian free company that takes the contracts other companies refuse—village suppressions, unlawful concerns, broken strikes—and charges a premium for never flinching. Captain Filazur Zighidek runs it as a business; the massacre at [[place-trianthion|Trianthion]], twenty-six years past, is the reputation he trades on and the wound the company has never closed.

[[affiliation-zeghetnelgu|Zeghet'Nelgu]]
: A hereditary order of hunter-wardens from the Meglay caste, charged under a seven-generation Gar-Au's charter with conducting sacred ritual hunts of hippopotami and crocodiles along the Upper Zumelesh River. With herds surging to unprecedented levels and the temple priesthood questioning their worthiness, Steersman Azunmat Khelafiru has called for the most ambitious hunt in a generation to save their charter—and their legacy.
