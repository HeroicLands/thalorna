## Overview {#overview}

The **University of Péravel** is the largest campus of the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]] outside the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]], and it is held by the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]. It stands on both banks and on the islands of [[place-beravriver|the Berav]] at [[place-beravel|Péravel]], and it is not one building but a federation of courts, each with its own gate, endowment and table of fellows. In Élavendri it is the _zétmarendon_.

The crown chartered it about 230 AF, and the house the empire's _felzos_ took after the treaty became its oldest court ([[lore-elvtribute|The Tribute of Élavendre]]). It teaches law, letters, natural philosophy and the arcane to the children of the nobility and to the sons and daughters of merchants who can pay, and the kingdom's clerks, magistrates and physicians are mostly its graduates.

## What It Controls {#what-it-controls}

**The teaching.** The courts teach every discipline the Epistémion teaches, and the arcane to the few the Order's examiners place in a Zulen. A student with talent is entered on the Order's register by the Registrar before the first term ends, and the family is told it is an honor.

**The book trade.** The University licenses the copyists, binders and translators of the capital's book quarter. A scribe without a license sells nothing a court will buy, and a license is withdrawn for copying what the Licenser has not read.

**The collections.** Each chapterhouse keeps a collection of objects brought back from expeditions, cataloged by the Keeper of the Collections. The catalog gives each object its form and its place on the shelf and records no provenance.

**The expeditions.** A licensed expedition begins at the University: the Fitter of Expeditions gets the crown's license, the crown's guides out of [[place-liranel|Liranel]], and the hired guards who go where the guides do not.

## The Courts and the Zulen Houses {#courts}

The **Oldest Court** is the _felzos_'s house, a long hall of dark stone beside the river, and the Provost keeps its rolls. Beside it stand the courts that the nobility endowed in later centuries, each with a hall, a library and a garden.

Four of the six Zulen keep a chapterhouse in the University. The two opposed fellowships among them stand on opposite banks of the river, and the fellowships' reading rooms are shut to each other, as they are in every house of the Order.

**[[affiliation-fisera|Fisera]]** keeps the largest house and a herbarium. The fellowship of Earth holds the alchemists and the herb-wives who supply the capital's apothecaries, and its herbarium keeps the strip of bark from [[place-wkngstnd|the Waking Stand]] that has stayed green.

**[[affiliation-neumenos|Neumenos]]** keeps the house across the river, a gated hall with a cellar. The fellowship of Spirit is the one the Sekran supervises most closely, and its cellar holds the construct of root and stone brought out of [[place-wrghthllw|the Wrought Hollow]].

**[[affiliation-hidalis|Hidalis]]** keeps the infirmary, where the physicians of the fellowship of Water treat the poor of the river quarter for a fee that the Rector does not enter in the court's accounts.

**[[affiliation-zefaris|Zefaris]]** keeps the smallest house, a narrow tower by the bridge. The fellowship of Air handles the University's letters to the Order's other chapters and is, by general understanding, the Order's ear at Péravel.

[[affiliation-pirethos|Pirethos]] and [[affiliation-sideris|Sideris]] keep no house at the University, and their fellows lodge as guests in the courts.

## Ranks {#ranks}

A student enters a court at fourteen or fifteen and remains a Student until a Fellow of the court sponsors an examination for the closed shelves. A Reader tutors, copies and waits for a table to fall vacant, which may take years. A Fellow is elected by the table of a court and holds the stipend for life or until the court deprives them of it. A Master holds a chair, and every Master has a vote in the Chapter of Courts, which meets in the Oldest Court at the turn of each year and decides what the University teaches.

A person struck from the roll is refused the courts, the libraries and the collections, and the book quarter will not employ them. Readmission is not granted.

## Masters {#masters}

[Ranthor Párdalen]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, **the Rector**, is a Master of the Oldest Court, sixty, courteous, and learned in the law. He holds the University's charter in trust for the crown and keeps the Order warm and the queen informed. He does not discuss what the fellows do beyond the tree line, and says so with some regret.

[Néthara Halren]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, **the Keeper of the Collections**, is a Master of the Fisera house, who knows every object in the four collections by its weight in her hand. She is meticulous, and she is the only person at the University who knows where each object came from.

## The Present Quarrel {#present}

The Order's study of the [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] is planned in these courts, and the Grove Council objects to the whole of it. Its elders have petitioned the court at Péravel about objects taken from groves and holy wells, and the Rector has answered each petition with an invitation to submit it formally, which is to say, to wait.

The hiring of guards is the University's most visible embarrassment. The Fitter of Expeditions hires them in the inns of the river quarter at good pay and in rooms the University does not own, and the bards of [[affiliation-bardicolgs|the Bardic Colleges]] sing in the same inns about the guards who did not come back.

[[being-ielaraskir|Prince Yélaras]], schooled in these courts, has friends among the fellows who are planning a survey of the thin places for the summer. Whether the Order is helped or threatened by a prince of the blood who asks is a question its chapter has not settled.

:::secret {title="The ledger"}
The catalog records no provenance because the Keeper of the Collections keeps it elsewhere. Néthara Halren holds a private ledger, written in a cipher of her own, in which each object is entered with the ground it came from, the day it was cut or lifted, and the name of the guard who did it. She keeps it for the Order, so that an Araelwen can be found again, and for herself, because she believes the day will come when what has been taken must be put back. She has told no one which of those she thinks is the more likely.

The Fitter of Expeditions does not know the ledger exists. The Sekran does.

**Hook.** Someone in the book quarter has copied a page of the Keeper's cipher, and wants to know what it says.
:::
