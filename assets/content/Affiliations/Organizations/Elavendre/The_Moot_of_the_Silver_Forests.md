## Overview {#overview}

The **Moot of the Silver Forests** is the gathering of the shamen whose tribes share a border with [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]]. It sits four times a year, on the day after each of the great seasonal rites of the Pact at [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]], in the stone-walled council hall beside the ceremonial one. It is not a court and not a government. The Council of Elders in each tribe decides, and the Moot advises the elders on what the forest asks of the people who live beside it.

What the Moot holds is the people's standing with the wood. The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] keep the watches and the boundary, and the Moot keeps the terms: the birch law, the protocols for entering the outer margin, and the understanding with the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale]] who share the forest. Questions that touch the Pact and the wood together come to it before they come to the elders.

## Who Sits {#who-sits}

A seat belongs to a tribe, not to a person. Three tribes hold seats because their ground meets the wood: the [Dhárgandi]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} on the Lodge side, the [Tírindi]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} along the outer margin and the road to [[place-sourimael|Sóurimel]], and the [Thanandi]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} on the lower slopes of the [[place-drenavrspn|Drenavar Spine]] where the forest climbs toward it. Each tribe sends its senior shaman, who is a **Shaman of the Moot** for as long as the tribe seats him. A village shaman at the forest's edge may be heard at a sitting on a matter of his own ground and is not seated.

Each seated shaman brings a **Hearer**, an apprentice who sits behind him and says nothing. The Hearers carry the Moot's matters home and are the ones who will sit one day, so a shaman's choice of Hearer is the first thing his fellows weigh about him.

The Moot names a **Speaker** at the first sitting of the year; this year it is [[being-talmenatirin|Tálmena]] of the Tírindi. The Speaker keeps the order in which the shamen speak and speaks for none of them. A **Carrier to Ethalosse** is named as the need arises: the Áelendan carry to the stone court of [[place-ethalosse|Ethalosse]] what has been agreed and add nothing, and the Moot chooses the one who carries.

A shaman who turns his power to private ends is shut out of the Moot. His tribe is asked to seat no other in his place until the Moot has heard its reasons.

## How It Decides {#how-it-decides}

The Moot speaks until the shamen agree and then stops. It takes no vote, and it commands nobody: its agreement is carried to the elders of each tribe and to the Wardens of the forest, who act on it or do not. A tribe that ignores it has not broken any law, and its shaman will find his word weighed lightly at the next sitting.

Three kinds of business come to it:

- **The wood's report.** The Wardens of the Silver Forests tell the Moot what the margin has seen: a cut that should not have been made, a watch kept short, a visitor turned back. The shamen say what the Kindred of the wood would ask in return.
- **What the tribes owe between them.** The tribes share the margin's hunting, its paths and its grazing. The Moot settles who takes what and when, so that the quarrel stays out of the wood.
- **Who may be offered the outer margin.** A stranger the Áelendan mean to escort into the outer portions of the forest is named at the Moot first. The Wardens carry out the offer. The Moot does not set its terms, because those are set by the forest.

The Moot differs from the common council of the tribes. The council's delegates speak for their tribes and may argue without binding. The Moot's shamen speak for the tribes' standing with the Kindred and advise the elders. A matter of grazing between two tribes goes to the council. A matter of what the wood permits goes to the Moot.

## What It Is Fighting About {#present}

The bark of [[place-wkngstnd|the Waking Stand]] is before the Moot this season. A strip was cut from one of the trees and sold at [[place-lisere|Lísere]]. It lies green in a chapterhouse collection at [[place-beravel|Péravel]], and the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] has licensed a fellow of the University to come back for a living cutting with hired guards. The Wardens of the forests know bark was taken and are looking for the man who took it.

The question is what to carry to the Sinale, and when. The two seated shamen, of the Tírindi and the Thanandi, hold that the Carrier must go to Ethalosse now and say what has been done, because the wood is shared and its neighbors should hear it from the people who keep it. The Dhárgandi have no shaman seated this season, and their elders have sent word that nothing should be carried until the man is found, because the Áelendan carry exactly and add nothing, and a thing told half-known is carried wrong. A seat belongs to a tribe, and what the Carrier takes to the stone court is the Moot's word for all three tribes, so the Speaker will not send it while one seat is empty and its tribe disagrees. She has kept the question open through two sittings, and the Dhárgandi's young Hearer is the one who can close it, once he is seated.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

A party does not attend the Moot. It meets the Moot's findings: a Warden who will not say why the margin is closed this season, a village shaman who asks an outsider to carry nothing out of the wood, a Hearer sent down to the party's camp with a single instruction and no explanation. The Moot's guest is one a Warden has already named, and a guest sits where he is put and speaks when the Speaker looks at him.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The empty place.** The senior shaman of the Dhárgandi died at midwinter and her Hearer is young. The Moot has not seated him, and the Dhárgandi ask someone from outside to carry a message to a village shaman at the forest's edge whose word they want before the next sitting.
- **Named first.** A scholar of high reputation has been offered the outer margin, and the Moot names him at its next sitting. A party who vouched for him is asked by a Warden what he carries in his baggage.
- **The Carrier's silence.** The Carrier to Ethalosse came back from the stone court a day early and has said nothing to anyone. The shamen want to know what he was told, and he will not say.
