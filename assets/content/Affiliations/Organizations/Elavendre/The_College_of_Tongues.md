## Overview {#overview}

**The College of Tongues** stands at [[place-liranel|Liranel]], where the worked forest of [[place-lirve|Lirve]] gives way to the old wood and the country of the [[lore-flksinale|Sinale]]. It is the [[affiliation-bardicolgs|Bardic College]] that teaches languages, and its teaching is the one the Colleges most carefully ration. Every bard of Élavendre is taught something of the old tongue. The College of Tongues teaches it entire, with the carved rune-row and the fragments of Sinalean the College holds.

## What It Controls {#what-it-controls}

The College controls the **interpreters**. A house, the crown or a foreign court that needs a man who can speak [[skill-aelendlng|Áelendi]] to a tribe, or read a boundary cut in the Thurarth runes, comes to Liranel, because the College is the only place that licenses one. An interpreter carries what is said exactly, as the Áelendan carry what the Sinale say, and a licensed one is known for adding nothing.

It also holds the College's library of carved texts: wards, boundary markers, old poetry and the magical texts of great antiquity that draw scholars up from the lowlands to read them in the original. A visitor may read there under watch and may not copy.

## Ranks {#ranks}

A student learns to hear before learning to read. The Áelendi he hears is the highland speech, with its hard sounds put back, and it takes years before a lowland ear can tell the tribes apart by it. A Bard of Tongues is licensed to speak for a house or the crown and to read what is carved. A **Bard of the Circle** from this College is trusted to carry word to [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]].

## Masters {#masters}

The **Master of the College**, [[being-kalvirneilsv|Kálvirne Ílsevende]], keeps the curriculum and says which pupils go up the valleys. The **Examiner of Tongues** tests each pupil in speech and in the carved word before the license is given.

## The Present Quarrel {#present}

The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] sends survey parties up from Liranel every season, with the crown's guides and hired guards, and each party wants someone who can read what is cut at the margins. The College is divided on whether a pupil who takes an Order party's pay is still a student. The Master says a reader of a ward is a man who can step round it, and the College does not teach the reading to the Order's interpreters. A faction of the College's bards holds that the Order's coin keeps the library open.

The Wardens have asked the College for the names of the pupils who go. The College gives them, and is divided on whether to go on giving them.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The reader of a ward.** A College bard is on the road to the margin with an Order party, and a Warden asks the party to find out whether he reads or only copies.
- **Examiner's mark.** A pupil failed the Examiner's test and was licensed anyway by a house. The Examiner wants to know who signed.
- **What the library does not lend.** A text in the College's closed shelf was copied, and the copy turns up in a Péravel chapterhouse collection.
