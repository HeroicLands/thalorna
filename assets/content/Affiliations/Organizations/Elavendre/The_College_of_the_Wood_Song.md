## Overview {#overview}

**The College of the Wood-Song** stands at [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]], on the lake shore where the mountains of [[place-sorvanda|Sórvanda]] give way to the basin of [[place-ellive|Éllive]]. It is the [[affiliation-bardicolgs|Bardic College]] for the old magic of voice and story: song-binding, memory-shaping, and the forms of inspiration and dispiriting that work through narrative. Those arts are distinct from the institutional arcana of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]], and the College guards the difference.

## What It Controls {#what-it-controls}

The College controls the **workings**: the songs that do something. It does not teach them openly, and no outsider has seen the whole curriculum. A stranger who asks at the gate is told that the College teaches the harp and the long forms, which it does, and is shown the harp-room, which is real.

The College's library is songs, and a good part of it comes from the wood. The [[place-silvrfrsts|Silver Forests]] are the source of many bardic songs, and the College's older bards have gone up in the thaw to hear what the Áelendan will sing to them. The closed songs are kept apart from that library, and the **Keeper of the Closed Songs** decides each time whether one is sung.

## Ranks {#ranks}

A student learns the open workings in order and is told nothing of the closed ones. A Bard of the Wood-Song has the open workings of voice and may use them at a feast, where everyone has agreed to be moved, and not elsewhere. A **Bard of the Circle** from this College is given the closed songs to hold, and gives up the right to sing them for pay.

## Masters {#masters}

The **Master of the College** decides which workings are shown to which pupil. The **Keeper of the Closed Songs** holds what is not shown.

## The Present Quarrel {#present}

The Master has put a petition to the [[affiliation-groveconcl|Grove Council]]: that a working long suppressed be sung once, at the autumn fair. The Council shares the College's doctrine and its friction, and it has not ruled. Grant the petition and a song the College put away is sung before the fair's crowd, tribal families among them. Refuse it and the College and the Council part publicly.

The Order's fellows come to the fair with hired guards. The College treats them with civilized rivalry inside the town and says nothing outside it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The petition.** The Master asks a party to carry a letter to a grove-elder who will not receive anyone from the College, and to say nothing about what the letter asks.
- **What the lake keeps.** A bard of the Circle went out on the lake at night before the petition and has not come back, and the boatmen say the lake has been listening.
- **The fellow at the fair.** An Order fellow offers a party coin to write down one song at the fair, any song, and does not say why.
