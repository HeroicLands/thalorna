## Overview {#overview}

**The Envoys' College** stands at [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]], the kingdom's chief harbor, where every foreign vessel and every foreign word comes ashore. It is the [[affiliation-bardicolgs|Bardic College]] for diplomacy and statecraft: protocol, persuasion, and the particular art of gathering information without seeming to. Élavendren bards serve across the western kingdoms as ambassadors, historians and, on occasion, spies, and a great many of them were taught here.

## What It Controls {#what-it-controls}

The College controls the **envoys**. A house that needs a word carried to a foreign court, or the crown that needs one carried where it does not wish to be seen sending it, asks the Master for a Bard, and the College gives one under its own name. The College's protection is a custom older than any treaty: an envoy of the College is heard, and a harm done one is answered by every College.

It keeps a library of foreign courts, as other Colleges keep songs: what each house of each realm expects at table, what it will not be asked and in what order it must be addressed.

## Ranks {#ranks}

A student begins in the public gallery of the [[affiliation-twncnclprtaln|town council]] of Port Aelion, listening, and is not allowed to speak in the College for a year. A Bard of the College is licensed to be an envoy. A **Bard of the Circle** from this College is sent on the missions the crown does not name.

## Masters {#masters}

The **Master of the College**, [[being-ryethsemroth|Ryéthesand Sémrothende]], chooses which envoy goes where. An **Envoy of the College** carries its word. The College keeps no post for a spy, and its Master says so whenever asked.

## The Present Quarrel {#present}

The Envoys' College takes the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]'s patronage, which the Circle's other Colleges refuse, and the Order pays for a study of what the foreign courts say of the Order. The College of the Line, the College of Tongues and the College of the Wood-Song object to the money. The [[affiliation-groveconcl|Grove Council]], which shares the Circle's doctrine and its friction, overrides the Master's rulings on questions of wild magic that the College's envoys carry abroad.

The Master holds that an envoy must know the Order to speak to it, and that the Colleges survive on royal patronage and not their own purity.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The study.** A foreign envoy at Port Aelion tells a party that the College's report on his court was sold to someone else before it was delivered.
- **Under the College's name.** An Envoy of the College needs a party to walk with him to a house beyond Péravel, because a letter he carries is read by three people before it is delivered.
- **The Circle's displeasure.** A Bard of the Circle from another College asks a party to find out how much the Order paid.
