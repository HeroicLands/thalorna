## Overview {#overview}

**The College of the Line** stands at [[place-beravel|Péravel]], in the basin of [[place-beravriver|the Berav]], among the oldest of [[affiliation-bardicolgs|the Bardic Colleges]]. It teaches the whole of the bardic curriculum, as every College does, and it is known for one part of it: who is descended from whom. The kingdom's houses rest their standing on a claim that their blood runs back to the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]], and the College of the Line is where the claim is proved.

## What It Controls {#what-it-controls}

The College holds the **proofs**. In the year the mountains closed ([[lore-sorvandaclose|The Sórvanda Closing]]) every house set the Colleges to prove its descent, and the College of the Line kept the result: a recital, a roll and the names of the witnesses who heard it, for each house of the basin. The proof of the crown, of the house of [[affiliation-hsekirethel|Kírethel]], is among them, as is the line of [[affiliation-hsehalvireth|Hálvireth]], which the house sings at every feast.

The College also keeps the kingdom's histories, in the long forms a bard carries in memory, and it holds the Bardic Circle's hall at Péravel, where the Lay of the Tree Line ([[lore-treelinelay|The Lay of the Tree Line]]) is sung at every crowning.

## Ranks {#ranks}

A student begins by learning rolls by ear, hearing them recited and repeating them, because a written roll can be altered and a sung one is checked by everyone who knows the tune. A Bard of the Line is licensed to prove a line and to sing it. A **Bard of the Circle** from the College sits in the Circle that chooses a new house when a line of kings ends.

## Masters {#masters}

The **Master of the College** keeps the curriculum and decides which proofs it will hear. The **Archivist of the Line** keeps the library and the proofs. A third post, the **Singer at the Crowning**, is held by one of the Circle's bards, who sings the Lay before the monarch swears.

## The Present Quarrel {#present}

Prince Íelaras says in company that learning and the old blood are not enemies, and he means to answer the doubters of his house's claim by having his line sung at the Elanmere fair before people who remember the genealogy differently. The College that proved the line is the College that must sing it there. Its Master has not said whether it will.

The houses that cannot pay for a proof are a smaller quarrel and an older one. A house whose proof was never made keeps its claim by custom, and a College bard who is asked to hear it at a lower fee has to choose between the house and the Roll.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The unpaid line.** A lesser house of the basin asks a party to carry its recital to the Master, because it cannot afford a bard's fee and its claim is under dispute before the court.
- **The roll with a gap.** An Archivist asks the party to find the one witness to a proof who left Péravel in the year the mountains closed, and whose name is cut out of the roll.
- **At the fair.** A bard of the Line is to sing the heir's line at Elanmere, and someone has told the Master that a Tradition Keeper means to be in the crowd.
