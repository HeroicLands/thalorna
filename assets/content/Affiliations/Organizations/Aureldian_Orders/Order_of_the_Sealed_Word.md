_Aurèldían: Ordo Verbi Signati—"The Order of the Sealed Word"_

- **Type:** Fighting religious order
- **Patron:** [[affiliation-janus|Jánus]]—Aurèldían god of oaths, gates, beginnings, transitions, and the dualities that separate one state from another
- **Region:** [[place-aureldirgn|Aurèldía]]—chapter houses in all five Aurèldían kingdoms, with the mother-house at Vénustria in [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]]

## Overview

In the Aurèldían tradition, an oath sworn before Jánus is the bond that holds the social order together. The merchant's contract sealed in Jánus's name, the noble's pledge of fealty, the marriage vow, the treaty between rival cities—all rest on the assumption that the sworn word will be honored, and that some adequate remedy exists when it is not. The civil courts handle the routine: the contract dispute, the inheritance claim, the breached commercial covenant. When the civil courts cannot—when the oath-breaker is too powerful for the local magistracy, when the breach is so grievous that ordinary remedy fails, when the sworn matter is one the magistracy will not touch—Jánus's faith provides the alternative. The Order of the Sealed Word is that alternative.

The order is, in effect, a knightly enforcement arm for Jánus's covenant. Its sworn brothers ride to where a binding oath has been broken in a manner the petitioner has been unable to address through ordinary channels, hear the matter, and—if the breach is established—compel the oath-breaker to remedy, by trial-by-combat if required and by force if necessary. The order does not displace the civil law; it answers when the civil law has been unable to. Its decisions are not, formally, binding on any kingdom's courts, but in practice they are honored because the order has earned, over centuries, the reputation of refusing petitions it cannot substantiate and ruling without favor on those it can.

## Membership and Structure

A candidate for the order must be a sworn devotee of Jánus, must be of sound character (an examination the order takes seriously, including discreet inquiry into the candidate's prior dealings), and must pass the order's examinations: martial competence, the law of binding oaths in Aurèldían tradition, the recognized procedures for trial-by-combat, the rhetorical conduct of an oath-hearing, and a personal interview before the **Council of the Seal**. The candidacy process typically takes three years, with substantial attrition.

There are four working ranks:

- **Sworn Brother of the Seal** (_Frater Signati_)—the working knight; rides his circuit or answers petitions assigned by his chapter.
- **Senior Brother** (_Frater Senior_)—elevated by chapter vote; permitted to take wards (apprentices), to convene formal oath-hearings independently, and to sit on the Council of the Seal.
- **Magister of the Order** (_Magister Ordinis_)—chapter leader; one per chapter house; presides over chapter affairs and represents the chapter before the Council.
- **First Magister** (_Magister Primus_)—the order's chair; one position; chosen by conclave of the Magisters for a fifteen-year term.

The order has chapter houses in each Aurèldían kingdom, but its operational presence concentrates where commercial life and oath-density are highest: the Tarvénian capital, the Provenzian merchant cities, the Élavendren judicial seats. The mother-house at Vénustria holds the order's archives—including the master register of every contested oath the order has ruled on in its three-century history.

## Doctrine and Practice

The order swears three principles:

- **The Word Holds**—that a sworn oath in Jánus's name binds the swearer absolutely until released by the oath's terms, by the oathed-to party, or by Jánus's faith. There are no other releases.
- **The Hearing Is Rendered**—that an oath-hearing convened by the order proceeds to its conclusion regardless of the parties' rank, station, or political weight. A petitioner whose oath has been broken by a king is owed the same hearing as a petitioner whose oath has been broken by a merchant.
- **The Combat Honored**—that where the law of the kingdom in question permits trial-by-combat, and where the matter is one in which combat is doctrinally appropriate, a sworn brother will offer combat on the petitioner's behalf if no other combatant is available. The order's brothers are trained for this work; the offer is not made lightly and is not declined without cause.

The order's procedures are formal and ritualized. A petition is presented at a chapter house, examined for substance, and either accepted (and the petitioner's claim assigned a brother who will travel to the alleged oath-breaker), or rejected (with explanation). The accepted petition triggers a journey—the brother rides to the oath-breaker's location, presents the order's writ, and convenes an oath-hearing under the order's procedures. The hearing reviews the original oath, the alleged breach, and the offered defense. The ruling is delivered in Jánus's name and recorded.

## Notable Members

- **First Magister Anselmo Veroniani**—current First Magister; a man in his sixties; presiding over the order in a period of increasing tension between the order and several of the Aurèldían crowns.
- **The Council of the Seal**—twelve Senior Brothers drawn from the five kingdoms; meets quarterly at Vénustria.

## Relations

- **The Aurèldían Pantheon's Priesthood**—religious foundation; the order is doctrinally subordinate to Jánus's senior priesthood but operationally independent.
- **The five Aurèldían Crowns**—the order operates in each kingdom under royal license, periodically renewed. The renewal has, in recent generations, become a political question; the order's rulings against powerful noble interests have made some crowns reluctant to confirm the order's standing.
- **The [[affiliation-crtgrphrsgldbyzrnlg|The Cartographers' Guild of the Byzarian League]]** and other professional guilds—clients in oath-disputes that the guild internal processes have been unable to resolve.
- **The [[affiliation-twinbldrdr|Twinblade Order]]**—sister order, also sworn to Jánus; the two orders coordinate on matters requiring both the Sealed Word's juridical authority and the Twinblade's threshold-guarding presence.
- **The [[affiliation-ordershnvw|The Order of the Ashen Vow]] and [[affiliation-ironcinder|Iron Cinder]]**—Black Flame orders; the relationship is mutually hostile but procedurally correct. The Black Flame's heresy is a matter for the religious courts of the Sacred Forge, not the order; the order does not pursue Black Flame members unless a specific oath-breach is at issue.

## Identifying Marks

A Sworn Brother wears the order's surcoat—deep blue with the Jánus sigil (a two-faced head facing both directions over a sealed scroll) in gold at the breast. The brother's writ of office—the parchment empowering him to convene an oath-hearing—is sealed in red wax bearing the chapter's mark and the brother's personal sigil; the writ is the visible token of his authority. Senior Brothers wear an additional pendant in the form of a small bronze key—a reference to Jánus's gates and to the keys to the order's archive at Vénustria.

## Plot Hooks

- **The King's Broken Word.** A petitioner has presented to a chapter house an oath broken by a sitting king. The petition is substantiated. The chapter must accept it under the doctrine that the hearing is rendered regardless of rank—and must do so knowing that the kingdom's renewal of the order's license is up for review.
- **The Rival's Petition.** Two merchants have presented mutually exclusive petitions arising from the same disputed oath; each claims the other is the oath-breaker. The chapter must rule on which petition to hear first, knowing the procedural choice will be decisive.
- **The Compromised Brother.** A Sworn Brother is suspected of having ruled in favor of a petitioner in exchange for a substantial bribe. The accusation is from a credible source but evidence is thin. The First Magister will have to decide whether to summon the brother to formal hearing—which, if the accusation proves false, will damage the brother's career and the order's confidence in its internal investigations.
- **The Combat Demanded.** A petition has been accepted and the oath-breaker has demanded trial-by-combat against the assigned brother. The oath-breaker is a celebrated swordsman; the brother is competent but not extraordinary; the doctrine requires the offer of combat to be honored. The chapter must determine whether to send the assigned brother or to reassign the matter to a brother of better blade.
