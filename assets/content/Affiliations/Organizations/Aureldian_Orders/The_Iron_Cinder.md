_Aurèldían: Cinis Ferreus—"Iron Cinder"_

- **Type:** Covert fighting religious order (heretical to the mainstream Aurèldían faith)
- **Patron:** [[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]]—Vúlcan as the conflagration of the mailed fist
- **Region:** [[place-aureldirgn|Aurèldía]]—no fixed bases; operates as a network of cells with no permanent stations; coordinated from sites that change every season

## Overview

Where the [[affiliation-ordershnvw|Order of the Ashen Vow]] is the Black Flame's doctrinal arm—sworn to a public theology of purification and committed to acts of cleansing whose justifications are themselves part of the doctrine—the Iron Cinder is its operational arm. The Iron Cinder does not preach. It does not justify. It does not, in the conventional sense, exist. What it does is the work that the Ashen Vow's doctrinal commitments make difficult: the targeted assassination, the discrete arson, the quiet removal of a witness who has become inconvenient, the destruction of an archive that has begun to record what the Black Flame would prefer not to be recorded.

The Iron Cinder is small. Estimates by the orthodox authorities put its sworn membership at perhaps forty operatives across the whole of Aurèldía, though the precision of these estimates is suspect because the order has been very effective at concealing its own numbers. It is also, by the consistent report of those who have encountered it, exceptionally capable: members are recruited from the most physically and tactically gifted of Ashen Vow candidates, are trained beyond ordinary order discipline, and operate with a level of professional ruthlessness that orthodox investigators have privately compared to the [[affiliation-velvethand|Velvet Hand]]'s direct-action cadre. The comparison would offend both organizations equally.

The Iron Cinder operates on the central assumption that, if exposed, it will be destroyed. The Sacred Forge would condemn it; the Aurèldían Crowns would suppress it; the Ordo Arcanis would hunt it; the Jánus orders would refuse it sanctuary; even the Ashen Vow's sympathetic noble protectors would be unable to publicly defend it. The order's structure, its operational discipline, and its working culture all reflect this assumption.

## Membership and Structure

Members are not recruited; they are selected. The selection is made by the **First Cinder** (the order's chair) from among Ashen Vow brothers who have demonstrated, over years of orthodox order service, the specific combination of physical capability, theological commitment, and personal psychology that the Iron Cinder's work requires. The selection is private. The selected brother is approached at a time and place of the First Cinder's choosing, presented with the offer, and either accepts (in which case he is detached from the Ashen Vow's cell structure and incorporated into the Cinder) or refuses (in which case the approach is denied to have occurred and the brother is left to continue his Ashen Vow service).

Refusal is rare. Acceptance is permanent.

There are two ranks beneath the First Cinder:

- **Cinder Hand** (_Manus Cineris_)—the working operative; assigned to operations by the First Cinder or by a Senior Cinder; operates alone or in small teams.
- **Senior Cinder** (_Senior Cineris_)—coordinates regional operations; reports directly to the First Cinder; perhaps four to six at any time.

The **First Cinder** (_Cinis Primus_) is the order's chair; one position; chosen by the surviving Senior Cinders on the death or incapacity of the prior holder; identity is among the most closely guarded secrets in Aurèldían heretical circles.

## Doctrine and Practice

The order does not, in any formal sense, have doctrine. It has _operational principles_:

- **The Operation Completed**—that an assigned operation is carried out to its specified end. An operation aborted in mid-execution is a failure that compromises every future operation.
- **The Mark Unrecorded**—that the order's involvement in any operation is, to the extent possible, indeterminate after the fact. A target's death looks like an accident, an illness, a private quarrel; a building's burning looks like a candle-fire or a lightning-strike; a missing archive looks like clerical incompetence.
- **The Brother Silenced**—that a Cinder Hand captured by the orthodox authorities must, by the same principle as the Ashen Vow's Sanctity of the Secret, find means to silence himself before being made to speak. The Cinder discipline is harder: the brother is trained from his induction in the techniques and the doctrinal acceptance of his own silencing.

Operational training is exhaustive: hand-to-hand combat with concealable weapons, the alchemy of incendiaries and poisons, the techniques of forced entry and quiet exit, the rhetoric of impersonation, the construction of false trails, the maintenance of cover identities sometimes held for years before activation. A Cinder Hand frequently lives, for long periods, an ordinary public life as a merchant or a clerk or a journeyman of some craft, activated only when needed.

The order does not maintain weapons of military scale. Operations are conducted with what one or two operatives can carry concealed and what local circumstances can supply.

## Notable Operations (attributed; none formally confirmed)

- The arson at the Sacred Forge cathedral library at Provenzal, twelve years ago, that destroyed a substantial portion of the Forge's catalog of suppressed Black Flame doctrine; widely attributed to the Cinder but never investigated to conclusion.
- The death of the Vénustrian Magister of the [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|Order of the Sealed Word]] seven years ago, ruled to be from natural causes; the timing—three days before he was to issue a major writ against a Black Flame-sympathetic baron—has produced persistent suspicion.
- The disappearance of three Ordo Arcanis investigators in the Tarvénian highlands four years ago; bodies never recovered; investigation closed.

The order has, by its own discipline, neither confirmed nor denied involvement in any of these.

## Relations

- **The [[affiliation-ordershnvw|Order of the Ashen Vow]]**—sister order; theological foundation; operational coordination. The two orders share doctrine and senior leadership lines but maintain strict operational separation. An Ashen Vow brother does not know which of his former brothers have been selected into the Cinder.
- **The [[affiliation-sacredforge|Sacred Forge]]**—the primary theological enemy and the primary investigative threat. The Forge maintains a small cadre of investigators dedicated specifically to identifying Cinder operatives.
- **The [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|Order of the Sealed Word]] and [[affiliation-twinbldrdr|Twinblade Order]]**—formal enemies; both orders carry standing arrangements with the Sacred Forge for cooperation against Cinder activity. The Cinder regards the Jánus orders as professional opponents to be respected, evaded where possible, and removed where evasion fails.
- **The Aurèldían Crowns**—formally suppressive; informally less so where Black Flame protection extends.
- **The [[affiliation-velvethand|Velvet Hand]]**—operational counterparty. The two organizations occasionally find themselves on opposite sides of the same operation; the recognition of professional capability has produced an informal protocol by which each will withdraw rather than engage the other if the recognition is mutual and the operations are not mutually exclusive.

## Identifying Marks

A Cinder Hand bears the Ashen Vow brand at his right forearm (the marker of his Ashen Vow induction, which preceded his Cinder selection) and a second, smaller mark at the inside of the left wrist: a single iron-black dot, given at Cinder induction. The second mark is the Cinder's own; its presence alongside the Ashen Vow brand is the certain identifier.

Outside operations, Cinders wear nothing identifiable and maintain ordinary public identities. Operatives have been known to maintain such identities for decades.

## Plot Hooks

- **The Investigator's File.** A Sacred Forge investigator has, over several years, assembled a file identifying—with substantial confidence—three Cinder Hands operating in a specific Aurèldían city. The investigator is preparing to deliver the file to her superiors. The Cinder has learned of the investigation and is preparing to address it; the investigator has not yet been identified by name but has been geographically located.
- **The Inherited Operation.** A Cinder Hand has died of natural causes mid-operation, leaving the operation incomplete and his cover identity at risk of exposure. The First Cinder must decide whether to assign a successor (who would have to be inducted into the operation's specifics, increasing exposure) or to abort (which would compromise the operation's purpose). The operation in question is the cleansing of a senior Élavendren judicial figure.
- **The Refused Selection.** A senior Ashen Vow brother, approached privately for Cinder selection, has refused. The refusal is rare and is, by Cinder protocol, denied to have occurred. The brother has, however, since the refusal, become increasingly visible in his orthodox order work—a pattern that the Cinder is finding it hard to interpret. Either he is, by his refusal, demonstrating his orthodox commitment, or he is preparing to expose the approach.
- **The Operation That Was Not.** A high-profile Sacred Forge death has been attributed by orthodox investigators to the Cinder; the First Cinder, however, ordered no such operation. Either a Cinder Hand has acted without orders (a discipline violation of the highest order), or a third party has executed an operation in such a way as to implicate the Cinder. Either possibility is dangerous; both require investigation; the investigation itself increases the order's exposure.
