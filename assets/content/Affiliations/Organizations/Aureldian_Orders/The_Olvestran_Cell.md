## Overview {#overview}

The Ólvestran Cell is the largest single cell of [[affiliation-ordershnvw|the Order of the Ashen Vow]], the sworn purifier-knights of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]]. It works from the hill-baronies around [[place-olvestria|Ólvestra]] in [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]], under the protection of the barons there, who for political reasons have declined to suppress the Black Flame. Every Aureldian crown keeps standing orders for the Ashen Vow's suppression, and the hill-baronies of Tarvénia are the qualified exception.

The order's largest cell thus works in the hills around the city where both of its sworn enemies keep their mother-houses: [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|the Order of the Sealed Word]] and [[affiliation-twinbldrdr|the Twinblade Order]], at [[place-twingate|the Twin Gate]].

## Membership {#membership}

A cell of the Ashen Vow is four to eight Sworn Brothers under an Elder, and the Ólvestran is the largest of them. A Sworn Brother sponsors a candidate, commonly one of the angry or the disillusioned, or a man whose own history includes a corruption the orthodox institutions failed to answer. The candidate spends a year at one of the order's remote chapter sites, in the Tarvénian highlands or the [[place-drenavrspn|Drenavar Spine]], learning the Black Flame doctrine and the use of arms, and is then branded at the inside of the right forearm with the order's small open flame and sworn for life before sitting brothers.

The vow permits no retirement. Only the Voice of the Ash releases a brother, and only for incapacity. An apostate is pursued with more fervor than any outside target, and a brother taken by the orthodox authorities is bound by the [Sanctity of the Secret]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} to silence himself before he can be made to speak.

Outside the chapter sites a brother wears nothing that marks him. The brand is the one sign.

## Leaders {#leaders}

An Elder of the Ash commands the cell. He answers to the [Council of the Ashen Vow]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} and to the Voice of the Ash, whom the Council chooses for life and whose identity is known only to the Elders and a few trusted sponsors.

## What It Wants Now {#present}

The order hunts, in this order, corruption inside [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]], the foreign heresies (the other Aureldian faiths' orders among them), and whoever of any faith the senior brothers judge beyond redemption. At Ólvestra the cell sits beside the Árdavon orders, which keep standing arrangements with the Sacred Forge to suppress the Ashen Vow in their territories. The Twinblades refuse Black Flame operatives at every threshold they guard, and the refusal is sometimes contested by combat. The Sealed Word treats Black Flame heresy as a matter for the Sacred Forge's religious courts and moves against a Black Flame member only when a specific oath-breach is at issue.

The Ólvestran Marendos of the Sealed Word died seven years ago, three days before he was to issue a major writ against a baron sympathetic to the Black Flame. The death was ruled natural, and suspicion of it has never gone away. It is among the operations attributed to [[affiliation-ironcinder|the Iron Cinder]], the Black Flame's covert strike order, which by its own discipline neither confirms nor denies any of them. The cell's protection is the barons' protection, and a writ that reaches one of those barons reaches the cell.

## How a Party Meets It {#how-a-party-meets-it}

A party meets the cell at second hand: in a refusal at the Twin Gate that is contested by combat, or in a hearing the Sealed Word convenes against a baron of the hills. A party knows a brother of the cell only by finding the brand.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The hearing.** A brother of the Sealed Word needs riders to bring an oath-breaker to a hearing, and the oath-breaker is a baron with friends in the hills around Ólvestra.
- **The captured brother.** The Tarvénian crown's investigators have taken a Sworn Brother of the order alive. By his vow he must silence himself before he talks, and his cell is racing to help him do it ([[affiliation-ordershnvw|the Order of the Ashen Vow]]).

:::secret {title="The commander"}
The cell's commander is Elder [Drogo of the Sealed Forge]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a name taken at his initiation; the brother's birth name is no longer used.
:::
