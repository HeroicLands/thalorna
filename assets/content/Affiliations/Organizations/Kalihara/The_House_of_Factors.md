## Overview

The House of Factors is the port council of [[place-ilovanu|Ilovanu]]. It stands above the quay, and the merchant councils that run the port's affairs meet in it, under the eyes of the harbor-wardens who answer to the island's councils. Three families hold it: the [Kubasari]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, the [Zazurari]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} and the [Yakodari]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}. Each carries the Valtari name of its trade on the quay and keeps a Kalihari name for its own people. Between them the three hold the warehouses, the lighters and the letters of credit through which the island's cardamom, cloves and colored stones reach the [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara Region]], the [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]] and the western kingdoms.

The House is a hall of the port and not of a free city. It sets the dues, settles disputes between traders and writes the letters that let a foreign hull go on to [[place-nalurithan|Nalurithan]], and it does all of it by the leave of [[affiliation-councilisland|the Council of the Island]]. A decision of the House that touches the foreign quarter's boundary, the pilots or the interior is not the House's to make, and the House knows the limit as well as any factor does.

## The Three Houses

The **Kubasari** are the reckoners (_kubasu_, to count), a counting-house family. They are the eldest of the three, and their head presides at the House. They hold the credit and keep the ledgers the other two houses settle against.

The **Zazurari** live by carrying other people's goods (_zazuru_, cargo). They hold the warehouses along the quay, and a cargo that lands at Ilovanu sleeps under their roofs until a buyer is found.

The **Yakodari** are the ship-people (_yakodu_, a boat). They keep the lighters and the small vessels that work the harbor and the coast.

## Character

A foreign master meets the House at the quay, in the form of a factor's seal on a bill of lading. The seal is a family's, the bill is entered by the Scribe of the House, and the cargo moves when the harbor-warden is satisfied and not before. A cargo held for inquiry stays held whatever the factor paid, and the House cannot hasten the wardens.

The House wants what a port wants: more hulls, lower pilot fees and the approaches open. The pilots answer to the Warden of the Approaches and not to any merchant, and the House pays the fees all the same. The approaches have begun to open of themselves, with foreign captains landing without a pilot, and the House profits from the trade whichever way the approaches go.

## Relations

The Council allows the House its trade and sends the harbor-wardens to watch it; the House sends its views to the Council through the Councilor of the ports. [[affiliation-wardensapproaches|The Wardens of the Approaches]] are its constant antagonist, since the pilots' fees and the harbor-wardens' inquiries fall on the House's cargoes. Nalurithan's merchant houses are Ilovanu's rivals for the gem trade, and the quarrel is kept to prices and letters. The language of the House's business is [[skill-valtarlng|Valtari]].
