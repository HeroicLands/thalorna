- **Type:** Chartered professional guild
- **Region:** [[place-byzariargn|Byzaría]], with chapterhouses in every League city
- **Charter:** Originally granted by the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Byzarian League]] Council; rolled up into the [[affiliation-magnumclgm|The Magnum Collegium]] when Byzaría was incorporated into Vylarian territory two centuries ago; both charters remain operative—the League charter as the guild's municipal foundation, the Collegium charter as its supra-realm standing
- **Magnum Collegium status:** Full member; the Grand Cartographer holds the Cartographers' seat on the Curia Collegiorum at Magnápolis

## Overview

The Cartographers' Guild holds the League's chartered monopoly on the production of maps for any purpose for which the state, the military, or a merchant house will pay coin: city plans, sea charts, trade-route surveys, fortification plans, frontier reconnaissance, mine plats, harbor diagrams, and the great commissioned wall-maps that hang behind every consul's desk in Byzaría. To practice cartography in the League for a paying client without guild license is a fineable offense; to produce a map of any League fortification or military disposition without guild oversight is treason.

This is, of course, an aspiration rather than a fact. The guild is the most prestigious mapmaking body in western Ankaris, but it cannot prevent its members from selling the same map twice—once to the patron who commissioned it and once to whoever else will pay—and it cannot prevent its rivals from copying its work. The guild's central tension, in this generation, is the discovery that the rot is internal: at least one master of standing has been selling League military maps to foreign powers and to the [[affiliation-crmsyndctsthlrn|Crime Syndicates of Thalorna]] for fifteen years, and the leadership does not yet know how deep the compromise runs.

## Membership and Structure

The guild is structured in the classical Byzarian three-tier mold: **apprentice** (admitted by examination, typically between twelve and sixteen years of age, trained in geometry, drafting, surveying, and the foundational pigments and inks); **journeyman** (qualified by an examined survey of an assigned territory, granted the right to take paid commissions under a master's seal); and **master** (qualified by a vote of sitting masters after a presented original work, granted the right to seal commissions, take apprentices, and sit on guild councils). The progression takes a minimum of fifteen years.

At the apex sits the **Council of Masters**, presently nine seats, governing examination standards, commission disputes, and the guild's relations with the League's civilian and military authorities. The Council's chair, the **Grand Cartographer**, is presently Olára Mareniês, an elderly woman whose late husband was a consul and whose own work is mostly administrative now. She is widely respected, modestly capable, and politically isolated—which is why the rot has run as deep as it has.

The guild maintains the **Cartographic Archive** in Byzaría, the central repository of every map sealed by a guild master since the charter was granted. The Archive is the guild's true treasure and its greatest vulnerability—anyone with the means to copy from it controls the League's geographic intelligence.

## Notable Members

- **Master [[being-damklsmpsn|Damáklios Sampsiôn]]**—among the finest cartographers in the known world, suspected by the League's city guard of espionage. The investigation is ongoing.
- **Master Vésalon** (deceased)—legendary mentor; trained Damáklios. Famous for the dictum, _the map is not the territory but the territory is judged against the map._
- **Master Méchon**—a younger, jealous talent, cultivating relationships with the city guard and quietly spreading insinuations against Damáklios. Méchon's motives are not entirely pure; he hopes to inherit Damáklios's commissions and is not above hastening the outcome.

## Doctrine and Practice

The guild teaches a particular discipline: that a map's authority derives from method, not from result. A guild-sealed map carries an implicit warranty—the surveying was done by a credentialed hand, the projections were checked, the symbology is consistent with guild standard, and the seal can be traced to a specific master who answers for any error. This is the source of the guild's premium pricing and of its political power: the League's military will not trust a chart that does not carry a guild seal.

In practice, the guild's standards have eroded under commercial pressure. Maps are commissioned faster than they can be properly surveyed; field corrections are skipped; older surveys are reused for new commissions and re-sealed without verification. Younger masters complain that the Council does not enforce; the Council complains that the younger masters do not respect the standards they did not earn.

## Relations

- **The League Council and Military**—primary client and overseer. The League's investigators currently include the guild in the same breath as the spy networks they are trying to dismantle.
- **The [[affiliation-magnumclgm|The Magnum Collegium]]**—supra-realm authority. The guild's standing throughout the western Vylarian world depends on its Collegium charter; the Censores Collegiorum have, in correspondence the guild's leadership has not made public, expressed pointed interest in the espionage investigation currently underway against Master Damáklios. The matter is being held at chapter level for now; if it reaches Curia attention the consequences for the guild's charter could be substantial.
- **The [[affiliation-crmsyndctsthlrn|Crime Syndicates of Thalorna]]**, particularly the **Theft Syndicate of the Eastern Docks**—quietly major purchasers of guild work for purposes the guild's charter forbids.
- **The [[affiliation-blackledgr|Black Ledger]]**—the guild's shadow. Most Ledger members are guild-trained; many hold guild credentials and use them as cover. The Council pretends the Ledger does not exist; the city guard pretends the Council does not know.
- **Foreign powers**—Tarvénia, an unidentified northern confederation, and at least one Vylarian province pay handsomely for League maps procured through irregular channels.

## Identifying Marks

A guild master's seal is a brass die carrying the master's personal mark and the year of issue; impressions in wax (red for civil commissions, black for military) attest the map's provenance. Masters wear a silver pin in the shape of a stylized dividers-and-compass, worn at the throat or pinned to the cap. Apprentices wear linen sleeves marked with the chevron of their master.

## Plot Hooks

- **The Archive Theft.** Several classical maps have disappeared from the Cartographic Archive—and others have been _copied_. The leadership must investigate without alerting the spy network that is almost certainly inside the building.
- **The Master's Trial.** Master Damáklios Sampsiôn is brought before the Council of Masters on charges of selling guild work to foreign agents. The Council is divided: some want to make an example of him, some want a quiet expulsion to avoid scandal, some are themselves compromised and want the trial to disappear.
- **The Apprentice's Discovery.** A first-year apprentice, tasked with cataloguing old surveys, notices that three "independent" maps of the same coast are in fact tracings of one common source. The original is signed by a master long dead. The discovery threatens to unravel three generations of falsified work.
- **The Reform Vote.** The Council debates new protocols requiring all sealed maps to be reviewed by a second master before delivery. The reform would cripple the espionage trade—and several Council seats would lose substantial private income. The vote will be very close.
