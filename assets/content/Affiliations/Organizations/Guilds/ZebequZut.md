## Overview

**Zebequ'Zut** (the Merchant Alliance) has no charter, no roll and no premises, and it is influential all the same. It is an informal network of the empire's major trading houses, bound by pacts of mutual protection, shared intelligence on trade routes and market conditions, and joint bargaining with foreign commercial powers. Its members control a large share of the empire's wealth.

## Character

A treasurer of the Gár-Hálzi at Amqel-Ger, asked how a newcomer finds the alliance, answers with a shrug: "You do not find it. You find a house, and the house has dined with other houses."

The alliance does its business where nobody keeps minutes: at banquets, on private estates and along the docks of the delta ports. What binds a house to it is practical. The houses protect one another by pact, pass along what they learn about routes and market conditions, and bargain together when a foreign commercial power has to be dealt with. A Halzi'a who crosses them can be made or broken by their combined weight, and the Halzi'a of Gar-Anlaghet rules a harbor shared with the Haradian factors and the Zebequ'Zut houses.
