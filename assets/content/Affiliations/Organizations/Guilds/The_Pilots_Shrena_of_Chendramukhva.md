_Vedyaran: Nau Shrena—"the boat guild"_

The **Pilots' Shrena of Chendramukhva** takes the deep-water hulls across the bar at [[place-chandrmukha|the Moon-Mouth]]. The channel moves with every flood season, and after every monsoon the shrena sounds it again and redraws its charts. It does not sell them.

## Overview

A hull that cannot cross the bar unaided waits in the roadstead for a pilot, and pilots are taken in the order of the shrena's queue. A master who comes in without one pays the same fee and takes the same risk. The fee, the queue and the season's chart are fixed by the council of master pilots, who sit in the pilots' quarter behind the yards.

The shrena stands under [[affiliation-chendrapur|Chendrapur]], whose galleys and merchant hulls all pass the bar, and it answers to the Maharava's harbor officers for the fee and for wrecks. Within the port it keeps its own rules.

## Membership and Structure

An apprentice rides the pilot boats for years before a master puts a name forward. A licensed pilot takes a place in the queue, and the season's fees are shared among the pilots in proportion to the crossings they make. A pilot who sells a chart, or lets a hull jump the queue for a private price, is struck off. A struck-off pilot may still work the delta channels, but no master of the shrena will take him aboard and no chart is shown to him.

## Relations

- The [[affiliation-mrchntclctvvdyr|Merchant Collective]] has the cargo and not the bar. Its factors pay the pilots' fee and ask for the chart at the start of each season, and the council refuses.
- The galley crews of Chendramukhva sail from the same beach and know the pilots by sight. The shrena does not train their captains, and the captains keep their own soundings.
- Foreign junks that lie outside the bar rather than take a pilot are the shrena's standing insult. The council has never found a way to charge them for it.

## Plot Hook

- **The Sold Chart.** This season's chart of the bar is being offered in a sail-loft, and the council has learned that someone is selling. It needs hands that do not wear the shrena's colors to find the seller first.
