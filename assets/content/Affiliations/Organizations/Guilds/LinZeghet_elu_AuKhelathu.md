## Overview

Lin'Zeghet elu Aû'Khelâthu is the chartered guild of hunters across [[place-aukhelathrgq|Aû'Khelâthu]], licensing both the ordinary taking of game and the handling of beasts dangerous enough to need a specialist. The guild issues those licenses and sets the terms under which a hunter may work a given territory, so two hunters are rarely chasing the same ground under conflicting claims. When the quarry is a dangerous beast, the license is the first thing a hirer asks to see, since an unlicensed hunter has answered to no one for the methods used.
