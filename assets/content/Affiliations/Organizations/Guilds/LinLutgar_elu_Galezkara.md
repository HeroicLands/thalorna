## Overview

Lin'Lutgar elu Galezkara is the association of innkeepers of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]], drawing its membership from every house that takes in travelers for pay. Members share what they learn of travelers passing through, an informal intelligence network that serves every house better than any one of them could alone. The association also stands together against the merchant houses that would rather own the capital's inns outright, and an independent keeper's best defense against a buyout offer is simply this shared refusal to sell.
