## Overview

Lin'Lagaru elu Aû'Khelâthu, known as the Quantity Merchants, is a guild of dealers across [[place-aukhelathrgq|Aû'Khelâthu]] who buy and sell on volume alone rather than on the quality of any single lot. Its members move goods in bulk, trading margin for turnover, and prize a supplier who ships quickly over one who ships well. A supplier who refuses to send inferior stock to fill an order is, in this guild's own reckoning, committing an affront to profit rather than keeping faith with quality.

## Character

The dealers are driven by turnover. They prize a supplier who ships quickly over one who ships well, and they read any refusal to send inferior stock as an affront to profit. The coalition is loose, and its members are held together by a shared reckoning of what a good deal is.

## Relations

- Suppliers—Valued for speed of shipment over quality, and resented when they hold stock back.

## Commerce and Currency

Members buy and sell in bulk and trade margin for turnover, so the volume moved matters to them more than the worth of any single lot.
