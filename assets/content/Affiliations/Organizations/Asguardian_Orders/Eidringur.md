An oath is only as good as the person who remembers it, and in [[affiliation-eidgar|Eidgar's faith]] the person who remembers it is an Oathsworn.

Eidringur, the Oathsworn, are priests who oversee the swearing of oaths and the keeping of honor, and who mediate wherever a dispute turns on a man's sworn word. A member witnesses the oath and keeps its memory. Either side of a quarrel can call on him, because he answers to neither. A Drottmadur witnesses and remembers but does not yet mediate a quarrel alone; the mediating belongs to the Dróttstjori.
