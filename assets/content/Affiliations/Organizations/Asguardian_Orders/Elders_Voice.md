Elder's Voice holds the rare standing of speaking between the elder races and mortals in [[affiliation-bjartr|Bjartr]]'s name, preserving ancient knowledge and keeping harmony between the two. No more than one or two members hold it at once, and the faith is often served by none, since the standing is claimed as it arises rather than filled to a schedule.
