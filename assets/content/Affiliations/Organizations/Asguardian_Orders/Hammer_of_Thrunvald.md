Hammer of Thrúnvald is the fighting order of [[affiliation-thrunvald|Thrúnvald]]'s faith, elite warriors who wield mighty war-hammers and carry the thunder-god's strength into battle. Its members defend his coastal hofs and the ships that sail under his blessing, standing apart from the ordinary clergy as the faith's answer to raiders and worse.
