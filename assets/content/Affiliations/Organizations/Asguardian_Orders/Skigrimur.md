If the Order of the Shifting Veil, does its work well, you never learn that you met it.

Skigrimur trains priests of [[affiliation-velgrimur|Velgrímur]] in illusion and disguise, and serves his hidden hovs in [[place-malagna|Malagna]]'s ports as spies and infiltrators. A member moves through a port city under a face not his own and reports to a hov whose location he alone may be trusted to know. Spies and infiltrators are led by a Dróttstjori; a Drottmadur still trains in illusion and disguise and is not yet trusted as an infiltrator.
