## Overview

A boy who comes home from his first voyage out of [[place-aelissium|Elissara]] is taken by the hand who sailed with him to the [Roll House]{.name data-type="place/structure"} on the [Long Quay]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, where the Marker sits at a table by the door with a needle and a pot of soot ink. The hand gives him the lesson while the needle works. "From today you are on the roll, and the roll is the guild. A captain who wants a crew comes here, and the Korvelos gives him the next names in turn, and you go when your name comes up. If a captain wants you and nobody else, he pays the guild for the asking, and that is what you work twenty years for. You sign for the voyage and you stay for the voyage. A man who walks off a ship in a foreign port gets a bar through that anchor, and after that he can sail out of Taruzel if they will have him, because nobody here will."

The Seamen's Guild of Elissara, called by its members the Korvela (the Roll), is the guild of the merchant seamen of the city: about three thousand hands, every one of them marked with a blue anchor inside a ring of rope on the right arm. It holds the city's one roll of seamen. Every ship that hires in Elissara hires from it, by the voyage, so no owner keeps a standing crew and no hand serves an owner rather than a ship. The guild is one of the trade guilds of the [[affiliation-tevranaelissium|Tevran of Elissara]], which governs the free city, and like every chartered guild of the empire it holds its standing under [[affiliation-tevran|the Tevran Maldor]].

## The Roll and the Turn

The roll is a book kept in the Roll House, one line for each hand, with the voyages he has made written after his name. When a captain asks for a crew, the _korvelos_ (keeper of the roll) gives him the next names that are ashore and unhired, in the order the roll stands, and the hands named sign for that ship's voyage and are paid off when it comes home. The turn spreads the work: a young hand with three voyages behind him is hired as readily as an old one, and no captain can fill his ship with his cousins.

A captain who wants a particular hand calls him by name and pays the guild a fee for it, a quarter of the hand's wages for the voyage. The called hands are the guild's pride. [[being-arklrdsklmkns|Arkalordas Kalmakanes]], who went to sea at seventeen and has been the senior hand on every deck he has stood on for ten years, is called on nearly every voyage he makes, and the fees for him have paid for a good part of the Roll House's roof.

## What the Guild Gives and Asks

The guild buys every hand's burial. A man who dies ashore is buried from the Roll House, and a man lost at sea is named on the wall of the house's inner court, where his widow draws a share of the called-hand fees for a year. The guild keeps a hall where a hand between voyages can sleep and eat on credit against his next wages, and it stands behind a hand in any quarrel with an owner over pay, food or a ship it judges unfit to sail. When the guild finds a ship unfit, it strikes the ship from the roll, and no hand in Elissara will sign for her until the owner has had her mended to the guild's satisfaction.

It asks three things. A hand sails when his name comes up, unless he is sick or the guild excuses him. He stays with his ship until she is home. And he signs nowhere but on the roll: a hand who takes a berth on a ship that hires outside it, from the beach or from a tavern, has signed off the roll. A hand who breaks the second or third is struck from the roll, and the Marker pricks a bar through his anchor so that every port on the coast can read it.

## Who Runs It

The _korvelos_ is chosen every year on the first day of the sailing year, when the northerlies set in, by a show of hands in the inner court of the Roll House among every hand who is ashore. The present Keeper of the Roll is [Volkema Satheris]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a captain's daughter who sailed eleven years as a hand and has kept the roll for six. She is the first woman to hold it, and she holds it because the old hands trust her arithmetic. The guild's _kelros_ (guild's council member) sits on the Tevran of Elissara for five years, and the Marker serves for as long as his eyes are good.

## Relations

- **The owners and captains** of Elissara are the guild's employers and its standing quarrel. The owners want crews of their own choosing and wages they set themselves, and the roll gives them neither. They outvote the seamen on the Tevran of Elissara, where every guild has one seat and the small craft guilds side with whoever pays them.
- **The [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]'s fleet** is short of seamen and is paying the guild for copies of its roll.
- **[[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Taruzel]]**'s privateers take Vylarian ships on the eastern runs, and a hand taken by them comes home, if he comes home, to a guild that pays nothing for ransoms.
- **The bargemen** of the river take the cargoes the seamen bring in up to the capital, and keep their own roll on the other side of the basin.

## The Present Quarrel {#present}

The roll is the quarrel, and two buyers want it. The fleet is short of seamen and is paying the guild for copies of the roll, and an officer of the fleet is in the city to collect one, which [Volkema Satheris]{.name data-type="being/npc"} wants checked name by name before it leaves the Roll House. The owners and captains want crews of their own choosing at wages they set themselves, and they outvote the seamen on the [[affiliation-tevranaelissium|Tevran of Elissara]], where the shipowners, merchants and small crafts hold most of the seats and [Zenothen Linthimis]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the shipwrights presides in his third year, two more than custom expects.

The city's charter is sealed anew by every Orzas, and the reigning one is dying. An advocate from [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] has asked every guild for its copy, the Roll House among them. The [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]]'s clerks collect the customs at the river mouth outside the walls, and on the eastern runs Taruzel's privateers take Vylarian ships whose hands come home, if they come home, to a guild that pays no ransoms.

## Hooks

- **The struck ship.** The guild has struck an owner's ship as unfit, and the owner says the guild did it because his captain refused to call the guild's men by name. He hires a party to prove the ship sound before the season turns.
- **The barred anchor.** A man with a barred anchor on his arm has signed onto a ship in the basin under another name, with the bar burned out of his skin. The Marker knows the scar, and the captain who took the man wants nobody told.
- **The navy's copies.** An officer of the fleet is in the city to collect a copy of the roll the guild has sold him, and Volkema Satheris wants the copy checked, name by name, before it leaves the Roll House.

:::secret
**For the GM:** The copies of the roll the guild is selling to the fleet carry every hand's name, his voyages and the street he lodges in. The fleet's officers do not want them to hire men. They want them for the press: the first day the fleet is ordered to sea against Taruzel, its officers mean to take every marked man in Elissara from his lodging by name, and the anchor on his arm will tell them they have the right man.

Volkema Satheris has guessed it. She has sold the copies anyway, because the guild needs the money and because she believes the fleet would take the men without them, and the copy she is checking for the officer has every called hand's lodging written wrong. The Marker knows. The old hands do not, and the _kelros_ would carry it to the Tevran of Elissara if he did.
:::
