A swordsman who wants the dragonfly does not apply for it. He fights. When a master of the Path of Breath has crossed blades with him often enough to be sure, she writes to the other living masters, and each of them comes to fight him in turn. If all of them agree, the last to fight him sets the tattoo on his neck herself, and from that day he is counted among them.

## Overview

**The Path of Breath** is a sacred martial order of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]. It admits only the most accomplished swordsmen, and its members are the masters of the sword discipline of the same name, the [[skill-pthbrth|Path of Breath]]. There are three living masters, among them [[being-krmdnsbrkrtys|Koremdanis Barkratis]] of [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]], the foremost swordmaster in the Empire.

## What It Asks

The order has no house, no head and no treasury. It is the living masters and nothing else, and they decide everything together: who is admitted, who is cast out, and who may be taught the advanced forms. A master teaches whom she chooses, but she teaches the Path only to a swordsman who already has the fundamental forms of the blade, and she answers to the others for every student she takes.

What a master is taught to carry, besides the sword, is the discipline's spiritual half: that a duel is a conversation between two skilled practitioners, and that the ability to kill is a responsibility. A master who kills in a quarrel that a duel could have settled, or who sells the forms, is cast out by the others. The tattoo cannot be taken back, and the masters stop answering to it.

The Path's five maneuvers, the Heron Counterstrike, the Flowing Guard, the Precision Strike, the Bind and Disarm, and the Tempo Form, are what a master teaches, and the [[skill-pthbrth|skill]] gives each one.

## The Order Before This One

The masters do not claim to have founded the Path. The oldest manuals they study are older than any master the order remembers, and they speak of the forms as having been taught first by another order, which the manuals do not name and nobody alive can place. The present order keeps the forms and the dragonfly, and claims nothing more.

## The Path and the Arcane Order

[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|The Order]] (the Thordimalon, the Arcane Order) classes the Path as tolerated: a discipline of the sword and of the mind, with no arcane working in it, and so outside its monopoly. A master may be asked once in her life to show the forms to an examiner of the Order, and the masters count the request a courtesy.

## What It Wants Now {#present}

The order wants the forms kept whole and taught only to swordsmen fit for them, and it is at peace with itself and with the Thordimalon. The three living masters weigh every candidate together, and many of the swordsmen they weigh come from the fencing academy of [[being-krmdnsbrkrtys|Koremdanis Barkratis]] in [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]], which the children of the Empire's most powerful families attend and whose students often become bodyguards and protectors. In a year when every great house counts the cost of a succession, a swordsman with the forms is worth a great deal to a household, and a master who sells the forms is cast out by the others.
