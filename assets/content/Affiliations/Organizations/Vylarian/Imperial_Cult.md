Pray to whichever god you like afterward; the [[place-pnthnrldnm|Temple of the Twelve]] is across the square. The coal is for heaven. The roll is for the city, and the roll is what a magistrate asks to see."

## Overview

The Imperial Cult, _zalvon_ in [[skill-vylarilng|Vylari]], is the state cult of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]: the veneration of dead Orzoi as divine or half-divine. Its temples stand in every city of the Empire, and taking part in it is a religious act and a declaration of political loyalty at once. It sits above the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]], whose gods rule the city's year, and above every other faith in the Empire, and it binds the subjects of all of them to one ritual of allegiance.

It is also a department of state, with its own revenue, its own appointments and seats at every public ceremony. The reigning Orzas holds the office of **Sarnaks Maldor**, its chief priest, so the cult's head and the state's head are one man. Below him a _holzathos_ named by the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] administers the cult as his officer and hears the oaths (_zaron_) sworn in court. An oath sworn before the _holzathos_ binds in the courts as well as before heaven, and breaking one is among the offenses Vylarians find hardest to forgive.

A Vylarian feels no conflict between the cult and his own gods. The Twelve rule the year, the ancestors rule the house, and the Imperial Cult is a courtesy owed to the throne.

## The Divine and the Half-Divine

A dead Orzas becomes a god only by the Seldan's vote. When he dies, the Senate votes on his consecration (_ulthandon_), and the vote has three answers.

Divine
: The dead Orzas receives a temple of his own, a feast day, a _zalvos_ to keep his rites and an endowment to pay for them.

Half-divine
: He receives an altar and a place among the masks in the cult's hall, and his name is spoken at the rites of the divine.

Neither
: His mask goes home to his house's cupboard, and the cult does not speak his name.

The Senate can also strike a consecration back out (_anulthathen_), and has done so. The vote is therefore one of the Senate's real powers over the throne. It has always been politics, and it is politics now: the reigning Orzas is old, the succession is in doubt, and every great house is counting votes on how he will be entered before he is dead. [[affiliation-mardovanes|House Mardovanes]] has asked in every generation for its Orzas, [Azénales]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, to be raised from half-divine to divine, and [[affiliation-varkalenis|House Varkalenis]], three of whose Orzoi are divine, has voted against it every time.

## The Offering

The state's days are the accession day of the reigning Orzas, the feast day of each divine Orzas, and the day of a triumph. On each of them the magistrates, the guilds, the town councils and the citizens make the offering before the Orzas's image: a pinch of incense on the coal and the name spoken aloud. A legion makes it before the portrait-standard of the reigning Orzas that it carries beside its eagle. A clerk enters every offerer on a roll, and the roll is kept. A magistrate taking office, an officer taking a commission and a witness about to swear an oath are each asked for their entry.

A subject who refuses the offering is entered too, as a refuser, and the entry follows him. No court accepts his oath, no magistracy takes him, and no patron of standing keeps him at the morning call. Most refusers belong to a faith that forbids the act. The cult leaves the faith alone and punishes the man, by the record and nothing else.

## The Hall of the Masks

The cult keeps the wax masks (_lavura_) of the divine and half-divine Orzoi in a hall of its chief temple in the [Vanor]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the Temple District of [[place-arvon|Arvon]], in the order of their reigns. The cult's own word for the hall is the Empire's cupboard by the door. When an Orzas dies, men of the right height and build put on the masks and walk behind his bier from the palace to the [[place-necropolisorzara|Necropolis of Orzara]], so that the whole line of the Empire's divine dead goes before the city in one procession.

The masks are the cult's splendor, and its temples are its decay. Each divine Orzas's temple was endowed when he was consecrated, and many endowments were land, rents or tolls that are now worth a fraction of what they were. The great temples on the processional routes are kept bright. Off them, a temple of an Orzas few citizens can name has a dry fountain, a porch let to a stallholder and a _zalvos_ who comes on the feast day and no other. The cult's treasurer lets what the endowments cannot keep, and the noble houses whose ancestors stand in those temples pay to stop him or watch it happen.

## The Provinces

Each province has a _zalvos_ of the province at its seat, who presides over the rites that bind it to the capital, and each city has its temples of the divine Orzoi. [[affiliation-provnchyln|Hilen]] is the cult's bastion: its old landed houses hold to the cult and the traditional rites of the Twelve against the merchants and the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] alike. At the edge of [[place-olcassafld|the Blood-field of Olkassa]], on the estate of [[affiliation-ganothandis|House Ganothandis]], the cult keeps a small shrine to both armies' Orzoi, the only shrine in the Empire to a defeated claimant.

## Politics {#present}

The cult's politics this year are the old Orzas's death. When he dies, the Seldan votes his consecration, and the cult's own Keeper of the Masks, [[being-dlmrsvrklns|Dalmeras Varkalenis]], chooses the men who walk in the masks behind his bier. Dalmeras is also one of the three men the court names for the adoption: poor, devout, descended from five Orzoi and the old houses' choice. Every house that hopes to matter after the funeral has called on him this year.

The death will open the post of _holzathos_, the most contested appointment in a generation, and [[affiliation-varkalenis|House Varkalenis]] wants it. [[affiliation-mardovanes|House Mardovanes]] wants its Orzas Azénales raised to divine at last. The next accession also brings [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]]'s petition to the new Orzas, as chief priest of the cult in his own person, for a place in the Forge-Lord's niche in the [[place-pnthnrldnm|Temple of the Twelve]], and the High Flame-Warden, [[being-trzmsbrkrts|Torzemos Brakarates]], has it drafted. On the Olkassa estate in Hilen the shrine to both armies' Orzoi keeps its lamp, paid for by [[affiliation-ganothandis|House Ganothandis]], whose heir marries the Grain House's daughter this season.

:::secret {title="The Keeper's offer"}
Dalmeras has sent word to Nartun, by a priest of the Sacred Forge, that he will proscribe the Black Flame when he reigns if Nartun's knights and temple coin stand behind his adoption. The Holy Mother has agreed to nothing. Torzemos has learned that a claimant's house sent a priest up the holy road, and has warned every household what such a promise would cost.
:::

## Hooks

- **The count.** A _kurthos_ (senator) who owes favors in three directions hires a party to learn, before the old Orzas dies, how the Seldan's votes on his consecration will fall.
- **The let porch.** A stallholder who rents the porch of a divine Orzas's temple finds a strongbox of old gold under the paving, and the temple's _zalvos_, the cult's treasurer and the stallholder each claim it.
- **The refuser.** A witness in a case before the imperial tribunals turns out to be entered as a refuser in a provincial roll he swears he never saw. Someone has added him, and his testimony is worthless until a party finds the clerk who did.
