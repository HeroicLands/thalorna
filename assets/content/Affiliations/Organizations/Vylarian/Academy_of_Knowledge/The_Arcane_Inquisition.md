A _sekeros_ (inquirer) opening a hearing reads the first clause of his commission aloud before he asks a single question. He reads it to a village wise-woman with a basket of charms as he would read it to a renegade master: "By the Kandrol, to establish what happened at Alessa, and who did it." The wise-woman has never heard of Alessa. The clause is read to her because, in the doctrine of the Arcane Inquisition (the Sekran), her hearing is part of that inquiry, and every hearing since 73 BF has been.

The Sekran is the enforcement arm of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|The Order]] (the Thordimalon). Its members, the **sekeroi**, find practitioners who work outside the Order's register and bring them under it, by enrollment if possible and by force if not. Its writ runs under imperial law throughout the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]], and on the _sekeros_'s own standing, his reputation and his hired help wherever the Order keeps a chapter beyond it. Its head sits in the Order's headquarters at [[place-orzara|Orzara City]], and its regional offices stand beside the great chapters, [[place-velysara|Velisara]]'s among them.

## The Commission

[[lore-kandrol|The Kandrol]], the charter of 73 BF, constitutes the Sekran a clause before it constitutes the Order, and gives it two clauses of commission. The second is the one most people know: to suppress the practice of arcane lore by anyone outside the Order. The first is to establish what happened at [[lore-recensio|The Therona of Alessa]] two years earlier, when an unchanneled release killed most of the Republic's senior arcanists and burned their books, and to find out who set it off. That commission has never been closed.

The second clause follows from the first. The Sekran's founding working theory is that the release at Alessa was set by a hand not on the register, so every hand not on the register is, in doctrine, a party to the crime. That doctrine shapes how the Sekran works:

- **Recruitment comes before killing.** A suspect enrolled and talking is worth more to an inquiry than a suspect dead, and enrollment is the outcome a _sekeros_ is praised for.
- **Files never close.** A hunt that ends with nobody found is set down, not finished. A _holsekeros_ (senior inquirer) may open a file again after twenty years because a name in it has turned up in another, and the Sekran's archives are full of files waiting for that.
- **Teaching an outsider is the worst offense a member can commit.** An outsider who has been taught is a new hand off the register, and the commission was written to find exactly that.

## A Hearing

Most people who meet the Sekran meet it at a hearing, in a chapter house or the back room of an inn, with a _sekeros_, a notary and two hired men at the door. The _sekeros_ reads the clause and then asks where the suspect learned what she does, from whom that person learned it, and who else she has shown. The notary writes down every name. The hearing ends in one of five outcomes, in the Sekran's order of preference: recruitment, confiscation, restriction, imprisonment or elimination. A hearing that ends with a name entered on the register is filed as the best outcome, and most hearings end that way.

The names matter more than the suspect. A wise-woman enrolled on restrictive terms goes home with a ward on her and a _therman_ (adept) who visits twice a year. The three names she gave go into three new files, and the Sekran goes looking for each.

## How It Is Built

The Sekran is an arm of the Order, not a separate order with a ladder of its own. Every _sekeros_ is a member of the Thordimalon first, usually a _therman_ or a _marendos_ (master), and keeps his rank in it while he serves. Nobody applies. A regional office asks a chapter for a mage it has had its eye on, the Verdan Maldor's head of the Sekran, [[being-krmskldrms|Kiramos Kaldremis]], approves, and the mage is told. The [[affiliation-pirethos|Pirethos]] fellowship sends more members than any other, and the [[affiliation-neumenos|Neumenos]] fellowship is the one the Sekran watches most closely.

A _sekeros_ who loses a file, or is found to have shown it to someone outside the Sekran, is dismissed and sent back to his chapter, and every file he kept is read again by a stranger.

The Sekran keeps holding places of its own across the western part of the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]] for those it judges too dangerous to enroll. The best known to the Empire's subjects is the inner keep of [Kezrora]{.name data-type="place/structure"} above [[place-sanctara|Ulthora]] in the mountains of [[affiliation-provncmktr|Province of Moktur]], inside the province's own prison and kept by the Sekran's warden.

## What It Hunts

The Sekran sorts what it finds by the Order's classification, from the sanctioned through the tolerated, the restricted and the rogue to the anathema (see [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]). Rogue practice is its daily work: hedge-mages, alchemists, cunning folk and fortune-tellers whose charms actually work. Tolerated practice is outside its writ, and a _sekeros_ who harasses a temple's star-reader is reprimanded. An anathema is the one finding that can end a hearing in a death without any other cause.

## Relations

The Sekran runs its own network of informants, which overlaps the Empire's intelligence apparatus without being part of it. Senior _sekeroi_ and imperial spymasters trade favors and watch one another. In [[place-helionis|Helionis]] the chapter's local traditions have negotiated terms with the Order, and the Sekran treads carefully there. In the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]] the merchant guilds restrict the Order's work, and a _sekeros_ works through imperial agents and paid informers rather than under any law.

At Velisara the Sekran's regional office stands across the city from the province's largest temple of [[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]]. The Order takes no side between the Forge-Lord's two faiths, and the Black Flame is a lawful faith. What brings the Sekran into the old quarter is the Flame's rite worked as arcane magic, which is anathema.

## What It Wants Now {#present}

The Sekran wants what it has wanted since 73 BF: the first clause answered and every hand on the register. Its file on the Therona stays open, and its daily work is the rogue practitioner. Its present hunts run into the Empire's quarrels. [[affiliation-thetamzir|The Tamzir]] carries [[being-cibelasrnt|Kivella Serenthis]], an arcane prodigy who escaped the Order, and the Sekran pursues her through imperial agents, guild contacts in the Taruzelian ports and hired bounty hunters. At [[place-velysara|Velisara]] its regional office watches the old quarter around the province's largest temple of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]], for the rite worked as arcane magic. Senior _sekeroi_ trade favors with the imperial spymasters, in a year when every house at court wants to know what the Order plans before the Order announces it.

# The First File {#truth .secret}

The Sekran's founding file is kept at Orzara in the head's own keeping. It holds the first clause, the roll of the dead of the Therona, the inventory taken after the fire, and nearly eight centuries of notes added by the heads who have kept it. It does not say what the inventory came up short of, and the first clause does not name what it is looking for. A _sekeros_ who is sent a copy of the file finds the second clause and everything after it struck through.
