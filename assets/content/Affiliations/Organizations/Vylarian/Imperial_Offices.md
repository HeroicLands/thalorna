The usher of petitions at the gate of the Offices gives the rule to every petitioner who has climbed to the Inner City on a feast day: "Bring it written, bring it sealed, and bring the fee in coin. I give you a number. You sit on the bench under that number until a clerk calls it, and if he does not call it today, you come back on the next feast day and sit under it again. A gift to the clerk does not buy you a better number. It buys you a clerk who remembers yours."

## Overview

The Imperial Offices are the bureaucracy of the Vylarian throne, gathered into a single walled compound of [[place-arvon|Arvon]], the Inner City of [[place-orzara|Orzara]]. Everyone calls them the **Offices**. In [[skill-vylarilng|Vylari]] the whole compound takes the name of its heart, _thizaralon_, the office of the seal, because everything the Offices do ends in a seal. Here thousands of clerks, scribes, tax-assessors, _theronoi_ (censors) and magistrates do the paper of governing an empire: census rolls, tax ledgers, military levies, the dole's records, court judgments and the throne's correspondence.

The compound holds more than the chancery. [[affiliation-tarmex|The Tarmeks Orzan]], the imperial treasury and central bank, keeps its strongroom and clearing house here. [[affiliation-tolvan|The Tolvan]], the Fiscal Court, sits in its Fiscal Hall. The throne's own imperial tribunals, which hear every cause touching the nobility, the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] or the crown, sit here and answer to the Orzas, never to the civic [[affiliation-verdanorzara|Orzara Courts]]. And [[affiliation-imprlscrblgld|the Imperial Scribal Guild]] keeps the imperial archive here, in the custody it took after the old archive burned ([[lore-archivefire|The Archive Fire]]). Each of these bodies has its own head and its own ladder. The Offices are the roof, the gate and the seal they share.

## The Seal

The **Keeper of the Seal** heads the chancery and, through it, the Offices. He is appointed by the Orzas and answers to him alone, and by custom he is a freedman of the imperial household: a man with no house of his own to serve before the throne's. An act of the throne is an act because the Keeper sealed it, and a document sealed in his absence is a forgery however genuine its words.

The present Keeper is [Holimar]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a freedman of sixty who has held the seal for nineteen years. He is soft-spoken, exact and very rich, and he is the most hated man in the Seldan. _Kurthoi_ (senators) of the founding houses wait on his bench like petitioners, because nothing they win in the chamber is law until he seals it. The post is a freedman's by custom, and the founder of [[affiliation-thalurates|House Thalurates]] was the son of a freedman who held it, and the Thalurates have served in the Offices since.

## The Desks

Beneath the Keeper the Offices work in desks, each under an _usos_ (prefect). The Census keeps the citizen roll for the Theronos, and the Dole keeps the roll of veterans owed a pension, each at the rate of the rank he left the legion with, and the books of its monthly payments. The Dole is the costliest desk in the compound: each pension on its roll is modest, the roll is very long, and much of what it pays out the Tarmeks pays for with borrowed money. The Levies keeps the rolls of the legions and enters their losses. The Provinces keeps one desk for every province on the chancery's roll, and the chancery's roll is longer than the Empire. The desk for [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Provenzia]] still keeps the forms of a province whose Charter the Empire recognized a century ago, and writes to its king in the chancery's own style. The desk for [[place-helionis|Helionis]] sends a province's letters to cities that govern themselves. The desk for [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Taruzel]], lost twelve years ago, still has its clerks, its ledgers and its usos, and it drafts each year the levy a province would owe.

The forms are the government, and the desks keep them. A clerk who misfiles a census return can unmake a citizen as surely as a court can, because the rule the law schools teach first is that what is not on the roll is not in the city ([[lore-firewords|The Words Kept from the Fire]]).

## The College of Heralds

The [[affiliation-imprlhrlds|Imperial College of Heralds]] is the desk of arms and precedence. Its heralds keep the roll of every house's arms, marks and liveries and the order in which the houses stand before the throne, and no act of the throne is proclaimed, no embassy received and no funeral of a founding house held until a herald has settled who stands where. Its Master sits among the _usoi_ of the Offices and answers to the Keeper of the Seal. The College keeps its own ladder of heralds and pursuivants, and trains them in the compound and in country schools outside the capital.

## What Goes Missing

The Offices' records are vast enough that documents are lost in them for generations, and a man with the right friends can make an inconvenient law, debt or birth-record disappear. A case can be buried in the Offices' archives as surely as in a grave, and the favor of a _veramus_ (curator of the city) is one of the ways to arrange it. The losses are older than the clerks. The fire took most of the first three centuries of edicts, and every original the Guild keeps here was written after it. An archivist asked for the muster rolls of the army sent across the sea in 377 brings the roll, open at the right place: it records the legions as they embark and does not record their return ([[lore-deltaexped|The Delta Expedition]]). The Senate's claimant in [[lore-brkensuccsn|the Broken Succession]], [Sylarion]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, died in a room of the Offices whose door was not opened for two days ([[lore-restorehouse|The Restoration]]), and the room is a ledger store now.

## What It Is Fighting About {#present}

The Offices are waiting for a new Keeper. A new Orzas appoints a new Keeper of the Seal, Holimar has held the seal for nineteen years under the present one, and the present one is seventy-eight and has adopted nobody. The houses that want the seal for one of their own freedmen court the claimants, and [[being-krznsthlrts|Korzenas Thalurates]], master of the house the Offices raised, means the post to stay in the Offices he knows.

The seal is busy meanwhile. The Orzas has no cash, and he sells offices, titles and Senate grants of citizenship quietly, for coin now. The Dole desk pays each month's pensions from silver [[affiliation-tarmex|the Tarmeks]] borrows. The desk for Taruzel has been asked for a copy of the levy it drafts each year for a province twelve years gone. The imperial tribunals in the compound lift cases out of the civic basilicas at the throne's word, and their clerks in gray stoles are watched in the Platan, because a proscription can be read from the platforms on any day the throne needs a house's property.

## Hooks

- **The number.** A party's patron needs a petition heard before the next feast day, and the usher's bench is full. The clerk who calls the numbers can be bought, and so can the clerk who wants his place.
- **The desk for Taruzel.** The desk still drafts Taruzel's levy every year, and this year a Taruzelian envoy has asked for a copy. Whoever gives it to him hands the Confederation an insult it can use, and the usos wants to know who in the Offices is selling the desk's papers.
- **The succession.** A new Orzas appoints a new Keeper of the Seal, and the houses that want Holimar's seal for one of their own freedmen are already courting the claimants.

:::secret
**For the GM:** Holimar keeps a private index of every record the Offices have lost on his instruction in nineteen years, with the name of the house that asked and the price it paid. It is the reason no great house has tried to remove him, and it is kept outside the compound, where no successor can seal it away.
:::
