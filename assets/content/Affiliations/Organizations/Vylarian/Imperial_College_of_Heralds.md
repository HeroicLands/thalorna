The living walk behind them in the order the living hold it. If you put a Silver house before a Gilded one, the Gilded house will remember it at the next three funerals and the Silver house will thank you at none of them. Learn the roll, and never guess."

## Overview

The Imperial College of Heralds, the Kalzothalon in [[skill-vylarilng|Vilari]], is the desk of arms and precedence of the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]], the bureaucracy of the Vilérian throne in [[place-arvon|Arvon]], the Inner City of [[place-orzara|Orzara City]]. Its heralds know every house's arms, marks and liveries and the order in which the houses of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] stand before the throne. No act of the throne is proclaimed, no embassy received and no funeral of a founding house held until a herald has settled who stands where. The College also sends heralds in the throne's name: to read an act aloud in a provincial forum, to stand at a foreign court's coronation, and to walk in a great house's funeral procession as the throne's witness.

The College is small beside the Offices' other desks. A few dozen heralds and their pursuivants work in the compound, and as many again teach and keep the roll in country houses outside the capital.

## The Roll

The College's whole authority is its **roll of arms**: every house's arms, its marks and liveries, the estate it stands in, and its place in the order of precedence. The four estates of the nobility, gold, silver, bronze and the personal cipher, are the roll's first division, and within each the houses stand by the age of their hallowing and the offices their dead have held. The [[affiliation-imprlscrblgld|Imperial Scribal Guild]] publishes the registry of the estates at the spring imperial address, and the Keeper of the Roll of Arms enters each change the same week. A house raised or unhallowed is a house whose place every herald must learn again before the next ceremony.

Precedence is the College's daily work, and precedence is a quarrel the College settles a hundred times a year. A house that thinks itself placed too low petitions the Master, and the Master's ruling stands until a herald finds a reason in the roll to change it. The heralds are paid by the throne and courted by every house that stands near the line between two places.

## Pursuivants and Schools

A herald learns the roll as an apprentice learns a craft, at a herald's side. The College trains its pursuivants in the compound and in country houses outside the capital, where a herald keeps a few young men at a time and teaches them the arms, marks, liveries and precedence of every house in the Empire. One such school is kept by [[being-lrdmlnvsbr|Lord Aimilon Visbari]] at his family's house outside [[place-polthara|Polthara]] in Hilen, where the pupils practice on the stewards' boards on market days. A pursuivant is sworn a herald when the Master is satisfied that he will never guess.

## The Heralds at the Palor

The embassies of the [Palor]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the Palace District of the Inner City, are received for the throne by the Herald of the Palor and the heralds under him, who keep the order in which foreign envoys stand at court. A great power's ambassador and a city-state's envoy each learn from a herald where they will stand at the next audience, and each knows that the herald's ruling is the throne's opinion of his master.

## The Present {#present}

The College's present quarrel is the succession. The reigning Orzas is old and has adopted nobody, and an accession is the greatest ceremony the College stages: the order in which the houses stand at the Orzas's Circle, who carries what in the procession, and which houses walk nearest the new Orzas. The Master's ruling on that order will tell the city which houses the new reign favors before any act is sealed. Every house that hopes to stand near the next Orzas is petitioning the Master now, and several have found reasons in the roll to stand higher than they did at the last accession.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The petition.** A house of the Silver estate pays a party to find, in the roll or in the archive, the precedent that would let it walk before its rival at the accession.
- **The order of the procession.** A claimant's steward pays a party to learn the Master's draft order for the accession before it is read at the Orzas's Circle.

# The Truth of the College {#truth .secret}

The College is the cover for the throne's own intelligence network. Lord Aimilon Visbari is the Orzas's spymaster, as his father was before him, and the network of informants and agents he inherited and has widened runs through every level of society from Provénzia to Taruzel. The College gives its heralds a reason to be at every court, every funeral and every embassy, and its roll gives them a reason to ask about every marriage and every heir. Most heralds know nothing of the network. Aimilon has no children, and he is looking for a successor among people the College has never heard of.

**The rival services.** Four bodies gather intelligence for Vilérian masters, and they watch one another as closely as they watch anyone abroad.

- **The throne's network**, in the College, under Aimilon. It answers to the Orzas alone, and the Orzas keeps the relationship concealed.
- **The Sekran's informants.** [[affiliation-sekran|The Arcane Inquisition]] runs its own network across western Ankaris to report unsanctioned magic. It overlaps the throne's without being part of it, and the senior _sekeroi_ and the throne's spymasters trade favors and watch one another.
- **The Order's own operatives.** The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] keeps intelligence operatives beyond the Sekran's hunters, drawn heavily from its Fellowship of Air, and its masters read the powerful by divination. The court plants agents in the Order's administration, and the Order knows it.
- **The Seldan's agents abroad.** [[affiliation-seldan|The Vilérian Senate]] runs its own apparatus, which plants agents inside foreign embassies and pays them with coin and the promise of a place in the Empire's diplomatic service.

The four cooperate when the Empire is threatened and betray one another on purpose when it is not. A report that reaches the Orzas through the College has often passed through one of the other three on the way, and the College cannot always tell which.
