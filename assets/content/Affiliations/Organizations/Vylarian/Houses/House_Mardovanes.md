## Overview

The usher of House Mardovanes briefs the musicians hired for the betrothal feast at the gate of the house on [[place-selvin|Selvin]]: "Play nothing from the west. The master's ancestor gave the west away, the Senate made him half a god for it, and the house is tender on both points. Play anything from Hilen. The bride's new family will be at the high table, and their grain paid for the torches."

House Mardovanes is an _ethlen_ (Gilded) house of [[place-orzara|Orzara]], and it is the newest of the Gilded. Its great man is the Orzas [Azénales]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who ruled for fifteen years while the far provinces went and then settled with the Aureldian crowns and the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]] what the Empire still was ([[lore-crownssettle|The Settlement of the Crowns]]). The house was raised to the Gilded in his reign, one of the few houses to reach that estate in two centuries. To the older Gilded Houses it is the house that signed away the west and was gilded for the signature.

## The House on the Laurel

The Mardovanes house stands near the crown of Selvin, the hill of the rising, behind a wall of dressed stone and a gate lit all night. Its gardens run down the slope in terraces fed by their own cistern, and on a feast night the house hangs lamps in every tree. The morning call is crowded: grain-brokers, officers of the dole, _kurthoi_ (senators) of the _ankren_ (Silver) houses and, lately, envoys from the Hilen estates. The cupboard by the door holds fewer masks than an old house's, and Azénales's mask stands at its head, its diadem gilded fresh every year.

The house is rich, and its money is grain. Its factors buy on the estates of [[place-hylen|Hilen]], see the wagons weighed at [[place-aravantia|Aravantara]] and sell into the state granaries that feed the capital's dole. The culture's oldest rule of politics is that whoever holds the granary decides, and the Mardovanes have built a house on knowing it.

## The Master of the House

[Nethoras Mardovanes]{.name data-type="being/npc"} is fifty-one, master of the house and a _kurthos_ of the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]]. He is a superb host. His dinners on Selvin are the best in the city, the succession is discussed at every one of them without the old Orzas ever being named, and every guest leaves owing him a small favor. He is patient, well informed and pleasant to people below him, which the old houses find suspicious in itself.

His daughter [Varelas Mardovanes]{.name data-type="being/npc"} is nineteen and betrothed to [Zetimas Ganothandis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the heir of the Olkassa estate in Hilen. The marriage joins the house that sells the capital's bread to the house that grows the best of it.

## Quarrels

- **[[affiliation-varkalenis|House Varkalenis]]**, whose vote has kept Azénales half-divine for a century, and whose master will not take a cup of wine at a Mardovanes table.
- **The [Conservative Faction]{.name data-type="affiliation"}** in the Seldan, which counts the Settlement as the Empire's surrender and the house's gilding as its price.

## What It Wants

The house wants Azénales given a temple of his own: the step from half-divine to divine, which only the Senate can vote. It wants to stand behind whichever claimant wins the succession, with the grain in its granaries when he does. And it wants the Olkassa wedding made, the estate's harvests coming in, and no change on [[place-olcassafld|the Blood-field of Olkassa]].

## Hooks

- **The bride's father.** The heir of [[affiliation-ganothandis|House Ganothandis]] wants the field settled by the rite of the [[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]] before his wedding. Nethoras hires a party to escort the priests out to the field and report to him, and he is plainly hoping the rite fails.
- **The votes.** Nethoras sends a party round the [Heights]{.name data-type="place/site"} with gifts for _kurthoi_ whose votes on Azénales's temple might be bought, and one of them already owes the Varkalenis a favor.

:::secret
**For the GM:** The house has sold the next three Olkassa harvests forward to the state granaries at a price no other field in Hilen could meet, because Olkassa's wheat comes in early and half again as heavy. If the field is settled and the quickening ends, the Mardovanes cannot fill the contract, and the house that feeds the capital cannot feed it. The master of Ganothandis knows of the sale and agreed to it. His heir does not.
:::
