## Overview

A tenant's reeve on the Olkassa estate gives a new hand his first rule in the yard before harvest: "You cut the field with the rest of us, you leave the last sheaf standing, and you say nothing about the wheat in the town. The house does not discuss it. The master lights the lamp at the shrine himself on the anniversary, and on that night you stay in your hut."

House Ganothandis is an old landed house of [[place-hylen|Hilen]], of _ankren_ (Silver) standing, named in the old way for its founder. It holds several estates in the province, and the greatest of them is Olkassa, where the army of the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] and the legions of [[place-moktur|Moktur]] killed one another ([[lore-olcassabtl|The Battle of Olkassa]]). The field they fought on is [[place-olcassafld|the Blood-field of Olkassa]], and it is the best wheat ground in the province. The house keeps a townhouse in [[place-aravantia|Aravantara]], as every Hilen house of standing does, and sits in its councils.

## The House and the Field

The estate farms the field, sells its grain into the state granaries, and does not discuss it. The wheat comes in a fortnight early and half again as heavy as on the estate's other fields, and the vines on its edge set fruit twice in a year. The house is rich on it, in the quiet way of Hilen, without the show of the capital.

The house is also the province's plainest example of Hilen's horror of civil war. The one battle the Empire fought between its own legions was fought on its land, and the house has married, voted and prayed against a second for three centuries. The [[affiliation-imperialcult|Imperial Cult]] keeps a small shrine at the field's edge to both armies' Orzoi, the only shrine in the Empire to a defeated claimant, and the Ganothandis pay for its lamp and its keeper. The house's loyalty to the cult is the loyalty of a family that has seen what follows when the eagles disagree, and it stands with the province's other old houses against the merchants and the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] alike.

## The Heir

[Zetimas Ganothandis]{.name data-type="being/npc"} is twenty-four and the house's named heir. He grew up on the estate, slept in the field-huts in the anniversary week on a dare at fifteen, and has not spoken of what he dreamed. He is earnest, devout and better read than his father, and he is betrothed to [Varelas Mardovanes]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of [[affiliation-mardovanes|House Mardovanes]].

He wants the field settled by the rite of the [[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]] before the wedding, so that he does not bring a bride home to it. The rite works perhaps half the time, and nobody knows whether settling the field would end the harvest. His father, the master of the house, is old and ill, and has given his son neither a yes nor a no.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

House Ganothandis wants no second Olkassa, and the old Orzas has adopted nobody. The house wants an orderly succession and an Orzas the Senate and the legions both accept. It is making the marriage that binds it to the capital's grain-buyers: Zetimas is betrothed to [Varelas Mardovanes]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, daughter of [[being-nthrsmrdvns|Nethoras Mardovanes]] and niece of [[being-klvnsmrdvns|Kolvenas Mardovanes]], the man the court expects to be named, who made the match. The wedding is the Laurel's event of the season.

The heir wants a field that is only a field. He wants it settled by the rite of Sélmoros before the wedding, his father has given him neither a yes nor a no, and Nethoras Mardovanes is plainly hoping the rite fails. The house has turned two surveyors of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] away from the estate's gate this year.

## Hooks

- **The rite.** Zetimas hires a party to bring a priest of Sélmoros out to Olkassa in the anniversary week, past the steward who keeps the gate, and to stay in the field-huts while the rite is said.
- **The surveyors.** Two surveyors of the Thordimalon have been turned away at the estate's gate twice this year. The third time they come with a writ, and the steward asks a party to find out who signed it.

:::secret
**For the GM:** The master of the house has agreed to the Mardovanes's forward sale of the next three Olkassa harvests, at a price only a quickened field could meet. If the rite settles the field, the house breaks its word to the house its heir is marrying, and Zetimas does not know the sale exists.
:::
