## Overview

A clerk of the provincial administration in [[place-skoraz|Soraks]], asked by a stranger for the Zarkedis house, takes him to the corner of the square and points. "That one, with the new shutters. It is not theirs. The creditors sold it with everything in it, the cupboard by the door included, and the buyer has not taken the faces out. You will find the old man in two rented rooms behind the tannery. Do not call him by the name in the street. He still answers to it."

House Zarkedis is a _thurden_ (Bronze) house of Soraks, one of the lesser cities of the Empire. Three generations ago a Zarkedis was a provincial magistrate, and the name carried weight in the local courts long after the wealth behind it had gone. The fall came through a commercial venture on borrowed money, faithless partners and creditors placed to profit from it. The father, a trusted bureaucrat in the provincial administration, was disgraced; the land was seized and the house sold to cover the debts. An old ally, the _arsir_ [[being-lophiriaghal|Lofiria Ghaliros]], turned against the house in that season and hastened it.

## A Name with Nothing Behind It

The house is still on the registry of the four estates that [[affiliation-imprlscrblgld|the Imperial Scribal Guild]] publishes every spring, among the Bronze Houses, because nobody has petitioned to take it off. A demotion is the gravest civil penalty short of attainder, and it costs a fee; the Zarkedis were not worth the fee. So the name stands on the roll with an heir behind it and nothing else: no seat, no land, no household beyond one freedwoman, and no masks. Under the culture's own rule a house's masks are its charter, and the Zarkedis masks hang in another man's cupboard across the square.

The master of the house, the disgraced father, lives on what his son sends him, and he is the last person in Soraks who speaks of the Zarkedis as a house.

## The Heir

[[being-klstrtsxrchds|Kalestratos Zarkedis]] is a mage of the [[affiliation-pirethos|Pirethos]] fellowship in the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] at [[place-velysara|Velisara]], a field _therman_ (licensed practitioner) with a reputation for talent and dangerous research. The fall of the house reached him in his last year as a student and made him what he is. He means to restore the name through arcane power and standing in the Order, which no creditor can call in, and he has taken the political support of Lady [[being-iltherethalu|Ilthere Thalurates]] of [[affiliation-thalurates|House Thalurates]] while despising the trade she offers.

## What It Wants

The house wants its masks back in a cupboard it owns. It wants a seat again, in Soraks or anywhere, and a name the Empire repeats; a Vylarian is a failure when the city stops saying his name, and the Zarkedis have heard it stop. Kalestratos wants more than that, and the Zarkedis name is what he says when he is asked why.

## Hooks

- **The cupboard across the square.** Kalestratos hires a party to buy the Zarkedis masks from the house's new owner, who refuses to sell and will not say who he bought the house for.
- **The ledger.** The creditors' sale was recorded at Soraks, and the page that names the buyer has been cut from the book. The clerk who cut it is still in the provincial administration.

:::secret
**For the GM:** The buyer at the creditors' sale was a factor of House Thalurates, and the house in Soraks, its cupboard and its masks belong to that house. Ilthere Thalurates knows. She has not told Kalestratos, and she means the masks to be the price she names when she asks him for the loyalty she wants.
:::
