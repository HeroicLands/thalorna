## Overview

A protocol officer of the Inner City, walking a new envoy past the gates of the Palace District, names the houses as they pass: "That is a kingdom's embassy, that is a banking league's, and that one, with the bronze gates and no metal on the livery, is Thalurates. They are not an embassy. They bought the address because an embassy stood on it, and they want you to make the same mistake you nearly made."

House Thalurates is a _pelerenos_ (Elevated) house, one of the families raised to nobility within the last two generations, and its name says so: houses the sovereign has raised take _-ates_, "of the line of". Its founder was the son of a freedman of the imperial household who rose in the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]] to keep the imperial seal, and the Orzas he served raised him and his line. The house has served in the Offices ever since. A Thalurates clerk, a Thalurates usher or a Thalurates factor stands somewhere in most of the business the Offices does.

## The House in the Palace District

The house's palace stands in [Palor]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the Palace District of [[place-arvon|Arvon]], among the embassies of the powers that treat with the Empire and the city palaces of the greatest families. An address there is a statement of reach, and the Thalurates paid for it in coin. The palace is new, clean and splendid, its marble bright and its gilding unworn, and it has the one fault money cannot mend: the cupboard by its door holds three masks.

The house is rich from service. Every act the Offices seal carries a fee, every petition a gift to the right clerk, and every record the right friends want found or lost has a price. The Thalurates have spent two generations learning which records matter, and they have begun to buy the debts of the old houses that despise them.

## The Member at Court

Lady [[being-iltherethalu|Ilthere Thalurates]] is the house's face at court: pragmatic, self-serving and well connected. She offers political support in exchange for help with key figures in the Empire, and her bargains are always with people the house can use. The most interesting of them is [[being-klstrtsxrchds|Kalestratos Zarkedis]], a fire-mage of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] and heir of the fallen [[affiliation-zarkedis|House Zarkedis]], whom she supports and who despises the kind of politics she practices while finding her indispensable.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

House Thalurates wants a hand in the succession and the throne in its debt. Its master, [[being-krznsthlrts|Korzenas Thalurates]], a former clerk of the seal, buys the notes, mortgages and failing estates of the old houses through factors in their own names, and a good part of the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] votes as he wishes on any question that matters to him. He wants _ankren_ (Silver) standing, which takes three generations of service, a substantial fee and the seal of the Dukran. He wants his heir married into a founding house, so that his grandchildren's masks stand in an old cupboard, and a daughter married to the next Orzas. A new Orzas makes a new Keeper of the Seal, and the house means the post to stay in the Offices it knows.

- **[[affiliation-varkalenis|House Varkalenis]]**, whose master will not dine with the Thalurates and votes against them on every question of standing.
- **The [Conservative Faction]{.name data-type="affiliation"}**, which holds that the Offices belong to freedmen and clerks and the Senate to the old houses, and that a house which keeps the seal should not also keep a vote.
- **The Khelathi factor**, [[being-mthszrtml|Methisi Azeret'Mulu]], who has begun buying the same houses' debts with Khelathi silver, at better prices.

## Hooks

- **The address.** A minor kingdom's envoy arrives at the Thalurates gate believing it is a shared embassy compound, and the house's steward lets him in. A party is hired by his own legation to find out what he signed before anyone told him where he was.
- **Ilthere's price.** Ilthere offers Kalestratos aid that will cost him his loyalty to House Thalurates, and asks a party to carry the offer, in writing, to [[place-velysara|Velisara]].

:::secret
**For the GM:** The house's factors hold two debts that would ruin two houses. One is a note against the temple stipends of House Varkalenis, which the house means to forgive when the Varkalenis heir marries a Thalurates daughter. The other is the creditors' sale of House Zarkedis: a Thalurates factor bought the Zarkedis house in Soraks, and the Zarkedis masks are still in its cupboard. Ilthere means them to be the price she names to Kalestratos.
:::
