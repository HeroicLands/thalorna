## Overview

The steward of House Varkalenis gives each new client the same instruction on his first morning at the door: "Stand on the third step, and look at the cupboard, not at the ceiling. Five of those faces were Orzoi, and three of them are gods. The plaster has waited since the fire, and it can wait for you."

House Varkalenis is an _ethlen_ (Gilded) house of [[place-orzara|Orzara]], one of the founding-era families of the Empire, and it still lives on [[place-antel|Antel]], the Old Hill, among the few old houses kept up by families too proud or too poor to leave. The Varkalenis are both. When the last Orzas of the Founder's line died without a son, he named a _felzos_ (legate) of the house to follow him, and the Varkalenis Orzoi who came after opened the Age of Conquest ([[lore-vylariages|The Vylarian Ages]]). The house has not held the throne in centuries, and it has never stopped being the house that held it first after the Founder.

## The House on the Old Hill

The house is a city palace of the Republic's age, built round two courts on the eastern shoulder of Antel. The street front carries the gilded cypher of the Gilded Houses on its doorposts, and the bronze doors are opened every morning for the call. Inside, the hall of the morning call is frescoed with the triumphs of the house's Orzoi, and the frescoes are flaking from the ceiling in plates the size of a hand; a slave sweeps the gilt from the floor before the clients arrive. The second court is shut, its rooms let to two antiquarians and a retired clerk of the Seldan. The garden behind it grows cabbages.

The cupboard by the door is the house's wealth. It holds the wax masks (_lavura_) of every Varkalenis who held office, the five Orzoi among them, each mask wearing the diadem of its reign in gilt wood. Three of the five are divine by the vote of the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]], and their temples belong to the [[affiliation-imperialcult|Imperial Cult]]. By custom a Varkalenis serves as _zalvos_ (priest of the cult) in each of the three, and the cult's stipends for those posts are most of what the house lives on. A Varkalenis therefore spends three mornings in every month offering incense to his own ancestor, and is paid for it.

The house's seat in the Senate is its other asset. A _kurthos_ (senator) of a founding house has a name that carries weight before he rises, and the Varkalenis vote stands with the [Conservative Faction]{.name data-type="affiliation"} on every question of precedence, the commission system and the citizenship.

## The Master of the House

[Ankelas Varkalenis]{.name data-type="being/npc"} is seventy-three, master of the house, _kurthos_ for fifty years and _zalvos_ of the temple of his divine ancestor. He walks down from Antel to the Seldan every morning on the arm of the house's freedman, because the house no longer keeps chair-bearers, and he walks slowly enough that the walk is noticed. He is courteous, exact about precedence and generous with the little he has, and he is the whole of the house's weight at court.

He has named no heir. Three cousins of the name each expect to be adopted, and each attends his morning call. A house name is meant to stand on the roll with an heir behind it, and the empty place behind his is the talk of the [Heights]{.name data-type="place/site"}.

## Quarrels

- **[[affiliation-mardovanes|House Mardovanes]]**, the house that gave the Empire the Orzas who settled the provinces away ([[lore-crownssettle|The Settlement of the Crowns]]). The Varkalenis vote has kept that Orzas half-divine for a century.
- **[[affiliation-thalurates|House Thalurates]]**, raised from the clerks of the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]] within living memory. Ankelas will not dine with them, and calls them the Offices' children.

## What It Wants

The house wants the post of _holzathos_, the Senate-named officer who administers the Imperial Cult and hears the oaths sworn in court: it pays well, it puts a Varkalenis at the head of the rites his ancestors receive, and the old Orzas's death will make it the most contested appointment in a generation. It wants the old Orzas's consecration vote to go the way its own interest goes. And it wants an heir it can afford.

## Hooks

- **The temple's deed.** The cult's treasurer means to let the porch of a Varkalenis Orzas's temple to a ropemaker unless the house can show the endowment that keeps it. The original deed burned with the imperial archive ([[lore-archivefire|The Archive Fire]]), and the house needs a copy from a provincial chapterhouse of [[affiliation-imprlscrblgld|the Imperial Scribal Guild]] before the treasurer's term ends.
- **The three cousins.** Each of the cousins hires a party to make the other two look unfit before Ankelas writes his will.

:::secret
**For the GM:** The house is in debt to the edge of its stipends. Its Keeper of the Seal borrowed against them from a moneylender of [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]], the note has been sold on, and a factor of House Thalurates holds it. Ankelas does not know who holds it. The holder means to forgive it in exchange for the house's heir marrying a Thalurates daughter.
:::
