## Overview

**The Path of Breath** is a sacred martial order of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Empire of Vylaria]]. It admits only the most accomplished warriors, devoted to uniting technique with spiritual discipline, and its members are the masters of the sword discipline of the same name, the [[skill-pthbrth|Path of Breath]].

## Membership

A member of the order bears a tattoo of a dragonfly on the neck. There are three living masters, among them [[being-krmdnsbrkrtys|Koremdanis Barkratis]], the foremost swordmaster in the Empire.
