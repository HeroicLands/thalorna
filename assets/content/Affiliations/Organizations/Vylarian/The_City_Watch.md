A watch-sergeant at the Spire Circle tells every new man the same thing on his first morning, before the cudgel is handed over: "You did twenty years for the eagle. So did the man on the stair you are about to walk up. He knows what the Empire owes him, and he knows you know. Keep the peace and keep his name out of the book if you can. Keep the fire out of his roof whatever it costs."

## Overview

The City Watch, the Zorala in [[skill-vylarilng|Vylari]], is the standing branch of [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|The Vylarian Military]] that keeps the peace of [[place-orzara|Orzara City]] beyond the [Inner Wall]{.name data-type="place/structure"}. It is legionary in recruitment, discipline and equipment, and it is a city institution: it takes no part in foreign campaigns, and it fights as soldiers only when a Circle riots. It patrols the wards, arrests, guards the gates and the state granaries, puts out the city's fires, holds the gate-roads across the [Clearance]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, and supplies the muscle behind the rulings of the [[affiliation-aediles|Pelthralon]]. It enforces and investigates, and it does not judge: a man the Watch takes goes before a _peltros_ (ward magistrate) or the Criminal Tribunal of the [[affiliation-verdanorzara|Orzara Courts]].

The Watch is filled with veteran-citizens, men discharged from the legions at about thirty-five and settled in the capital on the dole. A place in it is among the most prized careers after service, because the wage on top of the pension turns scraping by into a living. Many watchmen live in the wards they patrol and drink in the taverns of their old legions. The [[affiliation-sorvan|Sorvan]], the imperial guard, keeps the Inner City and the Watch keeps everything below it, and the two despise each other across the wall.

Lesser watches on the same model keep the peace in the Empire's other great cities, each under its own city's council.

## How the Watch Is Kept

The Watch is organized on the legionary pattern and bent to the city's shape. Each ward's watch-house quarters a watch-cohort, a _Parthal_ of four to five hundred men, broken into _Hundali_ (centuries) and the patrols that walk the lanes. Across the city the whole force comes to about a legion's strength, some five thousand men. It is officered entirely through the commoner track of the legions: career centurions and senior veterans, with no purchased commission anywhere in it. A legion's centurion pensioned off the frontier into the Watch counts it a soft billet or a comedown according to temperament.

The **Nokaran**, the _zoralus_ (prefect of the watch), commands the whole and answers to the Veramus of Order on the Verdan Orzara, the civic council of five. No general directs the Watch day to day. The arrangement is deliberate: the throne keeps the capital's standing muscle answerable to its civic appointees, so that no single commander holds the city's armed men, its granaries and its courts together.

By day the watchmen go lightly armed for the press of the streets, with a cudgel, a short blade and no more than a leather jack, because a man in full harness cannot chase a cutpurse up a stepped lane. Each watch-house can put its cohort into full legionary kit within the hour. On every dole-day the cohorts of the wards with pay-halls muster in that kit, and the queue at the Spire Circle stays orderly because of it.

## The Watch-Houses

A watch-house stands on or beside nearly every one of the Twelve Circles, with a further station guarding the [Velmiros]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the city's park. The watch-houses are not large, but each is walled and defensible, built to stand as an island of order when its Circle erupts. Each keeps an armory, a cache of food and water for several days, a small stable for mounted messengers and patrols, and its fire-gear: hand-engines, hooks, ladders and a cistern fed from the aqueduct. Those stables, with the Sorvan's on the palace hill, are almost the only stables of any size inside the city.

The houses differ with their wards.

- **The Spire Circle**, in the [[place-warrensorz|Warrens]], is the largest watch-house and the largest pay-hall in the city, and its cohort walks the tenements of [Nerval]{.name data-type="place/feature"}.
- **The River Circle**, on the [[place-wharvesorz|Wharves]], keeps a full cohort and a man at every water-stair, holds the river gate, and bars the gate of the Strangers' Row at dusk.
- **The Circle of Árdavon**, in the [[place-platandstr|Platan District]], keeps an armory deep enough to put its cohort in kit, because a verdict that goes the wrong way before a crowd can become a riot.
- **The Grand Market Circle**, in the [[place-grandmarketorz|Grand Market]], guards the counting-house doors and counts the stall-hands out at the closing bell.
- **The Racecourse Circle** stands between the racing factions, the [[affiliation-ethloi|Ethloi]] and the [[affiliation-zargoi|Zargoi]], on every race day.
- **The Circle of Marendas**, in the [[place-scholarsdstr|Scholars' District]], is a small house whose men spend their nights returning students to their colleges.
- **The Circle of Heroes**, on the [[place-heightsorz|Heights]], walks its lanes lightly and does not pass a great house's door without the master's leave or a writ.

## The Work

The Watch's charge is broad, and the city learns its parts one at a time.

**The peace.** The watchmen patrol by day and by night, break up brawls, take thieves and debtors, and bring the accused before the court that will hear them. They are the first answer to disorder in the city, and in a riot they are the only answer the city has before the throne sends the Sorvan down the hill.

**The fires.** The Watch is the city's fire-brigade. Each night a fire-watch walks the timber wards, and at the first cry the nearest watch-house runs its engines and hooks to the blaze. The watchmen pull down the buildings around a fire to starve it, and the Keeper of the Engines decides which ones. A ward whose tenements lean too far has been pulled down by the Watch to save the next one more than once.

**The gates and the line.** The Watch holds every gate-road across the Clearance, where its gate-sergeants keep the residence-check beside the customs-men of [[affiliation-tolvan|The Tolvan]]. A traveler's blade goes through the gate wrapped and declared. At dusk the Watch puts the non-citizens who have no waiver back across the Clearance, and in lean years it comes through to pull down or burn the lean-tos that creep out onto the strip. Its charter stops at the gate-yards beyond the line.

**The magistrates' muscle.** When a _peltros_ shutters a shop, condemns a tenement or orders an eighth story pulled down, the Watch carries out the order. The magistrates judge and the Watch does the work, and both are paid for not doing it.

**The bribe.** By long tradition the watchmen are thoroughly bribable. A cookshop pays the patrol a meal for a blind eye to a brazier, a landlord pays the sergeant for a slow answer to an inspector's complaint, and the price of a man's name staying out of the watch-house book is known on every stair.

## The Watch and Its Neighbors

[[affiliation-sorvan|The Sorvan]]
: the guard on the palace hill, whose writ stops at the Inner Wall. The guardsmen call the watchmen street-wardens, and the watchmen answer with the resentment of men who do the unglamorous work of keeping a million people from one another's throats.

[[affiliation-aediles|The Pelthralon]] and the courts
: the Watch enforces what the ward magistrates and the Criminal Tribunal decide, and brings them their accused.

The mercenary companies
: the Watch's veterans dislike every company in imperial pay, because a company draws the throne's silver without twenty years under the eagle. The Watch keeps the gate-yards where the purser of [[affiliation-goitsathal|Goitsa Thal]] stables his mule and unwraps his sword, and it put the captain of [[affiliation-hrendskari|Hrendskari]] across the Clearance at dusk with the other non-citizens when she came south to renew her contract. Its quarrel with [[affiliation-cleanshlds|The Clean Shields]] is daily: at the river gate a watch-sergeant and the company's river-master count each morning's barge string across to each other, and the two counts often differ.

The racing factions
: on a race day the Watch stands between the colors from the first race to the last, and then stands between them again on the way to the gates.

## The Present {#present}

The Watch's quarrel this year is with The Clean Shields. Last winter a watchman of the River Circle was knifed in a gate-yard brawl with men of the company, and the man who did it was paid off and turned out the same night. The River Circle's cohort wants him, and its captain cannot go into the outskirts after him, because the Watch's charter ends at the gate-yards. The veterans' stairs on the River Circle have petitioned the Orzara Courts to have the company removed from the river, and [[being-sthmrrdln|Sathemar Ardelen]], whom the stairs call their tribune, has taken up the petition. Two claimants' agents have dined at the company's camp this spring, and the Watch counts every armed man within a day of the city who answers to neither the legions, the Watch nor the Sorvan.

The succession is the Watch's other fear. The Watch answers to the Veramus of Order, and the Veramus holds his seat at the throne's pleasure. The reigning Orzas has adopted nobody, and a new reign brings a new council. Every claimant who expects to need the streets on the night the Orzas dies is courting the watch-house captains, one Circle at a time.

The lean years are the third. The lean-tos are on the Clearance again below the Salkalon Gate, and the Veramus of Order has ordered the strip cleared. The watchmen who will clear it are veterans whose own pensions are borrowed, and they know it.

## Hooks {#hooks}

- **The knifeman.** A watch-sergeant of the River Circle hires a party to find the man who knifed his comrade, somewhere in the outskirts where the Watch cannot follow.
- **The captain's dinner.** A watch-house captain has been invited to dine at a great house on the Heights, and a party is asked to learn which claimant is paying for the meal.
- **The strip below the Salkalon Gate.** The Watch clears the lean-tos at dawn, and the people living on them have nowhere to go.

# The Truth of the Watch {#truth .secret}

The Watch sells the right to sleep inside the line by the night. The watch-house on the Spire Circle issues small wooden tallies, notched for the month, to anyone who pays a Bit a night to its sergeants, and a watchman on a night sweep who finds a sleeper holding the right tally walks past. About three thousand people sleep in the [[place-warrensorz|Warrens]] on the tallies. The tallies are sold through the cookshops, and the coin goes to the sergeants, the centurions of the watch-cohort and its captain, in shares fixed by rank. The legions' stairs take a share on their own stairs and keep the secret for it.

The sweeps still happen. When the Veramus of Order wants to see numbers, the captain of the Spire Circle house sends his men up the stairs of the people who have not paid, and the Watch puts a hundred of them across the Clearance in a night. The Veramus counts the numbers and is satisfied.
