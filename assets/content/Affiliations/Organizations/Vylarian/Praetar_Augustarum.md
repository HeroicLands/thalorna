_Vylarian: Praetar Augustarum – "the Guard of the Augustars"_

- **Type:** Imperial military institution; household guard and Inner City garrison
- **Region of operation:** [[place-urbsaquiln|Urbs Aquilion]], the Inner City of [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]]; the imperial person wherever he travels
- **Seat:** The **[[place-castraprtr|Castra Praetoria]]**, on the palace hill within the Inner Wall
- **Answerable to:** The reigning **Augustar** alone

## Overview

The Praetar Augustarum are the imperial guard – the Augustar's personal bodyguard, and the keepers of order and justice within the Inner City of [[place-magnapolis|Magnápolis]] itself. They stand apart from the [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|line legions]] in every way that matters: they recruit on their own authority, promote through their own ranks, answer to no Dux Bellorum or Magnar but to the throne directly, and are quartered not in a frontier castra but in the [[place-castraprtr|Castra Praetoria]] on the palace hill, the one fortress inside the Inner Wall. They are elite, lavishly equipped, and intensely political – the praetorian muscle of the court, and the instrument through which the Augustar's grip on his own capital is made physical.

Their charge is twofold: the imperial person, and the peace of the Inner City. Within [[place-urbsaquiln|Urbs Aquilion]] they are guard, garrison, and magistrate's arm together – warding the [[place-palatimgnm|Palatium Magnum]], standing the gates and walls of the citadel, and keeping order among the senators, priests, magistrates, and great families who dwell or do business within. Their writ stops, sharply, at the Inner Wall. The vast city beyond is not theirs to police; that work belongs to the [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|City Watch]], and the division is deliberate.

## The Body That Makes and Unmakes

The central fact of the Praetar Augustarum – the one that shapes every reign – is that the body best placed to guard an Augustar is also the body best placed to unmake one. They hold the palace, the citadel, and the armed initiative at the very heart of the Empire. A guard that withdraws its protection at the wrong moment, or extends it to the wrong claimant, can end a reign in a single night without a legion ever marching.

Every reigning Augustar therefore courts the guard's loyalty with deliberate care – through donatives paid on accession and at intervals after, through privileges and prestige, through the careful watching of the officers who command the watches and could, between them, decide a succession. A throne that neglects the guard is a throne living on borrowed time; a throne that buys it too nakedly teaches the guard the price of its own loyalty, which is its own danger. The management of the Praetar Augustarum is one of the permanent arts of Vylarian rulership, and the histories are full of Augustars who mastered it and Augustars who did not.

## Recruitment and Standing

The guard recruits and promotes apart from the legions, and service in it is a privilege rather than a posting. Where the line army runs on its [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|dual-track structure]] – purchased noble commissions over a meritocratic commoner corps – the Praetar Augustarum is its own closed world, drawing picked men into a force that is better paid, better armored, and far closer to power than any frontier legion. A transfer into the guard is a mark of favor; a command within it is a position of real political weight, for its senior officers stand within reach of the imperial presence and the succession itself.

This closeness to power is also the institution's hazard. The same elite standing that makes the guard formidable makes it proud, factional, and acutely conscious of its own importance – and a guard that believes itself the maker of emperors is never wholly safe to keep.

## Relations

- **The Augustar** – patron, charge, and sole master. The guard answers to the throne directly and to no intermediate command; the relationship is one of mutual dependence and mutual wariness.
- **The [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|line legions and Dux Bellorum]]** – a separate world. The guard takes no orders from the war command and fights no frontier campaigns; the legions, for their part, eye the praetorians' privileges with a mixture of envy and distrust.
- **The [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|City Watch]]** – its opposite number below the hill. The guard keeps the Inner City; the Watch keeps the rest. The praetorians look down on the vigiles as glorified street-wardens, a contempt the Watch returns with the resentment of men who do the unglamorous work of policing a million people.
- **The [[affiliation-curiaurbis|Curia Urbis]] and the civic courts** – govern the city beyond the wall; the guard's jurisdiction inside the Inner City sits beside, and above, theirs.
- **The court factions** – the guard is courted by all of them. Its loyalty, real or purchased, is among the most consequential variables in any contest over the imperial succession.

## Identifying Marks

The Praetar Augustarum wear armor far richer than any line legion's – burnished and gilded, worked with the imperial Aquila, and meant to be seen on the walls and at the palace gates as a standing display of the throne's wealth and reach. Their standards and liveries set them visibly apart from the field army; a praetorian in full harness is unmistakable, and intended to be. The captaincy of the permanent palace detail is among the most trusted postings in the Empire, sharing (with the Quaestor Imperii and the Censor Maximus of the [[affiliation-aerarimmpr|Aerarium]]) the securing of the imperial strongroom.

## Plot Hooks

- **The Donative Withheld.** A new Augustar, pressed for funds, has delayed the customary accession-donative to the guard. The delay is being read, in the Castra, as either weakness or insult. The officers who command the watches are being quietly sounded out by more than one party.
- **The Wrong Gate.** On a night of crisis, the question of which way the gates of the [[place-castraprtr|Castra Praetoria]] open will decide a succession. A handful of watch-captains hold the answer; each is being courted, threatened, and bribed by rival claimants and their agents.
- **The Praetorian's Reach.** A senior guard officer has begun extending the guard's "keeping of order" within the Inner City into matters that properly belong to the imperial tribunals – arrests, detentions, quiet disappearances – under color of protecting the throne. The [[affiliation-curiaurbis|courts]] and the affected great houses are alarmed; the Augustar finds the overreach useful and is in no hurry to curb it.
