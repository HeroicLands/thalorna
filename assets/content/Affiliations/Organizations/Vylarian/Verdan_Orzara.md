## The Courts of Orzara

Justice and civic government in the imperial capital run along three separate tracks that meet only at the throne. The **[[place-orzara|Orzara City]] beyond the [Inner Wall]{.name data-type="place/structure"}**—its administration, its courts, its Watch, and its daily supply—is governed by the **Verdan Orzara**, the Council of the City. **[[place-arvon|Arvon]] (the Inner City)** keeps its own order under [[affiliation-sorvan|The Sorvan]], the imperial guard. And the **imperial tribunals**—the throne's own courts—sit apart from both, within the **[Imperial Offices]{.name data-type="place/structure"}** compound, answerable to the Orzas, the emperor, alone.

### The Verdan Orzara—the Council of the City

Orzara is not ruled by an _usos_ (prefect). The Empire learned long ago that a single man set over a city of a million—commanding its courts, its granaries, and its armed Watch all at once—is a man with the means to make himself a rival to the throne. So the city is governed instead by a **council of five**, the **Verdan Orzara**, whose members are appointed (and dismissed) by the Orzas, most often drawn from the senatorial order.

The five **veramusoi** (curators of the city) sit as equals and decide the weightier matters as a body, by majority. Each holds a standing portfolio:

The Veramus of Law
: the city's chief judicial authority; oversees the _peltroi_ (ward magistrates), the Civil Courts, and the Criminal Tribunal, and presides when the council sits as the supreme civic court.

The Veramus of Order
: commands the [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|City Watch]], and answers for public order, the watch-houses, and the city gaols.

The Veramus of the Grain
: the granaries, the dole, the licensed markets, and the unceasing daily supply on which the capital lives.

The Veramus of Works and Waters
: the fabric of the city: its streets and bridges, its aqueducts and sewers, its public buildings, baths, and the upkeep (or quiet neglect) of the Circles and avenues.

The Veramus of the Rolls
: the census, the citizen-rolls, the city's records, and its correspondence with the throne; by custom the council's coordinating seat.

The chair rotates among them so that none becomes "the" head of the city, and the Orzas takes deliberate care to appoint _veramusoi_ who will check rather than collude with one another. A council that grows too harmonious is a council the throne begins to watch. In practice the five are perpetual rivals—for the Orzas's favor, for the richest portfolios, and for the patronage that flows through each office—and a great deal of the city's real politics is the politics of the Verdan Orzara.

### The Civic Courts

Beneath the council runs a tiered court system. The [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|City Watch]] enforces and investigates; it does not judge. Judgment belongs to the courts.

A ward clerk of the [[affiliation-aediles|Pelthralon]], asked how the tiers look from the bottom, answers for most of the city: "Most people see one magistrate in their lives, and he is the _peltros_ of their ward. Everything above him is something that happens to somebody else."

The Pelthralon
: the magistrates of the wards, and the only court most Orzarans ever see. They keep the district law: markets and licenses, weights and measures, building and sanitation, nuisance and petty disorder. They levy fines, shutter premises, and condemn buildings; the Watch supplies the muscle behind their rulings.

The Civil Courts
: the civil courts, where citizen sues citizen over property, contract, debt, inheritance, and standing. Cases are heard by **Sentori** drawn from the propertied orders, the weightier disputes before panels.

The Criminal Tribunal
: for theft, violence, homicide, and sedition short of treason. The Watch brings the accused; the Tribunal judges; its sentences run from fines and forced labor to the arena and the executioner.

Appeals, and the gravest civic cases, rise to the **Verdan Orzara** sitting as a body under the _veramus_ of Law. There the civic system ends; it has no authority over the nobility, the Senate, or the crown.

### The Imperial Tribunals

Entirely separate from the civic courts stand the **imperial tribunals**—the courts of the throne itself. They sit within the Inner City, in the Imperial Offices, the great bureaucratic compound that also houses the treasury, the tax administration, and the imperial archives, and they answer to the Orzas and his appointed judges, never to the Verdan Orzara.

Their jurisdiction is everything the civic courts cannot touch. Any matter involving a member of the nobility, any cause touching the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] (Senate) or the imperial house, the great suits between provinces and crowns, and **treason** above all, are imperial business from the first moment—and a case can be lifted out of the civic courts into the imperial tribunals at the throne's word. For senatorial persons in the gravest matters, judgment may rise higher still, to the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] itself. To be summoned from the Pelthralon's bench to the Offices is, for most of the city, a terror in itself: it means one's affair has caught the attention of powers far above the ward.

### The Reach of Influence

For all its tiers and titles, the law of Orzara runs partly on influence, and everyone knows it. A defendant with the right friends, the right purse or the right patron finds the courts accommodating; one without learns how heavy they can be. The _peltroi_ are famously bribable; a _veramus_'s favor can move a case between courts or bury it in the Offices's bottomless archives; and the perpetual rivalry of the Verdan Orzara means that a well-placed enemy on the council can be as dangerous to a citizen as any charge. Among the quieter profits of the courts, too, is the trade in **residency waivers**—the papers that let a non-citizen dwell within the walls in some great house's service (see [[doc-vylrnctznshp|Vylarian Citizenship]]); granted through the courts, they move fast for the right consideration. Justice is real in Orzara—but it is also, always, a currency.
