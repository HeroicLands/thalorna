## Overview

Gar-Qeltu was the pearl-divers' house, a lineage whose charter once governed the pearl trade of [[place-aukhelathrgq|Aû'Khelâthu]] until the Gar-Aû's word dissolved it when that charter lapsed. It stands as the last major dissolution anyone in the empire can name, a single sharp example of what a withdrawn imperial charter actually means in practice. Every guild master reminded of Gar-Qeltu's fate renews a charter well before its term runs out, rather than test whether the Gar-Aû's patience matches the house's own confidence.
