## Overview

Lut-Zethu, the Imperial Library, is the empire's collection of texts, held under the hand of [[affiliation-linzethkhlth|the scribes' guild]] and closed to most who would read there. Access runs through the guild's own permission rather than any general right of scholarship, so a reader's standing with the scribes matters as much as the subject under study. What the library holds on the spirit-realm is kept behind a further door still, open to fewer readers than the rest of the collection combined.
