## Overview {#overview}

The Tha'Ulgau came out of the western hills with their horses and buried their dead with them. Their chief was crowned on [[place-yathtelgu|Yath-Telgu]] in 1612 ST (499 BF), after his horsemen destroyed the last army of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] on the west-bank floodplain below [[place-lenennezut|Lenen-Nezut]] ([[lore-thaulgau|the Coming of the Tha'Ulgau]]). The house held the throne for four reigns and fifty-nine years. Its fourth Gar-Au, [Legir'Uzner I]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, went to the West in 1671 ST (440 BF), and the priesthood closed the house and raised another ([[lore-hillhouse|the Closing of the Hill House]]).

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

The conquerors' kin were given the western horse-pastures and became a Halzi'a house, settled in 1671 ST. The house holds the selat of [[place-zuqeztunome|Zu-Qeztu]] from [[place-zileti|Zileti]], where its court sits beside the selat's chief temple of Uqa'a. It keeps the pastures and the watch against the raiders of the herding clans: the horses graze the margin and the levies ride its edge.

## Holdings {#holdings}

The grass west of Zileti holds the house's first graves, [[place-horsebarrows|the Horse Barrows]], earth mounds of the warriors and chiefs who rode down with the nomad chief, each laid with the horse or horses that carried him. The barrows came inside the empire's border with the house.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

The house owes a debt to the ground below Lenen-Nezut, where the dead of the old capital's last army lie unread. Each Gelet its Halzi'a rides in from Zileti with an offering and no escort and sets it at the edge of the field below the stela of [Kuz'Uqa]{.name data-type="being/npc"}. Its ancestors owe the offering, and the house has kept the debt as it keeps its horses. The raiders of the western clans carry arms from the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]], and the house holds the line they cross.
