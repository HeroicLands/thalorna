## Overview {#overview}

In 1790 ST (321 BF) the priesthood crowned [Anleth'Uqa I]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the first Gar-Au of **Thelqen'Relu**, the house of the sun. His throne name means the horizon of the sun-lord, and it stated his program. He was a devotee of [[lore-uqaadty|Uqa'a]] in the strong sense: he held that the [[affiliation-khelathpnthn|Twelve]] and the local gods were one god seen in different lights, and that the light was Uqa'a's ([[lore-sunhouse|the Sun-House at Magu-Thelqen]]). The house ruled for a generation and was gone by 1822 ST (289 BF) ([[lore-akhrestcrown|the Restored Crown]]).

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

The house holds no throne. Two Gar-Auu of the house laid the city out from the chief temple of Uqa'a and ruled from it, and the city began to empty when the great temples of the [[affiliation-qearet|Faith of Qe'aret]] and the [[affiliation-rethsaar|Faith of Reth'Sa'ar]] refused the house's doctrine of one sun behind every god and the court went home to [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]].

## Holdings {#holdings}

The house's work is the planned temple-city of [[place-maguathen|Magu-Thelqen]], left half-abandoned. The farmers keep the estates running while the city stands half empty, and in Magu-Thelqen the Legha'lutu's estates carry the weight the empty city cannot.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

The doctrine of one sun is the house's quarrel, and the temples of Qe'aret and Reth'Sa'ar that refused it still stand. The house's scholars set the doctrine down in a book, and the temple of Reth'Sa'ar at [[place-khelunu|Khelunu]] walled it into a chamber of its library-temple and declared its contents heretical. The chamber has stayed sealed since, and the scholars who have wanted it opened have been refused. The priesthood treats a Gar-Au who claims too much of the sun as a danger to the throne.
