## Overview {#overview}

"Every one of us is Meqes," a palace scribe tells a clerk new to the court, "and the number is how you tell which." **Zu'Meku** is the house that holds the throne of [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Au'Khelathu]]. Its head [Meqes I]{.name data-type="being/npc"} stood beside the child [Kuz'el'Retha III]{.name data-type="being/npc"} and was crowned in the child's second year ([[lore-childfloods|the Child of Three Floods]]), in 470 AF. He kept his own given name as his throne name, and every Gar-Au of the house since has been crowned Meqes. The house is two and a half centuries old, the longest-lived in the Near Count ([[lore-akhhsmeqes|the House of Meqes]]).

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

[[being-garaumeqesxv|Meqes XVI]] reigns from [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]], the sixteenth of the name. She is the daughter of Meqes XV and was crowned in 709 AF at thirty-five, and she is in her twelfth year on the throne. Her nephew [[being-anlaghur|Anlaghur]] is an Iru'palu whose blood places him near the succession. The house sits at the top of the ladder of ranks, and the Iru'palu of the most exalted houses stand below it.

## Holdings {#holdings}

The house holds the palace on [[place-yathtelgu|Yath-Telgu]] and, across the water at [[place-zugezer|Zu-Gezer]], the river-front of the royal necropolis: [Meqes VI]{.name data-type="being/npc"} carried the older houses out by [[lore-greatremovl|the Great Removal]] and set the house's own Way at the head of the death-road, splendid in dressed white stone, gold leaf and cobalt. The Way is still unfinished and lengthens with each royal burial.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

The house reigns while the flood fails. Nobody believes the Gar-Au is a god, everybody knows nobody believes it, and the fiction is kept anyway; two low floods in a row strain its legitimacy. The granaries are thinning and the governors of the selatu quietly hold back tribute. The succession question is asked seriously at court, and the house has two children's reigns in its own list, and the reign it ended to take the throne, to remind it what a succession can cost.

## History as Told {#history}

The king-list gives the house reign by reign in [[lore-garauu|Gar-Auu]]. The chartered guilds date themselves by it: [[affiliation-garnul|Gar'Nul]] holds its charter from [Meqes VII]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, and [[affiliation-garmelnu|Gar'Melnu]] from [Meqes XI]{.name data-type="being/npc"}.
