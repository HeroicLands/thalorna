## Overview {#overview}

In 1462 ST (649 BF) the throne of [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Au'Khelathu]] was in dispute between the river's two ends, and the upriver selatu, whose granaries fed the capital, had grain enough to carry a claim of their own. The head of **Geze'el'Anlaghu**, a house of the upper river, was crowned in his own city, in the temple of the ram at [[place-lenennezut|Lenen-Nezut]], and took the throne name [Zab'el'Psaqa I]{.name data-type="being/npc"}. The priesthood of Uqa'a performed the rite ([[lore-thronelnz|the Throne at Lenen-Nezut]]). Three of its Gar-Auu ruled from Lenen-Nezut and are buried there.

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

The Halzi'a's house at Lenen-Nezut is Geze'el'Anlaghu, descended from the Gar-Auu who were crowned and buried there. The dynasty that ruled from the town is gone, and the pride of having been the center stays with the Halzi'a, the temple and the town.

## Holdings {#holdings}

The house keeps the three royal tombs on the desert edge above the town, [[place-lnzways|the Lenen-Nezut Ways]], laid out in imitation of a dynasty's Way at [[place-zugezer|Zu-Gezer]] with no spine and no necropolis for it to cross.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

The house keeps a claim of royal blood that sits awkwardly beside the question of the next succession. The king-list enters the line as the legitimate one for sixty-nine years and gives no reign to the Galezkara claimants who held the delta at the same time ([[lore-akhdivriver|the Divided River]]). Its last Gar-Au died in the battle in the wheatland of Iqeru, and with him the line as a line of Gar-Auu ([[lore-twothrones|the War of the Two Thrones]]). A Halzi'a of the house still sits in a court that was a capital.
