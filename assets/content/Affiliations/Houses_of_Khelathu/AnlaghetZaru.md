## Overview {#overview}

"The house keeps the granary shut by order," a survey scribe of the selat says, "and the selat throws bread over the wall each year. Both are the house's doing." **Anlaghet'Zaru** is the Halzi'a house of [[place-anlaghzetun|Anlagh-Zetun]], whose head, [Gezanlaghur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, raised the upper river in 2378 ST (268 AF). His own levies came first, the temple levies of the upper river came with them, and the host broke the god-king's guard at the granary gates ([[lore-sealedgran|the Battle of the Sealed Granaries]]). His rising delivered the last Gar-Au of the Struck House to the temple ([[lore-akhstruckhs|the Struck House]]).

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

The house holds the Halzi'a's seat of the selat from Anlagh-Zetun. The selat is the heartland's heartland, broad, fertile, settled and stubbornly traditional, and its patron is Qe'aret, goddess of order, truth and right measure.

## Holdings {#holdings}

The house keeps [[place-eleventhgran|the Eleventh Granary]], the bricked dome at the end of the granary row, shut by order.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

The house owes the dead of the granary rising. Several thousand lay dead between the domes, the guard and the levies together, and the living had no grain to pay the embalmers, so the dead of both sides were shut into one emptied granary with no rite said and no name entered. The selat meant to open it when the rites could be paid for, and the house keeps it shut by order. Each anniversary the selat throws bread over the wall, the festival of the [Bread for the Gate]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, and the bread is the house's offering at the edge; the poor of [[place-amqelulegez|Amqelu-Legez]] eat what lands outside. The dome is worse in lean years, and the granaries are thinning.
