## Overview {#overview}

**Azlet'Kutu** is the second house of the king-list, entered with the First House in [[lore-akhfirsttab|the First Tablets]]. It took the throne in 621 ST. Its reigns shorten by tens of years as the age goes on, from a hundred to eighty, seventy, sixty and fifty, and the last of them runs twenty-six: [Lin'Uzner]{.name data-type="being/npc"}'s reign, the first of a length a man can live. The list enters the house as closed ([[lore-crossmound|the Crossing to the Mound]]).

## The House Now {#the-house-now}

The house holds no throne and has no head, and its one living bearer of the name, [[being-gariazletqut|Gari Azlet'Kutu]], holds no head's office. It kept its court in [[place-zugernome|Zu-Ger]], the Great Land, with the First House, and went downstream to the island of [[place-yathtelgu|Yath-Telgu]] to stand upon the mound at each crowning. When the court crossed to the mound in 1006 ST (1105 BF) the kings of the Great Land stayed where they lay.

## Holdings {#holdings}

The house's tombs lie in [[place-firsttablets|the Ground of the First Tablets]] above Zu-Ger, among the eleven Gar-Auu of the First House and Azlet'Kutu, in low flat-topped tombs of mudbrick and rough stone. The temple of [[affiliation-hezmuiri|Hezmu'iri]] at [[place-chegu|Chegu]] keeps watch over the ground.

## Quarrels and Debts {#present}

The house is owed a name. When the hill house closed, the priesthood of Uqa'a raised [Azlet'Ralu]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, and its first Gar-Au took the name Gez'el'Retha, the third to wear it: the first two belonged to the First House and to Azlet'Kutu, almost seven centuries before. A name from the oldest depth of the list on a throne the nomads had held says that the crown is the old crown again ([[lore-hillhouse|the Closing of the Hill House]]). Pilgrims come to the ground to be near the oldest kings, and foreign scholars come to count the First Tablets and are shown as much of the ground as the temple decides.
