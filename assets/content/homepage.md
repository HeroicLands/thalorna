Somewhere in the forests and mist-shrouded valleys of Thalorna, the Sinalë keep enclaves that appear on no human map, and the Khazári hold cliff-face cities cut into the mountains. Both peoples were here long before humanity. Above them, the empires, republics, tribes and faiths of the year 720 range from lush subtropical lands to vast deserts and icy wastes, and no two of them count the year the same way. Begin with [The World of Thalorna](place-worldthlrn/) for the shape of the world, then follow the continents and regions to the places and peoples within them.
