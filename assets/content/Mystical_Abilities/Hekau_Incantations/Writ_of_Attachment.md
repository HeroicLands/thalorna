The duty must be written out in full and without ambiguity, and this is where the working is
dangerous: the bound thing performs what the writ says, not what its author meant. The House keeps
a long instructional catalog of writs that did exactly as written – the guardian that admitted
the tomb's owner because the writ named his living self, the servant that carried water for eleven
years because no term was set.
