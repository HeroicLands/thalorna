The standard protection on restricted material, including the treatises at Per-Sefút and the
closed shelf of Per-Ren'sesh itself. Its weakness is structural and known: the naming is part of the
working, so a page that can be read by a named person can be read by anyone who obtains the name,
which is precisely the commodity this House exists to guard.
