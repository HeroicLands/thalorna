Named for its usual setting: the working sits inert and begins on the seventh day, by which time
the practitioner is elsewhere. In lawful hands it is a disposal method used at the paupers' fields
and after epidemics. In any other hands it is a weapon against granaries, and the empire has lost
harvests to it twice in recorded history, both times in seasons the king-lists describe with
unusual brevity.
