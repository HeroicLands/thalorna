Made for the archives, where a decaying scroll must be renewed without becoming a different
document, and it is what keeps the king-lists legible across three thousand years. That it also
produces an undetectable forgery is a fact the House acknowledges, regulates and has never solved;
the Corrector's office exists partly for this reason.
