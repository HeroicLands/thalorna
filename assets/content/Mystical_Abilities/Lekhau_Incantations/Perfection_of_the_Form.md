Not decoration and not improvement: the form is brought to the established standard, which in
Khelâthi doctrine is what makes it efficacious. A tomb painting so finished does not depict the
afterlife, it is the afterlife; a glyph so finished is live rather than bluff. This is the working
that makes the empire's art conservative, and the House regards innovation in form as a species of
sabotage.
