To cast Insight, the caster places hands upon a wounded creature and channels diagnostic [[affiliation-fisera|Fisera]] energy through the patient's body, gaining an immediate and comprehensive understanding of all physical traumas present—their severity, location, state of healing, and risk of complication. This grants the Healing talent bonus and provides a substantial bonus to all subsequent Physician tests to treat the injuries cataloged by the spell. The diagnostic impression is instantaneous and thorough, revealing information that might otherwise require hours of physical examination and probing.

At higher masteries the caster may share these diagnostic bonuses with another character through a shared language, effectively briefing a field surgeon on the patient's exact condition. The treatment bonuses increase further with expertise, and at the highest levels the spell may be cast upon a corpse to determine the precise cause of death—a capability that makes master Fisera practitioners invaluable to magistrates and inquisitors investigating suspicious fatalities. The spell's range expands with mastery, and its casting time is brief enough for battlefield use, though the caster must maintain touch with the patient throughout.
