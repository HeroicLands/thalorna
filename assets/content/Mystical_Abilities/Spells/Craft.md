The caster strikes the ground or a flat surface and a compact magical forge materializes – an anvil of gleaming metal, a trough of perpetually hot coals, a rack of essential smithing tools, all wrought from condensed arcane energy. The forge produces authentic heat suitable for working metal, and the tools handle with the balance and precision of master-crafted implements. Working at this enchanted station, even a moderately skilled metalworker produces results far exceeding their normal capability.

The magical forge compensates for deficiencies in mundane equipment and environment, providing the controlled heat, proper tool geometry, and stable working surface that quality metalwork demands. The enchantment cannot replace genuine skill, but it dramatically lowers the threshold of difficulty for complex work. Armor can be repaired in the field, weapons reforged from salvaged metal, and essential tools fabricated from raw stock. The station persists for several hours before the sustaining magic dissipates and the conjured equipment fades into nothing.
