The caster enfolds themselves or a willing target in a raging sheath of magical flame, transforming them into a walking conflagration. Fire streams from every surface of the body—hair becomes a mane of flame, clothing burns without being consumed, and the ground chars beneath each footstep. The heat radiating from the affected individual is intense enough to warp metal at close range and ignite anything flammable within arm's reach.

The inferno provides considerable protection—incoming projectiles are incinerated or deflected by the wall of heat, and melee attackers risk severe burns with every strike. The affected individual is immune to the flames but not to the smoke, heat exhaustion, or oxygen deprivation they produce, making sustained use in enclosed spaces dangerous. The spectacle of a figure wreathed in roaring flame is profoundly intimidating, and enemies often break and flee rather than engage an opponent who appears to embody fire itself.
