The caster touches the target and establishes a resonance with their deepest moral convictions—not imposing external judgment but amplifying the target's own innate sense of right and wrong until it becomes the dominant voice in their decision-making. Impulses rooted in selfishness, cruelty, or dishonesty are not suppressed so much as overwhelmed by the strengthened conscience, which makes their wrongness feel viscerally obvious and emotionally intolerable.

The effect is unique to each subject, as it works with their existing moral framework rather than imposing an external standard. A person whose deepest conviction is loyalty will find that conviction amplified; one whose core value is justice will feel that sense of justice become irresistible. The spell does not create virtue where none exists—a genuinely amoral individual might experience only mild discomfort—but for most people, whose better angels are merely drowned out by circumstance and temptation, the effect is transformative for its duration.
