The caster wraps a target or area in a cocoon of dense, moisture-laden air that devours sound. Voices drop to inaudible murmurs, footsteps vanish, and even the clang of steel is reduced to a dull, distant thud. The effect is not silence so much as smothering—sound waves are absorbed and scattered by the water-saturated air before they can propagate.

The spell is invaluable for covert operations, whispered councils that must not be overheard, and silencing a screaming patient during battlefield surgery. Those within the affected area can still hear each other, though everything sounds as if filtered through deep water—muffled and distorted. The boundary of the spell is sharply defined; a step outside it returns the world to full volume with startling abruptness.
