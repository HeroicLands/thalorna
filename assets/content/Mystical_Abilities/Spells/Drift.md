The target becomes buoyant beyond all natural expectation, rising gently in water until they float effortlessly at the surface regardless of what they carry or wear. Armored warriors bob like corks; heavy packs ride the surface as if weightless. The enchantment suffuses the target with a subtle lightness that water itself seems to recognize, cradling them against submersion.

Drift has saved countless lives on river crossings and sea voyages, and is one of the first spells taught to Hydälis apprentices serving aboard ships. The effect extends to anything the target holds or wears at the time of casting, though objects released after the spell takes hold sink normally. In still water, the target can propel themselves with gentle paddling; in rough seas, the spell merely prevents drowning rather than granting control.
