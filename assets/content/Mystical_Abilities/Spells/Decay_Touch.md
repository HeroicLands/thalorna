The caster curses a target with a wasting disease that gnaws at the victim's vitality from within, resisted only by a Spirit test whose success level must exceed the caster's – with ties broken in favor of the curse. Those who fail to resist accrue significant weakness fatigue for every victory step of the spell, their flesh growing pallid, their muscles trembling, and their joints aching as the magical disease takes hold. Every day throughout the spell's duration the victim must test Spirit again in an ongoing contest against the curse's progression: a Critical Failure accrues massive weakness fatigue and triggers a Shock Roll, a Failure adds further weakness, while a Success provides no additional effect and a Critical Success reduces the remaining duration by one day.

At the end of the spell's duration, any indefinite Shock State is lifted and the target receives an immediate Endurance Secondary Roll; each success reduces accumulated weakness fatigue, and the Endurance test is repeated daily until all weakness fatigue is eliminated. At higher masteries the initial fatigue inflicted in the opposed Spirit test increases, a Critical Failure inflicts even greater fatigue and triggers a more severe Shock Roll, and once the caster has perceived the target's True Form the line-of-sight requirement is waived. The curse persists for days proportional to the Spell Index, doubling with a Critical Success – a lingering, debilitating affliction that can reduce the strongest warrior to a bedridden wreck.
