The caster projects a tendril of draining energy into the target's psyche, and their will to act simply bleeds away. Motivation evaporates, replaced by a crushing apathy that makes even the simplest action feel pointless and exhausting. The target's thoughts slow to a crawl, their emotional responses flatten, and the driving force that normally propels conscious behavior gutters and dims like a candle in rain.

The effect mimics the deepest trough of emotional exhaustion, the state of mind that follows prolonged grief or unrelenting stress, compressed into moments rather than weeks. The target may understand intellectually that they should act—should fight, should flee, should resist—but the understanding carries no emotional weight, no urgency, no motivation to translate knowledge into action. Recovery is slow even after the spell ends, the drained reserves refilling gradually like a emptied well. Subjects describe the experience as profoundly disturbing, a glimpse into a state of being where nothing matters.
