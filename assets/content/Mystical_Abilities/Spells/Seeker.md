The caster shapes a bolt of flame and speaks the name or nature of their target, and the missile launches itself with purposeful intent. Unlike a normal projectile, the seeker veers and curves in pursuit, following its quarry around corners, through doorways, and over obstacles with single-minded determination. Its trail of sparks and flame marks its passage through the air, a line of fire that traces its hunting path.

The bolt's tracking ability is limited by the caster's original designation—it pursues the specific target identified at casting and will not transfer to a different quarry if the original escapes. Physical barriers stop it; it cannot pass through walls or closed doors, though it will seek any gap or opening. The bolt's speed is comparable to a running horse, meaning sufficiently fast targets can outpace it, though it never tires and maintains pursuit until its energy is spent or it reaches its mark.
