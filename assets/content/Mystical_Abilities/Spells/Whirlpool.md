The caster unleashes a churning vortex of raw, unstructured arcane energy at a targeted area, creating a howling maelstrom that tears at the fabric of any magic caught within its radius. Enchantments unravel, wards shatter, active spells detonate prematurely or collapse, and even latent magical properties are temporarily suppressed. The area becomes a zone of magical chaos where spellcasting is unreliable at best and catastrophically unpredictable at worst.

The vortex is indiscriminate – it affects allied magic as readily as hostile, making it a weapon of desperation or deliberate scorched-earth tactics. Practitioners caught within the maelstrom find their own spells twisting out of control, their carefully maintained enchantments buckling under the stress. The area remains magically unstable for some time after the vortex dissipates, a lingering turbulence that makes subsequent spellcasting in the zone difficult and dangerous.
