The caster draws upon the deepest reserves of Physéra power and merges their own body – along with up to half their Spirit Mastery Level in pounds of worn or carried gear – directly into stone, earth, or wood, passing through solid matter as though it were dense fog. The caster retains full solidity while melded, remaining subject to physical injuries and magical effects, but movement speed varies dramatically by material: earth permits a full Move action, while stone and wood allow only half speed with Difficult movement conditions. Forged metal, enchanted objects, and living material are completely impervious to the melding effect and block passage absolutely.

The danger is considerable: if the spell's duration expires while the caster is still embedded in solid material, the consequences are immediately lethal – death in stone or wood, and suffocation in earth. At higher masteries the spell can carry additional creatures who maintain skin contact with the caster, moving at the same restricted rates, though each additional passenger reduces the spell's remaining duration. The mage's own movement rates improve with expertise, and at the highest level the spell can be laid upon another person through touch. The spell requires three rounds to cast on self, with a duration of rounds proportional to the Spell Index that doubles with a Critical Success – demanding precise timing and confident navigation through the material world's hidden interior.
