The caster reaches into the target's mind and finds the places where fear lives—the primal anxieties, the childhood terrors, the carefully suppressed phobias—and drags them screaming to the surface. The target is suddenly overwhelmed by an irrational, consuming fear that may or may not relate to anything actually present in their environment. Rational thought drowns beneath the flood of primal terror, and the target acts on pure survival instinct: freezing in place, fleeing blindly, or lashing out at whatever seems closest to the source of their panic.

The specific manifestation of the fear is unique to each target, drawn from their own psychological landscape rather than imposed by the caster. One subject might suddenly perceive the shadows around them as alive and closing in, while another might become convinced they are falling from a great height. The terror feels absolutely real to the target, indistinguishable from genuine mortal danger, and its effects linger for minutes even after the spell's active influence has faded, leaving the subject shaken and emotionally raw.
