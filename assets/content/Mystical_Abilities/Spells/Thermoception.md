The caster's vision shifts into a strange new register, and the world is painted in gradients of thermal energy rather than reflected light. Living creatures glow with the bright warmth of their body heat, standing out vividly against the cooler background of stone, earth, and vegetation. Recently occupied spaces retain fading heat signatures—a warm footprint on cold flagstone, the lingering impression of a hand on a door handle, the heated air left behind by a passing breath.

The thermal vision penetrates total darkness, smoke, fog, and thin barriers with equal ease, as heat radiates through materials that block light entirely. Hidden creatures are revealed by their body warmth, and concealed fires or recently forged metalwork glow like beacons. The spell cannot see through thick stone or metal, and it provides no information about color, text, or surface detail—the world appears only as patterns of warmth and cold, requiring the caster to interpret what they perceive through experience and context.
