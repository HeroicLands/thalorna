The caster holds up a hand and rotates it slowly; the air before them fractures into a prismatic array, splitting ambient light into a dazzling fan of spectral colors that sweeps across the target area like a lighthouse beam. Those caught in the kaleidoscopic wash find their depth perception shattered—distances seem to compress and stretch, stationary objects appear to wobble, and the ground underfoot seems to tilt at impossible angles. The effect is not painful but profoundly disorienting, reducing coordinated action to a lurching, overcorrecting stumble.

The prismatic display is beautiful in an almost cruel way: victims are often momentarily entranced by the cascading rainbows before the vertigo strikes. The caster can hold the effect steady, bathing a fixed area in confusion, or sweep it across a broader front to disrupt an advancing line. In bright sunlight the spell is particularly vivid, while in darkness it creates its own sourceless illumination, painting the battlefield in lurid, shifting hues. The effect fades quickly once the caster's hand drops, but the lingering after-images can hamper vision for several seconds longer.
