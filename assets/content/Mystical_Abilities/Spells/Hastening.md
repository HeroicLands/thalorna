The caster channels the explosive, consuming energy of fire into the target's body, and their metabolism surges. Heart rate accelerates, reflexes sharpen, and movements that normally take a full second compress into a fraction of that time. The target moves with a fluid, almost blurred quickness that makes them difficult to track and harder still to hit, their actions unfolding with the rapid, flickering quality of a flame dancing in wind.

The acceleration affects both body and perception—the hastened individual processes information faster, reacts to threats before others have registered them, and can perform complex physical sequences with extraordinary speed. The cost is proportional: the body burns through its reserves at the same accelerated rate, and when the spell fades, the subject crashes into profound fatigue as though they had sprinted for hours. Extended or repeated use can cause dangerous exhaustion, muscle damage, and in extreme cases, cardiac strain.
