The caster touches a creature and channels a wave of soporific Physéra energy that plunges the subject into a deep, restorative slumber. An unwilling target may resist with a Spirit test whose success level must exceed the caster's; on a tie the creature is merely stunned into drowsiness for a number of rounds proportional to the Spell Index, with no standard recovery roll to shake off the grogginess. A subject who succumbs to the full deep sleep can only be roused by someone spending one minute and succeeding on a Physician test with a substantial bonus; otherwise the creature sleeps for the entire duration, awakening naturally at the end.

Upon waking from the full deep sleep, the subject receives a Healing or Course Roll for each physical trauma, with a bonus to the check – though each trauma may only receive one such roll from this spell, and these rolls do not stack with similar single-roll effects from other arcane healing sources. All windedness fatigue is removed and weariness is reduced as though the target had just completed a full eight hours of natural rest. At higher masteries the spell's range extends, a Critical Success amplifies the healing bonus for all affected traumas, and with the longest casting time a willing target receives two Healing or Course Rolls per trauma with an extended duration, making Rest the Physéra tradition's premier recovery spell for extended convalescence.
