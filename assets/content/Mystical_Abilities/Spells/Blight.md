The caster reaches out and channels corrupting Physéra energy into a single injury that already possesses the latent potential for infection—specifically, one whose Healing Rate places it in the vulnerable range. The spell forces immediate infection, bypassing the normal probability of contamination and ensuring that the wound begins to fester. The infected wound may be treated normally through mundane medicine, may run its natural disease course, or will clear if the spell itself is dispelled, giving healers multiple avenues of response.

At higher masteries the range extends to line of sight, and at the highest level the spell can force infection even in wounds that would not normally be susceptible—creating contamination with a significant Healing Rate in any injury location, even a previously clean one. The spell is cast with a single round's effort and its effect is indefinite, persisting until the infection is resolved by one means or another. Blight is considered one of the most morally troubling applications of earth magic, weaponizing the body's vulnerability to disease, and its use is condemned by most healing traditions even as battlefield pragmatists acknowledge its devastating tactical value against wounded enemies.
