The caster fixes their gaze upon a target and speaks a word of diminishment, unleashing an insidious curse that settles over the victim like an invisible weight. The bane erodes the target's natural resilience to magical effects, thinning the innate protective barriers that all living things possess against arcane manipulation. Spells that would normally glance off the target's defenses now bite deeper, and effects that would fade quickly instead linger.

The curse is subtle in its operation—the target may not immediately realize they have been baned, attributing their sudden vulnerability to fatigue or ill fortune. Only when spells begin striking with unusual efficacy does the truth become apparent. The bane can be detected and removed by skilled practitioners, but until then it remains a persistent handicap. Some casters layer Bane with offensive follow-up spells, using it as the opening move in a calculated magical assault.
