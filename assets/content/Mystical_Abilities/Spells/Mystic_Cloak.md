The caster weaves a dense cocoon of psychic interference around the target, creating a barrier that is opaque to all forms of mental and spiritual perception. Scrying spells slide off its surface like water off oiled leather. Empathic probes encounter only a blank void. Even the general sense of presence that psychically sensitive individuals develop—the ability to feel when someone is nearby—is completely occluded. To spiritual perception, the cloaked individual simply does not exist.

The cloak extends to everything the target carries and to any magical effects currently upon them, rendering their enchanted items and active spells equally invisible to arcane scrutiny. The protection is passive once established, requiring no concentration to maintain, though the caster must periodically renew the weaving as it slowly unravels under the constant pressure of ambient psychic currents. The cloak's primary limitation is its totality—it blocks all psychic interaction, meaning the cloaked individual cannot receive mental messages, benefit from empathic healing, or participate in bonded communications while the protection is active.
