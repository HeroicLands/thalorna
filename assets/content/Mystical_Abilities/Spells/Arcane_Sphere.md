The caster speaks a word of absolute containment and a sphere of crystalline arcane force snaps into existence around the target, enclosing them in a perfect bubble of shimmering energy. The sphere is extraordinarily resilient, absorbing physical impacts, deflecting projectiles, and scattering hostile magic across its curved surface. It moves with the enclosed subject, maintaining its protective integrity regardless of terrain or circumstance.

Nothing passes through the sphere in either direction—a fact that provides absolute protection but also prevents the enclosed subject from attacking, casting spells outward, or even communicating verbally with those outside. Air within the sphere is finite, placing a natural limit on the duration of enclosure. The sphere can be dismissed by the caster at will, or it can be shattered from outside by sufficient concentrated force, though the amount of energy required is formidable. Some practitioners use the sphere offensively, trapping enemies within while allies deal with other threats.
