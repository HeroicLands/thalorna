The caster carefully traces the history of a magical effect backward through time, identifying the precise sequence of forces and intentions that brought it into being, then systematically reverses that sequence. The targeted magic unravels in the exact opposite order of its creation, each layer peeling away cleanly until nothing remains. Curses dissolve, compulsions release their grip, transformed objects revert to their original state, and magical damage repairs itself as the injurious force is withdrawn.

This is far more sophisticated than simple dispelling—Undo doesn't merely break magic, it reverses it, restoring the pre-existing state as though the spell had never been cast. This makes it uniquely effective against transformative magic, curses, and other effects where simple dispelling would remove the magic but leave the damage behind. The difficulty scales sharply with the age and complexity of the effect; unraveling a curse that has been in place for years requires far more skill and energy than reversing a spell cast moments ago.
