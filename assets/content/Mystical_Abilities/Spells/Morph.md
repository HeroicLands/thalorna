The caster reaches out and touches a single plant, channeling Physéra energy to reshape its living structure into a particular configuration—bending a young oak into an archway, coiling vines into a rope-like braid, or with multiple castings shaping several bushes into a tight, impenetrable hedgerow. The transformation respects the plant's living biology; it doesn't cut or kill the vegetation but rather redirects its growth pattern to conform to the caster's vision. The shaped plant retains its new form permanently, continuing to grow in its altered configuration as though it had always been that way.

At higher masteries the spell can affect multiple plants simultaneously with the duration split evenly among them, the range extends significantly, and a Critical Success on the spellfire roll grants the caster the ability to modify the shape further at any point during the duration rather than committing to a single configuration at casting. The spell requires just one round and a touch, making it swift enough for impromptu field use—blocking a forest trail with a wall of woven branches, creating a living shelter from interlocked saplings, or fashioning crude tools from shaped wood. Morph is the signature utility spell of rural Physéra practitioners and the foundation of the living-architecture tradition.
