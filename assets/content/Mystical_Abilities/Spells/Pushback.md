The caster extends their palm and releases a surge of concussive force that slams outward in a broad wave, shoving everything in its path away from the caster with the irresistible momentum of a swinging battering ram. Opponents stagger backward, lose their footing, and are driven away from the caster's position. Loose objects fly outward. Projectiles in mid-flight are deflected or reversed. The force is invisible but unmistakable in its effect.

The repulsion is strongest at close range, diminishing with distance to a firm push that merely unbalances rather than throws. Heavily armored or exceptionally strong targets resist the effect more successfully, bracing against the force through sheer mass, while light objects and unprepared individuals can be hurled several paces. The spell is most effective as a defensive measure, creating breathing room in close combat, clearing a choke point, or deflecting an incoming volley of arrows. The force carries no heat or magical residue, leaving pushed objects and creatures physically unharmed.
