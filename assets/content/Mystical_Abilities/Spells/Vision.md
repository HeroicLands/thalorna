The caster's eyes take on a steely, metallic sheen and their vision transforms, allowing them to see through solid materials as though they were translucent glass. Stone walls become ghostly outlines through which the rooms beyond are visible. Wooden doors reveal the people standing behind them. The earth itself becomes a window into the chambers, passages, and voids that lie beneath the surface.

The penetrative sight has a limited depth that increases with the caster's mastery—a novice might see through a single wall, while a master can peer through several yards of solid stone. The vision reveals physical objects in their true forms but does not detect magical concealment or invisible creatures, which appear as the empty spaces they occupy. The caster can still see normally while the spell is active, the penetrative sight layering over ordinary vision like a ghostly overlay that can be disorienting until the practitioner learns to manage the dual perspective.
