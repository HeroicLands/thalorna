The caster creates a ring of fire that encircles and encloses a designated space, rising to waist height or higher depending on the caster's intent. Anything that attempts to cross the boundary – in either direction – meets a concentrated blast of flame that is far more intense than the visible fire suggests. The envelope burns with particular ferocity along its boundary, while the interior remains hot but habitable.

The enclosure serves as both prison and sanctuary depending on one's perspective. An enemy trapped within the envelope is contained, their escape routes cut off by walls of flame. A group sheltering inside is protected from assault, as attackers must brave the fire to reach them. The spell can be shaped to fit the available space – circular in open ground, rectangular to fill a room, or even irregular to follow the contours of natural terrain. The fire does not spread beyond its defined boundary, burning with precise magical discipline.
