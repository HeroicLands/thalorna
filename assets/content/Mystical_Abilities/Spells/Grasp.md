The caster gestures forcefully and a massive hand of gleaming metal assembles itself from ambient minerals and traces of metal in the surrounding environment. The construct is enormous – large enough to grip a person around the torso – and moves with surprising dexterity under the caster's mental direction. Its fingers can close with crushing force, pry open barred gates, or lift loads that would require a team of laborers and a system of pulleys.

The hand operates at the caster's direction with a slight delay, responding to mental commands rather than matching the caster's own hand movements. It can grasp, lift, push, and strike, but fine manipulation – picking a lock, threading a needle – is beyond its clumsy digits. The construct is physically tough, shrugging off sword blows and resisting fire, but it can be shattered by concentrated magical force or sustained physical assault. When the spell ends or the hand is destroyed, it collapses into a pile of metal fragments and mineral dust.
