The caster fortifies the boundaries of their own mind with layered walls of arcane reinforcement, creating a mental fortress that resists intrusion, manipulation, and magical compulsion. External attempts to read the caster's thoughts encounter smooth, impenetrable surfaces. Spells designed to confuse, terrify, or control find no purchase against the hardened will. The caster's thoughts remain their own, clear and undistorted even amid psychic assault.

The binding is felt as a deep calm certainty, an unshakeable sense of self that renders the caster immune to the doubt and hesitation that magical manipulation exploits. Extended use can make the caster appear unnervingly composed under circumstances that should produce fear or distress, a behavioral tell that perceptive opponents may recognize. The protection can be extended to touch willing allies, though maintaining multiple bindings simultaneously divides the caster's concentration and weakens each individual shield.
