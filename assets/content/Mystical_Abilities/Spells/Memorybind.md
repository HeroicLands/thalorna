The caster places their hands on the subject's temples and reaches gently into the deep architecture of their memory, following the tangled threads of association that connect experience to recollection. Faded memories brighten and sharpen, returning to the vivid clarity they possessed when first formed. Details that had been lost to time—faces, words, scents, the precise sequence of events—resurface with startling fidelity, as though the subject were experiencing the original moment anew.

The spell cannot create memories that never existed, nor can it restore memories that were never formed—moments lost to unconsciousness, extreme intoxication, or severe trauma may be genuinely absent rather than merely inaccessible. What it can do is recover memories that exist but cannot be voluntarily recalled, including those suppressed by psychological defense mechanisms. This last application requires particular delicacy, as forcibly surfacing repressed memories can cause significant emotional distress if the subject is not prepared for what they will remember.
