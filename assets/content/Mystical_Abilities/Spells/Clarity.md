The caster turns their sharpened perception upon a person, object, or situation, and understanding arrives not through analysis but through a sudden, intuitive flash of insight. Hidden connections become obvious. Concealed motivations reveal themselves. The significance of an overlooked detail suddenly snaps into focus with the clarity of a puzzle piece finding its place. The caster perceives not just what is present but what it means.

The insight is genuine but limited in scope—it illuminates a single aspect of the subject rather than providing comprehensive understanding. A single casting might reveal that a merchant's smile conceals desperation, that a seemingly ordinary ring carries emotional significance for its wearer, or that the arrangement of guards around a building has a specific vulnerability. The spell cannot predict the future or reveal information the caster has no basis for inferring; it clarifies what is already present but not yet understood.
