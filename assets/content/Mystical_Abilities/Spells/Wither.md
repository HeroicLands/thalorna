The caster chooses one of two modes at the moment of spellfire, each channeling the entropic aspect of Physéra to accelerate natural deterioration. When directed at a living creature, the spell doubles all Healing Periods for physical trauma for one round, functioning as a virulent disease in its own right with its own Healing Rate and a five-day period. Targets resist by testing Spirit at a success level higher than the caster's; those who fail find their wounds stubbornly refusing to close, infections blooming with alarming speed, and even minor scratches festering as though left untreated for days.

When directed at plant life over a ten-minute casting, the spell blights the soil itself: the Land Quality for the next yield of affected acreage is reduced, diminishing harvests and weakening vegetation across the targeted area. At higher masteries the creature-affecting version extends its range and can afflict additional targets simultaneously, while the agricultural version can devastate dramatically larger tracts of farmland and reduce plant yields even further. The spell embodies the dark mirror of Physéra's nurturing power—a reminder that the same force which quickens growth can just as readily hasten rot, making it feared by healers and farmers alike.
