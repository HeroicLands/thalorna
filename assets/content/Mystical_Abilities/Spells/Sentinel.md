The caster designates a living plant or creature as a watchpost and imbues it with a passive Physéra awareness that triggers a telepathic alert when any living, incarnate entity intrudes within the plant's natural perceptive range—roughly five feet for most vegetation. The guardian cannot detect spirits or entities existing solely in the Spirit World; only beings with physical, incarnate presence register to the spell's senses. The caster may instruct the sentinel to ignore certain categories of entity, such as insects or small animals, filtering out false alarms that would otherwise render the ward useless in a forest setting.

The caster must remain within a range proportional to their Spell Index to receive the telepathic notification, which conveys only that an intruder has been detected—not its precise identity or description. At higher masteries the alert provides general information about the intruder's nature (such as 'large humanoid' or 'huge reptile'), then specific details according to the guardian's own senses (appearance, scent, sound), and at the highest level the caster may limit the alarm to a specific class of entity. The spell requires two rounds to cast with a touch range, and the sentinel remains vigilant for minutes proportional to the caster's Spell Index, making it ideal for perimeter security during encampments or ambush preparation.
