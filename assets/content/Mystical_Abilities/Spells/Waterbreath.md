The caster transforms the target's ability to draw sustenance from the air, allowing them to extract breathable air directly from water as it passes over their skin. A faint shimmer appears across the target's face and throat as the enchantment takes hold, and they can submerge fully without discomfort, breathing as naturally underwater as they do on land.

The spell does not alter the target's body visibly – no gills sprout, no webbing appears – but rather creates an invisible magical membrane that separates oxygen from water on contact. The effect is deeply disorienting for first-time recipients, as the instinct to hold one's breath underwater is powerful, and learning to relax and inhale while submerged requires an act of trust. The spell does nothing to protect against cold, pressure, or underwater currents, and a waterbreathing warrior still fights at a disadvantage in the heavy, resistant medium.
