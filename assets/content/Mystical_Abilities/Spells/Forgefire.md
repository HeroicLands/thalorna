The caster raises a clenched fist overhead and a block of solid metal materializes high above the target – a massive, gleaming anvil of condensed steel that hangs motionless for a heartbeat before plummeting with gravity-assisted, magically accelerated force. The impact is catastrophic, combining the sheer weight of hundreds of pounds of solid metal with the velocity of a falling boulder. The ground craters, structures collapse, and anything directly beneath the anvil is simply obliterated.

The anvil is visible during its brief formation and descent, providing alert targets a narrow opportunity to move – though the magical acceleration makes the fall far faster than natural gravity would produce. The conjured metal persists after impact, leaving a genuine steel mass embedded in the crater it created. This makes the spell doubly useful: after the initial devastating strike, the remaining metal can be recovered and worked by conventional or magical means, providing a substantial quantity of quality steel.
