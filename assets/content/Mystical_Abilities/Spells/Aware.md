The caster touches the target's temples and channels a surge of mental energy into their perceptive faculties. The world snaps into sharper focus—colors intensify, sounds separate into distinct layers, and the skin becomes sensitive enough to feel the displacement of air from a passing insect. Tastes and scents become almost overwhelming in their richness, revealing details that normal perception simply cannot detect.

The enhancement is comprehensive and immediate, but the flood of amplified sensory data can be disorienting to the unprepared. Novice subjects frequently flinch at sounds that would normally pass unnoticed, or find themselves unable to concentrate as every flicker of movement in their peripheral vision demands attention. Experienced subjects learn to selectively focus their enhanced perception, using it to read lips at great distance, detect the faint scent of poison in food, or feel the subtle vibrations of approaching footsteps through a stone floor.
