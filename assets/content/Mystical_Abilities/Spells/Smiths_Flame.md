The caster conjures a zone of intense, precisely controlled heat centered on a work surface – a flat rock, an existing anvil, or even bare ground. Within this zone, the temperature reaches forge-ready intensity, sufficient to soften iron, melt copper, and work steel to a workable state. The heat is sharply bounded: the air a handspan outside the zone is merely warm, while inside, metal glows cherry-red and can be shaped with proper tools.

The magical furnace burns without fuel, produces no smoke, and can be maintained indefinitely as long as the caster sustains their concentration. The temperature is adjustable with precision no physical furnace can match, allowing the caster to work different metals at their optimal temperatures without the trial-and-error of managing real coals. This controlled environment makes Smith's Flame the ideal companion to Craft and Steelforge, together forming a portable smithy capable of producing work equal to a permanent workshop.
