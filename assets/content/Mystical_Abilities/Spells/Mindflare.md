The caster projects a concentrated burst of mental energy directly into the target's consciousness – not a message but a raw pulse of psychic force that disrupts whatever thoughts were currently in progress. The target experiences a blinding flash of white noise behind their eyes, a moment of total cognitive disruption during which they cannot think, speak, or act purposefully. The sensation is intensely unpleasant, like a thunderclap inside the skull.

The disruption is brief – a few seconds at most – but it is completely incapacitating for its duration, making it an effective opening move in both physical and magical combat. Unlike a physical blow, mindflare leaves no visible mark and can be delivered from a distance without warning. The target recovers quickly but is often left with a lingering headache and a sense of violated privacy. Repeated applications in quick succession can overwhelm the target's mental defenses, extending the period of disruption.
