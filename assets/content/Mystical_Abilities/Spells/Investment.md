Over the course of an extended ritual, the caster channels a substantial quantity of arcane energy into a prepared object or location, creating a deep reservoir of stored power far exceeding what a simple Infuse can achieve. The investment binds the energy to the vessel at a fundamental level, creating a semi-permanent enchantment that persists for months or even years. The stored power can be drawn upon gradually, released in a single burst, or set to activate under specified conditions.

The process is exhausting and cannot be rushed – the caster must carefully layer energy into the vessel over hours, allowing each stratum to settle and bond before adding the next. Attempting to force too much power too quickly risks destabilizing the entire investment, potentially destroying both the vessel and the surrounding area. The resulting artifacts are among the most valuable objects in the magical world, representing not just power but the irreplaceable time and effort of their creator.
