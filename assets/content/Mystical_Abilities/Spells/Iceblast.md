The caster compresses moisture into a dense sphere of ice and hurls it at devastating speed toward a target. The projectile forms in the caster's hand in a heartbeat—water freezing in visible spirals around a crystallizing core—and launches with a crack of displaced air. On impact, the sphere detonates into a spray of razor-sharp ice shards that shred flesh and embed in wood and leather.

The blast is one of the Hydälis school's primary offensive spells, favored for its combination of blunt impact and shrapnel damage. The initial sphere hits with the force of a hurled stone, but the fragmentation on impact is what makes it truly dangerous, filling a small area with lacerating splinters of ice. In cold environments, the spell is notably more powerful, producing larger and denser projectiles; in extreme heat, the ice may partially melt in flight, reducing its effectiveness.
