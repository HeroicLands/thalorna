The caster focuses concentrated force upon a single point within a solid object – a wall, a door, a suit of armor, a weapon – and releases it as a violent expansion that shatters the material from within. Cracks radiate outward from the focal point, and the object fragments explosively, hurling sharp debris in all directions. The destruction is dramatic and instantaneous, reducing a wooden door to splinters, a stone wall to rubble, or a glass window to a shower of glittering shards.

The spell targets structural weaknesses, meaning that objects with existing flaws shatter more completely than those of uniform quality. Magically reinforced materials resist the effect proportionally to their enchantment. The debris from the shattered object is propelled with genuine physical force, making proximity dangerous – bystanders can be struck by fragments of stone, wood, or metal. Skilled practitioners learn to predict and control the fragmentation pattern, using Smash not just to destroy but to weaponize the debris itself.
