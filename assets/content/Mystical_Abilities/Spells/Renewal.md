The caster lays hands upon a patient who has suffered the traumatic loss of a limb within the last several days proportional to their Spell Index, provided the severed member is at least ninety percent intact and can be physically reattached to the stump. This grants the Healing talent bonus. With successful spellfire the arm or leg bonds to the body and begins recovering as a Grievous-5E injury with a Healing Rate of 5 – a serious wound, but one with no chance of infection. The process is physiologically gruelling: at the moment of casting the patient must make an Endurance test with a bonus from the spell, and the result determines the Shock State they must endure throughout the long recovery.

Permanent impairment remains possible despite the magical reattachment – the steps of the normal Healing Sequence determine the final outcome, and incomplete recovery may leave the limb weakened or partially functional. Creatures may only be under the effect of one such spell at a time. At higher masteries the spell can reconnect a severed leg, then an ear, eye, or nose, and at the highest level it achieves true regeneration – regrowing the limb even without the severed part, though the injury recovers at a lower Healing Rate and the accompanying Endurance test suffers a significant penalty. The casting requires one hour at touch range, and the healing effect is instantaneous once established, following the normal recovery timeline from that point forward.
