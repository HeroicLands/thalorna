The caster clenches their fist and a massive construct of compressed metal forms above the target—a colossal fist, a falling hammer, a descending pillar of solid steel—and drops with devastating, earth-shaking force. The impact is catastrophic, cratering the ground, pulverizing stone, and crushing anything beneath it with the weight and momentum of several tons of solid metal. The concussive shockwave alone is sufficient to knock nearby individuals off their feet.

The construct forms and falls in seconds, giving alert targets a narrow window to dodge—the gathering of metal above them produces an unmistakable grinding sound and casts a sudden shadow. The spell requires a substantial quantity of ambient metal in the environment, drawing on mineral deposits in the earth, metallic dust in the air, or nearby man-made metal objects. In areas devoid of metal, the construct forms slowly and with diminished mass. The crushed remains of the construct persist as a physical mass of compressed metal.
