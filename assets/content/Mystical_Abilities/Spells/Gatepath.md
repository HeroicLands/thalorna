The caster plants both feet and thrusts arms skyward, summoning a column of uprushing wind powerful enough to lift armored warriors off the ground and carry them in a controlled arc over walls, chasms, or tangled terrain before setting them down gently on the far side. The wind-path is visible as a faint shimmering corridor, and those riding it feel a steady pressure beneath their feet as though standing on an invisible, moving floor. The sensation is eerie but stable, provided the caster's concentration holds.

The spell can lift multiple individuals in quick succession if they enter the wind-path promptly, though each additional passenger taxes the caster's reserves. Heavier loads – a mounted knight, a laden cart – require proportionally greater effort and may slow the transit to a crawl. If concentration breaks mid-flight, passengers descend in a controlled but rapid glide rather than plummeting outright, a safety mechanism woven into the spell's fundamental architecture. Gatepath cannot operate in a dead calm created by opposing Silence or Containment effects.
