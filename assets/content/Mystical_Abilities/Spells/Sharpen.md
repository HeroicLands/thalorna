The caster turns their focus inward, honing the flow of arcane energy through their being like a smith putting an edge on a blade. The scattered, imprecise currents of magical power that normally characterize spellcasting tighten into clean, efficient channels, and the caster's control over their art sharpens dramatically. Spells cast under this enhanced focus are more precise, more potent, and less wasteful of the caster's reserves.

The effect is temporary but profound—a practitioner under Sharpen's influence produces noticeably crisper magical constructs, sharper boundaries on their wards, and more accurate targeting on their offensive workings. The spell is particularly valued before critical castings where failure is unacceptable: delicate enchantments, surgical dispellings, or the precise calibration of a ritual's parameters. The heightened state is taxing, however, and most casters can sustain it only for a limited period.
