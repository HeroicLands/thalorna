Amplification lends force to another mystical working, carrying it beyond what its own practitioner could reach. The talent adds no working of its own; it strengthens one already under way. It is one of the arcane talents, the inborn gifts that the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] looks for in the children it registers.
