Telekinesis moves an object at a distance, within a weight the practitioner could lift by hand. It extends the practitioner's reach, not their strength. It is one of the arcane talents, the inborn gifts that the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] looks for in the children it registers.
