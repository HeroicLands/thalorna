Sensitivity perceives the feeling and intent of those nearby, without their words. The practitioner reads the people themselves rather than what they say. It is one of the arcane talents, the inborn gifts that the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] looks for in the children it registers.
