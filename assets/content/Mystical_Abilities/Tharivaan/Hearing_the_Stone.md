Hearing the Stone is the answering of [[affiliation-tharivaan#the-answering|Thári'vaan]]: a Kaliharan whom one of the works of [[lore-theithari|The Ithari]] answers knows the state of that work at any distance, whether it is working, working badly, stopped or dead. It is knowledge and nothing else. It tells nothing of any other work, does nothing to the work it reports on, and gives no help in tending, which any trained custodian performs whether a work answers that custodian or not.

Nobody chooses it or earns it. Which work answers whom follows no rule anyone has found, neither blood, office nor rank, and training in [[skill-tharivaan|Ritual: Thári'vaan]] neither brings it nor sharpens it. A work working badly may begin to answer someone new; a dead work answers nobody, and the person it answered feels it go.
