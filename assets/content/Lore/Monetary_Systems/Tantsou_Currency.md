## Overview

A merchant who crosses into the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] with a purse of Vylarian elthezoi is told at the frontier way-station what they are worth, and the number is not negotiable. Commerce in Tantsou runs on the same instrument as everything else in the empire, the register: a Shuonong house's standing with the bureaus is its credit, a debt is an entry a magistrate's clerk can read, and the classification tablet a trader carries is what a bargain is made against. The empire's money is a ledger before it is a coin, and the system stands apart from the coin-and-script of [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vylaria]], the weight-pieces of [[lore-aukhlthcrncy|Au'Khelathu]], the banking of [[lore-vdyrnbnkng|Vedyara]] and the kin-credit of the tribal regions.

## Ledger-Credit

The [[lore-castelaws|Caste Laws]] decide who may hold what. A [[lore-shuonong|Shuonong]] merchant may accumulate wealth, lend at interest under supervision and enter witnessed contracts; a [[lore-nouwen|Nouwén]] farmer renders tribute in produce and labor and holds no obligation a bureau would record; a [[lore-wanduo|Wánduo]] owns nothing, so nothing is owed to him. Between the houses that may contract, trade runs on recorded obligation. A significant sale is witnessed and entered, the entry is what a creditor produces before a magistrate, and bankruptcy voids nothing: the debtor's holdings are seized and distributed, and the entries stand against his house until they are cleared. Coin changes hands in the markets for the day's rice and the day's cloth, and the sums that matter move as credit between registered houses, under the eye of the Wohou administrators who audit the merchant caste.

Standing is part of the price. A house whose filings are in order and whose taxes are paid trades on better terms than one under inspection, so the bureaus' opinion of a merchant is a thing he can spend, and the [[affiliation-haluozhao|Black Lotus Society]] trades in exactly that: forged tablets, altered entries and bought omens, which is why control of the ledgers is a form of power inside the empire and not merely a convenience of trade.

## The Frontier Exchange

Vylarian, Khelathi and Vedyaran coin is exchangeable at the empire's frontier trading posts and in the walled merchant quarters of the port cities, and nowhere else. The rate is set by the bureau that administers the post, posted for the season, and applied to every trader alike; no moneylender in Tantsou negotiates it, and a trader who tries is told that the classification of foreign silver is not his to argue. Foreign coin bought at the frontier is held by the bureau against the trader's credit for the length of his classification, and what he carries out is settled when his tablet is surrendered at the gate. No coin of the empire's own striking is seen abroad, and the empire's trade with the west is reckoned in goods, in frontier credit and in the silver the west brings east.
