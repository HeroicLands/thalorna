## Overview

The Vedyaran banking system is the money and credit the [[affiliation-mrchntclctvvdyr|Merchant Collective]] runs across the five seats of the Assembly of the Compact. It stands apart from the Vylarian [[affiliation-aerarimmpr|The Aerarium Imperii]] and the Ta'Kheperan [[affiliation-perhati|Pér-Háti]] alike. The Collective has declined every overture from either of them for formal correspondent recognition, for generations, and keeps its own system self-contained.

- The Merchant Collective operates as the principal banking institution of Vedyaran commerce, in addition to its role as the federation of mercantile _kulas_ (the great hereditary trading families). Its **letter of credit** sits above the coined system: a house that trusts another house's paper moves value between janapadas and city-states without moving coin at all.
- The five seats of the Assembly of the Compact—[[affiliation-chandrapur|Chandrapur]], [[affiliation-vyalendra2|Vyālendra]], [[affiliation-vindhyalay|Vindhyālaya]] through its capital, [[affiliation-rajaprjnpd|Rājapur]] and [[affiliation-dhnrktjnpd|Dhanurkota]]—share the system; the Collective's standing as a supra-realm institution gives the banking infrastructure continent-internal scope. [[affiliation-suvrgrjnpd|Suvarnagiri]] keeps its own weighing and its own treasury and holds no seat.
- **Kingdoms and city-states strike coin; the janapadas do not.** A janapada's commerce runs on whichever city's or kingdom's coin its traders carry, cleared through the Collective rather than minted locally.

## The Three Coins

| Coin                              | Metal  | Struck by                                                                            | Common use                                                 | Conversion |
| --------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| **[[miscgear-suvarna\|Suvarna]]** | Gold   | [[affiliation-chandrapur\|Chandrapur]], from Suvarnagiri gold bought at the Weighing | Land, dowries, tribute, the largest commercial settlements | 16 candra  |
| **[[miscgear-candra\|Candra]]**   | Silver | The Moon House, at Chandrapur                                                        | Ordinary formal commerce—contracts, tolls, rents           | 8 tāmra    |
| **[[miscgear-tamra\|Tāmra]]**     | Copper | Every kingdom and city-state, independently                                          | Everyday trade—bread, wages, market change                 | 1 tāmra    |

A suvarna is therefore worth 128 tāmra. The tāmra's mint varies by court and its weight only approximately with it; ordinary trade does not distinguish a Chandrapur tāmra from a Dhanurkota one, and only a large settlement is weighed rather than counted.

## Exchange With What the Coin Actually Meets

Vedyara borders no Vylarian territory. Vylarian coin reaches it anyway, by two routes and at two removes: overland out of the [[affiliation-sultntmrdd|Sultanate of Amradad]]'s cities, where it is the principal medium and where the march caravans pick it up, and third-hand through Kheperi intermediaries at the sea. It arrives as metal rather than as money, at a discount nobody bothers to publish, and no Vedyaran exchange quotes it. The coin a Vedyaran trader actually exchanges is the coin standing at the three edges of the region—the northern passes, the southern sea, and the march road round the wall's end:

- **At the passes.** [[affiliation-khzrncnfdrtn|Khazryn]] runs on barter and tribal credit in its tribal interior and strikes no coin of its own. Its urbanized trading-cities have begun keeping moneylenders "along Vedyaran lines." Vedyaran coin circulates there by the metal in it and at no fixed rate, so a tāmra or a candra is weighed and not counted the far side of [[place-suryadvara|Sūryadvāra]].
- **At the sea.** [[affiliation-empirtkhpr|Ta'Kheperu]] attests metal by weight and strikes no coin: deben and kite of copper, silver and gold, where Vedyara has a suvarna, a candra and a tāmra. A Vedyaran coin reaching a southern port is assayed and exchanged for its metal content at the Kheperi rate, and never accepted at face value. The Collective's frontier moneylenders apply the same discount to Kheperi deben pieces moving the other way. Tānvüri traders reaching the eastern coast carry their own bureaucratic ledger-credit and exchange through the Empire's frontier posts on the same principle.
- **At the march road.** [[place-marchroad|The march road]] round the broken end of the wall is the one frontier a loaded camel crosses without climbing, and the bulk of what enters or leaves Vedyara by land goes that way. The Dunhari side of it settles in goods, in animals and in silver taken by weight, so a Collective factor at [[place-sandhyapur|Sandhyāpur]] or [[place-ashvapada|Ashvapada]] finances the season against letters and the coin never moves at all. The foreign pieces that do move east on it—Vylarian, Haradian—are weighed at the same counters and pass on their metal.
