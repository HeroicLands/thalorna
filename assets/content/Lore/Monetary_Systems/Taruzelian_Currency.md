A Vilérian changer who is handed a Taruzelian Solden puts it on the scale before he names a price, because it is about 7 percent light. Inside [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel]] nobody weighs it; it passes at face value from one end of the coast to the other. That difference is the whole character of the Taruzelian currency: the empire's denominations, struck and banked by a confederation that left the empire.

**Scope:** the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]] and the trading network of Taruzelian-affiliated merchants throughout the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] and the southern coasts.

## Overview

The Taruzelian currency is the Confederation's post-secession monetary system. It uses the same denomination structure as the [[lore-vylrncrncy|Vilérian currency]]—Solden (gold), Ardin (silver), Ogdel (Bit, 1/8 of an Ardin)—at the same nominal conversion ratios (1 Solden = 160 Ardin = 1,280 Bits), but the coins are _Taruzelian-struck_ and the supporting banking infrastructure is the Confederation's own [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]] ("House of the Treasury"), not the Vilérian treasury, [[affiliation-tarmex|the Tarmeks Orzan]].

The Taruzelian Solden is, by standard Vilérian assay, **slightly underweight**—about 7 percent below the Vilérian gold content. The discount is acknowledged in cross-border transactions but not advertised; in inter-Taruzelian commerce the coin passes at face value. Taruzelian Ardin are struck to the full Vilérian standard (about 1.5 g, three parts silver to one of alloy) and pass at full value in all systems where silver is silver.

The Merazuni issues script (the Taruzelian equivalent of Vilérian traveler's notes and master notes) honored at any Merazuni-affiliated moneylender house across the Confederation. The script is not honored at any chapter of [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]], the Vilérian institution; cross-border travel between Taruzelian and Vilérian territory requires conversion to physical coin at a changer's office (typically Helionite intermediaries who maintain accounts at both institutions).

The Taruzelian currency exists because the Confederation refused to remain dependent on Vilérian banking infrastructure after secession but had no realistic alternative to the existing denomination structure. The result is Vilérian denominations under Taruzelian sovereignty: a Confederation politically independent of the empire and commercially next door to it.

## Conversion Ratios

For game purposes, Taruzelian coin converts to Vilérian and Khelathi currencies as follows:

| Taruzelian          | Vilérian (effective)                                    | Khelathi (effective)               |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 1 Taruzelian Solden | 0.93 Vilérian Solden (~149 Ardin, due to gold discount) | ~¾ gold gezan (~7½ silver gezan)   |
| 1 Taruzelian Ardin  | 1 Vilérian Ardin                                        | ½ silver qelu (5 copper qelu)      |
| 1 Taruzelian Bit    | 1 Vilérian Bit                                          | 1/16 silver qelu (5/8 copper qelu) |

In casual commerce within Taruzelian territory the underweight Solden passes at full nominal value; the discount only emerges in cross-border transactions where Vilérian or Khelathi moneylenders assay the gold content.
