**The Precursors** is the name scholars give to the vanished people they propose as the makers of certain ancient artifacts across [[place-worldthlrn|the World of Thalorna]]. Few people study more than one of these sites. Those who do describe works alike in appearance and structure, uniformly ancient and untended, and unlike the works of either [[lore-flksenali|the Senali Folk]] or [[lore-flkkhazar|the Khazari Folk]]. From those comparisons they conclude that the works are artifacts of a people of long ago.

The conclusion gives the proposed makers a name, not a history. Who they were, what they intended, why they came or why they left remain unknown. The scholars cannot explain the works’ purposes. Their comparison is open to anyone with the time and resources to visit the sites and examine the records; it is a conclusion reached by study, not knowledge shared by everyone who lives beside a site.
