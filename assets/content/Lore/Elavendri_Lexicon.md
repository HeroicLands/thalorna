A bard of a great house in [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] is reciting the house's line at a feast, and he is doing it in two tongues at once without anyone at the table remarking on it. The names of the founders are highland names, said the way the highlands say them, with the clicks in; the words that join them are Élavendri, the same words with the clicks gone, the long vowels opened into diphthongs and the last vowel of each one let fall to a murmur. The house's claim to the old blood rests on the guests hearing the difference and believing the line runs across it.

This is the word-hoard of [[skill-elvndrlng|Élavendri]], the tongue of the lowland kingdom. It is the daughter of [[skill-aelendlng|Áelendi]], the highland tongue that came over the eastern pass, made over by the lowlands in the centuries since: its clicked stops merged with the plain ones, its back breathed stops weakened or fell, its long vowels broke into the diphthongs it is admired for, and its final vowels wore down to a reduced _ë_ that is written so that it is said. Because it is Áelendi's daughter it carries Áelendi's great layer of [[lore-flksinale|Sinalë]] words, the trees, the light, the veil and the art, and several of them came back nearly to their Sinalë shape on the way down. Because the kingdom owed the empire service for centuries, it carries a smaller layer of imperial words as well: the law, the coin, the letters and the University.

The words are arranged by what they say about the people who use them: the house and its old blood first, because a house is what an Élavendri owes before anything; then the land a house keeps, the bard who holds its history, and the art every well-bred Élavendri is expected to follow.

## How to read the tables

Each word table has six columns:

- **Form** is the Élavendri word as it is cited: a noun in its nominative, an adjective in its one form, a verb in its infinitive.
- **Source** names the Áelendi word it descends from, or the imperial Vylari word it was taken from, and the Form is exactly what the changes make of it.
- **Class** gives the word's kind and, for a noun, its gender.
- **Gloss** is its meaning in Élavendri.
- **Built from** is `—` for a word inherited or borrowed whole.
- **Attested** names a page that uses the word, or `—`.

## Sounds and spelling

Élavendri is written in these pages as its own scribes write it in the [[skill-semrnscrpt|Sêmarion]], with one acute over the stressed vowel of every word, which is the first vowel of the root. It writes no circumflex, because it keeps no long vowel: every long vowel it inherited became a diphthong or a short vowel. A word's last vowel, unless it is _-a_, is the reduced _ë_, written with its two dots so that it is said and not swallowed.

## From Áelendi

Thirteen changes, in this order, make an Élavendri word of an Áelendi one. The clicked stops merged with the plain ones. The breathed `kh` weakened to `h`; `gh` lost its breath at the start of a word and fell everywhere else, and the like vowels it had stood between ran together into one. A long vowel in the stressed open syllable broke into a diphthong, `â` to `ae`, `ê` to `ei`, `î` to `ie`, `ô` to `ou` and `û` to `ýe`, and every other long vowel shortened. Between vowels `p` softened to `v`, as it did after a nasal or a liquid, and `t`, `d` and `k` softened to `d`, `dh` and `g`. Before the new front diphthongs `k`, `g` and `s` became `ch`, `j` and `sh`. A final vowel other than _-a_ after a consonant was reduced to _ë_, and the stress, which never moved off the first syllable of the root, took an acute.

So the highland name _Khálden_ is _Hálden_, _kûthresa_ (a forfeit) is _chýethresa_, _sîrothathen_ (to seat a guest) is _shíerothathen_, and the Sinalë loan _kálpirno_, a stand of birch, comes back to _kálvirnë_, close to the Sinalë _kalvirno_ it started from.

The Sinalë words ride the same table, and many of them come back toward their Sinalë shape on the way down, because the lowland softening undoes what the highland mouth did to them: the Sinalë `v` the Áelendan said as `p` is `v` again between vowels. This is where the "fey" sound of Élavendri comes from. The words that open on _th-_ are disproportionately the Sinalë words, and the tongue's whole Brittonic colour, the colour of the lays, is the Sinalë layer heard through two mouths.

## The learned words

The imperial words came with the clerks in the centuries of vassalage and with the [[skill-semrnscrpt|Sêmarion]] and the University after. A learned word keeps its imperial shape more than an inherited one does: it loses its masculine ending and its long vowels, reduces its last vowel to _ë_, and takes the stress on its first syllable, but it keeps `b`, `f` and `z`, which no inherited word has. Several stand beside the inherited form of the same root: the inherited _lóuresand_ is a reciter who keeps a house's records in his head, and the learned _lórezand_ is a lecturer at the University.

## Words

### The house and the old blood

An Élavendri house is the whole descending line, the dead included, and its standing rests on one claim: that the line runs back to the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]]. The words the houses use to make it are the highland words, worn down. A clan name in the highlands is the founder's name with _-ndi_; in the lowlands the old houses carry the same ending as _-ndë_, and a house that can show _sóurethendë_, kin of one blood, on a genealogy that goes back to the crossing outranks one that cannot, whatever its acreage.

An old line is _théilaralon_, the word the empire uses for a noble house with a seat in its Senate, and an earned name, _kíramarlen_, is the one a bard gives. Whether all the claims are genuine is another matter, and a claim kept up gracefully for four hundred years has earned a standing of its own.

### The land in keeping

Land is a duty and not a possession. A lord who lets his people go hungry has failed the one thing his rank is for, and the land words are the herders' words of the highlands turned to the lowland estate. _Órmalis_ is the land a house keeps, _tárhelon_ the common store held against a bad winter, and _háresand_ the steward who answers for it. A house is judged by the state of the land in its keeping as much as by its bards.

### The bard and the word

Poetry is how a house's history is held, how a prophecy is passed on and how a judgment is made memorable enough to bind, and the bard's words are the oldest in the tongue's care. _Sóurimand_, a singer, is the highland singer at the gathering grown into a court officer; _lóuresand_, a reciter, is the highland Tradition Keeper, and in the lowlands he keeps a house's records in the head. A satire composed by a bard of standing is a weapon the law recognizes, and it is made of these words.

The letters came twice. The rune-row is still cut for magic and old poetry, and its staves are _shéimarson_. Everything else is written in the empire's [[skill-semrnscrpt|Sêmarion]], whose letter is _sémarzon_, the same word borrowed back from the coast.

### The art and the thin places

Magic is ordinary here, studied and discussed at table and expected of the well-bred, and its words came down through the highlands from the [[lore-flksinale|Sinalë]]. That is where the tongue's _th-_ comes from: so many of the Sinalë words open on it that _th-_ is the sound of the uncanny in the tongue. The art is _símmë_, a thin place _sírnastë_, a spirit _áithevë_, and the fae, one at a time, _pénallanë_. The lowland mage, _thórdimand_, is the word the highlands use for him, and the Élavendri do not mind it.

### The Pact, the oath and the judgment

The Pact is the last thing an Élavendri owes, and he does not explain it to a foreigner, partly from courtesy and partly because he is not sure of the terms. ### The crown, the law and the University

For centuries the kingdom owed the empire service, and the words of that service came north with the imperial clerks and stayed: the law, _vórnon_, the charter, _záktela_, the tax farmer, _sóhatand_. They are the only words in the tongue with `b`, `f` or `z`, and the three letters mark a clerk's word. The University and the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] brought a second band, and the kingdom treats the Ordo as a rival to be balanced, licensed and argued with, in the Ordo's own words: a lecturer is _lórezand_, the learned twin of the highland _lóuresand_, a reciter.
