When the [[lore-flksinale|Sinalë]] first reached [[place-klhrcntnnt|Kalihara]] and sought to settle there, the Kaliharans received them with courtesy and refused them. The custodians' record keeps one thing more of that visit: the Sinalë recognized something familiar in [[affiliation-tharivaan|Thári'vaan]]. No Kaliharan source says what it was, and whatever it was, it did not persuade the custodians to open their door any wider.

## What It Was

What the Sinalë recognized on Kalihara was [[lore-itharisul|ithári'sul]], the substance of the works of the [[lore-theithari|Ithári]]. The record sets the recognition in Thári'vaan, and to a Kaliharan the works and the stone they are made of are part of the Living Whole like a reef or a river, so the record and the stone do not disagree. The Sinalë have never said why the stone was familiar to them.

## What the Sinalë Say

Nothing. They have given no public account of their visit to the island, and their own histories pass around it in silence. They pass in the same silence around an earlier meeting elsewhere, recorded in [[lore-itharicntc|The Sinalë Meet the Ithári]], when their wayfarers came upon the Ithári themselves and were politely declined. They acknowledge the visit to Kalihara obliquely if at all, and of the meeting they will confirm only that it occurred.

## What Nobody Knows

- Where the Sinalë had met the stone before: at the afternoon they met the Ithári, at a work standing somewhere else on Thalorna, or somewhere no record names.
- Whether they ever put that afternoon together with the builders of Kalihara's works, and the stone was how they did it.
- Why they will not say.
- Why the custodians' record kept the recognition and not what was recognized.
