_Nágengir_ is the [[affiliation-nahild|Faith of Náhild]]'s name for its favored [[being-tereb|terebu]]: powerful, self-directed dead bound back into their bodies by necrotic force. The faith raises them through [[lore-nahilddty|Náhild]]'s dark magic and prizes them as warriors and sorcerers. They guard her temples and lead her undead armies, the mindless [[lore-haugverdir|hrúmverdir]] among them. Like every undead, they carry [[lore-theshadow|the Shadow]], and Náhild draws on it through [[lore-ginnauga|Ginnauga]], the Eye of the Void.

This name marks a particular kind of [[lore-undead|undead]], not every dead presence feared in the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]]. A spirit that remains near a place is not thereby a nágengir. The broader bestiary explains the difference between an immaterial haunting and a body made to walk after death.

## The Watch Beneath the Hidden Roof

One northern warning tale follows an unnamed traveler past a hidden hof of [[affiliation-nahild|Náhild]]. Its terror lies in a guardian that thinks, speaks, and commands the driven dead. The teller leaves its fate unanswered and carries the warning back to a living hearth.

: The Watch Beneath the Hidden Roof {#hidden-roof}

```poetry {form=epic lang=en}
Hold room beside the hearth;
hear those returning late.
Wind wears borrowed voices;
wood wakes with old sounds.
The dead may linger near us;
dear places keep their memories.
This tale tells of a body
bound to a darker will.

Snow sifted across the hills;
sunset sank behind them.
A traveller took the upland road,
turning toward hoped for shelter.
Cold crept beneath the cloak;
clouds covered the last light.
Below the ridge, a roof
received the gathered darkness.

No smoke streamed from its stones;
no supper scent came outward.
Yet movement marked the doorway;
a mouth offered welcome.
Come close; the cold grows bitter.
Come in; the wind grows wild.
The voice was clear and careful;
the visitor stood and listened.

A warrior waited within,
weapon held at the hand.
The shape seemed almost human;
the stranger sought its face.
Still eyes studied movement;
still feet held their ground.
The voice knew a measured pause;
its welcome wore good manners.

Speech should summon trust;
a sheltered guest owes gratitude.
The traveller took no step;
the threshold stayed between them.
What household holds this roof?
Whose hearth would I be sharing?
Who answers for the stranger
received within these walls?

Black candles burned behind it;
bone pale gleams stirred there.
An obsidian shard drank brightness;
its dark face gave back nothing.
The guardian smiled at the asking;
good manners masked its meaning.
Náhild, it named the mistress;
night held that name between them.

Snow fell upon the cloak;
silence filled the distance.
The traveller had heard whispers
of hidden hofs and offerings,
priests who praised the corpse goddess,
promises made against the living.
This roof received no traveler
whose life it meant to shelter.

We keep what others lose;
we outlast mortal winters.
So spoke the steady guardian,
so softly strength was offered.
Our goddess gives us favor;
our watch withstands the grave.
Come closer, cold road wanderer;
come hear what death permits.

The words were weighed and chosen;
the warrior watched their working.
No hunger shook it;
no stumbling stole speech.
A mind measured the visitor;
measured words made bait.
Patient purpose held the weapon;
persuasion held the offered door.

Boots bore dead weight;
shoulders filled the doorway.
Dead flesh stood dense;
dark binding moved it.
Here was no wandering whisper,
no wraith above an old mound.
The hidden hof had a defender;
its hand could wield that steel.

Behind it, bodies gathered;
broken faces turned toward it.
They waited for its direction;
the warrior watched and judged.
A word woke their movement;
a gesture gathered them still.
The thinker chose the moment;
the driven dead obeyed.

A priest passed from the darkness;
pale candlelight framed his shoulders.
He praised the favored guardian,
powerful beyond its dying.
Nágengir, his name for it;
Náhild's chosen, terrible servant.
The warrior heard its honor;
its eyes remained on the guest.

Temples need such keeping;
terrible armies need such leaders.
So ran the priest's praising;
so plainly purpose showed.
Warrior strength and sorcerers' skill
served the goddess's dark designs.
The traveller heard the promise
of death brought to the living.

What kin keep your hearth?
What hall holds your welcome?
The guardian questioned gently;
its gaze gathered each hesitation.
Which roads reach your village?
Who guards its doors at night?
The snow made silence costly;
the stranger kept those names.

No answer crossed the threshold;
no kin were named as targets.
The traveller tasted the cold,
thinking of distant fire.
That silence spoke refusal;
the dead understood its meaning.
The smile slipped from the mouth;
steel shifted beneath the hand.

The priest withdrew;
pale ranks stirred behind.
A measured order followed;
movement answered at once.
The traveller turned toward the ridge;
terror lent speed to the feet.
Snow swallowed the first footfalls;
still the hillside must be climbed.

Breath broke against the cold;
blood beat in the ears.
The slope offered no mercy;
snow made treacherous footing.
Stumbling, the stranger rose again;
strength served the wish to live.
Behind, a thinking foe commanded;
before, blind winter threatened.

No easy spell opened safety;
no shouted charm settled danger.
Distance demanded the body's labor;
dread drove the next step.
The traveller crossed the hill crest,
carrying a warning homeward.
Fear filled the failing breath;
fire filled the mind's eye.

Far off, a hearth light glimmered;
familiar roofs broke the dark.
The traveller took the road;
tired legs trembled.
Hands hammered a household door;
hard wood answered with movement.
The latch lifted within;
living faces looked outward.

Warm hands welcomed the weight;
wet clothing fell by the hearth.
A bench bore the stranger;
bread and a cup came near.
Here questions carried kindness;
here shelter meant protection.
What followed you through the darkness?
What fear has filled your breath?

The traveller told of the roof,
the terrible watch at its doorway,
the priest praising the guardian,
the ranks receiving commands.
Speech shook before it steadied;
silence held the listening hall.
No host dismissed the warning;
no hand withheld the warming cup.

They called for the cult's foes;
they called for counsel and guards.
Neighbor must warn neighbor;
night roads must carry the word.
A hidden hof held danger;
a hateful goddess had servants.
The living drew their people close;
the living watched the doors.

Old voices offered distinctions;
old knowledge steadied dread.
Some graves deserve their honoring;
some guardians guard particular bonds.
Restless presences make claims;
remembrance may belong to kin.
This warning named Náhild's servant;
this body served her cult.

No name should darken every mound;
no terror should erase discernment.
They kept the different tales clear,
keeping their courage with them.
Their fear had found its object;
their duty found its people.
A guest brought danger's warning;
the hall received its weight.

The song grants no certain triumph;
the sanctuary still holds its guardian.
Dark purpose remains beyond us;
dead ranks await their leader.
Hold room beside the hearth;
hear those returning late.
Warning belongs to the living;
warm doors must hold them together.
```
