## Overview

For three days and three nights at the end of autumn the temples of [[lore-selmorosdty|Sélmoros]], the Silent Judge, close their doors to the living. Inside, the priesthood of the [[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]] performs the funerary rites of the year's dead for every family that has asked for them. Outside, the dead are the household's business. At dusk masked priests carry torches through the streets in silence, and the people they pass fall silent too; in the windows of the houses black candles burn, and at the table each family speaks the names of its dead aloud, so that the god will guide them safely through the underworld. The Festival of the Silent Passage does not celebrate death. It acknowledges the god's sovereignty over it, which the faith holds to be the more honest thing to do.

The festival takes the last three nights of [Morvanandi]{.name data-type="lore/calendar"} in [[lore-commoncal|the Vilérian Calendar]], and the temples open again at dawn on 1 [Kalmethandi]{.name data-type="lore/calendar"}, the winter solstice, which begins the month of going to the ancestors, the god's own.

## The Three Nights

**The temples** stay shut from the first dusk to the last dawn. The Threshold Keepers, the Limori, perform the rites for each of the year's dead whose family requested them and paid, reading the name, the house and the day of death into the temple's records of the dead. Nothing is said to the living until the doors open.

**The streets** belong at dusk to the procession. The masked priests and the Novices of the Silent Watch carry their torches from the temple steps through the city and back, without a word or a drum, and a street falls quiet as it passes. Taverns close their shutters, carts stop, and children are brought to the doors to see it.

**The houses** keep the dead at home. At dusk on the first night a black candle is set in the window, renewed on each night after, and the household gathers at the table to say the names of its dead, the year's newest first and then the old. Offerings are what the faith asks of its feasts all year: flowers that have withered, grain gone soft, fruit past its ripeness, life given back to the cycle. In the [[place-byzariargn|Bizaria Region]] the last of the three is the night of the black candles, and [[lore-silkkeeping|The Keeping of the Silk House]] remembers its dead on it.

## What Is Read

Many lands keep a work for the Silent Passage, read in the house rather than the temple, since the temple is shut.

- The houses of the Taruzelian coast read [[lore-askedforayear|Of the One Who Asked for a Year]] at the table, one part each night, with the household's ledger open beside the roll.
- The College of Ethics of [[affiliation-thyrenae|Thirenae]] in [[place-helionis|Helionis]] reads [[lore-silentdinner|The Dinner of the Silent Passage]] on the first night.
- A widow of [[place-wyndmarch|the Wiindmarch]] says [[lore-windownaming|The Naming at the Window]] over her candle on the first night, for dead the wood took without graves.

## On the Taruzelian Coast

The coast of the [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]] keeps the Silent Passage as the settling of the year's dead. A debt a Taruzelian dies owing falls on his house, so the head of the house reads the dead's open accounts beside their names, and a house closes the Passage knowing what it has inherited. The roll the houses read is the coast's great poem of death, the story of a lender who sat three nights at the shut door of the god and was not even refused, and its children learn its refrain before they can read it.

In [[place-qudumel2|Kudumel]] the altar of Sélmoros in the hall of the [[place-twofirestemple|Temple of Two Fires]] is shut with every other temple of his, but the temple's Ashaian fire-house never closes. A family whose house patronizes both rites crosses the court on those nights and speaks its dead aloud at the eternal flame, where the Ashaian rite teaches that the dead cross the [Mozhelurbek]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, the Bridge of the Dead, under the eye of [[lore-azravandty|Azravan]].
