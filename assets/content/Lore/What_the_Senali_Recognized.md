> **GM-only historical account.** This is a private custodial record of the visit, not a Senali explanation of it.

When the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] first reached [[place-klhrcntnnt|Kalihara]] and sought to settle there, the [[lore-kaliharanclt|Kaliharan]] people received them with courtesy and refused them. The custodians' record keeps one thing more of that visit: the Senali recognized something familiar in [[affiliation-tharivaan|Thári-vaan]]. No Kaliharan source says what it was, and whatever it was, it did not persuade the custodians to open their door any wider.

## What the Record Establishes

The record preserves recognition, not an identification of its object. It does not name a material, a structure, a procedure or a teaching as the thing recognized. The custodians' understanding of the Living Whole does not supply that missing detail.

## What the Senali Say

The Senali have given no public account of their visit to the island, and their own histories pass around it in silence. The custodial record supplies no explanation from them of what was familiar or why. It cannot establish where they encountered it before.

Comparisons with other accounts and possible interpretations belong to [[doc-itharigm|The Ithari (GM)]]. None changes what this record says.
