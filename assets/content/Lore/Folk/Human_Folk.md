Humans live in farming villages, trading towns and great cities, and among
herding and wandering peoples. Their languages, laws, faiths and household
customs vary from one country to another. Families, neighbors and teachers pass
on the customs of their people.

The [[lore-pelwarpepl|Pelwar peoples]] include many of the human populations of
[[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris]]. They speak several languages and belong to different kingdoms, tribes
and cities. The [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan People]], the [[lore-nordheimnclt|Nordheimn]] and the [[lore-vylarianclt|Vylarian]] people share ancestry
while maintaining distinct customs and loyalties.

Human craft and learning carry traces of contact with the
[[lore-flkkhazar|Khazár Folk]] and [[lore-flksinale|Sinale Folk]]. Scholars call this
[[lore-longnhrtnc|the Long Inheritance]]. Its extent remains disputed, especially
in healing, song and magic. Relations with the Elder Folk vary by country; most
humans know the Sinale only through stories.
