**The Grukar of Eilwìth** are the [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar Folk]] established on [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]], and have been for the better part of **seven
hundred years**. They are not an invasion, a recent misfortune or anybody's crisis. They are a permanent
condition of the island, in the way rats are a permanent condition of a granary, and the comparison is
the one Eilwìthans themselves use. They are also one of its great pestilences: nests in the
forests and mountains of every range, each raiding every farm, steading and herd it can reach.

## How They Came

By the road the Grukar always take: **fission**.

A tribe that outgrows its ground sheds a faction outward; a starving tribe does something worse, when a
Sha sees the end of the line coming and conspires against its own Ahk. One or the other reached the
northern water seven centuries ago, and Grukar cannot build a ship—so they were carried, in the holds
of vessels crossing between the northern lands and this coast, in numbers too small for anyone aboard to
have noticed or cared. The landing is told in [[lore-grukarcomes|the Grukar Come to Eilwìth]].

That was long before the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]], long before Eilwìth's present realms, and
long before anybody kept the sort of record that would settle it. The island has argued about the
question for centuries and has never resolved it.

## Where They Are {#where-they-are}

In the empty third of Eilwìth, which is a great deal of country: the deep forest, the wooded folds of the
eastern and southern uplands, the crags and old workings of the mountains, and the wilder reaches of the
[[place-pshtrwldrns|Peshtar Wilderness]].

They keep out of open country. A nest needs cover to be built and dug, and a cave, a wooded ravine or a
cut hall in the rock is worth more to a spawner than any amount of grass. So the nests stand in forest
and mountain, and the farmland, the open downs and the moor see Grukar only as raiders, a night's run
from the trees. Many nests are in caves, and several are in places the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] cut
and left: the quarries and hunting halls the Deep Folk worked under [[lore-jointcrown|the Joint Crown]]
and abandoned when they went back inside the mountain after the Great War.

They hold no territory a realm would recognize and have no single head. There are tribes, each around
its own Ahk, and they grow and split and send fragments outward in every direction—which is precisely
why seven centuries of killing them has not reduced their number. **Every generation of Eilwìthans has
believed the problem was worse than it used to be, and every generation has been wrong; it is exactly
what it has always been.** They are endemic, and they cannot be eradicated.

Each nest is a tribe: one spawner and everything it has laid. The nest is the unit that matters, and each
one below is given by its ground, what it covets or guards, its enemy, and the trouble it is in now. The
nests lie in four ranges, with a few beyond them in the northern fells.

### The Central Highlands

The upland between the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]] and the lake is open grazing, and its nests are in
the rocky and wooded ground that breaks it: the crags of [[place-threesisters|the Three Sisters]], the
gorges of its northern edge and the wooded ravines above [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]]. They raid the
upland farms and horse runs, and the northern shore of the lake.

- **[[place-kingsdelf|Kingsdelf]]**, a Khazari quarry of the Joint Crown in the north face of the northernmost of the Three Sisters.
  - _Territory:_ the cut galleries, the saddle between the peaks where the [[place-stonyford|Stoniford]] breeders run their horses, and the folds below.
  - _Covets:_ the galleries themselves, dry, deep and easy to hold, with a Khazari cistern at their far end.
  - _Enemy:_ the Stoniford breeders, and the nest at Ravenkeld, which raids the same steadings from the north.
  - _Now:_ the Senate's levy is taking the herders north and the runs lie half watched. The spawner has laid Hushk to take them, and an old Hushk, [[being-greytusk|Greytusk]], leads the raids while the new clutch grows.
- **[[place-reedgill|Reedgill]]**, dens dug in the banks of a wooded ravine above Lake Eilìssa's northern bays.
  - _Territory:_ the ravine, the moor's edge above it, and the reed-beds and bays at its foot.
  - _Covets:_ the bays, their fish, and the boats and nets of the cutters who work them.
  - _Enemy:_ the reed-cutters of [[place-koldmere|Koldmere]] and of [[place-brynwold|Brinwold]] alike.
  - _Now:_ Koldmere and Brinwold quarrel over the beds. Each shore cuts the other's nets, and Koldmere blames Brinwold for a boat and two cutters lost in the autumn. The cutters of both shores have seen a thin Grukar standing in the shallows below the ravine at dusk, and call it [[being-stillwader|Stillwader]].
- **Ravenkeld**, caves at a spring-head in a wooded gorge at the Highlands' northern edge, under the Highwood.
  - _Territory:_ the gorge and the northern steadings of the Highlands below it.
  - _Covets:_ the spring, the one water in the gorge that never fails.
  - _Enemy:_ the Bradhar hill clans on the moor above it, and Kingsdelf to the south.
  - _Now:_ the legion's burning of the forest edge north of [[place-kaermouth|Kaermouth]] has driven the deer east and thinned the gorge's game. The spawner has turned its raiders onto the northern steadings' cattle.
- **Stoneholt**, tumbled crag caves in the wooded rocks above Lake Eilìssa's south-western shore, where the Highlands meet the Thornwold.
  - _Territory:_ the crags and the southern horse-grazing below them.
  - _Covets:_ the brood mares and foals of the southern breeders.
  - _Enemy:_ [[affiliation-durrask|the Durrask]], who take the same horses, and the breeders who ride after both.
  - _Now:_ the Durrask drove forty mares off the grazing in the summer and left less to take. The nest raids farther west, toward the runs below Stoniford, and the breeders count its losses against the Durrask.

:::secret {title="Reedgill and the reed-bed quarrel"}
Two shores working one set of beds put too many people on the water for a raid to go unseen, so the nest's Sha, Stillwader, keeps the shores watching each other instead of the ravine. The raiders cut nets on both shores and took Koldmere's lost boat and cutters.
:::

### The Peshtar Wilderness

The forest of the [[lore-peshtarclans|Peshtar]] is where the war without end is fought hardest. The
nests stand in the pine glens of [[place-highwood|the Highwood]], on the ridges of
[[place-stormwood|the Stormwood]] and in the cliffs below [[place-nordmoor|the Nordmoor]], and the three
tribes fight them as they have for more than five hundred years, alongside every other war they have.

- **Mossglen**, caves in the wall of a granite glen under the Highwood's high moor.
  - _Territory:_ the glen and the sheep moor above it.
  - _Covets:_ the summer flocks [[affiliation-bradhar|the Bradhar]] hill clans bring up to the moor.
  - _Enemy:_ the hill clans, the War-Leader [[being-taskain|Taskain]]'s own people.
  - _Now:_ the hill clans keep their flocks close and post watchers on the moor's edge. With Taskain's fighters gone south against the legion, the nest's raiders come out in daylight, and the watchers are too few to stop them.
- **Pinehollow**, a cave under a pine-grown scar in the Highwood's northern pines.
  - _Territory:_ the northern pines up to the high pastures.
  - _Covets:_ the herds on the high pastures between the Highwood and the Stormwood.
  - _Enemy:_ the nest at Hazelbrake, which hunts the same pastures from the other side, and the herdsmen of both tribes.
  - _Now:_ the pastures are crowded with armed Kelgar and Bradhar herdsmen fighting each other. The spawner has laid Hushk, for war with Hazelbrake over what the herdsmen leave.
- **Hazelbrake**, a sinkhole cave in a hazel-grown ridge of the Stormwood.
  - _Territory:_ the ridge and the glens below it.
  - _Covets:_ the deep water at the bottom of the sinkhole.
  - _Enemy:_ Pinehollow, and [[affiliation-kelgar|the Kelgar]].
  - _Now:_ it lost a faction and a reserve spawner to the founding of Kineglen, and Kelgar clans driven west from the plain have brought their herds into its range. The spawner is laying Uk to rebuild, and its raiders take Kelgar cattle by night.
- **[[place-kineglen|Kineglen]]**, a cave behind the deserted byres of the Kelgar glen nearest the Stormplain.
  - _Territory:_ the emptied glen and the forest edge below it, against the steadings of [[place-vuldstead|Vuldstead]].
  - _Covets:_ the byres and the glen's grazing, the first ground it has held.
  - _Enemy:_ the Kelgar clan whose glen it was, and Vuldstead's forest-edge district.
  - _Now:_ it is young and short of Hushk. Its raiders take cattle from Vuldstead's forest edge to feed a Hushk laying, and the district counts every loss as a Kelgar raid.
- **Gullstone**, sea caves in the cliffs below the Nordmoor's wooded folds.
  - _Territory:_ the cliffs and the cliff pastures above them.
  - _Covets:_ the seabird ledges and the fishing of the inlet below.
  - _Enemy:_ [[affiliation-lonagar|the Lonagar]] clan of the inlet.
  - _Now:_ with Stormveld's ships closing the inlets to the coasters, the clan fishes and fowls the cliffs harder and crowds the nest's ground. The nest takes the fowlers off their ropes and the boats off the shingle.

### The Ironfells

The mountain is where the Grukar meet [[place-vorgald|Vorgald]], and where the competition for caves is
fiercest. The nests are in crag caves and in the hunting halls the Khazari left in the range's empty
heights, and the hold kills what it finds of them.

- **[[place-hornhall|Hornhall]]**, a Khazari hunting hall cut into a crag in the heights north of the Vardain basin.
  - _Territory:_ the crags and high corries above the basin.
  - _Covets:_ the hall, the best-built nest in the range, which the hold has cleared and the Grukar have taken back.
  - _Enemy:_ [[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]].
  - _Now:_ the band it sent into the valleys was killed at the hold's outer workings in the autumn. Its Sha, [[being-fellwatch|Fellwatch]], is watching the high grazing for a weaker way down.
- **[[place-wolfgate|Wolfgate]]**, crag caves in the eastern Sunderfells above the path from Sundergate to the high valleys.
  - _Territory:_ the path and the crags above it.
  - _Covets:_ the traffic on the path: smugglers' ponies, and thralls running from the Stormplain to the Vardain valleys.
  - _Enemy:_ the smugglers, the garrisons at both pass-heads, and the valleys' watch.
  - _Now:_ the snow is on the pass and nothing crosses it. The nest has dug in for the winter on short stores, and waits for the thaw and the first runaways.
- **Hartfell**, crag caves above the royal forest of [[place-wyndmarch|the Wiindmarch]].
  - _Territory:_ the crag and the upper royal forest.
  - _Covets:_ the royal forest's deer.
  - _Enemy:_ the Forester's men.
  - _Now:_ the deer are going over the passes with the smugglers, and the forest's game is thin. The nest has turned to the stock of the forest-edge steadings, and the march sets the losses against the smugglers.

### South of the Lake and the Southern Woods

South of the lake, between the Highlands and Dúnavarre, lies [[place-thornwold|the Thornwold]],
whose oak copses and bog bottoms hold nests, and beyond it the forest that runs east along the edge of
[[place-hallowwood|Seirasto]] into the western woods of Dúnavarre. The [[affiliation-durrask|Durrask]] fight them wherever they meet
them in the copses.

- **[[place-aldermire|Aldermire]]**, dens dug in the bog islands of the northern Thornwold, above Lake Eilìssa's south-western shore near its south-eastern tip.
  - _Territory:_ the bog and alder woods, and the lake shore below them.
  - _Covets:_ the bog islands, which no horse and no army can reach.
  - _Enemy:_ the manors of the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]]'s lower shore, and the Durrask steads.
  - _Now:_ since the Durrask burned a shore mill in the autumn, the shore barons have armed their tenants and watch the shore by night. The nest has turned west, onto the Highlands' horse-grazing on the lake's western side.
- **Gorsering**, an old earth rampart on a low wooded ridge of the Thornwold north of Seirasto, dug through with warrens.
  - _Territory:_ the ring and the copses round it.
  - _Covets:_ the ring's banks, deep earth ready dug.
  - _Enemy:_ the Durrask steads nearest it.
  - _Now:_ the stead-heads are burning the copses round the ring to strip its cover. The nest raids the steads' pig herds in the oak folds farther off, while the War-Leader's spears are away raiding the realms.
- **[[place-fernbank|Fernbank]]**, caves in a fern-grown bank beside the old Crown Road in the Thornwold's oak woods, south of Dunmere.
  - _Territory:_ the wet forest on both sides of the road, from the ford below the last foresters' lodge toward Seirasto, but not the road itself.
  - _Covets:_ the mast of the old oak and beech avenue along the road, and the deer and swine it draws.
  - _Enemy:_ the Senali at Seirasto's edge, and the lake-shore steadings below Dunmere.
  - _Now:_ the Senali kill every raider that crosses south into the forest. The nest raids north to the ford and the lodge, and takes the carters who step off the road.
- **[[place-staghall|Staghall]]**, a Khazari hunting hall cut into a crag on a wooded ridge above [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]], in Dúnavarre's western forest on the edge of Seirasto.
  - _Territory:_ the ridge and the forest valleys below it.
  - _Covets:_ the hall, and the deer of the valley heads.
  - _Enemy:_ the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]] and the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]] alike.
  - _Now:_ the Order's road climbs toward its valleys every summer, and the war around it drives the deer off. The nest takes sentries from the road camps and gatherers from the Seilvri clearings.
- **Brambledene**, caves in a bramble-choked dene in the thorn and oak country along Seirasto's northern edge.
  - _Territory:_ the forest edge between the Thornwold and the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]]'s western estates.
  - _Covets:_ the oak mast and the swine that feed on it.
  - _Enemy:_ the Senali, who kill its raiders at the forest's edge.
  - _Now:_ it cannot range south past the edge. Its raiders strike north at the Durrask riding east to Dúnavarre, and at the estates themselves.

### The Northern Fells

The [[place-nordfells|Nordfells]] hold nests in their high folds and old workings, and
[[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Stormveld]] loses upland steadings to them most years.

- **Grimfold**, old workings nobody claims in the high folds of the central Nordfells.
  - _Territory:_ the high folds and the upland steadings below them.
  - _Covets:_ the deep galleries of the workings.
  - _Enemy:_ the upland steadings and the Jarldom's hunters, and the nest at Koldfell.
  - _Now:_ a hard winter killed the fell sheep and left the steadings little to take. Its raiders go farther down toward the plain's edge, and the Jarldom books its losses as raids by the uplanders who acknowledge no jarl.
- **Koldfell**, crag caves in the western Nordfells, above the High Jarl's burned steadings.
  - _Territory:_ the western fells and the ground of the four steadings the Kelgar burned.
  - _Covets:_ the burned steadings' ground, empty since the spring of 719.
  - _Enemy:_ Grimfold, and the Kelgar war-bands that cross its range.
  - _Now:_ the High Jarl's men want to rebuild the steadings, and the Kelgar come over the ridges to stop them. The nest takes stragglers of both.

## The Names {#names}

The Grukar give no names, so every name of theirs on the island is a human one, in [[skill-aelwthlng|Eilwìthi]].

- **A nest** is named for its site, in two plain parts: the place's look or use, then the ground—_-delf_, _-gill_, _-glen_, _-hall_, _-fell_, _-ring_, _-mire_, _-bank_, _-dene_, _-stone_. The tribe is the nest, and the nest's name is its only name.
- **A spawner** is not named. Hardly anyone has seen one, and it is "the Kingsdelf spawner" when it must be spoken of at all.
- **A known Grukar**, the rare Hushk or Sha that a frontier comes to recognize, is given a byname from its mark or its habit, in two plain parts: [[being-greytusk|Greytusk]], [[being-stillwader|Stillwader]], [[being-fellwatch|Fellwatch]].

The names favor short, hard elements of the island's common speech, in its own spellings: _k_ for the hard sound and never _c_, _kold_ for cold. They leave out _c_, _q_, _x_, a _y_ that is a vowel and the doubled _oo_, and they avoid the Eilwìthi given-name endings _-wald_, _-rik_, _-mund_ and _-ith_.

## The War Without End

**The Grukar are at war with everyone on Eilwìth.** No people, realm or tribe has a peace with any nest,
because a nest cannot make one and nobody can make one with it.

**The tribal peoples** carry the weight of it, and always have.

The Grukar are in the empty country, and the empty country is where the island's tribes live. The forest
communities of the interior and the eastern and southern woods have been fighting them **without pause
for more than five hundred years**—not a war with campaigns and treaties but a condition of existence,
fought in every generation, by everybody, forever.

This is most of what the lowlands are looking at when they call these people barbarians. A society
organized around an enemy that cannot be finished does not look like a society organized around
farming and inheritance, and it never will.

The **civilized realms** deal with them too, constantly and at the edges: the frontier steadings of [[place-wyndmarch|the Wiindmarch]], the upland farms of [[place-nordfells|the Nordfells]] and [[place-cntrlhghlnds|the Central Highlands]], the forest margins
of every realm whose land runs into trees. It is endemic rather than dramatic—a few farms a year, a
patrol that does not come back, a season when the woods are worse than usual—and every realm handles
it as routine expenditure.

[[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Aldorath]] has mounted formal campaigns perhaps a dozen times in its history.
Each was declared a success. There is no evidence any of them made a difference at all.

**The two elder races** are in a war with them that has no end at all. The Khazari of
[[place-vorgald|Vorgald]] kill every Grukar they find. The [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] of
[[place-hallowwood|Seirasto]] fight the nests along the forest's edge as long as there have been
nests there, and say nothing about it to anyone.

## The Khazari

The [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] of [[place-vorgald|Vorgald]] hate the Grukar, and the hatred is of a
different order from anybody else's.

Everyone on Eilwìth kills Grukar when they must, and treats it as one more hard necessity of living
here. The Deep Folk do not treat it as a necessity. They kill Grukar **immediately, wherever found,
without negotiation, without exception, and without the enormous patience they extend to every other
living thing on this island**—and it is the only subject on which the Khazari have ever been observed
to act quickly.

They compete for the same ground as well. Grukar go into caves, the Khazari live in the rock, and the
halls the Deep Folk cut and left in the empty heights are the best nest sites in the range. Vorgald has
cleared [[place-hornhall|Hornhall]] more than once, and a nest has taken it back each time.

The reason for the hatred is not competition, though. It is [[lore-khazarturn|Khazartúrn]].

In 2427 BF a [[lore-flksenali|Senali outlaw]] wielding [[miscgear-secondvoice|the Second Voice]] took tens of thousands of
Grukar from their spawners and destroyed the greatest city the Deep Folk ever built. The account—exact,
with every name lost—has been kept in the working archive of every hold since, and is read at
intervals. It is not treated as history.

The Khazari know the Grukar were compelled. It changes nothing, because the relic was never recovered
and their conclusion was never about guilt: **Grukar are ammunition**, and the only defense is that there
be none near a hold to be picked up.

So the hatred did not begin on Eilwìth and does not depend on anything the Eilwìth Grukar have done.
The Deep Folk had been in [[place-ironfells|the Ironfells]] some seven centuries when Khazartúrn fell,
and kept the account twenty-four centuries more before the first Grukar ever set foot here.
