Thuravark Spelling writes the roman spelling of [[doc-prnncthlrn|Pronouncing Thalorna]] in [[skill-thuravarkscript|Thuravark]] runes, in Thuravark Carved. A passage marked with `map=thuravark` is converted by the rows of this note before it is set, so the note's source stays in roman letters and the page shows the staves.

```text
[Hvarn]{lang=nord font=thuravark-carved map=thuravark}
```

## From the Spelling Guide to Thuravark

- **Thuravark has one case.** A capital is written as its stave.
- **The digraphs are staves:** _th_, _dh_, _kh_, _gh_, _ng_ and _hl_. _ngh_ is _n_ and _gh_.
- **A digraph with its first letter doubled** (_tth_, _nng_) is the digraph stave twice. A doubled consonant or vowel is its stave twice.
- **An acute vowel is the vowel and the acute mark**, which the face draws as the stave twice.
- **_y_ before a vowel is the glide _j_**, and anywhere else the vowel _i_.
- **The full stop** is the dash, and **the numerals** 0 to 9 are the Thuravark numerals.

A space draws nothing in the face. A hyphen, `'`, `,`, _c_, _q_, _x_ and any other character the script lacks pass through unchanged; the face draws nothing for a hyphen and prints the box for the rest.
