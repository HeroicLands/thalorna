Aelwythi Semaron Spelling writes [[skill-aelwthlng|Aelwythi]] in the
[[skill-semrnscrpt|Semaron Script]], in the Kuriale hand. Its input is the
ordinary readable spelling of the language's older register. The language note
selects this mapping and the font for a passage marked `lang=aelw`.

```text
[Whither goest thou?]{lang=aelw}
[Whither goest thou?]{lang=aelw map=aelwythi-semaron font=semaron-kuriale}
```

These are spelling rules. They convert text into the script automatically;
they do not calculate its pronunciation. The
[[doc-prnncthlrn#aelwythi|Aelwythi pronunciation rules]] give the historical
readings. A vowel, silent letter or consonant group keeps its word's reading:
_this_ and _thin_ share the Semaron _th_, and the _g_ in _gem_ keeps its soft
reading. A sequence of signs can carry a contextual reading, as a sequence of
roman letters does. The spelling preserves distinctions through the whole
word, rather than assigning a separate sign to every sound.

## The spelling rules

- **Case follows the source.** Each ordinary letter takes its Semaron form;
  _y_ keeps its own sign even when it writes a vowel. A doubled vowel remains
  two letters. An acute vowel or _ë_ in a borrowed name takes its plain vowel
  sign. Length, silence and stress follow the word's reading.
- **_th_ takes one sign.** It serves both the breath of _thin_ and the voice
  of _thy_. _ch_ and _tch_ take _t_, _s_, _h_; the first letter determines
  their case. _sh_, _ng_, _wh_, _ph_ and other groups keep their letters and
  their Aelwythi readings.
- **The script has no _c_, _q_ or _x_.** _c_ before _e_, _i_ or _y_ takes
  _s_, and elsewhere _k_; _qu_ takes _kw_; _q_ alone takes _k_; _x_ takes
  _ks_. These are spelling substitutions, with lexical exceptions recorded
  as native forms in the language's dictionary. _Exact_, _conquer_ and
  _sceptic_ have such forms. An unlisted word receives the rows' spelling;
  its output is not a guarantee of a sound-by-sound transcription.
- **Pauses take the point.** Full stops, commas, colons, semicolons, dashes,
  question marks, exclamation marks, ellipses and parentheses take the one
  Semaron pause sign. Digits take the Semaron numerals.
- **Quotation and apostrophes are unmarked in the script.** Quotes, possessive
  apostrophes and marks of omitted sounds disappear. Thus _'tis_ has no
  opening catch, and _summer's_ has no glottal stop. A hyphen gives a word
  space. Their roman source keeps its punctuation.

The frontmatter rows are the complete automatic spelling procedure. A
registered native form takes precedence for its whole word through the usual
language lookup; it is written in the script, not supplied as another roman
respelling. Authors add a native form only where the rows give a different
spelling. The shared `semaron` mapping serves the common transcription of the
other tongues.
