**Meshara the Wise** was an apothecary of [[place-ankhsetun|Ankh-Setûn]] who served the city's poor for decades and died at an extraordinary age. Before that she was a caravan head scout, a hard woman who ranged as far as the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] and taught wild-country craft to the porters and guards who showed a gift for it. Two of the most capable people in the [[affiliation-empirtkhpr|Empire of Ta'Kheperu]] call her their teacher — [[being-shebsthtms|Shebas Thutmosa]], who inherited her shop, and [[being-sentatsrkt|Sênta Ta'Sereket]], who learned the road from her — and each of them knew a different half of her life. She is dead. What she left behind is not.

## The Road

For the middle years of her life Meshara was head scout to the caravan master **Thoren the Keen-Eyed**, whose trains carried the trade of Ta'Kheperu across the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]] and onward overland through the [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]] country and into the Nordlands. She went ahead of the caravan and came back to it: reading the ground and the wind, calling the weather, finding the camps that were hidden and the beasts that were not yet seen, and choosing the one line a laden train could take through country that offered a dozen it could not. She was exact, and she did not forgive carelessness, because a scout who misses a sign gets a caravan killed. Those who worked under her remember a woman in her fifties with no softness in her at all, who was nonetheless the person every porter wanted beside them when the road went wrong.

The Nordlands run was the longest Thoren's caravans made, and Meshara knew the far end of it better than anyone in his service: the pass road climbing from [[place-steinmark|Steinmark]] in the [[affiliation-kingdmnrgd|Kingdom of Norgaad]], and above it the [[place-shtrdpks|Shattered Peaks]], five leagues of broken stone where a cache set in the right cleft is safe from anyone who does not already know the cleft.

A head scout in wild country is also the caravan's healer, and Meshara was. What grows along a route, what stops bleeding, what brings a fever down, what kills — she learned it on the road, from need, and it was the beginning of the knowledge that made her name in a city she had not yet settled in.

## The Shop

When the road was done with her she went to Ankh-Setûn and set up as an apothecary in the warehouse district, among the granaries and the trading houses where the caravans of the granary capital load and unload. Her patients were the poor of the lower quarters. She treated everyone with equal care, rich and poor alike, charging the poor minimally and the wealthy well, and her practice was sustained by the gratitude of the poor far more than by the coin of the rich. Her skill became legendary in the city, and it is the lower quarters that gave her the name she is remembered by. By the time she was old the whole district knew her door.

## What She Taught

Sênta Ta'Sereket came to Thoren's caravan at thirteen, as a porter. Within two seasons her climbing and her awareness had caught Meshara's eye, and Meshara taught her everything about reconnaissance, evasion, and survival in wild country: to move silently through scrubland and avoid a bandit's notice, to read wind patterns and predict weather, to see the subtle signs of dangerous predators and hidden camps. Sênta earned her first scout's blade at twenty, and her entry into the Mercenaries' Guild of Ta'Kheperu, on what Meshara had made of her.

Shebas Thutmosa came to Meshara's shop as a child of eight, after a plague took her younger brother despite every physician her family's money could buy. Meshara took her as an apprentice and kept her for sixteen years. She taught her herbalism and remedy-making, and beneath the craft the calling: that a healer's greatest power is not wealth but knowledge and compassion, and that the true measure of a healer's success is the health of the community, not the coins in her purse.

The two teachings are one. A scout who misses a sign kills a caravan; an apothecary who misjudges a measure kills a patient. Meshara taught both of her students the same thing, which was to look, and then to look again.

## Her Death

Meshara died in Ankh-Setûn at an extraordinary age. Shebas inherited the shop and its philosophy and has made of it what her teacher would recognize — a destination for the city's poorest, treated whether or not they can pay. Word of the death travelled more slowly to the road, where Sênta works the southern routes, and it arrived carrying something more than grief.

## What Remains

Wounded men talk to the woman who dresses their wounds. Over her years in the warehouse district Meshara treated guards and drivers off caravans that had been reported lost, and she knew the trade well enough to hear what their accounts added up to: senior officers of the Mercenaries' Guild of Ta'Kheperu in league with bandit companies, arranging the robberies and sharing the indemnities paid out on a lost caravan. She gathered proof of it, and she gathered it the way she had once scouted a route — patiently, exactly, and without telling anyone what she was doing.

She did not keep the proof in the shop. On the last long journey she made, before age kept her to the city, she carried it north and set it in the Shattered Peaks, at a spot only Sênta knows how to reach — a cleft Meshara had shown her on the road years before. Her final message, sent to reach Sênta after her death, says that she is dead and where the proof lies. It says nothing about what Sênta should do with it.

The shop holds the rest of what she knew. Her records, now in Shebas's keeping, name who was treated for what and when, and among the names are men from caravans the Guild paid out on. Anyone inside the Guild who learns that Meshara kept notes has a reason to want them, and Shebas does not yet know what she is holding.
