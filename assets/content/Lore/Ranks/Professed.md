## Where This Standing Is Held

35 bodies in Thalorna name this standing, at level 4 of their ladders. **Vratin** stands in 11, **Professed** stands in 9, and **Lekhau** stands in 8. It also stands singly, in one body each, as **Committed**, **Devotee**, **Holder of the Vow**, **Hrímmadr**, and **Pik'ob**, plus 2 more.
