What the cult of Náhild does to a man who gives away its hidden rites, in the faith of Náhild.

## What This Standing Is

Náhild's is the one worship the pantheon itself holds heretical: her clergy are suppressed in every kingdom, her hofs kept hidden, and her offerings refused by the community around them. A man who betrays the cult's secrecy—naming a hidden hof, an initiate, or a godi to outsiders—is a Návargr, cast from it and marked as the reason its secrecy failed.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

What the cult does to the man who gives it away is not ordinary exclusion: he loses the protection its secrecy extends to its own, and the hidden hof he betrayed owes him nothing further, neither shelter nor silence, where before it owed him both.

## Privileges

None. A Návargr holds nothing the cult confers.

## Obligations

None the cult recognizes toward him; what he broke was the obligation of secrecy the cult owed its own, not one he owes it now.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-nahild|Faith of Náhild]] alone.
