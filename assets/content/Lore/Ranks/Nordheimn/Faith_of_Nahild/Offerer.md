Every other faith of the [[affiliation-asguardian|Asguardian Pantheon]] treats the worship of the [[affiliation-nahild|Faith of Nahild]] as heresy, so turning to her is already an act. Her Offerer has turned and brings a hidden hov the offering it asks, and holds none of the cult's secret observances yet.

## What This Standing Is

Her offerer brings the offering a hidden hov asks, without holding any of the secret observances known only to the cult's own initiates. Turning to her is itself an admission of a kind.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is known to the hidden hov he offers to and shares in the secrecy that protects it, without yet being trusted with the observances that mark an initiate—a lesser trust than an initiate's, but a trust nonetheless, since naming him to the wrong ears endangers the hov he offers to.

## Privileges

He may bring his offering to a hidden hov and share in whatever protection its secrecy extends to those it knows.

## Obligations

He keeps the hov's secrecy as any offerer must, without yet being taught the observances that would let him keep more of it.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-nahild|Faith of Nahild]] alone.
