The [[affiliation-nahild|cult of Nahild]] admits by teaching. A hidden hov's gudi shows an offerer it has come to trust the secret observances only the cult's own initiates know, and the teaching is the admission.

## What This Standing Is

An initiate keeps the secret observances of Nahild's worship, known to the cult's own initiates and to nobody outside it. The hidden hov's gudi confers the teaching on an offerer it has come to trust, and that act is the cult's own admission.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is trusted with what an offerer is not: the observances the cult conceals from everyone outside it, and the naming of the hidden hov itself, which a mere offerer may not yet be told in full.

## Privileges

He keeps the cult's secret observances, and is trusted with a hidden hov's location and its members in a way an offerer is not.

## Obligations

He guards the secrecy of the observances he has been taught and the hov that taught them, on the same terms that made his teacher a betrayer's opposite.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None short of ordination as [[lore-cultgodirnk|Her Gudi / Gudja]].

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-nahild|Faith of Nahild]] alone.
