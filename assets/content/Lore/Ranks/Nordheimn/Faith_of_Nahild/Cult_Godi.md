Outsiders cannot name the person who keeps one of the hidden hovs of the [[affiliation-nahild|Faith of Nahild]], and that is what the secrecy is for. Her Gudi / Gudja takes the sacrifices of a hov in hiding, and every initiate and offerer keeps silent chiefly to protect that one person and the rite they keep.

## What This Standing Is

In a worship every other faith of the [[affiliation-asguardian|Ten]] holds heretical, no rank sits above this one: a hidden hov has no keeper anyone outside may name, and there is no great public blet for a higher standing to perform.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He carries the whole of the hov's exposure: naming him to the wrong ears is the act that turns a man into a betrayer, and the secrecy every initiate and offerer keeps protects him and the rite he keeps.

## Privileges

He takes the sacrifices offered at his hidden hov, and teaches the observances that raise an offerer to an initiate.

## Obligations

He keeps his hov's location and its people secret above all else, and answers for the secrecy of everyone he has taught or admitted.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None above it; a hidden hov answers to no higher office within the cult.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-nahild|Faith of Nahild]] alone.
