The cult of [[affiliation-nahild|Náhild]] lives on silence: hers is the one worship the pantheon itself holds heretical, her clergy are suppressed in every kingdom and her hofs are kept hidden. Of everything a member can do, giving the cult away ends them fastest.

## What This Standing Is

A man who betrays the cult's secrecy—naming a hidden hof, an initiate, or a godi to outsiders—is cast from it and marked as the reason its secrecy failed. The community around a hidden hof refuses Náhild's offerings.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

What the cult does to the man who gives it away is not ordinary exclusion: he loses the protection its secrecy extends to its own, and the hidden hof he betrayed owes him nothing further, neither shelter nor silence, where before it owed him both.

## Privileges

None. A betrayer holds nothing the cult confers.

## Obligations

None the cult recognizes toward him; what he broke was the obligation of secrecy the cult owed its own, not one he owes it now.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-nahild|Faith of Náhild]] alone.
