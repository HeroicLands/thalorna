A clerk at the Compact-Hall in [[place-hringstead|Hringstead]] tells a warrior leaving one signed company for another: "You hold no ring and no standing of your own before the **Council of the Hall**. Your old captain's oath and your new captain's are all you have." The captain testifies to the standing; the warrior does not swear it.

## What This Standing Is

A common warrior of a signed company wears no Compact mark beyond his company's own; his standing before the Compact is testified by his captain rather than sworn by him directly. What he holds by his company's signing is the Compact's truce and a legible oath when he crosses from one company to another.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is bound by whatever his captain has sworn: the Compact deals with his company through the captain who signed for it, and a warrior crossing companies carries the standing his old captain's oath and his new one's give him rather than any standing of his own before the Council.

## Privileges

He shares his company's standing under the Compact's protection and testimony, and the legible terms that let him cross from a signed company to another without dispute over what he is owed.

## Obligations

He holds to the terms his captain has sworn on the company's behalf, and answers to that captain rather than directly to the Council of the Hall.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None; a signed company's own posts—quartermaster, purser, navigator, bosun, master-at-arms, surgeon, standard-bearer—belong to the company's own structure rather than to the Compact.

## Where This Standing Is Held

[[affiliation-malldbndlg|Málalidabandalag]] alone.
