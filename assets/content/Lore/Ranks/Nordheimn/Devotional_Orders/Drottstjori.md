Each devotional order has a leader, and in every one the leader's standing is the last the order records. A dróttstjóri carries the order's purpose furthest and speaks or acts for it where a devotee cannot.

## What This Standing Is

A leader holds an order's fullest standing, the rung above which none of these orders records a further one. He leads its study, its healing, its guardianship or its fight, whichever the order was formed for.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He answers for the order's work at a level a devotee does not, and where the order keeps any authority above its ordinary members, a leader is who holds it.

## Privileges

He holds the order's full claim on what it confers, and leads its work in whatever form that takes—clerical, secret or martial.

## Obligations

He answers for the order's standing as a devotee does not, and bears whatever a hof's godi or a senior member expects of him beyond ordinary membership.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None recorded.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The devotional orders of the faiths of the Ten that confer this standing. Each order gives it the obligations of its own devotion; it is standing within that order, rather than authority over the other orders.
