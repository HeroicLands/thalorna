Ask how a devotional order makes a devotee a Drottvargr by putting him out and the answer is nearly always the bare fact: each order words its own expulsion, and most record only that it happens. What the orders share is the result. A devotee who is cast out loses the order's shelter and its purpose together.

## What This Standing Is

A devotee is put out by whatever authority his order answers to—a hov's own gudi where the order is a clerical one, a senior member or the cult's own chain where it is secret or militant. The standing is the same wherever it is found.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

No hov, hall or chapter of the order that cast him out will shelter him again, and the counsel, training or protection the order gave its own is closed to him absolutely.

## Privileges

None. A man cast out of the order holds nothing it confers.

## Obligations

None the order recognizes; whatever service bound him ends with the same act that casts him out.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The devotional orders of the faiths of the [[affiliation-asguardian|Asguardian Pantheon]] that confer this standing. Each order gives it the obligations of its own devotion; it is standing within that order, rather than authority over the other orders.
