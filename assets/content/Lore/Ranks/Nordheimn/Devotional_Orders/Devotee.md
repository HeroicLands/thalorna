The ordinary member of a devotional order of the faiths of the Ten, holding the least standing it confers.

## What This Standing Is

A devotee holds an order's basic membership: admitted to its work, whether clerical, secret or martial, and doing what the order was formed to do—study, healing, guardianship, mediation or the sword—without yet holding its full standing or its voice. What exactly admits him differs by order and is rarely recorded past the fact of his membership.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He answers to the order for how he carries out its work, and holds whatever shelter and training the order extends to its own, short of the standing a leader holds.

## Privileges

He draws the order's training, shelter and recognition as one of its own, and does the work the order exists to do.

## Obligations

He serves the order's purpose as its members understand it, and answers to whatever authority the order keeps for failing to.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None recorded.
