The working warden of the Order of the Green Wardens, holding a station or riding circuit relief.

## What This Standing Is

A Sworn Guardian holds a station—typically a particular valley or cluster of farmsteads for years at a time—or occasionally rides as circuit relief. He is sponsored by a sitting Guardian and presented at the order's annual chapter, examined in martial skill and in devotional knowledge of the harvest cycle and the order's vow, and sworn in with none of the candidate standing the order's siblings hold beneath their own sworn rank, because the Green Wardens admit nobody below it.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

The households under his care answer to him for their defense against bandits, beasts, and raiders, and he in turn is bound by the Defended Hearth and the Refused Quarrel: he may not stand aside from harm he could have prevented, and he may not take up a household's private quarrel as his own.

## Privileges

He works alongside the household he is attached to, stands as its armed presence against outside threat, and shares in the order's hospitality across the Nordlands.

## Obligations

He keeps the Defended Hearth, the Refused Quarrel, and the Honored Earth: diligence against outside harm, no part in the household's own disputes, and ordinary labor at the harvest and the calving alongside those he protects.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None above the rank itself short of advancement to [[lore-ordrseniorrnk|Elder Guardian]].

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-gronverdir|Order of the Green Wardens]] alone.
