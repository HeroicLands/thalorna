The worst thing a sworn order can do to one of its own is also the plainest: the council that admitted him takes back the habit and declares his vows void, and the houses that once took him in on his order's name close their doors.

## What This Standing Is

A member is put out by his order's own council or chapter—the **Council of Hands**, a **Chapter**, a conclave of Storm-Captains, or the assembled Fathers of the Halls—which takes back the habit and declares the vows void. Each order words its own expulsion differently, but the act is one: a trial before the body that admitted him, ending in permanent loss of the order's hospitality.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

No house of the order will receive him again: the network of hospitality that let a sworn brother cross the Nordlands on his order's name is closed to him absolutely, and no chapter elsewhere in the order may reopen it without the same body that closed it.

## Privileges

None. A man put from the order holds nothing it confers.

## Obligations

None the order recognizes; the vows that bound him are declared void by the same act that casts him out.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The four Asguardian fighting orders: the [[affiliation-eidhond|Order of the Sworn Hand]], the [[affiliation-gronverdir|Order of the Green Wardens]], the [[affiliation-ordrstrmspkrs|Order of the Storm-Speakers]], and the [[affiliation-thursborn|Order of the Giant's Children]].
