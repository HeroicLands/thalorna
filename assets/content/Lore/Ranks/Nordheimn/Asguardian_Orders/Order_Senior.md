The standing of Senior of a Sworn Order brings candidates to teach, heavier stations to hold and a seat in the body that chooses the order's chair. Each order names the standing in its own way.

## What This Standing Is

A senior member instructs the order's candidates, holds a heavier station than a working member, and sits in the body that chooses the order's chair. [[affiliation-eidhond|The Sworn Hand]] calls him an Elder Hand, permitted to take apprentices and to strip a knight of rank on the spot; [[affiliation-gronverdir|the Green Wardens]] an Elder Guardian, warden of a more important valley; [[affiliation-ordrstrmspkrs|the Storm-Speakers]] a Storm-Captain, leading tactical actions in the field; [[affiliation-thursborn|the Giant's Children]] the Hand of the Host, leading the order's gathered brothers and instructing candidates.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He may act on the order's authority in ways a working member cannot: an Elder Hand may strip a knight of rank on the spot pending trial, and every order's senior sits in the council—the [Council of Hands]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, the [Chapter]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, the conclave of Storm-Captains, the assembled Fathers of the Halls—that elects or chooses the order's own chair.

## Privileges

He instructs candidates, holds the order's heavier stations, and sits in the body that elects the order's chair; an Elder Hand of the Sworn Hand may also strip a knight of rank pending trial.

## Obligations

He answers to his order's own council for how he uses that authority, and continues to hold the field duties—leading actions, holding a station, instructing—that come with the standing.

## Offices Open at This Standing

A seat on the Council of Hands, the Chapter, or the equivalent body of whichever order, and a voice in the conclave that chooses the order's chair.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The Asguardian fighting orders, under each order's own vows and governing body. The standing confers no office in a sibling order.
