"Your life is mine until I judge you fit to compose your first saga," a master tells the pupil he has chosen. "Until the assembled skalds accept it, the Circle owes you nothing. If they reject it three times, we part without recrimination and without rank."

## What This Standing Is

A skald is made by a skald: an apprentice's progression begins when a sitting master identifies a candidate, typically in late childhood, and continues ten to fifteen years through memorization, composition, and presentation. His life is his master's life until the master judges him fit to compose his own first saga, the _gyldra_, delivered before assembled skalds at a **Skaldating**.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is entitled to nothing of the Circle's own—not its hospitality, not the binding weight of a sworn recitation—until his _gyldra_ is accepted; three rejections end the apprenticeship without recrimination but without rank, which is the whole of what the law does to him if his craft does not reach the standard.

## Privileges

He may present a _gyldra_ at a Skaldating, and if it is accepted he is sworn into the Circle by his own master and becomes a sworn skald in his own right.

## Obligations

He owes his master the years of memorization, composition, and presentation the craft demands, and his life is given over to that training until the master judges him ready.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None short of swearing as [[lore-skaldrnk|Skáld]].

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-skaldscrcl|Skalds' Circle]] alone.
