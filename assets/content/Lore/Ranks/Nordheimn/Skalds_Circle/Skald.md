Sworn keeper of a hall's record, entitled to food, shelter and safe passage at any hall, in the Skalds' Circle.

## What This Standing Is

A skáld is sworn into the Circle by his own master once his drápa is accepted at a Skaldating, and wears the silver arm-ring, the skaldhringr, forged at his swearing and bearing the marks of his master and his master's master. He is the trained, sworn keeper of his hall's record, his kingdom's history, and the impartial witness the Asguardian courts summon when a dispute turns on what was said or owed.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

His recitation is treated as documentary evidence: an inheritance dispute, a contested oath, a question of tribute owed three winters ago is settled by his recital, and the courts bind their rulings to what he recites in a way they bind them to no unsworn witness's word.

## Privileges

He is entitled to food, shelter, and safe passage at any hall in the Nordlands, and his recitation carries binding weight before the Asguardian courts.

## Obligations

He must perform if asked, in exchange for the hospitality every hall owes him, and must recite truly against his own employer when the truth of the recital demands it—a duty that has cost skalds their positions and occasionally their lives, and the reason the Circle memorializes any skald killed for honest recital.

## Offices Open at This Standing

The Hirdskáld, a court skald in formal service to a jarl or king, and the Lögskáld, specialized in the recitation of legal precedents and the witnessing of formal proceedings.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-skaldscrcl|Skalds' Circle]] alone.
