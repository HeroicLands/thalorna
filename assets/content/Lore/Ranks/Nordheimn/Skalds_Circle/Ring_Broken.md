The Circle has broken a skald's silver arm-ring perhaps seven times in living memory, and the offense is the same: reciting falsely what the skald was summoned to witness.

## What This Standing Is

A skald formally accused and convicted of breaking the truth of the recital has his silver arm-ring broken in his presence at the next **Skaldating**, the [[affiliation-skaldscrcl|Skalds' Circle]]'s annual gathering. The rarity of the punishment measures how seldom a sworn skald's word is doubted.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

There is no recovery from it: the ring that carried the marks of his master and his master's master back through the Circle's records is broken once and not reforged, and his recitation stops carrying the binding weight the Asguardian courts give a sworn skald's word.

## Privileges

None. A ring-broken skald holds nothing the Circle confers.

## Obligations

None the Circle recognizes; the oath that bound him to accurate recital is ended by the same conviction that breaks his ring.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-skaldscrcl|Skalds' Circle]] alone.
