Nobody enrolls in the [[affiliation-bjartur|faith of Bjart]]. You come to a rite, and from that night you are counted among the faith's people.

## What This Standing Is

The faithful are those who come to the [Blessing of Light]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, a ceremony held preferably in a forest where the priests bless those gathered against darkness and despair, and to the [Night of Dreams]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}, where they share their dreams and visions and seek Bjart's guidance. Coming to these rites is the whole of what admits a person to this standing.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is counted among the faith's people once he has received the Blessing of Light or joined the Night of Dreams, and is reckoned so until the [Aulirakko]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, the faith's council, withholds its accord from him and leaves him [[lore-unaccordedrnk|Unaccorded]] instead.

## Privileges

He may attend the Blessing of Light and the Night of Dreams, and receive the priests' blessing at either rite.

## Obligations

None beyond attending the rites he takes part in; the faith asks nothing further of the faithful short of a call to the deeper service an [[lore-tindesarrnk|Iltorin]] takes up.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None; the faith's offices attach to the clergy above this rank.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-bjartur|Faith of Bjart]] alone.
