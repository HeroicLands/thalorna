The [[affiliation-bjartur|faith of Bjart]] has no officer who pronounces an expulsion, because no single [[lore-calathirrnk|Ansorun]] presides over the [Aulirakko]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}. When the council will not reach accord on a man, its silence toward him does the work.

## What This Standing Is

The Aulirakko, the faith's council of radiance, speaks until it reaches accord on a matter before it, and can withhold that accord from a man it will not recognize. The gathering as a body holds this power, and a man it refuses accord to is cast from the faith's standing by the same body that would otherwise confer it.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is refused what the Aulirakko's accord would confer: no Ansorun will entrust him with the rites, the faithful, or a sacred place, and the council's silence toward him stands in place of any expulsion a presiding officer might otherwise pronounce.

## Privileges

None. A man unaccorded holds nothing this faith confers.

## Obligations

None the faith recognizes; the withholding is the Aulirakko's act rather than a duty laid on him.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-bjartur|Faith of Bjart]] alone.
