Nobody gives an adherent of one of [[lore-hrimthurspr|Hrímthur]]'s scattered factions his standing: he holds it for what he has made or understood, and his faction lets him keep it for as long as it agrees. There is nobody above a faction to say otherwise, because there is nobody above any of them.

## What This Standing Is

An adherent is attached to one of the scattered factions that keep Hrímthur's worship, each tied to a different hof and to the wound it keeps, and does that faction's work. Standing within a faction is a matter of what a person has made or understood, and is claimed rather than conferred by any officer of it.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is reckoned of his faction by what he has made or understood, and a faction that is finished with him puts him out—which, since no hierarchy stands above any faction to appeal to, is a judgment nothing else in the faith can overturn or confirm.

## Privileges

He shares in his faction's work and whatever standing that faction recognizes for what he has made or understood.

## Obligations

He does his faction's work and holds his claimed standing only as long as the faction he is attached to continues to recognize it.

## Offices Open at This Standing

Whatever role a faction has claimed for its own work, informally and without a conferring rite; none is an office of the faith as a whole.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-hrimthur|Faith of Hrímthur]] alone.
