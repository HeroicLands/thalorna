Newly called to Bjartr's service, learning dream-reading, healing and the tending of groves under a Calathir, in the faith of Bjartr.

## What This Standing Is

A tindësar, "star-seeker," is newly called to the faith's service and learns dream-reading, healing, and the tending of sacred groves under a Calathir's guidance. The bond is closer to apprentice and mentor than to subordinate and superior, though it is still the Calathir who admits and teaches.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is called and taught rather than merely faithful: he stands apart from those who come only for the Blessing of Light or the Night of Dreams, and his standing rests on the Calathir who guides him rather than on attendance alone.

## Privileges

He learns dream-reading, healing, and the tending of sacred groves directly from a Calathir, standing that no ordinary member of the faithful holds.

## Obligations

He owes his teaching Calathir the years of learning the calling asks, in the apprentice-and-mentor bond the faith holds rather than a subordinate's obedience.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None short of entrustment as [[lore-calathirrnk|Calathir]].

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-bjartr|Faith of Bjartr]] alone.
