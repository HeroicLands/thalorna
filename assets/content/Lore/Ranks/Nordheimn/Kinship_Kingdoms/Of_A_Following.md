Free and of no clan, answering through a following rather than through kinship, in the five kingdoms and the Jarldom of Stormveld.

## What This Standing Is

A lidmadr is free but holds no clan to answer for him, and reaches the ting through a following instead of through kinship: a manumitted thrall still bound in obligation to his former master, a drengr without a hall to seat him, or a freeman of another people settled under Nordmal law. Each stands apart from the polity's clans and each is vouched for by someone other than a kinsman.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is free, but not yet reckoned a full man of the ting: his voice is not heard on the assembly's floor, and where the law requires a clan's word behind a man's oath, his following stands in the clan's place.

## Privileges

He may work, travel, and hold property under the polity's law, and the man or company he follows answers for him where a clan would answer for one of its own.

## Obligations

He owes the following that vouches for him whatever service or bond first placed him there—a former master's claim, a captain's muster, or the terms his settlement was granted on—and he answers to it rather than to a clan.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None. A lidmadr's obligations run to the man or company he follows, not to an office of the polity.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The five kingdoms—[[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Nordheim]], [[affiliation-kingdomlgn|Malagna]], [[affiliation-kingdmnrgd|Norgaad]], [[affiliation-kingdmtrgd|Targud]], and [[affiliation-kngdmvthgrd|Vithgard]]—hold this standing under one Nordmal law, and the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] holds it under the same name.
