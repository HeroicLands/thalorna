Full clan membership, holding odal land and a voice at the ting, in the five kingdoms and the Jarldom of Stormveld.

## What This Standing Is

A bóndi is a free farmer of full clan membership, holding his own odal land—land held by inheritance and not by grant. He attends the ting, speaks there in his own name, and is heard in the assembly that settles disputes and proclaims law.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

The ting's decision binds him and binds the jarl over him alike, and a jarl who disregards it discovers quickly how much of his authority was consent. This is the polity's full member: the north's master, priest, and clan member are one level, and the bóndi is the Nordmal ladder's floor for that level.

## Privileges

He bears arms, speaks at the ting in his own name, and his odal land is his by inheritance rather than by any lord's grant, so no jarl may take it from him at will.

## Obligations

He answers the muster when it is called and pays the dues his kingdom sets. Where a kingdom holds a standing watch—Targud's rotating fort-duty against the Grukar is the worked case—the muster this rank owes is that watch.

## Offices Open at This Standing

A hersvald's muster, a landvördr's witness at a mark-walk, and a hirdman's oath—the last of which raises him to [[lore-hringberirnk|Hringberi]], because the ring a hirdman takes comes with the higher standing.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The five kingdoms—[[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Nordheim]], [[affiliation-kingdomlgn|Malagna]], [[affiliation-kingdmnrgd|Norgaad]], [[affiliation-kingdmtrgd|Targud]], and [[affiliation-kngdmvthgrd|Vithgard]]—hold this standing under one Nordmal law, and the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] holds it under the same name.
