A bundi's odel land is his because his father's was. No jarl granted it, so no jarl can take it back at will, and when the ting rules, the ruling binds the jarl over him as it binds him.

## What This Standing Is

A bundi is a free farmer of full clan membership, holding his own odel land—land held by inheritance and not by grant. He attends the ting, speaks there in his own name, and is heard in the assembly that settles disputes and proclaims law.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

The ting's decision binds him and binds the jarl over him alike, and a jarl who ignores it has quarreled with every bundi in his province. The bundi is the polity's full member: the north counts its masters, its priests and its clan members at one level, and the bundi stands at the floor of that level on the Nordmal ladder.

## Privileges

He bears arms, speaks at the ting in his own name, and his odel land is his by inheritance rather than by any lord's grant, so no jarl may take it from him at will.

## Obligations

He answers the muster when it is called and pays the dues his kingdom sets. Where a kingdom holds a standing watch—the rotating fort-duty of [[affiliation-kingdmtrgd|Targud]] against the [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukar]] is the worked case—the muster this rank owes is that watch.

## Offices Open at This Standing

A hersvald's muster, a landvordr's witness at a mark-walk, and a hirvman's oath—the last of which raises him to [[lore-hringberirnk|Hringberi]], because the ring a hirvman takes comes with the higher standing.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The five kingdoms—[[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Nordheim]], [[affiliation-kingdomlgn|Malagna]], [[affiliation-kingdmnrgd|Norgaad]], [[affiliation-kingdmtrgd|Targud]], and [[affiliation-kngdmvthgrd|Vithgard]]—hold this standing under one Nordmal law, and the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] holds it under the same name.
