Owned outright, owing labor rather than rent and holding no voice at the ting, in the five kingdoms and the Jarldom of Stormveld.

## What This Standing Is

A thrall is a man owned outright, bought or taken rather than born free. The Nordmen buy people at Skarnethra and along the Frostmark road, and what a thrall owes his owner is labor itself rather than a share of what his labor produces.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He bears no weapon by right, and no voice reaches the ting through him: he is not counted among the free men whose speech and hearing settle a dispute, and nobody stands bound to answer for harm done to him the way a free man's kin would answer for harm done to a free man.

## Privileges

None held in his own name. Whatever shelter, food, or protection he has comes from his owner's standing rather than his own.

## Obligations

Labor, at his owner's direction, without the wage or the rent a free tenant would hold instead. He answers no muster and speaks at no ting, because neither is opened to him.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The five kingdoms—[[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Nordheim]], [[affiliation-kingdomlgn|Malagna]], [[affiliation-kingdmnrgd|Norgaad]], [[affiliation-kingdmtrgd|Targud]], and [[affiliation-kngdmvthgrd|Vithgard]]—hold this standing under one Nordmal law, and the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] holds it under the same name.
