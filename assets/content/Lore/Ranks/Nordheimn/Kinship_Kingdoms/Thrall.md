Thrall is a condition of ownership, not a sentence the ting hands down. A thrall is owned outright, owes labor itself and holds no voice at the ting.

## What This Standing Is

A thrall is owned outright, bought or taken rather than born free. The Nordmen buy people at [[place-kruldheim|Kruldheim]] and along the [[place-nalthmark|Nalthmark]] road, and what a thrall owes an owner is labor itself rather than a share of what the labor produces. In [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Stormveld]] the standing is the condition of some 120,000 native Aelwythans, whose plain the Nordmen took. A thrall who is freed becomes a [[lore-lidmadrrnk|lidmadr]], still bound to the former owner.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

A thrall bears no weapon by right, and no voice reaches the ting through them: they are not counted among the free whose speech and hearing settle a dispute, and nobody stands bound to answer for harm done to them the way a free person's kin would answer for harm done to a free person.

## Privileges

None held in their own name. Whatever shelter, food, or protection a thrall has comes from the owner's standing rather than their own.

## Obligations

Labor, at the owner's direction, without the wage or the rent a free tenant would hold instead. A thrall answers no muster and speaks at no ting, because neither is opened to them.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The five kingdoms—[[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Nordheim]], [[affiliation-kingdomlgn|Malagna]], [[affiliation-kingdmnrgd|Norgaad]], [[affiliation-kingdmtrgd|Targud]], and [[affiliation-kngdmvthgrd|Vithgard]]—hold this standing under one Nordmal law, and the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] holds it under the same name.
