Outlawed at the ting and set beyond the law's protection, in the five kingdoms and the Jarldom of Stormveld.

## What This Standing Is

The ting casts a man out for an offense grave enough that the assembly refuses him its peace—killing within a truce, oath-breaking before witnesses, or a crime no wergild answers. The assembly speaks the sentence; no lesser body can pronounce it and no lesser body can lift it.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

Harm done to a níding is answered for by nobody. Killing him carries no wergild, hiding him is itself an offense, and no hall may feed him without answering to the ting that cast him out.

## Privileges

None. A níding holds no right the ting protects, and no rank below him can be reduced further—he stands outside the ladder rather than at its foot.

## Obligations

None the law recognizes; obligation presumes standing, and a níding has none. What is owed the ting by everyone else—the muster, the dues, an oath's truth—is not owed by him, because he is not addressed by it.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The five kingdoms—[[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Nordheim]], [[affiliation-kingdomlgn|Malagna]], [[affiliation-kingdmnrgd|Norgaad]], [[affiliation-kingdmtrgd|Targud]], and [[affiliation-kngdmvthgrd|Vithgard]]—hold this standing under one Nordmal law, and the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] holds it under the same name.
