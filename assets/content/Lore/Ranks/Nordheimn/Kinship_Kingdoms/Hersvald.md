The clan's own head, leading a district's men in war and speaking for them in peace by their consent, in the five kingdoms and the Jarldom of Stormveld.

## What This Standing Is

A hersvald is a local chieftain who leads the men of a district in war and speaks for them in peace, holding the standing by their consent rather than by any king's grant. Where a jarl answers for a province the crown has given him, a hersvald answers for a clan that chose him.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

Consent rather than grant is the whole of what sets him apart from a jarl: a weak hersvald's own grown sons can find themselves led instead by a cousin the district judges better, because the standing is held on the district's continued agreement and not on descent.

## Privileges

He leads the district's muster, speaks for it at the ting and before a jarl, and answers for its peace in his own name.

## Obligations

He answers to the men who hold him in his place: their consent placed him there and their consent can move to another. He owes the district his leadership in war and his voice in peace in return for the standing it gives him.

## Offices Open at This Standing

The Lawspeaker's seat at a kingdom's ting, the Hirdstjóri's command of the king's household troop, and the Landvördr's collection of a district's dues—none of which require a jarl's grant to hold.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The five kingdoms—[[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Nordheim]], [[affiliation-kingdomlgn|Malagna]], [[affiliation-kingdmnrgd|Norgaad]], [[affiliation-kingdmtrgd|Targud]], and [[affiliation-kngdmvthgrd|Vithgard]]—hold this standing under one Nordmal law, and the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] holds it under the same name.
