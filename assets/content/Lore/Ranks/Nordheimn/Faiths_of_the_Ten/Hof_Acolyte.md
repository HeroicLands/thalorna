Given to a hof young and years short of the priesthood, in the eight faiths of the Ten that keep the blót.

## What This Standing Is

A hofsmadr is given to a hof young, taking its observances and its labor for years short of the priesthood a godi holds. The hof admits a candidate on its own terms and may dismiss him on the same terms: a rank the hof confers, and not a stage every young Nordman passes through alike.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is bound to the hof's own rule rather than to the ting or to a clan: the hof that admitted him may set him aside without the ting's involvement, in a way the assembly's own casting-out of a níding cannot be undone by a private decision.

## Privileges

He learns the observances of his hof under its godi, and shares its blót with its people as a blótmadr does.

## Obligations

He owes the hof the labor and the years of observance his admission requires, and holds no standing to refuse them without leaving the hof's service.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None; the hof's offices attach to the ordained priesthood above this rank.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The eight faiths of the Ten that keep the blót: [[affiliation-odvar|Ódvar]], [[affiliation-thrunvald|Thrúnvald]], [[affiliation-eidgar|Eidgar]], [[affiliation-frodvin|Fródvin]], [[affiliation-solrun|Sólrún]], [[affiliation-velgrimr|Vélgrímr]], [[affiliation-svartbrandr|Svartbrandr]], and [[affiliation-motefnir|Mótefnir]], each under its own hof and its own godi.
