Not every young member of the [[lore-nordheimnclt|Nordheimn]] people spends years at a hov. A hofsmadur (hov acolyte) is given to a hov young, takes up its labor and learns its observances, and stands years short of the priesthood a gudi holds.

## What This Standing Is

The hov admits a candidate on its own terms and may dismiss him on the same terms; the rank belongs to the hov to confer.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is bound to the hov's own rule rather than to the ting or to a clan: the hov that admitted him may set him aside without the ting's involvement, in a way the assembly's own casting-out of a niding cannot be undone by a private decision.

## Privileges

He learns the observances of his hov under its gudi, and shares its blet with its people as a bletmadur does.

## Obligations

He owes the hov the labor and the years of observance his admission requires, and holds no standing to refuse them without leaving the hov's service.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None; the hov's offices attach to the ordained priesthood above this rank.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The faiths of the [[affiliation-asguardian|Ten]] that keep the blet, each under its own hov. The standing belongs to that hov's rites and clergy; it does not extend to devotions whose admission and authority rest on other observances.
