Declined the offering and the shared meal by a hof's godi, in the seven faiths of the Ten that keep the blót.

## What This Standing Is

The godi of a hof declines a man the offering and the shared meal that make up the blót. A blót not attended is a blót not accomplished, and because the rite ties a man to his clan as well as to his god, being refused it severs both at once.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is cut from the shared meal that binds a hof's people to one another and to their god: no godi of that faith will offer for him, and the clan standing the blót also confirms is left unconfirmed alongside the religious one.

## Privileges

None. A man refused the blót holds nothing this faith confers.

## Obligations

None the faith recognizes; the refusal is the hof's act and not a duty laid on him.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The seven faiths of the Ten that keep the blót: [[affiliation-odinn|Ódvar]], [[affiliation-thorr|Thrúnvald]], [[affiliation-tyr|Eidgar]], [[affiliation-freyr|Fródvin]], [[affiliation-freyja|Sólrún]], [[affiliation-loki|Vélgrímr]], and [[affiliation-surtr|Svartbrandr]], each under its own hof and its own godi.
