Refused the Blet is the standing a man holds when a gudi at the door of his hov refuses him the offering and a place at the meal. The blet ties a man to his clan as well as to his god, so the refusal cuts both at once.

## What This Standing Is

The gudi of a hov can decline a man the offering and the shared meal that make up the blet, and a blet he does not attend is a blet not accomplished.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is cut from the shared meal that binds a hov's people to one another and to their god: no gudi of that faith will offer for him, and the clan standing the blet also confirms is left unconfirmed alongside the religious one.

## Privileges

None. A man refused the blet holds nothing this faith confers.

## Obligations

None the faith recognizes; the refusal is the hov's act and not a duty laid on him.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The faiths of the [[affiliation-asguardian|Asguardian Pantheon]] that keep the blet, each under its own hov. The standing belongs to that hov's rites and clergy; it does not extend to devotions whose admission and authority rest on other observances.
