One who attends the blót, the laity of the seven faiths of the Ten that keep it.

## What This Standing Is

A blótmadr is one who attends the blót—the sacrifice and shared meal a hof's godi offers—and is the whole of what most of the faithful ever are. Admission is the offering itself: whoever is not refused it and comes to a hof's rite holds this standing.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is reckoned of the hof's people once he has shared its blót, and the offering that admits him is the same offering a godi can withhold to cast a man out; short of that refusal, attendance is the whole of the law's test.

## Privileges

He may attend the blót at his hof, share the meal it offers, and be counted among that hof's people.

## Obligations

He owes the hof the offering the blót asks—the goods, the labor, or the silver a rite calls for—and his attendance when a hof holds its rite.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None; the offices of a faith of the Ten attach to the priesthood above this rank.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The seven faiths of the Ten that keep the blót: [[affiliation-odvar|Ódvar]], [[affiliation-thrunvald|Thrúnvald]], [[affiliation-eidgar|Eidgar]], [[affiliation-frodvin|Fródvin]], [[affiliation-solrun|Sólrún]], [[affiliation-velgrimr|Vélgrímr]], and [[affiliation-svartbrandr|Svartbrandr]], each under its own hof and its own godi.
