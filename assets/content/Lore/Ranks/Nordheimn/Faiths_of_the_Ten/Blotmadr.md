Most of the faithful never become more than this. A Bletmadr comes to the hov when the blet is held, brings the offering it asks and shares the meal, and that is the whole of what the standing requires.

## What This Standing Is

A bletmadr attends the blet, the sacrifice and shared meal a hov's gudi offers. Admission is the offering itself: whoever is not refused it and comes to a hov's rite holds this standing.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is reckoned of the hov's people once he has shared its blet, and the offering that admits him is the same offering a gudi can withhold to cast a man out; short of that refusal, attendance is the whole of the law's test.

## Privileges

He may attend the blet at his hov, share the meal it offers, and be counted among that hov's people.

## Obligations

He owes the hov the offering the blet asks—the goods, the labor, or the silver a rite calls for—and his attendance when a hov holds its rite.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None; the offices of a faith of the Ten attach to the priesthood above this rank.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The faiths of the [[affiliation-asguardian|Asguardian Pantheon]] that keep the blet, each under its own hov. The standing belongs to that hov's rites and clergy; it does not extend to devotions whose admission and authority rest on other observances.
