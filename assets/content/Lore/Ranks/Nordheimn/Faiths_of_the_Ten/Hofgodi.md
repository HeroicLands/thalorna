No hov of the faith stands unless a priest of Hofgudi / Hofgudja standing has consecrated it, and no gudi keeps the blet unless a hofgudi has ordained him. That is the whole reach of the standing, and nothing in the faith sits over it.

## What This Standing Is

A hofgudi or hofgudja consecrates a hov, ordains the gudar who serve under it, and performs the great blet at [[lore-jol|Jel]], [[lore-sumarmal|Somarmál]], [[lore-midsumar|Midsomar]], or [[lore-veturnetur|Veturnetur]]. Nothing religious sits above this standing: no rank of the land reaches inside a faith, and no faith answers to a pontiff.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

His consecration is what admits a hov to the faith's law and his ordination is what admits a gudi to the priesthood: a hov no hofgudi has consecrated holds no standing to offer the blet, and a gudi no hofgudi has ordained holds no standing to keep one.

## Privileges

He consecrates hovs, ordains gudar, and performs the great blet at the turning of a season—acts no ordinary gudi's ordination extends to.

## Obligations

He answers for the hovs and gudar he has consecrated and ordained, and keeps the great blet at each season's turning without fail.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None above it within the faith; the Landsgudi who hallows the ting island at [[place-domsey|Domsey]] is an office of the King of All Clans assembly rather than a further rank of any one faith.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The faiths of the [[affiliation-asguardian|Ten]] that keep the blet, each under its own hov. The standing belongs to that hov's rites and clergy; it does not extend to devotions whose admission and authority rest on other observances.
