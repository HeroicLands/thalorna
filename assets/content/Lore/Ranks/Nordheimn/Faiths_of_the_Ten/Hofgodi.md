The high priest who consecrates a hof and performs the great blót at a season's turning, in the seven faiths of the Ten that keep it.

## What This Standing Is

A hofgodi or hofgydja consecrates a hof, ordains the godar who serve under it, and performs the great blót at Jól, Sumarmál, Midsumar, or Vetrnaetr. Nothing religious sits above this standing: no rank of the land reaches inside a faith, and no faith answers to a pontiff.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

His consecration is what admits a hof to the faith's law and his ordination is what admits a godi to the priesthood: a hof no hofgodi has consecrated holds no standing to offer the blót, and a godi no hofgodi has ordained holds no standing to keep one.

## Privileges

He consecrates hofs, ordains godar, and performs the great blót at the turning of a season—acts no ordinary godi's ordination extends to.

## Obligations

He answers for the hofs and godar he has consecrated and ordained, and keeps the great blót at each season's turning without fail.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None above it within the faith; the Landsgodi who hallows the ting island at Dómsey is an office of the King of All Clans assembly rather than a further rank of any one faith.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The seven faiths of the Ten that keep the blót: [[affiliation-odvar|Ódvar]], [[affiliation-thrunvald|Thrúnvald]], [[affiliation-eidgar|Eidgar]], [[affiliation-frodvin|Fródvin]], [[affiliation-solrun|Sólrún]], [[affiliation-velgrimr|Vélgrímr]], and [[affiliation-svartbrandr|Svartbrandr]], each under its own hof.
