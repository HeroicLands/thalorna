One of the company, carrying a charge and a share of the kill, in Wuldjagâr erund Gárskald.

## What This Standing Is

A hunter is one of the company, carrying a charge on the hunt and a share of the kill it brings home. All the hunters are equal in standing; what differs from one to the next is the charge he carries—tracking, spear, healing, hounds, or the ground he is sent ahead to scout—rather than any rank above another hunter.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is trusted with the spear at the kill and consulted before the company commits to a season's hunt, in a way a new hunter is not: the company's discipline binds him, and the forest does not open again to a hunter broken from it for breaking that discipline.

## Privileges

He carries his charge on the hunt, is trusted with a spear at the kill, and shares in the meat and hide the company brings home.

## Obligations

He keeps the company's discipline on the hunt, and answers for the charge he carries—the party he tracks for, the wounded he tends, the hounds he wardens, or the ground he scouts.

## Offices Open at This Standing

The Huntmaster's leadership of the company, the Track-Leader's command of a party working its own line, the Healer's keeping of the wounded, the Spear-Bearer's charge at the kill, the Skinner's charge of the take, the Dog-Keeper's wardship of the hounds, and the Scout's charge of unknown ground.
