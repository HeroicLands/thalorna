A Vandár is recently inducted and still learning the ways, watched for signs of weakness, in [[affiliation-wldjgrrndgrskld|Wuljagar erund Garskald]].

## What This Standing Is

A Vandár is recently inducted into the company and still learning its ways—carrying, skinning, hauling, and watching the Wurlár and the proven [[lore-erthjagarrnk|Erthjagár]]s at their work. [[lore-varokhiclt|Varokh]] hunters cannot afford squeamishness, and a Vandár is watched by the others for any sign that he cannot yet meet that standard.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is not yet trusted with the spear at the kill: he holds none of the company's authority and is admitted on the Wurlár's own terms, which can withdraw him from the hunt as readily as they brought him onto it.

## Privileges

He hunts with the company, learns its ways under its Wurlár, and shares in the meat the company brings home to the clan.

## Obligations

He carries, skins, and hauls as the company's work requires, and submits to the Wurlár's judgment of when he is ready to be trusted further.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None short of advancement to [[lore-erthjagarrnk|Erthjagár]].

## Where This Standing Is Held

[[affiliation-wldjgrrndgrskld|Wuljagar erund Garskald]] alone.
