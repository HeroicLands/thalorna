Recently inducted and still learning the ways, watched for signs of weakness, in Wuldjagâr erund Gárskald.

## What This Standing Is

A new hunter is recently inducted into the company and still learning its ways—carrying, skinning, hauling, and watching the huntmaster and the proven hunters at their work. Varokh hunters cannot afford squeamishness, and a new hunter is watched by the others for any sign that he cannot yet meet that standard.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is not yet trusted with the spear at the kill: he holds none of the company's authority and is admitted on the huntmaster's own terms, which can withdraw him from the hunt as readily as they brought him onto it.

## Privileges

He hunts with the company, learns its ways under its huntmaster, and shares in the meat the company brings home to the clan.

## Obligations

He carries, skins, and hauls as the company's work requires, and submits to the huntmaster's judgment of when he is ready to be trusted further.

## Offices Open at This Standing

None short of advancement to [[lore-hunterrnk|Hunter]].
