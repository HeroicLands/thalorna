One of the company, carrying a charge and a share of the kill, in Wuldjagár erund Gárskald.

## What This Standing Is

An Erthjagár is one of the company, carrying a charge on the hunt and a share of the kill it brings home. All the Erthjagárs are equal in standing; what differs from one to the next is the charge he carries—tracking, spear, healing, hounds, or the ground he is sent ahead to scout—rather than any rank above another Erthjagár.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

He is trusted with the spear at the kill and consulted before the company commits to a season's hunt, in a way a [[lore-vandarrnk|Vandár]] is not: the company's discipline binds him, and the forest does not open again to an Erthjagár broken from it for breaking that discipline.

## Privileges

He carries his charge on the hunt, is trusted with a spear at the kill, and shares in the meat and hide the company brings home.

## Obligations

He keeps the company's discipline on the hunt, and answers for the charge he carries—the party he tracks for, the wounded he tends, the hounds he wardens, or the ground he scouts.

## Offices Open at This Standing

The Wulfár's leadership of the company, the Erthrith's command of a party working its own line, the Sundmund's keeping of the wounded, the Brunjthúl's charge at the kill, the Ermmund's charge of the take, the Thrámund's wardship of the hounds, and the Dunkelrith's charge of unknown ground.

## Where This Standing Is Held

[[affiliation-wldjgrrndgrskld|Wuldjagár erund Gárskald]] alone.
