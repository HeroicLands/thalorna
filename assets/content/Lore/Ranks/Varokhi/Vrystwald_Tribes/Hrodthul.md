Bears a charge on the renown a deed has already won, assisting the elders in everyday duties, in the Vrystwald Tribes.

## What This Standing Is

A Hródthúl is drawn from the [[lore-druthmundrnk|Druthmund]] for a renown some deed has already won them, and assists the village's three elders in everyday duties rather than holding a seat of their own. The standing is few against the whole village: a Shaman takes one apprentice, a hunting band raises one leader, and an elder takes on whatever other help a seat's work requires, and no more than that.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

Their word carries further than a Druthmund's in the one matter their charge covers, and the elder they assist answers for what they do in carrying it, the way a kindred answers for its own.

## Privileges

The Shaman's apprentice keeps the herb-lore and the feud-cords at the elders' side and is taught what the Shaman knows. A hunting band's leader commands the band that raised him. An elder's other assistant carries whatever else a seat's work requires, and is heard before the elder who relies on them decides.

## Obligations

They answer to the elder whose work they assist for how that work is carried, and hold no vote where the three seats decide, however long they have carried it.

## Offices Open at This Standing

The Shaman's apprentice, who succeeds to the Shaman's seat by the Shaman's own designation; a hunting band's leader, who succeeds to the War Chief's seat by what he has done; and an elder's other assistant, who is acclaimed to the Other Chief's seat by the people. All three seats are filled from this one rung, each by its own measure.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vrystwald Tribes]] alone.
