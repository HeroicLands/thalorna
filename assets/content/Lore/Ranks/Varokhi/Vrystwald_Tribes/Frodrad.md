A Frodrád holds one of the three seats that govern a village together, so that the village's vote never ties, in the [[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vristwald Tribes]].

## What This Standing Is

A Frodrád holds one of the three seats a village divides its governance among: the Shaman, who tends the totems and speaks for the spirits; the War Chief, the Harthúl, who leads the warband and speaks for the village before other tribes and before settled powers; and the Other Chief, the Theodár, who answers for everything that is neither war nor the spirit's business. Three seats and not one is deliberate, so that a vote never ties, and none of the three stands above the other two: a people who will not let anybody answer to anybody keeps no single head.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

All three seats are filled from the [[lore-hrodthulrnk|Hrodthúl]], and each is reached by its own path and held on its own terms. The Shaman is chosen by the Shaman who held it before; the War Chief is raised by what he or she has done, by acclamation rather than by a council's preference; and the Other Chief is acclaimed by the people. Nothing carries any of the three seats to a child by descent alone, and a village that withdraws its support from a seat's holder can settle it on somebody else.

## Privileges

The Shaman speaks for the spirits in every matter the village brings before them. The War Chief leads the warband, speaks for the village before other tribes and before settled powers, and may be chosen Rikár when the elders of several villages gather to answer an imminent danger, keeping that gathering's peace and the order of its speaking. On the rare occasion the elders lay their fragments of the [Sundered Talisman]{.name data-type="miscgear"} together and acclaim one War Chief as the common Harthúl, that command over several villages dissolves the moment the danger that raised it passes. The Other Chief keeps the customs the tribes hold in common and arbitrates where two tribes' customs differ, as Domrád, and records the blood owed and the blood paid between kindreds, as Skathár.

## Obligations

Each answers to the village for the charge their own seat carries, and a matter that touches all three is settled by all three together rather than by any one of them alone.

## Offices Open at This Standing

The Shaman, the War Chief, called the Harthúl, and the Other Chief, called the Theodár—the three seats themselves, and nothing stands between reaching one and holding it.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vristwald Tribes]] alone.
