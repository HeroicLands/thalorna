Of a kindred's blood, entitled to its protection, its feud and its share, in the Vrystwald Tribes.

## What This Standing Is

A kinsman is of a kindred's blood, entitled to its protection, its feud, and its share, and holds a true name earned by a deed of significance at eth-kethrun, the rite that marks a Varokh's passage to adulthood. This is the same standing for a man and a woman alike: the man's voice at the moot and the woman's hand on the household, its stores, its stock, and its bondservants are two halves of one full membership.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

His oath spoken before a war-band is the whole of his security, in a people with no contract, no registry, and no written deed to fall back on instead: a kinsman's word is trusted the way a bondservant's or a taken-in stranger's cannot yet be, and his kindred owes him the vengeance and the hospitality that reach nobody outside their blood.

## Privileges

He speaks at the moot in his own name, shares his kindred's protection and its feud, and, if a woman, holds the household, its stores, its stock, and its reckoning of debts in her own right.

## Obligations

He answers for his own oath before the war-band that heard it, and owes his kindred whatever the moot's decisions and the feud's demands lay on every kinsman alike.

## Offices Open at This Standing

The Host-Caller, who summons the common muster when a threat concerns every tribe; the Truce-Warden, who holds the peace at the council and the seasonal markets; the Water-Warden, keeper of the wells, springs, or pastures the tribes share; the Guide, holder of the routes; and the Envoy, sent to treat with a settled power.

## Where This Standing Is Held

The [[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vrystwald Tribes]] alone.
