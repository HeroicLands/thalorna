## Where This Standing Is Held

30 bodies in Thalorna name this standing, at level 8 of their ladders. **First of the Council** stands in 13, **Speaker of the Council** stands in 6, **Sabhāpati** stands in 4, and **First Senator** stands in 2. It also stands singly, in one body each, as **First of the Seven**, **Speaker for the Island**, **Speaker of the Cols**, **Speaker of the Loom-Council**, and **Winter-speaker**.
