## Where This Standing Is Held

108 bodies in Thalorna name this standing, at levels 2, 3 of their ladders. **Lay Faithful** stands in 32, **Petitioner** stands in 19, **Upāsaka** stands in 15, **Fideles** stands in 14, **Blood-Giver** and **Debt-Bearer** each stand in 11, and **Lay** stands in 8. It also stands singly, in one body each, as **Admirer**, **Bereaved**, **Debt-Bound**, **Lamp-tender**, and **Lay Kaliharan**, plus 5 more.
