## Where This Standing Is Held

11 bodies in Thalorna name this standing, at levels 5, 6, 7 of their ladders, and each spells it in its own words: **Councillor**, **Assistant**, **House Head**, **Senator**, **Chieftain**, **Delegate**, **Chair**, **Matriarch of a House**, **Prince of a House**, **Clan-Chief**, **Elector**.
