## Where This Standing Is Held

55 bodies in Thalorna name this standing, at levels 5, 6, 7 of their ladders. **Councillor** stands in 23, **House Head** stands in 13, **Chieftain** and **Senator** each stand in 7, **Delegate** stands in 6, **Sabhāsad** stands in 4, and **Chair** stands in 2. It also stands singly, in one body each, as **Argentean**, **Council Officer**, **Curator**, **Elector**, and **Great Hunter**, plus 7 more.
