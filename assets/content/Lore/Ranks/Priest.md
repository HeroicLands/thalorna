## Where This Standing Is Held

114 bodies in Thalorna name this standing, at level 5 of their ladders. **Lem'Nelgir** stands in 18, **Hērbad** stands in 13, **Scribe or Priest** stands in 12, **Ch'ul'bal** and **Ritūja** each stand in 11, **Priest** stands in 10, and **Ácārya** and **Sacerdotes** each stand in 2. It also stands singly, in one body each, as **Calathir**, **Circle of the Threshold**, **Custodes Liminis**, **Custodes Viarum**, and **Darshaka**, plus 32 more.
