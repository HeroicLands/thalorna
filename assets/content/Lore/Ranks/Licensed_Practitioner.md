Licensed by a tradition to work its art on its own authority, and answerable to it for the working.

## What This Standing Is

The ordinary working practitioner of a mystical tradition. Having learned the
forms of a domain and been examined on them, the practitioner is licensed to work
them unsupervised: to speak a formula and to set it down, to work the technique
the tradition exists to teach, to do the thing an initiate may only watch. A
master sits above, holding the restricted forms and the right to teach; this
standing holds the ordinary forms and the right to use them.

The licence is granted on demonstrated exactness rather than on time served,
because a working outlasts the hand that made it. What is set down stands, and
stands wrong where it was set down wrong.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

A failed working is traced to the practitioner who made it, and the tradition
answers for it to whoever was harmed before recovering what it paid from him. The
licence is the thing at risk: it is withdrawn for a working outside its terms, and
withdrawal ends the practice without reaching anything else the person holds.

## Privileges

To work the tradition's art in its name, to charge for the work, and to be backed
by the tradition for as long as the method used is the method declared. Where a
tradition certifies work, this standing's certification holds until a master
contradicts it.

## Obligations

To hold the forms exactly as taught, to refuse work the licence does not cover,
and to answer for everything set down. Where a working has failed and stands
failed, the practitioner who made it is the one sent to correct it.
