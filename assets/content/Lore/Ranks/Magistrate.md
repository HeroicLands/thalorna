## Where This Standing Is Held

36 bodies in Thalorna name this standing, at levels 6, 7, 8 of their ladders. **Registrar** stands in 19, **Magistrate** and **Strategos** each stand in 8, and **Governor** stands in 4. It also stands singly, in one body each, as **Aedile**, **Iudex**, **Naukrátissa**, **Praetar**, and **Quaestor**, plus 1 more.
