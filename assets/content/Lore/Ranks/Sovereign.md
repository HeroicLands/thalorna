## Where This Standing Is Held

92 bodies in Thalorna name this standing, at level 8 of their ladders. **Gar-Aû** stands in 44, **K'uhul Ajaw** stands in 12, **Sovereign** stands in 8, **King** stands in 6, **Mahārāja** stands in 5, and **Emperor** stands in 3. It also stands singly, in one body each, as **Daudr Konungr/Konungrkvinde**, **Dómskonungr/Konungrkvinde**, **Eldjötunn Konungr/Konungrkvinde**, **Frídr Konungrkvinde**, and **Gródur Konungr/Konungrkvinde**, plus 9 more.
