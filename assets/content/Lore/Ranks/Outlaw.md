## Where This Standing Is Held

59 bodies in Thalorna name this standing, at level 0 of their ladders. **Exile** stands in 12, **Atimos**, **Marked**, and **Outlaw** each stand in 8, **Kinless** stands in 7, **Níding** stands in 6, and **Attainted** and **Exiled** each stand in 2.
