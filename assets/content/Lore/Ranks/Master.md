## Where This Standing Is Held

45 bodies in Thalorna name this standing, at levels 4, 5 of their ladders. **Guild Master** stands in 13, **Adept** and **Khel-Gebu** each stand in 8, **Master of the Art** stands in 7, **Master** stands in 3, and **Guildmaster** stands in 2. It also stands singly, in one body each, as **Ácārya**, **Adepti**, **Chartered Craftsman**, **Keeper of a Line**, and **Loom-Master**, plus 6 more.
