Changed by a body's central rite and holding standing by what the rite made of them.

## What This Standing Is

A body whose doctrine turns on a passage—death and return, interception at a
threshold, a reversal worked on a living person—can put a member through that
passage rather than only teaching it. One who goes through and comes out whole
holds a standing no office carries and no examination confers. The rite is the
qualification, the name is taken from the rite, and the standing sits above the
body's ordained ranks, because what the ordained perform on others has been
performed on this person.

Few survive it and fewer are offered it, so a body may hold the standing for a
generation with nobody in it.

## How the Law Treats a Person Here

No vote removes this standing, because no act undoes the passage. What a body can
dispute is whether the passage was completed, and that is the only dispute
available: a claim that the rite was survived is met by a claim that it was not,
and the body settles it the way it settles doctrine.

## Privileges

The authority of a demonstrated case. On any question the rite bears on, this
standing is heard before the ordained, and what it reports of the passage is the
body's evidence for its own doctrine.

## Obligations

To remain available as that evidence, and to keep whatever continuing observance
the passage left requiring maintenance. Where the body is proscribed, this
standing is its most dangerous asset, so secrecy binds harder here than lower
down.
