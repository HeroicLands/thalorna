## Where This Standing Is Held

87 bodies in Thalorna name this standing, at levels 6, 7 of their ladders. **Halzi'a** and **Iru'palu** each stand in 44, **Ajaw** and **Sahal** each stand in 12, **Greater Nobility** stands in 8, **Jarl** stands in 7, **Sāmanta** stands in 4, and **Great House** stands in 2.
