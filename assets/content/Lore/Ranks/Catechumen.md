## Where This Standing Is Held

41 bodies in Thalorna name this standing, at levels 1, 2, 3 of their ladders. **Uninitiated** stands in 14, **Water-Marked** stands in 11, **Novice** stands in 10, **Postulant** stands in 9, **Aspirant** stands in 7, and **Marked** and **Untaught** each stand in 2. It also stands singly, in one body each, as **Grieving**, **Hearer**, **Listener**, **Petitioner**, and **Supplicant**.
