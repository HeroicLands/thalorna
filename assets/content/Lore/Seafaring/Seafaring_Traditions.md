Six peoples build the hulls of Thalorna's seas. Each section below gives a people's warships, then the hulls that carry its trade, then how its yards build. The measures and formulas that hold for every vessel are in [[doc-vesselhulls|Vessel Dimensions and Burthen]], [[doc-vesselrigs|Vessel Rigs]], [[doc-vesselcrew|Vessel Crew and Oarsmen]], [[doc-vesselbuild|Shipbuilding Time and Cost]] and [[doc-vesselgrades|Vessel Grades]].

## Au'Khelathu

The navy of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]] is large and holds the mouths of the delta and the southern shore. Its battle galley is beamier and heavier than a Vilérian galley and ships more archers, who thin an enemy's deck before the hulls meet. The biggest Khelathi warship pairs two hulls under one broad deck that carries towers and catapults; it is a calm-summer ship and stays near home in any other season. Its oarsmen are reckoned hull by hull ([[doc-vesselcrew|Vessel Crew and Oarsmen]]).

Trade moves on the river. The barge is broad and draws little water; the prevailing wind fills its one sail going upstream, the current brings it back, and a big barge is towed or poled in a calm. The [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelathi]] leave the long sea routes to foreign hulls.

Khelathi shipwrights work in a river tradition even for seagoing hulls: broad square sails, a pair of steering oars aft and, on older hulls, a rope truss along the keel line that keeps a long hull from sagging. Hulls for the channels of [[affiliation-deltaselatu|The Delta Selatu]] are wide, shallow and cheap, and common in the channels of the Delta Selatu, though they weather storms worse than Vilérian ships.

## Aureldia

Ocean swell runs onto the coasts of [[place-aureldirgn|Aureldia Region]], in [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]], [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]] and [[place-elavendre|Élavendre Region]], and swamps a low hull, so galleys there are scarce, keep inshore and put out only in the fair months. They row one bank, set triangular sails and stand higher out of the water than southern galleys; a few together raid an enemy coast or guard a convoy. [[affiliation-crownships|The Crown's Ships]], Provénzia's navy, number only a handful of galleys, and in a hard year [[place-valarencity|Válaren City]]'s merchant captains sail their own ships in the Admiral's service. Aureldian strength at sea lies in sailing hulls.

Two kinds carry the trade: the tall merchantman, and a nimble two-master that points high into the wind.

Aureldian yards build carvel, raising the frame first and fixing the planks flush against it, seam to seam. They hang a rudder on the sternpost and mix square and triangular sails on one ship.

## The Nordlands

For war the [[lore-nordheimnclt|Nordheimn]] of the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands Region]] build longships and keep no galleys. A longship is long, narrow and low, draws little water and goes by one sail or one bank of oars. A king or a great jarl builds the largest of them. When a kingdom calls its levy ([[lore-nrdlndsraid|The Raid and the Longship]]), the fleet that answers is mainly deep cargo hulls refitted for war ([[doc-vesselwar|Merchant Ships in War]]).

Those cargo hulls carry the northern trade in peace.

Northern yards build clinker, each plank lapping the one beneath it, and rig square sails. A steering oar over the quarter guides most hulls, and the newest cargo ships hang a rudder on the sternpost instead.

## Tantsou

The war junk, the **Jari** (War Junk), is a merchant junk made lighter and sharper, manned by sailors and marines; it guards convoys and runs down pirates. The yards of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] and of the [[affiliation-jenchaiempr|Jenchai Empire]] both build it, and the Jenchai call it the Rija. On rivers and in still water the empire also uses the **Twenmou** (Paddle-Wheel Ship), whose enclosed hull is driven by men walking treadmills that turn its wheels, so it makes way upstream or with no wind; its walkers count as oarsmen ([[doc-vesselcrew|Vessel Crew and Oarsmen]]). The **Tinmou** (Charging Boat) is a rowed assault boat with no mast and rawhide over its upper works, built to close, grapple and board. The **Shouwou** (Tower Ship) is a fortress of several decks for river and estuary fighting, too top-heavy for open water.

Three hulls carry the trade. The **Wongri** (Ocean Junk) has a V-shaped bottom, planks laid in several thicknesses and between three and five masts, and its rudder draws up for shallow water. The **Kantong** (Sand Ship) settles level on the mud at low tide, which lets it into channels too shallow for a keeled hull. The **Haimou** (Tribute Barge) carries the grain tax along the canals to [[place-zhowen|Zhowen]] and the garrisons of the north.

Every [[lore-tantsouclt|Tantsou]] hull is divided by bulkheads into sealed compartments, hangs its rudder on the sternpost and is worked with long sculls over the stern. Every one under sail carries the battened rig and the grade step that comes with it ([[doc-vesselrigs|Vessel Rigs]]). Only the Wongri and the Jari are [[doc-vesseljunks|junks]].

## Vedyara

[[affiliation-chendrapur|Chendrapur]] keeps the only standing war-fleet in [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara Region]]. Its galleys are slim and single-banked under a triangular sail, are built in that city alone and carry no cargo, and [[affiliation-vyalendra2|Vyalendra City-State]] hires them season by season.

The coasting ship is the working hull of Vedyari trade. It runs from river mouth to river mouth within sight of land in the open season, under triangular sail, and leaves the long eastern passage to foreign hulls. Flat-bottomed river-boats work the rivers and never cross the bar.

Vedyari shipwrights tie their planks together with cord or fasten them with nails ([[doc-vesselbuild|Shipbuilding Time and Cost]]).

## The Vilérian Empire

The [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] fights at sea in galleys built for nothing else. On the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]] its navy runs from light scouts of two banks through three-banked galleys with bronze rams to heavy polyremes with fighting towers, and it takes merchant ships only to carry soldiers and stores.

The largest merchant hulls the empire builds are the grain ships that keep its capital fed.

Vilérian yards raise the shell before the frame, locking each plank to the next with mortise-and-tenon joints. The rig is square, usually a mainsail with a small sail on a foremast raked forward, and two oars at the stern steer.
