The common greeting of Okháris, _Okháré, né thalé_, hails a stranger as one of the people before it wishes him well. The two halves of [[lore-okharinclt|Okhárin]] religious life, the [[lore-zohira|zohira]] who are dealt with and the [[affiliation-nkaruthar|Eternal Flame]] who is worshipped, share one tongue, and this page is its word-hoard: the stems the clans and the cities use, what each ending makes of them, the rules a new word is built by, and the register of every name the setting gives in Okháris.

An [[skill-okharclng|Okháric]] stem names an act, and an ending says what part the act plays. _Korth-_ is to keep watch; _korthari_ is the guardian who keeps it, and _kortharira_ is a company of them. The same handful of endings is laid on every stem, and a title is two words: the office, then the thing it serves, set in the _-ing_ form that marks belonging. _Korthari Zohiring_ is the Guardian of the Spirits; _Korthari Nkaring_ is the Warden of the Flame.

The sound of Okháric is full and nasal. Words open freely on _mb_, _nd_ and _nk_, the rasping _kh_ falls anywhere, and _-ng_ and _-m_ close words that mark a class. Vowels come short or long, the long ones written with a circumflex, and stress falls on the second-last syllable.

## How to read the tables

Each word table has these columns:

- **Form** is the word as it is cited: a noun in its plain form, a verb in its _-om_ form. A form that closes on a hyphen is a bound stem, which never stands alone.
- **Class** says what kind of word it is, from the list below.
- **Gloss** is its meaning in brief.
- **Built from** is `—` for a root word. Otherwise it names the stem or word and the ending the form is made of, joined by `+`, and the form is exactly what the suffix table makes of them.
- **Attested** names the page that uses the word, or `—`.

## Building words

Two moves make every built word on this page. **An ending on a stem:** the stem is bound, and each ending says what part the stem's act plays. **A word and its belonging:** a noun takes _-ing_ in place of its closing vowel and stands after the word it qualifies, so a title, a spirit's name and a place's name are each a head word and a word of belonging.

### Suffixes

| Suffix | Stem                     | Makes                                                   | Example                                |
| ------ | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| `-u`   | added                    | the thing itself: a noun                                | _nkar-_ > _nkaru_, flame               |
| `-om`  | added                    | the plain act: a verb                                   | _thar-_ > _tharom_, endures            |
| `-ari` | added                    | the one who does it, by calling or office               | _korth-_ > _korthari_, a guardian      |
| `-elo` | added                    | the one it is done to, the one taken in hand            | _kwend-_ > _kwendelo_, a student       |
| `-ira` | replaces a closing vowel | many of them, a company or a kind                       | _korthari_ > _kortharira_, guardians   |
| `-ing` | replaces a closing vowel | of it, belonging to it; set after the word it qualifies | _nkaru_ > _nkaring_, of the flame      |
| `-in`  | added                    | bearing it, of its kind: the adjective                  | _thokor-_ > _thokorin_, high           |
| `-é`   | added                    | one of it: a person of a people or a land               | _okhár-_ > _okháré_, one of the people |
| `-is`  | added                    | the land or the seat of it                              | _okhár-_ > _Okháris_                   |
| `-o`   | added                    | a man's name                                            | _tákor-_ > _Tákoro_                    |
| `-a`   | added                    | a woman's name                                          | _ashénd-_ > _Ashénda_                  |

## Making names

**An Okhárin person** carries a given name and a tribe: [[being-kirobashkm|Kiroba of the Shókuma]] is Kiroba, of the Shókuma tribe, and the tribe is named in the reader's tongue as _of the_. A man's given name commonly closes on _-o_ or _-u_, and a woman's on _-a_ or _-i_; the name lists in [[skill-okharclng|Okháric]] hold the names in use.

**A tribe** is named for its founder or its ground, with no ending of its own: _Shókuma_, _Magara_, _Táluméng_. **A city or a land** takes _-is_ when it is a seat: _Okháris_, _Zarhánis_.

**A title** is the office and the thing it serves, the second word in its _-ing_ form: _Ambelari Zhéking_, the Prophet of Light. The High Pyremant is written half in the reader's tongue, _Pyremant Thokorin_.
