The unmaking: decay, curses, the serpent, entropy. Zelgút is what Azu'âthis is and what he never stops trying to restore. It is not evil in the moral sense so much as prior — the state that obtained before the world and would obtain again. It is taught in full because it has to be, since nothing can be warded against that is not understood, and it is proscribed in practice, which the temples do not attempt to explain to outsiders.
