The truth that judges. Qar'quzu is what Qe'âret imposed on the primordial darkness before creation, and it is the condition under which anything at all can continue to exist: law, judgment, binding, wards, oaths, and the weighing of the heart. It is not one domain among eight but the frame the other six work inside — a lekhau does not learn Athen'Uqa as one subject and Qar'quzu as another, but learns Athen'Uqa as one of the ways order is maintained against the alternative.
