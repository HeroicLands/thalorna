Of the eight domains, **Qar'kuzu** is the one the others stand on. It is the truth that judges: what [[lore-qearetdty|Qe'aret]] imposed on the primordial darkness before creation, and the condition under which anything at all can continue to exist. It holds law, judgment, binding, wards, oaths and the weighing of the heart.

It is not one domain among eight but the frame, with [[lore-zelgut|Zelgút]], inside which the other six work. A lekhau does not learn [[lore-athenuqa|Thelqen'Uqa]] as one subject and Qar'kuzu as another, but learns Thelqen'Uqa as one of the ways order is maintained against the alternative.
