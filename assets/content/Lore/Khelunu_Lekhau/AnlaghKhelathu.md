Look at any field the week the flood goes down and the line is plain: black silt on one side, sand on the other. That line is **Anlagh'Khelathu**, the living black land, one of the eight domains of the [[lore-khelunulekha|Khelunu Lekhau]]. It holds water, fertility, growth, healing and abundance.

It is one half of the country itself, set against [[lore-gezrulutu|Gezru'lutu]], the red waste. The valley is not the good half and the waste the bad one: the flood that makes the **Black Land** is measured against the Red every year.
