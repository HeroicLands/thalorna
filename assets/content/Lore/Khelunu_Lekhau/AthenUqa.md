The disk in its heat — light, sight, kingship, vitality, the day. Athen'Uqa is half of one circuit with Ithnetáu, and the tradition insists on the pairing: Uqa'â crosses the sky by day and the underworld by night, and neither half of the journey is the whole of it. A lekhau who commands the day and not the night can work only until sundown, in every sense the tradition cares about.
