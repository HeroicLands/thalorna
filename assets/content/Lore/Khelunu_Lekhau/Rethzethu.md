The name set down — speech, writing, true names, knowledge, memory, mind. Reth'zethu is named for the soul-component the funerary rites already preserve, the one inscribed on every available surface while the body is wrapped in linen. With Qelt'nu it makes the pair on which the whole tradition turns, and destroying either ends the person. This is why the erasure of a name is the gravest sentence the empire can pass.
