The underworld — the half of the circuit that runs beneath the world while [[lore-uqaadty|Uqa'â]] crosses it from west to east by night. The dead go by the same road, which is why the workings that protect them, guide the soul and ward the tomb belong to [[affiliation-garithnetau|Gar-Ithnetáu]] and to no other House. The tradition insists the road is one road: the day half and the night half are not two journeys but the two halves of one, and a lekhau who commands only the first can work only until sundown.
