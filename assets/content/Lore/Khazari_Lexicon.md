This is the word-hoard of [[skill-khazarlng|Khazári]]: every skeleton the language is known to use, what it
means, and the words its frames make from it. It is laid out for use at the table. A name needed in play
can be built from it in a minute, and a Khazári word met in play can be taken apart and read.

Every word here is made by the rules of the language note and by nothing else. A skeleton of three
consonants carries the meaning; a frame of vowels poured through it says what kind of word comes out.
The frames, the suffixes and the sound rules are set out in [[skill-khazarlng|Khazári]]; this note holds
the skeletons and what has been built on them.

## How a word is made

An author needs three moves, and the language allows no others.

**A frame on a skeleton.** Take a skeleton and pour a frame through it. From **z-t-g** "steel": the bare
frame gives _zatag_ "steel", the worker frame _zatagir_ "a steelwright", the deed frame _zutág_ "to make
steel", and the mastery frame _zuttág_ "to make steel as a master does". The cluster rule sets a short
_a_ between the second and third consonants, because _t_ is not one of the five that stand together.

**A gender on a noun.** A noun that is feminine or neuter adds its suffix to the frame. From **d-v-g** "a
parent": _davag_ "a father", _davagev_ "a mother". The skeleton is the same, and only the noun's gender
differs.

**Two skeletons compounded.** Set the first skeleton in the bare frame and the second in the deed frame,
with _a_ at the seam. From **s-n-dh** "a tomb" and **v-gh-n** "to seal": _sandhavughán_ "the sealing of
the tomb". This is how a house name or a hold's name is built, and it can be read the same way: the first
half is the thing, the second the work done to it.

A word no table lists is still Khazári if it is a listed skeleton through a listed frame. A meaning no
skeleton carries needs a new skeleton, which belongs in this note before it is used anywhere else.
