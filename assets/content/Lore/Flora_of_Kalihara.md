Flora of Kalihara encompasses an extraordinary range of plants in the jungle of [[place-klhrcntnnt|Kalihara Continent]], and two of them are part of the island's working life: the Stilt Tree of the eastern shore and the Ember Bloom of the terrace margins. The islanders do not gather either wildly. Each belongs to a part that a household tends, and each is taken from in the way the household's vaan'ari of [[affiliation-tharivaan|Thári'vaan]] allows.

## The Stilt Tree

**Manuluori**, in [[skill-kalihrlng|Kalihari Language]] "root canopy", is a tree whose canopy stands on its roots. It grows to some forty feet at the mangrove edge and in the wet forest behind the eastern shore. It sends down arching prop roots from its branches until the crown stands on a cage of roots a man can walk through. The roots grip the mud, hold the bank, and shelter small fish and crabs. The tree flowers at the start of the wet season.

The villages cut stakes from the dead prop roots for their fence walls, and the stakes do not rot, which is why the fences hold against the [[being-mngbhmth|Mangrove Behemoth]]. A tree is cut only for the roots it has let fall. A village that loses its stilt trees to a flood replants from the neighboring bank.

## The Ember Bloom

**Kalovael**, in Kalihari "ember gleam", for its flowers, is a waist-high shrub with leathery leaves and a crown of deep red flowers that open at dawn and hold until noon. It grows on the terrace margins and the sunny lower slopes of the ridges. The root gives a red dye that does not fade.

The dyers of [[place-nalurithan|Nalurithan]] and [[place-ilovanu|Ilovanu]], craftsmen under the island's ranks, buy the root from the terrace households that tend the margins. The dye is one of the few staples that leaves the island as cloth and not as plant. No living plant leaves. The root is lifted from each shrub only every few years.
