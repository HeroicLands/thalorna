- **Kind:** Animal-kin, of [[lore-thekindred|the Kindred]]
- **Seat:** [[place-drenavrspn|the Drenavar Spine]]
- **Territory:** the high corries and the winter dens beneath them
- **Posture:** Warded, then venerated
- **Met through:** [[skill-arthgensprt|Arthgen Spirit Power]]

Arthgen is the clearest case in Áelendan practice of a single Kindred requiring two postures, and
shamen use him to teach it. Between the first hard frost and the thaw he is warded against: the
corries are not entered, the protective songs are carried by anyone who must pass below them, and
a tribe that loses someone up there in winter does not go to look.

From the thaw to the frost he is venerated, and generously—he is credited with the health of the
high pasture, with the summer honey, and with warning of avalanche, and the summer rite in the
corries is among the larger occasions of the Áelendan year.

The Áelendan do not find this contradictory and are impatient with outsiders who do. It is the
same spirit, they say, in the same country, wanting different things at different times of the
year, exactly as a person does. What would be strange is a spirit that wanted the same thing
always.
