- **Kind:** Animal-kin, of [[lore-thekindred|the Kindred]]
- **Seat:** [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]]
- **Territory:** the lower wood, the field-edges and the middens
- **Posture:** Appeased
- **Met through:** [[skill-rhysgensprt|Rhysgen Spirit Power]]

Rhysgen holds the lower wood where the forest thins into field-edge and midden, which is to say
the ground where the Áelendan and the wild are most mixed up with one another. He is
correspondingly the least dignified of the named Kindred and the most frequently dealt with.

He takes. Poultry, cached meat, small worked things left outside – these go, and the tradition does
not regard this as theft but as a levy that has not been negotiated down. He is appeased rather
than venerated for exactly that reason: the relationship is a running argument about the size of
his share.

Alone among the Kindred the shamen deal with, he will renegotiate. A shaman who makes a case can
get the levy reduced for a season, and the case has to be good; he is not moved by need and is
visibly moved by cleverness. Shamen who like him say so quietly, because the elders consider
enjoying Rhysgen's company a professional weakness.
