- **Kind:** Animal-kin, of [[lore-thekindred|the Kindred]]
- **Seat:** [[place-drenavrspn|the Drenavar Spine]]
- **Territory:** one long ridge and the packs that run it
- **Posture:** Venerated
- **Met through:** [[skill-cadwyrsprt|Cadwyr Spirit Power]]

Cadwyr is the presiding spirit of the wolves of one long ridge—not a wolf, and not all wolves,
but the ancestral presence the packs of that ridge belong to. He is the Kindred most Áelendan hunt-
rites in that country are addressed to, and the relationship is the plainest in the tradition:
the tribe takes what the ridge offers and gives back what has been indicated.

What he requires is a share, given where the kill was made and not carried home first, and an
honest count. Shamen are firm that he is not deceived about the count and has never once been
deceived about it. A hunter who under-reports is not punished by Cadwyr; the tribe's next hunt on
that ridge simply finds nothing, and the tribe knows why, and deals with the hunter itself.

He is venerated rather than appeased because he is generous within his terms, and because the
terms have not changed in living memory.
