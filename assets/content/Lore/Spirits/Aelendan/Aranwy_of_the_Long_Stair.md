- **Kind:** Water-kin, of [[lore-thekindred|the Kindred]]
- **Seat:** [[place-longstair|The Long Stair]]
- **Territory:** the seven falls and the gorge beneath them
- **Posture:** Appeased
- **Met through:** [[skill-aranwysprt|Aranwy of the Long Stair Spirit Power]]

Aranwy is a river-mother of the old kind: her power runs the length of the seven falls and the
gorge beneath them, and stops where the water slows. She is not malicious and she is not kind.
She has requirements, they are specific, and she does not accept an approximation of them.

The offering is made at the lowest pool at first light, in silence, and consists of something the
giver made with their own hands and would rather have kept. What is given is not retrieved. Shamen
who have dealt with her a long time say she is more interested in the making than the losing.

She permits fishing in the lower water and forbids it above the third fall. She permits crossing
and forbids swimming. The prohibition Áelendan children learn first is that nobody climbs the
Stair by daylight; what the prohibition is protecting is not agreed on, and Aranwy has never been
asked directly, because asking would require climbing.
