- **Kind:** Animal-kin, of [[lore-thekindred|the Kindred]]
- **Seat:** [[place-drenavrspn|the Drenavar Spine]]
- **Territory:** the high air over the Spine, and the crags the hawks nest in
- **Posture:** Appeased
- **Met through:** [[skill-gwalchensprt|Gwalchen Spirit Power]]

Gwalchen holds the high air over the Drenavar Spine and the crags its hawks nest in. Of the named
animal-kin he is the most distant: he does not deal with the tribes at their lodges, he is not
addressed at the hearth, and he is met by going up.

He is appeased, and the appeasement is territorial rather than material. The nesting crags are not
climbed between the thaw and midsummer, and the prohibition is absolute; the tribes that keep it
have kept it without exception for as long as the songs record. What is given otherwise is small
and given high.

What he is consulted for is sight – weather coming over the Spine, movement on the far slopes, the
state of a pass. A shaman with a standing relationship to Gwalchen is valuable to a tribe in a way
that needs no explaining, and such shamen are correspondingly reluctant to travel.
