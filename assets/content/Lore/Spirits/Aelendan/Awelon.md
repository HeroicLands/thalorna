- **Kind:** Weather-kin, of [[lore-thekindred|the Kindred]]
- **Seat:** no fixed place
- **Territory:** wherever the west wind is blowing, and nowhere once it drops
- **Posture:** Venerated, briefly
- **Met through:** [[skill-awelonsprt|Awelon Spirit Power]]

Awelon is weather-kin, and weather-kin are the awkward case in a tradition built on standing
pacts: he holds no territory, cannot be located, and is present only while he is blowing. A tribe
cannot keep a compact with him in the way it keeps one with a river-mother, and does not try.

What the Áelendan do instead is address him in the moment—at the turn of a season, before a
crossing, when a storm is building off the water. The address is short, spoken aloud into the
wind, and is understood to have been heard or not heard with no way of telling which.

Shamen regard him as the most honest of the Kindred and the least useful. He does not deceive, he
does not bargain, and he does not stay. The old saying is that Awelon keeps no promises because he
has never been asked for one he could keep.
