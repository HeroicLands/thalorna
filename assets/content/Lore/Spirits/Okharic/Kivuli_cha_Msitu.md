- **Kind:** [[lore-zohira|Zohira]], emissary of [[affiliation-nkaruthar|the Eternal Flame]]
- **Domain:** The hunt
- **Also called:** the Shadow of the Forest
- **Met through:** [[skill-kivulisprt|Kivuli cha Msitu Spirit Power]], in the [[affiliation-njiayaroho|Njia ya Roho]]

Kivuli cha Msitu is the zohira of the hunt, and the one every mwalimu wa roho has a
relationship with whether or not they sought it—because the guardian predator a candidate tracks in
the **Spirit Hunt** is understood to be Kivuli's, named by him and given for the purpose.

The rite is therefore not a test of strength but a transaction with him: the candidate takes what he
has offered, consumes its heart, and is bound. Candidates who fail have not been defeated by an
animal. They have been refused.

Beyond initiation he governs ordinary hunting throughout the Okháric country—what may be taken,
in what season, with what given back. Shókuma shamans, whose tribe is most associated with the
Hunt, hold that he is the most exacting of the zohira about waste and the most indifferent of them
about cruelty, which they say tells you what he is.
