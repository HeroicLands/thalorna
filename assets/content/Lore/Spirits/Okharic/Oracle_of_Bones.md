- **Kind:** [[lore-zohira|Zohira]], emissary of [[affiliation-nkaruthar|the Eternal Flame]]
- **Domain:** Death and prophecy
- **Also called:** the Reader in the Yánda Maláika
- **Met through:** [[skill-orclbonessprt|The Oracle of Bones Spirit Power]], in the [[affiliation-njiayaroho|Njia ya Roho]]

The Oracle of Bones is seated somewhere in [[place-yandamalaika|Yánda Maláika]], the range
where the tribes hold the gods dwell. Its exact place is not agreed; the accounts of shamans who
claim to have reached it do not match one another, and the tradition has stopped trying to
reconcile them.

It is the zohira of death and of what death discloses. Bone-Readers attend the dead and read what
it is understood to have written in them, which is the ordinary and daily form of its service and
requires no journey at all.

The other form is the journey, undertaken rarely and by shamans who need an answer that nothing
nearer will give. It is a considerable undertaking with an uncertain end: some return with an
answer, some return without having found it, and some do not return. What is agreed by everyone who
has reached it is that it answers exactly the question asked, which is not the same as the question
the asker meant.
