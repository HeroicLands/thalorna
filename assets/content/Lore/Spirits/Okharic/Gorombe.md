Gorombe is the storm, and dealing with him is a matter of timing. He does not answer during
a storm; shamans who have tried report nothing, or worse. What can be done is done before the
season, at the cairns on high ground, and consists of a formal accounting of what the tribe intends
to build, plant and travel in the months to come.

The theology is that Gorombe is not being asked for mercy, which he does not give, but being
informed—so that what he breaks he breaks knowing. Shamans concede that outsiders find this
distinction thin, and hold to it anyway.

He is credited with the first rains and with the ends of droughts, and is therefore not resented in
the way a purely destructive spirit would be. The [[lore-okharinclt|Okharin]] saying is that Gorombe has never taken
anything he did not also give first.
