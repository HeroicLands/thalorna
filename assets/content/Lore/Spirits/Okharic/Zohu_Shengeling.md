Zohu Shéngeling is the zohira of the blade, and is unusual among the zohira in that he is
most often met not at a cairn but in an object: the [[affiliation-magaratrb|Magara Tribe]]'s [Magara Spirit Blades]{.name data-type="weapongear"}, weapons in
which he is understood to be seated, passed down through generations and able to cut both physical
and spiritual foes.

A Spirit Blade is not given and is not inherited by right. It **chooses**—the bond is formed at
an initiation in which the blade responds to a candidate or does not, and a Blade-Singer judges
which has happened. A Magara who is refused by the blade of their own family is not disgraced, but
is not going to carry it.

He is a war zohira and the priesthood of [[affiliation-nkaruthar|Nkaru'thar]] at [[place-vuthraka|Vuthraka]], the Wild Flame, takes a
close interest in him, which the Magara tolerate. The relationship between the temple's warrior-
priests and the tribes' blade-bearers is the friendliest point of contact between the religion and
the shamanic tradition, on the grounds that both parties would rather discuss weapons than
precedence.
