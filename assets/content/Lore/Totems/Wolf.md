Cooperative and strategic, wolves are pack animals that rely on teamwork and careful planning to hunt and protect their territory.

## Human Personality

A Wolf person values teamwork and planning and works well within a group. Strategy and cooperation are how the group's goals are reached.

Physical
: Lean, strong build; sharp, alert movements.

Behavioral
: Highly cooperative, skilled in social strategies and teamwork.

Positive
: Team player, strategic, and cooperative.

Negative
: May be overly reliant on group decisions and struggle with independence.

Minor Trait ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-tmrntd|Team-oriented]])
: Works best alongside others, naturally collaborative and most comfortable when acting as part of a trusted group.

Major Disorder ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-cdpndnt|Codependent]])
: Cannot function outside the pack, surrendering all personal judgment to the group and becoming paralyzed with indecision when forced to act alone.
