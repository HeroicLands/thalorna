Cooperative and strategic, wolves are pack animals that rely on teamwork and careful planning to hunt and protect their territory.

## Human Personality

Strongly valuing teamwork and planning, this individual operates effectively within a group. They rely on strategy and collaboration to achieve shared goals.

- **Physical:** Lean, strong build; sharp, alert movements.
- **Behavioral:** Highly cooperative, skilled in social strategies and teamwork.
- **Positive:** Team player, strategic, and cooperative.
- **Negative:** May be overly reliant on group decisions and struggle with independence.
- **Minor Trait ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-tmrntd|Team-oriented]]):** Works best alongside others, naturally collaborative and most comfortable when acting as part of a trusted group.
- **Major Disorder ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-cdpndnt|Codependent]]):** Cannot function outside the pack, surrendering all personal judgment to the group and becoming paralyzed with indecision when forced to act alone.
