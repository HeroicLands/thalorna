Social and vocal, parrots are intelligent birds that thrive on interaction and are known for their ability to mimic sounds and engage with their environment.

## Human Personality

A Parrot person is outgoing and talkative and enjoys the company of others. Articulate and loud, the person brings life to any gathering.

Physical
: Bright, expressive appearance; social body language.

Behavioral
: Highly social, vocal and communicative, enjoys interaction.

Positive
: Communicative, social, and interactive.

Negative
: Overly talkative, needs attention, and may not enjoy solitude.

Minor Trait ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-dramatic|Dramatic]])
: Open and animated in sharing their thoughts and feelings, naturally drawing the attention of those around them.

Major Disorder ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-nrcssstc|Narcissistic]])
: Desperately performs for the approval of others, growing frantic and emotionally erratic when they feel overlooked or unappreciated.
