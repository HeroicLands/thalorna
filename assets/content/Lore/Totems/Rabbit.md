Nervous and quick, rabbits are prey animals that rely on their agility and rapid breeding to survive in environments filled with predators.

## Human Personality

The Rabbit person is agile and easily startled, and trusts quick thinking and fast action. Others see a cautious person who reacts quickly to threats.

Physical
: Small, quick, agile; alert and reactive.

Behavioral
: Nervous, extremely quick responses, always on alert.

Positive
: Agile, quick-thinking, and responsive.

Negative
: Easily startled and anxious, may struggle with calm environments.

Minor Trait ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-nervous|Nervous]])
: A bit high-strung and easily startled, always alert to potential trouble and quick to seek safety at the first sign of danger.

Major Disorder ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-anxiety|Anxiety]])
: Trembles with constant anxiety, startling at the slightest disturbance and fleeing from situations others would find harmless.
