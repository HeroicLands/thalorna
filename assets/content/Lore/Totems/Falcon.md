Precise and relentless, falcons are master hunters that dive at incredible speeds to capture prey, showing unparalleled focus and determination.

## Human Personality

A Falcon person dives into a task with complete focus and precision. Concentration is what lets such a person see objectives through.

Physical
: Streamlined, athletic build; intense, unwavering gaze.

Behavioral
: Precise in actions, relentless in efforts and pursuits.

Positive
: Highly focused, determined, and efficient.

Negative
: Can be overly intense and may not tolerate distractions or setbacks well.

Minor Trait ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-precise|Precise]])
: Takes care with details and holds their work to high standards, sometimes spending longer than necessary to get things just right.

Major Disorder ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-obsssv|Obsessive]])
: Driven by merciless perfectionism, endlessly reworking and controlling every detail until nothing and no one can meet their impossible standards.
