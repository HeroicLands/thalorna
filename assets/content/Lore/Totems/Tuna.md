Driven and enduring, tuna are powerful swimmers that migrate vast distances across oceans, relentlessly pursuing schools of prey in their path.

## Human Personality

Tuna people are driven and resilient, and take up long pursuits with fixed determination. They are powerful, and they persist until the objective is reached.

Physical
: Robust, powerful build; exceptional stamina.

Behavioral
: Highly driven, endures through long challenges and pursuits.

Positive
: Highly motivated, resilient, and enduring.

Negative
: May overexert themselves and be overly single-minded.

Minor Trait ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-enrgtc|Energetic]])
: Throws themselves wholeheartedly into their pursuits, maintaining a pace and intensity that others find difficult to match.

Major Disorder ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-obsssv|Obsessive]])
: Driven by an obsessive, all-consuming focus on their goals, unable to rest or relent even when exhaustion and reason demand it.
