Driven and enduring, tuna are powerful swimmers that migrate vast distances across oceans, relentlessly pursuing schools of prey in their path.

## Human Personality

Highly motivated and resilient, this person embarks on long-term pursuits with unwavering determination. They are powerful and persistent in achieving their objectives.

- **Physical:** Robust, powerful build; exceptional stamina.
- **Behavioral:** Highly driven, endures through long challenges and pursuits.
- **Positive:** Highly motivated, resilient, and enduring.
- **Negative:** May overexert themselves and be overly single-minded.
- **Minor Trait ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-enrgtc|Energetic]]):** Throws themselves wholeheartedly into their pursuits, maintaining a pace and intensity that others find difficult to match.
- **Major Disorder ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-obsssv|Obsessive]]):** Driven by an obsessive, all-consuming focus on their goals, unable to rest or relent even when exhaustion and reason demand it.
