## Human Personality

Deceitful and intelligent, this individual uses their sharp mind to navigate challenges. They are skilled at outsmarting both opponents and obstacles, often finding clever solutions.

- **Physical:** Lean, quick; alert, watchful eyes.
- **Behavioral:** Smart, resourceful, able to outwit and maneuver through challenges.
- **Positive:** Clever, strategic, and resourceful.
- **Negative:** Deceitful, manipulative, and may use cunning at the expense of others' trust.
- **Minor Trait ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-rsrcfl|Resourceful]]):** Finds a way through most problems with quick thinking and charm, comfortable bending the rules when a straight path isn't available.
- **Major Disorder ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-antscl|Antisocial]]):** A habitual deceiver who manipulates everyone around them through lies and charm, viewing honesty as a weakness to be exploited.
