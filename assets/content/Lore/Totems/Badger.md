Tenacious and solitary, badgers are fiercely independent animals that will defend their territory with determination and persistence.

## Human Personality

A Badger person works alone by preference and holds to a few principles with great stubbornness. Anyone who trespasses on that private ground meets a defender who does not tire.

Physical
: Strong, stocky build; possibly sharper, more penetrating gaze.

Behavioral
: Highly determined, fiercely independent, unwilling to let go of tasks.

Positive Aspects
: Determined, fiercely independent, and resilient.

Negative Aspects
: Stubborn, isolated, unwilling to let go of tasks, and resistant to collaboration.

Minor Trait ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-selfsuf|Self-sufficient]])
: Prefers to handle things on their own, sometimes to a fault, rarely asking for help even when it would be wise.

Major Disorder ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-obsssv|Obsessive]])
: Consumed by rigid habits and unbending stubbornness, unable to adapt or compromise even when it harms them.
