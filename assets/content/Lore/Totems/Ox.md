Strong and dependable, oxen are resilient animals used in agriculture for their ability to carry heavy loads and work tirelessly in fields.

## Human Personality

The Ox person is the backbone of any group. Heavy responsibilities are carried without complaint, and the strength is well known.

Physical
: Extremely strong, robust frame; steady and solid stance.

Behavioral
: Dependable, reliable under pressure, does not tire easily.

Positive
: Reliable, strong, and hardworking.

Negative
: Predictable, resistant to change, and may be overly burdensome.

Minor Trait ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-rslnt|Resilient]])
: Endures hardship without complaint, drawing strength from familiar patterns and steady, dependable effort day after day.

Major Disorder ([[sohl-none-doctrauma-cdpndnt|Codependent]])
: Slavishly bound to familiar routines and structures, unable to function or make choices when their expected patterns are disrupted.
