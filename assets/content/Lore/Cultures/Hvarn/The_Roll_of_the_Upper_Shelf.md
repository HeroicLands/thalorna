**The Roll of the Upper Shelf** is the part of the Hvarn roll, _rouzen_, that walks the upper shelf of [[place-jnanadvar|Jnanadvar]], recited each winter by the Bone-bringer in the middle of the long house at [[place-nurvhrn|Nurvharn]]. The roll is not kept in the order people died. It is kept in the order the ground is walked: from the foot of a crossing to its summit, place by place, and at each place the dead found there, the oldest first. To recite the roll is to walk the road aloud, and a Roadwalker who has heard it every winter of his life knows each stone of the shelf before he has set a foot on it.

Each entry has the same six parts: the name and the hearth, the place, what the ground did there, whose road it was, who brought the body up, and the fire at which the name is carried now. When the Bone-bringer reaches a person whose name a living child carries, the child stands and says the place. Anyone may stop the recitation to put an entry right, and the Bone-bringer says the line again as corrected; this is the _vhardzhev_, the answer from the floor. A correction the floor cannot agree on is kept as two lines. The recitation below is the one given in the winter that [Dzorin]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the Hvurnash hearth was Bone-bringer, with the corrections that winter brought. Its places are the [Wet Step]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the [Butter Stone]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the [Knee]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the [Two Teeth]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the [Ear]{.name data-type="place/feature"} and the [Long Lip]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, which is the last ground before the summit.

## The Recitation

::: {.verse form=rouzen lang=en}
[Dzorin, the Bone-bringer:]{.speaker}/
Bank the fire to the half./
Put the rope down. Put the awl down./
This is the roll of the upper shelf./
I walk it. You walk it with me./
Where I put my foot wrong, say so.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
The floor is open to the roll./
Say it, Bone-bringer.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
Before the first of us, the shelf./
It lay on the face of the mountain/
the width of a beast and a man,/
and it went up, and it was not ours,/
and nobody had fallen from it./
That is the first place of the roll./
It has no name at it./
It is the shelf.

The shelf begins at the snow-line,/
where the beasts are left/
and the Drovers turn down to the grass./
From there the shelf is walked on one rope./
From there the roll is said.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
The shelf. Go on.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
The Wet Step./
The first stone past the grass,/
where the melt comes down the face in spring/
and lies on the step in a skin of ice/
till the sun is high./
Walk it after noon. Walk it on the inside.

[Ourn]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, of the Tsedvern hearth./
The Wet Step./
The ground: the ice was on the step at morning./
He went first, on the outer edge, himself./
The road was the Tsedvern hearth's./
Brought up by the Tsedvern hearth,/
three on one rope, in the same week./
The name is at the Klemverd fire.

[A girl of the Klemverd hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Wet Step.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/

The Wet Step.

Two at the Wet Step, and the same ice,/
and the same edge, and a long time between./
After noon. On the inside.

The Butter Stone./

that stands out of the shelf/

and for the grease the hands leave on it./
Put a hand on it going round. Everyone does.

She has it as her grandmother had it./
The second stone./
Try the lip with a stick. Try it every time.

The Knee./
Where the shelf bends back on itself/
round the rib of the mountain,/
and the man in front goes out of sight/
before the man behind has come to the bend,/
so that the rope is all there is between them./
At the Knee the rope is short./
At the Knee the one in front calls.

[Rhanvi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, of the Hvurnash hearth./
The Knee./
The ground: none. The ground held./
The call was not made, and the rope was long,/
and the one behind did not know she had stopped/
and walked on, and the slack came off the rope at once./
The road was the Hvurnash hearth's./
Brought up by the Hvurnash hearth./
The name is at the Tsedvern fire.

[A boy of the Tsedvern hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Knee.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
The ground held. Say that with it./
Not everything in the roll is the ground.

[Klemsi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, of the Vrestun hearth./
The Knee./
The ground: the inside of the bend,/
where the shelf is widest and looks safest,/
had a crack along the face behind the snow,/
and the snow and the shelf came off together./
He was a Roadwalker. He was on the guide's rope./
The guide was [Tudzelv]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, of the Vrestun hearth./
She gave the account at the council./
The road was the Vrestun hearth's./
Brought up by Tudzelv and the Bone-bringer,/
in the thaw, two on one rope, the second time they went down./
The name is at the Rhonzhel fire.

[A girl of the Rhonzhel hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Knee. The inside of the bend.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
The inside of the bend./
The wide place. Look behind the snow.

The Two Teeth./
Two stones standing up out of the shelf/
with a gap between them a pack will not go through/
and a man will, sideways,/
and the pack is handed over the top./
Hand it over. Do not let go of it till it is taken.

[Habvern]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, of the Klemverd hearth./
The Two Teeth./
The ground: the near tooth is loose at the root./
It was leaned on, handing a pack./
It went over, and he went with it./
The road was the Klemverd hearth's./
Brought up by the Klemverd hearth./
The name is at the Hvurnash fire.

[An old man of the Klemverd hearth, from the floor:]{.speaker}/
The far tooth./
It is the far tooth that is loose./
It was the far tooth he leaned on.

[An old woman of the Dzelbern hearth, from the floor:]{.speaker}/
The near tooth. I have had my hand on it./
It moves.

[The old man of the Klemverd hearth:]{.speaker}/
I have had my hand on the far one./
It moves.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
Both have walked it. Both stand,/
as they have stood since before my mother./
Say it both ways, Bone-bringer.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
The ground: the near tooth is loose at the root./
The ground: the far tooth is loose at the root./
It was leaned on, handing a pack./
It went over, and he went with it./
Brought up by the Klemverd hearth./
The name is at the Hvurnash fire.

[A boy of the Hvurnash hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Two Teeth.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
[Hevrel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, of the Klemverd hearth./
Under the upper shelf, below the Two Teeth./
The ground: the wind at the gap./
He was handing a pack over the top,/
and the wind took the pack, and he did not let go of it./
The road was the Klemverd hearth's./
Brought up by the Klemverd hearth, from under the shelf./
The name is at the Klemverd fire.

[Hevrel of the Klemverd hearth, the Fort-carrier, standing:]{.speaker}/
Under the upper shelf of Jnanadvar.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
A grown man stands for it this winter./
He has stood for it since he could stand./
If the wind takes the pack, let the wind have it.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
Lean on neither./
That is the roll for the Two Teeth, whichever it was.

The Ear./
A hollow in the face above the shelf/
the wind goes into and comes out of/
with a noise like breath at the ear./
Under the Ear the shelf is good./
The Ear is the place the storm comes up to first./
When the Ear speaks in daylight, the storm-reader has already spoken.

[Delzh]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, of the Dzelbern hearth./
The Ear./
The ground: none. The ground was good./
The storm came up the valley to the Ear/
and the party had been told to wait below the Knee/

The road was the Dzelbern hearth's./
Brought up by the Dzelbern hearth, when it was over./
The name is at the Vrestun fire.

[A girl of the Vrestun hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Ear.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
The ground was good./
Say that with it.

[Venesh]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, of the Rhonzhel hearth./
The Ear./
The ground: none./
The storm came to the Ear in the night./
The party was under the Ear, on the good shelf, roped,/

Brought up the same day by the party, on the rope./
The name is at the Nurvharn fire.

[A boy of the Nurvharn hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Ear.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
Six living. Say that with it.

The Long Lip./
The last of the shelf below the summit,/
where the shelf thins to a lip of rock/
over the deepest of the gorge,/
and it is long, and there is nothing else,/
and it is walked face in, with the rope fixed./
At the end of it the ushtakas' wall, and the fire, and the summit./
What is above the Long Lip is theirs./
They keep a count of their own on the cistern wall./
We do not say their count. They do not say ours.

[Berdnu]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, of the Tsedvern hearth./
The Long Lip./
The ground: a piece of the lip the length of a man/
came away under the third of the party./
The rope was fixed. The fixing held./
The rope was frayed where it went over the edge,/
and it parted at the fray./
The road was the Rhonzhel hearth's./
The road was the Klemverd hearth's./
Brought up by the Tsedvern hearth/
on two ropes, in three days./
The name is at the Klemverd fire.

[A boy of the Klemverd hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Long Lip.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
Grease the rope. Look at the rope where it goes over./
That is the roll for Berdnu.

[Shemli]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, of the Dzelbern hearth./
The Long Lip./
The ground: the lip, at the middle, in the dark./
She went to bring a lowland man's beast back/
that had gone on along the lip by itself,/
and she went, herself, off the rope./
The road was the Dzelbern hearth's./
Not brought up.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
Not brought up.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
Not brought up./
The Dzelbern hearth has been down twice./
I have been down once with them./
She is under the middle of the lip,/
on a ledge the rope does not reach,/
and the ledge is wet all summer./
The name is at no fire./
The Dzelbern hearth has not given it./
A child is named for a person who was found,/
and she is not found yet.

[An old woman of the Dzelbern hearth, from the floor:]{.speaker}/
We will go down again at the thaw.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
At the thaw./
I will be on the rope./
Shemli, of the Dzelbern hearth./
The Long Lip. Not brought up yet.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
Not brought up yet. That is the word for it./
Keep it so until it changes.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
That is the upper shelf./
From the grass to the ushtakas' wall./
The Wet Step, the Butter Stone, the Knee,/
the Two Teeth, the Ear, the Long Lip./
After noon. Round the stone with a stick./
Call at the Knee, and look behind the snow./
Lean on neither tooth. Let the wind have the pack./
When the Ear speaks, be below the Knee./
Look at the rope where it goes over./
One not brought up yet.

Before the first of us, the shelf./
It is there now./
Who walks it this year, the turn will say./
Whoever walks it has walked it already, here, on the floor,/
with the dead in their places./
Is there any word against the roll?

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
Is there any word against the roll?

[The house:]{.speaker}/
None.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
Then it is the roll, as said./
Build the fire up./
Pick the rope up.
