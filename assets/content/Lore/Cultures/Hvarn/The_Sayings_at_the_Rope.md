**The Sayings at the Rope** are said while a rope is greased. Through the closed months at [[place-nurvhrn|Nurvharn]] every rope the Hvarn own is taken down, laid out along the floor of the long house, and greased hand over hand from the knot at one end to the knot at the other, and the grease worked into it is the people's oldest upkeep. One saying goes to each arm-length, in a fixed order, and a rope of the long kind takes the sayings from the first to the last. A child learns them by greasing beside someone who knows them, saying each one back. A child who can say a rope can grease one.

The sayings are said by whoever is greasing, and anyone in earshot may put a word right, as the floor puts the roll right. They give no reasons. A Hvarn says what is done and not what it is for, and the sayings are the clearest case of it. Two of them come from the [[lore-tworoofs|Telling of the Two Roofs]], one from [[lore-hvarnaccount|the Account of Tudzelv]], and several from the roll. The order below is the one kept at the Tsedvern fire, set down with the child's answers as they come, and with one word put right from the next fire.

## The Rope

::: {.verse form=sayings lang=en}
[At the knot.]{.direction}

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
The rope is the party's./
The grease is yours./
Begin at the knot.

A knot is as good/
as the worst hand that tied it./
Tie it yourself./
Look at it yourself.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
Look at it yourself.

[Along the rope.]{.direction}

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
What you did, say _I_./
What the rope did, say _we_./
Do not trade them.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
Do not trade them.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
The Drover kept the beasts./
The Drover did not cross.

A rope does not know/
what you meant./
Neither does the floor.

Two fires, one rope.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
Two fires, one rope.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
The ground was there first./
It owes you nothing./
You owe it your eyes.

Snow lies flat/
over anything.

The wide place looks safe./
Look behind the snow.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
Look behind the snow.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
Try it with the stick./
Try it every time./
The stone that held last year/
is a year older.

At the Knee, call./
At the Knee, the rope is short./
At the Knee, nobody can see you./
Call.

The second stone of the lip.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
The second stone of the lip.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
Lean on neither stone.

[An old man, from the next fire:]{.speaker}/
Neither tooth./
The stones are the lip./
The teeth are the teeth.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
Lean on neither tooth.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
Lean on neither tooth.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
Whichever it was.

The beast is not the party./
Cut the beast.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
Cut the beast.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
Snow on the shelf in summer:/
the shelf is not the one you know./
Walk it as a new one.

When the Ear speaks in daylight,/
the Storm-reader has already spoken.

The Storm-reader says, _Not today._/
That is the word./
There is no word after it.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
There is no word after it.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
A fee does not walk the shelf./
The lowland man walks the shelf,/
and the fee stays in his belt.

The lowland man asks why./
Tell him where.

[At the middle, where the rope is folded to be carried.]{.direction}

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
Say the debt./
A debt not said/
is a body not brought up.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
A body not brought up.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
It went under my father./
It went under my sister.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
It went under my father./
It went under my sister.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
Nobody owns a road./
The road was there before the turn,/
and the turn is made from nothing.

The council cannot make you./
The council does not have to.

A hearth that lost a party/
walks to the gorge/
before it walks to the road.

Do not ask where the dead are./
Ask where they were found.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
Ask where they were found.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
Bring him up./
Then sit down.

The Bone-bringer goes down/
at the pace of the ground,/
not at yours.

A body on the scree/
is an account not finished.

The fee comes to the hearth./
The debt is between hearths./
The place at the fire is yours.

Your name has a place./
Know the place/
before you know the shelf.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
I know the place.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
Say it./
Not to me. To the rope.

[The child, naming the place of the person it is named for:]{.direction}

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
Good. That is your span./
Grease it yourself.

[Toward the far end.]{.direction}

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
The roll is the ground./
The old cannot walk the ground./
The old can hear the roll.

Put the roll right./
Put it right if it is your mother./
Put it right if it is you.

A room of your own/
is a room nobody comes into/
when you fall.

Keep the house clean enough to share./
You are sharing it.

Grease in the winter,/
home in the summer.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
Home in the summer.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
Every name you carry/
has been on the ground before you.

Count them in at the frost./
Count them again at the thaw./
Say who is missing, both times.

In a bad year, barley from the lowland./
In a worse year, what the hearths owe./
In the worst year, the grass.

The fort is fed./
That is the saying about the fort.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
That is the saying about the fort.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
Feed the stranger at your fire./
Feed the widow's children./
Feed the child of the roads.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
Feed the child of the roads.

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
The one the house has named/
no hearth feeds.

[The child says nothing back. Nobody does, for this one.]{.direction}

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
Grease to the end./
The end is where the hand slips.

The roll is long./
The night is longer./
So far.

[At the far knot.]{.direction}

[The one greasing:]{.speaker}/
The end of this rope/
is the front of the next party.

Look at the knot./
Look at it yourself.

Hang it where the whole house can see it.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
Where the whole house can see it.
:::
