**When the Roll Is Longer Than the Night** is said in the long house at [[place-nurvhrn|Nurvharn]] at the close of the recitation, on the longest night of the closed months, after the Bone-bringer has walked both crossings aloud and the floor has had its last word against the roll. A child of the house, chosen that winter, asks the question. The Winter-speaker opens the floor to it, and the hearths answer.

The question is the same every year, and every hearth's answer is its own and does not change, whoever is hearth-head; a hearth-head says his hearth's answer as the Bone-bringer says the roll, and the floor puts it right if he says it wrong. No answer is the house's. The Winter-speaker closes the floor with every answer standing. The form is the nearest thing the Hvarn tell to an account of the end, and the end it imagines is not the world's. The text below is the form as it was said in the winter that [Dzorin]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the Hvurnash hearth was Bone-bringer, with the voices as they spoke. The last question in it is not part of the form. It has been asked from the floor in some winters and not in others, and it is set down here because it was asked that winter.

## The Closing

::: {.verse form=rouzen lang=en}
[Dzorin, the Bone-bringer:]{.speaker}/
That is the scholars' door, from the grass to the ushtakas' wall./
That is the conch-door, from the grass to the ushtakas' wall./
That is the roll./
It is longer than last winter by three./
The fire was banked twice for it./
It will be longer next winter./
I say that every winter./
It has been true every winter.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
Is there any word against the roll?

[The house:]{.speaker}/
None.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
Then it is the roll, as said./
Who asks the question this winter?

[A girl of the Tsedvern hearth, standing:]{.speaker}/
I ask it./
The roll is longer every winter./
The longest night is the same length every winter./
What happens when the roll is longer than the night?

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
The floor is open to the question./
The hearths answer in the order of speaking./
Tsedvern.

[The hearth-head of the Tsedvern hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Tsedvern hearth says:/
then the roll is said in two nights./
The scholars' door on the longest night,/
and the conch-door on the night after./
The ground is in two crossings already./
The roll can be in two nights./
That is the Tsedvern answer.

[An old man of the Klemverd hearth, from the floor:]{.speaker}/
The roll is said in one night/
so that a Roadwalker walks both crossings in one sitting/
and has the whole of the ground in his head at once./
Two nights is two roads in two heads.

[The hearth-head of the Tsedvern hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Tsedvern hearth says two nights./
It has heard the Klemverd word./
It says two nights.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
The Tsedvern answer stands./
Klemverd.

[The hearth-head of the Klemverd hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Klemverd hearth says:/
the roll is long because the house is long./
Every name in the roll is carried at a fire in this house./
Every child who stands up at a place/
is a child this house has fed./
The roll did not get long by itself./
It got long because there were more of us to fall,/
and there were more of us to fall/
because there were more of us./
When the roll is longer than the night,/
the house will be longer than the roll./
We will cut the end wall./
That is the Klemverd answer.

[An old woman of the Rhonzhel hearth, from the floor:]{.speaker}/
Cut the end wall, or carry your roof, Klemverd?

[The house laughs.]{.direction}

[The hearth-head of the Klemverd hearth:]{.speaker}/
Whichever. The house will be longer./
That is the Klemverd answer.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
The Klemverd answer stands./
Rhonzhel.

[The old woman of the Rhonzhel hearth, standing:]{.speaker}/
The hearth-head of the Rhonzhel hearth is at the beasts' end with a sick beast./
I say the Rhonzhel answer./
The Rhonzhel hearth says:/
the roll is longer than the night/
on the winter when nobody comes down./
When there is nobody to count in at the frost,/
nobody stands up at a place,/
and nobody says the roll,/
then it is longer than any night,/
because there is no night it is said in./
That is the end of the roll./
It is not the end of the ground./
The ground was there first./
That is the Rhonzhel answer.

[Nobody speaks against it.]{.direction}

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
The Rhonzhel answer stands./
Nobody has a word against it./
Nobody has ever had a word against it./
Dzelbern.

[The hearth-head of the Dzelbern hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Dzelbern hearth says:/
the places do not get more./
The upper shelf is the upper shelf./
The Knee is the Knee./
The names get more./
So when the roll is longer than the night,/
let the Bone-bringer say the places/
and nothing else,/
and at every place, let everyone who carries a name from it/
stand up at once/
and say their own./
The whole floor saying the roll./
Every name said at once,/
and every place said once./
That is not long./
That is the Dzelbern answer.

[A boy of the Vrestun hearth, from the floor:]{.speaker}/
Then nobody hears anybody.

[The hearth-head of the Dzelbern hearth:]{.speaker}/
Then everybody has said it./
That is the Dzelbern answer.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
The Dzelbern answer stands./
Vrestun.

[The hearth-head of the Vrestun hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Vrestun hearth says:/
a hearth that has been down to the gorge/
for a body the length of three ropes/
does not ask how long the roll is./
It asks who goes down next./
The Vrestun hearth will be on the rope./
That is the Vrestun answer.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
The Vrestun answer stands./
Shulner.

[The hearth-head of the Shulner hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Shulner hearth gives no answer this winter./
It has said none any winter.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
That is the Shulner answer, and it stands./
Hvurnash. Nurvharn.

[The hearth-head of the Hvurnash hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Hvurnash hearth says what the Klemverd hearth says,/
and says it shorter./
The house will be longer./
And it says the Bone-bringer is of the Hvurnash hearth this winter/
and has an answer of his own,/
and the Hvurnash hearth would like to hear it.

[The hearth-head of the Nurvharn hearth:]{.speaker}/
The Nurvharn hearth has the oldest wall./
It says:/
the night has always been longer than the roll./
So far./
That is the Nurvharn answer, and it is the same every winter,/
and every winter it has been true.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
The Hvurnash answer stands. The Nurvharn answer stands./
Bone-bringer, the floor has asked for your word./
It is not in the order of speaking./
Say it if you have one.

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
I have one./
It is the same every winter./
The Bone-bringer's answer is: I go down./
When the roll is longer than the night,/
I will go down./
When it is shorter than the night,/
I will go down./
How long the roll is, is not the Bone-bringer's./
Where the Dzelbern woman is, under the Long Lip,/
is the Bone-bringer's./
Ask me again at the thaw./
That is the answer.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
It is said./
Is there any other word on the question?

[A young Roadwalker of the Dzelbern hearth, from the floor:]{.speaker}/
There is a question on the question./
The roll is the dead the ground took./
The ones the house has named/
are not taken by the ground./
They are found in the snow in the valley,/
between the door and the next valley./
Who brings them up?/
Where are they in the roll?

[Nobody speaks.]{.direction}

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
That is not the order of speaking.

[The young Roadwalker:]{.speaker}/
Bone-bringer?

[Dzorin:]{.speaker}/
The roll is the ground./
The valley under the door/
is not the ground of the roll./
It is the house's./
I have said that since I took the office./
I have brought up two./
I will not say from where./
Where they are is not in the roll./
That is what I say.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
That is not the order of speaking/
and it is not an answer to the question/
and the council has no form for it./
It is said./
It is not kept.

[The young Roadwalker:]{.speaker}/
It is said.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
Then the floor on the question is closed./
Two nights, Tsedvern./
A longer house, Klemverd and Hvurnash./
The winter nobody comes down, Rhonzhel./
Everyone their own at once, Dzelbern./
Who goes down next, Vrestun./
None, Shulner./
So far, Nurvharn./
I go down, the Bone-bringer./
Every answer stands./
None is the word./
The council rises./
I hold the order of speaking/
and when the council rises I hold nothing./
Build the fire up.

[The child of the Tsedvern hearth:]{.speaker}/
It is still dark.

[The Winter-speaker:]{.speaker}/
It is still dark./
The night is longer than the roll./
This winter.
:::
