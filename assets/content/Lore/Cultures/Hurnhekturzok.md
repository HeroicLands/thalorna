The Hürnhëktūrzōk are the tribes of Hürnhëktūr, the Black Wilds: the steppe and broken hill-country that lies north of the [[affiliation-tanvurempr|Empire of Tānvür]], beyond the [[place-grkrhlmrgn|Grukari]] marches. They are horse-riders who live from grazing, and their wells and grazing grounds are what they fight over.

## Tribes and feuds

For generations the tribes fought one another over grazing, wells and blood debts. The empire's border garrisons kept them divided with gifts to one chief and raids against another.

## The Master Tiger's confederation

[[being-vusgurthurtzhuk|Vüsgürt Hürtzhük]], the Master Tiger of the Steppes, began as the war-leader of a minor tribe and bound the others to him by marriage, by treaty and by slaughter. The tribes now swear to him as their single war-leader. Their horsemen ride in disciplined companies, strike supply lines and outposts rather than towns, and withdraw before an imperial army can force a battle.

Hard winters and failing grazing drove the tribes south, and their riders raid the empire's northern provinces. The confederation is held together by Vüsgürt himself and by plunder.
