The schools of [[affiliation-linzethrthsr|Lin'Zethu elu Reth'Sa'ar]] set this _rethet_ before a pupil in his first year, after the sign-lists and before anything the temple would miss if he spoiled it. He copies it on potsherds until his hand is steady and then once on a roll, which the teacher corrects in the margin and keeps.

It is **The Instruction of Khelayu**, by [Khelayu]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Keeper of the King-Lists in the precinct of [[affiliation-rethsaar|Reth'Sa'ar]] beside the [Great Temple of Uqa'a]{.name data-type="place/structure"} on [[place-yathtelgu|Yath-Telgu]]. He kept the list under [Gez'el'Retha III]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Gar-Au whose crowning opens the age the Keepers call [[lore-akhrestcrown|the Restored Crown]] ([[lore-hillhouse|the Closing of the Hill House]]), and he wrote it in that reign's thirtieth year. It has a prologue giving the father's titles, maxims for his son [Zinuri]{.name data-type="being/npc"} on the day of the son's ordination as a copyist, and a passage near the end that the schools copy and do not explain.

The copy given here is a school roll of the age of [[lore-akhhsmeqes|the House of Meqes]], in the hand of a boy of [[place-chegu|Chegu]] named [Gari]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, with the teacher's corrections where he made them and the colophon the boy wrote when he was done. The rendering keeps the pupil's errors where the teacher marked them.

## The Prologue

Keeper of the King-Lists Khelayu, Lem'Nelgir of Reth'Sa'ar, who copied the list for twenty-six years and has kept it for seven, who holds the master copy in the god's precinct on Yath-Telgu and answers for the second copy at [[place-khelunu|Khelunu]], who entered [[lore-hillhouse|the closing of the hill house]] in the year the house closed, says this to his son Zinuri, Lem'Nelgir of Reth'Sa'ar, ordained copyist, on the day of his ordination, in Year 30 of Gar-Au Gez'el'Retha III, in the first month of [[lore-sheluessn|Shelu]], with the roads open.

Sit, and do not copy anything yet. I said that to you this morning in front of the others, because it is what the Keeper says, and I say it again here, with the door shut. You will copy this list for forty years. First learn to read it. It does not say what it means, and the house has never once corrected it.

What follows is not the list. It is what I know that is not on it. A Keeper writes one of these for his son if his son is ordained, and most sons burn it, which is lawful. Keep this one or do not. It is entered nowhere, and it is not read at my Reading, which is why I can put in it what I have put in it.
