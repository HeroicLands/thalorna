**The Roll at the Thresholds** is said by a crossbow company of the valleys at its oath stone, the _goitsa_, on the first morning of the [Feast of Thresholds]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} at the turning of the year ([[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]]), when the lords and vassals of the pantheon's faiths renew their bonds in the temples. The company keeps the feast the way its valley keeps it. The village priest stands by the stone as witness and says nothing until the end, and the captain calls the muster roll, the _therna_, from memory, because a company's roll is a thing carried in the captain's head and in the company's, and not in a book.

The words are fixed and the names change. Each company says the roll of its own year: the living who marched, the dead who are answered for by the man who marched beside them, the names earned since the last feast and the boys who join. The roll below is the one the valleys teach a new captain, with the names of one company's year in it. A company that has struck a name from its roll, the _neharlo_, keeps the place where the name was and says nothing there, every year that anyone of the company who knew the name is still alive. The company in this roll had struck one.

At the end the captain and the company say the contract over before the stone, and the priest says the god's name over it, and the year begins. A contract renewed at the stone is the one the company holds to; [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|the Order of the Sealed Word]] hears a broken one, and the company's own roll is where a broken one is remembered.

```poetry {form=litany lang=en}
THE CAPTAIN
Is the stone here?
THE COMPANY
The stone is here.
THE CAPTAIN
Is it cold?
THE COMPANY
It is cold.
THE CAPTAIN
Then it is the stone.

THE CAPTAIN
The year goes out at the gate.
THE COMPANY
Let it go out.
THE CAPTAIN
The year comes in at the gate.
THE COMPANY
Let it come in.
THE CAPTAIN
The god of the gate has two faces, and sees it go, and sees it come.
We have one face each, and we turn it to the stone.
THE COMPANY
We turn it to the stone.

THE CAPTAIN
This is the roll. I carry it.
Who marched with me this year, and is here, say here.
Who marched with me this year, and is not here, his companion says it.
Who was struck, nobody says.
THE COMPANY
Call it.

THE CAPTAIN
Garrutsa, the Wolf.
GARRUTSA
Here, and kept.
THE CAPTAIN
Oztuk, the Crag.
OZTUK
Here, and kept.
THE CAPTAIN
Átseka, the ewe, as the company has it, who is not gentle.
ÁTSEKA
Here, and kept.
THE CAPTAIN
Tsala, the Bell.
TSALA
Here. Kept. And loud.
THE COMPANY
And loud.
THE CAPTAIN
Ozka, the Wind off the Peaks.
OZKA
Here, and kept.
THE CAPTAIN
Betsa, the Cheese, who came home lighter than he went.
BETSA
Here, and kept, and lighter.
THE CAPTAIN
The sergeant.
THE SERGEANT
Here, and kept.
THE CAPTAIN
The surgeon.
THE SURGEON
Here. Kept. Every one I closed is here or answered for.
THE CAPTAIN
The rest of you.
THE COMPANY
Here, and kept.
THE CAPTAIN
And I am here, and kept, and I say so before you,
and if I am not, say so now, before the stone, before the year comes in.
THE COMPANY
You are kept.

THE CAPTAIN
Now the ones who marched and are not here.
Kolzarra, the Scree, who went first up the ladder at the wall in the summer, and came down it.
HIS COMPANION
I marched beside him. He is not here. He went up first. He kept it.
THE COMPANY
He kept it.
THE CAPTAIN
Ziduák, the Vulture, who was always first at the dead after a fight, and stripped them for the company and not for himself, and who is with them.
HIS COMPANION
I marched beside him. He is not here. He took nothing that was not the company's. He kept it.
THE COMPANY
He kept it.
THE CAPTAIN
Bortsula, the Byre, who held the mule-line the night the camp burned, and the mules lived.
HIS COMPANION
I marched beside him. He is not here. The mules lived. He kept it.
THE COMPANY
He kept it.
THE CAPTAIN
Uguzta, the Goat, who carried me out of the river on his back in the spring,
and died of the river in the autumn, in his bed, of the cough he got there.
HIS COMPANION
I marched beside him. He is not here. He carried the captain. He kept it.
THE CAPTAIN
He carried me.
THE COMPANY
He kept it.
THE CAPTAIN
Dúrreka, the Fog, who shot the herald of our own paymaster by mistake in the fog,
and paid the company's penalty for it from his own pay for three seasons, every bit,
and was killed in the fourth season, owing nothing.
HIS COMPANION
I marched beside him. He is not here. He owed nothing. He kept it.
THE COMPANY
He owed nothing. He kept it.
THE CAPTAIN
The singer. Who sang the long poem for this company twenty years after supper,
who was there for all of it, and who came home, and died at home this year by his own fire, old.
HIS COMPANION
I did not march beside him. Nobody did. He walked behind us, to see.
He is not here. He saw it. He kept it.
THE COMPANY
He saw it. He kept it.
THE CAPTAIN
Who sings it now?
THE NEW SINGER
I do. I was there. I will sing it as he sang it, and add this year.
THE COMPANY
Then add it.

THE CAPTAIN
Now the roll goes on, in the order it has always gone.
After the Goat it went to the next. Hear the next.

          [The captain says nothing for the length of a name.
          The company says nothing. Nobody answers.]

THE CAPTAIN
That place is kept.
THE COMPANY
That place is kept.
THE CAPTAIN
Nobody fills it. Nobody says it. It goes on the roll empty
as long as one of us who marched with that name is alive to know it is empty.
When the last of us is under the stone, the roll closes over it, and it is gone.
THE COMPANY
And it is gone.

THE CAPTAIN
Now the names earned this year.
The man who came over the wall second, behind the Scree.
What do we call him?
THE COMPANY
Garrunda. The Peak. He was the top of the wall.
THE CAPTAIN
Garrunda. Say it to him.
THE COMPANY
Garrunda.
GARRUNDA
I have it. It is the company's. I will keep it.
THE CAPTAIN
The boy who carried the bolts to the line under the arrows on the river bank,
who was not on the roll, and was there.
What do we call him?
THE COMPANY
Ekutsa. The Sheepdog. Up and down the bank all day, and never bit.
THE CAPTAIN
Then he is on the roll. Ekutsa. Say it to him.
THE COMPANY
Ekutsa.
EKUTSA
I have it. I will keep it.

THE CAPTAIN
Now who comes in at the gate?
THREE BOYS
We do.
THE CAPTAIN
Are you any good?
THREE BOYS
We will be.
THE CAPTAIN
That is the right answer. Not yet. You are on the roll as not yet.
Here, and not yet kept.
THREE BOYS
Here, and not yet kept.
THE COMPANY
Not yet.

THE CAPTAIN
Now the contract. Hands on the stone.
I owe you your pay, all of it, on the day,
and the terms as they are said, and not a bit more asked of you,
and your rites, if you fall, wherever you fall, before I march,
or your names on the bank, and your pay home, if I cannot.
THE COMPANY
You owe it.
THE CAPTAIN
What do you owe me?
THE COMPANY
The season, and the terms, and no more.
We go where the contract goes. We hold where it says hold.
We come home when it says home. We do not run.
THE CAPTAIN
You owe it.
What is owed?
THE COMPANY
What was said.
THE CAPTAIN
What was said?
THE COMPANY
What is owed.
THE CAPTAIN
Who breaks it?
THE COMPANY
Nobody here.
THE CAPTAIN
And if somebody here?
THE COMPANY
Then the stone knows it, and the Sealed Word hears it, and the roll keeps the place.

THE PRIEST
The god of the gate has heard it, with both faces.
THE COMPANY
And the stone.
THE PRIEST
And the stone.

THE CAPTAIN
The year is in at the gate. The roll is called.
Take your hands off the stone. It is cold.
Go home to your houses and tell them you are kept.
THE COMPANY
Kept.
```
