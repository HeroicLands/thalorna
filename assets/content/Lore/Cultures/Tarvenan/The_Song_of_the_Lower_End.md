**The Song of the Lower End** is the long poem, the _oldemo_, that the high valleys of [[place-asturath|Asturath]] sing of [[place-horngorge|the Horn Gorge]] ([[lore-horngorgebtl|The Battle of the Horn Gorge]]). It does not sing the closing of the upper end, which belongs to the lord [Tórsaro]{.name data-type="being/npc"} and to the plateau of [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] above it. It sings the lower end, where the gorge opens on the valley and the legion, when it turned, had to come through: the twelve masterless men who held it for two days, and the man who led them, whose name had been struck from Tórsaro's roll before the legion came, and who was given the earned name [Belartsa]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Threshold, on the stone where he died.

A long poem is sung after supper by someone who was there, and this one keeps the rule by keeping its first singer. Every singer of it sings as **the herd boy with the sling**, who followed the masterless men down to the lower end, went up the gorge wall with their word and came back down with Tórsaro's, and who saw at the horse fair what the three witnesses swore they saw. He is never named; the poem calls him what he was. The singer takes his voice and his age with it, so that the old man by the fire who says _I carried stone_ is saying the song's line and not his own.

The poem is in verse paragraphs of uneven length, each held on one vowel at the line's end. The asking at the plateau gate is sung three times, from the gate, from Tórsaro's side of it and from the stone, and the recall is sung three times, from the legate, from the lower end and from the dark. The valleys sing all six. [[place-kavrenath|Kávrenath]] sings the asking twice and holds the third for the end of the evening. The song names neither the legate nor the legion, and it says nothing of what lay under the stones that were carried to the cairn. Every singer ends on the same refusal, and the company at the fire says it with him.
