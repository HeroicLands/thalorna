The imperial core is the Tantsou of the great river basins and the plains between them: the capital, the provincial seats of the Warlords, the crowded river cities and the terraced villages that feed them. It is the Tantsou the rest of [[lore-tantsouclt|the Tantsou]] measure themselves against. Its people keep the eight castes in their strictest form, the register of persons at its most complete, and the court's manner of speech at its most formal.

A person of the core is of medium height and light to medium frame, with straight black hair, dark brown eyes and skin that runs from light to golden. Faces are smooth or clear. The basin climate is continental, so the weathering of a field laborer shows and the court's ladies and scholars, who stay indoors, do not have it.

The core is also the culture a character takes when the Tantsou are named with no region: its profile is the Tantsou profile.

## Where the core ends

The core ends where the register thins. South of the central basins the coast turns subtropical and its [[lore-tantsoutribclt|tributary peoples]] keep their own kingdoms' ways under the Empire's seal. North and west of the basins the [[lore-tantsoumrchclt|frontier peoples]] hold the walled marches. A subject of the core who travels to either finds the same registers, the same castes and the same formal manner kept differently.
