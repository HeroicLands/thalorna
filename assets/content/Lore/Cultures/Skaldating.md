The **Skaldating** is the yearly gathering of the [[affiliation-skaldscrcl|Skalds' Circle]]. The five Nordland kingdoms host it in rotation, and Aelwyth or Vrystwald occasionally does.

## The Gyldra

An apprentice presents his first saga, the _gyldra_, to the assembled skalds. If the assembly accepts it, his master swears him into the Circle and he is a skald in his own right. If it is rejected he may try again the following year, and twice more after that; after three rejections the apprenticeship ends without recrimination and without rank.

## The Repertoire

The standing repertoire is reviewed and verified by collective recitation. A dispute over the accuracy of a contested verse is resolved by the assembled body, against the standard that the accepted sagas of the senior skalds set.

## Judgment

The assembled skalds convict a skald who has broken the truth of the recital, and his silver arm-ring is broken in his presence at the next Skaldating. A skald killed for honest recital becomes the subject of a saga at the next Skaldating, and the saga names the killer.
