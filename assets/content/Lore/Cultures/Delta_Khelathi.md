The **Delta Khelathi** are the [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelathi]] of the [[affiliation-deltaselatu|Delta Selatu]], where the Zumelesh breaks into channels, marshes and silt-islands on its way to the Vilérian Sea. They keep the gods, the script, the calendar and the law of the valley. What sets them apart is the water they live beside and the strangers who arrive on it.

## Where They Live

Delta Khelathi live in the port cities such as [[place-garanlaghet|Gar-Anlaghet]] and [[place-amqelmiglet|Amqel-Miglet]], in the marsh villages of the papyrus-cutters, fowlers and reed-boat crews, and on the villa-estates of the coastal garden strip, where citrus, olive, grape, fig and date grow in the sea air.

## How They Live

Trade shapes the delta household. Factors, scribes, customs men and free artisans fill the port cities, and [[lore-taruzelianclt|Taruzelian]] and [[lore-vylarianclt|Vilérian]] quarters stand beside the temple districts. A delta family has foreign kin by marriage or foreign partners by contract, and it talks easily about both. The valley calls this cosmopolitan and sharp-tongued, and the delta calls the valley's reserve provincial.

The Delta worships [[lore-thubaidty|Thuba'i]], god of prosperity and pleasure, with the rest of the Khelathi pantheon, and it keeps the ledger of obligation as strictly as the upper valley does. A delta merchant who defaults on a debt to a foreign factor is judged by the Genzet like any other Khelathi.

## What They Look Like

The delta population is the most mixed in the empire. Skin runs lighter on average than in the upper valley, from tawny and olive through brown to the deep brown of families with long upriver roots. Hair is more often dark brown than black, eyes more often brown or hazel, and a coast-reared complexion carries the sun in it. Builds are medium and rounded by good eating. Dress follows the valley's linen but takes foreign cloth, dyed wools and imported jewelry readily.
