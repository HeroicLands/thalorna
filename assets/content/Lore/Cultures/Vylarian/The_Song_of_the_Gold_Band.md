The Song of the Gold Band is the road-song of the [Karzen Ethlos Urzelthos]{.name data-type="affiliation"}, the legion of the northern march that took the [Felzen Hiltheren]{.name data-type="affiliation"}'s eagle out of the reeds below the [[place-moktur|Moktur]] passes ([[lore-blackmarshbtl|The Battle of the Black Marsh]]) and carries a gold band on its name for it. A road-song, _pozersorima_, is sung on the march to the step. One man calls the line, by custom the oldest Kamezos of the leading century, and the century answers with the half-line; the next century takes it up when the first runs out of breath. Every legion has one, and every legion steals verses from the others and denies it. The song's hero is [Gretalis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Kamezos who went into the water for the eagle when it was quiet, and the song gives him no house name, as soldiers give each other none.

The song has two halves, and everyone in the legion knows where the join is. The **parade verses** are the ones the legion's Holtakezos allows on the Velzorpozera and in front of the governor at [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]]: the valley, the water, the eagle, the band, and the four words of the manual at the end. The **road verses** are everything the centuries have added on the long stretch between [[place-dolvar|Dolvar]] and the towers, and nobody has ever written them down by order. Two of the road verses are the ones the noble officers do not hear. One is for the Felzen Hiltheren, whose eagle came home in the same year its name was struck from the roll. The other is for the [Doreken Hiltheren]{.name data-type="affiliation"}, the Twelfth, whose eagle did not come home at all ([[lore-twelfthloss|The Loss of the Twelfth]]), and the centuries sing it last and quietly, on the road west, when the [Kervaron Spine]{.name data-type="place/feature"} is in sight. A Ethorlusos learns in his first month on the march to find something to look at on the far side of the road while they sing it.

The recruits learn the song before they learn the oath. The text below is the song as the first cohort sings it on the road, the call set flush and the answer set in.
