The **Border Khelathi** are the [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelathi]] of the [[affiliation-borderselatu|Border Selatu]], the ten thinly peopled provinces where the empire meets the southern tribes, the eastern hills and the western herders. They keep the valley's gods, script and law, and keep them under arms.

## Where They Live

Border Khelathi live in garrison towns and fortress-lines along the southern cataracts, in the mining camps of the eastern hills, and in caravan-stations on the gold-road and the western grazing country. Settlements are small and walled, and a family expects to move with a posting.

## How They Live

A border household is a soldier's household. Sons and daughters grow up around drill-grounds, and the frontier sends the empire's hardest soldiers and most experienced officers to the valley. The mines, the caravans and the herds supply the garrisons, and weighed silver and gold circulate more freely here than grain does. Society is rougher and more mixed than in the valley: southern tribespeople, hill miners and western herders settle in the border towns, marry in, and are Khelathi within a generation or two.

[[affiliation-qeztu|Qeztu]], the war-god, is strong on the frontier and rare in the valley. The ledger of obligation holds here as elsewhere, and a soldier's debt of service is reckoned with the same care as a farmer's debt of grain.

## What They Look Like

Skin runs deep, from brown through dark brown to ebony, in the south and on the caravan roads. Builds are broad and hard, shaped by drill, mining and long marches, and the sun and wind leave faces weathered and sometimes leathery. People are on average taller and heavier in frame than the valley's. Dress is practical: short kilts, scale or leather armor, headcloths against sun and dust.
