**The Account of What Was Given** is the first account a [[lore-kaliharanclt|Kaliharan]] of the interior hears said through. Every household of [[place-klhrbowl|the Bowl]] and [[place-klhrring|the Ring]] recites it at [Dry-Turn]{.name data-type="lore/calendar"}, on the evening a child of six or seven is given her first sílhari account to hold. The eldest woman of the household says it, the household answers its one refrain with her, and the child says the last lines alone, which are the first entry of her own account. It is the opening of the island's record as the households hold it: the people [[lore-theithari|the Ithari]] found, what they gave, and the morning the island woke to their absence. It holds nothing from before the coming, because the record holds nothing.

Every household says it in the same words, and the custodians who visit in the week after the first rain hear it said and tell a household where its words have drifted. A household corrected goes back to the beginning of the passage, never to the slip. The households of the terraces and those of the Ring hold one line of the catalog differently, and each holds its own exactly; the rendering gives both. The recitation transcribed here is a household's of [[place-klhrestrrc|the Eastern Terraces]], and the household asked that its names be left out, so the rendering marks where they are said.

: The Account of What Was Given {#what-was-given}

::: {.verse form=account lang=en}
[The eldest:]{.speaker}/
I say my name, and the name of the one who held this before me./
[She says her full name, and her mother's.]{.direction}/
This is not my account./
This is the first account. It is the island's./
Every account on the island is held because this one is held./
Sit where you can hear. Keep your hands still./
When it is done you will be given your own.

The record opens with their coming./
What was before the coming is not held./
Nobody holds it, and nobody has held it./
Do not ask what was before. There is nothing to give you.

This much is held of us when they came./
We were on the island./
We were few, in bands, and a band did not know the next band's name./
We ate what fell, and what we could kill, and what came up on a stick./
We slept where the dark found us./
We had fire, and let it go out, and made it again, and let it go out./
We did not count the rain./
We did not know that the swifts come back./
When one of us broke a leg, that one stayed where the leg broke./
That is what is held of us. It is not much. It is held exactly.

They came to the island./
The record does not say from where. It says that they came./
They did not take the island. There was nothing on it to take./
They found us in the forest, a band here and a band there,/
and they took us in.

They were people./
Hold that before you hold anything else of them. They were people./
They were not the rain. They were not the mountain./
They were not the Whole, and they did not say they were./
They were impatient, and the record holds it./
They found things funny, and the record holds that./
When one of us lay down on the ground in front of them,/
they lifted him up and put a digging stick in his hand./
The record does not hold their faces. Do not give them faces./
The record does not hold their names. They gave none./
The record does not hold their voices./
Hear this, because you will want to give them voices:/
they did not speak.

A thing was not known, and then it was known,/
and nothing was said between./
That is how all of it came./
Every part of what you will be given came so.

We did not know that a hillside holds water if it is cut into steps./
Then we knew where to cut the step,/
and how high the wall stands, and which stone goes at its foot,/
and that the wall is the house's, and the house rebuilds it./

[The household:]{.speaker}/
It was not said. It was known.

[The eldest:]{.speaker}/
[The households of the Ring say this in place of the wall, and answer it the same:]{.direction}/
We did not know a slope./
Then we knew which slope holds and which comes down in Deepwater,/
and where the path goes, and where the cloud lies on it, and whose path it is.

[The household:]{.speaker}/
It was not said. It was known.

[The eldest:]{.speaker}/
We ate all that we gathered, to the last of it./
Then we knew to keep a part back,/
and put it in the ground at the Wet-Turn, and wait, and not eat it while we waited.

[The household:]{.speaker}/
It was not said. It was known.

[The eldest:]{.speaker}/
We did not know that a leg can be set./
Then we knew where to pull, and how far,/
and how the splint is bound, and how long the leg lies still,/
and the one whose leg broke walked again in the next turning.

[The household:]{.speaker}/
It was not said. It was known.

[The eldest:]{.speaker}/
We did not count the rain./
Then we knew to count it, every fall of it, from Wet-Turn to Dry-Turn,/
and to say the count at Dry-Turn, every house, as this house said it tonight/
before you were called in.

[The household:]{.speaker}/
It was not said. It was known.

[The eldest:]{.speaker}/
We looked at a thing when it was in front of us, and not after./
Then we knew to look at one thing every day, at one hour,/
the same ants, the same reeds, the same flock coming home,/
and to hold what we saw, and to say it at dusk./
That is the looking. You begin it tomorrow.

[The household:]{.speaker}/
It was not said. It was known.

[The eldest:]{.speaker}/
We took what was near until it was gone, and then we moved./
Then we knew that a part is tended,/
neither left nor worked to the bone,/
and that a band that tends its part does not need to move,/
and that a band that does not move becomes a house./
That is the tending. You will be given a corner of the garden.

[The household:]{.speaker}/
It was not said. It was known.

[The eldest:]{.speaker}/
We did a thing one way, and the next day another way, and forgot both./
Then we knew to do it the way it was done,/
and to teach it the way it was taught,/
and not to put in a better way where it matters./
That is the holding. It is why you are hearing these words and not others.

[The household:]{.speaker}/
It was not said. It was known.

[The eldest:]{.speaker}/
When two bands wanted one spring, the band with more spears had it./
Then we knew to sit down, both bands, on either side of the water,/
and hear the one and hear the other,/
and to hear a third who had no share in the spring,/
and to sit until the matter was settled, though it took the turning./
That is the council. You will not sit in it for a long time.

[The household:]{.speaker}/
It was not said. It was known.

[The eldest:]{.speaker}/
We knew the reef, and we knew the canopy, and they were two things./
We knew the rain, and we knew the terrace, and they were two things./
We knew ourselves, and the swifts, and they were two things./
Then we knew them as one thing./
The reef and the canopy, the rain and the terrace, the swifts and us in it,/
one thing, living, and every part of it answering to every other part./
That is the Whole. It was the last to come, and it is under all the rest.

[The household:]{.speaker}/
It was not said. It was known.

[The eldest:]{.speaker}/
None of it was said when it came./
The word for each thing was known with the thing,/
and nobody had said it./
Step, wall, seed, splint, the looking, the tending, the holding,/
the council, the Whole: every word came so./
The words were given as the rest was given./
We hold them as we hold the rest, and we do not change them.

They were with us a long time./
The oldest of us grew old while they were on the island,/
and the young grew old after them,/
and the young after those, more times than a house can count./
The record does not hold how many. It holds that it was long.

They left things on the island./
You know the stone at our spring. You have put your hand on it/
when the kaan-vaar was not looking, and it gave under your hand./
They left that, and the great ones you have not seen,/
in the lake and on the mountain and on the reefs./
We were shown where the hand goes on each, and in what order, and the words./
We were not shown why./
The kaan-vaar holds the how. Nobody holds the why./
Do not ask the stone why. It will not tell you, and the kaan-vaar cannot.

Then something went through them./
The record holds no more of it than that. Something went through them./
It does not say what. It does not say from where./
It does not say whether it hurt them./
In the night they went to the ones who kept the stones,/
and gave them more to hold, quickly,/
more than they had given in all the time before,/
and the keepers held it./
The keepers hold it still./
Nobody holds which part of what they hold was given that night.

In the morning the island woke, and they were not on it./
Not one of them. Not on the mountain, not in the lake, not on the reefs./
The record does not say where they went./
Do not ask where. Nobody holds it, and the ones who ask it go on asking./
That was the morning./
The Walks are counted from it, every Walk,/
and the count is kept on the mount in the lake, and it is not wrong.

We did not go back to the forest./
We did not let the fire go out./
We did not let the walls come down in the first Deepwater, or the next./
We counted the rain the next Dry-Turn, every house,/
with nobody on the island who had given us the count./
We kept what was given, every part of it./
We keep it./
That is the island.

That is what was given./
It is held as it was held, and so I hold it.

[The household:]{.speaker}/
So it is held.

[The eldest:]{.speaker}/
Now you./
Say your name, and your mother's./
[The child says her full name, and her mother's.]{.direction}/
Now say what you saw this morning. Say it the way you saw it.

[The child:]{.speaker}/
This is the first of my account./
Dry-Turn, the morning before this evening./
The swifts came back to the eaves./
I counted nine./
One went in under the ridge-pole and did not come out while I watched./
The sun came over the wall of the third terrace while I counted./
That is what I saw.

[The eldest:]{.speaker}/
That is held. Hold it./
Tomorrow, at the same hour, the same eaves.
