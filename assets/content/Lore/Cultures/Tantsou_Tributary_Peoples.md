The tributary peoples live along the warm, wet southern coast and in the river deltas behind it, where the climate is subtropical and mangrove shoreline marks the sea's edge. Their country is rice paddy, fishing channel and port. Each of its older kingdoms still sends tribute to the court and still keeps its own rulers, shrines and festivals under the Empire's seal, and each is written into the imperial registers all the same: a tributary subject holds a caste and a lineage page like any other Tantsou.

They share [[lore-tantsouclt|the Tantsou]] language, script and calendar, and speak it with the accents and borrowed words of the kingdoms they come from. The foreign merchant quarters of the southern ports are tributary country, and their people deal with outsiders more readily than the core does.

## Look

A tributary person is shorter and slighter than a person of the core, with a light frame. Skin is tawny to brown, warmed by the southern sun, and the face carries the sun-kissed look of people who work on water and in paddies. Hair is black and eyes are dark brown.
