Midsumar is the summer-solstice festival, when the sun barely sets and the northern coasts kindle bonfires on their promontories. Shared feasts celebrate light and community, with [[lore-bjartrdty|Bjartr]] central to the observance. The faithful leap low flames for luck, and the long bonfire-night brings the community together at the brightest turn of the year.
