**The Lessons of the Silver Veil** are the sayings a girl of the [Academy of the Silver Veil]{.name data-type="affiliation"} (see [[lore-heladsilverveil|the Founding of the Silver Veil]]) at [[place-kalydria2|Kalidria]] learns by heart, one year's lessons in each of the eight years from her entry at twelve to her license at twenty. She says them aloud to her teacher at the end of each year, all of them, from the first year's to the year she is finishing, and she may not go on to the next year's until she has said them without a fault. The Academy holds that [Lékhisira Kálides]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, its founder, said them, and the schools of [[affiliation-thyrenae|Thirenae]] hold that she said the first rule and nothing else; the sayings themselves say nothing about who said them.

Copies circulate outside the Academy, in every city of [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]] where a graduate has served, and the Academy neither owns nor disowns them. Every copy stops at the end of the seventh year. The eighth year's lessons are said and not written, and a copy that claims to have them is a forgery or a joke, and the Academy has never said which. Where the eighth year's lessons would stand, the copies write the first rule, in the founder's words, and nothing after it.

The sayings name no opponent and no master. A graduate quoting them at a governor's table is quoting the wisdom of her school, and she says so.
