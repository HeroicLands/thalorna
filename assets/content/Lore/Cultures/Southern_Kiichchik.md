The southern Kì'ìch'chik share the faith, the count of days, the debt and the tongue of the pyramid cities, and live on the stepped ground the road climbs through: the river valleys and iron mountains of [[affiliation-ixbaltzaku|Ish'bal Tsa'ku]], the thin-aired plateau of [[affiliation-kuxiixbal|K'ushi Ish'bal]] and the fortress-temples of [[affiliation-qapteltzakl|Q'aptel Tsa'kul]]. [[lore-kiichchikclt|The central note]] describes what they hold in common.

They differ from the heartland in how they live. Terraces and canals hold their fields, every town is two halves with a lord and kin groups for each, and the cord-keepers count households in twenties and in four hundreds. Long years of cold wind and high sun leave them shorter and broader in the chest than the people of the lowlands, with ruddy, weathered faces.
