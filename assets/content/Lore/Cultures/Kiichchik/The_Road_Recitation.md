**The Road Recitation**, _ch'umel kil ish'lan_ (the voice of the road), is the short form of the recitation of the trials of [[place-chaltzumaq|Ch'al Ts'umaq]] that the death-walkers of [[affiliation-tzuqilixbal|Faith of Ts'uqil Ish'bal]] teach to every road shrine keeper of [[affiliation-xilanixlan|Faith of Shilan Ish'lan]]. A traveler who dies on the white road is the keeper's charge. The keeper sits with the body through the night, says what he knows of the trials, and sends for the death-walkers of the nearest city, who come at first light and give the full recitation over the body. The death-walkers name many more chambers than the road form, which names five and says plainly that it names five.

The night watch has its rules from the house, and the road changes them. In a house the family sits and listens and does not speak, and only the Speaker of the Trials talks. On the road there is rarely any family, so whoever has stopped at the shrine that night sits as the family: a carter, a pilgrim, a merchant's porters, a runner waiting for his relief. The keeper asks nothing of them but silence. The keeper takes no fee. A family in the city pays for accuracy, and pays the most it can; the road form is free, and the keeper has said what it is before he begins.

This is the recitation as [K'ushi Ts'alu]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, keeper of the shrine at [Ts'ilil Ch'imik]{.name data-type="place/site"} ("Swallow Hill") on the great eastern white road, said it over [Shelem Q'aptel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a runner of the [[affiliation-chumishlan|Ch'um Ish'lan]]. Shelem Q'aptel came up the last stage before the shrine at a dead run in the second half of the night, gave his cord and his memorized message to the relief at the station beside the shrine, sat down on the step, and died there. The relief ran on before anyone knew he was dead, because a message cannot wait. Two merchants and a porter who were sleeping at the shrine sat the night. The keeper shaped the deceptions to a runner's road, which a keeper is allowed to do and the death-walkers are not: the death-walkers say each deception in the words of the codex, and a keeper says it in words the dead will know.

: ch'umel kil ish'lan {#ch-umel-kil-ish-lan}

::: {.verse form=recitation lang=en work=ch-umel-kil-ish-lan}
[The keeper:]{.speaker}/
Shelem Q'aptel./
You have put the cord down./
The relief has it. It is on the road./
You are on the road too./
Listen to me./
You know how to listen on the road.

I keep the road. I do not walk with the dead./
The death-walkers know many rooms./
I know five./
I will name the five./
The rest are theirs, and they are coming./
Walk until they reach you.

You go the way the sun goes at night./
He goes down in the west./
He comes up in the east./
Walk behind him./
Not in front of him. Not beside him./
Behind him./
He knows the way. You know how to follow a light on a road.

[The sitters do not speak. A merchant puts copal on the fire.]{.direction}

[The keeper:]{.speaker}/
This is the first room./
Its name is the Black Water./
You go down into it./
It is cold. It is not deep./
Behind you a light will come up,/
like the dawn coming up behind a runner on the eastern road./
It is not the dawn./
The dawn is in front of you./
Do not turn round./
Go on. The road goes on.

This is the second room./
Its name is the Hearth./
There is a fire in it, and people sitting at the fire./
They will make room for you./
They will say: sit, you have run a long way, the relief is not here yet./
Do not sit./
The ones who sit there are the lost./
They sat down once./
They did not get up./
Go on. The road goes on.

This is the third room./
Its name is the Narrow Place./
The walls are knives./
Go through it sideways,/
the way you go through the crowd at the city gate./
In the middle of it a lord stands in the road./
He has the face of your Sun Lord./
He has the sun on his breast./
He will tell you to stop./
He will tell you to give him the cord./
You have no cord./
And no lord may stop a runner on the road./
You know the law. Say it to him./
Then go round him./
Go on. The road goes on.

This is the fourth room./
Its name is the House of the Jaguar./
There is no lie in this room./
There is the jaguar./
He will look at you./
Let him look./
He does not hate the deer./
He does not hate you./
When he has looked, he lets you by./
Go on. The road goes on.

This is the fifth room./
Its name is the Owl's Door./
An owl sits over the door./
It will speak to you in a good voice./
It will say the road is a trap./
It will say: go back up, the cord is not finished,/
your mother is alive, your mother is at the gate./
It will show you a way up./
It is a real way. Some go by it./
They come back, and they come back wrong./
Do not go back up./
Your mother will hear it from the road./
Go on. The road goes on.

[The keeper:]{.speaker}/
That is the fifth room./
I do not know the names beyond it./
I have not sold you any of this./
You owe the road nothing./
The road owed you this, and I have paid it./
The death-walkers are coming at first light./
They will name the rest./
Walk behind the sun until they reach you./
Do not run./
You ran enough tonight.

[The sitters do not speak. The fire is fed.]{.direction}

[The keeper:]{.speaker}/
When the sun comes up behind you/
we will carry you to the shrine stone./
Every traveller leaves something of the road there./
We will leave the thong of your sandal./
You wore it through on the last stage./
Shelem Q'aptel./
Go on. The road goes on.
:::
