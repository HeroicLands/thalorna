**The Line of the House of Hálvireth** is a _sírnerne_, a roll of names, as the bard of a lowland house sings it at a _kurnis_, a feast at which the genealogies are sung. The house of [Hálvireth]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} holds river-meadows in the [Berav basin]{.name data-type="place/feature"} below [[place-beravel|Péravel]], and it gives this feast on the night a child of the house is named; the bard sings the line from the child up, so that the child is counted into it in the hearing of everyone who might dispute it. What stands here is the short line, the heads of the house only, which is sung at a naming; the long line, with every branch and every marriage, takes a night and is sung at a death.

The recital is in two forms. The bard's frame, which opens and closes it, is in rhymed couplets to the harp. The line itself is not composed and is not rhymed: it is said, each head in a set form, each line closing on the name that opens the next, exactly as [[affiliation-bardicolgs|the Colleges]] proved it, and a bard who changes a word of a proof has changed the proof. Each head is given one deed, the deed the house has chosen to keep, and the dead for whom trees stand are named with their trees. A debt of honor the line has never paid is sung at every feast until it is paid, and this line carries one, to a house whose line has ended.

The line was proved before the Colleges by [Árnolen]{.name data-type="being/npc"} in the year the mountains closed ([[lore-sorvandaclose|The Sórvanda Closing]]), when every house set the Colleges to prove its descent, and the proof joins the house to the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] through [Sírtala]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a woman of the [Dhárgandi]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} who came down to the basin and married a river trader. Above her the names are in the high tongue, and the house sings no further up the Dhárgandi line than three names, and hands the rest to the Tradition Keepers, whose it is. The Dhárgandi who come down to the autumn fair at [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]] keep no daughter of [Ronken]{.name data-type="being/npc"} who went down to the basin. The house has sung her at the fair every autumn since [Velthar]{.name data-type="being/npc"} went up and sang the line there, and nobody at the fair has stood up.

The verses are in iambic tetrameter, rhymed in couplets.

: The Line of the House of Hálvireth {#line}

The bard:

::: {.verse form=sirnerne lang=en work=the-line-of-the-house-of-halvireth-1}
The bard./
Set down your cups and close the door./
Trim the lamps; listen, house gathered here./
I sing the roll. No word here belongs to me./
I sing, and afterward we eat; the feast hears proof./
If you sing it differently, rise in this hall./
Speak tonight; do you hold another descent?/
If you do not, eat and keep it for a year./
The child is here and named already; hear her at the feast./
The house has named her; we count her into the roll./
From her, the smallest, to the first, the old descent speaks plainly./
Listen, house; listen, gathered people./
This is not mine; the clan holds its recitation.
:::

::: {.verse form=sirnerne lang=elav enc=elavsemaronenc work=the-line-of-the-house-of-halvireth-1}
Lyeronno./
Légathen tose al hírkon. súndathen al ganda./
Kérnathen tose al henthra. salkathen al haltha./
Lyero munde al sírnerne. nond en leithon mundri tis./
Lyero munde. málmathen nyes gonde. en saenar al kurnis./
Gyese lyero tose derar. róunithen tose rith halthon./
Ryenathen tose ti óssenda. he dárkathen tose al kenron./
Gyese nond. málmathen tose. géilathen uk kirdon rith kirdis./
En al kenlon tis. geinde lenthon. salkathen tose al kurnis./
Náemathen al halthon gene. deilnathen nyes gene rith sírnerne./
Threl gene. al lunnar. gierth al treinen. ryenathen al úrkenon sérne./
Salkathen tose. al halthon. salkathen tose al kentalon./
Nond en ti mundri. dárkathen al mougon al kenron.
:::
