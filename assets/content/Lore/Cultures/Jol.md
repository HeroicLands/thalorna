Jól is the twelve-night midwinter season around the solstice, when hofs keep their lights burning and halls open their feasts. The dead are invited to the table, and the living keep vigil against the long dark. [[lore-odvardty|Ódvar]] is prominent in the season; [[lore-thrunvalddty|Thrúnvald]]’s priests bless the hearth, [[lore-solrundty|Sólrún]]’s priestesses conduct rites for loves and marriages, and skalds sing the cycle of hero-sagas. A seeker may keep the howe-wake on its ninth night, with a völva’s customary leave, but a private answer from the dead must be supported by living evidence.
