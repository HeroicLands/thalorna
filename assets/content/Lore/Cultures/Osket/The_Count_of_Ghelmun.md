At the closing of the open months, when the last caravan of the year is down and [[place-tsokhar|Tsokhar]] turns back into a village living on its herds, each [[lore-osketclt|Osket]] hearth that holds a road says its count. The count is the _yoighboik_, the knot of the dead: the road said stage by stage, and at every stage the people the road has kept, each with the load carried and the day. The guide-mother says it and the [[miscgear-guidecord|cord-keeper]] ties the season's dead into the cord beside her as she reaches them. The Osket do not say where the dead go. The count says where they stopped.

**The Count of Ghelmun**, _Ghelmun yoighboik_, is the count of the hearth of [Ghelmun]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} at Tsokhar, which holds a share of [[place-meghadvar|Meghadvar]], the storm-door, the westernmost and shortest-windowed road over the wall. Its guide-mother, [Aungum]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, says it from the hearth with the household answering each of the dead, and its cord-keeper, [Qolkel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, ties. Ghelmun's guide-mother goes up behind the hearth's caravans while her knees hold, and says the morning on the road at the bend under the peak, so the dead of her own seasons are dead she saw stop. Every line of a count closes on how the guide-mother knows it, as every finite verb in [[skill-osketlng|Osket]] does: seen with her own eyes, told to her by the men who walked the road or by the woman who held the count before her, or read from what the road showed. A count therefore falls from _seen_ to _told_ as it goes back. Ghelmun's falls further, because the hearth lost a cord-keeper untimely in Aungum's grandmother's time, and with her the reading of the cord. What the hearth knows of the road's dead before that loss is what the second walking of the road found on it, and the count says it as _read_. Before the second walking it says nothing at all.

The count below is the one Aungum said at the closing in which Ghelmun tied three new dead, with a stranger wintering at the hearth let hear it, which Ghelmun allows and many hearths do not. The stages are named here as a stranger is told them, by what they are; their Osket names are the hearth's.

::: {.verse form=yoighboik lang=en work=ghelmunyoighboik}
[Aungum, guide-mother of Ghelmun, from the hearth. Qolkel sits beside her with the cord.]{.direction}

Meghadvar. Ghelmun./
This is the road, and these are the ones it kept./
I say the stage. I say who stopped there, and the load, and the day./
I say how I know it. Where I say it wrongly, Qolkel does not tie.

[The household:]{.speaker} Say it.

The last terrace, where the lowland ends and the road is ours./
Nobody stopped there.

The first water, a day above the terrace./
Nobody stopped there.

The black shelf, under the overhang, where the yaks go one and one./
[Shaung]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske}, with a load of salt, at the opening of the road in my mother's last year. Seen./
[Molshel]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske}, with a load of barley, in the second month, the year after. Seen.

[The household:]{.speaker} Kept.

..The shelf goes out from under the rock and the snow comes off the rock./
..That is the black shelf. I say the stage again, so Qolkel knows where we are./
..The black shelf.

The scree below the long shelf./
[Qölkom]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske}, with a load of copper, on the eleventh day of this season's second month. Seen.

[Qolkel ties.]{.direction}

[The household:]{.speaker} Kept.

..I said the morning. The road was open./
..The stone moved at noon and the morning had not said it would./
..The load went with him. The yak came down./
..This is a new knot. Qolkel has it.

The long shelf, two days of shelf, the ledge the road follows round the shoulder./
[Hülsh]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske}, with a load of nothing, coming back, in the cold at the end of last season. Seen./
[Ghomse]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske}, with two loads of indigo, in the first month of the year the bird circled the peak. Seen./
[Baidosk]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske}, with a load of salt, at the closing, before I held the count. Told.

[The household:]{.speaker} Kept.

..The year the bird circled the peak I held the caravan three mornings./
..On the fourth the bird was gone and I said the road was open./
..Ghomse was on the long shelf on the fourth day out./
..I do not say what the bird was doing. I say the mornings, and the shelf, and the load./

The hail stage, above the shelf, where there is no water and the shelter is a wall of stones./
[Ghaulum]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske}, with a load of wool, in the second month of a year my mother counted. Told./
[Hülkh]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske}, with a load of salt, in the same month. Told./
[Nushang]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske}, with a load of barley, on the same day. Told.

[The household:]{.speaker} Kept.

..Three on one day at the hail stage. My mother said the three each closing of her life,/
..and her mother told them to her. The wall of stones at the hail stage was built higher that year,/
..by the men of this hearth, a stone for each, and it is the wall the road sleeps behind.

The steps, where the yaks are unloaded and the loads go up on the men./
A man of the caravan, with a load of lowland cloth, carried on his own back, on the ninth day of this season's second month. Seen.

[Qolkel ties.]{.direction}

[The household:]{.speaker} Kept.

..His name is the caravan's. It went down with the caravan./
..He would not give the load to a man of this hearth at the foot of the steps./
..He carried it up himself, and the steps kept him, and the load came up./
..The caravan has the load. The road has the man.

The bend under the peak, where the cloud is read from./
Nobody stopped there. The morning is said there.

The col. The tower of the summit priests is on it, and the blessing is said./
That is theirs. Nobody of this road stopped on the col.

[The household:]{.speaker} Over.

..Over the col we go down. The count goes down as the road does.

The cold shelf, the first stage down, where the ice moves in the warm years./
[Khülom]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske}, with a load of tin, in a year my mother counted. Told./
[Aungur]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske}, with a load of salt, in the year before that. Told.

[The household:]{.speaker} Kept.

..The ice moves. In the year of Khülom it moved a man's length in a night,/
..and my mother's mother said at the closing that the shelf had moved and where,/
..and the cord has it, and I walk the shelf a man's length further from the ice than she did.

The two waters, where the streams come in from both sides./
[Hülsh]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske}, my sister's son, with a load of iron, coming back over to us, on the last day of this season. Seen.

[Qolkel ties.]{.direction}

[The household:]{.speaker} Kept.

..There are two named Hülsh at this closing. One is on the long shelf and one at the two waters./
..The wind is one word and two men had it. Qolkel ties them apart./
..He was coming back with the iron. The iron is here.

The scree of the far side, the long stone slope./
[Qolash]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske}, with a load of salt, at the closing, in my mother's mother's first year of holding the count. Told./
[Golkash]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske}, with a load of barley, in the second month of that year. Told.

[The household:]{.speaker} Kept.

Here my mother's mother began.

..Before her the cord was Uchom's./
..[Uchom]{.name data-type="being/npc" lang=oske} kept the cord of this hearth and was taken by a fever in the closed months, in her thirtieth year,/
..and had not given the cord to the one after her./
..The cord is in the store-room. Nobody has read it since. Nobody will.

[The household:]{.speaker} Unread.

..That year this hearth took no party over the road./
..My mother's mother and the men of the hearth walked it. They walked it in the open months, slowly, with no load,/
..from the last terrace to the far descent and back, stage by stage, and looked at it,/
..and what the road showed them is the count that comes next. It is read.

The long shelf. A rope in the ice, and the bones of a hand closed on it. Read./
The hail stage. A load of salt in the wall of stones, and no beast. Read./
The black shelf. A knife-handle of yak horn, and the snow over the rest. Read./
The steps. A shoe of iron of the old shape. Read./
The cold shelf. Two, side by side under the shelf, with nothing to say what they carried. Read.

[The household:]{.speaker} Kept.

..That is all the second walking found./
..The road kept more than that. We know it by the road. We do not know them by name./
..My mother's mother tied what she found, and the cord begins again from her knot.

Before the second walking./
..They are on the road. Every stage has them. I do not say them, because I do not have them./
..We walk past them on every stage, in the open months, with the loads./
..That is how they are reached.

[The hearth is quiet while Qolkel holds the cord without tying.]{.direction}

The stages of the dust, down to the far descent./
Nobody stopped there.

The far descent, where the road comes down to the edge-towns and is not ours./
Nobody stopped there.

Meghadvar. Ghelmun./
Three new knots. Qölkom on the scree. A man of the caravan on the steps. Hülsh at the two waters./
The road is shut. The count is tied./
Qolkel, put it in the store with the others. Bring it out at the opening.

[Qolkel:]{.speaker} It is tied.
:::
