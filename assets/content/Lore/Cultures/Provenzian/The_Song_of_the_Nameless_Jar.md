**The Song of the Nameless Jar** is a satire sung in the temple squares of the southern cities during the [Festival of the Golden Harvest]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} of [[affiliation-olvenia|Ólvenia]], when the merchants close their shops and the squares fill with singers. It is not sung at a table. The singers who sing it have no patron, which is why they can, and a sílador with a patron does not have it in his book. Its maker is not known; the song gives itself to a singer who asked a question at a great house in the spring and was not asked back.

Its subject is the quickened field at [[place-kyvarel|Kivarel]] (The [[place-bldfldkyvarel|Blood-field of Kivarel]]), whose ground grows the best fruit in the barony, and whose fruit goes into the finest conserves in the kingdom under no particular name. The song never names the field, the barony or the vondar. It praises the conserve in every stanza, as a guest praises his host's table, and comes back every time to the one question a guest does not ask. Every compliment in it carries the charge.

The poem is rhymed ABAB.
