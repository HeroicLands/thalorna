**The Five Defects** is the telling the clerks of the [Merchant Hall]{.name data-type="place/structure"} of [[place-khryshteikhos2|Krusteikhos]] give the city's new apprentices once a year, on the afternoon of [[affiliation-marendas|Marendas]]'s [Dedication of the Apprentice]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, after the priests have blessed them and before their masters take them home. It is the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]]'s foundation legend in the form Krusteikhos tells it: a prose telling, given in the Hall itself by the oldest clerk who can still stand, with a fixed declaration at its end that the apprentices say back to him, line by line, and are expected to know by the next Dedication. The other four cities tell it too, each to its own apprentices and each to its own credit, and the telling below notes where their tellings part from this one.

It is the story of [[lore-heladcompact|the Compact of the Five]], sworn in 157 BF at the plateau's edge the year after the steppe host died between the springs ([[lore-heladwells|the Battle of the Wells]]). The four things the Compact bound the cities to are history, and the telling keeps them as the history does. The rest is the telling's own: five envoys who could agree on nothing for three days, the commander of the springs who had them declare what each city lacked before any of them sealed, and the order of the seals that put the bond, and later the Hall, where the last seal went down. The envoys are [Ánevkar]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of [[affiliation-nesthiros|Nesthiros]], [Teíkhazur]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of [[affiliation-argyrteikhos|Argirteikhos]], [Órashazur]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of [[affiliation-orashion|Órashion]], [Tchirnalash]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of [[affiliation-karvashion|Karvashion]] and [Agórankar]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Krusteikhos, as the clerks name them, and no city's record gives other names for them or confirms these. The commander of the springs is [Strátimos Ghéranides]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the pass-city, whom the record of the Wells names.
