At [[place-dunkelwald|Dunkelwald]], inside [[place-vethwald|the Deepwood]], a household mistress (_othrún_) teaches her daughter the charms in _grema_, deep winter, when the fire is banked for the night and the traplines are frozen shut. **The Dunkelwald Charms**, _Dunkelwald vreld_, are nine _vreld_ as one household of the [[lore-bearttm|bear]] wesk keeps them, in teaching order: the ones said most often first, the ones said over a body last. A _vreld_ is a few lines laid on a hurt, a crossing, a beast or a night, and each has two parts, a precedent from the old days and then the command, which is what the hands do and what the mouth says while they do it: a leg bound, ash laid, a count called at the door.

The mistress says them at the sickbed and the threshold, and anyone with two hands says the fifth. Before each charm she tells the daughter what it is for and when to say it, and the household keeps those rubrics with the charms as their first lines, flat as she says them. Between the fifth and the sixth she teaches a run of small charms that are not counted, because no old days stand behind them. The ninth survives in its opening only; the rest belongs to the Shaman's apprentice.

For a bone. You say it with the splint in your hand and the bast in your teeth, and you say it once. It is the same over a man and over a dog.
