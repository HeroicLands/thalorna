**The Lay of the Giant Slayers**, _Dorgbán drunga_, is the lay of a landing kindred that calls itself the [Dorgbán]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, the Giant Slayers. Its reciter gives it at [[place-waldburg|Waldburg]] under the market peace, standing on the landing boards between the boat-sheds with a staff to keep the time, to whoever has furs or grain to wait over. Its giants belong to the age the reciters call [Before the Kindreds]{.name data-type="lore/history"} and its crossing to the age that follows it ([[lore-wintertally|The Tally of Winters]]). It tells how the people came out of the east over open grass to a forest the _dorg_ (giants) held, how a boy with no name yet vowed before the war-band to win the giants' ford, how the band crossed the river on the ice in _grema_ (deep winter) and he brought the last giant down at the ford, and why the Dorgbán say a giant at the head of their dead every [Weskskald]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}, before the oldest name of their own. The reciter holds it word for word from the man who held it before him, claims the line of reciters back to one who stood at the ford, and points to the kindred's mound at that ford as the lay's proof.

The lay names no people but its own and the giants. [[lore-takingwood|The Taking of the Wood]] gives the history the tellings keep in two accounts at once, one in which the people came out of the east and one in which they came out of the oaks, and the market custom lets a rival answer a lay. Where this one reaches the giant at the head of the dead, a reciter of [[place-dunkelwald|Dunkelwald]] in [[place-vethwald|the Deepwood]], whenever one is at the market, stands up by custom and gives the other account, and the Dorgbán reciter finishes his stanza after it. The objection is given below in full where it falls.
