Sumarmál is the seasonal blót of return: ships go back to the water, warriors leave winter quarters, and cattle go to pasture. Offerings seek a good growing and sailing year, with Fródvin prominent and Thrúnvald invoked for seafaring. Great tings hear disputes and witness public oaths. On the frontier, neighboring householders may walk their boundary stones with a witness for the landvördr, reading and striking each mark; a moved stone becomes a matter for the ting.
