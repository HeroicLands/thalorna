**The Sealing of the City**, _Zagathavughán_, is the lament the [[lore-khazariclt|Khazári]] sing below when the account of [[lore-khazarturn|Khazártúrn]] has been read. The keepers of the archive read the account by lamplight in the deep hall; when its last line is read, the old of the hold sing this, and nobody else sings it. It is a _kimghath_, a lament kept and sung again without change, in the broken course of the [[skill-khazarlng|Khazári]] deep song: a long half-line answered by a short one that falls away. Its first keener was **Kimghi** of Lakamakhulán, born to the remnant in the years after the sealing and given the _i_ of her name for the work of her father's mother, **Kamgha** of the same house, who keened over the carrying and died before her keening was set; Kimghi set it. The lament keeps to the account and sings in the voice of those who went in, as the account is written in theirs. Every name in it is a name the account holds and every count is the account's count, and a keener who varies either is corrected from the record. Of the one who carried the Voice it says what the account says, that he was [[lore-flksinale|Sinalë]], and no more; his name is known in every hold and is written in no Khazári text. The [[lore-grukarfolk|Grukar]] are in it as the account has them, as vermin, and the account's own reasoning about them is in it unchanged. In Khazári each of the eleven courses opens on a letter of [[skill-drthrkscrpt|Pirzath]] in the order of the row, and the course-heads count from _tob_ to _tob zund tob_, one to eleven. The rendering keeps the numbers and loses the letters, and the course of the birds, the four half-lines that stand before the eleventh head, opens on no letter and carries no number. The text is as the old sing it at [[place-vorgald|Vorgald]], where it arrived with the account.

```poetry {form=kamgh lang=en}
_Tob_

The account has been read.
  It is kept.
It is sung after, by the old.
  This is not the account.
Listen as you listen to stone,
  before the cut.

We were not in the city.
  So we went in.
We were at the far workings, at the salt,
  at the gate farms with the carts,
on the roads between the holds
  with a load of iron.
A hundred and ninety-one of us.
  The record has our names.

We came up the valley by the road we had made
  and the road was whole.
We came to the gate
  and the gate was down.

The vermin came up the valley in one order.
  The account gives the count.
They were spoken over.
  One voice in a great count of bodies.
They had not tired at the gate.
  They are made to tire.
The voice was _Zughán_. The one who carried it
  was of the forest folk.
We know his name as we know our line.
  It is not cut. It is not sung.
His people knew the Voice was in the world.
  They did not say.
They took him afterward, and it took them long,
  and they gave us nothing.
That is the whole of what is said of them here.
  Listen to the stone.

_Zik_

In the tower of Gurráz was the guarding,
  the gate and the deep.
Tanvaguráz had the gate,
  Famgadurákh the deep.
**Gerzen** had the gate that night.
  He was found at it.
**Gorzo** had the gate after him.
  She had not left it.
**Nilpin** of Nalpafurád had the high approach,
  the cold watch.
He was found on the approach.
  He had sent the word down.
The word came down. The gate was shut.
  Those who could went down to the deep.

Famgadurákh had the refuge,
  the holding of the deep.
**Famgan** had the door, and **Fimgi**.
  They were found at it, inside.
The door was made for what tires.
  They did not tire.
It was made for what breaks and goes back.
  They did not go back.
The door held a day and a night and a day.
  The account gives the hours.
Then the door.
  Then the people.

Then the holds came, by the roads, with lamps,
  and the account names each hold and each hand.
All who were found in the refuge were carried up,
  each to the chamber of his house.
Not one was laid where he was found.
  Each one was carried:
by the stair of the refuge, that is sixty-four steps,
  and by the long stair after,
and by the ways, to the towers, to the galleries,
  to the rooms they had kept.
The account gives the days of the carrying.
  A hundred and eighty-four days.
It is a long count.
  Count it.
Over them the words were not said.
  They were kept.

_Vem_

In the tower of Gullád the forges were cold.
  They had been cold nine days.
The smiths were laid at their hearths,
  each with the tool of his hand.
**Gelden** of Galdavulád, master of the hearth,
  with the hammer.
It was his own hammer, of his own making,
  warranted on the haft.
**Thamran** of Hamkadhunák, the striker,
  with the striker's hammer.
**Hemken** of Hamkadhunák, who sounded the blades,
  with a blade not sounded.
**Sekare** of Sakarapuláz, the folder of iron,
  with the tongs,
and in the tongs a bar folded four times.
  The fifth fold was not made.
**Pilzi** her apprentice, of Sakarapuláz,
  with the bar's other end.
**Vetame** of Valdasukár at the bellows,
  with the bellows.
**Balgan** of Balgatumák at the copper,
  with a basin half raised.
**Helben** of Halbazumád at the silver,
  with the touchstone.
His weights were on the bench, and they were counted:
  twelve, and all true.
**Zamda** of Halbazumád, who assayed,
  with the lead.
**Garfan** of Zanthaguráf at the whetstone,
  with an axe half-edged.
**Guldun** of Galdavulád, a child of the house,
  born in the hungry year,
who carried the charcoal to the hearths,
  with the charcoal.
The account has the smiths of Gullád
  to the number of five hundred and seventy.
It has each name with each tool.
  The lament has these.
The hearths were cleaned after, and laid,
  with charcoal and kindling,
as a hearth is laid at the end of a shift
  for the next shift.
There is no next shift.
  They are laid.
Over the smiths the words were not said.
  They were kept.

_Bozd_

In the tower of Dullák the cutters were laid
  among their cuts.
**Dalkan** of Dalkathumár by the wall he was dressing.
  It is dressed to the seam.
**Delke** of Kavalazunáth, hoped for by her house,
  by the pillar she was finishing.
The rock had left the pillar standing.
  She had touched it with three cuts.
Over the cutters the words were not said.
  They were kept.

_Zom_

In the tower of Rummák the layers of courses
  were laid in the halls.
**Ramkan** of Ramkasumád in the hall he had raised,
  two hundred and fifty-six feet to the vault.
**Rimki** of Vatamarumák, who took up her forebear's work,
  on the stair her forebear began.
**Thalgan** of Thalgahunáth, who made the roads,
  on the road inside the rock.
The great ways run straight in Khazártúrn.
  We wanted it so.
The stone had not offered it. We cut it,
  and it was warranted,
and it stands, and it is kept,
  and nothing is said against it.
Over the layers of courses the words were not said.
  They were kept.

_Nefk_

In the tower of Vurrán the followers of the line
  were laid in the workings.
**Khalnan** of Khalvagunáv in the unlit working,
  where the lamp had gone out.
The lamp had burned to the end of its oil.
  It was not put out.
**Revade** of Ravadarunáth at the vein,
  with the reckoning of the vein on wax.
**Molthon** of Malthapurán was at the salt with us.
  He carried his father up.
**Tevaren** of Tavarasuláth at the channel-head
  where the water comes in.
**Varthan** of Malthapurán, the bearer,
  with his load, at the foot of the stair.
The long tunnel of Vurrán needs no prop.
  It needed none that night.
It holds. It is in the dark. It holds.
  Nothing in it has moved.
Over the followers of the line the words were not said.
  They were kept.

_Fozd_

In the tower of Summágh the listeners
  were laid in the listening-places.
The apprentices were there with their slates.
  The slates were read.
**Nulpu**, born in the hungry year, had written:
  it rings sound.
The stone of that place rings sound.
  It rang sound that night.
**Sakala** of Khardavurán had the reading of the faces.
  She was laid in her reading-room,
with the valley before her, on wax, in her hand,
  face by face.
The one who found the valley is in the record
  from before the first tower.
**Samgha** of Khardavurán read the cliff from the valley floor
  and said: seven.
She said the cliff would hold seven.
  It held seven.
  It holds them.
Over the listeners the words were not said.
  They were kept.

_Kabr_

In the tower of Lummáv the keepers of the record
  were laid among the records.
**Lekamen** of Lakamakhulán, the lamp in the silence,
  had the archive lamp.
It was found trimmed. It was found out.
  He was found by it.
**Vanthan** of Vanthakhuván, who recited the oaths,
  among the oaths.
**Lamva** of Vanthakhuván had the day-record.
  She was found at it.
On the wax of that day, in the flowing hand, it reads:
  _On this day the gate sends down—_
The wax stops there. It is not smoothed after.
  It is kept as it is.
The assessment was on its shelf.
  It was taken down and read.
It is in the hand that wrote it,
  five hundred and seventy-three years gone,
and it reads:
  _Vermin of the deep country._
  _Dangerous at a frontier._
  _They cannot concentrate._
  _The spawners do not cooperate._
  _They will not make an army._
  _They cannot._
Every line of it is true.
  It is still true.
It was put back on its shelf.
  It is there.
The genealogies were there, every line to the seven,
  and every line has a gap at that year.
Over the keepers of the record the words were not said.
  They were kept.

_Khozm_

Then the city was made clean,
  as a house is made clean for its dead.
Every gallery. Every hall. Every stair.
  The account has them in order.
The stairs of Khazártúrn were washed step by step,
  and the steps were counted as they were washed:
thirteen thousand, two hundred steps,
  and seven.
The floors of the great ways were washed to the stone
  and dried.
The painted sky of the market circles was scored
  in three places.
It was mended from a scaffold,
  and the scaffold was carried out.
What was vermin was carried out by the gate.
  It was counted in loads.
Five hundred and eighty-four loads.
  It was burned on the valley floor.
The ash was turned into the ground of the valley.
  It is not counted after that.
They were spoken over. It is written.
  They were carried out all the same.
The lamps were filled and trimmed in every niche.
  They were not lit.
The doors of the chambers were shut on the dead,
  each door, by name.

_Zund_

Then the light was taken out of it.
  The account has the skylights by number:
sixty-seven skylights,
  and each by its hall.
Each was unmade from above, on the mountain's crown,
  the window-work lifted out whole,
carried down the shaft it had lit
  and laid in the hall beneath it.
Nothing was broken to take it out.
  It had been set to be taken out.
Then the granite.
  Sixty-seven blocks.
They were cut where the mountain held a seam for them,
  and nothing else was cut on the crown.
Each was dressed to the rock around it:
  to the bedding, to the stain,
to the weathering of that place
  and the weathering to come.
Each was set flush.
  There is no seam.
The masters who set them are in the record
  with how little they cut.
From above, the mountain reads as mountain.
The day went out of the halls shaft by shaft,
  from the east end to the west,
sixty-six times.
  One was left.

Then the water.
  The fountains were shut at the source.
Twenty-three fountains,
  each at its channel-head.
The water-leaders of Tavarasuláth who were with us
  shut what their dead had opened.
The city was to keep.
  It was shut so it would keep.
It keeps.

Before the last of the work
  the birds were put out.
They were put out first.
  Then the last of the work.

_Tob zund tob_

The last skylight was set flush.
  Then the deep was deep.
Then the gate.
  The last came out of it.
It was walled, and the wall was dressed to the cliff,
  bedding and stain.
**Dilkin** of Dalkathumár set the last block.
  It is set flush.
He had the first cut of it and the last.
  He cut it where the cliff had a block to give.
There is no warrant on it.
  It is the one work of his hand not signed.
The record has it instead,
  against his name, as his finest.
Nothing is to find it.
  Nothing is to open it.

Then the words were said, once,
  at the gate, for all of them,
as they are said over finished work
  and over the dead:
the course has been raised;
  the course will be struck.
For thirty thousand of them,
  by name,
and for the names the record could not confirm,
  by number: two hundred and fifty-two.
```
