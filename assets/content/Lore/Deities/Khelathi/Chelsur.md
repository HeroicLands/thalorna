_The Crocodile-God._

The [[being-crcdl|crocodile]] sacred to **Chelsur** is also the animal that takes farmers and children from the water's edge, and must sometimes be killed. That is the sharpest practical tension in the [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelathi]] religion. The cull is a religious act before it is a commercial one, and someone must license it.

His great cult sits in the eastern marsh among the river-beast hunters. He is patron besides of a dry, sun-burned garrison selat of desert wells and quarry-roads, and of the narrow gorge-stretch where the valley pinches almost to nothing. A spread like that suggests the god belongs to dangerous water and dangerous ground alike, not to crocodiles narrowly.

The working out of the tension is visible in [[affiliation-zeghetnelgu|Zeghet'Nelgu]], whose sacred hunt culls the river-beasts under temple dispensation and commends every kill to the river god for judgment.

The crocodile is the god's creature and not the god. A cull is therefore a service commended to him under a rite, and the dispensation under which Zeghet'Nelgu hunts governs the manner of killing, not the killing itself.

On one stretch of [[place-zumeleshrvr|the Zumelesh]] the same god is held under a noble family's charter and goes by the name [[lore-igelnarudty|Igelnaru]].
