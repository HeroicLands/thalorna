_Patron of the Hunt._

**Linqur** is the god of the clean kill. [[lore-linhurdty|Linhur]] watches the hunter; Linqur watches the act, and asks that it be done with the speed and efficiency the beast's nature permits. One god watches the man and the other the killing, so a hunter answerable to both cannot satisfy either by intention alone.

He is the second of **Aû'Khelâthu**'s two hunt-patrons and inseparable in practice from Linhur. [[affiliation-garnuw|Gar'Nuw]], the guild of hunters, conducts its senior elevations at both temples in tandem, and neither rite counts alone.

Why the hunt needs both gods is left open: kin, rivals and two aspects of one thing all fit what the rites show. Outside the hunt Linqur has no myth, no cosmic role and no place in the [[affiliation-khelathpnthn|Khelâthi Pantheon]].
