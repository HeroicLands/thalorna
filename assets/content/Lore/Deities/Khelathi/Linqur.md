_Patron of the Hunt._

**Linkur** is the god of the clean kill. In the guild's teaching, [[lore-linhurdty|Linhur]] watches the hunter; Linkur watches the act, and asks that it be done with the speed and efficiency the beast's nature permits. One god watches the man and the other the killing, so a hunter answerable to both cannot satisfy either by intention alone.

He is the second of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]]'s two hunt-patrons and inseparable in practice from Linhur. [[affiliation-garnuw|Gar'Nuw]], the guild of hunters, conducts its senior elevations at both temples in tandem, and neither rite counts alone.

In the guild's tradition, Linkur and Linhur are brothers, and the guild swears to both because a brother's judgement is never given alone. Beyond the hunt there is nothing to tell: Linkur is a guild god, and his whole being is the clean kill. He has no myth, no cosmic role and no place in the [[affiliation-khelathpnthn|Khelathi Pantheon]].
