_Guardian of Night Visions—a crescent moon with a single protective eye._

A [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelâthi]] child learns before almost any other prayer to ask **Nehle'ât** to keep watch while she sleeps. Nehle'ât is the Khelâthi name for [[lore-goddreams|the God of Dreams]], a deity far older than any single civilization. The **Asguardian** peoples call him [[lore-bjartrdty|Bjartr]], the **Aurèldíans** [[lore-theriadty|Aethería]], and the [[lore-flksinale|Sinalë]] have known him since time immemorial. The Khelâthi add intimacy: he is a companion who walks through the night beside the sleeper, and no distant celestial force.

In [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Aû'Khelâthu]] the sun burns long across the valley and the flood reshapes the land in cycles both predictable and obscure, and sleep is more than rest. It is _a sacred passage into the otherworld_, the hours in which mortals commune with the divine and the dead, when prophecy flows and truth shows itself free of daylight. Nehle'ât stands at the threshold of that passage and shields the vulnerable sleeper from the hostile spirits that stalk the night.

His is deliberately a small cult. The knowledge it holds cannot simply be taught; it demands natural aptitude as well as years of disciplined study. The priesthood therefore stays smaller and more specialized than the great valley cults, and it works in shrines and nightlong vigils instead of vast temple estates.

The Asguardian and **Aurèldían** cults of the god keep their own practice, and the Nehle'ât priesthood has not settled whether it counts them as the same practice or only as the same god, or how much of its knowledge the other cults share.
