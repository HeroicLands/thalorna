_The Cobra-Goddess._

**Shebazet** is a goddess of the delta's papyrus marshes, whose selat lives by fowling, reed-craft and the empire's paper-cutting. Her reach is quiet and very long. The empire runs on records, and its tax rolls, contracts, temple archives and unbroken king-lists are the substance of its government. The reed-beds her priests bless are the material that whole scribal empire is written on.

She stands outside the [[affiliation-khelathpnthn|Khelathi Pantheon]], and the disproportion is plain: a local marsh cult with no imperial standing supplies the physical basis of [[lore-rethsaardty|Reth'Sa'ar]]'s whole domain.

Her animal is the [[being-cobra|cobra]]. Cobras hunt the rats that ruin the reed harvest that makes papyrus, and in her cult's teaching she guards the reeds, so Reth'Sa'ar's writing rests on her.
