_The Cobra-Goddess._

Shebazet is a delta goddess of the papyrus marshes, whose selat lives by fowling, reed-craft and the
empire's paper-cutting. Her reach is quiet and very long. In a civilization that runs on records—whose tax rolls, contracts, temple archives and unbroken king-lists are the substance of its
government—**the reed-beds her priests bless are the material the entire scribal empire is written
on**.

She stands outside [[affiliation-khelathpnthn|Khelâthi Pantheon]], and the disproportion is striking: a local
marsh cult with no imperial standing supplies the physical basis of
[[lore-rethsaardty|Reth'Sa'âr]]'s whole domain.
