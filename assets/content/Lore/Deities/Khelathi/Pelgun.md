_The Fertility-God._

Pelgun is an upper-river god of harvest-festivals and virility cults, seated at the caravan-head for
the eastern desert roads. The pairing is his distinctive feature: **the same god is asked for the
increase of the fields and for the safe return of the caravans**, and his festivals mark both.

He stands outside [[affiliation-khelathpnthn|Khelâthi Pantheon]] and overlaps in domain with
[[lore-uzneradty|Uznêra]], whose fertility is the river's and the womb's; Pelgun's appears to be the drier
and more masculine kind, belonging to the desert edge rather than the floodplain.
