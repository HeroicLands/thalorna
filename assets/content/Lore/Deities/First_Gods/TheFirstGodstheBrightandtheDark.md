The Bright and the Dark are the two primordial Powers of the Áelendan, the ancestral pantheon of [[affiliation-firstgods|the First Gods]]. They are locked in a struggle that neither petition nor offering touches, and the celestial kindred of that pantheon serve one side or the other. They are named in the lore of [[affiliation-theoldway|The Old Way]], the shamanic faith oriented around the First Gods and their Kindred.
