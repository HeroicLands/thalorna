The Bright and the Dark are the two primordial Powers of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan People]], at the head of their ancestral pantheon, [[affiliation-firstgods|the First Gods]]. They are locked in a struggle that neither petition nor offering touches, and the Áelendan hold that the world turns because they contend: day and night, summer and winter, birth and death. Neither is a demon and neither is a saint, and no tribe takes one over the other.

[[affiliation-firstgods#the-bright|The Bright]]
: light, life, growth, flame and dawn

[[affiliation-firstgods#the-dark|The Dark]]
: darkness, death, stillness, cold, the grave and the deep places

The Powers are not invoked by name in ceremony and receive no offerings of their own. Áelendan practice is directed instead at [[lore-thekindred|the Kindred]] who serve them, some the Bright, some the Dark and some both by turns of the season. Through [[affiliation-theoldway|the Old Way]], the shamanic faith of the First Gods, the Kindred are venerated, appeased or warded against, and never worshipped.
