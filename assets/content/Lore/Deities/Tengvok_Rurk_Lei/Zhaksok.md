Zhāksōk is the Unsorted, a demon lord of the [[affiliation-yokveng|Yōkvëng]] in the [[affiliation-tngvkrrklei|Tëngvōk Rūrk Lëi]]. He holds confusion and the tiles that fall out of every order. His standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-zhaksok|Zhāksōk]] affiliation.
