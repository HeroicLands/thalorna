Rīrnhöt Dākvëng is the Lord of Wood, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tngvkrrklei|Tëngvōk Rūrk Lëi]]. Wood is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Wood is one of its appointed officials. His standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-rirnhtdkvng|Rīrnhöt Dākvëng]] affiliation.
