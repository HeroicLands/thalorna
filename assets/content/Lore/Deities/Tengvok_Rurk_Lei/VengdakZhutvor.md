Vëngdāk Zhütvōr is the Lord of Earth, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tngvkrrklei|Tëngvōk Rūrk Lëi]]. Earth is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Earth is one of its appointed officials. His standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-vngdkzhtvr|Vëngdāk Zhütvōr]] affiliation.
