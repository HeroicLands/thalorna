Vëkzāt is the Forger of False Records, a demon lord of the [[affiliation-yokveng|Yōkvëng]] in the [[affiliation-tngvkrrklei|Tëngvōk Rūrk Lëi]]. His lies enter the celestial archive as though true. His standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-vekzat|Vëkzāt]] affiliation.
