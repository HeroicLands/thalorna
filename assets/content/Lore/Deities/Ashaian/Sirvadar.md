Sirvadar, the Wandering Guide, is the deity of travel, trade routes, commerce and safe passage in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]], held to watch over the caravan roads of the desert. The symbol is a star chart etched on a golden disc, surrounded by a caravan route. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-sirvadar|Faith of Sirvadar]]. Every sacred site of the Ashaian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]], and served by the same priesthood of Roqeshekh, Delkoshekh and Shekhul.
