_**The Radiant One**—sunstone and silver leaf, a light older than the north and only lately theirs to keep._

An Ansorunno who tends a grove in [[place-vithgard|Vithgard]]'s long winter dark gives the one account of **Bjartr** every other faith of the [[affiliation-asguardian|Ten]] is missing: he is not a Nordman god who happened to answer northern prayers, but [[lore-goddreams|the God of Dreams]], worshipped by the [[lore-flksinale|Sinalë]] and the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazári]] since before humankind existed, met by northern seers under a name of their own coining. "The other nine gods are ours in a way I cannot claim for him," she says. "We did not make him. We were let in."

That difference in standing is the whole reason his clergy keep no godi, no hof, and no blót. His human worshippers took the Sinalëan structure outright rather than build one of their own—the only faith among the Ten to do so—because the elder races had already kept this god's rites for longer than the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] have had a ting to recite anything. The Faithful come to the [[affiliation-bjartr|Blessing of Light]] or the [[affiliation-bjartr|Night of Dreams]] and take a priest's blessing, asking nothing further of the faith. An Ilthorinno is newly called and learns dream-reading, healing, and the tending of groves under an Ansorunno's guidance, closer to an apprentice than to a subordinate. Every Ansorunno stands equal to every other, entrusted with the rites and the sacred places and nothing above them but the **Aulirarno**, the open convocation any Ansorunno may call, which decides what touches the whole faith by deliberation rather than decree. "We have no high priest to blame a bad decision on," the Ansorunno says. "That is slower. I have never found it worse."

Where most Asguardian faiths draw their following from warriors, jarls, and seafarers, Bjartr's human faithful tend to be seers, healers, and those who come to him through sleep rather than the sword. What the faith costs an Ilthorinno is years of apprenticeship with no ordination waiting at the end of them, since the standing never rises past Ansorunno; what it is held to win is the dream rightly read, the grove that keeps its peace, and a vision the elder races' own millennia of practice can still occasionally supply when a human priesthood's could not.

The southern faiths know the same presence as [[lore-theriadty|Aethería]], and the two human traditions' priesthoods meet with a courtesy neither extends to much else outside their own walls. "I will not claim the Aurèldíans have the truer name," the Ansorunno says, "any more than I would let them claim mine is. The god does not seem to mind having two."
