Karnavos, the Wild Beast, is the deity of chaos in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]], and of the wilderness no law reaches into. The symbol is antlers framing a swirling spiral. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-karnavos|Faith of Karnavos]], which is deliberately decentralized and anarchic: it has no High Priest and no hierarchy to rise through, only the loose, shifting Circle of the Threshold that anyone may claim to belong to and no one may speak for.
