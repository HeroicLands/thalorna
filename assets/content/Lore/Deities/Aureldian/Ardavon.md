Árdavon, the Keeper of the Gates, is the god of order in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]: justice, cosmic law, the sanctity of oaths and contracts, and honorable struggle. His symbol is a dual-faced mask, one side serene and the other stern. The [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]] serves him.

## The Two Faces

In the faith's teaching every threshold has two faces: the passage that opens and the passage that closes, the mercy a law allows and the penalty it exacts. The serene side of the mask is held to belong to compassion and the stern side to judgment, and the god is depicted wearing both at once. He is taught as a god of principle, not of kindness or of terror, and the faith asks the same of its members: duty before desire, and the strong defending the weak because it is right, not because it is easy.

The faith teaches that cosmic law is his keeping, and that the mortal order mirrors it. Fealty, the codes of knightly conduct, the laws of war and every contract witnessed in a temple fall under him in its doctrine, and to break a sworn oath is held to offend him directly and to bring divine punishment on the oath-breaker and their bloodline. In the teaching of the faith, [[lore-selmorosdty|Sélmoros]] judges the soul after death where Árdavon judges conduct among the living and assigns its consequences in the mortal realm.

## Worship

His temples serve as courts of law, archives of recorded oaths and contracts, and in some places fortified strongholds, and his priests act as judges and mediators whose rulings are held to carry divine weight. Worship is formal and ceremonial, and every rite follows fixed protocol; the faithful approach a temple with the reverence they would show a throne room. Households keep a shrine with a twin-faced image of the god, and the devout meditate before it on the balance between mercy and law.

The god's year turns on Kalpezandi, the gate's month, last of the year in the [[lore-commoncal|Vilérian Calendar]]. There the four-day [Feast of Thresholds]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} marks the turning of the year: lords and vassals reaffirm fealty, merchants renew contracts, spouses renew their vows, and the priests witness and record each oath, which the faith believes gives it divine force. In spring the [Sanctification of Swords]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} brings newly invested knights and soldiers before the altar, where priests bless their arms with holy oils; a bloodstained blade may be brought for cleansing and re-sanctification, which the faith holds frees the bearer from guilt when the cause was just.

## Priesthood and Temples

The [[affiliation-ardavon|priesthood]] is a hierarchy of acolytes of the Threshold, ordained Volsari, the Sentani who sit in the Sarnal and oversee the major temples, and the Volsarnor, the High Judge, above them all. It is the most powerful and politically influential of the Aureldian priesthoods, and its counsel weighs on kings and city councils across the west. The Volsarnor sits in the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]], where the Order of the Eternal Gate, the largest of the faith's knightly orders, enforces the faith's judgments; there its temples and those of [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] stand joined by covered passages. On [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]] the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]] is the army of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]]. In [[place-orzara|Orzara City]] its priests keep [[place-pnthnrldnm|The Temple of the Twelve]] and marshal the order in which the twelve priesthoods process to its center.

## Present

The nobility of much of the west follows the faith, because its doctrine underpins the feudal order itself. The Faith of Árdavon is at war with [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]], whose creed of domination is the opposite of its own, and is allied with the Sacred Forge, the [[affiliation-marendas|Faith of Marendas]] and the [[affiliation-olvenia|Faith of Ólvenia]] in keeping law, learning and trade in order.
