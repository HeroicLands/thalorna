Brazemas, the Thunderer, is the deity of storms in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. The symbol is a thunderbolt striking down from a spiral cloud. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-brazemas|Faith of Brazemas]], a temple priesthood of acolytes in training, ordained clergy and the Bravsarnor above them; the lay faithful keep the feasts without office.
