Brazemas, the Thunderer, is the god of storms, thunder and lightning in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. His symbol is a thunderbolt striking down from a spiral cloud. The [[affiliation-brazemas|Faith of Brazemas]] serves him.

## The Storm

The faith teaches that Brazemas commands the sky. In its teaching the same god sends the rain that fills fields, vineyards and olive groves and the tempest that flattens them, and its followers do not separate the two: a drought is read as his withholding and a hailstorm as his anger. He is taught as a conscious and responsive god who can be reasoned with by those who speak to him in language he understands, not as an inanimate force. The faith treats lightning as his direct manifestation and reads the pattern of where and when it strikes as his communication.

In the faith's teaching he governs the seas, with [[lore-pozerasdty|Pozeras]], and all travel that depends on wind and weather; the storms that wreck ships and the storms that drive them swiftly home are held to be the same god's work. The same teaching holds that his rains are a condition of the prosperity that [[lore-olveniadty|Ólvenia]] grants, so that her gifts cannot come without his cooperation.

## Worship

Worship is volatile and passionate, performed under open skies and open to the unpredictability of its subject. In drought the faithful beseech him to break the heat; in violent storm seasons the temples hold ceremonies of appeasement that ask him to turn his wrath away from inhabited lands. Individual devotion runs to vows: a farmer promises a share of the harvest and a festival if the rain comes, sailors and merchants keep small shrines aboard ship and offer wine or the first portion of a meal, and a traveler caught in a thunderstorm may bargain on the spot. The faith treats these vows as binding contracts and holds that breaking one invites his active malice.

At the onset of spring the [[lore-thunderheadvigil|Thunderhead Vigil]] lasts one full night. Priests and faithful gather on hilltops, city towers and ship decks to wait for the first thunderstorm of the season, and when lightning splits the sky they raise a wordless cry that they understand as acknowledgment of the god's return. The priesthood reads the first strike as his blessing for the coming year and announces its reading of the omen to the crowds. In late summer the [Invocation of the Waters]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} sends priests in procession through fields, vineyards and olive groves, chanting and making offerings to bring gentle rain instead of destructive downpours, with sprinkled water and waved implements that imitate wind and cloud. Pozerandi and Brazemandi, the third and fourth months of the [[lore-commoncal|Vilérian Calendar]], are in the keeping of Pozeras and Brazemas.

## Priesthood and Temples

The Bravsarnor, the High Priest of the Storm, is regarded by the faith as the primary voice of the god, interpreting his will through natural phenomena; kings and city councils consult him on agriculture, trade and sea ventures. He sits in [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Viléria]] and keeps a network of weather-watchers across the empire who track storms, record rainfall and report omens to the central temple. Below him the Bravsari, the Priests of Storms, keep the temples and the seasonal rituals in major cities and coastal settlements and advise farmers, merchants and sailors. The Bravlili, acolytes and apprentices, are often youths who survived a near-fatal storm or show unusual sensitivity to weather.

His temples stand on high ground, with towers and exposed courtyards built to draw lightning. Some carry copper conductors that lead a strike into chambers below, where inscriptions record where and when it fell, and the Bravsarnor reads these archives across generations for what the priesthood takes to be the god's messages. Coastal and trading cities keep the best-endowed temples, because the priesthood's weather readings are part of their safety and wealth.

## Present

The priests of the [[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pozeras]] work closely with the Faith of Brazemas in maritime communities, jointly blessing voyages. The smiths of [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] make the conductors, talismans and ceremonial implements the priests use, and the priests protect foundries from lightning in return; the partnership is strongest in the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]]. The faith watches the [[affiliation-zunelas|Faith of Zunelas]] with fascination and some tension, since a thunderstorm can still surprise priests who track weather with precision, and some of its more liberal theologians hold that [[lore-zunelasdty|Zunelas]] whispers to the storms.
