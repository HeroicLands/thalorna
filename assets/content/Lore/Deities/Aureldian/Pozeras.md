Pozeras, the Wayfarer, is the god of voyages, journeys, exploration and wanderlust in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. His name means the hallowed road in the [[skill-vylarilng|Vilari Language]]. His symbol is a staff entwined with serpents, crowned by a compass. The [[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pozeras]] serves him.

## The Wayfarer

The faith teaches that Pozeras protects travelers and inspires mortals to seek the unknown. It treats him as a spiritual guide and a very present protector against the plain dangers of the road, and its followers approach him with reverence and practical supplication together. His domain in its teaching is every crossing a person makes, by road, by sea, over desert and through mountain pass, and the knowledge of routes, maps and navigation that makes crossing possible.

With [[lore-brazemasdty|Brazemas]] he is taught to govern the seas and all travel that depends on wind and weather; travelers invoke the two together before they set out. The faith holds that [[lore-olveniadty|Ólvenia]] blesses commerce and abundance while Pozeras enables the movement of goods that makes trade possible. It teaches that [[lore-ardavondty|Árdavon]] supplies the law that guarantees contracts and safe passage, and Pozeras the protection of the journey itself, and that [[lore-lizelasdty|Lizelas]] blesses those who set out and those who will return. The faith stands against those who would restrict travel or knowledge, and [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]], which hoards power and closes access to distant lands, is its implied opponent.

## Worship

His worship is movement and adventure more than stationary ritual. The city has temples, but the most authentic worship takes place on roads, at crossroads, in harbor towns and wherever travelers gather to prepare to depart. Pozerandi, the third month of the [[lore-commoncal|Vilérian Calendar]], is in his keeping.

At the spring equinox, when travel grows safer after winter, the [Wanderer's Blessing]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} fills his temples with merchants bound for trade voyages, soldiers marching to distant postings and adventurers beginning quests, who present themselves for formal commissioning. The priest anoints each traveler with oil and gives a symbol of Pozeras, often a small carved staff or compass, and the traveler swears to travel honorably, to help other travelers in distress and, if possible, to return and make a further offering. At the autumn equinox, as the main travel season wanes, the [Celebration of Arrivals]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} fills the same temples with travelers who have come home safely and give thanks with substantial offerings. They tell their adventures, recount the dangers survived and discuss what they learned in distant lands, and the priesthood collects the accounts, sometimes compiling them into travel guides and maps.

Personal devotion is vows made before departure. A merchant promises a share of his profit to build a way-shrine; a soldier promises a weapon or armor taken in distant battle if she returns; sailors offer wine or the first catch before putting to sea, in the belief that safe passage depends on the cooperation of Pozeras and Brazemas.

## Priesthood and Temples

His priesthood is the most decentralized and mobile of the Aureldian faiths. The Tramar, the Master of Wayfaring, is its nominal apex, keeps headquarters in one of the great port cities and spends much of the year traveling among the secondary centers. The Tramar keeps the maps, route knowledge and wayfaring lore gathered over generations and settles questions of doctrine. The Tramori, the Keepers of Roads, are a scattered body of ordained priests stationed at junctions, ports and city centers throughout western Ankaris, who keep temples and waystations, oversee the way-shrines along the trade routes and advise merchants, soldiers and adventurers. Most are former travelers who know their territory intimately, and some specialize in overland routes, coastal and maritime navigation, desert crossing or mountain passage. The Acolytes of the Road learn by traveling with experienced priests, and many remain as guides and companions without ever being ordained.

The Way-Shrines are the faith's distinctive work: thousands of small temples and way-stations along the major routes of western Ankaris, from stone structures at the great junctions to plain roadside shelters that give protection from weather and room for a brief prayer. Donations from grateful travelers, the labor of the faithful and the vows of those who sponsor the building or upkeep of a particular shrine keep them standing, and they are open to all. Fees for navigation advice in dangerous country, collected offerings and the patronage of successful merchants and military leaders fund the priesthood.

## Present

Where the Faith of Pozeras and the [[affiliation-brazemas|Faith of Brazemas]] are both kept on the coast, way-shrines are shared and the priests of Pozeras keep close relations with the weather-readers of Brazemas, who help predict safe sailing. In the great trading cities the temples of Pozeras and the [[affiliation-olvenia|Faith of Ólvenia]] stand close together or share structures. Where law reaches, the priests of Pozeras and the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]] keep the roads safe together, and where it does not, the priests of Pozeras often arbitrate disputes Árdavan law cannot easily reach. Some temples commission talismans from craftspeople of [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] to give to travelers. Under [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] his priests work carefully and often in secret, without direct resistance.
