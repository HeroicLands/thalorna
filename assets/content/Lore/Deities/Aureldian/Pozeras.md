Pôzêrâs, the Wayfarer, whose name is [[skill-vylarilng|Vylari]] for the hallowed road, is the deity of voyages in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]]. The symbol is a staff entwined with serpents, crowned by a compass. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pôzêrâs]], a temple priesthood of acolytes in training, ordained clergy and the Tramar above them; the lay faithful keep the feasts without office.
