Pozeras, the Wayfarer, whose name means the hallowed road in the [[skill-vylarilng|Vylari Language]], is the deity of voyages in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. The symbol is a staff entwined with serpents, crowned by a compass. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pozeras]], a temple priesthood of acolytes in training, ordained clergy and the Tramar above them; the lay faithful keep the feasts without office.
