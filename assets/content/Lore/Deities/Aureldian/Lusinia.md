Lúsinía, the World-Weaver, is the deity of creation in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]]. The symbol is a spinning wheel encircled by vines. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-lusinia|Faith of Lúsinía]], a temple priesthood of acolytes in training, ordained clergy and the Magistra Vitae above them; the lay faithful keep the feasts without office.
