Lusinia, the World-Weaver, is the goddess of life, earth and creation in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. Her symbol is a spinning wheel encircled by vines. The [[affiliation-lusinia|Faith of Lusinia]] serves her.

## The World-Weaver

The faith teaches that Lusinia spins the fabric of existence and nurtures every living thing, from the smallest seed to the mightiest beast. Her wheel is depicted as standing for both the labor of creation and the interconnected growth of all life, and for that reason weavers and textile workers revere her, and many craft guilds keep a small shrine to her in the belief that all making flows from her hand.

Her creation is taught as patient and methodical. With [[lore-lizelasdty|Lizelas]], who is the joy of fertility and the burst of spring bloom, she is held to embody the full cycle of growth, and the two are invoked together in harvest blessings and pregnancy rites. [[lore-morvanadty|Morvana]] is the other half of her work in the faith's teaching: a plant must fall and rot to feed the soil that feeds new growth, a forest needs fire to renew itself, and the two faiths together stand for the eternal wheel, Lusinia emphasizing growth and Morvana endings. Against [[lore-karnavosdty|Karnavos]] she is taught to stand for nature brought into harmony with civilization; the faith accepts that storms bring rain and fire clears deadwood and releases seed, and holds that such chaos must be balanced with cultivation and order. Its followers believe that [[lore-marendasdty|Marendas]] helps unlock the secrets of growing things.

## Worship

Her temples are rarely grand stone fortresses. They are sanctuaries ringed by gardens, groves of ancient trees and cultivated ground where mortals and nature live in harmony, and worship is often held outdoors under open sky in clearings or beside springs and rivers that rise from sacred sources. The priesthood keeps extensive herb gardens, seed libraries and nurseries where saplings are raised for replanting forests damaged by logging or fire. Everyday devotion is tending: gardeners, farmers and herbalists invoke her as they work, homes keep a shrine with fresh flowers or growing herbs and set the family's first harvest of the season there before eating, and pregnant women make pilgrimage to her temples. The sick are brought to her healing sanctuaries, where priestesses prepare remedies from plants grown in the temple gardens. Offerings are simple and organic: flowers, grain, honey and fresh fruit.

Lusenar, the second month of the [[lore-commoncal|Vilérian Calendar]], is in her keeping. In early spring, as the land wakes from winter, the [Festival of First Growth]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} is a joyous celebration of ritual planting: the faithful go in procession through cities and countryside planting seed in communal gardens while priestesses bless the fields and pray for abundance. Young couples may pledge themselves to Lusinia during it, and such unions are thought to be blessed with fertility and long life. In rural communities the festival is a feast of early vegetables and last year's wine, with a portion left at her shrines.

## Priesthood and Temples

The Sirela, the High Priestess or High Priest, is typically a figure of great age and wisdom who has spent decades in service to the goddess and the natural world. Below the Sirela a council of Sarnori, ordained priests, maintain the temples, perform the rites and serve as healers and counselors; many are accomplished herbalists or naturalists, and their knowledge of plants, agriculture and healing counts as much spiritually as formal theology. Acolytes of the Spring work the temple gardens for years, learning every plant and its properties, before ordination is considered at all, and many never proceed to it and become master gardeners or herbalists who stay in respectful touch with the priesthood.

Major temples carry a Marendalon of Growing Things, a research and teaching house where scholars and priests study agriculture, botany, medicine and the cultivation of plants. Its libraries and seed archives preserve rare and ancient plant varieties, and noble patrons and wealthy merchants support it, understanding that agricultural prosperity is the faith's blessing made visible.

## Present

The temple libraries of the Faith of Lusinia and the [[affiliation-marendas|Faith of Marendas]] share what they know of agriculture, medicine and natural philosophy, and the priestesses of Lusinia and the [[affiliation-lizelas|Faith of Lizelas]] cooperate in festivals and healing work. The Faith of Lusinia teaches that creation and decay cannot be separated, and it treats the [[affiliation-morvana|Faith of Morvana]] with respect and without conflict.
