Morvana, the Mistress of the Waning Moon, is the deity of decay in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. The symbol is a wilting flower surrounded by mist. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-morvana|Faith of Morvana]], a temple priesthood of acolytes in training, ordained clergy and the Valmena above them; the lay faithful keep the feasts without office.
