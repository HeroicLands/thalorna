Ólvenia, the Bountiful One, is the goddess of prosperity, wealth, abundance and seduction in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. Her symbol is a tree with golden fruits encircled by a wreath. The [[affiliation-olvenia|Faith of Ólvenia]] serves her.

## The Bountiful One

The faith teaches that Ólvenia blesses people with prosperity and with physical love, and it treats the two as one gift. Good fortune in trade, the fruiting of what is tended and the pleasure of the body are all her abundance in its teaching, and honoring them with sensual celebration is held to be a rightful honor to the goddess and no licence.

The faith places her beside [[lore-lizelasdty|Lizelas]] in the cycle of plenty: Lizelas oversees the physical fertility of fields and bodies, and Ólvenia blesses the abundance that results and the commerce that grows from it, so farmers thank the one for the crop and merchants thank the other for the profit on it. It teaches that [[lore-ardavondty|Árdavon]] gives her merchants the order they need to trade safely, and that prosperous cities are stable cities, which pleases both gods. [[lore-marendasdty|Marendas]] is taught as enriching her in turn, since knowledge leads to sound business and prosperity pays for learning. The faith holds that [[lore-brazemasdty|Brazemas]]'s rains are a condition of her gifts, and the two are complementary, not allied.

## Worship

Her worship is more openly sensual than that of nearly any other Aureldian god. The temples are draped in silks and hung with incense of sandalwood, myrrh and rose oil, and her priestesses and priests dress in fine fabric, jewelry and cosmetics that celebrate the body. The core practice is the [Offering of Gain]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}: a merchant, banker or trader who has closed a profitable transaction presents part of the profit at her altar, as a joyful acknowledgment of good fortune shared, not as an obligation. The offering is perfumed oil, fine garments or golden tokens shaped like fruit or coins, commissioned from artisans, and merchants compete lovingly to give a more magnificent gift than the last. Before a major negotiation a merchant may spend the night at her shrine, making an offering and praying for charm and eloquence.

Elthezandi, the seventh month of the [[lore-commoncal|Vilérian Calendar]], is the gold's month and belongs to her. In late summer, when traders return with laden ships, the three-day [[lore-goldenharvest|Festival of the Golden Harvest]] is the most openly hedonistic celebration in the Aureldian religious year. Merchants and craftspeople close their shops, the streets fill with musicians, dancers and acrobats, wine flows freely, singers and actors perform love ballads and comedies in the temple squares, and wealthy traders hold feasts of rare delicacies. Her priestess leads a procession through the market districts, blessing new stalls, consecrating new trading companies and receiving the tributes of the past season.

## Priesthood and Temples

The priesthood is organized by temple instead of under a single head. Each major city's temple is governed by a High Priestess, or occasionally a High Priest, who is the spiritual head and the faith's intermediary between the goddess and her followers, and who often holds considerable influence in the city's merchant councils and trading guilds. The Priestesses of the Temple manage daily worship, keep the sacred spaces and counsel those seeking her favor; they are trained in negotiation, rhetoric and persuasion and are often as versed in accounting, trade law and contracts as in theology. Acolytes study the theology of prosperity and the festivals and serve as scribes and administrators for the temple's records of offerings and blessings; many go on to ordination and others become merchants, accountants or guild officers.

The faith's most distinctive office is the Temple Companion, a courtesan consecrated to the goddess and honored as a sacred priestess in her own right. A Companion undergoes a formal initiation, wears a token of her dedication and swears to practice with skill, discretion and integrity, and the temple's sponsorship of her, with training, housing, protection and a share of her earnings, is itself held to be an act of worship. Many merchant guilds, banking houses and trading companies dedicate themselves to Ólvenia and keep a shrine in the guildhouse where members make offerings before major deals. The guilds pay tithes and the priesthood blesses their ventures.

## Present

Ólvenian merchants donate to the temples of the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]], and Árdavan magistrates protect Ólvenian property by law. Her merchants patronize scholars, artists and philosophers as a form of worship, the temples and academies of the [[affiliation-marendas|Faith of Marendas]] receive their gifts, and Marendasan scholars advise trading companies on trade and negotiation.
