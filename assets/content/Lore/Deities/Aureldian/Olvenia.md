Ólvenia, the Bountiful One, is the deity of prosperity in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. The symbol is a tree with golden fruits encircled by a wreath. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-olvenia|Faith of Ólvenia]], a temple priesthood of acolytes in training, ordained clergy and the High Priestess of a Temple above them; the lay faithful keep the feasts without office.
