Múrkír, the Wayfarer, is the deity of voyages in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]]. The symbol is a staff entwined with serpents, crowned by a compass. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-murkir|Faith of Múrkír]], a temple priesthood of acolytes in training, ordained clergy and the Archivus Peregrinorum above them; the lay faithful keep the feasts without office.
