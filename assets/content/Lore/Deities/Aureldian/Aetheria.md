Aethería, the Veiled Dreamer, is the deity of dreams in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]]. The symbol is a freestanding stone arch with a radiant star beyond. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-aetheria|Faith of Aethería]], a temple priesthood of acolytes in training, ordained clergy and the Somniatrix above them; the lay faithful keep the feasts without office.
