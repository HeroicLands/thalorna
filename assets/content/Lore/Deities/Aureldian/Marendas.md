Mârendâs, the Keeper of Sacred Wisdom, is the deity of knowledge in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]]. The symbol is an open book with glowing stars. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-marendas|Faith of Mârendâs]], a temple priesthood of acolytes in training, ordained clergy and the Magistra Sapientiae above them; the lay faithful keep the feasts without office.
