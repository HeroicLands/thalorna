Ústaron, the Forge-Lord, is the deity of fire in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]], whose worship the Ústaran schism divides in two. One side honors the deity as the Sacred Forge, the creative aspect of fire, symbolized by a radiant anvil beneath a sheltering flame and kept in [[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]]. The other honors the deity as the Black Flame, the destructive aspect, symbolized by a mailed fist enclosed in black flames and kept in [[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]]. Each side keeps its own temple priesthood.
