Zunelas, the Veiled Dreamer, whose name is [[skill-vylarilng|Vylari]] for the hallowed night, is the deity of dreams in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. The symbol is a freestanding stone arch with a radiant star beyond. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-zunelas|Faith of Zunelas]], a temple priesthood of acolytes in training, ordained clergy and the Nirvena above them; the lay faithful keep the feasts without office.
