Zûnêlâs, the Veiled Dreamer, whose name is [[skill-vylarilng|Vylari]] for the hallowed night, is the deity of dreams in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]]. The symbol is a freestanding stone arch with a radiant star beyond. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-zunelas|Faith of Zûnêlâs]], a temple priesthood of acolytes in training, ordained clergy and the Somniatrix above them; the lay faithful keep the feasts without office.
