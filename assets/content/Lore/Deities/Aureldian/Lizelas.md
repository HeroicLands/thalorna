Lizelas, the Nurturer, is the deity of fertility in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. The symbol is a blooming flower encircled by a crescent. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-lizelas|Faith of Lizelas]], a temple priesthood of acolytes in training, ordained clergy and the Lizena above them; the lay faithful keep the feasts without office.
