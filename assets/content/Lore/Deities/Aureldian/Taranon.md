Táranon, the Thunderer, is the deity of storms in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]]. The symbol is a thunderbolt striking down from a spiral cloud. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-taranon|Faith of Táranon]], a temple priesthood of acolytes in training, ordained clergy and the Flamen Tempestas above them; the lay faithful keep the feasts without office.
