Ústaron, the Forge-Lord, is the god of fire in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]]. His symbol is a hammer over a flame-ringed anvil. Aureldian teaching holds that he embodies both aspects of flame, the forge that shapes and the inferno that consumes, and his worship is divided between them. The creative side is kept in [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] and the destructive side in [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]].

## The Forge-Lord

Aureldian teaching holds that a god of fire is a god of both what fire makes and what it takes. The forge-fire shapes raw metal into something useful, the hearth-fire warms a home and the beacon-fire guides a traveler to safety; the same fire levels a field, a city or an army. Ústaron is taught as the whole of that, and for much of the pantheon's history the duality was carried without difficulty. [[lore-ustaranschism|The Ústaran Schism]] ended that when a faction of his clergy declared that his true nature was destruction, purification through fire and the dominance of the strong over the weak, and that the forge was only destruction put to temporary use. Each of the two faiths that resulted holds the other to be worshipping half a god and calling it whole, so the quarrel is over one god and not over two.

Among the Twelve, the teaching of the Sacred Forge sets him beside [[lore-marendasdty|Marendas]] in the making of craft, and beside [[lore-ardavondty|Árdavon]] in the defense of civilization. The Black Flame's following courts [[lore-karnavosdty|Karnavos]] as a kindred power, and the mainstream of Karnavos's faith refuses it, holding that transformative chaos and destruction for its own sake are not the same.

## The Sacred Forge

[[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]] keeps Ústaron's creative aspect: craftsmanship, transformation, illumination and protection. Its symbol is a radiant anvil beneath a sheltering flame. Its temples are built around real forges, great stone hearths whose sacred fires burn day and night for years, and the faith holds the letting-out of one to be a catastrophe of cosmic weight. The [Blessing of the Anvil]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} consecrates every new smithy, armory or foundry, and the first object forged there is given to the temple as what the faithful regard as proof of the god's blessing. Thumbandi, the fifth month of the [[lore-commoncal|Vilérian Calendar]], is the forge's month, and the summer solstice falls on its first day; every smith brings the first piece of the year to the altar of the Sacred Forge at the Forge-Lord's feast. At the spring equinox the [Lanterns of the Night]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} carry blessed flame through the streets at twilight and gather it into the shape of an anvil, a hammer or a forge on a high point, and at the winter solstice the [Festival of the Hearth]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} carries flame from the temple's central forge to every household hearth, which the faithful believe shelters them from cold and darkness.

Its priesthood is led by the Hamsarnor, who sits in the [[place-provenzrgn|Provénzia Region]], where the faith holds its greatest political influence. Hammer-Priests lead the temples and are typically skilled smiths who keep their craft, and acolytes are often apprentices to master smiths. The Order of the Anvil and the Knights of the Sacred Forge serve as its warrior-priests. It is the established Ústaran faith in most of western Ankaris and is politically favored in Provénzia.

## The Black Flame

[[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]] keeps Ústaron's destructive aspect: fire as purifier, weapon and instrument of domination. Its symbol is a mailed fist enclosed in black flames. It teaches that true creation follows only from destruction, that the strong are meant to rule and that mercy is weakness. It has no single head and works through semi-independent cells and warbands under Flame-Wardens, whose authority is proved in strength. Initiates pass the [Ordeal of Flame]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, and the faithful regard the scars as badges of communion with the god's fury. Its [Night of Ashes]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} falls at the dark of the new moon, when the faithful burn captured weapons, broken chains and records of grievance in a great bonfire.

It is suppressed in most kingdoms and keeps secret orders and hidden shrines. In the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Empire of Viléria]], whose policy of religious tolerance has never proscribed it, it operates openly, with registered priests and a High Flame-Warden who oversees its temples from [[place-sarkenaelzen|the Ashen Temple]] in [[place-orzara|Orzara City]].

## Present

The enmity between the two faiths has not healed and decides which shrines a kingdom tolerates and which it burns. The Sacred Forge calls the Black Flame a perversion that mistook the ash for the fire, and the Black Flame calls the Sacred Forge a comfortable lie told by people who wanted a patron for their workshops. The [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]] is at war with the Black Flame and allied with the Sacred Forge. The scholars of the [[affiliation-marendas|Faith of Marendas]] share mathematics, metallurgy and engineering with the smiths of the Sacred Forge, and the priests of the [[affiliation-brazemas|Faith of Brazemas]] guard its foundries from lightning.
