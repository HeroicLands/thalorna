Sélmoros, the Silent Judge, is the deity of death in the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]]. The symbol is a black gate adorned with skeletal vines. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-thanatos|Faith of Sélmoros]], a temple priesthood of acolytes in training, ordained clergy and the Pontifex Mortis above them; the lay faithful keep the feasts without office.
