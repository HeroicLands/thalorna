Vëkthōr is the Forger of False Records, a demon lord of the [[affiliation-yokveng|Yōkvëng]] in the [[affiliation-tngvkvnlei|Tëngvōk Vān Lëi]]. His lies enter the celestial archive as though true. His standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-vekthor|Vëkthōr]] affiliation.
