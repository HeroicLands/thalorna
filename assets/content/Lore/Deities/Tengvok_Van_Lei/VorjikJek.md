Vōrjīk Jëk is the Lord of Fire, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tngvkvnlei|Tëngvōk Vān Lëi]]. Fire is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Fire is one of its appointed officials. His standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-vorjikjek|Vōrjīk Jëk]] affiliation.
