Zhütshüt Lüng is the Lord of Water, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tngvkvnlei|Tëngvōk Vān Lëi]]. Water is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Water is one of its appointed officials. His standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-zhutshtlng|Zhütshüt Lüng]] affiliation.
