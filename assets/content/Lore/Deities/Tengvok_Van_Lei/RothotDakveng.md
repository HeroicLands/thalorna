Röthöt Dākvëng is the Lord of Wood, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tngvkvnlei|Tëngvōk Vān Lëi]]. Wood is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Wood is one of its appointed officials. His standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-rothtdkvng|Röthöt Dākvëng]] affiliation.
