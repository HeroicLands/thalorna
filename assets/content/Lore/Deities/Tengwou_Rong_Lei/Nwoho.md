Nwoho is the Burning Dissolution, a demon lord of the [[affiliation-youweng|Youweng]] in the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. It holds destruction and the fire that takes a petition unanswered. It is held to have been born of a jurisdictional failure, when the Bureau of Returns and the Bureau of Transformation gave contradictory directives about the same material. What it consumes is not recycled but falls back into the [Hwenlei]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"} unclassified. It is invoked and never worshipped, and celestial law forbids the invocation. The [[affiliation-nuotsourui|Nwotsou Rwi]] has taken it as its principal patron, which the Celestial Marshals regard as the most alarming religious innovation in the empire in centuries; imperial suppression has not succeeded. Its cultus is described under [[affiliation-nuoho|Nwoho]].
