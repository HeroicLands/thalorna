Zhausou is the Unsorted, a demon lord of the [[affiliation-youweng|Youweng]] in the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. It holds confusion and the tiles that fall out of every order. It has an identity and a will, yet fits no category: it registers at once as a natural phenomenon, an ancestor spirit, a lesser demon and a geographic feature. It is held to have been caught mid-classification when the [Great Sorting]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"} moved on. It does not destroy but confuses: crops grow in the wrong season, rivers change direction, hierarchies invert. The [[affiliation-nuotsourui|Nwotsou Rwi]] invokes it in recruiting the classification-condemned. Its cultus is described under [[affiliation-zhaosou|Zhausou]].
