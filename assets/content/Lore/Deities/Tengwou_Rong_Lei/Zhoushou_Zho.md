Zhoushou Zho is the Lord of Water, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. Water is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Water is one of its appointed officials. He answers to [[affiliation-weizhuo|Weizhwo]] directly, and his petitions are less rigid in form than those to the Lords of Metal and Fire, though some communities hold their outcome less certain. Fishing communities keep shrines on rivers and honor him at the start of spawning season; farmers, millers and sailors petition him for irrigation, steady flow and calm seas. Petitioning and clergy are described under [[affiliation-zhoushouzho|Zhoushou Zho]].
