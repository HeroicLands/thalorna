Douyo is the Lost Road, a demon lord of the [[affiliation-youweng|Youweng]] in the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. It holds misdirection and the signpost that points three ways at once. It is said to have been a waymark spirit of the Bureau of Passage that began redirecting travelers, until caravans vanished from known routes and pilgrims arrived at the edges of the [[lore-infrnlrlms|Infernal Realms]]. The Bureau of Passage has never explained how one of its waymark spirits became corrupted. Its roads lead from despair to madness, from grief to oblivion, from ambition to ruin. The [[affiliation-nuotsourui|Nwotsou Rwi]] calls on it for evasion and misdirection. Its cultus is described under [[affiliation-douyo|Douyo]].
