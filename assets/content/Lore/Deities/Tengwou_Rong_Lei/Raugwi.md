Raugwi is the Minister of Storms in the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. He holds the weather as a portfolio of the celestial bureaucracy and administers it by schedule and precedent, not by caprice: every region's rainfall is an allocation fixed at the [Great Sorting]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"}. A drought is a petition incomplete, a counter-petition filed against it, or ancestors of low standing. His Bureau of Thunder and Rain also carries out punitive weather as recorded orders, which can be appealed to the Supreme Administrative Court. Villages petition him through his temples in farming regions and port cities. Petitioning and clergy are described under [[affiliation-raogui|Raugwi]].
