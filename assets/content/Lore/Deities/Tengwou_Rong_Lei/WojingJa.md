Wojing Ja is the Lord of Fire, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Téngwou Rong Lei]]. Fire is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Fire is one of its appointed officials. His standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-wojingja|Wojing Ja]] affiliation.
