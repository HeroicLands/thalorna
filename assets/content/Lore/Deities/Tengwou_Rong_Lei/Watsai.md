Watsai is the Forger of False Records, a demon lord of the [[affiliation-youweng|Youweng]] in the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. His lies enter the celestial archive as though true. He is said to have been an archival scribe, a Chougen, in the [[affiliation-wantou|Archive of All Records]] who began editing the classifications of the [Great Sorting]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"}, adding categories that never existed and shifting boundaries to leave gaps no bureau oversees. He fled into the [[lore-infrnlrlms|Infernal Realms]] with records no other being holds, and trades in what the celestial administration has chosen to forget. The [[affiliation-nuotsourui|Nwotsou Rwi]] serves him by corrupting the imperial registers from within. His cultus is described under [[affiliation-watsai|Watsai]].
