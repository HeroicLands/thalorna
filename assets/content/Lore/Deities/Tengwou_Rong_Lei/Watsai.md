Watsai is the Forger of False Records, a demon lord of the [[affiliation-youweng|Youwéng]] in the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Téngwou Rong Lei]]. His lies enter the celestial archive as though true. His standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-watsai|Watsai]] affiliation.
