Daotōu Wōchài is the Lord of Metal, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Téngwou Rong Léi]]. Metal is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Metal is one of its appointed officials. His standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-daotouwochai|Daotōu Wōchài]] affiliation.
