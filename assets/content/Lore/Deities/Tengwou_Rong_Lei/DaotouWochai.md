Daotou Wochai is the Lord of Metal, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. Metal is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Metal is one of its appointed officials. His standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-daotouwochai|Daotou Wochai]] affiliation.
