Wéngdao Zhòuwō is the Lord of Earth, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Téngwou Rong Léi]]. Earth is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Earth is one of its appointed officials. His standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-wengdaozhouwo|Wéngdao Zhòuwō]] affiliation.
