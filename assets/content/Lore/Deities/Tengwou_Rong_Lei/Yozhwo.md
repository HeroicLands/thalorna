Yozhwo is the Custodian of Lost Seals, a power of the [[affiliation-youweng|Youweng]] in the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]], neither a demon lord nor a lesser demon. It holds the broken seals by which a false warrant passes for a true one. Its standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-yozhuo|Yozhwo]] affiliation.
