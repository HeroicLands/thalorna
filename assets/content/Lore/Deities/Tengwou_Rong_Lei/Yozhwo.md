Yozhwo is the Custodian of Lost Seals, a power of the [[affiliation-youweng|Youweng]] in the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]], neither a demon lord nor a lesser demon. It holds the broken seals by which a false warrant passes for a true one. It is said to have been a scribe of the [[affiliation-wantou|Archive of All Records]] who made celestial seals and began producing counterfeits indistinguishable from the originals. It fled into the Youweng with an archive of them, and now keeps the [Infernal Archive]{.name data-type="place/site"} of stolen records and false documents. It asks a petitioner for a document from the celestial bureaucracy, preferably one bearing an actual seal. Its dealings are described under [[affiliation-yozhuo|Yozhwo]].
