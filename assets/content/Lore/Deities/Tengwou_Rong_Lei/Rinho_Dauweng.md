Rinho Dauweng is the Lord of Wood, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. Wood is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Wood is one of its appointed officials. He answers to [[affiliation-weizhuo|Weizhwo]] directly. He is held more accessible than the Lord of Metal, and his petitions allow more variation in form. Loggers petition him before working a new forest, carpenters for permission to work wood in a new form, and shipbuilders for the wood's integrity, alongside [[affiliation-wodou|Wodou]]. Logging communities keep shrines to him and observe seasonal festivals marking the turn from growing season to harvest. Petitioning, ranks and clergy are described under [[affiliation-rinhodaoweng|Rinho Dauweng]].
