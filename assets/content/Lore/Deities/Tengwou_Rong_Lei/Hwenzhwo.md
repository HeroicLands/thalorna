Hwenzhwo is the Lord of the Unclassified, a demon lord of the [[affiliation-youweng|Youweng]] in the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. It holds everything the celestial registry has no category for. The eldest of the demon lords, it formed within the [Hwenlei]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"} at the [Great Sorting]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"} as the part of primordial chaos that refused classification. Things near it lose their names, and mortals exposed to it lose the distinctions that make thought possible. The [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] consider any discussion of it dangerous, since thinking too precisely about anti-classification erodes the thinker's own categories. It cannot be invoked, since invocation requires categorizing, and the [[affiliation-nuotsourui|Nwotsou Rwi]] imitates it instead of praying to it. Its cultus is described under [[affiliation-henzhuo|Hwenzhwo]].
