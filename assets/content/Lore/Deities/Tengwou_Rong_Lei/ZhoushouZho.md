Zhòushòu Zhò is the Lord of Water, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Téngwou Rong Léi]]. Water is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Water is one of its appointed officials. His standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-zhoushouzho|Zhòushòu Zhò]] affiliation.
