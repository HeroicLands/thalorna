Wengdau Zhouwo is the Lord of Earth, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. Earth is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Earth is one of its appointed officials. He answers to [[affiliation-weizhuo|Weizhwo]] directly, and farming communities petition him through the territorial Touwo. His petitions are among the most formal and demanding. Farmers ask for fertile soil, quarry operators for a stone license, builders for leave to break ground, and explorers for leave to enter uncharted earth. He rejects petitions that lack documentation or merit. Petitioning and clergy are described under [[affiliation-wengdaozhouwo|Wengdau Zhouwo]].
