Tanzho is the First Classifier of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. He is said to have set the categories the celestial bureaucracy sorts the world into. Before him was the [Hwenlei]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"}, undifferentiated chaos; he drew the first boundary, the First Distinction, and then performed the [Great Sorting]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"}, and the gods were appointed to the offices it established. He is the principle of classification and not a minister: he hears no petitions and is not prayed to. The deepest fear in the Tantsou cosmology is that the bureaucracy holding the Hwenlei at the margins should fail. Mortals study the written record of his work in the [[affiliation-wantou|Archive of All Records]]. The tradition is described under [[affiliation-tanzho|Tanzho]].
