Dautou Wochai is the Lord of Metal, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. Metal is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Metal is one of its appointed officials. He answers to [[affiliation-weizhuo|Weizhwo]] directly and not to a bureau. Mortals petition him by offerings at temple shrines and by burning spirit-documents, and he accepts only formal petitions with correct spacing, seal placement and unambiguous language. Smiths ask him to keep their iron pure and responsive in the forging, miners ask leave to classify ore as extracted metal, and temple officials register new seals through his office. Petitioning, ranks and clergy are described under [[affiliation-daotouwochai|Dautou Wochai]].
