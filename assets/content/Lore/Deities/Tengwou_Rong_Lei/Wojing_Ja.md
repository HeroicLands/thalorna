Wojing Ja is the Lord of Fire, one of the five Elemental Lords of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. Fire is held as an office of the celestial bureaucracy, and the Lord of Fire is one of its appointed officials. He appears in dream-petitions and temple visions as a pillar of ranked flame, each tier a different classification of fire. He answers to [[affiliation-weizhuo|Weizhwo]] directly, and holds fire as the principle of transformation itself. Smiths petition him to keep a fire stable and responsive, alchemists to certify a transmutation as orderly reclassification, and mortuary workers for the dignity of a cremation. Petitioning and clergy are described under [[affiliation-wojingja|Wojing Ja]].
