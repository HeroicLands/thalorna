Youbwi is the Keeper of the Jade Seal in the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. Her seal authenticates the directives passed between the bureaus. Her domain is the moon, night, dreams, cycles and prophecy. Her standing is recorded in the [[affiliation-youwei|Youbwi]] affiliation.
