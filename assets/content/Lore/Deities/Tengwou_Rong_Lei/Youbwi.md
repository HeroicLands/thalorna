Youbwi is the Keeper of the Jade Seal in the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]]. Her seal authenticates the directives passed between the bureaus. She holds no bureau. Without her seal no order of the Supreme Administrative Court reaches the bureaus as legitimate authority, and she cannot be promoted, demoted or replaced, since she is the instrument of the Seal and not its holder. She is a witness who verifies that a document is in order and cannot modify it. Mortals do not petition her; most temples of the Jade Sovereign keep a small altar to her, where petitioners leave paper, ink and wax. She is ascendant in her lunar aspect in late autumn, at the Moon Festival, when the boundary between the sorted and the unsorted is thinnest, and offerings then ask her to keep the classification whole through the winter. The summit of Yujai is kept as a shrine of the moon in her name. Her seal is described under [[affiliation-youwei|Youbwi]].
