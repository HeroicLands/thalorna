Weizhwo is the Jade Sovereign of the [[affiliation-tengwouronglei|Tengwou Rong Lei]], at the head of the celestial bureaucracy and answerable for its whole administration. She is second only to Tanzho, and her authority is procedural: she enforces precedent and does not create law. When two bureaus claim the same jurisdiction she consults the master registry and applies the established classification. Her Supreme Administrative Court determines jurisdiction and hears appeals alleging procedural error. The Teng Wozhwo, the Celestial Marshals, are her enforcement arm, and the Zhiwo keep the star registry. Her temples are rarely crowded; the faithful come to study precedent and to have contracts and oaths sealed and registered. Her bureau is described under [[affiliation-weizhuo|Weizhwo]].
