Pik'ul Ts'umaq is the Owl of the Serpent, a fallen celestial of the Itsani tradition. He guarded the threshold of [[place-chaltzumaq|Ch'al Ts'umaq]] and now opens the boundary he was set to keep shut. He stands apart from the orthodox [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]], which counts him a nemesis, and is the focus of the veneration [[affiliation-pikultzumaq|Pik'ul Ts'umaq]].
