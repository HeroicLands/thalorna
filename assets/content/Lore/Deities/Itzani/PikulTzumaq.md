Pik'ul Tz'umaq is the Owl of the Serpent, a fallen celestial of the Itzáni tradition. He guarded the threshold of [[place-chaltzumaq|Ch'al Tz'umaq]] and now opens the boundary he was set to keep shut. He stands apart from the orthodox [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni Pantheon]], which counts him a nemesis, and is the focus of the veneration [[affiliation-pikultzumaq|Pik'ul Tz'umaq]].
