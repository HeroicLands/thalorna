K'anix Ha'kan is the Golden Giver of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni Pantheon]], the maize god from whose sacred dough humankind was made. He is beloved rather than feared, and his festivals feed even the slaves. He holds prosperity and the harvest, and is served by the [[affiliation-kanixhakan|Faith of K'anix Ha'kan]].
