K'anish Ha'kan is the Golden Giver of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]], the maize god from whose sacred dough humankind is said to have been made. He is beloved rather than feared, and his festivals feed even the slaves. He holds prosperity and the harvest, and is served by the [[affiliation-kanixhakan|Faith of K'anish Ha'kan]].
