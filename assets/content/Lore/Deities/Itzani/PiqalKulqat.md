P'iqal Kul'qat is the Builder of the Sacred Peak in the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]]. In its theology, the goddess has raised the world five times and teaches that failure is how one learns to build better. She holds creation, building and renewal, and is served by the [[affiliation-piqalkulqat|Faith of P'iqal Kul'qat]], which ranks its priests by craft rather than birth.
