P'iqal Kul'qat is the Builder of the Sacred Peak in the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni Pantheon]]. The goddess has raised the world five times and teaches that failure is how one learns to build better. She holds creation, building and renewal, and is served by the [[affiliation-piqalkulqat|Faith of P'iqal Kul'qat]], which ranks its priests by craft rather than birth.
