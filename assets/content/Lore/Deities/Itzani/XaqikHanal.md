Xaq'ik Ha'nal is the Blossom of Living Waters, the newest of the Itzáni Ten, holding fertility, healing and guardianship of the sacred pools. The deity belongs to the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni Pantheon]] and is served by the [[affiliation-xaqikhanal|Faith of Xaq'ik Ha'nal]], whose almost entirely female priesthood are the finest physicians in Ki'ichek civilization. Xaq'ik Ha'nal and K'anix Ha'kan are invoked together as the Twin Givers.
