K'ayik Ch'ul is the Sacred Flame of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni Pantheon]]. He alone turns the blood-offering into divine substance, and his fires are never allowed to die. He holds fire, transformation and sacrifice, and is served by the [[affiliation-kayikchul|Faith of K'ayik Ch'ul]].
