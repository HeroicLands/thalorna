P'uqil Ch'akun is the Feathered Storm-Serpent of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]]. In its theology, his rain feeds the maize, and his waking will end the [Fifth Age]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"}. He holds storms, chaos and cosmic dissolution, and is served by the [[affiliation-puqilchaqun|Faith of P'uqil Ch'akun]].
