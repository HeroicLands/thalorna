P'uqil Ch'aqun is the Feathered Storm-Serpent of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni Pantheon]]. His rain feeds the maize, and his waking will end the Fifth Age. He holds storms, chaos and cosmic dissolution, and is served by the [[affiliation-puqilchaqun|Faith of P'uqil Ch'aqun]].
