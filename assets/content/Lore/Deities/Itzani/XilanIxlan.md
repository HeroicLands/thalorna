Xilan Ix'lan is the Walker of Paths in the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni Pantheon]], the god of the sacbeob and the road shrines that carry word across the continent. The deity holds travel, trade and communication, and is served by the [[affiliation-xilanixlan|Faith of Xilan Ix'lan]], a priesthood that is mobile by design.
