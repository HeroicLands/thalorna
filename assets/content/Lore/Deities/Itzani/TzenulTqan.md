Ts'en'ul Tq'an is the Sun at the World's Edge, sovereign of the Itsani [Fifth Age]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"}. Itsani theology holds that his sunrise is bought with blood and never given freely. He holds order, cosmic law and the solar cycle among the gods of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]], and is served by the [[affiliation-tzenultqan|Faith of Ts'en'ul Tq'an]].
