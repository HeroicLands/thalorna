K'in'ul Tq'an is the Sun at the World's Edge, sovereign of the Itzáni Fifth Age. His sunrise is bought with blood and never given freely. He holds order, cosmic law and the solar cycle among the gods of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni Pantheon]], and is served by the [[affiliation-kinultqan|Faith of K'in'ul Tq'an]].
