Tz'uqil Ix'bal is the Death Jaguar of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni Pantheon]]: the jaguar that kills swiftly and the owl that watches the slow return to earth. The deity holds death, decay and the underworld, and is served by the [[affiliation-tzuqilixbal|Faith of Tz'uqil Ix'bal]], whose codices guide a soul through Ch'al Tz'umaq.
