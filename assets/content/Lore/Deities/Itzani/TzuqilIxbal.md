Ts'uqil Ish'bal is the Death Jaguar of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]]: the jaguar that kills swiftly and the owl that watches the slow return to earth. The deity holds death, decay and the underworld, and is served by the [[affiliation-tzuqilixbal|Faith of Ts'uqil Ish'bal]], whose codices guide a soul through [[place-chaltzumaq|Ch'al Ts'umaq]].
