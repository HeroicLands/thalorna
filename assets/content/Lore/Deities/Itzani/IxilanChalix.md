Ix'ilan Ch'alix is the Sacred Star of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni Pantheon]]: the deity of knowledge, writing and the calendar, who composed the first codex in the language of the stars. Veneration of her is carried by the [[affiliation-ixilanchalix|Faith of Ix'ilan Ch'alix]], whose priesthood alone advances on merit.
