Ish'ilan Ch'alish is the Sacred Star of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]], the goddess of knowledge, wisdom and writing, of the calendar and of astronomy. Her symbol is a star within an open codex. The [[affiliation-ixilanchalix|Faith of Ish'ilan Ch'alish]] venerates her, and her ritual is [[skill-ixilanchalix|Ritual: Ish'ilan Ch'alish]].

## The First Codex

In cosmological myth she was present at the creation of the world and composed the very first codex, a perfect record of creation written in the language of the stars, in planetary conjunction and stellar alignment that repeat eternally. All later human calendars are held to be imperfect copies of her original. [[lore-itsaniclndr|The Itsani Calendar]] is her sacred language, the means by which she speaks to mortals through celestial signs, and every celestial event is read as a word or phrase in her infinite discourse.

Unique among the Itsani gods, she is sometimes understood to know the future, including when the [Fifth Age]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"} will end.

## Worship

Worship of Ish'ilan Ch'alish is the least bloody and the most laborious in the Itsani tradition. Inscribing and copying codices is itself worship: preparing bark-paper, mixing inks and rendering glyphs are all conducted as ritual action. A night at the sighting stones recording a planet's position is understood as listening rather than measuring. Blood is given in small, precise quantities at astronomically fixed moments, a few drops at the instant of a conjunction, offered on the page rather than at an altar.

The Reckoner of Conjunctions issues the auspicious and forbidden days to every other branch and, through them, to the city. Her priesthood alone advances on merit: the senior priest, the Ish'tq'an (Star-Voice), must hold mastery of mathematics and astronomy, and advancement is by examination.

## Relations

- [[affiliation-tzenultqan|Faith of Ts'en'ul Tq'an]]: the solstice hours and the fifty-two-year realignment come from her observatories, while the solar priesthood claims to guard the knowledge of the age's ending that she is held to possess.
- [[affiliation-puqilchaqun|Faith of P'uqil Ch'akun]]: the serpent-keepers take her branch's calculation of the final configuration and seal it in chambers the astronomers may not enter.
- [[lore-piqalkulqatdty|P'iqal Kul'qat]]: the two branches advance by demonstrated competence, builder-priests raise the observatories, and setting a new sighting-stone is a joint rite.
- [[lore-xilanixlandty|Shilan Ish'lan]]: the road priests carry her branch's corrections and observations between cities, and astronomers laid the lines of the white roads.
- [[lore-naliktzuqaldty|Nal'ik Ts'uqal]]: the dream-keepers are her branch's one real intellectual rival, and the rivalry is amiable.
