Ish'ilan Ch'alish is the Sacred Star of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]]: the deity of knowledge, writing and the calendar, who is said to have composed the first codex in the language of the stars. Veneration of her is carried by the [[affiliation-ixilanchalix|Faith of Ish'ilan Ch'alish]], whose priesthood alone advances on merit.
