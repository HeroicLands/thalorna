Báhramis, the Thunder Rider, is the deity of storms, battle and protection in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]], held to guard those who stand in the open. The symbol is a spear wrapped in a spiral of clouds. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-bahramis|Faith of Báhramis]]. Every sacred site of the Ashaian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]], and served by the same priesthood of Roqeshekh, Delkoshekh and Shekhul.
