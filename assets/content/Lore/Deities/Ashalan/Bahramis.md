Báhrámiš, the Thunder Rider, is the deity of storms, battle and protection in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Āsháian Pantheon]], guarding those who stand in the open. The symbol is a spear wrapped in a spiral of clouds. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-bahramis|Faith of Báhrámiš]]. Every sacred site of the Āsháian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to Ātáröš, and served by the same priesthood of Āthravān, Hērbad and Mōbad.
