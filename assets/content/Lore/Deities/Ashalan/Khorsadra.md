Khórsadra, the Bearer of Eternal Light, is the deity of knowledge, wisdom and truth in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]], the truth on which the desert faith is built. The symbol is a golden sunburst with radiant beams. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-korsadra|Faith of Khórsadra]]. Every sacred site of the Ashaian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]], and served by the same priesthood of Roqeshekh, Delkoshekh and Shekhul.
