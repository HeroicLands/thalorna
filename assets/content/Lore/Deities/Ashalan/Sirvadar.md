Šírvádár, the Wandering Guide, is the deity of travel, trade routes, commerce and safe passage in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Āsháian Pantheon]], watching over the caravan roads of the desert. The symbol is a star chart etched on a golden disc, surrounded by a caravan route. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-sirvadar|Faith of Šírvádár]]. Every sacred site of the Āsháian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to Ātáröš, and served by the same priesthood of Āthravān, Hērbad and Mōbad.
