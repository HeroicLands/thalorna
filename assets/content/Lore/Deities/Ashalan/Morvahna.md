Mórvahna, the Silent Keeper, is the deity of decay in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]], holding the slow undoing of everything made. The symbol is a crescent moon cradling a wilting flower. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-morvahna|Faith of Mórvahna]]. Every sacred site of the Ashaian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]], and served by the same priesthood of Roqeshekh, Delkoshekh and Shekhul.
