Mórváhñä, the Silent Keeper, is the deity of decay in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Āsháian Pantheon]], holding the slow undoing of everything made. The symbol is a crescent moon cradling a wilting flower. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-morvahna|Faith of Mórváhñä]]. Every sacred site of the Āsháian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to Ātáröš, and served by the same priesthood of Āthravān, Hērbad and Mōbad.
