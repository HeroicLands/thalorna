Azravan, Lord of the Final Horizon, is the deity of death in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]], who receives the dead at the edge of the world. The symbol is a black sun with a golden edge. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-azravan|Faith of Azravan]]. Every sacred site of the Ashaian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]], and served by the same priesthood of Roqeshekh, Delkoshekh and Shekhul.
