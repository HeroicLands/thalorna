Ázráván, Lord of the Final Horizon, is the deity of death in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Āsháian Pantheon]], who receives the dead at the edge of the world. The symbol is a black sun with a golden edge. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-azravan|Faith of Ázráván]]. Every sacred site of the Āsháian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to Ātáröš, and served by the same priesthood of Āthravān, Hērbad and Mōbad.
