Míthara, the World's First Light, is the deity of creation, renewal, cosmic life and artisanship in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]], patron of the craft by which made things endure. The symbol is a tree with roots entwined in a circle, eternally renewing itself. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-mizhara|Faith of Míthara]]. Every sacred site of the Ashaian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]], and served by the same priesthood of Roqeshekh, Delkoshekh and Shekhul.
