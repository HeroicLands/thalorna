Míthárä, the World's First Light, is the deity of creation, renewal, cosmic life and artisanship in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Āsháian Pantheon]], patron of the craft by which made things endure. The symbol is a tree with roots entwined in a circle, eternally renewing itself. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-mithara|Faith of Míthárä]]. Every sacred site of the Āsháian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to Ātáröš, and served by the same priesthood of Āthravān, Hērbad and Mōbad.
