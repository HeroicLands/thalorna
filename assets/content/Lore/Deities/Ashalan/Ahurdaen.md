Ahurdaen, the Keeper of Harmony, is the deity of order in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]], holding harmony against the falsehood that erodes it. The deity's symbol is a golden flame encircled by stars. The cult is kept in the [[affiliation-ahurdaen|Faith of Ahurdaen]], which is aligned with the pantheon as a whole. Every sacred site of the Ashaian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]], and served by the same priesthood of Roqeshekh, Delkoshekh and Shekhul.
