Āhúrdáén, the Keeper of Harmony, is the deity of order in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Āsháian Pantheon]], holding harmony against the falsehood that erodes it. The deity's symbol is a golden flame encircled by stars. The cult is kept in the [[affiliation-ahurdaen|Faith of Āhúrdáén]], which is aligned with the pantheon as a whole. Every sacred site of the Āsháian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to Ātáröš, and served by the same priesthood of Āthravān, Hērbad and Mōbad.
