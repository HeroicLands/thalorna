Zúráthrä, the Mother of the Oasis, is the deity of fertility, water, life and healing in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Āsháian Pantheon]]. The symbol is a flowing spring encircled by palm fronds. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-zurathra|Faith of Zúráthrä]]. Every sacred site of the Āsháian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to Ātáröš, and served by the same priesthood of Āthravān, Hērbad and Mōbad.
