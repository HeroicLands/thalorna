Zúrathra, the Mother of the Oasis, is the deity of fertility, water, life and healing in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]]. The symbol is a flowing spring encircled by palm fronds. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-zurathra|Faith of Zúrathra]]. Every sacred site of the Ashaian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]], and served by the same priesthood of Roqeshekh, Delkoshekh and Shekhul.
