Aravesta, the Golden Giver, is the deity of prosperity, wealth, hedonism and sensual pleasure in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]]. The symbol is a golden chalice overflowing with wine and fruit, encircled by ivy vines. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-aravesta|Faith of Aravesta]]. Every sacred site of the Ashaian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]], and served by the same priesthood of Roqeshekh, Delkoshekh and Shekhul.
