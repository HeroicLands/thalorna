Árávēštä, the Golden Giver, is the deity of prosperity, wealth, hedonism and sensual pleasure in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Āsháian Pantheon]]. The symbol is a golden chalice overflowing with wine and fruit, encircled by ivy vines. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-aravesta|Faith of Árávēštä]]. Every sacred site of the Āsháian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to Ātáröš, and served by the same priesthood of Āthravān, Hērbad and Mōbad.
