Zárványä, the Weaver of Visions, is the deity of dreams in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Āsháian Pantheon]], holding dreams and what is read in them. The symbol is a silver crescent moon with trailing threads. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-zarvanya|Faith of Zárványä]]. Every sacred site of the Āsháian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to Ātáröš, and served by the same priesthood of Āthravān, Hērbad and Mōbad.
