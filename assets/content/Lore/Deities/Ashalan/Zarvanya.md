Zárvanya, the Weaver of Visions, is the deity of dreams in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]], holding dreams and what is read in them. The symbol is a silver crescent moon with trailing threads. Worship is kept in the [[affiliation-zarvanya|Faith of Zárvanya]]. Every sacred site of the Ashaian faith is a fire-temple, so this deity is worshipped at an altar beside the one kept to Ataros, and served by the same priesthood of Roqeshekh, Delkoshekh and Shekhul.
