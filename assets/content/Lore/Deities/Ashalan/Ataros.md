Ataros, the Sacred Flame, is the deity of fire in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]], and the fire in which the tradition works its purifications. The symbol is a flame atop an altar, encircled by sparks. Because every sacred site of the faith is a fire-temple, every temple keeps an altar to Ataros whichever deity it is focused on, and its priests are trained in the kindling and tending of the flame. The cult is kept in the [[affiliation-ataros|Faith of Ataros]].
