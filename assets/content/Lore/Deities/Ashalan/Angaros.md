Angaros, the Shadowed Serpent, is the deity of chaos in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]], and the falsehood the desert faith sets its whole cosmology against. The symbol is a serpent coiled around a broken blade. The fire-temple priesthood names him among the twelve, but it keeps no fire and performs no rite for him. His real worshippers are the forbidden cells of the [[affiliation-angaros|Faith of Angaros]], which meet in secret and answer to no temple.
