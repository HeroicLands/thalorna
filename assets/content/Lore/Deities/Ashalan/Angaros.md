Angaros, the Shadowed Serpent, is the deity of chaos in the [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Ashaian Pantheon]], and the falsehood the desert faith sets its whole cosmology against. The symbol is a serpent coiled around a broken blade. The [[affiliation-angaros|Faith of Angaros]] is nonetheless counted aligned with the pantheon, and is served by the same fire-temple priesthood as the other eleven.
