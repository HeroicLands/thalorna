_Lord of Silent Passage – a black hawk with gold-tipped wings._

Sēker guides souls through the underworld, keeps the tombs, and guards the boundary between the
living world and the realm of the dead. He is **neither cruel nor kind, but impartial and
inevitable**. As the sun sets each day into the western horizon, so every mortal passes westward
into his realm: there is no escaping him, no bargaining with him, and no cheating his dominion. He
is not, for all that, a god of terror. He is a god of order.

He does not judge. That belongs to [[lore-maatdty|Má'át]]; Sēker's charge is to see that the soul
comes before her in proper condition and properly prepared, and that afterward it is guided to its
destiny. Where [[lore-anubisdty|Ánubís]] presides over the transformation of the body, Sēker
presides over the passage of the soul – which is why their priesthoods share the mortuary temples
and divide the work between the embalming table and the rite.

The theology holds that death is not an ending but a transition, and the most important one in
existence. From that follows the reorientation of values his faith teaches, and with it much of what
outsiders find morbid in Ta'Kheperu: the monuments one builds will crumble, the kingdoms one
conquers will be conquered, the wealth one gathers will be inherited – and the afterlife is eternal.
The elaborate care of the dead is not an obsession with dying but a judgment about where value
actually lies.

Grave-robbery is, in his theology, not theft but **cosmic blasphemy**: it breaks the order he
maintains and threatens the rest of the dead.
