_The Crocodile-God._

Sobek is the god of the river-beasts, and his cult carries the sharpest practical tension in the
Kheperi religion: **the animal sacred to him is also the animal that takes farmers and children from
the water's edge**, and must sometimes be killed. The cull is therefore a religious act before it is
a commercial one, and someone must license it.

His great cult sits in the eastern marsh among the river-beast hunters; he is patron besides of a
dry, sun-burned garrison nome of desert wells and quarry-roads, and of the narrow gorge-stretch where
the valley pinches almost to nothing—a spread that suggests the god is understood as belonging to
dangerous water and dangerous ground alike, rather than to crocodiles narrowly.

The working out of that tension is visible in [[affiliation-sekhetneru|the Sekhet'Neru]], whose
sacred hunt culls the river-beasts under temple dispensation and commends every kill to the river
god for judgment.
