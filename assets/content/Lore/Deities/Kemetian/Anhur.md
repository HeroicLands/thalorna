_Patron of the Hunt._

Anhur is one of Ta'Kheperu's two hunt-patrons, and never invoked alone: he is paired in every rite
that matters with [[lore-shesmudty|Shesmu]], and the senior elevations of
[[affiliation-pernuw|Per'Nuw]], the Per-Aá's chartered guild of hunters, are conducted as **rites at
both temples performed in tandem** – neither counting without the other.

What distinguishes his patronage is that it does not end at the kill. The god's concern is
understood to extend to **the conduct of the hunter**, which is the religious weight behind the
guild's standing principle that the beast is a creature of the Per-Aá's lands and that wantonness in
its killing offends the gods as much as the imperial order.
