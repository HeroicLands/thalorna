_The Fertility-God._

Min is an upper-river god of harvest-festivals and virility cults, seated at the caravan-head for
the eastern desert roads. The pairing is his distinctive feature: **the same god is asked for the
increase of the fields and for the safe return of the caravans**, and his festivals mark both.

He stands outside [[affiliation-kemtnpnthn|the Twelve]] and overlaps in domain with
[[lore-isetdty|Íšét]], whose fertility is the river's and the womb's; Min's appears to be the drier
and more masculine kind, belonging to the desert edge rather than the floodplain.

cult or was simply attached to it because the caravans happened to start there.
