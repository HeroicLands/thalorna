_The Guiding Falcon—a falcon in flight with a compass rose._

Hórús is the god of travelers, explorers, and all who venture into the unknown. He manifests as a
great falcon soaring above the lands of Thalorna with sight keen enough to see distant horizons and
obstacles hidden from anyone on the ground. He watches over merchants guiding caravans, soldiers
marching into foreign country, pilgrims bound for distant temples, and anyone drawn by curiosity or
necessity into unmapped land.

He is emphatically **not a protector who shields the traveler from hardship**. What he offers is
sight, not safety.

The symbol says it twice over. The **falcon** sees from great height, navigates wind with precision,
and strikes decisively when it must; the **compass rose** is the human need to orient—to know
direction and purpose, and to find the way home from a great distance. Together they name a god who
perceives the larger patterns and roads that ground-bound mortals cannot.

His cult is unusual among the Kheperi in standing close to ordinary practical life. Rather than
concentrating in great temples it distributes itself through shrine-stations along the major travel
routes, and its priests travel constantly, so the knowledge of conditions and dangers stays current
and worth having.
