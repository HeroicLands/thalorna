_The Cobra-Goddess._

Wadjit is a delta goddess of the papyrus marshes, whose nome lives by fowling, reed-craft and the
empire's paper-cutting. Her reach is quiet and very long. In a civilization that runs on records –
whose tax rolls, contracts, temple archives and unbroken king-lists are the substance of its
government – **the reed-beds her priests bless are the material the entire scribal empire is written
on**.

She stands outside [[affiliation-kemtnpnthn|the Twelve]], and the disproportion is striking: a local
marsh cult with no imperial standing supplies the physical basis of
[[lore-thothdty|Thōth]]'s whole domain.
