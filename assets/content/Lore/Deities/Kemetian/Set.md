_Bringer of Tempests—a serpent coiled around a lightning bolt._

Sét is the god of storms, wind, and the ferocious power of nature at its most violent. The
distinction from [[lore-apophisdty|Ápōphis]] is doctrinally important and easily missed: where
Ápōphis is primordial chaos threatening all existence, Sét embodies the destructive and
**purifying** power of natural phenomena—the thunderhead that shatters ancient trees, the gale
that drives ships onto rocks, the lightning that burns away corruption, the wind that carries
necessary change. He is a god within the order, not against it.

He belongs to no one landscape. The Kheperi understand him as the breath and fury of the sky itself,
equally at home over the cultivated valley, the mountains, the sea and the far steppe: the late
summer thunderstorms over the river, the gales that churn the Vylarian coast, the squalls that come
down off the eastern hills and flatten standing grain in an hour.

To worship him is to acknowledge that not all divine power flows through gentle channels—that his
tempests are a reminder that nature runs on its own logic rather than on human convenience. His
worship is conducted with solemn formality and constant awareness of invoking a dangerous power, and
his temples are set on high ground where lightning is likeliest to fall.
