_The Solar Flame—a blazing sun disk with rays of fire._

Rā is the primordial fire from which all creation springs, but he is more than the sun in the sky: he
is **the principle of illumination itself**, the force that makes truth visible, purifies the
corrupted, and drives back the chaos that eternally threatens creation. Each day he crosses the
heavens; each night he descends into the underworld to war on [[lore-apophisdty|Ápōphis]] so that
morning will come at all.

To worship Rā is therefore to worship the perpetual triumph of order over chaos, and his demand on a
life is a hard one: clarity, purpose, and righteous intensity. As Rā illuminates the physical world,
the righteous are called to illuminate the moral one—to burn falsehood out of their own words
first. His heat is understood as purifying rather than hostile; it burns away weakness, indulgence
and corruption, and warriors who have killed come to stand in it until the stain is gone.

His cult holds the throne itself. The Per-Aá is understood as Rā's earthly manifestation, so a
Per-Aá lacking the solar priesthood's blessing lacks legitimacy—which is why more than one has
owed his throne to it, and why the priesthood has uncrowned more dynasties than any army.

Unlike the Aurèldían [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Vúlcani]], whose god split into a sacred forge and a
black flame, Rā has no schism. His nature is singular: fire as purification and illumination, never
as destruction or domination. Foreign observers find the unity suspicious and suspect hidden
mysteries; the Kheperi answer that the unity is itself one of the god's gifts.
