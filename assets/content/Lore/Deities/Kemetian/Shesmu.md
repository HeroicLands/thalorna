_Patron of the Hunt._

Linqur is the second of Aû'Khelâthu's two hunt-patrons, inseparable in practice from
[[lore-anhurdty|Linhur]]. The guild of hunters conducts its senior elevations at both temples in
tandem, and neither rite counts alone.

Where Linhur's scrutiny falls on the **hunter**, Linqur's falls on the **killing itself**—that it
be done with the speed and efficiency the beast's nature permits. The division gives the pairing its
point: one god watches the man, the other watches the act, and a hunter answerable to both cannot
satisfy either by intention alone.
