Shaq'ik Ha'nal is the Blossom of Living Waters, the goddess of fertility, water, healing, renewal, birth and life in the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]]. Her symbol is a water lily blooming from a jade spring. The [[affiliation-xaqikhanal|Faith of Shaq'ik Ha'nal]] serves her, and her ritual is [[skill-xaqikhanal|Ritual: Shaq'ik Ha'nal]].

## Goddess of the Pools

She is the newest of the Ten, formally elevated to divine status within the last three centuries. She guards the sacred pools (_ch'uqil_), the natural sinkholes where underground rivers break through to daylight, understood as portals to [[place-chaltzumaq|Ch'al Ts'umaq]]. The waters are understood as her body, and any contamination is grave sacrilege. Where [[lore-kanixhakandty|K'anish Ha'kan]] represents agricultural fertility and [[lore-piqalkulqatdty|P'iqal Kul'qat]] the creative act of building, she represents biological fertility, healing and the renewal that comes with water. She and K'anish Ha'kan are invoked together as the [Twin Givers]{.name data-type="lore/deity"}.

## Worship

Her worship permeates daily life. Mothers bring children to springs or sacred pools for anointing and prayers for health, young women desiring to conceive make pilgrimages to the pools for ritual bathing, and healers perform cleansing rituals with blessed water before treating the sick. At birth a midwife, understood as her earthly representative, guides the new person into existence, and the first thing a newborn receives is water on the lips. That rite makes a Ki'i'chek Water-Marked and a member of the faith.

The major ceremony is the [Ts'uqal Ne']{.name data-type="lore/custom"} ("Moon Water"), held at the first full moon of the rainy season, when all water sources are formally blessed by processions of women carrying flowers, incense and jade. She is given little blood and a great deal of water: offerings are flowers, incense, jade and clean water.

Her priesthood is almost entirely female. The Ch'alish Ha'nal (Keeper of Waters) is chief healer of the city-state, birth priestesses attend all significant births, and healing priestesses maintain gardens of medicinal plants near water sources. Their archives of healing knowledge make them the most advanced practitioners of medicine in Ki'i'chek civilization.

## Relations

- [[lore-kanixhakandty|K'anish Ha'kan]]: the Twin Givers ensure both agricultural and biological fertility, and their branches share a festival calendar and charitable distributions.
- [[lore-tzuqilixbaldty|Ts'uqil Ish'bal]]: the two branches share the sacred pools as their holiest ground.
- [[lore-kayikchalixdty|K'ayik Ch'alish]]: her branch buys graded ash from the fire-keepers for its healing paints.
- [[lore-naliktzuqaldty|Nal'ik Ts'uqal]]: the two branches ask least and give most, and in many cities their temples adjoin.
- [[lore-tzenultqandty|Ts'en'ul Tq'an]]: relations are correct and cool; the solar branch teaches that nothing is given freely.
