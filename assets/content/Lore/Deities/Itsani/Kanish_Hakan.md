K'anish Ha'kan is the Golden Giver of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]], the god of prosperity, agriculture, abundance and sustenance. His symbol is a maize stalk bearing golden flowers. The [[affiliation-kanixhakan|Faith of K'anish Ha'kan]] serves him, and his ritual is [[skill-kanixhakan|Ritual: K'anish Ha'kan]].

## The Maize God

In the faith's teaching there was maize before even the Four Agelings were shaped, the primordial plant that grew from the heart of chaos. The gods created the current race of humans from the finely ground dough of sacred maize mixed with water and divine blood, so that all human flesh is maize-flesh, and K'anish Ha'kan mediates between that sacred origin and the present age. He is beloved rather than feared, and his gifts flow freely to the righteous. Where the solar god speaks in commandments carved upon stone, he speaks in the language of the ripening ear, the swelling kernel and the harvest made manifest.

## Worship

At the planting season farmers gather at the temples carrying seeds of maize, cacao and beans for blessing in the [Chuqtik]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} ceremony. The harvest festivals are the most lavish celebrations in the Kì'ì'chek calendar: temples distribute food freely, markets overflow and even slaves eat from the communal stores. The Ts'enmah process to the temples as supplicants carrying sheaves of maize, not as sovereigns.

Everyday devotion is domestic. The first tortilla of a meal is broken and a fragment set aside, and a kernel dropped on the floor is picked up and touched to the forehead. Bloodletting in his honor is light, a few drops on the seed at planting and on the first ear at harvest, and the god is understood to prefer the labor to the blood. A field that has failed is cut and burned and the ground is spoken over.

## Relations

- [[lore-xaqikhanaldty|Shaq'ik Ha'nal]]: the two are invoked together as the [Twin Givers]{.name data-type="lore/deity"}, the divine pair ensuring agricultural and biological fertility, and their branches share a festival calendar and charitable distributions.
- [[affiliation-puqilchaqun|Faith of P'uqil Ch'akun]]: the seed is nothing without the rain, and the two branches bless the Chuqtik together in ordinary years.
- [[affiliation-tzenultqan|Faith of Ts'en'ul Tq'an]]: the solar priests fix the day of the planting and the harvest, and the agricultural priests decide what is planted, where and by whom.
- [[lore-xilanixlandty|Shilan Ish'lan]]: the road priests carry the branch's surplus, and the cacao trade runs on their causeways.
- [[lore-piqalkulqatdty|P'iqal Kul'qat]]: the branch shares terraces, irrigation channels and reservoirs with the builder-priests.
