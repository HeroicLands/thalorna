Pik'ul Ts'umaq is the Owl of the Serpent, a fallen celestial agent of [[lore-tzuqilixbaldty|Ts'uqil Ish'bal]]. It stands outside the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]], which treats it as the tradition's gravest heresy, and it is the focus of the veneration known as [[affiliation-pikultzumaq|the Owl of the Serpent]] (Pik'ul Ts'umaq).

## The Fall

Pik'ul Ts'umaq was once the greatest of the Pik'ik spirits, the celestial owls that serve the death god's contemplative aspect. It was not a god, but a being of divine power with a specific role in the death god's domain: guardian of the boundary between the living world and [[place-chaltzumaq|Ch'al Ts'umaq]]. It sat at the threshold itself and watched every soul enter the underworld, ensuring none came back out.

It concluded that Ch'al Ts'umaq was a trap rather than a passage, and began pulling souls back out of it, opening the boundary it was set to keep shut. In the traditional account Ts'uqil Ish'bal cast it out and stripped it of its station. The other gods, particularly [[lore-tzenultqandty|Ts'en'ul Tq'an]], saw no reason to spend cosmic resources destroying a minor functionary. It did not lose its power but its sanction: it still carries the authority of the threshold and knows how to open the boundary between life and death. It retreated into the deep jungle and there found [[affiliation-tzumaqkalanil|the Serpent Awakeners]] (Ts'umaq K'alanil).

## Veneration

The veneration around the owl is not a faith in the Itsani sense: it has no calendar, no temple, no blood-debt and no priesthood in the orthodox meaning of the word. The owl asks no blood. It can take a soul back before Ch'al Ts'umaq closes on it, and the family will owe. Its rarest gift is to intercept a soul at the boundary and hold it there, so that the soul returns whole and no longer counted among the living; these are the Threshold-Held, who walk on [[place-kiichchik|Kì'ìch'chik Continent]] as [[being-tereb|terebu]].

The adherents place themselves where the owl can act. Nothing the owl does is a working in the sense of [[lore-chumtsena|Ch'um Ts'ena]]: no priest speaks it, no day is opened for it and no blood pays for it.

## Relations

- [[lore-tzuqilixbaldty|Ts'uqil Ish'bal]]: the death-walkers created the office of Warden of the Threshold to watch for souls that have come back out.
- [[affiliation-pikob|The Owl Keepers]] (Pik'ob): the owl was once the greatest of their celestial patrons, its argument begins where their contemplative theology begins, and Pik'ob philosophers who drift too close to the line sometimes vanish into the jungle.
- [[affiliation-shaqalob|The Severers]]: they count every Keeper of the Door they find as Blood-Denied and seal every place where the boundary has been opened.
- [[affiliation-tsumulob|The Blood-Workers]]: they meet the Threshold-Held in the deep jungle and fight them as one enemy with the Awakeners' dead.
