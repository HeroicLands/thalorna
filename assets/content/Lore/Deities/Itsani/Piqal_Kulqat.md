P'iqal Kul'qat is the Builder of the Sacred Peak in the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]], the goddess of creation, building and renewal, of craftsmanship and architecture. Her symbol is a stepped pyramid beneath a crescent moon. The [[affiliation-piqalkulqat|Faith of P'iqal Kul'qat]] serves her, and her ritual is [[skill-piqalkulqat|Ritual: P'iqal Kul'qat]].

## The Builder

Before time had a name the world was chaos. She took clay and stone and obsidian and began to build: she raised mountains, carved canyons, dug sacred pools and built the firmament of the sky. She has done so five times. When previous ages ended and the structures crumbled back into chaos she came again with her tools and knowledge, rebuilding creation anew, never quite the same and always better. The [Fifth Age]{.name data-type="lore/cosmology"} is her masterpiece, the culmination of four previous attempts, and her work is not finished, for she teaches that creation is never finished. Every pyramid raised by human hands participates in it. She is the goddess of all intentional making, invoked by architects, mothers in labor, farmers breaking new ground and craftspeople of every kind.

Her theology is fundamentally optimistic. Where the solar god demands fidelity because failure ends the world, she teaches that failure is part of learning to build better, and the ruins dotting the continent are traces of her experiments.

On [[place-kiichchik|Kì'ìch'chik Continent]] the [[lore-dreadspawncrtr|Dreadspawn]] are held to be her work, called **Kul'ik P'iqal**, "Living Works of the Builder".

## Worship

[Kul'qat Tik'il]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} (foundation-laying) and [Kul'qat Ts'apel]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} (dedication) mark the beginning and completion of sacred building. At the foundation the ground is opened, the lines are set from the astronomers' alignments, and jade and blood go into the first course. Artisans of every kind begin their work with small offerings, a potter pressing the goddess's glyph into a vessel's base, a weaver tying a knot in invocation, a mason placing jade into mortar. Blood is given at foundations and dedications and rarely otherwise, since she is held to prefer work to blood.

Her priesthood is organized by craft specialization rather than theological rank, and the Ch'alish Pu'itz (Master of Sacred Work) is almost always a master mason.

## Relations

- [[lore-ixilanchalixdty|Ish'ilan Ch'alish]]: both branches advance by demonstrated competence, and the setting of a new sighting-stone is a joint rite.
- [[lore-tzenultqandty|Ts'en'ul Tq'an]]: the branch builds the solar pyramids, but its own doctrine holds that every one of them will fall.
- [[lore-kayikchalixdty|K'ayik Ch'alish]]: the two branches argue over which god finished the making of humankind, and both formulas are spoken at a dedication.
- [[lore-xaqikhanaldty|Shaq'ik Ha'nal]]: the builder's glyph is commonly scratched on the lintel of a birthing-room where her water-priestesses attend the mother.
- [[lore-xilanixlandty|Shilan Ish'lan]]: the builder-priests raise and repair the causeways.
