K'ayik Ch'alish is the Sacred Flame of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]], the god of fire, transformation, purification and sacrifice. His symbol is a flame rising from a jade altar. The [[affiliation-kayikchalix|Faith of K'ayik Ch'alish]] serves him, and his ritual is [[skill-kayikchalix|Ritual: K'ayik Ch'alish]].

## The Transformer

K'ayik Ch'alish is the god of transformation through burning and the sacred conduit through which mortal sacrifice reaches the celestial realm. Without his willing acceptance of the blood-offering the theological structure of Ki'i'chek civilization collapses, for he alone can transform the mortal into the divine. The earliest myths describe him as the fire-keeper of creation, who tended the cosmic hearth from which the world was shaped and who brought the sacred fire that completed the first humans' maize dough. Fire is never merely destructive; it is purification through transformation. The ash that remains is the most sacred substance of all, rubbed on the foreheads of the sick and mixed into healing paints.

He shares an epithet and a domain with Ataros, the Sacred Flame of [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|the Twelve of the Flame]], and is otherwise unrelated to that god.

## Worship

The most fundamental act of worship is the maintenance of the eternal flames in every major temple, which are fed with copal resin, ceiba wood and mahogany. To let a sacred flame die requires human sacrifice as expiation. Every offering of every other branch passes through his branch, since an offering that is not burned has not been delivered.

A household hearth is kindled from the temple flame at the new year and is not permitted to go out during the five liminal days at the year's end. Every fifty-two years, when the [[lore-itsaniclndr#tsen|Ts'en Cycle]] and the [[lore-itsaniclndr#tqan|Tq'an Cycle]] realign, every fire in the city is extinguished and the city keeps silence for a day and a night. The Master of the Living Flame then works obsidian mirrors against the rising sun until the tinder catches, and the Kindlers run the new flame to every quarter at the [K'ayik Kul'al]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} ("the Living Flame").

## Relations

- [[lore-tzenultqandty|Ts'en'ul Tq'an]]: the sun is understood as the fire at the world's edge, and at the K'ayik Kul'al the highest fire-priests ascend the pyramid beside the Presiding Ch'alish Tq'an rather than behind him.
- [[lore-tzuqilixbaldty|Ts'uqil Ish'bal]]: a body belongs to the death-walkers and an offering to the fire-keepers, and cremation is practiced only where the death-walkers concede it.
- [[lore-xaqikhanaldty|Shaq'ik Ha'nal]]: her water-priestesses buy graded ash for healing paints.
- [[lore-piqalkulqatdty|P'iqal Kul'qat]]: the two branches argue over which god completed the making of humankind, the builder who shaped the dough or the fire-keeper who finished it.
