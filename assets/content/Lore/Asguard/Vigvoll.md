Vígvöll is the plain where the defending gods and the [[lore-valdrengir|Valdrengir]] muster at [[lore-aldarlok|Aldarlok]]. [[place-valsal|Valsal]] and [[place-solvangr|Sólvangr]] empty as the honored dead draw up beside the gods for the final battle. The Asguardian telling holds that the defenders know the muster fails and gather anyway; the field gives that defiance its appointed meeting place.
