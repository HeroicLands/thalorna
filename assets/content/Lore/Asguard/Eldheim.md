The fire-home: the region of [[place-asguard|Asguard]] that burns, and the one the other three are arranged against. Eldheim is Svartbrandr's, and the tradition describes a country and not a furnace: slag plains, ash weather, rivers that run and set and run again, a host quartered on the ground and a lord who feeds them. The heat is not a torment there. It is the climate, and the fire-kin are at home in it.

What Eldheim is for is the muster. Svartbrandr keeps his kin in order against the day he rides out, and the Asguardian account of [[lore-aldarlok|Aldarlok]] begins with the gate of the burning region opening rather than with any act of the defending gods. The skalds are consistent that the host is already assembled and has been for a long age, which is why the north treats the end as a thing waiting rather than a thing building.

Svartbrandr sits among the Ten notwithstanding, and his priesthood is not proscribed as Náhild's is. The distinction the rune-priests draw is between a god who will destroy the world and a god who hates it: Svartbrandr keeps his oaths, takes his blót, answers a smith who calls him, and will burn everything at the appointed hour because that is the office he holds. His extreme adherents at the Fire-Weeks are the ones the kingdoms watch, not the ordinary faithful at the forge.
