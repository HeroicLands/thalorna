The **Fire-Weeks** are the defining ordeal of [[affiliation-svartbrandur|Svartbrandur's faith]], kept at its forge-hovs and led at a season's turning by the hov's Hofgudi or Hofgudja alongside the great blet. The Asguardian calendar counts them among the faith's high rites.

## The Ordeal

"You walk it on your own feet," a forge-hov's Hofgudi tells a postulant, "or you do not walk it again." A postulant proves devotion here. The faith's account of the rite names the [Ashen Walk]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} across hot coals as its trial: the postulant either survives it on his own feet or does not undertake it again. The faith's ceremonies describe the same walk as the rite in which high priests cross coals or flames to show their devotion and their immunity to fire.

## Who Keeps Them

- A _Hofsmadur_ is given to a forge-hov young and labors at its bellows and its Fire-Weeks for years before any claim on the priesthood.
- A _Gudi_ or _Gudja_ is ordained to keep a hov's rites and its Fire-Weeks outright.
- A _Hofgudi_ or _Hofgudja_ performs the Fire-Weeks, with the great blet, at a season's turning.
- A _Hofsgudi_ keeps a named forge-hov together with its Fire-Weeks, its stores and its blet.

## Refusal

A man whose offering the hov's gudi declines during the Fire-Weeks holds [[lore-blotrefusedrnk|Refused the Blet]] standing, called Bletlok. The refusal cuts him from Svartbrandur's clergy and from the ordeal that proves devotion there.

## The Orders

The faith's militant and craft orders take their part in the Fire-Weeks:

- [[affiliation-eldulfur|Eldúlfur]], the Infernal Blades, grant standing to a member who survives the Fire-Weeks and the fights that follow them, and no rite a gudi alone can perform confers the same.
- [[affiliation-eldverdir|Eldverdir]], the Flameguard, stand watch over a hov through its Fire-Weeks and answer any threat to it with the same flame the rite uses.
- [[affiliation-smideldur|Smideldur]], the Order of the Inferno, tend the bellows that keep a hov's ordeals burning through the Fire-Weeks and beyond them.
