Thursguard is the enclosed world into which the [[affiliation-asguardian|Ten]] drove the defeated [[lore-thursaett|Thursaett]] before the world stood. It is walled out in the _ginn_, the void beyond creation that [[lore-theshadow|the Shadow]] comes from, and the giants were driven past the edge of anything made to reach it. [[lore-hrimthurspr|Hrímthur]] alone was killed instead, his body cut into [[place-worldthlrn|Mannguard]] in the [[lore-thursblot|Thursblót]]. The Asguardian teaching distinguishes Thursguard from Mannguard and [[place-asguard|Asguard]], the two worlds joined by [[lore-heimsask|Heimsask]]. No telling describes Thursguard from within; the rune-priests teach only that the giant-kin are confined there and that nothing has come back out.
