"Nobody describes Thursguard from the inside," a rune-priest at [[place-odholm|Ódholm]] says to a newcomer, "and that is the whole of what we teach about it." The [[affiliation-asguardian|Ten]] drove the defeated [[lore-thursaett|Thursaett]] into it before the world stood. It is an enclosed world, walled out in the _ginn_, the void beyond creation that [[lore-theshadow|the Shadow]] comes from, and the giants were driven past the edge of anything made to reach it.

The teaching keeps Thursguard separate from the two worlds the living and the gods share. [[place-asguard|Asguard]] and [[place-worldthlrn|Mannguard]] are joined by the world-ash [[lore-heimsask|Heimsask]]; Thursguard has no part in that growth. Of the giant-kin only [[lore-hrimthurspr|Hrímthur]] stayed in the made world, killed instead of banished, his body cut into Mannguard in the [[lore-thursblot|Thursblót]].

The rune-priests say that the giant-kin are confined there and that nothing has come back out. No telling goes further, and the priests decline to say what the giants have become in the dark.
