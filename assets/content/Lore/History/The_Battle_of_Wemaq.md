The best water on [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] is [[place-wemaq|Wemaq]], and nobody camps at it. The reason is the morning in 312 AF when the second great orqwen came to it.

## The Last Refusal

The orqwen did not have the steppe yet. He or she had the tribes already sworn, and a union is made by swearing, so one tribe that held out was one too many. It was camped at Wemaq with its herds, some four thousand people. The orqwen came to the pool at the head of the sworn tribes and put them to the sword over a morning, and forbade anyone to lay them out. Their names were not spoken to the four winds. The tribe's dowek was made to watch.

## What Followed

Three years later the union went round the western end of the Grazian wall and became [[lore-hndrdbnnrs|the Storm of the Hundred Banners]]. The year the orqwen came to Wemaq is year 1 of the Hundred Banners in the [[lore-swdsclndr|steppe reckoning]].

The oasis is a moderate blood-field of the quickening kind: the palms fruit twice a year, a sleeper dreams the battle, and no tribe claims the water. It is the one oasis on the road that is nobody's.
