> **GM-only historical account.** This inherited record is not knowledge available to ordinary travelers.

The private record of [[affiliation-kalihara|Kalihara]] opens with **The Coming of the Teachers** and says nothing of what preceded it. In the islanders' account, the Ithari arrived from somewhere that is not [[place-worldthlrn|Thalorna]], settled the island, and did not conquer it: the first humans they met there were scattered bands in the most rudimentary conditions, and the teachers took them in.

The Kaliharans do not publish this account. It gives a bound rather than a date: the teachers were present before 10,000 BF, but the record does not say how long before. Their origin is not named.

[[lore-itharisul|Precursor Stone]] preserves the island's account.
