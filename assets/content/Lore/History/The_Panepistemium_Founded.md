The most significant of the Helionite institutions was the
**[[affiliation-panepistmm|Panepistēmion]]** — the _place of all knowledge_, which Vylarian tongues
later wore down to the Panepistemium and the Common tongue calls simply the Academy of Knowledge.

It was conceived as a federation of all philosophical inquiry. Each city-state had its own scholarly
traditions — some emphasising natural philosophy, others ethics, others the arcane — and the
Epistemium was an attempt to unite them under a shared framework of standards and mutual recognition.
It was not a guild or a government body: it maintained its standards through peer reputation and
scholarly consensus rather than legal authority, and it had no power to compel anyone.

For roughly a century the arrangement flourished, and the colleges were roughly equal. Its College of
Arcane Philosophy developed the foundational theories of elemental classification, oppositional
forces and structured magical practice — but the arcane was one college among many, and in its
original form the Epistemium gave equal standing to the study of ethics, logic, law, the nature of
mind and the workings of the natural world.

Then came [[lore-helionscnq|the wars]].
