The **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Republic]]** was established by patrician families who
overthrew the last Vylarian king – a monarchy the record mentions once, to say it was ended.

Governed by a Senate of landed aristocrats, the Republic grew steadily from a regional city-state into
the dominant power of the [[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]]. The era was one of expansion, legal
innovation, and increasingly of internal strife: as Vylarian power grew, so did the ambitions of
individual senators and successful generals.

It matters to the rest of the world for two reasons that have nothing to do with the Senate. Its
[[lore-helionscnq|conquest of Heliónis]] produced the institution that holds the monopoly on arcane
practice across half a continent; and [[lore-thefounding|the year its Republic ended]] is Year 1 of
the calendar most of western Ankaris still counts by.
