The Establishment of the Vylarian Republic came around 650 BF, when patrician families overthrew the last Vylarian king and founded the
[Vylarian Republic]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}. The monarchy they ended is one the record mentions once, to say it was
ended: we know the decade of the king's fall and nothing else about him.

The patricians put government in the hands of the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]], a Senate of landed aristocrats, and the
Republic grew steadily from a regional city-state into the dominant power of the
[[place-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]]. By 400 BF Vylaria held wide territory across [[place-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión Region]] and
was a major economic and political force there. Its courts and its law grew with it, and the legal tradition it
built is still adopted or adapted by courts across the continent. The era was one of expansion, legal innovation and,
increasingly, strife at home: as Vylarian power grew, so did the ambitions of individual _kurthoi_ (senators) and successful
generals.

The war that shaped everything after it was [[lore-helionscnq|The Conquest of Helionis]], which the Republic began in
335 BF and finished in 312 BF. The [[affiliation-ctysttshlns|City-States of Helionis]] fought with their mages, and the legions could not answer
them. The Republic's answer was the [Dragon Riders]{.name data-type="affiliation"}, warriors bonded to dragons near whom magic misfires, and the Riders
broke the great mages where the legions had failed. What the Republic gave the dragons for that arm is in no account
of the war (see [[lore-thebargain|The Bargain]]). In the year the last city fell, the
Senate took the arcane college out of the Helionite [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]] and put it under a magistracy,
the [Kandral]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}, which held the Republic's arcanists on a register.

The Republic's last century was its least orderly. In 75 BF an unchanneled release at
[[lore-recensio|The Therona of Alessa]] killed most of the Republic's senior arcanists, and in 73 BF the survivors
drafted [[lore-kandrol|The Kandrol]], the charter of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]. Around 45 BF
the dragons' standing lease was not renewed (see [[lore-ridersdisb|The Lease Refused]]), the Riders were disbanded and the [Eyrie of Arvel]{.name data-type="place/site"} in
[[place-arvel|Arvel]] was sealed. Civil wars, factional violence and the concentration of power in the
hands of successful commanders carried the Senate toward its end.

It matters to the rest of the world for two reasons that have nothing to do with the Senate. Its
conquest of Helionis produced the institution that holds the monopoly on arcane
practice across half a continent; and the year its Republic ended, [[lore-thefounding|The Founding]], is Year 1 of
the calendar most of the western part of [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]] still counts by.
