> **GM-only historical account.** This inherited record is not knowledge available to ordinary travelers.

**The Senali Meet the Earlier Strangers** records a brief meeting in the [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] account. The wayfarers did not seek the strangers out; they came upon them and were received with courtesy. Their own impression was that they were found quaint. No second meeting was arranged.

Their name for the people they met is _Ennosulkas Vennas_, “the Earlier Strangers.” They do not give the meeting place or identify where the strangers came from.

Senali accounts also say they received works from these strangers. They give neither the circumstances of that receipt nor the works' purpose. The meeting account does not establish that a gift or exchange occurred on that afternoon.

The record identifies neither the teachers remembered on Kalihara nor the makers of any other people's ruins. Those comparisons are outside what the Senali account establishes.
