> **GM-only historical account.** This inherited record is not knowledge available to ordinary travelers.

The Kaliharan record describes the beginning of **The Departure of the Teachers** as a disturbance. Something went through the Ithari—the word the custodians preserve carries no more than that. It does not identify a cause.

In their account, what followed was quick. The teachers went to the caretakers of the instruments and gave them a further set of instructions, hurriedly, beyond the procedures already taught. Then they left, all of them, and the island woke to their absence.

The instructions survive within the practice of [[affiliation-tharivaan|The Living Whole]]. Nothing in the inherited tending marks which procedures were added at the end, and the custodians cannot explain what the additions were for.

This is the one date in the teachers' history the Kaliharans hold precisely, because custodianship is counted from it. The arrival, the raising and the Senali visit are reckoned loosely. The record names no destination or subsequent fate.
