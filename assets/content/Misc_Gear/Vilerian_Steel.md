Vilérian Steel is made in one place: [Thalbezara]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the walled foundry-town of the [Steel Guild]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"} in the Smoke of Orzara's [[place-outskirtsorz|Outskirts]]. The guild calls it _thalbezon_ and never uses the word of the common iron a village smith beats out. It leaves the Steel Town in two forms. Bars a forearm long go to the capital's armorers, who work them into blades and harness under their own names. Finished blades from the guild's own benches go to whoever can pay. The best go to [[affiliation-sorvan|The Imperial Guard]], to the great houses and abroad, as gifts between crowns.

**The marks.** Every piece is struck with two marks: the guild's _thumisa_, the same mark carved over Thalbezara's gate, and the mark of the bench that made it. A buyer reads the marks before he tries the edge. A blade without them is not Vilérian Steel whatever it cuts like. A blade sold with a forged guild mark is broken and its seller sued by the guild for the price of a true blade, and the man who cut the mark is a forger of a registered mark, a capital crime before the Criminal Tribunal.

**What it proves.** A Vilérian Steel blade at a man's hip says that he, or someone who keeps him, has paid a farm's price for it. A party meets it on the bodyguard of a patron, on the officer of a great house, and on the stall in the [[place-gildedqrtr|Gilded Quarter]] that offers one too cheaply.

**Worth.** The listed value is a bar sold to an armorer. A finished sword from the guild's benches costs many times that, and a blade for the highest commissions costs whatever the guild asks.

:::secret {title="The watered blades"}
The watered blades, whose surface shows a pattern like moving water and which bend double and spring back, have not come out of Thalbezara for forty years. The last master of the Bench of the Fire died without an heir, and his third of the guild's secret died with him. For the highest commissions the guild sells blades from the old master's time out of its strongroom, re-hilted and struck with a new year's mark, and thirty-one are left. A party that can read a year's mark against a blade's wear holds the guild's ruin in its hands ([[place-outskirtsorz|Outskirts]]).
:::
