Master craftspeople at [[place-nekiahkul|Ne' Ki'ahkul]] carve obsidian into "mirrors that show a warrior's true face", and the heartland's temples buy them with the blades. The mirrors that matter most are the ones the Master of the Living Flame works against the rising sun, with polished jade, until the tinder catches at the close of each fifty-two-year round ([[lore-itsaniclndr|The Itsani Calendar]]).

The temples hold the mirrors that draw the fire and keep the roster of the runners who carry it ([[affiliation-itzanpnthn|The Itsani Pantheon]]). A mirror taken from a temple's fire-drawing set is a mirror the temple wants back.
