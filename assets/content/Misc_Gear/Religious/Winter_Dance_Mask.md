In the winter dances masked dancers act out the meetings of the first ancestors with the powers of the sea and the forest, and the masks are among the finest carving in the north ([[affiliation-kelwanuk|Kelwanuk]]). In the dark months the Keeper of the Masks of [[place-malkisis|Malkisis]] rules the dance house and orders the village while the summer chiefs sit lower than their commoners.

A mask is not a disguise. It belongs to the dance house, and the Keeper does not lend it or sell it. A party that carries one off carries the dance house's standing with it.
