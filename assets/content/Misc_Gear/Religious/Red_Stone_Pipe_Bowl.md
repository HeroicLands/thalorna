Red stone comes down from the quarry as pipe bowls, carried by delegates and traders under the compact's peace ([[place-watotenlargn|Long Grass]]). The bowl is the symbol of the [[affiliation-redstncmpct|Red Stone Compact]]: five nations send delegates to a summer council at the Red Quarry, where bloodshed is forbidden.

A bowl is the compact in a delegate's hands. The council binds no nation, and what holds the compact together is the oath sworn over the pipe; a bowl without a delegate to carry it is only red stone.
