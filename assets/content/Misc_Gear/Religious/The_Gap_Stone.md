The [[affiliation-lineagekuethori|lineage Kuethori]] keeps the Gap Stone as it keeps the sill: a living held stone (_kaan'sul_) of [[place-stillwatersill|the Still-Water Sill]], gray, giving, the size of a child's hand, and carried sealed in a small lacquered case.

## What It Does

Anyone who sees it carried takes it for a key. It opens nothing. Where the sill has a way in, the liturgy opens it, and a Kuethori carries the stone beside the one who speaks the liturgy. In the hand the stone is warm while the sill is tended; while the sill works badly the warmth comes and goes.

## When Its Work Goes Dark

If the sill stops, the stone goes cold in the hand and stays cold, though it looks and gives as it always has. If the sill goes white, the stone is a dead held stone from that day: bone-white and hard, with none of its give. The lineage Kuethori gives it up to the sanctuary at [[place-mirthaan|Mirthaan]], whose custodians tend the sill with the lineage.
