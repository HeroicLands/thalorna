The pilots' households of [[place-telvari|Telvari]] keep a living held stone (_kaan'sul_) of [[place-telvaristone|the Telvari Stone]], a disc of [[lore-itharisul|ithasul]] the size of a palm, gray and giving. Someone with no right to it has lifted it out of their keeping while the Telvari Stone still works.

## What It Does

In the hand the stone is warm while the Telvari Stone is tended at Telvari, and it stays warm wherever it is carried, in step with the tending. While the Telvari Stone works badly the stone is cooler in the hand than it should be.

## When Its Work Goes Dark

If the Telvari Stone stops, this stone goes cold in the hand and stays cold. If the Telvari Stone goes white, this stone is a dead held stone from that day: bone-white and hard, with none of its give. The households it was taken from have nothing left to give up to the sanctuary at [[place-thalari|Thal'ari]], and the stone stays where it was taken to.
