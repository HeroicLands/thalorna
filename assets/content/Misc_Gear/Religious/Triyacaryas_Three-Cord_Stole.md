Three cords, woven in the colors of [[lore-vyalendradty|Vyalendra]], [[lore-mahajayadty|Mahajaya]] and [[lore-rasikaradty|Rasikara]], plaited together at the shoulders and worn only by a Triyacharya—"Master of the Three," the [[affiliation-trimurtisampradaya|Triyanga-sampradeya]]'s rank difficult enough to attain that most priests specialize in a single form and serve under one instead. An ordinary priest's regalia carries a single cord.

The stole is put on at ordination and worn at every rite that calls for the three forms reconciled: the [Sealing of the Triad]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, the [Reconciliation of the Year]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, and the [Triple Blessing]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} that opens a groundbreaking, a coronation or a [Weighing]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} when a court asks the orthodox hierarchy to set the date in the [[affiliation-ganakashala|Ganoka-shalda]]'s place. A Triyacharya of any great Vedyari city sits the [Council of the Triyacharyas]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} by virtue of wearing it, and no substitute is recognized.
