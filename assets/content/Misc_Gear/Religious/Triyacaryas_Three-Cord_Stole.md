Three cords, woven in the colors of [[affiliation-vyalendra|Vyālendra]], [[affiliation-mahajaya|Mahājaya]] and [[affiliation-rasikara|Rāsikara]], plaited together at the shoulders and worn only by a Triyāchārya—"Master of the Three," the [[affiliation-trimurtisampradaya|Triyanga-sampradāya]]'s rank difficult enough to attain that most priests specialize in a single form and serve under one instead. An ordinary priest's regalia carries a single cord.

The stole is put on at ordination and worn at every rite that calls for the three forms reconciled: the Sealing of the Triad, the Reconciliation of the Year, and the Triple Blessing that opens a groundbreaking, a coronation or a Weighing when a court asks the orthodox hierarchy to set the date in the [[affiliation-ganakashala|Ganaka-shala]]'s place. A Triyāchārya of any great Vedyari city sits the Council of the Triyāchāryas by virtue of wearing it, and no substitute is recognized.
