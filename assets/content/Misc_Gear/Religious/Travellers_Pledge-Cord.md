A plain cord, unremarkable until it is read: a knot for every shrine passed, every pass crossed and every promise made on the strength of the road holding fair. It is [[affiliation-pavanajitras|Pavanajitras]]'s own observance rather than a temple's manufacture, tied by the traveller and not sold blessed, and a Pass-Shrine brahmin will glance at one before granting the summit blessing without ever touching it.

Almost every pilgrim on the road out carries one, and a cord with no knots at all marks a traveller who has not yet left safe country. An old cord, thick with knots from a lifetime of roads, is buried with its owner rather than passed down—the pledges it records were made by the hand that tied them and by nobody else.
