The Corn Pipe is kept by the bundle keeper of [[place-tansheyawa|Tanshe Yawa]], and the autumn trading opens when it is smoked. A pipe binds a promise and makes kin; this one binds the bands and the villages of the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]] to the peace of the trading, and disputes between a band and a village are settled under it ([[lore-tenlayaclt|the Tenlaya culture note]]).

It is not sold and has no price. The village that closes its cache pits against a band under the Corn Pipe does what the Burned Village did ([[place-tansheyawa|Tanshe Yawa]]), and a party that carries it off ends the trading and the peace with it. The Coyote band, the Forks and the One Fire each have a reason to want the trading to open on their terms or not at all.
