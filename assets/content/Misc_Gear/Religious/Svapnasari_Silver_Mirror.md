A disc of polished silver, kept in a monastery's divination chamber and read rather than looked into: a Dorshaka serving the [Mirror Year]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} sits before it for a full year, taking every petitioner who comes and refusing neither a reading nor a fee waived for the poor. The mirror itself is plain, without inlay or inscription—the [[affiliation-svapnasarisameja|Svapnasari-sameja]] holds that a reader's training is the instrument and the silver only the surface it works against.

A monastery keeps one such mirror rather than several, and a Dorshaka's Mirror Year at a particular monastery's glass is spoken of the way another tradition might speak of an ordination. It is never sold; a mirror leaves a monastery only if the monastery itself closes, and even then it is more often buried with the last Svopna-Achorya than passed on.
