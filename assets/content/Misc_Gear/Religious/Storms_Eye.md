The Storm's Eye is a jade disk the size of a palm with the serpent's eye cut through it. The serpent-keepers' branch keeps it in a box with its sealed codices, and at each turning of the moon the sealed codices are inspected before witnesses from two other branches ([[lore-itsaniclndr|The Itsani Calendar]]).

Its worth in jade is small and its worth to the branch is not. A temple branch will pay well to have it back, and a rival branch will pay as well to see it gone.

:::secret {title="What it does"}
Wherever the Eye is carried on worked stone, something follows it. Where it is kept and what comes after it are told at [[place-kulqattzumul|The Red Steps]].
:::
