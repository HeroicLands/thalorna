A sílhari manual is the small leather-bound book every Kaliharan is given at six or seven, in [Dry-Turn]{.name data-type="lore/calendar"}, and fills for life. The keeper's full name heads the first page. The entries are dates and counts: the day a tree flowers, the birds that come home to a roost, the weather at dawn, all written in [[skill-luthrscrpt|Luaithari]].

## What It Does

A manual fixes where its keeper stood at dawn on a given day, because every sighting in it is dated and belongs to one place. Read across years it gives that place as one person saw it every day of a life: the rains, the flowering, the flocks. A worn manual is copied fresh in [Deepwater]{.name data-type="lore/calendar"}, and a full one goes to the household archive. A manual missing from its household's shelf leaves years of one place unrecorded.
