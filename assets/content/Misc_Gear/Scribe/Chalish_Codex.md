The goddess of knowledge and writing is the goddess of the sacred codices of bark-paper ([[affiliation-ixilanchalix|The Faith of Ish'ilan Ch'alish]]). A temple's Keeper of the Codices holds its archive of funerary texts, astronomical records, dream registers and ruin-registers alike, and rules who may consult it ([[affiliation-itzanpnthn|The Itsani Pantheon]]). A codex is made by a scribe who does not begin a page without the opening formula.

A codex is read by priests and copied by priests. A copy outside a temple's walls is stolen or forged, and the second is the more common.
