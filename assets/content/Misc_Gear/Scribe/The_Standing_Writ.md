Every _sekeros_ (inquirer) of [[affiliation-sekran|The Arcane Inquisition]], the Sekran, carries the Standing Writ: a parchment copy of the two clauses of commission that [[lore-kandrol|The Kandrol]] gives the Sekran, naming him as the inquirer who holds them, under the seal of the Sekran's head in [[place-orzara|Orzara]]. It is folded in a case of oiled leather and worn under the coat. A _sekeros_ reads its first clause aloud before he asks a single question at any hearing, to a renegade master and to a village wise-woman alike: "By the Kandrol, to establish what happened at Alessa, and who did it." Then he reads the second, to suppress the practice of arcane lore by anyone outside [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|The Order]].

**What it opens.** Under imperial law, throughout the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], the writ lets its bearer compel testimony, search property, seize arcane materials and detain a suspect. Beyond the empire it is worth what the _sekeros_'s own standing, reputation and hired men make it worth. A magistrate shown the writ is being told that the hearing in front of him is part of an inquiry that opened in 73 BF and has never closed.

**At the table.** A _sekeros_ relieved of the commission gives up his writ with every file in his keeping. A writ in the wrong hands is a license to search any house in the empire for as long as nobody checks the seal, and the Sekran checks. Most _sekeroi_ have read the writ aloud a thousand times and have never asked what the first clause is looking for ([[lore-recensio|The Therona of Alessa]]).
