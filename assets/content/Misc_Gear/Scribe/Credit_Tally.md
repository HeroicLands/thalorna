The caravan peoples who land at [[place-qudumel2|Kudumel]] do not deal with guild paper, and the guilds' enforcers are not welcome in the city. A caravan master who takes the [[affiliation-balinuni|Balinuni]]'s silver for a venture takes it on a credit-tally: a stick of olive wood a forearm long, notched by a Balinuni clerk at the desks of the [[place-caravangate|Caravan Gate]] with one cut for each measure of silver advanced, and split down its length so that each half carries every notch. The clerk keeps one half on the house's racks. The caravan master carries the other on the road, usually tied inside his saddlebag.

**What it proves.** The two halves together are the debt, and neither half alone can be altered without the other showing it. When the caravan comes back over the strait the master lays his half against the house's at the desks, pays what the notches say and the share of the venture besides, and the clerk burns both halves on the spot.

**At the table.** A credit-tally is worth nothing to a thief and everything to the man it binds. A caravan master who owes the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]] owes the Balinuni, who keep the Compact's counting house in the city, and one who will not lay his half on the desk finds the ferries full, the warehouses full and every partner at the Gate informed. A tally whose half has been lost on the road is the start of a dispute the Warden of the Weights hears at the weigh-house door, and a tally whose notches do not match the house's is the start of a worse one.
