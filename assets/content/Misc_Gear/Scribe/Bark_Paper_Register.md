The Warden of the Debt keeps the city's account of blood in bark-paper registers, entered against the celestial events each gift was meant to answer, and announces at the close of each twenty-day month whether the city stands in surplus or deficit ([[lore-chumtsena|The Ch'um Ts'ena]]). The figure is the ceiling on what the city's priests may attempt.

Whoever alters an entry changes the figure, and so changes what the city's priests may attempt.
