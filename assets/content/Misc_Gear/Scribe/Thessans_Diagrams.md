Thessan's diagrams are sheets of measured drawings made by _Marendos_ (master) [Velaro Thessan]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a scholar from beyond the island who watched a failing work of the island from nearer than any outsider has stood. The sheets mark where the work was warm and where it was cool, on which days, and by how much against the scholar's own reckoning.

## What They Show

The diagrams show what the work did on the days he watched it. No sheet states a cause, and none shows how a work is made or mended. A reader who hopes for a method finds a record. Taken to [[affiliation-councilisland|The Council of the Island]], they show how near an outsider has come to a work; taken to a buyer abroad, they show a work failing.
