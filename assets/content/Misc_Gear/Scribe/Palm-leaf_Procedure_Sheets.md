At each of the four [Seasonal Recitations]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} the custodians assigned to a work gather, re-speak its full procedure before witnesses, and write the procedure out again from memory on fresh sheets of palm-leaf. These are the procedure sheets.

## What It Does

Set beside the archived record, a sheet that agrees word for word shows the procedure unchanged. A sheet that differs shows drift, and the difference is corrected against the archive before the custodians disperse. A sheet is only as good as its witnesses: one written outside a Recitation, or in a hand no witness saw write it, carries no standing, and a forged sheet that agrees with the archive says nothing about the procedure it names.
