Khelathi gold, weapons and military advisors were instrumental in the war that freed Taruzel, and the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]] denies the debt in public and honors it in private, through preferential trade agreements and quiet diplomatic coordination with the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]] ([[lore-taruzelwar|The Taruzelian War of Independence]]). A debt that size leaves paper behind it, and the quays call that paper the Khelathi bond. Nobody who talks about it on the quays has seen one.

**At the table.** A Khelathi bond is a rumor before it is an object. A party that hears of one hears that it would prove what both governments deny, and that every house that sat in the rebels' council would pay to see it buried. The same Khelathi lenders reach across the sea to [[place-orzara|Orzara]], where [[being-mthszrtml|Methisi Azeret'Mulu]] buys the debts of the old houses with Khelathi silver.

:::secret {title="The strongbox at the factorage"}
**What the bonds are.** Some are receipts for the war's gold, weapons and naval stores, written in two hands, Khelathi and Taruzeli, and signed by an envoy of the merchant princes. Others are notes on which single houses of [[place-besikar2|Besikar]] borrowed from Khelathi lenders in the first months of the rising, the [[affiliation-qizikuni|Qizikuni]]'s among them. Together they prove that Au'Khelathu paid for a war it has never acknowledged, and that the houses of the Grand Council owe for it. In a Taruzelian house's hands a bond is a secret to bury, in a rival's it is a knife, and in a Vilérian's it is the proof the Senate has always wanted that Au'Khelathu's hand was behind the war.

**Where they lie.** All of them are in the head factor's strongbox at the Khelathi factorage on Besikar's southern quay. [[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]] borrowed through a broker of the [[affiliation-mrchntryvl|Merchantry of the Veil]] when her strongroom ran low, on notes signed in the house's name and entered in none of its books. The head factor has never presented one, and Au'Khelathu wants none presented, because the notes are worth more as a hold than as a payment. Every lower harbor due a Khelathi hull pays at Besikar, and every Khelathi cargo landed first, is interest on them, and every house that owes knows it.

**The copied page.** A young clerk of the factorage has copied a page of the ledger listing the notes by house, and is trying to get it aboard a Khelathi ship. A bond cannot be sold, because no one who could pay it wants it shown; the copy can be, and every house on it would buy it ([[place-besikar2|Besikar]]).
:::
