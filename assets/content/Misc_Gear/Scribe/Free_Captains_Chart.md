Every one of the [[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captains]] keeps her own chart of the island channels. Most begin as a chart bought at the stalls on the Academy stair at [[place-bazunel2|Bazunel]], copied onto oiled linen so that sea water runs off it, and then corrected for years in the captain's own hand: the coves where a hull can lie unseen, the soundings at half tide over the bars, the bearings that bring a ship to a meeting at night, and the marks of the old flotilla for the places where two lanterns at the masthead are answered. No two charts are the same, and a captain's chart is the record of every hiding place she has ever used.

**What it opens.** The islands' back water. A ship with a Free Captain's chart and a master who can read it can go where [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]] galleys hunt her and lie where they never look. The charts stop at the edge of the [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]], where no chart shows the water and the [[affiliation-shoalsbrthd|Shoals Brotherhood]]'s pilots take over.

**At the table.** A chart never goes ashore. A captain who is taken burns hers if she has the time, and a chart that reaches the League is a dozen coves betrayed and every captain who uses them in danger. A party that comes by one holds something the League would pay for, the Free Captains would kill for, and the captain who drew it would trade almost anything to get back.
