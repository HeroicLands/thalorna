Dried and scored palm leaves, strung on a cord through two bored holes and written in the old abjad rather than the reckoners' syllabary, [[skill-vdykshrscrpt|Vedyakshara Script]]. [[place-rajavalilib|The Rajavali Library]] holds the largest single accumulation of them in the [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara Region]]—every chronicle, treaty and legal text of the kingdom-period, plus the continuous archive of every sabhra session since the dissolution—and recopies its holdings on a standing thirty-year cycle before the leaves crack past legibility—all but the kingdom-period originals in the cold vault of [[place-shitakoshtha|Shitakoshta]], which do not rot and have never been recopied.

A single manuscript outside the Library's own holdings is a scholar's possession rather than a merchant's stock; historians from across the region consult the Library directly rather than buying leaves on any open market, and a leaf offered for sale with no clear provenance draws exactly the kind of attention a forger does not want.
