The cord-keeper (K'otselbal) knots a valley's households, harvests, herds and stores into the k'otsel and reads them aloud to the inspector ([[affiliation-ixbaltzaku|Ish'bal Tsa'ku]]; [[affiliation-qapteltzakl|Q'aptel Tsa'kul]]). The Pel'ushmah answers to the Sun Lord for his valley's count and its labor, and the inspector walks a province without warning and counts its storehouses against the cords.

**Read it.** Only the cord-keeper reads his own ledger. A stranger reading it needs the cord-keeper, his convention, or both.

**Forge it.** Knots can be retied. A forged ledger changes who owes tribute, and whoever profits from the change has an interest in its keeper's silence.

**Sell it.** A ledger tells a rival lord what a valley holds. A Pel'ushmah will buy his neighbor's.

**Be hanged for it.** A false count is a false oath to the Sun Lord. The inspector puts a lord out of office whose count is false, and he does more than that to a stranger found with a lord's cords.
