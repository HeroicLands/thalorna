The Speaker for the Island alone treats with a foreign power, and an Envoy goes abroad only on the Council's business, briefed in what to say and, more carefully, in what not to. The Speaker's token is what the Envoy shows. It is a ring of dark carved wood the width of a hand, worn on a cord at the neck.

## What It Does

A foreign court that is shown the token treats its bearer as speaking for the island. The Envoy carries no letter and no seal besides it, and a court that is shown it by anyone else is looking at a forgery or a theft. A token found abroad without its Envoy places the Envoy in that harbor.
