A year-robe keeper paints a picture for each winter on the band's robe and recites its history in order ([[affiliation-tenlaya|Tenlaya]]). Nobody among the Grass People keeps records except on the robes: the year-robe keeper keeps the years, the society songs keep the law, and the council remembers what it decided ([[lore-tenlayaclt|the Tenlaya culture note]]).

The robe's spiral is read from the center outward. Every robe on the plains carries the same picture somewhere in it, a ditch with flames above it, and every band and village can tell what it means. A robe is not for sale. It can be read by anyone who has the keeper's recitation, and without the recitation it is a painted hide.
