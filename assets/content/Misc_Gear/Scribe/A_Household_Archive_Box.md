A household archive box is a lidded box of seasoned wood, long enough to lay a manual flat, in which a household keeps every filled [[miscgear-silharimanual|sílhari manual]] its members have written. The manuals lie in order of date, and the box of an old household holds three centuries of one lineage's looking.

## What It Does

A box gives a place as a lineage has seen it every day for three centuries: the rains of each year, the flowering of each tree, the flocks that came home and the years they did not. A terrace worked by one lineage for thirty generations has that lineage's whole account of it in the box. A household copies its worn manuals in [Deepwater]{.name data-type="lore/calendar"}, and a box with a gap in it shows which year was lost.
