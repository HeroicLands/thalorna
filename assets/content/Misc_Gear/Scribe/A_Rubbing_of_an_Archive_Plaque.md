A rubbing is charcoal drawn over bark-cloth laid on a carved slab or an inscribed plaque, and it takes up every cut of the original. It is the only form in which a slab leaves the archive. The slab is Kaliharan work in the oldest hand the island has, cut long before the [[skill-luthrscrpt|Luaithari Script]] syllabary existed, to set down what the first Kaliharans had been given. [[lore-theithari|The Ithari]] left no writing, and no work bears any, so a rubbing, for all that it is the nearest thing the island has to one, is not an Ithari document.

## What It Does

A rubbing gives a reader the marks without the slab. Most of the procedures the marks record are lost, and no custodian reads them. [[affiliation-silharicircle|The Sílhari Circle]] holds that a reader must be found, abroad if the island cannot supply one, and a rubbing is the form in which a slab can be put before that reader.
