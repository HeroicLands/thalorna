A rubbing is charcoal drawn over bark-cloth laid on a carved slab or an inscribed plaque, and it takes up every cut of the original. It is the only form in which a slab leaves the archive. The slab is Kaliharan work and bears a few of the Ithari's stone marks. The marks are too few to make a script, and each is a sign of meaning rather than text: what it stands for is held in the keepers' memory and recited, and the mark calls it up only for a keeper who knows it. To anyone else a rubbing is a few marks and nothing more, and the oldest are beyond even the keepers. [[lore-theithari|The Ithari]] left no writing, and no work bears any, so a rubbing, for all that it is the nearest thing the island has to one, is not an Ithari document.

## What It Does

A rubbing gives a reader the marks without the slab. Most of what the marks stand for is no longer recited, and no custodian can call it up. [[affiliation-silharicircle|The Sílhari circle]] holds that a reader must be found, abroad if the island cannot supply one, and a rubbing is the form in which a slab can be put before that reader.
