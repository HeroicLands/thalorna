The dole is paid in coin, once a month for life, to every veteran on the roll that the Usos of the Dole keeps in the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]]. The pension token is how a veteran shows he is on that roll. It is a bronze disc the size of a thumbnail, struck on one face with the mark of the rank he held when he left the legion, which sets his rate, and on the other with the mark of his ward's pay-hall and his number on the roll. A veteran wears it on a cord at his neck and is buried without it.

**On dole-day** the veterans of each ward queue at the ward's pay-hall, the largest of them at the Spire Circle in the [[place-warrensorz|Warrens]], with the Watch mustered in full kit. A clerk reads the number on the token against the Dole's book, and the paymaster counts out the coin for that rank. The token is not money and buys nothing. It is the roll's word, carried in a man's hand, that the empire owes him. A token whose number the book does not hold is a disc of bronze, and a man the book holds who has lost his token sits on the petitioners' bench at the Offices until a clerk strikes him another.

**At the table.** A ward's pay-hall strikes a man who fights in the queue off the roll for a month, and every veteran in the Warrens knows it. A token in a dead man's name, drawn on at another ward's pay-hall, is the commonest fraud on the dole. The men who sell such tokens buy them from widows and from the clerks who have not yet struck the dead. A short dole, the day the pay-halls cannot pay every token in full, is the proclamation the whole city fears ([[doc-vylrnctznshp|Vilérian Citizenship]]).

Every veteran-citizen of [[place-orzara|Orzara]] carries one, and so do the veterans of the provincial cities that keep a pay-hall. [[being-sthmrrdln|Sathemar Ardelen]], whom the veterans' stairs call their tribune, has carried his for twenty-six years.
