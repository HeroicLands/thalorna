A captain who sails under the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]] carries its warrant: a sheet of heavy paper under the League's seal from [[affiliation-yamuzel|Yamuzel]], naming the captain, the hull and the owner, and citing the charter the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]] granted the League to protect Taruzelian commerce. It is kept in the master's cabin in a tin case, and a harbormaster's clerk asks to see it before anything else.

**What it opens.** The privileges of a League member: a berth when the harbor is full, a customs house that waves the cargo through, and the right to carry weapons on any quay in Taruzel. At sea it is the difference between a corsair and a pirate. The League reads its charter widely, and a League captain who takes a cargo from a ship trading outside the guilds' agreements does it under the warrant; a captain without one who does the same is hunted by League galleys for the Auric Compact's bounty.

**At the table.** A League warrant has no price, because a captain struck from the League gives hers up and the League sends word to every harbor. A forged or stolen one is worth a great deal until the next League galley reads it. The [[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captains]] carry none, and [[affiliation-thetamzir|the Tamzir]] has refused one. A party aboard a ship whose master shows a warrant is aboard a ship the islands' law protects, for as long as the master's name stays on the League's roll.
