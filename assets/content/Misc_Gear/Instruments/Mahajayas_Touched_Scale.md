A great bronze beam scale, set under the blessing of [[affiliation-mahajaya|Mahajaya]] at [[place-suvarnagiri|Suvarnageri]]'s central temple, where the previous moon's panned gold is weighed in front of the sabhra at every new moon and the figure entered against the [Gold Constitution]{.name data-type="lore/law"}'s shares. A Reckoner of the [[affiliation-ganakashala|Ganoka-shalda]] checks the beam and the weights against the college's own assay constants before the weighing opens, and the whole mechanism the constitution rests on depends on this one scale reading the same true weight every month it is asked to.

There is one, and it has never left the weighing-station. Any Suvarnageri may attend and watch the weights recorded: that public [Weighing]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} is the point, not an incidental to it. The gold is visible, and the scale that makes it visible is not a thing the temple would sell at any price.
