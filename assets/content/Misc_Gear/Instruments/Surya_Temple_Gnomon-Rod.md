A graduated bronze rod, set upright on the forecourt of the [[place-suryatempl|Surva temple]] so that its shadow at dawn falls against the parapet's cut marks. Twelve priests of the [[affiliation-trimurtisampradaya|Triyanga-sampradeya]] tend it through the year, and the sighting it gives is what the temple announces to every court and temple that keeps [[lore-mdhvndrcnt|The Madustana Count]]—one day different from the [[affiliation-ganakashala|Ganoka-shalda]]'s own computed figure, every year, for as long as anyone has checked.

There is exactly one, and it has never been moved from the forecourt it was set in. A court's own Ganoka may carry a graduated board modelled on it, but the rod itself is not an item any trade touches; it belongs to the sighting the way the parapet marks do, and its worth is not a figure any assay has ever been asked to put on it.
