The Warm's Rod is a living held stone (_kaan'sul_) of [[place-sulvaan|the Warm]]: a rod of [[lore-itharisul|ithasul]] a forearm long and a thumb thick, gray and giving like the work it belongs to. The Bearer of the Crisis carries it, and the [lineage Marivoel]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, which holds [[place-klhrmassif|the Massif]], keeps it at [[place-veshvaan|Veshvaan]] from one Bearer to the next.

## What It Does

In the hand the rod is warm while the Warm is tended. The silkan keep it as the one reading of the Warm that can be held in the palm and carried down from the summit. Set against the oldest account of the Warm in the archive at Veshvaan, it reads cooler than that account records; the Bearer of the Crisis holds this and has told nobody. The rod does nothing to the Warm, and no tending given to the rod is tending given to the Warm.

## When Its Work Goes Dark

If the Warm stops, the rod goes cold in the hand and stays cold, looking and giving as it always has. If the Warm goes white, the rod is a dead held stone from that day: bone-white and hard, with none of its give. The lineage Marivoel gives it up to the sanctuary at Veshvaan, where it is kept with the other dead held stones.
