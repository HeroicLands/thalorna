Each Rain-Rod belongs to one of [[place-windstones|the Wind-Stones]] as a living held stone (_kaan'sul_): a rod of [[lore-itharisul|ithasul]] a forearm long, gray and giving, shaped for that stone and handed down in the lineage that walks it with the custodians out of [[place-mirthaan|Mirthaan]].

## What It Does

Held up on the crest while a storm comes in from the sea, the rod warms in the hand for a storm its wind-stone breaks and brings down as rain on the terraces. It stays cold for a storm that comes over the crest whole. The reading holds for the one stone the rod belongs to and for no other, and it comes before the storm does.

## When Its Work Goes Dark

A rod whose wind-stone works badly warms for some storms and not for others, and the pass lineages argue over which reading to trust. If the wind-stone stops, the rod goes cold and stays cold through every storm. If the wind-stone goes white, the rod is a dead held stone from that day: bone-white and hard, with none of its give. The lineage that kept it gives it up to a sanctuary.
