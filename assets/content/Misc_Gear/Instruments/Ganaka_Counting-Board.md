A frame of graduated slate and a wax-faced notation tablet, carried by any Ganaka hired out of the [[affiliation-ganakashala|Ganaka-shala]] to a court that keeps no hall of its own. The slate is ruled for the college's own tables—ephemeris, tide and assay constants copied out from the master boards at [[place-ganakahall|Chandrapur]]—and the wax tablet is where a working calculation is done and rubbed smooth again once it is copied into a court's own record.

It is unreadable to anyone who never learned the notation taught beside the Vedyákshara syllabary, which the college has never troubled itself to fix. A court that dismisses its Ganaka keeps his figures and returns his board; the board itself is worth little to anyone who cannot use it, and a stolen one is more often sold back to the college than fenced.
