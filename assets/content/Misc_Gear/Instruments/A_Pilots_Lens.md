A Pilot's Lens is a disc of [[lore-itharisul|ithasul]] the size of a palm and the thickness of a finger, gray and giving, set in a hardwood rim. It belongs to the current-stone off [[place-vaelukoa|Vaelukoa]] as a living held stone (_kaan'sul_), and the lineage Vaelukoa keeps it with the village's boats.

## What It Does

Held to the eye and turned on a channel, the lens shows the broken water of the approaches as still, whatever the set of the current beneath it. A pilot of the [[affiliation-wardensapproaches|Wardens of the Approaches]] whom the lineage trusts with it holds it to a channel before taking a hull in. While the current-stone works badly the water looks still on some mornings and not on others, and the lens does not say which morning it is.

## When Its Work Goes Dark

If the current-stone stops, the lens goes cold in the hand, and the water through it is as broken as the water without it. If the current-stone goes white, the lens is a dead held stone from that day: bone-white and hard, with none of its give. The lineage Vaelukoa gives it up to the sanctuary at [[place-thalari|Thal'ari]], which forms the custodians who tend the current-stones.
