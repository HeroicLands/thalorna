A small, thick milled gold coin of about 15 grams of fine gold, struck only at Ôrzâra. The obverse carries the profile of the reigning Ôrzâs, renewed at each accession, with older issues remaining legal tender; the reverse carries the imperial _ôrkhaimâ_ over the inscription _Zêl ôrkhaima thâlentôr ûthrimê_. The milled edge is there to make clipping visible.

The Solden is rare in circulation. Most wealth denominated in Soldeni is held as deeded ground, moneylender deposit or paper script rather than as coin, and most commoners never handle one. Producing an Solden at a market stall draws the kind of attention that drawing a blade does.
