The smallest sealed piece the temples issue, bronze at one qelu. Like the copper gezan it passes at its attested face rather than its metal, and the metal is worth a fraction of it.

This is the coin of daily life—a loaf, a cup of beer, a porter's tip, a handful of dates. Most people handle these every day, silver in a good week, and gold never.
