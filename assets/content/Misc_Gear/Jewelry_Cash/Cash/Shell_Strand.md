A strand (_hamil_) of small shell disks, ground smooth, drilled and strung on plant-fiber cord with its knots fixed in tar ([[lore-shellbeadcrncy|Shell-Bead Strands]]). It is long enough to reach once around a grown hand, and on the [[place-shuqhalaqrgn|Bead Coast]] every price is counted in strands: about one for a basket of acorn meal, four for a good bow, twenty for a life.

A strand is full or it is short. A seller winds it around her hand to see the ends meet, and a strand strung short is the commonest cheat on the coast. Strands from [[place-shuqwil|Shuqwil]] fetch a little more, because its counter restrings any short one before it leaves the island.
