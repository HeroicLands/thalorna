A dried bean of the cacao tree, the small money of every heartland market ([[lore-cacaojadecrncy|Cacao and Jade]]). Beans are counted by the twenty: twenty twenties fill a cloth bag, and twenty bags fill a sack of eight thousand. The large beans of [[place-xelemrgn|Shelem]] fetch the most.

A bean is also a cup of _ch'ishal_ waiting to be ground, and it molds after a few wet seasons, so nobody saves beans for long. A bean that feels light in the hand is an emptied husk packed with earth or wax. Passing one is a crime the judges try, at twenty good beans for every false one.
