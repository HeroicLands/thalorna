The smallest sealed piece the temples issue, bronze at one qelu. Like the copper gezan it passes at its attested face rather than its metal, and the metal is worth little more than a twentieth of it. Ten of them make a silver qelu, and any Gár-Hálzi chapter exchanges them for silver at that rate.

This is the coin of daily life—a loaf, a cup of beer, a porter's tip, a handful of dates. Most people handle these every day, silver in a good week, and gold never.
