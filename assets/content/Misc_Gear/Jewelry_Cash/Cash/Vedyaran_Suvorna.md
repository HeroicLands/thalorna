The **suvorna** is gold, struck at [[place-chendrapur2|Chendrapur]] and reckoned at sixteen [[miscgear-chendra|chendra]], a hundred and twenty-eight [[miscgear-tomra|tomra]]. [[place-moonhouse|The Moon House]] mines none of its metal and buys it at [[place-suvargeri|Suvargeri]]'s public [Weighing]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}. [[affiliation-mrchntclctvvdyr|The Merchant Collective]] can therefore trace every suvorna struck to a particular month's weighed extraction. The coin's own face carries nothing of that record, but it carries its gold: abroad a suvorna is assayed and exchanged for its metal, which comes to its face.

It settles land, dowries and a kingdom's tribute rather than a market stall's trade. A trader who produces one for an ordinary purchase draws the kind of attention a produced weapon would, and most Vedyarans outside a court or a great trading house never hold one at all.
