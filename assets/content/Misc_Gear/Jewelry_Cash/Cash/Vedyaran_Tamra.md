The **tāmra** is copper, and every Vedyaran kingdom and city-state strikes its own. It is token money, good at face only within the realm that struck it. At home it pays a tax or a temple offering, and its own treasury redeems it on demand; abroad it is worth its copper, which is next to nothing. It is the coin of bread, of a day-laborer's wage, of a market stall's small change, and most Vedyarans who are not merchants or priests handle nothing else.

Its face carries the mark of the treasury that struck it, a temple mark here and a royal cypher there, and the mark is what a stallholder looks at. The janapadas strike no coin and use the tāmra of their neighbors' markets. A traveler bound for another realm carries silver and buys the local tāmra where he stops.
