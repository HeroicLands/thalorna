A bead of green stone the size of a fingertip, polished and drilled for stringing ([[lore-cacaojadecrncy|Cacao and Jade]]). A common bead of middling color passes for about sixteen hundred cacao beans. A deep, clear green or a bead of [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]] cutting is worth many times that, and a cloudy one less.

Jade passes by weight against the weight-stones the temples keep, and the priesthood's assessors weigh any jade of consequence before they take the levy from it. Green serpentine and stained stone pass as jade to a careless buyer.
