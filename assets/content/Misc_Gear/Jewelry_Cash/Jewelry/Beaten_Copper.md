The greatest gift a Cedar chief gives is a copper (_lukwa_), a beaten sheet shaped like a shield with its own name and history ([[affiliation-kelwanuk|Kelwanuk]]). A copper's worth rises every time it changes hands. It is not carried to a fight and not worn.

A chief who breaks one, cutting off a piece and giving it to a rival, destroys a fortune before witnesses, and the rival must break one of equal worth or lose face. A party holding a copper holds a debt: whoever sold it, gave it or lost it has an account to settle with its owner, and the sheet itself is the receipt.
