The heartland's craftsmen work the copper brought from the Maple People's lake country into plates, gorgets and blades, hammering it cold ([[affiliation-tokchiaska|The One Fire]]). The plates a noble wears are embossed sheets, and the figure on them is the bird-man who strikes down the enemies of the sun, the same figure cut into the plates the Suns are buried with.

A plate marks rank. It is also copper, and copper is traded: [[place-kahnionrgn|the Western Lakes]] sell it to the One Fire for exactly this. A plate taken from a grave is robbery of the dead and the surest way to bring the town's warriors down on a party.
