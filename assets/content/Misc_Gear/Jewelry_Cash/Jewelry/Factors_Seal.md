A factor of one of the five merchant houses of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]] carries his house's seal wherever he trades for it: a stone cut with the house's mark, set in a ring or hung on a cord at the neck, and pressed into wax or lead on every contract, bale and letter he puts the house's name to. The [[affiliation-hitusuni|Hitusuni]] seal their bonded warehouses with it, the [[affiliation-qizikuni|Qizikuni]] their bonds, the [[affiliation-balinuni|Balinuni]] their partnerships at the [[place-caravangate|Caravan Gate]], the [[affiliation-tanaduni|Tanaduni]] their convoy places and the [[affiliation-rusakuni|Rusakuni]] their contracts with the yards.

**What it binds.** A Taruzelian court reads a sealed contract as the house's own act, whoever pressed the seal. That is the seal's whole value and its whole danger. A factor who loses his seal has lost his house a blank promise, and the house sends word to every counting house it deals with before the seal can be used. A factor struck from his house loses his seals with his name, and anything he seals after the striking binds nobody ([[miscgear-strikingwrit|Striking-Writ]]).

**At the table.** A seal is worth nothing on a jeweler's tray and a great deal in the right hands for a few days. A party that comes by one has the time it takes the house's letters to reach the next port, and a party hired to recover one is usually hired by the factor who lost it, before his house learns.
