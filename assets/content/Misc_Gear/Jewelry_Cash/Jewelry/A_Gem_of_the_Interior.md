A gem of the interior is an uncut stone of a kind the island alone yields, rounded by the river that carried it down from [[place-klhrring|The Ring]]. It is one of the colored stones that command prices abroad, and it leaves the island rough.

## What It Does

The stones reach the ports through the factors of [[place-nalurithan|Nalurithan]], whose rivers bring them down, and through the three families of [[place-ilovanu|Ilovanu]], who hold that they took the trade by standing nearer the strait. A stone does not say which river it came down. No cutter in the foreign quarter can tell a stone sold at Nalurithan from one sold at Ilovanu, and each port sells the stone as its own.
