Every _kurthos_ (senator) of [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] wears a signet ring on the first finger of his right hand, and its metal is his estate's, as his livery is: gold for a senator of an _ethlen_ (Gilded) house and silver for one of an _ankren_ (Silver) house ([[affiliation-vylrnmltry|Vilérian Military]]). The bezel is cut with his house's arms exactly as the [[affiliation-imprlhrlds|Imperial College of Heralds]] keeps them on its roll, and a ring whose arms the roll does not show is a forgery however good the gold.

**What it does.** The ring seals a senator's letters, his contracts and his vote when the chamber votes by tablet. It also tells a stranger what kind of man he is dealing with. The civic [[affiliation-verdanorzara|Orzara Courts]] have no authority over the nobility or the Senate, so a watchman who arrests a man wearing one must hand the matter to the imperial tribunals in the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]].

**Worth.** The listed value is a gold ring of a Gilded house; a silver one is worth a tenth of it. No goldsmith in [[place-orzara|Orzara]] buys one at any price, because a senatorial ring on a goldsmith's counter means a dead or ruined _kurthos_, and the heralds know every ring by its arms. A ring that turns up in a pawnshop is evidence, and the house it belongs to pays well to have it back quietly.
