Before a war the Falcon Chief leads the falcon dancers through the plaza in copper-plated headdresses and falcon-feather cloaks, dancing the bird-man ([[affiliation-tokchiaska|The One Fire]]). The headdresses are kept in the falcon house at [[place-hayopokta|Hayo Pokta]], with a copper falcon over its door, and young men of the western towns come to learn the dance there.

The headdresses are kept in the falcon house, and the dancers who hold the right to wear them before a war are the ones the Falcon Chief leads.
