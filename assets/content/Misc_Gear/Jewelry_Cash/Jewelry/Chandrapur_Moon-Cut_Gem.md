Rough stone reaches [[place-chandrapur2|Chandrapur]] from every workable ground in Vedyara and beyond, and a moon-cut gem is what the city's own sequence of trades makes of it: assayed in one street, sawn in another, cut in a third, polished in a fourth and set in a fifth, each stage held by a house that has done nothing else for generations. The temples bless a stone before the cutting begins, and a cutter who skips the blessing does not work in the city again—piety or quality control, the city has never found the question interesting enough to settle.

The finest cutting anywhere in Vedyara is done within Chandrapur's walls and nowhere else; the valley villages behind the city hold only the coarser stages. A moon-cut gem carries the city's reputation with it wherever it is sold, which is most of the reason nobody in four centuries has managed to spoil that reputation.
