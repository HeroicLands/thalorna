The Orzas of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] wears no crown. He wears the diadem, a band of gold over the brow with the imperial _orkaima_ (eagle) at its front, and each reign has its own. A new Orzas is crowned with a diadem made for him, as his profile replaces his predecessor's on the obverse of every new [[miscgear-solden|Solden]], and no Orzas wears another's.

**Who wears it.** The present diadem is [[being-zrmlshlrkls|Zarmelas Helarkales]]'s, worn over hair cut close in the cold rooms of the palace. The old Orzas has adopted nobody, so the next diadem has no head to be made for, and the question of whom [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] recognizes is the question of the empire. The Senate's power grows under a weak Orzas and contracts under a strong one.

**What it proves.** The diadem is the throne made visible, and it is the [[affiliation-imperialcult|Imperial Cult]]'s symbol, above a coal of incense. It proves nothing a man can sell: it has no price, no goldsmith would touch it, and it is never out of the palace except on the Orzas. Its likeness is everywhere else. The wax masks of [[affiliation-varkalenis|House Varkalenis]] each wear the diadem of their own reign in gilt wood, and a family that keeps an Orzas's mask keeps the shape of his diadem with it.
