A few copper bells cross the desert each year in the packs of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]]'s southern traders, with a little cacao and a few macaw feathers, and the Suns prize them above anything made at home and resent the price ([[place-hayopokta|Hayo Pokta]]). They are cast, not hammered: nobody in the north smelts ore, and a cast bell is proof of a southern smith.

One came back by sea in a Shell Chief's canoe, of southern make and not by way of the Turquoise People, and the Great Sun's court would like to know whether the voyage can be made both ways.
