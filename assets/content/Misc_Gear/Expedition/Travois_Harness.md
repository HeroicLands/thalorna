Two lodge poles cross over a dog's shoulders and trail behind it, with a platform of lashed hide between them, and the load rides on the platform. The hunting bands of the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]] follow the herds on foot "with dogs to drag their lodge poles and goods on travois", and in autumn they walk in to [[place-tansheyawa|Tanshe Yawa]] with the dogs dragging travois piled with dried meat and robes.

A dog hauls a load a person would otherwise carry. Nobody rides it, nobody rides anything: there is no riding animal on the continent, and the travois is how a camp moves in a day.
