In winter the whole delta freezes and becomes a road for sleds ([[place-taluqnapiqrgn|Lake Tundra]]). The hunters drag their kayaks out to the ice edge on sleds ([[affiliation-tikupilarit|The Ice-Edge People]]), and the widows and orphans of the coast villages send sleds to a village that has cut a whale ([[place-imlakkagaq|Imlak Kagaq]]).

The kin moving east along the arctic coasts bring dog teams with them. The dogs pull and nobody rides them; the driver walks beside the team or stands on the runners.
