A terrace mattock is a broad blade lashed to a short haft of hardwood. Every terrace household of the interior owns one, and it is the tool for everything a terrace asks of the people who work it: it digs the drain, resets a fallen wall-stone, breaks the crust before the Wet-Turn and clears the channel the rain runs down.

A terrace wall that stands is a wall whose household has kept the mattock in use, and the households hand the tool down with the terrace. At [[place-koathevoi|Koathevoi]] the lowest walls stand under the risen lake, and the mattocks that built them hang unused in the houses above.
