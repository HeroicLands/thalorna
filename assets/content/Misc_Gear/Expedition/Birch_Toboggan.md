A family moves its whole camp in a day on toboggans of split birch, with dogs pulling beside the people ([[place-kawiskakrgn|Kawiskak Region]]; [[lore-kawiskakclt|the Spruce People]]). The load is lashed under a hide cover and the runners curl up in front so the toboggan rides over drifts.

On bare ground or ice it is useless. It is a winter tool, left cached at the trail's end when the thaw comes.
