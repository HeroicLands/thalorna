In winter the [[lore-kawiskakclt|Spruce People]] walk on snowshoes and pull their goods on toboggans ([[place-kawiskakrgn|Kawiskak Region]]). The person at the front of the line works hardest, because each step sinks into soft snow and the snow has to be stamped down for the rest.

Without them a walker founders to the thigh. With them the cold is a matter of pace and of watching the rawhide, which stretches wet and stiffens to a crack when the temperature drops.
