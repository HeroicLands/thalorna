The strongroom of [[affiliation-tarmex|The Tarmeks Orzan]] in the compound of the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]] holds the largest store of Solden bullion in the empire, and it has never been breached in three centuries. It has three locks and three keys. The Imperial _Tarkelos_ holds one, the _holrozimos_ (chief auditor) of the treasury holds the second, and the captain of the imperial guard detail at the palace holds the third. Each key is a bronze bar the length of a hand, its bit cut for one lock only, and each holder wears his on a chain and gives it up only to his successor.

**What it opens.** Nothing alone. The strongroom opens when all three officers stand at its door together, and the rule that no one man may hold two keys is older than any of the men who hold them. A key out of its holder's keeping is a scandal before it is a theft, and the treasury changes the lock it opens.

**At the table.** The keys matter because of what stands behind the door. The bullion backs the empire's paper and cannot be spent without breaking it, so a strongroom that is light is a secret worth more than the gold. The yearly audit falls in the third week of the spring quarter, and on the morning of it the three keys are in one room. A party asked to take a wax of a key is being asked to bet against the credit on which the dole, and the throne's quiet, rest.
