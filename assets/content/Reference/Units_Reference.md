## Definitions

### League

One league is 3 nautical miles. This is the authoritative definition. Three nautical miles is exactly 5,556 meters, at 1,852 meters per nautical mile, which is about 3.45 statute miles or about 5.56 kilometers.

### Watch

A watch is four hours. A day is six watches.

A day of overland travel is at most two traveling watches. Part of each watch goes to rest, and the rest of the day to eating and drinking, making camp and breaking it. The travel rates assume travel alone, with no hunting or foraging.

### Seam

A seam is the unit of a vehicle's carrying capacity. One seam is 20 stone, which is 280 pounds, or 5 cubic feet. The stone is 14 pounds.

Eight seams make one tun, which is exactly one long ton (2,240 pounds) and 40 cubic feet.

## Conversions

| Unit   | Equals                                              |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| League | 3 nautical miles                                    |
| League | about 3.45 statute miles                            |
| League | about 5.56 kilometers (5,556 meters exactly)        |
| Seam   | 20 stone                                            |
| Seam   | 280 pounds                                          |
| Seam   | 5 cubic feet                                        |
| Tun    | 8 seams (40 cubic feet, one long ton, 2,240 pounds) |

Notes give distances in miles or leagues and carrying capacity in seams.
