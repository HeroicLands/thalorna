# Dossier {#dossier}

P'enqal Ne'ok is the Elder of the Patterned Cloaks, one of [[affiliation-takingsocs|The Taking Societies]] of the taking field at [[place-ishtalhun|Ishtal'hun]], and a warrior of the city's own wards. He is broad and gray, and his white cloak has no room left on its border for another captive. He has walked a treaty field as Marshal of the Hunt more often than anyone alive, and after each war month he sits with the other Elders and the Master of Ishtal'hun to hear every claim against a taker.

The Master of Ishtal'hun is the officer of [[affiliation-tsumulob|The Blood-Workers]] on the field, and his order takes the cleanest takers of the societies into the priesthood. This season the Master means to test the best young taker of the season for the order, and P'enqal Ne'ok wants the young man kept in the societies.

**Motivation:** to keep the best takers on the field and out of the priesthood.
