# Dossier {#dossier}

P'enqal Ts'alam K'ushi is the eldest of the Ch'eshmah of [[place-tsamash|Tsa'mash]], the elders of the merchant guild of [[affiliation-ixilankul|Ish'ilankul]] in the greatest market on the plateau. She owns more cacao groves than anyone alive, in the shaded ravines south of the city, and in the harvest months her porters climb the ravine paths with the beans, a hundred to a string. Cacao is money in Tsa'mash, so her groves are her mint.

The four lords of the city's quarters are heads of trading houses and Ch'eshmah of the guild, and they choose the prince who will trouble them least. Every market day she sits in the shade on the judges' platform cut into the side of the market's rock and judges nobody, because the judges all owe her money.

**Motivation:** to be owed by everyone who matters.
