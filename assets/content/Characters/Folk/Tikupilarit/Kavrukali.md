# Dossier {#dossier}

Kavrukali, Walrus Speaks, is the speaker (_kalitiq_) of the men's house of [[place-kavrumiq|Walrus Place]], the greatest men's house on the coast of the [[affiliation-tikupilarit|Ice-Edge People]], with room on its benches for every man and boy of the village and their guests from three others. He is the oldest man of the village and the most listened to on the coast. He opens and closes its councils, keeps the village's memory of names, gifts and feuds, and speaks for it to other villages. He sits under the harpoon of [Piqanmalu]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who ended the wars, which hangs from the roof above the speaker's place and which nobody may touch.

At the Bladder Feast four winters ago, Kavrukali said that the [Taking]{.name data-type="lore/history"} was a gift taken and not answered. Three generations ago a crew of [[place-imlakkagaq|Whale Point]] wintered among the people of the eastern tundra, were fed by them, and in spring killed their hunters over a whale and took their point. The coast holds that every gift must be answered, and Kavrukali said so of the coast's own people. Whale Point has not sent its bladders to Kavrumiq's ice hole since.

He is seventy-eight, bald, with a voice that fills the men's house without effort. A party that comes to Walrus Place as guests at a Messenger Feast sits where he tells it to sit. He listens to strangers' accounts of other peoples' customs with real hunger, because he is looking for one that answers a gift to the dead.

What he wants is the Taking answered before he dies, by Whale Point or by the coast for it.
