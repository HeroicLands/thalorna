# Dossier {#dossier}

Pinaqaklu, Wind Diving, is the shaman of [[place-napamiq|Cliff Place]], the island village of stilt houses on the cliff off the [[place-tikumanikrgn|Ice Coast]], and the coast holds her the strongest shaman alive. She was blown off the ice on a floe as a girl and was gone for nine days, and she has gone beneath more times than anyone can count since. Villages send men across the ice in late winter to see the Asking danced in Napamiq's men's house, where the island's carvers carve the masks to her description and the dancers come down the ladders from the cliff in the dark carrying lamps. Her Tellings are said to empty a men's house of every breach in it by morning.

Last winter the boat-holders of [[place-imlakkagaq|Whale Point]] sent for her. Their leading boat-holder, [[being-tikumalu|Tikumalu]], holds that the whales chose the Whale Point crews when they led them east, and that the [Taking]{.name data-type="lore/history"} was the whales' choice and no breach. He asked her to go beneath to the whales' house and ask them. She went. When the lamps were lit again she said that she would tell what the whales said at a Telling, and only at a Telling held by Whale Point and [[place-kavrumiq|Walrus Place]] together, in one men's house, with both villages on the benches. Neither village has asked the other.

What she wants is Whale Point and Kavrumiq on one set of benches, to hear what the whales said together.
