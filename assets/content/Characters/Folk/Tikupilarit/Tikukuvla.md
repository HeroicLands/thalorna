# Dossier {#dossier}

Tikukuvla, Ice Hunting, is a youth (_tiqlak_) of the men's house of [[place-kavrumiq|Walrus Place]], unmarried, with no kayak of his own, who hunts for his parents and his elders and is the best shot with a bow among the young men of the [[affiliation-tikupilarit|Ice-Edge People]]'s largest village. He is twenty-two and restless.

Since the speaker of his men's house, [[being-kavrukali|Kavrukali]], said at the Bladder Feast that the Taking was a gift taken and not answered, [[place-imlakkagaq|Whale Point]] has not sent its bladders to Kavrumiq's ice hole. Tikukuvla takes that as an insult to his village, and says so on the benches. He has been asking the old men which families still keep a set of the bone and ivory armor of the war years, and how it was laced. Wars between whole peoples have not happened in living memory; the coast's telling of how they ended hangs from the roof above the speaker's place, and Tikukuvla looks at it more than the old men like.

A party that wants a guide on the tundra, or a bow in a fight, finds him eager and good. A party that brings word from Whale Point is questioned hard, and a party that insults Walrus Place in his hearing has a feud on its hands.

What he wants is Whale Point brought to Walrus Place's ice hole with its bladders, whatever it takes to bring it.
