# Dossier {#dossier}

Nalvaqkuvla, Sea Hunting, is a boat-holder (_kalviaqtiq_) of [[place-imlakkagaq|Whale Point]] and one of the hardest whalers of the [[affiliation-tikupilarit|Ice-Edge People]]. He sleeps in the first men's house, whose Asking dances the mask of the boat-holder of the [Taking]{.name data-type="lore/history"}, and he is one of the two boat-holders its leading man, [[being-tikumalu|Tikumalu]], sends east along the northern ice every spring after the whales.

He has wintered twice in the east, with the East People, the coast's kin who followed the whales and stayed. He came home both times with a full boat and stories of a coast of good points and few people, and of the older people of the eastern tundra who hunt at the breathing holes on foot and have neither boats nor dogs. He calls them by the coast's name for them, Those Who Give Way, and he means it.

He is thirty-eight, handsome, generous with his catch and well liked by his crew, and he has said in the men's house that a point the whales give is a point the whales meant to be taken. The speaker of [[place-kavrumiq|Walrus Place]] says the Taking was a gift not answered; Nalvaqkuvla says it was a gift received. A party that wants to go east along the northern ice can ship in his boat, and finds out on the way what he means to do when he gets there.

What he wants is a point of his own in the east, whoever hunts from it now.
