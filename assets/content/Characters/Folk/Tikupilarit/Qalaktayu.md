# Dossier {#dossier}

Qalaktayu, Day Asking, is a woman of [[place-taqvamiq|Weir Place]], on the northern river of the [[place-taluqnapiqrgn|Lake Tundra]], who works the weirs in summer and does more of the village's spring trade than anyone. When the river opens, the forest hunters come down from the spruce country upriver with caribou hides, wolverine and marten fur and rolls of birch bark, and camp across the channel from the village. The trade is done with signs over a fire on the bank: oil and dried fish for hides and fur. The forest hunters do not enter the village, and nobody of the [[affiliation-tikupilarit|Ice-Edge People]] goes up the river past the first spruce.

Qalaktayu has sat at that fire for twelve springs, longer than anyone but the old men, and she has learned more of the forest hunters' signs and words than anyone in the village. She knows the names of three of them, which they gave her and which she has told nobody. She is thirty-three, quick, with a scar across one hand from a weir stake. Her husband thinks she asks too many questions, and the oldest woman of the village, [[being-taluqivi|Taluqivi]], thinks she does not ask enough.

A party coming down the river from the forest is met at the fire by her first. A party going up it needs someone who can make the signs, and she has been waiting years for a reason to go.

What she wants is to go up the river past the first spruce and see where the forest hunters live.
