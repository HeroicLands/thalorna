# Dossier {#dossier}

Tikumalu, Ice Running, is the leading boat-holder of [[place-imlakkagaq|Whale Point]], and a great-grandson of [Imlakaklu]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Whale Diving, the boat-holder of the [Taking]{.name data-type="lore/history"}. Whale Point's crews have followed the whales east along the northern ice every spring for three generations, and many have not come home: they winter in the east and stay. Tikumalu sends two boats east every spring, and the Asking of his men's house dances Imlakaklu's mask.

He holds, with the other boat-holders of Whale Point, that the whales chose the Whale Point crews when they led them east, that the Taking was the whales' choice and no breach of the Rules, and that a people the animals leave has broken the Rules somewhere. The speaker of the men's house of [[place-kavrumiq|Walrus Place]] answers that the camp in the east gave the crew a winter's food and was answered with killing, and Whale Point has not sent its bladders to Kavrumiq's ice hole since the Bladder Feast four winters ago. Last winter Tikumalu sent for the shaman [[being-pinaqaklu|Pinaqaklu]] and asked her to go beneath to the whales' house and ask them. She went, and she has said that she will tell what the whales said only at a Telling held by Whale Point and Kavrumiq together, in one men's house, with both villages on the benches. Neither village has asked the other.

What he wants is the whales' word, said aloud, that his great-grandfather did no wrong.
