# Dossier {#dossier}

Aliqkalvi, Summer Dancing, is the mask maker (_talquntiq_) of [[place-napamiq|Cliff Place]], the village on the cliff of a rock island off the coast of the [[affiliation-tikupilarit|Ice-Edge People]]. The island's shaman, [[being-pinaqaklu|Pinaqaklu]], goes beneath the sea in trance to the houses of the animals and the dead and comes back describing what she saw, and Aliqkalvi carves the masks to that description, in driftwood and ivory, and paints them.

Villages send men across the ice in late winter to see the Asking danced in Napamiq's men's house, where the dancers come down the ladders from the cliff in the dark carrying lamps and wearing her masks. After the Asking the masks are carried to the top of the island and left to the wind. Aliqkalvi carves every mask knowing she will see it danced once.

She is forty, small, with ivory dust in the creases of her hands. Napamiq's carvers also make the toggling harpoon heads the whole coast prefers, and she makes those too, between masks. A party that wants a harpoon head, a needle case or a carved figure can trade with her for it, and a party that has seen the masks of other peoples, and can describe them, is the only kind of visitor she seeks out.

What she wants is to carve one mask so true to what the shaman saw that the wind does not take it.
