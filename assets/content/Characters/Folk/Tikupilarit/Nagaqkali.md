# Dossier {#dossier}

Nagaqkali, Spring Speaks, is a widow (_ukanaq_) of [[place-imlakkagaq|Whale Point]], fed by right from the first shares of every catch in the village, as every widow of the [[affiliation-tikupilarit|Ice-Edge People]] is. Four springs ago she went east along the northern ice with her husband's crew when the leading boat-holder, [[being-tikumalu|Tikumalu]], sent two boats after the whales. They wintered among the East People, the coast's kin who followed the whales and stayed, and her husband drowned in a lead the next spring. She came home the summer after with her two children, in the other boat.

The boat-holders of Whale Point count it a blessing that so many crews stay east, proof that the whales chose them. Nagaqkali has seen the East People's points, and she says that the whales chose nothing: the East People are hungry, quarrelsome and short of women, and the older people of the eastern tundra, Those Who Give Way, keep well away from them. She has said it at the Feast of the Dead, which a widow may, and the first men's house has not forgiven her.

She is thirty-six, sharp-tongued and unafraid of the boat-holders. A party that wants to know what the east is really like should ask her before it ships with anyone. She knows the eastern points, the leads and the camps, and she can be persuaded to go back only for a reason she believes.

What she wants is Whale Point's boats kept home in spring, so that no other wife buries a husband in the east.
