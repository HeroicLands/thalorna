Kavrukuvla is a 41-year-old man who stands 5'5" tall with a medium build. He has tawny skin, leathery complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kavrukuvla, Walrus Hunting, is a provider of [[place-kavrumiq|Walrus Place]], and the [[lore-tikupilaritclt|Ice-Edge People]] hold him up as what a man should be. He was an orphan. His father drowned through the ice when he was four and his mother died two winters later, and he grew up an _ukanaq_, a child without a provider, sleeping at the edge of the men's house and eating what the old women gave him from other men's catches. He made his first kayak frame from driftwood the other boys had left, and his first float from the skin of a seal a widow gave him.

He is past forty now, and his catch feeds eleven houses. The first share of every animal he takes goes to the orphans and widows of the village before his own wife's kin take theirs, and he has done this every year since his first walrus. He takes walrus at the ice edge in spring with a crew of three kayaks, and he goes out in weather when others stay in, because a hungry village is worse than a rough sea. He sits nearest the elders in the men's house and speaks little there, and when he does, the elders stop talking. He has lost two kayak partners to the sea in twenty years, and his wife's family complains that the orphans of the village eat before her sisters do.

What he wants is a village where no child eats last, as he did.
