# Dossier {#dossier}

Taluqivi, Lake Giving Drink, is the oldest woman of [[place-taqvamiq|Weir Place]], on a channel of the northern river of the [[place-taluqnapiqrgn|Lake Tundra]], and the woman the [[affiliation-tikupilarit|Ice-Edge People]] name when they say what a woman should be. For fifty years she has given the drink of fresh water to every seal brought into the village, at the door, as [[lore-akluqun|Going Beneath]] requires, so that the seal goes home and tells the others it was treated well. Every girl of the village has learned from her how to cut a seal so that nothing is wasted and how to sew a seam that does not leak.

Eighteen years ago the fish failed. Taluqivi took charge of every cache in the village and divided them so that every house had the same, and every house came through the winter. The families who were fed that winter come back from all along the river for the Feast of the Dead at Weir Place, to honor her dead, and it is the best attended on the river.

She is seventy-six, nearly blind, and still sews. She sits by the racks in summer and tells the women which fish are cut badly. A party that winters at Weir Place eats from shares she has counted, and a party that needs a parka, a boot or a boat cover sewn to keep out the sea has nobody better to ask.

What she wants is to see the girls she taught give the seals their drink as well as she did, before she goes beneath.
