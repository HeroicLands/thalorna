# Appearance {#appearance}

A Wall-Warden of the Hinterland Towns is a Helionite officer in the colors of the city-state that sent him, with the gate keys at his belt and the dust of the ore road on his cloak.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Officer of a hinterland town. Keeps its wall and gate for the city-state that holds it.**

A Wall-Warden of the Hinterland Towns is the officer whom the city-state holding one of the twelve towns of [[place-khaddaria|Khaddaria]] appoints to command the town's garrison, keep its gate and answer for what leaves it. Every one of the twelve has one: the mining towns of [[place-khasidon|Khasidon]], [[place-ghalirea|Ghalirea]], [[place-nafrithos|Nafrithos]], [[place-tumaron|Tumaron]], [[place-rakiria|Rakiria]], [[place-jalimea|Jalimea]], [[place-sufanos|Sufanos]] and [[place-hadrithon|Hadrithon]], and the caravan towns at the foot of the last ridge, [[place-ussarea|Ussarea]], [[place-mubaria|Mubaria]], [[place-daminea|Daminea]] and [[place-zarakia|Zarakia]].

## Background

**The appointment.** The assembly or the council of the holding city-state names him. At Jalimea the patron families of [[affiliation-kalydria|Kalidria]] send one of their own, usually a younger son.

**The town.** He commands a garrison of levy spearmen, paid spearmen or hired riders, as his town affords, and he keeps the gate. The real business of the town belongs to others: a guild of mine-masters, smelter-masters or grinders in the mining towns, a council of caravan-masters or of the horse fair in the caravan towns, and he shares the town's rule with them.

**Now.** A warden who has served a few years speaks for his town to the assembly that appointed him as often as he speaks for the assembly to the town. A mining town that loses its grain road for one season starves, and a town that cannot send out its ore stops paying its garrison.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is careful, watchful and always short of men.

### Motivation

He wants the gate held, the grain road open and every weight of metal or every toll counted before it leaves.

### Strengths

He knows his road, its grain wagons and the bands that wait on it.

### Weaknesses

His garrison is smaller than his road, and the masters of his town know more of its business than he does.

## Social

### Others

- [[affiliation-thyrenae|Thirenae]], [[affiliation-theradon|Theradon]], [[affiliation-athenikos|Athenikos]], [[affiliation-kalydria|Kalidria]] and [[affiliation-korinthea|Korinthea]]: the city-states that hold the twelve towns and appoint their wardens.
- [[being-crvnmstrstprd|Caravan-Master of the Steppe Road]]: the council member who keeps the market and the wells of a caravan town while the warden keeps its gate.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The late grain.** The grain wagons are a week overdue on the pass road, and the warden wants riders to find them before the town learns how little the granary holds.
2. **The short weight.** A load leaves the gate lighter than the tally the warden signed for it, and the city-state that holds the town will ask him where the rest went.
3. **The two masters.** The town's guild asks the warden to shut the gate to a rival's wagons, and the assembly that appointed him trades with that rival.
4. **The spring bands.** Hill or steppe bands test the road each spring, and the warden must choose which train his few riders escort and which goes alone.
