# Appearance {#appearance}

Lofiria Ghaliros is a 47-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall with a medium build. She has olive skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and green eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lofiria Ghaliros is an arsir, a minor ruler on the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]'s borders. She was once an ally of [[affiliation-zarkedis|House Zarkedis]] and turned against it in its moment of need, hastening its downfall. She views [[being-klstrtsxrchds|Kalestratos Zarkedis]] as the insignificant remnant of a dead house.

## Psyche

Lofiria treats House Zarkedis’s ruin as settled. Its surviving heir’s anger does not persuade her that the name commands the standing it once held.

## Social

[[being-kldrmszrkds|Kaldremas Zarkedis]] remembers an ally whose withdrawal hastened his disgrace. Kalestratos means to restore the house and force Lofiria to reckon with it again.
