# Appearance {#appearance}

A Caravan-Master of the Steppe Road is a Helionite trader in a dust-colored riding coat, with a tally board under her arm and a price for everything a train carries.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Master of caravans at the foot of the last ridge. Sits on her town's caravan council.**

A Caravan-Master of the Steppe Road leads the trains that cross from the Helionite mountains to the steppe and the inland seas, and she sits on the caravan-masters' council of [[place-ussarea|Ussarea]], [[place-mubaria|Mubaria]] or [[place-zarakia|Zarakia]]. The council keeps the wells, the market and the lodging prices, and the town's [[being-wllwrdnhntrlnd|Wall-Warden of the Hinterland Towns]] keeps the gate.

## Background

**The road.** Her trains carry oil, wine, cloth and finished metal out to the steppe and bring wool, horses, hides and salt back. She buys grain off the caravans to sell on by the mule-load to the mining towns in the mountains.

**The council.** The council settles disputes between travelers. At Mubaria it hears the quarrels between Helionite merchants and steppe traders, and both sides bring witnesses.

**Now.** The steppe bands test the road each spring. At Ussarea a train that arrives late is counted as lost on the third day, and the council holds the gates against any train that cannot show where it has been.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is shrewd, patient with a buyer and impatient with a delay.

### Motivation

She wants her trains through on time and the wells kept by the council, not by the warden.

### Strengths

She knows every well on the road, the brokers who speak the steppe tongues and what a train's water costs at each stop.

### Weaknesses

She trusts the warden's accounts no more than he trusts hers.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-wllwrdnhntrlnd|Wall-Warden of the Hinterland Towns]]: the officer who keeps the gate of her town.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The escort.** A train is ready to leave Zarakia, and the hired swords waiting in its taverns are the only escort to be had.
2. **The witness.** A steppe trader brings a quarrel before the council at Mubaria against one of her drivers, and she needs a witness the council will believe.
3. **The third day.** One of her trains is two days late on the steppe road, and at dawn the council counts it as lost.
4. **The salt claim.** Two houses of caravan-masters claim the same stretch of the flats below Ussarea, and she wants the claim settled before it is argued with spears.
