# Dossier {#dossier}

Kitoskapo, Wolf Runs, is the trail-breaker of the Kopiskak, the Beaver band of the [[lore-kawiskakclt|Spruce People]], in the forests of the [[place-kawiskakrgn|Kawiskak Region]], and the forest bands name him when they say what a man should be. Nobody gives orders in a band. The trail-breaker is whoever the band follows: he chooses where it moves, when it moves and where it sets its nets, and it goes with him for as long as his choices feed it. Kitoskapo has led his band through eleven winters and has never lost one of its people to hunger, because he moves early. When the hares begin to fail he takes the band to the fish before anyone is weak, and he does not wait for the caribou he hopes will come.

Two winters ago he gave half his ground for a winter to a band of the [Barren-Ground People]{.name data-type="lore/culture"} that came south through the trees with nothing, and showed their hunters where his beaver lodges were. His band trapped less that winter, said so, and stayed with him. The Barren-Ground People are coming south again, pushed off their land, and each band that meets them decides alone what they are. Some trail-breakers say a band that feeds the hungry stranger never meets the starving spirit. Kitoskapo has done it once and says nothing either way.

He is forty-one, short, very strong, and patient on a trail. A party lost in the forest in winter is lucky to find his band. A party that wants a guide to the treeline, or to the copper trade beyond it, needs a trail-breaker who knows the barrens' people, and he has fed them.

What he wants is every family that winters with him alive at the summer lake.
