# Appearance {#appearance}

Osipisa is a 65-year-old woman who stands 5'1" tall with a medium build. She has tawny skin, weathered complexion, white hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Osipisa, Watches the Lake, is the lake keeper at the [Apowosipa]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the Loon Lake, the northernmost summer lake of the [[place-kawiskakrgn|Kawiskak Region]], a few days below the treeline. She opens the weirs each spring, settles the quarrels of the eight bands who meet there, and holds more of the stories of the [[lore-kawiskakclt|Spruce People]] than anyone alive. Her weirs are the best-kept in the north, and her memory of every band's ground and of every debt between bands keeps the peace at the lake.

Last winter a family of [Barren-Ground People]{.name data-type="lore/culture"} came to the frozen lake starving. A lodge-shaker of another band said they were _mohkisk_, and some of the young men wanted to drive them back onto the barrens. Osipisa stood on the ice in front of them and told the story of the [Loon Lake Killing]{.name data-type="lore/history"} from beginning to end. Then she fed the strangers from her own stores. They are still at the lake, and half the bands that meet there have not forgiven her.

The lodge-shaker of the Loon band, [[being-yahkinisa|Yahkinisa]], has proposed that the strangers be given the hot fat in front of the whole lake at the summer gathering, so that everyone can see whether there is ice in them. Osipisa has refused. The fat is for one of the people whose heart is turning, she says, and giving it to the strangers says that they are _mohkisk_ without a hearing, which is the thing the rule forbids. The strangers have been asked nothing.

What she wants is a hearing for the strangers before anyone at the lake judges them.
