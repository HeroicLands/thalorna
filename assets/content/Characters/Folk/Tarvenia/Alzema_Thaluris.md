# Appearance {#appearance}

Alzema Thaluris is a 61-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of light build. She has fair, clear skin, silver hair worn short and blue eyes, and she wears the pale gray surcoat of the Twinblades with the two-faced head over crossed gold-and-silver blades.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Alzema Thaluris is the Voice of the Twin Gate, the chair of the [[affiliation-twinbldrdr|Twinblade Order]], chosen by the assembled marendoi and serving for life or until resignation. She was the Marendos of the Ólvestran [[place-twingate|Twin Gate]], the mother-house of the order, before her elevation, and she keeps her seat at the gate in [[place-olvestria|Ólvestra]]. The order's whole calling is the keeping of thresholds.

The gate-guards of the city admit and refuse. Where an admission or a refusal is contested, the Twinblade on station confirms it, and no noble's rank overrides that word. The Twin Gate is one of the most consequential thresholds in Aureldia, and its ceremonial cadre attends the coronations, treaty-signings and embassies of the crowns that require a Twinblade's presence.

## Psyche

### Personality

Alzema is calm, exact and terrifying when displeased. She does not raise her voice and does not repeat a refusal.

### Motivation

She wants the gate kept so that nothing passes it that should not. She wants the order's chapter houses at the other great gates kept by people she has trained.

### Strengths

Her word at the gate is final and no crown has overridden it. She trained a generation of Sworn Twinblades and is trusted by each.

### Weaknesses

She is old and her knees are failing her. The order's rule of no exceptions has cost it friends among the barons.

## Social

### Companions

The station commanders of the gate and her ceremonial cadre.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

A baron of the hills who has been refused at the gate and does not intend to forget it, and the Black Flame operatives who try the gate.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-twinbldrdr|Twinblade Order]]**: Alzema is a Senior Twinblade and holds the office of Voice of the Twin Gate.
- **[[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]]**: She is a Volsar of the faith, and the god of oaths and gates is the order's patron.
