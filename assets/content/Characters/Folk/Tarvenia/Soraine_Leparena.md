# Appearance {#appearance}

Soraine Leparena is a 63-year-old woman who stands 5' 4" tall and is of light build. She has swarthy, weathered skin, black hair gone gray and dark brown eyes, and wears undyed highland wool with a pass-stone on a cord.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Soraine Leparena is an elder of Leparrun and holds the post of Water-Warden of the [[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]. She keeps the peace between clans over a shared spring and the order in which each draws on it, and she hears the quarrel over [[place-ibotsain|Ibotsain]]'s water before it goes to the [[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]].

## Psyche

### Personality

Brisk, plain-spoken and impatient with those who argue from memory without a ruling.

### Motivation

She wants the water order kept and no quarrel carried to the meadow that can be ended at the spring.

### Strengths

She knows the springs and the order of each from the pass to the valleys.

### Weaknesses

She can rule only on water and cannot bind a clan that refuses her.

## Social

### Companions

The elders of the clans round the springs.

### Patrons

None; the post is held by consent of the clans.

### Enemies

Any clan that draws out of turn and calls it an older order.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]**: Soraine is an Elder and holds the office of Water-Warden.
- **[[affiliation-eskrthsprts|The Spirits of Eskarath]]**: She keeps the shrines at the springs.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The ruling.** Soraine asks a party to carry her account of the water order to a clan that has not heard it.
2. **The turn.** A clan draws out of turn, and Soraine asks a party to watch the spring at dawn.
