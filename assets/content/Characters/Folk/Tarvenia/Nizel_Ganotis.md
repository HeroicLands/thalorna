# Appearance {#appearance}

Nizel Ganotis is a 48-year-old man who stands 6'3" tall with a medium build. He has olive skin, sun-kissed complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nizel Ganotis is a lord of the hills above [[place-thorgila|Thórgila]], a knight who holds his land by mounted service and calls himself Lord Nizel. He is accused of seizing land unlawfully and oppressing his peasantry, and his neighbors say he has never let the kingdom's laws stand in his way.

He sees the investigation of [[being-frstrndrvs|Father Ostaran Dorvás]] as a direct threat to his power and would use bribery, manipulation and even violence to stop it. His riders come to the autumn fair asking where the priest keeps his papers.

## Psyche

### Personality

Nizel is bluff, generous in public and cold in private. He does not raise his voice and does not forget who laughed.

### Motivation

He wants the inquiry buried and his land kept. He believes the Great Temple's seal will stop at the court.

### Strengths

He has friends at court and money to spend. His riders are many and loyal.

### Weaknesses

His neighbors hate him, and the fair is public. A witness who will speak against him before the inquiry is a danger he cannot buy off.

## Social

### Companions

His steward, his riders and Lady Zenotha Ulmis at court.

### Patrons

Zenotha Ulmis.

### Enemies

Ostaran Dorvás and the free men of Thórgila's council.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]**: Nizel stands at the rung of Knight and is bound to the ban.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The lord's riders.** Riders of Nizel Ganotis come to the autumn fair asking where Father Ostaran Dorvás keeps his papers, and they buy horses at prices that buy friends. Nizel asks a party to carry a letter to a magistrate.
2. **The seized land.** A freeman of the council claims a boundary Nizel moved in the night. Nizel offers a party a purse to find witnesses who say the old stone stood where it stands now.
3. **A duel.** Nizel intends to challenge Ostaran to a duel at the fair, and he asks a party to carry the challenge in a way that cannot be refused.
