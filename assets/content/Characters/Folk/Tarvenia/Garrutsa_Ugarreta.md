# Appearance {#appearance}

Garrutsa Ugarreta is a 25-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of light build. He is a lean young man with a runner's legs and a strong voice, black-haired, and he wears the king's colors on a tabard over his riding leathers.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Garrutsa Ugarreta is a herald of the king of [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Asturath]], a sworn man of the household of [[being-alzemozold|Alzemo Zolduregi]] at [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]]. He carries the king's word up the valleys and to other crowns, and a herald's person is protected even among enemies. He is a nephew of [[being-thelvarougar|Thelvaro Ugarreta]], the Warden of the Plateau Gate.

The High King's heralds and the king of Asturath's share one city, and Garrutsa has learned which doors to knock at as the king's man and which to pass.

## Psyche

### Personality

Garrutsa is eager, careful of his words and fond of the road. He carries a message exactly and does not add to it.

### Motivation

He wants to be the herald the king sends when the message matters, and he wants to ride the road when the hearth goes out.

### Strengths

He knows every valley road in the kingdom and every lord's hall on it.

### Weaknesses

He is young, and the older lords test him. His uncle's office makes him a channel for the gate's news, which some at court resent.

## Social

### Companions

The king's household and the gate's men.

### Patrons

King Alzemo Zolduregi, and his uncle, the Warden of the Plateau Gate.

### Enemies

None declared.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]]**: Garrutsa holds the office of Herald, as a Sworn Man.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The message up the valleys.** The king of Asturath sends his herald to the high valleys with a word he may not write down, and the herald wants companions on a road where a lowland lord's men are also riding.
2. **A herald's protection.** Garrutsa carried the king's word to a lord who sent him back without his horse. He wants it returned without his king having to ask.
