# Appearance {#appearance}

Ugarrek Tsoluregi is a 43-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is heavily built. He has olive, weathered skin, black hair and brown eyes, and a nose broken and set a little crooked. He wears a plain wool coat with the mountain house's iron clasp.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ugarrek Tsoluregi is the lord of the castle of [[place-durgalis|Dúrgalis]], a knight of an old hill house who holds the town and the valley below it from the castle on the hill, as a Tarvenan lord governs from a hilltop. He names the steward who speaks for him in the [[affiliation-cncldrgls|Council of Dúrgalis]] and the notary who writes and seals what the assembly agrees. From the castle wall he can watch the square and the home field in front of the castle.

The valley's tale of [[lore-adepthail|the Adept and the Hail]] names neither lord nor steward, and Ugarrek likes it that way. The Valaron house holds a small lordship beside his, on money the pilot [[being-dalenavlrn|Dálena Valaron]]'s ship brings home from the sea, and the two houses share a boundary and a long courtesy.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ugarrek is stolid, fair and slow to anger. He lets the assembly argue until the argument is over, and he does not break his word in front of the stone.

### Motivation

He wants the barley in, the fields counted fairly after the hail and the valley's name for honest dealing kept. He does not want a lowland house to hold the valley's debts.

### Strengths

He knows every farm and every field-boundary in the valley, and the free men trust that he will take nothing from the common matter that the assembly has not given.

### Weaknesses

He has no head for coin, and the valley's debts on the coast frighten him because he cannot read the notes. He leans on his steward more than he admits.

## Social

### Companions

His steward and his notary, both named from the freemen of the valley. The militia leader who took Tórsaro Thalidran into the valley's levy is his man.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

A Valthari house whose ship-owners' council holds the Valaron note, and any bandit band that raids the pass road.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-cncldrgls|Council of Dúrgalis]]**: Ugarrek is a free man of the valley and names its steward and notary.
- **[[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]**: He holds the castle at the rung of Knight.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The note on the ship.** Dálena Valaron's father has borrowed against the family ship to hold the Valaron lordship, and the lender sits on the ship-owners' council of Valthari. Ugarrek asks a party to learn whether the house means to call the note, before it is called against his neighbor.
2. **The day after.** Hail has come out of a clear sky onto a single field, and its owner swore in the square the day before. The assembly wants the lie found and answered before the autumn count, and the owner says the hail fell on the wrong field.
3. **The raiders on the pass road.** Bandits raid the farms below the pass. Ugarrek has more young men in the militia than experienced ones and asks a party to follow the raiders to wherever they winter.
