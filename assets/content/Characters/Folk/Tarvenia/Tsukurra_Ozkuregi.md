# Appearance {#appearance}

Tsukurra Ozkuregi is a 60-year-old woman who stands 5'2" tall and is of light build. She is small and brown and very old, white-haired, and she carries a bag of herbs on a cord round her neck and smells of thyme and smoke.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tsukurra Ozkuregi is the _tsinga_ (wise-woman) of [[place-tsaberis|Tsaberis]], the drove town north of [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]]. She lives below the cave a mile above the north gates where [[affiliation-menutsa|the Mother]] is kept and the shepherds leave cheese on the way up to the summer meadows and on the way down. She sees to the sick of the town and of its flocks, sets bones, delivers children, lifts the evil eye and makes a charm against the dead for a house that wants one, and in winter she tells the Mother's tale.

She asks the sick the Mother's way, in questions answered yes or no, and she does not write. The household she serves pays her. She is a cousin of the hermit [[being-tholmaozku|Tholma Ozkuregi]] of Zurgando.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tsukurra is sharp, warm and unimpressed by rank. She treats a mountain lord's fever and a ewe's foot rot with the same questions.

### Motivation

She wants the town and its flocks well, the cave's offerings left untouched and a girl of the town taught the herbs before she dies.

### Strengths

Every household in Tsaberis owes her a birth, a bone or a life, and the shepherds' council listens to her.

### Weaknesses

She is old and climbs to the cave more slowly every year. The town doctor of Lezkora calls her a peasant superstition.

## Social

### Companions

The shepherd families of the valleys and the women who run the town's holdings.

### Patrons

The households she serves.

### Enemies

Anyone who takes the cheese from the cave mouth.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-menutsa|The Mother]]**: Tsukurra holds the office of Village Wise-Woman, as a Wise-Woman.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]]**: She is a freeholder of the kingdom.
