# Appearance {#appearance}

Thélvara Volkemis is a 57-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall with a heavy build. She has light skin, weathered complexion, brown hair, and green eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Thélvara Volkemis is the priestess-keeper of the temple of [[affiliation-olvenia|Ólvenia]] in [[place-herthema|Herthema]], the town's great house of faith. She considers the work of the shipwright [[being-domthgrvth|Dometh Graveth]] a form of worship and sees to it that the temple's ships are always commissioned from him.

The temple is the place the poor come to [[being-palnsnrcth|Pálina Sánriseth]], and Thélvara has not sided with the priest [[being-gaimelkorvat|Gaimel Korvathandis]] in his agitation for stricter oversight.

## Psyche

### Personality

Thélvara is brisk, plump and earthy. She speaks of the goddess as a cousin.

### Motivation

She wants the temple's ships built by Graveth hands and the temple's poor fed. She wants Gaimel to retire.

### Strengths

She is High Priestess of a Temple and holds the temple's purse. The poor and the shipwrights both love her.

### Weaknesses

She cannot dismiss Gaimel without the council's leave. Dometh's trembling hands threaten her ships.

## Social

### Companions

Dometh Graveth and Pálina Sánriseth.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

Gaimel Korvathandis.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-olvenia|Faith of Ólvenia]]**: Thélvara is the High Priestess of the temple.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The temple's ship.** The temple has commissioned a hull from Dometh Graveth, and Dometh has not begun it. Thélvara asks a party to learn why.
2. **The oversight.** Gaimel Korvathandis wants witnesses to Pálina's work. Thélvara asks a party to find his funder.
3. **The reform faction.** Merchants who want Dometh discredited have asked the temple to withdraw its commission. Thélvara asks a party to carry her refusal.
