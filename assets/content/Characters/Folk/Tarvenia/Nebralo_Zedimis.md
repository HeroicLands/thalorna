# Appearance {#appearance}

Nebralo Zedimis is a 43-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of heavy build. He is broad and black-bearded, with a rider's thighs and a voice that carries across a market without his raising it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nebralo Zedimis is the Bailiff of the district around [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]], one of the sixteen districts of the [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]. He holds the district's court, raises its watch and answers for its dues, and his men keep the count at the gate of the cattle market, where every toll is paid on the bailiff's count. A matter a lord of the manor cannot settle comes to his court, and an appeal from it goes up the hill to the Court of Pleas.

He is a knight of a family that has held the bailiwick twice before him, and he means to hold it until his son can.

## Psyche

### Personality

Nebralo is hearty, shrewd and quick to settle. He would rather end a quarrel on the day than write it down.

### Motivation

He wants the dues paid, the market quiet and no case from his court overturned on appeal.

### Strengths

The drovers and the watch trust his count, and he knows every manor lord in the district by his debts.

### Weaknesses

His judgments are written in haste, and the advocates of Torvaleth make a living finding the holes in them.

## Social

### Companions

The watch at the market gate and the drovers who have used the market for thirty years.

### Patrons

Dorándara Hiltheris, the Master of the Pleas, who hears his appeals and has not overturned one.

### Enemies

The advocates of Torvaleth, and every drover who counted differently.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Nebralo holds the office of Bailiff, as a Knight.
- **[[affiliation-lusinia|Faith of Lusinia]]**: He keeps the farmers' faith of the plain.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Two counts.** A drover and the bailiff's man counted a herd differently, and the drover swears the bailiff's man was paid. Nebralo wants the truth before the drover appeals.
2. **The flood bank.** A village skimped on its stretch of the flood bank, and the snowmelt came over it. The bailiff needs witnesses to who was told to mend it.
