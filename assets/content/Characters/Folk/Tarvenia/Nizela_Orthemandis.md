# Appearance {#appearance}

Nizela Orthemandis is a 53-year-old woman who stands 5'2" tall and is of light build. She is small and white-haired, with a soft voice and hazel eyes that go first to a man's scales and only then to his face.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nizela Orthemandis is the Warden of the Weights of the [[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]], a freeman of the city named by the [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]]. She keeps the standard measures and weights in the weighing room of [[place-charterhall|the Charter Hall]], tests the scales of the markets and fines short weight, false measure and debased coin. The goods are forfeit, and the fine is hers to set.

She has held the post for twenty years and has fined three councilors' houses in that time, each of them once.

## Psyche

### Personality

Nizela is gentle, immovable and amused by anger. She has heard every reason a scale can be wrong.

### Motivation

She wants every measure in Valthari's markets true, because the city's whole trade rests on strangers trusting them.

### Strengths

The standard is in her keeping and the fine is hers, and no house has had one reversed.

### Weaknesses

She is old and has no house behind her. A councilor fined once remembers it at every naming.

## Social

### Companions

The weighers of the Charter Hall and the Master of the Mint.

### Patrons

The Konsezarath, which names her.

### Enemies

Every house she has fined.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]**: Nizela holds the office of Warden of the Weights, as a Freeman of the City.
- **[[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]]**: She keeps the god of oaths, on whom every measure in the weighing room is sworn.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The standard.** One of the bronze standards in the weighing room is lighter than its copy at the mint. The Warden of the Weights wants to know which is wrong and since when.
2. **A councilor's scales.** A councilor's warehouse is selling grain to the city short. Nizela wants proof the council cannot refuse.
