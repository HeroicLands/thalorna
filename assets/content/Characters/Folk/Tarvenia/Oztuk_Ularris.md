# Appearance {#appearance}

Oztuk Ularris is a 54-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. He is white-haired and leathery from forty years at sea, gray-eyed, and the tip of his left little finger went with a net line long ago.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Oztuk Ularris is the Warden of the Strand, the shipmaster who holds the harbor of [[place-durvolka|Durvolka]] for the king of [[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Galvaren]]. He collects the dues on every hull and every basket of fish landed, keeps the bell and the harbor mouth, and hears the quarrels of the waterfront at a bench in the fish market every morning the boats are in. A man whose net is cut or whose catch is stolen goes to that bench, and the warden settles it there with the masters of both boats listening.

The town has no council of its own, so the bench is most of what Durvolka has for a court. Oztuk has sat at it for eleven years. He wants the salt ship outside the bay named, and the curing-house mistresses mean that he shall not find it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Oztuk is gruff, fair and slow. He hears both masters out and decides before they have finished, and he is right often enough that the waterfront lets him.

### Motivation

He wants the harbor's peace and the king's dues, and he wants the Strand to stop treating the crown's salt as a quarrel it can win by smuggling.

### Strengths

Every master on the Strand has stood before his bench, and most of them have left it satisfied.

### Weaknesses

The mistresses of the curing houses say nothing to him, and the herring crews will not inform on their own.

## Social

### Companions

The bellman and the harbor men, and Brazema Betsuris, who has fished beside him.

### Patrons

King Ganoth Ervothandis.

### Enemies

Nobody openly. The mistresses of the Strand would like him slower.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]]**: Oztuk holds the office of Warden of the Strand, as a Shipmaster.
- **[[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pozeras]]**: He keeps the faith of voyages at his household shrine.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The ship outside the bay.** The Warden of the Strand wants the salt ship that anchors outside the bay at night named, and he pays in the harbor's favors.
2. **The cut nets.** Three herring crews have come to his bench with cut nets in a week, and none of them will say who they think did it.
