# Appearance {#appearance}

Thaluro Zenothis is a 58-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall with a heavy build. He has olive skin, ruddy complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Thaluro Zenothis is the Árdavonian archbishop at [[place-polthamis|Polthamis]], the prelate of the [[place-grttmplrdvn|Great Temple of Árdavon]]. He confirms the arbitrations of the faith's traveling priests under seal, and the commissions that send them into cases of noble corruption carry his seal too, with the protection those cases need from the complaints they draw.

He meets [[being-frstrndrvs|Father Ostaran Dorvás]] on the register four times a year. He is the brother of [[being-zedimazenoth|Zédima Zenothis]], Ostaran's mother, a kinship neither of them states. His protection holds only while the work never reaches the royal court.

## Psyche

### Personality

Thaluro is quiet and unreadable. He signs what he has read and says nothing of what he has not.

### Motivation

He wants the faith's priests protected in doing what the faith asks, and he wants his nephew alive. He is not willing to set the temple against the court to do it.

### Strengths

His seal opens doors across Tarvénia. The traveling priests trust him.

### Weaknesses

The seal's protection ends at the court, and Ostaran's inquiry is heading there. He cannot name his kinship without losing the seal's impartiality.

## Social

### Companions

His secretaries and the register's keeper.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

Nizel Ganotis and the court faction that protects him.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]]**: Thaluro is a Volsarnor of the faith and prelate at Polthamis.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The register.** The register of the traveling priests shows a commission the prelate did not sign. Thaluro asks a party to find who sealed it.
2. **A message for the nephew.** The prelate wants Ostaran warned that the inquiry is nearing the court, and he cannot write it. He asks a party to carry a spoken word to Thórgila.
3. **The sister.** Zédima Zenothis has stopped answering his letters. He asks a party to learn whether she is at Lezkora and who she is seeing.
