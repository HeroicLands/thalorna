# Appearance {#appearance}

Oltremo Thelvaris is a 26-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of light build. He is young for the post, thin and brown-haired, and dresses better than anyone else on Ezkoba's harbor wall.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Oltremo Thelvaris is the king's Harbor Reeve at [[place-ezkoba|Ezkoba]], the town in the only break in the black cliffs of [[place-kaurema|Kaurema]]. He is a Durvolka shipmaster's younger son, sent up the coast to hold the harbor for the crown of [[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Galvaren]]: he collects the dues on the catch, keeps the crown's store of oil and salt, and sends the lamp-due to the keepers of the Long Lamp with the oil.

The oil the crown sends has come late and short this year, and the Keeper of the Lamp asks him for it every morning. The town's quarrels go to its own assembly of boat-owners on the harbor wall, which lets him listen.

## Psyche

### Personality

Oltremo is eager, correct and lonely. He writes to Durvolka twice a week and is answered once a month.

### Motivation

He wants to be called back to Durvolka with credit, which means a winter with the lamp lit and the dues counted.

### Strengths

He keeps an honest store and an honest roll, and the boat-owners have begun to trust his count.

### Weaknesses

He is a stranger in the cleft, and Ezkoba's families settle things on the harbor wall before he hears of them.

## Social

### Companions

The boat-owners' assembly, which tolerates him, and the keepers of the lamp.

### Patrons

Oztuk Ularris, the Warden of the Strand, who sent him.

### Enemies

Whoever is lighting the false light south of the town.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]]**: Oltremo holds the office of Harbor Reeve, as a Freeman.
- **[[affiliation-brazemas|Faith of Brazemas]]**: He keeps the storm god, whose priest blesses the fleet and the lamp each spring.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The oil.** The Harbor Reeve needs oil for the Long Lamp, and the only large store on the coast belongs to a Durvolka curing house the crown owes money. He wants someone to carry the bargain he cannot make himself.
2. **The wreck goods.** Goods from a wreck under the cliffs are turning up in the Durvolka market. Oltremo wants to know whose boat brought them in before the Warden of Wrecks asks him.
