# Appearance {#appearance}

Kaldra Lizelis is a 35-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of medium build. She is weathered from the tower top, black-haired, and the back of her left hand carries the shiny scar of a beacon that caught too fast.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kaldra Lizelis is a Towerkeeper of the chain of watchtowers down the [Thalsira]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, holding the tower her family has kept for as long as the chain has faced the coast, on a bluff over the river between [[place-ronetho|Ornetho]] and [[place-bruthena|Bruthena]]. She keeps its beacon ready and its watch kept, takes the light-due in grain from every village in sight of the tower and answers to the Warden of the Towers, [[being-orthemoulmis|Orthemo Ulmis]].

Her tower looks over the reed channels where the marsh smugglers move goods past the bridge, and the lamp in its window can be seen from both.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kaldra is blunt, proud of the tower and impatient with anyone who thinks the light-due is a tax rather than a payment for being warned.

### Motivation

She wants the tower kept in her family, the light-due paid in full and the Warden's good opinion.

### Strengths

She knows the marsh, its channels and its weather, and she can read a light on the shore at night as well as any smuggler.

### Weaknesses

The Warden cannot remove her and she knows it, which makes her careless of his orders. Her family is poorer than the tower makes it look.

## Social

### Companions

The villages of the marsh edge, who pay her the light-due, and the other towerkeeping families of the lower river.

### Patrons

Orthemo Ulmis, Warden of the Towers.

### Enemies

The marsh people who know the channels better than she does.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Kaldra holds the office of Towerkeeper, as Gentry.
- **[[affiliation-brazemas|Faith of Brazemas]]**: She keeps the god of storms at her tower's hearth.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The light-due.** A village in sight of the tower has paid no grain this season and says the beacon was dark the night the marsh flooded. Kaldra wants to know who told them so.
2. **A boat in the reeds.** A boat lies sunk in the channel under her tower with its cargo still in it. Kaldra wants it raised before the Warden's boatmen find it.
