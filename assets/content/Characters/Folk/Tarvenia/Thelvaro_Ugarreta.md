# Appearance {#appearance}

Thelvaro Ugarreta is a 69-year-old man who stands 5' 11" tall and is of heavy build. He has tanned, weathered skin, black hair gone gray and brown eyes, and wears wool and iron with the gate's key-chain at his belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Thelvaro Ugarreta is a mountain lord of Asturath who holds the office of Warden of the Plateau Gate for life. The two-gated tower at the top of the climb to [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] is his, and so are the gate's men. He counts every lord's twelve sworn men through, keeps the plateau road and inspects the cisterns each spring.

## Psyche

### Personality

Slow, unamused and fair to every traveler who waits in the yard.

### Motivation

He wants the plateau held as it has always been held, and the names of the lowland lords' men written down.

### Strengths

He knows every face that has climbed the road in thirty years.

### Weaknesses

He answers to the king of Asturath and cannot refuse a lord of the three kingdoms his twelve.

## Social

### Companions

The gate's men and the captains of the plateau road.

### Patrons

[[being-alzemozold|Alzemo Zolduregi]], whose gate he keeps.

### Enemies

Lowland stewards who try to bring in more than twelve.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]]**: Thelvaro is a Mountain Lord and holds the office of Warden of the Plateau Gate.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The count at the gate.** Thelvaro asks a party to ride the plateau road and name the lords who sent the men coming up in twos and threes.
2. **The cistern.** One of the western cisterns has turned in a dry spring, and Thelvaro asks a party to learn whether the weather did it.
