# Appearance {#appearance}

Ervotha Kanarris is a 55-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of medium build. Her hair is silver and her dress is the plainest in the council square, and she wears the key of the charter room on a silver chain at her wrist.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ervotha Kanarris heads a house of wine and cork factors on the Roll of Great Houses and sits on the [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]], which has named her Chancellor of the [[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]. She keeps the royal charter itself, the city's treaties and its letters in the charter room of [[place-charterhall|the Charter Hall]]. The council has also made her Keeper of its Roll of Great Houses, so she writes a house onto the Roll when the council admits it and strikes off a house that fails.

The charter is bought from each High King in turn, and she is the one councilor who has read every copy of it since the first. The High King is old, and she has begun drafting what the city's councilor says at Lezkora when the terms are sworn.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ervotha is precise, unhurried and cold to flattery. She speaks in the council square only when she has a document to read from.

### Motivation

She wants the charter renewed on its old terms, word for word, whoever is elected.

### Strengths

She knows the charter's history and every promise each High King has made under it, and the Roll gives her a hand on every house's standing.

### Weaknesses

She has no party of her own in the council, and the houses she strikes from the Roll do not forgive it.

## Social

### Companions

The charter room's clerks and the Arbitrators of the Charter Hall.

### Patrons

None; she sits by the weight of her house.

### Enemies

Houses she has struck from the Roll, and councilors who would spend the charter's good name on a candidate.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]**: Ervotha holds the office of Chancellor, as a Councilor.
- **[[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]]**: She keeps its Roll of Great Houses, as Keeper of the Roll.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The first copy.** The charter room's oldest copy of the charter is missing a page. The Chancellor wants it found before any councilor learns it is gone.
2. **A house on the edge.** A great house is about to fail, and its striking from the Roll would void its contracts. Ervotha wants to know who holds its paper before she writes anything.
