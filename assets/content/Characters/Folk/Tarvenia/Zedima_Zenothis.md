# Appearance {#appearance}

Zédima Zenothis is a 62-year-old woman who stands 5'11" tall with a medium build. She has tanned skin, sun-kissed complexion, brown hair, and green eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zédima Zenothis is the mother of [[being-frstrndrvs|Father Ostaran Dorvás]] and the daughter of a well-known scholar. She married [Álvaro Dorvás]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a knight of the royal guard, and lives in [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]], in the house the guard's widows keep below the plateau wall.

She is the sister of Archbishop [[being-thalurozenot|Thaluro Zenothis]], a kinship neither of them states. She hears the news of both courts from the guard.

## Psyche

### Personality

Zédima is dry, quiet and well-informed. She asks about the weather when she wants to know about a death.

### Motivation

She wants her son alive and her brother's seal intact. She wants to leave the inquiry out of the High Hall.

### Strengths

She knows every guardsman's wife in the city. Her scholarly upbringing makes her a reader of letters.

### Weaknesses

She cannot name her brother. She is old and depends on the guard's pension.

## Social

### Companions

The wives of the royal guard.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

Zenotha Ulmis, who spreads rumors against her son.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]**: Zédima stands at the rung of Gentry.
