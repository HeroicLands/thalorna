# Appearance {#appearance}

Zenoth Oltris is a 44-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is heavily built. He has fair, clear skin, dark brown hair cut short and hazel eyes. He wears the red brick-colored cloth of the lowland court, finer than a mountain lord's, with a ring on each hand.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zenoth Oltris is the king of Lyenrik, the largest and richest of the three kingdoms. The house of Oltris has held the crown by descent since the kingdom was founded, and Zenoth keeps his court in the king's hall at [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]], above the ford, where the Court of Pleas sits in the lower chamber and the clerks keep the plea rolls and the Roll of Houses.

He means to stand at the next election. The lowland lords have begun sending their men up the road to [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] one and two at a time, and the king has not said who is paying for the climb. Lyenrik has contested the High Kingship more often than any other kingdom, and Zenoth has no intention of being the king who left the contest to the mountains.

## Psyche

### Personality

Zenoth is smooth, patient and exact about precedence. He remembers who walks before whom, and he is generous in small things so that he may refuse large ones. He prefers a record to an oath and sends for a clerk where a mountain lord would send for a stone.

### Motivation

He wants the High Kingship for Lyenrik and, through it, a free hand on the charter of Valthari and the toll at Bruthena. He believes his kingdom's wealth entitles it to the crown, and he expects the mountain lords to see that once enough of them have been paid.

### Strengths

His host is the largest in Tarvénia and the richest. His clerks hold records that move houses up and down the Roll of Houses, and the lords of the plain depend on him for every claim to precedence.

### Weaknesses

He is slow to gather a muster, because his lords argue precedence before they argue anything else. He is distrusted in the mountains as a king who buys what others swear, and he has been outvoted by lords who could not stand a Liénriki king.

## Social

### Companions

The Master of the Pleas, the Marshal of Lyenrik and the Crown Councilors sit with him in council. His heir learns the law in the Court of Pleas from his sixteenth year.

### Patrons

None; he is a sovereign and owes the High King only his presence at an election and his levy when the ban is called.

### Enemies

Alzemo Zolduregi and the mountain lords of Asturath, who hold the plateau road. Ularrek Garrundeta, whose election stood in the way of a Liénriki king.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Zenoth holds the crown at the rung of Sovereign.
- **[[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]**: He is an elector, with the right to climb to Lezkora with twelve sworn men and name a candidate.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The road up one and two.** The king pays a party to ride to Lezkora as ordinary travelers and learn which lowland lords have gone up, whom they have been seen with, and whether the Warden of the Plateau Gate is turning any of them back.
2. **The channel and the toll.** A Valthari house again asks leave to cut a channel through the salt marsh to bring barges past the toll at Bruthena. The king declines, and a Valthari agent offers a party coin to survey the line anyway.
3. **A house on the Roll.** The Keeper of the Roll of Houses has a claim from a house that wants a higher place, and the claim stands on a record that may not exist. The king would like to know which of his own councilors is behind it.
