# Appearance {#appearance}

Orthemo Tsukurreta is a 38-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall with a medium build. He has light skin, sun-kissed complexion, brown hair, and hazel eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Orthemo Tsukurreta is a merchant captain of [[place-herthema|Herthema]] who sails his own ship with wine and glass to the Provénzian ports. After years of childlessness he and his wife went to [[being-palnsnrcth|Pálina Sánriseth]] for a blessing, and the child was born within the year.

His family's loyalty to her is fierce. Fishing families seek her blessing before long voyages, and Orthemo's name is spoken among them as proof of what she can do.

## Psyche

### Personality

Orthemo is loud, red-faced and openly emotional. He weeps at christenings and laughs at storms.

### Motivation

He wants Pálina safe and his child raised. He wants his ship to bring wine and glass home without loss.

### Strengths

He is a skilled master with a loyal crew. The poor of the town listen to him because he stands up for Pálina.

### Weaknesses

He is credulous about miracles and slow to see danger to himself. His loyalty can be used against him.

## Social

### Companions

His wife, his crew and the fishing families who know the story.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

Gaimel Korvathandis and any man who calls the blessing a trick.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pozeras]]**: Orthemo is a Tramor of the road's faith.
- **[[affiliation-cnclhrthm|Council of Herthema]]**: He is a Freeman of the town.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The witnesses.** Gaimel Korvathandis wants witnesses to what Pálina Sánriseth does among the poor. Orthemo asks a party to stand at her door and send away any stranger who asks questions.
2. **The Provénzian cargo.** Orthemo's next cargo is figured glass from the Provénzian-trained master. He asks a party to ride with it and see that it arrives whole.
3. **A child's naming.** His child's naming day falls in the week of the town's quarrel. Orthemo asks a party to carry an invitation to Pálina's enemies and see that nothing is said.
