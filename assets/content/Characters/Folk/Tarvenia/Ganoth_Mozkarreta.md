# Appearance {#appearance}

Ganoth Mozkarreta is a 47-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. He is a plain, compact man with a soldier's bearing and a clerk's habits, brown-haired and brown-eyed, and he rides with saddlebags full of tallies.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ganoth Mozkarreta is a knight of the household of King [[being-alzemozold|Alzemo Zolduregi]] at [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]], and he holds two of the king's household offices together. As Keeper of the Roll he keeps the kingdom's muster roll, which records what each valley owes the ban in men and arms. As Steward of the King's Valleys he manages the valleys the king holds as a lord, whose rents and flocks are the crown's own revenue.

The two posts make him the man who knows what every valley of Asturath could send to a war and what the king himself can pay for one.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ganoth is methodical, reticent and loyal. He writes down what a mountain lord would sooner remember, and he is not ashamed of it.

### Motivation

He wants the roll true and the king's valleys paying, so that the king never has to ask a lord for what he can pay for himself.

### Strengths

He knows the kingdom's strength in men better than any lord, and the king trusts his tallies.

### Weaknesses

Mountain lords dislike a written roll of what they owe, and a steward's accounts make enemies of tenants.

## Social

### Enemies

## Plot Hooks

1. **The short valley.** A valley sent half its due to the last muster and says the roll is wrong. The Keeper of the Roll wants its men counted where they live.
2. **The king's rents.** The rents of one of the king's valleys have come short two years running, and its tenants blame a lowland buyer of their wool. Ganoth wants the buyer found.
