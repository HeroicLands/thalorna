# Appearance {#appearance}

Tharozo Ulmis is a 45-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall and is of heavy build. He is tall and dark, with a black beard and a gold earring, and he rides the cliff path in all weathers on a gray mare that does not mind the drop.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tharozo Ulmis is the Warden of Wrecks of the [[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]], the knight who claims for the crown what the sea brings ashore from a wreck whose owners are not living to claim it, and who holds the inquest on a body the sea gives back. He keeps his house at [[place-ezkoba|Ezkoba]], because the black cliffs of [[place-kaurema|Kaurema]] take more boats than the rest of the coast together.

A wreck's goods go to the crown only once its crew is counted dead, and the harbor teller of the [[affiliation-drownedroad|Drowned Road]] decides when that is. He and the teller of Ezkoba, [[being-nelmaular|Nelma Ularris]], have quarreled over it for as long as either has held the post.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tharozo is impatient, honest and unloved. He thinks the tellers' year and a day is a custom that feeds wreckers.

### Motivation

He wants the crown's wreck-right kept, the false light found and the wreckers hanged.

### Strengths

He knows the cliff path, the coves and the currents that carry wreckage, and the crown's courts back his inquests.

### Weaknesses

The coast takes the tellers' word over his, and what reaches the shore under Kaurema is often gone before he reaches it.

## Social

### Companions

Two of the crown's men who ride with him, and a Durvolka clerk who keeps his wreck roll.

### Patrons

King Ganoth Ervothandis.

### Enemies

The wreckers of the cliffs, and the harbor tellers who decide when a crew is dead.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]]**: Tharozo holds the office of Warden of Wrecks, as a Knight.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The false light.** A yellow light that is not the Long Lamp has put two boats on the reefs. The Warden of Wrecks pays for the name of whoever lights it.
2. **The year and a day.** A boat lost on Kaurema is a day short of its year, and its cargo lies in a cove. The Warden wants it guarded until the teller calls it in.
