# Appearance {#appearance}

Zurgade Ugarreta is a 33-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall with a heavy build. He has olive skin, weathered complexion, dark brown hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zurgade Ugarreta is the lord of [[place-thornhillmnr|Thornhill Manor]], which he holds of the crown of Lyenrik on the southern edge of the plain, a short ride north of [[place-valthari|Valthari]]. He is a heavy, weathered man in his thirties, a keen hunter and a generous host, who hunts often and with guests, among them greater lords than himself.

He relies on his huntswoman [[being-zoriladmvr|Zórila Dómivar]]. His standing rests on the politics of the Kingdom of Lyenrik, and a change at court that threatens him threatens everyone at Thornhill. The High King has not come down to his hall for a month.

## Psyche

### Personality

Zurgade is loud, generous and anxious. He laughs at his own jokes first and keeps count of his guests' rank.

### Motivation

He wants his name to stand among the guests' and his stag untouched. He wants no change at court.

### Strengths

His hunt is the best in the southern plain, and the greater lords come to it. His tenants are loyal.

### Weaknesses

His guests outrank him, so he cannot ask which of them shot his stag. He leans on Zórila more than a lord should.

## Social

### Companions

His steward, his family and the huntsmen under Zórila.

### Patrons

A greater lord of the plain who hunted as his guest and whom he cannot name.

### Enemies

The old huntsman who wants Zórila replaced.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Zurgade holds the manor as a lord with a fief in his own right, at the rung of Lesser Nobility.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The arrow.** At the last hunt one of Zurgade's guests shot a stag the lord had reserved for himself, and nobody has owned the arrow. Zurgade cannot ask his guests which of them did it, so he asks a party to learn it from Zórila Dómivar.
2. **The kinsman.** A kinsman of Zórila's family asks for a place in the retinue on the strength of her name, and the old huntsman takes him up. Zurgade asks a party to learn what the kinsman wants.
3. **A change at court.** The High King has not come down to the hall in a month, and Zurgade's greater friends are sending men up the road to Lezkora. He asks a party to learn whom they are backing.
