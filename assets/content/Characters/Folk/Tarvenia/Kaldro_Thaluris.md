# Appearance {#appearance}

Kaldro Thaluris is a 50-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of heavy build. He is heavy and genial, his black hair going gray, and he counts on his fingers while he talks.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kaldro Thaluris heads a shipping house on the Roll of Great Houses and sits on the [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]], which has named him Treasurer of the [[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]. He keeps the customs, the tolls and the loans the city raises against them, and every coin of the tribute the city pays the High King for its charter passes through his books.

The seamen's strike has emptied the customs house for a month, and the city is borrowing against a season it has not had.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kaldro is cheerful, persuasive and careful never to be the last man to know anything.

### Motivation

He wants the strike ended on terms that cost the city least, and he wants the year's tribute paid without a new loan.

### Strengths

He knows what every house owes the city and what the city owes every house.

### Weaknesses

His house's ships lie idle like everyone's, and the council suspects him of timing the city's loans to suit them.

## Social

### Companions

The clerks of the customs house and the factors of the moneylending houses.

### Patrons

None; he sits by the weight of his house.

### Enemies

Orthema Ikentsis, the First of the Council, whose house and his compete for the Taruzelian trade.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]**: Kaldro holds the office of Treasurer, as a Councilor.
- **[[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]]**: He sits on the council as a Councilor.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The empty customs house.** The Treasurer wants to know how long the strike can last before the city must borrow against next year's tribute, and he wants to learn it from the seamen, not the owners.
2. **The loan.** A house has offered the city a loan against the customs at a rate too kind to trust. Kaldro wants to know what the house wants in return.
