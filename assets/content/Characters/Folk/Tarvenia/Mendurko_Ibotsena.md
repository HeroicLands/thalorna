# Appearance {#appearance}

Mendurko Ibotsena is a 49-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. He is a straight-backed man with a dark beard, and he speaks Tarveni like a lowlander and Eskari like a herdsman.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Mendurko Ibotsena is an elder of the clan of the spring at [[place-ibotsain|Ibotsain]], a kinsman of [[being-elutsaribot|Elutsar Ibotsena]], and the Envoy of the [[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]]. He carries the council's word to a settled power, the crowns of Tarvénia, the barons below the range or the Wardens across the border, and custom protects him while he carries it.

He has carried the council's word to [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]], to [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]] and across the range to the Áelendan Wardens, and he knows [[being-tergadanmiri|Térgadan Mirindi]] from the last.

## Psyche

### Personality

Mendurko is courteous, curious and careful. He delivers the meadow's words exactly and keeps his own opinion until he is home.

### Motivation

He wants the settled powers to understand that the Biltzarrath cannot bind a clan, so that none of them mistakes a delegate's word for a promise.

### Strengths

He knows the lowland courts and their manners, and he speaks both tongues well.

### Weaknesses

He carries only what the meadow agreed, and the meadow agrees slowly.

## Social

### Companions

Elutsar Ibotsena and the herdsmen of Ibotsain.

### Patrons

None; the clans send him.

### Enemies

Lowland lords who treat an envoy's protection as a courtesy rather than a custom.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]**: Mendurko sits as a Delegate.
- **[[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]]**: He carries the council's word as Envoy.
- **[[affiliation-eskrthsprts|The Spirits of Eskarath]]**: He keeps the shrine at the spring.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A word for Torvaleth.** The Envoy is to carry the meadow's answer about the road to the court of Lyenrik, and he wants companions who can tell him what the court means by its reply.
2. **Across the range.** Mendurko wants the Wardens of the Toravar told that the Biltzarrath did not agree to any road, before their young men act on the stakes.
