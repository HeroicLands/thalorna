# Appearance {#appearance}

Zorgan Otsanena is a 58-year-old man who stands 5' 8" tall and is of medium build. He has swarthy, weathered skin, black hair gone gray and dark brown eyes, and wears undyed highland wool with a wolf's tooth at the throat.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zorgan Otsanena is the elder of the Otsanena, the clan of [[place-gerdoibun|Gerdoibun]], in the valley next to [[place-ibotsain|Ibotsain]]. His clan has drawn from the spring out of its turn three times and says an older order gave it the right. He has not carried the claim to the Water-Warden. His clan owes blood in a feud its elders cannot end, and he took the offering left at the spring's shrine as a sign that the spirit of the place hears him.

## Psyche

### Personality

Proud, wronged and slow to speak.

### Motivation

He wants the older order honored and the blood his clan owes ended.

### Strengths

His clan follows him without question.

### Weaknesses

He cannot prove the older order, and he is open to anyone who pays what his clan owes.

## Social

### Companions

The household heads of the Otsanena.

### Patrons

None; an elder holds by the consent of his clan.

### Enemies

Ibotsain's elders, and the kindred his clan owes blood.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]**: Zorgan is an Elder and speaks for his clan.
- **[[affiliation-eskrthsprts|The Spirits of Eskarath]]**: He keeps the spirits of his valley.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The older order.** Zorgan asks a party to find any elder who remembers the order he claims.
2. **The offering.** He asks a party to tell nobody who took the offering at the shrine.
3. **The blood.** A stranger offers to pay what the Otsanena owe, and Zorgan asks a party whom the stranger serves.
