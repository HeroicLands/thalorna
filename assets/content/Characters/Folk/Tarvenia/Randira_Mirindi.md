# Appearance {#appearance}

Rándira Mirindi is a 58-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of light build. She has tawny, weathered and wrinkled skin, white hair worn in two braids and amber eyes. A band of fine blue tattoo circles each wrist, and she sits very still while others talk.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Rándira Mirindi is the head of her hearth at [[place-mirgonlodge|Mirgon Lodge]], the winter lodge of the Mirindi in the western arm of the [[place-toravarrange|Toravar Range]], and the eldest voice of the lodge's Council of Elders. She has spoken for her hearth for thirty years. The council does not vote; it talks until it is agreed, and it waits for Rándira before it is.

She has buried a husband and a son, the son in the autumn of 694, when a baron's loggers came past the boundary cairn and the clan fought his valley through one autumn on the slopes below the grove. She does not want another such autumn, and she says so to the young men who do.

This spring she has held the clan's young men back from the cairn once. Men of a valley of the [[place-westernmrch|Western March]] have come up the shepherds' way twice since the thaw, past the cairn and onto the high pasture below the grove, and the young men wanted to meet them there with spears. Rándira told them to wait until [[being-tergadanmiri|Térgadan Mirindi]] has spoken with the barons. She has not promised to tell them so a second time.

## Psyche

### Personality

Rándira is patient, sharp and plain. She lets a speaker finish and then asks the one question he hoped to avoid.

### Motivation

She wants the grove whole, the herds up on the summer pasture by the right day and no son of the lodge dead on the slopes this year.

### Strengths

She knows the clan's ground, its law and its quarrels with the valleys for three generations back, and the young men will wait once on her word.

### Weaknesses

She is an elder and not a chief, and the council does not command the young men. She trusts no baron's promise she did not hear given at the cairn.

## Social

### Companions

The heads of the lodge's other hearths, and Térgadan Mirindi, the clan's Warden, whom she has known since he was a boy.

### Patrons

None; she answers to the Pact and the council.

### Enemies

Any baron of the March who sends men past the cairn.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]]**: Rándira is an Elder of the Mirindi.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The second time.** Rándira asks a party to carry the clan's word to the barons below: that the next men who come past the cairn uncalled will be met there, and that she will not hold the young men back again.
2. **The herder of last autumn.** She wants Kaldro Kanarris's herder brought up to answer at the cairn before the summer move, so that the clan has one quarrel with the valleys instead of two.
3. **The salt.** The lodge buys its salt in the valleys below, and this spring the valleys will not sell until the clan says whether it means to fight. Rándira wants the salt brought up without the question being answered.
