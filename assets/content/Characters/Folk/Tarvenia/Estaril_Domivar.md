# Appearance {#appearance}

Estaril Dómivar is a 39-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. His tanned skin is weathered by years on the frontier road, his brown hair is graying early and his eyes are hazel. His left knee does not bend fully, and he mounts from the right.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Estaril Dómivar is the Warden of the March, the king of Asturath's officer at the fortress of [[place-kavrenath|Kávrenath]], with powers of war and justice along the eastern road and its frontier that no lord holds in the interior. His garrison holds the road, the town gate and a chain of watch-posts up the valley toward the Kervaron passes. He is a knight, not a mountain lord, and he owes the post to the king's trust.

The traffic over the frontier is his work. Some of it is lawful: Vilérian merchants with steel, glass and cloth who have paid their passage-gifts to the Áelendan clans and their toll at the gate. Much of it is not, and the Warden must decide what the kingdom owes the refugees, the deserters and the smugglers before the king has told him.

## Psyche

### Personality

Estaril is deliberate, plain-spoken and tired. He weighs a decision on the road, writes it down at night and does not argue it afterward. He is kind to those under his command in small ways and does not say so.

### Motivation

He wants the frontier quiet enough that the king's orders arrive in time to be useful. Where they do not, he wants to be the one who answered for what he did rather than the one who waited.

### Strengths

He knows every watch-post, every valley path and every clan chief on the road to the Kervaron. His garrison trusts him because he has never ordered a man where he would not go himself.

### Weaknesses

His orders from Lezkora are weeks old when they arrive, and his rivals among the valley lords use every delay against him. His knee keeps him out of the saddle on long marches.

## Social

### Companions

Társila Thelvaris commands one of his garrison units and has his confidence. The garrison's captains and the town assembly's elders report to him.

### Patrons

The king of Asturath, who named him and who answers the Warden's letters slowly.

### Enemies

The valley lords whose law of their valleys meets the Warden's law of the road, and whose quarrels go to the king. Tholmar Gútharen, the captain of the neighboring sector, who wants the Kávrenath post for himself.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]]**: Estaril holds the office of Warden of the March at the rung of Knight.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The deserters' price.** A band of imperial deserters has come over the passes with good armor and a captain, and they want to sign on with a valley company that already has one. The Warden asks a party to find out who among them is not what he says.
2. **The refugee road.** Two hundred people are camped below the town gate, and the Warden's orders to send them back over the Kervaron are a month old. He asks a party to carry his letter to Lezkora and bring back an answer he can use.
3. **A toll unpaid.** A Vilérian merchant swears he paid his gift to the clans and his toll at the gate. The clans say he paid neither, and the Warden's clerk holds a receipt. Estaril wants the receipt's maker found.
