# Appearance {#appearance}

Volkema Ganotis is a 46-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of medium build. She is short and solid, her black hair going gray, and she wears a set of brass test weights on a cord at her belt through the three weeks of the fair.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Volkema Ganotis is the Master of the Fair, the officer of the harvest fair at [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]], the greatest fair in Tarvénia. For the three weeks of the fair she keeps its peace and its measures, takes its tolls and hears its quarrels on the day they arise, so that a merchant cheated at noon has an answer before dark. She is a gentlewoman of a family of the plain, and she has kept the fair for nine years.

At the last harvest fair she found short weight on the tallow of the merchant-lord [[being-bedarrekmozk|Bedarrek Mozkarreta]] and fined him for it, and he says the scales were changed in the night.

## Psyche

### Personality

Volkema is brisk, sharp-eyed and impossible to flatter. She believes a fair runs on the trust of strangers, and that every short measure spends some of it.

### Motivation

She wants the fair's name kept: a place where a man three weeks from home is not cheated, or is answered the same day if he is.

### Strengths

She knows the measures of every kingdom that sends merchants to the fair and the tricks of every trade that sells there.

### Weaknesses

Her court sits only while the fair lasts. A quarrel that outlives it goes to the bailiff, and the great merchants know how to make a quarrel outlive it.

## Social

### Companions

The fair's weighers and its watch, and the priests of Lusinia at the ford.

### Patrons

The king, who names the Master of the Fair.

### Enemies

Bedarrek Mozkarreta, who means to have her fine reversed.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Volkema holds the office of Master of the Fair, as Gentry.
- **[[affiliation-lusinia|Faith of Lusinia]]**: She keeps the harvest faith whose priests open the fair.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The changed scales.** Bedarrek Mozkarreta swears his scales were changed in the night. The Master of the Fair wants to know whether he is lying before he takes it to the Court of Pleas.
2. **A stranger's measure.** A merchant from across the sea is buying grain with a measure Volkema has never seen. She wants to know whose it is before the fair closes.
