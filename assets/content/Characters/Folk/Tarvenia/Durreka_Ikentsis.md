# Appearance {#appearance}

Dúrreka Ikentsis is a 39-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall with a medium build. She has olive skin, sun-kissed complexion, black hair, and amber eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Dúrreka Ikentsis is an ambitious trader of [[place-valthari|Valthari]], styled Lady Merchant, who belongs to the trading house of Ikentsis that ships wine and oil to the Taruzelian ports. She holds a Master's standing in her own trade and not yet a seat among the House Heads.

She regards the strict interpretation of contract law by [[being-hlrksbrnthr|Hálrikos Bránthira]] as an impediment, and she has tried to have him removed. The Charter Hall's arbitrators settle disputes between houses under the commercial custom, and she believes the custom is being read against her.

## Psyche

### Personality

Dúrreka is quick, handsome and impatient with delay. She treats a clause as a door that may be opened with a purse or a smile.

### Motivation

She wants a seat among the House Heads, and she wants the First of the Council to be someone who trusts her. She believes the old custom of the commercial law holds the city back.

### Strengths

Her factors read the Taruzelian markets faster than any rival. She has the First's attention and many councilors' favors.

### Weaknesses

She is overextended in debt to a house she cannot name. Her haste with a clause has already cost her a case before the arbitrators.

## Social

### Companions

Her factors, her clerks and a Taruzeli partner she rarely names.

### Patrons

A councilor who has bought her notes.

### Enemies

Hálrikos Bránthira, who cites the commercial custom against her, and the houses that trade only along the near coasts.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]**: Dúrreka is a Master of a Trade and seeks the standing of a House Head.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The clause in the contract.** A contract she signed contains a clause that the arbitrators read against her. Dúrreka asks a party to find the clerk who drafted it and learn who paid him.
2. **The marsh survey.** She backs the house that wants the channel through the salt marsh. She asks a party to survey a line, and the marsh people who carry the smugglers' goods do not want it surveyed.
3. **Hálrikos's archive.** Dúrreka offers a party coin to look into the city's archives for any record that would remove Hálrikos Bránthira from his post.
