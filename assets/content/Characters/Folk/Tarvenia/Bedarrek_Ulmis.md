# Appearance {#appearance}

Bedarrek Ulmis is a 43-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of light, wiry build. He has tanned, weathered and leathery skin, black hair going gray and brown eyes. His right palm carries the hard ridge a crook wears into a hand, and he walks the way men walk who have spent their lives on slopes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Bedarrek Ulmis is a shepherd of [[being-tharozozurg|Tharozo Zurgaregi]]'s valley in the [[place-westernmrch|Western March]], and the best-known bough-carrier of the March. For twenty years he has gone down [[place-grumagando|Grumagando]] under the green bough, a fir bough cut fresh and carried in the hand, with the ransoms of the March's families, and come back up it with the men they bought home. The border families of [[place-talothe|the Talote]] know his face, and he has carried their ransoms the other way when a Talote man was held in a March valley. Neither side has ever stopped him, because each side counts the men the bough brings home.

At the thaw the bough-carriers go down before the hosts, to bring home the men held through the winter. This year Bedarrek went down with the silver for a boy of [[being-kaldrokanar|Kaldro Kanarris]]'s valley, taken in an autumn raid and held by a border family below the pass. He should have been back in six days. It has been longer, and nobody on the Tarvenan side knows whether he reached [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]].

:::secret {title="Where Bedarrek is"}
His baron asked him to count the remounts in the muster's yard at Durvalis while he waited for the boy, and Bedarrek agreed, because his own lord asked it and his valley had wintered hungry. He cut the count as notches in the stick of his bough. The herald who receives the bough-carriers at the gate, [[being-arislkrsvr|Arisla Korisvar]], saw the notches when he laid the bough down to be counted out. She took him and the ransom silver into the keeping of the [Winter Assize]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} rather than give him to the marshal's officers, and the Assize has his case before it. It rises when the snow clears.

Bedarrek is ashamed and frightened, and he has said nothing about who asked him. He has carried the bough for twenty years and knows what he has done to it.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Bedarrek is patient, dry and slow to speak, with a shepherd's habit of counting everything he looks at.

### Motivation

He wants the men he carries ransoms for brought home, and he wants his own valley to eat through the winter. Until this spring he never had to choose between them.

### Strengths

He knows the pass in every weather, he speaks the Talote's speech well enough to bargain in it, and men on both sides trust the bough in his hand.

### Weaknesses

He does what his baron asks. He cannot lie to a face that trusts him, and he will not try.

## Social

### Companions

The shepherds of his valley, and the families on both sides of the pass whose men he has brought home.

### Patrons

Tharozo Zurgaregi, his baron.

### Enemies

None that he knows of.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]**: Bedarrek is a Freeman of the March.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The bough that did not come back.** The mother of the boy whose ransom Bedarrek carried asks a party to go down the pass and find out whether he reached Durvalis.
2. **The bough's carrier.** The families of the March need another carrier to go down with the spring's ransoms, and nobody will go until they know what happened to Bedarrek.
3. **The borrowed silver.** The boy's family raised the ransom against the summer's wool, and the man who lent it wants to know whether his silver is in the Talote or on the road.
