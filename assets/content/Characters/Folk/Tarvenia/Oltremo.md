# Appearance {#appearance}

Oltremo is a 51-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build, lean and long in the leg from a life in the saddle. He has tanned, weathered skin, black hair gone gray and cropped short, and dark brown eyes that go to a horse's legs before they go to its rider. He dresses in a herdsman's leather and undyed wool, keeps his voice low around animals and men alike, and smells of the stable.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Oltremo breeds horses on the high pastures of one of the hill-baronies around [[place-olvestria|Ólvestra]], and he pays the baron his grazing dues in colts. He sells remounts to the hill lords and to the Tarvenan companies that hire in the city, and he brings his strings down to the horse fair outside the walls in spring and in autumn. He goes by his given name alone, and the hill-folk ask no more of a man who pays his dues and whose horses are sound.

His trade takes him from one barony to the next and down to the fair, and he knows the hill roads, the lords' stables and the captains who buy from him. The companies that sign on at Ólvestra pay well for a horse that has been ridden on mountain stone.

## Psyche

### Personality

Oltremo is quiet, patient and exacting. He prices a horse once and does not haggle, and he will not sell a captain an animal he thinks the captain will ruin.

### Motivation

He wants good prices for his remounts, his grazing kept and his herdsmen paid.

### Strengths

He knows horses as well as any man in the hills, and he knows every barony's roads and lords. The barons buy from him and trust his word on a horse.

### Weaknesses

He was paid for his last string in notes cashed against the moneylending house the Tarmeks have suspended, and he cannot redeem them.

## Social

### Companions

His herdsmen, young men of the hills who ride his strings down to the fair.

### Patrons

The baron whose high pastures he grazes, who takes his dues in colts.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]**: Oltremo is a Freeman.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The frozen notes.** Oltremo's last string of remounts was paid for in notes cashed against the suspended Ólvestran house ([[affiliation-tarmex|the Tarmeks]]). He wants a party to find the captain who paid him before the company marches, and to bring back either good coin or the horses.
2. **The string on the hill road.** A brother of the Sealed Word is bringing a baron of the hills to a hearing, and his riders stop every string on the hill roads. Oltremo hires a party to bring his remounts down to the autumn fair without losing a week to the riders' questions.

:::secret {title="The commander"}
Oltremo is Elder **Oltremo of the Sealed Forge**, the Elder of the Ash who holds the office of Cell Commander and commands [[affiliation-olvstrncell|the Ólvestran Cell]] of [[affiliation-ordershnvw|the Order of the Ashen Vow]]. Oltremo is the name he took at his initiation, and his birth name is no longer used. He answers to the Council of the Ashen Vow and to the Voice of the Ash, and as an Elder he knows who the Voice is. He bears the order's brand, the small open flame on the inside of the right forearm, and keeps his right sleeve laced to the wrist in all weathers.

The horse trade is the cell's road. It takes him to every barony that shelters the Black Flame and lets him meet its lords in their own yards. A candidate goes to the chapter sites in the highlands as one of his herdsmen, riding a string up the drove roads, and comes back a Sworn Brother. A brother's message travels in a colt's halter. The barons who shelter the cell know who he is; the hill-folk do not.

He never passes the Twin Gate. The Twinblades refuse Black Flame operatives at every threshold they guard, so he sells at the fair outside the walls and sends his herdsmen into the city on his errands.

He came to the order as many brothers do. As a young man he served abroad in a Tarvenan company and came home with a grievance against a priest of [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] that no court of the faith would hear. A brother of the cell sponsored him. He hunts the Forge's corruption first, as the order does, and he holds the barons' protection above every other aim, because a writ that reaches one of those barons reaches the cell.

**Hooks for the GM:**

- **The captured brother.** The Tarvénian crown's investigators hold a Sworn Brother of the cell alive. By his vow he must silence himself before he talks, and Oltremo hires a party, as a horse-breeder whose herdsman has been taken, to learn where he is held.
- **The hearing.** The baron the Sealed Word is bringing to a hearing is one of the cell's protectors. Oltremo wants the names of the witnesses before the hearing sits.
