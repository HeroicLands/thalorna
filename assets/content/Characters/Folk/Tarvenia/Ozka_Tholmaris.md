# Appearance {#appearance}

Ozka Tholmaris is a 38-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. She is hard and brown, her black hair cropped, her nose broken and badly set in a quay brawl she won.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ozka Tholmaris is the Captain of the Watch of the [[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]], a freeman of the city named by the [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]]. She commands the _zórala_, the watch that keeps the streets and the quays, and the militia the chartered trades raise for the walls.

With the seamen refusing to sign on, a mountain company camped in the hiring yards and the quays full of idle men, her watch is the one thing between the strike and a fire in the lagoon.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ozka is calm, hard and fair. She prefers a word on the quay to a charge, and a charge to a riot.

### Motivation

She wants the strike to end without a burned boat or a dead seaman, and she wants nobody to order her watch against the seamen.

### Strengths

Her watch knows the quays and the yards, and the seamen know she has not taken the owners' side.

### Weaknesses

The watch is small for a city of Valthari's size, and the militia of the trades is slow to muster and slower to obey.

## Social

### Companions

The sergeants of the _zórala_ and the masters of the trades who raise the militia.

### Patrons

The Konsezarath, which names her.

### Enemies

Anyone who means to break the strike with hired men.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]**: Ozka holds the office of Captain of the Watch, as a Freeman of the City.
- **[[affiliation-brazemas|Faith of Brazemas]]**: She keeps the storm god of the quays.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Hired men.** A house has hired men from the yards to sail a ship out past the strike. The Captain of the Watch wants to know when, so that her watch is on the quay first.
2. **The fire in the lagoon.** Someone has set a boat alight in the lagoon at night. Ozka wants to know whether it was the seamen or someone who wants it to look like them.
