# Appearance {#appearance}

Elutsar Ibotsena is a 65-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. He has swarthy, weathered and wrinkled skin, white hair worn to the shoulder and hazel eyes. Two front teeth are gone, and he whistles faintly on his s.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Elutsar Ibotsena is an elder of [[place-ibotsain|Ibotsain]], the highland village of about six hundred round the spring in [[place-eskarath|Eskarath]]. The elders of the village's clans settle what the young dispute from their memory of custom and precedent, and Elutsar is the eldest of them, with the longest memory of what the Water-Warden ruled and when. A quarrel over the water that the elders cannot end goes to the [[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]] at the solstice.

Each household holds its own flock and its own people, and its head speaks for them. The spring and the pasture below it are shared with the clans of the neighboring valleys, and the Water-Warden of the Free Territory keeps the order in which each clan draws on them. A shrine to the spring's spirit stands at the water.

## Psyche

### Personality

Elutsar is mild, slow and thorough, and answers a short question with a long account that, in the end, turns out to be exactly the answer. He is gentle with the young and implacable with a forgotten precedent.

### Motivation

He wants the order of the water kept as he was taught it, and the shrine's offerings made. He has no son to take his seat among the elders and is teaching a granddaughter the precedents.

### Strengths

He remembers every ruling of every Water-Warden for sixty years and the name of the household each one touched. Strangers who come with a respectful gift find the way into the Biltzarrath's business through him.

### Weaknesses

He distrusts anyone who writes things down, and he does not hurry a ruling for any reason. His memory fails on recent matters.

## Social

### Companions

The other elders of the clans round the spring, and the household heads who speak for their flocks.

### Patrons

None; an elder holds by consent of the clans.

### Enemies

A neighboring clan that draws from the spring out of its turn and claims an older order.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]**: Elutsar is an Elder, the senior of a lineage whose recollection of custom settles disputes.
- **[[affiliation-eskrthsprts|The Spirits of Eskarath]]**: He is a Highlander and keeps the spring's shrine in the old way.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The water out of turn.** A clan from the next valley has drawn from the spring out of its turn three times and says the old order gave it the right. Elutsar asks a party to carry his account of the ruling to the Water-Warden before the Biltzarrath meets.
2. **The offering at the shrine.** The offering that has gone to the spring's shrine at the solstice has been taken by someone before dawn. Elutsar will say nothing of it to the clans until a party has learned who.
3. **A stranger's gift.** A Tarvenan factor arrives with a gift for the elders and a request that the Biltzarrath let a toll-road cross the pass. Elutsar asks a party to learn whom the factor serves before the council hears him.
