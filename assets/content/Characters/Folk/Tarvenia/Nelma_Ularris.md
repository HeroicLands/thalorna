# Appearance {#appearance}

Nelma Ularris is a 60-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of light build. She has pale, wrinkled skin, white hair worn in a long plait and grey eyes. She reads the roll without spectacles and speaks the names without looking at them.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nelma Ularris is the harbor teller of [[place-ezkoba|Ezkoba]], and the senior teller of the [[affiliation-drownedroad|Drowned Road]] along the whole coast. She lives in the house at the foot of the Long Lamp and keeps two rolls there: the roll of Ezkoba's own drowned, and the roll of the drowned of Kaurema, every boat and every man the cliffs have taken from any harbor. She reads it aloud on the Night of Lanterns from the top of the tower while the lamp burns behind her.

She lost her husband and both her brothers under the cliffs and has kept the roll for twenty-two years. The tellers of the other harbors come to Ezkoba when they disagree, and what the teller of Ezkoba says about a disputed name is what the coast does. She is failing, and she has not named who will keep the rolls after her.

## Psyche

### Personality

Nelma is spare, exact and very gentle. She does not repeat a name wrongly and does not forgive a family that lies to her about how its men were lost.

### Motivation

She wants the roll kept whole after her. She wants the families who lie to her to say the truth before they die, and she wants no more names than the sea takes.

### Strengths

She remembers every boat lost on Kaurema in her lifetime and most before it, and she knows which families have told her false stories at the Night of Lanterns.

### Weaknesses

She is failing, she has no chosen successor, and she will not be hurried into naming one. The two women who want the post are each good enough that choosing one will make an enemy of the other.

## Social

### Companions

The six keepers of the Long Lamp, and the grave-keepers who carry the drowned in when the sea gives them back.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

Wreckers, who reach what a wreck under Kaurema brings ashore before the Warden of Wrecks does.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-drownedroad|The Drowned Road]]**: Nelma is a Teller and holds the office of Teller of Ezkoba.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A false light.** Twice this autumn a boat has come onto the reefs south of the town steering on a yellow light that was not the Long Lamp. The keepers swear the lamp was lit, Nelma has two new names for the roll, and she wants the wrecker found.
2. **The oil fails.** A bad seal year and a crown slow to send the oil have left the lamp with a month's fuel. The only large store on the coast belongs to a Durvolka curing house that the crown owes money, and Nelma asks a party to bring oil north.
3. **The teller's successor.** Two women want the post, one a teller's daughter and one a widow who has learned the rolls by heart, and the tellers of the other harbors are taking sides. Nelma asks a party to learn which of them has been paying for votes.
