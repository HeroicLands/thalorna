# Appearance {#appearance}

Orthema Ikentsis is a 47-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She has smooth olive skin, black hair going gray at the roots, and dark brown eyes. She dresses in dark wool cut in the Taruzelian fashion, with a single silver seal-ring.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Orthema Ikentsis is the First of the Council of the [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]], the council of the great merchant houses that governs the Free City of [[place-valthari|Valthari]] under its royal charter. She presides over its sittings in the open square before the Charter Hall, holds the city's seal for her term and breaks a tie. Her own house ships wine and oil to the Taruzelian ports.

That makes her the council's weakest point. The houses that trade east would pay the toll the admiral at Yamuzel asks for the narrows, and the houses whose ships go no farther than the Provénzian and Galváreni coasts would not, and they say she cannot be trusted to argue the city's case. The council is divided over what to send up the road to Lezkora when the High King's hearth goes out.

## Psyche

### Personality

Orthema is quick, courteous and impossible to hurry. She decides what a vote will be in a counting-house before she calls it in the square, and she is never seen to count.

### Motivation

She wants the charter kept and the city's share of the eastern trade protected, and she wants a High King who will take the tribute and leave the free city alone. Her house's interest and the city's interest are the same, and she knows both are suspected.

### Strengths

She keeps the seal, the votes and the gossip of a dozen houses in her head. Her house's captains know the Taruzelian ports better than any in the city.

### Weaknesses

Her house's trade and her office point the same way, and the houses that disagree use that against her at every vote. She trusts few and is trusted by fewer.

## Social

### Companions

The Keeper of the Roll and the house heads of the eastern trade vote with her. Her factors in Taruzelian ports send her news weeks before the harbor hears it.

### Patrons

None; the First is the council's creature.

### Enemies

The houses that trade only along the Provénzian and Galváreni coasts, and any councilor who has bought a failing house's debts.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]]**: Orthema sits as a Councilor and holds the office of First of the Council.
- **[[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]**: She is a Councilor of the free city and the head of her house.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The road to Lezkora.** Orthema wants a party to ride to Lezkora and send word the day the High King's hearth goes out, before any other house's rider brings it, so that the council can choose its man first.
2. **The narrows toll.** The Taruzelian admiral at Yamuzel presses the Grand Council for a toll on every hull through the narrows. Orthema asks a party to learn how the other houses mean to vote before the First argues the city's case.
3. **The struck house.** The Keeper of the Roll has been asked to strike a failing house from the roll, and the house's head offers a party good money to find out which councilor bought its debts. Orthema would like the same answer.
