# Appearance {#appearance}

Átseka Volkemis is a 35-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of light build. Her olive skin is freckled from the terraces, her dark brown hair is bound up under a plain cap, and her green eyes miss little. At the vintage her hands are stained purple to the wrist like any grower's.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Átseka Volkemis is the lady of [[place-enthara|Enthara]], the wine town of the [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]. Her husband holds the town of the king, as his house has since the kingdom was founded, and he has been under contract with a company in the east for six years. She runs the town in his name. She holds the market charter, sits at the press-house when the vintage comes in, and answers to the crown's bailiff for the dues, and every document she seals carries her husband's name.

Half the terraces of Enthara owe wine to one Valthari house or another under the spring advance, and the growers call it the Valthari chain. Átseka stands in the press-house yard while the crown's share and the lord's are measured, with a Valthari factor beside her, and she is civil to him.

## Psyche

### Personality

Átseka is quiet, patient and hard to surprise. She listens to a grower's whole account of a fallen wall before she says anything.

### Motivation

She wants the terraces of Enthara held by the families who work them, and the town free of the advance.

### Strengths

She knows every terrace in the town, who owes on it and to whom. The growers trust her to settle a boundary wall.

### Weaknesses

She holds nothing in her own name, and a lord's house that the crown sees run by a wife for six years invites questions at Torvaleth.

## Social

### Companions

The growers of the upper terraces and the town's priest, who witnesses the spring price in the square.

### Patrons

Her husband, abroad, whose letters come home with the money he earns.

### Enemies

The factors of the Valthari houses, who buy the vintage on the vine.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Átseka is of the Gentry of the kingdom and governs Enthara for her husband.
- **[[affiliation-lusinia|Faith of Lusinia]]**: She keeps the farmers' faith of the plain.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A wall in the rain.** A terrace wall above the square fell in the winter rains and took a family's vines down the hill. The lady wants it rebuilt before the factors come up the road, and the family has no money for stone.
2. **The bailiff's question.** The crown's bailiff has asked when the lord of Enthara is coming home, and Átseka wants a letter carried east to her husband and his answer brought back.
3. **The spring price.** A factor has offered the growers a better advance than the others, and Átseka wants to know which house sent him before anyone takes it.

:::secret {title="The debts she buys"}
Átseka has been quietly buying the debts of the poorest terraces from the Valthari houses at a discount, with money her husband sends home from his contract. If she holds enough of them she can break the advance in Enthara by setting her own price at the next spring, and the Valthari houses that notice it first are already asking who in the town is selling to her.
:::
