# Appearance {#appearance}

Térgadan Mirindi is a 39-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of light, wiry build. He has tawny, weathered skin, black hair worn in a long braid and amber eyes. A band of fine blue tattoo circles his left forearm.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Térgadan Mirindi is a Warden of the Áelendan clans of the [[place-toravarrange|Toravar Range]], the forested mountain belt that closes Tarvénia's north and west. He holds with his clan the same [Kindred Pact]{.name data-type="lore/law"} as the clans of the Tribal Lands and answers to the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] alone. The high reaches are the clans' sacred country, and no Tarvenan crown claims them.

Frictions over straying herds, logging concessions and road surveys near sacred groves are handled by the local Wardens with the local baron, usually without reaching the crown. Térgadan is the Warden whom the barons of the western arm meet, and he settles each quarrel one valley at a time.

## Psyche

### Personality

Térgadan is quiet, direct and dry. He gives a baron the same courtesy he gives a goat-boy and the same warning once.

### Motivation

He wants the clans' ground whole and the barons' herds below it, with no mountain-war that the crown must settle through Élavendri hands.

### Strengths

He knows every sacred grove and every high pasture, speaks Tarveni without accent and keeps the Pact's law in his head. The barons trust that he will say what he means before he acts.

### Weaknesses

He is a Warden and not a chief, and a clan that wants a war can ignore him. He has no patience for a baron's bargaining.

## Social

### Companions

His clan's runners carry his word between the high reaches and the passes.

### Patrons

The Elder Wardens of his clan.

### Enemies

A baron who pushes his herds or loggers into ground the clan holds sacred.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]]**: Térgadan is a Warden, bound to the Pact and answerable to the clans alone.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The baron's loggers.** A baron's loggers have cut a stand beside a grove the clan holds sacred. Térgadan asks a party to carry his warning to the baron and to bring back the name of the man who gave the order.
2. **The western passes.** A host of the Western March has camped on high pasture the clan uses in summer. Térgadan asks a party to ask the baron at Kemthoza to move it before the clan's young men move it for him.
3. **A road survey.** Survey stakes have appeared near a sacred grove on the northern arm. Térgadan wants a party to find out who is paying for a road through the high reaches.
