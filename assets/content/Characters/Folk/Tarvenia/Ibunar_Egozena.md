# Appearance {#appearance}

Ibunar Egozena is a 61-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. He is small and bent, with a white beard and a voice that carries across the meadow when he recites.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ibunar Egozena is an elder of a clan of [[place-ibotsain|Ibotsain]] and the Lawkeeper of the [[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]]. At the sitting he recites the customs the clans of [[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Eskarath]] hold in common, and where two clans' customs differ he settles which one the sitting's question turns on. There is no written law in the highlands; there is Ibunar, and the Lawkeeper before him.

He and [[being-elutsaribot|Elutsar Ibotsena]] have argued the customs of the water at Elutsar's hearth for forty winters.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ibunar is precise, dry and fond of an old case. He answers a question with a precedent and a precedent with its exception.

### Motivation

He wants the customs remembered whole, and he wants a young elder to learn them before he dies.

### Strengths

He knows the customs of every clan and the rulings of every Lawkeeper he has heard of.

### Weaknesses

He has no apprentice who has learned them all, and he distrusts every one who offers to write them down.

## Social

### Companions

Elutsar Ibotsena and the elders of the village.

### Patrons

None; the clans send him.

### Enemies

None but time.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]**: Ibunar sits as a Delegate.
- **[[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]]**: He recites the common customs as Lawkeeper.
- **[[affiliation-eskrthsprts|The Spirits of Eskarath]]**: He keeps the shrine at the spring with his village.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The older order.** A clan claims an older order of drawing water than the Water-Warden keeps. The Lawkeeper wants witnesses found who remember the order their grandfathers kept.
2. **An heir to the customs.** Ibunar wants a young elder of another clan brought to his hearth for the winter, and the young man's clan does not trust Ibotsain.
