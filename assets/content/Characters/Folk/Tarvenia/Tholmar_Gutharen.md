# Appearance {#appearance}

Tholmar Gútharen is a 44-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall with a heavy build. He has olive skin, weathered complexion, dark blonde hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tholmar Gútharen is a captain of the garrison of [[place-kavrenath|Kávrenath]], commanding the sector next to that of [[being-tarsilathelv|Társila Thelvaris]]. He is a knight of the king of Asturath's household, ambitious and politically connected, and he holds his sector's tower for the king as its castellan, answerable for its garrison and stores.

He resents that she, younger and a Thelvaris, was given the more valuable Kávrenath posting. He documents her minor decisions and spreads rumors that her unit's success comes from brutality toward refugees.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tholmar is correct, cold and well-liked by his superiors. He keeps a notebook.

### Motivation

He wants Társila's posting. He wants to be a Warden of the March.

### Strengths

His sector is well-run and his reports are clean. He has friends at Lezkora.

### Weaknesses

His evidence is thin and his notebook can be read.

## Social

### Companions

His sergeant and a clerk at Lezkora.

### Patrons

A mountain lord of the king's council.

### Enemies

Társila Thelvaris and Estaril Dómivar.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]]**: Tholmar is a Knight in the garrison and holds the office of Castellan.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The refugee road.** Two hundred people are camped below the town gate. Tholmar writes a report that Társila turned them back by force. He asks a party to confirm it.
2. **The promotion.** Warden Dómivar means to knight Társila. Tholmar asks a party to bring a rumor to the Warden's ear.
3. **The deserters' price.** A band of imperial deserters has come over the passes. Tholmar asks a party to bring their captain to his sector, not hers.
