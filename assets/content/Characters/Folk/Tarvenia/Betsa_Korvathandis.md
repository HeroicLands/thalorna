# Appearance {#appearance}

Betsa Korvathandis is a 51-year-old woman who stands 5'3" tall and is of light build. She is small and wiry, gray-eyed, her hands dark with lamp smoke that no scrubbing takes out.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Betsa Korvathandis is the Keeper of the Lamp at [[place-ezkoba|Ezkoba]], the senior of the six keepers of the [Long Lamp]{.name data-type="place/structure"} on the clifftop. She answers to the crown of [[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Galvaren]] for the lamp-due and the oil, and to the town for the fire. The post passes in families, and she learned to trim the pans before she learned to row.

A bad seal year and a crown slow to send the oil have left the lamp with a month's fuel and the darkest months still to come. The keepers swear the lamp was lit on the nights the boats went onto the reefs south of the town, and Betsa swears it loudest.

## Psyche

### Personality

Betsa is fierce, plain-spoken and tireless. She takes a dark lamp as a death in the family.

### Motivation

She wants the lamp lit every foggy night of the winter, and she will get oil from anyone who has it.

### Strengths

Every fisherman between Ezkoba and Durvolka can name a night her lamp brought him home.

### Weaknesses

A keeper who lets the lamp go out on a foggy night loses the post the next morning, and she is answerable for every keeper's night.

## Social

### Companions

The other five keepers and their families, and Nelma Ularris, the teller, who lives at the foot of the tower.

### Patrons

The crown, which pays the oil when it remembers.

### Enemies

Whoever lights the false light, which says to the coast that her lamp was not lit.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]]**: Betsa holds the office of Keeper of the Lamp, as a Freeman.
- **[[affiliation-drownedroad|The Drowned Road]]**: She is of the Shore Kin and keeps the lamp the drowned are said to steer by.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A month's fuel.** The Keeper of the Lamp asks a party to bring oil up the cliff path from Durvolka before the dark of the year, and she has the lamp-due to pay with.
2. **The other light.** Betsa wants a watch kept on the reefs south of the town on the next foggy night, by someone the town cannot accuse of lighting it.
