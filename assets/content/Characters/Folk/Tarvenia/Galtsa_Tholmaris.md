# Appearance {#appearance}

Galtsa Tholmaris is a 39-year-old man who stands 5' 9" tall and is of light build. He has fair, clear skin, dark brown hair and hazel eyes, and wears brick-red lowland cloth too fine for the highland road.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Galtsa Tholmaris is a factor in the household of [[being-hiltheratsol|Hilthera Tsoluregi]], the Master of Commerce of Lyenrik. He has been sent to the highlands of [[place-eskarath|Eskarath]] with a gift for the elders and a request that the [[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]] let a toll-road cross the pass above [[place-leparrun|Leparrun]]. He has been told to be courteous, and he is. He does not know what the road is for beyond its toll.

## Psyche

### Personality

Courteous, patient and careful never to promise more than he has been given.

### Motivation

He wants the road agreed before the next election, because his mistress has said it matters.

### Strengths

He keeps accounts exactly and has the manners to sit at a highland hearth.

### Weaknesses

He does not understand the arrangement the highlands keep, and he buys what cannot be bought.

## Social

### Companions

A clerk, a surveyor and two guides hired at Leparrun.

### Patrons

Hilthera Tsoluregi.

### Enemies

[[being-elutsaribot|Elutsar Ibotsena]], who distrusts a gift that asks nothing.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Galtsa serves the Master of Commerce as a factor.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The gift's price.** Elutsar asks a party to learn what the gift is meant to buy.
2. **The guides.** Galtsa hires guides over the pass for a survey he will not explain.
3. **The blood owed.** He offers a clan the silver for a feud it cannot end, and a party that learns it holds the lever.
