# Appearance {#appearance}

Thelvar Mozkarreta is a 56-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. His tanned skin is weathered and lined, his hair is white and his brown eyes are quick. He has no little finger on his left hand, lost to a bit long ago, and he rubs the stump when he is thinking.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Thelvar Mozkarreta is the horse-judge, the _kornazdo_, of [[place-thorgila|Thórgila]]. At the autumn horse fair he reads each horse's teeth and says its age and soundness, and his word settles the sale. At the fair's verse-duel, the _gortsada_, he gives each bout its theme, its parts and its vowel, and says which way the fair's stamping went. The fair has judged between him and a lord before, and Thelvar has never been overruled.

He is a Freeman of the [[affiliation-cnclthrgl|Council of Thórgila]] who has been bought and sold horses all his life and has never kept one. A sale is sworn at the oath stone, and a seller is held to the word he gave there, so a horse-judge who is wrong is wrong before the whole fair.

## Psyche

### Personality

Thelvar is dry, patient and fond of a long argument over a short matter. He loves a good horse more than he loves most people and says so.

### Motivation

He wants every sale sworn at the stone to be a sale that stays sworn. He is training a niece to read teeth so that the office does not pass out of the town.

### Strengths

He can age a horse to within a season and tell a doctored mouth from an honest one by touch. His judgment of the verse-duel is trusted by both singers and crowd.

### Weaknesses

He is stubborn about his own readings and slow to admit that a young horse's teeth have fooled him. He drinks at the fair.

## Social

### Companions

The singers who come to the _gortsada_ and the horse-sellers of the valleys consult him all year. His niece rides with him.

### Patrons

The free men of the town's council, who name him each fair.

### Enemies

A horse-seller who swore a horse's age at the stone and has friends among the crossbowmen home from contract.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-cnclthrgl|Council of Thórgila]]**: Thelvar holds the office of Horse-Judge as a Freeman.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A buyer at the stone.** A horse-seller swears a horse's age at the oath stone and Thelvar reads its teeth and says otherwise. The buyer wants the seller answered before the same witnesses, and the seller has friends among the crossbowmen home from contract. Thelvar asks a party to stand by him at the stone.
2. **The lord's riders.** Riders of Nizel Ganotis are buying horses at the fair at prices that buy friends. Thelvar asks a party to learn who among the sellers has taken the lord's money.
3. **The theme of the year.** A singer asks Thelvar to give the verse-duel a theme that favors her, and the other singer's backers are offering him a horse. He wants a party to find out which of them is lying about what the other offered.
