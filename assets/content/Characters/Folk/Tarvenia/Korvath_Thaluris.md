# Appearance {#appearance}

Korvath Thaluris is a 48-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is broad and heavy. He has tanned, weathered and leathery skin, white hair and blue eyes. A salt-split scar runs across his left palm, and his hands are thick from thirty seasons on the hand-line.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Korvath Thaluris is the first master of the roll of [[place-ulhera|Ulhera]], the cod port of Galvaren's south-western shore, and a shipmaster of thirty seasons. He holds one of the town's seats at the Long Table at Durvolka and has been beyond the Far Grounds three times, to waters the charts of Valthari leave blank. He keeps his own book of soundings, currents and landmarks in his own shorthand, as every master does, and means to leave it to his heir.

He is the loudest voice at the Table against the crown's price for salt, and the king's council has learned to let him speak first and briefly. Ulhera is run in practice by its roll of masters, about sixty names, and the roll elects two seats at the Table each autumn.

## Psyche

### Personality

Korvath is loud, funny and unhurried, and swears by every sea-saint in the calendar. He is shrewd about weather and shrewder about men who owe him.

### Motivation

He wants salt at a price the boats can live on, a chart of the island shore beyond the Far Grounds, and a crew that comes home. He is slow to forgive a master who loses a man by cutting corners.

### Strengths

He reads a sea and a crew better than almost anyone on the coast. The masters of the roll listen when he speaks, and the curing-house mistresses lend to him.

### Weaknesses

His mouth makes enemies at the Table, and he drinks with those he should not tell secrets. He has no heir to take his book.

## Social

### Companions

His boat's crew has sailed with him for twenty years. The curing-house mistress who holds the notes on his hull is an old friend.

### Patrons

The curing houses that lend the masters the money for boats and salt.

### Enemies

The Keeper of the Salt, who sets the crown's price, and the rival master whose book of soundings has gone missing.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]]**: Korvath is a Shipmaster, entered on the roll of his harbor, and one of Ulhera's masters at the Long Table.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The book of soundings.** Korvath's old rival on the roll has died at sea, and his book of soundings, with the only chart of an island shore he claimed to have found beyond the Far Grounds, has gone missing from his widow's house. A Valthari shipping house has offered a sum for it, and Korvath wants it back before anyone can sell it.
2. **The salt runs out.** The autumn salt ship from the south is a month late and the crown's store is empty. Korvath wants a party to ride to Durvolka and come back with salt, or with a reason the Long Table will believe.
3. **A boat out too long.** A cod boat is in its eleventh month out, and its master is Korvath's brother-in-law. The teller will call it in at the Night of Lanterns, and Korvath wants to know why the master's wife has been seen meeting a man at the drying racks.
