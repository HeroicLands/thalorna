# Appearance {#appearance}

Mozkarra Betsuris is a 47-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. She is tall, weathered and green-eyed, and she still walks with a deckhand's roll.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Mozkarra Betsuris is the Harbormaster of [[place-valthari|Valthari]], a freeman of the city named by the [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]] to keep the port, its pilots and its wharves and to levy the dues on every hull that crosses the bar. She was a pilot herself before the council named her, and the Pilots' Guild still thinks of her as one of theirs.

Since the seamen refused to sign on, the roads are full of ships at anchor and her dues book is empty. The owners want her pilots to take a ship out with any crew that will sail, and the pilots are the seamen's kin.

## Psyche

### Personality

Mozkarra is blunt, practical and fond of her pilots. She says what the bar will allow and nothing about what the council would like.

### Motivation

She wants the port working, and she will not order a pilot across the bar against the seamen.

### Strengths

The pilots follow her, and nothing crosses the bar without one.

### Weaknesses

She serves at the council's pleasure, and the owners hold the council.

## Social

### Companions

The Pilots' Guild of Valthari, Kolrith Delvanor among them, and the pilot Dálena Valaron.

### Patrons

The Konsezarath, which names her.

### Enemies

The owners who want the bar crossed with strike-breakers.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]**: Mozkarra holds the office of Harbormaster, as a Freeman of the City.
- **[[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pozeras]]**: She keeps the faith of voyages.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The bar at night.** A ship crossed the bar last night without a pilot and without lights. The Harbormaster wants to know whose it was and who sailed it.
2. **The order.** The owners have asked the council to order the pilots out. Mozkarra wants the seamen to hear it from someone they trust before the order comes.
