# Appearance {#appearance}

Arruno Mendurena is a 55-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of heavy build. He is big and scarred, his brown hair going gray, and he wears the calling horn on a baldric across his chest.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Arruno Mendurena is an elder of the mountain clan at [[place-leparrun|Leparrun]] and the Host-Caller of the [[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]. When the High King of Tarvénia calls the ban, the [[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]] weighs the call against the terms of the crown, and only once it has agreed that the call stands does Arruno summon the warriors of the clans. When the council agrees that a threat concerns every clan, he summons the common muster.

He watches the survey stakes on Leparrun's pass the way another man watches a raiding party.

## Psyche

### Personality

Arruno is gruff, watchful and slow to call. He has seen what a muster costs the valleys and does not sound the horn lightly.

### Motivation

He wants the territory's warriors to go only where the arrangement sends them, and he wants no road over the pass that a host can use without asking.

### Strengths

The young men of the clans come when he calls, and he knows the passes as a hunter does.

### Weaknesses

He cannot call without the meadow's agreement, and he distrusts every lowlander on sight.

## Social

### Companions

The hunters and herdsmen of Leparrun, and the Water-Warden, Soraine Leparena.

### Patrons

None; the clans send him.

### Enemies

Whoever drove the survey stakes above Leparrun.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]**: Arruno holds the office of Host-Caller, as a Delegate.
- **[[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]]**: He summons the common muster as Host-Caller.
- **[[affiliation-eskrthsprts|The Spirits of Eskarath]]**: He keeps the shrine to the spirit of the pass.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The stakes.** The Host-Caller wants the stakes on the pass followed to where they end, and he will not pull them until he knows.
2. **A call out of season.** A lowland rider has asked whether the clans would answer a ban called for a war on Tarvénia's own lords. Arruno wants the rider's master named.
