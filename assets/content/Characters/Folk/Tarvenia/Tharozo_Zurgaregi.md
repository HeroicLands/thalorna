# Appearance {#appearance}

Tharozo Zurgaregi is a 41-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is heavily built, thick through the chest and short in the neck. He has olive, weathered and ruddy skin, dark brown hair cut close and hazel eyes. Two fingers of his left hand are stiff from a break that was never set, and he holds a cup in his right.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tharozo Zurgaregi is the baron of the high valley north of [[being-kaldrokanar|Kaldro Kanarris]]'s, the last valley of the [[place-westernmrch|Western March]] under the clans' ground of the [[place-toravarrange|Toravar]]. He holds it from a stone tower over the valley's one village, as his house has held it since the March's barons first went down the passes, and he brings its men down [[place-grumagando|Grumagando]] under his own banner every season the snow allows.

The last two seasons have gone badly for his valley. His host came home from [[place-talothe|the Talote]] with fewer men than it took down and less than it went for, and the valley wintered on what its herds could spare. This spring Tharozo has hired a coast company, [[affiliation-kraveldo|Kraveldo]], under [[being-pramizamozk|Prámiza Mozkarreta]], to go down the pass with his host. No baron of the March has brought a hired company to the war before, and the other barons' men say so in the yard at [[place-kemthoza|Kemthoza]]. Tharozo has not sent his own men up to the yard to wait for the snow's going.

:::secret {title="What Tharozo means to do"}
Tharozo means to go over before the barons meet in Kemthoza's hall, and not by the war pass. North of Grumagando the shepherds' way climbs from his valley past the Mirindi's boundary cairn above [[place-mirgonlodge|Mirgon Lodge]], crosses the clan's high pasture under its hallowed grove and goes over the ridge to come down on the Talote side above [[place-bldfldmalnebris|Malnebris]]. His men have walked it twice since the thaw began. A host that comes down that way falls on the road below the field from above, behind anyone watching the war pass, and Tharozo means to strike the Talote's muster in its camp below the field, where it rides up from [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] to hold the foot of the pass, and drive off its remounts before it is whole.

He knows the muster is thin this spring because he asked [[being-bedarrekulm|Bedarrek Ulmis]], who went down under the green bough at the thaw with a ransom, to count the remounts in the yard at Durvalis while he waited. Bedarrek has not come back. Tharozo does not know whether he was caught, and he has not asked anyone, because asking would say what Bedarrek was asked to do.

His contract with Prámiza names the war pass. He has not told her the road he means to take.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Tharozo is loud, generous at his own table and quick to take offense on his valley's behalf. He counts every slight and forgets every debt he owes.

### Motivation

He wants one good season: a host that comes home whole and heavy, his valley fed through the winter, and the first crossing that tells the other barons his house is not finished.

### Strengths

He is brave, he knows the high country above his valley as well as any shepherd, and his men follow him because he walks at the front.

### Weaknesses

He holds that the custom of the season is a thing the lucky barons keep, and he has stopped believing he is one of them. He does not see the clans' ground as anyone's.

## Social

### Companions

His valley's men, his steward and the shepherds who know the high paths.

### Patrons

None; he holds his valley of nobody and owes the High King the ban.

### Enemies

The border families of the Talote, and, he believes, every baron who has had a better season than his.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]**: Tharozo holds his valley at the rung of Knight and owes the High King the ban when it is called against an enemy from outside.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The baron's table.** Tharozo hires a party to ride down the pass with his host and offers it a share of the season, paid at the homecoming, and asks it to start before the other barons hear of the offer.
2. **The coast company.** Tharozo wants a party to keep Prámiza Mozkarreta's men and his valley's men from quarreling before the season opens.
3. **The man who went down.** A shepherd of Tharozo's valley carried a ransom down the pass at the thaw and has not come back, and his wife wants to know why the baron has sent nobody to ask after him.
