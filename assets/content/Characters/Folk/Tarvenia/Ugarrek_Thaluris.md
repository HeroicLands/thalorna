# Appearance {#appearance}

Ugarrek Thaluris is a 52-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of heavy build. He is big and ruddy, his black hair gone gray at the temples, and he wears a valley lord's leather and a cattleman's hat to the Long Table.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ugarrek Thaluris holds a valley lordship in the wet inland country of [[place-galvaren|Galvaren]] and sits at the [Long Table]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} at [[place-durvolka|Durvolka]] among the landed lords, on the side facing the shipmasters. The king, [[being-ganothervo|Ganoth Ervothandis]], has made him Marshal of the Ban: when the High King of Tarvénia calls the ban, Ugarrek musters the knights and freemen of the valleys and leads them.

He speaks for the lords at the Table, which is to say for the valleys' cattle trade with [[place-leonrik|Lyenrik]], for the crown's justice for their tenants and for peace on the one road between the two kingdoms.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ugarrek is loud, hospitable and stubborn. He likes the shipmasters across the Table as men and distrusts them as a party.

### Motivation

He wants the valleys' road to Lyenrik kept open and toll-light, and he wants the Table's harbor side kept from deciding the crown's business alone.

### Strengths

The valley lords follow him at the Table, and the freemen of the valleys come to his muster.

### Weaknesses

He knows cattle and not salt, and the harbor side runs rings round him on the crown's revenue.

## Social

### Companions

The landed lords of the valleys and the drovers of the Lyenrik road.

### Patrons

King Ganoth Ervothandis.

### Enemies

Korvath Thaluris of Ulhera, the loudest voice on the harbor side, who is no kin of his whatever the name.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]]**: Ugarrek holds the offices of Lord of the Table and Marshal of the Ban, as a Lord.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The vote across the Table.** The harbor side has the votes to carry a lower salt price. Ugarrek wants to know which lord of his own side has been promised what to sit on his hands.
2. **The road tolls.** Lyenrik has raised the toll on the road between the kingdoms again, and the Marshal wants a message carried to Torvaleth that is not quite a threat.
