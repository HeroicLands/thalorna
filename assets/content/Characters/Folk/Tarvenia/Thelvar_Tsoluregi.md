# Appearance {#appearance}

Thelvar Tsoluregi is a 53-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He is lean and leathery, his black hair gray at the sides, and he carries a shepherd's crook as a staff even on the town's paved street.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Thelvar Tsoluregi is the Drove-Warden of the [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]], a mountain lord who holds a valley of his own above [[place-tsaberis|Tsaberis]] and keeps the drove roads and the king's high meadows for the crown. He settles quarrels between valleys over grazing and collects the crown's due, one beast in forty that goes up. The shepherds' council sets the day of the drive and the order of the flocks, and the town's householders keep the fold and the market, and the three quarrel every spring and settle it before the drive.

He is a cousin of [[being-hiltheratsol|Hilthera Tsoluregi]], who sits on the king of Lyenrik's council, and the mountain lords mention it when he settles a quarrel against them.

## Psyche

### Personality

Thelvar is patient, wry and stubborn. He listens to a grazing quarrel the way he walks a meadow, slowly and all the way to its edge.

### Motivation

He wants the drive to go up and come down without a fight between valleys, and the crown's beasts counted honestly.

### Strengths

He knows every meadow's boundary and every valley's grievance, and the shepherds' council respects him.

### Weaknesses

His cousin's seat in the lowland court makes the mountain lords doubt him, and the shepherds' council can outwait him.

## Social

### Companions

The elders of the shepherds' council and the wise-woman below the cave.

### Patrons

King Alzemo Zolduregi.

### Enemies

Valley lords who push their flocks onto a neighbor's meadow and expect the Drove-Warden to look away.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]]**: Thelvar holds the office of Drove-Warden, as a Mountain Lord.
- **[[affiliation-menutsa|The Mother]]**: He leaves cheese at the cave above Tsaberis on the way up and on the way down.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The order of the flocks.** The shepherds' council has a quarrel over the order of the flocks for the autumn drive down. The Drove-Warden wants it settled before the snow, and the two valleys will not sit together.
2. **The beast in forty.** The count of the crown's beasts is short again. Thelvar wants to know whether the shepherds are cheating the crown or his own men are.
