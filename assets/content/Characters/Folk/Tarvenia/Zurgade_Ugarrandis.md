Zurgáde Ugarrándis is the Tarvénan lord of Thornhill Manor in the country around Valthári, known to his neighbors for its hunting. He hunts often and with guests, among them greater lords than himself, and relies on his huntswoman [[being-zoriladmvr|Zórila Dómivar]]. His standing rests on Tarvénian politics, and a change at court that threatens him threatens everyone at Thornhill.
