# Appearance {#appearance}

Thelvar Ikentsis is a 34-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He is smooth and well dressed in the Taruzelian fashion, dark-haired, with a ready smile and a careful eye.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Thelvar Ikentsis is a factor of the house of Ikentsis, which ships wine and oil to the Taruzelian ports, and the [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]] has named him Consul Abroad for the [[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]] at [[place-besikar2|Besikar]], the largest and wealthiest city of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]]. He keeps the city's seal there and settles the trade disputes of Valthari's merchants under it.

His house's head is the First of the Council, [[being-orthemaiken|Orthema Ikentsis]], and the idle ships in Valthari's roads are cargoes he promised Taruzelian buyers.

## Psyche

### Personality

Thelvar is affable, quick and hard to pin down. He makes a friend of every buyer and a promise to none he cannot keep.

### Motivation

He wants the Taruzelian trade kept and the city's merchants trusted in Besikar, where their late cargoes are eating that trust.

### Strengths

He knows the Taruzelian houses and their courts, and his seal settles a Valthari quarrel in a foreign port in a day.

### Weaknesses

He serves the city and his house at once, and the other houses in Besikar say so.

## Social

### Companions

The Valthari factors at Besikar and the Taruzelian merchants who buy from them.

### Patrons

Orthema Ikentsis, the head of his house.

### Enemies

Valthari houses in Besikar who think the consul settles their quarrels in his own house's favor.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]**: Thelvar holds the office of Consul Abroad, as a House Factor.
- **[[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pozeras]]**: He keeps the faith of voyages.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The buyer's clause.** A Taruzelian buyer means to claim the forfeit on a late Valthari cargo under the strike. The consul wants the Charter Hall's ruling on the strike carried to Besikar before the buyer's court sits.
2. **The other house.** A rival Valthari house at Besikar has its own seal and calls it the city's. Thelvar wants it taken from them without a quarrel in a foreign court.
