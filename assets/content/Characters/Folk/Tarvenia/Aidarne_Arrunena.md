# Appearance {#appearance}

Aidarne Arrunena is a 57-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of light build. She is small, dark-eyed and gray-haired, and she keeps a string of knotted cords at her belt that she runs through her fingers as she counts.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Aidarne Arrunena is an elder of a clan of [[place-leparrun|Leparrun]] and the Keeper of the Feud of the [[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]]. She reckons the blood owed and paid between the kindreds of [[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Eskarath]], so that the sitting can settle a feud, and she will not close a sitting until she has given her count.

The Otsanena owe blood in a feud they cannot end, and someone has offered to pay it for them. Aidarne has heard of the offer and not of its price.

## Psyche

### Personality

Aidarne is grave, exact and unhurried. She counts aloud, and she counts every time.

### Motivation

She wants every feud reckoned to the last life, so that it can be ended and not merely paused.

### Strengths

She remembers the count of every feud in the highlands, and the clans accept it.

### Weaknesses

She counts what is owed and cannot make a kindred pay it.

## Social

### Companions

The elders of the kindreds whose feuds she counts.

### Patrons

None; the clans send her.

### Enemies

Anyone who pays blood in silver to buy a clan's voice.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]**: Aidarne sits as a Delegate.
- **[[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]]**: She reckons the feuds as Keeper of the Feud.
- **[[affiliation-eskrthsprts|The Spirits of Eskarath]]**: She keeps the forebears of her clan.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The price of the blood.** The Keeper of the Feud wants to know who has offered to pay the Otsanena's blood debt, and what he wants for it, before the count at the solstice.
2. **A death on the pass.** A man of one kindred has died on the pass in a fall that the other kindred calls a killing. Aidarne wants to know which count it goes in.
