# Appearance {#appearance}

Gaimel Ornetis is a 41-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. He is lean and brown, with green eyes narrowed against the glare off the water, and his boots are always wet to the knee.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Gaimel Ornetis is the River Warden of the [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]], a knight who keeps the fords, ferries, quays and flood banks of the [Thalsira]{.name data-type="place/feature"} below [[place-ronetho|Ornetho]] and levies the river tolls on them. He lives at [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]], by the ford the capital is built on.

In late spring, when the snow melts in the mountains of Asturath and the river rises in the _mozkar_ (snowmelt flood), he calls the villages out to the banks, and in a heavy year he rides the river road for a week without sleeping in a bed.

## Psyche

### Personality

Gaimel is restless, practical and short with anyone who talks about the river without having stood in it.

### Motivation

He wants the banks held through the next flood and the river tolls paid by the bargemen who have learned where his men do not stand.

### Strengths

He can read the river's rise a day ahead, and the bargemen and ferrymen know him by sight.

### Weaknesses

The banks are the villages' to keep, and he can only call them out. A bad flood is blamed on him whatever its cause.

## Social

### Companions

The ferrymen and the bank-reeves of the river villages.

### Patrons

The king, who names him, and the Bailiff of Torvaleth, who judges the villages that fail him.

### Enemies

The bargemen who run past the toll quays at night.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Gaimel holds the office of River Warden, as a Knight.
- **[[affiliation-lusinia|Faith of Lusinia]]**: He keeps the faith of the plain and its harvest.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The early melt.** The river is rising early this spring, and the River Warden needs riders to carry the call to the villages below Ornetho before the water does.
2. **The night barges.** Barges are passing the toll quay at night with their lamps covered. Gaimel wants to know whose cargo is worth the risk.
