# Appearance {#appearance}

Alzemo Zolduregi is a 50-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall and is broad and heavy through the shoulder. He has tanned, weathered skin, black hair gone gray at the temples, and brown eyes under heavy brows. A pale scar crosses his right eyebrow. He dresses as a mountain lord does, in wool and iron, and wears the plain circlet of Asturath only in the council.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Alzemo Zolduregi is the king of Asturath, of the royal house of the Zolduregi, and a mountain lord too, with valleys of his own whose rents and flocks are the crown's revenue. The council of mountain lords acknowledged him at its oath stone, and each lord gripped his forearm and said the ban. He keeps his court in the castle at the north end of [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]], the city he holds by one road and one gate.

The High King of Tarvénia is often the king of Asturath, and he is not at present. [[being-ularrekgarr|Ularrek Garrundeta]], one of Alzemo's own mountain lords, sits in the High Hall as the king's guest, and the king's gate, the king's road and the king's bread keep him there. The two courts are courteous, and every lord in the kingdom knows the High King is old.

## Psyche

### Personality

Alzemo is large, unhurried and watchful, and he listens with his head lowered like a man judging a bull. He is generous with food and sparing with promises, and he lets a mountain lord finish a long grievance before he answers it in a sentence.

### Motivation

He wants the crown of Tarvénia to come back to Asturath's own, and he does not want it to go to the king of Lyenrik, whose wealth would buy the electors. He is content to wait for the old man to die while he keeps every road to Lezkora in his own hands.

### Strengths

He controls the plateau gate and so the electors' climb. He holds the loyalty of the council of mountain lords and the crown's officers at the gate, the iron, the meadows and the frontier, and he calls the ban of Asturath when he must.

### Weaknesses

He cannot make himself High King while the lords have already elected another, and he cannot be seen to hasten the old man's end. Every lowland lord who climbs the road is also a count of what Asturath may lose.

## Social

### Companions

The Warden of the Plateau Gate counts everyone through for him. The Keeper of the Roll and the Steward of the King's Valleys serve in his household, and his heralds carry his word to the valleys and to other crowns.

### Patrons

None. The king owes the mountain lords the ban's courtesy, and they owe him the ban.

### Enemies

Zenoth Oltris of Lyenrik, whose kingdom is the realm's rival to Asturath and whose men are filling the road. In Asturath itself, the valley lords who think the king too ready to let the old man's guesthood turn into a rival court.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]]**: Alzemo is the king, holding the crown by blood and the council's acknowledgment.
- **[[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]**: He is an elector, one of the lords who give their voices aloud at the oath stone when the crown falls vacant.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The count at the gate.** The Warden of the Plateau Gate asks a party to ride the plateau road and tell him which lowland lords' men are coming up in twos and threes and which lord sent them, because Alzemo wants the names before the next full moon.
2. **A cistern gone foul.** One of the western cisterns has turned in a dry spring. The king's steward wants to know whether the weather did it, and the party that finds the answer learns who in Lezkora has reason to foul the city's water while two courts share it.
3. **The guest's heir.** The king offers a party a great deal to carry a private letter to the Garrundeta valley and bring back an account of who in the old man's house is making ready for his death.
