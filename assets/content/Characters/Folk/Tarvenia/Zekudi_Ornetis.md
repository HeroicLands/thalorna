# Appearance {#appearance}

Zekudi Ornetis is a 34-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall with a medium build. He has olive skin, ruddy complexion, dark brown hair, and hazel eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zekudi Ornetis is a guard captain of the gates of [[place-ronetho|Ornetho]], skilled and praised for his work, and a trusted subordinate of [[being-hldndrgn|Haldena Durgana]]. He accepts bribes from the smuggling magnate [[being-garrutsagalt|Garrutsa Galtsuregi]] in exchange for letting contraband through the gates.

The castellan's garrison pays its captains poorly and Zekudi has two children in the city's school.

## Psyche

### Personality

Zekudi is cheerful, tired and loyal to Haldena, whom he deceives.

### Motivation

He wants his children to go to school and his debts to end. He wants Haldena not to find out.

### Strengths

He is an able officer and knows the gates better than anyone.

### Weaknesses

He is bribed, and Haldena's attention is on him.

## Social

### Companions

The gate-guards of his watch.

### Patrons

Garrutsa Galtsuregi.

### Enemies

Any honest guard.

1. **The traitor in her ranks.** Haldena Durgana suspects a bribe at the gate and asks a party to watch Zekudi's shift. Zekudi asks the same party to look the other way.
2. **The second wain.** A wain was stopped and a second let through. Zekudi asks a party to find who has been counting the wains.
