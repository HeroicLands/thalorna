# Appearance {#appearance}

Zekudi Ornetis is a 34-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall with a medium build. He has olive skin, ruddy complexion, dark brown hair, and hazel eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zekudi Ornetis is a guard captain of the gates of [[place-ronetho|Ornetho]], skilled and praised for his work, and a trusted subordinate of [[being-hldndrgn|Haldena Durgana]]. The castellan's garrison pays its captains poorly, and Zekudi has two children in the city's school.

## Psyche

### Personality

Zekudi is cheerful, tired and loyal to Haldena.

### Motivation

He wants his children to go to school and his debts to end.

### Strengths

He is an able officer and knows the gates better than anyone.

### Weaknesses

His pay does not cover his debts, and Haldena's attention is on every captain of the gates.

## Social

### Companions

The gate-guards of his watch.

### Patrons

Haldena Durgana, who trusts him.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Zekudi is a Freeman.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The second wain.** A wain was stopped and a second let through. Zekudi asks a party to find who has been counting the wains.

:::secret {title="The bribes at the gate"}
Zekudi accepts bribes from the smuggling magnate [[being-garrutsagalt|Garrutsa Galtsuregi]] in exchange for letting contraband through the gates, and he deceives Haldena, who suspects a bribe at the gate. He wants her not to find out. Garrutsa is his patron, and any honest guard is his enemy.

1. **The traitor in her ranks.** Haldena Durgana asks a party to watch Zekudi's shift. Zekudi asks the same party to look the other way.
2. **The school fee.** Garrutsa has paid his children's school fee and wants a favor. Zekudi asks a party to carry back the money.

:::
