# Appearance {#appearance}

Ilthera Hiltheris is a 51-year-old woman who stands 5'10" tall with a medium build. She has medium skin, weathered complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ilthera Hiltheris is a magistrate of [[place-valthari|Valthari]], an Arbitrator sworn at [[place-charterhall|the Charter Hall]], where she settles disputes between houses under the commercial custom. The Konsezarath enforces her rulings. She sits on the bench with two other arbitrators and presides when they differ.

She relies heavily on the counsel of [[being-hlrksbrnthr|Hálrikos Bránthira]] and has protected him from several attempts to diminish his authority. She knows her bench would founder without him.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ilthera is composed, precise and weary. She hears both sides to the end and gives her ruling in a single sentence.

### Motivation

She wants rulings that last, and the Charter Hall's reputation for fairness kept. She is afraid of the day Hálrikos dies.

### Strengths

Her rulings are cited in every counting-house. She cannot be hurried and cannot be bought for less than the city.

### Weaknesses

She depends on one man's memory. The Reformist Coalition has noticed.

## Social

### Companions

The other arbitrators, the clerks of the hall and Hálrikos.

### Patrons

None; the Konsezarath names her and she answers to the charter.

### Enemies

The Reformist Coalition and any house that has lost before her.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]**: Ilthera holds the office of Arbitrator as a Freeman of the City.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The impossible contradiction.** Two regulations of the city appear to contradict each other in a case before her. Ilthera asks a party to carry the papers to Hálrikos Bránthira and bring back his answer in his own hand.
2. **The coalition.** The Reformist Coalition has begun a campaign against Hálrikos. Ilthera asks a party to learn who funds it.
3. **A struck house.** A house struck from the roll asks Ilthera to rule that its contracts stand. She asks a party to learn who bought the house's debts.
