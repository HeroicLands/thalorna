# Appearance {#appearance}

Ervotho Tholmaris is a 70-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall with a medium build. He has olive skin, sun-kissed complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ervotho Tholmaris is the retired chief herald of the crown, titled Herald Emeritus, who lives in [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] in a house below the plateau wall. For many years he carried the High King's word to the lords and rode to every lord with a seat in the High Hall when a High King died. He is a gentleman of a house with a seat in the hall.

He views [[being-carengrvth|Kárenna Graveth]] as an upstart who lacks the gravitas for the post, and he has begun subtly undermining her authority with the king and his allies among the older nobility. Two courts share the city now, and the old herald is welcome at both.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ervotho is courtly, fussy and exact in ceremony. He remembers every form of address and holds a grudge for a missed one.

### Motivation

He wants the herald's office restored to the dignity it had in his day, which means his own style. He is afraid he will be remembered as the herald who allowed the court to become a rumor.

### Strengths

He holds every precedent of the crown's messages in his head and is trusted by the older lords. His word in the High Hall can settle a ceremony.

### Weaknesses

He is slow, vain and out of date. His influence rests on the goodwill of a High King who is failing.

## Social

### Companions

The Keeper of the Hall, an old friend, and the older heralds who still call.

### Patrons

The lords of the older houses with seats in the High Hall.

### Enemies

[[being-carengrvth|Kárenna Graveth]], Herald of the Crown, and the crown's authority behind her.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]**: Ervotho stands at the rung of Gentry and no longer holds the office of Herald of the Crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The old forms.** When the High King dies the crown's heralds must ride to every lord who has a seat in the hall. Ervotho tells a party that Kárenna Graveth does not know the order of the lords' names, and asks them to carry a roll of precedence to her, unsigned.
2. **A petitioner at the gate.** A man waiting in the yard between the gates says he has a quarrel with a lord that only the High King can hear. Ervotho recognizes the lord's livery and asks a party to find who is behind the petitioner.
3. **Two courts.** The king of Asturath's household has begun to use the forms of address that belong to the crown's herald. Ervotho asks a party to learn who authorized it.
