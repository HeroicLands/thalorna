# Appearance {#appearance}

Gaztoro Gerdoiena is a 66-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of light build. He is lean and white-haired, with a slow voice, and he walks with an oak staff carved with the marks of his clan.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Gaztoro Gerdoiena is an elder of the clan of the oaks at [[place-gerdoibun|Gerdoibun]] and the Speaker of the Council of the [[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]. At the summer solstice, in the high meadow of [[place-gaztorai|Gaztorai]], he presides over the [[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]]: he gives each delegate his turn in the meadow, keeps the order of speaking and closes the sitting when the clans have agreed or have agreed to go home. When the lords of Tarvénia make a High King, he goes up to [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] to hear the terms of the crown sworn, because the old arrangement with Eskarath is one of them.

He keeps no record of the sittings but his memory, and the clans hold that to be the point of choosing an old man.

## Psyche

### Personality

Gaztoro is slow, courteous and immovable on the order of speaking. He lets a delegate say everything and then asks the meadow what it heard.

### Motivation

He wants the arrangement kept to the word: the territory's warriors for the ban against a foreign enemy, and the rest of Tarvénia leaving the highlands alone.

### Strengths

Every clan accepts his order of speaking, and he remembers what each High King swore about Eskarath.

### Weaknesses

He presides and cannot bind, and a delegate who wants to stall the meadow can.

## Social

### Companions

The other delegates of the Biltzarrath and the elders of Gerdoibun.

### Patrons

None; the clans send him.

### Enemies

Anyone who would make the arrangement a matter of writing.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]**: Gaztoro holds the office of Speaker of the Council, as a Delegate.
- **[[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]]**: He presides over its sitting as Speaker of the Council.
- **[[affiliation-eskrthsprts|The Spirits of Eskarath]]**: He keeps the shrine in the oak grove of his own village.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The greeting and the gift.** A herald of the crown and a factor of Lyenrik both mean to speak in the meadow at the solstice. The Speaker wants to know before the sitting whether the two errands are one.
2. **The road to Lezkora.** The High King is old. Gaztoro wants companions who know the lowland road for the journey up to hear the next terms sworn.
