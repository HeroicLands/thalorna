# Appearance {#appearance}

Brazema Betsuris is a 57-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall with a medium build. She has olive skin, sun-kissed complexion, black hair, and amber eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Brazema Betsuris is the Master of the Fleet of Galvaren, a shipmaster who commands the king's six galleys at [[place-durvolka|Durvolka]] and every fishing boat of the coast when the king calls the boats to war. She has commissioned several of her hulls from [[being-domthgrvth|Dometh Graveth]] over two decades, and she waits years for each of them.

The harbor side of the Long Table wants her galleys sent against the pirates on the southern salt route, and the king, who is careful with the crown's money, has not given the order. She is a captain-admiral by title and a shipmaster by standing, and she is entered on the roll of Durvolka's masters.

## Psyche

### Personality

Brazema is forceful, shrewd and impatient with talk of cost. She stands on the quay in all weathers and addresses the king's clerks as if they were crew.

### Motivation

She wants her galleys at sea and her hulls built by Graveth hands. She wants a salt route her ships can protect.

### Strengths

Her galley crews are the best-drilled on the coast. The harbor side of the Table backs her and the king knows it.

### Weaknesses

She is blunt with a king who prizes caution, and she spends the crown's money faster than he likes. Dometh Graveth's trembling hands may cost her her best builder.

## Social

### Companions

The galley captains of the king's fleet, and the Warden of the Strand who holds the harbor.

### Patrons

Ganoth Ervothandis names her and pays her.

### Enemies

The pirates of the southern salt route, and the Valthari houses that pay the pirates' insurers.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]]**: Brazema holds the office of Master of the Fleet as a Shipmaster.
- **[[affiliation-brazemas|Faith of Brazemas]]**: She is a Bravsar of the god of storms.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Galleys south.** The harbor side of the Long Table demands her galleys against the pirates on the salt route, and Ganoth Ervothandis wants a plan before he orders anything. Brazema asks a party to learn which Valthari houses insure the pirates.
2. **The Graveth hull.** Dometh Graveth's hands have begun to tremble, and the hull she has waited eight years for is not finished. She asks a party to travel to Herthema, to see the keel for themselves and to bring back an honest word.
3. **The night ship.** The warden wants the ship that sells salt outside the bay at night found, and Brazema thinks she knows the hull. She asks a party to row out with her.
