# Appearance {#appearance}

Tsola Galtsuregi is a 33-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of light build. She is slight and quick, brown-haired, with a writing case slung at her hip and ink on the side of her right hand.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tsola Galtsuregi is the castle's notary at [[place-durgalis|Dúrgalis]], a free woman of the valley named by the lord of the castle, [[being-ugarrektsol|Ugarrek Tsoluregi]]. When the [[affiliation-cncldrgls|Council of Dúrgalis]] makes a contract as a whole, the other party and the valley swear it at the stone in the square, and Tsola writes it down, seals it with the castle's seal and reads it out when it falls due.

She keeps the valley's written contracts in a chest in the castle, and the count of the hail's fields at every autumn fair in a book of its own.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tsola is quick, literal and unsentimental. She reads a contract exactly as it was sworn, and she reads it aloud.

### Motivation

She wants every contract the valley makes kept to the word, so that nobody can say Dúrgalis broke one.

### Strengths

She can read Vilérian and Tarveni hands and the lowland houses' ledgers, which nobody else in the valley can.

### Weaknesses

She is young for the post, and the old men of the assembly argue with her readings.

## Social

### Companions

Brazemo Ugarreta, the steward.

### Enemies

Anyone who wants a contract read the way it was not sworn.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The old contract.** The notary's chest holds a contract the valley swore before her time, and a lowland house claims it falls due this autumn. Tsola wants a witness to the swearing found.
2. **The hail book.** Somebody has tried to buy a look at the notary's book of the hail's fields. Tsola wants to know who, and what he means to prove.
