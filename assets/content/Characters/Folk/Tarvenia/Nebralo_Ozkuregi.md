# Appearance {#appearance}

Nebralo Ozkuregi is a 33-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall with a light build. He has olive skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and green eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nebralo Ozkuregi is a knight of the Faith of [[affiliation-ardavon|Árdavon]] in the [Order of the Righteous Path]{.name data-type="affiliation/order"}, sworn to protect the weak and uphold justice. He lodges in [[place-olvestria|Ólvestra]], where two of Árdavon's orders keep their mother-houses, and he rides out from it on the order's writs.

His enmity with [[being-drzhvzhnr|Drazha Vezhnur]] began when Drazha's forces massacred an Árdavonian outpost, and he has sworn to bring him to justice. He is a knight of an old mountain house.

## Psyche

### Personality

Nebralo is grave, courteous and relentless. He keeps his vows aloud.

### Motivation

He wants Drazha Vezhnur brought before a hearing of the Sealed Word and wants the dead of the outpost named.

### Strengths

He is a fighter trained at a mother-house and a witness trusted before an oath hearing.

### Weaknesses

His vow narrows his sight. He has refused two commissions that would have led him to Drazha.

## Social

### Companions

The brothers of the Sealed Word and the Twinblades on station at the gate.

### Patrons

The First Marendos of the Sealed Word.

### Enemies

Drazha Vezhnur and the hill-baronies that shelter his friends.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]]**: Nebralo is a Volsar of the faith and a knight of the order.
- **[[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]**: He holds land of a lord by mounted service.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The hearing.** A brother of the Sealed Word needs riders to bring an oath-breaker to a hearing, and the oath-breaker is a baron with friends in the hills. Nebralo asks a party to ride with them.
2. **The refusal at the gate.** A baron of the hills was refused at the Twin Gate and says Nebralo's order arranged it. Nebralo asks a party to learn who spoke to the gate-guards.
3. **A name on the writ.** A writ bears Nebralo's seal on a date he was elsewhere. He asks a party to find the forger.
