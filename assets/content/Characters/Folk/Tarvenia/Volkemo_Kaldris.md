# Appearance {#appearance}

Volkemo Kaldris is a 52-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall with a medium build. He has olive skin, weathered complexion, dark brown hair, and hazel eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Volkemo Kaldris is the Marshal of Lienrik, the commander of the king's host, who calls the lords' musters and keeps the roll of the fees that owe the crown service. He holds a province of the crown directly and lives in a town house on the streets below the king's hall at [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]].

He has engaged the services of [[being-carengrvth|Kárenna Graveth]] repeatedly in mediating disputes with the neighboring kingdoms. He views her as a valuable ally and, in a relationship carefully concealed, an occasional lover.

## Psyche

### Personality

Volkemo is hearty, suspicious and proud of his records. He counts men aloud.

### Motivation

He wants the host of Lienrik to gather faster than the lords argue. He wants peace with Asturath until the election.

### Strengths

The host is the largest in Tarvénia and he commands it. He has the king's ear.

### Weaknesses

His lords argue precedence before they argue anything else. His secret liaison is a lever.

## Social

### Companions

The Master of the Pleas and the Crown Councilors.

### Patrons

Zenoth Oltris.

### Enemies

The younger lords who want his post.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lienrik]]**: Volkemo holds the office of Marshal of Lienrik at the rung of Greater Nobility.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The muster.** A muster called at the king's order has produced half the men owed. Volkemo asks a party to learn which lords stayed home.
2. **The child.** A noblewoman claims to have borne Volkemo a child by Kárenna Graveth, and he wants a party to learn her purpose.
3. **The marsh toll.** The channel through the salt marsh would bring barges past Bruthena. Volkemo asks a party to learn how the warden of the towers means to answer.
