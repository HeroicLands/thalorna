# Appearance {#appearance}

Uzarne Oldena is a 52-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. She is tall and gray-haired, green-eyed, with a sling wound about her wrist that she can have loaded before a man has drawn.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Uzarne Oldena is an elder of a herding clan of [[place-ibotsain|Ibotsain]] and the Truce-Warden of the [[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]. From the first day of the [[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]]'s sitting at [[place-gaztorai|Gaztorai]] to the last, she answers for the peace of the meadow, and she enforces it on anyone who breaks it, delegate, herald or stranger.

In the free territory a woman holds office outright, and nobody in the meadow mistakes her for anything but the Truce-Warden.

## Psyche

### Personality

Uzarne is level, terse and hard to provoke. She treats the meadow's peace as a thing she carries, and she does not set it down.

### Motivation

She wants every sitting to end with no blood on the grass, whatever is said in it.

### Strengths

The clans respect her peace, and the young men fear her sling.

### Weaknesses

Her peace holds only in the meadow and only while the council sits.

## Social

### Companions

The herdsmen of her clan, who keep the meadow's edge during the sitting.

### Patrons

None; the clans send her.

### Enemies

Anyone who would settle a feud in the meadow with a knife.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskarath]]**: Uzarne holds the office of Truce-Warden, as a Delegate.
- **[[affiliation-biltzarrath|Biltzarrath]]**: She holds the peace of the meadow as Truce-Warden.
- **[[affiliation-eskrthsprts|The Spirits of Eskarath]]**: She keeps the shrine at Ibotsain's spring with her village.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Strangers in the meadow.** A factor of Lyenrik is bringing guards to the solstice sitting. The Truce-Warden wants to know how many and whether they are armed before she lets them past the meadow's edge.
2. **A feud on the road.** Two kindreds meet on the road to Gaztorai every year, and this year one of them has hired a sellsword. Uzarne wants him turned back before the sitting.
