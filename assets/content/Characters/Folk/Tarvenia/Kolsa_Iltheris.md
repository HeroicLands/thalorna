# Appearance {#appearance}

Kolsa Iltheris is a 27-year-old woman who stands 5' 5" tall and is of light build. She has fair, clear skin, brown hair pinned under a clerk's gray cap and grey eyes, and carries a gray satchel in both hands.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kolsa Iltheris is a clerk of the Court of Pleas at [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]], where the plea rolls and the Roll of Houses are kept. She copied the terms of the crown twice in the form the king's clerks wrote them, and when she was asked for a third copy she refused. The Keeper of the Roll of Houses moved her house down a place. She keeps a copy in her own hand and will swear at the oath stone to what it says.

## Psyche

### Personality

Precise, frightened and stubborn in a quiet way.

### Motivation

She wants the High King to hear what the draft changes before the lords vote.

### Strengths

She writes a fair hand and remembers every line she has copied.

### Weaknesses

She has no protector, no weapon and a house that is already paying for her refusal.

## Social

### Companions

None. She travels alone and trusts few.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

The Keeper of the Roll of Houses, and the stewards who carry the fair copies up the plateau road.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Kolsa is a Freeman of Lyenrik and a clerk of its Court of Pleas.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Past the count.** Kolsa asks a party to walk her through the plateau gate before a lord's men reach her.
2. **The satchel.** A lowland steward pays a party to take her copy before she carries it upstairs.
3. **The house on the Roll.** Her house's lost place on the Roll of Houses rests on a record that may not exist, and she asks a party to look for it.
