# Appearance {#appearance}

Zedmo Galtsuregi is a 48-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He has olive skin weathered by the mountains, graying black hair cut short, and dark brown eyes. A pale scar runs across the inside of his left wrist where a crossbow string once took the skin, and two fingers of his right hand do not bend at the last joint. He stands with his hands behind his back and listens with his head a little to one side.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zedmo Galtsuregi is the _thrumar_ (captain) of [[affiliation-goitsathal|Goitsa Thal]], the crossbow company of the valleys above [[place-kavrenath|Kávrenath]] that holds the imperial road-head below the [[place-kervaron|Kervaron Spine]] for the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]. He is a son of an old mountain house of [[place-asturath|Asturath]], and he went down the valley to the company at sixteen, as his father and his grandfather had. He has been its captain for eleven years and has held the road for nine of them.

His company calls him by an earned name, the Reader, for the habit that defines him. He cannot read, and he has had every contract the company has sworn under him read aloud to him at the oath stone until he could say it back. When an imperial officer rides up to the fort with a demand, Zedmo says the relevant clause from memory and asks the officer to show him where the demand is written. Nobody has found it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Zedmo is plain, patient and very hard to move. He says what he means in as few words as will carry it, in the [[lore-tarvenanclt|Tarvenan]] way, and he takes a long answer from anyone else as a sign that something is being hidden. He is courteous to tribunes and to mule-drivers in the same tone, and he does not raise his voice. He laughs rarely and at the expense of people who are not present.

### Motivation

He wants to bring the company home with its name clean and its men paid, and to hand it to a successor with more men than he was given. The contract is the means to all three, so he keeps it exactly. He does not care who rules the empire, and he does care whose name is written in the contract.

### Strengths

He knows his trade: where to put a crossbow on a mountain road, how long a file of twenty can hold a gate, what a mule train needs and what the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan People]] expect of travelers. His men trust him because he has never sworn them to a contract the Named Men opposed. He forgets nothing that has been read to him.

### Weaknesses

He cannot read, and he depends on the purser and the company's clerk for everything written that reaches the fort. His literal reading of the contract makes enemies of men who could have been useful to him, and he does not ask questions whose answers the contract would oblige him to act on.

## Social

### Companions

His companion, [Oltremo Ugarreta]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, rides beside him and holds the company in his absence, and the two have marched together since they were boys. The company's purser and singer are both men of his valley.

### Patrons

The company's paymaster is the empire, through the war council of [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|The Vylarian Military]], and Zedmo treats the war council's clerks with exact courtesy and no warmth. The Warden of the March at Kávrenath, the king of Asturath's officer for the frontier, is the authority he answers to at home.

### Enemies

The legion's officers on the western road dislike him, and the ones whose cohort once held the Step dislike him most. They suspect his company of passing deserters over the mountains.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-goitsathal|Goitsa Thal]]**: Zedmo holds the captaincy as a Named Man of the company, and he has said the contract over at the red stone eleven springs running.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The clause about deserters.** A war council clerk arrives at the fort with a draft of next spring's contract that adds a clause binding the company to arrest deserters on the road. Zedmo will not swear it without the Named Men, and the Named Men will not swear it at all. He asks a party with business in Orzara to find out who on the war council wrote the clause, and why this year.
2. **The name in the contract.** A claimant's agent offers the company a year's pay in coin to hold the western road for his house when the old Orzas dies. Zedmo has the offer read to him three times and asks a party to carry his answer, which is a question about the agent's paymaster that the agent will not want to carry himself.

:::secret {title="What the captain does not ask"}
Zedmo knows that his companion, Oltremo Ugarreta, sells runaway legionaries their passage over the pass in the company's cloaks, and he has never asked how much it pays. The money goes into the roll as gifts from travelers. In his reading the contract does not cover deserters, so their passage is not the company's business, and he has kept himself from knowing enough to be obliged to stop it. The day the legion's officers prove it, he loses either his companion or his contract.

**The man in the company cloak.** A party traveling west over the passes with the company's half going home sees a man in a company cloak who does not speak Tarveni. The legion's officers would pay well to know his name, and the captain would pay more to know who else saw him.
:::
