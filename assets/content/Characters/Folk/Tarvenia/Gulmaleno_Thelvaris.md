# Appearance {#appearance}

Gulmaleno Thelvaris is a 45-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. He has tanned, sun-kissed skin, black hair and dark brown eyes, and a pierced left ear.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Gulmaleno Thelvaris is the First of the Council of [[place-nebralis|Nebralis]], chosen by the aldermen from among themselves, and a merchant-captain whose family has been gathering the harbor trade into its own hands. The harbor is the town's living, and the merchant houses that control it are taking hold of the council. His is the loudest of those houses and the quietest in its methods.

He sees the priestess [[being-alethraornet|Alethra Ornetis]] and the Freemen's Assembly as obstacles to the orderly town he intends to build, with formal magistrates in place of mediation and trained clergy in place of a priestess who works in the fields and on the boats. He has not asked the council to move against her, and he does not need to.

## Psyche

### Personality

Gulmaleno is affable, patient and thorough. He remembers a name, a debt and a grievance, and he is never in a hurry to collect any of them.

### Motivation

He wants Nebralis made orderly and accountable, which means ruled by the houses that pay for it. He believes the town's wealth will outgrow its assembly.

### Strengths

He holds the notes of half the harbor and the loyalty of the aldermen who owe him. He is the best-informed man in the council about what is coming over the quays.

### Weaknesses

He underestimates Alethra's standing with the fishing families and the assembly, and he cannot move openly against her without losing the farms above the town.

## Social

### Companions

The merchant aldermen of the harbor houses, and the ship-victualers who supply his vessels.

### Patrons

The merchant houses that control the harbor trade.

### Enemies

Alethra Ornetis, whose standing stands in the way of the changes he wants, and Dorándaro Orthemandis, who speaks for the common fishermen.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-cnclnbrls|Council of Nebralis]]**: Gulmaleno holds the office of First of the Council as an Alderman.
- **[[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pozeras]]**: He gives to the temple of the road for his ships' sake and attends no rite.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The bishop's return.** Bishop Ornetho Iltheris is coming back to Nebralis with orders to reform the temple's work. Gulmaleno asks a party to carry his compliments to the bishop and to learn what funds the bishop expects the council to redirect.
2. **The cut trace.** The best team of the teamster Estara Sánriseth bolted on the quay road a month before the guild's vote. Gulmaleno says he knows nothing of it and offers a party a purse to find the hand that cut the trace and to keep the name from the guild.
3. **The lord's steward.** The steward of the manor beside Alvarik Árenthar's land has bought the farmer's notes from the town's moneylender. Gulmaleno holds the moneylender's own notes and asks a party to deliver a message to the steward that is not in his name.
