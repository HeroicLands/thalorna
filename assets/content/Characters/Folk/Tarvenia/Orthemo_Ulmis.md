# Appearance {#appearance}

Orthemo Ulmis is a 51-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of heavy build. His olive skin is weathered from the tower tops, his hair is gray and cut short, and his brown eyes are patient. The forefinger of his right hand is stained with ink, because he keeps the toll roll himself.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Orthemo Ulmis is the Warden of the Towers, the officer of the [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]] who commands the chain of watchtowers down the [Thalsira]{.name data-type="place/feature"} from [[place-ronetho|Ornetho]] to the sea. He is castellan of [[place-bruthena|Bruthena]], keeps its bridge and its toll, and holds the town court in the castle hall. He is a landed lord on the Roll of Houses, and he has held the wardenship for twelve years.

The toll is the reason the castle stands, and it is his work. The merchant houses of [[place-valthari|Valthari]] pay it on most of what they buy in Lyenrik, and every few years one of them proposes a channel through the salt marsh that would carry barges to the port without passing the bridge. Orthemo writes the king's refusal each time.

## Psyche

### Personality

Orthemo is careful, exact and slow to anger. He counts what crosses the bridge himself on market days and believes the count more than any report.

### Motivation

He wants the toll paid in full, the beacons kept lit, and the marsh channels closed to anyone who goes round the bridge.

### Strengths

He knows the bridge's trade by the load and the marsh by the season, and the towerkeepers of the chain answer to him.

### Weaknesses

The towerkeepers hold their towers by inheritance and he cannot remove them. The marsh people know the channels better than his boatmen do.

## Social

### Companions

The towerkeeping families of the lower river, who keep the beacons and take the light-due.

### Patrons

King [[being-zenotholtr|Zenoth Oltris]], who keeps the toll at Bruthena as one of the crown's rights over Valthari's trade.

### Enemies

The Valthari houses that propose the channel, and the smugglers who move goods through the reed channels to avoid the bridge.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Orthemo holds the office of Warden of the Towers, as a Landed Lord.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A fire on the shore.** A fire was lit on the shingle after dark, and the castle's men found nobody beside it. The Warden wants to know who lit it and who was meant to see it.
2. **The channel survey.** Men with poles and lines have been seen on Valthari's side of the marsh, and the Warden wants to know whether a house is measuring for its channel again.
3. **The short count.** The toll roll and the warehouse keepers' tallies disagree by a few loads a month, and the Warden wants the difference found before the king's clerks find it.

:::secret {title="The towers that are paid twice"}
Some towerkeeping families of the lower river take money from the marsh smugglers and keep a lamp dark or lit on an agreed night to signal that the castle's boats are out. Orthemo suspects one family. There are three.
:::
