# Appearance {#appearance}

Tholmar Volkemis is a 48-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build. He is soft-spoken and round-shouldered from the desk, his brown hair going gray, and the cuffs of his good coat are stained with ink he does not notice.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tholmar Volkemis is the Keeper of the Roll of Houses, the written order of precedence of every noble house of [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Lyenrik]], which is kept in the lower chamber of the king's hall at [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]]. A house that thinks it deserves a higher place brings its claim to him with its records, a charter, a grant or a judgment of the old imperial courts, and he rules on what the records show. He is a gentleman of a minor house that has never risen far on the Roll it keeps.

When the clerk [[being-kolsailthe|Kolsa Iltheris]] copied a draft of the terms of the crown twice for the king's clerks and refused a third copy, Tholmar moved her house down a place on the Roll. The ruling stands on the record, and he says so.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tholmar is careful, courteous and unbending on a record. He believes that a house's place is a fact the records establish, and he dislikes being asked what the king would like the records to say.

### Motivation

He wants the Roll to be the one thing in Lyenrik that nobody can buy, and he wants the king to think of him as the man who keeps it so.

### Strengths

He knows the records behind every place on the Roll and the weak point in most of them.

### Weaknesses

His rulings serve the crown more often than he admits, and every house he moves down has friends at court. A forged record that he has accepted would ruin him.

## Social

### Companions

The clerks of the Court of Pleas and the adept who tests old records for the court.

### Patrons

King Zenoth Oltris, who names the Keeper.

### Enemies

Houses passed over on the Roll, and Kolsa Iltheris, who means to swear against the draft she copied.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Tholmar holds the office of Keeper of the Roll of Houses, as Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The clerk in the hills.** The Keeper wants Kolsa Iltheris found before she reaches Lezkora, and he says he wants only to give her the chance to appeal her house's place.
2. **A record that may not exist.** A house claims a higher place on a grant of the imperial courts that nobody else has seen. The Keeper wants the grant's maker found.
