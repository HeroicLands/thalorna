# Appearance {#appearance}

Ulma Ozkuregi is a 44-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build. She is spare and gray-eyed, her dark hair tied back from the forge heat, and there is always a smudge of charcoal somewhere on her face.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ulma Ozkuregi is the Warden of the Iron, the knight who keeps the king of [[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Asturath]]'s right in the iron hill at [[place-ardalis|Ardalis]]. She lives in the stone tower above the main forge, counts every smelting and takes the crown's bloom in ten. A matter the miners' and smiths' assembly cannot settle comes up the road to her.

The merchants of Lyenrik offer more for raw iron than the forges pay, and this year one mining family deep in debt has been offered a price no forge can match. The town's oldest rule holds only while every family keeps it, and Ulma has begun counting the blooms twice.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ulma is quiet, exact and stubborn. She trusts a count she has made herself and nothing else.

### Motivation

She wants the king's tenth and the town's rule kept, and she wants to know where the iron the forges did not make is going.

### Strengths

She knows every forge on the stream and every gallery in the hill, and the assembly accepts her judgment.

### Weaknesses

She has a tower and a few sworn men, and the iron goes out in a hundred carts a season up and down a road the whole kingdom uses.

## Social

### Companions

The smiths of the main forge and the priests of the Sacred Forge's temple.

### Patrons

King Alzemo Zolduregi, who holds the iron hill.

### Enemies

Whoever is buying raw iron from the indebted family.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]]**: Ulma holds the office of Warden of the Iron, as a Knight.
- **[[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]]**: She keeps the smiths' faith whose temple stands at the head of the town.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The second count.** The Warden of the Iron is counting the blooms twice, and nobody in Ardalis can say whom the second count is for. She wants a stranger to watch the road carts for a week.
2. **The debt.** A mining family is about to break the town's oldest rule to pay a lowland debt. Ulma wants the debt's holder named before the family sells.
