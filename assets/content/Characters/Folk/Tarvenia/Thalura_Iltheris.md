# Appearance {#appearance}

Thalura Iltheris is a 47-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She is a slim, dark woman with amber eyes and close-cut black hair, and the tips of her fingers are stained gray from the washes she uses on old ink.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Thalura Iltheris is an adept of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]], licensed to practice and one of the few members of its small chapter at [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]]. She was born in the town, the daughter of a clerk of the Court of Pleas, and she went to the Arcane Order's academy on the strength of a hand that could copy anything.

Her craft is records. The [Court of Pleas]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} and the Keeper of the Roll of Houses, [[being-tholmarvolk|Tholmar Volkemis]], send her the charters, grants and old imperial judgments that houses bring to support their claims, and she tells them whether the ink is as old as the date, whether the hand is the clerk's it claims, and whether the seal was cut by the man whose seal it is. A Tarvenan distrusts a mage who throws his weight around, and Thalura throws none. The lowland court tolerates her because she works for its clerks and for no lord.

She has asked the Castellan of Ornetho, [[being-hiltheroltr|Hilther Oltris]], for leave to see the imperial registers in the vaults under the old barracks, and he has refused her twice.

## Psyche

### Personality

Thalura is cool, exact and quietly proud. She says only what her tests show and refuses to guess aloud.

### Motivation

She wants to read the imperial archive at Ornetho before anyone else does, and she wants the Order's chapter at Torvaleth to outlast the kingdom's suspicion of it.

### Strengths

She can tell a forged record from a true one more surely than any clerk in Tarvénia, and the Court of Pleas relies on her for it.

### Weaknesses

Her place depends on the court's tolerance and the Keeper's patronage, and a lord who loses a claim on her word blames the mage, not the record.

## Social

### Companions

The other adepts of the chapter and the clerks of the plea rolls.

### Patrons

Tholmar Volkemis, Keeper of the Roll of Houses, who sends her most of her work.

### Enemies

Houses whose claims her tests have ruined; the castellan, who keeps the vaults locked against her.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]**: Thalura is a licensed adept of the Order's chapter at Torvaleth.
- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: She is a freewoman of the kingdom.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A grant she cannot place.** A house's claim on the Roll of Houses stands on an imperial grant whose ink is old and whose seal she cannot match to any clerk of the march. Thalura wants the seal's maker found among the records of the old courts.
2. **What she will say.** A house whose claim she is testing pays a party to learn what her answer will be before she gives it to the Keeper.
3. **The vault.** The adept wants a letter carried to Ornetho and placed in the castellan's own hand, asking a third time to see the imperial registers.

:::secret {title="The page from the vaults"}
A page of an imperial register came to Thalura a year ago through a clerk who would not give his name, and her tests show it genuine. It is part of the list of the Liénriki lords who took the Empire's offices during the occupation, and the house of a sitting Crown Councilor is on it. She has told nobody, and she keeps the page sewn inside the binding of an old plea roll in the chapter's library. Whoever stole it from the vaults at Ornetho knows the rest of the archive survives.
:::

:::secret {title="The record she passed"}
Three years ago Thalura passed a charter for the Keeper of the Roll that she suspected was copied, because its maker had a hand she could not prove false and the Keeper wanted it passed. The house it raised sits higher on the Roll for it. If the charter is ever proved forged, her license and the Keeper's office fall together, and she knows the Keeper knows it.
:::
