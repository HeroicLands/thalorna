# Appearance {#appearance}

Oltrema Lizelis is a 58-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall with a light build. She has medium skin, weathered complexion, dark brown hair, and amber eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Oltrema Lizelis is a wealthy widow of [[place-valthari|Valthari]] who heads her late husband's house and commissions major pieces for her private manor. She sits among the House Heads without a seat on the Konsezarath, and her taste is the talk of the quay streets.

She appreciates the originality of the glassworker [[being-eldiragarrun|Eldira Garrundeta]] and pays premium prices for her work. Her patronage makes a rival of every glassworker who wanted the commissions.

## Psyche

### Personality

Oltrema is languid, amused and unexpectedly firm. She says what she will pay once.

### Motivation

She wants her manor to hold things no other house in Valthari has. She wants her husband's house to outlive her.

### Strengths

She has a collector's eye and a head of house's purse. The guilds of the city compete for her notice.

### Weaknesses

Her patronage can turn on a whim. The councilors have not forgiven her husband's debts.

## Social

### Companions

A secretary who keeps her catalogue.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

The glassworkers of Herthema who envy Eldira's commissions.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]**: Oltrema is a House Head.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The commission.** Oltrema wants a window made for her manor in a style no guild has attempted. She asks a party to find Eldira Garrundeta and bring her an offer.
2. **The seamen's refusal.** The seamen of Valthari refuse to sign until the owners raise the crew's share. Oltrema, who owns a share in a ship, asks a party to learn which houses mean to break it.
3. **The stolen piece.** A piece in her collection has been replaced with a copy. She asks a party to find the thief without alerting her household.
