# Appearance {#appearance}

Brazemo Tholmaris is a 48-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He has tanned, sun-kissed skin, graying brown hair and hazel eyes, and he wears the good wool and silver clasp of a merchant of the council.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Brazemo Tholmaris is the First of the Council of [[place-herthema|Herthema]], chosen by the [[affiliation-cnclhrthm|council]] from among its merchants and master craftsmen to preside over its sittings and speak for the town. He deals in wine and in the jars that carry it, and he sits between the glass and the vintage.

The quarrel of the moment is whether the old standard should give way to the new, and it divides his own council. The reform faction among the merchants wants shipbuilding standardized and the shipwright [[being-domthgrvth|Dometh Graveth]] discredited. The younger glassworkers make figured glass for the merchants' houses. The priest [[being-gaimelkorvat|Gaimel Korvathandis]] is agitating for stricter oversight of the temple of Ólvenia.

## Psyche

### Personality

Brazemo is courteous, tidy and careful with his words. He gives the impression of agreeing with whoever spoke last and decides in private.

### Motivation

He wants Herthema's council to keep its name for sound ware and sound contracts and to keep its wine trade from being bought by a single house. He prefers a slow compromise to a swift victory.

### Strengths

He is trusted by the old masters and the new money alike to keep a fair account. He knows the sellswords' roll and the council's debts.

### Weaknesses

He avoids deciding a matter that his own interests touch, and his habit of delay has been taken for weakness by the reform faction.

## Social

### Companions

The Keeper of the Roll and the council's other merchants and master craftsmen.

### Patrons

The wine merchants who need ware that holds.

### Enemies

The faction that wants Dometh Graveth discredited and holds Brazemo's delay against him.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-cnclhrthm|Council of Herthema]]**: Brazemo holds the office of First of the Council as a Councilor.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The figured glass.** A wine merchant wants to know whether the Provénzian-trained glassworker's figured ware holds as well as Velmar Gútharen's plain ware before moving his vintage into it. Brazemo asks a party to carry both down a bad road and see which arrives whole.
2. **The reform faction.** The merchants who want Dometh Graveth discredited are gathering stories of Graveth hulls lost at sea. Brazemo asks a party to learn whether the wrecks were what the stories say.
3. **The oversight.** Gaimel Korvathandis wants witnesses to what Pálina Sánriseth does among the poor, and the council has been asked to name them. Brazemo asks a party to watch the priest instead.
