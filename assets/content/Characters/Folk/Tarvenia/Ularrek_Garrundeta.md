# Appearance {#appearance}

Ularrek Garrundeta is a 68-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of light build. His tanned face is weathered and deeply lined, his hair is white, and his grey eyes are steady. The left shoulder sits a little lower than the right, where a collarbone broke long ago and healed short. He wears a mountain lord's plain wool and carries no weapon in the hall.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ularrek Garrundeta is the High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]], an old lord of Asturath's high valleys who holds his valley of his own right and brings his own banner to the ban. The electors chose him about 709 AF because each of the greater candidates could block the others, and he knows it ([[lore-ularrekelect|The Election of Ularrek]]). He swore the terms of the crown at the oath stone of [[place-lezkorahall|the High Hall]]: to call the ban only against an enemy from outside Tarvénia, to judge between the lords who bring him their quarrels, to keep the charter of Valthari and the old arrangement with Eskarath, and to take nothing from any lord's land that the terms do not allow.

He holds his court in the High Hall at [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] as the guest of the king of Asturath, [[being-alzemozold|Alzemo Zolduregi]], passes through the king's gate and eats bread carried up the king's road. The Garrundeta house is one of the king's own mountain lords, so the High King lives in his king's city and owes that king his courtesy. He has not come down to the hall for a month.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ularrek is slow, dry and exact. He answers a question with the shortest true sentence he has and lets the silence do the rest, and he treats a lord who talks a long time as a lord who is bargaining. He is courteous to Alzemo without warmth and to the lowland lords without trust.

### Motivation

He wants to die with the terms of the crown kept and the hearth lit, and to see a successor elected who will not sell the mountains to the lowlands or the charter to the highest bidder. He does not want to be remembered as the king who was chosen because no one else could win.

### Strengths

He holds the respect of the mountain lords, who know his house and know that a Garrundeta keeps an oath. He remembers every quarrel that has been brought to the hall and how it ended, and he has judged between lords for decades without being found partial.

### Weaknesses

He is old and failing, and the whole of Tarvénia counts the days. He has no standing army, no bureaucracy and almost no revenue beyond his own valley's, and he cannot compel any lord who chooses to stay at home.

## Social

### Companions

The Keeper of the Hall lives in the High Hall and keeps its benches, its roof and its hearth for him. The crown's heralds carry his word to the lords, and the brothers of the [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|Order of the Sealed Word]] write his judgments in the hall's roll.

### Patrons

Alzemo Zolduregi shelters the court and keeps the gate. The High King owes the king of Asturath his seat's comfort and his security, and neither of them mentions the debt.

### Enemies

Zenoth Oltris, the king of Lyenrik, means to stand at the next election and is sending lowland lords up the road to Lezkora one and two at a time. The greater candidates who lost to Ularrek remember that he won only by blocking.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]**: Ularrek holds the crown as High King, elected by the lords who bring their banners to the ban.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Asturath]]**: He is a mountain lord of Asturath, with a valley of his own and a seat in the council of mountain lords.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The month upstairs.** The High King has not come down to the hall for a month. A herald of the crown asks a party to learn who has gone through his door in that time, because the Warden of the Plateau Gate counts the lords' men and nobody counts the king's visitors.
2. **A quarrel only he can hear.** A man waiting in the yard between the gates swears he has a quarrel with a lord that only the High King can hear, and the lord is already in the city. The man wants a party to get him past the king of Asturath's gate-wardens before the lord does.
3. **The tribute and the hearth.** A councilor of Valthari has come up to hear the terms read and to learn whether the old man will live to take the year's tribute. The Konsezarath is divided over what to send up the road, and the councilor will pay a party for a plain account of the High King's health.
