# Appearance {#appearance}

Itsurre Kanarris is a 40-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. She is freckled and fair, with steady blue eyes, and she wears the great seal on a chain under her collar, out of sight.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Itsurre Kanarris is the Keeper of the Seal of the [[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]], a knight of the king's choosing who keeps the great seal in the castle above the harbor of [[place-durvolka|Durvolka]]. Every writ, charter and grant of lordship the king issues passes under it, and she holds the post at the king's pleasure.

King [[being-ganothervo|Ganoth Ervothandis]] uses her for the errands a careful king will not be seen to give. When he wants the night ship outside the bay named before the Long Table sits, it is the Keeper of the Seal who asks.

## Psyche

### Personality

Itsurre is composed, discreet and patient. She says less than she knows and writes down less than she says.

### Motivation

She wants the king's writ to run on the coast as it runs in the valleys, and she wants the crown solvent.

### Strengths

The king trusts her, and nothing he signs reaches the coast without passing her desk.

### Weaknesses

She holds the post at the king's pleasure and has no lordship to fall back on. The harbor side sees her as the countinghouse king's countinghouse.

## Social

### Companions

The clerks of the seal and the Master of the Fleet, whom she likes.

### Patrons

King Ganoth Ervothandis.

### Enemies

Whoever on the Strand is buying salt past the crown.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]]**: Itsurre holds the office of Keeper of the Seal, as a Knight.
- **[[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pozeras]]**: She keeps the faith of voyages, as the coast does.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The night ship.** On the king's word, the Keeper of the Seal asks a party to name the ship that sells salt outside the bay before the Long Table sits.
2. **A grant under seal.** A grant of lordship bearing the great seal has turned up that Itsurre never sealed. She wants to know who copied the seal before the king hears of it.
