# Appearance {#appearance}

Dorándara Hiltheris is a 57-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of light build. Her hair is white and pinned close, her gray eyes are quick behind the crystal spectacles she wears on the bench, and her hands are never without a roll.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Dorándara Hiltheris is the Master of the Pleas of the [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]], the great lord who presides over the [Court of Pleas]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} in the lower chamber of the king's hall at [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]]. The court hears the cases between the lords of the kingdom and every appeal from a bailiff's court, and she keeps its plea rolls, which hold every case the kingdom has heard since it was founded. She sits in King [[being-zenotholtr|Zenoth Oltris]]'s council with the Marshal and the Crown Councilors.

She has held the office through two reigns and knows the rolls as well as any _lozo_ (advocate) who lives on searching them. The heir, [[being-zedmooltris|Zedmo Oltris]], sits on the bench below hers.

## Psyche

### Personality

Dorándara is exact, patient and dry. She lets an advocate finish and then asks the one question his case cannot answer.

### Motivation

She wants the rolls kept whole and the court's word to hold above any lord's. She regards a judgment that can be bought as a crack in the kingdom.

### Strengths

Her memory of the rolls, and the habit of the lords of bringing their quarrels to her court before they bring them to the field.

### Weaknesses

She is old, and the lords who lost before her are counting the years. Her court holds a record above an oath, which the mountain lords count against Lyenrik.

## Social

### Companions

The clerks of the plea rolls and the advocates of Torvaleth, whom she trusts in that order.

### Patrons

King Zenoth Oltris.

### Enemies

Lords whose cases she has decided, and advocates whose searches she has made worthless.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Dorándara holds the office of Master of the Pleas, as a Great Lord.
- **[[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]]**: She swears the court's oaths at the god's shrine in the hall.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The missing roll.** A plea roll from the first years of the kingdom is missing from its chest, and the Master of the Pleas wants it back before anyone learns which roll it is.
2. **The bought advocate.** An advocate of Torvaleth is selling the court's draft judgments before they are given. The Master wants him caught with one in his hand.
