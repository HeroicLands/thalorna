# Appearance {#appearance}

Zekudi Betsuris is a 59-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of medium build. He is an old, slow man with white hair and pale blue eyes, and the palms of his hands are scarred from the ropes of the cliff path.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zekudi Betsuris is the grave-keeper of the passage graves inside the stone ring at the northern end of the cliffs above [[place-ezkoba|Ezkoba]], one of the Shore Kin of the [[affiliation-drownedroad|Drowned Road]]. He keeps the graves' door and the [[being-lrgspdr|spider]] that lives in it, and every drowned man the sea gives back along [[place-kaurema|Kaurema]] he carries in along the cliff path, whatever harbor the man sailed from.

He has carried more of the cliffs' dead than anyone living, and two of them this autumn came off boats that steered onto the reefs by a light that was not the Long Lamp.

## Psyche

### Personality

Zekudi is gentle, silent and stubborn. He talks to the spider and to the dead and seldom to anyone else.

### Motivation

He wants every drowned man the sea gives back brought home to the ring, and nobody's pockets emptied on the way.

### Strengths

He knows the cliff path in fog and the coves where the current lands what the reefs take.

### Weaknesses

He is old, and the path is long. Wreckers reach the coves before he does.

## Social

### Companions

The keepers of the Long Lamp and the teller of Ezkoba.

### Patrons

Nelma Ularris, the teller, whose roll he serves.

### Enemies

The wreckers who strip the drowned before he reaches them.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-drownedroad|The Drowned Road]]**: Zekudi holds the office of Grave-Keeper, as one of the Shore Kin.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]]**: He is a freeman of the kingdom.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The emptied pockets.** The last two drowned men Zekudi carried in had nothing on them, not a ring or a knife. He wants to know who reached them first.
2. **The spider's door.** Someone has opened the door of the passage grave at night and closed it badly. The grave-keeper wants a watch kept on the ring.
