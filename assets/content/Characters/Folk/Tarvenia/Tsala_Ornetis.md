# Appearance {#appearance}

Tsala Ornetis is a 50-year-old woman who stands 5' 7" tall and is of medium build. She has tanned, weathered skin, graying brown hair tied back and blue eyes, and the loud, easy bearing of a woman used to giving orders over wind.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tsala Ornetis is a shipmaster of the roll of Durvolka. She lost two boats and their crews on the Far Grounds this year, and the harbor side of the Long Table has told her she should sit among those who set the crown's price for salt. She stands in the autumn election of the Masters' Hall against the king's own candidate. On the eve of the vote the herring crews have silver, and she has not asked where it comes from.

## Psyche

### Personality

Loud, sure of her case and sincerely grieved by the boats she lost.

### Motivation

She wants a lower price for salt, a lighter due on the catch and galleys sent against the pirates on the southern route.

### Strengths

The crews trust her because she sailed with them and lost men with them.

### Weaknesses

She does not know whose silver is buying her votes.

## Social

### Companions

A factor of a Valthari house, whom she believes to be a friend of the herring crews.
