# Appearance {#appearance}

Tholma Ozkuregi is a 69-year-old woman who stands 5'3" tall and is very thin. She has tanned, weathered and leathery skin, white hair cropped short and gray eyes, the left of them clouded. She walks the saddle without a staff.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tholma Ozkuregi lives on [[place-zurgando|Zurgando]], the high pass at the head of the valleys above [[place-kavrenath|Kávrenath]], in the shepherds' stone hut on the south side of the saddle. She restacks the cairns when the wind takes them down, puts a stone back on whichever cairn is nearest, and keeps firewood in both huts for whoever has to wait the fog out. The valleys bring her bread in autumn and she does not say thank you.

She was born in the valleys, the daughter of a house with a row of stones in its wall beside the door, and she keeps the Mother in the old way. The cave where the Mother is kept lies above the highest house, where the dead road turns up toward the saddle. Tholma knows the topmost cairn and whose stones are on it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tholma is terse, unsentimental and shockingly kind in practice. She does not argue, does not lie and does not answer a question she has not been asked.

### Motivation

She wants the line of cairns kept so that nobody leaves it in the fog and nobody is lost between one stone and the next. She wants her own stone, set when she goes down, to be placed by someone who does not ask for the names on the top cairn.

### Strengths

She walks the saddle in any weather, reads fog and snow, sets bones and knows which stones are owed. Companies pay her in silence and she keeps theirs.

### Weaknesses

She is old and alone and refuses help. Her one good eye is failing.

## Social

### Companions

A shepherd boy from the highest house brings her news, and the village wise-woman visits at midsummer.

### Patrons

The valleys, who feed her and ask nothing.

### Enemies

Smugglers who use the pass to avoid the fortress at Kávrenath and do not respect the cairns.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-menutsa|The Mother]]**: Tholma is a Householder of the Mother's faith and keeps her in the old way.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The man in the fog.** A man has come over Zurgando in the autumn fog without a company and without a stone, and Tholma has put him up in the north hut. She asks a party to take him down to Kávrenath, because the Warden's clerk is looking for him.
2. **The cairns grow.** The topmost cairn has grown by a stone nobody carried up in a company's season. Tholma asks a party to find who has been setting stones on the captain's cairn in the dark.
3. **A stone for a name.** A captain's widow asks to be shown the stone her husband left on the cairn, and Tholma knows he did not leave it on the road home. She asks a party to take the widow down, and say nothing of the stone.

:::secret {title="The stones that are never taken"}
Tholma knows which stones on the topmost cairn belong to captains who broke a contract abroad and were struck from every roll in the west. Some of those captains come home over the pass at night and in the fog, and she has fed them and said nothing for years. She will not name a stone to a party, whatever the party pays, and she would rather die on the saddle than be made to.
:::
