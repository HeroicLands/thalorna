# Appearance {#appearance}

Volkemo Hiltheris is a 56-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is heavily built, with a smith's shoulders and forearms. He has olive, weathered skin marked across both forearms with old burns, gray hair and a gray beard singed short at the edges, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Volkemo Hiltheris is the Hammer-Priest of [[place-ithrakemtho|Ithrakemtho]], the upper house of [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] on [[place-thumbalero|Thumbalero]], the pilgrims' path to [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]]. He came to the house from the temple on Lezkora's square thirty years ago and has kept it since: the forge, the lamp in the wall, the bench for pilgrims and the house's book. He works the anvil every afternoon, as a priest of the faith should, and the nails and mule-shoes of the path are his.

The path is busier this spring than he has known it, with young men in pairs in pilgrims' gray who carry no ember pot and do not know the words at his fire. He has begun writing them in the house's book, two by two, with the day each pair passed. He knows that the lords of the lowlands are sending men up to Lezkora one and two at a time before an election, and that a lord may bring only twelve sworn men past the gate. He has told nobody what his book adds up to.

## Psyche

### Personality

Volkemo is slow, warm and stubborn. He speaks to a pilgrim the way he speaks to iron, without hurry and without letting go.

### Motivation

He wants the house's fire and its door kept as they have always been kept, and he wants no pilgrim to come to harm on his stretch of the path.

### Strengths

He is a good smith and a patient priest, he knows every stone of the traverse, and pilgrims tell him things they tell nobody else.

### Weaknesses

He does not want the faith in a lords' quarrel, and he will put off a choice for as long as the choice lets him.

## Social
