# Appearance {#appearance}

Oztuk Iltheris is a 62-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. He has pale, wrinkled skin, white hair and grey eyes. He wears the deep blue surcoat of the order with the Árdavon sigil in gold at the breast and a small bronze key at the throat.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Oztuk Iltheris is the First Marendos of the [[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|Order of the Sealed Word]], the order's chair, chosen by its conclave. The order keeps its mother-house in [[place-olvestria|Ólvestra]], where its archive holds the master register of every contested oath it has ruled on in its three centuries. He wears the bronze key of the Senior Brothers, for Árdavon's gates and for the keys of the archive.

He presides over the order in a period of increasing tension between the order and several of the Aureldian crowns. The order works under each crown's license, and the renewal has become a political question, since its rulings against powerful nobles make some crowns reluctant to confirm it. Seven years ago the Ólvestran Marendos died three days before he was to issue a major writ against a baron of the hills.

## Psyche

### Personality

Oztuk is formal, thin-voiced and unfailingly precise. He is gentle in manner and unyielding in doctrine, and he treats an oath, a seal and a key as the same kind of object.

### Motivation

He wants the order's licenses renewed without bending its rulings, and he wants the death of the Ólvestran Marendos either explained or buried. He has not decided which he fears more.

### Strengths

He holds the order's archive in his head and the Council of the Seal's twelve Senior Brothers in his hand. No crown can easily refuse a petition he has examined for substance.

### Weaknesses

He is old and hopes to die before the crowns decide. His discretion has turned into secrecy about the Marendos's death.

## Social

### Companions

The Council of the Seal, twelve Senior Brothers drawn from the five kingdoms, meets in Ólvestra every quarter. His secretary is a young Sworn Brother of the Seal.

### Patrons

None; the order answers to the license each crown grants it.

### Enemies

The hill-barons around Ólvestra who shelter the Black Flame and the Ashen Vow's Ólvestran Cell.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ordrsldwrd|Order of the Sealed Word]]**: Oztuk is a Senior Brother and holds the office of First Marendos.
- **[[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]]**: He is a Sentan of the faith, as the order's chair.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The hearing.** A brother of the Sealed Word needs riders to bring an oath-breaker to a hearing, and the oath-breaker is a baron with friends in the hills, among them the Ólvestran Cell. Oztuk asks a party to ride with the brother.
2. **The Marendos's death.** The Ólvestran Marendos died seven years ago, three days before he was to issue his writ. His death was ruled natural. Oztuk asks a party to learn who was at his table that week, and tells them not to carry what they learn out of the city.
3. **The licenses.** The crown of one kingdom is hesitating to confirm the order's license for another term. Oztuk offers a party the chance to learn which councilor of that crown has been advising delay.
