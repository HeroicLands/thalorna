# Appearance {#appearance}

Lizela Volkemis is a 46-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of medium build. She is a square, gray-eyed woman with dark hair under a fisherwoman's shawl, and she speaks the names on her roll in a low, carrying voice.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lizela Volkemis is the harbor teller of [[place-durvolka|Durvolka]] for the [[affiliation-drownedroad|Drowned Road]]. She lives in the last house below the passage grave at the top of the Lantern Stair, keeps the roll of the harbor's drowned there, says each drowned name aloud at the door of the grave and calls in a boat that has been out a year and a day. The courts of [[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Galvaren]] take her calling-in as proof that a man is dead.

The teller of Ezkoba, [[being-nelmaular|Nelma Ularris]], is failing and has named no successor. The tellers of the other harbors are taking sides, and Lizela has not said which side she takes.

## Psyche

### Personality

Lizela is calm, private and unshakable on a date. She will not call a boat in a day early for anyone.

### Motivation

She wants the rolls kept true and the Teller of Ezkoba's successor chosen by the tellers, not by a court or a curing house.

### Strengths

The harbor trusts her roll, and her calling-in settles a widow's claim in the king's courts.

### Weaknesses

The Warden of Wrecks wants crews counted dead sooner than she will count them, and the families who lied to her about their men know she knows.

## Social

### Companions

The Shore Kin of the Lantern Stair, who set their lamps in the seaward windows.

### Patrons

Nelma Ularris, the senior teller.

### Enemies

Tharozo Ulmis, the Warden of Wrecks, who quarrels with every teller.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-drownedroad|The Drowned Road]]**: Lizela holds the office of Harbor Teller, as a Teller.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]]**: She is a freeman of the kingdom.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A year and a day.** A Durvolka boat comes due to be called in, and its master's wife swears she saw him on the Strand in the fog. The harbor teller wants to know whether the master is drowned or hiding.
2. **Nelma's successor.** Lizela wants a letter carried up the cliff path to Ezkoba and an answer brought back, and she does not want the other tellers to know she sent it.
