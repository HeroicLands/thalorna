# Appearance {#appearance}

Garrunda Iltheris is a 42-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of medium build. She is neat and dark, with amber eyes and small burn scars on her forearms from the melting room.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Garrunda Iltheris is the Master of the Mint of the [[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]], a freeman of the city named by the [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]]. She keeps the coin struck in the city's name and the standard it is struck to, and she trained as a silversmith before the council took her from the bench.

Valthari coin carries the city's name abroad, and the houses that pay the herring crews of Durvolka pay them in it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Garrunda is exacting, private and proud of her standard. She weighs every tenth coin herself.

### Motivation

She wants Valthari coin to be the coin no merchant tests.

### Strengths

She can tell the city's coin from a good copy by its ring and its edge.

### Weaknesses

She cannot say where a coin goes once it leaves the mint, and the council does not ask her.

## Social

### Companions

The moneyers and assayers of the mint, and the Warden of the Weights.

### Patrons

The Konsezarath, which names her.

### Enemies

Clippers and coiners, and any house that pays in debased silver.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]**: Garrunda holds the office of Master of the Mint, as a Freeman of the City.
- **[[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]]**: She keeps the smiths' faith of the hearth.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The new strike.** A purse of fresh Valthari coin from a strike the mint has not released has turned up on the coast. The Master of the Mint wants to know who took it out.
2. **The light coin.** Light coin with the city's name is passing in the hiring yards. Garrunda wants the die found before the companies refuse the city's silver.
