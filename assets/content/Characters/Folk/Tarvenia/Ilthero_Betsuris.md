# Appearance {#appearance}

Ilthero Betsuris is a 54-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. He is a soft-spoken gray man in very good wool, with a moneylender's ring cut with a balance on his right hand.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ilthero Betsuris heads one of the great moneylending houses of [[place-olvestria|Ólvestra]] and is the First of the [[affiliation-cnclolvstr|Council of Ólvestra]], the chartered council of the city's merchant and moneylending houses that governs it under the charter of the High King of [[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Tarvénia]]. He presides over the council, keeps the city's charter and seal, and answers to the High King for the city's yearly due.

The imperial treasury has suspended one of the city's major houses, and the notes other houses cashed against it cannot be redeemed. The council must decide how much of that loss the city carries, and Ilthero's own house holds some of the paper.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ilthero is courteous, patient and unreadable. He has never been seen to hurry, and he lends to everyone at a rate that says exactly what he thinks of them.

### Motivation

He wants the city's credit kept whole through the suspension and the city's charter renewed by the next High King on its present terms.

### Strengths

He knows what every house in Ólvestra owes and to whom, and the council follows his reckonings.

### Weaknesses

His own house is exposed to the suspended house's paper, and the council knows it. The hill-baronies around the city do as they please, and the charter gives him no power over them.

## Social

### Companions

The heads of the city's great houses and the Danvar's chapterhouse.

### Patrons

The High King, Ularrek Garrundeta, under whose charter the council governs.

### Enemies

The hill barons who shelter the Black Flame, whose debts to the city's houses are large and unpaid.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-cnclolvstr|Council of Ólvestra]]**: Ilthero holds the office of First of the Council, as a Councilor.
- **[[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]]**: He keeps the god of oaths, whose two orders keep their mother-houses in the city.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The frozen notes.** The First of the Council wants a list of every house in Ólvestra that cashed notes against the suspended house, and the houses will not tell the council.
2. **The charter renewed.** Ilthero wants an envoy at Lezkora when the next High King swears the terms, to learn whether Ólvestra's charter is spoken of before the city's due is asked.
