# Appearance {#appearance}

Ilthero Zedimis is a 66-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall with a medium build. He has tanned skin, sun-kissed complexion, brown hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ilthero Zedimis, called Ilthero III, is a Crown Councilor of Lienrik, a great lord sworn to the king's council at [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]], which advises the king on war, on the Roll of Houses and on Lienrik's claim to the High Kingship. He is the third lord of his house to bear his name and the oldest man on the council.

He values the counsel of the herald [[being-carengrvth|Kárenna Graveth]] and relies on her judgment in matters of diplomacy. Lienrik's claim to the crown of Tarvénia turns on the courtesies owed to the other two kings, and Ilthero is the councilor who keeps them.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ilthero is gentle, slow and exact, and he speaks of precedence as other men speak of weather. He forgets names and remembers insults.

### Motivation

He wants to see Lienrik's king elected High King before he dies. He knows he is unlikely to.

### Strengths

He holds the courtesies of every court in Tarvénia. The king trusts him and the clerks fear him.

### Weaknesses

He is old and his memory fails in the afternoon. He relies on Kárenna more than the council knows.

## Social

### Companions

The other Crown Councilors, the Master of the Pleas and Kárenna Graveth.

### Patrons

Zenoth Oltris.

### Enemies

The younger councilors who think the claim should be pressed by arms.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lienrik]]**: Ilthero holds a seat on the king's council as a Crown Councilor, at the rung of Greater Nobility.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The road up one and two.** Ilthero asks a party to ride to Lezkora with a letter for the Warden of the Plateau Gate and to bring back the count of the lowland lords who have gone up.
2. **The courtesy owed.** A letter from the king of Asturath omits a style that Lienrik's kings have always been granted. Ilthero asks a party to learn whether the omission was a clerk's or a policy.
3. **The herald's counsel.** Kárenna Graveth has not answered his last two letters. He asks a party to find out whether she is ill, away or being watched.
