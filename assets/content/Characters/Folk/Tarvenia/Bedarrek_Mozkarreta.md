# Appearance {#appearance}

Bedarrek Mozkarreta is a 46-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall with a medium build. He has olive skin, sun-kissed complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Bedarrek Mozkarreta is a merchant-lord of [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]], the red-brick capital of Lienrik. He buys cattle from the drovers at the east gate, sells hides and tallow to Valthari and holds a stall at the greatest harvest fair in Tarvénia. A gentleman by wealth and not by birth, he sits among the gentry of the plain by the weight of his purse.

He holds documented evidence of the herald [[being-carengrvth|Kárenna Graveth]]'s conflicts of interest and her entanglement with House Graveth's commercial interests, and he offers her a choice between commercial concessions and exposure to the king's justice. In Torvaleth a lord who wants a better place on the Roll of Houses spends years and a great deal of money finding a record, and Bedarrek has made a trade of the records.

## Psyche

### Personality

Bedarrek is genial, loud and quick to buy a man a cup. He keeps a clerk beside him and never mentions the clerk.

### Motivation

He wants a place on the Roll of Houses for his family, and he wants every tax and toll on the cattle road lowered. A concession from Kárenna would pay for both.

### Strengths

He knows what every record in Torvaleth is worth and who sells it. The drovers and the butchers owe him, and the clerks of the Court of Pleas take his invitations.

### Weaknesses

His evidence is only as good as its clerk, and his clerk can be bought. He cannot bring a charge against a herald of the crown without a lord's sponsorship.

## Social

### Companions

His clerk, who keeps the records, and the factors of the three cattle-buying houses he partners.

### Patrons

A lord of the plain who needs his money and sponsors his claim to precedence.

### Enemies

Kárenna Graveth, who opposed him in a negotiation, and the Keeper of the Roll of Houses, who rejected his first claim.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lienrik]]**: Bedarrek stands at the rung of Gentry by grant and seeks a higher place on the Roll of Houses.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The record for sale.** A clerk of the Court of Pleas has found a charter that could lift one house on the Roll of Houses and ruin another. Bedarrek will buy it, and he asks a party to carry the purse and to bring back the charter without its seller knowing the buyer.
2. **The weight at the fair.** The Master of the Fair has found short weight on Bedarrek's tallow at the harvest fair. Bedarrek swears the scales were changed in the night and asks a party to find the hand.
3. **A herald's price.** Kárenna Graveth has sent word that she will meet him at Thórgila's autumn fair rather than at court. Bedarrek asks a party to ride with him as witnesses to whatever is said.
