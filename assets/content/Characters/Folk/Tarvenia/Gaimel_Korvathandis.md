# Appearance {#appearance}

Gaimel Korvathandis is a 63-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall with a medium build. He has olive skin, ruddy complexion, black hair, and green eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Gaimel Korvathandis is an older priest of the temple of [[affiliation-olvenia|Ólvenia]] in [[place-herthema|Herthema]], a priestess's temple served by a priest of austere views. He is agitating for stricter oversight of the temple's work in the town, and his target is [[being-palnsnrcth|Pálina Sánriseth]], whom the common people of Herthema hold close to a saint.

Her work among the poor brings the temple little money and many complications, and he regards her as a corruptor of proper religious order. The temple's priestess-keeper, [[being-thelvaravolk|Thélvara Volkemis]], has not sided with him.

## Psyche

### Personality

Gaimel is gaunt, formal and unsmiling. He believes severity is a form of love.

### Motivation

He wants the temple's work done by rule and ledger, with the offerings counted and the ritual kept. He believes the town's piety has been confused with its kindness.

### Strengths

He knows the temple's rules and its accounts better than anyone, and the older masters of the town respect his austerity.

### Weaknesses

He has no support from the poor and little from the priestess-keeper. His own accounts would not bear close inspection.

## Social

### Companions

A few older masters of the town who attend his sermons.

### Patrons

A merchant of the council who wants the temple's funds redirected.

### Enemies

Pálina Sánriseth and the poor who love her.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-olvenia|Faith of Ólvenia]]**: Gaimel serves as a priest of the temple.
- **[[affiliation-cnclhrthm|Council of Herthema]]**: He is a Freeman of the town.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The oversight.** Gaimel wants witnesses to what Pálina Sánriseth does among the poor, and he will pay strangers to watch her. A party can take the purse, or can learn who gave him the money.
2. **The temple's accounts.** A ledger of the temple's offerings has a gap in it, and Gaimel says Pálina took it. Thélvara Volkemis asks a party to find who wrote the gap.
3. **The sermon.** Gaimel has called the temple's women to a sermon on the poor-woman's blessing, and a crowd of Pálina's neighbors means to attend. The council asks a party to keep the peace.
