# Appearance {#appearance}

Prámiza Mozkarreta is a 40-year-old woman who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. She has tanned, weathered skin, black hair cropped at the jaw and dark brown eyes, and the top of her left ear is notched.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Prámiza Mozkarreta is a mercenary captain who recruits in the back streets of [[place-herthema|Herthema]], the walled town that breeds sellswords. A man on the town's roll may take hire in Herthema lawfully, and a man off it may not, and the Keeper of the Roll of the [[affiliation-cnclhrthm|Council of Herthema]] enters and strikes the names. Prámiza fills her company, [[affiliation-kraveldo|Kraveldo]], from the roll and from the young men who hope to be put on it.

She is a captain of a crossbow company in the Tarvenan way: she swears her men to a contract they can recite, and she is sworn to them by it. Where she differs from the captains of the valleys is in what she will put her name to. She has taken contracts the Named Men of other companies would not touch, and she pays on the day.

## Psyche

### Personality

Prámiza is brisk, practical and amused. She counts her men aloud every morning and gives each the same look.

### Motivation

She wants her company well paid and kept alive, with enough profit left over to buy her own ship. She reads every contract twice and has never been caught in a clause.

### Strengths

She keeps her company paid on the day and her word to the letter, and she recruits well: the young men of Herthema want to sign with her. The merchants who employ bodyguards in Herthema know her name.

### Weaknesses

Captains who keep the older rule refuse to share a camp with her. A company that takes any contract is a company that eventually takes one it should not.

## Social

### Companions

Her lieutenant, a veteran of the valleys, and her company's clerk, who reads each contract aloud to the assembled company.

### Patrons

The merchants and minor nobility of Herthema, the employers of its bodyguards.

### Enemies

The bigger bodyguards of the town, who have lost work to men on the roll without a company, and the Named Men of the valley companies.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-cnclhrthm|Council of Herthema]]**: Prámiza is a Freeman of the town and recruits from its roll.
- **[[affiliation-kraveldo|Kraveldo]]**: Prámiza is the company's Captain. This season it is under contract to [[being-tharozozurg|Tharozo Zurgaregi]], a baron of the Western March.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A name struck.** The Keeper of the Roll has struck one of Prámiza's best sergeants, and the man says he has been betrayed. She asks a party to learn who in the council arranged it and what they gain.
2. **The client's clause.** A client in Herthema offers a contract at three times her rate, with a clause that binds her to arrest anyone the client names. Prámiza wants a party to find out whose name is first on the list.
3. **Banrik Káriselda.** The bodyguard Banrik Káriselda is on the roll and belongs to no company, and she wants him. She asks a party to carry her offer and to learn why he has refused three captains.
