# Appearance {#appearance}

Hilther Betsuris is a 51-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is very thin. He has tanned, sunburnt and chapped skin, graying brown hair and brown eyes. His right shoulder is stiff, and he holds his begging bowl in the left hand.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hilther Betsuris begs at the hiring yards behind the quays of [[place-valthari|Valthari]], where the Tarvenan companies sign on for service abroad and the young men of the valleys wait for a contract. He begs in the name of the [[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pozeras]], the road's faith, and the seamen and the sellswords give him a coin on the way to the quay because the road has not always been kind to them.

He carries the news of every company that has sailed from the quays, who came home and who did not, and he has told it so often that he can recite the rolls of the dead without seeing them. He sleeps in a doorway in the lane behind the customs house and eats at the cookshops by arrangement.

## Psyche

### Personality

Hilther is humble, sharp-eyed and sly. He has learned to look as if he understands nothing and remembers everything.

### Motivation

He wants to keep his doorway, his bowl and his news. He wants to be buried by a company that remembers his name.

### Strengths

He knows every hiring captain on the quays, which companies pay and which do not, and what became of the men who signed with those that did not. The seamen who refuse to sign tell him what they will not tell the owners.

### Weaknesses

He is a beggar and can be moved on by the watch, bought by a house or beaten by a man who thinks he knows too much. His shoulder gives him constant pain.

## Social

### Companions

The cookshop keepers and the lighter-men who feed him, and the pilots who hear what he hears.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

The factor of a house that does not want the seamen's refusal discussed outside the yards.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pozeras]]**: Hilther is a Devran of the road, a lay devotee who begs in its name.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Which way she will sail.** The seamen want to know which way the pilot Dálena Valaron will sail when the owners' council asks her to break their refusal, and Hilther has heard that she has already said. He asks a party to find out whom she told.
2. **A name on the roll.** A man signed on at the stone under a name that is not his own, and the company sailed. Hilther asks a party to carry a message to the captain, and to bring back the answer without anyone seeing him ask.
3. **The quiet factor.** A factor of the Thordimalon has been hiring guards who will not ask why the quarter has gone quiet around him. Hilther says that the beggars of the yards have stopped sleeping in a certain lane and asks a party to find out why.
