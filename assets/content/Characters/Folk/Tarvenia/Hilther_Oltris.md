# Appearance {#appearance}

Hilther Oltris is a 38-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. He has his brother's fair skin and hazel eyes, keeps his dark brown hair cropped like a garrison captain's and wears plain gray wool on the walls and the red brick-colored cloth of the court only when he rides down to Torvaleth.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hilther Oltris is the younger brother of King [[being-zenotholtr|Zenoth Oltris]] and the Castellan of [[place-ronetho|Ornetho]], the walled city where the [Thalsira]{.name data-type="place/feature"} comes out of its gorge. By custom the castellancy goes to a son or a brother of the reigning king, the kinsman a king of Lyenrik most wants kept busy and away from his own court, and Hilther has held it for twelve years. He holds the walls, the gates and their tolls, keeps the garrison and sits as judge in the castellan's court in the old imperial barracks.

He keeps the legions' rule of twelve at every gate, for the king's own companies as for any mountain lord's. He relies on the senior watch commander of the gates, [[being-hldndrgn|Haldena Durgana]], for what is entering the city and what it carries, has pressed her advancement in the gate watch and has told her she may ask him for men when an operation is too large for the watch. The vaults under the barracks, which hold what survives of the imperial march's archive, stay locked, as every castellan has kept them.

## Psyche

### Personality

Hilther is direct, dry and watchful. He prefers a report to a petition and a gate captain's word to a lord's, and he is courteous to the guilds without letting either hall sit nearer him than the other.

### Motivation

He wants the gates held, the tolls paid in full and the city quiet, so that the king has no reason to call him back to court and no excuse to give the castellancy to someone else.

### Strengths

The garrison is his, and the gate watch trusts him. He knows the city's two great guilds and their quarrel, and the mountain lords who come down the road know him as a man who keeps the rule of twelve against his own brother's men.

### Weaknesses

He is the brother the king keeps at a distance, and his reports reach Torvaleth through other men's hands. The courts at Torvaleth hear appeals from his gates, and smugglers' goods seized at the walls come back to their owners through them.

## Social

### Companions

Haldena Durgana and the gate captains of the watch; the guildmasters of the wool hall and the iron hall, who each want his ear.

### Patrons

His brother, King Zenoth Oltris, who gives the castellancy and can take it away.

### Enemies

The carters and smugglers who move goods round his gates, and the lords at court who would like the castellancy for a kinsman of their own.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Hilther holds the office of Castellan of Ornetho, as a Prince of Oltris.
- **[[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]]**: He keeps the god of gates at the temple in the north wall.

## Plot Hooks

1. **One man through.** The castellan wants one traveler let through the north gate without inspection, and he will not say why. Someone in the watch wants to know who the traveler is before the gate opens.
2. **The appeals.** Goods his gates seize keep coming back to their owner on appeal from Torvaleth. Hilther wants to know whose clerk is writing the appeals, without asking the court.
3. **The vault door.** A lock on the vaults under the old barracks has been tried. The castellan wants a watch kept on the door, by men who are not of his garrison.
