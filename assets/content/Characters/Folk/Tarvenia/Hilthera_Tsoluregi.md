# Appearance {#appearance}

Hilthera Tsoluregi is a 49-year-old woman who stands 5'10" tall with a heavy build. She has olive skin, smooth complexion, dark brown hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hilthera Tsoluregi is a Crown Councilor of Lyenrik, one of the great lords sworn to the king's council, and the kingdom's Master of Commerce. She advises the king on trade, the Roll of Houses and Lyenrik's claim to the High Kingship. Her house is old and mountain-bred, and she holds a province of the crown directly.

At [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]] she is the closest confidant and patron of the herald [[being-carengrvth|Kárenna Graveth]], whom she guides on the political currents of the royal household.

## Psyche

### Personality

Hilthera is calm, dry and watchful. She prefers advice given in a counting-house to counsel given in the hall.

### Motivation

She wants Lyenrik's trade protected and its claim to the crown served by wealth rather than war. She regards Kárenna as an investment.

### Strengths

She holds the king's confidence on every question of coin. Her province pays its dues on the day.

### Weaknesses

Her patronage of Kárenna links her to the herald's scandals. Her mountain blood makes the lowland lords distrust her.

## Social

### Companions

The other Crown Councilors and the Master of the Pleas.

### Patrons

Zenoth Oltris.

### Enemies

Merchants who resent her control of the toll and the harvest fair.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Hilthera holds a seat on the king's council as a Crown Councilor, at the rung of Greater Nobility.

1. **The channel and the toll.** A Valthari house again asks leave to cut a channel through the salt marsh. Hilthera asks a party to learn how much of the cost the king's own councilors have promised.
2. **The herald's ledger.** Bedarrek Mozkarreta claims to hold evidence of Kárenna Graveth's entanglements. Hilthera asks a party to find out how he got it.
3. **The harvest fair.** The Master of the Fair reports that the tolls at the harvest fair fell short. Hilthera asks a party to count the carts that came in by the river gate.
