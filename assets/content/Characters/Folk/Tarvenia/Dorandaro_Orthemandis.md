# Appearance {#appearance}

Dorándaro Orthemandis is a 50-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall with a heavy build. He has medium skin, sun-kissed complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Dorándaro Orthemandis is an alderman of [[place-nebralis|Nebralis]] and the leader of the fishing cooperative that represents the common fishermen. He holds the one seat on the [[affiliation-cnclnbrls|Council of Nebralis]] that belongs to the cooperative, and he speaks there for the fishing families against aldermen who are more and more the men of the harbor's merchant houses.

He has sat beside [[being-alethraornet|Alethra Ornetis]] in the Freemen's Assembly for six years and trusts her absolutely. He and the fishing families are the council's counterweight, and he knows the count of a vote before it is taken.

## Psyche

### Personality

Dorándaro is broad, slow-spoken and stubborn. He speaks in the voice of a man hailing across water and never says the same thing differently.

### Motivation

He wants the fishing families fed, the assembly kept and Alethra kept in her place. He wants the harbor's wealth to reach the quay as well as the counting-house.

### Strengths

The fishing families follow him and the assembly listens. He knows every boat, every net and every debt on the quay.

### Weaknesses

He is outvoted at every sitting and outspent at every election. He cannot read the merchant houses' ledgers.

## Social

### Companions

The captains of the cooperative's boats and Alethra Ornetis.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

Gulmaleno Thelvaris and the merchant aldermen, who find his arguments slow and his votes costly.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-cnclnbrls|Council of Nebralis]]**: Dorándaro holds a seat as an Alderman for the common fishermen.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The bishop's return.** Ornetho Iltheris is coming back to Nebralis with orders to reform the temple's work. Dorándaro wants a party to learn which merchant houses asked for him before the assembly meets.
2. **A seat on the council.** The cooperative's seat comes up for confirmation, and a merchant house has proposed a candidate who has never been to sea. Dorándaro asks a party to find who is paying for the candidate's friends.
3. **The lean year.** A run of poor catches has begun, and the assembly will have to decide what is shared. The merchant aldermen propose to buy the cooperative's fish at a price that leaves the families with nothing to put by. Dorándaro asks a party to count the houses' warehouses.
