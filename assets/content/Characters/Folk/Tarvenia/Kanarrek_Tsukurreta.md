# Appearance {#appearance}

Kanarrek Tsukurreta is a 36-year-old man who stands 5' 10" tall and is of medium build. He has tanned, weathered skin, dark brown hair cut short and brown eyes, and wears the valley's colors under a plain traveling cloak.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kanarrek Tsukurreta is the _thrumar_ of the crossbow company that the High King's own valley sends abroad. He swore the company's men at the oath stone of their valley and signed their contract to a Taruzelian buyer, and he reads its clauses each evening to men who know them by heart. The pay is most of what the valley has, and a day named in the contract is passing while the ship lies at anchor in the roads of [[place-valthari|Valthari]].

## Psyche

### Personality

Steady, short of sleep and exact about what a contract says.

### Motivation

He wants his company aboard before the day named, and the valley's pay home.

### Strengths

His men trust him because he has never signed them to a contract he could not recite.

### Weaknesses

He will not ask a house for help.

## Social

### Companions

The company's sergeants, and [[being-hiltherbetsu|Hilther Betsuris]], who begs among its tents.

### Patrons

The lord of his valley, who is the High King.

### Enemies

The house that holds the ship, and any agent who offers passage for the contract.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]**: Kanarrek is a Freeman of the realm, sworn captain of a valley company.
- **[[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pozeras]]**: He keeps the road's faith and gives to the beggar at the quay.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Three tides gone.** Kanarrek hires a party to find the company a hull and a crew before the day, with a third of the pay to the party.
2. **The steward's errand.** A man in the valley's colors says the steward went to the Taruzeli Quarter in the spring. Kanarrek asks a party to learn why without letting the company hear.
3. **The clause.** Kanarrek wants a reader to find the clause on causes beyond the master's control and carry it to the Arbitrators.

:::secret {title="The steward's borrowing"}
The valley's steward has borrowed against the company's pay through a factor in the Taruzeli Quarter of Valthari, and Kanarrek does not know it.

:::
