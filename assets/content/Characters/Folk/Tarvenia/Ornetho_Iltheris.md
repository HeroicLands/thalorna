# Appearance {#appearance}

Ornetho Iltheris is a 55-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall with a heavy build. He has tanned skin, weathered complexion, dark brown hair, and amber eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ornetho Iltheris is a bishop of the formal priesthood who lives at [[place-polthamis|Polthamis]], the city of the Great Temple, and visits the temples of the coast on the prelate's commission. He visited [[place-nebralis|Nebralis]] and was scandalized by the informal, unstructured approach of [[being-alethraornet|Alethra Ornetis]], who blesses the nets, attends births and mediates between families without a formal office.

He has recommended closer oversight of Alethra and the redirection of her temple's funding toward formally trained clergy, and he proposes consolidating the temple's informal healing and mediation roles into formal positions. He is returning to Nebralis with orders to reform the temple's work.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ornetho is tall, fastidious and sure of his order. He sees disorder as sin, however kindly meant.

### Motivation

He wants the faith in Tarvénia made regular, which means uniform. He believes unstructured piety is a door to heresy.

### Strengths

He has the prelate's ear and a clerk's love of procedure. The merchant aldermen of Nebralis welcome him.

### Weaknesses

He has never worked a field or a net and does not understand why the town listens to Alethra. He underestimates the assembly.

## Social

### Companions

His secretary and a pair of trained priests he means to install.

### Patrons

Thaluro Zenothis, the prelate of Polthamis.

### Enemies

Alethra Ornetis and the fishing families of Nebralis.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-lizelas|Faith of Lizelas]]**: Ornetho is a Lizena, a bishop of the formal priesthood.
- **[[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]]**: He is trained at the Great Temple.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The bishop's return.** Ornetho is returning to Nebralis with orders to reform the temple's work. A party hired by Dorándaro Orthemandis can learn which merchant houses asked for him, and a party hired by Ornetho can learn what Alethra does at births.
2. **The funds.** The bishop's secretary asks a party to find the temple's ledger in Nebralis, which Alethra does not keep.
3. **The formal clergy.** The two priests the bishop means to install are of a Polthamis family with a debt to the Great Temple. Ornetho asks a party to travel with them to Nebralis and see that they arrive unharmed.
