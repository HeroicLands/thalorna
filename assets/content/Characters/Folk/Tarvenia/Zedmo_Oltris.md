# Appearance {#appearance}

Zedmo Oltris is a 19-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of light build. He is fair and hazel-eyed like his father, long in the arm, and he carries a clerk's wax tablet at his belt beside his sword.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zedmo Oltris is the eldest son of King [[being-zenotholtr|Zenoth Oltris]] and the Heir of Lyenrik. The crown of [[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Lyenrik]] passes to the king's eldest son, and its heir sits in the [Court of Pleas]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} from his sixteenth year. Zedmo has sat there for three years, on the bench below the Master of the Pleas in the lower chamber of the king's hall at [[place-torvaleth|Torvaleth]], hearing the cases between the kingdom's lords and the appeals from the bailiffs' courts.

He has learned the plea rolls the way his father wants him to, and he has learned the lords too. A lord who loses a case before the heir remembers it, and so does the heir.

## Psyche

### Personality

Zedmo is grave, attentive and slow to speak in court. Away from the bench he is easy company and asks more questions than a prince is expected to.

### Motivation

He wants to be a king the Court of Pleas cannot surprise, and he wants his father's claim to the High Kingship to cost Lyenrik less than his father is willing to pay.

### Strengths

He knows the plea rolls and the Roll of Houses better than most advocates, and the clerks of the court like him.

### Weaknesses

He has never commanded in the field, and the lords of the host know it. He cannot act against a lord without his father's leave.

## Social

### Companions

The clerks of the Court of Pleas and the younger sons of the great houses who sit at the back of the chamber.

### Patrons

His father, King Zenoth Oltris. His uncle Hilther Oltris, the Castellan of Ornetho, writes to him.

### Enemies

None declared. Lords whose cases he has heard against them are patient.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Lyenrik]]**: Zedmo holds the office of Heir of Lyenrik, as a Prince of Oltris.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A judgment of the old courts.** A case before the Court of Pleas turns on a judgment of the imperial courts that nobody can find in the rolls. The heir wants it found before the court sits again.
2. **A quiet ride.** The heir wants to see his uncle at Ornetho without the court knowing he has gone, and needs riders who are not of his father's household.
