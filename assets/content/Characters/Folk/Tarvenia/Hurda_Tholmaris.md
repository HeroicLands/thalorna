# Appearance {#appearance}

Hurda Tholmaris is a 61-year-old woman who stands 5' 6" tall and is of light build. She has pale, wrinkled skin, white hair cut short and gray eyes, and carries a ring of iron keys at her belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hurda Tholmaris is the Keeper of the Hall, the crown officer who lives at [[place-lezkorahall|the High Hall]] and opens it to the lords. She has kept its benches and the chest of its roll for three kings. She puts out the hearth when a High King dies, and the chain on the ledge door beside the chair is hers.

## Psyche

### Personality

Dry, watchful and at ease only with the hall empty.

### Motivation

She wants the hearth lit and the hall kept as the terms require.

### Strengths

She knows who has stood in the hall for thirty years and where each lord sits.

### Weaknesses

She holds no authority beyond the hall's door and cannot compel a lord or the High King.

## Social

### Companions

The brother of the Order of the Sealed Word who keeps the roll between elections.

### Patrons

The High King.

### Enemies

Anyone who would have the hearth put out early.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]**: Hurda holds the crown's office of Keeper of the Hall.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The unchained door.** The old king has had the chain taken off the ledge door, and Hurda asks a party to stand watch at dawn.
2. **The benches.** A lowland steward asks to count the benches before the lords arrive, and Hurda wants to know why.

:::secret {title="The ledge"}
Hurda knows that one of the two High Kings who died on the ledge beside the chair did not fall. The outer stone of the ledge has been scraped again, and she has chained the door.

1. **The chisel.** Hurda asks a party to learn who has been at the ledge's outer stone.
   :::
