# Appearance {#appearance}

Ornetho Tholmaris is a 56-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of heavy build. He is broad and gray, with a shipwright's forearms and an adze scar on his shin that makes him favor the left leg on the slips.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ornetho Tholmaris is the Guild Master of the shipwrights of [[place-valthari|Valthari]], elected by the masters of his trade to speak for it before the [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]]. He has built hulls at [[place-vlthrshpyrds|the shipyards]] for forty years.

Half the slips stand empty since the strike began, and his journeymen are losing a season. He presses the houses and the seamen to settle, and he knows which slip has been quiet and which has not.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ornetho is loud, honest and impatient with councils. He thinks a dispute that cannot be settled on a slip is not worth having.

### Motivation

He wants timber on the slips and wages paid, and he wants the strike over on any terms that leave the seamen willing to sail the hulls he builds.

### Strengths

The shipwrights follow him, and he knows the yards' business better than any house.

### Weaknesses

He speaks for a trade, not a house, and the council hears him and does what the houses want.

## Social

### Companions

The master shipwrights and their journeymen.

### Patrons

None; the masters of his trade elect him.

### Enemies

The house whose slip has had no timber since the strike began, and which does not seem to mind.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]**: Ornetho holds the office of Guild Master, as a Master of a Trade.
- **[[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]]**: He keeps the craftsmen's faith of the hearth.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The quiet slip.** One house's slip has had no timber since the strike began, and its foreman will not say why. The Guild Master wants to know what the house is waiting for.
2. **The petition.** Ornetho wants a petition from the shipwrights carried to the First of the Council in her own hand, past the houses' clerks.
