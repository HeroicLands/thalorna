# Appearance {#appearance}

Zurgade Kaldris is a 49-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of heavy build. He is heavy and smooth-faced, gray-haired, with rings on three fingers, and he has not walked the Strand without two of the crown's men behind him for a year.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zurgade Kaldris is the Keeper of the Salt of the [[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]], a knight of the king's choosing. Galvaren makes no salt worth having, and he buys it by sea from the southern coast and sells it to the curing houses of [[place-durvolka|Durvolka]] at the crown's price from the king's salt-house at the Lyenrik Gate. The salt is the crown's richest revenue, and he has raised its price for the second year running.

The curing-house mistresses of the Strand have begun to buy from a ship that anchors outside the bay at night, and his ledgers show it before anyone tells him.

## Psyche

### Personality

Zurgade is cool, numerate and unbothered by dislike. He regards the salt as the crown's purse and himself as its lock.

### Motivation

He wants the salt-house's sales back where they were and the night ship found, and he wants the king to remember who pays for the galleys.

### Strengths

He knows to the cask what the coast uses, and his ledgers tell him which curing house has stopped buying.

### Weaknesses

He is the most resented man on the coast, and the harbor side would vote his office out of existence if it could.

## Social

### Companions

The clerks and porters of the salt-house.

### Patrons

King Ganoth Ervothandis, who needs the salt revenue more than he likes its keeper.

### Enemies

Korvath Thaluris of Ulhera and the harbor side of the Long Table; the curing-house mistresses of the Strand.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]]**: Zurgade holds the office of Keeper of the Salt, as a Knight.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The empty ledger.** Three curing houses have bought no salt since spring and are still curing fish. The Keeper of the Salt wants to know where their salt comes from.
2. **The price.** A Valthari factor has offered the Keeper a private price on the crown's own salt. Zurgade wants to know whom the factor serves before he answers.
