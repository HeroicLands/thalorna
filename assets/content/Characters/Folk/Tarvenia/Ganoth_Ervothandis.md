# Appearance {#appearance}

Ganoth Ervothandis is a 56-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. He has fair, weathered skin, graying brown hair and blue eyes, and the stoop of a man who reads accounts by a low lamp. He dresses in sea-gray wool.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ganoth Ervothandis is the king of Galvaren, of the house of Ervothandis. He came to the throne in 712 AF and is a careful man with the crown's money. He rules from the Headland at [[place-durvolka|Durvolka]] and presides at the Long Table, the royal council, where the landed lords of the valleys sit along one side by right and the shipmasters of the harbors sit along the other, elected each autumn.

He owes the High King of Tarvénia his presence at an election and his levy when the ban is called, and he owes him nothing else. The Table advises and the king decides, and Ganoth has learned that a king who decides against both sides of the Table at once seldom does it twice.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ganoth is quiet, exact and wary of enthusiasm. He asks what a thing costs before he asks whether it is right, and he writes the answers in a small book he keeps in his sleeve.

### Motivation

He wants the crown solvent. The crown's richest revenue is the price of salt it sells the curing houses, and its most resented office is the one that sets it; Ganoth wants the revenue without the riots.

### Strengths

He can hold two sides of a table apart without letting either leave it. He knows the harbors' rolls and the valleys' rents, and he reads a lord's claim against the crown's ledger faster than any clerk.

### Weaknesses

He is cautious to the point of seeming indifferent to the pirates on the southern salt route and the drowned of the coast. The shipmasters call him a countinghouse king.

## Social

### Companions

The Keeper of the Seal and the Keeper of the Salt stand nearest him. The Master of the Fleet commands the king's six galleys.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

The shipmasters of the Long Table's harbor side, who want salt cheaper and galleys sent south. The curing-house mistresses who buy from the night ship.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galvaren]]**: Ganoth holds the crown at the rung of Sovereign.
- **[[affiliation-kingdmtrvn|Kingdom of Tarvénia]]**: He is an elector, one of the lords of the three kingdoms who bring a banner to the ban.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The price of salt.** The Keeper of the Salt has raised the crown's price for the second year running, and the curing-house mistresses of the Strand have begun to buy from a ship that anchors outside the bay at night. The king asks a party to name the ship before the Table sits.
2. **A seat at the Long Table.** The Masters' Hall elects in autumn, and a shipmaster who lost two boats on the Far Grounds is standing against the king's own candidate. Someone is paying the herring crews to vote and someone else is threatening them, and Ganoth wants to know who.
3. **Galleys south.** The harbor side of the Table demands galleys against the pirates on the salt route. The king offers a party the chance to learn what the Valthari houses that pay the pirates' insurers think of the proposal.
