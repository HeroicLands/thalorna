# Appearance {#appearance}

Zenotha Ulmis is a 59-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall with a medium build. She has olive skin, sun-kissed complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zenotha Ulmis is a shrewd noblewoman of [[place-lezkora|Lezkora]] who plays a dangerous political game. She publicly supports the [[affiliation-ardavon|Árdavonian faith]] and is known at court as a friend of its priests.

Two courts share the city, and she is welcome at both. Her influence rests on the small matters that hold a court together.

## Psyche

### Personality

Zenotha is graceful, warm and exact. She remembers everyone's children.

### Motivation

She wants her friends at court safe and her own standing in both courts kept.

### Strengths

She hears everything in two courts and owes no one.

### Weaknesses

Her allies are less careful than she is.

Her enemies are Ostaran and his mother, [[being-zedimazenoth|Zédima Zenothis]].
