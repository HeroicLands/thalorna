# Appearance {#appearance}

Oltremo Brazis is a 55-year-old man who stands 5' 11" tall and is of medium build. He has clear olive skin, black hair graying at the temples and brown eyes, and dresses in a Valthari house's dark wool with a seal-ring on the left hand.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Oltremo Brazis heads a house of Valthari that owns pans at [[place-threskon|Threskon]], and the [[affiliation-consejarath|Konsezarath]] has named him Reeve of the salt town for the year. He keeps its order and takes its dues.

## Psyche

### Personality

Patient, courteous and fluent in the council's questions.

### Motivation

He wants the salt town orderly and its dues paid, and a price for salt that suits the pans.

### Strengths

He knows every pan, every master and every cask in the lagoon.

### Weaknesses

He is the head of a house that owns pans, and the town's other houses watch what he favors.

## Social

### Companions

The masters of the salt ships and the house's factor in Durvolka.

### Patrons

The house that owns the pans.

### Enemies

Any councilor who has bought his house's debts.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthari]]**: Oltremo is a House Head and holds the office of Reeve of Threskon.
- **[[affiliation-pozeras|Faith of Pozeras]]**: He keeps the temple on the quay.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The channel.** The spring storms have filled the channel through the dunes, and Oltremo asks a party to find which house will pay for digging it.
2. **The pans.** A panman of the town asks a party to carry his complaint about the water in the pans to the Reeve, because the Reeve's own house owns the pans.

:::secret {title="The night ship"}
The council has told Oltremo to find out whose ship sells salt to Durvolka's curing houses at night, and he asks the masters of the town's salt ships in public. The ship is his own house's. He keeps its master out of his questions, and he wants a Long Table at Durvolka that ends the crown's buying and selling of the kingdom's salt, so that Valthari sells to the curing houses directly. He cannot afford his house's name being said in the council.

1. **The council's question.** Oltremo hires a party to name the ship, and expects it to name another house's.
2. **The sheds.** A party that counts the salt in his house's sheds finds more than the pans can make.
   :::
