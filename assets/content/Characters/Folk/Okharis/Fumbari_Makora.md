# Appearance {#appearance}

Fúmbari Mákora is a 57-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of medium build. She has smooth brown skin, short hair gone gray at the temples and dark brown eyes that rest on a person's hands before their face. She wears a gold-hemmed temple robe and keeps a wax tablet tucked in her sleeve.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Fúmbari Mákora is the Master of the Market, in the temple's tongue the Ndumbari Zhabing, of [[place-kaljekor|The Golden Flame]]. She keeps [[miscgear-sclskaljekr|The Scales of Kaljekor]], on which trade oaths are sworn, and she answers to the priest-nobles who rule the city as the [Ngolu Nkaring]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}. The Servants of Gold weigh the offerings on her beam and the Flameweavers write down the oaths.

She is genial, exact and unhurried, and she weighs everything, including a compliment.

## Background

**The broker's daughter.** She was born in a Kaljekor broker's household and became a Student of the Flame at ten, learning to read the beam before she could read the registers.

**The climb.** She served as a Servant of Gold and as a Guardian of Wealth, and for twenty years she weighed the temple's offerings and the quay's bargains. The Flameweavers' record of the oaths she presided over has never been disputed.

**The mastery.** She has held the Mastery for twelve years. This year the council hears each Khelathi offer and answers none, and she holds that an open harbor is its best argument, since no one has forbidden the city to trade with every neighbor.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is warm, shrewd and patient, and she never raises her voice at the beam.

### Motivation

She wants the harbor open and the Scales trusted by every trader on the quay, and she wants the council's silence to look like a choice and not a failure.

### Strengths

Every trader at the Market of Flames takes her weighing as final, and she knows which houses buy and which only bargain.

### Weaknesses

She cannot answer a Khelathi gift without the council, and a Master who keeps her counsel is said to be keeping a price.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]]**: Priest, ordained to the rites of a temple of Kaljekor.
- **[[affiliation-nkaruthar|The Eternal Flame]]**: Master of the Market (Ndumbari Zhabing).

## Plot Hooks

1. **The offers at the quay.** A Khelathi house has pressed her for a favor in return for a gift. She wants a party to learn what the Ngolu Nkaring meant by its silence before the next ship sails.
2. **The false oath.** A merchant who swore on the Scales sees his year and a day running out and wants the record altered. She wants a witness at the Flameweavers' door.
3. **The tribute in coin.** A chieftain wants his tribute carried to the moot in a form that counts at the Scales, and she wants a party to carry the weights with it.
