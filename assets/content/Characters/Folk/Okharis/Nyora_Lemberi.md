# Appearance {#appearance}

Nyora Lémberi is a 48-year-old woman who stands 5' 6" tall and is of medium build. She has dark brown skin burned lighter on her forearms, gray-streaked black hair in tight braids and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nyora Lémberi is the chieftain of the [[affiliation-kirofatrb|Kirofa Tribe]] and lives at [[place-kirofavlg|Kirofa Village]]. Her tribe tends the date palms and fig trees along the river, and she wants the fruit weighed and sold while it keeps.

## Background

**Formation.** She climbed her family's palms as a girl and knows each tree on the bank by its crop.

**Accession.** She took the chieftaincy at thirty-eight by descent and the elders' consent, and the Keeper of the Groves allots the trees.

## Psyche

### Motivation

She wants the tribute taken in dried fruit at the weight the groves can bear, before the heat spoils it.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The spoiled load.** A dried-fruit load for Zarhanis has sat three days at a granary gate, and she wants a party to carry it before it turns.
