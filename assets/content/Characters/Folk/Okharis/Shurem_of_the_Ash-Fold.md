# Appearance {#appearance}

A Shurem of the Ash-Fold wears rough hide, carries a knife and a skin of water, and has no ashmark on the brow.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Among the [[lore-okharinclt|Okharik]], a person put out of the Flame is a **shurem**, "ash," whom no temple receives, who is given no ashmark at a passage, and whose fire is not reckoned descended from [[miscgear-emberfirst|The First Ember]]. At [[place-shuremis|The Ash-Fold]], a temple estate east of [[place-zarhanis|The First Flame]] that was struck from the rolls, about twenty of them live in the sunken floors of the old bondsmen's yard. They keep a hearth kindled from no temple fire, bury their dead in the ash of the cattle-ground without a mark, and give no sign to the herders who watch from the track.

## Background

Some were born in the yard and have never had an ashmark. Others were put out of the Flame as adults, for declining it or for a transgression the temple would not forgive, and came to the one place that does not turn the ash away.

## Psyche

### Personality

Proud, reserved and slow to trust.

### Motivation

The shurem wants the yard left alone and its dead given the rites they were denied.

### Strengths

The shurem knows the estate, the habits of the cattle-ground and the ways in and out of the yard, and has long practice at hearing a false name.

### Weaknesses

The shurem has no temple, clan or court to appeal to, and no one outside the yard to speak for the shurem.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

The yard keeps its own elder, who holds the hearth and the soured-milk bowl and speaks for the yard. No temple receives a shurem, and no clan claims one.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-shuremisadv|The Hearth Without Light]]: the yard's elder hands a stranger soured milk at the Ash-Fold, and the yard stands to see whether it is drunk.
2. **Hides for grain.** A shurem of the yard comes to a herders' camp to trade hides for grain, and the clan's chieftain wants a stranger to make the trade so that no herder is seen doing it.
3. **A passage with no mark.** A shurem wants a child of the yard attended at a passage by a shaman of [[affiliation-yenorzhrng|the Spirit Path]], which holds no rank in any temple, and needs an escort to the shaman's camp across the clans' grazing.
