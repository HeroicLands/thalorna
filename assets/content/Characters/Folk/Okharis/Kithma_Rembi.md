# Appearance {#appearance}

Kithma Rembi is a 43-year-old woman who stands 5' 5" tall and is of medium build. She has brown skin dusted with dry clay, black hair in a single braid and brown eyes that run over a jar's shoulder before they meet a face.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kithma Rembi is the chieftain of the [[affiliation-khamundatrb|Khamunda Tribe]] and lives at [[place-khamundavlg|Khamunda Village]]. Her tribe digs clay and fires the storage jars that the temple granaries hold their grain in, and she carries each season's count to the moot.

## Background

**Formation.** She learned the kilns as a girl and could name the clay-pit of any jar by its ring.

**Accession.** She took the chieftaincy at thirty-two by descent and the elders' consent, and the Master of the Kilns sets each firing.

## Psyche

### Motivation

She wants every jar she owes entered in the temple registers at the count her kilns made.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The missing jars.** The [[place-zarhanis|Zarhanis]] registers show her tribe short by a hundred jars, and she wants a witness at the granary court to count what stands there.
