# Appearance {#appearance}

Kwathor Mbasu is a 38-year-old man who stands 5' 10" tall and is of heavy build. He has dark brown skin roughened by smoke, black hair cropped short and dark brown eyes, and his hands are scarred with old burns.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kwathor Mbasu is the chieftain of the [[affiliation-dongarutrb|Dóngaru Tribe]] and lives at [[place-dongaruvlg|Dóngaru Village]]. His tribe fells hardwood and burns charcoal for the temple smithies, and he sits at the moots with the stacks counted in his head.

## Background

**Formation.** He grew up at the burners' earth-covered stacks and could tell a stack's heat by the smoke at twelve.

**Accession.** He took the chieftaincy at thirty by descent and the elders' consent, and the Master of the Stacks still sets the burning.

## Psyche

### Motivation

He wants the charcoal dues counted by the stack and not asked again at the festival.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The stack count.** He wants someone to stand with him at a burning and witness the yield before the Tribute-Warden's scribe comes.
