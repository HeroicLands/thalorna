# Appearance {#appearance}

Éwura Tébara is a 51-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She has dark brown skin weathered by open-court sun, close-cropped gray hair and brown eyes that she keeps half lowered when she speaks. A line of white ash lies across her brow, and she wears a plain white temple robe.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Éwura Tébara is the Oracle of [[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]], called in the temple's tongue the Ambelari Zheking, the Prophet of Light, and master of the temple at [[place-zarhanis|The First Flame]]. She holds its dreams, visions and communion with the ancestors, and the King puts his questions of consequence to her before he acts.

She is quiet, exact and slow to speak.

## Background

**The student.** A herding household of the Tébara brought her to Zarhanis at nine, and the temple received her as a Student of the Flame.

**The ascent.** She rose through Scholar of the Fire to Guardian of Spirits, and for fifteen years stood at the passage of the dead. The High Pyremant named her Prophet of Light at thirty-nine.

**The silence.** She spoke the Flame's sanction over [[being-takorozanethar|Tákoro Zanethar]] at his anointing. This year she has given no answer to his question whether he calls the Host, and she holds that no one may speak the answer in its place.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is still, dry and unhurried.

### Motivation

She wants the answer to come as the Flame gives it. She will not hurry it for the King, the council or the Warden.

### Strengths

The temple trusts what she says because she says nothing she has not been given.

### Weaknesses

She cannot explain the silence, and every faction reads it as it wishes.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nkaruthar|The Eternal Flame]]**: Prophet of Light, temple master at Zarhanis.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The queue at the pyramid.** Pilgrims, delegates and envoys wait in the outer court for her word. She wants someone to keep the line in order and note who comes to ask what.
2. **The Bone-Reader's claim.** A Bone-Reader at a village cairn says he has read an answer in a dead man's body that the Oracle has not been given. She wants the claim weighed away from the pyramid.
3. **The chieftains' question.** Three delegates ask to put a question of their own to her. She will hear them only with a neutral witness at the chamber door.
