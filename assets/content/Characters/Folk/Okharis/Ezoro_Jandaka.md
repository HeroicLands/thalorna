# Appearance {#appearance}

Ezoro Jándaka is a 33-year-old man who stands 5' 8" tall and is of medium build. He has brown skin stained at the fingers and forearms by tanbark, cropped black hair and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ezoro Jándaka is the chieftain of the [[affiliation-yeshirotrb|Yéshiro Tribe]] and lives at [[place-yeshirovlg|Yéshiro Village]]. His tribe strips acacia bark and tans the hides the herding tribes send, and he is the youngest chieftain at the moot.

## Background

**Formation.** He worked the tanning pits from the age of ten and knew every pit's liquor by its smell.

**Accession.** He took the chieftaincy at twenty-eight by descent and the elders' consent, and the Master of the Pits still keeps the tanning.

## Psyche

### Motivation

He wants the hides sent to him tanned and returned in leather, and the herding tribes' debts to his pits paid.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The unpaid hides.** A herding tribe owes his pits a season's leather, and he wants a witness when the debt is named.
