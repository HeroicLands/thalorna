# Appearance {#appearance}

Mbeleth Órungi is a 54-year-old man who stands 6' 0" tall and is of medium build. He is a tall, lean man with brown skin lined deep by the grass-country sun, short gray-black hair and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Mbeleth Órungi is the chieftain of the [[affiliation-talumengtrb|Tálumeng Tribe]] and lives at [[place-talumengvlg|Tálumeng Village]]. His tribe is the largest of the village tribes, and its wealth is cattle. The Khelathi embassies come to his fold, and he gives them water and no more.

## Background

**Formation.** He drove his father's cattle through the dry months from boyhood and learned the grazing rotation by heart.

**Accession.** He took the chieftaincy at thirty-nine by descent and the elders' consent, and the Herd-Reckoner counts the beasts every rains.

## Psyche

### Motivation

He wants the tribe's herds counted by its own Reckoner and no gift booked as tribute.
