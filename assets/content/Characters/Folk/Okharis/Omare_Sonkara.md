# Appearance {#appearance}

Omare Sónkara is a 47-year-old man who stands 6' tall and is heavily built. He has ebony skin, cropped black hair and dark brown eyes, and a long raised scar of the Brand runs the length of his left forearm. He wears a red war-mantle over his temple robe and moves with the economy of someone who has drilled for decades.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Omare Sónkara is the Warden of the Flame, in the temple's tongue the Korthari Nkaring, head of [[place-vuthraka|The Wild Flame]] and its temple of war. He joins the rule of the garrison town to command of the armies of [[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]] in war, and he defers to the High Pyremant of [[place-zarhanis|The First Flame]] in doctrine and acts on his own in the rest.

He is stern, direct and devout, and he thinks the frontier is held by drill and not by prayer, though he prays.

## Background

**The Trial.** He was born to a herding household and came to Vuthraka at sixteen. At nineteen he passed the Trial of Flames and took the scar of the Brand, which made him one of the Ashborn.

**The ranks.** He rose as a Soldier of Fire and a Guardian of Battle, and for twenty years he led the garrison's drills on the southern margin where the grassland meets the wall of the [[place-cntrlrnfrsts|Central Rainforests]].

**The wardenship.** The temple named him Warden six years ago. This year he asks the tribes for more grain, cattle and riders, and he reads the Oracle's silence as a reason to arm the frontier against whatever she will not say.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is formal, humorless and fair, and he drills the garrison himself before dawn.

### Motivation

He wants the frontier armed before the answer comes, so that no answer finds it short, and he wants the chieftains to pay what they owe.

### Strengths

The Ashborn and the garrison follow him without question, and he knows the southern margin better than any man in the realm.

### Weaknesses

He cannot call the Host without the King, and his demands make each chieftain's count come out shorter. He reads a late draw as an insult to the Flame.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nkaruthar|The Eternal Flame]]**: Warden of the Flame (Korthari Nkaring).

## Plot Hooks

1. **The short draw.** He counts each absent rider against a clan and wants a party to carry his count to three folds and bring back the reasons.
2. **The south gate.** He wants scouts to walk the forest margin ten days south and report what stands on it, since he will not arm against what nobody has seen.
3. **The dues in grain.** A cart-train of barley bound for the garrison is a week late on the Zarhanis road, and he wants a party to find where it stopped.
