# Appearance {#appearance}

Bérathi Kárambi is a 39-year-old woman who stands 5' 4" tall and is of light build. She has smooth dark brown skin, black hair in a crown of braids and dark brown eyes, and she rubs cloth between thumb and finger as she talks.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Bérathi Kárambi is the chieftain of the [[affiliation-zhikandetrb|Zhíkande Tribe]] and lives at [[place-zhikandevlg|Zhíkande Village]]. Her tribe grows flax in the river fields and weaves linen, and she sends the finest bolts to the moot as proof of what the tribe can pay.

## Background

**Formation.** She learned the looms at her aunt's side and wove her first bolt at nine.

**Accession.** She took the chieftaincy at thirty by descent and the elders' consent, and the Keeper of the Looms still judges the cloth.

## Psyche

### Motivation

She wants the tribe's linen priced as the finest in the temple-cities and the dues paid in bolts, not in flax.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The priced linen.** Her bolts are entered at the coarse rate in the [[place-zarhanis|Zarhanis]] registers, and she wants a trader to value them at the [[place-kaljekor|Kaljekor]] quays and report.
