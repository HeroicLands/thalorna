# Appearance {#appearance}

Mbika Pánawe is a 41-year-old woman who stands 5' 7" tall and is of medium build. She has brown skin, black hair in thin braids bound at the nape and brown eyes, and she walks the threshing floor with her feet planted wide.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Mbika Pánawe is the chieftain of the [[affiliation-wambuchitrb|Wámbuchi Tribe]] and lives at [[place-wambuchivlg|Wámbuchi Village]]. Her tribe grows barley and sorghum for the temple registers, a fixed entry that this year's festival raised, and she counts the sheaves at every floor.

## Background

**Formation.** She worked the threshing floors from childhood and learned the measures from the Keeper of the Threshing Floors.

**Accession.** She took the chieftaincy at thirty-one by descent and the elders' consent, and the Keeper still measures what is owed.

## Psyche

### Motivation

She wants the grain entry fixed again at the old measure and entered the same way every year.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The new measure.** The tribute measure at [[place-sembari|Sembari]] is a handbreadth larger than the village's, and she wants someone to stand at the weighing and report the difference.
