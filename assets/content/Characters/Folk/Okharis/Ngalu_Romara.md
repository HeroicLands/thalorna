# Appearance {#appearance}

Ngalu Rómara is a 35-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. He has brown skin weathered by the frontier sun, cropped black hair and dark brown eyes. He sits a horse like a chair and wears a worn riding cloak, with notched tally-sticks at his belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ngalu Rómara is Captain of the Host of [[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]], commander of the king's armed men, who are drawn from the temple-cities and the tribes together. He keeps his quarters at [[place-vuthraka|The Wild Flame]], where the garrison and the riders of the Host assemble, and he answers to the King, who alone may call the Host.

He is blunt, tireless and exact with numbers.

## Background

**The herder's son.** He was born in a cattle household of the [[affiliation-talumengtrb|Tálumeng Tribe]] and rode in the tribe's levy at fifteen.

**The garrison.** At nineteen he went to Vuthraka as a rider of the Host and served eleven years on the frontier under the Warden of the Flame. He learned to keep the temple's men and the clans' riders in one camp.

**The captaincy.** The Warden named him for the post and the King confirmed it, because a Captain must be trusted by the cities and the tribes alike. He has held it five years. This year he waits for a summons the King cannot give while the clans' yearly draw of riders comes in late and short.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is plain, patient with men and impatient with excuses.

### Motivation

He wants the Host able to ride the day the King calls it, and he wants to know which clan is holding its riders back before that day comes.

### Strengths

The riders of both the cities and the tribes will follow him, and he can say from memory how many horses each clan owes.

### Weaknesses

He cannot call the Host himself, and a captain with a full tally and no summons has nothing to do but count. He tends to read a late rider as a disloyal one.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-okharis|Okharis]]**: Captain of the Host, commanding the king's armed men.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The short draw.** He wants a party to ride into the folds and learn which clan keeps its riders home, and why.
2. **The envoy's offer.** A Khelathi envoy has asked to buy a company of riders. He wants someone at the chieftain's fire when the price is named.
3. **The count of horses.** His tally-sticks and the registers at Zarhanis disagree by forty horses. He wants the mounts at one fold counted.
