# Appearance {#appearance}

Omilo Tárombu is a 34-year-old man who stands 5' 11" tall and is of medium build. He has ebony skin, cropped black hair and dark brown eyes, and he stands with the weight on one foot as if about to mount.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Omilo Tárombu is the chieftain of the [[affiliation-magaratrb|Magara Tribe]] and lives at [[place-magaravlg|Magara Village]]. His tribe breeds and breaks horses on the open grass, and its families keep the Spirit Blades. The Khelathi offers for horsemen come to him first.

## Background

**Formation.** He broke his first horse at eight and rode raids against the neighbors' herds at sixteen.

**Accession.** He took the chieftaincy at twenty-six by descent and the elders' consent, after his father's death, and the War-Leader still takes the riders out.

## Psyche

### Motivation

He wants his riders spent only when the King calls the Host, and the Blade families' standing at every council kept.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The offer for riders.** A Khelathi general has asked for a company of horsemen, and he wants an outsider to read the terms before he answers.
