# Appearance {#appearance}

Ngoro Mbúlawe is a 45-year-old man who stands 5' 9" tall and is of medium build. He has dark brown skin, a shaved head and brown eyes, and a net-cord scar crosses his right palm.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ngoro Mbúlawe is the chieftain of the [[affiliation-ngorashatrb|Ngorasha Tribe]] and lives at [[place-ngorashavlg|Ngorasha Village]]. His tribe nets the river shallows from reed boats and smokes the catch, and he counts the dues against the boats that go out.

## Background

**Formation.** He worked a reed boat from the age of seven and learned the reaches from the Keeper of the Reaches.

**Accession.** He took the chieftaincy at thirty-four by descent and the elders' consent, and the Keeper of the Reaches still allots the shallows.

## Psyche

### Motivation

He wants the smoked-fish dues fixed to the catch and the shallows left to the boats that know them.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The empty nets.** The catch has thinned at the upper reach, and he wants someone to ride the bank with him and see why before the festival.
