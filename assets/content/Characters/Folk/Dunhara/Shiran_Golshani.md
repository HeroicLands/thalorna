# Appearance {#appearance}

Shiran Golshani is a 57-year-old woman who stands 6'0" tall with a heavy build. She has tawny skin, sun-kissed complexion, dark brown hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Shiran Golshani, styled Lady, is a widow and the holder of the date groves that her late husband's family planted in the wadi country round [[place-golmakhend|Golmakhend]]. She collects sacred objects made for use in a rite and not for show. She has commissioned three major pieces from [[being-darzshshvn|Darzash Shavan]], and she is the carver's most effective advocate in the circles of the court that Darzash will not enter.

## Background

**The groves.** She married into a family of grove-holders at nineteen and kept its accounts for twenty years. When her husband died she kept the groves, the water-shares that feed them and the house, and sent away the kinsmen who offered to manage them.

**The collection.** She began with a bronze lamp-stand from her husband's mother's dowry and buys directly from the maker where she can. She keeps the pieces in a room off the inner court and lights the stand every evening.

**The carver.** She found Darzash through a dealer who admitted he did not know the maker. She has commissioned a meditation staff, a reliquary and a vessel, and tells every guest who admires them the maker's name.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is plain-spoken, patient and formidable.

### Motivation

She wants the collection to go to a temple and not to a dealer, and the groves to pass undivided to her niece.

### Strengths

She knows what the wadi's flow is worth, and priests and merchants alike trust her word.

### Weaknesses

She cannot let a piece she loves be owned by anyone else, and she suspects the motives of anyone who offers to take her collection off her hands.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-darzshshvn|Darzash Shavan]]: The carver she champions. Darzash receives her visits and no one else's.
- [[being-ahrashtamira|Ahrash Tamiran]]: The spice trader who buys from the same carver and has twice tried to outbid her for a piece.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]**: a Notable of the wadi country, a landholder whose word carries in local matters.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The water-share.** The villages below the groves say her late husband's share of the wadi was never entered in the registers of the Keeper of the Wells. She needs a witness to carry the old deed from the registers at [[place-shebarakhend|Shebarakhend]] before the clerk at [[place-tazhinakhend|Tazhinakhend]] rules.
2. **The staff.** A Tellumi exile court wants to buy her meditation staff for its prince's chapel, and its agents have grown insistent since she refused. She asks a party to keep the staff out of their reach until the harvest festival, when she has promised it to the temple.
3. **The fourth piece.** She wants a fourth commission, a lamp for the grove-holders' shrine, and Darzash declines. She sends a party to learn what in a commission calls the carver, since the craftsperson will not say.
