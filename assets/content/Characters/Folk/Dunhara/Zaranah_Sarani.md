# Appearance {#appearance}

Zaranah Sarani is a 55-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall with a massive build. She has tawny skin, ruddy complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zaranah Sarani, styled Lady, is the largest horse-dealer of [[place-parzavad|Parzavad]] and the holder of the pastures that border the town's pens. She is fifty-five, massive of build, and the first buyer at the autumn fair, where the herding clans sell sheep, goats, horses and camels. She has contracted for the services of [[being-rudavahfrd|Rudavah Farrud]] since Rudavah was a girl and gives the ostler political protection when her stubbornness creates problems with authorities.

## Background

**The pastures.** Her father held a stud on the plain and left her the herd and a debt. She cleared the debt in nine years and has since quadrupled the herd.

**The ostler.** She hired the yard of Master Ostler Velkash for her stud, and the girl who kept its night watches was Rudavah. She has contracted for Rudavah's services ever since, and has put her name between the ostler and the officials Rudavah offends.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is jovial, loud and shrewd, and she judges a person by how he handles a horse.

### Motivation

She wants the finest stud on the plain, and she wants the fair's clans to sell their best colts to her before anyone else.

### Strengths

She knows horses and their breeding, and she has the money and the standing to keep a friend out of trouble.

### Weaknesses

She cannot refuse a fine animal at any price, and she bears a grudge for a bad sale for years.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-rudavahfrd|Rudavah Farrud]]: The ostler she protects and whose judgment she trusts above her own grooms'.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]**: a Notable of the plain, a landholder whose word carries at the fair.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The autumn fair.** Two kinds of raider want the fair's herds, and the clans bring their own riders. Zaranah hires a party to guard her string of horses from the pens to [[place-tahrodan|Tahrodan]].
2. **The ruling on the wells.** The clerk of the Keeper of the Wells at Parzavad has ruled against her stud's draw on the town's spring. She asks a party to take her appeal to [[place-shebarakhend|Shebarakhend]].
3. **The mare that was not hers.** A mare in her stud carries the brand of a clan that has not sold to her. Zaranah wants strangers to learn how it came there before the clan comes to the fair.
