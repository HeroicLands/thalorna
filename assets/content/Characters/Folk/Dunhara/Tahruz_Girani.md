# Appearance {#appearance}

Tahruz Girani is a 69-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall with a medium build. He has tawny skin, leathery complexion, black hair, and green eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tahruz Girani, called the Venerable, is the oldest merchant captain working out of [[place-valmakhend|Valmakhend]], the port of the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]. He is sixty-nine and has crossed the strait to [[place-qudumel2|Kudumel]] and [[place-pashinar|Pashinar]] more times than he can count, in the Balinuni's broad ferries as a boy and in his own coaster for nearly thirty years. He is the closest friend of the pilot [[being-venradzrdn|Venrad Azardan]], and he is one of the few people the pilot trusts.

## Background

**The ferry-boy.** He started on a Balinuni ferry at twelve, carrying mules and bales between the quay and the islands. He bought a share in a coaster at thirty and a coaster of his own at forty.

**The captain.** He has since sailed the coast and the strait without losing a hull to the [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]], which he credits to the pilots he hired and, in the last ten years, to the one he calls his godson. He offers Venrad navigation contracts whenever he has a cargo worth the risk.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is slow, humorous and watchful. He tells a story twice, the second time as a lesson.

### Motivation

He wants one more good crossing and a quay-house for his old age, and he wants Venrad to outlive the risks Venrad takes.

### Strengths

He knows every port on both shores and the debts of every factor in them.

### Weaknesses

He tolerates Venrad's recklessness because he sees himself in it, and he has stopped being able to say no to the young.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-venradzrdn|Venrad Azardan]]: The pilot he treats as a godson and hires for every dangerous crossing.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]**: a Notable of the port, a captain whose word carries on the quay.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The factor's ledger.** A factor of Pashinar who fled to Valmakhend with his ledger asks Tahruz for passage anywhere but home. Tahruz wants a party to learn what the ledger holds before he decides whether the Balinuni would pay more for it.
2. **The winter crossing.** A cargo of lamp oil must cross the strait before the winter wind closes the passage. Tahruz asks a party to guard it, and Venrad to pilot, and the party to keep Venrad from taking the short way.
3. **The short tally.** A cargo of hides Tahruz carries for a Balinuni factor is lightered ashore at Valmakhend by a crew he did not hire, and the tally comes out short by a tenth. He asks a party to learn which crew it was and whose ledger shows the missing bales.
