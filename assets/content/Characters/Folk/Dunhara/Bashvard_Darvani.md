# Appearance {#appearance}

Bashvard Darvani is a 61-year-old man who stands 6'2" tall with a medium build. He has tawny skin, weathered complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Bashvard Darvani commands the garrison of [[place-gharzadez|Gharzadez]], the barred fort where [[place-marchroad|the March Road]] comes out of the desert into the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]], and his men call him Fortress Commander. He holds the post as an Officer of the Court, answering to the _tagemader_ of the town for its walls and to the Host for his riders. He is sixty-one and relies on [[being-delnshfrdn|Delanash Ferudan]] as he relies on no other officer.

## Background

**The long service.** He took the Kingdom's pay as a spearman at sixteen and rose through the Host's mounted companies. He has held Gharzadez longer than any commander before him.

**The volunteer.** Twenty-one years ago a tall freckled girl of the desert tribes came to the gate asking for work. He saw a woman of great strength with no tribe to answer to, put her on the wall, and has promoted her at every chance since.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is dry, tired and exact. He keeps the fort's accounts himself and speaks of his own decline as of a wall that needs repair.

### Motivation

He wants the garrison to outlast him in the hands of an officer who knows its horses by name.

### Strengths

He knows every sheikh on the March Road, and the garrison would ride after him without a question.

### Weaknesses

He is aging, and he has let the fort's business gather round one officer.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-delnshfrdn|Delanash Ferudan]]: His senior officer in all but title.
- [[being-razasharperu|Razashar Perudan]]: A younger captain he is training for the next post up.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]**: an Officer of the Court.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The stage beyond the gate.** Two tribes of the edge each claim the right to escort trains across the desert stage beyond Gharzadez, and caravans wait inside the walls while they quarrel. Bashvard wants a party to carry the Kingdom's offer of a shared escort to both camps and to bring back what each would take.
2. **The late pay.** The garrison's pay has not come from the Yorvabun's clerks, and the grain is short. He sends a party to [[place-tahrodan|Tahrodan]] to learn who holds the silver.
3. **The riders in the reeds.** The kinless of the desert raid the last stage of the road where the trains are slowest, and his patrols find no tracks. He wants hired riders who can follow a trail into the dunes.

:::secret {title="Bashvard's unsent letter"}
Bashvard has drafted a letter to the Vashkader of the army recommending Delanash Ferudan as his successor. It would be the first such recommendation for a woman of the desert, and he has not sent it, for fear that naming her will end her chance before the Shebarader's grant can be won. He keeps it in the cover of the garrison ledger.

**Hook: the ledger.** Anyone who reads the garrison ledger finds the letter. Whoever carries it to the tagemader, or to Zarvash Kaveran, decides whether Delanash Ferudan wins or loses the command.
:::
