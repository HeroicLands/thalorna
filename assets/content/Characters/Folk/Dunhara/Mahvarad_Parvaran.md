# Appearance {#appearance}

Mahvarad Parvaran is a 44-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall with a medium build. He has tawny skin, leathery complexion, dark brown hair, and amber eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Mahvarad Parvaran is the chieftain of the [Soltrev Clan]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}, one of the clans that [[being-shadznkmrz|Shadezan Kamruz]] defeated. He is forty-four, and his people graze their flocks on the open plain round [[place-parzavad|Parzavad]], where the herding clans sell their flocks at the autumn fair. He harbors deep resentment, and he plots constant rebellion, seeking alliances with other displaced chieftains to overthrow Shadezan's rule.

## Background

**The clan.** The Soltrev were rivals of the Kamruz long before Shadezan's rise, and Mahvarad's father fought the old chieftain of the Kamruz on the day he fell. When Shadezan took his clan's chieftainship, the Soltrev were among the first he attacked.

**The plain.** The Soltrev were driven from their pastures in the desert and have wintered on the plain near Parzavad since. Mahvarad pays the Shebarader's herd tax in animals and trades his flocks at the fair, and he holds his clan together by the memory of what it lost.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is proud, patient and bitter, with an exile's trick of telling a grievance as if it were a prophecy.

### Motivation

He wants the Soltrev back on their pastures and Shadezan under the clan's knives.

### Strengths

He is a courtier and a warrior both, and the other displaced chieftains trust his word.

### Weaknesses

He has too few riders and too many grievances, and he talks of rebellion in front of men who sell what they hear.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-shadznkmrz|Shadezan Kamruz]]: The chieftain who defeated his clan.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-dunhartrbs|Dunhara Tribes]]**: a Chieftain by descent and consent, holding his clan without a territory.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The fair.** In a bad year the town at Parzavad holds more armed herders than garrison. Mahvarad brings more riders to the autumn fair than a herder needs, and the tagemader asks a party to learn why.
2. **The well-ruling.** The clerk of the Keeper of the Wells at Parzavad rules slowly and seldom in the clans' favor. Mahvarad asks a party to carry the clans' complaint to the Keeper of the Wells at [[place-shebarakhend|Shebarakhend]].
3. **The messenger.** A woman of a Soltrev household that stayed under Shadezan's rule arrives at Parzavad with word from home and no one to trust. Mahvarad asks a party to see her safely out of the fair.

:::secret {title="The count at the pens"}
Mahvarad reports fewer beasts at the fair's pens than the Soltrev own, and pays the herd tax on the smaller count. He keeps the rest in a hollow of the plain north of the town, a herd large enough to mount the clan's return.

**Hook: the hidden herd.** A party that follows his herdsmen at dusk finds the second herd, and the tagemader of Parzavad pays well for the knowledge.
:::
