# Appearance {#appearance}

Artavin Kaviran is a 73-year-old man who stands 6'2" tall with a medium build. He has tawny skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and green eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Artavin Kaviran, addressed as Father, is a Delkoshekh of the [Míthara Temple]{.name data-type="place/structure"} at [[place-venzelakhend|Venzelakhend]]: a teacher-priest of seventy-three who has taught the keeping of the flame to three generations of the town's fire-keepers. He is the one priest in Venzelakhend who sees in the carving of [[being-darzshshvn|Darzash Shavan]] a prayer as well as a craft, and he gives her both commissions and counsel.

## Background

**The grove villages.** He was born in a terrace village above the presses and came down to the temple at ten to feed its flame. He was ordained a Delkoshekh at thirty and has taught the rites of [[lore-mitharadty|Míthara]], the first light and the craft by which made things endure, ever since. He was never made Shekhul and says he is glad of it, because a Shekhul answers for the flame and a Delkoshekh answers for the pupil.

**The carver.** He first saw Darzash's work as a box left on the temple steps by a woman who gave no name. He sent for the maker, asked for a lamp-stand for the altar, and has asked for a piece each festival since.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is gentle, slow-spoken and entirely immovable on the order of a rite. He teaches by asking questions he already knows the answers to.

### Motivation

He wants the altar's furnishings made by hands that mean them before he can no longer see them.

### Strengths

He reads a flame as another reads a face, and the town seeks his counsel on matters that have nothing to do with the temple.

### Weaknesses

His sight is failing, and he hides it in the dark of the fire-house, where he does not need it. He holds to the rite where a kinder reading would serve.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-darzshshvn|Darzash Shavan]]: The carver he commissions. She comes to the temple's back court to talk and refuses to take a seat.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-mizhara|Faith of Míthara]]**: a Delkoshekh, and the senior teacher of the temple's fire-keepers.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]**: a Notable of the town by the standing of his temple.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The best pressing.** The press-owners of Venzelakhend hold back the first pressing for buyers from Taruzel, and the priests have asked the vazhmader to rule that the temples' endowments are owed it. Artavin carries the petition and wants strangers to find out what the press-owners have sold, and to whom, before the hearing.
2. **The altar-stand.** He wants a new stand for the altar carved before his sight goes, and Darzash will not work to order. He asks a party to bring her a length of timber from the oldest grove above the town, which its villagers would sooner keep.
3. **The lamp that went out.** A household's lamp, lit from the temple's flame, has gone out three nights running, and the household reads it as an omen. Artavin wants someone to learn whether the lamp's oil was cut before the household's fear reaches the quarter.
