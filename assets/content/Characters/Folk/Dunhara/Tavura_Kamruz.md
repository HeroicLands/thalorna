# Appearance {#appearance}

Tavura Kamruz is a 78-year-old woman who stands 6'0" tall with a medium build. She has tawny skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and green eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tavura Kamruz is the shaman of the Kamruz camp at [[place-ghavulavad|Ghavulavad]] and the mother of [[being-shadznkmrz|Shadezan Kamruz]]. She is seventy-eight, and the resident practitioner of the [[affiliation-strmcltsbzhrms|Storm Cults]] who conducts the camp's births, deaths and rites of war. She blessed her son with the rituals of [[lore-bazhramisdty|Bazhrámish]], the Thunder Rider, asking the god to give him strength and fury, and she still reads the wind for his camp.

## Background

**The camp.** She was born in a lesser tent of the Kamruz clan and apprenticed as a girl to the camp's old wind-reader. She became the camp's practitioner in her thirties and has held the place since.

**The blessing.** She conducted her son's naming at the first thunderstorm after his birth and his blessing before every raid he led as a young man. The camp counts his rise as the god's answer to her asking.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is gruff, shrewd and seldom wrong. She speaks of the god as of an in-law whose moods she knows.

### Motivation

She wants her son's house to outlast his temper and her grandsons to outlast each other.

### Strengths

She reads weather as exactly as any shaman in the camps, and the petitioners who cannot reach her son come to her tent.

### Weaknesses

She is old, and she is the one person in the camp who cannot refuse Shadezan and who knows what he is.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-shadznkmrz|Shadezan Kamruz]]: Her son, the chieftain the storm cults recognize as a vessel of their god.
- [[being-firavdprsk|Firavad Paresak]]: The storm-shaman of Ghavulavad, who consults her on the old rites.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-strmcltsbzhrms|Storm Cults of Bazhrámish]]**: the Camp Practitioner of her son's camp.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The old woman's tent.** Petitioners who cannot reach Shadezan come to her instead. She asks a party to carry a message to a sheikh that she does not want to give her son's riders.
2. **The dry gardens.** The palm gardens of Ghavulavad have gone a month without rain, and she calls the rain-rite, which the Rain-Callers say is theirs to call. She asks a party to fetch an old rain-stone from a camp that keeps one.
3. **The reading for a grandson.** One of Shadezan's younger sons comes to her tent asking for a reading, and will not say what he wants it to answer. She asks a party to sit outside the tent and learn who sent him.

:::secret {title="Tavura's reading"}
At her son's naming the wind told her that he would hold the desert and lose his sons to it. She asked the god for strength and fury, and she gave him both. She has told no one, and she reads the wind each spring in the hope that it has changed.

**Hook: the spring reading.** A party that sits with her at the spring reading hears her say a name that is not her son's, and the name belongs to one of his sons.
:::
