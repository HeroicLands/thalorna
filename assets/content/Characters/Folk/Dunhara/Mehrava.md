# Appearance {#appearance}

Mehrava is a 55-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of light build. She is thin and upright, with tawny skin, graying black hair in a single braid, narrow features and gray eyes. A forked mark runs across her left cheek. She wears indigo, and she has the stillness of someone listening to the sky.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Mehrava is the _ruzhogar_, the senior elder, of [The Lightning-Marked]{.name data-type="affiliation"}, the smallest and most prestigious of the five lineages of [[affiliation-strmcltsbzhrms|the Storm Cults of Bazhrámish]]. The cults regard the lineage as touched by the god in vision or by lightning, and she leads it by an authority that is personal and not inherited. The accuracy of what she reads in the sky is the source of it, and also of the friction: when her reading stands against the readings of the larger lineages, the cults have two readings and one season. She will not soften a reading to keep the peace among the lineages.

She sits in the Council of Elders at the [[place-stoneofranaz|Stone of Ranaz]] at the season-turns. The Council is deadlocked this year because the Wind-Readers, led by [Razashar]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, and the Lightning-Marked each withhold assent from the other's proposals, so no ruling passes, including the summons of the Sovidur storm-shaman that the northern chieftains want. Between the season-turns she lives at the lineage's salt-toll tent on the northern edge of the [[place-palefloor|Pale Floor]], and the toll is what keeps the lineage on the Floor at all. On the nights of the great storms she sits in the hall of the [[place-twrlghtnmrkd|Tower of the Lightning-Marked]] where the lineages sit, and climbs no higher.

Of [[being-vhrnmrzvn|Vahrun Marzuvan]] she says that he never died, and she declines to say more to outsiders. What she reads in the sky above the Floor she does not tell them either.

## Background

**The mark.** Mehrava was born in a herding camp at the edge of the desert, and at twelve the lightning struck her as she brought the goats in under a storm. She lived, with the forked mark across her cheek, and the Lightning-Marked took her as an apprentice that season.

**The lineage.** She learned the sky on the Pale Floor and was a young reader of the lineage when the Council denounced Vahrun Marzuvan. She is one of the elders who tell the end of his story in the lineage's few words.

**The elder.** Her readings proved true season after season, and when the senior elder before her died the lineage gave her the authority without a dissenting voice. She has sat in the Council of Elders since, and her quarrel with the Wind-Readers is the Council's deadlock.

## Psyche

### Personality

Mehrava is plain, still and unhurried. She speaks little and says each thing once. Misreading is the gravest failure a practitioner can commit, and she holds herself to it first.

### Motivation

She wants the storm's word kept honest. She wants the Floor safe to cross and the lineage's standing with the Council intact, and she does not trade the one for the other.

### Strengths

Her readings are accurate. The cults that argue with her still come back to ask for them, and a practitioner whose readings prove true carries what the camp has no other way to buy.

### Weaknesses

She cannot soften a hard truth, so the people who most need to hear one are those who stop asking. Her quarrel with the Wind-Readers has frozen the Council.

## Social

### Companions

The apprentices of the lineage who keep the salt-toll tent on the Floor and read the sky for the crossings.

### Patrons

The lineage, whose authority she holds, and the caravans that pay the toll and depend on her naming the day.

### Enemies

The senior elder of the Wind-Readers, whose lineage is the largest and who presides at most Council sittings. Anyone who would take the iron crown from the tower.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-strmcltsbzhrms|Storm Cults of Bazhrámish]]**: Mehrava holds the office of Ruzhogar, senior elder of the Lightning-Marked lineage, and sits in the Council of Elders.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The marked stone.** A party that tells Mehrava what it intends and tells her the truth can win the marked stone of her lineage, a flat pebble scored with a fork ([[scenario-irncrwnstrm|What Walks Above the Pale Floor]]).
2. **The deadlock at the Stone.** Petitioners with the two Old Promise cases camp at the Stone of Ranaz ahead of the next season-turn, and the Council cannot rule on either. A chieftain wants a party to carry an offer to Mehrava that the Wind-Readers will accept from no one else.
3. **The day named.** A caravan-master wants the party to witness the toll tent's reading of the sky, because he has been told the day she names is the day the storm takes the caravan that pays.

:::secret {title="The crown on the brow"}
Vahrun is not dead. He holds shut a crack in the sky above the tower, and Mehrava reads the crack every night of the great storms. She tells no lie and will not help the party kill him, because she cannot say that he is dead. She also has no wish to see him freed. When the storm ebbs he offers the true crown to anyone the storm accepts, and she says plainly what the cost to the wearer is.

A crown brought to the Stone sets the Council arguing for a season, and the Warrior's Circle is asked whether the Old Promise binds against a Dunhari who has become a door. If the party asks, she will carry his case to the Council of Elders, to have him named no master but a warden of the crack.
:::
