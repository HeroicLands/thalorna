# Appearance {#appearance}

Harumaz Baravi is a 59-year-old man who stands 6'3" tall with a heavy build. He has tawny skin, leathery complexion, chestnut hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Harumaz Baravi is a master jeweler of [[place-qarvelakhend|Qarvelakhend]] and the senior voice of the traditionalist faction of the town's master jewelers, whom the town calls Master Harumaz. He is fifty-nine, heavy-handed and respected, and he holds that a jeweler's patterns descend from the first masters and are not the maker's to change. He views the experimental work of [[being-prynbrmndn|Paryan Barmandan]] as disrespect to established convention and has quietly opposed her rise among the masters.

## Background

**The bench.** He took his master's mark at twenty-six and has worked the same bench for thirty-three years. The goldsmiths of Qarvelakhend know him as the man who will not alter a setting for a client's whim, and his clients return.

**The masters.** He led the traditionalist faction through two contested votes of the town's master jewelers and won both. He believes the masters' purpose is to keep the craft from drift, and his patterns hang in the town's workshops as the standard.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is slow, formal and unforgiving of carelessness. He spreads criticism by asking questions of his colleagues, never by statements.

### Motivation

He wants the craft handed on intact, and he wants Paryan's rise to stop before the masters' standards go with it.

### Strengths

He knows every old pattern and can date a piece within a generation by its setting.

### Weaknesses

He cannot judge work he cannot place in a tradition, and he has never seen Paryan's best.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-prynbrmndn|Paryan Barmandan]]: The younger master whose unorthodox settings he counts a threat to the masters' standards.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]**: a Notable of the town, a master whose word carries among the town's master jewelers.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The tray of old gold.** A dealer from the desert side brings Harumaz a tray of worn gold that he suspects came out of a grave. The priests of the town's fire-temple preach against the trade, and he asks a party to learn where the dealer found it before the priests learn that he held it.
2. **The royal commission.** A great house wants a classical set made for the Shebarader's household, and Harumaz and Paryan both bid. He asks a party to carry his pattern-book to the court's clerks of the household.
3. **The apprentice who left.** His best apprentice has gone to Paryan's bench. Harumaz wants him back, and he asks a party to learn what Paryan has offered him.
