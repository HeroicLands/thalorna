# Appearance {#appearance}

Deyena Shamayan is a 44-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall with a medium build. She has brown skin, weathered complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Deyena Shamayan, styled Captain, commands the mounted garrison of [[place-tahrodan|Tahrodan]] and, through it, the cavalry of the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]'s Host. She is forty-four and an Officer of the Court. Her riders keep the roads clear for a day's ride in every direction, meet the tribal escorts at the handover wells and ride the caravans down toward the coast. She contracts with [[being-rudavahfrd|Rudavah Farrud]] for the care and training of her best mounts.

## Background

**The saddle.** She joined the Host at seventeen as a trooper and rose by the roads: escort, patrol, the handover wells. She made captain at thirty-two.

**The mounts.** Her cavalry runs on horses bought at the fair at [[place-parzavad|Parzavad]] and trained at Tahrodan. She found Rudavah Farrud when a stallion two grooms had given up on recovered under her hands, and has paid whatever Rudavah asked since.

## Psyche

### Personality

She demands results and says so: blunt, fair and slow to praise.

### Motivation

She wants a cavalry that never loses a horse to carelessness, and an escort handover that never costs a man.

### Strengths

She reads ground and weather, knows her troop's horses by gait, and holds the loyalty of the tribes' escort-leaders.

### Weaknesses

She will not forgive a rider who mistreats a mount, and she trusts the roads too much to ask for help on them.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-rudavahfrd|Rudavah Farrud]]: The ostler whose judgment she values. Their relationship is complicated, because Deyena demands results and Rudavah demands that the animals be treated well.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]**: an Officer of the Court. Her riders are the Host's mounted arm.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The handover roster.** One tribe holds a blood-debt against a family in the garrison and refuses to hand over a caravan to any troop that carries that family's men. Deyena must choose which men ride the next handover, and she asks a party to learn from the tribe what it will accept.
2. **The short roster.** A fever has put a third of her riders in the infirmary a week before the autumn patrols, and she cannot man both the northern road and the handover. She hires a party to ride the handover in the troop's place and bring the tribe's caravan to the toll-house.
3. **The sealed bales.** The toll-house's clerk believes a priest has lent a temple's seal to a whole caravan, and the escort that rode with it was hers. She wants strangers to learn what the bales held before her troop is named.
