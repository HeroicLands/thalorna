# Appearance {#appearance}

Valhamin Goravan is a 57-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall with a light build. He has olive skin, ruddy complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Valhamin Goravan is a merchant lord of [[place-dorvakhend|Dorvakhend]], the walled caravan town on the southern shore of the [[place-bittersea|Bitter Sea]] where the road from Bizaria meets the Khazrin road. He is fifty-seven and a chartered Caravan-Master of the Shebarader, and every train bound west from the seas makes up its loads in his yards. He has employed the teamster [[being-farazunfrn|Farazun Afrun]] repeatedly, trusts him with his most valuable cargo and recommends him to other merchants.

## Background

**The yards.** He bought the first yard at Dorvakhend with the dowry of a wife who died young, and has bought a yard a decade since. He pays the town's dues without protest and holds the contracts that keep its two fresh streams in barley.

**The wagons.** Farazun first drove for him fourteen years ago. Valhamin saw that the man counted his animals before he counted his pay, and has trusted him with a load of his own ever since.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is large, unhurried and polite, with a banker's courtesy to those he means to outbid.

### Motivation

He wants every load that leaves Dorvakhend to pass the [[place-tahrodan|Tahrodan]] gate on his name, and he wants the road open in every season.

### Strengths

He knows the dues and the customs of three roads, and the herders of the plain sell to him first.

### Weaknesses

He underrates risks that do not cost him coin, and he is slow to trust any driver he has not hired for ten years.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The inspected load.** The tagemader's customs officers inspect loads at [[place-halzikos|Halzikos]] before they set out for Dorvakhend, and a load certified there arrives short on the scales. Valhamin wants a party to find where the seals are being changed.
2. **The camp-burners.** Black Flame warbands burn the herders' camps west of the town each summer, and the herders pay their tax late because the garrison does not ride. Valhamin will pay hired riders to guard the camps that sell to him.
3. **The wagon that did not come.** A wagon of Farazun's is two days late from the north, and Valhamin will not say that he is afraid. He asks a party to go up the Bizaria road and learn why.
