# Appearance {#appearance}

Yesera Parivan is a 41-year-old woman who stands 6'1" tall with a medium build. She has tanned skin, ruddy complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Yesera Parivan, styled Lady, commands the coastal survey ships of the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]] from [[place-valmakhend|Valmakhend]] as an Officer of the Court, answering to the Vashkader of the Waters. She is forty-one, and her ships chart the shoals, the landings and the coast's winds for the throne. She funds the most experimental work of the shipwright [[being-rshrnbrmndn|Rosharan Barmandan]] in return for first access to every new design.

## Background

**The chart-room.** She entered the Kingdom's coastal survey as a junior surveyor and learned the work in the pilots' hall of the port. She took command of the ships at thirty-five.

**The shipwright.** She commissioned Rosharan's first survey cutter after the old hulls proved too slow to map the strait crossing and the approaches to the [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]], and has bought each experimental design the yard has offered since. Her charts are the ones the pilots of the coast steer by.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is brisk, curious and impatient with ceremony. She writes everything down and trusts what she can measure.

### Motivation

She wants a chart of the whole coast, from the strait to the northern shore, drawn once and drawn right.

### Strengths

She reads a coast as a document, and she has a patron's eye for a hull that will carry a survey into water no one has sounded.

### Weaknesses

She spends her allowance faster than the Vashkader's clerks release it, and she cannot leave a gap in a chart alone.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-rshrnbrmndn|Rosharan Barmandan]]: The shipwright she funds. Yesera commissions the experimental hulls that the master shipwrights of the coast call reckless.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]**: an Officer of the Court.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The altered chart.** A copy of her chart of the Tamzir Shoals approaches has turned up in a chandler's shop with the soundings of one channel changed. Yesera wants strangers to learn who altered it before a hull is lost on the false marks.
2. **The cutter's trial.** A new survey cutter is ready for sea, and the Vashkader's clerks have not released its allowance. She hires a party as its first crew and promises the pay at the quay.
3. **The northern gap.** Her charts leave a gap on the coast north of the port, where the corsairs shelter. She asks a party to carry a leadsman's line there and measure what a cutter could use.
