# Appearance {#appearance}

The elders of the Lightning-Marked remember Vahrun Marzuvan as a tall, lean man with tanned weathered skin, black hair going gray and worn long, and a hooked nose under heavy brows, in a storm-walker's weathered wool, with an iron crown he forged himself on his head. Herders at the edge of the [[place-palefloor|Pale Floor]] describe a voice in the storm and nothing else.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Vahrun Marzuvan is a storm-walker of the _ghuzmogar_ and a _ruzhogar_ in his own lineage, and he stands among the Denounced of [[affiliation-strmcltsbzhrms|the Storm Cults]]. He took the title _Master of the Tower_ for himself. The Storm Cults are five lineages with no master over them, and the Council of Elders at the [[place-stoneofranaz|Stone of Ranaz]] never conferred it. The elders of [The Lightning-Marked]{.name data-type="affiliation"} say that he never died and that he went into the storm, and they say it the same way each time and decline to say what they mean by it.

Thieves are coming south for [[miscgear-crwnvahrun|his iron crown]], because a fence in [[place-qarvelakhend|Qarvelakhend]] pays well and asks nothing. The Warrior's Circle has a question it has never ruled on: whether the Old Promise covers a Dunhari the elders say walked into the sky.

## Background

**The lineage.** Vahrun was born into a storm-walking lineage of the _ghuzmogar_ and walked his first storm before he was grown. He rose to _ruzhogar_ of his lineage in middle life, by readings that proved true and a hand that could bring a bolt down where he pointed.

**The Floor.** In the years before about 690 AF he gathered the cults' youngest and boldest on the [[place-palefloor|Pale Floor]] and taught them to call the lightning down on purpose, to a spike and a place, and to read what he held to be the god's mood from where it fell. He took the title of Master of the Tower for the man who could do it.

**The denunciation.** About 690, near fifty, he claimed to be a vessel of [[lore-bazhramisdty|Bazhrámish]] without the assent of the gathered elders, and the Council denounced him ([[lore-vahrundenun|The Denunciation of Vahrun Marzuvan]]). The iron torc of a vessel was never given to him, so he wore a crown of his own forging in its place.

**The storm.** Soon after, a great storm came up out of a clear sky, and Vahrun climbed to the gallery of the [[place-twrlghtnmrkd|Tower of the Lightning-Marked]] alone. The bolt took the tower and the storm did not leave with it. In the morning he was gone, with no body, no ash and no crown.

## Psyche

### Personality

Vahrun was a man of great gifts and no patience for the Council. He is remembered as charismatic, exact and certain, a teacher who could make a bolt land where he chose and could not bear to be told that the god had not chosen it.

### Motivation

He wanted the lightning read and held, and wanted the title for the man who could do it. What he wants now, the elders of the lineage decline to say.

### Strengths

He called the lightning to a spike and a place, and he taught others to do it.

### Weaknesses

He took a title the Council never conferred and a vesselhood it never recognized, and he could not bear to be refused either.

## Social

### Companions

The apprentices he gathered on the Pale Floor.

### Patrons

None. He took what the Council would not give.

### Enemies

The Council of Elders that denounced him.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-strmcltsbzhrms|Storm Cults of Bazhrámish]]**: Vahrun stands among the Denounced, formally rejected before the lineages for claiming a vesselhood the Council never conferred.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The fence's commission.** A fence in Qarvelakhend pays a party to bring the iron crown out of the tower. A rival thief went south first and did not come back ([[scenario-irncrwnstrm|What Walks Above the Pale Floor]]).
2. **The pull of the storm.** Herders at the edge of the Floor tell of a voice in the storm, and of people who leave their camps at night and walk toward the tower. A sheikh wants a party to learn whose the voice is before more of his camp goes.
3. **The Circle's question.** The Warrior's Circle asks a party to carry to the Stone of Ranaz whatever it learns on the Floor, because the Old Promise cannot be ruled on until someone knows what Vahrun has become.

:::secret {title="The crown on the brow"}
The crown the elders call lost is the true crown, a ring of dull black metal burned into Vahrun's brow. He has never taken it off. He did not go into the storm to escape death: he went up to hold shut a crack that his own working made, and [[lore-theshadow|the Shadow]] answers him through it. He is not dead, and cannot let go.

A thin copy, iron with fused glass for gems, sits on a stool in the sealed room below the gallery. It is a lure wired to the iron of the stair: lifting it or moving the stool sends a bolt down every rung and marks the bearer to the storm.

When the storm ebbs, Vahrun offers the true crown to a storm-walker, or to anyone the storm accepts, who is willing to stand in his place. The cost falls on the wearer.
:::
