# Appearance {#appearance}

Aravdán the Stargazer is a 60-year-old man, 5'9" and light, with a scholar's stoop, tawny weathered skin, close-cropped gray hair and beard, bushy brows and dark eyes that settle on a speaker and stay. He wears the white robe of the flame and an ink stain on his right forefinger.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Aravdán the Stargazer is the High Shekhul of [[affiliation-ahurdaen|Ahurdaen]] at the [[place-grtfiretmpl|Great Fire-Temple of Amradad]] in [[place-shebarakhend|Shebarakhend]]: keeper of its flame, formal head of the Ashaian priesthood from the Vilérian shore to the Khazrin, and an astronomer of continental reputation. The faith is the established worship of the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom]], and he is as much a minister as a priest. He convenes the [[affiliation-cnclprinces|Council of the Princes]], the yearly meeting of the seven Tellumi houses, and has held the exile community together through three decades of small crises.

## Background

**The observatory.** He came to the temple at eleven, a grain-merchant's son who named the planets before he could read, and became the Delkoshekh of Astronomy at twenty-seven, after the sixteen years of study the Shekhulukhem require.

**The convenor.** He was named High Shekhul at thirty, and the Council passed to him with the temple. A convenor calls the session, keeps its order and rules on nothing.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is mild, exact and patient, and answers a quarrel with a question about the stars.

### Motivation

He wants the temple's reading of the sky to keep the throne's trust, and the exile courts to stay guests of the Kingdom.

### Strengths

He keeps the best tables in the Ashaian world, and the seven houses trust him because he rules for none.

### Weaknesses

He avoids a public quarrel until his silence is a ruling.

## Social

## Plot Hooks

1. **The scribe's tablet.** A scribe of Aravdán's observatory is held at the [[place-tahrodan|Tahrodan]] toll-house for noting the weights of a temple-sealed load. Aravdán asks a party to bring the scribe's tablet out before the clerk reads it, and does not say why it matters.
2. **The altered tables.** A copy of his tables lent to a Tellumi house has come back with the autumn figures changed, and he asks a party to learn at which court.
3. **The summons.** The seven houses meet at the opening of the session, and one prince has not answered. He asks a party to carry the summons to the oasis courts and to bring back the replies.

:::secret {title="The High Shekhul's silence"}
Aravdán judges that some priests at Tahrodan lend their temples' seals to loads no temple owns. He has said nothing, because a public word would give the governor a victory over every temple. He has asked the Keeper of the Fire-Lineage to list which seals are lent and by whom.

**Hook: the list.** A party that reads the list in the fire-lineage's archive finds priests he has not yet decided to protect.
:::
