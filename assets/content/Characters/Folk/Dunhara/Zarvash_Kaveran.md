# Appearance {#appearance}

Zarvash Kaveran is a 59-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall with a light build. He has olive skin, sun-kissed complexion, dark brown hair, and amber eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zarvash Kaveran is an elder of a tribe of the Dunhara Tribes that waters its herds at the outer springs of [[place-delmavad|Delmavad]] under the compact with the Kingdom. He is fifty-nine, pure-blood Dunhari, and the senior of his lineage. He holds that the garrison at [[place-gharzadez|Gharzadez]] promotes by merit and not by blood, and that this is how a warrior culture dies. He says so openly, and the conservative tribes listen.

## Background

**The lineage.** His household has grazed the western shore of the [[place-mirrorsea|Mirror Sea]] since before the town's walls. He became an Elder at forty, after the old man who held the office died in a feud he refused to end.

**The garrison.** He watched the garrison grow from a company of riders into a force the desert cannot ignore, and he does not forgive it for taking his sister's grandsons as soldiers.

**The campaign.** He names a freckled officer of the garrison to every sheikh he meets, as an outsider unfit for command and a symbol of foreign interests that mean to weaken Dunhari warrior culture.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is courteous, deliberate and hard.

### Motivation

He wants the desert's young men to follow a tribe's elders and not a garrison's captain.

### Strengths

He holds the loyalty of three lineages and knows every clause of the compact.

### Weaknesses

He mistakes habit for law and cannot hear that a rider might choose the garrison and stay a Dunhari.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-delnshfrdn|Delanash Ferudan]]: The officer he names an outsider unfit for command.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-dunhartrbs|Dunhara Tribes]]**: an Elder, whose recollection of custom settles the disputes the young cannot.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The broken compact.** The springs of Delmavad drop a hand's depth in the season the garrison at Gharzadez sinks a new well, and Zarvash says the compact is broken. He asks a party that belongs to neither side to measure the water before the tribes take their herds into the gardens.
2. **The elder's table.** He invites a party to eat his salt and carry his case against the garrison's rule of merit to the tagemader of Gharzadez; a guest who eats his salt is bound to him for the stated days.
3. **The outer gardens.** The kinless raid Delmavad's outer gardens at night. He will not let the garrison patrol the outer springs, which the compact gives to the tribes, and he wants strangers to guard the gardens without the garrison's colors.

:::secret {title="Zarvash's reader"}
Zarvash receives a monthly tally of Delanash Ferudan's decisions from a captain of the garrison, [[being-razasharperu|Razashar Perudan]], and reads it aloud to the conservative elders as proof. He persuaded Razashar that Delanash's command is corrupting Dunhari warrior traditions, and Razashar writes the tally knowingly, for Zarvash's campaign against her.

**Hook: the extra page.** A messenger of the garrison, not the captain, brings a page of the Gharzadez ledger to Zarvash's tent. It shows that Delanash's decisions have saved the garrison grain the elders have been told it lost.
:::
