# Appearance {#appearance}

Shuvar Shavan was a 54-year-old man who stood 5'11" tall with a light build. He had tawny skin, leathery complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Shuvar Shavan was a woodcrafter of the craft quarter of [[place-shebarakhend|Shebarakhend]], the capital of the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]], and the father of [[being-darzshshvn|Darzash Shavan]]. He taught her that every timber holds an inner form already, and that the craftsperson's duty is to find it and not to impose one. She worked at his bench until his death, when she was eighteen, and has not worked to anyone's order since.

## Background

**The bench.** He rented a bench in a shared yard near the garden quarter and made boxes, bowls and chair-frames for the households of the court. His work was good and sold slowly, because he would not hurry a piece, and he left debts as well as tools when he died.

**The teaching.** He let his daughter handle only offcuts for her first three years and gave her no instruction beyond one question: what is this wood already? He never told her whether her answers were right. She holds that this was the lesson.

**The death.** He died in the fall of her eighteenth year, of a lung sickness he had carried since the long seasons of sanding boxwood, and left her the bench, a chest of unfinished work and a roll of tools.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-darzshshvn|Darzash Shavan]]: His daughter, who learned the craft at his bench and keeps his tools.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]**: a free subject, owing the Shebarader's taxes and holding no standing.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The unfinished chest.** A house of the garden quarter still holds a chest he began for it and never finished, and its owners want it completed. Darzash will not touch it, and a party sent to persuade her finds that she has never opened the lid.
2. **The boxwood stock.** The seasoned boxwood he laid in for the court's commissions was pledged against a debt the year he died, and the lender's factor now offers it by the plank in the garden quarter. Darzash does not know it was ever pledged, and a broker who has found out names a price.
3. **The apprentice's bench.** A woodcrafter of the quarter claims to have been Shuvar's only apprentice and offers to complete his work. Darzash says her father had none, and the claim carries more weight with the court than her word.
