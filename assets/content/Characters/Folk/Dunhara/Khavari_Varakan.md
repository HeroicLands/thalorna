# Appearance {#appearance}

Khavari Varakan is a 49-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall with a light build. She has tawny skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Khavari Varakan, called the Sorcerer and styled Warlord, is a chieftain of the desert edge south of the [[place-mirrorsea|Mirror Sea]] in [[place-threeseas|the Three Seas]], where the open dunes begin. She leads a clan by descent and consent together, and she holds it by a power that her neighbors call by many names and she calls her craft. [[being-shadznkmrz|Shadezan Kamruz]] rules lands adjacent to hers, and he views her use of magic as cowardly, since it spares her the lance.

## Background

**The clan.** She took the chieftainship at twenty-six, when the chieftain before her fell to Khazrin raiders on the plain. She was the clan's only practitioner of the craft and the only one in sight when the raiders were driven back.

**The dispute.** Two tribes of the edge claim the right to escort the trains across the desert stage beyond [[place-gharzadez|Gharzadez]]. Her riders are one party to the dispute and Shadezan's the other, and neither can win it without the other losing.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is patient, ironic and quick to calculate. She speaks lightly of power she holds seriously.

### Motivation

She wants the clan's escort rights on the [[place-marchroad|March Road]] secured and her neighbor's contempt turned to fear.

### Strengths

She uses magic in battle, which few of the tribes can match, and she holds her riders by results.

### Weaknesses

She is outnumbered by Shadezan in riders and cannot afford a long war. The storm cults do not trust her craft.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-shadznkmrz|Shadezan Kamruz]]: The chieftain whose lands border hers and whose riders dispute the escort with hers.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-dunhartrbs|Dunhara Tribes]]**: a Chieftain, holding her clan by descent and consent.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The sorcerer's escort.** She offers a caravan-master a cheaper escort than Shadezan's riders and a promise that the train arrives. A party hired as the train's guards finds that it does.
2. **The rider's grudge.** A rider of Shadezan's was taken alive on the edge and is held in her camp. Shadezan sends an envoy and she asks a party to carry her terms.
3. **The challenge.** She dares Shadezan to settle the escort by a contest of champions and not of clans. She needs a party to carry the challenge and bring his answer back.

:::secret {title="Khavari's tithe"}
The kinless riders who raid the last stage of the March Road pay a tithe to her camp in exchange for water and a place to sell the plunder. Her escorts always arrive because the raiders she shelters spare her trains, and that is why caravan-masters hire them.

**Hook: the tithe.** A party hired to escort a train that the kinless raid finds that the raiders leave her riders alone, and that the plunder is sold at her camp.
:::
