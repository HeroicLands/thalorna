# Appearance {#appearance}

Mazinan Shirzari is a 54-year-old woman who stands 6'0" tall with a heavy build. She has tanned skin, weathered complexion, chestnut hair, and green eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Mazinan Shirzari is the Delkoshekh of Law at the [[place-grtfiretmpl|Great Fire-Temple of Amradad]] in [[place-shebarakhend|Shebarakhend]], the senior teacher-priest who interprets the divine justice beneath the Shebarader's law. Her colleagues call her the Inquisitor. She is fifty-four, answers to the High Shekhul [[being-aravdnstrgz|Aravdán the Stargazer]], and holds that the faith's reading of the heavens is a doctrine and not a trade. She has begun building a case against [[being-fakhrdtmrn|Fakhrad Tamaran]], whose country astrology she counts a heresy.

## Background

**The law-school.** She was a gifted student of the temple academies and was ordained a Delkoshekh at thirty-one. She has since held the temple's law-desk, which rules on every question of rite and doctrine put to the senior house.

**The case.** Fakhrad's tables came to her desk when a village priest asked whether a farmer's reading of the stars may stand beside the temple's. She has spent two seasons collecting statements that say it may not.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is exacting, dry and truthful, and she lets a bad argument finish before she answers it.

### Motivation

She wants the temple's reading of the sky to remain one reading, because a faith whose omens disagree cannot keep the throne's trust.

### Strengths

She knows every precedent of the senior house and writes a ruling no one can mistake.

### Weaknesses

She will not let a question rest until it has an answer, and she trusts the law-desk's procedure over the person in front of it.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-fakhrdtmrn|Fakhrad Tamaran]]: The country astrologer whose unconventional reading of the heavens she holds heretical.

### Others

- [[being-aravdnstrgz|Aravdán the Stargazer]]: The High Shekhul she serves.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ahurdaen|Faith of Ahurdaen]]**: a Delkoshekh, holding the law-desk of the Great Fire-Temple.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The depositions.** She needs statements from [[place-sorvadakhend|Sorvadakhend]], where the elders keep Fakhrad on retainer, and sends a party to take them without alarming him.
2. **The charge before the Shekhul.** She means to lay the case before the High Shekhul at the Festival of New Order, and she asks a party to learn whether the Delkoshekh of Astronomy will speak for or against it.
3. **The stolen tables.** A copy of Fakhrad's planting tables has gone missing from the temple's archive. Mazinan wants to know which of her colleagues took it before her case is known.
