# Appearance {#appearance}

Ahrash Tamiran is a 54-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall with a medium build. He has tanned skin, weathered complexion, chestnut hair, and amber eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ahrash Tamiran is a caravan-master of [[place-velgavad|Velgavad]], the walled well-town on the Khazrin road east of [[place-tahrodan|Tahrodan]], and the richest spice trader on that road. His charter from the Shebarader makes him answerable for his trains and their dues, and the street calls him the Merchant-Prince because his trade outweighs most princes' revenue. He keeps a house by the lined well and a standing order with the carver [[being-darzshshvn|Darzash Shavan]], whose work he values above all other work in the Kingdom.

## Background

**The camel-driver's son.** His father drove camels on the Khazrin road and died owing a season's wages. Ahrash worked the debt off at fifteen, bought a share of a train at twenty-two and held a charter of his own at thirty. He settled at Velgavad because every train bound for [[place-threeseas|the Three Seas]] must water there.

**The box.** Eight years ago a carved spice-box in the house of [[being-shirangolsha|Lady Shiran Golshani]] stopped him in the middle of a sale. He found the maker and has bought from her ever since. He has offered her a workshop and a salary, and she has declined each time. He does not understand the refusal and respects it.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is quick, genial and exact. He remembers every debt, owed and owing, and forgives the small ones in public. He is generous to artists and haggles over every skin of water.

### Motivation

He wants his trade to outlast him, and his name on something that will. Each carving he buys becomes a gift to a caravan-master or a temple, and each gift names her beside him.

### Strengths

He knows the price of every load on the three roads and pays his guides before his creditors.

### Weaknesses

He cannot leave a refusal alone and treats Darzash's as a bargaining position. He overbuys against a dry year.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-darzshshvn|Darzash Shavan]]: The carver whose work he buys, wraps and gives away. He has promised her patronage if she ever accepts formal support.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]**: a Notable by his standing, and a chartered Caravan-Master by the Shebarader's grant.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The price of water.** The tagemader of Velgavad raises the price of water in a dry season, and the caravan-masters protest to Tahrodan. Ahrash leads the protest and wants strangers to count the casks that leave the lined well each night, since the tagemader's accounts and the gate's differ.
2. **The gift for the strait.** He has bought a carved chest for a Balinuni factor at [[place-valmakhend|Valmakhend]] and wants it carried across the Kingdom with its seals unbroken, because he does not trust the seal-house at Tahrodan not to open anything a temple has blessed.
3. **The light train.** A train of his comes in from the [[place-farsea|Far Sea]] with every sack a handful short. He wants to know whether the loss was at [[place-dorvakhend|Dorvakhend]], where the loads are weighed, or on the plain, and he will not accuse his own drivers without proof.
