# Appearance {#appearance}

Razashar Perudan is a 32-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a light build. He has tanned skin, ruddy complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Razashar Perudan, styled Captain, is a Dunhari officer of the garrison at [[place-gharzadez|Gharzadez]]: thirty-two, desert-born, a rider who joined the Kingdom's pay for the silver and stayed for the horses. He is an Officer of the Court and commands the patrols that ride out to meet the trains on [[place-marchroad|the March Road]]. He admires [[being-delnshfrdn|Delanash Ferudan]]'s soldiering and has begun to ask what her command is doing to the garrison.

## Background

**The tent.** He was born in a householder's tent of a tribe of the road's edge and learned to ride before he could read the tribe's marks. He joined the garrison at nineteen and made captain at thirty, on the strength of a long ride after raiders that he finished on foot.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is earnest, proud and quick to take a slight on his people's behalf. He respects Delanash and cannot say why that is not enough.

### Motivation

He wants the desert's riders to be soldiers without ceasing to be Dunhari, and cannot decide whether Delanash shows it can be done.

### Strengths

He reads the March Road's tracks as other men read letters.

### Weaknesses

He listens too readily to anyone who speaks of tradition, and keeps his doubts until they are certainties.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The short patrol.** Razashar has too few riders for the next stage of the road and asks a party to ride with him as hired outriders. He does not say where he means to stop.
2. **The elder's gift.** A tribal elder arrives at Gharzadez with horses for the garrison and asks that Razashar host him. Razashar wants a party to learn what the elder expects in return.
3. **The young riders.** Three youths of a tribe of the edge ask to enlist, and Razashar vouches for them. Delanash asks a party to learn whether they come from the tribes that dispute the escort, before the garrison trusts them with the wall.

:::secret {title="Razashar's tally"}
Razashar writes down every order Delanash gives that a Dunhari warrior would not have given, and he sends the tally to [[being-zarvashkaver|Zarvash Kaveran]] by a tribal rider each month. Zarvash persuaded him that Delanash's command is corrupting Dunhari warrior traditions, and Razashar keeps the tally knowingly, for Zarvash's campaign to criticize her before the conservative elders.

**Hook: the tally.** The rider who carries it is waylaid on the road by the kinless. A party that recovers the packet finds Razashar's hand on every page and Delanash's name on every line.
:::
