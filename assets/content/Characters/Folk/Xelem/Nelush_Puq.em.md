Nelush Puq'em is a 41-year-old woman who stands 4'11" tall with a light build. She has brown skin, smooth complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nelush Puq'em is the P'ik'ulmah, the head, of one of [[affiliation-riverhouses|The River Houses]] of [[place-tsalukul|Ts'alukul]], and she sits on the town's council. She is a narrow, quiet woman. Her family has fewer dugouts than the greatest families of the river, and the best factor in [[place-tsamash|Tsa'mash]], the plateau's great market.

She is the council rival of [[being-chakulneshlm|Ch'akul Ne' Shelem]], this year's speaker of the town. A family of Ts'alukul holds its seat by its canoes and its trade, and one that loses its fleet loses its seat, so hers holds by what her factor sends home from the north.

**Motivation:** to make her house the greatest on the river without the dugouts the others count by.
