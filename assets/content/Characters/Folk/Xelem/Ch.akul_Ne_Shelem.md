# Appearance {#appearance}

Ch'akul Ne' Shelem is a 52-year-old man who stands 5'3" tall with a medium build. He has dark brown skin, smooth complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ch'akul Ne' Shelem is the head of the family with the most dugouts on the rivers of [[place-tsalukul|Ts'alukul]], and this year's Ts'omeshmah, the speaker the town's council chooses to speak for it to the envoys of [[affiliation-ixilankul|Ish'ilankul]] and [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]]. He has bargained for the town three times before. Like every head of [[affiliation-riverhouses|The River Houses]], he stands on his own landing to meet the canoes from upriver, opens the bundles himself and names his price there.

His rival on the council is [[being-nelushpuqem|Nelush Puq'em]]. He has heard that feathers of the river are being sold in [[place-tsamash|Tsa'mash]] below every landing's price, and he wants to know whose feathers they are.

**Motivation:** to keep the rivers closed to the north, so that the price is set on the landings.
