# Appearance {#appearance}

Hiluqkanan is a 45-year-old man who stands 5'4" tall with a medium build. He has brown skin, leathery complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hiluqkanan, Bright Sun, is the chief of [[place-tikoqhipay|North Door]], the last town of the [[affiliation-shuqash|Shell-Bead People]] before the empty shore that runs north to the [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Cedar People]], and the only one with a wall. Every spring, when the first Cedar trading canoes come south, he loads two of the town's plank canoes with beads gathered from the chiefs of the northern towns and sends them north as the [Northern Strand]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, and the Cedar war canoes pass the coast towns by. He counts the Strand to the bead in front of the other chiefs' heralds every spring.

He is a great-grandson of [Hiluqchim]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the chief whose herald guided the Cedar war canoes past the coast towns and up the rivers into the [[place-tulishlumuqrgn|Oak Valleys]] in the [Pointing]{.name data-type="lore/history"}, and he does not speak of his great-grandfather. The valley people do.

The town's most famous man is the canoe master [[being-sitiqnush|Sitiqnush]], who sails north three times a year to buy captives back from the Cedar villages. Hiluqkanan praises him at every feast and has never paid toward a voyage. He is forty-five, handsome, a fine speaker at a feast, and careful of his town's walls and his own name. A party that wants to go north to the Cedar beaches by land or sea starts at his town, and he is glad to give it hospitality and advice and nothing that costs him beads.

What he wants is his great-grandfather's name out of every mouth on the coast.
