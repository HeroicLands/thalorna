# Appearance {#appearance}

Sitiqyush is a 44-year-old woman who stands 5'3" tall with a light build. She has golden skin, weathered complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sitiqyush, Star Shining, is an astronomer-priest (_altiyup_) of the house at the landward end of the path at [[place-hipalhalaq|Crossing Shore]], and one of the chief's kin of the [[affiliation-shuqash|Shell-Bead People]] who paid the society's entry and learned the sky. She names children by the sky of their birth night, keeps the calendar, paints the caves and gives the dream drink, and at the [Feast of Strands]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} she stands on the ledge above the surf, sings the names of the dead, and throws a strand into the sea for each name she was paid to send.

She has the best voice in the house, and families from distant towns ask for her by name. She is forty-four, tall, with a singer's stillness, and she gives each mourning family more time than the house pays her for.

:::secret {title="The strands in the surf"}

The strands thrown from the ledge land on a rock shelf that the lowest tides of the year uncover. Every winter the town's divers bring up what the surf has not scattered, and the chief, [[being-tiyuplesh|Tiyuplesh]], and the astronomer-priests of the house divide it. The dives are made at night. Sitiqyush takes her share as the others do, and lately she has stopped spending it.

A party that asks her a direct question about the shelf at low tide gets a long silence and then a careful answer, and a party that offers her a way to end the dives without ending the Feast has her full attention. What she wants is to sing the names of the dead and know that the strands went across with them.

:::

A party bringing its dead to the headland may be given her as its singer.

What she wants is to give every family that asks for her the singing its dead are owed.
