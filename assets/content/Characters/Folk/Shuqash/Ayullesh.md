# Appearance {#appearance}

Ayullesh is a 51-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall with a light build. He has tawny skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ayullesh, Moon Running, is the headman of [[place-tulishwil|Oak Place]], the largest town of the [[place-tulishlumuqrgn|Oak Valleys]] behind the coast of the [[affiliation-shuqash|Shell-Bead People]]. Tulishwil has no rich chief. Ayullesh holds his place because his family owns the best oak groves on the canyon slopes, and because he speaks for the valley villages at the [Acorn Gathering]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}. His storehouse holds acorns, deer meat and baskets, and few beads.

The valleys pay no Northern Strand and remember the [Pointing]{.name data-type="lore/history"} as the coast's betrayal of its own kin, and Ayullesh speaks for that memory too. This autumn a girl of a valley village above the town died of a dream drink too strong, given her by her grandmother. At the gathering the Sky Elder, [[being-tiyupliq|Tiyupliq]], asked the headmen of the valleys to forbid the grandmothers' cup. Ayullesh said that he would forbid it in the year a priest gave the drink to a valley child for the price of a basket. The priests have not lowered their price, and the grandmothers are grinding the root again.

He is fifty-one, lean, a good hunter and a better arguer, and he is the first man a stranger meets who comes up the river into the valleys. A party that wants the valleys' help, against raiders or against the coast, has to persuade him, and he trusts nobody who arrived in a coast canoe.

What he wants is the dream drink given to valley children at a price a valley family can pay.
