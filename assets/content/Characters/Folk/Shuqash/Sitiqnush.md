# Appearance {#appearance}

Sitiqnush is a 39-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall with a light build. He has golden skin, sun-kissed complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sitiqnush, Star Walking, is a canoe master (_alkulay_) of the [[affiliation-canoekin|Canoe Kin]] at [[place-tikoqhipay|North Door]], the northernmost town of the coast of the [[lore-shuqashclt|Shell-Bead People]], and its most famous man. Three times a year he takes his canoe north along the coast, past the last town and into the waters of the [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Cedar People]], with a crew of eight and a cargo of beads. He buys back captives, people of the coast and people of the valleys alike, at whatever price is asked, and brings them home. The [[affiliation-bearhouse|Bear House]] at [[place-lamekis|Bear Beach]] sells to him at its own price and treats him as an honored guest. In twelve years he has brought back sixty-one people, and most of them were no kin of his. When the Cedar People's trading canoes come to Tikoq Hipay in autumn, he walks the beach looking at the faces of the paddlers.

He crosses rough water in a sewn canoe, bargains hard and pays exactly, so that no Cedar chief can say the Shell-Bead People cheat. He is also poor. His canoe is the oldest on the beach, its seams tarred over many times, his crew are mostly young men of the town who paddle for nothing, his own crossing strand is short, and his wife's family complains of it in public. The chief of Tikoq Hipay, [Hiluqkanan]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, praises him at every feast and has never paid toward a voyage. A party that wants to reach the Cedar People's beaches by sea needs a canoe master willing to take it, and Sitiqnush is the one who goes.

What he wants is every captive of the coast brought home, whoever's kin they are.
