# Dossier {#dossier}

Kulaynush, Canoe Walking, is a master sewer (_alsulik_) of the [[affiliation-canoekin|Canoe Kin]] and the brotherhood's **Keeper of the Store**, in the long reed house at the east end of the beach at [[place-kulaywil|Canoe Place]] where the [Paddle of Shiyawkal]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} hangs above the hearth. Every voyage of the brotherhood pays a share of its cargo into the store, and Kulaynush pays from it, for life, any brother drowned, crippled or too old to paddle, and his widow and his children after him. It is the only common store on the coast of the [[affiliation-shuqash|Shell-Bead People]], and it is a great part of why no chief commands the brotherhood.

He is sixty-six and has not paddled to the islands in ten years. His hands still fit a bow plank better than any but two of the master sewers, and he sits with the others to judge a brother. He has voted twice to make a man a Broken Canoe, and he pays the families of both from the store, because the oath protects the art and not the men who break it.

The chief of Kulaywil, [[being-shuqkanan|Shuqkanan]], would like the store's beads in her storehouse at a good rate of keeping, and he has refused her three times. A party can meet him at the brotherhood's house, where he hears requests for crossings and for canoes, and a party that brings home the body of a drowned brother, or news of one, is owed by the store and paid.

What he wants is no brother's widow left to her kin while the store holds a strand.
