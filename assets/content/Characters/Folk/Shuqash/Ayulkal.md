# Dossier {#dossier}

Ayulkal, Moon Arriving, is a grandmother of a valley village above [[place-tulishwil|Oak Place]], in the [[place-tulishlumuqrgn|Oak Valleys]] of the [[affiliation-shuqash|Shell-Bead People]]. Once in a life, at coming of age, a child of the coast takes the dream drink (_lipush_), brewed from the root of the thorn-apple, and meets in the dream the guardian that stands by it for life. The drink belongs to the astronomer-priests by their society's oath, and they mix it to the weight and age of the one who drinks, because a cup too strong kills. In the valleys the grandmothers give it to their own grandchildren, in a basket by a night fire, without beads and without a priest, and Ayulkal has given it to thirty children.

This autumn the thirty-first, her daughter's daughter, died of a cup too strong. At the [Acorn Gathering]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} the Sky Elder, [[being-tiyupliq|Tiyupliq]], asked the valley headmen to forbid the grandmothers' cup, and the headman of Tulishwil, [[being-ayullesh|Ayullesh]], refused while the priests charge for theirs. The priests have not lowered their price, and Ayulkal is grinding the root again.

She is sixty-eight, small and bent, and she does not explain herself to strangers. She knows the valley's plants better than anyone in it, and a party that needs a poison named, a fever broken or a root found in the oak country has nobody better to ask. A party sent by the priests to stop her finds the whole village between her house and the path.

What she wants is to give her grandchildren the sky's dreams with her own hands, whatever the priests or the dead girl's mother say.
