# Appearance {#appearance}

Hiluqyush is a 30-year-old woman who stands 5'2" tall with a medium build. She has tanned skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hiluqyush, Sun Shining, is a basket maker (_alsiliq_) of [[place-tulishwil|Oak Place]] in the [[place-tulishlumuqrgn|Oak Valleys]], who keeps the watch fires of the valley villages of the [[affiliation-shuqash|Shell-Bead People]]. The first of them stands on the ridge across the canyon, built by [Kowiqnaq]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Acorn Seed, the summer after the [Pointing]{.name data-type="lore/history"}. When war canoes are seen coming up from the bay, the fires are lit one after another down the valley, and the villages carry their children and their seed acorns into the hills. A boy of the town keeps the first fire ready from spring to autumn; Hiluqyush keeps the boys, the wood and the paths to the hiding places, and decides which fires burn.

She is thirty, and she has led her own village into the hills four times. Twice the raiders found nobody. Once they found an old man who would not leave, and once they took six people from a village whose fire was lit too late. She walks the ridges in the raiding season and knows every hiding place between the bay and the canyon head.

The [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Cedar People]]'s war canoes come up from the bay because the coast towns pay them to stay away from the coast, and she says so to any coast trader who comes up the river. A party in the valleys in summer may be taken at first for raiders' scouts. A party that can watch a river mouth, or that knows when the Cedar canoes leave their beaches, is worth more to her than beads.

What she wants is every valley child in the hills before a war canoe reaches the first village.
