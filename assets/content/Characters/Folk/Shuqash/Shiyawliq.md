# Dossier {#dossier}

Shiyawliq, Clear Sea, is the chief of [[place-shuqwil|Bead Place]], the largest town on Bead Island, where most of the beads of the [[affiliation-shuqash|Shell-Bead People]] are drilled. She was nine when her father died in a coughing sickness. The town's counter, [[being-ayulchim|Ayulchim]], held its beads for her for three winters, paid its debts, gave its feasts in her name, and at her first feast as chief counted every bead back to her in front of the town. The count came out to the bead.

She is fifteen now, tall for her age, and quicker than the island's canoe masters find comfortable. Shuqwil has little water and almost no wood, and everything it eats that the sea does not give it comes over from the mainland by canoe. The canoe masters who carry it would like a larger share of the cargoes, and every spring one of them tests her: a late crossing, a higher price, a cargo of wood that comes short. She has answered each test so far, twice by going over their heads to the chief of [[place-kulaywil|Canoe Place]], [[being-shuqkanan|Shuqkanan]], which won her the cargo and cost her a favor.

A party that can paddle, or that owns a canoe outside the brotherhood's reach, is worth a great deal to her, and she knows it. She is careful not to say so to anyone of the [[affiliation-canoekin|Canoe Kin]].

What she wants is to be a chief the canoe masters stop testing.
