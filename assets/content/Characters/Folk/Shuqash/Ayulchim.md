# Dossier {#dossier}

Ayulchim, Moon Rising, is the counter (_alpilik_) of [[place-shuqwil|Bead Place]], the bead-drilling town on Bead Island. She has drilled beads since she was seven and taught more than a hundred girls to drill them. Every strand that leaves Shuqwil passes her doorway, and she cuts and restrings any that is short before it goes, so mainland traders pay a little more for a Shuqwil strand, and a full-length strand anywhere on the coast is called an Ayulchim strand. The [[lore-shuqashclt|Shell-Bead People]] hold her up as what a woman should be.

When the chief of Shuqwil died in a coughing sickness, leaving a daughter of nine, Ayulchim held the town's beads for three winters, paid its debts, gave its feasts in the child's name, and at the child's first feast as chief counted every bead back to her in front of the town. The count came out to the bead. That chief, [[being-shiyawliq|Shiyawliq]], is fifteen now, and the island's canoe masters, who would like a larger share of the cargoes, test her every spring. Ayulchim has never been anything but a keeper, and she has no beads to speak of herself.

What she wants is to see Shiyawliq hold the town on her own count.
