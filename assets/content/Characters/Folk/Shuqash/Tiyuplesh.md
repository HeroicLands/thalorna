Tiyuplesh is a 55-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall with a heavy build. He has tawny skin, sun-kissed complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tiyuplesh, Sky Running, is the chief of [[place-hipalhalaq|Crossing Shore]], the town on the windward headland where every one of the [[affiliation-shuqash|Shell-Bead People]] expects to pass three days after death, on the way to the Far Island. He keeps the path along the headland, the pool where the souls bathe and the ledge where they go down to the canoe of the dead, and he takes a bead in ten of every mourner's gift, as any chief takes from a cargo landed on his beach. Hipal Halaq is a fishing town that lives on the dead, and its chief lives better than its fish would let him.

At the [Feast of Strands]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} families from every town bring beads, the astronomer-priests sing the names of the dead, and for each name they were paid to send they throw a strand from the ledge into the surf. The town feeds and houses the mourners, and the mourners pay for that too. Tiyuplesh greets each family himself.

:::secret {title="The strands in the surf"}

The strands thrown from the ledge land on a rock shelf that the lowest tides of the year uncover. Every winter the town's divers bring up what the surf has not scattered, and the chief and the astronomer-priests of the house divide it. Tiyuplesh holds that the strands have already paid for the crossing by the time they reach the shelf, and that what the sea leaves is the town's. The Sky Elder knows of the practice and has not stopped it. The dives are made at night, and the secret is the town's most valuable possession: the Feast of Strands, and the priests' fees for it, depend on every family believing that its strands went across with its dead.

A party that walks the headland at night in winter, at the lowest tides, sees something the town will pay a great deal to keep unseen. What he wants is the town's secret kept, and what the sea leaves on the shelf kept the town's.

:::

He is fifty-five, gray, courteous to grief and generous to the priests' house. A party that brings a dead companion to the Crossing Shore is treated with every kindness.

What he wants is the Crossing Shore as crowded with mourners in his grandchildren's time as in his own.
