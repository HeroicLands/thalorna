# Appearance {#appearance}

Nikhtira Hadrithos is a 36-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. She has tanned skin burned red across the nose, black hair knotted at the nape and brown eyes creased from squinting at a horizon. She wears an oiled sea cloak, and she keeps one hand on a rail or a table as if the floor were about to move.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nikhtira Hadrithos is a warrant-captain of [[affiliation-bethuanflt|The Bethuan Fleet]], a Nauklera of the Purple who sails under the crown's commission of private war. She owns her galley, arms it at her own cost, takes Vilérian shipping as lawful prize and keeps the bulk of what she takes. Her home port is [[place-ashira|Oshira]].

She is quick, cheerful and unsentimental about cargo, and she has never lost a prize she did not choose to let go.

## Background

**The marine.** She grew up on the Oshira quays in a small House, and at sixteen she shipped as a marine in a war-galley, fighting boarders in the press where a longer weapon is useless.

**The ship-mistress.** She served as a sailing-master's mate and then commanded a squadron galley as a Nauklera, learning the southern islands and the narrows between them.

**The Warrant.** She took her Warrant six years ago and arms her own galley. This year she wants a berth in the grain convoy and her Warrant kept in good standing while the Vilérian sweeps continue and the crown's escort is stretched.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is ready, funny and quick to count a price, and she is frank about the money.

### Motivation

She wants her Warrant renewed, her prizes condemned in the Prize-Court without question and a convoy berth that pays.

### Strengths

She knows the southern islands better than most of the Fleet's admirals, and her crew follows her through a bad squall.

### Weaknesses

A warrant-captain who becomes an embarrassment is disowned, and she knows how thin that line is. She trusts the crown's seal more than it deserves.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The standing of the Warrant.** She wants a party to stand with her as witnesses when her Warrant is reviewed, and to testify to how she has kept its terms.
2. **The prize in harbor.** A prize she brought in awaits the Prize-Court Assessor, and she wants a witness present who will say it was taken within the Warrant's terms.
3. **The southern pilot.** She wants a pilot who knows the narrows south of the islands, since the Vilérian sweeps have stopped the hunters' usual haven.
