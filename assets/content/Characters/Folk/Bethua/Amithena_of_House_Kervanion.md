# Appearance {#appearance}

Amithena of House Kervanion is a 51-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. She has smooth olive skin, long black hair streaked gray and dark brown eyes that stay on a speaker a beat longer than is comfortable. In public she wears [[miscgear-veilpurple|the Purple Veil]], and in her own rooms a plain priestess's robe with a silver spindle within reach.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Amithena of House Kervanion is the Metrissa of the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethua]], Queen and High Priestess, elected by the [[affiliation-sacredcollege|Sacred College]] and confirmed by the oracle of the [[affiliation-zunelas|Faith of Zunelas]]. She sits at [[place-bethura|Bethura]] in the [[affiliation-courtveil|Court of the Veil]], where the two pillars of the realm meet in her person.

She is careful and conservative, and she would rather outlast a rival than overrule one.

## Background

**The line.** House Kervanion descends from the Metrissai of earlier reigns, which made her eligible for the Veil. She entered the [[affiliation-lusinia|Faith of Lusinia]] as a Parthena at twelve.

**The priestess.** She was ordained a Hiora and rose to Hiérissa, a high priestess of the College, where she kept both pillars' envoys talking and led no faction.

**The Veil.** The College elected her at forty-five and the oracle confirmed the choice. She has worn the Purple Veil for six years. This year the [[affiliation-councilhouses|Council of Houses]] presses her to sell the surplus grain to the Khelathi patron, the College presses her to keep it in the cisterns, and she has not chosen.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is composed, exact and slow to commit.

### Motivation

She wants the two pillars in balance and a decision that neither can disown.

### Strengths

Neither council believes she has taken the other's side.

### Weaknesses

A Metrissa who sides with one council is half a sovereign, and her delay is becoming a choice.

- **[[affiliation-courtveil|Court of the Veil]]**: head of the sovereign's household.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The level of the cisterns.** She wants a trusted outsider to walk the cisterns of the [[affiliation-orderwaters|Order of the Waters]] and tell her what the interior would have in a dry year.
2. **The late tribute.** The tribute of the [[place-sthmrchsrgn|South Marches]] is late. She wants a party to ride to the Hégissa of the southern cantons and learn why.
3. **The envoy's audience.** A Khelathi envoy asks for an audience about the grain, and she wants a witness at the Court who can report which council sent him.
