# Appearance {#appearance}

Aselia of House Khalath-Mara is a 61-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of medium build. She has weathered tanned skin, gray-streaked black hair knotted at the nape and dark brown eyes that miss nothing in a ledger or a face. She wears a wine-dark robe and a heavy seal-ring.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Aselia of House Khalath-Mara is the Matriarch of the oldest and most prestigious of the Great Houses, whose ancestral seat is [[place-ashira|Oshira]] and whose trade is wine and olive oil. She is the senior voice of the [[affiliation-councilmatriarchs|Council of Matriarchs]], by age and precedence, and she is more often at [[place-bethura|Bethura]] than at home.

She speaks rarely, and the Houses count what she says as the Council's.

## Background

**The House.** She is the elder daughter of the previous Matriarch and grew up in the wine-yards and quays of Oshira, learning the factors' ledgers and the House's credit.

**The seat.** She took her mother's place thirty-five years ago, at twenty-six, and has held it since. Her word on credit has never needed a second, and the Council now counts her its senior voice by age and precedence.

**The question.** This year the [[affiliation-councilhouses|Council of Houses]] presses the Metrissa to sell the grain to the Khelathi patron, and Khalath-Mara's name carries weight in that room. A sale would lift the House's Khelathi accounts and a refusal would keep the cisterns full. She has not said which way the House leans.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is dry, deliberate and economical, and she answers a question with a pause before she answers it with a word.

### Motivation

She wants the federation's credit held to one standard whichever way the grain goes, and the Vilérian squeeze on the Houses' letters answered.

### Strengths

Rival Houses accept her word on a debt without a witness.

### Weaknesses

A matriarch who speaks only when she chooses answers for everything the Council does when she does not.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The letters of credit.** She wants a party to carry sealed letters from Oshira to the Gar-Hálzi correspondent at Bethura without anyone but the receiving clerk breaking the seal.
2. **The Khelathi accounts.** The House's Khelathi accounts ride on the Council's vote on the grain, and she wants a party to sit in the gallery of the Council session and report how each House votes.
3. **The ledger audit.** She wants a party to carry the House's ledgers to a Grammatissa of the Metrissa's court for an open audit before the Council meets.
