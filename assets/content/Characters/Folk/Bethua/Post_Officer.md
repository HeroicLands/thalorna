# Appearance {#appearance}

The Post Officer stands at the gate of the frontier post in a linen coat and knee boots, a shortsword at the hip and the patrol list in one hand, and answers a question in short sentences.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Post Officer commands a Bethuan frontier post on the southern edge of the cantons and answers to the Hégissa of the canton. She keeps the post's list of patrols and the list of stages the free companies hold, and the levy of the southern villages drills beside the post.

## Background

She joined the [[affiliation-spearsisterhood|Spear-Sisterhood]] as a Lorkhai and rode the southern line until its stages and their holders were as familiar as her barracks. She was given a post when the Hégissa needed an officer who could read a patrol list and say what it showed. The post's orders are to receive the tribute and to hold the line with the hands it has.

## Psyche

### Personality

Curt and certain.

### Motivation

She wants her list kept true and her post's honesty clear.

### Strengths

She knows every patrol that left the post and every stage a company holds.

### Weaknesses

She is certain of her own list, and a question about it is a question about her honesty.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

She answers to the Hégissa of the southern cantons and reports to the Sisterhood. The levy women of the nearby villages drill under her veterans.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-trbtftrdrv|The Tribute After the Drive]]: she lays out the patrol list and says that no company of hers has been south of the wall since spring.
