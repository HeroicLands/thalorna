# Appearance {#appearance}

The Izet-Mu is an unhurried woman in a linen robe with a stylus-callus on her fingers, who writes the College's account under her seal and rises from a qanat head's rim with dust to her knees.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Izet-Mu ("Overseer of the Waters") is the chief engineer-priestess of the [[affiliation-orderwaters|Order of the Waters]], a Khelathi loan-title in Bethuan use. She answers to the Metrissa directly and sits in the [[affiliation-sacredcollege|Sacred College]] by right of office.

## Background

She entered the Order as a girl, dug beside a gang of diggers as a Qanatissa and learned the qanats' gradients on her hands and knees. She rose to the office through the works she kept and the gate-rolls she wrote. She writes the College's account of the grain from the gate-rolls of the Order and the tallies of the channel-head granaries. She holds that the cisterns are not full enough to empty and that a sale of the whole surplus leaves the qanat-fed farms nothing in a dry year.

## Psyche

### Personality

Exact and unhurried, and wary of a patron who buys the bread.

### Motivation

She wants the cisterns kept full against a dry year of Bethua's own.

### Strengths

She commands the Order's works and its gate-rolls.

### Weaknesses

She takes the College's case as the whole of the truth.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

She answers to the Metrissa, [[being-amithenakrvn|Amithena of House Kervanion]], and sits among the Hiérissai. Her counterpart in the Council of Houses is [[being-izethalzi|The Izet-Halzi]].

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-hrvstcntdtwc|A Harvest Counted Twice]]: her count is the second roll on the Metrissa's table.

:::secret {title="The gate-rolls"}
**For the GM:** Her gate-rolls count the grain as it leaves the strips, and they show more grain sent north than the bonded warehouses have received. She has not set her count beside the treasury's, and the difference does not occur to her as hers to explain.
:::
