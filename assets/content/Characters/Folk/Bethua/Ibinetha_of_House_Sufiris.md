# Appearance {#appearance}

Ibinetha of House Sufiris is a 43-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. She has smooth tanned skin, black hair knotted at the nape and brown eyes with a habit of narrowing at a loose phrase. She wears a plain court robe and carries the canton's seal on a cord at her belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ibinetha of House Sufiris is the Hégissa of the coastal canton of the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethua]], a Dómissa appointed by the Metrissa to hold its courts, its levy and its waterworks-quotas in the sovereign's name. She sits at [[place-ashira|Oshira]], where her word and the great House's overlap.

She is dry, scrupulous and careful with every word, and she is known for finishing what she starts.

## Background

**The House.** She is the daughter of a small House with a good name and little land, and she was trained as a Grammatissa, learning the rolls and the law before she held any court.

**The House-Mother.** She became Dómissa of House Sufiris on her mother's death, ten years ago at 33, and has sat in the [[affiliation-councilhouses|Council of Houses]] since. In the Metrissa Amithena's first two years in office, six to four years ago, Amithena noticed her careful ruling on a water dispute between two Houses.

**The canton.** The Metrissa appointed her Hégissa four years ago, after she had held the rank of Dómissa for six years, and she still sits in the Council. In Oshira a dispute between a ship's master and a factor of Khalath-Mara goes to a court whose judge dines at the House's table, and she keeps her own court out of that dining room.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is formal, wary and fair, and she writes down what she is told the moment she is told it.

### Motivation

She wants the canton's courts to be the Metrissa's and no House's, and she wants the coast road west of Oshira watched while the patrols are drawn east and south.

### Strengths

She knows the law of waterworks and debt, and the House's factors and the ship-masters both bring her their cases because neither fears she is the other's.

### Weaknesses

She has few guards of her own and relies on the levy and the Spear-Sisterhood's goodwill. She has no way to enforce a judgment against the greatest House on the coast.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The dinner court.** A ship's master has a claim against a Khalath-Mara factor, and she wants a neutral witness at the hearing to note who sits at the bench and who at the House's table.
2. **The thin road.** Carters bound west for the Okharik frontier wait at Oshira for an escort. She wants a party to ride the coast road and tell her which stretches are unwatched.
3. **The berth list.** The Fleet's Harbor-Mistress holds the convoy berths, and she wants an outsider to keep the list honest while the grain question is open.
