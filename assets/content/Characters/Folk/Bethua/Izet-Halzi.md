# Appearance {#appearance}

The Izet-Halzi sits behind a table of tallies in a silk robe, a weighing balance at her elbow and her seal on the table beside her hand, and writes the Council of Houses' account while clerks stand. She reads a figure aloud before she writes it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Izet-Halzi ("Overseer of the Account") is the state treasurer of the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethua]] and head of the royal fisc. The title is a Khelathi loan, and the treasury keeps its accounts on the Khelathi model. Her clerks run the bonded warehouses on the _en-telekas_ at [[place-bethura|Bethura]], where carted grain is entered, tolled and held.

## Background

She came to the treasury as a young Grammatissa, learned the Khelathi model of accounts at a clerk's table and rose through the offices of the bond to the head of the fisc. She writes the Council's account of the realm's grain from the receipts of the bonded warehouses. It does not agree with the count of the [[affiliation-sacredcollege|Sacred College]], and neither office has set its figures beside the other's.

## Psyche

### Personality

Precise, and certain of her own roll.

### Motivation

She wants the count under her seal to stand.

### Strengths

She has the receipts of the bond and clerks who answer exactly.

### Weaknesses

Her clerks resent being checked, and she takes a question about her roll as a question about her office.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

She answers to the Metrissa, and [[affiliation-bthntrdhss|The Bethuan Trade-Houses]] press the Council of Houses through her figures. Her counterpart in the College is [[being-izetmu|The Izet-Mu]].

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-hrvstcntdtwc|A Harvest Counted Twice]]: her count is one of the two rolls the Metrissa lays side by side.

:::secret {title="The bond receipts"}
**For the GM:** Her count is honest and stands on what the bond sees. The bond records only what is unloaded at the bond, so grain that a House carries past it is missing from her roll without any clerk's fault.
:::
