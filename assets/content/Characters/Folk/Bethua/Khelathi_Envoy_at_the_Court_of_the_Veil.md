# Appearance {#appearance}

The envoy is received at the Court of the Veil with the marked deference Bethua shows every official of its patron, and is placed at the right of the senior voice of the Council of Matriarchs when he dines as a guest. He wears a silk robe, carries a seal and a rolled parchment, and answers every greeting in Bethuan.

# Dossier {#dossier}

A Khelathi envoy of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]] asks the Court of the Veil for an audience about the grain. He speaks of the throne's need, and he does not say who sent him.

## Background

He rose as a courtier of a selat household, learned Bethuan on embassies to the north coast and carries the temples' correspondence to Bethua as his habit. He is received everywhere in Bethua as an official of the patron.

## Psyche

### Personality

Deferential, courteous and well informed.

### Motivation

He wants the sale decided in the throne's favor.

### Strengths

He is received everywhere in Bethua as an official of the patron.

### Weaknesses

He reads the Court's face, and the Court reads his.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

In Bethura he is a guest of the Court of the Veil and sits beside [[being-aseliakhlthmr|Aselia of House Khalath-Mara]] when she receives him.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The audience.** He asks the Court for an audience about the grain and wants it before the Metrissa chooses.

:::secret {title="The mediators' errand"}
**For the GM:** The mediators of the [[affiliation-genzetpalu|Genzet'Palu]] sent him, and the bureaus did not. He has two errands: to learn whether the Metrissa means to sell, which decides whether the private lots go on, and to see that nobody at the Court learns of them first. He serves the mediators and reports to them.

1. [[scenario-hrvstcntdtwc|A Harvest Counted Twice]]: he asks for the audience, and he is the man whose master the Court wants named.
   :::
