# Appearance {#appearance}

Bera of House Bera-Sehel walks the horse-yards behind the quay at [[place-bethura|Bethura]] in a silk cloak over a silk shirt, with her House's seal on a cord at her belt. Grooms and carters step aside for her without being told, and she looks at a stall before she looks at the person who keeps it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Bera of House Bera-Sehel is the eponymous matriarch of House Bera-Sehel, one of the Great Houses of [[affiliation-bthntrdhss|The Bethuan Trade-Houses]]. The House breeds horses and supplies armies, holds substantial military-supply contracts with the Khelathi selat administrations and keeps a correspondent account at [[place-garanlaghet|Gar-Anlaghet]]. She is politically aligned with the reformist priestesses of the [[affiliation-courtveil|Court of the Veil]].

## Background

She grew up among the House's stalls and learned horses before she learned ledgers. As a young woman she sat at the House's table while its army contracts with the Khelathi selats were written, and she took the matriarchy when the House already stood among the Great Houses. Her years in the office have gone to widening those contracts and to keeping the House's credit sound.

## Psyche

### Personality

Composed and commercial, and sure of her own accounts.

### Motivation

She wants her House's grain and horses sold at their price and its letters of credit kept strong.

### Strengths

She commands the House's carters, transports, yard-women and guard-women, and she holds a correspondent account at Gar-Anlaghet.

### Weaknesses

She is proud of her House's books, and a rival who reads them closely finds her short-tempered.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

She answers to the [[affiliation-councilmatriarchs|Council of Matriarchs]] as the head of a Great House, and her factors work the quay for her. [[being-aseliakhlthmr|Aselia of House Khalath-Mara]], the Council's senior voice, sits in the same chamber.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The House's yards.** The yards behind the quay hire grooms, carters and guard-women for the army-horse trade, and a party that can tend or ride a warhorse finds work there.

:::secret {title="The granary count"}
**For the GM:** In her own reckoning she breaks no law of the realm: the grain is her House's, and the horses her transports carry are real. What she evades is the toll at the bond, the count that both pillars of Bethua argue from, and the Metrissa's choice. Her carters unload at the House's yards and not at the bond, and the transports sail at night. Her House's letters of credit have grown strong in a year when Viléria is squeezing the Houses' paper, and Aselia has seen her account at Gar-Anlaghet swell. A count that sets the bond's receipts beside the gate-rolls exposes her.

1. [[scenario-hrvstcntdtwc|A Harvest Counted Twice]]: the seller behind the night transports, whose factors learn within days who hired the party.
   :::
