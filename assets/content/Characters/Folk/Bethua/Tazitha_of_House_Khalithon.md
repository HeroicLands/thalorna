# Appearance {#appearance}

Tázitha of House Khalithon is a 47-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. She has sun-browned tanned skin, black hair in thick braids pinned up for work and dark brown eyes. She wears a field robe and walks the channels herself, with a roll of tenants in her hand.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tázitha of House Khalithon is the Dómissa of her House and holds the village of [[place-sehela|Sehela]] and the cantons around it. Its fields are her House's, its water belongs to the [[affiliation-orderwaters|Order of the Waters]], and the _katelun_ belongs to the realm. Her steward keeps the tenants' roll.

She is practical, blunt and ungracious about ceremony, and she knows every tenant's strip by name.

## Background

**The House.** She is the daughter of the previous Dómissa and grew up on the strips, learning the shares and the water-turns from her mother and the steward before she sat in any council.

**The guard.** As a young Dómissa she raised a household guard of her own and trained its women hard. She took a gifted girl, [[being-lmbhrpdhnn|Lámbira Pédhinion]], into it when she saw her talent.

**The quarrel.** This year she sits in the grain quarrel as a landholder. A sale to the Khelathi patron takes grain her tenants would need in a dry year, and a refusal leaves her House out of the profit. The levy drills under her guard's veterans, and the [[affiliation-spearsisterhood|Spear-Sisterhood]] counts it among the hands it can call for the southern posts.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is direct, hardworking and impatient with talk that does not end in a decision.

### Motivation

She wants her tenants fed in a dry year and her House's share of any sale kept fair. She wants the Hiora at the head gate and her steward to stop quarreling over whose word a gate answers to.

### Strengths

The tenants trust her, and the levy and the guard drill because she does.

### Weaknesses

The levy is drawn on for the southern posts, and she has fewer hands for the harvest. Her tenants' need and her House's profit pull in opposite directions.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The head gate.** The Order's Hiora and the Dómissa's steward quarrel over whose word a gate answers to. She wants a neutral witness to walk the line with both and report who opened what.
2. **The short levy.** The village levy is smaller than its roll. She wants a party to count the levy women who have gone south and tell her how many will be home by harvest.
3. **The carter's grain.** A carter has asked to take her tenants' barley north on the Marches road, and she wants an escort she can trust to see it to the strip town.
