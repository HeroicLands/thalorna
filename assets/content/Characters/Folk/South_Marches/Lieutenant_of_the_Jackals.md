# Appearance {#appearance}

The Lieutenant stands at the gate of the stage in a leather surcoat and knee boots, a shortsword at her hip and a roundshield on her arm, and the line of fighters behind her stands as she stands.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Lieutenant holds a western stage of [[place-khuqetzalu|Khuqet-Zalu]] and its well for [[affiliation-jcklsthmrchs|The Jackals of the South Marches]] and names the toll for water. She is a Bethuan woman of the Houseless, cast out of the realm's order, and she commands the Houseless core at the stage.

## Background

She was born in Bethua, lost her place there and rode south as an exile. The Jackals took her in, and she rose to hold a stage because she kept her fighters in a line and her word to the captain.

## Psyche

### Personality

Hard, orderly and unhurried.

### Motivation

She wants the stage held and the toll paid.

### Strengths

She commands a close line of Houseless fighters behind walls.

### Weaknesses

She answers to her captain and cannot bargain beyond what the captain has said.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

She answers to [[being-argyrirjckl|Argirira the Jackal]] and commands the fighters at the stage.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-trbtftrdrv|The Tribute After the Drive]]: she holds the western stage and names the toll for water and a look at the pens.
2. [[scenario-nxtwllst|The Next Well East]]: she leads a riding-party east behind a Bethuan caravan and names Argirira's price for its escort.
3. **The captain's word.** She cannot bargain beyond what Argirira has said, and a party that wants more than water at her stage must wait while a rider goes to the captain.
