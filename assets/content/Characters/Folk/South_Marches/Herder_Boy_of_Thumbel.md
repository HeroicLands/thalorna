# Appearance {#appearance}

The boy is thin and sunburned in a rawhide tunic and breeches, with a knife at his belt, and he stays close to the thorn beside the pen and looks at the ground when a stranger asks him a question.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Herder Boy of Thumbel minds the clan's stock beside the cistern at [[place-thumbelcamp|Thumbel Camp]], where the [[affiliation-dorumbetribe|Dorumbe Tribe]] clan keeps its pens. The clan's herders say that riders came to the pen by night, and he is the one who watched from the thorn.

## Background

He was born at the cistern, took the goats out with the older boys as soon as he could walk behind them and learned the thorn belt by hiding in it. He has been put to the pen since the stock was taken, and the adults ask him questions he cannot answer to their satisfaction.

## Psyche

### Personality

Shy and watchful.

### Motivation

He wants the stock back and the questions to stop.

### Strengths

He knows the thorn and the tracks around the pen.

### Weaknesses

He is a child among angry adults, and he tells whoever asks him what that person seems to want to hear.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

He answers to the [[being-thumbelhead|Clan Head of Thumbel]] and to the herders of his tent.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The thorn.** The boy minds the pen from the thorn beside it, and a stranger who wants to know what the clan's herders saw has to ask him gently.

:::secret {title="What he saw"}
**For the GM:** He remembers that one rider called another "sister" in Bethuan and that none of the riders wore a House's ornament.

1. [[scenario-trbtftrdrv|The Tribute After the Drive]]: the boy is the one who can be asked about the riders, and he remembers the two things that turn the herders' tale.
   :::
