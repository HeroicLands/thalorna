# Appearance {#appearance}

The Elder of the Wells sits on the lip of the deep well in [[place-dorumbecamp|Dorumbe Camp]] in a worsted cloak over a homespun tunic, a staff across the knees, where a stranger asks leave before a horse drinks.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Elder of the Wells of the [[affiliation-dorumbetribe|Dorumbe Tribe]] keeps every claim to water in the tribe's country and says which clan waters where and in what order. The tribe's order rests on those claims, and the elders sit at the well to hear them.

## Background

The Elder came to the office by way of the wells: a herd-hand at a clan's cistern, a rider on the western belt, a herd-master who settled quarrels over a trough, and then an elder of the circle. The Elder is not sure of the herders' tale of a raid. In the Elder's reckoning no Bethuan company has ridden as far as Thumbel's cistern in living memory without being seen on the road, and the tracks of a company bound home go north. The Elder has said so in the circle and has been answered with the herders' own eyes.

## Psyche

### Personality

Deliberate and unwilling to be hurried.

### Motivation

The Elder wants the claims to water kept in their order and the tale tested before the tribe acts on it.

### Strengths

The Elder knows every well and every claim, and who waters first.

### Weaknesses

The Elder is outvoted by what the herders saw.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

The Elder sits in the elders' circle with Headman [[being-mbareloddrmb|Mbarelo of the Dorumbe]].
