# Appearance {#appearance}

Khandoru of the Hasembe is a 37-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. He has dark brown skin, short black hair, a short beard braided with a strip of red wool, and brown eyes that look past a speaker's shoulder at the horse-lines. His nose was broken by a hoof and set badly. He wears a felted cloak and carries a short bow, unstrung, across his back when he bargains.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Khandoru of the Hasembe is the headman of the [[affiliation-hasembetribe|Hasembe Tribe]] and holds the tribe at [[place-hasembecamp|Hasembe Camp]] by descent and by consent. He took the place young, when his father died, and he has kept it by making the tribe's one business beyond herding pay. The Hasembe sell riders and water to every caravan that crosses their wells.

He is brisk and plain-spoken, and he names a price at the first meeting and holds to it at the last.

## Background

**The tribe's business.** Khandoru learned the hire of riders from the Hirer of Riders before he was made headman, and he knows what a rider is worth to a caravan and what the caravan will pay when the next well is held by someone else.

**The tribute.** The Marches' tribute is late, and the eastern wells feel it. The Dorumbe have called their horsemen home, and the Hasembe contract the Dorumbe riders as well as their own and still have more caravans than riders.

**The present.** He wants the escorts filled before a caravan goes to a company instead. He does not want [[affiliation-jcklsthmrchs|The Jackals of the South Marches]] to be that company, because a caravan that learns to pay the Jackals learns to stop paying him.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is quick, direct and impatient with delay, and his impatience is a bargaining manner as much as a temper.

### Motivation

He wants the Hasembe to be the tribe every caravan on the road asks for first. A rider hired, a cistern lidded, a price held are all one gain to him.

### Strengths

He knows every caravan master on the eastern road by name and what each paid last year.

### Weaknesses

He is short of riders this season, and everyone at the eastern wells knows it.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The unescorted caravan.** A caravan master stands at the eastern wells with no riders, and Khandoru will name a price for a party that takes the contract and brings the caravan to the next well whole.
2. **The young rider's coin.** A young horseman has come home from the Jackals' pay with more coin than a season's riding should earn, and Khandoru wants to know who is paying his riders to leave.
3. **Riders for the drive.** He needs a captain who will lend him horsemen for the autumn, and he will pay in the right to water at [[place-longwells|The Long Wells]].
4. [[scenario-nxtwllst|The Next Well East]]: he offers a party the escort of a Bethuan caravan past the eastern stages before the Jackals sell it one, and lends it two riders.
