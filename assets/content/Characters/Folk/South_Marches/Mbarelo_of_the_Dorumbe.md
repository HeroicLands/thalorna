# Appearance {#appearance}

Mbarelo of the Dorumbe is a 46-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. He has brown skin weathered by the dry months, short black hair going gray at the temples, a close-cut beard, and dark brown eyes that rest on a speaker and do not move. A rope scar crosses his left palm, and he limps when the weather turns damp. He wears an undyed felted cloak and the leather cord of the headman, knotted once for each tribute he has driven north.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Mbarelo of the Dorumbe is the headman of the [[affiliation-dorumbetribe|Dorumbe Tribe]] and sits at [[place-dorumbecamp|Dorumbe Camp]]. He holds the place by descent, as his father did, and by the consent of the elders, which he has to earn again each season. He speaks for every clan of the tribe at the frontier posts of the [[affiliation-mtrrchybth|Matriarchy of Bethua]].

He is slow to speak and quick to count, and he does not promise what he has not counted.

## Background

**The tribute.** The winter rains came short and the herds went into the dry months thin. He has sent word to the Hégissa of the southern cantons that the stock will come after the autumn drive and not before, and he knows Bethua wants it sooner.

**The present.** He wants the tribute paid in full and in stock. He tolerates and hires [[affiliation-jcklsthmrchs|The Jackals of the South Marches]] because they are a hard blade he cannot afford to move against.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is patient, dry and exact. He lets the old men run out of argument in the elders' circle before he gives a judgment.

### Motivation

He wants to hand his son a tribe that owes nothing, and he will not let a late tribute be called a refusal.

### Strengths

The elders trust his arithmetic, and the captains of the frontier posts take his word that the stock is coming.

### Weaknesses

His consent is the weaker leg of his place, and the thin herds are costing him it. He has no riders to spare for an escort.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The late tribute.** Mbarelo needs an escort for the stock he cannot afford to lose to a Bethuan patrol, and he will pay in water-rights at [[place-longwells|The Long Wells]], since he has little coin.
2. **The dispute at the cistern.** Two clans claim the same cistern, and he wants a witness from outside the tribe to hear the elders so that neither clan can say he took a side.
3. **A word to the posts.** He needs someone who can carry his explanation of the delay to the captain of a frontier post and be believed.
