# Appearance {#appearance}

The Houseless Fighter stands in a leather surcoat and knee boots with a roundshield and a spear, shoulder to shoulder with the next in line.

# Dossier {#dossier}

A Houseless Fighter is one of the core of [[affiliation-jcklsthmrchs|The Jackals of the South Marches]]: an exiled Bethuan woman or a cast-out Spada with no House and no order to return to. The core fights on foot in a close line with steel.

## Background

The fighter was born in Bethua, was cast out of its order or its House and rode south with nothing but the training. The Jackals gave the fighter a place in a line, and the fighter holds it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Disciplined and wary of strangers.

### Motivation

The fighter wants a place in the line and a share.

### Strengths

The fighter fights in a close line behind a shield.

### Weaknesses

Out of the line the fighter is one fighter alone.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

The fighter answers to the Lieutenant of the stage and to [[being-argyrirjckl|Argirira the Jackal]] above her.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-trbtftrdrv|The Tribute After the Drive]]: the Houseless core holds the western stage in a close line, and a fight inside its walls is one a party loses.
