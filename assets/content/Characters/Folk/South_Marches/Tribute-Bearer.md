# Appearance {#appearance}

The Tribute-Bearer works the pens beside the cistern at [[place-dorumbecamp|Dorumbe Camp]] in a homespun tunic and knee boots, a spear in the crook of the arm and a roundshield slung on the back, counting stock aloud as it passes the gap.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Tribute-Bearer is the deputy of the headman of the [[affiliation-dorumbetribe|Dorumbe Tribe]]. The Tribute-Bearer drives the tribute stock to the frontier posts and treats with the Hégissa for the grazing it buys.

## Background

The Tribute-Bearer rose as a rider on the drives, learned the stages of the road and the temper of the posts as a herd-master, and was named deputy when the headman needed someone who could speak to a Hégissa without losing a herd. The Tribute-Bearer drives whatever the elders' circle decides to the posts, and the posts have orders to receive the tribute and too few hands to drive it further north.

## Psyche

### Personality

Practical and answerable to the circle.

### Motivation

The Tribute-Bearer wants the stock delivered and the grazing kept.

### Strengths

The Tribute-Bearer knows the road, the stages and the posts.

### Weaknesses

The Tribute-Bearer drives what the circle decides, and a stock short of its count is the Tribute-Bearer's to explain.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

The Tribute-Bearer answers to Headman [[being-mbareloddrmb|Mbarelo of the Dorumbe]] and the elders' circle.
