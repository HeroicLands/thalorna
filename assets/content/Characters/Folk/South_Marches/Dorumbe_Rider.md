# Appearance {#appearance}

The Dorumbe Rider sits a hardy marcher horse in a homespun tunic and worsted cloak, a spear in the hand and a longbow on the saddle, and rides with the others in a loose line.

# Dossier {#dossier}

A Dorumbe Rider is one of the young horsemen of the [[affiliation-dorumbetribe|Dorumbe Tribe]], who ride the autumn drive to the frontier posts and the western belt of the tribe's country in the other seasons. The riders ride together against raiders and for hire.

## Background

A rider learned to sit a horse before he could carry a spear, drove stock beside his clan's herders as a boy and was taken into the line of riders when the Master of Riders judged him steady. He knows the stages and wells on the road north.

## Psyche

### Personality

Quick to anger and quicker to ride.

### Motivation

He wants the drive finished and the tribe's name kept clean.

### Strengths

He rides well, shoots well and knows the western belt.

### Weaknesses

He follows the loudest voice in the line.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

He rides under the [[being-tributebrer|Tribute-Bearer]] on the drive and answers to his clan head otherwise.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The drive.** The riders come home from the drive for the autumn, and a stranger who wants the road north described asks them.
