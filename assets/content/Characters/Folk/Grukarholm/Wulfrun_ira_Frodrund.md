# Appearance {#appearance}

Wulfrún ira Frodrúnd is a 24-year-old woman who stands 5' 5" tall and is of light build. She has a heart-shaped face, a narrow nose, thin brows, pale skin, straight blonde hair and gray eyes that stay on the tree line when she is spoken to. A burn scar marks her right hand.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 24. Watcher on the palisade of Hildholt. Wants the horn to blow in time, every time.**

Wulfrún ira Frodrúnd stands the night watch on the palisade of [[place-hildholt|Hildholt]], the refuge village of the battle copse in [[place-skathwald|Skathwald]]. A raiding party that left the walk at [[place-grimhreth|Grimhreth]] came out into her own village's fields in the winter and took it; she walked out of it with a burned hand and the names of those left behind. She tells the elders what she has seen, and the villages beside Hildholt listen to her before they listen to the horn.

:::secret {title="The byre"}
She was not at the well when the raiders came, as she told the elders. She was hidden in the byre with a child of the next house, and the sound of her household's death reached her there. She has not told whose child it was.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** She grew up in a village at the forest edge beside the cause-walk and was taught from a child to count the horns that crossed the clearing.

**The refuge.** She came to Hildholt at twenty-four, after the raid, with a cord of knots for the dead and nothing else.

**Now.** She holds a place on the palisade, a habit of sleeping by the gate and a name among the elders as the one who is never wrong about a sound.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is quiet, vigilant and blunt, and she says nothing about the raid unless she is asked.

### Motivation

She wants the refuge's horn blown early and its gate shut in time, and the families who shelter in the copse to stay once the horn is silent.

### Strengths

She hears a footfall on a log at three hundred paces, and the elders take her warnings without a vote.

### Weaknesses

She does not sleep, and a raider's name makes her hands shake.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vristwald Tribes]]**: Druthmund, a woman of a kindred by a true name, who stands the watch for the refuge.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The horn at dusk.** Wulfrún has heard something on the walk that the other watchers have not, and she wants a party to look at the edge of the copse before the horn is blown.
2. **The cord.** Wulfrún keeps a cord of knots for the dead of the raided village, and she wants a party to carry it to the clearing of the kinsmen, where the mound for those taken stands.
