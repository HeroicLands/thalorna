# Appearance {#appearance}

Skathrik ira Vandvith wur Mahnreth is a 33-year-old man who stands 6' 0" tall and is of medium build. He has a long face, a straight nose, bushy brows, weathered fair skin, straight dark blond hair, a long beard and blue eyes that go to the ground before they go to a person.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 33. Fur hunter of the margins of the Drowned Country. Wants to be across the [[place-grimhreth|Grimhreth]] walk before the thaw closes it.**

Skathrik ira Vandvith wur Mahnreth hunts for furs along the margins of [[place-skathengrund|Skathengrund]] from the autumn to the thaw, setting traps in the forest edge and taking beaver and marten from the slow water. He is of [[place-mahnryth|Mahnreth]] and reaches the margin by the cause-walk at Grimhreth, which he crosses in the dry weeks and on the winter ice. He sells his bales at the forts' yards at [[place-brvvsh|Brevavesh]] and [Sokavesh]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, where the buyers pay in silver and the wardens sell a night's passage.

:::secret {title="The dry logs"}
He has watched the nest's logs at Grimhreth for three seasons and knows which are rotten and which are sound. He sells the crossing times to hunters of the Velanthian side for a pelt, and the nest that keeps the walk has not once failed to lay the logs again.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He learned to trap from his father, a hunter of the clearing of the kinsmen, and earned his true name at eth-kethrun by bringing out a marten pelt from the first thaw.

**The margin.** He went to the margin alone at twenty-three, after a hunting-band he joined was lost to a nest, and has hunted alone since.

**Now.** He holds a trapline of forty snares, a name among the fort-yard buyers and the knowledge that no village will stop for him.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is silent, watchful and dry, and he will say nothing about the marsh to a person who has not seen it.

### Motivation

He wants a season's furs sold at the forts and the thaw past without a nest finding his line.

### Strengths

He reads a bank for crossings, a track for age and a sky for the thaw, and he has never been caught in the marsh.

### Weaknesses

He trusts no one with his line and has no one to help him carry a bad bale.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vristwald Tribes]]**: Druthmund, a man of a kindred by a true name earned at eth-kethrun, who hunts alone.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The walk before the thaw.** Skathrik has a trapline that needs clearing before the thaw closes the walk, and he wants a party that can carry bales out across it in a day.
2. **A hunter from the other side.** A Velanthian hunter asks Skathrik to guide him across the walk, and Skathrik wants a party to go with him so that no one blames him if the crossing fails.
