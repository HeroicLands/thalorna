# Appearance {#appearance}

Karvogor Lervikov is a 49-year-old man who stands 5' 11" tall and is of medium build. He has a square face, a crooked nose, heavy brows, weathered tanned skin, wavy graying brown hair, a short beard and gray eyes that look past a speaker toward the river. A scar runs across his left cheek.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 49. Warden of Torvogor Fort. Wants his wages, a full watch at dusk and one quiet season at the landing.**

Karvogor Lervikov holds [[place-trvgrfrt|Torvogor Fort]] for the prince of the [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]], with a garrison of watchmen, a bell on the hill and a village beneath the walls that gives a man to the watch in turn. His post looks down a river toward [[place-zvekbregad|Zvek Bregad]], the landing where raiding canoes come ashore, some hours from the fort. He answers to the captain of the forts, [[being-vrklrvgrch|Ovrik Lervogorich]], and is owed months of pay. He sells a fur hunter a night's passage past the fort when the hunter has the coin.

:::secret {title="The dusk watch"}
On most dusks the one man the village sends to the landing is a boy of twelve, because the men are in the fields. Karvogor writes the watch on the roll as a man's and has told the captain of the forts nothing different.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He grew up in the meadow village of Lervikov, took his turn at the watch from fourteen and rang the bell for the first time the autumn a war-band crossed the forest edge.

**The fort.** He joined the wardens at twenty and served at the gate of Brevavesh and in two other forts for twenty years, until the prince gave him Torvogor Fort at forty-one.

**Now.** He keeps the fort, the bell and the roll.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is gruff, tired and fair, and he says little to a stranger until he has seen how the stranger treats his men.

### Motivation

He wants a full watch at the landing at dusk, and the pay brought to the fort before the bell has to ring.

### Strengths

He knows the river, the landing and the raiding paths, and the village follows his orders in an alarm without a question.

### Weaknesses

He is owed so much that he has stopped counting, and he will take a coin to look away when a hungry household needs it.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]]**: Officer of the Forts, who as warden holds one paid fort and its village.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A night's passage.** A fur hunter has the coin for a night past the fort, and Karvogor wants a party to go with him as far as the landing and report whether canoes are on the bank.
2. **The months owed.** The garrison is unpaid, and Karvogor wants a party to carry the roll to the captain of the forts at Brevavesh and bring back silver.
