# Appearance {#appearance}

Ts'ultel Ne' is a 48-year-old man who stands 5'5" tall with a light build. He has brown skin, ruddy complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ts'ultel Ne' is the Ch'alish Tq'an of the fortress-temple of [[place-xelemchalix|Shelem Ch'alish]], the gate through which every party bound for the wastes goes out. He rules the court in the name of the Q'aptelmah of [[affiliation-qapteltzakl|Q'aptel Tsa'kul]], who sets the fee at the realm's seat. His priests read each day from the last table to reach the fortress, question every traveler who comes back through the south gate, and give each returner a _tq'anil_, the gate-name.

He is a lean, soft-spoken man. He has never been through the south gate himself, and he says so to every captain who asks. Toward the camp of the Blood-Denied outside the north wall he keeps a silence: he does not forbid its people to go out on a morning of their own choosing, and he does not name the gods for them when they do. A woman of that camp went out alone and came back a season later. [[affiliation-shaqalob|The Severers]] hold that her sentence still stands, and whether the gate gives her a gate-name is his to decide. He has not decided.

**Motivation:** to keep the gate's rites whole and to give no ruling the gate cannot take back.
