# Appearance {#appearance}

Dalmeras Varkalenis is a 41-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of light build. He has light skin gone pale from indoor work, black hair cut short and dark brown eyes, and the long, high-bridged nose of the Varkalenis masks, so like them that the clients at the morning call look from his face to the cupboard and back. His fingertips are faintly yellow with beeswax. He wears a _zalvos_'s (priest's) white mantle over a tunic turned twice, and the house's gilded wing on a black seal hangs from a cord at his neck because the ring it came from has been sold. He speaks quietly and formally, in the old court Vilari of his grandfather's generation.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Dalmeras Varkalenis is a nephew of the master of [[affiliation-varkalenis|House Varkalenis]], one of the three cousins of the name who attend the master's morning call on [[place-antel|Antel]], the Old Hill, and a _zalvos_ (priest) of the [[affiliation-imperialcult|Imperial Cult]]. He holds the cult's office of Keeper of the Masks: he keeps the wax masks of the divine and half-divine Orzoi in the cult's hall, makes the offering before them, mends them, and chooses the men who wear them at an Orzas's funeral. Five of those masks are his own ancestors'.

He is the old blood's claimant to the adoption. The Varkalenis gave the empire the Orzoi who followed the Founder's line, and the old houses who resent the new houses on [[place-selvin|Selvin]] would rather see a Varkalenis adopted than any man the grain or the bankers chose. He has no money to offer the Guard, no legion and no fortune. He offers what his house has always offered: precedent, the gods' favor and a name that stood before the throne for five reigns.

The old Orzas is failing, and the Keeper of the Masks will choose who walks in the masks behind his bier. Every house that hopes to matter after the funeral has called on Dalmeras this year.

## Background

**The poor nephew.** He was born in the house on Antel, the younger son of the master's younger brother, and grew up under the flaking frescoes with the cupboard of masks by the door. There was money for his schooling and none for a commission, so he was given to the cult, as younger Varkalenis have been for centuries, and served the rites of his divine ancestor's temple from the age of sixteen.

**The Keeper.** The Senate's _holzathos_ named him Keeper of the Masks eight years ago, because nobody else wanted a post that paid so little and asked so much care. He has made it his. He has restored the oldest masks with his own hands, rebuilt the record of who wore which mask at every funeral since the Restoration, and turned out a mask-maker who had been selling the cult's beeswax.

**The candidate.** He did not put himself forward. The [Conservative Faction]{.name data-type="affiliation"} in the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] put him forward this year, because the master of the house is seventy-three and could not be adopted, and because Dalmeras is the only Varkalenis of the right age whom nobody can accuse of anything. He accepted because he believes the gods have offered it, and his household has believed him since.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is devout, scrupulous and gentle, and he has a stubbornness that his gentleness hides until it is tested. He makes the offerings himself, on time, every day, whatever the weather. He keeps the house's accounts honestly, which means he knows exactly how poor it is. He has a long memory for slights to his ancestors and none for slights to himself.

### Motivation

He wants the gods given their due, in order: the gods, the ancestors, the house, the patron, the city, the empire. He believes the empire's troubles began when it put coin and convenience above that order, and that an Orzas who kept it would see the gods return their favor. He wants the throne for that, and for his house, which has waited centuries for it.

### Strengths

His name and his piety are real, and the old houses, the cult's priests and the conservative senators trust both. He holds an office every claimant needs at the funeral. He is honest, and in Orzara that is rare enough to be a kind of power.

### Weaknesses

He is poor. He has nothing to offer the Guard, the veterans or the crowd but his ancestors, and Orzara is not a city that sells its loyalty for ancestors. He has never commanded men or managed money beyond his house's thin accounts, and he believes too readily that what is right will be done.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The cracked mask.** One of the oldest masks in the cult's hall has cracked across the brow, and Dalmeras wants it mended before the old Orzas's funeral by the one wax-worker in the city who can match the color. The wax-worker has left Orzara. Dalmeras hires a party to bring him back.
2. **The funeral roll.** Three great houses have each asked for one of the masks at the old Orzas's funeral, and each has sent a gift that Dalmeras cannot afford to keep or to return. He hires a party to return them, politely, and to bring back what each house says.
3. **The missing page.** A page has gone from the record of the masks in the cult's hall: the page that lists who walked in which mask at the funeral of the old Orzas's adoptive father. Dalmeras hires a party to learn who took it, and why anyone wants it now.
4. **The beeswax.** A mask-maker the Keeper turned out of the cult's hall is selling wax portraits of the old Orzas in the Grand Market, made from the cult's own molds. Dalmeras wants the molds back, quietly.

:::secret {title="The old blood's offer to Nartun"}
Dalmeras has made an offer to [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|the Holy Kingdom of Nartun]]. In the spring a priest of the Sacred Forge, chosen by [[being-ankhelossath|Ankelos Satheris]] and blessed by him for the road, went up the holy road as a pilgrim and asked the Holy Mother for a private audience. He spoke for Dalmeras's house: if Nartun's knights and its temple coin stand behind his adoption, Dalmeras will proscribe [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] when he reigns, and the schism of 224 will be healed. At midsummer a second envoy carried the same offer in writing, unsigned.

The Holy Mother has agreed to nothing. She has answered that the temples lend no coin to any throne before the proscription, and that she will hear of knights only when the claimant swears the proscription in the Mother's Hall, with his hand on the oil, before the high priesthood. Dalmeras has not gone. He cannot leave the city with the old Orzas failing and the funeral his to order, and he does not know how he would explain the journey.

He means the offer. He believes the Black Flame is a heresy that worships destruction and that an Orzas who keeps the gods' order must put it down. He has not reckoned what it would cost. The Black Flame has patrons in the Seldan, officers in the legions, registered temples in every commercial city of the empire and preachers on every Circle in the Warrens, and no Orzas could proscribe it without a fight the throne might lose. The promise may never be delivered, by Dalmeras or anyone. If it becomes known, the Black Flame is his enemy before he is adopted.

The High Flame-Warden has already warned every claimant's household, privately, that a claimant who promises to proscribe the faith loses its officers, its patrons and its preachers, and gains a faith of the strong as his enemy. Dalmeras's steward thanked him and assured him that the house had made no such promise. Dalmeras told the steward to say it, and has made an offering of atonement for the lie twice since.

He does not know that the house's debts have been bought. The Keeper of the Seal of the House borrowed against the temple stipends from a moneylender of the Danvar, and the note now lies with a factor of House Thalurates, to be forgiven when the Varkalenis heir marries a Thalurates daughter. If Ankelas names Dalmeras his heir, the note's holder has a bride ready.

An adoption deed in his name lies drafted in the Orzas's strongbox beside two others, unsealed. Dalmeras does not know it. He has taken the old man's evenings with the record of the masks as an old man's piety, and it may be nothing more.

**Hook: the pilgrim's fever.** The priest of the Forge who first went up the holy road has come home with a fever, and he talks in it, in a pilgrims' hostel below Antel where anyone can hear. Dalmeras hires a party to carry him to the house on the Old Hill before the Black Flame's Scorchmasters learn what he is saying.
