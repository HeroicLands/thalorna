# Appearance {#appearance}

Tharkas Melvarandis is 46 years old. He is a narrow-shouldered man with light brown hair, gray eyes and a weathered face. He holds his robe clear of dirty paving and watches where the water runs. At a desk he leans over the drawing rather than making its owner turn it toward his chair.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tharkas is Veramus of Works and Waters on [[affiliation-verdanorzara|Orzara Courts]]. His portfolio covers streets, bridges, aqueducts, sewers, public buildings and baths. He wants the city’s working fabric repaired before a failure turns ordinary neglect into a council emergency. A leaking branch pipe interests him even when no great house has yet complained about the loss.

The hardest petitioners live on [[place-heightsorz|the Heights]]. Water reaches their houses through aqueduct branches and pressure-towers, and Tharkas sets the allotments. He cannot make the towers carry more than their capacity. A widened pipe above one house takes water from another farther down, however grand the first house’s ancestry.

## Background

**The appointment.** Tharkas comes from the senatorial order and serves at the Orzas’s pleasure. He holds one portfolio among five equal veramusoi, with a rotating chair and majority decisions on grave matters. His colleagues can press him to repair a street, but their petitions do not make it his only street.

**The allotments.** A house that exceeds its water share faces a fine. In a dry summer Tharkas can cut its pipe, leaving it to buy water by the jar for the rest of the year. He needs to distinguish excess use from a failing supply before an order punishes the wrong household.

## Psyche

### Personality

He prefers a place he can inspect to an argument conducted entirely in letters. He asks a complainant to show him the fault, then asks who uses the work on an ordinary day.

### Motivation

He wants repairs and allotments that continue to work after the petitioners leave his room. He resents the time spent arguing over a garden’s water while a public building waits for attention.

### Strengths

He follows the consequences of a proposed change beyond the house requesting it. He listens to the people carrying water and maintaining the works as well as to their employers.

### Weaknesses

His preference for direct inspection makes him slow to delegate a disputed case. Smaller complaints accumulate while he pursues one argument on the hills.

## Social

The old houses on [Torvel]{.name data-type="place/feature"} and the rising houses on [[place-selvin|Selvin]] each want a larger share. The bearers who carry jars up the steep lanes know where a household is already short; Tharkas values their accounts without letting an account replace a measurement. His allotment clerks face offers larger than the fines for drawing too much, and he needs evidence of what has been changed before he can settle a contested supply.

### Affiliations

- **Orzara Courts**: a Veramus holding Works and Waters, equal to the holders of Law, Order, Grain and Rolls.
- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: a Kurthos of the senatorial order.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The dry house.** A Selvin household has no water while the house above draws twice its share. The lower house hires the party to obtain proof for Tharkas; the upper household offers access to everything except the pipe.
2. **The offered wages.** An allotment clerk is offered a summer’s wages to move a valve on Torvel and asks the party to identify the paying house before he decides. Bringing the offer to Tharkas exposes the approach, but does not prove that a valve has already moved.
3. **The inspection route.** Several petitioners want Tharkas to inspect their complaints first. He asks the party to walk the routes and describe the actual failures, while each petitioner offers help reaching his own property and reasons to pass the others by.
