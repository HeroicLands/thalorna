# Appearance {#appearance}

Pirkelar Revothandis is a 71-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. She has pale skin coloured by the summers she has sat out in the street, grey hair that was black, and sharp green eyes. She wears a wool shawl with sawdust in its folds and sits on her bench as though it were a judge's chair. She calls everyone in the village by name and every stranger "carver" until told otherwise.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Pirkelar Revothandis is the village elder of [[place-dremenna|Dremenna]], the woodcrafters' village of about three hundred in a valley of the [[place-nartumkngdm|Nartun Kingdom]]. The village holds of a temple under the high priesthood and keeps its own peace, and the peace is hers. She settles disputes over shared timber and workshop boundaries from a bench in the street, and her word stands unless one party carries the quarrel to the temple's court in [[place-nartum|Nartun]]. Few do.

She treasures the work of the relief carver [[being-xadrosdmts|Zadros Damates]], the master of the six-generation Damates workshop, and she recommends him to every visitor of any standing who comes down the street. He is too shy to sell himself, so she does it for him.

## Background

**The street.** Pirkelar grew up in the workshops of Dremenna and knows the trade of every door on its one street, though she was never the best hand at any of them.

**The bench.** The village came to bring her its quarrels because she listened to both sides and remembered every agreement anyone had made. In time the bench outside her door became the village's court.

**The failing trade.** In her old age the forest above the village is dying and the cheap goods of the south are undercutting the carvers. She has decided that the village's best work must be seen by people who can pay for it.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is shrewd, blunt and fond of her village, and she likes to be right. She has no patience with a man who will not stand up for his own work.

### Motivation

She wants Dremenna's craft to outlive her, and she wants to know who in the village is letting the pedlars in.

### Strengths

The village trusts her judgment, and she knows everyone's business. The knight of the Order who rides through at the quarter-days has learned to ask her first.

### Weaknesses

She is old, proud and stubborn, and she will defend the village's ways against anyone, the high priesthood's men included, longer than is wise.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-ardmsvlthmn|Ardemos Velthamen]]: The lay baron who holds the forest above the village of the temple of Sélmoros, and who says the priors' cutting is killing it. She listens to him and does not repeat what he says.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The merchant's commission.** A rich merchant offers Zadros a commission and a place in his household while it is installed. Pirkelar wants a party to go with the shy carver and bring him home again.
2. **The cheap goods.** Mass-made bowls and boxes from the south are selling in the village market at half the craftsmen's price. Pirkelar suspects that someone in the village is letting the pedlars in, and she wants to know who.
3. **The stranger's timber.** A traveller with a load of wood from a distant forest will pay a fortune to have it carved. It resists the chisel, the old coopers will not touch it, and Pirkelar wants to know where it came from before she lets Zadros take the work.
4. **The dying stand.** The timber that comes down from the forest above the village comes in with a black stain under the bark. Pirkelar asks a party to go up to the cutting and learn whether the stands that feed Dremenna are dying.

:::secret {title="The Truth of the Withering"}
The Withering is a fungus, and it travels on felled timber with the bark left on and on the wheels of the timber wagons. Every unpeeled log that comes down to Dremenna's yards can carry it on into the valley, and it enters a living tree through a fresh wound. The remedy is plain work: peel and burn the bark where a tree falls and rest a valley the blight has reached. Pirkelar does not know this. If a party proves it to her, she can make the village peel what it buys, and she will want to tell [[being-ardmsvlthmn|Ardemos Velthamen]], whose quarrel with the priors it settles.
:::
