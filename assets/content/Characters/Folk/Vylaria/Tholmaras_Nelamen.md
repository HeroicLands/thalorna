# Appearance {#appearance}

Tholmaras Nelamen is a 57-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. He has light skin weathered by years on the frontier, black hair graying and cut short, and gray eyes. He holds his audience standing, in a soldier's cloak, and he wears the province's gray, the color his post-masters and his toll-keepers wear. He is courteous to everyone, and he is very well informed.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tholmaras Nelamen is the _therkos_ (governor) of the [[affiliation-provncmktr|Province of Moktur]], and he has held it from the Keep at [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]] for eleven years. He is a soldier of a Mokturan provincial family named for its estate of [Nelamenna]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} under the snows, and he commanded a cohort on the frontier before he was given the province. He holds his audience in the hall of the Keep at first light and hears petitions, appeals from the town benches and disputes between border lords in the same hour.

The capital can neither lose Moktur nor pay to hold it, and Tholmaras governs it in the gap. He pays some twelve hundred Varokh-born auxiliaries, horse, slingers and light foot, in coin, on the first day of every month, in the yard of the Keep, from the tolls of [[place-korrast|Korras]] and the province's share of the metal of [[place-ferrovan|Zargonara]], and they are sworn to him by name. They have never been paid late. The legion at [[place-dolvar|Dolvar]] is paid by the treasury in the capital, when its coin arrives.

He keeps the road as well. The _pozerus_ (warden of the roads) is his officer, the post-masters of the posting-stations wear his gray, and every letter from the north to the capital, the _vandraks_'s and the revenue officer's included, passes through a station he keeps. Each spring he names the commander of the gate at Korras, whose tolls pay his riders, and he cares more that the gate is held than who holds it. On the state's days he makes the offering before the Orzas's image in the provincial temple with the border lords behind him. Tirellan counts his rule as loyalty, because he pays his riders on the day and holds the frontier quiet.

## Background

**The frontier.** He is a Mokturan, of a provincial family, and he commanded a cohort on the frontier before the capital gave him the province eleven years ago.

**The province.** In his years at Tirellan the auxiliaries have become his, the road has become his, and the province has learned to govern itself on what he pays.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is courteous, attentive and exact, and he keeps his promises to the men he pays. He does not raise his voice in the hall of the Keep.

### Motivation

He wants the frontier held and the men who hold it paid on the day.

### Strengths

Twelve hundred auxiliaries are sworn to him by name, the road to the capital runs through his stations, and the province's coin is in his strongrooms.

### Weaknesses

He holds the province on the auxiliaries' pay, and the pay rests on the Korras tolls and the Zargonara silver, both of which every power in Moktur wants a share of. The capital neither commands him nor replaces him.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The pay chest.** The month's coin for the auxiliaries comes down from Korras under the _therkos_'s guard, and this month a war-band is on the march below the pass. The _tarkelos_ (treasurer) hires a party to bring the chest to the Keep by the first of the month.
2. **The offer to the towers.** A rider from Tirellan waits at Dolvar with the _therkos_'s sealed letter to the captain of [[affiliation-hrendskari|Hrendskari]]. It offers the company pay on the first of every month from the province's tolls, as his auxiliaries are paid, in return for its word given to him by name.
3. **The hedge.** At [[place-edrenburg|Edrenburg]] the garrison's commander wants a gate cut through the Varokh town's hedge for his patrols, and the elders refuse. Both sides want a party to carry their case to the _therkos_'s audience first.

:::secret {title="The letter in the strongroom"}
Tholmaras Nelamen has been recalled. Last autumn the chancery in Orzara sent a letter of recall under the old Orzas's seal, naming a _kurthos_ (senator) of the capital as the next _therkos_ and ordering Tholmaras south to give account of the province's revenue. At the posting-station on the provincial boundary the post-master signed for the bag, and the receipt went back south. The letter went on to the Keep and into the _therkos_'s strongroom, and its seal has not been broken. In law he has not received it, so he has not answered it. His routine reports go south every month as before, and they say nothing of it.

The capital holds the receipt, believes the letter delivered and waits to see whether he comes. The _kurthos_ named to succeed him waits in Orzara for word that never comes, and with [[being-zrmlshlrkls|the Orzas]] failing nobody in the chancery will ride north to ask why.

The recall was copied to the _vandraks_ at Dolvar with an order to see it carried out, and [[being-sthrskrmrs|Satheras Kiramaris]] has not acted on it. Through the last two winters, when the treasury's coin did not come, the legion was paid from the _therkos_'s strongrooms against the _vandraks_'s receipt. The two men have said nothing to each other about the letter.

Every dispatch from Dolvar to the capital is opened at the posting-station below Tirellan's North Gate, read by the _pozerus_'s clerk, resealed with a copy of the _vandraks_'s seal cut by a Tirellan engraver, and sent on a day late.

**Hook: the reading.** Whoever brings the recall into Tirellan's forum and reads it aloud makes the _therkos_ obey or refuse in public, and the capital can do nothing about either. Obedience leaves a province whose auxiliaries were sworn to the man who has gone; refusal comes in a season when the throne cannot afford to answer one. Nobody in the province wants it done, the capital's own officers included.
:::
