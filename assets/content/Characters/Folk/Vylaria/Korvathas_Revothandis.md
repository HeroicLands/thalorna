# Appearance {#appearance}

Korvathas Revothandis is a 48-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He has light skin, dark brown hair and brown eyes, and the smooth face of a man who has spent his life indoors in the capital. He wears the senator's stripe at his morning audience. He receives the shipping houses and the admirals' officers with the same courtesy on alternate days, and he gives neither the satisfaction of a quick answer.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Korvathas Revothandis is the _therkos_ (governor) of the [[affiliation-provincvld|Province of Salkalon]], a _kurthos_ (senator) of an old house of the capital, in his second year at [[place-mercavia|Delalara]]. The Senate sent him to Salkalon because it wanted the province governed by a man with no ships of his own. He holds the province for the emperor, commanding its garrison, its revenue and its courts.

He holds an audience every morning in the hall of the [Governor's House]{.name data-type="place/structure"} and is careful to receive the shipping houses and the admirals' officers on alternate days. The province's three powers, the merchants, the admiralty and the customs, meet in the Governor's House only when he asks them, and each wants something different from Taruzel. The fleet does not answer to him: its _vormeks_ (admiral) at [[place-castamar|Kastamar]] answers to Orzara. The customs are farmed to a _tolar_ of [[affiliation-tolvan|the Tolvan]], and the _karezos_ (revenue officer) who watches them reports past the _therkos_ to the emperor.

His one power over the harbor's trade with the enemy is the waiver. Every Taruzelian factor in Delalara keeps his house, his clerks and his harbor in the city by a waiver from the _therkos_, renewed every spring, and a factor whose waiver is refused has a month to leave. Korvathas has renewed every waiver so far, each a fortnight late, and the city watches every spring to see whose is late. This spring one factor's waiver has not come at all.

## Background

**The man with no ships.** He came to Salkalon from the capital, owning no hull and holding no share in any voyage. The shipping houses fill the town's council, and the first voice among them, [Ornetha Venthelis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, carries the book of dues to the Governor's House herself each year.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is careful and even-handed, and he is slow with every answer. He keeps the alternate days of his audience as strictly as a calendar.

### Motivation

He wants to govern a province that the Senate, the treasury and the admiralty each see differently, without becoming any one party's man.

### Strengths

He has no ships, no cargo and no debts in the harbor, so no house can buy him with its trade. The waiver is his alone to grant or delay.

### Weaknesses

He commands neither the fleet nor the customs, and every house on the quays knows more about the harbor's business than he does.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The late waiver.** One factor's waiver has not been renewed this spring, and his month is half gone. He asks a party to learn why from the Governor's House, and meanwhile to keep his ledgers safe from the three houses that owe him money.
2. **The petition.** Ornetha Venthelis sends the houses' petition for a trade treaty with the Confederation to Orzara by a trusted hand, because last year's reached the Senate a month late and opened. The admirals' men at the Orzara Gate watch who carries it.

:::secret {title="The ledger the islands read"}
The _therkos_ does not know who owns the Delalara customs. The farmer, [Solteras Tharozates]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, bid more for them than any _tolar_ before him with money borrowed from the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]]'s counting house in the Eastern Quarter, and he pledged the customs themselves as the security. Every week the Compact's master of the counting house, [Lamuna Pilmuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, reads a fair copy of the customs ledger: every cargo that crosses the wharf, whose it is, what it is worth and where it is bound. Twice she has asked that a particular cargo be let through the arcade without being opened, and twice it was.

The admirals do not know either, and they would call the loan treason and be right in law. Ornetha Venthelis suspects, because the customs clerks never open the Eastern Quarter's goods. If the Compact calls the loan, the province's largest revenue goes uncollected until the Tolvan finds another bidder, which in Salkalon would take a year.
:::
