# Appearance {#appearance}

Sorima Salken is 60 years old. She has a strong, bent back, gray hair under a smoke-dark cloth and dark brown eyes that water beside a newly lit rack. Her hands are steady around an ember pan. She turns to face a speaker fully, rather than trying to listen through the work’s noise.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sorima keeps [[miscgear-oldcoal|The Old Coal]] at [[place-braldvik|Bralvik]]. On Coal Night she carries it through town in [Hvirnmil]{.name data-type="being/npc"}’s iron helm, and each smokehouse lights its autumn fire from hers. That shared fire entitles a house to burn the northern rune brand into its barrels, the mark that brings the town’s fish its price.

She is the last of the Salken line to carry the name. She wants the ceremony and the trade it supports to survive her without being sold to an outside shipping house. She keeps a second coal banked against an attempt to extinguish the first, but will not disclose its cellar.

## Background

**The smokehouse.** She learns the work through the Salken household, which keeps the fire descended from [Dvilgvir]{.name data-type="being/npc"}’s banked hearth. Its care is a daily task before it is a public ceremony.

**The keeper.** Holding the coal makes her responsible to smokehouses that compete for the same fish and buyers. She gives each its fire without favoring the largest purchaser.

**The outside offer.** A [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] shipping house buys smokehouses in Tholkezora and wants the brand. Sorima must now defend a distinction the town usually takes for granted: a barrel’s origin is not something its buyer can buy separately.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is direct, patient with learners and unyielding about work done safely around a banked fire. She can laugh at a quarrel without conceding it.

### Motivation

She wants Bralvik’s households to keep control of the name and fire their labor makes valuable.

### Strengths

She knows the smokehouses, their keepers and the ceremony in detail. The town depends on a task she performs visibly and reliably.

### Weaknesses

There is no other Salken to inherit her name, and protecting the reserve coal means withholding information even from people who want to help.

## Social

The smokehouse families need her fire. Delalara’s factors need its mark, while the Kastamar admiralty has yet to settle the town’s Boat-Bill. None of those interests gives her authority to decide for every Bralvik crew.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: a free resident under imperial law without the citizenship.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The second cellar.** Sorima hires a party to watch the smokehouse during Coal Night while she carries the helm through town. A stranger is asking where she banks her reserve.
2. **The helm’s claimant.** A visitor from Hvirnmil’s northern hall asks for the helm back. Sorima wants the party to hear his account of whether the captain gives it as a gift or a pledge before the town answers.
3. **The answered bill.** Kastamar considers paying its old Boat-Bill in exchange for Bralvik crews. Sorima asks the party to bring the terms back in writing so smokehouse families and boat owners can debate the same offer.
4. **The barrel’s message.** Someone who can read the rune brand recognizes Dvilgvir’s old message to his mother. Sorima wants an accurate reading before supposed northern kin arrive with claims.
