# Appearance {#appearance}

Kaldremos Revothandis is 47 years old. He is a heavyset man with hazel eyes and black hair receding from his temples. Salt whitens the seams of his sample bags. His fingers are careful with dice, but he takes a loss with an easy shrug and asks whether the next round needs a drink.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kaldremos trades salt at [[place-edrenburg|Edrenburg]], where the market road separates the Varokh town from the imperial fort. He keeps lodging near the Hedge Gap and deals with traders who bring forest goods out to meet the southern wagons. He is well known at the inn’s dice table and has friends in the garrison.

A regular forest trader has missed two monthly markets. Kaldremos wants word from the man’s village before anyone sells his expected goods a second time. He offers payment for a journey into the pines and asks for a description of who answers the door, not merely a rumor carried back to the inn.

## Background

**The loads.** He entered the border trade carrying salt north and buying the goods the forest villages bring out. He learned which promises can survive a late season and which need a witness.

**The table.** Nine years at the Hedge Gap made him a familiar face to merchants and soldiers alike. He kept his trading business close to the road, where the garrison judges disputes involving Vilérians.

**The missing partner.** The present absence unsettles his usual dealings. He cannot go into every village himself without leaving the next delivery unattended, and seeks travelers who can return with facts.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is genial, attentive to newcomers and difficult to surprise at a gaming table. His questions seem casual until he repeats one that was left unanswered.

### Motivation

He wants the border trade kept open and wants reliable word of the people whose arrivals make his business possible.

### Strengths

He knows both the garrison’s habits and the forest traders’ schedules. His coin can hire help quickly.

### Weaknesses

His welcome at the fort makes him suspect inside the hedge. A trader who knows him well can still refuse to introduce him to a village elder.

## Social

The fort’s officers know him, and forest traders share his table without necessarily sharing his loyalties. [[being-vndrcgrwld|Vandarik ira Garwald wur Fródhaven]] is remembered at that table for a costly night of cheating.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: of the propertied order.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The missing trader.** Kaldremos hires a party to travel a day into the forest and learn why his partner has missed the market twice.
2. **The warrant.** A party learns of the unusually large reward for Vandarik. Kaldremos asks what it has heard about the fugitive before discussing the money he loses at the dice table.
3. **The hedge dispute.** The garrison wants a patrol gate through the town’s hedge. Kaldremos wants a party to carry the traders’ account of the market’s needs to Tirellan alongside the competing petitions.

:::secret {title="The payments at the dice table"}
Kaldremos is the garrison’s paymaster for forest informers. They come as traders and win exactly the coin their news earns, avoiding an open payment that would expose them at home. Vandarik cheats on a night when the pot is intended for an informer with news of a war-band. The informer returns unpaid, his village draws its own conclusion, and he does not emerge again. The garrison wants Vandarik because he sees the paymaster’s company and now lives among outlaws. The trader missing two markets is another informer.
:::
