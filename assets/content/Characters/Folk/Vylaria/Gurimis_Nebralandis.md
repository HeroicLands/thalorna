# Appearance {#appearance}

Gurimis Nebralandis is 49 years old. He has brown eyes, thinning black hair and a pale line across one eyebrow. His coat’s inner pocket holds a folded measuring cord. He looks at a gallery’s roof before its ore and asks a miner to point to the cut rather than describe it from the yard.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Gurimis is Master Surveyor of the lodes at [[place-ferrovan|Zargonara]]. He allocates cuts and decides disputes where a vein crosses a lease boundary. The lessees treat his word as final, because a line on his plan determines whose gang works a promising face.

He wants control of the next round of surveys kept in his own hands. Reports from the old galleries require care, the lessees press for productive cuts, and the province expects its silver. He can arrange access to a disputed level, but insists that everyone entering it be named before the party goes underground.

## Background

**The plans.** He learned to carry a surface boundary into a working that does not follow straight lines. He gained a reputation for remembering both the lease and the rock.

**The provincial post.** Appointment as Master Surveyor turned his judgment into a decision other people must work under. He learned to record an instruction before a lessee supplies a different account of it.

**The old workings.** More of his attention now goes to galleries cut by earlier miners. Their roofs, pillars and abandoned routes make a drawing less reliable than the men who walk it.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is restrained, exact about access and impatient with confidence unsupported by a measurement. He prefers a difficult question in private to a simple one shouted across the Silver Floor.

### Motivation

He wants to keep directing the lodes’ work without the province replacing his judgment with an inquiry run from the capital.

### Strengths

He can read the workings and their leases, knows the overseers, and understands what a change in a cut will cost a crew.

### Weaknesses

His authority depends on the records remaining credible. He is slow to admit a conclusion that would make earlier instructions indefensible.

## Social

The lessees need his rulings and the overseers need clear orders. The revenue clerks weigh silver rather than survey it, giving them a separate account of what the mines produce.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: a citizen.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The old level.** A gang breaks into a gallery occupied by screechbats. Gurimis hires a party to inspect its approaches before the overseer commits more workers to it.
2. **The matching books.** A revenue clerk asks why two independent books agree to the ounce for three years. Gurimis wants to know which entries she disputes before a public accusation halts the Silver Floor.
3. **The missing escort.** A mule comes down the Stennvold road with its silver panniers sealed and no driver. Gurimis asks the party to recover the man’s route and companions, while the family wants the man found.

:::secret {title="The exhausted vein"}
The great vein ended four years ago. Gurimis tells the lessees, who send gangs into old galleries to cut pillars and floors instead of reporting the loss. Two galleries fall; their dead are entered as lost to bad air. The lessees pay both Silver Floor clerks to enter the old weights and buy southern silver to make up the melt at [[place-stennvold|Stennvold]]. Gurimis estimates two winters of leavings remain. He knows the principal vein is gone, not merely that the current output is lower.
:::
