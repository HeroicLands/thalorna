# Appearance {#appearance}

Varthelas Zerkemis is a 58-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of light build. He has olive skin gone sallow from fasting, dark eyes, and a head shaved to the skin, with black brows going grey above them. He wears the grey robe of the priesthood of Sélmoros, the Silent Judge, with a knotted cord at the left wrist, and his long hands are usually folded inside his sleeves. He speaks seldom and quietly, waits a long moment before he answers anything, and does not fill a silence for anyone's comfort.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Varthelas Zerkemis is the High Priest of [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]], the Silent Judge, in the holy city of [[place-nartum|Nartun]], one of the twelve high priests who together are the high priesthood of the [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]]. His temple stands on the ring of the temple quarter, and its estates lie in the uplands, governed by priors who answer to him.

He is the voice of the move to judge the king. In his temple and in the high priests' council he teaches what the high priesthood has always taught, that the forest dies where its people neglect the rites, and he goes one step further than the others: a kingdom whose forest dies in every valley has a king who neglects the rites, and the anointing obliges the high priesthood to sit in judgment on him. The lower city has heard it, and so has the king.

## Background

**The judge's house.** The Zerkemis are a priest-noble family that has served Sélmoros for generations, and its sons have been examiners and judges of doctrine in the kingdom since the break. Varthelas sat as a temple examiner for twenty years before he became high priest, and he judged blasphemers, unlicensed mages and worshippers of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]].

**The Withering.** The dying stands reached his temple's estates first, in the valleys below Moktur, and his priors were the first to order the processions and the fasts. When the Withering spread despite them, he concluded that a village's penance could not answer for a kingdom's neglect.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is austere, patient and certain. He is not cruel and takes no pleasure in a sentence, but he has never doubted one. He respects the Holy Mother, distrusts the king, and has no patience for barons.

### Motivation

He wants a king who serves the faith first and the forest second, and he believes this king serves neither. He wants the judgment called, the vote won and the oil wiped away in the Mother's Hall, and he wants it done by the law of the anointing, so that nobody can call it a coup.

### Strengths

He was an examiner for twenty years, and he knows what most of the priest-noble families would rather nobody knew. He has the votes of the high priests who share his view of the Withering, and his temple's priors pay their dues in full.

### Weaknesses

He needs a majority and the Holy Mother's hand, and he has neither. He has said in public that the Withering is the king's fault, so a year in which it slows makes him a liar. The barons hate him, and they send their sons into the host.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The sermon at the gate.** Varthelas is to preach on the Withering at the main gate on the feast of Sélmoros, and the barons have let it be known that their men will be in the crowd. The temple hires a party to keep the peace without anyone seeing who hired it.
2. **The examiner's record.** A record from his years as examiner, the judgment of a baron's daughter for heresy, has gone missing from the temple's archive. The baron's family wants it found first.
3. **The proof of neglect.** Varthelas wants witnesses that the king missed the rites of a feast day while he hunted in the crown forest. He pays for a party to find a huntsman who will swear to it.

:::secret {title="What the judgment is for"}
The charge Varthelas means to bring is the Withering. The reason he means to bring it is the undercroft. He knows of the Holy Mother's warrants and of the worshippers of the Black Flame whom [[being-sldrsplvrn|Saldoros Pelvaren]]'s knights carry out of the Hilen march to be judged under the chapter house over the gate; as a former examiner, he wrote the form of the first warrant. He knows the king would stop the takings if he were told, and he wants a king who would let them go on. The king's elder cousin, [Ormelas Daronis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, has told him in so many words that he would, and three of the visits to Ormelas's estate were his.

He has also read what the high priesthood does not discuss. A forester on [[being-mlknssrvths|Melkanas Sarvothis]]'s estate sent him a report two summers ago, with a map, saying that the dying stands lie thickest in the valleys let to the big cutting contracts. Varthelas put it in his chest and did not answer it. Whatever kills the forest, the dues his temple asks of its priors are what the contracts pay for, and a judgment needs a guilty king, not a guilty prior.

**Hook: the forester's map.** The forester who sent the report has been moved to an estate on the Velanthian border, and he wants a party to take a copy of his map to someone in the holy city who will read it. He suggests the king.
:::
