# Appearance {#appearance}

Tholmaros Kalmethis wears the great gold collar of the Dukran over his court dress. At an audience he keeps the council's papers beside him and refers a disputed instruction to its written wording before hearing the next speaker.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tholmaros Kalmethis is the current **Dukran**, the chief war-minister of the [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|Vilérian Military]], appointed by the Orzas from the Gilded estate. His career is at court. He presides over the war council of the Sodari Orzas, receives the theater commanders' reports and carries military questions to the throne. The Kamdor officers hold command in the field; Tholmaros's authority comes from his appointment.

## The Present {#present}

The failing [[being-zrmlshlrkls|Orzas]] has adopted no successor, and the council must still settle commissions, hear disputes and give instructions to the army. The officer corps wants revenge for Taruzel, while the treasury cannot pay for the war it proposes. Tholmaros wants the council's orders honored through the accession and a new sovereign willing to confirm the arrangements the old one leaves.

The Vallar examination remains a standing grievance. It was imposed under the historical Dukran [Zomeris Kaldremis]{.name data-type="being/npc"} after the Taruzelian war. Tholmaros now shares responsibility for setting it with the panel of serving Kamdor officers; the noble commission market remains unchanged.

## Meeting Him

Petitioners reach him through the war council's business: a disputed commission, a company's contract or a commander's report. He asks what the throne has already promised and whose signature binds it. He has no field command to offer a petitioner in place of that answer.

## Hook

- **The examination papers.** A candidate's supporters ask a party to carry their evidence of a rigged Vallar examination to the Dukran before the next hearing. The serving panel wants the accusation kept within the council, and the Reformists want it read aloud.
