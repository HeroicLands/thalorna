# Appearance {#appearance}

Kauren Iltheris is a 51-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build. He has medium skin burned brown by years in the saddle, dark brown hair cropped to stubble, and hazel eyes under heavy brows. A spear scar crosses the back of his right hand. He walks with a rider's bowed legs and sits a horse as if he grew there. He wears a plain centurion's harness without a single ornament, and he speaks in short sentences, usually questions.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kauren Iltheris is the senior irregular-war officer of the legion's garrison in [[affiliation-provinclys|the Province of Alessa]], the officer whose riders scout the hill tracks and passes for smugglers, bandits and dissidents. He values competence above everything else. He treats his soldiers as tools, uses them hard, and protects the good ones because good tools are scarce.

He sees the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]'s decline clearly and speaks of it as fact rather than heresy, which makes him one of the few officers in the province who can be told the truth. He has said plainly that he will protect [[being-brakshrlns|Brakos Harlanis]] from court-martial as long as Brakos brings him accurate intelligence.

## Background

**The ranks.** Kauren enlisted at fifteen and came up the commoner track of the legions, from the line to a centurion's stick, the hard way.

**The patrols.** He spent his best years on mounted patrols in the hill country, where he learned that a report is worth more than a battle and that most reports are wrong.

**The garrison.** He commands the garrison's irregular riders now, and he has outlasted several commanders who did not want to hear what his riders found.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is grizzled, terse and unsentimental. He does not flatter, and he does not punish honesty. His dry humor is the only warmth he shows.

### Motivation

He wants accurate intelligence and men who can bring it back alive. He wants to finish his service without being made to lie about what the patrols see.

### Strengths

He reads ground and men well, he knows the hill tracks of the province, and his riders would follow him anywhere he chose to go.

### Weaknesses

His contempt for political appointees is obvious, and he makes enemies of people who can hurt him. He uses his men as tools and loses them as tools.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-orthemiskorv|Orthemis Korvathandis]]: The prefect sent from the capital, whose claims about bandit activity his riders' reports contradict.

### Others

- [[being-brakshrlns|Brakos Harlanis]]: His best rider, whom he shields from court-martial for the sake of the intelligence he brings. The bargain is transactional and real.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylrnmltry|The Vilérian Military]]**: a _hundar_ (centurion) of the commoner track, commanding the garrison's irregular riders.
- **[[affiliation-provinclys|The Province of Alessa]]**: a citizen by his service.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The mole.** Brakos tells Kauren that someone in the garrison knows the supply schedules and courier routes and is selling them. Kauren believes him, and he hires a party from outside the garrison to find the man without the prefect learning of it.
2. **The inspection.** An officer is coming from Orzara to inspect the legion. Kauren asks a party to ride out and meet him on the road, and to tell him which patrols to watch.
3. **The courier.** A dispatch rider brings Kauren sealed orders and stays a night longer than she needs to. Kauren wants to know whom else she has spoken to in the camp.

:::secret {title="The roll"}
Kauren knows that the legion's muster rolls carry a full legion while the camp holds rather more than half of one, and that the Vallar spends the missing men's pay on the camp's roofs, fuel and bread. His irregular riders are among the men who do exist. If the officer from Orzara counts the parade, Kauren's patrols are what the legion has, and he would rather the officer counted his riders than anything else.
:::
