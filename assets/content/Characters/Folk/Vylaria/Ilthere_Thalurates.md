# Appearance {#appearance}

Ilthere Thalurates is a 31-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build. She has fair skin with a high color, black hair dressed in the fashion of the court, and green eyes that stay on whoever she is bargaining with. Her clothes are new, costly and correct, and her one ornament is a ring of bright new gold cut with her house's cypher, a seal-ring on an open hand. She is pleasant, quick and never in a hurry.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lady Ilthere Thalurates is the daughter of [[being-krznsthlrts|Korzenas Thalurates]], master of [[affiliation-thalurates|House Thalurates]], and the house's face at court. The Thalurates are an Elevated house, raised from the clerks of the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]] within living memory, and their palace stands among the embassies in the Palace District of [[place-arvon|Arvon]], the Inner City of [[place-orzara|Orzara]].

Ilthere is pragmatic, self-serving and well connected. She offers political support in exchange for help with key figures in the Empire, and her bargains are always with people the house can use. The most interesting of them is [[being-klstrtsxrchds|Kalestratos Zarkedis]], a fire-mage of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] at [[place-velysara|Velisara]] and heir of the fallen [[affiliation-zarkedis|House Zarkedis]]. He despises her kind of politics and finds her indispensable.

## Background

**The house.** Ilthere was born into a house two generations old, rich from the fees and gifts of the Offices, and despised by the old houses whose debts it has begun to buy. She grew up in the new palace with three masks in the cupboard by its door.

**The court.** She is the house's face at court. She carries the house's offers, and makes its bargains with the people it can use.

**The succession.** The old Orzas has adopted nobody, and the next reign makes a new Keeper of the Seal. Ilthere works for what her father wants: the post kept in the Offices the house knows, and the house's daughters married well.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is charming, patient and unsentimental. She keeps her word to the letter and reads every bargain for what it leaves out.

### Motivation

She wants House Thalurates to have a hand in the succession, Silver standing and a place among the old houses, and she wants to be the one who won it.

### Strengths

Her house's coin, its clerks in the Offices and its factors in the debt markets stand behind every offer she makes, and she knows the court.

### Weaknesses

The old houses despise the Thalurates, and House Varkalenis votes against them on every question of standing. A bargain made with contempt on one side can break when it is most needed.

## Social

### Enemies

## Plot Hooks

1. **Ilthere's price.** Ilthere offers Kalestratos aid that will cost him his loyalty to House Thalurates, and asks a party to carry the offer, in writing, to Velisara.
2. **The address.** A minor kingdom's envoy has walked through the Thalurates' bronze gates believing it a shared embassy compound and signed something there. His legation hires a party to learn what, and Ilthere wants to know who hired them.
3. **The reception.** Ilthere invites a party with standing to a reception at the house's palace and introduces them to three senators in an evening. Next morning a factor of the house calls with an offer for a debt one of the party's friends owes.
4. **The opinion.** Ilthere wants to know what the scholars of the [[place-aulpnpstmm|Court of the Academy]] have answered on the succession before the answer reaches the Seldan, and pays a party to learn it.

:::secret {title="The masks of House Zarkedis"}
At the creditors' sale of House Zarkedis, the buyer of the Zarkedis house in [[place-skoraz|Soraks]] was a factor of House Thalurates, and the Zarkedis ancestor masks are still in its cupboard. The page of the province's registry naming the buyer has been cut from the book. Ilthere knows who bought the house. She has not told Kalestratos, and she means the masks to be the price she names when she asks him for the loyalty she wants.

She also knows of a sanctum where a conclave of Helionite fire-mages kept research too dangerous for their cities before the Order was founded, and she wants its secrets for her own purposes. She offers Kalestratos the house's aid in reaching it, at the cost of his whole loyalty to House Thalurates.

The house's factors hold a second debt, a note against the temple stipends of House Varkalenis, to be forgiven when the Varkalenis heir marries a Thalurates daughter.

**Hook: the cut page.** In [[scenario-rdtsrks|The Road to Soraks]], a clerk of Soraks has cut the registry page. A party that finds the page holds the name of the buyer, and if Kalestratos learns who bought his house, he has a quarrel with House Thalurates and a reason to want the masks back before Ilthere names her price.
:::
