# Appearance {#appearance}

Satheras Kiramaris is a 45-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of medium build. He has fair skin weathered by eight years of the northern march, light brown hair cut short under a helmet's line, and pale blue eyes. He wears the legion commander's harness on the walls and a plain officer's cloak in the fortress, and he looks at a man for a long moment before he answers him. He is careful and cold, and he asks more questions than he answers.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Satheras Kiramaris is the _vandraks_ (legion commander) of the legion at [[place-dolvar|Dolvar]], the fortress-town of the northern march, and a _kurthos_ (senator) of an old house of the capital. He took command eight years ago. He is the _therkos_'s deputy in the field for the [[affiliation-provncmktr|Province of Moktur]], and he answers to the capital by the road that runs through [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]].

Dolvar is not a chartered town. It is the legion's ground, and Satheras rules it by camp law: the fortress's officers judge the lower town's quarrels, the camp prefect sets its market prices, and a thief caught in the lower town is flogged at the fortress gate like a soldier. He keeps the truce with the nearest Vandstein villages, the frontier towers and the House of Guests, where the sons of the villages' War Chiefs live as surety for the truce. The wardens of the Nordman company [[affiliation-hrendskari|Hrendskari]], which holds seven of the towers, answer to him for every mark on their smoke tallies.

His legion is paid when the treasury's coin comes up the road through Tirellan, the road whose stations the _therkos_ keeps. This summer a tower's smoke tally is three days late, and he will not send a century into the hills on a guess. He has asked [[being-knrvsldvrnrk|Knarvselda Dvurnaruk]] for thirty more men for the towers above the pass, and she does not have them.

## Background

**The march.** He came north from the capital to take command of the legion at Dolvar eight years ago, and he has held the march through the summers of the truce market and the winters when the treasury's coin did not come.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is careful, cold and patient. He does not act on a guess, and he writes every dispatch knowing who else may read it.

### Motivation

He wants the truce kept and the march held, with a legion he cannot always pay and towers that are short of men.

### Strengths

He commands the only legion on the northern frontier, and he rules Dolvar outright. He is careful with the Nordman company and its captain, and he gets its towers manned and its captain sober.

### Weaknesses

His legion is paid late, his towers are short of men, and every letter he sends to the capital passes through the _therkos_'s stations.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The tally that did not come.** A tower's smoke tally is three days late and its dispatch rider has not come down. Satheras will not send a century into the hills on a guess, and he hires a party to ride up and see why the tower is silent.
2. **The tower that lit its fire.** A Hrendskari tower lights its basket at midnight, the signal for a war-band crossing, and no other tower saw anything. Satheras needs to know whether the warden lied or the other towers slept.
3. **What Tirellan is saying.** At Dolvar before the truce market, Satheras asks a party come up from the south what Tirellan is saying, and listens closely to the answer.

:::secret {title="Two things he has not done"}
Satheras Kiramaris received his copy of the recall of [[being-thlmrsnlmn|Tholmaras Nelamen]] by the legion's own courier, with an order to see it carried out, and he has not acted on it. Half his legion could reach Tirellan in a week, and it would meet twelve hundred auxiliaries paid on the day and sworn to the man they would be asked to give up. If the legion marched south, the frontier towers would stand empty in the raiding season. Through the last two winters, when the treasury's coin did not come, his legion was paid from the _therkos_'s strongrooms against his receipt. If the recall is ever read aloud in Tirellan's forum, he must march, or say plainly that he will not.

Every dispatch from Dolvar to the capital is opened at the posting-station below Tirellan's North Gate, read by the _pozerus_'s clerk, resealed with a copy of his seal and sent on a day late. He has known for years and writes accordingly.

One of the boys in the House of Guests is not who he is said to be. [Edrarith]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the son of the War Chief of [[place-vandgrund|Vandgrund]], died of a winter fever in the second month of last winter. Satheras did not send word north. He had the body buried at night in the garden of the House of Guests and put in Edrarith's place a half-Varokh stable-boy of the same age from [[place-pinnhald|Pinnhald]], who speaks the forest tongue and has been taught since the thaw to answer to the dead boy's name. The boys who share the House of Guests know, and two of them are sons of other War Chiefs. He has bought their silence with the promise of their own early return, which he has no authority to give.

He believes that the truth would break the truce in the first week of summer and bring Vandgrund over the pass in the second, with the towers short of men and the legion short of pay. He may be right. He has not told the _therkos_, because he will not hand a secret to a man who opens every dispatch from Dolvar.

**Hook: the uncle.** At the truce market the envoys see the boys across the marked stones, at the length of a spear-throw, with the cavalry between, and the stable-boy has passed once already. This summer Vandgrund's envoy is the dead boy's uncle, who knew him from the cradle and has asked to speak with him.
:::
