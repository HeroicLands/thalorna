# Appearance {#appearance}

Tharkos Tharozates is a 25-year-old man who stands 6'2" tall with a heavy build. He has fair skin, sun-kissed complexion, dark brown hair, and green eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tharkos Tharozates is the Overseer of the great prison, a political administrator appointed to manage it bureaucratically; its working Warden is [Nethoren Melvarandis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}. [[being-xrphnklmkns|Zerofon Kalmakanes]] views him as incompetent and corrupt. The two keep an awkward relationship of mutual necessity while harboring deep disdain for one another.

He lives at [[place-sanctara|Ulthora]], beneath Kezrora, the prison whose accounts he administers. Supply mules pass his door on their way up the rock road, and their keepers bring him bills for fodder, oil and bread. Nethoren holds the warden's appointment; Tharkos handles the papers, and Zerofon keeps the outer prison working. Tharkos has no key to the Sekran's inner keep and no authority over its prisoners.

## Background

**The appointment.** His advancement comes through a political appointment, not service among the prison guards. He arrives with an administrator's expectations: a signed order should produce a stated result, and a short account should explain an expense.

**The prison.** He learns that each total conceals work somebody has to do. Zerofon can explain why bread disappears faster on a cold week or why a replacement guard is not interchangeable with the man who knows a corridor. Tharkos needs those answers and resents needing their author.

**The accounts.** He now spends much of his time between the suppliers below the Shrine Stair and the men above it who certify delivery. He wants the next return to [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]] accepted without a demand that he appear and explain it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tharkos is punctilious about how requests reach him and less certain about what they mean. He is affable with petitioners who treat his post seriously, then dismissive when they bring him a problem he cannot solve from his desk.

### Motivation

He wants a successful administration recorded under his name and a career beyond the prison. A return rejected by the province threatens both.

### Strengths

He understands the forms his superiors expect and can obtain an audience for a request the guards cannot carry themselves. He remembers whose signature is needed on each kind of bill.

### Weaknesses

He depends on men whose judgment he resents. He can mistake a neatly balanced account for an adequately supplied prison, and he delays admitting an error until correcting it has become expensive.

## Social

### Others

- [Nethoren Melvarandis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the working Warden named in his appointment, visits Kezrora once a month. Tharkos needs his authorization and tries to keep their disagreements off the provincial return.
- [[being-xrphnklmkns|Zerofon Kalmakanes]], the jailer, knows what the prison actually requires. Their dislike does not remove their dependence on each other.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-provncmktr|Province of Moktur]]**: an appointed administrator responsible for Kezrora's bureaucratic business, distinct from its Warden and jailer.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The missing delivery.** A muleteer claims payment for lamp oil that the prison's storekeeper denies receiving. Tharkos hires a party to follow the delivery from the Ulthora shop to the gate before he signs either account.
2. **The provincial return.** Tirellan sends back an account with a request for its original vouchers. Tharkos asks a party to collect the suppliers' copies in town; one supplier demands an unpaid balance before releasing his.
3. **The prisoner's letter.** Word reaches Tharkos that a guard is selling a prisoner's letter below the Shrine Stair. He wants the letter brought to his office. Zerofon wants the seller named, and a party must decide which request it accepts.
