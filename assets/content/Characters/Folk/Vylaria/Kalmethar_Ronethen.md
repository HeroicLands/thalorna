# Appearance {#appearance}

Kalmethar Ronethen is an old man with gray hair and a carefully mended garrison cloak. He hears petitions standing at the morning parade, with the soldiers drawn up behind him.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kalmethar Ronethen holds the hereditary commission of the [[affiliation-cmmndnpdfrt|Command of the Unpaid Fort]] in the mountains of [[place-heladrgn|Hellád]]. The commission passes from parent to child. Its pay stopped in 619 AF, long before his own tenure, but it was never revoked. He governs [[place-unpaidfort|the fort]], its pass and the households under its walls as an imperial garrison, on its grain and tolls.

Each spring he sends the chancery at [[place-orzara|Orzara]] a report of the pass, stores and garrison. Each autumn the rider returns without an answer. Kalmethar keeps the drill and the accounts because those are the duties the commission gives him, whether the Empire pays or not.

## The Present {#present}

His heir refuses to take the commission without the chancery's confirmation. Kalmethar needs an answer while he is alive to receive it. The Bizarian League and the Helionite cities want the fort, and their envoys have reason to keep the report from reaching Orzara.

## Meeting Him

A traveler seeking shelter, a guide or a hearing comes before him at the morning parade. He settles household disputes there and expects the same plain account from a stranger that he requires of his keepers.

## Hook

- **The report.** Kalmethar hires a party to accompany the spring report to Orzara and bring back a chancery answer. The commission's unpaid age makes it easy to misfile and hard to refuse outright.
