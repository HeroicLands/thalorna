# Appearance {#appearance}

Sirema Linthimis is a 74-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of heavy build. She has tanned, weathered skin, black hair gone mostly white and braided back, and brown eyes. She wears the plain robe of a priestess of [[affiliation-lizelas|Lizelas]] with a gardener's apron over it more often than not, and she walks slowly with a hand on whoever is nearest. Her voice is soft and her questions are not.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Mother Sirema Linthimis is a _sarnor_ (ordained priestess) of the [[affiliation-lizelas|Faith of Lizelas]], one of the elders of the Temple's hierarchy. She is the patron within the Temple of [[being-rvthrbrmztr|Revothar Baremzator]], the circuit priest who walks twelve tenant villages around [[place-polthara|Polthara]], and she keeps the Temple's institutional support behind his circuit.

She has private reservations about him. His sympathies are radical, he says hard things about the Temple's indifference to the crisis in the countryside, and the conservative clergy would remove him if they could do it without trouble in his villages. Sirema holds that he does the goddess's work where it is most needed, and that the Temple is better served by keeping him inside it than by driving him out.

## Background

**The garden.** Sirema has served Lizelas through a long life in the work of its ordained clergy: healing, midwifery, the garden and the marriage rites.

**The hierarchy.** Age and long service have brought her standing in the Temple's hierarchy.

**The circuit.** When Revothar petitioned to leave the Temple's settled order and walk a circuit of his own, the Temple granted it, more to be rid of a persistent critic than because it shared his vision. He works under the Temple's sanction and with its resources, and Sirema keeps that support in place.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is gentle, plain-spoken and very stubborn.

### Motivation

She wants the sick tended and the hungry fed in the goddess's name, and she wants the Temple to remain the body that does it.

### Strengths

Her seniority, her long memory and the respect of the Temple's healers.

### Weaknesses

She is old, and her protection of Revothar lasts as long as she does. She does not know everything he does under it.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-rvthrbrmztr|Revothar Baremzator]]: The circuit priest of the Polthara villages, whom she shelters within the Temple despite her doubts about his radical sympathies.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-lizelas|Faith of Lizelas]]**: a _sarnor_, ordained priestess, and an elder of the Temple's hierarchy.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The report.** The Temple's conservative clergy ask Sirema to report on Revothar's circuit. She asks a party to walk it with him for a month and tell her what they saw, before she writes.
2. **The evidence.** A bishop in the capital is gathering evidence that Revothar is a heretic. Sirema wants to know what the bishop has, and hires a party to find out.
3. **The fever.** A fever reaches the Polthara villages on the grain carts. Sirema asks a party to carry medicines from the Temple's House of Healing to Revothar, and to bring back his account of the sickness.
4. **The successor.** A young woman of Revothar's villages asks to be trained to take over his circuit. Sirema wants to meet her before the Temple hears of her.

:::secret {title="What her protection covers"}
Revothar has begun to work outside the Temple's authority. He keeps hidden grain caches, moves grain by channels that blur charity into theft, and corresponds with reform movements within the priesthood that the Temple would suppress. A network calling itself [the Gathering]{.name data-type="affiliation/faithtradition"} coordinates with him over the moving of grain, and wants him to break with the Temple altogether. Sirema does not know any of this. Her support covers him, and if a cache is found, the conservatives will turn on her for having sheltered him.

**Hook: the cache.** If soldiers find one of Revothar's caches, the Temple's hierarchy demands his arrest, and Sirema must choose between her protégé and her standing. She hires a party to reach him before the Temple's order does.
:::
