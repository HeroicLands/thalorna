# Appearance {#appearance}

Tharkas Ganothandis is 73 years old. Illness leaves him thin and easily tired, with loose skin at his jaw and white hair combed back from a high forehead. He receives guests seated, remembers their names and apologizes when he cannot remain through supper. His courtesy gives no easy measure of how much strength a conversation costs him.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tharkas is master of [[affiliation-ganothandis|House Ganothandis]], an old Silver house of [[place-hylen|Hilen]]. He keeps its townhouse in [[place-aravantia|Aravantara]], holds its place in the city’s councils and sits in [[affiliation-seldan|the Seldan]]. Its greatest estate is Olkassa, where Vilérian legions killed one another and where [[place-olcassafld|the Blood-field]] bears wheat early and heavy.

His son and named heir, [Zetimas Ganothandis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, is twenty-four and wants the field settled by a priest of [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]] before his wedding. Tharkas has given him neither a yes nor a no. He wants his son married and the house secure, but each conversation about the rite ends with the decision still his to make.

## Background

**The lamp.** The house keeps the Imperial Cult’s shrine at the field’s edge to the Orzoi of both armies. Tharkas lights its lamp himself on the anniversary. The estate’s wealth rests on the same ground that gives its opposition to civil war such force.

**The marriage.** Zetimas is betrothed to [Varelas Mardovanes]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, daughter of [[being-nthrsmrdvns|Nethoras Mardovanes]]. Her uncle, [[being-klvnsmrdvns|Kolvenas Mardovanes]], makes the match. The wedding binds Hilen’s landed wealth to the capital’s grain-buyers. Tharkas receives the bride’s gifts graciously while his son asks for something a marriage settlement cannot promise: a field that is only a field.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is gracious and tired. He takes care to hear a guest’s errand before illness ends the audience, and resents an attempt to turn that courtesy into assent.

### Motivation

He wants an orderly imperial succession accepted by Senate and legions, a married heir and a house able to keep its obligations. He cannot make the question of the field disappear by arranging the rest carefully.

### Strengths

He understands the estate’s dependence on its harvest and the marriage’s importance to both houses. His long attention to Hilen’s councils gives him a clear sense of what the landed houses will defend together.

### Weaknesses

He postpones an answer his son needs before the wedding. His illness makes delay costly, and his refusal to decide leaves the steward to manage approaches to the estate without resolving the quarrel behind them.

## Social

Zetimas is earnest, devout and better read than his father. Tharkas does not mistake those qualities for obedience. Nethoras hopes the rite fails, while Kolvenas expects the marriage he arranges to strengthen both houses.

The estate’s steward keeps its gate. Surveyors of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Thordimalon]] meet a closed entrance, and a writ from outside the house raises a different question from a request by its heir. Tharkas’s kin and tenants remain under his hand; Zetimas’s status as heir does not give him the master’s authority.

### Affiliations

- **House Ganothandis**: Of the Name, holding Master of the House.
- **Seldan**: Ankren, seated for an established Silver house.
- **Vilérian Empire**: Kurthos of the senatorial estate.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The gifts.** The party brings the bride’s gifts to the Aravantara townhouse and needs Tharkas’s acknowledgment. Zetimas asks them to remain and help bring a priest to Olkassa; answering the heir does not settle his father’s consent.
2. **The third survey.** The Order’s surveyors return with a writ after being refused at the gate. The steward asks the party to establish who signs it and what it authorizes before Tharkas must answer a demand backed by an unfamiliar seal.
3. **The unanswered request.** Zetimas asks the party to speak to his father about the rite. Tharkas hears them, but an account of what settling the field might cost matters more to him than reassurance that everything will be well.

:::secret {title="The harvest and the Stone"}
Tharkas agrees to the Mardovanes’s forward sale of the next three Olkassa harvests at a price only the quickened field can meet. Zetimas does not know the sale exists. If the rite settles the field and ends its quickening, the house breaks its word to the house its heir is marrying.

Tharkas’s oath to the Compact of the Stone is the first sworn by a master at the meeting two springs ago. The houses promise to close Aravantara’s Yards to any claimant who brings a legion inside the capital’s boundary when the Orzas dies, until the Senate and legions accept one sovereign. The compact binds the same grain Tharkas has agreed to sell. The list of sworn masters lies in the provincial temple beneath the Orzas’s image, in the zalvos’s keeping. The therkos does not know of the compact.
:::
