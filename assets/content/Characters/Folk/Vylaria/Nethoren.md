# Appearance {#appearance}

Nethoren is a 42-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of medium build. He has olive skin gone sallow from indoor work, dark brown hair greying at the temples and brown eyes, and the second finger of his right hand carries a writer's callus. He dresses as a freedman of a great household, plainly and well, and he holds a sealed packet with both hands. He reads aloud in a clear, careful voice that reaches the bottom of the steps.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nethoren is the freedman secretary of [[being-kldrmslthzs|Kaldremas Elthezis]], the _therkos_ (governor) of the [[affiliation-provinclys|Province of Alessa]]. His master has kept to his villa at [[place-vennara|Vennara]] since the winter, and the province's acts come down to [[place-alyssar|Alessar]] sealed. On the morning after each packet arrives Nethoren reads its acts aloud from the steps of the [Governor's House]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, and the crowd of petitioners in the square listens for its own names.

He is the province's voice while its governor is away, and he is a freedman, with no standing of his own beside the officers who keep their offices round the square. The _karezos_ (revenue officer) outranks him, the advocates of Petitioners' Row are richer than he is, and every one of them watches whom he speaks to. He hires the guards who carry the province's packets up the north road to the Northern Bench at [[place-skoraz|Soraks]], and he pays them from his own purse.

## Background

**The household.** He is the _therkos_'s freedman, and his secretary.

**The steps.** Since his master kept to Vennara he has read every act of the province from the steps. He rides up to Vennara once a month.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is careful, precise and honest by his own lights, and he is frightened. He keeps his own counsel and keeps it well.

### Motivation

He wants the province's business done as his master would have done it, and he wants to be able to answer for every act when the day comes that he is asked to.

### Strengths

He knows the province's business better than any officer on the square, because a year of it has passed through his hands. He refuses every bribe he is offered, and Alessar knows it.

### Weaknesses

He is a freedman whose standing rests on a dying man. When his master dies, the next _therkos_'s staff decides what becomes of him.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The packet north.** Nethoren comes down the steps after the morning's reading with a sealed packet under his arm and looks for someone who is not from the province. He hires a party to carry it up the north road to the Northern Bench at Soraks, and pays from his own purse.
2. **The act's news.** A party that learns what the packet held can carry the news back to Nethoren in Alessar, and he wants to know who else has learned it.

:::secret {title="The ring and the ledger"}
The _therkos_ has not sealed an act himself since the spring. Kaldremas Elthezis is dying at Vennara, lost in his mind on most days, and his seal-ring is in Alessar in Nethoren's keeping. Nethoren seals the province's business with it and makes up the sealed packets that go up to Vennara with the couriers and come down again, so that the road sees every act come down from the villa. He seals what the _therkos_ would have sealed, he refuses every bribe for anything else, and he keeps a ledger of every act in his own hand, against the day he must answer for them.

In law his honesty makes no difference. An act sealed without the holder of the seal is a forgery, and every judgment, lease and assessment the province has issued since the spring can be challenged by anyone who proves the _therkos_ never saw it. The packet he hires the party to carry holds an act moving the Northern Bench and its registry from Soraks south to [[place-lentora|Lentora]], and it is sealed with the ring.

His ledger is a year of the province's business in one book. The first person to read it holds every family that lost a case this year. When the _therkos_ dies, the _karezos_ hears a day early, and the ledger goes into the fire or into a drawer unless someone has it first.

**Hook: the ledger.** If the _therkos_ dies while a party Nethoren trusts is in Alessar, they may reach the ledger before the _karezos_'s men clear the Governor's House.
:::
