# Appearance {#appearance}

Orthemis Korvathandis is a 33-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She has medium skin weathered by two years of provincial roads, auburn hair worn in a plain coil, and brown eyes that read a page faster than most people read a face. She dresses in the capital's fashion, sober and well cut, and wears the bronze seal-case of an officer of the Offices on a cord at her belt. She speaks precisely, smiles seldom, and listens with her head a little to one side.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Orthemis Korvathandis is an _usos_ (prefect) of [[affiliation-imperialoffices|the Imperial Offices]], a political appointee sent from [[place-orzara|Orzara]] two years ago to [[place-alyssar|Alessar]] with orders to strengthen imperial authority in [[affiliation-provinclys|the Province of Alessa]]. She writes to the capital about the province's bandits, its dissidents and the reliability of its officers, and the capital reads what she writes.

Her reports on bandit activity in the hills were contradicted by a rider of the garrison, [[being-brakshrlns|Brakos Harlanis]], who had evidence where she had speculation. Since then she has quietly obstructed his advancement and spread word that he is soft on dissidents, and she watches for any excuse to dishonor him. They have not spoken directly since.

## Background

**The capital.** Orthemis was born to a propertied family of the capital and entered the Offices young, rising to a prefect's seal on the strength of her pen.

**The commission.** She was sent to Alessa with orders to strengthen the throne's hand in a province whose governor keeps to his villa.

**The province.** In two years she has learned the square at Alessar and the names of the officers on it, and she has made her reports the measure of who is reliable.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is intelligent, cold and very sure of herself. She forgives a mistake more easily than a contradiction.

### Motivation

She wants her commission to succeed and to be seen to succeed in the capital, and she wants a better desk at the end of it.

### Strengths

Her pen reaches the capital, her seal opens doors on the square, and she is patient in a quarrel.

### Weaknesses

She mistakes a confident report for an accurate one. Her quarrel with a mere rider has made her look petty to the officers who know of it.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-brakshrlns|Brakos Harlanis]]: The rider whose report contradicted hers, whom she means to see disgraced.
- [[being-khaurenilthe|Kauren Iltheris]]: The garrison's irregular-war officer, who shields Brakos and makes no secret of his contempt for appointees.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-imperialoffices|The Imperial Offices]]**: an _usos_, holding no desk in the capital while she serves in the province.
- **[[affiliation-provinclys|The Province of Alessa]]**: a _gulmen_ of the propertied order.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The governor's acts.** The _therkos_ has not been seen in Alessar since the spring, and his acts come down sealed from his villa. Orthemis hires a party to ride to Vennara and see him with their own eyes.
2. **The leak.** Imperial dispatches have been found among a smuggler's goods in the hills. Orthemis wants the leak traced to the garrison's riders, and she pays a party to look there first.
3. **The deserter.** Brakos Harlanis has been called home to claim his family's estate. Orthemis asks a party to learn whether he has leave, and to tell her the day he rides.
4. **The court's list.** A place on the court's list is for sale at the innkeepers' tables on Petitioners' Row. Orthemis wants the clerk who sells it named in her next report.

:::secret {title="The bandit reports"}
Orthemis wrote her first report on the bandits of the hills before she had seen the hills, from what the officers on the square told her the capital wanted to hear. Brakos's report is the only paper in the province that contradicts it, and she has asked a clerk of the garrison to lose it. The clerk has not, and he keeps the copy against the day he needs a favor.
:::
