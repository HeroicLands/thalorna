# Appearance {#appearance}

Kelvaras Daronis is a 41-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall and is of heavy build. He has light skin weathered and reddened by the hunt, dark brown hair, a full beard cut square, and brown eyes that move to the door whenever it opens. A small patch of skin on his forehead is paler and smoother than the rest, and he touches it when he is thinking. He dresses as a forest lord rather than a king, in green wool and good boots, and wears the plain gold circlet only in the hall. The last joint of his left little finger is gone, lost to a boar-spear in his youth. He laughs loudly and often, and stops laughing all at once.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kelvaras Daronis is the anointed king of the [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]], of the royal house descended from [Daronos]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the first king the high priesthood anointed after it broke with the empire ([[lore-nartumrefuse|Nartun Refuses the Empire]]). He keeps the king's hall on the terrace below the Mother's Hall in the holy city of [[place-nartum|Nartun]], commands the host, holds the king's court and the crown forests toward the Moktur mountains, and is crowned by the Holy Mother's hand and by nothing else.

He has reigned eleven years. The lower city says that the high priesthood will sit in judgment on him before winter, and he has heard it.

## Background

**The refused cousin.** When the old king died without a son, the high priesthood divided between his two cousins. [[being-ilvnssrkls|Ilvenas Sarkelis]], the Holy Mother, refused the elder, [Ormelas Daronis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, for a winter, and then anointed Kelvaras, the younger. Ormelas went to his estate and stayed there. Kelvaras has never been sure what she saw in him, or what she saw in his cousin that she did not like.

**The forest king.** He was raised in the crown forests and has spent more of his reign in them than in the hall. He knows the foresters and the barons by name, rides with the barons' sons, and keeps open table for any baron who comes up to the city to renew a lease. The barons are the king's men in a kingdom where the temples own the land, and he has made sure of them.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is warm, loud and generous with the barons and the host, and stiff and careful with the priests. He does not like to sit still. He has a hunter's patience in the forest and none in council, and his Lord of the Seal has learned to bring him decisions already written.

### Motivation

He wants to keep his crown and die in it. Under that he wants to rule a kingdom rather than preside over a priesthood's, and he resents every reminder that the hall above his is higher.

### Strengths

The barons love him, the host follows him, and the Order of the Sacred Flame rides as his heavy horse in war. He is a good captain and an honest judge in his own court, and the freemen of the lower city like him for both.

### Weaknesses

The high priesthood can unmake him, and the priest-noble families fill his own offices: the man who keeps his seal sits in his temple's chapter the same afternoon. He has no coin of his own beyond the crown forests' dues, and the temples' treasuries lend to barons and merchants, not to him. He has no friend outside the kingdom.

## Social

### Companions

- [[being-ardmsvlthmn|Ardemos Velthamen]]: A lay baron of the temple forests, his hunting companion and his loudest friend among the barons.

### Rivals

- [[being-ilvnssrkls|Ilvenas Sarkelis]]: The Holy Mother, who anointed him and can take the oil back. He is courteous to her in the hall and does not go up to hers unless he is summoned.
- [[being-sldrsplvrn|Saldoros Pelvaren]]: Master of the Order, who rides with him in war and answers to the Holy Mother in peace.

### Enemies

- [[being-vrthlszrkms|Varthelas Zerkemis]]: High Priest of Sélmoros, who preaches that the Withering falls on a kingdom whose king neglects the rites, and who is counting the high priests' votes.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]]**: King, of the anointed house.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The count.** The king's chamberlain hires a party to learn how each of the high priests means to vote if the judgment is called. A priest-noble house wants the same answer, and has hired others.
2. **The cousin's guests.** Ormelas Daronis has had visitors from the temple quarter at his estate three times since spring. The king wants to know who, and wants no sergeant of his seen on the road.
3. **The king's hunt.** The king has called the barons to a great hunt in the crown forest toward Moktur, and the temples read it as a muster. A party hired as huntsmen sees which barons come, which send their sons, and which send excuses.
4. **The oil.** The temple of Marendas keeps the vessel of anointing oil, and a novice says it has been moved from its place. The king's household wants a party to find out whether someone is preparing to anoint another man.

:::secret {title="The king's letter to Orzara"}
Kelvaras knows enough to be afraid. He knows the judgment is real, he knows [[being-vrthlszrkms|Varthelas Zerkemis]] has the votes of at least four high priests, and he knows that his cousin is being visited. He does not know why. He has not been told of the Holy Mother's warrants or of the prisoners in the chapter house over the gate, and he does not know that the high priests who want him gone are the ones who keep that from him. He has noticed only that the Order's captains fall silent when he enters a room, and that the knights of the Ashenmere chapel ride hard horses.

He has one strong ally outside the kingdom, and it is the empire his kingdom refused. In the spring, after the first rumor of the judgment, he wrote to the Orzas in his own hand: if the high priesthood moves to unmake an anointed king, he will acknowledge the throne of [[place-orzara|Orzara]] as the kingdom's overlord in name, receive an imperial envoy at his court, and open the holy road to the empire's officers, in return for the Orzas's word that the legions will not suffer a deposition. He has not sent it. The letter lies in a cedar box in his bedchamber, sealed with his private ring, and nobody else has read it, not even his Lord of the Seal. If he sends it and it is known, he gives the high priesthood its judgment, and the kingdom its first civil war since the break.

**Hook: the courier.** The king asks a party of foreigners, people the temples do not know, to carry a sealed box to Orzara and put it into the hands of the Orzas's secretary and no one else. He does not say what is in it, and he tells them to burn it unopened if the Order's knights stop them on the road.
:::
