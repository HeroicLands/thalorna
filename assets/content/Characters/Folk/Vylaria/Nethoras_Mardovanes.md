# Appearance {#appearance}

Nethoras Mardovanes is a 51-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of heavy build. He has light skin with a high, healthy color, graying brown hair worn short in the court fashion and hazel eyes that settle on whoever is speaking and stay there. He is clean-shaven, broad in the jaw and comfortable in his body, and he moves through a crowded room like a host, touching an elbow here and asking after a son there. He wears the senator's stripe on good plain wool and a gilded sheaf on his right hand, the house's badge. His voice is warm and unhurried, and he laughs easily at other men's jokes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nethoras Mardovanes is master of [[affiliation-mardovanes|House Mardovanes]], the Grain House of [[place-orzara|Orzara City]], and a _kurthos_ (senator) of the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]]. His house's money is grain: its factors buy on the estates of [[place-hylen|Hilen]], see the wagons weighed at [[place-aravantia|Aravantara]], bring the sacks down the [[place-vylarisrvr|River Vylaris]] on barges under the house's contracts, and sell them into the state granaries on the [[place-wharvesorz|Wharves]]. The city holds about a week of grain, and the house that fills the granaries knows it better than anyone.

He gives the best dinners on [[place-selvin|Selvin]]. The succession is discussed at every one of them without the Orzas ever being named, and every guest leaves owing him a small favor.

## Background

**The newest Gilded house.** The Mardovanes were raised to the _ethlen_ (Gilded) estate in the reign of their own great man, the Orzas Azénales, a son of the house adopted into House Helarkales a century ago. The old houses have never forgiven the house that signed away the west and was gilded for the signature, and Nethoras grew up learning to smile at men who would not take a cup of wine at his father's table.

**The grain trade.** He served his father's chief factor for ten years before he inherited, riding the Hilen estates at harvest and standing on the quays at night while the barges came in. He knows the price of a barge string, the depth of the river at [[place-ostenna|Ostenna]] in a dry late summer and the name of every granary-keeper on the River Circle. He became master at thirty-six and has doubled the house's contracts since.

**The Olkassa wedding.** His daughter [Varelas Mardovanes]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, nineteen, is betrothed to [Zetimas Ganothandis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, heir of the Olkassa estate in Hilen, whose wheat comes in early and half again as heavy as any other field's. The marriage joins the house that sells the capital its bread to the house that grows the best of it.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is patient, well informed and genuinely pleasant to people below him, which the old houses find suspicious in itself. He remembers names, children and grievances, and he forgives the first two and never the third. He prefers a bargain that leaves the other man grateful to one that leaves him beaten, because a grateful man comes back.

### Motivation

He wants a duty on foreign grain. It would raise the price of every sack the Khelathi and the river shippers bring in, leave the Hilen houses the only cheap grain in the capital and give the throne revenue it need not borrow. He wants the Olkassa wedding made and the harvests coming in. He wants a temple for Azénales, the step from half-divine to divine that only the Senate can vote. Above all he wants the next Orzas to owe the Grain House his bread.

### Strengths

He controls the capital's bread more surely than the throne does. He is liked, which very few great men in Orzara are, and his dinners give him the best intelligence on Selvin. He thinks a season ahead of most of his rivals, and a season is as far as anyone in Orzara thinks.

### Weaknesses

The house's fortune rests on a few fields in Hilen and a river that runs low in dry years. The old houses will vote against him on principle. He needs the Senate for the temple and the throne for the duty, and both are in the hands of men who resent the house that has both.

## Social

### Patrons

The house has no patron above the throne. Its natural allies are these:

- [[affiliation-ganothandis|House Ganothandis]]: The Hilen house that holds Olkassa, chief of the Hilen houses, whose heir is betrothed to his daughter.
- [[affiliation-imperialcult|Imperial Cult]]: The state cult, which keeps Azénales half-divine.

### Enemies

- [[affiliation-varkalenis|House Varkalenis]]: Its master will not dine with him, and its vote has kept Azénales short of a temple for a century.
- [[being-mrndsvskln|Marendos Vaskelen]]: The shipping house on the Wharves, whose fast outside grain is the only thing that can undercut Hilen's in a lean month, and who wants trade free.
- [[being-mthszrtml|Methisi Azeret'Mulu]]: The Khelathi factor on the Strangers' Row, who offers the throne grain on credit. Nethoras calls it the first sack of a siege, at every dinner.
- [[being-amrzvlds|Amarza Veldis]]: Senior partner of the House of Veldis, whose bankers stand with the trade bloc against any duty, because a duty raises bread and bread raises riots.

### Family

- [[being-klvnsmrdvns|Kolvenas Mardovanes]]: His younger brother, the house's claimant to the adoption, who lives in the house on Selvin at the house's expense.

### Others

- [[being-sthmrrdln|Sathemar Ardelen]]: The tribune of the veterans' stairs, who has dined on Selvin twice this year. Nethoras courts him because the stairs eat the house's bread, and a duty that raises its price needs the tribune's silence.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-mardovanes|House Mardovanes]]**: master of the house, holding its masks, its heir's naming and its seat in the Seldan.
- **[[affiliation-seldan|The Seldan]]**: a _kurthos_ of the Gilded estate, in the newest of its houses.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The low river.** The river is low at Ostenna and the night's barges are late. Nethoras hires a party to ride up the river road, find his barge strings and bring their masters' tallies back to Selvin before the Grand Market opens.
2. **The bride's father.** The heir of Ganothandis wants the field of Olkassa settled by the rite of the [[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]] before his wedding. Nethoras hires a party to escort the priests to the field and report to him, and he is plainly hoping the rite fails.
3. **The votes.** Nethoras sends a party round the [[place-heightsorz|Heights]] with gifts for _kurthoi_ whose votes on Azénales's temple might be bought, and one of them owes the Varkalenis a favor.
4. **The Khelathi sacks.** A cargo of Khelathi grain landed on the Strangers' Row is said to be spoiled. Nethoras pays a party to buy a sack of it and bring it to his steward before the Grand Market opens.

:::secret {title="The Olkassa contract"}
The house has sold the next three Olkassa harvests forward to the state granaries at a price no other field in Hilen could meet. The [[affiliation-tarmex|Tarmeks]] advanced a third of the price on borrowed money, and the advance stands in the Imperial _Tarkelos_'s true accounts against grain that has not been grown. If the field of Olkassa is settled and its quickening ends, the Mardovanes cannot fill the contract, and the throne has paid for bread that does not exist. The master of Ganothandis knows of the sale and agreed to it. His heir does not.

So the Grain House is one of the houses that cannot survive a hard audit of the throne's books. Nethoras does not know which other houses share the danger. He knows only that [[being-drvnsrlln|Dorvanis Arelliani Septarius]] has never mentioned the advance at any meeting, and he has begun sending the _Tarkelos_'s household a gift on every feast day.

He does not know that two of the state granaries on the River Circle hold sand. If the reserve is opened and the sand found, the throne will buy grain at any price, and the house that sells it first will have the city's gratitude and its silver together. Told of it, he is shocked first and thoughtful after.
:::
