# Appearance {#appearance}

Satheros Venthelis is 68 years old. He has white hair, a long narrow face and a slight stoop that disappears when he stands to address a chamber. His fingers rest on the edge of the table while others speak. When discussion circles back to a settled point, he names the decision and waits for someone to challenge it openly.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Satheros is the zetmarendos of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Thordimalon]], elected for life by its holmarendoi. He presides over the Verdan Maldor at the domed chapter house on [Mernel]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the Scholars’ Hill of [[place-orzara|Orzara]]. He speaks for the Order to foreign powers and may overrule a chapter on matters that concern the whole Order. A local quarrel does not become such a matter merely because its losing side brings it to him.

He wants the Order’s answer to the dispute over [[place-magngndorery|the Orrery]] to survive both the council and the treasury. Reformers press to see the accounts; the [[affiliation-tarmex|Tarmeks]] and the Order each demand the other’s disclosure. Satheros asks what must actually be produced before allowing a speech about institutional dignity to consume the sitting. The question wins him no lasting alliance with either side.

## Background

**The council.** His election gives him the chair for life, but it does not give every member the same purpose. Imperial Traditionalists tie the Order’s future to the Empire; Reformers press its interests across the realms where it teaches and works. Satheros hears both. On business that can divide chapters, he wants the objections spoken in council before they travel abroad as rival promises.

**The charter.** The Order holds its own property, elects its own head and tries its members under [[lore-kandrol|the Kandrol]]. It answers to the state as a body. Satheros defends that settlement and the monopoly it grants; neither the throne nor a foreign employer gains command of the Order simply by asking for a mage’s service.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is courteous to a long-winded speaker once. After that he asks for a proposal the council can decide. He dislikes surprise motions more than open opposition.

### Motivation

He wants chapters to remain answerable to one Order even when they face different courts, laws and patrons. He also wants a decision on the Orrery’s accounts that does not leave every chapter to negotiate its own answer.

### Strengths

He can separate a dispute about jurisdiction from a dispute about money. He remembers which concessions a chapter can make locally and which bind the whole Order.

### Weaknesses

He waits for objections to be stated plainly. A master who obstructs an agreement through delays and intermediaries can occupy him longer than one who refuses it in council.

## Social

The holmarendoi are his electors and his colleagues, with chapters and former pupils behind them. [[affiliation-sekran|The Sekran]] answers to the Verdan as its enforcement arm. Satheros can demand an account of its work without turning its commission into his private instrument.

Abroad, [[affiliation-auricompct|the Auric Compact]] seeks the Order’s expulsion from Taruzel, while [[affiliation-mrchntryvl|the Merchantry of the Veil]] hires its mages and [[affiliation-corsairleg|the Corsair League]] wants battle-magic. These incompatible demands come to him as business the Order must answer. The chapter at [[place-beravel|Péravel]] presses into [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] despite the limits of the royal guides; its ambitions do not give Satheros authority over the forest’s wardens.

### Affiliations

- **Thordimalon**: Zetmarendos, elected by the holmarendoi for life and presiding over the Verdan Maldor.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The disputed account.** A reforming master asks the party to establish which Orrery records the treasury holds and which the Order holds. Satheros will consider a defined demand for documents; a list that reveals a gap gives the petitioner a stronger case than another general accusation.
2. **Terms abroad.** A Taruzelian employer offers work whose terms assume powers the local guilds deny the Order. Satheros needs the actual terms carried back before the chapter accepts, and the employer offers the party more money to deliver an easier version.
3. **The chapter’s report.** A Péravel expedition returns an account that leaves its route unclear. Satheros asks for testimony from its hired guides before judging the chapter’s request for more support; the party can establish where the expedition went without explaining what it found.
