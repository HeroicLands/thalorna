# Appearance {#appearance}

Kiramos Talothates is a 33-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of light build. He has tanned skin, brown hair worn short and neat, and brown eyes that stay on whoever is speaking. He dresses plainly for a man of his house, in good dark wool, and wears one ring, a merchant's signet. He is courteous, unhurried and quick to laugh, and he never seems to be in a room by accident.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kiramos Talothates is a merchant prince of [[place-belekos|Belekos]], the master of one of the great houses that live over their counting rooms above the grain valleys of Alessa. He trades in grain and river freight in the hall, buys art in public, and sits at the dinners where the houses settle the city's business before the council does.

He is also a buyer of exotic poisons and antidotes from [[being-hrkhsbrmyds|Harkis Baremides]], the poisoner of the underworld, and he moves between the legitimate and the shadow trade so easily that Harkis cannot say which one is his.

## Background

**The house.** Kiramos was born to a great house of Belekos and raised in its counting hall, learning grain prices before he learned his letters.

**The mastery.** He took the house young, and he has kept it among the great ones in a city where a house that falls leaves its townhouse, its warehouses and its contracts to a race of creditors.

**The contract.** He first sent for Harkis through a go-between and has dealt with him directly ever since. He pays well, on the day, and asks for things nobody else asks for.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is charming, patient and very careful. He enjoys a difficult bargain for its own sake and treats every acquaintance as a possible asset.

### Motivation

He wants his house to stand when others fall, and he wants to know more about his rivals than they know about him.

### Strengths

His house's credit, his connections in the counting rooms of Alessa and the capital, and a reputation for paying promptly in both trades.

### Weaknesses

His dealings with Harkis would ruin him if they were proved. He trusts nobody, which leaves him dependent on people he pays.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-hrkhsbrmyds|Harkis Baremides]]: The poisoner he contracts for poisons and antidotes, whom he treats as a valued craftsman and keeps at arm's length.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-provinclys|The Province of Alessa]]**: a _gulmen_ of the propertied order, as a master of a great house.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The fallen house.** A house that fell last year has left a townhouse, a warehouse and a gladiator under contract. Kiramos hires a party to reach the warehouse before the house's last son does, and says nothing of what he expects to find in it.
2. **The window.** A glassworker is making a window of a rival house's broken emblem. Kiramos wants to know which house ordered it, and he offers a party the glassworker's next commission as payment.
3. **The go-between.** A courier who carries Kiramos's letters to a man in the capital has not come back. Kiramos asks a party to find him and to bring back the letter unopened.
4. **The dinner guest.** Kiramos lodges a party as his guests in Belekos and asks them to call on a rival house, so that he can see how the rival receives them.

:::secret {title="The two ledgers"}
Kiramos's house is one of the great houses of Belekos that keep a second ledger of their accounts with Taruzelian banking houses and their shares in Taruzelian ships, written in the cipher all five houses share. The poisons and antidotes he buys from Harkis are his insurance in the quarrels between the houses: an antidote for every poison he has seen used in Belekos, and a poison he has not yet used.

The clerk who offers to sell a copy of a second ledger has sold the key to all five. Kiramos wants that clerk found before anyone reads the copy, and he will pay Harkis for the means if a party will not find him first.
:::
