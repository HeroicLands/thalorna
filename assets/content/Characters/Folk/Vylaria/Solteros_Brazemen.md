# Appearance {#appearance}

Solteros Brazemen is a 34-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build. He has olive skin weathered by the sea, brown hair tied back at the nape and brown eyes. He dresses like a master who wants the shipping houses to trust him with a cargo: a clean blue coat, a good belt with a purse and a knife on it, and a roll of papers under his arm. He is quick and cheerful in company, and he listens to a sailor's answer to the end.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Solteros Brazemen is a merchant captain of [[place-mercavia|Delalara]]. He sails for the shipping houses that own the hulls and buy the cargoes, crewing each voyage himself, and the Colonnade knows him as a master who brings ships home. He hires carefully, and when he has a voyage to crew the first person he looks for is [[being-brnlnsbrkls|Branalenis Barkalas]]. He knows her value better than most masters on the quays and puts up with her temper for it. Each time he hires her he offers her a permanent place in his crew, and each time she turns it down.

## Background

**Before the mast.** Solteros went to sea young from Delalara and learned the trade in the house ships, as most masters of the city do.

**Mate and master.** He rose through the berths of the merchant fleet by steady work, and a shipping house gave him his first command in his late twenties.

**The present.** He now sails for the houses as a hired master. He has his own way of choosing a crew, by what a sailor can do rather than whom she knows, and it has made him a reputation for lucky voyages.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is good-humored, patient and practical, and he is a better judge of seamen than of merchants. He would rather have one first-rate hand than three cheap ones.

### Motivation

He wants a crew he can trust and a ship that comes home. He wants Branalenis aboard for good, and he means to wait until she agrees.

### Strengths

He knows the sailors of the waterfront and what each is worth. The houses trust him with their hulls, and [[being-talotharzeno|Talothar Zenothenos]], the harbor-master, tells him and Branalenis which ships are short of hands.

### Weaknesses

He commands a hull he does not own and sails on a house's terms, so a house that dislikes his crew can take his ship. His wish to keep Branalenis makes him slow to rein her in when her temper costs him.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-brnlnsbrkls|Branalenis Barkalas]]: The best seaman he knows, whom he hires whenever she will come and hopes to keep.
- [[being-talotharzeno|Talothar Zenothenos]]: The harbor-master of Delalara, who gives him the news of the harbor before the Colonnade has it.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-provincvld|Province of Salkalon]]**: a palvethos (resident without the citizenship) of the province.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A crew for the east.** Solteros has a voyage east through the Taruzelian islands, with the harbormaster's dues, the pilot's fee and a convoy place to pay. He is two hands short and offers a party berths for the passage, with a share in the voyage.
2. **The rumor.** A rival sailor has been telling the masters of the waterfront that Branalenis cannot be relied on. Solteros wants the rumor stopped before the houses hear it, and asks a party to find where it started.
3. **The sea-loan.** The house that owns his ship has sold sea-loans on her next voyage, and a creditor of the house is asking questions about whether she will sail at all. Solteros hires a party to stand watch on the quay the night before she sails.

:::secret {title="The permanent berth"}
Solteros means to buy a hull of his own with shares sold at the Colonnade, and he wants Branalenis as his mate before he does it, because a ship of his own with her aboard is a ship the houses would trust with their best cargoes. He has not told her. He thinks she would refuse if she knew how much the plan rests on her, and he is probably right.
:::
