# Appearance {#appearance}

Eltheza Solterates is a 47-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of light build. She has tanned, sun-browned skin, brown hair pinned tight under her helmet, and hazel eyes that move constantly along the quay. She wears her cohort's mail and a captain's sash, and she carries a short baton that she taps against her palm while others speak. She walks fast, and her men walk fast behind her.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Eltheza Solterates commands the cohort of the province's urban cohorts that is quartered on the quays of [[place-mercavia|Delalara]], the port of [[affiliation-provincvld|the Province of Salkalon]]. The cohort guards the Customs House and walks the waterfront at night. Order on the quays has long rested on the shipping houses, their quay-masters and the harbor-master, and the cohort has come out with clubs only for a riot, an attack on the Customs House or a crime in the foreign quarters.

Eltheza means to change that. The city guard has been reorganized and the docks patrol put under her, and she calls the patrol soft. She wants searches on the berths, arrests on the quays and a cohort the waterfront fears. Her supervisor of the night patrol, [[being-lothnhrlns|Lothona Harlanis]], is ordered to carry her methods out.

## Background

**The cohort.** Eltheza came up through the urban cohorts of the province, by the gate and the forum rather than the quays.

**The ambition.** She has watched captains grow old on the waterfront while the houses kept the peace for them, and she believes the cohort's place has been given away.

**The command.** The reorganization gave her the docks patrol, and she means to make her name with it while she has it.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is brisk, certain and impatient, and she judges a patrol by the number of names in its book. She dislikes the waterfront's arrangements and the people who live by them.

### Motivation

She wants the quays under the cohort's hand and her own promotion out of it.

### Strengths

She is energetic and brave, and the province's officers on the forum like a captain who reports results.

### Weaknesses

She does not know the docks, and she will not learn them from Lothona. Her searches anger the houses that fill the council, and the houses have the ear of the Governor's House.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-lothnhrlns|Lothona Harlanis]]: The supervisor of the docks night patrol, who has kept the quays' balance for years and is now ordered to break it.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-provincvld|The Province of Salkalon]]**: a _gulmen_ of the propertied order, and a captain of the province's urban cohorts.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The searches.** Eltheza hires a party to stand with her men while they search a Taruzelian factor's warehouse in the Eastern Quarter, the spring his waiver is late.
2. **The customs.** A Hilen wine merchant has paid twice on one cargo, and he blames Eltheza's men. She wants a party to find out whose clerks took the second payment.
3. **The repainted ship.** A ship reported lost off the Tamzir Shoals has been seen in Kudumel repainted. Eltheza wants the house that owned her questioned before the harbor-master can warn it.
4. **The night patrol.** Eltheza asks a party to walk the quays with Lothona's patrol and tell her which of the night's arrests Lothona did not make.

:::secret {title="The nephew on the patrol"}
A young guard of Lothona's patrol, the nephew of a merchant family with political connections, takes a smuggler's money to let contraband past the patrol. Eltheza does not know of the bribe. Her searches have not touched the cargo he passes, and his family has just won a contract from the town council that keeps the harbor, so the arrest she would make if she knew would cost her the harbor-master's goodwill and the houses' along with it.
:::
