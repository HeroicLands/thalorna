# Appearance {#appearance}

Korvathos Volkemates is a 47-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build, though he has grown thinner than his clothes. He has tanned skin, black hair with little grey in it, and pale blue eyes in a face that has hollowed this past year. He coughs and does not acknowledge it. He dresses as a border lord does, in good wool and a fur collar, with a sword he still wears every day, and he greets guests at his tower's door himself.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Korvathos Volkemates is a lord of the march of [[place-moktur|Moktur]], the master of the Volkemates house. He keeps one of the square towers of the border lords in [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]], where his household's men sleep in the lower floors and the young men of the house learn the spear in his yard. His household keeps sworn fighting men against the raids of [[lore-varokhiclt|the Varokh]] that come over the march every summer, and the old huscarl [[being-arznlsbrds|Arzanalas Baredas]], who swore to the house at seventeen, trains them.

His street is safe because his men keep it so, and a shopkeeper robbed in the night on it comes to his tower in the morning. Like the other border lords, he stands at the state's offerings in the forum and watches the rest.

## Background

**The house.** Korvathos was born to the Volkemates house and raised in its tower and on its land on the march. He learned the spear from men of his father's household, Arzanalas among them.

**The march.** He has held the house for most of his life, sending its men to the _therkos_'s campaigns and its sons to the towers.

**The failing.** He is failing now, and his eldest son, who wants younger captains and a household drilled the capital's way, has begun to speak as the next master.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is shrewd, proud and practical. He keeps his word to the people who have kept theirs to him, and he judges the capital by what it does in Moktur, which is not much.

### Motivation

He wants the house to stand after him and the march to stay quiet in the meantime. He wants the old men of his household provided for, whatever his heir intends.

### Strengths

A sworn household, a tower in the city, long knowledge of the march and its raiders, and the loyalty of men who have served the house for decades.

### Weaknesses

His health, his heir, and dealings across the frontier that the capital would call treason.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-arznlsbrds|Arzanalas Baredas]]: The huscarl who has served the house for thirty-seven years. Korvathos keeps him out of gratitude and habit, and trusts him more than his son.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-provncmktr|The Province of Moktur]]**: a _gulmen_ of the propertied order, as a border lord.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The guest.** A party carrying a letter to Korvathos lodges in his tower as his guests and goes nowhere in the city without a man of his household beside them. He means it as courtesy, and it is also how he learns what they do.
2. **The ford.** A rival lord's new household captain, once Arzanalas's best pupil, has come back to Tirellan, and the two houses are about to quarrel over a ford. Korvathos wants a party to walk the ford's bounds and find the old marker stones.
3. **The heir.** Korvathos asks a party to carry a sealed letter to the _therkos_'s audience, naming the household's men he wants pensioned and how, before his son can name them himself.
4. **The raid.** A Varokh band comes over the march with the household's best men away, and Korvathos, too ill to ride, asks a party to ride with Arzanalas and the boys of his yard.

:::secret {title="The chief across the line"}
Korvathos has dealt with a Varokh chief across the line in ways the capital would call treason and Moktur calls sense. Arzanalas swore to the house, not to Orzara. He has seen enough to know, and someone from the capital has come to Tirellan to ask him what he has seen.

**The Letter Nobody Opened.** Korvathos is the border lord refused the command of Korras this spring, and at midsummer he comes for the gate with his household. The _therkos_ cares more that the gate is held than who holds it, and a lord who has taken it against a refusal has been confirmed afterward before.
:::
