# Appearance {#appearance}

Marendos Vaskelen is a 55-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of heavy build, broad in the chest and thick in the arm. He has tanned, weathered skin, dark brown hair cut short and a square beard going gray, and gray eyes narrowed from years of looking at water. Two fingers of his left hand are stiff and crooked from a rope burn. He dresses like a prosperous chandler, not a great merchant, in a good brown coat with a ledger-case at his belt, and he is on his own quays before dawn most days. His voice carries across a wharf without being raised.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Marendos Vaskelen is master of the [Vaskelen shipping house]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"}, the largest on the [[place-wharvesorz|Wharves]] of [[place-orzara|Orzara City]]. The house owns six warehouses on the quays below Nerval, a river fleet of some forty barges and lighters, and eleven sea hulls that trade from the mouth of the [[place-vylarisrvr|River Vilaris]] to the Bizarian ports and the Khelathi delta. Its counting-room keeps accounts with the Taruzelian, Nordman and Khelathi traders of the walled [Strangers' Row]{.name data-type="place/site"}, and most of what they land passes through a Vaskelen warehouse on its way to the [[place-grandmarketorz|Grand Market]].

The house is the capital's fastest grain. The barge strings of the Hilen houses come down the river at the river's pace and stop when it runs low; the Vaskelen sea hulls can bring grain from the delta to the river mouth in a fortnight, and his lighters can have it on the River Circle in four more days. In a lean month, his are the only sacks that can undercut the Grain House's.

## Background

**The river family.** The Vaskelen take their name from a landing upriver where the family kept a ferry and a few barges for generations. Marendos's father moved the house to the capital and bought its first warehouse. Marendos went to sea at fifteen as a supercargo, crossed to the delta eleven times and lost two fingers' use to a hawser in a storm off the Bizarian coast. He came ashore at thirty to run the house and built the sea fleet.

**The war.** The Taruzelian war closed the eastern sea to Vilérian hulls for a year and ruined half the shipping houses of the capital. Vaskelen survived it by carrying for the Bizarian League under Bizarian colors and by buying the ruined houses' warehouses cheap. He holds the citizenship by a Senate grant his father bought, and the old houses still call him a ferryman.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is blunt, practical and good-humored, and he dislikes the Heights and everyone on them. He trusts men who have been to sea and suspects men who have not. He keeps his word in trade and expects others to keep theirs, and he is slow to forgive a broken one.

### Motivation

He wants trade free: no duty on foreign grain, no duty on foreign cargo, nothing between his hulls and the Grand Market but the customs that already stand there. He wants the Khelathi contract, the right to carry the grain the Khelathi factor offers the throne from the delta to the River Circle, which would fill his sea fleet for a decade. And he wants the house to outlast him, which it will only if his son learns the sea before he learns the Heights.

### Strengths

He moves goods faster than anyone in the capital and knows every quay, lighterman and customs clerk on the River Circle. His warehouses hold a good part of the city's foreign stock at any time. The porters' and watermen's guilds trust him more than they trust most masters, because he pays on the day.

### Weaknesses

His sea fleet is his whole fortune, and it is insured with nobody, because no Vilérian house will insure a hull on the eastern sea and he will not pay Taruzelian rates. The old houses despise him and the Grain House wants him ruined. His trade runs through the Strangers' Row, and a riot that starts there would start in his warehouses.

## Social

### Patrons

- [[being-amrzvlds|Amarza Veldis]]: Senior partner of the House of Veldis, who banks the house's voyages and stands with it against a grain duty in the Council of the Danvori. He calls her the only person on the Heights worth a quarter-hour of his time.

### Enemies

- [[being-nthrsmrdvns|Nethoras Mardovanes]]: Master of the Grain House, who wants a duty that would close the Vaskelen grain trade overnight. The two men have spoken once, at a feast of the Imperial Cult, and did not finish the conversation.

### Others

- [[being-mthszrtml|Methisi Azeret'Mulu]]: The Khelathi factor on the Strangers' Row, who holds out the grain contract to him and has not yet given it.
- [[being-sthmrrdln|Sathemar Ardelen]]: The tribune of the stairs, who speaks for the porters of the River Circle, many of them veterans. Marendos pays them on the day and keeps the tribune's goodwill.

- **The Vaskelen shipping house**: master and sole owner.
- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|The Vilérian Empire]]**: a citizen by Senate grant, of the propertied order.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The delta convoy.** A Vaskelen hull is a week overdue from the delta with grain. Marendos hires a party to take a lighter down to the river mouth and learn what has happened to it.
2. **The warehouse fire.** Someone set a fire in a Vaskelen warehouse on the River Circle that the night porters put out. Marendos wants the man who set it, and he expects to be told it was the Grain House.
3. **The Bizarian colors.** A Bizarian shipmaster who carried for Vaskelen in the war is in the River Circle watch-house for a brawl. He sends word that he will say something Marendos would not like said unless Marendos gets him out before the magistrate sits.

:::secret {title="The Corsair cargo"}
Some of what the Vaskelen sea hulls carry was taken at sea. Since the war the house has bought cargoes in [[place-sazumun2|Sazumun]] from the warehouses of [[affiliation-hitusuni|House Hitusuni]], at a third under their worth: oil, cloth, Bizarian glass and once a hold of Vilérian arms, taken from merchant ships by captains of the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]] and sold on with fresh bills of lading. Some of those ships were Vilérian. The goods come up the river under the Vaskelen seal and pass the customs as the delta trade.

House Hitusuni keeps the original bills, and so the Taruzelians know. They have never threatened him. They have asked, twice, that a Vaskelen hull carry a sealed crate from Sazumun to the river mouth without opening it, and twice it has. Marendos does not know what was in them.

If the Seldan learns that the capital's fastest grain house deals in prizes taken from Vilérian ships, the house ends, and the Grain House has its duty by acclamation. The bills are the most valuable paper in Sazumun to Nethoras Mardovanes, and he does not know they exist.

**Hook: the third crate.** A third sealed crate waits in a Vaskelen warehouse on the River Circle for collection by a man who has not come. The warehouse-keeper, frightened, asks a party to open it.
:::
