# Appearance {#appearance}

Linthimas Orvelis is a 51-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. He has light skin, brown hair graying at the temples and cut in the capital's fashion, and hazel eyes. He wears the senator's stripe at his morning audience and at the tables of the Street of the Houses, where he is a frequent guest. He listens more than he speaks, and he is courteous to everyone who comes before him.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Linthimas Orvelis is the _therkos_ (governor) of the [[affiliation-provnchyln|Province of Hilen]], a _kurthos_ (senator) of an old house of the capital, appointed four years ago. He is the first _therkos_ in a generation with no estate in Hilen. He holds the province for the emperor from [[place-aravantia|Aravantara]], commanding its garrison, its revenue and its courts and answering to the Orzas alone.

He holds an audience every morning in the hall of the [Governor's House]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, and the province's business is brought to him there: petitions, appeals from the town magistrates' benches, and disputes between estates over water, boundaries and inheritance. He is courteous, careful and much entertained on the Street of the Houses, where the old landed families keep their townhouses. The houses agree among themselves what they will ask of him before they ask it. He suspects as much, and he is the one man in the city with no house to ask what was said.

## Background

**The outsider.** He was sent to Hilen from the capital four years ago, and he owns no land in the province he governs. Every man of standing who comes before him belongs to a house with an estate, a townhouse and a seat in the town's councils.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is courteous and careful, and he notices what a petition leaves out. He accepts every invitation the houses send him, and he keeps his own counsel at their tables.

### Motivation

He wants to govern the province he holds, and to know what is decided in it before it is brought to him as a petition.

### Strengths

He owes the houses nothing, and every house knows it. His office commands the garrison, the courts and the province's revenue, and no house can refuse him a seat at its table.

### Weaknesses

He has no house of his own in Hilen, no tenants, no stewards and no clients, and nobody in Aravantara tells him what is said on the Street of the Houses.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The audience.** Linthimas suspects that every petition he hears has been agreed before it reaches him. He wants a party with no house behind it to dine on the Street of the Houses and tell him what is said at table.
2. **The supper.** A party bringing gifts to the Ganothandis townhouse at supper finds the _therkos_ among the guests. If they carry a word for him or listen well at table, he asks them to come back.

:::secret {title="The oath he has not heard"}
The houses of Hilen have sworn to close the Yards. Two springs ago the masters of the great houses met by night at the Speakers' Stone and swore before the _zalvos_ of the province that when the old Orzas dies, no grain of Hilen is to be weighed at Aravantara for any claimant who brings a legion inside the capital's boundary. The masters call it the Compact of the Stone, and the list of those who swore is kept by the _zalvos_ in a box under the Orzas's image in the provincial temple.

The _therkos_ does not know. The morning last harvest when every yard clerk refused the [[affiliation-mardovanes|Mardovanes]] wagons until noon was the houses' trial of the clerks, and he heard of it as a joke at the factor's expense. The _vandraks_ (legion commander) of the province knows, because his legion is fed from the same granaries, and he has told the masters that he means to keep his legion in its camp. He has told the _therkos_ nothing.

A master's toast to "the Stone" at a supper on the Street of the Houses makes the table go quiet. A party that tells Linthimas what the toast means gives him the one fact his audience has been agreed to keep from him.
:::
