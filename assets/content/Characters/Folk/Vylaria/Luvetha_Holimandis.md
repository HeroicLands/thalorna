# Appearance {#appearance}

Luvetha Holimandis was a woman of 23 when she died, 5'8" tall and of medium build, with light, smooth skin, dark brown hair and hazel eyes. Those who knew her before her illness remember a tall young woman who laughed easily. The sickness that killed her wasted her slowly, and the people who sat with her at the end remember her thin and grey.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Luvetha Holimandis was the younger sister of the alchemist [[being-dmrthsvshrds|Domrithas Vishardas]], of a family of minor nobility in [[place-nartum|Nartun]] with a modest estate. She caught the [Withering]{.name data-type="lore"}, an incurable sickness that slowly turns its victims into husks, and she died of it fifteen years ago. Her brother tried every remedy he knew and many that his guild forbade, and failed. Her death is the wound his whole work since has grown from.

She matters now for two reasons: because her brother does what he does in her name, and because someone claiming to be Luvetha may come to Nartun, alive and unchanged, asking to speak with him.

## Background

**The family.** Luvetha grew up on her family's modest estate with her elder brother, who was already showing his gift for chemical transformation, and the two were close.

**The illness.** When she fell ill, Domrithas gave up the ordinary life ahead of him and threw himself into finding a cure. He read texts the guild called forbidden and made substances its doctrine said could not work.

**The death.** She died despite him, in the summer of 704. In her last moments she seemed to recognize something in his work, as if he were close to a terrible truth. He has never stopped trying to reach it.

## Psyche

### Personality

She was warm, quick and devoted to her brother, and the people who remember her remember her laughter.

### Motivation

In life she wanted to live, and she wanted her brother not to ruin himself trying to save her.

### Strengths

She had her brother's whole heart. The memory of her is the one thing that still calls up his old warmth and his horror at what he has become.

### Weaknesses

She was dying, and she was dependent on a brother whose remedies grew stranger as she weakened.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The day of her death.** Each summer, on the day she died, Domrithas leaves his work and spends the day at her grave. A party that needs the alchemist outside his laboratory has that one day to find him.
2. **The portrait.** A portrait of Luvetha is stolen from her brother's house. He pays a party well to recover it and will not say why it matters so much.
3. **The woman at the gate.** A woman who calls herself Luvetha Holimandis comes up the holy road and asks at the gate of Nartun for her brother. She looks no older than the girl who died fifteen years ago.

:::secret {title="The Sister's Return"}
Domrithas's experiments did not fail. Luvetha did not die as her brother and the city believe. She has been kept in hiding all these years. She claims that she cannot die now. She has watched what her brother has done in her name, the experiments on the unwilling and the deaths that followed, and she comes to Nartun with evidence of them to demand that he stop, and to stop him herself if he will not.

**Hook: the woman at the gate.** The woman of the third hook is Luvetha. A party she hires to bring her to her brother is the first to learn that his sister is alive, and the first he has reason to silence.
:::
