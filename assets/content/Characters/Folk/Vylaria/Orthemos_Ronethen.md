# Appearance {#appearance}

Orthemos Ronethen is 37 years old. He has dark eyes, tightly curled black hair and broad hands burnished by oil and worn at the nails. He wears a short work coat with a repaired shoulder. Before tasting oil he wipes his cup, then holds it where the light reaches it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Orthemos supervises a pressing crew at [[place-olivenna|Olivenna]], where five families control the presses and the town’s livelihood. He organizes the fruit arriving at the yard, the crew’s turns and the cleaned vessels that receive the oil. He can tell a buyer what happened at his press; he cannot answer for a stamp placed on an amphora’s clay stopper after it leaves.

He wants another season’s work for the crew without making their name the answer to every false stamp found in the capital. He asks buyers to keep the delivery slips as well as the stamped clay stopper, because a good stamp can travel much farther than the oil it first describes.

## Background

**The groves.** He begins by carrying fruit from the surrounding holdings. A late wagon teaches him how quickly the value of a crop can change before a press even touches it.

**The press.** He learns the pressing from older workers and gains charge of a crew by keeping its vessels clean and its deliveries distinct. He remains a hired man in a town governed by owners.

**The marks.** Reports of false Olivenna stamps make the crew’s ordinary work political. He starts keeping a separate list of the wagons he actually sees loaded.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is deliberate, sociable over a meal, and stubborn about cleaning work that cannot be inspected once an amphora is sealed.

### Motivation

He wants buyers to pay for the crew’s actual work and wants the crew paid even when an owner disputes a shipment.

### Strengths

He knows the pressing sequence, the workers’ hands and the difference between a delivery he witnesses and a name repeated on a slip.

### Weaknesses

The press families can replace him. His separate record has no official authority, and his insistence on making one annoys men who otherwise value his work.

## Social

The crew trusts him to count its days accurately. He deals with the presses’ stewards as an employee, not a partner, and avoids promising favors on their behalf.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: a free resident under imperial law without the citizenship.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The false stamp.** A buyer brings back an amphora with an Olivenna-stamped clay stopper and bad oil. Orthemos hires a party to trace its wagon against his loading list before the crew loses its pay.
2. **The tenant’s lease.** A grove tenant loses his tenancy after his press refuses his olives and he takes them to another press. Orthemos asks the party to bring the tenant’s account to a notary without passing it through the press steward.
3. **The capital’s visitor.** A stranger from the capital asks about a well-kept older worker. Orthemos wants to know whether the inquiry concerns wages, debt or a warrant before answering questions about anyone on his crew.
