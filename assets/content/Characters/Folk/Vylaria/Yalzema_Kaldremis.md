# Appearance {#appearance}

Yalzema Kaldremis is 50 years old. She has brown eyes, wind-reddened cheeks and black hair streaked with gray, tied clear of her face. Her robe is shortened for the shrine steps. She watches the horizon while speaking and cups a small offering against the wind until it reaches the altar.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Yalzema keeps the shrine of the [[affiliation-brazemas|Faith of Brazemas]] at [[place-kurzalora|Kurzalora]], with a dozen acolytes. A sailor’s daughter, she reads storms for the Kastamar fleet before receiving the shrine. At the Thunderhead Vigil she watches from the tower and reads the first lightning for the sailing year.

She wants the height kept under the shrine’s authority. The admiralty asks three times in five years to use the Vigil Tower as a signal station, and she refuses each request. Marines and a surveyor now carry a letter of leave attributed to the governor; she doubts that he signs it. The shrine’s ancient right of refuge makes control of the height more than a question of where a signal mast stands.

## Background

**The sailor’s household.** She learns the coast’s weather as a matter of who comes home, then enters the storm-god’s service.

**The fleet.** Reading weather for Kastamar teaches her the difference between a warning and an order an officer wishes to hear. She keeps the habit of saying what she sees.

**The height.** At Kurzalora she receives pilgrims, keeps their vows and protects the shrine’s grant of a year and a day to those who enter the god’s service.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is plain-spoken, hospitable at the altar and immovable when a visitor treats a request as permission already granted.

### Motivation

She wants the shrine’s duties and refuge preserved without a military presence taking control of its doors.

### Strengths

She knows the fleet, the coast and the shrine’s rights. Her acolytes work together and the pilgrims know where to find her.

### Weaknesses

A refusal that protects the shrine also raises the admiralty’s suspicion. She cannot match a governor’s armed order with soldiers of her own.

## Social

The acolytes answer to her on the height; the town magistrates govern the street below. Lodging-house keepers depend on pilgrims and watch the approach of uniformed visitors closely.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: a free resident under imperial law without the citizenship.
- **[[affiliation-brazemas|Faith of Brazemas]]**: a Bravsar, the Priest of Storms keeping Kurzalora’s shrine.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The letter of leave.** Yalzema asks a party to establish whether the governor authorizes the survey before the marines begin measuring the tower.
2. **The unnamed plank.** A ship’s carved plank arrives without a house’s name. She needs its loss identified before burning it with the proper offering.
3. **The silver vow.** A captain promises a silver ship in a gale and sends tin. His crew refuses to sail. Yalzema asks the party to obtain his answer in person rather than substitute another offering for him.

:::secret {title="The road out of the fleet"}
For nine years Yalzema lets deserters who vow themselves to Brazemas leave within a month rather than stay a year. They go down the landward slope by night and reach a [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] lodging-house that supplies new names and merchant berths. Shipping houses pay the shrine a gift for each man. She begins after the fleet hangs two deserters and believes an unpaid fleet has no right to keep them. The acolytes and lower lodging-houses know; the admiralty only knows that too many men enter the refuge and too few emerge at the expected time.
:::
