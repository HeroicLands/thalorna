# Appearance {#appearance}

Tholmarar Orthemandis is a 54-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. She has light skin, browned by the sun, black hair worn in a severe knot, and amber eyes. She wears the dark robe of a _marendos_ (master) of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] without ornament, and she sits very still while others talk. Her voice is low and precise.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tholmarar Orthemandis is a _marendos_ of the Thordimalon in the Zulen (fellowship) [[affiliation-fisera|Fisera]], the fellowship of earth and substance, and she chairs its examination board, which decides which aspirants are ready to take the oath. Her influence in the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]] has grown steadily, and the examination board is the seat from which she uses it.

Thirteen years ago [[being-azatodmzns|Azato Damzanis]], a fellow _marendos_ of the Fisera, took apart her theoretical framework on mineral essences in a published response. She has not forgiven him. She has begun careful inquiries into his methods, through the channels her office gives her.

She keeps rooms at the [[place-aulpnpstmm|Court of the Academy]], the Ombalon in [[place-arvon|Inner City]], where she is available when the senior scholars need an account of the Fisera's examinations or a judgment on a disputed mineral specimen. The board's teaching and examinations remain outside the Inner Wall in the [[place-scholarsdstr|Scholars' District]]. Residence at the court gives her access to requests from great houses; it gives her no place on the Order's governing Verdan.

## Background

**The fellowship.** Tholmarar trained in the Fisera and rose to _marendos_ on the strength of her theory of mineral essences.

**The response.** Azato's published response dismantled the framework point by point. It was read across the Order.

**The board.** She has rebuilt her standing by work and by office. As chair of the examination board she sees every aspirant and every master's report on them, and she remembers what she reads.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is formal, exact and cold to people she does not respect. She forgives a student's error easily and a colleague's contempt never.

### Motivation

She wants to be proven right about mineral essences, and failing that, she wants Azato proven wrong about something that matters.

### Strengths

Her office puts the fellowship's aspirants and their masters' reports in her hands. She is patient, and she uses the Order's own rules.

### Weaknesses

Her grudge is known, so anything she brings against Azato is read as a grudge first.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-azatodmzns|Azato Damzanis]]: The _marendos_ who demolished her framework in print. She is watching his work, and he knows she is.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]**: a _marendos_, with a vote in her chapter's council.
- **[[affiliation-fisera|Fisera]]**: chair of the fellowship's examination board, the Warden of the Threshold, the last check before an aspirant's oath.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The examination.** An apprentice of Azato Damzanis comes before Tholmarar's board, and she fails him on a point of mineral theory that is Azato's, not his. The apprentice's family hires a party to learn whether the failure was fair.
2. **The reader.** Tholmarar hires a party to find and buy every copy of Azato's published response that is held outside the Order, and she does not say what she means to do with them.
3. **The lecture.** She wants a party at a public lecture Azato gives, to write down every claim he makes about mineral essence, word for word.
4. **The aspirant.** A gifted aspirant who has never been enrolled comes before her board under a false name. Tholmarar wants to know who taught him before she decides whether to report him.

5. **The court's specimen.** A house asks the Ombalon whether a mineral offered as security is what its seller claims. Tholmarar needs a party to obtain a second specimen from the named source, with a record of who handles it, before she gives an opinion.

:::secret {title="What she suspects about Azato"}
Tholmarar suspects that some of Azato's research is done outside the Order's sanction, and she is right. He keeps a second name, Kuthstan Crey, for that work. She does not know the name. Her inquiries so far have gone through the examination board's records and the reports of Azato's apprentices, and they have found the gaps in his record where the unsanctioned work goes, but not the work itself.

**Hook: the gap.** Tholmarar shows a party a year in which Azato's laboratory book records nothing, and asks them to find out where he was. The answer takes them to his rented estate outside [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]], and to the contacts who know him only as Kuthstan Crey.
:::
