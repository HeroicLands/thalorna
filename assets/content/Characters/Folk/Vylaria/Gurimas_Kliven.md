# Appearance {#appearance}

Gurimas Kliven is 27 years old. She is a slim woman with brown eyes, straight black hair and a small white scar across one knuckle from a broken bottle. Her coat is good cloth without a courtier’s trimming. She checks the bung of a sample cask herself before asking a buyer to taste it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Gurimas belongs to the Kliven wine family of [[place-vindara|Vindara]]. Her future is entangled with the terrace boundary disputed by the Kliven and Ronalen households for eleven years. A steward is already dead in that quarrel, and four provincial hearings have settled nothing. Gurimas wants the houses to sell wine instead of spending another harvest on their claims.

Her family expects a betrothal to a shipping family of [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] this autumn. She attends the discussions and asks closely about cargo space, storage and the buyer’s guarantees. She gives no public explanation for her reluctance to let anyone fix the ceremony’s day.

## Background

**The terraces.** She grew up with a boundary quarrel treated as household history. She learned the grapes and cellar work from people who must share roads with the supposed enemy.

**The trade.** Dealings with Delalara brought her beyond the town’s inherited argument. She learned that a reliable arrival can matter more to a shipping house than the honor a vintner claims for a disputed strip.

**The proposed match.** Her family now connects her marriage to its next trading agreement. She tries to keep both discussions open without making a promise she cannot keep.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is personable, cautious with commitments and quick to change a quarrel into a question about the work waiting outside.

### Motivation

She wants a life and a business whose terms she chooses, while keeping the workers dependent on her family’s vineyards employed.

### Strengths

She understands the trade beyond Vindara and can speak to both work crews and shipping agents without treating either as an audience.

### Weaknesses

Postponement is her usual answer to a decision that will hurt someone. The more time she gains, the more other people commit on her behalf.

## Social

The Kliven household treats her as the future of its trade. Its dispute with the Ronalen household makes even an ordinary meeting across the terrace road a subject for questioning.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: of the propertied order.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The disputed strip.** Gurimas hires a party to obtain copies of the four provincial hearings before the families spend another season presenting the same arguments.
2. **The promised cargo.** The Delalara family asks for a guarantee of autumn deliveries before settling the betrothal. Gurimas wants the party to inspect the proposed storage and report to her directly.
3. **The false brand.** Wine offered under Vindara’s brand tastes nothing like the stated cellar’s crop. Gurimas asks the party to follow the casks before an accusation ruins a genuine shipment.
