# Appearance {#appearance}

Ganothis Dorandates is a 58-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of heavy build. He has tanned skin, dark brown hair going gray at the temples, and green eyes in a broad, lined face. He walks with the slow roll of a man whose knees have done too many stairs, carries his cudgel as if he has forgotten it is there, and greets half the shopkeepers of the central district by name. His voice is low and unhurried, and he seldom raises it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ganothis Dorandates is the senior guard captain of the province's urban cohort quartered in the watch-house below the Governor's House in [[place-alyssar|Alessar]], the seat of [[affiliation-provinclys|the Province of Alessa]]. He commands the patrols of the central district, the richest and most crowded quarter of the city, where merchants, minor nobles and prosperous tradespeople live beside their servants and the city's poor. He is near the end of his service, and he has taken the young guard [[being-xenoksbrds|Zénokos Bardas]] under his wing as an unofficial mentor.

He keeps the peace by knowing the district. He knows which shop pays whom, which noble house has friends on the square, and which quarrels can be settled with a word on the doorstep.

## Background

**The legions.** Ganothis served his twenty years in the legions and came out a citizen, with the dole and the habit of command.

**The cohort.** He took service in the urban cohort at Alessar after his discharge and has walked the central district for most of his life since, rising from patrol to sergeant to captain.

**The last years.** He is close to retiring. He has watched the guard grow more cynical as the empire's coin grows thinner, and he has kept Zénokos from the guards who would teach him to take bribes.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is patient, dry and kind to the young, and he has long since made his peace with what he cannot change. He knows where the lines are drawn in his district, and he does not cross them.

### Motivation

He wants to finish his service in good order, keep his district quiet, and leave behind at least one guard who is honest.

### Strengths

He knows the central district and its people better than anyone on the square. His men trust him, and the shopkeepers would rather deal with him than with any other officer.

### Weaknesses

He is tired, and he has learned to bend where the district's powerful families lean. He tells Zénokos when a crime is not worth the station's trouble, and he is usually right about the trouble.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-xenoksbrds|Zénokos Bardas]]: The young guard he mentors and protects, whose sincerity reminds him of what he was.
- [[being-zurmelosorth|Zurmelos Orthemandis]]: The new senior officer of the station, whose heavy hand undoes the quiet Ganothis has spent years keeping.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-provinclys|The Province of Alessa]]**: a citizen by his twenty years, and a captain of the province's urban cohort.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The petitioners' inns.** At assize time Ganothis's men walk the inns of Petitioners' Row at night, and a petitioner has been robbed of the papers for his hearing. Ganothis asks a party to find them before the case is called.
2. **The broken bridge.** A carter swears that the north road's levy has been collected twice this year. Ganothis wants to know whether to believe him before he takes it to anyone on the square.
3. **The young guard.** Ganothis asks a party to keep an eye on Zénokos, who has been asking questions about a spice importer of the central district, and to warn the boy off before someone else does it harder.

:::secret {title="The quiet district"}
Ganothis knows that a guard of Zénokos's squad has taken money from a spice importer of the central district for years, and he knows that the guard is too well connected for the station to touch. When Zénokos brings him the corruption or a noble's crime, Ganothis tells him to let it go, because the station would pay for the arrest, and he has never told the boy how many such things he has let go himself.
:::
