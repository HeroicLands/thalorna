# Appearance {#appearance}

Kalmethar Tharkis is a 79-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build, thinner than his clothes were cut for. He has tanned, weathered and deeply lined skin, thin white hair and pale blue eyes that are sharper than the rest of him. His right hand shakes, and a small cup of some dark tincture is always within his reach. He dresses richly and warmly, in furs even in summer, and he asks a visitor's age before he asks his business.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Baron Kalmethar Tharkis is a lay baron of the [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]], wealthy and powerful, who holds forest land with its hall, court and bondsmen for rent and service and keeps a house in the holy city of [[place-nartum|Nartun]]. He is old and he is afraid of dying. He is the patron of the alchemist [[being-dmrthsvshrds|Domrithas Vishardas]]: he pays for his work, keeps him from the scrutiny of the [Alchemist Guild of Nartun]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"}, and sees in that work his best hope of a longer life or of no death at all.

## Background

**The baron.** Kalmethar inherited his land as barons of Nartun do, and he has held it a long time and grown rich on its forest.

**The fear.** As his friends died around him, he came to fear his own death more than anything the temples promise after it. He went looking for a remedy, and he found Domrithas, who wanted the same thing for his own reasons.

**The patron.** He has kept the alchemist for years, and the guild has not touched him while the baron's name stands between them.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is shrewd, impatient and frightened, and he hides the fear behind a lord's manners. He measures every person and every thing by whether it can lengthen his life.

### Motivation

He wants not to die. Each winter makes him want it more urgently, and his patience with the alchemist grows shorter each time he coughs.

### Strengths

He is rich, he has a baron's standing, and he holds an estate whose people cannot leave it without his leave. His protection is real.

### Weaknesses

His fear rules him. Anyone who promises him time can lead him, and a man who frightens him further can drive him to demands nobody can meet.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-zetimakholsa|Zetima Kolsamis]]: The keeper of the temple of [[affiliation-ardavon|Árdavon]], who calls his alchemist's work heresy and would bring it before an examiner. His protection is the wall she has to get past.

### Others

- [[being-dmrthsvshrds|Domrithas Vishardas]]: The alchemist he keeps, whose work is the only thing he truly cares about.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]]**: a baron, holding forest land for rent and service.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The deadline.** Kalmethar has given Domrithas a date by which he wants a finished remedy, and the alchemist is hiring people to fetch what he needs faster than the guild would allow. A party hired for an errand finds the baron's men watching them.
2. **The guild's evidence.** The guild-master of the alchemists has gathered evidence against Domrithas and needs it carried past the baron's people to the temple quarter. Kalmethar's steward offers the party more to lose it on the road.
3. **The dying stand.** The Withering has reached a stand on the baron's land, and the prior of the temple he holds of orders a penance and raises the dues for the year. Kalmethar cannot bear to watch anything die on his land, and he hires a party to learn why the trees are dying before the prior's penance costs him another season's rent.

:::secret {title="The baron's subjects"}
Kalmethar gives Domrithas more than coin and protection. He gives him test subjects from the bondsmen of his estate, who cannot leave the land, marry or sell without his leave and cannot refuse him. The baron has never asked what becomes of them.

His patience is nearly gone. He means to have a finished transformation within a time no alchemist could meet, or to withdraw his protection and let the guild and the temple have the man. Whatever Domrithas gives him at the end of it is likely to fail, or to change the baron into something nobody can control.

**Hook: the missing bondsman.** A bondsman's family on the estate asks a party to learn where their son went when he was taken up to the baron's house in Nartun. A bondsman cannot leave without his lord's leave, and nobody has seen him since.
:::
