# Appearance {#appearance}

Talothar Zenothenos is a 43-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of heavy build. He has light skin weathered by the harbor wind, black hair cut short and dark brown eyes that keep moving to the water while he talks. He wears a plain dark coat with the town's badge on a cord at his chest and good boots wet to the ankle. He stands in the stern of a harbor boat as if it were a floor, and his voice is pitched to carry from one end of a crowded berth to the other.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Talothar Zenothenos is the harbor-master of [[place-mercavia|Delalara]], the busiest harbor left to the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|empire]]. The town's council of shipping houses appoints him and his pilots, and he keeps the harbor for it: every ship that enters inside the Long Mole takes one of his harbor boats to the berth he gives her, pays the town's berth-fees, and answers to him when her crew fights another. A fight between two crews costs both ships a fine, and he collects it before either sails. The customs on the quay are not his business. They belong to the tax-farmer's clerks in the arcade of the Customs House, and Talothar keeps clear of them.

He knows every hull at the quays, her master, her owner and what she is short of. He passes word of incoming ships that need hands to [[being-brnlnsbrkls|Branalenis Barkalas]], and now and then he steps between a less scrupulous captain and the crew he means to cheat.

## Background

**The harbor boats.** Talothar grew up on Delalara's waterfront and learned the harbor from its boats, sculling ships to their berths through the crowded water inside the mole. He came to know the berths, the depths and the masters better than anyone on the quays.

**The pilots.** The town took him on as one of the harbor-master's pilots, bringing ships in past the Mole Light in every weather of the sailing year.

**The harbor.** The council made him harbor-master, and he has kept the berths since. He has kept them honestly, which some of the houses that appointed him find inconvenient.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is blunt, busy and fair. He decides a quarrel on the quay as quickly as he can hear both masters out, and he does not change his mind for a house's name. He likes a good seaman and has little patience for a bad captain.

### Motivation

He wants the harbor to run: berths given in order, fines paid, crews paid what they signed for. He wants a good sailor to find a good ship.

### Strengths

He knows the harbor, its berths and its masters better than anyone in the city, and his harbor boats see every ship that comes in. The council's appointment gives him authority on the water that no single house can override.

### Weaknesses

He holds his post at the council's pleasure, and the council is the shipping houses. A berth given in order, rather than to the house that asks for it, has made him enemies among the merchants he serves.

## Social

### Enemies

- **Certain merchants of the houses**: Traders who see the harbor-master as an obstacle to their business, and who work quietly to undermine his authority. His friends on the quays, Branalenis among them, have become targets of the same quiet campaign.

### Others

- [[being-brnlnsbrkls|Branalenis Barkalas]]: A seaman of rare skill with no ship of her own, to whom he passes word of incoming vessels seeking crew.
- [[being-solterosbraz|Solteros Brazemen]]: A merchant captain of the city, one of the masters who comes looking for Branalenis when he has a voyage to crew.
- [[being-nebralenelth|Nebralen Elthezis]]: Master of the _[Tempest's Mercy]{.name data-type="vehicle"}_, a captain of the city who pays his berth-fees on time.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-provincvld|Province of Salkalon]]**: a palvethos (resident without the citizenship) of the province, holding the town's office of harbor-master by the council's appointment.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The crew short of pay.** A captain has paid off his crew at half what they signed for, and the crew want the harbor-master's fine laid on the ship before she sails at the morning tide. Talothar needs witnesses to what was signed, and the captain's house needs them gone.
2. **The ship that was lost.** A Delalara ship reported lost off the [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]] has been seen in [[place-qudumel2|Kudumel]] repainted. Talothar keeps the harbor's book of every hull that has berthed at Delalara, with her marks, and he will show a party the page if they bring him word of her.
3. **The hidden dock.** A sailor's child has gone missing from the waterfront, and her mother has come to Talothar, because the slaver [Devorin]{.name data-type="being/npc"} works from a dock somewhere on the mile of quays. Talothar knows every berth the town lets, and Devorin's dock is not one of them. He wants it found, and he cannot search a house's private wharf without the council's leave.
4. **The obstacle.** A shipping house wants a berth that Talothar has given to another ship in her turn. A party hired by the house is asked to find something in the harbor-master's accounts that the council would care about.

:::secret {title="The berths of the Eastern Quarter"}
Talothar's harbor boats see every cargo cross the quays, and he has noticed what [Ornetha Venthelis]{.name data-type="being/npc"} has noticed: the tax-farmer's clerks never open the goods of the Eastern Quarter. He does not know why, and he has told no one. The customs are the farmer's business and the treasury's, he holds his post at the council's pleasure, and the houses that sit on the council trade with the Taruzelian factors every day.

The merchants working against him are not one party. Some want berths out of turn, and some want a harbor-master who does not keep a book of every hull's marks, because a ship that is lost and comes back under another name has to come back to some harbor.
:::
