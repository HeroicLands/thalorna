# Appearance {#appearance}

Venthelen Kaldremis was a woman of 5'9" and heavy build, and she was 59 when she died. She had olive skin weathered by forty years at sea, dark brown hair gone grey at the temples and kept in a short plait, and grey eyes that missed nothing aboard her ship. She dressed like her crew and was louder than any of them. In her last year the wasting illness thinned her, and she was seen on the quays of Delalara in a coat too big for her.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Venthelen Kaldremis was captain of the merchant ship _[Seaflower]{.name data-type="vehicle"}_ out of [[place-mercavia|Delalara]], a gruff but fair-minded master who ran a hard ship and an honest one. She is remembered on the waterfront chiefly as the captain who made [[being-brnlnsbrkls|Branalenis Barkalas]], whom she took aboard as a six-year-old from the docks and trained in every part of seamanship. She died three years ago, not at sea but of a wasting illness in Delalara, and Branalenis has drifted from ship to ship since.

## Background

**The Seaflower.** Venthelen commanded the _Seaflower_ in the trade of Delalara for many years, in a merchant fleet whose ranks were held by men and whose hierarchy disliked change.

**The girl from the docks.** She saw in a six-year-old girl of the waterfront a rare mix of fearlessness, quick wit and strength, and took her aboard. She started Branalenis as a deckhand on the meanest work, then trained her in rigging, navigation and the handling of cargo, until by sixteen the girl was the ship's bosun in all but name.

**The informal rank.** Venthelen never gave Branalenis a formal rank. A young woman of common birth promoted openly would have set the fleet against her, so Venthelen kept her advancement informal and unofficial, and so less threatening to the men who held rank.

**The illness.** The wasting illness took her ashore in Delalara, and she died there in 716.

## Psyche

### Personality

She was gruff, exacting and fair, with a short temper and a long memory for good work. She taught by making a sailor do the job again until it was right.

### Motivation

She wanted a good ship and a crew fit to sail her, and in her later years she wanted Branalenis to have the career the fleet would not give her openly.

### Strengths

Her seamanship and her judgment of people were both first-rate, and she knew how to work inside a hierarchy without fighting it head-on.

### Weaknesses

She protected Branalenis by keeping her advancement quiet, and so left her, at her death, with a great reputation and no rank.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The captain's grave.** Branalenis has never visited Venthelen's grave in Delalara. A party that sails with her and puts in at the city may be the reason she finally does, or the witnesses when she refuses.
2. **The old crew.** The men who sailed the _Seaflower_ under Venthelen are scattered through the ships of Delalara. A party looking for an honest crew in the city can find several of them, and each has a story of the captain and of the girl she trained.
3. **The sealed letter.** An elderly sailor who says he knew Venthelen asks, on his deathbed, that a sealed letter be given to whoever succeeds in captaining a ship of true honor, and dies before saying more. A party that carries it must decide who that is.

:::secret {title="What she did not say"}
Venthelen meant Branalenis to command a ship of her own, and believed the fleet would accept it in time if the girl's rank came quietly, a step at a time, under a captain the houses respected. She never told Branalenis so. When she fell ill there was no time left for the quiet way, and no paper of hers names Branalenis for anything.
:::
