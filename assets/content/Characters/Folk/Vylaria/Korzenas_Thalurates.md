# Appearance {#appearance}

Korzenas Thalurates is a 63-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. He has fair skin gone pale from a lifetime indoors, silver hair brushed flat and green eyes that are always reading something, and he carries his shoulders in the slight stoop of a man who spent his youth at a clerk's desk. He dresses as richly as any _kurthos_ (senator) and wears it awkwardly, as if he has borrowed the clothes. On his left forefinger is the house's cypher, a seal-ring on an open hand. He is soft-spoken, exact and pleasant, and he never forgets a figure anyone tells him.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Korzenas Thalurates is master of [[affiliation-thalurates|House Thalurates]], the _pelerenos_ (Elevated) house raised from the clerks of the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]] within living memory. The house's palace stands in Palor, the Palace District of [[place-arvon|Arvon]], among the embassies, behind bronze gates and a livery with no metal on it. Its money comes from service, and lately from the debts of the old houses. The house's factors buy notes, mortgages and the paper of failing estates in their own names, so that the house's name appears on none of them, and the debtors learn who holds them when the house needs a vote.

The old houses despise him and owe him. A good part of the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] votes as the master of House Thalurates wishes on any question that matters to him, and the house's own vote is the least of them.

## Background

**The clerk.** His grandfather, a freedman's son, rose in the Offices to keep the imperial seal, and the Orzas he served raised his line and gave it the name Thalurates. Korzenas entered the Offices at fourteen as a _thilartos_ (clerk) under his father, rose to _usos_ (prefect) of the Levies desk and held it for eleven years. He learned which records matter, what a house will pay to have one found, and what it will pay more to have one lost.

**The broker.** He left the Levies desk at forty-five when his father died, and turned the house's service fortune to the purchase of debts. Since then the house has bought the paper of a dozen old houses, and each debtor has learned who held his note on the morning the house needed his vote.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is patient, polite and humorless about money. He takes no pleasure in humiliating the old houses and knows that they think he does. He is a devoted father and an exacting master, and he reads the household accounts himself every evening, as he read the Levies desk's.

### Motivation

He wants _ankren_ (Silver) standing for the house, which takes three generations of service, a substantial fee and the seal of the Dukran. He wants his heir married into a founding house and a daughter married to the next Orzas. He wants the Keeper of the Seal's post to stay in the Offices he knows when the new reign appoints a new Keeper. And he wants the throne in his debt, as the old houses are.

### Strengths

He holds the debts of houses that hold the Senate's votes. The Offices are full of his family's clerks, ushers and factors, and very little is sealed in Arvon without someone of the house knowing it. He is unembarrassable, because the old houses have already said every unkind thing about him that there is to say.

### Weaknesses

He has no blood, no masks and no friends among the old houses, and every one of them would celebrate his ruin. His power lies in debts that are worth only what the debtors fear, and a house he presses too hard may decide it prefers ruin to obedience.

## Social

### Patrons

- [[being-zrmlshlrkls|Zarmelas Helarkales]]: The Orzas, who finds the house useful: it delivers votes the throne needs, and the throne has sold it the standing it has. Korzenas does not mistake usefulness for favor.

### Enemies

- [[affiliation-varkalenis|House Varkalenis]]: Its master will not dine with him.
- [[being-amrzvlds|Amarza Veldis]]: Senior partner of the House of Veldis, the old money that lends to the throne and regards Thalurates as a clerk with ambitions.
- [[being-mthszrtml|Methisi Azeret'Mulu]]: The Khelathi factor, who has begun buying the same houses' debts with Khelathi silver, at better prices.
- [[being-drvnsrlln|Dorvanis Arelliani Septarius]]: The Imperial _Tarkelos_, who has refused every gift the house has sent him and keeps the Tarmeks's books closed to its clerks.

### Family

- [[being-iltherethalu|Ilthere Thalurates]]: His daughter, the house's face at court.

His heir, a son of thirty-four, holds a desk in the Offices and is not yet married, because Korzenas is waiting to learn which founding house will need to sell him a bride.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-thalurates|House Thalurates]]**: master of the house, holding its three masks and its heir's naming.
- **[[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]]**: an _usos_ (prefect) on the roll by long service, though he holds no desk.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The address.** A minor kingdom's envoy arrives at the Thalurates gate believing it is a shared embassy compound, and the house's steward lets him in. His legation hires a party to learn what he signed before anyone told him where he was.
2. **The morning of the vote.** A _kurthos_ of a founding house learns on the morning of a vote that Thalurates holds his note. He hires a party to find the factor who bought it and learn what else the factor has bought.
3. **The seal's successor.** Korzenas pays a party to learn which freedmen each claimant's household has promised the Keeper's seal, and how much each has paid for the promise.

:::secret {title="The house's first fortune"}
The house's first fortune was made by losing a record. Korzenas's grandfather, as Keeper of the Seal, kept the forfeiture rolls of the proscription board. When a senatorial house was proscribed in his time, he lost the roll that listed three of its estates, so that the treasury never claimed them. A Thalurates factor bought them a year later from the proscribed house's widow for a tenth of their value. They are still the house's richest lands. In law they are forfeit to the throne, and have been for sixty years.

The roll is not lost. A fair copy lies in the archive of the [[affiliation-imprlscrblgld|Imperial Scribal Guild]], filed under the wrong year by a scribe who was paid to misfile it and not to burn it. Korzenas knows it exists and has never found it. Any Orzas who found it could take the estates back by proscription without a vote.

He is also buying the throne's paper. Through four factors and two Helionite houses he has bought up the Veldis loans that smaller houses took a share in, and he now holds about a tenth of the throne's debt without the throne knowing whose it is. He means to offer the next rollover himself, at a lower rate than the House of Veldis asks and without the Veldis riders, and to replace Amarza Veldis as the throne's banker in a single quarter. He needs the _Tarkelos_ to accept the offer, and the _Tarkelos_ will not take his gifts.

The house's factors also hold two debts that would ruin two houses: a note against the temple stipends of [[affiliation-varkalenis|House Varkalenis]], to be forgiven when the Varkalenis heir marries a Thalurates daughter, and the creditors' sale of [[affiliation-zarkedis|House Zarkedis]]. A Thalurates factor bought the Zarkedis house in [[place-skoraz|Soraks]], and the Zarkedis ancestor masks are still in its cupboard. Ilthere means them to be the price she names to Kalestratos Zarkedis.

**Hook: the Zarkedis masks.** A party is hired by a cousin of the fallen House Zarkedis to steal the house's ancestor masks from the cupboard of the Thalurates house in Soraks.

**Hook: the misfiled year.** Korzenas hires a party to search the Scribal Guild's archive for a forfeiture roll he describes only as "a list of three estates". He does not say whose they are.
:::
