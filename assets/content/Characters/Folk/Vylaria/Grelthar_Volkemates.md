# Appearance {#appearance}

Grelthar Volkemates is 54 years old. He is a long-limbed man with iron-gray hair, hazel eyes and a weathered face. His coat is mended at the cuffs and his boots are kept dry before any ceremonial gear is cleaned. He listens to a houndsman with the same still attention he gives an order from the capital.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Grelthar commands the wardens and houndsmen of [[place-cervalia|Kervalara]], some two hundred people responsible for the imperial game forest. He has kept it for sixteen years. His orders come from the Orzas’s household, and the provincial governor at [[place-aravantia|Aravantara]] has no authority over the reserve.

He wants enough beaters for the court’s autumn visit without driving the neighboring villages into open refusal. The latest extension of the paling takes fields those villages still remember working. He enforces the boundary but cannot reduce it on his own word; the separate forest magistrate decides the sentences.

## Background

**The rides.** He began with the routine work of keeping rides clear and finding deer before a hunt. He learned which village households supply reliable beaters and which lodges need repairs before a visit.

**The command.** Taking charge of the reserve gave him the crown’s orders and the town’s wages to answer for. Over sixteen years he has learned how a small change in the paling can displace an entire household.

**The visit.** The court is due after three years away. He must prepare the Lodge while several villages refuse to provide the men the hunt needs.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is economical with promises and attentive to small practical needs. He can be courteous while refusing an appeal that he has no intention of carrying further.

### Motivation

He wants the forest’s work to continue without a confrontation that brings the capital’s officers into every village along the paling.

### Strengths

He knows the reserve and its workers, can organize a large hunt, and hears grievances before they reach the Lodge’s court.

### Weaknesses

Keeping the peace has become more important to him than treating neighboring villages alike. He can preserve an arrangement long after the people living under it stop consenting.

## Social

The wardens answer to him, the magistrate does not, and the town’s chosen headman must work around his practical command. The tenants blame his men for boundaries ordered elsewhere.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: a citizen.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The beaters.** Grelthar hires a party to help prepare the hunt and discover whether the refusing villages will supply anyone under different terms.
2. **The missing tenant.** A woman seeks her husband inside the reserve after the paling takes their cottage. Grelthar can grant a supervised search; she wants him found before a warden arrests him.
3. **The boy on the green.** A village seeks help for a boy condemned for taking a hind. Grelthar can provide the arrest account, but the magistrate controls the sentence.

:::secret {title="The grove and the bargain"}
Grelthar keeps a hidden altar to [[lore-karnavosdty|Karnavos]] deep in the imperial forest, inherited from the previous head warden. At each season’s turn he brings older wardens, houndsmen and village headmen to hunt and eat a stag there. Villages whose headmen attend are spared arrests for poaching; others are not. The villages refusing the hunt have stopped attending. The magistrate knows nothing of the grove. Discovery by the imperial household would put Grelthar before the same hanging judge whose court protects his boundary.
:::
