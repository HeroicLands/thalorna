# Appearance {#appearance}

Ronethos Zurmelates is a 58-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of light build. He has medium skin browned by the road, black hair going gray at the temples and gray eyes that rest on whoever is speaking. He dresses for travel in a plain coat and boots, and he carries his own satchel of files rather than leave it with a clerk. He asks his questions quietly and writes down the answers himself.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ronethos Zurmelates is a _sekeros_ (inquirer) of [[affiliation-sekran|The Arcane Inquisition]] (the Sekran), the enforcement arm of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]], and he holds its commission: he may compel testimony, search property, seize arcane materials and detain a suspect, and he answers for every file he opens. His reputation in the Sekran is for persistence. He does not drop a file because it has gone quiet.

His present file concerns two things that may be one: minerals stolen from the stores of the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]], and new alchemical compounds that have begun to appear in black-market channels. He has no evidence against [[being-azatodmzns|Azato Damzanis]], the alchemist of the Order at [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]], but their paths cross more often than he likes, and he has noticed it.

He keeps his rooms and active files at [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]], close enough to the provincial traffic to question the carters who bring mineral cargoes down from the heights. His landlord knows him as a quiet tenant who pays for dry storage before better food. Witnesses meet him by appointment; he will not leave a frightened informant waiting in a public corridor.

## Background

**The Order.** Ronethos is a mage of the Thordimalon first, as every _sekeros_ is, and he keeps his rank in the Order while he serves the Sekran.

**The commission.** The Sekran does not take volunteers. A regional office asked his chapter for him, the head of the Sekran approved, and he was told. He has held the commission since, and he has a name for finishing files other men set down.

**The present file.** The thefts from the Epistémion's stores are small and careful, and the compounds on the black market are better made than any hedge-alchemist's. That combination points to a trained hand, and Ronethos is looking for it.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is patient, courteous and dry, and he is hard to hurry or to offend. He reads the clause of the Sekran's commission aloud before every hearing, as the Sekran requires, and he means it.

### Motivation

He wants the file finished. A hand off the register is, in the Sekran's doctrine, a party to the old crime at Alessa, and a trained hand selling to the black market is worse than a hedge-mage.

### Strengths

He holds the commission and its powers under imperial law. He has the Sekran's archives behind him, and he remembers names.

### Weaknesses

He has no proof, and an inquirer who accuses a _marendos_ (master) of the Order without proof loses his commission. He works alone more than the Sekran likes.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The compound.** Ronethos buys a sample of a new compound from a black-market dealer and wants it traced back to its maker. He hires a party to buy more, without the dealer learning who is asking.
2. **The stores.** The Epistémion's storekeeper says the thefts are a clerk's errors. Ronethos asks a party to watch the stores for a month of nights and to write down everyone who comes in after the bell.
3. **The hearing.** A cunning-woman whom Ronethos has summoned to a hearing has fled, and he asks a party to bring her back unharmed, because she has named a buyer he wants to hear about.
4. **The informant.** A carter offers a party information on the black-market compounds for a price, and says the Sekran's _sekeros_ will pay double for it.

:::secret {title="The trained hand and the dormant file"}
The Sekran's file on the death of [[being-hiltheristho|Hiltheris Tholmaris]], set down sixteen years ago, has Azato Damzanis's name in it, and its agents never lost their suspicion of him. Ronethos has not read it. If his own file brings Azato's name to a _holsekeros_, the old file can be opened again, and Ronethos becomes the man who reads both.

Azato keeps a second name, Kuthstan Crei, for work that skirts what the Sekran would sanction, and a merchant's widow of the coast trade who buys mineral specimens for him knows him only by it. Ronethos does not know the name. The day he hears it in a hearing and puts it beside Azato's, his file has a suspect.
:::
