# Appearance {#appearance}

Halzoras Vendaren is a 52-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall and is of heavy build. He has olive skin weathered by twenty years of campaigns, black hair going gray and cut to the legion's length, and gray eyes set wide in a broad face. A sword scar runs from his left ear to the angle of his jaw, and two fingers of his right hand do not close fully, so he takes a cup in his left. He wears a legate's plain red cloak over good wool in the city, and his own officers' gift on his breast: a small silver eagle on a black cord. His voice carries across a parade ground without effort, and he has to remember to lower it in a drawing room.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Halzoras Vendaren is a _vandraks_ (legate) of [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|the legions]], commander of a legion now in its winter camp on the northern march, and the claimant to the adoption whom [[affiliation-sorvan|the Imperial Guard]] prefers. He has been in [[place-orzara|Orzara City]] since the autumn to sit the war council's hearing on the frontier estimates, in a house he rents on the [[place-heightsorz|Heights]], and the hearing has run longer than anyone expected.

He was a watch-captain of the Guard before he was a legate, and the officers he promoted then command its watches now. The Guard expects a donative from whoever is adopted. The man the court expects to be named has promised one and paid nothing, and Halzoras's household has offered the officers a larger sum in coin now, before anyone is adopted. Where a legate of a provincial house finds that much coin is asked at every dinner on the Heights.

He carries the legion's name for good and ill. The legion is still the strongest army in the west, and it is in disrepute after the war in which Taruzel won its independence. Halzoras fought that war and brought his legion home from it whole, and the city has never agreed whether that was the best thing done in the war or the worst.

## Background

**The bought commission.** The Vendaren are a provincial family of standing in [[affiliation-provinclys|the Province of Alessa]], named for the estate of Vendara, rich enough to buy commissions and too far from the capital to be asked to dinner. His father bought him a _lusel_ (junior tribune) commission at eighteen and a second at twenty-two. He served on the western frontier, where the senior centurion of his cohort taught him what the purchase had not.

**The Guard.** At twenty-eight he was taken into the Guard, and in the first year of the present reign the new Orzas, [[being-zrmlshlrkls|Zarmelas Helarkales]], made him a watch-captain at the Guard Camp. He held the watch for six years. He was strict, fair and generous with his own money at the feasts, and the Guard remembers him as one of its own.

**The war.** His family bought him a legion at thirty-four, and two years later the legion went east to the war with Taruzel. It won every field it fought on. After the squadron was lost at [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]] and the eastern garrisons were cut off from the sea, Halzoras marched his legion overland through the hills to the frontier, with its eagle and most of its men, a week before the war council's order to do so reached him. The forts that could not march surrendered. The Dukran's report on the war, which leaked to the senatorial houses within months, says he marched on his own judgment. The Guard and his own men call it the only honorable thing done in the east. The veterans of the garrisons left behind call it running.

**The Compromise.** His command was purchased, and [[lore-commissioncmp|the Commission Compromise]] left it untouched. Reformers name him in the Seldan as a purchased legate who happened to be competent, and say the next one may not be.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is direct, energetic and impatient with forms, and he is better liked by soldiers than by senators. He remembers the names of men he served with and their wounds, and he gives his officers credit in public and blame in private. He has a soldier's contempt for debt and a legate's habit of spending money he has not got.

### Motivation

He wants the adoption, and he believes the empire needs a soldier on the throne after a reign that lost a province. He wants the legion restored in the city's regard, its arrears paid and its next war a victory. He speaks in the war council for the hawks: a war for tribute against Helionis, and a reckoning with Taruzel when the treasury can bear it.

### Strengths

The Guard prefers him, and the Guard holds the palace. The legions respect him, and he can speak to soldiers in their own words. He has a record no other claimant has, and an eagle that came home.

### Weaknesses

He has no great house behind him, no seat in the Seldan and no fortune of his own. The Dukran's report hangs over him, and the veterans of the eastern garrisons hate him. The Senate's majority fears a soldier on the throne more than it fears an empty treasury, and every senator who remembers [[lore-brkensuccsn|the Broken Succession]] knows what follows when the Guard chooses an Orzas.

## Social

### Patrons

- [[being-zrmlshlrkls|Zarmelas Helarkales]]: The Orzas, who made him a watch-captain in the first year of the reign, and who said at a feast this year that a man who brought his eagle home could carry a heavier one. The court has repeated it since.
- [[affiliation-sorvan|The Imperial Guard]]: The watch-captains he once served beside, who favor him over every other claimant.

### Enemies

- [[being-klvnsmrdvns|Kolvenas Mardovanes]]: The Senate's claimant, who presided over the sitting that voted the Commission Compromise and has promised the Guard a donative he has not paid.
- [[being-dlmrsvrklns|Dalmeras Varkalenis]]: The old blood's claimant, whose friends among the Gilded call Halzoras a provincial with a bought commission.
- [[being-sthmrrdln|Sathemar Ardelen]]: The tribune of the veterans' stairs, who has refused his household's invitation to dine, because the stairs of the eastern legions will not sit at a table with the man who left the forts.
- [[being-shttrkssm|Shattar Kassumi]]: Captain of the Clean Shields, a Taruzel-born company on the city's grain road. Halzoras has spoken against the foreign companies in the war council, as every legion officer does.

### Others

- [[being-drvnsrlln|Dorvanis Arelliani Septarius]]: The Imperial _Tarkelos_, who paid the legion's arrears from the war nineteen months late. Halzoras has not forgotten it.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The garrison's survivor.** A veteran who was in one of the eastern forts when it surrendered has come to Orzara to speak against Halzoras at the next dole. Halzoras hires a party to find him first and bring him to dinner, because he would rather answer the man than silence him.
2. **The coin chest.** Halzoras's steward hires a party to escort a chest of coin from the rented house on the Heights to a cookshop below the Guard Camp at night, and to see it handed to a named sergeant and nobody else.
3. **The report.** A copy of the Dukran's report, with a page that the leaked copies lack, is offered for sale in the Gilded Quarter. Halzoras pays a party to buy it before a reformer does, and to bring it to him unread.
4. **The officers' altar.** Several of his legion's officers keep the Night of Ashes and have asked leave to light its fire in the legion's winter camp. Halzoras sends a party north with his answer, and wants to know how the camp takes it.

:::secret {title="Whose coin the legate offers"}
The donative Halzoras offers the Guard is the House of Veldis's. [[being-amrzvlds|Amarza Veldis]] lends it to him through the house's chapter at [[place-mercavia|Delalara]], in the name of his brother-in-law, a cloth merchant of that city who has never seen so much silver. Her price is a letter in Halzoras's own hand, signed and sealed: that in his reign the throne's paper will be honored whole and at the rates written, and that the Danvar's _rozimoi_ (auditors) will stay out of the great houses' chapter books. He has signed it, and the chapter at Orzara keeps it until it can be sent south to the strongroom at Delalara. He does not know why the auditors matter to her, and he has not asked.

She has also told him, without naming it, that when the night comes there will be an armed company on the grain road outside the city's line, a mile from the river gate, ready to stand for the claimant who honors the throne's debt. He has guessed which company. In the spring his steward dined at [[affiliation-cleanshlds|the Clean Shields]]' camp, and in the autumn Halzoras met [[being-shttrkssm|Shattar Kassumi]] on the river road at night, without his cloak. He speaks against the company in the war council, and he means to use it if he must. He does not know that the offer is the house's to give to whichever claimant signs, and Amarza has not told him.

He does not know that an adoption deed in his name lies drafted in the Orzas's strongbox beside two others, unsealed. He takes the old man's remark at the feast as a promise, which it may not be.

He knows the Guard's favor is bought as well as earned. If the Veldis coin stopped, half the watch-captains who toast him would go to whoever paid next, and he knows which half.

**Hook: the courier's other paper.** A Veldis courier carrying master notes to Delalara is robbed on the coast road, and Halzoras's brother-in-law writes that the letter was in the same bag. Halzoras hires a party to recover it before the robbers learn whose seal it bears, and does not tell them what it says.
