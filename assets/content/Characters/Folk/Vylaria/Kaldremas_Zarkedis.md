# Appearance {#appearance}

Kaldremas Zarkedis is 64 years old. His gray hair is thin, his face long and his shoulders narrow. He smooths the cuffs of a carefully mended tunic before receiving anyone. He speaks with the deliberate courtesy of an administrator conducting business, even when the visitor stands in the doorway of his rented rooms.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kaldremas remains master of [[affiliation-zarkedis|House Zarkedis]], a Bronze house still entered on the registry. He lives in two rented rooms behind the tannery in [[place-skoraz|Soraks]], supported by money his son sends. The house’s land is seized, its home sold and its masks left in the buyer’s cupboard. One old freedwoman stays with the family; the roll of the name has three people on it.

He wants the masks returned to a cupboard the family owns. It is a modest wish only in the space it would occupy. The buyer refuses to sell, and the money from [[being-klstrtsxrchds|Kalestratos Zarkedis]], his thirty-three-year-old son and named heir, keeps the rented rooms rather than buying back a house.

## Background

**The fall.** Kaldremas is the disgraced bureaucrat whose borrowed-money venture, faithless partners and creditors cost the family its property. His disgrace costs him the provincial administration’s trust. [[being-lophiriaghal|Lofiria Ghaliros]], an old ally, turns against the house in that season and hastens its ruin. The news reaches Kalestratos in his final year as a student.

**The name.** The house remains among the Bronze Houses on the spring registry. Nobody pays to have it struck off. Kaldremas insists on the distinction between losing the property and losing the name, and still names his heir. That claim gives him no seat, land or restored household.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is formal with strangers and easily wounded by pity. A visitor who asks about the family’s records receives a fuller answer than one who asks whether he has enough to eat.

### Motivation

He wants something tangible to pass to Kalestratos besides the cause of his anger. The masks matter first; a place in Soraks where the family can keep them matters next.

### Strengths

He knows the forms of provincial business and the difference between a clerk’s account of an act and its entered record. He can explain what a petition needs even when he cannot afford its fees.

### Weaknesses

He speaks as though the house’s standing should still secure attention. He finds it difficult to ask plainly for help, especially from people who know what his son sends him.

## Social

Kalestratos practices with the [[affiliation-pirethos|Pirethos]] fellowship of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Thordimalon]] at [[place-velysara|Velisara]]. He means to restore the name through arcane power and standing, beyond a creditor’s reach, and accepts Lady [[being-iltherethalu|Ilthere Thalurates]]’s political support while despising the trade she offers. Kaldremas depends on his son’s money, but the son’s ambitions do not make him master of the house.

The freedwoman who refuses to leave is the household that remains to Kaldremas. Across the square the masks hang in another owner’s cupboard. The buyer’s refusal leaves him with a dispute he cannot settle through the deference his name once commanded.

### Affiliations

- **House Zarkedis**: Of the Name, holding Master of the House; Kalestratos remains the named heir.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The masks.** The party comes to negotiate for the masks on Kalestratos’s behalf. Kaldremas can explain what each return would mean to the house, but cannot make the buyer name a price.
2. **The missing page.** The creditors’ sale record lacks the page naming the buyer. Kaldremas asks the party to establish who handles the book before approaching the provincial administration; the clerk who cuts the page remains in its service.
3. **The petitioner’s fee.** Kaldremas has a petition ready but cannot afford to submit it. He asks the party to help him obtain a hearing about the masks without pledging his son’s next remittance, and offers his knowledge of provincial procedure in return.

:::secret {title="The concealed purchaser"}
The buyer at the creditors’ sale is a factor of House Thalurates. Ilthere holds the Soraks house, its cupboard and its masks through that purchase. She knows and has not told Kalestratos; she means the masks to be the price she names for his loyalty. Kaldremas’s wish to recover them does not reveal that bargain.
:::
