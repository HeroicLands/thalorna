# Appearance {#appearance}

Kolsame Linthimis is a 46-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of heavy build. She has light skin weathered by years of street duty, brown hair cut short under a plain cap, and brown eyes that go to a crowd's hands before its faces. She wears the leather jack and cudgel of an ordinary watchman with a captain's red cloak over them, and she walks the lanes at a patrol's pace. She speaks little and asks short questions, and she writes the answers down herself.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kolsame Linthimis is the Nokaran, the _zoralus_ (prefect of the watch) of [[affiliation-citywatchorz|the City Watch]] of [[place-orzara|Orzara]]. She commands the watch-houses of the Twelve Circles, their cohorts, their gaols and their fire-gear, and she answers for all of it to the Veramus of Order of [[affiliation-verdanorzara|the Orzara Courts]]. When the Veramus wants numbers, she gives him numbers. When a crowd gathers at a pay-hall or the grain halls, a Watch picket stands at the end of the lane, and often she is with it.

Her other work is the poisoners. The Watch is the office that hunts the underground apothecaries, the men and women who sell poisons and antidotes outside any guild, and for years the one she has wanted most is [[being-hrkhsbrmyds|Harkis Baremides]], the master poisoner whose work turns up in the capital's crimes.

## Background

**The legions.** Kolsame came up the commoner track, as every officer of the Watch does: twenty years under the eagle, a centurion's stick at the end of it, and the citizenship with her discharge.

**The Watch.** She took a watchman's cudgel in the capital, rose to captain a watch-house cohort, and was named Nokaran over the heads of older captains because she could make a Circle quiet and could show the Veramus on paper that she had done it.

**The hunt.** A poisoning brought her to Harkis's work early in her command, and she has kept the file open since. She has spent more of the Watch's men on it than the Veramus knows.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is blunt, tireless and suspicious, and she trusts a written record over a sworn word. She has no patience with captains who cannot say how many men they have on a stair. She is not cruel, and she is not gentle either.

### Motivation

She wants the city quiet and the Veramus satisfied, in that order when she can choose and the other way round when she cannot. She wants Harkis Baremides in a Watch gaol before she leaves the post.

### Strengths

She commands some five thousand armed veterans and every gate-road across the Clearance. Her captains fear her and her watchmen respect her. She remembers names and faces from a single hearing.

### Weaknesses

Her command is the Veramus's to give and take, and the Veramus holds his seat at the throne's pleasure. Her pursuit of Harkis has become a private matter, and a party offering her a lead on him can lead her a long way.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-hrkhsbrmyds|Harkis Baremides]]: The poisoner she has hunted for years, who has slipped every net she has set.
- [[affiliation-sorvan|The Sorvan]]: The imperial guard on the palace hill, whose officers call her watchmen street-wardens.
- [[affiliation-cleanshlds|The Clean Shields]]: The company at the river gate, one of whose men knifed a watchman of the River Circle last winter and was paid off and turned out the same night.

### Others

- [[being-sthmrrdln|Sathemar Ardelen]]: The veterans' tribune of the stairs, who brings men from the River Circle stairs wherever a crowd gathers. She needs him to calm crowds and does not trust him to.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-citywatchorz|The City Watch]]**: a Captain of the Watch, holding the office of Nokaran.
- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|The Vylarian Empire]]**: a citizen by her twenty years.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The lean-tos.** The Veramus of Order has ordered the strip below the Salkalon Gate cleared. Kolsame hires a party to walk the Clearance with her captains the night before and count how many people the dawn will put on the road.
2. **The captain's dinner.** Every claimant to the succession is courting the watch-house captains. Kolsame asks a party to learn which of her captains has dined on the Heights this month, and with whom.
3. **The knifeman.** The man who knifed the River Circle watchman is in the outskirts beyond the Watch's charter. Kolsame cannot send her men after him, and she would be glad if someone else brought him to the gate.
4. **The apothecary's trail.** A fever that is not a fever has killed a grain broker on the Wharves, and Kolsame believes it is Harkis's work. She wants a party to find the seller before the Veramus hears that the Watch has lost the trail again.

:::secret {title="The Watch's numbers"}
The sweeps Kolsame orders to show the Veramus numbers fall on the stairs of people who have not paid for the night tallies, because her captains choose the stairs. The Spire Circle house sells the tallies, and its sergeants, centurions and captain share the coin by rank. Kolsame takes none of it, and she has never asked why every sweep of the Warrens finds the same stairs.

**Half a Ward Short.** When the Spire Circle pay-hall shuts at noon, Kolsame wants names, so that her sweeps have someone to sweep and the Veramus has someone to blame. If the party does nothing, the Watch puts a hundred sleepers without tallies across the Clearance on the second night.

**The Sand Beneath the Crust.** At the opening of the river-gate halls Kolsame commands the Watch picket. She holds the crowd for whoever the Veramus of the Grain names, and a party standing beside the granary-keeper stands where she will look first.
:::
