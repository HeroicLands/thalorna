# Appearance {#appearance}

Revothos Linthimis is 55 years old. He has a broad sun-browned face, gray eyes and dark hair bleached at the ends. Three missing fingers make his gestures abrupt, but he handles a cup without spilling it. His heavy coat smells faintly of lamp oil even when it is clean.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Revothos is first among the boat-owning captains who govern [[place-tholkezora|Tholkezora]]. Thirty years hunting sea-beasts cost him two boats and three fingers. He has never been caught with anything he should not carry. The town’s crews know that his welcome means a place at a captain’s table, not freedom to ask about every cave beneath the headland.

He wants a new customs cutter dealt with after it takes two boats in a month. Its captain will not accept a payment. Revothos is willing to hear persuasion, warning or a plan to wreck the cutter; other captains must still decide what risks to take with their crews.

## Background

**The far grounds.** He learns the week-long fishing runs before joining the seasonal hunt. A boat must come home with enough catch to pay for the days beyond sight of shore.

**The lost boats.** Losing two hulls leaves him attentive to boats, lines and men who claim a beast is already beaten. He learns to distinguish a bold crew from one that cannot afford to turn back.

**The council.** His long survival gives him a place at the head of the captains’ discussions. He represents their interests without owning their boats or deciding their shares.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is sociable with an introduced guest and brusque with an uninvited question. He admires competence more than a boast about fearlessness.

### Motivation

He wants his town’s boats free to make a profitable voyage and return without surrendering the result at a customs barrier.

### Strengths

He knows the far grounds, commands the captains’ attention and can supply boats and experienced hands for a difficult journey.

### Weaknesses

He judges customs enforcement as an attack on his town even when a cutter acts within its orders. He may invite a confrontation his neighbors must also endure.

## Social

The council’s captains are his peers. Bralvik crews sail with them when the pay is good, and Delalara buyers seek the oil and goods their boats bring home.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: of the propertied order.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The cutter.** Revothos hires a party to speak to the new captain and return with terms, or with an exact account of why no terms are possible.
2. **The harpoon.** A sea-beast comes in with a Taruzelian harpoon in its back. A Delalara factor asks how far the boats travel; Revothos wants to know who pays for that question.
3. **The cave.** A shipping house buys smokehouses and asks to rent a cave the captains refuse. Revothos offers the party work guarding the refusal while the buyer offers work investigating it.

:::secret {title="The buyers on the far grounds"}
For eight years Revothos and four other captains buy stolen guild cargoes from Taruzelian outlaws far offshore, pay a third of their worth in imperial silver, and sell them through the caves to [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] buyers. Kastamar’s officers know and protect the trade because it harms the island guilds and supplies naval intelligence. The new cutter’s captain is sent by the Delalara tax-farmer without that knowledge. Compact assessor [Termuza Ezzinu]{.name data-type="being/npc"} arrives as an oil buyer after tracing three stolen bales; Revothos does not automatically know her purpose.
:::
