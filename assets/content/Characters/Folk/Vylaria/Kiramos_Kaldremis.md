# Appearance {#appearance}

Kiramos Kaldremis is 54 years old. His black hair is gray at the temples, his face broad and his dark eyes heavy-lidded. He sits with his hands folded until a witness gives a teacher’s name; then he reaches for the record. His voice is quiet enough to make a crowded hearing wait for him to finish.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kiramos is Head of [[affiliation-sekran|the Sekran]], the Arcane Inquisition of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Thordimalon]]. The Verdan Maldor chooses its holsekeros and seats him among its holmarendoi. He lives in [[place-orzara|Orzara]], answers to that council and keeps the register of the hunted. The office makes him responsible for the commission’s cases, not master of the council that appoints him.

He wants reports that can survive a hearing. A frightened neighbor’s account may start an inquiry; it cannot tell him where a practitioner learned or whom that practitioner has taught. Kiramos asks for the chain of teaching before he asks what punishment a hunter recommends. He means to bring unregistered practitioners inside the Order’s monopoly, and he does not confuse a patient inquiry with permission to remain outside it.

## Background

**The commission.** The Sekran’s first inquiry, into [[lore-recensio|the Therona of Alessa]], remains open. Its commission under [[lore-kandrol|the Kandrol]] also enforces the Order’s monopoly. Kiramos approves the particular mages the office asks for as recruits; a candidate’s application does not oblige him to hear it.

**The reach of a hearing.** Within the Empire the commission carries imperial law. Abroad a hunter depends on local standing, negotiated rights or hired help. Kiramos expects reports from [[place-helionis|Helionis]] to respect its negotiated traditions and reports from [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel]] to distinguish information bought from an informer from authority the guilds have actually granted. The [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] is a lawful faith; its rites become the Sekran’s concern only where they are arcane practice.

## Psyche

### Personality

He lets a witness finish even when the account wanders. With his own hunters he is less indulgent: an accusation returned without its evidence remains the hunter’s work to complete.

### Motivation

He wants the Order to obtain teachers and their pupils before an inquiry becomes a trail of dead practitioners. A living teacher can name the next link in the chain.

### Strengths

He separates what a report establishes from what its author suspects. He can also tell a hunter exactly which unanswered question prevents a case from proceeding.

### Weaknesses

He keeps a case open long after its witnesses have scattered. His patience with incomplete files consumes attention that his hunters want for immediate threats.

## Social

The Verdan Maldor can demand his account of the commission. The regional office at [[place-velysara|Velisara]] sends him cases, while senior sekeroi trade information with the throne’s intelligence service without becoming part of it. Kiramos treats a favor between services as a debt to remember, not a transfer of authority.

[[being-cibelasrnt|Kivella Serenthis]], aboard [[affiliation-thetamzir|the Tamzir]], remains among the practitioners the Sekran hunts. A report placing her in a foreign harbor presents him with both a target and a jurisdiction he must reckon with.

### Affiliations

- **Sekran**: Holsekeros, holding Head of the Sekran by the Verdan’s choice.
- **Thordimalon**: Holmarendos, seated among the Verdan’s senior masters.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The teacher’s name.** A hunter brings Kiramos an accusation with no account of the suspect’s teacher. Kiramos employs the party to find the witness who supplied the name before the hunter seeks an arrest.
2. **The foreign harbor.** A report places the Tamzir in a Taruzelian harbor. Kiramos pays for confirmation of Kivella’s presence and the local terms under which his agents could approach; a confident sighting gives him no imperial warrant abroad.
3. **The lawful rite.** A temple asks for help after a hunter treats worship as proof of unsanctioned magic. Kiramos will hear evidence of what was actually performed, and the party must bring an account that distinguishes the rite from the hunter’s accusation.
