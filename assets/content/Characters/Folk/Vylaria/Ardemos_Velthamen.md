# Appearance {#appearance}

Ardemos Velthamen is a 51-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of heavy build. He has skin tanned and toughened by a life outdoors, green eyes, a nose broken and set crooked, and graying brown hair worn in a single long braid. He dresses like his own foresters, in leather and undyed wool, with a baron's silver chain over it only when he goes up to the holy city. His hands are scarred across the knuckles by rope and bark. He talks loudly, swears by the trees, and laughs at his own jokes before he finishes them.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ardemos Velthamen is a lay baron of the [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]]. His family holds the forest of the upper valley above the woodcrafters' village of [[place-dremenna|Dremenna]] of the temple of [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]], for rent and service, and keeps a timber hall above the village with a manor court and men to send to the host. He holds no priestly office and never will, and he takes his orders on the forest from the temple's prior, [[being-mlknssrvths|Melkanas Sarvothis]].

He is the loudest of the barons who blame the priors' cutting for [the Withering]{.name data-type="lore"}. The temples teach that the forest dies where its people neglect the rites. Ardemos says, at the king's table and in front of priests, that it dies where the contract crews have been, and that no procession with lamps ever put a dead tree back on its feet.

## Background

**The barons' house.** The Velthamen have held the upper valley for nine generations, and take their name from it. Their sons go into the host and the [[affiliation-sacredflame|Order of the Sacred Flame]]; his younger son, [Holvar Velthamen]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, is a Knight of the Flame at the chapel below [[place-ashenmere|Ashenmere]].

**The king's friend.** Ardemos and [[being-klvrsdrns|Kelvaras Daronis]] hunted together in the crown forest when the king was a boy, and the king still calls him to every hunt. Ardemos was among the barons who came up to the holy city in force the winter the Holy Mother refused the king's cousin.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is blunt, generous and quick-tempered. He loves his forest and knows every stand in it, and he is at ease with fellers and carvers in a way the priest-nobles find vulgar. He has no patience for theology and a long memory for insults.

### Motivation

He wants his valley's forest kept alive and his family on it. He wants the priors' contracts stopped and the barons given a say over the cutting, and he wants the king kept on his throne, because the barons have no other friend in the holy city.

### Strengths

His men are woodsmen who know the uplands better than anyone in the kingdom, and they follow him without question. The king listens to him. The carvers of Dremenna depend on his valley's timber, and the village stands with him.

### Weaknesses

He holds his land of a temple and cannot defy its prior for long. He speaks before he thinks, and he has made an enemy of a high priest in front of witnesses.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The gray stand above Dremenna.** A stand at the head of the valley has gone gray in midsummer, the first in Ardemos's forest. The village elder of Dremenna wants it cut and burned before the prior hears of it, Ardemos wants it left for the king to see, and the carvers want a party to tell them which of the two to obey.
2. **What the woodsmen fear.** His oldest woodsmen will not go into the upper valley after dark since the stand went gray, and will not say why. Ardemos thinks they are being foolish, and wants a party to spend a night there and prove it.
3. **The baron's case.** Ardemos means to put his case against the contracts to the king's court and needs evidence: the dates each valley was cut and the dates each stand died. The prior's forester keeps the book, and will not lend it.
4. **The king's hunt.** Ardemos is gathering the barons for the king's hunt toward Moktur and wants a party to ride ahead and see that no temple's men are waiting in the forest.

:::secret {title="The baron's rent"}
Ardemos is two years behind with his rent to the temple of Sélmoros, and he has borrowed from its treasury besides, at the interest the temples call an offering, to keep his hall and his men through two bad winters of falling charcoal prices. The innkeeper of the Charcoal Wagon in the holy city knows, because Ardemos's steward drinks there. [[being-mlknssrvths|Melkanas Sarvothis]] holds the rent book and has offered to forgive the whole debt if Ardemos lets a contract crew into the upper valley, the last uncut valley on the estate. Ardemos has not answered. He cannot pay, and he cannot say yes without becoming the thing he has denounced in the king's hall.

He does not know that his son Holvar rides across the border stream at night with the knights of the Ashenmere chapel, or what they bring back. He would be proud of the faith of it and furious at the danger, and he would tell the king.

**Hook: the steward's tongue.** Ardemos's steward has been heard at the Charcoal Wagon telling a stranger exactly how far behind his master is. The baron wants to know who the stranger was and who sent him, before the debt is talked about in the temple quarter.
:::
