# Appearance {#appearance}

Torzemos Brakarates is a 49-year-old man who stands 6'2" tall and is of heavy build. He has olive skin, amber eyes, and a head and jaw shaved clean. His left forearm and hand are shiny and ridged from old burns to the elbow, and he leaves the sleeve pushed back so they show. He wears a black robe cut like a legion officer's cloak, open at the throat over the brand of a clenched gauntlet on the right side of his chest. He stands like a soldier, speaks like an orator, and never raises his voice, because he has never needed to.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Torzemos Brakarates is the High Flame-Warden of Viléria, who rules [[place-sarkenaelzen|the Ashen Temple]] in the Vanor of [[place-orzara|Orzara City]] and from it oversees the temples of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] across the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]. The faith recognizes no authority above a Flame-Warden, and he holds the office as the faith holds every authority: by strength. The provincial Flame-Wardens defer to him because of his standing, his patrons in [[affiliation-seldan|the Seldan]] and his Scorchmasters, and any of them who thinks himself stronger may challenge him.

The Black Flame is a real power in the empire. Its temples stand openly in the capital and in the commercial cities of [[place-alyssa|Alessa]]; its faithful include _kurthoi_ (senators), officers of the legions and the sons of houses who will never inherit; and its preachers draw larger crowds every year on the Circles below the hill. Torzemos speaks for all of it at once.

He has the next accession petition drafted. At every accession the Black Flame asks the new Orzas, as chief priest of the state cult, for a place in the Forge-Lord's niche in [[place-pnthnrldnm|the Temple of the Twelve]], where its flame may burn beside the Sacred Forge's, and at every accession the Árdavan keepers refuse it. The reigning Orzas is old and has adopted nobody, and Torzemos and his patrons are weighing every claimant on the question.

## Background

**The officer.** He is the third son of a house the throne raised in his grandfather's time, with no inheritance to wait for. He served twelve years in the legions and rose to command a cohort on the eastern frontier, and he took the Mark of the Fist in a camp before a battle, from a Flame-Warden who rode with the legion. He came home after the Taruzelian war with the burned arm and a following among the officers who had served with him.

**The Warden.** He entered the Ashen Temple at thirty-nine, passed the ordeals faster than any Aspirant of his year, and was a Scorchmaster at forty-three. When the old High Flame-Warden died, two Flame-Wardens claimed the office. Torzemos had the larger following among the officers and the Seldan, and the other withdrew to his provincial temple without a challenge.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is controlled, articulate and wholly convinced. He believes that the strong are meant to rule and that the empire is weak because it has forgotten it, and he says so on the Circles in words a veteran understands. He is courteous to his enemies in public and never forgets one.

### Motivation

He wants the niche, or failing it, a reign that will not press the Ashen Temple. Beneath that he wants the Black Flame recognized as what he believes it is, the god's truest face, and its faithful in the empire's high places as of right.

### Strengths

He has patrons in the Seldan, a following among the officers and the veterans, and the most attended temple in the capital. He can bring a crowd onto the Spire Circle in an afternoon.

### Weaknesses

The faith's authority is strength, and a stronger Flame-Warden can take his office from him. The Vanor's law punishes open conflict between faiths without mercy, so he fights only with votes and sermons. The orthodox clergy, the Árdavan keepers and Nartun all count him an enemy.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-osmrthlvns|Osmera Thalvenis]]: The Árdavan keeper of the Temple of the Twelve, who will refuse his petition at the next accession as her predecessors refused it at the last.
- [[being-ankhelossath|Ankelos Satheris]]: An archimandrite of the Sacred Forge, who presses the city's magistrates to restrict the Black Flame's preaching and pays for counter-sermons on the Circles.
- [[being-ilvnssrkls|Ilvenas Sarkelis]]: The Holy Mother of Nartun, whose kingdom broke with the empire over the Black Flame.
- [[being-sldrsplvrn|Saldoros Pelvaren]]: Master of the Order of the Sacred Flame, the knights who keep the god's word without the courage to use it.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]]**: High Flame-Warden of Viléria, a Flame-Warden.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The petition's carrier.** A clerk of the Flame-Wardenate wants a party to carry the draft petition to a claimant's house on the [[place-heightsorz|Heights]] without the Árdavan keepers' friends learning which house received it.
2. **The challenger's road.** A provincial Flame-Warden is on the road to Orzara with his Scorchmasters, and Torzemos wants to know before he arrives whether he means to pray or to challenge, and who in the Seldan has been writing to him.
3. **The Night of Ashes.** At the next Night of Ashes the City Watch means to stand its engines at the Vanor's gate rather than at the foot of the hill. Torzemos wants to know which magistrate ordered it and why.
4. **The counter-sermon.** A preacher paid by the Sacred Forge has begun answering the Black Flame's preachers on the Spire Circle, and a brawl there would break the Vanor's peace. Torzemos hires a party to see that it does not, and to learn who pays the preacher.

:::secret {title="The Flame-Warden's ledger and the claimant's offer"}
Torzemos reads the grievances. On the Night of Ashes his Scorchmasters take the written grievances of officers, senators' clients, clerks of the Offices and men of the Watch out of the crowd's hands and put them in a sack instead of the fire, and he reads them afterward. The ledger built from them tells him who hates whom in the Seldan and which officers will listen to the faith, and it is how he chose the patrons who made him High Flame-Warden. Nobody outside the Flame-Wardenate knows it exists.

He also keeps the names of the households of the Hilen march that knights of Nartun's Order have carried across the border stream by night, eleven of them. He has not given them to his patrons, because raising it in the Seldan asks the throne to act against Nartun, and he would rather keep the names until they are worth more.

A grievance in last spring's sack told him that a claimant's house had sent a priest of the Sacred Forge up the holy road to Nartun. He does not know which house or the terms. He can guess them: the only thing Nartun has ever wanted from an Orzas is the proscription of the Black Flame. He has let it be known, privately and to every claimant's household, that a claimant who promises to proscribe the faith loses its officers in the legions, its patrons in the Seldan and its preachers on every Circle in the Warrens, and gains a faith of the strong as his enemy before he is adopted. Every household has thanked him and assured him that it has made no such promise.

**Hook: the sack.** A Scorchmaster has quarreled with the Flame-Wardenate and is offering to sell what he knows of the sack. A party is hired by a senator's household to buy it, and Torzemos's Scorchmasters are already looking for him.
:::
