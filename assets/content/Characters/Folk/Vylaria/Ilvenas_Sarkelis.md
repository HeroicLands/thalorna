# Appearance {#appearance}

Ilvenas Sarkelis is a 66-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She has fair skin, deeply lined and clear of any mark, gray hair bound under a white linen veil, and gray eyes that rest on a speaker until he has finished. A smudge of ash sits on her brow, put there at the dawn rite and never wiped away before evening. She wears the plain undyed wool of a temple priestess under a mantle of white, and she moves slowly and without a cane. Her voice is low and carries in the Mother's Hall without effort, and she speaks only the kingdom's old ritual Vilari, even to foreigners, who learn to wait for the interpreter.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ilvenas Sarkelis is the Holy Mother of the [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]], the high priestess who sits in the [Mother's Hall]{.name data-type="place/structure"} at the summit of the holy city of [[place-nartum|Nartun]]. The high priests chose her from among themselves for life nineteen years ago. Eight years later she poured the oil on the forehead of [[being-klvrsdrns|Kelvaras Daronis]] and set the crown on his head after it, and she is the hand that wipes the oil away with ash if the high priesthood finds against him. The king's hall stands on the terrace below hers.

She confirms the Master of the [[affiliation-sacredflame|Order of the Sacred Flame]], and the Master answers to her. She does not command the temple treasuries, which belong to their temples, but no high priest lends a large sum without asking her first.

## Background

**The house of the temples.** The Sarkelis are among the oldest priest-noble families of the kingdom, and they have given it four Holy Mothers. Ilvenas was raised in the temple of [[affiliation-marendas|Marendas]], learned the ritual dialect and the [[skill-thrvrknscrpt|Tharavarkon]] runes there, and rose to be its high priestess at forty. She was a scholar before she was a ruler, and her commentary on the rite of anointing is read in the temple schools.

**The anointing.** The old king died without a son, and the high priesthood was divided between two cousins of the royal house. Ilvenas refused the elder for a winter and anointed the younger, Kelvaras. The elder cousin's friends have not forgotten it, and neither has Kelvaras, who knows that he owes his crown to a refusal and that she can refuse again.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is patient, exact and very hard to surprise. She listens more than she speaks, and when she speaks it is to put a question the speaker had hoped to avoid. She holds that the kingdom is the true heir of the old faith and the empire a corruption of it, and she says so to imperial envoys in the same courteous tone she uses for everything.

### Motivation

She wants the faith kept whole: the rites said in the old tongue, the Flame's word kept by its knights, and [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] given no room. She wants a king who rules as the anointing requires, and she wants it understood in Nartun and in [[place-orzara|Orzara]] alike that the crown comes from her hand.

### Strengths

She anoints, and she alone can take the anointing back. The high priests chose her, the Order answers to her, and the temples' schools teach her commentary. She is the most respected voice in matters of faith from the holy city to the commercial cities of the empire.

### Weaknesses

She cannot depose a king alone; she needs a majority of the high priests, and they have their own reasons. She governs a kingdom whose income is its forest, and the forest is dying in stands she cannot save by penance. She is sixty-six, and the high priests are already counting who would sit in her chair.

## Social

### Companions

- [[being-sldrsplvrn|Saldoros Pelvaren]]: Master of the Order of the Sacred Flame, whom she confirmed and who answers to her.

### Rivals

- [[being-klvrsdrns|Kelvaras Daronis]]: The king she anointed. They meet at every feast and speak little, and he never forgets which hall stands higher.
- [[being-vrthlszrkms|Varthelas Zerkemis]]: High Priest of Sélmoros, who presses for the king's judgment. She listens to him and has not said what she thinks.

### Enemies

- [[being-trzmsbrkrts|Torzemos Brakarates]]: High Flame-Warden of Viléria, at the Ashen Temple in Orzara. He preaches that she keeps the god's word without the courage to use it, and she has never spoken his name aloud.

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **The marriage blessing.** A widow from [[place-hylen|Hilen]] has come up the holy road to ask the Holy Mother's blessing on a marriage her family forbids. Ilvenas will hear her only if a party can show that the groom's family keeps the rites of the Twelve.
2. **The commentary.** A copy of her commentary on the anointing has reached Orzara with a passage that is not hers, saying that a Holy Mother may refuse a king for cause known to her alone. She wants the forger found before the high priests read it.
3. **The interpreter.** Her interpreter for imperial envoys has fallen ill, and the temple of Marendas needs someone who speaks both the old Vilari and the empire's for an audience that cannot be postponed. The envoy has come about the light imperial silver the House of Scales has been finding on the holy road.

:::secret {title="The Holy Mother's warrants and the claimant's envoys"}
Ilvenas signs the warrants. Every worshipper of the Black Flame whom the Order's knights carry out of the Hilen march by night is taken on a warrant in her hand, given to [[being-sldrsplvrn|Saldoros Pelvaren]] in the chapter house over the gate and kept there. The prisoners are judged by a temple examiner in closed session in the chapter house's undercroft. She has never told the king, and the high priests who know keep it from him, because he would stop it. A single imperial subject found in the undercroft would give the empire the quarrel it has wanted for five centuries.

The judgment is her lever as much as his danger. Several high priests want a king who would let the takings go on if he were told, and the king's elder cousin, [Ormelas Daronis]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, whom she refused, is that man. She has not decided whether to let the judgment come to a vote. If the king learns of the warrants, or reaches for Orzara, she will let it.

**The claimant's envoys.** This spring a priest of the Sacred Forge from Orzara came up the holy road as a pilgrim and asked for a private audience. He spoke for a claimant's house to the throne of Viléria: if Nartun's knights and its temple coin stand behind the claimant's adoption, the claimant will proscribe the Black Flame when he reigns, and the schism of 224 is healed. A second envoy came at midsummer with the same offer in writing, unsigned.

She has agreed to nothing. She knows that the Black Flame has patrons in the Seldan, officers in the legions and open temples in every commercial city of the empire, and that a promise to proscribe it is easy to make and very hard to keep. She has answered that the temples lend no coin to any throne before the proscription, and that she will hear of knights only when the claimant has sworn the proscription in the Mother's Hall, with his hand on the oil, before the high priesthood. No claimant has agreed to come.

**Hook: the second envoy.** The envoy who brought the written offer is lodged at the Charcoal Wagon under a merchant's name, and someone has searched his room. Ilvenas wants a party to learn who, without the king's sergeants learning why the envoy is in the city.
:::
