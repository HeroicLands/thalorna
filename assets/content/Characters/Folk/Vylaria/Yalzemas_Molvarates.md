# Appearance {#appearance}

Yalzemas Molvarates is a 44-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of medium build. She has tawny skin freckled across the nose, a round face with a small, sharp chin, black hair pinned up in coils and amber eyes that are always counting. She dresses in the bright, costly silks of the newly raised and wears them well. On a chain at her neck hangs a brass collection-tessera of the Fiscal Court, its contract number turned outward, which no other woman on Selvin would be seen wearing. Her voice is quick and cheerful, and she laughs at herself before anyone else can.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Yalzemas Molvarates is mistress of [House Molvarates]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, a _pelerenos_ (Elevated) house of [[place-orzara|Orzara City]] raised from the _tolari_ (tax-farmers) of [[affiliation-tolvan|the Tolvan]] within living memory. The house holds the contracts to collect the land tax across most of [[place-hylen|Hilen]], bid for each year against the Tolvan's estimates and paid for in advance into [[affiliation-tarmex|the Tarmeks]]. Every coin its clerks collect above the bid is the house's own. Its new villa on [[place-selvin|Selvin]] is one of the houses the old families call bought.

She wants the Hilen contract for ten years. She has offered the throne three years' bids in advance for it, paid the day the contract is sealed.

## Background

**Tolar-spawn.** Her grandfather was a commoner who bid for the customs of a river town and kept his clerks honest enough to be hated rather than hanged. Her father bought the house's Elevated standing from the throne. Yalzemas was his only child, kept his books from girlhood and rode the Hilen circuits with his clerks. When he died she was thirty-one, and three cousins and the Tolvan's _karvan_ (provincial officer) for Hilen expected the contracts to pass to someone else. They passed to her.

**The lost customs.** Three years ago the house bid for the customs of [[place-mercavia|Delalara]], the busiest harbor left to the empire. [Solteras Tharozates]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, of a rival house of tax-farmers, outbid her, and the house has held nothing on the coast since.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is cheerful, shrewd and entirely without shame about her trade. She enjoys the old houses' contempt, because it costs her nothing and tells her who will need to borrow from her next. She is generous to her people, merciless to defaulters and never cruel for its own sake. She thinks in contract-years, which is longer than most of Orzara thinks and much shorter than the land does.

### Motivation

She wants the ten-year contract. It would make the house the richest of the tax-farmers for a decade and put the next reign's Hilen revenue in her hands before the next reign begins. She wants it sealed by this Orzas, because a new reign will appoint a new Talkor of the Tolvan and reopen every contract.

### Strengths

The throne needs silver now, and she is offering it. Her clerks know every estate in Hilen and what it can bear. She is not afraid of anyone's opinion, and she cannot be shamed into anything.

### Weaknesses

She does not have three years' bids in hand. The Hilen estates hate her clerks, the old houses despise her name, and a province squeezed too hard rises against its tax-farmer first. The _rozimoi_ (auditors) of the Tolvan are prosecuting eight great tax-farmers this year, and her house is not yet among them.

## Social

### Patrons

She has none, and prefers it. The house bought its standing and owes nobody for it.

### Enemies

- [[being-nthrsmrdvns|Nethoras Mardovanes]]: Master of the Grain House, whose grain comes from the Hilen estates her clerks assess. A squeezed Hilen sells its grain dearer, and a ten-year Tolar on its estates is the last thing the Grain House wants.
- [[being-amrzvlds|Amarza Veldis]]: Senior partner of the House of Veldis, who refused to lend the house the advance and said why: the throne would be spending the next reign's money, and the House of Veldis expects to be lending to the next reign.
- [Solteras Tharozates]{.name data-type="being/npc"}: The tax-farmer who outbid her for the Delalara customs.

### Others

- [[being-drvnsrlln|Dorvanis Arelliani Septarius]]: The Imperial _Tarkelos_, who would receive the advance into the Tarmeks. He has said neither yes nor no.
- [[being-zrmlshlrkls|Zarmelas Helarkales]]: The Orzas, whose seal the contract needs. She has offered his secretary a gift for every month the contract lies at the top of his pile.

### Affiliations

- **House Molvarates**: mistress of the house, holding its contracts in her own name.
- **[[affiliation-tolvan|The Tolvan]]**: a _tolar_ under its contracts, holding the house's brass tesserae of collection; the Fiscal Court audits her and does not count her among its officers.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The tally-house.** Villagers in the Hilen hills have burned a Molvarates tally-house and driven out her clerks. She hires a party to go with her new clerks and see the next collection through without a death on either side.
2. **The Talkor's dinner.** The Talkor of the Tolvan, [[being-vldsorthmrs|Veldas Orthemaris]], is to dine on Selvin. Yalzemas wants a party at the table as her guests, to learn whether he means to approve the ten-year contract or leave it to the next reign.
3. **The village headman.** A headman of a Hilen village comes to Orzara to petition the Tolvan against the house's assessments, and Yalzemas asks a party to bring him to her villa first, unharmed, so she can make him an offer.

:::secret {title="Where the advance comes from"}
The three years' bids are Khelathi silver. When the House of Veldis refused her, Yalzemas went to the Strangers' Row, and [[being-mthszrtml|Methisi Azeret'Mulu]] agreed to lend the whole advance through a Helionite partner whose name will stand on the loan, with the ten-year contract itself pledged as security. If the contract is sealed, a Khelathi creditor holds a lien on the land tax of the empire's richest province for a decade, and the throne will have spent that decade's revenue before the next Orzas is crowned.

She knows what it is, and she has decided it is not her concern. The loan is lawful in form, the contract is the throne's to grant, and a Tolar who asked where every coin came from would never bid for anything.

She also suspects how Solteras Tharozates paid for Delalara. She has seen his clerks pass the Eastern Quarter's goods without opening them, and she believes he borrowed from the Taruzelian houses. She is right. If she can prove it before the Hilen contract is sealed, it ruins a rival and makes her own Khelathi loan look like patriotism by comparison.

**Hook: the rival's money.** Yalzemas pays a party to learn where Solteras Tharozates found the money for his Delalara bid, and to bring back something in writing.

**Hook: the Helionite partner.** The Helionite whose name stands on the loan has gone missing between Delalara and the capital, with the signed agreement. Yalzemas hires a party to find him before anyone opens his satchel.
:::
