# Appearance {#appearance}

Zenothen Gurimandis is a 69-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He has tanned, ruddy skin, brown hair thinned and gone gray at the edges, and amber eyes that crease when he listens. He wears the plain robe of a priest of [[affiliation-marendas|Marendas]] with a book on a cord at his belt, and there is always ink on the fingers of his right hand. He speaks slowly, asks short questions and lets silences run.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Brother Zenothen Gurimandis is a _dasken_ (ordained teacher) of the [[affiliation-marendas|Faith of Marendas]] at the [Temple of Wisdom]{.name data-type="place/structure"}, which stands on the east side of the forum of [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]] beneath the Keep. He hears the confessions of several scholars of the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]], and he is one of the few people with whom [[being-azatodmzns|Azato Damzanis]] speaks with anything like candor.

Once a month the two men meet, and the talk is of theology. Both of them know that it is about more than that. Zenothen has no illusions about Azato's capacity for warmth. He holds that a brilliant mind kept in balance goes on adding to what is known, and he counts the monthly talks as the work of keeping it there.

He is aging, and he has begun to train a young priest to take the confessions after him.

## Background

**The temple.** Zenothen has served at the Temple of Wisdom as a teacher, keeping its school and teaching the sons of the town's merchant houses their letters and their sums.

**The confessor.** The scholars of the Epistémion who live in Tirellan began to come to him because he understood their work and did not gossip about it. Azato is among them, and he comes every month.

**The successor.** Zenothen has chosen a young priest of the temple to follow him, and he is teaching him the scholars' confessions one by one.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is patient, dry and kindly, and very hard to shock. He listens more than he speaks.

### Motivation

He believes that learning is a service to the goddess, and that the learned need someone to keep them human. He wants the scholars who confess to him kept whole.

### Strengths

He knows the scholars of Tirellan better than they know one another. The temple and its school give him standing in the town, and his discretion is trusted.

### Weaknesses

He is old and tired, and he does not have long left to keep the confessions himself.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-azatodmzns|Azato Damzanis]]: The alchemist of the Order who comes to him monthly. Zenothen respects his mind and expects nothing of his heart.

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **The successor.** Zenothen's chosen successor is idealistic and warm, and worried for Azato's soul rather than his usefulness. Zenothen asks a party to keep an eye on the young man, who has begun to visit Azato's estate uninvited.
2. **The schoolroom.** A merchant house of Tirellan has taken its son out of the temple school after a quarrel with a border lord's son. Zenothen asks a party to carry his apology to both towers, because he wants the boy back.
3. **The book.** A volume has gone missing from the temple's shelf, a commentary on the essences of things that several scholars have asked to read this year. Zenothen wants it found without accusing any of them.
4. **The surveyor.** The clerk of the [[affiliation-imperialoffices|Imperial Offices]] measuring the province's road lodges near the forum and asks Zenothen for the temple's old maps. Zenothen asks a party to see that the clerk returns them.

:::secret {title="The confessor's purpose"}
Zenothen does not hear Azato's monthly talks for Azato's soul. He hears them because he believes Azato's detachment, left alone, would slip into something worse, and he means to stand in its way while he can. Azato understands this and comes anyway.

Zenothen knows more of Azato's mind than anyone in Tirellan. What Azato has told him, he keeps under the seal of the confession. He has not been told of the name Kuthstan Crey.

**Hook: the young priest.** If the successor takes the confessions, Azato faces the kind of human connection he has spent his life avoiding, and Zenothen is not sure whether that will steady him or break the balance he has kept.
:::
