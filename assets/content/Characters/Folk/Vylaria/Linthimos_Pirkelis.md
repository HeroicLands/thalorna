# Appearance {#appearance}

Linthimos Pirkelis is 62 years old. He is a narrow-shouldered man with silver hair, brown eyes and a slight tremor that settles when his hands rest on a desk. His wool coat is carefully brushed. He uses a ruler to keep his place in an account and wraps his seal separately from the keys.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Linthimos keeps the Yormalen house’s counting-room at [[place-messora|Messora]]. Between harvests he tends its accounts and stored papers; at the settling he works at the house’s stone table while the villages wait beneath the arcade. He reads a disputed line aloud slowly enough for the household concerned to repeat it.

He wants witnesses who belong to no great house to attend the opening of a strongbox in his room. He will explain the request, the payment and who must be absent, but will not explain the contents in the square. He regards independence as a practical requirement: witnesses who owe a factor a favor are no use to him.

## Background

**The household.** He spends his early working life in service to [Nizelas Yormalen]{.name data-type="being/npc"} and learns the estate’s records there. Freedom does not end his obligations to the house that grants it.

**The counting-room.** He becomes the man who remains in Messora when the harvest factors leave. He can find an old lease by the village’s description of a field even when the family has forgotten its written name.

**The new master.** Nizelas dies three years ago and his nephew [Revothas Yormalen]{.name data-type="being/npc"} holds the estate. Linthimos continues at the same table, owing service to a new patron and keeping papers from the old one.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is painstaking, formal and slow to accept reassurance. Once he decides what a document says, affection for its owner does not change his reading.

### Motivation

He wants an obligation to his late patron settled in a way that cannot simply be denied afterward.

### Strengths

He knows the house’s leases and can distinguish an original document from the copy made for a harvest table.

### Weaknesses

His livelihood and legal duties bind him to the same household whose permission he is trying to do without.

## Social

The town’s council is largely made up of men in positions like his. Their familiarity makes them useful neighbors and unsuitable independent witnesses. The tenant families know his voice more readily than his personal history.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: a freedman.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The strongbox.** Linthimos hires a party to attend its opening and keep a record of who is present. A house’s factor offers to supply witnesses of his own.
2. **The carried debt.** A tenant family disputes three years of carried debt at the Yormalen table. Linthimos asks the party to obtain the family’s old receipts before the settling ends.
3. **The absent notary.** A free farmer insists a deed records a sale he never agreed to. Linthimos can identify the hand but needs travelers to find the notary who has left the district.

:::secret {title="The second will"}
Nizelas’s final will frees some three hundred slaves and gives each family its holding at a fixed share, while leaving the estate to Revothas. The will read at the funeral is an older one. Revothas burns the estate’s copy of the later document, but Linthimos keeps the sealed copy entrusted to him with orders to hold it until asked. Nobody asks. After three years he means to open it before outsiders so it cannot be buried again. He has not decided what to do afterward; the families do not know they are free.
:::
