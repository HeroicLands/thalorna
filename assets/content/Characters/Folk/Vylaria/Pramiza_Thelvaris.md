# Appearance {#appearance}

Pramiza Thelvaris is a 64-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of heavy build. She has olive skin, dark brown hair going grey under a widow's veil, and brown eyes that miss nothing on a stage or off it. She wears a widow's dark gown with very good jewels, takes the best seat in any house without being shown to it, and laughs loudly at the places in a play where nobody else has dared to.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lady Pramiza Thelvaris is an immensely wealthy widow of [[place-orzara|Orzara]] and a patron of the arts. Her chosen cause is the playwright [[being-mkthsvnrds|Makithos Vanardis]], whose work divides the critics of the capital between those who call it overwrought pretension and those who call it peerless. She has championed him through all of it. She commissions his most ambitious productions, pays for them when nobody else will, and gives him the financial stability that his own temper would never give him.

The companies of the [[place-magntheatredstr|Theatre District]] live on their patrons, and with the succession open a company's patron is a political fact as much as a purse. Pramiza is among the largest purses in the district.

## Background

**The widow.** Pramiza is a widow, and the fortune her husband left her is immense.

**The playwright.** She took up Makithos while the critics were still divided over him, and she has been his patron since, commissioning his most ambitious productions and keeping him solvent between them.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is loud, shrewd, stubborn and generous, with a taste for the difficult and a contempt for timid art.

### Motivation

She wants the work she believes in seen and remembered, and she wants her name remembered with it.

### Strengths

She has money enough to mount any production in the capital, a seat in every house that matters, and the nerve to back a playwright the critics despise.

### Weaknesses

Her money is her influence, and it makes her a target for every flatterer and every rival patron. Her faith in Makithos is close to blind.

## Social

### Others

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **The impossible play.** Makithos has written a play so demanding that no company will mount it, and Pramiza has promised him a cast. She hires a party to find the actors, or to be them.
2. **The burned manuscript.** The only copy of Makithos's greatest work burns with his chambers. Pramiza wants to know whether it was an accident before she pays to rebuild anything.
3. **The critic's death.** The critic who made Makithos's name by attacking him dies in doubtful circumstances, and suspicion falls on the playwright. Pramiza hires a party to clear him, and she expects them to succeed.
4. **The jubilee commission.** The court commissions a jubilee play from Makithos, an honor with impossible terms. Pramiza wants a party to keep the court's men from rewriting it.
