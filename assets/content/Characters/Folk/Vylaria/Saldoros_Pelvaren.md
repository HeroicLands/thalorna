# Appearance {#appearance}

Saldoros Pelvaren is a 54-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of heavy build. He has fair skin weathered by years on the walls, close-cropped grey hair, and pale blue eyes, and an old lance scar crosses the bridge of his nose. He wears the white surcoat with the lamp on the breast over plain mail even in the chapter house, and walks with a stiff right knee that he does not favor in company. His voice is a parade-ground voice kept low indoors, and he listens with his arms folded.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Saldoros Pelvaren is the Master of the [[affiliation-sacredflame|Order of the Sacred Flame]], the knights of the [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]]. The council of elder knights chose him for life twelve years ago and the Holy Mother confirmed him. He lives in the chapter house over the main gate of the holy city of [[place-nartum|Nartun]], holds its walls and gates for the temples and the crown together, and commands the order's companies when they ride with the king's host.

In peace he answers to the Holy Mother and the high priesthood. His knights escort the temples' coin between treasuries, guard pilgrims on the holy road, and arrest at a priest's word those whom an examiner has summoned.

## Background

**A baron's younger son.** The Pelvaren are a family of lay barons who hold temple forest on the kingdom's western ridges. Saldoros was the second son, sent to the chapter house at fourteen as younger sons of barons are, and he kept his vigil at seventeen.

**The Two Lights.** He spent eleven years as Keeper of the Lamp at the chapel on the kingdom's bank of the border stream below [[place-ashenmere|Ashenmere]], facing the forge-shrine of [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] across the water. He came back to the holy city as the most respected elder knight of the order.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is plain-spoken, devout and loyal, and he takes orders from the priesthood without asking why. He likes his knights, dislikes the court, and is uneasy with anything he cannot settle with a sword or an oath.

### Motivation

He wants the Flame kept and the gate held, the order's whole rule, and he wants [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] gone from the world. He wants the order to stay out of the quarrel between the king and the high priesthood, and he does not expect it to.

### Strengths

He commands four hundred of the best heavy cavalry in the west, and he holds the only gate into the holy city. The knights trust him completely, and the Holy Mother trusts him nearly as much.

### Weaknesses

The order answers to two masters, the Holy Mother in peace and the king in war, and he cannot serve both if they quarrel. He is a soldier among priests and is out of his depth in the temple quarter's politics.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The coin road.** A temple's coin, carried under the order's escort from an upland estate, arrives at the House of Scales a twentieth light. Saldoros wants to know whether it was clipped on the road or on the estate, before the high priests ask him.
2. **The Forsworn knight.** A knight whose lamp was put out last year has been seen at an inn in Ashenmere, spending imperial silver. Saldoros wants him found and brought back, and will not say what oath he broke.
3. **The lamp.** On a still night the lamp over the main gate went out for the length of a prayer. The knight on watch swears he did not let it, and Saldoros wants to know who was on the stair.

:::secret {title="The Master and the warrants"}
Saldoros acts on the Holy Mother's warrants. Knights of the Ashenmere chapel ford the border stream by night and carry worshippers of the Black Flame out of the villages of the Hilen march, and he receives them in the chapter house over the gate, where a temple examiner judges them in closed session in the undercroft below the stables. Those who renounce the faith go to work out a penance on a temple-estate under new names. Those who refuse stay in the cells. The order's captains in the king's host are under his orders not to speak of the crossings in the king's hearing.

The names come from across the water. The Hammer-Priest of the forge-shrine at Ashenmere, [Huraden]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, holds the Black Flame to be the corruption of his own god's fire, and Saldoros knew him from his years at the chapel. Six years ago, as Master, he sent a knight to ask Huraden for names, and the chapel's signal followed: the lamp shuttered and shown three times after dark, and the next name sent back across the stream. He still keeps the warrants in his own strongbox. The knight of the Ashenmere chapel who rides most often at the ford is [Holvar Velthamen]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the younger son of [[being-ardmsvlthmn|Ardemos Velthamen]], and his father does not know.

The Holy Mother has asked him, in private, how many knights could ride to [[place-orzara|Orzara]] to stand behind a claimant to the empire's throne. He answered that none could while the gate stood, and he has not asked her why she wanted to know. He fears that she means to send them, and he fears more that the king will hear of it first.

**Hook: the knight who talks.** A knight of the Ashenmere chapel got drunk at the Bridge Lamp and told a carter that the slavers of the hills wear white. Saldoros wants the carter found and quieted before the customs officer hears it, and he is careful to say he means quieted with silver.
:::
