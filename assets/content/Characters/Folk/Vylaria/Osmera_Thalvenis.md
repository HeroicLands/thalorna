# Appearance {#appearance}

Osmera Thalvenis is a 62-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of medium build. She has medium skin, lined but smooth, white hair worn short under the plain hood of the priesthood of Árdavon, and brown eyes that she narrows behind a pair of crystal spectacles when she reads. A ring of old iron keys hangs at her belt and announces her down every corridor of the temple. She speaks precisely and without hurry, quotes the law of precedence from memory, and has a dry, sudden smile that she allows herself rarely.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Osmera Thalvenis is a Sentan, a Temple Judge, of the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]], and the senior of the Árdavan priests who keep [[place-pnthnrldnm|the Temple of the Twelve]] in the Vanor of [[place-orzara|Orzara City]] by ancient arrangement. She marshals the great calendar of festivals so that twelve priesthoods may each process to the center in their season, judges the precedences and jealousies of a building shared by a dozen clergies, and keeps the endowment the Seldan votes the Temple.

She keeps the Forge-Lord's niche for [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]]. At every accession the priests of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] petition the new Orzas for a place in the niche, and at every accession the Árdavan keepers answer that the Twelve have twelve niches and one fire in each. The next accession is near, the High Flame-Warden has his petition drafted, and Osmera will give the keepers' answer. No Orzas has overruled them, and she does not mean to be the keeper under whom one does.

## Background

**The judge.** The Thalvenis are an old priestly family of the capital, and Osmera was given to the temple of Árdavon at nine. She sat as a judge of oaths and contracts in the temple courts for twenty years and was named a Sentan at thirty-nine. She has kept the Temple of the Twelve for twenty-two years.

**The quarrel she inherited.** Her predecessor refused the Black Flame's petition at the last accession, before the Seldan and the new Orzas, and was dead within the year of a fever. The Ashen Temple's preachers called it a judgment. Osmera preached his funeral oration and has never forgiven them.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is exact, formal and unbending on a point of law, and kind to novices and petitioners. She believes in order as other priests believe in their gods: that a world held within its frames is a world that can be lived in. She dislikes noise, display and the Seldan.

### Motivation

She wants the niche kept for the Sacred Forge and the Black Flame kept outside, and she wants the next Orzas to confirm it without being asked. She would like the Temple's endowment voted without a fight for once.

### Strengths

She holds the keys of the most sacred building in the empire, and the Árdavan priesthood stands behind her. Every priesthood in the Vanor needs her for its feast days, and she knows the law of the Temple better than any magistrate.

### Weaknesses

The endowment depends on the Seldan, and the Black Flame's patrons there raise the niche at every vote. The Vanor's law forbids her any open act against the Black Flame, so she has only words and precedent. She is sixty-two, and the Árdavan Sarnal is already weighing her successor.

### Companions

### Enemies

- [[being-trzmsbrkrts|Torzemos Brakarates]]: High Flame-Warden of Viléria, at the Ashen Temple, who has the petition for the niche drafted and the patrons to press it.

### Others

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **Ash in the brazier.** A priest of the Sacred Forge who tends the Forge-Lord's niche finds black ash in its brazier at the morning rite. Osmera wants a party to learn who passed the sacristans' night watch before the Sacred Forge's priests hear of it.
2. **The feast-day reckoning.** The disc of light from the oculus fell on the wrong god at a feast-day rite, by the width of a hand. Osmera wants to know whether the reckoning was wrong or the floor has been tampered with, and she wants it known before the next feast.
3. **The endowment vote.** A senator who patronizes the Ashen Temple has promised proof that the keepers misspend the endowment. Osmera hires a party to learn what he thinks he has.

:::secret {title="The keeper's endowment and the keepers' candidate"}
The senator is right. For six years Osmera has paid part of the Temple's endowment, entered in its books as the upkeep of the Forge-Lord's niche, to the Sacred Forge's archimandrite [[being-ankhelossath|Ankelos Satheris]], who spends it on the counter-sermons on the Circles and the petitions to the city's magistrates. It is not theft by her lights: the niche is the Forge's, and the Forge's defense is its upkeep. The Seldan would not see it that way, and the Black Flame's patrons would use it to cut the endowment and the keepers together.

She has also chosen a candidate. Through a priest of Árdavon who confesses a claimant's household, she has let one claimant's house know that the keepers and the Sacred Forge will stand behind him if he answers the Black Flame's petition at his accession as every Orzas has. She does not know that a claimant's house has sent a priest of the Forge up the holy road to Nartun, and if she learns it she will want to know whether it is the same house.

**Hook: the upkeep account.** A clerk of the Temple's endowment has noticed that the niche's upkeep costs four times what the eleven other niches cost together, and has asked the Árdavan Sarnal for an audit. Osmera hires a party to learn which Sentan of the Sarnal received his letter, and what that Sentan means to do with it.
:::
