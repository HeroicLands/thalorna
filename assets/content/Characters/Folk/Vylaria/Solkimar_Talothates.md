# Appearance {#appearance}

Solkimar Talothates is 61 years old. He is broad through the shoulders, with thinning brown hair and a square face softened by heavy jowls. His clothes are carefully brushed and his cuffs carry no ink. He puts the papers for each trade in a separate pile, and straightens a pile before calling its representative to speak.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Solkimar is the Tevraks, First of the [[affiliation-tevran|Great College of Guilds]], whose council sits in [[place-orzara|Orzara]]. He holds the imperial seal of the office and presides over the council of its thirty-six chartered trade-categories. The throne appoints the Tevraks for a renewable ten-year term. Solkimar is in his eighth year, and the question of renewal reaches him through almost every case brought before the council.

He wants an institution that can enforce a charter without making every guild appeal to a rival court patron. A faction at court wants his successor to tighten the discipline of conquered-territory guilds. The Danvar’s charter review gives those arguments a particular case to gather around, and Solkimar must hear it without treating the bankers’ wealth as either proof of innocence or proof of guilt.

## Background

**The treasury desk.** Solkimar serves as a Vilérian treasury officer before his appointment. He learns to ask who can actually make a promised payment and what other account depends on it.

**The imperial seal.** Appointment brings the right to preside, not ownership of the trades. Each trade’s senior masters choose its council representative. He works with those representatives through disputes whose practical details they know better than he does.

**The eighth year.** Renewal is presumed by some and contested by others. He receives assurances through intermediaries and gives few in return. A decision made to purchase another term can leave a precedent that lasts much longer.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is patient with a complicated account and impatient with a flattering introduction. He remembers an undertaking in the words used to make it and brings those words back at inconvenient meetings.

### Motivation

He wants renewal on terms that leave the College able to judge its members’ charters rather than deliver a court faction’s verdicts.

### Strengths

Treasury work gives him a practical grasp of the money behind a guild’s argument. He can distinguish a sanction a trade can absorb from one that stops its work across several territories.

### Weaknesses

He tries to keep too many routes of compromise open. Officers who need an unequivocal instruction can spend days learning whether his hesitation is caution or opposition.

## Social

The senior auditor [Isolmera Vornelis]{.name data-type="being/npc"} brings cases whose rigor makes them difficult to set aside. Across the border the Vezakuni’s [[being-qizkshmglni|Qizikush Mugiluni]] runs an institution whose separate name the Vilérian College refuses to acknowledge. Solkimar cannot settle that diplomatic insult by a friendly letter of his own.

### Affiliations

- **Great College of Guilds**: a Kelros holding the office of Tevraks by imperial appointment.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The renewal witness.** A trade representative offers evidence that an audit is being steered to damage Solkimar’s renewal. He asks a party to verify the underlying transactions before he hears the representative’s charge.
2. **The charter’s reach.** A merchant carries credentials issued by the Vezakuni for a recovered Taruzelian craft. Solkimar needs witnesses to the actual work before a recognition dispute becomes a ruling on every recovered trade.
3. **The auditor’s journey.** A witness needed for the Danvar charter review refuses to enter Orzara without an independent escort. Solkimar can pay for the journey; he cannot promise what the council decides afterward.

:::secret {title="The shared investigation"}
A scandal involving smuggling, false credentials and murders crosses the Vilérian–Taruzelian border. Solkimar and Qizikush privately prefer a joint investigation, but neither can openly request one. A Helionite intermediary is trying a back channel and is being paid by both sides without either knowing. Solkimar needs usable evidence without a public concession that his opponents can turn into the renewal’s deciding charge.
:::
