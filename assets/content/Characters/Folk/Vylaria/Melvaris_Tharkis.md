# Appearance {#appearance}

Melvaris Tharkis is a 69-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of light build. He has fair, ruddy skin, dark brown hair cut close and gone grey at the temples, and green eyes. The backs of both hands are seamed with old burn scars. He moves like a younger man, wears a _marendos_'s dark robe belted for walking, and watches a room before he enters it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Melvaris Tharkis is a _marendos_ (master) of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] and one of the most respected practitioners of its chapter at [[place-velysara|Velisara]], where he holds a seat on the chapter's council. The chapter is famous across western Ankaris for its [[affiliation-pirethos|Pirethos]] tradition of fire-magic, and Melvaris is a veteran of that fellowship. He served for years as a field operative of [[affiliation-sekran|The Arcane Inquisition]] (the Sekran), whose regional office shares the chapter's precinct.

He was the master of [[being-klstrtsxrchds|Kalestratos Zarkedis]] through the boy's eight years as a _galethos_ (student), and he still guides his former student's career. He encourages Kalestratos's research into the old fire-magic before the Order, quietly and in a way he can deny.

## Background

**The fellowship.** Melvaris trained in the Pirethos at Velisara and rose to _marendos_ in it.

**The Sekran.** The Pirethos sends more of its members to the Sekran than any other fellowship, and Melvaris was one of them. He learned the physical conditioning and the field-craft the Sekran asks of its operatives, and he came back to the chapter with both.

**The student.** When Kalestratos was advanced to _galethos_ at fifteen, the chapter gave him to Melvaris. Melvaris saw talent and anger in him, the mix that makes either a mage or a disaster, and trained him to channel the fire rather than smother it: rigorous Pirethos theory with the tactics of a Sekran field operative.

**The council.** Melvaris sits on the chapter's council, where a quarrel touching one of its members is decided, and where Kalestratos's research is a matter he has to manage.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is calm, watchful and sparing with praise. He teaches by setting harder tasks, and he does not explain himself.

### Motivation

He sees greatness in Kalestratos and he sees the danger in him, and he is not certain which will win. He wants to be there when it is decided.

### Strengths

His standing on the chapter council, his long service in the Sekran and his friends in its regional office give him room to protect a student and to know what the inquirers are watching.

### Weaknesses

His support for Kalestratos's research can be denied only while nobody looks closely, and the research grows harder to hide.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-klstrtsxrchds|Kalestratos Zarkedis]]: His former student, a field _therman_ (licensed practitioner) of the chapter, whose independence and research into forbidden techniques make complications Melvaris has to manage.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]**: a _marendos_, with a seat on the council of the Velisara chapter.
- **[[affiliation-pirethos|Pirethos]]**: a master of the fire fellowship, and its veteran at Velisara.
- **[[affiliation-sekran|The Arcane Inquisition]]**: a veteran field operative, still known to its regional office.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The council's question.** The chapter council asks Melvaris to report on Kalestratos's research. He asks a party to learn what the Sekran's office across the precinct already knows, before he writes a word.
2. **The courier.** A courier from the capital carries sealed offers to Kalestratos at the chapter. Melvaris wants to know who is writing to his former student, and asks a party to find out without opening the letters.
3. **The training ground.** A _kosthemos_ (newly inducted student) of the chapter loses control of a fire on the training ground, and the boy's family blames his master. Melvaris asks a party to carry the chapter's answer to them.
4. **The old forge.** Kalestratos has found references to a forge of the old fire-mages hidden beneath Velisara. Melvaris hires a party to go down with him, and to bring him back.

:::secret {title="What Melvaris has not told his student"}
Kalestratos has learned of a sanctum where a conclave of Helionite fire-mages kept research too dangerous for their own cities, before the Order was founded. Melvaris knows more of where it lies than he has told Kalestratos, and he has kept it back for reasons of his own, and they are not the reasons Kalestratos would guess.

Lady [[being-iltherethalu|Ilthere Thalurates]] also knows of the sanctum and wants its secrets for her own purposes, and her written offer travels to Velisara like the others. She offers Kalestratos the means to reach it, at the price of his loyalty to her house.

**Hook: the master's map.** If Kalestratos accepts Ilthere's offer, Melvaris hires a party to reach the sanctum before either of them, and gives them the directions he never gave his student.
:::
