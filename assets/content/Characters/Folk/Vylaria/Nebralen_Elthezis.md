# Appearance {#appearance}

Nebralen Elthezis is a 54-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of heavy build. He has medium skin weathered by thirty years of sun and spray, black hair cut short, a short black beard going gray at the chin, and green eyes with deep lines at their corners. He wears an old sea-coat and a wide hat, and he moves about his deck slowly and without wasted effort. He speaks quietly, asks few questions and smiles more than a ship's master usually does.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nebralen Elthezis is master of the merchant ship _[Tempest's Mercy]{.name data-type="vehicle"}_, out of [[place-mercavia|Delalara]]. He carries cargo for the shipping houses and for anyone else who pays, and when his holds are light he takes passengers, asking no more questions of them than they ask of him. A party with business in the Eastern Quarter that wants to leave Delalara quietly is likely to be sent to him.

The waterfront knows him as the captain who found a starving girl of seven stealing bread from the docks and, instead of turning her over to the watch, gave her work aboard his ship. That girl is [[being-grmdnshrndns|Goremadanis Herendanis]], now twenty-two and one of the best young hands in the city. He has refused several offers to buy her contract, and he insists that she stay free to choose the ships she works.

## Background

**The Tempest's Mercy.** Nebralen has sailed out of Delalara all his working life, and he has commanded the _Tempest's Mercy_ for most of it. He is a careful master rather than a bold one.

**The girl on the docks.** Fifteen years ago he caught Goremadanis, newly orphaned, stealing bread on the quays. He took her aboard to clean, haul and run messages, and the crew raised her. By sixteen she was helping him pilot, and by nineteen she could navigate by the stars alone.

**The present.** He still sails the _Tempest's Mercy_, hires Goremadanis whenever he has need of her, and lets her work other ships when he does not.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is kind, unhurried and discreet. He believes that a person is more than what she did when she was hungry, and he acts on it. He is slow to anger and very hard to move once angry.

### Motivation

He wants Goremadanis safe and free, and he wants her to have the ship of her own she dreams of. He wants his own ship to pay her way, which in Delalara's dues and debts is never certain.

### Strengths

He is a sound seaman with a loyal crew, and the masters and harbor boats of Delalara trust him. His discretion makes him the captain people go to when they need to leave without a fuss.

### Weaknesses

He is getting old, his ship is not new, and he cannot be on the quays every night. His protection of Goremadanis has made him an enemy of a dangerous man.

## Social

### Enemies

- [Devorin]{.name data-type="being/npc"}: A slaver who works from a hidden dock in Delalara and has tried more than once to take Goremadanis. Nebralen has kept her out of his hands so far.

### Others

- [[being-grmdnshrndns|Goremadanis Herendanis]]: The orphan he took off the docks and raised aboard his ship, who owes him her life and her trade and is fiercely loyal to him.
- [[being-talotharzeno|Talothar Zenothenos]]: The harbor-master of Delalara, who gives the _Tempest's Mercy_ her berth.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-provincvld|Province of Salkalon]]**: a palvethos (resident without the citizenship) of the province.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Passage out.** A party that needs to leave Delalara without the Customs House or the admirals' men noticing is pointed to Nebralen. He takes them, and on the second night out tells them who else is aboard.
2. **The missing child.** A sailor's child has gone missing from the waterfront, and Goremadanis is sure it is Devorin's work. Nebralen will not let her go looking alone, and he asks a party to go with her.
3. **The offer for her contract.** A respectable agent offers Nebralen a sum for Goremadanis's contract that would clear his ship's debts. He refuses, and the agent says the offer will come again, larger.
4. **The convoy.** Nebralen has a cargo for the islands and cannot afford a place in a convoy under the [[place-straitfortress|Strait Fortress]]. He asks a party to sail as guards for the run through the channels.

:::secret {title="Who wants her contract"}
Nebralen is sure that the offers for Goremadanis's contract come from Devorin, made through agents with clean hands, because no honest master would pay what they offer for a hired hand. He has not told Goremadanis, who would go looking for Devorin herself. He has also not told the watch, because he has no proof, and because a captain who accuses an agent of a house without proof loses his cargoes. He knows that the next offer is the last one Devorin will make with money.
:::
