# Appearance {#appearance}

Thalrith ira Erthwald wur Stennvold is 58 years old. He is broad, slow-spoken and gray-haired, with dark eyes narrowed against furnace glare. His heavy coat carries scorch marks at the hem. When he examines a sample he waits for it to cool, however impatiently its owner watches.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Thalrith owns three furnaces at [[place-stennvold|Stennvold]] and speaks for the smelt-masters to [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]]. Families work the furnaces in crews, paid at the end of each charge, and their loyalty rests on the master who actually pays them. He settles disputes between crews before they reach a town magistrate.

He wants the charcoal road safe and the wages met, but a furnace stands cold and the province’s buyer wants it burning before the next convoy. He has no public explanation to offer for its master’s refusal. The town is also burying children after the winter cough, and the crews’ families increasingly bring questions that an argument over a charge cannot answer.

## Background

**The furnace.** He learns the work among the kindreds who speak the border tongue at the fires and Vilari at the counting-house. A good charge depends on men who trust the next shift to take over.

**The three fires.** Ownership makes him responsible for ore, fuel and wages together. He gains his place among the masters by keeping crews paid through interruptions in supply.

**The envoy.** As the town’s voice to Tirellan he must explain delays without accepting every price the province offers. The stalled furnace brings the buyer to him for an answer on behalf of the town.

## Psyche

### Personality

He hears a worker out and answers slowly. He dislikes shouting across a furnace yard and can make his refusal more intimidating by keeping his voice low.

### Motivation

He wants his crews and household to survive a trade on which the whole valley depends.

### Strengths

He understands the furnaces, the costs of a charge and the arguments that provincial buyers will hear.

### Weaknesses

He is accustomed to settling difficult choices privately and presenting only the result. That habit makes honest questions from his crews feel like challenges to his authority.

## Social

The other smelt-masters rely on him in dealings with the province. The furnace families expect him to protect their wages, and the buyers expect metal on the convoy day.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: of the propertied order.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The cold furnace.** The provincial buyer hires a party to discover why a furnace stands cold. Thalrith offers an introduction to the counting-house but refuses to promise a relighting on the town’s behalf.
2. **The charcoal road.** Fuel carriers are robbed three times in a season. Thalrith pays for an escort and wants the thieves identified before the shortage closes more fires.
3. **The children’s cough.** A crew’s mother seeks help against what she believes is a rival kindred’s curse. Thalrith asks the party to hear the families before making accusations in the yard.

:::secret {title="The assayer’s warning"}
An assayer tells Thalrith that the ore from [[place-ferrovan|Zargonara]]’s old galleries releases a dangerous metal in roasting smoke and slag. He knows the smoke causes the children’s cough. He moves his household to clean spring water and sends his grandchildren to relatives in [[place-pinnhald|Pinnhald]], but keeps silent because the mine lessees pay him to add bought silver to the melt. His cold furnace roasts the worst ore, and he cannot bring himself to relight it. The crews would put out the fires if they knew; their wages and the province’s silver depend on the work continuing.
:::
