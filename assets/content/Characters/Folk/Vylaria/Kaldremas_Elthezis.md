# Appearance {#appearance}

Kaldremas Elthezis is a 74-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of light build, and he has grown thinner over the winter than his clothes allow for. He has light skin gone wan and creased, white hair kept short by his household, and grey eyes. He is seldom seen outside the house on the north hill at Vennara, and the few who are let in find him in a chair by the window in a senator's gown that hangs loose on him. His seal is a ram above a wheatsheaf.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kaldremas Elthezis is the _therkos_ (governor) of the [[affiliation-provinclys|Province of Alessa]], a _kurthos_ (senator) of an old house, and he has held the province for eleven years. In law the province is his: he holds it in the emperor's name, commands its garrison, its revenue and its courts, and answers to the Orzas alone. Its acts are sealed with his ring and read out from the steps of the [Governor's House]{.name data-type="place/structure"} at [[place-alyssar|Alessar]].

He has not been seen in Alessar since the spring. He has kept to his villa on the north hill at [[place-vennara|Vennara]] since the winter, which no great man does, while the town's other villas stood shuttered around it. Couriers come up from Alessar and go down again sealed, and his freedman secretary, [[being-nethoren|Nethoren]], reads the acts from the steps the morning after they arrive. His steward buys for the house in the town as though for a sickroom: broth, linen, lamp oil and the physician's wine. Vennara has noticed and says so only among itself, and in Alessar the province is said to be governed by an empty chair.

## Background

**The province.** He was given Alessa eleven years ago, the heartland province that holds the imperial capital within its bounds and does not govern it. Its _karezos_ (revenue officer) reports past him to the emperor, as every _karezos_ does, and the two men seldom agree.

**The villa.** He keeps a house on the north hill at Vennara, the villa-town in the hills where the court and the senatorial families spend their summers. He went up to it last year and did not come down for the winter.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is old, and the house on the north hill holds him. His household speaks of him carefully and to nobody outside it.

### Motivation

He keeps to his villa and receives no one, and his household speaks for him.

### Strengths

His name and his office still carry the province: every act sealed with his ring is obeyed from Alessar to the northern bench at [[place-skoraz|Soraks]].

### Weaknesses

His health is failing, and the province has learned to do without him.

:::secret {title="The seal without its holder"}
Kaldremas Elthezis is dying slowly in the house on the north hill. Most days he does not know where he is, and on the days he does he asks for his seal-ring, which has been in Alessar since the spring in Nethoren's keeping. He has not sealed an act himself since then. The couriers carry up letters he does not read, and they carry down the sealed packets that Nethoren made up in Alessar and sent up with them, so that the road sees every act of the province come down from Vennara. Nethoren rides up once a month and sits with him. The steward and the physician know.

In law an act sealed without the holder of the seal is a forgery, so every judgment, lease and assessment Alessa has issued since the spring can be challenged by anyone who proves that the _therkos_ never saw it.

The _karezos_ at Alessar knows, because his own assessments for the year went out under the same seal, and he has paid the steward to send him word the day the _therkos_ dies, a day before anyone else. A day is enough to clear the Governor's House of anything the next _therkos_'s staff should not read.

**Hook: the day's warning.** A party hired into a Vennara household for the summer is close enough to the north hill to see the steward's word go down to Alessar on the day the _therkos_ dies. Whoever reaches the Governor's House in that day decides what the next _therkos_'s staff finds there.
:::
