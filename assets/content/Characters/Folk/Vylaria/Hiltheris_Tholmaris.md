# Appearance {#appearance}

Hiltheris Tholmaris died at fifty-two, a woman of 5'8" and medium build. In life she had fair skin browned by the sun, auburn hair and brown eyes, and she wore the plain dark robe of a _marendos_ (master) of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] with its sleeves pushed up for work.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hiltheris Tholmaris was a _marendos_ of the Thordimalon (the Order) and a scholar of mineral essence. Her theories were the ones [[being-azatodmzns|Azato Damzanis]] took apart in public as "fundamentally confused about the nature of mineral essence", and the quarrel between them was the talk of the Order for a season. She died soon after, in circumstances never fully cleared. The death prompted an inquiry by [[affiliation-sekran|The Arcane Inquisition]] (the Sekran) that touched Azato at its edge, and the inquiry was set down without a finding.

She belongs in play as a name in other people's files: the Sekran's, the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]]'s records, and Azato's memory.

## Background

**The bench.** Hiltheris rose through the Order to the rank of _marendos_ and a vote in her chapter's council. Her work was on the essences of minerals, the field Azato also worked, and for a time she and Azato worked it together.

**The rupture.** The partnership ended in public. Azato published his demolition of her theories, and the Order read it.

**The death.** She died sixteen years ago. The Sekran's inquiry into the death touched Azato, found nothing it could prove, and was closed. Azato moved his laboratory to a rented estate outside [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]] and took an alias for his private work soon after.

## Psyche

### Personality

The record keeps little of her temper. She held her theories firmly enough to defend them against Azato in public, and she kept pupils.

### Motivation

She wanted the essences of minerals understood, and she wanted the credit for understanding them.

### Strengths

She had a master's standing in the Order and a vote in her chapter.

### Weaknesses

She trusted her partner, and she lost the argument with him in front of the Order.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-azatodmzns|Azato Damzanis]]: Her partner in the work and then her public critic, the last colleague she quarreled with before her death.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]**: a _marendos_, with a vote in her chapter's council, until her death.
- **[[affiliation-sekran|The Arcane Inquisition]]**: the inquirers who looked into her death, and whose file on it is set down rather than closed.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The old records.** An apprentice of the Epistémion finds references in old records to a project Hiltheris and Azato Damzanis worked on together before their public rupture, and asks innocent questions about it. A party asked to find the rest of the records finds that someone has been asking for them before.
2. **The pupils.** A former pupil of Hiltheris, now a _therman_ (licensed practitioner), wants to republish her mistress's theory with a defense of it, and hires a party to find Hiltheris's working notes, which went missing at her death.
3. **The grave.** Hiltheris's family asks a party to escort her remains from a temporary tomb to the family's own, and the Sekran's notary asks to be present when the tomb is opened.

:::secret {title="The shared project and the dormant file"}
Before their quarrel, Hiltheris and Azato worked together on a project that neither of them entered in the Order's records. The references the apprentice finds are to that work. Azato's public demolition of her theories came after it, and what the work was, and why it ended in a rupture, is known only to Azato.

The Sekran's file on her death is not closed. Its agents keep their suspicions of Azato, and his name is in the file. Under the Sekran's rule a _holsekeros_ (senior inquirer) may open a dormant file again when a name in it turns up in another, and [[being-ronethoszurm|Ronethos Zurmelates]], who is looking into thefts from the Epistémion's mineral stores, keeps crossing Azato's path. If Azato's name enters Ronethos's file, her file can be opened again.

**Hook: the notary at the tomb.** If the tomb is opened, the Sekran's notary takes samples from the remains. A party that learns what the notary is looking for learns that the Sekran has not set aside the question of how Hiltheris died.
:::
