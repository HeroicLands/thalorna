# Appearance {#appearance}

Zetima Kolsamis is a 31-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of light build. She has light skin with a summer's colour on it, brown hair cut short and plain, and level grey eyes. She wears the unadorned robe of a priest of the order-god and carries the temple's witness-book under her arm, and the middle finger of her writing hand is stained with its ink. She speaks quietly and exactly, and she waits for an answer.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zetima Kolsamis is a _volsar_, an ordained priest of the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]], and the keeper of the god's temple in the holy city of [[place-nartum|Nartun]], one of the twelve that ring its hill. She witnesses oaths and contracts, keeps the temple, and performs its ceremonies of blessing and of condemnation.

She holds that the work of the alchemist [[being-dmrthsvshrds|Domrithas Vishardas]] corrupts the natural order, and she has declared his search for a way past death heretical. She works to discredit him and to protect the people he would use. She cannot judge him herself: in Nartun heresy is judged by a temple examiner, who answers to the high priesthood, and a heretic with a baron's protection is a hard case to bring.

## Background

**The threshold.** Zetima trained as an Acolyte of the Threshold, learning the god's law before she could witness under it, and she was ordained young.

**The temple.** She came to keep Árdavon's temple in Nartun, where order is the god's whole teaching and the priests of every other temple bring their contracts to be witnessed.

**The alchemist.** She came to know of Domrithas's work through the people it harmed, and she has spent the years since gathering what she needs to bring it before an examiner.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is grave, careful and unbending, and she believes that every thing has its place and its end. She is patient with the frightened and has no patience with the powerful who think the law is for others.

### Motivation

She wants Domrithas's work stopped and condemned, and the people it has hurt protected and heard.

### Strengths

She knows the law, she is trusted as a witness by every temple in the quarter, and she keeps careful records. Her temple's word on a contract is final.

### Weaknesses

She has no power to arrest or judge, and the alchemist's patron is a baron. She is young for her place, and the older priests think her zeal unseemly.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-dmrthsvshrds|Domrithas Vishardas]]: The alchemist whose search for deathlessness she has declared heresy.
- [[being-kalmethartha|Kalmethar Tharkis]]: The baron who keeps the alchemist and shields him from the guild and from her.

### Others

- [Akethrynas]{.name data-type="being/npc"}: The guild-master of the Alchemist Guild of Nartun, who has spent years gathering evidence against Domrithas for his own reasons. Their ends meet, and neither trusts the other.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]]**: a _volsar_, keeper of the god's temple in Nartun.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The witness.** Zetima needs a witness who has seen inside Domrithas's workshop to swear before an examiner. She asks a party to find one who will live long enough to testify.
2. **The contract.** A merchant brings a contract to be witnessed at her temple, and Zetima sees the alchemist's name among its parties. She asks a party to learn what is being bought.
3. **The fugitive.** A frightened man asks sanctuary at the temple door, saying he has run from a house in the city where people are kept. Zetima shelters him and asks a party to find where he came from.
4. **The guild-master's offer.** The guild-master of the alchemists offers Zetima his evidence if she will put her temple's name to the charge, and she asks a party to learn what the guild gains if Domrithas falls.

:::secret {title="The widow's sanctuary"}
The witnesses Zetima is looking for exist. The widow [Selene]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who was Domrithas's lover until she understood what he does, keeps a secret network that hides the people who have escaped his experiments, and she trusts no priest with them, because a baron's men can follow a priest. A party that wins Selene's trust can bring Zetima the testimony she needs; a party that leads the baron's men to Selene's door ends the network.

Once the guild-master's evidence and a living witness reach an examiner together, Domrithas has three roads: to flee and lose his workshops, to defend himself against evidence he cannot answer, or to hurry his work toward a result great enough to protect him.
:::
