# Appearance {#appearance}

Kolvenas Mardovanes is a 46-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. He has light skin, brown hair cut short in the court fashion and hazel eyes, and the heavy Mardovanes jaw, narrower than his brother's. He is clean-shaven and holds himself very straight, like a man who has spent years being looked at from a magistrate's chair. He wears the senator's stripe and the gilded sheaf of his house, and over them, on state days, the purple border a former _tavos_ (chief magistrate) keeps for life. A thin white scar crosses the back of his left hand. He speaks slowly and well, in the full periods of the Seldan, and pauses before he answers a question as if he were weighing it in public.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kolvenas Mardovanes is the younger brother of [[being-nthrsmrdvns|Nethoras Mardovanes]], master of [[affiliation-mardovanes|House Mardovanes]], and a _kurthos_ (senator) of the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]] in his own right, by the offices he has held. He has been _tavos_, the chief magistrate of a year, and he governed the province of [[place-hylen|Hilen]] for three years after it. He lives in his brother's house on [[place-selvin|Selvin]], in the wing over the terraces, and keeps his own morning call there.

He is the man the court expects to be named. The old Orzas, [[being-zrmlshlrkls|Zarmelas Helarkales]], has adopted nobody, and the house has a precedent no other house can show: the Orzas Azénales was a Mardovanes whom an heirless Orzas adopted into House Helarkales a century ago. The Senate's majority wants an orderly succession and a man it knows, and Kolvenas is the man it knows best. The Hilen houses want an Orzas who will keep the grain trade as it is, and the Grain House's own brother is the surest of those. He has promised [[affiliation-sorvan|the Imperial Guard]] a donative on the day of his adoption, as every expected heir does, and has paid nothing on the promise.

The court has expected a man to be named before. The old Orzas was himself adopted in the last winter of a dying man's life, a careful choice and not a loved one, and Kolvenas knows the story as well as anyone on the Heights.

## Background

**The second son.** He was raised for the Seldan while his brother was raised for the grain. He studied law at the University under the best _marendoi_ (masters) his father could buy, spoke his first oration at twenty-two and was the youngest _vornos_ (magistrate of the courts) of his year. The old houses, who would not sit at his father's table, came to his court because his judgments held on appeal.

**The magistrate and the governor.** He was _tavos_ ten years ago, the year of [[lore-commissioncmp|the Commission Compromise]], and he presided over the sitting of the Seldan that voted it, leaving the purchased commissions untouched after the Taruzelian war. He governed Hilen for three years after his year, from [[place-aravantia|Aravantara]], and came home with a reputation for clean accounts, which very few governors bring home. He knows the master of [[affiliation-ganothandis|House Ganothandis]] from those years, and the Hilen houses speak of him as their own.

**The Olkassa match.** He made his niece's marriage. It was Kolvenas who opened the talks with Ganothandis while he governed the province, and he signed the betrothal contract for Varelas Mardovanes as the house's witness. It joins the house that sells the capital its bread to the house that grows the best of it. He was married once, to a daughter of a Silver house, and has been a widower for nine years, with one son at the University.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is careful, correct and slow to commit, and he is proud of all three. He reads every paper put in front of him and asks for the one that was left out. He is courteous to everyone and warm to very few, and he has none of his brother's ease at a dinner. He believes in the forms of the Seldan, the order of precedence and the law as written, because they have always served him.

### Motivation

He wants the adoption, and he wants it on terms he can defend in the Seldan afterward. He wants the empire's credit kept and its forms respected.

### Strengths

The Senate trusts him, and its majority will vote whatever an adoption needs if the adopted man is Kolvenas. He has the Hilen houses, the grain and his brother's dinners behind him. He knows the law of adoption and the precedents of every succession since the Restoration better than the Orzas's own secretaries.

### Weaknesses

He has no coin of his own. Every promise he makes is paid, if it is paid, by his brother. He is the Grain House's man in the eyes of every rival, and every grievance against the house is laid at his door. The legions do not know him, and the Guard has heard his promise and seen no silver.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The guardian's escort.** Kolvenas hires a party to escort his niece's betrothal gifts up the river road to the Ganothandis townhouse at Aravantara, and to bring back the signed acknowledgement before the next sitting of the Seldan.
2. **The precedent.** A scribe of the Offices claims to have found the original record of Azénales's adoption, with a clause no copy carries. Kolvenas pays a party to buy it before another claimant's household does, and to say nothing of its contents until he has read them.
3. **The officers' dinner.** Kolvenas sends a party to a supper of Guard officers in a cookshop below the Guard Camp, as his steward's guests, to learn which watch-captains still speak of his promise and which have stopped.
4. **The mask's wearer.** At an Orzas's funeral, men of the great houses walk in the masks of the divine Orzoi, and the Keeper of the Masks chooses them. Kolvenas wants a Mardovanes to walk in Azénales's mask at the old Orzas's funeral, and hires a party to learn what the Keeper will want for it.

:::secret {title="The expected man's empty purse"}
Beneath the goals he avows, he wants to be his own man: the brother who reigns and not the brother whom the Grain House set on the throne.

The donative is unpaid because the house will not pay it. Nethoras has told his brother that the Grain House pays for a thing when it is delivered, and an adoption deed is not delivered until it is sealed. Kolvenas cannot pay it himself. He does not know that the house's silver is tied to the forward sale of three Olkassa harvests, or that the Tarmeks has advanced a third of the price on grain not yet grown. He believes his brother is simply making him wait, and he resents it more each month.

So he has gone elsewhere. Twice this year he has been carried to the Strangers' Row after dark, in a closed chair with no badge, to see [[being-mthszrtml|Methisi Azeret'Mulu]]. He has asked the Khelathi factor to lend him the donative, in silver, against the grain contract of his own reign: a fleet a year from the delta, on Khelathi credit, the very thing his brother calls the first sack of a siege. Methisi has not refused. He has asked what lien the next Orzas could give that the Orzas after him could not repudiate, and Kolvenas has not yet answered. If the Heights learn of it, the Grain House turns on its own claimant, and the veterans' society on the River Circle that means to kill the factor will have a second name.

He also believes he has been chosen. The Orzas's secretary, for a gift, has told him that an adoption deed in his name lies drafted in the strongbox. That is true. The secretary did not tell him that two other deeds lie beside it, each in a different name, and none of the three is sealed.

He has given [[being-krznsthlrts|Korzenas Thalurates]] no answer about marrying again. He knows exactly what the question means: Korzenas wants a daughter married to the next Orzas, and would carry the Senate's doubtful votes as her dowry.

**Hook: the closed chair.** A chair-bearer of the Strangers' Row gate has noticed that the same closed chair is let through after the gate is locked, and that the Watch sergeant on duty is paid to look away. He offers a party the night of the next visit, and the name of the man inside, to whoever pays most.
:::
