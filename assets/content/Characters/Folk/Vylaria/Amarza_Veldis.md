# Appearance {#appearance}

Amarza Veldis is a 58-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of light build. She has olive skin, a narrow face with a long straight nose, gray hair dressed plainly under a dark veil, and dark brown eyes that rest on a document longer than on a face. She dresses in black wool of the best quality and wears no jewelry but the gold balance-scale pin of a great house's senior partner at her throat. There is ink on the second finger of her right hand. She speaks softly and precisely, and she never raises her voice to be heard over a room; she waits for it to fall quiet.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Amarza Veldis is the senior partner of the [House of Veldis]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"}, one of the great houses of [[affiliation-danvar|the Bankers' Guild]]. The house is Vilérian and conservative, with old ties to the _ethlen_ (Gilded) estate and to the Dukran's office, and it banks the commercial cities of the empire. Its chapter accounts stand at [[place-orzara|Orzara]], [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] and the river towns, and its notes, sealed in the house's mark, pass at par from [[place-hylen|Hilen]] to the sea.

She holds a larger block of the throne's borrowed paper than any other lender in the empire. The throne pays the veterans' pension with loans from the Danvar's great houses and rolls them over as they fall due ([[affiliation-tarmex|The Tarmeks Orzan]]), and the Veldis loans are the largest of them. Every quarter, when the loans fall due, the Imperial _Tarkelos_ comes to the house on Torvel, and every quarter Amarza names her price for rolling them on.

## Background

**The house.** The Veldis have banked in Orzara for seven generations. Amarza was the second daughter of the senior partner and the first of his children to understand the chapter books, and she kept them from the age of sixteen. Her brother inherited the partnership and died of a fever in his fifth year of it. The partners chose her over two cousins, and in twenty-two years none of them has regretted it aloud.

**The throne's lender.** The house lent to the throne before her time, as every great house does, because Viléria's credit is excellent and a loan to the throne is the safest paper in the commonwealth. She made the house the throne's largest lender in the years after the Taruzelian war, when the war's charges and the pensions of a generation of discharged men came due together and the smaller houses grew nervous. She lent when they would not, and the throne remembers it.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is cool, courteous and unhurried, and she treats every conversation as a negotiation whether or not anyone else in it knows. She keeps her word to the letter and rarely gives it. She has no taste for display and a great taste for detail, and she reads every contract she signs, including the ones her clerks have already read.

### Motivation

She wants the riders on the next rollover: the Danvar's own _rozimoi_ (auditors) kept out of the great houses' chapter books and the throne's share of the 2% fee cut for the houses that carry its debt. She wants an heir who owes the House of Veldis his throne. She does not want the throne to default, and she does not want the city to burn, because the house's fortune is the throne's debt.

### Strengths

The throne cannot pay the pension without her loans. Her house's notes are trusted from the Wharves to the sea, and the other great houses follow where she leads at the Council of the Danvori. She is patient, she is never surprised in public, and she has outlasted three Danvori Maldor and a generation of court favorites.

### Weaknesses

Her fortune is the throne's debt, so she is the one creditor who cannot afford to press it to the end. A default, a riot or a reign that repudiates its predecessor's paper would end her house. The Orzas knows this, and so does every rival who would like to see her replaced.

## Social

### Patrons

The house has no patron, and is patron to many: smaller houses that clear through it and Silver families whose debts it carries.

### Enemies

- [[being-krznsthlrts|Korzenas Thalurates]]: Master of House Thalurates, who buys the debts of old houses and would like to buy the throne's. She regards him as a clerk with ambitions and watches him more closely than anyone else in the city.
- [[being-mthszrtml|Methisi Azeret'Mulu]]: The Khelathi factor, who offers the throne silver for its paper. Every coin he lends is a coin the throne need not borrow from her.
- [[being-nthrsmrdvns|Nethoras Mardovanes]]: Master of the Grain House, who wants a duty on foreign grain. She opposes it in the Council of the Danvori and in every drawing room on the Heights, because a duty raises bread, and the riots that follow dear bread end at the chapterhouse doors.

### Others

- [[being-zrmlshlrkls|Zarmelas Helarkales]]: The Orzas, her largest debtor and the one man in the empire who can ruin her by proscription. They treat each other with great courtesy.
- [[being-drvnsrlln|Dorvanis Arelliani Septarius]]: The Imperial _Tarkelos_, who negotiates each rollover with her. She has known him for thirty years.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-danvar|The Bankers' Guild]]**: senior partner of a great house, sitting on the Council of the Danvori.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The rider.** The rollover falls due at the quarter day, and the house's rider would keep the guild's auditors out of its chapter books. A Reformist _kurthos_ (senator) pays a party to learn the rider's terms before the Seldan hears of them.
2. **The failing house.** Rumor on the Grand Market says a great house is insolvent behind its clearing. Amarza hires a party to learn which, quietly, because if it is a house that clears through Veldis she must know before the auditors do.
3. **The courier.** A Veldis courier carrying master notes to Delalara is robbed on the coast road. Amarza wants the notes back unbroken, and she wants to know whether the robbers knew what they were carrying.
4. **The Khelathi offer.** Amarza pays a party to attend a dinner on the Strangers' Row as a merchant's guests and bring back the exact terms on which the Khelathi factor is offering the throne silver.

:::secret {title="The house beyond its reserves"}
The House of Veldis has issued notes beyond its reserves. When the smaller houses would not lend after the war, Amarza lent anyway, and the silver came from paper: notes sealed in the house's mark against reserves it did not hold, cleared through its own chapters, never presented together. The gap has grown with every rollover since. It is now about a fifth of the house's issue, and an audit of the chapter strongroom against the notes outstanding would find it in a day. That is why the riders matter more to her than the fee.

She cannot let the throne default and she cannot let the city burn. A short pension day, a bread riot or a broken succession could each end with a reign that repudiates its predecessor's paper, or a crowd at the chapterhouse doors wanting silver for notes she cannot cover. [[affiliation-cleanshlds|The Clean Shields]] are her answer. The house pays the company, through an account at its own chapter that only the company's purser and its captain, [[being-shttrkssm|Shattar Kassumi]], have seen. When the fire comes, the only armed company outside the legions' command sits on the city's grain road a mile from the river gate, and the house can offer it to whichever claimant agrees to honor the debt. She means to sell the city the means of putting out the fire, at the price of the next reign's signature.

She knows the Imperial _Tarkelos_ has seen the clearing totals and can reckon the gap. He has never said so, and she has never asked. Her household sends his a gift on every feast day.

**Hook: the purser's book.** A clerk of the Veldis chapter at Orzara has noticed that one account receives the house's silver every month and pays it out in coin the same day, and has copied a page. He wants a party to tell him what the account is before he decides who to sell the page to.
:::
