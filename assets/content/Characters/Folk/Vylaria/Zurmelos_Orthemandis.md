# Appearance {#appearance}

Zurmelos Orthemandis is a 39-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of heavy build. He has light, smooth skin, auburn hair cut close, and green eyes that measure a room for who in it matters. His captain's cloak is new and brushed every morning, and his boots are better than any guardsman's. He speaks clearly and a little too loudly, in the manner of a man used to addressing a parade.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zurmelos Orthemandis is the new senior officer of the province's urban cohort in the watch-house below the Governor's House in [[place-alyssar|Alessar]]. He has been given the central district, and he means to show the square a district with fewer reported crimes, by searches, arrests and a heavier hand on the street. The guards are expected to support him. The older ones, who keep the peace by knowing the district, do not like what he is doing to it.

## Background

**The commission.** Zurmelos is of a propertied family of the province and came to the cohort through its officer posts rather than its ranks.

**The ambition.** He sees the watch-house as a step, not a career. A captain who makes the central district quiet on paper is a captain the officers on the square remember when a better post falls vacant.

**The posting.** He arrived this year and took command over the head of [[being-ganothisdora|Ganothis Dorandates]], who has walked the district for most of his life.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is energetic, confident and impatient with custom. He listens to his superiors carefully and to his men hardly at all.

### Motivation

He wants promotion, and he wants the numbers that earn it.

### Strengths

He works hard, keeps good records and is well connected on the square. He is not corrupt in the way the old guards are, because he wants something larger than their bribes.

### Weaknesses

He does not know the district, and his searches anger the people whose quiet cooperation the station has always relied on. He makes enemies among the merchants faster than he makes arrests.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-ganothisdora|Ganothis Dorandates]]: The old senior captain whose methods he is replacing, and whose men still go to him first.
- [[being-xenoksbrds|Zénokos Bardas]]: A young guard of the station, whom Zurmelos expects to follow orders and who keeps looking at Ganothis before he does.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-provinclys|The Province of Alessa]]**: a _gulmen_ of the propertied order, and a captain of the province's urban cohort.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The sweep.** Zurmelos orders a search of every shop on a street of the central district on the same night, and hires a party to stand on the street's far end and stop anyone who runs.
2. **The numbers.** A clerk of the watch-house asks a party to help him copy the station's books, because Zurmelos's reports to the square do not match them and the clerk does not want to hang for the difference.
3. **The merchant's complaint.** A spice importer of the district has complained on the square that Zurmelos's men broke his doors. Zurmelos wants the complaint answered with proof that the importer deserved it.

:::secret {title="The sealed commission"}
Zurmelos's commission came down from the Governor's House this spring, sealed with the _therkos_'s seal and read from the steps by the freedman Nethoren. The _therkos_ has sealed nothing himself since the spring, so the commission is one of the year's acts that anyone who proves it can challenge as a forgery. Zurmelos does not know it. The captain he displaced, and every merchant whose doors he has broken, would be glad to learn it.
:::
