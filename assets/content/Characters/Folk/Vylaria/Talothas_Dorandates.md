# Appearance {#appearance}

Talothas Dorandates is 57 years old. He has close-cropped gray hair, brown eyes and a long, blunt face marked by an old break in the nose. His armor is excellent but undecorated beyond the office’s necessities. He asks a watch-captain to repeat an order before releasing him, and remembers the words at their next meeting.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Talothas is the Thaurusos, commander of [[affiliation-sorvan|The Imperial Guard]], living at [[place-thaurzurgalis|The Guard Camp]] in [[place-arvon|Inner City]] of [[place-orzara|Orzara City]]. He answers to [[being-zrmlshlrkls|Zarmelas Helarkales]], the reigning Orzas. His guard holds the Inner City; the City Watch governs order beyond its wall, and he has no commission to command the field legions.

He wants the palace approaches and camp gates to answer to one chain of command when the succession is named. Every watch expects a donative, while the throne has no ready cash and rival households court the officers. He demands explicit orders for a change of watch, but cannot turn an unpaid promise into coin by signing it.

## Background

**The guard.** His career develops within the household guard, whose recruitment and advancement are distinct from the legions. He learns the palace’s routine before receiving a command over its approaches.

**The watches.** Leading watch officers teaches him that a guard’s loyalty is sustained by pay, privileges and confidence in its commander, not by a ceremonial oath alone.

**The throne’s commander.** As Thaurusos he answers directly for the whole body. The old Orzas’s failure to adopt a successor leaves him keeping a present command while every captain is approached about the next one.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is formal, attentive to exact orders and sparing with assurances. He dislikes a flattering report more than an unwelcome one that names its witnesses.

### Motivation

He wants the guard to remain a functioning command through the accession and wants its gates to open on an order he can account for.

### Strengths

He knows the watches, holds direct access to the throne and can demand an account from officers whom ordinary magistrates cannot summon.

### Weaknesses

He depends on captains who have offers of their own. His authority over them does not pay their men, and an order the throne permits can still make the guard enemies in the tribunals.

## Social

The Orzas appoints and commands him. Watch-captains command the men at the gates, while [[being-kholsamelint|Kolsame Linthimis]] keeps the separate City Watch below the Inner Wall. Their jurisdictions meet at a boundary neither can disregard.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: of the propertied order.
- **[[affiliation-sorvan|The Imperial Guard]]**: Thaurusos, commander of the guard and answerable to the Orzas.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The promised payment.** Talothas asks a party to obtain evidence that a claimant’s donative is funded before another watch changes its expectations on the strength of rumor.
2. **The steward’s warrant.** A house’s steward is detained in the camp. Talothas wants the party to bring the household’s copy of the order and establish who claims authority for it before he answers the petition.
3. **The gate message.** Two watch-captains receive incompatible instructions about an expected visitor. He sends a party to trace the messages’ delivery while the captains keep their existing posts; their evidence can expose a forged order without deciding the succession for him.
