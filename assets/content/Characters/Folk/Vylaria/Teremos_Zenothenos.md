# Appearance {#appearance}

Teremos Zenothenos is a 45-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of heavy build. He has olive skin browned by the road, auburn hair cut short, and steady brown eyes. He dresses plainly, in a dark traveling coat with no mark of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] on it until he chooses to show one. He sits square at a table, speaks little, and writes in a small, even hand while others talk.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Teremos Zenothenos is a _marendos_ (master) of the Thordimalon and a _sekeros_ (inquirer) of [[affiliation-sekran|The Arcane Inquisition]] (the Sekran), working its investigations in the west. His field is "irregular practice": hedge-healers, cunning folk and alchemists whose remedies actually work and who learned them from somebody off the Order's register.

His present inquiry is in the borderlands of [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]], where the Order's reach is thin. He goes from village to village, holds his hearings in the back rooms of inns, and asks the same questions everywhere: who treats the sick here, where did that person learn it, and from whom.

His permanent rooms are in [[place-orzara|Orzara]], where he leaves the copies he cannot safely carry on his western inquiries. A chapter messenger receives his letters there and forwards the records he requests. The room is usually shuttered now; his work in Eilwìth keeps him away, and a visitor seeking him needs a forwarding letter rather than an audience at his door.

## Background

**The master.** Teremos rose to _marendos_ in the Order before the Sekran asked his chapter for him. He keeps his rank in the Order while he serves.

**The west.** The Sekran set him to its western inquiries, where the Order keeps chapters but holds no law, and a _sekeros_ works on his standing, his reputation and his hired help.

**The healer.** In the Eilwìth borderlands he has heard, more than once, of a traveling healer and scholar who sells remedies in the less-settled villages and knows more than a hedge-healer should.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is austere, patient and unsentimental. He does not threaten, and he does not need to.

### Motivation

He wants every trained hand on the register. A healer who learned from the Order and left it is, in the Sekran's doctrine, the worst kind of offender, because whoever he teaches is a new hand off the register.

### Strengths

He has the Sekran's archives, the authority of its commission inside the Empire, and the patience to correlate a villager's description with an old record.

### Weaknesses

Outside the Empire he has no law to stand on, only his standing and the coin he pays informants, and the borderland villages protect their own healers.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The guide.** Teremos hires a party to guide him through the borderland villages and to stand at the door of his hearings, and he pays well and on time.
2. **The hearing.** A village wise-woman whom Teremos has summoned asks a party to sit in on her hearing, because she has heard that the Sekran's hearings end with names.
3. **The records.** Teremos sends a party with a sealed letter to an Order chapterhouse, asking for the record of every student who left the [[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]] in a span of twenty years.
4. **The remedy.** A sick child in a borderland village recovers on a remedy that no hedge-healer in the district could have made. Teremos wants the bottle, and so does the child's mother, who will not give it up.

5. **The rooms in Orzara.** Teremos sends a signed letter authorizing a party to collect an old register from his rooms in Orzara and put it aboard the next westbound ship. His landlord will release the chest only to its named carriers, and the ship’s factor wants its freight settled before taking it.

:::secret {title="The healer called Thalos Graymoor"}
The healer is [[being-alfrdbrnwl|Wilred Barnwell]], a lapsed adept of the Epistémion who left the Order and lives as Thalos Greimoor, out of [[place-kingsholow|Kingshollow]]. Teremos does not know his name. A villager has described the healer to him in passing detail, and he is matching the description against the Epistémion's old records of students who left. He is asking the right questions.

Wilred has a network of contacts and will hear of the inquiry. He can disappear, make an alibi, or offer Teremos something a _marendos_ cannot refuse. He also has a friend inside the Epistémion's chapterhouse at [[place-dunavarre|Dúnavarre]] who sends him copies of restricted texts, and if Teremos's request for the records passes through that chapterhouse, the friend sees it first.

**Hook: the letter.** The party carrying the third hook's letter is met on the road by a stranger who offers them twice Teremos's fee to let him read it.
:::
