# Appearance {#appearance}

Tharoza Talothates is a 43-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build. She has light skin weathered by northern winters, black hair worn in a short braid, and steady brown eyes. She wears a plain mail shirt under a gray wool cloak, the _therkos_'s color, and carries an officer's sword she rarely touches. She is calm and economical in speech, and she keeps her hands behind her back while she listens.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tharoza Talothates is the _zoralus_ of [[affiliation-provncmktr|the Province of Moktur]], its commander of order and the highest officer of its law enforcement. From her rooms in the Keep at [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]] she commands the province's urban cohorts, the city watch of Tirellan among them, and keeps the peace of the Keep, the forum and the market. The streets of Tirellan are kept by the border lords' towers, the _therkos_'s riders answer to the _therkos_ alone, and her cohorts hold everything between.

She respects the judgment of [[being-xrphnklmkns|Zerofon Kalmakanes]], the jailer of Kezrora above [[place-sanctara|Ulthora]], who served in the city watch of Tirellan before he went to the prison, and she defers to his recommendations on how the prison is run.

## Background

**The house.** Tharoza is of a propertied family of the province, raised on the march and in Tirellan.

**The cohorts.** She served her way up through the urban cohorts of the province, at the gates and in the forum, and learned the city from its watch-houses.

**The office.** The _therkos_ gave her the cohorts, and she has kept them loyal to the province and out of the quarrels of the border lords.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is careful, discreet and orderly. She listens far more than she speaks and keeps her opinions of the province's powers to herself.

### Motivation

She wants the province quiet and the _therkos_'s peace kept, and she wants her cohorts to come through whatever comes without choosing a side before they must.

### Strengths

She commands the only armed men in Tirellan who are neither the _therkos_'s riders nor a border lord's household. She knows every officer of the province by name, and she knows who reads whose letters.

### Weaknesses

She serves a governor the capital neither commands nor replaces, and her office is his to give. She defers to men who have been longer at their work, sometimes past the point of sense.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-xrphnklmkns|Zerofon Kalmakanes]]: The jailer of Kezrora, an old soldier of the city watch whose judgment she trusts on everything to do with the prison.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-provncmktr|The Province of Moktur]]**: a _gulmen_ of the propertied order, holding the office of _zoralus_.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The offering at the shrine.** Someone leaves a cup of wine and a strip of red cloth in the Eagle Shrine by night. Tharoza asks a party to sit up in the forum and learn who, quietly, before the families of the Fourth's cashiered officers hear that the watch is looking.
2. **The surveyor.** A clerk of the Imperial Offices is measuring the road, and Tharoza wants him alive and on his way south. She asks a party to guard him in the city and to tell her who follows him.
3. **The towers' quarrel.** Two border lords' men have fought at a street corner, and each lord says the other's tower started it. Tharoza wants the truth before it reaches the _therkos_'s audience.
4. **The letter from Kezrora.** A guard of the prison is selling a letter smuggled out of it. Zerofon wants the guard named, and Tharoza lends a party her authority to name him.

:::secret {title="The Keep's peace"}
In The Letter Nobody Opened, Tharoza keeps the peace of the Keep while the _therkos_'s recall lies unopened in his strongroom. She has not been told of the letter. If it is read aloud in the forum, her cohorts are the one armed force in the city that might stand for either the capital or the _therkos_, and she has decided nothing. The auxiliaries would fight for the _therkos_ against anyone, and she knows she could not stop them.
:::
