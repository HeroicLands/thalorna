# Appearance {#appearance}

Nizelen Iltheris is 34 years old. She is a compact woman with black hair, brown eyes and hands roughened by nets and salt. She wears a dark wrap without mourning ornaments. In a conversation she watches the speaker’s face rather than the boat or money being offered to her.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nizelen lives at [[place-volkhemara|Volkemara]], where her husband [Tholmaros Iltheris]{.name data-type="being/npc"} is counted among the sea’s dead after the autumn gales. The town’s custom gives a widow his boat share and forbids asking how the sea takes a man. She refuses the share and quietly asks who went out with him that night.

She wants an account she can trust before deciding what to accept from the net-masters. Her refusal makes ordinary errands uncomfortable: the same households own the boats, settle the beach’s quarrels and agree the price of the fish. She speaks most freely away from their hall.

## Background

**The household.** Work around the boats and curing sheds teaches her how a catch becomes a payment, and how long a family must wait between the two.

**The marriage.** As Tholmaros’s wife she shared the obligations of a net-master’s household without holding his place in the owners’ agreement. She learned to read the papers that determine what the boats owe.

**The loss.** His name is given to the water, and the town expects her to take the customary share. Refusing it is the first public act she can take that does not let the question end with the rite.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is restrained, persistent and unwilling to let sympathy replace an answer. She listens closely to disagreements between people who claim to remember the same night.

### Motivation

She wants to know what happened to her husband before choosing what future to make with the property left behind.

### Strengths

She understands the household’s business, reads contracts, and can distinguish a practical offer from an attempt to end a conversation.

### Weaknesses

The owners she questions control much of her livelihood. She has few safe ways to ask them directly without warning everyone else involved.

## Social

The net-masters offer her the expected settlement. The Priest of Storms performs the customary rite but supplies no account of the voyage. Neither action answers the question she asks.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: a free resident under imperial law without the citizenship.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The three companions.** Nizelen hires a party to learn who was aboard when Tholmaros left in the gale and to bring back each account separately.
2. **The stranger’s boat.** A boat without painted eyes fishes the town’s grounds by night. Nizelen asks the party whether its owner has business with her husband’s former boats before the net-masters drive it off.
3. **The late salt.** The overdue Thremuzara delivery threatens the curing sheds. Nizelen can introduce the party to households losing their catch, who may speak more freely while the owners argue over supplies.

:::secret {title="The contract and the offered price"}
Tholmaros agreed to sell his boats and grounds to the Venthelis shipping house of [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] outside the net-masters’ agreement. Three net-masters rowed out with him on the first night of the autumn gales and returned without him. Nizelen holds the signed and sealed sale contract: the house owns the boats and grounds once it pays. She has not told the Venthelis she has it. The net-masters offer twice her husband’s share to burn it, and she wants to know which of the three held him under before she decides. Her possession of the contract and the murder are not public knowledge.
:::
