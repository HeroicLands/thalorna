# Appearance {#appearance}

Linthimas Tharozates is 55 years old, a small, neat man with silver-gray hair, dark eyes and a rounded chin. He lowers his voice when a petitioner grows loud. He squares a stack of papers against the desk before answering and keeps his place in the upper sheet with one finger.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Linthimas is Veramus of the Rolls on [[affiliation-verdanorzara|Orzara Courts]]. He oversees the census, citizen rolls, civic records and the city’s correspondence with the throne. His seat coordinates the council’s work by custom, but he has no superior vote: all five veramusoi are equals, their chair rotates and grave decisions require a majority.

He wants the pay-halls supplied with certified rolls before their queues form. A missing entry becomes a household without its payment, and an unexplained delay becomes a crowd on a Circle. He is courteous to people whose papers are in order and quick to send an incomplete claim back to the ward that must certify it.

## Background

**The seat.** Linthimas belongs to the senatorial order and holds office at the Orzas’s pleasure. Coordinating the council’s correspondence puts many requests across his desk; it gives him no right to decide a colleague’s portfolio in that colleague’s absence.

**The copies.** The roll that sets Orzara’s dole is kept in the [Hall of the Five]{.name data-type="place/structure"} in [[place-platandstr|the Platan District]]. Working copies are made in [[affiliation-imprlscrblgld|the Scribal Guild’s]] hall across the Circle. Linthimas certifies the roll against which [[affiliation-tarmex|the Tarmeks]] pays, and a dispute over a name can run between the ward, the copyists and his desk before any money changes hands.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is accommodating about the wording of a request and firm about where it must be filed. He remembers a petitioner’s earlier visit and uses that small courtesy to make another delay easier to accept.

### Motivation

He wants the rolls paid on time and his own place preserved through the succession. He prefers a correction made quietly at a desk to a dispute carried onto a public platform.

### Strengths

He can trace a disputed entry through its ward return, working copy and certified roll. He knows which apparent discrepancy is a copying error and which requires a fresh declaration.

### Weaknesses

He treats a complete paper trail as a reason to stop asking questions. His insistence on the correct form can make a sound complaint too expensive for its claimant to pursue.

## Social

Ward clerks supply the returns; [[affiliation-imprlscrblgld|the Imperial Scribal Guild]] supplies the working copies. The treasury needs his certification, while the reform faction of [[affiliation-seldan|the Senate]] presses to test citizens’ claims and trim the rolls. Linthimas must answer that pressure without claiming authority over the Senate or promising his colleagues money the treasury has not supplied.

### Affiliations

- **Orzara Courts**: a Veramus holding the Rolls, with the council’s customary coordinating duty.
- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: a Kurthos of the senatorial order.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The two spellings.** A veteran’s household finds different spellings of his name on its ward return and the pay-hall copy. Linthimas requires the earlier record before certifying the correction; the family hires the party to recover it before payment day.
2. **The unposted list.** A scribe is offered ten Soldeni for an early copy of a list on its way to the Platan’s boards. He asks the party to learn what the list contains and who needs it before he risks his place.
3. **The missing return.** A ward’s certified return fails to reach the copyists before the pay-hall needs its roll. Linthimas asks the party to follow its route through the clerks, who each insist the delay begins at the next desk.

:::secret {title="The dead who still draw"}
Linthimas knowingly takes a share of the fraudulent dole drawn in the names of the dead. Ward clerks in [[place-warrensorz|the Warrens]] and [[place-wharvesorz|Wharves]] pocket the monthly payments, and a freedman collects his share from them. About four thousand dead names remain on the roll. The other four veramusoi do not know, and the Tarmeks pays against his certification.

Two senior Guild copyists have copied the same dead names for twenty years and keep a private list. Linthimas wants the fraud to remain a clerical matter that never reaches his colleagues, and a Senate audit threatens both his income and his seat. The copyists’ list is worth a fortune to him and a council seat to whoever brings it to the other four first.

**The price of a correction.** One of the copyists approaches a party able to carry the list safely. Linthimas can pay to recover it, but handing it to him leaves the wards drawing the dead men’s shares. Taking it to his colleagues puts the carrier between the council, the ward clerks and the freedman who collects.
:::
