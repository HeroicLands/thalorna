# Appearance {#appearance}

Thelvaros Barkratis is a 64-year-old man who stands 6'3" tall and is of heavy build. He has light, weathered skin, brown hair going gray and brown eyes. He is a big man who sits as if the chair belonged to him, and he wears a good coat a few seasons out of fashion. He talks about his daughter to anyone who will listen.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lord Thelvaros Barkratis is the head of [House Barkratis]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, a noble family of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] in [[place-salkhalon|Salkalon]] whose old prominence had faded into obscurity and reduced means by the time his daughter was born. That daughter is [[being-krmdnsbrkrtys|Koremdanis Barkratis]], counted the foremost swordmaster of the empire, and her renown has raised the house from a declining name to one of prestige in the province. He keeps the house among the estate families of [[place-terravia|Therkara]], in the province's inland country.

## Background

**The faded house.** Thelvaros inherited a name worth more than its lands. His sons were raised for the legions or the offices, as younger men of a poor noble house are.

**The daughter.** His daughter cared only for the blade from childhood. He indulged it at first as a whim. When she held to it through her girlhood, he saw that it was a gift, and he went looking for the finest teacher he could find. He found [[being-pelerarsathe|Pelerar Satheris]] in the dueling circles of Orzara and placed her with him at twelve.

**The return of the name.** Her fame has done for the house what his management never did. The families of Therkara who had stopped asking the Barkratis to dinner ask them again.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is proud, affable and a little vain, generous with what he has and quick to boast of his daughter. He likes company and dislikes accounts.

### Motivation

He wants House Barkratis restored to the standing he believes it should have, and he wants his children settled. He sees his daughter's fame as the road to both.

### Strengths

He has a noble name, a famous daughter and the goodwill of the estate families who share his table. He made one great decision well, when he found her a teacher.

### Weaknesses

He has little money and less judgment about it, and he leans on his daughter's name more than she would like.
