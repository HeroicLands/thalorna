# Appearance {#appearance}

Volkemar Zurmelates is a 29-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He has fair, smooth skin that has seen little weather, dark brown hair cut in the fashion of Hilen, and lively brown eyes. He dresses better than anyone else in a timber yard, in a fur-trimmed coat and boots that have never stood in a charcoal pit, and wears a heavy silver seal-ring. He is friendly, quick to laugh and quick to name a price.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Volkemar Zurmelates is a trader of [[place-nartum|Nartun]] who runs charcoal works on a great scale and to a lower standard than the old burners. Where the burners of [[place-kalderenna|Kalderenna]] cut chosen trees and tend a clamp for days, his crews burn the dead wood of the stands the Withering has killed, cut by the contract crews on the temple-estates, and he sells the charcoal cheap, down the road to [[place-hylen|Hilen]] and to anyone in the holy city who does not ask how it was made. He has undercut the prices and the name of [[being-brnksksrns|Branakis Kaseranis]], the best charcoal-burner in the kingdom, steadily and on purpose.

He is one of the buyers who now want the Order's knight at Kalderenna to find Branakis in breach of the estate's rules.

## Background

**The trade.** Volkemar was born into the trade of the holy city, where every coin is weighed at the House of Scales and every price is chalked as a weight of silver. He learned to buy cheap and sell in bulk, and he learned it young.

**The dead stands.** When the Withering began to kill the stands on the temple-estates, and priors such as [[being-mlknssrvths|Melkanas Sarvothis]] let the dead wood to the contract crews, Volkemar saw a supply of timber that nobody else wanted. He set up charcoal works to burn it.

**The present.** His works now turn out more charcoal than all the old burners of Kalderenna together, and the smiths of Hilen buy it because it is cheap.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is cheerful, energetic and entirely without sentiment about the forest. He thinks the old burners' craft is a fine thing that the market no longer pays for, and he says so to their faces.

### Motivation

He wants the charcoal trade of the kingdom, and the only thing between him and it is the reputation of the old burners and the guild that judges them.

### Strengths

He has money, the contract crews, and the goodwill of priors who like their dues paid promptly. He is a fast and clever dealer.

### Weaknesses

His charcoal is poor, and the smiths who need the best still go to Branakis. His supply is the forest's death, and the barons and the burners blame the contracts for the Withering. He has few friends among the old families of the kingdom.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-brnksksrns|Branakis Kaseranis]]: The charcoal-burner of Kalderenna who will not make poor charcoal for any price and who names Volkemar's works as everything wrong with the trade.

### Others

- [[being-mlknssrvths|Melkanas Sarvothis]]: The prior who lets his estate's dead stands to the contract crews, on whose seal Volkemar's supply depends.
- [[being-sorimarronet|Sorimar Ronethen]]: The head of the Woodworkers' Guild, whose judging stands between his charcoal and the guild's mark.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]]**: a freeman of the kingdom.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The breach.** Volkemar wants Branakis found in breach of the estate's rules before the next court sits, and he hires a party to go up to Kalderenna and look at the old burner's clamps with a knowing eye.
2. **The rival bid.** Two contract crews want the same valley on Melkanas's estate, and one has offered the prior a better price. Volkemar is behind one of them, and wants a party to learn who is behind the other.
3. **The cheap cargo.** A smith of Hilen has had a blade crack in the forging and blames Volkemar's charcoal. He wants a party to bring back a sack of it and a sack of Branakis's, so that he can prove it.

:::secret {title="The apprentice"}
The pleasant youth who has attached himself to the charcoal grounds at Kalderenna, asking the burners how they judge a clamp's heat, is Volkemar's. He is paid to learn Branakis's methods for grading charcoal, which Branakis guards jealously, so that Volkemar's works can sell a better grade at the same low price. Branakis suspects him. The youth is a talented burner in his own right, and he likes the old man, which Volkemar has not considered.

**Hook: the apprentice.** If Branakis takes the youth's part instead of expelling him, Volkemar loses his spy and gains a grievance, and he asks a party to bring the youth back to Nartun, by persuasion if possible.
:::
