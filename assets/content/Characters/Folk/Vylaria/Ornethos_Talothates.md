# Appearance {#appearance}

Ornethos Talothates is 51 years old. His graying hair is cut close, his brown eyes are patient, and his pale linen coat carries no ornament beyond the fastening at its throat. He holds a wax tablet flat in his palm while a carter speaks. He rarely raises his voice over the noise of the bridge.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ornethos keeps the imperial Customs House at [[place-ashenmere|Ashenmere]]. He answers to the revenue officer at [[place-aravantia|Aravantara]], not to the town’s magistrates, and has eight provincial soldiers to search wagons and guard the bridge. Carters reckon him honest: his clerks collect the stated customs and nothing besides.

The stream divides imperial ground from the [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartun]]. Ornethos wants disputes settled before either bank turns them into a claim against the other. He will explain a tariff twice, but he will not let a merchant push a wagon past the barrier while its load is still in question.

## Background

**The revenue.** He learns the work in service to the revenue and keeps it after gaining freedom. He values a receipt because it can defend a man with no powerful family.

**The bridge.** His present posting lasts nine years. He knows the regular grain dealers and timber merchants, their wagons and the marks their crews put on harness.

**The two banks.** Priests, pilgrims and merchants cross the same bridge for different reasons. He learns to ask about goods before asking about belief, and to keep a quarrel out of the search yard when he can.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is devout, reserved and exact. He gives a poor carter time to find a receipt and gives a rich dealer no leave to skip it.

### Motivation

He wants the bridge to keep earning its lawful revenue without becoming the place where an argument between kingdoms begins.

### Strengths

Nine years of traffic give him a reliable memory for altered wagons and unfamiliar drivers. His clerks trust his ruling on a customs charge.

### Weaknesses

He treats quiet as evidence that he has handled a problem well. His reluctance to send a difficult report upward can leave him alone with a decision he cannot repair.

## Social

The magistrates provide a jail for smugglers but do not command his search detachment. Merchants seek his judgment; the priests on the far bank expect courtesy without accepting imperial authority.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: a freedman.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The false bottom.** Ornethos holds a grain wagon whose owner denies knowing about its hidden compartment. He asks a party to find the carpenter who altered it before the magistrates hear the charge.
2. **The absent tenants.** A steward asks to see the bridge’s traffic records after another tenant household disappears. Ornethos asks a party to establish when the family was last seen on the imperial road.
3. **The preacher’s return.** A priest bound for the Hilen estates needs an escort back to the bridge. Ornethos offers the party the customs hours and a safe place to leave its baggage, but wants any threat against the crossing reported to him.

:::secret {title="The unreported crossing"}
Two winters ago a patrol of Ornethos’s detachment meets knights of the [[affiliation-sacredflame|Order of the Sacred Flame]] at a ford above the bridge. They are taking imperial worshippers of the Black Flame into Nartun. Ornethos reports nothing: his devotion makes him accept their judgment of the smith they take. The current disappearances continue that traffic. He knows the knights cross; the forge-priest Huraden supplies their names. His silence, rather than a bribe, is the act that could bring a charge against him.
:::
