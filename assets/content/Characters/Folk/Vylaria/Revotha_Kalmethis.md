# Appearance {#appearance}

Revotha Kalmethis is a 36-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. She has tanned skin, black hair drawn straight back without ornament, and brown eyes that do not leave the person standing before her. She dresses in dark wool cut like a magistrate's robe even off the bench, sits very still, and speaks in complete sentences that sound written. She does not smile in public.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Revotha Kalmethis is a magistrate of [[affiliation-provncmktr|the Province of Moktur]], sitting at [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]] in the courts the _therkos_ commands. She is called the Strict, and she has earned the name: thieves, debtors to the revenue and men who draw a blade in the city's streets leave her bench with the longest sentences the law allows, and many of them go up the rock road to Kezrora, the province's great prison above [[place-sanctara|Ulthora]].

She values the prison's gaoler, [[being-xrphnklmkns|Zerofon Kalmakanes]], because he carries out her sentences exactly as she gives them, without cruelty and without mercy. She does not know him well and has no wish to.

## Background

**The house.** Revotha was born to a propertied family of the province, educated in the law and expected to marry well. She chose the bench.

**The bench.** She came to the province's courts young and made her name in a province where raiders, smugglers and border lords' men all believe the law is for other people.

**The name.** She has been called the Strict for most of her time on the bench, and she has never tried to lose the name.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is cold, exact and incorruptible. She believes that a law not enforced is worse than no law, and she says so from the bench.

### Motivation

She wants the law of the province to be feared, because she believes Moktur holds together only while it is.

### Strengths

Nobody has bribed her. She knows the law of the province by heart, and the _therkos_'s court trusts her judgments.

### Weaknesses

She has no mercy to give and little curiosity about a case once she has judged it. An appeal against her is an appeal against her pride.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-xrphnklmkns|Zerofon Kalmakanes]]: The gaoler of Kezrora, whose reliability she values and whose doubts she has never asked about.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-provncmktr|The Province of Moktur]]**: a _gulmen_ of the propertied order, sitting as a magistrate of the province.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The dispute between the towers.** Two border lords bring a quarrel over a ford to the _therkos_'s audience, and the _therkos_ sends it to Revotha's bench. Each lord asks a party to find a witness the other has hidden.
2. **The surveyor's leg.** The last clerk sent from the capital to measure the road came back with a broken leg. Revotha wants the man who broke it before her bench, and she asks a party to bring him.
3. **The prisoner's letter.** A letter smuggled out of Kezrora claims its writer was imprisoned without trial. Revotha wants to know whether he was sentenced from her bench, and asks a party to bring her the letter.

:::secret {title="The sentence she gave"}
Several of Kezrora's long-term prisoners were sentenced from Revotha's bench on evidence she did not test, because the evidence came from the _therkos_'s officers and she trusts them more than she trusts the accused. If Zerofon comes to believe that one of his prisoners is innocent, it is her judgment he is questioning, and she will defend the sentence before she reads the evidence again.
:::
