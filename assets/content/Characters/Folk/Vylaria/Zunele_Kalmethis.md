# Appearance {#appearance}

Zunele Kalmethis is 43 years old. She is a lean woman with brown eyes, black hair tied under a linen kerchief, and a habit of counting an arriving team before greeting its driver. Her cuffs smell of leather dressing. She keeps a spare length of harness cord at her belt and repairs small failures while listening to larger complaints.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zunele keeps the post-house at [[place-delara|Delara]], where the market road brings carters, drovers and official packets into the same yard. She can offer a stable, a dry bench and the name of the last carrier through. She cannot promise that a sealed bag contains everything its sender put in it.

She wants the yard to remain a dependable stopping place as short deliveries and quarrels over weights make merchants distrust the road. She records when a wagon arrives and how many animals draw it; she refuses to certify its cargo without seeing it unloaded.

## Background

**The stable.** She begins her working life tending other people’s teams. A lame horse teaches her more about a missed delivery than a merchant’s explanation does, and she learns which repairs can safely wait.

**The counter.** Taking charge of the house brings packets and accounts as well as horses. She keeps the two sorts of work together: a carrier must settle the animal’s care before collecting the next bag.

**The road now.** Delara’s market traffic still pays the bills, but a missing packet can cost her more custom than a month of late wagons. She now asks a second person to witness each handover.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is brisk without hurrying a tired animal, hospitable without extending easy credit, and unembarrassed about asking a traveler to repeat a name.

### Motivation

She wants carriers to trust the post-house enough to report a loss while there is still a chance of tracing it.

### Strengths

She knows the working condition of the teams that use her yard and can distinguish a broken journey from a fabricated excuse.

### Weaknesses

Her record stops at the gate. She can establish who left together, but cannot make the road beyond Delara safe or compel a household to open its mail.

## Social

Her standing comes from the carters and drovers who use her yard, not from a magistracy. The town’s merchants need her witnesses when they dispute a delivery, and resent her refusal to supply a certainty she does not have.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: a free resident under imperial law without the citizenship.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The postbag.** A bag expected at Delara has not arrived, though its carrier’s hired horse has returned. Zunele pays a party to trace the horse’s last stopping place before another rider takes it out.
2. **The short load.** Two drivers quarrel in her yard over sacks that weighed differently at departure. She asks a party to accompany them to the market’s weighing rather than let the dispute become a fight in the stable.
3. **The missing herd.** A drover leaves his return hire unpaid after his animals fail to reach the market. Zunele offers his last route and a place to lodge to a party willing to find him; she wants an answer before she accepts the accusation that he sold the herd.
