# Appearance {#appearance}

Lizelen Satheris is 38 years old. She has a square face, gray eyes and dark hair braided close to her head. Her writing hand is stained at the side from copying figures. She keeps a wool wrap over her chair for the cold that comes through the gate arch, and puts it on before the queue begins.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lizelen works behind the counter of the toll-house at [[place-korrast|Korras]]. Commanders arrive each spring with their own men and expectations; she remains with the provincial clerks who explain the scale, the chest and the accounts to them. She counts the monthly remittance for [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]] and records what the gate actually receives.

She wants the next remittance sent without a dispute that leaves the governor’s riders unpaid. A muleteer challenges the figures painted on the tariff board, while a rejected border lord threatens to take the gate. She needs the same records to survive either quarrel.

## Background

**The copy desk.** She learns the province’s accounts by copying receipts, first checking the marks on loads and then the money paid against them.

**The changing banner.** Service at Korras teaches her that an arriving commander often knows less about his gate than the carters waiting outside it. She supplies the facts without making a public lesson of the difference.

**The board.** Repainting the tariff leaves old figures visible beneath the new ones. She keeps dated copies at the desk, but a copy alone does not settle what a traveler was shown.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is dry, punctual and unimpressed by a newly announced title. She explains a charge in full, then waits for the customer to identify the part being disputed.

### Motivation

She wants a trustworthy account of each collection, one that remains useful after the next commander arrives.

### Strengths

She knows the tariff’s revisions, the remittance routine and the long-serving workers at the gate.

### Weaknesses

Her careful records can incriminate an officer without protecting her from him. She has no armed following and cannot keep the pass open by paperwork.

## Social

She answers through the province’s revenue staff. The town magistrates can hear a quarrel inside the walls, but the commander controls a dispute at the gate where she works.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: a free resident under imperial law without the citizenship.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The old figures.** Lizelen asks a party to find a dated receipt for the muleteer’s last load and compare it with the older figures beneath the paint.
2. **The remittance.** A threat against the gate delays the coin convoy. She hires additional escorts willing to deliver the sealed chest and obtain a receipt at Tirellan.
3. **The widow’s account.** A herder’s widow will speak only to travelers with no border lord behind them. Lizelen can introduce the party without making it part of the commander’s retinue; she wants the death accounted for before rumor becomes another excuse to seize the gate.
