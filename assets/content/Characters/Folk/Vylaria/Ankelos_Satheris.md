# Appearance {#appearance}

Ankelos Satheris is a 23-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of medium build. He has light skin weathered by the forge, auburn hair cropped short, and brown eyes. His forearms carry a smith's burn scars, and he wears a priest's robe of the [[affiliation-sacredforge|Sacred Forge]] with a hammer at his belt. He speaks quickly and loudly, and he leans in when he argues.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ankelos Satheris is an archimandrite of [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]], ordained clergy of the Forge-Lord's creative aspect, in the capital, [[place-orzara|Orzara]]. He is young and fervent, and he has made the growing influence of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] his personal crusade.

The quarrel between the two faiths of [[lore-ustarondty|Ústaron]] ([[lore-ustaranschism|the Ústaran Schism]]) is fought in Orzara with votes, sermons and slanders, never in the streets, because the law of the Temple District punishes open conflict between faiths without mercy. Ankelos fights it with everything the law leaves him. He presses the city's magistrates to restrict the Black Flame's preaching on the Circles, works to discredit its known sympathizers, and pays for counter-sermons wherever its preachers have been heard, in the capital and in the towns of Alessa. The law protects the Black Flame's right to exist, so he cannot act against its priests directly.

## Background

**The forge.** Ankelos trained as an acolyte of the Sacred Forge alongside a master smith, as many of its acolytes do, and was ordained young.

**The capital.** He serves in the Forge's temple in the Temple District of [[place-arvon|Arvon]], among the senior clergy of the Forge in the capital. The fire in the Forge-Lord's niche in the [[place-pnthnrldnm|Temple of the Twelve]] is the Forge's to tend.

**The crusade.** The Black Flame's preachers have grown bold on the Circles of the Warrens, and its patrons sit in the [[affiliation-seldan|Seldan]]. Ankelos answers them with sermons, petitions and money.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is zealous, quick-tempered and brave. He believes what he preaches and cannot understand people who do not.

### Motivation

He wants the Black Flame silenced in the Empire, and he wants the next Orzas to keep it out of the Forge-Lord's niche.

### Strengths

His conviction, his friends among the keepers of the Temple and the old houses, and money for sermons.

### Weaknesses

His temper. Even his friends fear it, and a man who loses his temper in the Temple District can be made to look like the one who broke its peace.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-trzmsbrkrts|Torzemos Brakarates]]: The High Flame-Warden of Vylaria at the [[place-sarkenaelzen|Ashen Temple]], who holds the Black Flame's temples in the Empire and has its accession petition drafted.
- [[being-myrnklypss|Mirene Kalethos]]: A mendicant priestess and fire-reader of the Black Flame in Alessa. He counts her one of the most dangerous agents the faith has produced, because nobody can arrest a woman for having conversations.

### Others

- [[being-dlmrsvrklns|Dalmeras Varkalenis]]: The Imperial Cult's Keeper of the Masks and a claimant to the succession, who shares his devotion and his dislike of the new houses.
- [[being-osmrthlvns|Osmera Thalvenis]]: The Árdavan Temple Judge who keeps the Temple of the Twelve, who admires his zeal and fears his temper.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]]**: an archimandrite of the Hammer-Priests, the Forge's ordained clergy, in the capital.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Ash in the brazier.** A priest of the Sacred Forge who tends the Forge-Lord's niche finds black ash in its brazier at the morning rite. Ankelos wants the culprit found and named before the Temple's keepers can hush it up.
2. **The counter-sermon.** Ankelos hires a party to stand by his preacher on a Circle of the Warrens, where the Black Flame's men have broken up the last two sermons, and to see that nobody lays a hand on anyone first.
3. **The mendicant.** Ankelos wants a party to follow Mirene Kalethos through the towns of Alessa and write down every house that receives her.
4. **The petition.** The High Flame-Warden's accession petition has been drafted. Ankelos wants to know which claimant's house will receive it, and pays a party to watch the Ashen Temple's couriers.

:::secret {title="The Forge's purse and the road to Nartun"}
The money for the counter-sermons and the petitions to the magistrates is not all the Forge's. For six years [[being-osmrthlvns|Osmera Thalvenis]] has paid him part of the Temple of the Twelve's endowment, entered in its books as the upkeep of the Forge-Lord's niche. The niche's upkeep costs four times what the other eleven niches cost together, and a clerk of the endowment has noticed.

Ankelos has also chosen a priest of the Sacred Forge for [[being-dlmrsvrklns|Dalmeras Varkalenis]] and blessed him for the road. In the spring the priest went up the holy road to [[place-nartum|Nartun]] as a pilgrim and offered the Holy Mother, in Dalmeras's name, that if Nartun's knights and temple coin stand behind his adoption, Dalmeras will proscribe the Black Flame when he reigns. The Holy Mother has agreed to nothing, and asks for the oath sworn in person in the Mother's Hall. Osmera does not know of the offer.

**Hook: the pilgrim's return.** The priest Ankelos sent to Nartun is on his way back, and the High Flame-Warden's people are watching the road. Ankelos hires a party to meet him at the posting-station and bring him into the city by night.
:::
