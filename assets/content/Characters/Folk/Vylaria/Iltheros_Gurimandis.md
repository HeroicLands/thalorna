# Appearance {#appearance}

Iltheros Gurimandis is 64 years old. He has white hair, brown eyes and a stooped back, with the careful dress of a merchant who preserves good cloth for years. He carries the column ledger in a leather cover rubbed smooth at the corners. He runs a thumb along a mason’s mark before accepting a buyer’s description.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Iltheros is senior tavemos of [[place-skoraz|Soraks]], a town with four thousand residents in a city built for thirty thousand. He is a cloth merchant and signs every sale of its abandoned columns, whose price pays a tax assessment fixed for a far larger place. He knows what each column brings and what remains to sell.

He wants the Broken Bridge repaired so the old north road can carry traffic again. The Northern Bench and its registry bring litigants to the town now, but the provincial clerks outrank the town magistrates even in their own streets. He must ask those same officials for accounts and assistance.

## Background

**The cloth trade.** Trading cloth taught him the cost of a journey and the value of a road that reaches another market. The broken crossing narrows his business as it narrows the town’s.

**The council.** He joined a council of twelve sitting in a house built for a hundred. Responsibility for the assessment makes selling unused stone a recurring necessity.

**The bridge.** He spends his public life seeking its repair.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is courteous, persistent and acutely conscious of a price paid to meet an immediate need. He can name a vanished shop when a buyer sees only its columns.

### Motivation

He wants Soraks to earn its living from a working road again before the council sells the fabric that could help restore it.

### Strengths

He knows the council’s sales and the town’s surviving trades.

### Weaknesses

Each year’s assessment forces him to make the next sale. His authority over local matters gives him little influence over the province’s priorities.

## Social

The remaining councilors share liability for the town’s taxes. Provincial registrars supply much of its business without answering to him. House Zarkedis’s ruined standing is a warning of how little a town’s old name can protect.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]]**: of the propertied order.
- **[[affiliation-provinclys|Province of Alessa]]**: a Tavemos, elected as one of Soraks’s two town magistrates.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The columns sold twice.** Two agents carry sealed bills for the same twelve columns, while Iltheros’s ledger records one sale. He hires a party to trace both documents before either buyer loads the stone.
2. **The far road.** A Lentora merchant proposes repairing the bridge with Soraks stone. Iltheros asks the party to cross the river and report the condition of the road beyond it before the council commits more material.
3. **The missing registry page.** The page naming the buyer of the Zarkedis house is cut out. Iltheros cannot order the provincial clerk to answer, so he seeks a party able to find a surviving copy through the sale’s witnesses.

:::secret {title="The levy and the sealed transfer"}
Four written requests to inspect the bridge accounts receive refusals, yet he keeps the letters rather than treating the question as settled.

The bridge levy is spent on roads nearer the capital while the accounts list it as repair work at Soraks. Iltheros knows his requests are refused, not the full use of the money. An act moving the Northern Bench and registry to [[place-lentora|Lentora]] is already sealed in [[place-alyssar|Alessar]] and waits to be read. Nobody in Soraks has seen it. The clerk who cuts the Zarkedis page has heard of the move and hopes to go with the registry. A party can uncover the transfer; Iltheros does not begin with that knowledge.
:::
