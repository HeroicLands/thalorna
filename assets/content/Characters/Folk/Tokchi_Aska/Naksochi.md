# Appearance {#appearance}

Naksochi is a 44-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a heavy build. He has tawny skin, sun-kissed complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Naksochi is the Falcon Chief (_Kiyo Imala_) of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]], who comes to [[place-kiyopokta|Falcon Mound]] to raise a war when the Great Sun calls one against the plains. He is the son of the Sun Mother, [Shimaya]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, and a sister's son of the Great Sun, [[being-opahiko|Opahiko]]: a hard, popular man of about forty-five who has led three wars west and spends more of the year at Kiyo Pokta than at [[place-hayopokta|Sun Mound]]. The Keepers of the Fire favor him and the western towns love him. He and the Mound Chief of Kiyo Pokta, [Ikinko]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, are civil to each other in the falcon house.

When the Sun Mother chooses the Great Sun's heir, his rival is her sister's son, [Sohiyo]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, whom the Shell Chiefs call their own.

:::secret {title="The Falcon Chief's war"}

He wants a war, and wants it before the Great Sun dies. A victory would make him the obvious heir when the Sun Mother chooses, and captives from it would give the Keepers the following they want for the old Great Sun's funeral. He is pressing it on two fronts: west against the plains, which Ikinko is quietly resisting by warning the band leaders he trades with, and north into the [[place-hanalxilanrgn|Ha'nal Shi'lan Region]] through the war parties of [[place-hokpahosa|Two Rivers]]. If either war starts, the shell towns read it as a funeral being stocked.

:::

:::secret {title="The Falcon Chief's northern raids"}

The war parties of Two Rivers go up the valleys on Naksochi's orders while their pipe kinship with the Maple People still runs.

:::

What he wants is to be named the Great Sun's heir, with a victory to show for it.
