# Appearance {#appearance}

Liyahi is a 40-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a medium build. He has brown skin, smooth complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Liyahi is the Mound Chief (_Poktimala_) of [[place-hokpahosa|Two Rivers]], the northern gate of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]], where the river from the [[place-hanalxilanrgn|Ha'nal Shi'lan Region]] meets the great river and the copper of the north is bought. He is a Noble of about forty who has smoked the red pipe with more of the [[affiliation-osetakona|Maple People]] than any man of the heartland. He collects the town's maize and earth, judges its clans, leads its warriors and keeps the peace of the landing.

Every summer he sees off the One Fire's delegates to the [[affiliation-redstncmpct|Red Stone Compact]], who go up the great river from his landing toward the Red Quarry, and every autumn he sends war parties up the eastern valleys against the Maple People's new villages. He sees no contradiction in either.

:::secret {title="Raiding under the pipe"}

The Maple People accuse the One Fire of raiding Ha'nal Shi'lan under cover of pipe kinship, and in Hokpa Hosa the charge is true. Liyahi's war parties go up the valleys while their pipe kinship still runs, on the orders of the Falcon Chief, [[being-naksochi|Naksochi]], who wants a war with the Maple People before the Great Sun dies. A war would bring captives for the funeral the Keepers want and a victory for the man who hopes the Sun Mother names him heir. Liyahi has worked out that a war would also close his landing, and he has not decided which of the two to betray.

What he wants is his landing kept open, whichever of his masters he has to betray to keep it.

:::

He is tall, cheerful and sharp, and he makes strangers welcome on his landing because strangers pay. A party going north into Ha'nal Shi'lan or to the Red Quarry starts from his landing, and a party that can tell him which way the Maple People's councils are leaning has something he will buy.

What he wants is his landing kept open.
