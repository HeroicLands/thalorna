# Appearance {#appearance}

Tiloho is a 55-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall with a medium build. He has dark brown skin, smooth complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tiloho is the Shell Chief (_Koyo Imala_) of [[place-koyalolint|Last Shore]], the southernmost shell town of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]], among the mangrove islands at the end of the [[place-koyalorgn|Shell Coast]]. He is a former captain with one hand, who lost the other to a shark and talks about it freely. His town builds the largest dugouts on the coast, cut from whole cypress trunks and lashed two by two under a platform of poles, and its captains are the only people in the north who go out of sight of land on purpose. The great double canoes of the One Fire's war fleet on the gulf are built here.

He is fifty-five, loud, generous and a teller of tall stories, which are the town's chief export: islands with no people, towns of strangers who paint their faces blue, a coast farther south where the people have seen men from the south itself.

:::secret {title="The bell"}

The town's captain [[being-chasise|Chasise]] has made the voyage both ways. Two years ago he followed the coast south for forty days to a river mouth where a town of fishers traded with southern merchants, and came home with a cast copper bell of southern make, a bolt of dyed cotton and the knowledge that the southern merchants pay well for shell, feathers and slaves. Tiloho and [[being-otoki|Otoki]] of [[place-koyohasko|Great Shell]] have kept the voyage quiet. They mean to sell the route to the next Great Sun, and the Keepers of the Fire at [[place-hayopokta|Sun Mound]] do not know that it exists.

What he wants is the Last Shore made the coast's door to the south, with the tolls that come of keeping it.

:::

A party that wants to go south by sea, or to have a double canoe built, comes to him. He takes its measure over a meal and a story, and he gives nothing away that he has not been paid for.

What he wants is a Last Shore canoe under every chief on the gulf who can pay for one.
