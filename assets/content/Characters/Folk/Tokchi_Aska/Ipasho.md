# Appearance {#appearance}

Ipasho is a 70-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a heavy build. He has tawny skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ipasho is the Eldest Keeper (_Tokchi Hapilo_) of the great mound at [[place-hayopokta|Sun Mound]], the head of the hereditary priesthood of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]] that feeds the fire on the mound, keeps the bones of the dead Suns, recites the line of the Suns and conducts every funeral. He is an old man with a famous memory and a dry tongue, who has recited the line at forty New Fires and buried two Great Suns.

He holds that the Third Gathering has lasted because its Suns kept the rites, and that a Great Sun buried without a full following is a Great Sun sent up alone. He wants the dying Great Sun, [[being-opahiko|Opahiko]], buried with his wives, his retainers and his volunteers, as the rites require. The Shell Chiefs say that one coastal death, even a volunteer's, breaks the [Shell Promise]{.name data-type="lore/law"}. Ipasho answers that the promise covers the coast's people, and that nobody has asked him to bury a fisher. He favors the Falcon Chief, [[being-naksochi|Naksochi]], and does not hide it.

He is seventy, thin, and courteous in a way that makes most people uneasy. He distrusts the knowers of the swamp towns and has twice tried to have [[being-kachima|Kachima]] of Ash put out. A party that needs the Keepers' leave for anything on the great mound needs his, and a party that can tell him something true about a knower is very welcome.

What he wants is Opahiko sent up to the sky with the full following the rites require.
