# Appearance {#appearance}

Kelkawa is a 69-year-old woman who stands 5'3" tall with a medium build. She has brown skin, smooth complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kelkawa is the eldest woman of the largest clan of [[place-ishochi|Ash]], the swamp-edge town of the [[place-wikopihirgn|Dark Water]] that takes in the households whose fires have been put out elsewhere in the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]]. The town's Mound Chief is a Noble sent there after a quarrel with the Keepers, who has decided he likes it. Its real authority is Kelkawa, who decides which put-out households stay.

The town says it was founded by the coast women whom [Pakalo]{.name data-type="being/npc"} took down the river the night before the [Funeral of the Hundred]{.name data-type="lore/history"}, and that every old family in it descends from one of them. Kelkawa's family is the oldest. She tells the story every New Fire, after the coal is lit, and the shell towns send a canoe to hear it.

:::secret {title="Pakalo's grave"}

Pakalo is buried in Ishochi's mound. He came to the town with the coast women, lived there twenty years, and was buried high in the mound's flank, which the Keepers would call a sacrilege: a traitor sent up with the sun. His bones are in a cedar box under the Mound Chief's house, and Kelkawa and three other clan mothers know which post marks it. If the Keepers learned of it, they would demand the bones, and the shell towns would go to war to stop them.

A party sent by the Keepers to look for anything in Ash is shown everything except what it came for.

What she wants is Pakalo's bones left where they lie, and the Keepers never told.

:::

She is sixty-nine, deaf in one ear and impossible to hurry. A party fleeing the heartland can ask her for a place in the swamp, and she asks hard questions before she gives one. A party sent by the Keepers is shown round Ash courteously and slowly.

What she wants is Ash kept a refuge, and the Keepers' men kept out of it.
