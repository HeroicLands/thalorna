# Appearance {#appearance}

Sohiyo is a 30-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a medium build. He has brown skin, sun-kissed complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sohiyo is a Sun of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]], a son of the Sun Mother [[being-shimaya|Shimaya]]'s sister and one of the Suns she can choose the next Great Sun from. Since the [Shell Promise]{.name data-type="lore/law"} was made, the Sun Mother has sent one boy of the Suns to be raised at [[place-koyohasko|Great Shell]] as its pledge, from his seventh year to his twentieth. Sohiyo was that boy. He ate the coast's fish, learned to handle a canoe on the gulf, and came back to the great mound of [[place-hayopokta|Sun Mound]] speaking Tokchili with a salt accent. The coast calls him its own Sun.

He is thirty, easy-mannered, a good swimmer and a poor dancer, and he is the rival of the Falcon Chief, [[being-naksochi|Naksochi]], for the choice the Sun Mother has not made. He says he would keep the promise, and the coast believes him.

:::secret {title="The coast's candidate"}

The Shell Chiefs mean to make Sohiyo the next Great Sun. They have told the Sun Mother through his mother that the coast will keep taking the coal if he is chosen, and that it will take the coal from nobody if the old Great Sun is buried with a single coastal attendant. [[being-otoki|Otoki]] of the Great Shell also knows what the captains of [[place-koyalolint|Last Shore]] found down the gulf, and means to offer that route to Sohiyo, and to no one else, for a lighter tribute.

:::

A party that comes up the river from the coast finds him an easy host and a curious one. A party that serves him well in the next year serves the man who may hold the fire on the great mound, and makes an enemy of the man who also wants it.

What he wants is to be chosen Great Sun, and to keep the Shell Promise whole once he is.
