# Appearance {#appearance}

Tinkapi is a 66-year-old woman who stands 5'1" tall with a heavy build. She has brown skin, smooth complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tinkapi is the eldest woman of the house descended from the sister of [Apiyo]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Eastern Sun, who lit a rival fire on the mound of [[place-tokchihinch|Other Fire]] at the end of the Second Gathering. For three lifetimes the hill towns of the [[place-poktaskorgn|Old Ranges]] were a nation of their own, and they remember that their Eastern Suns fed them better than the river did. The [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]] won them back without a war, and the town still carries its own coal from the great mound at the New Fire, by its own Keeper, walking.

The heartland ranks Tinkapi's line as Nobles. In Tokchi Hinchaka her household is called the Eastern Mothers, she is served at feasts before the Mound Chief, and the town's clan mothers bring their quarrels to her before they bring them to anyone else. She is sixty-six, tall and heavy, and she says little at the feasts she is served first at.

:::secret {title="The fire in the hills"}

The Other Fire was never put out. A day's walk up a side valley, in a house that looks like any hill farmer's, a woman of Tinkapi's household keeps a coal of Apiyo's fire alive in a clay pot, fed every day since the Scattering, and it passes from mother to daughter. Tinkapi does not mean to use it. She means to have it if the old Great Sun's funeral splits the nation, and the hill towns' clan mothers know where it is.

What she wants is a coal of the Eastern Sun's fire kept alive, and never needed.

:::

A party in the hill towns hears her name in every quarrel, and a party that needs the hills' help, salt, mica, skins or a safe road east, gets it through her or not at all.

What she wants is the hill towns' quarrels settled in the hills, and the river's men kept out of them.
