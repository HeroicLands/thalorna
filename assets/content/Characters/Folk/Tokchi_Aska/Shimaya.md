# Appearance {#appearance}

Shimaya is a 68-year-old woman who stands 5'2" tall with a light build. She has tawny skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Shimaya is the Sun Mother (_Hayo Hanna_) of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]], the eldest woman of the Suns, who chooses each Great Sun from among her sons and her sisters' sons and advises him for as long as she lives. She lives in the Suns' houses on the great mound of [[place-hayopokta|Sun Mound]], and the Great Sun, [[being-opahiko|Opahiko]], is her brother, old and dying. She has not yet named his heir, and the whole nation is waiting to see whom she chooses.

The two Suns who stand out are her own son, the Falcon Chief [[being-naksochi|Naksochi]], a war leader the Keepers of the Fire favor and the western towns love, and her sister's son, [[being-sohiyo|Sohiyo]], who was the coast's pledge at [[place-koyohasko|Great Shell]] for thirteen years and whom the Shell Chiefs call their own. Each man's friends tell her what the other's choice would cost. The Keepers want Opahiko buried with a full following; the Shell Chiefs say that one coastal death breaks the [Shell Promise]{.name data-type="lore/law"} the Sun Mother Hoyani made at the start of the Third Gathering.

:::secret {title="The coast's candidate"}

The Shell Chiefs have told her, through Sohiyo's mother, that the coast will keep taking the coal if Sohiyo is chosen, and that it will take the coal from nobody if the old Great Sun is buried with a single coastal attendant.

:::

She is sixty-eight, small, sharp-eyed and very hard to read. A party that brings her true news from the coast, the plains or the north is heard. A party that is caught carrying a message for either candidate is not heard again.

What she wants is an heir from whom every town of the nation takes the coal.
