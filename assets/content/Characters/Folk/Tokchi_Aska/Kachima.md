# Appearance {#appearance}

Kachima is a 60-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall with a medium build. She has dark brown skin, smooth complexion, dark brown hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kachima is a knower (_iklilo_) of [[place-ishochi|Ash]], the largest swamp-edge town of the [[place-wikopihirgn|Dark Water]], and the most famous knower in the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]]. The knowers heal, dream and read the swamp, and they bargain with the Water Panther for what it holds. She is said to have gone into [Ne' P'uqan]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the Black Water, for three days and come back with a drowned Mound Chief's son alive. She is also said to keep a seeing stone (_lokaka_), a stone as clear as water and no bigger than a thumb's end, fed twice a year with deer's blood, in which its keeper sees a sick person's chances, a lost man's road, an enemy coming up the river.

The Keepers of the Fire distrust the knowers, because a knower who can bargain with the Water Panther can also send it, and every accusation of witchcraft in the One Fire falls on a knower first. The Keepers have tried twice to have Kachima put out. Both times the swamp towns refused to hand her over. The Keepers hold that there is no such stone.

She is sixty, broad, slow-moving and amused by most things. Every knower in the nation is being asked what will happen to those who go with the old Great Sun, and she has been asked more often than any. A party with a sickness, a lost companion or a question about the swamp can hire her, and she sets her price by what she thinks the party can bear.

What she wants is the knowers left to work the swamp without the Keepers' leave.
