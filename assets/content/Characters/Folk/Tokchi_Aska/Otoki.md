# Appearance {#appearance}

Otoki is a 50-year-old man who stands 5'3" tall with a heavy build. He has tawny skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Otoki is the Shell Chief (_Koyo Imala_) of [[place-koyohasko|Great Shell]], the largest of the shell towns of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]]: an island of heaped shell cut by canals, the ground of the Shell Fair and home of the Sun's pledge. He is a broad, slow-spoken man of about fifty whose family has held the summit house for as long as the coast remembers. Thirty smaller towns send him tribute, and he sends part of it up the river to [[place-hayopokta|Sun Mound]] in shell, dried fish and canoes. At every New Fire he stands on the summit and recites the [Shell Promise]{.name data-type="lore/law"} word for word before he takes the coal, with a Keeper from the great mound beside him to say that the promise is kept. Otoki recites it louder than any Shell Chief on the coast.

The Keepers want the dying Great Sun buried with a full following, and Otoki has said that one coastal death, even a volunteer's, breaks the promise.

:::secret {title="The coast's candidate"}

The Shell Chiefs mean to make [[being-sohiyo|Sohiyo]], the pledge raised in the Great Shell, the next Great Sun. They have told the Sun Mother through his mother that the coast will keep taking the coal if he is chosen, and that it will take the coal from nobody if the old Great Sun is buried with a single coastal attendant. Otoki also knows what the captains of [[place-koyalolint|Last Shore]] found down the gulf, and he means to offer that route to Sohiyo, and to no one else, for a lighter tribute.

What he wants is a lighter tribute for the coast, and a Great Sun of the coast's own choosing to grant it.

:::

:::secret {title="The bell"}

The Last Shore's captain [[being-chasise|Chasise]] followed the coast south for forty days to a river mouth where a town of fishers traded with southern merchants, and came home with a cast copper bell of southern make. Otoki and the Last Shore's Shell Chief, [[being-tiloho|Tiloho]], have kept the voyage quiet, and the Keepers of the Fire at the great mound do not know that the route exists.

:::

A party at the Shell Fair deals with his people, and a party that wants anything carried up the river to the great mound can send it with his tribute canoes.

What he wants is the Shell Promise kept to the letter at the Great Sun's funeral.
