# Appearance {#appearance}

Opahiko is a 73-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a medium build. He has tawny skin, scarred complexion, white hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Opahiko is the Great Sun of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]], and he is old and dying. His fire burns on the great mound of [[place-hayopokta|Sun Mound]], the seat of the Third Gathering, which has lasted five Great Suns since [Tamoska]{.name data-type="being/npc"} the Gatherer founded the town and his mother [Hoyani]{.name data-type="being/npc"} made the [Shell Promise]{.name data-type="lore/law"} with the shell towns: no person of the coast is ever again given to a Sun's funeral.

When a Great Sun dies he is buried in the great mound with his attendants, and the quarrel over who goes with him opens again. The Eldest Keeper wants Opahiko buried with a full following. The Shell Chiefs say that one coastal death, even a volunteer's, breaks the Shell Promise, and they have let it be known that they will put their fires out. Every household in Hayo Pokta has an opinion, and every knower in the nation is being asked what will happen to those who go with him.

:::secret {title="Who the Shell Chiefs are talking to"}

Two Shell Chiefs have already sent canoes down the gulf coast, and one came back with a cast copper bell of southern make. If Opahiko's funeral breaks the promise, the coast means to deal with the south on its own account.

:::

The Sun Mother, [Shimaya]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, has not yet named his heir. Her son, the Falcon Chief [[being-naksochi|Naksochi]], wants a war before the Great Sun dies, to win the choice and to give the Keepers their following. Her sister's son, [Sohiyo]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, was the coast's pledge for thirteen years, and the Shell Chiefs call him their own.

What he wants is to die without his funeral breaking the Gathering.
