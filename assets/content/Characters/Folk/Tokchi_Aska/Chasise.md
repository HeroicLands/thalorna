# Appearance {#appearance}

Chasise is a 41-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a medium build. He has brown skin, sun-kissed complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Chasise is the best-known captain of [[place-koyalolint|Last Shore]], the southernmost shell town of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]]. Every spring, well before the storm season, the town's captains take their double canoes south along the coast and the islands farther than any heartland canoe has gone, and come back in early summer with shell, turtle, dried fish and stories. Chasise has made the southern run nine times and tells fewer stories than anyone. He is a lean, quiet man of forty-one who watches the sky more than the people he is talking to.

:::secret {title="The bell"}

Chasise has made the voyage both ways. Two years ago he followed the coast south for forty days to a river mouth where a town of fishers traded with southern merchants, and he came home with a cast copper bell of southern make, a bolt of dyed cotton and the knowledge that the southern merchants pay well for shell, feathers and slaves. His Shell Chief, [[being-tiloho|Tiloho]], and [[being-otoki|Otoki]] of [[place-koyohasko|Great Shell]] have kept the voyage quiet. They mean to sell the route to the next Great Sun, and the Keepers of the Fire at [[place-hayopokta|Sun Mound]] do not know that it exists.

He does not like what the southern merchants pay for, and he has not said so to his chief. What he wants is to sail south again, past the river mouth where the bell came from, and see what the merchants' own country is.

:::

A party that wants to go south by sea needs a captain who can find the way, and he is the best on the coast. He takes passengers who can paddle and keep quiet, and he sets his own course once the shore is out of sight.

What he wants is to sail south again, farther than the southern run goes, and see where the coast ends.
