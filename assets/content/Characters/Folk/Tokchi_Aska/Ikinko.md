# Appearance {#appearance}

Ikinko is a 62-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a medium build. He has tawny skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ikinko is the Mound Chief (_Poktimala_) of [[place-kiyopokta|Falcon Mound]], the double-walled town on the western bank where the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]] musters against the plains bands and the falcon dancers train. Every autumn, under the red pipe, the bands bring robes, dried meat, fat and hides to a trading ground below the outer wall, and the town's women trade maize, beans, pots and tobacco for them. Every spring and summer, when the pipe kinship has lapsed, the young men of both sides go raiding. Captives cross the grass in both directions.

Ikinko is an old warrior, sixty-two, with a limp from an arrow and a long memory. He knows the band leaders by name, and wants the autumn trade more than another war. He and the Falcon Chief, [[being-naksochi|Naksochi]], are civil to each other in the falcon house.

:::secret {title="The Falcon Chief's war"}

Naksochi wants a war west against the plains before the Great Sun dies, and Ikinko is quietly resisting it by warning the band leaders he trades with.

A party that rides with the plains bands, or speaks their tongue, is the kind of messenger he needs and cannot be seen to use.

:::

A party crossing between the One Fire and the grass passes his walls, and he can find it a band that will receive it.

What he wants is the autumn trade with the plains bands kept, and no war west while he holds the mound.
