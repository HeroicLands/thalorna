Lord Vaskan holds the [[place-southrnmrch|Southern March]], the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]]'s southern frontier district, on a commission from the League council. He hunts with trained beasts—hounds, hawks and coursing cats—and pays well for them, which is how a [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelathi]] beast-trainer came to work an upland estate a long way from [[place-zumeleshrvr|The Zumelesh]]. His choice of one trainer over another began a feud that has outlasted the commission that caused it.
