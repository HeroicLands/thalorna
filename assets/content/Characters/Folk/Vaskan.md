Lord Vaskan holds the Southern March, the Byzarian League's southern frontier district, on a commission from the League council. He hunts with trained beasts—hounds, hawks and coursing cats—and pays well for them, which is how a Khelâthi beast-trainer came to work an upland estate a long way from the Zumélesh. His choice of one trainer over another began a feud that has outlasted the commission that caused it.
