# Appearance {#appearance}

Orlen Salthar is a 49-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of heavy build. He is broad, short-necked and ruddy, with gray hair cropped close under a tarred cap and the forearms of a man who hauled rope for thirty years before he gave berths. The left forefinger ends at the first joint. He talks with his hands in his belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Orlen Salthar is the Reeve of the Harbor, chosen by the Quay-Councilors of [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]] from among themselves to preside over [[affiliation-sarmelisconcl|the Quay Council]]. He gives the berths, speaks to the sheriff and the Admiral's lieutenant for the town, and answers for the boom. He is a boat-master of forty-nine who still goes out in the herring season.

The council is divided on the Kalipsa trade. The outer quay's Kalipsan captains pay well for berths, and the Quay-Councilors of the inner quay hold that every barrel entered at the tally-house in part is a barrel the council has not weighed. Orlen holds the middle: he gives the Kalipsan ships their berths and gives the sheriff's clerk a place to stand beside them. An Order scholar newly up from Kalipsa is hiring the town's best young hands for guards, and the Fish-Reeve's motion to forbid any crew to sign with him in the herring season stands before the council, not carried.

## Psyche

### Personality

Orlen is shrewd, jovial in the Lamp and Oar and silent in council until he knows the count. He settles a quarrel by finding what each side will give up and making the other side give up the same.

### Motivation

He wants the quay to stay the town's and the herring season to find crews. He would sooner lose a Kalipsan berth than a boat-master's loyalty, and has not told either side so.

### Strengths

Every boat-master on the inner quay trusts his count. He knows the price of salt-fish before the Fish-Reeve has weighed it.

### Weaknesses

He holds the middle in a council that has no middle left. A vote on the scholar's guards will make him choose, and he has not decided which friends he can afford to lose.

## Social

### Companions

The Fish-Reeve weighs the catch and keeps the tally from which the north's price is set. The Keeper of the Boom answers to him when the Lamp Tower's fire is lit.

### Patrons

The boat-masters of the inner quay choose the councilors from whom he is chosen.

### Enemies

The Kalipsan factors who count him a friend, and the Fish-Reeve who counts him a hostage.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-sarmelisconcl|Quay Council of Sarmelis]]**: Orlen holds the office of Reeve of the Harbor at the rung of Quay-Freeman.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: He is a Freeman of the crown's town of Sarmelis.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The motion.** The Fish-Reeve wants a party to learn whose money is behind the Order scholar's guards before the council votes. Orlen wants the same answer and does not want the Fish-Reeve to have it first.
2. **The berth.** A Kalipsan captain has claimed a berth that belongs to a boat lost last winter, and the lost boat's master's widow has come to the council to contest it. Orlen asks a party to learn whether the Kalipsan captain was ever promised the berth, and by whom.
3. **The crew for the lugger.** A boat-master offers a party a winter's crew-share on a lugger and asks them to be at sea when the scholar sails. Orlen says the offer is the council's and wants to know who put the boat-master up to it.
