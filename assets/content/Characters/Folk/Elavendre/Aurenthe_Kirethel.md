# Appearance {#appearance}

Áurenthe Kírethel is a 51-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build. She has pale skin lined at the eyes, gray hair worn long and bound with a plain silver cord, and gray eyes that rest on a speaker until he has finished. Her fingers are long and calloused at the tips from the harp, which she plays well and only in private, and a thin white scar crosses her left palm. In court she wears dove-gray and green with nothing on her head; she keeps the crown for the crowning and for the dead. She listens far more than she speaks, and when she answers she chooses words that read two ways.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Queen Áurenthe Kírethel reigns over the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]] in her own right, from the court at [[place-beravel|Péravel]], and is the head of the royal [[affiliation-hsekirethel|House of Kírethel]]. Her consort is dead. Her heir is her only son, [[being-ielaraskir|Prince Yélaras]].

She wants to keep the balance every reign of her house has kept, and to keep it without incident, which none has managed. She keeps the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] warm inside the walls and guided beyond them, the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] content, and the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] teaching. She would like, once in her reign, to be asked up to [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa]], and she has not been asked.

## Background

**The house.** Áurenthe was born at Péravel, the eldest child of the reigning king, and was taught the harp by the house's bard and the law by the chancery. She learned the crown's line as a child does in Élavendre, at the feast, in the hearing of everyone who might dispute it, from the first crowned of Orethan to the king of Kírethel the market singers lament.

**The crowning.** She was crowned at thirty in the hall at Péravel. She stood bareheaded through [[lore-treelinelay|the Lay of the Tree Line]] and swore with both hands open that her road ends where the Wardens' keeping begins. Bards who sang at it say she did not move once through the whole of the lay.

**The reign.** She has reigned in the manner of her house. Order embassies are received at court and their scholars dine with her; an Order party that asks to go inward is given excellent guides out of [[place-liranel|Liranel]], and comes back with real notes about nothing of consequence. She has never asked what the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] do about the men with the sketchbooks, and takes considerable care not to.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is patient, courteous and very hard to hurry, with a long memory and no taste for open quarrels. Power casually shown is vulgar in Élavendre and power precisely applied is admired, and she is admired. She does not give a plain answer to an open thing.

### Motivation

She wants the crown handed on whole: the Order kept, the Áelendan kept, the Sinale relation carried by Áelendan intermediaries and not withdrawn. She knows that relation could be ended without a word said to anyone at Péravel ([[place-ethalosse|Ethalosse]]), and that her house's right to the throne rests on the old blood.

### Strengths

She has the crown's revenue from the river toll, the chancery and the sheriffs, a court that the Order, the [[affiliation-bardicolgs|Bardic Colleges]] and [[affiliation-groveconcl|the Grove Council]] all attend, and the goodwill of the mage [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Synod]] of Dúnavarre across the water.

### Weaknesses

Her son. Yélaras was schooled at the University and says in company that learning and the old blood are not enemies, and the bards have heard him. She knows the judgment the crown fears is never pronounced, only reached, and that it falls on a prince as easily as on a king.

## Social

### Family

- [[being-ielaraskir|Yélaras Kírethel]]: Her son and heir. She loves him, trusts his intelligence and does not trust his company.

### Others

- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|The Thordimalon]]**: Its scholars dine at her table, and she gives them guides.
- **[[affiliation-bardicolgs|The Bardic Colleges]]**: They keep her house's proof of descent and the satire against its founder, and she consults them on precedent, protocol and lineage.
- **[[affiliation-alndnwrdns|The Áelendan Wardens]]**: The crown governs the interior only through them, and they answer to the tribes, not to her.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The guides.** An Order expedition at court asks for guides to go inward, and the queen grants them at once. She hires the party, quietly, to go with it and see that the guides lead it somewhere real and of no consequence, and that it comes back.
2. **The man three valleys away.** An Order surveyor working the countryside on his own account has not come back, and his colleagues petition the queen to ask the Wardens where he is. She cannot ask. She would like to know the answer, and would like the party to be the ones who do not ask.
3. **The fair.** The queen sends a party up to [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]] for the autumn fair to hear what the market singers sing on the first night, and to tell her whether her son's name is in it.
