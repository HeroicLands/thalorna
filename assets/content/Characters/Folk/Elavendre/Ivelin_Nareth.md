# Appearance {#appearance}

Ivelin Nareth is a 43-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. He is eager and well fed, with brown hair kept short and hazel eyes bright with purpose. He dresses in University wool and carries a map he does not unfold.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ivelin Nareth is a fellow of [[affiliation-neumenos|Neumenos]] in the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]]. The Order keeps one made thing in a cellar at Péravel, folded and silent, and he wants a second that still walks. He hires sellswords at [[place-liranel|Liranel]] for a night in Siralonke and plans to be out of the forest before dawn.
