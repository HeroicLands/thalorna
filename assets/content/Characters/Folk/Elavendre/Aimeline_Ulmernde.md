# Appearance {#appearance}

Áimeline Úlmernde is a 34-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. She is tall and pale, with dark brown hair pinned high and blue eyes ringed with fatigue. Her smooth skin is paler than her family's, and she wears the Úlmernde green with a small silver flask at her belt. She moves slowly on bad days and does not allow anyone to say so.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Áimeline Úlmernde is a minor noblewoman of [[place-liranel|Liranel]] with a keen interest in alchemical remedies. She suffers from a rare blood disorder and commissions tonics and elixirs from [[being-sevnmyrkls|Sevren Mírkelis]], one of whose patrons she is. Her house holds a modest estate near the forest town and sits low in the order of precedence.

Sevren's way into the town runs through [[place-heligstrom|Hélithron]], where the men of [Úrthaven Tárvenel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a captain of hired guards out of Liranel, have been seen watching the road, and the alchemist will not walk it alone. Her tonics are late, and she has begun to ration them.

## Psyche

### Personality

Áimeline is witty, determined and private. She reads her own symptoms like a scholar and accepts no pity. She is generous with the people who treat her as a person.

### Motivation

She wants the disorder understood, if not cured, and Sevren kept alive and willing. She wants her house's place in the order of precedence recovered before she dies.

### Strengths

She has a clear mind and a long memory for cures. Her patronage is steady, and Sevren trusts her to pay.

### Weaknesses

Her health is the center of her life and her enemies know it. A late tonic is a bad week.

## Social

### Companions

A household nurse and the family's steward. The apothecaries of Liranel know her by her flask.

### Patrons

She is Sevren Mírkelis's patron.

### Enemies

Úrthaven Tárvenel, whose men watch the road her tonics travel.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Áimeline is a Noble, of a minor house of the forest country.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The late tonic.** Sevren has not come, and Áimeline's last tonic is three days gone. She hires a party to meet him at the edge of Hélithron and bring him in, whatever he has with him.
2. **A cure in the old wood.** A hedge-affiliate has told her of a root that grows only at the margin of the old forest. She asks a party to fetch it, by the crown's guide if they must, and not to cross the margin.
3. **The flask.** Her flask has been swapped for one like it, and she does not know who by or when. She asks a party to learn what the second flask held, and quietly.
