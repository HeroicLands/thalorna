# Appearance {#appearance}

Urnath Dórkimnde is a 44-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of light build. He is tall and lean, with dark blond hair swept back and a weathered face. His green eyes are heavy-lidded. He wears the green of a house of the basin, a great deal of rings and a cane with a silver head that he points when he speaks.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Urnath Dórkimnde is a nobleman of [[place-lamanis|Lámanis]], the orchard town of the middle basin, and the holder of its largest presses. He is called the Cruel, and the name is earned: his servants and dependents are beaten for a spilled cask, and the press-hands who run are returned in irons. It violated every principle that [[being-idriskrsvr|Ídrise Kórisvar]] holds, and she left his service.

Lámanis is held of the crown and keeps its own court of the orchard-owners, which meets in the cooperage hall at the turning of the year to settle boundaries, damages and the assessment. Urnath sits on it as the largest owner. The old orchard's holy well has gone dry, and the grove-elder who tries the cases that concern trees has written to the court at Péravel. Urnath wants no quarrel with the University that buys the cider, and he has said so at the court.

## Psyche

### Personality

Urnath is imperious, genial in company and cruel in private. He calls his servants by their trades and his guests by their titles. He keeps accounts of kindnesses done him and of offenses.

### Motivation

He wants the presses running, the University's order filled and no petition lying on the court's table at the autumn assessment. He wants to be owed.

### Strengths

He is rich, well connected at Péravel and generous to a patron. His presses pay the town's levy.

### Weaknesses

His people run, and the grove-elder has a ready ear for the ones who reach the old orchard. A man he has beaten has a long memory and an axe.

## Social

### Companions

A steward, three overseers and the cooperage master serve him. His bodyguard now is a hired man from the south.

### Patrons

The University's cider-buyers, who are loyal to the cider.

### Enemies

Gulmand Élvannde, who wants the well sung back to life. Ídrise Kórisvar, who left him.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Urnath is a Noble, the holder of the largest presses of Lámanis.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The runaway press-hands.** Two press-hands have reached the old orchard and the grove-elder will not give them up. Urnath will pay a party to bring them back quietly, and the grove-elder asks the same party to stand in the way.
2. **The assessment.** The orchard-owners' court sits at the turning of the year, and Urnath wants an inquest into the well's drying put off past the assessment. A party hired to find the lip-stone may find he has hired a second party to find it first.
3. **A guard for the cruel.** Urnath is hiring a bodyguard, having lost his last to the road, and is offering good wages to a stranger. Ídrise Kórisvar has refused him, and a party that takes the work learns why.
