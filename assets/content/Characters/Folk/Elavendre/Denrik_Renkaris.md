# Appearance {#appearance}

Denrik Rénkaris is a 48-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of scant build. He is thin and upright, with white hair and clear gray eyes, and he went into the wood in a Marendoi's traveling gown.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Denrik Rénkaris is a Marendoi of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]], a Master of the Art of [[affiliation-sideris|Zulen Sideris]] and a Fellow of the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]]. He went into [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]] under a leave to survey sealed in the Chancellor's household, with a hired company of Áuldalon sell-swords, a clerk and a guide from [[place-sourimael|Sóurimel]]. The Order records the expedition as lost in the northern interior, and the chapterhouse at Péravel holds his license and not his survey notes ([[place-rngtrndpths|The Ring of Turned Paths]]). A Lísere carter sold a quantity of Sórvanda silver to a master of Péravel about the time he set out.

## Psyche

### Personality

Denrik is courteous, proud and lucid.

### Motivation

He wants the Order's reading of the paths proved right, under his name.

### Strengths

He is a Master of the Art of metal, and he has surveyed under license for a decade.

### Weaknesses

He is proud, and he holds the Order's good opinion above his own safety.

## Social

### Companions

His clerk, and the sell-swords' sergeant [[being-parkeldarel|Parkel Darel]], who went into the wood with him.

### Patrons

The Fitter of Expeditions, [[being-pelraorsel|Pelra Orsel]], who fitted the survey and does not expect to see it again.

### Enemies

The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]], whose sacred ground lies across the district his leave names.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The lost survey.** A party that goes looking for the Order's lost expedition in the Silver Forests finds the only line in the wood that does not bend ([[scenario-pggdmnbrchrng|Eight Birches Wound in Silver]]).

:::secret {title="The working"}
Denrik came into the Silver Forests to prove that the Senali paths can be bound straight by metal. In a hollow ringed by eight living birch he drove thirty-one silver pegs, strung silver wire between them and worked a Straight Road upon it. The working took. Every straight line in the hollow now ends at the ring, and each dusk the working takes the footing of another walker ([[place-rngtrndpths#history|what was done at the Ring of Turned Paths]]).

He stands in the working's eye, alive because it needs him. A season inside it has wasted him: he looks thirty years older than he is, his skin is thin enough to show the silver threads that run under it from the wire at his feet, and his gray eyes are very tired. He wears the ruin of his traveling gown and stands upright on bare ground, because he cannot sit. He cannot undo the working without unmaking himself, and he has not asked the wood to turn the paths, because to ask is to admit that the Order's reading of the paths is wrong. He wants a relief: someone to hold the center in his place.

He knows he has made a mistake and speaks of it as a problem in the art. He wants out of the ring with his reading intact. He would rather be relieved than rescued, and rather rescued than proved wrong.

He holds the working and every walker in it. He can bind a foot to the ground, wind the wire round a limb and slow a body to a crawl, and nothing that walks to him reaches him. He cannot move, he cannot sit, and he has nothing left to protect but the Order's good opinion, which he is about to lose.

His clerk, who packed lead pegs plated in silver to confuse a rescuer, and Parkel Darel both walk the circuit now. The guide [[being-alenaalvrn|Alena Alverin]] has told him what she thinks of him every dusk for a season.

**Hook: the ring of birch.** A party that follows the silver wire finds the _marendos_ in the middle of the ring, asking whether they will take his place.

**Hook: the chest.** The _marendos_'s chest holds a decade of licensed survey: lintels, a spring-cup, a harp-key of white birch, each tagged with a place and a date. He bargains for his life with what he knows of where each came from.
:::

:::secret {title="The letter"}
The chest also holds the letter of leave to survey the Silver Forests, sealed in the Chancellor's household at Péravel, with the district written wide. Denrik knows the Clerk of the Leaves wrote it wide for the Fitter of Expeditions, and that whoever holds the letter holds the crown's cordiality toward the Order by the throat.
:::
