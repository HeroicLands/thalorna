# Appearance {#appearance}

Tralkar Hérselnde is a 43-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of heavy build. He is tall and broad, with auburn hair tied back, a close beard and a gold ring in the left ear. His green eyes are quick and bitter. He wears a merchant captain's good blue coat, a heavy belt and the manner of a man who expects to be listened to.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tralkar Hérselnde is a successful merchant captain of [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]] whose ship ignored the warnings of [[being-tarelblthl|Tárella Pelthal]] and met disaster. An investigation absolved her, but he still holds her responsible for his lost crew and missing cargo, and spreads the story that her predictions are unreliable.

The harbor's captains sit together in a mariners' guild that the [[affiliation-twncnclprtaln|Town Council]] hears on every question of the wharves, and Tralkar is one of them. The town's own people treat as plain fact what outsiders dismiss as sailors' tales: tides that follow their own rhythms, and travelers who vanish into the mist and reappear days later. He lost his crew in that kind of weather and will not use the word.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tralkar is proud, grieving and loud. He tells the story of the wreck in the same words each time and does not hear the question that follows. He is generous to his crews.

### Motivation

He wants a name to blame and a verdict he can live with. He wants the cargo's owners paid and his own name clean.

### Strengths

He is well liked on the quays and has the Guild's ear. He is a first-rate seaman.

### Weaknesses

He ignored a warning, and he knows it. The investigation absolved Tárella and does not absolve him, and his insistence is the only thing keeping him upright.

## Social

### Companions

His surviving mates and the widows of the crew he lost, whom he supports.

### Patrons

The mariners' guild, which has taken no side.

### Enemies

Tárella Pelthal. The Admiral, whose trust in her is public.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-twncnclprtaln|Town Council of Port Aelion]]**: Tralkar is a Freeman of the town.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: He is a Freeman of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The widows' petition.** The widows of the crew want the cargo's owners to answer, and Tralkar has agreed to stand with them. He asks a party to carry the petition to the Guild, and to keep the Admiral's name off it.
2. **The missing cargo.** Part of the lost cargo has surfaced in a Kalipsan hold. Tralkar asks a party to learn who sold it, and does not ask what the answer will mean for his crew.
3. **The warning he did not read.** A mate who survived the wreck says the astrologer's warning never reached the ship. Tralkar will pay to find out whether he is right, and will pay more not to have it said.
