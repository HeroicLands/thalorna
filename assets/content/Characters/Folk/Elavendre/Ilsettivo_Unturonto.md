# Appearance {#appearance}

Ilsettivo Unturonto is a 951-year-old man who stands 6'4" tall and is of light build. His black hair is cropped close, and his green eyes are never still. There is a notch in the rim of his right ear. He wears gray and moss-green and carries a short bow he has rarely drawn at anything but a deer.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ilsettivo Unturonto is a hunter of [[affiliation-nclvsrnthl|the Enclave of Serenthale]] who keeps the paths at the enclave's edge of the old forest of [[place-lirve|Lirve]]. The paths that lead to a Senali enclave lead elsewhere, and in the sea wood he is one of those who keep them leading elsewhere. Past the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]], who turn back anyone who goes too far into the country Serenthale is in, a walker meets the turning of the paths, and Ilsettivo is often near it.

He hunts deer in the deep wood for the enclave, and knows the Wardens of [[place-siralonke|Siralonke]] by sight and by step. They have not seen him.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is watchful, dry and sparing of words.

### Motivation

He wants the searchers out of the deep wood alive and unaware of how near they came. The Senali do not fight, and he does not want a lost searcher on Serenthale's account.

### Strengths

He moves through the sea wood unseen, and the paths turn at his will.

### Weaknesses

He can turn a walker but not drive him out, and a determined party lost in the deep wood is in danger of more than lostness.
