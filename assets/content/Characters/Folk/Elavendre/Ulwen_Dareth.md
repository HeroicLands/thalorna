# Appearance {#appearance}

Ulwen Dareth is a 38-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. He is stooped and pale, with black hair thin on top and brown eyes narrowed from close work. His fingers are stained with ink to the second joint.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ulwen Dareth is a copyist of the book quarter of [[place-beravel|Péravel]]. Someone paid him to copy a page of cipher and did not collect it. He does not understand it, and he hires a party to learn what it says.
