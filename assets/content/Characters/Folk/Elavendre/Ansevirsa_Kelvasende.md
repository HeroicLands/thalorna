# Appearance {#appearance}

Ánsevirsa Kélvasende is a 48-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of medium build. She is round-faced and freckled, with chestnut hair under a plain linen cap and brown eyes behind spectacles of Vilérian glass, which she wears on a cord and puts on to read and to refuse. Her sleeves are always pinned back from the wrist.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ánsevirsa Kélvasende is the Treasurer of the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]. The treasury she heads at [[place-beravel|Péravel]] takes the toll on the river traffic through the capital, a fixed share of every pilot's fee from [[place-sirlathen|Sirlathen]] and the road tolls the sheriffs collect, and it pays the crown's ships, its militia and its court. She is of a gentry family of the book quarter that has kept the counting-tables of the river for three generations, and she came to the office from the river toll-house, where she was the first clerk to find a bargemaster's false bottom with a plumb.

She wants the crown solvent and its revenue counted honestly at every desk it crosses. The treasury is short this year. The sheriffs' road tolls come in late, the wall money from [[place-valdun|Valdun]] comes in thin, and the furs counted at the causeway of the Morthal March do not agree with the furs counted at [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]]. She has begun to send her own clerks to read the sheriffs' rolls, and the sheriffs have noticed.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ánsevirsa is dry, patient and immovable on a sum. She is warm to clerks and cool to lords.

### Motivation

She wants every toll entered where it is taken and every entry to agree with the next, from the tally-post to the treasury chest.

### Strengths

She can read a roll faster than its keeper can explain it. The Queen trusts her figures, and the goldsmiths of Péravel trust her assay.

### Weaknesses

She has no house to stand behind her, and a gentlewoman who audits knights has made enemies who outrank her.

## Social

### Companions

The treasury clerks, the toll-house clerks of the river, and the goldsmith [[being-sheimarathol|Shéimara Thólmarnde]], whose assay the treasury uses.

### Patrons

[[being-aurenthekir|Queen Áurenthe]], who named her.

### Enemies

The Chancellor, [[being-hlvlnsrdlnd|Hálvilon Sérdalnde]], who keeps the chancery's seal fees in the chancery. The sheriffs whose rolls she reads, among them [[being-lotrenilther|Lotren Ílthernde]] of Valdun, who answers to her for the wall money.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Ánsevirsa holds the office of Treasurer, as Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The furs.** The Tally-Serjeant's count of the furs that crossed the causeway in autumn does not agree with the count at Sarmelis. The Treasurer hires a party to ride the bay road and find out which count is wrong.
2. **The wall money.** Valdun's wall money has come in thin three years running. Ánsevirsa wants the sheriff's hall at Valdun read by someone who is not her clerk and not the sheriff's.
3. **The pilot's share.** A pilot of Sirlathen has paid the treasury's share in clipped coin. The Treasurer wants to know who clipped it before she asks the Master Pilot.
