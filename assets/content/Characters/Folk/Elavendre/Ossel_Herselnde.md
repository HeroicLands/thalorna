# Appearance {#appearance}

Ossel Hérselnde is a 70-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. He is stooped and wiry, with white hair cut close, a deeply lined face burned dark by sixty years of shore wind, and pale blue eyes that still find a light on the water before the young ones do. Both his palms are scarred from the beacon baskets. He walks the shingle-stone paths without a stick and does not like to be offered one.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ossel Hérselnde is the Chain-Keeper of [[affiliation-beaconfamlys|the Beacon Families]], the head of the oldest line of a beacon station on the stretch of the [[place-lisere|Lísere]] shore toward [[place-durmelen|Durmelen]]. The council chose him to hold the roster of stations, the order of the signals and the tally of fires, and he speaks for the chain to the Castellan at Durmelen, [[being-morenothral|Moren Othral]], whose father he served with.

The chain is under strain. An Order scholar's gold at [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]] is pulling lamp-hands off the stations, and a winter beacon with one man on it is a beacon a raid can darken. Ossel has asked the council to forbid its lamp-hands to hire out, and the council is divided. Three beacons between Hendrel and Sarmelis failed to answer on the same night, and he has not been told why.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ossel is gruff, exact and stubborn. He says the signals aloud in his sleep, his household says.

### Motivation

He wants every lamp lit and answered through the dark nights. He wants the young hands at their stations and not on the Order's payroll.

### Strengths

He knows every station, every headland and every signal on the shore, and every Head of a Line owes him a favor.

### Weaknesses

He is seventy, and he cannot ride the stations himself any more.

## Social

### Companions

The lamp-hands of his own station, who are his grandsons.

### Patrons

The council of the Beacon Families, which chose him, and the Castellan at Durmelen.

### Enemies

The Order scholar's quartermaster, who hires the hands he needs.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-beaconfamlys|The Beacon Families]]**: Ossel is a Head of a Line and holds the office of Chain-Keeper.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: He is a Freeman of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The dark chain.** Ossel asks a party to ride the stations from Hendrel to Sarmelis and learn why three beacons failed to answer on the same night.
2. **The vote.** He wants to know whose money pays the Order scholar's guards before the council of the Beacon Families votes on his motion.
3. **A beacon short.** A station on his own stretch is down to one lamp-hand, and Ossel will pay a party to stand its watch for the dark of the moon.
