# Appearance {#appearance}

Torlan Salreth was a 29-year-old man who stood 5'10" tall and was of medium build. He was fair and long-limbed, with blonde hair and blue eyes, and he wore a charcoal-burner's cloak with a red patch on the shoulder, which made him easy to know at a distance.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Torlan Salreth was a charcoal-burner's son of [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]] who went poaching with [[being-vendrikothrl|Vendrik Othral]]'s band for the money. The band hunts the deer of [[place-hntrsrng|the Hunter's Ring]] from the old keepers' lodge below it.

:::secret {title="The first night"}
On the first night after the band robbed the envoy, Torlan stepped away from the band's fire in the Hunter's Ring and was taken. The next dusk he walked back to the keepers' lodge in his own red-patched cloak and called [[being-norvenhalren|Norven Halren]] by name through the door. The hunter wears him still.

He was taken in the last days of 719, one night before [[being-astherenwrdn|Ástheren]] died.
:::
