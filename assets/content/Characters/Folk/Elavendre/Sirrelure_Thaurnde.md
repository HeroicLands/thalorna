# Appearance {#appearance}

Sírrelure Thaurnde is a 64-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of light build. She is tall and very still, with white hair worn long and unbound, pale skin and gray eyes with a violet cast in lamplight. Every fingertip carries a harp-callus. Her speaking voice is low and carries farther than it should.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sírrelure Thaurnde is the Master of [[affiliation-clgwoodsng|the College of the Wood-Song]] at [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]], the College of [[affiliation-bardicolgs|the Bardic Colleges]] that keeps the old magic of voice and story, closely held. She decides which workings are shown to which pupil, and the Keeper of the Closed Songs holds what is shown to none. In the thaw she takes the College's older bards up the valleys to hear what the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] will sing.

The College has a petition before [[affiliation-groveconcl|the Grove Council]]: that one working, long suppressed, be allowed for a single performance. The Council has not ruled on it in a year, and the Circle meets on it at every sitting. Sírrelure holds that the working is the College's to judge and the Council's only to hear of, and the grove-elders hold otherwise.

## Psyche

### Personality

Sírrelure is grave, proud and patient. She waits a long time for an answer and then acts as if it had been given.

### Motivation

She wants the petition granted and the working sung once, as it was made to be. She wants the College's closed songs kept closed from the Order.

### Strengths

She is the finest singer of the old workings alive, and the tribes' singers will sing in her hearing.

### Weaknesses

She has begun to believe that the Council's silence is permission.

## Social

### Companions

The older bards of the College, who go up with her in the thaw.

### Patrons

The Bardic Circle, and the Áelendan singers who share their songs with her.

### Enemies

The grove-elders who sit on her petition, and the Order fellows who would pay for one closed song.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-clgwoodsng|The College of the Wood-Song]]**: Sírrelure is a Bard of the Circle and holds the office of Master of the College.
- **[[affiliation-bardicolgs|The Bardic Colleges]]**: She sits in the Bardic Circle as Master of a College.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: She is Gentry of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Council's answer.** Sírrelure asks a party to carry the College's petition to the Grove Council at the next hinge-day and to come back with an answer, any answer.
2. **A closed song sold.** An Order fellow at Elanmere has been heard humming a closed song of the College. She wants to know which of her bards taught it to him.
3. **The thaw.** She needs companions for the walk up the valleys in the thaw, because the Wardens have closed one of the ways she has always taken.
