# Appearance {#appearance}

Taldor Lidren is a 34-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of scant build. He is thin, bearded and nearly mad, with matted brown hair and brown eyes that do not settle. He wears the rags of a surveyor's coat.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Taldor Lidren is a surveyor of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] who went down into [[place-hldhr|the Held Hour]] on a rope with a sighting-rod and a water-clock, meaning to be drawn up in an hour. His colleagues wait at the lip. His notes are the most exact record of an Araelwen the Order has ever had.

:::secret {title="What became of the surveyor"}
Taldor waited a month on the floor for the rope to move, and cut it himself because it hung slack. He lives on the last of his stores and the rain that runs down the rock, and keeps a tally of his sleeps on the stone.
:::
