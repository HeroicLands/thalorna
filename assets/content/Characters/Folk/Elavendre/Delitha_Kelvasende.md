# Appearance {#appearance}

Délitha Kélvasende is a 60-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of light build. She is a short, slight woman with red hair under a plain linen cap and a calm, smooth, fair face. Her green eyes are warm and see everything. Her hands are stained with garden soil to the wrist, whatever the season.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Délitha Kélvasende is the senior priestess of the temple of [[affiliation-lizelas|Lizelas]] at [[place-liranel|Liranel]]. She is a Sarnor of the faith, a healer, a midwife and a gardener, and she keeps the temple's infirmary and herb garden at the edge of the forest town. She respects the work of [[being-velarithrl|Velthra Othral]] among the poor, but has warned that the temple cannot indefinitely support a ministry that generates no revenue and serves a population with no political voice.

Liranel is where people hire guides into the old wood, and where some families wait for someone who does not come back. They come to the temple to sit in the garden and to ask the priestesses whether anything can be done. Délitha has no answer to give them, and the garden's bench has never been empty.

## Psyche

### Personality

Délitha is patient, practical and plainspoken. She is not sentimental about the poor and never lets one be turned away. She prays in the garden before dawn.

### Motivation

She wants the temple solvent and the infirmary open. She wants Velthra to survive her own generosity, and she wants a quiet place in the garden for the families who wait.

### Strengths

Liranel trusts her. Her herb-lore is deep, and the temple's infirmary treats the guides' wounded and the foresters' alike.

### Weaknesses

The temple's allowance is small and the poor are many. A hard word to Velthra costs her a friend, and a soft one costs the temple.

## Social

### Companions

Two acolytes of the Garden and the infirmary's nurse. Velthra Othral reports to the temple at her direction.

### Patrons

The faith's High Priestess holds the allowance Délitha spends.

### Enemies

The Forester's clerks, who want the herb garden's licensed timber measured. Nobody else that she would name.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-lizelas|Faith of Lizelas]]**: Délitha holds the office of Sarnor, ordained, as the temple's senior priestess.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: She is of the Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The warning to Velthra.** Délitha asks a party to carry the temple's last offer to Velthra Othral, who does not come to the temple often, and to stay for the answer.
2. **The families who wait.** A family at the garden bench has waited a season for a guide who was last seen at the margin. Délitha asks a party to learn where he was last seen, so that she can say something true.
3. **The infirmary's guests.** A man has come into the infirmary with a wound that is not a forester's, and will not say how he got it. The Forester's clerks and a Warden's runner have both asked for him, and Délitha will not give him up to either until a party tells her which should have him.
