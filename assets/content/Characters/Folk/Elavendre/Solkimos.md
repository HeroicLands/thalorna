# Appearance {#appearance}

Solkimos stands 5'9" tall and is of light build, and looks thirty-six. He is spare and hard, with black hair cropped in the legion's fashion and dark eyes in a face carved by cold, and old frostbite has eaten the rims of both ears. He wears an officer's harness of the old striking, rotted at every strap and buckled tight, and a short sword he has not drawn in a long time.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Solkimos is a _Lusar_ of the [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|Vilérian Military]], a tribune of a legion that struck into the hills of [[place-auldalon|Áuldalon]] in the war that ended at [[place-valdun|Valdun]] ([[lore-elvtribute|The Tribute of Élavendre]]). His cohort followed a path at the forest margin and was not seen again, and the _felzos_'s house at [[place-beravel|Péravel]] records it as deserted. The drovers say the men in the hall under the hill they call [[place-kptfr|the Kept Fire]] are the ones who stayed. He receives strangers at the head of that hall with perfect courtesy and asks each whether they have come to take the door.

## Psyche

### Personality

Solkimos is courteous, rigid and very tired. He gives an order once and expects it obeyed, and he has not had an order of his own in a long time.

### Motivation

He wants to be relieved. A Vilérian _Lusar_ does not leave a post he swore to hold.

### Strengths

The cohort obeys him absolutely, in good order with locked shields. He knows every name on the roll and every stone of the hall.

### Weaknesses

He is rigid, and he has held one order so long that he cannot see past it.

## Social

### Companions

His senior sergeant, [[being-thelvarnkpt|Thelvaran]], and the Kept of his cohort.

### Enemies

Anyone who comes to take the hall rather than relieve it.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vylrnmltry|Vilérian Military]]**: Solkimos holds the rank of _Lusar_ in a legion of the old army, and the cohort he commands is on no roll but its own.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The relief.** A party that reaches the head of the hall is asked, courteously, whether it will take the door ([[scenario-drkptfr|The Roll Beneath the Hill]]).

:::secret {title="The oath"}
Solkimos is 589 years old, and he has looked thirty-six since the winter he took the door. Harried toward the forest margin, starving and frostbitten, he led his cohort on a winter night into a fold of the hills and found a hall with a lit door. He did not ask. He demanded shelter in the Sovereign's name, and the one within, the Giver, gave exactly what was demanded: a hall, a fire and bread. He swore that his cohort would hold the place against all comers while the Sovereign's name stood, and the Giver took him at his word ([[place-kptfr|The Kept Fire]]).

He holds it still. His men do not age, do not sleep and cannot leave, and the roll of those who hold the door grows by every guest who eats the hall's bread. He will not ask a barbarian spirit for anything, so he waits for a relief that has not come. He cannot see the one way out, because it is asking. He believes the oath binds him, when it was he who bound it.

**Hook: the tablets.** The _Lusar_'s tablets of record show that the cohort held its post and was never recalled. A clerk of the _felzos_'s house at Péravel would pay for them, and the cohort's descendants are owed its arrears.

His patron is the Sovereign whose name he swore by, who is centuries dead.
:::

:::secret {title="The door"}
The Giver did not hold the cohort, and the door has stood open the whole time. The Giver answers an asking and nothing else. If Solkimos asks it to release the cohort, it does, and the Kept's years come due at once: they die in the gray before dawn, most of them on the hill's slope with their faces to the east, and the _Lusar_ dies last.
:::
