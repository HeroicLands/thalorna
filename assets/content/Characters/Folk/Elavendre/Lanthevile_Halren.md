# Appearance {#appearance}

Lánthevile Halren is a 52-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of heavy build. She is broad and sun-browned, with red hair braided down the back and brown eyes that look more at the ground than at faces, a squint line at each. She wears the grovekeeper's plain band of unworked wood, a brown wool dress kilted for walking and good boots that she has resoled many times. A rolled map is tucked in her belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lánthevile Halren is a grovekeeper of the [[affiliation-groveconcl|Grove Council]] attached to a grove near several sites of [[affiliation-blackflame|Black Flame]] activity. She investigates the burned shrines and scorched clearings in the forests around [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]] and maps where they fall. She brings her reports to [[being-dharnvldrn|Daran Véldarin]] and to council member [[being-glyencrdth|Lyenna Kardeth]], asking for a more thorough investigation.

Elanmere is the high town of the western foot of the mountains, where mountain cheese and herbs and the tribes' small release of silver come down to the lake shore. The lake is understood to be inhabited, and the boatmen keep observances about where a net may be put down. A grovekeeper who walks the forest edge knows where the observances stop.

## Psyche

### Personality

Lánthevile is patient, stubborn and methodical. She draws what she sees, writes what she infers on a separate page and does not confuse them. She believes in due process.

### Motivation

She wants the burnings understood and stopped. She wants the Council to act and the crown's garrison to take her map seriously.

### Strengths

She knows the forest and the lake. Her map is the best record of the burnings that exists, and she has dated each site by the growth of ferns.

### Weaknesses

She trusts the wrong audience. She has not told the Council what her reports have turned up, and has told the people who have the most to lose.

## Social

### Companions

The villagers of the lake road bring her news of fires. A fisherman rows her to the far shore.

### Patrons

The Grove Council, whose circlet she is working toward.

### Enemies

Whoever lit the fires.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-groveconcl|The Grove Council]]**: Lánthevile is a Grovekeeper.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: She is a Freeman of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The map.** Lánthevile asks a party to carry her map and a letter to the Grove Council at the next hinge-day, and to bring back the Council's answer.
2. **A night at a burned shrine.** She wants to see a burning begun, and asks a party to sit a night at the next shrine on her map with a lantern.
3. **The unburned grove.** A grove between two burned ones has not been touched. She asks a party to find out who is keeping it safe, and to do it before she names the keeper.

:::secret {title="To whom she brings her reports"}
Daran Véldarin is a priest of the Black Flame who leads a cell reaching across the region, and Lyenna Kardeth serves the same cell. Lánthevile is mapping the burned shrines and scorched clearings for the people who made them.

Neither has stopped her. A map in their hands tells them what the Council has learned.

**Hook: the map.** If a party warns her, she will not believe it. If a party shows her her own map in Daran's hand, she will.
:::
