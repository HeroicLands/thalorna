# Appearance {#appearance}

Nélamis Ólnarnde is a 40-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. She is tall, fair and elegant, with dark blonde hair dressed high, a beauty mark at the corner of the mouth and amber eyes that look amused at the world. She dresses in the best that Péravel's tailors make, in dark jewel colors, and carries a silver chain with a theater-box key. She smiles often and gives away nothing.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nélamis Ólnarnde is a noblewoman of [[place-valdun|Valdun]] of considerable wealth and refined taste. She is the patron of [[being-veldrblthl|Veldor Pelthal]] and of the theater, and her box is the best in the town's playhouse. She spends freely on the players and the painters, and the town counts her a friend of the arts.

Valdun is a market town on a border that has never been quiet. The wall is rebuilt every few generations, the gates shut at dusk, and the hill levy musters under the town's eyes. A patron with a free hand and a house on the ridge road is received in every circle, and no one asks what the box is for.

## Psyche

### Personality

Nélamis is witty, warm and thorough. She remembers the name of every player's child and every councilor's mistress. She is generous without being careless.

### Motivation

She wants the theater thriving, her players safe and her reputation unmarked. She wants a certain kind of friend in a certain kind of room.

### Strengths

She is wealthy and well connected. She hears everything said in the town's best seats.

### Weaknesses

She trusts the patrons she cultivates and the players she shelters. A scandal at the playhouse reaches her through the box.

## Social

### Companions

Veldor Pelthal and the playhouse company. Her steward and her coachman.

### Patrons

None. She is independently wealthy.

### Enemies

Rival patrons and a magistrate or two. Those who do not know her cover and those who do.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Nélamis is a Noble, of a house of the eastern march.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The box.** Nélamis has lent her box for the evening to a councilor of the town who is new to her circle. She asks a party to attend as her guests and tell her what he says in the interval.
2. **The player's debt.** A player of the company owes the wrong people a great deal. Nélamis asks a party to buy the debt before the creditors realize whose patron he is.
3. **The new play.** The company means to stage a satire on a lord of the march. Nélamis asks a party to read it first and say how many powerful people it will offend.

:::secret {title="The patron's cover"}
Her patronage of the theater is a cover for her part in a network of rebel agents working to undermine the current regime. She has told no one outside the network, and she has begun to look among her players for a man who can use his access to noble circles to gather intelligence on a potential conspirator.

**Hook: the interval.** The councilor her guests overhear is the person she is watching. A party cannot tell, from the box, whether he is the conspirator or her contact.
:::
