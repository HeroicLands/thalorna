# Appearance {#appearance}

Nélamis Ólnarnde is a 40-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. She is tall, fair and elegant, with dark blonde hair dressed high, a beauty mark at the corner of the mouth and amber eyes that look amused at the world. She dresses in the best that Péravel's tailors make, in dark jewel colors, and carries a silver chain with the key of the company's strongbox. She smiles often and gives away nothing.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nélamis Ólnarnde is a noblewoman of [[place-valdun|Valdun]] of considerable wealth and refined taste. She is the patron of [[being-veldrblthl|Veldor Pelthal]] and of the company of players he leads, pays its winter keep and hosts its best nights in her own hall. She spends freely on the players and the painters, and the town counts her a friend of the arts.

Valdun is a market town on a border that has never been quiet. The wall is rebuilt every few generations, the gates shut at dusk, and the hill levy musters under the town's eyes. A patron with a free hand and a house on the ridge road is received in every circle, and no one asks what the company's nights are for.

## Psyche

### Personality

Nélamis is witty, warm and thorough. She remembers the name of every player's child and every knight's mistress. She is generous without being careless.

### Motivation

She wants the company thriving, her players safe and her reputation unmarked. She wants a certain kind of friend in a certain kind of room.

### Strengths

She is wealthy and well connected. She hears everything said in the town's best seats.

### Weaknesses

She trusts the patrons she cultivates and the players she shelters. A scandal in the company reaches her through her hall.

## Social

### Companions

Veldor Pelthal and the company of players. Her steward and her coachman.

### Patrons

None. She is independently wealthy.

### Enemies

Rival patrons and a magistrate or two. Those who do not know her cover and those who do.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Nélamis is a Noble, of a house of the eastern march.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The guest.** Nélamis has seated a knight of the march who is new to her circle beside her for the company's first night of the winter. She asks a party to attend as her guests and tell her what he says in the interval.
2. **The player's debt.** A player of the company owes the wrong people a great deal. Nélamis asks a party to buy the debt before the creditors realize whose patron he is.
3. **The new play.** The company's play-maker, [[being-prthncrdth|Parthun Kardeth]], has written a satire on a lord of the march. Nélamis asks a party to read it first and say how many powerful people it will offend.

:::secret {title="The patron's cover"}
Her patronage of the players is a cover for her part in a network of rebel agents working to undermine the current regime. She has told no one outside the network, and she has begun to look among her players for a man who can use his access to noble circles to gather intelligence on a potential conspirator.

**Hook: the interval.** The knight her guests overhear is the person she is watching. A party cannot tell, from the box, whether he is the conspirator or her contact.
:::
