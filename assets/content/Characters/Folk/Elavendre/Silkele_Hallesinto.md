# Appearance {#appearance}

Sìlkele Hallesinto is a 3,368-year-old woman who stands 6'0" tall and is of light build. Her silver hair is braided in one long rope, and her eyes are green. Her voice is low in speech and carries a long way in song.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sìlkele Hallesinto is a lord of [[affiliation-nclvsrnthl|the Enclave of Serenthale]] and the enclave's singer of the long songs. She was raised for a winter in which three of Serenthale's singers died, when she sang every song they had held, night after night, until each was in another mouth. A Senali praise-song praises the deed and withholds the doer, and the song Serenthale made of that winter does not name her.

Every song Serenthale keeps is in her keeping or in the keeping of someone she taught. None of them has come out of the enclave. The songs the [[affiliation-bardicolgs|Bardic Colleges]] treasure as Senalian gifts came out of [[place-aelirosse|Aelirosse]] and [[place-ethalosse|Ethalosse]], never Serenthale.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is grave and exact, and impatient only with a song sung carelessly.

### Motivation

She wants every song of Serenthale held whole in at least two mouths, and she wants none of them sung outside the enclave.

### Strengths

She has more of the old songs than any Senalo of Lirve, word for word.

### Weaknesses

She will not hand a song to anyone she has not judged ready, and she judges slowly.
