# Appearance {#appearance}

Tenral Salthar is a 50-year-old man who stands 6'2" tall and is of light build. He is tall, lean and brown, with blond hair cut short and a smooth face tanned by salt and sun. His gray eyes narrow against glare. He wears the admiral's dark blue, plain and well worn, and moves with the rolling gait of a man who has spent more years on decks than ashore.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tenral Salthar is the captain-commander and supreme naval commander of [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Élavendre]]'s maritime forces, the Admiral of the crown's ships and coast-cutters. He keeps his flag at [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]], the kingdom's chief harbor, and he commands the Lieutenants who keep the cutters at the harbors. He employs [[being-tarelblthl|Tárella Pelthal]] for predictions.

Port Aelion's tides sometimes follow their own rhythms, and travelers occasionally vanish into the mist and reappear days later claiming that no time has passed. The Admiral has a cutter's log that agrees with the second claim and a sea that disagrees. He trusts an astrologer more than a pilot in a fog, and the Mariners' Guild has noticed.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tenral is crisp, pragmatic and close-mouthed. He listens to the whole of a report before speaking and gives orders in a sentence. He does not discuss his reasons.

### Motivation

He wants the coast held with the ships he has and the harbor open. He wants a forecast he can use and a report he can show the queen's chancery.

### Strengths

His officers follow him. He knows the Lísere coast, the Nordmen's habits and Port Aelion's fogs.

### Weaknesses

He is leaning on an astrologer's word in a court that distrusts astrologers. A lost cutter is a lost cutter, and a wreck with an investigation is a wreck with a rumor.

## Social

### Companions

His flag-lieutenant and the Lieutenants of the Admiral at Sarmelis and elsewhere. The harbor-clerk files his counts.

### Patrons

The queen's chancery and the Treasury at Péravel.

### Enemies

Tralkar Hérselnde, whose public blame of Tárella has begun to reach the Admiral by implication.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Tenral holds the office of Admiral at the rung of Knight.
- **[[affiliation-twncnclprtaln|Town Council of Port Aelion]]**: He is a Freeman of the town where he keeps his flag.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The log that disagrees.** A cutter of the Admiral's came into Port Aelion three days late, and her log shows a single night. Tenral asks a party to sit with her crew and learn what they will say off the record.
2. **The forecast.** Tárella has told the Admiral that a fleet will come in on the next ebb, and the Mariners' Guild has refused to move its ships. He asks a party to learn whether the Guild is being paid.
3. **The slander.** Tralkar Hérselnde's public blame of Tárella has begun to stick. Tenral wants it stopped and will not be the one to stop it.
