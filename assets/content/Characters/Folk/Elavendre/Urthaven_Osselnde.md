# Appearance {#appearance}

Úrthaven Ósselnde is a 42-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of heavy build. He is stout and ruddy, with black hair oiled flat and brown eyes that water in the cold. He dresses well and plainly, in the crown's gray with a broad belt, and wears the Butler's taster's cup of silver on a chain round his neck.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Úrthaven Ósselnde is the Butler of the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]. He holds the crown's right in wine and cider and its levy on them, and his levy-clerks collect it at the quays of [[place-beravel|Péravel]], [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]] and [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]] and at the cooperages of the Éllive orchards. He is of a gentry family of wine-factors on the Péravel quay and bought the factor's trade out of his own pocket when the Queen named him, because a Butler who trades in wine is a Butler nobody trusts.

He wants the levy collected in full and on time, and he wants it collected on cider as on wine. The crown's right in cider is old and was not taken for a century, and he took it again. The orchard town of [[place-lamanis|Lámanis]] has not forgiven him, and its court of the orchard-owners petitions the crown each winter to give the cider back.

His other trouble is the bay. Kalipsan wine comes ashore at the outer quay of Sarmelis and on the beaches of the Lísere shore, and much of it never meets a levy-clerk. He believes the smuggler [[being-dorlenkirs|Dorlen Kirsal]] runs most of it and has never been able to prove so.

## Psyche

### Personality

Úrthaven is genial, shrewd and stubborn. He likes his wine and does not let anyone see him drink more than a taster's cup.

### Motivation

He wants the levy whole, wine and cider, river and bay, and the treasury to stop calling his office the leakiest in the kingdom.

### Strengths

He knows every vintage on the river by smell and every factor on the quay by name. A cask cannot be relabeled in front of him.

### Weaknesses

He is hated in the orchards and laughed at in Sarmelis, and his levy-clerks on the bay are too few to watch the beaches.

## Social

### Companions

His levy-clerks, and a cooper of Lámanis who still drinks with him.

### Patrons

The Treasurer, [[being-nsvrskelvas|Ánsevirsa Kélvasende]], into whose chest the levy goes.

### Enemies

The orchard-owners of Lámanis, and Dorlen Kirsal and whoever on the Kalipsa run buys his wine.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Úrthaven holds the office of Butler, as Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The beach casks.** The Butler pays a party to watch a beach of the Lísere shore on a moonless night and count the casks that come ashore.
2. **The cider petition.** The orchard-owners of Lámanis send a party to Péravel with their winter petition and a cask of the year's best, and the Butler receives them at his own table.
3. **The relabeled cask.** A cask sold at court as the crown's own vintage holds Kalipsan wine. Úrthaven wants to know whose cellar it came through.
