# Appearance {#appearance}

Issaltela Liserinto is a 2,406-year-old woman who stands 6'2" tall and is of light build. Her pale gold hair is worn long under a gray hood, and her green eyes are bright. Her speaking voice is quiet, and her singing voice fills the court of worked stone.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Issaltela Liserinto is a lord of [[affiliation-nclvthlss|the Enclave of Ethalosse]]. When the court at [[place-beravel|Péravel]] asked how the [[lore-senaliclt|Senali]] hold that the world ends, she gave the two carriers [[lore-gatheringend|The Gathering on the End]] at Ethalosse over three evenings, singing the speeches and saying the rest. When the court sent them up again for the gathering's answer in one word, she gave them its standing the same day: "We have not agreed. Ask again when you have a speech of your own." The carriers did not record her name.

She keeps the account of the gathering as it stands, every speech in its order, and sings it whole when a sitting is called. She was raised for that keeping.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is patient, exact and quietly amused by the court's questions.

### Motivation

She wants the gathering kept open and kept whole. If the court sends a speech of its own, she wants it heard, and she has said nothing about whether it would be answered.

### Strengths

She has the whole of the account by heart, the speeches withheld from the carriers included.

### Weaknesses

She will not give a carrier a word the gathering has not given her, however long the day.
