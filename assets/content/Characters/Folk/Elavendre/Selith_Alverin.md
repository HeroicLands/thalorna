# Appearance {#appearance}

Selith Alverin is a 42-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of medium build. She is broad-handed and smoke-dark, with graying brown hair under a leather cap and brown eyes red from the kiln. Her forearms are scarred where the turf of a charcoal stack has fallen in on them.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Selith Alverin burns charcoal at the edge of [[place-sourimael|Sóurimel]], and her household is one of the oldest in the village. Her daughter [[being-alenaalvrn|Alena]] took an Order party into [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]] in the spring for silver and has not come back. The Wardens will not escort a search into sacred ground. Selith wants her daughter home, and she will pay in honey, hazel and the standing of those who go, which in Sóurimel is worth more than coin.
