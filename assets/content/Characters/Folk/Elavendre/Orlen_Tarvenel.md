# Appearance {#appearance}

Orlen Tárvenel is a 51-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. He is a comfortable, ruddy man with graying brown hair and the soft hands of someone who counts coin. He wears good wool in the crown's green and carries his gate-house keys on his belt, where the town can hear him coming. His signet hangs on a cord inside his collar.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Orlen Tárvenel is the crown's charter-reeve of [[place-gandelis|Gandelis]], the chartered town on the rim of the Berav basin where the last royal road ends at a stone arch. He holds the gate-house, collects the market dues and keeps the peace within the walls. A quarrel inside the gate goes to him. A quarrel outside it goes to nobody: it is the Wardens' ground, and the Wardens' judgment there is final.

Gandelis outfits the companies of hired guards that go into the forest, and the reeve's gate-house is where each company is entered. An Order scholar and eight guards lodge in the largest inn tonight and leave at first light. The Warden under the gate has watched them lodge and has said to the reeve, in four words, that he would like to speak to the scholar. The scholar has declined, and Orlen has to decide what he is willing to carry.

## Psyche

### Personality

Orlen is genial, tidy and careful with his words. He greets every company the same way and remembers each one by its cook. He does not argue with a Warden and does not argue with a scholar.

### Motivation

He wants the town kept open, its charter unquestioned and both the crown and the Order content with it. He tells himself that what he does keeps Gandelis from being refused.

### Strengths

He knows every inn, carter and guide of the town. His writs are clear, and the market runs on time because he fines early.

### Weaknesses

He is a man who has made himself useful to two masters who do not speak, and he cannot say where either would draw the line. The Warden under the gate knows it.

## Social

### Companions

The gate-warders and the market clerks answer to him. The town's magistrates take a dim public view of the companies that cross the line, and a pragmatic private one.

### Patrons

The crown, whose charter he keeps, and the chancery at Péravel.

### Enemies

The Warden under the gate, who has not once raised his voice to him. The innkeepers, who want the companies to stay a week.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Orlen is the crown's charter-reeve at the rung of Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The last warning.** The Warden under the gate asks a party to carry a word to the scholar that he will not accept from a Warden: that the way he means to go is not a path.
2. **The hired guards.** One of the eight guards, a woman from the south, wants to know what she has been hired to do before she crosses the line, and will pay a party to find out ([[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]]).
3. **The cutter.** A guide of the town has not been seen since the autumn, and the Wardens are asking after him. His wife says he left for Péravel with a sealed crate and a purse, and the reeve would rather the party asked her than he.

:::secret {title="The reeve's second ledger"}
Orlen Tárvenel is paid by the Fitter of Expeditions at the University for the name of every company he outfits, the route it asks about and the day it leaves. He tells himself that the money keeps the Order content with a town that would otherwise be refused, and that the Wardens already know where everyone goes.

The Wardens do. They have not told him that they know he sells it, and they let him go on, because a ledger in the Order's hands tells them how much the Order knows. The scholar in the inn carries a map that is better than it should be.

**Hook: the ledger.** The second ledger is in the gate-house chest. A party that takes it holds the names of the guides, the carters and the mercenaries the Order has used in the last ten years, and the Wardens would pay to see it.
:::
