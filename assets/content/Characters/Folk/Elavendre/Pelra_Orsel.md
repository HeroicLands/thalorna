# Appearance {#appearance}

Pelra Orsel is a 50-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. She is brisk and well dressed, with dark brown hair pinned high and gray eyes that price what they see. She wears University wool and a ring of keys.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Pelra Orsel is the Fitter of Expeditions of the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] and a Master of its courts. She buys the stores, guards and wagons for every licensed survey of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] at [[place-beravel|Péravel]], and she settles the accounts of the guards she hires in the inns of the river quarter.

:::secret {title="What the Fitter does not know"}
Pelra does not know that a ledger of the University's collections exists. She would be dismayed to learn it.
:::
