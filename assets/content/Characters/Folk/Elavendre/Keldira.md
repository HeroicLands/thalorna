# Appearance {#appearance}

Kéldira is a 29-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She is wiry and windburned, with dark hair cropped for the wet and gray eyes that do not blink at a dark window. Her cheeks are chapped raw from nights by the cleft. She wears a Warden's oiled leathers black with bog water and carries a spear, a bow and two lean dogs at her heel.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kéldira of the Dhárgandi is a Warden of the tribe of the Lodge, set by turns to the watch at [[place-fndr|the Fen Door]], the [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] keep farthest from the mountains. One of the two Wardens who came down at the autumn turn was killed at the cleft at midwinter, and she keeps the Door alone with two dogs, sleeping by day in the turf hut on the dry ground a bowshot off. No relief can come down from the mountains until the passes open.

She buys flour, salt and bread on the first of every month at the end of the bake-house yard in [[place-harnasel|Harnasel]] and goes back by the path the village does not walk. She leaves no message but the list, and she has let the beacon families of the nearest stretch send word up to the Lodge by whoever will carry it. Her home is [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]], where her kin are, and where the dead Warden's spear is to be carried when the thaw comes.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kéldira is silent, steady and stubborn. She warns a trespasser once, plainly, and means it. She sings to the dogs at night and does not sing in front of strangers.

### Motivation

She wants the watch kept until the passes open, and the dead man's spear carried up to his kin before the thaw. She wants nothing from anyone but hands she can trust.

### Strengths

She knows the bog, the alders and the cleft's moods by feel. She can sit a night of cold without moving and sing the song that turns what she can turn.

### Weaknesses

She is alone where the order's custom is two. She sleeps in daylight, and she cannot hunt anything and keep the watch at once.

## Social

### Companions

Two dogs, the lean one and the white-muzzled one, are the only company she has had since midwinter. The village of Harnasel sells her bread and does not ask.

### Patrons

The Wardens of her territory and the Tradition Keepers who hold the song the watch sings. Neither can reach her this winter.

### Enemies

The Order scholar who has hired guards on the Lísere coast to camp at the mound. She makes no distinction between the scholar and the guard, and warns each once.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]]**: Kéldira is a Warden of the watch at the Fen Door.
- **[[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]]**: She is a Kinsman of the Dhárgandi, the tribe of the Lodge.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Hands she would not otherwise accept.** The lone Warden needs a second at the cleft through the dark nights, and no Warden can come down before the passes open. Sólmere Kálmereth of Harnasel will carry her request to a party that is not the Order's.
2. **The fallen Warden.** The Warden killed at midwinter lies under a cairn on the dry ground. His kin in the mountains want his spear carried up to them before the thaw, and Kéldira will not leave the Door to carry it.
3. **The scholar's guards.** An Order scholar's hired guards mean to camp at the mound to watch what comes through, and Kéldira has told the village she will warn them once. She asks a party to be at the edge of the wood when they come, to keep the warning from being the last thing they hear.

:::secret {title="What keeps her at the cleft"}
What came through at midwinter is still on this side. It killed the Warden who went at it alone with fire and the wrong song, and it lies by day in the black water of Olvalonke and hunts along the forest edge on dark nights, never into the light of a beacon. Kéldira cannot hunt it and keep the watch at once. While she sits by the cleft, nothing else has come through, and if she leaves for a night, something else does.

She has told the village of Harnasel nothing, and it is her silence that has the villagers saying wolves. If the Order's guards keep her away from the mound, the Door is unwatched.

**Hook: the watch.** A party that sits a night with her sees her rise at a sound it cannot hear and walk toward the water, and has to decide whether to follow her or keep the fire.
:::
