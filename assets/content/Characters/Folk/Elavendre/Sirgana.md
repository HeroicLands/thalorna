# Appearance {#appearance}

Sírgana is a 31-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. She is spare and windburned, with black hair in a single braid and gray eyes that keep returning to the trees. She wears a Warden's gray-green cloak and carries a spear and a long knife, and no bow.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sírgana of the [Tírindi]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} is a Warden of the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] who keeps the envoys' road below [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]] with her partner [[being-astherenwrdn|Ástheren]]. Ástheren took the envoy [[being-devrikveldr|Devrik Véldarin]] up the road toward [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]] with a sealed casket for the great council, and neither has come to the Lodge. She has followed the road to [[place-hntrsrng|the Hunter's Ring]], found four boundary stones overturned, and stopped at the edge. A Warden alone in the ring is prey, and she needs company to go in.

She wants Ástheren and the envoy found and the stones set right. She knows parts of the Wardens' song of the ring and sings them only for a price in kind.

## Psyche

### Personality

Sírgana is quiet, controlled and exact. She says what the law is and waits.

### Motivation

She wants her partner brought home, the ring closed and the law of the ring kept, whatever it costs a lowlander.

### Strengths

She knows the ring's song, the line of its stones and the habits of the hunter. She is patient in the dark.

### Weaknesses

A Warden walks ahead, and in the ring the one ahead is alone. Ástheren is the one person she would break the law for.

## Social

### Companions

Ástheren, her partner on the road since they were young Wardens together.

### Patrons

The Elder Wardens at Ésalosa Lodge.

### Enemies

Whoever pried the silver from the stones.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]]**: Sírgana holds the rank of Warden.
- **[[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]]**: She is of the Tírindi.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A companion for one night.** At dusk on the envoys' road a Warden steps out of the trees, looks at a traveler for a long moment, and asks for company for one night ([[scenario-lodgesilvbgh|The Lodge beneath the Silver Boughs]]).
2. **The price of the song.** Sírgana sings the ring's song for the truth of why each of the party is there, and she can tell when she is lied to.

:::secret {title="The law of the ring"}
The Wardens' last verses make the ring a judgment. One who sheds kin-blood or Warden-blood on Warden ground, and is taken alive, is walked into the ring at dusk and left alone there. Sírgana does not know what happened at the lodge door. When she hears Ástheren's last words from the trees, she knows what [[being-norvenhalren|Norven Halren]] did, he becomes her enemy, and she asks that he be left in the ring.

The spring melt at a ford took her bow out of her hands, and she has not yet cut another she likes. Only Ástheren knew it.
:::
