# Appearance {#appearance}

Devrik Véldarin is a 34-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of light build. He is slight and pale, with brown hair in a court cut and hazel eyes. He travels in a good coat, and his cuffs are stained with ink from the journal he keeps.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Devrik Véldarin is a bard of [[affiliation-clgenvoys|the Envoys' College]] in the service of the crown, sent up the envoys' road with a sealed casket carrying the crown's word to the great council at [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]] and a gift for the Lodge. The Warden [[being-astherenwrdn|Ástheren]] escorted him. He has not come to the Lodge, and he is missing with his escort on the road beside [[place-hntrsrng|the Hunter's Ring]].

:::secret {title="The journal"}
Poachers robbed Devrik at dusk below the Hunter's Ring, and Ástheren kept him at a fire by a standing stone while his two servants were taken one by one. He wrote it all down. His journal's last line says that Ástheren came back for him, smiling.

He hangs in the cradles of the tree at the ring's heart, alive and silent. His hair has grown out of its court cut, his coat is torn, and his hazel eyes do not close. He wants to be cut down, and he wants the casket brought to Ésalosa unopened, because he gave his word that it would be.
:::
