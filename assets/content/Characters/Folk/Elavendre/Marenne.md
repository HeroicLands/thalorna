# Appearance {#appearance}

Marenne is a 33-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. She is wiry and sun-darkened, with dark brown hair in a braid and gray eyes narrowed against the light. She wears oiled leathers and sits with her back to the bowl.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Marenne is a Warden who keeps the watch at the lip of [[place-hldhr|the Held Hour]]. She is one Warden short in summer, when the herds are up. She has seen the Order's tracks below the ring of mark-stones and asks a party to sit the lip with her.
