# Appearance {#appearance}

Adrila Salreth is a 29-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of light build. She is quick and freckled, with dark blonde hair under a knitted cap and green eyes that go to the bar before they go to a visitor. She carries a brass spyglass on a strap and a wax tablet in her coat.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Adrila Salreth is the crown's harbor-clerk at [[place-sirlathen|Sirlathen]], the pilots' village at the mouth of the Berav. She lives in the stone house above the pilots' hall, counts every hull that enters with its flag, tonnage and cargo, and files the count with the sheriff at [[place-beravel|Péravel]]. Her cousin [[being-rhalizlrth|Ralva Salreth]] is a pilot of the fellowship.

She wants her count true. The pilots bring every hull up the channel and see every deck, and they tell her what the charter requires and nothing more. A Vilérian trader lies off the bar with eleven men on deck who are not sailors, and the Master Pilot has said nothing to her about it.
