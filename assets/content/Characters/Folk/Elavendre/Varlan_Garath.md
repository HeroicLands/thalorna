# Appearance {#appearance}

Varlan Gárath is a 40-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of light build. He is lean and dry, with dark brown hair going thin at the brow and gray eyes, and a smile that has learned to fit his face. He wears a good shore-cloak, a beacon-keeper's signet on his thumb and a knotted cord at his belt, and he carries a leather satchel he never sets down.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Varlan Gárath is a lamp-hand of the [[affiliation-beaconfamlys|Beacon Families]] at [[place-hendrel|Hendrel]] and a cousin, through his mother, of its oldest line, the line of [[being-taldorpardal|Taldor Párdalen]]. The coast tells that a beacon-keeper of the shore asked the thing of the reeds to stand between the raiders and the land, and that the thing answered with [[place-dskfrd|the Dusk Ford]]. Varlan calls himself a factor, and he deals with buyers on the [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] run.

## Psyche

### Personality

Varlan is affable, careful and ashamed. He is pleasant to everyone and trusts no one.

### Motivation

He wants money, and a life away from the lamps that his cousins never offered him.

### Strengths

He knows the shore, and he knows the buyers on the Kalipsa run by sight.

### Weaknesses

He trusts no one, and he does not sleep well.

## Social

### Companions

None he keeps close.

### Patrons

The buyers on the Kalipsa run, who pay well and ask nothing.

### Enemies

His cousin Taldor Párdalen, whose line keeps him a lamp-hand.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-beaconfamlys|The Beacon Families]]**: Varlan is a Lamp-Hand of the Hendrel line.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: He is a Freeman of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The buyers.** A Kalipsan captain at the outer quay of Sarmelis has a crew that does not speak, does not eat and does not tire, and asks a party where such men are bought.

:::secret {title="The satchel"}
The Toll-Keeper favors the oldest line of Hendrel, and its kin cross the Ford free. Varlan crosses free as they do. He has learned what the line has never wanted to know: that the forfeits in the tally-house on the far bank can be held, and that a man who holds a raider's name commands the raider. He keeps a satchel of knotted cords, a company of the Forfeit-Men follows him at a word, and he sells the rest to buyers on the Kalipsa run. He wants to keep selling, and he would also like, very much, to be able to stop.

He crosses the Ford free and holds names that command strong men. His line does not know what he does, and the Toll-Keeper does. He lost a name of his own in his youth, and he would give a great deal to have it back. The Forfeit-Men whose cords he holds are his only company, and they are not company. His enemies are his cousin Taldor, if he ever learns of the satchel, and the Bardic Colleges, whose student found out too much.

**Hook: the satchel.** At the near end of the causeway a man with a lamp and a leather satchel waits for the party coming back across the Ford, and bargains before he sends the Forfeit-Men ([[scenario-tlldskfrd|The Toll You Will Not Miss]]).
:::

:::secret {title="His own forfeit"}
At fifteen Varlan crossed at dusk on a dare and gave the Toll-Keeper the name of the girl he meant to marry, thinking he would not miss it. He did not cross free that night, because he had not yet learned the cord. The forfeit hangs in the tally-house still. A party that finds and names it can buy the satchel with it.
:::
