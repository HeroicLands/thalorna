# Appearance {#appearance}

Heltan Kardeth is a 27-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of light build. He is young and lean, with black hair cropped short in a soldier's cut, a healed cut across the left eyebrow and blue eyes that rest on a doorway before they rest on a face. His skin is sun-browned from service abroad. He wears a house coat in plain colors, and sits with his back to a wall.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Heltan Kardeth is a young nobleman of [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]] and the cousin of [[being-kenrelkardet|Kenrel Kardeth]], newly returned from service abroad. He asks pointed questions about the circumstances of his cousin's death, with a soldier's eye for details that do not add up. His inquiries are social rather than formal, in conversation at gatherings and in letters to relatives, and they trouble [[being-glyencrdth|Lyenna Kardeth]].

Kenrel was killed three years ago on the road between his estates and [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]], in what was reported as a bandit raid. The attack was unusually well-coordinated for common bandits: the escort was ambushed at a narrow point, and Kenrel was specifically targeted while his servants were left alive to carry the news. The crown's garrison at Elanmere investigated and concluded that it was a raid. Heltan has obtained a copy of the garrison's report.

## Psyche

### Personality

Heltan is direct, watchful and impatient with euphemism. He speaks little at table and listens to who else does not. He is courteous to his cousin's widow and keeps his hand where she can see it.

### Motivation

He wants to know who killed his cousin and why. He wants the family to stop treating the question as bad manners.

### Strengths

He has a soldier's eye for ground and timing. He has a name that opens doors and the patience to wait outside them.

### Weaknesses

He has no ally in the house, and he asks his questions in a house where the person he is asking about is its head. He does not know who he is dealing with.

## Social

### Companions

A servant who survived the ambush has agreed to speak to him in private.

### Patrons

None. His own pay from abroad is his support.

### Enemies

Lyenna Kardeth, who has not understood how much he knows.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The report.** Heltan has found three inconsistencies in the garrison's report and asks a party to ride the road between the estates and Elanmere and tell him whether the narrow point is where the report says it is.
2. **The servant who lived.** A servant who survived the ambush will speak to Heltan only in private, and has asked for safe conduct out of Port Aelion. He asks a party to carry her to him.
3. **The private meeting.** Heltan has asked Lyenna Kardeth for a private meeting to discuss his concerns. He asks a party to be in the next room, and to say nothing.

She has begun to weigh a plan to deal with him, and she is choosing between reassurance, discrediting and something more permanent.
