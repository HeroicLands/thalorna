# Appearance {#appearance}

Ormen Halren is a 34-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He is square and sunburned, with brown hair under a knitted cap and brown eyes that avoid the forest. He wears a cutter's leather apron and carries no axe.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ormen Halren is a woodcutter who works the licensed coppice behind [[place-liranel|Liranel]]. He lost his grandfather's axe at the edge of the wood, and a neighbor has told him where lost things in that wood are found. He offers silver to anyone who will bring it out, and tells his story to everyone.
