# Appearance {#appearance}

Méirendand Ílsevende is a 57-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. He is weathered, spare and white-haired, with pale blue eyes that have read a million fathoms of river. His right palm is hardened by the lead line. He wears a pilot's tarred coat and a flat blue cap, and he stands on the quay with his feet apart as though the stones moved.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Méirendand Ílsevende is the Master Pilot of [[place-sirlathen|Sirlathen]], the pilots' village at the mouth of the Berav. The fellowship elects a Master Pilot for life, who assigns hulls, keeps the roll and answers for the channel. No hull goes up the river without a pilot of Sirlathen, which is why [[place-beravel|Péravel]] has never been sacked from the sea.

The crown's harbor-clerk lives in a stone house above the hall and counts every hull that enters, with its flag, tonnage and cargo, and files the count with the sheriff at Péravel. He has never been refused the count, and the pilots have never been offered a reason to refuse it. Tonight a deep-hulled trader out of Viléria has anchored off the bar with a request for a pilot, and eleven men on deck who are not sailors.

## Psyche

### Personality

Méirendand is gruff, honest and dry. He speaks of the river as of a relative, with respect and impatience. He keeps a pilot's silence and expects it of others.

### Motivation

He wants every hull brought up safely, counted and paid for. He wants to know who is asking him to bring one up without a count.

### Strengths

He knows the channel as no one living does. The pilots follow his word.

### Weaknesses

He is old, and the fellowship's life tenure means they will keep him until he is lost. He has been told by a letter on the University's paper to take the hull up on the night tide without a count, and has not decided whether the University or the Order is asking.

## Social

### Companions

The fellowship's pilots and apprentices. The crown's harbor-clerk, who lives above the hall.

### Patrons

None. The fellowship elects him.

### Enemies

Anyone who would have him break the count.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Méirendand is a Freeman of the crown, a pilot of the Berav mouth.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The night tide.** The Master Pilot wants a party to be aboard the Vilérian hull when it comes up, as witnesses who are not the University's and not the crown's.
2. **The harbor-clerk's count.** A harbor-clerk who has been told nothing asks the party to find out what the pilots are hiding from him, and offers the crown's good opinion for it.
3. **The letter.** The letter on the University's paper has a seal Méirendand does not know. He asks a party to carry it to the Provost of the Oldest Court at Péravel and bring back whether it is a real writ.

:::secret {title="The eleven"}
The men on deck are men of the Sekran's own household guard, in plain wool, and their contract is to join the University's hired guards on the next licensed expedition and to report on them. The Order does not trust the mercenaries it hires. It has been losing scholars in the interior, and it has decided that some of the men it pays to protect them are selling the Wardens the routes.

Their leader carries a sealed warrant to arrest any guard he finds carrying out an object from an Araelwen on his own account.
:::
