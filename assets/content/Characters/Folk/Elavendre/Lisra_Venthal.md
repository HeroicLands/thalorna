# Appearance {#appearance}

Lisra Venthal is a 26-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of light build. She is slight and quick, with chestnut hair under a scholar's cap and green eyes that seldom rest. She carries a roll of ruled paper and a case of pens.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lisra Venthal writes music by ear and by hand, and the Order's scholar [[being-palvenorsel|Palven Orsel]] has hired her for that. She has heard the song of [[place-sngngtrn|the Singing Tarn]] and does not agree with Palven that it is only wind. She is afraid of it, and wax is not made for her ears because he needs her to hear.
