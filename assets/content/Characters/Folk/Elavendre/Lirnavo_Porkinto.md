# Appearance {#appearance}

Lirnavo Porkinto is a 1,128-year-old man who stands 6'3" tall and is of medium build, broad in the shoulder for a Senalo. His white hair is cut short, and his gray eyes are steady. His long fingers are stained gray-green with sap.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lirnavo Porkinto is a shaper of the living wood at [[affiliation-nclvlrss|the Enclave of Aelirosse]]. The enclave has no streets and no walls: its dwellings are worked into and around the living silver birch over the course of centuries, and he is one of those who work them. He does not cut. A dwelling of Aelirosse is sung and bent and waited for, a branch at a time, and a room he begins is finished by someone else.

The Senali prefer to grow what they build, and he holds the preference as a craft. The birch law, that no standing silver birch is felled, is to him the law of his trade.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is patient, practical and blunt, and he has little time for anything that hurries.

### Motivation

He wants the man who cut the bark from [[place-wkngstnd|the Waking Stand]] found and the bark brought back to the wood. Its trees are not birch and are not still, and a cut on them troubles him more than he says.

### Strengths

He knows every tree of the enclave by its growth, and the living wood answers his hands.

### Weaknesses

He is slow to forgive a cut tree, and slower to forgive the one who cut it.
