# Appearance {#appearance}

Néthara Halren is a 55-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She is small and spare, with silver hair twisted into a knot and gray eyes that rest on an object a long moment before they rest on a person. She wears the dark wool of a Master of the Oldest Court, a Fisera green sash and white cotton gloves she takes off only to lift a thing. Her hands are very still.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Néthara Halren is the Keeper of the Collections at the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]], a Master of the Fisera house and a Marendoi of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]. Each of the four chapterhouses keeps a collection of objects brought back from expeditions, and she catalogs them all: the form of each object and its place on the shelf. The catalog records no provenance.

She is meticulous, courteous and unhurried, and she is the only person at the University who can say where each object came from. She does not say. The Grove Council has petitioned the court at Péravel about objects taken from groves and holy wells, and the Rector has answered each petition with an invitation to submit it formally. Her catalog is what the petitioners are asking about, and she keeps it as she has always kept it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Néthara is precise, quiet and dry. She answers a question about weight or form at once and a question about origin with a question of her own. She is gentle with students and with objects.

### Motivation

She wants every object in her care kept whole, shelved and safe from the Fitter of Expeditions' next season. She wants it also to be possible, one day, to put what was taken back, and she has not told anyone how much of her wants that.

### Strengths

She knows the collections as a farmer knows a field. She is trusted by the Order and respected by the Grove Council's more careful petitioners, and has never been seen to take a side.

### Weaknesses

She is alone with a secret she cannot decide the use of. The Fitter of Expeditions sends her objects faster than she can shelve them, and a Keeper who falls ill leaves four collections without a memory.

## Social

### Companions

The Provost keeps the rolls of the Oldest Court where she lodges. A pair of Readers copy her catalog by hand.

### Patrons

The Rector, who trusts her catalog and does not ask what is not in it.

### Enemies

The Grove Council's petitioners, who think she knows more than she says, and are right. The Fitter of Expeditions, who wants objects fast and does not care where they were lifted.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]]**: Néthara holds the office of Keeper of the Collections at the rung of Master.
- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|The Thordimalon]]**: She holds the rung of Marendoi in the Fisera house.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: She is of the Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The petition.** A grove-elder from the basin asks a party to carry a petition into the Oldest Court past the Proctor, who has been told not to admit it. The Keeper is the one person in the court who will read it.
2. **The strip of bark.** The Fisera herbarium keeps a strip of bark from [[place-wkngstnd|the Waking Stand]] that has stayed green. The Wardens of the Silver Forests want the man who cut it, and the Keeper's catalog names the strip and its shelf and not the hand that brought it in.
3. **The weight of a hand.** A cataloged object has been moved quietly from one chapterhouse to another before a grove-elder's petition reached the court, and a Reader who copied the transfer asks a party to find out who ordered it.

:::secret {title="The ledger"}
The catalog records no provenance because Néthara keeps it elsewhere. She holds a private ledger, written in a cipher of her own, in which each object is entered with the ground it came from, the day it was cut or lifted and the name of the guard who did it. She keeps it for the Order, so that an Araelwen can be found again, and for herself, because she believes the day will come when what has been taken must be put back. She has told no one which of those she thinks is the more likely.

The Fitter of Expeditions does not know the ledger exists. The Sekran does.

**Hook: the page.** Someone in the book quarter has copied a page of the Keeper's cipher and wants to know what it says.
:::
