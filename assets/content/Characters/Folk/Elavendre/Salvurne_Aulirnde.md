# Appearance {#appearance}

Sálvurne Áulirnde is a 32-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of light build. She is tall, lean and hard-handed, with dark brown hair cropped to the scalp, a dark scar across the right cheek and green eyes. She dresses in miner's leather and carries a short iron-shod club. Her voice carries without being raised.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sálvurne Áulirnde is the foreman of a working above [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]], a drift cut into ground on the edge of [[place-sorvanda|Sórvanda]] that the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] have released by arrangement, held by a factor of [[place-beravel|Péravel]]. She is a ruthless operative. She regards the warnings of the old miner [[being-arevynlydr|Árevin Lidren]] about the timbering of her drift as expensive obstacles to her quota, and the two have clashed again and again.

The marginal workings are dangerous and poor, and a foreman who meets her quota is a rare thing. Sálvurne meets it. She hires the best hands the lake town has, and the hands who ask questions are the first to be let go. Her drift has been cut past the timbering Árevin laid out, and the miners in it say the roof talks at night.

The ore-road from [[place-kngscut|the King's Cut]] comes down into the town this year with prospectors on it who say they have a map, and Sálvurne has been buying them drinks.

:::secret {title="The silver carriers"}
Sálvurne runs the carriers who bring silver-bearing rock down off the spoil heaps of the King's Cut by night, for a carter in [[place-lisere|Lísere]] who asks no questions. The quota of her drift is met partly out of the King's Cut's spoil. The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] have seen the tracks and not yet the carriers.

She wants the deepest adit at the Cut opened, because the best silver is behind its seal, and she does not know that it is a grave.

**Hook: the carriers.** A party sent by the Wardens or the Lísere sheriff to find the night carriers follows them to Sálvurne's yard, where a sack of gray rock lies under her drift's tally.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Sálvurne is blunt, tireless and coldly efficient. She remembers every miner's tally and not one miner's name. She does not shout, and she does not forgive.

### Motivation

She wants the quota met and the seam worked out before the roof Árevin warns of comes down. She wants her share and does not intend to be the one who explains the dead.

### Strengths

She gets work out of miners. She has the factor's backing and knows which of the prospectors at the fair are worth listening to.

### Weaknesses

Her quota is higher than the drift will bear. A foreman who ignores a warning is the one blamed when the roof goes, and she knows it.

## Social

### Companions

A half-dozen shift-bosses who are afraid of her and a clerk who keeps two sets of tallies.

### Patrons

The factor of Péravel who holds the arrangement for her drift.

### Enemies

Árevin Lidren. The miners she has dismissed, who drink at the lake-shore inns.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Sálvurne is a Freeman of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The drift.** Árevin Lidren says the drift will not hold another season. Sálvurne says it will, and has told her shift-bosses the hands who stop work are dismissed. A party hired by either is asked to look at the timbers and say what they see.
2. **The dismissed.** Seven dismissed miners mean to take their claim for back pay to the royal court before the fair. Sálvurne asks a party to stand at the court door, and the miners ask the same party to stand beside them.
3. **The second tally.** Her clerk's second book is missing. Sálvurne asks a party to find it before the factor arrives for the fair, and does not say what is in it.
