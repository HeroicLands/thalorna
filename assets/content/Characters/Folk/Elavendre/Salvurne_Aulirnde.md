# Appearance {#appearance}

Sálvurne Áulirnde is a 32-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of light build. She is tall, lean and hard-handed, with dark brown hair cropped to the scalp, a dark scar across the right cheek and green eyes. She dresses in miner's leather and carries a short iron-shod club. Her voice carries without being raised.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sálvurne Áulirnde is the foreman of the [Deepmine Consortium]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"}'s workings in the hills behind [[place-liranel|Liranel]], a ruthless operative. She regards the safety rules of [[being-arevynlydr|Árevin Lidren]] as expensive obstacles to profit, and the two have clashed repeatedly over Árevin's warnings about unstable shafts.

Liranel is a town of timber and everything that follows from timber, and its workings feed the yards where the squared logs go down to Port Aelion. The licenses to cut and the licenses to dig are issued at the same table, and Sálvurne has learned which clerk to ask. She hires the best hands the town has, and the hands who ask questions are the first to be let go.

## Psyche

### Personality

Sálvurne is blunt, tireless and coldly efficient. She remembers every man's tally and not one man's name. She does not shout, and she does not forgive.

### Motivation

She wants the quota met and the Consortium's seam worked out before the shaft that Árevin warns of gives way. She wants her bonus and does not intend to be the one who explains the dead.

### Strengths

She gets work out of men. She has the Consortium's backing and the clerks of the forest court's attention.

### Weaknesses

Her quota is higher than the seam will bear. A foreman who ignores a warning is the one who is blamed when the shaft goes, and she knows it.

## Social

### Companions

A half-dozen shift-bosses who are afraid of her and a clerk who keeps two sets of tallies.

### Patrons

The Consortium's factor at Péravel.

### Enemies

Árevin Lidren. The men she has dismissed, who drink at the Lamp.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Sálvurne is a Freeman of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The shaft.** Árevin Lidren says the east shaft will not hold another season. Sálvurne says it will, and has told her shift-bosses the hands who stop work are dismissed. A party hired by either is asked to look at the timbers and say what they see.
2. **The dismissed.** Seven dismissed miners have gathered at the Lamp and mean to ask the forest court for their back pay. Sálvurne asks a party to stand at the court door, and the miners ask the same party to stand beside them.
3. **The second tally.** Her clerk's second book is missing. Sálvurne asks a party to find it before the Consortium's factor arrives, and does not say what is in it.
