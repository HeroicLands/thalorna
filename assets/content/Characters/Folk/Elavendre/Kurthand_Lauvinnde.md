# Appearance {#appearance}

Kurthand Láuvinnde is a 61-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of light build. He is slight and upright, with white hair worn long and tied at the nape and the hands of a harper, every fingertip callused. His brown eyes are patient. He dresses in the gray-green of a Bard of the Circle and carries nothing written.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kurthand Láuvinnde is the Master of the [[affiliation-clgline|College of the Line]] at [[place-beravel|Péravel]], among the oldest of the [[affiliation-bardicolgs|Bardic Colleges]]. The College holds the proofs of the kingdom's houses: for each house of the basin, a recital, a roll and the names of the witnesses who heard it. He decides which proofs the College will hear. He holds the Bardic Circle's hall at Péravel, where the Lay of the Tree Line is sung at every crowning.

[[being-ielaraskir|Prince Yélaras]] means to answer the doubters of his house's claim by having his line sung at the Elanmere fair, before people who remember the genealogy differently. The College that proved the line is the College that must sing it there, and Kurthand has not said whether it will. A smaller and older quarrel stands behind it: the houses that cannot pay for a proof keep their claims by custom, and a bard who is asked to hear one at a lower fee must choose between the house and the Roll.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kurthand is courteous, ironical and slow to answer. He quotes a precedent when he refuses and a song when he agrees. He teaches by asking a student to recite a line back and then asking for it again.

### Motivation

He wants the Roll kept honest and the College out of a quarrel between the crown and the Áelendan. He would like to sing the prince's line and cannot decide whether it is true enough.

### Strengths

His memory holds every proof the College has heard. The Circle trusts him, and so do the lesser houses who cannot pay.

### Weaknesses

He is old, and the Roll lives in mouths. The satire of the Lament at the Road's End has found a line in his own College's collection that he cannot sing.

## Social

### Companions

The Archivist of the Line keeps the library and the proofs. The Singer at the Crowning is a bard of the Circle who sings the Lay before the monarch swears.

### Patrons

The Bardic Circle, which chose the royal house and keeps its roll.

### Enemies

Any house that has bought a proof the College has not heard. A Tradition Keeper who may stand in the crowd at the fair.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The unpaid line.** A lesser house of the basin asks a party to carry its recital to the Master, because it cannot afford a bard's fee and its claim is under dispute before the court.
2. **The roll with a gap.** The Archivist asks a party to find the one witness to a proof who left Péravel in the year the mountains closed, and whose name is cut out of the roll.
3. **At the fair.** A bard of the Line is to sing the heir's line at Elanmere, and someone has told the Master that a Tradition Keeper means to be in the crowd. He asks a party to learn what the Keeper intends before the bard stands up.
