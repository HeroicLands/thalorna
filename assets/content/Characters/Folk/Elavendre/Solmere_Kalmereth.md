# Appearance {#appearance}

Sólmere Kálmereth is a 59-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of heavy build. She is broad and slow-moving, with gray hair braided and pinned flat under a wool cap and a weathered face that gives nothing away. Flour lives in the creases of her knuckles whatever the hour. She walks the flat-stone path to the beacon-house in the dark without a lantern.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sólmere Kálmereth is the head of the oldest line of [[place-harnasel|Harnasel]], the last beacon village before the [[place-coastroad|Coast Road]] leaves [[place-lisere|Lísere]] for the cliffs of Lirve. She keeps the beacon-house on the point, sits on the council of [[affiliation-beaconfamlys|the Beacon Families]] and holds the beacon-roster and the bake-house accounts in her head. The grove-elder of the coast tries the village's cases when he comes down, and he stays for the bread.

One customer is not on the crown's books. On the first of every month a Warden of the night watch at [[place-fndr|the Fen Door]] comes out of the wood to the end of the bake-house yard with a sack and a list, pays in silver and goes back by a path the village does not walk. Since midwinter the Warden has come alone and left no message but the list. Sólmere has said twice, unasked, that the Door wants hands, and that she will take them from anyone except the Order's.

## Psyche

### Personality

Sólmere is slow-spoken, blunt and watchful. She feeds a hungry stranger without a question and sits a long time with a guest she has not decided about.

### Motivation

She wants the watch at the Door kept, the lamps of the chain lit and her village out of the Wardens' business. She wants these three things in that order and cannot have them all.

### Strengths

The village does what she says. She knows the chain's stations, the Wardens' monthly habit and the weather in the mist over Olvalonke.

### Weaknesses

Half her village would send word up the path that the Order is coming, and the other half remember that the Wardens forbid every stranger that path. She holds both halves with a bake-house's authority and no more.

## Social

### Companions

The bakers, the cooper and the charcoal-burners are her kin by one marriage or another. The beacon-house is stood by her own household.

### Patrons

The council of the Beacon Families, which she sits on, and the Castellan at Durmelen, whose Great Fire her lamps answer.

### Enemies

The Order scholar's quartermaster, who buys bread by the month and asks the way to the Door.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-beaconfamlys|The Beacon Families]]**: Sólmere is Head of a Line, with a seat on the council.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: She is a Freeman of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Warden's word.** Sólmere carries the lone Warden's message to any party that asks and is not the Order's: the watch needs hands for the dark nights, and she cannot offer a bed, a fire or an explanation.
2. **The forest edge.** The charcoal-burners have lost a dog and a child's pony and want someone to sit a night at the end of the Wardens' path with a lantern and see what the wolves are.
3. **The Order's bread.** The scholar's quartermaster buys a month's bread and asks for it to be delivered to the end of the path, where the guards will take it on. Sólmere will sell it, and asks a party to be in the yard when it goes.
