# Appearance {#appearance}

Lénira Sérdalnde is a 37-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of light build. She is lean and brown, with hair twisted into a plain braid and a weathered face that is easy to read until she chooses otherwise. She wears the crown guide's green armband, good boots and an oiled cloak, and carries a staff worn smooth where her hand goes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lénira Sérdalnde is a crown guide of [[place-liranel|Liranel]], one of the courteous, capable and officially provided escorts who conduct licensed expeditions out of the forest town to the margins of the tribal country and back. The crown provides a guide to every licensed expedition, and an expedition guided out of Liranel sees real country, meets real tribesfolk and returns with real notes about nothing of consequence. Lénira is very good at it.

Half the town knows which of its guides are the crown's and which are for hire, and nobody explains the difference to visitors. Lénira knows every margin and every Warden's patrol path, and has never once been refused a courtesy at the tree line. The Order's expeditions have learned to treat her with great respect and to ask her for nothing she cannot give.

## Psyche

### Personality

Lénira is warm, easy and unreadable. She tells a scholar the truth about every tree and every stream, and nothing about where she is not taking him. She laughs at herself and never at the Wardens.

### Motivation

She wants to keep the line where the crown drew it, bring every party home alive and keep her license. She also wants to know what the Order would do with a guide it could buy.

### Strengths

She knows the margins as a farmer knows a field. The Wardens know her by name and speak to her, which they do not do with the for-hire guides.

### Weaknesses

She is a servant of the crown and the crown's ceiling is known: she is paid to lead nowhere that matters. An offer from a fellow who will pay three times her fee is a conversation she has learned to have in the open.

## Social

### Companions

The tally-house clerks know her papers. The Crown's forest court treats her as a witness.

### Patrons

The queen's chancery, which provides the guides. The Forester, who issues her license.

### Enemies

The for-hire guides, who undercut her and take parties where she will not. The Order's agents, who go round her.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Lénira holds the office of Crown Guide at the rung of Freeman.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The expedition that saw nothing.** A fellow of the University has asked for Lénira by name and the Fitter of Expeditions has agreed. She asks a party to walk in the expedition's train as hired hands and tell her whether the fellow's second map matches his first.
2. **Past the margin.** A Liranel guide who takes pilgrims past the margins has not come back, and the Wardens are asking who he took. Lénira knew him and asks a party to learn what he was paid before the Wardens decide it was a Liranel killing.
3. **A sellsword's question.** A guard home from an Order survey of the interior wants to know whether what he was paid to do was murder under the law of the ground he stood on. He has asked Lénira, and she has sent him to a party.
