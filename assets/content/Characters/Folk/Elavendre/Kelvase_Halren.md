# Appearance {#appearance}

Kélvase Halren is a 47-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of heavy build. She is broad and steady, with chestnut hair going gray under a woolen hood, brown eyes and the forearms of a woman who has handled timber all her life. She wears a haulier's notched tally-stick at her belt and checks it without looking.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kélvase Halren is the reeve of [[place-heligstrom|Hélithron]], a village of about 300 in the licensed forest of [[place-lirve|Lirve]], a few miles from [[place-liranel|Liranel]]. The village elects her each spring to keep its coppice rights, its hauling roster and its tally against the forest court at Liranel, whose tally-house measures every licensed stem the village cuts. She is a woman of the Halren woodcutting families of the coast.

The village has strangers on its road. Men nobody in Hélithron knows have been seen watching the way through the village, and the alchemist [[being-sevnmyrkls|Sevren Mírkelis]] of Liranel, whose remedies come by that road, says they are the men of [Kervan Redblade]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a warlord and privateer he once refused. Kélvase does not care whose men they are. The hauling season begins at the thaw, and the village's carts go down that road to the yards every day of it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kélvase is blunt, practical and hard to hurry. She counts the carts before she counts the coin.

### Motivation

She wants the road clear for the hauling season and the village's tally honest at the forest court. She wants no part of an alchemist's quarrel.

### Strengths

She knows every licensed block of the village's coppice and every haulier on the coast by name.

### Weaknesses

She will not ask the crown's sheriffs for help, because the sheriffs read the tally when they come.

## Social

### Companions

The hauliers of the village, and her brothers, who cut under the village's license.

### Patrons

The village that elects her, and the yards of Liranel that buy its timber.

### Enemies

The men on the road, and the tally-clerks of the forest court who find the village's count short.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Kélvase is a Freeman of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The men on the road.** Kélvase asks a party to learn what the strangers on the road are waiting for, and to move them on before the first carts go down.
2. **The short tally.** The forest court says the village cut more than its license last season. Kélvase wants the tally-house roll read by someone the clerks do not know.
3. **The alchemist's road.** Sevren Mírkelis wants company through Hélithron, and Kélvase wants him to take a different road.
