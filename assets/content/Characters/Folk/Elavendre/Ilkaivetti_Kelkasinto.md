# Appearance {#appearance}

Ilkaivetti Kelkasinto is a 1,588-year-old man who stands 6'2" tall and is of light build. His dark brown hair is tied back from a face that has stood in a great deal of wind, and his eyebrows are white with salt. His gray eyes rarely rest on anything near.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ilkaivetti Kelkasinto is an elder of [[affiliation-nclvsrnthl|the Enclave of Serenthale]]. He spends more of his life at the edge of the wood above the coast cliffs of [[place-lirve|Lirve]] than in the enclave, where the deep forest runs almost to the sea, and he looks west.

The Senali of Serenthale are held, by other Senali, to be concerned with the ocean in some way that the mountain enclaves are not. Ilkaivetti is the part of that concern that stands in the open air. Fishermen working the coast below the cliffs have seen a tall figure among the trees at the top, and say so in the taverns of [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]].

## Psyche

### Personality

He is quiet and inward, and kind to animals and to the very young.

### Motivation

He wants to keep the watch, and to keep it unremarked.

### Strengths

He reads weather, water and birds at sea as other Senali read the wood.

### Weaknesses

He has watched so long that the enclave's own business reaches him late.

## Social

### Patrons

[[being-rukkolaelvir|Rukkola Elvìrinte]], the Lord of the Enclave, who receives what he sees.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvsrnthl|Enclave of Serenthale]]**: Ilkaivetti is a Member of the Enclave.

:::secret {title="The sea-watch"}
Serenthale keeps a watch on the sea, unbroken since before there were humans on the coast, and Ilkaivetti keeps it now. He sees every sail that passes and reports each to the Lord of the Enclave. What the enclave watches the sea for, Serenthale keeps, and Ilkaivetti does not say it even among the Senali of other enclaves.
:::
