# Appearance {#appearance}

Hálmesar Véldarin is a 68-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall and is of heavy build gone spare with age. He has white hair cropped in a soldier's cut, gray eyes under heavy brows, and a pale, weathered face. He walks with a stick for a stiff left knee and sets it down as if it were a spear butt. He dresses in plain good wool in the house's colors, and keeps his old commission framed on the wall of the hall.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hálmesar Véldarin is the head of the [[affiliation-hseveldarin|House of Véldarin]], a minor noble house with a long tradition of service on the kingdom's borders, and he keeps its hall above the lake shore at [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]]. He commanded on the border for thirty years, as his father did before him, and his father fell fighting raiders. He came home with a stiff knee and no wish to talk about it, and he has run the farms and the house's name since.

His son and heir, [[being-dharnvldrn|Daran]], is the captain of the crown's garrison at Elanmere. Hálmesar's name among the crown's captains keeps his son's commission sound, and he has made it clear that the family's patience has limits. Daran's two reprimands are known in the town, and the old man has heard both stories from the people who were there.

## Psyche

### Personality

Hálmesar is gruff, exact and old-fashioned. He keeps the feasts, sends for the house's bard at the naming of a child, and stands for a grove-elder in the market. He says what he thinks once and does not repeat it.

### Motivation

He wants the house's name kept clean, his son's career to end better than its middle, and the lake road quiet.

### Strengths

He knows the border, its captains and its habits, and a word from him reaches officers his son cannot reach. He reads a report the way he once read ground.

### Weaknesses

He trusts the garrison's way of doing things because it was his way. He does not see much of his son, and he would rather not hear that the house's heir has done anything worse than be rude.

## Social

### Companions

His steward, and two old serjeants of his command who live on the farms.

### Patrons

None. He was a patron of younger officers for thirty years and has kept the habit.

### Enemies

None he would name. He has quarreled with the grove-elders' account of the lake road and listened to it anyway.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-hseveldarin|House of Véldarin]]**: Hálmesar is Of the Line and holds the office of Head of the House of Véldarin.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: He is a Noble of the crown, retired from its service.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The father's word.** Hálmesar asks a party to ride the lake road and tell him, plainly and without the garrison's manners, what the burned shrines along it are.
2. **The heir.** The old man asks a party to sit at the garrison's table for a week as his guests and tell him what kind of officer his son has become.
3. **The commission.** A captain of the [[affiliation-brdrmltia|Border Militia]] owes Hálmesar a favor from thirty years back, and Hálmesar asks a party to carry a letter calling it in.
