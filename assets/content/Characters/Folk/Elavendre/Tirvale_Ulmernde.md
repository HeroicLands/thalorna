# Appearance {#appearance}

Tírvale Úlmernde is a 50-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. He is spare and weathered, with gray hair cut short and brown eyes in a lined face. He wears an old knight's coat dyed the crown's gray and a heavy cloak against the sea wind, and he coughs through the wet months.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tírvale Úlmernde is the crown's Sheriff of [[place-lirve|Lirve]], the western seaboard of cliffs and Senali forest. He tries the crown's pleas in the shire, holds the posse and collects the levy and the road tolls, and his hall stands above the harbor of [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]]. He is a knight of the Úlmernde of [[place-liranel|Liranel]], and the uncle of [[being-aimelineulme|Lady Áimeline Úlmernde]], whose health he worries over.

Lirve is the hardest shire in the kingdom to keep. The forest behind the coast is licensed only in its inner belt, the deep wood is the Wardens', the Senali keep [[place-serenthale|Serenthale]] and nobody's law reaches them, and the fae country of the seaboard is the thickest outside the mountains. His writ runs in the towns, on the roads and in the licensed belt, and he says so to every complainant who wants it to run further.

He wants Lirve's towns kept in the crown's peace and the town council of Port Aelion kept out of the crown's pleas. The council holds that the harbor's charter gives it the trial of its own freemen, the sheriff holds that it does not, and the chancery has not answered either of them.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tírvale is patient, tired and dry. He has been told no by the forest so often that he finds it restful.

### Motivation

He wants his pleas heard, his tolls in and the charter quarrel settled in the crown's favor before he retires.

### Strengths

He knows the limits of his writ exactly and never pretends otherwise, and the Wardens of Lirve deal with him for it.

### Weaknesses

He is ill in winter and has no heir to the office. The council of Port Aelion knows that he is tired and waits.

## Social

### Companions

His reeves on the coast road, and the Forester at Liranel, [[being-thrndlsrnd|Thaurand Lísernde]], with whom he hunts.

### Patrons

The crown at Péravel, and his house at Liranel.

### Enemies

The town council of Port Aelion and its Speaker, [[being-lamaineolnar|Lámaine Ólnarnde]].

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Tírvale holds the office of Sheriff of Lirve, as a Knight.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The charter clause.** The sheriff wants a party to find the council's own copy of the Port Aelion charter and read him the clause the council claims.
2. **A complainant from the trees.** A freeman of Port Aelion swears a Warden took his son in the deep wood. Tírvale cannot hear it and asks a party to take the father to someone who can.
