# Appearance {#appearance}

Háresand Othral is a 38-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. He is weathered and square, with brown hair tied back for the sea and blue eyes with salt-white creases at their corners. He wears a cutter-captain's oiled coat over the crown's gray and goes bareheaded in all weather.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Háresand Othral is the Lieutenant of the Admiral at [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]]. He keeps the crown's three cutters at the mole, sends them out along [[place-liserebay|Lísere Bay]] after smugglers and raiders, and answers to the Admiral, [[being-tenralsaltha|Tenral Salthar]], not to the sheriff on the quay. He is a knight of the shore, and a cousin of [[being-morenothral|Moren Othral]], the Castellan at [[place-durmelen|Durmelen]].

He wants the bay watched and the cutters manned. The sheriff wants the cutters at his call, the Quay Council wants them out of the harbor's way, and the Order's wages have taken two of his crews' best hands this year.
