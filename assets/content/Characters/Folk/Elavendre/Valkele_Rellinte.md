# Appearance {#appearance}

Valkele Rellìnte is a 418-year-old woman who stands 5'11" tall and is of light build. Her black hair is cropped short, and her gray eyes are the gray of the court's stone. There is stone dust in the grain of her palms.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Valkele Rellìnte is a keeper of the court of worked stone at [[affiliation-nclvthlss|the Enclave of Ethalosse]]. The court is older than the forest around it and older than stone should be, with grass in its cracks, and the Senali keep it, though they prefer to grow what they build. She keeps it. She sets back a stone the frost has lifted, clears what the wood drops into it and leaves the grass in its cracks where it grows.

The Senali can raise fine stone buildings when they must, and the few that stand are rare works among them. The court is one the enclave keeps rather than one it raised.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is careful, practical and contented, and she talks to the stone as she works.

### Motivation

She wants the court kept as it is, neither repaired into something new nor let go.

### Strengths

She knows every stone of the court by its seat.

### Weaknesses

She is protective of the court past reason. A carrier who sets a pack down on the wrong stone hears about it.
