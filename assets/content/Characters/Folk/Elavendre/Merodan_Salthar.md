# Appearance {#appearance}

Merodan Salthar is a 52-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He is correct and heavy-lidded, with graying brown hair under a clerk's cap and brown eyes that look past the petitioner. He wears the Proctor's black gown.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Merodan Salthar is the Proctor of the gate to the Oldest Court at [[place-beravel|Péravel]]. He has been told what to say about a petition from the Grove Council, and he says it without looking at the paper.
