# Appearance {#appearance}

Sáunarine Darel is a 55-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of medium build. She is broad-handed and steady, with gray hair in a single braid, brown eyes and a weathered face that gives a stranger nothing. She wears the undyed wool of the village's own weaving and a knotted cord of the same wool at her wrist.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sáunarine Darel is the Keeper of the Spring at [[place-kelpasi|Kélpasi]], the sorhand whom [[affiliation-thrglnklps|the Thárgalon of Kélpasi]] has chosen to keep the cold spring at the head of the village and to lead what is done there. She heads her own hearth and sits at the council on the flat stones beside the spring, and she does not discuss the spring with anyone who was not born to the village. On [[lore-springguest|the Spring Guest]]'s night she sets the place at the spring.

The council is a voice short at the wrong time. The Speaker to the Lowlands took the winter cloth down to [[place-valdun|Valdun]] and has not come back, and a priest from the lowlands is expected within the month to see that the village is devout. The council cannot choose another Speaker while the Speaker's hearth sits empty at the spring, and Sáunarine will not meet the priest herself.

## Psyche

### Personality

Sáunarine is calm, unhurried and closed. She answers a question about the weather fully and a question about the spring with a question about the weather.

### Motivation

She wants the spring kept as it has always been kept, and the lowland priest met at the last bend of the track by someone with the manner for it.

### Strengths

The village trusts her, and every hearth will do what she asks at the spring without asking why.

### Weaknesses

She cannot lie, and she knows it, which is why she will not meet the priest.

## Social

### Companions

The hearth-heads of the council, and the Speaker, whose absence she feels.

### Patrons

The council that chose her.

### Enemies

None she would name. She does not trust the lowland priests' curiosity.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-thrglnklps|Thárgalon of Kélpasi]]**: Sáunarine is a Sorhand and holds the office of Keeper of the Spring.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: She is a Freeman of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Speaker's absence.** Sáunarine asks a party to bring the Speaker home from Valdun before the priest arrives.
2. **The priest.** If the Speaker cannot be found, she asks a party to meet the priest at the last bend of the track and keep him courteously on the near side of the fields.
3. **A guest from the high country.** An Áelendan traveler has asked to be at the spring on the Guest's night, and Sáunarine wants to know who sent him before she sets the place.
