# Appearance {#appearance}

Hethar Pelthal is a 41-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. He is composed and well kept, with graying brown hair and blue eyes that hold steady. He wears University wool and carries a folding table.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hethar Pelthal is a fellow of the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] and of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]]. He holds a crown license to survey the Spine and no leave from anyone who keeps it, and he hires guards for the season. He knows the bowl disturbs time and not by how much, and he wants Taldor Lidren's notes more than he wants Taldor.
