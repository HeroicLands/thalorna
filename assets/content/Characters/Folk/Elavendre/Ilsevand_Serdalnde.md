# Appearance {#appearance}

Ílsevand Sérdalnde is a 40-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of light build. He is thin and pale, with brown hair worn long over the collar and a scholar's squint that comes of reading by lamplight. He carries a leather tube for maps across his back and dresses in the dark wool of the University, without ornament, under an oiled sea-cloak that does not fit him.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ílsevand Sérdalnde is a Fellow of the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] and a Thermoi of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]], licensed to practice on his own. He surveys, and he has surveyed for the Order in the lowland woods for ten years. He has taken rooms over a sail-loft on Salt Row in [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]], where he is paying guards' wages above the sheriff's rate, and he means to camp at [[place-fndr|the Fen Door]] through a fortnight of dark nights to see what comes through.

He holds, with the University's theorists, that the cold of the mound is a bog vapor rising through a fault and that what the coast sees on dark nights is marsh light. He heard at [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] that the Door is thinly kept, and he reads that as an opening. The Warden there will warn him once. He does not believe the Wardens' law reaches him as a Fellow of the University licensed by the crown to survey.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ílsevand is earnest, precise and patient with students and impatient with fools. He listens courteously to what a guide says and writes his own conclusion at the end of the page.

### Motivation

He wants to be the Fellow who sees the Door with his own eyes and writes it down, and he wants it done before the University's other theorists reach the coast. He regards the guards he has hired as a precaution, not a statement.

### Strengths

He is a careful observer with a good eye for ground. He pays on time and writes a clear contract.

### Weaknesses

He believes the crown's license protects him, and it does not reach the cleft. He does not know what the coast calls the Door, and his guards are the best young hands the shore had to spare.

## Social

### Companions

Eight hired guards are due by the first dark. His quartermaster buys bread at Harnasel.

### Patrons

The Fitter of Expeditions at the University has furnished his license, his credit and his reading list.

### Enemies

The Wardens, who warn once. The Quay Council of Sarmelis, whose Fish-Reeve has moved to forbid any crew to sign with him in the herring season. The beacon families, whose lamp-hands he is hiring.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|The Thordimalon]]**: Ílsevand holds the rung of Thermoi.
- **[[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]]**: He is a Fellow of a court.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: He is of the Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Eight guards by the first dark.** The scholar hires the party on the Lísere coast to keep him at the Fen Door for a fortnight of dark nights, and the Warden there is alone. He pays in Kalipsan silver.
2. **The song in the sail-loft.** A bard has been singing on Salt Row about guards who did not come back from the interior, and four of the scholar's men have asked for their advance to be returned. Ílsevand wants the bard stopped or the men replaced by someone who does not drink on Salt Row.
3. **Leave of the Warden.** He asks a party to carry a courteous letter to the Warden at the Door requesting leave to watch from the dry ground. He does not expect the answer to be yes, and wants it in writing for the Fitter of Expeditions.
