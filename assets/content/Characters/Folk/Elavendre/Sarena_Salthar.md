# Appearance {#appearance}

Sárena Salthar is a 49-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of medium build. She is a small, straight-backed woman with gray hair under a linen cap and pale blue eyes that count. Her hands are red from the wash-house. She wears the pilgrim-house's brown wool and a ring of keys that she does not rattle.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sárena Salthar is the keeper of the pilgrim-house at [[place-kalvanel|Kalvanel]], the chartered village of three hundred and eighty in a fold of the foothills where the last meadow runs into birch and the Tribal Lands begin. The post passes by the house's own choice, not by any lord's. She has held it for thirty years, a widow of fifty who has never failed to count the pilgrims out as carefully as she counted them in.

The village's charter binds it to lodge any pilgrim for three nights and to refuse every hired company. A Tradition Keeper comes down from the hills when a pilgrim is waiting, usually within three days, and decides there whether the pilgrim may go up with an escort. Most are refused, and the house does not say so in advance. This week a young man has sat in the long hall for two days and no Keeper has come.

## Psyche

### Personality

Sárena is kind, firm and impossible to hurry. She feeds everyone the same bread and sets the same rules. She prefers silence to a lie and will say "I do not know" without shame.

### Motivation

She wants every pilgrim fed, counted and sent home alive. She has never lost one to the wood and does not intend to start with a student.

### Strengths

Thirty years of pilgrims have taught her which of them mean what they say. The Keepers trust her count.

### Weaknesses

She is alone in the hall at night with the pilgrims, and has other pilgrims to feed. She will not leave the house to ask four men on mules why they turned back.

## Social

### Companions

Two village girls help in the kitchen and the wash-house. The birch-cutters bring her wood.

### Patrons

No lord. The house chooses its keeper, and the village is the house's patron.

### Enemies

Any hired company that tries the door. The crown's chancery has twice asked her to relax the rule, and she has twice sent them to the charter.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Sárena is a Freeman of the crown's chartered village of Kalvanel.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The young fellow.** Sárena needs someone to sit with the young man until a Keeper comes, because she has other pilgrims to feed and does not want to be alone in the hall if the four mules return.
2. **The mules.** Someone has to ask the four men why they turned back at the last meadow, and the village will not do it. Sárena will pay a party for the answer in beds and bread.
3. **Three nights.** A company of hired guards has reached the village and asks to lodge as pilgrims. The charter says no, the innkeeper of Gandelis says the charter is old, and Sárena asks a party to stand with her at the door.

:::secret {title="The pilgrim"}
The young man is a friend of [[being-ielaraskir|Prince Yélaras]], a fellow of the University and the leader of the summer survey the prince means to join. He has come ahead to find out whether a party that asks, and is refused, will be treated as a trespasser or as a guest. The four on the mules are the crown's, sent by the queen's chancery to see that he is not harmed.

He sincerely wants leave. If it is refused and he goes anyway, the Wardens will warn him once, and the prince's name will be in the mouth of whoever finds the body.

**Hook: the leave.** Sárena has guessed more than she says. If a party brings her proof of who he is, she will not turn him out and will not send him up, and she will ask the party which they would prefer.
