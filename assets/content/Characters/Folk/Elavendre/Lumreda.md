# Appearance {#appearance}

Lúmreda is a 65-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of medium build. She is broad and still, with white hair braided in a crown and a deeply seamed face. Her gray eyes close when she recites. She wears a plain gray wool dress, an apron of undyed linen and no ornament. She carries nothing written, and she carries nothing she has not been told.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lúmreda of the Móghorendi is a Tradition Keeper, one of the specialist memorizers who hold the unwritten Kindred Pact, the tribal songs and the genealogies. She teaches and consults in the Keepers' House at [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]], a long, low house at the upper end of the compounds where writing is forbidden, and where a guest who carries a pen is asked to leave it at the door and is not asked twice.

Training to become a Keeper begins in childhood and continues across decades, and the Keeper's chosen child lives in the house. The Keepers hold the counter-songs the Wardens sing at the tarn and the ridge, and the song the Wardens of the watch at Talve are taught. The Keepers teach the Talve song to the Wardens and to nobody else.

## Psyche

### Personality

Lúmreda is patient, exact and humorous. She corrects a recital by repeating it the right way once and does not comment. She takes silence as an answer.

### Motivation

She wants the Pact held whole in the next generation, her chosen child ready and the songs not lost. She wants no one to ask her to write.

### Strengths

She has the whole of the Pact and most of the genealogies in her head. The Wardens trust her without question.

### Weaknesses

She is old, and there is only one child. A Keeper who forgets forgets for the whole people.

## Social

### Companions

The chosen child, a girl of eleven. The Keepers of the other tribes who come to consult.

### Patrons

None. The Keepers hold the Pact by the people's consent.

### Enemies

Pens, ledgers and the people who carry them.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]]**: Lúmreda is an Elder, the senior of a lineage of Keepers.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The counter-song.** A Warden of the meadow has fallen ill, and the one left on the shore needs someone to fetch a Tradition Keeper from the Lodge before the next still night. Lúmreda cannot go, and asks a party to sit with the Warden until the child she sends arrives.
2. **In the crowd.** A bard of the College of the Line is to sing the heir's line at the Elanmere fair, and Lúmreda means to stand in the crowd and hear it, to learn whether the genealogy has been sung as it is held. She asks a party to take her.
3. **The pen in the house.** An invited guest left a pen in the Keepers' House last night, and Lúmreda cannot tell whether it was a mistake. She asks a party to watch the guest hall.
