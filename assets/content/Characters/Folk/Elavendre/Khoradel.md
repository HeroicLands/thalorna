# Appearance {#appearance}

Khoradel is a 62-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He is broad-shouldered and slow-moving, with white hair cut at the shoulder, a weathered face and dark brown eyes. At his throat he wears a single dark stone from the Lodge's hearth.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Khoradel of the Dhárgandi is an elder of the tribe of the Lodge and the Speaker of the common council of [[affiliation-aelndntrbs|the Áelendan tribes]], the presiding officer who keeps its peace and the order of speaking when the delegates of the seven tribes meet at [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]]. The Dhárgandi are heard first when the Lodge calls the tribes together.

The council's delegates argue and do not bind, and this season they are divided. Some tribes hold that the Wardens' warning and their judgment are enough. Others want the council to tell the crown plainly that a guard hired by the Order and taken on an Araelwen is dealt with as a desecrator, so that no lowland father can say his son was not told. A silver casket sent up for the council is overdue, and its envoy with it. Khoradel keeps the order and has not yet let the question come to the fire.

## Psyche

### Personality

Khoradel is genial, slow and shrewd. He lets a quarrel run until it tires and then asks the question it was about.

### Motivation

He wants the council to speak with one voice to the crown, or not at all. He wants the casket found before the council sits without it.

### Strengths

Every chieftain at the Lodge has eaten at his hearth, and he remembers what each of them said there.

### Weaknesses

He is Dhárgandi, and the Kháldendi delegates do not believe he keeps the order fairly when the feud is in the room.

## Social

### Companions

[[being-ulmeraosseln|Úlmera Ósselnde]], the chieftain who sits nearest him at the council.

### Patrons

The elders of the Dhárgandi, and the delegates who keep him in the office.

### Enemies

The Kháldendi delegates, by inheritance rather than by choice.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]]**: Khoradel is an Elder and holds the office of Speaker of the Council.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The overdue casket.** Khoradel asks a party to go down the envoys' road and learn what became of the casket and its envoy, before the council sits without them.
2. **The word to the crown.** If the council speaks, its word to the crown needs carrying down to the Liaison to the Tribes exactly as it is given, and Khoradel wants carriers who will add nothing.
3. **The feud in the room.** A Kháldendi delegate has accused him of keeping the order against them, and he asks a party to sit through a day of the council and say plainly whether he did.
