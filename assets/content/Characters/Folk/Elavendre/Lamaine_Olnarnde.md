# Appearance {#appearance}

Lámaine Ólnarnde is a 57-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She is slight and very upright, with silver hair worn long and loose in the manner of the harbor's older families and green eyes that rest on the far side of a speaker. Her skin is pale and finely lined. She wears gray and sea-green, and a ring of green glass on her smallest finger that she turns while she listens.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lámaine Ólnarnde is the Speaker of [[affiliation-twncnclprtaln|the Town Council of Port Aelion]], chosen by the Councilors from among themselves to preside over its sittings and to speak for [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]] to the crown's officers. She sits on the council by custom, as the head of one of the town's older families, the families the town says have fae blood. She has not said whether hers does.

The council governs by principles no law code holds, and she is the one who knows them. This winter it is hearing the harbor's quarrel over the astrologer [[being-tarelblthl|Tárella Pelthal]], whom the merchant captain [[being-tralkarherse|Tralkar Hérselnde]] blames for the ship and crew he lost in the mist, and whom an inquiry has cleared. The Keeper of the Wharves, [[being-naldorkardet|Naldor Kardeth]], follows Tárella's readings and has prospered by them, and half the captains count him her accomplice. Lámaine wants the matter settled at the council's table, where the town has always settled its business, and not in a royal court that would ask what the mist is.

## Psyche

### Personality

Lámaine is courteous, slow and certain. She lets every Councilor speak and then says what the council has decided, and is seldom wrong about it.

### Motivation

She wants the town's quarrels kept the town's. She wants the balance between the freemen and the older families kept by custom, as it has always been kept.

### Strengths

She remembers every compromise the council has made in her lifetime and the one it made before.

### Weaknesses

She will not let the crown's law into a question the council can answer, even when the council cannot.

## Social

### Companions

The heads of the older families, who sit by custom beside her.

### Patrons

The council that chose her, and the older families who sit beside her by custom.

### Enemies

Naldor Kardeth, who keeps the wharves and would like to keep the council.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-twncnclprtaln|Town Council of Port Aelion]]**: Lámaine is a Councilor and holds the office of Speaker of the Council.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: She is Gentry of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The mist.** Lámaine asks a party to learn, quietly, whether the mist that took Tralkar's ship was weather, before the captains take the question to the royal court.
2. **The Keeper's ledger.** She wants to know how much of the timber Naldor's wharves load was ever licensed at Liranel, and she does not want the crown to learn it first.
3. **The College's report.** A foreign envoy has complained to the council that the Envoys' College sold its report on his court, and Lámaine wants the truth of it before the Master is asked.
