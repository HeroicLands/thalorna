# Appearance {#appearance}

Ilvane Thorsel is a 25-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She is quick and laughing, with auburn hair coming loose from its pins and green eyes that are always on the prince. She wears University green with ink on her second finger.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ilvane Thorsel is a fellow of the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] and a friend of [[being-ielaraskir|Prince Yélaras]]. She believes his company will carry her party past the Wardens at the margin of the Silver Forests. She laughs too much.
