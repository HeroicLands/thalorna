# Appearance {#appearance}

Raltila Oltessanto is a 2,932-year-old woman who stands 6'4" tall and is of light build. Her silver hair is bound at the nape with a cord of plaited grass, and her violet eyes are steady and unhurried. She wears plain gray and no ornament.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Raltila Oltessanto is the Lord of [[affiliation-nclvthlss|the Enclave of Ethalosse]], the threshold enclave at the eastern margin of the [[place-alndntrblnds|Áelendan Tribal Lands]] and the place where the [[lore-senaliclt|Senali]] conduct business with the world. The Senali raise a lord for things done and by every voice, and no human record names her.

The Senali do not treat with human powers directly. A warning, a refusal, a question about a disturbed site or the return of something taken comes up to Ethalosse in the mouths of [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] elders, and what goes back down is what the enclave has said. When [[affiliation-nclvlrss|the Enclave of Aelirosse]] or [[affiliation-nclvsrnthl|the Enclave of Serenthale]] deals with the world, it deals through Ethalosse, and through her. The carriers are received by [[being-ansorulamasse|Ansorula Masserinto]] in the court of worked stone, and no answer goes back down until Raltila holds that the enclave has made it.

The carriers come up more often this season. The questions are about the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]]'s camps above the tree line and the things its parties carry down to [[place-beravel|Péravel]], and the enclave's answers go back down without a word added, to whoever needs them, the crown included.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is measured, exact and dry, and she prefers a short answer to a kind one.

### Motivation

She wants the carrying kept as it is: exact, through the Áelendan and no one else. The relationship between the Senali and the crown of [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Élavendre]] is the tribes' to grant, and she wants it to stay so.

### Strengths

She has received more human questions than any Senalo living, and she knows what a question is asking before the carriers have finished it.

### Weaknesses

She gives no answer the enclave has not made, and an urgent question waits on the enclave like any other.
