# Appearance {#appearance}

Kélvira Áulirnde is a 35-year-old woman who stands 5'10" tall and is of light build. She is tall and composed, with brown hair bound back, gray eyes and fair skin. She wears a moss-green cloak with a strip of birch bark knotted at the collar, and carries her harp in a case on her back. She inclines her head before she speaks, and when she greets a stranger she half sings it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kélvira Áulirnde is a Bard of [[affiliation-clgline|the College of the Line]] at [[place-beravel|Péravel]] and the bard of the [[affiliation-hsehalvireth|House of Hálvireth]], whose line she sings at its feasts exactly as the Colleges proved it. She is of the house's people, not its blood: her mother held a meadow of the house below the capital, and the house paid for her years at the College.

She meets newcomers on the quays of Péravel when the house has an interest in them, which is more often than they suppose, and she asks which way they mean to go. Every autumn she goes up to the fair at [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]] and sings the house's claim to the old blood, through Sírtala of the Dhárgandi, before the Dhárgandi camped on the shore. This year a Dhárgandi elder has sent word down that he means to hear it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kélvira is courteous, patient and exact. She chooses words that read two ways and enjoys it. She does not hurry a song or an answer.

### Motivation

She wants the line sung true, the house's claim to stand at Elanmere, and her own name to be counted among the College's best before she is fifty.

### Strengths

Her voice and her memory are very good, and she knows every house's line in the basin as well as her own house's.

### Weaknesses

She sings a claim she has not proved herself. She knows that the Dhárgandi keep no daughter of the man the house names as Sírtala's father, and she has not told Taeren how much that worries her.

## Social

### Companions

The house's harper, and the other bards of the College at Péravel.

### Patrons

[[being-taerenhalvir|Taeren]], head of the House of Hálvireth, who keeps her.

### Enemies

None of her own. The house's rivals are hers by office.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The quay.** Kélvira meets a party off the barge at Péravel and offers the house's hospitality for a night, and in the morning asks them to escort her to the Elanmere fair.
2. **The elder at the fair.** The Dhárgandi elder who means to hear the line is camped on the shore, and Kélvira asks a party to learn, before she sings, what he remembers.
3. **The College's question.** The Master of the College, [[being-kurthandlauv|Kurthand Láuvinnde]], has asked Kélvira whether the house would accept a second hearing of its proof, and she wants to know who asked him to ask.
