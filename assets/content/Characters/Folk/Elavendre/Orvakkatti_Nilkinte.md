# Appearance {#appearance}

Orvakkatti Nìlkinte is a 3,699-year-old man who stands 6'8" tall and is of light build. His silver hair falls loose to his waist, and his gray eyes are deep-set and slow to move. A thin ring of old scarring circles his right wrist. He wears gray, with a cloak the soft green of birch leaves in early summer.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Orvakkatti Nìlkinte is the Lord of [[affiliation-nclvlrss|the Enclave of Aelirosse]], the Senali of [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]], whose dwellings are worked into living silver birch. The [[lore-senaliclt|Senali]] raise a lord for things done and by every voice. No human record names him.

Aelirosse and the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] keep one law between them: no standing silver birch in the Silver Forests is felled, by anyone, for any reason ([[lore-blacksnow|The Black Snow]]). He is the enclave's voice in that law. This year a strip of green bark cut from [[place-wkngstnd|the Waking Stand]] lies in a chapterhouse collection at [[place-beravel|Péravel]], the Wardens want the man who cut it, and the enclave has said nothing of it through [[place-ethalosse|Ethalosse]]. The silence is his decision.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is warm with the Áelendan and formal with everyone else, and he keeps the two manners apart.

### Motivation

He wants the birch law kept by those who made it, the Áelendan and Aelirosse, without the crown or the Order drawn into a matter that is not theirs. He wants the Wardens to find the man who cut the bark themselves.

### Strengths

The Áelendan of the Silver Forests trust him as their grandparents did, and Aelirosse trusts his judgment of them.

### Weaknesses

The law is under more pressure than it has been in a generation, and his silence is read at the Moot of the Silver Forests as indifference.

## Social

### Companions

[[being-lauppivosolme|Lauppivo Solmerinto]] and the lords of Aelirosse, and the elders of the Áelendan households of the forest's edge.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvlrss|Enclave of Aelirosse]]**: Orvakkatti is a Lord, and holds the office of Lord of the Enclave.

:::secret {title="Why Aelirosse is silent"}
Orvakkatti knows what was done at [[place-rngtrndpths|the Ring of Turned Paths]], and he chose that Aelirosse turn the paths outward and do nothing more, because the Senali do not fight. He holds the cut bark and the ring to be one trouble, the Order working on ground that is not its own, and he will not carry either to the crown. He waits for the Áelendan to ask, as the law between them has always been asked for.
:::
