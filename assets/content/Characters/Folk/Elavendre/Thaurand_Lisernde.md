# Appearance {#appearance}

Thaurand Lísernde is a 46-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of heavy build. He is broad and sunburned, with brown hair tied back and hazel eyes creased from squinting up trunks. He wears a green coat of the crown's forest court over a timber-man's boots, and carries the court's measuring-rod, marked in spans, as a walking staff.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Thaurand Lísernde is the Forester of the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]], who holds the crown's forest court at [[place-liranel|Liranel]]. The court licenses every stem cut in the licensed belt of the inner coast, sets the bounds of every licensed block and has every stem measured at the tally-house before it goes down to the wharves of [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]]. He also issues the licenses of the crown's guides. He was a forester of the court for twenty years before he was its head, and he has walked every block in the belt.

He wants the licensed belt cut by the license, every stem counted and the bounds of the groves and the Áelendan ground left where the roll puts them. This season the roll is the trouble. A ring of old oak inside the block that [[being-garrelpelth|Garrel Pelthal]] holds under license has been felled. The grove-elder [[being-evranetolsel|Evrane Tolsel]] says it stood inside her grove, the Wardens say it was Áelendan ground, and the tally-house roll puts it inside the block. One of the three is wrong, and the roll is his ([[scenario-grvthtsntrs|The Grove That Is Not Ours]]).

## Psyche

### Personality

Thaurand is blunt, practical and honest. He trusts a measured stem more than a sworn word, and says so.

### Motivation

He wants the roll to be right. He has not yet admitted to himself that somebody at his own court may have made it wrong.

### Strengths

He knows the belt stem by stem. The hauliers and the guides respect him, and the factors cannot frighten him.

### Weaknesses

He is slow with paper and trusts his clerks to keep it. The Grove Council thinks him the factors' man, and he has given it reason.

## Social

### Companions

The foresters of the court, and his tally-clerk [[being-orlenveldar|Orlen Véldarin]], who keeps the roll.

### Patrons

The Chancellor's clerks at Péravel, who seal the licenses he grants, and the Treasurer, who takes the timber dues.

### Enemies

Evrane Tolsel and the grove-elders of the Liranel country, who hold that the court licenses what is not the crown's to license.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Thaurand holds the office of Forester, as Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The disputed bound.** The Forester hires a party to walk the bounds of Garrel Pelthal's block with the roll in hand and find whether the line on the parchment keeps to the line on the ground.
2. **The unmeasured timber.** A shipment of squared oak loaded at a wharf of Port Aelion was never measured at the tally-house. Thaurand wants to know where it was cut before the Treasurer asks him.
3. **The guide's license.** A hired guide of Liranel is carrying a crown guide's license the court never issued.

:::secret {title="The roll"}
The bounds were moved. The roll in use at the tally-house puts the oak ring a hundred paces inside Garrel Pelthal's block, and an older roll in the court's keeping puts it outside. The tally-clerk Orlen Véldarin was paid to move the bounds on the roll, and the surveyor who set the boundary stone on the ground took the clerk's figures. The ring was Áelendan ground.
:::
