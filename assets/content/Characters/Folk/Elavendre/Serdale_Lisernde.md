# Appearance {#appearance}

Sérdale Lísernde is a 76-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. She is tall, upright and unbowed, with brown hair in a coil and a clear face. Her gray eyes are clear. She wears a necklace of smooth river stones that click when she moves, and she walks without a staff, one hand trailing along the wall.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sérdale Lísernde is the oldest and most respected pure mystic shaman of a tribe at [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]], an elder healer. She and [[being-rnthrprdln|Ranthor Párdalen]] differ in their approaches, but she respects his results and refers patients to him for practical healing when her own spiritual work is insufficient.

The Lodge is the center of Áelendan collective life, and its compounds hold about fifteen hundred people. She treats the Lodge's sick beside the Washing Pool, where everyone washes before the great rites, and she sings with the patient until the patient sings. She has never been to a lowland town.

## Psyche

### Personality

Sérdale is serene, funny and uncompromising. She talks to the dying as to the living, and to the living as if they might be dying. She believes in no healing she cannot hear.

### Motivation

She wants each patient to be heard before treated, and she wants her apprentices to know when to refer. She wants a peaceful death in the compounds, and she has told no one which.

### Strengths

She has the longest memory of illness in the Lodge. She is trusted and loved.

### Weaknesses

She is old and frail. She will not send a patient to a lowland healer, and Ranthor is the single exception.

## Social

### Companions

Three apprentices, the youngest under twelve. The Tradition Keepers consult her.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

None she would name. Disease.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]]**: Sérdale is an Elder, the senior of a lineage.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A Warden of the meadow.** A Warden of the upper meadow has fallen ill and the one left on the shore needs someone to fetch a healer before the next still night. Sérdale will not leave the Lodge, and asks a party to bring the Warden down.
2. **The referral.** A patient has been sent to Ranthor Párdalen in Valdun, and Sérdale asks a party to carry her written message, which she is not supposed to have written, and to wait for his answer.
3. **The youngest apprentice.** Sérdale's youngest apprentice has heard the singing at the tarn in her sleep. Sérdale asks a party to sit with the child at night and say whether anyone answers.
