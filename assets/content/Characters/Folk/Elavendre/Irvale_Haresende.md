# Appearance {#appearance}

Írvale Háresende is a 45-year-old woman who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. She is lean and windburned, with auburn hair cropped short and gray eyes that look past a speaker to his horse. She rides in a knight's leathers with the crown's badge on the shoulder, and carries a sheriff's white wand in her boot.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Írvale Háresende is the crown's Sheriff of [[place-ellive|Éllive]], the heartland basin of the Berav. She tries the crown's pleas in the shire, holds the posse and collects the levy, the road tolls and the duty entered at the tally-houses through her clerks and reeves. Her hall is at [[place-beravel|Péravel]], and she holds the shire court there and in the grain hall at [[place-ruthelmar|Ruthelmar]], and sends her reeve up to [[place-lamanis|Lámanis]] when the orchard levy is in dispute. The harbor-clerk at [[place-sirlathen|Sirlathen]] files the count of every hull with her. She is a knight of a basin house, and the cousin of [[being-chenirahares|Chénira Háresende]], the crown's Liaison to the Tribes.

She wants the shire quiet, the tolls in and the posse never called. The basin feeds the capital, and nine hundred thousand people live in it, and she believes a sheriff of Éllive who is in the news has failed. This year she is in it. The Treasurer's clerks are reading her rolls, a Vilérian trader with eleven men on deck who are not sailors lies off the bar at Sirlathen, and the bargemasters of Ruthelmar say her reeve takes the toll twice.

## Psyche

### Personality

Írvale is brisk, plain-spoken and tireless. She is happiest on a horse and least happy at a table.

### Motivation

She wants the shire's order kept without anyone noticing that it is kept.

### Strengths

She knows every road and ferry in the basin, and her posse turns out in an hour.

### Weaknesses

She is impatient with paper, and her reeve's honesty is something she has assumed and never checked.

## Social

### Companions

Her reeves and the riders of the posse, and her cousin Chénira, whom she sees twice a year.

### Patrons

The crown at Péravel, which named her, and the Justiciar's courts, where her pleas are heard.

### Enemies

The Treasurer, [[being-nsvrskelvas|Ánsevirsa Kélvasende]], whose clerks are reading her rolls, and the bargemasters of Ruthelmar.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Írvale holds the office of Sheriff of Éllive, as a Knight.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The reeve.** The bargemasters of Ruthelmar say the sheriff's reeve takes the toll twice. Írvale asks a party she does not know to ride a barge down with a cargo and pay whatever is asked.
2. **The ship off the bar.** The harbor-clerk at Sirlathen has sent word of a Vilérian hull with soldiers on its deck. The sheriff wants it watched until it sails or lands.
