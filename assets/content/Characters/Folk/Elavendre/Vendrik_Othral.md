# Appearance {#appearance}

Vendrik Othral is a 40-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. He is wiry and red-haired, with a gap in his front teeth and green eyes, and he wears a poacher's hood.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Vendrik Othral leads a band of poachers out of [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]] who use the old keepers' lodge below [[place-hntrsrng|the Hunter's Ring]] as a hunting camp, since the ring holds deer that nobody else hunts. A fence in [[place-beravel|Péravel]] has paid him for silver of an old pattern before.

:::secret {title="The casket"}
Vendrik's band robbed the envoy [[being-devrikveldr|Devrik Véldarin]] at dusk on the road below the Hunter's Ring and fled uphill through the ring with the envoy's silver casket, prying the silver bands from the boundary stones as it went. He took the casket deeper into the ring, and the hunter took his men one by one and then him.

He hangs alive in a woven cradle of bough and bark in the tree at the ring's heart, three branches below his own pack and the casket, with his knees drawn up and his hood still on, and his eyes have stopped looking at anything. He wants to be cut down, and he will say anything to anyone who reaches him.
:::
