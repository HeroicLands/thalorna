# Appearance {#appearance}

Teiren is a 36-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. He is open-faced and quick, with brown hair, hazel eyes and the house's habit of listening with his head a little to one side, as if for music. He wears a harper's thumb-ring that was his father's and does not play.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Teiren is the head of [[affiliation-hsehalvireth|the House of Hálvireth]], a lowland house of the middle rank holding river-meadows in the basin of [[place-beravriver|the Berav]] below [[place-beravel|Péravel]], and he keeps a house at the capital, where he holds a chair on the royal council as a Councilor of the Crown. He holds the meadows, and his people eat. His heir is his infant daughter, Sílvera, counted into the line at her naming feast in the hearing of everyone who might dispute it.

The house claims the old blood through Sírtala, a woman of the Dhárgandi who came down to the basin, and every autumn its bard goes up to the fair at [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]] and sings her line before the Dhárgandi camped on the shore. Nobody has ever stood up. This year a Dhárgandi elder has sent word down that he means to hear it, and the Dhárgandi keep no daughter of the man the house names as Sírtala's father. The house's standing rests on the old blood, and Teiren knows it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Teiren is warm, quick and anxious under it. He laughs at feasts and lies awake after them.

### Motivation

He wants the claim to stand at Elanmere, his daughter's place in the line kept, and the house counted in the first rank while it still keeps a harper and sends its children to the art.

### Strengths

His people are loyal, his meadows are good, and his bard is very good.

### Weaknesses

He does not know whether the claim is true, and he has never dared ask the Tradition Keepers.

## Social

### Companions

The house's bard, [[being-kelviraaulir|Kélvira Áulirnde]], who sings the line, and the harper his father kept.

### Patrons

[[being-kurthandlauv|Kurthand Láuvinnde]] and the College of the Line, which proved the house's line in the year the mountains closed.

### Enemies

The Dhárgandi elder who means to hear the claim, and any house that would profit from its failing.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-hsehalvireth|House of Hálvireth]]**: Teiren is Of the Line and holds the office of Head of the House of Hálvireth.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: He is a Noble of the crown and holds the office of Councilor of the Crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The bard to the fair.** Teiren hires a party to escort the house's bard, [[being-kelviraaulir|Kélvira Áulirnde]], up to Elanmere and to stand by her when the Dhárgandi elder hears the line.
2. **A fosterling for Orethan.** A stranger at Péravel says he is of Orethan's blood and asks for the fosterling the house owes. Teiren wants him proved or disproved before he answers.
3. **The Keepers' word.** Teiren asks a party to learn, quietly, whether the Tradition Keepers at Ésalosa remember Sírtala at all.
