# Appearance {#appearance}

Orlen Veldarin is a 31-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. He is pale and neat, with brown hair parted exactly and blue eyes that drop when he is asked a question. His cuffs are stained with ink.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Orlen Veldarin keeps the rolls at the tally-house of [[place-liranel|Liranel]]. He was paid to move the bounds of a licensed block on the roll, and the boundary stone on the ground was set by a surveyor who took his figures. He is polite and frightened.
