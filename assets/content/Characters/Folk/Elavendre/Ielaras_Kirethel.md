# Appearance {#appearance}

Íelaras Kírethel is a 23-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. He has pale, clear skin, dark brown hair cut shorter than the court wears it, and his mother's gray eyes, narrowed a little from reading by lamplight. There is usually ink on the second finger of his right hand. He dresses well and plainly, in the green of the house, and he has a University man's habit of answering a question with a better question. He laughs easily and argues happily, and he says plainly what other people at court leave open.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Prince Íelaras Kírethel is the heir to the crown of the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]] and the only son of [[being-aurenthekir|Queen Áurenthe]], head of the royal [[affiliation-hsekirethel|House of Kírethel]]. He lives at the court in [[place-beravel|Péravel]], and he was schooled at the [University of Péravel]{.name data-type="affiliation"}, where the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] educates the children of the nobility. His friends are its fellows.

He wants to be crowned as what he is, a prince of the old blood who has read the Order's books, and he holds that the two are not enemies. He says so in company. The bards of the Circle have heard him, and he means to answer them in the way a house answers a doubted claim: by having his line sung at the autumn fair at [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]], before the people who remember the genealogy differently.

## Background

**The University.** Íelaras was sent to the University at twelve, as the children of the nobility are sent. He studied law and the histories, and he reads Vilari as easily as Élavendri. He came back to court at twenty with a library and a set of friends his mother receives with perfect courtesy.

**The fair.** At the autumn fair the market singers sing [[lore-roadsendlmnt|the Lament at the Road's End]] on the first night, a satire against a crown that names the founder of his house. Íelaras has read every version the Colleges keep. He wants to stand at the fair the night after it and have his line sung, as [Velthar]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of [[affiliation-hsehalvireth|Hálvireth]] once went up and sang his, and was heard.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is quick, warm and certain, and he believes that a good argument well made settles a question. He is generous to his friends and fair to his opponents, and he does not believe that in Élavendre a plain answer to an open thing is the rudest word a guest can bring.

### Motivation

He wants the crown and the University reconciled in his own person, and he wants the Áelendan to see it. He wants, in time, to be the monarch the Lodge asks up.

### Strengths

He is the heir by law. He is learned, liked by the scholars and the younger courtiers, and braver in argument than his mother.

### Weaknesses

The judgment the crown fears is never pronounced, only reached, and he is giving the bards the words to reach it. He does not see that the Áelendan count the Order's welcome, and not any single theft, as the offense.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The bard of standing.** A bard of the Circle is singing a satire on the prince in the dove-gray gown in the harp-rooms of Péravel. Íelaras hires a party to learn who is paying the bard, and refuses to believe the answer may be nobody.
2. **The line at the fair.** Íelaras wants his line sung at the Elanmere fair this autumn, and he wants a bard of a College to sing it. He sends a party up with the bard, and the queen sends a second party to see that nothing is sung that cannot be unsung.
3. **The tutor.** His old tutor, a fellow of the University, has gone inward from [[place-liranel|Liranel]] without the crown's guides. Íelaras wants him brought back before the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] find him, and before his mother hears of it.

:::secret {title="The survey of the Cut"}
The royal archive at Péravel keeps the survey [Hóuradel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}'s engineers made of the workings above the tree line, adit by adit ([[lore-sorvandaclose|The Sórvanda Closing]]). Íelaras had it copied for his tutor at the University, as a matter of history, in the year he came back to court. The tutor who has gone inward from Liranel is carrying the copy.

The queen does not know. If the Wardens take the copy from the tutor and read it, they know at once where it came from, and the Áelendan judge the prince by it.

**Hook: the copy.** Íelaras sends a party after his tutor with a second purpose he states only once he trusts them: bring back the survey, or burn it, before anyone above the tree line reads it.
:::
