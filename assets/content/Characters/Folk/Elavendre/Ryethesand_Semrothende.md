# Appearance {#appearance}

Ryéthesand Sémrothende is a 53-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of medium build. He is handsome and well kept, with dark brown hair graying at the temples, olive skin and brown eyes that are always pleasant. He wears the plain good cloth of a man who is received at foreign courts, and the College's signet on a chain under it, never on his hand.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ryéthesand Sémrothende is the Master of [[affiliation-clgenvoys|the Envoys' College]] at [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]], the College of [[affiliation-bardicolgs|the Bardic Colleges]] for diplomacy, protocol and the gathering of information without seeming to. He chooses which envoy goes where, and when a house or the crown needs a word carried where it does not wish to be seen sending it, he gives a Bard under the College's name. The College keeps no post for a spy, and he says so whenever he is asked.

The College takes the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]'s patronage, which the other three Colleges refuse, and the Order pays for a study of what the foreign courts say of the Order. Ryéthesand holds that an envoy must know the Order to speak to it, and that the Colleges live on royal patronage and not their own purity. The Circle meets on the money at every sitting, and the Master of [[affiliation-clgline|the College of the Line]], [[being-kurthandlauv|Kurthand Láuvinnde]], leads the objection.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ryéthesand is charming, attentive and impossible to surprise. He agrees with everyone until he is asked to act.

### Motivation

He wants the College to have the money and the Circle to have the argument, and he wants the argument to go on long enough that the money becomes custom.

### Strengths

He knows what every court of the western kingdoms expects at table and what it will not be asked.

### Weaknesses

A foreign envoy in the harbor says the College's report on his court was sold before it was delivered, and Ryéthesand does not know who sold it.

## Social

### Companions

The Envoys of the College, whom he sends out and receives back.

### Patrons

The crown, which uses his envoys, and the Order, which pays for his study.

### Enemies

Kurthand Láuvinnde and the Colleges that refuse the Order's money.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-clgenvoys|The Envoys' College]]**: Ryéthesand is a Bard of the Circle and holds the office of Master of the College.
- **[[affiliation-bardicolgs|The Bardic Colleges]]**: He sits in the Bardic Circle as Master of a College.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: He is Gentry of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The sold report.** Ryéthesand hires a party to learn who sold the College's report on a foreign court before it was delivered, and to tell him before they tell the Circle.
2. **Under the College's name.** An Envoy of the College needs companions to walk with him to a house beyond Péravel, because the letter he carries is read by three people before it is delivered.
3. **The study.** The Order wants the College's study of the foreign courts delivered early, and Ryéthesand wants a party to carry it by a road the Order does not watch.
