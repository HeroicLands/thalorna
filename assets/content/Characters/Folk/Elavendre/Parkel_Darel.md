# Appearance {#appearance}

Parkel Darel is a 47-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall and is of heavy build. He is big and pale, with graying black hair and blue eyes, and a nose broken and badly set. He wears mail and carries a short spear.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Parkel Darel was the sergeant of a company of sell-swords out of [[place-valdun|Valdun]] that the Order fellow [[being-denrikrnkrs|Denrik Rénkaris]] hired to guard his survey of [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]]. The company marched in and did not march out, and the Order records the survey as lost in the northern interior.

:::secret {title="On the circuit"}
Parkel walks at the head of the Pegged now, round and round the circuit of silver pegs in [[place-rngtrndpths|the Ring of Turned Paths]], and he cannot stop. He is pale as tallow and his eyes look at nothing. His mail is rusted at the shoulders, his boots are worn through at the heel, and he still carries his short spear. If a stranger stands against the circuit he lowers the spear as a rail, not a weapon. What is left of him wants his men out, and his wife at Valdun paid for that.
:::
