# Appearance {#appearance}

Seittavo Nwominto is a 2,625-year-old man who stands 6'6" tall and is of light build. His black hair is cut level at the shoulder, and his eyes are a pale, clear blue. He wears a gray cloak over gray, and he carries nothing, not even a staff.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Seittavo Nwominto is a lord of [[affiliation-nclvsrnthl|the Enclave of Serenthale]]. A Senali lord holds a standing raised by acclaim, not a holding, and Seittavo governs nothing. He was raised for the years he spent walking Lirve's sea wood beside the first [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] who came to keep it, learning their speech and teaching them the edges of the wood.

When Serenthale chooses to speak to the Áelendan, he is the one who goes. He comes out of the wood alone, to a Warden's watch or an elder's hearth, says what the enclave has to say, hears the answer and goes back. He does not say where he goes back to, and nobody asks.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is easy in company, by Senali measure, and fond of the Áelendan. He laughs rarely and with his whole face.

### Motivation

He wants the bond between Serenthale and the Áelendan kept, rare as Serenthale holds it. He wants the Wardens of Lirve to have what they need from the enclave to keep the sea wood, and nothing more.

### Strengths

He knows Lirve's deep forest and its Wardens as well as anyone living, and he speaks Áelendi as the Áelendan speak it.

### Weaknesses

He cannot speak for the enclave beyond what it has decided. A Warden who needs more than that from him has to wait for the enclave.
