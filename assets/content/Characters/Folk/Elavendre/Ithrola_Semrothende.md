# Appearance {#appearance}

Íthrola Sémrothende is a 24-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of heavy build. She is young, tall and solid, pale and freckled, with dark brown hair braided back and anxious gray eyes that she has trained to look level. She wears riding clothes by choice. A child's blue hair-ribbon is tied round her left wrist, and she touches it when she thinks nobody sees.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Íthrola Sémrothende is a young noblewoman whose domain lies on the grain and cattle roads above [[place-ruthelmar|Ruthelmar]], the market town at the head of barge navigation on the Berav. Its farms feed the barges that go down to [[place-beravel|Péravel]], and the Gandelis road starts at her door. Her domain is three days' ride from the cottage of [[being-alyrklmrth|Álira Kálmereth]].

She has heard extraordinary tales of Álira's toymaking, and has a daughter whose nightmares she hopes the toymaker can capture. The cattle factors of the autumn drove need an escort for a herd going down the Gandelis road, which crosses ground where the Wardens are watching more closely than usual, and some of that herd is hers.

## Psyche

### Personality

Íthrola is brisk, attentive and anxious. She works a long day at the grain-hall and a longer night at her daughter's bedside, and she apologizes for neither.

### Motivation

She wants her daughter to sleep and her domain to keep its tenants through the winter. She wants a market town that runs on its own and a reeve who tells her the truth.

### Strengths

She is honest with her tenants and fair with the factors. She has her house's name and its steady income.

### Weaknesses

She is young and tired, and she will try an uncertain remedy before she asks a hard question. She does not know which of her neighbors is selling to the Order.

## Social

### Companions

Her steward and the shire's reeve keep the books. A nurse holds the child through the night.

### Patrons

Her mother's house, which has the ear of the chancery.

### Enemies

A neighbor who has been buying her tenants' debts. The bargemasters' fellowship, who will not carry her crates.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Íthrola is a Noble, the holder of the domain above Ruthelmar.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The heir's figures.** Íthrola commissions twelve figures from the toymaker, one for each month, each with a cryptic instruction, and asks a party to carry the first of them to Álira's door and wait.
2. **The drove.** The factors of the autumn drove need an escort for a herd going down the Gandelis road. Íthrola has cattle in it and asks a party to ride beside it, and to keep the herd on the road.
3. **The bargemasters' refusal.** No bargemaster will carry a certain crate of hers downriver, and she does not know why. She asks a party to learn what the fellowship is afraid of.
