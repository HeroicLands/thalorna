# Appearance {#appearance}

Hersel Órhaimnde is a 38-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build. He is wiry and weathered, with brown hair gone stiff with salt and hands crosshatched with old reed cuts. His brown eyes squint against glare off the water. He wears a thatcher's apron over a wool shirt and a belt hung with a knife, a plumb-line and a tide-whistle.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hersel Órhaimnde is the reeve of [[place-osselen|Ósselen]], a village of about six hundred and fifty reed-cutters on a bank of firm clay in the tidal reach of the Berav a few miles below [[place-beravel|Péravel]]. The village is chosen at the autumn gathering to pick a reeve, and the reeve's first duty is to keep the channel marks. Three stakes of black oak mark the safe way through the beds from the river to the village, and a reeve who lets one go loses the office.

Disputes go to the sheriff at Péravel only when a body is involved, and a body has come down on the ebb and fouled in the hurdles at the edge of the beds: a man in a hired guard's quilted coat, a strongbox-key on a cord at his neck, a black-fletched Áelendan arrow through the ribs. Hersel wants him buried before the sheriff's clerk comes down. The hedge-affiliate wants the arrow burned. The cutters want nobody to learn that the tide brought him in from the capital reach and not from the sea.

## Psyche

### Personality

Hersel is practical, taciturn and stubborn. He reads the tide by the smell of the water and a quarrel by the way the speakers stand. He keeps a stake or a promise.

### Motivation

He wants the village out of the capital's business. He wants the channel marks standing at the end of his term and the body gone from the beds before it brings the sheriff's clerk.

### Strengths

The village trusts his marks. He knows every channel of the beds and every cutter's boat.

### Weaknesses

He is a reed-cutter and not a magistrate. A body is the one thing the village cannot settle, and he has chosen to settle it.

## Social

### Companions

The cutters of the village, the hedge-affiliate who keeps the knotted cord of the Grove Council, and the eel-trappers.

### Patrons

None. The autumn gathering is his master.

### Enemies

The sheriff's clerk. Whoever put the body in the river.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Hersel is a Freeman of the crown, reeve of Ósselen by the village's choice.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The reeve's burial.** The reeve will pay a party to take the body to Péravel in a boat without a flag, and to bury it in the guards' ground there under another name.
2. **The key.** The strongbox-key is worth a hired guard's life to someone, and a party that carries it into the capital will be followed. Hersel asks a party to take it to the guards' house and say where it was found.
3. **The arrow.** The hedge-affiliate wants the arrow burned and Hersel wants it kept. A party is asked by each to carry it, and to learn which of them is right.

:::secret {title="The arrow"}
The guard was hired at an inn in Péravel for a season in the interior and killed on the road below Gandelis by a man of his own company, not by a Warden. He had heard what the scholar he guarded meant to do at the margin and said he would not stand to it. The arrow is a Warden's, picked up from an earlier skirmish, and was driven in afterward so that his death would read as an Araelwen death.

The body went into the river at the capital quays and the tide brought it here. The strongbox-key opens a box in the hired guards' house at Péravel, which holds the scholar's contract and a second page nobody was supposed to read.
:::
