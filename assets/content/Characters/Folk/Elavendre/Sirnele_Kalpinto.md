# Appearance {#appearance}

Sìrnele Kalpinto is a 331-year-old woman who stands 6'2" tall and is of light build. Her silver hair is cut at the jaw, and her blue eyes are kind and tired. She carries a satchel of worked birch bark that smells of fern and smoke.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sìrnele Kalpinto is a healer of [[affiliation-nclvlrss|the Enclave of Aelirosse]], of the hearth of Kalpinto, the hearth of pale wood. She sits with the sick in the enclave and, more often than any other Senalo of Aelirosse, at the hearths of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] of the forest's edge.

Marriage, fosterage and long friendship happen at the edge where the Senali and the Áelendan meet, rarely and quietly. She fostered an Áelendan girl at Aelirosse, who heard the sayings of the Oak-Warden [Olmasitti Ropelinto]{.name data-type="being/npc"} passed among the Senali in runs and carried them to the Tradition Keepers ([[lore-saunari|The Oak-Warden's Sayings]]). The girl is a grown woman of the tribes now, and visits.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is gentle, practical and stubborn at a sickbed.

### Motivation

She wants the Áelendan of the forest's edge well, and she wants her foster-daughter's children to know the wood as their mother did.

### Strengths

She is a fine healer by Senali measure, which is beyond any human one, and the Áelendan let her in at any hour.

### Weaknesses

She is younger than most of Aelirosse and gives more of herself to the Áelendan than the enclave thinks wise.
