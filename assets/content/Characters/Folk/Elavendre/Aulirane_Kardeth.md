# Appearance {#appearance}

Áulirane Kardeth is a 21-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of medium build. She is young and slight, with brown hair in a plain braid and blue eyes that read the room before she enters it. She dresses in the Kardeth green without ornament, save a bronze Áelendan clasp at the shoulder that she wears in public and in private.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Áulirane Kardeth is a young noblewoman with ancestry among the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan People]] who holds the noble seat of [[place-cardeth|Kardeth]] near [[place-liranel|Liranel]]. She is intelligent and ambitious, and she seeks out the alchemist [[being-belrkflwyn|Melrik Salvin]] for discreet remedies.

Kardeth is a village of about 250 on the coast of Lirve. The lady holds the seat under the crown, and among the Élavendri the land in a lord's keeping is a duty and not a possession. In a bad year her stewardship is the first thing the village draws on. The crown's forest court at Liranel issues the licenses and measures every stem cut, and past the licensed belt the old forest is the country of the Senali and the Wardens.

## Psyche

### Personality

Áulirane is clever, ironic and quick to calculate. She shares a certain pragmatism about the gap between public virtue and private necessity. She asks a servant what a stranger said before she asks the stranger.

### Motivation

She wants Kardeth fed, the seat's standing secure and her own purposes private. She wants to know what the alchemist makes of her, and whether the Bardic Circle sent him to find out.

### Strengths

She has her house's standing and a clear head. The village trusts her stewardship, and a few of the Áelendan treat her as a cousin.

### Weaknesses

She is young and has few allies. She cannot say what her remedies are for, and so she cannot be seen to depend on the one man who knows.

## Social

## Plot Hooks

1. **The test.** Melrik Salvin wants to know whether Lady Áulirane is testing him for the Bardic Circle before he makes her next remedy, and he cannot ask her. He pays a party to spend a few days at Kardeth and learn whom the lady answers to, if anyone.
2. **The bard who came to hear.** A bard of the College of the Line has arrived to hear her house's proof, and Áulirane would rather he did not. She asks a party to keep him lodged and amused.
3. **The Áelendan cousin.** A kinsman from the tribes has come to the village gate asking for her by her Áelendan name. She asks a party to meet him, and to learn what he wants before she does.
