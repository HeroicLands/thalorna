# Appearance {#appearance}

Palven Orsel is a 48-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of light build. He is thin and stooped, with graying brown hair worn long and gray eyes that look at an object rather than at a person. He dresses in University wool and keeps his instruments in a canvas roll.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Palven Orsel is a fellow of the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] and of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]]. He holds that the song of the [[place-sngngtrn|Singing Tarn]] is wind across the corrie lip, shaped by the water, and he means to have it on paper. He hires guards for the clerk and wax for their ears, and does not wear wax in his own until the last.
