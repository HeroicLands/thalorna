# Appearance {#appearance}

Shéimara Thólmarnde is a 44-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of light build. She is small and quick, with chestnut hair bound under a cloth and hazel eyes that never quite stop measuring. Burn scars mark the left wrist. A loupe hangs from a cord at her neck. Her apron is leather, and she pulls her sleeves above the elbow before she touches silver.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Shéimara Thólmarnde is a goldsmith of [[place-beravel|Péravel]], where the goldsmiths and gem-cutters have made an art of using almost no metal. Her workshop stands on the river bank below the University quarter, and she works a hair of silver round a stone until the stone seems to float in it. The University, the court and the families who value a setting more than its weight in metal all come to her door.

Everything the kingdom produces passes through Péravel, the little metal and gemstone that [[place-sorvanda|Sórvanda]] releases among it. The tribes release it by arrangement and in small quantities, so a goldsmith knows the grain of the Sórvanda silver at once. Silver of that grain is for sale in the capital in larger lots than the tribes have released, and she is asked to work it without a word about its source.

## Psyche

### Personality

Shéimara is sharp, witty and fastidious. She cleans her bench after every job and believes that a stone sets itself if the hand will let it. She is generous to apprentices and cold to patrons who haggle.

### Motivation

She wants to work the finest stones in the kingdom and never to be asked where a stone came from. She wants also to refuse a bar of silver she cannot account for, and has not found the price at which she will.

### Strengths

She has an eye that can read a stone's cutter and a bar's mine. Her setting is the best in Péravel, and the Treasury trusts her assay.

### Weaknesses

The University's commissions are most of her income, and the families who ask no questions are the others. She can name three clients who owe her a debt and none she could ask to stand for her.

## Social

### Companions

Two apprentices, a glassmaker next door and the copyist in the book quarter who reads her contracts.

### Patrons

The University's Fitter of Expeditions has twice ordered fittings from her, and the court pays her slowly.

### Enemies

A Treasury clerk who wants every bar assayed and entered. The bargemasters' fellowship, who will not carry her crates.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Shéimara is a Freeman of the crown's capital.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Silver without a source.** A client offers her a lot of silver bars with the grain of Sórvanda ore in larger weight than the tribes have released, and asks no price for her silence. She asks a party to find out where the silver came out of the ground before she decides whether to work it.
2. **The carter who asked no questions.** She has learned that silver from the spoil heaps of the King's Cut was bought from a Lísere carter who asked no questions, and the Wardens are asking after the carter. She asks a party to find him before the Wardens or the Order do.
3. **What came off the mount.** A Reader at the University brings her a cataloged object to clean and reset, and she recognizes the mount as one that came off an Araelwen. She wants a party to take the object back to the Keeper of the Collections in her name, and say she did not look.
