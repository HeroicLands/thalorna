# Appearance {#appearance}

Olthe Mórlineth is a 43-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of medium build. She is calm and solid, with graying brown hair tied in a scarf and brown eyes that do not look away from a body. Her fingers are stained from the salves she uses to keep the dead fit to look at.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Olthe Mórlineth is the crown's coroner of [[place-lisere|Lísere]]. She holds the crown's inquest on every sudden death and every wreck on the shore, from the Lirve border to [[place-durmelen|Durmelen]], and her finding goes to the sheriff at [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]]. She was a midwife before the sheriff named her, and the shore villages still send for her for both.

She wants every death on the shore explained. A sheriff's rider lies dead at the reed-gap of the Coast Road with a knotted cord in his hand and his purse untouched, and she has found no wound on him at all. She is the sister of [[being-theirnmrlnth|Théironand Mórlineth]], the Warden of Morthal.
