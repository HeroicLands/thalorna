# Appearance {#appearance}

Ostren Maldeth is a 43-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of light build. He is spare and unremarkable, with graying brown hair and gray eyes that rest on a face a moment too long. He wears plain gray wool and carries a purse and a short blade.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ostren Maldeth is an inquirer of the [[affiliation-sekran|Arcane Inquisition]] at [[place-beravel|Péravel]]. He offers purses for papers in cipher, follows what he buys and takes what he is not given. He serves as a stock inquirer wherever the Inquisition needs one.
