# Appearance {#appearance}

Norgatha is a 51-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. She is spare and straight, with long black hair braided with owl feathers and dark eyes that hold a stare. Her olive skin is weathered. She wears a gray wool dress, a fur-edged cloak in all weather and a knife of black stone, and she moves quietly after dusk.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Norgatha of the Thanandi is a Death-Speaker, a shaman whose rapport is with the spirits of the dead. She sits for her tribe at [[affiliation-mtslvrfrsts|the Moot of the Silver Forests]] and keeps her lodge at [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]], coming and going between it and the Owl Hollow in the lower reaches of the Drenavar Spine.

The Thanandi are the tribe of the lower Spine nearest the Hollow, where [[lore-ossendaspr|Óssenda, the White Owl]] is seated. Death-Speakers of several tribes travel there to ask the narrow and urgent question of whether the recently dead have gone. The Thanandi permit the asking and charge nothing, on the principle that the answer is Óssenda's to give. The asking is done at night, at the beck, and the answer is not always given at once.

## Psyche

### Personality

Norgatha is composed, dry and exact. She does not lower her voice for the dead. She gives a mourner a hard truth in a soft voice and treats a liar as one already dead.

### Motivation

She wants the dead settled and the Hollow kept free to every Speaker who comes. She wants the Thanandi's principle not to be bought.

### Strengths

She has the Hollow's trust and has waited three nights for an answer and gone home with none. She is respected by the Keepers and the Wardens alike.

### Weaknesses

She is tired. Speakers come to her in haste, and she has begun to refuse the ones she cannot reach before dawn.

## Social

### Companions

Two young Speakers she is training. The tribe's Warden of the Hollow walks her to the offering-stone.

### Patrons

None. The Hollow is the Thanandi's trust.

### Enemies

Outsiders who come to the Hollow on their own account and are turned back at the upper path, and who think the turning-back is a price.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-mtslvrfrsts|The Moot of the Silver Forests]]**: Norgatha is a Shaman of the Moot for the Thanandi.
- **[[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]]**: She is an Elder of the Thanandi.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Whether he has gone.** A woman of Péravel has come to the Lodge, wanting to know whether her son, a hired guard, has gone. The Wardens would turn her back, and Norgatha asks a party to take her to the Hollow, as outsiders have been taken when someone's dead were involved.
2. **Speakers in haste.** Death-Speakers from three tribes have arrived in one week, all asking the same question about the same dead. Norgatha asks a party to learn who they are asking about.
3. **Said in the dark.** An undertaking is to be made at the beck between two parties who trust no other way to bind each other, and Norgatha asks a party to be the witness the Hollow will not forget.
