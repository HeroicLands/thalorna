# Appearance {#appearance}

Élenor Thaurnde is a 45-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of light build. She is short, lean and straight, with chestnut hair in a close crop, amber eyes, a notched left ear and the stiff carriage of a career soldier. She wears the border militia's gray coat with a captain's cord and carries a plain sword she uses.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Élenor Thaurnde is a captain and the commander of [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Élavendre]]'s border militia, a professional soldier with her garrison at [[place-valdun|Valdun]]. She respects the competence of [[being-belrktrvnl|Melrik Tárvenel]] and has explicitly protected him from reassignment despite his social awkwardness.

Valdun is what a border town looks like when the border has never been quiet. The wall encloses the market as well as the houses, the stock pens are inside it and the gates are shut at dusk as a matter of routine. The hill levy musters there, and the militia and the levy share the walls without sharing a plan. Élenor holds the crown's commission and the levy holds the ground.

## Psyche

### Personality

Élenor is exact, dry and loyal to the man below her. She writes her orders in short sentences, reads reports twice and never raises her voice.

### Motivation

She wants the border held by soldiers who can be relied on and the levy brought into one plan. She wants her best men kept where they are good.

### Strengths

She is a first-rate organizer and a hard fighter. She has the confidence of the crown's chancery.

### Weaknesses

Her orders arrive late and her men are few. The levy follows Jeldor Mórlineth, not her, and she cannot say so.

## Social

### Companions

A lieutenant, a sergeant-major and the militia's twenty troopers.

### Patrons

The queen's chancery, which named her.

### Enemies

Vargen Hálmesende, who would like her post for a friend. The levy's officers, who do not like to be told.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Élenor holds the office of Captain of the Border Militia at the rung of Knight.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The reassignment order.** The chancery has ordered Melrik Tárvenel moved to a post at Péravel. Élenor has not read it, she says, and asks a party to carry her reply.
2. **One plan.** A Varokh war-party is reported two days east, and Élenor wants the levy and the militia in one line. She asks a party to carry her plan to Jeldor and bring back what he says.
3. **The helmet.** A hill hunter has brought back an imperial helmet that has not rusted, and the Order's antiquarians want to know where it was found. Élenor wants it in the garrison's strongroom and asks a party to bring the hunter to her before the antiquarians reach him.
