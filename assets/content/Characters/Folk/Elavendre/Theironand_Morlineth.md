# Appearance {#appearance}

Théironand Mórlineth is a 48-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. He is weathered and spare, with brown hair cut short for a helmet and gray eyes narrowed against the salt wind of the causeway. He rides a hill pony too small for a man of his rank and does not care. The horn of the Watch hangs across his chest on a strap worn smooth by thirty years of the road.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Théironand Mórlineth is the Warden of Morthal, the Captain whom the crown names to command [[affiliation-morthalwtch|the Morthal Watch]] on the bay road through [[place-morthalmrch|the Morthal March]]. He keeps the Watch's rolls in a house at [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]], at the edge of the March's country, judges its quarrels, and speaks for it to the crown through the Captain of [[affiliation-brdrmltia|the Border Militia]]. He is a cousin of the hill Mórlineths of [[place-valdun|Valdun]] and a knight of the crown.

The Watch is stretched along a road that is longer than its roll. He asked the crown for more Riders at the last council and was given a promise, and in the meantime he has pulled Watchmen off the hill posts to keep the causeway manned. His Riders chase Varokh and Grukar raiders up the ridges and stop at the tree line with the raiders in sight, because the crown's oath stops there, and he has twice written to the crown's Liaison to the Tribes, [[being-chenirahares|Chénira Háresende]], asking what the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] do beyond it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Théironand is plain, dry and patient. He counts before he speaks and says the count first.

### Motivation

He wants the road held with the men he has until the crown sends the men it promised. He wants the heights closed behind a raid and has been told that they already are.

### Strengths

He knows every post, ford and causeway log of the March, and every steading feeds his Riders.

### Weaknesses

He trusts the tally more than the men who keep it, and the Tally-Serjeant's count of the autumn furs does not agree with the count at Sarmelis.

## Social

### Companions

The Post-Serjeants who hold the towers, and the Tally-Serjeant on the causeway.

### Patrons

The crown, which names him, and the Captain of the Border Militia, through whom his orders come.

### Enemies

The war-parties that come round the bay on foot, and the Order agents who come to the March because nobody the crown pays watches the hills above it.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-morthalwtch|The Morthal Watch]]**: Théironand is a Captain and holds the office of Warden of Morthal.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: He is a Knight of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The fourth party.** Three Order parties have been turned back at the tree line this season and a fourth has not been found. Théironand wants a party that is not the crown's to learn whether it went up past the line.
2. **The tally.** The Watch's count of the furs that crossed in autumn does not agree with the count at Sarmelis, and he wants to know which of them is wrong before the sheriff does.
3. **The Rider past the line.** A Rider of the Watch followed a raider past the tree line two nights ago and has not come back, and Théironand cannot send another after him.
