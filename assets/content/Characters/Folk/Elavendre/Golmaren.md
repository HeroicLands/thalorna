# Appearance {#appearance}

Gólmaren is a 43-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of medium build. He is tall and rangy, with black hair going gray in a warrior's knot and a weathered face that gives away only his temper. His gray eyes are narrow from long watching. He wears oiled leathers and a Warden's cord of knotted gray wool, and he carries a spear, a bow and a dog's whistle.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Gólmaren of the Kárandi is an Elder Warden, among the most experienced Wardens of his territory, on whom its leadership falls by the recognition of his peers and not by appointment. The Wardens have no headquarters. The senior Wardens meet in the Wardens' Yard at [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]], a walled yard of beaten earth with kennels along one wall and a long hall on the other, to share what has been seen at the margins and to train the next generation: spear, dog, song and fire.

He counts the places he cannot cover. An Order party camps below [[place-hldhr|the Held Hour]], another waits above [[place-sngngtrn|the Singing Tarn]], a survey on [[place-wndrngrdg|the Wandering Ridge]] has lost a guard, and the watch at [[place-fndr|the Fen Door]] is down to one. A standing birch was cut at [[place-fldstnd|the Felled Stand]] three nights ago, and the Wardens of the Lodge have doubled their dawn walk.

## Psyche

### Personality

Gólmaren is terse, unsparing and fair. He warns once and means it. He trains the young by walking them through the winter's account until they can recite it.

### Motivation

He wants every Araelwen kept, every watch two deep and the Order at the tree line. He wants the cutter of the birch found.

### Strengths

He knows the Spine's patrols and the Wardens' habits. The young Wardens would follow him into the Fen.

### Weaknesses

There are not enough Wardens, and he will not ask the crown for help. His quick temper has cost him a friend at the council.

## Social

### Companions

Kéldira of the Dhárgandi serves under him at the Fen Door. His dog, a gray bitch, follows him.

### Patrons

The peers who recognized him and the Tradition Keepers whose songs the Wardens sing.

### Enemies

The Order and its hired guards, whom he warns once and does not distinguish.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]]**: Gólmaren is an Elder Warden of the Spine.
- **[[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]]**: He is a Householder of the Kárandi.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The warning carried.** A Warden of the Spine asks a party to carry a warning to the Order's hired guards below the Held Hour, plainly, so that no guard can say he was not told.
2. **Who cut the birch.** The Wardens of the Lodge want to know who cut the standing birch at the Felled Stand, and they want it asked outside the Lodge. Gólmaren asks a party to ask at Sóurimel and at Elanmere.
3. **Word for the Door.** The lone Warden at the Fen Door needs a second, and no Warden can come down before the passes open. Gólmaren cannot send one, and asks a party to carry his word, and a dog, to Kéldira.
