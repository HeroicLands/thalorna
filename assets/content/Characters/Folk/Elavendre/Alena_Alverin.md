# Appearance {#appearance}

Alena Alverin is a 21-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of light build. She is small and hard, with brown hair hacked short and brown eyes. She wears a guide's leather jerkin over a charcoal-burner's smock, and the charcoal is ground into her knuckles for good.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Alena Alverin is a charcoal-burner's daughter of [[place-sourimael|Sóurimel]], the one Élavendri village inside [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]], who guided lowland strangers along the margin of the wood for coin. She left with the Order fellow [[being-denrikrnkrs|Denrik Rénkaris]] and his party and did not come back. Her mother keeps her hearth lit, and tells anyone who stays long enough that a lamp was seen walking in a straight line through the trees on a night when no lamp could.

## Psyche

### Personality

Alena is practical, angry and honest.

### Motivation

She guides for coin, and she brings the people she guides home.

### Strengths

She grew up in the wood and knows the birch law as a village girl knows it.

### Weaknesses

She takes coin for work her good sense tells her to refuse.

## Social

### Companions

Her mother, [[being-selithalvrn|Selith Alverin]], and the charcoal-burners of Sóurimel.

### Patrons

Nobody, now. The Order's silver bought her once.

### Enemies

Her own good sense, which told her not to go.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Alena is a Freeman of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The charcoal-burner's daughter.** Alena's mother asks a party to find her in the Silver Forests, where the Wardens will not escort a search ([[scenario-pggdmnbrchrng|Eight Birches Wound in Silver]]).

:::secret {title="The cold hearth"}
Alena was paid in silver to take Denrik Rénkaris's party to a hollow she knew to avoid, and she took them. She says so, and she does not forgive herself or ask anyone else to.

She sits now by a cold hearth inside [[place-rngtrndpths|the Ring of Turned Paths]]. The working takes one walker each dusk, the one furthest from its footing, and the wood knows hers, so she keeps her mind. Her eyes are clear for an hour at each dusk and blank the rest of the day. She knows the party by name, how many walked in and how many are left, and that she is the next. She will not leave until the circuit is stopped for the others as well.

She wants the walkers freed, the silver out of the ground and the _marendos_ made to do the one thing he would not do. She held the lamp when he drove his own pegs, and she knows which ones they were. The wood has kept her mind, and it may not keep it another night. She has told the _marendos_ what she thinks of him every dusk for a season.

**Hook: what the song says.** Alena tells a party what the _marendos_'s journal leaves out: that the first Dhargan did not command the wood, and that he sat four days with his hands bare.
:::
