# Appearance {#appearance}

Rukkola Elvìrinte is a 4,130-year-old woman who stands 6'3" tall and is of light build. Her hair is white and worn loose, and her gray eyes are the gray of the sea under cloud. She dresses in undyed gray, as the Senali of Serenthale do, and goes barefoot on every ground. She is very still, and when she turns her head the whole of her attention turns with it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Rukkola Elvìrinte is the Lord of [[affiliation-nclvsrnthl|the Enclave of Serenthale]], the sea-facing enclave in the old forest of [[place-lirve|Lirve]] and the most withdrawn of [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]]'s three. The [[lore-senaliclt|Senali]] raise a lord for things done and by every voice, and Serenthale raised her. No human record names her.

Serenthale's contact with the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] is real, respectful and rare, and it happens at the enclave's initiative and never at anyone else's. The initiative is hers. When the enclave speaks, she has decided that it will, and when it says nothing, as it says nothing to the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] expeditions that come to Lirve looking for it, she has decided that too. What Serenthale has to say to a human power, on the few occasions it has anything, goes down to [[affiliation-nclvthlss|the Enclave of Ethalosse]] and out through the Áelendan who carry.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is patient past any human measure and courteous without warmth to strangers. She listens to the whole of a thing before she answers it, and her answers are short.

### Motivation

She wants Serenthale left unfound. The Order is looking for it again this year, with hired guards and a guide out of [[place-liranel|Liranel]], and she wants the search to end where every search has ended, in the wood, without anyone hurt by it.

### Strengths

The enclave trusts her judgment of when to speak, and the paths of the sea wood answer her.

### Weaknesses

She holds that a silence kept long enough settles any question. The Senali do not fight, and a search that does not give up is a question her silence does not settle.

## Social

### Companions

[[being-seittavonwomi|Seittavo Nwominto]], who goes out when the enclave speaks, and the lords and elders of Serenthale.

### Enemies

None by name. The Order's expeditions are a trouble she wants to outlast.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvsrnthl|Enclave of Serenthale]]**: Rukkola is a Lord, and holds the office of Lord of the Enclave.

:::secret {title="What Serenthale raised her for"}
Rukkola was raised for the closing of Serenthale. When the first human settlers came to Lirve's coast, she held that the enclave should keep to itself even from those who came to keep the wood, and every voice agreed. The paths of the sea wood have led away from Serenthale since, the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] included, and the Wardens do not know where the enclave is because she chose that they should not. Serenthale keeps its concern with the sea, and she keeps the reason for it.
:::
