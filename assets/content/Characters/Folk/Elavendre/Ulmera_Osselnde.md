# Appearance {#appearance}

Úlmera Ósselnde is a 56-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. She is tall and broad-shouldered, with blond hair in a long braid and a freckled face, its brow cracked by wind, that rarely changes. Her green eyes are steady. She wears a chieftain's torc of braided leather, a hill-wool cloak and the plain boots of a woman who walks.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Úlmera Ósselnde is the chieftain of a tribe of the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]], leading it by descent and by consent together. She and [[being-rnthrprdln|Ranthor Párdalen]] have worked together for two decades, and she relies on him for counsel on matters both spiritual and martial. She sits at the great council at [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]], where the elders meet to decide what touches more than one tribe.

The great council meets at the turn of the season, and the senior Wardens have come in early. They count the places they cannot cover: an Order party camped below the Held Hour, another above the Singing Tarn, a survey on the Wandering Ridge that has lost a guard, a lone Warden at the Fen Door. A silver casket sent up for the council is overdue, and its envoy with it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Úlmera is stern, fair and slow to speak. She hears every elder before she says anything, and says it once. She has the habit of the long silence.

### Motivation

She wants her tribe unbroken and the Pact kept. She wants the Order held at the margins and the crown's courtesy not mistaken for protection.

### Strengths

Her people follow her. The Wardens and the Keepers trust her judgment, and she has Ranthor's counsel on the crown's affairs.

### Weaknesses

She leans on one man's advice and he is not of the tribe by birth. The council has not agreed on how to answer the Order, and she will be judged by what it does.

## Social

### Companions

The tribe's elders and the Lawkeeper. A Warden of her own clan runs her messages.

### Patrons

None. A chieftain holds by descent and consent.

### Enemies

The Order's parties and the lowland lord who would have the tribal ground opened.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]]**: Úlmera holds the office of Chieftain at the rung of Chieftain.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The overdue casket.** An envoy bearing a sealed casket for the great council is missing on the road, and the Lodge would welcome it found ([[scenario-lodgesilvbgh|The Lodge beneath the Silver Boughs]]). Úlmera has not asked for help, and has let it be known that a hand offered in the right way would not be refused.
2. **An invitation.** A scholar of high reputation, or a party that has earned Áelendan trust, is invited up as a guest, and the invitation says nothing of the terms. Úlmera asks a party to carry it.
3. **The word from the Door.** The lone Warden at the Fen Door has sent a message by the beacon families of Lísere, and nobody can carry it up to the Lodge before the passes open. Úlmera asks a party to learn what it says.
