# Appearance {#appearance}

Saikila Mollunto is a 1,323-year-old woman who stands 6'1" tall and is of light build. Her black hair is worn in a crown of braids, and her eyes are violet. She dresses in gray, and in a white mantle on the nights she sings.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Saikila Mollunto is a lord of [[affiliation-nclvlrss|the Enclave of Aelirosse]]. [[lore-ellevirsa|Guest-Right]], the origin song cut at Sirmenne, is sung at Aelirosse over a guest on the first night the guest sleeps there, and it is Saikila who sings it. She was raised for the night she sang it over a whole Áelendan household that came into the wood in a killing winter and slept a season in the enclave.

The song carries the custom its title names: the welcome a guest owes to whoever comes after. In Aelirosse the guests are nearly always Áelendan, and the singing is the enclave's word that they are welcome.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is serene, observant and slow to welcome. Once she has sung over a guest, she treats the guest as kin.

### Motivation

She wants guest-right kept as it was given, and she wants no guest of Aelirosse ever to go hungry or cold while in the wood.

### Strengths

She sings the whole of Guest-Right from memory, the broken places included, and she knows every household of the forest's edge that has slept in the enclave.

### Weaknesses

A guest she has sung over can ask a great deal of her, and she gives it.
