# Appearance {#appearance}

Lauppivo Solmerinto is a 2,258-year-old man who stands 6'5" tall and is of light build. His fair hair is long and braided with birch catkins in spring, and his blue eyes are quick to smile. He wears gray, and an Áelendan cloak-pin of carved horn given to him by a chief of the forest's edge.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lauppivo Solmerinto is a lord of [[affiliation-nclvlrss|the Enclave of Aelirosse]]. He was raised for the songs he gave the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]]. The songs [[affiliation-bardicolgs|the Bardic Colleges]] treasure as Senalian gifts were, most of them, first heard at Aelirosse and carried out by Áelendan who had been given them freely, and a good many were given by him. [[lore-seventowers|The Seven Towers]] is not among them.

The Senali of Aelirosse attend some Áelendan observances and are attended at some of their own. Lauppivo goes to more of them than any other Senalo. The Áelendan households along the forest's edge know him by sight and greet him by his whole name.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is open, generous and fond of talk, and he listens as well as he sings.

### Motivation

He wants the long neighborliness between Aelirosse and the Áelendan kept as it is: cordial, unhurried and not an alliance.

### Strengths

He is trusted on both sides of the wood's edge, and he hears what the households of the forest are saying before the Moot does.

### Weaknesses

He is fonder of the Áelendan than the enclave is, and he knows it.

## Social

### Companions

The Áelendan households of the forest's edge, and the Tradition Keepers who hold his songs.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A song at the observance.** A party guesting with an Áelendan household at the edge of the Silver Forests is present when a tall Senalo comes to the observance, sings, and goes back into the trees. A bard among the guests wants the song.
2. **The neighbors' word.** The Wardens want to know whether Aelirosse has heard who cut the bark from the Waking Stand. Lauppivo has heard what the households say, and the Wardens ask a party to sit with him at the next observance.
