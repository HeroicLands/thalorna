# Appearance {#appearance}

Moren Othral is a 51-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He is gray at the temples and stooped from nights on the tower stair, and his face is weathered the color of the fort's beach stone. His hands are square and scarred from rope. He wears the garrison's coat unbuttoned at the throat and keeps the muster roll folded in his belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Moren Othral is a knight of fifty-odd winters and the Castellan of [[place-durmelen|Durmelen]], the crown's fort at the narrows of [[place-liserebay|Lísere Bay]]. His father held the fort before him. He answers to the crown for its wall, its stores, its four oared cutters and the Great Fire on the tower, and he judges the [[affiliation-durmelengrsn|garrison]] himself. A case between a soldier and a townsman of Sarmelis goes to the sheriff.

He keeps the crown's oath against the Nordmen to the letter: the fort's gate has never opened to a Nordman who came under a flag. Toward the shore's other raiders he keeps the measure of what keeps his men alive. He knows the Varokh furs reach Sarmelis with Kalipsan marks and has chosen not to ask, on the principle that a Varokh who trades is a Varokh who is not landing. The fort is thin this winter, and a Varokh boat under a green branch has lain on the beach in the lee of the point for days.

## Psyche

### Personality

Moren is careful, tired and a stickler for the muster roll. He says little in council, writes everything down at night and does not argue a decision once it is made. He is kind to the levymen in small ways and would be embarrassed to hear it said.

### Motivation

He wants the Great Fire lit every night of the year by men who can be trusted to stand it, and the fort handed to his successor with the roll in order. He has asked Péravel for sixty men and wants an answer that is not "under consideration."

### Strengths

He knows the narrows, the signals and every village head on the shore. The levy comes up for him because his father asked and they came for him.

### Weaknesses

His garrison is short, his best recruits are on the quay at Sarmelis, and he has not decided whether a fort that has refused every Nordman may hear a Varokh who comes unarmed. He puts off decisions that have no precedent.

## Social

### Companions

The Muster-Serjeant keeps the levy rolls and the Keeper of the Great Fire keeps the lamp-hands. The chandler and the smith are his oldest neighbors.

### Patrons

The crown, which named him and takes its time to answer.

### Enemies

The Order's gold, which is stripping the shore of young men. Salten Elarven at Sarmelis, who wants the same levymen back from the same wages.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Moren holds the office of Castellan at the rung of Knight.
- **[[affiliation-durmelengrsn|Garrison of Durmelen]]**: He is its Castellan and a Captain of the garrison.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The green branch.** The Castellan lets the Varokh woman speak, and what she says is a warning of a muster of boats across the bay. He asks a party to carry the message to the sheriff and to find out whether it is true before Péravel is told.
2. **A thin watch.** The Great Fire is short two lamp-hands, and one night in five a single man stands it. Moren will hire a party to stand the beacon watch for a month if they can be trusted with the iron basket and the signals.
3. **The oath.** A Nordman sailor has washed up on the point alive and asks for the Castellan's hospitality. The garrison is divided on what the oath requires, and Moren asks a party to sit with the sailor and learn what ship he came off before the garrison decides.
