# Appearance {#appearance}

Pelra Dareth is a 26-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. She is tall and laughing, with black hair cut short and brown eyes that follow Ilvane. She wears University green and a leather satchel of notes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Pelra Dareth is a fellow of the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] and a friend of [[being-ielaraskir|Prince Yélaras]]. She rides with the prince's company to the margin of the Silver Forests, in the hope that his presence will carry her party past the Wardens.
