# Appearance {#appearance}

Norven Halren is a 43-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of heavy build. He is thick-shouldered and unshaven, with dark brown hair matted with sweat and brown eyes red-rimmed from two nights without sleep. He wears a poacher's patched leathers and a cloth pressed to his side, and he holds a boar-spear across his knees whose head is bound on with fresh sinew.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Norven Halren is a poacher of [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]], one of the band under [[being-vendrikothrl|Vendrik Othral]] that hunts the deer of [[place-hntrsrng|the Hunter's Ring]] from the old keepers' lodge below it. He has barred himself into that lodge with an arrow wound in his side, and smoke has been seen from it for two days. A Warden's gray-green cloak hangs by his hearth, and he will not look at it.

Anyone at the door gets his story: that his friend [[being-torlanslrth|Torlan Salreth]] was taken from the fire and walked back to the lodge door the next dusk in his own cloak, calling Norven by name, and that the Warden [[being-astherenwrdn|Ástheren]] came to the hearth at dusk already dead, sat there and went out again leaving his cloak. He wants to live until the paths unfold.

## Psyche

### Personality

Norven is coarse, frightened and quick to strike. He talks too much when he is afraid, and he is more afraid than he has ever been.

### Motivation

He wants out of the ring alive.

### Strengths

He knows the ring's lower ground and where its stones lie, and he is a strong man with a spear.

### Weaknesses

He is fevered and has not slept.

## Social

### Companions

The band under Vendrik Othral.

### Patrons

Vendrik Othral, who leads the band.

### Enemies

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **The barred door.** A Warden asks a party to help her into the lodge at the Hunter's Ring, where a poacher has barred himself in with a story she does not like ([[scenario-lodgesilvbgh|The Lodge beneath the Silver Boughs]]).
2. **The man from the wood.** A wounded poacher comes down to Elanmere from the Silver Forests begging to be taken to the crown's courts, any court, before the Wardens find him.

:::secret {title="The robbery"}
The band robbed the envoy [[being-devrikveldr|Devrik Véldarin]] on the road below the Hunter's Ring and fled uphill through the ring with the envoy's casket, prying the silver bands off the boundary stones as it went. Vendrik planned the robbery and promised Norven a share of the casket. The Warden Ástheren put an arrow into Norven's side in the fight. When the band split, Norven held the old keepers' lodge with the sack of pried silver, and he knows where the band went in. Most of the band is taken now.

Everything Norven says about Torlan, the stones, the silver and the lost men is true. His story has one invented part, and the cloak hangs by his own hearth. He wants nobody ever to know what happened at the lodge door.
:::

:::secret {title="The lodge door"}
Ástheren came to the lodge door alive, at dusk, to bring the poachers to account and take back the casket. Norven held the spear on him, let him in on his word, and put the spear through his back when he turned to bar the door. The spear-head broke off in the wound. Norven dragged the body out, the cloak came away in his hands, and the hunter took what lay alone outside. He sanded the floor and bound a spare head onto the shaft. The hunter calls the Warden's last words from the trees every dusk ([[place-hntrsrng#truth|the truth of the Hunter's Ring]]).

The Wardens' law has a verse for a man who sheds Warden blood on Warden ground.
:::
