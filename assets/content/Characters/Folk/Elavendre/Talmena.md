# Appearance {#appearance}

Tálmena is a 58-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of light build. She is small and weathered, with dark hair streaked with gray worn in two braids, and gray eyes that rest on whoever is speaking until that person has finished. She carries a staff of silver birch deadwood, gathered fallen, as the birch law requires.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tálmena of the Tírindi is a shaman of the tribe of the outer margin of [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]], and the Speaker of [[affiliation-mtslvrfrsts|the Moot of the Silver Forests]], named at the Moot's first sitting of the year. She keeps the order in which the shamen of the Dhárgandi, the Tírindi and the Thanandi speak at [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]] after each great seasonal rite, and she speaks for none of them.

The Moot is divided over the Senali. Someone cut a strip of bark from [[place-wkngstnd|the Waking Stand]], and it lies green in a chapterhouse collection at Péravel. One part of the Moot holds that its Carrier must go to [[place-ethalosse|Ethalosse]] now and say what has been done, because the wood is shared and its neighbors should hear it from the people who keep it. The other part holds that nothing should be carried until the man is found. Tálmena keeps the order of speaking and the peace, and the decision is not hers.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tálmena is quiet, patient and immovable. She does not interrupt, and nobody interrupts while she is Speaker.

### Motivation

She wants the Moot to reach one mind before it carries anything, and she wants the man who cut the bark found.

### Strengths

Every shaman of the three tribes trusts her to keep the order fairly, because she has never once used it.

### Weaknesses

She will not speak her own mind while she holds the order, and the Moot has never needed it more.

## Social

### Companions

[[being-norgathathan|Norgatha]], the Death-Speaker of the Thanandi, with whom she has sat at the Moot for twenty years.

### Patrons

The shamen who named her, and the elders of the Tírindi.

### Enemies

Whoever cut the bark.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-mtslvrfrsts|The Moot of the Silver Forests]]**: Tálmena is a Shaman of the Moot and holds the office of Speaker of the Moot.
- **[[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]]**: She is an Elder of the Tírindi.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The man who cut the bark.** Tálmena asks a party that is not Áelendan to go down to Péravel and learn who brought the bark into the chapterhouse, because no Warden can go.
2. **The Carrier.** If the Moot decides to carry, its Carrier needs companions on the way up to Ethalosse, and Tálmena chooses them.
3. **A Hearer's errand.** She sends a Hearer down to a party's camp with a single instruction and no explanation: leave the birch-wood where it lies.
