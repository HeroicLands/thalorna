# Appearance {#appearance}

Hálmesar Thólmarnde is a 38-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of light build. He has dark brown hair going thin at the crown, brown eyes and the weathered face of a man who spends his summers out of doors. He wears a satchel across his chest and does not set it down, even to eat.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hálmesar Thólmarnde is a Fellow of the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] who reads the history of the Sórvanda workings: the King's Cut, its adits and the reigns that opened and closed them. He tutored [[being-ielaraskir|Prince Yélaras]] in history for four years, and the prince still writes to him.

This summer he has gone inward from [[place-liranel|Liranel]] without the crown's guides and without a Warden's leave, meaning to stand at the head of [[place-kngscut|the King's Cut]] and match the old workings to the records. He told his court he was going to Liranel to read in the College of Tongues' library, and he did go there first.

:::secret
He carries a copy of the royal survey that [Hóuradel]{.name data-type="being/npc"}'s engineers made of the Cut, adit by adit. Yélaras had it copied for him from the royal archive as a matter of history, and the queen does not know. If the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] take it from him and read it, they know at once where it came from, and the Áelendan judge the prince by it.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Hálmesar is patient, precise and impossible to hurry. He takes a fact as settled only when he has stood where it happened.

### Motivation

He wants the history of the Cut written whole, from the records and the ground together.

### Strengths

He knows the archive's account of the workings better than anyone living, and he walks well.

### Weaknesses

He does not believe that a place he has a reason to visit can be closed to him.

## Social

### Companions

None on the road. He hired a mule in Liranel and no guide.

### Patrons

[[being-ielaraskir|Prince Yélaras]], his former pupil.

### Enemies

None he knows of. The Wardens of the Silver Forests have not yet met him.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]]**: He is a Fellow of a court.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: He is Gentry of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Bring him back.** Yélaras wants his tutor brought back before the Wardens find him, and says why only when he trusts the party.
2. **The satchel.** Whoever reaches him first wants what he carries: the Wardens, a party sent by the prince, or a buyer who has heard there is a copy of the Cut's survey above the tree line.
3. **The grave.** Hálmesar means to go down the deepest adit of the Cut. He does not know what is at the bottom of it.
