# Appearance {#appearance}

Dalren Mórlineth is a 45-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. He is pale and restless, with black hair cut ragged and brown eyes that move from door to door. His weathered face carries a faint scar over the left eyebrow. He wears a heavy coat in warm weather. A row of pins is fixed along his collar, and he fingers them when someone approaches.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Dalren Mórlineth is a nobleman whom [[being-idriskrsvr|Ídrise Kórisvar]] has guarded before. He seems paranoid and frightened, and is convinced that a hired assassin is hunting him. He has taken rooms at [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]] ahead of the autumn fair, where the lake shore fills with tribal families and Péravel factors and a stranger is not remarked.

Elanmere is where the high country and the low do business, and the fair is the event of the year. A man who wants to disappear among the fair's crowds could not choose a better place, and a man who wants to be killed in one could not choose a worse.

## Psyche

### Personality

Dalren is nervous, courteous and exact. He locks every door twice and sleeps in a different room each night. He says thank you to servants with an intensity that frightens them.

### Motivation

He wants to live through the fair and to learn who wants him dead. He wants a guard he trusts and has not been able to find one.

### Strengths

He has money and a memory for faces. He notes everything.

### Weaknesses

His fear isolates him, and no one who watches him can tell the fear from the cause. He cannot name a reason for his fear and cannot name a person.

## Social

### Companions

A valet who has been with him twenty years.

### Patrons

His house, which has not answered his letters.

### Enemies

Whoever is hunting him, if anyone is.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Dalren is a Noble, of the house of Mórlineth.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A guard for the fair.** Dalren asks a party to guard him through the fair's fortnight. Ídrise Kórisvar, whom he would have asked, will not come.
2. **The note under the door.** A folded note was pushed under his door in the night. He will not read it, and asks a party to open it and tell him whether to run.
3. **Who buys a name.** He believes someone has bought his name from a broker of such things. He asks a party to learn the broker's name and the price.
