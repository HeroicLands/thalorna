# Appearance {#appearance}

Hálvilon Sérdalnde is a 53-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. He is long-faced and composed, with graying brown hair worn short and gray eyes that rest on a document before they rest on its bearer. He wears the chancery's dark blue, a University graduate's hood folded on the shoulder and the small seal of the chancery on a ribbon at his wrist.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hálvilon Sérdalnde is the Chancellor of the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]. The chancery he heads keeps the crown's writs and the charters of its towns, its pilots and its [[affiliation-univperavel|University]], and it issues the leaves under which the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]]'s scholars survey the kingdom. He is a noble of a basin house whose descent the Colleges have proved, and a graduate of the University, as most of his clerks are.

He believes the crown governs by paper, and that a thing written down and sealed is a thing the crown can answer for. A leave to survey names a district and a season, and the chancery seals it when the forms are right. The chancery does not ask where a survey goes once it has passed the crown's guides at [[place-liranel|Liranel]], and the Chancellor has never thought that the chancery's business.

This year a leave sealed in his household names the outer margin of [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]], and the Order fellow who carried it has not come out ([[scenario-pggdmnbrchrng|Eight Birches Wound in Silver]]).

## Psyche

### Personality

Hálvilon is courteous, exact and unhurried. He answers a question with the precedent for it, and he is uneasy with a question that has none.

### Motivation

He wants the crown's writ to run whole in the cities and the shires, its charters kept and its seal trusted. He wants the chancery's forms to be the one thing in the kingdom nobody quarrels with.

### Strengths

He knows the charters of every town by heart. The Queen trusts his paper, and every magistrate in the kingdom was taught by men he studied with.

### Weaknesses

He trusts his clerks as he trusts his forms. A leave can pass under his household's seal that he never read.

## Social

### Companions

The clerks of the writs, University men every one, and the Rector, [[being-thorenveldar|Thoren Véldarin]], who dines with him on the first day of each month.

### Patrons

[[being-aurenthekir|Queen Áurenthe]], who named him.

### Enemies

The Grove Council's petitioners, who hold that a leave to survey is a leave to steal. The Treasurer, [[being-nsvrskelvas|Ánsevirsa Kélvasende]], who wants the fees the chancery takes for its seals paid into the treasury.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Hálvilon holds the office of Chancellor, as a Noble.
- **[[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]]**: He holds the rung of Reader, which he took as a graduate.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The charter of Port Aelion.** The town council of Port Aelion claims a clause in its charter that the chancery's copy does not carry. The Chancellor wants the town's own copy read by someone the council will let into its chest.

:::secret {title="The Clerk of the Leaves"}
The Clerk of the Leaves, who drafts every leave to survey, is paid by the Order's Fitter of Expeditions for a list of the leaves before they are sealed, and for a district written wide where the forms allow it. The Silver Forests leave was written wide. Hálvilon sealed none of it himself.

**Hook: the leave.** A letter of leave to survey the Silver Forests, sealed in the Chancellor's household, has come out of the wood in an Order fellow's chest. Whoever brings it to Hálvilon learns that he did not know of it, and that he wants the clerk who sealed it found before the Grove Council does.

**Hook: the list.** A copy of the Clerk's list, in his hand, is for sale in the book quarter.
:::
