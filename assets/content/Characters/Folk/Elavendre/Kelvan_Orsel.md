# Appearance {#appearance}

Kelvan Orsel is a 31-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of light build. He is quiet and watchful, with brown hair worn long and gray eyes that rest on the baggage. He rides at the back of the company with a case of moss and packing-straw.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kelvan Orsel is a fellow of the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] who holds a crown license to bring a living cutting out of the Silver Forests. The license is real and gives him no leave from anyone who keeps the stand. He has hired guards of his own and is sometimes alone with them.
