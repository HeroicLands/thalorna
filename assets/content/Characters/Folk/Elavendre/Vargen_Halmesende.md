# Appearance {#appearance}

Vargen Hálmesende is a 44-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of light build. He is tall and handsome in a hard way, with brown hair swept back from a high forehead, a long white scar along the left jaw and brown eyes that do not warm. He dresses in black and green with a single black stone ring. He smiles seldom and entirely.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Vargen Hálmesende is an ambitious lord of [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Élavendre]]'s eastern march, with a seat at [[place-valdun|Valdun]], rumored to have ties to dark magic and plainly given to military ambition. He treats the peace-brokering of [[being-rnthrprdln|Ranthor Párdalen]] as an obstacle to his plans for territorial expansion, and has twice tried to have him discredited or eliminated.

:::secret {title="The truth of Vargen's magic"}
The rumor is true. Vargen's ties to dark magic are real.
:::

Valdun is where the march's armies are made and its opinions formed. The hill levy muster there, the Border Militia keeps its garrison, and the Order's antiquarians come asking after the Tribute Road and the lost cohort's camp. A lord with a taste for the frontier and a grudge against the peace finds friends in all of them.

## Psyche

### Personality

Vargen is cold, charming and patient. He asks after your family and notes the names. He never lies when the truth would serve and always arranges for it to serve.

### Motivation

He wants the march extended, the tribes' ground opened to the crown's roads and his name sung beside the First Crowning's. He has already chosen the tribe he wants to start with.

### Strengths

He has the court's ear and a seat at the council. He commands a retinue of professionals and the respect of men who want to fight.

### Weaknesses

He needs the queen's writ and has not got it. His rumored ties to dark things are an asset until a Warden's runner finds a footprint.

## Social

### Companions

A retinue of twenty paid men and a steward who knows what the lord hides.

### Patrons

Rumored friends at the court of Péravel.

### Enemies

Ranthor Párdalen, twice. Élenor Thaurnde, who sees through him.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Vargen is a Noble, holding a seat on the eastern march.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The muster beyond the march.** Vargen has offered the hill levy pay to muster beyond the march. Jeldor Mórlineth refuses, the levy is divided, and a party is asked by each side to learn what he is really after.
2. **The second attempt.** Ranthor Párdalen has survived a second attempt to discredit him, and a witness to it has come to Valdun. A party is asked by Ranthor's friends to keep her alive.
3. **The stone ring.** A hunter has found a black stone like Vargen's ring in the lost cohort's camp. The Order's antiquarians want it, and so does the lord.
