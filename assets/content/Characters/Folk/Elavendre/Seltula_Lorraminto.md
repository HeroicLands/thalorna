# Appearance {#appearance}

Seltula Lorraminto is a 607-year-old woman who stands 5'11" tall and is of light build. She is young by Senali measure, with fair hair cut short so that it stays out of the warp and eyes of a deep blue. Her fingertips are hard from the loom.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Seltula Lorraminto is a weaver of [[affiliation-nclvsrnthl|the Enclave of Serenthale]], of the hearth of Lorraminto, the hearth of woven cloth, which keeps the enclave's looms. The undyed gray wool every Senalo of Serenthale wears comes off them. She weaves to song, and the cloth she makes while [[being-silkelehalle|Sìlkele Hallesinto]] sings is held the finest.

She has never been out of Lirve's deep forest, and has never seen a human closer than the edge of the wood.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is cheerful, quick and curious, by Serenthale's standard restless.

### Motivation

She wants to see the sea from the cliffs where [[being-ilkaivetkelk|Ilkaivetti Kelkasinto]] keeps his watch, and to weave what she sees there.

### Strengths

Her cloth is tight, warm and light, and it does not catch on thorn.

### Weaknesses

Her curiosity about the world outside the wood is a thing the enclave notices.

## Social

### Companions

Sìlkele Hallesinto, and the weavers of her hearth.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvsrnthl|Enclave of Serenthale]]**: Seltula is a Member of the Enclave.
