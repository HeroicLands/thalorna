# Appearance {#appearance}

Naldor Kardeth is a 45-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall and is of heavy build. He is tall, heavy and smooth-faced, with auburn hair oiled flat, rings on every finger and a soft double chin. His gray eyes are shrewd and friendly. He dresses in the best blue the harbor sells, and he walks the wharves every morning as if he owned them, which a good share of them he does.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Naldor Kardeth is a wealthy merchant prince of [[place-portaelion|Port Aelion]], whose fortune grew considerably because he consistently followed the advice of [[being-tarelblthl|Tárella Pelthal]]. He sits on the [[affiliation-twncnclprtaln|Town Council of Port Aelion]] as a Councilor and holds the office of Keeper of the Wharves, giving berths to the ships that load the timber of Liranel and the salt-fish of the coast.

The timber that crosses his wharves has been measured at Liranel's tally-house, and the council's wharves load it without measuring it again. He knows how much is licensed and how much is not, and he has never been asked. The captains of the Guild count him a friend, and the loudest of them counts him an accomplice of the astrologer.

## Psyche

### Personality

Naldor is jovial, calculating and loyal to a fault. He remembers every captain's first voyage and repays a kindness tenfold. He believes in his astrologer because she has been right.

### Motivation

He wants the wharves busy and the council content. He wants Tárella protected, and his own fortune not to be called luck.

### Strengths

He is trusted on the wharves and feared in the counting-house. He has deep pockets and a good memory.

### Weaknesses

His fortune is tied to one woman's reading. If she is wrong once, he is the man who bet on her.

## Social

### Companions

His factor, his clerks and the wharfingers who work for him.

### Patrons

Tárella Pelthal is the nearest he has to a patron, and he calls her his friend.

### Enemies

Tralkar Hérselnde, who calls the astrologer a fraud and him a fool.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-twncnclprtaln|Town Council of Port Aelion]]**: Naldor holds the office of Keeper of the Wharves, at the rung of Councilor.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: He is of the Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The unmeasured timber.** A shipment of squared oak loaded at his wharf was never measured at Liranel's tally-house. A clerk of the Forester's court asks a party to learn whose stamp is on it before the Keeper does.
2. **The astrologer's warning.** Tárella has told him to hold his ships in harbor this week and he has done so at a cost. He asks a party to find out what she saw, and to do it without her noticing.
3. **The loud captain.** Tralkar Hérselnde has begun to say Naldor bought the investigation. Naldor asks a party to find who is feeding the story.
