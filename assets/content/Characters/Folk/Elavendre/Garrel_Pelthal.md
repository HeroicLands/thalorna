# Appearance {#appearance}

Garrel Pelthal is a 47-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of heavy build. He is broad and courteous, with brown hair oiled flat and brown eyes that smile first. He dresses in good blue wool, with a heavy gold ring on the right hand.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Garrel Pelthal is a timber factor of Port Aelion who holds a crown license for a block of the licensed belt behind [[place-liranel|Liranel]]. The block's bounds are written in the tally-house roll, and the roll puts the felled oak ring inside them. He is courteous and certain that the license covers him.
