# Appearance {#appearance}

Evrane Tolsel is a 53-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of medium build. She is upright and steady, with graying brown hair under a circlet of woven living oak and green eyes that look at the map in her hand. She wears a gray robe stained at the hem with leaf-mold.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Evrane Tolsel is a grove-elder of the [[affiliation-groveconcl|Grove Council]]. She learned the bounds of her grove as a girl from a grove-keeper who is dead, and she believes the felled oak ring lies inside them. She asks a party to learn whose the ring is before the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] act.
