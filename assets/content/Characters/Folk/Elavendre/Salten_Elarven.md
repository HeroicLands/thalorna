# Appearance {#appearance}

Salten Elarven is a 43-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. His face is weathered by years on the Coast Road, his brown hair is going gray at the temples, and his gray eyes rest on a speaker a moment longer than manners ask. The nose has been broken and set flat. He wears the crown's green under a plain oiled cloak and carries the sheriff's short staff in his belt, not in his hand.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Salten Elarven is the sheriff of Lísere, the crown's officer over the northern shore, with his hall at the top of [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]]. He tries the crown's pleas, holds the posse and collects the road toll and the duty on whatever is entered at the tally-house, through a clerk at the harbor and reeves in the villages. A killing, a theft from a crown cargo or a raider found in the town comes to his hall. A quarrel in the market goes to [[affiliation-sarmelisconcl|the Quay Council]].

He holds that the quay is wherever a crown cargo lies, and the council holds that the quay is the town's. The two offices are courteous in public. The courtesy has a price he counts every season: a good part of what the Kalipsan ships carry out is entered at the tally-house only in part, and the furs in it are not Élavendren. He keeps a short roll of the men he can send to the posse on a night's notice, and this winter it is shorter than it was, because the best hands of the shore are on the quay, hired by an [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] scholar at wages above his.

## Psyche

### Personality

Salten is patient, dry and thorough. He asks the same question three times in three forms and notes which answer changes. He is courteous to the Reeve of the Harbor and gives the council no occasion to complain of him, and he does not forget a slight.

### Motivation

He wants the crown's duty entered whole, the shore's levy at strength and the scholar's gold off his quay before the herring season. He would also like to learn who in his own hall has been telling the Kalipsan factors which cargoes the clerk will count.

### Strengths

He knows the shore: every landing, every reeve and which headmen can be trusted with a posse. The crown's name carries in his mouth, and his clerks keep a better roll than any in the north.

### Weaknesses

He has few men and a harbor that is not his to command. His quarrel with the council is older than his office, and the Castellan at Durmelen is competing with him for the same levymen.

## Social

### Companions

The crown's clerk of the tally-house and the reeves of the shore villages carry his writs. A lieutenant of the Admiral keeps the crown's three cutters at the mole and answers to the Admiral, not to him.

### Patrons

The crown at [[place-beravel|Péravel]] named him, and the chancery there answers his letters in its own time.

### Enemies

The Kalipsan factors on the outer quay pay for berths and for blindness. Behind them stand the men who hire the shore's boys for the scholar, and he has not learned who that is.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Salten holds the office of Sheriff at the rung of Knight.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The boom and the fire.** The sheriff and the Castellan at [[place-durmelen|Durmelen]] each want the other's levymen back from the Order's wages, and each will pay a party to find out who is paying them. Salten adds a second errand: the boom was closed last night on a false fire, and nobody on the headland will say who lit it.
2. **The mark on the furs.** A tally-clerk has found a Varokh knot under the Kalipsan mark on a bale of furs. Salten cannot send his own clerk across the bay and asks a party to learn whose boat brought it, without letting the quay learn that he asked.
3. **The scholar's roll.** The Order scholar's list of hired guards is longer than the sheriff's posse. Salten will pay a party to sign on, count the men, and bring him the names of those who are on the levy roll at Hendrel and Harnasel as well.
