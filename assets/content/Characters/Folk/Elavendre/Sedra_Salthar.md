# Appearance {#appearance}

Sedra Salthar is a 36-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of medium build. She is small and neat, with brown hair under a white cap and blue eyes red at the rims. She wears a good wool dress and carries a basket.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sedra Salthar keeps house in [[place-liranel|Liranel]]. Her husband [[being-berinsalth|Berin]], a guide, went into [[place-siralonke|Siralonke]] with a second party after the Order's scouts and has not come home. She asks a party to find him, and she fears the Wardens will find him first.

:::secret {title="What Sedra does not know"}
The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Wardens]] already hold Berin at a turf watch-hut at the forest edge, and they are sending him out of Lirve's woods for good. They would let her see him.
:::
