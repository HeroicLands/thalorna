# Appearance {#appearance}

Kenrel Kardeth was a 40-year-old man who stood 6'1" tall and was of light build. He was an easy, comfortable man, tall and loosely built, with dark brown hair and blue eyes that did not argue. His right shoulder was stiff from an old fall. He dressed in the house's colors without ornament and was easily recognized by his walk.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kenrel Kardeth was the husband of [[being-glyencrdth|Lyenna Kardeth]] and fifteen years her senior. He held the family's estates and, as head of one of the town's older families, its seat on the [[affiliation-twncnclprtaln|Town Council of Port Aelion]], a decent, well-liked and politically moderate man content to manage his lands. He was killed three years ago on the road between his estates and [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]], in what was reported as a bandit raid.

The crown's garrison at Elanmere investigated under Captain [[being-dharnvldrn|Daran Véldarin]] and concluded that it was a raid. His cousin [[being-heltankardet|Heltan]] has come home asking whether it was.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kenrel was quiet, fair and unambitious. He tried every case on its merits and did not remember a grudge. His wife found him dull.

### Motivation

He wanted his people fed and his lands in order. He wanted a quiet life and the council to keep out of his way.

### Strengths

He was liked on the road and trusted at the council, and he balanced a ledger.

### Weaknesses

He was passive, and he was married to a woman who was not. He did not see the shape of what was behind him.

## Social

### Companions

His household and the council members who sat beside him.

### Patrons

None; the house's lands were his own.

### Enemies

None he would have named. He died without an enemy he knew.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The narrow point.** The ambush happened at a narrow point of the Elanmere road where reinforcement was impossible. Heltan asks a party to walk it and say whether anyone who knew the road would have chosen the place.
2. **The council seat.** Kenrel's seat has been voted by his widow since his death, and a councilor who sat beside him says it has not been voted as he would have voted it. He asks a party to learn why.
3. **The servant's message.** One of the servants left alive to carry the news has died of a fever, leaving a message for Heltan with a friend. The friend asks a party to carry it to him, and to see that it reaches him unopened.

:::secret {title="Who killed him"}
The attack was arranged by Lyenna Kardeth, who serves the Black Flame. His moderate politics and passive temperament were obstacles, and as his widow she inherited control of the house's affairs, its council seat and the freedom to act without a husband's oversight. The bandits were hired; the servants who were left alive were left to carry word that it was a raid.

She feels no guilt over it. The garrison's report was the report she needed.
:::
