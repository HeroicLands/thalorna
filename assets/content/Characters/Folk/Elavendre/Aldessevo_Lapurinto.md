# Appearance {#appearance}

Aldessevo Lapurinto is a 2,020-year-old man who stands 6'7" tall and is of light build. His white hair is long and loose, and his gray eyes are pale as ash. An old burn crosses the back of his left hand, the only scar on him.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Aldessevo Lapurinto is a lord of [[affiliation-nclvthlss|the Enclave of Ethalosse]]. He was young by Senali measure on the night raiders came over the pass of [[place-nelgan|Nelgan]] with brands, and he was among the Senali who walked out through the fire behind [Hauletta]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, into the light of the raiders' own fire, in sight of men who had never seen them ([[lore-firesnelgan|The Fires on Nelgan]]). He was among those who laid the wounded Dhargan in the court of worked stone, and he heard Hauletta give the wood to the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] there. The carrying dates from that day.

Ethalosse raised him for the carrying. He was the first Senalo to receive an Áelendan who came up to the court with a word to carry, and he taught the first carriers to carry exactly.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is warm, grave and slow to speak, and the Áelendan he has known since their great-grandparents' time find him easy company.

### Motivation

He wants the Áelendan kept as they were given the wood: asking, not driven. He wants nothing carried for them that they would be ashamed to have carried.

### Strengths

He remembers the first carriers, and every carrier since knows it.

### Weaknesses

He is fonder of the Áelendan than of any human power, and he does not hide it from the crown's questions.
