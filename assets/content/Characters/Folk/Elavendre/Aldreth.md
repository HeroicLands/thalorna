# Appearance {#appearance}

Aldreth is a 36-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. He is dark and rangy, with black hair tied back and brown eyes red-rimmed from sleeplessness. He wears oiled leathers and a Warden's gray cord, and he speaks in a husk.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Aldreth of the Tírindi is a Warden of the Drenavar Spine, set to the shore of [[place-sngngtrn|the Singing Tarn]]. His partner has fallen ill with a fever, and a Tradition Keeper must come from [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]] to teach the counter-song to a replacement. Until then he sings it himself, and he is tiring.
