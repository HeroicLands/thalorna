# Appearance {#appearance}

Ansorula Masserinto is a woman who stands 6'1" tall and is of light build. She is tall, slender and very still, with silver hair that falls straight to the knee and violet eyes that seem to see past the speaker. Her pale skin is faintly translucent. She wears a plain gray robe of undyed wool and nothing else, and she moves so quietly that a visitor does not hear her cross a stone floor.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ansorula Masserinto is a Senalo of the enclave of [[place-ethalosse|Ethalosse]], the smallest of Élavendre's three enclaves and the only one placed for access rather than seclusion. It is where the Senali conduct business with the world, and she is the one who receives the carriers in the court of worked stone. A warning, a refusal, a question about a disturbed site or the return of something taken goes up to Ethalosse in the mouths of [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] elders who have been asked to carry it.

The Áelendan carry; they do not represent. What is carried is carried exactly, and Ansorula hears it in the carrier's own words and gives it back in the enclave's. The court's last great question stands unanswered: when Péravel asked how the Senali hold that the world ends, what came down was [[lore-gatheringend|The Gathering on the End]], the account of a convocation that has never reached accord. The enclave's answer was: we have not agreed. Ask again when you have a speech of your own.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ansorula is grave, unhurried and entirely courteous. She names a visitor by the whole of his name, and she does not shorten her own. She considers a question before she hears it.

### Motivation

She wants the carrying done exactly and no carrier made to embellish. She wants the Senali's few words to reach those who ask, whole.

### Strengths

She hears the carriers true. She is old past counting, and what she says is what the enclave holds.

### Weaknesses

The enclave does not agree on much, and she cannot give an answer the enclave has not made. A human who presses her for more is told so once.

## Social

### Companions

The carriers who come up from the lowlands, and the other Senali of the court.

### Patrons

[[being-raltilaoltes|Raltila Oltessanto]], the Lord of the Enclave, whom no human record names.

### Enemies

None she would name. The Order, which is turned back at the tree line by the Wardens before it reaches her.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvthlss|Enclave of Ethalosse]]**: Ansorula is a Member of the Enclave.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A speech of your own.** The enclave said to ask again when you have a speech of your own, and a bard of the College of the Tongues has composed one. A carrier asks a party to see him safely up the long open way and to be out of the court before he rises.
2. **A question about a disturbed site.** The Wardens of the Silver Forests have sent a question up about the bark cut from the Waking Stand. An Áelendan elder is carrying it, and asks a party to bring the enclave's answer down exactly as it is given, without a word added.
3. **The invitation.** An Order scholar has learned that a human has a realistic chance of being invited to Ethalosse, and has set out with a guide who says so. A party is asked by the Wardens to turn them back, and by the carriers to see them to the edge of the long way.
