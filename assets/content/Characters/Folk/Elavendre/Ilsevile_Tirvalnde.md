# Appearance {#appearance}

Ílsevile Tírvalnde is a 51-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. She is tall and exact, with brown hair in a severe knot and a pale face that has settled into attentiveness, marked by a deep line between the brows. Her brown eyes rest on the speaker. She wears a magistrate's dark gown and the chain of her court, and she folds her hands in her lap as she listens.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ílsevile Tírvalnde is a magistrate and judge, a graduate of the University and a magistrate of the royal court at [[place-valdun|Valdun]]. She sits in a royal court of the city, hears the crown's pleas and asks first whether a complainant was warned. She has clashed with [[being-prthnblthl|Parthun Pelthal]] on numerous occasions over the interpretation of law and regulation.

A magistrate hears no complaint against the tribes, none from anyone hurt at an Araelwen and none from anyone who entered sacred ground unauthorized. At Valdun that question arrives with every returned hired guard, and she asks it, and she has begun to ask it of herself. Her court sits in a town whose wall has been rebuilt three times and whose law is built on the road.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ílsevile is cool, rigorous and fair. She quotes the statute and the precedent in the same sentence and dismisses a case on a technicality in a voice that makes it sound like a favor.

### Motivation

She wants the law applied as written and the court's name unspotted. She wants Parthun's regulations tested against the statute.

### Strengths

She is respected across the march. Her rulings are clear and cited, and the court's clerks are her allies.

### Weaknesses

The question of the warning has begun to feel like a trap she walks into each time. She is not sure the answer is always the law's.

## Social

### Companions

The court's clerk and a pair of University-trained advocates who plead before her.

### Patrons

The Justiciar, who heads the royal courts and trained her.

### Enemies

Parthun Pelthal and his regulations.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Ílsevile holds the office of Magistrate at the rung of Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Was he warned.** A noble house's son, hired as a guard by the Order, has come back from an Araelwen with a Warden's arrow in him, and his father petitions the magistrates. Ílsevile asks only whether he was warned, and asks a party to learn the answer from the Wardens' side.
2. **The regulation.** Parthun Pelthal has issued a regulation on drove tolls that contradicts the statute. Ílsevile asks a party to find a drover who will bring the case.
3. **Treasure and the helmet.** A hunter's find of an imperial helmet that has not rusted is before her court as treasure-trove. She asks a party to find who else has seen it, and who has already offered to buy.
