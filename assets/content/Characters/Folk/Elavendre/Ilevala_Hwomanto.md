# Appearance {#appearance}

Ilevala Hwomanto is a 479-year-old woman who stands 5'10" tall and is of light build. Her dark brown hair is tied up out of the way, and her green eyes look a long time at whatever she is shown. There is earth in the creases of her hands that does not wash out.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ilevala Hwomanto is an Oak-Warden of [[affiliation-nclvlrss|the Enclave of Aelirosse]]. She was the Oak-Pupil of [Olmasitti Ropelinto]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, and his counsel to her, given in the grove, at the water-court, at the sickbed and once at the ford, is [[lore-saunari|The Oak-Warden's Sayings]], which every Oak-Pupil of Aelirosse learns. He died the autumn the oak dropped acorns, and Aelirosse says his name one word longer now. The last saying is hers: she has put four of the acorns in the ground by the water-court, and they are grown when the child who watches the enclave is a grandmother.

An Oak-Warden tends the grove, sits at the sickbed, reads the dreams held to be sent by [[lore-goddreams|the God of Dreams]] and keeps a silence. She does all four.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is grave for her years, plain-spoken and fond of the young.

### Motivation

She wants to be the Oak-Warden her teacher was, and she wants the four oaks grown.

### Strengths

She has the whole of the sayings, and she reads a dream truly.

### Weaknesses

She measures herself against a teacher who kept three watches, and she is not sure she has a saying of her own.
