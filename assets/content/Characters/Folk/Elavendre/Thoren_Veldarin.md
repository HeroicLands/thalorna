# Appearance {#appearance}

Thoren Véldarin is a 60-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of light build. He is courteous and spare, with white hair worn short and gray eyes that give nothing away. He wears the Rector's black gown and the chain of the Oldest Court.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Thoren Véldarin is the Rector of the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]] and a Master of the Oldest Court, learned in the law. He holds the University's charter in trust for the crown and keeps the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] warm and the queen informed. He does not discuss what the fellows do beyond the tree line, and says so with some regret.
