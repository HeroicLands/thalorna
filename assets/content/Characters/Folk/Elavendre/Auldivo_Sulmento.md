# Appearance {#appearance}

Auldivo Sulmento is an 813-year-old man who stands 6'5" tall and is of light build. His dark brown hair is tied back, and his blue eyes are far-sighted and seldom blink. He wears gray and green and sits very still, often for a whole day.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Auldivo Sulmento is an elder of [[affiliation-nclvthlss|the Enclave of Ethalosse]]. At Ethalosse the long open way to the court of worked stone is watched, and visitors are seen arriving for a considerable time before they arrive. He is the one who watches. He sits above the way from first light, and the court knows who is coming up it, and how many, and whether they carry anything, well before they reach it.

Ethalosse is the one Senali settlement in Élavendre a human has a realistic chance of being invited to, which is not the same as a realistic chance. A path that leads to it uninvited leads somewhere else, and on the long way the turning is his.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is quiet, patient and quietly funny about the people he watches.

### Motivation

He wants the carriers to reach the court and everyone else to reach the bottom of the way again, unhurt and none the wiser.

### Strengths

He sees far and forgets nothing he has seen.

### Weaknesses

He trusts the Áelendan who carry so completely that a stranger in a carrier's company can walk farther up the way than the enclave intends.
