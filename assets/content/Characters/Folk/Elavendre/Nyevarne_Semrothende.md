# Appearance {#appearance}

Nyevarne Sémrothende is a 57-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of light build. She is tall and narrow, with white hair braided in a crown and pale blue eyes. She wears black, a Master's hood of the Oldest Court and no ornament but her house's ring, and she speaks quietly enough that a room goes silent to hear her.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nyevarne Sémrothende is the Justiciar of the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]], the head of the royal courts of the cities and shires. Every magistrate in the kingdom sits under her, and every one of them was trained at the [[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]], where she held a Master's chair in the law before the Queen named her. Her branch of the Sémrothende is a house of the first rank, its descent proved, an artist or a mage in each of the last two generations, and a chair at court. The Sémrothende of the grain roads above [[place-ruthelmar|Ruthelmar]] are a cadet branch of the middle rank.

She keeps the courts to the law as it stands. A magistrate asks first whether a complainant was warned, and the royal courts hear no complaint against the tribes, none from anyone hurt at an [[lore-aralwen|Araelwen]] and none from anyone who entered sacred ground unauthorized. She believes those rules are what keeps the crown's law from running past the tree line, and she does not apologize for them.

This year the rules are being tested at every bench. The Order's hired guards come back from the interior with wounds and petitions, and a noble house has a son with a Warden's arrow in him.

## Psyche

### Personality

Nyevarne is austere, precise and fair. She listens to the end of a case before she says a word, and the word is usually short.

### Motivation

She wants the royal courts to hear what is theirs and refuse what is not, every time, so that no one beyond the tree line has cause to doubt where the crown's law stops.

### Strengths

The magistrates fear and trust her. The Grove Council counts her as the one officer at court who has never shifted a bound for a friend.

### Weaknesses

She will not bend a rule for mercy, and the families of the guards the Order hires call her cruel for it. She has never been past the tree line.

## Social

### Companions

Her clerks of the rolls, and the magistrates she trained, among them [[being-ilseviletirv|Ílsevile Tírvalnde]] at Valdun.

### Patrons

[[being-aurenthekir|Queen Áurenthe]], and the Oldest Court of the University, which keeps her chair for her.

### Enemies

The noble houses whose sons the Order hires, who want the courts to hear their grief, and the Order's advocates at court, who want a magistrate to hear a complaint from an Araelwen once, so that there is a precedent.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Nyevarne holds the office of Justiciar, as one Of the First Rank.
- **[[affiliation-univperavel|University of Péravel]]**: She holds the rung of Master in the Oldest Court.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The petition from the interior.** A noble house petitions the Justiciar to hear its son's wound from an Araelwen as a matter of the crown's peace. She asks a party to find out whether he was warned, and by whom.
2. **A magistrate who heard it.** A magistrate in a Lirve town has heard a complaint against a Warden and found for the complainant. The Justiciar wants the record brought to Péravel before the tribes hear of it.
