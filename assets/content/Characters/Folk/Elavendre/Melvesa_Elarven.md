# Appearance {#appearance}

Mélvesa Elarven is a 62-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. She is tall, spare and upright, with auburn hair pinned under a faded blue cap and a clear face. Her hazel eyes are steady. Honey and herb stains darken her cuffs. She walks with a hazel staff she does not lean on and sings under her breath when she thinks no one listens.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Mélvesa Elarven is the senior priestess of the temple of [[affiliation-lizelas|Lizelas]] at [[place-sourimael|Sóurimel]]. She is a Sarnor of the faith, a healer and gardener, and she is the mentor of [[being-sarenprdln|Sárena Párdalen]], whom the temple supplies and sends out on her circuit.

Sóurimel is a settled, permanent, largely Élavendri community of some six hundred souls in the Silver Forests, where such things are not normally permitted. It exists by an arrangement with the Áelendan whose terms are known to everyone in general and to nobody in particular, and the temple keeps something besides the Aureldian calendar: the village keeps the old festivals. The villagers live on charcoal, honey, hazel, withy and the herbs the physicians of Péravel pay absurd prices for.

## Psyche

### Personality

Mélvesa is calm, humorous and practical. She treats a toothache and a heresy with the same remedy: bread, honey and time. She does not lecture.

### Motivation

She wants the village kept on its sufferance, the festivals kept and Sárena's circuit supplied. She wants the arrangement with the Áelendan not tested by an Order scholar.

### Strengths

She knows the herbs and the village. The Wardens pass through the village without comment, and she has never asked why.

### Weaknesses

She does not know the terms of the arrangement, and neither does anyone else. A new scholar in a guide's coat could break it.

## Social

### Companions

Two acolytes and the village's herb-wives. The Warden of the spring patrol stops for bread.

### Patrons

The faith, through its temple at Liranel. The village is her patron in practice.

### Enemies

The Order's scholars, who ask about the village's terms.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-lizelas|Faith of Lizelas]]**: Mélvesa holds the office of Sarnor as senior priestess of the temple.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: She is of the Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The lamp that walked.** The mother of Alena Alveryn, a charcoal-burner's daughter who left with lowland strangers and did not return, says a lamp was seen walking in a straight line through the trees. Mélvesa asks a party to hear her out and to go no farther than the village's edge.
2. **The circuit's supply.** Sárena Párdalen's circuit is short of herbs, and the picking grounds are on Áelendan land. Mélvesa asks a party to ask the Wardens' leave for her.
3. **The festival guest.** An outsider has arrived for the festival, is made welcome and is watched. Mélvesa asks a party to be the ones who watch.
