# Appearance {#appearance}

Dorlen Kirsal is a 34-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of light build. He is lean and quiet, with black hair under a knitted cap and brown eyes that watch the water. He wears tarred oilskins and carries a short knife and a shuttered lantern.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Dorlen Kirsal runs untaxed cargo along the [[place-lisere|Lísere]] shore on dark nights, landing it in coves the sheriff's clerk does not visit. He is wary of strangers, armed with a knife and a boathook, and runs rather than fights unless he is cornered. He serves as a stock smuggler wherever a shore needs one.
