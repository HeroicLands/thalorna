# Appearance {#appearance}

Taldor Párdalen is a 43-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of light build. He is lean and stooped, with black hair going gray and a long weathered face. He ducks at every low door out of habit and walks the shingle-stone path to the beacon-house with the unhurried stride of a man who has done it in the dark ten thousand times.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Taldor Párdalen is the head of the oldest line of [[place-hendrel|Hendrel]] and the keeper of its beacon-house on the point. He sits on the council of [[affiliation-beaconfamlys|the Beacon Families]]. The village judges its own quarrels in the beacon-house, the grove-elder who tries cases on this coast comes down twice a year, and the crown's sheriff collects what Hendrel owes through a clerk who comes in the autumn and does not stay the night.

Hendrel loses fewer people to raiders than any village on the shore, and the village treats that as a matter of being careful and nothing else. A scholar of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Order]] has passed through, asking about the Dusk Ford and then about the families' kin, and Taldor told him the road is open and the Ford is shut after dusk. His daughter means to go with the scholar's hired guards, and he has not said why the place they are bound for worries him.

## Psyche

### Personality

Taldor is quiet, careful and exact. He answers a question with the shortest true sentence he has and watches what the asker does with it. He lights the beacon only when he has seen the thing twice.

### Motivation

He wants his village left as it is, his daughter home and the Ford unasked-about. He wants to keep what his line holds from the younger families without lying to them.

### Strengths

The village trusts him on the weather, the tides and what is out on the water. Every lamp-hand on his station is a kinsman.

### Weaknesses

His daughter will not be told. He is a poor liar who has made a trade of saying little, and a scholar who asks the right question twice will see the hesitation.

## Social

### Companions

His wife keeps the household and the stone path. The lamp-hands of the station are his sister's sons and his own.

### Patrons

The oldest woman of the village is the line's memory and its counsel, and Taldor does nothing important without speaking to her first.

### Enemies

No one he would name. He distrusts the Order scholar who asked about the Ford, and he does not trust the dark shape on the water.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-beaconfamlys|The Beacon Families]]**: Taldor is Head of a Line, with a seat on the council.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: He is a Freeman of the crown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The daughter.** Taldor asks a party to bring his daughter home from the Order's guards before they set out for the Fen Door, and will not say why the Door worries him.
2. **The dark shape.** The beacon-watch has seen a darker shape on the water for three nights running, carrying no lamp. Taldor does not want to light the fire until someone else has seen it too.
3. **The Ford after dusk.** The Order scholar who asked about the Ford has sent his guards to look at it, and Taldor asks a party to be at the gap in the reeds before them and to turn them back by any means that does not bring the crown.

:::secret {title="The line that asked"}
Taldor's line is the line of the beacon-keeper who asked the Toll-Keeper of the Dusk Ford to stand between the raiders and the land. Its kin cross the Ford free by day or dusk, and they carry a knotted cord at the belt that the Toll-Keeper knows. The line keeps this from the village's younger families and from the crown, because a bargain that favors one line would set the coast against it.

Taldor knows what the Ford has taken from raiders. He does not know what the Toll-Keeper asks of his line when the pact comes due, and he fears the daughter would be the one asked.

**Hook: the cord.** A party that wears the knotted cord is let across by the Ford and another party that does not is not. Taldor will say so only to someone he has watched for a month.
:::
