# Appearance {#appearance}

Válena Drindal is a 20-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of light build. She is slight and freckled, with red hair in a student's braid and blue eyes, curious and gentle. She wears the plain gray of a student of the Colleges and carries a small harp, and her left fingertips are hard from its strings.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Válena Drindal is a student of [[affiliation-clgwoodsng|the College of the Wood-Song]] at [[place-elanmere|Elanmere]], of a gentry family that sent her to the art. The Colleges keep a ballad of the Forfeit-Men whose last verse the bards do not sing, and she went to the Lísere shore to find out why. At dusk three nights ago she crossed [[place-dskfrd|the Dusk Ford]], and she has not come back.

## Psyche

### Personality

Válena is gentle, curious and stubborn, and she asks more questions than she answers.

### Motivation

She wants the last verse of the ballad, and the reason the bards do not sing it.

### Strengths

She plays well, and she knows the tunes of the Colleges.

### Weaknesses

She goes where she has been told not to go.

## Social

### Companions

Her fellow students at Elanmere, who think she has gone home.

### Patrons

The Master of the College, [[being-sirrelurethr|Sírrelure Thaurnde]], who has sent word along the coast road asking who saw her go.

### Enemies

None that she knows of.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-clgwoodsng|The College of the Wood-Song]]**: Válena is a Student.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: She is of a gentry family.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The missing student.** The Master of the College of the Wood-Song pays a patron's debt to anyone who brings back his student, who crossed the Dusk Ford three nights ago ([[scenario-tlldskfrd|The Toll You Will Not Miss]]).

:::secret {title="The tally-house"}
At the first stone the Toll-Keeper asked Válena what she would give that she would not miss until she was across. She gave the memory of the verse she was looking for, and with it she lost the memory of who she is.

She sits on a bench by the door of the tally-house on the far bank, puzzled, with her harp across her knees. She cannot tell anyone her name, and she does not know what she is looking for, only that she was looking for something. She wants the thing she came for, and she no longer knows what it is. She still knows how to play, and a tune of the Colleges calls the forfeit that belongs to its player. She has nothing to name herself with, and a girl who cannot remember who she is follows anyone who is kind.

[[being-varlangrth|Varlan Gárath]] is her enemy, because she saw what was in his satchel before she crossed.

**Hook: the last verse.** Válena's verse is a forfeit on a shelf of the tally-house, and it can be reclaimed only by someone who knows what she was looking for.
:::
