# Appearance {#appearance}

Thelvaran stands 5'7" tall and is of heavy build, and looks forty-one. He is barrel-chested and slow-spoken, with graying brown hair and brown eyes in a broad, kind face. He wears a centurion's harness rotted to its rivets and speaks [[skill-vylarilng|Vilari]] with the accent of the hills he was recruited from.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Thelvaran is the _Hundar Kerval_, the senior centurion, of the Vilérian cohort in the hall under the hill the drovers call [[place-kptfr|the Kept Fire]], under its _Lusar_, [[being-solkimoslsr|Solkimos]]. He sits nearest the guests at the long benches and tells them, kindly, to eat. He is bored, loyal and desperate for news.

:::secret {title="The watch"}
Thelvaran is 594 years old, and he has looked forty-one since the night the door was taken. He wants the watch relieved, and when he is asked what he would do if it were, he answers after a long silence that he does not remember what he did before it.
:::
