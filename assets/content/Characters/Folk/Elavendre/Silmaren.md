# Appearance {#appearance}

Silmaren is a 58-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of light build. He is gray and stooped, with gray hair to his shoulders and gray eyes that look a long time at the trees. He wears a Warden's gray-green cloak gone pale at the seams, and the cord a Warden wears at the wrist is missing: he tied it to a birch on a day he could not enter the hollow.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Silmaren of the Dhárgandi is an Elder Warden of the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] who keeps the eastern stretch of [[place-silvrfrsts|the Silver Forests]] between [[place-sourimael|Sóurimel]] and [[place-aelirosse|Aelirosse]]. For three seasons the paths have turned him from one hollow, and he has guessed what lies in it: a working of the art, on sacred ground, that the wood is turning everyone away from, Wardens included ([[place-rngtrndpths|The Ring of Turned Paths]]).

He cannot ask a lowlander into sacred ground. He wants the working ended and the hollow given back to the wood, and he can fail to notice a stranger walking past him into the trees. He wants the man who made it brought out alive to answer to the Wardens.
