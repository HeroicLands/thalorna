# Appearance {#appearance}

Chénira Háresende is a 42-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of heavy build. She is tall, heavy-set and straight, with brown hair cropped for a helm, a pale freckled face and a scar running from the left ear to the jaw. Her blue eyes miss nothing. She wears the crown's green, a captain's cord on the shoulder and no ornament but a plain bronze ring.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Chénira Háresende is a decorated captain of the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]'s forces and the crown's liaison to the tribes. She carries the crown's word to the Áelendan and the tribes' word back, by agreed channels, to [[place-eshalshldg|Ésalosa Lodge]]. Her post is the gate-house at [[place-gandelis|Gandelis]], where the last royal road ends in a stone arch with the crown's arms on one face and a plain staff cut into the other.

She keeps a formal relationship with [[being-rnthrprdln|Ranthor Párdalen]], with hints of deeper mutual respect and perhaps unresolved personal history. She has seen companies of hired guards leave Gandelis for the forest every season for eleven years, and she knows that the crown's licenses and the Wardens' law do not meet at the arch.

## Psyche

### Personality

Chénira is formal, quick and laconic. She states the crown's position in a sentence and the Wardens' in a second, and leaves the third to the listener. She has a soldier's humor.

### Motivation

She wants the line at the arch held without a death. She wants the crown's word to reach the Lodge whole, and she wants to know why the Lodge has stopped answering.

### Strengths

She is trusted by both sides of the line, which is rare. She knows the Wardens' patrol habits and their words.

### Weaknesses

The crown's chancery does not tell her what it is licensing. A liaison who is sent word late is a liaison who is blamed for it.

## Social

### Companions

A sergeant and six troopers hold the arch. She meets the Warden under the gate each dawn.

### Patrons

The queen's chancery at Péravel.

### Enemies

The Order's agents, who go round her. The captains who think a liaison is a diplomat in a uniform.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Chénira holds the office of Liaison to the Tribes at the rung of Knight.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A word for the Lodge.** The crown's last message to the Lodge has gone unanswered for a month, and Chénira cannot send her own troopers past the arch. She asks a party to carry the crown's word up the agreed channel and bring back an answer, a refusal or nothing at all.
2. **The companies at the arch.** Every company of hired guards leaving Gandelis is entered at the gate-house, and Chénira asks a party to learn which of them is carrying a map that the crown's guides did not draw.
3. **Ranthor's letter.** A letter from Ranthor Párdalen waits at the gate-house, asking for a private meeting. She will not go alone and will not say why, and she asks a party to ride with her and stay at the edge of the hearing.
