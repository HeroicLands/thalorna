# Appearance {#appearance}

Rupena is a 44-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of medium build. She is brown and weary, with brown hair in a plain braid and brown eyes that watch the water. She wears a herd-wife's gray wool and a child's cap tucked in her belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Rupena is a herdwife of the summer meadow below [[place-sngngtrn|the Singing Tarn]]. Her son Tamrik went up after a stray at dusk, and the tarn took him. She sits at the shore every evening just outside the Wardens' line, and the watch has carried her down twice.
