# Appearance {#appearance}

Jeldor Mórlineth is a 35-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of heavy build. He is short, thick and ruddy, with dark brown hair tied back and the stiff left hand of a man who has held a rein in winter. His brown eyes are sharp. He rides a hill pony, wears a leather jack with the levy's badge sewn on and carries a short hill-lance across the saddle.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Jeldor Mórlineth is a hill knight of [[place-valdun|Valdun]], holding his steading of a house of the hills for service in arms. He is the Hill-Captain of Valdun of [[affiliation-brdrmltia|the Border Militia]] and leads the muster of the hill levy: drovers, foresters, smiths and their sons, mounted on Áuldalon ponies, who have done this before and expect to do it again. Its officers are local men, and Jeldor is the one the others follow.

The levy musters at Valdun and is not a noble force and does not pretend to be. Its opinion of the court's cavalry is unprintable, and its record against Varokh war-parties and Grukar bands is better. The market town between raids is prosperous, with great drove fairs and the iron of the eastern bloomeries, and the pack-trains from the east pay their escort money in Valdun's yards, to men who would in any case have been the ones to worry.

## Psyche

### Personality

Jeldor is plain, loud and loyal. He greets every man in the muster by name and every officer from the court by rank. He has no patience for a plan that begins in Péravel.

### Motivation

He wants the levy fed and led by men who know the ground. He wants the next muster called in time for the cattle to be driven within the walls.

### Strengths

He knows the hill roads, the fords and the raiders' habits. The drovers will follow him into a frozen pass.

### Weaknesses

His quarrel with the Border Militia's captain wastes the same time the raiders use. He has no patience for the magistrates and no gift for the paper they need.

## Social

### Companions

The levy's other officers are drovers and smiths he has known since boyhood. His steading's men go with him.

### Patrons

The house of the hills he holds of.

### Enemies

The court's cavalry officers who call the levy rustics. Vargen Hálmesende, who wants it for his own uses.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]]**: Jeldor is a Knight, holding land of a house of the hills for service in arms.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The late muster.** The muster was called in time for the cattle only because a drover rode three nights to bring the word. Jeldor asks a party to learn who sat on the order at Péravel.
2. **The escort money.** A pack-train master from the east swears he paid his escort money in Valdun's yards and was given no escort. Jeldor asks a party to find the man who took the silver, since it was not one of his.
3. **Vargen's offer.** A lord of the march has offered the levy pay to muster outside the march. Jeldor asks a party to learn what for before he refuses.
