# Dossier {#dossier}

Qelti is a given name borne by many across the empire — architects, magistrates, playwrights, captains and merchant lords among them — so the name alone identifies nobody in particular. One bearer, a Master Playwright at [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]], has mentored the performer [[being-thiryegezaqe|Thirye Gez'Aqêu]] toward imperial notice. Asking after "Qelti" in the capital means specifying which one before anyone can answer.
