# Appearance {#appearance}

The Go-Between of Yaravesh is a broad, gray-haired woman with a set of scales, who waits on the far bank of the ford and is not in a hurry.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Go-between of the war-band at the ford. Takes coin and refuses promises.**

The Go-Between of Yaravesh comes from a forest landing, where the captive trade is kept by the women. She names a price once, takes coin and refuses promises, and keeps the custom of the ford: a price named there is not raised, and a captive handed over there is not taken again on the road home.

## Background

**Formation.** She was raised at a forest landing, where women keep the captive trade.

**The office.** She weighs the coin on her scales and gives a captive back for the price she named.

**Now.** She asks a warden's price for a man who wore a warden's coat, and she gives time until the first ice and not a day past it.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is unhurried and exact, and she sells what she was given to sell.

### Motivation

She wants the price paid in coin at the ford.

### Strengths

The custom of the ford protects every bargain struck on the gravel bank.

### Weaknesses

She lowers a price only for someone who convinces her that the captive is a villager.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vristwald Tribes]]**: a woman of a forest landing, trading with the river princes at the ford.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A warden's price.** She asks a prince's price for a man in a fort's coat and says why when asked.
2. **The custom of the ford.** She keeps her word to the day, and a captive taken back by force ends the custom for her war-band.
3. **Who chose the post.** Asked, she says that the war-band watched it for three nights and saw one man on it each night.
