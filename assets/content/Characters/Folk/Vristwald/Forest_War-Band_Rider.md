# Appearance {#appearance}

A Forest War-Band Rider is a Varokh warrior in forest dress, at home among trees and on a river path, and armed for the season's raiding.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Warrior of a village war-band. Follows his War Chief for a season.**

A Forest War-Band Rider is a warrior of a village war-band of the [[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vristwald Tribes]]. Every autumn the war-bands cross the edge below the forts and take captives, and in another season the same warriors hold a fort beside a prince's men.

## Background

**Formation.** He is a warrior of a village war-band and follows its War Chief for a muster.

**The war-band.** He scouts the opposite bank of a crossing, waits at a ferry for a purse and stands at a go-between's back at the ford.

**Now.** He keeps the war-band's custom at the ford and its bargains with a village.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is watchful and wastes no words with strangers.

### Motivation

He wants the season's pay and the war-band's bargain kept.

### Strengths

He moves through the forest without being seen.

### Weaknesses

He holds a gate only on a bargain the war-band trusts.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vristwald Tribes]]**: a warrior of a village war-band, under its War Chief.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The ferry.** A band waits at a ferry where the road crosses a side-stream, having heard that a broker rides with a purse.
2. **Behind the go-between.** Two riders stand at the back of the go-between at the ford and let no captive cross.
3. **The season's service.** A war-band offers its service for the season to a paid fort whose warden has no silver.
