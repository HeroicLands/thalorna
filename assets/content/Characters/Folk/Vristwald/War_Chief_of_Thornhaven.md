# Appearance {#appearance}

The War Chief of Thornhaven receives a visitor in the village hall, with the war-band behind him.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**War Chief of Thornhaven. Wants the season's pay and the crossing.**

The War Chief of [[place-thornhaven|Thornhaven]] leads the village's war-band and speaks for it to outsiders. His standing rests on managing the Thornwald frontier rather than on war alone.

## Background

**Formation.** He holds the War Chief's seat, which is won by what a warrior has done.

**The office.** He leads the war-band and treats with the princes' agents, who go to him and never to the Other Chief.

**Now.** He likes the crossing toll and agrees to the heir's bargain readily.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is ready to agree and slow to ask what a bargain costs later.

### Motivation

He wants the season's pay and the crossing.

### Strengths

The war-band follows him for the season.

### Weaknesses

A bargain he makes is not a bargain until the Other Chief witnesses it.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vristwald Tribes]]**: one of the three seats of Thornhaven.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The toll.** He receives the heir at once and asks about the crossing.
2. **Silver the heir does not have.** At the first late payment he asks for silver, and the war-band walks out at the gate.
3. **Who commands the fort.** His huscarl asks who commands when the prince's men and the war-band disagree.
