# Appearance {#appearance}

Sirethos Kolsamis is a 38-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall with a medium build. He has tanned skin, smooth complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sirethos Kolsamis is the Blazewarden of [[affiliation-sonsmuspell|the Sons of Eldheim]], a military order of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]], and leads its campaign against the [[lore-varokhiclt|Varokh]]. He has sworn to burn the Varokhi forest and to sacrifice its Shamans to [[lore-ustarondty|Ústaron]], and has fought [[being-thrnkbldtscbr|Thornak Bludtusk Barsk]]'s forces on several occasions.

The campaign sails from [[place-stormveld|Stormveld]] to the western bay. At [[place-arnwern|Arnwern]], his order killed or took the villagers and burned their Shaman before the eagle stone. The Sons fortify the clearing with the village’s timber; patrols wait for scavengers who return for what the fire left.

## Psyche

Sirethos treats conquest as sacred work. He wants ground on the mainland from which his order can reach into the Nordlands, and his oath against the Shamans makes the forest’s keepers his declared targets.

## Social

Thornak’s band raids the Sons’ outposts and ambushes their patrols. The villages have a common enemy in Sirethos, but their elders have not agreed to the common muster Thornak seeks.

## Plot Hooks

The burned clearing
: Families want belongings and captives recovered from Arnwern. Sirethos’s patrols guard the approaches while the Sons build their foothold, and a rescue needs a route through both the shore watch and the occupied clearing.

The next landing
: Unfamiliar sails appear off the western bay. [[place-arnholt|Arnholt]]’s watch sends warning while the Sons prepare to land; the neighboring villages must decide how much help to send before another settlement burns.
