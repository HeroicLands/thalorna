# Appearance {#appearance}

The Other Chief of Thornhaven is an old woman who listens to everything and speaks last.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Other Chief of Thornhaven. Witnesses a bargain spoken before both peoples.**

The Other Chief of [[place-thornhaven|Thornhaven]] holds the seat that speaks for the village to strangers and witnesses a bargain between the village and outsiders. A bargain is not a bargain until she witnesses it, and no prince's agent has asked her for anything in a century.

## Background

**Formation.** She holds the Other Chief's seat, to which an elder's assistant is acclaimed.

**The office.** She witnesses a bargain only if it is spoken before both peoples at the place it binds, and she refuses one with tribute in it.

**Now.** She has had less to say to the world than any Other Chief in Vristwald.

## Psyche

### Personality

She listens to all of it and says only that a bargain is spoken at the place it binds.

### Motivation

She wants a bargain with no tribute in it and an answer to who feeds the garrison in a season without silver.

### Strengths

Her witness makes a bargain whole.

### Weaknesses

Her seat has been passed over for a century, and the War Chief has less reason to need her.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vrystwldtrbs|Vristwald Tribes]]**: one of the three seats of Thornhaven.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The witness.** She wants strangers at the bargain who belong to neither side.
2. **The question.** She asks who feeds the garrison in the season the prince's silver does not come.
3. **The place it binds.** She witnesses the bargain only at the gatehouse, before both peoples.
