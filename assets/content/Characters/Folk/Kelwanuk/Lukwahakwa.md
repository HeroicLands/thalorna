# Dossier {#dossier}

Lukwahakwa, Copper Lifted, is the ninth chief of the [[affiliation-eaglehouse|Eagle House]] to hold its highest name, and with it the first seat on the coast of the [[lore-kelwanukclt|Cedar People]]. His house is the largest of the twelve on [[place-tsilkasis|Eagle Beach]], and its posts are the work of the Raven House's master carver, [[being-kwemlatsuma|Kwemlatsuma]]. His nephew [[being-tuklaskapala|Tuklaskapala]] holds the coast's seat at the council of the [[affiliation-redstncmpct|Red Stone Compact]].

The first to hold his name gave the [Killing Feast]{.name data-type="lore/history"} on this beach, and the people of Tsilkasis do not speak of it to guests. The house has sat first on the coast for four generations of chiefs since.

The [[affiliation-bearhouse|Bear House]] of [[place-lamekis|Bear Beach]] has given two feasts in four years that the Eagle House has not matched, and gives a third this winter. Lukwahakwa has broken one copper already to answer the second, and his commoners have worked two summers at the weir and the rendering pits for the feast he has not yet given. If he cannot answer, the next feast of the coast seats the Bear House first. Tuklaskapala has told him that the Bear House's wealth is beads paid by frightened towns, and that the Eagle House should say so at its feast. Lukwahakwa has answered that the first chiefs of the Eagle House grew rich on captives too, and that a house that asks where its rival's wealth came from invites the same question about its own.

What he wants is a feast great enough to answer the Bear House, so that the first seat stays with the Eagle House.
