# Dossier {#dossier}

Kwayukapala, Returns from the North, is a noble of the [[affiliation-eaglehouse|Eagle House]] at [[place-tsilkasis|Eagle Beach]], who leads its few big canoes north every midsummer past the glaciers to [[place-simimanik|Trading Shore]], the southernmost village of the [[affiliation-tikupilarit|Ice-Edge People]]. She takes bent cedar boxes, eulachon grease, cedar-bark mats and now and then a sheet of copper, and brings home walrus ivory, fox and wolverine furs, gut parkas and carved bone for the Eagle House's feasts. The trade lasts three days. Neither side sleeps in the other's camp, and the bargaining is done with signs and the few dozen words each has learned of the other's speech, of which she knows more than any other noble of the [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Cedar People]].

The watch on the headland at Trading Shore looks for Cedar war canoes, and has reason to. Kwayukapala comes in trade canoes, lands under the headland where she is expected, and keeps her paddlers on their own side of the beach. She is thirty-five, weather-burned, and impatient with her chief's feasts; she thinks the ivory she brings home is worth more to the Eagle House than another broken copper.

This winter the Eagle House must answer the [[affiliation-bearhouse|Bear House]]'s third feast, and its chief, [[being-lukwahakwa|Lukwahakwa]], needs every tusk and fur she can bring. Her canoes take a few passengers who can paddle, and a party that wants to reach the [[place-tikumanikrgn|Ice Coast]], or to come home from it, can earn a place in one.

What she wants is a road to the north that only the Eagle House can paddle.
