# Appearance {#appearance}

Kwemulkita is a 49-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a medium build. He has brown skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kwemulkita is the chief of the Orca House at [[place-malkisis|Orca Beach]], the fifth of the [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Cedar People]] to hold the name, and he holds it knowing whose it was. The first Kwemulkita killed twenty slaves at the [Killing Feast]{.name data-type="lore/history"}, and lies in his box on the mortuary pole behind the village. The fifth gives feasts in the same house, and at the last three of them he has led a slave out of the back of the house to the front, given the man or woman a blanket and a small name, and told the guests that the person was a gift. His Keeper of the Count, [[being-kalunukwas|Kalunukwas]], counts each one aloud as a gift given.

The guests of the [[affiliation-eaglehouse|Eagle House]] have twice walked out before the blankets rather than accept a freed slave. Kwemulkita says nothing about it afterward. He is a quiet chief at his own fire and a patient one at other men's.

:::secret {title="Why the fifth Kwemulkita frees slaves"}

Kwemulkita frees slaves because Kalunukwas told him to, privately, five winters ago, and told him why. He believes he is shaming the Eagle House out of the first seat. She believes the first Kalunukwas was right, and that a living slave is somebody. Neither has asked the other which it is.

A gift that guests refuse cannot be answered, and each time the Eagle House walks out, the coast watches the Orca House give and the Eagle House fail to receive.

What he wants is to see the Eagle House shamed out of the first seat by gifts it will not take.

:::

Last winter the Hungry Power took a slave boy of his house into the forest, and the Keeper of the Masks, [[being-gunistalwa|Gunistalwa]], sang him back and gave him a winter name. The boy is a slave again in summer. Kwemulkita has not freed him and has not said why. A party that comes to Malkisis at feast time can be seated as witnesses, and a chief who means to free a slave before the Eagle House wants witnesses who will remember it on every beach.

What he wants is the Orca House's feasts remembered on every beach, and the fifth Kwemulkita's name remembered apart from the first's.
