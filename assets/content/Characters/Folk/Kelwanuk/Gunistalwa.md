# Dossier {#dossier}

Gunistalwa, the Mask Speaks, is the Keeper of the Masks (_gunismis_) of [[place-malkisis|Orca Beach]], and a commoner of the [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Cedar People]]. In summer he holds no name and no seat at any feast. In the dark months he rules the winter dance house at the forest edge, and the village is ordered by the dances while its summer names are put away, so for half the year the chief of the Orca House takes his place where Gunistalwa tells him to sit.

At the start of last winter the Hungry Power took a boy of the Orca House into the forest. The boy was a slave, born to a woman taken in a raid on the oak valleys of the south. Gunistalwa sang him back over four nights as he would any Wild One, and gave him a dance place and a winter name. In winter the boy dances with a seat no chief can take from him; in summer he is a slave again, with no name.

The [[affiliation-eaglehouse|Eagle House]] says that a power meets a person, that a slave is not a person, and that the Bringing Back was a mockery. Gunistalwa answers that he did not choose the boy, the power did, and that the Eagle House is welcome to go to the far end of the forest and tell the Hungry Power its mistake. He is sixty, lean, and tired of being asked.

A party wintering at Malkisis lives under his order whether it likes it or not. He can teach a stranger which dances may be watched, and he wants to know what the powers of other countries do with the people nobody counts.

What he wants is the winter's order kept, so that what a power gives in the dance house no summer name can take back.
