# Appearance {#appearance}

Melumniksala is a 43-year-old woman who stands 5'3" tall with a heavy build. She has brown skin, weathered complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Melumniksala, Carries the Grease, is a noble of the Wolf House at [[place-mukwasis|Wolf Beach]] and the leader of its carriers on the [Grease Trail]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, the one road the [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Cedar People]] keep over the mountains. Each early summer, once the snow is off the pass, she takes the carriers twenty days east with boxes of eulachon grease on their backs to the salmon falls of the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]] at [[place-hiyespito|Falling Water]], and brings them back before the snow with robes, dried roots and now and then copper. She has walked the trail twenty-two times and speaks enough of the Grass People's tongue to bargain without signs.

Hers is the only road from the coast toward the Red Quarry, so the Eagle House's Seat Holder, [[being-tuklaskapala|Tuklaskapala]], walks over the mountains with her each summer to hold the coast's seat at the [[affiliation-redstncmpct|Red Stone Compact]].

This summer the [[affiliation-bearhouse|Bear House]] sent a captive girl of about twelve up the inlets, taken in the oak valleys behind the Shell-Bead coast, and asked the Wolf House to sell her at Falling Water for robes. No captive had ever gone over the Grease Trail for sale. Her chief, [[being-ulsemhakwa|Ulsemhakwa]], agreed, and Melumniksala carried the girl over the pass. The girl is at Falling Water now, with a price of twenty robes on her that nobody there has paid. Melumniksala has said at the Wolf House's fire that if the plains do not buy her, she will not carry the girl back over the pass. She has not said what she will do instead.

She is forty-three, sun-dark from the dry country, and as sure-footed on the trail as anyone alive. A party crossing the mountains in summer can walk with her carriers, and she has a use for strangers who are not Cedar People and owe the Wolf House nothing.

What she wants is the Grease Trail kept a road for grease, and never a road for slaves.
