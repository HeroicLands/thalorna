# Dossier {#dossier}

Dusdoltalwa, Thunder Speaks, is the chief of the Thunderbird House at [[place-gwemlasis|Thunderbird Beach]], whose poles are the oldest on the coast of the [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Cedar People]]. His house says it sat first for longer than anyone can count, until the [[affiliation-eaglehouse|Eagle House]] climbed past it four generations of chiefs ago, and climbed on canoes the Thunderbird House sold it. Dusdoltalwa says so without bitterness, because he does not think his house has finished.

He sells canoes to every house that pays. In five years he has sold six to the [[affiliation-bearhouse|Bear House]] of [[place-lamekis|Bear Beach]], more than to any other house, and the Bear House pays in beads from the south and climbs the order of seats faster than any house in living memory. Dusdoltalwa has not said whether he is helping it climb or helping it climb past the Eagle House and then fall. The Thunderbird House rises in either case. His village has no eulachon river and buys its grease from the inlets, and in a bad salmon year he trades a canoe for a winter's grease.

He is fifty-two, heavy and genial, a generous host and a slow payer. His master builder, [[being-kelwatsuma|Kelwatsuma]], has never sat at his feasts. A party that wants a canoe, or wants to know who is buying them, talks to Dusdoltalwa. He tells the truth about a buyer when the truth weakens a rival, and the party that carries it does his work for him.

What he wants is the Thunderbird House first on the coast again, whichever house has to fall to put it there.
