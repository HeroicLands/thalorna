# Appearance {#appearance}

Kwemlatsuma is a 55-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall with a heavy build. He has golden skin, chapped complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kwemlatsuma is the master carver (_tsumamis_) of the Raven House at [[place-tsilkasis|Eagle Beach]], and a commoner. He has no seat at any feast, and he has carved the house posts and crest poles of half the noble houses of the northern fjords, the posts of the [[affiliation-eaglehouse|Eagle House]] next door among them. The [[lore-kelwanukclt|Cedar People]] hold him up as what a man should be.

He chooses his cedar in winter, walks to it, tells it what it will be and asks it to give, and he will not cut a tree that stands too near a stream, because the salmon need its shade. A pole of his takes a year, and he has never delivered one late. When he was young, a chief ordered a pole in a hurry for a feast, and Kwemlatsuma told him that the pole would take a year or the chief could find another carver. The chief waited and moved the feast.

The commoners of a house do the fishing, rendering, weaving and carving that make a feast's gifts, and the chief gives them away. Kwemlatsuma's poles stand before other men's doors, and some of the coast's commoners hold that this is the whole of the arrangement.

What he wants is work done in the time the cedar gives it, whoever is waiting for it.
