# Dossier {#dossier}

Kelwatsuma, Carves the Cedar, is the master builder of the Thunderbird House at [[place-gwemlasis|Thunderbird Beach]] in the [[place-yatsalargn|Outer Islands]], and a commoner of the [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Cedar People]]. He has built more war canoes (_yakwis_) than any man living. Each begins as a standing cedar in the island forest that he has walked to, spoken to and asked to give, and each takes two years from the felling to the paint. A Gwemlasis canoe painted black and red with a thunderbird on the bow is the best hull on the coast, and every one is his work or his apprentices'.

The price of every canoe goes into the boxes of his chief, [[being-dusdoltalwa|Dusdoltalwa]]. Kelwatsuma has no seat at any feast, and every war canoe that took a captive in the last twenty years was his. He says he builds canoes, and that what the buyers put in them is the buyers' affair. He is sixty, with burn scars up both forearms from the steaming stones, and he speaks to cedar more readily than to people.

He knows every war canoe on the coast by the set of its bow, and he knows which house owns it now. A party hunting a raiding canoe, or the captive it carried, can ask him whose it is, and he can answer if he chooses. A party that wants a canoe of its own must get it from his chief.

What he wants is a seat at a feast for the man who built the coast's canoes, before his hands give out.
