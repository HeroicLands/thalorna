# Appearance {#appearance}

Yaldukita is a 43-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a medium build. He has tawny skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Yaldukita, Gives Wealth, is the chief of the [[affiliation-bearhouse|Bear House]] of [[place-lamekis|Bear Beach]], the third of his house to hold its highest name and the first to make it matter. He has given two feasts in four years greater than anything the [[affiliation-eaglehouse|Eagle House]] of [[place-tsilkasis|Eagle Beach]] has answered, and this winter he gives a third. If the Eagle House cannot answer it, the coast seats the Bear House first.

His wealth is captives and beads. The Bear House's war canoes take captives from the valleys behind the coast of the [[affiliation-shuqash|Shell-Bead People]], and he sells them on his beach to any house that can pay, in blankets, grease and copper. Three times a year the canoe master [[being-sitiqnush|Sitiqnush]] comes north to buy captives back, and Yaldukita sells to him at his own price and treats him as an honored guest. Every spring the northern coast towns send two canoes of beads up the coast to Lamekis, the [Northern Strand]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}, and he gives them away at his feasts, a strand to each guest in the order of seats.

His Keeper of the Count is his mother's sister, [[being-tsamalkalu|Tsamalkalu]], Counts the Salmon, who has kept the house's count for thirty years and has never been wrong in public.

:::secret {title="The Strand buys less"}

She knows what he has not admitted. Each strand buys less every year, the debts his feasts create are coming back to Lamekis at the new worth, and the house is further in debt than the coast knows. Yaldukita means to cover it with more captives, and he has told his war canoe captains that after this winter's feast they may raid the Shell-Bead coast towns themselves, Strand or no Strand.

:::

What he wants is the first seat, taken before the count catches up with him.
