# Appearance {#appearance}

Kalunukwas is a 59-year-old woman who stands 5'1" tall with a medium build. She has tawny skin, smooth complexion, white hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kalunukwas is the Keeper of the Count (_kalumis_) of the Orca House at [[place-malkisis|Orca Beach]], the seventh to hold the name. She holds in memory every gift the house has given and received for forty years: who gave what, at which feast, before which witnesses, and what is still owed. At every feast she stands behind her chief, the fifth [Kwemulkita]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, and says the count of each gift aloud. Houses in dispute over an old debt send for her, pay her in blankets and accept what she says, though many of them would rather not. The [[lore-kelwanukclt|Cedar People]] hold her up as what a woman should be, and few chiefs on the coast want her at their feasts, because she can name any house's unpaid debts to its face.

The first Kalunukwas refused to count the twenty slaves killed at the [Killing Feast]{.name data-type="lore/history"}, and her ruling that a dead man is given to nobody is the law of every feast on the coast. At his feasts Kwemulkita frees a slave before the guests, and she counts each one aloud as a gift given. The [[affiliation-eaglehouse|Eagle House]]'s people have twice walked out before the blankets rather than accept one.

:::secret {title="Why the fifth Kwemulkita frees slaves"}

The seventh Kalunukwas holds that the first was right, and that the ruling reaches further: if a dead slave was given to nobody, then a living slave is somebody. Five winters ago she told Kwemulkita privately to free a slave before the guests at his feasts, and told him why. Kwemulkita believes he is shaming the Eagle House out of the first seat. Neither he nor she has asked the other which it is.

:::

Three winters ago Kwemulkita wanted to give a feast to shame a rival, and she told him before his whole house that the count could not pay it and the house would be in debt for ten years. He did not give it. The [[affiliation-bearhouse|Bear House]] has asked her whether she will count the [Northern Strand]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} as its gift, and she has said that she will count what is given at the feast and nothing about where it came from.

What she wants is a freed slave counted as a person on every beach of the coast.
