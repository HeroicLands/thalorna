# Dossier {#dossier}

Dimasnilkwa, Sees the Dream, is the healer whom the northern inlets of the [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Cedar People]] send for. She is a woman of the Wolf House at [[place-mukwasis|Wolf Beach]], and the Thunderbird taught her in a fever when she was fifteen. She has gone to the [Village of the Dead]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} for a stolen soul more times than she will say, and she has stood in the bow of more canoes than any healer of the inlets.

In the [[lore-nakpulalas|Meeting]], the faith of the coast, a soul that one healer's trance cannot bring back is fetched by the canoe of the dead (_melis medak_): four to eight healers standing in two lines on the floor of a dance house at night, each holding a painted board that is the side of a canoe, paddling to the Village of the Dead and back. The household pays every healer in blankets and feeds the whole house when it is over, so a canoe costs as much as a small giving feast. Dimasnilkwa stands in the bow, where the soul is caught, and charges every chief exactly the same for it. The chiefs take that as an insult and pay.

She is forty-six, broad-handed and impatient with ceremony she has no part in. She speaks to the dead with more courtesy than she gives most of the living. No house has ever paid for a canoe to go for the soul of a slave, and she has never offered to go for nothing; the question of what she would ask for one, and from whom, is a question nobody on the coast has put to her.

A party can be sent up the inlet by a chief who wants a soul brought back and cannot persuade her to come, or can bring her a sickness from another country that no power of the coast has taught her to sing out.

What she wants is to be owed nothing by any chief and to owe none of them anything, whatever they pay.
