# Appearance {#appearance}

Tuklaskapala is a 33-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall with a light build. He has tawny skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tuklaskapala, Returns from the Mountain, is a nephew of [[being-lukwahakwa|Lukwahakwa]], chief of the [[affiliation-eaglehouse|Eagle House]] of [[place-tsilkasis|Eagle Beach]], and for six summers he has been the house's Seat Holder (_tukwamis_). Each summer he walks with a Speaker to the [Red Quarry]{.name data-type="place/site"}, over the [Grease Trail]{.name data-type="place/feature"} from [[place-mukwasis|Wolf Beach]] with the grease carriers and on across the plains, to hold the coast's seat at the council of the [[affiliation-redstncmpct|Red Stone Compact]]. He can promise nothing but what the first seat agreed before he left, which is usually nothing.

At the last council the delegates of the [[affiliation-teyumi|Turquoise People]] asked him what the [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Cedar People]] would do if the [[affiliation-shuqash|Shell-Bead People]] smoked the compact's pipe and became pipe kin of every nation, the Cedar People included. He had no answer to give, and he has carried the question home. He has told his uncle that the [[affiliation-bearhouse|Bear House]]'s wealth is beads paid by frightened towns, and that the Eagle House should say so at its feast. Lukwahakwa has refused, saying that a house that asks where its rival's wealth came from invites the same question about its own.

What he wants is an answer to carry back to the compact, and a coast willing to give one.
