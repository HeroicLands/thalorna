# Dossier {#dossier}

Geron practices alchemy out of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]], and treats independent practitioners as rivals to be opposed on principle. Women working outside male supervision draw a particular hostility, more than independent practice from men does. Anyone studying alchemy near Geron learns which arrangements will be tolerated and which will not.
