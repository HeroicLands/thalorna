# Appearance {#appearance}

A Bujalur Camp Head is a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] householder of middle years or more in a good felt coat, who comes to the trough with two riders behind him and lets them hold his horse.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Head of a [[affiliation-bjlrtrb|Bujalur]] winter camp. Answers for its riders.**

A Bujalur Camp Head heads one of the Bujalur winter camps in the foothills of the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]]. He answers for every rider of the camp to the dovaq and to the League's truce, and the camp heads together choose the dovaq at their summer spring.

## Background

**The camp.** He brings his camp in under the truce each autumn and takes it out at the thaw.

**The judgment.** A camp that breaks the truce is put out of the winter ground by the other camps before the League can do it, and the camp heads give that judgment together.

**Now.** The March's officers are counting the camps, and he wants the count done and the truce kept.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is careful, formal and protective of his camp's name.

### Motivation

He wants the foothill grass for his camp next winter.

### Strengths

His riders obey him, and the dovaq listens to him.

### Weaknesses

He answers for every rider of his camp, including the ones he cannot see.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-bjlrtrb|Bujalur]]**: camp head, answerable for his riders.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The camp heads' council.** The dovaq calls the camp heads together at night, and a stranger the March trusts is asked to sit outside the tent and wait.
2. **A rider to answer for.** A rider of his camp is accused at the outer caravanserai, and he wants the accusation heard before the League acts on it.
3. **The count.** He wants to stand beside the March's officer while his camp is counted, so that no number is written that he has not heard.
