# Appearance {#appearance}

Bujalyitboq Bujalur is a man of about forty who meets a stranger at the door of his tent with every courtesy the custom asks and no more. His camp stands apart from the others, at the eastern end of the line, and his riders stay in it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Head of the easternmost winter camp of the [[affiliation-bjlrtrb|Bujalur]].**

Bujalyitboq Bujalur heads the easternmost of the Bujalur winter camps in the foothills of the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]], the camp farthest from the forts and nearest the grass. He answers for every rider of it to the dovaq, [[being-bujalqiimod|Bujalqiimod Bujalur]], and to the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]]'s truce.

## Background

**The camp.** He brings his camp in under the truce every autumn and takes it out at the thaw, and it winters at the eastern end of the line, the last the March's officers reach.

**Now.** The Lord Commander's officers are counting the Bujalur camps. He gives his numbers short, keeps his camp apart and keeps his riders home.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is polite, careful and closed. He keeps a guest's custom exactly and offers nothing the custom does not require.

### Motivation

He wants his camp's herds to grow and his camp to stand higher among the Bujalur than its place at the end of the line.

### Strengths

His riders are loyal to him before the dovaq, and his camp can strike its tents and ride east faster than any other.

### Weaknesses

His camp winters farthest from the forts and from the dovaq's tent, and word reaches it last.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-bujalqiimod|Bujalqiimod Bujalur]]: The dovaq, who holds the truce and rides ahead of the count.
- [[being-evodlunbjlr|Evodlun Bujalur]]: His wife, who keeps his tent.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-bjlrtrb|Bujalur]]**: camp head, answerable for his riders to the dovaq and the truce.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The short count.** The March's officers want to know why the easternmost camp counts fewer riders than it waters at the trough.
2. **The far end of the line.** A patrol riding the line of winter camps reaches his camp last, and wants a guide who knows the foothills between.

:::secret {title="The riders at the end of summer"}
At the end of summer the riders of the dovaq who is gathering the northern tribes came to Bujalyitboq's camp. He gave them salt and heard their offer of herds, and he said nothing of it to Bujalqiimod, who had told the same riders that the Bujalur swear to no one. Bujalyitboq has not sworn to the gathering man. He has not refused him either. A caravan master who saw the riders at his camp has told the March. When the dovaq learns it, the custom of the tribe asks the camp heads to put Bujalyitboq's camp out of the winter ground: [[scenario-trcecmpsmrch|The Truce Camps of the March]].

His wife and his riders know what he knows, and a guest who has eaten his salt hears it at his fire.

**Hook: the struck tents.** His camp has struck its tents before the thaw and is ready to ride east, and the dovaq wants it held until he has spoken to its head.

**Hook: the guest's question.** A guest at his fire who asks about the riders who came at the end of summer hears his wife answer before he can.
:::
