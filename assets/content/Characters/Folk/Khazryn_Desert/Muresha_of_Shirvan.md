# Appearance {#appearance}

Muresha of Shirvan is a 40-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She has golden skin, a clear complexion, black hair worn long and plaited down her back in the court manner of Shirvan, and dark brown eyes that settle on a speaker and stay there. She dresses in the white of the house, plain linen with a border of blue at the hem, and wears one ornament, a clay bead on a cord at her throat. There is ink in the creases of her right thumb and forefinger, and a small old burn on the back of her left hand. She speaks quietly and in complete sentences, and she asks where a thing was found before she asks what it is.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Muresha of Shirvan is the heir-in-exile of the [[affiliation-hseshirvan|House of Shirvan]], the senior of the seven princely houses of the [[affiliation-khzrncnfdrtn|Tellumi Confederation]], and by custom First of the Seven: at every session of the [[affiliation-cnclprinces|Council of the Princes]] she speaks first. Her court sits in [[place-shebarakhend|Shebarakhend]], the court city of the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]], in a rented house with a walled garden, a household of a few dozen and a library of a few thousand tablets.

The library is the case. Muresha has spent her life building one argument, that the [[place-oasishirvn|Oasis of Shirvan]] belongs to her house by right and that the [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Atarzád]] have nothing to set against the right but a promise. Every treaty, deed, water-roll and king-list that bears on Shirvan is in it, copied or original, and every tablet that comes out of the city is entered in it the day it arrives. The case is never finished, and she does not expect it to be. She means it to be ready on the day it is needed, and she does not know when that day is.

## Background

**The house.** Muresha is the only child of the last heir-in-exile, who died when she was twenty-six. She grew up in the library and learned the Tellumi syllabary before she could reliably walk across the court, and the burn on her hand is from a lamp she knocked over at seven, reading after the household slept. Her father left her the house, the seat at the Council, [[miscgear-shirvantab|the first tablet]] and a debt to a Tellumi banker of the Kingdom that she paid off in nine years by selling copies of the house's commentaries to the schools of Amradad.

**The Council.** She took the first seat at twenty-six, and the princes listened to her because her house is first and stopped interrupting her within two sessions because she was always right about what the tablets said. She has never married. The elders of the house have proposed four matches and she has refused all four, each time on the ground that the house cannot afford a second claim tied to a second house.

**The present.** Some of the exile courts see her as the focus of a more active phase of the claim, the voice that could turn ninety years of waiting into a demand. She has not said whether she wants to be one. She has said, at the Council, that the [[affiliation-hsemernagord|House of Mernagord]]'s talks with Atarzád merchants are treason, and the session that followed was the worst-tempered in years.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is courteous, exact and patient, and her patience has an edge to it: she can wait a long time for a fact and does not forgive a person who wastes it. She is warmer than she looks, with the household's children and with the old water-masters who carry tablets out of the city, and she is the only head of a house who answers every letter from the Shirvan quarter herself.

### Motivation

She wants Shirvan back, and before she has it she wants it proved. She believes that a claim argued well is a claim half won, because the Kingdom, the Council and some of the Atarzád themselves respect a record, and a reconquest without a record would be only a second Breaking.

### Strengths

She knows the archive of her house better than anyone alive, reads the old syllabary at sight and writes the Tellumi tongue in three hands. The first seat gives her the opening word at the Council, and a reputation for never misquoting a tablet gives her the last word in most of its arguments. Water-masters, guides and clerks of the Shirvan quarter send her what they find.

### Weaknesses

She has no army and no money beyond the court's revenues and the copies she sells. Her case needs tablets that come out of the city, and every tablet is carried by someone who risks the water-tax assessors and the [Ozhmarod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} informers to bring it. She is lonely, and she knows it.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Tribes of Atarzád]]: The people who hold her city. She does not hate the Atarzád she meets in Shebarakhend, and she has corresponded for years with a [Tozhirod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} clerk about a disputed boundary tablet. She means to take Shirvan from them.

### Others

- [[being-hemvanaymern|Hemvanay of Mernagord]]: The ruling prince of Mernagord, who leads the houses of the west against her house in the oldest argument at the Council, the coming down, and who listens to Atarzád merchants about a compact she calls treason.
- [[being-narvunakethr|Narvuna of Kethramír]]: The head of the priest-house and the one prince she counts a friend. Narvuna argues the faith and Muresha argues the deeds, and between them they hold the case the Confederation makes in public.
- [[being-gureshayzarg|Gureshay of Zargandur]]: The young heir who trains for a reclamation. She thinks him brave and premature, and she has told him that a city taken without the case is a city that can be taken back.
- [[being-nimeshayashk|Nimeshay of Ashkarad]]: The richest of the exile princes, who has offered more than once to pay for the library's copyists. She has accepted once.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-hseshirvan|House of Shirvan]]**: heir-in-exile and head of the house, and its own Keeper of the Case.
- **[[affiliation-cnclprinces|Council of the Princes]]**: a seated prince, and First of the Seven.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A tablet out of the basin.** A water-master of the Shirvan quarter has a tablet from the channel diggings under the lower basin and no safe way to send it. Muresha pays a party to carry it to Shebarakhend past the assessor at the basin gate, and pays more to learn who else was offered it.
2. **The boundary tablet.** Her Tozhirod correspondent has died, and his letters to her lie in his rooms at [[place-kethramir|Kethramír]]. A Tozhirod magistrate who reads them will find a clerk of the tribes discussing Shirvan's deeds with its heir. She wants the letters back before anyone reads them.
3. **The match.** The elders of the house have found a fifth match, a younger son of the [[affiliation-hseushtraber|House of Ushtra-ber]], and they ask a party Muresha trusts to learn what kind of man he is before she refuses him.

:::secret {title="The bricked chamber"}
Last spring the quarrymen at the western end of the [[place-vurinkarvem|Vurinkarvem]] cliff over Shirvan cut back into the stone and opened a chamber that had been walled up. One of them is a Tellumi of the lower town, and he sent Muresha word with a tablet from the chamber's threshold, pressed in a script she reads and a hand she does not know. The quarrymen bricked the chamber up again and are asking a price to open it. Muresha has paid that price twice over, to keep it bricked until a man of her own can stand inside it, and the quarrymen have taken her money and gone on asking.

She does not know what is in the chamber. She knows that the cliff is Ozhmarod ground, that the Ozhmarod would burn whatever in it honors another god, and that a library under the cliff of Shirvan would be worth more to her case than all the tablets she owns. She has told no one at the Council.

**Hook: the man of her own.** Muresha hires a party to go into Shirvan with the lower town's guides, climb to the western ledges at night, unbrick the chamber, copy what they can and brick it up again before the quarry opens at dawn. The quarrymen have sold the same night to a buyer from Kaldar.
:::
