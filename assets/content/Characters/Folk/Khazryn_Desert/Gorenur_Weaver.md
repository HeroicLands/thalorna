# Appearance {#appearance}

A Gorenur Weaver is a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] woman at a ground loom in the lee of a tent, with felts and saddle-rugs folded beside her, each bordered with a line's mark.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Weaver of the [[affiliation-grnrtrb|Gorenur]]. Keeps a line's mark.**

A Gorenur Weaver keeps the mark of one of the five Gorenur lines and weaves it into every felt and carpet the line makes, so that a tent is known by its door-felt and a saddle-rug by its border.

## Background

**The mark.** She learned her line's mark from the weaver before her and teaches it to the girls of the line.

**Now.** She weaves the felts and rugs of the line's tents and sells what the line does not need.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is shrewd, practical and proud of her work.

### Motivation

She wants a good price and her line's mark known on the grass.

### Strengths

She reads every line's mark at a glance and can weave any of them.

### Weaknesses

She sells to whoever pays enough.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-grnrtrb|Gorenur]]**: weaver of a line.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A felt of any mark.** She will sell a door-felt with another line's mark on it to whoever pays enough, and the felt makes a tent look Gorenur to anyone who reads marks.
2. **Reading a mark.** A party holding a felt or a saddle-rug wants to know which line made it, and she can say.
3. **The rugs for the market.** She wants her rugs carried to the outer caravanserais to sell, and a carrier the March will not stop.
