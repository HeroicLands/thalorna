# Appearance {#appearance}

Khuvanghakhiq Gorenur is a man of about seventy with a white beard and a rider's bowed legs, who sits at the moot with his line's mark on the felt beneath him and speaks slowly enough to be interrupted.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**White-beard of the fifth line of the [[affiliation-grnrtrb|Gorenur]]. Sits at the moot of the five lines.**

Khuvanghakhiq Gorenur is one of the white-beards of the fifth of the five Gorenur lines, the eldest men who rule a line and sit together as the tribe's moot at [Khelid]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the tribe's chief well on the edge of the [[place-hekardesert|Desert of Hek'ar]].

## Background

**The union.** He was a young rider in the winter after the last union of the steppe broke, and he remembers what that winter cost the tribe.

**Now.** A dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes, and the white-beards of four lines have said at Khelid that the Gorenur should ride with him. The fifth line has said nothing, and he is one of the reasons.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is slow, stubborn and courteous, and he listens to a stranger longer than to his own dovaq.

### Motivation

He wants no union, no war with the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]] and his line's spring grass kept.

### Strengths

His line obeys its white-beards before the dovaq, who has no word inside a line.

### Weaknesses

He is old, and the young riders of his line want horses and a banner.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-gorenyemok|Gorenyemok Gorenur]]: The dovaq, who leads the raids and wants the fifth line to ride.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-grnrtrb|Gorenur]]**: white-beard of the fifth line, seated at the moot.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The silent line.** The fifth line's tents stand a day apart from the others, and he wants to know what the March would give a line that does not ride.
2. **The spring grass.** He would ask the League for his line's spring grass, if he trusted a messenger to carry the asking.
3. **The young riders.** His grandsons want to ride with the dovaq this spring, and he wants someone to show them what a union costs.

:::secret {title="What the fifth line saw"}
The fifth line did not ride on the first raid of this spring, and its white-beards know the four lines drove the March's herd through the Bujalur winter ground on purpose. He will say before the League's captain that the four lines rode and the Bujalur did not, if the League promises the fifth line its spring grass: [[scenario-trcecmpsmrch|The Truce Camps of the March]].
:::
