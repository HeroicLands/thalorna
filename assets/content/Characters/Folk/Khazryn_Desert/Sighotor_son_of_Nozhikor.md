# Appearance {#appearance}

Sighotor son of Nozhikor is an 80-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of scant build, little more than bone under a gray wool mantle. He has tawny skin gone leathery and deeply lined, a bald crown with a fringe of white hair, a white beard to the chest, and amber eyes clouded at the edges. His hands shake until he folds them. He wears the plain gray of the keepers, barefoot on the stone in every season, and he carries nothing. He speaks so quietly that the keepers repeat his words to the pilgrims, and he often says nothing at all for a long time before he speaks.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sighotor son of Nozhikor is the Toqel, the Keeper of the [[place-unconsflame|Unconsuming Flame]] on [[place-mountshofar|Mount Shofar]] and the senior religious authority of the [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Tribes of Atarzád]]. By custom the Keeper is always a [Burqalod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}, always elderly, and raised only when the previous Keeper dies, and Sighotor has held the office for twenty-six years. He lives in the low stone house at the back of the shelf with the keepers who serve him, and comes down to [[place-beitshofar|Beit-Shofár]] at the mountain's foot only for the Festivals of the Promise and when the [[affiliation-cncltwelve|Council of the Twelve]] sends for him.

His office has one part in government. When the Council considers acclaiming a Zhudek, a judge with broad authority for one crisis, it consults the Toqel first, and he reads the will of [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]] in the Flame. A Toqel who comes down from the shelf and says nothing has given an answer too. Sighotor is widely held to have spoken with Ataros in dreams and to have dreamed of [Yotekhor]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, and the tribes hold him in a reverence that no Rezhul commands.

## Background

**The keeper.** Sighotor was born at Beit-Shofár to a Burqalod lineage of keepers, the son of a Burqalod elder named for [Nozhikor]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Zhudek of the Conquest. He climbed the stair for his first night's watch at sixteen and has slept on the shelf more nights than in any bed.

**The office.** He was raised Toqel at fifty-four, on the death of his predecessor, over two older keepers. The Council asked his reading twice in his first ten years, and both times the Zhudek it acclaimed put down the crisis and gave the office back. It asked a third time fourteen years ago, for the Tellumi rising in the Shirvan district, and that Zhudek died in office when the rising was done.

**The present.** He is old and failing. The keepers carry him up the last flight of the stair, and the Burqalod elders have begun, carefully, to talk about who comes after him.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is gentle, silent and unhurried, and he listens to everyone who reaches the ring of stones, pilgrim or prince, for as long as they wish to speak. He laughs at very small things. He has a dry humor that the keepers do not always notice, and an iron patience that the Council does.

### Motivation

He wants the tribes to keep the Covenant and to keep the Flame, and he wants to die on the shelf. Beyond that he has become, in his old age, less sure what the Covenant asks of a people that holds a subject people, and he wants an answer before he dies.

### Strengths

His word on the Flame is the one word the Council cannot act without, and the tribes revere him. The keepers obey him without question, and no tribe's warrior society would raise a hand against him.

### Weaknesses

He is dying, and everyone who wants something from his successor knows it. He cannot climb the stair alone. His silence is read as an answer, and he cannot always control what it is read to mean.

## Social

### Enemies

- [Aqeshat]{.name data-type="being/npc"}: The young prophet who preaches that Yotekhor will return in the flesh and that the Tellumi must be expelled from the held cities. Her claim goes past anything the tribes allow the Toqel, and the Council cannot place her without him. She has not come to the shelf.

### Others

- [[affiliation-cncltwelve|Council of the Twelve]]: The council that must hear him before it acclaims a Zhudek. He comes down to its session when it sends for him and returns to the shelf the same night.
- [[being-narvunakethr|Narvuna of Kethramír]]: The Tellumi theologian whose book the strict burn. He has read it, and they have never met.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-tribestrzd|Tribes of Atarzád]]**: an elder of the Burqalod, and Keeper of the Unconsuming Flame.
- **[[affiliation-ataros|Faith of Ataros]]**: the senior master of fire of the tribes.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The pilgrim.** A party comes up the stair outside a festival and keeps behind the ring, as the keepers allow. The Toqel sends a keeper to ask one of them a question about the road they came by, and listens to the answer for an hour.
2. **The succession.** A Burqalod elder hires a party to learn, quietly, which of the keepers the Ozhmarod and Aqeshat's followers mean to see raised Toqel when Sighotor dies, and what they have promised him.
3. **The scholars.** Scholars of [[place-caldar|Kaldar]] have asked again to examine the shelf, and the Toqel has refused again. One of them has come anyway, as a pilgrim, and the keepers want him found before he reaches the ring.

:::secret {title="The dream and the letter"}
Sighotor has dreamed of Yotekhor every night since Aqeshat began to preach, and in every dream the prophet stands on the shelf with his back to him and says nothing. He has told no one. The tribes believe that the Toqel's dreams are the will of Ataros, and he does not know what a silent prophet wills.

The Council will want his reading soon. A man is gathering the steppe tribes into a union, and if he rises the Council must acclaim a Zhudek or face him with twelve warrior societies and no one to lead them. Sighotor believes the union is the crisis the office exists for, and he believes that a Zhudek acclaimed to fight it will be acclaimed again and again until the tribes have a king in all but name.

Last winter he sent a Burqalod keeper in pilgrim's gray to the Tellumi school of the Kingdom with an unsigned letter for [[being-narvunakethr|Narvuna of Kethramír]], asking one question about the Promise. He has not had an answer. He wants it before the Council sends for him, because he means to give the Council the answer he reaches, whatever it is, as his last reading.

**Hook: the keeper on the road.** The keeper carrying the letter's answer, if Narvuna gives one, must come back through the held cities in pilgrim's gray. Aqeshat's followers know a keeper went west, and are watching the belt road for him to come back.
:::
