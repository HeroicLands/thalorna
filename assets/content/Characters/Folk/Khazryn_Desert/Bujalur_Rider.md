# Appearance {#appearance}

A Bujalur Rider is a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] herder in a felt coat on a steppe horse, with a bow case at his knee and a knife at his belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Kinsman of a [[affiliation-bjlrtrb|Bujalur]] winter camp. Herds, rides and guards.**

A Bujalur Rider is a kinsman of one of the Bujalur winter camps, entitled to its protection, its feud and its winter ground. The young ones sell horses at the springs and the outer caravanserais of the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]]; the grown ones guard the camp's herd and its tents.

## Background

**The camp.** He rides with his camp west to the March's foothills at the end of summer and out onto the grass at the thaw.

**The truce.** He answers to his camp head, who answers for him to the dovaq and to the League's truce.

**Now.** The March's officers are counting the camps, and the young riders want the camps out of the March before the League turns them out in the snow.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is proud, quick to take offense for his camp and slow to draw on a soldier of the March.

### Motivation

He wants good horses, a share of the herd and the camp's winter grass kept.

### Strengths

He rides before he walks and shoots from the saddle.

### Weaknesses

He gives way to his camp head and his dovaq, and not to strangers.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-bjlrtrb|Bujalur]]**: a kinsman of a winter camp.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Horses at the fair.** A boy at the outer caravanserai has a better horse to sell than his age suggests, and a good price for it.
2. **The gate.** Riders of a camp sit their horses all night outside a spring-fort's gate, waiting for boys the patrol has taken inside.
3. **The herd guard.** A camp's riders turn back strangers who come too near the herd at night.
