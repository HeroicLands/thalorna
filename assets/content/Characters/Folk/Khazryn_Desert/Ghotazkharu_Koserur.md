# Appearance {#appearance}

Ghotazkharu Koserur is a 71-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build, with a rider's bowed legs and a stiffness in the left shoulder where an arrow went in forty years ago. He has brown skin gone leathery from the steppe wind, grey hair in two thin plaits, a sparse grey beard and brown eyes that are slow and warm. He wears a felt coat with the Koserur sign stitched on the back, much patched, and keeps his hands in the sleeves. He sits low by the fire, speaks last, and pours the tea himself for a guest of any rank.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ghotazkharu Koserur is the dovaq of the [[affiliation-ksrrtrb|Koserur]], a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe of perhaps three hundred families on two hundred square miles of mixed steppe in the [[place-hskrrgn|Ghoshiqor]], whose grass touches [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] on its last day's march west of [[place-qisomrktcmp|Qiiso]]. His tent stands at [Juchonkelod]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the tribe's central well and the last halt before the market-camp, in every season. His name means _tent-patience_, and the tribe says he has earned both halves.

He has held a three-well tribe together for twenty-two years by patience: by marrying a daughter where a quarrel threatened, by selling guides to caravans on honest terms, and by keeping a warband that makes raiding the Koserur cost more than it pays. That arrangement is failing. A drought dried the seasonal water two years ago, the [[affiliation-qbdrtrb|Qibudur]] raided the herds, three families crossed to the [[affiliation-jbzrtrb|Jobizur]], and the [[affiliation-kndrtrb|Kenodur]] hold eight of the tribe's people, four women and four children, for a ransom of fifty horses, a share of Juchonkelod's water and the southern grazing for ever.

## Background

**The rider.** Ghotazkharu rode in the warband as a young man and took the arrow in a Qibudur raid at thirty. He was a guide on the road for fifteen years after that, and he can still name every herdsman between the sands and Qiiso.

**The dovaq.** The elders chose him at forty-nine, over a fiercer cousin, because the tribe had just lost a well to a feud and needed a man who would not lose another. It has not lost one since.

**The present.** He has given the war captain of [[affiliation-tghsvrnksrr|the Tigh'Savaran]], [Orqazkenod Koserur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, leave to act as he sees fit for one season to bring the captives home. He has also opened talks with the Jobizur about a protection that would end the Koserur's independence in all but name. He is leaning toward the second, and the warband toward the first.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is patient, gentle and stubborn, and he dislikes a hard stroke because he has seen what a hard stroke costs a small tribe. He is a good host, a careful listener and a poor liar, and he knows it, so he says less.

### Motivation

He wants the Koserur to last, as people, herds and families, even if the name does not. He would rather the tribe's children grow up Jobizur than die Koserur, and he has not said so aloud to anyone but the Jobizur dovaq.

### Strengths

The caravans trust him, the moot respects him, and the tribe's honest dealing on the road is his work. He knows every term, oath and old debt between the Koserur and their neighbors, held in his memory as the Sovidur hold all such things.

### Weaknesses

He is old, the tribe is losing confidence in him, and the warband thinks him tired. If Orqazkenod brings the captives home, the tribe will follow the captain; if Orqazkenod fails, the tribe may follow no one.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[affiliation-kndrtrb|Kenodur]]: The ransom-raiders to the south who hold the eight captives. Their dovaq has never lowered a price, and Ghotazkharu has never paid one.
- [[affiliation-qbdrtrb|Qibudur]]: The horse-breeders to the east who raided the herds in the drought.

### Others

- [[being-khilodlunbii|Khilodlun Biilokur]]: The Jobizur dovaq, with whom he is in quiet talks. He trusts her more than his own war captain does, and less than he would like.
- [[affiliation-tghsvrnksrr|Tigh'Savaran of the Koserur]]: The warband, his tribe's last weapon, whose captain holds his leave for one season.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ksrrtrb|Koserur]]**: dovaq of the tribe, speaking last at its moot.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The terms.** Ghotazkharu asks a party of guests, who owe no tribe anything, to carry his terms to the Jobizur dovaq and bring back her answer. A Koserur rider seen riding north would tell the warband what its dovaq is doing.
2. **The pans at Gorenevod.** A storm has filled the pans at [Gorenevod]{.name data-type="place/site"} for the first time since the drought, and the first tribe to camp there this season holds the water. The Kenodur have sent riders, and the Koserur have nobody to send.
3. **The caravan.** A lightly guarded caravan of spices and silk is crossing Koserur grass, and some of the riders want to take it. Ghotazkharu asks a party riding with the caravan to see it to Qiiso untouched, and to tell him which of his riders came near it.

:::secret {title="The third offer"}
A rider came to Juchonkelod at the new moon from the man who is gathering the steppe tribes into a union, and made Ghotazkharu a third offer. The man will bring the eight captives home, because the Kenodur will not refuse him, if the Koserur swear to his banner under open sky before the autumn market. A tribe that refuses him, the rider said, is replaced by a cousin who will swear.

Ghotazkharu has told no one, not the moot, not Orqazkenod and not Khilodlun. The offer is the only one of the three that brings the captives home without blood, and the only one that makes the Koserur part of a union. If he takes the Jobizur's protection first, the Koserur are counted in whatever the Jobizur choose. If he takes the union's, the Jobizur have a sworn tribe of the union on their southern grass.

He knows which of his cousins the rider meant.

**Hook: the cousin.** A party carrying his terms north meets a Koserur rider on the road who is carrying other terms, from the fiercer cousin the elders passed over twenty-two years ago, to the union's camp.
:::
