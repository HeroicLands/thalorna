# Appearance {#appearance}

Valsuma is a 78-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build, still upright. She has golden skin weathered and deeply lined, white hair cropped close under a red linen cap, and amber eyes that look straight at a speaker and then close while she thinks. Both forearms carry the white scars of the old fire-ordeals. She wears the deep red of a Shekhul of the Sacred Flame with nothing else on it, and she sits so still in meditation that the young Fire-Keepers have been known to check whether she is breathing. When she speaks, which is seldom, the temple goes quiet.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Valsuma is the High Priestess of the great fire-temple of [[affiliation-ataros|Ataros]] on the basin's edge at the [[place-oasishirvn|Oasis of Shirvan]], the temple the [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Atarzád]] raised from the stones of the Tellumi shrines after the Conquest, whose offering flame burns at the foot of the [[place-vurinkarvem|Vurinkarvem]] cliff. As its Shekhul she is answerable for its eternal flame and keeps the record of its fire-lineage, which begins with a lamp carried down from the [[place-unconsflame|Unconsuming Flame]]. She has served the god for sixty years.

The temple raised [[being-shrmnhthvn|Sharminah Tahvan]], a foundling pulled from a burned settlement, and Valsuma mentored her from the age of six. Now quite elderly and spending most of her time in deep meditation, she remains the highest authority in the temple and Sharminah's spiritual mother. She does not restrain Sharminah's zealotry, and the temple's senior priests take her silence for approval.

## Background

**The foundling.** Valsuma came to the temple as an infant with no lineage, a generation after the Conquest. The temple raises a child with no lineage as its own, and the name she was brought in with was the only thing she brought.

**The priestess.** She was ordained a Fire-Keeper at eighteen, a Delkoshekh at thirty and the temple's Shekhul at fifty-two, the first woman to hold the flame of Shirvan. Under her the temple has never let its fire fail, and she has carried [[miscgear-shirvanlamp|the lamp of its lineage]] up Mount Shofar to be relit every festival for twenty-six years.

**The present.** She has given the temple's daily work to its senior priests and keeps the night watch over the flame herself. Sharminah, whom the temple sends into the other held cities as its enforcer, comes back to Shirvan to kneel at her feet after every mission.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is austere, patient and very hard to read, with a warmth that she shows only to the temple's children. She believes in the discipline of the fire, which answers to correct technique and not to opinion, and she has come to believe less firmly in a great deal else.

### Motivation

She wants the flame kept and the temple's children raised well. She also wants, in her old age, an answer to the question her life has posed her, and she does not believe she will get one.

### Strengths

She commands the obedience of the most important fire-temple of the held cities, the reverence of the [Ozhmarod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} who hold Shirvan, and the love of the one warrior-priestess every heretic in the belt fears.

### Weaknesses

She is old and tired, and she has let the temple's senior priests and Sharminah act in her name for years. What they do in it, she has not stopped.

## Social

### Companions

- [[being-shrmnhthvn|Sharminah Tahvan]]: Her pupil and spiritual daughter, raised by the temple as she was. Valsuma loves her and is afraid for her, and of her.

### Others

- [[being-kenbeshay|Kenbeshay]]: The priest of the Northern Temple of Kethramír, whose teaching of mercy Sharminah despises. Valsuma has read his sermons, copied out by a Fire-Keeper, three times.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ataros|Faith of Ataros]]**: Shekhul of the great fire-temple of Shirvan, master of its fire and keeper of its fire-lineage.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The lamp.** Valsuma is too frail to carry the temple's lamp up Mount Shofar at the next festival, for the first time in twenty-six years. She asks a party of pilgrims to escort the priest who carries it in her place, because the senior priests disagree about who that should be.
2. **The audience.** A party seeking the temple's blessing for a caravan is brought before Valsuma at night. She asks them one question about the Tellumi quarter, listens to the answer, and gives the blessing.
3. **The successor.** The senior priests are already choosing her successor, and Sharminah is their favorite. Valsuma asks a party to find her a priest of the temple who would keep the flame and not the sword.

:::secret {title="The hidden writings"}
Valsuma was born in the Tellumi lower town of Shirvan to a Tellumi bondswoman, in the years when the survivors of the war were still held in bondage, and her mother left her at the temple door with her name pressed on a scrap of clay. Valsuma is a name of the Tellumi shape, and nobody in the temple has asked about it in seventy years. She has known where she was born since she was twelve, when an old Tellumi water-master recognized the clay and told her. She has kept the scrap all her life. She has never told the temple, and she has never visited the lower town.

In a chest in her cell are sixty years of writings in her own hand, suggesting that compassion and mercy might be compatible with serving Ataros, and that the god who warms the Flame-born warms the Tellumi too. She has never shown them to anyone. Sharminah may find them, and Valsuma has not decided whether she wants her to.

**Hook: the chest.** Valsuma asks a party to carry a sealed chest from her cell to [[being-kenbeshay|Kenbeshay]] at the Northern Temple of Kethramír, on the day after her death, and to tell Sharminah nothing. A senior priest who watches her cell has already noticed the party.
:::
