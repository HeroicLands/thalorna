# Appearance {#appearance}

Vurinai was a man who stood 6'0" tall and was of light build. The tablets that describe him give him light skin with a smooth complexion, black hair worn long and bound with a cord, brown eyes, and long-fingered hands that a later teller compared to a scribe's. They say he dressed in undyed linen so that no dye would spoil a scent, and that he smelled of nothing at all, which those who met him found stranger than any perfume.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Vurinai the Ascendant is a perfumer of the ancient age of [[place-swoasisbelt|Tellumel]], long before [The Breaking]{.name data-type="lore/history"}, whom the Tellumi perfumers style Saint. He is said to have made a fragrance so perfectly balanced that merely smelling it brought a state of spiritual clarity, and to have taken the formula with him when he went up into the mountains in his old age and did not come back. His name means _holy_, and the perfumers' guilds of the belt count their lineages from his pupils.

[[being-kharnhkhfr|Kharanah Khafur]], a perfumer of [[place-ashkarad|Ashkarad]], hears that fragments of his formula have surfaced in a crumbling monastery high in the [Kuran Mountains]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, and that the collector [[being-revunaykishn|Revunai Kishneshun]] is assembling an expedition to find them first.

## The Story

**The perfumer.** The tablets say Vurinai was born in a family of the old perfumers' quarter of Ashkarad and worked for forty years at the house of a prince, blending the oils of the court and the incense of the temples. They say that he could name the garden a rose was grown in by its smell, and the hour it was cut.

**The fragrance.** In his sixtieth year he made the fragrance the tablets call _the clear_. A prince who smelled it is said to have given up his throne; a priest who smelled it wept for a day and then taught for forty years. The tablets do not agree what was in it, and the perfumers of the belt have argued about it since.

**The ascent.** He went up into the mountains with two pupils and a mule carrying his oils, and the tablets say he ascended. His pupils came back without him and without the formula, and would say only that the master had kept it.

## Psyche

### Personality

The tellers make him patient, gentle and wholly absorbed in his work, a man who spoke little and listened to scents the way other men listen to music.

### Motivation

In every telling he wanted one thing: to make a fragrance that would let a person smell the world as the gods do.

### Strengths

His nose, his patience and his pupils, who founded the perfumers' lineages of the belt.

### Weaknesses

He trusted no one with the formula, not even the pupils he loved, and the tellers say that is why it was lost.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-kharnhkhfr|Kharanah Khafur]]: The perfumer of Ashkarad who counts her own lineage from his pupils and wants his formula found by a perfumer.
- [[being-revunaykishn|Revunai Kishneshun]]: The collector who wants it found by his own men.

### Affiliations

- He belongs to no living body. The perfumers' lineages of Ashkarad keep his name at the head of their rolls.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The pupils' tablets.** The perfumers' lineage Kharanah belongs to keeps a tablet said to be pressed by one of Vurinai's two pupils. The lineage's elder will let a party read it only if they promise to bring the formula to a perfumer and not a collector.
2. **The monastery.** The monastery in the Kuran Mountains is a ruin the herders of the high valleys avoid. A party who reaches it finds that Revunai's guides have been there first and left in a hurry.
3. **The scent on the road.** Traders on the belt road report a perfume being sold at the gates of Ashkarad, in tiny clay bottles, that the Teghumod factors call _the clear_. It smells of nothing at all.

:::secret {title="What the pupils kept"}
The pupils came back with the formula and lied. They split it in two before they came down, each keeping half in memory, so that the fragrance could not be made without both lineages agreeing, and they never agreed. One half is the tablet Kharanah's lineage keeps, which nobody living can read because it is written as a list of gardens and hours rather than oils. The other half went into the mountains with the second pupil's line, whose descendants kept the monastery, and the fragments that have surfaced there are its last copy.

Revunai does not know there are two halves. Kharanah's lineage does.

**Hook: the two halves.** A party that brings the monastery's fragments to Kharanah's lineage can make _the clear_, if they can learn to read a list of gardens as a recipe, and if the gardens still grow.
:::
