# Appearance {#appearance}

Sunhema Karvemun is a 51-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of medium build. She has tanned skin, sun-kissed and weathered, black hair bound up under a caravan-master's indigo wrap, and dark brown eyes set in the squint lines of thirty years on the road. A camel-bite scar marks her left forearm. In Mernagord she wears the good wool of the house; on the road she dresses exactly like her drovers. She is brisk, warm and loud, she shakes hands like a man, and she does a sum in her head faster than her clerk can write it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sunhema Karvemun is the head of the [Karvemun]{.name data-type="affiliation"}, a caravan house of [[place-mehrnagord|Mernagord]] that runs strings over [[place-wstrndscnt|the Western Descent]] to the Kingdom of Amradad and east along the belt road to the gates of the held cities. She is a kinswoman of the [[affiliation-hsemernagord|House of Mernagord]] by her mother and its Caravan-Master by appointment, which means the prince's salt goes west in her strings, and the western merchants call her the merchant princess.

She proposes a bold venture to the perfumer [[being-kharnhkhfr|Kharanah Khafur]] of Ashkarad: fund an expedition across the desert to a fabled land where unique plants are said to grow nowhere else in the world. The investment would transform Kharanah's business, and it carries a risk Sunhema describes with great honesty and some omissions.

## Background

**The road.** Sunhema was born into the caravan house and rode her first string at eleven, with her father, over the Descent. She ran strings of her own at twenty and has crossed the Descent more than a hundred times.

**The house.** She took the house at thirty-eight, when her elder brother lost a string of sixty camels and its drovers to a sandstorm and did not come home. She rebuilt it in six years on the salt trade.

**The present.** The road east is changing. Tolls at the held cities' gates have risen, the steppe is uneasy, and a caravan master who goes beyond the belt buys passage tribe by tribe. Sunhema wants a road nobody else has.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is bold, practical and cheerful, and she enjoys risk more than she admits. She is loyal to her drovers, generous with her table and ruthless in a bargain.

### Motivation

She wants the Karvemun to be the house that opened a road, and she wants to see the far side of the desert before she is too old to ride. She also needs money, more than she says.

### Strengths

She knows the western road better than anyone in Mernagord and the eastern road well enough. She has the prince's favor, the trust of her drovers, and contacts among the guides of the steppe.

### Weaknesses

The house is overextended. A failed expedition would ruin it, and she cannot afford to send one without someone else's money.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The proposal.** Sunhema asks a party to escort a sample of the fabled land's dried leaves from Mernagord to Kharanah in Ashkarad, through a Teghumod gate that taxes every bale.
2. **The guide.** The only guide who claims to know the way to the fabled land is a man of the deep sands who sells water knowledge and nothing else. Sunhema wants a party to ride with him for a week and judge whether he is honest.
3. **The salt string.** A string of the prince's salt has been held at a Teghumod gate on a charge of a false toll mark. Sunhema wants the string released before the Kingdom buyer leaves.

:::secret {title="The house's debts"}
The Karvemun owe a Vedyaran moneylender of Mernagord more than the house is worth. Sunhema borrowed to rebuild it after her brother's loss, and the salt trade has never quite paid the interest. The lender has given her until the spring market. If the expedition is funded, she means to repay him from Kharanah's money before a camel leaves the city.

The leaves she has are real. She bought them at Qiiso from a guide of the deep sands, who said they came from very far east, and she does not know whether the land they grew in is real or how far beyond Qiiso it lies. She means to find out on someone else's money, and she believes she will.

**Hook: the lender.** The Vedyaran lender hires a party to learn whether the expedition is real before he lets the spring market pass.
:::
