# Appearance {#appearance}

Narvuna of Kethramír is a 53-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of light build, with a scholar's stoop at the shoulders. She has light skin lined at the eyes and mouth, gray hair worn short under the plain cap of the Shekhulukhem, and gray eyes that rarely blink. Between her brows is the small blue dot of a Delkoshekh's ordination. She dresses in the unbleached robe of a teacher-priest with nothing of her rank on it but the house's sun worked in thread at the shoulder. She speaks slowly, as if dictating, and she stops a speaker who is wrong with a raised finger and then lets him finish.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Narvuna of Kethramír is the head of the [[affiliation-hsekethramir|House of Kethramír]], the priest-house of the [[affiliation-khzrncnfdrtn|Tellumi Confederation]], in exile in the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]]. She is also a Delkoshekh of the [Shekhulukhem]{.name data-type="affiliation"}, ordained after nineteen years of study, and she teaches law and theology at the Tellumi school of [[place-shebarakhend|Shebarakhend]] three mornings a week. Her court is a house beside the school, and the two are hard to tell apart.

She is the most rigorous of the exile theologians, and her best-known work, _[On the Ungrantable Land]{.name data-type="lore/literature"}_, is the formal refutation of the Atarzád Promise: that no god, [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]] included, can give one people the land of another. It is required reading in Tellumi religious education and contraband in Atarzád territory, and a copy passes through [[place-kethramir|Kethramír]] in every caravan from the west, hidden in a bale of cloth.

## Background

**The priest-house.** Narvuna was born in Shebarakhend into the house's senior line, the daughter of a Delkoshekh and a copyist. Her house took the worst of the killing of the clergy in [The Breaking]{.name data-type="lore/history"}, and the house's children learn its losses by rote, recited without a change of voice. She entered the Shekhulukhem's schooling at nine and was ordained at twenty-eight.

**The book.** She wrote _On the Ungrantable Land_ in her thirties, after a [Tozhirod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} scholar's defense of the Promise reached the school in a merchant's luggage. She wrote it for Atarzád readers as much as Tellumi ones, in the plainest Tellumi she could write, and she has rewritten its third chapter twice in answer to Tozhirod replies.

**The house.** She became its head at forty-four, when her elder brother died. She has no children; her heir is a nephew of nineteen in the school.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is severe, honest and dry, with a teacher's habit of asking a question she already knows the answer to. She has no small talk and does not miss it. She is fair to an opponent's argument, and she quotes it more exactly than he does.

### Motivation

She wants Kethramír back, and she wants the argument won first. She counts it a victory each time her book is read on the far side of the line, because a people that cannot answer it will in time stop believing the Promise, and a people that has stopped believing will give the city back.

### Strengths

She has the Shekhulukhem's learning, the reputation of the Confederation's best theologian, and readers among the Tozhirod who argue with her by letter. Her students are in every Tellumi school of the Kingdom and the ports.

### Weaknesses

Her book is a reason to burn things. The preacher [Aqeshat]{.name data-type="being/npc"} the Prophet calls for the Tellumi to be expelled from the held cities, and her followers burn the book where they find it, and sometimes the house it is found in. Narvuna knows that the bale of cloth is carried by people who can be hanged for it, and she has never carried one.

## Social

### Enemies

- [Aqeshat]{.name data-type="being/npc"}: The young Atarzád prophet whose followers burn the book. Narvuna has read everything Aqeshat is reported to have said, and she believes Aqeshat means it.

### Others

- [[being-mureshashirv|Muresha of Shirvan]]: The First of the Seven and her friend. Muresha argues the deeds and Narvuna argues the faith, and they read each other's drafts.
- [[being-nartemayusht|Nartemai of Ushtra-ber]]: The prince of the eastern oasis, whose archive keeps the oldest forms in the belt. He has granted her access twice, and she thinks him the most learned prince at the Council and the hardest to like.
- [[being-sighotornozh|Sighotor son of Nozhikor]]: The Toqel of the Atarzád. They have never met.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The book and the answer.** A Tozhirod scholar in Kethramír wants a copy of _On the Ungrantable Land_ in order to refute it, and a Delkoshekh of the Tellumi quarter wants the scholar's refutation in order to refute that. Narvuna pays a party to carry both ways, and the scholar pays them not to tell her what he wrote.
2. **The burned house.** Aqeshat's followers have burned a house in the Tellumi quarter of [[place-oasishirvn|Shirvan]] where a copy was found. The family has fled to [[place-mehrnagord|Mernagord]] and asked the house for shelter. Narvuna sends a party to bring them on to Shebarakhend before the Atarzád ask Mernagord for them.
3. **The bale.** A cloth merchant who carries the book east has stopped answering letters. Narvuna wants to know whether he has been taken, and whether he has talked.

:::secret {title="The Toqel's question"}
The unsigned letter from [[being-sighotornozh|Sighotor son of Nozhikor]] asked one question: _if a god cannot grant a land, what did the Flame promise?_ Narvuna has not answered it. She has written eleven answers and burned them all, because each one either gives the Atarzád a promise she cannot admit or takes from Ataros a power her own faith says he has. She has not told Muresha about the letter.

She suspects the letter was meant to be found. A Toqel who corresponds with the author of the book the Atarzád burn is a Toqel the strict could repudiate, and the old man is dying. If she answers, she may give him something he means to say to the Council before he dies, or she may give his enemies the letter that ends him.

**Hook: the courier.** A Burqalod keeper in pilgrim's gray comes to Narvuna's school asking for her answer, and says the Toqel has a month. A second man asking the same question at the school gate the next morning is one of Aqeshat's.
:::
