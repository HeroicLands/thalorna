# Appearance {#appearance}

Nartemay of Ushtra-ber is a 50-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of light build. He has light skin chapped and weathered by the wind off the Eastern Reach, black hair and a close-cropped beard both going grey, a long jaw and dark brown eyes. The tip of his right little finger is gone, lost to frost on the upper terraces as a boy. He dresses in the dark green of the green peak's house, cut as it was cut a century ago, and sits very straight on a plain stone seat. He says nothing to a petitioner until the Keeper of the Tablets has read the petition aloud, and then says only what he has decided.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nartemay of Ushtra-ber is the ruling prince of [[place-ushtraber|Ushtra-ber]], the last founded of the seven Tellumi cities, at the far eastern end of [[place-swoasisbelt|Tellumel]] where the belt narrows against the foot of [[place-estrnreach|the Eastern Reach]]. His [[affiliation-hseushtraber|house]] governs about twenty thousand people in the city and perhaps seventy thousand more in the terraced villages of its valley, with a household guard of fifty men.

His court keeps ceremonial forms forgotten everywhere else in the belt. A petitioner kneels at a marked place on the floor and says nothing until the Keeper of the Tablets has read the petition aloud, and every decision is pressed in clay before the petitioner leaves the room. A stranger who presents himself as he would at an exile court in Amradad is sent back to the door to do the whole ceremony again. Nartemay hears every petition himself, from a water dispute between two terraces to a foreign scholar's request to read the archive, and he has never delegated one.

## Background

**The valley.** Nartemay grew up on the upper terraces, where the house's heirs are sent at ten to keep the summer watch with the herders' sons, and lost the fingertip there in an early frost. He came down at sixteen and sat behind his father's seat for twenty years, learning the forms by watching.

**The prince.** He succeeded at thirty-six. In his first year he restored three forms of the court his father had let lapse, and dismissed a Keeper of the Tablets for reading a petition in the wrong order. The valley thinks him hard and fair, and the second word matters more there than the first.

**The east.** The house's real business is the valley and the east: the [[affiliation-hvarnguides|Hvarn]] who come down from the Reach each autumn to buy grain, and the few caravans that rest a week in the city before the long crossing to the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Tantsou frontier]]. He buys their news as carefully as their goods.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is exact, reserved and proud, and he believes that the forms are not decoration but the substance of a court: a prince who keeps them is bound by them, and a prince who drops them is bound by nothing. He is kind to children and to the very old, and impatient with everyone between.

### Motivation

He wants Ushtra-ber left alone, and so far it has been. Beyond that he wants the belt to remember what it was, and he counts the forms of his court as the one part of the old Tellumel that the Breaking did not touch.

### Strengths

His valley is easy to hold and far from the Atarzád, whose eastern tribe, the [Tozhirod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}, find his archive more interesting than his land. His court's archive keeps ritual and legal practice the rest of the belt has lost, and the scholars of Amradad and Kaldar want it.

### Weaknesses

He has made few friends at the Council and wants none. His insistence on form has set him against the head of the [[affiliation-hsevahurdash|House of Vahurdash]], and the exile houses resent being told that their ceremonial has slipped in ninety years of foreign hospitality.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-khorsadvahur|Khorsád-Vahur]]: The head of the House of Vahurdash, who holds his title by acclamation. Nartemay is the most exacting of those who say an acclamation cannot make a prince, and he has refused to stand when Khorsád-Vahur enters the Council's hall.

### Others

- [[being-narvunakethr|Narvuna of Kethramír]]: The priest-house's head, whom he has granted access to the archive twice. He respects her learning and suspects her of preferring argument to form.
- [[being-mureshashirv|Muresha of Shirvan]]: The First of the Seven, whose case he thinks sound and whose court he thinks careless.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-hseushtraber|House of Ushtra-ber]]**: Prince of Ushtra-ber, ruling the city and its valley.
- **[[affiliation-cnclprinces|Council of the Princes]]**: a seated prince, who speaks about form.
- **[[affiliation-khzrncnfdrtn|Tellumi Confederation]]**: a ruling prince of a house.
- **[[affiliation-zurathra|Faith of Zúrathra]]**: one of the faithful, who lights the single lamp at the valley's spring on the first night of each year.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The old forms.** A scholar from Amradad or [[place-caldar|Kaldar]] wants copies from the archive. Nartemay grants access to those who learn the forms and earn his respect, and refuses everyone else, so the scholar wants a party to earn it for him.
2. **The Hvarn's news.** The Hvarn who came down this autumn told the court of strangers on the Reach who paid in a coin nobody in the valley knows. Nartemay wants a party to go up with the Hvarn and see the coin's owners for him.
3. **The stream.** In a warm summer the stream from the glacier rises without warning. The Watchman of the Upper Terraces has not sounded his horn this year, and he has not come down for his pay.

:::secret {title="The treaty of the first seat"}
The archive of Ushtra-ber holds the court's copies of the treaties between the seven cities, the records the [[affiliation-hseshirvan|House of Shirvan]] wants most for its case and believes burned with Shirvan. One of them dates the custom nobody at the Council can date. It is a treaty of the seven, pressed in a year of the old king-lists, by which the other six princes gave the prince of Shirvan the first word at their meetings in return for the spring's water in a drought. The first seat is not as old as the houses. It was granted, and what six princes granted by treaty, six princes can revoke.

Nartemay has read it and told no one. He believes in the first seat because it is the form, and he will not be the prince who breaks a form with a tablet. He also knows that [[being-hemvanaymern|Hemvanay of Mernagord]], who leads the west against Shirvan in the argument over the coming down, would use the treaty to take the first word from Shirvan the moment he learned of it, and that the Council would not survive the session.

**Hook: the copyist.** A copyist of the court has made a fair copy of the treaty without leave and is trying to sell it in the caravan yard of [[place-vahurdash|Vahurdash]], where an agent of a rival house buys tablets. Nartemay wants the copy back and the copyist brought to his court, where the forms will be observed. [[being-mureshashirv|Muresha of Shirvan]] has heard that a treaty tablet is for sale and sends her own buyer.
:::
