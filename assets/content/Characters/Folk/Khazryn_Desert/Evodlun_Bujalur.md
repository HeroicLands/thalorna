# Appearance {#appearance}

Evodlun Bujalur is a woman of about thirty-eight who serves a guest's salt and tea herself and sits at the side of the fire nearest the door. She listens to every question put to her husband and does not hide that she does.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Householder of the [[affiliation-bjlrtrb|Bujalur]]. Keeps the camp head's tent.**

Evodlun Bujalur is the wife of [[being-bujalyitboq|Bujalyitboq Bujalur]], head of the easternmost Bujalur winter camp in the foothills of the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]]. She keeps his tent, its herds and its guests' welcome.

## Background

**The tent.** A Bujalur householder holds a tent's herds and speaks for them in the camp. She speaks for her husband's tent when he is out with the horses, and the camp's women bring their quarrels to her fire.

**Now.** The March's officers are counting the camps, and her husband keeps his riders home and his camp apart.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is direct, quick and hospitable, and she says what she thinks to a guest who has eaten at her fire.

### Motivation

She wants her children to winter on the foothill grass, and the camp kept under the truce.

### Strengths

The camp's women trust her, and she hears everything said at her husband's fire.

### Weaknesses

She cannot keep a guest's question unanswered when the answer worries her.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-bujalyitboq|Bujalyitboq Bujalur]]: Her husband, head of the easternmost camp.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-bjlrtrb|Bujalur]]**: householder of a tent of the easternmost camp.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The guest's welcome.** A stranger who comes to the easternmost camp is fed at her fire before her husband has decided whether to receive him.
2. **The women's quarrel.** Two families of the camp dispute a mare, and she wants a witness the League will believe that the mare was bred in the camp.

:::secret {title="What she knows"}
She knows that the riders of the man gathering the northern tribes ate her husband's salt at the end of summer and offered him herds, and she fears what the custom asks of a camp that broke the truce. Asked about those riders as a guest, she answers before he can: [[scenario-trcecmpsmrch|The Truce Camps of the March]].

**Hook: the answer at the fire.** A guest who asks about the riders who came at the end of summer hears her answer first.
:::
