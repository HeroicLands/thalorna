# Appearance {#appearance}

Nimeshai of Ashkarad is a 58-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of heavy build. He has olive skin kept smooth with oil, black hair going gray at the temples and oiled back from a broad forehead, and brown eyes that are quick and amused. He dresses better than any other exile prince, in Kingdom silks dyed the deep blue of the old Ashkarad glaze, and wears a heavy silver ring on each thumb and none on his fingers. He walks with a slight limp on the right and does not hurry it. He laughs easily, offers a guest food before business, and remembers what the guest ate the last time.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nimeshai of Ashkarad is the heir-in-exile of the [[affiliation-hseashkarad|House of Ashkarad]], the craft-house of the [[affiliation-khzrncnfdrtn|Tellumi Confederation]], and the richest of the four exile princes. His court sits in [[place-shebarakhend|Shebarakhend]], the court city of the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]], in a house on the market side of the city with a counting room on the ground floor and the court above it, and the two floors are not entirely separate.

He grew rich on the trade of the [[place-vylarianse|Vilérian Sea]]: Ashkarad's tools, harness and glazed jars carried west through Kingdom ports, and cloth, glass and metals carried back. He spends the money in two places. Some goes, quietly, into Tellumi agitation in the held cities. More goes back to [[place-ashkarad|Ashkarad]] as loans to craft families who would be ruined by a [Teghumod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} price, since a family sells its work to a Teghumod factor at the price the factor sets. Everybody in the old quarter of Ashkarad knows whose silver it is, and the families take it anyway. A workshop that owes Nimeshai is a workshop that stands to the house when the house asks.

## Background

**The house.** Nimeshai was born in Shebarakhend to a younger line of the house, and he was never meant to head it. He went into the Kingdom's coastal trade at sixteen as a factor's clerk, and he was a shipowner at thirty. When the last heir-in-exile died without children, the elders of the house chose the cousin who could pay its debts over the cousins who could recite its genealogy better, and the court has been solvent since.

**The limp.** A bale fell on him in a warehouse at the coast when he was twenty-two. He tells the story at dinner, and the moral he draws from it changes with the company.

**The present.** He keeps a seat at the [[affiliation-cnclprinces|Council of the Princes]] and does not often speak in it. He pays for things instead: the copyists of the House of Shirvan's library once, the schools of the Tellumi quarter in Shebarakhend every year, and, by an arrangement nobody at the Council discusses, the young [[being-gureshayzarg|Gureshai of Zargandur]]'s horses.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is genial, generous and curious, and he enjoys being liked. He is also patient in the way of a man who has lent money for forty years: he does not ask for a thing he can buy, and he does not buy a thing he can be given. He loves the craft of Ashkarad more than the city, and he can talk for an hour about a glaze.

### Motivation

He wants Ashkarad back with its workshops whole: the craft councils solvent, their masters alive and their secrets kept, so that the city the house recovers is still the city it lost. Until then he wants the craft families to owe the house, because a family that owes the house has not forgotten it.

### Strengths

He has money, ships, a counting house with factors in three Kingdom ports and the gratitude of half the old quarter of Ashkarad. He is well liked at the Kingdom's court, where a Tellumi prince who pays his bills is a rarer thing than a Tellumi prince with a genealogy.

### Weaknesses

His loans are a pattern, and the Teghumod have noticed it. Every coin he sends into Ashkarad travels through hands the Teghumod can watch. His money is made on the sea trade, and a bad year on the coast reaches the craft families of Ashkarad within a season.

## Social

### Enemies

- [Teghumod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}: The merchant tribe of the Atarzád, who hold Ashkarad and its gates. He trades with Teghumod houses in the Kingdom's ports and dislikes nothing about them except that they hold his city. They have noticed the loans, and they need the craft families' goods more than they need a quarrel.

### Others

- [[being-mureshashirv|Muresha of Shirvan]]: The First of the Seven, whose library he paid to have copied once. He admires her and finds her exhausting.
- [[being-gureshayzarg|Gureshai of Zargandur]]: The young heir whose horses he pays for. Nimeshai has not decided whether he is buying a reclamation or a reason for the young man not to attempt one.
- [[being-hemvanaymern|Hemvanai of Mernagord]]: The prince of the largest free oasis, whose salt he would carry if Hemvanai asked. Hemvanai has not asked.
- [[being-kharnhkhfr|Kharanah Khafur]]: A perfumer of Ashkarad who buys Kingdom oils through his factors. He thinks her the best nose in the belt.

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **The loan.** A glaze-maker's family in the old quarter of Ashkarad needs Nimeshai's silver before the next Teghumod bid, or it sells its kilns. Carrying the silver into the quarter is easy to arrange and hard to do unseen.
2. **The handle.** The house's bronze adze needs a new handle from a palm of the house's own groves below the walls of Ashkarad. Nimeshai pays a party to cut a length of palm on Teghumod ground and bring it to Shebarakhend without a toll mark on it.
3. **The blue glaze.** A craft council of Ashkarad says the formula for the deep blue glaze died with a master in the war. A trader in the Kingdom's ports is selling jars glazed that blue. Nimeshai wants to know where the jars are made, and who taught the maker.

:::secret {title="The agitators' money"}
The money Nimeshai sends into the held cities for agitation is less than the Council believes and goes somewhere the Council would not approve. Most of it pays informers inside the Teghumod counting-houses of Ashkarad, who tell him which craft families the factors mean to break next, so that his loans arrive the week before the bid. The rest he sends to the Tellumi quarter of [[place-zargandur|Zargandur]] by way of Gureshai's men, and he has told himself it buys the quarter bread.

It buys the reclamation arms. Gureshai's men have been carrying Nimeshai's silver into Zargandur for two years and spending it on bows, and Nimeshai half knows it. He has not asked, because a man who asks has to decide. If the Teghumod trace the silver from his counting room to a cache of bows in Zargandur, the Atarzád have a reason to search every Tellumi workshop in Ashkarad, and he will have lent the craft families into a rising he never voted for.

**Hook: the factor's ledger.** A Teghumod factor in a Kingdom port has bought a ledger page from one of Nimeshai's clerks. Nimeshai hires a party to buy it back before the factor learns what the column headed _horses_ pays for.
:::
