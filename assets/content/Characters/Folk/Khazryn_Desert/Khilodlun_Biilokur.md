# Appearance {#appearance}

Khilodlun Biilokur is a 54-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build. She has tanned skin weathered by the northern grass, dark brown hair worn in one thick plait wound with a red cord, and brown eyes that crease when she is pleased. A small blue banner is tattooed on the back of her right hand, the mark of the family she was born to, and she is missing an upper tooth from a fall off a horse at twelve. She wears a long riding coat of gray felt, good boots and no gold at all. She talks to everyone at a fire, the herd-boys first, and she laughs before she bargains.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Khilodlun Biilokur is the dovaq of the [[affiliation-jbzrtrb|Jobizur]], the largest of the [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribes that share the central steppe, who hold the northern grass of the [[place-hskrrgn|Ghoshiqor]] and more than a dozen wells. She winters with the moot at [Ekon]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the deepest of the tribe's stone wells, in the lee of a black mesa on the southern grass, and summers with the camps on the high grass toward the forest of [[place-gwtkrrgn|Ghotaqor]].

She was born to a family of the [Biilokur]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}, the _banner people_, a small tribe of the north grass that came in to the Jobizur in a bad winter of her grandmother's youth. The Jobizur let a taken-in family keep the tribe-name it arrived with for as long as it chooses, and the Biilokur have kept theirs. Hers is the first such family to give the tribe a dovaq. She consults the shaman [[being-rznshmrvrn|Razanash Mervaran]] before the elders, and the elders let her, because the herds have been fat in every year of her office.

## Background

**The girl.** Khilodlun grew up as a taken-in child, which on the Jobizur grass means a child who must be twice as useful. She rode the borders as a scout at fourteen, ran the tribe's fur party to the forest bands at twenty-five, and came back each autumn with more sable than the party before her.

**The dovaq.** The elders chose her at forty-six, when the last dovaq died in a hard spring, because she had kept the forest bands' trust for twenty years and because no family of the old lines could agree on a man. She has held the office eight years.

**The present.** Under her the Jobizur have grown by receiving. Three Koserur families who left in the last bad year found land, herds and a seat in the moot waiting for them, and she is in quiet talks with the [[affiliation-ksrrtrb|Koserur]] dovaq, [[being-ghotazkharu|Ghotazkharu Koserur]], about a protection that would end the Koserur's independence in all but name.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is warm, shrewd and hard to hurry. She enjoys people, remembers their children's names, and enjoys winning, and she does not see why a person should choose. She is proud of being taken-in and says so to anyone who seems to think less of it.

### Motivation

She wants the Jobizur strong enough that no drought, raider or forest winter can break them, and she believes a tribe grows strong by being the place families go when they are in trouble. She also wants the old families of the tribe to admit that a Biilokur was the right choice.

### Strengths

The Jobizur have water, grass and numbers, and under her they have friends: the forest bands, the caravans of the northern branch, and every family the tribe has taken in. The moot trusts her judgment because it has been right, and Razanash's counsel is the best on the steppe.

### Weaknesses

The other tribes of the central steppe count her dependents as closely as she does, and to them a tribe that takes in vassals one family at a time resembles an orghun before he has a banner. Her family's name, the banner people, does not help. Some of the old Jobizur lines have never forgiven the elders for passing them over.

## Social

### Companions

- [[being-rznshmrvrn|Razanash Mervaran]]: A shaman of the tribe and its best-known healer and diviner in the south. Khilodlun has granted her considerable freedom in her shamanic work and consults her on every matter of tribal importance.
- [[being-tijodorquns|Tijodorghun Savaghur]]: The eldest shaman of the tribe's circle, who taught Razanash and who reads the weather for every move of the camps.

### Others

- [[being-ghotazkharu|Ghotazkharu Koserur]]: The Koserur dovaq, whose terms she is weighing. She likes him and does not trust his warband.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-jbzrtrb|Jobizur]]**: dovaq of the tribe, speaking last at its moot.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The witness.** Khilodlun wants a witness from outside the steppe to the spoken terms of the Koserur protection, because the Koserur trust no witness from another tribe. A witness who hears the terms becomes a party to them, and the Koserur warband will want to know what was said.
2. **The fur party.** The Jobizur fur party that went north to the forest bands this summer is a month overdue. Its leader carried the tribe's salt and the dovaq's gift to a band that has never failed to send the party home.
3. **The old line.** A son of one of the old Jobizur lines has begun to say at the fires that a dovaq who takes in a whole tribe is building a banner of her own. Khilodlun wants to know who he is talking to.

:::secret {title="The son in the east"}
A rider has come to Ekon twice from the man who is gathering the steppe tribes into a union, offering the Jobizur first place among his tribes and a marriage for Khilodlun's daughter to his son. Khilodlun told the elders she refused, and she did. She also sent her own son east with the rider the second time, as a guest of the union's camp, to learn the man's terms, the size of his following and whether he can be stopped.

Her son has not come back, and the season he was given is over. She does not know whether he is a guest, a hostage or sworn. The Koserur protection she is building is meant, in her own mind, as a wall: a central steppe united under the Jobizur can refuse a union, or name its price for joining one. She has not decided which.

**Hook: the guest.** Khilodlun asks a party to ride east to the union's camp as her envoys, ask for her son by name and bring him home, and to see on the way what the man has gathered.
:::
