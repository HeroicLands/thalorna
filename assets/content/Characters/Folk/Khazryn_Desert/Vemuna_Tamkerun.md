# Appearance {#appearance}

Vemuna Tamkerun is a 69-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of medium build, with a smith's heavy shoulders still under the shawl. She has light skin with a sun-kissed complexion, grey hair cropped short, and amber eyes that look at a piece of work before they look at the person holding it. Both forearms are freckled with small burn scars from fifty years at the forge. She is deaf in the left ear and turns her head to hear, and she wears a leather apron over her good clothes even in retirement. She is short with everyone and quickly kind to anyone who asks a real question.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Vemuna Tamkerun is a retired master armorer of the Tellumi quarter of [[place-kethramir|Kethramír]], the High Artisan of the [Kethramír Artisans' Guild]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"} for twenty years, and the teacher of most of the quarter's living smiths. For forty years her workshop made mail, lamellar and helmets for the warrior society of the [Tozhirod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}, the Atarzád tribe that holds the city, because the Tellumi of a held city may bear no arms and may make them for their masters.

She recognized the dexterity and creative instinct of the twelve-year-old [[being-afzndhprnzr|Afzandah Parnazar]] and took her as an apprentice. She takes pride in her former student's accomplishments and occasionally directs difficult technical problems to her for consultation, and she tells everyone that Afzandah's ornament is a waste of good steel.

## Background

**The forge.** Vemuna was born in the quarter to a family of coppersmiths, as her surname says, and was apprenticed to an armorer at eleven. She was a master at twenty-six, and she took the High Artisan's seat at forty-nine.

**The society's armorer.** The Tozhirod society's quartermaster came to her workshop every spring for forty years with a list, and she filled it. She knows the measurements of three generations of Tozhirod warriors, and their fathers' before them.

**The present.** She gave up the forge at sixty-five when her hands began to shake. She lives above her old workshop, which a former apprentice runs, and goes down every morning to criticize.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is gruff, blunt and impatient, and she means very little of her rudeness. She values good work above every other thing, and she has no opinion about anything that cannot be forged, which she will tell you at length.

### Motivation

She wants the quarter's craft to outlast her: the secrets kept, the apprentices trained and the work done well whoever wears it.

### Strengths

She knows more about armor than anyone in the belt, and every smith in the quarter owes her something. The Tozhirod quartermaster trusts her.

### Weaknesses

She is old, half deaf and her hands shake. She has made arms for the people who hold her city for forty years, and some of the young men of the quarter have not forgiven her.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The master's piece.** A young smith's master's piece is before the guild, and Vemuna has called it flawed. The smith's family hires a party to find out whether she is right or merely old.
2. **The quartermaster's list.** The Tozhirod quartermaster has brought a list twice as long as any in forty years. Vemuna's former apprentice wants to know why, and Vemuna already suspects.
3. **The commission.** A stranger asks Vemuna to repair a helmet of the old Kethramír princes' guard, dented and very old. She asks a party to find out where he found it before she touches it.

:::secret {title="The cellar"}
For forty years Vemuna has kept the offcuts of the Tozhirod society's commissions, and for forty years she has forged them, at night, into small plain things: spearheads, arrowheads, the plates of a hundred brigandines. They are under the floor of her old workshop, in a cellar her former apprentice does not know he is standing on.

They are not for a reclamation. She has never believed in one. They are for the night the preacher Aqeshat's followers come to expel the quarter, which Vemuna has expected since she was a girl, and she means the quarter to have something in its hands when they do.

**Hook: the apprentice's floor.** Her former apprentice is having the workshop floor relaid. Vemuna asks a party to move the cellar's contents before the masons come, and to tell no one in the quarter, including the young men who would use them tomorrow.
:::
