# Appearance {#appearance}

Bujalqiimod Bujalur is a man of about fifty-five in a felt coat, older-looking than his years after thirty winters in the foothills. He stands at the officer's elbow when the camps are counted and answers in the League's tongue, slowly and one question at a time.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Dovaq of the [[affiliation-bjlrtrb|Bujalur]]. Holds the tribe's truce with the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]].**

Bujalqiimod Bujalur is the dovaq of the Bujalur, a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe that summers on the grass of the southern [[place-wlqtrrgn|Volketor]] and winters in the foothills of the League's [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]] under its truce. His name means _seal-silent_. The camp heads chose him at [Tuvaq]{.name data-type="place/site"}, the tribe's summer spring, and he answers to the League for every camp that comes in under the truce.

## Background

**The horse-seller.** He has sold horses to the March at the outer caravanserai-forts for thirty winters, and he learned the League's tongue at the horse fairs. The garrison's officers know his face, and some of them have bought every remount they ride from him.

**The dovaq.** As dovaq he holds the truce in the tribe's name. Each autumn his truce-rider swears the camps in at the outer caravanserais, and each spring he takes them back out onto the grass before the March's herds go up to the spring grass. A camp that breaks the truce is put out of the winter ground by its own tribe, before the League can do it, and the judgment is his and the camp heads'.

**Now.** Word has come down the road that a dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes, and the Lord Commander's officers are counting the Bujalur camps, the riders in each and the horses they hold. He knows what the count is for, and he rides from camp to camp ahead of the officers so that every camp head hears it from him first.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is patient, tired and courteous, and he says less than he knows. He answers the question asked and waits for the next one.

### Motivation

He wants the truce kept and the foothill grass his tribe's next winter, because it is the only winter grass the Bujalur have.

### Strengths

The March trusts his word, and the camp heads trust his judgment. He knows every officer of the outer forts who has bought a horse from him, and what each of them paid.

### Weaknesses

The young riders who want the camps out of the March before the League turns them out do not wait for his word.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[affiliation-grnrtrb|Gorenur]]: The raiding tribe of the Hek'ar edge, at feud with the Bujalur since the March burned a Bujalur camp for a Gorenur raid.

### Others

- [[being-mazitosrekha|Mazitos Rekhazur]]: The Lord Commander of the Eastern March, who has ordered the count.
- [[being-bujalyitboq|Bujalyitboq Bujalur]]: Head of the easternmost camp, polite and closed, who keeps his riders home.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-bjlrtrb|Bujalur]]**: dovaq, chosen by the camp heads, holder of the truce with the League.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Beside the truce-rider.** He wants someone the March trusts to stand beside his truce-rider when the officers come to count the camps, because the count is the first step of something: [[scenario-trcecmpsmrch|The Truce Camps of the March]].
2. **The boys' horses.** Bujalur boys hold good horses their fathers did not breed, and some carry the March's brand. He wants to know who sold them before a patrol asks.
3. **The camp heads' judgment.** If a camp has broken the truce, the camp heads must put it out of the winter ground themselves. He wants a witness the League will believe when the tribe has done it.
