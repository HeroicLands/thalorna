# Appearance {#appearance}

Kenbeshai is a 49-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. He has olive skin with a smooth complexion, black hair cut close, a short black beard and brown eyes that are steady and kind. Between his brows is the blue dot of a Delkoshekh's ordination, and the backs of both hands are fine-scarred from forty years of tending temple fires. He wears the red-brown robe of a priest of the Sacred Flame, patched at the elbows, and his sleeves smell of smoke and of the barley soup the temple gives out at dusk. He has a soft voice that carries surprisingly well across a crowded court.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kenbeshai is a Delkoshekh, a teacher-priest, of the Northern Temple, the one fire-temple of [[affiliation-ataros|Ataros]] that stands in the Tellumi quarter of [[place-kethramir|Kethramír]]. In the held cities the Tellumi may worship no god in the open but Ataros, so the Northern Temple is the one place in the quarter where a Tellumi may pray aloud. It keeps the fire, feeds the poor of the quarter at dusk and lodges the sick in its back court.

He preaches that the Sacred Flame warms before it burns: that Ataros asks for mercy as he asks for the first fruits, and that a fire kept without compassion is only smoke. The [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Atarzád]] magistrates of the [Tozhirod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} tolerate him because his temple keeps the quarter quiet. The warrior-priestess [[being-shrmnhthvn|Sharminah Tahvan]] despises his teaching as weakness in faith, and she would destroy him to keep it from spreading.

## Background

**The priest.** Kenbeshai was born in the quarter to a family of lamp-makers and entered the Shekhulukhem's schooling at ten. He was ordained at twenty-nine and came to the Northern Temple as a Fire-Keeper's teacher, and he has served it twenty years.

**The break.** He broke with the orthodox teaching of the strict temples fourteen years ago, when the last Tellumi rising in the Shirvan district was put down and the temple's back court filled with the wounded of both peoples. He tended them all, and preached the next feast day on the god who warms. He has preached it since.

**The present.** His temple feeds more of the quarter every season, and the Tozhirod magistrate has twice asked him, politely, to feed fewer. The poet [[being-kamdkhvndr|Kamid Khavandar]] sleeps in its back court when the City Guard drives him from the streets.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is gentle, humorous and unafraid, and he is more stubborn than he looks. He listens to anyone, argues with nobody in public, and does not change his mind. He likes the poor of the quarter and is liked by them.

### Motivation

He wants the fire kept, the hungry fed and the quarter's people treated as people by both its masters, the Atarzád magistrate and the Tellumi elders. He believes that if the god he serves is the god both peoples share, then that god is the one place they might meet.

### Strengths

The quarter loves him, the magistrate finds him useful, and the Shekhulukhem respects his learning. His temple's back court is the best hospital in the quarter.

### Weaknesses

His teaching makes enemies on both sides: the strict of the Atarzád think it soft, and the militant of the Tellumi think it surrender. He has no protection but his usefulness.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-shrmnhthvn|Sharminah Tahvan]]: The warrior-priestess of the strict temple, who has preached against him by name.

### Others

- [[being-kamdkhvndr|Kamid Khavandar]]: The vagrant poet who sleeps in the temple's back court. Kenbeshai likes his poems.
- [[being-kireshay|Kireshai]]: The captain of the quarter's watch, who drives the beggars from the streets and lets them sleep at the temple because there they are off the streets.

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **The soup.** The magistrate has ordered the temple to feed no more than a hundred at dusk. Two hundred come. Kenbeshai asks a party to help him decide who goes hungry, knowing he will not.
2. **The sermon.** Sharminah Tahvan has come to Kethramír and asked to preach at the Northern Temple on the next feast day. Kenbeshai has said yes, and he asks a party to stand in the court while she speaks.
3. **The wounded.** A wounded Atarzád fire-warden and a wounded Tellumi boy are brought to the back court on the same night, from the same fight. Each one's kin want to know what the other said.

:::secret {title="What the Northern Temple keeps"}
The altar of the Northern Temple is crowded on every feast day of every god the Tellumi may not name because those feast days are kept there. The temple's Fire-Keepers light the altar flame on the day of [[lore-zurathradty|Zúrathra]], of [[lore-ahurdaendty|Ahurdaen]] and of the rest, as the shared priesthood of the faith is trained to do, and the prayers said into the fire of Ataros on those days are said to other gods. Kenbeshai has kept the practice for fourteen years. The magistrate does not know, and the Tellumi elders of the quarter do.

If it is proved, the temple is closed, the quarter loses the one place it may pray aloud, and Kenbeshai is hanged. Sharminah suspects it. She has asked to preach on the next feast day because the next feast day is Zúrathra's.

**Hook: the feast day.** Kenbeshai asks a party to find a reason, before dawn of the feast, for the Fire-Keepers to light the altar for Ataros alone, without the quarter learning that the temple was afraid.
:::
