# Appearance {#appearance}

Revunay Kishneshun is a 57-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. He has tanned skin with a sun-kissed complexion, dark brown hair, a beard dyed glossy black, and brown eyes that price things. He wears rings on every finger but the thumbs, each with a stone he can name the mine of, and a coat of Kingdom silk cut in the western fashion. He smells faintly of whatever perfume he bought last. He is charming, funny and attentive, and he looks at a guest's jewelry before he looks at the guest.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Revunay Kishneshun is a landholder of the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]], a Tellumi of the exile community whose grandfather bought orchards on the Kingdom's coast after [The Breaking]{.name data-type="lore/history"} and whose family has grown rich on them since. The merchants of the western ports, who buy his dates and sell him curiosities, call him the Duke, and he lets them. His house in [[place-shebarakhend|Shebarakhend]] is a museum of other people's treasures: Tellumi tablets, Khelathi seals, Vilérian glass and a room of perfume bottles nobody else may open.

He is assembling an expedition to a crumbling monastery high in the [Kuran Mountains]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, where fragments of the formula of [[being-vurinay|Vurinay]] the Ascendant, a perfumer of the ancient age, are said to have surfaced. He wants the formula for the collection, and he does not much care who else wanted it first.

## Background

**The orchards.** Revunay inherited the orchards at thirty and the collection at the same time. His father collected Tellumi tablets of the lost cities, to keep them for the houses; Revunay sold half of them to a Kaldar scholar in his first year and bought perfume bottles with the money.

**The collector.** He has spent twenty-five years buying the rare, the old and the unobtainable, and has learned that the unobtainable is a matter of price. He has funded three expeditions into the Grazian ruins, and two came back.

**The present.** The Vurinay formula is the prize he wants most. He has hired guides, guards and a scholar who can read the monastery's old hand, and he has not told any of them what the expedition is for.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is charming, greedy and amused, and he means very little of what he says. He is generous to people who are useful to him and forgets them when they are not. He is genuinely moved by beautiful things, and by nothing else.

### Motivation

He wants to own what no one else can, and to be known for owning it. The formula is the most unobtainable thing he has heard of.

### Strengths

He has money, Kingdom standing, a network of dealers in every port of the Vilérian Sea and an eye for a forgery. He pays well and promptly.

### Weaknesses

He is careless of the people he hires and reckless of the places he sends them. The exile courts despise him for selling his father's tablets, and the houses would be glad to see him embarrassed.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The expedition.** Revunay hires a party as the expedition's guards, at good pay, and tells them the monastery holds nothing but old books.
2. **The scholar.** The scholar he hired to read the monastery's hand has vanished from Shebarakhend with an advance. Revunay wants him found, and does not care much whether he is found alive.
3. **The bottle.** A perfume bottle has been stolen from the room nobody may open, and Revunay suspects a servant who has been with the house twenty years. He hires a party to search the servant's family's house in the Tellumi quarter of the city.

:::secret {title="The sold tablets"}
Among the tablets Revunay sold to the Kaldar scholar in his first year was a treaty of the seven cities from the archive of Shirvan, which his grandfather carried out of the burning city. Revunay did not read it. The scholar did, sold it on to a dealer of the western ports, and the tablet has been in a Vilérian collection for twenty years.

The House of Shirvan does not know it survived. If it learns, it will want the tablet, the name of the man who sold it, and an apology Revunay has no intention of making.

**Hook: the dealer's letter.** A dealer of the western ports writes to Revunay offering to sell him back a Tellumi treaty tablet from Shirvan, at ten times what he sold it for. Revunay hires a party to authenticate it, and to make sure Muresha of Shirvan does not hear of the sale.
:::
