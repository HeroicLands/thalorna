# Appearance {#appearance}

Mavesha is a 51-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of light build. She has golden skin with a sun-kissed complexion, black hair worn loose and long, and amber eyes that hold a crowd. A burn scar runs along her left jaw to the ear, smooth and shiny, and she does not cover it. She goes barefoot in every season and dresses in a plain dun robe dyed at the hem to the blue of evening. She speaks as if to one person even in a crowd of hundreds, quietly, and the crowd leans in to hear.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Mavesha is the prophet of the [Cult of the Twilight]{.name data-type="affiliation/faithtradition"}, a sect that teaches that at dusk, when the household fire is lit and the first star rises, the light of the Sacred Flame and the lights of the other gods are one light, and that a prayer said at that hour reaches every god at once. Her followers light the hearth at dusk with a word to [[lore-atarosdty|Ataros]] and look at the evening star while they say it.

The teaching is a heresy to the strict of the [[affiliation-tribestrzd|Atarzád]], because it says Ataros shares his light, and it is a heresy to the strict of the Tellumi, because it says the gods the Atarzád forbid can be prayed to without being named. It has followers on both sides of the line. The warrior-priestess [[being-shrmnhthvn|Sharminah Tahvan]] has hunted the sect for its moderate theology, and Mavesha has moved from quarter to quarter ahead of her for eight years. She preaches now in the poor quarter of [[place-zargandur|Zargandur]], in the shadow of the walls.

## Background

**The widow.** Mavesha was born in the Tellumi quarter of [[place-ashkarad|Ashkarad]] and married a potter at seventeen. Her husband and two children died in a fire in the workshop when she was thirty, and the burn on her jaw is from going back in for them.

**The vision.** She sat in the ashes of the workshop for a day and a night, and at the next dusk, as the household fires were lit up and down the street, she saw them all as one fire. She began to preach the next week, at the well, and the first to listen were the widows of the street, Tellumi and Atarzád.

**The hunted years.** The Ashkarad magistrate fined her, the Tellumi elders of the quarter forbade her to preach, and Sharminah burned the house where she slept. She has preached in four held cities since and slept in none of them for more than a season.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is calm, gentle and fearless, with a grief that she does not hide and does not speak of. She listens more than she preaches and remembers everyone's dead. She has a rough humor that surprises people who expect a saint.

### Motivation

She wants the two peoples to stop killing each other in the gods' names, and she believes they will stop when they understand that they pray to one light. She does not want a temple, a following or a name, and she has all three.

### Strengths

Her followers protect her, feed her and hide her, in every quarter of every held city. She is a gifted speaker, and her teaching is simple enough for a child to keep. Some Atarzád of the [Teghumod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} merchant houses quietly give her money.

### Weaknesses

She is hunted by the strict of both peoples, she has no protector with power, and the Council of the Twelve could repudiate any Atarzád who follows her. Her followers are poor, and the poor are easy to frighten.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-shrmnhthvn|Sharminah Tahvan]]: The warrior-priestess who hunts the sect. Mavesha views her as an abomination, a priestess who has made the god's fire into a weapon, and works to counter her influence in every quarter Sharminah visits.

### Others

- [[being-kenbeshay|Kenbeshai]]: The priest of the Northern Temple of Kethramír, who teaches a mercy close to hers. They have met once, at night, and agreed never to be seen together.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ataros|Faith of Ataros]]**: one of the lay faithful, whom no temple ordained and every strict temple condemns.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The dusk prayer.** Mavesha preaches at dusk in a courtyard of the Zargandur quarter, and a party staying in the quarter is asked by her followers to watch the courtyard's gate for fire-wardens.
2. **The move.** Sharminah has come to Zargandur. Mavesha must leave the quarter before morning, and she wants a party to take her out through the [Zhoqarod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} gate in a caravan's string.
3. **The convert.** A young Atarzád warrior of the Zhoqarod society has begun to come to the dusk prayers. His elder has learned of it and wants to know who has bewitched him.

:::secret {title="The Atarzád widow"}
The first follower who listened to Mavesha at the well in Ashkarad was an Atarzád widow of the Teghumod, and the widow is alive, old and rich, and her son is an elder of the tribe's counting houses. The sect's money comes from her, and through her the Teghumod know where Mavesha sleeps every night. They have kept it from Sharminah for eight years, because a merchant tribe whose trade runs on Tellumi hands has reasons to want a teaching that makes the two peoples one.

Mavesha does not know it. She believes her followers keep her hidden, and they do, but every quarter she reaches is one the Teghumod chose to let her reach.

**Hook: the price.** The widow is dying, and her son is less sentimental. A party hired to see Mavesha safely out of Zargandur learns from a Teghumod factor that the son means to sell her hiding place to Sharminah, in return for the temple's goodwill at the next Council.
:::
