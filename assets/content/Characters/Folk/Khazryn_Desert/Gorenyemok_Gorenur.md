# Appearance {#appearance}

Gorenyemok Gorenur is a man of about thirty-five who sits a lean, long-legged Gorenur horse as if he were part of it. His saddle-rug carries his line's mark in its border, and he meets a stranger at [Khelid]{.name data-type="place/site"} with a quick, measuring look and an easy smile.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Dovaq of the [[affiliation-grnrtrb|Gorenur]]. Leads the spring raids.**

Gorenyemok Gorenur is the dovaq of the Gorenur, a [[lore-sowidesclt|Sovidur]] tribe of five lines on the edge of the [[place-hekardesert|Desert of Hek'ar]] in the southern [[place-wlqtrrgn|Volketor]]. His name means _storm-oath_. The white-beards of the five lines chose him at Khelid, the tribe's chief well, to lead the raids and speak for the tribe to other tribes; inside a line he has no word at all.

## Background

**The raids.** He has led the spring raids on the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]] for six years and has never come home without horses. A Gorenur raid rides out in the first storm of spring, crosses the open grass inside it and comes on the March's foothill herds while the patrols are in the forts, and nobody is killed who does not fight.

**Now.** A dovaq of the northern steppe is gathering tribes by victory and by marriage, and Gorenyemok wants to ride with him. The white-beards of four lines have said so at Khelid; the fifth line has said nothing.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is bold, quick and generous with what he takes. He offers a stranger a share before he offers a fight.

### Motivation

He wants a place under the gathering man's banner for the Gorenur, and the March's whole herd with it.

### Strengths

He knows the storms, the springs and the ground between the March and Khelid, and his riders follow him into weather no patrol rides in.

### Weaknesses

He leads the raids and nothing else. Inside a line the white-beards rule, and a line that will not ride does not ride for him.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[affiliation-bjlrtrb|Bujalur]]: The tribe that winters in the March under the League's truce, at feud with the Gorenur since the March burned a Bujalur camp for a Gorenur raid.

### Others

- [[being-mazitosrekha|Mazitos Rekhazur]]: The Lord Commander of the Eastern March, whose herds the Gorenur raid every spring.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-grnrtrb|Gorenur]]**: dovaq, chosen by the white-beards of the five lines to lead the raids.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The first storm.** He offers a party a share of the spring raid's horses if it keeps a March patrol in its fort on the night of the first storm.
2. **Guests at Khelid.** Strangers who ride into the Gorenur range are brought to his tent at Khelid, fed, and asked what the March is doing.
3. **The fifth line's silence.** He wants the white-beards of the fifth line to say at the moot that they will ride, and a stranger the old men listen to could carry his word.

:::secret {title="The gift for the gathering man"}
Gorenyemok means the first raid of this spring as a gift to the gathering man: the March's horses, driven east through the winter ground of the Bujalur, with the League hunting the Bujalur for them while the Gorenur ride north. He wants the Bujalur ruined as well as the herd taken. The white-beards of the fifth line, who remember the winter after the last union broke, can say before the League what the four lines did.

**Hook: a share to say otherwise.** At Khelid he offers a party that has followed the track a share of the herd to go home and say the Bujalur took it: [[scenario-trcecmpsmrch|The Truce Camps of the March]].

**Hook: the gift.** The best of the March's herd goes north to the gathering man, and the riders who take it need the road clear.
:::
