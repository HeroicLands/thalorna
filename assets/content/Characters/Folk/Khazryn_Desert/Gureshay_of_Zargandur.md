# Appearance {#appearance}

Gureshay of Zargandur is a 27-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of medium build. He has tawny skin weathered by the Kingdom's border marches, black hair cropped short in the soldier's fashion of Amradad, and amber eyes under straight black brows. A healed saber cut runs white across his left forearm, and his palms are callused from the reins. In the court of his house he wears the black of Zargandur with a horse worked small in silver on the collar; on duty he wears a Kingdom officer's coat and carries the officer's lance-saber as if he had been born holding it. He stands still when others fidget, and he looks at a room's doors before he looks at its people.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Gureshay of Zargandur is the heir-in-exile of the [[affiliation-hsezargandur|House of Zargandur]], the war-house of the [[affiliation-khzrncnfdrtn|Tellumi Confederation]], and he is the one Tellumi prince who talks about taking his city back by force. He holds a commission in the cavalry of the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]], because Kingdom law forbids the exile retinues any military activity and the Kingdom's own ranks are the one place a Tellumi prince can learn war in the open. His court is a small house in [[place-shebarakhend|Shebarakhend]] with a stable bigger than the house.

His house was the first to fall in [The Breaking]{.name data-type="lore/history"}. On the last morning of [[place-zargandur|Zargandur]] its cavalry rode out of the north gate to meet the [Zhoqarod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"} host and was killed on the [[place-henhulpedin|Henhutpedin]], the blood-field, to the last rider, the princes of the house among them. The house keeps the riders' names on a tablet that has never been read over them, and Gureshay has known every name since he was five.

## Background

**The boy.** Gureshay's father, the last heir-in-exile, was a scholar of the old commentaries who died of a fever when the boy was eleven. His mother was of a Kingdom family of officers, and her brothers taught him to ride the long-legged desert horse that Zargandur bred, the breed the Zhoqarod keep now.

**The soldier.** He took his commission at eighteen and has served on the Kingdom's southern marches against Dunhari raiders, where he took the cut on his forearm and learned that the Kingdom's officers respect a Tellumi who fights. He commands a troop of eighty, half of them Tellumi of the exile community.

**The present.** He is widely understood to be preparing a reclamation, though he has set no date and says nothing about one in public. The militant faction of the exile courts organizes round him more each year, and the [[affiliation-cnclprinces|Council of the Princes]] neither endorses his preparations nor forbids them. Its private consensus is that no reclamation will come in this generation. He has heard the consensus, and he is twenty-seven.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is direct, disciplined and short with words. He listens to older soldiers and does not listen to older princes, whom he thinks have mistaken waiting for a policy. He is courteous to women, kind to horses and frightening to men who have lied to him. He laughs rarely and well.

### Motivation

He wants Zargandur, and he wants the riders' names read over the field where they died. He believes that the Atarzád took the belt by being willing to take losses no one else would take, and that no one will give it back to a people unwilling to take the same.

### Strengths

He is a good officer and a better horseman, and the Tellumi of his troop would ride anywhere behind him. He has the Kingdom's training, a soldier's standing among its officers, and the money of [[being-nimeshayashk|Nimeshay of Ashkarad]] for his horses.

### Weaknesses

He has no army. The Kingdom will not lend him one, the Council will not raise one, and the Tellumi quarter of Zargandur, which would rise for him, is a poor district in the shadow of walls the Zhoqarod raised higher after the war. What he does not know about the garrison is what he would need to know first.

## Social

### Enemies

- [Zhoqarod]{.name data-type="affiliation/polity"}: The largest and most martial of the twelve tribes, who hold Zargandur as a garrison city and give the tribes their Zhudek more often than any other tribe. He studies them as a man studies a horse he means to ride.

### Others

- [[being-nimeshayashk|Nimeshay of Ashkarad]]: The merchant prince who pays for his horses. Gureshay knows he is being kept, and he has not worked out for what.
- [[being-hemvanaymern|Hemvanay of Mernagord]]: The prince of the largest free oasis and the house most opposed to his plans, whose city lies nearest the war he means to start. They are polite to each other at the Council.
- [[being-mureshashirv|Muresha of Shirvan]]: The First of the Seven, who has told him that a city taken without the case is a city that can be taken back. He thinks she is right and thinks she will never stop preparing the case.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The count.** Gureshay pays, in Shebarakhend, for a true count of the Zhoqarod at every gate of Zargandur, at the weirs and on the three bridges, and of how fast the garrison is reinforced from the north pastures. A party caught counting is treated as spies.
2. **A signet.** The court pays a fortune for a prince's signet out of the trench at [[place-relvanvesul|Relvanvesut]], where the palace household of Zargandur lies and where raiders already dig. Gureshay wants to know which of his house died in the palace and where.
3. **The troop.** A Tellumi trooper of his has deserted with two horses and a Kingdom saber, and has been seen riding east on the belt road. Gureshay wants him found before the Kingdom asks why a Tellumi prince's man is riding toward the held cities armed.

:::secret {title="The bows in Zargandur"}
Gureshay has a date. He means to ride on Zargandur on the night of the summer Festival of the Promise in four years' time, when most of the Zhoqarod warrior society is on [[place-mountshofar|Mount Shofar]] and the garrison is old men and boys. His men have been carrying silver into the Tellumi quarter of Zargandur for two years, and the silver has bought bows, which lie under the floors of three workshops near the north gate. Nimeshay's counting room pays for the silver and does not ask where it goes.

The count he is buying is for that night. He has two counts already, from men he trusts, and they disagree by four hundred riders.

He has not considered the steppe. A man is gathering the steppe tribes into a union, and a Zhoqarod garrison that sees a Tellumi rising in the same season as a union's riders on the uplands will not wait for the Council of the Twelve to acclaim a Zhudek.

**Hook: the third count.** A party Gureshay hires to make the third count finds that the Zhoqarod have already found one cache of bows, have left it where it lies, and are watching the workshop.
:::
