# Appearance {#appearance}

Knurvorv Hnirvendik is a 58-year-old man who stands five feet ten inches tall and is of medium build. His fair face is weathered and clean-shaven, which few men on the quay are, his black hair is graying and cropped, and his gray eyes are steady and give nothing away. He wears the guild master's chain of small brass anchors over a sober black coat. A seamed scar runs along his jaw.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 58. Guild Master of the pilots of Kalipsa. Wants the pilots cleared at the Sea-Law Hall, and the hearing over.**

Knurvorv Hnirvendik is the Guild Master of the pilots' guild of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]], chartered by the [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]], and he keeps the guild's house on the lower quay of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]]. He licenses the pilots, keeps the roster of the pilots' boat, collects the fees the Council fixes and pays the guild's men. He piloted for twenty years before the guild chose him, and his son is a pilot of the boat's crew. In clear weather the boat keeps its station at the edge of the approaches by day, and a master who arrives at night lies off under [[place-lantrnklps|the Lantern]] until morning. When fog lies on the approaches the boat keeps its station through the night as well, sounding a horn, because a master in fog cannot see the Lantern to lie off by it.

The autumn has put his guild before [[place-sealawhall|the Sea-Law Hall]]. [the Syndicate of the Gull]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"} has refused the bonds on three [[affiliation-hsenorodari|Norodari]] hulls lost in the fog, on the rule that a bond is not paid where a master refused a pilot. The three were lost on three nights within a fortnight, and Knurvorv has told the hall that the boat kept its station on every one of them and that none of the three masters hailed it. [[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]] says the boat was not there to hail.

:::secret {title="The third night"}
The boat came in early on the night the third hull was lost. Knurvorv's son was aboard it, the fog had thickened until the crew could not see the Hounds from the boat, and Knurvorv, standing on the quay, called it home with the horn two hours before dawn. The roster he gave the hall shows the boat on its station until morning, and the men of its crew have agreed to remember it so. The third Norodari hull went onto the Hounds in those two hours. On the nights the first two were lost the boat kept its station, and neither master hailed it.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Knurvorv is formal, careful and proud of his guild. He answers questions exactly and no further, keeps his temper in public, and keeps a ledger of every slight to the pilots.

### Motivation

He wants the guild's name clean. A pilot's license is the city's word that its holder knows the approaches, and he believes the city's harbor is safe because masters trust that word.

### Strengths

He knows every pilot in the city, the boat's every station and every master's habits on the Run. The Council relies on the guild and seldom questions its master.

### Weaknesses

He is defending the guild against a charge the Norodari can afford to press, and the guild's men are tired of being called liars on the quay.

## Social

### Companions

The senior pilots of the guild, and his son.

### Patrons

The [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]], which charters the guild and fixes its fees.

### Enemies

[[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]], who has called the guild liars at the Sea-Law Hall.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]**: He is a citizen and holds the office of Guild Master for the pilots' guild.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The guild's honor.** Knurvorv wants a stranger to stand in the pilots' boat on its station through the next fog and swear at the hall that it kept its place.
2. **A pilot's license.** A master from Sarmelis wants a Kalipsan pilot's license for a coaster's mate, and Knurvorv will grant it only after the mate has rowed the approaches with the boat for a season.
3. **The fee.** The newer houses want the Council to lower the pilot's fee for coasters of the Run, and Knurvorv needs someone to count how many hulls enter without a pilot already.
