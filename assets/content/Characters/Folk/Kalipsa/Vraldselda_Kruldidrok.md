# Appearance {#appearance}

Vraldselda Kruldidrok is a 49-year-old woman who stands five feet seven inches tall and is of medium build. She has light skin and a clear complexion, dark blonde hair coiled at the nape, and brown eyes that she narrows over figures. She wears spectacles of Vilérian glass to read the book and a ring with a gull cut in jet, which she uses to seal the Gull's bonds. A small mole sits beside her mouth, and her spectacles have left pinched marks on the bridge of her nose.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 49. Syndic of the Syndicate of the Gull. Wants the Gull to survive the autumn with its name and its book.**

Vraldselda Kruldidrok is the syndic of [the Syndicate of the Gull]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"}, one of [[affiliation-bondsyndcts|the Bond Syndicates]] of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]. Its underwriters chose her eight years ago. The Gull writes most of the bonds on [[place-kalipsarun|the Kalipsa Run]], and its counting-room on the quay of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]], under a painted gull, is the busiest of the syndicates' in the grain season. She sets its rates, keeps its book, and sits with the other syndics twice a year.

The autumn was the worst in the Gull's history. Fog on the eastern approaches took five hulls the Gull had bonded, three of them [[affiliation-hsenorodari|Norodari]]. The Gull paid the bonds on the other two at once and refused the three, on the rule that a bond is not paid where the master caused the loss by refusing a pilot, and the loss is before [[place-sealawhall|the Sea-Law Hall]]. Her underwriters say that paying all five would wind the syndicate up. She has asked [the Syndicate of the Two Ships]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"} to take a share of the loss, and the Two Ships has not answered.

:::secret {title="What the referee knows"}
The two hulls whose bonds the Gull paid at once belonged to two lesser houses whose heads hold large shares in the Gull's own book. Vraldselda paid them without a hearing to keep those shareholders from selling, because the Gull has sold more of its book to buyers in [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Viléria]] than its money with the [[affiliation-danvar|Danvar]] moneylenders can cover. If the Norodari bonds are paid, the Gull must call on its foreign shareholders before word of the losses reaches them, and she does not believe they will answer.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Vraldselda is brisk, precise and pleasant across a counting-table. She can reckon a premium in her head faster than her clerks can on a slate, and she does not enjoy being argued with.

### Motivation

She wants the Gull to survive. Its underwriters chose her to keep its book sound, and she means to hand it on sound. She believes the refusal is just on the rule as written, and she knows it is also the only answer the Gull can afford.

### Strengths

She knows the Run's risks to the last cask, and the Gull's sign is good in every port of the north. The syndics respect her judgment of a hull.

### Weaknesses

The Norodari can say she paid two bonds without a hearing and refused three, and she has not explained the difference.

## Social

### Companions

The Gull's underwriters, most of them masters' widows and younger sons of the lesser houses, who meet in the counting-room after the quay closes.

### Patrons

She has none. The syndics answer to their own companies.

### Enemies

[[affiliation-hsenorodari|The Norodari]], who have called the refusal a theft before the hall.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]**: She is a citizen.
- **[[affiliation-bondsyndcts|The Bond Syndicates]]**: She is the syndic of the Gull.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Gull's book.** Vraldselda wants to know how much of the Gull's book has been sold abroad and to whom, before word of the autumn's losses reaches the buyers, and she cannot ask her own clerks.
2. **The Two Ships' answer.** She needs a messenger to carry the Gull's offer to the Two Ships' syndic, and to bring back what the old houses would ask in return for sharing the loss.
3. **A bond for a stranger.** A stranger wants a bond written on a hull or cargo for a voyage the Gull has never written, and Vraldselda will write it at a high premium if he will carry a sealed letter for her on the way.
