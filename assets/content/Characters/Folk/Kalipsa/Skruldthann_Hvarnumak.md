# Appearance {#appearance}

Skruldthann Hvarnumak is a 72-year-old man who stands five feet seven inches tall and is of light build. His fair skin is wrinkled and weathered, his white hair is thin and long at the neck, and his gray eyes are sharp under heavy lids. He walks with a blackthorn stick and wears on his left hand the plain iron ring that the Council gives a Harbormaster when the chain passes on. His right knee is stiff, and the backs of his hands are spotted with age.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 72. Past Harbormaster and head of Hvarnumak. Wants his last years on the Council to be spent as the voice it listens to first.**

Skruldthann Hvarnumak was Harbormaster of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]] from the spring sitting of 714 to the spring of 717, when he passed the chain to [[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]]. He is the eldest of the Past Harbormasters living, and by the custom of the [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]] he speaks first after the Harbormaster on every question. He is also the head of [Hvarnumak]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, an old house of the old city of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] that sails a handful of hulls in the wine trade to the south and has sat on the Council for as long as Tvarnidrok has.

His term is remembered for the levy. He raised it twice to pay for the squadrons' new hulls, and the houses that pay it have not forgiven him. He sits with the old houses and dines in the Tvarnidrok hall, and the Council takes his first word on any question as the old houses' first word too.

:::secret {title="What the referee knows"}
Hvarnumak's own tonnage fell below the census floor two springs ago. Two hulls on the census roll in the house's name are Tvarnidrok hulls, sold to Hvarnumak on paper for a sum never paid, and they keep its seat. Skruldthann casts his house's voices as [[being-knilthtvrn|Knilthorv Tvarnidrok]] asks, and would lose the seat, the precedence and the house's name on the roll if the census counted only what Hvarnumak owns.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Skruldthann is dry, courteous and slow to speak, and he enjoys being waited for. He is vain of his precedence and of his memory, which is still good, and he forgives nobody who interrupts him.

### Motivation

He wants to keep his seat and his first word until he dies, and to see his granddaughter head of the house after him. He believes the old houses are the city, and that his own house's decline is a run of bad seasons.

### Strengths

He has sat on the Council for forty years, knows every precedent and every house's debts, and his first word sets the tone of a sitting. Past Harbormasters are honored for life, and nobody on the quay is openly rude to him.

### Weaknesses

His house is poor, and its hulls are few and old. He tires by the end of a sitting, and his granddaughter does more of the house's business than he admits.

## Social

### Companions

His granddaughter, who keeps the house's counting-room, and the old men of the old houses who dine at Tvarnidrok's table.

### Patrons

[[being-knilthtvrn|Knilthorv Tvarnidrok]] treats him as an elder and an ally, and Skruldthann takes the treatment as his due.

### Enemies

The newer houses, who remember his levy, and [[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]], who mocks it in the Council.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]**: He is a Past Harbormaster and a Councilor.
- **[[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]]**: He sits for Hvarnumak and speaks first after the Harbormaster.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The first word.** Skruldthann will speak first on the census rule at the spring sitting, and both parties want to know what he will say. He has asked to hear the arguments from someone who belongs to neither.
2. **The southern wine.** A Hvarnumak hull is held at a southern port for a debt, and the house needs someone to carry the money there before the wine spoils.
