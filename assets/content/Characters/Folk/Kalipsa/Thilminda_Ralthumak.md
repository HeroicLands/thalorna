# Appearance {#appearance}

Thilminda Ralthumak is a 64-year-old woman who stands five feet six inches tall and is of light build. Her light skin is finely wrinkled, her white hair is cut short under an arbitrator's gray hood worn back on her shoulders, and her hazel eyes are bright and unblinking. Her voice is low and carries to the harbor doors of the hall without being raised. Her nose is narrow and hooked, and deep lines bracket her mouth.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 64. Master arbitrator of the Sea-Law Hall. Wants the Sea-Law recited exactly, whoever it ruins.**

Thilminda Ralthumak is one of the master arbitrators of [[place-sealawhall|the Sea-Law Hall]] on the quay of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]]. She learned the [[lore-sealaw|Sea-Law]] over seven years of recitation from a master arbitrator who was old when the Thraskumak yards built the navy's oldest hull, and she has heard cases for forty years. She can give back word for word the rules and the verdicts that bear on any dispute a house can bring. She examines the young arbitrators, fixes the order of hearings with the other masters, and keeps the chest where the roll of hearings lies.

Every great house on the [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]] has chosen her as its arbitrator at least once, and every great house has lost a hearing before her. Her fees are smaller than a bought arbitrator's, and the houses she has ruled against choose others when they can. The hearing on the three lost [[affiliation-hsenorodari|Norodari]] hulls has given her the worst year of her life: the two chosen arbitrators cannot agree on the third chair, both parties have named her privately, and neither will name her aloud.

:::secret {title="What the referee knows"}
Thilminda remembers a verdict that bears on [[being-knilthtvrn|Knilthorv Tvarnidrok]]'s census rule. A hull bought in winter, before its first voyage, was held by the hall about 590 to be wholly its buyer's from the day the price was paid, and the verdict has been recited with the rule on shares ever since. She has not recited it to anyone outside a hearing. She knows that a master arbitrator has been approached on Tvarnidrok's behalf, she does not know which, and she suspects the one whose grandson has lately been taken as an apprentice without paying a fee he could not have afforded.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Thilminda is dry, exact and courteous to everyone in the same degree. She enjoys a hard case and has no conversation outside the Sea-Law. She does not gossip, and she listens to gossip with great attention.

### Motivation

She wants the Sea-Law kept as it was given to her, recited exactly and applied without fear. She believes the harbor's whole credit rests on the hall's verdicts being bought by nobody, and she has given her life to that one thing.

### Strengths

She knows the Sea-Law better than anyone living. Her word on what a rule says is final in the hall, and the houses respect her even when they avoid her.

### Weaknesses

She is old, tired and poorer than her colleagues, and she has no house of her own behind her. Her refusal to bend has left her without friends on the Council, and a great house that wanted her out of a hearing could keep her out.

## Social

### Companions

Her apprentices, two young arbitrators who are reciting the rules on shares, and a sister in the old city who keeps her house.

### Patrons

She has none, and an arbitrator who had one would be suspect.

### Enemies

None by name. The houses she has ruled against are many, and they choose other arbitrators.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]**: She is a citizen and holds the office of Arbitrator.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The third chair.** The two chosen arbitrators cannot agree on the third, and Thilminda wants to know which of the master arbitrators each party has approached, so that the hall can choose a third whom nobody has touched.
2. **A stranger's case.** A stranger has a dispute with a Kalipsan house over a cargo, and Thilminda advises him to bring it to the hall and pay his half of the fee, because the house expects him not to.
3. **The roll.** Pages of the hall's roll of hearings have been cut from the chest, and Thilminda wants to know whose hearings were on them before anyone else notices.
