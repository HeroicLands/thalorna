# Appearance {#appearance}

Hvurnrinna Vrathumak is a 59-year-old woman who stands five feet five inches tall and is of medium build. Her fair face is weathered and chapped, her black hair is graying and tied under a dark kerchief, and her brown eyes are hard. Her hands and forearms are stained purple-red to the elbow by forty years at the dye-vats, and the iron key of the village spring-house hangs on a thong around her neck. Her lower lip carries a healed split.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 59. Elder of Vrellnes. Wants the spring kept for the village and its vats, and the city told so to its face.**

Hvurnrinna Vrathumak is the elder of [Vrellnes]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, the dye-village on the black headland of [[place-calypsashore|the South Shore]] of the [[place-calypsaisle|Isle of Kalipsa]]. The boat-owners chose her after the autumn fishing six years ago, and they have chosen her every autumn since. She keeps the key of the spring-house, settles quarrels over boat-places and nets, and speaks for the village to the city over the ridge. She has gathered dye-shellfish on the rocks at the spring tides since she was a girl and still works the vats.

The spring of Vrellnes is the best on the shore, and the vats need it. When the city's cisterns ran low in the dry summer, the Harbormaster's office sent water-carts over the ridge to buy from the village spring-houses at the city's price. [Dvonvík]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} sold. Hvurnrinna locked the spring-house and turned the carters away, and they went back to the city with empty casks and a complaint to the Master of the Watch, [[being-krildtvlgr|Krildthira Tvulgaruk]]. She has since sent word to every village on the shore that a spring-house that opens to one cart opens to all.

:::secret {title="What the referee knows"}
The spring-house of Vrellnes has a cellar under its floor, and in it stand half the season's sealed dye-jars, which the village told a cloth-merchant's factor in the city had gone over the ridge to him. Hvurnrinna kept them back to sell to a Provénzian buyer who has offered twice the factor's price at the Dvonvík quay in the spring. The jars are one reason the spring-house stays locked: its cellar steps go down straight among them.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Hvurnrinna is stubborn, blunt and proud of her village. She speaks the shore's older [[skill-nordmalng|Nordmal]] and pretends not to understand the city's when it suits her, and she is warm to anyone who has worked with their hands.

### Motivation

She wants the villages to be heard by a city that has never given them a seat. The spring is the village's by the same custom that gives it its cove, and she believes that if Vrellnes gives way this year the city will take the shore's water every dry summer from now on.

### Strengths

The village stands behind her, and the other villages listen to her. She knows the dye, the rocks and the tides better than anyone living, and the city needs the dye.

### Weaknesses

She has made the Master of the Watch her enemy, and the watch has a post at Dvonvík half a day away. She has made the spring-house a matter of pride, and she cannot open it now without losing face.

## Social

### Companions

The dye-women of the vats, and the boat-owners who chose her.

### Patrons

She has none, and the shore has none on the Council.

### Enemies

[[being-krildtvlgr|Krildthira Tvulgaruk]], the Master of the Watch, and [[being-brildmdvrn|Brildmil Dvurnumak]], the elder of Dvonvík, who sold.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]**: She is a citizen of the city-state and the elder of Vrellnes.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The locked spring-house.** Hvurnrinna wants witnesses from outside the island to hear the village's elders swear that the spring was never the city's, before the watch comes over the ridge.
2. **The dye-jars.** A cloth-merchant's factor from the city has come over the ridge asking where his season's dye went, and Hvurnrinna wants someone to walk him back to Dvonvík by the long way.
3. **The rocks at the spring tide.** A gatherer has not come back from the rocks below the headland, and the tide is turning.
