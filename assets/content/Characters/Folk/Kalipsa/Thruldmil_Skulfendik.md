# Appearance {#appearance}

Thruldmil Skulfendik is a 66-year-old man who stands five feet eight inches tall and is of heavy build. His fair skin is ruddy and leathery, his white hair sticks out from under a seal-skin cap he does not take off indoors, and his blue eyes are shrewd and merry. He smells faintly of rendering oil, as everyone in his village does. Both his forearms carry old burn scars from the kettles, and two of his front teeth are gone.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 66. Elder of Glervhavan. Wants a city buyer for his village's oil, and the Lantern is the buyer he means to have.**

Thruldmil Skulfendik is the elder of [Glervhavan]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, the rendering village at the mouth of the one sheltered inlet of [[place-calypsashore|the South Shore]] of the [[place-calypsaisle|Isle of Kalipsa]]. Its kettles boil down fish and seal for lamp oil, which lights the shore's houses and much of the city's poor quarter. He has been elder for eleven years and rendered oil for fifty.

He has seen his chance in the price of olive oil. The [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]]'s treasurers want the cost of [[place-lantrnklps|the Lantern of Kalipsa]] cut, and Thruldmil has offered the city his village's oil for the beacon at a third of the price. [[being-snurvdrskr|Snurvinda Druskaruk]], the keeper, has refused it, and he has asked for a cask of it to be burned beside the olive oil for one night before witnesses from the Council. A contract for the Lantern would keep Glervhavan's kettles boiling for a generation, and he has told the other villages so.

## Psyche

### Personality

Thruldmil is jovial, persuasive and shameless in a bargain. He tells long stories, laughs at his own jokes and never forgets a price.

### Motivation

He wants Glervhavan to prosper, and he believes the Lantern's oil is the best trade the shore could win from the city. He also likes being right, and he is sure the keeper is wrong about the color of the flame.

### Strengths

He can talk to the city's treasurers in their own terms, and the shore's elders respect him as the one of them who understands money. His oil is good of its kind.

### Weaknesses

His oil burns red, and he does not believe that matters. He would sell his village's water to the city if the price were right, and Vrellnes knows it.

## Social

### Companions

The renderers of his village, and the coaster masters who carry his oil to the city.

### Patrons

The Council's treasurers, who have taken his offer to the Council.

### Enemies

[[being-snurvdrskr|Snurvinda Druskaruk]], who will not let his oil into the lantern room.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]**: He is a citizen of the city-state and the elder of Glervhavan.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The trial lamp.** Thruldmil wants a cask of Glervhavan's oil carried up the headland path to the Lantern and burned for a night before the Council's witnesses, and he wants strangers to carry it so that the keeper cannot say his own men spoiled the trial.
2. **The seal-hunt.** The seals have left the skerries off Dvonvík, and Glervhavan needs hunters to follow them along the cliffs before the winter.
3. **A sail in the inlet.** A boat with no lights has twice put into Glervhavan's inlet at night, and Thruldmil wants to know whose it is without asking the watch.
