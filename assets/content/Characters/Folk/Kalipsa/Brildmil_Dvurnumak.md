# Appearance {#appearance}

Brildmil Dvurnumak is a 53-year-old man who stands five feet nine inches tall and is of medium build. His fair skin is weathered and freckled, his auburn hair and beard are thick and going sandy at the edges, and his hazel eyes are wary. He lost the little finger of his right hand to a net-line as a boy, and he wears a sealskin waistcoat with silver buttons bought in the city. His nose was broken flat long ago.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 53. Elder of Dvonvík. Wants his village to do well out of the city, and the rest of the shore to stop calling him a traitor for it.**

Brildmil Dvurnumak is the elder of [Dvonvík]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, the largest village of [[place-calypsashore|the South Shore]] of the [[place-calypsaisle|Isle of Kalipsa]], in the deep cove at the island's southern point. It has the shore's one stone quay, the end of the cart road from the city over [Hlomfell]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, and the watch's post. He owns three boats, sends his catch to the city on the carts, and has been elder for eight years.

Dvonvík lives closer to the city than the other villages, and Brildmil has always dealt with it. When the city's water-carts came over the ridge in the dry summer he sold them the village spring's water at the city's price, and the carts went on to Vrellnes and were turned away. The other villages call him the city's man, and Vrellnes has told them that a spring-house that opens to one cart opens to all of them. He says that Dvonvík's spring rose again with the autumn rain, that the money bought the village's winter grain, and that the city will take the water anyway if it is not sold.

## Psyche

### Personality

Brildmil is practical, friendly and anxious to be liked. He gives way in an argument and then does what he meant to, and he cannot bear to be thought a fool.

### Motivation

He wants Dvonvík fed and safe. He believes the city is too strong to be refused, and that the shore does better to sell what the city will take anyway than to have it taken.

### Strengths

He knows the city's officers and its carters, the watch's men at the post and the masters of the coasters that call at the quay. Dvonvík trusts him to get a fair price.

### Weaknesses

The other villages distrust him, and Vrellnes has made him the example of what the shore must not do. He is not sure himself that he chose rightly.

## Social

### Companions

The boat-owners of Dvonvík, and the watchmen of the post, who drink at his table.

### Patrons

He has none, though the shore believes the city is his.

### Enemies

[[being-hvurnvrthm|Hvurnrinna Vrathumak]], the elder of Vrellnes, who calls him the city's man to every village on the shore.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]**: He is a citizen of the city-state and the elder of Dvonvík.
