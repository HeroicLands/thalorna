# Appearance {#appearance}

Ralthann Rultharuk is a 45-year-old mason with stone dust worked into the cuffs of his coat. He carries a short straightedge and tests a footing before putting his weight on it. His speech becomes slower when someone tries to hurry a measurement.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 45. Master mason of Kalipsa. Wants the Old Mole secured before the next storm.**

Ralthann maintains the city’s masonry at [[place-oldmole|The Old Mole]]. The lower courses are older than his craft’s records, and the [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]] forbids moving them. He repairs the upper work in smaller stone and mortar and leaves the fitted old blocks alone.

## Background

He learns his work on the harbor’s exposed masonry, where a sound-looking face can conceal a joint washed empty by the sea. The settled block near the mole’s head gives him a problem his ordinary repair work cannot settle: the storm shifts it, and the standing order still forbids him to move it.

## Psyche

### Personality

He demonstrates a fault with a straightedge before arguing about it. He listens to another craftsperson’s objections and has little patience for a confident guess from the quay.

### Motivation

He wants the block wedged and the upper courses rebuilt before a storm enlarges the gap.

### Strengths

He knows which masonry can carry a line and which must be kept clear of workers.

### Weaknesses

His responsibility for the harbor exceeds his authority to touch its old courses. Delay makes him ready to act before the Harbormaster gives leave.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

He works for [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]] and petitions [[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]] for leave. [[being-flarnulthd|Flarnthann Ulthidrok]]’s dockers know the quay’s lifting lines. [[being-marvindnalth|Marvinda Naltharuk]] asks him to delay covering the newly exposed carving.

2. He asks a party to carry a measured account of the damage to the Harbormaster before the next tide.
