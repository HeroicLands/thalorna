# Appearance {#appearance}

Flarnthann Ulthidrok is a 34-year-old man who stands six feet tall and is of heavy build. His fair skin is freckled and scarred across the forearms, his red hair is cropped short, and his blue eyes are cheerful and quick. His left ear is split from a boarding fight, and he wears a docker's cargo hook through his belt wherever he goes. His nose has been broken more than once.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 34. Docker on the Old Mole and a boarding man of the levy. Wants his gang paid and his berths kept.**

Flarnthann Ulthidrok is the boss of a gang of twelve dockers who work the deep-sea hulls on the inner quay of [[place-oldmole|the Old Mole]] in the harbor of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]]. They load and unload the old houses' cargoes, chiefly [[affiliation-hsetvarnidrok|Tvarnidrok]]'s iron and timber from the north and wine and cloth for the return, and they are paid by the cask and the bale. He grew up in the old city, speaks [[skill-nordmalng|Nordmal]] and dock-talk and no other tongue, and has worked the Mole since he was twelve.

When the Council calls the levy, he and his gang serve in the boarding crews of the navy, who go over a raider's rail with short axes and hooks while the bowmen keep its deck clear. He has fought three times in the summer squadrons, and he is proud of the ear. On the morning of the census his gang dresses the old houses' hulls with flags and warps them into their berths for the tally-men.

:::secret {title="What the referee knows"}
The factor of the [[affiliation-hsenorodari|Norodari]] counting-room on the lower quay has paid Flarnthann's gang to work for the Norodari on the morning of the spring census, warping three of the house's coasters from the lower quay to the far end of the Mole after the tally-men have counted them, so that they are counted again. He took the money because the old houses pay his gang late. He does not know whether [[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]] knows of it, and he has told nobody, least of all [[being-hvulgmrvnd|Hvulgorv Murvendik]].
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Flarnthann is loud, good-humored and quick to his fists, and just as quick to buy a drink afterward. He is loyal to his gang above everything and has no opinion about the election he will say aloud.

### Motivation

He wants his gang paid on time and his berths on the Mole kept, and he wants to be thought a hard man in a fight. He resents the old houses, who pay late and talk down to him, and needs their berths.

### Strengths

He is strong, fearless on a deck and knows every bollard, hawser and warp on the Mole. His gang will follow him anywhere, and the dockers of the whole quay know him.

### Weaknesses

He drinks, he fights and he spends his money before it is earned, and he is a poor liar.

## Social

### Companions

His gang, and the boarding crews he has fought beside.

### Patrons

He has none, and wants none.

### Enemies

The dockers of the lower quay, whose gangs and his own fight on the quay every census day.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]**: He is a citizen and serves in the levy.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The short-paid gang.** Flarnthann's gang has not been paid for the autumn's grain, and he wants a stranger with a straight face to carry his reckoning to a Tvarnidrok steward.
2. **The census-day brawl.** The dockers of the lower quay have promised to break his gang's heads on the morning of the census, and Flarnthann wants a few more fists on the Mole.
3. **A boarding crew.** The navy is short of boarding men for a winter cruise against raiders at the mouth of Lísere Bay, and Flarnthann will vouch for any stranger who can fight on a wet deck.
