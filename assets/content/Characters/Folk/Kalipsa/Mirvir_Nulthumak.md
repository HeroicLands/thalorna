# Appearance {#appearance}

Mirvir Nulthumak is a 39-year-old man who stands five feet nine inches tall and is of light build. His pale skin is clear from a life indoors, his blond hair is fine and receding, and his hazel eyes are tired. The second finger of his right hand is stained with ink, and he carries the Council's seal on a cord inside his coat. He stoops from years at a desk.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 39. Clerk of the Council of Kalipsa. Wants the census roll to be beyond question, because it is his.**

Mirvir Nulthumak is the Clerk of the Council of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]], a salaried officer of the city and not a Councilor. He keeps the census roll, the record of every sitting of the [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]], its charters, its correspondence and its seal, in the chamber above the customs house on the quay of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]]. When the Harbormaster puts a question, he counts the voices by the roll. His clerks copy the roll for anyone who pays the fee.

He was a house clerk of the lower quay before the Council chose him, and he has kept the roll for nine years. Each spring he enters the totals [[being-tvilgrlthr|the Customs Officer]] brings him from the tonnage census, house by house, with every sale of a hull or a share that the parties have registered in the year. This spring the roll decides the Harbormaster's election, and he has never been so popular.

:::secret {title="A sale not paid"}
Two springs ago Mirvir entered the sale of two hulls from [[affiliation-hsetvarnidrok|Tvarnidrok]] to [Hvarnumak]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, and the sale came to him without a [[affiliation-danvar|Danvar]] receipt for the price. He entered it anyway, because the roll records what the parties register and not what they pay, and because the houses were Tvarnidrok and a Past Harbormaster's. He has kept the paper, and he knows that without those two hulls Hvarnumak has no seat.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Mirvir is careful, soft-spoken and anxious. He takes pride in a clean hand and a correct total, and is easily flustered by Councilors who raise their voices.

### Motivation

He wants the roll to be beyond question, and he wants to keep his post when the chain passes. He believes the roll is the city's honesty written down.

### Strengths

He knows every house's registered tonnage, every sale and every share for nine years. Both parties need him, and the Council's seal is in his keeping.

### Weaknesses

He enters what the parties register, and he is not brave. A Councilor who leaned on him hard enough could make him enter anything.
