# Appearance {#appearance}

Marvinda Naltharuk is a 38-year-old factor with a case of clean sheets, folded cloth and carefully stoppered ink. She labels a copy before discussing it and turns a sheet toward the person who is speaking so both can point to the same mark.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 38. The Thordimalon’s factor in Kalipsa. Wants a faithful copy of the Old Mole’s new carved face.**

Marvinda arranges local purchases and hired work for [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|The Order]] in [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]]. Her present commission concerns [[place-oldmole|The Old Mole]], where a shifted block exposes writing no living scholar can read. She asks the Harbormaster for permission to copy it and offers to pay the masons for a delay.

## Background

Her trade is obtaining and delivering records intact: agreeing a price, hiring hands, recording where a copy comes from and keeping it dry on its voyage. She can recognize the difference between a copied mark and an artist’s repair of a broken one. She cannot translate the mole’s writing.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is precise about what a contract buys and readily admits a gap in her knowledge.

### Motivation

She wants the face recorded before it disappears under the repaired upper work.

### Strengths

She can organize copying materials, wages and transport quickly, and keeps duplicate records when the work permits it.

### Weaknesses

She underestimates how little working time a tide leaves and depends on local workers to set a safe limit.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

She is a citizen of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]] employed as the Order’s factor, not a Councilor. [[being-ralthannrulth|Ralthann Rultharuk]] wants to finish the repair. [[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]] has granted neither petition.

## Plot Hooks

1. Marvinda hires a party to take a copy at low water: [[scenario-oldmoleface|Before the Stone Is Covered]].
2. She needs a second drawing from a different angle to distinguish the carving from a crack.
3. She pays for a copy carried dry and intact to a ship’s master, with its source and orientation recorded.
