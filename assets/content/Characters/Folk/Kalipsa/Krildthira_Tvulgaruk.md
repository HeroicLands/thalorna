# Appearance {#appearance}

Krildthira Tvulgaruk is a 46-year-old woman who stands five feet ten inches tall and is of medium build. Her fair face is weathered, with a white scar across the bridge of her nose, her dark brown hair is cut at the jaw, and her gray eyes are level and unfriendly to strangers until they have given her reason otherwise. She wears the watch's blue coat with a short sword and an iron baton at her hip. Her left shoulder is stiff from an old wound.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 46. Master of the Watch of Kalipsa. Wants the island kept quiet through the spring census and the election.**

Krildthira Tvulgaruk is the Master of the Watch of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]. She commands the city guard of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]], the garrisons of the forts on the headland and the round tower that carries the harbor chain, the fire-station on [Hlomfell]{.name data-type="place/feature"} and the watch's post at [Dvonvík]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} on [[place-calypsashore|the South Shore]]. She answers to the Harbormaster. She served twelve years in the navy's squadrons before she came ashore, and the boarding crews still salute her on the quay.

The census and the election will put the crews of both great parties on the quays in the spring, and she means to keep them from each other's throats. The South Shore has given her a second quarrel. The city's water-carters, sent over the ridge in the dry summer to buy water from the village spring-houses, came back from [Vrellnes]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} with empty casks and a complaint, and the complaint is on her table. The city holds that every spring on the island is the city's, and the Harbormaster has not yet told her what to do about it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Krildthira is hard, fair and short-spoken, and she enjoys order. She has no taste for politics and less for politicians, and she keeps her opinions of the Council to herself.

### Motivation

She wants the peace kept. She believes a free port lives on strangers trusting its quays, and that a riot on census morning or a fight at a village spring-house costs the city more than any election gains it.

### Strengths

The watch is disciplined and loyal to her, and the navy's men respect her. She knows the city's taverns, its smugglers and its brawlers by name.

### Weaknesses

The watch is too small to hold the quays and the South Shore at once. The villages see her as the city's fist, and she has no way to change their minds.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The census-day watch.** Krildthira needs extra hands on the quays on the morning of the census, strangers who belong to neither party, to stand between the crews.
2. **The carters' escort.** The water-carters want an escort back to Vrellnes, and she would rather send strangers who can talk than a squad of the watch.
3. **The empty post.** The watch's man at Dvonvík has not reported in a week, and the cliff path is bad in the rain.
