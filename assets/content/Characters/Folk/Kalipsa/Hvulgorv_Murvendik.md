# Appearance {#appearance}

Hvulgorv Murvendik is a 55-year-old man who stands five feet ten inches tall and is of medium build. His skin is tanned to leather by thirty years at sea, his graying brown hair is tied back, and his gray eyes look at the sky before they look at the person asking him a question. Three fingers of his left hand are scarred white by a running rope. He dresses like any master of the quay, in a tarred coat and sea-boots, and nobody would take him for the most respected man in the harbor.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 55. Master sworn to Tvarnidrok. Wants every hull he takes out brought home with every man aboard.**

Hvulgorv Murvendik is a master sworn to [[affiliation-hsetvarnidrok|Tvarnidrok]], and he has sailed the house's hulls to [[place-gnarthborg|Gnarthborg]] and the south for thirty years. He has never lost a crew. He has never sailed against an owner's written order, nor entered the harbor of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] without a pilot, and three houses have offered him a seat as a partner and he has refused all three. When fog lies on the eastern approaches the other masters of the quay wait to see whether he sails, and when he does not, nobody does.

He is paid a full share of every voyage and keeps a modest house on the lower quay, where he lives with his wife. His two sons sail for other houses. He was at sea in the autumn's fog, bringing a Tvarnidrok hull home from the north, and he lay off the Hounds for two nights with his crew at the lead-line before he came in with the pilot at dawn.

:::secret {title="The horn before dawn"}
On the night the third [[affiliation-hsenorodari|Norodari]] hull was lost, Hvulgorv was lying off the approaches in the fog and heard the guild's horn on the quay call the pilots' boat home two hours before dawn. He heard the boat's own horn go quiet, and a little after it, from the east, a hull's bell and then nothing. Nobody has asked him, and he has not said, because the answer would help the Norodari against the house he is sworn to. He will not lie if he is asked from the witness's stone at the Sea-Law Hall.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Hvulgorv is quiet, patient and dryly funny. He speaks of the sea in short sentences and of everything else hardly at all, and he dislikes being admired.

### Motivation

He wants his crews home. He believes a master's whole duty is to the men aboard, and that the houses' quarrels are the business of the people who sit on the Council.

### Strengths

He reads weather better than anyone on the quay, and the masters of every house follow his lead in fog. His word is believed by everyone, and he has never given anyone reason to doubt it.

### Weaknesses

He is sworn to a house in the middle of a quarrel he wants no part of. He would rather say nothing than take a side, and this winter both sides want his word.

## Social

## Plot Hooks

1. **A berth in the crew.** Hvulgorv needs two hands for a winter voyage to Gnarthborg, and he takes strangers who can pull a rope and keep their mouths shut.
2. **The overdue hull.** A Tvarnidrok hull with seasoned oak aboard is overdue from the north, and Hvulgorv believes he knows the cove she would run for in a westerly.
3. **The question nobody asks.** Hvulgorv was at sea through the autumn fog, and the Norodari have not thought to ask him what he saw.
