# Appearance {#appearance}

Glurvmil Hvurnaruk is a 56-year-old man who stands five feet eleven inches tall and is of heavy build. He is broad in the chest and shoulders, his fair face is weathered and chapped by spray, and his brown hair and full beard are shot through with gray. His blue eyes go to the water before they go to a face. He wears an oiled coat in all weathers and the pilot's brass badge on a cord at his throat. A white seam of scar crosses his left cheekbone.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 56. Licensed pilot of Kalipsa. Wants every hull he boards brought in, and the pilots' good name kept clean.**

Glurvmil Hvurnaruk is one of the licensed pilots of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]], and he lives on the lower quay of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] with his wife and her sister's family. He rows out with the pilots' boat to the edge of the approaches, boards each incoming hull off [the Hounds]{.name data-type="place/feature"}, and brings it in between the headland and the head of [[place-oldmole|the Old Mole]] under [[place-lantrnklps|the Lantern]]. He greets every master in [[skill-nordmalng|Nordmal]] and every stranger in the same words, _fridma tiv thuv_, peace to you, and he gives his name, his office and his quay before he asks for the master's papers.

He has piloted for thirty years and has never lost a hull he took. He says so without boasting, as a fact about the Hounds. The autumn's losses have put the pilots on trial at [[place-sealawhall|the Sea-Law Hall]] in all but name: the pilots' guild says none of the three lost Norodari masters took a pilot, and the Norodari say the pilots' boat was not on its station. Glurvmil was ashore with a fever through the fortnight in which the three hulls were lost, and he has said so to anyone who asks.

## Psyche

### Personality

Glurvmil is calm, plain and courteous, slow to anger and slower to forget. He likes strangers, tells them more about the Hounds than they want to know, and laughs at his own stories.

### Motivation

He wants the guild's honor held, because a pilot's word is all a master has to go on in a fog. He believes the guild's account of the autumn, and he wants it proved rather than merely recited.

### Strengths

He knows the approaches, the Hounds and the set of every tide by heart, and he can bring a hull in by the Lantern alone on a black night. Every master on the Run knows his voice.

### Weaknesses

He trusts the guild completely. He has never doubted its master and would take it hard to be given cause.

## Social

### Companions

The other pilots of the boat's crew, and the masters of the Run, who buy him a drink on the lower quay whenever he has brought them in.

### Patrons

[[being-knurvhnrvn|Knurvorv Hnirvendik]], the Guild Master of the pilots, who licensed him and keeps the boat's roster.

### Enemies

None. The Norodari's charge against the boat has made some of their masters cold to him.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]**: He is a citizen and holds a pilot's license from the Council.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The fog roster.** Glurvmil wants to know who took the boat out on the nights he was ashore, and the guild's roster names men who say they do not remember.
2. **A stranger's passage.** He needs an extra pair of oars in the pilots' boat for the winter, and offers a stranger a place, with all the gossip of the approaches that goes with it.
3. **The Hounds.** A hull has gone onto the Hounds in clear weather, and Glurvmil saw a light on the rocks that should not have been there.
