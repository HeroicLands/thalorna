# Appearance {#appearance}

Hrundselda Thraskumak is a 57-year-old woman who stands five feet eight inches tall and is of medium build. Her fair face is weathered from the yards and the quay, her graying brown hair is pinned up under a plain cap, and her gray eyes are level and slow to move. Her hands are a shipwright's, scarred across the knuckles, and the first two fingers of her left hand are stiff from an old adze cut. She wears the Harbormaster's chain over a dark wool coat with tar on its cuffs.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 57. Harbormaster of Kalipsa and head of Thraskumak. Wants the census counted so that nobody can say she counted it for her house.**

Hrundselda Thraskumak is the Harbormaster of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]], elected by the [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]] at the spring sitting of 717 for the single three-year term the office allows. She holds the harbor, the forts, the customs and the navy between the Council's sittings, and she calls and keeps the sittings themselves. She is also the head of [[affiliation-hsethraskumak|Thraskumak]], the shipwrights' house whose yards build the navy's hulls below the old city of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]].

She was apprenticed in the yards at nine and could lay a keel before she could sit a Council sitting. The Council chose her because she belonged to neither party, and because a shipwright Harbormaster could tell them to the plank what the squadrons cost. Her term ends at the spring sitting of 720, and the last thing it asks of her is the census that decides who follows her.

## Psyche

### Personality

Hrundselda is plain-spoken, patient and stubborn. She listens to a whole argument without interrupting and then answers the part of it that was true. She dislikes being flattered and distrusts anyone who does it well.

### Motivation

She wants to leave the office with the navy afloat and her name clean. The navy is her craft and her house's living, and she believes the levy cannot be lowered while the squadrons sail. Her name is the harder of the two, because the census she must certify will decide an election, and the house that would gain most from her counting is building two hulls in her yards.

### Strengths

She knows the cost of every hull in the harbor and the state of every hull in the navy. The yards owe her their loyalty, the squadrons' officers respect her, and both parties on the Council need her more than she needs either.

### Weaknesses

Her house's yards are building for [[affiliation-hsenorodari|the Norodari]], and [[affiliation-hsetvarnidrok|Tvarnidrok]] says so in every sitting. She has kept the olive oil coming to [[place-lantrnklps|the Lantern of Kalipsa]] out of the harbor revenue against the treasurers' wishes, and the treasurers have told the Council. She is tired, and she has begun to let others see it.

## Social

### Companions

The master shipwrights of her yards, with whom she still eats at midday when the Council lets her. She relies on her officers, [[being-tvilgrlthr|Tvilgorv Rultharuk]] at the customs and [[being-krildtvlgr|Krildthira Tvulgaruk]] of the watch, more than on any Councilor.

### Patrons

She has none. A Harbormaster answers to the Council, and the head of Thraskumak answers to nobody.

### Enemies

None by name. [[being-knilthtvrn|Knilthorv Tvarnidrok]] and [[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]] each want her counting on his side, and each will be her enemy if she counts against him.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]**: She is the Harbormaster.
- **[[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]]**: She sits as a Councilor and holds the office of Harbormaster.
- **[[affiliation-hsethraskumak|Thraskumak]]**: She is the head of the house.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A count nobody bought.** Hrundselda wants the spring census taken by people in neither party's pay, and the Customs Officer's tally-men are in both. She will pay strangers to walk the quays beside them and count every hull.
2. **The Norodari hulls.** The two hulls building in her yards must be launched before the census, or the old houses will say she held them back; launched in time, and they will say she rushed them. She wants someone to bring the seasoned oak from the late timber ship before either is said.
3. **The Lantern's oil.** The next cargo of olive oil for [[place-lantrnklps|the Lantern]] is overdue, and she suspects the treasurers of buying it up to force the Council's hand.
