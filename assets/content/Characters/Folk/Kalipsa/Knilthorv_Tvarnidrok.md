# Appearance {#appearance}

Knilthorv Tvarnidrok is a 61-year-old man who stands six feet two inches tall and is of heavy build. His fair face is ruddy and weathered from forty years of quays and decks, his gray hair and beard are cut close, and his blue eyes are pale and steady. He wears a fur-trimmed coat of dark blue wool in every season, and on his right arm a heavy gold arm-ring of Malagnan work that his house has handed from head to head since the Malagnan marriage. A broad white scar crosses the back of his right hand.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 61. Head of Tvarnidrok and of the old houses' party. Wants the Harbormaster's chain at the spring sitting of 720.**

Knilthorv Tvarnidrok is the head of [[affiliation-hsetvarnidrok|Tvarnidrok]], the oldest seafaring house on the [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]] of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]], and he sits for it on the Council. He keeps the house's hall at the top of the old city of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]], its deep berths on the inner quay of [[place-oldmole|the Old Mole]], its shares in the Malagnan trade and its part of the book of [the Syndicate of the Two Ships]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"}. At every feast of the house he recites its line back to the Nordmen who first tied up in the harbor.

He sailed the house's hulls to [[place-gnarthborg|Gnarthborg]] for twenty years before he took its seat, and he talks of the sea as a master does, in hulls and weather. He has led the old houses on the Council for a decade, and on last year's census they have the voices to make him Harbormaster when [[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]]'s term ends. [[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]] has bought hulls all winter to take those voices away, and Knilthorv has answered with the census rule: a hull bought in the winter should not be counted at the spring census until it has made a voyage for its house.

:::secret {title="What the referee knows"}
Knilthorv has not trusted the hearing on the lost Norodari hulls to the Sea-Law. Through one of the house's sworn masters he has paid the apprenticeship fee of the grandson of a master arbitrator, the one whom Tvarnidrok's chosen arbitrator keeps proposing as the third chair. The arbitrator has taken the fee and given no promise, and Knilthorv does not know which way he will recite. If the payment comes out before the hearing, the old houses lose the arbitrator, the hearing and very likely the election, and Kubasor Norodari is already looking for proof.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Knilthorv is courteous, unhurried and certain. He speaks to a deckhand and to a Councilor in the same measured voice, remembers the names of his crews' children, and gives way on nothing that matters to his house. He believes in the line he recites.

### Motivation

He wants the chain because four heads of his house have worn it and he would be the fifth, and because a Norodari Harbormaster would set the levy and the census for the newer houses. He holds that the old houses built the harbor, the navy and the Sea-Law Hall, and that a fortune made on unentered furs in ten years has not earned the right to rule them.

### Strengths

The old houses follow him, the deepest berths in the harbor are his, and Gnarthborg's yards open to his hulls as to no other house's. He knows the Council's precedents better than its Clerk, and he has the patience to wait for a sitting to come round to him.

### Weaknesses

He needs last year's census to hold, and the census rule he has asked for has never been applied. His own factors buy furs from the same Varokh boats he denounces in the Council, and the Norodari know it. He is too sure of the old houses to see which of them are wavering.

## Social

### Companions

The masters sworn to his house, and [[being-skruldhvrn|Skruldthann Hvarnumak]], the eldest of the Past Harbormasters, who dines in the Tvarnidrok hall most weeks.

### Patrons

He has none. The Malagnan crown remembers the marriage, and Knilthorv takes care never to ask it for anything.

### Enemies

[[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]], whom he regards as a rival to be outvoted rather than an enemy to be broken.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The rule unwritten.** Knilthorv wants a master arbitrator who remembers a verdict that supports the census rule, and pays well for anyone who can find one among the old men of the hall.
2. **The furs on the quay.** He wants proof of where the Norodari furs come from to lay before the Council, and he does not want the people who find it to look at his own factors' books.
3. **The Malagnan hull.** A Tvarnidrok hull is overdue from Gnarthborg with seasoned oak in her hold, and Knilthorv wants a fast boat to look for her before the census.
