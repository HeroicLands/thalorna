# Appearance {#appearance}

Telusor Bazunari is a 47-year-old man who stands five feet eight inches tall and is of medium build. He has olive skin with a smooth complexion, dark brown hair oiled back from a high forehead, and brown eyes that smile before his mouth does. He wears rings on three fingers of each hand and keeps the city's seal of office, a small bronze hull, on a chain under his coat. One of his front teeth is crooked, and it shows when he smiles.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 47. Consul of Kalipsa at Sarmelis and broker of hull shares. Wants the trade of the outer quay to go on as it is, with his share in it.**

Telusor Bazunari is the Consul Abroad of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]] at [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]], and he has lived behind the outer quay for twenty years. He holds the city's seal for disputes among Kalipsan traders on Élavendren ground, speaks for Kalipsan captains to the [[affiliation-sarmelisconcl|Quay Council of Sarmelis]] and the crown's sheriff, and settles what can be settled before it crosses to [[place-sealawhall|the Sea-Law Hall]]. He was born on the lower quay of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] to a [[skill-valtarlng|Valtari]] market family, and he trades in [[skill-valtarlng|Valtari]] and [[skill-nordmalng|Nordmal]] with equal ease.

His living is brokering shares in hulls. A house that wants a coaster finds one through him, and a master who wants to sell a share of his hull sells it to the buyer Telusor finds. He brokered most of the coasters [[affiliation-hsenorodari|the Norodari]] bought this winter, and the old houses call him a Norodari man, which he denies with good humor.

:::secret {title="What the referee knows"}
Telusor holds, through a cousin's name, a share in three of the coasters he sold the Norodari this winter, and a Norodari defeat at the hearing would make them worth half what he paid. He knows the witness the Norodari want from the outer quay: a berth-keeper of the Hawser who heard the first of the three lost masters swear that he would pay no Kalipsan pilot's fee for a coaster. Telusor has twice paid the man to miss his passage, and has told [[being-kubasornrd|Kubasor Norodari]] that the man is ill.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Telusor is charming, talkative and easy to like, and he remembers everyone's prices. He treats every question as a negotiation and enjoys the negotiation more than the answer.

### Motivation

He wants the outer quay's trade to go on as it has: Kalipsan hulls, Élavendren duty paid in part, and a broker in the middle taking a share of every hull that changes hands. He believes the city's consul and the city's broker serve the city's interest together.

### Strengths

He knows every Kalipsan captain at Sarmelis and every clerk at the crown's tally-house, and he can find a buyer for anything that floats. The Quay Council treats him as Kalipsa's voice on the bay.

### Weaknesses

The old houses distrust him, and the consul's seal and the broker's trade do not always sit easily in one man's hands.

## Social

### Companions

The Kalipsan factors behind the outer quay, and the keepers of the Hawser, where he takes his supper.

### Patrons

The [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]], which appoints the consul and renews his seal every three years.

### Enemies

The old houses' factors at Sarmelis, who say he steers the best coasters to the newer houses.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]**: He is a citizen and holds the office of Consul Abroad at Sarmelis.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A coaster for sale.** Telusor has a coaster at the outer quay whose master has died, and he wants her sailed across to Kalipsa before the crown's cutters take an interest in her hold.
2. **The consul's seal.** Two Kalipsan masters have come to blows over a berth at the outer quay, and Telusor wants witnesses to the fight before he settles it under the city's seal.
3. **The missing witness.** The Norodari want their witness from the outer quay brought across before the hearing, and Telusor offers to help find him.
