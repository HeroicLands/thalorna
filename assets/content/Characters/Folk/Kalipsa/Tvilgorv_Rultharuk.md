# Appearance {#appearance}

Tvilgorv Rultharuk is a 50-year-old man who stands five feet six inches tall and is of heavy build. He has light, ruddy skin, a bald crown fringed with brown hair, and brown eyes that rest on a cargo as if weighing it. A slate and stylus hang at his belt, and his coat bears the Harbormaster's badge of a chained tower. His face is heavy at the jowls.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 50. Customs Officer of Kalipsa. Wants the spring census counted so that nobody can fault his roll.**

Tvilgorv Rultharuk is the Customs Officer of [[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]. He answers to the Harbormaster, keeps the customs house on the quay of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] below the Council chamber, and commands the officers who board every hull before it berths to take its manifest and set its dues. The dues are the city's main revenue, and he knows to the Ogdel what each house paid last year. He began as a tally-man and rose under three Harbormasters.

Each spring he takes the tonnage census of the [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]], sending his tally-men from berth to berth to count every hull a house owns or holds a share in, and he gives the totals to the Clerk of the Council for the roll. This spring's census decides the Harbormaster's election, and he knows that men of both parties have been buying his tally-men's drinks, and some of them more than drinks. He has told the Harbormaster, [[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]], so, and she has asked him to count as if nobody had been bought.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tvilgorv is affable, exact and tired. He jokes with masters while his officers search their holds, and he cannot be flattered out of a single cask's duty.

### Motivation

He wants to keep his office and his good name through an election that will make someone angry whatever he counts. He believes the customs are the city's spine, and that a roll which can be faulted is worse than one that is merely disliked.

### Strengths

He knows every hull in the harbor, its tonnage and its owners, and he can reckon a cargo's duty at a glance. His officers are loyal to him, even those he suspects.

### Weaknesses

He cannot watch every tally-man on census morning. He is weary of the quarrel and wants to be done with it, and a man who wants a thing done fast can be hurried.

## Social

### Companions

His officers, and [[being-mirvirnlth|Mirvir Nulthumak]], the Clerk of the Council, with whom he checks the roll.

### Patrons

[[being-hrundthrsk|Hrundselda Thraskumak]], the Harbormaster he answers to.

### Enemies

The smugglers of the Run, who have twice tried to pay him and once to drown him.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]**: He is a citizen and holds the office of Customs Officer.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A count beside the count.** Tvilgorv will hire strangers to walk the quays on census morning a step behind his tally-men and count every hull again, and he wants their slates handed to him alone.
2. **The unentered furs.** A coaster has landed bales with no manifest and no mark, and Tvilgorv wants to know whose they are before either party claims them.
3. **The bought tally-man.** One of his tally-men has bought a house on the old city's slope, and Tvilgorv wants to know who paid for it without asking him.
