# Appearance {#appearance}

Gnaldrinna Driskumak is a 41-year-old woman who stands five feet six inches tall and is of light build. Her fair face is weathered from the headland, her blond hair is worn in a single long braid, and her blue eyes go to the horizon whenever she stops talking. She wears a whale's tooth carved with a ship on a cord at her throat, and her speaking voice is low and carries; her singing voice carries further. A long white scar from a whale-line runs down her left forearm, and the corners of her eyes are deeply lined from squinting at the sea.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 41. Lookout of Hvelmnes and a singer of the old songs. Wants to see the whales come back in the spring, and to be paid properly for the songs.**

Gnaldrinna Driskumak keeps the lookout of [Hvelmnes]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, the whale-watchers' village on the eastern headland of [[place-calypsashore|the South Shore]] of the [[place-calypsaisle|Isle of Kalipsa]]. From the hut on the headland she watches for whales in the spring, when their spouts bring the boats out, and for strange sails in every season. The lookout keeps a fire laid on the headland, and she lights it to answer the watch's fire-station on [Hlomfell]{.name data-type="place/feature"}. On a clear day she can see the sails of [[place-kalipsarun|the Kalipsa Run]] to the east.

In winter, when the boats are hauled up, she walks over the ridge to [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] and sings at the weddings of the old city, where the families want the old [[skill-nordmalng|Nordmal]] drinking-songs sung beside the Aureldian hymns and the shore's singers know them best. A wedding of the old city in winter goes on for three days, and she sings on all three. The money keeps her mother and her two children through the dark months.

## Psyche

### Personality

Gnaldrinna is cheerful, sharp-eyed and quick to laugh, and impatient with people who talk while she is watching the sea. She is proud of her songs and of her eyes.

### Motivation

She wants her children fed and the lookout kept, and she wants the old city to pay a singer what it pays a fiddler from the south. She loves the songs and resents that the city treats them as an ornament.

### Strengths

She has the best eyes on the shore and the best voice. She knows the old city's families from their weddings and the shore's people from the lookout, and both talk freely in front of her.

### Weaknesses

She is often away from the lookout in winter, and the village grumbles. She has seen things from the headland that she has not reported, because reporting them would bring the watch.

## Social

### Companions

The whale-boat crews of Hvelmnes, and the wedding-guests of the old city, who buy her drinks.

### Patrons

The old houses who hire her for their weddings each winter.

### Enemies

None.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-calypsa|Kalipsa]]**: She is a citizen of the city-state and the lookout of Hvelmnes.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The sail without lights.** Gnaldrinna has twice seen a hull lying off the southern coves at night this winter, showing no light, and she wants someone to watch with her and find out where its boat goes in.
2. **A wedding singer.** A great house of the old city wants three days of singing at a winter wedding, and Gnaldrinna needs a second voice, or a fiddler, and an escort over the ridge.
3. **The whales.** The spring's first whales are late, and the boats of Hvelmnes want to go further out than they have ever gone to look for them.
