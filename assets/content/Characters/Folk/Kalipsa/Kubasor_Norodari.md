# Appearance {#appearance}

Kubasor Norodari is a 44-year-old man who stands five feet nine inches tall and is of medium build. He has olive skin, a clear complexion, black hair worn short and brown eyes that move from face to face while he talks. His fingers are quick and stained with ink, and he wears a plain gray coat with a silver north-pointing arrow, his house's mark, pinned at the collar. A thin scar runs through his left eyebrow.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 44. Head of the Norodari and of the newer houses. Wants the census counted as it stands, and Tvarnidrok kept from the chain.**

Kubasor Norodari is the head of [[affiliation-hsenorodari|the Norodari]], a house of the lower quay of [[place-calypsacity|Kalipsa]] with a [[skill-valtarlng|Valtari]] trade name, and he sits for it on the [[affiliation-captnscncl|Captains' Council]]. His mother was a factor at [[place-sarmelis|Sarmelis]] who bought furs from the boats when the old houses would not, and she bought the house's first hull with borrowed money. He bought the rest. The house sails about fourteen hulls on [[place-kalipsarun|the Kalipsa Run]], keeps counting-rooms at both ends of it, and carries more of the outer quay's furs than any other.

He took the house's seat when its tonnage first passed the census floor, and the newer houses that rose with the Run's trade follow him because a [[affiliation-hsetvarnidrok|Tvarnidrok]] Harbormaster would hold the levy on the old houses' terms. All winter he has bought and ordered hulls, two of them building in the [[affiliation-hsethraskumak|Thraskumak]] yards, to change the voices before the spring census. Three of his hulls went down in the autumn fog, and he borrowed against their bonds to pay for the new ones. [the Syndicate of the Gull]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"} has refused the bonds, and the loss is before [[place-sealawhall|the Sea-Law Hall]].

:::secret {title="What the referee knows"}
The Norodari owe the [[affiliation-danvar|Danvar]] moneylenders of the quay more than the house is worth without the three bonds. The notes fall due at the spring census, and the moneylenders hold the two hulls building in the Thraskumak yards as their surety. If the Gull does not pay, Kubasor loses the new hulls on the morning they would have been counted, and the house falls below the census floor within a year. He has told nobody but his factor at Sarmelis, and he speaks in the Council as a man with money to spare.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Kubasor is quick, cheerful and never still. He reckons aloud, makes a joke of every setback, and remembers every slight the old houses have offered his mother. He is generous with crews and hard with rivals.

### Motivation

He wants the newer houses to have the weight their hulls give them, and he wants the house his mother founded to outlast the old houses' contempt for it. The election is the means. A Harbormaster of his party would keep the census as it stands and lower the levy on the trade that pays most of it.

### Strengths

He knows the Run, its weather, its factors and its prices better than any Councilor, and he can raise a cargo or a crew in a day. The newer houses trust him to keep his word, and the masters who sail for him are paid on time.

### Weaknesses

His fortune rides on three bonds he cannot collect, and the furs that made it come across the bay from [[lore-varokhiclt|Varokh]] hands and leave Sarmelis unentered. He takes the old houses' contempt personally, and it makes him hasty.

## Social

### Companions

His factors at the two counting-rooms, and the masters of the newer houses who drink with him on the lower quay.

### Patrons

He has none, and says so with some pride.

### Enemies

[[being-knilthtvrn|Knilthorv Tvarnidrok]], whose office he means to deny, and whose house's contempt he means to outlive.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The pilots' boat.** Kubasor wants a crew to go out on the eastern approaches in the next fog and see whether the pilots' boat keeps its station through the night.
2. **The witness from the bay.** He needs the man at Sarmelis who saw his three masters leave the outer quay brought over before the hearing, and the man has twice failed to sail.
3. **One arbitrator.** He wants proof that the old houses have bought an arbitrator before the hall chooses the third chair, and he will pay for it in bonds as readily as in coin.
