# Appearance {#appearance}

Lamiran Dammuni is 55 years old, smooth and unhurried, with dark eyes, neatly combed gray hair and narrow hands kept free of rings. His coat is light enough for Shamular’s warm square, and he speaks in a low voice that makes a hurried petitioner pause. He offers a seat before asking what security a borrower can provide.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lamiran is First of the [[affiliation-gldbrdshmlr|Guild Board of Shamular]], living at [[place-shamular|Shamular]] on [[place-milazun|Milazun]]. He is a moneylender sitting for the still-house factors: his house lends against the coming harvest and is counted among their trade. He holds the town’s seal and presides over a board elected for four-year terms.

He wants the town’s loans repaid and its flasks trusted in every island market. Cut attar and an unmatched seal threaten the price of the harvest securing his money. He can bring a complaint before the board; comparison of seals belongs to [Meraza Latari]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Warden, who is an officer without a vote.

## Background

**The harvest loans.** The Dammuni house lends to the still-houses for three generations. Lamiran’s familiarity is with the interval between paying for work and selling the sealed flasks, and with the losses that arrive during that interval.

**The board seat.** His lending connects him to the factors’ seats rather than the perfumers’ three. He watches licenses as closely as harvests, because a license can carry a vote as well as a livelihood.

**The square.** [[being-kdmyqbsn|Kadumuya Kubasuni]] holds the oldest perfumer’s license and its unelected seat. Her uncertain attendance does not diminish the value of that seat to the houses competing around her.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is courteous, exact about conditions and difficult to hurry. He lets a borrower propose a concession before naming the one he wants.

### Motivation

He wants his house’s money to secure lasting influence in the town whose harvests secure its loans.

### Strengths

He follows obligations through suppliers, factors and workshops. He can distinguish a poor season from an account arranged to resemble one.

### Weaknesses

He regards another person’s dependence as a reliable tie. A craftsperson who sacrifices a shop rather than accept his terms can ruin a calculation he considers settled.

## Social

Meraza’s seal cabinet supplies evidence he cannot manufacture by a vote. [[being-tndnhlrn|Tanadan Helaruni]] makes the locks protecting the town’s valuable stock, and the accusation against one of his locks threatens confidence beyond its owner’s strongbox.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-gldbrdshmlr|Guild Board of Shamular]]**: a Member of the Board for the still-house factors and its elected First.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The unknown mark.** Lamiran pays a party to trace the flask Meraza cannot match to a still-house, taking it to the seller before rumor ruins a genuine seal.
2. **The opened box.** A strongbox owner accuses Tanadan of keeping a key. Lamiran wants an account of the missing ambergris and its last sale before the board rules.
3. **The harvest security.** A borrower offers sealed stock in place of money. He hires a party to carry a sample to the Warden and witness the opening, with the borrower present.

:::secret {title="The license behind the debts"}
Lamiran’s house buys every debt Kadumuya owes through two factors who do not use its name. The intended beneficiary is the rival perfumer’s house on the square, run by a Dammuni cousin, better kept and less gifted than Kadumuya’s. He means to present the notes in the week before the next board election. Her inability to pay hands over her lease, license, seat and formulas to the holder.

The opened strongbox is a separate deception. Its owner sells the ambergris to a Besikar buyer and reports a theft because his debt to the Dammuni exceeds the ambergris’s value. Tanadan keeps no key. Lamiran need not begin knowing how this debtor manufactures his loss; a party can expose it while uncovering the First’s own purchase of Kadumuya’s debts.
:::
