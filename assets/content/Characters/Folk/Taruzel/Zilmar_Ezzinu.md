# Appearance {#appearance}

Zilmar Ezzinu is a 64-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He has olive skin turned leathery by forty years on the quays, white hair cropped close, a white stubble he shaves once a week, and patient brown eyes. He walks with a stick on a stiff left knee, and two fingers of his left hand are gone. He dresses in a clean, much-mended dock foreman's coat, and he keeps the broadsheet of the war folded in its inside pocket, soft as cloth from handling. He speaks slowly and reasonably, and he is listened to.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zilmar Ezzinu keeps a veterans' hall on the waterfront of the dock district of [[place-besikar2|Besikar]], in a tarred warehouse loft above a net store, and he is the eldest Keeper among the Besikar halls. Before each session of the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]] he puts on his good coat, walks to the Arch-Consul's residence beside the [[place-grandcnclhall|Grand Council Hall]], and reads the veterans' petition aloud to whoever holds the seal: the land, the guild places, the pensions and the voice they were promised. A councilor of Yamuzel sponsors the petition every session, and it has usually been read on the last afternoon.

He is the voice of patience in the Besikar halls. He argues for petitions, for strikes called in good order and for patience with the Council, and the younger Links call him the old man of the paper. The Compact's clerks call him reasonable.

## Background

**The docks.** Zilmar was a dock foreman of Besikar for twenty years before the war, and he could unload a hull faster than any gang on the quay. When the war came he led the dock militia of Besikar, lost his fingers to a guardsman's blade on the quay in its first month, and took the knee in the same fight.

**The peace.** He was discharged with a purse of silver and a promise. He opened his hall in the loft with the purse, and pasted the broadsheet on the end wall the same week.

**The hall.** He has kept it since. His wife died in the fever year after the peace. His son, who was a boy in the militia's boats, works the quays and lives with him above the hall.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is mild, patient and deliberate, and he believes in doing things properly. He keeps the hall's accounts to the copper and reads the names of its dead without a list. He is kind to the young Links and does not like to see them hurt.

### Motivation

He wants the veterans paid, and he wants nobody else killed getting it. He says that the Council can be shamed into paying if the halls stay patient, and he says it so well that most of the Besikar halls believe him.

### Strengths

The Besikar halls respect him, the Yamuzel halls trust him, and the Arch-Consuls have all received him. He knows every dock gang, moneylender's runner and marine sergeant on the waterfront.

### Weaknesses

He is old and lame, and his son is all he has.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]]: The head of the Qizikuni, whose Compact holds the war debt that has been read first in past sessions while his petition was read last. He has stood at her doors with the petition three times, and been given coffee and nothing else.

### Others

- [[being-rzznpddn|Razzina Padduni]]: The Keeper of a hall in Yamuzel's Veterans' Quarter, who fought beside him in the war and trusts him as her one sure friend in Besikar. He is godfather to her brother's son.
- [[being-dnshkmn|Danashu Kumuni]]: The Arch-Consul of the year, who gives him coffee and listens to the whole petition, as no Arch-Consul has before.
- [[being-llmnblnn|Ellamuna Balinuni]]: The head of the Balinuni, whom he has met at the Arch-Consul's table. She was civil to him, and he has noticed that the Arch-Consul looks at her before he answers anyone.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-unchained|Unchained]]**: an Old Hand, and Keeper of his hall.
- **[[affiliation-besikar|Besikar]]**: a freeman of the city, with no vote.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The petition.** Zilmar asks a party with standing in Besikar to come with him to the residence and stand behind him while he reads the petition, so that the Arch-Consul sees that the veterans have friends who are not veterans.
2. **The Monument's dock.** Veterans of his hall sit up in the [[place-tamzirmonument|Tamzir Monument]]'s dock at night with lanterns and boat-hooks, saying someone means to burn her. Zilmar wants to know who, and he wants his men home before the Harbormaster's men come for them.
3. **The runner.** A moneylender's runner was beaten on the waterfront and his satchel taken. The runner names a Link of Zilmar's hall. Zilmar wants the truth found before the Compact's enforcers find a version of it.
4. **The son's debt.** Zilmar's son has borrowed from a lender of [[affiliation-crimsnldgr|The Crimson Ledger]] to buy a share in a fishing boat. Zilmar wants the debt bought out quietly, before the Ledger sells it on.

:::secret {title="The sold hall"}
Zilmar's hall keeps the oath, and he has informed for the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]] since the first year of the outlawing, when its enforcers took his son for a debt and gave him back for a name. The name was a Breaker's, and the Breaker was hanged. Three Breakers of the Besikar halls have been taken in the last two years, each on the night of a strongroom, and every one of those nights was decided in his hall. He meets the Compact's enforcer on the first night of each month in a chandler's back room on the quay, and he is paid in the forgiveness of debts the enforcer never names. He has never been paid in coin, and he tells himself that this makes it something other than selling.

He believes his own speeches about patience. He has seen what happens to halls that are not patient, and he informs, as he tells himself, to keep the younger Links from dying in strongrooms.

The Old Hands of Yamuzel suspect a Besikar hall and do not know which. [[being-rzznpddn|Razzina Padduni]] has sent the date of the rising, the day of the [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]], to the Besikar halls by Zilmar's hand. He has not passed it to the enforcer. The next first of the month is before the Review.

**Hook: the chandler's back room.** A party following the theft in the third hook watches Zilmar go into a chandler's shop at midnight and an enforcer of the Compact come out of it an hour later. They are the first people outside the chandler's back room to see both men on the same night, and Zilmar knows it before they reach the end of the quay.
:::
