# Appearance {#appearance}

Danashu Kumuni is a 63-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of heavy build. He has tawny skin, a round face with a short gray beard, gray hair thinning on top, and warm brown eyes. He wears the Arch-Consul's chain of linked silver stars over a coat of good brown wool, and he smells, always and faintly, of roasted coffee. His voice is soft, and he speaks slowly. He greets every visitor as an old friend, with both hands, and asks them to sit before they have said their names.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Danashu Kumuni is this year's Arch-Consul of the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]] of the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]]. The office passes each year to the next city in the charter's order, and this is the year of [[affiliation-qudumel|Kudumel]], whose council named him on the votes of the [[affiliation-balinuni|Balinuni]], the caravan house of the city. He holds the Confederation's seal, receives envoys, signs the Council's acts and keeps the Arch-Consul's residence beside the [[place-grandcnclhall|Grand Council Hall]] in [[place-besikar2|Besikar]], where his kitchen feeds half the councilors at the noon break.

He has no more vote than any other councilor and cannot make a measure pass. What he can do is set the order of business, and a measure read on the last afternoon of a session is a measure that is never counted.

## Background

**The coffee house.** Danashu's father roasted coffee on the market of [[place-qudumel2|Kudumel]] and sold it by the cup, and Danashu turned the stall into a warehouse that buys the beans off the caravans at the Caravan Gate and sells them to every city of the islands. His sons keep it now. The Kumuni have no house in the sense the great families mean, no fleet and no bank, and his seat on Kudumel's council came to him late, on the Balinuni's say.

**The office.** The Balinuni needed an Arch-Consul who would owe them the office and frighten nobody, and a well-liked coffee merchant with no house behind him fitted. Danashu knows why he was chosen. He accepted anyway, because no Kumuni had ever held the seal.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is kind, unhurried and fond of company, and he hates to refuse anyone to their face. He listens well. He is not clever in the way the bankers of Besikar are clever, and he knows it, and he is wiser than most of them about what people want.

### Motivation

He wants a quiet year, a good table and no enemies, and he wants to go home to Kudumel at the end of it still welcome at the Balinuni's. He wants his sons to keep the warehouse and a seat on the council after him.

### Strengths

Everyone likes him, and almost everyone underestimates him. The order of business is his to set, and his table hears more of the Council's business than the floor does.

### Weaknesses

He owes the Balinuni his office and his seat in Kudumel. He finds it hard to say no, so he says yes to more people than he can satisfy.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The envoy's gift.** An envoy from Orzara has sent the Arch-Consul a gift too valuable to keep and too courteous to refuse. Danashu asks a party to learn what the envoy will ask for in return, before the envoy asks.
2. **The audit.** Three houses want the audit of the treasury read first in the summer session, and two want it never read at all. Danashu hires a party to tell him honestly what each of them will do if he disappoints it.
3. **The coffee caravan.** A caravan carrying the Kumuni coffee has been held at the Caravan Gate because the ferries are full. Danashu's son writes that the ferries have never been full in that season, and Danashu wants a party to find out who filled them.
4. **The residence guest.** A stranger has been sleeping in the residence's kitchen for a week, fed by the cook, who says he is her brother. He is a veteran with a broadsheet in his coat, and the marines of the Hall's guard want him out before the session.

:::secret {title="What the Arch-Consul has promised"}
Danashu wants to be remembered for something, one act of his year that outlasts it, and he knows the Balinuni did not choose him for that.

Danashu has promised the councilor of Yamuzel who sponsors the veterans' petition that it will be read first in the summer session, before the debt and before the toll, a few weeks after the [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]]. He has told nobody else. He has also promised [[being-llmnblnn|Ellamuna Balinuni]] that the hill tribes' grievance against her house will not be read at all, and he means to keep both promises.

He does not know that the order of business is sold. The Clerk's senior copyist writes a second copy of each night's list and sells it before dawn to whichever house has paid for the session, and this session the buyer is the Balinuni. The night Danashu gives the Clerk a list with the veterans' petition at the head of it, Ellamuna will read it before he has had his breakfast.

He has heard Tirmana Qizikuni's proposal for council seats that pass from a head to an heir, from three councilors at his own table. A closed roll would have kept him off Kudumel's council, and it would keep his sons off it for good. He will not put it on the order while he holds the seal, and he has not said so.

**Hook: the morning list.** A party that learns how the Balinuni read the order before the Council does can carry the news to Danashu, to Ellamuna, or to the Yamuzel councilor, and each of them will pay for it in a different coin.
:::
