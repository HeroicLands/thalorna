# Appearance {#appearance}

Salmuya Shilmuni is 54 years old, with a broad face, dark brown eyes and black hair streaked with gray at the temples. She wears loose sleeves gathered closely at the wrists and rubs a thumb along a seam while listening. Her quiet voice carries well across a table; in a crowded theater she cups both hands around her mouth and shouts with everyone else.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Salmuya lives in [[place-bazunel2|Bazunel]] and holds the city’s seal as First of the Council of [[affiliation-bazunel|Bazunel]]. Her fellow councilors elect her for a fixed term. The houses, crafts, academies and old families each have a voice in the government she presides over, and she needs their votes to settle its business.

She wants the harbor’s theaters full through the season, with patrons paying companies to perform instead of using their support to demand public declarations of loyalty. The company whose patron withdraws in midseason needs a paying audience and another sponsor. Salmuya offers introductions, but an introduction from the First can itself look like a demand to take sides.

## Background

**The harbor.** Her daily business runs through the painted warehouses and the steps above them. She knows the difference between a promised payment and coin available for wages, and asks a patron which one he is offering.

**The chair.** She conducts meetings by asking each speaker what decision the council can actually make. A guild warrant from another city has no hearing in Bazunel’s courts; a debt under seal does. She keeps those questions separate when a petitioner tries to present them as one.

**The season.** Proposals for hereditary seats put the crafts’ and academies’ votes at stake. She wants their arguments heard in the council itself, even when a crowded harbor is a more tempting audience.

## Psyche

### Personality

She enjoys company and remembers the part of a conversation that has nothing to do with business. An impatient interruption makes her insist on hearing the interrupted speaker to the end.

### Motivation

She wants the city’s public life to remain worth sharing, including for people whose work depends on a patron they dislike.

### Strengths

She can bring people to the same table without promising that they agree. Her practical questions expose the costs hidden inside generous offers.

### Weaknesses

She is reluctant to end a discussion while someone still asks to speak. Council business can wait too long while she tries to make a refusal bearable.

## Social

[[being-brshrskn|Abarash Rusakuni]] combines a council seat with generous theater patronage and a quarrel over the [[place-designyards|Design Yards]]’ charter. Salmuya wants his help with the companies without committing the council to his charter campaign. The magistrate [[being-tarrukeshkar|Tarruk Eshkarum]] presses for clearer sides than she likes to declare.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Seats for the company.** Salmuya asks a party to bring prospective patrons to the theater whose sponsor withdraws. She needs someone willing to pay for the rest of the season, and the manager needs terms the company can accept.
2. **The sealed debt.** A newcomer facing a foreign guild’s demand asks for her help. She sends a party to obtain the creditor’s actual bond for the magistrates, so the claim can be heard under the city’s law without handing over the debtor.
3. **A voice from the roofs.** The [Academy of the Tides]{.name data-type="affiliation"} wants its missing channel chart recovered. Salmuya asks a party to hear the cartographer’s account before taking offers from any of the buyers asking after it on the harbor.
