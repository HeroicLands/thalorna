# Appearance {#appearance}

Dammush Parimu is 63 years old, a heavyset man with a bald crown, close white beard and dark brown eyes. He speaks with a resonant voice and reads at a pace that leaves room for an objection. Away from the hall he wears a soft cap and loosens his collar. He turns his whole body toward a speaker on his left, where his hearing is poorer.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Dammush lives in [[place-sazumun2|Sazumun]] and serves as Speaker of the House of Factors for [[affiliation-sazumun|Sazumun]]. A Merchant Prince chosen by the House, he calls the guilds’ councils to sit in the [[place-houseoffactors|House of Factors]] and reads their agreements into its register before the assembled masters. The councils decide the city’s tariffs and monopolies together.

He wants the pilot fined over a grounding on the bar to obtain a hearing before the loss of a year’s wages puts it beyond his reach. Calling the House is within Dammush’s office; reversing the fine is a matter for its members. He wants the pilot’s account clear enough to answer questions from masters whose goods depend on the same channel.

## Background

**The agreement.** Dammush’s working habit is to read a bargain aloud before asking for assent. At the House the practice has legal weight: an agreement binds when he reads it into the register, and its terms appear in the arcade afterward.

**The stair.** The black board below the hall names people outside protocol. A Khelathi captain asks what offense puts his name there and receives no explanation. Dammush has to conduct the House’s business among masters whose confidence in its discipline exceeds their willingness to discuss individual cases.

**The season.** The House meets often while three foreign merchantmen lie stranded on the bar. A call to sit costs the masters time at their own desks, and he faces complaints both when he calls them and when he does not.

## Psyche

### Personality

He likes a plain account with a beginning and an end. He asks a quiet speaker to move closer rather than raising his own voice, and is readily embarrassed by a joke he mishears.

### Motivation

He wants an agreement spoken in his voice to be understood by the people bound to carry it out.

### Strengths

He catches small differences between the terms people believe they accept. His steady delivery can give an anxious petitioner enough time to finish.

### Weaknesses

He is comfortable with a properly stated agreement even when its terms are harsh. A person who objects to the whole arrangement can find him much less patient than someone arguing over a clause.

## Social

[[being-ashurazhitus|Ashuraz Hitusuni]] sits for the house with the longest standing in the hall and seeks a moneylender’s charter. Harbor Master [Nibura Visizuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"} answers to the House and keeps the tower’s flags; Dammush’s chair is in the hall below her. He needs her account of a ship’s refusal or grounding, while the House retains the power to decide an appeal.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The pilot’s place.** The fined pilot asks a party to stand beside him at his appeal. Dammush needs his witnesses ready when the House meets, before the next tide takes them to sea.
2. **The captain’s question.** A party working for the Khelathi captain asks for the protocol behind his exclusion. Dammush can put that question before the assembled masters; obtaining an answer requires confronting the people whose agreement he reads.
3. **Terms on the arcade.** A foreign merchant needs the posted tariff and a proposed sale explained before accepting an offer. Dammush asks a party familiar with the merchant’s tongue to bring both versions to a broker who can hear the disagreement in person.
