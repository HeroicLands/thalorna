# Appearance {#appearance}

Yushuduya Jakuduni is a 58-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of very thin build. She has olive skin gone sallow from years indoors, a narrow face with a long jaw, gray hair cut short, and gray eyes. She dresses in the plain dark gown of a Vezakuni officer with the open-ledger seal of the guild-wardens at her collar, and her fingertips are stained with ink on both hands. She speaks precisely and briefly, she reads every paper put before her twice before she says a word about it, and she eats at her desk, from a bowl, without looking at it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Yushuduya Jakuduni is the Kubasum, the Counter, of the [[affiliation-vezakuni|Vezakuni]], and the keeper of its treasury arm, the [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]], in the Vezakar at [[place-besikar2|Besikar]]. The Merazuni strikes the Confederation's coin, holds the bullion the moneylender houses deposit, issues master script against it and clears the day's paper every evening. She has kept it since the Confederation made it, and she is commonly held to be the second most powerful officer of the Vezakuni after the Dirabum.

The treasury also keeps the scales of the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]]. Each city's weight in the Council's votes is the bullion and deposits its houses hold with the Merazuni, read from her register at the opening of each session by a clerk she seconds to the Council. The great houses would like the Merazuni in their own hands, and the [[affiliation-qizikuni|Qizikuni]] of Besikar press the Council for an audit of her strongroom and have a candidate for her chair.

## Background

**The Danvar.** She was born in Besikar to a family of moneylenders and kept her father's counter for ten years before she went into [[affiliation-danvar|the Danvar]]'s houses in the empire at twenty-six. She spent twenty years among the empire's moneylenders, rose to keep the clearing books of a Danvar chapter, and was known as the most exact reckoner in it.

**The freezing.** When the war began, the empire froze the clearing accounts that carried every Taruzelian merchant's script, and within weeks the harbor cities could not pay one another. Yushuduya came home on the last ship the Danvar would clear for her, and in a single winter she built the Merazuni in borrowed cellars on the Guild Quarter: a strike, a strongroom, a book and a seal. The harbor cities had paper they could trust before they had a fleet that could win.

**The present.** She has kept the treasury since the Confederation's charter. The script is honored at no Vilérian house, and Helionite moneylenders grow rich converting between the two treasuries, which she tolerates for as long as no agreement with Orzara is possible.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is exact, private and tireless. She has no small talk and no patience for flattery, and she distrusts a generous host on principle. She is never rude. She simply waits, in silence, until the other person has said what he came to say.

### Motivation

She wants the coast's paper to stay good. She built it, she holds that a merchant who takes a Merazuni note on trust is owed the truth of it, and she believes that she alone can keep the trust and the truth together.

### Strengths

Nobody in Taruzel knows the treasury, the clearing and the moneylender houses as she does. The Dirabum backs her, the Council confirms her, and the moneylenders of the Treasurers' Guild, whose script stands on hers, will not see her pulled down lightly.

### Weaknesses

She has no house, no fleet and no friends among the great families, and she has never wanted any. The treasury's reserves are small, and every house that wants her chair knows it.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The clerk who did not come back.** A clerk of the ledger has not returned from a holiday on Khamisar, and his desk has been searched by someone who knew what to look for. Yushuduya wants him found before anyone else finds him.
2. **The Helionite house.** A Helionite moneylender house that converts between the two treasuries has begun asking for more Merazuni script than its trade needs. Yushuduya hires a party to learn what the house means to do with it.
3. **The second key.** The marine captain who holds the second key of the strongroom is to be replaced, and the Admiral's Council of Yamuzel has put forward a man the Kubasum does not know. She wants to know who he owes.
4. **The forged star.** Notes struck with a false eight-pointed star have appeared on the quays of Sazumun, good enough to pass at a moneylender's counter. Yushuduya wants the plates.

:::secret {title="The strongroom and the book"}
The strongroom holds less than the treasury's books say. Among the reserves Yushuduya has entered as held is the war indemnity that Viléria owes the Confederation and refuses to pay, written in the ledger as though it were gold in the vault. It was the only way to make the first script good when there was almost nothing behind it, and it has stayed on the books since. The script is sound so long as nobody counts the strongroom against the ledger. An audit would find the gap in a day, and it would ruin her and might ruin the coast's paper with her. The Qizikuni believe the strongroom is short; they do not know why.

The register that weighs the Council's votes comes from the same book. She has never bent a weight, and she knows that whoever keeps the book after her may.

The head factor of the Khelathi factorage on the Guild Quarter has made her an offer, twice, at a table with no one else at it: bullion lent to the Merazuni, enough to make the book and the vault agree, against the Confederation's acknowledgment of what it owes [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Au'Khelathu]] for the war. She has refused twice. She knows that a debt the Confederation denies cannot be acknowledged by a treasury, and that the factor is courting Orzara's treasury in the same season with offers of his own.

**Hook: the clerk who has seen both.** The missing clerk of the first hook kept the strongroom's tally one night when the Keeper of the Strongroom was ill, and he has seen both the vault and the book. Yushuduya needs him found and brought home quietly, and the Qizikuni need him found first.
:::
