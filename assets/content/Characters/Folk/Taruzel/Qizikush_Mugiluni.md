# Appearance {#appearance}

Qizikush Mugiluni is 64 years old. He is a square-shouldered man with graying black hair, deep-set brown eyes and a careful, carrying voice. His hands lie flat on the table while another person speaks. In session he wears the Vezakuni’s great gold pectoral; outside it he favors a plain coat whose stitching receives more attention than its color.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Qizikush is Dirabum of [[affiliation-vezakuni|the Council of the Guild-Wardens]], living at [[place-besikar2|Besikar]]. He presides over the [Gatitush]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} as one of its sitting wardens, elected for a renewable seven-year term. He is in his second term, with a third quietly canvassed. The council’s masters give him his authority, and the merchant princes whose coalition sustains it can make his work difficult without dismissing him.

He wants the guilds to settle their own affairs on Taruzelian terms while keeping trade across the sea possible. He refuses official correspondence addressed to him by his imperial title. A disputed credential or a cargo held for want of recognition still needs an answer, and he takes particular care over the intermediaries who carry those answers.

## Background

**The master.** His career begins in the [[affiliation-tevran|Vilérian Tevran]], where he holds substantial standing as a guild master. He knows its examinations and procedures from working within them.

**The founding.** In the second year of the secession war he leaves that service. The founding leadership of the Vezakuni falls to him, and twelve years of its charters, disputes and refusals of Vilérian titles tie its public reputation to his own.

**The council now.** Younger wardens press for stronger disciplinary powers against the merchant-prince factions. The treasury’s clearing books satisfy some questioners and leave others demanding a strongroom audit. He must bring these disputes before a council whose votes he cannot supply himself.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is punctilious about forms and patient with a nervous petitioner. He repeats a proposed concession in plain language before agreeing to it.

### Motivation

He wants the Vezakuni’s credentials to open foreign workshops and markets without conceding the empire’s claim to name his office.

### Strengths

He understands both guild systems and knows which disputes concern workmanship, which concern jurisdiction, and which merely borrow those words.

### Weaknesses

His identification with the founding makes a concession feel personal. His council also depends on the houses its discipline officers are asked to investigate.

## Social

[[being-yshdyjkdn|Yushuduya Jakuduni]], the Kubasum, controls financial business that touches daily commerce more directly than his presidency. Chief Overseer [Pazasuya Lataruni]{.name data-type="being/npc"} supports the younger wardens’ enforcement proposal. Both have responsibilities he must negotiate with, rather than absorb into his own office.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vezakuni|Council of the Guild-Wardens]]**: a sitting warden of the Gatitush and its elected Dirabum.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The returned letter.** A Vilérian official sends another demand under the wrong title while a merchant’s cargo waits for an answer. Qizikush needs a party to carry a correctly addressed inquiry through a neutral intermediary.
2. **The contested credential.** A newly chartered Recovered Compact seeks work abroad. He asks a party to obtain the foreign guild’s objections and a sample of the disputed craft before the council commits its name.
3. **The enforcement vote.** Wardens weighing the proposed powers want evidence of an actual charter violation. Qizikush pays for witnesses who can appear before the council and answer questions in person.

:::secret {title="The joint inquiry"}
Qizikush and [[being-slkmrtlthts|Solkimar Talothates]], the Vilérian Tevraks, privately prefer a joint investigation of the smuggling, false credentials and murders crossing their jurisdictions. Neither can openly ask for it. A Helionite intermediary carries the back channel and is paid by both sides without either knowing of the other payment. A party carrying evidence must decide how much of that arrangement to reveal.

The [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]]’s missing reserves are a separate secret: Yushuduya’s books count the unpaid Vilérian indemnity as held. An audit brings that gap before the council and forces Qizikush to answer for the institution he publicly defends.
:::
