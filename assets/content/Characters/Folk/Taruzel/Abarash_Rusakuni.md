# Appearance {#appearance}

Abarash Rusakuni is a 66-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of light build. He has olive skin, white hair cropped short, and bright brown eyes in a lean, lined face. He wears silk, always, and the silk is always spattered with chalk and paint. His long, knob-knuckled hands have chalk ground into their creases. He is on his knees more often than not, on the loft floor among the draughtsmen, and he gets up quickly for a man his age and talks while he does it. He talks about hulls with more warmth than he talks about anything else alive.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Abarash Rusakuni is the master designer of the [[affiliation-rusakuni|Rusakuni]], the ship-design house of [[place-bazunel2|Bazunel]], whose loft above the [[place-designyards|Design Yards]] draws a large share of the hulls built in Taruzel. He has not drawn a line on paper in ten years. He draws full size, in chalk, on the loft floor, with cord and pegs, beside the young draughtsmen. Every new galley of the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]] is built to his lines at the Naval Yards of [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]], and the League pays the house a fee on each, when it pays.

He sits for the shipwrights on the council of [[affiliation-bazunel|Bazunel]] and is the most generous patron of the theaters of the [[place-paintedharbor|Painted Harbor]]. The masters of the shipwrights' guild, most of them his kin, keep it closed to women by their votes, and he has never voted against them.

## Background

**The loft.** Abarash was apprenticed in the loft at twelve, under his grandfather, and drew his first accepted line at nineteen: a coaster still built in the yards of three islands. He became master designer at forty, and the long low galley the coast knows as the Bazunel line is his.

**The war.** In the war he drew the flotilla's merchant warships, converted from traders in the yards of Bazunel in a single winter, and he went down to the harbor to watch the first of them launched. He has never been to the Shoals. He keeps a half-model of every ship of his that fought there on a shelf of its own, and he has never put the six captured imperial warships among them.

**The present.** The League owes the house its fees on several years of galleys. Abarash has borrowed against what he is owed, from a broker of the [[affiliation-mrchntryvl|Merchantry of the Veil]] who takes part of the interest in copies of the drawings. What Abarash wants is a charter of the [[affiliation-vezakuni|Vezakuni]] that makes the Design Yards a trade of their own, free of the League, so that the house can sell its lines to any yard on the sea.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is warm, quick and easily delighted, and he cannot hide the delight. He is generous with money, praise and his time, and stingy with credit for a line. He speaks of the League the way he speaks of bad weather.

### Motivation

He wants his lines on every sea. A galley built to his drawing in a foreign yard does not trouble him; a galley never built because the League forbade the sale does. He wants to die master of the best loft on the sea, and he wants the house to keep it after him.

### Strengths

Nobody alive draws a hull better. His loft trains the best draughtsmen in Taruzel, his vote carries in the shipwrights' guild and on the city's council, and the theaters and their people love him.

### Weaknesses

He cares more for lines than for the people who draw them, and he lets a great deal go unasked so long as the lines are good. He is owed more than he can collect and has borrowed against it from people who want something other than coin.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]]: The admiral of Yamuzel and a founder of the League, whose house's galleys are built to Rusakuni lines and paid for slowly. Abarash likes him and wants to be rid of him.

### Others

- [[being-ashurazhitus|Ashuraz Hitusuni]]: The merchant prince of Sazumun, who stands behind the Merchantry's broker in the eyes of everyone on the Painted Harbor. Abarash has dined at his table and been asked nothing about his drawings, which worries him more than questions would have.
- [[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]]: The banker of Besikar who holds the mortgages on the League's newest galleys. She has written to him about seats on the councils that pass from a head to his heir, and he has not answered.
- [[being-blgnrskn|Belgina Rusakuni]]: A kinswoman, the daughter of [Jiraman Rusakuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of Pashinar, and the shipwright of [[affiliation-thetamzir|the Tamzir]]. She learned the craft without being apprenticed. He admires her work, and the masters who want her recalled are his kin.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-rusakuni|Rusakuni]]**: master designer and head of the house, holding its drawings and its loft.
- **[[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]]**: a member, by the house's bond to it.
- **[[affiliation-bazunel|Bazunel]]**: Councilor of the Crafts, for the shipwrights.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The hereditary seat.** A seat on Bazunel's council that passed to the Rusakuni heir forever would be the end of the craftsmen's vote. Abarash asks a party to carry his answer to Besikar unopened, and to bring back what the Qizikuni said when they read it.
2. **The lines on the Amradi shore.** A yard on the Amradi shore is said to be building a coaster to the old Bazunel line, which the house sold to nobody there. Abarash pays a party to cross the strait, look at the hull on the slip, and tell him whose drawing it was built from.
3. **The theater's debt.** The company of the Painted Harbor's oldest theater has borrowed against its own costumes. Abarash pays a party to learn who holds the loan, because whoever holds it is buying the theater cheaply.
4. **The galley's fee.** The League's purser offers Abarash a part of what is owed, in League privileges instead of coin. He hires a party to learn what the privileges are worth on the Painted Harbor before he says yes.

:::secret {title="The drawings and the lines"}
**The drawings.** Abarash's nephew [Lushar Rusakuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the keeper of the drawings, carries the copies to the Merchantry's broker on his uncle's order. Abarash does not know that Lushar has begun carrying more than he is ordered, to pay his own debts at the card tables, or that among the drawings now in the broker's hands is the set for the League's newest galley, with a buyer waiting in a yard of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]]. When the League's captains learn that a Bizarian yard is building to Rusakuni lines, the house's bond to the League ends, and Abarash will be the last to learn why.

**The lines.** The last two lines the loft accepted from a journeyman, [Pazasu Rasuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, were drawn by the journeyman's sister, [Helaruya Rasuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who has never been apprenticed. Abarash knows. He watched Pazasu chalk out the second line and saw a man copying what he did not understand. He has said nothing, because the guild's masters would strike the brother from the yards and Abarash wants the lines. If he is pressed he will want her inside the loft with her name kept off the drawings.

**Hook: the copy in Yamuzel.** A Vilérian agent in the roads below the Strait Fortress has the lines of the League's newest galley, bought from a Yamuzel shipwright. The League's Master of the Naval Yards comes to Abarash first, because the lines are his, and asks the party who brought him the news to find out who else has a copy.
:::
