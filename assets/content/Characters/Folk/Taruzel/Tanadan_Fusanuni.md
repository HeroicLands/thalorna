# Appearance {#appearance}

Tanadan Fusanuni wears a short wool coat suitable for the quay and carries his papers in a leather case under his arm. He asks captains about their cargo before asking about their destination.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tanadan Fusanuni works as a trade factor in [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]], arranging provisions and working credit for small shipowners. He places orders with chandlers, checks deliveries against their receipts and settles with a captain when the cargo is sold. His customers include fishing-boat owners buying a share in a hull and masters preparing a longer voyage.

## The Present {#present}

The coming [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]] fills the roads with ships and the quays with work. Tanadan wants his customers supplied before the procession interrupts their loading. He hires carriers who know the waterfront and can obtain a signed receipt for everything they deliver.

## Meeting Him

A party with a hull to provision can find him among the chandlers. He pays promptly for a completed errand and disputes any charge that arrived without a receipt.

:::secret {title="The collection accounts"}
Tanadan sits on the collective Council of Principals of [[affiliation-crimsnldgr|The Crimson Ledger]]. His managers run lending and collection on the Yamuzel quays, with handlers between them and the veterans and dock gangs they hire. He is one principal among several, with no authority to command the council alone.

The talk of a veterans' rising threatens his collections and the obedience of his hired men. He wants the veterans paid for enforcement kept apart from the debtors in the halls. A handler who receives contradictory orders may take the dispute to a manager and give a party its first chance to follow the chain above the quay.
:::
