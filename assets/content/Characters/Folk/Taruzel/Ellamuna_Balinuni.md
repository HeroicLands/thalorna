# Appearance {#appearance}

Ellamuna Balinuni is a 52-year-old woman who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. She has tawny skin weathered by years on the ferry decks, a long face with deep laugh lines, brown hair going gray worn in one thick braid, and hazel eyes. She dresses like a caravan master rather than a banker, in a dust-colored riding coat over good linen, and a tally-board on a cord at her belt. A white scar runs across the back of her left hand. Her voice carries across a quay without effort, and so does her laugh. She greets strangers in Taruzeli, Dunhari or the hill speech, whichever they answer to.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ellamuna Balinuni is the head of the [[affiliation-balinuni|Balinuni]], the richest house of [[place-qudumel2|Kudumel]]. The house never owns a caravan and takes a share of nearly every one that lands at the [[place-caravangate|Caravan Gate]]: a caravan master on the Amradi shore borrows the house's silver as a partnership (_balinu_) and pays the house its share when his goods are sold. The house keeps the ferries over the strait, the warehouses of the Caravansary Quarter and the counting house of the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]] in the city, which is the Balinuni's own.

She sits for the house on the council of [[affiliation-qudumel|Kudumel]], where the hill tribes have seats beside the merchants, and she keeps the best table in the city and seats the tribal councilors at it. The house's votes named this year's Arch-Consul, [[being-dnshkmn|Danashu Kumuni]].

## Background

**The ferries.** Ellamuna grew up on the ferries. Her mother was the house's ferry-master, and Ellamuna crossed the strait every week of her childhood, learning Dunhari from the camel men and the hill speech from the guides. She took the scar on her hand at sixteen, catching a rope that would otherwise have taken a deckhand over the side.

**The war.** In the war the Balinuni carried supplies over the hill roads when the coast roads were held by the legions, with tribal guides and tribal escorts. Ellamuna led three of those caravans herself. Her uncle, then head of the house, promised the tribes a share of the hill-road tolls once the war was won, by clasped hands and before witnesses. The house has never written the promise down and has never paid it.

**The present.** She became head of the house after her uncle's death, six years after the peace. Through the Compact she has bought the debts of the factors of [[place-pashinar|Pashinar]], the caravan town that takes the trade she wants, and she calls them in one at a time. The Compact's calls on the cities have grown tighter this year, and she collects them in Kudumel with the house's usual courtesy: a partner who will not pay finds the ferries full.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is generous, loud, shrewd and very good company. She means every kindness she does, and she counts every one of them. She enjoys a hard bargain more than an easy one, and she has a rule that she never cheats a partner who keeps his word.

### Motivation

She wants the whole caravan trade of the strait landing at Kudumel, and she wants the house's word to stay worth more than anyone's writing. A debt the house never wrote is, to her, no debt, and to pay it would teach every partner at the Gate that the house can be made to pay.

### Strengths

She knows every caravan master on both shores and every tribal councilor by name. The Compact's money in Kudumel passes through her hands, and the Arch-Consul owes her his seal.

### Weaknesses

The house's word is her pride, and the hill tribes hold the one promise the house has broken. She is used to being liked, and she reads a man's courtesy as agreement more often than she should.

## Social

### Patrons

The house has no patron in Taruzel. It is patron to [[being-dnshkmn|Danashu Kumuni]], who holds the Council's seal on its votes, and to half the caravan masters of the Amradi shore.

### Others

- [[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]]: The head of the Qizikuni of Besikar and the Compact's heaviest voice. They vote together, and Ellamuna is the one person in the Compact who makes Tirmana laugh.
- [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]]: The admiral of Yamuzel, who wants a League toll on the narrows. Every caravan cargo bound east through the channels would pay it, and Ellamuna has told him so over his own wine.
- [[being-zlmrzzn|Zilmar Ezzinu]]: An elder of the veterans' halls of Besikar, who brings the halls' petition to the Arch-Consul's table every session. She has met him there and thinks him a sensible man.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-balinuni|Balinuni]]**: head of the house, holding its capital, its ferries and its seal.
- **[[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]]**: a master of a counting house, keeping the Compact's house in Kudumel.
- **[[affiliation-qudumel|Kudumel]]**: a councilor of the city.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The called debt.** A Pashinar factor whose debt the house has called has fled across the strait with his ledger, and Ellamuna wants the ledger more than the man. She hires a party that can cross to the Amradi shore and come back.
2. **The stranger's surety.** Ellamuna's nephew, the ferry-master [Nirabu Balinuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, has stood surety (_rumukum_) for the party on the quay. She invites them to the house's table that night and asks them, as a favor between friends, for a small service in return.
3. **The Arch-Consul's guests.** Danashu Kumuni has had visitors from Yamuzel at his residence, late in the evening. Ellamuna wants to know what they asked him and what he said.
4. **The Order's mage.** A Thordimalon mage has asked to cross on the house's ferry with a sealed chest. Ellamuna wants to know what is in it before she decides whether the ferries are full.

:::secret {title="The hill-road share"}
The tribes have stopped asking. Their councilors have told Ellamuna that if the hill-road share is not paid by the end of the sailing season, the next Balinuni caravan into the hills will not come out, and she believes them. She does not mean to pay. She means to send the caravan anyway, under guards who do not know what they are walking into, and Nirabu is to command them.

She has asked Danashu Kumuni to keep the tribes' grievance off the Council's order of business. Her steward buys the second copy of that order from the Clerk's senior copyist before dawn on each sitting day, so the house reads what its man intends before he does it, and Danashu does not know. She does not trust him to keep his word to her if he is offered something better, and she is right.

**Hook: the guards.** Nirabu hires the party he stood surety for as the caravan's guards. A tribal guide who walked with Ellamuna in the war finds the party at the Gate the night before it leaves and tells them, in the hill speech, which pass the caravan should not take.
:::
