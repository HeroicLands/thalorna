# Appearance {#appearance}

Tushina Gimruni is 56 years old, tall and narrow-shouldered, with brown eyes and graying hair braided close to her head. She stands still when she speaks, even on a windy quay. Her voice is dry and low, and she leaves a question hanging until someone answers it. She folds a cloak over one arm rather than trusting a servant to bring it when the weather turns.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tushina lives in [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]] and is First of the Council of [[affiliation-yamuzel|Yamuzel]]. The [Admiral’s Council]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} elects her from its Councilors for a fixed term. She presides over its meetings and holds the city’s seal, with authority resting on the houses represented around her.

She wants the council to hear a clear account of the marked plots on [[place-khamisar|Khamisar]] before acting on the request to send marines there. Veterans say the land is promised to them; the Tanaduni hold its lease. The approaching [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]] makes every delay conspicuous. Tushina asks for the claims and witnesses without promising either side a favorable decision.

## Background

**The height.** Naval families count their standing in commands and battles. Tushina knows the order in which their claims arrive at the council, and how quickly a question about a lease becomes an argument about service at the Shoals.

**The chair.** She expects the officers responsible for the fortress, yards, marines and harbor to answer questions about their own work. Presiding over the council does not put a marine patrol or a galley under her personal command.

**The quarter.** Veterans’ halls discuss a rising while the city prepares its display of victory. She walks the switchback road to hear petitioners below the height and returns with fewer easy assurances than her colleagues would like.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is reserved in company and exact about a promise. In a private conversation she has a quick, dry laugh, especially at a story against its teller.

### Motivation

She wants the council to take responsibility for the decision it makes, rather than leaving a field officer to settle a dispute by arriving with armed men.

### Strengths

She can keep an angry speaker to a specific claim. She remembers what an officer actually promises and asks about it at the next meeting.

### Weaknesses

Her restraint can sound like indifference. People who need an immediate assurance often leave her with only another question to answer.

## Social

[[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]] presses a toll through the narrows as his house’s lease becomes a public quarrel. [[being-rzznpddn|Razzina Padduni]] wants witnesses on the terraces. Tushina needs accounts both can challenge in the chamber. The Commander of the Marines has his own responsibilities, including orders for the Review’s last boat; she seeks his answer through the council.

### Affiliations

- **Yamuzel**: a Councilor elected First of the Admiral’s Council for a fixed term.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The marked ground.** Tushina asks outsiders acceptable to both sides to walk the disputed plots, copy the marks and bring the witnesses to the council. The veterans want those witnesses present when the marines come as well.
2. **The cost of passage.** Foreign masters oppose Delzuru’s proposed toll. She asks a party to collect their stated objections for a hearing while his supporters seek the same captains over dinner.
3. **The missing yard man.** A shipwright is absent and the yards’ timber tally is short by a keel. Tushina wants the Master of the Naval Yards’ account before the council attributes the loss to the unrest; a party can seek the worker’s movements outside the yard gate.
