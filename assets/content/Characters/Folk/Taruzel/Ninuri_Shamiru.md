# Appearance {#appearance}

Ninuri Shamiru is a 32-year-old woman who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. She has olive skin weathered by years on harbor walks, black hair bound up under a scarf of good plain cloth and steady brown eyes. She dresses as well as any merchant of the Painted Harbor and wears no jewelry but a seal on a cord. She is tall enough to look most men in the eye, and she does, calmly. She speaks softly and is seldom interrupted.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ninuri Shamiru keeps the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]]'s counting house in [[place-bazunel2|Bazunel]]. Bazunel gives the Compact no seat on its council, and the Compact keeps its agents in the city instead, and Ninuri is the chief of them: she extends its credit to the houses of the [[place-paintedharbor|Painted Harbor]], values their cargoes and answers to the Compact for its capital in the city. The quays call her the Merchant-Mother.

She is one of the gentler members of the Compact, and the city has noticed. She quietly steers some of the Compact's money toward the clinic of [[being-ytlshpshtm|Yutalush Pushutum]] below the temple of [[affiliation-lizelas|Lizelas]], and she has never asked him for anything in return but his discretion.

## Background

**The counting room.** Ninuri learned the Compact's trade young, as a factor placed in a port to carry out its business, and then as an assessor who valued cargoes and set the terms of loans.

**The counting house.** The Compact made her master of its counting house in Bazunel, a city where it has money and no vote, and where its business is done by persuasion rather than by the council.

**The clinic.** She came to know Yutalush as everyone in Bazunel does, as the one healer who treats whoever comes. Since then the clinic has had a friend in the Compact.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is calm, exact and private. She is courteous to everyone and close to no one, and she keeps her own counsel about why she does what she does.

### Motivation

She wants the Compact's business in Bazunel to prosper, and she wants the clinic kept open. She believes the city is steadier with Yutalush in it than without him, and she believes a steady city repays its loans.

### Strengths

She commands the Compact's credit in a city that needs it, and the merchant houses, the old families and the guilds all owe her counting house money. Very little moves on the quays without her hearing of it.

### Weaknesses

Her help to the clinic is not something the Compact's other masters would approve. A magistrate with a grudge against the clinic could make a great deal of it.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-tarrukeshkar|Tarruk Eshkarum]]: A magistrate of the council who presses the temple authorities to bring Yutalush's clinic under their control, and whose campaign against the clinic is what her money works against.

### Others

- [[being-ytlshpshtm|Yutalush Pushutum]]: The healer-priest whose clinic she supports, and who has kept his side of the bargain by saying nothing.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]]**: a Master of a Counting House, the Compact's chief in Bazunel.
- **[[affiliation-bazunel|Bazunel]]**: a freeman of the city, enrolled in its register.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The supplies.** The clinic's supplies have begun to go astray between the harbor and the temple. Ninuri hires a party to follow the next shipment up from the quay and to see who stops it.
2. **The stolen chart.** A cartographer's chart of the channels has been stolen in Bazunel, and a factor of the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]], a broker of the [[affiliation-mrchntryvl|Merchantry of the Veil]] and a Helionite merchant have all asked after it in the harbor taverns. Ninuri wants to know which of them finds it, and she pays for the news.
3. **The surety.** A party's ship arrives with no house to stand surety for her. Ninuri's counting house lends the deposit at a fair rate, and asks the party, in passing, to carry a letter to the clinic.
4. **The audit.** An assessor of the Compact arrives from Besikar to go through the Bazunel books. Ninuri wants to know what he has been told to look for.

:::secret {title="The clinic's account"}
The Compact's other masters do not know what Ninuri does for the clinic. What reaches it is entered in the counting house's books under other heads, and an assessor who reads them closely can find it. The audit of the fourth hook is the first close reading her books have had.

She supports the clinic for the reason she gives, a steady city, and for one she does not: Yutalush treats the council's men, the houses' men and the Compact's own, and what he knows about the health of Bazunel's powerful is worth more to her kept in his discretion than in anyone's hands. She has never asked him for any of it. She is careful to make sure that no one else does.
:::
