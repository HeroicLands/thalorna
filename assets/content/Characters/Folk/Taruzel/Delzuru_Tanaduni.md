# Appearance {#appearance}

Delzuru Tanaduni is a 57-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall and is of heavy build. He has olive skin weathered and reddened by forty years at sea, gray hair worn long and tied back in a sailor's queue, a broad nose broken more than once, and gray eyes. A white scar runs through his left eyebrow. He dresses in a plain dark sea-coat with the black anchor of his house embroidered small on the breast, and nothing else marks his rank. He is big, loud and cheerful, he takes a stranger's hand in both of his, and he laughs before he has heard the end of a joke.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Delzuru Tanaduni is the admiral of the [[affiliation-tanaduni|Tanaduni]], the greatest of the admirals' houses of [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]]. The house keeps war-galleys and hires them out through the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]], escorts the convoys that gather in the roads below the [[place-straitfortress|Strait Fortress]], and lets berths in the harbor. He sits on the [Admiral's Council]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} that governs the city and in the councils of the League, which he helped found.

He commanded the house's squadron at [[lore-tamzirbattle|The Battle of Tamzir Shoals]], and the scar is the only mark of it he wears. He pays for a table at the [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]] every year, stands drinks in the Veterans' Quarter, and some of the veterans drink with him. The others remember that when the League was turned from a brotherhood of the Shoals into a shipping cartel, he voted with the shipowners, and that Tanaduni galleys sail in the League patrols that hunt the outlawed captains.

## Background

**The squadron.** Delzuru went to sea in his father's galleys at twelve and commanded one at twenty-six. When the war came he took the house's squadron into the flotilla, and on the last morning at the Shoals his merchant warships met the imperial squadron in the one channel deep enough to carry it out. He has told the story at the Review table every year since, and he tells it well.

**The vote.** After the peace the League's founders swore to keep their ships together and share the convoys and the prizes. Within two years the shipowners who held the largest hulls had taken the League's councils, and Delzuru voted with them. His new galleys were already building on Qizikuni money, and a galley that does not sail pays nothing back. Some of the captains he voted against were outlawed within the year.

**The present.** The house's newest galleys were built to the lines of the [[affiliation-rusakuni|Rusakuni]] of Bazunel and paid for with money borrowed from the [[affiliation-qizikuni|Qizikuni]] of Besikar, so they are mortgaged to a Compact house and crewed by the League. Delzuru means to sail out of that debt on a toll (_sazumush_) on every hull that passes the narrows, collected by League galleys, which in practice means his own. His daughter [Katira Tanaduni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the house's convoy master, is betrothed to the Qizikuni heir.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is cheerful, open-handed and good company, and none of it is false. He is also a shrewd judge of how a vote will fall, and he can be cheerful with a man he has just voted against. He does not like to think about the vote, and he does not.

### Motivation

He wants the house out of debt and the League above the Compact. He believes the League is the only thing standing between Taruzel and a Vylarian fleet in the narrows, and he wants the Grand Council to pay for it as such. He wants the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]'s battle-magic aboard the League's galleys, and says so to anyone who asks why the League does not want the Order expelled.

### Strengths

He is a fine seaman and a brave one, the veterans of his squadron would still follow him into a channel, and Yamuzel loves him on the Review day. He knows every captain in the League by name and most of their debts.

### Weaknesses

He owes the Compact for his galleys and the veterans for his vote, and he pays neither in full. He trusts his own cheerfulness to carry him past the people he has disappointed.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]]: The banker who holds the mortgage on his galleys, and soon his daughter's grandmother-in-law. He calls her the most dangerous woman on the sea and means it as praise.
- [[being-nzzntrshn|Nazzuna Tarshuni]]: A Free Captain who sailed in his squadron at the Shoals and was outlawed in the year of his vote. The League's patrols hunt her, and his galleys are among them.

### Others

- [[being-brshrskn|Abarash Rusakuni]]: The master designer whose lines his galleys are built to and whose fees the League pays late. Delzuru likes him, and envies him the theaters.
- [[being-dnshkmn|Danashu Kumuni]]: The Arch-Consul of the year, who decides when the toll is read in the Council. Delzuru has been his guest at the residence twice this winter.
- [[being-zbzmjkdn|Zabuzum Jakuduni]]: The captain of the Tamzir, a hero of the Shoals who refused the guilds. Delzuru sends him an invitation to the house's Review table every year, and has never had an answer.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The toll.** The foreign masters in the roads have heard of the toll, and one of them pays a party to learn how the Admiral's Council will vote on it. Delzuru learns of the party within the day and invites them to dinner.
2. **The Review table.** Delzuru wants a guest at his Review table who can sit beside the captain of the Tamzir if the captain comes, and say the right things if he does not. He asks a party with friends in the Veterans' Quarter.
3. **The battle-mage.** Delzuru has hired a mage of the Thordimalon to sail with his flagship through the summer. The Compact wants the mage's contract broken, and a party is offered good money to see that the mage misses the sailing.
4. **The old squadron's flag.** A strip of the scorched sail the Shoals squadrons fly at the Review has been stolen from the house. Delzuru wants it back before the fourteenth of Brazemandi, and the thief has left a note saying where it can be bought.

:::secret {title="The admiral's offer"}
Delzuru would sooner hire the outlaws than hunt them, and he has done more than say so. Through a League captain who still drinks in the Veterans' Quarter, he has offered the [[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captains]] a bargain: if the Council grants the toll, he will ask it in the same session to lift the outlawry of any captain who takes Tanaduni service, and their hulls will collect the toll in the house's name. It would give him thirty fast hulls the Compact holds no mortgage on, and it would put the outlaws' grievance in his pay. The offer has reached [[being-nzzntrshn|Nazzuna Tarshuni]], who has not answered. If the Compact learned of it, the Qizikuni would call the mortgage.

He does not know that his daughter pays a share of the convoy fees, quietly, to the old sailors of his Shoals squadron, who were discharged with a small purse and nothing else, and tells them it is the house's pension. He would be proud of her, and the League would ask him to choose between her and his guild.

He does know that the veterans of the Quarter are talking of more than a strike before the Review, and he has told nobody on the Admiral's Council. He thinks a rising that frightens the Compact without burning the yards would serve the League very well, and he does not believe he would ever have to choose a side in one.

**Hook: the answer.** Nazzuna Tarshuni sends her answer to the admiral's offer by a party she trusts, and the answer is a question: what the house will do about the Hitusuni receipts. Delzuru does not know what the receipts would prove if they were presented, and he wants the party to find out before he replies.
:::
