# Appearance {#appearance}

Nazzuna Tarshuni is a 38-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. She has golden skin browned by the sea, dark brown hair in a long plait tied with tarred twine, and amber eyes that do not stay long on any one thing. On the inside of her left wrist is a seaman's tattoo of two lanterns, one above the other, and both her palms are scarred by rope. She dresses as any coaster's master does, in a salt-stiff coat and bare feet on deck, and she carries an oilcloth satchel across her chest that she does not take off, even to sleep. She laughs easily and stops laughing at once.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nazzuna Tarshuni is one of the [[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captains]], the outlawed masters of the island channels of [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel Region]]. She commands a fast coaster that scouted for the flotilla in the war, owes for it to [Yazila Ilammuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the Free Captains' banker in [[place-pashinar|Pashinar]], and sails against the cargoes of the guilds that outlawed her. The Compact's cargoes come first with her, as with every Free Captain, then those of the houses that sit in the Grand Council. She lets fishing boats go, and she lets a master who shows the papers of a veterans' hall keep her hold.

A council of the captains chose her Keeper of the Receipts. During the war the flotilla's captains stored their prize cargoes in the bonded warehouses of the [[affiliation-hitusuni|Hitusuni]] of [[place-sazumun2|Sazumun]], under the house's seal, and when the captains were outlawed the house kept the cargoes. Nazzuna carries the house's receipts, a bundle of sealed papers in the oilcloth satchel, aboard her own coaster and never ashore. The Free Captains hold them as the one honest account of the war anyone gave them. Nobody has ever presented one, because a receipt presented by an outlaw would be taken from her with the outlaw.

## Background

**The flotilla.** Nazzuna was the daughter of a coaster's master of Pashinar and sailed with her father from the age of ten. In the war she was mate of a merchant warship in the squadron of the [[affiliation-tanaduni|Tanaduni]] at the Shoals, and on the last morning her ship met the imperial squadron in the deep channel. Her father's coaster scouted for the flotilla, and she took it over when he died of a fever in the winter after the battle.

**The outlawing.** After the peace she would not sell her coaster to the shipowners who took the League's councils, and she would not stop asking where the prize cargoes were. The Grand Council declared her an outlaw on a charge of piracy with the rest of the flotilla's stubborn masters, two years after the Shoals. She has been one since.

**The Keeper.** The council of captains gave her the receipts five years ago, when the old Keeper was taken by a League galley and hanged at Besikar. She has kept them through two League sweeps and a winter wreck.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is quick, cheerful and hard, and she trusts a person only as far as she has seen them act. She is fair to the crews she takes and generous to the ones who sail with her. She keeps her word, and she keeps a long count of everyone who has not kept theirs.

### Motivation

She wants the Confederation to pay what it owes the flotilla, in public, and she wants the men who outlawed her to stand in the Grand Council Hall while it is read. She does not want a pardon, which would say she had done something to be pardoned for.

### Strengths

She knows the channels, the coves and the Shoals' edges as well as anyone alive, and the [[affiliation-shoalsbrthd|Shoals Brotherhood]]'s pilots take her through. The veterans' halls of every port city will hide her, and the other captains trust her with the one thing they all hold in common.

### Weaknesses

She is an outlaw with a price on her, and she carries the flotilla's receipts wherever she goes. She would sooner lose her coaster than the satchel, and the people hunting her know it.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-ashurazhitus|Ashuraz Hitusuni]]: The head of the house that holds the flotilla's cargoes. She has never met him, and she has sworn on the Shoals' sand to see his seal proved false.
- [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]]: The admiral of the squadron she fought in at the Shoals, who voted with the shipowners within two years and whose galleys hunt her now. She does not hate him, which she finds worse.

### Others

- [[being-rzznpddn|Razzina Padduni]]: The Keeper of a veterans' hall in Yamuzel, who sailed beside her at the Shoals in the harbor boats. Nazzuna's crew go to Razzina's hall when they need a surgeon or a bed.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captains]]**: a Free Captain, and Keeper of the Receipts.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The taken merchantman.** A party aboard a merchantman taken by Nazzuna's coaster is asked whose cargo it is. If it is a Compact house's, she offers them passage to a cove of their choosing in exchange for carrying a letter ashore.
2. **The manifest.** Nazzuna pays a fair price for a manifest the League would kill to keep: the sailing dates of the Compact's bullion convoy to Helionis. She wants it from a party the League does not know.
3. **The old Keeper's grave.** The last Keeper of the Receipts lies in the felons' ground outside Besikar. Nazzuna wants him lifted and brought to the Shoals to be buried at sea, and the ground is watched.
4. **The warning that did not come.** A League captain who sailed in the flotilla warns the Free Captains before a sweep of the outer channels, by a fishing boat that comes into the coves at dusk. This season the fishing boat has not come, and its crew are in the marines' lockup at Yamuzel. Nazzuna wants to know what they have said there.

:::secret {title="What the Keeper carries"}
**The promise.** Folded among the receipts in the satchel is a paper no other Free Captain has seen: the original of the promise that the rebels' council made the flotilla's captains in the first year of the war, the prize money of every imperial ship and cargo taken in the war, to be paid by the Confederation once it stood, under the council's seal and the signatures of the men who sit in the Grand Council now. Six warships were taken at the Shoals alone. Yazila Ilammuni's ledger holds a copy, and the Compact has learned that a copy exists. Nobody has learned that the original exists, or that it sails with the receipts. Presented to the Grand Council by a freeman, the promise and the receipts together would be read. Presented by a Free Captain, they would be burned with her.

**Who wants them burned.** The house of Hitusuni would pay a great deal to see the receipts burned, because one of the cargoes they record still lies under its seal.

**The rising.** The Free Captains will be on the water at the Review if the halls rise, and Nazzuna has said in council that the promise should be read to the coast that day, from the narrows, whatever else happens. The other captains do not know what she means to read.

**The lot.** This year the captains' lot fell to Nazzuna to bring the last boat through the narrows on the fourteenth of Brazemandi. She does not know that the Commander of the Marines has put a League galley off the cape to wait for her.

**The offer.** [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]] has offered, through a League captain, to ask the Council to lift the outlawry of any captain who takes Tanaduni service, if the Council grants his toll. Nazzuna has not answered, and has not told the other captains.

**Hook: the freeman.** Nazzuna needs a freeman with no outlawry on him to carry the promise and the receipts into the Grand Council Hall and lodge them as a petition. She chooses a party she has seen keep their word, and the Compact, the Hitusuni and the Qizikuni each learn of the choice before the party reaches Besikar.
:::
