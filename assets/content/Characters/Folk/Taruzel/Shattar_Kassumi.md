# Appearance {#appearance}

Shattar Kassumi is a 51-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. He has olive skin, graying black hair and a close-trimmed beard in the old legionary fashion, and dark brown eyes. He dresses plainly in a gray coat of very good cloth. On a cord at his neck he wears the bronze token of an auxiliary officer of the empire, with a chisel-cut across its face where the cashiering clerk defaced it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Shattar Kassumi is the captain of [[affiliation-cleanshlds|The Clean Shields]], the company that keeps the grain road and the barge moorings above the river gate of [[place-orzara|Orzara City]]. He was born in the Taruzelian islands to a family that had sent its sons into the empire's auxiliaries for three generations, and he went into them himself at nineteen. He served the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] for twenty years, the last six of them commanding an _alvan_ (auxiliary unit) of Taruzelian archers in the legions of the east.

In the first month of the war in which the [[affiliation-cnfdrtntrzlnstts|Confederation of Taruzelian States]] won its independence, his unit was cashiered whole, with every other Taruzelian unit in the eastern legions, on the order of officers who could not tell which of them would defect. None of his men had deserted. Cashiering forfeited everything his service had promised him, and the clerk who did it cut his token across with a chisel in front of his men. He kept the token. He spent the war in the capital, where a Taruzel-born man was watched by everyone, and four years ago he took up a river contract with no company to fill it and had three hundred men under arms within a season.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is polite, precise and unhurried, and he answers every question about his company's money with the same two words, "good management", in the same mild voice. He keeps the old legion's habits in small things: his camp is laid out as a legion's camp, his men are inspected at dawn, and he eats what they eat. He does not drink in company and does not lose his temper, and the men who have tried to make him lose it have learned nothing from the attempt.

### Motivation

He wants what the legion took from him, the standing a man earns by twenty years' service, and he wants it for his men as well as for himself. He does not want revenge on the empire. He wants the empire to owe him and to pay.

### Strengths

He is a good officer. He knows the legion's methods from the inside, the river from four years on it, and the capital from the long years of being watched in it. His men are well paid and well led, and they know that he would not pay one of them off without cause. He keeps his own counsel completely.

### Weaknesses

He is Taruzel-born in a city that hates Taruzel, and every faction's first guess about his paymaster is the one that would hang him. His company exists at the pleasure of a contract that one vote in the war council could end and of a paymaster whose purposes are not his own. He knows that, and it is the one subject on which his calm is a performance.

## Social

### Companions

His lieutenant, a Helionite cavalryman who commands the company's horse, and his purser, a former quartermaster's clerk of the legions, are the men closest to him. Neither has been to his house in the city, and he has no family there.

### Patrons

The imperial contract is let by the war council of [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|The Vilérian Military]], on the recommendation of one of its members who has never explained it. His other paymaster he names to nobody.

### Enemies

The veterans of the City Watch and the veterans' stairs of the River Circle want him gone from the river, and some of them want him dead for the islands he was born in. [[affiliation-slntlncmpny|The Silent Talon Company]] has been hired to learn who pays him.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-cleanshlds|The Clean Shields]]**: Shattar holds the captaincy as a Veteran of the company he raised, and holds the imperial contract in his own name.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The defaced token.** A collector in the [[place-gildedqrtr|Gilded Quarter]] is selling auxiliary officers' discharge tokens from the cashiering of the Taruzelian units, and one of them is said to be Shattar's. He is wearing his own. He wants to know whose the other is and who is selling the dead men's names.
2. **The dinner guest.** A claimant's agent invites a party to the company's camp as his escort to dinner with the captain, and asks them to watch which of the captain's officers leaves the table during the evening and where he goes.
3. **The cashiering clerk.** The clerk who defaced Shattar's token is now a senior man of the war council's staff. He asks a party for protection against a threat he will not describe, and he believes it comes from the river.

:::secret {title="What the captain is promised"}
Shattar knows who pays the company and what it is kept for. His purser knows where the coin comes from, and nobody but Shattar knows what it is for. His price is the one thing the legion took. His paymaster has promised him, and every man who serves the company ten years, a grant of the citizenship from the Senate, bought with the paymaster's influence in the first Senate of the next reign. He trusts the promise, because a paymaster that pays three hundred men on the day has never failed to pay anything. He has not considered what he will do if the paymaster's choice of the next Orzas loses.
:::
