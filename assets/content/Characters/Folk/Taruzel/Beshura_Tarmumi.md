# Appearance {#appearance}

Beshura Tarmumi is a 43-year-old woman who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. She has golden skin browned darker by the sun, brown hair tied back with a strip of sailcloth and hazel eyes that keep finding the door. She dresses ashore like a fisherman's wife and walks like a ship's master, and she favors her left side when she sits. A long scar runs across that side from ribs to hip, and she does not hide it at sea. She is quiet with strangers and blunt with everyone else.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Beshura Tarmumi is a [[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captain]], master of her own hull in the island channels of [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel]] and outlawed with the rest of them. She takes the cargoes of the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]] first and the cargoes of the houses that sit in the Grand Council after, lets fishing boats and island ferries go, and sells what she takes across the strait or in harbors where nobody asks for a Taruzelian seal. The [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]]'s patrol galleys hunt her as they hunt the others.

She comes ashore at [[place-bazunel2|Bazunel]], by night, in the lanes of Zatturel behind the eastern quays, and it is the nearest thing she has to a home. Years ago she was carried into the clinic of [[being-ytlshpshtm|Yutalush Pushutum]] below the temple of [[affiliation-lizelas|Lizelas]] with a wound that should have killed her, and he closed it. She counts him a friend and owes him a life-debt, and she knows that the friendship of an outlaw is dangerous to him if the oligarchs of the council learn of it.

## Background

**The trade.** Beshura is a seaman of the islands, and she knows their channels, coves and tides as well as any master in the Confederation.

**The outlawing.** She sails with the Free Captains, whose first masters were the war's flotilla outlawed by the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]], and whose number has grown since with captains ruined by the Compact's calls on their debts and captains struck from the League.

**The wound.** Years ago her crew brought her to Yutalush in Bazunel with a wound that should have killed her, because there was nowhere else to take an outlaw. He treated her as he treats everyone, and asked nothing.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is hard, wary and loyal. She keeps her word, pays her crew to the copper and forgives very little. With Yutalush she is gentler than anyone who has seen her on her deck would believe.

### Motivation

She wants the Compact to pay for the war it took from the flotilla, and in the meantime she wants her crew fed and her hull afloat. She wants Yutalush kept out of her quarrels.

### Strengths

She is a skilled master and a hard fighter with a loyal crew, and she knows the coves and the flotilla's lantern signals. The [[affiliation-unchained|Unchained]] and the [[affiliation-shoalsbrthd|Shoals Brotherhood]] will help her as they help the other captains.

### Weaknesses

She is an outlaw, and any harbor she enters by day is a risk. Her debt to Yutalush is a lever on her that anyone who learns of it can pull, and it is a lever on him as well.

## Social

### Enemies

- **The [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]]**: Its patrol galleys hunt the Free Captains in the outer channels, and the warnings that used to come before a sweep have stopped coming.
- **The [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]]**: The cartel whose cargoes she takes first and whose bounties are on her head.

### Others

- [[being-ytlshpshtm|Yutalush Pushutum]]: The healer-priest of Bazunel who saved her life and asked nothing for it, and whom she will not see harmed.
- [[being-nzzntrshn|Nazzuna Tarshuni]]: The Free Captain who keeps the flotilla's receipts, beside whom Beshura sits in the captains' councils.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captains]]**: a Free Captain, master of her own hull and entitled to sit in any council of the captains.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The cargo.** Beshura takes a Compact merchantman with a party aboard, asks whose cargo it is, and offers them passage to Bazunel if they will carry a purse up to the clinic below the temple without saying where it came from.
2. **The surgeon.** One of Beshura's crew is badly hurt in the channels, and she cannot bring him into Bazunel by day. She asks a party to fetch Yutalush to a cove on Tashilar's shore at night.
3. **The lanterns.** A party with something to carry that the guilds would stop is told in a veterans' hall which cove to wait in and which night to show two lanterns. Beshura's hull answers.
4. **The sweep.** A League squadron is sweeping the outer channels, and Beshura needs to know which coves it will search. She pays well for a party that can find out at Yamuzel.

:::secret {title="The life-debt"}
Yutalush treated an outlawed captain and told no one. If the council's magistrates learn that the clinic below the temple has kept a Free Captain alive and kept quiet about it, its neutrality is finished, and the man looking hardest for a reason to end it is [[being-tarrukeshkar|Tarruk Eshkarum]]. Beshura knows it, and it is why she comes ashore only by night.

The Free Captains mean to be on the water when the halls rise at the [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]], and Beshura will sail with them. She has not told Yutalush, and she has asked him nothing about which of the council's men he treats.
:::
