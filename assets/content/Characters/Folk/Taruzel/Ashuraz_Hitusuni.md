# Appearance {#appearance}

Ashuraz Hitusuni is a 49-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of heavy build. He has olive skin browned by the sun, a full black beard going gray at the chin, black hair worn short under a blue silk cap, and brown eyes that are always friendly. He dresses in loose robes of blue and white, the colors of his house's breaking wave, and wears a sigil ring on the smallest finger of his left hand. He is soft-spoken, so that people lean in to hear him, and he asks after the health of everyone's children before he asks anything else. He remembers their names.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ashuraz Hitusuni is the head of the [[affiliation-hitusuni|Hitusuni]], the bonded-warehouse house of [[place-sazumun2|Sazumun]], and a merchant prince of the [[place-houseoffactors|House of Factors]]. Nearly every cargo landed on Sazumun's quays spends a night or a season under his house's seal, waiting for its tariff to be paid, and he knows to the bale what every merchant in the city has in store. That knowledge binds his house to the [[affiliation-mrchntryvl|Merchantry of the Veil]], and it makes him the best-informed man in the city.

He is intelligent, well connected and very careful. A warehouse guard, [[being-pzsnkhdzm|Pazasan Khidizum]], has spent years writing down what leaves the house's doors without a tariff, and no court in Sazumun has made a charge against Ashuraz stick. Ashuraz answers him the Taruzelian way, with accusations that Pazasan is corrupt and biased, made at good dinners to the right people.

## Background

**The house.** Ashuraz was the eldest son of the head of the house and was raised in its warehouses, sleeping as a boy in the warden's room above the main door. He learned the seals before he learned to write, and he learned from his father that a warehouse is worth what it knows, not what it holds.

**The war.** During the war the captains of the flotilla stored their prize cargoes in Hitusuni warehouses, under the house's seal, to be sold when the war was won. Ashuraz kept the receipts for his father. When the guilds outlawed the most stubborn of those captains, the house kept the cargoes, and the house became his a year later with the cargoes still in it.

**The present.** He wants a moneylender's charter of the [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]], so that his house can draw clean script against its own deposits, and he courts the Kubasum's clerks for it with patience and very good dinners. He keeps two mages of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] on the house's account to ward its strongrooms, and he says plainly in the House of Factors that the Merchantry hires whoever does the work best.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is warm, attentive and unhurried, and his warmth is real: he likes people, and he likes knowing things about them. He never threatens anybody. He lets them learn what he knows and decide for themselves what it means.

### Motivation

He wants the Hitusuni to be the house every other house must ask. A charter, a rival ruined, a cargo moved where nobody else could move it are all the same gain to him: one more thing the city cannot do without him.

### Strengths

His warehouses see everything that passes through Sazumun, and the Merchantry tells him what is coming. He has friends in every house and owes none of them. He never puts anything in writing that he could say instead.

### Weaknesses

His house kept the flotilla's cargoes, and the receipts for them are in the hands of outlaws. His house's standing depends on its seal being good, and a seal proved false would end it.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-pzsnkhdzm|Pazasan Khidizum]]: The warehouse guard who documents what leaves the house unsealed. Ashuraz has nothing personal against him, and means to see him dismissed and disgraced.
- [[being-nzzntrshn|Nazzuna Tarshuni]]: The Free Captain who keeps the flotilla's receipts. He has never met her.
- [[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]]: The head of the Qizikuni, who opposes his charter in the Compact's councils and in the Grand Council. He sends her a gift every feast day and expects nothing from it.

### Others

- [[being-yshdyjkdn|Yushuduya Jakuduni]]: The Kubasum, who decides on the charter. He courts her clerks and has never asked her directly.
- [[being-brshrskn|Abarash Rusakuni]]: The master designer of Bazunel, who borrows from a broker of the Merchantry. Ashuraz knows what the broker takes as interest.

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **The guard's book.** Pazasan Khidizum's record of unsealed cargoes has gone missing from his lodgings. Ashuraz did not take it, and he hires a party to learn who did, because whoever has it now has the power to ruin him and has not said what they want.
2. **The dinner for the clerks.** Ashuraz gives a dinner for the Merazuni's clerks of the script and needs guests who can talk to clerks: a party with a scholar or a reckoner among it is welcome at his table and asked, afterward, what the clerks said when he left the room.
3. **The Tamzir's cargo.** Ashuraz offers the captain of [[affiliation-thetamzir|the Tamzir]] a cargo of Merchantry silk for the Amradi shore and needs someone to carry the offer, since the captain will not come to Sazumun to hear it.
4. **The seal that does not match.** A Hitusuni seal on a cargo landed in Besikar is a forgery, and the cargo is the Compact's. Ashuraz wants the forger found before the Compact asks him why the house's mark is on it.

:::secret {title="What the warehouses sell"}
The Merchantry sells to both sides of the trouble coming, and Ashuraz's warehouses are where it does it. Since the winter, crates of crossbows, boarding pikes and Vilérian silver have come into a Hitusuni warehouse under a Merchantry seal, paid for by an agent of the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] who deals through a broker and has never shown his face. They go out by night to the coves where the [[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captains]] lie, and to the veterans' halls of Yamuzel. Ashuraz has also sold, through another broker, the dates and coves of three of those deliveries to an enforcer of the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]]. He chose which three. If the Vilérian hand in the crates were proved, it would be cause for war, and Ashuraz has arranged things so that the proof would lead to the broker and stop there.

He would use the rising if it comes. A rising that breaks the Compact's hold on the cities for one season would leave the Merazuni frightened and the Council in need of friends, and a moneylender's charter would come cheaply then. So he courts the man every side courts: he has offered [[being-zbzmjkdn|Zabuzum Jakuduni]] work, and passage, and more coin than a cargo is worth, and the captain has taken none of it.

**The receipts.** One cargo of the flotilla's still lies under the house's seal, in the deepest warehouse on the quay. If a Free Captain proved it was there, the house would be a thief on its own seal, and no house in Taruzel survives that. Ashuraz knows that [[being-nzzntrshn|Nazzuna Tarshuni]] keeps the receipts, and has not learned where she lies. He would pay a great deal to see the captain's receipts burned before anyone proves it.

**Hook: the wrong cove.** A party hired to guard a Merchantry delivery to a cove on the outer islands finds a League galley waiting there. The crew they delivered to believe the party sold them, and the only person who knows otherwise is a merchant prince in Sazumun who has every reason to say nothing.
:::
