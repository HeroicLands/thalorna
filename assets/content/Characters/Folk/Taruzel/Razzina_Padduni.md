# Appearance {#appearance}

Razzina Padduni is a 44-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of medium build. She has olive skin weathered by the harbor, black hair cut short as a marine's, and dark brown eyes. A burn scar runs over her left forearm and the back of the hand, and the first two fingers of her right hand are bent and callused from the bowstring. She dresses in a fisherman's smock and a marine's old belt, and wears a broken iron link on a cord at her neck where anyone can see it. She has a carrying voice, a dry way of putting things, and no patience for a man who will not say what he means.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Razzina Padduni keeps a veterans' hall on the lower slope of the Veterans' Quarter of [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]], the tenements the quarter's own people call Zabuzun, the place where men go unbound. Her hall is a long room over a cooper's shop, with the broadsheet that raised the war pasted on the end wall under a lamp, and it is full every evening. The veterans who drink there are dock workers, fishermen and old marines, and their grown children, who were small at the Shoals and have inherited the grievance with the broadsheet.

When the quarter's people talk of what they were promised, they talk of land, the guild, the pension and the voice, and it is Razzina's hall where they say it loudest. She speaks at harbor strikes, she sends the hall's men to the Fleet Review's last row of small boats, and she sees that the dead are read every year on the night of the Review. The guilds' clerks know her name and have never had a reason to use it.

## Background

**The boats.** Razzina is a fisherman's daughter of Yamuzel. In the war she sailed as an archer in the harbor boats of the flotilla, and at the Shoals her boat closed on an imperial warship on the morning of the last day. She took the burn on her arm from a fire-pot the warship's crew threw down into the boat, and she kept shooting.

**The peace.** She was discharged with the rest, with a small purse of silver and a promise of land. Her brother was among the marines who held out longest for the land, and he was among the first sent to work off a debt to a Compact moneylender when the guilds called their loans. He was bound seven years. She has not forgiven anybody for it.

**The hall.** The Old Hands of the hall chose her its Keeper eight years ago. She keeps the hall, its broadsheet and the list of its sworn, which is written nowhere, and she answers for every night's work the hall does.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is blunt, funny and steady under pressure, and she does not waste words or coin. She keeps her temper in public and loses it in private, at the people she loves. She has a soldier's respect for anyone who keeps an oath and none for anyone who does not.

### Motivation

She wants what the broadsheet promised, for the people in her hall and for their children. She has stopped believing that petitions will get it. She does not want the Confederation broken, because she fought for it. She wants it to pay what it owes.

### Strengths

The quarter trusts her, and the marine patrols, whose sergeants grew up in it, walk carefully past her door. She knows the harbor, the boats, the halls of every port city and the coves the outlaws lie in. She is calm when other people are frightened.

### Weaknesses

She trusts the people she fought beside more than she should, and she cannot imagine one of them selling her. She is angry, and the anger is beginning to decide things for her.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-trmnqzkn|Tirmana Qizikuni]]: The head of the Qizikuni, whose house holds the war debt and whose Compact called the loans that bound her brother. Razzina has never seen her.
- [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]]: The admiral who stands drinks in the quarter, who led a squadron at the Shoals, and who voted with the shipowners. Razzina drinks with him, and counts what he spends.

### Others

- [[being-nzzntrshn|Nazzuna Tarshuni]]: A Free Captain who sailed beside her at the Shoals. When Nazzuna needs a crew, a surgeon or a week ashore, she comes to Razzina's hall.
- [[being-zlmrzzn|Zilmar Ezzinu]]: An elder of the veterans' halls of Besikar and an old comrade from the war, who carries the halls' petition to the Arch-Consul. She trusts him as she trusts no one else outside Yamuzel.
- [[being-zbzmjkdn|Zabuzum Jakuduni]]: The captain of the Tamzir and a hero of the Shoals who refused the guilds. He has drunk in her hall, and the quarter counts him one of its own.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-unchained|Unchained]]**: an Old Hand, and Keeper of her hall.
- **[[affiliation-yamuzel|Yamuzel]]**: a freeman of the city.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The plots.** Veterans of the quarter have begun to plow the marked plots on Khamisar's southern terraces, saying the land was promised them, and the marines have been told to clear them before the Review. Razzina wants witnesses on the terraces when the marines come, people with no stake in the quarrel whom the Council will believe.
2. **The bondsman's bond.** A dock worker of the quarter has been bound for a debt he says he has paid, and his bond lies in a moneylender's strongroom in Besikar. Razzina needs someone a moneylender's clerk does not know by sight to learn which strongroom.
3. **The song.** A Compact factor has paid the harbor taverns not to let the veterans sing [[lore-paidattheshoals|What Was Paid at the Shoals]] this year. Razzina asks a party with a singer among it to sing it first.
4. **The surgeon.** A Free Captain's mate has come ashore with a wound that needs a surgeon who asks no questions, and the quarter's surgeon has been taken in by the marines. Razzina asks a party to find another before morning.

:::secret {title="What the halls are planning"}
**The rising.** The Old Hands of the Yamuzel halls that keep the oath, the [[affiliation-freecaptains|Free Captains]] and the harbor veterans mean to rise at the [[lore-fleetreview|Fleet Review]]. The veterans' boats close the line through the narrows, and on the fourteenth of Brazemandi the Council's councilors stand on the fortress wall within a bowshot of them. What happens then is not settled, because the rising has no head. Razzina's hall is where the plan is talked over, and she has argued every night that it needs a captain the whole coast would follow.

**The captain.** The man every side courts is [[being-zbzmjkdn|Zabuzum Jakuduni]]. Razzina has asked him, once, in her hall after the lamps were out, and he did not answer. She knows the Merchantry has offered him work and passage, and she has seen a man she takes for a Vilérian agent's servant on the Tamzir's quay. She does not know what he will do, and nor does anyone else.

**The crates.** Crossbows, boarding pikes and a chest of Vilérian silver are under the floor of the cooper's shop below her hall. They came by night from a warehouse in Sazumun under a Merchantry seal, and Razzina has guessed whose coin paid for them and has not said it aloud. The oath forbids a Link to draw blood except to save a life, and she has told the Old Hands that a rising is saving lives. Some of them believe her.

**The informer.** The Old Hands of Yamuzel suspect that a waterfront hall in Besikar informs for the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]], and they do not know which. Razzina has sent the date of the rising to the Besikar halls by the one man she trusts there, [[being-zlmrzzn|Zilmar Ezzinu]].

**Hook: the galley off the cape.** The Commander of the Marines has put a League galley of his own choosing in the roads, out of the line, with orders to wait off the cape for the Review's last boat. A party that learns of it can carry the word to Razzina's hall the evening before the Review, and what she does with it decides who is on the water in the morning.
:::
