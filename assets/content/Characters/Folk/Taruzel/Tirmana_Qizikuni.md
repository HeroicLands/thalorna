# Appearance {#appearance}

Tirmana Qizikuni is a 61-year-old woman who stands 5'1" tall and is of light build. She has olive skin lined deeply at the eyes and mouth, gray hair pinned close under a plain linen cap, and dark brown eyes that move across a page faster than across a face. She dresses in undyed wool of very good quality and wears no rings, no chain and no seal: the house's seal lives in a drawer at her table. Her hands are small and square, with a pen callus on the middle finger. She speaks quietly and courteously to everyone, and she does her own sums aloud under her breath while the other person is still talking.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tirmana Qizikuni is the head of the [[affiliation-qizikuni|Qizikuni]], the oldest banking and underwriting house of [[place-besikar2|Besikar]]. She keeps the table at the far end of the house's counting room on the Guild Quarter, where the slips of lost ships are paid, and she sits for the house among the masters of the [[affiliation-auricompct|Auric Compact]] and on the council of [[affiliation-besikar|Besikar]]. The Confederation owes the Compact for the war, and a large share of what it owes the Compact it owes, in the end, to her strongroom.

She wants three things, and says two of them aloud. She wants the [[affiliation-merazuni|Merazuni]] kept by the people who hold the Confederation's debt, and presses the [[affiliation-grandcouncil|Grand Council]] for an audit of its strongroom. She wants the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] expelled from the Confederation, because she holds its mages to be the empire's instrument in Taruzelian cities. The third she says only in the back rooms of the houses: that the councils of the five cities should close their rolls, so that a seat a house holds now passes to its heir, and no new name ever buys its way onto a council again.

## Background

**The house.** Tirmana was born into the Qizikuni as the daughter of a younger son, and she came to the counting room as a clerk at fourteen, not as an heir. She priced cargoes for twenty years before the house's elders made her head over two cousins who outranked her, because she was the only one of the three who had never lost the house money.

**The war.** When the merchant princes resolved to raise the war, the first gold of the Compact's loan came out of the Qizikuni strongroom on her signature. She lent when other houses waited to see whether the rising would last, and the Confederation's charter, the Compact's hold over the Council and the interest read first at every session all followed from that loan. She counts it the best bargain the house ever made.

**The present.** Her son died at sea in the war. Her grandson [Zarutan Qizikuni]{.name data-type="being/npc"} is the heir (_qizikum_), betrothed to [Katira Tanaduni]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the convoy master of the [[affiliation-tanaduni|Tanaduni]] of [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]]. The marriage binds the Compact's heaviest house to an admirals' house of the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]], and Tirmana arranged it.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is patient, polite and very hard to surprise. She remembers every figure anyone has told her and very few faces, and she treats the two as the same kind of fact. She is courteous to the people she is ruining, because rudeness costs nothing to the person who receives it and something to the person who gives it.

### Motivation

She wants the house to outlast the Confederation, as it outlasted the empire. A treasury in the Compact's hands, a council roll that cannot change and a Thordimalon outside the walls are, to her, three ways of saying the same thing: that nothing should happen in Taruzel that the houses have not already priced.

### Strengths

She is owed more than anyone in Taruzel, and her name on a bond is read as gold from Helionis to Kalihara. She holds the Compact's councils, and through the Council's weight she holds most of what Besikar wants. She never acts in haste and never in public until the votes are counted.

### Weaknesses

Her house's fortune is the Confederation's debt, so she needs the Confederation to pay, and a rising, a default or a broken treasury hurts her first. She sees the veterans' halls as a question of order and the harbor's anger as a price, and she underestimates both.

## Social

### Enemies

- [[being-yshdyjkdn|Yushuduya Jakuduni]]: The Kubasum, who keeps the treasury Tirmana wants. They have known each other for forty years, and they speak to each other with great respect and no warmth.
- [[being-ashurazhitus|Ashuraz Hitusuni]]: The head of the Hitusuni of Sazumun, whose house courts the Merazuni for a moneylender's charter. Tirmana calls the Merchantry of the Veil a thieves' market with good manners, and she means to see that the charter never comes.

### Others

- [[being-llmnblnn|Ellamuna Balinuni]]: The head of the Balinuni of Kudumel, the other great Compact house, who collects for the Compact on the caravan shore. They vote together in the Compact's councils and trust each other as far as their ledgers agree.
- [[being-dlzrtndn|Delzuru Tanaduni]]: The admiral whose galleys the house holds on mortgage, and whose daughter is to marry her grandson. She is his creditor and soon his kin.
- [[being-dnshkmn|Danashu Kumuni]]: The Arch-Consul of the year, who sets the order in which the audit is read. He owes the Balinuni his office, and Tirmana takes him as the Balinuni's man.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The candidate.** Tirmana has a candidate for the Kubasum's chair: a Qizikuni clerk of the Merazuni's ledger. She hires a party to learn which of the Kubasum's keepers would serve under him, and which of them would have to be bought.
2. **The broadsheet.** A broadsheet pasted on the house's bronze doors overnight names the Qizikuni as the lender that took the veterans' land. Tirmana wants the printer found, quietly, and does not want the broadsheet torn down until she knows who reads it.
3. **The closed roll.** Tirmana sends a party to Yamuzel and Bazunel with sealed letters for the heads of the Tanaduni and the Rusakuni, sounding them on hereditary seats. Each head asks the party, in turn, what the others answered.
4. **The mage's lease.** A mage of the Thordimalon has leased a house in Besikar through a Merchantry broker. Tirmana wants the lease broken by the owner, not by the Council, and hires a party to find the owner a reason.

:::secret {title="What the house owes"}
The gold the Qizikuni lent the war was not all their own. When the strongroom ran low in the first months of the rising, Tirmana borrowed from Khelathi lenders through a broker of the [[affiliation-mrchntryvl|Merchantry of the Veil]], on notes signed in the house's name and entered in none of its books. The notes lie in the head factor's strongbox at the Khelathi factorage on the Guild Quarter. Zarutan does not know they exist.

The factor has never presented a note, and he has stopped being content to hold them. [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Au'Khelathu]] spent more on Taruzel's war than it meant to, and it wants returns: at every dinner this season the head factor has asked, gently, when the houses mean to pay something down. Tirmana pays, a little each quarter, through the Merchantry's broker, and she finds the coin by calling in what the cities owe the Compact sooner and dearer. The harbor towns pay the Compact, and the Compact's tightened calls fall on shopkeepers, fishermen and the veterans' halls. She knows where the cost lands and counts it a cheaper price than a Khelathi note read aloud in the Council.

She believes the Merazuni's strongroom is short. She does not know by how much or why, only that the treasury's reserves cannot be what its register says, and she wants the audit to prove it so that the chair falls to her candidate. She has not considered what the coast's paper is worth on the morning after the audit proves her right.

**Hook: the marriage contract.** Katira Tanaduni has asked to read the Qizikuni books before she signs. Tirmana hires a party to learn what Katira suspects and who told her, and to see that the factor's strongbox has nothing in it with the house's name on it before the wedding.
:::
