# Appearance {#appearance}

Qazima Zazuruni is 45 years old, a broad, quick woman with dark eyes, black hair pinned close to her head and a strong voice that carries over a loading yard. She moves from one speaker to the next before a slow conversation settles. Nobody has seen her write anything down. She asks a clerk to read an entry aloud, then remembers which cargo and warehouse it concerns.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Qazima is First of the [[affiliation-gldbrdpshnr|Guild Board of Pashinar]], presiding from the toll-house above the ferry quay at [[place-pashinar|Pashinar]]. Her house owns the largest warehouses on the quay. She holds the town’s seal for a two-year term, while the seven-member board governs through its votes and its appointed officers.

She wants cargo to keep landing in a town whose factors’ debts are being bought by the [[affiliation-balinuni|Balinuni]]. A new gate on the pass road sends caravans back to hire hulls. The warehouses can fill while the businesses that rent them lose their independence, and she wants to know whose next payment can turn a local factor into another house’s agent.

## Background

**The quay.** Her house’s warehouses put her at the point where a cargo stops moving and starts costing money. She follows the space a load occupies, the next ferry due and the buyer who has not collected it.

**The board.** The factors and caravanserai keepers choose the board from their own trades. Its First must make the room reach a decision; the seal does not confer the Warden’s authority to hear oaths or the quay keeper’s work of berthing ferries.

**The called debts.** The Balinuni call the purchased notes one at a time. Each debtor brings an urgent individual request, while the consequences fall across the whole quay.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is brisk, sociable and impatient with an account that conceals the goods behind it. She asks for a cargo’s weight and destination before hearing why its owner deserves indulgence.

### Motivation

She wants the factors to keep enough credit to trade on their own account, and her warehouses to remain part of a working market.

### Strengths

She connects debts to actual storage, freight and buyers. A petitioner cannot easily describe the same missing cargo twice as two different losses.

### Weaknesses

Her own warehouses make every concession look interested. Speed also tempts her to settle an argument before a quieter witness finishes speaking.

## Social

[Ashuran Dapumi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Warden of the Weights, hears the oaths behind [the Forgetting]{.name data-type="lore"}. [[being-rsnmgttn|Rasunum Gatituni]] keeps the board’s books and has done so under four Wardens. Qazima needs their records, but neither office is hers to perform.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-gldbrdpshnr|Guild Board of Pashinar]]**: a Member of the Board and its elected First.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The called note.** A board factor needs three caravan debts collected before his own note falls due. Qazima pays a party to locate the debtors and establish which can pay in goods.
2. **The disputed name.** A Dunhari master on the Forgetting swears he broke no bargain. She asks a party to bring the witnesses’ accounts before the board, whose power to strike a name is collective.
3. **The road’s new price.** A caravan returns to the quay after meeting the new toll-gate. She wants its master accompanied back far enough to identify who takes the toll and what authority they claim.

:::secret {title="The Warden’s entries"}
Ashuran accepts payment to believe witnesses and enters four caravan masters on the Forgetting over two seasons. Rasunum recognizes repeated witnesses and keeps a separate copy of the entries in his writing-case. Qazima’s office gives her no automatic knowledge of either fact. Evidence brought to her can force a board hearing whose cost reaches beyond one disputed name.
:::
