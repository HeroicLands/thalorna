# Appearance {#appearance}

Tarruk Eshkarum is a 30-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of light build. He has olive skin touched by the sun, brown hair cut close and neat, and hazel eyes that rest on a speaker until the speaker stops. He wears the plain good robe of the bench and carries a writing-case under his arm. He is courteous, unhurried and precise, and he sits very still when he listens.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tarruk Eshkarum is one of the magistrates of [[place-bazunel2|Bazunel]], who sit each morning in the arcade below the council hall and hear complaints in the order they are made. He is young for the bench and rising, and he is plainly a man of the oligarchs who rule the city rather than of the crafts or the academies that share their council.

He has made one cause his own. [[being-ytlshpshtm|Yutalush Pushutum]], the healer-priest of [[affiliation-lizelas|Lizelas]], treats whoever comes through his clinic's door, the poor of Zatturel and the council's own men alike. Tarruk says from the bench that this even hand is a refusal to take a side, and the city argues over whether it can afford to take none. He presses the temple authorities, slowly and patiently, to bring the clinic under their control.

## Background

**The register.** Tarruk is a freeman of Bazunel, enrolled in the city's register and entitled to plead in its courts, and he learned the law in them.

**The bench.** He came to the magistrates' bench young and has made a name there for exactness and for hard judgments against those who trouble the order of the quays.

**The rise.** The oligarchs of the council have noticed him, and he has made sure they notice. The clinic is the cause by which he means to be known.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is cool, precise and patient. He does not raise his voice, and he does not forget a refusal. He believes in order, and he believes that a man who will not take a side has taken the wrong one.

### Motivation

He wants the council's rule secure and himself higher in it. He sees in the clinic a place where the council's enemies are kept alive and kept quiet, and he wants it answerable to someone who answers to the council.

### Strengths

He knows the law of the city exactly, and the magistrates' bench gives him a public voice every morning. He has patience and patrons among the oligarchs.

### Weaknesses

The city likes Yutalush, and many of the council's own men owe him their lives. Tarruk's campaign makes him look ungrateful to people who matter, and it has made him enemies with money.

## Social

### Enemies

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **The warrant.** An agent of a Besikar guild brings a warrant to the magistrates for a copyist living in Zatturel. The bench refuses to hear it, as the law requires, and Tarruk asks the agent, privately and after the session, where the copyist goes for help. The agent hires a party to bring the copyist out, and the copyist goes to the clinic.
2. **The complaint.** A man of the council's watch complains to the bench that the clinic treated a man he was chasing and would not say where the man went. Tarruk wants witnesses, and he hires a party to sit in the clinic's waiting room for a week.
3. **The temple's audit.** Tarruk has persuaded a senior priest of Lizelas to ask for the clinic's accounts. He wants a party who can read accounts to go through them and tell him where its money comes from.
4. **The dark stage.** A theater patron has withdrawn in the middle of the season, and Tarruk wants to know why before the council does. He offers a party a favor from the bench for the answer.

:::secret {title="The clinic's supplies"}
Tarruk has been quietly undermining the clinic's supply chains, delaying the physic it buys at the quay, discouraging the merchants who sell to it and urging the temple to give it less. He means the clinic to fail for want of supplies, so that the temple can take it over as a rescue rather than a seizure, and he means the temple to hand its keeping to men the council trusts. What he wants from it is not its beds but its knowledge: who comes, wounded by whom, and who sends them.

He has not yet learned whose money keeps refilling the clinic's shelves. When he learns that it is the Compact's, through [[being-ninurishamir|Ninuri Shamiru]], he has to choose between the cause he has made his name by and the cartel whose credit the council lives on.
:::
