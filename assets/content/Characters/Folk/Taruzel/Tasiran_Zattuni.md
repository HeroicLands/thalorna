# Appearance {#appearance}

Tasiran Zattuni is 58 years old, a weathered man with brown eyes, grizzled hair and broad hands roughened by rope. He speaks slowly, leaving a silence after a question. His wool coat smells of salt and smoke, and he keeps its cuffs rolled above the wrist even in a council sitting.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tasiran is First of the [[affiliation-cnclhtsl|Council of Hitusel]], chosen four springs running by the boat- and pit-owning households of [[place-hitusel|Hitusel]]. His family owns six boats, more than any other household in the village. That gives him much to lose when the catch sells poorly and many relatives to offend when the council settles a quarrel over beach space.

He keeps the council’s chest and speaks for the village to [[place-sazumun2|Sazumun]]’s buyers and tax collectors. The householders decide together; the Keeper of the Strand allots the beach and drying racks and maintains the reef marks. Tasiran cannot offer a stranger the channel as a private favor.

A Sazumun boat passes the reef at night without a Hitusel pilot and returns whole. He wants to find who showed it the channel before accusation divides the households whose taxes and trade he negotiates together.

## Background

**The boats.** His household’s boats make the council’s decisions part of his daily livelihood. A lost net, a damaged rack and a disputed share arrive at the same beach where his own crews unload.

**The chest.** Four spring elections entrust him with the village’s common dealings. He keeps the collectors facing the council’s chest rather than bargaining with each household separately.

**The pits.** [[being-dpmnjkdn|Dapuman Jakuduni]] sits for the tanning pits but speaks seldom. His failing hands and back, with no son or apprentice to succeed him, threaten a trade the boats also need for their leather jerkins.

## Psyche

### Personality

He hears a complaint through before asking for the other account. His slowness is deliberate; a quick answer is difficult to take back in a village that meets on the same beach.

### Motivation

He wants Hitusel to keep its bargaining strength with the city while its households continue to trust one another enough to share the cove.

### Strengths

He knows the boats, their owners and the seasonal claims on the beach. He can put a proposed price before the people who must live on it.

### Weaknesses

His large family fleet makes impartial decisions costly. A ruling favorable to his household needs better witnesses than one against it.

## Social

Dapuman brings the pits’ concerns to the council. The Keeper of the Strand holds practical knowledge of the reef that Tasiran needs, but the office’s duties remain separate from his own. Sazumun’s buyers prefer one settled annual price to forty quarrels.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-cnclhtsl|Council of Hitusel]]**: a Householder and its elected First.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The stranger’s passage.** Tasiran hires outsiders to learn where the Sazumun boat went before and after its night passage. He asks for evidence of the transaction, not a name supplied by a neighbor’s enemy.
2. **The willing apprentice.** A buyer offers money to recruit a hideworker for Dapuman. Tasiran asks a party to bring someone willing to live at the pits and learn, rather than a traveler seeking a season’s wage.
3. **The beach hearing.** Two households dispute a damaged rack after a crowded landing. He wants a party who saw the boats arrive to describe the order before the council assigns restitution.
