# Appearance {#appearance}

Kadumuya Telmuni dresses in plain, finely woven linen and keeps a pen tucked into the binding of her account book. She lets a customer finish speaking before she opens it, then asks for the receipt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kadumuya Telmuni owns a counting house in [[place-besikar2|Besikar]]. Merchants bring her cargo accounts, payments due in other ports and disputes over what a voyage earned. Her clerks copy both parties' figures onto the same sheet, and she works down the differences with them at her desk. She attends guild dinners and the Arch-Consul's receptions as a prosperous member of the port's trading community.

## The Present {#present}

The season's dearer credit has made her customers slow to settle. She wants balances paid before the next cargo goes out, and offers work to messengers who can carry a receipt to another port and return with an acknowledgment.

## Meeting Her

She receives customers in the counting room and keeps appointments by the order of their arrival. A party can hire her clerks to reconcile a disputed cargo account or take paid work carrying its papers to the merchant at the other end.

:::secret {title="The council account"}
Kadumuya is a principal of [[affiliation-crimsnldgr|The Crimson Ledger]] and shares its collective Council of Principals. Her managers pass the network's laundering revenue through legitimate cargo accounts. She knows which managers report to her; the local enforcers know only their handlers.

She wants the Vezakuni's proposed stronger investigative powers defeated before its Pazasuni can follow the accounts between businesses. A receipt entrusted to a party may establish that two apparently separate ventures share the same money. The receipt proves the transfer, not her place on the council.
:::
