# Appearance {#appearance}

Burakan Zupumi is 30 years old. He is a lean man with dark eyes, short black hair and a pale rope scar across his left palm. His clothes are patched for work in shallow water, with nothing loose enough to catch a line. He carries the Caller’s horn where he can reach it without letting go of an oar.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Burakan is Caller of the [[affiliation-shoalsbrthd|Shoals Brotherhood]], living with its crews in the hidden lagoon of [Sulimar]{.name data-type="place/site"} among the [[place-tamzirshoals|Tamzir Shoals]]. Elected at the spring muster for the sailing season, he commands the boats in a fight and a chase. At anchor he has the same voice as any Brother, and each crew decides its own affairs.

He wants the crews fed and able to move together when the [[affiliation-corsairleg|Corsair League]] hunts the channels. Their boats can cross water a galley cannot follow, but bread, tar and arrows must come from ashore. A stopped credit in [[place-yamuzel2|Yamuzel]] can strand a boat as effectively as damage to its hull.

## Background

**The boats.** He was a boy in the boats at the Shoals during the war. He knows the Elders as the people who fight there and establish the code the Brotherhood lives by.

**The keeping.** He stands as a Brother, with the share and vote that follow a full year under the [Qatilush]{.name data-type="lore"}. He is the first Caller who is not an Elder. His election gives him a command, not the Elders’ right to interpret the code or judge an accusation under it.

**The channels.** The League’s patrols make every passage to the coast a calculation. He needs pilots who can get a hull home and partners ashore who deliver what a crew pays for.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is quick to choose a course afloat and less comfortable waiting for a crew to argue at anchor. He listens closely to reports of food and equipment, even when the teller expects him to ask about prizes.

### Motivation

He wants the Brotherhood to provide for its people as well as defend its water.

### Strengths

He can coordinate shallow boats by horn and understands the difference between a channel that admits a hull and one that lets it return on the next tide.

### Weaknesses

He is tempted to treat an urgent need as permission. His authority ends precisely where a hungry crew’s debate begins.

## Social

The Elders hold the Qatilush and judge its breaches. The Keeper of the Lots counts takings and divides shares; Burakan receives a Caller’s share and a half but does not own that tally. [[being-rzznpddn|Razzina Padduni]] and the Yamuzel halls help keep the crews supplied from shore.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-shoalsbrthd|Shoals Brotherhood]]**: a Brother, elected Caller for the sailing season.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The chandler’s credit.** A Yamuzel chandler stops the hall’s credit for bread and tar. Burakan needs a party to carry payment and bring the supplies to a rendezvous beyond the patrols.
2. **The pilot’s share.** A merchant disputes the pilot’s price after reaching safe water. He asks a party to bring witnesses to the crew’s hearing at anchor, where his command gives him no deciding vote.
3. **The missing signal.** A boat fails to answer the muster horn after a patrol crosses its route. He needs a shallow craft to look for the crew before another tide removes their tracks.

:::secret {title="The dead’s share"}
The Keeper of the Lots pays the dead’s families nothing for two seasons after the League doubles its patrols. Burakan means to pay them. By night, with his own crew, he raises two of the squadron’s bronze rams and sinks them on the seaward side of the ring, away from the lagoon’s count.

A Vilérian factor at [[place-mercavia|Delalara]] offers their weight in silver for both, delivered aboard a ship off the Shoals before the autumn storms. The Elders do not know. Burakan needs a hull capable of carrying a ram and a crew outside his own.

The fifth article forbids the sale. Discovery before delivery brings judgment and divides the crews over giving him to the sand. Discovery afterward makes the Brotherhood’s breach public and costs it the Free Captains’ refuge traffic. His plan can feed the families and destroy the code that binds their protectors.
:::
