# Appearance {#appearance}

Gakhoti Gul'Zetu is a 50-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a medium build. He has olive skin, clear complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Gakhoti Gul'Zetu is the Overseer of the Sea-Gate of [[affiliation-deltaselatu|The Delta Selatu]]. The Gar-Au names him and he keeps office at [[place-garanlaghet|Gar-Anlaghet]], where he collects her customs duty at the ten delta selatu and licenses the foreign factors and their warehouses. Each selat keeps its own harbor fees.

## Background

**Formation.** He is the youngest son of a delta house that lives by the quays, and he learned the Bethuan, Vilérian and Khelathi weights as a boy in a factor's counting-room.

**The office.** The Gar-Au named him Overseer in 711 AF. He raised the duty collected by pricing the foreign warehouses honestly, and the treasury he built outweighs most Halzi'a's.

**What he holds.** The customs rolls of every delta quay and the licenses of the foreign factors, who treat him as the empire's face. A low flood leaves the duty untouched and the throne needing it more.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is genial, quick and unhurried, and counts the people he meets as carefully as the cargoes.

### Motivation

He wants the customs treasury, which he has built, to be the throne's answer to a hungry year.

### Strengths

The foreign factors tell him the truth about their ships because they have found it costs less.

### Weaknesses

He has made himself necessary to too many people and cannot refuse a favor without losing a friend.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-deltaselatu|The Delta Selatu]]**: Overseer of the Sea-Gate, over the ten delta ports' customs.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The seized warehouse.** A Bethuan factor's warehouse is sealed on the Overseer's mark and the factor asks a party to find who ordered it.
2. **The light stones.** A harbor-master reports that the weights at one quay run light against the Overseer's standard. The Overseer asks a party to re-weigh the quay's stones before the harbor-master does.
3. **The invitation.** A Halzi'a of the delta offers him a feast. He asks a party to attend as his household.

:::secret {title="The empty ballast"}
Gakhoti lands small lots of Bethuan grain duty-free at night. The customs rolls enter the ships as ballast. The grain is the Gar-Au's purchase, which [[being-khelansisgth|Khelansi Sa'Gathau]] books to another selat. Gakhoti asks no questions, because the throne owes him a favor of this size.

**Hook: the ballast roll.** A harbor-master brings a party a customs roll in which three ships of ballast have each ridden low enough to carry grain.
:::
