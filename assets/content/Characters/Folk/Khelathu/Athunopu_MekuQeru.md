# Appearance {#appearance}

Athunopu Meku'Qeru is a 38-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall with a medium build. He has tawny skin, clear complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Athunopu Meku'Qeru is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatiqeru|The Selat of Iqeru]], the sun-temple wheatland. He collects the render and holds the levy from [[place-iqeru|Iqeru]], where the temple of the [[affiliation-uqaa|Faith of Uqa'a]] holds the tithe and most of the land. He pays the throne from his own rolls, leaves the temple granaries alone, and sits beside the Canal-Warden's assize when two villages quarrel over a gate.
