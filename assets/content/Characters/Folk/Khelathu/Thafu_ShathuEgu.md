# Appearance {#appearance}

Thafu Shathu'Egu is a 50-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a light build. He has olive skin, sun-kissed complexion, chestnut hair, and amber eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Thafu Shathu'Egu is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatchathu|The Selat of Chathu]], the sea-margin of salt-pans and natron flats. His revenue is salt and the curing of the lesser villages' catch, and he sets the pan-gangs' work by the tide and the weather. His house at [[place-chathu|Chathu]] smells of brine in every season.
