Igu Reth'El'Qetu is a 38-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall with a medium build. He has golden skin, clear complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Igu Reth'El'Qetu is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatseltltr|The Selat of Selat-Leteru]], a quiet pilgrimage selat of marsh-temples and few people. He governs from [[place-yathleteru|Yath-Leteru]] by the calendar of festivals and receives the pilgrims' offerings at the landing. He commands the fewest soldiers and levies the lightest tax of any Halzi'a in the delta.
