# Appearance {#appearance}

Akhegego Zu'Gezaru is a 56-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall with a medium build. He has golden skin, weathered complexion, chestnut hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Akhegego Zu'Gezaru is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatzuger|The Selat of Zu-Ger]], an ancient royal selat and the old burial-ground of the first dynasties. From [[place-chegu|Chegu]] he keeps the tombs of kings who no longer have a house, and on the feast days he has the old king-lists read aloud in the court.
