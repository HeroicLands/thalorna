# Appearance {#appearance}

Leshi Reth'El'Khelensu is a 51-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall with a medium build. He has olive skin, sun-kissed complexion, gray hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Leshi Reth'El'Khelensu is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatkhelzrt|The Selat of Khelzuret]], whose temple-city and western-bank necropolis make it the empire's great mortuary selat. His revenue is embalming, tomb-building and the burial fees of every family that can afford a tomb. He sits at [[place-khelzuret|Khelzuret]] between the temple and the tomb-workers' guilds and takes the side of neither.
