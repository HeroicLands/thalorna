# Appearance {#appearance}

Balira Sa'Gathau is a 47-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall with a heavy build. He has olive skin, clear complexion, black hair, and amber eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Balira Sa'Gathau is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatgekhenu|The Selat of Gekhenu]]. He keeps his court at [[place-gekhenu|Gekhenu]] beside the library-temple of the [[affiliation-rethsaar|Faith of Reth'Sa'ar]] and defers to its astronomers on when the river should rise. His steward holds the revenue and the granaries, and Balira signs for the tax rolls, the stores and the calendar alike.
