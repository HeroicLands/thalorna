Minraqi Zu'Lemetu is a 48-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall with a medium build. He has golden skin, clear complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Minraqi Zu'Lemetu is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatlinmut|The Selat of Linmut]], whose island fortress at [[place-linmut|Linmut]] stands in the second cataract and tolls every barge that passes. He reads the river's stage each morning, because the channel closes to loaded barges below a set height, and he fixes the toll by that reading.
