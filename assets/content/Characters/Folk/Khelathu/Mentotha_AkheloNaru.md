# Dossier {#dossier}

Mentotha Akhelo'Naru is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatyathlmt|The Selat of Yath-Lemet]], the smallest of the upper selatu, a narrow gorge where the valley pinches to almost nothing. He holds a small court at [[place-yathlemet|Yath-Lemet]] and owes a small render, and the Overseer of the Granaries lists his selat last in every reckoning.
