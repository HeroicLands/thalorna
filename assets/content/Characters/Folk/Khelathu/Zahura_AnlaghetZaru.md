# Dossier {#dossier}

Zahura Anlaghet'Zaru is a lord commander, the military officer involved in the scandal of [[being-thamiyragezz|Thamiyra Geza'Uzu]]. He is now openly resentful of her continued elevation while his own advancement stalled after the incident; he spreads rumors about her whenever possible and has hired informants to discover new scandals he might use against her.

He is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatnlghztn|The Selat of Anlagh-Zetun]] and head of [[affiliation-anlaghetzaru|Anlaghet'Zaru]], the house that keeps [[place-eleventhgran|The Eleventh Granary]] shut. He keeps court at [[place-anlaghzetun|Anlagh-Zetun]] as a land-proud soldier whose advancement at the capital has stalled since the scandal.
