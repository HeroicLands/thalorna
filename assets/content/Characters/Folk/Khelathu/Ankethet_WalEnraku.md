# Appearance {#appearance}

Ankethet Wal'Enraku is a 49-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall with a heavy build. He has dark brown skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ankethet Wal'Enraku is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatzuzllgl|The Selat of Zu-Zalu-Legulu]], the great southern march. He holds the fortress-capital of [[place-balehen|Balehen]] as a hereditary military governor, commands the cataract forts and escorts the southern road himself. He answers the court's letters briefly and the tribal embassies at length, and he says the border war that never quite ends has been quiet and that the quiet costs more than usual.
