# Appearance {#appearance}

The senior apprentice is quick and pleasant, and wears new sandals.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The senior apprentice serves the keeper of [[place-themeasure|The Measure]] and sleeps by turns in the gatehouse with the other apprentices. He offers to show strangers the stair, and he goes across [[place-yathtelgu|Yath-Telgu]] to the temple supper with the Reader's cloak over his arm.

## Background

Every apprentice at the Measure is made to read the cut cubits aloud from the bottom of the stair before he is given any other task. On a crest morning when the Reader of the Measure cannot go down, the keeper sends his senior apprentice with the Reader's tally-board, and the apprentice walks the count across the island to the [[place-greattempluqaa|Great Temple of Uqa'a]].

:::secret {title="The paid count"}
He has been paid in silver pieces attested at a temple upriver to carry a count one cubit lower than the water stands, low enough that the priests proclaim a failed Measure. At the temple supper on the eve of the crest he puts a purge in the Reader's cup, enough to keep the old priest off the stair until noon. The new sandals and the gatehouse debts he has paid this week are the early signs.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Pleasant and quick, and easy in company.

### Motivation

He wants to rise from the stair to a place of his own.

### Strengths

He knows the stair and the wall, and the people who cross the island.

### Weaknesses

He cannot turn back once he has carried a count into the forecourt.
