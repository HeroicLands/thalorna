# Appearance {#appearance}

A Burial Club Widow wears a plain linen tunic and leather sandals and carries a small clay jar wrapped in cloth, which holds her husband's dues receipt and a handful of the club's copper tokens.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Every working village of the [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelathi]] valley has a burial club. The members pay a copper piece a month into a common purse, and the purse buys each of them the third grade of burial, a wash, a wrapping and a prayer, and a niche in the club's own vault. A widow keeps her husband's dues receipt as the proof that his burial was paid for, and the club's roll is the proof that he was a member. When a club fails, its roll goes with it, and a temple will not enter a name it has no record of. Among the Khelathi poor nothing is feared so much as dying unspoken.

## Background

She married into a working household, paid the dues with her husband and kept the receipts, and she counts as a member of the club in her own right.

## Psyche

### Personality

Patient and stubborn.

### Motivation

She wants her husband spoken over and his name entered.

### Strengths

She knows the club's members, its work-song and its formula, and she holds the proof of membership.

### Weaknesses

She has only copper to pay with and no standing at any temple.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

She belongs to her husband's club and to the village it buried, and she lives with kin in a river town.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-clublstvltadv|The Unwritten Vault]]: her husband lies in his club's walled vault in the western margin, refused the rite at two temples, and she asks to be taken to his niche.
2. **The last of the purse.** The club's treasurer went downriver with the last of its copper, and she wants a party to find out whether it is owed back to the members' families.
3. **A name for the niche.** She asks a party that can cut the common hand to cut her husband's name on a stone for his niche, because nobody at the temples will write it.
