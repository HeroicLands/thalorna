Amqelzinet Zu'Gathau is a 40-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a light build. He has olive skin, clear complexion, gray hair, and amber eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Amqelzinet Zu'Gathau is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatiuthnet|The Selat of Iuthnet]], home of the great cult of the mother-goddess. From [[place-iuthnet|Iuthnet]] he pays the healing-temples' physicians out of the selat's revenue and keeps the birth-shrines' register, which the temples cite whenever they ask the throne for grain.
