# Appearance {#appearance}

Aguri Itha'Letu is a 44-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a light build. He has tanned skin, weathered complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Aguri Itha'Letu is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatgadjuml|The Selat of Gadju-Mulu]], the Green Waters, the wettest of the upper selatu. From [[place-gadjumulu|Gadju-Mulu]] he governs a floodbasin where the water stands longer than elsewhere on the river, and he keeps a survey of its channels that he has the villages re-cut every season.
