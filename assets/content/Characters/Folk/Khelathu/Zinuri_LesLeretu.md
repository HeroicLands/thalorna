# Appearance {#appearance}

Zinuri Les'Leretu is a 44-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall with a light build. He has dark brown skin, clear complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zinuri Les'Leretu is the Overseer of the Granaries of [[affiliation-upperrivrslt|The Upper River Selatu]]. The Gar-Au names him and he keeps office at [[place-anlaghzetun|Anlagh-Zetun]], where after each harvest he reckons the grain-render of the eighteen selatu and reports to the throne what each owes. The stores themselves stay under the Halzi'a of Anlagh-Zetun, [[being-zahuraanlagh|Zahura Anlaghet'Zaru]].

## Background

**Formation.** He is the son of a Zelar granary-clerk who paid for his schooling at the scribal schools of [[place-khelunu|Khelunu]]. He rose through the bureaus by being the one clerk whose sums were never wrong.

**The rank.** A Zelar by birth, he married into the titled house of Les'Leretu and bears its name, which makes him a Zabu by marriage and gives him the standing the office requires.

**The office.** The Gar-Au named him Overseer of the Upper River selatu in 716 AF, on the strength of his sums and not of his house. The Halzi'a of the upper river have not forgotten that he was born a Zelar.

**What he holds.** The render rolls of eighteen selatu, which after two low floods no longer agree with the flood's height. He has the only complete account of who sent what.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is meticulous, soft-spoken and stubborn. He does not argue a figure, he shows the roll.

### Motivation

He wants to report to the throne what each selat truly owes, whoever is embarrassed by it.

### Strengths

His rolls have never been shown wrong, and the scribes of the eighteen selatu send him warning before they send the Halzi'a their reports.

### Weaknesses

He has no soldiers and no house behind him. Every Halzi'a he names can hurt him and he cannot hurt them back.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-upperrivrslt|The Upper River Selatu]]**: the Overseer of the Granaries, who reckons their render for the throne.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The roll that will not close.** One selat's render has come in short three times by the same sum. He asks a party to follow a barge of it to the sea-gate and learn where the sum goes.
2. **The clerk who was dismissed.** A clerk of his was turned out by a Halzi'a's steward for reading a roll aloud. He asks a party to find the man before the steward does.
3. **The offered post.** A Halzi'a offers him a house and a Zabu's household in exchange for a smaller figure. He asks a party to carry his refusal in person.

:::secret {title="The copy for Anlaghur"}
Zinuri has sent a full copy of his reckoning to the household of [[being-anlaghur|Anlaghur]], showing which Halzi'a held back how much. He tells himself it is a duty to the throne's heir. He also hopes for the nephew's favor if the succession is decided.

**Hook: the courier.** A party stops a clerk carrying a sealed roll across Anlagh-Zetun toward the nephew's household and finds it in the Overseer's own hand.
:::
