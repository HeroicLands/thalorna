# Appearance {#appearance}

The Thaz'Lekhau stands in the forecourt of the [[place-greattempluqaa|Great Temple of Uqa'a]] with the priests on the morning of the crest.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Thaz'Lekhau of Uqa'a is the High Priest of the [[affiliation-uqaa|Faith of Uqa'a]] and, after the Gar-Au, the most powerful figure in the realm. The sitting Gar-Au chooses him in consultation with the outgoing holder, a succession the priesthood has more than once contested.

## Background

Under him serve the Keeper of the Inner Sanctuary, the Keeper of the Observatory, the Reader of the Measure, the Bearer of the God's Image and the Keeper of the Solstice Rites. At the crest he proclaims the number he is handed, and the boats carry it up and down the river.

## Psyche

### Personality

Grave, and bound by the weight of the office.

### Motivation

He proclaims the number he is handed.

### Strengths

He holds the office second only to the Gar-Au in power.

### Weaknesses

He proclaims the number that arrives, and a correction follows it slowly.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

He heads the Faith of Uqa'a at [[place-yathtelgu|Yath-Telgu]], and the keeper of [[place-themeasure|The Measure]] reports the true reading to him.

1. [[scenario-nightbfrrdng|The Night Before the Reading]]: he proclaims the number the carrier brings to the forecourt at dawn.
