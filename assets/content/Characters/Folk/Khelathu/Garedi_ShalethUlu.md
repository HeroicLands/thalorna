# Appearance {#appearance}

Garedi Shaleth'Ulu is a 49-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a light build. He has olive skin, sun-kissed complexion, chestnut hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Garedi Shaleth'Ulu is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatlegirus|The Selat of Legirusi]], where the quarries and the temple-workshops cut the monuments the empire raises. At [[place-legirusi|Legirusi]] he allots the quarry faces by season and the masons by guild, and he releases no block to any house until the temple's order is filled.
