# Appearance {#appearance}

A royal guard stands at the island gate by day and night, and a sergeant reads a summons by torchlight.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The royal guard holds the island end of the [[place-bridgecomingfrth|Bridge of the Coming-Forth]] and the gate beyond it, and watches [[place-yathtelgu|Yath-Telgu]] day and night. It is a body apart from the city's [[affiliation-meglai|The Meglai]], sworn to the person of [[being-garaumeqesxv|Meqes XVI]] and to the holy ground.

## Background

Its sergeants decide who goes on. A sergeant keeps a list of everyone he passes, and the guard moves only on the Gar-Au's word.

## Psyche

### Personality

Watchful and close-mouthed. A guard tells a petitioner that everything on the island is spoken for.

### Motivation

He wants his post kept and his list complete.

### Strengths

The guard holds the island's gate and the stair head against anyone without a summons or a household token.

### Weaknesses

A guard acts on the Gar-Au's word alone.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

The royal guard answers to its sergeants and, above them, to the Gar-Au.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-nightbfrrdng|The Night Before the Reading]]: a sergeant reads the party's summons twice by torchlight and lists the people who crossed to the island.
