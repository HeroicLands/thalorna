# Appearance {#appearance}

Ahmuze Qelt'Leru is a 54-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a light build. He has dark brown skin, sun-kissed complexion, chestnut hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ahmuze Qelt'Leru is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-seltkhqtmntq|The Selat of Khuqet-Imntiqa]], the far western desert march, and keeps his court at [[place-khuqet|Khuqet]] among the oasis-forts. He knows the trade-tracks of the western desert as a caravan master does, taxes by what he sees on them, and pays the oasis-fort commanders out of the tolls.
