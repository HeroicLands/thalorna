# Appearance {#appearance}

Khelansi Sa'Gathau is a 51-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall with a medium build. He has golden skin, clear complexion, gray hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Khelansi Sa'Gathau is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-capitalselat|The Capital Selat]], the central selat of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]], and keeps his court in [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]]. The Gar-Au names the Halzi'a of the capital and keeps him at her pleasure, and in practice he is a senior officer of her court.

## Background

**Formation.** He is the second son of a scribal Zabu household, sent to the scribal schools of [[place-khelunu|Khelunu]] and brought home reckoning. He measured fields after the flood for the bureaus through his twenties.

**The office.** In his thirties he served as Halzi'a's Steward of the Capital Selat and learned the granaries that feed the capital between floods. The Gar-Au named him Halzi'a in 714 AF, passing over two Zabu of older houses.

**What he holds.** The capital's granaries, the canals round the sacred island, the city guard of the Meglai and, across the river, the guard of [[place-zugezer|Zu-Gezer]]. Two low floods have turned his routine reckoning into evidence.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is precise, dry and courteous to scribes, and wary of nobles. He speaks in numbers and trusts a tally-cord over an assurance.

### Motivation

He wants the capital's granaries to carry the city to a good flood, so that nobody in Galezkara goes hungry on his reckoning.

### Strengths

He reads a granary account as others read a face, and the capital's scribes would follow him anywhere.

### Weaknesses

He cannot hand the Gar-Au a figure she does not want, and he pads the count with hope.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-capitalselat|The Capital Selat]]**: its Halzi'a, answering to the Gar-Au alone.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The second count.** The same granary has given two different tallies in one month. He asks a party that belongs to no bureau to count it by hand.
2. **The necropolis payroll.** The Zu-Gezer guard has been paid in arrears, and he wants strangers to learn why without alarming the captain.
3. **The west-bank lamps.** The necropolis watch reports lamps moving on the west bank after the gates close. He asks a party to learn who is out there before the captain of the guard does.
