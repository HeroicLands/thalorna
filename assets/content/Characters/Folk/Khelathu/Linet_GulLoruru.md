# Dossier {#dossier}

Linet Gul'Loruru is a merchant prince and wealthy spice trader from the eastern provinces, a member of the Zebequ'Zut. He depended on the Iqe'Lêru network long before the death of Gethor and now relies on the combined temple-and-commercial authority of [[being-thaluraiqelr|Thalura Iqe'Lêru]] for political protection, providing exotic goods and useful intelligence from foreign ports in return.
