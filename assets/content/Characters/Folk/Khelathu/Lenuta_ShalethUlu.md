# Appearance {#appearance}

Lenuta Shaleth'Ulu is a 42-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall with a light build. She has tanned skin, clear complexion, dark brown hair, and amber eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lenuta Shaleth'Ulu is the warden of [[place-khelautithnt|Khelaut-Ithnet]], the vast general necropolis of [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]], where everyone below the throne is buried. She keeps the graded plots and the roll of those who lie in them, assigns the sand-graves of the poor and the mastabas of the nobility from the same register, and walks the fields each morning against those who dig where they have no right.
