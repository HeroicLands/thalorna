# Dossier {#dossier}

Linnuseret Gaset'Zabu is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatzbtrlpt|The Selat of Zebt-Relepet]], a workmanlike mortuary selat of cavern-tombs and natron. At [[place-zebtrelepet|Zebt-Relepet]] he sells natron to the embalmers of [[place-khelzuret|Khelzuret]], charges the tomb-diggers by the cavern, and keeps his accounts as plainly as he keeps his tombs.
