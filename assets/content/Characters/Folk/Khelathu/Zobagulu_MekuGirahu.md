# Dossier {#dossier}

Zobagulu Meku'Girahu is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatltchlsr|The Selat of Lut-Chelsur]], an eastern marsh of crocodile temples and river-beast hunters. At [[place-lutchelsur|Lut-Chelsur]] he settles the quarrels between the temple that keeps the sacred animals and the hunters who kill the ones that are not sacred.
