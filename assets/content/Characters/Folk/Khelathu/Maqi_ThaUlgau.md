# Appearance {#appearance}

Maqi Tha'Ulgau is a 47-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall with a heavy build. He has olive skin, clear complexion, gray hair, and amber eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Maqi Tha'Ulgau is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatzuqeztu|The Selat of Zu-Qeztu]], the western grazing margin, and head of [[affiliation-thaulgau|Tha'Ulgau]], the kin of the hill-nomad conquerors. He breeds the horses that draw the empire's chariots on the pastures around [[place-zileti|Zileti]]. He rides the margin each season to count foals and read the tracks of raiders, and he trusts the herders' report over the garrison's.
