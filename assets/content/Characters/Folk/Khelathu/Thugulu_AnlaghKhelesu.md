Thugulu Anlagh'Khelesu is a 53-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall with a heavy build. He has tanned skin, sun-kissed complexion, chestnut hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Thugulu Anlagh'Khelesu is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatanztqlt|The Selat of Anzet-Qelt]], where the jackal cult keeps the desert-edge tombs. He answers to the embalmers for the tombs' upkeep and to the Overseer of the Granaries for his render, and he drills his tomb-guards at [[place-anzet|Anzet]] more often than he audits his granaries.
