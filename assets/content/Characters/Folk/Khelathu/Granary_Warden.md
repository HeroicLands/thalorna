# Appearance {#appearance}

A granary warden walks the wall of a granary row.

# Dossier {#dossier}

A Halzi'a's wardens walk the wall of his granaries and keep the door bricked. In [[place-anlaghzetun|Anlagh-Zetun]] they are [[being-zahuraanlagh|Zahura Anlaghet'Zaru]]'s wardens on the granary row.

## Background

The wardens keep the domes shut when the Halzi'a wants his selat shown full in the Overseer's reckoning.

## Psyche

### Personality

Watchful and loyal to the selat that feeds them.

### Motivation

He wants the granary door kept shut.

### Strengths

He knows the granary row and who comes and goes on it.

### Weaknesses

He opens nothing without his Halzi'a's word.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

A warden answers to the Halzi'a of his selat.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-rndrgorge|The Render of the Gorge]]: Zahura's wardens stand on the granary row when the party asks him to open the domes.
