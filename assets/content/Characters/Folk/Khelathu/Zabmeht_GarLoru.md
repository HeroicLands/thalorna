Zabmeht Gar'Loru is a 51-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall with a heavy build. He has golden skin, sun-kissed complexion, chestnut hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zabmeht Gar'Loru is the hereditary Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatbelgnzl|The Selat of Belgen-Zalu]], holding [[place-belgen|Belgen]] at the end of the empire's writ, where the last fort stands before the tribal frontier. He keeps the garrison at full muster and the tribal envoys waiting in the outer court, and he reads the Gar-Au's decrees in the order the courier brings them, whatever their dates. What he promises a frontier chief, he does.
