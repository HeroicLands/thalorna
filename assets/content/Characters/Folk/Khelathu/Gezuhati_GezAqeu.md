# Appearance {#appearance}

Gezuhati Gez'Aqeu is a 43-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall with a heavy build. He has golden skin, clear complexion, gray hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Gezuhati Gez'Aqeu is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatgrshbzt|The Selat of Gar-Shebazet]], whose marshes supply the empire's papyrus. He oversees the cutting and the paper-makers at [[place-garshebazet|Gar-Shebazet]] and sells the best sheets to the temple scribes at a price he fixes himself. The bureaus of the capital use his price as the empire's paper rate.
