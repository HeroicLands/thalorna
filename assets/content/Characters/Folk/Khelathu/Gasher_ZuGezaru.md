# Appearance {#appearance}

Gasher Zu'Gezaru is a 48-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall with a medium build. He has dark brown skin, sun-kissed complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Gasher Zu'Gezaru is the Captain of the Necropolis Guard of [[affiliation-capitalselat|The Capital Selat]], the commander of the guard of [[place-zugezer|Zu-Gezer]] and of the burial fields across the river from the capital. He is a Zabu of a house that has furnished the guard's captains for as long as the royal dead have lain at Zu-Gezer, and he walks the royal tombs on the night before each Inundation to see that the seals are whole.
