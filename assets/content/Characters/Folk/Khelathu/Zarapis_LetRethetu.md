# Appearance {#appearance}

Zarapis Let'Rethetu is a 55-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a light build. He has golden skin, sun-kissed complexion, dark brown hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zarapis Let'Rethetu is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatgarzant|The Selat of Gar-Zanit]], whose flax fields feed the linen-weaving towns that clothe the empire. From [[place-garzanit|Gar-Zanit]] he sets the retting season and the loom-guilds' quotas, and his steward carries bolts of the finest weave to the capital after each flood.
