# Appearance {#appearance}

Genreku Let'Lerau is a 48-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall with a medium build. He has tanned skin, weathered complexion, gray hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Genreku Let'Lerau is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatzuzlqtt|The Selat of Zu-Zel-Iaqtet]], the eastern mining march. He holds the mines, the smelter-towns and the quarry-roads as a hereditary military governor and keeps his court at [[place-balqu|Balku]], where his Steward keeps the granaries and the corvée. He is a soldier first, and his court sits beside that of the Commander of the Marches.
