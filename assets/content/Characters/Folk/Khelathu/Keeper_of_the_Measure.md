# Appearance {#appearance}

The keeper is an old man who sits at the top step of the stair with a lamp beside him, in the plain dress of the gauge's servants.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The keeper of [[place-themeasure|The Measure]] stays at the gauge with his apprentices while the royal guard watches the gates of [[place-yathtelgu|Yath-Telgu]] around it. He has not left the stair head on a crest night in thirty years, and he keeps a lamp on the top step.

## Background

He makes every newcomer read the cut cubits aloud from the bottom of the stair before he lets them stand at the top. The rule he teaches every apprentice is that the number is read as the water stands, and that it is not bent for the god-king or for anyone.

## Psyche

### Personality

Exacting and unhurried, and attached to the stair above anything beyond it.

### Motivation

He wants the number carried to the [[place-greattempluqaa|Great Temple of Uqa'a]] exactly as the water stands.

### Strengths

He knows the wall cubit by cubit and trusts what he has seen by his own lamp.

### Weaknesses

He does not leave the stair head, so what is said at the temple supper or on the island reaches him late. On a crest morning when the Reader of the Measure cannot go down, his practice is to send his senior apprentice with the Reader's tally-board.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

He keeps the Measure for the priests of the [[affiliation-uqaa|Faith of Uqa'a]]. His apprentices sleep by turns in the gatehouse, and the Reader of the Measure goes down his stair each day through [[lore-azletssn|Azlet]], the Inundation.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-nightbfrrdng|The Night Before the Reading]]: on the eve of the crest the keeper takes on extra hands to keep the curious off the stair head, and at dawn he sends his senior apprentice down with the tally-board.
