# Appearance {#appearance}

Anlagher Gar'Raku is a 57-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall with a heavy build. He has tanned skin, weathered complexion, gray hair, and amber eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Anlagher Gar'Raku is the Commander of the Marches of [[affiliation-borderselatu|The Border Selatu]]. He is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatiaqetlq|The Selat of Iaqet-Leqa]], the dry garrison selat of the eastern wells, and the Gar-Au named him Commander from among the frontier Halzi'a. He keeps his court at [[place-balqu|Balku]], beside the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatzuzlqtt|The Selat of Zu-Zel-Iaqtet]], where he calls the ten marches' garrisons and levies together when a raid or a war runs past one selat.

## Background

**Formation.** He is the son of a house of soldiers and was trained in the officer schools at [[place-garqeztu|Gar-Qeztu]] to command chariots. He spent his twenties against the herders of the western margin.

**The office.** He took the Commander's baton at fifty-three, still holding Iaqet-Leqa, when the Gar-Au wanted a march-holder who owed her more than he owed his neighbors.

**What he holds.** The pay and the loyalty of ten garrisons. The pay of ten garrisons passes through his hands.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is blunt, tireless and fair to the men who serve him, and short with those who do not.

### Motivation

He wants his garrisons paid in full and on time, so that none of the ten marches ever learns to follow its paymaster instead of its Commander.

### Strengths

The garrison commanders trust his word, and he can raise every march in a season.

### Weaknesses

He mistakes what the garrisons need for what the throne is owed, and he does not ask the throne first.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-borderselatu|The Border Selatu]]**: Commander of the Marches over all ten garrisons.
- **[[affiliation-selatiaqetlq|The Selat of Iaqet-Leqa]]**: Halzi'a.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The tolled wells.** Two Halzi'a quarrel over a well on their shared margin, and each claims the Commander's judgment. He asks a party to survey the well and give him a ruling he can sign.
2. **The horsemen's offer.** An embassy from the Okharik King offers riders to any general who asks, and he wants a party to travel with the embassy as his eyes.
3. **The late pay.** A garrison commander writes that his men have gone unpaid for two months. Anlagher says the pay left Balku on time and asks a party to find where it went.

:::secret {title="The crown's share"}
For two years Anlagher has paid the garrisons out of the crown's share of the Balku mines, which the Overseer of the Mines weighs, and reported the full share to the treasury. The Overseer's roll shows the gap. He means to repay it when the flood returns, and he has told no one.

**Hook: the mine roll.** A clerk of the Overseer of the Mines brings a party a roll that does not match the gold sent to the capital and asks who should read it.
:::
