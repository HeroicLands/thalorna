Meranlaghu Shethes'Ralu is a 42-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall with a medium build. He has tanned skin, sun-kissed complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Meranlaghu Shethes'Ralu is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatmaguthn|The Selat of Magu-Thelqen]], which holds the planned temple-city of a zealous dynasty, half empty and still inhabited. He keeps the occupied quarter of [[place-maguathen|Magu-Thelqen]] in repair and the abandoned half closed, and he charges nothing to anyone who asks to live in the empty streets.
