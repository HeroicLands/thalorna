Imsithe Melu'Khelariu is a 41-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall with a medium build. He has dark brown skin, clear complexion, chestnut hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Imsithe Melu'Khelariu is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatizetge|The Selat of Izet-Ge]], whose date-palm plantations and wine-presses around [[place-izet|Izet]] make the delta's sweet wines. He is a generous host and a careful assessor: his tax on the vintage is the same for the smallest grower and the greatest.
