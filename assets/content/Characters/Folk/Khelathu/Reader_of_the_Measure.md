# Appearance {#appearance}

The Reader is an old priest of the Faith of Uqa'a who wears a cloak for the walk across the island.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Reader of the Measure is the office of the [[affiliation-uqaa|Faith of Uqa'a]] that reads the gauge daily through [[lore-azletssn|Azlet]], the Inundation. The Reader goes down to the stair, reads where the water stands and carries the count to the [[place-greattempluqaa|Great Temple of Uqa'a]]. At the crest the priests proclaim the reading to the city and the empire.

## Background

The Reader serves under the [[being-thazlekhau|Thaz'Lekhau of Uqa'a]] at the temple and attends the temple supper on the eve of the crest. The keeper and apprentices of [[place-themeasure|The Measure]] stay at the gauge.

:::secret {title="The purged cup"}
At the temple supper on the eve of the crest the keeper's senior apprentice puts a purge in the Reader's cup, enough to keep the old priest off the stair until noon.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Methodical and devout, and bound to the stair through the flood.

### Motivation

The Reader wants each day's count carried to the temple as the water stands.

### Strengths

Long practice at reading the cut cubits.

### Weaknesses

The Reader is old, and a sickness on a crest morning keeps the Reader off the stair.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

The Reader reports to the Thaz'Lekhau and depends on the keeper's apprentices at the gauge.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-nightbfrrdng|The Night Before the Reading]]: before dawn on the day of the crest the Reader is too sick to go down the stair.
