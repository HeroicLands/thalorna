# Dossier {#dossier}

Zabegezu is a lord of [[place-garanlaghet|Gar-Anlaghet]] who has kept the same household in service for over a decade. Stable, respectful service is the return he gets for exacting standards consistently applied. The household has learned exactly what those standards require, which is why it has lasted this long.

He is the Halzi'a of [[affiliation-selatgrnlght|The Selat of Gar-Anlaghet]], the first of the delta and the richest province of the empire, and governs it from the port. His fortune is the largest of any noble outside the royal house, and the throne borrows silver from him at interest.
