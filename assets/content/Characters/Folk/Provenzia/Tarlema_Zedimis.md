# Appearance {#appearance}

Tarlema Zedimis is a 52-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of heavy build. Her tanned skin is weathered, her gray hair is tied back under a kerchief, and her brown eyes are shrewd. She has a farmer's broad hands and a voice that carries across a field.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tarlema Zedimis holds a tenancy on the [[place-bldfldkyvarel|Blood-field of Kivarel]], the quickened field in the barony of [[place-kyvarel|Kivarel]], which her family has farmed for generations and did not choose. The field's families chose her to speak for them twenty years ago and have not chosen anyone since.

The tenants have no drying yards of their own, and their crop goes to the baron's yards at [[place-elthrembis|Eltrembis]] and pays the share. Tarlema keeps the house there where the field's families lodge in harvest, and she spends more of the year in it than on her holding. Three frosts in five years on their other ground have left the families owing the yards more than one harvest can pay.

She will discuss the yield with anyone and the field with no one.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tarlema is blunt with everyone but a stranger, practical, and stubborn in the families' cause.

### Motivation

She wants the families out of debt, and then she wants the field settled.

### Strengths

The families' trust and the best ground in the barony.

### Weaknesses

The debt to the yards, which the steward holds over the families.

## Social

### Companions

The tenant families of the field.

### Patrons

The baron, from whom the families hold.

### Enemies

None. The factor and the priests are merely in the way.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Tarlema is a freewoman holding a tenancy of the Baron of Zuravlen and Kivarel.
