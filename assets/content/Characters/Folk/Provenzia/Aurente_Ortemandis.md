# Appearance {#appearance}

Aurente Ortemandis is a 37-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. He has smooth olive skin, red hair cut in the old style of the coast, and gray eyes set a little too close. He dresses with exact correctness, in the colors his grandfather wore, and his courtesy is so complete that it takes a stranger some time to notice it is hostile.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Aurente Ortemandis is the head of the oldest noble family of [[place-lizela|Lizela]], the salt town on the southern coast, and a member of its charter council. He is a traditionalist, and he regards the small school that [[being-arnelkrsvr|Arnela Kórisvar]] founded to teach navigation and commercial law to the sons of the harbor as an insult to the proper order: educating commoners above their station, he says, weakens the order of society. He has blocked her proposals on the council at every turn, always with impeccable [[lore-provenzianclt|Provénzian]] courtesy.

His family's standing is older than its money. The Ortemandis lands are small, and the salt that made the newer houses rich made his family only respectable. The crown's treasurer has now asked the coastal houses for a loan against the salt revenue, and Lizela must answer. Aurente means to answer whichever way Arnela does not, and to make the town follow him.

## Psyche

### Personality

Aurente is proud, punctilious and patient. He never raises his voice and never forgets a slight.

### Motivation

He wants Lizela governed by the families that have always governed it, and the Kórisvar house put back in the place it kept for generations, which was out of the way.

### Strengths

The oldest name in the town, a sure vote on the council and allies among the traditionalist Peers.

### Weaknesses

He would rather see Lizela lose than see Arnela win.

## Social

### Companions

The older families of Lizela, who dine at his house and vote with him.

### Patrons

The traditionalists among the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]], who oppose every extension of chartered rights.

### Enemies

Arnela Kórisvar, and the merchant families who send their sons to her school.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: He is of the Lesser Nobility.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The answer to the treasurer.** Aurente wants to know how Arnela means to answer the crown's request for a loan before the council votes, and he will pay well for a sight of her letters.
2. **The match.** He has proposed a marriage between his younger brother and Arnela, as a reconciliation of the old families and the new. He does not expect her to accept, and he means her refusal to be the insult that turns the council against her.
