# Appearance {#appearance}

Cholsama Tulemis is a 52-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of medium build. Her olive skin is clear, her dark brown hair is bound back for work, and her hazel eyes are sharp behind spectacles of ground crystal that hang on a cord when she is not reading. Her hands are scrubbed red. She wears a physician's plain gown with the keys of the archive at her belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 52. Rector of the Academy of Medicine and Manuscripts. Wants to know who paid her scriptorium to put an account of Sirmenne into every treasure-seeker's hands.**

Cholsama Tulemis is the Rector of the [[affiliation-acadmedcn|Academy of Medicine and Manuscripts]] at [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]], chosen by its masters for life. She keeps the roll, the charter and the keys of the archive, licenses physicians and copyists on the masters' vote, and takes the scriptorium's commissions. She is a physician first: she kept the poor of Aurelane for the city's council for ten years before the masters chose her, and she still keeps a morning clinic in the academy's porch.

The scriptorium is copying every opinion and oration on the crown's salt loan for both parties, and she has refused to give either side a sight of the other's copies before they are collected. She is also uneasy. This summer the scriptorium copied a scholar's account of [[place-sirmenne|Sirmenne]] for a factor who paid for more copies than the academies could read, and the copies are now in the borderland woods in the hands of treasure-seekers.

## Psyche

### Personality

Cholsama is brisk, kind and suspicious. She treats a patient gently and a customer as a question to be answered.

### Motivation

She wants her scriptorium trusted by every party in the kingdom, and she does not want it used.

### Strengths

The archive holds a copy of every opinion and oration sent to be copied, and she holds its keys.

### Weaknesses

The scriptorium copies for anyone who pays, and she cannot refuse a commission without saying why.

## Social

### Companions

The masters of the academy, her copyists and her patients, and the Rectors of the other two academies, [[being-nelamzsatr|Nelameza Sateris]] and [[being-kolmetgrdl|Kolmete Gredalis]].

### Patrons

The council of Aurelane, which pays for the physicians who keep its poor.

### Enemies

Whoever paid for the copies of the account of Sirmenne, whom she has not found.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-acadmedcn|Academy of Medicine and Manuscripts]]**: Cholsama is its Rector.
- **[[affiliation-marendas|Faith of Marendas]]**: She keeps the faith of learning, and blesses the scriptorium's new students at the Dedication of the Apprentice.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: She is of the Gentry by her house.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The factor.** Cholsama hires a party to find the factor who paid for the copies of the account of Sirmenne, and to learn whom he paid for ([[scenario-engmsrmnn|The Enigma of Sirmenne]]).
2. **The copies for both sides.** A clerk of the scriptorium has been offered silver for a sight of the crown's copies before the Peers' lawyers collect theirs. Cholsama wants to know who offered it.

:::secret {title="The factor's client"}
The factor is the Bataren bookseller who keeps the shop below [[being-zdmdrvs|Zedime Diravis]]'s rooms, and he bought the copies for Zedime, the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]'s magister at Bataren, who let the account circulate to bring treasure-seekers into the woods while the Order's men went into the ruin by night.
