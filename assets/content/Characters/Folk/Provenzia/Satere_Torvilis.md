# Appearance {#appearance}

Satere Torvilis is a 48-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of heavy build. He has the ruddy, fair skin of the hill Tanevar, red hair going gray, and brown eyes that watch the door. A cloth hangs over one shoulder, and his hands are busy with a cup whenever a question is asked.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 48. Innkeeper of Belsaren. Wants the old lord's son back and his own neck unstretched.**

Satere Torvilis keeps the inn at [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] in [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]], the one place in the village where a stranger can sleep, and so the first place a stranger asks questions. He served at table in the hall of the Tanevar lords before the Marshal deposed them, and he speaks of the old lord with a warmth he does not trouble to hide from his Tanevar customers.

He is the man the Tanevar of the village listen to. He is guarded with strangers and openly cold to anyone who comes from the manor, and he warns a newcomer that people who ask too many questions about the bandits tend to meet them. When pressed he sends the questioner to someone else.

## Psyche

### Personality

Satere is shrewd, warm with his own and close with everyone else. He frightens easily and hides it badly.

### Motivation

He wants the lordship back in Tanevar hands and his village left alone.

### Strengths

He knows every family in Belsaren and every rumor that passes through his door.

### Weaknesses

He cannot bear the thought of the gallows, and a lord who threatens him with it will have the truth from him.

## Social

### Companions

His wife and grown sons, who run the inn with him, and the Tanevar families of the village.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

[[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas]], the new lord, and [[being-revotakrms|Revota Kiramis]], the reeve, who speaks for the lord's side of the village.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Satere is a Freeman.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Let them find you.** Satere tells a party that nobody finds the outlaws in their own woods, and that a party wanting to meet them had better be found instead.
2. **The question.** After a raid on the village square, Satere is to be put to the question. He asks a party to speak for him before the lord does.

:::secret {title="What Satere does"}
Satere sends food from the inn's kitchen to the outlaws every week, and passes on what the lord's guards say over their cups. He knows where the cave is: below the ridge, south of the village, near a twisted oak.
:::
