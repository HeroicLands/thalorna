# Appearance {#appearance}

Dolvere Kórisvar was a big man, 6'0" tall and of heavy build, with olive skin browned by the coast, red hair that faded late and green eyes. In the portrait his daughter keeps in the hall at Lizela he is painted in a plain coat, with his hand on a ledger and nothing behind him that could be mistaken for ambition. He died at fifty-nine.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Died 704 AF, aged 59. Head of the Kórisvar house of Lizela. Kept his head down.**

Dolvere Kórisvar was the head of the [[affiliation-hsekorisvar|Kórisvars]], a minor noble house of [[place-lizela|Lizela]] whose wealth rested on coastal trade and the salt pans east of the town, and the father of [[being-arnelkrsvr|Arnela Kórisvar]], who holds the house now. He was a cautious man. Through the long maneuver between the crown and the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]] he kept his house out of the way of both, and when he died in 704 AF he left his daughter an estate that was solvent and unremarkable: a manor above the harbor, a handful of tenant farms, fishing rights along the coast and a seat on the town's charter council that nobody coveted.

His caution may have been less complete than it looked. A merchant from [[place-taruzelrgn|Taruzel]] has come to Lizela with papers that say Dolvere borrowed heavily near the end of his life to cover losses his daughter never knew of, and that the debts were sold and resold until they reached him.

## Psyche

### Personality

He was careful, courteous and dull by choice, and he believed that a small house survives by being uninteresting to great ones.

### Motivation

He wanted the house passed on intact and unnoticed.

### Strengths

Patience, and a good head for the salt trade.

### Weaknesses

He was not as careful with money as with politics, if the Taruzel papers are true.

## Social

### Companions

The minor houses of Lizela, who kept their heads down beside him.

### Patrons

None he would admit to.

### Enemies

None in his lifetime that anyone remembers.
