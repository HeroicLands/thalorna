# Appearance {#appearance}

Dirave al Kaldremis is a 32-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. He has the fair, freckled skin of the hill Tanevar, burned and weathered by two years in the open, a long white scar across the back of his right hand, auburn hair grown long and green eyes. He dresses in what the caravans carried and wears his father's signet ring on a cord round his neck, because a ring on the hand can be seen.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 32. Outlaw. Wants Belsaren back, and has stopped caring what it costs the people he means to rule.**

Dirave al Kaldremis is the heir of the Tanevar house that held [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] in [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] before the Marshal at [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]] accused it of treason. His father died in the Marshal's keeping before the charge was heard. The lordship went to [[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren]], and Dirave went into the woods with a few of his father's men.

He leads the outlaws of the vale from a cave in a rocky outcrop at the foot of a forested ridge south of the village. Two years ago his band was one of several. This year he has gathered them, and they strike the Belsaren road with a precision that surprises everyone, Dirave included. Supplies of good quality have begun to appear where his men can find them, and he calls them a blessing.

The Tanevar of Belsaren remember his father kindly, and most of them feed his men and send word of the patrols. He promises them their old lord's justice. He takes food and tools from the caravans more than coin, and he forbids killing that the band does not need. He is growing desperate, and the forbidding is wearing thin.

## Psyche

### Personality

Dirave is proud, eloquent and quick to anger. He was raised to rule and still speaks as a lord, and the longer he lives as an outlaw the more he resents the people who did not rise for him.

### Motivation

He wants his house's name cleared and Belsaren in his hands. Revenge on Tarlas and the Marshal has begun to matter to him more than either.

### Strengths

A skilled swordsman and a better tactician, with a gift for ambush and traps. He knows every path in the woods below the ridge.

### Weaknesses

His pride will not let him bargain until he is beaten. He does not ask where his help comes from, or what his newer lieutenants pray to.

## Social

### Companions

His band of outlaws, a score of men and some women, among them three lieutenants who joined him this year and carry a flame emblem on a cord.

### Patrons

None that he knows of.

### Enemies

[[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas]], who holds his lordship, and [[being-jarvelplnvr|the Marshal]], who took it.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Dirave is outlawed, his house attainted for treason.

2. **The informant.** A beaten Dirave offers to name the person who knows what the lord of Ilovren is really after, if the party lets him go.
