# Appearance {#appearance}

Lauvina Solkimis is a 33-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build. Her olive skin is clear, her auburn hair is dressed in the Válaren fashion even on the road, and her hazel eyes miss nothing in a basket. She wears good linen that is always a little dusty and keeps a clerk at her elbow.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lauvina Solkimis is a factor of a merchant house of [[place-valarencity|Válaren City]], which buys fruit, wine and preserves in the west and sells them on the richest tables of the south. She has bought in the Zuravlen valley for six harvests, and she knows what the first mark fetches in Válaren to the Solden.

This harvest she has traced the barony's earliest and largest fruit to [[place-kyvarel|Kivarel]], and to one field there, the [[place-bldfldkyvarel|Blood-field of Kivarel]]. She means to sell its fruit under its own mark, at a price that would make the field's tenants rich, and she does not care what the field is. Nobody in Kivarel will tell her what it is, which makes her more determined.

## Psyche

### Personality

Lauvina is frank, energetic and honest by her own lights. She thinks silence is a negotiating position.

### Motivation

She wants a mark of her own on the best fruit in the west, and the commission that comes with it.

### Strengths

Her house's money, a clerk who counts honestly and an eye for fruit.

### Weaknesses

She does not understand why a district would rather be poor than talked about.

## Social

### Companions

Her clerk, and the carters who take her fruit south.

### Patrons

Her house in Válaren.

### Enemies

None yet. The baron's steward is close.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Lauvina is of the Gentry by her house.
