# Appearance {#appearance}

Revota Kiramis is a 35-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of medium build. She has tanned olive skin, dark brown hair bound up under a kerchief and hazel eyes. She wears the reeve's tally sticks at her belt and walks the village as if she were counting it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 35. Reeve of Belsaren. Wants the raids ended and the people who feed the raiders named.**

Revota Kiramis is the reeve of [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] in [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]], chosen from the village to oversee the lord's fields and labor, and the voice of its Provénzian families. Her family came to Belsaren as tenants of the old lords two generations ago and has done better under the new one. She argues that sheltering the outlaws will ruin the village and that the lord, whatever his faults, is the only protection it has.

She is more willing than anyone in the village to help a party sent by the lord, and more careful than she looks about being seen to do it, because she lives among the Tanevar families she is helping to accuse. She knows that the outlaws watch the Old South Road, and that the carts they strike are the ones that look easy.

## Psyche

### Personality

Revota is brisk, blunt and sure of her case. She does not like the lord and does not pretend to, and she likes the outlaws less.

### Motivation

She wants the raids stopped before her family's harvest goes the way of the caravans.

### Strengths

She knows the fields, the roads and the lord's household, and the Provénzian families follow her.

### Weaknesses

She is quick to call every Tanevar neighbor a traitor, and she will push the lord toward cruelty when she is frightened.

## Social

### Companions

The Provénzian families of the village, and the beadle who keeps the lord's peace in the lanes.

### Patrons

[[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas]], whose favor she has and does not trust.

### Enemies

[[being-sateretrvl|Satere Torvilis]], the innkeeper, whom she suspects with good reason.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Revota is a Freeman.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Old South Road.** Revota tells a party that the outlaws watch the Old South Road and strike the carts that look weakest, and offers them a cart.
2. **The rocks in the square.** When the outlaws strike the village square, Revota gathers the Provénzian families to fight, and she needs someone to keep them from killing the Tanevar instead.
