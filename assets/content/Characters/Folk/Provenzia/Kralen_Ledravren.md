# Appearance {#appearance}

Kralen Lédravren is a 40-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. He has olive skin, dark brown hair cut close at the temples, and brown eyes that settle on whoever is speaking. A thin white scar crosses the back of his right hand. He dresses as well as the court expects and no better, and he wears the crown's ring and no other.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 40. King of Provénzia. Wants a crown with revenue of its own, so that it need not ask the Council of Peers for every levy.**

Kralen Lédravren is the king of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]], of [[affiliation-hseledravren|House Lédravren]], and he reigns from [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]]. He is the son of the younger brother of [[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]], who had no son, and he was raised as a prince of the blood with no expectation of the crown until it was plainly coming to him. When Vélarik died in 709 AF the Peers, the cities and the great officers sent their oaths within the month, and nobody put up another claimant ([[lore-velarikreign|The Reign of Vélarik III]]). He was twenty-nine.

He holds the crown under the [[lore-chrtrrghts|Charter of Rights]], which binds him to take counsel of the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]], to levy only by the estates' consent and to leave the chartered cities their own law. Every king since [[lore-warprinces|the War of the Princes]] has worked to make the crown stronger inside those forms, and Kralen is no exception. He kept the crown lands his uncle resumed from the princes of [[place-gwefavel|Gefavel]], because they pay for his household, and the Gefavel grievance stands open before the Council because of it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kralen is patient, courteous and hard to read. He listens to the end of every argument and then asks what the speaker wants, and he treats a refusal as an opening offer. He is good company at table and gives nothing away there.

### Motivation

He wants to leave the crown richer and less dependent than he found it, without ever being seen to break the Charter. A king who breaks it hands the Peers their cause; a king who keeps it and buys his independence a manor and a toll at a time wins without a fight.

### Strengths

He has the crown's patronage: the great offices, the grants, the marriages and the places at court, and he spends them carefully. He has no faction of his own to repay, because nobody expected him to reign, and he can bargain with every party at once.

### Weaknesses

The crown's revenue does not cover the frontier, the navy and the court together, and every shortfall sends him back to the Council. He has no child, and the Charter gives the Council no ruling on who follows him.

## Social

### Companions

[[being-neltavaolnr|Neltava Olnaris]], his Chamberlain, has kept his household since he was a prince with a small one, and she decides who reaches his door.

### Patrons

Kralen answers to no patron. The Charter of Rights binds him, and the great officers he appoints are his to keep or dismiss: [[being-velzoreaitv|Velzore Aitevis]] keeps the great seal, and [[being-gredalekalv|Gredale Kalvezis]] the treasury.

### Enemies

[[being-itrolaldrvn|Itrola Lédravren]], the Princess of Gefavel, is his cousin and wants back what his uncle took. [[being-rutalplnvr|Rutal Pélanvri]], the Earl of Degavel, is First Peer and leads the Council against every widening of the crown's prerogative. Beyond the passes, the lords of [[place-westernmrch|the Western March]] go down into [[place-talothe|the Talote]] every season the snow allows.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Kralen holds the crown as King, the Sovereign of the realm.
- **[[affiliation-hseledravren|House Lédravren]]**: He is the head of the royal house.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The salt loan.** The Treasurer has asked the coastal houses for a loan against the salt revenue of [[place-lizela|Lizela]], and the king wants to know which houses mean to refuse before he asks them himself. A party that can sit at the right tables in Lizela and [[place-kalveza|Kalveza]] is worth more to him than a letter.
2. **What Gefavel has offered.** The Princess of Gefavel's petition for her grants comes before the Council at its next sitting. Kralen wants to know what she has promised the Count of Atavel's party for their votes, and whether the Earl of Degavel knows.
3. **Two reports from one frontier.** The Warden of the Talote and the Marshal of the Western Army send the king accounts of the same raid that cannot both be true. He wants a third account from someone who owes neither man anything.
