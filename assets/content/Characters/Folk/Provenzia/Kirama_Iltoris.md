# Appearance {#appearance}

Kirama Iltoris is a 21-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. She has fair, clear skin, red hair she wears pinned up and plain, and hazel eyes that do not drop when an older Peer stares at her. She dresses in the gray northern wool of Bataren at a court that wears silk, and she means it to be noticed.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kirama Iltoris is a countess and a Peer of the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]], the youngest who sits there, and the leader of its reformist faction. She keeps her house at [[place-batarencity|Bataren City]], the kingdom's harbor on the cold ocean, and goes down to [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] when the Council is summoned.

She inherited her father's county at nineteen, with a seat in the Council and a library of the writers of the [[affiliation-acadlaw|Academy of Law]] and the [[affiliation-acadrhetrc|Academy of Philosophy and Rhetoric]] on law and government, and she came to the Peers' hall with opinions she had read rather than inherited. She wants chartered rights extended to more towns, so that the towns that pay the crown's revenue govern their own local affairs as the great cities do. The traditionalists among the Peers and the old houses of the coast oppose every such extension. Kirama is too young to carry a reform on her own name, so she works through the minor nobles of the towns, and [[being-arnelkrsvr|Arnela Kórisvar]] of [[place-lizela|Lizela]] is her ablest ally and her most useful instrument.

Bataren has taught her what the Council will not look at. This year an Élavendren merchant was beaten in the fish market, a Provénzian warehouse burned and a crown notary who refused to register an Élavendren inheritance was found in the harbor, and the crown's officers are more anxious to keep the news from Jalnasdal than to find who did it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kirama is earnest, quick and impatient, and she argues better than she persuades. She has not learned to let an older man believe her idea was his.

### Motivation

She wants the towns governed by the people who live in them, and she wants to be taken seriously by a Council that has seen reformers come and go.

### Strengths

A Peer's voice, a good legal education and the energy of someone who has never been defeated.

### Weaknesses

She uses allies as she is used, and she has not counted what it costs them.

## Social

### Companions

The young lawyers and town deputies who gather at her lodging in Jalnasdal when the Council sits.

### Patrons

She holds of the crown and owes [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] her counsel.

### Enemies

The traditionalist Peers, and the old houses of the coast whose towns she would charter out from under them.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]]**: Kirama sits as a Peer.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: She is of the Greater Nobility.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The notary in the harbor.** Kirama does not believe the crown notary of Bataren drowned by accident, and she wants a party to learn what inheritance he refused to register, and for whom, before the crown's officers bury the matter.
2. **Two more towns.** Kirama has asked Arnela Kórisvar to champion the chartering of two more towns before the Council. If Arnela wins, the reform advances; if she loses, it is Arnela who pays, and Kirama wants to know which Peers can be bought before she lets her try.
