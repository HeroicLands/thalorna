# Appearance {#appearance}

Chalzime Cholsamis is a 60-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of light build, long-limbed and spare. His black hair has gone gray only at the temples, which he regards as a personal achievement. He has tawny skin, clear and carefully kept, brown eyes, and a wine merchant's habit of holding a cup to the light before he drinks from it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Chalzime Cholsamis is a wine merchant of [[place-valarencity|Válaren City]], the head of a house that ships the kingdom's best wines and the luxuries that travel with them, and one of the patrons of standing of the courtesan [[being-kelnstylgn|Kelena Estilgon]].

His house buys from the estates of the southern hills and sells through Válaren to the Vilérian world, and it has made him rich enough to call himself a merchant-prince where he is not overheard. He treats Kelena as an investment and a mark of standing: he gives her gifts and the run of the merchant houses' tables, and he expects her at his feasts, where her presence tells his guests what kind of house they are dining in.

The harbor mouth is his trouble this season. Three hulls without colors have been seen off the coast, the Vilérian masters who carry his wine are putting in at [[place-kalveza|Kalveza]] instead, and his partners are calling in short loans. Chalzime has a warehouse of wine bought on credit and no ship willing to load it at Válaren.

## Psyche

### Personality

Chalzime is genial, vain and shrewd, and he measures everyone by what they would fetch.

### Motivation

He wants his house to come through the trouble at the harbor mouth larger than it went in, and he wants to be seen to be the man who could afford Kelena Estilgon.

### Strengths

A palate the coast trusts, a long list of debtors and a house that has survived the last blockade.

### Weaknesses

His credit is tied up in wine that cannot sail.

## Social

### Companions

The heads of the wine houses of Válaren, his rivals and his dinner guests.

### Patrons

None. He is a patron.

### Enemies

The factors of Kalveza, who are taking the masters who loaded his wine at Válaren.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: He is of the Gentry by a grant his grandfather bought.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The cargo that cannot sail.** Chalzime wants a party to carry a cargo of his best wine overland to Kalveza and see it loaded there before the Kalveza factors learn whose it is.
2. **The hull without colors.** He believes one of his rivals is paying the ships off the coast to keep the masters away from Válaren, and he wants proof he can take to the city's commander.
