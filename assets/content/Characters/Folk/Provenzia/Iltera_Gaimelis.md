# Appearance {#appearance}

Iltera Gaimelis is a 43-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of medium build. Her face is windburned from the clifftop, her brown hair is cropped short under a wool cap, and her gray eyes squint from long summers on the white pans. Her hands are cracked from salt. She carries a reeve's tally-stick at her belt and a weighing-beam's brass pin on a cord round her neck.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 43. Reeve of Tremuzel for the Earl of Hárivren. Wants the valley's salt trade back from Volsater, and the earl's leave to take it.**

Iltera Gaimelis keeps the port of [[place-tremuzel|Tremuzel]] for [[being-avaarlzlvrn|Avaarl Zilavren]], the Earl of [[place-harivrencity|Hárivren]], who holds the town and its salt pans himself. She was born in the town, raked the pans as a girl and kept the weighing shed's tallies before the earl's court made her reeve. She measures every crop the pans give, takes the earl's share there before the salt ever reaches the toll at Hárivren City, sets the order in which the pans are opened in spring, and settles the beach's quarrels over boat-places and nets.

Her trouble is [[place-volsather|Volsater]]. The barony of [[affiliation-brnyzrvln|Zuravlen]] buys its salt from its own harbor, and Volsater's masters are cutting their price in the valley markets. Iltera believes the baron's steward, [[being-plvzornts|Palveze Ornetis]], is helping them, and she has asked the earl's court for leave to sell below the toll.

## Psyche

### Personality

Iltera is plain-spoken, careful with figures and slow to trust anyone from the valley. She counts everything twice and says what she has counted once.

### Motivation

She wants Tremuzel's salt in every preserving house in the valley, and she wants the earl to see that she put it there.

### Strengths

She knows the pans, the boats and the cliff better than anyone in the town, and the beach trusts her measure.

### Weaknesses

She cannot act on the toll without the earl's court, and the court is two days inland and in no hurry.

## Social

### Companions

The salt-rakers of the clifftop, most of whom she grew up with, and the carters who take the salt inland.

### Patrons

The Earl of Hárivren, whose reeve she is.

### Enemies

Palveze Ornetis, the steward at [[place-elthrembis|Eltrembis]], whom she blames for Volsater's prices.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Iltera is a freewoman.
- **[[affiliation-erldmhrvrn|Earldom of Hárivren]]**: She is the earl's reeve at Tremuzel.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The cart train.** If the earl's court grants her leave, Iltera needs a party to take a train of salt up the cleft and through the barony's markets before the steward's men can stop it on the road.
2. **The north pans.** A cragclimber has taken the cliff below the north pans, and the egg-gatherers will not go down. Iltera pays for it to be moved off.

:::secret {title="The boat in the fog"}
The boat that did not come in this spring carried salt to Volsater by night, sold under the earl's toll by its master and two of the rakers. Iltera found the shortfall in her tallies and has told no one, because the master's family are her cousins and the earl's court would ask why his reeve did not see it sooner.
:::
