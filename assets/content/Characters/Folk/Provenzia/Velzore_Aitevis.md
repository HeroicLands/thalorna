# Appearance {#appearance}

Velzore Aitevis is a 53-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. He has sallow olive skin, graying black hair and hazel eyes behind spectacles he wears on a cord. The fingers of his right hand carry ink that no washing removes. He wears the Chancellor's black and the great seal's chain, and he carries the seal itself in a purse under his gown.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 53. Count of Atavel, Peer, and Chancellor of the realm. Wants to keep the seal, keep his county, and keep the Earl of Degavel from the majority.**

Velzore Aitevis is the Count of [[place-athavel|Atavel]], whose seat is [[place-atavelcstl|Atavel Castle]], and the Chancellor of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]. As Chancellor he keeps the great seal, issues the crown's writs, charters and grants under it, and reads the king's business to the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]] at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]], where he also sits as a Peer. [[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]] gave him the seal in 704 AF, and [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] kept him in it.

He is embattled on two fronts. In the Council he is the Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]]'s great rival, and the quarrel between Atavel and Degavel is the one that most often leaves the Council without a majority. In his own house, his younger brother holds the county's richest manors under their father's will and contests whether Velzore's son or the brother himself is the heir to the rest. The two quarrels feed each other, because every vote Velzore buys in the Council is a vote his brother can try to buy back.

## Psyche

### Personality

Velzore is dry, precise and tireless. He reads every document twice and seals nothing he has not read, and he enjoys the Council's procedure for its own sake. He is not warm, and he does not try to be.

### Motivation

He wants the crown strong enough to govern and his own house secure enough to survive him. He believes the Charter is a set of procedures, and that whoever knows them best governs.

### Strengths

The seal is the crown's voice, and nothing the king grants is valid until Velzore has sealed it. He knows the Charter, the writ forms and the Council's precedents better than anyone at court.

### Weaknesses

His county is divided and his brother is patient. The Princess of Gefavel wants his office back for her branch, and she has the law on her side often enough to worry him.

## Social

### Companions

The clerks of the chancery at Jalnasdal are his household, and he trusts them more than his kin.

### Patrons

Kralen Lédravren keeps him in office because he is useful, and both of them know the king could take the seal back with a word.

### Enemies

[[being-rutalplnvr|Rutal Pélanvri]], the Earl of Degavel and First Peer, opposes him in every sitting. [[being-itrolaldrvn|Itrola Lédravren]], the Princess of Gefavel, wants the seal.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Velzore is Chancellor of the realm, and of the Greater Nobility as Count of Atavel.
- **[[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]]**: He sits as a Peer for Atavel.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The writ he will not copy.** The Princess of Gefavel wants a sight of Vélarik III's writ of resumption, and Velzore refuses it. He wants to know first whether the writ was issued with the Council's counsel, and he will pay a party to find out from the old king's clerks before she does.
2. **His brother's notary.** Velzore's brother has hired a notary at [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]] to draw a case on their father's will. Velzore wants the draft before it reaches the high courts.
3. **One more voice.** The First Peer is chosen again at the Council's first sitting of the year. Velzore needs one more Peer's voice to deny it to Rutal Pélanvri, and the one Peer who has not declared is the Earl of Hárivren.
