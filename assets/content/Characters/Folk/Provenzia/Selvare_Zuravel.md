# Appearance {#appearance}

Selvare Zuravel is a 47-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of heavy build. His skin is browned by the valley sun, his chestnut hair is thinning at the crown, and his brown eyes crease when he smiles, which is often. He has a farmer's thick forearms, and in the weeks of the harvest his nails are stained with the fruit. He dresses well for the fair and badly for everything else.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 47. Baron of Zuravlen and of Kivarel. Wants every harvest sold under the Zuravlen name, and Kivarel's field farmed without anyone asking why it grows so well.**

Selvare Zuravel is the Baron of [[affiliation-brnyzrvln|Zuravlen]], the valley of [[place-zuravlenrgn|the Zuravlen Region]] that grows the finest peaches on the [[place-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]], and he is also Baron of [[place-kyvarel|Kivarel]] in the western valleys. He holds both for the crown through his overlord, [[being-avaarlzlvrn|Avaarl Zilavren]], the Earl of Hárivren, and he sits in a hall above [[place-elthrembis|Eltrembis]] rather than in the town. The barony's water-rolls, manor customs and grading records are kept in the market hall below him.

His income is the drying yards south of the town, where every holding without a yard of its own must dry its crop and pay him a share, and the tolls are his overlord's. He appoints the graders who sort the fruit at the grading table, and he is accused in every tavern in the valley of appointing them too carefully. He is of the [Zuravel]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} clan, which is divided on the kingdom's faith quarrel, and his line's prominent support of the [[affiliation-brnngbrnd|Order of the Burning Brand]], which stands with [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]], gives a small agricultural barony more weight in Provénzian affairs than its size suggests. His kinsman [[being-kaldinlnvl|Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren]] holds [[place-leravren|Leravren]] in the eastern march, and Kaldin's branch keeps the Black Flame.

The best land in Kivarel is the [[place-bldfldkyvarel|Blood-field of Kivarel]], a quickening where the fruit sets in years when the rest of the district loses its blossom to frost. Its tenants have twice refused the clergy of [[affiliation-selmoros|Sélmoros]] the rite of settlement, and Selvare has twice let the refusal stand.

## Psyche

### Personality

Selvare is genial, shrewd and a little vain. He likes to be known as a farmer among barons and a baron among farmers, and he has a story for every orchard in the valley. He changes the subject of Kivarel without seeming to.

### Motivation

He wants the Zuravlen mark to stay the best in Ankaris and the barony's income to grow with it. He wants Kivarel left alone.

### Strengths

The fruit and the wine are known in every market, the autumn fair makes Eltrembis rich for eight days, and the Earl of Hárivren values a vassal whose valley pays.

### Weaknesses

The drying-yard share is the most resented charge in the barony. The quickened field is the best thing he owns and the one he cannot discuss.

## Social

### Companions

The graders of Eltrembis, whom he appoints, and the factors of [[place-batarencity|Bataren]] who buy the dried fruit at the fair. His younger sister, [[being-ormelazrvl|Ormela Zuravel]], is Master of the Order of the Burning Brand.

### Patrons

Avaarl Zilavren, Earl of Hárivren, his overlord, through whose city the valley's fruit and wine pass.

### Enemies

The smallholders who must dry their crops in his yards, and the clergy of Sélmoros, who would settle Kivarel's field if he let them.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-brnyzrvln|Barony of Zuravlen]]**: Selvare holds the barony as its Baron.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: He is of the Lesser Nobility, holding of the Earl of Hárivren.
- **[[affiliation-brnngbrnd|Order of the Burning Brand]]**: His line is its firmest sworn friend.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Through the harvest.** Selvare wants Kivarel's field farmed through this harvest without a factor, a priest or a Peer asking why the fruit is so large. A party that can turn away questions politely is worth a season's wages to him.
2. **A grader for sale.** Somebody is selling first marks at the grading table, and the baron must find the grader before the factors of Bataren stop trusting the Zuravlen name.
3. **The yard share.** The smallholders of the upper valley refuse to dry their crop in the baron's yards this year and have built racks of their own. Selvare wants the quarrel settled before the fair, without the manor courts and without blood.
