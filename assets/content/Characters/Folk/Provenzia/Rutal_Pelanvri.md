# Appearance {#appearance}

Rutal Pélanvri is a 51-year-old man who stands six feet tall and is of heavy build. His tanned face is weathered and ruddy, his graying brown hair is cut short, and his brown eyes are steady. His nose was broken in his youth and set slightly crooked. He wears a soldier's plain cloth under the Peer's mantle and sits in the Council as if he were in the saddle.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 51. Earl of Degavel and First Peer of the Council. Wants the Council's consent to stay the price of every royal act, and Atavel kept out of office.**

Rutal Pélanvri is the Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]], the earldom of the southern country of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]], and the leading lord of the south, with his seat at [[place-degavelcstl|Degavel Castle]]. The Peers chose him First Peer of the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]], so he calls its sittings when the king is absent, puts the question and counts the voices at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]]. His leadership in the south gives him weight in military matters as well as at court.

The earldom is a family affair. His younger brother, [[being-jarvelplnvr|Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]], is Marshal of the Western Army at [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]], and another of the [[affiliation-hsepelanvri|Pélanvri]] holds [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] under the Marshal. Rutal's great rival is the Count of [[place-athavel|Atavel]], [[being-velzoreaitv|Velzore Aitevis]], who holds the great seal as Chancellor, and the contest between Council and crown runs, in the Council's chamber, as a contest between the two of them.

## Psyche

### Personality

Rutal is loud, direct and shrewder than he lets on. He prefers a plain vote to a clever procedure, and he has learned that sounding like a soldier wins him the benefit of the doubt from Peers who think the Chancellor too clever.

### Motivation

He believes the Charter is the realm's protection against a king who would govern without the great houses, and that the Council's consent must cost the crown something every time. He also wants Atavel humbled, and he does not trouble to separate the two aims.

### Strengths

He commands the southern levy and the respect of the soldiers, and his brother holds the frontier. As First Peer he controls the order of the Council's business.

### Weaknesses

His house's power on the frontier rests on what his brother does there, and every complaint against the Marshal's appointees in Keravel Vale reaches the Council through the Chancellor's people. The First Peer is chosen again each year, and Atavel's party needs only one more voice.

## Social

### Companions

His brother Jarvel is the one man he trusts without reservation.

### Patrons

He holds of the crown directly and answers to no patron below the king, whom he opposes.

### Enemies

Velzore Aitevis and the Atavel party. [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] is his king and his opponent in every argument over prerogative.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Rutal is of the Greater Nobility as Earl of Degavel.
- **[[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]]**: He sits as a Peer and holds the office of First Peer.
- **[[affiliation-hsepelanvri|The Pélanvri]]**: He is the head of the house.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The year's first sitting.** The Peers choose the First Peer again at the first sitting of the year, and the Earl of Hárivren has not said how he will vote. Rutal wants to know what Hárivren wants, without asking him in a way the Chancellor will hear of.
2. **Letters from the vale.** Complaints from [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] about the lord of Belsaren have reached Jalnasdal, and the Count of Atavel's people are collecting them. Rutal wants them answered before they are read in the Council.
3. **A levy for the Talote.** The crown asks the Council for a levy to pay for the frontier, where the Marshal is his brother. The First Peer must choose between his house and his principle in public.
