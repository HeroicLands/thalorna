# Appearance {#appearance}

Dilmara Volchemis is a 44-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of medium build. She has light skin, dark brown hair pinned under a plain cap and hazel eyes. She wears the black of the high courts under a wolfskin cloak, which she bought her first winter in Durvalis and has worn every winter since.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 44. Justice of the Winter Assize. Wants the Assize's judgments to stand, whoever they offend.**

Dilmara Volchemis is the Justice of the [[affiliation-wntrassize|Winter Assize]], the court of the eastern march that sits at [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] from the closing of the passes to the thaw. She is an advocate of the high courts at [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]], and the Justiciar, [[being-solkimervtn|Solkime Revotandis]], has named her to the Assize for several winters running. She lives in Durvalis now, the year round, because a justice who leaves in spring is a stranger again by autumn.

The Assize hears everything the campaign season had no time for: stolen stock, disputed ransoms, the standing quarrels of border families and, now and then, a charge against an officer that could not safely be brought while he was needed. It is the most honest court in Provénzia in the estimation of the people who appear before it, and Dilmara means to keep it so.

## Psyche

### Personality

Dilmara is plain-spoken, patient and stubborn. She dislikes ceremony and likes evidence, and she has a dry humor that the border families have come to enjoy.

### Motivation

She wants the march to bring its quarrels to her court rather than settle them with a raid, and she wants the Assize's word to hold against a Marshal as well as a shepherd.

### Strengths

She knows the march's customs as well as the received law, and the border families trust her because she stayed.

### Weaknesses

Her authority lasts only as long as the Justiciar keeps naming her, and the Marshal of the Western Army has friends who would like her replaced.

## Social
