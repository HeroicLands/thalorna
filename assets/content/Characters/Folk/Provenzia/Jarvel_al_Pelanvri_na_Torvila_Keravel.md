# Appearance {#appearance}

Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel is a 46-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. His tanned face is weathered, and a sword scar runs from his left ear down the line of his jaw. He has brown hair and brown eyes, and he wears his mail under a surcoat of the Pélanvri colors more often than he wears anything else.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 46. Marshal of the Western Army. Wants the vale quiet and in his own appointees' hands before anyone at court asks how it got that way.**

Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel is the Marshal of the Western Army of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]], and he commands it from the castle of [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]] in [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]]. The army takes its name from the old province of the West that the kingdom grew from, not from where it stands; its charge is the frontier with [[place-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]], the defense against Tarvénian incursion and the banditry that thrives along a contested border. He is brother to [[being-rutalplnvr|Rutal Pélanvri]], the Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]].

As Marshal he is also liege of the vale's lesser lords, and he uses that power freely. He deposed the Tanevar family that held [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] on a charge of treason and installed his kinsman, [[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren]], in its place. Tarlas's future there depends on whether he can end the bandit trouble in the vale before the Marshal decides someone else would do it better, and [[being-kilanilvrl|Kilan Ilovrel na Ilovren]] of [[place-ilovren|Ilovren]] tells the Marshal at every audience that he would.

## Psyche

### Personality

Jarvel is hard, practical and quick to decide. He judges men by whether they get a thing done and forgives a great deal in one who does. He is courteous to his brother's allies and short with everyone else.

### Motivation

He wants the frontier held, the vale's timber and ore in loyal hands, and his house's place at court secured by what he does on the border. He does not care whether the Tanevar of the vale love the lords he gives them.

### Strengths

He commands the crown's standing host on the frontier and the lords of the vale, and his brother is First Peer. He is a capable soldier who knows the passes.

### Weaknesses

He trusts results more than men, which makes him easy to flatter with them. His command overlaps the Warden of the Talote's, and the two men's reports to Jalnasdal disagree more often than either admits.

## Social

### Companions

His captains at Torvila Keravel, most of them men of Degavel, and his kinsman Tarlas at Belsaren.

### Patrons

His brother the earl, whose standing in the Council keeps the Marshal's conduct from being questioned there.

### Enemies

[[being-talvenekmrl|Talvene Komrelis]], the Warden of the Talote, regards the Western Army as a guest in his march. The bandits of the vale, and the Tanevar villagers who feed them, regard the Marshal as the author of their troubles.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Jarvel holds the office of Marshal of the Western Army and is of the Lesser Nobility.
- **[[affiliation-hsepelanvri|The Pélanvri]]**: He is the earl's younger brother.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Clear the road.** The Marshal wants the bandits of Keravel Vale broken before the next sitting of the Council, and he pays well and asks few questions about how it was done.
2. **The Warden's captain.** A captain of the Warden's garrison at [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] has refused an order from the Marshal during a raid, and Jarvel wants him before the Winter Assize. He needs witnesses who will stand up in a court he does not control.
3. **Ilovren's offer.** Kilan of Ilovren has offered to end the bandit trouble himself if he is given Belsaren. Jarvel wants to know what Kilan would do with it before he answers.

:::secret {title="The Charge Against Belsaren"}
The treason charge against Dirave's family was fabricated. Jarvel brought it to put Belsaren's timber and ore in Pélanvri hands, and he knows that the charge would not survive an honest hearing. He does not know that Kilan of Ilovren is feeding the bandits to make Tarlas fail, or that Kilan works for [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]].
