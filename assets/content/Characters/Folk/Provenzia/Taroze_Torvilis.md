# Appearance {#appearance}

Taroze Torvilis is a 55-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. His olive skin is ruddy from preaching in the open, his gray hair stands up however it is combed, and his brown eyes are hot. A forge burn from his novice years crosses the back of his right hand. He wears the Forge's leather apron over his robe in the pulpit, as the old preachers did.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Taroze Torvilis is a priest of [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] at [[place-batarencity|Bataren City]], and he keeps the Forge's chapel by the fish market. Every morning he preaches to a congregation of the families who have walked in from the farms around [[place-bldfldyharvalen|Yarvalen]], and every morning he tells them that [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] is rousing the field that is killing their country.

He cannot prove it. The Forge is an interested party, the Flame has an earl in [[place-harivrencity|Hárivren City]] whom nobody at court will touch, and the crown's officers in Bataren regard Taroze as a disturber of the peace. His bishops have petitioned the crown to take the western march from the Flame earl, and the petition lies before the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]] waiting for evidence that Taroze has spent a year trying to find.

## Psyche

### Personality

Taroze is loud, brave, tireless and certain. He mistakes certainty for persuasion.

### Motivation

He wants the Flame's working at Yarvalen seen and stopped, and he wants the Forge's petition to carry.

### Strengths

A congregation that trusts him, a defector from the Flame who has come to his door, and a voice that fills the fish market.

### Weaknesses

Nobody with authority believes the Forge, and Taroze has given them every reason to stop listening.

## Social

### Companions

The families off the farms, who fill his chapel, and a frightened girl from a household of the Flame whom he keeps in a room behind it.

### Patrons

The Forge's bishops in the south, who want his evidence more than his sermons.

### Enemies

The Black Flame, and the crown's officers in Bataren, who want no trouble from either faith.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]]**: Taroze is its preacher at Bataren.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: He is a freeman of Bataren.

## Plot Hooks

1. **See for yourself.** Taroze asks a party to come out to the broken henge at Yarvalen on the next new moon and see what burns there.
2. **The witness.** Taroze has a sworn witness to a burning at the last Grand Games, and someone has tried to frighten her off.
