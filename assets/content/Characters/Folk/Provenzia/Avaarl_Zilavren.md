# Appearance {#appearance}

Avaarl Zilavren is a 58-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall and is of medium build. He has fair skin, silver hair worn to the shoulder and pale gray eyes. An old burn scar crosses the palm of his left hand, and he does not hide it when he offers the hand. He dresses in black and deep red and wears a small iron flame at his throat.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 58. Earl of Hárivren and a Peer. Wants the western march left to him, the Flame left alone in it, and his price paid for every vote the Council needs.**

Avaarl Zilavren is the Earl of Hárivren and holds the western march of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]] for the crown from [[place-harivrencity|Hárivren City]], where everything the west produces is carried inland, sorted, taxed and sold. He succeeded his father in 690 AF and has governed the march since, and it is quiet, well governed and pays. Among his vassals is [[being-selvarezrvl|Selvare Zuravel]], Baron of [[affiliation-brnyzrvln|Zuravlen]] and of [[place-kyvarel|Kivarel]], whose fruit and wine pass through Avaarl's city and pay his tolls ([[affiliation-erldmhrvrn|Earldom of Hárivren]]).

He is also the most senior noble of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] in Provénzia, and he does not conceal it. His household observes the faith, the ceremonies of the [Lordeme]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} that his family has shaped for four generations involve a great deal of fire, and [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] has protested about him for thirty years and been thanked for its concern. The usual explanation given at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] is that a Flame lord who can be seen is worth more than a dozen who cannot.

He sits in the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]] and belongs to neither Degavel's party nor Atavel's. The Count of Ravenshold holds aloof from both as well, but he deals in his abstention, and the Atavel party needs one more voice to take the First Peer's office. Avaarl is the uncommitted Peer whose price is a vote. In a Council that so often lacks a majority, that makes his voice the most expensive in the chamber.

## Psyche

### Personality

Avaarl is calm, courteous and unhurried. He speaks of his faith plainly when asked and never raises it unasked, and he gives the Forge's priests the same grave hospitality he gives everyone. He enjoys being underestimated by people who think devotion makes a man simple.

### Motivation

He wants the march, the Lordeme and the Flame's open place in Provénzia to outlast him, and he wants his heir to inherit an earldom nobody dares question. In the Council he wants whatever the vote is worth to whoever needs it.

### Strengths

The western march's revenue, the prestige of the Games and a voice that decides the Council's majority. He has held the earldom for thirty years and knows every family of the west.

### Weaknesses

Everything the Black Flame does anywhere in the kingdom is laid at his door, whether he had a hand in it or not. The Forge's complaint comes back every five years with the Games, and it is louder each time.

## Social

### Companions

His household at Hárivren, which keeps the faith with him, and the officers of his court, who keep the march's tolls.

### Patrons

He holds the march of the crown and owes the king his fealty and his levy. [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] has never asked him for more.

### Enemies

The Sacred Forge, which wants him gone and cannot move the crown to do it.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-erldmhrvrn|Earldom of Hárivren]]**: Avaarl holds the earldom as its Earl.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: He is of the Greater Nobility, holding the western march of the crown.
- **[[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]]**: He sits as a Peer for Hárivren.
- **[[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]]**: He keeps the faith openly, with his household.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Both parties at his table.** The Earl of Degavel and the Count of Atavel have each sent an envoy to Hárivren to learn the price of his vote for First Peer. Avaarl wants a party to carry his answer to one of them without the other learning which.
2. **The Forge's petition.** The Sacred Forge's bishops have petitioned the crown to take the western march from him, and the petition lies before the Council. The earl wants to know which Peers have promised to second it.
3. **The champion's cup.** Rumors of poison and arranged defeat go round every Games, and some years they are true. The earl wants the champion who carries Hárivren's colors kept alive and honest through the next Games, and he does not trust his own household to do it.
