# Appearance {#appearance}

Orneta Sateris is a 26-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. Her skin is tanned and weathered by the high country, her brown hair is cropped short for the road, and her brown eyes do not meet anyone's now. A mule bit her left forearm when she was a girl and left a crescent of white scar. She wears a muleteer's leather and carries a stick she uses to draw in the dust.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Orneta Sateris is a mule-guide of [[place-batarencity|Bataren City]], from the mule-yards by the north gate, and until this spring the best guide in the city for the high country of [[place-sathvothe|Satvote]]. She has taken pilgrims up the customary way onto [[place-hydravenmnt|Mount Hidraven]] every summer since she was sixteen, and knows the paths the [[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan]] allow and the ones they do not.

In the spring she led a party of pilgrims up the mountain with packs too heavy for bread, for a fee she did not ask about. She came down alone, and she has not said a word since, to anyone, her sister included. She eats, works the mules and sleeps. Asked about the mountain, she looks at the floor.

## Psyche

### Personality

Orneta was quick, cheerful and talkative before the spring. She is now silent and watchful, and keeps every promise to the letter.

### Motivation

She wants to be left alone with the mules, and she wants someone to go up the mountain and see what she saw.

### Strengths

She knows the high paths better than anyone in Bataren, and she can draw a route in the dust that a stranger can follow.

### Weaknesses

She took a fee she did not ask about, and she will not explain herself.

## Social

### Companions

Her elder sister, who keeps the mule-yard and wants her back.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

None who know her, though the Áelendan watchers know her face.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Orneta is a freewoman of Bataren.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The silent guide.** Orneta's sister wants to know what happened on the mountain, and Orneta will draw the way to the great spring for anyone who promises to go up as a pilgrim.

:::secret {title="The truth"}
Orneta swore to the Áelendan watchers that she would speak of the mountain to no one, as the price of being let down alive after they found the Order's surveyors at the great spring. She has kept the oath by speaking to no one at all. [[being-zdmdrvs|Zedime Diravis]] hired her for the climb, and she will not be hired by him again. She wants to keep the oath, and she wants someone to mend what she helped break on the mountain.
:::
