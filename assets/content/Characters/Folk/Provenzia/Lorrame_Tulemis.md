# Appearance {#appearance}

Lorrame Tulemis is a 57-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. His olive skin is smooth and well kept, his gray hair is curled in the Válaren fashion, and his black eyes are amused. He wears Válaren silk and a ring on every finger, and he watches the world from balconies.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lorrame Tulemis heads a merchant house of [[place-valarencity|Válaren City]] that has prospered for two generations on the custom of the Pélanvri, and he is a client of [[being-rutalplnvr|Rutal Pélanvri]], Earl of Degavel and First Peer. His house remembers the last blockade and lends short, calls early and keeps its silver where it can reach it.

This season, with hulls without colors off the harbor mouth and the city's credit tightening, his house is the only one in Válaren buying. He is also a great wagerer, and at every Grand Games in Hárivren his syndicate makes the longest book in the city.

## Psyche

### Personality

Lorrame is genial, unhurried and contemptuous of anyone who panics. He takes his patron's hints as orders and does not trouble his patron with the details.

### Motivation

He wants his house the greatest in Válaren and his patron's party master of the Council.

### Strengths

Silver held close, a patron at the head of the Council and a syndicate that can buy a man.

### Weaknesses

His ledgers, kept by a factor in Kalveza, and a cupbearer in Hárivren who drinks.

## Social

### Companions

His syndicate's runners, and the Earl of Degavel's people at Jalnasdal.

### Patrons

[[being-rutalplnvr|Rutal Pélanvri]], Earl of Degavel.

[[being-chalzimechol|Chalzime Cholsamis]], who suspects him.

## Plot Hooks
