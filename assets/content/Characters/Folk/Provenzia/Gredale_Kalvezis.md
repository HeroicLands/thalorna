# Appearance {#appearance}

Gredale Kalvezis is a 49-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of medium build. He has the pasty olive skin of a man who works by lamplight, black hair oiled flat and dark brown eyes. His chin has gone soft, and a ring of keys hangs on a chain at his belt. He wears a merchant's good wool rather than a courtier's silk.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 49. Treasurer of the realm. Wants a loan against the salt revenue agreed before the frontier's bills fall due, without going to the Council for a levy.**

Gredale Kalvezis is the Treasurer of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]. He receives and audits the crown's revenue, from its estates, its tolls and whatever levies the estates grant, pays out what the court, the frontier and the coasts cost, and borrows against what is coming. He sits in the treasury at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] and goes home to nobody: his family's money house is at [[place-kalveza|Kalveza]], and he has not been back to it in years. He is of the gentry by birth and has never wanted more.

The crown spends more than it takes. The Western Army, the Talote's garrison and the ships all cost silver every month, and a levy needs the estates' consent through the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]], which costs the king something every time. So Gredale has asked the coastal houses for a loan against the salt revenue of [[place-lizela|Lizela]], and every house that answers places itself in the long contest between the crown and the Peers. [[being-ordnbrylnd|Ordan Brilonde]] is one of the houses he has asked. Every fee counts as well: his clerks sell licenses to search the ruin of [[place-sirmenne|Sirmenne]] in the north to anyone who pays, on the ground that the crown's claim to the site is unambiguous.

## Psyche

### Personality

Gredale is mild, methodical and impossible to hurry. He speaks in figures and assumes everyone else can follow, and he is quietly pleased when they cannot.

### Motivation

He wants the books to balance and the king never to need the Council for money. He also wants the Admiral to stop asking for ships.

### Strengths

He knows where every solden of the crown's comes from and goes, and the money houses of the coast trust a Kalvezis.

### Weaknesses

The houses he must borrow from are the same houses whose kinsmen sit with the Peers, and every loan is a favor owed.

## Social

### Companions

His clerks, and the factors of the [[affiliation-danvar|Danvar]] who handle the crown's paper.

### Patrons

[[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]], who appointed him and wants exactly what he wants.

### Enemies

[[being-jaltezamlvt|Jalteza Malvotis]], the Admiral, who wants ships he cannot pay for.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Gredale holds the office of Treasurer and is of the Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Who will lend.** The Treasurer wants to know, before he asks a house outright, whether it will lend against the salt, and the answer is at dinner tables in Lizela, not in the counting-houses.
2. **The missing toll.** The tolls of one coastal road have come in short for three quarters. Gredale wants to know whether the collector is a thief or the road is.
