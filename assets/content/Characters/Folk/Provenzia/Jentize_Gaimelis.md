# Appearance {#appearance}

Jentize Gaimelis is a 43-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. His fair skin is smooth and his hands are flecked with old wax burns. His brown hair is neatly kept and his hazel eyes are kind. He smells of tallow and beeswax, and he wears a good brown coat with a chandler's apron over it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Jentize Gaimelis keeps a chandlery in the north quarter of [[place-batarencity|Bataren City]], the third generation of his family to pour candles in the same shop. His tallow lights the north quarter's houses and his beeswax lights its chapels, and he is thanked in every lane for the candles he gives to the families who have walked in from the farms around [[place-bldfldyharvalen|Yarvalen]] with nothing.

He is a widower, a careful man of business and a pleasant neighbor, and he attends to his trade. He is the last man in the north quarter anyone would name in a quarrel.

## Psyche

### Personality

Jentize is gentle in manner, generous and patient, and wholly certain of a faith he does not discuss with his customers.

### Motivation

He wants his shop to keep its good name for a fourth generation, and the families from Yarvalen fed through the winter.

### Strengths

A good name, a sound trade and the gratitude of the north quarter.

### Weaknesses

He keeps late hours below the shop, and the lane has noticed the light in his cellar window.

## Social

### Companions

The households of the north quarter, who buy their candles from him.

### Patrons

None. He has never sought one.

### Enemies

[[being-trztrvls|Taroze Torvilis]] of the Forge, who suspects him and cannot prove it.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Jentize is a freeman of Bataren and a master of the chandlers.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The book in the cellar.** A man of the north quarter pays a party to bring a book out of a chandler's cellar, and does not say why he wants it.
2. **The client.** A dealer from Válaren is bidding for the Ménavar relic for a client in Bataren whose name he will not give.

:::secret {title="The truth"}
Jentize is a priest of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] and leads its congregation in the north, a dozen households who keep the faith in their cellars. On the night of each new moon they walk out to the broken stones of [[place-yharvalenhenge|Yarvalen Henge]] and burn at them, and the withering spreads outward a farm at a time. He is also the client behind the Válaren dealer bidding for the relic of [[place-menavarabbey|Ménavar Abbey]], because a relic that might heal the country around the henge is a threat to the working.

He wants the working carried through, and the field at Yarvalen fed until it is what his faith holds it should be. The dozen households would die for him, and the shop is cover nobody suspects. He preaches in his own cellar, and a frightened congregant can carry his words to the wrong door. In his sermons he names [[being-avaarlzlvrn|Avaarl Zilavren]], Earl of Hárivren, as the faith's great patron. Nothing shows that the Earl has ever heard of him.
:::
