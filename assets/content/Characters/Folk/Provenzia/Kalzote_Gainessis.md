# Appearance {#appearance}

Kalzote Gainessis is a 39-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He has smooth olive skin, brown hair worn long and tied back, and pale blue eyes that look through a man to the horse behind him. He rides better than he dresses, and he dresses in whatever the last merchant's string was carrying.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kalzote Gainessis is the captain of a band of brigands that lives in [[place-thornwood|Thornwood]], the woodland of [[place-korvathis|Korvatis]] close to the great crossroads above [[place-lizela|Lizela]]. The band has no name but his. It strikes the coast road and the great road and is back under the trees by dark, and no lord sends men after it, because no lord's land ends at the wood. Kalzote himself keeps a room in [[place-lizela|Lizela]] under another name, where he hears which strings are leaving the crossroads before they leave.

Kalzote was a horse-thief before he was a captain, and he is still a horse-thief first. He knows a good animal at a hundred paces and will leave a merchant's silver to take his mare. He is cruel by policy: a carter who resists is beaten in front of the others, so that the next carter does not. This spring his men took two horses from the stable of the Dumvaral, the crossroads inn, and at midsummer a merchant's whole string on the road. The ostler [[being-kjrvngjrln|Chorvan Jarlen]] has twice followed stolen animals into the wood and brought them out again, and Kalzote has said what he will do to him the third time.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kalzote is cold, patient and vain about his eye for a horse. He is generous to his own men and pitiless to everyone else.

### Motivation

He wants the best horses on the southern roads, and the fear that lets him take them without a fight.

### Strengths

A fine rider and judge of horseflesh, a careful planner of an ambush, and the master of every path in Thornwood.

### Weaknesses

He cannot leave a good horse alone, even when taking it is the wrong risk.

## Social

### Companions

His band, a score of men and a few women who know the wood as well as he does.

### Patrons

None. He sells what he takes to dealers on the far side of the hills who ask no questions.

### Enemies

Chorvan Jarlen, the chief ostler of the Dumvaral, and every merchant who has lost a string on the crossroads road.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: He was born a Freeman and has given the crown no reason since to count him one.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The third time.** Kalzote has let it be known that he means to take the Count of Ravenshold's brood mares out of the Dumvaral's stable before the autumn traffic, and to take the ostler with them.
2. **The dealer beyond the hills.** The band's stolen horses are sold through one dealer, and whoever finds him finds the band's money and the path it travels.
