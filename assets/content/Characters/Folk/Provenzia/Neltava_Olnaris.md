# Appearance {#appearance}

Neltava Olnaris is a 38-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of light build. She has fair, freckled skin, auburn hair braided close to her head and green eyes. She moves quickly and quietly through the palace and carries a small book in which she writes down every name that asks for the king.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 38. Chamberlain of the realm. Wants the king's door kept, and the king kept from owing anyone for it.**

Neltava Olnaris is the Chamberlain of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]. She keeps the king's chamber and household at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]], his private purse and the door to his person. Every petitioner who wants [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] himself, and not one of his officers, reaches him through her or not at all. She came into his household when he was a prince with a small one, the daughter of a knight who served his father, and she has kept it ever since. He made her Chamberlain the week he was crowned.

Access to the king is the most valuable thing at court, and everyone knows who sells it. Neltava does not sell it, which makes her harder to deal with than a Chamberlain who did.

## Psyche

### Personality

Neltava is brisk, watchful and dry. She is kind to servants and cool to Peers, and she has a long memory for who was rude to the king's household before it was the king's.

### Motivation

She wants the king safe, rested and unbeholden. She guards his private purse more fiercely than the Treasurer guards the crown's.

### Strengths

She knows everyone who has asked to see the king and why. The king trusts her completely.

### Weaknesses

She has made enemies of every Peer she has kept waiting, and she has no house of standing behind her.

## Social

### Companions

The king's household servants, who would do anything for her.

### Patrons

Kralen Lédravren, whose household she has kept for half her life.

### Enemies

Courtiers who have found her door shut. [[being-itrolaldrvn|Itrola Lédravren]] has asked for a private audience with her cousin four times and received it once.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Neltava holds the office of Chamberlain and is of knightly family.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A name in the book.** Someone has been entering names in the Chamberlain's book in a hand that is not hers. She wants to know who, and what they have been selling.
2. **The king's private errand.** The king wants something carried to the Princess of Gefavel that no officer may see, and Neltava chooses who carries it.
