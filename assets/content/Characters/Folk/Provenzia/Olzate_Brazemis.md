# Appearance {#appearance}

Olzate Brazemis is a 30-year-old man who stands 6'3" tall and is of heavy build. His olive skin carries the small white scars of a lifetime in the lists, his black hair is tied back, and his brown eyes are easy. A blade took a nick out of his right ear at his first Games. He grins at crowds and they cheer him for it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Olzate Brazemis is a knight of the Earl of Hárivren's household at [[place-harivrencity|Hárivren City]] and the swordsman who carries Hárivren's colors at the [Lordeme]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}, the Provénzian Grand Games. He has won the sword games at the last two Games, and the city expects him to win a third time and loves him for it.

He is the son of a Hárivren armorer, knighted by [[being-avaarlzlvrn|Avaarl Zilavren]] after his first victory, and he has never forgotten that the Earl made him. A Válaren syndicate offered him a purse to lose this year's final bout, and he told the man who brought it where to put it, in front of witnesses.

## Psyche

### Personality

Olzate is cheerful, proud, generous and not at all subtle. He thinks an honest man has nothing to fear.

### Motivation

He wants to win a third time, honestly, for the Earl and for the city.

### Strengths

The best sword arm in the west and the love of a whole city.

### Weaknesses

He drinks from any cup the Earl's household hands him.
