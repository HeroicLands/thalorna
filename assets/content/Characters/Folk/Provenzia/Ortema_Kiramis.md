# Appearance {#appearance}

Ortema Kiramis is a 58-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of light build. Her olive skin is lined, her white hair is covered by the green veil of the faith, and her brown eyes are steady and tired. She has a healer's clean, short-nailed hands and a soft voice that carries.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ortema Kiramis is the prioress of the house of [[affiliation-lizelas|Lizelas]] at [[place-batarencity|Bataren City]], the faith's house of healing in the north. Her sisters nurse the city's sick, and since the field at [[place-bldfldyharvalen|Yarvalen]] woke they have nursed the families who walk in from the dying farms, more of them every month.

[[place-menavarabbey|Ménavar Abbey]] was founded by a healer of her faith, and its relic, the founder's hand in a silver reliquary, was credited with cures before the Nordheim fleets gutted the house. The faith has never relinquished its claim. Ortema holds one of the three plans of the abbey that the brothers sent south, and she means the relic to be found, carried east to the withering country and used there, whatever it can or cannot do.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ortema is patient, practical and immovable. She has outlasted three bishops and expects to outlast the magister.

### Motivation

She wants the relic in the faith's hands and at work among the dying farms.

### Strengths

The trust of the city's poor, one plan of the abbey, and the faith's archive at Kalveza, which holds another.

### Weaknesses

She will not take a relic out of the ruins by force, and the others will.

## Social

### Companions

The sisters of her house and the families they nurse.

### Patrons

The faith's house at Kalveza, which keeps its archive.

### Enemies

[[being-zdmdrvs|Zedime Diravis]] of the Order, whom she suspects of wanting the relic for a cabinet.
