# Appearance {#appearance}

Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren is a 36-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. He has smooth olive skin, a thin white scar through his left eyebrow, black hair cut short in the coast fashion and gray eyes that rest on a visitor's purse before his face. He wears the Pélanvri colors, a ring on every finger of both hands, and a smile he puts on to greet a guest and takes off when the guest has gone.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 36. Lord of Belsaren. Wants the outlaws hanged before the Marshal decides another man would hang them sooner.**

Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren holds the lordship of [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] in [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] of his kinsman [[being-jarvelplnvr|Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]], the Marshal of the Western Army. The Marshal deposed the Tanevar house of [[being-diravekldrm|Dirave al Kaldremis]] on a charge of treason and gave Belsaren to Tarlas, and the deposed heir has led the vale's outlaws against him since.

Tarlas holds the Tanevar of his village in open contempt and does not trouble to hide it. He answers every raid with patrols, fines and hangings, and he uses the raids as his reason to take more: more labor on his fields, more of the timber money, more of the commons for his own flocks. The Provénzian minority of the village has fared well under him, and he rewards their loyalty in public so that the Tanevar can see it.

He keeps a map of Belsaren spread on the table of his hall, marked with every raid. He has hired [[affiliation-slntlncmpny|the Silent Talon]] under [[being-branwldrgr|Branval Dorgal]] by the month, and he hires strangers besides, because strangers owe the village nothing. He has a wife and a young daughter, whom he keeps away from his audiences.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tarlas is cordial in a room and cold when the door closes. He flatters a man he needs and threatens him in the same breath, and he believes that everyone is as careful of appearances as he is.

### Motivation

He wants to keep Belsaren and to be seen by the Marshal to deserve it. Its timber and ore are the first wealth he has held in his own right.

### Strengths

He has the Marshal's favor and the Pélanvri name, a full purse, and the loyalty of the village's Provénzian families.

### Weaknesses

His contempt for the Tanevar feeds the outlaws he is trying to starve. He relies on others to do his fighting and will not go near it himself.

## Social

### Companions

His four household guards, his steward, and the Provénzian reeve of the village, [[being-revotakrms|Revota Kiramis]].

### Patrons

His kinsman the Marshal, whose patience with him is running out.

### Enemies

[[being-diravekldrm|Dirave al Kaldremis]], who means to take Belsaren back. [[being-kilanilvrl|Kilan Ilovrel na Ilovren]], who tells the Marshal at every audience that he could keep Belsaren's roads better.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Tarlas is of the Lesser Nobility, lord of a manor held of the Marshal.
- **[[affiliation-hsepelanvri|The Pélanvri]]**: He is a kinsman of the earl and the Marshal.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The pressing matter.** Tarlas writes to a party of strangers, promising coin and the gratitude of his house for help with a pressing matter at Belsaren, and asks them to be discreet.
2. **Put to the question.** After a raid on the village, Tarlas means to put the Tanevar innkeeper and a shepherd to the question in the square. He asks the party to stand with his guards while he does it.

:::secret {title="What Tarlas knows"}
Tarlas knows which Tanevar families feed the outlaws, and he withholds the names from anyone he hires until it suits him to give them. He does not know that the charge that made him lord was fabricated, and he does not know that Kilan of Ilovren is keeping the raids going.
:::
