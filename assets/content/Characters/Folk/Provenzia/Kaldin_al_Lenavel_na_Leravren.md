# Appearance {#appearance}

Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren is a 41-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of heavy build. His olive skin is weathered by the mountain road, his black hair and beard are cut short, and his brown eyes are steady and unfriendly. An old scar runs across the back of his left hand. He wears a black iron flame on a chain at his throat, outside his shirt where anyone can see it, and rides armed on his own land.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 41. Lord of Leravren and a lord of the Black Flame. Wants the Maul of Gashang in the faith's keeping, and the Forge's knights off his land.**

Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren holds [[place-leravren|Leravren]], the lordship northeast of [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] in the [[place-talothe|Talote]] that holds the road into the mountains and forests, and he keeps his hall at [[place-leravrncstl|Leravren Castle]] on that road. The timber and ore of the high valleys come down through his land, and the lords of the vale pay his tolls. He holds of the crown through the Marshal of the Western Army at [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]], and he obeys the Marshal in what touches the frontier and in nothing else.

He is of the Lénavel branch of the [Zuravel]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} clan, and his branch keeps [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]], while the line of his kinsman [[being-selvarezrvl|Selvare Zuravel]], Baron of Zuravlen, backs the [[affiliation-brnngbrnd|Order of the Burning Brand]]. The clan is divided on the kingdom's faith quarrel, and Kaldin's branch is the Flame's link in the march. He keeps his devotion openly, as the Earl of [[place-harivrencity|Hárivren]] does, and in a kingdom where [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] grows more dominant every year, Leravren is one of the places the Flame still holds.

He and [[being-kilanilvrl|Kilan Ilovrel na Ilovren]] of [[place-ilovren|Ilovren]] are on good terms, which the Forge's knights mark.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kaldin is blunt, devout and stubborn. He says what his faith is to anyone who asks, and he takes every slight to it as a slight to himself.

### Motivation

He wants the Flame strong in the march, the Forge's knights gone from the edge of his land, and the relic the Flame believes lies in [[place-bloodhills|the Blood Hills]] in the faith's keeping.

### Strengths

The road the march needs, the tolls it pays, and a household of soldiers who share his faith.

### Weaknesses

His faith is open, so every Forge knight in the vale watches him, and the Marshal would be glad of a reason to give Leravren to a Pélanvri.

## Social

### Companions

The priests of the Flame who keep his hall, and the captain of his household.

### Patrons

He holds of the crown through the Marshal, and looks to the Earl of Hárivren as the Flame's senior lord in the kingdom.

### Enemies

The Knights of the Sacred Forge, who keep their vigil at the Blood Hills on the edge of his land every year. [[being-jarvelplnvr|Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]], the Marshal, whom he asks every year why the Forge's knights ride armed through a lordship that is not theirs.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Kaldin is of the Lesser Nobility, lord of Leravren.
- **[[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]]**: He is a devotee and a patron of its priests in the march.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The toll on the ore road.** The lords of the vale say Kaldin's tolls rise every time the Forge's knights ride through Leravren. A carters' company hires a party to take its ore down the road and find out what the toll is this month, and why.
2. **The vigil's escort.** Kaldin demands that the Forge's knights riding to the Blood Hills vigil take an escort of his men through his land, and the knights refuse. Both sides want a neutral party on the road.

:::secret {title="Kaldin's soldiers"}
Kaldin is Kilan's ally in Kilan's game against [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]]. He has lent Kilan Black Flame soldiers, who serve in Kilan's household as Kilan's own men, and three of them are placed with the outlaw [[being-diravekldrm|Dirave al Kaldremis]]. They are ready to hold Belsaren once the Marshal removes its lord. Kaldin has promised Kilan a high place in the faith if he brings the [[scenario-maulgashng|Maul of Gashang]] out of its shrine, and his envoys meet Kilan at a grove near Ilovren at each new moon ([[scenario-webdeceit|The Web of Deceit]]).
