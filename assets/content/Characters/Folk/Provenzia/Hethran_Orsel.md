# Appearance {#appearance}

Hethran Orsel is a 67-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall and is of light build, stooped a little now. His fair skin is weathered by sixty years of the north quay, his white hair is long and his white beard is braided in two, as the old men of the north quarter wear them. His gray eyes are grave. He dresses in the gray wool of the quarter and wears no ornament but a ring of his house.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hethran Orsel is an elder of the [[lore-elavendrnppl|Élavendren]] of [[place-batarencity|Bataren City]], the head of a family that has kept warehouses on the north quay since before the present crowns. He trades timber, furs and fish north to [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]] and beyond, and the elders of the north quarter meet in the room behind his warehouses, under the genealogies of the old families.

By the elders' custom the Orsel warehouses pass to his sister's son, as they have for generations. Provénzian law does not recognize it, and the crown's notary refused to register the inheritance. The notary was found in the harbor this year. Hethran has a list of eleven Élavendren holdings that fell to the crown when their inheritances were refused, and he has noticed that all eleven were leased within the year to the same family.

## Psyche

### Personality

Hethran is grave, slow to speak and slower to anger, and he has believed all his life that the law would come round. He is beginning to doubt it.

### Motivation

He wants his sister's son to inherit, the young men of the quarter held back from the street, and a law that recognizes the elders' custom.

### Strengths

The elders' trust, a list of eleven holdings and the patience of a man who has outlived three bailiffs.

### Weaknesses

His young men will not wait much longer, and he cannot hold them with nothing to show.

## Social

### Companions

The elders of the north quarter, and his sister's son, who wants to fight.

### Patrons

[[being-kiramailthor|Kirama Iltoris]], the countess, who has offered to take the refused registrations before the high courts.

### Enemies

Whoever stands behind the name on the eleven leases, and whoever put the notary in the harbor.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Hethran is a freeman of Bataren and an elder of its Élavendren.
