# Appearance {#appearance}

Solkime Revotandis is a 63-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of light build, stooped now so that he looks shorter. His fair skin is deeply lined, his hair is white and his blue eyes are clear and sharp. His right hand trembles when he writes, and he dictates his judgments. He wears the black of the high courts at all hours.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 63. Justiciar of the realm. Wants the law kept at Aurelane and the court kept out of it.**

Solkime Revotandis is the Justiciar of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]], its chief justice, and he presides over the high courts at [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]], where Provénzian law, Vilérian law as received, amended and argued over, is still made. He appoints the justice of the [[affiliation-wntrassize|Winter Assize]] each autumn, and the [[lore-chrtrrghts|Charter of Rights]] is argued before him whenever a party claims the crown has broken it. If the king were abroad or could not rule, the Justiciar would rule as regent. He has held the office since the last years of [[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]].

He is of the old Aurelane families who regard [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] as a counting-house that has acquired a throne, and he keeps to Aurelane. The king comes to the high courts; the Justiciar does not go to the king.

## Psyche

### Personality

Solkime is courteous, slow and immovable. He hears every argument out, and he reserves his judgment longer than any party can bear.

### Motivation

He wants the high courts to outlast every king and every Peer, and the law decided by reason at Aurelane rather than by bargaining at Jalnasdal.

### Strengths

The whole profession of law in the kingdom trained under him or under men he trained, and his judgment carries more weight than his office.

### Weaknesses

He is old and tires, and the Princess of Gefavel's petition is coming to his courts whether the Council decides it or not. Any judgment he gives in it makes him an enemy at Jalnasdal.

## Social

### Companions

The justices and advocates of the high courts, and the masters of the [[affiliation-acadlaw|Academy of Law]], whose Rector, [[being-kolmetgrdl|Kolmete Gredalis]], was his fellow student.

### Patrons

[[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]], who kept him in office and has never asked him to judge a case a particular way.

### Enemies

None he would name. The Chancellor's clerks resent a court they cannot seal against.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Solkime holds the office of Justiciar and is of the Lesser Nobility.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Gefavel case.** The Princess of Gefavel's lawyers mean to bring her claim to the high courts if the Council will not hear it. The Justiciar wants an honest account of the writ of resumption before anyone argues it in front of him.
2. **An appointment for the Assize.** The Justiciar must name the justice of the Winter Assize before the passes close, and the Warden of the Talote has asked him not to send the same one again. He wants to know why.
