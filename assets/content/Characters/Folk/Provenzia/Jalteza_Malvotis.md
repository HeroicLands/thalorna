# Appearance {#appearance}

Jalteza Malvotis is a 43-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build. Her tanned face is weathered by sea and wind, her black hair is cropped short and her brown eyes are narrowed against a glare that is not there. Two fingers of her left hand were lost to a hawser when she was young. She wears a sea-officer's plain coat at court and sees no reason to change it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 43. Admiral of the realm. Wants enough ships that no blockade can close Válaren again.**

Jalteza Malvotis is the Admiral of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]. She commands [[affiliation-crownships|the crown's ships]] and the coasts they guard, and she keeps her flag at [[place-valarencity|Válaren City]], the great southern port that lives wholly on the sea. She came up through the ships of a Válaren merchant house, took a crown command in the years after the last blockade of the city, and was made Admiral by [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] in his first years. She holds a knight's fee for it.

Válaren has never been taken by assault, and a blockade is the one thing it fears. The last one held the roads and the water for a quarter of a year, and the damage to the city's standing outlasted it by a generation ([[scenario-siegevalrn|The Siege of Válaren]]). Jalteza means it never to happen again, and the crown has fewer ships than she needs to keep that promise.

## Psyche

### Personality

Jalteza is blunt, impatient and loyal. She says what a thing costs before anyone asks, and she has no patience for a court that measures a fleet by its banners.

### Motivation

She wants ships, crews and the silver to keep them at sea in winter, and she wants the coasts of the south and the west both guarded, not only the rich one.

### Strengths

The captains of the south respect her, and the merchant houses of Válaren, who remember the blockade, will lend her what the crown will not.

### Weaknesses

The Treasurer controls her money, and the Council of Peers has never voted a levy for ships. She has no patience for either.

## Social

### Companions

Her captains, and the merchant captains of Válaren who run their own ships under her flag in a bad year.

### Patrons

Kralen Lédravren, who made her Admiral and has not given her the fleet she asked for.

### Enemies

[[being-gredalekalv|Gredale Kalvezis]], the Treasurer, who answers every request for ships with a page of figures.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Jalteza holds the office of Admiral and is of knightly rank.
- **[[affiliation-crownships|The Crown's Ships]]**: She commands them as Admiral.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Ships nobody owns.** Ships with no house's mark have been seen off the coast below Válaren, one at a time and never together. The Admiral wants to know whose they are before the merchant houses start hoarding grain.
2. **A captain's loan.** Jalteza wants a Válaren house to fit out two ships at its own cost in return for a share of the prizes, and she needs someone to carry the offer whom the Treasurer does not know.
