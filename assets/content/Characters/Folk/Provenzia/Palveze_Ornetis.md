# Appearance {#appearance}

Palveze Ornetis is a 45-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. His olive skin is sallow from the counting room, his black hair is cut short, and his brown eyes are quick. He is always walking somewhere, and he carries the barony's keys on a chain at his belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Palveze Ornetis is the steward of [[being-selvarezrvl|Selvare Zuravel]], Baron of [[affiliation-brnyzrvln|Zuravlen]] and of [[place-kyvarel|Kivarel]], and he has served the baron for twenty years. He runs the barony from the market hall at [[place-elthrembis|Eltrembis]]: the drying yards, the water-rolls, the manor customs, the rents and the baron's share of every crop dried in the yards, which is the barony's largest income.

Every harvest more fresh fruit goes down the cut to [[place-volsather|Volsater]] and south on the fast boats, and none of it pays the share. Palveze has made the grading of fresh fruit at the head of the cut his cause, and the Volsater masters hate him for it. At Kivarel he has told the factor and the priests of Sélmoros that the field is the tenants' business, and he would quietly forgive part of the tenants' debt to the yards to keep the field out of anyone's mouth, if the baron let him.

## Psyche

### Personality

Palveze is brisk, loyal, tireless and humorless. He does not ask how a thing is done if it is done.

### Motivation

He wants the yards full and the baron's income growing, and he wants the boats brought to heel.

### Strengths

The barony's books, the yards' men and twenty years of the baron's trust.

### Weaknesses

He gives orders without asking how they will be carried out.

## Social

### Companions

The yard foremen and the clerks of the market hall.

### Patrons

[[being-selvarezrvl|Selvare Zuravel]], the baron.

### Enemies

The masters of Volsater's fast boats, and the smallholders who refuse the yard share.

- **[[affiliation-brnyzrvln|Barony of Zuravlen]]**: Palveze is the baron's steward.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The grader at the cut.** Palveze hires a party to guard the grader he has sent to the head of the cut, where the masters have threatened to throw him in.

:::secret {title="The truth"}
Palveze told the foreman of the drying yards that the boats' fruit must become a scandal before the fair. The foreman worked a rat purge into three baskets waiting at the head of the cut, and an old man died at a Válaren table. Palveze learned of it when the death was reported, sent the foreman up to the high pastures, and has been covering it since. The order he gave without asking how it would be carried out was carried out with a purge.
:::
