# Appearance {#appearance}

Tolveze Melvarandis is a 52-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of heavy build. His olive skin is florid, his brown hair is oiled back, and his brown eyes are friendly. He wears the crown's bailiff's signet on his right hand and a good coat that has been let out twice.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tolveze Melvarandis is the crown's bailiff at [[place-batarencity|Bataren City]]. He collects the crown's dues in the city and the low country around it, keeps the rolls of the crown's holdings there, and leases or sells for the crown the holdings that fall to it. In Bataren most of those are Élavendren houses whose inheritances the crown's notaries would not register, because they pass by the elders' custom in ways Provénzian law does not recognize.

He has held the office for nine years, and he is liked in the harbor quarter, where he dines well and lends money to friends. He is careful to keep the city's troubles from [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]], and the crown's other officers in the north thank him for it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tolveze is affable, generous with other people's money, and patient in pursuit of his own. He believes that everybody has a price and offers it promptly.

### Motivation

He wants the north quay's warehouses, and he wants nobody at Jalnasdal asking how the crown's escheats are leased.

### Strengths

The crown's office, its rolls, and the gratitude of every officer whose troubles he has kept quiet.

### Weaknesses

Every lease he has granted on an Élavendren holding is in one family's name, and the rolls say so.

## Social

### Companions

The harbor quarter's dining tables, and his wife's family.

### Patrons

The crown, whose officer he is.

### Enemies

[[being-hthrnorsl|Hethran Orsel]] and the Élavendren elders, and [[being-kiramailthor|Kirama Iltoris]], who asks too many questions.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Tolveze is of the Gentry and holds the crown's office of bailiff at Bataren.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A lease of your own.** Tolveze offers a party that has been useful to him the lease of a good holding on the north quay, at a price nobody else could get.

:::secret {title="The truth"}
For six years Tolveze has bought the best of the escheated Élavendren holdings himself, through his wife's brother's name, at prices he set. He pays men off the dying farms to keep the communities at each other's throats, so that the notaries refuse every registration while the city is unsettled and nobody at Jalnasdal asks why. When the notary who had refused the Orsel inheritance told him he would register it after all, Tolveze had him put in the harbor.
:::
