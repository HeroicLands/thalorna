# Appearance {#appearance}

Itrola Lédravren is a 35-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She has olive skin, black hair worn up under a net of plain silver, and gray eyes. Her narrow face has the long Lédravren jaw that the coins give the king. She dresses in the dark colors of a house in mourning for its standing and wears no jewel the crown granted.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 35. Princess of Gefavel, of the blood. Wants her father's grants and the great seal restored to her branch.**

Itrola Lédravren is the Princess of [[place-gwefavel|Gefavel]], the head of a cadet branch of [[affiliation-hseledravren|House Lédravren]] and so a princess of the blood of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]. Her branch descends from a younger son of the house, and its princes held crown lands by grant and, in her father's time, the great seal as Chancellor. In 688 AF [[being-kingvelarikiii|Vélarik III]] resumed the grants and took the seal, and the branch lost its standing at court ([[lore-velarikreign|The Reign of Vélarik III]]). Her father died without recovering either, and Itrola has held Gefavel since she was seventeen.

She sits in the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]] by right of her blood, keeps a house at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] for its sittings, and governs Gefavel from her stronghold, [[place-gefavelcstl|Gefavel Castle]], in the seasons between. Her petition to the Council for the grants stands on the Charter: she holds that the writ of resumption was issued without the Council's counsel, and that a grant the Charter protects cannot be taken by writ alone.

## Psyche

### Personality

Itrola is controlled, exact and unforgiving. She is gracious in the Provénzian manner and keeps an account of every slight beneath it, and she has never once raised her voice in the Council.

### Motivation

She wants the grants back and the seal back, in that order, and she wants them by the Charter, so that no king can take them again. She does not want the crown. She has said so, and the court has not believed her.

### Strengths

Her blood gives her a seat in the Council that no king can take away and a claim nobody can dismiss as a stranger's. She knows the Charter's text better than most of the high court's justices, and she is patient.

### Weaknesses

Gefavel is poorer without the grants, and she has borrowed to keep her house's state at Jalnasdal, where the court of love she holds in the Council's sittings is the one the Peers come to ([[lore-courtlylove|Courtly Love]]). She needs votes she does not have, and the party that can give them is the Count of Atavel's, whose head holds the seal she wants.

## Social

### Companions

Her household is small and loyal, and her lawyers are justices' sons from [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]] who see a great case in her petition.

### Patrons

She has no patron and wants none. Her cousin the king is the one person who could give her what she wants without a vote, and he has chosen not to.

### Enemies

[[being-velzoreaitv|Velzore Aitevis]], the Count of Atavel, holds the great seal as Chancellor. [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] keeps the resumed lands because they pay for his household.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Itrola is a Princess of the Blood.
- **[[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]]**: She sits as a Peer by right of her blood and of Gefavel.
- **[[affiliation-hseledravren|House Lédravren]]**: She heads its Gefavel branch.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A sight of the writ.** Itrola wants a copy of the writ of resumption from the Chancellor's archive at Jalnasdal, and the Chancellor will not give one. A party that can get her a true copy, honestly or otherwise, earns a princess's gratitude.
2. **The price of Atavel's votes.** The Count of Atavel's party will vote for her petition if she takes his side against his brother in the quarrel over the Atavel inheritance. She wants to know what that quarrel is really about before she takes a side in it.
3. **The creditors at Jalnasdal.** A moneylender of the [[affiliation-danvar|Danvar]] holds her notes and has begun asking about Gefavel's revenue. She needs the debt carried until the Council sits, and the lender needs a reason to wait.
