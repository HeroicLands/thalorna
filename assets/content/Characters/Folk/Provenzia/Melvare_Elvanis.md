# Appearance {#appearance}

Melvare Elvanis is a 49-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. He keeps his black hair and beard close-cut and his olive skin smooth, and he dresses in the sober dark cloth of a man who can afford to be understated. His gray eyes are pleasant and give nothing away, and a deep crease between his brows is the only line his face admits to. He wears one ring, a signet of red stone on his smallest finger, and turns it when he is thinking.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 49. Count of Ravenshold and a Peer. Wants his standing kept, his vote kept uncommitted, and his private affairs kept private.**

Melvare Elvanis is the Count of [[place-ravenshold|Ravenshold]] and holds his county of the crown of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]. The county makes him rich, his steward keeps it from [[place-ravensholdcstl|Ravenshold Castle]], and he lives at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] for most of the year, where the court and the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]] are. He inherited young and has held Ravenshold for more than twenty years.

He is a patron of the theater as a great Provénzian is expected to be, and more generous than most. He is a patron of standing of [[affiliation-prvnzthtrclctv|the Provénzia Theater Collective]], he is seen at every endorsed play of the season, and the companies that want a new piece staged without trouble go first to his steward. He also breeds horses, and keeps his best mares at the inn on the crossroads above [[place-lizela|Lizela]], where the ostler [[being-kjrvngjrln|Chorvan Jarlen]] has charge of them.

In the Council he belongs to neither of the parties that divide it. He holds aloof from the quarrel between [[being-rutalplnvr|Rutal Pélanvri]], Earl of [[place-degavel|Degavel]], and [[being-velzoreaitv|Velzore Aitevis]], Count of [[place-athavel|Atavel]], and both men court him for it. A count who can be relied on by neither side is valuable to both, and his discretion is what makes him so.

## Psyche

### Personality

Melvare is courteous, unhurried and witty in the Provénzian way, which is to say that he can cut a man inside a compliment and leave him smiling. He enjoys plays, horses and company, and he takes care that nobody sees which of the three he enjoys most.

### Motivation

He wants his standing at court kept exactly where it is, between the parties and above them, because that is where his vote is worth most. He wants the theater free enough to be worth patronizing and prudent enough not to embarrass him.

### Strengths

The revenue of a rich county, a Peer's voice in a Council that seldom has a majority, and the gratitude of half the playwrights in the capital.

### Weaknesses

His value rests on his discretion, and he has a private life that would not survive being talked about.

## Social

### Companions

His steward, who keeps his accounts and his door, and the players he dines with after a first night.

### Patrons

He holds of the crown, and owes [[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]] his fealty and his counsel in the Council.

### Enemies

None declared. Both parties of the Council would like him committed, and either would like him ruined rather than committed to the other.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Melvare is of the Greater Nobility, holding the county of Ravenshold of the crown.
- **[[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]]**: He sits as a Peer for Ravenshold.
- **[[affiliation-prvnzthtrclctv|The Provénzia Theater Collective]]**: He is a patron of standing.

:::secret {title="The count's arrangement"}
For four years the count has kept an arrangement with [[being-kelnstylgn|Kelena Estilgon]], a courtesan of [[place-pirnebris|Pirnebris]]. It is more transaction than intimacy, though he is fond of her: he provides substantial financial support, and she provides her company and her discretion about his private affairs. What she knows of those affairs would cost him his place between Degavel and Atavel if either learned it.
:::

## Plot Hooks

1. **The First Peer's vote.** Envoys of Degavel and Atavel have each asked the count how he will vote when the First Peer is chosen at the year's first sitting. He wants a party to learn what each envoy has been told to offer the Earl of Hárivren, so that he can price his own abstention.
2. **The play that names a count.** A company of the Collective has a new comedy of a magnate who keeps two households, and the Convocation will hear it at its next meeting. The count wants to know who wrote it and whom it means, before the First Speaker decides whether to endorse it.
3. **The mare at the crossroads.** His best brood mare has sickened in the inn's stable above Lizela, and the count wants her guarded while the ostler finds out what ails her.
