# Appearance {#appearance}

Kolmete Gredalis is a 66-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of heavy build. His fair skin is ruddy, his white hair is cropped close, and his gray eyes are pouched and shrewd. His voice is deep and carries across a courtroom without his raising it. He wears the black of the high courts with the academy's gray hood, and he walks with a stick he does not need.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 66. Rector of the Academy of Law. Wants the academy's opinion on the salt loan to be one opinion, and his.**

Kolmete Gredalis is the Rector of the [[affiliation-acadlaw|Academy of Law]] at [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]], chosen by its masters for life. He keeps the academy's roll and charter, licenses the advocates who plead before the high courts on the masters' vote, and carries the academy's opinions to the Justiciar. He pleaded before the high courts for thirty years before the masters chose him, and he has known [[being-solkimervtn|Solkime Revotandis]], the Justiciar, since they were students together.

The Justiciar has asked the academy for its opinion on the crown's loan against the salt revenue, and the masters have given three. Kolmete must carry one to the Justiciar or tell him the academy has none, and he would rather not tell his oldest friend that the academy cannot agree. At the same time the masters are divided over whether the recited record of the skald [[being-bldnstylgn|Balden Estilgon]] can be heard in evidence, and the academy's best advocate has been hired to argue that it cannot.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kolmete is genial, stubborn and proud. He is a good host and a bad loser, and he has never once conceded a point in a lecture.

### Motivation

He wants the academy's opinion to carry the weight it carried when he was young, and he wants the salt loan decided by law at Aurelane rather than by bargaining at Jalnasdal.

### Strengths

Most of the justices of the high courts were his students, and the Justiciar is his friend.

### Weaknesses

His masters know he wants one opinion, and the ones who disagree with him are writing for whoever pays.

## Social

### Companions

The masters of the academy, the justices of the high courts, and the Rectors of the other two academies, [[being-nelamzsatr|Nelameza Sateris]] and [[being-cholsamtlm|Cholsama Tulemis]].

### Patrons

None. The academy licenses its own advocates and owes the crown nothing but its charter.

### Enemies

The masters who drew the other two opinions, and the advocates of both parties who are paid to say the academy is divided.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-acadlaw|Academy of Law]]**: Kolmete is its Rector.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: He is of the Gentry by his house.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The missing draft.** A master's draft opinion on the salt loan has gone from his rooms before it reached Kolmete, and a copy is said to be at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]]. Kolmete wants it found before the Justiciar hears of it.
2. **The skald's hearing.** Kolmete wants Balden Estilgon heard, because he wants to hear him, and he asks a party to see the skald reaches the court on the day.
