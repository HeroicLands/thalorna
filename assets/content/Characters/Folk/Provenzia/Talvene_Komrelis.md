# Appearance {#appearance}

Talvene Komrelis is a 50-year-old man who stands six feet tall and is of heavy build. His tanned face is weathered and chapped by mountain winters, his black hair is going gray, and his gray eyes are pale against it. He limps on the right leg, where a Tarvénian arrow went in years ago, and he will not use a stick. He wears a sheepskin over his mail from first frost to the thaw.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 50. Warden of the Talote. Wants the march held by the people who live in it, and the Western Army kept out of his garrison's business.**

Talvene Komrelis is the Warden of the Talote, and he holds [[place-talothe|the Talote]], the eastern march of the [[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]], for the crown from the walls of [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]]. He keeps the fortress and its garrison, musters the march's levy every spring and disperses it every autumn, and keeps the peace while the [[affiliation-wntrassize|Winter Assize]] sits through the winter. He is of a border family of the march and has spent his life on the frontier, raiding and being raided below the level at which either crown calls it a war.

The march also holds the Western Army, which the Marshal at [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]] commands for the crown. Where the Warden's charge ends and the Marshal's begins has never been written down, and in a raid the question is which of them a captain obeys.

## Psyche

### Personality

Talvene is gruff, patient and stubborn. He talks little and listens to his scouts more than to his officers, and he trusts nobody who has not wintered in Durvalis.

### Motivation

He wants the passes watched, the levy paid and the border families answered when they are wronged. He wants the Marshal to stop treating the march as a place to take lords from.

### Strengths

The border families follow him because he is one of them, and he knows every pass and every family on both sides of it.

### Weaknesses

He has no friend at court, and the Marshal's brother is First Peer.

## Social

### Companions

The captains of the Durvalis garrison, and the heads of the border families.

### Patrons

[[being-kralenldrvn|Kralen Lédravren]], who confirmed him and reads his reports, and is sent the Marshal's too.

### Enemies

[[being-jarvelplnvr|Jarvel al Pélanvri na Torvila Keravel]], the Marshal of the Western Army, whose orders reach the Warden's captains during raids.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Talvene holds the office of Warden of the Talote and is of the Lesser Nobility.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The captain who refused.** One of the Warden's captains refused an order from the Marshal during a raid, and the Marshal means to charge him before the Winter Assize. The Warden wants the captain's account proved before the passes close.
2. **Hands for the raiding season.** The levy is short, and a border family that has fought its neighbor across the pass for longer than anyone remembers needs hands. The Warden will pay a party to ride with them.
