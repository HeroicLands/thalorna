# Appearance {#appearance}

Darisen al Olrun is a 57-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. His olive skin is lined, his hair is white and thin, and his blue eyes are mild and attentive. He wears a scholar's black gown worn through at the hem, and his fingers are stained with ink to the second knuckle.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 57. Scholar. Wants the Maul of Gashang out of the Blood Hills, and wants someone else to fetch it.**

Darisen al Olrun keeps two rooms over a chandler's shop in the castle town of [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]] and earns his bread copying deeds and letters for the Marshal's clerks. His learning runs to old faiths and old battles, and on the history of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] in [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] he is the best-read man in the province. He knows the accounts of [[lore-bloodhillsbtl|the Battle of the Blood Hills]], the legend of the shrine, and the prayers the Flame's zealots wrote into it.

He is generous with what he knows. A party that comes to him with a symbol, a fragment of a map or a name finds him eager to help, unwilling to take payment and ready to go through his books with them all night.

## Psyche

### Personality

Darisen is gentle, dry and patient, and he listens better than he talks. He is never surprised and never in a hurry.

### Motivation

He wants the relic of the Blood Hills taken out of its shrine and given to the keeping he thinks it belongs in.

### Strengths

His learning, his patience and the trust a harmless old scholar is given everywhere.

### Weaknesses

He cannot go into the Blood Hills himself, and he must trust strangers to do what he cannot.

## Social

### Companions

The chandler below his rooms, and the Marshal's clerks who bring him copying.

### Patrons

None that he names.

### Enemies

He names none.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Darisen is a Freeman.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The symbol.** A party that shows him a flame emblem taken from an outlaw's body finds him able to name the Black Flame cell that uses it, and the shrine it keeps.
2. **The grove at the new moon.** A letter signed "a friend" asks the outlaw of Belsaren to meet its writer in the grove near Ilovren at the new moon. Darisen knows the hand.

:::secret {title="The Limor"}
Darisen is a Limor, a Threshold Keeper of [[affiliation-selmoros|the Faith of Sélmoros]], and keeps it to himself in a vale where the Flame and the Forge both watch the town's priests. His faith has never settled the dead of the Blood Hills, because its priests will not go where the Black Flame keeps a shrine. He wants the maul out of the shrine and laid in a Sélmoran crypt, where the dead it made can be given their rites, and he means to have the party carry it there.

He wrote the letter "a friend" sent to Dirave. Its cipher names Kilan's scheme and closes by naming Darisen as Dirave's ally. He has watched Kilan's go-betweens for a year.
:::
