# Appearance {#appearance}

Tuleme Serdalis is a 60-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of medium build. He has olive skin, clear for his age, dark brown hair he dyes, and brown eyes that are always adding something up. He wears good cloth cut plain, as a man does who supplies soldiers and does not want them to see how well he does by it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tuleme Serdalis is a supply merchant of [[place-pirnebris|Pirnebris]], the walled cherry town of the eastern march, and the most important of the merchants who keep the last market before the frontier. His house sells the garrison of [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] its boots, rope, carts and salt, and his wagons are on the road to the passes from the thaw to the first snow.

He has done well for forty years by being reliable and by never asking what the march would do without a war. Pirnebris has shut its gates once already this summer, with the fruit setting and half the orchard hands outside the walls, and the merchants who supplied the garrison did very well out of the week. Tuleme did best of all. He sits at the head of the merchants who want the tavern philosopher [[being-vornantlgn|Vornan Tolgen]] silenced before the [[affiliation-wntrassize|Winter Assize]] sends for him, because the officer to be tried there will defend himself with what Vornan taught him, and the question Vornan asks is what Tuleme's trade is built on.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tuleme is affable, careful and unsentimental. He is generous at the temple and hard at the contract.

### Motivation

He wants the garrison contracts to stay his and the question of who profits from the frontier never to be asked in a courtroom.

### Strengths

The garrison's contracts, a warehouse inside the walls and the debts of half the carters of the march.

### Weaknesses

His accounts would not survive being read aloud at the Assize.

## Social

### Companions

The other supply merchants of Pirnebris, and the garrison officers he dines.

### Patrons

The garrison's quartermaster at Durvalis, who has bought from his house for twenty years.

### Enemies

Vornan Tolgen, and the junior officers who listen to him.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: He is of the Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The teacher silenced.** Tuleme wants Vornan Tolgen out of Pirnebris before the snow, and he is offering money to anyone who will see to it, by persuasion or otherwise.
2. **The housekeeper's ledger.** Tuleme's housekeeper, [[being-lyrnvlskyr|Liren Valskir]], has seen the ledger that records what his house charged the garrison for the week the gates were shut.
