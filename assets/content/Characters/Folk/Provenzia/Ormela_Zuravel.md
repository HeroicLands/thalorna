# Appearance {#appearance}

Ormela Zuravel is a 44-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. Her olive skin is weathered from the road, her dark brown hair is cut short under a riding cap, and her gray eyes take a room in before she takes a seat. A burn scar crosses the back of her right hand. She wears the order's badge, a brand in a mailed fist, on a plain surcoat, and she wears it at court as she wears it on the road.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 44. Master of the Order of the Burning Brand. Wants the western march taken from a Flame earl, without her brother paying for it.**

Ormela Zuravel is the Master of the [[affiliation-brnngbrnd|Order of the Burning Brand]], the sworn order of Provénzian knights and lords that stands with [[affiliation-sacredforge|the Sacred Forge]] against [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]]. The knights chose her for life, and she keeps the order's chapter house at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]], calls its knights out and speaks for it to the Forge's bishops and to the Peers.

She is the younger sister of [[being-selvarezrvl|Selvare Zuravel]], Baron of [[affiliation-brnyzrvln|Zuravlen]], whose line is the order's firmest backer. Their clan, the [Zuravel]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, is divided on the kingdom's faith quarrel: their kinsman [[being-kaldinlnvl|Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren]] keeps the Flame at [[place-leravren|Leravren]], openly, on the road the march needs.

The Forge's petition to take the western march from [[being-avaarlzlvrn|Avaarl Zilavren]], Earl of Hárivren, lies before the [[affiliation-councilpeers|Council of Peers]], and Ormela presses it. Her brother holds his barony of that earl, and his fruit pays the earl's tolls. She has not asked him to choose, and she knows that a petition which succeeds puts him under a new overlord who owes the order a favor.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ormela is direct, devout and patient. She speaks plainly in a court that prizes ornament, and she has learned to wait for a vote as long as it takes.

### Motivation

She wants the march in the hands of a lord who keeps the Forge's faith, the Flame without an open place in the kingdom, and her brother's barony whole at the end of it.

### Strengths

The order's knights trust her, the Forge's bishops need her voice in the anterooms, and her brother's silver keeps the chapter house open.

### Weaknesses

Her brother's position is the order's weakness, and her kinsman at Leravren is a Zuravel the order's enemies can point to.

## Social

### Companions

The knights of the order who keep the chapter house with her, and the Hammer-Priest who hears their vows.

### Patrons

Her brother, Selvare Zuravel, whose line backs the order.

### Enemies

Avaarl Zilavren, the Earl of Hárivren, whom the order's petition would unseat. Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren, her kinsman, who keeps the Flame and calls her brother's patronage a baron buying a bishop's favor.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-brnngbrnd|Order of the Burning Brand]]**: Ormela is its Master.
- **[[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]]**: She keeps the faith as one of its lay faithful.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: She is of knightly rank.
