# Appearance {#appearance}

Kilan Ilovrel na Ilovren is a 44-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of light build. His olive skin is pale for a man of the vale, and his black hair is streaked with gray at the temples. He has long hands that are seldom still, dark brown eyes, and a sleeve he keeps buttoned to the wrist on the hottest day of the summer.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 44. Lord of Ilovren. Wants Belsaren, and wants the Marshal to offer it to him.**

Kilan Ilovrel na Ilovren holds the lordship of [[place-ilovren|Ilovren]] in [[place-ceravelvale|Keravel Vale]] from a fortified hall above its village, of the Marshal at [[place-caerceravel|Torvila Keravel]]. His family has held Ilovren longer than the Pélanvri have held anything in the vale, and he is courteous to the Marshal's appointees in the way of an old house to a new one.

He has made the vale's trouble his argument. At every audience he tells [[being-jarvelplnvr|the Marshal]] that [[place-belsaren|Belsaren]] needs a lord who can keep its roads, and he offers his own men to keep them. His household patrols the Belsaren road, and the patrols always seem to reach a raid after the outlaws have gone. He has taken on new men this year who keep to themselves and are not from the vale.

He is on good terms with [[being-kaldinlnvl|Kaldin al Lénavel na Leravren]], the Black Flame lord of [[place-leravren|Leravren]] beyond the vale, which the Forge's knights mark and the rest of the vale remembers.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kilan is patient, soft-spoken and exact. He never says more than he means to and never hurries. He enjoys watching other men act on what he has arranged for them to believe.

### Motivation

He wants Belsaren's timber, its ore and its road into [[place-bloodhills|the Blood Hills]].

### Strengths

An old name, a fortified hall, a household of archers, the Marshal's ear and a gift for letting others do his work.

### Weaknesses

He trusts his own cleverness more than any ally.

## Social

### Companions

The captain of his household, and the new men he took on this year.

### Patrons

The Marshal, whom he flatters and means to use.

### Enemies

[[being-tarlasplnvr|Tarlas al Pélanvri na Belsaren]], whose lordship he wants.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Kilan is of the Lesser Nobility, lord of a manor held of the Marshal.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Ilovren's offer.** Kilan offers a party a better wage than the lord of Belsaren pays, to serve the man who will hold Belsaren next.
2. **The late patrol.** One of Kilan's archers asks a party to ride the Belsaren road on a night she knows the patrol will be sent, and to see when it arrives.

:::secret {title="Kilan's game"}
Kilan is a secret devotee of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]]; the scar on his wrist is the mark of its first ordeal. He has been feeding the outlaw [[being-diravekldrm|Dirave al Kaldremis]] supplies and false word of the Belsaren escorts through go-betweens, enough to keep the raids going and never enough to let them grow, so that the Marshal loses patience with Tarlas and gives Belsaren to the man who can restore order. Dirave does not know where the help comes from.

Kaldin of Leravren has lent him Black Flame soldiers, who serve in his household as his own men, and Kaldin's branch of the Zuravel clan has promised him a high place in the faith if he delivers the [[scenario-maulgashng|Maul of Gashang]] from its shrine in the Blood Hills. Belsaren's road into the hills is the road to it, and Kilan has begun to believe the promises the Black Flame makes him.
:::
