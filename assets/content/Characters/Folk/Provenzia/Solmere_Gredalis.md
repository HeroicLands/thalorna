# Appearance {#appearance}

Solmere Gredalis is a 36-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of light build. His fair skin is freckled, his brown hair falls in his eyes, and his green eyes are eager behind a scholar's squint. He carries a notebook in his coat at all times and writes in it while people are talking to him.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Solmere Gredalis is a scholar of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] at [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]], where the Order keeps its southern house under the crown's oversight. His work is the kingdom's old and strange things: dead speech, hill observances, the places the songs remember and the maps forget. He travels with a writ of the crown's oversight and a notebook, and he has filled forty of them.

This spring he went up into the [[place-thuravelhills|Túravel Hills]] from [[place-palvothe|Palvote]] with a hill guide and two servants, following the ballads of the hill villages. He came down a week later without the guide, and said in Palvote only that he had lost his way. He has stayed in the town all summer, and at night he goes to the spring above it and writes.

## Psyche

### Personality

Solmere is eager, clever, tactless and brave about everything except his own mistakes.

### Motivation

He wants to see what nobody has written down and to write it down first.

### Strengths

A good ear for old speech, a crown writ and the Order's library behind him.

### Weaknesses

He thinks a form of words is a password, and he will not admit what his notebook cost.

## Social

### Companions

His servants, who have stopped asking about the guide.

### Patrons

The Order's house at Aurelane, which reads his notebooks.

### Enemies

The hill families of the Túravel, who know he came down alone.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]**: Solmere is a scholar of its house at Aurelane.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: He is of the Gentry and travels under the crown's oversight.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A second journey.** Solmere hires a party to guard him on a second journey into the Túravel Hills, and says the first went wrong because of the weather.
2. **A dead speech.** Solmere has heard of a fisherman on Lake Tiravel who speaks a speech nobody has spoken for centuries, and wants him brought to Aurelane before the autumn.

:::secret {title="The truth"}
Solmere went past the edge of the Túravel shrine with his notebook open, against his guide's shouted warning, and the guardians kept the guide as forfeit. Solmere is writing down the prayers at Palvote's spring so that he can go back and speak them himself.
:::
