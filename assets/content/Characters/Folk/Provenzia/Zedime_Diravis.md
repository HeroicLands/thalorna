# Appearance {#appearance}

Zedime Diravis is a 48-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of light build. His fair skin is pale from rooms with small windows, his black hair is cut close and going thin at the temples, and his gray eyes rest on a speaker a moment longer than is comfortable. His fingers are stained with ink. He dresses in the Order's dark wool with a crown's brass license-badge at the collar, and he is courteous to everyone.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zedime Diravis is the magister of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]] at [[place-batarencity|Bataren City]], the senior mage of the Order in [[place-sathvothe|Satvote]]. Mages in Provénzia answer to the crown as well as to the Order, and Zedime holds the license under which the Order works in the north: every survey, excavation and study it undertakes there goes out under his writ, and every writ carries the crown's oversight.

He keeps rooms above a bookseller's near the customs house, with a library of travelers' accounts of the north and a map of the high country that is better than the crown's. The north is full of things the Order wants to understand, the springs of [[place-hydravenmnt|Mount Hidraven]], the ruin of [[place-sirmenne|Sirmenne]], the relic of [[place-menavarabbey|Ménavar Abbey]], and most of them are held sacred by people who will not let a scholar near them. Zedime has spent fifteen years finding ways round that, and the Áelendan know his name.

The Chancellor, [[being-velzoreaitv|Velzore Aitevis]], seals the writs that carry the crown's oversight, and he and Zedime understand each other.

## Psyche

### Personality

Zedime is patient, courteous and entirely without scruple where knowledge is concerned. He does not lie when a silence will serve.

### Motivation

He wants the north understood and written down, by the Order, before anyone else does it.

### Strengths

A crown license, a good library, the Chancellor's ear and fifteen years of the north's back roads.

### Weaknesses

He treats sacred ground as a locked door and its keepers as a lock, and the keepers have noticed.

## Social

### Companions

The fellows of the Order who come north on his writs, and the Bataren bookseller who keeps his secrets with his books.

### Patrons

[[being-velzoreaitv|Velzore Aitevis]], the Chancellor, who seals his writs.

### Enemies

The [[affiliation-aelndntrbs|Áelendan Tribes]], who turn back his surveys, and the prioress of Lizelas at Bataren, who thinks he wants the Ménavar relic for a cabinet.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]**: Zedime is its magister at Bataren.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: He is of the Gentry and works under the crown's license.

2. **The cart.** Zedime wants a covered cart brought from the forest edge below Sirmenne to Bataren without its cover lifted.
