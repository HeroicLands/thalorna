# Appearance {#appearance}

Nelameza Sateris is a 58-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. Her olive skin is lined, her gray hair is pinned up under a scholar's black cap, and her brown eyes rest on a speaker until he has finished. The second finger of her right hand is stained with ink that no washing takes out. She wears the academy's black gown over good cloth, and she speaks quietly enough that a room goes still to hear her.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 58. Rector of the Academy of Philosophy and Rhetoric. Wants the academy's charter whole and its masters free to write for anyone who pays.**

Nelameza Sateris is the Rector of the [[affiliation-acadrhetrc|Academy of Philosophy and Rhetoric]] at [[place-aureliane|Aurelane]], chosen by its masters for life. She keeps the academy's roll and its charter, sends down the unruly and answers for the academy to the crown and to the city. She read rhetoric at the academy as a girl of a minor Aurelane house, lectured on the forms of the oration for twenty years, and was chosen Rector because the masters agreed she would take no side.

The crown's loan against the salt revenue is before the high courts, and her masters are writing orations for both sides of it. She has forbidden nobody to write, and she has not said what she thinks. The academy's students are the sons of both parties, and the academy's charter, which forbids the crown to name a master, is under attack from the traditionalists among the Peers.

## Psyche

### Personality

Nelameza is patient, dry and exact. She listens to every argument to its end, and her own arguments are short.

### Motivation

She wants the academy to outlast every quarrel it is hired for, and the charter that keeps the crown out of its masters' choices to outlast her.

### Strengths

Every house of standing in the kingdom has a son who heard her lecture, and the masters trust her to take no side.

### Weaknesses

Her neutrality is a policy, and both parties at Jalnasdal mean to test it before the high courts give judgment.

## Social

### Companions

The masters of the academy, and the Rectors of the other two academies, [[being-kolmetgrdl|Kolmete Gredalis]] and [[being-cholsamtlm|Cholsama Tulemis]].

### Patrons

None. The academy's charter is her patron, and she keeps it that way.

### Enemies

The traditionalists among the Peers who have asked the crown to name a master of the academy.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-acadrhetrc|Academy of Philosophy and Rhetoric]]**: Nelameza is its Rector.
- **[[affiliation-marendas|Faith of Marendas]]**: She keeps the faith of learning, and gives the academy's lecture at the Festival of Illumination.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: She is of the Gentry by her house.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The unsigned oration.** An oration for the Peers' side of the salt loan circulates at [[place-jalnazdal|Jalnasdal]] under no name, and the crown's lawyers want its author. Nelameza wants to know first, and quietly.
2. **The countess's reading.** [[being-kiramailthor|Kirama Iltoris]] cites the academy's writers in the Peers' hall, and the traditionalists blame the academy for her. Nelameza wants a party to carry a letter to the countess asking her to cite them less often.
