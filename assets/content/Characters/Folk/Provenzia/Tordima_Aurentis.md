# Appearance {#appearance}

Tordima Aurentis is a 50-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of medium build. Her fair skin is clear, her gray hair is pinned under a plain cap, and her blue eyes are magnified by the spectacles she wears on a silver chain. She dresses in good northern wool with no ornament, and she pours wine for her visitors herself.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tordima Aurentis heads the underwriting house in the rooms above the customs house at [[place-batarencity|Bataren City]], and she insures more of the hulls in the [[place-batarengulf|Gulf of Bataren]] than any other underwriter. Her house takes the risks of the Élavendren shipping houses of the north quarter and the Provénzian houses of the harbor quarter alike, and it is the one house on the waterfront both communities trust. The pilots of Bataren pay her house a share of their fees for the custom it sends them.

This season, with hulls without lights seen in the gulf, she has raised her rates and the pilots have doubled their fees. She has said, to everyone who asks, that she regrets it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tordima is calm, exact and pleasant, and she has never in her life raised her voice. She thinks of fear as a market.

### Motivation

She wants to know what every ship in the gulf carries, because knowing is the whole of her trade.

### Strengths

The trust of both communities, the ledgers of every hull in the gulf and a partner in each quarter.

### Weaknesses

Her business depends on both communities believing she belongs to neither, and her ledgers can prove otherwise.

## Social

### Companions

Her Élavendren partner, a shipowner of the north quarter, and the pilots who pay her house its share.

### Patrons

None. She lends to patrons.

### Enemies

None who know what she does.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Tordima is of the Gentry by her house.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The rates.** A small shipping house that cannot pay the new premiums asks a party to find out why no ship has been lost to the ghost ships and the rates have doubled anyway.

:::secret {title="The truth"}
The two hulls without lights that stop ships in the Gulf of Bataren are Tordima's, held through two names that are not hers and kept in a cove on the gulf's far shore. Their hooded crews shadow and search merchantmen and write down every cargo, declared and undeclared. The fear raises her premiums and the pilots' fees, and the lists tell her house what every ship in the gulf carries.
:::
