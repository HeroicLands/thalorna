# Appearance {#appearance}

Velire Talotis is a 58-year-old man who stands 6'2" tall and is of heavy build. His tanned skin is weathered to leather, his gray hair and beard are cropped short, and his gray eyes are cold until he laughs. A Tarvénian sword cut his left eyebrow in two thirty years ago. He carries a shepherd's crook and a sword, and uses both.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Velire Talotis is the head of the Talotis, a border family of [[place-talothe|the Talote]] who hold high pastures and a tower above [[place-pirnebris|Pirnebris]], under the passes into Tarvénia. They raise sheep, send their sons to the levy at [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]] every spring, and have fought the family across the pass, raid for raid, since before the oldest of them can remember. Velire comes down to the Pirnebris market twice a year, for salt and for hands.

Last autumn the Talotis took a son of the far family in the last raid before the snow, and Velire has held him all winter in the tower. The young man's family brought a price to the Winter Assize, and Velire refused every price. He has one daughter, and he has not spoken her name for two years.

## Psyche

### Personality

Velire is gruff, honest, proud and fair to his hired hands. He forgives an enemy more easily than a child.

### Motivation

He wants his pastures held through the season, and he wants his daughter back, and he will not say the second.

### Strengths

A tower with its own spring, a family that fights together and forty years of the passes.

### Weaknesses

His pride, and his sons away with the levy.

## Social

### Companions

His wife, his youngest son and the women of his house.

### Patrons

[[being-talvenekmrl|Talvene Komrelis]], the Warden of the Talote, under whose levy his sons ride.

### Enemies

The family across the pass, and the Assize's justice, who would settle what he will not.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: Velire is of the Gentry, holding his pastures of the crown in the march.
