# Appearance {#appearance}

A hunter of the Spotted Guard wears a leopard-skin cloak and carries two throwing spears and a long knife, and is most often seen from below, crouched on a watch-post in the trees.

# Dossier {#dossier}

A Hunter of the Spotted Guard is a member of the [[affiliation-kpalulatribe|Kpalula Tribe]] who hunts and keeps watch for [[place-lpdkngcrt|the Leopard King's Court]]. The Guard keeps a ring of watch-posts in the trees around the Court's valley, knows each neighboring tribe's approach, and visits a tribe whose gift is late.

## Background

A hunter learns the valley's trails as a child, takes a voice in the Hall of Pelts as a Wibo, and goes up to the watch-posts. The Guard does not shout a challenge. It drops a spear in a stranger's path, and a stranger who steps over it is an enemy. The hunters who patrol the rim of [[place-slntclrng|the Silent Clearing]] carry torches and oil as readily as spears.

## Psyche

### Personality

Silent and patient.

### Motivation

The hunter wants the valley kept and the neighbors' gifts coming.

### Strengths

The hunter fights from above and from cover, and vanishes into the canopy when a fight turns.

### Weaknesses

On open ground, away from the trees, the hunter is one spear among many.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

A hunter answers to the Leader of the Spotted Guard and, through the Guard, to the Court.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-trbtlpdkng|The Unseen King of the Spotted Court]]: hunters of the Spotted Guard watch the gift road from the trees, and one carries the Court's terms to a neighboring chieftain.
2. **A spear across the trail.** A party crossing the Court's valley without a caravan finds a spear lying in its path, and the hunter above waits to see whether it steps over.
3. **Oil for the rim patrol.** A hunter back from the rim of the Silent Clearing wants oil and torches carried up from the river landings before the next patrol goes out.
