# Appearance {#appearance}

Sini Luwala is a 61-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. His ebony skin is weathered, his head is shaved, and what hair shows at the temples is gray. His long face is heavy-browed, and the river-fever has left scars on his forearms. He wears a bark-cord bracelet knotted once for each boat of the dead he has pushed out.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Sini Luwala is the leader of the [[affiliation-luwalatribe|Luwala Tribe]] and sits at [[place-luwalacamp|Luwala Camp]], the last river camp on the [[place-luwagbasa|Luwa Gbasa]]. Every boat that goes upriver meets him first. The elders named him at the landing, and on this river they unmake a leader more readily than on any other.

## Background

**The black water.** The Luwala fish the dark water with weirs and spears, and its catch comes and goes without warning. He has had four good seasons and two bad ones.

**The forbidden reach.** The elders hold the water above the camp forbidden, and he does not ask them why. Boats bound for [[place-drwndzgrt|the Drowned Ziggurat]] pass his landing, and he has turned back more of them than he can count.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is grave and economical with words, and he does not argue with the river or the elders.

### Motivation

He wants the forbidden reach left alone, and he wants the elders to stop speaking against his name before a bad season gives one of them reason to speak at the landing.

### Strengths

He knows every sandbar of the lower river, and no boatman who has been turned back at his landing has come back angry.

### Weaknesses

He will not explain the bar to outsiders, and a crew with iron and no patience is a danger to a leader whose elders are divided.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The woman with iron.** A salvage crew has sent a woman ashore with iron to buy a guide, and Sini wants her turned back without a fight.
2. **The elder's word.** An elder has spoken against his name, and he asks a party to listen at the landing and tell him which other elders agree.
3. **The bark for the dead.** The Maker of the Ewudi needs bark from the forbidden bank, and Sini wants an outsider to cut it.
