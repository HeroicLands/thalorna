# Appearance {#appearance}

Gbulu Lihola is a 52-year-old woman who stands 5'3" tall and is of medium build. Her dark brown skin is weathered by years on the water, her cropped hair is going gray, and her oval face is set in a steady, unhurried look. A weir-cord scar crosses her right wrist. She wears bark cloth tied at the hip and a string of river-snail shells that she touches when she is deciding.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Gbulu Lihola is the leader of the [[affiliation-liholatribe|Lihola Tribe]] and sits at [[place-liholacamp|Lihola Camp]]. The elders named her at the landing, in a night of names that no one spoke against, and they can unmake her the same way. She decides where the bands camp, settles quarrels over fishing ground and speaks to other tribes. She does not trade.

## Background

**The naming.** She was a weir-mistress when the elders sat the night, and her name was the first no one spoke against by dawn. She has held it through three fish runs, and each one has made the elders more watchful.

**The nephew.** The trade-friendships passed to her sister's son, [[being-kobolulihola|Kobolu Lihola]], as they pass to every keeper's sister's child. She does not touch his stock, and she knows the bands count it.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is plain-spoken and slow to decide, and she waits for the river to show her what the season will be.

### Motivation

She wants the tribe through the season with no elder speaking against her name, and she wants the northern boats back at the gorge landings before the metal runs out.

### Strengths

The elders trust her fairness over fishing ground, and the trade-keeper's stock is safe because she will not use it.

### Weaknesses

A leader under whom the river gives bad years seldom lasts, and the thin metal is being counted against her.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The token.** She needs a trade-friendship token taken to the gorge landings by someone outside the tribe, so that her nephew's stock stays out of her hands.
2. **A hearing.** A stranger the river brought has outstayed the three nights, and Gbulu wants a witness for the fourth-day hearing who owes the tribe nothing.
3. **The Court's fish.** She wants the smoked fish for [[place-lpdkngcrt|the Leopard King's Court]] carried late and the lateness explained.
