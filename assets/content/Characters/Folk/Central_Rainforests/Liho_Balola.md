# Appearance {#appearance}

Liho Balola is a 50-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of medium build. His dark brown skin is weathered, his short hair is going gray and his long face is lined and still. A string of Sengala glass beads hangs at his throat, and he limps from a spear wound that did not heal straight. He wears a bark-cloth wrap and a cloak of savannah cloth.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Liho Balola is the leader of the [[affiliation-balolatribe|Balola Tribe]] and holds [[place-balolacamp|Balola Camp]] at the southern edge, where the Sengala traders bring iron north. The members took his gift of iron knives, and he holds the place until the gifts come back to his door. He treats with the Sengala at the post and with the Court's gift-bearers.

## Background

**The iron.** His gift was a knife for every family, which he bought from the Sengala at a price that took two seasons to pay. The Keeper of the Iron holds the store.

**The late season.** The Sengala are weighing sides in the war between the Ruwozi and the Kolembozi, and their iron has not come. The Court's gift-bearers ask why the tally is short.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is dry, patient and exact with a debt, and he does not say what he has not counted.

### Motivation

He wants the iron back on the trail before the bands count the store, and he wants the Balola kept out of a war that is not theirs.

### Strengths

The Sengala traders keep faith with him, and the Court needs his iron more than he needs the Court.

### Weaknesses

The store is thin, and the Court's tally is exact, so a short count makes him a debtor to the one neighbor he cannot afford.

1. **The road closed.** He wants a party to ride to the Sengala post and learn whether the traders are coming or the clan has closed the road.
2. **The rider.** A Ruwozi rider has come to the edge asking for a guide into the forest, and Liho wants to know who sent him.
3. **The Court's count.** The Receiver of Gifts has sent a bearer to count the iron, and he wants a witness to the count.
