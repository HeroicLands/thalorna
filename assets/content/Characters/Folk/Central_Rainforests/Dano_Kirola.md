# Appearance {#appearance}

Dano Kirola is a 44-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build. His tawny skin is browned by the sun, his black hair is braided close to the scalp, and his oval face is open and attentive. He wears a cloth cape of Okharik weave over a bark-cloth wrap and carries an iron knife with a bone handle, the best cloth and the best knife in the camp, which are the leader's by custom and not his own.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Dano Kirola is the leader of the [[affiliation-kirolatribe|Kirola Tribe]] and holds [[place-kirolacamp|Kirola Camp]] on the northern edge. The members took his gift, an iron blade for each family, and the member whose gift most members take is leader. He treats with the [[lore-okharinclt|Okharik]] herders at the line and holds the place until the gifts come back to his door.

## Background

**The gift.** He went out past the edge alone and came back after a dry season with a blade for every family. Most members have taken his gift and one has left a blade at his door.

**The line.** The herders bring their cattle deeper into the forest edge each dry season. He speaks a few words of their tongue and carries the Pewaye claim that the forest is the people's and the outside is not.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is courteous, plain and watchful, and he is careful to hear a refusal as an offer.

### Motivation

He wants his gift taken by every family before the next dry season, and he wants the herders kept out of the forest without a feud.

### Strengths

He speaks to the herders in their own words and gives them no reason to think the Kirola are weak.

### Weaknesses

One blade at his door is the beginning of the door filling, and he does not know who left it.

## Social

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **The blade at his door.** He wants a party to find which member left the blade and why, and not to ask the member outright.
2. **The salt for the pasture.** A herders' headman offers salt for grazing the tribe has not agreed, and Dano wants a party to carry the answer.
3. **The cut post.** A grave-post has been cut down on the edge, and he suspects someone is crossing at night.
