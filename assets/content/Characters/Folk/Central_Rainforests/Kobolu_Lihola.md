# Appearance {#appearance}

Kobolu Lihola is a 36-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of light build. He has smooth dark brown skin, short black hair, a round face and quick, arched brows. A northern glass bead hangs from his left ear, and the paddle has darkened the calluses on both palms. He wears a bark-cloth wrap and keeps his trade-tokens on a cord at his belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kobolu Lihola is the gbenindo of the [[affiliation-liholatribe|Lihola Tribe]], the keeper of its trade-friendships on the [[place-luwabolu|Luwa Bolu]]. The friendships came to him through his mother's line, as they pass from a keeper to a sister's children, and every one of them is a bond between a Lihola family and a family at the gorge landings. He is not the leader and does not choose one. His aunt, [[being-sogolihola|Sogo Lihola]], is.

## Background

**The tokens.** Each friendship is a carved token held by one family at each end. He carries the forest's honey, ivory and hides to the gorge landings of the [[affiliation-morolatribe|Morola]] and brings back iron, glass beads and cloth, and he knows which family at the lower landing will take a load and which will wait for a better price.

**The thin stock.** The northern boats have come up the river less often, and the knives and spear-heads in his store are the first thing the bands count.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is courteous, exact and a little vain of his bead, and he remembers every price he has paid.

### Motivation

He wants his aunt kept in her place, since the bands would not trust a trade-keeper with no leader beside him, and he wants the iron to come before the elders count the store.

### Strengths

Families at the gorge landings will take his token without a hearing, and he reads a trader's grade from how the man paid before.

### Weaknesses

He cannot lead and is watched for wanting to, and a thin season exposes how much of the tribe's peace depends on his stock.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-liholatribe|Lihola Tribe]]**: trade-keeper, an elder by standing.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The iron run.** He needs two paddlers to take a load of ivory to the gorge landings in the fish run, when no Lihola can be spared.
2. **A cheated token.** A family at the lower landing has taken a Lihola token and paid half its worth, and he wants someone to ask why.
3. **The aunt's name.** An elder has asked him whether the bands would follow a leader who held the trade, and he wants to know who put the question.
