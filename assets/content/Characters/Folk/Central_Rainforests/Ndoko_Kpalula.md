# Appearance {#appearance}

Ndoko Kpalula is a 27-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of heavy build. He has ebony skin, cropped black hair, a square face and a dangerous smile that arrives before he speaks. He carries a spear painted red to the haft, which is how the neighbors know him, and wears a leopard-skin cloak cut short at the knee.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ndoko Kpalula, called Red-Spear, leads the Spotted Guard of [[place-lpdkngcrt|the Leopard King's Court]], the leopard-cloaked hunters who patrol the valley and visit a neighbor whose gift is late. He is the Court's war-leader and does not hide his view that the Court should be led in the open, with its leader seen. He thinks [[being-dagbekpalula|Dagbe Kpalula]], the Speaker, is hiding something.

## Background

**The visits.** The Guard visits a tribe that stops sending its gift, and he has led most of the visits of the last three seasons. He is careful to ask first and to take only the gift.

**The Hall.** He says in the Hall of Pelts that a leader no one sees is a leader no one can follow, and he is saying it more loudly than he did a season ago.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is quick, charming and ambitious, and he does not hide what he thinks.

### Motivation

He wants the King to show himself and the Court to be ruled in the open, and if the King will not, he means to lead the Court in the open himself.

### Strengths

The Guard follows him without question, and the neighbors know his spear and fear it.

### Weaknesses

His impatience is plain to the Speaker, who has noted it, and a visit gone wrong could turn a tribe that only fears the King into one that fears the Guard.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kpalulatribe|Kpalula Tribe]]**: leader of the Spotted Guard, a member by standing.

## Plot Hooks

1. **An ally among visitors.** He seeks a party who will say, at the Hall, what they saw on the Lower Terrace, and offers a place in the Guard's patrols for it.
2. **The Speaker's silences.** He asks a party to learn what the Speaker tells delegations that she does not tell the Court.
3. **A courteous visit.** A neighbor's gift is a season late, and he wants a party to walk with the Guard's visit and say afterward that it was courteous.
