# Appearance {#appearance}

The Ash-Warden is gaunt and streaked with soot, wears a bark-cloth wrap, and carries a club, a bundle of torches and a tally-cord knotted past counting.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Ash-Warden keeps the rim of [[place-slntclrng|the Silent Clearing]], the circle in the deep forest where nothing grows and the dead rise. The Warden burns the Quiet Dead, the [[being-damut|damutu]] that climb out of the gray soil at dusk, renews the ash-paths into the Clearing each season, and ties a knot in a tally-cord for each of the dead burned. The rite of the bells passes from one Warden to a successor.

## Background

A Warden learns the rim from the Warden before: the ash-paths, the safe stones, the bell-posts with their clappers cut away, and the hour the dead rise. Years among those who do not speak leave a Warden answering a gesture sooner than a word.

## Psyche

### Personality

Silent, suspicious and fiercely protective. Long silence has left the Warden half mad.

### Motivation

The Warden wants the dead kept inside the bells and the living kept out.

### Strengths

The Warden knows the safe paths and the false stones, and burns what the Warden fights.

### Weaknesses

The Warden speaks little and strikes first, and cannot tell a seeker from a fool.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

The Warden answers to no tribe and knows the hunters of [[place-lpdkngcrt|the Leopard King's Court]] who patrol the rim.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-hshcrclslnt|The Hush at the Heart of the Wood]]: the Ash-Warden keeps the line of bells at the rim of the Silent Clearing and meets anyone who comes to cross it.
2. **Oil before the burning.** The Warden's oil runs short before the season's burning, and a party that brings it up from the rivers is shown the safe stones.
3. **A cord past counting.** The Warden's tally-cord has no room left for knots, and the Warden wants it carried to the Leopard King's Court by someone the Guard will let pass.
