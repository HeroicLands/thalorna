# Appearance {#appearance}

Dagbe Kpalula is a 63-year-old woman who stands 5'2" tall and is of light build. Her cropped hair is gray, her dark brown skin is deeply lined, and her oval face is calm. She wears a plain cloak with no pelt on it and speaks with her open hands a little forward, palms up.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Dagbe Kpalula is the Speaker of [[place-lpdkngcrt|the Leopard King's Court]], who gives the King's judgments in his name and receives the delegations that wait on the Lower Terrace. She is intelligent, careful and tired. She does not lie, but she chooses what to say, and she tests the honesty of each newcomer with small questions.

## Background

**The Terrace.** Delegations of neighbors wait their turn on the Lower Terrace, and she hears each gift-bearer's business before the King does. She sends the Hall of Pelts word of what is owed.

**The two seasons.** No delegation has met the King in two seasons, and she is the one the gift-bearers ask about it. She answers each with a question of her own.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is mild, exact and patient, and she waits to speak until the other has finished.

### Motivation

She wants the gifts to keep coming and the Court to keep its peace with the neighbors, and she does not want the Court's leader ruled by the young.

### Strengths

The neighbors trust her word because she has never told them a thing that was false.

### Weaknesses

She is answerable for what the King says and cannot always know it, and Red-Spear has marked her silences.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The small questions.** A party that joins a gift caravan is tested by her, one question at a time, and a lie told to her is remembered.

3. **The unanswered question.** A gift-bearer asks when the King last received a delegation, and she answers with another question that a party can try to read.
