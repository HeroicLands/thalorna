# Appearance {#appearance}

A river-port salvager wears a patched tunic and breeches, carries a knife and a coil of rope, and has the scraped hands of someone who climbs wet stone for a living.

# Dossier {#dossier}

A River-Port Salvager works the crews that leave the [[lore-khelathiclt|Khelathi]] and Okharik river-ports for the ruins of the forest, drawn by the story of a temple roofed in green stone and furnished with bronze. A salt-factor or a trader pays the crew, and the crew takes what it can carry. The few who know the way up the black rivers do not say how they learned it.

## Background

A salvager starts as a ferry hand or a boatman's helper on the rivers below the forest and learns the back channels from a crew that came home.

## Psyche

### Personality

Practical and wary.

### Motivation

The salvager wants bronze, a share and a way home.

### Strengths

The salvager knows the back channels, climbs wet stone and always carries rope.

### Weaknesses

The salvager's loyalty lasts as long as the share, and a crew that has lost hands fights dirty.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

A salvager answers to the crew's boss and, through the boss, to the factor who pays.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-whlsdrwnzgrt|Hands That Never Stop Turning]]: salvagers of the river-ports are already in the Drowned Ziggurat, some the ragged survivors of a lost crew and some come in by the back channel with a rope.
2. **Iron for a guide.** A crew bound up the Dark River needs a guide past the landing of the [[affiliation-luwalatribe|Luwala Tribe]] and has iron to pay for one.
3. **The crew that stayed upriver.** A salvager who came home alone wants a party to tell the factor what happened, so that the factor pays the families of the rest.
