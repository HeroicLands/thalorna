# Appearance {#appearance}

Rano Ngotola is a 58-year-old man who stands 4'11" tall and is of light build. His brown skin is weathered, his short hair is going gray, and the pale scars of a dozen bee-stings dot his neck. His round face has a wide mouth that laughs easily. He wears a loincloth and a netting-cord looped over one shoulder, and nothing that marks him as leader.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Rano Ngotola is the leader of the [[affiliation-ngotolatribe|Ngotola Tribe]], and the bands camp at his fire at [[place-ngotolacamp|Ngotola Camp]]. No one named him. They came to his fire at the honey gatherings, brought him their quarrels and gave him the first comb of the season, and after a few seasons he was leader. He decides nothing alone.

## Background

**The first comb.** The first comb of the flowering goes to his fire, and he divides it among every band before he eats. A leader who kept back food would stop being leader the same day, since the bands would camp elsewhere.

**The nets.** He hunts with the nets like every other member and has taken a share of every kill he divided. The elders, not he, hold the trails and the songs.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is easy and generous, quick to laugh and slow to give a word that sounds like an order.

### Motivation

He wants the bands to keep camping at his fire through the flowering, and he wants the hunting back before the young carry their honey to the river for metal.

### Strengths

Every band knows he keeps nothing back, and the elders take his questions as questions.

### Weaknesses

He has no authority to compel anyone, and if the Keeper of the Trails and the Caller of the Sogola disagree, he cannot decide between them.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-ngotolatribe|Ngotola Tribe]]**: leader, recognized by the bands who camp at his fire.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The first comb.** He needs a comb carried to the gathering before another band camps at a different fire, and he cannot spare a hunter.
2. **The lost trail.** He asks a party that can read elephant sign to find where the herd went and say nothing of the paths to anyone else.
3. **The young and the metal.** Three of the young want to trade honey to the river-keepers for knives, and Rano wants an outsider to tell them what the river-keepers charge.
