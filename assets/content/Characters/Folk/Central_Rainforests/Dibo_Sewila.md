# Appearance {#appearance}

Dibo Sewila is a 39-year-old woman who stands 5'0" tall and is of light build. Her dark brown skin is clear, her head is shaved, and her round face is quiet and attentive. A band of plaited vine circles her upper arm, and a thin scar runs down her left shin where a serpent once bit her and she did not strike it. She walks softly and touches little.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Dibo Sewila is the leader of the [[affiliation-sewilatribe|Sewila Tribe]], and the tribe gathers at [[place-kubisewi|Kubi Sewi]] at every new moon. She sat the night alone at the pool, and in the morning the kelendo reckoned that the spirit had answered her. She leads the bands between moons and keeps none of the court's manners herself.

## Background

**The night.** She was named with three others, and she remembers only a pale shape under the water, a sound like a long breath and nothing else. The kelendo, [[being-mbekosewila|Mbeko Sewila]], decided the answer by what each of the four told him in the morning.

**The warning.** She has known the story of the court on the black river since before she knew her own court's manners, and she keeps the rule that a leader never keeps the court.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is quiet, careful of other beings' houses, and slow to give an order that is not a request.

### Motivation

She wants the Sewila's court kept as it is and her own place kept small, since a leader who gathers power around a spirit-place has begun to ruin it.

### Strengths

The tribe trusts her because she does not touch the court, and the kelendo's teaching has made her the best-mannered guest in a stranger's house.

### Weaknesses

The strangers asking the way to the drowned plain are her first hard choice, and she does not know how long the wrong river will keep them from the pool.
