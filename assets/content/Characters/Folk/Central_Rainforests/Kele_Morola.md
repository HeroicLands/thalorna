# Appearance {#appearance}

Kele Morola is a 55-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of heavy build. His ebony skin is smooth, his cropped hair is going gray and his broad face is heavy-browed. Rope-scars cross both shoulders from years of carrying. He wears a short work-wrap of bark cloth and keeps a cape of northern cloth for gatherings.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kele Morola is the leader of the [[affiliation-morolatribe|Morola Tribe]] and holds [[place-morolalanding|Morola Landing]] above the first rapid of the [[place-luwabolu|Luwa Bolu]]. The members and elders agree he has run the fairest portage for a few seasons, and nothing was conferred on him. He is the leader as long as the landings trust his scales and no canoe turns away.

## Background

**The carry.** He carried loads on the trail from a boy and has set the share at both landings. A trader who pays fairly is graded fairly, and the grade goes ahead of him down the river.

**The two keepers.** The keepers of the Upper and Lower Landings quarrel over the division of the shares in the low water. He sits between them and rules on neither until both have named their price.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is blunt, even and unhurried, and he will not be pressed on the share.

### Motivation

He wants the carry kept fair, the two landings at peace and the Khelathi post's fixed fee refused without making an enemy of its factor.

### Strengths

Traders trust his scales, and the members trust his hands.

### Weaknesses

A leader who cheats a landing is dropped, and the low water gives every keeper a reason to say that he has.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The fixed fee.** The post's factor wants the carry priced by the season, and he wants a witness who will say what a fair trader pays.
2. **The two keepers.** Each keeper asks a party to carry the same cargo past the other's bench.
3. **The light load.** A porter has been caught with a load lighter than he was paid to carry, and the members want a hearing.
