# Appearance {#appearance}

Dipo Gbelela is a 66-year-old man who stands 5'3" tall and is of medium build. His brown skin is weathered, his short hair is gray and his broad face is lined around the eyes. Dye-plant stains redden both his palms, and his left thumb has lost its nail. He moves with the unhurried care of a man who works with the offerings.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Dipo Gbelela is the leader of the [[affiliation-gbelelatribe|Gbelela Tribe]], whose court is [[place-kubigbele|Kubi Gbele]], the great hardwood. The spirit of the tree answered him when the tribe last sat the night, and the kelendo reckoned it so. He leads the bands between new moons and speaks to buyers at the river landings. He does not keep the court's manners.

## Background

**The dyes.** The tribe sends dyes and medicinal plants to the river tribes and to the Court, and he has worked the dye-pits since he was a boy. The red on his hands does not wash off.

**The buyers.** A Khelathi factor has offered iron for ten trunks from the stand around the tree, and some members say the tree's relations are only trees. The offerings on the lowest bough have been taken three nights in a row.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is stubborn, dry and unhurried, and he refuses an offer by changing the subject until the buyer leaves.

### Motivation

He wants the stand left standing and the tribe kept from the iron, and he wants to know what is taking the offerings before the young decide the tree is empty.

### Strengths

He has held every buyer off with civility, and no one has felled a tree within sight of the crown.

### Weaknesses

He is old and the tribe is small, and he cannot explain the empty bough, which weakens his argument that the tree is listening.
