# Dossier {#dossier}

Lumawesa, Rain Walking, of the Cloud clan, is a dry farmer of a canyon town north of [[place-kwatuhosa|South House]], and the canyon towns of the [[affiliation-teyumi|Turquoise People]] name him when they say what a man should be. He plants his corn deep on the mesa top, a forearm down where the snowmelt has soaked, and waits for it with a patience his neighbors call stubborn until the corn comes up.

He is a runner (_ponuyo_) as well, one of the young men who carry word along the straight roads between the great houses, word for word, and whom every town must feed and let pass. In a dry spring three years ago he carried word of a southern war party, sighted at the desert's edge, from Kwatuhosa to the [[place-hosaoyalargn|Canyon of Houses]] in two days and a night, gave his message word for word to the rain chief, and walked home to finish planting.

He is thirty-five, wiry, and talks very little. A party that needs word carried along the roads can ask a runner, and a party that needs it carried fast and exactly asks for him. A party that wants a guide to the desert's edge, or to the Old Houses beyond it, finds that he leaves cornmeal at every one.

What he wants is his corn up on the mesa top in a dry year, and nobody's help in getting it there.
