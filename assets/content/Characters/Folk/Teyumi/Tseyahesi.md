# Dossier {#dossier}

Tseyahesi, Corn Star, of the Corn clan, is the seed keeper of her clan in a canyon town north of [[place-kwatuhosa|South House]], and the women of the canyon towns of the [[affiliation-teyumi|Turquoise People]] name her when they say what a woman should be. She keeps the clan's seed corn in sealed jars in the back rooms of her house, enough for five plantings, and she is the only person who may open them.

In the last dry run her own grandchildren went hungry while the jars stood full, and she did not open them. When the first wet spring came, the Corn clan planted its fields from her jars while its neighbors begged seed at the great house. Three summers have come up short again, and her grandchildren are older now, and so is she.

She is sixty-two, small and straight-backed, and she knows the corn of every clan in the canyons: which seed comes up in a dry spring, which in a wet one, which the south sends and which the north grows. A party that brings seed from another country is the most interesting thing she has seen in years. A party that asks her to open the jars to anyone outside her clan is refused, politely, every time.

What she wants is the Corn clan planting from its own seed in the first wet spring, whatever the dry years cost before it.
