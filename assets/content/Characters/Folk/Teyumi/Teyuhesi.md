# Dossier {#dossier}

Teyuhesi of the Corn clan is the house head (_hosamota_) of [[place-hepuhosa|Door House]] in the [[place-hosaoyalargn|Canyon of Houses]], a woman of forty of the line of [Hepuluma]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Rain at the Door, who let down this house's ladders at the end of the [Year of Shut Doors]{.name data-type="lore/history"} and fed everyone below the walls. Her kin cut a doorway in the outer wall afterward, so that the house would never again need its ladders let down to feed anyone, and the oath every house head of the [[affiliation-teyumi|Turquoise People]] swears comes from her.

Three short summers have sent families down the roads from the [[place-wahaulahuvorgn|Cave Mesas]] and the dry towns of the west, and they come to the Door House because it is the door they have heard of. Teyuhesi feeds them, as the oath says she must. Her storerooms are half empty in the autumn, and she has begun sending her own kinsmen to the [[place-yaahosa|Grandmother House]] to ask for corn. Its rain chief, [[being-waimahalu|Waimahalu]], has said that she is gathering every dry heart in the north into one canyon. She has answered him in the one way the canyon respects, which is by saying nothing and opening another storeroom.

She is tall, tired and stubborn, and she does not ask the people at her door where they came from. A party that arrives hungry is fed. A party that brings corn is welcome beyond anything it expects, and a party that stands with the Door House at the canyon feast, when the two houses meet over this, is remembered.

What she wants is to keep the Door House's door open through another short summer without the canyon turning on the people inside it.
