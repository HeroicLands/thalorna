# Dossier {#dossier}

Aalohalu, Macaw Dance, is the Keeper of the South Road (_kwatuyo_) at [[place-kwatuhosa|South House]], the southernmost great house of the [[affiliation-teyumi|Turquoise People]] and the first on the routes out of [[place-chakultzklrgn|Ch'akul Tsa'kul Region]]. He is a man of the house's great-house kin. Every trader and pilgrim out of the desert is brought to him, fed once, and asked what he carries, and the Keeper decides what goes north and at what price. Cacao, macaw feathers and copper bells go north from his house, and turquoise goes south. He speaks enough of the southern tongue to bargain in it, and the traders learn only the few words of the [[skill-teyumilng|Teyumi Language]] that the house teaches them.

The other towns say that the South House has heard the south for so long that it has begun to sound like it. Aalohalu says that somebody has to listen, and he hears first when a southern expedition is on the way.

This winter his hold on the trade has a crack in it. A southern trader came up out of the desert by a western route to the [[place-kenuvahosa|Canal House]] on the lowland canals, sold his cacao and copper bells there, and went home with turquoise without ever coming to the South House. Aalohalu sent a runner to the Canal House to ask why, and its canal chief, [[being-kenuvawesa|Kenuvawesa]], answered that a guest who comes to the door is fed.

He is forty-five, sleek, courteous and quick, and he wears more southern copper than any man in the north. A party that comes up out of the desert meets him first, and a party that can tell him who uses the western route, and when, can name its price.

What he wants is every trader out of the desert brought to the South House, and to no other door.
