# Dossier {#dossier}

Yalutseya of the Cloud clan is the house head (_hosamota_) of [[place-yaahosa|Grandmother House]], the first great house of the [[affiliation-teyumi|Turquoise People]], on the floor of the [[place-hosaoyalargn|Canyon of Houses]]. She is a heavy, quiet woman of sixty who has kept its storerooms for twenty years, and she swore her oath, that her storerooms open before her chambers close, in the doorway of [[place-hepuhosa|Door House]] across the canyon. The Grandmother House kept its ladders up through all nine years of the [Year of Shut Doors]{.name data-type="lore/history"}, and its rain chiefs have answered for that at every feast since.

Her younger brother, [[being-waimahalu|Waimahalu]], is the rain chief. At the canyon feast he speaks first, and she decides who eats. The two have stopped agreeing. Three summers have come up short of rain, and at last year's feast he said aloud that the bear doctors should be asked to search the towns for the dry hearts keeping it away. No Dry Search has been held at the feast in Yalutseya's lifetime. She has said nothing in public, which in the canyon is itself a statement. Her house has twice given corn to the Door House, whose head, [[being-teyuhesi|Teyuhesi]], is feeding the families who come down the roads from the dry towns, and Waimahalu has said that the Door House is gathering every dry heart in the north into one canyon.

A party at the Grandmother House for the feast is fed from her storerooms, and a party that can tell her truly who is coming down the roads, and why, gives her something to say when she decides to speak.

What she wants is the canyon feast ended without a Dry Search, and with every town fed.
