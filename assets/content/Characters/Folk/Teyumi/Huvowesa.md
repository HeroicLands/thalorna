# Appearance {#appearance}

Huvowesa is a 70-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall with a medium build. He has tawny skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Huvowesa, Mesa Walking, of the Bear clan, is the oldest bear doctor (_matsauyo_) in the [[place-hosaoyalargn|Canyon of Houses]], an initiate of the Bear chamber of the [[affiliation-teyumi|Turquoise People]] who cures sickness by sucking it out and names who put it there. In a dry year the bear doctors search a town for the dry hearts at the rain chief's request, looking at each person through their crystals and listening to what the neighbors say, and the rain chief of the [[place-yaahosa|Grandmother House]], [[being-waimahalu|Waimahalu]], has asked for a search at the canyon feast. If it is held, Huvowesa would lead it.

The last Dry Search he took part in, as a boy carrying the meal, named his mother. She walked out of her town with her sister and was never heard of again. He has not said whether he will lead this one.

He is seventy, stooped, and gentle with the sick. He has cured more people in the canyon than anyone living, and he has named only three of them as having had a sickness put into them by a neighbor, all three in his first years. The bear doctors are on both sides of the quarrel, and the younger ones look to him. A party with a sickness in the canyon is taken to him, and a party that asks him about his mother is answered, once, and not again.

What he wants is to learn, before he looks through his crystal at the canyon, whether his mother's heart was dry.
