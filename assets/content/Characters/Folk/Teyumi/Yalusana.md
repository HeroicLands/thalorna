# Dossier {#dossier}

Yalusana, Moon Flame, is a clown (_pukiya_) of [[place-yaahosa|Grandmother House]], an initiate of the [[affiliation-teyumi|Turquoise People]] who dances among the cloud dancers in the rain dances and mocks, by name, whoever has misbehaved since spring. A clown's mockery is part of the rite, and nobody may answer it in the plaza.

This summer, on the last day of the rain dances, two clowns swept the plaza with a turkey feather, peering under the pots and the ladders for dry hearts, while a third followed them with a lump of quartz held to his eye and named every dog, every pot and every child he passed. Yalusana was one of the two with the feather, and the search was her idea. The rain chief, [[being-waimahalu|Waimahalu]], who has asked for a Dry Search at the canyon feast, laughed as he had to. He has not sent the clowns' chamber its corn.

She is twenty-eight, small, quick and very funny, and off the plaza she is shy. Her chamber is eating from its members' own stores, and some of its older members blame her for it. A party at the Grandmother House at feast time may find itself mocked by name in front of the whole canyon, which is an honor, and a party that helps her chamber through the winter finds her a loyal friend.

What she wants is to dance at the canyon feast and make the whole plaza laugh at the Dry Search before the bear doctors begin it.
