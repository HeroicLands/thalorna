# Appearance {#appearance}

Teyukwa is a 38-year-old woman who stands 5'1" tall with a medium build. She has brown skin, chapped complexion, dark brown hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Teyukwa, Turquoise Flower, is one of the families who live all year at [[place-teyupotama|Turquoise Hill]], the camp among the turquoise pits in the bare hills north of the [[place-hosaoyalargn|Canyon of Houses]], and the woman the work parties of the [[affiliation-teyumi|Turquoise People]] ask which pits are safe. The camp's families keep the pits braced with juniper poles and the work parties fed, and its storerooms are filled from the [[place-yaahosa|Grandmother House]]. She came to the camp from a dry town fifteen years ago with a husband and a child, and stayed. The camp feeds anyone who picks up a maul and does not ask where he came from, and the canyon holds its people to be a little strange for it.

The deep pits on the eastern hill have gone quiet. A work party came up out of one at midsummer and would not go down again: they had heard something moving below the lowest brace, and the cornmeal at the lip was gone in the morning. Teyukwa told them a pit that deep always sounds like that. The canyon's rain chiefs have sent no party there since, and the stone from the eastern hill was the bluest in the hills.

:::secret {title="What is in the pits"}

People are in them. A band of the Dry, expelled from their towns in the last two short summers, has been living in the worked-out galleries that join the deep pits under the eastern hill, coming up at night for water and for the cornmeal at the pit mouths. Some of the camp's families know and leave food for them, because some of the camp's families were Dry themselves before the camp took them in without asking. Teyukwa's is one of them. The band numbers about thirty, and its oldest member was a chamber elder.

What she wants is the eastern pits left quiet, and no work party sent down them until the rains come back.

:::

She is thirty-eight, strong-armed and calm. A party that wants turquoise can dig for it at the camp if it works, and a party that wants to go down the eastern pits is told, firmly, that they are not safe.

What she wants is every work party up out of the pits with as many hands as it took down.
