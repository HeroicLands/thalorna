# Appearance {#appearance}

Aldrun Somerlei is a 43-year-old woman of 5'5" and light build, with fair, clear skin, dark blonde hair worn up under a net and hazel eyes. She dresses in the Vilérian fashion of twenty years ago, which is the newest Thornbury has.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 43. Ambassador of the Republic of Ondareth. Wants to know whose man the Speaker of Veldareth is.**

Aldrun Somerlei carries the business of the [[affiliation-senatondrth|Senate of Ondareth]] to foreign powers and reports to it on her return. She is the daughter of a shipping house of the _gulmenoi_, she has been to the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vilérian Empire]] twice with the Senate's embassies, and she speaks better Vilari than anyone in the chamber, which the chamber does not know.

This year the Senate has sent her to [[place-veldareth|Veldareth]]. Her instructions are to learn whether [[being-leofwldchlwl|Leofwald Chilwell]], the master of the chapterhouse and Speaker of the city's Table, answers to the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Dúnavarre Synod]], and to buy copies of his letters to Ravenmoor if she cannot learn it otherwise.

:::secret {title="The secretary"}
Aldrun has paid Leofwald Chilwell's secretary for copies of the Speaker's letters to Ravenmoor since the winter. The letters show nothing worse than a scholar's correspondence with his order, and she has not told the Senate so, because the Senate's suspicion is what keeps her in her post.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Aldrun is charming, watchful and amused by the Senate's pretensions, which she shares less than she pretends.

### Motivation

She wants to be useful to the republic and indispensable to its First Senator.

### Strengths

She has seen the mainland, and she knows exactly how the Senate looks from there.

### Weaknesses

She cannot hold office, and her post lasts as long as the First Senator wants it to.

## Social

### Companions

Her household and her secretary, and the envoys of Dúnavarre at the Two Doors in Veldareth.

### Patrons

[[being-harwingrymr|Harwin Greimore]], the First Senator.

### Enemies

[[being-leofwldchlwl|Leofwald Chilwell]], whose position she is instructed to undermine, and who suspects it.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]**: Aldrun is of the propertied order and the Senate's Ambassador.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Letters for the Senate.** Aldrun wants a packet of letters carried from Veldareth to Thornbury unopened, by a road that does not pass the Dúnavarre envoys.
2. **A mainland visitor.** A Vilérian merchant has asked Aldrun to present him to the Senate, and she wants to know whom he really serves before she does.
