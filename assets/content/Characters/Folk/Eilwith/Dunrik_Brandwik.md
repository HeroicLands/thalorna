# Appearance {#appearance}

Dunrik Brandwik is a 38-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. He has tanned, weathered skin, chestnut hair worn long and tied back and brown eyes. One of his upper front teeth is missing. He wears forest green and a horn on a baldric.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 38. Forester of the royal forest. Wants the forest court's fines paid without driving the village into open poaching.**

Dunrik Brandwik is the Forester of the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]]. He keeps the royal forest above [[place-kingsholow|Kingshollow]] under forest law: its deer, its timber and its grazing, the foresters under him, and the forest court that tries those who break the law. He is of a gentry family that has held the office three generations, and he lives at the forest lodge above the village.

His office is the village's oldest grievance. The tenants may not cut the timber beside their own doors, and each midwinter the king gives them one standing tree, which the Forester marks. For three years the king has kept midwinter at [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]], and the tenants have sung their asking to the Steward instead ([[lore-kingswassail|the Wassail at Kingshollow]]). The poaching has grown with each of those years, and the forest court's fines go unpaid.

## Psyche

### Personality

Dunrik is easygoing, observant and slow to anger. He likes the village and knows it does not like his office.

### Motivation

He wants the forest kept and the village fed, and he knows those pull against each other.

### Strengths

He knows the forest and everyone who walks in it, and he can turn a blind eye at the right moment.

### Weaknesses

He answers to the crown, and the crown is not at Kingshollow to see what its law costs.

## Social

### Companions

The under-foresters of the lodge and the bailiff of the royal hall.

### Patrons

King [[being-aldrikdrthng|Aldrik Drething]], whose forest he keeps.

### Enemies

The poachers of the forest, some of whom he drinks with.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]]**: Dunrik holds the office of Forester and is of the Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The deer gone east.** Deer taken in the royal forest are being sold over the passes to the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]], and Dunrik wants the buyers found without hanging the village boys who sell to them.
2. **This year's tree.** The tenants have asked Dunrik to mark a particular oak this midwinter, one he has never marked, and he wants to know why before he says yes.
