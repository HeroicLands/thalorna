# Appearance {#appearance}

Thraskvir Svalthendik is a 35-year-old man of 5'10" and heavy build, with fair skin weathered and chapped by salt, dark blonde hair and beard and blue eyes. His palms are scarred from rope, and he never takes off his sea-boots.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 35. Skipstjori of Drasknes. Wants open water and a crew that comes home.**

Thraskvir Svalthendik is master of the ship of [[being-draskmilglrv|Draskmil Glarvendik]], jarl of [[place-drasknes|Drasknes]]: forty oars, a crew of the jarl's men and a hull that has crossed to the [[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]] and back every summer since he was given her. His clan holds a fishery below the headland, and he has known the inlets of [[place-nordfells|the Nordfells]] since he could row.

Since the High Jarl's pact closed the inlets to the free coasters, Draskmil has told him to bring a coaster into Drasknes past the High Jarl's ships before Midsomar. Thraskvir knows the inlets better than any man on the High Jarl's ships, and he knows what follows if his jarl's ship and the High Jarl's meet.

## Psyche

### Personality

Thraskvir is calm, careful and slow to anger. He talks to his ship more than to his crew.

### Motivation

He wants his ship and his crew safe, and his jarl's honor kept.

### Strengths

He knows every inlet, shoal and current of the north shore.

### Weaknesses

He is loyal to a jarl who takes risks he would not take.

## Social

### Companions

His crew, and the fishers of his clan below the headland.

### Patrons

[[being-draskmilglrv|Draskmil Glarvendik]], jarl of Drasknes.

### Enemies

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **A hull to bring in.** Thraskvir needs a coaster's master willing to sail for Drasknes, and a cargo worth the risk.
2. **The Thornbury crew.** Thraskvir knew the master of the coaster the High Jarl's ships took, and he wants to know whether the man is alive.
