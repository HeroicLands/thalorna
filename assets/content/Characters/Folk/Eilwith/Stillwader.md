# Appearance {#appearance}

Stillwader is a [[being-grkrsh|Grukar-Sha]] of 5'2" and light build, thin through the body and long in the legs and arms, with oily olive skin, a black crest of bristles and yellow eyes. It holds its narrow head forward, and it stands for long stretches without moving, up to its knees in the shallows, with pale lake mud drying on its legs. It carries a cutter's reed-knife. Use the Grukar-Sha's statistics.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Sha of Reedgill. Watches the reed-beds of Lake Eilìssa's northern shore. Wants its nest fed and the reed-beds open to its raiders.**

Stillwader is a Sha of the [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] nest at [[place-reedgill|Reedgill]], the wooded ravine above the reed-bays at the northern end of [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]]. The reed-cutters of [[place-koldmere|Koldmere]] and of [[place-brynwold|Brinwold]] have both seen it at dusk, standing in the shallows below the ravine, watching the beds, and they named it for the heron it stands like. When a boat comes near it steps back into the alders and is gone.

It has watched the two shores for seasons. It has heard the cutters shout across the water at each other, and it understands more of what they shout than either shore suspects.

## Psyche

### Personality

Stillwader is patient, careful and silent, and it never takes a risk it can give to a Hushk instead.

### Motivation

It wants its nest fed and the reed-beds open to its raiders. It also wants to stay useful to the spawner and no more than useful, because a Sha that knows too much is invited to dinner.

### Strengths

It understands the shore folk's words, and it knows the beds and the cutters' habits as well as the cutters do.

### Weaknesses

It is weak in a fight and cannot survive one alone.

## Social

### Companions

The raiders of its nest, whom it steers through the Hushk that lead them.

### Patrons

The Reedgill spawner, which protects it from the Hushk.

### Enemies

The reed-cutters of both shores, and the reed-reeve of Koldmere, [[being-elfithwgdn|Elfith Wigden]], whose voice it knows best.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The watcher.** The cutters of Koldmere want the thing in the shallows killed before the spring cutting, and pay in fish and reed.
2. **The quarrel.** Elfith Wigden will listen to anyone who can tell her where her lost boat went.

:::secret {title="The nets"}
Stillwader keeps the reed-bed quarrel alive. On dark nights the nest's raiders cut the nets of both shores in the bays, and in the autumn they took Koldmere's boat and its two cutters, which Koldmere blames on Brinwold. Two villages watching each other do not watch the ravine. Stillwader has learned enough of the shore speech to know whose names to leave beside a cut net, and it has begun to fear that the spawner has noticed how much it knows.
:::
