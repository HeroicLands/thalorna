# Appearance {#appearance}

Mildith Barnley is a young novice in the faith’s black, speaking softly and keeping a small knife near her hand. She watches a doorway through a whole conversation and marks what she sees before offering an explanation.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Novice of the Silent Watch.**

Mildith Barnley is a novice of the Silent Watch within [[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]]. Her work brings her to [[place-threnhallow|Threnhallow]], whose returns to the faith have stopped.

## Background

Her training is in the care and rites of the dead. The faith sends her to ask what has become of a house’s accounts; she carries its rites as well as its questions.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is observant, quiet and more willing to state what she sees than what she thinks it means.

### Motivation

She wants the house’s dead given their rites and an honest account taken back to the faith.

### Strengths

She keeps the old funeral words and remembers instructions under pressure.

### Weaknesses

She has little authority outside her faith and cannot compel a householder to answer.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

The Silent Watch is her order. She knows the teachings about keepers and the dead, without knowing every house’s history.

## Plot Hooks

1. Mildith needs an escort to a house that has stopped sending its returns.
2. The faith wants a copy of an old rite-book checked against its present teaching.
3. A family asks the novice to attend to a missing traveler’s possessions.

:::secret {title="The adventure’s truth"}
She has survived four days in Threnhallow’s bell-loft and draws up its ladder. Her tally distinguishes bell-rings from the drowned’s trips below, but she does not know that they work the gates. She knows the warning not to answer them and can speak the true rite. Do not let her explain the sluice before the party sees it. See [[scenario-bellsgreylvl|The Bells Beneath the Greylevels]].
:::
