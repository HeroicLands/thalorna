# Appearance {#appearance}

Taskain is a 29-year-old woman of 5'7" and light build, with tanned, weathered skin, dark brown hair cropped short and green eyes. A burn scar runs across her left forearm. She wears a deerskin jerkin and a grey wool mantle the color of the moor, and carries a hunting bow and a spear.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 29. War-Leader of the Bradhar. Wants the legion out of the Highwood and the burned ground to stop growing.**

Taskain is war-chief of a hill clan of the high moor and War-Leader of [[affiliation-bradhar|the Bradhar]], named at the autumn gathering of 719. In the summer the legion at [[place-kaermouth|Kaermouth]] burned the forest edge a mile further north, across the lower end of a glen where the river clans winter their cattle. In the autumn she took her clan and two others across the ash at night, took two watch-burgs, killed their crews and burned them.

The legion is coming into the [[place-highwood|Highwood]] in the spring. Taskain means to meet it in the pines, where it cannot form, and she needs the river clans nearest Kaermouth to do it. They live by the trade at the forest edge, and their shamans have not agreed to follow her.

## Psyche

### Personality

Taskain is patient, terse and sure of herself. She listens to the shamans to the end and then does what she meant to do.

### Motivation

She wants the burning stopped, and the Highwood's southern glens kept.

### Strengths

She is the best night fighter of her tribe, and the burgs fell without the loss of one of her people.

### Weaknesses

Half the tribe has not followed her, and the Kelgar are taking her hill clans' summer grazing while she faces south.

## Social

### Companions

The fighters of her clan and of the two clans that crossed the ash with her.

### Patrons

None. The shamans named her, and the clans that agree follow her.

### Enemies

[[being-godstanhrdl|Godstan Hardli]], the Vandraks at Kaermouth. [[being-alfmundkrtn|Alfmund Kaerton]], who has come north to take the field. [[being-bronnakh|Bronnakh]] of the Kelgar, whose clans graze the high pastures.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-bradhar|The Bradhar]]**: Taskain is the War-Leader.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The river clans.** Taskain wants a stranger to sit at the river clans' fires and tell her honestly whether they will fight or trade.
2. **The spring road.** Taskain will pay a guide who knows the legion's camp to tell her which road the new levies will take into the pines.

:::secret {title="The warning"}
A war-chief of the river clans warned the merchant factors at Kaermouth that the hill clans were coming, to keep his own glen out of the legion's answer. The factors kept the warning to themselves rather than bring the legion down on the clans they trade with, and the burgs were taken unready. Taskain learned of it from one of the factors' carriers. She has told no one, and she means to use it to bring the river clans to her side.
:::
