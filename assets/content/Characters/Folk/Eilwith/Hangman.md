# Appearance {#appearance}

The Hangman unfolds beneath stone ribs, a long shape with many joints. Its limbs end in hooks. A band of dark metal is sunk into one limb, cut with unreadable marks. Its hooks scrape against the stone as it moves.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Hangman is the garrison’s name for the hooked made thing in the heights of [[place-sunderwynd|Sunderwiind]]. Gouges cross the beams and ceilings; ropes run through gaps in the masonry.

## Presentation

Its long body bends at many joints and unfolds beneath the stone ribs. Hooks scrape against its holds. A dark metal band in one limb carries marks nobody at the post can read.

:::secret {title="The voices in the heights"}
The Hangman has no voice. The calls belong to living captives whom it lets call because a rescuer approaches within reach. Play each as an actual person reporting what he sees, not as the creature’s mimicry.
:::

## In the Land

It comes from the high country some weeks before the post loses contact with [[place-caerwynd|Kaerwiind]]. Its maker and purpose remain unknown. It is one of the things called [[lore-dreadspawncrtr|Dreadspawn]].

## Key Behaviors

It moves across walls, ceilings, beams and narrow spaces in the stonework. It watches movement from overhead.

:::secret {title="The dusk round"}
It hangs living captives under the arms and feeds on them slowly. Each dusk it waters them in the cistern, visiting the cistern, pine and tower in that order. While it is out on the pine, the other two places are unwatched. Whatever falls from its ropes draws its attention immediately.
:::

## What the Trail-Men Do

Smugglers use the other pass. The trail-men warn against climbing alone or answering a voice from above. Kaerwiind sends relief when the post’s signals stop.

## Signs

- Parallel gouges across plaster and ceiling beams, with none on the floor.
- Figures hanging from the dead pine above the gorge.
- Voices carrying from the heights on still evenings.
- Boots dripping shortly after dusk.

## Combat Strategy

:::secret {title="Overhead and on the ground"}
The Hangman waits beside a calling captive and reaches down for an approaching rescuer. It stays on overhead holds. The open yard is safe because nothing above gives it an approach. Brought to the ground, it is slow and clumsy, with short reach. Bringing down the cistern’s loose vault risks everyone hanging beneath it.
:::

## Attack Methods

The rescue and confrontation follow [[scenario-hangingdead|The Hanging Dead of the Ironfells]].

## Special Abilities

:::secret {title="Keeping the captives"}

### Taking and hanging

It lifts people by rope or reach, carries them upward and hangs them under the arms. A captive cut loose without a lowering line can fall into the deep cistern with arms bound.

### Watering

It lowers each living captive into the cold water to the chest, waking and sustaining the person before carrying them back. The wet boots and the voice’s answers reveal what the post’s daybook misunderstands.
:::

## Attributes

The body is long, jointed and light for its size. Hooks give it purchase on the post’s high stonework and beams.
