# Appearance {#appearance}

Neilwa is a 67-year-old woman of 5'3" and light build, with brown, deeply lined skin, long white hair worn loose and dark brown eyes. Fine blue lines are tattooed on the backs of both hands. She wears undyed wool and a cloak of furs, and she walks the forest without a staff.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 67. Elder of the Seilvri. Wants the people's dead in the Hushwood settled, and the people alive to keep them.**

Neilwa is the Elder of the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]], chosen by the households for life. She speaks for the people at their gathering in the heart of [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]], keeps the names of the woods and settles quarrels between households. She also keeps the rite of the dead, which a Seilvri elder performs over three nights in the wood where the dead lie.

She was at [[place-hushwood|the Hushwood]] about 703 and came out of it ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]). The dead of that wood have lain unsettled since, because no Seilvri has been able to reach them past the Synod's posts and the Order's patrols. She holds that the people's first duty is to them. The war-leader, [[being-arvanir|Arvanir]], holds that the living must come first, and the gathering is divided between them.

## Psyche

### Personality

Neilwa is calm, dry and stubborn, and she speaks rarely and briefly at the gathering.

### Motivation

She wants the dead of the Hushwood settled before she dies, and the people kept together.

### Strengths

The households trust her, she knows the forest better than anyone living and she has outlived every Knight-Captain sent against her people but two.

### Weaknesses

She is old, the rite needs three nights in a wood the Order patrols, and the people cannot spare the fighters to hold it for her.

## Social

### Companions

The households of the gathering, and Arvanir, whom she watched grow up.

### Patrons

None. The households chose her, and she answers to them.

### Enemies

The [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]], and its Knight-Captain of the West, [[being-merraddovvrn|Sir Merrad Dovavren]]. The Order's chaplain, [[being-edrintalvvl|Edrin Talvavel]], who calls her rites no rites.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]]**: Neilwa is the Elder.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Three nights.** Neilwa needs to be taken into the Hushwood and kept there for three nights while she keeps the rite, past the Synod's posts and the Order's patrols.
2. **The ones who came out.** Neilwa wants word carried to the Seilvri settled on the estates, and to learn whether any of them would come home.

:::secret {title="The envoy"}
Neilwa has sent an envoy of the households to Veldareth, without Arvanir's knowledge, to ask the Table to hear the people's case. She does not expect the Table to save them. She wants someone outside the forest to have heard it.
:::
