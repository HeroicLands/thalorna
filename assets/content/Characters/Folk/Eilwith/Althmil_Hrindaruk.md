# Appearance {#appearance}

Althmil Hrindaruk is a 31-year-old man of 6'3" and heavy build, with fair, ruddy skin, blonde hair tied at the nape and blue eyes. He is the tallest man in his father's hall and wears a mail shirt to the board.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 31. Hirvstjori of Stormveld. Wants his father's household kept, and no one's leave to keep it.**

Althmil Hrindaruk is the son of the High Jarl, [[being-ulthorvhrnd|Ulthorv Hrindaruk]], and the marshal of his household men at [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]]. He commands the hall's guard, trains its young men and is master of the common muster when the war-arrow goes out. His father made him Hirvstjori three years ago.

The law of the jarldoms ties no seat to a man's children, and [[being-draskmilglrv|Draskmil Glarvendik]] of Drasknes has made Althmil's post the charge against his father: that Ulthorv has made the High Jarl's household his son's, and means the seat to follow it. Althmil says he holds the post because he is the best man for it, and the household men agree with him.

## Psyche

### Personality

Althmil is cheerful, physical and blunt. He prefers the practice-yard to the hall.

### Motivation

He wants to be judged by what he does, and he is tired of being judged by whose son he is.

### Strengths

The household men would follow him anywhere, and he has beaten every one of them in the yard.

### Weaknesses

He has never been to a moot as anyone but his father's son, and he does not know how to answer Draskmil there.

## Social
