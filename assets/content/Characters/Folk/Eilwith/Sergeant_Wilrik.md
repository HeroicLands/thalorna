# Appearance {#appearance}

Sergeant Wilrik counts the people in his party whenever the trail turns. He speaks each name distinctly and waits for an answer before moving on.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Relief Patrol Sergeant.**

Sergeant Wilrik leads the relief patrol from [[place-caerwynd|Kaerwiind]] to [[place-sunderwynd|Sunderwiind]]. He knows the patrol’s names and the load assigned to each soldier.

## Background

Relief duty carries men and stores up a narrow gorge to a post whose signals cannot explain every failure. Wilrik’s work is to bring the whole party there and the relieved men home.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is steady with frightened soldiers and impatient with an unanswered roll.

### Motivation

He wants every member of his patrol counted and returned.

### Strengths

He knows which soldier is missing from a group and who last stood beside that person.

### Weaknesses

He takes responsibility for people he cannot reach as readily as for those beside him.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

His patrol relieves Captain [[being-aldmundhardwik|Aldmund Hardwik]]’s post.

## Plot Hooks

1. A traveler can help Wilrik carry stores on the final climb.
2. A family asks him to carry a letter to a soldier at the post.
3. The patrol needs a guide to bring its wounded back through the gorge.

:::secret {title="The adventure’s truth"}
The relief is taken shortly before the adventurers arrive. Wilrik hangs alive in the cistern with three of his men and two of the garrison. A patrolman in the pine calls for him, and his answer proves the voices belong to living people. Once lowered, he identifies the survivors. See [[scenario-hangingdead|The Hanging Dead of the Ironfells]].
:::
