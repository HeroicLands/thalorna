# Appearance {#appearance}

Kessa Torrek is a 47-year-old woman of 5'4" and light build, with light, clear skin, greying brown hair pinned up under a cap and hazel eyes. Ink is worn into the creases of her fingers. She dresses in dark wool and carries a bundle of tally-sticks on a thong at her belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 47. Tally-Keeper of the Valleys. Wants the reckoning true, whatever it says.**

Kessa is of one of the four tally families of [[place-barakth|Hudakal]], which have kept the reckoning of the valleys for longer than the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]] has had kings. As Tally-Keeper she keeps the count of every delivery owed up the mountain and every consignment coming down before it reaches [[place-vargate|Vargate]], and when a conduit fails or a granary does not keep, she is the one who sends word up to the Gate. The Khazari have never once queried a figure of hers.

At the autumn tally of 719 the Reeve, [[being-davrimhssk|Davrim Hessek]], gave the Speaker at the Gate a number for how many more mouths the granaries would carry. Kessa has not said whether her tallies agree with it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kessa is precise, dry and unhurried, and she trusts a tally-stick before any person.

### Motivation

She wants the valleys reckoned truly, because a miscount found in February cannot be mended.

### Strengths

She can carry a season's deliveries in her head, and her figures are believed above the mountain and below it.

### Weaknesses

She will not bend a figure for a friend, and she has few friends because of it.

## Social

### Companions

The tally families of Hudakal.

### Patrons

None. The tally families keep the reckoning by right, and she answers to the reckoning.

### Enemies

None. She likes Davrim Hessek, the Reeve, and does not trust him with a number.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]]**: Kessa is the Tally-Keeper.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The missing sacks.** A delivery of rye from Kalm arrived at Vargate three sacks short of Kessa's tally, and she wants to know where they went before the Gate asks.
2. **The question.** Kessa will send a stranger's question up to the Gate for them, once, if they can say why the hold should hear it.
