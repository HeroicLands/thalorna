# Appearance {#appearance}

The Reeve of Wenlorne is a tall, dry-faced host in a gray coat, carrying keys and a lantern. He bows at the gate, writes a guest’s name in his ledger and speaks in a level, dusty voice.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Reeve of Wenlorne keeps [[place-wenlornestage|Wenlorne Stage]], offering supper, fire and a bed without payment. The pale lamps lead to his gate at dusk.

## Presentation

He bows, takes a guest’s name and asks for news of the road in a level, dusty voice. He returns every conversation to the safety of travelers and the duties of his office.

:::secret {title="The oath’s host"}
The dead Reeve believes the Great War is in its last night. The Shadow holds him to his oath that no guest leaves while the road is unsafe. His apparent solicitude is exact: he keeps the guests under his roof indefinitely.
:::

## In the Land

The stage stands on the old road south from [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]] through [[place-thornwold|The Thornwold]]. The crown’s marks remain on its lamps and strongbox. Porters carry belongings and set out supper.

## Key Behaviors

The Reeve records arrivals and news of the road, then counts his guests. The board in the court bears their names. He is attentive to anyone who claims authority over the road.

## What the Foresters Do

Dunmere’s foresters count nine missing patrols and stop sending more. The last steadings’ carters speak of a free bed at the lit house. [[lore-vardain|The Vardain]] want the old ledger’s names recovered.

## Signs

- Pale lamps burning steadily without heat.
- A swept court and open stable doors in an otherwise abandoned stretch of road.
- More porters at the doorways than a visitor remembers counting.

## Combat Strategy

:::secret {title="Keeping the guests"}
The Reeve fights to keep guests inside. He brings the porters toward an escape route and counts the party before using the cord of his office. Breaking the lamps and board weakens him until dawn. Sounding the road-horn draws the dead into the court.
:::

## Attack Methods

:::secret {title="The porters"}
The Reeve fights to keep guests inside. His porters move toward an escape route and crowd the doorways, and he lays his dry, cold touch and the cord of his office on whoever tries to leave; use [[being-damut|Damut]] for the porters and for the Reeve’s own body, with the abilities below. His confrontation follows [[scenario-lampswenlorne|Pale Lamps on the Crown Road]].
:::

## Special Abilities

:::secret {title="The names and the oath"}

### The guest’s keeping

Eating accepts the hospitality. The board takes the guest’s name; a struck name marks the guest for the Reeve, and by the third night that person follows the porters. His touch is dry cold. While a guest remains in the court and a name stands on the board, he cannot be struck down.

### Ending the oath

The first reeve’s bones and seal ring lie beneath the court. Carrying the bones beyond the road and burying them ends the oath. The horn’s closing call and the board offer ways to confront its terms. Destroying the Reeve with the board standing leaves the trap. A Vardain girl among the porters remembers the first reeve and can give the burial clue.
:::

## Attributes

The host is tall and dry-faced, wearing a gray coat and carrying keys and a lantern. His formality does not vary with a guest’s alarm.
