# Appearance {#appearance}

Nerise Kavalren is a 39-year-old woman of 5'5" and medium build, with fair, freckled skin, auburn hair pinned up and green eyes. She wears the Synod's grey with the sleeves rolled, and she is usually followed by a student carrying her books.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 39. Master of Kosthemoi of the Dúnavarre Synod. Wants every child of talent on the island under the Synod's instruction.**

Nerise Kavalren governs the Kosthemoi of the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Dúnavarre Synod]], the students admitted to its instruction, who are taught in the chapterhouse of [[affiliation-panepistmm|the Academy]] beside the hall at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]]. She sets their lessons, keeps their discipline and decides which of them go on to the Galethoi.

Her other work is finding them. The five chapterhouses on the island send her word of every child with a talent for the art, and she decides which families are offered a place at Ravenmoor. She corresponds with the master of every chapterhouse, [[being-leofwldchlwl|Leofwald Chilwell]] at [[place-veldareth|Veldareth]] among them.

## Psyche

### Personality

Nerise is brisk, warm with her students and impatient with their parents. She remembers every child she has placed.

### Motivation

She wants the island's talented children taught well and taught by the Synod.

### Strengths

Her students love her, and the chapterhouses' masters answer her letters first.

### Weaknesses

A family that refuses her offer is reported to the Sekeros, and she knows what follows for some of them.
