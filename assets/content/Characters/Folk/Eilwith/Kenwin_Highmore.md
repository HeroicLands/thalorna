# Appearance {#appearance}

Kenwin Highmore is a 58-year-old man of medium build who stands 5'8" tall, with pale freckled skin, auburn hair gone thin and gray eyes. He wears the lord captain's plain dark coat on the quay, with a tally-slate at his belt, and he stands facing the bar while he talks, so that a visitor speaks to his shoulder.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kenwin Highmore is the lord captain and commander of [[place-greywater|Greiwater]]'s harbor guard. He has grown suspicious of the lost ships, suspects smuggling or darker activities, and is beginning to believe that [[being-audryhrdng|Audri Harding]] may be involved.

Every hull bound for the mainland calls at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]] to be inspected, and Kenwin's guards see each one out over the bar and in again. A ship that clears Greiwater and does not reach Ravenmoor is therefore his loss before it is anyone's claim, and he keeps its date, its master and its weather in his slate before the owners ask.

## Psyche

Kenwin trusts what he can count: hulls out, hulls in, pilots paid. He is slow to name a suspect and slower to withdraw the name. He holds the town's resentment of the inspection at Ravenmoor and keeps his guards from voicing it where the Synod's officers can hear.

## Social

The pilot families guard their knowledge of the bar, and Kenwin works through them rather than over them. He reads Audri's claims by vessel and date for what they show of the lost hulls, and he watches whom she meets at the quay.

## Plot Hooks

The slate and the ledger
: Kenwin asks the party to compare his record of departures with the claims the shipmasters have filed, and to say which hulls appear in one and not the other.

The suspect's quay
: Kenwin wants a stranger to watch Audri Harding's dealings for a week, because his own guards are known on the quay. The party learns what she does there before it decides what to report.
