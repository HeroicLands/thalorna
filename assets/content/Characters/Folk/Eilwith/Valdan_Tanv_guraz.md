# Appearance {#appearance}

Valdan Tanv-guráz (_VAHL-dahn tahnv-goo-RAHZ_) is a Khazár man of 5'1" and heavy build, with pale, rough skin, grey hair and a grey beard worn short and square, and grey eyes. An old scar runs along the line of his jaw. At the Gate he wears a plain grey mantle over mail, and he carries no weapon there.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Speaker at the Gate of Vorgald. Wants the valleys fed, the hold fed, and the arrangement between them kept exact.**

Valdan is an elder of the house of Tanv-guráz, the guarding of the gate, and sits for it at the council of [[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]]. The council has sent him to the Gate as its Speaker, to hear what the [[lore-vardain|Vardain]] bring up from the Weighing Yard of [[place-vargate|Vargate]] and to give the hold's answer. The Vardain call him the lord of the hold. Inside the rock no one does, and he does not correct the valleys.

At the autumn tally of 719 he asked the Reeve of the Valleys, [[being-davrimhssk|Davrim Hessek]], how many more mouths the granaries would carry through a snowbound winter, because people still come over [[place-sunderfells|the Sunderfells]] from the plain and the valleys still take them in. The Reeve gave him a number. Valdan has carried it to the council.

## Psyche

### Personality

Valdan is courteous, slow and exact. He answers a question only after he has heard all of it, and he does not repeat an answer.

### Motivation

He wants the hold and the valleys to come through every winter whole, as his house has kept the gate through every one before.

### Strengths

The council trusts him, the valleys trust him, and he has not been wrong about a harvest in living memory.

### Weaknesses

He knows the valleys only from the Gate, and the Vardain tell the Gate what they think it needs to hear.

## Social

### Companions

The elders of the council, and [[being-rimiktbrslth|Rimik Tabar-suláth]], the Master of Works.

### Patrons

None. The council sends him, and he answers to it.

### Enemies

The [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]], whom his house's guards kill wherever they are found.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]]**: Valdan is an elder of the council and its Speaker at the Gate.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The letter.** Valdan has a letter of the Dúnavarre Synod on the council's table, unanswered, and wants to know from someone who has met the Synod what it would do with an answer.
2. **The tracks.** Valdan wants to know how the Grukar band that reached the outer workings above Dorrin crossed the valleys unseen.

:::secret {title="The number"}
The council has already agreed to open the hold's refuge caches to the valleys if a winter fails, and the caches hold more than any Vardain knows. Valdan did not need the Reeve's number. He asked for it to learn whether the Reeve would give him a true one.
:::
