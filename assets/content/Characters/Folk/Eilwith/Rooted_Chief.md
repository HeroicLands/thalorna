# Appearance {#appearance}

The Rooted Chief rises at the oldest oak, an antlered shape of gray wood and bone. Its great body follows the split in the trunk, and its voice comes from beneath the bark.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Rooted Chief is the ancestor figure at the oldest oak of [[place-nethermootholw|Nethermoot Hollow]]. Clan tokens and the burial ground associate that tree with the first chief of the nine fords.

## Presentation

A question addressed to the ancestor receives a slow answer through the wood. Its antlered shape follows the split in the trunk.

:::secret {title="The voice beneath the Shadow"}
The Shadow wears the first chief’s remains and speaks through the black seam in the oak. The chief’s own will can still answer beneath it. Addressing him as a clansman gives the party a way to hear that difference.
:::

## In the Land

The oak stands at the hollow’s lowest point among the clan’s burial trees. [[being-nanwithkrake|Brodet]] tends the hollow, while [[being-haldrekvane|Gorlen]] speaks for the living clans. The founder belongs to the ground’s oldest accounts.

## Key Behaviors

:::secret {title="The binding’s command"}
The figure calls the root-sworn against intruders and against Brodet as she tries to hold the seam shut. The dead want release from the binding. Killing Brodet leaves that binding intact.
:::

## What the Clans Do

Gorlen’s warriors skirt the dell’s north side and leave the witch alone. The neighboring shamans say Brodet broke their rule concerning the dead. Their funeral words and the old clan tokens survive among the living.

## Signs

- Faces in the inward-leaning oaks.
- Gray figures in old mail and plaid at the dell’s rim.
- A black seam in the oldest trunk, smoking in cold air.

## Combat Strategy

:::secret {title="At the oldest oak"}
The Shadow calls the root-sworn to defend the seam. Cutting the oak wounds the great body, but does not free the dead. The chief beneath the voice can be addressed while Brodet struggles at the roots.
:::

## Attack Methods

:::secret {title="The rooted body"}
The Chief fights from the oldest oak. Its great antlered body reaches along the split in the trunk at anyone who comes near the seam, while the root-sworn gray figures from the dell’s rim press in against intruders; use [[being-damut|Damut]] for the rooted dead’s close fighting. It does not leave the oak. The choices at the seam are in [[scenario-yearsinoaks|Ten Winters in the Bark]].
:::

## Special Abilities

:::secret {title="The omitted funeral rite"}

### The binding

A clansman’s name and wooden token permit the omitted funeral rite. Freeing the dead closes the seam; cutting the oak without that rite spreads the Shadow into the forest. The released grove’s oaks die within the season.
:::

## Attributes

The figure is huge, antlered and formed of gray wood and bone. Its body rises along the oldest oak’s split.
