# Appearance {#appearance}

Aldmund Hardwik wears the practical kit of an upland captain. His speech is economical, and his instructions name a person and a task rather than the honor of the service.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Watch-Post Captain.**

Aldmund Hardwik commands [[place-sunderwynd|Sunderwiind]], the watch-post supplied from [[place-caerwynd|Kaerwiind]]. He keeps its daybook and accounts for its patrols, stores and tolls.

## Background

His command includes a tower, barracks and cistern. The post’s older pay vault is part of the charge he inherits, and he records the search for its entrance in his daybook.

## Psyche

### Personality

He thinks in practical orders and expects those orders to be repeated back correctly.

### Motivation

He wants his soldiers accounted for and the post’s obligations met.

### Strengths

He knows the fabric of the post and keeps a useful written record.

### Weaknesses

He cannot make a remote post self-sufficient; relief and supplies must still come up the gorge.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

[[being-kenrikbrandley|Kenrik Brandli]] serves him, and [[being-wilriksunder|Sergeant Wilrik]] leads the relief from Kaerwiind.

## Plot Hooks

1. The captain’s signals stop, and an old comrade wants to know why.
2. A search of the post’s old stonework needs someone who can read the earlier mason’s marks.
3. Kaerwiind needs the garrison’s accounts and daybook carried down before snow.

:::secret {title="The adventure’s truth"}
The Hangman takes Hardwik while he writes the daybook. He remains alive in the tower’s roof-room, roped under the arms above his own table. The drip through the ceiling comes from his watering. He knows that the pay-vault door is a dry-set slab beside the mason’s mark. A party reaching him early earns the revelation that the hung are alive.
