# Appearance {#appearance}

The Elder of Vorgald is a Khazár woman of 4'10" and heavy build, with pale, weathered and lined skin, grey hair braided close to the head, a long grey beard bound at the end, and dark brown eyes. Her fingers are stained with ink. At the council of the hold she wears a plain mantle and keeps her house's ledger open before her under a reading lamp, and she does not look up until a question is finished.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**An elder of the council of Vorgald Hold. Wants every judgment of the council grounded in the archive, and every winter's count of mouths grounded in the stores.**

## Background

The Elder sits for one of the houses of [[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]] at the council of its elders, which governs the hold together with no elder first among them. The council judges the hold's quarrels from its archive, apportions its stores and sends its people to their work, and she reads the archive before every judgment and the stores before every apportioning.

She holds no other office. [[being-valdantnvgrz|Valdan Tanv-guráz]] carries the council's answers to the Gate as Speaker, and [[being-rimiktbrslth|Rimik Tabar-suláth]] keeps the valleys' works as Master of Works, and both answer to the council she sits on. This winter the council weighs the number the Reeve of the Valleys gave the Speaker at the autumn tally, and she has asked to see the tallies of [[place-barakth|Hudakal]] before the council answers.

## Psyche

### Personality

Patient and exacting. She speaks last at the council, and rarely.

### Motivation

She wants the hold to decide nothing it cannot find grounds for in the archive or the stores.

### Strengths

She knows the archive as well as any Khazár of the hold, and the council listens when she cites it.

### Weaknesses

She trusts a written tally over a spoken word, and the valleys speak more than they write.

## Social

### Companions

The elders of the council, among them Valdan Tanv-guráz and Rimik Tabar-suláth.

### Patrons

None. Her house sends her to the council, and she answers to it.

### Enemies

The [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]], whom the hold kills wherever it finds them.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]]**: The Elder sits for a house at the council of the hold.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The tallies.** The Elder wants someone outside the hold and the valleys to read the Hudakal tallies against the Reeve's number, without the Reeve knowing who asked.
2. **The nest at Hornhall.** She will not vote to send the hold's guards against the nest at [[place-hornhall|Hornhall]] until someone has counted the Grukar there and found the way they came to the outer workings above [[place-dorrin|Dorrin]].
3. **Word of the plain.** She wants a stranger who has seen the thrall-fields of the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] to tell the council how many more people are likely to come over [[place-sunderfells|the Sunderfells]] before the snow.

:::secret {title="The answer in the archive"}
The Elder has written an answer to the letters of the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Dúnavarre Synod]], unasked by the council, and keeps it in the archive among her house's papers. She means to lay it before the council the winter the hold needs grain it cannot grow, and not before.
