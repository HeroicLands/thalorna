# Appearance {#appearance}

Knilthinda Brildendik is a 62-year-old woman of 5'5" and light build, with pale, wrinkled skin, long white hair worn loose and grey eyes. She sits very still when she recites and walks with a staff carved with the notches of the years she has spoken.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 62. Lawspeaker of Stormveld. Wants the law recited as it is, and the jarls to hear it.**

Knilthinda Brildendik keeps the law of the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] in memory and recites it at the common moot at [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]] each Midsomar, a third of it each year, and declares what the law is when the moot asks her. She crossed from Nordheim as a child of three with the taking, learned the law from the first Lawspeaker of the plain, and has spoken it for twenty-five years.

At the Midsomar moot of 719 the High Jarl, [[being-ulthorvhrnd|Ulthorv Hrindaruk]], asked the moot for payment for steadings a forest tribe burned in answer to the cattle pact of the jarls of Vuldstead and Drasknes. Knilthinda declared that a pact binds only the jarls who swear it, and that the steadings were not on their ground, and the moot awarded nothing.

:::secret {title="The law not recited"}
There is an older law, brought from Nordheim with the taking and not recited on the plain in Knilthinda's time, which makes jarls who swear a pact answer together for its harm wherever it falls. Knilthinda knows it. She did not recite it at Midsomar 719 because the plain had not heard it in her time, and she would not raise a law out of Nordheim to settle one jarl's claim in the High Jarl's favor. In the autumn the High Jarl swore his inlet pact with the jarl of Vuldstead, and the same law would now make the two of them answer for whatever follows from it. She has not decided whether to recite it this year.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Knilthinda is calm, precise and unbending. She does not argue, and she does not need to.

### Motivation

She believes the law is all that keeps the jarls from each other's throats, and that it must be spoken whole.

### Strengths

No one else in the Jarldom holds the whole law in memory, and every jarl fears what she may recite.

### Weaknesses

She is old and has trained no successor, and the jarls know it.

## Social

### Patrons

Knilthinda answers to the moot and to no jarl.

None she would name.
