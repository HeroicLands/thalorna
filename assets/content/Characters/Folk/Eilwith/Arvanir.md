# Appearance {#appearance}

Arvanir is a 29-year-old man of 5'10" and light, wiry build, with light, weathered skin, black hair tied back and green eyes. A burn scar runs down the right side of his neck to the shoulder. He wears greens and browns and goes barefoot in the summer, and he carries a long bow and a knife.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 29. War-Leader of the Seilvri. Wants the Order's road stopped, and the people alive.**

Arvanir is the war-leader of the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]], chosen by the people's fighters and followed for as long as they choose. He leads the bowmen who hold the valley heads of [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]] above the Order's road, and he answers to the Elder, [[being-neilwa|Neilwa]], at the gathering.

He was a boy of thirteen at [[place-hushwood|the Hushwood]] about 703, and he was carried out of it with the burn on his neck ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]). He has fought the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]] since he could draw a bow. His bowmen take the Order's sentries at night and its scouts on the ridges, and the road goes a few miles deeper every summer regardless. He loses fighters he cannot replace.

He holds that the living must come first, and that the people cannot spend their fighters holding the Hushwood for the Elder's rite. He loves Neilwa, and they disagree at every gathering.

## Psyche

### Personality

Arvanir is quiet, quick and fierce, and he laughs more than a man in his place has reason to.

### Motivation

He wants the road stopped and the people alive, and he will trade almost anything but the forest for it.

### Strengths

He knows every valley of Teilvari, his bowmen would die for him, and he has outfought the Order's scouts for ten years.

### Weaknesses

He has a few hundred fighters against an order with a kingdom behind it, and he knows how the war ends.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Iron.** Arvanir needs arrowheads, and the forest-edge smiths who sold them to the people have been hanged. He will pay in furs for anyone who brings iron into Teilvari.
2. **The truce.** Sir Merrad has offered to meet him under truce, and Arvanir wants someone he can trust to see whether it is a trap.
