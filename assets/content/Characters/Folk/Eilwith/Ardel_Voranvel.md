# Appearance {#appearance}

Ardel Voranvel is a 55-year-old man of 5'11" and medium build, with light, lined skin, white hair cropped close and grey eyes. He wears the black of a temple judge of Árdavon with the god's two-faced mask on a chain, and he sits very still on the bench.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 55. Justiciar of Dúnavarre and a temple judge of Árdavon. Wants the law applied evenly, the Synod's law included.**

Ardel Voranvel is the Justiciar of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]], chief of its courts, and a Sentan, a temple judge, of the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]]. He sits in the temple court of Árdavon at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]], which is the realm's high court, and the stewards' courts on the estates answer to him. The law he applies is written by the Synod and may be revised by it at will, and he applies it as written.

This year his court has heard the appeals of tenants of the western estates hanged, or sentenced to hang, for carrying food to the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]]. The law is plain and he has upheld every sentence. He has also written to the session asking whether the law means what it says.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ardel is grave, courteous and slow to speak, and he is never heard to raise his voice.

### Motivation

He wants the law kept, and he wants the law to be just, and he has begun to doubt that the two are the same in the west.

### Strengths

Árdavon's faithful trust his judgment, and the Synod has never had to overrule him.

### Weaknesses

He has upheld every sentence he doubts.
