# Appearance {#appearance}

Elfrun Thornley is a 34-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She has pale, freckled skin, red hair with a white streak above the left temple and green eyes. A faint scar crosses the bridge of her nose. She wears the gray of the court seers and goes bareheaded in any weather.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 34. First Seer of the court. Wants to give a reading on the king's son that neither the king nor the barons can turn into a weapon.**

Elfrun Thornley is the First Seer of the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]]. The court seers read the mists of [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]] from the shore below the castle at [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]], and the king and the council both ask them before a campaign, a marriage or a crowning. The First Seer is the one the court hears first. Elfrun is of a gentry family of [[place-brynwold|Brinwold]], went down to the shore at fourteen, and has held the office for four years.

King [[being-aldrikdrthng|Aldrik Drething]] has asked the council of barons to accept his son as king-to-be, and both he and [[being-godwaldstrl|Godwald Storley]], the Elder of the Council, have asked her what the lake shows of it. She has read the mists three times since the autumn and has told each of them that the reading is not clear. The court's advisor from [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Order]] has asked to stand with her the next time she reads, and she has refused him.

:::secret {title="What the mists showed"}
The reading is clear. Three times the mist showed her the hill behind the hall at [[place-kingsholow|Kingshollow]] standing open, and the boy at its foot with no crown. The tenants' carol says the first king lies in the hill until the crown is asked for again ([[lore-kingswassail|the Wassail at Kingshollow]]). Elfrun does not know whether the reading means the boy will never reign, or that the crown is about to be asked for, and either reading in the wrong hands starts a quarrel she cannot stop.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Elfrun is quiet, watchful and hard to flatter. She answers questions with questions and is uncomfortable at court.

### Motivation

She believes the lake shows true things and that the seers' duty is to the realm, not to the king or the barons. She is afraid of what this reading would do.

### Strengths

The court and the council both need her, and neither can replace her without being seen to want a different answer. She knows the lake's shore and its mists better than anyone living.

### Weaknesses

She is young for the office and has no family of weight. Delay is the only defense she has, and it does not last.

## Social

### Companions

The other seers of the court, who know she has read three times and suspect what she has not said.

### Patrons

[[being-aldrikdrthng|King Aldrik]], whose court she serves.

### Enemies

None declared. The Thordimalon advisor's interest in her readings troubles her.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]]**: Elfrun holds the office of First Seer and is of the Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A second shore.** Elfrun wants to read the mists from another shore, far from Dunmere, and needs an escort to the reed-beds at the lake's northern end, which Aldorath's Brinwold and the free village of Koldmere both claim.
2. **Who is asking.** Someone has paid one of the court's servants to report when the First Seer goes down to the water. Elfrun wants to know who.
