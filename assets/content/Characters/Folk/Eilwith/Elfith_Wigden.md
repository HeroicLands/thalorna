# Appearance {#appearance}

Elfith Wigden is a 37-year-old woman of 5'6" and medium build, with fair, weathered and freckled skin, red hair braided out of the way and blue eyes. Her palms are crossed with old reed-cuts, and she wears a cutter's leather apron over everything.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 37. Reed-reeve of Koldmere. Wants the village's boats back and the reed-beds left to it.**

Elfith Wigden has been the reed-reeve of [[place-koldmere|Koldmere]], a free village on the north-western shore of [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]], for four springs running. She sets the days of the cutting, keeps the village's boats and speaks for it to the Ondarethan senators at [[place-stonyford|Stoniford]], who buy its fish and reed.

The best of the village's reed-beds lie at the lake's northern end, where the hill tenants of [[place-brynwold|Brinwold]] in the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]] claim them too. This winter Brinwold's men took two Koldmere boats and the reed in them. Elfith has asked the Stoniford senators to take the matter to [[place-thornbury|Thornburi]] and put the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]'s weight behind the village, and they have promised to.

## Psyche

### Personality

Elfith is practical, sharp-tongued and fair, and she does not forgive a cut net.

### Motivation

She wants the village fed and its reed-beds its own.

### Strengths

She knows every bay and bed on the north shore, and the village will follow her onto the water in any weather but mist.

### Weaknesses

The senators she has asked for help want something from her in return, and she has nothing to give them.

## Social

### Companions

The reed-cutters and fishers of Koldmere.

### Patrons

Elfith has no patron. The senators of Stoniford promise help and send none.

### Enemies

The Brinwold men who took the village's boats.

### Affiliations

Elfith belongs to no realm. Koldmere is a free village, and its reeve answers only to it.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The boats.** Elfith wants the two Koldmere boats taken by Brinwold's men brought home, without starting a fight on the water.
2. **A light in the mist.** The cutters have seen a light moving on the reed-beds when the mist is down, and Elfith wants to know whether it is Brinwold's men or something the lake has put there.
