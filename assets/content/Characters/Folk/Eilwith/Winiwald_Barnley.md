# Appearance {#appearance}

Winiwald Barnley is a 66-year-old man of 5'7" and light build, with pale, wrinkled skin, thin white hair and grey eyes. His left hand shakes, and he writes with his right and keeps the left in his sleeve.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 66. Theronos of the Republic of Ondareth. Wants Kuthwald Ashden in the chair, and the rolls kept to the letter.**

Winiwald Barnley keeps the rolls of the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]: the citizen roll, the census of property on which every rank depends, and the roll of the [[affiliation-senatondrth|Senate of Ondareth]]. A man's name on the first decides whether he votes, on the second whether he may hold office, and on the third whether he sits. The theronos can strike a senator from the roll for poverty or disgrace, and Winiwald has done it four times.

He held the year as _tavos_ long ago and is _tavalen_ for life. He has backed [[being-kuthwldashdn|Kuthwald Ashden]] since Kuthwald's first seat and means to see him First Senator, and he keeps a list of the senators whose property no longer meets the census.

## Psyche

### Personality

Winiwald is dry, exact and patient. He says little in the chamber and is listened to when he does.

### Motivation

He believes the rolls are the republic, and he wants to leave them in the hands of a man who will keep them.

### Strengths

Every senator knows that Winiwald can strike him, and the rolls are in his own hand.

### Weaknesses

He is old and failing, and the man who follows him as theronos is the First Senator's to choose.

## Social

### Companions

The clerks of the roll, and [[being-osrithgrydn|Osrith Grayden]], the Clerk of the Senate, who copies his rolls into the Senate's record.

### Patrons

Winiwald answers to the Senate and to no man in it.

### Enemies

[[being-harwingrymr|Harwin Greymore]], who wants a second term.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]**: Winiwald is a tavalen and the theronos.
- **[[affiliation-senatondrth|Senate of Ondareth]]**: He sits as a tavalen.
