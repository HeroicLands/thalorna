# Appearance {#appearance}

Hallesitti Eilavanto is a man of 2,420 years who stands 6'7" tall and is of light build. His silver
hair is cut short, his eyes are a pale blue, and his fair skin is smooth. The last two fingers of his
right hand are gone at the second joint. He dresses in plain undyed wool, and he carries a long knife
at his belt and nothing else.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 2,420. Tellamesa, lord of Seirasto. Wants the hearths kept safe inside the wood, and
never sent out of it again.**

Hallesitti Eilavanto is a lord of the [[affiliation-nclvhllwwd|Enclave of Seirasto]], and a
**Tellamesa**: his deeds are many enough that the hearths have made a praise-song of them. Like every
such song it praises the deeds and withholds the doer. He holds nothing by his standing, and he lives
in his mother's hearth of Eilavanto, the hearth of the wide sky.

He was a warrior of [[lore-jointcrown|the Joint Crown]] and fought through the
[Great War]{.name data-type="lore/history"}. He has not stood on an open field since
[[place-threesisters|the Three Sisters]]. He teaches the wardens the bow and leads them against the [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]], with whom
the Senali are at war without end. He sits with the Keeper,
[[being-oltessalasslnt|Oltessala Esselinto]], when the hearths gather, and speaks there seldom and
always for keeping the wood closed.

## Psyche

### Personality

Hallesitti is gentle, dry and very still, and laughs more easily than most of the wood's elders. He
does not talk about the war.

### Motivation

He wants the hearths kept safe inside the wood. He wants no Senalo of Seirasto ever to stand on
an open field again.

### Strengths

He is the finest bowman in the wood and the wardens' teacher. The hearths listen when he speaks,
because he speaks so seldom.

### Weaknesses

He cannot hear the Khazari named without leaving, and every embassy that asks about the crown loses
him from the gathering.

## Social

### Companions

The Keeper, whom he has stood beside since before the crown, and the wardens he has taught, among
them [[being-selkitteilvnt|Selkitte Ilevanto]].

### Patrons

None. The hearths raised him.

### Enemies

The Grukar of Eilwìth.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvhllwwd|Enclave of Seirasto]]**: Hallesitti is a Tellamesa.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The bow.** A party that has proved itself to the wood is offered a lesson with the bow by
   Hallesitti, and the lesson turns out to be a test of whether they will leave the wood when asked.
2. **Iron from the saddle.** A worked piece the herders of the Three Sisters turned up in the turf has
   been sold at [[place-stonyford|Stonyford]] and is on its way to a scholar at
   [[place-thornbury|Thornbury]]. Hallesitti wants it, and will not say why.

:::secret {title="The deed the song keeps"}
The first deed of Hallesitti's praise-song is the withdrawal. When the king fell he brought the living
Senali off the field at the Three Sisters and led them south into the wood, and he did not stop to
tell the Khazari standing beside them. He has kept, all the centuries since, the names of the Khazari
of [[place-vorgald|Vorgald]] who fell on that field near the Senali line, and he has never sent the
list to the hold.
:::
