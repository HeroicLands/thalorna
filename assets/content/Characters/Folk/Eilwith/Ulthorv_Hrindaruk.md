# Appearance {#appearance}

Ulthorv Hrindaruk is a 58-year-old man of 6'2" and heavy build, with fair, weathered skin, white hair and a white beard bound in three iron rings, and pale blue eyes. A notch is cut out of his right ear, and he wears a bear-fur cloak in the hall in every season.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 58. High Jarl of Stormveld. Wants to keep the seat, and to be paid for what the cattle pact cost him.**

Ulthorv Hrindaruk was born on the plain the year after the Nordmen took it, the son of a Nordheim sworn man who was given land at [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]], and he has been jarl of Stormveil and its plain for thirty years. The jarls elected him [[lore-highjarlrnk|High Jarl]] at the Midsomar moot of 709, after his household men held the moot-field against a rival's, and he has held the seat since by settling the jarls' quarrels and leaving their ground alone.

In the spring of 719 a forest tribe answered the cattle pact of the jarls of [[place-vuldstead|Vuldstead]] and [[place-drasknes|Drasknes]] by burning four steadings of Ulthorv's sworn men in the western Nordfells. The moot awarded him nothing, and he paid his men's losses himself. In the autumn he swore a pact of his own with [[being-vrithorvmrvd|Vrithorv Marvidrok]] of Vuldstead, to close the northern inlets to the free coasters, and their ships took a Thornburi coaster within the month. The coasters are Drasknes's trade, and its jarl, [[being-draskmilglrv|Draskmil Glarvendik]], has called for the seat to be put to the jarls at Midsomar.

:::secret {title="The sailing days"}
Ulthorv chose the Thornburi coaster because a factor at Stormveil sold him its sailing day. The factor serves the shipping house of [[being-harwingrymr|Harwin Greimore]], the First Senator of Ondareth, and the coaster belonged to a rival house. Ulthorv has bought two more sailing days for the spring.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Ulthorv is slow-spoken, patient and vindictive. He laughs easily in the hall and remembers every slight.

### Motivation

He wants to die High Jarl, and he wants his son to be the obvious choice after him, whatever the law says.

### Strengths

His household men are the best in the Jarldom, and the jarls who are not quarreling with him have no wish to fight for his seat.

### Weaknesses

He is old, his son commands his household, and every jarl can see what that means.

## Social

### Companions

His household men, his skalds and the jarls of the plain who drink at his board.

### Patrons

Ulthorv answers to no patron. He holds his seat by the jarls' election and his land by his father's.

### Enemies

[[being-draskmilglrv|Draskmil Glarvendik]], jarl of Drasknes.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]]**: Ulthorv is High Jarl, and jarl of Stormveil.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The ransom.** Ulthorv wants the ransom for the Thornburi crew carried from Thornburi to Stormveil, and a party that is neither Ondarethan nor his own man to carry it.
2. **The votes.** Ulthorv wants to know which jarls Draskmil Glarvendik has seen since the winter, and what he offered them.
