# Appearance {#appearance}

Kenwald Highden is a 46-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He has tanned, weathered skin, dark brown hair cropped short and hazel eyes. A long scar runs from his left ear down his jaw. He wears a mail shirt under a plain surcoat whenever he is outside the keep.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 46. Warden of the Wiindmarch. Wants the barons' levies at Kaerwiind on time and at full strength before the passes open.**

Kenwald Highden is the Warden of the Wiindmarch for the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]]. He holds [[place-caerwynd|Kaerwiind]] and the garrisons, levies and beacon chain of [[place-wyndmarch|the Wiindmarch]], watches the two passes over [[place-sunderfells|the Sunderfells]] into the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]], and answers for the forest margin where the [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] and the forest tribes raid the steadings. He is a knight of a small Wiindmarch family and has served on the march all his life.

The march lives on the barons' levies, and the levies come late and short. Each baron owes men for a season, and each sends them when his own harvest is in. He has written to the king twice this year that he cannot hold the passes and the forest both with the men he has.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kenwald is plain-spoken, tired and steady. He says what he needs and does not decorate it.

### Motivation

He wants the march held with as few dead as possible. He has buried enough of his garrison.

### Strengths

He knows every ford, beacon and steading of the march, and his garrison trusts him.

### Weaknesses

He has no voice in the council of barons, and the barons who owe him men owe him nothing else.

## Social

### Companions

The captains of the Kaerwiind garrison and the beacon-keepers of the march.

### Patrons

King [[being-aldrikdrthng|Aldrik Drething]], who appointed him and reads his letters.

### Enemies

The Stormveld raiders who come over the passes, and the smugglers on both sides who keep the passes open for them.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]]**: Kenwald holds the office of Warden of the Wiindmarch and is a Knight.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The smugglers' path.** Stock stolen on the march reaches Stormveld by a path over the Sunderfells that the beacons do not watch. Kenwald wants it found before the snow goes.
2. **The forest margin.** The Grukar of Hartfell have turned on the forest-edge steadings' stock as the deer thin, and Kenwald wants the nest found and counted before he asks the king for men to clear it.
