# Appearance {#appearance}

Thalmil Braldaruk is a 31-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall with a massive build. He has light skin, freckled complexion, red hair, and gray eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 31. Guard.**

Thalmil Braldaruk serves among the household guards at [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]], under [[being-thorrldskr|Ilthann Braldaruk]]'s instruction. He is married with children. A wasting illness makes the steps to the hall difficult, and he rests against his spear when he thinks the watch can spare him.

His household depends on the hall's support. He wants useful paid work that he can manage without a long watch on the height. The harbor sheds need hands and the common moot needs attendants, but an offer for a few days does not feed a family through a season.

## Psyche

Proud and worried, Thalmil minimizes his exhaustion until it becomes impossible to ignore. He wants his children secure and dislikes having their needs discussed as a reason to pity him. He accepts a definite task more readily than a gift.

## Social

Ilthann watches his condition without mocking it. [[being-wilriththorn|Ilthira Rultharuk]] directs the guard detail and can recommend lighter duties. Neither can promise the High Jarl's permanent support on his behalf.

## Plot Hooks

A watch he can keep
: Thalmil asks the party to help him seek seated work checking deliveries at the harbor sheds. The request needs the harbor-reeve's consent as well as his guard captain's release.

The household allowance
: A steward offers food while Thalmil remains on the guard list, but says nothing about his wife and children if he leaves it. Thalmil asks for a witness to a frank conversation before signing away his place.
