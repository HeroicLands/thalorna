# Appearance {#appearance}

Rennim Kollek is a 58-year-old man of 5'7" and heavy build, with light, ruddy skin, grey hair and beard and blue eyes. Flour is worked permanently into the lines of his hands. He wears a miller's smock, dusted white, and a fuller's leather apron over it on cloth days.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 58. Miller of Koldbek. Wants the wheel turning and his own reckoning kept.**

Rennim keeps the mill of [[place-coldbeck|Koldbek]], the mill village of [[place-vardainvalleys|the Vardain Valleys]], on the fast water below the basin's rim. He grinds the grain of every village and fulls their cloth, and every sack of meal that goes up to the Gate passes his stones first. He was born in the valleys the year after the taking of the plain and has never been below the passes.

In the autumn a delivery of rye from [[place-kalm|Kalm]] reached [[place-vargate|Vargate]] three sacks short of the tally of [[place-barakth|Hudakal]], and the Tally-Keeper has asked him how many sacks he ground.

## Psyche

### Personality

Rennim is genial, talkative and shrewd, and he remembers every bargain he ever made.

### Motivation

He wants the mill handed to his grandson in good order, and nobody's suspicion on his family.

### Strengths

He knows the valleys' grain better than anyone, sack by sack.

### Weaknesses

He keeps a reckoning of his own beside the Tally-Keeper's, and it does not always agree.

## Social
