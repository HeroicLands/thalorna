# Appearance {#appearance}

Ithrel Ildavel is a 51-year-old man of 5'8" and medium build, with light, indoor skin, short grey hair and hazel eyes behind spectacles he wears on a ribbon. He carries the Synod's seal on a chain under his robe and touches it when he is thinking.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 51. Chancellor of the Synod and Archivist of the realm. Wants the archive whole.**

Ithrel Ildavel keeps the seal of the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Dúnavarre Synod]] and every writ, charter and license issued under it, and as Archivist of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]] he keeps the Synod's records in the vaults under the hall at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]]. Every act of the Synod passes through his hands, and none of them carries a name.

He is a careful scholar of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Order]] who prefers the archive to the session, and he is the Prime's oldest friend. The Prime has asked him to find the session's record of why the Synod declared the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]] a danger in about 694 ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]).

## Psyche

### Personality

Ithrel is precise, mild and stubborn about small things. He reads every writ he seals.

### Motivation

He wants the archive complete and the seal used rightly.

### Strengths

He knows where every record of the realm is, and every officer needs his seal.

### Weaknesses

He trusts paper, and he is slow to believe that a record can lie.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A copy abroad.** The session's writs of 694 were copied for the Order of the Just Blade, and Ithrel wants the copy in the roll-room at Kelivren, which the Order does not lend.
2. **The seal.** A writ under the Synod's seal has reached the western estates that Ithrel did not seal, and he wants the steward who holds it to show it to him.

:::secret {title="The cut leaf"}
Ithrel has found that a leaf has been cut from the session's register for 694, the year the war was declared. Only the Elder Marendos and his own clerks may open the register, and he has not yet told the Prime what he found.
:::
