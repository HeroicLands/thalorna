# Appearance {#appearance}

Gnarthorv Dvalgidrok is a 53-year-old man of 6'1" and heavy build, with fair, scarred and weathered skin, grey hair and beard and grey eyes, and a broad scar across his throat that leaves his voice rough. He carries a long-hafted axe and leans on it.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 53. Truce-Warden of Stormveld. Wants the Midsomar moot to end without blood on the field.**

Gnarthorv Dvalgidrok holds the peace declared for the common moot of the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] at [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]]. While the moot sits, any man who draws a weapon on the moot-field answers to him, and the law lets him kill to keep the peace. He was elected to the office by the moot, and has held it for eighteen years.

He was on the moot-field in 709, when the High Jarl's household men held it against a rival's, and he did not stop them. This year the jarl of Drasknes means to ask the moot for a new High Jarl, and Gnarthorv has said he will hold the moot's peace against any hall, the High Jarl's included.

## Psyche

### Personality

Gnarthorv is grim, deliberate and hard to provoke. He speaks seldom, because speaking hurts.

### Motivation

He wants to make up for 709 by keeping this moot clean.

### Strengths

Every jarl fears his axe, and the moot trusts his word.

### Weaknesses

He taught the High Jarl's son the axe, and the jarls who oppose the High Jarl doubt his word to hold against him.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Men for the field.** Gnarthorv wants a few hands from outside the Jarldom to stand with him at the edges of the moot-field, men no hall can claim.
2. **The 709 field.** Gnarthorv wants to know which of the jarls who held the moot-field in 709 are still alive and which hall they serve now.
