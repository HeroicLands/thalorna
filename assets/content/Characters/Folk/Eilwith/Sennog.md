# Appearance {#appearance}

Sennog is a 56-year-old man of 5'8" and medium build, with light skin weathered and lined by the sea wind, white hair and a white beard, and blue eyes. Two fingers are missing from his right hand, taken by a line in his youth. He wears a sealskin coat over undyed wool and a knife at his belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 56. War-Leader of the Lonagar. Wants the coasters back at his inlets and salt in his clans' stores before spring.**

Sennog has been war-chief of a Lonagar clan of the easternmost inlet for thirty years, and has made its bargains with the coasters' masters for all of them. In the autumn of 719 the ships of the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]]'s inlet pact took a Thornbury coaster off the [[place-nordfells|Nordfells]] with the Lonagar's winter salt aboard, and before the snow they put men ashore at his inlet and burned his clan's boats on the strand. The shamans named him War-Leader of [[affiliation-lonagar|the Lonagar]] at midwinter.

He has no ships to fight the jarls on the water. He has sent his fighters east along the cliffs to kill the crews of any of the pact's ships that land, and has sent word south to [[affiliation-kelgar|the Kelgar]], who are at war with the same Jarldom.

## Psyche

### Personality

Sennog is shrewd, courteous and slow to anger, a bargainer before he is a fighter, and he has not forgiven the burning of his boats.

### Motivation

He wants the trade back, and his clans fed through the spring.

### Strengths

Every coaster's master who has worked the northern water knows him and trusts his word.

### Weaknesses

He is old for a War-Leader, and the clans of the western inlets, which the jarls' ships have not reached, trade on quietly and do not want his war.

## Social

### Companions

The boat-holders of his clan, and the coasters' masters he has dealt with for thirty years.

### Patrons

None. The shamans named him.

### Enemies

[[being-ulthorvhrnd|Ulthorv Hrindaruk]], the High Jarl, and [[being-vrithorvmrvd|Vrithorv Marvidrok]], the jarls of the inlet pact.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-lonagar|The Lonagar]]**: Sennog is the War-Leader.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A cargo of salt.** Sennog will pay well in hides and fish for salt landed at his inlet before spring, and he knows a Thornbury master who will sail if he has fighters aboard.
2. **The crew at Stormveil.** The crew of the taken coaster are held at Stormveil for ransom, and Sennog wants word carried to them that the Lonagar have not forgotten them.

:::secret {title="The cliff road"}
For twenty years Sennog's clan has passed thralls who run from the Stormplain along the cliffs to its inlet and onto the Ondarethan coasters, a few each year, and the coasters' masters land them in the south. The master of the taken coaster carried two of them. The High Jarl does not know why his pact's ships found the strand at Sennog's inlet worth burning, and Sennog fears that someone aboard the coaster told him.
:::
