# Appearance {#appearance}

Aldgar Wraimon sits low at the oars and watches the channels between strokes. He speaks in short answers, with his attention on the mudbanks and the last stones of the causeway.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Pilot and Fowler.**

Aldgar Wraimon rows the channels of [[place-greylevels|The Greilevels]] and keeps a steading on their lower grazing. He knows the way to [[place-threnhallow|Threnhallow]] and the local tellings about its bell.

## Background

He works the flats as a pilot and fowler. His household depends on the grazing staying dry, and he has little patience with the commission taking credit for work he never sees its men perform.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is blunt about danger and careful with a passenger who cannot read the water.

### Motivation

He wants his son home and his steading safe from the tide.

### Strengths

He knows the channels and can give the local account of the Keeper’s repeated returns.

### Weaknesses

The house frightens him, and concern for his son can take him past the time he would ordinarily turn back.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

His son rows passengers too. Aldgar deals with the other fowlers and the households on the lower grazing.

## Plot Hooks

1. Aldgar asks for companions to look for his son at the mound.
2. He can carry a party to Threnhallow if it agrees when to return.
3. A dispute over the lower grazing needs someone who knows where the water stood in the bad years.

:::secret {title="The adventure’s truth"}
His son is among the drowned at Threnhallow. Aldgar believes the dead once worked the gates but now only hunt; he does not know that they still work every ebb. His grumble about the dry grazing is a clue, not an explanation. He wants his son’s body brought home after the truth is found. See [[scenario-bellsgreylvl|The Bells Beneath the Greilevels]].
:::
