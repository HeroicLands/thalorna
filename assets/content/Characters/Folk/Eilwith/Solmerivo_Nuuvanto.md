# Appearance {#appearance}

Solmerivo Nuuvanto is a man of 928 years who stands 6'2" tall and is of medium build, broad in the
shoulder for a Senalo. His black hair is tied up out of the way, his eyes are grey, and his light skin
is smooth, though his hands are stained green-brown to the wrist from bark and sap. He wears a leather
apron over plain wool, and he smells of cut wood.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 928. Tree-shaper and bowyer. Wants the wood's living work kept as well as it was made.**

Solmerivo Nuuvanto is a tree-shaper of the [[affiliation-nclvhllwwd|Enclave of Seirasto]], of
the hearth of Nuuvanto, the hearth of starlight. He grows the wood's dwellings into living oak and
beech, bends a young tree over years into the shape a hearth needs, and makes the wardens' bows from
yew he has trained to the bow while it stood. The Senali sing what they make, and he sings to the
trees while he works them.

He knows the craft the Senali used for the avenues of [[lore-jointcrown|the Joint Crown]], the trained
oak and beech along the roadbeds and the bridges of grown timber, though he was born long after the
crown was gone. He has walked the edge of the wood where the Crown Roads stop and studied the old
avenues from inside the trees.

## Psyche

### Personality

Solmerivo is cheerful, absorbed and practical, and he talks to trees more than to Senali. He finds
the elders' silences long.

### Motivation

He wants the wood's living work done well, and he wants to see the work of the crown's growers kept.

### Strengths

He is the finest tree-shaper on the island and a fine bowyer. He can tell a tree a Senalo trained
from one that grew by itself at a glance.

### Weaknesses

He is curious about the world outside the wood, which the hearths keep away from.

## Social

## Plot Hooks

1. **The felled avenue.** Someone has begun felling the old avenue along the Crown Road from
   Dunmere for timber, and Solmerivo wants a party to learn who, and how many trees are down.
2. **A bow for a guest.** A party received in the wood is offered a bow of Solmerivo's making, and the
   bow will not draw for anyone who means the wood harm.

:::secret {title="The night work"}
Solmerivo goes out of the wood at night along the Crown Road toward Dunmere, past the place
where Selkitte keeps watch, and tends the old avenue under the trees: binding storm-split limbs,
clearing ivy, singing to oaks no Senalo has sung to since the withdrawal. The hearths do not return
for the crown's avenues, and Selkitte, who lets him pass, has told no one.
:::
