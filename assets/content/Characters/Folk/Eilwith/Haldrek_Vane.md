# Appearance {#appearance}

Haldrek Vane travels with a small company in the mail and plaid of the forest clans. He speaks for the company at the trackstones and expects his companions to wait while he finishes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**War-Chief of the Nine Fords.**

Haldrek Vane speaks for the forest clans of the nine fords around [[place-nethermootholw|Nethermoot Hollow]]. His warriors pass the hollow on its north side and leave [[being-nanwithkrake|Nanwith Krake]] undisturbed. He asks the neighboring steadings to do the same.

## Background

His authority rests on the warriors who follow him and the households whose quarrels he takes up. The shamans still have their own judgment of a chief’s conduct. A chief who loses their support has to answer at the gathering.

## Psyche

### Personality

He gives an order as though the listener already owes him its fulfillment.

### Motivation

He wants his household protected and his following kept together.

### Strengths

He can assemble an armed escort quickly and knows the forest approaches.

### Weaknesses

He needs the shamans’ recognition as well as his warriors’ support.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

His daughter seeks a cure for her mother at the hollow. His warriors respect the chief’s command to leave the cottage alone.

## Plot Hooks

1. Haldrek asks for his daughter to be escorted home from the hollow.
2. A neighboring household wants witnesses when Haldrek names what it owes him.
3. The shamans ask travelers to carry an account of the hollow to their gathering.

:::secret {title="The adventure’s truth"}
Haldrek commissioned the binding of his own clan’s dead to win a feud ten years ago. He knows what Nanwith does and conceals his part when he hires the party. He follows them to the cottage with six warriors. The cloth on her loom names him as the first buyer; shamans who hear that evidence cast him out. He pays to recover the cloth and sends warriors after anyone who keeps it. See [[scenario-yearsinoaks|Ten Winters in the Bark]].
:::
