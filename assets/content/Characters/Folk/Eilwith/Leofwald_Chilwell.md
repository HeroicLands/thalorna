# Appearance {#appearance}

Leofwald Chilwell is a 53-year-old man of 5'8" and light build, with fair skin, thinning grey hair and grey eyes. He wears a scholar's plain gown with the Epistémion's seal at the collar and speaks softly, so that a room quiets to hear him.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 53. Master of the chapterhouse of Veldareth and Speaker of the Table. Wants Veldareth neutral, and believed to be.**

Leofwald Chilwell is the master of the chapterhouse of [[affiliation-panepistmm|the Academy]] at [[place-veldareth|Veldareth]], one of the five on the island, and by the city's charter the Speaker of its Table, the council of its burgesses ([[affiliation-freeveldareth|Free City of Veldareth]]). He presides over the Table and over every meeting of the envoys of Dúnavarre and Ondareth held in the chapterhouse hall.

He is an Eilwìthan scholar of law and treaty, born in [[place-ashford|Ashford]], and he has been Speaker for eleven years. As every chapterhouse master does, he answers to the hierarchy of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Order]], and on Eilwìth that is the Synod at Ravenmoor. Ondareth's envoys have begun to ask whose Speaker that makes him, and he answers that the chapterhouse's scholarship and the Arcane Order's hierarchy are different things.

## Psyche

### Personality

Leofwald is patient, courteous and precise, a listener who sums up a quarrel better than either side can.

### Motivation

He wants Veldareth's table to stay the one place the two realms meet, and he wants it trusted.

### Strengths

Both realms value his judgment, and the chapterhouse's library holds every treaty between them.

### Weaknesses

He answers to the Synod's hierarchy, and he cannot prove to Ondareth that it does not matter.

## Social

### Companions

The scholars and students of the chapterhouse, and the burgesses of the Table.

### Patrons

The Epistémion, and through it the Arcane Order's hierarchy at Ravenmoor.

### Enemies

None he would name. Ondareth's envoys want him off the Table.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-freeveldareth|Free City of Veldareth]]**: Leofwald is Speaker of the Table.
- **[[affiliation-panepistmm|Epistémion]]**: He is master of its chapterhouse at Veldareth.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A Seilvri envoy.** A Seilvri envoy has asked the Table to hear the people's case, and Leofwald wants her kept safe in Veldareth until the Table decides whether it will.
2. **The letters.** Ondareth wants copies of Leofwald's letters to Ravenmoor, and Leofwald wants to know who in his household has been offered money for them.
