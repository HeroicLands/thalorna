# Appearance {#appearance}

Dorvan Melthavel is a 43-year-old man of 6'0" and heavy build, with tanned, ruddy skin, thick dark brown hair and brown eyes. He laughs easily and loudly, and he wears good boots under the Synod's grey.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 43. Master of Galethoi of the Dúnavarre Synod. Wants every court on the island to need the advisors he sends.**

Dorvan Melthavel governs the Galethoi of the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Dúnavarre Synod]], the accomplished young mages who are beginning to be trusted with its business. He decides where each is posted: to a district or a garrison of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]], or as an advisor to one of the island's courts.

The second kind of posting is the Synod's leverage over the island. When a court offends the Synod, its advisors take a courteous leave, and Dorvan is the man who finds that no replacement is available. He has done it once, to a baron of Aldorath, and the baron came back.

## Psyche

### Personality

Dorvan is genial, generous and a good host, and he never forgets which court owes him a favor.

### Motivation

He wants the island's courts to want his mages, and to know they can lose them.

### Strengths

He knows every court on the island and what each one needs.

### Weaknesses

He likes being liked, and the courts know it.
