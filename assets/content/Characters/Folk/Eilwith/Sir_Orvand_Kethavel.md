# Appearance {#appearance}

Sir Orvand Kéthavel is a 59-year-old man of 6'1" and heavy build, with fair, weathered skin, white hair and beard cut short and blue eyes. A lance scar crosses his jaw, and he walks with a stiff left knee he does not mention. He wears the sword-badge of the Order on a plain surcoat at court and in the field alike.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 59. Knight-Marshal of the Order of the Just Blade and Captain-General of Dúnavarre. Wants the war in the west finished in his lifetime.**

Sir Orvand Kéthavel is the Knight-Marshal of the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]], chosen by its Knight-Captains for life, and by the Synod's appointment Captain-General of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]]. He keeps the Order's commandery at [[place-kelivren|Kelivren]] and its roll, and each spring he presides over the [[lore-kelivrentrny|Royal Kelivren Tournament of Chivalry]] and names its champion.

He has fought the war on the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]] since its first year, and he commanded the Order at [[place-hushwood|the Hushwood]] about 703 ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]). He holds the war to be Árdavon's work and the Order's duty, and he means to finish it. This year he has asked the Synod for the largest muster since the Hushwood and a final campaign into the heart of [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]], and the Bursar has asked him what it will cost.

## Psyche

### Personality

Orvand is blunt, devout and generous to his knights, and he keeps every oath he has sworn.

### Motivation

He wants the war won, the Order honored and the realm safe, and he does not distinguish between the three.

### Strengths

The Order's knights would follow him anywhere, and the realm honors him as the image of its chivalry.

### Weaknesses

He has never asked why the war began, and he does not like being asked what it costs.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The muster.** Orvand has written to every house that owes the Order a knight, and wants the letters carried and the knights brought to Kelivren before the tournament.
2. **The champion.** Orvand wants this year's champion to ride west after the tournament, and the likeliest champion has said she will not.
