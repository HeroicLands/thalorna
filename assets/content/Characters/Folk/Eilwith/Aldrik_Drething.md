# Appearance {#appearance}

Aldrik Drething is a 43-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of heavy build. He has fair, weathered skin, brown hair going gray at the temples and gray eyes. His nose was broken once and badly set, and the two smallest fingers of his left hand do not close. He wears the plain circlet of Aldorath at court and a hunting coat everywhere else.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 43. King of Aldorath. Wants the council of barons to accept his son as king-to-be while he lives, so that the crown passes without the barons being asked for it.**

Aldrik Drething is the king of the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]], and he reigns from [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]] on the shore of [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]]. He is of the line of [Drethar of Kingshollow]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the first king, whom the eastern barons crowned in the chapel at [[place-kingsholow|Kingshollow]] about 120 AF ([[lore-kingshlwcrwn|the Crowning at Kingshollow]]), and the royal line carries the byname of that descent. He was crowned in the same chapel in 711 AF, after his father was buried in the ground behind the hall.

Every king of Aldorath is crowned once the barons have buried his father and accepted him, and Aldrik means to be the first to settle it sooner. His son is nine. He has asked the council of barons to accept the boy at Kingshollow as king-to-be while his father lives, so that the barons have no empty throne to bargain over at his death. [[being-godwaldstrl|Godwald Storli]], the Elder of the Council, holds that the crown is the barons' to give each time it falls vacant, and most of the council stands with him. The king has kept the last three midwinter feasts at Dunmere instead of Kingshollow, so that his steward and not he stands at the hall door when the tenants sing [[lore-kingswassail|the Wassail]] and its verses about a gift that may be asked for again.

## Psyche

### Personality

Aldrik is blunt, physical and impatient with ceremony, and he has learned to sit through it. He hunts whenever the court allows and thinks best in the saddle. He keeps a grudge a long time and keeps a promise longer.

### Motivation

He wants his son to inherit a kingdom and not a negotiation. His own crowning cost him a summer of promises to the barons, and he is still paying for some of them.

### Strengths

He is a good soldier, and the march and its garrisons trust him. The crown's patronage is his: the household offices, the royal forest and its grants, the places at court. He reads people quickly.

### Weaknesses

He has no lever on the barons that they cannot pull back harder. A muster, a levy or a campaign needs their consent, and each favor he buys for his son costs him another. He dislikes the seers' influence at court and cannot rule without it.

## Social

### Companions

[[being-aldrithwlfl|Aldrith Wolfli]], the Steward of his household, has kept his house since before his crowning and answers for him at Kingshollow when he does not go.

### Patrons

Aldrik answers to no patron. He holds the crown by the barons' consent, and he knows it.

### Enemies

[[being-godwaldstrl|Godwald Storli]] leads the council against the boy's acceptance. Beyond the passes, the Nordmen of the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] raid the Wiindmarch whenever they can.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]]**: Aldrik holds the crown as King, the Sovereign of the realm.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The barons' votes.** The council sits at Dunmere at midsummer. The king wants to know which barons of the Aldorvale would accept the boy for a grant of royal forest, and which have already promised the Elder their voice.
2. **The seer's reading.** [[being-elfrunthrnl|Elfrun Thornli]] has read the lake's mists three times on his son's matter and given no reading. The king wants to know what she saw, and whether anyone in the Elder's household has asked her first.
3. **The march's levy.** [[being-kenwaldhghd|Kenwald Highden]], Warden of the Wiindmarch, reports that the barons' levies come to [[place-caerwynd|Kaerwiind]] late and short. The king wants a party to ride the march and tell him which barons are holding back men, and why.
