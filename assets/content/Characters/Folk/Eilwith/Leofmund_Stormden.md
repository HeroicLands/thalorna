# Appearance {#appearance}

Leofmund Stormden is a 52-year-old man of 5'9" and medium build, with smooth olive skin, black hair oiled back and dark brown eyes. There is an old burn on the back of his left hand, and he wears a ring over the scar.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 52. Tarkelos of the Republic of Ondareth. Wants the treasury's money where he can see it.**

Leofmund Stormden answers for the treasury of the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]: its revenue from the harbors and the land, its disbursements to the legion and the fleet, and the accounts of every magistrate who spends its money. He keeps the treasury's chests in a vault under the Senate house at [[place-thornbury|Thornbury]] and the keys on his belt.

This year the Senate has asked him to pay for a legion's levies on the frontier and for the fleet the shipping houses want sent north against Stormveld. He says the treasury cannot pay for both, and he has paid for neither.

:::secret {title="The Flame's purse"}
Leofmund has served [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] since his first seat, which its shrines paid for. The treasury cannot pay for the fleet and the frontier because a third of what it held at the year's start has gone, through Leofmund's hands, to the candidates the Flame is buying for next year's magistracies. The burn on his hand is the Flame's mark, and the ring covers it.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Leofmund is smooth, attentive and careful never to be the loudest voice in a room.

### Motivation

He wants power without being seen to hold it.

### Strengths

Every magistrate's accounts pass through his hands, and he knows what each of them has spent and on what.

### Weaknesses

He has made enemies of the fleet and the legion in one year, and both have friends in the chamber.

## Social

### Companions

### Enemies

[[being-edstanbrnwk|Edstan Brinwik]] and [[being-godstanhrdl|Godstan Hardli]], whose fleet and legion he will not pay for.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The legion's pay.** Leofmund wants a party to carry a chest of the treasury's silver to Kaermouth, enough to quiet the legion and not enough to pay it.
2. **A clerk who counts.** One of the treasury's clerks has asked to see the year's ledger, and Leofmund wants to know who sent him.
