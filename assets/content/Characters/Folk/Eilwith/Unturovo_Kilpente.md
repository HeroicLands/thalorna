# Appearance {#appearance}

Unturovo Kilpente is a man of 2,608 years who stands 6'5" tall and is of light build. His silver hair
is long and unbound, his eyes are violet, and his pale skin is clear. His eyes do not reflect
firelight. He wears a robe of undyed wool and walks with a staff of pale birch he does not need.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 2,608. Seer, _iltorin_, of Seirasto. Wants the dead of the wood gathered and the dead of
Haulonna loosed.**

Unturovo Kilpente is the seer (_iltorin_) of the [[affiliation-nclvhllwwd|Enclave of Seirasto]],
and he keeps the rite of [[lore-goddreams|the God of Dreams]], whom the Senali call Aulveira. The
rite was once held in common with the Khazari ([[lore-oldcompact|the Old Compact]]), and the two
traditions have diverged so far that neither would recognize the other's. He is of the hearth of
Kilpente, the hearth of the far call.

He opens every lament the hearths sing on the god's name, so that the dead hear it before their own,
and it is he who leaves the closing line of [[lore-lamenthaulonna|the Lament of Haulonna]] unsung.
When a Senalo of the wood is dying, which is seldom, Unturovo sits with them and hears their dreams.

## Psyche

### Personality

Unturovo is gentle, slow and absent, and he often answers a question asked the day before. He laughs
at himself readily.

### Motivation

He wants the dead of the wood gathered as they should be. He wants the dead of Haulonna loosed, and
the line sung over them at last.

### Strengths

He reads dreams truly, and the hearths take his reading as settled. He is the oldest seer of the
Senali on the island.

### Weaknesses

He is far from the waking world for days at a time, and an envoy who needs him needs patience.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A dream of the north.** Unturovo has dreamed of a human party at the pool above Haulonna, and asks
   the Issaltenno to find them.
2. **A dreamer at the edge.** A human child of the Dúnavarre lowlands has begun to dream in Senali, and
   her mother brings her to the eastern edge. Unturovo wants to hear the dreams, and the Synod's envoy
   waiting there wants the child.

:::secret {title="What the seer saw"}
Unturovo was at the Three Sisters, behind the Senali line, and he saw what met the king. He has
dreamed it every year since on the same night. He has told the Keeper what he saw and no one else,
and the Keeper has told him never to dream it aloud.
:::
