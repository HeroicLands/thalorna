# Appearance {#appearance}

Godwald Storli is a 60-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of light build. He has fair, wrinkled skin, white hair and pale blue eyes, and he keeps his long white beard in a single plait. He dresses in the dark wool of the Aldorvale's old houses and carries a staff he does not need.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 60. Baron of the Aldorvale and Elder of the Council. Wants the crown to stay the barons' gift, given anew at every vacancy.**

Godwald Storli holds manors in [[place-aldorvale|the Aldorvale]] of the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]] that his family held before there was a king, and he keeps a house at [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]] for the months the court and the council sit. The barons chose him Elder of the Council, and he speaks for them to the king, calls the council when it must meet without the crown, and keeps the barons' record: the account in which the crown is the barons' gift to [Drethar of Kingshollow]{.name data-type="being/npc"} ([[lore-kingshlwcrwn|the Crowning at Kingshollow]]).

He reads that record plainly. A king is crowned when the barons have buried his father and accepted him, and not before. King [[being-aldrikdrthng|Aldrik Drething]] has asked the council to accept his nine-year-old son as king-to-be in his own lifetime, and Godwald holds the council against it. He says that the barons who accept a boy now have given away the one thing that makes them barons and not tenants.

:::secret {title="The regency"}
Godwald has sounded two barons of the Aldorvale about a regency council. If the king dies before his son is grown and the boy is not accepted, the council must govern until it crowns someone, and the Elder of the Council would lead it. He has told neither baron that this is the reason he holds the council against the boy, and he has written nothing down.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Godwald is courteous, patient and dry. He never raises his voice and never forgets a precedent, and he quotes [[lore-reevesthrees|the Reeve's Threes]] at the king more often than the king enjoys.

### Motivation

He believes the barons' consent is what keeps Aldorath's crown honest. He also enjoys being the man the king must ask.

### Strengths

He knows the council: every baron's debts, marriages and grievances, and which of them can be moved by what. His family's age gives him a standing no grant from the crown can match.

### Weaknesses

He is old, and the barons of the Aldorvale are not all of his mind. A king who wins two or three of them with royal forest or a marriage can break his majority.

## Social

### Companions

The older barons of the Aldorvale, who sat in the council under the king's father.

### Patrons

Godwald answers to no patron. He holds his land of the crown and his office of the barons.

### Enemies

[[being-aldrikdrthng|King Aldrik]], whose wish he is blocking.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]]**: Godwald is a baron and the Elder of the Council.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The record.** The barons' record of the crowning is kept at Godwald's house at Dunmere, and he wants a copy made and carried safely to a baron of [[place-brynwold|Brinwold]] who has asked to read it before midsummer.
2. **A baron's price.** Godwald suspects the king has offered a young baron of the Aldorvale royal forest for his voice, and wants proof before the council sits.
