# Appearance {#appearance}

Skraldrinna Nulthumak is a 29-year-old woman of 5'7" and light build, with fair, clear skin, black hair cut short as a boy's and green eyes. She wears the rings her verses have earned, seven of them, on a chain around her neck.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 29. Skald of Drasknes. Wants her verses sung in every hall on the plain by Midsomar.**

Skraldrinna Nulthumak is the skald of the hall at [[place-drasknes|Drasknes]]. She keeps its memory, makes the praise of its jarl, [[being-draskmilglrv|Draskmil Glarvendik]], and makes its scorn of his enemies. Her clan is a clan of skalds, and she learned the craft from her mother, who was skald at Stormveil.

This spring she is making scorn-verses against the High Jarl, [[being-ulthorvhrnd|Ulthorv Hrindaruk]], who closed the northern inlets to Drasknes's trade, and she is carrying them over the fells to every hall that will hear them before Midsomar. In the Jarldom a skald's scorn is a weapon, and Ulthorv's household men have been told to stop her singing.

## Psyche

### Personality

Skraldrinna is bold, sharp-tongued and delighted by her own work.

### Motivation

She wants to be the skald whose verse brought down a High Jarl.

### Strengths

Her verse is the best in the Jarldom, and the halls want to hear it whatever it says.

### Weaknesses

She cannot resist a dangerous line, and she has made enemies in halls she has never visited.

## Social

### Companions

The hall of Drasknes, and the skalds of the plain, who learn her verses whether they wish to or not.

### Patrons

### Enemies

1. **The road over the fells.** Skraldrinna wants an escort over the fells to the halls of the eastern plain, where the High Jarl's men are waiting for her.
