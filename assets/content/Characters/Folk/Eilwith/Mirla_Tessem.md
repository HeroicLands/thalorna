# Appearance {#appearance}

Mirla Tessem is a 69-year-old woman of 5'2" and light build, stooped from fifty years of digging, with pale, deeply wrinkled skin, white hair under a dark hood and grey eyes. She wears undyed grey and carries a spade as other old women carry a staff.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 69. Keeper of the Dead at Threndale. Wants every one of the last free generation buried by someone who remembers Vardanreach.**

Mirla keeps [[place-threndale|Threndale]], the burial valley where the dead of all [[place-vardainvalleys|the Vardain Valleys]] are brought, and she buries them. She was ten when the Nordmen took the plain in 660, and she came up the passes with her grandmother. She is one of the last living Vardain born free in [[place-vardanreach|Vardanreach]], and the graves of that generation are still being added to, a few each winter.

She keeps the valleys' rites for the dead, which are older than the valleys' worship of the [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aureldian Pantheon]], and she keeps the names of the villages on the plain that the conquerors renamed.

## Psyche

### Personality

Mirla is gentle, stubborn and unafraid, and she speaks of the plain as if she left it last year.

### Motivation

She wants the old names kept alive after her generation is gone.

### Strengths

She remembers Vardanreach, and every family in the valleys has brought her its dead.

### Weaknesses

She is old, and no one in the valleys has asked to learn the names from her.

## Social

### Companions

The families of the valleys, and [[being-davrimhssk|Davrim Hessek]], the Reeve, whose mother she knew on the plain.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

None living.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]]**: Mirla is the Keeper of the Dead.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The old names.** Mirla wants someone to write down the names of the plain's villages from her before she dies, and she will tell them anything they ask in return.
2. **A grave on the plain.** Mirla's grandmother's sister is buried at a steading of the Stormplain, and Mirla wants the stone that marks her grave brought up to Threndale.
