# Appearance {#appearance}

Ralthann Snarvidrok is a 41-year-old man of 5'8" and light build, with tanned, weathered skin, brown hair braided back and brown eyes. He is lean from riding, and wears the war-arrow's empty quiver at his hip as the sign of his office.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 41. Host-Caller of Stormveld. Wants never to carry the arrow against another Stormvelder.**

Ralthann Snarvidrok bears the war-arrow of the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]]. When the High Jarl calls a common muster, Ralthann carries the arrow from hall to hall across the plain and the fells, and every jarl who receives it owes his men to the host. He has carried it twice, both times to hold the passes of [[place-sunderfells|the Sunderfells]] against Aldorath, and rides the circuit of the halls each year so that he knows the roads.

The arrow is kept at [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]], in his charge. The High Jarl, [[being-ulthorvhrnd|Ulthorv Hrindaruk]], has not called a muster since the cattle pact's trouble began, and the jarls of the quarrel are asking Ralthann whom he would carry the arrow for, if it came to it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ralthann is cheerful, discreet and tireless, and he knows everybody.

### Motivation

He wants the Jarldom to stay one host, whoever leads it.

### Strengths

He knows every road and every hall in the Jarldom, and every hall knows him.

### Weaknesses

The arrow is the High Jarl's to send, and whoever holds the seat holds Ralthann.

## Social

### Companions

The riders of the circuit, and the hall-keepers of the plain and the fells.

### Patrons

### Enemies

None he admits.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A false arrow.** A copy of the war-arrow has been seen at a hall in the Nordfells, and Ralthann wants to know who made it and whom it is meant to summon.
2. **The circuit.** Ralthann wants company on the spring circuit of the halls, because the roads past Vuldstead are no longer safe.
