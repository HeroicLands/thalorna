# Appearance {#appearance}

Vadra Senek is a 34-year-old woman of 5'8" and light build, with fair, weathered skin, black hair cropped short and grey eyes. The tip of her right ear is gone to frostbite. She wears a grey hooded coat and carries a horn, a short bow and a long knife.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 34. Watch-Warden of Dorrin. Wants to know how the Grukar reached the outer workings without her watch seeing them.**

Vadra keeps the watch at [[place-dorrin|Dorrin]], the highest village of [[place-vardainvalleys|the Vardain Valleys]], over the eastern approaches and the sea cliffs. Her watchers are the valleys' eyes, and when anything comes, they are the first to know and the ones who carry word to the Gate.

In the autumn her people found the tracks of a band of [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] crossing the high ground above Dorrin, a day before the band reached the outer workings of [[place-vorgald|Vorgald]] and was killed there. A herder of [[place-highfold|Highfold]], [[being-telvamdrk|Telva Midrek]], had found the same tracks on the high grazing a week before. Neither of them knows where the band came from, and Vadra is certain it did not come past her watch.

## Psyche

### Personality

Vadra is watchful, terse and tireless, and takes every failure of the watch as her own.

### Motivation

She wants the way the Grukar came found and closed.

### Strengths

She knows the eastern approaches better than anyone in the valleys and can hold a watch-post alone through a storm.

### Weaknesses

She is too proud to admit the watch missed something, and too honest to pretend it did not.

## Social

### Companions

The watchers of Dorrin, and Telva Midrek of Highfold.

### Patrons

[[being-valdantnvgrz|Valdan Tanv-guráz]], the Speaker at the Gate, to whom the watch carries its word.

### Enemies

The Grukar.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]]**: Vadra is the Watch-Warden.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The way in.** Vadra wants strangers who can climb to search the cliffs and old workings above Dorrin for the way the Grukar came.
2. **The sea cliffs.** Vadra's watchers have seen a ship standing off the sea cliffs twice since the autumn, and she wants to know whose it is.
