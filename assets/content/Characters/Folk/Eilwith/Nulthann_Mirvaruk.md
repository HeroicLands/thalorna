# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 29. Sworn Man.**

Nulthann Mirvaruk is a sworn man of the High Jarl's hall at [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]]. He advises on watches and household discipline and presses for a hard answer to every slight. He wants to be heard beside [[being-althmilhrnd|Althmil Hrindaruk]], who commands the household, without remaining merely another voice at the board.

He calls [[being-thorrldskr|Ilthann Braldaruk]] soft for restraining troublemakers instead of injuring them. The coming common moot makes that argument consequential: the Truce-Warden claims the field's peace, while Nulthann insists that the High Jarl's men must look strong on the approach to it.

## Psyche

Ambitious and proud, Nulthann treats deference as evidence of competence. He can organize a watch but takes a measured objection as a challenge to his standing. He wants a larger command and advertises his readiness for it at every gathering.

## Social

He holds his place through sworn service, not a hereditary office. [[being-wilriththorn|Ilthira Rultharuk]] answers his demands for harsher discipline with the guard's actual duties. Their disagreement is public in the hall.

## Plot Hooks

The visible watch
: Nulthann wants extra armed men stationed along the moot's approach. He hires messengers to seek permission from the Truce-Warden, expecting an agreement they may find much narrower than his orders suggest.

An account before the hall
: Nulthann challenges Ilthann's handling of a quarrel among the captive crew. He calls for witnesses to speak before the household, where precision about injuries matters more than a stirring accusation.
