# Appearance {#appearance}

Keeper Leofwald stands in the crypt water, a grave man in the black of a Threshold Keeper with one hand on a bell-rope. His voice is gentle and his manner patient.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Keeper Leofwald is the Threshold Keeper named in the registers of [[place-threnhallow|Threnhallow]]. He speaks of the house and its obligations with grave courtesy.

## Presentation

He asks visitors to consider what the house requires. Its old plans and accounts connect his office with both the rites of the drowned and the sluice below the mound.

:::secret {title="Behind the presentation"}
Leofwald is a [[being-tereb|Tereb]] on his thirteenth and last life. He raises drowned workers when the house cannot pay for living sluice labor; his novices later raise him with his altered rite. Close to, his face does not quite hold. He knows that another destruction is final.
:::

## In the Land

The house receives the drowned of [[place-greylevels|The Greilevels]] for the rites of [[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]]. The lower grazing and the commission of sewers have claims upon its drainage work. The endowment records remain on the mound.

## Key Behaviors

He speaks patiently about plans, registers and the house’s duties. He holds the bell-rope in the crypt water and expects visitors to account for what they ask him to abandon.

:::secret {title="Behind the behavior"}
His drowned wear out. He rings the bell for living replacements, who are drowned in the crypt, raised and entered in the guests’ book. The workers turn the sluice at every ebb. He refuses to abandon that work, but can bargain over how it continues.
:::

## What the Levels Do

The Levels’ households tell of his repeated returns and leave the mound alone. The commission disputes the endowment and claims its newer banks keep the grazing dry. The faith has sent no Threshold Keeper to the house in a generation.

## Signs

- A bell heard beneath the tide.
- Repeated signatures in the guests’ book, each hand less steady.
- Rust on the sluice-wheel’s rim and bright iron on its handles.

## Combat Strategy

:::secret {title="In a fight"}
Leofwald keeps the congregation and deep water between himself and a threat, gives ground and offers talk. While talking he does not ring. Knowledge of his remaining life presses him toward a bargain for living workers or the sea’s own drowned instead of further takings.
:::

## Attack Methods

:::secret {title="The attacks"}
Leofwald lets the drowned congregation fight for him, with the water and the bell’s ring behind them, and keeps it between himself and a threat; use [[being-damut|Damut]] for the congregation. When it reaches him he fights as a [[being-tereb|Tereb]], gently and without haste. His use of the bell and the water follows [[scenario-bellsgreylvl|The Bells Beneath the Greilevels]].
:::

## Special Abilities

:::secret {title="The abilities"}

### The altered rite

The bell drives the drowned; silencing it leaves them idle. The true rite lays a damut down and, spoken over Leofwald by name, unmakes him. His thirteenth destruction is final by any means. Twelve bodies and repeated signatures record the preceding returns.

### The gates

Without the Keeper’s direction the gates stop unless living workers take over. The endowment chest can pay those workers. Destroying him ends the takings and leaves the drainage obligation in living hands.
:::

## Attributes

He stands in the water in the black of a Threshold Keeper, gentle-voiced and patient. His courtesy persists even under a direct threat.
