# Appearance {#appearance}

Godrun Winli is a 55-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is heavily built, with light, ruddy skin, dark brown hair and blue eyes. She wears the high priestess's vestments with a heavy chain of office, and she receives visitors seated, her hands flat on the arms of the chair, as though the temple's weight rested there.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Godrun Winli is the high priestess of the temple of the [[affiliation-olvenia|Faith of Ólvenia]] in [[place-ashford|Ashford]]. She tolerates [[being-aldthchltn|Dunith Chilton]] because Dunith's salons generate substantial coin and her piety, however self-interested, brings prestige to the order.

She governs the temple as a house with a ledger: its stipends, its repairs, its standing with the barons who pass through the town on the way to [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]]. A bargain Ólvenia's faith witnesses carries weight in Ashford, and Godrun guards that weight more carefully than any single bargain.

## Psyche

Godrun is shrewd, unhurried and unsentimental about money. She prefers an arrangement that holds to a gesture that impresses, and she counts a donation by what it obliges the temple to do.

## Social

She leaves the suppers to Dunith and the daily worship to [[being-edwalddunmor|Edwald Dunmore]], and she reads both for what they bring in and what they cost the temple's name. The barons' stewards and the merchant families' factors seek her out before a season of lending.

## Plot Hooks

The witnessed bond
: A baron's steward and a merchant family's factor ask Godrun to witness a loan before the council of barons sits. She asks the party to verify the baron's holdings and the family's terms before the temple's name goes on the page.

The cost of a supper
: Godrun asks a discreet guest to learn whether the suppers bind the temple to any baron or lender, since she will not learn it from Dunith and will not ask Edwald.
