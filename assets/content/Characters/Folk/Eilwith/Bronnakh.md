# Appearance {#appearance}

Bronnakh is a 35-year-old man of 6'0" and heavy build, with fair, weathered and freckled skin, red hair worn long and braided at the temples, and grey eyes. His clan's cattle-mark is burned into the back of his left hand. He wears a checked wool mantle pinned with bronze, and carries a long knife and a short spear.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 35. War-Leader of the Kelgar. Wants his clan's thirty people back from the thrall-halls, and the jarls of the plain to stop counting the Stormwood as theirs to raid.**

Bronnakh is war-chief of the Kelgar clan whose glen lay nearest [[place-stormplain|the Stormplain]], and War-Leader of [[affiliation-kelgar|the Kelgar]], named by the shamans at the midsummer gathering of 718. That summer the men of [[place-vuldstead|Vuldstead]] and [[place-drasknes|Drasknes]] raided his clan's pastures under their jarls' pact, took most of its herd and thirty of its people, and left its glen empty.

In the spring of 719 he led the Kelgar's answer across the forest into the western [[place-nordfells|Nordfells]] and burned four steadings held by the High Jarl's sworn men. The moot of the [[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]] awarded the High Jarl nothing for them. Bronnakh has said he will take the war to Vuldstead next, and he has also sent a shaman north to learn what a Nordman hall will pay for its own people.

## Psyche

### Personality

Bronnakh is loud, generous and quick to laugh at a gathering, and silent on a raid. He holds a grudge as carefully as he holds a herd.

### Motivation

He wants his thirty people home and his clan's glen grazed again.

### Strengths

He knows the Stormwood and the Nordfells' western slopes as well as any man living, and the clans follow him because the burning of 719 went well.

### Weaknesses

His clan is poor now, the clans driven west are fighting [[affiliation-bradhar|the Bradhar]] for grazing, and the shamans who named him can name someone else.

## Social

### Companions

The fighters of his clan, and the shaman who keeps its rites.

### Patrons

None. The shamans named him, and the clans follow him while the war goes well.

### Enemies

[[being-vrithorvmrvd|Vrithorv Marvidrok]] and [[being-draskmilglrv|Draskmil Glarvendik]], the two jarls of the cattle pact. [[being-anthorvrlthr|Anthorv Rultharuk]], whose district holds the forest edge against him. [[being-taskain|Taskain]] of the Bradhar, whose clans kill Kelgar herdsmen on the high pastures.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kelgar|The Kelgar]]**: Bronnakh is the War-Leader.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The ransom.** Bronnakh wants someone who can walk into Vuldstead and Drasknes as a trader to learn which halls hold his thirty people and what each hall would take for them.
2. **The pastures.** Bronnakh needs the Bradhar off his back before he strikes Vuldstead, and he will pay in cattle for a go-between who can carry word to the Bradhar's War-Leader without either tribe losing face.

:::secret {title="The man from Drasknes"}
The Kelgar did not choose the Nordfells by chance. In the winter of 718 a thrall who had run from Drasknes came to Bronnakh's fire with word, from a man of the jarl's hall, of which steadings in the western Nordfells were held by the High Jarl's sworn men and how few watched them. Bronnakh does not know who sent it, or why a hall of the cattle pact would point the Kelgar at the High Jarl. He has kept the thrall at his fire.
:::
