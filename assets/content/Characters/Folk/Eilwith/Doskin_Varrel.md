# Appearance {#appearance}

Doskin Varrel is a 43-year-old man of 5'10" and medium build, with tanned, weathered skin, brown hair and a short beard and brown eyes. A thrall's brand from [[place-vuldstead|Vuldstead]] marks his right shoulder. He wears a farmer's smock and works his terraces barefoot from spring to harvest.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 43. Farmer of Kalm. Wants his sister out of Vuldstead.**

Doskin was born a thrall on [[place-stormplain|the Stormplain]], in the fields of a Vuldstead steading, and in 701 he ran south over [[place-sunderfells|the Sunderfells]] to [[place-vardainvalleys|the valleys]], where he was taken in. He farms rye and barley on the terraces of [[place-kalm|Kalm]], the grain village, which swelled most when the refugees came up. His younger sister is still a thrall at Vuldstead.

In the autumn the frost broke the conduit above his fields and brought down two terraces below them. The Khazari engineers mended the conduit within the week and Stonihaugh's masons are rebuilding the terraces, and he has seen the engineers working in the old channel above, where no Vardain has seen them work.

## Psyche

### Personality

Doskin is quiet, hard-working and grateful to the valleys, and he does not speak of the plain.

### Motivation

He wants his sister brought over the passes.

### Strengths

He knows the Stormplain's roads, its steadings and the thralls' ways of moving at night.

### Weaknesses

The valleys cannot take his kin's part against Stormveld, and he will not ask them to.

## Social

### Companions

The farmers of Kalm, and the other runaways who came up from the plain.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

The jarl of Vuldstead, [[being-vrithorvmrvd|Vrithorv Marvidrok]], whose steading held him.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]]**: Doskin lives under the hold's protection.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The sister.** Doskin will tell a party everything he knows of Vuldstead's thrall-halls, and give them all he has, if they bring his sister over the Sunderfells.
2. **The channel.** Doskin wants to know what the engineers found above his fields, and whether his terraces are safe.
