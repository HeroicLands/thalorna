# Appearance {#appearance}

Oltessala Esselinto is a woman of 3,028 years who stands 6'3" tall and is of light build. Her hair is
white and falls loose to her waist, her eyes are grey and very steady, and her pale skin is clear and
unlined. A thin white scar crosses the palm of her left hand. She wears a long gray-green robe and goes
barefoot in the wood in every season.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 3,028. The Lonkunno, Keeper of the Wood. Wants Seirasto kept, and kept to itself.**

Oltessala Esselinto is the **Lonkunno** of the [[affiliation-nclvhllwwd|Enclave of Seirasto]],
the Keeper of the Wood, raised by every voice of the hearths at the heart of the wood. She holds the
[[place-hallowwood|Seirasto]] and nothing beyond it. She decides which embassies the wood receives,
gives the answers they carry away, and drives out the outlaw. Her hearth is Esselinto, the hearth of
the still surface.

She was born in the southern forest before the trust of [[miscgear-secondvoice|the Second Voice]] was
agreed, and she was old when [[lore-jointcrown|the Joint Crown]] was raised. The hearths have made a
praise-song of her deeds, and the song is sung in the wood without her name. What she did in the
crown's six centuries, and at [[place-threesisters|the Three Sisters]], she does not discuss with
anyone outside the wood.

## Psyche

### Personality

Oltessala is quiet, patient and exact. She listens to the end of everything said to her, answers in
few words, and does not repeat an answer once given.

### Motivation

She wants the wood kept as it is: closed, whole and asking nothing of the island. She wants the hearths
to agree, and she waits for them rather than deciding without them.

### Strengths

The hearths trust her completely. She has seen the whole of the island's history with the elder
races, and nothing an envoy can say to her is new.

### Weaknesses

She does not change her mind, and she has no answer to give the Khazari that she is willing to give.
