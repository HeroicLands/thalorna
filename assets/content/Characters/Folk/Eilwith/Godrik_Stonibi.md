# Appearance {#appearance}

Godrik Stonibi appears beyond a dark window, his face close to the glass. His clothes are soaked, his palms raw, and he asks calmly to be let inside before the tide comes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Godrik Stonibi is a traveler recorded in the guest book of [[place-threnhallow|Threnhallow]]. His boots and pack remain in a guest cell, carefully placed beside the bed.

## Presentation

He asks patiently to be admitted before the tide comes. He knows his own name and recognizes his possessions.

:::secret {title="The newly drowned traveler"}
Godrik is a newly raised [[being-damut|Damut]], confused enough to believe himself alive. When he leans toward the light at the window, the water’s damage to his eyes becomes visible. His urgency is that of a worker late for the sluice, which he cannot explain clearly.
:::

## In the Land

The house stands at the edge of [[place-greylevels|The Greilevels]], where the tide covers its causeway. His guest-book entry and belongings give a party the means to identify the figure at the window.

## Key Behaviors

He repeats his request and worries about the approaching tide. If he hears visitors give their names, he uses them.

## What the Fowlers Do

The local fowlers avoid the causeway after dark. They warn that the house’s dead call in the voices of people taken before and that answering brings the next bell for the one who replies.

## Signs

- Boots and a pack left in a guest cell.
- A wet face close to a dark window.
- Raw palms pressed to the sill.

## Combat Strategy

:::secret {title="Water and the turn"}
Godrik joins the drowned congregation wherever the house’s water reaches. He cannot follow onto dry stone. At the turn of the tide he goes with the others to the sluice, leaving the confrontation for the work.
:::

## Attack Methods

:::secret {title="The drowned traveler"}
Godrik does not pursue. He reaches for visitors with raw hands from the window and from wherever the water comes, and he fights as part of the drowned congregation; use [[being-damut|Damut]] for his physical attacks. He cannot follow onto dry stone. [[scenario-bellsgreylvl|The Bells Beneath the Greilevels]] describes the flooded house and his part in its congregation.
:::

## Special Abilities

:::secret {title="The house’s water"}

### Bound to the water

He goes only where the water comes. A body dragged clear falls still. A party answering his call is counted by the Keeper through the altered rite.
:::

## Attributes

His clothing is soaked and his palms worn raw. His voice remains calm and his request sounds reasonable before the light reaches his face.
