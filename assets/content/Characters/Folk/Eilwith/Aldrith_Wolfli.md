# Appearance {#appearance}

Aldrith Wolfli is a 51-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of medium build. She has light, ruddy skin, black hair going gray under a linen cap and brown eyes. The first two fingers of her right hand are stained with ink. She wears the household's badge on a plain gown and keeps a tally-stick in her sleeve.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 51. Steward of the royal household. Wants the household's accounts to come out even this year, and the king back at Kingshollow for midwinter.**

Aldrith Wolfli is the Steward of the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]]'s royal household. She keeps the king's house at [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]] and his old hall at [[place-kingsholow|Kingshollow]], its servants, its stores and its purse, and the crown's revenue passes through her exchequer at Dunmere before the king spends it. She is of a gentry family of the Aldorvale and has kept the king's house since he was a prince of twenty.

For three midwinters the king has kept the feast at Dunmere, and Aldrith has gone down to Kingshollow in his place. She stood at the shut hall door when the tenants sang [[lore-kingswassail|the Wassail]], gave them the tree the forester had marked, and heard the verse they do not sing to the king. She has told him what it says, and she has told him that the barons at Dunmere hear of it within the week.

## Psyche

### Personality

Aldrith is brisk, exact and kind to servants. She counts everything and says what the count is.

### Motivation

She wants the household paid for and the king well served. She also thinks the king is making a mistake at Kingshollow and has said so once.

### Strengths

She knows what everything in the king's house costs, and who in Dunmere is owed and who owes. The king trusts her completely.

### Weaknesses

She has no standing among the barons, who treat her as a servant, and the household's revenue depends on their dues.

## Social

### Companions

The household's clerks and the bailiff at Kingshollow, and [[being-dunrikbrndw|Dunrik Brandwik]], the Forester, who marks the midwinter tree.

### Patrons

King [[being-aldrikdrthng|Aldrik Drething]], whose house she keeps.

### Enemies

None of her own. The Elder of the Council's household treats her with a courtesy she does not trust.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]]**: Aldrith holds the office of Steward and is of the Gentry.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The short purveyance.** The manors that owe provisions to the household have sent short for two seasons. Aldrith wants to know whether the reeves are cheating the household or the barons have told them to.
2. **The hall at Kingshollow.** The old hall's roof needs lead it has not had in a decade, and Aldrith wants a reliable party to escort the lead and the money for it from Dunmere.
