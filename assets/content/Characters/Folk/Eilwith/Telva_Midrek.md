# Appearance {#appearance}

Telva Midrek is a 20-year-old woman of 5'5" and light build, with fair, freckled skin, blonde hair in a single braid and green eyes. A white scar runs through her left eyebrow, from a ram's horn. She wears a herder's hooded cloak and carries a crook and a sling.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 20. Herder of Highfold. Wants the high grazing safe, and to be believed about what she saw on it.**

Telva herds sheep and black cattle on the summer pastures above [[place-highfold|Highfold]], the highest grazing in [[place-vardainvalleys|the Vardain Valleys]], and makes the cheese that goes up to the Gate on the tally's schedule. In the autumn, the week before a band of [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] reached the outer workings above [[place-dorrin|Dorrin]], she found tracks on the high grazing that were not deer and not human, and three of her ewes gone. She told the Watch-Warden, [[being-vadrasnk|Vadra Senek]], who believed her.

## Psyche

### Personality

Telva is sharp, impatient and fearless on the hill, and shy anywhere else.

### Motivation

She wants to know where the tracks came from, and that they will not come again.

### Strengths

She knows every fold of the high pasture and can follow a strayed ewe across bare rock.

### Weaknesses

She is young, and the elders of Highfold think she frightened herself.

## Social

### Companions

The herders of Highfold, and [[being-vadrasnk|Vadra Senek]] of the watch at Dorrin.

None.

None.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The tracks.** Telva will guide a party up to the high grazing in the spring to find where the tracks entered the valleys.
2. **The ewes.** Telva wants her three ewes found, or what is left of them.
