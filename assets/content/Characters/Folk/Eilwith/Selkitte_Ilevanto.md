# Appearance {#appearance}

Selkitte Ilevanto is a woman of 2,168 years who stands 6'0" tall and is of light build. Her blonde
hair is braided tight to the head, her eyes are grey, and her fair skin is smooth, with a long pale
scar down the outside of her right forearm. She dresses in gray and brown that vanish under the
trees, and carries a longbow and a quiver of gray-fletched arrows.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 2,168. Warden of the northern edge. Wants nothing that comes down the north road to reach the
trees.**

Selkitte Ilevanto is a warden (_ortinno_) of the [[affiliation-nclvhllwwd|Enclave of Seirasto]],
and she keeps the northern watch. Her ground is Seirasto's northern edge, from the raiders'
country toward [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]] to the southern end of the
[Crown Road]{.name data-type="place/feature"} that comes south from
[[place-dunmere|Dunmere]] through the Thornwold. She sees a party coming long before it sees her, and turns it,
politely and without conversation. She is of the hearth of Ilevanto, the hearth of the clear place,
which lost [Porkila Ilevanto]{.name data-type="being/npc"} at [[place-haulonna|Haulonna]].

She is also one of the wood's hunters in its war with [[lore-grukaraelwyth|the Grukar]], which the
Senali never stop fighting: the bands that come at the northern edge out of the forest along the road
are hers to meet, and she tracks them back toward their nests.

She watches the lit house of [[place-wenlornestage|Wenlorne Stage]] from the trees at the road's end,
and she turns back the carters who come down the road to see it and walk on to the wood. Two of them
she has sent home this year who would not have come home otherwise.

## Psyche

### Personality

Selkitte is blunt, watchful and short of patience with talk. She is kind to the lost and has no
kindness at all for the curious.

### Motivation

She wants the north road closed to everything that walks it. She wants Wenlorne's lamps put out.

### Strengths

She knows every tree of the northern edge and every Grukar trail beyond it, and she is a fine archer,
taught by [[being-hallesittilvnt|Hallesitti Eilavanto]].

### Weaknesses

She will not set foot on the Crown Road itself past the end of the trees, for any reason, and hunts
the Grukar beside it rather than along it.

## Social

### Companions

The wardens of the northern edge, Hallesitti, who taught her the bow, and the tree-shaper
[[being-solmerivonvnt|Solmerivo Nuuvanto]].

### Patrons

The Keeper of the Wood.

### Enemies

The Grukar of the forest along the road. She thinks the Issaltenno,
[[being-kelkasivotlknt|Kelkasivo Tellakinto]], talks to humans too much, and tells him so.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvhllwwd|Enclave of Seirasto]]**: Selkitte is a Member of the Enclave.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The lamps.** A party that has been inside Wenlorne Stage and come out reaches the trees at the
   road's end, and Selkitte, who has turned back everyone for centuries, comes out to ask what they
   saw.
2. **The ninth patrol.** A forester of the last patrol the king's foresters at [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]] sent down the road
   has come out of the forest at Seirasto's edge, half-starved, and Selkitte wants him taken back
   north by humans before he wanders in.

:::secret {title="The envoy at the gate"}
Selkitte was the envoy. On the last night of the war she came down the Crown Road from the wood to
Wenlorne to ask for the king's body, and the reeve would not open the gate to anyone. She went back
up the road without it. She knows the reeve still keeps the stage, and that he is held to an oath he
swore that night, and she wants him freed and the lamps put out without ever going back through that
gate.
:::
