# Appearance {#appearance}

Gerolt Sarne stands amid the largest kiln’s heat, a great figure in black mail. Pale bone shows between the plates; the outline wavers in the rising air. His voice has the rasp and thrust of bellows.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Gerolt Sarne is the Flame-Warden named in the accounts of [[place-blackkilnpriory|Blackkiln Priory]]. His voice answers from the largest kiln, where the air ripples with heat.

## Presentation

He speaks with a priest’s certainty, answering questions about his charge and demanding a stranger’s authority before accepting an order. His mail bears a stamp of [[affiliation-sacredforge|The Sacred Forge]].

:::secret {title="The Warden in the fire"}
Sarne leads [[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]] cell two generations before the present. The cell’s old accounts identify the officials who paid for the priory under the border office’s contract. He burns captive raiders as offerings, and the Shadow enters the fire with them. The clans throw him into his own kiln. What stands there believes it feeds [[lore-ustarondty|Ústaron]], but the fire is the Shadow’s door.
:::

## In the Land

The ruined chapel and kilns stand among dead pines at the border of [[place-pshtrwldrns|Peshtar Wilderness]]. [[being-tamsenorlik|Tamsen Orlik]] keeps a vigil in the chapel within hearing of the kiln. The old accounts identify the Warden.

## Key Behaviors

He demands that the kiln be tended and returns an unfamiliar request to the forms of his rite. The figures around the pit carry offerings to the fire.

## What the Border Families Do

The farms keep children indoors on autumn nights. The clans that burn the priory warn against disturbing it, and the charcoal-burners speak of a voice like bellows among the kilns.

## Signs

- Pines gray and dead within a bowshot of the works.
- Hot iron’s smell on the charcoal road.
- Air rippling over a fire that gives no smoke or light.

## Combat Strategy

:::secret {title="The kiln confrontation"}
Sarne meets intruders at the pit among the cell’s dead. He believes he conducts a necessary offering. The party can fight him, interrupt the offerings, offer his own forge’s iron or challenge the purpose of the rite. His certainty gives a conversation something to work upon.
:::

## Attack Methods

:::secret {title="The Warden’s body"}
Sarne meets intruders at the pit among the cell’s dead and fights as a priest conducting an offering, striking with the burning touch described below; use [[being-damut|Damut]] for his body and for the dead around him. He answers talk and challenge as readily as blows. The confrontation follows [[scenario-blackfireblkkln|Cinders in the Dead Pines]].
:::

## Special Abilities

:::secret {title="The black fire"}

### Burning touch

Where Sarne strikes, the air ignites.

### The fire’s door

Water from a spring untainted by the Shadow or the rites of [[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]] spoken for him by name can quench the fire. Killing him alone leaves it available to the next flame-priest who touches it. When the fire is quenched the dead fall and the kilns cool.
:::

## Attributes

His great shape is black mail and pale, heated bone. The air around it wavers as over a forge.
