# Appearance {#appearance}

Kelkasivo Tellakinto is a man of 1,358 years who stands 6'4" tall and is of light build. His black
hair is bound back at the neck, his eyes are green, and his light skin is clear, with a faint silver
mark like a frost-fern at the left temple. He wears a gray cloak over a robe of fine dark green, and
he is the only Senalo of the wood an outsider is at all likely to see.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 1,358. Issaltenno, Tevannisa. Wants the wood's guests answered exactly and sent away
satisfied that they were answered.**

Kelkasivo Tellakinto is the **Issaltenno** of the [[affiliation-nclvhllwwd|Enclave of Seirasto]],
the one of the edge, named by the Keeper from among the lords to meet the wood's guests. The hearths
raised him **Tevannisa**, a lord for one deed, which the wood sings and he does not mention. He was
born in the hearth of Tellakinto, the hearth of the small bell, more than three centuries after the
withdrawal, and he is one of the few lords of the wood who never saw the crown.

When an embassy comes to the wood's edge, Kelkasivo decides when it is fetched in, walks it to the
place where it is heard, listens, and gives it the answer of the Keeper,
[[being-oltessalasslnt|Oltessala Esselinto]], in as few words as will carry it. He meets
the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]]'s envoys at the eastern edge and the embassies of
the [[affiliation-kngdmlvndr|Kingdom of Élavendre]] and the [[lore-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] on the southern shore. He
speaks the island's tongue, and he knows the Synod's envoys by name.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kelkasivo is courteous, curious and cool. He likes the envoys more than he lets them see, and he asks
them more questions than they ask him.

### Motivation

He wants the wood's answers given exactly and believed. He wants to understand the human realms well
enough that none of them can surprise the wood.

### Strengths

He knows the island's politics better than any other Senalo of the wood. He is patient enough to keep
an envoy waiting a season.

### Weaknesses

He is young for a lord of the wood and knows it, and he is curious enough to listen longer than the
Keeper would.

## Social

### Companions

The envoys he receives, after a fashion, and the wardens of the edges, who bring him word that a party
is coming.

### Patrons

The Keeper of the Wood, who named him to the edge.

### Enemies

None. [[being-selkitteilvnt|Selkitte Ilevanto]], who keeps the northern watch, thinks he talks to
humans too much and tells him so.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvhllwwd|Enclave of Seirasto]]**: Kelkasivo is the Issaltenno, and a
  Tevannisa.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The waiting envoy.** The Synod's envoy at the eastern edge has waited since midsummer. Kelkasivo
   asks a party camped near her what she has been writing.
2. **A guide for the shore.** An Élavendren embassy is due on the southern shore, and Kelkasivo needs
   the coast road's travelers kept away from the beach on the day it comes.

:::secret {title="The envoy's maps"}
Kelkasivo believes the Synod's envoy at the eastern edge is a scholar of
[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Order]], and that what she writes each night is a map of the edge and
of the places where the paths turn travelers aside. He has not fetched her in because he wants to see
what she maps, and he has not told the Keeper.
:::
