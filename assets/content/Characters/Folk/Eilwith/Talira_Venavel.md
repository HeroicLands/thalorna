# Appearance {#appearance}

Talira Venavel is a 37-year-old woman of 5'9" and medium build, with tanned, weathered skin, chestnut hair tied back and brown eyes. A rope-burn scar circles her left wrist. She wears a sea-coat over the Synod's grey, and she boards every ship she inspects herself.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 37. Warden of the Ports of Dúnavarre. Wants nothing to cross the channel that the Synod has not seen.**

Talira Venavel keeps the harbors of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]], their customs and the compulsory call: every ship crossing between the mainland and [[place-eilwith|Eilwìth]] puts in first at the inspection quay at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]], and her officers search it. Anything magical is recorded, and she decides what is detained. She is not a mage, and the Synod's mages examine what she holds.

She came up through the harbor-masters' offices at [[place-wraymouth|Wraimouth]], and the harbor-master there, [[being-alfrithbrnly|Alfrith Brinlei]], was her clerk. This year the Order of the Just Blade wants outbound ships searched for Seilvri furs and wax as well as relics, and she has not yet given the order.

## Psyche

### Personality

Talira is direct, tireless and fair with sailors, who respect her for climbing their ladders herself.

### Motivation

She wants the call kept without exceptions, and she wants it kept for the reasons the Synod gives.

### Strengths

She knows every captain who crosses the channel and most of their tricks.

### Weaknesses

She is not a mage, and she cannot always tell what she has detained.

## Social

### Companions

Her inspectors at the quay.

### Patrons

The Synod, which appointed her.

### Enemies

Smugglers on both sides of the channel.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]]**: Talira is its Warden of the Ports.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A detained relic.** A mainland captain swears that the worked brooch Talira's officers detained is his mother's, and he wants it back before he sails.
2. **The furs.** The Order wants a Wraimouth ship searched for Seilvri furs, and Talira wants to know who sold the Order the tip.
