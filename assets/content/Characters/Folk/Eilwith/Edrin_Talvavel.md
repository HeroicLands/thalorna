# Appearance {#appearance}

Edrin Talvavel is a 49-year-old man of 5'9" and medium build, with fair skin, brown hair going grey and brown eyes. He wears the robes of a priest of Árdavon with the god's two-faced mask on his breast, and he has a fine, carrying voice.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 49. Chaplain of the Order of the Just Blade. Wants the Seilvri brought to Árdavon's threshold.**

Edrin Talvavel is a priest of the [[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]] and the chaplain of the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]], appointed by the faith at the Knight-Marshal's asking. He keeps the chapel inside the walls of [[place-kelivren|Kelivren]], hears the oaths of the Order's knights and blesses their swords each spring at the [Sanctification of Swords]{.name data-type="lore/custom"}.

He preaches the war on the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]] ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]). The Seilvri, he teaches, live outside every oath, every law and every god's keeping, and the Order carries the light of [[lore-ardavondty|Árdavon]] to them: to spare those who kneel at the god's threshold and to end those who will not. He rides west with the Order each summer and receives the Seilvri who accept its terms into the faith himself.

## Psyche

### Personality

Edrin is eloquent, sincere and gentle in private, and he believes every word he preaches.

### Motivation

He wants the Seilvri brought into Árdavon's law, as many of them alive as will come.

### Strengths

The knights love his sermons, and the Seilvri who have knelt to him speak well of his kindness.

### Weaknesses

He has never heard the Seilvri's own account of their dead, and he does not believe they have a rite worth the name.

## Social

### Companions

The knights he has sworn, and the Seilvri converts he has settled.

### Patrons

[[being-orvandkthvl|Sir Orvand Kéthavel]], the Knight-Marshal, and the faith that ordained him.

### Enemies

The Seilvri elder, [[being-neilwa|Neilwa]], whose rites he calls no rites.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]]**: Edrin is its Chaplain.
- **[[affiliation-ardavon|Faith of Árdavon]]**: He is an ordained priest of the faith.
- **[[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]]**: He is a subject of the realm.
