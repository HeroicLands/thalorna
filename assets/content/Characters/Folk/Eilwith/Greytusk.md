# Appearance {#appearance}

Greytusk is a [[being-grkrhshk|Grukar-Hushk]] of 6'5" and massive build, with olive hide gone leathery and crossed with the scars of every rival it has outlived, black bristles grizzled at the shoulders, and amber eyes. Its left tusk is broken short and has gone gray, which is how the breeders of [[place-stonyford|Stoniford]] know it, and a notch is cut out of its right ear. It carries a herder's long-handled axe taken years ago and a shield of horsehide on a wicker frame. Use the Grukar-Hushk's statistics.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Old Hushk of Kingsdelf. Leads the nest's raids on the horse runs. Wants the foals, and to keep its place near the chamber.**

Greytusk is the oldest Hushk of the [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] nest at [[place-kingsdelf|Kingsdelf]], the Khazari quarry in the north face of the northernmost of [[place-threesisters|the Three Sisters]]. It has outlived every rival of its laying and of two layings since, and it leads the nest's raids on the saddle between the peaks, where the Stoniford breeders run their horses, and on the steadings in the folds below.

The breeders have known it by its broken tusk for years. The fair-court at Stoniford has posted a horse's price for its head, and the breeders add to it with every foal they lose. Nobody has collected. This spring the levy is taking the herders north, the runs lie half watched, and Greytusk's raiders take foals in daylight.

## Psyche

### Personality

Greytusk is patient on a raid and savage in a fight, and it knows more about the Stoniford breeders than any other Grukar of its nest: which runs are watched, which herders carry bows, and which dogs bark.

### Motivation

It wants what the nest wants, food and the runs, and it wants to stay alive and near the chamber.

### Strengths

It has learned from every fight it survived. It does not attack a guarded run, and it does not stay to finish a fight it is losing.

### Weaknesses

It is old, and the younger Hushk of the nest are not.

## Social

### Companions

The raiders of its nest, Uk driven by its boot and a Sha at its elbow.

### Patrons

The Kingsdelf spawner.

### Enemies

The breeders of Stoniford and their herders, the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]'s officers when they come up, and the nest at Ravenkeld, whose raiders it fights where their ranges meet.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The bounty.** The Stoniford fair-court pays a horse's price for Greytusk's head, and the breeders have added to it all winter.
2. **The foals.** A breeder whose best mare's foal was taken off the saddle pays well for its return, and does not believe it has been eaten.

:::secret {title="The laying against it"}
The Kingsdelf spawner has laid a clutch of Hushk, and it is laid as much against Greytusk as for the raids. Greytusk has lived long enough to begin to see what the chamber is worth, and the spawner means it to spend its last strength fighting the young Hushk when they hatch. A party that waits a season may find Greytusk dead in the quarry, or find it come down the hill alone and maddened, with nowhere to go.
:::
