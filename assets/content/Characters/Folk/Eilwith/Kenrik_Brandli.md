# Appearance {#appearance}

Kenrik Brandli holds a crossbow with steady hands. His clothes are filthy, his face hollow with missed sleep, and his eyes keep returning to the beams above a doorway.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Patrolman.**

Kenrik Brandli serves the garrison of [[place-sunderwynd|Sunderwiind]], on the Ironfells approach from [[place-caerwynd|Kaerwiind]]. He knows its rooms, yard, waterworks and the trail that supplies it.

## Background

He is a working patrolman rather than an officer. He takes his post’s stores and messages seriously and serves under [[being-aldmundhardwik|Aldmund Hardwik]].

## Psyche

### Personality

He asks direct questions and holds his ground while he listens to the answers.

### Motivation

He wants his captain and the men of his post brought safely down the mountain.

### Strengths

He knows the post’s paths and recognizes its soldiers’ voices.

### Weaknesses

Exhaustion makes it difficult for him to separate what he has witnessed from the explanation he has given it.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

Captain Hardwik commands him. Kaerwiind sends relief and supplies to the post.

## Plot Hooks

1. Kenrik needs help carrying a message to Kaerwiind.
2. A relief party wants a patrolman who knows the cistern passage.
3. The garrison needs its men brought down before the pass closes.

:::secret {title="The adventure’s truth"}
Kenrik survives three nights in the open yard after the Hangman takes his captain. He honestly mistakes the living captives’ voices for mimicry and has refused to answer the captain. He knows the creature stays overhead, but not why it dips captives. When the captain answers a new question, Kenrik wants to climb to him at once. Keep his error sincere. See [[scenario-hangingdead|The Hanging Dead of the Ironfells]].
:::
