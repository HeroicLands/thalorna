# Appearance {#appearance}

Torvin Asmavren is a 48-year-old man of 5'10" and light build, with olive skin, black hair going grey at the temples and dark brown eyes. The fingers of his right hand are stained with ink. He wears the Synod's grey in the hall and a merchant's brown in the harbor taverns, and he looks at home in both.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 48. Sekeros of the Dúnavarre Synod. Wants proof of the Umbral Court, and the Seilvri elder alive.**

Torvin Asmavren is the Sekeros of the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Dúnavarre Synod]], its investigator, charged with unlicensed magic in the realm and with the conduct of the Synod's own members. He keeps a small office off the hall at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]] and spends his evenings in the harbor taverns.

His great work is [[affiliation-umbralcourt|the Umbral Court]], whose existence the Synod officially denies. He has spent six years building the proof the session would have to accept, and the session has not yet accepted it.

He takes a different interest in the war on the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]]. He has asked the Order of the Just Blade to bring the Seilvri elder, [[being-neilwa|Neilwa]], to Ravenmoor alive, and he has not told the Order why.

## Psyche

### Personality

Torvin is quiet, patient and amused by very little. He remembers everything said to him and writes most of it down.

### Motivation

He wants the Synod to see what he sees, and he wants to learn what the Seilvri keep before the Order burns it.

### Strengths

He is a capable mage, an exact questioner and a man the harbor does not notice.

### Weaknesses

The session's denial of the Court is its policy, and a Sekeros who proves it embarrasses the people who appointed him.

## Social

### Companions

His clerks and a few informers in the harbor quarter.

### Patrons

The Prime, [[being-velisndorrvl|Velisande Orravel]], who keeps him in office.

### Enemies

The Umbral Court, which knows his face.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Dúnavarre Synod]]**: Torvin is its Sekeros.
- **[[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]]**: He is a member of the Synod.
- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|The Order]]**: He is a mage of the Arcane Order.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The elder alive.** Torvin pays for word of where the Seilvri gathering sits, and pays more for anyone who can bring Neilwa out of Teilvari unharmed.
2. **The quiet room.** He has found a room in the harbor quarter that his workings cannot see into, and he wants someone to rent it.

:::secret {title="The wrong knight"}
Torvin knows that a Knight-Captain of the Order of the Just Blade passes word to the Umbral Court. He suspects Dame Torad Taravren, whose silence about the crusade he has noticed, and he is wrong.
:::
