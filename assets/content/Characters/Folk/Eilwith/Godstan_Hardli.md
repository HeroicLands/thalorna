# Appearance {#appearance}

Godstan Hardli is a 49-year-old man of 6'1" and heavy build, with light, weathered skin, greying black hair cropped to the scalp and grey eyes. He walks with a stiff left knee and wears his armor more often than his mantle.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 49. Vandraks of the frontier legion. Wants the burned watch-burgs answered and his legion paid.**

Godstan Hardli commands the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]'s frontier legion at [[place-kaermouth|Kaermouth]], and in Kaermouth his word is the law. He has served on the frontier for thirty years and has commanded for six, and he knows the burned strip north of the river and the forest edge beyond it as well as any man in the republic.

In the autumn the tribes of the forest burned two watch-burgs north of the river and killed their crews. Godstan has answered with patrols into the trees and has asked the Senate for another legion for the spring. The Senate has voted him the levy and not the pay. This spring the year's field command belongs to the _tavos_ [[being-alfmundkrtn|Alfmund Kaerton]], who wants the frontier quiet by midsummer, and Godstan thinks him a horse-dealer playing at war.

:::secret {title="The patrol"}
The watch-burgs were burned three days after a patrol of Godstan's crossed into the forest and drove off the cattle of a clan camped at the edge, which held trading terms with the factors of Kaermouth. Godstan ordered the patrol. He has reported the burnings to the Senate as an unprovoked attack.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Godstan is hard, honest with his own men and with nobody else, and contemptuous of the Senate.

### Motivation

He wants the frontier held and his men fed, and he wants the forest to fear the legion.

### Strengths

His legion is the best-drilled force on the island, and it follows him rather than the Senate.

### Weaknesses

He wants a war the Senate cannot pay for, and he does not trust the tavos set over him to fight it.

## Social

The captains of the legion and the provosts of the camp.
