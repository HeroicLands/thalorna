# Appearance {#appearance}

Harwin Greymore is a 60-year-old man of 5'8" and heavy build, with light, ruddy skin, white hair cut close and grey eyes in a heavy face. He wears the bordered mantle wherever he goes, a gold ring on every finger of his right hand, and walks with a cane he leans on only in the chamber.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 60. First Senator of the Republic of Ondareth. Wants a second term in the chair, and the rock after it.**

Harwin Greymore presides over the [[affiliation-senatondrth|Senate of Ondareth]] at [[place-thornbury|Thornbury]]. He calls the chamber, sets its business and speaks first in every debate. His family's shipping house is one of the oldest on the quays, and he has been a senator for thirty years and _tavalen_ for twenty.

His three years in the chair end with this one, and he wants a second term, which the Senate has never given a First Senator. The houses at the foot of the Tyrant's Rock have been bought in the name of one of his freedmen, and the city takes it for the first step toward building on the rock where [Tralkar]{.name data-type="being/npc"} kept his castle ([[lore-ondarethfall|the Fall of the Last King of Ondareth]]). [[being-kuthwldashdn|Kuthwald Ashden]], who wants the chair, has put the rock before the Senate to make him speak to it.

:::secret {title="The coasters"}
Harwin's shipping house has sold the High Jarl of Stormveld, through a factor at Stormveil, the names and sailing days of the free coasters that run the Nordfells inlets. The Thornbury coaster the jarls took in the autumn belonged to a rival house that backs Kuthwald Ashden. Harwin argues in the Senate against sending the fleet north.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Harwin is genial, patient and unforgiving. He remembers every favor he has done and every one refused him.

### Motivation

He wants to be the first man in the republic and to be seen to be it, and he wants his house to outlast his rivals'.

### Strengths

Half the chamber owes him money or a magistracy, and he knows the price of every man in it.

### Weaknesses

He is old, his vanity is public, and every senator who wants the chair can name the rock against him.

## Social

### Companions

The senators of the shipping houses, and his freedmen, who carry his money in their names.

### Patrons

Harwin answers to no patron. He is the Senate's First Senator and its richest member.

### Enemies

[[being-kuthwldashdn|Kuthwald Ashden]], who wants the chair.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]**: Harwin holds the rank of First Senator.
- **[[affiliation-senatondrth|Senate of Ondareth]]**: He presides over the chamber as its First Senator.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The freedman.** Kuthwald Ashden's people are asking questions about the freedman who bought the houses below the rock, and Harwin wants the freedman out of Thornbury until the debate is over.
2. **A second term.** Harwin wants a party to carry his offers to three senators of the Highlands who have not yet said how they will vote.
