# Appearance {#appearance}

Nanwith Krake works a loom beside a table of stoppered jars, her white hands moving carefully. She looks about sixty. Her voice stays quiet, and she puts down her work before naming a price.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Healer and Witch.**

Nanwith Krake lives at [[place-nethermootholw|Nethermoot Hollow]], where people seek cures, curses and the finding of lost things. She states the price before a bargain and keeps the terms she gives. The neighboring clans say she has looked the same age longer than their elders remember.

## Background

She trained among the shamans of the nine fords. Those shamans say she broke their rule concerning the dead; the war-chief [[being-haldrekvane|Haldrek Vane]] says the hollow should be left alone. Her cottage now holds her jars, loom and black cat.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is exact, courteous and unwilling to haggle once she has stated a price.

### Motivation

She wants her bargains kept and visitors to leave her working room in order.

### Strengths

She can name a remedy and its cost plainly, without hiding behind a riddle.

### Weaknesses

She gives little explanation of her work, which leaves visitors dependent on their own questions.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

The sick come from the border steadings, while the neighboring shamans refuse her company. Haldrek’s warriors leave her jars alone.

## Plot Hooks

1. An apothecary wants a cure brought back from Nanwith’s cottage and has not agreed who pays its price.
2. A family needs an escort along the trackstones to ask for a remedy.
3. A former buyer wants Nanwith to explain the terms of a bargain before another relative accepts one.

:::secret {title="The adventure’s truth"}
Ten years ago Haldrek asked Nanwith to bind the clan’s dead for his feud. She takes years from buyers to sustain the rooted dead, and the Shadow now feeds through the binding. She cannot release them by her own command. The names woven into her cloth record the buyers; Haldrek’s is first. She fears the opening in the oldest oak. Her cat carries her calls to the grove; use [[being-cat|Cat]] for the animal. The price, the Rooted Chief and the ways to free the dead are in [[scenario-yearsinoaks|Ten Winters in the Bark]].
:::
