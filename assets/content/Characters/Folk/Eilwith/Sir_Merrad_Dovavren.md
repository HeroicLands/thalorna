# Appearance {#appearance}

Sir Merrad Dovavren is a 42-year-old man of 5'11" and medium build, with tanned, weathered skin, brown hair cut short for the helm and hazel eyes. The last two fingers of his left hand are gone, taken by a Seilvri arrow. He wears field harness more often than court dress, and the sword-badge of the Order on both.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 42. Knight-Captain of the West in the Order of the Just Blade. Wants Teilvari brought under Árdavon's law.**

Sir Merrad Dovavren commands the war on the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]] for the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]]. Each spring he rides in the tournament at [[place-kelivren|Kelivren]], and when the stands come down he leads the Order's knights and every guest knight who chooses to follow up the valley into [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]]. Each summer his companies cut the road a few miles deeper, burn the clearings it reaches and offer the Order's terms to any Seilvri they find ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]).

He was a young knight at [[place-hushwood|the Hushwood]] about 703, and he has fought in the west every summer since. He believes what the Order's chaplain preaches: that the Seilvri are a people outside every oath and law, and that bringing them to Árdavon's threshold is the god's work. He offers the terms honestly, and he kills those who refuse them.

He is respected across Dúnavarre as a knight who keeps his oath, is gentle with the weak and is first into every fight.

## Psyche

### Personality

Merrad is earnest, brave, plain-spoken and kind to his own people. He prays before every fight.

### Motivation

He wants the war finished, the forest open and the Seilvri who kneel settled in peace under the Order's protection.

### Strengths

He is the best field commander in the Order, and his knights trust him with their lives.

### Weaknesses

He cannot see the war as anything but a duty, and the Seilvri war-leader has learned to read him.
