# Appearance {#appearance}

Halvra Senavel is a 45-year-old woman of 5'7" and medium build, with pale, clear skin, straight black hair cut at the jaw and blue eyes. She keeps a wax tablet at her belt and writes figures on it while other people talk.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 45. Bursar of Dúnavarre. Wants to know what the war in the west costs, and what it buys.**

Halvra Senavel keeps the revenue of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]] and the endowment of the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Dúnavarre Synod]] from the counting-house beside the hall at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]]. Every customs duty from the compulsory call, every rent from the estates and every silver piece the Order of the Just Blade spends passes through her books.

The war on the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]] is the largest line in them ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]). She has asked the Knight-Marshal, [[being-orvandkthvl|Sir Orvand Kéthavel]], how many more summers the road into [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]] needs and what the forest is worth when it is taken, and he has answered that the question is not hers. She has taken it to the session.

## Psyche

### Personality

Halvra is exact, dry and unhurried, and she does not mind being disliked by people who spend money.

### Motivation

She wants the realm's books to balance and the session to know what it is paying for.

### Strengths

She knows the realm's money better than anyone alive, and the Prime listens to her.

### Weaknesses

The Order is the realm's pride, and a Bursar who counts its dead is easy to call disloyal.

## Social

### Companions

Her clerks, and the harbor-masters who send her the customs, [[being-alfrithbrnly|Alfrith Brinli]] of Wreimouth among them.

### Patrons

[[being-velisndorrvl|Velisande Orravel]], the Prime Marendos.

### Enemies

Sir Orvand Kéthavel, who wants the final campaign she is costing.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]]**: Halvra is its Bursar.
- **[[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Dúnavarre Synod]]**: She is a marendos of the Synod.
- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|The Order]]**: She is a mage of the Arcane Order.

## Plot Hooks

1. **An honest count.** Halvra wants a count of the Order's dead and wounded on the road this summer, from someone who is not the Order.
2. **The furs.** Seilvri furs reach the mainland through Wreimouth, and Halvra wants to know what the trade is worth before the Order closes it.

:::secret {title="A letter to Veldareth"}
Halvra has written to Leofwald Chilwell, the Speaker of the Table at Veldareth, asking whether the Table would hear an envoy of the Seilvri. She has not told the session, and she has not told the Prime.
:::
