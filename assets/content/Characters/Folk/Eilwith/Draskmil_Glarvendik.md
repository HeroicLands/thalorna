# Appearance {#appearance}

Draskmil Glarvendik is a 39-year-old man of 5'10" and medium build, with fair, weathered skin, dark blonde hair cut short and grey eyes, and a silver ring through his left eyebrow. He wears a seal-skin coat and talks with his hands.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 39. Jarl of Drasknes. Wants the inlets open and a new High Jarl at Midsomar.**

Draskmil Glarvendik is the jarl of [[place-drasknes|Drasknes]], the one harbor of [[place-nordfells|the Nordfells]], on Stormveld's northern coast. His hall lives on the free coasters who put in there rather than at [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]], from Ondareth, from Nordheim and from the fells' own inlets, and he takes his toll from them and asks them nothing else.

In 718 he swore the cattle pact with [[being-vrithorvmrvd|Vrithorv Marvidrok]] of Vuldstead, and raided a forest tribe beside him. In the autumn of 719 Vrithorv swore a second pact, with the High Jarl, [[being-ulthorvhrnd|Ulthorv Hrindaruk]], to close the northern inlets to the coasters, and no coaster has put in at Drasknes since. Draskmil has called for the High Jarl's seat to be put to the jarls at the Midsomar moot. His charge is that Ulthorv has made the High Jarl's household his son's.

## Psyche

### Personality

Draskmil is quick, charming and reckless, and he enjoys an argument more than he should.

### Motivation

He wants his harbor's trade back, and he wants Ulthorv out of the seat for taking it.

### Strengths

Half the coasters of the north shore owe him favors, and his skald is the best in the Jarldom.

### Weaknesses

His hall is the smallest of the three in the quarrel, and it is three days over the fells from the moot.

## Social

### Companions

His shipmaster, [[being-thraskvrsvlt|Thraskvir Svalthendik]], his skald, [[being-skraldrnnlth|Skraldrinna Nulthumak]], and the coasters' masters who drink in his hall.

### Patrons

Draskmil owes allegiance to the High Jarl, and is trying to choose a new one.

### Enemies

[[being-ulthorvhrnd|Ulthorv Hrindaruk]], the High Jarl, and [[being-vrithorvmrvd|Vrithorv Marvidrok]], who changed sides.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]]**: Draskmil is a jarl, holding Drasknes.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A coaster through.** Draskmil wants one coaster brought into Drasknes past the High Jarl's ships before Midsomar, to show the jarls that the pact cannot hold the north shore.
2. **Votes at the moot.** Draskmil wants the jarls of the eastern plain sounded about a new High Jarl, and he cannot go to them himself without Ulthorv hearing of it.
