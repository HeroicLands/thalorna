# Appearance {#appearance}

Dunmund Adlei is a 31-year-old man of 5'11" and medium build, with fair, freckled skin, auburn hair and green eyes. He dresses above his means and is usually somewhere he is not expected, checking a measure or a drain.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 31. Peltros of the Republic of Ondareth. Wants a good year and a senator's seat at the end of it.**

Dunmund Adlei keeps the city of [[place-thornbury|Thornbury]] for the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]] this year: its markets and their measures, its streets, its water, and the games the Senate gives at the festivals. The office is the usual first step in a public career, and the young men who hold it spend their own money on the games to be remembered at the next election.

Dunmund is of a family of the _gulmenoi_ whose money comes from salt, and [[being-harwingrymr|Harwin Greimore]] paid for his election. He takes the work more seriously than the office requires, which the market-holders find inconvenient.

## Psyche

### Personality

Dunmund is eager, honest so far, and too sure of his own judgment.

### Motivation

He wants a senator's seat and wants to earn it.

### Strengths

He knows the city's markets and its streets better than any peltros in years, and the market-holders know he cannot be bought cheaply.

### Weaknesses

He owes his office to Harwin Greimore, and Harwin expects something in return.

## Social

### Companions

The market-wardens and water-keepers who work under him.

### Patrons

[[being-harwingrymr|Harwin Greimore]], the First Senator.

### Enemies

The market-holders whose false measures he has broken.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]**: Dunmund is of the propertied order and the peltros for the year.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The bad water.** Two of the city's fountains have run foul since the spring, and Dunmund wants to know whether the cause is in the conduit or in someone's cellar.
2. **The games.** Dunmund cannot pay for the midsummer games without a loan, and Harwin Greimore has offered one.
