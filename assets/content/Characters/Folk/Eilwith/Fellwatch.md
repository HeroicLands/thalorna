# Appearance {#appearance}

Fellwatch is a [[being-grkrsh|Grukar-Sha]] of 5'3" and light build, thin and stooped, with rough olive skin, black bristles and amber eyes. Its hands and ears carry the pale scars of frostbite from seasons spent lying out on the crest. It wraps itself in a gray sheepskin and carries a Vardain shepherd's crook. Use the Grukar-Sha's statistics.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Sha of Hornhall. Watches the high grazing of the Vardain valleys. Wants a way into the valleys the Khazari do not guard.**

Fellwatch is a Sha of the [[lore-grukaraelwyth|Grukar]] nest at [[place-hornhall|Hornhall]], the abandoned Khazari hunting hall in the heights of [[place-ironfells|the Ironfells]] north of [[place-vardainvalleys|the Vardain Valleys]]. The herders of [[place-highfold|Highfold]] have seen a thin figure on the crest above their summer pastures twice this winter, sitting still in the snow and watching, and they call it Fellwatch.

In the autumn a band from Hornhall crossed the high grazing above Highfold and [[place-dorrin|Dorrin]], took three of [[being-telvamdrk|Telva Midrek]]'s ewes, and reached the hold's outer workings above Dorrin, where the [[lore-flkkhazar|Khazari]] killed it. Fellwatch did not go with the band. It watched.

## Psyche

### Personality

Fellwatch is cold, patient and precise. It counts the herders, the dogs, the hours of the watch and the Khazari at the workings, and it forgets none of them.

### Motivation

It wants its nest fed from the valleys' flocks. It also wants to come home to the hall alive and useful, and not so useful that the spawner invites it to dinner.

### Strengths

It can lie on the crest unseen for days, and it learns from what it watches rather than from what it survives.

### Weaknesses

It is alone on the crest, and a Sha alone dies in any fight.

## Social

### Companions

None on the crest. In the hall, the Hushk who will lead the next band.

### Patrons

The Hornhall spawner.

### Enemies

[[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]], which kills every Grukar it finds, and the Watch-Warden of Dorrin, [[being-vadrasnk|Vadra Senek]], whose watch it is studying.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The figure on the crest.** The Highfold herders pay in cheese and wool for whatever is watching them from the crest to be driven off or killed.
2. **The tracks.** Vadra Senek wants strangers who can climb to search the crest for the way the band came down.

:::secret {title="What the band taught"}
The band was the spawner's wager, and Fellwatch was sent to watch it spent. From the crest it saw where the hold's guards stand at the outer workings, how quickly they came, and that none of them looked up the ridge. It went home with that, and the spawner is laying for a second band to come down the hunters' road along the crest and go around the workings, into the valleys from the north, where Dorrin does not look.
:::
