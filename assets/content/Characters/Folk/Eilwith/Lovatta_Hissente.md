# Appearance {#appearance}

Lovatta Hìssente is a woman of 617 years who stands 5'11" tall and is of light build. Her dark brown
hair is worn in a single braid, her eyes are green, and her light skin is smooth, with the tips of
both ears whitened by old frost. She travels in a hooded cloak of oiled wool and soft boots, and
carries a small wooden pipe on a cord at her neck.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 617. Singer and wayfarer. Wants word from the north to be the word, once.**

Lovatta Hìssente is a singer of the [[affiliation-nclvhllwwd|Enclave of Seirasto]] and one of
the youngest of its hearths, the hearth of Hìssente, the hearth of the held breath. She is a
wayfarer, which few of the wood's Senali are: she goes north alone, over the water and through the
kingdoms of men, to [[place-haulonna|Haulonna]], the valley of the north the
[[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Kingdom of Nordheim]] calls Thalmdal, to see whether it stands as it stood.

She went up in the spring and came down in the leaf-fall, and she said that it stands. The hearths
sang [[lore-lamenthaulonna|the Lament of Haulonna]] over her word, as they sing it every time word
comes and it is not the word. She sings among the voices that call the five dead.

## Psyche

### Personality

Lovatta is warm, restless and quick, and she talks to humans more easily than any Senalo of the wood
but the Issaltenno. She is tired, this year, in a way she does not show.

### Motivation

She wants to bring down the word that Haulonna's dead are loosed, so that the lament can end on its
line.

### Strengths

She knows the roads of the north and the ports between Eilwìth and the mainland, and she passes among
humans with her hood up as one of them.

### Weaknesses

She goes alone, and a valley held by the Shadow is no place to go alone.

## Social

### Companions

The seer [[being-unturovokilpnt|Unturovo Kilpente]], who opens the lament she brings word for.

### Patrons

None but the hearths.

### Enemies

The servants of the [[affiliation-nahild|Faith of Nahild]] in the north.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvhllwwd|Enclave of Seirasto]]**: Lovatta is a Member of the Enclave.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Company on the road.** Lovatta means to go north again in the spring, and for the first time she
   is looking for company. She is looking among humans, at the ports.
2. **Followed.** Lovatta comes home each time by the port of [[place-veldareth|Veldareth]] and the coast
   road, and this leaf-fall someone followed her from the harbor to the trees. She wants to know who,
   and asks a party in Veldareth to find out.

:::secret {title="What she brought down"}
At the pool above Haulonna Lovatta found a cut stone with runes on it that she did not cut and could
not read, laid at the water's edge since her last visit. Someone else has been to the pool. She has
it in her pack, and she has told no one in the wood.
:::
