# Appearance {#appearance}

Kadrum is a 32-year-old man of 5'10" and heavy build, with tanned, weathered skin marked by old cuts, black hair tied back and brown eyes. Three blue lines are tattooed across his cheekbones, one for each year the spears have sworn to him. He rides a stolen Ondarethan horse and carries a long spear and a round shield of hide.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 32. War-Leader of the Durrask. Wants the count paid down, his brother's killers named, and a fourth year sworn to him.**

Kadrum is the War-Leader of [[affiliation-durrask|the Durrask]], sworn by the spears of the steads at the autumn fire of [[place-thornwold|the Thornwold]] in 717, 718 and 719. Few War-Leaders are sworn to three times, and Kadrum has done it by raiding all three realms the Durrask can reach and bringing most of his spears home.

In the autumn of 719 his raiders burned a mill on Aldorath's shore of [[place-eilissa|Lake Eilìssa]] a morning's ride from [[place-dunmere|Dunmere]] and drove off the plough-oxen of three manors of the [[affiliation-kngdmldrth|Kingdom of Aldorath]]. The knights of the [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]] hanged five of his raiders on a western estate of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]], and he has raided the estates twice since. In the summer his brother rode with the raid that took forty brood mares from the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]'s southern highland grazing, and the herdsmen who followed killed him at a ford.

## Psyche

### Personality

Kadrum is hard, cheerful and quick, generous with plunder and merciless with a spear who runs. He laughs at the count when the Grey Heads recite it, and never forgets a name on it.

### Motivation

He wants his brother's death set against the herdsmen who caused it, and a fourth year sworn to him.

### Strengths

He picks his raids well, and the spears trust him to bring them home.

### Weaknesses

He is fighting three realms at once, and a War-Leader whose year goes badly is not sworn to again.

## Social

### Companions

The spears of his stead, and the war-bands that ride with him.

### Patrons

None. The spears swore to him, and they can decline to swear again.

### Enemies

King [[being-aldrikdrthng|Aldrik Drething]] of Aldorath. The [[affiliation-justbladeordr|Order of the Just Blade]] and its knights, and [[being-merraddovvrn|Sir Merrad Dovavren]] among them. The herdsmen of the southern Highlands who killed his brother.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-durrask|The Durrask]]**: Kadrum is the War-Leader.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The ford.** Kadrum will pay a stranger in horses to learn the names of the herdsmen who killed his brother.
2. **The hanged men.** Kadrum wants the bodies of the five hanged raiders cut down from the Dúnavarran roadside and brought home to the Thornwold for the rites.

:::secret {title="The baron's silver"}
A baron of Aldorath's lower lake shore pays Kadrum in silver to keep his raiders off the baron's own manors and send them against his neighbors', and the mill burned in the autumn stood on a neighbor's land. Kadrum takes the silver and keeps the baron's name from the Grey Heads, since a Durrask who takes a lowlander's pay to spare him may not be sworn to again.
:::
