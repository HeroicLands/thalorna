# Appearance {#appearance}

Rimik Tabar-suláth (_REE-meek tah-bahr-soo-LAHTH_) is a Khazár woman of 4'8" and heavy build, with medium, weathered skin, black hair braided close to the head, a short black beard and dark brown eyes. Her hands are stained grey to the wrist by the lime of the channels. She wears a leather apron over a working coat and carries a sounding hammer at her belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Master of Works of Vorgald Hold. Wants every conduit in the valleys running, and every flaw in them mended before it shows.**

Rimik is an elder of the house of Tabar-suláth, the pouring of the deep water, and sits for it at the council of [[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]]. Her given name lays on her the work of a forebear taken up again, and her house has kept the valleys' water since the hold was cut. As Master of Works she keeps the reservoirs, conduits, terraces, flues and granaries of [[place-vardainvalleys|the Vardain Valleys]], and she sends the engineers down when the [[place-barakth|Hudakal]] tallies report a fault.

In the autumn a conduit above [[place-kalm|Kalm]] failed in the frost, and her engineers mended it within the week. They then recut a stretch of the old channel above it, which no Vardain alive has seen them touch, and Rimik went down to oversee the work herself.

## Psyche

### Personality

Rimik is brisk, blunt and absorbed in her work, and has no patience for a question asked twice.

### Motivation

She wants the works her house holds kept sound, so that no fault in them stands against her house's dead.

### Strengths

She reads a channel by ear and by hand as well as any Khazár of the hold, and her engineers will go anywhere she sends them.

### Weaknesses

She holds every human in the valleys at arm's length and does not notice when it costs her.

## Social

### Companions

The engineers of her house, and [[being-valdantnvgrz|Valdan Tanv-guráz]], the Speaker at the Gate.

### Patrons

None. The council of the hold sent her to her work.

### Enemies

None in the valleys. The frost, the damp and the slow failing of old stone.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]]**: Rimik is an elder of the council and its Master of Works.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The recut channel.** The Vardain of Kalm want to know what the engineers found in the old channel above their fields, and Rimik will not tell them.
2. **A watcher.** Rimik has seen a lowlander on the slopes above the conduits, sketching, and wants the Reeve to learn who he is.

:::secret {title="The old channel"}
The stretch of channel above Kalm is not Khazari work. It was cut under the Joint Crown, by Senali and Khazari together, and the Senali part of it is failing. Rimik's house is mending the work of the people Vorgald has never forgiven, and the council has told her to keep that inside the rock.
:::
