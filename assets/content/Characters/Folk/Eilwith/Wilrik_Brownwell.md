# Appearance {#appearance}

Wilrik Brownwell is a 38-year-old man of 5'9" and light build, with tanned, weathered skin, black hair and brown eyes, and two fingers gone from his left hand from a net-winch. He has the loudest voice on the assembly ground and dresses like the fishermen he grew up among.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 38. Lusos of the Republic of Ondareth. Wants the levy stopped and the citizens heard.**

Wilrik Brownwell is the _lusos_ of the [[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]], elected by the citizens to speak for them and to interpose against a magistrate's act on their behalf. He is not of the propertied order. He was a fisherman's son on the Thornbury quays and a net-maker until he lost two fingers, and the citizens elected him because he says in the assembly what they say on the quays.

This year he has made the frontier levy his cause. The Senate has voted another legion's worth of levies for [[place-kaermouth|Kaermouth]] and no pay to feed them, and Wilrik speaks against the levy at every assembly. The citizens of the coast cheer him, and the senators call him a demagogue.

## Psyche

### Personality

Wilrik is loud, funny and quick, and angrier than he lets the crowd see.

### Motivation

He wants the citizens' levies paid and fed, and the senators who vote them to serve in them.

### Strengths

The citizens of the coast trust him, and the Senate cannot easily remove a lusos in his year.

### Weaknesses

He has no money and no house behind him, and every senator who wants a voice in the assembly is trying to buy him.

## Social

### Companions

The fishermen and net-makers of the quays, and the citizens who come to the assembly ground to hear him.

### Patrons

Wilrik has no patron and has refused three.

### Enemies

[[being-godstanhrdl|Godstan Hardli]], whose levy he opposes, and [[being-mildrikhshwl|Mildrik Hushwell]], who dismisses the citizens' suits.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-repblcndrth|Republic of Ondareth]]**: Wilrik is a citizen and the lusos for the year.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A witness.** Wilrik has a witness who saw the vornos's clerk take money for a ruling, and he wants her kept safe until the assembly hears her.
2. **The levy's dead.** Wilrik wants the names of every levy man who has died at Kaermouth this year, to read them out in the assembly, and the legion will not give them.
