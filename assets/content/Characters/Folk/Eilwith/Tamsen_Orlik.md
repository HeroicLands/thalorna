# Appearance {#appearance}

Tamsen Orlik sits beside a cold brazier, gaunt in his gray robe. His sack holds salt, his bell hangs within reach, and his record lies open across his knees. He speaks courteously and takes time to rise.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Lay Brother.**

Tamsen Orlik keeps the vigil of [[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]] at [[place-blackkilnpriory|Blackkiln Priory]]. He records the fire’s reach, watches the dead pines and receives the few travelers who enter the chapel.

## Background

The faith sent him eleven years ago to watch the fire. The vigil has become his household: stool, brazier, salt, bell and the book in which he records another night. He knows the chapel and keeps the crypt key.

## Psyche

### Personality

He listens before answering and offers a place beside the brazier even when he has no food to share.

### Motivation

He wants the vigil relieved and the dead given rest.

### Strengths

His record preserves the names and dates of the vigil.

### Weaknesses

He is tired and reluctant to ask another person to take his place.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

He serves the Faith of Sélmoros and spends his days within hearing of [[being-geroltsarne|Gerolt Sarne]].

## Plot Hooks

1. Tamsen needs salt and a sound bell brought along the charcoal road.
2. A traveler can copy the vigil’s record for the faith if Tamsen can be persuaded to part with it long enough.
3. He wants an escort out of the priory when a relief finally comes.

:::secret {title="The adventure’s truth"}
His record also identifies the officials who paid for the cell. Tamsen feeds the black fire small offerings of his own blood to quiet the dead. It has begun to answer him. He conceals this from visitors and his faith. Patient questioning or the marks of his offerings can expose it; he then asks to be taken out. He has the crypt key and knows that the fire answers to Sarne’s name. If abandoned, he is taken by the fire in the coming autumn. See [[scenario-blackfireblkkln|Cinders in the Dead Pines]].
:::
