# Appearance {#appearance}

Vrithorv Marvidrok is a 44-year-old man of 5'11" and medium build, with fair, freckled skin, red hair and a forked red beard, and green eyes. An arrow-scar runs through his right forearm, and he wears the cattle-tally of his last raid on a cord at his belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 44. Jarl of Vuldstead. Wants the forest edge pushed back, and to be on the winning side of every pact.**

Vrithorv Marvidrok is the jarl of [[place-vuldstead|Vuldstead]], the hall-town of the western [[place-stormplain|Stormplain]] where the grain land runs up to the [[place-pshtrwldrns|Peshtar forest]]. His grandfather was given the western plain at the taking, and the jarls of Vuldstead have pushed the fields west into the forest ever since.

In the spring of 718 he and [[being-draskmilglrv|Draskmil Glarvendik]] of Drasknes swore a pact against a tribe of the forest west of the plain. That summer their men raided the tribe's pastures together and divided five hundred cattle and thirty captives. The tribe's answer fell the next spring on the High Jarl's steadings in the Nordfells, not on Vuldstead's. In the autumn of 719 Vrithorv swore a second pact, with the High Jarl, [[being-ulthorvhrnd|Ulthorv Hrindaruk]], to close the northern inlets to the free coasters, which turned Draskmil against them both.

## Psyche

### Personality

Vrithorv is genial, greedy and unashamed of either. He keeps a pact exactly as long as it pays.

### Motivation

He wants land, cattle and thralls, and he wants to be the jarl the others must deal with.

### Strengths

His men are the Jarldom's best at the forest edge, and his granaries are the fullest on the plain.

### Weaknesses

Every jarl has seen him change sides once, and none of them trusts him.

## Social

### Companions

His hall's sworn men, and the men of the forest-edge district under their hersvald.

### Patrons

Vrithorv owes allegiance to the High Jarl, and nothing more.

### Enemies

[[being-draskmilglrv|Draskmil Glarvendik]], his partner of the first pact.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]]**: Vrithorv is a jarl, holding Vuldstead.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The captives.** One of the thirty captives at Vuldstead is said to be the child of a war-leader of the forest, and Vrithorv wants to know whether that is true and what the child is worth.
2. **The next answer.** Vrithorv wants the forest watched for the tribe's next raid, and he will pay a party that can tell him where it will fall.
