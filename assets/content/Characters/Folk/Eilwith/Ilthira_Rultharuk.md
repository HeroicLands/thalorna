# Appearance {#appearance}

Ilthira Rultharuk is a 44-year-old woman who stands 5'3" tall with a heavy build. She has light skin, weathered complexion, auburn hair, and blue eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 44. Guard Captain.**

Ilthira Rultharuk leads a guard detail at the High Jarl's hall in [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]], under the household commander, [[being-althmilhrnd|Althmil Hrindaruk]]. She posts the watches, hears the ordinary disputes and knows which men need a second pair of eyes beside them.

She supports lighter work for [[being-thorrldskr|Ilthann Braldaruk]] and [[being-alfwinstormw|Thalmil Braldaruk]], whose strength cannot sustain a full watch. That practical choice puts her at odds with [[being-dunwaldraven|Nulthann Mirvaruk]], who wants discipline made visible through punishment. The moot approaches, and she wants guards capable of keeping an instruction when a rival hall shouts at them.

## Psyche

Direct and unsentimental, Ilthira measures loyalty by work done under strain. She wants her detail steady and fed. She dislikes appealing to Althmil over a quarrel she believes she ought to settle herself.

## Social

Her authority covers the detail, not the whole household and not the common moot. She consults the Truce-Warden, [[being-gnarthrvdvlg|Gnarthorv Dvalgidrok]], about where the hall's watches meet his peace.

## Plot Hooks

Where the peace begins
: Ilthira asks for witnesses to walk the boundary between the hall's guard posts and the moot-field with the Truce-Warden's men. She wants the same instruction heard at both ends before rival retinues arrive.

A guard's provision
: She asks the party to accompany Ilthann when he seeks lighter duty from the household. Her recommendation states his useful skills; she needs the steward to state what support goes with the new work.
