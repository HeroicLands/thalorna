# Appearance {#appearance}

Anthorv Rultharuk is a 41-year-old man of 5'9" and light build, with tanned, leathery skin, brown hair cut short and brown eyes, and a front tooth missing. He wears hunter's leathers and carries a bow more often than an axe.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 41. Hersvald of the forest edge. Wants his district's men home from the watch, and the next raid to be the last.**

Anthorv Rultharuk leads the men of the forest-edge district under [[place-vuldstead|Vuldstead]] to the muster and speaks for them at the jarl's moot, by their continued consent. His clan has kept the steadings nearest the [[place-pshtrwldrns|Peshtar forest]] since the taking, and he knows the forest edge as a hunter knows it.

His men did most of the fighting in the cattle raid of 718, and since the tribe's answer burned the High Jarl's steadings in the Nordfells, they have stood to arms on the forest edge through every season. He has asked the jarl, [[being-vrithorvmrvd|Vrithorv Marvidrok]], how long the district must keep the watch for cattle the hall has already eaten.

## Psyche

### Personality

Anthorv is quiet, wary and dryly funny. He listens more than he speaks.

### Motivation

He wants his district's steadings safe and its men at their own work.

### Strengths

He knows the forest edge, and his men would choose him again tomorrow.

### Weaknesses

The district that raised him can set him aside, and the jarl can make that happen.

## Social

### Companions

The men of his district and their households.

### Patrons

[[being-vrithorvmrvd|Vrithorv Marvidrok]], jarl of Vuldstead.

### Enemies

The forest tribe his men raided, which has not yet struck at his district.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Jarldom of Stormveld]]**: Anthorv is a hersvald under the jarl of Vuldstead.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The tracks.** Anthorv's watchers have found tracks along the forest edge that do not belong to any of the tribe's war-bands, and he wants to know whose they are.
2. **A peace in cattle.** Anthorv wants word carried into the forest that his district would return its share of the raided cattle for an end to the watch, without his jarl knowing he asked.
