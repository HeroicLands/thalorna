# Appearance {#appearance}

Brenik Saldem is a 29-year-old man of 5'11" and heavy build, with fair, ruddy skin, dark blonde hair cut short for the forge and brown eyes. Small burn scars cover both forearms. He wears a scorched leather apron over his shirt in all weathers.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 29. Smith of Vellik. Wants to make one piece the Khazari would sign.**

Brenik is a smith of [[place-vellick|Vellik]], the smiths' village of [[place-vardainvalleys|the Vardain Valleys]], whose people are the only humans anywhere taught anything of Khazari technique and are forbidden to teach it onward. He makes the tools, wire and fittings that go down the mountain from [[place-vargate|Vargate]] as Vardain work, and some of them are.

In the autumn a smith from [[place-ashford|Ashford]] came up with the caravan, watched Brenik's forge for a day from the road, and offered him a house, a forge and a fortune to come down and work in the lowlands.

## Psyche

### Personality

Brenik is cheerful, proud of his hands and restless.

### Motivation

He wants to be the best smith on the island, and to be known for it.

### Strengths

His work is good enough that lowland smiths take it for Khazari.

### Weaknesses

He wants the lowlands to know his name, and the valleys' trade depends on their not knowing it.

## Social

### Companions

The smiths of Vellik, and [[being-kadlisvrrm|Kadlis Vorrem]] of Stonihaugh, who cuts the stone for his forge.

### Patrons

[[being-davrimhssk|Davrim Hessek]], the Reeve, who sells his work at Vargate.

### Enemies

None.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-vrgldhld|Vorgald Hold]]**: Brenik lives under the hold's protection.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The offer.** Brenik wants a stranger to go down to Ashford and learn whether the smith's offer of a house and a forge is real.
2. **The signed piece.** Brenik has made a blade he thinks is as good as anything from the Gate, and he wants someone to carry it to a lowland market under his own name.
