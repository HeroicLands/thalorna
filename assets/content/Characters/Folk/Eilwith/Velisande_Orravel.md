# Appearance {#appearance}

Velisande Orravel is a 57-year-old woman of 5'6" and light build, with fair, smooth skin, silver hair worn in a single braid and grey eyes. A thin white scar runs through her left eyebrow. She dresses in the plain dark grey of the Synod, without ornament, and the seal-ring of the session is the only ring she wears.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 57. Prime Marendos of the Dúnavarre Synod. Wants the session to decide the war, and to know why it began.**

Velisande Orravel presides over the closed session of the [[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Dúnavarre Synod]] at [[place-ravenmoor|Ravenmoor]], where the Elder Marendos govern the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]]. The Elder Marendos chose her from among themselves, and the Prime speaks last in every debate. No writ of the Synod carries her name, and every officer in Ravenmoor knows whose decision it is.

She came to the session as a marendos of [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|the Order]] who kept the realm's harbors well, and she was raised to the Prime's chair for a quality the session values above any other: she does not lose her temper. She has held it through the embassy to Tantsou, which she carried against half the session, and through the Order's demand for a final campaign into [[place-teilvari|Teilvari]], which the session has not yet decided.

The war on the [[affiliation-saelvri|Seilvri]] was ordered before she sat in the session ([[lore-saelvricrusade|The Seilvri Crusade]]). The Elder Marendos who argued for it in about 694 are dead, and the session's record of their reasons is not among the papers the archive has given her. She has asked the Chancellor to find it. She has never spoken against the war, and the Bursar's question of how many more summers it needs is now hers to answer.

## Psyche

### Personality

Velisande is cool, courteous and patient, and she listens to every Elder Marendos to the end. She keeps her own view until she has to give it.

### Motivation

She wants the Synod to govern well and be seen to govern well, and she wants the war in the west decided by the session rather than by the Order's momentum.

### Strengths

The session trusts her judgment, the realm's officers carry out her writs without asking whose they are, and she has the patience to wait out any faction in the room.

### Weaknesses

She governs a war she did not begin and cannot explain, and the Knight-Marshal has more friends in the realm than she has.

## Social

### Companions

The Chancellor, [[being-ithrelildvl|Ithrel Ildavel]], who keeps the seal and the archive, and who has been her friend for thirty years.

### Patrons

The Elder Marendos who chose her, and who can choose again.

### Enemies

None she names. [[being-orvandkthvl|Sir Orvand Kéthavel]], the Knight-Marshal, presses the session for the final campaign, and the Elder Marendos who opposed the embassy wait for it to fail.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-dunavarsynod|Dúnavarre Synod]]**: Velisande is its Prime Marendos.
- **[[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]]**: She is its head of state.
- **[[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Thordimalon]]**: She is a mage of high rank in the Arcane Order.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The session's reasons.** Velisande wants the record of the session that declared the Seilvri a danger in about 694, and she wants it found by someone the Synod does not employ.
2. **A writ she did not seal.** A writ under the Synod's seal has reached a steward on the western estates, and the Prime says the session never decided it.
