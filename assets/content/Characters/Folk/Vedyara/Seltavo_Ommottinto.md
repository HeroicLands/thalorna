# Appearance {#appearance}

Seltavo Ommottinto is a man of 1,620 years who stands 6' 5" tall and is of medium build, broader in the shoulder than most of his people. His black hair is braided close to his head, his eyes are a dark green, and his skin is tanned by long seasons in the open hollows. Both forearms carry old burn scars, pale against the brown. He wears a leather jerkin blackened at the cuffs and carries a long knife and a pot of banked embers on a cord.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 1,620. Lord of Tilvaisto. Wants the deep hollow burned out before it kills again.**

Seltavo Ommottinto is a lord of the [[affiliation-nclvtlvst|Enclave of Tilvaisto]], raised by every voice of the hearths for the hollows he has burned clean of [[being-glowvine|glowvines]] below the ridge the upper Bharava calls [[place-standlights|the Standing Lights]]. The standing is respect and holds no land, hearth or office. His hearth is Ommottinto, the hearth of the hollow.

## Background

Seltavo learned fire from the wardens of the ridge, who burn each glowvine that creeps out of the deep hollow along the roots, and he made it his work. Over the centuries he has burned more of those creeping vines than any warden living, slowly and close to the ground, so that no flame rises above the trees and no smoke is seen from the margin. The scars on his arms are from vines that struck while they burned. The hearths raised him a lord for the hollows he has kept clear, and for the gatherers who walk through them now without knowing what grew there.

The deepest hollow, below the stream the lost band followed, where the vines hang thickest and from which they creep, he has never burned. It is too large to burn low, and a fire big enough to clear it is seen from every hostel on the upper river.

## Psyche

### Personality

Seltavo is blunt, impatient with long counsel and quick to act. He respects the ridge's silence and does not think it is worth a life.

### Motivation

He wants the deep hollow burned out before the keeper's license sends another band into it.

### Strengths

He reads a hollow and a wind as well as any Senalo living, and he can burn a stand of vines without singeing the tree it climbs.

### Weaknesses

He weighs the cost of acting against the cost of waiting badly, and he counts every dead gatherer against the hearths that told him to wait.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Burned ground below the ridge.** Gatherers at the margin have found hollows of black ground below the ridge, the vines in them burned to ash and the trees above them unmarked. The keeper of the last estate wants to know who burns his license, and Seltavo wants nobody looking.
2. **The stream below the ridge.** Seltavo wants to know how many gatherers the keeper means to license next season, and whether any of them follow the stream.
3. **A vine at the ridge's foot.** A licensed gatherer has found a glowvine in a hollow at the foot of the ridge, closer to the margin than any glowvine the gatherers remember. Seltavo wants to know whether it crept there along the roots over many seasons without the wardens seeing it, or was carried down from the deep hollow by something that brushed it.

:::secret {title="The fuel in the hollow"}
Seltavo has already carried dry wood into the rim of the deep hollow and laid it where the wind comes up the stream. He has not lit it, because the hearths have not agreed. If another band is licensed past the old line before they agree, he lights it, and stands before the hearths afterward to answer for the fire on the ridge.
:::
