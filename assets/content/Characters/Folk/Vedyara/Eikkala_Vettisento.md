# Appearance {#appearance}

Eikkala Vettisento is a woman of 1,210 years who stands 6' 0" tall and is of light build. Her auburn hair is braided in a single plait down her back, her eyes are green, and her pale skin is smooth. Her palms are stained brown by bark and sap. She wears a plain tunic of undyed cloth and hums under her breath when she thinks nobody is listening.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 1,210. Singer of the Crest. Wants the old trees of the crest unharmed.**

Eikkala Vettisento is a member of the [[affiliation-nclvtlvst|Enclave of Tilvaisto]] who sings the old trees of the crest into the shapes of hearths, on the ridge the upper Bharava calls [[place-standlights|the Standing Lights]]. The [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] would rather grow what they need than build it, and the hearths of the ridge stand in trees she and the singers before her led into rooms. Her hearth is Vettisento, the hearth of the willow.

## Background

Eikkala learned to sing the living wood from the singer of the crest before her and took the work over when that singer asked to be let go. She knows each tree of the crest, its age, its roots and what it bears, and she grows a new hearth slowly, over a human lifetime, so that the tree takes no harm from it. The lamps the margin sees are set in hearths she grew.

## Psyche

### Personality

Eikkala is warm, absent-minded and stubborn about anything that touches the trees.

### Motivation

She wants the old trees of the crest unharmed, whatever the enclave decides about the gatherers.

### Strengths

She knows the trees of the ridge, roots and all, as well as any Senalo has known a wood.

### Weaknesses

She cares more for the trees than for the gatherers, and the hearths that disagree with her know it.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The tree that sang.** A gatherer swears that a great tree at the margin hummed when he set his blade to it, and his band will not tap it. The keeper of the last estate wants the tree cut to prove it is only a tree.
2. **Sap that will not set.** The lac and resin taken past the old line this season does not harden as lac should. The buyers downriver want to know why before they pay.
3. **A sapling carried off.** A licensed band dug a sapling from the margin to plant at the keeper's house. Eikkala wants it back in its own ground before it dies.

:::secret {title="The roots of the deep hollow"}
The deep hollow lies over the roots of the oldest trees of the crest, and the glowvines there are rooted among them. Eikkala holds that a fire big enough to clear the hollow burns down into those roots and kills the trees that hold four of the enclave's hearths. She has told Ossendatta and nobody else, because telling the hearths ends the argument and she would rather Seltavo were persuaded than outvoted.
:::
