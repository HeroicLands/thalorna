# Appearance {#appearance}

Rìssetti Loussinto is a man of 2,610 years who stands 6' 4" tall and is of light build. His silver hair falls long and straight, his eyes are gray, and his pale skin is unlined. He wears a cord of dried rowan berries at his throat and a long robe the dark green of shade under leaves, and he walks so slowly that the young of the enclave match their pace to his without noticing.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 2,610. Helsainno of Helsaisto. Wants guest-right kept for whoever sits and asks nothing, and for nobody who comes asking.**

Rìssetti Loussinto is the Helsainno, the Keeper of the Shade, of the [[affiliation-nclvhlsst|Enclave of Helsaisto]], the Senali at the head of the valley in [[place-forestnoroad|the Forest of No Road]] that the hostel-keepers of the upper Bharava call [[place-hermitshollw|the Hermits' Hollow]]. The hearths raised him to the office by every voice, and he holds it for as long as they do not raise another. His hearth is Loussinto, the hearth of the thing left for another.

## Background

Rìssetti was born in the hollow and has lived nowhere else. In his first thousand years he was the one who set fruit on the flat stone for whoever sat there, which is the work of his hearth, and he learned the renunciants as a keeper learns a season: when they come, how long they sit, how they leave. The hearths raised him a lord for the long care he took of guests who never knew they were guests, and raised him Helsainno for the same care.

He holds the enclave's silence toward the renunciants as the heart of its guest-right: the hollow gives a sitter shade, fruit and peace, and asks nothing of him, not even that he know who gives it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Rìssetti is slow, courteous and unmovable once he has reasoned a thing through. He asks more questions than he answers.

### Motivation

He wants guest-right kept as the hollow has kept it, for the one who sits and asks nothing, and he does not count a crowd that comes asking as guests.

### Strengths

He knows the renunciants better than any hearth of Helsaisto, and his patience outlasts any argument the hearths can bring him.

### Weaknesses

He reasons from custom and is slow to see when a case is new.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The crowd at the stream's foot.** Forty pilgrims wait at the hostel at the foot of the stream for a returned renunciant to lead them into the hollow. Rìssetti wants them sent home before they try the stream without him.
2. **Fruit out of season.** The returned renunciant has shown the hostel a fruit he says he found on the stone, of a kind that does not grow in the forest. Its doorkeeper wants to know what it is.
3. **The doorkeeper's letter.** The doorkeeper of the hostel has written down the river asking leave to send the crowd on, and the answer has not come. He asks a party to carry a second letter to the temple that keeps the hostel.

:::secret {title="The renunciant at the stream's foot"}
Rìssetti set the fruit on the stone for the renunciant at the hostel through all twelve of his years, and counts him the longest guest the hollow has kept in Rìssetti's own lifetime. He has not decided what the hearths owe Lernuvo Vaultinto for speaking to him, and privately he agrees with Nelpela that the speaking kept guest-right better than the silence would have. He has said so to nobody, because the Helsainno says aloud what the hearths agree, not what he thinks.
