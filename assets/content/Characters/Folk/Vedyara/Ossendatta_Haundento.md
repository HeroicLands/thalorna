# Appearance {#appearance}

Ossendatta Haundento is a woman of 2,380 years who stands 6' 3" tall and is of light build. Her silver hair falls loose to her waist, her eyes are a clear gray, and her olive skin is without a line. She wears a ring of braided grass on her left wrist, replaced each spring, and a long robe of undyed cloth the color of bark. She speaks quietly and seldom first.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 2,380. Tilvainno of Tilvaisto. Wants the ridge kept as the hearths have always kept it.**

Ossendatta Haundento is the Tilvainno, the Keeper of the Seam of Light, of the [[affiliation-nclvtlvst|Enclave of Tilvaisto]], the Senali on the ridge in [[place-forestnoroad|the Forest of No Road]] that the gatherers of the upper Bharava call [[place-standlights|the Standing Lights]]. The hearths raised her to the office by every voice, and she holds it for as long as they do not raise another. Her hearth is Haundento, the hearth of the hidden.

## Background

Ossendatta was born on the ridge and has lived nowhere else. She was a singer of the crest in her first centuries and a keeper of the paths in her next, and she learned the margin below the ridge as well as any warden: which hollows hold glowvines, which streams the gatherers follow, where the lamps of the crest can be seen from below and where they cannot. The hearths raised her a lord for the long seasons she kept the paths without a traveler harmed, and raised her Tilvainno when the last holder asked to be let go.

As Tilvainno she says aloud what the hearths agree and nothing they have not, and she has never needed to drive anyone out.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ossendatta is patient, exact and slow to speak. She listens to every hearth before she says what she thinks, and when she says it she says it once.

### Motivation

She wants the ridge kept as the hearths have kept it: closed, unharmed and unseen.

### Strengths

She knows the margin and its people by long watching, and she can tell from the ridge which gatherers are lost and which are only slow.

### Weaknesses

She trusts the ridge's keeping to answer every trouble, and she is slow to see a trouble it does not answer.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The keeper's surveyors.** The keeper of the last estate on the upper river sends men with cords and stakes to mark his boundary at the foot of the ridge. Ossendatta wants them turned back without harm and without a stake left standing.
2. **The kin at the hostel.** The kin of the lost gatherers mean to go up the stream themselves at the next new moon. Ossendatta wants them stopped before they reach the hollow where their kinsmen died.
3. **The lamp at the margin.** A gatherer's child has carried home from the margin a small lamp that gives light without flame. Ossendatta wants it back on the ridge before the child's people take it down the river to sell.

:::secret {title="The kin at the hostel"}
Ossendatta has gone down to the margin by night and stood at the edge of the hostel's clearing, listening to the kin of the lost band mourn five people when two of them are alive among the Hvarn. She has come to think Hìrretti is right, that the gatherers must be told something they cannot mistake, and she has not said so to the hearths. If she says it, the hearths agree, and Tilvaisto speaks to humans for the first time in her life.
:::
