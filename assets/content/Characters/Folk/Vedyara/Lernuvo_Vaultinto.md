# Appearance {#appearance}

Lernuvo Vaultinto is a man of 330 years who stands 6' 3" tall and is of medium build. His blond hair is cut short, his eyes are green, and his fair skin carries a fresh scratch across the left cheek from a thorn. He wears a short brown cloak and carries a bow, and he has a young man's habit of moving too fast for the shade.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 330. Hunter of the Hollow. Wants the renunciant he spoke to kept from leading the crowd in.**

Lernuvo Vaultinto is the youngest hunter of the [[affiliation-nclvhlsst|Enclave of Helsaisto]], at the head of the valley the upper Bharava calls [[place-hermitshollw|the Hermits' Hollow]]. His hearth is Vaultinto, the hearth of the ford. He is the first hearth of Helsaisto to speak to a renunciant at the stone.

## Background

Lernuvo learned to hunt from [[being-huvettavotrknt|Huvettavo Tìrkente]] and has walked the paths above the stone beside him since. This spring he found the renunciant who had sat at the stone for twelve years lying in fever with the rains a month away, and [[being-nelpelaaunkkn|Nelpela Aunekkento]] told him leaves would not save the man. Lernuvo knelt beside him and told him, in his own tongue, to go home before the rains. The renunciant went. Since then the crowd has gathered at the hostel, and the hearths have argued over what Lernuvo did without deciding.

## Psyche

### Personality

Lernuvo is earnest, quick and restless, and has not learned the long patience of the hearths.

### Motivation

He wants the renunciant he spoke to kept from leading the crowd into the hollow, because if he does, it is Lernuvo's doing.

### Strengths

He is a fine hunter and a fast runner, and he speaks the tongue of the hostels better than any hearth of the hollow, from long listening at the stone.

### Weaknesses

He acts before he weighs, and having acted once he finds it easier to act again.

## Social

### Companions

- Huvettavo Tìrkente: The hunter who taught him.
- Nelpela Aunekkento: The healer who sent him to the dying man.

### Patrons

Nelpela Aunekkento, who means to stand beside him before the hearths.

### Enemies

None. Some hearths of the hollow want him to answer for speaking.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvhlsst|Enclave of Helsaisto]]**: Lernuvo is a Member of the Enclave.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The voice at the stone.** The returned renunciant says that a voice told him to go home before the rains, in the tongue of the road. The pilgrims want to hear the voice themselves, and mean to sit at the stone in a crowd until it speaks.
2. **The renunciant's refusal.** The returned renunciant has refused to lead the crowd into the hollow, and some of them have begun to press him. Lernuvo wants him kept safe at the hostel.
3. **Tracks in the hostel yard.** The doorkeeper has found the prints of long bare feet in the soft ground at the edge of the hostel yard, and asks a party to watch the yard by night.

:::secret {title="The second time"}
Lernuvo has gone down to the edge of the hostel yard by night and spoken to the renunciant a second time, asking him not to lead anyone in. The renunciant promised. The tracks at the edge of the yard are Lernuvo's, and if the hearths learn of the second speaking, the argument over the first is over, and not in his favor.
:::
