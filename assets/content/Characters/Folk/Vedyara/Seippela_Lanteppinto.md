# Appearance {#appearance}

Seippela Lanteppinto is a woman of 940 years who stands 6' 2" tall and is of light build. Her white hair hangs long and straight, with a single thread of blue dye tied in it at the left temple, her eyes are violet, and her olive skin is clear. She wears a gray-green cloak that is hard to see against the trees and goes barefoot on the forest floor.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 940. Keeper of the Paths. Wants the margin quiet and the gatherers content to go home.**

Seippela Lanteppinto is a member of the [[affiliation-nclvtlvst|Enclave of Tilvaisto]] who keeps the paths at the foot of the ridge the upper Bharava calls [[place-standlights|the Standing Lights]]. A traveler who walks toward the lamps of the crest and comes down at dusk on the bank he left has been walked there by her keeping. Her hearth is Lanteppinto, the hearth of the river.

## Background

Seippela learned the paths from [[being-ossendattahndn|Ossendatta Haundento]], who kept them before she was raised Tilvainno, and she has kept them since. She does her work without ever being seen: she leads a traveler's feet, not his eyes, and he goes home thinking the forest confused him. She takes pride that nobody has come to harm on her paths, and the hearths hold her in respect for it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Seippela is soft-spoken, careful and fond of the gatherers in the distant way a keeper is fond of what she keeps.

### Motivation

She wants the margin quiet, and every traveler on her paths sent home unharmed and content.

### Strengths

No Senalo of the ridge turns a traveler more gently. She can walk a whole band in a circle for a day without one of them suspecting it.

### Weaknesses

She thinks in seasons and does not see how a single season's mistake can grow.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The band that lost its way.** The band that came back from the ridge with a full season of lac cannot say which way it walked home, though its headman has worked the margin for forty years. He wants to know how the forest turned him.
2. **The blue thread.** A gatherer found a thread of blue dye caught on a thorn beside a path that was not there the next morning, and keeps it as a charm. A party that asks about the ridge at the margin is shown it.
3. **The keeper's guide.** The keeper of the last estate has hired a guide from the lowlands who claims he cannot be turned in any forest. He means to lead the next licensed band up to the ridge.

:::secret {title="The full season"}
Seippela let the first licensed band gather its lac for seven days before she turned it, so that it would go home with a full season and no cause to come back. The keeper of the last estate took the full season as proof that the ground past the old line is rich, and licensed more bands. Seippela knows her kindness is the reason the gatherers keep coming, and she has told nobody.
:::
