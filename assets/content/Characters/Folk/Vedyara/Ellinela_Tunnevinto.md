# Appearance {#appearance}

Ellinela Tunnevinto is a woman of 1,450 years who stands 6' 1" tall and is of light build. Her black hair is braided in many fine plaits gathered at the nape, her eyes are a deep blue, and her olive skin is clear. A small pipe of pale wood hangs from a cord at her belt. Her speaking voice is low, and the hollow goes quiet when she sings.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 1,450. Lord of Helsaisto. Wants the hollow closed until the crowd at the hostel has gone home.**

Ellinela Tunnevinto is a lord of the [[affiliation-nclvhlsst|Enclave of Helsaisto]], raised by every voice of the hearths for her singing, at the head of the valley the upper Bharava calls [[place-hermitshollw|the Hermits' Hollow]]. The standing is respect and holds no land, hearth or office. Her hearth is Tunnevinto, the hearth of the refrain.

## Background

Ellinela is the hollow's singer. It is her singing, more than any other hearth's, that divides the stream where it does not divide and brings a traveler down again, and she has kept the hollow's ways for most of her life. The hearths raised her a lord for a long song she made of the hollow's trees, which they sing at the turning of the year.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ellinela is proud, quick and protective, generous to the hearths and impatient with anything that troubles the hollow's peace.

### Motivation

She wants the hollow closed to everyone, the solitary renunciants at the stone included, until the crowd at the hostel has given up and gone home.

### Strengths

Her singing holds the hollow's ways closed more surely than any other hearth's, and the hearths listen when she speaks.

### Weaknesses

She trusts her song to settle troubles that are not the song's to settle, and she does not like to be refused.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The pipe at night.** The pilgrims at the hostel hear a pipe playing up the stream after dark, and some of them mean to follow it. The doorkeeper wants them kept in the yard.
2. **The stream that divides.** A pilgrim who tried the stream alone came back saying it divided twice where there is only one stream, and both ways brought him down. His companions want a guide who can keep to it.
3. **The song for the turning year.** A returned renunciant hums a tune he says he heard every year at the same season in the hollow, and a singer of the road has taken it up. Ellinela wants it out of human mouths.

:::secret {title="The night the hollow closed"}
Ellinela has already sung the hollow shut once, for a single night, to show the hearths it can be done. A renunciant who had sat at the stone for three years woke that night to find no path down and no stone, wandered, and has not been found since. The hearths do not know, and Huvettavo Tìrkente is searching the lower hollow for him at her asking.
:::
