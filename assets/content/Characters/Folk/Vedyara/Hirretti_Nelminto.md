# Appearance {#appearance}

Hìrretti Nelminto is a man of 610 years who stands 6' 1" tall and is of medium build. His dark brown hair is cropped close, his eyes are brown, and his skin is tanned by the open hollows. The back of his left hand is laced with fine white scars from glowvine thorns. He wears a moss-green cloak over a leather jerkin and carries a short bow, a long knife and a pot of banked embers.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 610. Warden of the Hollows. Wants the gatherers kept out of the deep hollow, by any sign that works.**

Hìrretti Nelminto is a warden of the [[affiliation-nclvtlvst|Enclave of Tilvaisto]], one of those who walk the wet hollows below the ridge the upper Bharava calls [[place-standlights|the Standing Lights]] and burn the [[being-glowvine|glowvines]] that grow in them. His hearth is Nelminto, the hearth of the moss.

## Background

Hìrretti took up the warden's bow young and learned to burn vines from the lord [[being-seltavommttnt|Seltavo Ommottinto]], walking behind him hollow after hollow. He knows the gatherers of the margin better than any hearth of the ridge, because he has watched them at their work for centuries: their camps, their songs, the way they mark a tree they mean to tap and the way they bury their dead.

A month ago he found the lost band in the deep hollow, too late for three of them. He carried one of the two living and led the other out of the forest by a path the ridge keeps, and left them at the edge of the Hvarn grazing under [[place-estrnreach|the Eastern Reach]], where herders would find them in the morning.

## Psyche

### Personality

Hìrretti is quiet, watchful and warm under it, with more patience for the gatherers than for the hearths' long counsel.

### Motivation

He wants the gatherers kept out of the deep hollow, and he thinks the ridge's silence is too high a price for one more of them dead.

### Strengths

He moves through the hollows without a sound, finds a glowvine by its smell before he sees its light, and knows the gatherers' ways as well as his own.

### Weaknesses

He grieves for the dead of the margin as if they were his own hearth's, and grief makes him act before the hearths agree.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A pale arrow in a tapped tree.** A gatherer at the margin finds an arrow of pale wood standing in a lac-tree at the mouth of the stream, fletched as no people of the forest fletch it. He asks a party to learn whose it is and what it means.
2. **The smell in the hollow.** Hìrretti wants anyone who goes up the stream told to turn back where the air smells of overripe fruit, and has no way to tell them.
3. **Two strangers among the Hvarn.** A Hvarn hearth under the Eastern Reach is nursing two lowland gatherers it found at the edge of its grazing, who speak no Hvarn and cannot say how they got there. The hearth wants them taken home.

:::secret {title="The arrow and the two at Nurvharn"}
The arrow in the lac-tree is Hìrretti's, set where the stream leaves the margin as a warning the hearths have not agreed to give. He has thought of walking to the Hvarn grazing to see the two gatherers home himself, and the hearths would count it a breach of the ridge's silence as plain as speaking. The two survivors are sheltered at a hearth of [[place-nurvhrn|Nurvharn]]'s summer grazing.
:::
