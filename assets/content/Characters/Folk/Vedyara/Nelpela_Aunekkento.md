# Appearance {#appearance}

Nelpela Aunekkento is a woman of 830 years who stands 5' 11" tall, short for her people, and is of light build. Her dark brown hair is braided and pinned up out of the way, her eyes are brown, and her skin is light and clear. Her fingertips are stained green by crushed leaves. She carries a satchel of herbs wrapped in leaves and smells faintly of crushed mint.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 830. Healer of the Hollow. Wants the sitters at the stone kept alive.**

Nelpela Aunekkento is the healer of the [[affiliation-nclvhlsst|Enclave of Helsaisto]], at the head of the valley the upper Bharava calls [[place-hermitshollw|the Hermits' Hollow]]. She tends the hearths, and she tends the renunciants at the stone too, with herbs she leaves among the fruit where a sitter finds them without seeing who set them there. Her hearth is Aunekkento, the hearth of the valley.

## Background

Nelpela learned healing from the herb-gatherers of the hollow and came to the sitters at the stone by way of the fevers the rains bring every year. A renunciant who sits long in the wet shade sickens, and for most of her life she has left the leaves that bring a fever down where the sitter finds them in the morning. She was the one who saw that the renunciant now at the hostel was past what leaves could do.

## Psyche

### Personality

Nelpela is practical, plain-spoken and fond of the sitters at the stone, whom she knows by their coughs.

### Motivation

She wants the sitters at the stone kept alive, and she holds that guest-right that lets a guest die in silence is not guest-right.

### Strengths

She knows every herb of the forest and every fever of the rains, and she can judge a sitter's health from the trees.

### Weaknesses

She says what she thinks in the hearths' counsel before the hearths are ready to hear it.

3. **Two at the stone.** Two sitters at the stone have fallen sick this season, and one of them has a brother in the crowd at the hostel who wants him carried down.

:::secret {title="What the leaves cannot do"}
Nelpela left leaves for the renunciant now at the hostel for three nights before she judged he would die without more than leaves, and it was she who told Lernuvo Vaultinto so. She holds that Lernuvo spoke for her as much as for himself, and if the hearths ask Lernuvo to answer for speaking she means to stand beside him and say so. The two sitters at the stone now are as sick as he was.
