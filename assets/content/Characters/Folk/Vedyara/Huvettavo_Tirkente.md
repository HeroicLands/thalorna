# Appearance {#appearance}

Huvettavo Tìrkente is a man of 520 years who stands 6' 2" tall and is of medium build. His brown hair is cut short, his eyes are hazel, and his skin is tanned. He wears a barred owl's feather tied behind his left ear, a short cloak of mottled brown and a quiver of pale arrows, and he can stand still among the trees long enough for birds to land near him.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 520. Hunter of the Hollow. Wants the crowd at the hostel kept out of the hollow.**

Huvettavo Tìrkente is a hunter of the [[affiliation-nclvhlsst|Enclave of Helsaisto]] who keeps the paths above the renunciants' stone at the head of the valley the upper Bharava calls [[place-hermitshollw|the Hermits' Hollow]]. Every traveler who comes up the stream he sees before the traveler sees the stone. His hearth is Tìrkente, the hearth of the peak.

## Background

Huvettavo hunted the hollow with the young of the enclave and took up its paths when he could follow a deer through the shade by night. He taught [[being-lernuvovltnt|Lernuvo Vaultinto]] to hunt, and the two of them watch the stream together. Since the crowd gathered at the hostel he has spent his nights in the trees at the edge of its clearing, counting the pilgrims and listening to them.

## Psyche

### Personality

Huvettavo is dry, quiet and loyal, more at ease in the trees than in the hearths' counsel.

### Motivation

He wants the crowd kept out of the hollow and the sitters at the stone left in peace.

### Strengths

He sees in the shade as well as an owl, moves without being seen, and can follow anyone through the forest by night.

### Weaknesses

He keeps faith with a friend before he keeps it with the hearths, and keeps secrets he ought to carry to them.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Eyes in the trees.** The pilgrims at the hostel say someone watches them from the trees at night, and two of them have taken to sitting up with torches. The doorkeeper wants it settled before somebody sets the margin alight.
2. **The pilgrim who is no pilgrim.** One of the forty at the hostel carries a surveyor's staff wrapped in his bedroll and walks the stream's bank at dusk, sighting along it toward the east. The doorkeeper asks a party to learn who sent him and what he is measuring.
3. **The lost sitter.** A renunciant who sat at the stone for three years has not come down, and his brother at the hostel wants him found.

:::secret {title="The surveyor and the lost sitter"}
The man with the surveyor's staff is the hired man of the ordained keeper of the last estate on the upper river, who claims unendowed ground up to the ridge the gatherers call [[place-standlights|the Standing Lights]] and wants the forest above his line walked and marked. He has come up with the pilgrims so that nobody asks why he is there, because the keeper wants to know whether any stream of the forest's western side climbs east toward the ridge, and so runs through ground he can claim. Huvettavo has watched him walk the stream's bank at dusk, sighting along his staff toward the east and writing down what he sees. By night Huvettavo also searches the lower hollow for the renunciant lost on the night Ellinela sang the hollow shut, at her asking, and he has told neither Lernuvo nor the hearths.
:::
