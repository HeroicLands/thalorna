# Appearance {#appearance}

Knarvselda Dvurnaruk is a 37-year-old woman who stands 5'11" tall and is of heavy build. She has fair skin chapped and weathered by the ridge winds, dark blonde hair worn in a single braid bound with gray leather, and gray eyes. A healed spear-cut runs along the left side of her jaw. She wears a plain iron ring on her right thumb, the place where a signed captain of the north wears the Compact's bronze.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Knarvselda Dvurnaruk is the captain of [[affiliation-hrendskari|Hrendskari]], the Nordman company that holds seven watchtowers of the [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]] march above [[place-dolvar|Dolvar]] for the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]]. She was born on a fishing stead on the coast of the [[affiliation-kngdmnrdhm|Kingdom of Nordheim]], into a clan whose name records a charge kept, and she went south at nineteen with a company that took a season's contract on the Vristwald march. She came home with silver and went south again. She has captained Hrendskari since its sixth year, when its first captain died of a winter fever in a tower, and she brought it to Dolvar six years ago.

The iron ring is her answer to the [[affiliation-malldbndlg|Málalidabandalag]]. A captain signed to the Compact wears the bronze ring of the Hall on the right thumb, and the empire's contracts carry a clause no signed captain can swear. Knarvselda wears iron where the bronze would go, and tells any Nordman who asks that she will take the bronze when the Hall can get her company paid on a ridge in Moktur.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is steady, dry and blunt, and she does not like to be thanked. She keeps the legion's rules on her towers more strictly than some of the legion's own officers keep them on theirs, because her company's contract depends on its tallies being better than the legion's. She drinks with her Old Hands at the fire and with nobody else, and she remembers every man's name and his wife's.

### Motivation

She wants her company paid, her towers held and her dead sent home, in that order. Beneath that she wants a contract that will last long enough for the company's children in Dolvar's lower town to grow up in one place, which a free company seldom gives anyone.

### Strengths

She knows the march: the towers, the smoke, the Varokh bands and their seasons, and the legion's clerks. Her tallies are trusted at Dolvar, and the legion's centurions treat her as one of their own. Her company would follow her down off the ridge or into the forest, and it knows she would not ask either without cause.

### Weaknesses

She has no hall behind her. If the empire stops paying, her only appeal is to a court in a city that put her out at dusk, and her only buyer is the governor at [[place-tyrellan|Tirellan]]. She is short of men, and she has promised the legion commander towers she can barely hold.

## Social

### Companions

Her Old Hands, above all the warden of the company's highest tower, a woman of her own stead who went south with her the first time. The company's purser, who keeps its debts in the lower town, has a Mokturan wife and five children, and he is the one who reminds her what a late month would cost.

### Patrons

The empire pays her through the war council of [[affiliation-vylrnmltry|The Vylarian Military]], and she answers for her towers to the _vandraks_ (legion commander) at Dolvar, [Satheras Kiramaris]{.name data-type="being/npc"}. He is careful with her and she is careful with him, and the arrangement suits them both.

### Enemies

The legion's officers who signed away the towers want them back. The Iron Wolves' captain has not forgiven her refusal, and has said so to men who carried it to her.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-hrendskari|Hrendskari]]**: Knarvselda holds the captaincy as an Old Hand of the company, sworn to by every Spear-Sworn at a spear-point when they joined.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The letter from Tirellan.** The governor's rider waits in Dolvar's lower town for her answer to a sealed letter. She wants a party to find out, before she answers, whether the governor's offer is the governor's or someone else's in his name.
2. **The draft that is late.** The thirty new men she wrote north for have not come, and the truce market is a week away. A party that can fill a tower's watch for a fortnight, or find her thirty men she can trust, earns her company's friendship and a fair wage.
3. **The burned jar.** A jar of a dead Spear-Sworn's ashes, sent north with a man going home, has come back to Dolvar unopened with the man's own name scratched on it. Knarvselda wants to know where the man is, and who knew he was carrying it.
