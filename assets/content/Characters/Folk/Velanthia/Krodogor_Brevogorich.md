# Appearance {#appearance}

Krodogor Brevogorich is a 52-year-old man who stands 6'1" tall and is of medium build. He has a square face, a crooked nose, heavy brows, weathered tanned skin, cropped graying dark brown hair, a short beard and gray eyes. His left ear is notched.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 52. Prince of Brevavesh. Wants the forts fed, the wardens paid on time and no bargain with a Grukar nest.**

Krodogor Brevogorich holds the forts on the edge of the Grukar country by descent and lives in the upper hall of the fort at [[place-brvvsh|Brevavesh]], not in a house of his own. He appoints every warden and sees the rolls each quarter, when the port's silver arrives.

## Background

**Formation.** He is the son of the last prince and stood his first night on the bell hill at twelve. At eighteen he held a river tower as a warden, on a warden's pay.

**The office.** He became prince at thirty, when his father died holding the crossing, and he has kept the forts since.

**Now.** The lowland grain and the port silver both arrive late. He knows which wardens sell a night's passage to the fur hunters and keeps their posts filled anyway.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is blunt, tired and loyal to his men, and he says what the garrison is owed before he says anything else.

### Motivation

He wants the forts manned and the margin held, because the villages below them have nothing else.

### Strengths

He has stood every post himself, and the wardens know it.

### Weaknesses

He cannot feed his garrisons and must trust two princes to do it.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The pay-day.** The garrison is owed months, and Krodogor wants the quarter's silver brought from the port's mint before the wardens sell more nights.
2. **A fur hunter's bale.** A bale marked with a warden's tally has crossed the river, and he wants to know which post sold the passage.
