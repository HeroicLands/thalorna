# Appearance {#appearance}

The Wagon Master of the Last Train is a drover in a heavy coat, with a tally-board and a rented team in the yard of [[place-tvskvsh|Tveskavesh]].

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Lowland wagon master with the season's last grain. Needs to cross before the snow.**

The Wagon Master of the Last Train drives the last grain of a grain-town house to the March. He has a heavy team rented at Tveskavesh, and a bargain struck in the square with a buyer in the March that ends when his wagons pass the south gate.

## Background

**Formation.** He drives for a grain-town house of the lowlands.

**The bargain.** His buyer in the March holds him to a price that lapses when his wagons pass the gate.

**Now.** He needs riders over the pass and a go-between to settle what the riders and the customs officers each take.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is practical and short of time.

### Motivation

He wants his wagons across the pass before the snow closes it.

### Strengths

He knows the road, the stables and the teams.

### Weaknesses

His bargain ends at the gate, and each toll he pays comes out of the grain.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[place-tvskvsh|Tveskavesh]]**: where he changes teams and strikes his bargains.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Escort before the snow.** He needs riders over the pass, since the Host's roster is thin until the pay comes.
2. **The two tolls.** He needs a go-between to settle what the March's customs officers and the Sevret riders each take.
3. **Under the monks' grant.** His wagons pass the south gate under the monks' grant, or do not.
