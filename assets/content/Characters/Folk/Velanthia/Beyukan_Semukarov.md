# Appearance {#appearance}

Beyukan Semukarov is a 43-year-old man who stands 5' 8" tall and is of medium build. He has an oval face, a broad nose, arched brows, weathered tanned skin, straight black hair, a moustache and dark brown eyes that crease at the corners when he is not smiling.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 43. Headman of the river village of Semukarov. Wants the village to pay one toll and not two.**

Beyukan Semukarov is the headman of [[place-smkrv|Semukarov]], the village of fishers and barley farmers that the [[affiliation-sqrtrtrb|Soqritur]] hold. He keeps the river law among the households, settles a quarrel over a net or a mooring by the old custom and sits beside the tribe's river-warden when the fish and grain are shared out. The [[affiliation-hstkhrd|Khorad Host]] has told the village to stop paying the tribe, and the tribe has told it to keep paying. Beyukan has told both that the village pays for protection and will pay whoever gives it.

## Background

**Formation.** He fished through the ice from the age of nine, with the household that taught the river law to every child before it could haul a net.

**The river law.** The village chose him headman at thirty-five, after the old headman refused the tribe's first demand for a second share and was put out of his house.

**Now.** He holds the tally-staff of the river law, the village's say in the sharing and the good opinion of a river-warden who needs him.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is calm, wry and stubborn, and he answers a threat by offering a bowl of fish.

### Motivation

He wants the village's households fed and the tolls on the river to come to one, so that the river law is the only law his people answer.

### Strengths

He knows the custom of the river and the temper of both its masters, and every household keeps its word to him.

### Weaknesses

He is bound to the tribe by a law he did not make and to the Host by a debt he did not owe, and both know it.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-sqrtrtrb|Soqritur]]**: River-Folk, who as headman keeps the river law among the households of Semukarov.
