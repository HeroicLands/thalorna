# Appearance {#appearance}

Duvash Torvogorich is a 39-year-old man who stands 5' 9" tall and is of medium build. He has an angular face, an aquiline nose, straight brows, weathered tanned skin, straight brown hair, a long beard and hazel eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 39. Toll-keeper at the gate of Volkavesh. Wants the toll paid in full before anyone argues.**

Duvash Torvogorich collects what the caravans owe at the gate of [[place-vlkvsh|Volkavesh]], the seat of the [[affiliation-hsttrvl|Tarvál Host]]. He counts horses, salt and silver against the price the Host has set, writes each sum on a tally-stick and gives the silver to the ataman's yard at the day's end.

## Background

**Formation.** He rode with the Host's herds from twelve and learned the road's prices from the caravan-masters.

**The gate.** He was made toll-keeper at thirty, after the old keeper was found short by a tenth and sent back to the herds.

**Now.** He holds the gate and a tally for every wagon.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A caravan-master with a letter.** A caravan comes with a prince's letter excusing its toll, and Duvash wants a party to learn whether the seal is true before the ataman is asked.
