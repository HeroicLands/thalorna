# Appearance {#appearance}

The Heir of Brevavesh is a man of twenty-seven with his father's square face and none of his father's tiredness.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Elder son of the prince of Brevavesh. Wants Theodburg held again.**

The Heir of Brevavesh is the elder son of [[being-krdgrbrvgrch|Krodogor Brevogorich]]. He has ridden every forest post of his father's frontier himself, and he has counted the wardens there on a strip of birch bark.

## Background

**Formation.** He is twenty-seven and has ridden the frontier posts since he was a boy.

**The count.** He keeps a strip of birch bark on which he has marked the wardens he met at each post.

:::secret {title="The heir's count"}
He found fewer wardens at the posts than his father pays, and he takes the gap for desertion. The gap is the one the captain of the forts keeps in his second roll, and the heir does not know it.
:::

**Now.** He wants a war-band of the forest in the fort at Theodburg beside his own men, and he has the crossing and nothing else to give.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is earnest and impatient.

### Motivation

He wants to hold the forest edge through another autumn without asking his father for silver.

### Strengths

He has ridden every post himself, and he offers a fair bargain.

### Weaknesses

He has no silver and little patience for a bargain that takes long to strike.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]]**: the prince's elder son, whom his father neither forbids nor pays.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The heir's road.** He wants companions who are neither his father's wardens nor a war-band's men to ride with him to Thornhaven.
2. **The strip of bark.** It holds his count of the wardens at each post, and the captain of the forts wants it.
3. **The bargain at the gate.** He has the crossing to give and no silver, and he must answer who feeds the garrison.
