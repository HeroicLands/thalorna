# Appearance {#appearance}

Lervash Vlidogorich is a 43-year-old man who stands 5' 10" tall and is of light build. He has a long face, a narrow nose, thin brows, weathered fair skin, straight graying brown hair and gray eyes that rest on a sack until it has been counted.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 43. Granary-Keeper of the monastic house at [[place-dbrvsh|Dobravesh]]. Wants the barn's count to be one the house can stand behind.**

Lervash Vlidogorich holds the keys to the barn of the [[affiliation-grnrymnstrs|Granary Monasteries of Lizelas]] house on the market square of Dobravesh and answers to his starbrat for every sack in it. Across the cobbles the prince's keeper counts the sacks he can see, and Lervash declines to say how many he cannot. He walks the barn with a lamp before the morning bell and weighs a grower's cart himself when the grower disputes the scale.

:::secret {title="The east aisle"}
After the roof of the east aisle fell, he sold forty sacks of the house's grain through a carter bound for [[place-tvskvsh|Tveskavesh]] to pay for the timbers. He is putting the grain back out of his own share of each harvest and has told neither his starbrat nor the heads, because the grain was not his to sell.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He came to the house at twelve, a laborer's son from the villages around Dobravesh, and learned his letters from the monks who teach the sons of the neighboring halls.

**The keys.** The house gave him the barn's keys at thirty-five, when the old keeper's eyes failed, because Lervash could say what every bin held without opening the ledger.

**Now.** He keeps the barn by the ledger and lets the prince's keeper see the bins on the market side of the barn and no others.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is quiet, exact and dry, and he talks about the barn as if it were a patient he has nursed through bad winters.

### Motivation

He wants the barn full before the snow and every sack in it accounted for, to the house and to no one else.

### Strengths

He remembers what every bin held and when it was filled, and the villages trust his word on a weight over the prince's scale.

### Weaknesses

He lies badly, and the courtesy of the prince's keeper makes him anxious.
