# Appearance {#appearance}

Ovrik Lervogorich is a 50-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of medium build. He has a square face, heavy brows, weathered tanned skin, cropped graying brown hair, a stubble of beard and gray eyes. A scar runs through his left eyebrow.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 50. Captain of the Forts. Wants the silver in the barracks before the wardens sell another night.**

Ovrik Lervogorich commands the wardens of the [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]] and keeps their rolls and the pay owed them in the fort at [[place-brvvsh|Brevavesh]]. He shows the prince the rolls each quarter, and he sets the post of every warden.

:::secret {title="The second roll"}
His second roll shows that a third of the wardens the prince pays are not in the barracks. Ovrik pays their wages to the bell-ringers, cooks and village watches beneath the forts, because the silver comes late and the grain later. The prince would call it theft.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He was a village watchman below the river tower at sixteen and held the tower as a warden at twenty.

**The office.** The prince made him captain at forty-one, when the old captain died on a night the bell was not rung.

**Now.** He keeps the garrison fed by the rolls and asks no warden where his silver goes.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is gruff, tireless and loyal, and he keeps count of every man's debts in his head.

### Motivation

He wants the forts manned and no warden left to starve on a post.

### Strengths

He knows every warden, tower and bell by name, and the villages answer his call.

### Weaknesses

He trusts no clerk with the pay, and the prince sees only what he is shown.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A silent post.** The forest watch has not sent in its quarter's tally, and Ovrik wants a party to ride out and learn why.
2. **The pay-day gate.** On pay-day with no silver, he wants a party at the gate of the fort to keep the wardens' tempers from the villagers.
