# Appearance {#appearance}

Zhitovod Dobrovodich is a 58-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of heavy build. He has a broad face, heavy brows, weathered tanned skin, wavy graying brown hair, a full beard and brown eyes. His left knee is stiff, and he rides to the mills rather than walk to them.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 58. Prince of [[place-zhtvsh|Zhitavesh]]. Wants the Hosts paid in full at harvest and the monasteries' barns counted.**

Zhitovod Dobrovodich holds the lowland river, its mills and its tithe by descent, and lives in the hall beside the princely granary at Zhitavesh. His wealth is sacks, not silver. He pays the [[affiliation-hstsvrt|Sevret Host]] and the [[affiliation-hstkhrd|Khorad Host]] in grain and silver, and the pay is his whole hold on them.

:::secret {title="The pledged tithe"}
Zhitovod has pledged a third of the autumn tithe to a grain-town house against the silver for the Hosts' pay. A short harvest leaves him owing grain he does not hold, and he has told neither his keeper of the granary nor the Hosts.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He is the eldest son of the last prince and grew up in the granary yard, learning the tally before the sword. At nineteen he rode with the hall's retinue on the pass roads and learned what a Host asks for its pay.

**The office.** He took the river at thirty-four, by descent and the hall's oath, and he rules by the customary law his judge keeps him to.

**Now.** The late rain on the eastern fields leaves him answerable for a short harvest to both Hosts and the forts of [[place-brvvsh|Brevavesh]].

## Psyche

### Personality

He is heavy, patient and sparing of speech, and he counts before he answers.

### Motivation

He wants the barns full before the snow and no one else's price on his grain.

### Strengths

He knows every mill, ferry and barn of the lowlands and who owes what at each.

### Weaknesses

His grain is worth what others will pay, and the monastic reserve is closed to him.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The late rain.** Zhitovod wants a party to ride the eastern fields and tell him what the ear will yield before the Hosts' pay falls due.
2. **Grain upriver.** He wants wagons escorted up the pass road to be sold at [[place-tvskvsh|Tveskavesh]], where the grain-town price is better, if the wagons can pass the port houses' agents in the square uncounted.
