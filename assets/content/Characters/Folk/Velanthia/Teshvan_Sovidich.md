# Appearance {#appearance}

Teshvan Sovidich is a 48-year-old man who stands 5' 10" tall and is of medium build. He has a long face, a hooked nose, straight brows, weathered pale skin, straight graying black hair, a moustache and gray eyes that hold still while he listens.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 48. Ransom broker of [[place-brvvsh|Brevavesh]]. Wants every captive he brings out to be the last one he is asked to bring.**

Teshvan Sovidich rides between Brevavesh and the ford town of [Yaravesh]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} with a family's coin in a belted purse and a letter in a clerk's hand. At the ford he counts the sum out to the war-band's go-between, and he brings the captive back by the river road if the price is paid. A family that cannot pay can pay him a fee to ask. The wardens know his face and let him through at any hour, since it is in no warden's interest to stop a ransom.

:::secret {title="The tenth"}
A tenth of every family's coin never reaches the ford. He keeps the difference and has never kept a ransom entire, and no family has counted what was sent against what was paid.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He was a grain-factor's clerk in Brevavesh until a raid took his brother, and he learned how a ransom is carried by carrying his own.

**The ford.** He made his name at the ford at thirty-one, when he brought out three captives for a village that had given them up.

**Now.** He rides the road to Yaravesh six times in a quiet year and twenty in a bad one.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is soft-spoken, scrupulous in manner and exact about what he has been paid, and he does not offer comfort.

### Motivation

He wants the ransoms to go through and to have enough set by to leave the river road before the war-bands know his face.

### Strengths

He knows the go-betweens at the ford, how a price is named and how it is lowered, and no warden stops him.

### Weaknesses

He is hated by the families he fails and distrusted by the families he serves, and he has no friend in either camp.

## Social

### Affiliations

- [[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]]: its wardens let him pass at any hour on a ransom, and ask no price for the courtesy.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Coin for the ford.** A family has the price of a captive and no one to carry it, and Teshvan wants a party to ride beside him, since the road is riskier than he says.
2. **An unransomed name.** A captive's name appears on a ransom list at the ford that no family has paid for, and Teshvan wants a party to find who is asking.
