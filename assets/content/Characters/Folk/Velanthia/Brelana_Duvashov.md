# Appearance {#appearance}

Brelana Duvashov is a 42-year-old woman who stands 5' 6" tall and is of medium build. She has an oval face, an aquiline nose, arched brows, weathered olive skin, straight black hair and hazel eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 42. River-trader of the slow rivers. Wants to know where the marsh has moved before she pushes off.**

Brelana Duvashov poles a flat boat up the slow brown rivers from the forts to the villages of the forest edge with salt, iron and needles. She asks at each bank where the [[being-ncrtcmr|Necrotic Mire]] was last seen, because her trade runs along the edge of [[place-skathengrund|Skathengrund]] and a wrong guess costs a boat.

## Background

**Formation.** She was born in the river village of [Duvashov]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} and poled her first boat at ten, in a spring flood.

**The place.** She went upriver at twenty-two with a boat and a debt, and found the forts needed salt.

**Now.** She holds a route of eleven landings.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is dry, practical and wary of strangers, and she asks about the marsh before she asks anyone's name.

### Motivation

She wants to know where the marsh has moved before she pushes off, and to clear the debt she went upriver with.

### Strengths

She holds a route of eleven landings, and the villages of the forest edge tell her what the forts do not.

### Weaknesses

A wrong guess costs her boat, and she will not stay a night in a village she does not know.

## Social

### Allies

- **The villages of the forest edge**: They buy her salt, iron and needles and tell her where the marsh was last seen.
- **The forts**: They need her salt.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Where the marsh moved.** Brelana has heard that the Mire crossed a cause-walk near her route, and she wants a party to look at the bank before she poles through it.
