# Appearance {#appearance}

Sulvan Tveskogorich is a 35-year-old man who stands 5' 9" tall and is of medium build. He has an oval face, a straight nose, straight brows, smooth tawny skin, wavy dark brown hair, a moustache and brown eyes that settle on a speaker's hands.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 35. Member of a great merchant house at Brelavesh. Wants his house to hold the tolls fixed by contract for a year.**

Sulvan Tveskogorich holds a share of the ledgers of a great house of the [[affiliation-mrchnthsrvr|Merchant Houses of the Rivers]] and stands in its warehouses on the quays of [[place-brlvsh|Brelavesh]] when a barge is unloaded. He signs the house's receipts, argues its tolls with the port-warden's clerks and takes a visitor of consequence to the guest hall behind the quays. His house lends against each year's harvest, and he knows to the sack how much grain it has already promised to buyers who have not seen it.

## Background

**Formation.** He was born into the house, the second son of a head's brother, and learned to weigh a bale by hand before he learned to read a bill.

**The warehouse.** The house put him on the quay at twenty-two, in the warehouse where the grain is counted in, and gave him a share of the ledgers at thirty.

**Now.** He is the face of the house on the quays, and his own cousins decide what he may promise.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is courteous, careful with his words and slow to commit to anything his house has not already committed.

### Motivation

He wants the port's tolls fixed by contract, so that no prince can raise them in a thin year and leave the house to pay.

### Strengths

He can read a ledger across a table upside down, and every clerk in the warehouses answers to his tone.

### Weaknesses

His cousins can overrule him, and he flinches when a prince's man mentions the mint.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A witness for the audit.** The port-warden's clerks are to audit the house's tolls, and Sulvan wants a witness to the count who is not one of his cousins.
2. **A promise of grain.** A buyer is asking for grain the house has already promised elsewhere, and Sulvan wants a party to find out which of the buyers is lying.
