# Appearance {#appearance}

The Headwoman beneath Sedrovod is a woman of the village under the fort's wall, exact in what she says and frightened for the man taken from the bell hill.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Headwoman of the village beneath the fort. Wants the village's coin counted out before witnesses.**

The Headwoman beneath Sedrovod speaks for the village under [[place-sdrvdfrt|Sedrovod Fort]]. She writes every household's share of a ransom on a tally-stick in front of the village, and she asks the broker who carries the purse whether it is enough.

## Background

**Formation.** She speaks for the village under the fort's wall, whose households each gave to the purse.

**The office.** She keeps the tally-stick, the village's only record of what each household gave.

**Now.** She has emptied the village's chests to raise the price of a man taken from the watch.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is exact and frightened, and she asks a plain question and waits for a plain answer.

### Motivation

She wants the man home and the village's coin accounted for.

### Strengths

She knows what every household gave and can say it from the stick.

### Weaknesses

The village has never seen a ransom paid, and she does not know how a price is named or lowered.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]]**: her village lies under one of its forts and sends its men to the watch in turn.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Witnesses for the count.** She wants strangers to watch the coin counted out at the ford, since the village has never seen a ransom paid.
2. **The tally-stick.** She holds the only figure that can be set against the purse a broker carries, and she lends it to a party that asks.
3. **The warden's coat.** She asks why a villager was wearing the fort's coat when the war-band took him.
