# Appearance {#appearance}

Gorena Milvanich is a 51-year-old woman who stands 5' 5" tall and is of light build. She has a heart-shaped face, a straight nose, arched brows, smooth olive skin, coily graying black hair and hazel eyes that take in a patient's color before his words.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 51. Infirmarian of the great house at [[place-zhtvsh|Zhitavesh]]. Wants every sick-bed she keeps to stay a bed and not a message.**

Gorena Milvanich keeps the sick-beds of the great granary house on the market square of Zhitavesh, under the starbrat [[being-rdrbskvnch|Radora Boskvanich]]. She sets bones, lances boils and sits up with fevers, and she treats the poor, the riders of the prince's hall and the Hosts' wounded in the same row of beds without asking whose they are. The guest barn sends her its sick, and the town's inns send her what they cannot stand to keep.

:::secret {title="A rider's fall"}
A rider of the prince's retinue came to her with a sword-cut the captain of the retinue swore was a fall from a horse. She wrote the true wound in her sick-book and has kept the page, and she has shown it to no one.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** She was a midwife's daughter from the lowland villages and came to the house at nineteen with a bag of herbs and no letters.

**The sick-beds.** The monks taught her to read and to keep a sick-book, and she took the infirmary at thirty-six, when its old keeper could not climb the stairs.

**Now.** She keeps the beds, the herb-loft and a book in which every patient is written down, with the day and the cause.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is brisk, kind in deeds and sparing in words, and she scolds a bad bandage before she asks the patient's name.

### Motivation

She wants the house's sick-beds to stay neutral ground, so that no prince and no Host can say the monks took a side.

### Strengths

Her hands are sure, her herb-loft covers every ordinary fever, and no hall rider or Host rider will refuse her word.

### Weaknesses

She is stretched thin in a winter fever, and she will not leave a fever bed for a summons, whoever sends it.
