# Appearance {#appearance}

Duvek Radovodich is a 53-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. He has a heart-shaped face, a narrow nose, thin brows, fair freckled skin, short graying brown hair, hazel eyes and a clean-shaven chin. His left forefinger is crooked.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 53. Keeper of the Princely Granary. Wants an outsider to count the monastery's barn.**

Duvek Radovodich answers to the prince for every sack in the princely granary at [[place-zhtvsh|Zhitavesh]] and counts the stores against what the monasteries hold. He has twice been refused entry to a barn, and he cannot give the prince a figure he has not seen.

:::secret {title="The short tally"}
His own tally comes out a tenth under the barn's true stores every year, which he has covered from his own stores to keep the prince from asking. He cannot say where the grain goes, and he fears it is the barn's.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He is a miller's son and counted sacks into the barns at ten. By twenty he could name a barn's weight by the sag of its floor.

**The office.** The prince made him keeper at thirty-six, when the old keeper's count was found short.

**Now.** He holds the keys and the tally-rods and answers for the stores.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is anxious, exact and kind to every sack, and he repeats a figure until he is sure of it.

### Motivation

He wants the count true and the prince told the truth of it.

### Strengths

He knows every barn of the lowland and the miller of each.

### Weaknesses

He is not a bold man, and the monks know it.
