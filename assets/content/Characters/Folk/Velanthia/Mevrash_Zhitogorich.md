# Appearance {#appearance}

Mevrash Zhitogorich is a 34-year-old man who stands 5' 8" tall and is of light build. He has an oval face, an aquiline nose, arched brows, clear olive skin, straight black hair, a short beard and dark brown eyes that follow a wagon until its wheels leave the square.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 34. Agent of the port houses at the south gate of Tveskavesh. Wants his house's list of the southbound wagons to be the one the port reads.**

Mevrash Zhitogorich sits on a bench by the south gate of [[place-tvskvsh|Tveskavesh]] with a tally-board on his knee and counts the wagons that go through, with their teams, their loads and the names painted on their sides. He is a factor of the [[affiliation-mrchnthsrvr|Merchant Houses of the Rivers]], sent by a port house to learn how much grain goes south that the port does not see, and he pays the carters a fair price for the lists they would rather not give. The reeve, [[being-tvsknsdrgrch|Tveskana Sedrogorich]], tolerates him because he pays the gate's clerks on time.

:::secret {title="The monks' wagons"}
His house believes his list covers every wagon that goes south. It leaves out the monastic wagons, because a carter in the lane behind the south gate pays him a silver a month to look the other way.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He was a clerk in a port house's warehouse at fifteen and learned to read a ledger for the lie in it before he could read it for the sum.

**The bench at the gate.** The house sent him to Tveskavesh at twenty-six, with a bench, a tally-board and the order to find out where the grain was going.

**Now.** He knows every teamster by his wagon's squeak and is trusted by few of them.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is pleasant, patient and curious in a way that makes people tell him more than they meant to.

### Motivation

He wants a place in the port house itself, in the room where the ledgers are kept, and not on a bench in the cold.

### Strengths

He never forgets a wagon, and the gate's clerks and the stable-hands treat him as one of their own.

### Weaknesses

He is owed his own house's gratitude and has never been given it, and he is easier to buy than he looks.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-mrchnthsrvr|Merchant Houses of the Rivers]]**: Factor, who counts the southbound wagons for his house.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The list for sale.** Mevrash offers a fair price for the number of wagons that crossed the south gate in the last month, and he wants a party that can get it from the stable-yards without the reeve's clerks noticing.
2. **The same wagon twice.** A wagon he counted in the morning crosses the gate again at night with a different load, and he wants a party to find out where the first load went.
