# Appearance {#appearance}

Teshodar Sulvanich is a 57-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of heavy build. He has a round face, a broad nose, arched brows, smooth olive skin, curly graying black hair, a short beard and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 57. Master of the Houses. Wants the port houses and the grain-town houses to agree the harvest price before the rains.**

Teshodar Sulvanich presides over the assembly of the [[affiliation-mrchnthsrvr|Merchant Houses of the Rivers]] at [[place-brlvsh|Brelavesh]] and speaks for the houses to the princes. His house lends to every prince, and the houses' credit is the harvest.

:::secret {title="The pledged paper"}
His house holds the paper on the lowland prince's pledged tithe, bought through a grain-town house. He knows a short harvest leaves [[being-zhtvddbrvdch|Zhitovod Dobrovodich]] owing grain he lacks, and he has told neither the assembly nor the prince.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He began at fourteen as a clerk in his uncle's counting-room and learned the landings' weights before the boats' names.

**The office.** He became head of his house at forty. The assembly deadlocked over the grain towns' credit, and at fifty it chose him, the one head both sides could bear to see preside.

**Now.** He settles debts between houses and lends to every prince.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is genial, patient and never the first to name a figure.

### Motivation

He wants the quarrel ended by the assembly and no prince's hand on the houses' credit.

### Strengths

He knows what every house owes and is owed, and both sides trust his arithmetic.

### Weaknesses

Both sides watch for the day he favors the other, and his own house's paper makes him a party.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The price before the rains.** Teshodar wants a party to carry a sealed offer to the grain-town houses without the port houses learning its terms.
2. **The audit.** A house asks him to name a witness to the port-warden's audit, and he wants a clerk who owes nothing to any house.
