# Appearance {#appearance}

The Captive of the Bell Hill is a man of the village under [[place-sdrvdfrt|Sedrovod Fort]], held with his hands untied in the fort's coat.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Villager taken from the watch. Wants to go home before the last boat.**

The Captive of the Bell Hill earns a wage in a warden's coat as the village's man on the watch. A war-band took him from the path below the bell hill, and at the ford of Yaravesh he is priced as a warden.

## Background

**Formation.** He lives in the village beneath Sedrovod Fort, where each household sends a man to the watch in turn.

**The post.** He stood the post below the bell hill, alone, wearing the fort's coat.

**Now.** He waits on the far bank of the ford beside the go-between, and his mother has given her cow's price to the purse.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is a plain village man who does not know why his price is high.

### Motivation

He wants to go home.

### Strengths

He is the one person who knows what the war-band asked him on the way.

### Weaknesses

The coat makes him a warden in the war-band's eyes, and he cannot take it off.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]]**: held as a warden by the war-band and as a villager by his village.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The price of a coat.** He learns at the ford that he is priced as a warden, and a party that tells the go-between he is a villager lowers his price.
2. **What he saw.** He can say how long the band watched the post and what it asked him on the trail.
3. **The last boat.** If the price is not paid before the first ice, he is among the captives loaded for the journey down the river.
