# Appearance {#appearance}

Belukan Kelodov is a 61-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of light build. He has an angular face, an aquiline nose, heavy brows, leathery tanned skin, black hair going gray with the sides shaved, a long beard and brown eyes. A finger of his left hand is missing.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 61. Ataman of the Khorad Host. Wants the river communities' toll for his Host and the prince's arrears paid.**

Belukan Kelodov commands the riders of the [[affiliation-hstkhrd|Khorad Host]], presides at its council of clan heads and treats with the steppe tribes for the prince of [[place-zhtvsh|Zhitavesh]], whom the Host serves for pay. He keeps his seat at [[place-krdshv|Krodashov]], where the Host's crossbred horses are bred.

:::secret {title="The guest-son"}
Belukan's younger son lives in a steppe camp as the tribe's guest, which keeps him from riding past the river to enforce the toll he demands of the communities. He tells no clan head where the boy is.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He is the son of the last ataman, of the founding line, and carried the Host's gifts across the frontier as its envoy to the tribes from thirty.

**The office.** The clan heads accepted him at forty, after his father's death, and the tribes knew his face already.

**Now.** He tells the communities to stop paying the tribes and sends riders to say so.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is dry, patient and courteous, and he reads a rider's silence as carefully as his words.

### Motivation

He wants the toll for himself and the Host's pay in full, without a quarrel the prince will not settle.

### Strengths

The tribes trust his word, and his herds are the best on the margin.

### Weaknesses

Each side watches which way he turns, and he cannot turn without cost.
