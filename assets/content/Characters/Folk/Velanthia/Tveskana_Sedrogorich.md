# Appearance {#appearance}

Tveskana Sedrogorich is a 41-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build. She has an oval face, an upturned nose, arched brows, light freckled skin, a long brown braid and green eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 41. Reeve of the Pass Market. Wants every wagon through her gate in her book.**

Tveskana Sedrogorich tolls the wagons that pass the south gate of [[place-tvskvsh|Tveskavesh]] and the order of its stables, for the prince of Zhitavesh. She answers to the captain of the retinue at the seat and keeps a small guest room for travelers with the prince's letter.

:::secret {title="The monks' wagons"}
The monastic wagons pay no toll by an old grant, so she keeps a private count of them, and it grows every year past the grain the monasteries admit to holding. She has shown it to no one, because the captain would show it to the prince.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** She was a stable-yard girl at the pass market and learned the toll by watching the old reeve count wagons.

**The office.** The prince made her reeve at thirty-three, when the old reeve's eyes failed.

**Now.** She holds the gate by her book and rents the stable lofts to those who hire teams.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is brisk, fair and unimpressed by rank, and she remembers every wagon.

### Motivation

She wants a true book and no wagon past her gate unseen.

### Strengths

She can tell a loaded wagon from an empty one by the axle's sound.

### Weaknesses

The port's agents count her wagons from the square, and she cannot dismiss them.

1. **The escort.** A wagon-master with monastic grain needs an escort over the pass that does not report to her, and she asks to know whose it is.
