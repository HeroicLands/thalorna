# Appearance {#appearance}

Torvik Mevranich is a 45-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He has a round face, a snub nose, straight brows, ruddy olive skin, wavy black hair, a moustache and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 45. Port-Warden of Brelavesh. Wants every house's toll to match its cargo ledger.**

Torvik Mevranich keeps the landings and the order of the quays of [[place-brlvsh|Brelavesh]] through the tally-clerks who meet every boat. He audits the sums the [[affiliation-mrchnthsrvr|Merchant Houses of the Rivers]] pay in tolls, which the houses resent.

:::secret {title="The short sums"}
Two houses are short by the same fraction every quarter, which means they share a false ledger. Torvik has told no one, because the prince would raise the toll on every house, and a raised toll ends his audit's quiet.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He was a tally-clerk on the quays at fifteen and learned the houses' marks by the bales they stamped.

**The office.** The prince made him port-warden at thirty-eight, after Torvik's tally caught a boat that had landed twice.

**Now.** He holds the quays by ledger and has not drawn a sword in his office.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is neat, patient and fair-spoken, and he writes down what is said to him.

### Motivation

He wants the quays run by ledger and the tolls fair across every house.

### Strengths

He can read a bale's weight by eye, and every landing clerk answers to him.

### Weaknesses

He has no soldiers, and the houses can refuse his audit.

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **The audit.** A merchant house wants a witness to Torvik's count, and Torvik wants the witness to bring a clerk of its own.
2. **A boat landed twice.** A barge's tally matches no ledger on the quay, and Torvik wants a party to find which house it belongs to.
