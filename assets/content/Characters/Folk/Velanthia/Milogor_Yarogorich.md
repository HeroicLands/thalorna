# Appearance {#appearance}

Milogor Yarogorich is a 49-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. He has an angular face, an aquiline nose, arched brows, smooth olive skin, straight graying black hair, a goatee and grey eyes. The tip of his left little finger is missing.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 49. Prince of Brelavesh. Wants a larger share of the grain trade for his own barges and the tolls his to set.**

Milogor Yarogorich holds [[place-brlvsh|Brelavesh]], its landings, its tolls and its mint by descent. The [[affiliation-mrchnthsrvr|Merchant Houses of the Rivers]] pay his toll as a sum and not by the load, and he lives on it. He has begun to buy mills upriver, which the lowland prince reads as a threat.

:::secret {title="The caravan share"}
Milogor has promised the Tarvál ataman a fixed share of the port's toll on the eastern caravans, in writing, for three years. He has kept the paper from the houses, who would call it a gift out of their tolls.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He is the second son of the port house and grew up on the quays. At fourteen he was set to the keeper of the mint to learn the weight of silver.

**The office.** His elder brother drowned at the landing, and Milogor became prince at thirty-one with the port-warden's oath and the houses' credit.

**Now.** He rules by tolls and a mint, with riders on the walls and few soldiers beyond them.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is smooth, exact and never hurried, and he raises a toll in the same tone he lowers one.

### Motivation

He wants the port the richest place in Velanthia and the toll no one's to fix but his.

### Strengths

He knows what every house owes and what its boats carry.

### Weaknesses

The port has the fewest soldiers in the land, and its credit is the houses' and not his.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The quarter's silver.** The coin for the wardens of Brevavesh waits at the mint, and Milogor wants it escorted up the river road without a word to the houses.
2. **The mills upriver.** He wants a party to report what the lowland prince's millers say of his purchases.
