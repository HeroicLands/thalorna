# Appearance {#appearance}

Kodvan Teshogorich is a 47-year-old man who stands 5' 11" tall and is of medium build. He has an angular face, a straight nose, heavy brows, weathered wrinkled tanned skin, straight gray hair, a short beard and blue eyes that sweep a ridgeline as other men sweep a table.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 47. Warden of the Passes of the Sevret Host. Wants a rider on every pass post on the day the pay is due.**

Kodvan Teshogorich is the clan head of the [[affiliation-hstsvrt|Sevret Host]] who keeps the patrol rosters of the pass roads and the signals between the posts, in the ataman's hall at [[place-tvsknv|Tveskanov]]. He knows which rider is on which post on any day of the season and which rider has been owed pay the longest. The ataman, [[being-svdnvlktch|Sovidan Volketich]], decides where the Host's riders will stand, and Kodvan makes it so. The posts hold the passes to the grain road, and the Host takes its toll there if the harvest pay is late.

## Background

**Formation.** He rode the passes as an escort at fifteen, guarding the grain wagons that came up from the lowlands, and learned every post by a night spent in it.

**The roster.** The Host made him warden of the passes at forty-five, after he led the patrol that held the upper post through a blizzard that killed two escorts.

**Now.** He holds the roster, the signal codes of the posts and a clan of riders who would follow him if the ataman asked them to.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is slow-spoken, dry and calm in bad weather, and he writes everything down on the roster before he speaks of it.

### Motivation

He wants the passes held and the riders paid, and he will say which of the two he gave up first if the prince pays late.

### Strengths

He reads weather, snow and tracks without error, and the riders of every post answer to his signal.

### Weaknesses

He has no patience for the lowland court and lets the reeve of the pass market know it.
