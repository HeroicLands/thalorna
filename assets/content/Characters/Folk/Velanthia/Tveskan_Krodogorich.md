# Appearance {#appearance}

Tveskan Krodogorich is a 30-year-old man who stands 5' 7" tall and is of medium build. He has a round face, a snub nose, bushy brows, ruddy fair skin, curly auburn hair, a short beard and amber eyes that go to the fur before they go to the seller.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 30. Buyer of a merchant house in the sheds at Radavesh. Wants the best pelts of the season sold to his house before they reach the port.**

Tveskan Krodogorich buys for a house of the [[affiliation-mrchnthsrvr|Merchant Houses of the Rivers]] in the fur sheds at [[place-rdvsh|Radavesh]]. The fur boats of [[place-vrystwald|Vristwald]] tie up at the landing, and he meets each one with his rod and his scales, grades the bales by the hair and pays the boatwomen in silver by the bale. A boat's mistress who sells to him sells at once and sails home without carrying silver through the port's tolls.

:::secret {title="The grade"}
He grades the boatwomen's best pelts one grade low, tells them the port pays no more and sells them on to his house at the higher grade, and the difference is his.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He was a tanner's son from the landing and learned the hair of a pelt from the hide-yard, before the house offered him a stool in the sheds.

**The sheds.** He became the house's buyer at twenty-seven, after the old buyer died of a fever that went through the sheds one winter.

**Now.** He holds the sheds' best stool and a name among the boatwomen as a man who pays on the spot.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is cheerful, round-voiced and generous with ale, and he remembers the name of every boatwoman's children.

### Motivation

He wants a house of his own on the quays, and every grade of pelt is a step toward the price.

### Strengths

He can grade a bale by touch in the dark, and every boatwoman on the river knows his stool.

### Weaknesses

He cannot refuse a profit, and his grading is only as good as the boatwomen's trust in it.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-mrchnthsrvr|Merchant Houses of the Rivers]]**: Factor, who buys the fur-boats' bales for his house.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A short bale.** A boatwoman of Vristwald wants a party to find out why the port paid her partner more for the same pelts, and Tveskan wants the party to stay at the landing instead.
2. **The best of the winter.** A fur hunter has pelts he wants graded by someone Tveskan will not have bribed, and Tveskan wants to be that someone.
