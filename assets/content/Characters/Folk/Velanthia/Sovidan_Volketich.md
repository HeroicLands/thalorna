# Appearance {#appearance}

Sovidan Volketich is a 38-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of light build. He has an angular face, thin brows, sun-browned tawny skin, wavy dark brown hair that reaches his shoulders and hazel eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 38. Ataman of the Sevret Host. Wants the harvest pay in his riders' hands before the pass tolls tempt them.**

Sovidan Volketich commands the riders of the [[affiliation-hstsvrt|Sevret Host]], presides at its council of clan heads and speaks for it to the prince of Zhitavesh, whom the Host serves for pay in grain and silver. His seat is the muster town of [[place-tvsknv|Tveskanov]], and the pass roads above [[place-tvskvsh|Tveskavesh]] are his work.

## Background

**Formation.** He is the son of the last ataman, of the founding line, and rode the pass patrols from fourteen.

**The office.** The clan heads accepted him at twenty-nine, when his father fell from a horse on the scree road, and he is the youngest ataman of the three Hosts.

**Now.** He holds the pass roads, rides escort for the drovers and counts the days to harvest.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is quick, plain-spoken and impatient with princes, and he keeps his word to a drover as he keeps it to a clan head.

### Motivation

He wants his riders paid on the day it is promised, so that none collects a toll for himself.

### Strengths

He knows every post and signal on the roads, and his riders will follow him anywhere on them.

### Weaknesses

He is young among older clan heads, and a late harvest would show it.

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **The escort.** A drover's party wants a rider on the pass road who is not on the Warden of the Passes' roster, and Sovidan lends one for a price in grain.
2. **The burned post.** A rider has found a signal post burned on the northern side, and Sovidan wants it looked at before his council hears.
