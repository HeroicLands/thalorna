# Appearance {#appearance}

The Starbrat of the Ferry Granary is the head of the house on the river, in the monks' dress, who keeps the hours of the long table.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Starbrat of the Ferry Granary. Refuses a count and an advance on a bare word.**

The Starbrat of the Ferry Granary is chosen by the monks of [[place-frrygrnry|Ferry Granary]] and answers to no abbot of another house. She gives a count of the barn to nobody on a bare word.

:::secret {title="What the barn holds"}
The barn is nearly empty. The house lent most of its reserve to the [[affiliation-mrchnthsrvr|Merchant Houses of the Rivers]] and has advanced grain to the Sevret Host on the same terms, so the grain it is owed sits in the merchants' warehouses. She refuses the keeper a count because a count would show the heads of the houses and the Host that the reserve is gone, and she refuses the ataman an advance because there is little left to advance.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** She was chosen by the monks of the house.

**The office.** She keeps the house's ledgers through its scribe and answers for the barn.

**Now.** She refuses the ataman an advance on his bare word, refuses the prince's keeper a count, and has sent for the starbrat at Zhitavesh to hear both.

## Psyche

### Personality

She gives neither man what he asks on his word.

### Motivation

She wants the house's reserve left in her own keeping until the next harvest.

### Strengths

The house's ferry and long table give her a hearing from every wagon train.

### Weaknesses

She answers a hard request with a delay, and both men leave the table unsatisfied.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-grnrymnstrs|Granary Monasteries of Lizelas]]**: starbrat of the Ferry Granary, answering to no abbot of another house.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The count.** She has refused the prince's keeper twice, and a witness he trusts may change her answer.
2. **The advance.** The ataman wants grain against the pay the Sevret Host is owed, and she has refused him on his word.
3. **The two men at the table.** The keeper and the ataman arrive the same night, and she sends for the starbrat at Zhitavesh.
