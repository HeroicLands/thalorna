# Appearance {#appearance}

Radora Boskvanich is a 66-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall and is of light build. She has an oval face, thin brows, fair wrinkled skin, cropped white hair and gray eyes that rest on a speaker until he stops.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 66. Starbrat of the great granary at Zhitavesh. Wants the reserve left uncounted and the princes and Hosts kept from each other's throats.**

Radora Boskvanich heads the great house of the [[affiliation-grnrymnstrs|Granary Monasteries of Lizelas]] on the market square of [[place-zhtvsh|Zhitavesh]], chosen by her monks. She keeps the hours of the [[affiliation-lizelas|Faith of Lizelas]], answers to no temple priest, and is the mediator the princes and the Hosts both send to.

:::secret {title="The true reserve"}
The barn holds enough to feed the lowland villages through two bad years, not one. She keeps the figure from the prince, who would tax it, and from the heads' meeting, where a head would borrow it.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** She is the second daughter of a lowland hall, given to the house at twelve. She learned letters there and copied the customary law for the hall's judges.

**The office.** Her monks chose her starbrat at forty-nine, in the winter the villages ate from the barn.

**Now.** She sits in the heads' meeting and holds the barn that feeds the lowlands.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is mild, exact and immovable, and she answers a prince's question with a question of her own.

### Motivation

She wants the barns full when the villages are hungry and no prince's price on any sack.

### Strengths

The villages trust the house, and both Hosts accept her word as a mediator's.

### Weaknesses

She trusts no outsider with a count, and a famine would test that.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-grnrymnstrs|Granary Monasteries of Lizelas]]**: Starbrat of the great house, speaking for the network as far as the other heads allow.
- **[[affiliation-lizelas|Faith of Lizelas]]**: She keeps its feasts as a layperson of the faith.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The guest barn.** The house asks a party that has eaten at its table to carry a sealed letter to the starbrat of Dobravesh before the rains.
2. **The condition.** Radora will admit an outsider to count the barn only if the count is sealed and given to her alone, and goes neither to the heads' meeting nor to the prince.
