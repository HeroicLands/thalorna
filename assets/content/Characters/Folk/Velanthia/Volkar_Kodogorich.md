# Appearance {#appearance}

Volkar Kodogorich is a 47-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of medium build. He has a long face, a hooked nose, bushy brows, weathered tanned skin, braided black hair, a moustache and amber eyes. A scar runs across the back of his left hand.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 47. Ataman of the [[affiliation-hsttrvl|Tarvál Host]]. Wants autonomy, the Host's pay and the right to what the roads yield.**

Volkar Kodogorich commands the riders of the Tarvál Host, presides at its council of clan heads and speaks for it to the prince of [[place-brlvsh|Brelavesh]], whom the Host serves for pay. He lives at [[place-vlkvsh|Volkavesh]] and carries the battle-shrine to the gathering ground.

:::secret {title="Two offers"}
Volkar holds a written promise from [[being-mlgryrgrch|Milogor Yarogorich]] of a fixed share of the caravan toll, and a larger standing offer from a steppe tribe that shares his roads. He has answered neither, and he will take the one that is larger on the day the pay falls late.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He is the son of the last ataman, of the founding line, and learned the roads riding escort for his father's caravans from fourteen.

**The office.** The clan heads accepted him in council at thirty-one, when his father died, and the Host's law holds the office in the line.

**Now.** He holds the toll posts on the eastern roads and takes what the caravans owe.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is easy, watchful and slow to promise, and he laughs at a prince's offers.

### Motivation

He wants the Host paid more each year and free to leave.

### Strengths

The clan heads follow him, and he knows every post and well on the roads.

### Weaknesses

His pay comes from one prince, and the tribes' offers keep him owing a favor to both sides.

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **A late caravan.** Volkar wants a party to find out why a caravan of salt and silver has not reached a toll post.
2. **The prince's envoy.** An envoy from the port is feasted at Volkavesh, and Volkar wants a stranger at the feast to tell him afterward what the envoy offered the clan heads aside.
