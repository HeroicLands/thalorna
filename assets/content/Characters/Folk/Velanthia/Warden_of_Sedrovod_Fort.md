# Appearance {#appearance}

The Warden of Sedrovod Fort is a tired man in the fort's coat, with a watchman's boots and the barracks' keys at his belt.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Paid warden of Sedrovod Fort. Wants nobody to ask who was wearing the coat.**

The Warden of Sedrovod Fort holds the fort on the forest road for the prince of Brevavesh. The post below the bell hill was held by a villager in a warden's coat.

:::secret {title="The roll and the barracks"}
His roll names a paid warden at the post below the bell hill, and that warden is not in the barracks; the captain of the forts keeps the same gap across the prince's wardens in his second roll. The wages go to the bell-ringers, cooks and village watches beneath the fort, and the villager at the bell hill stood the paid warden's post. A question about the coat uncovers the gap between the roll and the barracks, and he cannot defend the roll if it is read closely.
:::

## Background

**Formation.** He is a paid warden of the prince's forts.

**The office.** He answers to the captain of the forts and keeps the garrison's watch.

**Now.** A war-band has taken a man from his watch, and he answers questions about the coat with care.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is tired and guarded, and he answers a hard question with a true statement that is not an answer.

### Motivation

He wants the matter kept out of the upper hall.

### Strengths

He knows the forest road and the posts along it.

### Weaknesses

He answers a question about his roll with care.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-prncdmbrvvsh|Princedom of Brevavesh]]**: a paid warden of its forts, answering to the captain of the forts.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The question of the coat.** He says that the village sends a man to the watch in turn, and a party that presses him learns only that he is guarded.
2. **The post below the bell hill.** He wants a party to see what the war-band watched for several nights before it moved.
3. **The quarter's rolls.** He wants the matter settled before the rolls go up to the prince.
