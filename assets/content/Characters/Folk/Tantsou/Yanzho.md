# Appearance {#appearance}

Nobody outside the enclave sees Yanzho. The city knows him as a name in the chancellery's newest entries and as a household behind the wall on the island, where only the Zhazho and their attendants go. There he is a 61-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of medium build, with the look of the Zhazho: nails grown almost impossibly long and lacquered in brilliant colors, a painted face and painted teeth.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Yanzho (Cloud Dragon) is the Retired Emperor of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]. He held the [[lore-mandatehvn|Mandate of Heaven]] and withdrew from the throne, and the young Emperor [[being-tengrauemp|Tengrau]] reigns in his place. He lives inside the walled enclave of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]] at [[place-zhowen|Zhowen]], in the background of the court.

## The Withdrawal

**The newest entries.** The chancellery reckons the years by the reigning Emperor, and each reign is its own era. Yanzho's withdrawal and the young Emperor's accession are the newest entries in the record, and they opened the era the chancellery is still cross-referencing to the old one.

**The background.** He holds no office in the empire's governance. The Princes press their claims on the young Emperor, not on him, and the court's game turns around the throne and not around its former holder.

## Psyche

### Personality

He keeps to the enclave and to the background, and he does not put himself forward at court.

### Motivation

He wants the Mandate to stay with the throne he left, and the reign that follows his to hold.

### Strengths

He is of the first caste, and he knows the enclave, the Princes and the Speaker's door from the throne itself.

### Weaknesses

He holds no office. What he once decreed is filed under an era that is closed.

## Social

### Court

- [[being-tengrauemp|Tengrau]]: the young Emperor, who reigns in the era his withdrawal opened.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]**: of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]], the first caste, without office.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The old era's filing.** A petition granted under Yanzho's reign waits in the chancellery while its clerks cross-reference it to the new era, and the household that holds it asks a party to learn whether the grant still stands.
2. **The quiet household.** A Prince's officer wants to know which of the other Princes has called on the Retired Emperor this season. Only the Zhazho and their attendants enter the enclave, and an attendant who crosses back to the shore can be asked.
3. **The two eras.** A Tanchei of the chancellery has found a filing entered under both eras at once, and wants a party to carry the question of which era holds it to an office that will answer.
