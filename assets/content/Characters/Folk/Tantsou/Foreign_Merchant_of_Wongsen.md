# Appearance {#appearance}

The merchant is a 46-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of medium build. He wears a traveler's coat and boots, carries a silk purse, and keeps the tablet he was issued in his hand and not in his sleeve.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The merchant is a trader of a country beyond the borders of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] who has come to [[place-wongsen|Wongsen]] to sell. The registers know him by the description his tablet carries, First Son of the Merchant of No Clan, from Beyond the Border, which names neither a clan nor a country. The commercial administration issued him the _Shwonong Dou Chou_, the merchant transit document, with a tablet to carry, and the province enters him as a Foreign Resident, kept in [[place-wongsenfrgnqrtr|The Foreign Quarter of Wongsen]]. His goods wait in a warehouse of the quarter, documented and taxed, for the [[lore-shuonong|Shwonong]] factors who buy them. His classification is good for the length of his business, and it runs out in days.

## What the Date Means

**The morning after.** A foreigner without classification cannot own property, sign a contract or seek the law's protection. A merchant whose classification runs out is that foreigner the morning after, and his goods stay in the warehouse when he cannot.

**The price.** The factors who buy his goods know the date on his tablet, and the price they name falls as the date comes nearer.

:::secret {title="The back court"}
He has been to the court behind the warehouses already and heard the price of a tablet no bureau issued. He has not bought it, and he does not say that he asked.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

He is exact about his accounts, courteous to officers and short with anyone who wastes a day.

### Motivation

He wants his goods sold or moved and his standing kept, and he wants it done in a way the law can read.

### Strengths

He knows what his goods are worth and what the tax on them is.

### Weaknesses

The date on his tablet is known to everyone who buys from him, and he has nothing to bargain with but time he does not have.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

- **[[affiliation-wongsenprv|Wongsen Province]]**: Foreign Resident, kept in the walled quarter of the port.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-tbltwrnghnd|A Tablet from the Wrong Hand]]: his classification runs out at sunset on the fourth day with his goods still in a warehouse, and he asks the people the law can see to help him.
2. **The buyer's price.** The Shwonong factor [[being-shwnngfctrwng|First Daughter of the Grain-Factor of the Clan of Scales at the Open Door, near Wongsen]] offers less each day, and the merchant wants a party to find a second buyer before the date.
3. **The extension.** The Warden's office has taken his request for an extension, and he wants a party to learn which desk of the commercial administration holds it.
