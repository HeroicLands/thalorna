# Appearance {#appearance}

The headman of Hachai is the man the villagers look to when a stranger climbs into the fold of the ridge. Strangers are looked over before they are fed, and he is the one who decides whether they are fed at all.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The headman leads [[place-hachai|Hachai]], a village of about 180 people in a fold of the ridge in the high country of [[place-mwonouplnd|the Mwonou Uplands]], a long day's climb above the uppermost terraces, where no road reaches. The village lives on millet, goats and the trade of the herders who cross the pass. It has no shrine in the lowland style, no registrar and no magistrate.

## The Rule

:::secret {title="Who lives in Hachai"}
**The struck households.** Every household of Hachai is struck from the register: outlaws who fled a sentence, and [[lore-noutsou|Nwitsou]] who escaped their owners. [[place-jenshouhall|The District Hall of Jenshou]] has no page for the village, so no tribute, levy or tablet is owed for it. The headman is struck from the register like every household he leads.
:::

**The first rule.** He sets the village's rule himself, and the first rule is that nobody leaves to be counted.

:::secret {title="How Hachai could be found"}
**The market.** One household sends a boy down to trade at the Weichai market and takes the risk for the village. A year in which the register is checked against the land is the year Hachai is found, and a magistrate who learns of the place can sell his silence or make his name.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

He is wary, slow to trust and hard on anyone who puts the village at risk.

### Motivation

He wants Hachai left alone in its fold of the ridge.

### Strengths

The village obeys him, and the ridge hides it.

### Weaknesses

The village has one road to the lowlands, a boy sent down to the Weichai market, and whatever the boy says there reaches farther than the headman can see.

## Social

### Affiliations

:::secret {title="Who lives in Hachai"}
On the rolls of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] he is an outlaw, struck from the register.
:::

## Plot Hooks

1. **The boy at the market.** The boy Hachai sends down to the Weichai market has not come back, and the headman wants a party to learn what became of him.

:::secret {title="What the headman fears"}

2. **The count.** Word comes up the pass that the register is to be checked against the land this year, and the headman wants to know which district's clerks will walk the high country, and when.
3. **The magistrate's price.** A magistrate has learned of the village and named a price for his silence. The headman wants a party to learn whether the magistrate has told anyone else before he pays.

:::
