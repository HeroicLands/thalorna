# Appearance {#appearance}

Rinzho has the look of the Zhazho: nails grown almost impossibly long and lacquered in brilliant colors, a painted face and painted teeth. He is seen at Wongsen as the other Princes see him at court: as the Prince whose granaries are full. His factors weigh rice on the quays of the delta port under his seal, and the barges that carry it to the capital travel under the same seal.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Rinzho (Red Dragon) is the Prince of [[affiliation-wongsenprv|Wongsen Province]], the richest of the four provinces of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], a _Zho Zhei_ of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]] who holds the delta and the coasts for the Emperor with their armies and sorcerers. He rules from the port city of [[place-wongsen|Wongsen]], and he is the strongest of the four Princes and the most feared.

## Water and Rice

**The Wandwo.** Among his [[lore-wanduo|Wandwo]] is a gifted set with an affinity for water. The rain they call falls on the delta and its coast and has raised his harvests while the rain fails in other provinces.

**The granaries.** The other Princes come to his granaries to buy rice, and the throne's granaries at [[place-zhowen|Zhowen]] buy from them too, at the price he names. His Keeper of the Granaries weighs and sells the rice the other provinces buy.

**The saying.** Some at court say his sorcerers are behind the drought in the other provinces. Nobody has proven it, and the saying is itself a move in [[lore-weizhairui|The Jade Fan Dance]] (_Wei Zhai Rwi_).

## Psyche

### Personality

He is patient and sure of his price. He lets the other Princes come to him.

### Motivation

He wants the throne to need his rice, and the young Emperor to be shaped by the Prince who feeds the capital.

### Strengths

He holds the richest province, the full granaries and the rain.

### Weaknesses

No advantage holds for long among the Princes. The saying about his sorcerers grows with every dry season, and a rain that falls only on his land is the reason the other three can unite against him.

## Social

### Rivals

- [[being-chengzhodlwn|Chengzho]]: the Prince of Doulwen, rising fast at the young Emperor's side, whose embassy Rinzho reads as a move against him.

### Others

- [[being-hozhomwnou|Hozho]]: the Prince of Mwonou, who buys Wongsen's rice for his famine country at Rinzho's price.
- [[being-tengrauemp|Tengrau]]: the young Emperor, whose granaries buy from his.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-wongsenprv|Wongsen Province]]**: Prince, holding the delta and the coasts for the Emperor.
- **[[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]**: of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]], the first caste.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The factor's barges.** Wongsen rice lies at a canal lock above Zhowen on a question of seals, and Rinzho's factor wants a party to get it released before the price on both shores moves any further.
2. **The proof.** A Tanchei of a dry district wants a party to bring back from the delta a reading of where the rain falls and where it does not, and the Prince's officers watch every Tanchei who comes asking.
3. **The price at Jaunou.** The magistrate of [[place-jaunou|Jaunou]] needs Wongsen rice for a starving town and cannot pay Rinzho's price, and he wants a party to carry an offer down the river to the Prince's granary.
