# Appearance {#appearance}

The Governor sits in his own hall in the administrative center of the shore, facing the island across the water, in the harness and robe of a [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] officer of the throne. The reports of the quays, the locks and the bridge feet come to his table, and he reads the seals on them before he reads the words.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Governor of Zhowen is known by his description: Eldest Son of the Gate Captain of the [Clan of the Heron on the Wall]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, near [[place-zhowen|Zhowen]]. He is the officer who holds the capital for the throne of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]: its two shores, their administrative centers and their caste quarters, everything outside the walls of the enclave of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]]. He answers to the throne alone.

## The City He Holds

**The court.** On the shores a case climbs from the street to the magistrate, and above the magistrate to the Governor's court, which in the capital takes the place a Prince's court holds in a province.

**The locks.** His officers keep the locks where the canals meet the river, and the quays where the barges unload. Rice from the granaries of Wongsen reaches the city in barges that his officers hold at the locks until the seals are checked, and a barge held there moves the price of rice on both shores.

**The bridges.** The officers at the foot of each bridge keep the day's list of those who hold written permission to cross to the island. A person who sets foot on a bridge without it is shot dead by the archers on the enclave walls, and no court hears the case.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is exact about seals and patient about delay, and he does not hurry a question that a delay will answer.

### Motivation

He wants the city held in order for the throne, and no Prince's hand on the locks or the quays.

### Strengths

The locks, the quays, the bridge lists and the highest court on the shores are his, and he answers to nobody but the throne.

### Weaknesses

Every barge he holds raises a price someone pays, and every Prince reads his delays as a move in the [[lore-weizhairui|Wei Zhai Rwi]].

## Plot Hooks

1. **The rice at the lock.** A string of rice barges from Wongsen lies at a canal lock above the city on a question of seals. Wongsen's factor wants them released, and a grain merchant who gains from every day they wait wants them held.
2. **The list.** A servant of a foreign merchant has vanished from the traders' compound, and the merchant believes he was sent to a bridge with a forged permission. The officers at the bridge foot keep the list, and they answer to the Governor.
3. **The appeal.** A household that lost its case before a magistrate of the shore wants it heard again in the Governor's court, and wants a party to find the witness the magistrate never called.
