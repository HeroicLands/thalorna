# Appearance {#appearance}

The captain is a 41-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He wears the lacquered scale of the soldiers he commands, with an officer's sword at his hip, and he stands at the place where the escort waits as if the place had been surveyed around him. A wooden tablet hangs on a cord at his belt, and he turns it to its seal for anyone who asks.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The captain of the escort is a [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] officer of [[affiliation-mwonouprv|Mwonou Province]] at the frontier way-station of [[place-kwisen|Kwisen]], known by his description: Third Son of the Gate Warden of the [Clan of Red Gate in Autumn]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, near [[place-mwonou|Mwonou]]. His soldiers meet every party that comes over [[place-jnanadvar|the Scholars' Door]] at the col and not a step sooner, and walk it down to the station.

## The Escort

**The post.** The escort stands at the place where it starts, and a party that wants it comes down to that place. He does not send his soldiers up the pass to a party in the weather.

**The road.** His soldiers walk ahead of a party in silence down a road of fitted stone, and on the third evening the station rises out of the pines.

**The gate.** At the gate his soldiers check the wooden tablet on a cord at every belt, turn it to its seal and hand it back.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is exact, silent on the road and entirely without deference to a foreigner, whatever the foreigner's rank.

### Motivation

He wants every party that comes down the pass delivered to the station's gate in the order the escort keeps, and none of it lost on the way.

### Strengths

He keeps his post and his orders exactly, and his soldiers keep both.

### Weaknesses

He cannot go a step above his post for any party, however it asks.

## Social

### Others

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-pgrgstrhld|A Page the Register Can Hold]]: weather shuts the col with the embassy above the place where the escort starts, and the captain does not bring his soldiers a step higher.
2. **The silent road.** A traveler who walked down behind the escort wants to know whether the soldiers can be asked anything at all, and what the captain answers when his men do not.
3. **The tablet at the gate.** A party arrives whose members carry no tablets, and the captain wants to know who is to answer for them at the gate.
