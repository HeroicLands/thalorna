# Appearance {#appearance}

He is a [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] officer in a soldier's harness, and he signs every paper put before him with the one name. The valley below Tengmou knows him by it, and a stranger who asks after the Zhowou Clan's soldiers is told it first.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Weng Hen Rau Chai, "Wind Tiger Roars Across Thunder Mountain", is the Tantsou's admired soldier. He was a Wohou officer known for years only as the Second Son of the Shield Bearer of the [Zhowou Clan]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, near [[place-tengmou|Tengmou]], a description a registry clerk could file and no more. He led his troops to a victory that saved a province, and his liege lord granted him the name for the fury of his charge. He has signed nothing else since.

## The Name

**The grant.** A personal name is an honor the empire grants for outstanding service, and only the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]], the Wohou and the [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] may receive one. His was conferred before witnesses and entered in the registry beside his old description, which is never struck out ([[lore-personlnms|Personal Names]]).

**The clan.** The Wohou of the Zhowou Clan near Tengmou send their sons to arms, and he is the son of theirs the empire knows.

**The cost.** The Tantsou admire in him a man of the third caste, neither Prince nor Emperor, who did the work his classification gave him so thoroughly that the court registers had to find room for it. Everything he does is read against the charge, and an officer who is only ever his best day is expected to produce it again.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is plain and dutiful, and he does not try to be more than the deed his name records.

### Motivation

He wants to do the work his classification gives him, and to be equal to the name when it is asked of him again.

### Strengths

He is a proven commander whose name every soldier of the empire knows.

### Weaknesses

His name is a single deed, and every new command is measured against it.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-mwonouprv|Mwonou Province]]**: an officer of the Wohou.
- **[[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]**: of the Wohou, the warrior nobility.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The charge again.** The garrisons of the northern marches need a commander whose name the soldiers already know, and a Prince's officer wants a party to carry the offer to Tengmou.
2. **The valley's sons.** The households of the Tengmou valley whose sons are with the garrisons are short of hands to clear the ditches, and they ask the Zhowou Clan's most famous son to speak for them.
3. **The other account.** A veteran of the battle that saved the province wants a party to carry his own account of the charge to Weng Hen Rau Chai, before it reaches anyone else.
