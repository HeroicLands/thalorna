# Appearance {#appearance}

Almost nobody sees Tengrau. A petitioner who crosses the bridge on his written permission waits in the gate-court outside the enclave wall and hears the Speaker give the Emperor's answer; the Emperor himself stays behind the wall, among his attendants. The Princes, who alone besides those attendants may speak to him, see a young man still learning how much of what he says will be obeyed.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tengrau (Heaven's Thunder) is the Emperor of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]], the _Tengzhei_ or Son of Heaven, who holds the [[lore-mandatehvn|Mandate of Heaven]] by cosmic appointment. He reigns from the palace on the island at [[place-zhowen|Zhowen]], inside the walled enclave of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]]. He is young and newly on the throne, and he is still finding the reach of his own power.

## The Reign

**The new era.** Each Emperor's reign is its own era of the calendar, and his accession opened a new one. The chancellery on the two shores is still cross-referencing every filing of the old era to his, and a filing that waits in that work can wait a year.

**The door.** Only his attendants and the Princes speak to him. Everyone else, a minister, a general or an ambassador, speaks to the Speaker, who whispers the question to him and speaks his answer aloud, and the answer the Speaker gives is the answer on the record.

**The counselor.** His chief counselor is the Caste-Registrar, [[being-cstrgstrtnts|Third Son of the Archivist of the Clan of Lanterns Along the Bank]], who keeps the rolls on which every subject's caste is entered.

**The Princes.** Each of the four Princes means to be the one who shapes him. [[being-chengzhodlwn|Chengzho]], the young reformer who holds Doulwen, has his ear. [[being-rinzhowngsn|Rinzho]] of Wongsen sells rice to the throne's granaries at the price he names. [[being-hozhomwnou|Hozho]] of Mwonou and [[being-tuizhokwzng|Tuizho]] of Kwozheng are bitter rivals, each glad of the other's loss.

**The embassy.** When the embassy of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]] asks for direct relations with the Empire, it asks for a door that has never existed, because a foreigner reaches the Emperor only through the Speaker. Granting it would shift power among the Princes, so [[lore-weizhairui|The Jade Fan Dance]] (_Wei Zhai Rwi_) decides it, and not the Emperor.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is young and watchful, and he listens to the Princes more than he answers them.

### Motivation

He wants the reach of his own power: to be the Emperor the Princes obey, and not the one they shape.

### Strengths

He holds the Mandate, and every final decree is his. The Princes can make and unmake emperors, but none of them can rule without him.

### Weaknesses

He is new to the throne, and he sees the empire only through his attendants, his Princes and the Speaker's whisper. Each Prince who comes to him comes wanting something.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The whisper.** A petitioner wants a question put to the Emperor in exactly the words he wrote, and the Speaker is the only one who carries it. A party is asked to learn what was whispered and what came back.
2. **The first audit.** At the Festival of Harmonious Lei the Emperor performs the year's audit of the empire's Lei, the first of his reign. Each Prince wants his province's accounts entered well, and the Tanchei who verify heaven's accounts are courted by all four.
3. **The pressed decree.** A Prince presses the young Emperor for a decree before the other three hear of it, and an attendant who crosses back to the shore is the only way to learn what was asked.
