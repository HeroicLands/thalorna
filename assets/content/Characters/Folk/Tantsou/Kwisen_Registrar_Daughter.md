# Appearance {#appearance}

She is a woman in a scholar's blue robe, her hair pinned with a single plain comb. She comes to the edge of the veranda and bows to a newcomer precisely and not deeply, and when she sends a classified traveler on his way she walks him to the eastern gate and points down the valley with her closed fan.

# Dossier {#dossier}

She is the [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] who receives foreigners at [[place-kwisen|Kwisen]], the frontier way-station beyond [[place-jnanadvar|the Scholars' Door]], and the second daughter of the station's registrar. She names herself by her description: Second Daughter of the Registrar of the [Clan of Moon Rises Over Still Water]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, near [[place-weichai|Weichai]]. The Tanchei of that clan keep registers as far off as Kwisen.

## The Fresh Page

**The newcomer.** She gives her description and her caste, and she waits. When it is plain that a newcomer has no caste to answer with, she inclines her head, seats him at the lowest end of a long bench, sets millet and pale tea in front of him, and opens a fresh page in a bound register he is not allowed to see.

**The register.** The station's register has been kept without a gap for four hundred years, and no Vedyari visitor is ever shown it. A merchant carrying foreign coin is told at the station what it is worth in the empire, and the figure is not negotiable.

**The valley.** "Everything below us is written down," she tells a newly classified traveler at the gate. "Every terrace has a lineage, every lineage has a page, and every page has a seal."

## Psyche

### Personality

She is courteous, exact and entirely without deference to a foreigner, and she does not hurry.

### Motivation

She wants every traveler who comes down the pass on a page the register can hold before he goes down the valley.

### Strengths

She knows the forms of classification by heart, and nothing a traveler says goes unwritten.

### Weaknesses

A traveler the register cannot hold is a problem she cannot set aside.

## Social

### Family

- Her father, the registrar of the station, of the Clan of Moon Rises Over Still Water.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-mwonouprv|Mwonou Province]]**: a subject, entered in the registers of the province.
- **[[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]**: of the [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]].

## Plot Hooks

1. **The second tablet.** A traveler she classified last season comes back up the valley with a tablet that does not match her page, and she wants to know which of the two is wrong.
2. **The caravan's entry.** A Vedyari caravan master wants to know what the station's register says of his caravan, and no Vedyari visitor is ever shown it.
3. **The Hvarn table.** [[affiliation-hvarnguides|The Hvarn]] who guide travelers over the pass go no farther than the station and eat at their own table, and she wants a party that shared their road to tell her how many travelers turned back at the col this season, since the guides will not.
