# Appearance {#appearance}

The district registrar sits among the cases of the register in the District Hall, on the mound of the lower town, in a scholar's blue robe. The district's seal lies on her desk, and the clerks who copy the famine petitions bring each one to her before it is entered.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The district registrar of [[place-jenshou|Jenshou]] is a [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]] known, as every registrar is, by her description: Second Daughter of the Seal-Cutter of the [Clan of Cold Spring Under Pines]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, near [[place-mwonou|Mwonou]]. She keeps the rolls of every person of the district at [[place-jenshouhall|The District Hall of Jenshou]], under the Caste-Registrar of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]].

## The Office

**The rotation.** A registrar is rotated from district to district, and the district's seal stays with the office, never with the person. She holds Jenshou by rotation, and when the rotation moves her on, the seal of Jenshou stays in the hall.

**The sealed case.** The entry for the flood of the seventh year lies in a sealed case that the district registrar alone may open. The schools read the flood in [[lore-annaljenshou|The Annal of Jenshou]], not in that entry.

**The hungry year.** The rain has failed over the uplands, and each petition for a ration, each famine petition and each death passes through her register. The hall's clerks write more in a hungry year than in a good one.

**The shelves.** The hall sets rice before the tablets of the lines the old flood left without an heir, and before the tablets of [[place-rinnou|Rinnou]], at the district's cost. The households in arrears say that not all of it arrives there, and the clerks say it does.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is exact and reserved, and she answers a question by reading the entry that answers it.

### Motivation

She wants the register of Jenshou complete and correct when the rotation takes her on, and the hungry year entered without a gap.

### Strengths

The district's rolls, its seal and the key to the sealed case are hers while she holds the office.

### Weaknesses

She is a stranger to the district by the nature of the office, and the clerks who have served the hall longer than she has know its pages better than she does.

## Social

### Patrons

- [[being-cstrgstrtnts|Third Son of the Archivist of the Clan of Lanterns Along the Bank]]: the Caste-Registrar, under whom she keeps the rolls.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]**: District Registrar of Jenshou.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The ration for the dead man.** A household draws a ration for a member the register enters as dead, and the registrar wants a party to learn whether he lives before she strikes the ration.
2. **The seal's impression.** An impression of the Jenshou seal, which stays with the office, has turned up on a ration pass at Mwonou, and she wants to know whose hand pressed it.
3. **The upper villages' count.** The famine has moved households off the dry upper terraces, and she wants a party to walk the upper villages with her clerk and count who is still where the register says.
