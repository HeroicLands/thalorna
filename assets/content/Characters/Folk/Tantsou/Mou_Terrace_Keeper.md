# Appearance {#appearance}

He is a terrace farmer of the hill above Weichai, at work on the wall course of the Third Terrace or on the river dike with the corvée. At midwinter he stands before his household's shrine while the spirit money burns and reads the account aloud, once.

# Dossier {#dossier}

He is the heir and keeper of the [Third Terrace]{.name data-type="place/site"} of the [Mou Clan]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, a [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] household of [[place-zhengnou|Zhengnou]], the terrace village on the hill above [[place-weichai|Weichai]]. He is known by his description, Eldest Son of the Terrace-Keeper of the Mou Clan, near Weichai, and his description and his father's are the same words, as they are in most terrace households, where the eldest son of a terrace-keeper keeps the terrace in his turn.

## The Account

**The terrace.** The Mou Clan has kept the Third Terrace in the register for thirty generations. Its lineage cut it out of the hillside by hand, wall course on wall course, and keeps it in the register by its boundary-stones. The men of the village go down to the corvée on the river dike every Month of Earth.

**The reading.** In the first year after his father's death he read the household's account to its dead at the [Festival of the Dead]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"}: [[lore-hearthaccount|The Hearth Account of the Third Terrace]]. The household allowed the Leigen of Weichai to keep it as the model, and they teach every terrace heir the form from it.

**The debit.** At the center of the account is the household's grandmother, [Eldest Daughter of the Thatcher of the Lei Clan]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who fed the children from the tablets in the famine of [the Year the Rice Lay Down]{.name data-type="lore/history"}, and the debit three generations of the household are paying for it.

**The hungry year.** The rain has failed over the uplands, and the terraces at the top of the hill are the first to go dry.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is plain and careful, and he says a thing once.

### Motivation

He wants the Third Terrace kept and the household's debit paid down, and the dead fed before anyone in the house has eaten.

### Strengths

He keeps a terrace his lineage has held for thirty generations, and every heir of the hill knows his account.

### Weaknesses

A dry year on the hill is a year the household's debit grows, and the dead are fed before the living.

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **The boundary-stone.** A neighboring terrace disputes a boundary-stone of the Third Terrace, and the quarrel goes to the clan elders. He wants a party to find the register's entry before they sit.
2. **The model account.** A household of another hill wants its heir taught the account by the household it came from, and asks a party to carry the request up to Zhengnou.
3. **The dry wall course.** The top of the hill has gone dry, and the household of the Third Terrace needs hands to carry water up the wall course while its men are at the corvée.
