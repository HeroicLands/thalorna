# Appearance {#appearance}

Chengzho is a 31-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall and is of light build. He has the look of the Zhazho: nails grown almost impossibly long and lacquered in brilliant colors, a painted face and painted teeth. He is the Prince the court watches most closely. When he crosses a bridge to the enclave, his officers already have the day's papers in hand.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Chengzho (Rising Dragon) is the Prince of [[affiliation-doulwenprv|Doulwen Province]], a _Zho Zhei_ of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]] who holds the western basins and the passes toward the [[place-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazrin Desert Region]] for the Emperor. He rules from [[place-doulwen|Doulwen]], where the caravans of [[lore-celestialroad|the Celestial Road]] unload. He is a younger Prince and rising fast, he stands for reform, and he has the ear of the young Emperor, [[being-tengrauemp|Tengrau]].

## The Rise

**The ear.** He is the Prince the young Emperor listens to. The other three Princes watch him in [[lore-weizhairui|The Jade Fan Dance]] (_Wei Zhai Rwi_), since the court's favor shifts with every reading of the stars, and a Prince who rises quickly is the one they work hardest to bring down.

**The embassy.** When the embassy of the [[affiliation-kingdmdnvr|Kingdom of Dúnavarre]] reaches [[place-zhowen|Zhowen]] asking for direct relations with the Empire, he takes it up first and openly, for the foreign weight it adds to his hold on the Emperor's ear. The court reads the request as a breach of the Speaker's protocol, and the other three Princes each read the embassy as a move against them and work to turn it against him ([[scenario-embsydnvrtantsou|Embassy from Dúnavarre to Tantsou]]).

## Psyche

### Personality

He is quick, open in his sponsorship and impatient with delay.

### Motivation

He wants reform, and the young Emperor's ear held long enough to make it.

### Strengths

He has the Emperor's ear, a province at the head of the Celestial Road and the foreign embassy's weight.

### Weaknesses

He rises quickly, which makes him every other Prince's first target, and his hold rests on the favor of a young Emperor still finding his own power.

## Social

### Patrons

- [[being-tengrauemp|Tengrau]]: the young Emperor, whose ear he has.

### Rivals

- [[being-rinzhowngsn|Rinzho]]: the Prince of Wongsen, the strongest of the four.
- [[being-hozhomwnou|Hozho]] and [[being-tuizhokwzng|Tuizho]]: the Princes of Mwonou and Kwozheng, bitter rivals of each other, who each work to turn the embassy against him.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-doulwenprv|Doulwen Province]]**: Prince, holding the western basins and passes for the Emperor.
- **[[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]**: of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]], the first caste.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-flngnwrgn|A Filing in the New Reign]]: Chengzho's officers need the embassy's papers filed in the right office of the chancellery ahead of the cross-referencing that would hold them for a year, and three other households want them misfiled.
2. **The reading.** A reading of the stars could raise his family or destroy it, and his officers want a party to learn which Tanchei is to give the next reading before the other Princes reach him.
3. **The foreign weight.** He wants the embassy seen with him and not without him, and asks a party to keep an embassy servant from carrying a message to another Prince's officers.
