# Appearance {#appearance}

The prefect is a man of middle years in the silk robe of his office, kept clean in captivity. He sits in the place of a guest, unshackled, with his hands folded, and he looks at his host as an equal he withholds forgiveness from.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The prefect is an official of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] who held a prefecture on the mainland's southern coast until the raiders of the [[affiliation-rinjaidmn|Rinjai Domain]] took him in a raid and brought him to [[place-rinwu|Rinwu]]. [[being-hojarinjai|Rinjai Hoja]] keeps him in the red hall as a guest. He is not mistreated. The Jenchai court wants him returned as a gesture of heaven's magnanimity, [[being-henzoshuiwo|Shuiwojai Henzo]] wants him ransomed, and an envoy of his empire has asked for him at the Dawn Court.

## Background

He holds his office by appointment and answers for the revenue and courts of his prefecture, and his family on the mainland holds silver and standing enough to be worth a ransom. Neither his family nor his empire has offered anything.

## Psyche

### Personality

Composed, formal and patient. He says nothing in the hall until he is asked, and then he says exactly what he means.

### Motivation

Go home with his dignity whole, and leave by a road that does not shame his family or his empire.

### Strengths

He is the one person in the hall whose word carries the standing of his family and his office.

### Weaknesses

He is a captive on an island whose people despise his empire, and every side that wants him wants him for something other than himself.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

He serves the Empire of Tantsou and answers to it for his prefecture, and his family on the mainland waits for word of him.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-prfctrdhll|The Prefect in the Red Hall]]: the court, the Strait and the mainland each want the prefect, and his host has to choose whom to give him to.
2. **What he would give.** A party asks the prefect what he would give for his freedom, and he answers exactly.
3. **The family's waiting.** His family on the mainland waits for word of him, and the prefect asks a party whether any road to the mainland lets word through without shaming his office or his empire.

:::secret {title="What his family will pay"}
The prefect knows what his family will pay and on what terms. They pay only for a man known to be alive, a letter in his own hand is what tells them, and he is the only person in the hall who can write it. If he goes home through the court as the throne's gift, his family pays Henzo's ransom in goods.

1. **The letter home.** The prefect asks a party to carry a letter to his family that tells them he is alive.

:::
