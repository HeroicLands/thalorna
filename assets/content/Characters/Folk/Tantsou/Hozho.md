# Appearance {#appearance}

Hozho is a 57-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of medium build. He has the look of the Zhazho: nails grown almost impossibly long and lacquered in brilliant colors, a painted face and painted teeth. He holds court in the city of Mwonou, in the upland basin below the mountains, where the famine petitions of his districts are read out to him by the dozen. The ancestral hall a Prince of the province raised in a past reign, nine terraces high with nine bells hung in it, stands in his city.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hozho (Fire Dragon) is the Prince of [[affiliation-mwonouprv|Mwonou Province]], a _Zho Zhei_ of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]] who holds the terraced uplands of the southwest and the long passes toward [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara Region]] for the Emperor. He rules from the city of [[place-mwonou|Mwonou]], and his officers hold [[place-mwonouplnd|the Mwonou Uplands]] district by district.

## Drought and Rivalry

**The famine.** The rain has failed in his country, and the famine of the southern uplands is his famine. At [[place-jaunou|Jaunou]] the river is dry in its bed and the granaries are empty, and in other districts of the uplands the [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] have risen against their lords and against the magistrates who collect the tribute.

**The rice.** He buys rice from the granaries of [[affiliation-wongsenprv|Wongsen Province]], whose Prince's sorcerers bring rain to the delta, and he pays the price that Prince names.

**The rival.** He and the Prince of [[affiliation-kwozhengprv|Kwozheng Province]] are bitter rivals, over an old marriage between their houses and an heir who died. In [[lore-weizhairui|The Jade Fan Dance]] (_Wei Zhai Rwi_) each takes the other's loss as his own gain.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is proud and slow to forgive, and he counts every debt his province runs up to another Prince.

### Motivation

He wants rain or rice for his uplands at a price he can pay, and the Prince of Kwozheng's loss.

### Strengths

He holds a province of some 26 million people, its passes and its Wohou, and the tribute of its districts.

### Weaknesses

His province is dry, his granaries are empty and he must buy from the strongest Prince. A Prince who must buy rice is a Prince who can be shamed at court.

## Social

### Rivals

- [[being-tuizhokwzng|Tuizho]]: the Prince of Kwozheng, his bitter rival.
- [[being-chengzhodlwn|Chengzho]]: the Prince of Doulwen, whose embassy he works to turn against him.

### Others

- [[being-rinzhowngsn|Rinzho]]: the Prince of Wongsen, who sells him rice at the price he names.
- [[being-jaunoumgstrt|Eldest Son of the Commandant of the Clan of Red Gate in Autumn]]: his magistrate at Jaunou, who hears the famine petitions.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-mwonouprv|Mwonou Province]]**: Prince, holding the southwestern uplands and passes for the Emperor.
- **[[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]**: of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]], the first caste.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The price of rice.** Hozho wants a party to learn what the Prince of Wongsen will take, besides cash, for rice for the uplands, before he must offer it at court where the Prince of Kwozheng can watch him pay.
2. **The risings.** The Nouwen of an upland district have risen against the magistrate who collects the tribute, and Hozho wants the rising ended before his rival hears of it.
3. **The rival's loss.** An officer of the Prince of Kwozheng has been seen in Mwonou, and Hozho wants a party to learn what he came to buy.
