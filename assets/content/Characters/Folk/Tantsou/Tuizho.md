# Appearance {#appearance}

Tuizho holds court in the city of Kwozheng, in the hard-wintered north, where the reports of the watchtowers and the signal fires of the marches come to him before they go on to the capital.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tuizho (Iron Dragon) is the Prince of [[affiliation-kwozhengprv|Kwozheng Province]], a _Zho Zhei_ of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]] who holds the north and its marches for the Emperor with their armies and sorcerers. He rules from the city of [[place-kwozheng|Kwozheng]]. His province holds the cold coast, the basins of the north and the fortified marchland of watchtowers and garrison colonies.

## The North and the Rival

**The marches.** Beyond the marches lies the steppe of the [Black Wilds]{.name data-type="place/region"}, and the riders of [[being-wogenhenzhuo|Wogen Henzhwo]] have come into the northern districts. His Commandant of the Marches holds the watchtowers and the garrison colonies, and his Keeper of the Muster Rolls keeps the rolls by which each colonist's land is held and each muster called.

**The rival.** He and the Prince of [[affiliation-mwonouprv|Mwonou Province]] are bitter rivals, over an old marriage between their houses and an heir who died. In the [[lore-weizhairui|Wei Zhai Rwi]] each takes the other's loss as his own gain.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is hard and unforgiving, and he keeps account of every slight to his house.

### Motivation

He wants his marches held, and the Prince of Mwonou shamed before the court.

### Strengths

He holds the armies and the garrison colonies of the north, and the frontier is a reason the throne cannot do without him.

### Weaknesses

The steppe riders are in his northern districts, and a frontier that does not hold is a shame the other Princes will use.

## Social

### Rivals

- [[being-hozhomwnou|Hozho]]: the Prince of Mwonou, his bitter rival.
- [[being-chengzhodlwn|Chengzho]]: the Prince of Doulwen, whose embassy he works to turn against him.

### Enemies

- [[being-wogenhenzhuo|Wogen Henzhwo]]: the steppe warlord whose riders raid the northern districts.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-kwozhengprv|Kwozheng Province]]**: Prince, holding the north and its marches for the Emperor.
- **[[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]]**: of the [[lore-zhazho|Zhazho]], the first caste.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The muster rolls.** A garrison colony's muster has come up short, and Tuizho's Keeper of the Muster Rolls wants a party to learn whether the missing colonists fled south or rode north.
2. **The rival's rice.** Tuizho wants to know what the Prince of Mwonou has paid the Prince of Wongsen for rice, so that the court can hear of it.
3. **The report.** A report from the marches must reach the capital in Tuizho's words before another Prince's officers tell the court that the frontier is failing.
