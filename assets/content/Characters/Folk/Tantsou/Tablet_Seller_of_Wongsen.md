# Appearance {#appearance}

The seller is a 38-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. He waits in the narrow court behind the warehouses at dusk with something wrapped in a cloth.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The seller is a [[lore-noutsou|Nwitsou]], and the registers know him only as Ninth Bondman of the Rope-Maker of the [Clan of Tar on the Slipway]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, near [[place-wongsen|Wongsen]]: his owner and a number, with no name of his own. He works the court behind the warehouses of [[place-wongsenfrgnqrtr|The Foreign Quarter of Wongsen]], where the sailors of the quarter say that a tablet can be bought that no bureau issued. He names a price and asks no names. The seal on the tablet is good for a day, and he says so.

## The Trade

**The tablet.** A tablet no bureau issued is a forgery, and forging a tablet is punished by execution in public, because it is an attempt to put oneself in a category the cosmic order did not assign.

**The buyers.** A foreigner whose classification has run out, or is about to, is the buyer he looks for.

:::secret {title="Whose tablet"}
If the tablet is a forgery, he is a member of the Wongsen cell of [[affiliation-haluozhao|the Black Lotus Society]], which trades in forged tablets, and he knows only its Handler. If it is a trap, he is the Warden's man and the tablet is the work of the Warden's officers. The Game Master chooses, and he sells it the same way in both cases.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

He is quiet and quick, and he never argues over a price.

### Motivation

He wants the tablet sold and himself gone from the court before the watch changes.

### Strengths

He knows the quarter's back ways and the hour each gate changes its officers.

### Weaknesses

He is the one a buyer can name, and the law has an execution for the one who sells.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

- **[[affiliation-wongsenprv|Wongsen Province]]**: a Subject of the Prince, entered in the registers whatever the caste.

He knows the sailors of the quarter and nobody else by name.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-tbltwrnghnd|A Tablet from the Wrong Hand]]: he offers the party a tablet behind the warehouses, and a buyer who is found with it is a forger.
2. **The wrapped cloth.** A foreign merchant has seen a man with a cloth in the back court and wants a party to learn what is in it before he does anything he cannot undo.
3. **The saying.** A sailor of the quarter says a tablet can be bought behind the warehouses, and a party that asks who sells it finds a man who will not give his name.
