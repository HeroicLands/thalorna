# Appearance {#appearance}

The Warden is a 52-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of heavy build. He wears an officer's robe over light armor, a sword at his belt and the seal of his office at his waist.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Warden of the Foreign Quarter is a [[lore-wohou|Wohou]] officer of [[being-rinzhowngsn|Rinzho]], the Prince of [[affiliation-wongsenprv|Wongsen Province]], known by his description: Second Son of the Tower Captain of the [Clan of Banners Above the Harbor]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, near [[place-wongsen|Wongsen]]. He keeps the walls and the gates of [[place-wongsenfrgnqrtr|The Foreign Quarter of Wongsen]] and taxes and watches the merchants inside them. His officers keep the gates, and a foreigner going in or out shows his tablet with the seal of the bureau that issued it.

## The Quarter He Keeps

**The gates.** His officers read each tablet's date and seal, and the reverse of the plaque, before a foreigner goes in or out.

**The warehouses.** Foreign goods wait here, documented and taxed, for the [[lore-shuonong|Shwonong]] factors who buy them, and a merchant's goods stay here when his classification does not.

**The requests.** His office takes a merchant's request for an extension and passes it to the commercial administration that issued the classification.

:::secret {title="The court behind the warehouses"}
The court behind the warehouses lies inside his quarter. He keeps it open or watched as the Prince's interest requires, and he does not say which.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

He is correct and unhurried, and he does not raise his voice at a gate.

### Motivation

He wants the quarter's walls kept, its taxes paid and nothing in it that the Prince has not seen.

### Strengths

He knows every merchant in the quarter by tablet and by date.

### Weaknesses

He answers to the Prince, and what happens at his gates is read in Wongsen as the Prince's doing.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

- **[[affiliation-wongsenprv|Wongsen Province]]**: Warden of the Foreign Quarter, an officer of the Prince's.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-tbltwrnghnd|A Tablet from the Wrong Hand]]: a foreign merchant's classification runs out at the gate at sunset on the fourth day, and the Warden's officers come for the tablet.
2. **The embassy's party.** A party of the Dúnavarre embassy has come to the quarter on the strength of its tablets, and the Warden wants to know what it came for.
3. **The officer across the strait.** A coastal magistrate wants a captured officer back from the Jenchai houses across the strait, and wants the offer carried by a foreigner of the quarter, whom the Warden's officers must let through the gates.
