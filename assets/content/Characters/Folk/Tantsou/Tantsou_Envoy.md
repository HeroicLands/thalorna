# Appearance {#appearance}

The envoy wears the robes and cap of his rank in the mainland's fashion, more elaborate than anything at the island court. His attendants stand a pace behind him, and he bows to the throne precisely as far as protocol requires.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The envoy has crossed [[place-easternstrt|The Eastern Strait]] for the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] and been received at [[place-lengwou|Lengwou]], the Dawn Court, where foreign envoys are received and go no farther. He has asked [[being-weilengjnch|Weileng]] for the return of the prefect whom the raiders of [[place-rinjai|Rinjai]] hold at [[place-rinwu|Rinwu]].

## Background

He serves the mainland court, and the coastal magistrates of his empire want the raids stopped and their captured officers returned. He finds the islanders crude and violent, and they find his empire decadent and scheming.

## Psyche

### Personality

Courteous, elaborate and unyielding. He treats the island court's ceremony as a performance he has agreed to endure, and he offers language about his empire's goodwill in place of coin.

### Motivation

Bring the prefect home without paying for him, and without being seen to ask the islanders for anything.

### Strengths

His empire's pride and its trade are both at stake, and the island houses need the mainland's jade, tea, bronze and silver.

### Weaknesses

He cannot go beyond Lengwou, and every word he sends to Rinwu passes through other hands.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

He serves the Empire of Tantsou. His attendants carry his word where he cannot go, and one of them goes to the red hall at Rinwu as his agent when a party takes him there.

## Plot Hooks

1. The envoy asks at the Dawn Court for the prefect, and the throne wants to learn what he would give if goodwill were not enough: [[scenario-prfctrdhll|The Prefect in the Red Hall]].
2. The envoy hires a party to carry his agent across the strait to Rinwu.
