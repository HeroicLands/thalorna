# Appearance {#appearance}

The Leigen stands beside a household's shrine while its heir reads, with the smoke of the spirit money between them, and speaks only when the dead have something to say. Between festivals he walks up the terraced hill above Weichai from one household to the next.

# Dossier {#dossier}

He is a Leigen, a spirit master of [[lore-ancstrwrshp|Ancestor Worship]], at [[place-weichai|Weichai]], known by his description: Second Son of the Miller of the [Clan of Reeds Bend in the Wind]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, near Weichai. He is a [[lore-nouwen|Nouwen]] by caste, born with the gift of speaking to the dead, and every caste respects him.

## The Work

**The dead's business.** He carries a household's business to its ancestors and brings back the answer, reads ancestral commands, treats sickness from the spirit side, leads the rites and settles disputes by asking the ancestors concerned to rule. A command through a Leigen is treated as a directive from heaven.

**The account.** The Leigen of Weichai stand beside each heir at the [Festival of the Dead]{.name data-type="lore/gathering"} and give the answers of the ancestors, and they teach the form of the reckoning from [[lore-hearthaccount|The Hearth Account of the Third Terrace]], the account of the household of the Third Terrace of [[place-zhengnou|Zhengnou]]. Each heir who learns from it puts his own dead in its places and keeps its order, its figures and its refrain.

**The hungry year.** The rain has failed over the uplands. The terraces at the top of the hill are the first to go dry, and households that planted rice there plant millet. A household that cannot keep the Festival of the Dead properly faces a year with weakened advocates in heaven, and the heirs ask him what the dead will accept.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is patient and plain-spoken, and he gives the dead's answer whether the household wants it or not.

### Motivation

He wants every household of the hill to keep its account with its dead through the hungry year, and no line to fall into default.

### Strengths

Every caste respects him, and the households trust his word on what their dead require.

### Weaknesses

He is a peasant by caste. Those above him who consult him do it in private, since acknowledging a peasant's authority openly would break caste.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-mouterrckpr|Eldest Son of the Terrace-Keeper of the Mou Clan]]: the heir of the Third Terrace, whose account the Leigen of Weichai teach from.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-mwonouprv|Mwonou Province]]**: a subject, entered in the registers of the province.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The millet offering.** A household of the upper terraces has only millet to set before its tablets this year, and asks a party to come up the hill when the Leigen gives the dead's answer.
2. **The private consultation.** An officer of the Prince's household wants the Leigen brought to him by night, unseen, to put a question to a dead ancestor, and a party is hired as the escort.
3. **The dispute.** Two terraces quarrel over a boundary-stone that the clan elders cannot settle, and the Leigen is asked to let the ancestors concerned rule on it.
