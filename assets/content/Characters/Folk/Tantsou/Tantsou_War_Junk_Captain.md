# Appearance {#appearance}

The captain wears lacquered armor over his officer's robe and keeps to the high stern of his junk, where the empire's flag flies. He holds himself stiffly and speaks to strangers through a junior officer.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The captain commands a war junk of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] that has come across [[place-easternstrt|The Eastern Strait]] and anchored off the coves at its mouth, where the raiders of the [[affiliation-rinjaidmn|Rinjai Domain]] watch it from the rocks. He has not lowered a boat, and nobody ashore has read his orders or knows what he has been told.

## Background

He is an officer of the empire, sent with a crew of sailors and marines. The empire has no fleet that can clear the strait, and its captains know the islands' waters have wrecked every invasion fleet sent against them.

## Psyche

### Personality

Stiff, cautious and correct. He acts on his orders and on nothing else.

### Motivation

Carry out his orders and bring his ship home.

### Strengths

His junk is larger than any single raider and carries more men, and the empire's flag stands behind it.

### Weaknesses

He is in strange water among hostile coves, far from any harbor of his own empire.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

He serves the Empire of Tantsou. His officers and marines follow him, and the template serves for any of them who parley in his place.

## Plot Hooks

1. The junk lies off the coves of [[place-rinjai|Rinjai]] with its boat unlowered, and the raiders on the rocks wait for their lord's word: [[scenario-prfctrdhll|The Prefect in the Red Hall]].
2. The captain asks a pilot to carry a message ashore, and the pilot asks a party to carry it in his place.

:::secret {title="The captain's orders"}
The captain is ordered to hold at anchor until word comes to him from the envoy or from the shore, or until he is attacked. He does not know what the envoy has asked at [[place-lengwou|Lengwou]], and Rinjai's lord does not know what the captain has been told. The referee decides his errand: to fetch the prefect, to carry a message for the lord of Rinjai, or to burn the harbor at [Jawo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}. A parley is the word from the shore he waits for, and he answers it according to his errand. A party that fires on the junk, or lets the raiders fire, releases him from his orders to hold.

1. A party that goes aboard under a flag of parley learns what the junk has come for and what the captain knows of the envoy's request: [[scenario-prfctrdhll|The Prefect in the Red Hall]].

:::
