# Appearance {#appearance}

The Caste-Registrar is a man in the scholar's blue robe of the [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]], at a desk in the registry offices of the administrative center on the shore, with a roll open before him and clerks waiting at his elbow. Princes' officers wait longer in his anteroom than they wait anywhere else in the city.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Caste-Registrar of the [[affiliation-tantsouempr|Empire of Tantsou]] is known, in the register and at court, by his description: Third Son of the Archivist of the [Clan of Lanterns Along the Bank]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, near [[place-zhowen|Zhowen]]. He keeps the rolls on which every subject's caste, and therefore every subject's law, is entered, and he is the chief counselor of the young Emperor, [[being-tenglengemp|Tengleng]].

## The Rolls and the Ear

**The rolls.** Every subject of the empire is on the rolls of a district, and every district has its registrar. The district registrars keep their rolls under him and are rotated from district to district, and each district's seal stays with the office when its holder moves on. His offices and the census stand in the administrative centers on both shores of Zhowen.

**The counselor.** The young Emperor takes his counsel first. Every Prince courts him for that reason, and an officer of each of the four is in his anteroom more often than the business of the rolls requires.

**The game.** In the [[lore-weizhairui|Wei Zhai Rwi]] a misfiled petition can do as much harm as an army. The office that keeps the rolls is the office every player in the game would most like to have on his side.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is exact, unhurried and formal. He works out the precedence of a conversation before it begins and says nothing that has not been established.

### Motivation

He wants the rolls complete and correct, and the young Emperor's reign well entered in them.

### Strengths

He keeps the classification of every subject of the empire, and he has the Emperor's ear.

### Weaknesses

Each of the four Princes wants him, and favor to one is read by the other three as a move against them.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The anteroom.** An officer of one Prince wants a party to learn what an officer of another Prince brought to the Caste-Registrar's anteroom, and what he carried away.
2. **The misfiled roll.** A household's entry has gone to the wrong district in the work of the new era, and the household has no standing in Zhowen's courts until the Caste-Registrar's clerks find it.
3. **The embassy's caste.** The Dúnavarre embassy's members are unclassified until a bureau says otherwise. The Prince of Doulwen wants them classified high and quickly, and the other three Princes want the classification delayed.
