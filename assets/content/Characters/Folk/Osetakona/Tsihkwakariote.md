# Appearance {#appearance}

Tsihkwakariote is a 70-year-old woman who stands 5'3" tall with a light build. She has tanned skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tsihkwakariote is the rememberer (_sakwentahen_) of the Loon clan at [[place-tsiorhenten|Dawn Place]], and the keeper of the memory belts of the Dawn People of the [[affiliation-osetakona|Maple People]]. A rememberer keeps the Grove's agreements in memory, reads the belts of white and purple beads and recites them at every great council. Her longhouse stood at the old village on the terrace above the cove until a boat of men out of the sea burned it last summer, and she moved inland with the rest when the clan mother, [[being-tsiorhensiwen|Tsiorhensiwen]], took the village to the spruce hills.

She is seventy, and her eyes are failing, which is common in rememberers and no hindrance: she reads a belt with her fingers.

:::secret {title="The belt over the sea"}

The men from the water took the Dawn People's joining belt, the memory belt that records the day the Dawn People came into the Grove, which she kept in her longhouse at the old village. It is now on a shelf in a jarl's house across the ocean, among things nobody there can read. Only Tsiorhensiwen and Tsihkwakariote know it is gone. A nation whose joining belt is lost has, by the Grove's own reckoning, nothing to show for its place in the Grove, so she is stringing a new belt from memory, at night, in a closed longhouse, row by row, and means to carry it to the next great council as the old one. In a people that holds secrecy to be the root of evil, the two women are doing the one thing the Grove calls witchcraft. The belt is also the most discussed thing in the jarl's house.

She keeps to her longhouse more than she did, and works late. A party that trades in purple shell, the hardest bead to come by on the [[place-tsiorhenrgn|Dawn Shore]], finds her a quiet and generous buyer.

A party that has been across the sea, or means to go, may find her asking, very carefully, what the people there keep on their shelves. What she wants is a belt at the next great council that no rememberer in the Grove can tell from the one that was taken.

:::

A party that comes to the Dawn Place on council business finds her the one who can say what any old agreement of the Grove says, and who was there when it was made.

What she wants is every belt of the Dawn People read aright at the next great council.
