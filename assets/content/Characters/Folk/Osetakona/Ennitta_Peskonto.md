# Appearance {#appearance}

Ennitta Peskonto is a woman of 900 years who stands 5' 11" tall and is of light build. Her black hair is braided and pinned up in a coil, her eyes are brown, and her olive skin is lined at the corners of the eyes. Her hands are stained green with pitch to the wrist and cannot be washed clean. She wears a long apron of oiled leather over a plain gown and carries a knife made of a single horn.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 900. Grower of the Living Pine. Wants the pines to lean the way they are asked.**

Ennitta Peskonto is the grower of the [[affiliation-nclvhrsllnk|Enclave of Hersellonku]]. The Senali build by singing and growing what they need, and she is the one the hearths ask to grow a dwelling: she sings a young pine into a house of living wood on an island or a stony shore, and the house is there for as long as the hearth keeps it. Her own hearth is Peskonto, the hearth of the thin smoke.

The canoe makers of the Grove say that the pines on the far lakes lean, all of them toward the water and none toward the land. They say it as a curiosity. It is her work.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ennitta is brisk, plain-spoken and impatient with anything unfinished, and she sings under her breath while she works without noticing that she does.

### Motivation

She wants the pines of the far lakes to lean the way they are asked, and she wants her dwellings to outlast the hearths that she builds them for.

### Strengths

No one grows as well. A pine she sings keeps the shape of a house for a thousand winters.

### Weaknesses

She sings for a long time without stopping to see what the song has done, and she is impatient with anyone who asks her to stop.

## Social

### Companions

The seer [[being-sennilarvnnnt|Sennila Rovennanto]], beside whom she has sung for centuries, and the young hearths who ask her for dwellings.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

None she would name.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvhrsllnk|Enclave of Hersellonku]]**: Ennitta is a Member of the Conclave.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A canoe-maker's pine.** A canoe maker of the Grove asks leave to fell one pine from the edge of the far wood for a hull. Ennitta gives leave for one tree and does not say which, and the canoe maker needs a guide who can tell a tree that is spoken for from one that is free.
2. **The leaning pines.** A scholar of the Grove has measured the lean of the far pines and wants to know who has been bending them. Ennitta tells the party who bring the scholar's question that she does not know what he means.

:::secret {title="The song she cannot stop"}
The turning of the paths is partly Ennitta's work. She sings the roots along the ridge paths so that a way into the pines bends back out of them, and since the bands came she has sung it stronger. This autumn, one stretch of the ridge has begun to turn everyone who walks it, including the conclave's own children, and she cannot end the song. She has not told Kolpavo Haltunto, who relies on the turning, or the ommenda.
:::
