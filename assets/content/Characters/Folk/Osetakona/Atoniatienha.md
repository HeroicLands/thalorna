Atoniatienha is a 28-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall with a medium build. He has tawny skin, smooth complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Atoniatienha is the war leader (_rotsikwen_) of the young men of [[place-tsiorhenten|Dawn Place]], the village of the Dawn People of the [[affiliation-osetakona|Maple People]] that a boat of men out of the sea burned last summer. The raiders killed six people and carried four away. The clan mother of the Loon clan, [[being-tsiorhensiwen|Tsiorhensiwen]], refused a mourning war, because there was no one within reach who had done the village harm, and moved the village a day's walk inland to a clearing with no view of the water.

The young men call her old and cold. Some of them want to take a canoe out along the coast to look for the men from the water, and Atoniatienha has agreed to wait one more year. He is twenty-eight, a strong paddler and a good shot, and the young men follow him because he has kept them from going without him. He sends two of them at a time to the headland above the old cove from the thaw to the first snow. They have lit the signal fire four times since the raid, each time for a whale.

He has been asking every trader who comes to the [[place-tsiorhenrgn|Dawn Shore]] what lies across the sea and who builds boats that cross it. A party that has seen such a boat, or the country it came from, is the first thing in a year he has wanted more than the end of his promise.

What he wants is the men from the water found, and made to answer for the four.
