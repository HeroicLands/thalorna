# Appearance {#appearance}

Osetarahion is a 62-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall with a medium build. She has golden skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Osetarahion is a clan mother (_kontiesa_) of [[place-tsinikasiwa|First Sugar]], the village of the Rapids People beside the [First Grove]{.name data-type="place/site"} where the Grove of the [[affiliation-osetakona|Maple People]] began. The Rapids People are the Grove's second nation, and they are tired of being second. The great council has sat in the Falls People's villages for most of eleven generations, and the [[place-ostakaten|Kettle]] is about to move again. The Dawn People want it moved to the sea, and the Falls People want it to stay among their own villages.

Osetarahion has offered First Sugar, which stands between the two, beside the stand of maples where the Grove began. She has sent sugar from the First Grove to every clan mother of the Falls People with the offer. None has answered, and the Rapids People know that a Falls clan mother who does not answer has not said no.

She is sixty-two, warm, patient and relentless. Her village's rememberer, [[being-ohnakensakwenta|Ohnakensakwenta]], is the best in the Grove, and she makes sure every clan mother knows it. A party carrying word between the Grove's nations is useful to her, and a party that brings a Falls clan mother's answer, either way, is welcome at her fire for as long as it likes.

What she wants is the great council's Kettle at First Sugar, beside the trees where the Grove began.
