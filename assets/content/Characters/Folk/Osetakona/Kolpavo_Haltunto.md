# Appearance {#appearance}

Kolpavo Haltunto is a man of 640 years who stands 6' 2" tall and is of medium build. His dark brown hair is cropped close, his eyes are hazel, and his skin is tanned dark by wind off the lakes. The first two fingers of his right hand are whitened by a bowstring callus. He wears a leather jerkin stitched with pine root and carries a short bow and a long knife, and he moves through the pines without a sound.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 640. Warden of the Far Ridges. Wants the ridges kept as the hearths hold them.**

Kolpavo Haltunto is a warden of the [[affiliation-nclvhrsllnk|Enclave of Hersellonku]], a hunter and a bowman, who walks the far ridges in every season. His hearth is Haltunto, the hearth of the hollow log. He has never fired on a man and has never been asked to.

## Background

Kolpavo was the youngest hunter of his hearth for two centuries and learned the ridges from the lord [[being-hapirivovssnt|Hapirivo Vessonto]], walking behind him shore after shore. He took up the warden's bow when he could find any lake from any point in the pines, and he teaches the young hunters now as he was taught.

:::secret {title="The hearths on the far lakes"}
The warden keeps the paths that lead out of the far ridges. Kolpavo teaches the young one thing before all the others: every path leaves the pines by the way it entered, and a hunter who forgets that is lost with the pines' help and not their harm.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Kolpavo is quiet, dry and watchful, with a hunter's patience and a young man's temper that he keeps well under his hand. He does not explain himself to the young. He shows them.

### Motivation

He wants the far ridges kept as the hearths hold them: closed, known to those who live there and to nobody else.

### Strengths

No one reads the ridges better. He can find any lake from any point in the pines in any weather, and cross a ridge without being seen.

### Weaknesses

He does not trust a stranger's reason for coming, and the more reasonable the reason sounds, the less he does.

## Social

### Companions

- Hapirivo Vessonto: The lord with whom he walks the ridges.
- The young hunters of the ridges: He teaches them.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

The hunting bands pressing south into the pines, though he bears no man of them ill.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvhrsllnk|Enclave of Hersellonku]]**: Kolpavo is a Member of the Conclave.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The lake on the skyline.** A party that has strayed onto the far ridges walks all day toward a lake on the skyline and comes down at dusk on the shore it left at dawn. Its guide from the hunting bands goes no farther and turns for home.
2. **The pale arrow.** A young hunter of the Pine People finds an arrow of pale wood lying across his snare line at the edge of the far ridges, fletched in a way no people of the Grove fletch. He asks a party to learn whose it is.

:::secret {title="The one who did not come back"}
Kolpavo leads the hearths that say the far ridges belong to the Senali. He is the one who walks the bands in their circles and sets them down by lakes they have already passed, and he has begun to do it harder this season. One hunter has not come back from the pines because he walked on past the lake where Kolpavo set him down, refusing to be turned, and the ridges kept him. He has not told the ommenda, and he has not told Hapirivo Vessonto.
:::
