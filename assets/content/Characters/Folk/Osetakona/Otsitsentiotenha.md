# Appearance {#appearance}

Otsitsentiotenha is a 58-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall with a heavy build. She has tawny skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Otsitsentiotenha, Sturgeon Waits, is the clan mother of the Sturgeon clan at [[place-tsiontaha|New Clearing]], the farthest south any village of the [[affiliation-osetakona|Maple People]] has ever stood. Two autumns ago a war party of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]] burned it, killed fourteen people and carried three south as captives. The village rebuilt on the same ground the next spring, behind a double palisade.

The war leader, [[being-tiahwentahkon|Tiahwentahkon]], followed the raiders south and brought back two of the three captives, and he wants a mourning war for the dead raised from every River People village. Without the consent of the clan mothers whose names it is meant to raise, no mourning war goes out, and Otsitsentiotenha has refused. She says the village came too far south, and that the clan mothers who chose its site chose it for the soil and forgot whose land it was beside. Tiahwentahkon answers that she chose it herself. Both are right.

:::secret {title="The third captive"}

The third captive taken from New Clearing, a girl of twelve of the Sturgeon clan, was not brought back because she would not come. She is Otsitsentiotenha's sister's daughter. A household of the One Fire's northern landing has taken her in as a daughter, and she has sent word north with a trader that she is well and means to stay. Otsitsentiotenha knows this and has told no one, because a mourning war would be fought against the town where her sister's daughter lives.

A party traveling south from New Clearing into the One Fire's country may be asked by her to carry something small, a belt or a pair of moccasins, to a household she does not name until the party has agreed.

What she wants is no mourning war sent against the town where her sister's daughter lives.

:::

She is fifty-eight, tall and grave, and she speaks less in council than any clan mother of the River People.

What she wants is no mourning war, and New Clearing left standing on its own ground.
