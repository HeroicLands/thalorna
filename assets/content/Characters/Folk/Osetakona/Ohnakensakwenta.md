# Dossier {#dossier}

Ohnakensakwenta, Remembers the Corn, is the rememberer (_sakwentahen_) of [[place-tsinikasiwa|First Sugar]], the Rapids People's village beside the [First Grove]{.name data-type="place/site"} where the Grove of the [[affiliation-osetakona|Maple People]] began. He is an old blind man of the Crane clan, and he is said to know the Founding better than any rememberer at the [[place-ostakaten|Kettle]]. The other rememberers come to First Sugar to check their recitations against his, and he sends them home corrected.

A rememberer keeps the Grove's agreements in memory, reads the memory belts and recites them at every great council. Ohnakensakwenta has not walked to a great council in six years. He recites the Founding every spring under the oldest trees of the First Grove, from the morning the sap runs, and anyone may sit and listen.

He is seventy-eight. He has outlived two apprentices, and has not taken a third, because he says nobody now alive has the patience to learn it right. A party that wants to know what the Grove agreed, and when, and in whose words, can ask him and get the whole answer whether it wanted the whole answer or not. A rememberer who can tell a true belt from a false one by the feel of the rows is a rarer thing than the Grove knows, and he is one.

What he wants is one young rememberer who can recite the Founding as he does, word for word, before he dies.
