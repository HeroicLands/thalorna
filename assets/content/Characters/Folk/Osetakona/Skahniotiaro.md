# Dossier {#dossier}

Skahniotiaro of the Elk clan is a speaker of the Birch People at [[place-kaniestraten|Birches]], on the western edge of the [[place-kahnionrgn|Western Lakes]] where the forest ends and the grass begins. He was born among the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]] and taken in a mourning war at nine. An Elk clan mother whose son had drowned that spring adopted him and raised her son's name onto him, and he became her son in full: he hunted for her longhouse, cleared her daughters' fields and grew up speaking two tongues. Six years ago she named him her clan's speaker.

He keeps nothing. Every hide he brings home goes to the longhouse, and he is the poorest man in his village and the best fed. He carries the council's word without adding to it, and he has never been caught saying more than was agreed. The great council of the [[affiliation-osetakona|Maple People]] usually names him one of the Grove's two speakers to the [Red Quarry]{.name data-type="place/site"}, a few days' walk from Birches, because no speaker of the Grove talks to the Grass People better. Twice he has carried a compact pipe to his birth people, stood in their camp as a Maple speaker talking in their own tongue, and brought back a truce. The western villages hold him up as what a man should be.

The last raid on Birches, two springs ago, came from his birth band, and two of the village's young men died. He carried the pipe to the band that summer and brought home a truce and two of their sons as hostages, raised in the village as Elk kin. Half the village calls it the best answer anyone could have given. The other half asks why the raid came from his band, and why the truce was so easy to get. No one, Skahniotiaro included, can say which people he would stand with if a truce failed, and some in his village still watch him after thirty years.

What he wants is a peace that never makes him choose between the people who bore him and the people who raised him.
