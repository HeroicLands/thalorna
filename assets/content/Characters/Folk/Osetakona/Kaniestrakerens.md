Kaniestrakerens is a 75-year-old woman who stands 5'3" tall with a medium build. She has tawny skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kaniestrakerens, Birch Hears, is an Elk clan mother of [[place-kaniestraten|Birches]], on the western edge of the [[place-kahnionrgn|Western Lakes]] where the forest of the [[affiliation-osetakona|Maple People]] ends and the grass begins. She is old now and sits by the fire, and the village's clan mothers ask her first.

Years ago a mourning war brought home a boy of nine from the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]], and Kaniestrakerens adopted him in place of a son of hers who had drowned. Six years ago she named him her clan's speaker. He is [[being-skahniotiaro|Skahniotiaro]], who speaks both tongues, has twice carried a compact pipe out across the grass to his birth people and twice brought home a truce. In the Grove an adopted captive is kin in full, and watched until his clan trusts him; she trusted him first, and the village followed her.

She is seventy-five, nearly deaf, and sharper than most people half her age. A clan mother can take back a speaker's title, and she is the only person in Birches who could take back his. A party that wants the Birch People's help on the grass, or wants a word carried to the Grass People's camps, can ask her son, and it will find that his mother has already heard about them.

What she wants is to see her son's truce with the grass outlive her.
