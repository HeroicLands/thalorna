# Dossier {#dossier}

Kwentsatiatoren, Moon Answers, is the eldest healer (_ehniatehen_) at [[place-ostakaten|Kettle]], the village by the Great Falls where the great council of the [[affiliation-osetakona|Maple People]] sits. She is a woman of the Porcupine clan who was healed of a fever at six and joined the healers at the cure, because in the [[lore-tiatorenhsa|Answering]] the price of being healed is to heal. She has worn a Face for fifty years, the twisted mouth and copper eyes of a power of the deep woods carved from the burl of a living maple.

Twice a year, at the thaw and in the Long Night, the healers go through every longhouse wearing their Faces and shaking rattles of corn husk, and drive the Faces' mischief out through the smoke hole. Between those visits a household with a sickness sends for one of them, and the healer comes with a Face in a bag and asks the sick person what they have dreamed. Kwentsatiatoren is said to read a dream from its first three words. She is fifty-six, small, and very still, and people who have told her a dream tend to tell her more than the dream.

The healers of the Grove call it witchcraft that the Pine People's speaker, [[being-okwiahasterio|Okwiahasterio]], has let a band's spirit-doctor build a lodge outside his village, and the question has gone to the great council. A party that tells her its dreams is answered in front of the household, as the Answering requires. A party that asks her about the doctor's lodge hears a long account of what can be seen and what cannot.

What she wants is every power answered where the people can see what is asked and what is given.
