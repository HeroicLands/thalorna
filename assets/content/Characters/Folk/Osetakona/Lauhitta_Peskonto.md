# Appearance {#appearance}

Lauhitta Peskonto is a woman of 900 years who stands 5' 11" tall and is of light build. Her black hair is braided and pinned up in a coil, her eyes are brown, and her olive skin is lined at the corners of the eyes. Her hands are stained green with pitch to the wrist and cannot be washed clean. She wears a long apron of oiled leather over a plain gown and carries a knife made of a single horn.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 900. Singer of the Pines. Wants the pines to grow the way they are asked.**

Lauhitta Peskonto is a member of the [[affiliation-nclvhrsllnk|Enclave of Hersellonku]] who sings the living pine. The [[lore-flksenali|Senali]] would rather grow what they need than build it, and she is the one the hearths ask when a pine is to be grown into a shape. Her own hearth is Peskonto, the hearth of the thin smoke.

The canoe makers of the Grove say that the pines on the far lakes lean, all of them toward the water and none toward the land. They say it as a curiosity.

## Background

Lauhitta learned to sing the pines from the singer before her, and for her first two centuries she did little but listen to the trees answer. She has sung for the hearths ever since, and a young hearth comes to her first.

:::secret {title="The hearths on the far lakes"}
Lauhitta grows Hersellonku's dwellings: she sings a young pine into a house of living wood on an island or a stony shore of the far lakes, and the house stands for as long as the hearth keeps it. The leaning pines of the far lakes are her work.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Lauhitta is brisk, plain-spoken and impatient with anything unfinished, and she sings under her breath while she works without noticing that she does.

### Motivation

She wants the pines of the far lakes to grow the way they are asked, and she wants her work to outlast the hearths she does it for.

### Strengths

No one sings the pines as well. A pine she has sung keeps the shape she gave it for as long as it lives.

### Weaknesses

She sings for a long time without stopping to see what the song has done, and she is impatient with anyone who asks her to stop.

## Social

## Plot Hooks

1. **A canoe maker's pine.** A canoe maker of the Grove means to fell a pine he chose at the edge of the far wood for a hull. Three times he walks toward it, and three times he comes out beside a younger pine of the same height. He hires a party to learn whether the wood is offering him that one instead.
2. **The leaning pines.** A scholar of the Grove has measured the lean of the pines on the far lakes and found every one leaning toward the water. He hires a party to cross a far ridge and measure the pines beyond it.

:::secret {title="The song she cannot stop"}
The turning of the paths is partly Lauhitta's work. She sings the roots along the ridge paths so that a way into the pines bends back out of them, and since the bands came she has sung it stronger. This autumn, one stretch of the ridge has begun to turn everyone who walks it, including the conclave's own children, and she cannot end the song. She has not told Kolpavo Haltunto, who relies on the turning, or the ommenda.
:::
