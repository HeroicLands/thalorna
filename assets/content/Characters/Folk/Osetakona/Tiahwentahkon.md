# Dossier {#dossier}

Tiahwentahkon, Otter Comes, is the war leader (_rotsikwen_) of [[place-tsiontaha|New Clearing]], the River People's village on the southern edge of the Grove of the [[affiliation-osetakona|Maple People]], a few days' paddle from the northern towns of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]]. Two autumns ago a One Fire war party burned it after the summer's pipe kinship had lapsed, killed fourteen people and carried three away. Tiahwentahkon led the party that followed the raiders south and brought back two of the captives.

He is thirty, scarred along one arm, and the best fighter in the [[place-tionhsekargn|Southern Rivers]]. He wants a mourning war for the dead, a large one, raised from every River People village, and he has asked the River People's council for it twice. The Sturgeon clan mother, [[being-otsitsentiotenha|Otsitsentiotenha]], has refused her consent, and without it no mourning war goes out. The war party's men had traded at New Clearing's landing that summer and seen its walls, and he calls the raid a raid under cover of the pipe. The One Fire calls it a fair act of war against a village built on its land. Both accounts are told at every council of the [[affiliation-redstncmpct|Red Stone Compact]].

He posts the young men on the ridges while the women work the black bottomland. A party passing through the Southern Rivers meets his scouts first. A party willing to go south and bring back word of the One Fire's northern towns, their walls and their war parties, can name its price to him.

What he wants is a mourning war raised from every River People village for the fourteen dead of New Clearing.
