# Appearance {#appearance}

Kenrisatsiotie is a 60-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall with a heavy build. She has brown skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kenrisatsiotie is a clan mother (_kontiesa_) of [[place-kahnekwaroten|Hammerstone]], the village of the Copper People of the [[affiliation-osetakona|Maple People]], where the Grove's copper is hammered. Her clan's speaker is the smith [[being-tsikenhakahtiron|Tsikenhakahtiron]], whom she named to his title and can take it back from.

Hammerstone sells copper to the traders of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]] every summer, by way of the [[place-kahsiotargn|Falls Country]] and the rivers south, and the war clubs that burned the River People's village at [[place-tsiontaha|New Clearing]] were headed with it. The River People have asked the great council three times to stop the copper going south, and three times Hammerstone has refused. Tsikenhakahtiron says that copper is the Copper People's maize, and that a village does not stop planting because someone might steal its corn. Kenrisatsiotie has not told him which answer she wants him to give at the next council, and the village knows she is thinking about it.

She is sixty, broad and quiet, with burn scars on her hands from her girlhood at the hearths. She has grandchildren married into River People longhouses. A party that brings her news from the south, of who carries Hammerstone's copper and what it is used for, gets a hearing and a meal, and a party that tells her what the One Fire would pay for copper elsewhere has given her something to weigh.

What she wants is a council answer that costs Hammerstone neither its copper nor its kin downriver.
