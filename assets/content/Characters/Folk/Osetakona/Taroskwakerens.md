# Dossier {#dossier}

Taroskwakerens, Crane Hears, is one of the three saplings (_tsiotsi okwaria_) at [[place-ostakaten|Kettle]], where the great council of the [[affiliation-osetakona|Maple People]] sits. A sapling is a man, or more rarely a woman, who has done something the Grove wants to keep hearing from, given a seat and a voice in council without any line's title. No clan mother can unseat a sapling, and the seat dies with its holder. Against some sixty titles there are three.

He is a Crane man of the Falls People with Island blood. The Crane clan of the Falls People are mostly descendants of the last Island clan mother, who taught her granddaughters the Island songs at night, and they recite the Breaking at every Feast of Bones while the rememberers recite the Grafting. Taroskwakerens was raised for carrying a pipe through the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]]'s country in a war year. His defenders point to him as proof that the titles are open. His clan mothers point out that it took a deed of that size to seat one Crane man, and that he can leave his seat to no one.

He is forty-five, lean from walking, and the best talker on the grass the Grove has. He speaks for nobody's line, so he says what he thinks, which no titled speaker may. A party at the great council finds him the easiest man there to approach and the hardest to bargain with, because nobody can order him to agree.

What he wants is a seat the Crane clan can pass on to someone after him.
