# Appearance {#appearance}

Sorvelitta Kersanto is a woman of 2,050 years who stands 6' 4" tall and is of light build. Her white hair falls straight past her waist and is held at the nape by a cord of braided pine root, her eyes are a steady grey, and her pale skin is without a line. Her left thumb is stained amber by pine resin. She wears a plain tunic of undyed wool the color of dry needles and goes barefoot on the moss.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 2,050. The Mervunno, Keeper of the Stillness. Wants every matter the ommenda takes up spoken to its end.**

Sorvelitta Kersanto is the **Mervunno** of the [[affiliation-nclvhrsllnk|Enclave of Hersellonku]], the Keeper of the Stillness, raised by every voice of the hearths and held for as long as they do not raise another. She sits in the _ommenda_, the council that speaks until it agrees, and keeps the silence in which it speaks. When the hearths have concluded she says aloud what they concluded, in the words they reached and no more. Her own hearth is Kersanto, the hearth of the red pine.

She drives out an outlaw when the ommenda asks it, and she answers for the conclave to the few who ask. Nobody has asked in her lifetime: the Pine People of the [[place-tsitarargn|Pine Country]] know the far ridges only as the place the tall people hold, and no gift has passed in either direction.

## Psyche

### Personality

Sorvelitta is still, unhurried and exact. She leaves a silence after a question long enough that the speaker hears what he has asked, and she answers in the fewest words that are true.

### Motivation

She wants every matter the ommenda takes up spoken to its end, and the word it reaches said aloud once, plainly, to whoever is owed it.

### Strengths

The hearths trust her to say what was concluded and nothing she would have preferred. Her silence is the one thing in the ommenda that nobody argues against.

### Weaknesses

She will not hurry a council for anyone's season or deadline, and a visitor who needs an answer by a date leaves without one.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A word for the far ridges.** [[being-okwiahasterio|Okwiahasterio]], the speaker of the Pine People, wants the ridges asked whether a winter band of hunters may camp at the foot of them. He has no one to ask. A party able to walk out onto the ridges and come down again on the far side is given the question, and any answer comes as the Mervunno gives it: aloud, once, after a long silence.
2. **Days late.** A band's hunters come back from the pines nine days late, each one swearing he was gone for one, and two do not come back at all. The band's leader asks a party to find out what became of them and to learn by what right the far ridges keep a man.

:::secret {title="The side the silence takes"}
Sorvelitta has a view and has never said it aloud. She holds that the ridges are kept by the hearths for the wood and are not owned by them, and she lets the ommenda go on arguing because the day the argument ends, one side of the hearths will leave. She has not told Hapirivo Vessanto that she is on his side of it, and she has not told the lords on the other side that she is not on theirs.
:::
