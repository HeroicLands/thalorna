# Appearance {#appearance}

Auverila Halkorinto is a woman of 1,420 years who stands 5' 9" tall and is of light build. Her long gray hair is loose and unbound, her skin is pale and fine as paper, and her left eye is clouded the color of lake ice. Her right eye is a clear gray. She wears a long gown of undyed linen and sits for hours at the edge of a lake with her feet in the water.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 1,420. Seer of the Lakes. Wants to read what the water shows and to say it plainly.**

Auverila Halkorinto is the seer of the [[affiliation-nclvhrsllnk|Enclave of Hersellonku]]. She reads the still surface of the far lakes, a craft the Senali keep among the oldest they have, and gives what she has read and not what she thinks of it. Her hearth is Halkorinto, the hearth of the cold shore.

The Pine People tell of a pale woman sitting at a lake's edge who does not look up when a hunter passes. A hunter who has seen her says he was not looked at and was not afraid.

## Background

Auverila was a singer before she was a seer. In her third century the glare off the lake ice took the sight of her left eye, and in the clouded eye she began to see what the water showed. She has read the lakes for the hearths for more than a thousand years.

:::secret {title="The ommenda and its Keeper"}
The _ommenda_ asks Auverila before it sits on a hard matter, and she gives her reading to the council in the words it was given.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Auverila is dreamy, exact and very kind, and she cannot be hurried when she is reading. She speaks as if the answer had been given to her.

### Motivation

She wants to read what the water shows truly, and to give it to the hearths in the words it was given, without bending it toward what they hope.

### Strengths

Her readings have never been wrong, and the hearths know it. She can see across a lake what is seen across no other water.

### Weaknesses

She reads only what the water offers, and when it offers nothing, the hearths have no help from her and do not like to say so.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A question for the water.** A spirit-doctor of a hunting band wants a party to carry his question to a far lake and sit at its edge from dawn to dusk: can a closed lodge see across the sea? They are to bring back whatever the water shows them.
2. **What the lake kept.** A hunter's bone-handled knife has turned up on a market stall in the Grove with pine pitch on its blade, and the hunter's band says that nobody sold it. A party is asked where it came from.

:::secret {title="The shore with no pines"}
Auverila has read a lake and seen a shore with no pine on it. She does not know whether it is a shore of the past or of what is to come, and she has told no one in the conclave, because the ommenda would take it for a sign that settles an argument it has not finished.
:::
