# Appearance {#appearance}

Auverila Halkorinto is a woman of 1,420 years who stands 5' 9" tall and is of light build. Her long grey hair is loose and unbound, her skin is pale and fine as paper, and her left eye is clouded the color of lake ice. Her right eye is a clear grey. She wears a long gown of undyed linen and sits for hours at the edge of a lake with her feet in the water.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 1,420. Seer of the Lakes. Wants to read what the water shows and to say it plainly.**

Auverila Halkorinto is the seer of the [[affiliation-nclvhrsllnk|Enclave of Hersellonku]]. She reads the still surface of the far lakes, a craft the Senali keep among the oldest they have, and the _ommenda_ asks her before it sits on a hard matter. She gives what she has read and not what she thinks of it. Her hearth is Halkorinto, the hearth of the cold shore.

The Pine People tell of a pale woman sitting at a lake's edge who does not look up when a hunter passes. A hunter who has seen her says he was not looked at and was not afraid.

## Psyche

### Personality

Auverila is dreamy, exact and very kind, and she cannot be hurried when she is reading. She speaks as if the answer had been given to her.

### Motivation

She wants to read what the water shows truly, and to give it to the ommenda in the words it was given, without bending it toward what the hearths hope.

### Strengths

Her readings have never been wrong, and the hearths know it. She can see across a lake what is seen across no other water.

### Weaknesses

She reads only what the water offers, and when it offers nothing, the ommenda has no help from her and does not like to say so.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A question for the water.** A spirit-doctor of a hunting band has sent a question to the far lakes, asking whether a closed lodge can see across the sea. He wants a party to sit at a far lake's edge from dawn to dusk and bring back whatever the water shows them.
2. **What the lake kept.** A hunter's bone-handled knife has turned up on a market stall in the Grove with pine pitch on its blade, and the hunter's band says that nobody sold it. A party is asked where it came from.

:::secret {title="The shore with no pines"}
Auverila has read a lake and seen a shore with no pine on it. She does not know whether it is a shore of the past or of what is to come, and she has told no one in the conclave, because the ommenda would take it for a sign that settles an argument it has not finished.
:::
