# Appearance {#appearance}

A Tsaihwo soldier wears a padded coat in his house's color, with a lacquered helmet and a spear or a bow, and in a garrison a short sword besides. He carries no granted name, and his house badge is the only mark of whom he serves.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Each house of the [[affiliation-jenchaiempr|Jenchai Empire]] raises its soldiers from its castle town and villages at two and a half men for every hundred bales assessed, and the [[affiliation-zenjaidmn|Zenjai Domain]] raises four. They hold the gates of the castle towns and the garrisons of the passes, crew the toll-boats and ships of the houses that keep fleets, and march when the lord musters. A soldier stands where his retainer captain puts him.

## Background

A soldier comes from the streets of a castle town or from a village whose headman answers for its quota. He serves under a retainer who holds the office of captain, and he is fed from the lord's granary.

## Psyche

### Personality

Steady, plain-spoken with his equals and silent before his betters. A soldier at a gate asks the questions his captain has told him to ask.

### Motivation

Keep his post, keep his captain's favor, and come home to his village.

### Strengths

Soldiers hold ground in numbers and know the roads and passes of their own domain.

### Weaknesses

A soldier does not act beyond his captain's order, and a gate held by soldiers whose captain is away waits for him to return.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

Each soldier serves the house that raised him and the retainer who commands his post. The same template serves for the garrison men of the [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|Muwojai Domain]], the crews of the [[affiliation-shuiwojaidmn|Shuiwojai Domain]]'s toll-boats and the retainers' escorts of any house.
