# Appearance {#appearance}

A Maiju officer wears a fur-lined riding coat and boots stiff with frost, and carries a notched tally for counting horses. He moves his lips when he counts and does not stop for anyone until he has finished.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The [Maiju]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} hold the office of Keeper of the Stables in the [[affiliation-zenjaidmn|Zenjai Domain]]. They keep the horse pastures at [Maidu]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, enter the horses that come down to the stable-yard below the hall at [[place-buiwu|Buiwu]], and mount the officers of the northern levy. Every rider answers to a roll the officer can recite.

## Background

An officer of the lineage learns the pastures and the roll from boyhood, and holds his place by the lineage's office and by [[being-zenjaitsuiwo|Zenjai Tsuiwo]]'s trust.

## Psyche

### Personality

Blunt, sleepless when the count is wrong, and careful to report a shortfall to his lord before anyone else hears of it.

### Motivation

Keep the roll true and the levy mounted.

### Strengths

He knows every horse of the levy by its marks and every groom by his habits.

### Weaknesses

A short count is his to answer for, and he cannot leave the stable-yard while one stands.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

He serves Zenjai Tsuiwo and the Zenjai Domain through the Maiju's office. The grooms of the stable-yard work under him.

## Plot Hooks

1. A Maiju officer receives guests at the stable-yard below the hall at Buiwu in the first week of the cold, while the stewards of two blocs look for a reason to be in the yard: [[scenario-rcbfrsnw|Rice Before the Snow]].
2. Strangers who come into the stable-yard are counted along with the horses, and the officer wants to know whose colors they wear.

:::secret {title="The short count"}
The officer has found a short count on the horse roll and has reported mounts missing from the levy. The roll is the one document in Buiwu that his lord does not want a bidder to read, because it tells a bidder how few riders the north can mount. The lord wants the horses found before the bidders learn it, and the referee decides whether they strayed, were sold or were taken. A trail of frozen dung leads to the shoulder road in any case.
