# Appearance {#appearance}

A pilot stands in the bow with a sounding line coiled over one shoulder and his eyes on the color of the water. Salt has whitened the seams of his coat, and he speaks without turning his head.

# Dossier {#dossier}

[[place-easternstrt|The Eastern Strait]] is narrow enough to cross in a day or two and treacherous enough to wreck a fleet. A hull that crosses between the great island and the lesser one, or between the islands and the mainland, takes a Jenchai pilot aboard for the set of the water, and passes within sight of the fleet of the [[affiliation-shuiwojaidmn|Shuiwojai Domain]] and the raiders of the [[affiliation-rinjaidmn|Rinjai Domain]].

## Background

A pilot learns the strait from a pilot before him, and his trade passes in his family. He serves whoever pays him, and he pays the tolls of whichever house holds the stretch of water he crosses.

## Psyche

### Personality

Taciturn, superstitious about the water and plain about money.

### Motivation

Bring the hull through, take the coin and keep his name good with every house that watches the strait.

### Strengths

He knows where a ship lies at anchor, whether it has lowered a boat, and which cove holds which house's hulls.

### Weaknesses

He tells every house that asks him what he has seen, the hull he pilots included.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

Pilots serve no single house. They keep the offerings at the headland shrines and expect the crews they carry to keep them too.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-prfctrdhll|The Prefect in the Red Hall]]: a pilot takes a party across the watched water to [[place-rinwu|Rinwu]] and tells it where the mainland [[doc-vesseljunks|war junk]] lies.
2. **The headland offering.** A pilot refuses a crossing because a crew would not leave an offering at the first headland shrine.
3. **Every house that asks.** Three houses ask a pilot at one anchorage what he has seen, and he wants a party's help deciding which answer keeps his name good with all of them.
