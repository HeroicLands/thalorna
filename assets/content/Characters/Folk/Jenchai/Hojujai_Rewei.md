# Appearance {#appearance}

Hojujai Rewei is a 38-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of light build. She has light skin, a clear complexion, long black hair worn bound low and dark brown eyes that do not leave the person speaking. The keys of the castle and its granary hang at her sash, and a white cord marks mourning for her husband.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Hojujai Rewei (Dawn Jade) is the widow of [[being-henwohojujai|Hojujai Henwo]], the last Teng Ja. Since his death in 709 AF she has governed the [[affiliation-hojujaidmn|Hojujai Domain]] as regent for her son [[being-zijahojujai|Hojujai Zija]], the Dauzhwo, who is fourteen. She sits at [[place-yanwu|Yanwu]] and decides in his name. Hojujai leads the loyalist bloc with [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|Muwojai Domain]], and the bloc wants the office of Teng Ja for the Hojujai heir when he comes of age, or for nobody.

## Background

**The formation.** She was born the daughter of a Shwizhwo of the domain and learned a granary roll before a castle's etiquette.

**The marriage.** She married Henwo, eighteen years older, and kept the household and granary of Yanwu while he held the office of Teng Ja. Their son Zija was born in 705 AF.

**The regency.** Widowed at twenty-eight in 709 AF, she held the mountain for her three-year-old son. The court was ready to confer the office on her regency, and the War of the Empty Seat followed. She kept the house whole, with 1,750 men and 20 ships.

**The verdict.** The mountain is rumbling, and the omen-readers are divided on whether it condemns a widow's regency. An eruption before Zija is of age would settle it against her.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is grave, exact and stubborn. She holds the house by being seen to hold it.

### Motivation

She wants the office for Zija when he comes of age, or for nobody, and she wants to hand him a house whole.

### Strengths

She holds the keys, the granary and the household's loyalty, and the islands honor her faith.

### Weaknesses

Her authority rests on a reading of the mountain that the next tremor can reverse. She has named a price to the independent houses, and the granary must meet it.

## Social

### Family

- [[being-zijahojujai|Hojujai Zija]]: Her son, for whom she governs.

### Others

- [[being-henzoshuiwo|Shuiwojai Henzo]]: Her rival, the favorite for the office.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-hojujaidmn|Hojujai Domain]]**: Regent, governing in the name of the Dauzhwo with the keys of the castle and its granary.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The tremor.** Hojai shook in the night, and the shrine's Tanchei and the sorcerers disagree on what it means. Rewei hires a party to carry the shrine's record to an omen-reader on Zijai who will read it without regard to her.
2. **The bid.** Henzo's silver and Rewei's offer both stand before the lords of Wojai and Zenjai. She hires a party as envoys to carry her price.
3. **The envoy at Yanwu.** The Shuiwojai envoy sends word to Hawu after every tremor. Rewei asks a party to learn what he has said.
