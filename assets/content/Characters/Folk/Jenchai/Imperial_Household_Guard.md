# Appearance {#appearance}

A guardsman of the Emperor's household wears a plain dark coat without a house badge, because the household serves no house. On duty at the court he carries a sword and stands where he is put. On the road he carries a lacquered case for the court's letters and holds it in both hands at every crossing.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The household guard is the whole of the force the Emperor commands. Its guardsmen keep the doors of the halls at [[place-lengwou|Lengwou]] and on [[place-zojai|Zojai]], stand at the audiences the throne grants to envoys, and carry the throne's sealed answers to the lords who petitioned for them. Because the Emperor does not leave Zojai, every answer he gives leaves it in a guardsman's hands.

## Background

Guardsmen come from retainer families of the eight houses and swear to the throne in place of a lord. A guardsman who carries a letter crosses every ferry and gate under his own name, enters it in every roll he is asked to sign, and does not open what he carries.

## Psyche

### Personality

Silent, exact and patient. A guardsman answers the question he is asked and no other, and he treats every lord's hall as a place he is passing through.

### Motivation

Deliver what the throne gives him whole, to the hand it is addressed to, and return with the receipt.

### Strengths

His errand is the throne's, and no lord can stop him on the road without being seen to stop the Emperor.

### Weaknesses

He travels alone or with a guard of one, his name is on every roll he passes, and the throne has no other force to send after him.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

He answers to the [[affiliation-jenchaiempr|Jenchai Empire]] and to [[being-weilengjnch|Weileng]] alone. On the road he depends on the passes and the toll-keepers of the [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|Muwojai Domain]], whose lord keeps every road to the capital.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-ltrforhawu|A Letter for Hawu]]: a guardsman carries the Emperor's sealed answer over [[place-zujufrry|The Zuju Ferry]] to [[place-hawu|Hawu]].
2. [[scenario-prfctrdhll|The Prefect in the Red Hall]]: a guardsman stands at the audience the throne grants a mainland envoy, and carries the court's request to [[place-rinwu|Rinwu]] when no one else does.
3. **The receipt.** A guardsman is back from a lord's hall without the receipt he was sent to bring, and the throne wants to know whose hall kept it.
