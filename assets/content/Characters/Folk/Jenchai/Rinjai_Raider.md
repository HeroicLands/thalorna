# Appearance {#appearance}

A raider of [[place-rinjai|Rinjai]] goes barefoot on deck and in a short red jacket ashore, with a bow, a boarding knife and rope-hardened hands. A captain among them carries a sword and stands at the rail.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The ships of the [[affiliation-rinjaidmn|Rinjai Domain]] are the raiders of the Nine. They lie in the coves at the mouth of [[place-easternstrt|The Eastern Strait]], take tolls on the water and cross to raid the mainland's southern coast, which the lords of the Nine count as their ancestral right. They are crewed from the harbor streets of [[place-rinwu|Rinwu]], and their captains are the retainers of [[being-hojarinjai|Rinjai Hoja]], who commanded raiding ships with them before he held the red mountain.

## Background

A raider is raised among hulls in the harbor town and goes to sea as a boy. A captain holds his seat in the red hall by the hulls his household commands.

## Psyche

### Personality

Quick, loud on the quay and silent on the water. A raider wants to fire the first arrow and waits for his lord's word.

### Motivation

Take a prize, come home with his hull, and stand nearer the front of the hall.

### Strengths

Raiders know the set of the strait, the coves and the mainland shore better than any crew that comes against them.

### Weaknesses

The domain's rice is thin and its hulls are few. A raid that goes wrong costs ships the lord cannot replace.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

Raiders serve Rinjai Hoja and the Rinjai Domain, which stands with [The Strait]{.name data-type="affiliation"} bloc and its lord [[being-henzoshuiwo|Shuiwojai Henzo]].

## Plot Hooks

1. Raiders watch a mainland war junk from the rocks of the coves, held back from the first arrow only by their lord's word: [[scenario-prfctrdhll|The Prefect in the Red Hall]].
2. A raiding captain brings home more captives than his lord can keep as guests.
