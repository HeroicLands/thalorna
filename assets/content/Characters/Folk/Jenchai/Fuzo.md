# Appearance {#appearance}

Fuzo is a 54-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of light build. He has golden skin, a long face lined at the eyes, a shaven head and dark brown eyes. He wears the plain gray robe of his vows with a rosary of black wood on the left wrist.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Fuzo (Calm Dragon) was Emperor of [[affiliation-jenchaiempr|Jenchai Empire]] until he abdicated in 712 AF, the price of the peace that ended the [War of the Empty Seat]{.name data-type="lore/history"}. He took vows and a new name, and the old name is not used. He keeps a household in [[place-lengwou|Lengwou]], below the palace where his son [[being-weilengjnch|Weileng]] reigns. The Dauzhwo resent a retired Son of Heaven who eats while the throne goes short.

## Background

**The reign.** Born in the palace above Lengwou, he came to the throne young and held it for decades. In 693 AF he conferred the office of Teng Ja on [[being-henwohojujai|Hojujai Henwo]], and the houses kept the peace for sixteen years.

**The abdication.** In 712 AF the lords made a Son of Heaven step down to pay for their quarrel, and the Nine count that their own sin. Fuzo is the evidence. He took his vows that year and left the throne to his eleven-year-old son.

**The household.** Attendants, scribes, cooks and a small guard who do not answer to the court live in it, at part of the 9,000 bales the court spends against the 4,100 it received in 719 AF.

:::secret {title="The household's unpaid bills"}
The household owes the rice factor in Lengwou for months of deliveries, and it hides the bills from the Dauzhwo. If the factor stops delivering or talks, every lord learns that the court's poverty has reached the Retired Emperor's table.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

He is composed and unhurried, and answers a question with a question.

### Motivation

He wants his son's reign to outlast the bargain that bought it, with the Emperor advised by counsel and not by command.

### Strengths

He was Son of Heaven himself, so a house that wins his ear has a voice the throne hears. He holds no authority; the Teng Ja is his son's to confer.

### Weaknesses

His household costs more than the court can pay, and the houses resent paying it. He holds no office and no force.

## Social

### Family

- [[being-weilengjnch|Weileng]]: His son, the Emperor, whom he advises.

### Affiliations

## Plot Hooks

1. **The thin measure.** The rice sent to the household's gate has been short for weeks. Fuzo's steward asks a party to learn from the factor in Lengwou why.
2. **The private word.** A Dauzhwo's envoy pays well for a private word on the Teng Ja. Fuzo will not give it, and the envoy offers a party a fee to change that.
3. **The guard's captain.** The household guard does not answer to the court. Its captain has found a new purse among his men's belts and asks a party to learn which house is paying them.
