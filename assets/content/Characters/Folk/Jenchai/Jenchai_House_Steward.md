# Appearance {#appearance}

A steward wears his house's color in plain silk, carries a brush case and a knotted tally-cord, and stands with his hands in his sleeves when he is kept waiting. Ink stains the side of his right hand.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Every house of the [[affiliation-jenchaiempr|Jenchai Empire]] keeps its wealth as rice, and a steward keeps the rice: the lord's roll of villages and their assessments in bales, the granary where the lord's four parts in ten are delivered, and the accounts of the stipends paid from it. When a house names a price in rice to another, its steward carries the figure and answers for it.

## Background

A steward comes from a retainer family that writes a fair hand and holds the office by inheritance. He answers to the lord, or to whoever holds the keys of the granary for the lord, and he knows to the bale what the granary holds.

## Psyche

### Personality

Exact, cautious and reluctant to name a figure he has not counted.

### Motivation

Keep the granary sound and the roll honest, and keep his house from promising rice it does not hold.

### Strengths

He knows what his house has, what it owes and what it can move before the roads close.

### Weaknesses

He can only answer for a figure he has been shown, and a lord who names a price without him leaves him to find the gap.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

Each steward serves one house and the lord or regent who holds its keys. The same template serves for the stewards of the Strait and the loyalists who carry their blocs' bids, and for the steward of a regent's granary.

2. A steward asks a party to count a granary with him before the roads close.
