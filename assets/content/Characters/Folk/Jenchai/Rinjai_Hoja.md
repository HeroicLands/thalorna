# Appearance {#appearance}

Rinjai Hoja is a 29-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of light build. He has tawny, sun-kissed skin, an angular face, black hair in a topknot and dark brown eyes that watch the water more than the person speaking to him. He carries a sword in a red lacquered scabbard, the color of his house and his hall, and a rope burn runs across his right palm. He talks fast about ships and slowly about anything else.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Rinjai Hoja (Fire Blade) is the Dauzhwo of the [[affiliation-rinjaidmn|Rinjai Domain]]. He holds [[place-rinjai|Rinjai]], the red mountain of the lesser island at the strait's mouth, by the Emperor's patent and rules it from the castle town of [[place-rinwu|Rinwu]]. He musters 650 men and 25 ships, and his ships are the raiders of the Nine. He is sworn to [[being-henzoshuiwo|Shuiwojai Henzo]], and Rinjai stands with [The Strait]{.name data-type="affiliation"} bloc, which wants Henzo named Teng Ja.

## Background

**The formation.** He was born at [[place-rinwu|Rinwu]] in 690 AF and raised among hulls, and went to sea as a boy.

**The captain.** He commanded raiding ships on Tantsou's southern coast before he held the mountain, and the captains who followed him then are his retainers now.

**The oath.** He took the red mountain young, in the years of the War of the Empty Seat, and swore to [[being-henzoshuiwo|Shuiwojai Henzo]], eighteen years his elder, whose ships and silver carried the Strait's side.

**The prefect.** His raiders took a Tantsou prefect off the mainland coast. The court wants the man returned as a gesture of heaven's magnanimity, and Henzo wants the ransom.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is quick, bold and loyal. He decides by the nearest obligation and answers for it afterward.

### Motivation

He wants to keep the oath and the prefect both, and he wants the one deed that leaves him standing before the court and Henzo.

### Strengths

He holds the sea gate of the realm, and the raiders of Rinjai follow him without being asked twice.

### Weaknesses

His rice is thin and his muster small. A raid that goes wrong on Tantsou's shore costs him hulls he cannot replace.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-henzoshuiwo|Shuiwojai Henzo]]: His liege and the lord of the Strait.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-rinjaidmn|Rinjai Domain]]**: Dauzhwo, holding Rinjai by the Emperor's patent.
- **[[affiliation-jenchaiempr|Jenchai Empire]]**: Dauzhwo of the Empire, sworn to the Emperor.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The prefect in the hall.** Hoja keeps the prefect as a guest in the red hall. A party hired by a Tantsou envoy offers to buy the man out, and a party hired by Henzo offers to take him to Hawu.
2. **The strait watch.** A Tantsou war junk has been sighted off the coves, and Hoja wants a party to learn whether it comes to fetch the prefect or to burn the harbor at [Jawo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}.
3. **The red mountain.** A shrine Tanchei of Rinjai brings a reading of the mountain that favors Henzo's claim, and Hoja wants a party to learn who paid for it.
