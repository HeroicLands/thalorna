# Appearance {#appearance}

Wejai Rowo is a 40-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. He has tawny, weathered skin, short black hair, stubble, an oval face, an upturned nose, straight brows, a small mouth and dark brown eyes. His hands are cracked by salt, and a toggle of whale-bone hangs from his sword-cord. His coat is oiled sailcloth over plain lacquer.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Wejai Rowo, called Thunder Warrior, is the Dauzhwo of the [[affiliation-wejaidmn|Wejai Domain]]. He holds [[place-wejai|Wejai]], the open-ocean coast of the great island, by the Emperor's patent and rules it from [[place-zuwu|Zuwu]]. His muster is 600 men and 15 ships, whalers and coastal guard boats built to stand off the typhoons.

## Background

**The strand.** Whatever the sea throws on the reefs belongs to the lord by wreck-right, and his officers walk the shore after every storm.

**The wreck.** A typhoon drove a foreign hull onto the reefs of [Wewo]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}. A survivor claims to be an envoy to the court, and his letters are in the cargo.

**The choice.** If Rowo keeps the cargo, he keeps the letters and knows what no other house knows. If he sends the man to [[place-zojai|Zojai]], the letters cross the [[affiliation-muwojaidmn|Muwojai Domain]]'s roads.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is shrewd and restless, and he is at ease only near salt water.

### Motivation

Wejai has the fewest people of the four independents, and Rowo wants a better seat at the [Dawn Rite]{.name data-type="lore/custom"} than his 600 men and 15 ships have bought him. In the contest for the vacant Teng Ja he has named his price to no bloc, and the letters are his bargaining weight. Zenjai and Wojai are the houses being courted, and he wants to be asked next.

### Strengths

He holds letters no other house has seen, and the typhoon coast is a wall to any fleet that does not know it.

### Weaknesses

He has the letters and no one he trusts to read them.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-tsuijairezo|Tsuijai Rezo]]: a fellow independent. Each of the four houses has its own price.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-wejaidmn|Wejai Domain]]**: Dauzhwo, holding Wejai by patent.
- **[[affiliation-jenchaiempr|Jenchai Empire]]**: one of the eight Great Lords.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The unread letters.** Rowo wants someone to read the envoy's letters without learning more than he intends, and pays in a berth on a whaler.
2. **The road to Zojai.** The survivor must reach the court, and Rowo wants an escort that keeps the letters out of Muwojai's hands, or a reason not to send him.
3. **The strand after the storm.** Cargo is missing from the wreck before it was counted, and Rowo wants the thief found before the survivor learns of it.
