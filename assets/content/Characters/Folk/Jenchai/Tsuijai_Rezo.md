# Appearance {#appearance}

Tsuijai Rezo is a 52-year-old man who stands 5'5" tall and is of light build. He has smooth skin of a medium tone, short dark brown hair, a long face, a narrow nose, thin brows and mouth, and black eyes. Old burn scars cross both forearms. He dresses in undyed hemp and a lacquered iron-plate vest, and the smell of the smelters is in everything he wears.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tsuijai Rezo, called Bright Dragon, is the Dauzhwo of the [[affiliation-tsuijaidmn|Tsuijai Domain]]. He holds [[place-tsuijai|Tsuijai]], the iron mountain of the north-west uplands, by the Emperor's patent and rules it from [[place-tsuiwu|Tsuiwu]]. His muster is 550 men, none of whom sails, and his forge arms them and sells to every other house.

## Background

**The forge.** Rezo learned the smith's trade at [Tsaigen]{.name data-type="place/settlement"} and still works at the forge himself. The [Jaju]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} keep the armory and the forge-village of [Tsaigen]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, and every blade that leaves it is entered in their roll. An [[being-embrhnd|Emberhound]] lies across the forge-yard's threshold.

**The blade.** A new Teng Ja receives a blade forged at [Tsaigen]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, and the smiths will not begin it until the Emperor names a holder. Someone has asked them to begin early, and the Jaju have not said who.

**The iron.** Tsuijai alone has the iron and the swordsmiths of the Nine, so every lord comes to him to buy. He sells to each and lets none of them own him.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is precise and dry, and he takes his time with a question.

### Motivation

He wants every lord of the Nine to keep buying at [Tsaigen]{.name data-type="place/settlement"}, which means the balance must stand. In the contest for the vacant Teng Ja he has named his price to no bloc: it is the forging of the blade, whoever carries it. He wants to know who asked the smiths to start early.

### Strengths

He holds the one forge the other lords cannot do without.

### Weaknesses

He has 550 men and no fleet, and a bloc that stops buying or takes his iron by force is more than he can answer.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Who asked.** Rezo wants the party to find who asked the Jaju to begin the blade early, and for which bidder it was meant.
2. **The order for blades.** An envoy of one bloc wants blades beyond any peacetime need. Rezo wants to learn whether the order is for war before he fills it.
3. **The binding.** The lineage renews the Emberhound's binding with each master of the forge, and the Jaju ask Rezo for a witness who belongs to no house.
