# Appearance {#appearance}

An agent at a landing wears a plain coat and a small house badge he does not draw attention to. An agent in another lord's hall wears his house's color openly and stands near the door. Either one listens more than he speaks.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The houses of the [[affiliation-jenchaiempr|Jenchai Empire]] watch each other through their own retainers. A house posts an agent where news passes: at a landing whose toll roll names every party, in the castle town of a house whose granary or mountain matters to it, or in the hall of a sworn lord, to remind him whose oath he swore. An agent reports to his own lord's steward or to the lord himself.

## Background

An agent is a retainer with a granted name, chosen for his patience and his memory. He pays clerks, innkeepers and ferrymen for what they see, and he sends word home by runner or by the next hull.

## Psyche

### Personality

Courteous, attentive and slow to commit. An agent asks his questions as though they were idle.

### Motivation

Learn first what his house needs to know, and be seen by no one while he learns it.

### Strengths

He knows who has been asking what, and he has silver to buy the rest.

### Weaknesses

The badge on his coat names his house to anyone who reads it, and following him to his lodging names his errand.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

Each agent serves one house. The same template serves for a house's agent at [[place-zujufrry|The Zuju Ferry]], the [[affiliation-shuiwojaidmn|Shuiwojai Domain]]'s envoy in [[place-yanwu|Yanwu]] and [[being-henzoshuiwo|Shuiwojai Henzo]]'s liaison in the red hall at [[place-rinwu|Rinwu]].

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-ltrforhawu|A Letter for Hawu]]: an agent has been asking at the Zuju landing which parties came through this month.
2. [[scenario-rcbfrsnw|Rice Before the Snow]]: an envoy in Yanwu sends word to [[place-hawu|Hawu]] after every tremor of the mountain.
3. [[scenario-prfctrdhll|The Prefect in the Red Hall]]: a liaison stands by the door of the red hall and reminds a raider lord whose oath he swore.

:::secret {title="What the agents are sent to do"}
A house's agent waits on the far bank at Zuju for the throne's courier, and the Game Master chooses whose. He knows the courier's name and the hour he crossed, which only the roll can tell. Henzo's agent offers to take the letter on to Hawu in the courier's place and to pay for the favor. Another house's agent offers to look at the seal and promises to put it back as he found it.

The Shuiwojai envoy in Yanwu reports to Hawu what the granary above the town holds, and the Strait learns of any shortfall from him whether or not anyone else tells it.
