# Appearance {#appearance}

Zenjai Tsuiwo is a 33-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall and is of heavy build. He has light, weathered skin, black hair shaved at the sides and tied at the crown, a square face, straight brows, a thin mouth and brown eyes. His left ear has lost its lobe to frost. He wears fur-lined lacquered armor in the hall and sits with his hands on his knees.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Zenjai Tsuiwo, called Iron Warrior, is the Dauzhwo of the [[affiliation-zenjaidmn|Zenjai Domain]]. He holds [[place-zenjai|Zenjai]], the cold mountain of the north, by the Emperor's patent and rules it from [[place-buiwu|Buiwu]]. The house keeps no fleet and raises the northern levy of four men to the hundred bales, 1,520 in all. He is blunt and short of speech.

## Background

**The levy.** The [Maiju]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} keep the horse pastures and mount his officers, and every rider answers to a roll he can recite.

**The half-ration winter.** The north came through last winter on half rations, and Tsuiwo knows the cost of it village by village.

**The bidding.** [[affiliation-shuiwojaidmn|Shuiwojai Domain]] and the regent [Hojujai Rewei]{.name data-type="being/npc"} are both bidding for him in rice they do not have. He will put 1,500 spears behind whichever bloc feeds his province.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is stern and practical, and he treats a promise as a debt not yet paid.

### Motivation

He wants the granaries of the north full before the first snow, and that matters more to him than who holds the office of Teng Ja. Zenjai joining either bloc ends the balance of the Nine, and he means to sell that for grain, not for titles.

### Strengths

His high pastures raise the horses of the Nine, and the north obeys him.

### Weaknesses

He is hungry. A bloc that delivers rice owns him, and he has not decided how to prevent it.

## Social

### Others

- [[being-wojaidoja|Wojai Doja]]: the other lord whose choice ends the balance, who answers his bidders with a patience Tsuiwo lacks.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-zenjaidmn|Zenjai Domain]]**: Dauzhwo, holding Zenjai by patent.
- **[[affiliation-jenchaiempr|Jenchai Empire]]**: one of the eight Great Lords.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The real rice.** Tsuiwo commits his levy to whoever delivers grain to Buiwu before the snow. A party hired by either bloc to carry it can tip the realm.
2. **The empty granaries.** He sends the party to count what Shuiwojai and Hojujai actually hold, because both bids may be in rice that does not exist.
3. **The horse roll.** A Maiju officer reports mounts missing from the levy. Tsuiwo wants them found before the bidders learn how few he can field.
