# Appearance {#appearance}

A shrine Tanchei wears the plain robe of the shrine and carries the mountain record in a lacquered box. Ash from the summit settles in the folds of the sleeves.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Each mountain's summit shrine is kept by the domain's [[lore-tanchei|Tanchei]]. They keep the mountain record, which holds every eruption, earthquake and spring of the mountain with the year and the lord in whose time it fell, and they read the mountain's moods. An eruption is read as heaven's attention, and in a disputed year a lord's claim to heaven's favor rests on what the shrine says.

## Background

A Tanchei of a summit shrine learns the record and the readings at the shrine, and the shrine is fed from the villages whose four parts in ten go to it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Grave and careful, aware that every reading is weighed by more than one house.

### Motivation

Keep the record true and the shrine fed, and give a reading the shrine can stand behind.

### Strengths

A reading of the mountain carries heaven's authority, and no lord can dismiss it openly.

### Weaknesses

A reading is worth silver to every house that wants heaven's verdict on its side, and a Tanchei who accepts that silver has sold the shrine's word.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

Each Tanchei serves the summit shrine of one mountain, and through it the domain and the court.
