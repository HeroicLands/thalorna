# Appearance {#appearance}

Hellurivo Tolkunto is a man of 1,760 years who stands 6' 5" tall and is of light build. His white hair hangs loose to the middle of his back, his eyes are a deep leaf-green, and his tanned skin is smooth as a young man's. Both palms are stained green to the wrist. He wears a tunic of undyed bark-cloth and carries a staff of grown wood that bears no mark of a blade.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 1,760. The Lirnanno, Keeper of the Vines. Wants the old jungle left as it is.**

Hellurivo Tolkunto is the **Lirnanno** of the [[affiliation-nclvlrnst|Enclave of Lirnasto]], the Keeper of the Vines, raised by every voice of the hearths from among its lords and held for as long as they do not raise another. He keeps the old jungle, drives out an outlaw, and answers for the understanding the conclave keeps with [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]] to the few who ask. His hearth is Tolkunto, the hearth of the buttress root.

Nobody outside Lirnasto knows who he is. The realm's Ts'enmah deal with the edge of the jungle and never with the conclave, and the Lirnanno deals with the realm only by what the jungle does. He has never come to an edge town and has never been asked to.

## Psyche

### Personality

Hellurivo is gentle, slow-spoken and entirely without hurry. He is courteous to everything that lives, the vines included, and speaks to a trespasser's tracks as he would to the trespasser.

### Motivation

He wants the old jungle left as it is, and the understanding kept by the realm without having to be spoken of.

### Strengths

The hearths raise him by every voice, and he has never had to lift his own. The paths at the forest's edge answer to him.

### Weaknesses

He is slow to act, and he waits for the hearths to agree when an hour would have decided it.

## Social

The lord [[being-nantoruttahmmr|Nantorutta Hummarinto]], who is his eyes at the edge, and the seer [[being-pallevottakssn|Pallevotta Kossanto]].

## Plot Hooks

1. **The knotted cord.** A Ts'enmah of an edge town finds a cord of knotted vine on his threshold the morning after a Kulmah's foresters have marked a stand of the old trees. Nobody saw who left it, and nobody in the town can read it. He asks a party to find someone who can.
2. **A causeway taken back.** The vines at the forest's edge grow across a causeway in a single night and the Ha'nalmah of the nearest town stops laying stone. He asks a party to learn who in the old jungle has been offended and what is wanted.

:::secret {title="The first stand"}
Hellurivo has decided in private what the conclave has not: if fire is set in the old trees, he will go out alone to the first stand marked and stand in it, and let the realm decide whether to burn a Senalo with the trees. He has told no one, the lords least of all, because they would not let him.
:::
