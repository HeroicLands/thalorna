# Appearance {#appearance}

Hellurivo Tolkunto is a man of 1,760 years who stands 6' 5" tall and is of light build. His white hair hangs loose to the middle of his back, his eyes are a deep leaf-green, and his tanned skin is smooth as a young man's. Both palms are stained green to the wrist. He wears a tunic of undyed bark-cloth and carries a staff of grown wood that bears no mark of a blade.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 1,760. Lord of Lirnasto. Wants the old jungle left as it is.**

Hellurivo Tolkunto is a lord of the [[affiliation-nclvlrnst|Enclave of Lirnasto]], raised by every voice of the hearths. His hearth is Tolkunto, the hearth of the buttress root. He has never come to an edge town of [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]] and has never been asked to.

## Background

Hellurivo was born in the old jungle and has never left it. He spent his early centuries among the growers, learning to coax the living wood, and the hearths raised him a lord for the patience with which he tended the oldest trees through a drought that killed the young ones.

:::secret {title="The conclave behind the trees"}
Hellurivo is the Lirnanno, the Keeper of the Vines, raised by every voice of the hearths from among the lords and holding the office for as long as they do not raise another. He keeps the old jungle, drives out an outlaw, and answers for the understanding with K'ankul to the few who ask. Nobody outside Lirnasto knows who he is: the realm's Ts'enmah deal with the edge of the jungle and never with the conclave, and the Keeper deals with the realm only by what the jungle does. The paths at the forest's edge answer to him.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Hellurivo is gentle, slow-spoken and entirely without hurry. He is courteous to everything that lives, the vines included, and speaks to a trespasser's tracks as he would to the trespasser.

### Motivation

He wants the old jungle left as it is, and the understanding kept by the realm without having to be spoken of.

### Strengths

The hearths raise him by every voice, and he has never had to lift his own.

### Weaknesses

He is slow to act, and he waits for the hearths to agree when an hour would have decided it.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The knotted cord.** A cord of knotted vine hangs across the end of a causeway where it stops short of the old trees, and the Ha'nalmah's patrol will not cut it down. Nobody in the town can read it, and the Ts'enmah asks a party to find someone who can.
2. **A causeway taken back.** The vines at the forest's edge grow across a causeway in a single night and the Ha'nalmah of the nearest town stops laying stone. He asks a party to learn who in the old jungle has been offended and what is wanted.

:::secret {title="The first stand"}
Hellurivo has decided in private what the conclave has not: if fire is set in the old trees, he will go out alone to the first stand marked and stand in it, and let the realm decide whether to burn a Senalo with the trees. He has told no one, the lords least of all, because they would not let him.
:::
