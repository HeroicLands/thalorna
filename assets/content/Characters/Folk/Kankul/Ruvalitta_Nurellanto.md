# Appearance {#appearance}

Ruvalitta Nurellanto is a woman of 830 years who stands 5' 8" tall and is of light build. Her auburn hair is braided with a strand of green cord, her eyes are a clear blue, and her fair skin is freckled at the nose. Her palms are ridged with calluses from cord work. She wears a plain gown of bark-cloth and has a hank of vine cord looped at her belt that she knots without looking.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 830. Weaver of Living Cord. Wants the reckoning kept true.**

Ruvalitta Nurellanto is the cord-weaver of the [[affiliation-nclvlrnst|Enclave of Lirnasto]]. The Senali build by singing and growing, and her work is the living vine: she spins it into cord and rope and knots into it the signs the hearths use to mark the edge of their country. Her hearth is Nurellanto, the hearth of the hanging moss.

She also keeps the long reckoning, the cord on which the conclave records what it has to count. It is the one record the Senali of Lirnasto keep that is not sung.

## Psyche

### Personality

Ruvalitta is exact, humorous and slow to be shocked, and she talks while she knots and does not lose her place.

### Motivation

She wants the reckoning kept true, and she wants every knot on it to be one the hearths would own.

### Strengths

She never mis-ties a knot, and she remembers every cord she has handled in a thousand years.

### Weaknesses

She keeps a record of everything and cannot be persuaded to leave something uncounted, even when counting it hurts.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A marker on a tree.** A rubber-tapper's child has taken a knotted cord from a tree at the edge of the old jungle and carried it into town, and since then the paths at the line turn the town's tappers back before noon. The Ha'nalmah asks a party to carry the cord back and tie it where it hung.
2. **Cord for a seller.** A tapper found a length of living vine cord tied across a path at the line, and a rope-seller of the edge towns says it is stronger than any rope he makes. He wants more of it and hires a party to learn where it comes from, though the line turns back everyone who goes to look.

:::secret {title="The cord she leaves unknotted"}
The stands the Kulmah's foresters have marked for burning have outrun the length of the long reckoning, and she has begun a second cord. She has stopped knotting the newest ones, because the hearths read the cord when they gather and she does not want them to count.
:::
