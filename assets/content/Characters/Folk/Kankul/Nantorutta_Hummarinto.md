# Appearance {#appearance}

Nantorutta Hummarinto is a woman of 1,090 years who stands 6' 0" tall and is of light build. Her black hair is braided and pinned in a crown, her eyes are brown, and her olive skin is faintly translucent. A line of small leaf marks runs along the inside of her left arm, one for each path she has walked. She wears a tunic of dyed bark-cloth in the greens of the forest floor, and she is almost always barefoot.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 1,090. Lord of Lirnasto. Wants the edge kept and the Keeper well informed.**

Nantorutta Hummarinto is a lord of the [[affiliation-nclvlrnst|Enclave of Lirnasto]], raised by every voice of the hearths for one dry season: when the paths of the old jungle began to fail, she walked every one of them and sang each whole again, so that no Senalo of the conclave and no trespasser was lost in them. Her standing is respect and holds no land, hearth or office. Her hearth is Hummarinto, the hearth of the heavy vine.

She keeps the edge of the conclave's country. She is the one the Keeper sends to the line where the old trees begin, and she brings back what the line says.

## Psyche

### Personality

Nantorutta is dry, watchful and very still. She speaks seldom and is not often heard to laugh, though she is warm to the hearth's children and to the old.

### Motivation

She wants the edge kept and the Keeper told truly what lies beyond it, and she wants the hearths never to have to be told that the line has failed.

### Strengths

She knows the edge better than anyone, and she is never seen when she does not wish to be.

### Weaknesses

She keeps what she knows to herself, and she judges the hearths' temper before she judges their cause.

## Social

### Companions

The Keeper, [[being-hellurivotlknt|Hellurivo Tolkunto]], and the warden [[being-kelsipavokssnt|Kelsipavo Kossanto]], with whom she shares the edge.

### Patrons

None. The hearths raised her.

### Enemies

None she would name.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvlrnst|Enclave of Lirnasto]]**: Nantorutta is a Lord.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A tapper turned back.** A rubber-tapper who went in past the old trees and was turned back by the jungle's own paths insists that a woman in green stood at the clearing's edge and watched him go. His Ha'nalmah asks a party to learn whether the woman is real.
2. **What the line says.** A priest of the Star House at an edge town has begun to keep a daily record of what the old trees do at dawn and wants an observer who can sit beside the forest from dusk until the birds stop.

:::secret {title="Beyond the line"}
Nantorutta goes beyond the line. She walks at night through the fields and sheds of the edge towns, unseen, and counts the stands the Kulmah's foresters have marked for burning. The Keeper's knowledge of them comes from her, and she has never been seen. She has begun to mark, in her own count, which Kulmah's household sleeps lightly and which does not.
