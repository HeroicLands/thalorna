# Appearance {#appearance}

Ts'ultel Ishkul K'ushi is a 63-year-old man who stands 5'3" tall with a light build. He has tawny skin, smooth complexion, gray hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ts'ultel Ishkul K'ushi is the Ts'enmah of [[place-kankul2|K'ankul]], the seat of the realm of [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]]. He was a court reckoner until his brother died and the city passed to him, and he still checks his own Ts'obanal's figures. He feeds the reckoners of [[affiliation-ishilanshulum|The Star House]], guards their tower and takes the credit for their tables in every court he visits, and he has no say in what the tables contain.

He is a thin, nearsighted man. The court of [[place-ixilankul2|Ish'ilankul]] calls K'ankul's partnership in the faith a courtesy, and an envoy of its Q'emulmah is on the road to settle what the realm sends in the new round. Ts'ultel Ishkul wants to know what the envoy means to ask before he arrives. He trusts the fast net of [[affiliation-chumishlan|Ch'um Ish'lan]] no further than its dispatch at [[place-kultaqan|Kul'taq'an]], which he believes reads more than it carries, and his letters to [[being-ishkulnktsl|Ishkul Ne'ok Ts'ultel]] of [[place-neokbaate2|Ne'ok Ba'ate]] go by other roads.

**Motivation:** to keep K'ankul a partner in the faith and never a tributary.
