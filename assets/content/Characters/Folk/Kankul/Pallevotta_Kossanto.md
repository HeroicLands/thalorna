# Appearance {#appearance}

Pallevotta Kossanto is a woman of 1,300 years who stands 5' 10" tall and is of light build. Her silver hair falls loose past her knees, her eyes are a pale gray that seem to rest on something just beyond the speaker, and her skin is white as the limestone she kneels on. A thin mark crosses her throat. She wears a plain gown and kneels at the pools barefoot in the wet.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 1,300. Seer of the Pools. Wants to read what the water gives.**

Pallevotta Kossanto is the seer of the [[affiliation-nclvlrnst|Enclave of Lirnasto]]. She reads the sacred pools that open in the limestone of the old jungle, the pools in which no priest of the realm has ever poured an offering. Her hearth is Kossanto, the hearth of the sunken pool, and she is the elder kin of the warden [[being-kelsipavokssnt|Kelsipavo Kossanto]].

The priests of the Star House know the pools from their edges and have never been invited to a closer look.

## Background

Pallevotta came to the pools as a child, sent to carry water for the seer before her, and stayed. She took up the reading when the old seer fell silent, and she has read the pools for more than a thousand years.

:::secret {title="The conclave behind the trees"}
Pallevotta gives what she reads to the Keeper, [[being-hellurivotlknt|Hellurivo Tolkunto]], before anyone else.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Pallevotta is deep, mild and slow to answer, and she speaks to a pool as another would speak to an old friend. She is not afraid of anything she has seen in the water.

### Motivation

She wants to read what the water gives and to give it on, faithfully.

### Strengths

She has never given a false reading.

### Weaknesses

She reads only what is shown, and when the water shows nothing, she cannot give the certainty that is wanted of her.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A priest at a pool.** A priest of the Star House, caught reading a pool at the old jungle's edge by dawn, has been told by his own temple that he has seen nothing. He asks a party to learn what the water showed him, since the temple will not.
2. **Water that went still.** A cenote on the realm's side of the line has gone still and clear in a single night, and the Ha'nalmah's people refuse to draw from it. A party is asked who in the old jungle has done it and whether it can be undone.

:::secret {title="The pool that showed nothing"}
The Keeper asked her what fire would do in the old trees, and she read the pool for it three times. It showed her neither smoke nor clear water. She holds that the pool is waiting for the conclave to decide, and she has said nothing about it to the Keeper, who thinks the answer has simply not been given.
:::
