# Dossier {#dossier}

Ne' Ish Q'emul is the court reckoner of [[place-nechakalkul|Ne' Ch'akalkul]], a city-state of [[affiliation-kankul|K'ankul]], and a scribe of its royal house. For thirty years she has taken the dawn sight of the bright star K'inish'ilan from the crown of [[place-smoothstep|The Smooth Step]] on every morning of its season, and she walks with a stoop from it. The city's count of the star's cycle is the most exact on the continent. Her tables are carved on a row of stone panels in the gallery at the pyramid's foot, and astronomers come from the other cities of K'ankul and from [[place-netsumul|Ne' Ts'umul]] to copy them, for a fee and a gift.

In K'ankul a city's new calculation is announced as its Ts'enmah's own, and the stones record it as his. This season the court reckoners of Ne' Ch'akalkul are dining well and saying nothing, and the Keeper of Errors of [[affiliation-ishilanshulum|The Star House]] wants to know what she has found.

**Motivation:** to see her count proved right before every city's envoys.
