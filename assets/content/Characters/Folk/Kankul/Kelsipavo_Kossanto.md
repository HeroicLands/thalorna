# Appearance {#appearance}

Kelsipavo Kossanto is a man of 560 years who stands 6' 3" tall and is of medium build. His short brown hair is bound with a strip of green bark, his eyes are hazel, and his tanned skin is tattooed on the shoulders with the paths of the old jungle in a fine blue line. A small white scar lies at the base of his throat, above a hollow reed on a cord. He wears a sleeveless jerkin of tanned hide and carries a throwing knife and a long bow.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 560. Warden of the Paths. Wants no stranger hurt for walking a path.**

Kelsipavo Kossanto is a warden of the [[affiliation-nclvlrnst|Enclave of Lirnasto]], a hunter who walks the paths of the old jungle. His hearth is Kossanto, the hearth of the sunken pool, and the seer [[being-pallevottakssn|Pallevotta Kossanto]] is his elder kin.

## Background

Kelsipavo is young by the conclave's reckoning. He learned the paths from the lord [[being-nantoruttahmmr|Nantorutta Hummarinto]] and had each one tattooed on his shoulders as he learned it, and he has kept them beside her for two centuries.

:::secret {title="The paths at the forest's edge"}
A path into the old jungle leads out by the way it went in, and the warden keeps it so: Kelsipavo walks each path in turn, mends what a season has broken and hums a path whole where the land has begun to wander. He turns back a hunter, a tapper or a gatherer at the line and sees him on his way without speaking to him, and most never know he was there.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Kelsipavo is easy and quick, a young warden by the conclave's reckoning, and he is always humming the line he is mending.

### Motivation

He wants every path of the old jungle kept, and no stranger hurt for walking one.

### Strengths

He is the fastest in the conclave and the quietest, and he can follow a trespasser through dense growth without disturbing a leaf.

### Weaknesses

He is young for a warden and thinks the jungle can always be mended, and so far he has not been wrong.

## Social

### Companions

Nantorutta Hummarinto, with whom he walks the edge, and the weaver [[being-ruvalittanrlln|Ruvalitta Nurellanto]], who ties his reed bells.

### Patrons

None.

### Enemies

Fire, though he has never seen it in the old trees.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvlrnst|Enclave of Lirnasto]]**: Kelsipavo is a Member of the Conclave.

## Plot Hooks

1. **Turned at the line.** A party that crosses the line of old trees on a dare is turned back by paths that bend the way they came, and one of them glimpses a tall figure among the trees behind them the whole way out.
2. **The wandering path.** A path at the edge of the jungle has taken to wandering, and the town's Ha'nalmah has lost two of his hunters in it. He asks a party to find whoever keeps it and to ask that it be mended.

:::secret {title="The blaze inside the line"}
Kelsipavo has found a forester's blaze cut into a trunk deep inside the old jungle, well beyond the line. It is the first time a path has not turned a man back. He has told the Keeper and told no one else, and he walks the path every night now to learn how the forester came in and how he went out unturned.
:::
