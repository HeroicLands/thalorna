# Dossier {#dossier}

Khelarven trains animals out of [[place-garanlaghet|Gar-Anlaghet]], by domination and fear rather than by patience. He has sabotaged commissions and poisoned beasts in his own care when it suited him, and has lately found patrons darker than his old ones. Hiring him means accepting that the animal's training, and perhaps the client's business, may cost more than the fee.
