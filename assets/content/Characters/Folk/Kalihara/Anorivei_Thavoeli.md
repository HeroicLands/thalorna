# Appearance {#appearance}

Anorivei Thavoeli is a 54-year-old man who stands 5'8" tall with a light build. He has brown skin, clear complexion, black hair, and amber eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Anorivei Thavoeli is the merchant-prince of [House Thavoeli]{.name data-type="affiliation/venture"}, a merchant house of [[place-ilovanu|Ilovanu]] in rare spice and cut stones. His house has hired [[being-tvnrthr|Tavonei Reithuri]] for its three richest trains each year for over a decade, and he asks for Tavonei by name for any cargo bound to a foreign hull, trusting no one else with goods worth a fortune.

## Psyche

Anorivei values a cargo’s safe arrival above a cheap escort. His trust in Tavonei rests on more than a decade of guarded trains, and he asks for him by name when a shipment must reach a foreign hull.

## Social

House Thavoeli keeps a factor in [[place-qudumel2|Kudumel]] to arrange its guarded cargoes. Tavonei is its preferred guard for the richest trains.

## Plot Hooks

The guarded shipment
: House Thavoeli hires Tavonei to escort a train from Kudumel’s Caravan Gate to a ship at the quay. The bales contain the regalia of a deposed royal line, and armed pursuers follow them. Tavonei needs to learn what his employers have committed him to carrying before the train reaches the water.
