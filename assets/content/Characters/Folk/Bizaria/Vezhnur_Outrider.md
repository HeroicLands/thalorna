# Appearance {#appearance}

A Vezhnur Outrider is a lean hill-rider in a weather-stained coat, with a short bow slung where he can reach it and a horse that is nearly as quiet as he is.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Rider of the [Vezhnur holds]{.name data-type="place/region"}. Scouts ahead of clan parties and hired caravans on the pass road above [[place-vezhrelion|Vezhrelion]].**

A Vezhnur Outrider is a young warrior of the clan-country in the high passes above [[place-orashion2|Órashion]], where no city's charter runs and the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]] tolerates the clans as its eastern shield. The clans descend from a cohort settled in the passes ([[lore-heladvezhnur|The Vezhnur Settlement]]). He rides a day ahead of a clan party or a caravan hired at [[place-vezhrelion|Vezhrelion]], reads the road, the springs and the other riders on it, and comes back with the news. The holds fight the desert raiders in country the garrison cannot hold, and the outriders are the first of them to see a raiding band.

## Background

**The holds.** He grew up in a hold of the high passes, learned the hills before the letters, and rode his first scout at fourteen.

**The road.** He is hired at Vezhrelion's guest hall by caravan masters and by clan parties, and he is paid by the day.

**Now.** Mountain bands that owe nothing to any hold raid the lower road, and each side says the other should deal with them. An outrider is paid to see the bands first.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is quiet, watchful and proud of his hold. He answers a question with the shortest true thing he knows.

### Motivation

He wants a name among the riders of his hold and his pay in iron and cloth.

### Strengths

He tracks, rides and shoots well, finds water in dry hills and knows every turn of the road above the town.

### Weaknesses

He keeps his hold's business to himself and holds an outsider's word cheap. He is a poor hand in a city.

## Social

### Others

- [[affiliation-orashion|Órashion]]: The city-state that holds Vezhrelion, where he is hired and where no clansman goes armed through the market street.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The scout's price.** A caravan master cannot pay the outrider he needs and offers a party his goods on credit for escorting him, and the outrider asks first who else rides with the caravan.
2. **The empty road.** An outrider returns to Vezhrelion with nothing to report on a road that is never empty, and a party that rides it finds the reason.
3. **The band he did not see.** A caravan loses wagons on a stretch of road its outrider cleared, and the caravan's owner wants the outrider found.
4. **The guest hall.** A clan party hires a party to ride with its outrider as witnesses, and it does not say what the outrider is to look for.

:::secret {title="Whom he tells"}
The outrider tells someone besides the party that hires him what the caravan carries, what road it takes and when. Whom he tells (his hold's warlord, the chapter of [[affiliation-blackflame|the Black Flame]] that gathers at the clan guest hall, a merchant house's factor or someone else) is the referee's choice, and so is what the listener does with it.

He does not think of it as betrayal. He thinks of it as what a rider owes the hold, and a party that asks him straight hears a flat denial.

**Hook: the second rider.** A rider who is not the outrider is seen at a spring a day behind a caravan, and he flees when called.

**Hook: the price of the road.** The outrider offers a party a safe stretch of road for a share of what the caravan carries.
:::
