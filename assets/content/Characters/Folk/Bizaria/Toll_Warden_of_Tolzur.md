# Appearance {#appearance}

A Toll Warden of Tolzur is a Bizarian officer in the town's color, with the toll-keepers' scales behind him and the gate watch within call.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Warden of the gate of Tolzur. Takes the town's toll for Argirteikhos.**

[[place-tolzur|Tolzur]] stands where the pass road turns toward [[place-bittersea|the Bitter Sea]] and the [[affiliation-kngdmmrdd|Kingdom of Amradad]], and every caravan bound for Amradad passes its gate. The Toll Warden of Tolzur sits in the gatehouse and answers to the military governor of [[affiliation-argyrteikhos|Argirteikhos]]. His toll-keepers weigh, count and mark each load, and the town's gate watch takes its orders from him. Outside the wall the League's post, staffed under an officer of the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]], takes the League's toll and the imperial tariff, so a caravan pays twice and argues about both.

## Background

**The gate.** The town lives on its tolls, storage, fodder and water, and the gate toll is his to take.

**The guest room.** He keeps a guest room for officials, and what an official sees of the town he sees first from the warden's table.

**Now.** The warden and the March officer quarrel over which of them may seize a load that has dodged a toll. Merchants use the quarrel, and the smugglers of the mule-tracks have grown bolder.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is punctilious, proud of his office and quick to take offense at the March officer.

### Motivation

He wants every load tolled at his gate and the right to seize whatever dodges it.

### Strengths

He knows the caravans, their marks and the tricks of a clerk who wants a load to weigh less.

### Weaknesses

His quarrel with the March officer is worth more to the smugglers than any bribe.

## Social

### Others

- [[affiliation-argyrteikhos|Argirteikhos]]: the city-state whose military governor he answers to.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The dodged toll.** A string of mules has slipped past the gate by the mule-tracks, and the warden wants the load found and brought to his gate before the March officer seizes it.
2. **The back room.** Muleteers and clerks gather in a back room after the gates close, and the warden wants to know whether any of his own gate watch go with them.
3. **The guest.** An official lodging in the warden's guest room asks to see the toll ledgers, and the warden wants to know who sent him.
