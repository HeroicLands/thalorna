# Appearance {#appearance}

Kofios Bolalidek is a 46-year-old man who stands 6'3" tall with a medium build. He has tanned skin, ruddy complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kofios Bolalidek is a swordmaster called the Undefeated. He serves the [[affiliation-vylarinmpr|Empire]]'s rival power and harbors intense envy of the reputation of [[being-krmdnsbrkrtys|Koremdanis Barkratis]]. The two have not yet formally dueled, though both know it is inevitable.

## Psyche

The title “Undefeated” sits uneasily beside Koremdanis’s greater reputation. Kofios wants a contest that settles which name deserves to stand foremost.

## Social

His service to a rival of the Empire makes a duel with Koremdanis politically charged. Their rivalry is personal even when their patrons can profit from its outcome.
