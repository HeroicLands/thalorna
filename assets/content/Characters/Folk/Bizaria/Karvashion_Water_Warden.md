# Appearance {#appearance}

A Karvashion Water-Warden is a Bizarian official with a measuring jar at his belt and a ledger of fees, who stands beside the water as a treasurer stands beside a strongbox.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Keeper of a caravan town's water. Sets the fee.**

A Karvashion Water-Warden is sent by the caravansary masters of [[affiliation-karvashion|Karvashion]] to keep the water of one of its caravan towns. At [[place-halzikos|Halzikos]], on the shore of [[place-bittersea|the Bitter Sea]], he is the cistern-warden: he keeps the sweet water, sets the price of a skin and decides who camps where. At [[place-krenel|Krenel]], on the road to [[place-dunharargn|Dunhara]], he is the spring-warden: he sets the water fee and settles disputes over the troughs.

## Background

**The water.** Both towns live by the caravans, and at Krenel the water fee is most of what the town earns.

**The masters.** He answers to the caravansary masters of Karvashion.

**Now.** At Halzikos the salt-masters want a wall around their pans, and the warden says that the cistern is the one thing he must guard first. At Krenel the Dunhari caravan masters complain that the fee has risen, and the warden answers that the spring is the town's only income.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is dry, exact and unmoved by complaint.

### Motivation

He wants the water clean, the fee paid and the caravans kept coming.

### Strengths

He knows every caravan master on his road and how much a string of animals drinks.

### Weaknesses

The fee is his answer to everything, and a season of poor winter rain would test it.

## Social

### Others

- [[affiliation-karvashion|Karvashion]]: the city-state whose caravansary masters appoint him.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The fouled cistern.** Someone fouls the sweet water at Halzikos the night before a large caravan arrives, and the warden needs to know who before he names a price.
2. **The fee.** A Dunhari caravan master refuses the new fee at Krenel and camps his train outside the wall, and the warden wants him persuaded rather than driven off.
3. **The inspectors.** Amradi customs officers from [[place-dorvakhend|Dorvakhend]] cross the border to inspect loads at Halzikos, his complaints to Karvashion have gone unanswered, and he wants witnesses to what the officers do.
