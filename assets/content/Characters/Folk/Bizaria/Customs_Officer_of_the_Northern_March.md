# Appearance {#appearance}

A Customs Officer of the Northern March is a plain-spoken Bizarian hill-farmer in the League's coat, standing at the post beyond the crest.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Officer at the March's customs post. Taxes what the riders leave.**

A Customs Officer of the [[place-northrnmrch|Northern March]] keeps the post beyond the crest of the pass, where the imperial tariff and the League's toll fall on every wagon that crosses the frontier. The officers wait to see what the Sevret riders leave on the wagons.

## Background

**Formation.** He is a Bizarian uplander, plain-spoken and clannish, who has intermarried with Velanthian drovers along the frontier.

**The post.** He weighs and tolls each wagon at the crest.

**Now.** He waits on the far slope for the last trains and for the figure the riders will name.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is stubborn and honest in roughly equal measure.

### Motivation

He wants the tariff and the toll collected on every wagon that crosses.

### Strengths

He knows the grain road and its wagons.

### Weaknesses

The riders' toll shrinks what is left to tax, and he must wait for them to name a figure.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]]**: an officer of the frontier post, collecting the League's toll.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The figure.** The officers wait for the riders to name what they take before they name their own.
2. **The two tolls.** A wagon master needs a go-between to settle what the officers and the riders each take.
3. **What is left to tax.** The officers wait on the far slope to see what the last trains carry.
