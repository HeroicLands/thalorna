# Appearance {#appearance}

A March Garrison Soldier is a Bizarian conscript in the garrison's armor, with a sword at his hip and the mud of a patrol on his boots.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Conscript of the [[place-eastrnmrch|Eastern March]] garrison. Serves under a commissioned captain.**

A March Garrison Soldier is a conscript of the joint army the five city-states of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]] raise, posted to the Eastern March under a commissioned captain. He garrisons a way-fort, a spring-fort or an outer caravanserai, rides the patrols of the foothills and fills his jar at the spring-fort's trough.

## Background

**The levy.** He is a farm boy of the plateau or the coast, taken by the levy for a term.

**The March.** He serves at the posts of the March and rides its patrols as far as a horse can go and return in a day. A term in the March turns into a career when the aptitude is there.

**Now.** The Lord Commander's officers are counting the steppe camps in the foothills, and the patrols ride with them.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is tired, wary of the steppe camps and loyal to his captain.

### Motivation

He wants his term served and his pay on time.

### Strengths

He holds a wall and a line, and he knows the posts and springs of his stretch of the March.

### Weaknesses

He is no match for a steppe rider on open grass.

## Social

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]]**: a conscript of the League's joint army in the March.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The patrol.** A patrol rides into a winter camp to take back branded horses, outnumbered and angry.
2. **The night at the gate.** Soldiers at a spring-fort's gate watch a camp's riders sit their horses outside it all night.
3. **The order.** Conscripts from the forts are sent to drive a camp out into the sleet, and some of them do not want to go.
