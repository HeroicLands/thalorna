# Appearance {#appearance}

A Bailiff of Siderash is a Bizarian clerk of the capital in a good coat gone rust-colored at the hem, with the iron tallies of three houses in one ledger.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Bailiff of the iron houses at Siderash. Sits in the gatehouse.**

The Merchant Hall of [[affiliation-khryshteikhos|Krusteikhos]] farms the iron of [[place-siderash|Siderash]] to three houses that buy it at a fixed price each season. The Bailiff of Siderash is the man those houses choose to live in the town and sit in its gatehouse. He counts the bar iron, ingots and rough forgings that go down to [[place-khryshteikhos2|Krusteikhos]] in the empty grain wagons, and a squad of the city's guards keeps the road.

## Background

**The farm.** He speaks for the three houses in the town, and the smelters and the bloomeries deal with the houses through him.

**The March.** The Lord of the [[place-northrnmrch|Northern March]], who is by custom a Krustéikhian, has no authority inside the walls, and the bailiff says so politely and often.

**Now.** The fixed price has not moved while the cost of grain has. The smelters grumble that the houses profit from bread and from iron both, and the bailiff has asked the Merchant Hall for a better price.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is polite, firm and tired.

### Motivation

He wants a better price from the Hall before the smelters' grumbling turns into something worse.

### Strengths

He knows the weights, the wagons and the charcoal burners whose bad winter is the town's bad year.

### Weaknesses

He serves three houses at once, and every season one of them suspects him of favoring another.

## Social

### Others

- [[affiliation-khryshteikhos|Krusteikhos]]: the city-state that holds the town and farms its iron.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The watched wagons.** Bands from the mountain country watch the ore wagons on the side road, and the bailiff wants guards for a heavy load that the city's squad cannot spare.
2. **The letter.** The bailiff needs his letter to the Merchant Hall carried to Krusteikhos and answered before the autumn grain is bought.
3. **The March's claim.** A rider of the Northern March comes to the gate to seize a load, and the bailiff wants him refused without offense.
