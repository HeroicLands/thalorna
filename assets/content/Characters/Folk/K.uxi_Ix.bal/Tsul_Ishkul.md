# Appearance {#appearance}

Tsul Ishkul is a 54-year-old man who stands 5'4" tall with a heavy build. He has brown skin, weathered complexion, greying black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tsul Ishkul is the Sh'ukelmah of [[place-qptlulneok|Q'aptel'ul Ne'ok]], the garrison lord who holds the town for the Ts'enmah of [[affiliation-kuxiixbal|K'ushi Ish'bal]]. He is one of the Sun's kin, as every Sh'ukelmah is. He keeps the walls, calls the levy and answers for the stores that outfit every expedition leaving for the south, and he takes a share of every sale the town's outfitters make, for the storehouses.

He is a heavy, unhurried man who has walked every storehouse at least once a month since he was named, and he can say from memory how many cloaks are in each. He counts the brothers of [[affiliation-wallsworn|The Wall-Sworn]] the same way, and knows how many of them have crossed the flats. This season more expeditions are coming up the road than his storehouses can outfit, because the temples took their share of the plateau's herds first, and he means to ask the lake temples of [[place-ixkulchalix|Ishkul Ch'alish]] for a herd, with his count of cloaks to show that he asks for no more than he needs.

**Motivation:** that no expedition goes south from his town short of what it needs.
