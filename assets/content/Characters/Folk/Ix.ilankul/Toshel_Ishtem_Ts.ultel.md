# Dossier {#dossier}

Toshel Ishtem Ts'ultel is the Ts'enmah of [[place-kultaqan|Kul'taq'an]], a city-state of [[affiliation-ixilankul|Ish'ilankul]], chosen from its royal line by the four lords of its quarters and confirmed by its Presiding Ch'alish Tq'an. He holds the quarters, their wards and levies, and the fees of the great plaza. The precinct of [[place-tzomalchalix|The Sacred Foundation]], the dispatch and the pilgrims' hostels are not his, and he takes no tribute from their estates, so he rules the holiest city on the continent as one Sun Lord among the others.

He is a lean man who has led the city's captive-hunts against [[place-qokulqat|Q'o Kul'qat]] in nine war months and taken eleven captives himself, and he wears the white cloak of the Patterned Cloaks of [[affiliation-takingsocs|The Taking Societies]] to his court. The round turns at the Living Flame of 722. He needs new feather regalia for his warriors before it does, and the price of plumes from [[place-xelem2|Shelem]] is already climbing.

**Motivation:** to stand his warriors before the new fire dressed as no other city's are.
