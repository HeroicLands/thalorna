# Dossier {#dossier}

Ish'mel Ts'uma Ch'upul, Ish'mel to her household, is a Warden of the Sealed Chambers among the serpent-keepers of the [[affiliation-puqilchaqun|Faith of P'uqil Ch'akun]] at [[place-netsumul|Ne' Ts'umul]]. The Wardens keep the serpent-skin calculations of the age's ending in the binding temples, and open them before witnesses of two other branches at each inspection of the seals. She is proud and quick, and she knows the machinery of the binding temples from her branch's copy of the plans.

Her branch sent her to [[place-kulqattzumul|The Red Steps]] to bring back the temple's seal-stone, the [[miscgear-stormseye|Storm's Eye]], and its codices before the seals are next inspected. She trusted the register of the Warden of the Ruins, which records the temple's guardian as failed, and she went with a boy attendant, two guards of the household and porters with an empty chest.

**Motivation:** to present the Storm's Eye at the next inspection, for her branch's standing.
