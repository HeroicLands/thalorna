# Appearance {#appearance}

Ishkul Ne'ok Ts'ultel is a 45-year-old man who stands 5'10" tall with a medium build. He has golden skin, smooth complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ishkul Ne'ok Ts'ultel is the Ts'enmah of [[place-neokbaate2|Ne'ok Ba'ate]], the city that holds the land road across the isthmus, and the seat of the realm of [[affiliation-neokbaate|Ne'ok Ba'ate]]. He is a tall, quick-tempered man with a merchant's head for numbers, and his weighing houses on the great plaza take the city's share of every load that crosses it. His sister is married to the Ts'enmah of [[place-qokulqat|Q'o Kul'qat]], and his eldest daughter is promised to a son of the royal house of [[place-netsumul|Ne' Ts'umul]].

He keeps the forms of the [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itsani Pantheon]] with a local flavor, and he resents the demands of tributary status. His envoy at the court of [[place-ixilankul2|Ish'ilankul]] speaks for him there and is answered as if he were the Ts'enmah himself. Every cord the isthmus sends north passes through the dispatch at [[place-kultaqan|Kul'taq'an]], and Ishkul Ne'ok trusts a sealed gourd carried by his own household, by road and by sea to [[place-tzililnetzik|The Island of Swallows]], more than any runner.

**Motivation:** that the isthmus, and not the north, sets what the isthmus sends.
