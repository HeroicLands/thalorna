# Dossier {#dossier}

Ish'bal Ne'ok K'ushi is the Ish'kanmah of [[place-tzililnetzik|The Island of Swallows]], the island port off the eastern coast of [[affiliation-neokbaate|Ne'ok Ba'ate]]. She holds it in her own right, because the island's royal line passes through its daughters. Kulmah hold the lesser islands of the archipelago for her, the heads of the merchant families sit at her court, and she keeps an envoy at the court of the realm's Ts'enmah, [[being-ishkulnktsl|Ishkul Ne'ok Ts'ultel]], to whom she sends tribute in shell, salt and the services of her canoes.

She is a square, weathered woman who paddled in the war canoes as a girl and still goes out with them to inspect the archipelago. They are the only war fleet in the realm, and they answer to her and no one else. The island competes with [[place-tzokilkul|The Walled City]] for every cargo of K'ankul, and the priestesses of the island's spring have noticed that her canoes go out on nights when there is nothing to escort.

**Motivation:** that her canoes go where she sends them and answer to no one else.
