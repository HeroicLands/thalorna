# Dossier {#dossier}

Moshentiyu, Heart of the Lodge, is a lodge owner (_moshenka_) of [[place-tansheyawa|Bison Ford]], the largest of the river villages of the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]], and the villages name her when they say what a woman should be. She raised her own earth lodge, cutting and setting its four great posts with her sisters, and she owns it, its fields and its cache pits, and sits in the village's council. She has quilled more robes than any woman of the village.

Four winters ago, when the herds went west and three bands came to Tanshe Yawa starving in the snow, she opened her cache pits to them in midwinter, long after the trading, when the [[miscgear-cornpipe|Corn Pipe]] bound nobody, and fed a hundred people through the worst of the winter. Her lodge planted that spring from borrowed seed. The bands have not forgotten it, and some of them have not repaid it.

She is forty-five, heavy-handed and warm, with a laugh that carries across the village. A party that comes to Bison Ford hungry is fed at her lodge before anyone asks what it wants. A party that wants robes quilled for a gift that must impress a chief brings them to her, and waits a winter.

What she wants is a winter in which no band comes to Bison Ford starving.
