# Appearance {#appearance}

Toshewiwan is a 45-year-old man who stands 5'5" tall with a medium build. He has brown skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Toshewiwan, Cliff Stands, of the Cliff band, is this autumn's hunt chief (_yuchanka_) at [[place-kuntontoshe|Thunder Cliff]], where the bands of the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]] drive the herd along the cairn lanes and over the edge. Thunder Cliff belongs to no band; the bands that want to drive there agree at the summer gathering and name one hunt chief among them, and while a herd is being steered his word is law. He is a heavy, slow-spoken man in his forties who has led the drive here twice before.

Last autumn the drive went too well: well over a thousand animals went over the cliff in one run, and half of them rotted before the bands could butcher them. The herds have been thin in the west since. The Cairn band's bundle keeper, [[being-mahintayu|Mahintayu]], says the herd went back under the earth because it was wasted, and will not open the bundle on the cliff until the bands that drove there give away a robe for every animal that rotted. Toshewiwan says that the herds are thin across the whole west, and that the bands should not be made to pay for a dry summer. Neither will drive against the other, and the bands are already gathering in the valley below the cliff.

He is honest, stubborn and not cruel, and he has the warrior societies behind him, whose members police the drive. A party in the valley below Thunder Cliff this autumn is asked which of the two it believes, and a party that can find where the herd has gone has found what both of them need.

What he wants is the drive at Thunder Cliff run this autumn, with the bundle opened or without it.
