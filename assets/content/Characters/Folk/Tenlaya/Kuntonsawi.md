# Dossier {#dossier}

Kuntonsawi, Thunder Runs, is a contrary (_kewitaka_) of the Cairn band of the [[place-wehioshtergn|Cairn Plain]], a young man of the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]] who dreamed of the Thunders in the spring. He says yes for no and no for yes, washes in dust and dries himself in the river, walks backward into the camp and shivers in the sun, and he will do so until a spring storm frees him. A contrary holds no office in council and is the first into any fight, and everyone watches him, because he is one of the Thunders' people.

He is among the Cairn band's lodges in the valley below [[place-kuntontoshe|Thunder Cliff]] this autumn, where the bundle keeper, [[being-mahintayu|Mahintayu]], and the hunt chief, [[being-toshewiwan|Toshewiwan]], will not drive against each other. On the first day in the valley he said loudly that the herd would certainly come and the drive would be a great success. Nobody knew how to take it.

He is twenty, wiry and very serious, and he finds being a contrary harder than any fight. A party that meets him is asked the opposite of what he means, and a party that answers him the right way round is the first company in months that has not watched him for omens.

What he wants is to learn what the Thunders dreamed him for, and to be freed once he has done it.
