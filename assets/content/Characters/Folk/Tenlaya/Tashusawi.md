Tashusawi is a 72-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall with a heavy build. He has tanned skin, leathery complexion, black hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tashusawi, Wind Runs, is the head of the western bands at [[place-hiyespito|Falling Water]], the salmon falls of the [[place-potontayuntorgn|Root Fields]] where the bands of the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]] take fish from platforms over the water. He is the oldest man of the falls band and owns the platform where the first fish is taken. He settles quarrels over platforms and keeps the peace of the trading, and his word holds because his band owns the best rocks on the river.

The falls are the great market of the west, and every summer the [[affiliation-kelwanuk|Cedar People]]'s traders come over the coast range with shell, dried sea fish, horn spoons and cedar boxes. This summer they brought a captive to sell: a girl of about twelve from the [[affiliation-shuqash|Shell-Bead People]] of the southern coast, kept as a slave in a Cedar house, with a price of twenty robes on her. The Grass People take captives in war and adopt them as kin, but they do not buy and sell people. The camp is divided. Some families want to buy her and adopt her, because a child bought out of a Cedar house is a child saved. Others say that a band that pays for a person teaches the Cedar People that the plains will pay. The carriers' leader, [[being-melumniksala|Melumniksala]], has said she will not take the girl back over the pass.

Tashusawi is seventy-two, white-haired and slow, and he has not decided. The traders are packing to go back before the snow. A party at the falls is asked what its own people do with a bought child, and its answer is weighed.

What he wants is an answer about the girl that the whole camp at the falls can live with after the traders have gone.
