# Dossier {#dossier}

Loshiwinte, Gives Corn, is a lodge owner (_moshenka_) of [[place-kopenmokin|Flint Hill]], the northernmost village of the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]], under the bluffs whose flint is the best on the plains. The pits belong to the lodge owners, each lodge to its own stretch of the bluff, passed from mother to daughter with the lodge, and Loshiwinte's stretch is the largest. A band that wants to dig asks the lodge owner whose ground it is, pays her in meat or robes, and digs. Nobody refuses a band, and nobody digs without asking. She speaks for the pits at the autumn trading.

She is a broad, cheerful woman of fifty-five who has quilled eagle feathers into the hair of three husbands and outlived two of them, and she is said to know the worth of a flint blank by its sound when she drops it. A band that digs on her ground unasked has its blanks taken off it at the foot of the bluff, and she enjoys telling the story afterward, because it is always a young war leader in a hurry.

Flint Hill is also the last village up the river, and when the northern bands come back in autumn from trading with the [[lore-kawiskakclt|Spruce People]] beyond the aspen country, Flint Hill trades for their furs, birch-bark canoes and northern copper first, and she bargains first among the lodge owners. A party that wants flint, a guide north, or a birch-bark canoe deals with her, and she gives good weight to anyone who asks first.

What she wants is the bluff kept with the lodges that own it, and every band asking before it digs.
