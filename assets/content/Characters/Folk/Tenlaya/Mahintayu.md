# Appearance {#appearance}

Mahintayu is a 54-year-old woman who stands 5'4" tall with a light build. She has tanned skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Mahintayu, Sky Dream, is the bundle keeper (_chekanka_) of the Cairn band of the [[place-wehioshtergn|Cairn Plain]]. The Cairn band keeps the drive lanes at [[place-kuntontoshe|Thunder Cliff]], restacks their fallen cairns each autumn and leads the singing over the bundle before a drive, and on the morning of the drive Mahintayu opens the bundle at the top of the cliff. The band's bison stone is kept in her bundle, and it has not sung for three summers.

Last autumn the drive at Thunder Cliff went too well: well over a thousand animals went over the cliff in one run, and half of them rotted before the bands could butcher them. The herds have been thin in the west since. Mahintayu says, as the bundle keepers of the west say in [[lore-nipanheshehin|Earth's Breath]], that the herd went back under the earth because it was wasted, and that it will not come to Thunder Cliff this autumn unless the bands that drove there give away a robe for every animal that rotted. She will not open the bundle on the cliff until they do. This autumn's hunt chief, [[being-toshewiwan|Toshewiwan]] of the Cliff band, says that the herds are thin across the whole west, and that the bands should not be made to pay for a dry summer. Neither will drive against the other, and the bands are already gathering in the valley below the cliff.

What she wants is the waste answered with a robe for every animal that rotted, so that the herds come back.
