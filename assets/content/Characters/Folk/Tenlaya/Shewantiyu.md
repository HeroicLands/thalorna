# Appearance {#appearance}

Shewantiyu is a 55-year-old woman who stands 5'3" tall with a medium build. She has tanned skin, leathery complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Shewantiyu, River Heart, is the eldest lodge owner (_moshenka_) of [[place-henushewan|The Forks]], the river village of the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]] that a war party of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]] burned last year. Eighteen lodges burned, hers among them, and thirty-one people were carried away. Her youngest daughter is among the thirty-one.

The war party came because the Coyote band of the [[place-watotenlargn|Long Grass]], under its war leader [[being-kanwewiwe|Kanwewiwe]], had raided two One Fire towns the autumn before. At the end of the winter Shewantiyu called the lodge owners together in the bundle lodge, and they agreed that when the bands come in this autumn the Forks will open its cache pits to every band that comes, as the [[miscgear-cornpipe|Corn Pipe]] binds it to, except the Coyote band. They have sent word that if the Coyote band wants corn from the Forks, it can bring back the thirty-one first.

She is a thin, hard woman of fifty-five, living in a lodge her sisters' husbands are still roofing. The village's speaker, [[being-hetanwinte|Hetanwinte]], says the Forks is doing what the [Burned Village]{.name data-type="place/site"} did; she says the Burned Village shut its pits against the hungry, and the Coyote band is not hungry. A party willing to go east into the One Fire's country after the captives has her blessing, her best robes, and the whole village's.

What she wants is her youngest daughter brought home from the One Fire, by the band whose raid let her be taken.
