# Dossier {#dossier}

Kanwewiwe, Coyote Fights, is the war leader (_yashenka_) of the Coyote band (Kanweya) of the [[place-watotenlargn|Long Grass]], the greatest raiding band of the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]], whose warriors paint coyotes on their shields. He has counted more coups on the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]] than any man alive. He is the sister's son of [Kanwesawi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, Coyote Runs, the war leader killed at the [Red Quarry]{.name data-type="place/site"} by a One Fire noble, and his band still keeps the robe Kanwesawi wore, with the hole in it.

The autumn before last he raided two of the One Fire's western towns and brought home robes, copper and a dozen captives. The One Fire's war party struck back at what does not move. It burned eighteen lodges of the river village of [[place-henushewan|The Forks]] and carried off thirty-one people, while the Coyote band was a hundred miles away on the winter grass. The lodge owners of the Forks have said they will shut their cache pits against his band this autumn unless it brings back the thirty-one first.

He is thirty-eight, tall, scarred, generous and proud, and he laughs at the Forks' demand in public and has not laughed at it in private. A party that wants to ride east to the One Fire's towns, or wants the Coyote band's help in a fight, finds him delighted. A party that asks him to go and fetch the thirty-one is asking him to raid the One Fire again, which he has been waiting to be asked.

What he wants is his mother's brother's death at the Red Quarry paid for by the One Fire.
