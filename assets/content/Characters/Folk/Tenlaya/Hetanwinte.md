# Dossier {#dossier}

Hetanwinte, Sun Gives, is the village speaker (_hoyanka_) of [[place-henushewan|The Forks]], chosen by the earth-lodge village of the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]] to bargain with the hunting bands, set the price of corn in meat and robes and keep the peace of the autumn trading. He is a careful man who has done it for fifteen years and has kin in three of the bands.

The lodge owners of the Forks, led by [[being-shewantiyu|Shewantiyu]], have agreed to shut the village's cache pits this autumn against the Coyote band, whose raid brought the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]] down on them. Hetanwinte has said in council that a village which shuts its pits against a band under the [[miscgear-cornpipe|Corn Pipe]] is doing what the [Burned Village]{.name data-type="place/site"} did, the crime the Grass People count the worst they know. The lodge owners answered that the Burned Village shut its pits against the hungry. Both sides have sent runners to the bundle keeper of [[place-tansheyawa|Bison Ford]], [[being-kohanipo|Kohanipo]], who keeps the Corn Pipe, and he has not yet answered either.

He is fifty, soft-spoken, with a trader's memory for every debt on the river. He lost a sister's son in the raid. A party at the Forks this autumn is asked to witness the trading, and a party that can carry a word to the Coyote band's camp on the winter grass, and bring one back, is doing what he cannot be seen to do.

What he wants is the Forks' cache pits open to every band under the Corn Pipe this autumn, the Coyote band among them.
