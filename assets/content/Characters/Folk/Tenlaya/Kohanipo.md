Kohanipo is a 70-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall with a medium build. He has tawny skin, leathery complexion, dark brown hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Kohanipo, Mountain Sits, is the bundle keeper (_chekanka_) of [[place-tansheyawa|Bison Ford]], the largest of the river villages of the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]], a small, bald old man who has kept the village's bundle and the [[miscgear-cornpipe|Corn Pipe]] for thirty winters and has never once been seen in a hurry. The Corn Pipe is smoked at the opening of every autumn trading, and it binds every village to open its cache pits to the hungry before it sets a price, as the peace that [Loshitayu]{.name data-type="being/npc"} carried to the villages after the [Winter of the Ditch]{.name data-type="lore/history"} requires.

Every village speaker on the river and every society head of the bands knows his lodge, and this summer runners from both sides of one quarrel have been in it. The lodge owners of [[place-henushewan|The Forks]], burned by the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]] in answer to the Coyote band's raid, mean to shut their pits against the Coyote band. Their speaker, [[being-hetanwinte|Hetanwinte]], says that is the crime the Corn Pipe was made to prevent. Kohanipo has not answered either.

He is seventy and will not be rushed. A party at Bison Ford before the trading may be asked by him to go to the Forks, or to the Coyote band's camp, and come back and tell him what it saw, because what he answers will be the Corn Pipe's answer, and he means to answer once.

What he wants is an answer for the Forks after which the Corn Pipe still binds both sides of the quarrel.
