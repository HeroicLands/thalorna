# Dossier {#dossier}

Keshunwinte, Gives Robes, is a hunter of the Cairn band of the [[place-wehioshtergn|Cairn Plain]], and the [[affiliation-tenlaya|Grass People]] name him when they say what a man should be. He was born Kuntonwiwan, Stands in Thunder, and earned the name he carries in his twentieth winter, when he came home from his first raid with four robes taken from a town of the [[affiliation-tokchiaska|One Fire]] and gave all four away before he reached his mother's lodge. He has counted seven coups since. He has been hunt chief of his band twice, and both times he laid the office down on the day the season ended, which the bands admire more than either drive.

He is thirty-five, lean, quiet, and owns almost nothing; what he takes he gives, and what he is given he gives again. His band keeps the drive lanes at [[place-kuntontoshe|Thunder Cliff]], and this autumn he is among the lodges gathering in the valley below it, where the bundle keeper, [[being-mahintayu|Mahintayu]], will not open the bundle until the bands that wasted the herd have paid for it. He has already given his robes.

A party on the eastern grass that needs a guide, a hunter or a fighter who keeps his word can do no better. A party that offers him wealth finds he has nowhere to keep it.

What he wants is to die with nothing left in his lodge to give.
