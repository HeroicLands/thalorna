# Appearance {#appearance}

Toshel Ne'ok is a 61-year-old woman who stands 5'1" tall with a light build. She has dark brown skin, scarred complexion, graying black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Toshel Ne'ok leads the largest of the six kin groups of [[place-nekiahkul|Ne' Ki'ahkul]] that know how the town's steel is made, and she speaks for all six of [[affiliation-steelkin|The Steel Kin]]. Her kin group makes the breastplates of the best warriors of [[affiliation-ixbaltzaku|Ish'bal Tsa'ku]]. She is small and scarred, and she has no patience for visitors. When the capital's inspectors come up to weigh the steel against the cords, she meets them at the edge of the town and walks them from forge to forge herself.

Steel beyond the Ts'enmah's weight goes down to [[place-nepaxel|Ne' P'ashel]] struck with the mark of the kin group that made it, and a piece without a mark is steel that should have been in the Sun Lord's weight. She has seen unmarked steel on sale in that market, and she wants the seller's name before the inspectors hear of it.

**Motivation:** to keep the making of the steel within the six kin groups, and every piece of it marked.
