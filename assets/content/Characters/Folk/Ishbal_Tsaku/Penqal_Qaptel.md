# Dossier {#dossier}

P'enqal Q'aptel is the Ts'ikmah of [[place-tzalamtzltl|Ts'alam Ts'ultel]], the fortress of the highest pass in the mountains of [[affiliation-ixbaltzaku|Ish'bal Tsa'ku]]. As lord of the upper half he is one of the Sun's kin, named by the Ts'enmah, and he commands the garrison and the pass. He is a gray, exacting man who walks both walls twice a day.

Every load that crosses the mountains is opened, named and knotted at his gates, and the inspectors read the cords of the pass against the cords of the towns the trains came from. A merchant whose loads were lighter at the pass than at the market below swears they were not, so either the market's count or the pass's is false, and P'enqal Q'aptel wants to know which.

**Motivation:** that no count of the pass is ever found false.
