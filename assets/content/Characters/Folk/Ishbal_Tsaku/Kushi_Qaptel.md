# Appearance {#appearance}

K'ushi Q'aptel is a 49-year-old man who stands 5'4" tall with a heavy build. He has brown skin, weathered complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

K'ushi Q'aptel is the Captain of [[affiliation-stormfleet|The Storm Fleet]], the war canoes of the storm god's temple at [[place-tzalamne|Ts'alam Ne']], and a Ch'alish of the [[affiliation-puqilchaqun|Faith of P'uqil Ch'akun]], named by the temple's Ch'alish Tq'an. He is a quiet, heavy-shouldered priest who has steered the coast from end to end, and he reads cords better than any Keeper of the Levy the temple has had. He presents the account of the sea levy to the temple at each month's close.

He holds that the cords of [[being-chakulish|Ch'akul Ish]], the Ne'kalmah, are not the only ones that count the Ne'kalmah's ships. He pays for proof of any cargo that came into the harbor uncounted, and pays best for proof of one of the Ne'kalmah's.

**Motivation:** to catch the lord of the sea with a cargo the temple never counted.
