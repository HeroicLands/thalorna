# Appearance {#appearance}

Ch'akul Ish is a 47-year-old man who stands 5'9" tall with a medium build. He has brown skin, smooth complexion, black hair, and dark brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ch'akul Ish is the Ne'kalmah of [[place-tzalamne|Ts'alam Ne']], the lord of the sea who rules the great port of [[affiliation-ixbaltzaku|Ish'bal Tsa'ku]] for its Ts'enmah. He keeps the city's fleet and the cords that count it, and he pays its tribute to the capital in metal, cacao and feathers. The inspectors who walk the inland valleys without warning come to the coast by appointment, and they count the warehouses he shows them.

He is a tall, cheerful man who has sailed every reach of the coast from shrine to shrine, and he is said to keep a second set of cords that the inspectors never see. [[being-kushiqaptel|K'ushi Q'aptel]], Captain of [[affiliation-stormfleet|The Storm Fleet]], counts the Ne'kalmah's ships by what he sees rather than by what the Ne'kalmah's cords say, and the two fleets share one harbor.

**Motivation:** to keep his ships and his own counsel, whoever counts them.
