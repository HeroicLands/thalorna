# Appearance {#appearance}

Ndowela nu Ékunda is a 40-year-old woman who stands 5'5" tall and is of medium build. She has dark brown skin, black hair braided close to the scalp, and dark brown eyes that crinkle when she is bargaining. A water-gourd hangs from a cord at her hip, and she drinks from it before she sits. She wears a plain wrap and a trader's cloak and walks barefoot in the village.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ndowela nu Ékunda is the chieftain of the [[affiliation-ekunda|Ékunda Tribe]] and lives at [[place-ekundavlg|Ékunda Village]]. The elders of the village run it more than she does. Her work is the tribe's standing among the clans, since every clan waters its cattle at the spring in a bad year.

She is friendly, quick and plain-spoken, and never the first at the spring to say whose side she is on.

## Background

**Formation.** She grew up at the spring village and learned to bargain on the trading ground as a girl. In her twenties she served as the tribe's Envoy, protected by custom while she carried the word, and learned each clan's temper.

**Accession.** At thirty-four she took the chieftaincy by descent and the elders' consent, and the elders who run the village answer to her only so far as they choose.

**The spring.** Herders of all five clans water at the spring, and the Keeper of the Spring closes it to anyone who sheds blood. With the [[affiliation-ruwozi|Ruwozi]] and the [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]] at war, the spring is where their households may meet. The tribe's delegate may speak for it at the [[affiliation-greatlubemo|Great Lubemo]] and may bind it only with the elders' agreement.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is warm, shrewd and tireless, and she makes a guest of every stranger at the spring.

### Motivation

She wants the spring kept for every clan and the tribe's neutrality seen as a service.

### Strengths

Every clan needs the water, and [[affiliation-sengala|Sengala]] traders and foreign travelers use her trading ground and trust its fair price.

### Weaknesses

The elders can overrule her, and the tribe is four hundred souls in a country where a muster is thousands. She has no way to enforce the spring's peace except the clans' own need of it.
