# Appearance {#appearance}

Nyabesa nu Kolembozi is a 42-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall and is of slim build. She has dark brown skin, black hair in tight braids bound with river-reed, and dark brown eyes. A band of crocodile-tooth scars runs along her left forearm. She wears a short wrap of woven reed-cloth and a cloak of fish-skin when she travels, and she walks the way a canoe-woman does, with the knees a little bent.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nyabesa nu Kolembozi is the paramount of the [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]] and sits at [[place-nyangulovlg|Nyangulo]]. She speaks for the clan at the [[affiliation-greatlubemo|Great Lubemo]]. She answers for the river's flow and for the ferrying of every clan's herds, and she asks the senior spirit-speaker, [[being-lusheyaklm|Lusheya nu Kolembozi]], before the clan makes war.

She does not shout or hurry, and she names a grievance once.

## Background

**Formation.** She grew up in the fish-camps that follow the river and learned the reaches by name from the canoe-women of her household. A crocodile bit her left forearm at a crossing when she was a girl, and her household named it and settled the matter as the clan does. In her twenties she poled ferries under the Master of the Canoes.

**Accession.** At thirty-six, a woman of the paramount's line, she became paramount when the council of elders consented after the death of the paramount before her. This is her second Lubemo in the seat.

**The channel.** The dry season left the river-reaches low and a channel was turned aside. She holds that the reaches answer to the clan and not to the herds that need them, and she goes to [[place-hetolubemo|Heto Lubemo]] to sit across from a Convener who is a party to the war and to ask the other three clans, as the [[affiliation-ruwozi|Ruwozi]] do, to choose.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is cool and watchful, still as a person waiting at a landing for a current to turn.

### Motivation

She wants the rivers' flow under the clan's keeping and no Ruwozi herd crossing it without leave.

### Strengths

Every clan sends for her spirit-speakers. Her canoes can move a muster or stop one.

### Weaknesses

The river is the clan's whole wealth, and she cannot afford to close it. The senior spirit-speaker's word on war binds her, and the spirit-speakers do not hurry.
