# Appearance {#appearance}

Nzobu Ruwozi is a 50-year-old woman who stands 5'9" tall and is of medium build. She has dark brown skin weathered by the grass-country sun, black hair going gray and braided in the lineage patterns of the senior house, and dark brown eyes that stay on a speaker until the speaker has finished. She wears a plain herdswoman's wrap and a cloak of cured hide, and carries no weapon.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nzobu Ruwozi is the sixth to bear the name Nyoli Ruwozi, paramount of the [[affiliation-ruwozi|Ruwozi]], and sits at [[place-mbolewevlg|Mbolewe]]. As Convener she summons the [[affiliation-greatlubemo|Great Lubemo]], keeps its order of speaking and speaks last. This is her fourth Lubemo.

She is known across all five clans for her command of the totemic law, slow to answer and brief when she does.

## Background

**Formation.** She is the daughter of [Temoyi Ruwozi]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the fifth to bear the name, and grew up in the senior lineage's herds at Mbolewe. The lineage elders trained her in the totemic law, and she stood in her mother's delegation at two Lubemos.

**Accession.** When Temoyi died after her last Lubemo, the elders talked the succession out through a dry season, and Nzobu, in her mid-thirties, took the name by descent and their consent. She has presided over three Lubemos since.

**The seat now.** She summons this fourth as a party to its first business. After the dry season and the diverted channel her households need water that runs through [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]] country. She holds that the Pact's third term gives the herds the rivers, and that its first term, that the five do not lift a weapon against the five, binds her as it binds [[being-nyabesnklm|Nyabesa nu Kolembozi]].

## Psyche

### Personality

She is dry, patient and exact, and she lets a long silence do the work of an argument.

### Motivation

She wants the Lubemo to end in convergence and not in a muster, and she wants her cattle watered.

### Strengths

The elders of every clan trust her memory of the law, and the regiments answer her call within days.

### Weaknesses

She is Convener and party at once, and a paramount who judges her own quarrel is not believed whatever she decides.
