# Appearance {#appearance}

The Guardian stands at the gap in the dry-stone wall with empty hands, in a rawhide tunic with a cluster of eagle feathers at the shoulder, and asks each arrival one question.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Guardian of [[place-hetolubemo|Heto Lubemo]] is an eagle-shaman of the [[affiliation-ngonzi|Ngonzi]] who holds this enclosure and no other. The Guardian makes the offerings, receives each delegation at the wall, and says who may pass.

## Background

The Guardian was marked young as a child of the eagle and spent the years of apprenticeship among the terraces and the offerings of the enclosure. The Guardian holds the wall because the Ngonzi hold it, and the office passed to the Guardian from an elder of the clan. No weapon passes the stone, and nobody passes it who is not somebody's guest. The Guardian asks each arrival whose guest it is.

## Psyche

### Personality

Formal and unhurried.

### Motivation

The Guardian wants the wall kept and every arrival named.

### Strengths

The Guardian is the host's keeper, and the enclosure answers to the Guardian's word.

### Weaknesses

The Guardian's authority is that of a host and a keeper and not of a ruler.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

The Guardian serves the Ngonzi paramount, [[being-ndamesongnz|Ndameso nu Ngonzi]], and receives the Convener's summons for the Lubemo.
