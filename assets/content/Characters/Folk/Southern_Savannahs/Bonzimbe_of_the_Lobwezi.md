# Appearance {#appearance}

Bonzimbe of the Lobwezi is a 70-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall and is of slim build. He has brown skin, short white hair, and gray eyes clouded white and unseeing. He walks with a hand on the shoulder of a griot's apprentice. A knotted cord hangs at his belt, a knot for each Lubemo whose judgments he carries. He wears a faded wrap and speaks in a low, level voice that carries across a fire.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Bonzimbe of the Lobwezi is the senior griot of the Long Pact, of the [[affiliation-lobwezi|Lobwezi]], and lives at [[place-lombesavlg|Lombesa]]. He carries the terms of the Pact, the genealogies of the five paramounts and the recorded judgments of twenty-three Lubemos, and he recites them at the renewal. He enters into the record of the [[affiliation-greatlubemo|Great Lubemo]] which clan holds each claim.

All five clans hold him the nearest thing the Nyáluba have to a neutral arbiter. He rules nothing, and he is the man who is asked.

## Background

**Formation.** As a boy he was given to the senior griot of a village at Lombesa, and he spent his youth learning the Pact's terms, the genealogies and the judgments of the earlier Lubemos by recital, until he could give them back without a halt. His sight clouded in his forties.

**Accession.** When the griot who taught him died, he was named Senior Griot of the Long Pact in his fifties, and he has recited the Pact at the renewals since.

**The apprentices.** His memory is the only complete copy, and three apprentices are learning the genealogies and the judgments. None has the whole. This Lubemo's renewal follows a war, and the first term of the Pact is the one the war strains.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is gentle, unhurried and amused, and he answers a hard question with a story.

### Motivation

He wants the Pact's terms handed to his apprentices whole before his memory fails.

### Strengths

No clan disputes his record, and every paramount takes his question as a courtesy and not an accusation.

### Weaknesses

He is blind and old and cannot travel without help.
