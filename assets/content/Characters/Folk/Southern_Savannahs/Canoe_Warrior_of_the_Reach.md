# Appearance {#appearance}

The Canoe Warrior stands in the stern of a reed canoe in a rawhide tunic and breeches, a spear across the knees, and the canoe moves through the papyrus without a sound.

# Dossier {#dossier}

A Canoe Warrior is a boatman of the [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]] who musters in an age-set regiment and fights from reed canoes in channels no other clan can read. The Kolembozi strike from the papyrus and withdraw into it, and they use drums to coordinate scattered parties.

## Background

The warrior was put in a canoe as a child, joined an age-set regiment as a young man and learned the channels of his reach as a man learns a street. A Kolembozi muster controls water, and in this country that is controlling the ground.

## Psyche

### Personality

Wary and watchful.

### Motivation

He wants strangers out of the reach's water or in it as guests.

### Strengths

He moves through papyrus and channels that no other clan can read.

### Weaknesses

He is a boatman with a spear, and on dry ground he is only a man with a spear.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

He answers to the Reach-Elder and to the Master of the Canoes through the age-set's drums.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-cnvnrspkslst|The Convener Speaks Last]]: canoe parties of the reach above watch strangers who cross, and a stranger crosses as a guest or not at all.
