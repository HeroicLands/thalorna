# Appearance {#appearance}

Nkesheya nu Sengala is a 43-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of slim build. She has dark brown skin, black hair gathered in a high knot, and dark brown eyes that settle on a speaker's hands before they settle on a face. A collar of glass beads from the forest trade lies at her throat. She wears fine northern cloth in a plain cut and a cloak of cloth painted with leopard spots, and she moves quietly.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nkesheya nu Sengala is the paramount of the [[affiliation-sengala|Sengala]] and sits at [[place-nkabewivlg|Nkabewi]]. She speaks for the clan at the [[affiliation-greatlubemo|Great Lubemo]]. She answers for the clan's bargains with the rainforest tribes, and she keeps the clan's trade open to every clan.

She speaks little and listens a great deal. She makes an offer, and she does not make it twice.

## Background

**Formation.** She was raised in a trading house at Nkabewi and went north with the caravans as a young woman, learning the rainforest tribes' speech and their marriage exchanges, and she later headed her house's frontier trade.

**Accession.** At thirty-eight the elders of the lineages and the heads of the trading houses consented to her as paramount by descent. She has kept the clan's trade open to every clan since.

**The war and the trade.** The salt, fish and riverine goods that the [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]] bring up the coast are the goods the Sengala carry onward, and the [[affiliation-ruwozi|Ruwozi]] cattle-drives cross the northern belt in the dry season. A war between the two clans cuts both. She reaches [[place-hetolubemo|Heto Lubemo]] having told no one which way the clan will go, and she explains nothing to the Ruwozi.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is composed, courteous and hard to read.

### Motivation

She wants the goods of both clans to keep moving, and she wants the Lubemo to end without a muster.

### Strengths

Her traders are in every clan's country, and she learns what a clan means to do before it says so.

### Weaknesses

She has the most to lose by the war and the least standing to stop it. The clan's long memory of the drought-war weighs on her whenever a muster is spoken of.
