# Appearance {#appearance}

The Trail-Reader walks the edge of the cracked channel bed in a rawhide tunic and breeches, a spear on the shoulder, stopping to crouch and touch the clay before writing nothing down.

# Dossier {#dossier}

A Fénjaran Trail-Reader is a tracker of the [[affiliation-fenjara|Fénjara Tribe]] who reads ground, water-marks and landings for what passed over them and says what was found and where. The tribe offers one thing wherever it goes, ground known.

## Background

The trail-reader learned the river country and the grass behind a hunter of the tribe, rose from carrying the quiver to walking ahead of it and was called to the readings when a clan asks the tribe for a witness. The reading says the hand and nothing about the right.

## Psyche

### Personality

Patient and plain-spoken.

### Motivation

The trail-reader wants the reading true and the tribe's name clean of either side.

### Strengths

The trail-reader reads clay, bank and canoe-mark as a griot reads a genealogy.

### Weaknesses

The trail-reader says what was found and refuses to say what it means.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

The trail-reader answers to [[being-mbadekofnjr|Mbadeko nu Fénjara]] and walks beside whoever the chieftain sends.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-cnvnrspkslst|The Convener Speaks Last]]: the trail-reader reads the channel for the party and says what was found and where, and nothing about what it means.
