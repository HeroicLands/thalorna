# Appearance {#appearance}

The young man stands near his clan's camp in a rawhide tunic and breeches, a spear and a dagger at his belt, and watches the other camps across the grounds.

# Dossier {#dossier}

A Young Lineage Representative is a young man a clan sends to the sitting at [[place-hetolubemo|Heto Lubemo]] to watch and learn the way the elders speak. The young of the two warring clans come with the hurt of the war in them.

## Background

He was raised in his lineage's fold, joined his age-set's regiment and was chosen to go because an elder wanted someone with a good memory and a bad temper to see how a sitting works.

## Psyche

### Personality

Proud and quick to take offence.

### Motivation

He wants his lineage's grievance heard and answered.

### Strengths

He remembers every word said at the sitting.

### Weaknesses

He is spoiling for an incident.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

He answers to the elders of his lineage and sits at the edge of the camp.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-cnvnrspkslst|The Convener Speaks Last]]: the young of both warring clans come to watch the sitting, and a careless word at the camps' edge becomes an incident.
