# Appearance {#appearance}

The Water-Warden sits by the pools in the dark in a rawhide tunic with a spear across the knees and a torch unlit at hand, and marks each herd that comes down to drink.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Water-Warden watches the spring at [[place-ekundavlg|Ékunda Village]] by night and tells the [[being-springkeeper|Keeper of the Spring]] who has watered. The spring is open to every clan, on separate days.

## Background

The Water-Warden took the night watch as a young householder of the village, learned the herds by their sounds before their marks and keeps the count in memory.

## Psyche

### Personality

Quiet and exact.

### Motivation

The Water-Warden wants the count true and the pools quiet.

### Strengths

The Water-Warden sees in the dark and hears a herd a long way off.

### Weaknesses

The Water-Warden is one watcher at three pools.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

The Water-Warden answers to the Keeper of the Spring.

1. [[scenario-trnsatsprng|The Turns at the Spring]]: the Water-Warden watches the pools by night and tells the Keeper who has watered.
