# Appearance {#appearance}

Mbadeko nu Fénjara is a 45-year-old man who stands 6'0" tall and is of medium build. He has dark brown skin weathered to leather, cropped black hair, a stubble of beard, and dark brown eyes that look at the ground as often as at a speaker. A notch is cut from his right ear. He wears a belted tunic of bark-dyed hide and soft-soled shoes, and he is rarely heard approaching.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Mbadeko nu Fénjara is the chieftain of the [[affiliation-fenjara|Fénjara Tribe]] and lives at [[place-fenjaravlg|Fénjara Village]]. The tribe is hunters and trackers who herd nothing, and he is more often in the bush with them than in the village.

He speaks briefly. He says what was found and where, and he does not say what it means.

## Background

**Formation.** He grew up in the bush with the tribe's trackers and learned to read a trail from the Trail-Readers. He served as a Trail-Reader and then as Master of the Hunt.

**Accession.** At thirty-eight he became chieftain by descent and the elders' consent. The place is his only while both hold.

**The ground.** The Fénjara measure themselves in ground known. Their trackers have walked between the river country and the gallery forest and have seen the herds driven toward the [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]] pools and the channel that was turned aside. Both clans want that knowledge and neither can order it. A Fénjaran seen to carry word for one side costs the whole tribe its standing at the [[affiliation-greatlubemo|Great Lubemo]], and the elders have agreed to nothing.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is quiet, patient and watchful, and he answers a question with a question.

### Motivation

He wants the tribe heard on what was seen and not on what was wished, and no Fénjaran tracker used as a clan's scout.

### Strengths

His trail-readers find what others cannot, and the tribe is known to every clan. [[being-tabesnfnjr|Tabeso nu Fénjara]] is his best-known tracker.

### Weaknesses

The tribe is small, and the Lubemo can ignore it. He has no standing but the trackers' reputation, and a single misread trail would cost it.

## Plot Hooks

1. **A trail to read.** Both clans ask the Fénjara to read the channel, and Mbadeko wants a witness from outside the clans to stand at the reading so that neither can say it was bent.
2. **A tracker in a clan's pay.** A Fénjaran youth has been seen carrying word for a camp, and Mbadeko needs the youth brought home before the Lubemo hears of it.
3. **The deep bush.** A party hired by a clan asks to be guided through the gallery forest, and Mbadeko will guide them only if they tell him to whom they answer.
