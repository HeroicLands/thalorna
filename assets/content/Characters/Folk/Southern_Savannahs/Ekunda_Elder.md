# Appearance {#appearance}

The Ékunda Elder sits among the other elders in the shade of the village in a rawhide tunic with a staff, and answers each messenger once.

# Dossier {#dossier}

An [[affiliation-ekunda|Ékunda Tribe]] Elder is a senior of a lineage, one of the few who decide who waters at the spring and when. The elders run the village more than the chieftain does.

## Background

The elder was a herder, a householder and a witness to others' quarrels before the lineage named an elder, and the elders have sat at the pools through every dry season in memory.

## Psyche

### Personality

Slow to speak and slower to change.

### Motivation

The elder wants the spring kept open to every clan.

### Strengths

The elder holds the village's memory of the spring.

### Weaknesses

The elders refuse to say more than they have said.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

The elder sits with the other elders and with [[being-ndowelaeknd|Ndowela nu Ékunda]].

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-trnsatsprng|The Turns at the Spring]]: the elders run the village, refuse both clans' messengers and refuse to say more.
