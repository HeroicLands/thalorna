# Appearance {#appearance}

Ndameso nu Ngonzi is a 53-year-old man who stands 5'11" tall and is of medium build. He has brown skin, short black hair gone gray over the ears, a short close beard, and dark brown eyes. Gold wire is wound round his left wrist, and the hand that wears it is scarred with old burns. He dresses in plain dyed wool and a cape of eagle feathers worn only at the Lubemo.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Ndameso nu Ngonzi is the paramount of the [[affiliation-ngonzi|Ngonzi]] and sits at [[place-ndelushivlg|Ndelushi]]. He answers to the other four clans for the welcome of the delegations at [[place-hetolubemo|Heto Lubemo]], which his clan keeps for the [[affiliation-greatlubemo|Great Lubemo]]. He receives the Guardians of [[place-oldhetos|The Old Hetos]] at Ndelushi and holds the clan's goldwork through the Master of the Gold.

He speaks softly and sits very still.

## Background

**Formation.** He was apprenticed at the highland forges as a boy, and the burns on his hand are from it. He learned the walls and the offerings from the Guardian of Heto Lubemo, and he served the clan as Master of the Gold before the paramountcy.

**Accession.** At forty-four he took the place by descent and the council's consent. This is his third Lubemo as paramount.

**The welcome.** He has apportioned the camping grounds so that the [[affiliation-ruwozi|Ruwozi]] and the [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]] are not in each other's sight, and the order within the wall is the Guardian's to keep and his to answer for. His clan holds that the stone-builders were its ancestors, and he upholds the Guardians' refusal of outsiders at every Old Heto but Heto Lubemo, whose enclosure the delegations of the five-clan council enter under his clan. The [[affiliation-lobwezi|Lobwezi]] griots say otherwise, and he does not argue the point in front of them.

## Psyche

### Personality

He is courteous, reserved and exact about precedence, and he prefers a gift to a speech.

### Motivation

He wants a Lubemo that ends with the enclosure unspoiled and the clan's offerings accepted by all five.

### Strengths

Every delegation owes him the debt of guest to host, and the Guardians receive him as one of their own.

### Weaknesses

A war inside the enclosure's walls would fail the Ngonzi's one duty, and he cannot take a side without losing the clan's standing as host.
