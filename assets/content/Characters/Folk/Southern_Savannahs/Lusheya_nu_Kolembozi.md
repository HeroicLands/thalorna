# Appearance {#appearance}

Lusheya nu Kolembozi is a Kolembozi woman, a spirit-speaker of the crocodile.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Lusheya nu Kolembozi is the senior _zherami nyende_ of the [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]], the spirit-speaker of the crocodile. The other clans send for the clan's spirit-speakers when a matter needs judgment, healing or a bargain with a difficult spirit, and she is the senior of them. Her line is held by the griot of the Kolembozi, and it is not recited with the record of her initiation.

At her initiation she walked into one of the [[place-oldhetos|Old Hetos]] and came out speaking [[lore-stonetongue|the Stone Tongue]], the speech the [[affiliation-ngonzi|Ngonzi]] elders say belonged to the stone-builders and no living Nyáluban understands. The Ngonzi confirm the report, and the Lobwezi griots have entered it into the record of the [[affiliation-greatlubemo|Great Lubemo]]. She says nothing of what the tongue is for.

The Nyáluba count her their admired woman. She stood at the border of the world the clans share with the dead and came back without claiming what she brought.

## Background

**Initiation.** In her initiation she walked into an Old Heto, and she came out of it speaking the Stone Tongue. The griots recite the entry as it was received, and the story told at the fire belongs to no record and is told differently by every teller.

**The office.** The senior spirit-speaker judges what the river-spirits have taken or are owed, and is asked before the clan makes war. [[being-nyabesnklm|Nyabesa nu Kolembozi]], the paramount, asks her before the clan goes to war, and her word on war binds the paramount.

**The war.** The Kolembozi are at war with the [[affiliation-ruwozi|Ruwozi]] over the water. [[being-nzoburwz|Nzobu Ruwozi]], the Ruwozi paramount, convenes the Lubemo and holds the higher rank. Lusheya holds the harder office.

## Psyche

### Personality

She shows the restraint the griots show with words, turned to the spirits.

### Motivation

She keeps what she brought out of the Old Heto unclaimed.

### Strengths

Every clan sends for the Kolembozi spirit-speakers, and she is the senior of them. The paramount asks her before the clan makes war.

### Weaknesses

She cannot say what she knows. The Ngonzi hold one answer on whose dead the builders were and the baobab griots another, and the record holds only that the two differ.
