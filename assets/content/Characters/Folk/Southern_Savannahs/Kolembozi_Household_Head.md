# Appearance {#appearance}

The Household Head leads her people and her herd down to the pools at first light in a rawhide tunic with a spear in hand, and her household files behind her in order.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The Kolembozi Household Head leads a river household of the [[affiliation-kolembozi|Kolembozi]] that waters its herd at the spring at [[place-ekundavlg|Ékunda Village]] on the Kolembozi days. The [[affiliation-ruwozi|Ruwozi]] water on their own.

## Background

She grew up in a river household, poled and herded as a girl and took the household's lead when her elders died or grew too old to walk the pools. She carries her household's water-right from the channels to the spring.

## Psyche

### Personality

Composed and careful of her people.

### Motivation

She wants her household's herd watered and her people home.

### Strengths

Her household follows her and moves quickly.

### Weaknesses

Her household cannot stay long where the Ruwozi water.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

She answers to the Reach-Elder of her landing and to the Kolembozi camp at Heto Lubemo.

## Plot Hooks

1. [[scenario-trnsatsprng|The Turns at the Spring]]: her household comes to the pools at first light on the Kolembozi day.
