Amqelenset is a 49-year-old man who stands 5'7" tall with a light build. He has ebony skin, gray hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Amqelenset, a nobleman and architect of [[place-amqelulegez|Amqelu-Legez]], has shaped a good part of the capital's great buildings through commissions only he chooses to accept. He is the City Theater Company's principal patron as well, and has grown captivated enough by the performer [[being-thiryegezaqe|Thirye Gez'Aqêu]] to fund productions she alone wants built. Winning his favour, in building or in theatre, means winning more than money.
