# Dossier {#dossier}

Makuptas is a lord of the eastern reaches, home in [[place-iqu|Iku]], who keeps a performer at his house whenever that performer returns to the capital. He sees in the performer the idealist he and the performer both once were. The hospitality he offers is, in part, an argument with his own younger self.

He holds [[affiliation-selatsltplgn|The Selat of Selat-Pelgun]] from [[place-iqu|Iku]], the caravan-head for the eastern desert roads, and when he is at court in [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]] he keeps the performer at his house there.
