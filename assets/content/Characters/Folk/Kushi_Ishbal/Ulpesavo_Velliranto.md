# Appearance {#appearance}

Ulpesavo Velliranto is a man of 1,500 years who stands 6' 3" tall and is of light build. His long white hair is bound at the nape with a heron's quill, his eyes are grey, a thin pale scar runs through his left eyebrow, and his pale skin is almost without color. He wears a long robe of woven reed and sits at the lagoon's edge every evening, motionless, until the last flight has gone up.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 1,500. Seer of the Herons. Wants the flights read as they are.**

Ulpesavo Velliranto is the seer of the [[affiliation-nclvlkvst|Enclave of Ilkaivesto]]. Every evening the herons rise from the deep swamp in flights larger than anywhere else on the lowland, and he reads them: the order they rise in, the direction they turn, the hour. His hearth is Velliranto, the hearth of the heron's call, and he is kin of the lord [[being-tessikalavllrn|Tessikala Velliranto]]. Whatever the hearths do with his readings is theirs to decide.

## Background

Ulpesavo spent his youth calling herons for the seer before him, and a startled bird gave him the scar through his left eyebrow in his first season. He took up the reading when his teacher's eyes failed, and he has read the flights for more than a thousand years.

:::secret {title="The hearths among the herons"}
Ulpesavo reads the flights from the lagoon where the herons roost among the hearths, and he gives the Keeper, Tessikala Velliranto, what they say in the words in which he reads them.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Ulpesavo is dry, attentive and kind in a way that costs him effort. He rarely finishes a sentence in one breath, and he stops to let a heron cross the water.

### Motivation

He wants the flights read as they are, and given on before the hearths have decided what they wish them to say.

### Strengths

His readings have been right for more than a thousand years. The herons rise for him as for no other.

### Weaknesses

He reads only what the herons show, and he cannot make the hearths listen to an answer they dislike.

## Plot Hooks

1. **An evening at the lagoon.** A hunter of the plateau claims to have watched the herons rise in a pattern he has not seen again, and a scholar of the temples wants a party to find the pattern and the one who reads it.
2. **Plumes in the market.** A trader's stall at the foot of the plateau holds white heron plumes of a luster the buyers call strange. A buyer from the coast wants a party to learn which part of the swamp they came from.

:::secret {title="What the flights say this season"}
This season's flights say that the herons will leave the lagoon if the plume trade goes on. He has given the Keeper the reading and told no one else, because the hearths that hold the herons as kin would act on it at once.
:::
