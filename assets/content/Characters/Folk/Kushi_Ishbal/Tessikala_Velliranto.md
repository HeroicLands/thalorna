# Appearance {#appearance}

Tessikala Velliranto is a woman of 1,940 years who stands 6' 2" tall and is of light build. Her white hair is worn loose and long, her eyes are a clear grey, and her pale skin has the faint sheen of a pearl. She wears a white heron's feather behind her right ear and a plain gown the color of the reeds. She stands in water to the ankle as readily as on a bank.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Age 1,940. Lord of Ilkaivesto. Wants the herons left in peace.**

Tessikala Velliranto is a lord of the [[affiliation-nclvlkvst|Enclave of Ilkaivesto]], raised by every voice of the hearths. Her hearth is Velliranto, the hearth of the heron's call. Neither the Ts'enmah nor the temples of [[affiliation-kuxiixbal|K'ushi Ish'bal]] have ever sent anyone to the conclave, and the rim hunters say only that the deep swamp belongs to the tall people.

## Background

Tessikala was born in the deep swamp in a year of great floods and spent her first wet season in a reed cradle on the water. She learned the herons' calls before she learned the hearths' songs, and the hearths raised her a lord for the long patience with which she has kept the deep swamp as it is.

:::secret {title="The hearths among the herons"}
Tessikala is the Hemmikanno, the Keeper of the Marsh, raised by every voice of the hearths and holding the office for as long as they do not raise another. She governs the conclave, drives out an outlaw, and answers for the herons that roost in the swamp in the thousands, which the hearths hold in a kind of kinship that they do not explain.
:::

## Psyche

### Personality

Tessikala is calm, cool-eyed and very patient, and she moves through water without ripple. She speaks of the herons seldom and never lightly.

### Motivation

She wants the herons left in peace, and the swamp's deep kept as it is.

### Strengths

The hearths trust her judgment, and she gives it without counting the cost to herself. The swamp itself is her ally.

### Weaknesses

She is slow to act on any trouble that begins outside the swamp, and it reaches her late.

## Social

### Companions

The lord [[being-ennumivospllnt|Ennumivo Sepullanto]], whom she trusts, and the seer [[being-ulpesavovllrnt|Ulpesavo Velliranto]], her kinsman.

### Patrons

None. The hearths raised her.

### Enemies

The plume trade, though she has never named it.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-nclvlkvst|Enclave of Ilkaivesto]]**: Tessikala is a Lord.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The price in his head.** A feather-trader of the north has set a price on white heron plumes and a rim hunter has gone into the deep swamp after them. The trader asks a party to find the hunter, who owes him for a season's credit, and bring back either the man or the plumes.
2. **Days late.** A rim hunter comes out of the swamp days late and empty-handed and says that nothing happened to him. His wife wants a party to find out why he cannot say what he saw.

:::secret {title="The kin she cannot judge"}
Tessikala's own hearth is among those that hold the herons as kin, so she answers for the herons as one of them, and for that reason she cannot be the one to decide what is to be done to the plume trader. She has asked Ennumivo Sepullanto to judge it for her and has told the hearths she will decide at the next flight. She does not intend to decide it herself, and the hearths do not know.
:::
