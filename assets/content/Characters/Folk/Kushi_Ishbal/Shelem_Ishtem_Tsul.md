# Dossier {#dossier}

Shelem Ishtem Tsul is the First of the Wall of [[affiliation-wallsworn|The Wall-Sworn]], the sworn brotherhood of the garrison companies at [[place-qptlulneok|Q'aptel'ul Ne'ok]]. His name is a wall-name, given him by the Keeper of the Wall-Names when he took the wall. Nobody in the order knows the name he set aside, and the Keeper says he has forgotten it.

He is a small, bow-legged man with a voice like gravel, and he has guided more expeditions across the flats of [[place-qapteltzaklrgn|Q'aptel Tsa'kul Region]] to the gate of [[place-xelemchalix|Shelem Ch'alish]] than any brother living. A Severer at the gate of the town has named one of his brothers as a priest who fled the Blood-Denied sentence in the north, and he refuses to say what name that brother set aside.

**Motivation:** that a brother's wall-name stands on him in place of whatever the north still holds against him.
