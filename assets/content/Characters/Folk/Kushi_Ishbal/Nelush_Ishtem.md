# Appearance {#appearance}

Nelush Ishtem is a 71-year-old man who stands 5'6" tall with a light build. He has tawny skin, weathered complexion, white hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Nelush Ishtem is the Ch'alish Tq'an of the lake temples of [[place-ixkulchalix|Ishkul Ch'alish]], the temple-city of the sacred lake of [[affiliation-kuxiixbal|K'ushi Ish'bal]], and an old priest of the [[affiliation-tzenultqan|Faith of Ts'en'ul Tq'an]]. In K'ushi Ish'bal the priesthood's word prevails over the Ts'enmah's wherever the two differ, and at Ishkul Ch'alish there is nothing else. He rules the city, names its two half-lords from among the temple's own servants and puts them out of office when he chooses. The water priestesses of the [[affiliation-xaqikhanal|Faith of Shaq'ik Ha'nal]] keep the bathing pools and the rites of the shallows under him.

He is famous for his memory and for never raising his voice. The lake's southern shore has turned bitter over a long reach, and the priests have closed it. At each turning of the moon's count a priestess tastes the water at the edge of the bitter reach and reports to him, and what she reports goes no farther, not even to the Ts'enmah, who has asked.

**Motivation:** to keep the lake's secrets with the temples, beyond the Sun Lord's reach.
