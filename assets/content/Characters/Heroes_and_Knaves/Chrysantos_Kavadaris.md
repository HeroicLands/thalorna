# Appearance {#appearance}

![[chrysntskvdrs|Chrysántos Kavadáris]]{float: top-left}

Everything he wears was chosen, and chosen to look as though it was not. He is 5'10" and lightly built, pale, black-haired, dark-eyed, with an oval face, a straight nose, a sharp jawline, sculpted cheeks and dark brows, and a flame tattooed on one leg that he does not explain.

# Dossier {#dossier}

His older brother got the family's trade. Chrysántos, the second son of a Byzarian merchant house with interests across the League, got an education, some capital, and the expectation that he would make his own. He did not compete with his family. He found the buyers the family did not serve — nobles and the very rich who wanted a thing made to order, a rare import, an object with a history or a holiness attached to it — and made himself the man between those buyers and the craftsmen, suppliers and sources who could produce what they wanted.

The network runs through the Byzarian League, the City-States of Vedyara, the Kingdom of Tarvénia and past them: master craftsmen, artisans of the first rank, the people who can get rare materials, and on the other side nobles, merchant lords, clergy and collectors who will pay a great deal for one particular thing. He knows things about all of them, and manages what he knows with care. To a certain kind of rich client he has become the person who finds what they did not know existed and buys what they would rather not be seen buying.

## Psyche

### Personality

Appetite and ambition, under several layers of good manners. He is clever and well spoken and can talk about history, art, letters and philosophy convincingly in any drawing room in the League, though the learning is stock: he collects facts about art the way he collects the art, for what they are worth in conversation.

He is charming when it pays. Some of the charm is real interest in the other person, and some is his estimate of what they want to hear. He is not cruel. He is indifferent, completely, to anyone outside his own circles; a family ruined by one of his manoeuvres is a figure in a ledger. He trusts his own taste and judgement, delivers his opinions on what a thing is worth as settled fact, and is not often argued with.

### Motivation

More: wealth, influence, standing, and the deference that comes with having more resources and better connections than the person he is talking to. He wants his house to be the first luxury broker in the known territories and to stand higher than his family's. He wants beautiful and rare things, as stock and for himself, because owning them says something about him. And he would like it shown that the family picked the wrong heir.

### Strengths

- **Mercantile Genius**: He counts fast, bargains in two languages, understands what a thing is really worth to the person who wants it, and wins at the table.
- **Network and Connections**: Hundreds of craftsmen, collectors, nobles, priests and fellow brokers who will take his message; the network is the business.
- **Discourse and Eloquence**: He talks his way past doors, out of a reluctant seller's treasure, and into a client's purse for things they did not come to buy.
- **Perception and Reasoning**: He reads what a person wants under what they say they want, and sees the path to advantage in a tangled situation.
- **Survival and Practical Knowledge**: More useful in rough country than he looks — he can handle an animal, find his way and keep himself alive where the rare things come from.

### Weaknesses

- **Moral Flexibility**: Tomb goods, objects made with other people's suffering, deals that hurt people who could not object: he has brokered them all.
- **Arrogance**: He is sure of his own judgement and dismisses anyone of lower station as slower than himself, and misses what they could have told him.
- **No Fighter**: Untrained, not strong, not quick; in a fight he depends on the men he has hired.
- **The Brother**: His resentment of his older brother is the one place he can be reached, and anyone who finds it can steer him by his pride.
- **Nothing but the Network**: If a major contact turns, or his name for discretion cracks, or his patrons stop trusting his eye, there is nothing underneath.

## Social

### Patrons

- **Lord Daemon Voss, Master of the Byzarian League's Eastern Territory**: Protection and access to restricted goods on one side; discretion and exclusive use of Chrysántos's best contacts on the other.
- **The Collector of Reliquaries**: A buyer — a person, or an institution, he has never been sure — who commissions him to find and acquire particular objects of history or holiness, and pays better than anyone else does.
- **Master Artificer Kaelvos**: A craftsman of the first rank whose work Chrysántos sells and whose commissions he brokers. It suits them both, and Kaelvos has made clear what would happen if it stopped suiting him.

### Enemies

- **Merchant-Lord Tharenis**: A rival broker in the League's central territories, who has undercut him on several commissions and is courting his clients. Neither has declared it, and it is war.
- **The Puritan Orders**: Monks and priests who have noticed that relics and sacred objects are reaching private collectors through him without the church's leave, and are quietly poisoning his name with bishops and magistrates alike.
- **Unknown Parties with Claims to Artifacts**: The descendants and heirs of the tombs, ruins and battlefields his goods came from are asking where things went. Some have found out. Some have made threats.

### Affiliations

- **Merchant's Guild of the Byzarian League**: His standing, his protection, and his ear to the ground.
- **Prosperity Cults of Vénusia**: The flame on his leg marks at least an initiate of the fertility and prosperity mysteries of Vénusia. How far in he is, nobody who knows him can say.
- **The Collector's Circle**: Rich collectors, merchants and brokers who trade among themselves and pass word of what is coming to market.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Cursed Artifact**: He bought a relic of real age from a seller in Vedyara he could not place and sold it to a collector in the Kingdom of Tarvénia. The collector has since lost his health, his money and his reason, blames the relic, and has come after Chrysántos to get it back — first with lawyers, then with worse. The seller, it turns out, answers to people who want it too. It may be exactly what the collector says it is.

2. **The Stolen Masterpiece**: A piece made by **Master Artificer Kaelvos** for a noble with friends at court was stolen on the road, and the noble holds the broker responsible. Two lunar cycles to recover it, or ruin and a magistrate. The thieves are of the [[affiliation-adanasreni|Ādānaśreṇī]] — **[[being-chndrkrtsndrjyvl|Chandrakīrtisundarī Jayavalli]]** may have been among them — hired by a rival to wreck his name. He will have to bargain with the guild and find the rival at the same time.

3. **The Competitor's Downfall**: Tharenis has collapsed in a few weeks — clients gone, name gone, house gone — and his trade has fallen into Chrysántos's lap. Someone did it, and did it for him. The someone will name a price, and it will be that Chrysántos becomes their broker and finds them things they do not explain.

4. **The Repatriation War**: The heirs of an old culture want back several sacred objects he acquired and sold, and have documents showing the objects were taken in conquest. They have appealed to the **Byzarian League's** cultural authorities. His clients refuse to give the objects up and threaten to sue him if the sales are voided. One of the pieces, he suspects, is not what he sold it as, and returning it would be a fraud on the people demanding it.

5. **The Brother's Gambit**: His brother, after years of bad blood, proposes to join the two houses: Chrysántos's brokers and contacts with the family's ships, warehouses and capital. It would be the largest merchant enterprise in the League. It would also, he thinks, put him under his brother's hand or strip his network into the family's. Refuse, and the brother has the means to spend years taking him apart.
