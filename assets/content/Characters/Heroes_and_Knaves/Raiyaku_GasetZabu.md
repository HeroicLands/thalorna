# Appearance {#appearance}

![[raiyaqugstzb|Raiyaku Gaset'Zabu]]{float=top-left}

Raiyaku is a tall, light-built woman of 5'8" with dark skin, black hair dressed simply and dark brown eyes under straight dark brows. Her nose is noble and her jaw clean, and her cheekbones are prominent. She dresses in fine silk of understated color rather than the shades that mark rank, wears one signet and no more, and pays more attention to the cut of an artisan's work than to anything she wears. At her own gatherings she is at the door herself, and she repeats every name once.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**A Daughter of Privilege**

Raiyaku was born into the [House of Gasezabu]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, one of the oldest and most respected noble families in the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]], with ancestry tracing back through two centuries of administrators, generals, and patrons of the arts. Her father, the deceased Lord [[being-alunetgasezb|Alunet Gasezabu]], was a celebrated diplomat and scholar who served the Empire in diplomatic missions to neighboring realms. Her mother, Lady [[being-zezabugasezb|Zezabu Gasezabu]] (née Qelti), brought connections to the military elite through her own lineage. From childhood, Raiyaku was groomed for the courtier's life: trained in languages, the arts, diplomatic forms, and the arithmetic of noble alliance-building.

**Cultivation of Influence Through Compassion**

Where many young nobles of her station view their position primarily as privilege to be enjoyed, Raiyaku early developed a real concern for the artisans, merchants, and common people whose talents and labor sustain the empire's cultural and economic life. By her early twenties, she had begun hosting salons where talented artists, musicians, and craftspeople could exhibit their work to influential nobles, often using her personal fortune to commission new works or subsidize promising talents. Unlike patronage given only for return, Raiyaku's support comes with authentic interest in the creators' vision and well-being. This unusual combination of compassion with aristocratic influence has made her beloved among the creative classes and respected (if sometimes with envy) among her noble peers.

**Current Position**

Now at twenty-seven, Raiyaku serves on [[affiliation-genzetzabu|Genzet'Zabu]], the Local Nobility Council, where she speaks for the support of the arts and the protection of artisan guilds. She maintains a palatial residence that doubles as a venue for cultural events, and her guest lists are among the most coveted in the empire. She has thus far resisted pressure to marry, preferring to remain independent and focused on her works of cultural patronage. Her reputation for integrity is such that even those who might not appreciate her reforming values understand that her word is absolute and her loyalty steadfast.

## Psyche

### Personality

Raiyaku is defined by an almost radical authenticity unusual in courtly circles—she does care about the people she meets, remembers their names and concerns, and follows up with meaningful support. It is a mature choice, not naïveté, to live according to her values despite considerable pressure to view others as tools for advancement. She possesses a natural charisma that draws people into her orbit, yet she uses this magnetism not for personal aggrandizement but to elevate those around her.

Yet for all her grace and warmth, Raiyaku is no mere pleasant decoration. Her mind is sharp, her perception keen, and her understanding of political currents is deep. She simply chooses to deploy these talents in service of her idealistic vision rather than petty personal advancement. She has a gift for finding common ground between opposing factions and convincing people of the value of compromise—not through manipulation, but through helping each side see the humanity in their opponents. She is given to moments of enthusiasm about ideas, art, and human potential that can seem almost naive to the more cynical, but which speak to a kind of wisdom that surpasses cynicism.

### Motivation

Raiyaku Gasezabu is driven by a vision of the Empire of Au'Khelathu as a place where talent, merit, and creativity are recognized and nurtured regardless of birth. She believes that the greatest cultural achievements of her society have emerged from the contributions of humble artisans, and she sees her patronage and advocacy as both a moral obligation and an investment in the empire's future. She is motivated equally by a personal hunger for beauty, learning, and authentic connection—she surrounds herself with talented and interesting people because she enjoys their company and conversation. Beyond personal satisfaction, Raiyaku seeks to prove that privilege, when wielded with conscience, becomes a means for others to flourish rather than for her own gain.

### Strengths

Charisma and Social Grace
: Raiyaku's presence immediately puts people at ease; her interest in others makes even minor encounters feel meaningful. This translates to extraordinary influence in courtly and social contexts.

Keen Perception and Political Acumen
: She has a sharp and analytical mind; she reads social situations with precision and understands the currents of power and loyalty that move through noble society.

Eloquent and Persuasive Speech
: When she chooses to advocate for a position, her combination of rhetorical skill and authentic conviction proves nearly irresistible; even opponents find themselves moved by her arguments.

Vast Network of Talented Allies
: Her years of patronage have created a web of artisans, musicians, scholars, and craftspeople across multiple cities who owe her their success and maintain deep loyalty to her.

Financial Resources and Access
: As a noble of significant independent wealth, Raiyaku can fund ventures, commission services, and open doors that would be closed to others of lesser station.

Creative Problem-Solving
: Her exposure to artists' and creators' perspectives has given her unconventional ways of approaching difficult problems; she often sees solutions others overlook.

### Weaknesses

Idealism Outpacing Reality
: Raiyaku's fundamental belief in people's essential goodness and capacity for growth sometimes blinds her to malice or deep-rooted corruption; she can be taken advantage of by those who exploit her trust.

Privileged Blind Spots
: For all her compassion, there are realities of struggle and desperation that her comfortable life has not made viscerally real to her; her proposed solutions sometimes lack the practical grounding that hardship provides.

Vulnerability to Emotional Manipulation
: Because she cares about people's well-being, those skilled in exploiting her sympathy can sway her judgment, particularly if they present themselves as artists or creative talents in need.

Insufficient Political Ruthlessness
: In a nobility where alliances shift on advantage, Raiyaku's reluctance to play cutthroat politics puts her at disadvantage against more pragmatic rivals who take it as natural that one house's gain is another's loss.

Overextension Through Generosity
: Her tendency to commit resources to worthy causes without fully reckoning the sum can leave her financially strained or unable to deliver promised support.

Inexperience with Hardship
: She has never truly suffered loss, failure, or deprivation, leaving her emotionally unprepared for the kinds of betrayals and cruelties that may come if her idealism is tested severely.

## Social

### Patrons

Lady [[being-qeketqeltag2|Qeket Qelt'Agetu]]
: An elder noblewoman of celebrated taste who has been Raiyaku's mentor in artistic patronage and cultural politics. She provides counsel on the shifting alliances of the factions.

Master Architect [Laraket]{.name data-type="being/npc"}
: A celebrated designer and builder who has benefited tremendously from Raiyaku's commissions and patronage; he is devoted to her vision of elevating artisan crafts and advises her on building and public works.

[[affiliation-genzetuznera|Genzet'Uzn'era]]
: The priesthood of the fertility goddess that shares Raiyaku's values concerning growth and renewal. They provide her with spiritual counsel and, occasionally, practical support for her endeavors.

### Enemies

Lord [[being-zelegezu2|Zelegezu]]
: A calculating noble of middling talent and significant wealth who views Raiyaku's influence over the artistic community as a threat to his own status. He has spread insinuations that her patronage of common-born artisans is unseemly, and he quietly funds rival patrons to dilute her influence.

[[affiliation-linzuwaretgl|Lin'Zuwaret elu Igelu]]
: An organization of wealthy traders who view Raiyaku's advocacy for artisan rights and protections as interference in free trade; they resent her efforts on the council to fix minimum prices and conditions of work.

Faction of Conservative Traditionalists
: A loose coalition of older nobles who view Raiyaku's reforming ideas about advancement by merit and the arts for all as threats to the proper order of society; they mock her behind her back as naïve and dangerous.

### Affiliations

Local Nobility Council
: Raiyaku is an active member, though her reform-minded positions often put her at odds with the council's traditional conservative faction.

[Artisans' and Craftspeople's Alliance]{.name data-type="affiliation"}
: While not formally a member, Raiyaku works closely with this organization, speaking within noble circles for the alliance's interests.

[[affiliation-genzetzabqar|Genzet'Zabqar]]
: An informal network of noble families and wealthy individuals committed to cultural patronage; Raiyaku is among the most active and influential members.

## Plot Hooks

The Fire in Lut-Lemu
: A fire that begins in a potter's kiln runs lane to lane through the craft quarter before the Meglai's fire-gangs pen it in. Three workshops she has commissioned are lost, and the council vote to fix minimum prices for artisans' work falls in two days. The potter swears the kiln was banked. A trader's association that opposes the vote has a factor who was seen on the lane the evening before.

The Disputed Masterpiece
: A young faience-maker she has championed finishes a work that causes a sensation among the high patrons, and a rival claims the design belongs to an older master. Raiyaku funded the work and is the first to be asked to vouch. The proof is in the workshop of the master, who is in the pay of Lord Zelegezu.

The Two Houses
: Two noble houses are on the point of violence over a boundary and a marriage, and the Genzet'Zabu cannot agree to hold the quarrel. Raiyaku proposes that both commission a joint work for the Festival of the Going-Down, which would oblige them to share a workshop for a season. Neither house will accept it unless the other agrees first, and the festival is near.

The Library on Her Own Walls
: A guest at one of her evenings leaves behind a scroll of a work that the temples have banned. Copies are being made and passed along in the quiet of her gatherings, and the temple inquisitors know her house is the place. She can name the one responsible, or accept that her door is part of the trade.

:::secret {title="What the referee knows"}
Her mentor, Lady Qeket Qelt'Agetu, commissions the thefts of banned texts and uses Raiyaku's gatherings as the place where the copies change hands. Raiyaku's reputation for an absolute word is the cover, and Qeket chose her salons for that reason.

The fire was set by Lord Zelegezu's steward, who paid the kiln's apprentice. The trader's factor was there to make sure the blame would fall on the association instead.
