# Dossier {#dossier}

## Background

### Early Mastery

Born into the distinguished [Fefes]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} merchant family of [[place-byzaris|Bizaris]], a leading craft town of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]], Dókhehran demonstrated an uncanny affinity for the arcane from her earliest years. While her siblings learned commerce and trade, she filled the family library's margins with translations of ancient texts, much to her parents' bewilderment and concern. At thirteen, she formally petitioned the chapterhouse of [[affiliation-panepistmm|The Academy]] for apprenticeship—an extraordinary acceptance that bypassed the usual age of entry and marked her as extraordinary. Her Epistémion (the Academy) masters recognized immediately that she possessed genius rather than mere curiosity—a mind attuned to the hidden frequencies of magical reality itself, with particular brilliance in pattern-recognition and linguistic analysis.

### Scholarly Pursuits and Dangerous Experiments

For nearly two decades, Dókhehran devoted herself to the systematic cataloging and analysis of forgotten languages and archaic magical texts, studying linguistic patterns in spellwork and discovering connections between phonetic structures and the deeper mechanics of [[affiliation-zemorhodhis|The Six Ways]]. Her three published treatises on ancient thaumaturgical traditions earned her reputation as the foremost expert in her field throughout the Bizarian League and beyond, yet also made her a controversial figure. Her most recent work, _[On the Wards of the Sundered Kingdoms]{.name data-type="lore"}_, suggested that the fragmentation and decline of the ancient [[lore-helioniteclt|Helionite]] city-states was not inevitable catastrophe but the result of deliberate arcane workings by powers whose names and purposes have been deliberately erased from history. This thesis attracted both deep admiration from scholars who sought vindication of lost traditions and fierce denunciation from conservative factions within the Epistémion who viewed such speculations as destabilizing and potentially heretical.

### Present Circumstances

Now at the height of her intellectual powers, Dókhehran maintains an impressive private archive in a tower overlooking Bizaris. Her patron, the mysterious nobleman **Lord [[being-bufomazultch|Bufomazul Charzilzát]]**, a man of wealth and connections but ambiguous loyalties, has funded her recent expeditions into the sealed vaults beneath abandoned Helionite temples, seeking artifacts and texts that might reveal the true nature of the ancient kingdoms' fall and the magical knowledge they possessed. She commands respect within the Epistémion through sheer intellectual force and the prestige of her publications, yet her increasingly esoteric research has begun to isolate her from more traditional peers—particularly from the conservative faction within the Epistémion's leadership—who view her methodical descent into obscure magical theory and speculative history with growing unease and suspicion.

## Psyche

### Personality

Dókhehran is the scholarly temperament taken to its logical extreme—brilliant, focused, and wholly consumed by her work. She speaks with measured precision, rarely using colloquial language, and maintains a polite but emotionally distant demeanor with most acquaintances. Her sharp wit can be cutting, though never without purpose, and she has little patience for those who mistake enthusiasm for understanding. Those who get past her austere manner discover a woman capable of surprising warmth and a wry humor that catches most people off guard.

She is driven by an unquenchable thirst to understand the fundamental mechanisms underlying the fragmentation of the ancient Helionite civilization, convinced that the knowledge contained in those lost kingdoms holds keys to preventing a second such catastrophe. This intellectual obsession sometimes manifests as dangerous impulsivity—she has been known to risk catastrophic magical harm in pursuit of a single scrap of knowledge, rationalizing such risks as the necessary price of progress toward a truth she believes the Epistémion establishment would prefer to suppress.

### Motivation

Dókhehran's deepest motivation flows from a conviction that the decline of the Helionite kingdoms was not natural decay but the result of deliberate arcane interference by forces whose identities and purposes have been systematically erased from history—erased, she believes, by the very institutions that now govern magic. She is driven to recover this deliberately suppressed knowledge before it is lost entirely, and the conviction grants her the courage to pursue avenues of study that would terrify most practitioners and the moral certainty that her work justifies risks others would find unacceptable.

### Strengths

Arcane Linguistics Mastery
: Few scholars living can decipher pre-Collapse written languages with Dókhehran's fluency. She reads the dead tongues of the ancient kingdoms as readily as her native speech, giving her access to knowledge sealed away for centuries.

Thaumaturgical Theory
: Her understanding of magical resonance, ritual structure, and the underlying principles of spellcraft is of the first rank, allowing her to construct new rituals and sometimes achieve effects other mages consider impossible.

Scholarly Authority
: Her publications and reputation grant her considerable influence within academic and magical circles throughout the Bizarian League and beyond.

Perception and Analysis
: With sharp perception and reasoning, she notices patterns others miss and can synthesize seemingly unrelated information into deep insights.

Ritual Precision
: Her command of complex ritual magic is flawless; she executes elaborate ceremonies with the perfection of a master musician performing a memorized composition.

### Weaknesses

Physical Frailty
: Despite her powerful mind, Dókhehran possesses only modest physical strength and endurance. She would be quickly overcome in any direct combat.

Obsessive Recklessness
: Her consuming passion for forbidden knowledge sometimes overrides prudent judgment. She has narrowly escaped several magical catastrophes through sheer fortune rather than wisdom.

Isolation
: Her arrogant certainty and emotional distance have alienated many potential allies, leaving her dependent on a small circle of supporters.

Magical Overreach
: Her confidence in her abilities can lead her to attempt rituals whose consequences she has not fully considered, risking not only her life but potentially those around her.

## Social

### Patrons

Lord Bufomazul Charzilzát
: A Bizarian nobleman of mysterious motivations and connections who has funded Dókhehran's expeditions for the past three years. Their relationship is transactional but cordial; **Bufomazul** provides substantial resources—both gold and access to restricted locations—in exchange for copies of all significant discoveries and first right of refusal on any artifacts recovered. His interests and the source of his wealth remain obscure.

_Holmarendos_ (prelate) [[being-khezotontros|Kezoton Trosuskar]] of the [[affiliation-zefaris|Zefaris]] Zulen (fellowship)
: The elderly senior master of the Epistémion (the Academy of Knowledge) in Bizaris who mentored her in her early years and continues to defend her controversial theories against institutional pressure from more conservative factions. His support has been crucial to her institutional survival, though he increasingly urges her toward "more orthodox" lines of inquiry.

### Enemies

_Marendos_ (master) [[being-raghumonkreg|Raghumon Kregokhash]]
: A conservative member of the Zulen [[affiliation-fisera|Fisera]] whose theoretical work on the stability of ancient magical foundations Dókhehran publicly discredited in print five years ago, devastating his reputation and prospects. **Raghumon** has spent those years cultivating allies within the Epistémion's leadership, gathering what he claims is evidence of dangerous methodological flaws in her work and mounting a systematic campaign to discredit her scholarship. His vendetta has evolved from academic disagreement into something more personal and institutional.

The Conservatives of the Chapterhouse
: A faction within the Epistémion's leadership, drawn from several Zulethalar (fellowships) but dominated by the Zulen [[affiliation-sideris|Sideris]] (Metal) with its emphasis on order, binding, and control, views Dókhehran's research into hidden magical knowledge as dangerous and potentially heretical. They believe that certain knowledge was sealed away by the ancients for good reason, and they have begun to lobby for formal restrictions on her access to restricted texts.

### Affiliations

[[affiliation-panepistmm|The Academy]]
: Her official membership and primary institutional affiliation, though her relationship with the organization has grown increasingly complicated as her theories gain both devoted followers among younger scholars and vehement opponents among the conservative leadership. Her tenure is no longer secure, and she is acutely aware that powerful figures within the Epistémion now view her work as a threat to institutional stability and orthodox doctrine.

## Plot Hooks

The Closed Stacks
: The chapterhouse council of [[place-byzaris|Bizaris]] votes to restrict Dókhehran's access to its restricted texts, and the order takes effect at the end of the month. She needs one volume before then and asks a party to copy it while the stacks are still open to her. _Holmarendos_ [[being-khezotontros|Kezoton Trosuskar]] has asked her to wait for the council's leave, and the leave may never come.

The Mistranslation Charge
: Marendos [[being-raghumonkreg|Raghumon Kregokhash]] lays before the council a passage of her third treatise that he says she mistranslated, and he asks the chapterhouse to withdraw the work. Dókhehran needs an independent reading of the source tablet, which lies in a Helionite ruin, and asks a party to bring back a rubbing before the council meets. If the reading disagrees with hers, she must decide whether to publish the correction.

The Tablet and the Patron
: An expedition funded by Lord [[being-bufomazultch|Bufomazul Charzilzát]] brings back a Helionite tablet, and his first right of refusal on any artifact covers it. Kezoton wants it kept in the chapterhouse's library, where Raghumon's faction can also read it. Dókhehran asks the party to carry the tablet to one side or the other before the patron's agents arrive.

:::secret {title="Further hooks"}
Lord Bufomazul has not told Dókhehran that he wants her research to give him leverage over the [[affiliation-cncltwlvbzrs|Council of Twelve of Bizaris]]. Once she learns it, she can end the arrangement and lose the gold, or stay and keep the patron close.

In her deepest trances Dókhehran hears voices. The voices ask her to perform a rite in a sealed vault, and she asks a party to stand watch and break the trance if it goes wrong. Whether the voices are the past or her obsession is the referee's to decide.
:::
