# Dossier {#dossier}

Ralthvir is a skald from the far northern reaches of [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]], where he wandered from clan to clan, composing songs and telling tales of great heroes and battles. He is clanless, having been exiled for an unknown crime—though some whisper that he had an affair with a powerful lord’s wife, others say he was involved in a conspiracy against his clan. [[being-grosdrnrgd|Tvarninda]] met Ralthvir at a village blet, where the skald’s sharp wit and knowledge of ancient lore impressed her. Ralthvir’s songs often contain veiled warnings and prophecies, hinting at the deeper wisdom he possesses. When he is not on the road with Tvarninda, he winters at [[place-flarnbrekka|Flarnbrekka]], the silver camp whose cold hospitality he has made a theme of his verse.

## Psyche

### Personality

Cynical and secretive, Ralthvir is a skilled manipulator and strategist. He is not above bending the truth or playing both sides to his advantage, but his loyalty to [[being-grosdrnrgd|Tvarninda]] is sincere. Ralthvir sees the Seid as a kindred spirit, both outcasts who walk between worlds.

### Motivation

Ralthvir’s interest in [[being-grosdrnrgd|Tvarninda]] lies in the Seid’s connection to the spirit world, which fascinates him. He sees [[being-grosdrnrgd|Tvarninda]]’s journey as an opportunity to witness something truly extraordinary and to compose the greatest saga ever told.

## Plot Hooks

The Vein Verse
: Two parties at [[place-flarnbrekka|Flarnbrekka]] each claim to have found the same vein first, and the camp's council of elders asks Ralthvir, who is owed nothing by either, to sing how the vein was found. A verse that names one party settles the dispute and makes an enemy of the other. He can sing what he saw, sing what suits the elder who feeds him, or sing nothing and be called a coward by the camp that shelters him.

The Exile's Price
: A man of Ralthvir's old clan comes up the road before the thaw and says he knows what Ralthvir was exiled for. He offers to take the exile back if Ralthvir sings a certain verse before the clan's ting. Ralthvir can sing it and learn what the verse is for only after it is heard, or refuse and let the clan keep its own account of why he was cast out.

The Jarl's Last Tale
: A dying jarl of [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]] summons Ralthvir to record his life, with wealth and renown to follow. The deeds he tells include some that would break alliances between clans. Ralthvir can sing a flattering saga, sing the true one and risk the jarl's heirs, or leave the saga unfinished.

:::secret {title="What the Game Master knows"}
An old skald tells Ralthvir a captivating story and vanishes before the end. Ralthvir begins to hear its continuation in dreams and rumors. The unfinished tale holds a clue to Tvarninda's vision of the sea, and the person he heard it from knew it would be Ralthvir who carried it on.
:::
