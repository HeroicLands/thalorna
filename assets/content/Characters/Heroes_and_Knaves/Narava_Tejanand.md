# Appearance {#appearance}

![[nrvtjnnd|Narava Tejanand]]{float=top-left}

Narava is a man of 6'2" and heavy build, with golden brown skin, jet black hair, dark brown eyes and a full, expressive mouth. His forearms and hands are a map of small burns, white and pink against the brown, and his eyes are always a little red from the furnace. He wears a leather apron over a stained linen shirt and does not take it off to receive patrons.

# Dossier {#dossier}

## The Furnace Born

Narava was born to a merchant family of standing in [[place-vyalendra3|Vyalendra]], the first son of a spice house. At seven his father took him to the workshop of Master [Davalakar]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, the foremost glassworker of the city, and he stood watching a gather of molten glass become a bowl. At twelve he gave up his inheritance and was apprenticed. Five years passed before he was allowed to make anything of his own.

He inherited Davalakar's furnaces in his twenties. His reputation is secure, and his pieces fill the clerestory frames of temples and the cabinets of the loom households. He is thirty-seven, and he has spent more on his experiments than his commissions have repaid.

## The Workshop

Vyalendra's great halls are lit from clerestories, and the [Academy of the Shining Pattern]{.name data-type="affiliation"} of the [[affiliation-vyalendravada|Vyalendravada]], whose Achoryas designed those windows, buys research glass from him and gives him access to its laboratory. The temple of [[affiliation-jnanasura|Jnanasura]] at [[place-suryagarha|Suryagarha]] has commissioned its stained glass through him. High Priestess [[being-viduliyadan|Viduliya Danasur]] pays him a stipend. The merchant [Jabaloka]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of [House Chendra]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} buys for his own estates and tolerates the delays.

The creditor [Ashuk]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a moneylender who advanced the cost of a new furnace, has begun threatening to seize the workshop's stock. A rival glassworker, [Surendor]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, makes sound work at a lower price and has begun competing for the commissions Narava once took as his own.

## Psyche

### Personality

Narava is an artist before he is a tradesman. He can talk about refraction and the color of fire for an hour and has no interest in his patrons' deadlines. His frankness about their taste has lost him commissions. Underneath it is a fear that the piece he is trying to make, glass that holds the nature of light itself, is not possible.

### Motivation

He wants to make the one piece that shows what light is, and he wants his years of work to have been right.

### Strengths

The Glass
: He can work colors and forms that other masters call impossible, and he reads heat and viscosity by eye.

Materials
: His knowledge of mineral additives rivals an Achorya's, and he tests new compounds without waste.

Judgment of Form
: He sees a flaw in color or balance across a hall.

### Weaknesses

Debt
: He spends on materials without a plan to recover the cost, and his deliveries run late.

Perfectionism
: He cannot call a piece finished.

Ruined Eyes and Hands
: Years at the furnace have left him with burn scars and eyes that tire.

## Plot Hooks

The Seized Stock
: Ashuk gives Narava until the next new moon before he seizes the workshop's stock to cover the furnace. Jabaloka has offered to buy the debt at a price that would make Narava his glassworker for life. Narava asks the party to find out what Ashuk has been promised, because the moneylender's patience has changed.

The Clerestory
: The Achoryas of the Academy offer Narava the clerestories of a new great hall. It is the largest commission of his life and fixes his name, but their geometry requires panes of one size and a single color, and nothing like his work. He can take it and secure the workshop, or refuse and lose it.

The Rebel Apprentice
: A brilliant and difficult apprentice, [Devaksha]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, argues that glass should be cheap enough for the villages and has left to open a rival workshop with his master's compounds. Narava wants the compounds back and does not want the apprentice harmed. The party must find the new workshop before Ashuk does, because a debtor's stock includes the formulae.

The Broken Panes
: Several of Narava's early works in temples and great houses have cracked and fallen in the past season. It looks like sabotage. The houses that own them ask the party to find who is doing it before the commissions in hand are withdrawn.

:::secret {title="What the referee knows"}
Nobody is breaking the panes. Narava introduced a mineral additive in his early colored glass that crystallizes slowly and splits the pane after some years. He has known for a long time and has told no one, because it would end his name. The pieces he is making now use the same compound.
:::
