The last [[lore-garauu|Gar-Aû]] of [[affiliation-garzinelrelu|Gar-Zin'el'Rêlu]], who was judged at the Great Temple for blasphemy and a hidden famine ([[lore-failfloods|the Failing Floods]]), then bound, sealed living into his own tomb and given over to the curse ([[lore-judgfalseuq|the Judgment of the False Uqa'â]]). His name and his house's were struck from every wall, so that the records hold only the epithet, and the road to his tomb is death on sight. He reigned fourteen years, from 2365 to 2378 of the count (255–268 AF), and the king-list keeps those years under a struck name. The oldest servants of the necropolis still whisper what he was called.
