The last [[lore-garauu|Gar-Aû]] of [[affiliation-garzinelrelu|Gar-Zin'el'Rêlu]], who sealed the living into his tomb and was judged, bound and given over to the curse at the Great Temple. His name and his house's were struck from every wall, so that the records hold only the epithet, and the road to his tomb is death on sight. He reigned fourteen years, from 2365 to 2378 of the count (255–268 AF), and the king-list keeps those years under a struck name. The oldest servants of the necropolis still whisper what he was called.
