Agélara stands 5'7" tall with a light build. She has olive skin, dark brown hair, and brown eyes. Her features include a narrow face, an aquiline nose, a pointed chin, well-defined lips, and dark brows.

# Dossier {#dossier}

## Background

### A Merchant's Daughter

Agélara was born the youngest of five daughters to **Merchant-Prince [[being-selvazhikonz|Selvazhikon Zigakit]]**, a wealthy trader in the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]] whose fortune came from controlling spice routes. Her childhood was one of abundance and expectation—she was educated in languages, music, and the arts of hospitality, groomed to become a merchant's wife in an advantageous alliance. Her father had already begun negotiations with three different families when Agélara, at seventeen, politely but firmly declined all offers and requested instead to be sent to apprentice with Maestra [Melanthehran Vuzigazur]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, proprietor of the famous [Gilded Gryphon Inn]{.name data-type="place/structure"}.

### The Making of an Innkeeper

Her father's fury was spectacular but ultimately futile against Agélara's quiet determination. She spent five years working every position in Melanthehran's Inn—kitchen maid, server, scribe, manager. She learned how to cook fine meals and provide comfortable beds, and beyond that the philosophy underlying true hospitality: that an inn is a sanctuary, that the innkeeper holds a sacred trust to shelter the traveling soul. She discovered she had an almost supernatural gift for reading people—understanding what a merchant truly needed (information), what a soldier craved (human company), what a scholar sought (community). By age twenty-two, she had become Melanthehran's primary partner in running the establishment.

### Taking Flight

When her mentor decided to retire at sixty, Melanthehran offered Agélara the option to purchase the Inn at a price far below its worth, a gift disguised as business. With her own savings and loans from trusted merchants who recognized her worth, secured against the inn, Agélara purchased the Inn three years ago and has transformed it into the most sought-after inn in [[place-thalassos|Thálassos]]. The Gilded Gryphon has become more than a place to sleep: a meeting place, a source of gossip and information, a space where deals are brokered and secrets shared under the assumption of absolute discretion.

## Psyche

### Personality

Agélara possesses a warmth so unforced that it disarms even the most cynical travelers. She remembers names, preferences, and personal details with great accuracy—she knows which merchant prefers his wine chilled, which scholar takes her tea with honey, which nobleman prefers privacy to socializing. This isn't calculated kindness; she cares about the people who pass through her doors. She listens far more than she speaks, a quality that has made her an unofficial confessor to dozens of people seeking someone who will hear their troubles without judgment.

Her optimism is both her greatest charm and her most dangerous vulnerability. She believes in the inherent goodness of people and assumes that honest dealing and fairness are universal values. She speaks several languages for business, and because she delights in understanding different cultures and perspectives. Her humor is quick and playful, and she has an impressive talent for defusing tense situations with gentle wit or perfectly-timed laughter.

### Motivation

Agélara's driving passion is simple: she wants to create sanctuary. In a world where travel is dangerous and strangers are often threats, she creates a space where people can be safe, fed, heard, and valued. She is motivated not by profit (though her Inn is profitable) but by the deep satisfaction of seeing a weary traveler's face relax as they step through her doors, knowing they are cared for. Her secondary motivation is independence—she has deliberately chosen a life path that allows her autonomy rather than subservience to a husband's authority, and she fiercely protects this freedom even when it costs her profit or safety.

### Strengths

Hospitality and Social Grace
: She possesses an almost supernatural ability to make any guest feel welcomed, valued, and comfortable. People who stay at her Inn often report it as one of the most pleasant experiences of their travels.

Masterful Cooking and Beverage Craft
: Her culinary skills are impressive, and her knowledge of brewing, wine selection, and spice pairings allows her to create experiences that delight even the most discerning palates.

Memory and Attention to Detail
: She remembers names, preferences, and personal details with eerie accuracy, creating connections with guests that extend far beyond standard innkeeper-customer relationships.

Linguistic Ability
: She speaks four languages fluently, allowing her to communicate with travelers from across the known world and making her a bridge between peoples.

Emotional Intelligence
: She reads people with extraordinary accuracy, understanding their needs and desires often before they articulate them. This makes her an excellent mediator and confidante.

Financial Acumen and Record-Keeping
: Despite her people-focused nature, she is surprisingly skilled with the business and accounting aspects of running an establishment, maintaining detailed ledgers and watching her coin carefully.

### Weaknesses

Excessive Trust and Naiveté
: Her fundamental belief in human goodness makes her vulnerable to manipulation and deception. She has been financially taken advantage of multiple times by charming swindlers.

Difficulty with Hard Negotiations
: She struggles with business dealings that require aggressive negotiation or stating firm boundaries. She often accepts unfavorable terms rather than risk conflict with suppliers or tradesmen.

Emotional Vulnerability
: Her fellow-feeling, while her greatest strength, also makes her emotionally vulnerable. She absorbs others' pain and troubles, sometimes to the detriment of her own wellbeing.

Limited Combat or Physical Defense Ability
: She has no martial training and would be completely helpless in physical conflict. Her Inn's safety depends on maintaining good relationships with local authorities and avoiding conflict, not on her ability to defend herself.

Overconfidence in Her Own Judgment
: While her intuition about people is often accurate, she occasionally misreads situations, particularly those involving organized deception or malice, and then doubts herself excessively when proven wrong.

Lack of True Ambition
: She is content with modest success, which some potential allies view as lack of drive; she is unlikely to pursue aggressive expansion or take major risks for greater profit.

## Social

### Patrons

Her Mother, Lady [[being-tcharzilinaz|Charzilina Zigakit]]
: Though officially estranged from her father, Agélara maintains a close and loving relationship with her mother, who provides emotional support. Charzilina has also become one of Agélara's best sources of information about the social machinations of the wealthy.

Master Melanthehran Vuzigazur
: Her mentor and former owner, now retired, who still frequents the Gilded Gryphon to share wine with Agélara and offer wisdom on business and life matters.

Merchant-Lord [[being-kezhvalikosb|Kezhvalikos Bolalidek]]
: A worldly trader and collector of fine things who has become both a regular guest and a social ally, using his position to recommend the Gilded Gryphon to other wealthy travelers and defending Agélara's reputation when necessary.

### Enemies

Her Father, Merchant-Prince Selvazhikon Zigakit
: Though not actively hostile, Agélara's father views her choice as a personal rejection of his authority and the family's social position. He has pressured her to close the Inn and return to her "proper" role in the family business. The emotional distance between them causes Agélara real pain despite her outward success.

Unspoken Jealousy
: Some of the older innkeepers in the region resent her success and beauty, spreading rumors about her propriety and hinting that her popularity stems from something improper rather than true hospitality.

### Affiliations

[[affiliation-olvenia|Faith of Ólvenia]]
: Agélara keeps the faith of the prosperity goddess [[lore-olveniadty|Ólvenia]], and through its worshippers she maintains connections with female merchants, scholars, and influential women in the harbor towns of the League.

## Plot Hooks

A Guest the Shrine Shelters
: A woman takes a room at the Gilded Gryphon and tells Agélara that she is fleeing a dangerous marriage. Her husband is politically connected and has hired agents to find her. The priests of [[lore-pozerasdty|Pozeras]] shelter those who flee pursuit, but the agents are asking at every inn on the waterfront. Agélara can keep the guest in the inn or move her to the shrine and give up the watch over her.

The Altered Ledger
: A merchant who ran up large debts at the Gilded Gryphon disappears after his crimes are exposed, and the magistrate's men come to the inn to ask whether Agélara hid his money. Her ledgers should clear her, but entries have been altered to show payments she never took. She can open the ledgers to the magistrate and risk what else he finds in them, or learn who broke into her records before the hearing.

Full House in the Storm Month
: A storm shuts the harbor and the inn fills with the crews of the ships it holds. A shipmaster demands every room for his officers, and Agélara's regular guests, who recommend the inn to other wealthy travelers, say that they will leave if she gives theirs up. She can keep her regulars and refuse the shipmaster, or take the better-paying officers and lose the guests who recommend her to others.

Her Father's Offer
: Merchant-Prince [[being-selvazhikonz|Selvazhikon Zigakit]] arrives in Thálassos with an offer: he will buy the Gilded Gryphon for twice its worth and put a manager in it, if Agélara returns to his house. The money would repay the merchants' loans at once. Agélara can accept and lose the independence she chose, or refuse and face the pressure he has put on her to close the inn.

:::secret {title="What the referee knows"}
Charzilina helped Agélara buy the inn with money her husband does not know she sent. If Selvazhikon learns of it, he can call his wife's gift a theft from the family and press the magistrate to name the inn his property.
