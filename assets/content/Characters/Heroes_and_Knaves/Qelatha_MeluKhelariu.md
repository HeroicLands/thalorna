# Appearance {#appearance}

![[qelathmlkhlr|Qelatha Melu'Khelariu]]{float=top-left}

Qelatha is a tall, spare woman of 5'10" with dark brown skin, black hair bound back in a leather thong and black eyes in a long face with a pointed chin. She is quiet in a way that fills a room less than it empties it. A spider is tattooed on her shoulder, which her militia tunic covers. She wears the plain tunic and sandals of the militia on duty and a hunter's dull-dyed hide off it, and carries a well-made leather quiver, a whetstone and a map of the borderlands drawn by her own people.

# Dossier {#dossier}

## Background

### Born to the Hunt

Qelatha was born in the borderlands of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]], in a region where civilization gradually gives way to vast wilderness. Her people—the [Meikhathi clan]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}—were traditionally keepers of sacred hunting grounds and custodians of a careful balance between human use and ecological preservation of the lands. Her childhood was spent learning the arts of tracking, archery, and survival in environments where a single mistake could prove fatal. Her father, [[being-wahrthmykhth|Wahrethu Meikhathi]], was the clan's most respected tracker; her mother, [[being-isethu|Isethu]], was a healer and wise woman who taught Qelatha to read the spiritual significance of plants and animals. By the age of eight, Qelatha was hunting alongside the adults, and by thirteen, she had already established herself as the most naturally gifted archer in the clan.

### The Violation of the Lands

The serenity of Qelatha's world fractured when she was sixteen years old. Foreign merchants and mining and timber interests, backed by imperial military authority, began entering the sacred hunting grounds with intent to exploit them. Logging camps scarred the forests; mining operations poisoned streams; poachers hunted indiscriminately for profit rather than sustenance. The Meikhathi resisted, but they were relatively few in number and lacked military power comparable to what the empire could bring to bear. Qelatha lost her father in a confrontation with imperial soldiers who were protecting logging operations. Rather than seeing justice for his death, she watched as the empire claimed the lands and effectively dissolved the Meikhathi's traditional way of life within a generation.

### Current Circumstances

Now twenty-seven years old, Qelatha exists in a state of displacement. She works officially as a yeoman archer in the local militia, compensated well enough to survive, but her heart remains attached to the destroyed lands of her childhood. She spends much of her off-duty time tracking the poachers and illegal loggers who continue to exploit the borderlands, working to protect what remains of the natural world and to disrupt those who profit from its destruction. She is part of an informal network of individuals—hunters, rangers, concerned citizens—who oppose the continued exploitation of the region, though this network operates in the shadows of legality. She has made enemies of several merchant interests and has reason to believe the empire may be compiling evidence against her for her unauthorized vigilante activities.

## Psyche

### Personality

Qelatha is quiet and reserved, speaking only when she has something to say, and then with precision and directness. She is most comfortable in natural environments and becomes visibly tense in crowded settlements or formal social situations. She possesses a dry humor that catches people off guard, and those who take time to know her discover warmth and loyalty beneath her taciturn exterior. Her emotions run deep but are carefully controlled; she does not demonstrate vulnerability easily.

She is capable of great patience when tracking prey or enemies, yet also prone to sudden, fierce action when she perceives a threat to something she values. She is guided by a strong moral code rooted in her people's traditions: respect for life, reciprocal exchange rather than exploitation, and responsibility toward the natural world. This code sometimes puts her at odds with the laws and expectations of the settled world.

### Motivation

Qelatha is driven by a combination of grief, duty, and determination to preserve what remains of her people's way of life and the lands they traditionally protected. She seeks to protect the natural world from exploitation and to hold accountable those who profit from environmental destruction. At a deeper level, she is driven by a need to honor the memory of her father and to prove that the Meikhathi way of life—and her people's identity—have value even if the empire refuses to recognize that value. She carries a quiet fury at the injustices her people have endured, a fury that manifests not in reckless rage but in methodical, relentless opposition to those who exploit the land.

### Strengths

Masterful Archery
: Qelatha's skill with bow and arrow approaches the level of true mastery. She has great accuracy, a rapid rate of fire, and the ability to make shots that most archers would consider impossible.

Tracking and Survival Mastery
: Her ability to read signs in the natural world—tracks, disturbances in vegetation, changes in animal behavior—is second to none. She can survive for extended periods in wilderness with minimal resources.

Speed and Agility
: Her dexterity and agility allow her to move with grace and speed that belies her height. She is capable of executing acrobatic feats and moving through terrain with little wasted effort.

Stealth and Fieldcraft
: Her understanding of how to move undetected through various environments, use terrain and vegetation for concealment, and approach prey without alerting it, is comprehensive and practically instinctive.

Fletching Expertise
: She is skilled at crafting arrows as well as using them, and does so with great precision. She can create specialized arrows for specific purposes—broadheads, blunt heads, arrows with special properties.

### Weaknesses

Limited Melee Strength
: While capable with a blade at close range, Qelatha lacks the physical strength needed for sustained melee combat against heavier, more powerful opponents. She is vulnerable if forced into direct hand-to-hand confrontation with trained warriors.

Poor Urban Navigation
: She becomes tense and disoriented in dense settlements and crowded urban environments. Her advantages—stealth, knowledge of terrain, tracking—are significantly diminished in civilized spaces.

Emotional Volatility About Her People
: While normally controlled, Qelatha can become emotionally reactive when confronted with evidence of ongoing exploitation or injustice directed toward her people or their lands. This can lead to poor decisions.

Moral Rigidity
: Her strong ethical code, while admirable, sometimes prevents her from taking pragmatic actions that might serve her longer-term interests. She can be too uncompromising.

Difficulty Maintaining Relationships
: Her emotional reserve, her tendency to be secretive about her vigilante activities, and her deep attachment to the wilderness make it difficult for her to maintain close personal relationships.

## Social

### Patrons

Captain [[being-luyatgarraqu|Luyat Gar'Raku]]
: The militia commander who employs Qelatha and has developed real respect for her skills and character. Luyat is unaware of her vigilante activities and tacitly allows her considerable freedom in exchange for her skill and loyalty. He has subtly protected her from official scrutiny on several occasions.

[[affiliation-linwalgir|Lin'Walgir]]
: An informal network of individuals dedicated to protecting the borderlands and resisting exploitation. While not a formal patron, this network provides Qelatha with information, occasional resources, and companions who share her purpose.

### Enemies

Merchant-Lord [[being-lennithotenr|Lenni Thoten'Ralu]]
: A wealthy merchant whose logging and mining operations in the borderlands have destroyed significant portions of Qelatha's ancestral lands. Lenni has reason to suspect Qelatha has been sabotaging his operations and has hired mercenaries to track and eliminate her.

[[affiliation-gargezanu|Gar-Gezanu]]
: An imperial organization tasked with collecting taxes and enforcing the laws of trade. They view Qelatha's vigilante activity as theft (of harvested goods) and assault (against their authorized contractors), and they have compiled a dossier on her activities.

### Affiliations

Local Militia
: Her official employment, which provides her with legitimacy and access to resources, though it also constrains her ability to operate against imperial interests.

[[affiliation-linwalgir|Lin'Walgir]]
: An informal network of wilderness protectors and defenders of the land who work in coordination though without formal hierarchy or structure.

## Plot Hooks

The Merchant's Offer
: The merchant-lord Lenni Thoten'Ralu, whose logging and mining have eaten the Meikhathi grounds, offers Qelatha a handsome retainer and tells her he would rather have her with him than against him. If she refuses, hired swords start asking for her at the militia post. She asks the party to gather proof of what his camps have done to the streams, before the offer expires and the mercenaries arrive.

Roof-Beams for the Domes
: The granary-keepers buy timber to re-roof the granary domes, and Lenni's barges deliver it to the row. The beams come from the sacred ground her father died defending, and her captain posts her at the gate to guard the unloading. She can stand watch, or she can use the unloading to make the granary-keepers' clerks ask where it was felled, and either path costs her the militia post.

The Clerk's Price
: A survey-clerk who works the rolls of the granary bureau tells her he can name the officer who ordered the patrol that killed her father, and the price is a roll stolen from the bureau's strong-room. The clerk is frightened and half-drunk. He may be the best chance she has to learn who gave the order, or a man the Overseer's office wants her to approach.

The Closing Dossier
: The Exaction Service of Gar-Gezanu has assembled a file on the thefts and sabotage in the borderlands, and she has about two weeks before the arrest. She can surrender to a trial she will probably lose, flee and leave the lands unwatched, or give the party the means to discredit the file before it reaches the Halzi'a's court.

The Forbidden Remembrance
: The Meikhathi hold their yearly rite of remembrance at a site that now lies inside Lenni's lease, and he has forbidden it. Qelatha means to hold it anyway and asks the party to guard the participants. Lenni's guards are paid by the day, and so are the militia who are ordered to arrest anyone found on the lease.
