# Appearance {#appearance}

![[vrldmylhrfnsvld|Vrildmil Hrafansvald]]{float=top-left}

Vrildmil stands 6'0" tall with a medium build. He has light fair skin, brown hair, and gray eyes. His features include a rectangular face, a slightly upturned nose, a square jawline, a firm-set mouth, thick brows, and hollow cheeks.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Vrildmil was born in a rural settlement of the [[affiliation-kngdmvthgrd|Kingdom of Vithgard]], the son of [[being-dvrnvrhrfnsvld|Dvarnvir Hrafansvald]], a soldier who enlisted in Vithgard's levy when Vrildmil was only three years old. With his father away fighting in the border conflicts, Vrildmil was raised primarily by his mother on the family's small farm in the borderlands—a region prone to bandit raids and territorial disputes. He grew up knowing his father only through stories and the occasional brief visit between campaigns.

At fifteen, his family's lands were attacked by a raider band. Vrildmil's mother died defending the property, and his younger brothers were too small to help. By the time word reached [[being-dvrnvrhrfnsvld|Dvarnvir]], it was over. The experience crystallized something in the young man: a burning desire to become strong enough to protect others from similar suffering—and a quiet resentment toward the father who had not been there when it mattered most.

At eighteen, Vrildmil joined the local militia, impressing his commander almost immediately with his dedication and aptitude. **Captain [Bruldthann Stonefist]{.name data-type="being/npc"}**, a grizzled veteran of thirty years' service, recognized something in the earnest young warrior and took him under personal tutelage. In many ways, Bruldthann became the father figure Vrildmil had lacked growing up. For the past eleven years, Vrildmil has served with distinction, progressing from raw recruit to one of the militia's most capable fighters. He has become known for his quick thinking in chaotic situations, his willingness to volunteer for dangerous assignments, and an almost self-sacrificial approach to protecting the people he serves.

[[being-dvrnvrhrfnsvld|Dvarnvir]], meanwhile, returned from military service around the same time, settling in [[place-vithgard|Vithgard]] as a tentmaker. The two live in the same kingdom now but their relationship remains complicated—marked by unspoken guilt on Dvarnvir's part and unresolved anger on Vrildmil's. They see each other occasionally, and there is love beneath the tension, but neither man finds it easy to bridge the years of absence.

Recently promoted to the rank of sergeant in the levy that musters at the port of [[place-dvarnvik|Dvarnvík]], Vrildmil stands at a crossroads. **Captain Bruldthann** has suggested he could rise further, perhaps eventually assume leadership of the militia itself. Yet Vrildmil feels the weight of something larger stirring—hints that bandit activity is coordinated by unseen powers, suggestions that the relative peace of the region may be ending.

## Psyche

### Personality

Vrildmil is earnest and direct, with little interest in political maneuvering or social posturing. He speaks plainly and acts decisively. What he lacks in years of experience he compensates for with determination and an almost supernatural capacity to learn from each encounter. In conversation, he is respectful and humble, showing particular deference to experienced fighters who have wisdom to share.

There is an underlying idealism to Vrildmil that sometimes puts him at odds with the cynicism of older soldiers. He believes that order can be maintained, that justice serves a purpose, and that individual actions matter. This idealism has occasionally made him reckless—charging into situations that more experienced heads would have cautioned against—but it also makes him beloved by the common people he protects.

### Motivation

Vrildmil is motivated by a desire that no one else should suffer what his family endured—to create a world where families need not live in constant fear of violence while those who should protect them are elsewhere. He wants to be the kind of soldier and leader that **Captain Bruldthann** has shown is possible—someone who acts with both strength and integrity, who is present when it matters. There is also ambition beneath the idealism: a drive to rise in status, to gain the resources and authority to affect larger changes. And somewhere deeper, a need to prove to his father—and to himself—that he became something worth being proud of, despite everything.

### Strengths

Agility and Reflexes
: Vrildmil's physical reactions are among the fastest in the militia. He can exploit openings in combat that others cannot perceive quickly enough to use.

Melee Combat Mastery
: His skill with spear and shield is advanced for his age. He has trained obsessively under **Captain Bruldthann** and has extensive real combat experience.

Tactical Flexibility
: Though he lacks the long view of older commanders, Vrildmil adapts rapidly to changing battlefield conditions and thinks creatively about problem-solving.

Inspirational Presence
: Younger militia members naturally follow his lead, and common citizens respond positively to his straightforward manner and evident commitment to their protection.

Quick Learning
: Vrildmil absorbs lessons rapidly, both from training and from field experience. Each encounter teaches him something new, and he integrates those lessons quickly.

### Weaknesses

Inexperience
: At twenty-nine, Vrildmil has significant combat experience but lacks the decades of perspective that true mastery requires. There are situations he has never encountered and pitfalls he cannot anticipate.

Impulsiveness
: His earnestness sometimes becomes recklessness. He occasionally rushes into dangerous situations without fully considering alternatives or waiting for support.

Limited Strategic Perspective
: While tactically sound in immediate situations, Vrildmil sometimes misses the larger consequences of his actions. He can be manipulated by those with longer-term designs.

Naiveté about Politics
: Vrildmil tends to trust authority and has limited understanding of the political maneuvering that occurs at higher levels. He could be used as a pawn by ambitious nobles.

Emotional Investment
: His care for the people he protects sometimes clouds judgment. He may take risks to save individuals that endanger larger objectives.

## Social

### Patrons

Captain Bruldthann Stonefist
: Vrildmil's commanding officer and mentor. Bruldthann became the father figure Vrildmil lacked growing up, and his opinion matters more to Vrildmil than almost anyone else's.

The Bundi of the District
: The free farmers whose lands Vrildmil's levy protects recognize him as their primary defender. They speak for his promotions at the ting and provide support for the levy's operations.

The Village Elder [Dvarnselda Stonekeeper]{.name data-type="being/npc"}
: An influential elder who sees potential in Vrildmil and has been mentoring him in aspects of leadership beyond combat.

### Enemies

A Raider Band
: A particularly organized bandit gang that has proven difficult to eliminate. Their leader, [Skraldmil]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, has specifically marked Vrildmil, seeing him as an obstacle to their plans.

Corrupt Local Officials
: Some landvordar and collectors of the king's dues profit from allowing banditry to continue unchecked. Vrildmil's aggressive pursuit of raiders threatens their arrangements.

Ambitious Militia Rivals
: Some older militia members resent Vrildmil's rapid advancement and question whether his youth and idealism suit him for leadership.

Skraldthann's connections among nobility have occasionally proven useful, and Vrildmil's martial reputation lends a certain rugged credibility to the Hrafansvald name that his uncle quietly appreciates.

## Plot Hooks

Two Calls at Once
: Word comes the same night that the raider band is burning a farm under Vrildmil's protection and that a levy outpost near [[place-dvarnvik|Dvarnvík]] cannot hold its post. He has men for one. Whichever he leaves loses people, and the loss will be spoken of at the next ting.

The Collector's Ledger
: Vrildmil traces the raiders' route to farms whose dues a collector of the king's was late in reporting, and the collector's name appears beside every raid. The hersvald's own landvordar would be the ones to try him, and a sergeant who accuses an officer of the crown without proof loses his place. He can bring the ledger to Captain Bruldthann, take it to the ting himself, or watch the collector until he is sure.

Bruldthann's Chair
: Captain Bruldthann is hurt and thinks of retiring from the post he holds in the levy. Vrildmil and an older officer are the two men the hersvald will choose between, and the older man's friends have been reminding the hersvald that Vrildmil is young and quick to charge. His Father's Tent
: Dvarnvir Hrafansvald, now a tentmaker, sends word that he will pitch his tent beside the levy's camp at the autumn muster and wants to speak. Vrildmil has not forgiven him for being away when his mother died. He can meet him before the whole levy, or refuse and let the camp see it.

:::secret {title="What the referee knows"}
Vrildmil's mother did not die in a chance raid. Powerful interests wanted her family's land, and the raid on it was ordered. The raider band's leader Skraldmil knows who paid, and so does the collector whose name is in the ledger. Vrildmil can learn it and have to decide what to do with the man who paid, or let it stay buried in the hope that he keeps the levy's trust.
:::
