# Appearance {#appearance}

![[erythnslnds|Érython Ûselîndâs]]{float: top-left}

Érython stands 6'1" tall with a medium build. He has light fair skin, brown hair, and green eyes. His features include a heart-shaped face, a narrow nose, a narrow chin, expressive brows, soft cheeks. A distinguishing mark is a tattoo of a serpent on the back.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Érython was born in the [[affiliation-hlykngdmnrtm|Holy Kingdom of Nartûm]], in the transitional lands where forest gives way to settled agriculture and the devotion to Flórania runs deep in the soil itself. His childhood was spent in a small yeoman's holding, where his father taught him to hunt to put food on the table and his mother instilled in him the spiritual disciplines of the Aurèldían faith. At fifteen, he underwent the trials of the local militia, and his extraordinary natural gift for archery was immediately apparent. By his twenty-second year, he had risen to become the militia's foremost marksman and the personal archer-guard to **Lord Keldarion**, the aging noble who oversaw these lands.

For a decade, Érython has served in this capacity—maintaining peace, hunting threats, and honing his craft to near-perfection. The serpent tattoo on his back was earned five years ago when he undertook a sacred pilgrimage to the heart of the Verdant Sanctum, seeking wisdom and blessing from Flórania for a failed hunt that cost a family their livelihood. The serpent, he was taught, represents regeneration and the cyclical nature of life—a reminder that even failure contains the seeds of renewal.

## Psyche

### Personality

Érython is a man of discipline and quiet intensity. He speaks little, preferring action to words, and when he does speak, his observations tend to be direct and unadorned. He possesses a meditative quality, the result of hours spent in focus and waiting—crucial skills for an archer. Yet beneath his calm exterior lies a passionate nature; he cares deeply about justice and the protection of the innocent, and he experiences genuine anguish when his skills fail to prevent tragedy. He struggles with perfectionism and harbors a subtle arrogance regarding his abilities, though this ego is tempered by his spiritual faith and genuine humility about the role luck plays in any successful shot.

### Motivation

Érython's primary motivation is to perfect his craft and to use that mastery in service to his community and faith. He believes that his extraordinary skill is a gift from Flórania and carries with it a responsibility to protect those who cannot protect themselves. He seeks to prove himself worthy of the gift through constant improvement and through the ethical application of his abilities. Beyond this, he harbors a quiet ambition to transcend the limits of his current position—to perhaps establish a school for archers, or to undertake a greater pilgrimage that would deepen his spiritual understanding.

### Strengths

- **Master Archer**: Érython's skill with the bow is exceptional; his accuracy, distance, and rate of fire exceed those of nearly any bowman in the kingdom, and his understanding of wind, distance, and trajectory is intuitive.
- **Exceptional Perception**: His vision extends far beyond normal human capability, allowing him to perceive details at great distances and to notice subtle signs others would miss.
- **Emotional Calm Under Pressure**: Years of high-stakes hunting and militia duty have honed his ability to remain focused and controlled even in dangerous circumstances.
- **Survival Skills**: His knowledge of woodcraft, hunting, tracking, and wilderness navigation is comprehensive and practical.
- **Spiritual Discipline**: His training in the Aurèldían faith provides him with both mental fortitude and a philosophical framework for understanding his purpose.

### Weaknesses

- **Limited Close-Combat Ability**: Without his bow, Érython's martial prowess drops dramatically; he lacks the training and instinctive familiarity with melee weapons that would make him formidable in close quarters.
- **Over-Reliance on Equipment**: He is psychologically dependent on his bow; separated from it or forced to improvise, he experiences genuine distress.
- **Emotional Distance**: While his calm is an asset, it can manifest as emotional coldness; he struggles to form deep personal connections.
- **Perfectionism and Self-Criticism**: His high standards for himself can lead to harsh self-judgment and diminished confidence when he makes even minor mistakes.
- **Limited Social Grace**: His taciturn nature and preference for action over conversation make him awkward in court settings or complex social situations.

## Social

### Patrons

- **Lord Keldarion**: The aging nobleman who employs Érython values him not merely as a skilled guard but as a trusted counselor; their relationship has deepened over the decade of service into something approaching genuine friendship.
- **High Priestess Liríndâ of the Verdant Sanctum**: The spiritual leader who marked Érython with the serpent tattoo maintains contact with him, offering guidance and occasionally requesting that he undertake special missions.
- **Master Fletcher Aldunor**: The finest bowyer and fletcher in the region has become something of a mentor and friend, constantly pushing Érython to refine his technique.

### Enemies

- **Vraknar the Poacher**: A dangerous criminal operating in the borderlands who poaches sacred game and sells protected animals; Érython has pursued him repeatedly and views him as a direct affront to both law and faith.
- **Thaleris Swiftshot**: A rival archer from a neighboring kingdom who publicly challenged Érython's reputation three years ago; though Érython defeated him in a formal competition, Thaleris harbors bitter resentment and spreads rumors about luck and fairness.
- **The Crimson Cult**: A faction of heretics who have targeted the Verdant Sanctum and its priests; Érython's connection to High Priestess Liríndâ has made him a target for their agents.

### Affiliations

- **The Holy Militia of Nartum**: Érython maintains an active rank and deep involvement with the kingdom's primary military organization.
- **The Archers' Circle of Nartum**: A prestigious guild and social organization for skilled bowmen throughout the kingdom.
- **The Devotees of Flórania**: Though not a formal organization, Érython maintains a strong spiritual commitment to the faith and participates in seasonal rituals and celebrations.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Sacred Stag**: Hunters report sightings of a magnificent stag—enormous, ancient, and bearing strange markings—in the deep forest. Local legend claims it is a divine manifestation of Flórania herself. When Érython encounters it during a hunt, he finds himself unable to kill it, despite the easy shot. This begins a strange spiritual journey in which the stag appears again and again, as if trying to communicate something. The party must help Érython understand the meaning behind the encounters, leading them into contact with druidic powers and ancient magics.

2. **The Poisoned Arrow**: Érython is framed for an assassination attempt when his distinctive arrows are found at the scene of a crime—yet he did not fire them. Investigation reveals someone is craftily duplicating his arrows and implicating him in murders and attacks. The conspiracy leads deeper into the kingdom's politics than Érython could have imagined, and he must prove his innocence while uncovering who has gone to such lengths to destroy his reputation.

3. **The Verdant Sanctum Under Siege**: High Priestess Liríndâ sends an urgent message that the Verdant Sanctum is under attack by forces she cannot identify—possibly cultists, possibly dark magic. Érython must lead a rapid journey to aid the sanctuary, only to discover that the attack was a diversion for something far darker happening at the shrine's heart.

4. **The Archery Tournament**: A grand tournament is announced that will attract the finest archers from across multiple kingdoms. Érython is strongly encouraged to participate, but strange portents surround the event. The party discovers that the tournament is actually a cover for darker purposes—slave trading, assassination, or the identification of skilled warriors for conscription into a shadowy force.

5. **The Fallen Student**: A young apprentice archer Érython once trained seeks him out bearing terrible news—others who studied under Érython are being systematically hunted and killed. The common thread is their connection to him, and he must discover why he has become a target. This investigation could reveal that his past contains secrets he himself does not fully understand, or that a powerful enemy seeks to eliminate all his connections.
