# Appearance {#appearance}

![[arkhekrkss|Arkhea Kourkásios]]{float=top-left}

Arkhea stands 5'5" tall with a scant build. She has pale weathered and wrinkled skin, gray hair, and brown eyes. Her features include a long face, an aquiline nose, a pronounced chin, slightly pursed lips, thick dark brows. A distinguishing mark is a limp.

# Dossier {#dossier}

## Background

### A Woman of Property Lost

Arkhea was born into the merchant class of the [[affiliation-byzarianlg|Bizarian League]], daughter to a moderately successful fabric trader. Her early life held promise—education, musical training, the prospect of an advantageous marriage. That life ended abruptly when her father invested heavily in a trading venture that was destroyed by pirates. The family's social collapse was swift and thorough. Her mother, unable to survive the loss of status, withdrew into illness. Her father took his own life. By twenty-five, Arkhea found herself without protection, resources, or a place in the world that once claimed to honor her.

### The Long Descent

The decades that followed were a gradual erosion of circumstance and dignity. First came the loss of housing, then employment became impossible—no merchant house would hire someone of her ruined status. The streets of the Bizarian League became her home. She learned the careful economics of survival: which bakeries discarded day-old bread in which alleys, which merchant wives might spare copper coins out of lingering conscience, which shelter houses might provide warmth on the coldest nights. She watched friends disappear into illness or despair. She survived through a combination of ruthless pragmatism and what some might call grace.

### Present Circumstances

Now in her seventh decade, Arkhea has achieved an almost monastic acceptance of her condition. She no longer rails against fate or harbors dreams of restoration. Instead, she has become something unexpected: a keeper of stories, a listening ear, a node in the network of the invisible poor. She knows secrets about the town that would shock the noble houses—things she has overheard while sitting unnoticed in doorways, things confessed to her by others living in the margins. This knowledge, though she would never weaponize it, grants her a peculiar form of power and protection.

## Psyche

### Personality

Arkhea possesses a weary wisdom that comes only from surviving long enough to see cycles repeat. She speaks little, having learned that silence attracts less attention and consumes less energy than conversation. Yet when she does speak, her words carry unusual weight—the perspective of someone who has lost everything and found unexpected treasures in the ruins.

Her humor is dark and subtle, finding ironic amusement in the cruelties and ironies of fate. She nurses no illusions about human nature but holds no general bitterness either—people are what they are, kind or cruel, and survival requires understanding both equally. Those who encounter her often remark on an unexpected peace in her presence, as though her acceptance of loss somehow frees others from their own anxiety about falling into similar circumstances.

### Motivation

Arkhea no longer operates from personal ambition; that luxury departed decades ago. Instead, she moves through the world driven by what might be called compassion, though she would disavow the term as too grand. She helps other street-dwellers because she understands their suffering intimately. She protects younger beggars from exploitation because she remembers being young and unguarded. She listens to the confessions of those in despair because she understands that sometimes being heard is the only remaining mercy. There is nothing aspirational in her motivation—she seeks no redemption, no restoration to lost status. She simply continues, surviving, witnessing, and offering small kindnesses that cost her but often mean everything to those who receive them.

### Strengths

Masterful Reader of Souls
: Arkhea's decades on the streets have made her extraordinarily perceptive about human intention, motive, and character. She can discern truth from lies with uncanny accuracy.

Resourceful Beyond Measure
: She understands how to survive on nothing, how to acquire necessities through non-monetary means, how to navigate bureaucratic systems designed to exclude people like her. Her Guile and Intrigue are refined to necessary art forms.

Musical Talent
: Despite everything, Arkhea maintains her training in music. Her voice, though aged, still carries unexpected beauty. Singing occasionally provides small income and more importantly, unexpected moments of transcendence.

Brewing and Culinary Expertise
: From necessity and instinct, she has become skilled at creating remedies, herbal concoctions, and edible preparations from foraged materials and cast-off ingredients.

Network Among the Invisible
: Arkhea maintains connections throughout the town's beggar community, merchants' servants, and dock workers. She knows things that never reach the ears of the powerful.

### Weaknesses

Physical Fragility
: Her body, weakened by decades of malnutrition and harsh living, fails her with increasing frequency. A simple cold can become life-threatening. The limp is constant pain.

Prone to Illness
: Immune system compromised, dental health deteriorated, and chronic conditions from long-term poverty make her susceptible to seasonal sicknesses and slower to recover.

Limited Physical Capability
: She cannot fight, cannot run, cannot perform strenuous labor. Any confrontation relying on physical strength is lost.

Social Invisibility as Weakness
: While she is invisible to the powerful, this invisibility also means she has no formal protections, no authorities to appeal to if she is wronged. She is vulnerable.

Accumulated Trauma
: The weight of sixty-two years, half spent in poverty, has left her prone to melancholy and difficulty with hope. She sometimes retreats into resignation that borders on suicidal thinking.

## Social

: The abbess of a local convent dedicated to fertility, growth, and mercy periodically provides shelter and food to Arkhea. They share a spiritual understanding despite their vastly different circumstances.

Young Scholar [Dmitris]{.name data-type="being/npc"}
: A university student who trades philosophical conversation and occasional bread for Arkhea's stories and hard-won wisdom about the human condition.

[Zerarzan]{.name data-type="being/npc"}

### Affiliations

The [[affiliation-lizelas|Order of Lizelas]] (Fertility Aspect)

## Plot Hooks

Protection Money Owed
: The Brass Knuckles, a crew of thugs led by the enforcer Rahvárion, run extortion against the beggars of [[place-karvashion2|Karvashion]] and have beaten Arkhea before for protection money she could not pay. They set a date for the next payment, and the street-dwellers look to her for what to do.

Arrested for Vagrancy
: The official Zerarzan, who views beggars as urban blight, has Arkhea arrested for vagrancy again, and each arrest has been harsher than the last. Mother Superior Hezogeze Sogipidek of the House of Lizelas can shelter her for a time, but each arrest makes it harder for Arkhea to go back to the corners she has kept.

Fever in the Beggars' Market
: A fever with delirium spreads through the beggar community. Arkhea knows herbs and the invisible network that carries news, and she must nurse the sick without drawing the attention of authorities who would blame the victims.

An Infant on the Doorstep
: Arkhea finds an abandoned infant on a doorstep, marked in a way she has not seen before. Keeping the child means leaving the corners she has held for decades, and handing it over means giving it to a town that would exploit it.

:::secret {title="Further hooks"}
The Overheard Plot
: Sitting unnoticed in a merchant house vestibule, Arkhea overhears a plot to poison a town official. No one would believe a beggar could have such evidence, and she must warn the target without handing the plot to those who would use it.

The Apprentice Who Saw a Murder
: [Shahrikos]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a young merchant's apprentice, confides in Arkhea that he saw his master murder a rival. He is paralyzed by fear, and Arkhea must decide whether to help expose the crime or help the boy escape with his conscience intact.

A Letter in Her Mother's Hand
: A well-dressed stranger brings Arkhea a letter in her dead mother's hand, dated decades ago, saying her fall into poverty was arranged. It offers a chance of restoration if she will seek it.
:::
