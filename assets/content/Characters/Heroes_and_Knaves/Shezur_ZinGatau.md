# Appearance {#appearance}

![[shezurzingat|Shezur Zin'Gatau]]{float=top-left}

Shezur is a big man, 6'3" and heavy in the shoulder and chest, with warm brown skin, very dark brown hair and amber eyes that go still when he is judging a weld. His mouth is full and rarely moves. A serpent is tattooed across his back, which the heat of the forge shows more often than the street does. He wears a leather apron and a tool belt over a bare working tunic, and his forearms carry the old burn-scars of thirty years at the fire. He keeps hammer, tongs, chisel and an anvil stand in the forge and a stock of iron and steel ingots against the back wall.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Shezur was born to the [Zin'Gatau clan]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"} during the flooding season, third son of a metalworker's family. His father, [[being-zabmehtzingt|Zabmeht Zin'Gatau]], recognized early that young Shezur possessed the strength and focus required for the finest crafts. Rather than compete for inheritance, Shezur was apprenticed at age twelve to Master [[being-thaharqo2|Thaharqo]], keeper of the Imperial forge in the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]]'s merchant quarter. For twenty-three years, he honed his art under Thaharqo's exacting eye, learning to shape metal and to understand its voice—when to strike, when to let fire breathe, when to cool with deliberate patience.

Upon his master's retirement, Shezur inherited the forge and established himself as the foremost supplier of functional weaponry to the Empire's military apparatus. His reputation spread quickly: soldiers entrusted their lives to Zin'Gatau blades and armor, knowing every piece had been tested and refined beyond simple adequacy. He married late at thirty-five, gaining two stepchildren from his union with [[being-megara|Megara]], a merchant's widow, though she passed seven years ago. Without heirs of his own blood, Shezur has begun mentoring promising apprentices, though he guards his knowledge jealously.

Today, Shezur maintains his forge with obsessive dedication. Every dawn, before the city stirs, he stokes the fires that have burned continuously for nearly three decades. His workshop has become an institution—mercenary captains route themselves through [[place-aukhelathrgq|Au'Khelathu]] specifically to commission pieces from his anvil.

## Psyche

### Personality

Shezur is a man of few words but absolute conviction. He measures success not in gold or prestige, but in function—a weapon must cut, armor must protect, and anything beyond utility is mere decoration distracting from purpose. This philosophy makes him prized by warriors and infuriating to aesthetes. He respects dedication and honest labor above all else; a person who works hard and without pretense will find in him a loyal ally, while idleness or deception earns only his contempt.

His gruff exterior covers a deep spirituality. He observes the rites of [[affiliation-thubai|Thuba'i]] with great care, believing that the prosperity god guides his hands in the shaping of metal. He keeps a small altar in the forge's corner, making daily offerings of oil and bread. To Shezur, his craft is not separate from his faith—they are inseparable aspects of a sacred covenant with his deity.

### Motivation

Shezur seeks to leave a legacy of excellence that will outlast his mortal years. With no biological heirs, he is driven to see that the standards he has established persist through those he trains and the weapons that bear his name. He also harbors a quiet determination to prove that functionality and durability are higher virtues than ornamentation—a philosophy born from both principle and wounded pride from years of being overshadowed by flashier craftsmen.

### Strengths

Masterful Metalcraft
: Shezur's command of the forge has no equal within a hundred leagues. His weapons and armor are known for their durability and perfect balance, often surpassing military specifications.

Unyielding Work Ethic
: He labors from before dawn until long past sunset, maintaining standards that lesser craftspeople cannot sustain. His reliability is absolute—commissions are always completed on schedule.

Tactical Insight
: Despite his humble profession, Shezur possesses deep understanding of how weapons will be used in combat. He designs pieces with pragmatic knowledge of warfare, making them surprisingly effective.

Ritualistic Precision
: His spiritual devotion translates into close attention to detail. Every hammer strike, every fold in the metal, every fitted joint receives the care of sacred work.

Merchant Relations
: Decades of dealing with military quartermaster and wealthy patrons have taught him shrewd negotiation skills and the ability to manage complex commissions.

### Weaknesses

Disdain for Artistry
: Shezur's dismissal of decorative elements alienates potential patrons and fellow craftspeople. He refuses commissions that include inlay, filigree, or embellishment, limiting his clientele.

Rigidity in Design
: His insistence on proven, functional forms leaves him vulnerable to competition from those with new ideas. He struggles to adapt to new battlefield tactics that might require novel weapon designs.

Poor Teaching Patience
: While he understands his craft deeply, explaining it to apprentices exhausts him. He grows frustrated with slow learners and has driven away several promising students.

Social Awkwardness
: Small talk is foreign to Shezur, and his directness often reads as rudeness. He has few friends outside his trade and no social standing beyond his guild.

Aging Body
: Decades of physical labor are exacting their toll. His strength, while still impressive, is waning, and he experiences chronic pain in his shoulders and back.

: Commander of the [Au'Khelathu Imperial Guard]{.name data-type="affiliation"}, who has purchased from Shezur for seventeen years. Trusts no other source for his cohort's weapons and considers Zin'Gatau blades worth their weight in gold.

Captain [[being-tharaset|Tharaset]]

Lord Merchant [[being-zekhenti2|Zekhenti]]

[The Bridge District Militia]{.name data-type="affiliation"}
: The informal neighborhood watch that maintains peace in Au'Khelathu's merchant quarters. They scrape together funds to purchase Shezur's work whenever possible.

### Enemies

: A rival craftsman who specializes in ornate, jeweled weapons intended for nobility. Khelamose publicly mocks Shezur's "crude militarism" and has twice attempted to undercut his prices at military auctions.

[[affiliation-linmlnqnwglz|Lin'Melnu-Qenuwa elu Galezkara]]
: A merchant organization representing aesthetic craftspeople across the Empire. They view Shezur as an enemy of true artistry and have lobbied to exclude his work from noble exhibition halls.

Priest [[being-aqenmose2|Aqenmose]]
: A mid-ranking cleric of the [[affiliation-psaqaru|Faith of Saq'aru]] (devoted to [[lore-psaqarudty|Saq'aru]], god of craftsmanship and architecture) who resents that Shezur worships Thuba'i. Aqenmose has suggested that Shezur's "heretical practices" compromise his quality and spreads this falsehood among religious merchants.

### Affiliations

[[affiliation-linmelnkhlth|Lin'Melnu elu Au'Khelathu]]
: Though Shezur maintains independence and refuses guild leadership positions, he remains formally registered and pays his annual dues. The guild recognizes him as the highest-ranked active craftsperson.

[Imperial Military Quartermaster's Office]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"}
: While not an official affiliation, Shezur maintains standing contracts with the Empire's military procurement division, granting him priority access to the finest raw materials in imperial warehouses.

## Plot Hooks

The Whispering Blade
: A young soldier asks Shezur for a replacement for the sword he lost in a skirmish, and says the old blade whispered to him in dreams and guided his arm. Working the new steel, Shezur has begun to see the same battlefield in his sleep. The old blade was forged by a smith whose name the guild struck from its roll, and the soldier's company will not say who sold it to him.

The Arsenal in Secret
: A cloaked figure pays in rare coin and exotic metals for an arsenal to be delivered in secret to a place Shezur is told not to know. The Halzi'a of Anlagh-Zetun controls the bread and opens the granaries on his word alone, and factions court him for the succession. Shezur has never made a weapon for anyone but a lawful buyer, and the fee is more than the imperial guard spends in a year.

The Pretender at the Next Forge
: A young smith opens a forge in the lower town, calls herself [[being-meshlen|Meshlen]] the Talented, says she trained under Shezur and sells at half his price. He has never taken a pupil by that name. When the militia discovers that several of her blades are failing in the field, the complaints reach him first.

The Heir to the Forge
: The metalworkers' guild asks him to name his successor. His most promising apprentice has been learning the decorating arts at Master Khelamose's workshop, in secret. Shezur sees it as a betrayal of everything the forge stands for, and the guild sets a date that does not wait for him to forgive it.

:::secret {title="What the Game Master knows"}
The cloaked buyer is a steward of the faction that wants Anlaghur, the Gar-Au's nephew, on the throne, and the coin is minted from the same ingots as the imperial treasury's gift-coinage. The arsenal is meant to hold the granary row on the day the Halzi'a is asked to open a dome.

Khelamose's workshop is the one that sold the whispering blade to the soldier. It is cast from a smith's discarded work, and the dreams are what the maker's grief left in it.
:::
