# Appearance {#appearance}

Meqes XVI is a 46-year-old woman who stands 5'7" tall with a light build. She has dark brown skin, clear complexion, dark brown hair, and brown eyes.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Meqes XVI is the reigning Gar-Au of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]] and the sixteenth of her house, [[affiliation-zumeku|Zu'Meku]], to reign as Meqes in the line of the [[lore-garauu|Gar-Auu]]. She rules from the sacred island at [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]], and every tax roll and contract in the realm is dated by her years. Nobody believes she is a god, everyone knows nobody believes it, and she keeps the fiction because the river has kept its side of it. Her nephew [[being-anlaghur|Anlaghur]] stands near enough the succession that the court asks the question.

## Background

**Formation.** She is the daughter of [Meqes XV]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, who held the throne for forty-five years. She grew up among the scribes of the [[affiliation-rethsaar|Faith of Reth'Sa'ar]], and she learned the render of every selat before she learned a prayer.

**The throne.** She was crowned in 709 AF at thirty-five. She trusts astrologers further than her councilors do, listens to [[being-amqltzlmlglq|Amqelet-Zelemu Legulu’akun]] on what the stars allow, and leans on the auguries of [[being-anlaghafu|Anlaghafu]], high priest of the [[affiliation-qearet|Faith of Qe'aret]], against the faction leaders who press her to act.

**Now.** [[place-themeasure|The Measure]] has read short at two Inundations, and the reckonings from the selatu run smaller than the flood explains. She has answered no Halzi'a in writing.

## Psyche

### Personality

She is practical, exact and patient, and prefers a late answer to a wrong one.

### Motivation

She wants a good flood and a settled succession, in that order: the river high enough to let the fiction rest, and an heir named before a quarrel over her chair becomes a quarrel over her reign.

### Strengths

She knows the render of every selat and reads a short crop in a late barge.

### Weaknesses

She weighs omens too heavily, and the factions have learned to arrange the omens she reads.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The unanswered render.** A Halzi'a has sent coin where grain was due. She asks a party outside the bureaus to learn which selat's reckoning is wrong.
2. **The night before the reading.** The priests of the [[affiliation-uqaa|Faith of Uqa'a]] will proclaim the Measure's height at dawn. She asks a party to stand at the gauge through the night and report what they see.
3. **The nephew's table.** Anlaghur invites a party to dine, and she asks them to tell her who else is seated.

:::secret {title="The household treasure"}
Meqes has drawn on the throne's own household treasure to buy small lots of Bethuan grain from the Trade-Houses' private stores through the mediators of [[affiliation-genzetpalu|Genzet'Palu]], well short of the large sale that waits on the Metrissa's choice, and has told the bureaus nothing. She has set a date by which she will announce a failed flood or confess the purchases, and the date is close.

**Hook: the empty strongroom.** A household clerk shows a party a ledger with the strongroom short of what the rolls show and asks them to find the thief.
:::
