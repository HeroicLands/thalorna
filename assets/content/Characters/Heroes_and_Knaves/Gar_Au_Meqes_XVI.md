# Dossier {#dossier}

Gar-Aû Meqes XVI reigns from [[place-galezkara|Galezkara]], the sixteenth to bear the name on the throne. The regnal count alone marks a lineage long enough that the number has become part of the title. Every tax roll and contract in the realm carries this reign's year, whether or not the signer has ever seen the throne. She trusts the warnings of astrologers further than her councillors do, and leans on the judicial auguries of [[being-anlagherhafu|Anlagherhafu]], high priest of [[lore-qearetdty|Qe'âret]], as a counterweight to the more aggressive faction leaders at court. That reliance is why his judgement is so hard to move against.
