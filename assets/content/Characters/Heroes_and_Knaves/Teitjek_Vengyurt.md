**"Iron Spear Piercing the Clouds"**

> **Draft.** This note exists so that the general the
> [[lore-ttjkvngyrt|Iron Spear Piercing the Clouds]] threat describes has a
> being to point at. His statistics, his caste standing and his affiliations are
> not yet written.

Tëitjëk Vëngyürt was a respected general of the [[lore-vushok|Vüshōk]] caste
until he defied a direct imperial order during a critical campaign. The defiance
won the campaign, which made it worse rather than better: a victory won by
disobedience indicts the order, and the celestial administration cannot allow
that to stand.
