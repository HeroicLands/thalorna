# Appearance {#appearance}

![[drmpldvmkt|Darmapalda Vimuktan]]{float=top-left}

Darmapalda stands 6'1" tall with a medium build. He has warm golden brown skin, dark black hair, and dark brown eyes. His features include an oval face, a broad nose, a narrow chin, an expressive mouth, warm golden brows, and high cheeks. He has a tattoo of a flower on his ankle.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Darmapalda emerged from the lower stations of the town below the bow-fort at [[place-danurkot|Danurkot]], where survival and advancement required more than talent—it required ruthlessness, adaptability, and an ability to read human intention faster than others could act. His early years remain obscured by deliberate obscurity, though rumors suggest he was orphaned young and entered service to a Shroshtin known for employing street-born fighters. A Karmija bears arms only as a levy or by an academy's grant, and he carries the grant of one of [[affiliation-dnrktjnpd|Danurkot's academies]], which take students of any station. By his early twenties, Darmapalda had distinguished himself as a bodyguard, accumulating a reputation for keeping his employers alive through situations that should have proven fatal.

The flower tattoo appeared around the time he entered the bodyguards' shrena proper, marking either a binding oath or a commemoration of personal loss—Darmapalda offers no explanation. Over the next decade, he refined himself from street-fighter to bodyguard of the first rank, developing the foresight and eye for threats that set him apart from merely competent soldiers. He studied the ways of the kingdoms and the cities, [[place-vyalendra3|Vyalendra]] most of all, learning their hierarchies and vulnerabilities, and began taking commissions that demanded not brute force but insight into people and inventive thinking.

Now he operates from the margins of [[lore-vedyariclt|Vedyaran]] society, available to those wealthy and discreet enough to seek him through proper channels. Though nominally affiliated with the bodyguards' shrena, Darmapalda maintains fierce independence and careful separation from entanglements with other shrena members or formal power structures.

## Psyche

### Personality

Darmapalda is economical with trust and conversation. Those who encounter him at his work often mistake his silence for coldness or dim-wittedness—a misreading that has occasionally cost his would-be attackers their lives. In truth, he takes in the world through constant evaluation and tactical weighing, a mental orientation that leaves little room for casual pleasantry. When he does speak, his words carry weight and precision; he has learned that brevity itself can be a form of power.

Yet the austere exterior covers an unexpected capacity for dark humor and moments of real warmth with those few individuals who have earned his regard. He maintains a stoic philosophy that borders on fatalism, viewing death as an inevitable eventuality that neither requires dwelling upon nor permits excessive caution. This makes him at once careful (avoiding unnecessary risks) and bold (willing to take calculated dangers that more fearful men would flee). He is driven by a code of honor that has nothing to do with conventional morality and everything to do with loyalty to those who have fairly hired him and respect for enemies who prove worthy.

### Motivation

Darmapalda is motivated by the pursuit of mastery—the relentless refinement of his abilities toward an undefined perfection. He accepts commissions for the challenge they represent as much as for the income, seeking situations that will test his capabilities and force him to evolve. A deeper motivation underlies this: a hunger for significance and permanence in a world that seems determined to render his existence transient and forgettable. Each successful protection of a client, each threat neutralized before it matures into danger, represents a small defiance against irrelevance. The flower tattoo suggests something else—perhaps a love lost, a vow unfulfilled, or a spiritual commitment—but Darmapalda guards this motivation carefully, allowing it to surface only in unguarded moments.

### Strengths

Agility
: His movement is poetry rendered into flesh—he can scale structures other warriors would consider impassable, contort through impossible spaces, and achieve acrobatic feats that seem to violate natural laws.

Threat Perception
: His perception and awareness are honed to supernatural sensitivity, capable of detecting threats hidden in crowd sounds, fleeting expressions, and small changes in the surroundings that others would miss entirely.

Tactical Mastery
: His reasoning and planning are cold and precise, allowing him to calculate approaches to protection problems with mathematical certainty and adapt instantly to changing circumstances.

Melee Perfection
: His dagger and short sword work demonstrates thousands of hours of drilling and refinement, resulting in a style that is minimalist, economical, and devastatingly effective.

Psychological Resilience
: He maintains emotional equilibrium under stress that would shatter weaker minds, allowing him to function effectively in situations of extreme danger and uncertainty.

### Weaknesses

Emotional Isolation
: His distance from human connection leaves him vulnerable to manipulation by those who can offer real warmth or false kinship, and makes him unpredictable when emotions do surface.

Analytical Paralysis
: His tendency toward exhaustive threat-analysis can result in inaction or overly conservative approaches when situations demand swift, bold action.

Limited Physical Strength
: While graceful and capable, he lacks the sheer muscle power of larger warriors, making direct confrontations against multiple heavy-armored opponents tactically disadvantageous.

Mercenary Amorality
: His willingness to work for any sufficiently wealthy patron regardless of moral dimension has alienated potential allies and created dangerous enemies among the righteously inclined.

Dangerous Unpredictability
: His stoic exterior masks internal pressure that occasionally erupts in disproportionate violence, particularly when encountering situations that evoke the unspoken wound he carries.

## Social

### Patrons

Shroshtin [Ashera]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of eastern Vedyara
: A widow and trader in luxury goods who regularly contracts Darmapalda's services and has developed a real (though carefully unspoken) affection for him.

Shroshtin [Kavendra the Banker]{.name data-type="being/npc"}
: An elderly financier who values Darmapalda's discretion and tactical thinking as much as his combat abilities, and has kept him on quasi-retainer for sensitive commissions.

Master Surgeon [Devak]{.name data-type="being/npc"}
: A healer of some renown who once treated Darmapalda for a near-fatal wound and has since become one of the few individuals he considers a friend, and one of the fewer still who call him Darmu.

### Enemies

[[being-nagaritgaj|Nagarit]], Commander of [[affiliation-vyalendra2|Vyalendra's Watch]]
: A corrupt official who views Darmapalda's independence from his authority as insult and has made his removal a personal crusade, generating false charges and attempting to cut off his legitimate work.

[[affiliation-gargezru|Gar-Gezru]]
: A criminal organization that attempted to hire Darmapalda for unforgivable purposes; when he refused and reported them to the Watch in Vyalendra, they marked him for death and have pursued this vendetta with cold determination.

[Kanduta the Knife]{.name data-type="being/npc"}
: A rival bodyguard and occasional shrena-mate who views Darmapalda as having stolen commissions that should have been his, and who harbors deep resentment over a past failure to protect a client where Darmapalda succeeded.

### Affiliations

The bodyguards' shrena
: Formally affiliated but deliberately maintaining distance from organizational involvement and factional disputes.

## Plot Hooks

A Family Dying by Slow Poison
: A merchant family that has retained Darmapalda for three years begins to die, first servants, then junior members, then its head, by poisons that leave no trace. The family's own suspicion makes his protection almost impossible, and a rumor says he is the poisoner.

A Daughter Held across the Border
: A woman of a lesser great house of [[place-chendrapur2|The Moon City]] asks Darmapalda to recover her young daughter, kidnapped by rivals in a feud among the houses and held in a fortress across the border. The family cannot go to the authorities without endangering the child.

False Charges from Vyalendra's Watch
: Nagarit, Commander of Vyalendra's Watch, who treats Darmapalda's independence as an insult, has false charges drawn up and cuts off his legitimate work. Darmapalda must clear his name without the shrena's help or the Watch's goodwill.

:::secret {title="Further hooks"}
The Flower's Mark
: A woman who bears a mark identical to the flower tattoo on Darmapalda's ankle claims it ties them to a vow he made years ago and has never acknowledged. She says someone is killing everyone who bears it, and accepting her help means facing the past he has suppressed.

The Commander's Bargain
: Nagarit comes to Darmapalda and admits that the Crimson Syndicate has him in its power and forces him to pursue the false charges, and he offers to clear Darmapalda's name if Darmapalda helps him escape. It may be a trap, but his desperation looks real.
:::
