# Appearance {#appearance}

![[chndrkrtsndrjyvl|Chandrakīrtisundarī Jayavalli]]{float: top-left}

Whoever is looking gets the face they came for. Under it: dark brown skin, warm brown eyes, dark black hair, a diamond-shaped face with a prominent nose, a narrow chin, an expressive mouth, rounded cheeks and dark brows; 5'6" and of medium build.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Pushti Jayavalli**, a Vedyaran courtesan, kept her quarters in the Port District of Vedyara and, when she could, her daughter in them. The father was a Byzarian merchant who came once and did not come again. What Chandrakīrtisundarī learned before she was ten, between her mother's rooms and the streets, was that beauty was something to sell, that what people knew was money, and that anyone not watching the door would be hurt by whoever came through it.

At fourteen she went to the **Twilight House**, one of the first establishments of Vedyara, on a loose arrangement. Her looks and her quickness were worth having, and the house soon found another use for the quickness: she listened to what its clients said in their cups and their beds, carried it to nobles and merchant lords, and carried messages between powerful people who did not want to be seen together. At sixteen the **[[affiliation-adanasreni|Ādānaśreṇī]]** recruited her, quietly, through the house, on the understanding that a woman who gathered secrets and a guild that traded in them would find their interests overlapping. Often they have.

Since then she has walked a narrowing line: the house's clients, the guild's errands, and enough of a life kept back that she could survive if either turned on her. She reads people better than almost anyone in the city, and gets what she wants out of them without their noticing. Each year the errands are more dangerous, the compromises worse, and the day when one side finds out about the other closer.

## Psyche

### Personality

She has built so many selves that she is no longer sure which, if any, is the original. To a client she is eager, warm, wholly there. To a guild contact she is cool and exact and interested only in the task. In the few hours that are her own a third woman appears — clever, haunted, turning over the distance between who she meant to be and who she has had to become.

Her resting state is watchfulness. She reads the twitch, the change of tone, the sentence that does not fit, and knows when she is being lied to; she is also, therefore, never at rest, and sees her own duplicity in everyone. She is kind, and could be close to someone, and will not let anyone near enough to find where she is soft. Her humour is dark, fast and mostly at her own expense, and it is what she keeps between herself and intimacy.

### Motivation

Out. Enough money and enough leverage to leave the Ādānaśreṇī and the Twilight House both, and reappear in a distant city as someone with no history. Fear drives it as much as ambition: the sense that if she stays much longer the weight of what she owes will crush her, or someone she has betrayed will find out. And under the fear a dangerous wish for something like redemption — a life in which she does not have to perform. Vyālendra, goddess of creation, is who she prays to, because a goddess of creation might make her again.

### Strengths

- **Perception and Intuition**: She sees the small thing — the tone, the pause, the detail that is wrong — and knows what is under a polished surface.
- **Physical Grace and Agility**: She climbs, dances and moves through tight and dangerous places as if she were made for them, and gets in and out by ways nobody thought were ways.
- **Stealth and Concealment**: Through a crowded room, past a guard, into a private chamber, without being noticed — by movement, timing and knowing where people are not looking.
- **Weaponry and Close Combat**: Trained with bow and blade from girlhood, and deadly with a knife in close.
- **Seduction and Intrigue**: Charm, intrigue and guile; she gets secrets out of people in bed or over a cup, and gets them to act against themselves without knowing it.

### Weaknesses

- **Guilt**: The gap between what she does and what she believes is a weight she carries always — every betrayal, every time she has used closeness as a weapon.
- **Emotional Fragility**: Real kindness from someone can go straight through the masks, and what comes out is not always wise.
- **Weak Will and Limited Endurance**: She cannot hold out long against pressure, pain or a long effort, and would break quickly under torture.
- **Inability to Trust**: She assumes everyone is working her as she works them, and is alone in consequence.
- **No Way Back**: Her history closes every respectable door. If the guild or the house drops her, she has very few honest places to go.

## Social

### Patrons

- **The Twilight House Administration**: Run by **Madame Síralina**; the house gives her a roof, clients, and a way into the rooms of the rich, and protects her from outside threats as long as she is profitable and obedient.
- **Nobles of Vedyara (Various)**: Merchants, minor nobles and guild men who keep her, pay for her discretion, and each hold something over her — a debt, a secret — to make sure of it.
- **The Ādānaśreṇī (Covertly)**: Not a patron in name, but the guild has invested in her as a source, and its members protect her as they would a good tool, for as long as it works.

### Enemies

- **The Guild of Rivals**: Members of the Ādānaśreṇī who think she serves too many masters. **Master Thief Gorrán** in particular has marked her as someone who will sell the guild's secrets to one of her other keepers sooner or later.
- **Discarded Lovers**: Former clients who took the performance for love, and some of whom have become dangerous — demanding, threatening, or talking.
- **Apothecary [[being-cndrthrchrymkhysvr|Càndrathar Āchāryamukhyēśvara]]**: She found that [[being-cndrthrchrymkhysvr|Càndrathar]] was making poisons for hire and tried to use it as a lever. He noticed. He now counts her a danger to the secrecy his life depends on, and has begun dosing her, small amounts, trying compounds, learning what will kill her when he decides to.

### Affiliations

- **Ādānaśreṇī**: Her guild in name; she keeps what freedom she can and serves herself first, the guild second and her clients third.
- **Twilight House**: Home and workplace, protection and cage; a valued asset, and a piece of stock.
- **Vyālendra's Temple**: An informal devotion. She prays to the goddess of creation for transformation, and goes to the temple seldom and carefully.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Blackmailer's Gambit**: Someone has sent her a complete account of her work for the Ādānaśreṇī and the Twilight House — dates, targets, methods, outcomes — and a choice: spy on both for this unknown third party, or the account goes to **Master Thief Gorrán** and **Madame Síralina**, and one of them kills her and the other sells her. Five days, and no idea who is holding the paper.

2. **The Patricide Commission**: The guild has given her a killing: a rich merchant whose daughter is close to one of the guild's leaders. The target is her father — the Byzarian merchant who came to her mother once. The guild found him and chose him for her on purpose, to test her or to finish her by making her too compromised to keep. She can betray the guild, kill her father, or find a third road out of both.

3. **The Servant's Secrets**: A slave of one of her regular clients has been found dead in a way that looks like accident, and the city guard suspects Apothecary [[being-cndrthrchrymkhysvr|Càndrathar]]. She thinks he is trying his poisons on people nobody will miss, and that she is on the list. To tell the guard what she knows is to tell them what she is, and the guild and the house would both hear of it. She can go to him, have him taken quietly, or leave the city faster than she had planned.

4. **The Twilight House's Fall**: **Madame Síralina** has been arrested for trafficking and forced servitude, and the house's protection has gone with her; its women are open to every creditor, enemy and violent client they ever had. The arrest was arranged by a rival merchant faction that wants the house and its money. She can help put the house back, and lock herself in; leave the others to it; or bargain a place with the new owners and be part of whatever the house becomes.

5. **The Guild's Final Test**: The Ādānaśreṇī has decided her divided loyalty is a liability and set her a test: an impossible theft — a relic from a guarded temple, a paper from the royal archive, a particular gem from a merchant lord's own rooms — within a lunar cycle, for rank in the guild's upper reaches or a mark of death. She has done it. Doing it, she has seen what the guild's leaders are really about: slaving networks, ritual killing, power taken by terror. She is inside the guild's worst secrets now, and there is no way out that does not make her a thing they have to remove.
