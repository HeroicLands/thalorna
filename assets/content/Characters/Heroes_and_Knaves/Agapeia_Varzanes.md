# Appearance {#appearance}

![[agapevrzns|Agápeia Varzánês]]{float: top-left}

At 6'1" she is taller than most of the men she works beside, and built for it. Golden-toned skin, dark hair, dark hazel eyes; an oblong face with a straight nose, a pointed chin, high cheeks, arched brows, and lips that are usually a little pursed in thought.

# Dossier {#dossier}

## Background

### The Mines of Byzar

Her mother could put a hand to the rock and say where the seam ran. Istâ Varzánês was known for it through the mining towns of the Byzarian League — for finding deposits nobody else had found and getting the stone out with almost nothing wasted — and nobody, Istâ included, could say how it was done. Her father, Korlâ, was the other half: a methodical man who understood why a gallery held and why it fell. The Varzánês had worked those depths for generations and kept the old reverence for Flórania as the earth's giver of every ore and stone, and Agápeia grew up with three rules: respect the earth, read the stone, listen to the mountain.

### Coming of Age in Darkness

She was underground with her parents from childhood. By her teens she could tell rock by touch, judge whether a passage would hold, and move through bad ground without hurting herself, and she had begun to work out better ways to take gems out of the matrix with less damage around them. That caught the eye of Overseer Dûrûm, thirty years in the Byzarian mines, who took her on informally at sixteen and taught her the rest: how to deal with the merchants, how to run a crew, and how a freelance miner survives alongside the companies that hold the richest ground.

### Independent Prospector

At twenty-two she went out on her own, moving between the League's mining operations as a prospector for hire. She found what others had missed. Her crews had fewer accidents than the average and were paid fairly, which in that trade is unusual enough to be remembered. She takes what is needed and wastes nothing, gives thanks, and puts money back into the towns she works from; that is what her devotion to Flórania looks like in practice. By twenty-eight she had modest wealth and had turned down several offers to manage larger operations. She preferred the ground.

### Present Circumstances

She works now between the League's larger mining settlements, taking the prospecting jobs others call too dangerous or too doubtful, with good relations among the local companies and the jewelers who count on her for reliable stones. What troubles her is the direction of the trade. The companies are squeezing out the independents; the newer magnates have no reverence and dig recklessly, and the collapses and the ruined ground follow; and she has begun to suspect that some of the big operations are pulling rare magical materials out of the deep by methods that should not be used. She has not acted on any of it. She is thinking about it.

## Psyche

### Personality

Quiet, and she misses nothing. She says what needs saying without ornament, and she is not cold — she feels the mood of a crew as clearly as a change in the air — but she does not spend words on small talk. With her own people, the miners and merchants she has worked with for years, she is warm and can be relied on. Her humor is a small smile and a look.

She knows what she can do and does not advertise it. Her measure of other people is whether they do their work honestly, and those who put profit ahead of the crew, or use people, earn a contempt she never voices. She does not judge other people's choices in the abstract. She will not work with anyone she thinks is fundamentally untrustworthy, and she has walked away from money to keep that.

### Motivation

Her own work, done her own way, close to the ground. Not wealth for its own sake, though she values the security honest work brings. Under that, her faith: she believes she is answerable to Flórania for how the earth's riches are taken, and she has come to feel complicit, just by working, in practices she thinks are wrong. She is looking for a way to stay in her trade and keep faith with that, and the search is pushing her toward doing something about the League's mines rather than only watching.

### Strengths

- **Expert Prospecting**: She reads ore and stone, judges quality and quantity and the right way to take it, and has an instinct for where the valuable material lies that is close to her mother's.
- **Physical Competence**: Sure-footed, strong-handed and quick in bad ground, with the coordination for exact work in cramped places.
- **Leadership Without Ego**: Crews follow her because she is fair, careful of their lives, and never asks what she would not do herself.
- **Diverse Practical Skills**: Survival, animals, metalwork and whatever else a field camp needs. She fixes what breaks and eats what she finds.
- **Combat Readiness**: A miner who can fight — with a blade, a thrown weapon or a bow — because she has had to, against bandits and against things that live in the deep.

### Weaknesses

- **Limited Eloquence**: She cannot make a speech or win an argument with an official. In a formal negotiation she is at a disadvantage.
- **Underestimation by Others**: She has no flash, and people who judge by appearance or manner take her for less than she is.
- **Emotional Restraint**: She cannot rouse a crowd or carry a room. Where a leader needs passion, she has only steadiness.
- **Modest Analytical Ability**: Sound, practical and short-range. Long plans and abstract problems are not her strength.
- **Vulnerability to Manipulation**: Her feeling for people can be turned against her by anyone who knows how to use guilt or a plea.

## Social

### Patrons

- **Master Jeweler Sâvâlî**: One of the League's best gem-cutters, who commissions her regularly to find and extract particular stones. Years of good dealing on both sides.
- **Overseer Dûrûm**: Her old mentor, still working in the big operations, who sends her specialized jobs and word of new deposits.
- **The Clergy of Flórania**: The local temple uses her to source materials for its rites and pays her a small stipend for mining as the goddess would have it done.

### Enemies

- **Mining Baron Valdûm Thex**: Owner of several of the League's largest operations, to whom independent miners are obstacles. He has bought up mineral rights in ground she works and offered her regular buyers better prices, quietly, to cut her out.
- **The Blackstone Consortium**: Corporate mining interests using alchemical and magical extraction that she believes is dangerous. Her former colleagues are being drawn into it, and she has begun saying so in public.

### Affiliations

- **The Byzarian Miners' Free Union**: The independent miners' mutual society. She pays into its fund and turns up when asked, without holding office.
- **The Temple of Flórania**: She keeps the seasonal rites and sometimes guides temple expeditions into the sacred mineral sites.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Lost Deposit**: Garrin Stonestep, an old miner she knows by reputation, has come to her dying with a secret: a great deposit of rare crystal in the deep caves under the Byzarian heartland, which he could not work and dared not report, because the Blackstone Consortium would seize it and ruin it. His maps are incomplete. The ground is Baron Valdûm Thex's. And what is down there may be more than a deposit.

2. **The Escaped Crew**: A crew she trained, now working for one of the big operations, has sent word that they are running. They have seen what their employer is digging up — something unnatural, and the earth is shaking for it — and they need help getting out with proof and finding somewhere the Consortium cannot reach. Helping them means choosing a side she has so far only complained about.

3. **The Cavern Below**: The Temple of Flórania has hired her to survey the deepest sacred caverns, unvisited for generations, for signs that the secular mines have broken into them. They have. And the caverns are not empty: there are traces of people who lived there long ago, and stone formations that pulse with something, and she has to decide who to tell.

4. **The Merchant's Gamble**: A merchant named Korvûm Silvertongue wants to fund a large expedition north, where old surveys promise emerald and more, and wants her to lead the prospecting. The money would set her up for years. Korvûm is vague about where the money comes from, and her instinct says the Blackstone Consortium is somewhere behind it.

5. **The Whispers from Below**: Miners across the League have been hearing sounds from the deep levels that match no creature and no natural cause, and some refuse to go down. The Miners' Union has asked her to find out what it is. Deeper than she has ever been, the stone itself begins to seem wrong, and she begins to find signs of something very old and very large living under everything she knows.
