# Appearance {#appearance}

![[gasherltrtht|Gasher Let'Rethetu]]{float=top-left}

Gasher is a tall man of 6'1" with a medium build, weathered ebony skin, very dark brown hair and brown eyes. A scar crosses his left eye from brow to cheekbone, pale against the skin, and he keeps his face turned toward the listener so that it shows. His mouth is expressive and quick to smile. He wears the plain traveling cloak of a musician over a bright tunic, and he carries a fine lyre in a padded case, a notebook of lyrics and compositions, and a pouch of healing herbs his mother taught him to carry.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Early Years**

Gasher was born in the merchant quarter of Galezkara, the capital of the [[affiliation-empireakhlth|Empire of Au'Khelathu]], to a family of modest means. His mother, [Linet]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, was a healer of some renown, while his father, [Het'rem]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, worked as a scribe in the [Temple of Chaq'ur]{.name data-type="affiliation"}. From childhood, Gasher showed an unusual gift for music and voice, teaching himself the lyre by listening to court musicians and recreating their melodies from memory alone. By his fourteenth year, he had already begun performing in the streets, and by his twentieth, he had attracted the patronage of a wealthy merchant's daughter named [[being-aleziyazaqq2|Aleziya]], who sponsored his first real performances in the halls of nobility.

**The Scar and Its Meaning**

The tragedy that scarred his face forever transformed his art. During a grand performance at a noble's estate when he was merely twenty-three winters old, a drunk reveler—the jealous husband of a woman Gasher had befriended—lunged at him with a knife mid-song. The blade caught him across the eye, and for months Gasher lay fevered and uncertain whether he would ever see again from that eye. He did recover, though the scar remained, and more importantly, the experience taught him that true music speaks to the deepest wells of human emotion. His songs thereafter became more honest, more vulnerable, reaching souls in ways mere technical perfection could never achieve.

**Current Circumstances**

Now at the height of his artistic powers, Gasher finds himself torn between the security of a patron's court and the freedom of the open road. For the past five years, he has been a sometime-member of [[affiliation-linzamlkhlth|Lin'Zamlu elu Au'Khelathu]], the Minstrels' Guild, though his participation is sporadic at best—he values his independence too highly to submit fully to their rigid structures. He carries with him a harp of fine craftsmanship, gifted to him by the Guild Master's own son, alongside a worn notebook filled with lyrics of his own composition. His reputation has grown such that small lords compete for his presence at festivals and celebrations, yet he rarely commits to more than a season or two in any one place. Of late, rumor has it that he is searching for something—some lost song or ancient melody that he believes might yet unlock the deepest magic of music itself.

## Psyche

### Personality

Gasher is a man of real warmth tempered by a restless soul. He is the sort of person who can sit with a common farmhand over ale and make them feel like they are the most fascinating being alive through his attentive listening and curiosity about their struggles. Yet this same warmth never quite permits him to put down roots; just as people begin to rely upon his presence, he feels the familiar itch to move onward. He speaks with the eloquence of one educated by travel rather than formal schooling, his words woven with proverbs and observations gleaned from a hundred villages. Though he maintains a philosophical outlook on life—believing that hardship refines the soul as fire refines metal—he is not immune to melancholy, and on dark nights his songs take on an almost mournful quality that leaves listeners moved to tears.

His relationship with his own scar is complex. He wears it without shame but rarely speaks of how he received it unless pressed by those he trusts. To him, it is proof that a person broken can become more beautiful and more honest. This philosophy extends to how he treats others—he has a gift for making the wounded feel that their damage does not diminish their worth.

### Motivation

Gasher seeks to capture in music the full spectrum of human experience: joy and sorrow, triumph and defeat, love and loss. He believes that song has a power akin to magic, capable of healing the spirit in ways that nothing else can. His wanderlust also carries a quest—rumors speak of an ancient cycle of songs believed lost when [[lore-akhfirsttab|the First Tablets]] ended, melodies said to hold within them the gods' first utterances. Should such songs be recovered and performed, it is said they could reshape the world itself. Gasher's restlessness is in part this quest, though he cannot admit even to himself whether he truly believes such legends, or whether the search itself is merely an excuse to keep moving, to keep living.

### Strengths

Master Vocalist
: His voice is his greatest instrument, capable of ranging from a whisper that draws crowds to silence to a thundering declaration that shakes the rafters. Years of training and natural gift have made him one of the finest singers in Au'Khelathu.

Lyricist and Composer
: Beyond performance, Gasher creates original songs that capture the essence of stories and emotions in ways that move audiences deeply. His notebook is filled with lyrics of great beauty and poignancy.

Emotional Resonance
: He possesses an almost supernatural ability to sense the mood and needs of a crowd, shifting his performance to speak directly to their hearts. This has made him beloved among common folk who see in his songs reflections of their own struggles.

Persuasive Presence
: His interest in people and his warmth make him a natural diplomat and peacemaker. Many conflicts have been soothed by his intervention and his willingness to hear all sides.

Skilled Instrumentalist
: His mastery of the harp and lyre is of the first order, allowing him to accompany his own voice with complex and beautiful arrangements.

### Weaknesses

Commitment Aversion
: His inability to settle in one place for long periods makes him unreliable as a permanent patron or employee. He will inevitably leave, no matter how comfortable his circumstances.

Court Politics Bewilderment
: The formalities of noble courts baffle and frustrate him. He often says the wrong thing, fails to understand subtle insults, and shows little patience for courtly intrigue.

Physical Vulnerability
: While not unskilled with a blade, he is not a warrior. His scar is a constant reminder of his vulnerability, and he prefers to flee danger rather than face it.

Restless Judgment
: His wandering nature sometimes manifests as contempt for those who have chosen to remain in one place, as if he sees their stability as a failure of imagination or courage.

Dependence on Mood
: His performances and even his composition are deeply tied to his emotional state. A sustained bout of dark temper or melancholy can render him unable to perform.

## Social

### Patrons

[[being-aleziyazaqq2|Aleziya Zaqqara]]
: A wealthy merchant widow of advancing years who once sponsored his rise to prominence. Though their romance ended decades ago, she remains his most reliable patron, offering him shelter and patronage whenever he returns to Galezkara. She sees in him the young idealist she once knew.

Lord [[being-maquptas|Makuptas]] of the [Eastern Reaches]{.name data-type="place"}
: A provincial lord who has competed with other nobles for the privilege of hosting Gasher's performances. He values Gasher's ability to enhance the prestige of his court and treats the musician with unusual respect and generosity.

Master [Uzner]{.name data-type="being/npc"} of the Minstrels' Guild
: The current Guild Master, herself a retired performer, who recognizes Gasher's talent and occasionally pressures him to take greater responsibility within the Guild's structure—pressure he consistently resists.

### Enemies

[The Jade Serpent Collective]{.name data-type="affiliation"}
: A cabal of traditional court bards who view Gasher's "common" style and his independence from established guild hierarchies as a threat to their monopoly on the ear of nobles. They have been known to spread rumors that his music is somehow tainted or that his scar marks him as cursed.

The Shadowing Rival
: A rival musician who has attempted to shadow Gasher from town to town, attempting to learn his methods and his original compositions. Gasher suspects him of attempting to steal his work for his own profit.

### Affiliations

Minstrels' Guild of Au'Khelathu
: Though only loosely connected, he maintains membership and appears at Guild gatherings sporadically.

[[affiliation-linwalthu|Lin'Walthu]]
: An informal network of traveling performers and storytellers who share information about safe roads, hostile regions, and potential performance venues.

## Plot Hooks

The Songs Before the Mound
: [[being-legirgulu2|Legirgulu]], a reclusive scholar and priestess of the [[affiliation-chaqur|Faith of Chaq'ur]], tells him she has found references to songs of creation in the oldest temple texts and wants someone who can set them. The texts are in the archives of the great temples of Galezkara, and every enclave that holds a fragment guards it. He can work from copies made in secret, or sign a contract with the temples that owns what he writes.

The Stolen Cycle
: The notebooks of his greatest work, a song cycle he calls The Descent of Galezkara, were lost when a merchant caravan was ambushed on the road. Pieces of it have begun to appear under another musician's name in the halls of the nobles, who think it is new. Proving the cycle is his means the notebooks, and the notebooks are in the hands of whoever bought the loot.

The Coming-Forth
: An emissary of the high priests of the [[affiliation-uqaa|Faith of Uqa'a]] asks him to compose and lead the music for the midsummer Festival of the Coming-Forth, when the sun rises down the Sun's Road. The commission is the greatest of his life and binds him to the temple for the season. The Jade Serpent Collective, whose bards have always performed it, are already telling the temple that his scar marks a curse.

The Other Story of the Scar
: The man who cut him is a ruined drunk, and he is telling a story that contradicts Gasher's: that the scar came from a fight over a woman Gasher wronged. The story is spreading in the places where he plays. He can leave the stage and the story with it, or find the man and the one who is feeding him drink.

:::secret {title="What the Game Master knows"}
The man with the knife was paid. The Jade Serpent Collective arranged the attack, to break Gasher's rise before it threatened the court bards, and has been supporting the drunk's new story.

Legirgulu is honest. The fragments of the creation songs are real, and one of them names the Mound before the Crossing, a claim the temple of Uqa'a would rather not have sung.
:::
