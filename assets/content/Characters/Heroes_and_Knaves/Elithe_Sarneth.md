# Appearance {#appearance}

![[elyseskyrn|Élithe Sarneth]]{float=top-left}

Élithe Sarneth is a 26-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She has fair, lightly freckled skin, dark blonde hair that falls loose around her face, and green eyes. She has an easy smile and a quick, warm laugh that put frightened patients at ease. She carries herself with the quiet competence of someone who has seen the inside of too many wounds to be squeamish about anything.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Élithe Sarneth grew up as the daughter of an apothecary in a small village, learning the healing arts from her father and grandmother before she could read. Her natural aptitude for medicine and alchemy—combined with a charm that put even the most fearful patients at ease—led to her being sent to study in [[place-beravel|Péravel]], [[place-elavendre|Élavendre]], where she received formal training in both conventional healing and alchemical science.

Despite her success in Péravel, Élithe grew restless. Academic study was too slow, too contained. She wanted to see rare herbs in the wild, test remedies under real conditions, and learn techniques that no chapterhouse could teach her. When the opportunity arose to join [[affiliation-slntlncmpny|the Silent Talon]] as their field medic, she took it without hesitation. War brings patients, and patients bring learning.

To everyone who knows her, she is a very good healer who is quietly guarded about her years at Péravel and quick to change the subject when they come up.

Her relationship with [[being-torniskbry|Tornis]] has recently added an unexpected complication to her carefully ordered life.

:::secret {title="The hidden gift"}
Élithe's skills extend beyond conventional healing. She has an arcane talent, and a bad experience in her past has made her deeply cautious about revealing it. She keeps it from the band and from Tornis. Its nature and extent are known only to herself.

**Hook: the hidden gift.** Someone discovers, or suspects, Élithe's concealed talent: a fellow mage who senses her power, an old acquaintance from Péravel, or an enemy who saw something she thought no one saw. Élithe must decide whether to flee, fight, or finally reveal what she has been hiding.
:::

## Background

### Skills and Abilities

Healing
: Élithe's core expertise. She is skilled in wound treatment, herbal remedies, poultices, surgical stitching, and the management of battlefield injuries. She is the reason the Silent Talon's casualty rate is low.

Alchemy
: Formally trained in the preparation of potions, tonics, and elixirs. She can identify and process rare herbs and compounds, and is always experimenting with new formulations.

Herbalism
: An expert botanist with an encyclopedic knowledge of medicinal plants. She gathers specimens wherever the band travels, building a growing collection of rare ingredients.

Charm
: Élithe has a bubbly, disarming manner that makes people trust her instinctively. This is not an act—she is warm—but it also is effective camouflage for the things she chooses not to reveal.

## Psyche

### Personality

Élithe is calm, resourceful, and empathetic, with an optimism that lifts the spirits of those around her. She is focused on her work, valuing knowledge and skill above all else. Beneath the warmth, however, she is quietly guarded—she prefers to keep personal matters to herself and is cautious about letting others too close to the parts of her life she has chosen to hide.

### Motivation

Élithe's primary goal is the discovery of new alchemical recipes, medical remedies, and techniques. She dreams of one day opening her own healing house, and everything she does with the Silent Talon is in service of that ambition. The band gives her access to rare herbs, unusual injuries, and the kind of field experience no academy could provide.

## Plot Hooks

The Plague Contract
: The Silent Talon is hired to escort supplies to a plague-stricken town. Élithe's skills are put to the ultimate test as she must identify the cause, develop a treatment, and keep the band healthy—all while someone in the town may be deliberately spreading the sickness.

The Survey of the Thin Places
: The University's Fitter of Expeditions, [[being-pelraorsel|Pelra Orsel]], has offered the Silent Talon the guarding of a summer survey of the thin places beyond the tree line, the survey [[being-ielaraskir|Prince Yélaras]] means to join. The pay is good, the bards in the river-quarter inns sing about the guards who did not come back, and Élithe would be back in Péravel among teachers who knew her before she learned to hide.

The Rare Ingredient
: Élithe learns of an extraordinarily rare herb growing deep in dangerous territory. Obtaining it could be the breakthrough she needs for a remedy she has been developing for years—but the expedition would put the entire band at risk for the sake of her personal ambition.
