# Appearance {#appearance}

![[anrjhrdvmbjkr|Anûraja Harshadēvāmbujakar]]{float: top-left}

Anûraja stands 6'0" tall with a medium build. He has golden brown skin, dark brown hair, and deep brown eyes. His features include a diamond-shaped face, a straight nose, a soft jawline, full lips, golden brows, and rounded cheeks. He has a tattoo of a tiger on his forearm.

Most striking is the tiger tattoo on his right forearm, a creature rendered in fine detail that seems to move in candlelight. The tattoo marks his lineage: it indicates his training within the Vedyaran tradition of shadow-work. His hands, with their precise fingers and evident dexterity, are as dangerous as any blade; despite their slender appearance, they possess devastating capability.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Anûraja was born into a city-state family of middling merchants in [[place-vedyarargn|Vedyara]], the youngest child of a father whose business interests were built upon careful political positioning rather than trade itself. From earliest childhood, he displayed an unsettling affinity for violence—not the hot-blooded rage of a typical child, but a cold, analytical interest in how the most injury could be inflicted with the least effort.

At age eight, his father, recognizing something unusual in the boy, engaged instructors from the Vedyaran shadow-brotherhoods—organizations of trained assassins who maintain the balance of power through selective elimination of threats and inconvenient persons. For the next fifteen years, Anûraja underwent an intensive education unlike any formal schooling. He learned poisons from **Master Devan**, studied the art of silent movement under **the Night-Walker**, mastered the philosophy of clean assassination from the elderly **Grandmother Vex**, and trained in weaponry under **Kesh the Scarred**. By age twenty-three, he had completed his training and taken his first paid contracts.

Now, at thirty-one, Anûraja has become something of a legend within certain circles—an assassin of such skill that the wealthy and powerful quietly compete to engage his services, and the paranoid take extraordinary precautions merely knowing he exists in the world. He has never failed a contract, a perfect record maintained through careful planning, ruthless decision-making, and a willingness to abandon assignments that show any sign of compromise. He operates independently, taking contracts only from sources he verifies personally, and keeping his work in absolute secrecy.

## Psyche

### Personality

Anûraja is defined by an emotional distance so complete it approaches the sociopathic. He experiences the world intellectually rather than emotionally—he understands human motivation through study rather than intuition, and conducts his dealings with people through memorized patterns rather than any felt connection. This does not make him overtly threatening in casual company; he has trained himself to mimic human emotion convincingly enough that most people fail to notice the absence beneath the surface.

In his work, he is careful to the point of obsession. Every contract is researched exhaustively; every variable is planned for; every contingency is prepared. He is willing to abandon lucrative assignments if conditions suggest risk inadequately compensated by payment. This rational detachment has allowed him to survive longer than most assassins—he takes calculated risks, never personal vendettas.

With those rare few he deals with repeatedly, Anûraja develops something approaching fondness, though he would never use such a sentimental term. These relationships are transactional but reliable; he honors agreements with them and maintains their secrets as he maintains his own. Beyond these rare connections, he views humanity with a scholar's interest—observing patterns, studying motivation, but keeping himself wholly apart.

### Motivation

On the surface, Anûraja is motivated by money and pride in his craft. The contracts he accepts pay extraordinarily well, and his perfect record represents the kind of mastery within his chosen field that most humans can never achieve. This ambition is real but secondary to deeper drives.

Fundamentally, Anûraja is motivated by the philosophical principles he absorbed during his training—the belief that the world contains too many people, that selective elimination creates balance, that some individuals are threats that must be neutralized for the greater good. He regards his work as a kind of dark priesthood rather than murder-for-hire, keeping the balance through the careful removal of those who would upset it.

Anûraja is also driven by a need to understand the limits of his capability. He accepts contracts for the income and to test himself against new challenges, different opponents, and unexpected variables. Each assassination is a puzzle to him—how best to reach the target, how to avoid detection, how to accomplish the impossible.

### Strengths

- **Lethal Dexterity**: His dexterity, combined with four specializations in melee combat, makes Anûraja devastating at close quarters. His hands and daggers move with such speed and precision that most opponents never realize they are dead until the knowledge becomes irrelevant.
- **Extraordinary Agility**: His agility lets him move with liquid grace, climbing impossible angles, balancing on surfaces others would fall from, and executing acrobatic movements in combat that confound opponents' expectations.
- **Master Climbing and Acrobatics**: His climbing, with three specializations, and his dancing let him cross vertical spaces others consider impassable. He can scale walls, traverse rope bridges, and position himself in impossible locations.
- **Advanced Creative Problem-Solving**: His creativity gives him fresh approaches to the problems of assassination. When conventional methods are blocked, his mind quickly generates alternatives.
- **Stealth Mastery**: His stealth, awareness, and tracking capabilities allow him to observe without being observed and to move through spaces without disturbing them. He can infiltrate secure locations and observe targets for weeks without detection.
- **Intellectual Capability**: His reasoning combined with his training makes him excellent at planning complex operations, anticipating counteractions, and adapting to unexpected developments.
- **Exceptional Weapons Capability**: His throwing and melee skills, each with four specializations, and his knowledge of weaponcraft make him dangerous with any instrument of violence.

### Weaknesses

- **Emotional Atrophy**: His near-total inability to connect with others emotionally makes him vulnerable to manipulation through the emotions of others. Those he cares about (few as they are) can be used against him.
- **Questionable Morality**: His willingness to kill for money and his philosophical distance from the consequences of his work leave him morally isolated. The positions he accepts sometimes place him in conflict with principled opposition.
- **Limited Empathy**: He has little feeling for others and cannot reliably predict how people will react to emotional appeals or why some individuals value concepts like loyalty or family above survival.
- **Overconfidence**: His perfect record has created a dangerous certainty that he can execute any contract. This confidence occasionally leads him to accept commissions that contain hidden variables.
- **Reduced Physical Resilience**: His focus on precision and stealth rather than durability means his strength and endurance are modest. Direct confrontation against physically superior opponents is dangerous for him.
- **Isolation**: His self-imposed separation from human connection leaves him fragile in ways he does not recognize. Disruption to his solitary routine can produce unexpected consequences.

## Social

### Patrons

- **The House of Vedyan Merchants**: A noble merchant family that has contracted Anûraja's services repeatedly over eight years, always for high-stakes elimination of political or commercial rivals. They pay extraordinarily well and understand the necessity of discretion.
- **The Shadow-Brotherhoods of Vedyara**: While Anûraja operates independently, he maintains loose affiliation with the organizations that trained him. They occasionally provide contracts, intelligence, or assistance when his own projects require additional capability.

### Enemies

- **The Righteous Order of Preservation**: A loose confederation of paladins, clerics, and holy warriors who view assassins as abominations requiring eradication. They have a standing contract to hunt Anûraja, though he has so far managed to avoid direct confrontation through his superior intelligence and stealth.
- **Vex the Undying**: Anûraja's former mentor, Grandmother Vex, has begun to hunt him for reasons he does not fully understand. She seems to believe that Anûraja has strayed from the philosophical principles of shadow-work and must be eliminated. Their war is conducted in shadows, with neither able to gain decisive advantage.
- **The Guild of Ironhands**: A mercenary organization that views Anûraja as competition. They have attempted several times to eliminate him or recruit him forcibly, none of which has succeeded.

### Affiliations

- **The Shadow-Brotherhoods**: While not formally affiliated, Anûraja maintains connections with these organizations, taking contracts through them when it suits him, though his independent commissions generally pay far better.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Impossible Target**: Anûraja is approached through his usual channels with an extraordinary contract: eliminate an individual who appears to have no fixed location, no predictable pattern, and no clear identity. The fee is the largest he has ever been offered, and the employer is someone of sufficient power that refusal would be dangerous. As he begins investigation, he discovers that the target may not be human—or may not even be singular. The contract forces him into territories where his usual methods prove inadequate.

2. **The Betrayal Revealed**: An intelligence asset Anûraja has relied upon for verification of contracts turns out to have been feeding him false information. One of his previous "assignments" was actually an innocent person whose death was arranged by a rival assassin attempting to frame him. The revelation that he has broken his own code—killing the wrong target—shakes his carefully ordered existence. He begins investigating who manipulated him and why, determined to correct the error.

3. **The Mentor's Ultimatum**: Grandmother Vex approaches Anûraja directly with an offer rather than an attempt at assassination. She reveals that she is dying and wishes to pass her knowledge to a successor. She believes Anûraja has strayed from the true path of shadow-work, but thinks he can be redeemed. She offers him a choice: spend six months in intensive retraining under her supervision, learning the "philosophy" he abandoned, or face her as an enemy knowing she will dedicate her final years to his elimination.

4. **The Conscience Awakens**: A contract goes catastrophically wrong when Anûraja discovers the target is a young woman bearing an uncanny resemblance to his sister—a sister he believed dead in childhood. Investigation reveals that his sister may have survived, that this target may actually be his sister, and that someone deliberately arranged this contract knowing it would create exactly this moral crisis. Faced with the possibility of a living relative, Anûraja sets out to learn whether the resemblance is coincidence, manipulation, or truth.

5. **The Sacred Weapon**: A wealthy collector approaches Anûraja with an unusual proposition: not a contract for murder, but a commission to acquire a famed artifact—a blade of such craftsmanship and alleged supernatural properties that its mere possession has sparked wars. The artifact is currently in the hands of a military order that will defend it to the death. Anûraja must employ assassination, theft, and manipulation in concert to obtain the blade, all while realizing that the artifact is exactly what his mentor Grandmother Vex has been seeking, and that she will kill him to possess it.
