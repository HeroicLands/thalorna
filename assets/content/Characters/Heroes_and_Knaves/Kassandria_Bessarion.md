# Appearance {#appearance}

![[kasndrbsrn|Kassándria Bessarîon]]{float: top-left}

Slight, 5'5", and usually the last person in the room to notice that the room has emptied. Warm olive skin, black hair, brown eyes; a diamond-shaped face with an aquiline nose, a pronounced chin, sculpted cheeks and strong brows.

# Dossier {#dossier}

## Background

### Early Mastery

The book that made her enemies is called _On the Wards of the Sundered Kingdoms_, and its argument can be put in a sentence: the Helionite city-states did not fall, they were felled, by a working whose authors have since been cut out of every record. Scholars who wanted the old kingdoms vindicated loved it. The conservative side of the Epistemium called it destabilising, and then heretical, and has not stopped.

Kassándria was born a Bessarîon, of the merchant family of Byzaris, richest city of the Byzarian League, and while her brothers and sisters learned the counting-house she filled the margins of the family library with translations of things nobody else in the house could read. At thirteen she petitioned the Panepistemium herself, and was taken — years early — because the Epistemium masters who examined her found a mind that heard the pattern in a spell's sounds and could take a dead language apart by ear.

### Scholarly Pursuits and Dangerous Experiments

Nearly twenty years followed of cataloguing forgotten tongues and old thaumaturgical texts, tracing how the shape of a word ties to the deeper workings of the Héx Hodäi. Three treatises on the ancient traditions made her the first authority in the League and past it. The third was the one above.

### Present Circumstances

She keeps a private archive now in a tower above Byzaris. **Lord Mikhael Verath**, a nobleman of wide connections and unstated loyalties, has paid for her last three years of expeditions into the sealed vaults under abandoned Helionite temples, where she looks for what the old kingdoms knew and how they were ended. The Epistemium still defers to her on the strength of the work. Its leadership, and the conservative faction in particular, watches what she is digging toward with growing dislike, and her old friends there have become fewer.

## Psyche

### Personality

Brilliant, narrow, and consumed. She speaks precisely and never casually, is polite and distant with almost everyone, and has a wit that cuts and is never idle; people who mistake enthusiasm for understanding get it. The few who get past that find a warmth that surprises them and a dry humour that catches them off guard.

What drives her is the conviction that whatever felled the Helionite kingdoms could do it again, and that the knowledge to stop it is in those vaults. It has made her reckless: she has risked disasters for a page, and called the risk the price of a truth the Epistemium would rather bury. And in private there is a question she puts away every time it comes: whether the voices she hears in her deepest trances are the past, or something else that has learned what she wants.

### Motivation

She believes the fall was arranged, and that the arrangers' names were erased by the same institutions that govern magic now. She believes — it is nearly a faith — that she was chosen to recover what they buried, by fate, or by the voices, or by something she has no word for. That belief lets her go where other mages will not and gives her a certainty that answers every objection. It does not answer the one she asks herself: whether the voices are contact, or an excuse for something she can no longer stop.

### Strengths

- **Arcane Linguistics Mastery**: She reads the tongues of the kingdoms before the Collapse as easily as her own. Very few living can.
- **Thaumaturgical Theory**: Resonance, ritual structure, the grammar under a spell — she understands them well enough to build rites other mages call impossible, and make them work.
- **Scholarly Authority**: Her books carry weight in every academic and magical circle in the League and beyond it.
- **Perception and Analysis**: She sees the pattern others miss and fits together scraps that do not seem to belong together.
- **Ritual Precision**: Her ceremonies are flawless, performed the way a master musician performs a piece she has by heart.

### Weaknesses

- **Physical Frailty**: A great mind and a body that any street thug could fold.
- **Obsessive Recklessness**: Several narrow escapes from magical disaster, none of them by wisdom.
- **Isolation**: Her certainty and her coldness have driven off most of the people who might stand with her.
- **Magical Overreach**: She attempts rites whose consequences she has not thought through, and not only her own life rides on them.

## Social

### Patrons

- **Lord Mikhael Verath**: A Byzarian noble of unknown purposes who has funded her digs for three years, in return for copies of everything she finds and first refusal on any artefact. Cordial, transactional, and lately she has wondered what he is really buying.
- **Praelatus Aleksandr of the Zephäris Sodalitas**: The old senior master of the Panepistemium in Byzaris, who taught her, defends her against the conservatives, and asks her, more often each year, to work on something more orthodox.

### Enemies

- **Magister Petros Varthis**: Of the Sodalitas Physéra. She demolished his work on the stability of ancient magical foundations in print five years ago and his advancement stopped there. He has spent the years since collecting allies in the leadership and what he calls evidence of dangerous flaws in her method. It is personal now.
- **The Keepers of the Sealed Archive**: A faction in the leadership, drawn from several Sodalitates but led by the Sodalitas Sidéros (Metal) and its instinct for binding and order. They hold that what the ancients sealed was sealed for reason, and are lobbying to bar her from the restricted texts.

### Affiliations

- **Panepistemium**: Her institution, and no longer a secure one. Younger scholars follow her; the leadership has begun to think of her work as a threat to the doctrine, and her tenure with it.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Sealed Vault Discovery**: In an unexplored section of the vaults beneath the Flórania Temple in Byzaris she has found a fragment describing a way to undo the working that broke the kingdoms — perhaps to reach what is left of them. The last pages were removed, generations ago, by another scholar. The man who may have them is **Oravex Solemnios**, old, self-exiled in the Khazryn Desert, and there for a reason.

2. **The Patron's Purpose**: Verath has let slip — to others, not to her — that he speaks for an exiled faction that means to use what she recovers to reorder the League, and that he wants from her one particular ritual: one that would break the balance among the five merchant cities. She does not know. Whoever tells her will have to decide first whether Verath might be right about what happened to the kingdoms.

3. **The Heresy Trial**: Varthis and the Keepers have got their inquiry: her publications, her research, a charge of breaching prohibited categories and endangering the wards. She wants witnesses and evidence. What is there to find is that her last ritual, in the vault under the Flórania Temple, did shake a seal that had held something for centuries — and nobody can yet say whether that thing is real or the story the Keepers need.

4. **Voices in the Archive**: The voice in her trances has a name now, and calls itself the preserved mind of a Helionite archivist. It gives her knowledge, and it asks for rituals in return, each one larger, each to reopen communication across the rifts. It may be older than any archivist and very much less human. She may already have promised it something. The last ritual is close.

5. **Race for the Hidden Archive**: A Helionite archive hidden since before the fall has been located, and three parties know it: the Keepers, who want it left sealed; Verath's agents, who want its contents; and the Ordo Arcanis, whose interest is sudden and unexplained. She wants to be there first. What is inside will prove her life's work or show it to be a delusion, and every faction that has hidden this place for centuries has a reason it does not share.
