Tvarninda stands at 5’8" with a slender, wiry frame, weighing around 140 pounds. Her pale complexion and long, ashen-blonde hair give her an ethereal presence, often kept loose or in a simple braid. Her piercing blue eyes seem to see beyond the physical world, reflecting her connection to the spirit realm. Tvarninda dresses in dark, flowing robes, adorned with runes and charms, reflecting her role as a seid, and she carries a simple wooden staff inscribed with ancient symbols of power.

# Dossier {#dossier}

## Background

### Origin and Early Life

Tvarninda was born into the [Nalthendik clan]{.name data-type="affiliation/lineage"}, a small, isolated lesser free clan of [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]], a land of rugged fjords, deep forests, and frozen seas, home to fierce warriors and hardy seafarers. Growing up in the [[place-gnaldurfjord|Gnaldurfjord]] region, her family was primarily involved in fishing and hunting, eking out a living from the sea and the sparse lowlands that fringed the fjords. From a young age, Tvarninda exhibited signs of being different—she had strange dreams, could sense emotions in others, and felt a deep connection to the spirits of the natural world. Her village feared her, but they also recognized her gifts, for such powers were rare and valuable in [[place-nordheim|Nordheim]].

### The Awakening

As a young woman in Norgaad, Tvarninda’s talents were only just beginning to show. She was an apprentice healer, learning the herbs and chants from her village elder, with no inkling of the spiritual path that awaited her. One winter night, under the rare, brilliant display of the northern lights, Tvarninda felt an overwhelming urge to wander into the nearby forest alone, a compulsion she couldn’t explain.

In the quiet of the snow-covered woods, she heard whispers that seemed to dance with the lights above. These voices weren’t of this world but the spirits of the forest—ancestors and lost souls who had watched over Norgaad for generations. Trembling yet compelled, Tvarninda closed her eyes and listened as they spoke, guiding her to perform her first seid ritual. With the gestures and words that sprang unbidden to her mind, she called upon the spirits to heal a sick child back in the village, whom medicine alone couldn’t save.

By morning, word had spread that the child’s fever had broken, and Tvarninda’s fate as a seid was sealed. That night marked not only her initiation but also her first taste of the sacrifice required by the spirits; Tvarninda returned to the village exhausted, feeling the life force she had offered in exchange for the child’s healing. From that moment, she was bound to the spirits, forever marked by her first ritual under the northern lights.

The people called her a Seid, a wise woman with psychic abilities and a connection to the spirit world, akin to a shaman.

### Early Training

Tvarninda’s talent for Seid magic was recognized by the local village elders and she was sent to train with an older Seid who lived alone in the mountains. Her training was harsh and involved deep spiritual practices, fasting, and long journeys into the wild where she would commune with spirits. During these rites, she developed a unique bond with the [Crow Spirit]{.name data-type="being/creature"}, a creature known for its intelligence, cunning, and its ability to traverse both the physical and spiritual realms. Over time, Tvarninda began to adopt aspects of the crow herself, often staring at people intensely and developing a love for shiny objects, which she would collect obsessively.

Tvarninda became a mendicant, traveling from clan to clan, offering her services as a Seid. She would read the bones, summon spirits for advice, and perform powerful rituals to aid in battle or bring good fortune. The people of Nordheim both revered and feared her. Some saw her as a wise woman, a bridge between the mortal world and the spirits. Others whispered that she was cursed, for her association with the crow—a bird of death—was unsettling.

### The Transformation

Tvarninda’s transformation from a wandering Seid to a woman with a deeper purpose came when she received a powerful vision during a midwinter blet. In the vision, she was shown the unraveling threads of fate in Norgaad—her homeland would face internal strife, foreign invasions, but most of all the rise of an ancient evil from the depths of the sea. [[being-njorven|Njorven]], the Sea Wraith, a mythic creature said to have been overthrown long ago by **[[lore-thrunvalddty|Thrunvald]]**, is slowly awakening, his influence seeping back into the seas of Norgaad, causing unprecedented storms and sea unrest. His goal is to break free and exact revenge on the mortal world, especially the coastal clans that once worshipped him but have since turned to [[affiliation-thrunvald|Thrunvald]]. The Crow Spirit guided her in the vision, showing her that she must acquire three ancient relics said to have been created by Thrunvald to seal away Njorven—**[[miscgear-sprsigrid|the Spear of Skraldthira]]**, **[[miscgear-crwnwyrm|the Crown of the Wyrm]]**, and **[[miscgear-hornnjordur|the Horn of Hafvald]]**—and use the ancient rite _[[lore-njordurritlbinding|The Ritual of Binding]]_ to either re-imprison the spirit or appease his wrath. Her powers, and those of key companions, will be the key to saving her people.

This singular event shook Tvarninda to her core and set her on a journey beyond the safety of her fjord-bound homeland. No longer content to be a wandering wise woman, Tvarninda knew she had to gather allies and confront the dangers that lay ahead, both physical and spiritual. Her mission now takes her into the heart of Nordheim and beyond, seeking to understand the forces at play and to restore balance before the darkness consumes Norgaad.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tvarninda is quiet and hard to read, often lost in thought or staring into the distance. She is prone to bouts of eerie silence followed by cryptic statements, and her mannerisms reflect her deep connection to the crow totem. She tends to stare at people as if seeing something beyond them, and she sometimes shakes her body or fluffs her cloak as though ruffling feathers. Tvarninda also has an unusual fondness for shiny objects, often collecting small trinkets or tokens. Though she is kind at heart, her detached and strange behavior makes it difficult for others to truly understand her.

Tvarninda is wise beyond her years, with a deep understanding of the spiritual world and the forces that shape destiny. However, she carries the weight of her vision heavily, and she is constantly searching for answers. She fears that she may not be able to stop the coming doom and worries that the Crow Spirit’s warnings may not be enough.

### Strengths

Spirit Communion
: Tvarninda can communicate with spirits, summoning them for guidance, protection, or learning hidden truths.

Seer of Visions
: Tvarninda has the ability to see glimpses of the future, reading omens in the flight of birds or the alignment of stars.

Psychic Powers
: Tvarninda can manipulate the minds of others, casting illusions or influencing emotions.

Shamanic Healing
: Tvarninda uses rituals, herbs, and chants to heal wounds and cure sickness.

## Plot Hooks

The Boats That Went West
: Skeldvir Hafthannsen and the younger men of [[place-gnaldurfjord|Gnaldurfjord]] ask the seid they know best for an omen for a fourth western crossing, and the widows of the last one ask her for the opposite. An omen read for either side will send men to a voyage or hold them from one. Tvarninda can read what she sees, read what she is paid to see, or tell both parties that her own vision points to the sea nearer home.

The Crown in the Howe
: Tvarninda's vision says the Crown of the Wyrm is one of the three relics she needs, and the [[affiliation-grimvar|Grimvar]] keep it in a howe at [[place-braldheim|Braldheim]] in Malagna. The [[affiliation-hrafanvar|Hrafanvar]] say that fear and not piety keeps the Grimvar from wearing it. Asking the Grimvar means taking sides in the quarrel over the next king. Asking the Hrafanvar means a promise she cannot keep to either clan.

The Phantom Fleet
: Reports reach Tvarninda of longships with no crews raiding the coastal villages of [[place-norgaad|Norgaad]]. Her divination names the sea's old host. The clans of the coast want a seid to turn them away, and each wants the seid to start at its own shore. She can protect one village and leave the others, or follow the fleet to its source.

The Rival Seid
: A rival seid tells the clans that Tvarninda's visions are false and sends a malicious spirit to haunt her. The clans will follow whoever wins the contest of magic and will that settles the question. Tvarninda can fight and risk her bond with the Crow Spirit, or decline and watch her name fall.

During a midwinter ritual Tvarninda hears her ancestors warn her of an ancient betrayal tied to her bloodline in the Nalthendik clan. The truth is hidden in her family's history. It may be linked to the Crow Spirit's guidance, and Tvarninda must decide whether to trust a spirit whose counsel might be part of the betrayal.
