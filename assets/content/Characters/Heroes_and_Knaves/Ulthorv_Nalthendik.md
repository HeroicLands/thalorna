# Appearance {#appearance}

Ulthorv stands 6'1" tall with a medium build. He has tanned ruddy skin, gray hair, and blue eyes. His features include an angular face, a slightly upturned nose, an angular jawline, narrow lips, thick brows, and hollow cheeks. He has a tattoo of a tiger on his forearm.

# Dossier {#dossier}

## Background

### Life Before the Mines

Ulthorv was born free in [[place-nordheim|Nordheim]]'s borderlands, son of a leather-worker and a wandering warrior-woman who passed through the region in pursuit of her own destiny. His father taught him the craft, but Ulthorv's heart always burned with his mother's restlessness. At twenty, seeking adventure and fortune, he ventured to the great mining settlements of the north, where a young man could theoretically rise through skill and determination. His tiger tattoo was earned through a rite of passage—a test of strength and will in the sacred valleys. For a decade, he worked as a free miner, his strength and shrewd judgment bringing him respect and decent wages.

### Descent into Bondage

At thirty, Ulthorv made the critical error of witnessing something he shouldn't have—he saw the mine overseers falsifying records of a catastrophic collapse, suppressing knowledge of trapped workers to avoid costly rescue operations. When he attempted to expose the conspiracy, the corrupt officials moved swiftly. False charges of theft were leveled against him. His free status was revoked through a sham court, and he was declared an unfree laborer bound to a jarl's estate near [[place-horgsvangur|Horgsvangur]], legally property rather than a person. The transformation from free man to slave happened with terrifying speed.

### Years of Captivity

Eight years have passed since that judgment, spent as an unfree laborer on a jarl's estate near [[place-horgsvangur|Horgsvangur]]. Ulthorv has survived through sheer physical resilience and an unbreakable will, though his spirit has been beaten down repeatedly by the overseer's lashes and the grinding monotony of the work. He has attempted escape six times—each attempt more desperate than the last, each foiled and punished with increasing severity. The scars on his back tell the story of his resistance. Yet something in him refuses to break completely. His fellow workers whisper that Ulthorv Nalthendik still dreams of freedom, though such dreams are considered dangerous luxuries on the estate.

## Psyche

### Personality

Years of captivity have stripped Ulthorv of illusions but not of pride. He is direct to the point of bluntness, with little patience for flowery language or social niceties—the years of bondage have burned away such softness. He carries a grim humor, dark and often bitter, that occasionally surfaces in sardonic comments about the absurdity of his situation. With his fellow workers, he shows unexpected kindness and solidarity; he shares his meager portions with the weaker laborers and has developed a reputation as someone who can be relied upon in emergencies.

Ulthorv's defining characteristic, however, is his stubborn refusal to accept his fate as permanent. Others have resigned themselves to dying on the estate, becoming hollow shells going through the motions of work. Ulthorv has not. Each day, he studies the guards' routines, assesses the roads and the lie of the land, and plans. He is not broken, only confined. This makes him dangerous to those who profit from his captivity.

### Motivation

Ulthorv burns with a singular focus: freedom. That freedom may come through legitimate appeal, a miracle of the law, or violent escape; he will pursue it with every ounce of his considerable will. Beyond freedom itself, he is driven by a deep need for justice—to see the mine officials who orchestrated his enslavement held accountable, to expose the conspiracy that cost innocent lives, and to reclaim the dignity that was stolen from him.

### Strengths

- **Phenomenal Physical Strength** - Eight years of forced labor, after a decade in the mines, have left Ulthorv a monument of functional muscle. He can lift loads that would require teams of ordinary workers, and his endurance in physical labor is almost superhuman.
- **Endurance** - Beyond strength, his will to persist through exhaustion is extraordinary. He can work through pain and fatigue that would collapse lesser men, a trait that has kept him alive when others have perished.
- **Keen Mineralogical Knowledge** - His decade in the mines gave him an intimate understanding of rock types, ore deposits, and geological formations. He can navigate unmapped tunnels and read the earth like a book.
- **Tactical Mining Engineering** - Though trained as a laborer rather than an engineer, his practical experience has given him intuitive understanding of how tunnels are structured, where weaknesses exist, and how to navigate or even collapse sections deliberately.
- **Loyal Companion** - To those he considers truly allied, Ulthorv is as loyal as mountains are immovable. He will shield companions with his own body and has on multiple occasions.
- **Resilience and Survival** - He has survived conditions that have killed stronger men simply through sheer determination and practical knowledge of how to endure in the harsh conditions of bondage.

### Weaknesses

- **Reckless Impetuousness** - His escape attempts, while understandable, have been increasingly desperate and poorly planned, often endangering himself and others. Desperation overrides planning.
- **Damaged Trust in Authority** - His betrayal by the legal system has left him deeply cynical about legitimate channels. He's increasingly inclined to reject any solution that doesn't involve either personal action or revolutionary change.
- **Limited Education** - Though intelligent, Ulthorv's practical experience hasn't equipped him with the literacy, formal knowledge, or political understanding that might help him win vindication in law.
- **Physical Limitations Beyond Strength** - While very strong, he is not particularly agile or swift. His combat skills are raw rather than refined, and he would be outmatched by skilled warriors despite his power.
- **Isolation** - Years of bondage have isolated him from the broader world. He knows little of what has transpired outside, and his understanding of politics and society is now years out of date.
- **Bearing of Trauma** - The weight of captivity on body and mind has left scars that even freedom might not fully heal. He struggles with rage that sometimes surfaces unpredictably.

## Social

### Patrons

None formally, though older laborers and fellow workers view him as a de facto leader, and there are whispers among the enslaved workers that **Ulthorv's Mercy**—his willingness to share provisions with the dying—represents a kind of moral leadership the estate hasn't seen in generations.

### Enemies

Overseer [[being-knalthyrathe|Knalthira the Merciless]]
: The brutal taskmaster directly responsible for most of Ulthorv's torments. Knalthira takes personal pleasure in crushing the spirit of the enslaved and has specifically targeted Ulthorv for additional punishment because of his refusal to break.

The Landvordur [[being-gnarthorv|Gnarthorv]]
: The corrupt collector of the king's dues and holder of his court who orchestrated Ulthorv's enslavement, initially to silence him about the tunnel collapse, and who now maintains vested financial interest in keeping him enslaved and controllable.

The Contractors
: The men who buy what the unfree produce before the king's dues are counted, who profit from unfree labor and have an interest in keeping Ulthorv in it.

### Affiliations

The Enslaved Workers
: - Though he has no formal group affiliation, Ulthorv is increasingly seen as a leader figure among the unfree laborers, a symbol of resistance and dignity in a system designed to strip both away.

## Plot Hooks

The King's Officer
: An officer of the crown rides through the jarl's estate near [[place-horgsvangur|Horgsvangur]] where Ulthorv is kept, to count what the king is owed. It is the one day a free officer stands among the unfree. Ulthorv holds the record of the collapse that was covered up, and can reach him with it, but if the Landvordur Gnarthorv hears of it first, Ulthorv is sent where no officer rides. He can speak to the officer, find a free man to carry the word, or wait for an officer who owes Gnarthorv nothing.

The Road Out
: Ulthorv has tried six times to leave. The first carts down the road after the thaw are the only ones that leave unwatched. He can hide in a cart and risk the drop at the pass, or send a younger man in his place and stay.

The Overseers' Stair
: Ulthorv finds the overseers' store-loft above the estate's byre held up by one rotten beam. Brought down at the right moment it would bury the overseers' stores and arms. It would also fall on whoever is working in the byre below. The Word from Other Estates
: An escaped worker called [Flarninda Frostborn]{.name data-type="being/npc"} reaches Ulthorv with a plan to rise at once in several estates of the kingdom, inspired by tales of his refusal to submit. A rising that fails will be crushed and the estates made harder. He can join, ask for time, or refuse and be called a coward by those who believe the stories.

:::secret {title="What the Game Master knows"}
Laborers cutting a new store-pit on the estate break into the ruin of a structure far older than the estate, holding objects and knowledge that could change the politics of the north. Treasure-seekers and the king's men both want it, and whoever holds Ulthorv's story holds the one witness to how the overseers have been hiding what lies under the estate.
:::
