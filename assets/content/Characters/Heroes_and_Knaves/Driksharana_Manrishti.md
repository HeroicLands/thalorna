# Appearance {#appearance}

![[drikaranamn|Driksharana Manrishti]]{float=top-left}

Driksharana stands 5'7" tall with a light build. He has warm honey skin, dark brown hair, and deep brown eyes. His features include a round face, a narrow nose, a soft jawline, a wide mouth, dark brows, soft cheeks. A distinguishing mark is a tattoo of a waterfall on the shoulder.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Born in one of the nine quarters of [[place-chendrapur2|The Moon City]], Driksharana was the youngest of seven children to a textile weaver's family. His childhood was marked by modest comfort and the constant hum of looms, his mother's lullabies for her youngest, whom she called Driksha, layered beneath the rhythmic clacking of threads. At fourteen, he was apprenticed to the household of [Vedastira]{.name data-type="being/npc"}, a rising administrator in the household of one of [[affiliation-chendrapur|the Nine Houses]], where his natural grace and keen observational abilities quickly earned him a position as personal attendant. It was during these formative years that he received the waterfall tattoo—a spiritual marker from a wandering priest of [[affiliation-rasikara|Rasikara]] who saw in the young Driksharana the fluidity and adaptability that would define his path.

For the past decade, Driksharana has served his current employer with dedication, managing the social choreography that accompanies life in a great-house household. His duties range from ceremonial service at grand feasts to the subtle work of anticipating his master's needs before they are voiced. He has become indispensable to the household's smooth operation, yet he remains largely invisible—the way a truly skilled servant becomes part of the architecture of privilege itself.

## Psyche

### Personality

Driksharana possesses a quiet observational intelligence that borders on prescience; he notices the small tensions in a room before they surface, reads the unspoken desires in a guest's glance, and moves through the world with an almost meditative calm. Yet beneath this serene exterior lies a sensitive soul prone to overthinking, to internalizing slights that others would dismiss, and to doubting his own worth despite consistent validation from those who rely upon him. He is unfailingly polite—sometimes painfully so—and his courtesy can veer into self-effacement. He speaks with precision when addressed, prefers listening to conversation, and possesses a dry, understated humor that catches people off guard when it emerges.

### Motivation

Driksharana seeks neither power nor wealth, but rather a deeper sense of purpose and belonging. The tattoo he wears is more than decoration; it represents his belief that life, like water, should flow around obstacles rather than resist them. He harbors a quiet ambition to earn respect—not through dominance but through becoming so perfectly attuned to those around him that his service goes beyond duty and becomes something approaching art. Beneath this lies a half-buried longing to understand the world beyond the servants' passages, to trace the currents that move through power itself.

### Strengths

Attentiveness
: Driksharana possesses an almost supernatural awareness of his surroundings and the emotional states of those near him, allowing him to anticipate needs and respond with grace.

Social Grace
: Years of service in elite households have honed his understanding of precedence, etiquette, and the unspoken rules governing polite society.

Resilience Through Flexibility
: Like the waterfall of his tattoo, he adapts to circumstance, bends without breaking, and finds paths forward when direct resistance would fail.

Culinary and Textile Skills
: His domestic expertise ranges from expert cookery to fine needlework, making him useful in household management.

Climbing and Acrobatic Ability
: His light frame and years of navigating grand estates have made him surprisingly nimble, able to reach high shelves or move through spaces others cannot access.

### Weaknesses

Lack of Assertiveness
: His deferential nature makes him vulnerable to manipulation and exploitation; he finds it nearly impossible to refuse even unreasonable demands.

Self-Doubt
: Despite his competence, he constantly questions his value and second-guesses his decisions.

Limited Martial Ability
: His gentle nature and lack of combat training leave him defenseless against violence or physical aggression.

Social Invisibility
: While his discretion is an asset, it has left him without a true circle of peers; he is known but not truly known.

Emotional Vulnerability
: He internalizes stress and criticism, leading to periods of quiet melancholy that can affect his work.

## Social

### Patrons

Vedastira
: The merchant administrator who first employed Driksharana now advocates for him as the finest house servant in the city. Their relationship is one of quiet mutual respect, though Driksharana often fears disappointing his patron.

[Chintaya]{.name data-type="being/npc"}
: Vedastira's wife has grown to rely on Driksharana's counsel in matters of household aesthetics and entertaining; she treats him with warmth rare in the great-house households.

### Enemies

[Kharun Manrishti]{.name data-type="being/npc"} (distant cousin)
: A fellow servant from a rival household who resents Driksharana's reputation and spreads subtle rumors about his competence and loyalty.

The Hierarchy Itself
: Driksharana's greatest adversary is the rigid structure of servitude; several higher-ranking servants view him as a threat to their position and work to undermine his standing.

### Affiliations

The servants' shrena
: Driksharana maintains loose ties with the city's servants' shrena, which advocates for fair treatment and standard wages in the households of the nine quarters.

## Plot Hooks

A Rumor Carried Across a Wall
: Kharun Manrishti, a fellow servant in a rival household, spreads the word that Driksharana carries tales between the houses. A dispute between two men of one quarter goes to its Kulena and a dispute across a wall goes to the court, so Driksharana must choose which hearing to ask for, and he finds it nearly impossible to press a claim of his own.

Standard Wages in the Nine Quarters
: The servants' shrena, with which Driksharana keeps loose ties, asks him to speak for standard wages at a meeting of the households. Speaking marks him to the higher-ranking servants who already see him as a threat, and keeping silent disappoints the shrena.

:::secret {title="Further hooks"}
A Crime Seen over the Master's Shoulder
: While attending his master at a diplomatic gathering, Driksharana sees a high-ranking official commit a crime. His place gives him access to the conversations that could expose it, but speaking would destroy the political alliance of the household he serves, and silence leaves the crime unanswered.

Passages beneath the Lower Courses
: Some of the servants Driksharana trusts belong to a network that moves runaway bonded servants, refugees and the persecuted through hidden passages in [[place-lowercourses|The Lower Courses of Chendrapur]]. He must decide whether to join it or keep his comfortable ignorance.

Vedastira's Hidden Child
: Vedastira fathered a child with a servant woman, denied it and paid to keep it hidden. When the child surfaces, Driksharana must choose between his master's reputation and the child's claim, and parties hostile to the family are ready to use the scandal.

:::
