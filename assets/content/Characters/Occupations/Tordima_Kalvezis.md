# Appearance {#appearance}

Tordima Kalvezis is a 48-year-old woman who stands 5'6" tall and is of light build. She has light, lined skin, brown hair going gray under a plain hood and gray eyes. The fingers of her right hand are stained with ink to the second joint, and she keeps that hand in her sleeve when she is not writing.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Tordima Kalvezis is a Limor of the [[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]], one of the Threshold Keepers who say the funeral rites and keep the temple's record of the dead. She keeps the record at [[place-durvalis|Durvalis]], the fortress town of [[place-talothe|the Talote]], and every winter, while the passes are shut and the [Winter Assize]{.name data-type="affiliation/governmental"} sits, she enters the season's dead of the frontier in it by name: the garrison's, the border families' and, where anyone can tell her their names, the Tarvenans'.

The record has a gap she cannot close. Men of both sides die on the road below the pass in most seasons, at the [[place-bldfldmalnebris|Blood-field of Malnebris]], and nobody brings them home to be entered. The faith's priests hold that the field was made once, by the first raid, and is not being remade. Tordima keeps to that teaching, and she still wants the rite of settlement said on the field, because the dead who lie there were never placed in the god's keeping, whoever they were. The rite would have to be said in the campaign season, on ground both crowns claim, and neither crown will give the other's priests safe conduct. She has asked her own crown's marshal twice and been refused.

## Psyche

### Personality

Tordima is quiet, exact and stubborn. She says what the rite can do and what it cannot, and she says that it works perhaps half the time.

### Motivation

She wants every name of the frontier's dead in the record and the dead of Malnebris placed in the god's keeping before she is too old to walk up the pass.

### Strengths

She knows the frontier's families on both sides by their dead, she reads and writes both tongues of the march, and she is not afraid of the field.

### Weaknesses

She will not say the rite under a lie, and she will not walk onto the field under arms or behind them.

## Social

### Companions

The garrison's chaplain, the gravediggers of Durvalis and the herald who receives the bough-carriers at the gate, [[being-arislkrsvr|Arisla Korisvar]].

### Patrons

The temple of Sélmoros that sent her to the frontier.

### Enemies

None; the marshal's officers find her tiresome.

### Affiliations

- **[[affiliation-selmoros|Faith of Sélmoros]]**: Tordima holds the office of Limor, a Threshold Keeper.
- **[[affiliation-kngdmprvnz|Kingdom of Provénzia]]**: She is a Freeman of the kingdom.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The names from the far side.** Tordima asks a party going up the pass to bring her the names of the Tarvenans who died on the road below it last season, so that she can enter them.
2. **The rite on the field.** She wants a way to reach Malnebris in the season without a crown's leave, and she will hear any proposal that does not put her behind a sword.
3. **The unclaimed man.** A man of the garrison died over the winter with no family to bury him and a Tarvenan name on the cord round his neck, and Tordima wants to know which name to write.
