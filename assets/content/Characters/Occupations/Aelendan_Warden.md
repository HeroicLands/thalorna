# Appearance {#appearance}

An Áelendan Warden meets travelers at the margin of sacred ground, equipped for long watches outdoors. A spear, bow, dog and a kept fire belong to the order’s ordinary watch. Clothing and age vary with the individual and the territory.

# Dossier {#dossier}

The [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] keep sacred boundaries through long watches and close knowledge of the land. A patrol answers for its own territory and carries warnings to anyone approaching forbidden ground.

## Background

Wardens come from Áelendan communities by family line or tribal selection and train from youth in tracking, archery, survival, sacred geography, fae lore and [[skill-aelendlng|Áelendi Language]]. They answer to their territory’s recognized Elder Wardens and tribal tradition. The order has no central headquarters.

## Psyche

### Personality

A Warden states a boundary plainly and expects an answer. Individual manners vary; the duty does not require cruelty or willingness to debate the existence of the boundary.

### Motivation

Keep the sacred site and the terms of the Kindred Pact.

### Strengths

Knowledge of local ground, patient watches and practiced cooperation with a partner make the keeper difficult to bypass unnoticed.

### Weaknesses

The duty is tied to a place. Pursuing a trespasser away from the margin can leave the charge unguarded.

## Social

### Affiliations and Companions

The Wardens of the patrol territory, their Elder Wardens and their tribal communities sustain the watch. A paired patrol keeps a time-disturbing Araelwen from outside and does not enter it. The order warns an accidental trespasser once and escorts the person out; a return or desecration brings its severe response. A working of the art inside an Araelwen counts as desecration.
