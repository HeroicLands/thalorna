# Appearance {#appearance}

Mirthana Kervanion is a 22-year-old woman who stands 5'8" tall and is slender. She has an oval face with wide-set cheekbones, a wide forehead, and a strong jaw that leads to a square chin. Her hooded green eyes sit beneath heavy brows, lending her a guarded gaze. A prominent nose and expressive lips complete her features. She has medium-toned skin with a weathered complexion. Her black hair is pinned in an elegant twist.

# Dossier {#dossier}

Born in the [[place-helionis|Helionis]] region to a freeman family of [[lore-helioniteclt|Helionite]] heritage, Mirthana Kervanion came into the world of the man-at-arms (sapper) through a combination of circumstance and aptitude.

Mirthana grew up in the town and was drawn to the art of engineering. She has trained as a sapper and quickly gained a reputation for her skills in tunnel construction and demolition. Known for her quick thinking and resourcefulness, Mirthana often devises creative solutions to challenges. She aspires to become a master sapper and help design fortifications.

## Psyche

### Personality

Sometimes overconfident, struggles with heavy lifting.

### Motivation

Mirthana is driven by the desire to master her craft and secure a stable future. The uncertainties of life in man-at-arms (sapper) work keep her vigilant and adaptable.

### Strengths

Creative problem solver, skilled in demolition, agile.

## Social

Mirthana is affiliated with the local [Engineers' Guild]{.name data-type="affiliation/guild"}.

As a Helionite man-at-arms (sapper), Mirthana occupies a recognized social niche within Helionis society.

## Companions

### Patrons

Mirthana's primary patrons are local military leaders and builders. This relationship provides both opportunity and obligation.

### Enemies

## Plot Hooks

A Charge Near the Vents
: The alum and sulfur at [[place-sufanos|Sufanos]] come from vents, and a following of [[affiliation-blackflame|The Black Flame]] keeps a shrine beside them. Mirthana is asked to bring down a rock face near them, and she is overconfident about her charge.

Walls for the Marble Road
: The one paved road to [[place-hadrithon|Hadrithon]] is the weak point of [[place-korinthea2|Korinthea]]'s marble supply, and marauders take the oxen. The Engineers' Guild asks Mirthana to plan a fortified stretch, her first chance at the design work she wants.

The Master's Trial Cut
: The Engineers' Guild sets Mirthana a trial before it will name her master sapper: a cut through the rock above the paved road to Hadrithon, planned and fired by her own team. The rock needs more hauling than she can do herself, so she must choose which of her team to trust with the charge.
